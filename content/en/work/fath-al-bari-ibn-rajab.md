## Summary
*Fath al-bari, sharh Sahih al-Bukhari* is Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali's (d. 795 AH) commentary on Bukhari's collection ([[person/ibn-rajab|his biography]]). It was left unfinished. It opens with the book of faith and takes Bukhari's chapters one by one. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 7}

## What volume one covers
Volume one covers the book of faith and the opening of the book of ghusl. The title page says the commentary reached the book of funerals, and quotes a remark that completing it would have been one of the marvels of the age. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 1}

## Method
Each chapter opens with its number and title as Bukhari gives them. The commentary then explains the words, and then the views of scholars on the question. For the first chapter, "Islam is built on five", it sets out the dispute over whether action ('amal) is nobler than doing (fi'l). It answers the view that action is nobler by noting that the Qur'an also calls the doing of evil deeds 'amal', and suggests the reverse argument would be stronger. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 7}

The commentary relies on verses and hadiths, gathers scholars' views on each question, and uses legal-theory points and the meanings of words to explain the hadiths. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 7}

## Structure of the volume
The volume follows the chapters of the book of faith, from the first, "Islam is built on five", through "Your calling on Him is your faith" and "Matters of faith", to later chapters on the Islam of a person, faith, modesty and prayer. The first chapters begin on scan pages 7, 21 and 31. The volume ends in the book of ghusl, on scan page 393, at the chapter on washing what touches a woman's private parts. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 21}, {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 31}, {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 393}

## A note on the source
The views and chains in this record are those given by Ibn Rajab and the editors, and they have not been re-checked against other books. Page numbers are scan pages. {cite:fath-al-bari-ibn-rajab-1417 p. 7}
