## Summary

*Qur'an and Woman: Rereading the Sacred Text from a Woman's Perspective* is an English book by Amina Wadud, first published in Kuala Lumpur by Fajar Bakti with Oxford University Press in 1992 in 118 pages. {cite:openlibrary-quran-and-woman-1992} A revised edition appeared from Oxford University Press in New York in 1999. According to its contents, the chapters treat how perceptions of woman influence the interpretation of the Qur'an, human creation, the Qur'anic view of woman in this world, recompense in the hereafter, and the rights and roles of women and the controversies about them, closing with an appendix listing the female characters mentioned in the Qur'an. The publisher's description calls it an interpretive reading arguing that patriarchal interpretation, not the religion itself, has limited women's role. {cite:archive-org-quran-and-woman-1999}
