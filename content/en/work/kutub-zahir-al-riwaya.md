## Summary
The six authoritative books of the Hanafi school, the zahir al-riwaya corpus. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2552}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
Zahir al-riwayah, in The Oxford Dictionary of Islam, is the manifest meaning of a text — the canonical writings of a law school's eponym and his disciples, which form the doctrinal skeleton of the madhhab, arguments running against them being discredited. For the Hanafis that canon is the six books of Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani, the subject of this record. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2552}
