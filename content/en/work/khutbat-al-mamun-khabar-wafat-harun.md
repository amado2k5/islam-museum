## Summary
Al-Ma'mun's address on receiving news of Harun al-Rashid's death in 193 AH. He offers condolence, reminds the people of their pledge and promises fairness, and gives a second address on accepting the caliphate.

The book gives two addresses by al-Ma'mun from Uyun al-Akhbar and al-Tabari. The first, on news of Harun's death, offers condolence and reminds the listeners that death comes to all. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.119}

The second, at the people's pledge of allegiance, promises not to shed blood except rightfully and not to take anyone's property without right. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.119}
