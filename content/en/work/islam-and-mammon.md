## Summary

*Islam and Mammon: The Economic Predicaments of Islamism* is an English book by the economist Timur Kuran, published by Princeton University Press on 12 April 2004 in 232 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islam-and-mammon-2004} {cite:google-books-islam-and-mammon} The publisher's description says the doctrine of "Islamic economics" entered debate in the mid-twentieth century, that its most visible practical achievement is banks meant to avoid interest, and that Kuran criticises it as simplistic and incoherent. According to its contents, its chapters treat the economic impact of Islamism, Islamic economics, its genesis within the politics of Muslim identity, the notion of economic justice in contemporary Islamic thought, and Islam and underdevelopment. {cite:archive-org-islam-and-mammon-2004}
