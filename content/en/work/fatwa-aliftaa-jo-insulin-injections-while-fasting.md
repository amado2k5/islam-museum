## Summary

A question about insulin injections while fasting, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that they do not break the fast because they go under the skin and do not reach the interior. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4146}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on an insulin injection for someone fasting. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4146}

## The answer

The Department answered that an insulin injection does not break the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4146} It explained that the injection is under the skin and does not pass into the body's interior. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4146}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4146, dated 1 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4146} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
