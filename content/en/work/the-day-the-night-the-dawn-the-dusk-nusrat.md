## Summary
The Day, the Night, the Dawn, the Dusk is a qawwali album by the Pakistani singer [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] on Shanachie, with three long pieces of his own; it runs 54 minutes. {cite:wikipedia-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk-en} {cite:wikidata-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk} The article dates it 1991 and Wikidata 1990; the museum records both. {cite:wikipedia-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk-en} {cite:wikidata-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk}

## The pieces
'Halka Halka Suroor' runs 18 minutes, 'Mera Piya Ghar Aaya' 8 and 'Main Jana Jogi De Naal' 27. {cite:wikipedia-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk-en} The Washington Post wrote in 1991 that the lyrics, embracing both Sufi mysticism and matters of the heart, are sung, chanted and declaimed, adding to qawwali's hypnotic and cathartic appeal and compensating for the language barrier. {cite:wikipedia-the-day-the-night-the-dawn-the-dusk-en}
