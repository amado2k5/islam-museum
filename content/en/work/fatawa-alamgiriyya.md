## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam the Fatawa al-Alamgiriyya is a collection of the authoritative Hanafi legal opinions. It was ordered by the Mughal ruler Aurangzeb Alamgir, who ruled from 1658 until 1707, and was prepared by a group of religious scholars under the direction of Shaykh Nizam Burhanpuri. The Dictionary ranks it among the fullest Hanafi compendia and says it still carries great weight in South Asia. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e638}
