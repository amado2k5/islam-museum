## Summary

A question about a new Muslim who is afraid of circumcision, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that he is not required to be circumcised if a trustworthy doctor says it would harm him, and if it is only fear it may be postponed until he is at ease and not put off Islam, while keeping the area free of impurity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4377}

## The question

The questioner asked about someone who has recently become Muslim and is afraid of having a circumcision. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4377}

## The answer

The Department answered that if this person would be harmed by circumcision, based on the opinion of a trustworthy doctor, he is not required to have it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4377} It said that if it is merely fear of the operation, it may be postponed until he feels at ease with it and is not put off Islam, while taking care to remove impurity from the covered area. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4377}
