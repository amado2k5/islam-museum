## Summary
Not Afraid to Stand Alone is the second studio album of the American hip-hop group [[group/native-deen|Native Deen]], released in November 2007 on the group's own label, recorded at Omega Recording Studios in Maryland and produced by its member Abdul-Malik Ahmad; it runs 75 minutes. {cite:wikipedia-not-afraid-to-stand-alone-en} {cite:wikidata-not-afraid-to-stand-alone}

## What it is
The album mixes fusion, R&B and hip-hop, uses only percussion instruments, and was also released in a voice-only version. {cite:wikipedia-not-afraid-to-stand-alone-en} Its themes range over praise of God, the hardships of being a Muslim, steadfastness in practice, fear of God, the value of life, prayer, the Prophet, dawah, the hajj, trust in God and pride in being Muslim; the article, citing a review in Black Grooves, names as its main novelty South African-style singing and darker reflections on the discrimination Muslims face in America. {cite:wikipedia-not-afraid-to-stand-alone-en}

## Songs and guests
Its songs include 'Subhan Allah', 'Stand Alone', 'Lord Is Watching', 'Sea of Forgiveness', 'Pray Before...', 'Tala'al Badru', 'The Message', 'Labayk', 'Eid Morning', 'Rain Song' and 'M-U-S-L-I-M'. {cite:wikipedia-not-afraid-to-stand-alone-en} Isam Bachiri of [[group/outlandish|Outlandish]] sings on 'Still Strong' and the South African singer Zain Bhikha on 'Zamilooni', and spoken interludes feature Siraj Wahhaj, Ingrid Mattson and Rami Nashashibi. {cite:wikipedia-not-afraid-to-stand-alone-en}
