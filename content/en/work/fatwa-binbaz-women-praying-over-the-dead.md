## Summary

A question on women attending funerals, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] distinguished praying over the deceased from following the bier to the cemetery. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4012}

## The question

The questioner said it is established that women are barred from visiting graves, and asked whether they may attend funerals. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4012}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that there is no harm in a woman praying over the deceased with the people, noting that women prayed over the Prophet's body. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4012} He said what they are barred from is following the deceased to the cemetery and visiting graves. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4012} He described their praying over the dead together with the people as legitimate. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4012}
