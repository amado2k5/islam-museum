## Overview
The novel by al-Tayib Salih (1966): the return of Mustafa Sa'id from London to a Sudanese village on the Nile, after years in which he embodied what the West wanted, and the tragedy of the encounter between coloniser and colonised is revealed. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It was chosen among the hundred most important novels in history and was translated as 'Season of Migration to the North'; it is taught as a foundational text of postcolonial literature. {cite:wikidata}
