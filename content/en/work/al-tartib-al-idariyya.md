## Summary
Al-Tartib al-Idariyya, presented by Tareq al-Suwaidan as the oldest printed Muslim reference on administration, written in the seventh Hijri century around 600 AH, and still in print in bookshops {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:17:30}.

## Its contents
Al-Suwaidan explains that its author produced a job description for every post of the state: the judge, his qualities and duties and whom he reports to, the army commander and the other posts — which he takes as proof that the questions of planning and organisation were already settled among Muslims then (needs_source: the author's name is garbled in the transcript) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:18:00}.
In the same context he mentions that Umar ibn al-Khattab built the diwans (ministries) on the Persian system with heavy development of it {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:18:30}.
