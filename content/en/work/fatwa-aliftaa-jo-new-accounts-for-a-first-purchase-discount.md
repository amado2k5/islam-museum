## Summary

A question about opening new accounts to get a first-purchase discount, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a Muslim must be truthful, and that opening a new account for the same buyer must respect the site's rules; if it breaches them it is not permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4475}

## The question

The questioner asked about opening a new account each time a customer buys from an online store that gives a discount on the first purchase. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4475}

## The answer

The Department answered that a Muslim must be truthful in word and deed, citing [[passage/quran-8-27|Surat al-Anfal 8:27]], [[passage/quran-9-119|Surat al-Tawba 9:119]] and a hadith it attributes to [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] whose meaning is that whoever cheats the Muslims is not of them. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4475} It said that opening a new account for the same buyer must respect the website's instructions; if it breaches them, it is not permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4475}

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4475, dated 21 May 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4475} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
