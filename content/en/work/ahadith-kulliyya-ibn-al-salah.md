## Overview
A session dictated by the hadith scholar [[person/ibn-al-salah|Abu Amr ibn al-Salah]], in which he gathered the comprehensive hadiths on which the religion is said to rest, and similar brief, comprehensive sayings. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.26}

## Contents
It contains twenty-six hadiths. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.26}

## Its link to the Forty
Al-Nawawi took these hadiths and added to them until they reached forty-two, which is the [[work/arbaun-nawawi|Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi]]. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.26}
