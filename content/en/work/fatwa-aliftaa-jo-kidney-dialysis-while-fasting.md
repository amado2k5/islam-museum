## Summary

A question about kidney dialysis while fasting in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it breaks the fast, that the patient must make the day up, and that if he cannot, he pays the fidya. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4151}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on kidney dialysis for someone fasting in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4151}

## The answer

The Department answered that kidney dialysis breaks the fast, because, as physicians know, the dialysis fluid is nourishing, and because it causes substances to enter the body's interior. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4151} It said that the patient must make that day up after Ramadan, and if he cannot, he pays the fidya, feeding one poor person for each day not fasted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4151}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4151, dated 2 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4151} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
