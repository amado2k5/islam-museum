## Summary

A question about fasting a Friday, Saturday or Sunday on its own, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that for an unrestricted voluntary fast it is disliked to single out any of these days, and that there is no dislike if a day before or after is added or the day coincides with a day it is sunna to fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4175}

## The question

The questioner asked about fasting a Friday, a Saturday or a Sunday on its own. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4175}

## The answer

The Department answered that for an unrestricted voluntary fast it is disliked to single out a Friday, a Saturday or a Sunday for fasting. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4175} It said there is no harm if one fasts a day before or after the Friday, and likewise with Saturday and Sunday, and no dislike in fasting Friday, Saturday and Sunday together. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4175} It added that if one of these days coincides with a day it is sunna to fast, such as the day of Arafa or Ashura, there is no dislike in fasting it on its own. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4175}
