## Summary

*Safwat al-Tafasir* is a commentary on the Qur'an by Muhammad Ali al-Sabuni; its subtitle describes it as combining the transmitted (*al-ma'thur*) and the reasoned (*al-ma'qul*). {cite:archive-org-safwat-al-tafasir-bibalex} The title page of the scanned copy names the author as a professor at the Faculty of Sharia of Umm al-Qura University in Mecca. {cite:archive-org-safwat-al-tafasir-bibalex} Each sura opens with an introduction that outlines its themes; Surat Fussilat, for example, is introduced as a Meccan sura dealing with creed: the oneness of God, the message, resurrection and recompense. {cite:archive-org-safwat-al-tafasir-bibalex}

The Bibliotheca Alexandrina collection on the Internet Archive holds scans of its volumes, catalogued under Dar al-Qur'an al-Karim, 1981; one volume states that it was printed in Riyadh by the Saudi Arabian Printing Company. {cite:archive-org-safwat-al-tafasir-bibalex} Selections have been translated into English, among them the commentary on Surat al-Kahf, which the bookseller Wardah Books lists under al-Sabuni's name. {cite:wardahbooks-safwat-al-tafasir-selections}
