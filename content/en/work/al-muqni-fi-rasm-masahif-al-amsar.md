## Summary
Al-Muqni' fi Ma'rifat Marsum Masahif Ahl al-Amsar by Abu Amr Uthman ibn Sa'id al-Dani is printed. In the introduction to the Itqan al-Suyuti lists it first among 'the books of rasm', with al-Sakhawi's and Ibn Jubara's commentaries on the Ra'iyya, that is, on [[work/aqilat-atrab-al-qasaid|'Aqilat Atrab al-Qasa'id]]. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 37} The bibliography of the King Fahd edition gives it as al-Muqni' fi Rasm Masahif al-Amsar, edited by Muhammad Ahmad Dahman, Dar al-Fikr, Damascus, 1403 AH. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 3028}

## What the notes of the Itqan show
The notes cite the Muqni' at points where eastern and western spelling differ, such as the first alif of 'Haman', which the Maghribis keep and the Mashriqis drop, and the alif of lil-dar and lilladhi, dropped when the preposition lam joins the alif of the hamzat al-wasl. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2202} They also report al-Dani in the Muqni' saying Ikrima, the reporter from 'Uthman, did not meet him, and the editor on that ground rules a report about 'Uthman's mushaf cut off. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 1237} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 1238}

## A modern announcement
Otto Pretzl, who edited the Taysir, says he will follow it with the publication of al-Muqni' fi Ma'rifat Rasm Masahif al-Amsar together with the Kitab al-Naqt. {cite:taysir-pretzl-istanbul-1930 p. 10} See [[work/al-taysir-fi-al-qiraat-al-sab|al-Taysir]] and [[work/al-muhkam-fi-naqt-al-masahif|al-Muhkam fi Naqt al-Masahif]].

## A note
Do not confuse this book with [[work/al-muqni|al-Muqni']] of Hanbali jurisprudence, which shares its short name. The text itself was not read for this record, so nothing is attributed to it beyond what the notes of the Itqan state.
