## Summary
A series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "Min Kunuz al-Sunna" (From the Treasures of the Sunna), pausing at Prophetic hadiths, explaining them and drawing lessons. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 2: part of the excellence of a person's Islam is leaving what does not concern him
- He says Anas reported from the Prophet a few words of deep meaning, part of the excellence of a person's Islam is his leaving what does not concern him, and that it bears on the safety of society and of people from people. See [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]]. - He describes curiosity as seeing a strange car at a neighbour's door and becoming busy with who came and why, says the curious person spies on people's secrets, and that leaving what does not concern one brings rest to him and others. See [[concept/fudul|curiosity]] and [[concept/tajassus-wa-tahassus|spying and prying]]. See [[concept/tashabbuh-imitation|imitation]]. This is his view.
