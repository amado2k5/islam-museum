## Summary

*Islam and the Moral Economy: The Challenge of Capitalism* is an English book by the politics scholar Charles Tripp, published by Cambridge University Press in 2006 in 229 pages, with a bibliography on pages 202–224. {cite:openlibrary-islam-and-the-moral-economy-2006} {cite:archive-org-islam-and-the-moral-economy-2006} The publisher's description says the book examines the challenge capitalism has posed to Muslims over the last sixty years through the works of noted Muslim scholars, that it is framed around the development of their ideas on Islamic socialism, Islamic economics and the rationale for Islamic banking, and that most of them sought innovation, compromise and interaction with global capitalism. {cite:archive-org-islam-and-the-moral-economy-description}
