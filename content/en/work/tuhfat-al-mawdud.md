## Overview

Tuhfat al-Mawdud bi-Ahkam al-Mawlud by Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya (d. 751 AH), on the rulings of the newborn — 'aqiqa, naming and the like; Hajji Khalifa lists it under the title 'Ahkam al-Mawlud' {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 215}.
