## Summary

A question about using eye drops while fasting, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that they do not invalidate the fast, even if the fasting person tastes them in the throat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4117}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on eye drops for someone who is fasting. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4117}

## The answer

The Department answered that eye drops do not invalidate the fast, even if the fasting person tastes them in the throat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4117} It explained that the eye is not an open passage to the body's interior (al-jawf). {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4117}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4117, dated 27 January 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4117} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
