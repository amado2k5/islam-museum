## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with four episodes; it was the first series on which the museum tried its method for recorded lessons. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} In the first episode he says that Islam has many enemies, and prays that God make his listeners defenders of His religion and His Book. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:03:00} He holds that among Muslims there are some who show hostility to Islam at every turn under the banner of being Muslims, and whose words and conduct are not those of Muslims. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:06:00} This is the speaker's view and judgement; the museum reports it as his and does not adopt it.
