## Summary

*Misquoting Muhammad: The Challenge and Choices of Interpreting the Prophet's Legacy* is a book by Jonathan A. C. Brown, first published by Oneworld in North America, Great Britain and Australia in 2014. {cite:archive-misquoting-muhammad-pdf}

## Publication and editions

It has seven chapters and four appendices; the second chapter draws a map of the Islamic interpretive tradition, from Abu Hanifa, Malik and al-Shafi'i to the collection and criticism of hadiths, legal theory, Sufism and revival movements. {cite:archive-misquoting-muhammad-pdf} Later chapters deal with the fragile truth of scripture, clinging to the canon in a ruptured world, what he calls 'Muslim Martin Luthers', the use of unreliable hadiths, and cases where scripture seemingly cannot be taken at face value; the appendices assess particular hadiths by the ratings of hadith critics. {cite:archive-misquoting-muhammad-pdf}
