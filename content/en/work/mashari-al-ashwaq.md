## Overview
A full book on holy war written by [[person/ibn-al-nahhas-al-dimashqi|Ibn al-Nahhas al-Dimashqi]], which he abridged himself. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.113-114}

## The author's fate
Ibn al-Imad reports that the author said, in the first rhymed prose of the book: "I praise you, O God, and ask you for the highest ranks of martyrdom." He was then killed at Damietta in battle with the Franks, and this was counted as an answer to his prayer. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.113-114}
