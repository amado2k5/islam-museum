## Summary
A lesson series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "The Hereafter" (al-Dar al-Akhira), thirty-three episodes in the Assabile archive, on what befalls a person from the deathbed to the grave and beyond, with practical etiquette around death. Episode 1 opens with a list of questions on the punishment and comfort of the grave and on whether reward reaches the dead, then a preface on death, the etiquette of visiting, and visiting the sick. Episode 2 covers the Prophet's death, the dying, the will and the first stage of the grave and the angels' questioning; episode 3 covers wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj. The recordings are garbled in many places, so whatever is unclear is marked needs_source. Episodes 4 and 5 cover the causes of punishment in the grave and the deeds that save from it. Episode 6 covers the minor signs of the Hour. Episode 7 covers the rest of the minor signs of the Hour, and episode 8 the major signs: the smoke, the Dajjal, the descent of Jesus, and Gog and Magog. Episode 9 covers resurrection, the gathering and the great intercession. Episode 10 covers the events and names of the Day of Resurrection and the question of abortion. Episode 11 begins the reckoning: being called by name, the books of deeds, God's covering, and wrongs between people. Episode 12 continues the reckoning: the seven in God's shade, the scale, the headings of reckoning, and the reckoning of nations.
