## Summary
A lesson series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "The Hereafter" (al-Dar al-Akhira), thirty-three episodes in the Assabile archive, on what befalls a person from the deathbed to the grave and beyond, with practical etiquette around death. Episode 1 opens with a list of questions on the punishment and comfort of the grave and on whether reward reaches the dead, then a preface on death, the etiquette of visiting, and visiting the sick. Episode 2 covers the Prophet's death, the dying, the will and the first stage of the grave and the angels' questioning; episode 3 covers wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj. The recordings are garbled in many places, so whatever is unclear is marked needs_source. Episodes 4 and 5 cover the causes of punishment in the grave and the deeds that save from it.

## Episode 3: wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj
