## Summary
A lesson series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "The Hereafter" (al-Dar al-Akhira), thirty-three episodes in the Assabile archive, on what befalls a person from the deathbed to the grave and beyond, with practical etiquette around death. Episode 1 opens with a list of questions on the punishment and comfort of the grave and on whether reward reaches the dead, then a preface on death, the etiquette of visiting, and visiting the sick. Episode 2 covers the Prophet's death, the dying, the will and the first stage of the grave and the angels' questioning; episode 3 covers wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj. The recordings are garbled in many places, so whatever is unclear is marked needs_source.

## Episode 3: wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj

- He presents what he gives as Abu Bakr's counsel to Umar: God has a due by night He will not accept by day and a due by day He will not accept by night, and He accepts no voluntary act until the obligatory one is done. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dar-al-akhera/ep03 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dar-al-akhera/ep03 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dar-al-akhera/ep03 00:27:30}
- He continues the counsel: the scales ([[concept/mizan|mizan]]) of some are light on the Day of Resurrection because they followed falsehood in this world and found it light, and the scales of others are heavy because they followed truth and found it heavy.
