## Summary
A lesson series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "The Hereafter" (al-Dar al-Akhira), thirty-three episodes in the Assabile archive, on what befalls a person from the deathbed to the grave and beyond, with practical etiquette around death. Episode 1 opens with a list of questions on the punishment and comfort of the grave and on whether reward reaches the dead, then a preface on death, the etiquette of visiting, and visiting the sick. Episode 2 covers the Prophet's death, the dying, the will and the first stage of the grave and the angels' questioning; episode 3 covers wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj. The recordings are garbled in many places, so whatever is unclear is marked needs_source. Episodes 4 and 5 cover the causes of punishment in the grave and the deeds that save from it. Episode 6 covers the minor signs of the Hour. Episode 7 covers the rest of the minor signs of the Hour, and episode 8 the major signs: the smoke, the Dajjal, the descent of Jesus, and Gog and Magog. Episode 9 covers resurrection, the gathering and the great intercession. Episode 10 covers the events and names of the Day of Resurrection and the question of abortion. Episode 11 begins the reckoning: being called by name, the books of deeds, God's covering, and wrongs between people. Episode 12 continues the reckoning: the seven in God's shade, the scale, the headings of reckoning, and the reckoning of nations. Episode 13 covers the scale, the Bridge, the revelation of God, prayer on the Prophet, and kindness to parents. Episode 14 covers crossing the Bridge, Iblis's address, the stations of the righteous, the Pond and the kinds of intercession; episode 15 covers the arches of the Bridge. Episode 16 covers the Pond: Salih's she-camel, the Pond described, and eight kinds kept from it. Episode 17 covers the people of Paradise and of the Fire; episode 18 covers the Fire: fear and the degrees of Hell. Episode 19 covers the Fire: the most comfortable of this world dipped in it, the two breaths of Hell, what is charged to it, and its people's calls. Episode 20 covers the food, drink and clothing of the people of the Fire, and the mountain and valley named in the lecture. Episode 21 covers God's mercy, the intercession of the Prophet and the believers, and bringing the monotheists out of the Fire. Episode 22 begins the talk on Paradise with its keys: repentance, courtesy with God, trial and hope. Episode 23 continues the keys to Paradise: the night prayer, seeking forgiveness, the doors and degrees of Paradise, and what is given in it. Episode 24 covers the doors and degrees of Paradise: the doors of the groups, the four raids, courtesy in recitation, and what is removed from its people on entering. Episode 25 covers what Paradise is built of, the parable of the house and the banquet, the upper chambers and following the imam; the published recording repeats the same lesson about nineteen times. Episode 26 covers the names of Paradise: the abode of peace, of eternity and of lodging, the garden of refuge, the gardens of Eden, the abode of life, the garden of delight, the safe station and the seat of truth. Episode 27 covers the traits of the servants of the Merciful, gentleness in the story of the Bedouin, a listing of major sins by tongue, eye and heart, and warnings against killing and bribery. Episode 28 covers the servants of the Merciful and what they leave: killing, adultery and false witness; moral killing, abortion, the story of the judge Shurayh, kinship and kindness. Episode 29 covers scenes of Paradise in the suras Hud, al-Ra'd, Muhammad and al-Furqan, kindness on the Day of Resurrection, the nearness of Paradise to the godfearing, the returning and guarding servant, and the story of Habib the carpenter. Episode 30 covers the argument of the believer of Pharaoh's household, the worst theft, the straight path, the abode of settled permanence, friends on the Day of Resurrection and vessels of gold in Paradise. Episode 31 covers joining what God ordered joined, fear of the evil reckoning, patience and repelling evil with good, the gardens of Eden, Abraham and the building of the Kaaba, and the three groups of the Event. Episode 32 covers scenes of Paradise in al-Waqia, al-Haqqa, al-Dhariyat and al-Tur: the dying hour, the kinds of people at death, the righteous, night prayer and the joining of offspring to their parents.
