## Summary
A lesson series by Omar Abdelkafy titled "The Hereafter" (al-Dar al-Akhira), thirty-three episodes in the Assabile archive, on what befalls a person from the deathbed to the grave and beyond, with practical etiquette around death. Episode 1 opens with a list of questions on the punishment and comfort of the grave and on whether reward reaches the dead, then a preface on death, the etiquette of visiting, and visiting the sick. Episode 2 covers the Prophet's death, the dying, the will and the first stage of the grave and the angels' questioning; episode 3 covers wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj. The recordings are garbled in many places, so whatever is unclear is marked needs_source. Episodes 4 and 5 cover the causes of punishment in the grave and the deeds that save from it. Episode 6 covers the minor signs of the Hour. Episode 7 covers the rest of the minor signs of the Hour, and episode 8 the major signs: the smoke, the Dajjal, the descent of Jesus, and Gog and Magog. Episode 9 covers resurrection, the gathering and the great intercession. Episode 10 covers the events and names of the Day of Resurrection and the question of abortion. Episode 11 begins the reckoning: being called by name, the books of deeds, God's covering, and wrongs between people. Episode 12 continues the reckoning: the seven in God's shade, the scale, the headings of reckoning, and the reckoning of nations. Episode 13 covers the scale, the Bridge, the revelation of God, prayer on the Prophet, and kindness to parents. Episode 14 covers crossing the Bridge, Iblis's address, the stations of the righteous, the Pond and the kinds of intercession; episode 15 covers the arches of the Bridge. Episode 16 covers the Pond: Salih's she-camel, the Pond described, and eight kinds kept from it. Episode 17 covers the people of Paradise and of the Fire; episode 18 covers the Fire: fear and the degrees of Hell. Episode 19 covers the Fire: the most comfortable of this world dipped in it, the two breaths of Hell, what is charged to it, and its people's calls.

## Episode 3: wailing and condolence, Abu Bakr's will to Umar, and the death of al-Hajjaj
