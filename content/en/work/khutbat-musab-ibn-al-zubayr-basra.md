## Summary
Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr's sermon in Basra in 80 AH, when his brother Abdullah made him governor of Iraq. The book opens with Qur'anic verses, whose text is not reproduced here, and the speaker describes what Pharaoh did to the people. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.180}

During the sermon he gestures toward the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq, naming the regions, which marks his appeal for support across the provinces. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.180}

The book notes that the sermon is recorded in al-Bayan wa al-Tabyin, al-Iqd al-Farid and al-Tabari's history. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.180}
