## Summary

*al-Fiqh al-Islami wa-Adillatuh* is a comparative fiqh encyclopedia by Wahbah al-Zuhayli that sets out legal rulings with their evidence across the schools. It was published by Dar al-Fikr in Damascus; its editions include a third edition of 1409/1989 and a fourth revised edition of 1997. {cite:openlibrary-al-fiqh-al-islami-wa-adillatuh} {cite:archive-org-al-fiqh-al-islami-wa-adillatuh-1989-vol1}
