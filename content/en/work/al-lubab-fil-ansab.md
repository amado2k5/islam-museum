## Overview
"Al-Lubab fi Tahdhib al-Ansab" (The Essence in Refining the Genealogies), an abridgement by Izz al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad, Ibn al-Athir al-Jazari, who died in 630 AH, of al-Samani's book "Al-Ansab". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 653-654}

## What Hajji Khalifa reports
Ibn al-Athir added things to it and made corrections to what had been left out. It is in three volumes, and he finished it in Jumada al-Ula 615 AH. According to Ibn Khallikan, it is better than the original. Al-Suyuti then abridged it and called the abridgement "Lubb al-Lubab". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 653-654}
