## About
'Al-Akhlaq al-Hamidah' (noble ethics) is a four-episode series by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] kept in the Assabile archive; all four episodes are listed on the series page, and what is written here covers its four lessons, on trustworthiness (amana), sincerity (ikhlas), truthfulness (sidq) and fidelity (wafa'). {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim el-akhlaq-el-hameeda} The speaker opens each lesson with praise of God and blessing on the Prophet, then builds it from verses and hadith locations, examples from daily life and closing reflections. What follows is the speaker's own speech as the machine transcript renders it; anything not traced to a source, or read from a line that is unclear in the transcript, is marked as needing a source.
