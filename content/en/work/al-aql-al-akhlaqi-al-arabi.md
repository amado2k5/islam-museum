## Summary

*Al-'Aql al-Akhlaqi al-'Arabi* (Arab Ethical Reason) is the fourth part of Mohammed Abed al-Jabri's series *Naqd al-'Aql al-'Arabi* (Critique of Arab Reason). Its subtitle is 'an analytical and critical study of the systems of values in Arab culture'. Its first edition was published by the Centre for Arab Unity Studies in Beirut in March 2001. {cite:archive-org-al-aql-al-akhlaqi-al-arabi-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book opens with an introduction on the present state of Arab ethical thought and a chapter on method. Its first part treats the ethical question in the heritage: defining ethics, adab and a system of values, the first crisis of values, debates on freedom and responsibility, and whether ethics rests on reason or on transmitted tradition. Its second part sets out the value systems of Arab culture as five inheritances: the Persian, an ethics of obedience; the Greek, an ethics of happiness; the Sufi, an ethics of self-annihilation; the purely Arab, an ethics of muruwwa (manly virtue); and the Islamic, the search for an Islamic ethics. {cite:archive-org-al-aql-al-akhlaqi-al-arabi-scan}
