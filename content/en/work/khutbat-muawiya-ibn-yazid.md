## Summary
Mu'awiya II's sermon after he took the caliphate, as the histories transmit it. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.189}

He opens by urging the fear of God, then warns against this world, which he describes as sweet and green yet deceiving to its people and passing quickly. He supports the point with Qur'anic verses, whose text is not reproduced here. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.189}

The sermon is brief in its reflection on the world's passing and the reminder of the Hereafter. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol2 p.189}
