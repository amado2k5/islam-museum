## Summary

*The Qur'an and the Aramaic Gospel Traditions* is a book by Emran El-Badawi, published by Routledge in the Routledge Studies in the Qur'an series on 19 December 2013 in 284 pages. {cite:worldofbooks-quran-aramaic-gospel-traditions}

## Publication and editions
