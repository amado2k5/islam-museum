## Summary

A question about a 'wheel of fortune' prize draw, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that there is no objection if entry is free and given by the company to the customer, in which case it is a gift from the merchant. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4307}

## The question

The questioner asked about a 'wheel of fortune' prize game. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4307}

## The answer

The Department answered that if entry into the competition is free and not paid for, with the company giving it to the customer so that he enters a prize draw, there is no harm or objection in it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4307} It said that in that case it counts as a gift from the merchant, relying on Iftaa Council resolution no. 3/2010. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4307}
