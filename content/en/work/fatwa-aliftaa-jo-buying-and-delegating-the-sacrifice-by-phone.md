## Summary

A question about buying the sacrifice and delegating its slaughter by phone, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is better for the sacrificer to slaughter it himself or be present, but there is no objection to appointing a butcher to buy and slaughter it, provided he designates it and buys it for the sacrificer before slaughter. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4393}

## The question

The questioner asked about buying sacrificial animals and appointing someone to slaughter them by phone. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4393}

## The answer

The Department answered that it is better for the sacrificer to slaughter his animal with his own hand, following the Sunna, or to attend the slaughter if possible. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4393} It said there is no objection to appointing the butcher to buy an animal for him and slaughter it on his behalf, whether the animal belongs to the butcher or, according to the Hanbalis, the butcher is an agent for selling it, provided the butcher designates the animal and buys it for the sacrificer before slaughtering it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4393}
