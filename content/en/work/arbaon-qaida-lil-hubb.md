## Overview
The novel by Elif Shafak (2009): two parallel stories, an American housewife who reads a manuscript about the meeting of Jalal al-Din Rumi and Shams of Tabriz, and her life changes through forty Sufi rules of love. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of the most widely circulated Turkish novels in the world; it reintroduced Sufism to millions of Western readers in the form of modern love. {cite:wikidata}
