## Summary
Al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn is al-Hakim al-Nisaburi's collection of hadiths he held to meet the criteria of al-Bukhari and Muslim, or one of them, though left out of their two Sahihs; the hadith scholars objected to some of its inclusions {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 127-128}.

## Criticism and standing
Al-Dhahabi placed al-Hakim in Tadhkirat al-huffaz as the shaykh of Islam yet also listed him in Mizan al-i'tidal for errors in the Mustadrak; despite the criticism he kept an honoured place among the traditionists {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 90-91}.
