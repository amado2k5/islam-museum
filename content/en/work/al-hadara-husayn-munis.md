## Summary

*al-Ḥaḍāra: A Study of the Origins and Factors of Its Rise and Development* is a book by Husayn Muʾnis, published in the ʿĀlam al-Maʿrifa series in Kuwait in 1978. {cite:openlibrary-al-hadara-munis-1978} As its title says, it studies the origins of civilization and the factors behind its rise and development. {cite:openlibrary-al-hadara-munis-1978}

## Publication and editions

The Open Library catalogue record gives the Kuwait edition of 1978 in 354 pages. {cite:openlibrary-al-hadara-munis-1978}
