## Summary

*Architecture of the Islamic World: Its History and Social Meaning* is a collective book edited by George Michell, with texts by Ernst J. Grube and others. {cite:openlibrary-architecture-of-the-islamic-world-1978} As its title says, it covers the architecture of the Islamic world, its history and its meaning in society. {cite:openlibrary-architecture-of-the-islamic-world-1978}

## Publication and editions

Thames and Hudson published it in London in 1978 in 288 pages (ISBN 0500340765); Open Library also lists a 1984 edition of the same work. {cite:openlibrary-architecture-of-the-islamic-world-1978}
