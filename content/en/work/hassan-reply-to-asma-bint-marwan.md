## Overview
Four verses that Ibn Ishaq says [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]] used to answer [[work/asma-bint-marwan-verses-against-the-ansar|the verses of Asma bint Marwan]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286} They turn her satire back on her: the Banu Wa'il, the Banu Waqif and Khatma are placed below the Banu al-Khazraj. They then describe what happened to her: whenever she called for folly, woe to her, her husband and the approaching deaths came, and a noble youth of a generous lineage and of a generous manner struck her, and she was stained with clotted blood after the calm of the night, without sin. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286} The editors explain "tadrij" as staining with blood, and "al-huduw" as an hour of the night. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286}

## Notes on the transmission
- The verses describe the killing in the past tense. In Ibn Ishaq's account they are both a reply to her poetry and a record of her death, although the account places the reply before the killing. This is noted in [[event/killing-of-asma-bint-marwan|the entry on the account]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286}
