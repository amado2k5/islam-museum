## Overview
A book by al-Zamakhshari, who died in 538 AH. Hajji Khalifa describes it as large in size and great in content, and as one of the pillars of the art of literature, indeed its foundation. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 375-376}

It treats linguistic metaphors, literary qualities and the expressions of eloquent writers, and it arranges its material in the manner of "Al-Maghrib". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 375-376}
