## Summary

A question about the ruling on the Friday ghusl, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is an emphasised sunna recommended for everyone who intends to attend the Friday prayer, and that someone in major impurity who intends both lifting that impurity and the Friday ghusl gains both. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4072}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on the ghusl, the full ritual bath, on Friday. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4072}

## The answer

The Department answered that the Friday ghusl is an emphasised sunna, citing a hadith reported by Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, who graded it good (hasan), that whoever performs wudu on Friday does well, and whoever performs ghusl, the ghusl is better. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4072} It said that it is recommended for everyone who intends to attend the Friday prayer, even someone not obliged to attend, such as a woman or a boy. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4072} It added that someone in a state of major impurity who intends by one bath both to lift the impurity and to perform the Friday ghusl gains both, while intending only one of them gains only that one. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4072}
