## Overview
Naguib Mahfouz's trilogy (Palace Walk, Palace of Desire, Sugar Street, 1956–1957), which narrates three generations of an Egyptian family in the Husayn quarter of Cairo, from the 1919 revolution to the 1940s. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The foundation of Mahfouz's Nobel Prize in 1988; for the Arab reader it became the first home in which modern Cairo is known as houses, families and religious and political debates. {cite:wikidata}
