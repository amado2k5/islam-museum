## Summary
'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise' («المبشرون بالجنة») is a series of twenty-eight episodes by [[person/mosaad-anwar|Mosaad Anwar]] kept in the Assabile archive, each about twenty-four to twenty-seven minutes long. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar} In the first episode he announces it as a new series he offers his listeners 'this year' under this title, stopping in each episode with one of those to whom the Prophet gave the glad tidings of Paradise to draw lessons and morals from the story; he says they number in the hundreds, men and women, and so he begins by describing the Paradise they were promised. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:02:00} In the second episode he specifies that the programme covers not all the Companions but those among them promised Paradise. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:22:00} It is not confined to the famous ten: six of them appear, [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], [[person/said-ibn-zayd|Sa'id ibn Zayd]], [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]], [[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha ibn Ubayd Allah]], [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]] and [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]], beside other Companions, men and women, whom he says the Prophet promised Paradise. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep09 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep27 00:01:30} In the twenty-first episode he says those promised Paradise are hundreds, even thousands, that most people barely know more than the ten, and that this is why he made these episodes. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep21 00:01:30}

Each episode opens and closes with a sung nashid, followed by the greeting, the sermon of need and the phrase 'a welcome return to those given glad tidings of Paradise'; the episodes on individual figures open with a riddle listing the subject's qualities and asking 'who is he?' before naming him, and close with a prayer that the listeners be gathered with him. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep11 00:01:30} Neither the episode pages nor the episodes give a recording date, and the twenty-eighth episode closes like the others, with no farewell to the series and no next episode announced. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep28 00:24:00} The archive's files for episodes 24 to 28 are numbered 25 to 29, so no file numbered 24 is kept. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar} The fourth episode breaks off mid-sentence at the twenty-fourth minute. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:24:00}

What this page holds is his account and explanation; hadiths and scholars' gradings of them are as he attributes them, and numbers, ages and dates are his statements. What he says on the strife among the Companions and on the Sunni–Shi'i dispute is not written here; it is recorded in the lane's log.

## The episodes
**Episode 1: Paradise.** [[person/mosaad-anwar|Mosaad Anwar]] opens the series by announcing a new series that he offers his listeners "this year" under the title 'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise', stopping in each episode with one of them and drawing lessons and morals from the story {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:01:30}. He says those promised Paradise, men and women, number in the hundreds, and that he chose to begin by describing the Paradise they were promised {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:02:00}. He lists some of its names, among them the Abode of Peace, the Abode of Eternity and the Gardens of Eden {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:02:30}, describes its eight gates of gold and attributes to the two Sahihs that one of them, al-Rayyan, is entered only by those who fast {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:06:30}, and relates through Abu Hurayra the description of the first group to enter it {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:09:00}. He then describes its trees and the tree Tuba, in whose shade a rider travels a hundred years {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:13:30}, its rivers and springs, and al-Kawthar, whiter than milk and sweeter than honey {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:15:30}, and the food, buildings, garments, servants and women of its people {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:21:30}. Among its joys he counts the company of the prophets and of Abu Bakr and Umar {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:23:00}, and makes the greatest of them looking upon the face of God, relating from Sahih Muslim the account of the Day of Increase, when the veils are lifted {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:23:30}. He closes with the teaching that a person is gathered with those he loves, hoping his listeners will be gathered with those promised Paradise {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep01 00:25:30}. (See [[concept/jannah|Paradise]].)

**Episode 2: The Companions, the unique generation.** He states that this is the second episode of the series {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:01:00}, describes the Companions as the best generation humanity has known, chosen by God for His Prophet's company, and says that those promised Paradise among them are hundreds {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:01:30}. He cites verses in their praise: the verse on men who kept their covenant with God, which he says was revealed especially about the martyrs of Uhud, naming among them Hamza, Mus'ab ibn Umayr, Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' and Anas ibn al-Nadr {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:03:30}, and the verses of God's pleasure with those who pledged under the tree and on the foremost believers {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:06:30}. He relates the hadith of the excellence of the Prophet's generation through Imran ibn Husayn {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:09:30}, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri's hadith that spending the like of Mount Uhud in gold would not reach a handful of theirs {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:11:30}, the hadith that the stars are a safeguard for the sky and the Companions a safeguard for the community {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:18:00}, the hadith of the two funerals {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:19:00}, and the hadith of the bands of fighters granted victory because Companions are among them {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:20:30}. He explains that the programme covers those of the Companions promised Paradise, not all the Companions {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:22:00}, and lists what he sees as a Muslim's duties towards them, such as praying for them, studying their lives, telling their stories to children and naming children after them {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep02 00:22:30}. He also discusses attacks on some Companions, the words of some imams about those who disparage them, and the sects that in his view arose after them, which is not written here. (See [[concept/ashara-mubashshara|the Ten promised Paradise]].)

**Episode 3: Bilal ibn Rabah.** He introduces Bilal as a riddle: a slave black of face and white of heart, freed by Abu Bakr, one of the first Muslims {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:01:30}, whose kunya was Abu Abd al-Karim and who was "the first muezzin" {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:03:00}. He relates from al-Hakim, through Ibn Mas'ud, that of the first seven who were tortured he alone did not give the polytheists what they wanted {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:04:30}, and describes Umayya ibn Khalaf's torture of him as he repeated "One, One" and Abu Bakr's buying him for five ounces of gold {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:07:00}. He tells of Bilal forgiving Abu Dharr for taunting him over his mother {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:11:00}, says he was given the glad tidings of Paradise twice, and gives the hadith of the Prophet hearing his sandals in Paradise {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:14:00}. He then relates his longing for Mecca while sick with fever in Medina {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:16:00}, his proposal to the sister of Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:17:30}, the story of the adhan and his being chosen for his fine voice {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:19:30}, his call to prayer from the top of the Ka'ba on the day of the conquest {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:22:00}, his weeping when he called the adhan after the Prophet's death {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:23:00}, his going to Syria and his adhan at al-Aqsa at Umar's request {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:24:30}, and his words to his wife as he died, that tomorrow he would meet the beloved ones {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep03 00:25:00}. (See [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]].)

**Episode 4: Ukasha ibn Mihsan.** He presents Ukasha as a hero of the Muhajirun, promised by the Prophet entry to Paradise without punishment or reckoning, who died in Abu Bakr's time fighting the apostates {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:01:30}. He relates his command of the raid on al-Ghamr and his return with two hundred camels {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:03:30}, and the stick of firewood that became a sword in his hand at Badr, which he reports from Ibn Kathir {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:04:00}. He then relates the hadith of the nations shown to the Prophet and the seventy thousand who enter Paradise without reckoning, and Ukasha standing to ask the Prophet to pray that he be one of them {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:08:00}, followed by hadiths on how many of the community will enter Paradise {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:12:00}. He dwells at length on the qualities of the seventy thousand: ruqya and the scholars' views on it {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:16:00}, cautery {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:17:30}, taking omens and its common forms {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:18:30}, and then trust in God, with the examples of Hajar and Mary {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:23:00}. At the end of the recording he returns to Ukasha and his longing for Paradise after the Prophet's death, and the available recording breaks off at the twenty-fourth minute {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep04 00:24:00}. He also discusses folk practices against the evil eye, which is not written here. (See [[person/ukasha-ibn-mihsan|Ukasha ibn Mihsan]].)

**Episode 5: Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf.** He presents him as "an honourable model of the Muslim businessman" {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:01:30}, of Banu Zuhra and one of the Ten promised Paradise {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:02:00}. He relates his embracing Islam through Abu Bakr and the Prophet's changing his name from Abd Amr to Abd al-Rahman {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:04:00}, his two emigrations, to Abyssinia and then to Medina {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:05:00}, his brotherhood with Sa'd ibn al-Rabi', his declining Sa'd's wealth and asking for the market {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:08:00}, and his marriage for a date-stone's weight of gold {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:08:30}. He relates the Prophet's praying one rak'a of the dawn prayer behind him {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:10:30}, and then dwells on his generosity, reporting from al-Dhahabi: the people of Medina dependent on him, his bequest to the veterans of Badr, his gift to the Army of Hardship {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:16:00}, his plain dress, and his weeping while fasting as he remembered Mus'ab ibn Umayr {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:17:30}. He relates his indifference to power and his prayer to die before Uthman {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:20:30}, and his death in 32 AH and burial in al-Baqi' {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep05 00:24:30}. (See [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]].)

**Episode 6: Umm Zufar.** He introduces her as a woman Companion of the people of Paradise, Khadija's hairdresser, an Abyssinian Black woman {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:02:00}, and relates through Ata' from Ibn Abbas her story: she complained to the Prophet of seizures in which she became uncovered, chose patience and Paradise over a prayer for healing, and asked him to pray that she would not become uncovered {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:02:30}. He draws lessons from it: that the people of Paradise are known by their qualities or by name through a text, and that no one is declared bound for Paradise or the Fire without a text {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:07:00}; the definition of backbiting and the six cases in which the law permits mentioning an absent person {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:13:00}; the kinds of complaint, what this woman did being complaint with contentment {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:20:00}; and the two kinds of epilepsy and their remedies {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:22:00}. He says that during her seizures she was seen clinging to the coverings of the Ka'ba {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:23:30}, and closes with the hadith on shame {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep06 00:25:00}. He also comments on one of the suitors in the story of Fatima bint Qays and compares her modesty with the dress of some girls today, which is not written here. (See [[person/umm-zufar|Umm Zufar]].)

**Episode 7: Fatima bint Muhammad (part 1).** [[person/mosaad-anwar|Mosaad Anwar]] calls Fatima "the sweet basil of the Chosen One" and says she was born in Mecca five years before the mission, while Quraysh were rebuilding the Ka'ba, and was the Prophet's youngest daughter and the child most like him {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:03:00}. He relates from al-Bukhari, through Ibn Mas'ud, how she threw off her father's back the camel's afterbirth that Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt had laid between his shoulders as he prostrated at the Ka'ba, after which the Prophet invoked God against the leaders of Quraysh, whom Ibn Mas'ud later saw dead in the well of Badr; he draws from it her courage {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:08:00}. He then recalls the siege in the ravine and Khadija's death after it, the Prophet calling Fatima "her father's mother" {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:09:30}, her emigration, her care for the wounded at Uhud and her stopping her father's bleeding with the ash of burnt matting {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:12:30}. He tells of her marriage to Ali after Badr, her dowry of a Hutami coat of mail, her modest household goods and Hamza's wedding feast, drawing the lesson that an easy engagement and a light dowry are a woman's blessing {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:19:00}. He closes with her request for a servant from the captives, the Prophet's preference for the people of the Suffa and the glorification, praise and magnification he taught her and Ali, advising the weary to turn to remembrance of God {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep07 00:24:00}. He also discusses inflated marriage inventories today and figures on unmarried women that he links to informal marriage, which is not written here. (See [[person/fatima|Fatima]].)

**Episode 8: Fatima bint Muhammad (part 2).** He continues her life, holding that no household is free of disagreement but that the Prophet's household referred to revelation and settled its quarrels quickly {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:02:00}. He tells the story of "Abu Turab": Ali left the house annoyed and slept in the mosque, and the Prophet came and woke him playfully and led him home; he warns parents against intruding in their married children's homes {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:04:30}. He recalls Ali's love for her and his two verses to the arak twig, and urges husbands to speak tenderly to their wives, as the Prophet did with Aisha {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:14:00}. He then counts her glad tidings of Paradise and her likeness to her father in speech, and the Friday when the Prophet came down from the pulpit to carry al-Hasan and al-Husayn {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:15:30}, and the secret of his final illness that made her weep and then laugh {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:17:30}. He says she lived six months after him and dates her death to 3 Ramadan 11 AH; Ali washed her, al-Abbas led the prayer and she was buried at night in al-Baqi' beside her sisters {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep08 00:22:30}. He also discusses Ali's proposal to the daughter of Abu Jahl, and her request to Abu Bakr for her inheritance and what passed between them afterwards, which is not written here. (See [[person/fatima|Fatima]].)

**Episode 9: Sa'id ibn Zayd.** Mosaad Anwar presents him as an early emigrant who took part in every campaign but Badr, and as one of the ten named in the hadith he attributes to al-Tirmidhi {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep09 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep09 00:04:00}. He says Sa'id's father Zayd died a monotheist before the mission, and that Sa'id was Umar's cousin and brother-in-law {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep09 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep09 00:03:30}. **Episode 10: Abd Allah ibn Salam.** Mosaad Anwar presents him as a rabbi of the Children of Israel in Medina, once named al-Husayn and renamed Abd Allah by the Prophet, who read in the Torah the description of a final prophet and longed to meet him {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep10 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep10 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep10 00:04:30}.
