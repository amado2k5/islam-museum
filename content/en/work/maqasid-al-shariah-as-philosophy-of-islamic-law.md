## Summary

*Maqasid al-Shariah as Philosophy of Islamic Law: A Systems Approach* is a study by Jasser Auda that proposes reading the maqasid and legal theory through systems theory. It was published by the International Institute of Islamic Thought in London and Washington in 2008 in 347 pages. {cite:openlibrary-maqasid-al-shariah-auda-2008} {cite:archive-org-maqasid-al-shariah-auda-2008}

## Publication and editions

After the introduction the book has seven chapters: the maqasid from a contemporary perspective, systems as a philosophy and method of analysis, a historical survey of Islamic law, its imams and schools, classical and then contemporary theories of Islamic law, a systems approach to the juridical theories, and conclusions. The bibliography fills pages 259 to 328. {cite:archive-org-maqasid-al-shariah-auda-2008}
