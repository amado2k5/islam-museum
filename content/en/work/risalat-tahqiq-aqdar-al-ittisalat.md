## Summary
"The Epistle on Verifying the Quantities of Applications" is a work by [[person/al-battani|al-Battani]] that has not reached us; its title is that of chapter 54 of [[work/al-zij-al-sabi|the Zij]]. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28} [[person/ibn-al-nadim|Ibn al-Nadim]] called it a well-known epistle of his. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 29}

## Its subject
Nallino suggests that in it al-Battani may have restored the astrological doctrine of the projection of rays to sound geometrical principles for the first time; Nallino sets out its subject in his notes on chapter 54. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28}

## The man it was written for
Al-Battani wrote it for Abu al-Hasan [[person/ali-ibn-al-furat|Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Musa ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Furat]], born on 22 Rabi' II 241, three times vizier to al-Muqtadir and put to death at his order on 13 Rabi' II 312, a patron of poets and of learning. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28} {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 29} Nallino counts Ibn al-Furat's request among the signs of the esteem in which al-Battani's contemporaries held him. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 26}

## Distinct from the book on ascensions
Nallino rejects the view that it is the same as [[work/kitab-marifat-matali-al-buruj-battani|the Book on Knowing the Ascensions of the Signs]], noting that al-Qifti and Ibn Khallikan list the two separately and that the problem of ascensions is quite other than that of applications. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 29}
