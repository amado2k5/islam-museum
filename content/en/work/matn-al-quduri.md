## Summary
Mukhtasar al-Quduri in Hanafi fiqh by Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Quduri of Baghdad (d. 428 AH) — a prose manual of some twelve thousand mas'alas gathering the student's provision of the school's fiqh; one of the great Hanafi teaching mutun on which the shuruh and hawashi are built {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: القدوري فقيه الحنفية وعميد مدرسة بغداد في قرنه.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليه شروح منها شرح اللكشمي وشرح ابن مودود? لا — عليه شروح المدرسة الحنفية الكبرى وهو عمدة تدريس الحنفية {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.
