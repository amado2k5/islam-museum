## Summary

al-Nahw al-Wafi is a work of Arabic grammar, ascribed by a research summary supplied to the museum to [[person/abbas-hasan-nahwi|Abbas Hasan]]. This page sets out where it stands in a list of the most-cited works on the Arabic sciences: its field, its author and its date as the summary gives them, its presence in the Shamela library, and what is not known of it here.

The summary ascribes the book to [[person/abbas-hasan-nahwi|Abbas Hasan]] and gives its date as: author died 1979 CE / 1399 AH {cite:wikipedia-al-nahw-al-wafi-ar}.
