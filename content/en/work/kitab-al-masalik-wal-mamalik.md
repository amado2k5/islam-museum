## Summary
Kitab al-Masalik wa-l-Mamalik is Ibn Khurdadhbih's book of routes, postal stations, tax registers and merchants' itineraries — one of the earliest works of the routes-and-realms genre in Arabic Islamic geography. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih}

## Details
Ubaydallah ibn Khurdadhbih — director of the barid (postal-intelligence service) in al-Jibal — composed the book, gathering the courses of the roads between cities and realms, the distances between stations and postal relays, the description of the land-tax and the levies of the provinces, and reports of merchants and the routes their trade follows east to China and India and west to Byzantium and the sea coasts. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih}

It is among the oldest surviving works of the "routes and realms" genre — a literary kind combining administration and geography, official postal intelligence beside country descriptions and travel itineraries — and it became the model followed by later geographical books such as the works of al-Ya'qubi, Ibn Rusta, al-Istakhri and Ibn Hawqal. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih}
