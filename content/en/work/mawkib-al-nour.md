## Summary
A daily Ramadan programme by Omar Abdelkafy titled "Mawkib al-Nour" (Procession of Light), pausing each day at one or two verses and drawing a lesson for real life, in long episodes of about forty minutes. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.
