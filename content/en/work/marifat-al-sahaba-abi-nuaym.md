## Overview
Abu Nu'aym al-Isfahani's book on the names of the Companions. It is one of the four works on knowing the Companions that Ibn al-Athir surveyed in "Usd al-Ghaba", to which he gave the mark (a). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.398}
