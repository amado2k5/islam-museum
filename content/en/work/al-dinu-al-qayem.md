## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with thirteen episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} It is a broadcast programme in which he greets 'our honoured viewers', {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:00:30} and at its opening he says that he is continuing the programme al-Din al-Qayyim 'in the new season'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:02:30} In the first episode he explains that [[concept/al-din-al-qayyim|al-qayyim]] is that which has the supreme worth no other worth equals, and that the religion is so called because it sets human life upright. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:13:30}
