## Summary
al-Idah fi Ulum al-Balagha is a book on rhetoric (balagha), by [[person/jalal-al-din-al-qazwini|al-Khatib al-Qazwini (Jalal al-Din)]]. The file gives the author's death as 739 AH / 1338 CE; this is the date of the author's death, not of the book's composition. Yes, the book is in the Shamela library: Book 7380. {cite:wikipedia-en-al-idah-fi-ulum-al-balagha} {cite:shamela-ws-book-7380}

## The book and its field
al-Idah fi Ulum al-Balagha appears in a research summary supplied to the museum that lists sixty of the most-cited classical and modern Arabic works across five sciences, namely dictionaries, grammar, morphology, prosody and rhetoric, twelve for each; this book stands in its section on rhetoric (balagha). [[concept/ilm-al-balagha|The science of balagha]] is the science of the ways of speech, its clarity and its beauty. The title is given in the summary as "al-Idah fi Ulum al-Balagha". {cite:wikipedia-en-al-idah-fi-ulum-al-balagha}
