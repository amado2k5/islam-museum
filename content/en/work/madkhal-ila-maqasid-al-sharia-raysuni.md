## Summary

*Madkhal ila Maqasid al-Shari'a* is a short book by Ahmed al-Raysuni, first published in 1431/2010 in about 80 pages and later reissued by Dar al-Kalima in al-Mansoura in the series of the author's complete works. {cite:shepangaropustaka-madkhal-ila-maqasid-raysuni} {cite:archive-org-madkhal-ila-maqasid-raysuni-scan} The publisher's foreword says the book was written to explain the aims of the sharia simply, the five necessities among them, for jurists, preachers and ordinary believers. Its introduction begins with the term maqasid al-sharia and then with why we need to know them. {cite:archive-org-madkhal-ila-maqasid-raysuni-scan}
