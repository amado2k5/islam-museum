## Summary
Tayyibat al-Nashr fi al-Qira'at al-Ashr is a poem by Ibn al-Jazari (751-833 AH), listed among his works in the biography in Dahman's edition of al-Nashr. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 8} Its sixth printing, established by Muhammad Tamim al-Za'bi, says it records what is sound of the readings and limits itself to two transmitters for each of the ten imams and two paths for each transmitter: western and eastern, Egyptian and Iraqi. {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 7}

## Its relation to the Shatibiyya and the Durra
The editor says in the preface to the first printing that he had printed the text of the Shatibiyya and al-Durra, with which the ten lesser readings are completed, and then followed them with the greater Tayyiba so that all the mutawatir readings would be complete. {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 7} In the Shatibiyya and al-Durra, he says, there are ten readers, two transmitters for each and a path for each transmitter, except that Idris from Khalaf has two paths in the Durra, so there are twenty-one paths in all; in the Tayyiba he counts eighty verified paths branching into nine hundred and eighty. {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 8} These figures are the editor's, not Ibn al-Jazari's. See [[work/hirz-al-amani-wa-wajh-al-tahani|the Shatibiyya]], [[work/al-durra-al-madiyya|al-Durra]] and [[work/al-nashr-fi-al-qiraat-al-ashr|al-Nashr]].

## Establishing the text
Al-Za'bi says earlier printings mostly followed the vocalisation of Shaykh 'Ali al-Dabba', and that for the fifth printing he checked the text against the copy that Shaykh Radwan al-'Uqbi read to the author and against commentaries including al-Nuwayri's. {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 4} {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 5} The verses themselves were not read here.
