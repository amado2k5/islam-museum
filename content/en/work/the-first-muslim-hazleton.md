## Summary

*The First Muslim: The Story of Muhammad* is a book by the journalist Lesley Hazleton, published by Riverhead Books in New York in 2013 in 320 pages, and the same year by Atlantic Books in London. {cite:openlibrary-the-first-muslim-hazleton-2013} {cite:archive-org-the-first-muslim-hazleton-2013}

## Publication and editions

The book is divided into three parts titled Orphan, Exile and Leader. The publisher's description says the author draws on the early sources and on history, politics, religion and psychology, following Muhammad's rise from powerlessness to power. {cite:archive-org-the-first-muslim-hazleton-2013}
