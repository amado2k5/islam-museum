## Summary

*Major Themes of the Qur'an* by [[person/fazlur-rahman-malik|Fazlur Rahman]] (1919–1988) presents the Qur'an through its themes; library cataloguing classes it under the theology of the Qur'an. {cite:archive-org-major-themes-of-the-quran-2009} Its eight chapters are: God; Man as individual; Man in society; Nature; Prophethood and revelation; Eschatology; Satan and evil; and the Emergence of the Muslim community. {cite:archive-org-major-themes-of-the-quran-2009} Two appendices follow: one on the religious situation of the Muslim community in Mecca, the other on the People of the Book and the diversity of religions. {cite:archive-org-major-themes-of-the-quran-2009}

## Publication and editions

Bibliotheca Islamica published the book in Minneapolis in 1980, in 180 pages. {cite:openlibrary-major-themes-of-the-quran-1980} The University of Chicago Press issued it again in 2009, in 180 pages with twenty pages of front matter. {cite:archive-org-major-themes-of-the-quran-2009}
