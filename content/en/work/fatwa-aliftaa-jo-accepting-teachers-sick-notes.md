## Summary

A question on whether a head teacher sins by accepting teachers' documented sick notes or casual-leave excuses, in which Jordan's General Iftaa Department answered that he does not, since he is acting within his powers. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a school head sins by accepting the documented medical or casual-leave excuses a teacher submits. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810}

## The answer

The Department answered that the head commits no sin in accepting a documented excuse, whether medical or casual, because the ministry has delegated the authority to him, casual leave falls within his remit, and the regulations require only that the absence be justified between head and teacher with proof attached. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810} It urged heads not to be obstinate over justified, documented excuses or to make things hard for teachers, citing a hadith that prays against whoever makes things hard for the community and for whoever is gentle with it, with al-Nawawi's commentary on it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810} Conversely, it said that neither a teacher nor anyone else may take leave without right or reason, since that is forbidden lying. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810} It added that a doctor who gives an employee sick leave without a medical reason sins and deceives the Muslims. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3810}
