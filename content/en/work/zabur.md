## Overview
The Zabur in Islamic conception is the book revealed to David; Ibn al-Nadim counts it among the books God sent down — a hundred and fifty psalms {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}.

## In the sources
Al-Tha'labi names the Zabur with the Tawrat, Injil and Furqan among the books God sent down {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition}.
