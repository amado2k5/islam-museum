## Overview
The Zabur in Islamic conception is the book revealed to David; Ibn al-Nadim counts it among the books God sent down — a hundred and fifty psalms {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}.

## In the sources
Al-Tha'labi names the Zabur with the Tawrat, Injil and Furqan among the books God sent down {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition}.

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam gives zabur as the Arabic word for the Psalms. It says the Qur'an describes the Psalms of David as a revelation sent to David, who is considered a prophet, and cites [[passage/quran-4-163|4:163]], [[passage/quran-17-55|17:55]] and [[passage/quran-21-105|21:105]] for this. God, it adds, is regarded as the author of the psalms, and the verse [[passage/quran-21-105|21:105]] has a direct parallel in the biblical Psalm 37:29. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1887}
