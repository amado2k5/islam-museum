## Summary

A question about arranged invoices to obtain cash from Islamic banks, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that using stratagems and arranging invoices with a seller to obtain cash from Islamic banks is not permitted, because it brings the sale close to an interest-based transaction. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4500}

## The question

The questioner asked about obtaining financing from an Islamic bank by producing invoices arranged with the seller and then taking cash once the financing is approved, to pay debts and buy a car. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4500}

## The answer

The Department answered that the apparent purpose of the transaction is to obtain cash through a stratagem, and that using stratagems and colluding with other parties to obtain liquidity is not permitted, because such devices bring the sale close to the form of an interest-based transaction. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4500} It cited a hadith reported by [[person/ibn-batta-al-ukbari-ubayd-allah|Ibn Batta]] in [[work/ibtal-al-hiyal|Ibtal al-Hiyal]] (p. 47), graded hasan by many scholars, whose meaning is a warning against making God's prohibitions lawful through the slightest stratagems, and referred to its earlier fatwa no. 516 on the prohibition of stratagems against Islamic banks. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4500}
