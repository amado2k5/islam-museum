## Summary

A question about a Friday prayer held on a weekly outing, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the Friday prayer is invalid in the form described because its conditions—two sermons, forty people obliged to attend, and a built-up settlement—are missing, so the group must repeat it as zuhr and repeat the asr combined with it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4439}

## The question

The questioner described a group that leaves before dawn every Friday on an outing of more than 81 km; at Friday prayer time one of them gives a single admonitory talk and leads two rak'as intended as Friday prayer, and they combine and shorten asr with it. He asked whether this is valid and whether the Friday prayer lapses during travel. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4439}

## The answer

The Department answered that the Friday prayer has conditions of validity, including being prayed in congregation in the time of zuhr, being preceded by two sermons each fulfilling its pillars, being held within a built-up settlement, and being attended by forty of those on whom it is obligatory, citing [[work/umdat-al-salik|Umdat al-Salik]] by Ibn al-Naqib (p. 82). {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4439} It said the prayer described is not a valid Friday prayer because several conditions are missing: it was preceded by a single admonitory talk; a person who sets out on a long journey before dawn is not among those obliged to attend, so his presence does not count; and it was not held in a built-up settlement. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4439} It concluded that they must pray zuhr instead, choosing between shortening it to two rak'as or praying four; their two rak'as with the intention of Friday prayer are invalid, as is the asr combined with it, since a valid combination depends on the first prayer being valid, so they must repeat both zuhr and asr. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4439}
