# Umma.ru

## Summary

Umma.ru is a Russian Islamic website, the official site of Shamil Alyautdinov, imam-khatib of the [[building/memorial-mosque-moscow|Memorial Mosque in Moscow]]. It publishes his sermons, articles, Qur'an commentary and answers to questions, has a page for putting questions to the imam, and is closely tied to an online shop set up to distribute his books. {cite:wikipedia-umma-ru-tt}

## Beginnings

The site's archived captures of 2001 and 2002 are blank pages; the earliest capture with content, of 30 January 2003, has sections for news, the Prophet Muhammad, questions, articles, hadiths, prayer, audio, books, fatwas, prayer times, 'ask a question' and a glossary, with '2002 Umma.ru' at the foot. {cite:wayback-umma-2003} That capture does not name the site's owner.

## Today

The site is at umma.ru; it has a short Tatar Wikipedia article and the Wikidata item Q20571889. {cite:wikipedia-umma-ru-tt} This record will be filled out when a press or academic source on it is read.
