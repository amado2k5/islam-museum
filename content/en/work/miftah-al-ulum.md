## Summary
Miftah al-Ulum is a book on rhetoric (balagha), by [[person/al-sakkaki|Abu Ya'qub Yusuf al-Sakkaki]]. The file gives the author's death as 626 AH / 1229 CE; this is the date of the author's death, not of the book's composition. Yes, the book is in the Shamela library: Book 26581. {cite:wikipedia-en-miftah-al-ulum} {cite:shamela-ws-book-26581}

## The book and its field
Miftah al-Ulum appears in a research summary supplied to the museum that lists sixty of the most-cited classical and modern Arabic works across five sciences, namely dictionaries, grammar, morphology, prosody and rhetoric, twelve for each; this book stands in its section on rhetoric (balagha). [[concept/ilm-al-balagha|The science of balagha]] is the science of the ways of speech, its clarity and its beauty. The title is given in the summary as "Miftah al-Ulum". {cite:wikipedia-en-miftah-al-ulum}
