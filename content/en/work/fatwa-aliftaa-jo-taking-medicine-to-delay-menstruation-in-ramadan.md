## Summary

A question about a woman who took medicine to delay menstruation and fasted, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that her fast is valid, but that she is not advised to do so as there is no necessity, and that taking it is forbidden if it harms her, even possibly. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4112}

## The question

The questioner asked about the fast of a woman who took medicine to delay her period. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4112}

## The answer

The Department answered that if a woman takes medicine, sees no menstrual blood and fasts, her fast is valid. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4112} It said she is not advised to do this since there is no necessity for it, and if the medicine harms her, even possibly, taking it is forbidden for her. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4112}
