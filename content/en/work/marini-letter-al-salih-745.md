## Overview
A copy of a letter in which Sultan Abu al-Hasan al-Marini, king of the Maghrib, addressed the king al-Salih Isma'il, son of the king al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.390-391}

## Its arrival
The letter reached Egypt in the middle of Sha'ban 745 AH; some say in its last ten days. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.390-391}

## Its titles
The sender describes himself as Amir of the Muslims, who strives in the way of God, sultan of the two shores, and protector of the two frontiers. He addresses the recipient as "our son". Among the titles given to al-Nasir Muhammad in it are "father of the good sultans" and "reviver of the traces of jihad". {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.390-391}
