## Summary

A practical arithmetic book by [[person/abu-al-wafa-al-buzjani|Abu al-Wafa]], for scribes and officials, distinct from his trigonometry and artisan constructions. The sources describe seven sections but differ in their summaries of financial headings and ordering; they agree on integers, fractions, measurement and transactions {cite:abu-wafa-mactutor arithmetic} {cite:iranica-abu-wafa-works work1}.

## Audience and presentation

The book uses numbers in words and verbal calculation rather than relying on positional Indian numerals. The discussion interprets this as accommodation to practical readers, not evidence that the author did not know those numerals or belonged to a hostile rival school {cite:abu-wafa-mactutor arithmetic} {cite:abu-wafa-youschkevitch-dsb PDFp2}.

## Operations and applications

The sections cover proportion, multiplication, division, measurement, taxation, exchange and other transactions. Each source's account is retained rather than fabricating one definitive list from differently ordered summaries {cite:tdv-ebul-vefa-buzcani works2} {cite:iranica-abu-wafa-works work1}. The second section includes [[invention/abu-wafa-debt-multiplication|complement multiplication with a debt intermediate]]:3×5 uses a debt of2 multiplied by10 and adds35, giving15. This is a bounded example, not the foundation of every negative-number theory {cite:abu-wafa-youschkevitch-dsb PDFp2}.
