## Overview
A book by [[person/ibn-rushd|Ibn Rushd]] answering al-Ghazali, that is, answering [[work/tahafut-al-falasifa|The Incoherence of the Philosophers]]. Al-Zirikli counts it among some fifty books that Ibn Rushd wrote. {cite:al-aalam p. 849}
