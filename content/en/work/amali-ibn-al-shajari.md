## Overview
"Al-Amali" (Dictations) by Abu al-Sa'adat Hibat Allah ibn Ali, Ibn al-Shajari, who died in 572 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.597}

It runs to eight volumes and covers five branches of literature. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.597}
