## Overview
The novel by Michel Houellebecq (2015): in 2022 France, a moderate Islamic party wins the presidency, and a Sorbonne professor reflects on Paris turning slowly and gently into a Muslim society. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
A controversial novel read by some as a transcendent fiction and by others as a mirror of European fears; it provoked debate about Islam and democracy in France. {cite:wikidata}
