## Overview
"Al-Ansab" (Genealogies) by the Imam, the hadith scholar Abu Sa'd Abd al-Karim ibn Muhammad al-Marwazi al-Shafi'i, who died in 562 AH. Hajji Khalifa describes it as a great book in this field, complete in eight volumes, but rare to find. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}
