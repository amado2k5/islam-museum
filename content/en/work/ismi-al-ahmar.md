## Overview
The novel by Orhan Pamuk (1998): Istanbul in 1591, where Ottoman miniaturists are killed one after another over a secret book of the sultan that breaks the rules of Islamic art with Western perspective. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among the most translated novels in modern Turkish; it makes Islamic art a philosophical question: who looks at the world, and with what eye? {cite:wikidata}
