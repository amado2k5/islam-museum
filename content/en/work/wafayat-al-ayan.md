## Overview
"Wafayat al-Ayan wa Anba Abna al-Zaman" (The Deaths of Notables and the News of the Sons of the Age), a book of biographies of notables by [[person/ibn-khallikan|Ibn Khallikan]]. Al-Dhahabi said of it that its author had compiled a precious book on the deaths of notables. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 379}
