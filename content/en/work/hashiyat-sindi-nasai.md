## Summary
al-Sindi's gloss on al-Nasa'i's Sunan is a short commentary by Abu al-Hasan al-Sindi on the [[work/sunan-al-nasai|Sunan]] of al-Nasa'i. It explains the wording of the Sunan and clarifies rare words and grammar, and it states al-Nasa'i's conditions for including reports. {cite:hashiyat-sindi-nasai-copy p.2}

## al-Nasa'i's conditions as the gloss states them
The gloss says that al-Nasa'i did not include many of the men that Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi included, and that he avoided reports from a group of men whom the sahih compilers used. For this reason, it says, it was said that his condition for men was stricter than that of others. {cite:hashiyat-sindi-nasai-copy p.2}

## The state of this copy
The photographed copy has 148 pages. The scan is of very poor quality, so only the first cover and the opening of the introduction can be read. Nothing beyond that can be relied on without a clearer scan. {cite:hashiyat-sindi-nasai-copy p.1}

## For young visitors
The gloss is a commentary on al-Nasa'i's Sunan. It explains the wording and the meanings, and shows how al-Nasa'i chose his narrators.

## Research notes
- The attribution to Abu al-Hasan al-Sindi comes from the cover and introduction. Identifying him with the Medina scholar Muhammad ibn Abd al-Hadi al-Sindi is an assumption from the person record and needs checking.
- Do not record judgements on hadiths from this copy until a clearer scan is available.
