## Summary
"Al-Hawi fi al-Tibb" (The Comprehensive Book on Medicine) by Abu Bakr [[person/al-razi-abu-bakr|al-Razi]], a large medical work. The copy read here is its first volume, which contains three parts: diseases of the head, diseases of the eye, and diseases of the ear, nose and teeth {cite:hawi-razi-dar-kutub-ilmiyya p.1}.

## The first part
The first part begins with diseases of the head {cite:hawi-razi-dar-kutub-ilmiyya p.5}.

## A note on the edition
The text read is the Beirut edition published by Dar al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyya. Its year of publication and the details of its review have not yet been verified. The title page gives the author's date of death in a form that is hard to read in automatic extraction, so that date is not recorded here.
