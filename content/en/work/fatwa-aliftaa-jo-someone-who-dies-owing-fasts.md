## Summary

A question about someone who dies owing fasts, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that someone who died before being able to make up the days owes nothing, while for someone who died after being able, one mudd of food per day is paid from his estate, and his heir may fast on his behalf. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4157}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on someone who dies while owing fasts. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4157}

## The answer

The Department answered that someone who died before being able to make up the fasts, such as a person whose excuse continued until death, owes no make-up, no fidya, and bears no sin. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4157} It said that if he died after being able to make them up, what he missed must be remedied by paying one mudd of food from his estate for each day, relying on a hadith reported by [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]] about feeding on behalf of the dead. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4157} It added that his heir may fast on his behalf, relying on a hadith reported by [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] and [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]], with another version adding 'if he wishes', and concluded that both feeding and fasting on his behalf are permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4157}
