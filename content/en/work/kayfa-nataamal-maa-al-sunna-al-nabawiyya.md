## Summary

*Kayfa Nata'amal ma'a al-Sunna al-Nabawiyya: Ma'alim wa-Dawabit* (How we approach the Prophetic Sunna: landmarks and guidelines) is a book by Shaykh Yusuf al-Qaradawi; its first edition was published in 1990 by Dar al-Wafa in al-Mansura with the International Institute of Islamic Thought in Herndon, in 185 pages. {cite:openlibrary-kayfa-nataamal-qaradawi-1990} A review by Qutb Mustafa Sano of the third edition (1991, 187 pages) says the book aims to set out guidelines that guard against misunderstanding and mishandling the Sunna. {cite:archive-review-kayfa-nataamal-qaradawi}

## Publication and editions

According to the review, the book has three parts: the first on the standing of the Sunna and our duty towards it, the second on the Sunna as a source for the jurist and the preacher, and the third on the principles for understanding the Sunna well. {cite:archive-review-kayfa-nataamal-qaradawi} The reviewer praises its fluent style, while finding that some of its guidelines overlap and could better be merged. {cite:archive-review-kayfa-nataamal-qaradawi}
