## Overview
"Inbah al-Ruwat ala Anbah al-Nuhat" (Informing the Transmitters About the Histories of the Grammarians) by Jamal al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Yusuf al-Qifti, who died in 646 AH. Hajji Khalifa describes it as a history of the grammarians. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.641}
