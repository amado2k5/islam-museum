## Summary
Al-Muqaddima al-Jazariyya in tajwid by Shams al-Din Ibn al-Jazari (d. 833 AH) — about a hundred and seven rajaz lines, called 'al-Muwqidda'; it gathers the articulation points and qualities of the letters, the recitation rulings and the rasm, the standard text for the masters of tajwid {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

المؤلف: ابن الجزري شيخ القراء في عصره وصاحب النشر في القراءات العشر.
