## Summary
*Nukhab al-Afkar fi Tanqih Mabani al-Akhbar fi Sharh Sharh Ma'ani al-Athar* is [[person/badr-al-din-al-ayni|Badr al-Din al-'Ayni]]'s ten-volume commentary on al-Tahawi's [[work/sharh-maani-al-athar-tahawi|Sharh Ma'ani al-Athar]], which the biography printed with 'Umdat al-Qari calls the widest work on the hadiths of rulings, one no group of jurists can do without. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 16} Al-'Ayni says in the preface to [[work/umdat-al-qari|'Umdat al-Qari]] that he composed a commentary on Ma'ani al-Athar after returning to Egypt, before commenting on al-Bukhari. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 20}

The biography also lists his two-volume work on the book's narrators, *Ma'ani al-Akhyar fi Rijal Ma'ani al-Athar*, which gives each generation — Companions, Successors and their followers — a section of its own. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 16-17}
