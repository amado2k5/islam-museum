## Summary
The book was famous among the early and middle Hanbalis; no book served the madhhab as it did {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 424}. Izz al-Din al-Misri registered three hundred commentaries on it, of which he examined about twenty {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 424}. Abu Ishaq al-Barmaki counted its questions — they reached two thousand three hundred {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 425}.
