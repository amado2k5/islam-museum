## Overview
The novel by Leila Aboulela (1999): Sammar, a Sudanese widow in London and Aberdeenshire, between her faith and her orientalist friend Rae, between one prayer and another. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The first novel in English to make a Sudanese Muslim woman a spiritual centre rather than an ethnographic subject; Aboulela founded a new Arab-English voice. {cite:wikidata}
