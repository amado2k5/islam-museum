## Overview
"Al-Rawd al-Unuf fi Sharh al-Sira al-Nabawiyya" (The Untouched Meadow: a Commentary on the Prophetic Biography), a commentary by [[person/al-suhayli|al-Suhayli]] on the biography of Ibn Hisham. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.751}

Ibn al-Imad also mentions among al-Suhayli's works in "Shadharat al-Dhahab" "Al-I'lam bima Ubhima min al-Asma wal-A'lam" and "Nata'ij al-Nazar". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.751}
