## Summary
"Wa Nalqa al-Ahibba" (And We Meet the Beloved) is a lecture series by Amr Khaled, recorded on video and held in the Assabile archive, with 79 lessons by the series page's own count. It opens with the Rightly Guided Caliphs and with Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, setting out his lineage, his names and titles, his conduct before and after Islam, and the presenter's view of his character. What follows is the speaker's own speech as the machine transcript of the recording renders it. Anything not traced to a source, or read from a line that is unclear in the transcript, is marked as needing a source.

:::perspectives
- **The speaker's view (opinion):** he holds that the key to Abu Bakr's personality is his love of the truth and his attachment to it, that truthfulness, mercy, wisdom and humility were among his most marked qualities, and that what set him ahead of others was not chiefly the number of his prayers and fasts but something that settled in his heart. {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:31:30}
- **The sources he names:** he names books on Abu Bakr, among them a work by Ali al-Tantawi (the name is read from a garbled line) and al-Aqqad's "Abqariyyat al-Siddiq", together with "Usd al-Ghaba" and "al-Isaba fi tamyiz al-sahaba" as older standard references. These are the speaker's recommendations; the books were not checked for this entry. {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-amr-khaled wa-nal9a-al-aheba/ep01 00:07:30}
