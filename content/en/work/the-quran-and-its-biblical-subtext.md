## Summary

*The Qur'an and its Biblical Subtext* is a book by Gabriel Said Reynolds, published by Routledge in London and New York in 2010 in 304 pages, as volume 10 of the Routledge Studies in the Qur'an. {cite:openlibrary-quran-biblical-subtext-2010} The lending-library record places its bibliography on pages 259–282 and notes indexes. {cite:archive-org-quran-biblical-subtext-2010}

## Publication and editions

It opens with an introduction, 'Listening to the text', on the crisis of Qur'anic studies, the scholarly conflict over the Qur'an and the book's method, then presents Qur'anic case studies. {cite:openlibrary-quran-biblical-subtext-2010} These include the prostration of the angels, Abraham, Haman and the tower to heaven, Jonah and his people, the nativity of Mary, the companions of the cave and Muhammad. {cite:openlibrary-quran-biblical-subtext-2010} It then turns to the relation between Qur'an and tafsir and to the exegetes' devices, such as occasions of revelation and variant readings, and closes with reading the Qur'an as homily, its comparison with Christian homily and the problem of translating the Qur'an. {cite:openlibrary-quran-biblical-subtext-2010}
