## Overview
"Al-Istiab fi Marifat al-Ashab" (The Comprehensive Account of Knowing the Companions), a book by Ibn Abd al-Barr al-Qurtubi on the names of the Companions. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 398}

## Its place among books on the Companions
It is one of four works on knowing the Companions that Ibn al-Athir surveyed in "Usd al-Ghaba", and to which he gave the mark (b). It has marginal notes, from which the author of "al-Tajrid" benefited. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 398}
