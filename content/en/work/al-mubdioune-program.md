## Summary
'Al-Mubdi'un' (The Creatives) is a programme by Tareq al-Suwaidan presenting the lives of great names from the history of Islamic civilisation — from the well-known like Ja'far al-Sadiq, al-Bukhari, Ibn Sina, Ibn Taymiyya, al-Razi, al-Ghazali and al-Farabi, to those whose very names the youth have not heard, like Yusuf ibn Tashfin, Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, al-Fudayl ibn Iyad, Sufyan al-Thawri and al-Awza'i {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:01:30}.
## Its aim
He says his aim goes beyond entertaining tales to setting exemplars, changing convictions and reshaping interests through story — citing that the Qur'an is full of stories whose goal is change — so that if the individual changes, collective work follows and the ummah rises {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:07:00}.
## Its civilisational message
In the opening he argues that the ummah led humanity for long then fell back for centuries; that imitating America, Europe or Japan will not restore glory — the imitator is a follower chewing over others' achievements — and that imported towers and factories produce a superficial 'urbanity' (madaniyya), not a civilisation with idea and spirit; greatness is made only by creatives who outstrip others and bring the new and unique, and though creativity today may be collective through institutions, institutions without creative individuals are worthless {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:04:30}.
## The methodology
The programme does not stop at biographies but analyses them on his methodology [[concept/rubaiyyat-sinaat-al-mubdiin]] — four stages of four elements — exploring a figure each episode {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:23:00}.
He announced the first figure to be the imam Ja'far al-Sadiq {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep01 00:23:30}.
