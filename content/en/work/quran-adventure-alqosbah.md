# Al-Qur'an Adventure (alQosbah)

## Summary
*Al-Qur'an Adventure* is an interactive Indonesian Qur'an from the publisher alQosbah, combining the Qur'anic text and the official Indonesian translation with visual and digital media; the reviewer calls it a "one-stop Qur'an" for the range of aids it offers. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah}

## Publisher and context
AlQosbah was founded in 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, by a group of entrepreneurs, publishes only Qur'ans, has distributed over ten million copies in Indonesia, Malaysia and Brunei, and in 2022 received the Innovative Quran Publisher Award from the Ministry of Religious Affairs' Lajnah Pentashihan Mushaf. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah} The reviewer notes estimates of annual demand for printed Qur'ans in Indonesia of about 37 million copies against production of about seven million. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah}

## What it contains
It uses the official Ministry translation with thematic subheadings, and beneath the text a word-for-word translation based on the linguistic exegesis of [[work/tafsir-ibn-kathir|Ibn Kathir's tafsir]]. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah} Every page carries a QR code opening animated stories and videos; there are 31 stories and 76 lessons supported by hadith, colour-coded tajwid, and marks to help pausing, breathing and memorisation. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah} Yulia Riswan observes that its translations are still not fully child-friendly, as they put accuracy of meaning first. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah}

## Today
The publisher now issues translations in other languages and took part in the Frankfurt Book Fair in 2023 and 2024. {cite:gloqur-quran-adventure-alqosbah}
