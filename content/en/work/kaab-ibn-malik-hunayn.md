## Overview
A qasida by [[person/kaab-ibn-malik|Ka'b ibn Malik al-Ansari]] on the day of Hunayn and the army's march to besiege al-Ta'if. It describes God's victory for His Prophet, the defeat of idolatry and the demolition of the fortresses of al-Ta'if. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

It relates to [[event/battle-of-hunayn|the Battle of Hunayn]] and mentions [[event/siege-of-taif|the siege of al-Ta'if]]. The museum's data dates it to Shawwal 8 AH; this is a derived date tied to the transmission. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses.
