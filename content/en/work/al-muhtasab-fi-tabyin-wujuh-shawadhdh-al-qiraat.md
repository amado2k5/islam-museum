## Summary
In the introduction to the Itqan al-Suyuti names al-Muhtasab fi Tawjih al-Shawadhdh by Ibn Jinni among the Arabic-language books of use to the commentator; the note identifies the author as Uthman ibn Jinni, Abu al-Fath, the philologist, who died in 392 AH, and says the book is printed as al-Muhtasab fi Tabyin Wujuh Shawadhdh al-Qira'at wa-l-Idah 'anha. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 26}

## Among the books explaining the readings
Al-Suyuti puts it with those devoted to explaining the readings: al-Hujja of Abu 'Ali al-Farisi, al-Kashf of Makki, al-Hidaya of al-Mahdawi and Ibn Jinni's al-Muhtasab for the anomalous readings. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 536} See [[work/al-hujja-lil-qurra-al-saba|al-Hujja]] and [[work/al-kashf-an-wujuh-al-qiraat-al-sab|al-Kashf]].

## In the notes of the Itqan
The editors fix anomalous readings by turning to al-Muhtasab, among them a reading of Sa'id ibn Jubayr that they say was set right from the Muhtasab, a reading of Ibn Muhaysin and a reading of 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, paired with Ibn Khalawayh's Mukhtasar. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 66} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 311} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 312} The text of the book itself was not read here.
