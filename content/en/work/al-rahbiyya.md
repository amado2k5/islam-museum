## Summary
Al-Rahbiyya in the science of inheritance shares by Abu Bakr al-Rahbi (d. after 571 AH) — about two hundred and fifteen basit lines; the most famous of the fara'id mutun and one of the most commented {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: الرحبي شافعي المذهب من أهل الشام.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شرح القرافي وشرح الشنقيطي وشرح ابن حجر? لا — عليها شروح الشافعية المتأخرين وهي عمدة درس الفرائض {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Al-Rahbiyya is the verse text for the shares of inheritance in the Shafi'i school.
