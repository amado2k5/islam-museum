## Overview
"Nasab Quraysh" (The Genealogy of Quraysh) by Abu Abdullah [[person/al-zubayr-ibn-bakkar|al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar]] al-Qurashi, who died in 256 AH. An abridgement of it was made by Abu Fayd Mu'arrij ibn Amr al-Basri, the grammarian. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 655}

This is what Hajji Khalifa reports in "Kashf al-Zunun". Anything beyond this needs a further source.
