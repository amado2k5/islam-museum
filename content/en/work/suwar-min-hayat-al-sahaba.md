## Summary
*Suwar min Hayat al-Sahaba* ("Portraits from the Lives of the Companions") is a book by [[person/abd-al-rahman-rafat-al-basha|Dr Abd al-Rahman Ra'fat al-Basha]], first published by Dar al-Nafa'is, Beirut, in 1412/1992. {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.1} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.3} It opens with a prayer in which, out of love for the Prophet's Companions, he asks God to unite him with them on the Day of the Great Terror. {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.5}

## Structure
It consists of seven "books" of about ten portraits each — the first includes Sa'id ibn Amir al-Jumahi, al-Tufayl ibn Amr al-Dawsi, Abd Allah ibn Hudhafa al-Sahmi, [[person/umayr-ibn-wahb-al-jumahi|Umayr ibn Wahb]], al-Bara' ibn Malik, Umm Salama, Thumama ibn Uthal, Abu Ayyub al-Ansari and Amr ibn al-Jamuh — and ends with an alphabetical index of the Companions and the table of contents. {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.6} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.507} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.509} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.510} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.511}

## Method
Each portrait is narrative, often told in the subject's own voice — as in the portrait of [[person/rabia-ibn-kab-al-aslami|Rabi'a ibn Ka'b]]: "Rabi'a ibn Ka'b said: I was a young lad…" — with difficult words glossed in the notes, and a "for further reading" list of biographical and hadith works with volumes and pages at the end. {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.361} {cite:suwar-min-hayat-al-sahaba-nafais-1992 p.368}

## For young visitors
A book that tells the stories of the Prophet's Companions like short tales, some told by the Companions themselves.
