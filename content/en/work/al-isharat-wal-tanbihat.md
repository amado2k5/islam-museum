## Overview
"Al-Isharat wal-Tanbihat" (Pointers and Admonitions), a book on logic and philosophy by [[person/ibn-sina|Ibn Sina]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 339}

## Its commentaries
The Imam Fakhr al-Din Muhammad ibn Umar al-Razi, who died in 606 AH, commented on it and summarised it in "Lubab al-Isharat". Among its commentaries is that of Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Tusi, and "al-Muhakama bayn al-Sharihayn" by Qutb al-Din. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 339}
