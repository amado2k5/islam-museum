## Summary

A question about paying oath expiation through a charity, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that there is no objection to appointing a charity that feeds the poor to pay the expiation, since agency in distributing zakat, expiation and vows is permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4337}

## The question

The questioner asked about giving oath expiation to a charity that feeds the poor and needy. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4337}

## The answer

The Department answered that there is no objection to a Muslim appointing someone to pay the expiation on his behalf, and that jurists stated that agency is permitted in distributing zakat, expiation and vows. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4337} It quoted [[work/mughni-al-muhtaj|Mughni al-Muhtaj]] (3/237) that a person may pay the zakat on his wealth himself and may also appoint an agent to do so. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4337}
