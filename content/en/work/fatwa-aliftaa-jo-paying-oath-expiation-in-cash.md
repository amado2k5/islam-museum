## Summary

A question about the cash amount of expiation for a broken oath, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is feeding ten poor people with 600 grams of rice each, and that its value may be paid in cash, set at one dinar. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4236}

## The question

The questioner asked how much the expiation for a broken oath is in cash. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4236}

## The answer

The Department answered that the expiation for an oath is feeding ten poor people, set at 600 grams of rice for each. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4236} It said its value may be paid in cash, set at one dinar. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4236}
