## Summary

A question about a woman who performed wudu or ghusl while wearing nail polish, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the polish must be removed beforehand because it is a barrier that keeps water out. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4049}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling for a woman who performs wudu or ghusl while her nails are coated with polish. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4049}

## The answer

The Department answered that nail polish must be removed before wudu or ghusl so that water is sure to reach what lies beneath it, because it counts as a barrier that prevents water from reaching the nail. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4049} It cited a hadith narrated by Ali warning anyone who, in the ghusl for major impurity, leaves even the spot of one hair untouched by water. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4049}
