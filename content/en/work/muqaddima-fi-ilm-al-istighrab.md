## Summary

*Muqaddima fi 'Ilm al-Istighrab* (Introduction to Occidentalism) is a book by the Egyptian thinker Hassan Hanafi, part of his Heritage and Renewal project, published by al-Dar al-Fanniyya in Cairo; the Bibliotheca Alexandrina copy is a 1991 printing. The copy does not say whether it is the first edition. {cite:archive-org-muqaddima-fi-ilm-al-istighrab-1991}

## Publication and editions

In his opening words the author says he does not reject the West; he calls for creativity of the self in place of imitation of the other, and for making the other an object of knowledge rather than its source. Chapter one, 'What does Occidentalism mean?', explains that the Heritage and Renewal project has three fronts: our stance toward the old heritage, toward the Western heritage and toward reality. This book is the theoretical statement of the second front, written after the first part of the first front, *Min al-'Aqida ila al-Thawra*, had appeared. {cite:archive-org-muqaddima-fi-ilm-al-istighrab-1991}
