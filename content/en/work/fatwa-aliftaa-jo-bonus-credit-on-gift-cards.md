## Summary

A question on a gift card bought for 190 dinars and loaded with 212, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that buying it is permissible, since the extra is a gift from the seller. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3825}

## The question

The questioner said she works as an agent through an app, and one of the rewards is a gift card she buys for 190 dinars that is loaded with 212, paid through online banking, after which she receives a code to use when buying on the site; she asked about its ruling. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3825}

## The answer

The Department described the card as prepaid for online purchases from the issuer's site, and said its research showed that the site's policy is to add credit beyond the amount paid. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3825} It said that, in fiqh terms, the card represents cash value up to what the customer paid, and the extra credit is a gift from the seller to the cardholder to encourage buying from it alone. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3825} It concluded that there is no objection to buying the card. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3825}
