## Overview
'Al-Imaniyyat' (the articles of faith) is a thirteen-episode series by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] kept in the Assabile archive; the series page lists thirteen episodes, and all thirteen mined in this record cover faith in God and its pillars and its bearing on righteous action, then faith in the Books, then faith in the messengers, then faith in the angels and their works, then faith in the Last Day, its signs and the reckoning of deeds, and finally faith in divine decree in its legal and creative senses. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat} The speaker opens each lesson with praise of God and blessings on the Prophet, then presents each episode with an opening Qur'anic or hadith reference followed by examples and reflections; what appears here is the speaker's words as shown by the machine transcript of the recording, and anything not attributed to a source or occurring in an unclear line is marked as needing a source.
