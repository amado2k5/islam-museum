## Overview
'Al-Imaniyyat' (the articles of faith) is a thirteen-episode series by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] kept in the Assabile archive; the series page lists thirteen episodes, and the seven mined in this record cover faith in God and its pillars and its bearing on righteous action, then faith in the Books, then faith in the messengers. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat} The speaker opens each lesson with praise of God and blessings on the Prophet, then presents each episode with an opening Qur'anic or hadith reference followed by examples and reflections; what appears here is the speaker's words as shown by the machine transcript of the recording, and anything not attributed to a source or occurring in an unclear line is marked as needing a source. The remaining eight episodes of the series have not yet been mined.

## Episodes
**Episode 1: Faith in God.** The speaker opens by defining iman as belief that admits no doubt: when a servant says 'I believe', he has affirmed a settled conviction in which there is no room for hesitation. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:02:00} He then describes the basis of creed as believing in God first and then in what God revealed, citing the completion of the blessing in God's word that He has perfected their religion for them today, [[passage/quran-5-3|al-Ma'idah, verse 3]], and giving the example that one who disbelieves God by claiming to be Muslim and then disbelieves what He revealed no longer has faith. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:01:30} He mentions that faith has three foundations: certainty in the heart, verbal affirmation, and action of the limbs, the first of them being the intention that moves outward action, citing the well-known saying that deeds are judged by intentions, and noting that one who fed the poor for God's face meant God's face, not showing off. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:05:30} He then mentions the supplication 'guide us on the straight path' from the Fatiha, [[passage/quran-1-6|al-Fatiha, verse 6]], and cites God's word in Surat al-Nisa' on the obligation to believe in God, His messenger, the Book sent down to him and the Book sent down before, [[passage/quran-4-136|al-Nisa', verse 136]], explaining that the Qur'an was revealed over twenty-three years in stages while the earlier Books were sent down at once on their pages. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:23:30} He closes the episode with the qualities of faith given in Surat al-Baqarah, [[passage/quran-2-285|al-Baqarah, verse 285]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep01 00:24:00}

**Episode 2: The three foundations of faith.** The speaker continues by describing the three foundations on which faith stands: certainty in the heart, verbal affirmation and belief confirmed by **Episode 3: Faith and righteous action.** The speaker continues the question of whether faith alone suffices without righteous action, or action without faith, and concludes that true faith requires righteous action; he mentions a hadith he attributes to the Prophet about the first three who will be judged on the Day of Resurrection, without quoting its wording since the line is unclear in the transcript, and explains that when the hypocrites claimed faith with their tongues without deeds they belied it in their hearts, citing a community of believers who say 'we believe' while saying what they do not do, [[passage/quran-49-14|al-Hujurat, verse 14]], while noting that the sura name in the transcript is not certain. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:19:00} ⁦(needs_source)⁩ He then sets out the conditions of righteous action: a deed is accepted only with sincerity and seeking God's face, and it must conform to the Qur'an and the Sunna or it is not righteous however fine it looks, and mentioning an example about drinking wine within the discussion of a deed conforming to the Law, with a reservation on the clarity of its line in the transcript. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:21:30} ⁦(needs_source)⁩ In closing he returns to the three foundations of faith and counts righteous action as a pillar that cannot be dispensed with. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep03 00:26:00} A long repetition segment around 00:23:00–00:24:00 with no meaning in the transcript is excluded.

**Episode 4: Faith in the Books.** The speaker moves to the article of faith in the revealed Books, recalling that God sent the Books down with the messengers to the earlier nations to guide them by them, and that the Qur'an is the last of the Books and their seal, citing verses of the Qur'an and hadiths he narrates without quoting their wording. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:02:00} He then speaks of the Qur'an's descent piecemeal, not all at once, to be recited to the people at leisure, citing God's word in Surat al-Qadr that the Night of Decree is better than a thousand months, [[passage/quran-97-3|al-Qadr, verse 3]], and mentioning that it is preserved on a guarded tablet, [[passage/quran-85-22|al-Buruj, verse 22]], and that God made clear none can bring its like since it came with the truth and the best explanation, [[passage/quran-25-33|al-Furqan, verse 33]], with a reservation that this citation's line is unclear in the transcript. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:24:00} ⁦(needs_source)⁩ He concludes that the Qur'an is the final, comprehensive Book and the seal of the Books. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep04 00:26:30}

**Episode 5: Faith in the messengers.** The speaker opens the article of faith in the messengers, recalling that God chose messengers from among the angels and human beings to convey His message, and that faith in the messengers is part of the faith the Prophet set out in the hadith of Jibril: to believe in God, His angels, His books and His messengers; he mentions that the messengers were human beings like us who ate, drank, slept and married, that God reveals the unseen to no one except whom He pleases among His messengers, and that they received revelation and conveyed it as they were commanded, while noting that much of the middle of the episode is unclear in the transcript. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep05 00:19:30} ⁦(needs_source)⁩

**Episode 6: The messengers are human, their revelation and their tawhid.** Continuing on faith in the messengers, the speaker holds that the messengers were human beings like us who ate, drank, slept and died, and that nothing of the attributes of divinity belongs to them; they received revelation from God and conveyed it as they were commanded, and defines the miracle as an extraordinary event that breaks the usual order and is brought about by God through a messenger, not of his own doing, mentioning the miracles of the messengers and examples of the miracles of earlier prophets. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:21:00} He then explains that the task of all the messengers was the call to tawhid and the worship of God, and repeats in later segments that the messengers were callers to tawhid with no shirk in it, while noting that some segments contain long repetition with no meaning in the transcript. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep06 00:26:00}

**Episode 7: Faith in the messengers and their seal.** The speaker continues the teaching on faith in the messengers, presenting tawhid as prior to all other meanings in the prophetic message, and recalling that the first thing the peoples were called to in the messengers' call was tawhid: 'there is no god but God' negated and affirmed; he cites God's word in Surat al-Baqarah on making this community witnesses over the people, [[passage/quran-2-143|al-Baqarah, verse 143]], and explains that Muhammad is the seal of the messengers and the best of them, in whom the people have a good example, citing God's word in Surat al-Ahzab on the good example in the Messenger of Allah, [[passage/quran-33-21|al-Ahzab, verse 21]], and mentioning God's favours to him from the mission until the gathering on the Day of Resurrection, while noting that the transcript line 'by God's leave' is unclear. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:20:00} ⁦(needs_source)⁩ He closes with exhortation to the learners to hold fast to faith and to keep up righteous deeds in Ramadan and after, with long repetition segments that carry no meaning in the transcript excluded. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim imaneat/ep07 00:27:00}
