## Summary
"Al-Wasatiyya" is a televised panel programme presented by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]. Opening the first episode, he says the programme takes up issues that people live with in Arab and Muslim life, on which there are several points of view and real disagreement, and that it tries to hear those different views so that the participants may reach the middle way together. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:01:00} The Assabile archive holds forty episodes of the programme. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya}

## The shape of an episode
An episode opens with the presenter setting out the issue and its questions. He then welcomes guests, experts and scholars, some of whom have come from abroad and from the West, and a large audience of young men and women, most of them university students. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:03:30} The programme usually begins with a general poll: the presenter puts a question with two or more choices, the audience votes on handheld devices, and the result is announced after the break. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:05:30} Young people from the audience and the guests then take turns, with further polls along the way, and the presenter closes with a summing-up in which he sets out what he sees as the middle position. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 01:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 01:20:00}

The vote results in these episodes are the opinions of that episode's studio audience, not representative surveys, and the museum presents them as such.

## The close of the first episode, as an example
