## Summary

A question about passing the nearer mosque to pray in a farther one, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is better to pray in the farther mosque with the larger congregation unless the nearer mosque's congregation would suffer from his absence, in which case the nearer one is better. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4259}

## The question

The questioner asked about passing the nearer mosque to pray in a farther one. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4259}

## The answer

The Department answered that if the farther mosque has a larger congregation and the nearer mosque's congregation would not suffer from his absence, it is better to pray in the farther mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4259} It said that if the nearer mosque's congregation would suffer from his absence, because he is its imam or people attend when he attends, then praying in the nearer mosque is better, so that congregations are held in two places in the town. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4259} It relied on a hadith reported by [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]], [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] and [[person/al-nasai|al-Nasa'i]] that a man's prayer with others is purer than his prayer alone, and that the larger the congregation the more beloved it is to God. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4259}
