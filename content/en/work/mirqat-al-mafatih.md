## Summary
*Mirqat al-mafatih sharh Mishkat al-masabih* is Ali ibn Sultan Muhammad al-Qari's (d. 1014 AH) commentary on the *Mishkat al-masabih* of al-Khatib al-Tibrizi ([[person/ali-al-qari|his biography]]). For each hadith it gives the wording, explains the meaning, and reports the scholars' views. {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 5}

## Volume seven
The Dar al-Kitab al-Islami volume seven opens with the book of manumission: the chapter on freeing a man or woman (scan page 7), the chapter on freeing a jointly owned slave and buying a relative (page 11), the chapter on oaths and vows (page 26), a chapter on vows (page 38), and the chapter on what is not liable in injuries (page 93). {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 7}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 11}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 26}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 38}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol7 p. 93}

## Volume eleven
The Dar al-Kitab al-Islami volume eleven opens with the chapter on the beginning of creation and the prophets (page 6), the chapter on the virtues of the Master of the Messengers (page 47), and the chapter on revelation and the end of the prophetic route (page 54). {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol11 p. 6}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol11 p. 47}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-dar-kitab-vol11 p. 54}

## The Jamal Itani edition
In the edition by Shaykh Jamal Itani, the chapters read from the Qur'an onward. They include the chapter on the etiquette of recitation and the study of the Qur'an (page 71), the chapter on the variant readings and the collection of the Qur'an (page 89), and the chapter on the remembrance of God and drawing near to Him (page 137). The first chapter in the readable part is on the Prophet's ascent (page 31). {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-jamal-itani-2019 p. 31}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-jamal-itani-2019 p. 71}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-jamal-itani-2019 p. 89}, {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-jamal-itani-2019 p. 137}

## A note on the source
The three volumes are separate printings. The volume number of the Itani edition is not read from its title page. Page numbers are scan pages. {cite:mirqat-al-mafatih-jamal-itani-2019 p. 5}
