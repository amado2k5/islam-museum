## Overview
A book by al-Mas'udi on the reports of the pure and righteous elect. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673}

In it he gives the account of Ammar ibn Yasir when mentioning the fifty notables who pledged to Ali to the death. Al-Mas'udi mentions it together with "Hadaiq al-Adhhan" at the end of the chapter on Ali. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.714}
