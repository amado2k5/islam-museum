## Summary
Al-Manzuma al-Bayquniyya in hadith terminology by Umar ibn Muhammad al-Bayquni (d. 1080 AH) — thirty-four rajaz lines memorising the divisions of hadith and the conditions of isnad and matn; the first mustalah text in the lessons {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: البيقوني شامي حنفي? — شامي من أهل دمشق عاش في القرن الحادي عشر.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شروح منها شرح المؤلف «التنقيح» وشرح البرماوي وشرح محمد عبد الكريم عبد الله {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Al-Bayquniyya is the short poem for the names and divisions of hadith.
