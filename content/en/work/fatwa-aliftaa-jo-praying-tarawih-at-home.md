## Summary

A question about praying tarawih at home, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the sunna is to pray it in congregation in the mosque, and that there is no objection to sometimes praying it in congregation with one's family at home for a reason. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4184}

## The question

The questioner asked whether tarawih may validly be prayed at home. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4184}

## The answer

The Department answered that the sunna is for a Muslim to pray tarawih in congregation in the mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4184} It said that if he sometimes prays it in congregation with his family at home for some reason, there is no objection. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4184}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4184, dated 5 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4184} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
