## Summary
Bulugh al-Maram min Adillat al-Ahkam is Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani's collection of the hadiths on which legal rulings rest, arranged by the chapters of jurisprudence as an entry point for the learner into the evidence behind the questions of law. {cite:bulugh-al-maram}

## Details
Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani (d. 852 AH) gathered in this book the hadiths on which jurists rely in deriving rulings, ordered by the chapters of fiqh from purity to inheritance, with indications of each hadith's transmission, grading and which of the canonical collections recorded it. {cite:bulugh-al-maram}

It became one of the most widely taught manuals of legal hadith: read with teachers and covered by commentaries and glosses, it has served generations of students as the bridge to the larger hadith compilations, gathering each text, its chain and its ruling in one place. An early printed edition is that of the Matba' Qayyumi at Kanpur, India, in 1323 AH. {cite:bulugh-al-maram}
