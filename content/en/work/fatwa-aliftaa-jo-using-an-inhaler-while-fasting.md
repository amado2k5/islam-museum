## Summary

A question about a patient using an inhaler during the day in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that an inhaler through the nose or mouth breaks the fast; someone who needs it on some days uses it, keeps refraining and makes those days up, while someone who needs it every day uses it, keeps refraining and feeds a poor person for each day. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4128}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on a patient using an inhaler during the day in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4128}

## The answer

The Department answered that using an inhaler through the nose or mouth breaks the fast, because the medicine in these inhalers is meant to reach the lungs, which are part of the body's interior. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4128} It said that someone who uses it on some days uses it while fasting, keeps refraining from food and drink, and makes those days up later. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4128} It added that someone who uses it every day uses it while fasting, keeps refraining, and feeds one poor person for each day. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4128}
