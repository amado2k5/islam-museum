## Summary
*Fayd al-Qadir Sharh al-Jami' al-Saghir* is [[person/abd-al-rauf-al-manawi|'Abd al-Ra'uf al-Munawi]]'s large commentary on [[person/al-suyuti|al-Suyuti]]'s [[work/al-jami-al-saghir-suyuti|al-Jami' al-Saghir]]. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 14} [[person/al-albani-muhammad-nasir-al-din|Al-Albani]] describes it as criticising the hadiths of the Jami' and stating their grades at length without covering them all; it treats the original only and not the *Ziyada*, which makes up nearly half of al-Fath al-Kabir. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 14}

## On the sigla
Al-Albani quotes al-Munawi's commentary that al-Suyuti included many weak hadiths in the Jami', that he ought to have followed each hadith with the word "sound", "good" or "weak", and that the sigla in some copies — a head of sad, ha' or dad — should not be trusted, as copyists so often corrupted them. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 21-22} He also quotes him that al-Suyuti often cites a hadith to its compiler when the compiler had added a remark impugning its chain, and leaves that remark out. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 27} Al-Albani points to places where the siglum in the very copy carrying al-Munawi's commentary contradicts what al-Munawi says the author's siglum was. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 23}
