## Overview
A qasida by Abbas ibn Mirdas al-Sulami describing the events of the day of Hunayn. In it he praises the valour of Banu Sulaym and the steadfastness of the Muslims after the first collapse, and he answers the poets of Hawazin. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.102-112}

## Context
The poem relates to the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Battle of Hunayn]] and mentions [[group/banu-sulaym|Banu Sulaym]]. The data dates it to Shawwal 8 AH; this is a derived date. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.102-112}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the poem.
