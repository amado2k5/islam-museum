## Summary

A question from a latecomer who caught the imam in the last unit of the sunset prayer, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] answered that after the imam's salutation he performs a second unit, sits after it for the first sitting, then rises for a third unit with al-Fatiha. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4085}

## The question

The questioner said he arrived in the last unit of the sunset prayer and caught that unit and the final sitting with the imam, so that his own second unit fell at the place of the sitting; he asked how to complete his prayer. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4085}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that he sits after the second unit, which he performs after the imam's salutation, and recites the first sitting's formula, then rises and performs a third unit in which he recites al-Fatiha. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4085}
