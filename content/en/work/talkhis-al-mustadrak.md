## Overview
*Talkhis al-Mustadrak* is the abridgement that [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] (d. 748 AH) made of al-Hakim's [[work/al-mustadrak-ala-al-sahihayn|*al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn*]]. The publishers of the Hyderabad edition say that in it he "drew attention to [al-Hakim's] laxity and his authentications". {cite:mustadrak-nizamiyya-1341-v3 p. 2}

## Contents
Al-Dhahabi gives each hadith with a shortened chain and then agrees or disagrees with al-Hakim's verdict. Among the comments Muqbil al-Wadi'i quotes are his verdict that Anas's hadith of the Prophet and the four caliphs reciting the basmala aloud is fabricated, that "I am the city of knowledge" is among the false reports, and his remark about a narrator al-Hakim authenticated that al-Hakim himself had called him a liar in his history. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 18-20}

Al-Dhahabi estimated that about a third of the *Mustadrak* meets the condition of the two masters or one of them, devoted a separate booklet to the hadiths he judged fabricated, and still called the *Mustadrak* "a useful book, which I have abridged". {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 9}

## Editions and limits
The *Talkhis* was printed beneath the *Mustadrak* at the Nizamiyya press in Hyderabad, with 1341 AH on the title page of volume 3. {cite:mustadrak-nizamiyya-1341-v3 p. 2} The Dar al-Haramayn edition reproduces al-Dhahabi's comments, each marked with the word "al-Dhahabi". {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 38} Al-Wadi'i holds that it cannot replace the *Mustadrak*, because al-Dhahabi drops part of the chain and the defect may lie in what he dropped. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 37}
