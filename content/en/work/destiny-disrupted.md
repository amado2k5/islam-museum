## Summary

*Destiny Disrupted: A History of the World Through Islamic Eyes* is a book by Tamim Ansary, published by PublicAffairs in New York in 2009. {cite:openlibrary-destiny-disrupted-2009} In his introduction the author recalls growing up in Afghanistan with a narrative of world history different from the one schoolchildren in Europe and the Americas learn. {cite:archive-org-destiny-disrupted-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book has seventeen chapters, beginning with what it calls the Middle World, the Hijra and the birth of the caliphate, passing through the Umayyads and Abbasids, scholars, philosophers and Sufis, the arrival of the Turks and the Mongol invasions, then Europe, the West's arrival in the East and the reform movements, and ending with the crisis of modernity; an appendix on the structure of Islamic doctrine follows. {cite:archive-org-destiny-disrupted-scan}
