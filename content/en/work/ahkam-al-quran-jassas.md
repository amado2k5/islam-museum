## Overview
A book on the legal rulings of the Qur'an by Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Ali, known as al-Jassas al-Razi the Hanafi, who died in 370 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.212}

Hajji Khalifa lists it in "Kashf al-Zunun" among the works in this field. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.212}
