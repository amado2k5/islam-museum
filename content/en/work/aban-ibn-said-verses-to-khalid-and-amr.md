## Overview
Two verses that Ibn Ishaq attributes to [[person/aban-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Aban ibn Said]], addressed to his brothers [[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid]] and [[person/amr-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Amr]] when they embraced Islam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Ibn Ishaq mentions that their father, [[person/said-ibn-al-as-abu-uhayha|Said]], had died at al-Zurayba. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses. Their attribution to Aban has not been confirmed from another source, so they are recorded here as Ibn Ishaq's account.
