## Summary

*Mafhum al-Nass: Dirasa fi 'Ulum al-Qur'an* (The Concept of the Text: A Study in the Qur'anic Sciences) is a book by the Egyptian scholar Nasr Hamid Abu Zayd, first published by al-Markaz al-Thaqafi al-'Arabi in Beirut and Casablanca in June 1990. In his preface he says that many of the study's ideas grew out of exchanges with his students. The publisher reprinted it many times: the 2014 edition, issued jointly with Mu'minun bila Hudud in 328 pages, states that eight earlier printings had come from al-Markaz al-Thaqafi al-'Arabi. {cite:archive-org-mafhum-al-nass-1990} {cite:archive-org-mafhum-al-nass-2014}
