## Overview
"Kitab al-Zahi" (The Book of the Radiant), a book by al-Mas'udi on the imamate. He mentions it, along with "Al-Safwa" and "Al-Istibsar", among the books in which he discussed the acceptance of Islam by Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.574}

These books set out the dispute among the Shi'a between the view that there is an explicit designation (nass) and the view that the community chooses. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.574}
