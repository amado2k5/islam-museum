## Summary
Intoxicated Spirit is a live album by the Pakistani singer [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] with his Party, released in 1996 on Shanachie, recorded in Pakistan with the voices of his troupe, tabla and harmonium, and supported by a North American tour. {cite:wikipedia-intoxicated-spirit-en} {cite:wikidata-intoxicated-spirit} The article reports, from the Recording Academy, that it was nominated for the Grammy for Best Traditional Folk Album, without giving the year. {cite:wikipedia-intoxicated-spirit-en}

## Reception
Robert Christgau wrote that its Sufi ecstasy runs closer to the surface and wilder than on The Last Prophet; Newsday found that the Western trappings of his recent performances were gone, leaving him free to weave Sufi poetry and driving percussion; and The Washington Post wrote that 'Ruk Pe Rehmat Ka' reaches a transcendent elation. {cite:wikipedia-intoxicated-spirit-en} It has four pieces: 'Yeh Jo Halka Halka', 'Ruk Pe Rehmat Ka', 'Be Wafa' and 'Meri Saqi Saqi Yeh'. {cite:wikipedia-intoxicated-spirit-en}
