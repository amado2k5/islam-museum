## Summary

*Reclaim Your Heart: Personal Insights on Breaking Free from Life's Shackles* is an English book by Yasmin Mogahed, published in 2012 by FB Publishing in San Clemente, California, with an IDIFY edition in 2015. Its back cover describes it as a manual for the heart's journey through life, about hope and renewal and about facing love, loss, happiness and pain. Its sections are Attachments, Love, Hardships, Relationships with the Creator, Women's status, Ummah and Poetry. {cite:openlibrary-reclaim-your-heart-2012} {cite:archive-org-reclaim-your-heart-2012}
