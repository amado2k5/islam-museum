## Summary

A question about breaking the fast for people in hard physical work, such as bakers and concrete workers, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that they may not start the day without fasting, but must intend the fast and begin it, breaking it only if fasting becomes a real hardship and making the day up later. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4104}

## The question

The questioner asked whether someone doing hard physical work, such as a baker or a concrete worker, may break the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4104}

## The answer

The Department answered that someone doing hard work may not break the fast from the outset; he must intend the fast from the night before and begin it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4104} It said that if he later reaches a point where fasting becomes a hardship, he breaks the fast and makes the day up at another time. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4104}
