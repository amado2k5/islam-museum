## Summary

*Interpreting the Qur'an: Towards a Contemporary Approach* is a book by Abdullah Saeed, published by Routledge in New York; the catalogue record dates it to 2005. {cite:openlibrary-interpreting-the-quran-saeed} The record notes that it includes bibliographical references and an index. {cite:openlibrary-interpreting-the-quran-saeed}

## Publication and editions

Its chapters begin with the context of the debate on interpretation and with revelation and interpretation, then turn to interpretation based on tradition and textualism and interpretation based on reason. {cite:openlibrary-interpreting-the-quran-saeed} Later chapters treat flexibility in reading the text, abrogation and reinterpretation, meaning as an approximation, the complexity of meaning and socio-historical context. {cite:openlibrary-interpreting-the-quran-saeed} It closes with ethico-legal texts and a hierarchy of values. {cite:openlibrary-interpreting-the-quran-saeed}
