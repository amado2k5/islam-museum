## Summary
Insan ("Human") is the second album of the Egyptian singer-songwriter [[person/hamza-namira|Hamza Namira]], released in 2011 by [[listing/awakening-records|Awakening Records]] and produced by Namira with Mohamed Diab and Hazem Wefy; it has fourteen songs in Arabic and Egyptian Arabic and two bonus tracks. {cite:wikipedia-insan-hamza-namira-ar} {cite:wikidata-insan-hamza-namira}

## The songs
They include 'Insan', 'El-Midan', 'Hansa', 'Tazkarti (Al-Taghriba 2)', 'Ya Mehawen', 'Ibn El-Watan', 'Baladi Ya Baladi', 'Hasir Hisarak', 'Ala Bab Allah' and 'Sot', followed by 'Heila Heila Ya Matr' and a live 'Esmy Masr'. {cite:wikipedia-insan-hamza-namira-ar} Videos were released for 'Insan', a live 'El-Midan', 'Heila Heila Ya Matr' and 'Esmy Masr'. {cite:wikipedia-insan-hamza-namira-ar} The Guardian wrote in 2012 that Namira shot to prominence with 'El-Midan', a song in solidarity with the Egyptian revolution. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012}
