## Summary
al-Farghani's treatise on the science of the spheres, printed at Amsterdam in 1669 with the Arabic text, a Latin translation and the notes of Jacob Golius, who oversaw the edition {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 5}{cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 7}. In thirty chapters it sets out the principles of astronomy, from the calendar of months and days to the eclipses {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 10}.

## The edition and its contents
The publisher tells the reader that Golius, professor of mathematics and oriental languages at Leiden, collated the Arabic text and annotated it, and that he died before finishing the work, so the book remains as he left it, with his notes and an index {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 7}{cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 8}{cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 9}. The index gives the titles of the thirty chapters, and at the end an index of places orders the seven climes and their countries alphabetically {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 15}.
