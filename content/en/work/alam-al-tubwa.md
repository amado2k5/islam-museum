## Overview
"A'lam al-Nubuwwa" (The Signs of Prophethood) by Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Mawardi, the Shafi'i. It is an abridgement in twenty-one chapters. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.518}

It has two concerns: what is specific to the signs of prophethood, and what varies in their kinds and rulings. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.518}

The title appears in the data as "A'lam al-Tubwa", which is most likely a transcription error for "al-Nubuwwa". A further source is needed.
