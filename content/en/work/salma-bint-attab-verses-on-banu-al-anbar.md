## Overview
Two verses that Ibn Ishaq gives after the account of the captivity of [[group/banu-al-anbar|Banu al-Anbar]] in [[event/sariyya-of-uyayna-to-banu-tamim|the expedition of Uyayna ibn Hisn]], in the words of [[person/salma-bint-attab|Salma bint Attab]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270} Their sense is that Adi ibn Jundub, a clan of Banu al-Anbar in Ibn Hisham's account, fell into a steep and dangerous pit of trouble, and that enemies surrounded him from every side while his honour and his fortune were absent from him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270} The museum gives the sense here rather than the text; the text is read in the edition.

## In context
The two verses come between a list of those of Banu al-Anbar who were killed and taken captive, and the verses of [[person/farazdaq|al-Farazdaq]] that Ibn Hisham gives about [[person/al-aqra-ibn-habis|al-Aqra ibn Habis]] and his release of the captives. The first is a lament for the defeated, and the second is praise for the intercessor. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270}
