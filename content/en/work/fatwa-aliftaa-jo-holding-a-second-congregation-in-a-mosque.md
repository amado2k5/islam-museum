## Summary

A question about holding a second congregation in a mosque, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is disliked to deliberately hold one in a neighbourhood mosque with a regular imam unless he permits it, and that second and third congregations are permitted in busy mosques such as those in markets. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4258}

## The question

The questioner asked about holding a second congregation in a mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4258}

## The answer

The Department answered that it is disliked to deliberately hold a second congregation in a mosque that is not a thoroughfare, such as a neighbourhood mosque, when it has a regular imam, unless the imam permits it, because it implies criticism of the imam. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4258} It said that in a busy mosque, such as a market mosque, second and third congregations are permitted whether or not it has a regular imam and whether or not the imam permits it, because people come to it in large numbers and several congregations imply no criticism of the imam. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4258}
