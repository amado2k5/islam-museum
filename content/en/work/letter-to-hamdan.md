## Overview
A letter the Prophet wrote for the delegation of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] when they came to him with [[person/malik-ibn-al-namt|Malik ibn al-Namt]]. See [[event/delegation-of-hamdan|the delegation of Hamdan]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/245}

## Its content
It opens with the basmala, then: this is a letter from the Messenger of God, Muhammad, to the districts of Kharif and the people of the slope of al-Hadb and the dunes of sand, with their envoy Dhu al-Mash'ar Malik ibn al-Namt and those of his people who have embraced Islam. Their upland and lowland are theirs for as long as they keep up the prayer and give the alms, eating the fodder and grazing the fresh growth of the land. For this they have the covenant of God and the protection of His Messenger, and the emigrants and the Ansar are witnesses. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/245} See [[concept/hima|the hima]].

## Commentators' glosses
"Al-Hiqaf" is the plural of "hiqf", a round sand dune; "al-Fira'" are the high parts of the land; "al-Wihat" is the low, level part of it; "al-Ulaf" is the fruit of the acacia; "'afiha" is its abundant vegetation. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/245}
