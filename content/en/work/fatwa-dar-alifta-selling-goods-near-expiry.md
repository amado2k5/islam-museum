## Summary

A question on selling goods close to their expiry date at a discount without telling buyers why, which Egypt's Dar al-Ifta permitted on conditions. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 9006}

## The question

The questioner asked about selling goods nearing their expiry date through promotional price cuts, without disclosing to buyers that the approaching expiry is the reason for the discount. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 9006}

## The answer

Dar al-Ifta answered that this is permitted if the goods are sound, fit for consumption and free of anything harmful to the consumer's health, with the expiry date left printed on them, neither altered nor obscured. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 9006} It said that if any of these conditions is missing the sale is forbidden and the buyer has the option to return the goods. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 9006} It based this on the principle that sales are permissible so long as there is full mutual consent and no fraud or concealment, noting that the Malikis required the seller to disclose how old the goods are and how long he has held them. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 9006}
