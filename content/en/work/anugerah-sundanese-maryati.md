# Anugerah Al-Quranul-Karim Tarjamah Basa Sunda (Maryati Sastrawijaya)

## Summary
*Anugerah* ("A gift from God") is a complete Sundanese rendering of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by Mariyah Maryati Sastrawijaya; among the hundreds of Qur'an translations made in Indonesia, it is to date the only complete one by an Indonesian woman. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese}

## The translator
Maryati was born in Ciamis in 1943, lectured in Sundanese language and literature at Padjadjaran University, Bandung, and is known for translating Sundanese literature into Indonesian; she did not study at a pesantren, and her interest in Islamic teaching grew through the mosque near her home. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese}

## How it was made
She published a partial translation of the first juz', *Al-Hikmah*, in 2009, completed the whole Qur'an in 2011, renamed it *Anugerah*, and the Padjadjaran University Alumni Association published it in five red-covered volumes. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese} Her main source was the official Indonesian government translation in the 1990 King Fahd Complex edition, whose column layout hers follows, and she consulted Indonesian commentaries such as Quraish Shihab's *Tafsir al-Mishbah*. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese}

## What it contains
The translation is interpretive rather than literal, in everyday rather than classical Sundanese, and keeps strictly to the language's registers of respect; she was moved to write it because some earlier Sundanese translations used too coarse a register for the Prophet. She spells Arabic terms as Sundanese speakers pronounce them, and gives no footnotes. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese}

## Reception
Jajang Rohmana notes that she did not set out to bring a feminist perspective and that she follows the Saudi edition of the official translation closely, with few exegetical choices of her own beyond Sundanese vocabulary; he concludes that a translation by a woman does not necessarily reflect a reading concerned with women's interests. {cite:gloqur-maryati-sundanese}
