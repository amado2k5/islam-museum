## Summary

A question about cooking for someone who is not fasting in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that cooking for someone who is not fasting without an excuse is forbidden because it is helping in wrongdoing. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4228}

## The question

The questioner asked about cooking food for someone who is not fasting in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4228}

## The answer

The Department answered that it is forbidden to cook for someone who is not fasting in Ramadan without an excuse, because it helps in wrongdoing, and helping in wrongdoing is itself wrongdoing. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4228} It cited the verse forbidding cooperation in sin and aggression in [[passage/quran-5-2|Surat al-Ma'ida 5:2]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4228}
