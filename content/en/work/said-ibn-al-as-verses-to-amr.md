## Overview
Two verses that Ibn Ishaq attributes to [[person/said-ibn-al-as-abu-uhayha|Said ibn al-As, Abu Uhayha]], addressed to his son [[person/amr-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn Said]]. They ask about the son's condition when his strength matures. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses. Their attribution to Said has not been confirmed from another source, so they are recorded here as Ibn Ishaq's transmission.
