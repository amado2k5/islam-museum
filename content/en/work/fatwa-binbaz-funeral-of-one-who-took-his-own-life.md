## Summary

A question on washing and praying over a person who took his own life, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] answered that he is washed, prayed over and buried among the Muslims. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4006}

## The question

The questioner asked whether one who has killed himself is washed and prayed over. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4006}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that he is washed, shrouded, prayed over and buried among the Muslims, because killing oneself is a sin, not unbelief. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4006} He held that it is good for the ruler, the judge, the governor of the town or others of standing to refrain from leading his funeral prayer, as a public disavowal of the act, so that no one thinks they approve of it. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4006} He said that other Muslims still pray over him. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4006}
