## Overview
The novel by Ahmed Saadawi (2013), winner of the Arabic Booker: Hadi al-Attak gathers the remains of victims of the Baghdad bombings into one being, which rises and avenges each part on its killers, and does not stop. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The first Iraqi novel to win the Booker and to be translated into English; it read the violence of the sectarian war in Baghdad through the eye of black fantasy. {cite:wikidata}
