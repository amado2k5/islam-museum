## Summary
*Qut al-Qulub* ("The Nourishment of Hearts") is a book by [[person/abu-talib-al-makki|Abu Talib al-Makki]] on the works of the heart, such as patience, gratitude, love, reliance and tawhid. This page currently presents it through an answer by [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] in volume 10 of [[work/majmu-al-fatawa|Majmu' al-Fatawa]], where he was asked about *Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din* and *Qut al-Qulub*. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 551}

## Its link with the Ihya'
Ibn Taymiyya says that al-Ghazali's [[work/ihya-ulum-al-din|Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din]] "follows it" in what it says on the works of the heart – patience, gratitude, love, reliance, tawhid and the like. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 551}

## Ibn Taymiyya's assessment
Ibn Taymiyya holds that Abu Talib knew hadith, reports and the words of the masters of the sciences of the heart, Sufi and other, better than Abu Hamid al-Ghazali, and that his words are "sounder, better verified and further from innovation", though *Qut al-Qulub* contains weak and fabricated hadiths and many things to be rejected. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 551} In the same answer he says that most of what the Ihya' says on "the destroyers" – pride, vanity, ostentation, envy – is taken from the *Ri'aya* of [[person/al-harith-al-muhasibi|al-Harith al-Muhasibi]]. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 551} {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 552}
