## Overview
The novel by Khaled Hosseini (2007): Mariam and Laila, two generations of Afghan women who meet in one house from Kabul to Kabul across thirty years of rule and war. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Its second book sold more than the first; it became one of the most read novels about the lives of Afghan women. {cite:wikidata}
