## Summary

*La peur-modernité: conflit Islam démocratie* (Fear of Modernity: The Conflict of Islam and Democracy) is a French book by the Moroccan sociologist Fatima Mernissi, published by Albin Michel in Paris in 1992 in 250 pages and reissued in 2010 as *Islam et démocratie*. Its English translation, *Islam and Democracy: Fear of the Modern World*, came from Addison-Wesley the same year, 1992. Its publisher's description says the book examines how opposing sides, from defenders of democracy and women's rights to hard-line militants, draw on the same sacred texts to prove different views, and raises questions about the prospects for democracy and human rights in the Muslim world in the light of the Gulf War. {cite:openlibrary-peur-modernite-1992} {cite:mernissi-literary-agency-books} {cite:archive-org-islam-and-democracy-mernissi-1992} According to its table of contents, the 2002 English edition from Basic Books is arranged in chapters named after fears: fear of the foreign West, fear of the imam, fear of democracy, fear of freedom of thought, fear of individualism, fear of the past and fear of the present. It closes with a conclusion titled "Simorgh is us!". {cite:archive-org-islam-and-democracy-mernissi-2002}
