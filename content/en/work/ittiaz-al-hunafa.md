## Overview
"Ittiaz al-Hunafa bi-Akhbar al-Fatimiyyin al-Khulafa" (The Admonition of the Upright in the Reports of the Fatimid Caliphs) by Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Ali al-Maqrizi, who died in Egypt in 845 AH, according to Hajji Khalifa. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.198}

Hajji Khalifa vocalises the name al-Maqrizi with a fatha on the mim, after Maqriz, a quarter in Baalbek. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.198}
