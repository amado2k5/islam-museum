## Summary

A question about wudu with adhesive on the hand, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that if what is on the limbs of wudu does not form a barrier preventing water from reaching the skin, wudu is valid with it, but if it prevents water reaching the skin, wudu is invalid and it must be removed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4489}

## The question

The questioner asked about performing wudu with an adhesive substance on the hand that is hard to remove. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4489}

## The answer

The Department answered that the general rule is that if what is placed on the limbs of wudu does not form a barrier preventing water from reaching the skin, wudu is valid with it; if it prevents water from reaching the skin, wudu is not valid and it must be removed so that water reaches the hair or skin unobstructed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4489} It quoted [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]] in [[work/al-majmu-sharh-al-muhadhdhab|al-Majmu]] (1/529) that if wax, dough, henna or the like prevents water from reaching any part of a limb, purification is invalid whether the amount is large or small, whereas a remaining trace or colour of henna without its substance, or a trace of liquid oil over which water still flows on the skin, does not affect validity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4489}
