## Summary

al-Mufassal fi San'at al-I'rab is a work of Arabic grammar, ascribed by a research summary supplied to the museum to [[person/al-zamakhshari|al-Zamakhshari]]. This page sets out where it stands in a list of the most-cited works on the Arabic sciences: its field, its author and its date as the summary gives them, its presence in the Shamela library, and what is not known of it here.

## The author and the date

The summary ascribes the book to [[person/al-zamakhshari|al-Zamakhshari]] and gives its date as: author died 1143–1144 CE / 538 AH; composed c. 1120–1122 CE {cite:wikipedia-al-mufassal-fi-sanat-al-irab-en}. That is all this page says of the author and the date; a reader who wants the author's life should look for it in the author's own record where there is one, and one who wants the detail of the dating should go to the documenting page the summary names.

## Place in the canon

al-Mufassal fi San'at al-I'rab appears in a research summary supplied to the museum: a list of sixty of the most-cited works on the Arabic language, twelve in each of its five sciences, where it stands in the section "Grammar". The summary ascribes the work to [[person/al-zamakhshari|al-Zamakhshari]] and gives its date as: author died 1143–1144 CE / 538 AH; composed c. 1120–1122 CE. It names the English Wikipedia page “Al-Zamakhshari” as the page documenting the work {cite:wikipedia-al-mufassal-fi-sanat-al-irab-en}. On its presence in the Shamela library the summary says: Yes, it is in the Shamela library as book 759, with a link to page 389 {cite:shamela-al-mufassal-fi-sanat-al-irab}.

The museum took this from the summary as it stands. The founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026, and the museum did not open the pages it names. The museum has not read the book itself. This record therefore says nothing of the book's contents, chapters, editions or commentaries beyond what other sections of this page cite. The Shamela book number is a bibliographic pointer only; no text was copied from it.
