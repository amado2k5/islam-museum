## Summary
*Jala' al-Afham fi Fadl al-Salat wa-l-Salam ala Muhammad Khayr al-Anam* ("Clearing Understandings on the Merit of Invoking Blessings and Peace upon Muhammad, the Best of Creation") is a book by Imam [[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Shams al-Din Abu Abdullah Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya]] al-Zur'i, a Hanbali, in five chapters. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.3} In his preface he calls it "a book unique in its subject; no one preceded us to its like in the number and abundance of its benefits". {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.4}

## The author's plan
Ibn al-Qayyim summarises the book: he set out the hadiths on invoking blessings and peace on the Prophet, distinguishing the sound from the good and the defective and explaining the defects, then the secrets and dignity of this supplication and the wisdom and benefits it contains, then the occasions and places for invoking blessings on him, then the amount that is obligatory, the scholars' disagreement on it and the weighing of the views. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.4}

## Chapters
- **Chapter 1: Reports on invoking blessings on the Messenger of God**, opening with the hadith of Abu Mas'ud in which Bashir ibn Sa'd asks how to invoke blessings on the Prophet and is taught the Abrahamic formula. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.4}
- **Chapter 2** on the meaning of invoking blessings on the Prophet and on his family, the interpretation of "family" (al), why the blessing on him is likened to the blessing on Abraham and his family rather than on any other prophet, the closing with the two names "the Praiseworthy, the Glorious" (al-Hamid al-Majid), and the meaning of peace, mercy, blessing and "Allahumma", in ten sections. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.140}
- **Chapter 3** on the occasions when invoking blessings on the Prophet is emphasised, as an obligation or a strong recommendation; the first and most important, in his view, is at the end of the tashahhud in prayer, where he reports that Muslims agree it is prescribed and differ on whether it is obligatory. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.380} Among the questions of this chapter is invoking blessings between the extra takbirs of the festival prayer and the positions of the schools on it. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.520}
- **Chapter 4** on the benefits and fruits of invoking blessings on him, which he lists one by one: obeying God's command; joining God and His angels in blessing him; receiving ten blessings from God and being raised ten degrees; its being a cause of the Prophet's intercession when joined to asking for al-wasila for him, of forgiveness of sins, of God's sufficing the servant in his concerns, and of nearness to the Prophet on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.521} {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.522}
- **Chapter 5** on invoking blessings on others than the Prophet; it opens by stating that blessings and peace are invoked on all the prophets and messengers, citing the verses of peace upon Noah and Abraham in Surat al-Saffat. {cite:jala-al-afham-nushayri-atat p.537}
