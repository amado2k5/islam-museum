## Summary
A daily Ramadan series by Omar Abdelkafy with a presenter, in which each episode explains one aphorism from the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah al-Iskandari and branches out into stories, reports and reforming opinions. This set has twenty-seven episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain is marked as needing a source.
