## Summary
A daily Ramadan series by Omar Abdelkafy with a presenter, in which each episode explains one aphorism from the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah al-Iskandari and branches out into stories, reports and reforming opinions. This set has twenty-seven episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 2: remembrance of the heart and of the limbs
- He likens the heart to a bird whose head is love of God and His Messenger and whose wings are hope and fear, and says the sound heart is the one that lives this system, as the hearts of the Companions did. ## Episode 3: the aphorism "when He gives He shows you His kindness, and when He withholds He shows you His power"

## Episode 4: the aphorism "how can wonders be wrought for you when you have not broken your own habits"
- He distinguishes wonders from habits and says among our blameworthy habits are a taste for hearing rumours and tracking people's faults, mentioning that the Prophet said of the thief say he took and of the madman say he is afflicted. - He explains psychological projection as a person putting his ailments on others so that he does not see his faults, and says saints' wonders do not come to one who has not stopped his bad habits such as eating what is forbidden and hurting his family with his tongue.
