# IslamiCity (islamicity.org)

## Summary

IslamiCity is an American English-language Islamic website that says it was launched in February 1995. It presents itself as a non-sectarian, comprehensive and holistic view of Islam, and says it is active in promoting universal values, cooperation and dialogue among civilisations. It is operated and maintained by a 501(c)(3) non-profit. {cite:islamicity-about}

## Beginnings

Its earliest capture in the Internet Archive is dated 8 May 1999, on islamicity.com, titled "IslamiCity in Cyberspace", with sections for commerce, community, education, a guide, an endowment and conferences. {cite:wayback-islamicity-1999}

## Today

The site is active at islamicity.org, asks its visitors for support, and calls itself the oldest and largest online Islamic site; that is its own description. {cite:islamicity-about} It has no Wikipedia article.
