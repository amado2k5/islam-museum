## Summary

A question about a fasting person using an oxygen device, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it does not affect the validity of the fast, because oxygen is air with no physical substance, unless medicinal substances with physical substance are added to it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4122}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a fasting person's use of an oxygen breathing device affects the validity of the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4122}

## The answer

The Department answered that using an oxygen device does not affect the validity of the fast, because oxygen is air without physical substance. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4122} It said that if medicinal substances that have physical substance are added to the oxygen, using it during the day in Ramadan breaks the fast, because they enter the interior through a normal passage. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4122}
