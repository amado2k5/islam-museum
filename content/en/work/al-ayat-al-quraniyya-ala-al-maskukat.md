## Summary
"Al-Ayat al-qur'aniyya ala al-maskukat al-islamiyya: dirasa muqarana" ("Qur'anic Verses on Islamic Coins: A Comparative Study") is a book by [[person/farajallah-ahmad-yusuf|Farajallah Ahmad Yusuf]], published by the King Faisal Center for Research and Islamic Studies in Riyadh, first edition 1423/2003, 440 pages. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 1} {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 6}

## Origin
It began as a doctoral thesis submitted to the Department of Islamic Archaeology, Faculty of Archaeology, Cairo University, in 1418/1997, supervised by Hasan al-Basha and Ra'fat al-Nabarawi. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 15}

## Method
The author studies [[concept/quranic-inscriptions-on-coins|Qur'anic verses on coins]] from [[event/abd-al-malik-coinage|their Arabisation in 77 AH]] under Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, following each verse from its first appearance on coins, analysing why it was inscribed and linking it to the political, sectarian and economic conditions of each age. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 15} The study covers 72 verses, and the author publishes 76 previously unpublished dinars, dirhams and fulus. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 15}

## Chapters
After a preface and an introduction on pre-Islamic Arab coinage and the Arabisation of coinage, a section is given to each verse in order of appearance, followed by the newly published coins, tables of the verses on coins, sources, indexes of persons and places, and plates. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 7} {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 13}
