## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] says the first bound volume he bought in his life was al-Shawkani's «al-Fawa'id al-Majmu'a fi al-Ahadith al-Mawdu'a», for three pounds, in a luxury binding, and that he still has it. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep03 00:19:30} He bought it with the excellence bonus he had begun to receive at his faculty, and says the binding alone would be worth twenty-five pounds today. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep03 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep05 00:13:00} Beside it in his small student library was Ibn Arraq's «Tanzih al-Shari'a al-Marfu'a an al-Ahadith al-Shani'a al-Mawdu'a», in two volumes for nine pounds, the dearest of those books to him. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep05 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep05 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep05 00:20:30} The first room of his library today holds the books on weak and fabricated hadith, beginning with al-Albani's «al-Da'ifa». {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep01 00:02:30}
