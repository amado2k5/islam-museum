## Overview
*Al-Tatabbu' li-ma fi al-Mustadrak min awham* is [[person/muqbil-ibn-hadi-al-wadii|Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i]]'s set of notes on al-Hakim's [[work/al-mustadrak-ala-al-sahihayn|*Mustadrak*]], printed beneath the text in the Dar al-Haramayn edition under the title "Tracing al-Hakim's errors on which al-Dhahabi was silent". {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 1, 6}

## How it was compiled
Al-Wadi'i says that, seeing al-Hakim lax in authentication yet his book indispensable, he resolved some twenty years earlier, while at the Institute of the Meccan Haram, to note useful points from al-Munawi's *Fayd al-Qadir*. He then added what he found in his own research, his colleagues' research and the books of al-Albani, and allowed his colleagues to record a note each with their name beneath it. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 5} Finally he read through the book and completed what he had found "in the briefest wording", as al-Dhahabi had done, without claiming to be exhaustive. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 6}

## The introduction
His introduction, dated 4 Rabi' I 1414 AH, covers scholars' praise of al-Hakim, what was held against him, the views of al-Dhahabi, Ibn Hajar, Ibn al-Salah, Ibn Kathir, Ibn Taymiyya, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn 'Abd al-Hadi and al-Zayla'i on the *Mustadrak*, examples of al-Dhahabi's comments, al-Hakim's error about the two masters' condition, and examples of hadiths he added that are already in the Sahihs. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 6-38} See also [[question/tasahul-al-hakim-fi-al-mustadrak|how al-Hakim's authentications in the Mustadrak are to be treated]].
