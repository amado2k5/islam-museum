## Summary
"Akadimiyyat I'dad al-Qada" (the Academy for Preparing Leaders) is a filmed training course in which [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] teaches the trainees of a residential leadership academy held in Turkey. The Assabile archive keeps twenty-two of its lessons. They run from about three-quarters of an hour to an hour and a half, and each opens and closes with the academy's song. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep18 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:31:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep04 00:02:00} These are training lessons, not lectures for the general public: the trainees work with paper, pen and printed forms, and in several lessons they fill in tests or play team exercises. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep21 00:02:00} He also speaks to viewers at home. He tells them, for example, that the tests will be posted on the academy's website. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep15 00:04:00}

In the first lesson he says the academy lasts twenty-one days, but it is meant to prepare those who will lead the community fifteen years from now. He puts its aim in four words: "a trained, pioneering Islamic network". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:04:00} He counts about 130 trainees, about fifty of them young women and eighty or so young men, plus about twenty staff, and he urges them to learn from the experience of the twenty countries they come from. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:32:00} He sets out the difference between teaching and training. Teaching passes on information. Training adds skills and changed convictions, and then a change of behaviour. He quotes a principle he says he learned from an American trainer of trainers: if behaviour has not changed, training has not happened. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:33:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep01 00:36:00}

Two clues date the lessons. In the tenth he calls a leadership theory "of 2007" one of the newest. In the eighteenth he takes 2008 as the year whose plan is being carried out. Together these suggest the recordings were made around 2007–2008. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep10 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep18 00:35:30} The course book is his own "Creative Training and Teaching" («التدريب والتدريس الإبداعي»): the personality test is on its pages, and so are the team exercises. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep22 00:04:00} The lesson on power and influence was given after the academy moved to a new town, Abant. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep19 00:02:00}

The modern management and leadership authors and models he draws on are his references, and he names them as such: Stephen Covey, Jack Welch, Michael Porter, Henry Mintzberg, Pfeiffer's planning model, SWOT, DISC and situational leadership. The figures and percentages he gives are his own statements, attributed to him at their anchors. The archive lists the lessons only as numbered episodes, so the topics below are taken from what each lesson contains.

## The plan of the course
- **Opening** (lesson 1): the "quartets" for getting the most from the academy.
- **Managing one's life** (2–3): choosing a field of life and writing a life plan. See [[concept/idarat-al-hayat|life management]].
- **Writing and speaking** (4–6): [[concept/fann-al-talif|the art of authorship]], then [[concept/fann-al-ilqa|the art of public speaking]].
- **Analysis** (7–8): his "1442" model for [[concept/tahlil-al-munazzamat|analysing organisations]], and [[concept/tahlil-disc|DISC personality analysis]].
- **Making the leader** (9–10): the definition of [[concept/al-qiyada|leadership]], the leader's six roles, [[concept/al-qiyada-al-mawqifiyya|situational leadership]] and sixteen leadership traits.
- **Creativity** (11–12): [[concept/al-ibda-wal-tafkir-al-ibtikari|creativity and innovative thinking]].
- **Planning and change** (13–18): [[concept/al-takhtit-al-istratiji|strategic planning]], [[concept/taghyir-al-munazzamat|change in organisations]] and [[concept/al-takhtit-al-tashghili|operational planning]].
- **Influence** (19–20): [[concept/al-quwwa-wal-nufudh|power and influence]].
- **Practice** (21–22): team exercises on [[concept/fariq-al-amal|teamwork]], conflict and communication.

## What is not written here
Several things are left out of these pages. Asides on present-day rulers, parties and states are omitted, and so are remarks that amount to legal rulings. Polemical passages about other faiths and about Islamic groups are not reproduced either. The verses of the Qur'an and the hadith he recites are only referred to, never typed. The song at the start and end of each lesson, and the recap and preview clips spliced between lessons, are not counted as lesson content. A single-occurrence name or number that the recording does not render clearly has been dropped rather than guessed.
