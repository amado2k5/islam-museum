## Summary

*A Quiet Revolution: The Veil's Resurgence, from the Middle East to America* is an English book by Leila Ahmed, published by Yale University Press in New Haven in 2011 in 352 pages; it won her the University of Louisville's Grawemeyer Award in Religion in 2013. The publisher's description says she grew up in 1940s Cairo among women who did not wear the veil, and asks why the veil returned so swiftly and what that means for women, Islam and the West.
