## Overview
Abu Bakr Ibn Manda al-Isfahani's book on the names of the Companions. It is the earliest of the four works on knowing the Companions. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.398}

Abu Musa al-Madini added a supplement to it. Ibn al-Athir surveyed it in "Usd al-Ghaba" and gave it the mark (d). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.398}
