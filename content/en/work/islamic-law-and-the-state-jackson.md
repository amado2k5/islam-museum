## Summary

*Islamic Law and the State: The Constitutional Jurisprudence of Shihab al-Din al-Qarafi* is a study by Sherman A. Jackson of al-Qarafi's thought on the relation between law and political authority, published by E. J. Brill in Leiden and New York in 1996 in 249 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islamic-law-and-the-state-1996}
