## Summary

A short question on acting upon a weak hadith, in which Ibn Baz answered that "weak" covers grades with different rulings. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a weak hadith may be acted upon. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014}

## The answer

Ibn Baz divided reports described as weak into several kinds. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014} A report that is hasan with a good chain is, in his view, acted upon. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014} A severely weak report is not acted upon. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014} As examples of severe weakness he gave a liar in the chain, a narrator of very poor memory, or a break in the chain. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014}

## About this record

The answer is published on binbaz.org.sa, the official site of Ibn Baz's legacy, as fatwa 1014 in the al-Jami al-Kabir series. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1014} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
