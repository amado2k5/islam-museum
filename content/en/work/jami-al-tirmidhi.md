## Overview
"Al-Jami al-Mukhtasar min al-Sunan an Rasul Allah" (The Concise Collection of Sunan from the Messenger of God) by Abu Isa [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|Muhammad ibn Isa ibn Sawra al-Tirmidhi]] (d. 279 AH). It is one of the [[concept/kutub-al-sitta|Six Books]]. {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}

Its distinctive feature is that its author states the grade of each hadith (sound, good, or strange), and mentions the views of the jurists on each topic. The author also wrote "Al-Shama'il al-Muhammadiyya", on the description of the Prophet. {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}

## Structure and size
Sunnah.com describes Jami' al-Tirmidhi as containing roughly 4,400 hadith (counting repetitions) in 46 books, and as one of the six canonical collections {cite:sunnah-com tirmidhi}.
