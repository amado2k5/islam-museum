## Overview
"Maqalat al-Islamiyyin" (The Doctrines of the Muslims), a book by Abu al-Hasan [[person/al-ashari|al-Ash'ari]] which sets out the views of the Islamic sects and the doctrines of the theologians. {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin}

## The end of the first part
In the edition read here, the first part ends with the account of what the people of the hadith and the People of the Sunna say (pages 345–352 of the printed text). Al-Ash'ari then says that he holds to it. He then mentions the views of the followers of Abdullah ibn Sa'id al-Qattan, Zuhayr al-Athari and Abu Mu'adh al-Tumni. {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin}
