## Summary
Night Song is a joint album of the Pakistani singer [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] and the Canadian musician Michael Brook, recorded in 1995 at Real World Studios in Wiltshire and released on 20 February 1996 by [[listing/real-world-records|Real World Records]], the last album the label released in his lifetime. {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en} {cite:wikidata-night-song-nusrat}

## How it was made
Having found on [[work/mustt-mustt-nusrat|Mustt Mustt]] that Khan soared highest when given time to improvise, Brook chose a new method: Khan recorded improvisations, some an hour long, onto multitrack tape, and Brook chose the best sections and built each track part by part, with no structure to aim for, a manual process that predated easy digital editing; Sound on Sound called it a "technological nightmare". {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en} Musicians from Baaba Maal's Senegalese band took part, among them the kora player Kauwding Cissokho, with Dildar Hussain on tabla and Farrukh Fateh Ali Khan on harmonium. {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}

## The music
The album joins qawwali vocals to Brook's electronic ambient music and is often counted Khan's most experimental; the two wrote the songs together, with lyrics about relationships and "spiritual cleanliness", and Consumable Online wrote that they make a pronounced effort to lead the listener towards pure spiritual ecstasy. {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}

## Reception and honours
It reached number 4 on [[award/billboard-world-albums|Billboard's world albums chart]] and 65 on the [[award/uk-albums-chart|UK Albums Chart]], Mojo ranked it 21st among the albums of 1996, and it was nominated for the [[award/grammy-awards|Grammy]] for Best World Music Album at the 39th ceremony in 1997, which went to the Chieftains' Santiago. {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en} Five of its tracks were remixed on [[work/star-rise-nusrat|Star Rise]] in 1997, and the label reissued it on 4 May 2015. {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}
