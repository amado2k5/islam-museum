## Overview
A book by [[person/isa-ibn-lahia-al-misri|Isa ibn Lahia al-Misri]] on horse races. It records every race in which horses ran in the pre-Islamic and Islamic periods, as al-Mas'udi also reports. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.529}
