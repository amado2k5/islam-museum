## Summary

*The Arabs: A History* is a book by Eugene Rogan, published by Allen Lane in London on 5 November 2009 in 720 pages, and in the same year by Basic Books in New York in 553 pages with 16 pages of plates. {cite:google-books-the-arabs-rogan} {cite:archive-org-the-arabs-rogan-basic-2009}

## Publication and editions

The book has fourteen chapters, beginning with the move from Cairo to Istanbul under Ottoman rule and passing through Muhammad Ali's Egyptian empire, the first wave of colonialism in North Africa, the First World War and the British and French empires in the region, then the rise and decline of Arab nationalism and the age of oil, and ending after the Cold War. {cite:archive-org-the-arabs-rogan-basic-2009}
