## Overview
The novel by Youssef Ziedan (2008), winner of the Arabic Booker: the scrolls of the monk Hiba in upper Egypt in the fifth century, between the Christianity of Alexandria and the remnants of ancient deities. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It caused wide church controversy in Egypt and is among the best-selling Arabic novels; it was temporarily banned in some countries and translated into more than thirty languages. {cite:wikidata}
