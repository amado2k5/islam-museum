## Summary
Ghayth al-Mawahib al-'Aliyya bi-Sharh al-Hikam al-'Ata'iyya ("The Rain of Lofty Gifts, Commenting on the Hikam") is [[person/ibn-abbad-al-rundi|Ibn 'Abbad al-Nafzi al-Rundi]]'s commentary on [[work/hikam-al-ataiyya|the Hikam of Ibn 'Ata' Allah al-Iskandari]], first in the list of his works given by Muhammad 'Abd al-Maqsud Haykal. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 35} Haykal says al-Rundi was the first to comment on the Hikam and most later commentators relied on him, among them 'Abd Allah Hijazi al-Sharqawi, whose [[work/al-minah-al-qudsiyya|al-Minah al-Qudsiyya]] is almost a summary of al-Rundi's commentary. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 36}

## Why it was written and its standing
Haykal thinks al-Rundi wrote it when some companions asked him to explain the Hikam to them, and that his attachment to the Shadhiliyya came by that route. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 30} He holds that with this commentary al-Rundi made the Shadhili views known "in a way no one had before", spreading them in the Maghrib and al-Andalus, and that it and his other works are basic references for any Shadhili wishing to learn the manners of the path. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 31} {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 35} He characterises al-Rundi's works generally by eloquent style, precise wording and frequent citation of Qur'an and hadith. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 35}

## On the links between the maxims
Haykal reports that scholars differed over whether the maxims are logically connected: Ibn 'Ajiba and Zarruq link each maxim to what precedes and follows it, while Ibn 'Abbad and al-Sharqawi point to such links only occasionally and do not hold to them. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 40}

## In the Ahram edition
In the Taqrib al-Turath edition Haykal chose selections from the Hikam, giving for each maxim the text of Ibn 'Abbad's commentary and then a remark of his own in plain language. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 10} {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 13} An example is his closing remark on the maxim about blessing in a lifetime: what counts is not the length of a life but the blessing in it. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 305}
