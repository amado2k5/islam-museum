## Summary

*Al-Fikr al-Islami: Qira'a 'Ilmiyya* (Islamic Thought: A Scientific Reading) is a book by the Algerian thinker Mohammed Arkoun, published in Arabic by Markaz al-Inma' al-Qawmi in Beirut in 1987, with an introduction by Hashem Saleh titled 'Between the concept of orthodoxy and the dogmatic mind'. It has eight chapters: contemporary Islam facing its heritage; Islam in history; applying the human and social sciences to the study of Islam; Islam, historicity and progress; an introduction to the links between Islam and politics; the concept of supreme sovereignty in Islamic thought; 'the marvellous' in the Qur'an; and a closing account of Qur'anic studies and their prospects. Some chapters are followed by the record of a discussion. {cite:archive-org-al-fikr-al-islami-arkoun-1987}
