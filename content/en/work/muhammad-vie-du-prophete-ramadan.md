## Summary

*Muhammad: vie du prophète* is a book in French by Tariq Ramadan, published by Presses du Châtelet in Paris in 2006; its English translation, *In the Footsteps of the Prophet*, was published by Oxford University Press in New York in 2007. {cite:encyclopedia-com-tariq-ramadan} {cite:openlibrary-muhammad-vie-du-prophete-ramadan} The publisher's description of the English translation calls it the author's first book written for a wide audience, a biography that brings out the spiritual and ethical teachings in Muhammad's life. {cite:openlibrary-in-the-footsteps-of-the-prophet}
