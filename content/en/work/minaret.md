## Overview
The novel by Leila Aboulela (2005): Najwa, a daughter of a rich Sudanese family, falls from the palace to the kitchen in London, and finds in the mosque community what she did not find in the palace. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among the first English novels to show everyday Islam among migrants from within, without apology or self-praise. {cite:wikidata}
