## Summary

*Islam: The Straight Path* is an English book by the Islamic studies scholar John L. Esposito, published by Oxford University Press in New York in 1988 in 230 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islam-the-straight-path-1988} According to its contents, its five chapters treat Muhammad and the Qur'an as messenger and message, the Muslim community in history, religious life in belief and practice, modern interpretations of Islam, and contemporary Islam. A bibliography is on pages 213–219. {cite:archive-org-islam-the-straight-path-1988}
