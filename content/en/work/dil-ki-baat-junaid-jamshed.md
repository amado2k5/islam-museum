## Summary
Dil Ki Baat ("What the Heart Says") is an album by the Pakistani pop singer [[person/junaid-jamshed|Junaid Jamshed]] on Sony BMG, his last pop album before he left the genre for religious reasons, as the article reports from WE News and The News. {cite:wikipedia-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed-en} {cite:wikidata-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed} The article dates it 2002, after recording in 2001, and Wikidata 2001; the museum records both. {cite:wikipedia-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed-en} {cite:wikidata-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed}

## The songs
Thirteen songs, among them 'Tum Kehti Ho', 'Aashnaa', 'Dil Ki Baat', 'Intezaar' and 'Sanwala', with UK versions of 'Na Tu Aayegee' and 'Gori'. {cite:wikipedia-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed-en} The museum records it because Junaid Jamshed is in the browser for his naats, under the founder's decision of 7 November 2026; his first naat album, [[work/jalwa-e-janan-junaid-jamshed|Jalwa-e-Janan]], followed in 2005. {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en}
