## Overview
Verses attributed to [[person/al-numan-ibn-adi|al-Numan ibn Adi]] when he was governor of [[place/maysan|Maysan]]. When they reached Umar ibn al-Khattab, he dismissed him because of them, as recorded in [[event/umar-dismisses-al-numan-ibn-adi|the account of the dismissal]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## The account
A note by the editors says that the verses were written to his wife. The end of the verses is reported differently in the book of the biography and in the "Mu'jam al-Buldan". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses. Their attribution to al-Numan has not been confirmed from another source in this data.
