## Overview
A book by Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Maghribi al-Maqqari, who lived in Egypt. Hajji Khalifa reports that Shihab al-Din mentioned it in "Al-Khaba'ya". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 380}
