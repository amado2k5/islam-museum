## Summary

A question about buying an online game account in instalments, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the sale of an online game follows the ruling on the game itself, and that if it is permissible the friend may buy and own it and then sell it on in instalments at a profit. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4309}

## The question

The questioner asked about acquiring an online game account that a friend would buy for him and that he would then pay for in instalments with a profit added. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4309}

## The answer

The Department answered that the ruling on selling online games follows the ruling on the game itself: if the game is permissible its sale is permissible, and if it contains violations of the Sharia that make playing it impermissible its sale is not valid, referring to its guidelines on online games in fatwa no. 3369. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4309} It said that if the game is permissible, the friend may buy it and, once he owns it, sell it in instalments at a price higher than he paid, noting that the Department permits murabaha provided its conditions are observed and referring to its fatwa no. 2861. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4309}
