## Summary

A question about dividing one sa' of zakat al-fitr among several poor people, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the sa' or its value may be divided among several poor people, because the number of recipients is not fixed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4212}

## The question

The questioner asked whether one sa' of zakat al-fitr, or its value, may be divided among more than one poor person. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4212}

## The answer

The Department answered that zakat al-fitr is fixed at one sa' per person, but the number of people who may receive that sa' is not fixed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4212} It concluded that the sa' may be distributed among more than one poor person. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4212}
