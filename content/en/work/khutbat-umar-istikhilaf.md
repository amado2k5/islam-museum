## Summary
The first sermon Umar ibn al-Khattab delivered when he took over the caliphate, as collected by Ahmad Zaki Safwat in Jamharat Khutab al-Arab from al-Tabari's history, Ibn al-Athir's Kamil and Ibn Qutayba's Uyun al-Akhbar. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 210}

## Details
When Umar was invested he mounted the pulpit and said: "I shall say words, so respond 'amen' to them" — the first utterance he made upon his accession — and he likened the affairs of the Arabs to a docile camel led by its halter while its driver watches where to set it, adding: "By God, were I leading the Ka'ba itself I would carry you onto the right path." {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 210}

Ibn Qutayba in Uyun al-Akhbar adds that when Umar took office he ascended the pulpit, praised God, then said: "Recite the Qur'an and you will be known by it; act upon it and you will be among its people," and: "No man of truth may claim full obedience in disobedience to God." {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 210}
