## Summary
Dunya is the debut album of the English nasheed singer Nazeel Azami, recorded and released in 2006 by [[listing/awakening-records|Awakening Records]], on 1 September; it runs 36 minutes and 44 seconds. {cite:wikipedia-dunya-nazeel-azami-en} {cite:wikidata-dunya-nazeel-azami}

## What it is
The album combines different languages, themes and cultures, and expresses pain as well as joy; its songs and recitals are reflections on some of Azami's personal experiences and on life as a "seeker" aspiring to be at one with the world of God, as the article reports from nasheed retailers. {cite:wikipedia-dunya-nazeel-azami-en} The article files it under nasheed, hamd, naat, world and folk music, in English, Arabic, Urdu and Bengali. {cite:wikipedia-dunya-nazeel-azami-en}

## The songs
Ten songs: 'Opening' in Arabic, 'Rahmanur-Rahim', 'Realisation', 'Ishara' in Bengali and English, 'Knowledge', 'Salam', 'Traveller', 'Heart to Heart', 'Confession' and 'Light' in Arabic, Urdu and English. {cite:wikipedia-dunya-nazeel-azami-en} Awakening lists it among its 2006 releases. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en}
