## Summary
Al-Sullam al-Munawraq in the science of logic by Abd al-Rahman al-Akhdari (d. 983 AH) — a didactic poem of a hundred and forty-four rajaz lines gathering the divisions of speech, the simple and the compound, conceptions and assents, syllogisms and proofs; the first logic text of the schools {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: الأخضري المالكي الجزائري صاحب متن الآجرومية المشهور? لا — الآجرومية لابن آجروم، والأخضري صاحب السلم ومتن مذهب مالك.
