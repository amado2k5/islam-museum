## Summary

*Taysir Mustalah al-Hadith* (Hadith terminology made easy) is a book by Mahmud al-Tahhan; its first edition appeared in 1397/1977 and its second, corrected edition in 1978, in 239 pages. {cite:idhaatululuum-taysir-mustalah-al-hadith} {cite:openlibrary-taysir-mustalah-al-hadith-1978} In the preface to the seventh edition, dated Kuwait 1405/1985, when he taught hadith at Kuwait University, the author says six printings had sold out and that the seventh, from Maktabat al-Ma'arif in Riyadh, was vowelled and corrected. {cite:idhaatululuum-taysir-mustalah-al-hadith}

## Publication and editions

The book has an introduction and four parts: the report, impugning and validating narrators, transmission and its rules, and the isnad and knowledge of narrators; the author divides each topic into numbered paragraphs that begin with a definition and an example. {cite:idhaatululuum-taysir-mustalah-al-hadith} He says he followed the arrangement of Ibn Hajar's *Nukhbat al-Fikar* and its commentary, drew his material mainly from Ibn al-Salah's *Ulum al-Hadith*, al-Nawawi's *al-Taqrib* and al-Suyuti's commentary *Tadrib al-Rawi*, and meant the book as a key to the older works, not a substitute, for students in shari'a faculties. {cite:idhaatululuum-taysir-mustalah-al-hadith}
