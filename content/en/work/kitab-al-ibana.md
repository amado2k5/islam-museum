## Overview
A book by [[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]] devoted to the difference between the Mu'tazila and the people of the imamate, and to what set each group apart from the other on the question of the imamate. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.210}

Al-Mas'udi mentions it in "Muruj al-Dhahab" and says that he chose it for himself. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.210}
