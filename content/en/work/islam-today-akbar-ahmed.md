## Summary

*Islam Today: A Short Introduction to the Muslim World* is an English book by the anthropologist Akbar S. Ahmed; its revised edition was published by I. B. Tauris on 15 March 1999 in 272 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islam-today-ahmed-1999} According to the contents of a later printing, it opens with an introduction raising questions, then treats what Islam is, the challenge of the past in empires and dynasties, the meeting of worlds between Muslim nations and Western modernity, Muslims as minorities, and Islam in the age of the Western media. A bibliography is on pages 241–243. {cite:archive-org-islam-today-akbar-ahmed-2008}
