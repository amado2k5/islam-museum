## Summary
The *zij* of [[person/al-khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]] is a set of astronomical tables mentioned by Ibn al-Nadim in the *Fihrist*, in a passage the editors of the Cairo edition of the [[work/al-jabr-wal-muqabala|Book of al-Jabr and al-Muqabala]] quote from the Cairo printing of the Fihrist. {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.22}

## What Ibn al-Nadim says
Ibn al-Nadim says that al-Khwarizmi was one of the experts in astronomy, that before and after the observations people relied on his first and second tables, known as the Sindhind, and that he wrote "the Book of the Zij" in two versions, a first and a second, besides a book on the sundial, two books on using and making the astrolabe, and a book of history. {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.22}

## Among later scholars
The editors note that [[person/al-biruni-muhammad-ibn-ahmad|al-Biruni]] refers to al-Khwarizmi's tables and astronomical works and wrote at least three works, all commentaries on al-Khwarizmi's books. {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.23} {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.24} They report Suter's opinion that Muhammad ibn Musa was one of those whom [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] charged with measuring a degree of the earth's circumference. {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.24}
