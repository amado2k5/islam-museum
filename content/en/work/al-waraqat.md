## Summary
Al-Waraqat in usul al-fiqh by Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni (d. 478 AH) — a two-page primer gathering the headings of legal theory: divisions, command and prohibition, the general and the specific, the ambiguous and the clarified, abrogation, consensus, reports, analogy, ijtihad, taqlid, the ordering of the proofs and fatwa; the first usul text in the curriculum {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: عبد الملك الجويني إمام الحرمين شيخ الشافعية في عصره.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شروح كثيرة أشهرها شرح الشرواني وشرح ابن الفركاح ونظمه ابن حجر وابن تاج الدين السبكي? لا — ونظمه العمريطي والفضالي {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Al-Waraqat is the beginner's first usul al-fiqh text in most study circles.
