## Overview
A book by Abu Hamid [[person/al-ghazali-abu-hamid|al-Ghazali]] criticising the doctrines of the philosophers ([[concept/falsafa|philosophy]]). Al-Zirikli lists it among his books, together with "Ihya", "Al-Iqtisad fil-Itiqad", "Maqasid al-Falasifa" and "Al-Munqidh min al-Dalal". {cite:al-aalam p. 970}

About a century later [[person/ibn-rushd|Ibn Rushd]] answered it with the book [[work/tahafut-al-tahafut|The Incoherence of the Incoherence]]. {cite:al-aalam p. 849}
