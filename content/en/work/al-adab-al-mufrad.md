## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's «Difaf al-Kawthar»
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] cites the book on the question of praying for someone's long life. The Prophet prayed for Anas ibn Malik, when his mother Umm Sulaym brought him, for abundant wealth and children; the addition of long life is not in the Sahihs of al-Bukhari and Muslim, but al-Bukhari narrated it in «al-Adab al-Mufrad». {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:21:30} He says «al-Adab al-Mufrad» is a book separate from the Sahih, which has its own Book of Adab, so al-Bukhari gave this book the name "al-Mufrad", that is, singled out from the Sahih, so the two would not be confused. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:22:00} He gives the takhrij rule that saying "narrated by al-Bukhari" without qualification means the Sahih to all scholars; if the hadith is in another of his books it must be named: "narrated by al-Bukhari in al-Adab al-Mufrad". {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:22:30}

He notes that al-Bukhari titled a chapter in the Sahih after the Prophet's prayer for his servant's long life although the words on long life are not in it, and says it is al-Bukhari's habit, when he narrates part of a hadith, sometimes to title the chapter by the part he did not narrate; he takes the title as evidence that such a prayer is permitted. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep05 00:25:00}
