## Summary
"Qissa wa-Fikra" (A Story and an Idea) is a television programme presented by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]. The Assabile archive keeps twenty-nine episodes. Most run about twenty-seven to thirty minutes; the twenty-fifth, on Alfred Nobel, is about twenty-two. He greets "dear viewers" and breaks for an advertisement in the middle of each episode. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep27 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep25 00:21:00}

Each episode joins one story to one idea. He names the idea at the start, tells the life of a single historical figure, stops along the way to draw lessons from it, and closes with a list of lessons and a saying of the figure or of a writer. In the second episode, for example, the idea is change, which he defines as moving from an unwanted reality to a hoped-for vision, and the story is the life of Malcolm X. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:26:30} His figures come from Islamic history (a Companion, scholars, rulers and caliphs, a poet, the last king of Granada) and from world history (Machiavelli, Gandhi, Edison, Martin Luther, Trotsky, Mao Zedong, the French Revolution, Alfred Nobel, Nelson Mandela, Napoleon). When the story is from outside Islam he usually ends by weighing the idea against Islamic teaching or against an episode of Islamic history. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep13 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep21 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep25 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep26 00:27:00}

The dates, figures and judgements in the programme are his own statements, and the stories are his accounts; they are attributed to him at their anchors. Several episodes refer to recent events: the index of Transparency International for 2013 (episode 24), the death of Nelson Mandela in December 2013 (episode 26) and world military spending for 2013 (episode 25). The series was therefore recorded in 2014 or later. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep24 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep26 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep25 00:00:30} The Assabile manifest gives real titles only to the first three episodes; it names the rest "Episode 04" to "Episode 29". The twenty-ninth episode ends with his usual closing formula and does not take leave of the series.

## The episodes
**Episode 1. Sacrifice for the true idea.** The search of [[person/salman-al-farisi|Salman al-Farisi]], from the fire-temple of his Magian father near Isfahan, through Christian teachers in al-Sham, Mosul, Nusaybin and Amorium, to slavery in Yathrib and his meeting with the Prophet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep01 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep01 00:22:00}

**Episode 2. Change.** Malcolm X, from Malcolm Little of Omaha to the Nation of Islam, to the Hajj of 1964 and Sunni Islam. See [[person/malcolm-x|Malcolm X]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep02 00:16:30}

**Episode 3. Does the end justify the means?** Niccolò Machiavelli and *The Prince*. He opens with Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud's stratagem at the Battle of the Trench, and closes with the view he presents as Islamic: both the aim and the means must be lawful. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:23:30}

**Episode 4. The power of the word.** [[person/al-mutanabbi|Al-Mutanabbi]], his poems for Sayf al-Dawla and Kafur, and his death. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep04 00:19:30}

**Episode 5. Scholars and rulers.** [[person/izz-al-din-ibn-abd-al-salam|Al-Izz ibn Abd al-Salam]], "the sultan of the scholars", and the rulers of Damascus and Cairo. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep05 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep05 00:16:00}

**Episode 6. McCarthyism.** Senator Joseph McCarthy and the campaign of accusations in the United States from 1950. He calls it the "assassination" of opponents' characters by mass accusation without evidence. He opens with the Prophet's prayer, related by Thawban, that the community not be destroyed by an outside enemy. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep06 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep06 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep06 00:05:30}

**Episode 7. Peaceful resistance.** Mahatma Gandhi, from South Africa to the Salt March and India's independence in 1947. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep07 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep07 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep07 00:23:30}

**Episode 8. Surrender.** [[person/boabdil-muhammad-xii|Abu Abdallah al-Saghir]], the last king of Granada, with the cavalry commander Musa ibn Abi Ghassan as the counter-figure. See also [[event/fall-of-granada-897|the fall of Granada]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep08 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep08 00:18:00}

**Episode 9. Loyalty to the idea.** [[person/alija-izetbegovic|Alija Izetbegovic]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep09 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep09 00:02:00}

**Episode 10. Creativity.** Thomas Edison, whom he quotes on genius as one per cent inspiration and ninety-nine per cent effort, and the lamp. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep10 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep10 00:01:30}

**Episode 11. Assassination as the failure to answer ideas.** [[person/hasan-i-sabbah|Hasan-i Sabbah]] and the Nizari "Assassins" of Alamut. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep11 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep11 00:01:30}

**Episode 13. Priestly authority and the revolt of reform.** Martin Luther, the 95 Theses of 1517 and the German Bible. **Episode 14. Achievements of civilisation.** [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Sultan Suleiman the Lawgiver]] and the box buried with him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep14 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep14 00:08:30}

**Episode 15. Making a revolution, and turning against it.** Leon Trotsky and the theory of permanent revolution. He closes with the observation that peoples make revolutions but people with ideas steer them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep15 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep15 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep15 00:27:30}

**Episode 16. High aspiration and the "resting mind".** [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] in [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|the Mihna]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep16 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep16 00:02:00}

**Episode 17. The corrupt inner circle.** [[group/barmakids|The Barmakids]] and [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep17 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep17 00:01:30}

## His recurring ideas
Across the series he returns to a few claims. A just ruler seeks the counsel of sincere scholars, while an unjust one persecutes them. Reform that is gradual and comes from within is better than revolution. Despotism is the root of corruption. Ideas outlive the people who carry them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep05 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep19 00:28:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep20 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:25:00} In his closing lessons he often tests a figure from outside Islam against an Islamic principle: Machiavelli against the rule that the means must be lawful, Luther against the absence of a priesthood in Islam, and Nobel against the Islamic restraint on war. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep03 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep13 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep25 00:20:00}

## What is not written here
Episode 23, on Mirza Ghulam Ahmad and the Ahmadiyya, is listed but not written up. The episode is a sustained polemic against the founder of a living community, and most of the doctrines it ascribes to him are reported second-hand and disputed. Also left out:
- his asides on present-day rulers, coups, courts and media, which recur in most episodes;
- his judgements on living people and on present-day religious groups;
- the legal rulings he states;
- the machine-repeated windows;
- names and figures that came out garbled or that contradict one another.

All of these, with their anchors, are logged in the lane's progress file. The non-Muslim figures of episodes 3, 6, 7, 10, 13, 15, 20–22, 25 and 26 have no records in the museum; they are described only in the list above.
