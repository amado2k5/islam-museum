## Overview
"Al-Tanbih wal-Ishraf" (The Notice and the Review), the last of the books of [[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]], with which he concluded his works. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

It contains a summary of what he had set out in his earlier books on the sciences and reports of the peoples. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
