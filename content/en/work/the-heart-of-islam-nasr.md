## Summary

*The Heart of Islam: Enduring Values for Humanity* is an English book by the thinker Seyyed Hossein Nasr, published by HarperSanFrancisco in 2002. {cite:openlibrary-heart-of-islam-2002} It has 338 pages. Its jacket description says it presents the core spiritual and social values of Islam (peace, compassion, social justice and respect for the other) and shows their counterparts in the Jewish and Christian traditions. According to its contents, its chapters treat the unity of truth and the multiplicity of revelations, the spectrum of Islam (Sunnism, Shi'ism and Sufism), divine and human laws, community and society, compassion, love, peace and beauty, justice, peace and the question of war, human responsibilities and human rights, and the ethical and spiritual nature of human life. {cite:archive-org-heart-of-islam-2002}
