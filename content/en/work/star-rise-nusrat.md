## Summary
Star Rise is a remix album drawn from [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] and Michael Brook's two collaborations, dedicated to the memory of Khan, who died in August 1997, and released posthumously later that year by [[listing/real-world-records|Real World Records]]. {cite:wikipedia-star-rise-en} {cite:wikidata-star-rise}

## The tracks
Five tracks come from [[work/night-song-nusrat|Night Song]] and four from [[work/mustt-mustt-nusrat|Mustt Mustt]], remixed by artists including Joi, Talvin Singh, Asian Dub Foundation, State of Bengal, Aki Nawaz, Nitin Sawhney and the Dhol Foundation with Fun-Da-Mental; it runs 58 minutes 30 seconds. {cite:wikipedia-star-rise-en} By the liner notes, Nitin Sawhney's remix of 'Tracery', made after Khan's death, is the only track that did not receive his feedback. {cite:wikipedia-star-rise-en}
