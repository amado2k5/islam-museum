## Overview
"Al-Majisti" (The Almagest), a book on astronomy by [[person/batlamus|Ptolemy]]. Al-Mas'udi mentions it among the earlier authorities in the science of the heavenly configurations. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 519}

## The book of Geography
Al-Mas'udi also mentions the "Geography" of Ptolemy, which he says lists four thousand and two hundred cities. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 519}
