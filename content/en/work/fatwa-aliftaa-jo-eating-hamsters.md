## Summary

A question on eating hamsters, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is forbidden, since the hamster belongs to the mouse family. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847}

## The question

The questioner asked whether eating hamsters is allowed, and whether the hamster is one of the animals on which the texts are silent, so that the rule that things are permitted by default applies. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847}

## The answer

The Department described the hamster as a small rodent widely kept in Europe and North America, counted in the mouse family and distinguished by wide cheeks in which it stores food. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847} It said earlier jurists said nothing about eating it, since it was only discovered in the nineteenth century. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847} Since it belongs to the mouse family, it takes the ruling on mice, and eating mice is forbidden; it quoted [[person/jalal-al-din-al-mahalli|Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli]] that animals the sharia encourages killing, such as mice, may not be eaten. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847} It concluded that eating hamsters is forbidden. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 3847, dated 26 December 2023. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3847} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
