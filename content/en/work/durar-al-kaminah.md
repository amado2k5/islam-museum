## Overview
Al-Durar al-Kamina is [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]]'s dictionary of the notable people of the eighth Islamic century. He arranged it alphabetically and began, as a blessing, with those named Ibrahim, though strictly the long hamza should have come first. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/6} In his introduction he names among his sources al-Dhahabi's continuation of Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', Qutb al-Din al-Halabi's history of Egypt and Ibn Fadl Allah's work on his contemporaries. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/5}

## The edition
Salim al-Krenkow corrected it against three manuscripts, among them an old copy in the hand of the author's student al-Sakhawi; Dar al-Jil in Beirut printed it in 1414/1993. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/3}
