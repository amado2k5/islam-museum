## Summary

*Al-Sunna al-Nabawiyya bayna Ahl al-Fiqh wa-Ahl al-Hadith* (The Prophetic Sunna between the jurists and the hadith scholars) is a book by Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali; its first edition was published by Dar al-Shuruq in Beirut and Cairo in 1409/1989, in 160 pages. {cite:openlibrary-al-sunna-al-nabawiyya-ghazali-1989} {cite:ipsajis-review-al-sunna-al-nabawiyya-ghazali} A 2022 review in the journal *al-Wasatiyyah* counts it among al-Ghazali's best-known and most widely read books and says it caused a great intellectual stir among scholars. {cite:ipsajis-review-al-sunna-al-nabawiyya-ghazali}

## Publication and editions

According to the review, al-Ghazali takes on those who derive rulings from a single report without gathering the evidence on a topic and weighing it against the Qur'an, sets out how the jurists derived rulings, and urges preachers to put the community's great questions, such as justice and freedom, before secondary matters. {cite:ipsajis-review-al-sunna-al-nabawiyya-ghazali} The book is not divided into parts and chapters; after a preface and an introduction it gathers topics including women, the hijab and women's testimony, singing, the etiquette of dress and of houses, possession, 'understanding the Book first', the hadiths of tribulations, means and ends, and predestination and compulsion. {cite:ipsajis-review-al-sunna-al-nabawiyya-ghazali}
