## Summary
The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with seventeen episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} In it he explains [[work/hikam-al-ataiyya|the Hikam]] of [[person/ibn-ata-allah-al-iskandari|Ibn Ata Allah al-Iskandari]], and says he chose the book because the heart needs a charge of faith and a sound creed by which the servant rises through the stations of worship. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:03:30} The lesson is a [[concept/majalis-al-ilm|study circle]], which the Sheikh holds the heart cannot do without, since the Friday sermon alone is not enough to sustain it through the week. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:02:00} The recordings of this series are of poor sound and many windows of the transcript are unintelligible, so only what reads clearly has been written here; the rest is listed among the excluded items in the lane's progress file.

## Episode 1: asking and requesting
- He begins Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that a servant's asking of his Lord can amount to suspecting Him, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:06:30} and says it does not mean giving up [[practice/dua|supplication]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:11:00}
- He tells how [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]] in the fire told Gabriel that his Lord's knowledge of his state spared him from asking. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:10:00}
- He says that the angels see from on high the houses whose people pray at night as glittering stars, and that when the owner misses a night through illness or travel they ask after him and pray for him; this is his account. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:09:30}
- He says that when a servant makes himself known to God through humility God makes him known through honour, and when through poverty God supplies him with riches. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:10:30}
- He explains [[practice/istikhara|istikhara]] and sets it against the pre-Islamic custom of the [[concept/tatayyur-omens|sanih and barih]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:15:30}
- He gives his view on the supplication 'I do not ask You to turn back the decree' and on the settled and conditional decree. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:18:00}
- He relates [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]'s state on the day the Prophet died and Abu Bakr's recitation of the verse. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:20:00}
- He tells the story of [[person/yunus|Yunus]] on the ship and in the whale. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:24:30}
- He distinguishes ghiba (speaking of a brother as he would dislike) from ghayba (absence), explaining Ibn Ata Allah's saying that a servant's asking for his Lord is an absence from Him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:31:00}
- He faults the teaching of Descartes' 'I doubt, therefore I am' to children in philosophy lessons; this is his view (criticism). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:29:00}
- He relates the pledge of a group of Companions never to ask people for anything (see [[concept/al-masalah|asking people]]). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:31:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:32:30}
- He explains the next maxim, on the seeker's aspiration, the voices of reality and the trial of outward created things. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:51:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:59:30}
- He describes the Prophet's night prayer: at a verse of mercy he would stop and ask God for His mercy, at a verse of punishment he would stop and seek refuge. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:49:00}
- He holds that someone who keeps retelling his good deed moves from joy to [[concept/riya|boasting]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 01:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 01:05:30}

## Episode 2: turning to God at the beginning
- He speaks of the merit of [[concept/majalis-al-ilm|study circles]], of the one who walks to the house of God in a state of purity, and of the angels who surround a study circle and lower their wings for the seeker of knowledge. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:05:00}
- He explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that a sign of success at the end is turning to God at the beginning, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:46:00} and applies it to the choice of spouses and to [[concept/husn-al-khatima|a good end]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:23:30}
- He relates the account of [[person/al-khansa|al-Khansa']] and her four sons. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:20:00}
- He mentions Pharaoh's sorcerers, who believed in the Lord of Moses and Aaron when they saw the sign. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:41:00}
- He explains the maxim on the difference between those who reason from God to things and those who reason from things to God, speaks of those who seek to prove God's existence by arguments, and quotes Ibn al-Qayyim on this. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:49:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:50:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 01:07:00}

## Episode 3: fleeing from what cannot be escaped
- He explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim of wonder at the one who flees from what he can never be free of and seeks what will not stay with him, returning to it at several points in the lesson. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:42:00}
- He explains the next maxim: a servant should not raise his need to anyone but the One who brought it upon him, for whoever cannot lift his own need from himself cannot lift it from another. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:50:00}
- Most of this episode is unintelligible in the transcript, so nothing else from it has been written.

## Episode 4: companionship and travelling to God
- He explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that one may be doing wrong yet think oneself doing well because one keeps company with someone in a worse state. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:15:30}
- He speaks of the meaning of [[concept/la-ilaha-illa-allah|'there is no god but God']], of the one who says it without acting on it, and of the sinners among the believers in God's oneness being brought out of the Fire. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:22:00}
- He relates the hadith of the last man to leave the Fire and enter Paradise: he asks his Lord to turn his face from the Fire, then to bring him near one tree after another, until he stands at the gate of Paradise and is given in it the like of this world; he mentions that Ibn Mas'ud laughed when he related it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:28:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:30:30}
- He explains the maxim not to keep company with one whose state does not lift you and whose words do not lead you to God, applying it to [[concept/companionship-suhba|companionship]] and the choice of spouses. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:46:00}
- He explains the maxim not to travel from one created thing to another but from created things to their Creator, pairing it with the hadith 'whoever's emigration is to God and His Messenger'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:49:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:58:00}
- He speaks of the patience of [[person/yaqub|Ya'qub]], and of [[person/fatima|Fatima]] asking for a servant and being taught glorification at bedtime. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:51:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:53:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:53:30}
- At the end of the lesson he answers questions from those present, among them one about giving zakat to one's brother. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:59:30}
