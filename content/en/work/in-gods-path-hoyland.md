## Summary

*In God's Path: The Arab Conquests and the Creation of an Islamic Empire* is a book by Robert G. Hoyland, published by Oxford University Press in October 2014 in 320 pages. {cite:openlibrary-in-gods-path-2014} In his acknowledgments the author names Patricia Crone as his undergraduate teacher and doctoral supervisor. {cite:archive-org-in-gods-path-hoyland-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book has an introduction and seven chapters: the setting, the first battles (630–640), eastward and westward (640–652), the push for Constantinople (652–685), the great leap forward (685–715), retrenchment and revolt (715–750), and the making of Islamic civilization, followed by an appendix of source-critical remarks, a timeline and a list of principal figures. {cite:archive-org-in-gods-path-hoyland-scan}
