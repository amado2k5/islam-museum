## Summary

A question about appointing an agent for the sacrifice outside Jordan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a person or charity may be appointed to slaughter the sacrifice even outside the sacrificer's country, provided the agent observes its conditions, though it is better to do it oneself. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4380}

## The question

The questioner asked whether an agent may be appointed for the sacrifice outside Jordan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4380}

## The answer

The Department answered that one may appoint a person or a charity to slaughter the sacrifice even in a country other than the sacrificer's, provided the agent observes the conditions of the sacrifice regarding freedom from defects, age, time of slaughter and distribution. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4380} It said it is better for the sacrificer to carry it out himself so that he gains the full reward and blessing of the sacrifice. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4380}

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4380, dated 31 March 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4380} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
