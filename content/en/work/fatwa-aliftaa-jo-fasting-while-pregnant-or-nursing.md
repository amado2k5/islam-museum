## Summary

A question about fasting while pregnant or nursing, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that fasting is obligatory for both, that if they fear for themselves they break the fast and only make it up, and that if they break it out of fear for the child alone they owe the fidya as well as making it up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4098}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a pregnant or nursing woman should fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4098}

## The answer

The Department answered that fasting is obligatory for a pregnant or nursing woman, but if she fears harm that cannot normally be borne, to herself or to herself and the child, she breaks the fast and owes only making it up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4098} It said that if she breaks the fast out of fear for the foetus or baby alone, she owes the fidya in addition to making it up, because only the child benefited from breaking the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4098} It explained that the fidya is feeding one poor person for each day not fasted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4098}
