## Summary

A question about reading from a printed Qur'an in tarawih, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permitted provided the person praying makes no excessive movements that invalidate the prayer, and that it is better for the imam to recite from memory. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4187}

## The question

The questioner asked whether one may read from a printed copy of the Qur'an during tarawih. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4187}

## The answer

The Department answered that reading from a printed copy of the Qur'an in tarawih is permitted, provided the person praying makes no excessive movements that would invalidate the prayer. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4187} It said it is better for the imam to have memorised the Qur'an and to recite from memory. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4187}
