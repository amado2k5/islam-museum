## Summary

*al-Fikr al-Maqasidi: Qawa'iduh wa-Fawa'iduh* is a short book by Ahmed al-Raysuni, first published by Manshurat al-Zaman in Rabat in December 1999 as no. 9 of the Kitab al-Jayb series. {cite:raissouni-al-fikr-al-maqasidi-pdf}

## Publication and editions

In a 2020 study of al-Raysuni's thought, Jamil Hamdawi cites the book's definition of maqasid thought as thinking that is attentive to the aims of the sharia, relies on their rules and draws on their benefits, and presents al-Raysuni as a defender of al-Shatibi's maqasid project. {cite:raissouni-al-fikr-al-maqasidi-pdf}
