## Summary

Sharh Ibn Aqil ala Alfiyyat Ibn Malik is a work of Arabic grammar, ascribed by a research summary supplied to the museum to [[person/ibn-aqil-baha-al-din|Ibn Aqil]]. This page sets out where it stands in a list of the most-cited works on the Arabic sciences: its field, its author and its date as the summary gives them, its presence in the Shamela library, and what is not known of it here.

## The author and the date

The summary ascribes the book to [[person/ibn-aqil-baha-al-din|Ibn Aqil]] and gives its date as: author died 1367 CE / 769 AH {cite:wikipedia-sharh-ibn-aqil-ar}. That is all this page says of the author and the date; a reader who wants the author's life should look for it in the author's own record where there is one, and one who wants the detail of the dating should go to the documenting page the summary names.

## Place in the canon

Sharh Ibn Aqil ala Alfiyyat Ibn Malik appears in a research summary supplied to the museum: a list of sixty of the most-cited works on the Arabic language, twelve in each of its five sciences, where it stands in the section "Grammar". The summary ascribes the work to [[person/ibn-aqil-baha-al-din|Ibn Aqil]] and gives its date as: author died 1367 CE / 769 AH. It names the Arabic Wikipedia page “شرح ابن عقيل على ألفية ابن مالك” as the page documenting the work {cite:wikipedia-sharh-ibn-aqil-ar}. On its presence in the Shamela library the summary says: Yes, it is in the Shamela library as book 9904, with a link to page 926 {cite:shamela-sharh-ibn-aqil}.

The museum took this from the summary as it stands. The founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026, and the museum did not open the pages it names. The museum has not read the book itself. This record therefore says nothing of the book's contents, chapters, editions or commentaries beyond what other sections of this page cite. The Shamela book number is a bibliographic pointer only; no text was copied from it.
