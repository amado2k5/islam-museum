## Summary
*Al-Fath al-Kabir fi Damm al-Ziyada ila al-Jami' al-Saghir* is [[person/yusuf-al-nabhani|Yusuf al-Nabhani]]'s book merging the supplement *Ziyadat al-Jami'* into [[person/al-suyuti|al-Suyuti]]'s [[work/al-jami-al-saghir-suyuti|al-Jami' al-Saghir]] as though by a single author. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 36-37} [[person/al-albani-muhammad-nasir-al-din|Al-Albani]] says he arranged the two acceptably, sparing researchers the labour of hunting for a hadith and giving them new material nearly half the size of the original. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 14}

## Al-Nabhani's work
In his preface al-Nabhani says the *Ziyada* belonged with the original, since its standing and author are the same, so he joined them, marked the *Ziyada* hadiths with the letter zay, and took care to order the hadiths by the letters of the first word and of those after it, noting that al-Hifni had pointed out in his gloss where the Jami' fails to keep its order. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 36-37} He removed from the *Ziyada* hadiths found word for word in the original and kept repeats that differ in wording or source. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 37} He says he did not find many of the *Ziyada* hadiths in *al-Jami' al-Kabir* and saw no commentary on it, except a report that [[person/abd-al-rauf-al-manawi|al-Munawi]] commented on part of it as *Miftah al-Sa'ada bi-Sharh al-Ziyada*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 36} He prefaced the book with six points and closed it with a note by Muhammad Habib Allah al-Shinqiti introducing the *Ziyada*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 33}

## The count
Al-Nabhani's preface gives 4,440 hadiths for the *Ziyada* and about 14,450 for the whole; al-Albani notes that numbering the *Ziyada* hadiths separately makes it possible to check this, and [[person/zuhayr-al-shawish|Zuhayr al-Shawish]] states after printing that the total is 14,700, of which 4,347 are from the *Ziyada*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 19-20}

## Al-Albani's grading
Al-Albani used al-Fath al-Kabir as the index of his verdicts, then divided it into [[work/sahih-al-jami-al-saghir|Sahih al-Jami' al-Saghir wa-Ziyadatuh]] and [[work/daif-al-jami-al-saghir|Da'if al-Jami' al-Saghir wa-Ziyadatuh]], appending al-Nabhani's preface to the first with his own notes. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 16} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 32-33}

One of those notes objects to the tawassul in al-Nabhani's closing prayer, a disputed question:

:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Yusuf al-Nabhani**: closes his preface by asking God to make the book beneficial, seeking it through the standing (jah) of the Prophet, and to gather him with its author among those accepted by God and by His Prophet. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 37}
- **Sunni: al-Albani**: holds in his note that this tawassul is not legitimate, says Abu Hanifa and other imams rejected it, and refers to [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]]'s *Qa'ida Jalila fi al-Tawassul wa-l-Wasila*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 37}
:::

See also: [[concept/tawassul|tawassul]].

## The introduction of six points
In the Dar al-Kutub al-'Arabiyya al-Kubra printing (Mustafa al-Babi al-Halabi and brothers), al-Nabhani's preface is followed by "an introduction containing six important points". {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 1} {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 4}
- First: al-Suyuti's method, from the preface of [[work/jam-al-jawami-suyuti|Jam' al-Jawami']], of signalling soundness and weakness by the source cited, and al-Nabhani's view that most hadiths of the two books are sound or good. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 4-5}
- Second: a list of the books al-Suyuti read for Jam' al-Jawami', which al-Nabhani saw on the back of a copy. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 5-6}
- Third: that al-Suyuti gathered a hundred thousand hadiths in al-Jami' al-Kabir and did not complete it. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 6}
- Fourth: the counts — about ten thousand in the original by his own count, and 4,440 in the *Ziyada* by a companion's count, 14,450 in all. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 6}
- Fifth: a life of al-Suyuti from al-Sha'rani and Najm al-Din al-Ghazzi: born 849, died 911, he said of himself that he knew two hundred thousand hadiths by heart, and his works exceeded five hundred; al-Nabhani also relates reports of al-Suyuti's dreams, of his seeing the Prophet while awake, and of wonders, which he takes as proof of his sainthood — matters on which views differ. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 6-8}
- Sixth: his chains: he attended Shaykh Mustafa al-Ishraqi's lessons on al-Jami' al-Saghir at al-Azhar in 1287 AH, and transmits al-Suyuti's books by licence through several routes, the highest through Ibrahim al-Saqqa. {cite:fath-kabir-halabi-v1 p. 8-9}
