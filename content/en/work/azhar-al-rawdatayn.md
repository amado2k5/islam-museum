## Overview
A volume by Abu Shama al-Dimashqi, Shihab al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Ismail, who died in 665 AH, on the history of the two states of Nur al-Din and Salah al-Din. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 372-373}
