## Summary

*The Garden of Truth: The Vision and Promise of Sufism, Islam's Mystical Tradition* is an English book by Seyyed Hossein Nasr, published by HarperOne in New York in 2007. The publisher presents it as an account of the beliefs and vision of Sufism, with a history of its saints and schools. Its chapters move from what it means to be human to truth, love and beauty, goodness and action, the path, and Sufism today, with appendices on the Sufi orders and the theoretical tradition. {cite:openlibrary-garden-of-truth-2007} {cite:archive-org-garden-of-truth-nasr-2007}
