## Overview
Three poems by [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]] lamenting the commanders of the army of Mu'ta. The first is for the three together, the second is specifically for [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]], and the third is for [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd]] and [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abdullah ibn Rawaha]]. Ibn Ishaq transmits them in the context of the Battle of Mu'ta. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The date of composition is given in the data as around 8 AH, as a matter of transmission; the attribution has not been confirmed from another source. The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses.
