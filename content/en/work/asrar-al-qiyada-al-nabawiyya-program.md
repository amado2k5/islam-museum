## Summary
"Asrar al-Qiyada al-Nabawiyya" (Secrets of Prophetic Leadership) is a television programme that [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] presented on al-Risala channel. The Assabile archive keeps all thirty episodes, each roughly fifty to fifty-eight minutes long, opening and closing with a song. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:01:00} Al-Suwaidan says it followed his programmes "Life Taught Me" and "Winds of Change": the first on lessons from life in more than sixty topics, the second on a project of renaissance and civilisational change after the Arab Spring; the natural next step, he says, was to discover the leaders who would carry that project. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:01:00}

He describes the programme as reversing the usual method. Instead of narrating the Prophet's life and drawing leadership lessons from it, as he had done in earlier programmes such as "The Leader Messenger", he sets out leadership science methodically in models, some of which, he says, are shown for the first time; he developed them from more than twenty-five years of teaching leadership, and then applies evidence from the Sira to them without "twisting the texts". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep13 00:01:00} Western leadership books, he says, illustrate with Gandhi, Kennedy and Roosevelt, while Muslims have the greatest model in the Prophet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:05:00} In the ninth episode he says the models took him nine months of work. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:01:00}

In the first episode he lists the programme's aims: how to discover leaders; the skills and talents they need; the foundations of leadership upbringing and training; the environment that fosters leadership; and how to evaluate leaders so that people choose well. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:11:30} He asks viewers to keep paper and pen at hand, because it is "not a sermon programme". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:11:00} Each episode closes with a question for discussion on the programme's Facebook page, which he promises to answer after three days, and with a chosen saying from an old scholar or a modern writer. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep06 00:51:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:50:30} The figures, dates and percentages he gives are his own statements and are attributed to him at their anchors; the modern management and leadership authors he quotes are his references.

The episodes speak of the Arab revolutions and of Muammar al-Qaddafi as already dead, so they were recorded after 2011. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:48:00}

## The plan of the programme
The programme runs through a sequence of models in order, which he recaps at the start of nearly every episode: {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:01:00}
- **Understanding leadership** (episodes 1–8): its definition as "the ability to move people towards a goal" and the four parts of that definition, then the bad leader, then the study of followers and their eight kinds. See [[concept/al-qiyada|leadership]], [[concept/al-qiyada-al-sayyia|bad leadership]] and [[concept/ilm-al-atba|followership]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep01 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep02 00:04:30}
- **Discovering leaders** (9–13): six signs, and ways of looking for them. See [[concept/istikshaf-al-qada|discovering leaders]].
- **Leadership upbringing** (14–18): five elements with five parts each. See [[concept/al-tarbiya-al-qiyadiyya|leadership upbringing]].
- **Training in general leadership** (19–27): the leader's eight roles, then the competencies each role needs and the skills that train them. See [[concept/adwar-al-qaid-al-thamaniya|the leader's eight roles]].
- **Training in specialised leadership** (28). See [[concept/al-qiyada-al-takhassusiyya|specialised leadership]].
- **The environment that fosters leadership** (29–30): six parts. See [[concept/al-bia-al-muhaffiza-lil-qiyada|the leadership-fostering environment]].
