## Summary

A question about a mother paying her zakat to her children, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a mother may give her zakat to her children if they are poor and eligible and not already adequately maintained by others. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4350}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a mother may pay the zakat on her wealth to her children. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4350}

## The answer

The Department answered that a mother may give zakat to her children if they are eligible for it, for example if they are poor, have no wealth and are not adequately maintained by someone else. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4350} It cited a hadith narrated by [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] about Zaynab, the wife of [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abdullah ibn Mas'ud]], whose meaning is that her husband and her child were the most deserving of her charity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4350} It quoted [[person/al-mawardi|al-Mawardi]] in [[work/al-hawi-al-kabir|al-Hawi al-Kabir]] (8/537) that a wife may pay her zakat to her husband, arguing from the general wording of the verse on the recipients of zakat, [[passage/quran-9-60|Surat al-Tawba 9:60]], and from the hadith of [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] about Zaynab. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4350}
