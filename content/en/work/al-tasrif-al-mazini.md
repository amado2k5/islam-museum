## Summary
al-Tasrif is a book on morphology (sarf and tasrif), by [[person/abu-uthman-al-mazini|Abu Uthman al-Mazini]]. The file gives the author's death as 249 AH / 863 CE; this is the date of the author's death, not of the book's composition.
