## Summary
"Jala' al-adhhan fi zij al-Battani" ("The Polishing of Minds on al-Battani's Zij") is a book by [[person/al-biruni-muhammad-ibn-ahmad|Abu al-Rayhan al-Biruni]] on [[work/al-zij-al-sabi|al-Battani's Zij]]. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 42} Nallino reports that al-Biruni, who praised al-Battani in his writings, himself testifies that he wrote a book under this title. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 42}

## Context
Nallino mentions it while tracing the attention later scholars of the fifth Islamic century gave the Zij: Ali ibn Ahmad al-Nasawi judged it the most perfect observational zij; an anonymous pupil of Ahmad al-Sijzi cited it in his book on making the astrolabe; and al-Biruni wrote this book. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 42} Nallino says nothing of its survival or manuscripts. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 42}
