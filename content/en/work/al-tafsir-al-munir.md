## Summary

*Al-Tafsir al-Munir fi al-'Aqida wa al-Shari'a wa al-Manhaj* is a complete commentary on the Qur'an by [[person/wahba-zuhayli|Wahbah al-Zuhayli]] (d. 1436/2015). {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir} In his foreword the author says he wanted a commentary that would connect Muslims and non-Muslims with the Book of God, combining the transmitted and the reasoned, drawing on the most reliable old and modern commentaries, and giving the most sound opinion according to the wording's closeness to Arabic usage and to the verse's context. {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir}

He sets out his method in steps: dividing the verses into thematic units with headings; summarising what each sura contains; explaining the language; giving the occasions of revelation from the soundest reports and discarding weak ones; then the commentary itself, the rulings derived from the verses, and their rhetoric and grammar. {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir} He adds that he will, as far as possible, follow thematic commentary, bringing together verses on a single subject such as jihad, the hudud penalties, inheritance, marriage, usury and wine, and that he treats each Qur'anic story fully at its first occurrence and refers back to it when it recurs. {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir} Al-Maktaba al-Shamila quotes Shaykh Abd al-Karim al-Khudayr describing it as a detailed, analytical commentary with useful points and fine observations, though not of the standing of the works of the leading verifying exegetes such as [[work/tafsir-ibn-kathir|Ibn Kathir]] and [[work/jami-al-bayan-al-tabari|Ibn Jarir]]; modern commentaries, he says, are useful for what they add to the classical works. {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir}

A 2025 study in *Jurnal Iman dan Spiritualitas* of UIN Sunan Gunung Djati, Bandung, says al-Zuhayli wrote the commentary after his [[work/usul-al-fiqh-al-islami-zuhayli|*Usul al-Fiqh al-Islami*]] and [[work/al-fiqh-al-islami-wa-adillatuh|*al-Fiqh al-Islami wa-Adillatuh*]], and that it has been translated into Indonesian, Malay, Turkish and other languages. {cite:uinsgd-jis-zuhayli-munir-social-ethics} The study examines the principles of social ethics in the commentary, among them lowering one's voice in speech, verifying news (*tabayyun*), reconciling disputing parties, fairness, and refraining from mocking others. {cite:uinsgd-jis-zuhayli-munir-social-ethics}

## Publication and editions

Its first edition was published by Dar al-Fikr in Damascus and Dar al-Fikr al-Mu'asir in Beirut in 1411/1991, in thirty-two parts, thirty of commentary and the rest indexes; al-Maktaba al-Shamila presents it with the pagination of the printed edition. {cite:shamela-al-tafsir-al-munir} The Indonesian study, for its part, describes the first edition as sixteen volumes totalling more than ten thousand pages. {cite:uinsgd-jis-zuhayli-munir-social-ethics}
