## Overview
The novel by Khaled Hosseini (2013): the story of an Afghan family branching from Kabul to Paris and California, asking how a people scatters across continents and yet stays connected to the first catastrophe. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The most temporally intricate of his novels; it extended Afghan diaspora literature to questions of memory and identity across generations. {cite:wikidata}
