## Overview
"Al-Isaba fi Tamyiz al-Sahaba" (The Attainment in Distinguishing the Companions) by the hadith scholar Shihab al-Din Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Hajar al-Asqalani ([[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]]), in five large volumes. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 462}

## What Hajji Khalifa mentions
It gathers what is in "Al-Istiab", its supplement, and "Usd al-Ghaba", and it corrects many of their omissions. Al-Suyuti abridged it and called the abridgement "Ayn al-Isaba". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 462}
