## Overview
"Al-Muzaffari", a book by al-Muzaffar ibn al-Aftas, king of Badajoz, in about fifty volumes. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.470}

The author of "Nafh al-Tib" describes it as "al-Tadhakkur al-Muzaffari", and says that it covers arts and sciences including campaigns, biographies, proverbs, reports, and all the sciences of literature. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.478}
