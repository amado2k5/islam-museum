## Summary

*Islamic Philosophy from Its Origin to the Present: Philosophy in the Land of Prophecy* is an English book by Seyyed Hossein Nasr, published by State University of New York Press in Albany in 2006 in its SUNY series in Islam, in 380 pages. Its catalogue record lists fifteen chapters. They begin with the study of Islamic philosophy in the West and the meaning and role of philosophy in Islam, pass through questions of existence, quiddity and knowledge, then the schools of Shiraz, Isfahan and Tehran and Mulla Sadra, and end with reflections on Islam and modern thought. {cite:openlibrary-islamic-philosophy-origin-present-2006} {cite:archive-org-islamic-philosophy-origin-present-nasr-2006}
