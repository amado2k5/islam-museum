## Summary

A question about eating or drinking by mistake during an oath-expiation fast, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that someone who eats or drinks by mistake does not break the fast and continues it, whatever kind of fast it is. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4345}

## The question

The questioner asked about someone who ate or drank forgetfully while fasting as an oath expiation. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4345}

## The answer

The Department answered that whoever eats or drinks forgetfully has only been fed and given drink by God, so his fast is not broken and he continues fasting. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4345} It said this applies generally, whether the fast is obligatory, a make-up, an expiation or voluntary. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4345}
