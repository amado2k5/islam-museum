## Summary
Verses Ibn Hisham attributes to al-Dahhak ibn Khalifa, describing what befell him when he plunged through from the house's back, saying in them he will not return to its like. The museum does not reproduce the verses' text. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## Note
An addition of Ibn Hisham, in an isnad containing "al-Thiqa" (p. 160). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}
