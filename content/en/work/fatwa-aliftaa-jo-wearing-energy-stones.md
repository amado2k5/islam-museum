## Summary

A question about wearing energy stones, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that there is no objection to adorning oneself with customary gemstones, and that energy stones used for treatment are governed by experiment and scientific study: if their benefit is established they may be used, provided one does not believe the stone itself benefits or harms. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4429}

## The question

The questioner asked about wearing so-called energy stones. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4429}

## The answer

The Department answered that there is no objection to a woman adorning herself with precious stones such as rubies, agate and the like, when they are among the things customarily worn for adornment. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4429} It said that so-called energy stones, when used for treatment, are a matter governed by experiment and scientific studies showing whether they have an effect; if studies or experience show a benefit to human health, there is no objection to using them, since they are part of God's creation like medicines taken from plants and other created things. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4429} It stipulated that a Muslim must not believe the stone itself is what benefits or harms; it is only one means among others, and the matter belongs to God alone. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4429}
