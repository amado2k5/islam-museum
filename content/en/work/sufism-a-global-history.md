## Summary

*Sufism: A Global History* is an English book by Nile Green, published by Wiley-Blackwell in 2012. The publisher's description says it traces the spread and influence of Sufism from its emergence in the ninth century through the Middle East, Asia and Africa and on into Europe and the United States, places it in its social and cultural contexts, and covers its links with music, poetry, architecture and folk traditions, without assuming prior knowledge of Islamic history. {cite:openlibrary-sufism-global-history-2012} {cite:archive-org-sufism-global-history-green-2012}
