## Summary
Al-Matalib al-Aliya bi-Zawa'id al-Masanid al-Thamaniyya is a hadith collection by the hadith master [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]] (773–852 AH). It gathers the additional reports found in eight musnad collections that are not in the six main books, arranges them by book and chapter, and states for each report what its isnad is worth. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.1}

## Structure and method
Each chapter opens with a numbered heading, such as "36 — the Qur'an contains all the rulings, in summary and in detail". The report follows with its full isnad as the musnad compiler gave it. The editor then adds three parts: a section on the grade of the report, where the isnad and its narrators are examined; a takhrij, which lists where the report appears in other sources; and notes in the margin on variant readings. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.17}

Volume 13 of the Dar al-Ghayth edition (1420 AH, edited by Qasim Salih al-Qasim) covers hadiths 3100 to 3373. It starts at the end of the Book of Knowledge, chapter 36, and moves into the Book of Remembrance. The chapter headings in this volume are:

- **36, the Qur'an containing the rulings:** the first report is a saying of Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud urging seekers of knowledge to reflect on the Qur'an, which he says holds the knowledge of the first and the last (hadith 3100). {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.17}
- **37, warning against lying:** opens with hadith 3101, about a man who brought the camels of his charity to the Prophet, and the question of what counts as a gift and what counts as charity. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.19}
- **38, refreshing the hearts so they may understand:** the chapter heading only, at this point in the scan. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.22}
- **39, warning against lying about the Messenger:** the editor notes that the meaning of this text has many witnesses that reach the level of tawatur, citing Ibn Hajar's Fath al-Bari. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.25}
- **40, chapter on the interpretation of a Qur'anic phrase:** the heading is not legible in the scan and should be checked in the edition. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.53}
- **The Book of Remembrance:** begins with the chapter on the usual lifespan, then the chapter on remembering death and shortening hope, then the chapter on useful counsels. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.55}

## Content and judgement
Ibn Hajar sets out the extra reports; the editor judges their grade. Many reports rise to "good for another" or "sound for another" because of their witnesses, and the editor says so in each case. These are the editor's judgements in this edition. They are neither Ibn Hajar's own judgements nor the book's. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.19}

## The edition used
The first Dar al-Ghayth printing, Riyadh, 1420 AH (2000 CE). Qasim Salih al-Qasim edited it and Sa'd ibn Nasir al-Shathri coordinated it. Volume 13 is the one used here. {cite:matalib-aliya-qasim-v13 p.3}

## For young visitors
Al-Matalib al-Aliya is a book by Ibn Hajar that gathers hadiths found in eight musnad collections but not in the six main books. This volume has reports on the Qur'an, lying, refreshing the soul, death and counsel.

## Research notes
- Grades in this volume belong to the editor. Attribute each grade to him, not to Ibn Hajar.
- Hadith numbers are the edition's numbering and run through the volumes. Check each one in its place.
- The takhrij of each report should be read in the edition itself. This article does not replace it.
- Related: [[concept/kutub-al-sitta|the six books]], the wider frame that these extra reports supplement.
