## Summary

*Ruh al-Hadatha: al-Madkhal ila Ta'sis al-Hadatha al-Islamiyya* (The Spirit of Modernity: An Introduction to Founding an Islamic Modernity) is a book by the Moroccan philosopher Taha Abderrahmane, published by al-Markaz al-Thaqafi al-Arabi in Casablanca and Beirut in 2006 in 288 pages. {cite:archive-org-ruh-al-hadatha-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book opens with a general theoretical introduction on 'the spirit of modernity and the right to creativity'. It sets out three principles of the spirit of modernity, maturity (rushd), critique and universality, and asks how to move from an imitative modernity to a creative one. Each principle then gets a part on its Islamic application. The part on critique covers globalization and the Western family system. The part on maturity covers translation and modernist readings of the Qur'an. The part on universality covers citizenship and brotherhood. {cite:archive-org-ruh-al-hadatha-scan}
