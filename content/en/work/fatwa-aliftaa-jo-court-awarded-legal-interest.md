## Summary

A question about court-awarded legal interest, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the real costs of the lawsuit may be deducted from it, and the rest returned to the person it was imposed on, or, if that is a public body, spent on public interests or given to the poor. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4302}

## The question

The questioner asked about legal interest awarded by a court. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4302}

## The answer

The Department answered that someone awarded legal interest by a judge may deduct from it the real costs of bringing the lawsuit. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4302} It said that anything beyond that should be returned to whoever it was imposed on if that is a specific person, and if it is a public body, spent on the public interests of Muslims, such as schools, mosques and roads, or given in charity to the poor and needy. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4302}
