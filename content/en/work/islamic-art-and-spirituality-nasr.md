## Summary

*Islamic Art and Spirituality* is a book by [[person/seyed-hossein-nasr|Seyyed Hossein Nasr]], published by State University of New York Press in 1987. {cite:google-books-islamic-art-and-spirituality} The publisher's description says Nasr shows Western and Muslim readers that each art form in the Islamic tradition rests on a science of nature concerned not with the outer appearance of things but with their inner reality. {cite:google-books-islamic-art-and-spirituality} According to the same description, it ranges across calligraphy, painting, architecture, literature, music and the plastic arts, and shows the role art plays in the life of individual Muslims and of the community: to inspire the remembrance and contemplation of God. {cite:google-books-islamic-art-and-spirituality} It then traces the creative act to its source in inner knowledge and barakah, or grace. {cite:google-books-islamic-art-and-spirituality}

## Publication and editions

The paperback edition appeared on 12 February 1987, in 213 pages (ISBN 0887061753). {cite:google-books-islamic-art-and-spirituality} Open Library records the same Albany edition under ISBN 0887061745, and another 1987 edition from Golgonooza Press in Ipswich. {cite:openlibrary-islamic-art-and-spirituality-1987}
