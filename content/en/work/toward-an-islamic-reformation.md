## Summary

*Toward an Islamic Reformation: Civil Liberties, Human Rights, and International Law* is an English book by the Sudanese scholar Abdullahi Ahmed An-Na'im, published by Syracuse University Press in 1990 in 253 pages, with another edition in July 1996.
