# MuslimMatters (muslimmatters.org)

## Summary

MuslimMatters is an English-language Muslim group blog. Its earliest capture in the Internet Archive is dated 13 March 2007, where it describes itself as "Discourses in the Intellectual Traditions, Political Situation, and Social Ethics of Muslims", with sections for About, Chronicles, M-Media and Connect. {cite:wayback-muslimmatters-2007}

## Today

The blog is active at muslimmatters.org. Its own pages on its origins (2012) and on its 2007 listing among influential religion blogs no longer open and were not read. {cite:wayback-muslimmatters-2007}
