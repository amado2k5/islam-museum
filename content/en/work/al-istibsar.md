## Overview
"Al-Istibsar" (Insight), a book by al-Mas'udi ([[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]]) on the doctrines of the adherents of religions and sects. He refers to it in the question of the faith of Abd al-Muttalib, alongside his book "Al-Maqalat fi Usul al-Diyanat". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 447}
