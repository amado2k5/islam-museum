## Summary
"Practical Management" (al-Idara al-'Amaliyya) is a recorded training course by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]. Opening it, he says he gave it this name because in his long years of training in management he noticed that the field has a theoretical side and an applied side, and that many people want from the theories what can actually be applied; so he gathered in this course what can be taken and used at once. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:02:00} The Assabile archive holds five of its lectures; their files are named the first, second, fourth, fifth and sixth lectures, so the third lecture is not in the archive. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities}

## What he promised at the start
In the first lecture he listed the course's topics: actually writing a strategic plan, in an unconventional way, on a single page, with the mission, the vision, the indicators, a five-year plan and an annual plan; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:02:30} leadership, which he said occupies him because he teaches it and because he runs a leadership training centre for young people where he has tested which theories work and which do not; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:02:30} the practical difference between leadership and management, with equations by which trainees can measure their own practice, and the analysis of leadership personalities; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:03:30} changing organisations and individuals, and what someone who takes over a struggling institution should change in the short and the long term; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:05:00} and a personal plan by which a person orders his life, one decision-making method that can be applied at once, and then creativity and teamwork as time allowed. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:06:00}

## When the course was given, as it suggests
He said at the start that he had come back from Canada on the Thursday, and that he had found there a new leadership book called *Know How*, published in 2007 the week before, which he bought, read and summarised for the trainees. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:04:00} This is his statement; the archive gives no recording date.

## The lectures and their topics
- First lecture: an introduction to management, the history of its theories, and strategic planning — [[concept/ilm-al-idara|management science]], [[concept/al-takhtit-al-istratiji|strategic planning]], [[concept/taqsim-al-amal|division of labour]], [[concept/nazariyyat-al-dafiyya|motivation theories]], [[concept/al-idara-al-islamiyya|Islamic management]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01}
- Second lecture: the vision as the most important part of strategic planning, then the mission, indicators, business units and family companies — [[concept/al-ruya-wa-al-risala|vision and mission]], [[concept/kpi-muashirat|performance indicators]], [[concept/strategic-business-unit|strategic business unit]], [[concept/al-sharikat-al-ailiyya|family companies]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 00:02:00}
- Fourth lecture (archive episode 3): leadership and its roles, the Pareto law, the laws of monkeys, situational leadership and DISC analysis — [[concept/al-qiyada|leadership]], [[concept/qanun-parito|Pareto law]], [[concept/qawanin-al-qurud|the monkey laws]], [[concept/al-qiyada-al-mawqifiyya|situational leadership]], [[concept/tahlil-disc|DISC analysis]], [[concept/al-khibra-wa-al-khidma|experience and service]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03}
- Fifth lecture (episode 4): motivation, the decision matrix and organisational change — [[concept/al-tahfiz|motivation]], [[concept/musfafa-ittikhadh-al-qarar|decision matrix]], [[concept/taghyir-al-munazzamat|organisational change]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04}
- Sixth lecture (episode 5): managing life and time, teamwork and meetings, and the nominal group — [[concept/idarat-al-hayat|life management]], [[concept/idarat-al-waqt|time management]], [[concept/fariq-al-amal|teamwork]], [[concept/al-majmua-al-shakliyya|nominal group]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05}

## People and books he cites
In the course he draws on figures from the history of management and beyond, among them [[person/sun-tzu|Sun Tzu]] and his [[work/fann-al-harb|The Art of War]], [[person/adam-smith|Adam Smith]], [[person/frederick-taylor|Frederick Taylor]], [[person/henry-ford|Henry Ford]], [[person/elton-mayo|Elton Mayo]], [[person/abraham-maslow|Abraham Maslow]], [[person/vilfredo-pareto|Vilfredo Pareto]], [[person/jack-welch|Jack Welch]], [[person/william-oncken|William Oncken]] and [[person/kenneth-blanchard|Kenneth Blanchard]] with their [[work/one-minute-manager-meets-the-monkey|The One Minute Manager Meets the Monkey]], [[person/stephen-covey|Stephen Covey]] and [[person/abdel-rahman-al-sumait|Abdul Rahman al-Sumait]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05}
