## Summary

A question about Muslim labourers working on the building of a Christian cemetery, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permissible, being kindness and the good dealing that is the rule towards non-Muslims, and cited the Qur'an, the Sunna, the reports of the early Muslims and the jurists of the schools on shrouding and burying a non-Muslim and digging his grave for a fee. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513}

## The question

The questioner asked: may Muslim workers take part in building a Christian cemetery in which Christian dead will be buried? {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513}

## The answer

The Department answered that Muslim workers may build and prepare the cemeteries of non-Muslims, including the Christians living in their country and others, since this is kindness and good dealing; the rule in dealing with non-Muslims is that it rests on justice, kindness and the avoidance of ill-treatment, by the text of [[passage/quran-60-8|al-Mumtahana 8]], and it is one of the rights of protected non-Muslims over the Muslims and a sign of God's honouring of the human being in [[passage/quran-17-70|al-Isra 70]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513} It said the Sunna, the reports of the early Muslims and the jurists' statements allow a Muslim to lend a hand, whether for pay or as a kindness and voluntarily, to non-Muslims in what upholds human dignity, including burying them after death: the Prophet ordered [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] to bury his uncle [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]], who died outside Islam, as in [[work/sunan-al-nasai|al-Nasa'i's Sunan]], and it is reported from [[person/said-ibn-jubayr|Sa'id ibn Jubayr]] that a Christian man died leaving a Muslim son who did not follow his bier, and [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] said he should have followed it, buried him and sought forgiveness for him during his life, as Ibn Abi Shayba records in his Musannaf. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513} It quoted [[person/imam-al-haramayn-al-juwayni|al-Juwayni]] in [[work/nihayat-al-matlab-juwayni|Nihayat al-Matlab]] (3/17) that shrouding and burying a non-Muslim who has no guardian of his own faith is a collective duty on the Muslims; [[person/al-khatib-al-shirbini|al-Shirbini]] in [[work/mughni-al-muhtaj|Mughni al-Muhtaj]] (2/32) that the sounder view is that shrouding and burying a protected non-Muslim is obligatory from the treasury, and failing that on the Muslims, when he has no property and no one bound to maintain him; and the authoritative Hanbali works, following what is reported from [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]], that a Muslim may dig a grave for protected non-Muslims, as [[person/al-buhuti-mansur-ibn-yunus|al-Buhuti]] says in [[work/kashshaf-al-qina|Kashshaf al-Qina']] (3/560) that there is no harm in digging a grave for a protected non-Muslim for a fee. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513} It concluded that there is no blame on a Muslim who works on non-Muslim cemeteries, since nothing in sharia opposes it; rather the rule in dealing with non-Muslims is justice and kindness towards them. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4513}
