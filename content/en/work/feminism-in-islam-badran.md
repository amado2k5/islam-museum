## Summary

*Feminism in Islam: Secular and Religious Convergences* is an English book by the historian Margot Badran, published by Oneworld in Oxford; one catalogue record dates it to 25 September 2008, and its title page gives 2009, in 349 pages. The publisher's description calls it a collection of her essays over three decades tracing the history and interaction of secular and Islamic feminisms in Muslim societies since the nineteenth century.
