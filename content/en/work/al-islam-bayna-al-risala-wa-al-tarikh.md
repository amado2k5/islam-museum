## Summary

*Al-Islam bayna al-Risala wa-l-Tarikh* (Islam between Message and History) is a book by the Tunisian thinker Abdelmajid Charfi, professor of Islamic thought and Arab civilisation at the Faculty of Letters of the University of Tunis I in Manouba. Its first edition came from Dar al-Tali'a in Beirut in January 2001 and its second in July 2008, in the publisher's series "Naqd al-Fikr al-Dini" (Critique of Religious Thought). {cite:archive-org-al-islam-bayna-al-risala-wa-al-tarikh-2008} In the preface to the second edition, the author says the book grew out of years of research on questions of Islamic thought. Rather than analyse in detail the evidence he had gathered from works of theology, law and legal theory, he chose a concise account of the main ideas for general readers. He notes that the book, in its Arabic original and its French translation, drew contradictory reactions, from praise to attack; that an English translation had been prepared and a German one was under way; and that he reissued it unchanged. One of its chapters treats Sufism as a historical and social phenomenon that shaped political, economic and intellectual life. {cite:archive-org-al-islam-bayna-al-risala-wa-al-tarikh-2008}
