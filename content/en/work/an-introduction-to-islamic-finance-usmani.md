## Summary

*An Introduction to Islamic Finance* is an English book by the Pakistani judge and jurist Muhammad Taqi Usmani; its first edition was published by Idaratul Ma'arif in Karachi in 1998 in 246 pages. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-finance-usmani-1998} According to the contents of an uploaded copy, it opens with the difference between capitalist and Islamic economies and asset-backed financing, then gives chapters to musharakah, mudarabah and diminishing musharakah, murabahah, ijarah, salam and istisna', Islamic investment funds and the principle of limited liability, and closes with a realistic evaluation of the performance of Islamic banks. {cite:archive-org-introduction-to-islamic-finance-usmani-text}
