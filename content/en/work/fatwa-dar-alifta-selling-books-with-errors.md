## Summary

A question on selling books said to contain religious violations, which Egypt's Dar al-Ifta answered according to the purpose of the sale and the buyer. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 8948}

## The question

The questioner said a friend had given him books to sell on his behalf, and other friends told him they contained religious violations; he asked whether he may sell them. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 8948}

## The answer

Dar al-Ifta answered that selling scholarly books is permitted when the seller owns them and they contain something of benefit. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 8948} It said books that the competent scholarly committees have found to contain violations and errors, and have not licensed for publication, may be sold to scholars, specialists and students for study and refutation, or for pulping and recycling. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 8948} Selling them to others for circulation and use of their contents it held to be forbidden, as helping falsehood, on the rule that means take the ruling of their ends. {cite:dar-alifta fatwa 8948}
