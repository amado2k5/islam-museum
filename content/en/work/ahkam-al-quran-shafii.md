## Overview

Ahkam al-Qur'an attributed to Imam al-Shafi'i (d. 204 AH); Hajji Khalifa notes he was the first to compose in the genre of Qur'anic legal rulings, the text being gathered from al-Shafi'i's legal corpus {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 210}.
