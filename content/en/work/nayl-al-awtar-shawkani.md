## Summary
*Nayl al-Awtar min Asrar Muntaqa al-Akhbar* is [[person/al-shawkani|Muhammad b. 'Ali al-Shawkani]]'s commentary on *Muntaqa al-Akhbar*; the title pages of the old printing call him "the imam of the verifying scholars, shaykh al-Islam". {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.3 p. 4} {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.5 p. 2}

## Method
Al-Shawkani gives the hadiths of each chapter of the base text, then explains their wording, opening each lemma with "his words", and quotes the base text's author with "the author said". {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.2 p. 11} {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.2 p. 14} The book follows the books and chapters of fiqh, as the volume tables show:
- Vol. 2: avoiding impurities and the places of prayer; the description of prayer; what invalidates prayer and what is disliked or permitted in it. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.2 p. 3}
- Vol. 3: congregational prayer; the imamate and the qualities of imams; the traveller's prayer; combining two prayers; Friday; the two Eids; eclipses; then the funeral chapters — washing the dead, the shroud, the funeral prayer, carrying the bier, burial and the rulings on graves. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.3 p. 5-9}
- Vol. 5: the book of sales, opening with what may and may not be sold. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.5 p. 2}

## The edition in the library
An old printing in volumes, with [[person/siddiq-hasan-khan|Siddiq Hasan al-Qannawji]]'s [[work/awn-al-bari|'Awn al-Bari li-Hall Adillat al-Bukhari]] in the margin and, at the end of each volume, a list of corrections to the printing of 'Awn al-Bari; the library holds vols. 2, 3 and 5, and their imprint was not read. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.2 p. 3} {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.3 p. 4}
