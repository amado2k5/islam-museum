## Summary

A question about Jordan Lotto, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is a new form of the charity lottery and is forbidden gambling. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883}

## The question

The questioner asked for the sharia ruling on Jordan Lotto. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883}

## The answer

The Department answered that the Lotto is in reality a new format of the so-called charity lottery, and that the idea behind it is to revive the Jordanian charity lottery. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883} It referred to its fatwa no. 1303, which concluded that the charity lottery is forbidden because it rests on gambling and is part of maysir, and that spending its proceeds on charity and the poor does not make it lawful, since the end does not justify the means. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883} It said a ticket buyer buys it hoping for gain and ends up either winning or losing, and concluded that the Lotto is a form of gambling, since a person pays money and is left between gain and loss, which is forbidden in the sharia. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 3883, dated 30 May 2024. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3883} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
