## Overview
"Al-Filaha al-Kabir wal-Saghir" (The Large and Small Agriculture) by Ibn Wahshiyya al-Kaldani, Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Ali ibn al-Mukhtar al-Kasbadani, a Sufi from Qasin. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 433}

Ibn al-Nadim lists among his books on magic and talismans: "Tard al-Shayatin" (Driving Away the Devils), "Al-Sihr al-Kabir" (The Great Magic), "Al-Sihr al-Saghir" (The Small Magic), "Asrar al-Kawakib" (The Secrets of the Stars), "Al-Asnam" (The Idols) and "Al-Qarabin" (The Offerings). His companion Abu Talib al-Zayyat transmitted these books from him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 433}
