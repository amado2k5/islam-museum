## Overview
The novel by Elias Khoury (1998): the story of Yunis, a Palestinian fighter, and his wife Nahila in the cave of 'Bab al-Shams' between two mountains, narrating the Nakba through the language of love and memory. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of the greatest Arabic novels about Palestine; it became a film in 2004, and its title became the name of a village of resistance founded by Palestinians in 2013. {cite:wikidata}
