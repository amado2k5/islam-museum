## Summary

*The Canonization of al-Bukhari and Muslim: The Formation and Function of the Sunni Hadith Canon* is a book by Jonathan A. C. Brown, published by Brill in Leiden and Boston in 2007 in 431 pages, as volume 69 of the series Islamic History and Civilization. {cite:openlibrary-canonization-bukhari-muslim-2007} {cite:archive-org-canonization-bukhari-muslim-scan}

## Publication and editions

It has two parts: the first opens with an introduction on earlier scholarship on the Sahihayn and on treating them as a canon, followed by a chapter on the study of canons and canonization; the second, 'The Canon and the Needs of the Community', treats the Sahihayn as a measure of authenticity and an authoritative reference. {cite:archive-org-canonization-bukhari-muslim-scan} Chapter one opens with the Seljuq vizier Nizam al-Mulk in 465/1072–3, when he heard of a scholar who held an authoritative copy of al-Bukhari's Sahih, and sets this in the context of his effort to foster common Sunni ground. {cite:archive-org-canonization-bukhari-muslim-scan}
