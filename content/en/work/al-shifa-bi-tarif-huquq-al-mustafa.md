## Overview
"Al-Shifa bi-Ta'rif Huquq al-Mustafa" (The Healing by Knowing the Rights of the Chosen One), a book by the judge Iyad on the rights of the Prophet and his qualities. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.619}

Ibn al-Imad reports in "Shadharat al-Dhahab" that no one had come before it with a book like it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.619}
