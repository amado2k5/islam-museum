## Summary

*Binyat al-'Aql al-'Arabi* (The Structure of Arab Reason) is the second part of the Moroccan thinker Mohammed Abed al-Jabri's series *Naqd al-'Aql al-'Arabi* (Critique of Arab Reason). Its subtitle is 'an analytical and critical study of the systems of knowledge in Arab culture', and it was published by the Centre for Arab Unity Studies in Beirut. {cite:archive-org-binyat-al-aql-al-arabi-scan}
