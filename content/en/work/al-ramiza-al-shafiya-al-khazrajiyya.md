## Summary
al-Ramiza al-Shafiya (al-Khazrajiyya) is a book on prosody (arud and qawafi), by [[person/diya-al-din-al-khazraji|Diya al-Din al-Khazraji]]. The file gives the author's death as 626 or 627 AH; this is the date of the author's death, not of the book's composition.
