## Summary
An account Ibn Ishaq transmitted from al-Zuhri, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdullah ibn Ka'b, from his father Abdullah, from Ka'b ibn Malik, on his staying behind from Tabuk and his repentance. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175-181}

## The isnad
Ibn Ishaq said: al-Zuhri Muhammad ibn Muslim ibn Shihab mentioned, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdullah ibn Ka'b ibn Malik, that his father [[person/abdullah-ibn-kaab-ibn-malik|Abdullah]] — who had guided his father when his sight was lost — said: I heard my father Ka'b ibn Malik telling his story. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175}

## The content
It begins with [[person/kaab-ibn-malik|Ka'b]]'s account of staying behind and ends with his vow of truthfulness, covering [[event/the-three-left-behind|the three left behind]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175-181}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text is Ka'b's first-person speech; it is not copied here but summarised with reference to the page.
- In the isnad is the link al-Zuhri from Abd al-Rahman (p. 175).
