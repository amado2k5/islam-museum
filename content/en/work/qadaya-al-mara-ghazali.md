## Summary

*Qadaya al-Mar'a bayna al-Taqalid al-Rakida wa-l-Wafida* (Women's Issues between Stagnant and Imported Traditions) is a book by Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali. The edition the catalogue records is the second, published by Dar al-Shuruq in Cairo in 1990 in 222 pages; no readable catalogue gave the year of its first edition.
