## Summary
*Al-Jami' al-Saghir min Hadith al-Bashir al-Nadhir* is [[person/al-suyuti|Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti]]'s collection of short hadiths. In his preface, as [[person/yusuf-al-nabhani|al-Nabhani]] quotes it, he says he put thousands of the Prophet's sayings into it, kept to the brief ones, arranged them alphabetically by the opening of each hadith and what follows, and gave it this name because it was excerpted from his large *Jam' al-Jawami'*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 34-35} Al-Nabhani reports that al-Suyuti wrote at its end that he finished it in 907 AH, and that it was widely received and much commented upon. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 35-36}

## Sigla and supplement
Al-Suyuti marks the compilers of each hadith with letters, among them *kh* for al-Bukhari, *m* for Muslim, *q* for both, *4* for the four Sunan, *hm* for Ahmad, *k* for al-Hakim, *tb* for al-Tabarani's large Mu'jam, *hb* for al-Bayhaqi's Shu'ab al-Iman and *kht* for al-Khatib. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 35} He then wrote a supplement, *Ziyadat al-Jami'*, with the same sigla and order, and al-Nabhani merged the two in [[work/al-fath-al-kabir|al-Fath al-Kabir]]. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 36} [[person/abd-al-rauf-al-manawi|'Abd al-Ra'uf al-Munawi]] commented on it in [[work/fayd-al-qadir|Fayd al-Qadir]]. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 14}

## Assessments
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Suyuti in his preface**: he says he took great care over the citations and "kept it free of what a forger or liar alone transmitted". {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 35}
- **Sunni: al-Munawi**: holds that this claim is "mostly true, or only claimed", that al-Suyuti included many weak hadiths, and that the sigla for sound, good and weak in some copies cannot be trusted because of copyists' corruptions. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 21} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 27-28}
- **Sunni: al-Albani**: holds that it contains thousands of weak and objectionable hadiths and hundreds of fabricated ones and that its sigla cannot be relied on, explaining that al-Suyuti gathered but could not sift; he therefore divided al-Fath al-Kabir into sound and weak. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 13} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 28} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 32}
:::

Al-Albani also finds its alphabetical order not strictly kept, giving examples from the chapter on "kana". {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 12-13}
