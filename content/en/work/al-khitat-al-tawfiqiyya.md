## Summary
Al-Khitat al-Tawfiqiyya al-Jadida — Ali Pasha Mubarak's (1823–1893) twenty-part topographical survey of Egypt, modeled on al-Maqrizi's Khitat. {cite:al-aalam}

## Details
Al-Zirikli's entry describes Ali Pasha Mubarak as a self-made scholar-historian and minister: born at Birnbal in Daqahliyya, he learned Arabic and some sciences, traveled with the Egyptian mission to Paris in 1260 AH to study fortification, explosives and military movements, returned to rise through the army to the rank of amir alay, and took part in the Russo-Turkish war of 1270. {cite:al-aalam}

He was then made director of the Egyptian awqaf with education added to his charge, founding many schools — the Egyptian National Library in Cairo among his lasting works — headed the Public Works ministry in 1297, resigned with his cabinet colleagues after the Urabi affair, and last held the education portfolio in 1305; he died in Cairo. His works include al-Khitat al-Tawfiqiyya in twenty parts, following al-Maqrizi's plan, and a novel called Alam al-Din containing religious and social studies. {cite:al-aalam}
