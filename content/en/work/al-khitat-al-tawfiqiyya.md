## Overview
"Al-Khitat al-Tawfiqiyya al-Jadida" (The New Tawfiqi Plans), in twenty volumes, by Ali Pasha Mubarak (1823–1893 CE). It follows the model of al-Maqrizi's "Khitat". {cite:al-aalam tarjama}
