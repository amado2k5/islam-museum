## Overview
A book by al-Wahidi, the Qur'an commentator, who died in 468 AH. Hajji Khalifa describes it as the most famous work written on the occasions of revelation. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 381-382}

He reports that the first to write in this field was Ali ibn al-Madini, and that Ibn al-Jawzi, Ibn Hajar and al-Suyuti also wrote works on it. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 381-382}
