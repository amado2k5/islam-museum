# Generoso Corano (Sheikh Othman)

## Summary
*Generoso Corano* ("The Generous Qur'an") is an Italian rendering of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by Sheikh Othman, first published in 2015 and in a second edition in 2020; it is one of the newest Muslim-authored translations into Italian, a language with more than ten complete Qur'an translations. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano}

## The translator
Little is known of Sheikh Othman beyond his affiliation with the Islamic Cultural Center of Fondi, in Lazio, and his translations of other Arabic texts, including the whole of *Riyad al-Salihin* as *I Giardini dei Giusti*. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano}

## Editions
The first edition's introduction dates the completion of the translation to 19 September 2015. A second edition, prepared in 2018, was published in 2020 by the Conveying Islamic Message Society of Alexandria, with a letter in Arabic from al-Azhar's General Department for Research, Writing and Translation dated 2010 at its end. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano} Mykhaylo Yakubovych notes that the letter predates the translation and thinks it was probably taken from another Italian translation project. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano} There is also a pocket edition for missionary use; the reviewer's copy came from the free-literature shelf of the [[building/masjid-ibn-tulun|Mosque of Ibn Tulun]] in Cairo. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano}

## What it contains
The translation follows the Hafs reading, in modern Italian without archaisms; it is mostly literal with some footnotes, and gives the Arabic names of prophets and nations in parentheses. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano} The edition mirrors the printed mushaf in its 604 pages, its prostration marks and its division into quarters, and keeps some refrains in their place on the page. {cite:gloqur-generoso-corano}
