## Summary
"Nisa' Khalidat" (Immortal Women) is a television programme presented by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]. The Assabile archive keeps thirty video files of it, most between twenty-one and thirty-six minutes long. He speaks alone to camera, and each episode opens and closes with the same sung nasheed addressed to "my mother". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep09 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:25:30} He says he chose the subject at a time when people argue about women's rights and some attack Islam over what it gave women, and that he wanted to answer through stories and historical models rather than theory. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:01:00}

Each episode tells the life of one woman, sometimes two, from the women around the prophets Abraham, Moses and Solomon to the Prophet's wives and daughters, women Companions, and women of the Umayyad and Abbasid courts. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:02:30} He closes each story with the lessons he draws from it, often addressed to the young women of today. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep06 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep19 00:28:30} He says he drew on fifty or sixty books and chose variety rather than covering only the Companions and the ascetics. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:02:00}

The dates, numbers and judgements in the programme are his own statements, and the stories are his telling of them; they are attributed to him wherever they are used.

## The archive copy
The Assabile list gives no titles, only "Episode 01" to "Episode 30". The episodes are numbered here as in the archive list. Two points about the copy:
- The files of episodes 8 and 9 carry the same recordings as episodes 6 and 7. The file of episode 7 stops in the middle of the lecture; episode 9 is the complete copy. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep08 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep09 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep09 00:29:00}
- The archive's file names for episodes 28 to 30 carry the numbers 29, 30 and 31 ("the last"), and no file is numbered 28. At least one lecture is missing: episode 27 promises a story of Umm al-Banin with the poet Abd Allah ibn Qays al-Ruqayyat, and episode 28 refers back to a Zubayda story already told, but neither is in the archive. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep28 00:02:00}

## The episodes
**Episode 1, introduction.** He sets out the aim of the programme and its plan, then tells short stories of what Islam changed: an Ansari widow whose daughters were left nothing and the verse on women's share of inheritance, the custom of inheriting a widow, a woman's cry to the caliph al-Mu'tasim at Amorium, and a woman who asked the Prophet about women's share of merit. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep01 00:24:30}

**Episode 2, [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]].** Her emigrations, the separation of her family at the Hijra, her marriage to the Prophet, and her counsel at al-Hudaybiya. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep02 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep02 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep02 00:21:00}

**Episode 5, Moses' wife, and Asiya's end.** The daughter of the old man of Midian who came to Moses walking shyly and advised her father to hire "the strong, the trustworthy", which he calls a golden rule of management; the marriage for eight or ten years of work; her keeping the family together in Sinai. ## What is not written here
Left out of these pages:
- his statements about women in other religions and societies before Islam, and his sweeping comparisons with the West and with Western women;
- his remarks on Jews, Christians and present-day groups, and the curses he adds to historical figures;
- his asides on present-day politics and rulers;
- the legal rulings he states;
- the tafsir readings he gives on disputed verses;
- passages on conflicts among the Companions and on al-Husayn and Karbala;
- passages the transcription engine repeated;
- names and numbers that came out garbled or that contradict one another.

Each is recorded, with its place in the recording, in the lane's progress file.
