# The Majestic Qur'an (Musharraf Hussain)

## Summary
*The Majestic Qur'an* is an English rendering of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by Musharraf Hussain, published by Invitation Publishing in Nottingham in 2018, one of the recent translations that put plain English first. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-hussain}

## The translator
Hussain is an al-Azhar graduate and a longstanding educationist in the UK; Sohaib Saeed notes that he belongs to the Barelwi strand of Sunni Muslims and states his adherence to orthodox commentators. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-hussain}

## What it contains
The front matter carries an endorsement from Egypt's Dar al-Ifta and others; each sura has an introduction, the text has topical headings, footnotes are used sparingly, and the margins carry questions for reflection. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-hussain}

## The revision
After publication, criticisms, mostly on points of doctrine, were discussed at a meeting of Barelwi imams, whose statement said the work would be withdrawn pending corrections; the translator published an errata list and said the revised work was fully in line with the creed of al-Nasafi, the standard Sunni creed. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-hussain} Saeed judges some corrections improvements and others awkward, and sees the case as showing how translation and doctrine interact: an individual project, corrected at the prompting of a committee of British imams. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-hussain}
