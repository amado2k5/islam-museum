## Summary

A question about unintended oaths (yamin al-laghw), in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that whoever swears by God without intending an oath or resolving to bind himself owes nothing, while one should not swear often. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4346}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on unintended oaths. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4346}

## The answer

The Department answered that whoever swears by God without intending an oath and without resolving to bind himself to it owes nothing, citing [[passage/quran-2-225|Surat al-Baqara 2:225]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4346} It said a Muslim should not swear often, so that his tongue does not become used to it, citing [[passage/quran-2-224|Surat al-Baqara 2:224]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4346}
