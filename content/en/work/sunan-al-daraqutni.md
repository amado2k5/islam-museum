## Summary
*Al-Sunan* is the book of [[person/al-daraqutni|Abu al-Hasan 'Ali b. 'Umar al-Daraqutni]] of Baghdad. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 7} [[person/al-khatib-al-baghdadi-ahmad|Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi]] holds that the Sunan shows its author's concern with fiqh, since only one already versed in juristic disagreement could compile what it contains. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 8} In this edition the book opens with hadiths on water, among them Ibn 'Umar's report "If the water is two qullas, nothing renders it impure", with several chains. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 12-13}

## Transmitters and copies
Shams al-Haqq al-'Azimabadi devotes a chapter to those who transmitted the Sunan from al-Daraqutni: {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9}
- Abu Bakr Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Malik b. Bishran. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9}
- Abu Tahir Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahim. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9}
- Abu Bakr Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Ghalib al-Barqani (336–425 AH), of whom al-Khatib said he saw none among his teachers more reliable. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9-10}
- The judge Abu al-Tayyib Tahir b. 'Abd Allah al-Tabari, and the sharif Abu al-Husayn Muhammad b. 'Ali b. al-Muhtadi bi-llah, the last to narrate from al-Daraqutni (d. 465 AH). {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 10}

He quotes [[person/shah-abd-al-aziz-dihlawi|'Abd al-'Aziz b. Wali Allah al-Dihlawi]] in *Bustan al-Muhaddithin* that the three copies — Ibn Bishran's, Abu Tahir's and al-Barqani's — differ in order, in narrators' lineages and in some wording, and that each contains all the hadiths except Abu Tahir's, which lacks the "Book of Racing". {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 10}

## Commentary on it
Abu al-Tayyib Muhammad [[person/shams-al-haqq-al-azimabadi|Shams al-Haqq al-'Azimabadi]] wrote notes on it, [[work/al-taliq-al-mughni|al-Ta'liq al-Mughni 'ala al-Daraqutni]], and gives his chain to the Sunan through his teacher Muhammad Nadhir Husayn al-Dihlawi, from Muhammad Ishaq al-Dihlawi, from 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Wali Allah, from his father [[person/shah-waliullah-al-dahlawi|Wali Allah al-Dihlawi]] — seventeen men to al-Daraqutni. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 10-11}
