## Overview
A geographical encyclopaedia by Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.11-14}

## Its divisions
The first part is on the earth: its size, its climes, its seas, the direction of prayer, its roads, and the kingdoms of its regions. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.11-14} The second part is on its inhabitants: the groups of peoples, religions and religious communities, and the states before Islam and under Islam. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.11-14}
