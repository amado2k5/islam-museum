## Overview
A one-volume book of biographies and stories, which tells in a simplified narrative style the lives of the women around the Prophet. It was prepared by Muhammad Ali al-Qutb, Muhammad Umar al-Da'uq and Ahmad Abd al-Jawad al-Dumi. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul}

## Its sections
The book has five sections, in this order:
- The mothers of the Messenger (pp. 11–34): Amina, Halima, Umm Ayman and Fatima bint Asad.
- The wives of the Messenger (pp. 35–178): his thirteen wives and Maria the Copt.
- Women who lived in the time of the Messenger (pp. 179–388): twenty-five Companions.
- The daughters of the Messenger (pp. 389–438): his four daughters.
- The granddaughters of the Messenger (pp. 439–461): three of his granddaughters.

Then comes the table of contents (pp. 462–464). {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul}
