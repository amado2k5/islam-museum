## Overview
A book of hadith by [[person/al-baghawi-al-husayn-ibn-muhammad|al-Baghawi]]. Ibn al-Imad counts it among his useful works, together with "Ma'alim al-Tanzil", "Al-Jam bayn al-Sahihayn", "Al-Tahdhib" and "Sharh al-Sunna". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.529}
