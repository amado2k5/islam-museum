## Summary

A question about paying fasting compensation (fidya) to a poor granddaughter, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permitted if she is poor and not already provided for by those obliged to maintain her. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4253}

## The question

The questioner asked whether she may pay her fidya for the Ramadan fasts she did not keep to her son's poor daughter. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4253}

## The answer

The Department answered that a woman may pay her fidya for the Ramadan fasts she did not keep to her son's daughter. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4253} It set as conditions that the girl be poor and not already provided for by those who are obliged to maintain her. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4253}
