## Summary
Jawharat al-Tawhid by Ibrahim al-Laqani al-Maliki (d. 1041 AH) — a didactic poem of a hundred and forty-four rajaz lines gathering the foundations of belief in the divine, the prophetic and the reports; the Azhari school's aqida text {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

المؤلف: اللقاني مالكي أزهري من علماء القرن الحادي عشر.

## For younger visitors
Jawharat al-Tawhid is the aqida poem memorised in Azhari circles.
