## Overview
"Kitab al-Mujtaba" (The Book of the Chosen), a book by Ibn Duraid devoted to gathering the words of the Prophet and a summary of his sayings. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 594}

Al-Mas'udi mentions it among those who collected the words of the Prophet in their books, together with Abu Ishaq al-Zajjaji, Abu Abdullah Nafutuya, and Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Mawsili. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 594}
