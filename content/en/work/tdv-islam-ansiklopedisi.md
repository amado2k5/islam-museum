# TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi

## Summary

The TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi (Türkiye Diyanet Foundation Encyclopedia of Islam) is a Turkish-language encyclopedia published by the Türkiye Diyanet Foundation. It covers the Islamic sciences, the terms of Islamic culture and civilisation, the figures of the Muslim world in religion, scholarship, politics, art and literature, and the books that shaped Muslim life. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## Entries

Its scholarly boards set its headwords after surveying some 500 basic sources: 15,441 entries, rising to 16,915 when the sub-sections written by specialists of different fields for some entries are added, with 6,539 cross-reference entries. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr} More than two thousand authors wrote for it. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## History

Preparations began in 1983. The first volume appeared in 1988 after twelve months' work, the second in nine months and the third in seven, and the encyclopedia was completed with volume 44 in 2013, after twenty-five years. Two supplementary volumes followed in 2016, bringing it to forty-six. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## Online

The content of every volume and the list of entries are available on the encyclopedia's website, with search, copying and PDF download. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## Recognition

It received the Turkish Presidential Grand Prize for Culture and Arts in 2014. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## An earlier encyclopedia

It is a different work from the Turkish [[work/islam-ansiklopedisi|İslâm Ansiklopedisi]] begun in 1940; Wikidata records the two as different works. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}

## Today

The site is at islamansiklopedisi.org.tr; the encyclopedia has Wikipedia articles in sixteen languages and the Wikidata item Q21527102. {cite:wikipedia-tdv-islam-ansiklopedisi-tr}
