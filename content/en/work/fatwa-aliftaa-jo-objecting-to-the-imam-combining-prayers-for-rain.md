## Summary

A question about objecting to the imam combining prayers because of rain, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that worshippers may not object to the imam over this, whether to demand combining or to oppose it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4240}

## The question

The questioner asked about objecting to a mosque's imam over combining prayers because of rain. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4240}

## The answer

The Department answered that none of the worshippers may object to the imam in the mosque, whether the objection is to demand that he combine the prayers or that he not combine them. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4240} It explained that the imam is presumed to have the knowledge of the Sharia that qualifies him to decide this matter. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4240}
