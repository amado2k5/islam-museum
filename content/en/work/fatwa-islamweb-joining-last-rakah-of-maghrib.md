## Summary

A question about someone who prayed the last rak'a of the sunset prayer with the imam, which the IslamWeb fatwa centre answered according to where he joined. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling for someone who prayed the last rak'a of the sunset prayer with the imam. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136}

## The answer

The centre answered that if he caught the bowing, he has caught one rak'a and has two left. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136} If he joined the imam after the third bowing, in prostration or the final tashahhud, he completes what he joined with the imam and then prays three rak'as after the imam's closing salutation. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136} It said the different shape of the made-up prayer does no harm, and that he gains the reward of congregation, though less than one who joined from the start. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136}

## About this record

The IslamWeb fatwa centre published this fatwa as no. 136, dated 8 August 1999. {cite:islamweb fatwa 136} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
