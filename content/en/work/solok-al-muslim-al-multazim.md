## Overview
«Suluk al-Muslim al-Multazim» (the conduct of the committed Muslim) is a lesson series by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] preserved in the Assabile archive's video-lesson section; the episodes say they were broadcast on the Amjad channel. The series page lists twenty-six lessons and the first thirteen have been treated in this work so far. The lecturer opens by explaining that "conduct" means character, that "Muslim" is the one who submits, yields and is led by God's commands, and that "the committed" is one who takes Islam as a complete way of life, not a part of it. He then divides Muslim character into three: one's appearance, one's speech and one's deeds — treating appearance and personal cleanliness in the early episodes, then the Muslim's speech in the daily remembrances and guarding the tongue, then deeds in eating, dress, greeting, kindness to parents living and dead, neighbours' rights and the ethics of driving and the road, then turning to the Muslim's conduct with God: faith as certitude, utterance and deed, the two conditions of righteous work — sincerity to God and conformity with the revealed law — and the distinction between the knowledge of the seen and the knowledge of the unseen, on which he builds faith in what is unseen. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim} What is reported here is the lecturer's account as the machine transcript shows it; the literal transcript text is in many places unintelligible or meaningless repetition, so the views and rulings he mentions are told as his narration rather than as independently verified facts, and any line that cannot be read clearly is marked as needing a source.

## Episodes

**Episode 1: Islam is the religion of character.** The lecturer opens the series by explaining its title: conduct means character, the acts a person performs, and he says the people of his time have been absorbed in politics until character declined, and that he wants a return to the fundamentals — the Qur'an and the Sunna — citing God's word in al-Furqan "they bring you no example but We bring you the truth and a better explanation", [[passage/quran-25-33|al-Furqan, verse 33]], and the close of Surat Qaf "so remind by the Qur'an whoever fears My threat", [[passage/quran-50-45|Qaf, verse 45]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:02:30} He rules that Islam concentrated on character, mentioning that God praised His Prophet for his great character in Surat al-Qalam, [[passage/quran-68-4|al-Qalam, verse 4]], and that A'isha described his character as the Qur'an; he reviews the hadiths he relates on the merit of good character: the heaviest thing placed in the believer's scale on the Day of Resurrection is good character, the thing that most admits people to Paradise is God-consciousness and good character, and the thing that most admits them to the Fire is the mouth and the private parts. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:10:00} He relates from Abu Dawud that the Prophet guarantees a house in the middle of Paradise for the one who abandons argument while in the right, and a higher one for the one who abandons lying even in jest, pausing on the difference between lying and a joke: a joke known as such is not a lie because the hearer knows it did not happen, and he illustrates with the hadith of the man who erred from joy at finding his camel and said the opposite of what he meant, while the Prophet said God rejoices more at His servant's repentance than that man at his camel. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:14:30} He transmits from Abdallah ibn al-Mubarak that the chain of transmission belongs to the religion and without it anyone could say anything, and from Ibn Sirin that this knowledge is religion so look to whom you take your religion from; he defines good character in Ibn al-Mubarak's words as a cheerful face, freely doing good and withholding harm, and he mentions what is related in Sahih Muslim about the player of nardashir (backgammon), and in Ahmad's Musnad that whoever plays backgammon has disobeyed God and His Messenger. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep01 00:19:30} It is recorded that between roughly the fifteenth and sixteenth minutes the lecturer digresses into a joke about a former American president that has no bearing on the lesson; it is excluded entirely and nothing of it is written, as is his remark about the fundamentalists near the episode's opening.

**Episode 2: the Muslim and the committed.** The lecturer explains that the Muslim is the one who submits, yields, is obedient and led by the commands of God, that the deity is the one worshipped, the worshipped is the one obeyed, and obeyed means hearing His word in doing the lawful and leaving the unlawful; he cites "obey God and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you", [[passage/quran-4-59|al-Nisa', verse 59]], making obedience to those in authority mean the scholars or the rulers who judge by God's law. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:08:00} He cites Surat al-Anbiya' "Say: it is revealed to me that your god is one God — will you then submit?", [[passage/quran-21-108|al-Anbiya', verse 108]], and Surat al-Hajj "your god is one God, so to Him submit, and give glad tidings to the humble", [[passage/quran-22-34|al-Hajj, verse 34]], and the story of Abraham with his son when they submitted and he laid him on his brow, [[passage/quran-37-103|al-Saffat, verse 103]], and Surat al-Furqan on the one who takes his desire as his god, [[passage/quran-25-43|al-Furqan, verse 43]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:10:30} He mentions the sect of the Murji'a who content themselves with words without deeds, and transmits from Hassan al-Banna in the "Twenty Principles" that they do not declare a Muslim who pronounces the two testimonies, acts on them and performs the obligations an unbeliever unless he denies what is known of the religion by necessity or commits what can only bear unbelief — presenting this as the lecturer's report of its author, not as the museum's ruling. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:13:30} He then explains "the committed" as one who takes Islam as a complete way of life, citing Surat al-An'am "Say: my prayer and my sacrifice and my living and my dying are for God, Lord of the worlds; no partner has He", [[passage/quran-6-162|al-An'am, verse 162]], and Surat al-Baqarah "O you who believe, enter into submission all together", [[passage/quran-2-208|al-Baqarah, verse 208]], and he gives examples from people's weddings, funerals and inheritances that the religion is taken whole, not in parts. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep02 00:19:00}

**Episode 3: your appearance — looks and cleanliness.** The lecturer announces that Muslim character is three things: one's appearance, one's speech and one's deeds, and devotes this episode to appearance. He begins with the hair, citing what is related from the Prophet "whoever has hair, let him honour it", the prohibition of qaza' — shaving the sides of the head — and God's word "and for them is the like of what is upon them, with fairness", [[passage/quran-2-228|al-Baqarah, verse 228]], meaning the man adorns himself for his wife as he loves her to adorn herself for him. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:03:30} He then speaks of the eyebrows and plucking them, of filing the teeth for beauty, and of tattooing, mentioning what is related in Bukhari and Muslim cursing the woman who plucks and the one who is plucked, those who file teeth for beauty and alter God's creation, and the tattooers; he cites the Sahih Muslim hadith about two kinds of the people of the Fire, among them women dressed yet naked, swaying and causing others to sway, their heads like the humps of lean camels, and he argues for the veil with Surat al-Nur "let them draw their coverings over their bosoms", [[passage/quran-24-31|al-Nur, verse 31]], and Surat al-Ahzab "let them draw their wraps over themselves; that is more likely that they be known and not molested", [[passage/quran-33-59|al-Ahzab, verse 59]], and Surat al-A'raf on Satan stripping the two parents of their clothing, [[passage/quran-7-27|al-A'raf, verse 27]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:08:30} He rules that every address to men is an address to women unless the law specifies otherwise, citing Surat al-Tahrim on Mary daughter of Imran "and she was of the devout" in the masculine form by the precedence of the male gender, [[passage/quran-66-12|al-Tahrim, verse 12]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:11:30} He then details personal cleanliness: the tooth-stick and keeping the mouth clean, with the mention that whoever eats garlic or onion should stay away from the mosques; trimming the nails and concealing human waste; plucking the armpit and shaving the pubic hair; washing the feet after removing shoes — citing Surat al-Baqarah "God loves the repentant and loves those who purify themselves", [[passage/quran-2-222|al-Baqarah, verse 222]], and Surat al-Tawbah "and He loves those who purify themselves", [[passage/quran-9-108|al-Tawbah, verse 108]] — and he closes with dress by Surat al-A'raf "children of Adam, We have sent down on you clothing to cover your nakedness and finery; and the clothing of God-consciousness — that is best", [[passage/quran-7-26|al-A'raf, verse 26]], making covering the nakedness the essential and finery the perfection. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep03 00:19:30}

**Episode 4: your speech — the tongue and the daily remembrances.** The lecturer opens that speech is a person's title proving his commitment, and that the tongue is God's blessing created for speech and taste, citing Surat al-Balad "did We not give him two eyes, a tongue and two lips?", [[passage/quran-90-9|al-Balad, verse 9]], and Surat al-Rahman "taught him clear speech", [[passage/quran-55-4|al-Rahman, verse 4]], and Surat al-Rum on the difference of tongues and colours, [[passage/quran-30-22|al-Rum, verse 22]], then guarding the tongue with Surat Qaf "he utters no word but there is with him a ready watcher", [[passage/quran-50-18|Qaf, verse 18]], and the hadith of Mu'adh that people are flung into the Fire on their faces for nothing but the harvest of their tongues. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:04:30} He then reviews the Muslim's remembrances through his whole day: naming God when sleeping, the waking prayer, seeking refuge from a bad dream, the prayer for entering the privy and leaving it — noting that "I ask Your forgiveness" on leaving is a weak hadith applied for the merit of deeds on its conditions — the name of God and the meal prayer, the prayer for dressing and looking in the mirror, the prayers for leaving home, riding and travelling; he cites Surat al-Zumar on God taking souls at their death and the one that did not die in its sleep, [[passage/quran-39-42|al-Zumar, verse 42]], and Surat al-Ahzab "you have in God's Messenger a good example", [[passage/quran-33-21|al-Ahzab, verse 21]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:17:30} He distinguishes praise with the tongue from thanks with deeds, citing Surat Saba' "work, family of David, in gratitude; and few of My servants are grateful", [[passage/quran-34-13|Saba', verse 13]], and mentions that whispering comes from Satan to grieve the believers, [[passage/quran-58-10|al-Mujadalah, verse 10]], and for condolence he cites Surat al-Baqarah "we are God's and to Him we return", [[passage/quran-2-156|al-Baqarah, verse 156]], and Surat al-Qasas "everything perishes but His face", [[passage/quran-28-88|al-Qasas, verse 88]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:23:30} He closes by classifying prayer upon the Prophet in three kinds: a prayer from God upon the servant which is mercy, a prayer from the servant to God which is supplication, and a prayer from the angels to God which is seeking forgiveness — citing Surat al-Ahzab "God and His angels send blessings on the Prophet", [[passage/quran-33-56|al-Ahzab, verse 56]], and Surat Ghafir on the bearers of the Throne seeking forgiveness for the believers, [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir, verse 7]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep04 00:25:30}

**Episode 5: your deeds — faith is certitude, utterance and deed.** The lecturer rules that the third sign of commitment is deeds and that a Muslim is known by his work; he objects to calling the non-Muslim "the other", saying the Qur'an names "among you an unbeliever and among you a believer" in Surat al-Taghabun, [[passage/quran-64-2|al-Taghabun, verse 2]], and Surat al-Kafirun which ends "for you your religion and for me mine", [[passage/quran-109-6|al-Kafirun, verse 6]], and "whoever seeks other than Islam as religion, it will not be accepted of him", [[passage/quran-3-85|Al Imran, verse 85]] — distinguishing coexistence from acceptance, related as the lecturer's view. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:03:00} He then breaks faith into three: certitude in the heart, utterance on the tongue, and confirmation by deeds, citing Surat al-Baqarah "righteousness is not turning your faces toward the east or the west; rather, the righteous is the one who believes in God and the Last Day and the angels and the Book and the prophets" then giving wealth, establishing prayer, giving alms, keeping pledges and the patient in hardship and adversity and in time of conflict, [[passage/quran-2-177|al-Baqarah, verse 177]], and he pauses on "the patient" being in the accusative, reading it as the accusative of specification rather than coordination with "those who keep pledges", citing Surat al-Zumar "the patient will be given their wage without measure", [[passage/quran-39-10|al-Zumar, verse 10]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:12:30} He then begins detailing deeds: sleeping on the right side with the palm under the cheek; entering the privy on the left foot, cleansing with the left hand and leaving on the right foot; eating with the right hand with three fingers — relating from the Prophet "boy, name God, eat with your right and eat of what is near you", and that whoever eats or drinks with the left — Satan eats and drinks with his left; he tells the story of an Iraqi guest at a table about the right and left hands in eating. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep05 00:18:00}

**Episode 6: your deeds — the etiquette of eating, dressing, sneezing, greeting and parents.** The lecturer resumes deeds: the Muslim eats and drinks with the right because Satan eats and drinks with the left, citing Surat Fatir "Satan is an enemy to you, so take him as an enemy", [[passage/quran-35-6|Fatir, verse 6]], and the hadith "boy, name God, eat with your right and eat of what is near you", and he tells the joke of the man who threw the pebbles veiled so Satan would not know him, ruling that a Muslim must not imitate Satan. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:03:30} Then the etiquette of dressing on the right first and removing on the left, and the etiquette of sneezing: saying "praise be to God", answering with "may God have mercy on you" and "may God guide you and set your state aright", and he mentions that a settled heart is happiness and peace of mind. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:07:00} Then the etiquette of greeting: peace be upon you and God's mercy and blessings, mentioning the hadith of the three men who came to the Prophet and earned ten, twenty and thirty rewards according to the greeting, and "shall I not tell you of something which, if you do it, you will love one another? Spread the greeting of peace among you", preferring the Islamic greeting over foreign morning and evening greetings. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:13:00} Then deeds toward parents: kissing their hands and fingers — he relates that he did this with his parents and found his youngest son kissing his fingers — and he criticises what he calls legalistic foolishness in dishonouring parents while kissing mothers' feet as in some acted scenes; he mentions that saying "fie" to one's parents is among the grave sins, citing Surat al-Isra' "do not say to them 'fie', nor rebuke them, but speak to them a noble word", [[passage/quran-17-23|al-Isra', verse 23]], and "lower to them the wing of humility from mercy and say: my Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me small", [[passage/quran-17-24|al-Isra', verse 24]]; he defines marital betrayal as a betrayal of God before the wife, citing Surat al-Anfal, [[passage/quran-8-27|al-Anfal, verse 27]], and he criticises films and songs that spread indecency, citing Surat al-Nur "those who love that indecency be spread among the believers will have a painful punishment", [[passage/quran-24-19|al-Nur, verse 19]], and Surat al-Buruj "those who persecuted the believing men and women and then did not repent will have the punishment of Hell and the punishment of the burning", [[passage/quran-85-10|al-Buruj, verse 10]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep06 00:19:30}

**Episode 7: kindness to parents, living and dead.** The lecturer completes the Muslim's deeds toward his parents, confining kindness to them while alive in three duties: honouring them — exceeding respect in calling them by the dearest names, kissing their hands, not walking ahead of them except in darkness, not standing above them and not eating before them; obeying them; and spending on them if they are in need — citing Surat al-Baqarah "and speak to people kindly", [[passage/quran-2-83|al-Baqarah, verse 83]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep07 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep07 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep07 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim solok-al-muslim-al-multazim/ep07 00:06:00} He details the limits of obedience in three cases where they are not obeyed: polytheism and unbelief, citing Surat Luqman "if they strive to make you associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them, but keep them company in the world with fairness", [[passage/quran-31-15|Luqman, verse 15]], with Surat al-Ahqaf on kindness to parents, [[passage/quran-46-15|al-Ahqaf, verse 15]]; sin, citing what is related "there is no obedience to a creature in disobedience to the Creator"; and hardship — their commanding what harms their child, such as imposing divorce on his wife or preventing their daughter from marrying whom she accepts, citing Surat al-Baqarah "do not prevent them from marrying their husbands if they agree among themselves with fairness", [[passage/quran-2-232|al-Baqarah, verse 232]]; **Episode 8: the neighbour and the ethics of the road and driving.** The lecturer classifies neighbours in three: a neighbour with one right — the right of neighbourhood even if not a Muslim; a neighbour with two rights — the Muslim, who has the rights of neighbourhood and of Islam;
