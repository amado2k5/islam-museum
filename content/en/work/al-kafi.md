## Overview
"Al-Kafi fi Ilm al-Din" (The Sufficient in the Science of Religion) by Muhammad ibn Yaqub al-Kulayni ([[person/al-kulayni|al-Kulayni]]), one of the most comprehensive hadith collections of the Shi'a. {cite:duha-islam p. 850, 891}

## What Ahmad Amin says
Ahmad Amin says that it contains sixteen thousand hadiths, which he divided into sound, good, weak and very weak, as the Sunni scholars did. It is in three parts: the first on the principles, and the second and third on the branches of the law. Its compilation took twenty years. {cite:duha-islam p. 850, 891}
