## Overview
Verses composed by [[person/malik-ibn-awf|Malik ibn Awf al-Nasri]] when he came to the Prophet as a Muslim at al-Ji'rana. The Prophet returned to him his family and his property and gave him a hundred camels. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Context
They were composed in Dhu al-Qa'da 8 AH at al-Ji'rana (a derived date; its status in the data is that of a transmission). They are part of [[event/distribution-of-hunayn-spoils|the distribution of the spoils of Hunayn]], and they mention [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Opening
Its opening line, according to what the data records, reads: "I have not seen nor heard of anyone like him among all the people, like Muhammad." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
