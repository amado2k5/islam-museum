## Summary
Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi ("In the courts of the hadith qudsi") is a filmed television programme of which the Assabile archive keeps thirty episodes, each about half an hour long. Every episode opens with the reading of a hadith, naming the collection that records it and the Companion who reports it. The programme's presenter then explains it in a commentary that brings together verses, the views of the commentators and science films on the cosmos, the body and animals, and segments are inserted in which [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi]] speaks on the same hadith. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:10:00} The first episode begins by defining the [[concept/hadith-qudsi|hadith qudsi]] and its place between the Qur'an and the prophetic hadith. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:09:30}

Some of the hadiths the programme presents in this series are prophetic hadiths that the Prophet does not attribute to his Lord, such as the hadith of Adam's creation, the hadith of the stages of the embryo, the hadith of the tooth-stick and the hadith of the rabbi. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep21 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep29 00:00:00} After its opening, episode 16 repeats most of the material of episode 15, and episode 19 repeats segments of episodes 17 and 18; only the first occurrence is used here. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep19 00:10:30} What al-Sha'rawi says on this page is his view, attributed to him; the scientific readings of verses given by the presenter are the programme's view, attributed to it, not a judgement of the museum.

## The episodes
**Episode 1, the witness of the limbs.** A hadith in which the Prophet laughs at a servant's dispute with his Lord on the Day of Judgement; the presenter says Muslim records it from Anas ibn Malik. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:00:00} Al-Sha'rawi explains that what the Prophet conveyed from his Lord is of two parts: the Qur'an, by which God challenged the Arabs in the art they excelled in, and another speech that carries no challenge and is not recited in worship, the hadith qudsi. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:07:30} He then says that the limbs are not held to account but only bear witness, and that punishment falls on the conscious self, not on the limb. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep01 00:24:00} The material is under [[concept/yawm-al-qiyama|the Day of Resurrection]].

**Episode 2, the gift of the senses.** A hadith in which God lists to a servant on the Day of Judgement what He gave him; the presenter attributes it to al-Tirmidhi from Abu Hurayra and Abu Sa'id al-Khudri. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep02 00:00:00} The presenter explains hearing, sight and the hearts (af'ida), holding that the af'ida are not senses but the sum of the faculties of awareness and reason. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep02 00:04:30} Al-Sha'rawi says that whatever a person learns he is tested in, and that the chain of teachers goes back to a first teacher who can only be God. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep02 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep02 00:26:00}

**Episode 3, rain and the stars.** The hadith of al-Hudaybiya on those who woke believing in God and those who credited the rain to a star, from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:00:00} The presenter distinguishes ghayth from matar in Qur'anic usage, and al-Sha'rawi says that the stars have a function assigned by their Creator and no power of their own. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:21:00} The material is under [[concept/anwa|al-anwa']].

**Episode 4, the challenge to create an atom.** A hadith in which God challenges whoever would create as He creates to create an atom or a grain, as the presenter reads it from al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:00:00} The presenter reviews the history of atomic theory. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:03:30} Al-Sha'rawi says that creating is bringing into being from nothing, that God did not withhold the word from people, attributing it to others and describing Himself as the finest of those who create, but that what a person makes is inert, without life, and does not reproduce. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:07:00}

**Episode 5, the whisper "who created God?".** The hadith of people asking questions until they ask who created God, from Muslim from Anas, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/al-waswasa|whispering]].

**Episode 6, Adam's creation and the greeting of peace.** The hadith of Adam created sixty cubits tall and greeting the angels, from al-Bukhari through Ma'mar from Hammam from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:00:00} The material is under [[person/adam|Adam]].

**Episode 7, the stages of the embryo.** Ibn Mas'ud's hadith on the stages and the angel who writes provision, term, deeds and wretchedness or happiness, from al-Bukhari, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/janin|the unborn child]] and [[concept/qadar|divine decree]].

**Episode 8, the mountains and what is stronger.** The hadith of the swaying earth steadied by the mountains and the angels' question about what is stronger, from al-Tirmidhi from Anas, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep08 00:00:00} Al-Sha'rawi argues from the verses on mountains that the earth moves, with the image of a train passenger who sees the telegraph poles run past. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep08 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep08 00:17:30} He says the charity of the right hand hidden from the left is stronger than the wind, because its giver has overcome the wish to be seen and has spared the dignity of the one who takes. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep08 00:27:30}

**Episode 9, the sick and the angels.** The hadith of the two angels sent to a sick person, from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from 'Ata' ibn Yasar, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep09 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/ibtila|trial]].

**Episode 10, the prophet and the ant colony.** The hadith of the prophet stung by an ant who had the colony burned, from al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep10 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/ghadab|anger]] and [[person/sulayman|Sulayman]].

**Episode 11, Yunus son of Matta.** The hadith forbidding a servant to say he is better than Yunus son of Matta, from al-Bukhari from Ibn Abbas, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep11 00:00:00} The material is under [[person/yunus|Yunus]].

**Episode 12, Musa and the righteous servant.** The hadith of Musa and the righteous servant at the meeting of the two seas, from al-Bukhari through Sa'id ibn Jubayr from Ibn Abbas from Ubayy ibn Ka'b, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep12 00:00:00} The material is under [[person/al-khidr|al-Khidr]]. Al-Sha'rawi says that Surat al-Kahf holds "caves" of hidden matters: the first is the people of the cave, and another is the owner of the two gardens who thought his wealth came from his own skill, which he calls "the cave of causes". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep12 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep12 00:17:00}

**Episode 13, al-Kawthar.** The hadith of the revelation of Surat al-Kawthar after the Prophet dozed, from al-Nasa'i from Anas ibn Malik, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep13 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep13 00:18:00} The material is under [[concept/al-kawthar|al-Kawthar]].

**Episode 14, the two breaths of the Fire.** The hadith of the Fire's complaint to its Lord and His permitting it two breaths, from al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep14 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/jahannam|Jahannam]].

**Episodes 15 and 16, the slaughter of death.** The hadith of death brought on the Day of Resurrection and slaughtered, from the authors of the Sunan from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep15 00:00:00} Episode 16 opens with Suhayb's hadith on the people of the Garden seeing their Lord, then repeats the material of episode 15. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep16 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/yawm-al-qiyama|the Day of Resurrection]] and [[concept/ruyat-allah|the vision of God]].

**Episodes 17, 18 and 19, the covenant.** The hadith of the covenant taken from Adam's back at Na'man, from Ahmad through Sa'id ibn Jubayr from Ibn Abbas, as the presenter states, and the hadith challenging whoever would create as God creates to make a gnat or a grain. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/dhurriyyat-adam-fi-zahrih|Adam's descendants latent in him]]. In episode 18 al-Sha'rawi says that the image-maker does not create but imitates God's creation, and that a picture gives the form of a thing without its effects. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:14:00} He relates that on the desert road to Alexandria he saw the sand scour the number plates off the cars, as proof that every meeting of two things changes each of them. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:12:00}

**Episode 20, the people of 'Arafat.** The hadith of God boasting to the angels of the people of 'Arafat, from Ahmad from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep20 00:00:00} The material is under [[place/arafat|'Arafat]].

**Episode 21, the tooth-stick and the descent.** The hadith of the tooth-stick at every ablution and the delaying of the night prayer, followed by the hadith of the descent in the last part of the night, from Ahmad, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep21 00:00:00} The material is under [[practice/miswak|the miswak]].

**Episode 22, fasting set apart for God.** The hadith in which God sets fasting apart as His alone among a person's deeds, from Ahmad from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep22 00:00:00} The material is under [[practice/sawm-ramadan|fasting]].

**Episode 23, loving the meeting with God.** The hadith matching a servant's love of meeting his Lord with God's love of meeting him, from al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra and in another version from 'Ubada ibn al-Samit, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/hubb-allah|love for God]].

**Episode 24, the earth grasped and the heavens folded.** The hadith of God grasping the earth on the Day of Resurrection and folding the heavens, from al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep24 00:00:00} The presenter describes the solar system and the galaxy. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep24 00:19:30} The material is under [[concept/yawm-al-qiyama|the Day of Resurrection]].

**Episode 25, the womb and kinship.** The hadith of the name of kinship (al-rahim) derived from the name al-Rahman, from Ahmad from Abu Hurayra and from al-Tirmidhi from 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep25 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/arham-fiqh|kinship ties]].

**Episode 26, the loss of sight.** The hadith of one whom God tries with the loss of his sight and who is patient, whom He compensates with the Garden, from al-Bukhari from Anas, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep26 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/sabr|patience]].

**Episode 27, the address to the son of Adam.** The hadith of God's promise to forgive whoever calls on Him and hopes in Him, however many his sins, so long as he associates nothing with Him, from al-Tirmidhi from Anas, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep27 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/istighfar|seeking forgiveness]] and [[person/adam|Adam]].

**Episode 28, those who love one another for God.** The hadith of those who love one another for God's majesty and whom He shades on the Day of Resurrection, from Muslim from Abu Hurayra, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/hubb-allah|love for God]].

**Episode 29, the rabbi and the fingers.** The hadith of the rabbi who said God would hold the heavens and earths on fingers, at which the Prophet laughed and recited the verse of al-Zumar, from Muslim from Ibn Mas'ud, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep29 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/ruyat-allah|the vision of God]].

**Episode 30, the angel and the drop.** The hadith of the angel who enters upon the drop after forty or forty-five nights, from Muslim from Abu al-Tufayl from Hudhayfa ibn Asid, as the presenter states. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep30 00:00:00} The material is under [[concept/janin|the unborn child]] and [[concept/qadar|divine decree]].

## Evolution and scientific reading
In episodes 4 and 6 both al-Sha'rawi and the programme's presenter reject the view that the human being evolved from another kind, and in many episodes the presenter offers scientific readings of verses and hadiths in the manner of [[concept/al-ijaz-al-ilmi|the "scientific miracle" (al-i'jaz al-'ilmi)]]. These are views attributed to those who hold them:

:::perspectives
- **Sunni scholar's view, the speaker's own (al-Sha'rawi)**: he says everything was created in its own form, that creation has ranks in which each link stays in its place and does not turn into another, and that since it was said that man descends from an ape no ape has come near to being a man. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep04 00:13:30}
- **The programme's view (the presenter)**: he says that genetics, founded by Mendel, shows the genetic code to be fixed, so each kind began as itself, and that the evolution he does not deny concerns outward traits such as height and colour, not the kind. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:15:00}
