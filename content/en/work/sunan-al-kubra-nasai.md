## Summary
*Sunan al-Kubra* is the larger collection of Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb al-Nasa'i (d. 303 AH) ([[person/al-nasai|his biography]]). It is distinct from the smaller *Sunan al-Sughra*, known as *al-Mujtaba*. {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 1}

## Volume two
The second volume of the Mu'assasat al-Risala edition opens with the book of night prayer and voluntary prayer during the day (scan page 111). It continues with a chapter on angels writing down the worshippers in the mosque in order of arrival (page 270), the book of rain prayer (page 315), the book of the eclipse of the sun and moon (page 331), and the book of shortening the prayer on a journey (page 357). Near its end are the book of Friday (page 502) and the book of the festival prayers (page 503). {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 111}, {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 315}, {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 357}, {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 503}

## The full scan
A full scan of 5,886 pages of the same edition is held, edited by Hasan Abd al-Mun'im Shalabi under Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut's supervision. Its volume number was not read, and no chapters are drawn from it here. {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-set p. 1}

## A note on the source
The second volume appears twice in the drive folder with identical content, so it is recorded as one source. Page numbers are scan pages. {cite:sunan-al-kubra-nasai-arna-vol2 p. 1}
