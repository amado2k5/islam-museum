## Summary
*Khulasat al-Wafa bi-akhbar dar al-Mustafa* is the third and shortest of [[person/al-samhudi|al-Samhudi]]'s books on the history of Medina; the editor of [[work/wafa-al-wafa|Wafa' al-Wafa]] describes it as an abridgement "of about half of Wafa' al-Wafa, gathering its aims and improving its description". {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.7} The editor says that this book and Wafa' al-Wafa had been printed several times before his edition, without proper division or vocalization, and he cites the Khulasa when correcting readings of Wafa' al-Wafa. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.7} {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.152} He lists it among the author's works in his biography. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.9}
