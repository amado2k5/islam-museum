## Summary

*Le Sceau des saints: prophétie et sainteté dans la doctrine d'Ibn Arabî* (The Seal of the Saints) is a French book by Michel Chodkiewicz, published by Gallimard in Paris in 1986 in 231 pages in its Bibliothèque des sciences humaines. The publisher's description says that Ibn 'Arabi's hagiology is the first comprehensive and coherent formulation in Islamic thought of a doctrine of sainthood, defining its nature and function and typing the saints by the notion of prophetic inheritance. It adds that the book ends by describing the two phases of the initiatory journey: the ascent to God and the descent to creatures. {cite:openlibrary-le-sceau-des-saints-1986} {cite:archive-org-le-sceau-des-saints-text}
