## Summary

*al-Fiqh al-Manhaji 'ala Madhhab al-Imam al-Shafi'i* is a teaching series in Shafi'i fiqh written jointly by Mustafa al-Khin, Mustafa al-Bugha and Ali al-Sharbaji and published by Dar al-Qalam in Damascus; its editions include a fourth edition of 1413/1992 in eight parts. {cite:shamela-al-fiqh-al-manhaji}
