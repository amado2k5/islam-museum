## Summary

*Hadith: Muhammad's Legacy in the Medieval and Modern World* is a book by Jonathan A. C. Brown, published by Oneworld in Oxford in 2009. {cite:openlibrary-hadith-muhammads-legacy-brown} The catalogue describes it as a wide-ranging introduction to the collection and criticism of hadith; it notes that much of Islamic law comes from hadith rather than from the Qur'an alone, and that the late writing-down of many reports has faced scholars with complex questions of historical authenticity. {cite:openlibrary-hadith-muhammads-legacy-brown}

## Publication and editions

It also examines the controversy over the place of hadith in modern Islam, and includes a glossary, an extensive bibliography and explanatory diagrams. {cite:openlibrary-hadith-muhammads-legacy-brown}
