## Summary

*Adab al-Ikhtilaf fi al-Islam* is a book by Taha Jabir al-Alwani on the causes and etiquette of disagreement among scholars. Its English translation, *The Ethics of Disagreement in Islam*, was published by the International Institute of Islamic Thought in Herndon, Virginia, in 1992 in 158 pages. {cite:openlibrary-ethics-of-disagreement-1992}

## Publication and editions

A note on terms in the English translation explains that adab, rendered 'ethics' in the title, also carries the senses of norms, discipline, etiquette and good manners, which is why some Arabic terms are kept and a glossary is added. Chapter one, 'The Malaise of Discord', opens by describing the ills of the contemporary Muslim world, discord and strife among them. {cite:archive-org-ethics-of-disagreement-alwani-scan}
