## Overview
A book on horses by [[person/ibn-durayd|Ibn Duraid]]. Al-Mas'udi reports from it in "Muruj al-Dhahab" that Solomon son of David provided a horse to some people of Azd to hunt on, and that it was named "Zad al-Rakib" (Provision of the Rider). {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.529}
