## Overview
"Al-Ahkam al-Sultaniyya" (The Principles of Government), a volume by the Imam Shaykh Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Mawardi, the Shafi'i, who died in 450 AH. He arranged it in twenty chapters, as Hajji Khalifa reports. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.213}
