## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with twenty-nine episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} Each episode lasts about ten minutes and opens with a song beginning 'that soul and what proportioned it'; at its start and end the programme is announced as sponsored by the Sheikh Ali bin Abdullah Al-Juffali Charitable Foundation and the Jeddah office of the Saudi Scientific Society for the Holy Qur'an and its Sciences (Tibyan). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:01:00} In the first episode the Sheikh says he will take in each episode one observation or heading on the Qur'an's training of the human soul, and how the Qur'an formed the souls of the Companions. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:06:30}

## Episode 1: the human soul and the perfect soul

He opens by calling the soul the most complex thing God created, lists [[concept/maratib-al-nafs|the degrees of the soul]], makes the Prophet's soul the measure of souls, announces a heading on [[concept/tarbiya|the Qur'an's training]] of the soul in each episode, and tells the story of [[person/abu-dharr-al-ghifari|Abu Dharr]], the Bedouin and Surat al-Zalzala. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep01 00:07:30}

## Episode 2: trust in God

[[concept/tawakkul|Trust in God]] and the taking of means, the man with the umbrella at the prayer for rain, the three prayers at the doctor's, and [[person/hajar|Hajar]] and Zamzam. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep02 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep02 00:06:30}

## Episode 3: supplication

God's nearness to the one who calls, praying in ease and hardship, [[person/nuh|Nuh]]'s perseverance, an unfulfilled prayer as an answer, and the manners of [[practice/dua|supplication]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep03 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep03 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep03 00:07:00}

## Episode 4: short measure in dealings

He reads the opening of Surat al-Mutaffifin and extends [[concept/tatfif-short-measure|short measure]] to dealings between siblings and spouses, to [[concept/kafaa|suitability]] in marriage and fairness among children, citing [[person/yaqub|Ya'qub]] and Yusuf. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:00}

## Episode 5: affection at home and dialogue

The evaporation of affection and mercy from homes, [[concept/al-muasharah-bil-maruf|living together in kindness]], [[concept/fann-al-hiwar|the art of dialogue and listening]], and social intelligence. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep05 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep05 00:07:00}

## Episode 6: it may be that you dislike a thing

[[concept/al-minha-fi-al-mihna|The gift within the trial]]: Musa's mother, [[person/musa|Musa]], Yusuf, Hajar and [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep06 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep06 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep06 00:08:00}
