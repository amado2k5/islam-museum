## Overview
"Al-Maqamat" of al-Hariri of Basra, which follows the model of [[work/maqamat-badi-al-zaman|the Maqamat of Badi al-Zaman]]. Among them is the twenty-first maqama, the Razi maqama, which describes the assembly of the preacher Ibn Sam'un. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.135}
