## Summary

*Al-Asas fi al-Tafsir* is a complete commentary on the Qur'an by Sa'id Hawwa (d. 1409/1989). {cite:shamela-al-asas-fi-al-tafsir} It belongs to a series by the author entitled *al-Asas fi al-Manhaj*; after the publisher's foreword it opens with an introduction to that series and then the introduction to the commentary. {cite:shamela-al-asas-fi-al-tafsir}

After al-Fatiha it divides the suras into four sections, *al-tiwal* (the long suras), *al-mi'un* (those of about a hundred verses), *al-mathani* and *al-mufassal*, following the traditional grouping of the suras by length. {cite:shamela-al-asas-fi-al-tafsir} It closes with a section entitled “A last word on the overall Qur'anic context” and then the conclusion of the commentary. {cite:shamela-al-asas-fi-al-tafsir}

## Publication and editions

Its first edition was published by Dar al-Salam in Cairo in 1405/1985, in eleven continuously paginated volumes; al-Maktaba al-Shamila presents it with the pagination of the printed edition. {cite:shamela-al-asas-fi-al-tafsir}
