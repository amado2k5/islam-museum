## Summary
The Tawrāt in Islamic conception is the book revealed to Mūsā; Aḥmad Amīn calls it "the most important source of Jewish culture," noted in the Qur'an and described as a book of guidance and light {cite:duha-islam p.298}.

## The Torah in Qur'an and hadith
Ahmad Amin notes that the Qur'an spells out certain rulings that occur in the Torah, such as retaliation, and that the hadiths referred to it and mentioned some of its rulings {cite:duha-islam p.299}. In his chapter on Jewish culture he reviews the Torah's divisions, order and dating among modern scholars, setting it beside the other revealed books in the Islamic imagination {cite:duha-islam p.303}.

## For young visitors
The Torah is the scripture of the People of the Book revealed to the prophet Musa; the Qur'an affirms it was sent down and speaks of changes that entered it.
