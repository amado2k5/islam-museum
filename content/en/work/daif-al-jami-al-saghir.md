## Summary
*Da'if al-Jami' al-Saghir wa-Ziyadatuh (al-Fath al-Kabir)* is the second part of [[person/al-albani-muhammad-nasir-al-din|al-Albani]]'s grading of [[work/al-fath-al-kabir|al-Fath al-Kabir]], given over to what he judges unusable as proof — weak, very weak and fabricated — the first part being [[work/sahih-al-jami-al-saghir|Sahih al-Jami' al-Saghir wa-Ziyadatuh]]. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 32} It is published by al-Maktab al-Islami. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 5}

## Count and printing
The publisher [[person/zuhayr-al-shawish|Zuhayr al-Shawish]] gives a provisional count of 6,479 hadiths, 1,075 of them from the *Ziyada*. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 10} In the new edition he kept al-Albani's introduction only at the head of Sahih al-Jami' and referred to it in Da'if al-Jami', since it would otherwise be repeated. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 7} In the introduction to the Sahih, al-Albani refers to Da'if al-Jami' by number for his examples of hadiths in al-Jami' al-Saghir that he judges weak. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 14-15}
