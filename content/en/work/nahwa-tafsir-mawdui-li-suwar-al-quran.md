## Summary

*Nahwa Tafsir Mawdu'i li-Suwar al-Qur'an al-Karim* (Towards a Thematic Commentary on the Suras of the Qur'an) is a book by Muhammad al-Ghazali, first published by Dar al-Shuruq in Egypt in 1416/1995 in 552 pages. {cite:andalus-tamil-translations-nadwi-ghazali-2024} The imprint page of a scanned copy lists later printings by the same publisher, up to a fourth in 2000. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali} Shah al-Hamid al-Umari translated it into Tamil. {cite:andalus-tamil-translations-nadwi-ghazali-2024}

## Publication and editions

In his preface al-Ghazali says that his aim is a thematic commentary on every sura, and he distinguishes it from the verse-by-verse commentary that explains a verse or group of verses through its words, constructions and rulings. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali} He attends to the unity of theme within each sura and to the links between its opening and its close, and says that in this he followed Muhammad Abd Allah Draz's treatment of Surat al-Baqara in *al-Naba' al-'Azim*. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali} He explains that, for brevity, he selects the verses that bring out the outline of each sura, and that this commentary does not replace verse-by-verse commentary but completes it. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali}

He notes a second sense of thematic commentary, tracing a single idea across the whole Qur'an, and says he had given examples of it in his books *al-Mahawir al-Khamsa li-l-Qur'an al-Karim* and *Nazarat fi al-Qur'an*. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali} He writes that he had lived with the Qur'an since childhood and memorised it before the age of ten, and that he wrote this commentary in his eighth decade; the book opens with Surat al-Fatiha. {cite:archive-org-nahwa-tafsir-mawdui-ghazali}
