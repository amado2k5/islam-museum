## Summary
*Majmu' al-Fatawa* ("The Collected Fatwas") is the great gathering of the fatwas, answers, treatises and "rules" written by Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Abd al-Halim [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] of Harran (d. 728 AH). It was collected and arranged by Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Qasim, helped by his son Muhammad, as the title page of the King Fahd Complex printing states. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 1} It is not a book Ibn Taymiyya composed in this order: it is the compiler's arrangement of scattered texts under subject headings, many of which open with "He was asked" followed by "He answered", or "Shaykh al-Islam said" followed by "Section". {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 149} {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 553}

## Editions present
- **The King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Qur'an, Medina**, printed by order of King Fahd ibn Abd al-Aziz under the supervision of the Ministry of Islamic Affairs, Endowments, Da'wa and Guidance, in 1425 AH / 2004; its volume 10 has 792 pages. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 1} {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 2}
- **Dar al-Wafa', Mansura**, with Obeikan of Riyadh, under the title *Majmu'at al-Fatawa*, edited with references for its hadiths by Amir al-Jazzar and Anwar al-Baz; first edition 1418 AH / 1997. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 6} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 7} Its volume 37 records a second edition in 1422 AH / 2001 and a third in 1426 AH / 2005. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v37 p. 4}

## The volumes read here
### Volume 7: Faith
Titled "al-Iman" in the Dar al-Wafa' printing, it holds two books: *Kitab al-Iman al-Kabir* and *Kitab al-Iman al-Awsat*. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 9} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 423} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 429} The first opens by stating that faith (iman) and submission (islam) together comprise the whole religion, that the dispute over their reality has gone on since the Kharijites came out, and that the author will set out what can be learned from the words of God and His Messenger before mentioning people's disagreements. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v7 p. 11} See [[work/kitab-al-iman-ibn-taymiyya|Kitab al-Iman]].

### Volume 8: Destiny
Titled "Kitab al-Qadar", it opens with a section on the power of the Lord. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 437} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 439} Its sections, according to its table of contents, include contentment with God's decree, arguing from prior destiny, the three kinds of Qadariyya, the debate of Adam and Moses, human capacity, the wisdom behind God's acts, whether reason judges acts good or bad, and the agreement of the early generations that human beings have will, power and action. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 762} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 763} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 764} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 765} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v8 p. 766} See [[question/did-adam-argue-from-destiny-to-excuse-his-sin|Did Adam argue from destiny to excuse his sin?]]

### Volume 10: The science of the spiritual path
Titled "Kitab 'Ilm al-Suluk". {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 3} It opens with [[work/al-tuhfa-al-iraqiyya|al-Tuhfa al-'Iraqiyya fi al-A'mal al-Qalbiyya]] (pp. 5–90), followed by *Amrad al-Qulub wa-Shifa'uha* ("The Diseases of the Hearts and Their Cure", pp. 91–149), then the treatise [[work/al-ubudiyya|al-'Ubudiyya]] (from p. 149), then answers on the prayer of Dhu al-Nun and the protection of prophets from sin, and sections on the [[concept/fana-sufi|annihilation (fana')]] found in the words of the Sufis, on the names of the ascetics and the term "Sufi", on lawful and innovated forms of worship including the Sufi retreats, and on [[question/is-seclusion-better-than-company-for-the-seeker|seclusion and company]]. {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 770} {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 772} {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 773} It also contains a commentary on sayings from Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani's *Futuh al-Ghayb*, a discussion of al-Junayd's dispute with some of his companions over [[concept/al-jam-wal-farq|gathering and separation]], an answer on *Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din* and *Qut al-Qulub*, and *al-Wasiyya al-Sughra* ("The Short Counsel"). {cite:majmu-fatawa-qasim-v10 p. 773}

### Volume 37: The general indexes
A detailed subject index of the whole collection, referring to volume and page, beginning with the topics of the *Introduction to the Principles of Tafsir and the Qur'anic Sciences*, such as revelation and its kinds and the names of the Qur'an. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v37 p. 5} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v37 p. 7} At its end is an index of the books Ibn Taymiyya names in the collection, among them al-Qushayri's *Risala*, al-Jilani's *Futuh al-Ghayb*, Ibn Arabi's *al-Futuhat al-Makkiyya*, Ibn Sina's *al-Shifa'*, the *Hikmat al-Ishraq* of al-Suhrawardi "al-Maqtul", the *Rasa'il Ikhwan al-Safa'*, and al-Khallal's *Kitab al-Sunna* and *Kitab al-'Ilm*. {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v37 p. 604} {cite:majmu-fatawa-wafa-v37 p. 605}
