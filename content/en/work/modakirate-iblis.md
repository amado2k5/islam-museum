## Summary
A Ramadan programme by Omar Abdelkafy titled "Mudhakkirat Iblis" (Memoirs of Iblis), imagining Iblis writing his memoirs as a way to show his approaches and tricks, in long episodes of about half an hour. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 1: the sound heart and the sick heart in the first memo

He says the memoirs fall in two parts: a report on what he has done to Muslims and the children of Adam, and a future plan to lead them astray in the east and the west. See [[passage/quran-7-16|al-A'raf, verse 16]] and [[passage/quran-7-17|al-A'raf, verse 17]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:00}
- He says Iblis blocks the most important tributary of faith, which is [[concept/shukr|gratitude]], and feeds the sources of despair, hopelessness and distance from God, and that the aim of presenting the memoirs is for us to train together to know his tricks and gaps, and how he whispers and adorns. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:30}
- He says Iblis neither sleeps nor naps, and that it is written for him to live until God inherits the earth and all on it, when his soul is taken like the soul of every creature. - He raises a question: if the rebellious devils are bound, why do we see evil in our societies? He answers that the devils of the jinn are bound, but the devils of mankind are scattered everywhere, adorning evil, setting barriers before good deeds and opening the doors of disobedience, and that globalisation and the internet have made the world like a small alley. See [[concept/shayatin-al-ins-wal-jinn|devils of mankind and jinn]]. This is his picture. - He says Iblis's aim is to keep Muslims off the straight path, to make them despair of God's mercy or feel secure from His plan, to make the forbidden lawful and the lawful forbidden, and to distract them from their real livelihood and their return, saying that we are between a livelihood and a return. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:07:30}

- He describes the sound heart as yearning for good as a child yearns for its mother's breast, so that it seizes every chance: a gathering of knowledge, charity, two night rak'as, reading the Qur'an, attending a funeral, sharing in weddings with nothing that angers God, and visiting the sick, feeling that God is there. ## Episode 2: making obedience heavy and disobedience light
- He presents the day's memo, saying the true believer finds the world sweet only through the love of God and obedience to Him, and the hereafter sweet only through seeing God, and that Iblis therefore writes that he will make the servant hate obedience and love until obedience becomes heavy and bitter and disobedience light and sweet. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:01:30}
- He cites the heaviness of prayer except for the humble. See [[passage/quran-2-45|al-Baqara, verse 45]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:02:00}
- He says faith has a sweetness tasted by whoever is content with God as Lord, mentioning this by sense without a chain, compares it to the scent of a rose that is felt and not described, and gives a line of verse without naming its author. (needs_source: the hadith on tasting the flavour of faith is quoted by sense and the line of verse is not ascribed to anyone) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:03:30}
- He lists as signs of the sweetness of faith an open chest, optimism, hope and contentment with what God has apportioned even when means are tight, and says true wealth and poverty are what is shown before God, and that the rich man is the one called at the scales as blessed with a happiness after which he is never wretched. (needs_source: the call at the scales is quoted without a chain) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:04:30}
- He says Iblis does not want the servant to be in need of his Lord but wants him self-sufficient through his money, status, post, tribe and passport, mocking the idea of a passport for entering Paradise, and says the whole world is not worth a mosquito's wing to God and that lawful money is a blessing for the righteous servant who uses it in obedience. (needs_source: an allusion to the hadith of the mosquito's wing, with no wording or chain) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:05:30}
- He gives the example of a king who honours someone who served his country with a medal or certificate, and says God looks at whether the servant served His religion through the day and honours him by letting him stand before Him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:07:00}
- He relates that Iblis ties three knots on the head of the sleeper, so that when he rises one is undone, when he makes ablution the second, and when he prays the third, leaving him with an open chest; to the other the devil says wait, delays him through the call to prayer and the second call until sleep overcomes him, and he wakes with the sun risen and a tight chest. This is by sense and without a chain. (needs_source: the hadith of the devil's knots and the remark on the devil urinating in his ears are quoted by sense without a chain) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:08:30}
- He says today's memo is making obedience heavy and disobedience light and sweet, and gives the example of a television serial for which one says five more minutes before praying, so that the time leaks away, adding that time is like a sword, which cuts you if you do not cut it, without attributing it. (needs_source: the saying that time is like a sword has no speaker) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:09:30}
