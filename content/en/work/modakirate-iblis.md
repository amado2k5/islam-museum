## Summary
A Ramadan programme by Omar Abdelkafy titled "Mudhakkirat Iblis" (Memoirs of Iblis), imagining Iblis writing his memoirs as a way to show his approaches and tricks, in long episodes of about half an hour. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 2: making obedience heavy and disobedience light
- He presents the day's memo, saying the true believer finds the world sweet only through the love of God and obedience to Him, and the hereafter sweet only through seeing God, and that Iblis therefore writes that he will make the servant hate obedience and love until obedience becomes heavy and bitter and disobedience light and sweet. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep02 00:01:30}

## Episode 3: the barrier between the servant and his Lord
- He presents a new memo in which Iblis writes that he wants to set a barrier between the believer and his Lord and keep him from knowing Him. He gives the example of a child taken to a puppet theatre who thinks the puppets move by themselves, while the adult knows there are threads and someone moving them, and says the people of this world look at causes and the people of religion look at the One who causes. See [[concept/al-asbab-wal-qudra|causes and power]]. This is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:02:30}
- He distinguishes thinking from reflection, saying everyone thinks and animals have perception and instincts, while the one who reflects stops with care at the realities of matters, and he points to the Qur'an calling on people to reflect on it. See [[concept/tadabbur-al-quran|reflecting on the Qur'an]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep03 00:03:30}
- He says that if a servant knows everything and does not know his Lord he knows nothing, and calls such a person ignorant even if he has won Nobel prizes.
