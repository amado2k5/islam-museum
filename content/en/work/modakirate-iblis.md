## Summary
A Ramadan programme by Omar Abdelkafy titled "Mudhakkirat Iblis" (Memoirs of Iblis), imagining Iblis writing his memoirs as a way to show his approaches and tricks, in long episodes of about half an hour. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 1: the sound heart and the sick heart in the first memo
- He opens a new programme whose idea is to imagine [[person/iblis|Iblis]] on leave for the whole of [[concept/ramadan-month|the month of Ramadan]] because he is bound together with his chief helpers among the rebellious jinn, so that he sits down to write his memoirs. He says the memoirs fall in two parts: a report on what he has done to Muslims and the children of Adam, and a future plan to lead them astray in the east and the west. See [[passage/quran-7-16|al-A'raf, verse 16]] and [[passage/quran-7-17|al-A'raf, verse 17]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:00}
- He says Iblis blocks the most important tributary of faith, which is [[concept/shukr|gratitude]], and feeds the sources of despair, hopelessness and distance from God, and that the aim of presenting the memoirs is for us to train together to know his tricks and gaps, and how he whispers and adorns. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:03:30}
- He says Iblis neither sleeps nor naps, and that it is written for him to live until God inherits the earth and all on it, when his soul is taken like the soul of every creature. This is his statement. (needs_source: the claim about how long Iblis lives is without a source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:04:00}
- He raises a question: if the rebellious devils are bound, why do we see evil in our societies? He answers that the devils of the jinn are bound, but the devils of mankind are scattered everywhere, adorning evil, setting barriers before good deeds and opening the doors of disobedience, and that globalisation and the internet have made the world like a small alley. See [[concept/shayatin-al-ins-wal-jinn|devils of mankind and jinn]]. This is his picture. - He imagines that some devils of mankind have surpassed their master, and relates a saying put on the tongue of one of them, that he was one of Iblis's soldiers and then Iblis became one of his. He explains that the name Iblis comes from iblas, meaning distance from God's mercy. - He says Iblis's aim is to keep Muslims off the straight path, to make them despair of God's mercy or feel secure from His plan, to make the forbidden lawful and the lawful forbidden, and to distract them from their real livelihood and their return, saying that we are between a livelihood and a return. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:07:30}
- He presents the first memo: Iblis looks for the gaps through which he can enter the fortress of the heart when it is fortified by remembrance of God. He whispers to those at prayer to take away their humility, reminds them in prayer of things, and makes them doubt the purity of the garment, the place, the body and the ablution, and whether they recited the Fatiha and how many times they bowed and prostrated. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy modakirate-iblis/ep01 00:09:00}

- He says the first thing Iblis wants is to turn the sound heart into a sick one, because the heart is where the Creator looks, and he gives by sense that God does not look at forms but at hearts and deeds, and gives the example of an oil stain on a white garment which its wearer is ashamed of before people. Once the heart is sick, he says, Iblis stays behind it until it dies, so that it sees neither truth as truth nor falsehood as falsehood. - He defines the sound heart as one for which soundness has become a fixed attribute: soundness from servitude to anything but God and soundness from taking any judge other than His Messenger. He ascribes the definition to the scholars. See [[concept/qalb-salim|the sound heart]].
