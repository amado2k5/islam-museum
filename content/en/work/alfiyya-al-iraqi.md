## Overview
"Al-Alfiyya fi Usul al-Hadith" (The Thousand-Line Poem on the Principles of Hadith) by the hadith scholar Zayn al-Din Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Husayn al-Iraqi, who died in 805 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.593}

He summarised Ibn al-Salah's "Ulum al-Hadith" in it and added to it. He finished it at Taiba (Medina) in Jumada al-Akhira 768 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.593}
