## Summary

A question about students using ChatGPT to prepare research, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a student may not have the technology produce the core intellectual effort of a research paper and present it as his own, since that is lying and cheating, but may use it for formal tasks such as gathering information and sources and proofreading. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781}

## The question

The questioner asked about students using ChatGPT, which writes a complete research paper from a prompt, so that a hard-working student ends up on a par with others and the use cannot be detected. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781}

## The answer

The Department answered that ChatGPT is an interactive model based on artificial intelligence, developed by the American company OpenAI and launched in November 2022, which can be used to create content, write articles and help with research; the ruling on using it for research and assignments depends on the kind of effort involved. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781} The original effort that is the point of the work, namely formulating information, drawing conclusions, ordering ideas and discussing open questions, may not be produced with this technology and attributed to oneself to mislead supervisors, since research is a means of assessment, and this earns an undeserved grade and puts the student on a par with those who worked hard. It cited a hadith agreed upon by [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] and [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]] whose meaning is that one who pretends to have what he was not given is like one wearing two garments of falsehood, [[passage/quran-9-119|Surat al-Tawba 9:119]], and a hadith narrated by Muslim whose meaning is that whoever cheats us is not of us, calling this lying, cheating and forgery. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781} As for formal effort, such as using search engines, gathering information, statistics and sources, and proofreading, there is no objection to using any artificial-intelligence tool that gives the research strength and scholarly authenticity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781} The Department advised students and researchers to avoid lying, cheating and forgery, to keep their own personal effort in writing their studies, and to use these technologies in ways pleasing to God. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3781}
