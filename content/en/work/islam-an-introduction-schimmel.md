## Summary

*Islam: An Introduction* is a book by the German scholar Annemarie Schimmel; its English translation was published by State University of New York Press on 14 May 1992 in 166 pages. {cite:google-books-islam-an-introduction-schimmel} The catalogue record says it is a translation of her German *Der Islam*. According to its contents, its chapters treat Arabia before Islam, Muhammad, the expansion of Islam, the Qur'an and its teachings, the tradition, the law, theology and philosophy, the Shia and related sects, mystical Islam and the Sufi brotherhoods, popular piety and the veneration of saints, and modern developments inside Islam. A bibliography is on pages 145–150. {cite:archive-org-islam-an-introduction-schimmel-1992}
