## Summary

*Fi Fiqh al-Awlawiyyat: Dirasa Jadida fi Daw' al-Qur'an wa-l-Sunna* is a book by Yusuf al-Qaradawi on ordering priorities among rulings and deeds, published by Maktabat Wahba in Cairo; its editions include a second edition whose year cannot be read in the scanned copy. {cite:archive-fi-fiqh-al-awlawiyyat-qaradawi}
