## Summary

*Seeing Islam as Others Saw It: A Survey and Evaluation of Christian, Jewish and Zoroastrian Writings on Early Islam* is a book by Robert G. Hoyland, published by the Darwin Press in Princeton in 1997 in 872 pages. {cite:openlibrary-seeing-islam-as-others-saw-it-1997}

## Publication and editions

Its introduction opens with the Byzantine emperor Heraclius' victory over Persia and closes with the caliph al-Mahdi (775–785), the span the book studies. Part I sets out the historical background and the nature of the sources; the next part begins with incidental references to Islam in Greek sources such as Sophronius, patriarch of Jerusalem, and Maximus the Confessor. {cite:archive-org-seeing-islam-as-others-saw-it-scan}
