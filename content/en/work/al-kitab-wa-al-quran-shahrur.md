## Summary

*Al-Kitab wa-l-Qur'an: Qira'a Mu'asira* (The Book and the Qur'an: A Contemporary Reading) is a book by the Syrian engineer and thinker Muhammad Shahrur; the copy read is the al-Ahali edition from Damascus. It begins with a presentation of 'the linguistic method of the book' by Ja'far Dak al-Bab, then the author's introduction and a preliminary chapter on terms. The chapters of its first part, 'The Book and the Qur'an', deal with the Qur'an and the 'seven mathani', prophethood and message, inzal and tanzil (two kinds of sending down), the inimitability and interpretation of the Qur'an, and 'the tree of dhikr'. The second part is titled 'The dialectic of the universe and the human being'. {cite:archive-org-al-kitab-wa-al-quran-shahrur}
