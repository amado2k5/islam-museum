## Summary

*Al-Qur'an wa-l-Sultan: Humum Islamiyya Mu'asira* (The Qur'an and the Sultan: Contemporary Islamic Concerns) is a book by the Egyptian writer Fahmi Huwaydi, first published by Dar al-Shuruq in Beirut and Cairo in 1981 in 244 pages. An uploaded copy is on the Internet Archive, but its machine-read text is mostly unreadable. No readable copy was available, so this article is limited to catalogue data. {cite:openlibrary-al-quran-wa-al-sultan-1981}
