## Summary
Bad' al-Amali in the science of creed, a didactic poem attributed to Ali ibn Uthman al-Ushi al-Farghani al-Hanafi (d. ca. 575 AH) — a hundred and seventy-four rajaz lines summing the headings of Maturidi Hanafi belief; one of the first student mutun {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: ينسب البدء إلى الأوشي الفرغاني وفي نسبته إليه كلام عند المحققين.
