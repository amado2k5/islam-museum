## Overview
"Al-Mabadi wal-Tarakib" (Principles and Compositions), a book to which al-Mas'udi ([[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]]) refers together with his book "Al-Zulaf", to explain how the sun, the moon and the four natures have their effects. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 518}
