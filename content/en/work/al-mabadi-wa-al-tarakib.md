## Summary
Al-Mabadi' wa'l-Tarakib ("The Beginnings and the Combinations") is a work of al-Mas'udi himself, which he cites in Muruj al-Dhahab in the discussions of the influence of the sun and moon and the elements. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 518}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi notes in Muruj al-Dhahab that many people disagreed with the doctrine of the influence of the celestial sphere's form, holding that effects come rather from the humours and the other of the four natures — a matter he says he has clarified in his book entitled al-Zulaf and in Kitab al-Mabadi' wa'l-Tarakib, concerning how the sun and moon exercise influence. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 518} Al-Mabadi' wa'l-Tarakib is thus one of al-Mas'udi's treatises to which he refers the reader for questions of nature, the elements, and the attendant influence of the spheres; his habit in Muruj al-Dhahab is to give the question in brief and refer to his detailed books where he treated it at length. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 518}
