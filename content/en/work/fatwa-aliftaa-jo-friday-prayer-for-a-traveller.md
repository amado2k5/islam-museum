## Summary

A question about friday prayer for a traveller, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is not obligatory on a traveller who leaves home before dawn and does not intend to stay four days or more, but becomes obligatory if he sets out after the dawn call to prayer. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4300}

## The question

The questioner asked about Friday prayer for a traveller and the conditions under which it is lifted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4300}

## The answer

The Department answered that Friday prayer is not obligatory on a traveller, whether the journey is long or short, provided that in the Shafi'i school he leaves his place of residence before dawn and does not intend to stay at his destination four days or more, excluding the days of arrival and departure. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4300} It said that if he sets out after the dawn call to prayer, he must attend Friday prayer in his town or in any town where it is held, quoting this from Bushra al-Karim (1/383), a Shafi'i work, and explained that a short journey is one under 81 km and that the concession begins on leaving the town's built-up area. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4300}
