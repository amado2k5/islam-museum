## Summary

*Islam Between East and West* is a book by Alija Izetbegović, first published in English in 1984 by American Trust Publications in Oak Brook, Illinois, with a second edition in 1989 and a third in 1993. It has two parts. Part I, 'Premises: Consideration about Religion', has chapters on creation and evolution, culture and civilization, the phenomenon of art, morality, culture and history, and drama and utopia. Part II, 'Islam — Bipolar Unity', has chapters on Islam and religion, the Islamic nature of law, ideas and reality, and 'the third way' outside Islam, and ends with a supplement and a table of opposites. {cite:archive-islam-between-east-and-west} {cite:openlibrary-islam-between-east-and-west-1985}
