## Overview
"Amthal al-Quran" (The Parables of the Qur'an) by the Imam Abu al-Hasan Ali al-Mawardi the Shafi'i. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.606}

Hajji Khalifa notes that in the same field there are other books by Abu Abd al-Rahman Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Sulami al-Nisaburi, and by Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr, Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.606}
