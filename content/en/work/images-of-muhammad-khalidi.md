## Summary

*Images of Muhammad: Narratives of the Prophet in Islam Across the Centuries* is a book by the historian Tarif Khalidi, published by Doubleday in New York in 2009 in 342 pages. {cite:openlibrary-images-of-muhammad-khalidi-2009} {cite:archive-org-images-of-muhammad-khalidi-2009}

## Publication and editions

In ten chapters the book follows the image of Muhammad through successive kinds of writing: the Qur'an, Hadith, the Sira, adab literature, Shiite biographies and Sufi literature, then the Sira in a new canonical age, later medieval biography and modern biography, ending with the contemporary Sira. {cite:archive-org-images-of-muhammad-khalidi-2009}
