## Summary
Harun al-Rashid's sermon after taking the caliphate. He praises God and bears witness to the Prophethood, then urges the people to fear God and to keep their pledge of allegiance.

The speaker opens with praise of God and the testimony to the Prophethood, describing the Prophet as sent in a time between messengers. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.82}

He urges the people to fear God and to prepare for the Day of Resurrection, and asks them to keep their pledge. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.82}

The book attributes the sermon to Harun on Tabari's authority; the date is inferred from the preceding page's account of al-Hadi's death, not stated in the sermon itself. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.82}
