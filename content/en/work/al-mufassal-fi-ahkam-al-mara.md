## Summary

*al-Mufassal fi Ahkam al-Mar'a wa-l-Bayt al-Muslim fi al-Shari'a al-Islamiyya* is a fiqh encyclopedia by the Iraqi scholar Abd al-Karim Zaydan on the rulings concerning women and the Muslim household, published by Mu'assasat al-Risala in Beirut in eleven volumes. {cite:archive-org-al-mufassal-zaydan-menhagmuslim}
