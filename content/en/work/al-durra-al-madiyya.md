## Summary
Al-Durra al-Madiyya fi al-Qira'at al-Thalath al-Mardiyya is by Ibn al-Jazari. The biography of the author in Dahman's edition of al-Nashr says he versified the Hidaya as the completion of the ten and called it al-Durra, and that he composed it at eighteen, and that some of his teachers may have memorised it. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 8}

## With the Shatibiyya and the Tayyiba
The editor of the Tayyiba says he printed the Shatibiyya and al-Durra, by which the ten lesser readings are completed, and then followed them with the greater Tayyiba; he adds that the two together count ten readers with twenty-one paths in all. {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 7} {cite:tayyibat-nashr-zabi-2015 p. 8} Its title points to three readings that complete the seven, but the sources read here do not name their owners. See [[work/hirz-al-amani-wa-wajh-al-tahani|the Shatibiyya]] and [[work/tayyibat-al-nashr|the Tayyiba]].

## A note
Do not confuse it with [[work/al-durra-al-mudiyya-fi-al-radd-ala-ibn-taymiyya|al-Durra al-Mudiyya fi al-Radd ala Ibn Taymiyya]], a different book in the museum. The verses of al-Durra were not read here.
