## Summary

A question about x-rays while fasting in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that an X-ray itself does not break the fast, but taking a contrast substance through an open passage to the interior does. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4153}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on X-ray imaging for someone fasting in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4153}

## The answer

The Department answered that X-ray imaging in itself does not break the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4153} It said that if the fasting person takes a medicine or substance to make the image visible through an open passage to the interior, such as the mouth or the anus, it breaks the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4153}
