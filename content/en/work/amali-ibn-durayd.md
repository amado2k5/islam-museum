## Overview
"Al-Amali" (Dictations) by Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr, the philologist, on the Arabic language, attributed to Ibn Duraid. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.597}

Jalal al-Din Abd al-Rahman al-Suyuti abridged it and called his abridgement "Qatf al-Warid". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.597}
