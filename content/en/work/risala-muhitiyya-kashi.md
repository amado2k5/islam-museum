## Summary

[[person/al-kashi|Al-Kashi]]'s Treatise on the Circumference computes the circumference-to-diameter ratio with high precision. MacTutor dates it to July1424 in the Samarkand scientific setting. It differs from the Key to Arithmetic and the Chord and Sine. The museum links it to the [[invention/al-kashi-pi-calculation|circumference contribution]], not to an origin claim for all circle computation {cite:kashi-mactutor Circumference treatise}.
