## Summary
Barakah is a studio album by the British singer-songwriter [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], released on 1 February 2016 by Andante Records and marketed as the first of a series of recordings, the Spiritique Collection (Vol. 1); it runs 64 minutes and 24 seconds. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} {cite:wikidata-barakah-sami-yusuf}

## The songs
It has fourteen tracks: 'The Parties', 'Inna Fil Jannati', 'Hamziyya', 'Ya Rasul Allah' in two parts, 'Fiyyashiyya', 'Ya Nabi', the Turkish 'Ben Yürürüm Yane Yane', 'Araftul Hawa', 'Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum', 'Taha', 'Asheqan', 'Barakah' and 'Iron'. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} Sami Yusuf released videos for several of them, among them 'Inna Fil Jannati', 'Taha', 'Hamziyya', 'Fiyyashiyya' and 'Ben Yürürüm Yane Yane'. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} The titles 'Ya Rasul Allah', 'Ya Nabi' and 'Taha' address [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en}
