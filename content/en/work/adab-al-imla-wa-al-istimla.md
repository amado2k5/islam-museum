## Overview

Adab al-Imla' wa-al-Istimla' by the hafiz 'Abd al-Karim b. Muhammad al-Sam'ani (d. 562 AH); rules of hadith dictation and the etiquette of samā' — one of the foundational texts in this genre {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 284}.
