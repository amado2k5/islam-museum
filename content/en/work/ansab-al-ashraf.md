## Overview
"Ansab al-Ashraf" (Genealogies of the Nobles) by Abu al-Hasan Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Baladhuri. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}

Hajji Khalifa describes it as a large and very useful book. Twenty volumes of it were written, and the author did not complete it. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}
