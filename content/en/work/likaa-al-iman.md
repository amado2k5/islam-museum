## Summary
"Liqa' al-Iman" (Meeting of Faith) is a television interview programme of which the Assabile archive keeps twenty-nine video episodes, each roughly eight to thirteen minutes long. The presenter opens every episode by welcoming "the great Islamic preacher" [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi]], then puts to him a run of questions on law, creed, Qur'anic interpretation, the Prophet's life and the affairs of family and society, and al-Sha'rawi answers in Egyptian colloquial Arabic. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:01:30} In his closing words the presenter describes the programme as a reply to questions raised "in all kinds of dealings and on all kinds of occasions". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:09:00} In episode 24 he notes that the recording was made two or three days before the start of Ramadan. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep24 00:02:00}

The questions are the presenter's words and serve here as context, not as the shaykh's statements; what is attributed to al-Sha'rawi is his answers alone, and they are his views, attributed to him, not rulings of the museum. Many questions are short and their answers shorter still, so this page gathers the answers that read clearly in the machine transcript and leaves aside what is unclear.

## The episodes
**Episode 1, the reproach in Surat 'Abasa; travel and emigration.** The presenter asks about the popular saying "we are reading Surat 'Abasa" and its link to the story of [[person/abd-allah-ibn-umm-maktum|Ibn Umm Maktum]] ([[passage/quran-80-1|Surat 'Abasa]]). {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:01:30} Al-Sha'rawi answers that the Prophet burdened himself in his call beyond what God had charged him, out of eagerness for its success, and that God tells him his duty is only to convey. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:02:30} He says that had God wanted people's faith by force He would have sent them a sign they could not escape, but He wants hearts, not moulds. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:03:00} He holds that this reproach was not for a failing but for taking on more than was asked, like a father who sees his son stay up till dawn before an exam and tells him he is killing himself: there is a difference between a reproach for the other's good and a reproach for disobedience. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:04:30} He says the same of the Prophet forbidding himself what God had made lawful for him: "our Lord did not fault Muhammad; our Lord is being merciful to Muhammad". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:05:30} He then distinguishes "travel for reflection", by which the traveller grows in faith at what he sees of God's creation, from "travel for gain", by which he seeks a living, drawing on the difference in wording between two verses that command people to journey through the earth. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:07:00} He recounts that he told the United Nations that the Qur'an has a verse that solves their difficulty: that the whole earth was laid down for all creatures, and that the world's problem is "land without men and men without land" because of borders and barriers. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:08:30} He asks anyone emigrating for a living to weigh two things: bodies, protecting himself against the country's diseases, and religion: if he can keep his faith there without pressure the journey is lawful, otherwise it is forbidden. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:09:30} He warns those who have daughters, and says that whoever finds weakness in himself must turn back. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep01 00:11:00} The legal material is in [[concept/hijra-fiqh|emigration]].

**Episode 2, healing by the Qur'an, zakat on savings, repeated pilgrimage.** The presenter asks about the trend of healing by the Qur'an; al-Sha'rawi says that what has a known cause is treated by returning to the causes God has given, and that what lies beyond a person's reach is referred to God's wisdom and accepted. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:02:00} He sees incantation and supplication as the entreaty of the desperate, whom God answers, and says that a sick person in distress clings to anything. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:03:00} On money a young man saves for marriage or a home, he says zakat is due on it as long as it exceeds his basic needs, and that one should not save for a future need while leaving a poor man hungry today. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:04:00} The material is in [[concept/zakat|zakat]]. On someone who performs the pilgrimage every year, he says that what exceeds the obligation is the owner's free choice, and that the money spent on the pilgrimage benefits many people. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:07:00} On the "tables of the Merciful" (free Ramadan meals) he holds that the point is not the charity alone but that the great sit beside the small and the rich beside the poor at one meal, and that the truly needy may be held back by shame. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep02 00:08:30}

**Episode 3, the slander and the acquittal of the innocent.** The presenter asks what an innocent person accused can learn from the acquittal of [[person/aisha|'A'isha]]. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep03 00:01:30} The material is on her page.

**Episode 4, the "people of the house", unregistered marriage, questions on fasting.** Al-Sha'rawi says a house is the place a person withdraws to from the bustle of life, and is only a house if it is safe and needs are met in it. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:00:30} He notes that the Qur'an uses "people of the house" in two senses: of Ibrahim's wife when she was given the glad tidings of a son, and of the Prophet's wives in [[passage/quran-33-33|Surat al-Ahzab]]. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:02:30} He holds that the glad tidings came to Ibrahim's wife from the angels so that nobody would accuse her when she gave birth in old age. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:03:00} He then mentions the narration in which the Prophet placed his cloak over [[person/fatima|Fatima]], [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan]] and [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]] and said they were the people of his house. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:03:30} The presenter cites [[person/mahmud-shaltut|Mahmud Shaltut]]'s view that an unregistered ('urfi) marriage fulfils all its pillars, and asks about those who rule it void. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:04:00} Al-Sha'rawi says that 'urfi means what people recognise, as opposed to the registered; registration exists for civil courts to accept claims and is not a condition of the Sharia; but in modern times 'urfi has become a marriage that is hidden, and a marriage concealed is not valid, since its basis is announcement and festivity so that people know. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:05:00} If the announcement is there, the marriage is valid. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep04 00:05:30} The questions on fasting in this episode are in [[practice/sawm-ramadan|the fast of Ramadan]].

**Episode 5, jinn and charlatans.** The material is in [[concept/jinn|jinn]].

**Episode 6, the sciences of the universe and the science of the lawful and forbidden; al-Sayyida Nafisa.** Al-Sha'rawi divides knowledge in two: knowledge of the universe on which people's interests depend, such as knowing that heat expands bodies so that a key or a nail can be made from iron, and heavenly knowledge that teaches the lawful and the forbidden. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:01:30} He says that the gift of Lordship reaches believer and unbeliever alike and comes before any obligation, while the gift of Godhood is command and prohibition. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:03:00} He defines civilisation as what gives you the highest return in the least time and with the least effort. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:05:00} He holds that life runs on material values from the universe and values from heaven, that the scholar of the Sharia should not intrude into the laboratory nor the scientist into the lawful and forbidden, and cites the hadith on pollinating the date palms. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:06:30} He says you will not find Russian electricity and American electricity, whereas the theoretical sciences differ between ideologies. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:07:00} The material is in [[person/al-sayyida-nafisa|al-Sayyida Nafisa]].

**Episode 7, Islamic banks, usury and work.** The material is in [[concept/riba-fiqh|usury]] and on the shaykh's page. He goes on to say that farming is what gives the food that keeps life going, and that Islam wanted the Muslim to be independent of others. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:06:00} He tells the story of a Companion who complained of need, whose goods the Prophet sold for two dirhams, which he calls "the first auction in Islam"; the Prophet told him to buy an axe with one, gather firewood and stay away fifteen days, and when he came back having earned, told him this was better than coming on the Day of Resurrection with the mark of begging on his face. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:07:00} He remarks that the command to leave trade for the Friday prayer singles out selling, because the seller always wants to sell while the buyer may not want to buy. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:07:00} He holds that God placed the treasures of the earth below its surface and reveals some of them through a volcano or an earthquake to push people to search. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep07 00:10:30}

**Episode 8, the story of Shu'ayb and women going out.** Al-Sha'rawi says the fighters in Chechnya may break the fast in Ramadan. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:00:30} The main material is in [[person/shuayb|Shu'ayb]]. He then says that the garden in which Adam lived was a training for his task in life, by command and prohibition, and that the warning of toil was addressed to Adam in the singular because labour falls on the man. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:08:00} He holds that the woman is to be provided for because she has a hard task, and that gold and silk were forbidden to men and permitted to her as things particular to her. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep08 00:09:00}

**Episode 9, the spirit; whether the dead benefit from others' deeds.** The material on the spirit is in [[concept/al-nafs-wal-ruh|the soul and the spirit]]. On whether the dead benefit from what is gifted to them, al-Sha'rawi mentions the hadith in which a dead person's deeds cease except for three, one of them a righteous child, then says the story of the Cave contains "three caves": the owner of the two gardens, who forgot that acknowledging the Giver is what guards the gift, and Musa with the righteous servant whom God taught secrets He had not taught His messenger. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:05:30} He explains that holing the ship kept it for its poor owners rather than letting the king seize it, and that the wall was propped up until the two orphans came of age and could take out their treasure; he holds they were twins since they come of age at once, and that their father's righteousness benefited them, so a parent's deed benefits the child. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep09 00:10:00} The story is in [[person/al-khidr|al-Khidr]].

**Episode 10, fasting without prayer, changing wrong, envy.** Al-Sha'rawi says prayer is the one pillar that never lapses, so that even a sick person lying down prays by moving his head, and that God imposed it directly, not through an intermediary, as a superior summons in person someone he wants to tell something important. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep10 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep10 00:02:30} The material on changing wrong is in [[concept/amr-bil-maruf|commanding right]]. Of someone who performs the obligations but harms people he says he is rewarded for the one and held to account for the other, because Islam is safety. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep10 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep10 00:06:30} The material on envy is in [[concept/hasad|envy]].

**Episode 11, whether the dead hear, a grave in a mosque, reading non-Muslim books.** The material on greeting the dead is in [[practice/ziyarat-al-qubur|visiting graves]]. On a father's zakat he says he does not give it to his married daughter but to her husband if he is poor, because a woman's maintenance falls on her husband. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:02:30} On the number required for the Friday prayer he notes that al-Shafi'i required forty, and says analogy is with Abu Hanifa and that this is the stronger view to him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:03:30} Of a man who built a mosque and placed a tomb for himself in it he says there is nothing wrong with it, citing the Prophet's tomb in his mosque and the tombs of saints in mosques; he calls the prohibiting view "pedantry", and holds that the hadith cursing those who took the graves of prophets as places of prayer is applied out of place, because a grave is restricted to burial. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:05:30} He says Muslims pray in the Rawda with the tomb on their left, and argues that the Qur'an relates the words of those who prevailed over the affair of the Sleepers of the Cave that they would raise a place of prayer over them, without rejecting them. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:06:00} This is a matter on which Sunni scholars differ, and the museum gives his view attributed to him, without a verdict. (needs_source: the views of the Sunni scholars who prohibit it have not yet been gathered in this run.) On reading non-Muslim books he mentions the Prophet's disapproval when he saw 'Umar with a sheet of the Torah, and says reading is permitted for knowledge, research and exposing contradiction; the material is in [[person/ismail|Isma'il]]. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:07:30} He lists the forms of change in the earlier scriptures: forgetting, concealment, shifting words from their places, and adding words said to be from God. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:08:00}

**Episode 12, his life.** The presenter asks about his life, and al-Sha'rawi tells of his upbringing, schooling, years abroad and ministry; the material is on [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|his page]].

**Episode 13, desires and hardships, following a school, divorce by allusion.** Al-Sha'rawi says that if a Muslim cannot practise his religion in a country, it is forbidden for him to stay there. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep13 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep13 00:01:00} He explains the hadith in which the Fire is surrounded by desires and Paradise by what the soul dislikes: desire is the soul's pull towards what pleases it now and harms it later, and Paradise is surrounded by what the soul finds hard, such as charity and pardon, so do not sell the deferred Paradise for the hasty desire. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep13 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep13 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep13 00:02:00} The material on following a school is in [[concept/madhhab|madhhab]], and on divorce in [[concept/talaq|talaq]].

**Episode 14, Ayyub and medical treatment, trial, the orphan's property.** The material on Ayyub and on trial is in [[person/ayyub|Ayyub]]. On a brother who raised his young siblings and then claimed what he had spent when the estate was divided, he says that the sound practice is to divide the estate at the death so each takes his share, and for the guardian to manage the orphan's property faithfully. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep14 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep14 00:06:30} He holds that God called the property of the incompetent "your property" in the verse forbidding handing their wealth to them because the guardian is answerable for it, until on reaching maturity it is handed over. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep14 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep14 00:07:00}

**Episode 15, the waiting period, jealousy and proposal, the punishment of the grave.** The material on the waiting period is in [[concept/iddah|'idda]]. Al-Sha'rawi contrasts a young man prowling round a girl's house, at whom her father is angry, with one who knocks on the door to propose, whom the father welcomes and offers a drink, and holds that the difference is that the second takes her by God's word, so that the lawful cuts off jealousy. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:03:30} On the punishment of the grave he says it belongs to the unseen, for which there is no proof but the report of God and His messenger, and argues from the verse on Pharaoh's people being exposed to the Fire morning and evening before the Hour comes, which can only be in the grave. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep15 00:07:30}

**Episode 16, maturity and marriage, herbal medicine.** Al-Sha'rawi holds that a young man who reaches maturity becomes independent, and that maturity means being able to beget one's like, as a watermelon is known to be ripe only when its seed turns black and can grow another melon. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:05:00} Of a father who stops his capable son from marrying until his sisters marry he says this is meddling in what is not his concern, because marriage answers an instinct that, if not given a lawful outlet, turns to wrong, while the father may judge whether the bride's family are good people. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:06:30} He says the hadith of the black seed is sound and established. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:08:00} He observes that the kebab seller adds parsley, the fish seller pickled peppers, and whoever eats salted fish eats onion, and that a donkey in the field eats green mint and leaves dry hay by instinct, and sees in this the guidance the Creator placed in His creatures. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:10:30} He says experience leads to a truth, and the truth becomes a constitution people live by. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep16 00:12:30} The material is close to [[concept/al-tibb-al-nabawi-wal-badil|prophetic medicine]].

**Episode 17, the basmala, imported meat, questions between the schools.** The material on the basmala is in [[concept/al-basmala|the basmala]].
