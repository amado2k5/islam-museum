## Overview
"Al-Mudkhal al-Kabir ila Ilm al-Nujum" (The Great Introduction to the Science of the Stars), a book on astrology by [[person/abu-mashar|Abu Ma'shar]]. In it he describes how each planet appears in different forms when it rises. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 470}

Al-Mas'udi cites it in the chapter on the ghoul and what appears in the deserts when the stars rise. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 470}
