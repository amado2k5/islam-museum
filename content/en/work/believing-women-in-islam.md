## Summary

*"Believing Women" in Islam: Unreading Patriarchal Interpretations of the Qur'an* is an English book by the Pakistani-American scholar Asma Barlas, published by the University of Texas Press on 15 June 2002. {cite:openlibrary-believing-women-in-islam-2002} It has 254 pages, with a bibliography on pages 237–248. According to its contents, it opens with a chapter on the Qur'an and Muslim women, between reading patriarchy and reading liberation. Part I treats texts: the Qur'an, tafsir and hadith, and the Sunna, Shari'a and the state. Part II treats the image of the father in ideas of the divine and of the prophets, the Qur'an on sex, gender and sexuality between sameness, difference and equality, and family and marriage. {cite:archive-org-believing-women-in-islam-2002}
