## Overview
«The Prophet's Campaigns» is a series of eight episodes by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] preserved in the Assabile archive's audio-lesson section; the series page lists eight lessons and all eight have been treated in this work: the introduction and the first campaign after the emigration, its events and lessons, the Treaty of Hudaybiyya, the Battle of the Confederates (the Trench), the campaign of Khaybar, the campaign of Tabuk, the battle of Hunayn, and finally the conquest of Mecca. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim ghazawat-el-nabi-sala-allaho-alayhi-wa-salam} The lecturer announces at the outset that the series will move through the Prophet's campaigns, their causes, and the lessons drawn from them, with remarks on the conduct of the fighters, the organisation of the army, and family affairs; what is reported here is the lecturer's account as the machine transcript shows it. The literal transcript text is in places unintelligible or repetitive, so the events he mentions are told as his narration rather than as independently verified facts, and any line that cannot be read clearly is marked as needing a source.
