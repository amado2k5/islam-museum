## Summary

*Les croisades vues par les Arabes* is a book in French by Amin Maalouf, published in 1983. Jon Rothschild's English translation, *The Crusades Through Arab Eyes*, appeared in London from Al Saqi in 1984 and in New York from Schocken in 1985, and an Arabic edition came out from Dar al-Farabi in Beirut in 1993. {cite:openlibrary-croisades-vues-par-les-arabes} {cite:encyclopedia-com-amin-maalouf}

## Publication and editions

The publisher's description of the English translation says Maalouf drew on the works of a score of contemporary Arab chroniclers of the Crusades, eyewitnesses and often participants in the events, retold their accounts in their own style and retraced two decisive centuries of Middle Eastern history. {cite:archive-org-crusades-through-arab-eyes}
