## Summary
Jalwa-e-Janan is the first religious and spiritual album of the former Pakistani pop singer [[person/junaid-jamshed|Junaid Jamshed]], recorded and released in 2005, recorded at QTV and issued by Heera Stereo. {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en} {cite:wikidata-jalwa-e-janan}

## The naats
Its most popular naat is 'Muhammad Ka Roza Qareeb Aaraha Hai', as The Express Tribune reported. {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en} It also includes 'Tu Ne Poochhi Hai' to poetry by Muhammad Iqbal, 'Jagah Ji Lagane Ki' to poetry by Aziz al-Hasan Ghouri, the supplication at the Multazam 'Ilahi Teri Chokhat Par' to poetry by Taqi Usmani, 'Yeh Subhe Madina', the Pashto 'Rasha Makawa', and a recitation from [[passage/quran-23-1|Surat al-Mu'minun]], which the museum points to and never types. {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en}

## Legacy
The Express Tribune wrote in 2017, after Junaid Jamshed's death, that the industry felt the void of his naats as Ramadan approached, and the magazine Images reported that his sons recited his naats in tribute. {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en}
