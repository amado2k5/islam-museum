## Overview
The novel by Hanif Kureishi (1995): a British Muslim witness between his Pakistani family, his English wife and his friend, who is enchanted by a book that burns souls, against a fatwa resembling the Rushdie case. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of the first novels to enter the Muslim Asian Britain from within after the 'Satanic Verses' affair. {cite:wikidata}
