## Summary
Al-Safadi's great biographical dictionary, arranged alphabetically in about thirty volumes; he split off the notables of his own lifetime into a separate six-volume work, A'yan al-'asr wa-a'wan al-nasr {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 88}. The Wafi remains indispensable to scholars; twenty-two edited volumes had appeared by 1993 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 760}.
