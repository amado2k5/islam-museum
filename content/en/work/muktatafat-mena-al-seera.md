## Summary
Omar Abdelkafy's series "Muktatafat min al-Sira": Monday lessons presenting excerpts from the life of the Prophet Muhammad — from the state of the Arabs before Islam to the hijra, the founding of the state and the conquests — and drawing lessons from them for Muslims. The first episode introduces the series, poses the question "why was Arabia chosen as the home of the message?" and surveys pre-Islamic Arab character. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy muktatafat-mena-al-seera/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy muktatafat-mena-al-seera/ep01 00:07:00}

## Episode one: an introduction to the sira and the rise of the Arabs
- He opens his Monday lesson after an absence of about four years from this appointment and announces a new series, "Muktatafat min al-Sira", in which he presents the life of the Prophet Muhammad: the state of the Arabs before Islam, his mission, the hijra to Medina and the founding of the Muslim state, and the conquests — then draws lessons for Muslims, among them raising the youth, Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi as military leader and jurist, and the character of the Companions and the Prophet's wives. - He pauses on the question "why was the Arabian peninsula chosen as the home of the message?" and surveys the surrounding nations — Persians and Romans — and the pre-Islamic Arab character connected to generosity: a generosity that surpassed the wind's speed, courage, and jealousy over honour to the point that they used to bury their daughters alive for fear they would be looked at, and marriage to close kin — a daughter, a paternal aunt, a sister — was not forbidden among them.
