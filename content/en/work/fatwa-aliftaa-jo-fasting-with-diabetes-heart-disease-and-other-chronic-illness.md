## Summary

A question about fasting for people with diabetes, heart disease, high blood pressure, kidney disease or ulcers, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that someone wholly unable to fast breaks the fast and pays the fidya without making up the days, someone able on some days fasts those and makes up the rest, and someone burdened in summer but able in winter breaks the fast and makes it up later. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4095}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on fasting for people with diabetes, heart disease, high blood pressure, kidney disease or ulcers. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4095}

## The answer

The Department answered that someone wholly unable to fast does not fast and owes the fidya, feeding a poor person, but no making up, citing the verse on the fidya in [[passage/quran-2-184|Surat al-Baqara 2:184]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4095} It said that someone able to fast on some days of the month but not others fasts what he can and makes up the missed days after Ramadan whenever he is able, with no fidya. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4095} It added that a sick person for whom fasting is a hardship in the long, hot days of summer but who can make up the days in the short days of winter breaks the fast and makes it up when able, with no fidya. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4095}
