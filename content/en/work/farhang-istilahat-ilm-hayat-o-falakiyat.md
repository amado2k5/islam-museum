## Summary
*Farhang-i istilahat-i ilm-i hay'at o falakiyat* belongs to the publication series of the Anjuman Taraqqi-e Urdu Pakistan and was printed in Karachi; it is a glossary giving Urdu and Arabic equivalents for English astronomical terms, arranged in English alphabetical order from A to Z. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 1} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 59} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 60}

## Method
- It sets one or more equivalents against each English term, for example "Altitude" as *irtifa'*, subdivided into true, apparent and observed, and "Almucantars" as *al-muqantarat* or circles parallel to the horizon. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 4} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 5}
- Many entries carry a brief explanation, and related sub-terms are grouped under one heading, such as kinds of year (solar, lunar, leap, sidereal) and kinds of angle. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 5} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 6} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 58}
- It notes that many star names in European astronomy are corruptions of Arabic originals, saying for example that "Achernar" derives from *akhir al-nahr* ("the end of the river") and that "Algol" is *al-ghul*, adding that Abd al-Rahman al-Sufi described it as reddish whereas today it is brilliant white. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 1} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 3}
- Under "Almagest" it calls it Ptolemy's famous book of astronomy, and under "Astrolabe" a metal disc used to find the altitude of celestial bodies. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 4} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 9}

## Significance
It represents the Anjuman Taraqqi-e Urdu's effort to build an Urdu scientific vocabulary drawing on the Arabic astronomical heritage, restoring the Arabic origin of English terms where one exists. {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 1} {cite:anjuman-farhang-istilahat-ilm-hayat-1949 p. 3}
