## Summary
Al-Suyuti says it is important to know the grounds of the readings and that the imams devoted books to it, among them al-Hujja of Abu 'Ali al-Farisi. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 536} The note identifies the author as al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Ghaffar al-Fasawi, the grammarian (d. 377 AH), and says al-Hujja lil-Qurra' al-Sab'a is a commentary on Ibn Mujahid's Sab'a and is printed. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 536}

## With the other books of explanation
Al-Suyuti pairs it with Makki's al-Kashf, al-Mahdawi's al-Hidaya and Ibn Jinni's al-Muhtasab, and quotes al-Kawashi that the use of explanation is to serve as evidence for what is evidenced or to tip the balance, with a warning against favouring one reading so strongly that it nearly cancels the other, since each is mutawatir. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 536} See [[work/kitab-al-sabaa-ibn-mujahid|Kitab al-Sab'a]], [[work/al-kashf-an-wujuh-al-qiraat-al-sab|al-Kashf]] and [[work/al-muhtasab-fi-tabyin-wujuh-shawadhdh-al-qiraat|al-Muhtasab]].
