## Summary

*Jihad: expansion et déclin de l'islamisme* is a French book by the French scholar Gilles Kepel on Islamist political movements, published by Gallimard in Paris in 2000 in 708 pages. Its English translation, *Jihad: The Trail of Political Islam*, came from the Belknap Press of Harvard University Press in 2002, with a bibliography on pages 379–429.
