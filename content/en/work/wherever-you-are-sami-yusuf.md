## Summary
Wherever You Are is the third official studio album of the British singer-songwriter [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], produced by himself and released on 29 October 2010 by ETM International; it runs 65 minutes. {cite:wikipedia-wherever-you-are-sami-yusuf-ar} {cite:wikidata-wherever-you-are-sami-yusuf} Sami Yusuf counted it as his true third album, after [[work/without-you-sami-yusuf|Without You]], which Awakening had issued without his consent. {cite:wikipedia-without-you-sami-yusuf-en}

## How it was made
He recorded it in Britain, Egypt, South Africa and the United Arab Emirates; it has eleven main songs and bonus tracks in Arabic, English, Turkish and Persian, all written by Sami Yusuf, two with Conner Reeves and one with the British singer Ian Brown, as the album's e-book records. {cite:wikipedia-wherever-you-are-sami-yusuf-ar}

## The songs
Its songs include 'Wherever You Are', 'Salaam', 'Without You', 'You Came To Me' in four languages, 'Fragile World', 'In Every Tear, He Is There', 'Make Me Strong' and 'Healing'; 'Without You' and 'In Every Tear' are new versions of songs first issued on Without You. {cite:wikipedia-wherever-you-are-sami-yusuf-ar} {cite:wikipedia-without-you-sami-yusuf-en} Videos were made for 'You Came To Me', 'Healing', 'In Every Tear', 'Make Me Strong', 'Wherever You Are' and 'Salaam'. {cite:wikipedia-wherever-you-are-sami-yusuf-ar}
