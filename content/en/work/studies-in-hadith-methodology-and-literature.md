## Summary

*Studies in Hadith Methodology and Literature* is a book by Muhammad Mustafa Azami, published by American Trust Publications in Indianapolis in 1977 in 122 pages, with later editions in 1978 and 2002. {cite:google-books-studies-in-hadith-methodology} In his introduction, dated Rajab 1397/1977 in Riyadh, the author says he wrote it for college students and educated general readers who do not read Arabic, avoiding detail and technical language as far as possible. {cite:archive-org-studies-in-hadith-methodology-azami-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book has two parts: the first, on hadith methodology, has eight chapters, including the meaning and authority of hadith, the carrying of knowledge, the recording of hadiths, the isnad system, the history of hadith criticism, and the causes of fabrication and the means of eliminating it. {cite:archive-org-studies-in-hadith-methodology-azami-scan} The second part, on hadith literature, introduces the six principal books and six other authors who each mark a stage in the compiling of hadith, from Malik and Ahmad ibn Hanbal to al-Tabarani, Ibn al-Athir, al-Haythami and al-Suyuti. {cite:archive-org-studies-in-hadith-methodology-azami-scan}
