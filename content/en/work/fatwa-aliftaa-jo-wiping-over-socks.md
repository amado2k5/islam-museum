## Summary

A question about wiping over socks in wudu, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that most socks worn today may not be wiped over because they do not meet the conditions for wiping, and set out those conditions. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4060}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on wiping over socks in wudu. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4060}

## The answer

The Department answered that wiping over most of the socks known today is not permitted, because they do not meet the conditions for wiping. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4060} It said that the dispensation came for wiping over leather footwear (khuffs) and socks under conditions: that they are thick and do not let poured water through, that one can keep walking in them, that they are put on in a state of purity, that they cover the whole foot up to the ankles, meaning the protruding ankle bones, and that they are not torn. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4060}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4060, dated 18 January 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4060} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
