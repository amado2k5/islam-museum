## Overview
A book by Abu Ma'shar the astrologer. In it he described the structures and the great buildings whose construction happens in the world every thousand years. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}

His student Ibn al-Mazyar selected from it in [[work/muntakhab-kitab-al-uluf|"Al-Muntakhab min Kitab al-Uluf"]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}
