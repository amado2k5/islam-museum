## Summary
*Great Women of Islam, Who Were Given the Good News of Paradise* is a book on the lives of the women Companions promised Paradise, by [[person/mahmood-ahmad-ghadanfar|Mahmood Ahmad Ghadanfar]], translated into English by Jamila Muhammad Qawi, revised by Sheikh Safiur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri, edited by Muhammad Ayub Sapra and Muhammad Farooq, and published by Darussalam, Riyadh. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.1}

## Purpose
The publisher's note says the book presents the lives of the women Companions given the good news of Paradise and highlights their service in war — treating and giving water to the wounded — and in spreading Islam. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.5} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.6}

## Structure
It opens with a chapter on the women Companions' achievements, then gives the Mothers of the Believers — Khadija, Sawda, A'isha, Hafsa, Zaynab bint Khuzayma, Umm Salama, Zaynab bint Jahsh, Juwayriya, Safiyya, Umm Habiba and Maymuna — followed by Fatima bint Muhammad, Fatima bint Asad, Umm Ruman, Sumayya, Umm Haram bint Milhan, Asma' bint Abi Bakr, Umm Sulaym, Umm Ammara Nusayba, al-Rubayyi' bint Mu'awwidh, al-Furay'a bint Malik, Umm Hisham bint Haritha, Asma' bint Yazid, Umm Sa'd Kabsha bint Rafi', Umm al-Mundhir Salma bint Qays, Umm Waraqa and Umm Ayman. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.3} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.4} Each chapter opens with the hadith of good news that places its subject among those promised Paradise, such as the hadith on the people of the [[event/bayat-al-ridwan|Pledge of Ridwan]] in the chapters on [[person/al-furaya-bint-malik|al-Furay'a]], [[person/umm-hisham-bint-haritha|Umm Hisham]] and [[person/umm-al-mundhir-salma-bint-qays|Umm al-Mundhir]]. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.218} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.224} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.246}

## Method
A popular book without notes, telling the lives in a hortatory style and digressing into the men of each woman's family — the chapter on [[person/kabsha-bint-rafi|Umm Sa'd]], for example, retells how Sa'd ibn Mu'adh and Usayd ibn Hudayr accepted Islam through Mus'ab ibn Umayr. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.240} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.241} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.242}

## For young visitors
An English book that tells the stories of women Companions of the Prophet who were promised Paradise, such as his wives, his daughter Fatima, and women of Medina.
