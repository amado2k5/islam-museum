## Overview
A treatise on the creed of the People of the Sunna and the Community, written by [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] in 698 AH in reply to a request from one of the judges of Wasit. A session to discuss it was held in Damascus in 705 AH. {cite:aqida-wasitiyya}

The cover of the edition states that it was written in 698 AH at the request of one of the judges of Wasit. This is a single source, so the date of composition needs a further source.

The treatise opens with an introduction on the creed of the saved sect. It then has sections on the divine names and attributes, the speech of God and the Qur'an, the vision of God, the Last Day, predestination, faith, the Companions, the family of the Prophet, the miracles of saints, following the early Muslims, and enjoining good. The edition adds an account of the debate held in 705 AH. {cite:aqida-wasitiyya}
