## Summary

A question about someone who dies in debt and leaves no wealth, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that debts are paid from the estate before it is divided, and that if there is no estate or it is not enough, the heirs are not obliged to pay from their own wealth but it is recommended for them. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4277}

## The question

The questioner asked about someone who died in debt and left no wealth, and whether the heirs must pay the debts. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4277}

## The answer

The Department answered that when someone dies owing rights to people, such as debts, they are paid from his estate before it is divided if he left wealth, and that settling debts comes before the heirs' right to inherit, relying on a hadith graded sound (hasan) by [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]] that a believer's soul remains suspended by his debt until it is paid on his behalf. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4277} It said that if the deceased left no estate, or left wealth that does not cover the debts, the heirs are not obliged to pay them from their own wealth, but it is recommended for them, because the deceased remains burdened by the debt and it is not filial devotion for a son to leave his father so burdened. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4277} It added that all the children can contribute and cooperate to settle their father's debts, even in instalments according to their means. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4277}
