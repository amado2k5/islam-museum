## Summary

*Women and Gender in Islam: Historical Roots of a Modern Debate* is an English book by the Egyptian-American scholar Leila Ahmed, published by Yale University Press in New Haven in 1992 in 296 pages. {cite:openlibrary-women-and-gender-in-islam-1992} According to its contents, the book is in three parts: the pre-Islamic Middle East, in Mesopotamia and the Mediterranean Middle East; "Founding Discourses", from the rise of Islam to the medieval period; and "New Discourses", covering social and intellectual change, the discourse of the veil, the first feminists and divergent voices. A bibliography is on pages 249–279. {cite:archive-org-women-and-gender-in-islam-1992}
