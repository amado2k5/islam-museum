## Summary
A Qur'an commentary delivered orally by [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi]] and later transcribed in books titled Tafsir al-Sha'rawi or al-Khawatir. This card rests on a scanned copy whose edition has not been verified, and covers the scan pages 2501 to 2522 on Surat al-Nisa. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2501}

## Method as it appears in this section
He takes the verses in order, explains words by derivation and usage, and then moves to general commentary on life, conduct and belief. On the verse of greeting he sets out rules for greeting; on the verse of truthfulness he distinguishes the statement from the speaker; on the verses about the hypocrites he explains the word "faction" and traces the word "hypocrisy" to the jerboa, a desert animal. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2501} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2510} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2515} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2516}

## Questions he treats at length
In this section he treats truthfulness in the statement and the speaker {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2509} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2510}, the decree and the acts of servants {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2519} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2522}, and compulsion in religion and fighting {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.2513}. These are his views; they appear on the concept cards linked to them, and no ruling is given here.
