## Overview
A book by the Imam al-Shafi'i, Muhammad ibn Idris, who died in 205 AH. Ibn Hajar mentions it in "Al-Majma al-Mu'assas", according to "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 250}
