## Overview
A book on astronomy and chronology by [[person/al-biruni-muhammad-ibn-ahmad|Abu Rayhan Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Biruni al-Khwarazmi]]. He wrote it for Shams al-Ma'ali Qabus, and in it he set out the calendars used by different peoples and the differences in the principles on which they are based. Hajji Khalifa describes it as a useful book. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.179}
