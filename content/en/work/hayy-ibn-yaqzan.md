## Summary
Hayy ibn Yaqzan — Ibn Tufayl's philosophical treatise, in print; al-Zirikli lists it in his entry together with Asrar al-Hikma al-Mashriqiyya. {cite:al-aalam p. 937}

## Details
The al-A'lam entry gives Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Tufayl, Abu Bakr, an Andalusian philosopher who died in 581 AH, and names among his books the treatise Hayy ibn Yaqzan (printed) and Asrar al-Hikma al-Mashriqiyya (printed), noting that the French scholar Léon Gauthier wrote a book in French on Ibn Tufayl. {cite:al-aalam p. 937}

It is a philosophical tale by an Andalusian thinker who lived under the Almohad state, and it had become famous enough that orientalists were already translating and studying it within al-Zirikli's lifetime. {cite:al-aalam p. 937}
