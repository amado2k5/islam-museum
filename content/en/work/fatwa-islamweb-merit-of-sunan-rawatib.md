## Summary

A question on the merit of the regular sunna prayers, which the IslamWeb fatwa centre answered by setting out what is reported about them. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032}

## The question

The questioner asked what the merit of the regular sunna prayers is. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032}

## The answer

The centre answered that their merit is great, citing the hadith of Umm Habiba in Muslim's collection that whoever prays twelve voluntary rak'as each day will have a house built for him in Paradise. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032} It gave al-Tirmidhi's version, which lists them: four before the noon prayer and two after it, two after the sunset prayer, two after the night prayer and two before the dawn prayer. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032} It then gave what is reported on the merit of particular ones, such as keeping four rak'as before the noon prayer and four after it. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032}

The IslamWeb fatwa centre published this fatwa as no. 446032, dated 17 August 2021. {cite:islamweb fatwa 446032} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
