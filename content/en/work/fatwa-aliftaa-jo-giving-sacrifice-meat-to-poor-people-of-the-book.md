## Summary

A question about giving sacrifice meat to poor People of the Book, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that they may be given meat from a voluntary sacrifice just as they may be given charity, a view among the Shafi'is favoured by [[person/muhibb-al-din-al-tabari|al-Muhibb al-Tabari]] and [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4389}

## The question

The questioner asked whether poor People of the Book may be given meat from the sacrifice. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4389}

## The answer

The Department answered that poor People of the Book may be given meat from a voluntary sacrifice, just as they may be given charity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4389} It said this is a view within the Shafi'i school that al-Muhibb al-Tabari and al-Nawawi inclined towards, referring to the Hashiya of Ibn Qasim al-Abbadi on [[work/tuhfat-al-muhtaj|Tuhfat al-Muhtaj]] (9/365). {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4389}
