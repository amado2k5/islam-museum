## Summary
Prayers of the Last Prophet is a spoken-word album by [[person/yusuf-islam|Yusuf Islam]], produced by himself on the Jamal label as the follow-up to [[work/life-of-the-last-prophet-yusuf-islam|The Life of the Last Prophet]], and gathers supplications used by [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. {cite:wikipedia-prayers-of-the-last-prophet-en} {cite:wikidata-prayers-of-the-last-prophet} The article dates it 14 September 1999, while Wikidata and the article's category place it in 2003; the museum records both. {cite:wikipedia-prayers-of-the-last-prophet-en} {cite:wikidata-prayers-of-the-last-prophet}

## Its order
The album runs in five parts through the day: general prayers first, then the night with prayers for bedtime and the night vigil, then dawn with the call to prayer and the morning prayer, then the day with istikhara and prayers for leaving home, travel, visiting the sick, entering and leaving the mosque and visiting graves, then the evening with entering the home, eating, and prayers for spouses, children and parents, closing with blessings on the Prophet. {cite:wikipedia-prayers-of-the-last-prophet-en} The museum does not type the supplications.
