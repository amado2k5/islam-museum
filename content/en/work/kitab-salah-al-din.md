## Overview
The novel by Tariq Ali (1998): a Jew from Cairo recalls Saladin on the day of the recovery of Jerusalem, as a ruler and not a myth: his weakness, his generosity and his policies. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The first novel in Ali's 'Islamic Pentalogy'; it presented Saladin to Western readers as a human politician, not a legendary hero. {cite:wikidata}
