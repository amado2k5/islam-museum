## Summary
Al-Thabat 'inda al-Mamat ('Steadfastness at the Moment of Death') is a book of Ibn al-Jawzi that al-Dhahabi counts among his works, and its title indicates that it concerns steadfastness at death. In the books I read I found no description of its contents or arrangement; they give its name in the list of the author's writings. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2194} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 45 p. 290}

## What the books say
In the Siyar al-Dhahabi lists Ibn al-Jawzi's works, among them al-Thabat 'inda al-Mamat and al-Mawt wa-ma Ba'dahu, followed by the volumes of his diwan and others, and says he left aside many he had not seen. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2194} In Tarikh al-Islam it appears in the same list of works between Nasim al-Riyad and al-Wafa bi-Fada'il al-Mustafa. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 45 p. 290} The book index at the end of that volume points to the place. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 45 p. 564}
Al-Dhahabi in this entry points to many books he had not seen, so his naming of a title does not show that he had read the text of the book. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2194}

## What I did not find
I found in these books no description of the book, no arrangement and no quotations from it, and no report assigned to this book in particular. From the title alone I know only its subject. As for the report on al-Ghazali's death from his brother Ahmad attributed to Ibn al-Jawzi, I found nothing that fixes which book of his gave it; see [[event/death-and-burial-of-al-ghazali-at-tabaran|Death and burial of al-Ghazali at al-Tabaran]].
