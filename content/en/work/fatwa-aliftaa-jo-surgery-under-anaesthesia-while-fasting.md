## Summary

A question about surgery under anaesthesia while fasting, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that anaesthesia itself does not break the fast, provided the patient is not under it for the whole day, but that another fast-breaking act may occur during the operation and require making the day up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4152}

## The question

The questioner asked whether undergoing surgery under anaesthesia invalidates the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4152}

## The answer

The Department answered that anaesthesia itself does not break the fast, because anaesthetic gases have no physical substance, and anaesthetic injections under the skin are likewise. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4152} It set the condition that the patient not remain under anaesthesia for the whole day: if he was awake at the start of the day his fast is valid, and likewise if he wakes after the operation even a moment before sunset. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4152} It said that something else that breaks the fast may occur during an operation, such as substances entering the interior, and if that happens he must make the day up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4152}
