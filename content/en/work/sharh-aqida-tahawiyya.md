## Overview
A long commentary written by [[person/ibn-abi-al-izz-al-hanafi|Ibn Abi al-Izz al-Hanafi]] on [[work/aqida-tahawiyya|the Tahawi Creed]]. For each article it sets out the views of the sects and schools and discusses them. {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}
