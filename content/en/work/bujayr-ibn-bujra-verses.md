## Summary
Verses Ibn Ishaq attributes to a man of Tayy' called Bujayr ibn Bujra, recording the Prophet's word to Khalid that he would find Ukaydir hunting wild cows, and what the cows did that night until they drew him out. The museum does not reproduce the verses' text. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}
