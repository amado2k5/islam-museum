## Summary

A question about paying zakat al-fitr in cash, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the basic rule is to pay it from the land's staple food, and reported that Hanafi jurists permit paying its value in cash. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4209}

## The question

The questioner asked about paying zakat al-fitr in cash. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4209}

## The answer

The Department answered that the basic rule is to pay zakat al-fitr from the land's staple food, which in Jordan is wheat or rice, set the amount at 2,500 grams per person, said it is easy to give this amount of rice to the poor and needy, and said this is correct in all the schools. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4209} It reported that Hanafi jurists permit paying the value in cash, because it is more useful to the poor and easier for the giver. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4209}
