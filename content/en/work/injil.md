## Overview
The Injil in Islamic conception is the book revealed to 'Isa ibn Maryam; al-Tha'labi names it among the books God sent down {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition}.

## In the sources
The Qur'an mentions the Injil as confirming the Tawrat before it and as guidance and light; Ibn Kathir details the chapters {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
