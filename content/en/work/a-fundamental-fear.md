## Summary

*A Fundamental Fear: Eurocentrism and the Emergence of Islamism* is an English book by the British scholar Bobby S. Sayyid, published by Zed Books in London and New York in 1997 in 185 pages, and reissued in 2003 and on 12 February 2015. {cite:openlibrary-a-fundamental-fear-1997} {cite:google-books-a-fundamental-fear} According to the contents of the 2003 edition, its chapters treat the framing of "fundamentalism"; thinking Islamism and rethinking Islam; Kemalism and the politicisation of Islam; Islam, modernity and the West; and Islamism and the limits of the "invisible empire". A bibliography is on pages 162–178. {cite:archive-org-a-fundamental-fear-2003}
