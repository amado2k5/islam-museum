# Islam.ru

## Summary

Islam.ru is a Russian-language web portal devoted to setting out the provisions of Islam and its social and cultural role, with a full English version. Several Russian scholars, Alexey Malashenko and Roman Silantyev among them, count it among the largest Islamic sites of the Russian-speaking internet. {cite:wikipedia-islam-ru-en}

## Beginnings

The site was established in 2001 by the [[group/spiritual-administration-of-muslims-of-dagestan|Spiritual Administration of Muslims of Dagestan]] with several foundations. According to Kavkazskiy Uzel (Caucasian Knot) it was financed by the Dagestani businessman Abusupyan Kharkharov, and its first chief editor was Marat Sayfutdinov. {cite:wikipedia-islam-ru-en}

## What it publishes

It publishes news, articles and religious texts, among them the texts of prayers, and has an "Online Consultation" section for psychological, legal and theological advice; Russian media such as REGNUM, Interfax-Religion and Kommersant cite its material. {cite:wikipedia-islam-ru-en}

## Changes

In February 2011 the site was redesigned; Murad Osmanov, an employee of the Spiritual Administration of Muslims of Dagestan, became its director, and its headquarters moved to Dagestan. {cite:wikipedia-islam-ru-en} On 5 March 2018 Magomed Yusupovich Magomedov was appointed chief editor. {cite:wikipedia-islam-ru-en}

## Today

The site is active at islam.ru in Russian and English and has articles in the English and Russian Wikipedias. {cite:wikidata-islam-ru}
