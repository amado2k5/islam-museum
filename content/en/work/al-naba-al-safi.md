## Summary

The Assabile archive lists 'Al-Naba' al-Safi' among Omar Abdelkafy's video series, with twenty-four episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} It is a programme in which the Sheikh announces that he will take their calls after the break and asks that they keep to the episode's theme, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep01 00:32:30} and then takes the callers. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep01 00:38:00} In the eleventh and twelfth episodes he tells the story of Hatim and his eight lessons. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep11 00:07:00}

- He answers, to a question, that the Muslim's time is precious so he does not follow these channels in earnest or in jest, and will be asked about his life and youth, and advises keeping them out of homes so their owners stop. - To the one who asks about lawful ruqya broadcast on channels he says lawful ruqya is known and the Prophet and the Companions practised ruqya with the Fatiha, called the healing, tells (in sense) the account of reciting the Fatiha over a stung man, and describes that the reciter puts his hand on the place of pain, reads the Fatiha, asks for healing, says in the name of God three times and seeks refuge in God's might and power from the evil of what he finds and fears seven times, and cites the verses of healing. This is the lecturer's answer; the procedure and accounts are in sense without chains. - He closes that these channels have no place in a Muslim's house and boycotting them is the greatest practical admonition, that we call scholars to explain a ruling or the meaning of a verse and not to prattle of what we do not know, warns against the simple thinking that everyone who appears on screen has become like Abu Hanifa and Ahmad, calls to rely on sincere supplication and boycott all charlatanry, and prays that God spare us the devils of men and jinn.
