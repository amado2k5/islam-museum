## Summary
Puji-Pujian ("Praises") is the debut album of the Malaysian nasheed group [[group/raihan-band|Raihan]], produced by Farihin Abdul Fattah and released by Warner Music Malaysia; the English article dates it to October 1996 and the discography of the Malay article to 2 January 1997, and the museum records both. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}

## The songs
It has ten songs in Malay: 'Puji-Pujian', 'Peristiwa Subuh', 'Iman Mutiara', 'Sifat 20', 'Rakan Selawat' in two versions, one for the Mawlid with Ustaz Amaluddin, 'Sesungguhnya', 'Rayuan Rindu', 'Kembali' and 'Assolatuwasalam', also with Ustaz Amaluddin. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}

## Reach
The English article reports sales of more than 750,000 copies in Malaysia and, citing the group's official site, calls it Malaysia's best-selling album, twelve times platinum, and double platinum in Singapore. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} The same article gives total sales figures that vary between 1.5 and 3.5 million and no date for the certifications, so the museum tells them as attributed reports rather than dated certifications. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en}
