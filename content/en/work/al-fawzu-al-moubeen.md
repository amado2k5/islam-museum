## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with fourteen episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} In the first episode he presents it as a practical path to [[concept/al-fawz-al-mubin|the manifest triumph]] through simple remedies, and argues that people belittle such remedies and think the triumph comes only through great deeds like those of the Companions. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep01 00:01:00} In the ninth episode he reminds listeners that the subject of the series is the manifest triumph. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:00:00}
