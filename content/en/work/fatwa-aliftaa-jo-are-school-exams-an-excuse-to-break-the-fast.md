## Summary

A question about whether school exams are an excuse to break the fast in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that school and university exams are not an excuse that permits breaking the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4105}

## The question

The questioner asked whether school exams count as an excuse permitting one to break the fast in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4105}

## The answer

The Department answered that school and university exams do not count as an excuse permitting one to break the fast, because most students sit their exams while fasting without hardship beyond the ordinary. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4105} It said that fasting does not conflict with preparing for exams, and that a Muslim draws on acts of obedience for help with the tasks of this world and the next. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4105}
