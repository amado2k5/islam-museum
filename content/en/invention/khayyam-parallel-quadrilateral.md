## The story

Khayyam objected that earlier attempts used assumptions no clearer than the postulate being explained. He especially rejected introducing motion into geometry,in the analysis of his criticism of Ibn al-Haytham. He wanted a starting point grounded in concepts of line and angle and premises he regarded as philosophically immediate,rather than a moving drawing that looked convincing to the eye {cite:vahabzadeh-khayyam-mathematician author-p2-p3}.

Vahabzadeh identifies premises about intersecting lines diverging away from intersection,converging lines meeting,and convergence not turning into divergence in its own direction.
