## What it is

A quadrant is a quarter-circle; a sine quadrant represents trigonometric relationships through lines and graduations. Dizer describes the latter as an instrument for standard spherical-astronomy problems and identifies the complete quadrant as its development. The name does not mean that one surface knows everything or that an untrained user can read every marking:it expresses a programme of broader operation {cite:tdv-ibn-shatir-dizer instruments}.

It differs from the universal astrolabe also described in the biography,and from the astrolabic quadrant's different representation of the sky. Several instruments belonging to one maker are not automatically synonyms. This record concerns the complete quadrant and [[work/al-naf-al-amm-shatir|General Benefit on Operations with the Complete Quadrant]]; an astrolabe's construction date or a sundial's dimensions must not be transferred to it {cite:tdv-ibn-shatir-dizer instruments-and-works6-10}.
