## Summary
When a government has many bureaus, who compares their accounts and connects their work? Al-Duri describes a zimam associated with each bureau under [[person/al-mahdi|the Abbasid al-Mahdi]] in162AH, followed by a central zimam al-azimma in168AH, checking accounts, supervising work and coordinating with the vizier and other bureaus {cite:tdv-divan-duri Abbasid administration}. Nahide Bozkurt also describes connecting provincial zimam offices to the centre {cite:tdv-mehdi-bozkurt p.377–379}. This is an improvement within existing administration, not the invention of accounting or the world's first audit. The image is John Carlin's1835–56ledger cover at the Met: later context, not an Abbasid register or a visible internal account form {cite:met-account-ledger-20365 object20365}.

## What it is
