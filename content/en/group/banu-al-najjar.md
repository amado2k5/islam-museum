## Overview
Banu al-Najjar is a branch of [[group/al-khazraj|al-Khazraj]] in Yathrib. In the account of The Sealed Nectar they were the maternal uncles of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]]: [[person/hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] married [[person/salma-bint-amr|Salma bint Amr]] of Banu Adi ibn al-Najjar, and Abd al-Muttalib grew up in her father's house. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.49} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/137}

## Their support for Abd al-Muttalib
When [[person/nawfal-ibn-abd-manaf|Nawfal]] seized Abd al-Muttalib's water-skins and none of the men of Quraysh supported him, he wrote verses to his maternal uncles of Banu al-Najjar asking for help. His uncle [[person/abu-sad-ibn-adi|Abu Sad ibn Adi]] set out with eighty riders and camped in al-Abtah in Mecca, and drew his sword on Nawfal until the water-skins were returned. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}

When [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] saw Banu al-Najjar support Abd al-Muttalib, they said: "We gave birth to him as you did, so we have a better claim to support him", because the mother of Abd Manaf was from them. They then allied themselves with Banu Hashim. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} See [[event/recovery-of-abd-al-muttalib-rights|the recovery of Abd al-Muttalib's water-skins]].

## Not covered here
Banu al-Najjar among the Ansar after the migration, and the Prophet's stay with them, are covered in their proper entries.

## For young visitors
Banu al-Najjar were people of Medina who were the maternal uncles of Abd al-Muttalib, the grandfather of the Prophet, and they came to support him when he was wronged.
