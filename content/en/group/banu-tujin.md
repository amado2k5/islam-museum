## Overview
Banu Tujin are among the peoples of Banu Badin, of the third class of the Zanata. Ibn Khaldun describes them as among the greatest and most numerous of their tribes. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Homelands and leadership
Their homeland was on both banks of the Wadi Shelif, facing the mountain of Wanshrish. Leadership belonged to Banu Dafaltan, among them Atiyya ibn Dafaltan and Luqman ibn al-Mu'tazz. Luqman sent his son to Badis, before the encounter, to offer obedience and allegiance. Their people numbered about three thousand, and they had a part in the wars of Hammad ibn Buluggin. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Changes
Leadership passed to Banu Abd al-Qawi, and then Banu Abd al-Wad overcame their homelands. Among their later emirs was Atiyya al-Hayw, who was killed in the year 607 AH by a plot of the governor of Tlemcen. Among them was Nasr ibn Umar, lord of the mountain of Wanshrish, until the last days of Abu Hammu. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
