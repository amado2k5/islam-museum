## Overview
Masmuda are among the greatest Berber tribes, and among the branches of the Barnas according to the genealogists. Their homelands are the Dern mountains and their plains in the far Maghrib. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In Ibn Khaldun
Ibn Khaldun says that among their branches were Hintata, Barghawata, Bana and Hinta, and that the state of the Mahdi ibn Tumart and the Almohads arose among them. He says that at the beginning of Islam they followed the Safriyya of the Kharijites, until the caliphs and their worshippers turned them back from it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
