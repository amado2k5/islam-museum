## Name and meaning
Azali is the name given to those Babis who followed Mirza Yahya, called Subh-i Azal, after the death of the Bab. The name marks the branch of the Babi movement that followed Subh-i Azal, as distinct from those who followed other claimants. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 858}
