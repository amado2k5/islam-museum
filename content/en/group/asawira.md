## Overview
The Asawira were Persian horsemen who entered Islam en masse during the conquests. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

According to al-Baladhuri, they lived opposite the Daylam. When the Muslims reached them at Qazvin, they embraced Islam in the same way as the Asawira of Basra. They came to Kufa and settled there, and some of those whom Ziyad had moved from Hamra Daylam to Basra joined them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
