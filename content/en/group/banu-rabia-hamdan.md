## Overview
Banu Rabi'a are of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]]; the earlier batch met them at "al-Hisn". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} In the war at [[place/athafit|Athafit]] they were in the centre of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi's]] army, with Banu Ubayd of them on the left and Banu Malik of them in the centre. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} Al-Hadi's camps were in their land: [[place/karawi-banu-rabia-yemen|Karawi]] and [[place/al-darb-banu-rabia-yemen|al-Darb]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} They came to him asking to raid Arhab's villages for three of their dead and for the weapons and clothes taken from their group with Abu Umar, and he stopped them, saying he would begin by fighting them if they did. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.105} They have no link to Rabi'a ibn Nizar or to [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar]]. This record comes from the text alone and no descent is claimed for them.
