## Overview
Banu Ma'mar are of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], by the book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] put them with Banu Rabi'a in the centre on the first day of the war at [[place/athafit|Athafit]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100}, then on the right with Banu Sarim and Khawlan in the second battle. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} At a place of theirs [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn al-Hadi]] and his party arrived on the night of the Fitr before he set out to al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} The book gives no descent for them. See [[event/first-day-of-the-battle-of-athafit-285]].
