## Overview
"Banu Khuthaym" are a group at Najran named by the narrator in the cutting of their palms and then in their request for safety {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}.

## Detail
Seeing their palms cut, they told Ibn Humayd either to seek safety for them together or let them part, he agreed, and Ibn Bustam pleaded for them with al-Hadi, who gave them safety {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. The editor gives neither their descent nor their place, and the name is printed with thaa; the museum does not link them to the [[group/al-khushaymiyyun|Khushaymis]] registered with shin, and the two records are to be merged if they prove one. See [[event/banu-khuthaym-and-ibn-bustam-ask-safety-and-it-is-given-292|the event]].
