## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, the Samaritans are Jews, and they are of two kinds: the Dustan and the Kushan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.215-216}

## Peace and tribute
Abu Ubayda made peace with them in Jordan and Palestine, on condition that they be spies and guides for the Muslims, in return for a poll tax on their heads and feeding of their land. See [[event/treaty-with-samaritans|the treaty with the Samaritans]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.215-216}

Al-Baladhuri says that Yazid ibn Mu'awiya set the land tax on their lands and placed five dinars on each head. Their land later entered the estates of the caliphate after the great plague of al-Rashid, and some of the land tax was lifted from them in the year 246 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.215-216}
