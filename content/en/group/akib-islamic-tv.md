## Summary
Akib Islamic TV is a Bengali-language YouTube channel for Islamic moral short drama — natok — the main channel of the actor, director and writer Akib Ahmed. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} ThoughtLeaders' profile likewise describes it as a Bengali channel hosted by Akib Ahmed, focusing on entertainment with Islamic and societal themes, its library including dramas. {cite:thoughtleaders-akib-islamic-tv} The channel (@akibislamictv) counted 5,380,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure, with 3,500 videos and 2,145,289,055 total views recorded on the same page. {cite:vidiq-akib-islamic-tv}

## The dramas and the channel
The channel's own About text presents Akib Ahmed as a content creator and influencer, and its keywords name 'islamic natok' and 'Islamic short film'. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} His videos carry the Creative Media signature, and their 2025–2026 credits repeat a pattern: on 'Vikkhar Babsa' (2025), 'Chacha M.P.' (2025) and 'Onnayer Poton' (2025) he is named cast, director and script writer, while on other titles, among them 'Kormocharir Koshto' and 'Jora Kobor' (2026), another man directs and he remains script writer and cast, so the credits vary by title. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} vidIQ records the channel as started on 9 December 2019 with its country set to Bangladesh; its most-viewed uploads are natok titles, among them 'Lalosha' (about 14.5 million views) and 'Ayna' (about 10.4 million), and it gained 90,000 subscribers in the 28 days to 8 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-akib-islamic-tv} The channel is active: its most recent upload read on 9 November 2026 dated to about two weeks earlier and had drawn about 2.3 million views. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv}
