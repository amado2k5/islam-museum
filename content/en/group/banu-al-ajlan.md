## Summary
A clan to which Ibn Ishaq traces Ma'n ibn Adi, then says: "or his brother Asim ibn Adi". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text attaches the affiliation to Ma'n, with Asim mentioned as an alternative possibility (p. 174).
