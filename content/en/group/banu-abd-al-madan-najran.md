## Overview
Banu Abd al-Madan were a clan at Najran whom the narrator names in the account of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]]'s attack on the night with seventeen days of Ramadan 286 gone; Madani men were with [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|Ahmad ibn Muhammad]] and three of them were killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} The Banu Abd al-Madan who were with the two asked leave to go to their homes for fear of ruin and were allowed, then called their clients and fought Banu al-Harith until morning. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} Afterwards the Hamdan said they did not trust Banu Abd al-Madan toward the two men and that it was established that they had agreed to Banu al-Harith's demands {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}, and later some of them sent with [[person/al-mujahir-ibn-ziyad-al-qatani|al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad]] to Abu Muhammad to pledge support. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} This is the Zaydi narrator's account of a clan whose positions changed, and the museum gives no judgement. See [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]] and [[event/pledge-at-najran-and-the-fight-below-minas-286]].
