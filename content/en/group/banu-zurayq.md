## Overview
Banu Zurayq, a branch of [[group/al-khazraj|al-Khazraj]]; their lineage is Zurayq ibn Amir ibn Zurayq ibn Abd Haritha. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.218-220}

## Homes
They were fewer in number than [[group/banu-bayada|Banu Bayada]] and feared that the Bayada would outnumber them, so shortly before Islam they left their home for their known dwelling before the prayer-ground and the market of [[place/medina|Medina]], at Dhuruwan and its surroundings. They built fortified houses, among them one at the corner of the house of Kabir ibn al-Salt at the prayer-ground, and the fortress "al-Rayyan", near "the Rayyan canopy" of the family of Saraqa. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.218-220}

## War and blood money
A state of war continued between them, Banu Bayada and Banu Habib, which messengers kept trying to settle with blood money until they agreed to pay it. They gave the Bayada a lane called "the Lane of the Blood Money". Al-Samhudi mentions that they were, with Banu Bayada and Banu Ghafr, "the slowest to retreat and the quickest to return to the attack". {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.218-220}
