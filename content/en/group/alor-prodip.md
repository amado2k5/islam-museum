## Summary
ALOR PRODIP is a YouTube channel (@alorprodip) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures and publishes recordings of religious gatherings. The channel was created on 2 May 2018 and counted about 548 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-alor-prodip} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-alor-prodip}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 759 videos and about 32.1 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to the United States and whose category is Religion; the country setting is a platform fact about a Bengali-content channel. {cite:vidiq-alor-prodip} Its most-viewed content is mahfil recordings of Mamunul Haque: a gathering of 11 May 2024 with about 7.66 million views, another of 3 September 2024 with about 2.65 million, and a third of 24 November 2024, alongside material by Mufti Arif bin Habib and Dr A.F.M. Khaled Hossain. {cite:vidiq-alor-prodip} This record describes the channel only: its carriage of a preacher's mahfils carries no implication about that preacher and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-alor-prodip}
