## Summary
A Yemeni tribe, which Ibn Ishaq counts with Murad among [[group/madhhij|Madhhij]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
[[person/amr-ibn-madikarib|Amr ibn Ma'd Karib]] came to the Prophet with people of Banu Zubayd and embraced Islam; see [[event/delegation-of-zubayd|the arrival of Amr ibn Ma'd Karib]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230}. The Prophet appointed [[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk]] over Murad, Zubayd and all of Madhhij {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}. When the Prophet died, Amr apostatised among his people of Banu Zubayd {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/231}. In the lists of the Abyssinian emigrants, Mahmiyya ibn al-Jaz' is said to be one of them, an ally of Banu Sahm {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}.

## On the cause of Hilf al-Fudul (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum, p. 60)
The book says that a man of Zubayd came to Mecca with merchandise. [[person/al-as-ibn-wail|al-As ibn Wa'il]] of Sahm bought it from him and withheld his payment. He appealed to the allies (al-Ahlaf), who paid him no heed, so he climbed [[place/jabal-abi-qubays|Mount Abi Qubays]] and called out verses describing his wrong. That was the cause of [[event/hilf-al-fudul|the Pact of al-Fudul]], which won him his right {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}.
