## Overview
The Hanafi school is named after [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]]. According to Ahmad Amin it is the widest of the schools in its use of personal opinion. If circles were drawn to represent the schools' use of opinion, the smallest would be the Zahiri circle, then the Hanbali, then the Maliki, then the Shafi'i, and the largest the Hanafi. {cite:duha-islam p.502}

## Istihsan
The Hanafis upheld istihsan (juristic preference). As Ahmad Amin explains it, this means that the jurist leaves analogy in favour of judging a matter by what justice requires, seeking absolute justice. The Shafi'is rejected it, and a saying of al-Shafi'i in disapproval is transmitted: "Whoever practises istihsan has made a law." {cite:duha-islam p.504}
