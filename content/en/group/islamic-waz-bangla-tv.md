## Summary
Islamic waz bangla tv is a YouTube aggregator channel publishing waz in Bengali; its channel country is set to the United States, making it one of the diaspora-run Bengali waz channels. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bangla-tv} Its channel (@islamicwazbanglatv) counted about 1.17 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bangla-tv} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no individual is asserted for it here, and the country setting is a channel fact, not a verified residence for any person. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bangla-tv}

## Programmes and platforms
The channel was created on YouTube on 26 October 2019, and vidIQ records 1,176 videos and about 77 million total views for it. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bangla-tv} Its content is mahfils and lectures by many Bengali speakers, gathered from Bangladesh for a diaspora audience; it is an aggregator of other speakers' work, so no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bangla-tv}
