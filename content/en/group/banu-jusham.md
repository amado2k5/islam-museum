## Summary
A clan of [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]] famous for its knights and poets; among them was the veteran knight [[person/durayd-ibn-al-simma|Durayd ibn al-Simma]], and they fought on the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|day of Hunayn]] and at [[event/expedition-to-awtas|Awtas]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-82}.

## Their role
Banu Jusham attended Hunayn without advancing a banner of their own — their affairs ran with Hawazin's — and Durayd ibn al-Simma was their war-mind and counsellor, the one who objected to Malik ibn Awf's plan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-81}.

## For young visitors
A brave branch of Hawazin; among them was Durayd ibn al-Simma, one of the Arabs' most famous knights and poets before Islam.
