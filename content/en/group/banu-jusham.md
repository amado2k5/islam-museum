## Summary
A branch of [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]], known for its knights and poets. Among them was the old knight [[person/durayd-ibn-al-simma|Durayd ibn al-Simma]]. They took part in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Day of Hunayn]] and at [[event/expedition-to-awtas|Awtas]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-82}.

## Their role
Banu Jusham attended Hunayn without fielding a separate banner; their affairs were bound up with Hawazin's. Durayd ibn al-Simma was their military mind and their counsellor, and he was the one who objected to Malik ibn Awf's plan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-81}.

## For young visitors
A brave branch of Hawazin. Among them was Durayd ibn al-Simma, one of the most famous knights and poets of the Arabs before Islam.

## Rifa'a ibn Qays at al-Ghaba
Ibn Ishaq reports that a man of theirs of name and standing, [[person/rifaa-ibn-qays-al-jushami|Rifa'a ibn Qays]] or Qays ibn Rifa'a, stopped at al-Ghaba among a great branch of Jusham, wanting to rally Qays for war against the Prophet, and that Ibn Abi Hadrad killed him. See [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-al-ghaba|the report]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/278}
