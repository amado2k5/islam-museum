## Overview
Raihan is a Malaysian nasheed group founded in 1996, one of the best-known nasheed groups in Southeast Asia. It is known for the album 'Bujangga' and for Malay-language nasheeds that reached a whole generation in Malaysia and Indonesia. {cite:wikidata}
