## Summary
Raihan is a Malaysian nasheed group, named from the Arabic word rayhan, founded in 1996 by five members, Nazrey Johani, Che Amran Idris, Abu Bakar Md Yatim, Amran Ibrahim and Azhari Ahmad as leader, and known for its debut album [[work/puji-pujian-raihan|Puji-Pujian]] on Warner Music Malaysia. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikidata-raihan} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}

## The group
The founding member Azhari Ahmad died of a heart attack in 2001; the English article dates it to 29 August, after the Era Awards, and the Malay article to 29 September, a week before the album Demi Masa. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms} The others kept recording, and the line-up changed: Nazrey Johani left in 2006 to preach in his home state of Sabah, Nordin Jaafar replaced him until 2007 or 2008, Zulfadli Mustaza sang with them from 2007 to 2015, and today the members are Abu Bakar Md Yatim, Che Amran Idris and Ahmad Fadzli Ab Aziz. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms} [[person/yusuf-islam|Yusuf Islam]] sang 'Seal of the Prophet' and 'God Is the Light' with them on their second album, Syukur (1997), and in 2000 they starred in the film Syukur 21. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}

## Concerts and tours
In 1997 they were invited to perform at the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting in Edinburgh and received an honorary letter from Prince Charles for their part in the Commonwealth concert; they met Queen Elizabeth II again during her visit to Malaysia for the 1998 Commonwealth Games. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} They were the third Malaysian act to perform at London's Royal Albert Hall and returned in 2008 for an Islamic Relief evening, where they launched Selawat, an album of salawat for the Mawlid sung without instruments. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms} They toured France in 2004 and British cities, played Toronto in 2005, and have performed in Australia, Canada, China, Russia, South Africa, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Indonesia, Thailand and elsewhere. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en}

## Works
Between 1996 and 2011 the group released albums including Syukur, Senyum, Demi Masa, Allahu, Ameen, Tawakkal and Musafir Perjuangan, worked with the Islamic rap group Mecca2Medina on Ameen (2005) and with the Kuwaiti singer Muhammad Al-Husayyan. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms} In Indonesia their compilation Koleksi Nasyid Terbaik sold more than 200,000 copies. {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en}
