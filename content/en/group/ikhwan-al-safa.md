## Overview
Ikhwan al-Safa was a literary-philosophical circle in fourth-century Basra, authors of the fifty-one Epistles of the Brethren of Purity {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house}.

## In the sources
Al-Dhahabi's Siyar records Abu Hamid al-Ghazali's engagement with the epistles and his entanglement with the philosophers' doctrines, and describes the epistles elsewhere through their critics' words {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house}.
