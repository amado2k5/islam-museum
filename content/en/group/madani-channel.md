## Summary
Madani Channel is the television station of Dawat-e-Islami, the Pakistan-based Islamic movement, and the movement's YouTube channel under the handle @madanichannelofficial. {cite:wikipedia-dawat-e-islami-en} The television channel has operated since 2008, while the YouTube channel was created on 6 January 2009 and counted about 3.63 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-madani-channel} {cite:wikipedia-dawat-e-islami-en}

## The channel
Dawat-e-Islami launched Madani Channel with Urdu broadcasts and afterwards added channels in English, Arabic and Bengali; the station is free of commercial advertisements, runs on charity, and broadcasts programmes of Islamic teaching and moral guidance. {cite:wikipedia-dawat-e-islami-en} vidIQ records 60,108 videos and about 694.9 million total views for the YouTube channel, whose country is set to Pakistan; its most-viewed video is a 2021 Durood e Taj recitation with Urdu translation by Junaid Shaikh Attari, at about 4.89 million views, followed by Urdu dream-interpretation talks. {cite:vidiq-madani-channel} The YouTube channel is distinct from the movement's main organisation channel @dawateislami, which is documented separately in this museum. {cite:vidiq-madani-channel}
