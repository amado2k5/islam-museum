## Overview
A branch of [[group/banu-al-najjar|Banu al-Najjar]]: Banu Adi ibn Amr ibn Malik; Maghala was the mother of Adi. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.222-223}

## The house and the fortress of Fari'
They settled on the side to the right of anyone who stood at the end of the paved way facing the Prophet's Mosque in [[place/medina|Medina]]. They built the fortress called "Fari'", which faces the houses of Banu Talha ibn Ubayd Allah, and which later came into the house of Ja'far ibn Yahya al-Barmaki. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.222-223}

In this fortress Safiyya, the Prophet's aunt, was during the day of the Trench, and with her was [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]]. Al-Zayn al-Maraghi said that Thabit was Hassan's father. Ibn Sayyad was found in it in a hadith of Muslim. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.222-223}
