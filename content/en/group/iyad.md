## Overview
Iyad is one of the four great tribes that branched from the sons of Nizar ibn Ma'add ibn [[person/adnan|Adnan]]: Iyad, Anmar, [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar|Rabi'a]] and [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}

## Not covered here
The Sealed Nectar does not go beyond counting Iyad among the sons of Nizar. It says that Rabi'a and Mudar are the two whose branches multiplied. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} The accounts of Iyad in al-Mas'udi are in the data of this entry.

## For young visitors
Iyad is a tribe descended from Nizar, the brother of Mudar and Rabi'a.
