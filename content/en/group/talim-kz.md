## Summary

TALIM KZ is a Kazakh-language Islamic education channel on YouTube, publishing at @talimkz. Its About text expands its name as "TALIM KZ — TAĞYLYM, TANYM, TÄLIM" — edification, knowledge, instruction {cite:youtube-talim-kz}. By vidIQ's count, read on 5 November 2026, it had about 612 thousand subscribers, 3,599 videos and some 99.6 million total views — figures that are dated platform measurements and change over time {cite:vidiq-talim-kz}.

## Origins

The channel was opened on 13 February 2013, with its country set to Kazakhstan {cite:vidiq-talim-kz}. Nothing in the channel's published materials names a founder or an operating organisation: the channel presents itself under the TALIM KZ name alone {cite:youtube-talim-kz}. The founding recorded for the channel is therefore its own creation.

## Content

TALIM KZ is a multi-speaker channel, not one preacher's page: its most-viewed videos are sermons by Abdugappar Smanov, and its catalogue carries lessons and question-and-answer talks by many Kazakh preachers, among them Nursultan Kasimov, Zharkyn Myrzataev, Arystan Ospanov, Zhasulan Zhusipbekov, Mukhitdin Tulebekov and Nurlan Baizhigituly {cite:vidiq-talim-kz} {cite:youtube-talim-kz}. Alongside the sermons it publishes practical religious guidance, including Umrah and Hajj material {cite:youtube-talim-kz}.
