## Overview
Qays Aylan is a branch of the tribes of [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]], with many branches including Hawazin, Sulaym and Ghatafan in The Sealed Nectar's division of the Adnanite tribes. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} The book mentions it as a party in [[event/harb-al-fijar|the war of Fijar]] against [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] and those with them from [[group/kinana|Kinana]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/184-187}

## In the war of Fijar
Qays had the victory at the start of the day over Kinana, but by midday the victory was Kinana's over Qays. The leader of Quraysh and Kinana was [[person/harb-ibn-umayya|Harb ibn Umayya]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/184-187} According to al-Mas'udi the war was between Qays Aylan and Banu Kinana, and they permitted fighting in the sacred months. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.567-568}

## Not covered here
The book does not detail the lineage of Qays Aylan, their dwellings or their other battle-days. The tribes mentioned in this entry have their own entries.

## For young visitors
Qays Aylan is the name of a large group of Arab tribes, who stood on the opposite side to Quraysh in the war of Fijar, which the Prophet witnessed as a child.
