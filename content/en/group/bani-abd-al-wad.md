## Overview
A Zanata state in the central Maghrib, founded by Yaghmurasan ibn Zayyan in Tlemcen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1707}

## Founding
Yaghmurasan besieged Tlemcen for eight years, then entered it and took the name al-Mu'tadid. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1707}

## After Yaghmurasan
Their state continued after him between the Marinids in the Maghrib and the Hafsids in Ifriqiya, until their trace disappeared. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1707}
