## Summary
A tribe; Ibn Ishaq reports on Abu Ruhm's tongue that a band of them were allies in Banu Ghifar, and that the Prophet counted Aslam with Quraysh, the Ansar and Ghifar when speaking of those left behind. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172-173}

## In the Tabuk expedition
- The Prophet asked Abu Ruhm about men he described, and he remembered them as a band of Aslam who were allies in Banu Ghifar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173}
- He said the dearest of his people to him were that the Muhajirun of Quraysh, the Ansar, Ghifar and Aslam not stay behind him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173}
