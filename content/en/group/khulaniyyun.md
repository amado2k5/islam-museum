## Overview
The Khulaniyyun are the followers of Mulih al-Khulani, who was a pupil of Babak ibn Bahram. Babak was a pupil of Shayli, who had been one of the Mughtasila and then opposed them. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.477}

Ibn al-Nadim reports that al-Khulani agreed with Shayli and held back from the Jews. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.478}
