## Overview
The Ansar, meaning "the helpers": the Aws and the Khazraj of Yathrib, who pledged to the Prophet and sheltered him. Their pledge moved Islam from the call of the vulnerable to an established state.

## History
The Aws and the Khazraj entered two pledges at [[event/pledges-of-aqaba|al-Aqaba]], by which Yathrib accepted to host the Prophet and the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] and to protect them {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}. After the Hijra, the Ansar became the mainstay of the people: the men of the market, the orchard and the sword, at Badr, Uhud, the Trench and after {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

At the [[event/saqifa|Saqifa of Banu Sa'ida]] the question of authority was raised, and it was settled for Quraysh under Abu Bakr with the agreement of the Ansar. Sunnis see this event as a swift settlement; the Prophet's family and their followers see it as a betrayal of the promised 'Alid succession {cite:watt-1956-medina}.

## Legacy
For Sunnis the Ansar are a lasting support, of whom it is said: "none loves them but a believer". In the Shia account they are the party to the dispute that won. Both sides agree that Medina could not have been without them {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.
