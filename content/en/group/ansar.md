## Overview
The Ansar — "the Helpers" — Yathrib's Aws and Khazraj who pledged to the Prophet and sheltered him; their pledge carried Islam from a vulnerable call to a standing state.

## History
The Aws and Khazraj entered the two pledges of [[event/pledges-of-aqaba|Aqaba]], in which Yathrib undertook to host the Prophet and [[group/muhajirun|the Muhajirun]] and protect them {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}. After the hijra the Ansar became the pillar of souls: the people of market, orchard and sword at Badr, Uhud, the Trench and after {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

At the [[event/saqifa|Saqifa of Banu Sa'ida]] the charge of rule was raised, and it settled on Quraysh under Abu Bakr by the Ansar's concurrence — an event Sunnis see as a swift settlement, while the Prophet's household and their Shia see it as a betrayal of the promised Alid succession {cite:watt-1956-medina}.

## Legacy
The Ansar are Sunnis' everlasting support — "none loves them but a believer" — and in the Shia account they are the winning side of the dispute; both sides agree Medina could not have been without them {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.
