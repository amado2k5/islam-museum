## Overview
The Chishti order is among the greatest Sufi orders of South Asia; traced to Chisht in Khurasan, it spread through India until it became among its most widespread orders {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
Academic study describes the Chishtiyya as the leading Indian Sufi order, extending through the sultanate and Mughal eras {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.
