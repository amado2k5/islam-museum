## Summary
tvMU is Muhammadiyah's official television channel in Indonesia, broadcasting since its launch in November 2013 under the tagline Cerdas Mencerahkan, 'smart, enlightening'. {cite:khabar-tvmu-launch-2014} Its YouTube channel, tvMu Channel (@televisimuhammadiyah), counted about 415,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count on a reading last updated 8 November 2026, with 39,300,000 views and about 27,034 videos. {cite:vidiq-tvmu-channel}

## From a community channel to a national station
Muhammadiyah's chairman in charge of television, Dadang Kahmad, told Khabar Southeast Asia that the channel's establishment followed a 2005 Muhammadiyah national assembly directive, and that a community television channel had been established in Yogyakarta in 2010 before the national launch. {cite:khabar-tvmu-launch-2014} At the January 2014 report the new channel was broadcasting daily from 5 a.m. to 9 a.m., accessible on the Telkom 1 satellite and streamed worldwide, with plans to go digital by 2017. {cite:khabar-tvmu-launch-2014} Its programming features educational, cultural, women's studies and religious content, along with 30-minute news programmes and interviews with noted religious figures. {cite:khabar-tvmu-launch-2014}
