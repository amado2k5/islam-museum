## Summary
MyMecra is a Turkish media channel on YouTube (@mymecra) presenting itself as MY Media, describing itself as principled and entertaining, protecting the family structure and attaching importance to culture and art and to education. {cite:youtube-mymecra} The channel counted 1,270,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 16 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-mymecra}

## Serdar Tuncer and the channel's launch
The KÜRE Encyclopedia states that Serdar Tuncer, the poet, author and television presenter born in Ankara in 1978, launched the MyMecra YouTube channel in 2020, stepping into digital broadcasting. {cite:kure-serdar-tuncer} vidIQ dates the channel's start to 16 July 2020 and records its channel country as Türkiye. {cite:vidiq-mymecra} Tuncer is the channel's best-known face, fronting its HâsılıKelâm conversation series and its Biri Bir Gün series, and his association with the channel is stated in prose only, with no relation to him written in this record's data. {cite:vidiq-mymecra} The channel is a multi-presenter operation, also carrying series by other presenters, and its carriage of any presenter's programmes assigns none of that presenter's own audience to it. {cite:vidiq-mymecra}

## Reach and limits of the record
vidIQ recorded 310,978,876 total views and 2,123 videos for the channel as of 16 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-mymecra} The channel itself is the group's own YouTube presence, and its video descriptions link its website mymecra.com and its social accounts under the name MyMecra. {cite:youtube-mymecra} These figures are dated platform counts as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-mymecra} No Wikipedia article for MyMecra and no Wikidata item for it were verified at build on 16 November 2026, and that absence is recorded in this record's provenance rather than left blank. {cite:youtube-mymecra}
