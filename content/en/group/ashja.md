## Summary
Banu Ashja' ibn Rayth ibn Ghatafan ibn Sa'd ibn Qays Aylan — a Ghatafani clan on the Tihama coast; from them the Successor Masruq al-Ashja'i and the Saba' al-Ashja'iyyun, and they stood with Ghatafan at the Trench {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

## Lineage and home
النسب والموطن: أشجع بن ريث بن غطفان؛ من بطون غطفان الثلاثة عبس وذبيان وأشجع، ودارهم بساحل الحجاز {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## In Islam and history
