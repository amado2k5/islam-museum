## Overview
The Kharijites are a sect whose doctrine turns, as Ahmad Amin observes, on the question of unbelief and belief, like the Murji'a but in opposition to them. {cite:duha-islam p.944}

## What unites them
Al-Ash'ari, in "The Differences between the Sects", says that what unites the Kharijites despite the divergence of their doctrines is their declaration of unbelief against Ali and Uthman, the people of the Camel, the two arbitrators and everyone who accepted the arbitration, and the obligation to rise against an unjust imam. Al-Ka'bi and al-Ash'ari differed on whether declaring the committers of grave sins to be unbelievers was agreed upon by all of them or was the view of most of them, since the Najadat do not declare the committers of grave sins to be unbelievers. {cite:duha-islam p.944}

## Their view of belief
They held a narrow view of belief: no one was counted a believer among them unless he kept clear of grave sins. They are opposed to the Shi'a on two points, one of which is that the Shi'a sanctify Ali and the Kharijites declare him an unbeliever. They regard his killer, Abd al-Rahman ibn Muljam, as one of the best of creatures, as in the verses of Imran ibn Hittan. {cite:duha-islam p.945}

## Their culture
Ahmad Amin holds that their culture, because of the predominance of the desert, was pure Arabic, linguistic and literary, with no trace of foreign cultures. {cite:duha-islam p.949}

See also [[group/ibadiyya|the Ibadiyya]] and [[group/hururiyya|the Harouriyya]].

## In the Book of the Sunna of Abu Dawud
In the Book of the Sunna of the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan]], [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] has a chapter "on killing the Kharijites" and another on fighting them. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 78-79}
- **Holding to the community and bearing with rulers**: the chapter opens with Abu Dharr's report, "whoever leaves the community by a hand's span has thrown off the halter of Islam from his neck", which [[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|al-Khattabi]] explains as the collar on a beast's neck that keeps it from straying; then his report of rulers who will keep the spoils for themselves, with the command to be patient, and [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]]'s report of rulers "some of whose deeds you will approve and some reject" — asked, "Shall we not fight them?", he said, "No, as long as they pray." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 78}
- **Their description in the reports**: he gives the report of the man "with sunken eyes, prominent cheeks, a bulging forehead, a thick beard and a shaven head" who objected to the Prophet's distribution to those whose hearts were being won over, and the words, "From the stock of this man… will come people who recite the Qur'an but it does not pass their throats; they pass through Islam as an arrow passes through its quarry; they kill the people of Islam and leave the idolaters"; al-Khattabi explains *di'di'* as root or stock — his descendants, or his followers who build their opinion on his. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 80} In the report of Abu Sa'id and Anas: "they speak well and act badly… their mark is shaving", and Abu Dawud explains *tasbid* as removing the hair entirely. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 81}
- **'Ali and al-Mukhdaj**: he relates [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|'Ali]]'s report, "at the end of time will come people young in years and foolish in mind", and the account of the search for al-Mukhdaj among the dead: he was found lowest, next to the ground, and 'Ali said "God is greatest" and "God has spoken the truth and His Messenger conveyed it"; 'Abida al-Salmani made him swear three times, and he swore. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 81-83} He relates from Abu Maryam that al-Mukhdaj was called Nafi', "the one with the breast-like hand", a poor man who sat with them in the mosque, and Abu Dawud says that people know him as [[person/harqus-ibn-zuhayr|Harqus]]. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 83}

Elsewhere in the Book of the Sunna al-Khattabi notes that the Kharijites and those who declare Muslims unbelievers for sins cite "the adulterer is not a believer while he commits adultery" wrongly (see [[question/is-the-grave-sinner-still-a-believer|Does a grave sin take a person out of faith?]]). {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 45} These are their opponents' descriptions in the reports and commentaries, given with their attribution; the Kharijites' own statements have not been added here.
