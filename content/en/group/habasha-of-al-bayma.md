## Overview
The Habasha of al-Bayma remained after the conquest of Egypt, fighting for seven years, and no one could overcome them. Al-Baladhuri mentions that they did not burst the waters in the thickets. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.314}

They are linked in the museum's data to [[event/amr-dismissal-egypt|the account of Amr's dismissal from Egypt]].

This needs a second source: the summary is short and does not name their leaders or say how their fighting ended.
