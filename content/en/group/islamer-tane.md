## Summary
ISLAMER TANE is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@islamertane) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures by many preachers. The channel was created on 26 November 2019 and counted about 689 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 6 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamer-tane} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-islamer-tane}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 965 videos and about 68.4 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Bangladesh and whose category is Religion. {cite:vidiq-islamer-tane} Among its top videos is a lecture by Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan dated 22 August 2026, which shows the channel active in 2026, alongside lectures by Kazi Ibrahim and others; a channel's carriage of a preacher's lectures makes none of its subscribers that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between them. {cite:vidiq-islamer-tane} The channel is distinct from Taw Haa Tv, Adnan's lecture channel, which this museum documents under its own record: the handle differs, the creation date is earlier and the subscriber count is roughly half, and the shared "Islamer" prefix in the two names is a generic branding coincidence in this scene. {cite:vidiq-islamer-tane}
