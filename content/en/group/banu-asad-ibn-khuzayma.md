## Summary
An Arab tribe that is not Qurayshi. Ibn Ishaq names from it "Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh", an ally of Banu Umayya, and Qays ibn Abdullah. It is not the Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza of Quraysh {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}.

## Members of this tribe in the chapter on the emigrants to Abyssinia
- [[person/ubaydullah-ibn-jahsh|Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}
- [[person/qays-ibn-abdullah-asadi|Qays ibn Abdullah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}

## In the introduction to The Sealed Nectar
Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma are among the branches of [[person/ilyas-ibn-mudar|Ilyas ibn Mudar]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21}. Banu Asad lived east of [[place/tayma|Tayma]] and west of [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. Between them and Tayma were the abodes of Bahtar of [[group/tayy|Tayy]], and between them and Kufa were five nights' journey {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/15-16}.
