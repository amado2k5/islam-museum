## Summary
A non-Quraysh Arab tribe; Ibn Ishaq names of it "Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh", ally of Banu Umayya, and Qays ibn Abdullah. It is not Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza of Quraysh. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}
