## Summary
Islamer Rasta is a Bangladeshi YouTube aggregator channel publishing waz and mahfil material in Bengali; its published description offers Bangla waz, mahfil recordings, Islamic lectures and Jumu'ah khutbahs. {cite:vidiq-islamer-rasta} Its channel (@islamerrasta) counted about 5.14 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamer-rasta} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no owner or head is asserted for it here. {cite:vidiq-islamer-rasta}

## Programmes and platforms
The channel was created on YouTube on 5 February 2017, and vidIQ records 2,821 videos and about 882.4 million total views for it; its channel country is set to Bangladesh and it ranks #75 among the country's channels by subscribers. {cite:vidiq-islamer-rasta} Its content is mahfil recordings and lectures by many Bengali speakers, uploaded at a near-daily rate: it is an aggregator of other speakers' work, not one preacher's channel, and no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-islamer-rasta}
