## Summary
Ibn Ishaq lists this clan among the Abyssinian emigrants under the lineage "Zuhra ibn Kilab ibn Murra". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Of this clan in the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
**Carried in the two ships:**
- [[person/amer-ibn-al-waqqas|Amir ibn al-Waqqas]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
- [[person/utba-ibn-masoud|Utba ibn Mas'ud]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

**From the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants:**
- [[person/al-muttalib-ibn-azhar|al-Muttalib ibn Azhar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

**Those born in the land of Abyssinia:**
- [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-muttalib-ibn-azhar|Abdullah ibn al-Muttalib ibn Azhar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the chapter on the Prophet's family in al-Raheeq al-Makhtum (p. 53)
The chief of this clan in lineage and nobility in the generation of Abd al-Muttalib was [[person/wahb-ibn-abd-manaf|Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab]]. His daughter [[person/amina-bint-wahb|Amina]], mother of the Prophet, was chosen by [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] for his son [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abdullah]]; at that time she was counted the best woman of Quraysh in lineage and standing {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.53} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/156-158}. Zuhra ibn Kilab was the brother of Qusayy, so the Prophet's maternal uncles came from this clan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48}.

## For Sprouts
Banu Zuhra, a clan of Quraysh by the line 'Zuhra ibn Kilab ibn Murra'; from this clan come the Prophet's uncles — his mother Amina bint Wahb — and some of the Abyssinia migrants.

## For Explorers
Banu Zuhra, a clan of Quraysh by the line 'Zuhra ibn Kilab ibn Murra' in Ibn Ishaq's lists of the Abyssinia migrants. Of them in the migration were Amir ibn al-Waqqas, Utba ibn Mas'ud and al-Muttalib ibn Azhar, and Abdullah ibn al-Muttalib ibn Azhar was born in Abyssinia. The clan's chief in descent and honor in Abd al-Muttalib's generation was Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab, whose daughter Amina is the Prophet's mother — Abd al-Muttalib chose her for his son Abdullah, held the best woman of Quraysh in lineage and standing; and Zuhra ibn Kilab was Qusayy's brother, so from this clan come the Prophet's uncles.

## For Scholars
Banu Zuhra, a clan of Quraysh by the line 'Zuhra ibn Kilab ibn Murra' in Ibn Ishaq's lists of the Abyssinia migrants. Of them in the migration were Amir ibn al-Waqqas and Utba ibn Mas'ud, carried in the two ships, and al-Muttalib ibn Azhar among the rest of the migrants, while Abdullah ibn al-Muttalib ibn Azhar was born in Abyssinia. The clan's chief in descent and honor in Abd al-Muttalib's generation was Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab, whose daughter Amina is the Prophet's mother — Abd al-Muttalib chose her for his son Abdullah, held then the best woman of Quraysh in lineage and standing; and Zuhra ibn Kilab was Qusayy's brother, so from this clan come the Prophet's uncles.
