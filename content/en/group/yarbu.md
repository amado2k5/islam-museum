## Summary
Banu Yarbu' ibn Hanzala ibn Malik ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim — an upper-Najd Tamimi clan; from them in Islam al-Qa'qa' ibn Amr ibn Malik, a horseman of the Ridda wars and the conquests, and Yarbu' was among the Arab tribes richest in riders {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir} {cite:isaba}.

## Lineage and home
النسب والموطن: يربوع بن حنظلة بن مالك بن زيد مناة بن تميم؛ دارهم باليمامة ونجد {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.
