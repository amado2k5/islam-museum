## Summary
Banu Lakhm ibn Malik ibn Himyar — the kings of al-Hira under the Persians and the first Arab polity in Iraq; among them al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir of the Nasrids, Amr ibn Hind, and Ka'b ibn al-Nu'man; they were the Arabs' bridge to Persia {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.25-27} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}.
