## Overview
A branch of [[group/al-khazraj|al-Khazraj]]: Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj al-Akbar ibn Haritha, known as Bali al-Harith. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.210-211}

## Their homes
They settled in al-'Awali, east of the valley of Bathan and the grave of Su'ayb, in [[place/medina|Medina]]. They built fortified houses, and the district of [[place/al-sunh|al-Sunh]] was named after them. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.210-211}

Jusham and Zayd, the sons of al-Harith, lived in al-Sunh. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] had a house there with his wife, the daughter of Kharija ibn Zayd, and it was a mile from the Prophet's house. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.210-211}

## Their rank among the Ansar houses
In the excellence of the sound report, they are third among the houses of the Ansar. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3807}
