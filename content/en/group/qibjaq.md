## Overview
The Qipchaq were nomadic Turkic tribes of the steppes beyond the Oxus and the Caucasus, to whom the "Qipchaq tongue" and the Qipchaq element of Mamluk Egypt are traced {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut} {cite:umari-masalik-1924}.

## In the sources
Yaqut mentions the Qipchaq in the Mongol irruption through Derbent, their sweep over Khazar lands and the killing of the Qipchaq; al-Umari's Masalik al-Absar surveys a mountain in the Qipchaq steppe and their land {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut} {cite:umari-masalik-1924}.
