## Overview
Zanata are one of the greatest Berber tribes. The genealogists count them among the branches of the Barnas. Ibn Khaldun says that their homelands were the central and far Maghrib, Sijilmasa and its surroundings, and that they were both nomads and settled people. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Their classes
Ibn Khaldun gives them three classes. The first is Maghrawa, Banu Yafran and Miknasa. The second is [[polity/awlad-mandil|Awlad Mandil]], the Masmuda and those related to them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The third is Banu Badin, among whom are Banu Tujin, Banu Marin, kings of Fez, and Banu Abd al-Wad, kings of Tlemcen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In history
He says that Abu Yazid, the man of the donkey, rose from among them against the Fatimid state. At times they followed the Umayyad call and at times the Shi'i call, until the [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|Almoravid state]] arose. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
