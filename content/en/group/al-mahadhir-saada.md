## Overview
The Mahadhir are a body of the Rabi'a of Khawlan the book names among those who fought [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] with [[group/al-akiliyyun-saada|the Akilis]], calling them their cousins. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.189} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} Al-Hadi left the Mahadhir's vines at [[place/afqayn-yemen|Afqayn]] and cut those of the Akilis. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} When their animals there were raided they sought safety from him and complained of the loss of their goods, and he returned to them the fifth he had received, to win them over and reconcile their hearts, according to the book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} The name is as Apple Vision reads it on the page; the print has it garbled once. See [[event/raid-on-afqayn-and-the-return-of-the-fifth-to-the-mahadhir-287]].
