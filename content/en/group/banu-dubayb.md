## Overview
The Dubayb are the kinsfolk of [[person/rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|Rifa'a ibn Zayd]] of [[group/judham|Judham]]. They embraced Islam upon the Prophet's letter that Rifa'a carried to them, and for this reason they went out to al-Hunayd ibn Aws when he raided Dihya al-Kalbi, and recovered his goods. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/260} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261} Later [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|the expedition of Zayd ibn Haritha]] struck them along with Judham. Hassan and Unayf, the sons of Milla, and Abu Zayd ibn Amr rode to Zayd, then to Rifa'a, then to the Prophet, and what had been taken was returned to them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/264}
