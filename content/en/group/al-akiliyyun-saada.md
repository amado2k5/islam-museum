## Overview
The Akilis are a body headed by [[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]], from whom Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah met al-Hadi at Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155} [[group/banu-sad-khawlan|Banu Sa'd of Khawlan]] fought them in the evening and burned some of their houses. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} Next day intercessors asked safe-conduct for them, al-Hadi granted it and Abbad al-Akili and the notables of al-Rabi'a came to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} The index lists "al-Akiliyyun" at pp. 155, 133, 189 and 197 and 406-412, among others. See [[event/fight-between-banu-sad-and-the-akilis-at-saada-286]] and [[event/safe-conduct-for-the-akilis-and-al-hadis-verses-from-saada-286]].
