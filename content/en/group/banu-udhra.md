## Overview
Banu Udhra are a tribe of [[group/qudaa|Qudaa]] living in the north of the Hijaz. Ibn Ishaq mentions them in two places in the fourth volume: the expedition of [[event/expedition-of-dhat-al-salasil|Dhat al-Salasil]] was "from the land of Banu Udhra", {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/272} and Qutba ibn Qatada al-Udhri commanded the right wing of the Muslims at [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/18}
