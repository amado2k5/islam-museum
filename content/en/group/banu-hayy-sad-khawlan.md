## Overview
Banu Hayy are a branch of [[group/banu-sad-khawlan|Banu Sa'd of Khawlan]], whom the book calls al-Hayyiyun. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} A party of them stayed behind [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at the foot of the rise and clashed with the Akilis at Sam'in. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} They went out with him on the night of mid-Sha'ban and fought in the valley of Allaf, and killed a rider called Ibn Abd al-A'la, killed by one of them, Muhammad ibn al-Akram. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.192} On the second sortie al-Hadi put them on the left wing with the Yarsamis. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} See [[event/the-night-march-of-mid-shaban-and-the-fight-at-allaf-287]].
