## Overview
The Qadariyya, according to the book's description, claim that capacity, will and power belong to them, that they own their deeds, good and evil, and that servants know their deeds without God's prior knowledge of them. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96} The book holds that their view resembles that of the Magians and the Christians, and that it is the root of heresy. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96}
