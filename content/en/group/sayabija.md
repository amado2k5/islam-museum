## Overview
The Sayabija were people who served in the Persian army, among those whom the Persians had taken captive from the people of Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Their conversion
Al-Baladhuri reports that when they heard of the affair of the Asawira, they embraced Islam and came to Abu Musa, who settled them in Basra among Banu Hanzala. A group of them were assigned to guard the treasury of Basra, said to be forty men, or said to be four hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
