## Overview
The Mawlawi order is a Sufi order traced to Jalal al-Din al-Rumi (d. 672 AH/1273 CE), famous for its rotating sama' — the 'whirling dervishes' — and among the orders closest to Ottoman state circles {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
The order arose in Seljuk Anatolia after Rumi's death and spread across the Ottoman world until it became among its best-known presences in letters, music and building {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.
