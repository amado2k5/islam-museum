## Overview
A state Islamic university in Jakarta; the Grand Imam delivered his address to the Muslims of Asia and the world there on 23 February 2016, and its rector said the university's study follows the curricula of al-Azhar's faculties {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 79}.
