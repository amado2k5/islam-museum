## Summary
A clan from whom Ibn Ishaq names Tha'laba ibn Hatib and Wadi'a ibn Thabit. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In this batch
Ibn Ishaq traces [[person/thalaba-ibn-hatib|Tha'laba ibn Hatib]] to "Banu Umayya ibn Zayd", and [[person/wadia-ibn-thabit|Wadi'a ibn Thabit]] to "Banu Umayya [ibn Zayd], fellows of Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The addition "[ibn Zayd]" in Wadi'a's lineage is from manuscript (a) per the editors (p. 174, note 2).
