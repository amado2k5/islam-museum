## Summary
A clan from which Ibn Ishaq names Tha'laba ibn Hatib and Wadi'a ibn Thabit. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In this batch
Ibn Ishaq traces [[person/thalaba-ibn-hatib|Tha'laba ibn Hatib]] to "Banu Umayya ibn Zayd", and [[person/wadia-ibn-thabit|Wadi'a ibn Thabit]] to "Banu Umayya [ibn Zayd], kin of Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The addition "[ibn Zayd]" in Wadi'a's lineage comes from manuscript (a), according to the editors (p. 174, note 2).

## Asma bint Marwan
Among them was [[person/asma-bint-marwan|Asma bint Marwan]], who was married to Yazid ibn Zayd al-Khatmi. She composed verses disparaging Islam and its people, and Umayr ibn Adi killed her. See [[event/killing-of-asma-bint-marwan|the report and its variants]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285}
