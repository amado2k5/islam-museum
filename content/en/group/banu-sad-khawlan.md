## Overview
Banu Sa'd of Khawlan are a body who met al-Hadi at Sa'da and came out to him when he sent a crier. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} They charged the Akilis and burned some of their houses without his leave and he blamed them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} Al-Hadi halted at al-Batna, a land of theirs, and a great army of them gathered to him, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} and criers were sent to them at al-Ghayl on the night of Adha. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} The index lists "Banu Sa'd" at pp. 155, 156, 157, 160 and 163 among others.
