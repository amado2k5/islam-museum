## Summary
In Ka'b's account, "the king of Ghassan" sent him a letter with a Nabataean of Syria's Nabataeans inviting him to join him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/179}

## In this batch
The letter's text and Ka'b's reply to it are in [[event/ghassan-kings-letter-to-kaab|the letter's event]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/179}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text neither names the king nor attributes anything to him but the title "king of Ghassan"; this is a tribe/kingdom page per the text, not a historical investigation (needs_source).
