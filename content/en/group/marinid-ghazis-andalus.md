## Overview
They are the Ai'as, descendants of Abd al-Haqq ibn Abd al-Haqq of the Marinids, who crossed to al-Andalus as volunteers for the holy war from the year 661, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.2034}

## Their standing
Their deeds were admired and their standing was honoured by the Banu al-Ahmar, to the point that they shared power with them and held the leadership of their jihad on their own. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.2034}

## Command
Ibn al-Ahmar placed the Zanata warriors for holy war under their command, and the emirate passed between Banu Rahu and Banu Abi al-Ala until the time of Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.2035}
