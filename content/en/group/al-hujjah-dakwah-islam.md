## Work and reach

youtubers.me records 7,102 videos and 91,018,934 total views for the channel, and ThoughtLeaders describes an Indonesian-language channel from Indonesia dedicated to Islamic teachings and education, uploading at a sustained cadence since its first upload on 12 January 2013. {cite:youtubers-me-al-hujjah-dakwah-islam} {cite:thoughtleaders-al-hujjah-dakwah-islam} Its catalogue is lecture content presented by various scholars, in ThoughtLeaders' description — the mark of an aggregator rather than one teacher's channel. {cite:thoughtleaders-al-hujjah-dakwah-islam}
