## Overview
The "Tabaris" are a body who emigrated to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], by the narrator's account about fifty men; they reached [[place/saada|Sa'da]] in the pilgrimage season of 285 AH and the pilgrims heard of them, then stayed some days and went out to al-Hadi at Khiwan, arriving in the best state. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116} The narrator takes their coming as God's support of al-Hadi and a grief to [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116} The text does not give their origin in detail, and it is not asserted here that they came from [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]] beyond what the name suggests; no line of descent is given. The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers. See [[event/ali-ibn-muhammad-reaches-saada-and-the-tabari-emigrants-285]].
