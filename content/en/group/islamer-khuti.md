## Summary
Islamer khuti is a Bangladeshi YouTube aggregator channel publishing Bengali waz mahfils; its name is a generic Bengali religious phrase meaning "pillar of Islam". {cite:vidiq-islamer-khuti} Its channel (@islamerkhuti) counted about 734 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamer-khuti} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, and this record connects the channel to no individual preacher. {cite:vidiq-islamer-khuti}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 1,632 videos and about 109 million total views for the channel, which ranks #1,206 among Bangladesh's channels by subscribers; its most recent uploads at the read were Mufti Amir Hamza mahfils dated 1, 2 and 5 November 2026, which date its current activity. {cite:vidiq-islamer-khuti} It is an aggregator of other speakers' work: carrying a preacher's mahfils makes him neither its owner nor its head, and he is not credited with its audience, nor is it credited with his. {cite:vidiq-islamer-khuti}
