## Overview
Junoon is a Pakistani rock band founded by Salman Ahmad in Lahore in 1990. It blended Western rock with Sufi qawwali, and its best-known songs are 'Jazbaa Junoon' and 'Sayonee Botah'. It received international recognition for its cultural role. {cite:wikidata}
