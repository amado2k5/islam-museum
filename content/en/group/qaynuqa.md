## Summary
The most warlike of Medina's Jews and its goldsmiths; the first of the Jewish tribes to break their pact with the Prophet — he besieged them until they surrendered on his judgement in 2 AH, and Abdullah ibn Ubayy obtained their exile to Adhri'at in al-Sham {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}.

## Lineage and home

## In Islam and history
في السيرة: قصتهم من قصص نقض العهود في المدينة، وعذر النبي في جلائهم ما قيل إنه نزل فيه شأنهم {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}.

## For younger visitors
Banu Qaynuqa' were the first Jewish tribe to leave Medina in the Prophet's time, after breaking the pact.
