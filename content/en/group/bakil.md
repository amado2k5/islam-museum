## Overview
One of the two wings of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] and a brother of [[group/hashid|Hashid]]: Bakil ibn Jusham ibn Hibran ibn Nawf ibn Hamdan. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab pp. 93-95}

## Lands and markets
Al-Hamdani locates their lands from the half of the plain of Sana'a to Najran, al-Jawf, Shakir and Sarwah. Among them was Sufyan ibn Arhab, from whom the Hadniyya in Khiwan descend. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab pp. 93-95}

Bakil had no market of its own. Its markets were Warr, Ghurq and Rida, in the land of Hashid. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab pp. 93-95}
