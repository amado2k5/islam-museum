## Summary
Rahe Islam is a YouTube aggregator channel publishing waz in Bengali; its channel country is set to Italy, so its operators work from the diaspora. {cite:vidiq-rahe-islam} Its channel (@raheislam502) counted about 831 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure whose daily snapshot that day still showed growth. {cite:vidiq-rahe-islam} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, and the country setting is a channel fact, not a verified residence for any person. {cite:vidiq-rahe-islam}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 472 videos and about 56 million total views for the channel — a smaller catalogue than the largest aggregators in its scene, and still growing at the read. {cite:vidiq-rahe-islam} Its content is mahfils and lectures by Bengali speakers, gathered for a Bengali-speaking diaspora audience; it is an aggregator of other speakers' work, so no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-rahe-islam}
