## Overview
Ghumara are a Berber people, inhabitants of the Ghumara mountains and the Rif country in the far Maghrib, between Tangier and the Moulouya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

They embraced Islam in the Umayyad state at the hands of Salih ibn Mansur al-Himyari, and they built the city of Nukur. They adopted, from the call of the Idrisids, shrines and monuments. According to Ibn Khaldun, those of the earlier period were known for practising sorcery and soothsaying. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Among their greatest clans were Banu Razin and Banu Warsikh. Their affairs continued in their lands under the banners of the successive kings of the Maghrib, up to the time of Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
