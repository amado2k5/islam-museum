## Overview
Banu Mazyad ibn Asad, Asadi people of Tamim, who were the emirs of the desert between Iraq, Syria and Najd. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.283}

## Wasit and al-Hilla
The notables and the Shi'a leaders of Wasit sought their support when their quarters burned in the strife between Sunnis and Shi'a, around the year 406 AH. They became the defenders of the people of Wasit. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.283}

They then took al-Hilla, and from it their well-known state arose. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.283}
