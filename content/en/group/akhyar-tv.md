## Summary
Akhyar TV is an Indonesian dawah television venture established in 2016 by the preacher Adi Hidayat with his two friends Heru Sukari and Roy Winarto, as the media arm of the Quantum Akhyar Institute he had founded in 2013. {cite:ghozali-adi-hidayat-rhetoric-2022} Secondary biographical retellings of the same account specify November 2016 for the founding. {cite:ghozali-adi-hidayat-rhetoric-2022}

## No standalone channel — the count belongs to Adi Hidayat Official
Akhyar TV has no standalone YouTube channel with a pinnable count: the venture's YouTube operation is the Adi Hidayat Official channel (@adihidayatofficial). {cite:ghozali-adi-hidayat-rhetoric-2022} {cite:vidiq-adi-hidayat-official} That channel counted 6,190,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as updated on 10 November 2026, with 648,800,000 views and 2,789 videos. {cite:vidiq-adi-hidayat-official} The 6,190,000 figure belongs to Adi Hidayat Official, and it is not presented here as the subscriber count of a standalone Akhyar TV channel, because no such channel could be found. {cite:vidiq-adi-hidayat-official}

## From television to YouTube
Ghozali's 2022 journal study records that Adi Hidayat's dawah activities developed in 2016 through the establishment of Akhyar TV, which focused on raising ulama, and that the transition of those activities from Akhyar TV to YouTube was done accidentally. {cite:ghozali-adi-hidayat-rhetoric-2022} The person and the venture are catalogued separately: Adi Hidayat's own record stands as a person record, and this record covers the Akhyar TV venture alone, with the founder link written once, here. {cite:ghozali-adi-hidayat-rhetoric-2022}
