An Arab tribe; Ibn Ishaq traces "Fatima bint Safwan ibn Umayya ibn Muharrith" to it, calling her "al-Kinaniyya". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}
