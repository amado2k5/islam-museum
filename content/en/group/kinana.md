## Summary
An Arab tribe. Ibn Ishaq names "Fatima bint Safwan ibn Umayya ibn Muharrith" as belonging to it, calling her "al-Kinaniyya". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Of this tribe in the chapter on the emigration to Abyssinia
- [[person/fatima-bint-safwan|Fatima bint Safwan]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the introduction to al-Raheeq al-Makhtum
Kinana ibn Khuzayma is one of the clans of [[person/ilyas-ibn-mudar|Ilyas ibn Mudar]], and from Kinana came [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]], the descendants of [[person/fihr-ibn-malik|Fihr ibn Malik]] ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}. The clans of Kinana remained in Tihama when the sons of Adnan scattered {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22}. The postponement (nasi') of the sacred months belonged to Banu Tamim ibn Adi of Banu Kinana {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.29} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/119-122}. Banu Bakr ibn Abd Manaf ibn Kinana helped Khuza'a drive out Jurhum, and then fought with Khuza'a against Qusayy {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.28} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.30}. The author cites the hadith in Muslim: God chose Kinana from the descendants of Isma'il, and from Banu Kinana, Quraysh {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:sahih-muslim 2/245}.
