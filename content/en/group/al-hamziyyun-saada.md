## Overview
The Hamzis are a body of Banu Hamza that the book names with [[group/banu-hayy-sad-khawlan|the Hayyiyun]] among the foot of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} He placed them on the right wing at al-Buq'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} and again on the right on the second sortie. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} The book gives no lineage beyond the name; the index lists Banu Hamza at pp. 133, 156 and 407 among others. See [[event/the-night-march-of-mid-shaban-and-the-fight-at-allaf-287]].
