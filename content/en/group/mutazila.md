## Overview
The Mu'tazila, according to the book's description, say what the Qadariyya say, and deny the punishment of the grave, intercession and the Basin. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96} The book reports that the Mu'tazila do not regard prayer behind those who differ from them among the people of the qibla as valid, and claim that the deeds of servants are not in the Preserved Tablet. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96}
