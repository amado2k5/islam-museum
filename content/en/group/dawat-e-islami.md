## Summary
Dawat-e-Islami is a Pakistan-based Islamic movement, and this record documents its main organisation YouTube channel, @dawateislami, which the movement's own videos name as "Dawat-e-Islami's Official YouTube Channel". {cite:wikipedia-dawat-e-islami-en} {cite:vidiq-dawateislami} The channel was created on 1 May 2015 and counted about 1.15 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-dawateislami}

## The movement and its channel
Dawat-e-Islami was officially founded in Karachi in September 1981 by leading scholars who selected Ilyas Qadri as its main leader, and it runs the Madani Channel television station alongside its educational institutions and online courses. {cite:wikipedia-dawat-e-islami-en} vidIQ records 1,636 videos and about 107.7 million total views for the @dawateislami channel, whose country is set to Pakistan; its most-viewed video is the 2024 episode "Important Questions with Maulana Ilyas Qadri", at about 1.19 million views. {cite:vidiq-dawateislami} The channel is distinct from the movement's television channel on YouTube, @madanichannelofficial, which is documented separately in this museum. {cite:vidiq-dawateislami}
