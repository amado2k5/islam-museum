## Overview
Descendants of Idris al-Alawi, who held the caliphate in al-Andalus during the Umayyad period after the civil strife. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 250}

## The story of the poet
Al-Maqqari reports that they conducted themselves as the Abbasid caliphs did: when a poet or a petitioner attended them, the caliph spoke from behind a curtain, and the chamberlain answered at the screen. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 250}

Ibn Maqana al-Ashbuni attended before the chamberlain of Idris ibn Yahya al-Hammudi in Malaga and recited his ode rhyming in nun. When he reached the description of the caliph's face, the caliph lifted the curtain himself and said: "Look as you wish." He then rewarded him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 250}
