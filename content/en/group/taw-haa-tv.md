## Summary
Taw Haa Tv is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@TawHaaTv) carrying the Bengali lectures (waz) of the preacher Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan. Created on 8 March 2021, it counted about 1.43 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-taw-haa-tv}

## The lectures channel
vidIQ records 1,024 videos and about 219.7 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Bangladesh; every one of its five most-viewed videos is a lecture by Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan, the most popular of them — a 2023 lecture on escaping despair — drawing about six million views. {cite:vidiq-taw-haa-tv} The channel is distinct from the preacher's personal channel (@abutawhaamuhammadadnan), which is documented with his person record in this museum. {cite:vidiq-taw-haa-tv}
