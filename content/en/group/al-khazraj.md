## Overview
Al-Khazraj is one of the two tribes of Yathrib, with [[group/al-aws|al-Aws]]. The Sealed Nectar counts them among the branches of [[group/kahlan|Kahlan]] of the Qahtani lineage, and makes them sons of Haritha ibn [[person/thalaba-ibn-amr|Thalaba ibn Amr]] of [[group/al-azd|al-Azd]], who settled in [[place/medina|Medina]] after the migration of the Azd from Yemen. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.231-235}

## A dispute over lineage
The author notes that Sulayman al-Nadwi inclined to the view that the Ansar of the Aws and al-Khazraj were of the family of [[person/nabit|Nabit ibn Ismail]], not of Qahtan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:tarikh-ard-al-quran 2/78-86}

## Wars between kin
The author gives the Aws and al-Khazraj as an example of how rivalry for honour often led to wars between tribes that share one father. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.45}

## Not covered here
The pledges of al-Aqaba and the support of al-Khazraj for the Prophet after the migration are presented under [[group/ansar|the Ansar]] and in the entries on the events.

## For young visitors
Al-Khazraj are a tribe of Medina, originally from Yemen. Together with al-Aws they became the "Ansar", the helpers who welcomed the Prophet.

## For Sprouts
Khazraj, one of Yathrib's two tribes with the Aws, of the Qahtani clans of Kahlan — two sons of Haritha ibn Tha'laba ibn Amr of Azd, who settled Medina after Azd's migration from Yemen.

## For Explorers
Khazraj, one of Yathrib's two tribes with the Aws; the Raheeq counts them among the Qahtani clans of Kahlan and makes them two sons of Haritha ibn Tha'laba ibn Amr of Azd, who settled Medina after Azd's migration from Yemen. The author transmits that Sulayman al-Nadwi preferred that the Ansar of Aws and Khazraj descend from the house of Nabit ibn Isma'il, not Qahtan. And the author takes Aws and Khazraj as his example that rivalry in honor often ended in wars between tribes sharing one father.
