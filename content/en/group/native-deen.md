## Overview
Native Deen is an American Muslim hip-hop trio from Washington, D.C., founded in 2000. It sings faith-based lyrics, often without accompaniment, and is known for the albums 'Deen of Native' and 'Not Afraid to Stand Alone'. {cite:wikidata}
