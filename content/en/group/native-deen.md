## Summary
Native Deen is an American group from the Washington, D.C. area that combines hip-hop and R&B styles with lyrics grounded in Islam; "deen" in its name is the Arabic word for religion or way of life. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en} {cite:wikidata-native-deen} Its members, Joshua Salaam, Naeem Muhammad and Abdul-Malik Ahmad, were born and raised in the District of Columbia, identify themselves as Muslim and wear kufis and shalwar kameez. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en}

## Origins and style
The three met through Muslim Youth of North America; each appeared on MYNA RAPS, an album showcasing young Muslim songwriters, and they joined together after performing as solo acts. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en} Their songs aim to help young people keep their faith amid daily pressures and are counted as "positive hip-hop"; the music uses drums, synthesised instruments and vocals, and never wind, string or metal instruments, following, the article says, the view of most scholars on the matter. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en}

## Concerts and charity
The group performs at Islamic conferences, fundraisers and Ramadan and Eid gatherings, is well known among Muslim youth in North America and has toured abroad. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en} At Islamic Relief's third "Evening of Inspiration" in Costa Mesa, California, Naeem Muhammad narrated a slideshow of children in need and said the charity helps the most vulnerable people from all walks of life. {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en}

## Albums
The group has three albums: Deen You Know (2005, Jamal Records), Not Afraid to Stand Alone (2007) and The Remedy (2011). {cite:wikipedia-native-deen-en}
