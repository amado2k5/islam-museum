## Overview
Banu Kilab ibn Rabi'a ibn Amir ibn Sa'sa'a, a tribe of Mudar. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Dwellings
They spread between al-Jazira and Syria on the bank of the Euphrates, and their pastures were in the outskirts of Aleppo. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Power
They were like subjects of the Banu Hamdan, paying tribute. Then their affairs grew strong, and Salih ibn Mirdas of their number founded the Mirdasid state in Aleppo. Among their sub-branches were Banu Khafaja and Banu Ghayr. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
