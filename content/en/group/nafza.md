## Overview
Nafza are a Berber tribe, remnants of the Barnas. Ibn Khaldun says that their homelands were the Atlas of the far Maghrib and the land between it and the coast. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In Ibn Khaldun
He mentions that among them was al-Randi, whom the Ghumara made their ruler when they apostatised and drove out Salih ibn Mansur before they repented. Among them was Zaynab bint Ishaq al-Nafzawiyya, famous for her beauty and leadership, wife of the emirs of the Maghrib. Their affairs continued in their mountains until the Almoravid state and after it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
