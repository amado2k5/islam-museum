## Summary
Diyanet TV is a Turkish television station owned and operated by the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Turkey. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-en} It broadcasts in Turkish, and its former names are TRT Anadolu and TRT Diyanet. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-en} It is catalogued here as a broadcaster only, with no endorsement language. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-tr}

## Founding and broadcast
The channel was founded in 2012 by TRT and the Presidency of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Turkey. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-tr} Its test broadcasts began on 24 June 2012 and its regular broadcasts on 8 July 2013. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-tr} Its headquarters are in Çankaya, Ankara, and its owner is the Presidency of Religious Affairs. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-tr} The 16 May 2009 date in the English Wikipedia article belongs to its TRT Anadolu predecessor lineage and is kept separate from the founding of Diyanet TV itself in 2012. {cite:wikipedia-diyanet-tv-en}
