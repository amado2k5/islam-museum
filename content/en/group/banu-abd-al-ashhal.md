## Overview
A branch of [[group/al-aws|al-Aws]]: Banu Abd al-Ashhal ibn Jusham ibn al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj al-Asghar. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

## Their homes in Medina
They settled their homes south of the house of Banu Zafar, at the edge of the eastern Harra in [[place/medina|Medina]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204} They built fortified houses (utum) called "Waqim", which belonged to Hudayr ibn Simak, and the district took its name from it, and another called "al-Ra'al", named after a rock, the mother of Banu Abd al-Ashhal. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

## The war with Banu Haritha
There was a war between them and [[group/banu-haritha-al-aws|Banu Haritha]], in which they drove them out of the shared house, and those moved to another part of Yathrib. Al-Samhudi mentions that they took part in [[event/yawm-buath|the day of Bu'ath]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

## Their rank among the Ansar houses
In the excellence of the sound report, they are second among the houses of the Ansar. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3807}
