## Overview
Tanukh is a tribe whose lineage al-Mas'udi gives as follows: Tanukh ibn Malik ibn Fahm ibn Taym al-Lat ibn al-Azd ibn Wabara ibn Tha'laba ibn Hulwan ibn Imran ibn al-Hafi ibn Qudaa ibn Malik ibn Himyar. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.425}

## Their kings
Al-Mas'udi reports that the first to rule over Tanukh was al-Nu'man ibn Amr ibn Malik, then his son Amr, then al-Hawari ibn al-Nu'man. He says that no one else ruled over Tanukh besides them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.425}

Then Salih came to Syria, overcame Tanukh and became Christian. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.425}
