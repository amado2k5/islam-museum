## Summary
A clan from whom Ibn Ishaq names Malik ibn al-Dukhshum and Abu Khaythama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162, 174}

## In this batch
Ibn Ishaq traces [[person/malik-ibn-al-dukhshum|Malik ibn al-Dukhshum]] to Banu Salim ibn Awf, saying they were his fellows — among them the mosque was burned and demolished when the two men arrived. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} In batch 6 he traces to them [[person/abu-khaythama|Abu Khaythama]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
