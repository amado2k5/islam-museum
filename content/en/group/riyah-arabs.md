## Overview
Riyah ibn Hilal is a branch of [[group/banu-hilal|Banu Hilal]] ibn Amir. Ibn Khaldun describes them as among the most numerous Arab peoples in the Maghrib. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.478}

## Their homelands
Their lands were in the regions of Constantine, al-Salam and al-Zab. Among them were Utba in the regions of Bejaia, and a great many in the far Maghrib. Among them were the Banu Ali, emirs of Gabes. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.478}

Ibn Khaldun quotes Ibn Hazm's view of them: that they were among the branches of Banu Hilal "who corrupted Ifriqiya". This is Ibn Hazm's judgement as Ibn Khaldun relays it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.478}
