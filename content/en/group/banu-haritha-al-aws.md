## Overview
A branch of [[group/al-aws|al-Aws]]: Banu Haritha ibn al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj al-Asghar. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

## The shared house and the war
They first settled with [[group/banu-abd-al-ashhal|Banu Abd al-Ashhal]] at the edge of the eastern Harra, and built the fortified house called "al-Musayyar". Then war broke out between them, in which Simak ibn Rafi' was killed by Mas'ud Abu Mahisa al-Haritha. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

Their opponents drove them out to the land of Banu Sulaym. They settled in [[place/khaybar|Khaybar]] for about a year, then reconciled through the mediation of al-Shifra'. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

## The second house
They refused to return to the shared house, and settled in a part of Yathrib west of the shrine of Hamza. This was their dwelling when Islam came. They built the fortified house "al-Rayyan" near their mosque. On the night of Uhud the Prophet spent the night at the houses of the two sheikhs among them. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}
