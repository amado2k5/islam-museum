## Overview
Adwan was a tribe that, under the rule of [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] over Mecca, had the right of departure (ifada) from Jam' to [[place/mina|Mina]] on the morning of the sacrifice. It was the second of three privileges that remained to the tribes of [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]] alongside the right of [[group/sufa|Sufa]] and the postponement of the sacred months by Banu Tamim ibn Adi. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.29} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/119-122}

## In the time of Qusayy
[[person/qusayy-ibn-kilab|Qusayy]] confirmed the Nasa'a, the family of Safwan, Adwan and Murra ibn Awf in the offices they held, because he regarded this as a religious matter in itself that should not be changed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.30} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/124-125} See [[concept/manasib-quraysh|the offices of Quraysh]].

## Not covered here

## For young visitors
Adwan was a tribe that led the pilgrims in the morning from Muzdalifa to Mina in the pre-Islamic period.
