## Overview
Yam is a Yemeni tribe the book lists in the delegation of [[place/najran|Najran]] to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.66} and which met him, with Wadi'a, Shakir, Thaqif and the Ahlaf, on his reaching upper Najran and pledged to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The editor's footnote, citing the Mu'jam al-buldan, says Yam is "a tribe of Yemen, to which a district of Yemen on the right of Sanaa was added". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} In the events of Ramadan 284 AH the text says al-Hadi's governor at Najran flogged a man of Yam for drinking wine with eighty lashes and he died of the penalty; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} this is a Zaydi report attributing the act to the governor, and the museum gives no ruling on it. See [[event/al-hadi-expedition-to-daa-of-shakir-and-return-to-saada-284]].
