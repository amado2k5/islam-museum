## Summary
Warbaahinta Nolosha Islaamka — Somali for "Media of Islamic Life", and the channel styles its own name with the double-a spelling "Warbaahinta" — is a Canada-based Somali-language YouTube channel aggregating lectures and lessons by Somali scholars alongside nasheed. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka} The channel (@warbaahintanoloshaislaamka7774) counted 282,000 subscribers, with 43,769,442 total views across 6,943 videos, by vidIQ's count as of 8 November 2026 — dated platform figures. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka} The channel names no individual author or operator in any source read, and the museum records it as a channel brand rather than a person. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka}

## The channel
vidIQ records the channel as started on 20 February 2018 with its country set to Canada, ranked #269,881 worldwide and #2,950 in Canada by subscribers. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka} The content sampled on the channel is Somali-language lectures (muxaadaro) by many scholars — among them Maxamuud Xaliiki and Xuseen Cali Jabuuti — together with nasheed, and the same speakers are carried by the scene's other aggregator channels, so carriage is not affiliation in either direction. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka} The channel is active, with about 461,000 views in the 28 days to the read. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka} The museum records no founder and no parent organisation for it, because none is documented in the sources read, and no carried speaker's lectures are converted into audience credit for that speaker or into a graph relation. {cite:vidiq-warbahinta-nolosha-islaamka}
