## Summary
The Knights of St John, the crusading military order based on Rhodes in the early Ottoman era {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.

## A precious hostage
Al-Salabi records that Prince [[person/jem-sultan|Cem]] sought refuge with them after his defeats by [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]]; his compact with their Grand Master was broken under Bayezid's pressure, and Cem became their prisoner — a hostage that bought privileges now from Bayezid, now from his Cairo backers — until they sold him to Pope [[person/innocent-viii|Innocent VIII]] for a great price {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.
