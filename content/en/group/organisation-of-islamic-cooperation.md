## Overview
An intergovernmental international organisation of states with Muslim-majority populations, whose General Secretariat is based in Jeddah. The organisation describes itself as "the collective voice of the Muslim world". {cite:oic-official-site}

## Headquarters
The organisation states on its official website that its address is in Jeddah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. {cite:oic-official-site}
