## Summary
The last and wealthiest of Medina's Jewish tribes, allies of al-Aws; they broke the pact at the Trench by treating with the Confederates, so the Prophet besieged them twenty-five nights until they submitted to the ruling of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh in 5 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}.

Banu Qurayza were the last Jewish tribe in Medina, besieged after the treachery of the Trench.
