## Summary
The Department of Islamic Development Malaysia, popularly known as JAKIM, is a federal government agency that administers Islamic affairs in Malaysia. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en} It was established on 1 January 1997 to take over the powers and role of the Islamic Affairs Division of the Prime Minister's Department. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en}

## Halal
Its Policy Sector includes a Halal Management division, and on 1 October 2003 a new standardised halal certification logo was introduced for all states. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en} The Secretary of the Malaysian Halal Council, responsible for recognising foreign halal certification bodies, sits directly under its director-general. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en}

## Its other work
The department plans the development of Islamic affairs, formulates their policy and coordinates enforcement. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en} Its Broadcasting division produces religious programming for Radio Televisyen Malaysia, including Salam FM, launched on 15 February 2009. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en} In March 2022 Hakimah Mohd Yusoff became its first woman director-general. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en}

## The debate over its founding
G25, a group of former senior civil servants, criticised its establishment as not aligned with the constitution. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en} The former prime minister Mahathir Mohamad defended it as set up to bring the government in line with Islamic teachings, and the former religious-affairs minister Mujahid Yusof Rawa supported that view. {cite:wikipedia-jakim-en}
