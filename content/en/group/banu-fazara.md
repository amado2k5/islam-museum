## Overview
A tribe of [[group/ghatafan|Ghatafan]], whose leaders were Banu Badr. Ibn Ishaq mentions them in [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-banu-fazara-and-umm-qirfa|the expedition of Zayd ibn Haritha]]: Zayd met them at Wadi al-Qura, some of his companions were struck and he was carried away wounded. He swore not to wash from a state of major impurity until he had raided them, so the Prophet sent him against them, and he killed them at Wadi al-Qura. [[person/umm-qirfa|Umm Qirfa]] and [[person/masada-ibn-hakama|Masada ibn Hakama]] were killed. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/265} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266} Among Fazara was [[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna ibn Hisn]], whom Ibn Ishaq mentions in other delegations and expeditions. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/269}
