## Overview
"Al-Ahlaf" is the name of a group that appears in Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq among those who came to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] from [[place/najran|Najran]], {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.66} and who met him, with Wadi'a, Shakir, Thaqif and Yam, on his reaching upper Najran and pledged to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The editor gives no definition of it in the footnotes as he does for Wadi'a, Shakir and Yam, so no descent or place is attributed to it here, and it is not to be confused with other "Ahlaf" in other contexts. See [[event/al-hadi-at-najran-oath-between-hamdan-and-banu-al-harith-284]].
