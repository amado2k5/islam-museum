## Overview
A branch of [[group/al-khazraj|al-Khazraj]]: Banu Bayada ibn Amir ibn Zurayq. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.216-220}

## Their homes
They settled in the western Harra, north of Banu Salim and south of Banu Mazin, in [[place/medina|Medina]]. Their homes held nineteen fortified houses, thirteen of which belonged to Banu Umayya ibn Amir ibn Bayada alone. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.216-220}

In the year 160 AH the register of the Ansar was transferred to them. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.216-220}

## Fighting with Banu Zurayq
Their homes included Banu Zurayq, Banu Habib, Banu Adhara, Banu al-Layn and Banu Ajda'. When Habib ibn Abd Haritha asked them to take up arms, they killed him. Then two branches of them fought [[group/banu-zurayq|Banu Zurayq]] in a garden called "the Garden of Death". {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.216-220}

Al-Samhudi cites a saying about al-Aws and al-Khazraj: that Banu Bayada, Banu Zurayq and Banu Ghafr were the slowest to retreat and the quickest to return to the attack. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.216-220}
