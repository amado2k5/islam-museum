## Overview
A tribe from [[place/najran|Najran]]. The scholars say that Najran is a region between Yemen and Hajar. They embraced Islam at the hands of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] in the year 10 AH, without fighting. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/239}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
The Prophet sent Khalid to them in Rabi' al-Akhir or Jumada al-Ula of the year 10 AH, and ordered him to invite them to Islam three times before fighting them. Khalid sent riders in every direction calling out, "People, embrace Islam and you will be safe", and the people embraced Islam. Khalid stayed among them teaching them Islam, the Book of God and the Sunna of His Prophet. See [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|the mission of Khalid to Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/239} Then Khalid brought their delegation to the Prophet: [[person/qays-ibn-al-husayn-dhu-al-ghussa|Qays ibn al-Husayn Dhu al-Ghussa]], [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-madan|Yazid ibn Abd al-Madan]], Yazid ibn al-Muhajjal, Abd Allah ibn Qurad al-Ziyadi, Shaddad ibn Abd Allah al-Qanani, and Amr ibn Abd Allah al-Dababi. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/240}

When the Prophet saw them he said, "Who are these people, who look like the men of India?" He asked them how they had prevailed over those who fought them in the pre-Islamic period. They said: "We used to gather and not separate, and we did not begin any man with injustice." He said: "You have spoken truly." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/240} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/241} He appointed Qays ibn al-Husayn over them, and they returned in the rest of Shawwal or early Dhu al-Qa'da. They did not remain after their return for more than four months before the Prophet died. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/241} After their delegation, the Prophet sent [[person/amr-ibn-hazm|Amr ibn Hazm]] to them to teach them the religion and collect their alms, and he wrote for him [[work/charge-to-amr-ibn-hazm|a letter of instructions]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/241}
