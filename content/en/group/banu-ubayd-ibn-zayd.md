## Summary
A branch of Banu Amr ibn Awf that Ibn Ishaq mentions in Khidham ibn Khalid's lineage. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- It appears as "of Banu Ubayd ibn Zayd, one of Banu Amr ibn Awf"; the name surfaced as "Unayd" in the machine reading and was corrected to "Ubayd" after checking the page image (p. 174).
