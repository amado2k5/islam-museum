## Summary
WAZ VISION BD is a YouTube aggregator channel publishing waz in Bengali; its channel country is set to the United States, so it is operated from the diaspora. {cite:vidiq-waz-vision-bd} Its channel (@wazvisionbd) counted about 768 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-waz-vision-bd} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, and the country setting is a channel fact, not a verified residence for any person. {cite:vidiq-waz-vision-bd}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 1,944 videos and about 127 million total views for the channel, whose catalogue holds recordings by many speakers, among them Allama Lutfur Rahman. {cite:vidiq-waz-vision-bd} It is an aggregator of other speakers' work, gathering Bangladeshi mahfils for a diaspora audience: no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-waz-vision-bd}
