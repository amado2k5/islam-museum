## Overview
Banu Muawiya ibn Harb are a clan of the Arabs among whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stopped on the road to Yemen. [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says he asked some of them about their descent and was told they were a clan of Qays called Banu Muawiya ibn Harb; he then recalled his grandfather Ubaydallah ibn al-Abbas's report about the man of Yemen and knew that this was the master of the affair. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.39} Al-Hadi spoke to them, reminded them of the days of God and asked for their support, and some of them went out with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.39} When he reached Sa'da he had fewer than fifty men, counting those who followed him from this clan and from others on the way. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.42}

This link between the people who told him and the report ascribed to his grandfather is the narrator's own. See [[concept/reports-foretelling-the-man-of-yemen-in-sirat-al-hadi]].
