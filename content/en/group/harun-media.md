## Summary
Harun Media (Amharic: ሃሩን ሚዲያ) is an Ethiopian Amharic dawah media outlet publishing lectures and event coverage — Friday khutbahs, scholars' lectures, funerals and conferences. {cite:youtube-harun-media} {cite:vidiq-harun-media} Its main channel is its Facebook page (facebook.com/haruntube), which counted about 570 thousand followers by Facebook post metadata on a post dated 1 March 2026, read by the wave harvest on 7 November 2026. {cite:facebook-harun-media} Its YouTube channel (@haruntube2) counted 398,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-harun-media}

## Content and platforms
The YouTube channel was created on 2 January 2017, and vidIQ records 4,224 videos and 41,882,793 total views for it; the channel's country setting is the United States, while its content and audience are Ethiopian and work in Amharic. {cite:vidiq-harun-media} Its most-viewed uploads include a Friday khutbah by Ustaz Yasin Nuru from January 2024, a lecture by Ustaz Nuru Turki, and funeral coverage of the Badr Mosque imam Sheikh Abdu Yasin from January 2024. {cite:vidiq-harun-media} Read on 7 November 2026, the channel itself showed recent uploads branded HarunMedia, among them news reports, a Friday khutbah by Ustaz Kamil Taha and a blood-donation programme. {cite:youtube-harun-media} The outlet's own founding date is not documented in the sources read, and neither is the Facebook page's creation date, so no date of either kind is asserted here. {cite:vidiq-harun-media} {cite:facebook-harun-media}
