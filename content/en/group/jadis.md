## Overview
Jadis is one of the [[group/al-arab-al-baida|perished Arab peoples]], which The Sealed Nectar counts with 'Ad, Thamud, Tasm and Amaliq: the ancient Arabs whose history cannot be known in sufficient detail. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}

## Not covered here
The settlement of Jadis in Yamama and their story with Tasm, according to al-Mas'udi, are in the data of this entry.

## For young visitors
Jadis was an ancient Arab people who lived in Yamama and vanished long before Islam.
