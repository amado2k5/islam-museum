## Overview
The Bektashi order is an Anatolian-Balkan Sufi order traced to Haji Bektash, famous for its Janissary link and its Alid doctrines {cite:birge-bektashi-1937}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni frame:** counted among the tarîqas of the Ottoman realm, with reservations recorded about its heterodox features {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.
- **Academic:** a composite order combining Sufi, Alid and local saint traditions, deeply implanted in the Balkans {cite:birge-bektashi-1937}.
