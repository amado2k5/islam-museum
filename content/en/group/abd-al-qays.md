## Overview
A tribe of Rabi'a in [[place/bahrayn|Bahrain]]; Ibn Ishaq calls [[person/al-jarud-al-abdi|al-Jarud]] "brother of Abd al-Qays". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/221}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
al-Jarud came with [[event/delegation-of-abd-al-qays|the delegation of Abd al-Qays]] to the Prophet, embraced Islam, and his companions embraced it too. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/221} When the Prophet died, those of the tribe who had converted returned to their former religion under [[person/al-gharur-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Gharur ibn al-Mundhir]]; al-Jarud stood up and called them back to Islam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222} Before the conquest of Mecca the Prophet had sent [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-sawa|al-Mundhir ibn Sawa al-Abdi]], who embraced Islam; al-Ala was the Prophet's governor over Bahrain. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}
