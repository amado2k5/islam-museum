## Summary
The Perished Arabs (al-Arab al-Ba'ida) are the first of the three divisions of the Arabs in the historians' classification: ancient peoples about whom sufficient detail of their history cannot be obtained, such as 'Ad, Thamud, Tasm, [[group/jadis|Jadis]], the Amaliq and others {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

## In the historians' classification
Historians divided the Arab peoples, by the lineages they descend from, into three divisions: first the Perished; then the [[group/qahtan|Pure Arabs]] (al-'Arba), descended from Ya'rub ibn Yashjub ibn Qahtan; and then the [[group/adnan|Arabised Arabs]] (al-Musta'riba), descended from the loins of Ishmael {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}. The Sealed Nectar does not go beyond naming the peoples of the Perished. See the general entry [[concept/arab-peoples-before-islam|The location of the Arabs and their peoples]].

## What is not covered here
The accounts of 'Ad and Thamud in the Qur'an, and the accounts of Tasm and Jadis in Yamama as the narrators of history relate them, are set out in their own entries.

## For young visitors
The Perished Arabs were very ancient peoples who lived in the Arabian Peninsula and then vanished. Only a little of their story remains.
