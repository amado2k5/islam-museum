## Overview
Madhhij is one of the best-known branches of the Qahtani [[group/kahlan|Kahlan]] in The Sealed Nectar's count, with Hamdan, Anmar, Tayy, Kinda, Lakhm, Judham and al-Azd. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/11-13}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography (volume 4)
The Prophet appointed [[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk al-Muradi]] "over Murad, Zubayd and all of Madhhij", and sent Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As with him to collect the alms. Ibn Ishaq counts [[group/murad|Murad]] and [[group/banu-zubayd|Zubayd]] among Madhhij. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}

## Not covered here
The Sealed Nectar does not go beyond counting Madhhij among the branches of Kahlan. Its lineage according to al-Mas'udi is in the data of this entry.

## For young visitors
Madhhij is a large Yemeni tribe of Qahtan.
