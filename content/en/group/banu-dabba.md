## Overview
Banu Dabba ibn Ud ibn Tabikha ibn Ilyas ibn Mudar: a Mudari tribe, one of the sons of al-Rabab. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461-465}

## "O family of Khindif"
They were the first to call "O family of Khindif" when Banu Asad called on them for help on the day of al-Nisar, and that was the first time they took the name of Khindif. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461-465}

## The day of al-Shaqiqa
Among their battles was the day of al-Shaqiqa, in which they killed Bistam ibn Qays, chief of Shayban. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461-465}
