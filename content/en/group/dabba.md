## Summary
Banu Dabba ibn Udd ibn Tabikha ibn Ilyas ibn Mudar — a northern Mudar clan between the Hijaz, Iraq and al-Sham; among them al-Harith ibn Zalim al-Murri and the Bedouins of the Syrian steppe {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

## Lineage and home
النسب والموطن: ضبة بن أد بن طابخة؛ دارهم بوادي القرى وتيماء والجوف إلى مشارف الشام {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.
