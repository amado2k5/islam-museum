## Summary

The Hazret Sultan Mosque channel is the official YouTube channel of the Hazret Sultan Mosque in Astana, Kazakhstan's national mosque, publishing at @aziretsultanmosque {cite:wikipedia-hazret-sultan-mosque-kk} {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}. By vidIQ's count, read on 5 November 2026, it had about 406 thousand subscribers, 6,562 videos and some 57 million total views — figures that are dated platform measurements and change over time {cite:vidiq-hazret-sultan-channel}.

## The mosque and its channel

The mosque stands on Tauelsizdik Avenue in Astana; it was built in 2010–2012 and inaugurated on 6 July 2012, with room for 5,000 to 10,000 worshippers {cite:wikipedia-hazret-sultan-mosque-kk}. Its YouTube channel was created on 13 June 2012, in the weeks before that inauguration {cite:vidiq-hazret-sultan-channel}. The channel is the outward face of an in-house media operation: the mosque's third floor houses the Hikmet television and radio studio and the editorial office of Muslim.kz {cite:ihsan-hazret-sultan-studio}, and older videos on the channel carry Muslim.kz branding {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}. The same handle runs the mosque's accounts on Telegram, TikTok, Threads and Instagram {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}.

## Content

The channel publishes the mosque's teaching output: Qur'an and tajwid lessons, including an Arabic-and-Qur'an alphabet course, verse-by-verse tafsir series, fiqh talks and sermons {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}. Its named series include a multi-part lecture course by Erbolat Zhusipov, whom the Kazakh Wikipedia lists as the mosque's imam {cite:wikipedia-hazret-sultan-mosque-kk} {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}, and talks by the mosque's deputy imam Tamirlan Darkhanuly {cite:youtube-hazret-sultan-channel}.
