## Summary
Labbaik Media Cell is the media cell of Tehreek-e-Labbaik Pakistan (TLP) and the party's YouTube channel under the handle @LabbaikMediaCell313. {cite:youtube-labbaik-media-cell} The channel describes itself as the social media network of Tehreek-e-Labbaik Pakistan, which it describes as an Islamist political party in Pakistan. {cite:youtube-labbaik-media-cell} The YouTube channel was created on 26 April 2021, and it counted about 192,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count updated on 6 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-labbaik-media-cell}

## The channel
The channel publishes the speeches and bayans of the party's leaders in Urdu; its own keywords name Allama Khadim Hussain Rizvi and Allama Saad Hussain Rizvi, and its most-viewed videos are Saad Hussain Rizvi bayans from 2022–23, the most-viewed being a Pindigheb bayan at about 431,000 views. {cite:youtube-labbaik-media-cell} {cite:vidiq-labbaik-media-cell} vidIQ records 909 videos and about 20.63 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Pakistan. {cite:vidiq-labbaik-media-cell} The channel was still uploading in 2025: its page lists its live stream of the central Eid Milad un Nabi rally in Lahore as streamed one year before it was read on 7 November 2026, which dates that stream to the Eid Milad un Nabi of September 2025, alongside live streams from Masjid Wazir Khan in Lahore from the same period. {cite:youtube-labbaik-media-cell} Tehreek-e-Labbaik Pakistan itself is a separate and older body than the channel: the party was founded on 1 August 2015 by Khadim Hussain Rizvi at Nishtar Park, Karachi, is led by Saad Hussain Rizvi, and was banned on 23 October 2025. {cite:wikipedia-tehreek-e-labbaik-pakistan-en}
