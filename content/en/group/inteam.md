## Summary
Inteam is a Malaysian nasyid group established in 1998 by four students at the UIA Matriculation Center in Malaysia, among whose earliest members were Syah, Hazamin, Izzat Mirwana and Kamarulzaman, also known as KZ. {cite:mawlidfest-inteam} The group performs nasheed songs in Malay, English and Arabic, and was introduced through the song 'Kasih Kekasih' around the year 2000 under the management of Hijjaz Records. {cite:mawlidfest-inteam} Its YouTube channel, Inteam Digital (@inteamdigital), counted 1,140,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as updated on 10 November 2026, with 354,500,000 views and 1,725 videos. {cite:vidiq-inteam-digital}

## Work and reach
The record rests on the group's own label channel: the 1,140,000 subscribers, some 354.5 million views and 1,725 videos are vidIQ's dated platform count for @inteamdigital as of 10 November 2026, not a figure the museum verifies. {cite:vidiq-inteam-digital} The group is catalogued as a group in its own right; its members are persons only on their own counts, and no member is credited with the channel's audience here. {cite:vidiq-inteam-digital}

## Limits of the record
The 1998 founding and the earliest membership rest on the Mawlid Festival's artist profile alone, the only founding source read in this run. {cite:mawlidfest-inteam} Secondary audience figures exist for the group's other accounts — an Instagram figure recorded in April 2026 and a stale Spotify figure — but neither is relied on here, and the dated vidIQ count above is the record's only audience figure. {cite:vidiq-inteam-digital}
