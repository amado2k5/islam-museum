## Overview
Sanhaja is, according to Ibn Khaldun, one of the three greatest Berber peoples, with Lamta and Hawwara. It is among the [[group/berbers|Berbers]]. Its homeland is the desert and its mountains. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}

## Their branches
Among them are the veiled ones: the tribes of Lamtuna, Jaddala and Masufa, who were the people of the Almoravid state. Among them are the Banu Ziri and the Banu Hammad, kings of Ifriqiya and al-Qal'a. Ibn Khaldun says that their remnants in the desert were still in their first condition until his time. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1657}
