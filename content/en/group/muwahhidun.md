## Overview
The Almohads were the call of the Masmuda, established by Muhammad ibn Tumart, known as al-Mahdi, in the cause of monotheism and in fighting those he named the anthropomorphists. The state of Abd al-Mu'min ibn Ali then arose on this call and ruled both Maghribs. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1668-1669}

## The name
Ibn Khaldun says that Ibn Tumart called his followers the Almohads, as a pointed allusion to the Almoravids of Lamtuna. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1668-1669}
