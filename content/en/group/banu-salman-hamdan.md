## Overview
Banu Salman are a people of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], by the book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.98} The narrator says they disliked the settlement between [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], lest al-Hadi rule them and take the alms from them, and that they took [[person/arhab-ibn-al-daam|Arhab ibn al-Da'am]] up with them to [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] and then entered [[place/athafit|Athafit]], where Abu Umar was killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.98} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} These are the Zaydi narrator's judgments of his opponents and are attributed to him. The book gives no descent for them and no lands beyond "the land of Banu Salman" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.98}, so no origin or place is claimed for them.
