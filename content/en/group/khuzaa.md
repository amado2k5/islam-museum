## Summary
A Tihama tribe neighbouring [[place/mecca|Mecca]], kin by marriage to the Prophet's house; it entered the Prophet's pact and covenant on the day of the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|al-Hudaybiyya peace]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}. [[group/banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] attacked it at [[place/al-watir|al-Watir]] with Quraysh's backing; it called Medina for aid — and its plea was among what stirred the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest of Mecca]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31-37}.

## The old pact
Khuza'a's pact with the Prophet's house is old: Abd Manaf's mother — Hashim's father — and Qusayy's mother Fatima bint Sa'd ibn Sayl were of Khuza'a, so lineage joins the two houses {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}. In [[person/amr-ibn-salim|Amr ibn Salim]]'s plea-verses: "the pact of our father and his father of old" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}.

## The al-Watir attack
During the al-Hudaybiyya truce [[person/nawfal-ibn-muawiya|Nawfal ibn Mu'awiya]] ambushed them by night among [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] at al-Watir; they struck a man of them and pursued them until they took refuge in the houses of [[person/budayl-ibn-warqa|Budayl ibn Warqa']] and their client [[person/rafi-mawla-khuzaa|Rafi']] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32-33}. Quraysh supplied the attackers with weapons {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}.

## The plea
[[person/amr-ibn-salim|Amr ibn Salim]] went out to Medina and stood before the Prophet pleading with his verses; then Budayl came in a band telling him of the pact's breach {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36-37}. Ibn Ishaq said: "that was among what stirred the conquest of Mecca" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}.

## For young visitors
A tribe living near Mecca with an old pact with the Muslims' prophet. Attacked by night while at peace, its men went to Medina seeking help — and its poet called out the most famous plea-verses.
