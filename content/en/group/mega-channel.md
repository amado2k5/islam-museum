## Summary
Mega Channel is a Bangladeshi YouTube aggregator channel for pure Bengali waz, its most-viewed catalogue dating to 2021–2024. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel} Its channel (@megachannelwaz) counted about 730 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no individual is asserted for it here. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel}

## Programmes and platforms
The channel was created on YouTube on 19 April 2017, and vidIQ records 1,583 videos and 66,231,151 total views for it; its channel country is set to Bangladesh and it ranks #3,511 in Religion. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel} Its top videos are lectures by Shoaib Ahmed Ashrafi, Amir Hamza, Maulana Mahmudul Hasan and Abul Kalam Azad Bashar, and views still accrue on that 2021–2024 catalogue — about 121,000 in the 28 days before the read — while upload recency beyond the catalogue was not separately evidenced in this pass and is not stated here. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel} It is an archive-style aggregator of other speakers' work: no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-mega-channel}
