## Overview
Hawwara are branches of the Baranis, among the Berber tribes. They were settled nomads, and some of them crossed the sand into desert lands. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Their homelands were on the side of Tripoli and what lies beyond it in Barqa, and the Hawwara mountain, named after them, in Ifriqiya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun says that among them was Abd al-Wahid ibn Yazid, who rose against Handhala ibn Safwan together with Ukasha al-Fazari, and defeated and killed them in the year 124 AH. Yahya ibn Funas of them rose against Yazid ibn Hatim in the year 156 AH. Mujahid ibn Muslim of them was among the commanders of Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib. Named men of them also crossed to al-Andalus with Tariq. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
