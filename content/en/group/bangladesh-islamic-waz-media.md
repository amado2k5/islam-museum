## Summary
Bangladesh Islamic Waz Media is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@bangladeshislamicwazmedia) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures and publishes them at scale. It counted about 529 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-bangladesh-islamic-waz-media} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-bangladesh-islamic-waz-media}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 4,179 videos and about 25.9 million total views for the channel. {cite:vidiq-bangladesh-islamic-waz-media} The channel carries lectures by a range of Bangladeshi preachers, among them Mufti Amir Hamza, whose videos appeared in its September 2024 catalogue; its carriage of a preacher's lectures makes none of its audience that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-bangladesh-islamic-waz-media}
