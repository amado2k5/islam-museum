## Overview
Banu Sarim are a people "of the notables of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] and its men", in the narrator's words. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} They stood on the right of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi's]] army in the war at [[place/athafit|Athafit]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101}, and at a pond of theirs he spent the night of Friday and the morning of Friday. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} They came to him with Banu Rabi'a asking to raid the villages of [[person/arhab-ibn-al-daam|Arhab]], and he refused. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.105} The book gives no descent for them. See [[event/al-hadi-at-al-darb-and-abu-al-qasim-joins-285]].
