## Summary

Tarbiyah Sunnah Channel is a Bandung-based Indonesian kajian channel on YouTube (@tarbiyahsunnahchannel), published by the Tarbiyah Sunnah foundation. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} It counted 198,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 15 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} No single presenter-operator could be pinned for it in any source read, and it is recorded with the foundation as its named publisher. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel}

## Work and reach

The YouTube channel was created on 15 February 2013, its channel country is set to Indonesia, and vidIQ records 6,453 videos and 19,780,876 total views for it. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} Its most-viewed uploads carry several different teachers of the foundation's circle — among them Ustadz Gigih Surya Nugraha, in a February 2026 appeal for the foundation's Bandung radio station, and Ustadz Abu Haidar as-Sundawy — the catalogue of a foundation's study sessions, not one presenter's programme. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel}

## Impact and audience

The channel's 198,000 subscribers clear the lane's bar for a group on a current, dated count. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} The audience is the foundation's, and it is credited to no individual teacher whose sessions the channel carries. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} These figures remain dated platform counts, as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel}

## Limits of the record

The channel's name is its own self-description ("Tarbiyah Sunnah"); it is recorded as a name, and it settles nothing about any person or about the foundation beyond what the sources state. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel} No founding date for the foundation is documented in the sources read, so none is given beyond the channel's creation. {cite:vidiq-tarbiyah-sunnah-channel}
