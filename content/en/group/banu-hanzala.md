## Overview
A branch of [[group/banu-tamim|Tamim]]: Banu Hanzala ibn Malik ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## With Malik ibn Nuwayra
The Prophet appointed Malik ibn Nuwayra to collect their alms. When he died, Malik released what he held of the obligatory dues and said to them: "Your wealth is your own affair, Banu Hanzala." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu Qatada al-Ansari testified, in the account of Malik's killing, that they had laid down their weapons and given the call to prayer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sajah
Among them was Sajah bint Aws, or it is said bint al-Harith, who claimed prophethood during the events of the apostasy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
