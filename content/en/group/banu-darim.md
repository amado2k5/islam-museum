## Overview
A branch of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]], as Ibn Hisham gives the lineage: Darim ibn Malik ibn Handhala ibn Malik ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
From Banu Darim came [[person/utarid-ibn-hajib|Utarid ibn Hajib]], [[person/al-aqra-ibn-habis|al-Aqra ibn Habis]] and [[person/al-hutat-ibn-yazid|al-Hutat ibn Yazid]] in [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|the delegation of Banu Tamim]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207} In the verses transmitted for al-Zibriqan, there is a claim that there is nothing in the land of the Hijaz like Darim; the editors note that Darim belongs to Banu Tamim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/211} Hassan, in his reply, addressed them: "Banu Darim, do not boast." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/212} The editors note elsewhere that al-Hutat was of Banu Mujashi', who are of Darim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206}
