## Overview
Anmar is one of the four great tribes that branched from the sons of Nizar ibn Ma'add ibn [[person/adnan|Adnan]]: [[group/iyad|Iyad]], Anmar, [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar|Rabi'a]] and [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}

## Not covered here
The Sealed Nectar also mentions an Anmar among the best-known branches of the Qahtani Kahlan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} This is a different branch with the same name. The book does not detail the history of Anmar ibn Nizar.

## For young visitors
Anmar is a tribe descended from Nizar, the brother of Mudar and Rabi'a.
