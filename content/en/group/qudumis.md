## Overview
The Qudumis are the people of the land of [[place/qudum-yemen|Qudum]]; p. 216 says that a party the narrator calls Kharijis, who did not follow the line of the Prophet's household, was at Qudum {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216}. The two men put out of the prisons of Al Ya'fur went to them, and many fools gathered to them in the narrator's account; [[person/sasaa-ibn-jafar-al-hamdani|Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far]] rose with them at al-Bawn and shared the horses among them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. With the people of al-Masani' they agreed to climb the mountain of Bayt Dhukhar {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}, then entered the village of Shibam on the night of Sunday and broke the prison {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}, and fled when Abu al-Atahiya and Muhammad ibn al-Da'am came down {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. The descriptions are the narrator's and are attributed to him with no verdict from the museum. See [[event/the-qudumis-enter-shibam-break-the-prison-and-ibn-abi-abbad-is-killed-288]].
