## Summary
Ibn Ishaq lists this clan among the Abyssinian emigrants under the lineage "Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Of this clan in the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
**Abyssinian emigrant women:**
- [[person/umm-habiba-bint-abi-sufyan|Umm Habiba bint Abi Sufyan]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## For Sprouts
Banu Umayya, a clan of Quraysh by their line 'Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf'; Ibn Ishaq brings them in the lists of the Abyssinia emigrants.

## For Explorers
Banu Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf, a clan of Quraysh; Ibn Ishaq brings them by their line in the lists of the Abyssinia emigrants, counting among them in that chapter Umm Habiba bint Abi Sufyan of the women who migrated.

## For Scholars
Banu Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf, a clan of Quraysh; Ibn Ishaq brings this clan in the lists of the Abyssinia emigrants by its line 'Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf', counting of it in that chapter Umm Habiba bint Abi Sufyan among the women who migrated.
