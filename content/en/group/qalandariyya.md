## Summary
A Persian term, as The Oxford Dictionary of Islam explains it, for a kind of begging Sufi attached to no particular shaykh or order. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1914}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam explains qalandar as a Persian word for a general kind of begging Sufi attached to no particular shaykh or order. These men, it says, were known for an unkempt appearance and for flouting public opinion. It notes that they had no tangible organization but kept links and rules of their own, and that they were especially known for showing their devotion in public. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1914}
