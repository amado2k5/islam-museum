## Summary
The people of 'Ad in the Qur'an are the nation to whom Hud was sent: they dwelt in the sand-dunes of al-Ahqaf between Yemen and Hadramawt and built Iram of the pillars; they denied their prophet and the barren wind destroyed them in days of ill omen.

## In the texts
God tells them in al-A'raf, Hud, al-Mu'minun, al-Shu'ara', Fussilat, al-Ahqaf, al-Dhariyat, al-Qamar, al-Haqqa and al-Fajr: their strength, their building and their pride; Hud's words to them; and the barren wind that destroyed them seven nights and eight days {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01} {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Sira and history
For the chroniclers 'Ad is of the vanished mother-Arabs: the first 'Ad who were destroyed, and the second 'Ad, Thamud, on one report; they name their kings Shaddad and Shaddid of Iram, and how their wind destroyed them save Hud and those who believed with him {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-farmawi-1997}.

## For younger visitors
The people of 'Ad were the first Arab nation God destroyed; He sent Hud to them, they denied him, and the barren wind took them.

## Notes for researchers
For the chroniclers the people of 'Ad are Iram of the pillars — «have you seen how your Lord dealt with 'Ad, Iram of the pillars?» — so keep the nation and the city apart, and the first 'Ad from Thamud.
