## Overview
Himyar: the most famous of Qahtan's clans along with Kahlan; it prevailed over the kingdom of Saba' around 115 BCE and became Yemen's kings until the Abyssinian occupation.

## Life
In the era from 115 BCE to 300 CE the tribe of Himyar prevailed over Saba''s kingdom, making the town of Raydan its capital in place of Ma'rib — later called Zafar; in this era their decline began as their trade failed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Of Himyar are Yemen's last kings: Tubba' As'ad Abu Karib who brought Judaism into Yemen; his son Yusuf Dhu Nuwas, master of the Najran trench; then Ma'di Yakrib ibn Sayf Dhi Yazan who led Yemen's independence from Abyssinia in 575 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. Himyar's delegations' writs of Islam are recorded in the Sira after Tabuk {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Himyar were Yemen's ancient kings; they ruled after Saba', and among them the unjust king who burned the people of Najran.
