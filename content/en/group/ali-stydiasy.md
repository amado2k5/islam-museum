## Summary

ÁLI stýdıasy ("Ali Studio") is a Kazakh-language Islamic studio channel on YouTube, publishing at @ali_stydiasy. In its own About text it welcomes viewers to "the ALI stydiasy Islamic educational channel", and its video descriptions call it "a platform for spiritual upbringing and useful sermons" {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy-channel}. By vidIQ's count, read on 4 November 2026, it had about 1.09 million subscribers, 10,995 videos and some 298.6 million total views — figures that are dated platform measurements and change over time {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy}.

## Origins

The channel was opened on 9 February 2013, with its country set to Kazakhstan {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy}. Nothing in the channel's published materials names a founder or an operating organisation: the channel presents itself under the studio name alone, and its video descriptions invite viewers to support its development with donations {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy-channel}. The founding recorded for the studio is therefore the channel's own creation.

## Content

ÁLI stýdıasy works as a studio and distribution channel rather than as one preacher's page: its catalogue carries sermons and lessons recorded from many Kazakh preachers. Its most-viewed videos are sermons by Nursultan Rysmaganbet, and the channel is a distribution channel for his preaching, not his property {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy}. The preachers evidenced across its videos and playlists include Abdugappar Smanov, Arman Kuanyshbayev, Nursultan Kasimov, Elbek Tasbolatuly, Erlan Akatay, Zhomart Kersheev, Danabek Alipbayuly and Serikbay kazhy Oraz {cite:youtube-ali-stydiasy-channel}.
