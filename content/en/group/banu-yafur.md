## Overview
Al Ya'fur are a Yemeni family named in the book's accounts of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] and Shibam. Abu al-Atahiya asked al-Hadi to imprison Al Ya'fur and Al Tarif; the two agreed on it and al-Hadi ordered them held. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18}

## In the war against al-Hadi
A man of the line of Abu al-Khayr ibn Ya'fur left Shibam and joined others in an alliance against al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} War then flared between al-Hadi and Al Ya'fur and Al Tarif after he entered Sana'a in Rajab 288 AH; Uthman ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur and As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur took charge, As'ad staying at Shibam, and Abd al-Hakim ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur was in Sana'a. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} See [[group/banu-tarif]].
