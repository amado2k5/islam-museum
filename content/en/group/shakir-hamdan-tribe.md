## Overview
Shakir is a Yemeni tribe of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], as the editor says in a footnote citing the Muntakhabat fi akhbar al-Yaman. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The book lists it in the delegation of [[place/najran|Najran]] to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.66} and it met him at upper Najran and pledged to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} Near Najran was a town called "Da'a" in the land of Shakir; its people had come to al-Hadi at Sa'da and Najran and pledged to uphold the truth, and he sent men with them to collect their alms. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} A man of theirs called "Nu'aym", in a manuscript "Rahim", rose against his agents and al-Hadi went out against him in Ramadan 284 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} See [[event/al-hadi-expedition-to-daa-of-shakir-and-return-to-saada-284]]. It is a Zaydi report, and this record is not to be confused with the Banu Shakir, the astronomers, of another record.
