## Summary
Islamic Waz Bogra is a Bogra-based regional YouTube aggregator channel in Bangladesh publishing Bengali waz mahfils by many speakers. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bogra} Its channel (@islamicwazbograofficial) counted about 4.74 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bogra} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no individual is asserted for it here. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bogra}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 7,403 videos and about 1.14 billion total views for the channel, which ranks #92 among Bangladesh's channels by subscribers; its video listing shows mahfils by many speakers, among them Mawlana Abdus Salam and Golam Rabbani. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bogra} It is a regionally focused aggregator of other speakers' work, publishing at a high rate: no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-bogra}
