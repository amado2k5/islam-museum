## Overview
The people of Khirfan, with the people of [[group/al-subay-arhab|al-Subay']], are a people of Arhab and cousins of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], whom he used for raids and to strengthen himself in his war on [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], by the narrator. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} When al-Hadi arrived he found their villages empty and the people on the mountain tops; he sent them safe-conduct, some elders came down, and the fodder some soldiers had taken was returned. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} They then all came down to him; he gave them safety and had them swear to the right, to stand with him, to hear and obey, and they and their governors swore. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} This angered al-Da'am, since they had been his support. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} The text gives no descent for them and the museum asserts nothing more.
