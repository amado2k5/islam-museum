## Summary
Siyer TV is the YouTube channel (@siyertv) of the Siyer Foundation (Siyer Vakfı) in Istanbul, publishing sirah and history lectures and series. {cite:vidiq-siyer-tv} The channel counted 719,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 13 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-siyer-tv}

## The foundation and Muhammed Emin Yıldırım
A published author biography of Muhammed Emin Yıldırım states that in October 2010 he pioneered the establishment of the Siyer Foundation in Eyüp, Istanbul, and that his weekly lectures there are published on the Siyer TV website. {cite:siyer-publications-yildirim-bio} That association is stated in prose only, and no founded_by relation is written in this record's data, because Yıldırım's person record does not exist. {cite:siyer-publications-yildirim-bio} The channel's carriage of his lectures creates no other relation and assigns none of his audience to the channel. {cite:vidiq-siyer-tv}

## Reach and limits of the record
vidIQ recorded 148,084,156 total views and 4,897 videos for the channel as of 13 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-siyer-tv} The channel itself is the foundation's own YouTube presence, carrying its uploads under its name. {cite:youtube-siyer-tv} These figures are dated platform counts as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-siyer-tv} No founding date is written for the channel itself, because no source read documents one; the October 2010 date belongs to the foundation as stated in its publisher's biography. {cite:siyer-publications-yildirim-bio}
