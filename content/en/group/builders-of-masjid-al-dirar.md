## Summary
Twelve men whom Ibn Ishaq names as builders of the mosque the Prophet ordered demolished; they are of Medina's people and its quarters. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## The members
Khidham ibn Khalid, Tha'laba ibn Hatib, Mu'attib ibn Qushayr, Abu Habiba ibn al-Az'ar, Abbad ibn Hunayf, Jariya ibn Amir and his two sons Mujammi' and Zayd, Nabtal ibn al-Harith, Bahzaj, Bijad ibn Uthman, and Wadi'a ibn Thabit. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} The table is in the [[dataset/builders-of-masjid-al-dirar|dataset]].

## The group's description
Ibn Ishaq describes the mosque — on the Prophet's tongue — as "the mosque whose people do harm", and mentions that a verse descended concerning them; the judgement on their intentions in the book is reported from the account, not the museum's own word. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq's phrase "its builders" enumerates twelve men (p. 174); the name count matches if Jariya and his two sons are counted as three.
- Wadi'a ibn Thabit: "of Banu Umayya [ibn Zayd]" — the bracketed addition is from manuscript (a) per the editors.
