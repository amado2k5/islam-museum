## Overview
Lakhm: the Kahlan clan from which came the house of the Manadhira, kings of al-Hira and Persia's governors over the Arabs of the desert.

## Life
Among the Lakhmis was Nasr ibn Rabi'a, father of the Manadhira kings at al-Hira {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

After Jadhima's reign Sabur appointed over al-Hira Amr ibn Adi al-Lakhmi, and kingship ran in their house over Iraq's Arabs for centuries, serving Persia as a barrier against desert raids and a counterweight to Syria's Arabs, the Byzantines' auxiliaries {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

## For young visitors
Lakhm is the tribe that ruled al-Hira for the Persians; its last king fell in 632 CE before Islam's armies.
