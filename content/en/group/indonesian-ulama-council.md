## Overview
A council gathering scholars of different madhhabs in Indonesia, headed at the time of the visit by Dr Ma'ruf Amin {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 63}; it received the Grand Imam on 23 February 2016, and he praised its experiment in uniting the madhhabs' scholars in one council, calling for a single opinion on each question to lift the questioner's difficulty {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 150}.
