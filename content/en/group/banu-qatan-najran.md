## Overview
Banu Qatan are a group to which the book gives no lineage or home; it says [[person/al-mujahir-ibn-ziyad-al-qatani|al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad]] was of them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} He came with a party of Banu Bishr and sent with some of [[group/banu-abd-al-madan-najran|Banu Abd al-Madan]] to Abu Muhammad, and they pledged support. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} The book's index lists "Banu Qatan" at pp. 147 and 345, and p. 345 has not been read yet. See [[event/pledge-at-najran-and-the-fight-below-minas-286]].
