## Overview
The Shadhili order is among the greatest Sufi orders of the Maghreb and Egypt, traced to Abu'l-Hasan al-Shadhili and carried on by his disciple Abu'l-Abbas al-Mursi {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari} {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
Al-Maqqari calls Abu'l-Abbas al-Mursi "heir of his shaykh al-Shadhili in Sufism, Ash'ari in creed," recording his transmission from him; academic study describes the order's spread through the Maghreb and the wider Islamic world {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari} {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.
