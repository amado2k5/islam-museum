## Overview
Banu Qaynuqa' — the craftsmen and market of Yathrib's Jews, the first of its tribes expelled — the first open confrontation between Medina's state and its Jews after [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]].

## History
Qaynuqa' were the boldest of the Jews in the covenant, companions of the Khazraj Ansar. After Badr came the "market of Qaynuqa'" incident — an affray over a headdress that ended in deaths — and the state besieged them a fortnight until they surrendered {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}. In the Sunni account [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]] intercedes for their release — the Prophet accepting ransom in the form of their expulsion with their wealth, but not their weapons' iron — and they emigrated north to Adhri'at {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

Historians see in the event the internal siege applied to those who refused the Charter of Medina after Badr shifted the balance, more than punishment for the affray itself {cite:watt-1956-medina}.

## Legacy
Qaynuqa' is the first of the three models (Qaynuqa', then al-Nadir, then [[group/banu-qurayza|Qurayza]]) in the treatment of Medina's Jews — a sequence the Sunni tradition reads as graduated treachery and punishment, and historians read as the gradual dismantling of the independent tribes {cite:watt-1956-medina}.
