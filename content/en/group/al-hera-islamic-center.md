## Summary
AL HERA ISLAMIC CENTER is a Bangladeshi Islamic-center-style media operation whose YouTube channel publishes waz mahfil videos, speeches by many scholars and Qur'an recitation by international reciters, according to the channel's published description. {cite:vidiq-al-hera-islamic-center} Its channel (@alheraislamiccenter) counted about 4.94 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-al-hera-islamic-center} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no individual is asserted for it here. {cite:vidiq-al-hera-islamic-center}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 10,412 videos and about 1.06 billion total views for the channel, which ranks #84 among Bangladesh's channels by subscribers; this wave's harvest reports it as operating since 2015 without capturing an exact creation date, so no founding date is stated here. {cite:vidiq-al-hera-islamic-center} Its content is in Bengali with Arabic recitation, and it is an aggregate of mahfils and speeches by other preachers, scholars and reciters: no speaker or reciter it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any of theirs. {cite:vidiq-al-hera-islamic-center}
