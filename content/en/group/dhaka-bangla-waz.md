## Summary
Dhaka Bangla Waz is a YouTube aggregator channel publishing waz in Bengali; it carries Dhaka in its name, while its channel country is set to the United Arab Emirates, making it a diaspora-operated channel. {cite:vidiq-dhaka-bangla-waz} Its channel (@dhakabanglawaz) counted about 875 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-dhaka-bangla-waz} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, and the country setting is a channel fact, not a verified residence for any person. {cite:vidiq-dhaka-bangla-waz}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 1,643 videos and about 175 million total views for the channel, and its video listing shows mahfils by many speakers, among them Hafizur Rahman Siddiqui of Kuakata. {cite:vidiq-dhaka-bangla-waz} It is an aggregator of other speakers' work, gathering Bangladeshi mahfils for a diaspora audience: no speaker it carries is credited with its audience, nor is it credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-dhaka-bangla-waz}
