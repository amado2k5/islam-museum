## Summary
Banu Shayban is a branch of [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr ibn Wa'il]], the tribe that defeated the Persians on [[event/battle-of-dhu-qar|the day of Dhu Qar]]. In al-Raheeq al-Makhtum it was the first day on which the Arabs won against the non-Arabs {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/29-32}.

## The deposit
Their chief was [[person/hani-ibn-masud|Hani' ibn Mas'ud]]. [[person/al-numan-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir]] secretly left his family and property with him before he went to Khosrow. When [[person/iyas-ibn-qabisa|Iyas ibn Qabisa]] demanded the deposit on Khosrow's order, Hani' refused out of pride to hand it over, and the king declared war {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26}.

## The battle
Khosrow's marzbans and his battalions came in Iyas's procession, and a mighty battle took place between the two sides at [[place/dhu-qar|Dhu Qar]]. Banu Shayban prevailed, and the Persians were routed in a shameful defeat {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26}. The author counts Hani's story among the examples of keeping faith in the pre-Islamic era {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.47}.

## For young visitors
Banu Shayban is the tribe that defeated Persia's great army on the day of Dhu Qar. The tradition relates that its chief kept faith with al-Nu'man's trust.
