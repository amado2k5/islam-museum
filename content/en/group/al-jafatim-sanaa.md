## Overview
The [[group/al-jafatim-sanaa|Jafatim]] are the non-Arab troops of Jund Jaftam who were with [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]], who feared they would oppose him if he handed the matter over to al-Hadi; the narrator calls them sinners and wrongdoers and reports at second hand that they took property by force. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} Abu al-Atahiya then ordered them to al-Sirr to lie in ambush, and they believed what he said. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} The museum gives no verdict on the narrator's charges, and the name of the troop is written as printed. See [[event/al-hadi-goes-by-madar-and-kharaqa-to-hadaqan-near-sanaa-288]].
