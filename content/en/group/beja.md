## Overview
The Beja are a people who travel in the desert of Aydhab. Al-Idrisi reports that the people of Aydhab buy from them ghee, honey and milk. An agent from their chief stays with them in the town, and he says that they protect the town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.134-135}
