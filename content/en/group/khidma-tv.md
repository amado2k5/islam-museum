## Summary
Khidma TV is a Tanzanian Swahili dawah media outlet publishing news and sermons of various sheikhs, Friday khutbahs and mosque lectures. {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} {cite:tiktok-khidma-tv} Its main channel is its Facebook page (@MzeewaKhidma), a professional account under the name Khidma TV, which counted 184,577 followers by a direct read of the page itself on 8 November 2026. {cite:facebook-khidma-tv} That direct figure supersedes the wave-196 harvest's earlier figure of about 180,000 followers, which had been read on a third-party post dated January 2026 and was not a direct read. {cite:facebook-khidma-tv}

## Work and reach
The descriptions of the outlet's YouTube channel (@khidmaonlinetv350) list its social accounts together as its own: Facebook /mzeewakhidma, Instagram /khidma_tv, TikTok /khidmatv1 and YouTube /@khidmaonlinetv350. {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} The videos read include a Friday khutbah at Masjid Idirssa in Kariakoo by Sheikh Abdulrahman Kizundu and a live muhadhara at Masjid Songea in Ilala by Sheikh Abdulrahman, and the channel's keyword tags name Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi and Congo. {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} Posts on its TikTok account (@khidmatv1) invite viewers in Swahili to follow it for news and sermons of various sheikhs, under the slogan hashtag #UtumishiKwaWote ("service for all"), and its bio directs viewers to its YouTube channel and gives a Tanzanian contact number with the +255 country code. {cite:tiktok-khidma-tv} No follower count for the YouTube channel or the TikTok account was obtained in this run, so no count is stated for either; the only verified reach figure is the Facebook figure above. {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} {cite:tiktok-khidma-tv}

## Impact and audience
The outlet's audience is primarily Swahili-speaking and East African, as its posts' language, the Dar es Salaam mosques it covers and its channel's geographic tags indicate. {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} The limits of what could be verified are stated plainly: this pass found no Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item for the outlet, no source read documents a founding date for it, and no source read names a separate parent organisation, a legal owner or an individual manager — its identity here rests on its own cross-platform self-presentation, as a media outlet standing under its own brand rather than any one sheikh's page. {cite:facebook-khidma-tv} {cite:youtube-khidma-tv} {cite:tiktok-khidma-tv} It is distinct from the Tanzanian Khidmatulquran Islamic Foundation, the Qur'an-competition foundation; no link between the two appears in any source read. {cite:facebook-khidma-tv}
