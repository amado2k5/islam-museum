## Summary
Hayalhanem is a Turkish Islamic media collective producing animated stories, lessons and short films, and its YouTube channel is @hayalhanem. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem} The channel counted 5,560,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 12 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem} SPEAKRJ independently recorded the same subscriber figure in a dated entry of 12 November 2026, so the subscriber count is corroborated by two independent sources on the same day. {cite:speakrj-hayalhanem}

## Work and reach
vidIQ recorded 1,527,899,907 total views and 1,763 videos for the channel as of 12 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem} The channel itself is the collective's own YouTube presence, carrying its uploads under its name. {cite:youtube-hayalhanem} These figures are dated platform counts as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem}

## Limits of the record
No source read for this record names a specific operator for the collective, so no operator, founder or presenter is named here as an operator. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem} No founding date is written for the collective, because no source read documents one. {cite:youtube-hayalhanem} The collective is catalogued separately from any speaker whose talks appear on its channel, and the channel's carriage of a speaker's talks creates no relation and assigns none of that speaker's audience to it. {cite:vidiq-hayalhanem}
