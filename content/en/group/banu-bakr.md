## Summary
A tribe of [[group/kinana|Kinana]] that entered Quraysh's pact and covenant on the day of the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|al-Hudaybiyya peace]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}. Its clan [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] seized upon the truce and ambushed [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] by night at the water of [[place/al-watir|al-Watir]] — with Quraysh's backing — the breach on which the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest of Mecca]] followed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}.

## The old enmity
Between Banu Bakr and Khuza'a lay a Jahili feud: before Islam Khuza'a killed Banu al-Aswad ibn Razn — Salama, Kulthum and Dhu'ayb — chiefs of Banu al-Dil, at Arafa by the Sanctuary's boundary stones {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31}. Khuza'a also killed the merchant [[person/malik-ibn-abbad-al-hadrami|Malik ibn Abbad al-Hadrami]] and took his wealth; his alliance was with Banu al-Aswad {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31}.

## Its role in the al-Hudaybiyya breach
During the truce [[person/nawfal-ibn-muawiya|Nawfal ibn Mu'awiya al-Dili]] went out among Banu al-Dil — not all of Banu Bakr followed him — and ambushed Khuza'a by night at al-Watir {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}. When his companions appealed to him at the Sanctuary's bounds he refused to turn back, saying: "take your revenge" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}.

## For young visitors
A tribe that had an old feud with its neighbours. When a peace pact came, some of its men seized the chance and attacked their neighbours at night at a well — and that was the start of the whole pact's collapse.
