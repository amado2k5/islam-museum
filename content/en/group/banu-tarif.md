## Overview
Al Tarif are a Yemeni family named in the book together with [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]]: Abu al-Atahiya asked [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] to imprison both, and they were held. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} When al-Da'am and Abu al-Atahiya asked that those in his custody, among them Banu Tarif, be put to death, al-Hadi refused, saying it was not right for him to do so while they were in his custody, and released them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} After he entered Sana'a in Rajab 288 AH the man who led the fighting against him was [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf ibn Tarif]], with Banu Tarif and other Yemenis who backed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}
