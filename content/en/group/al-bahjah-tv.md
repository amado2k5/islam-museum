## Summary

Al-Bahjah TV is the media channel of Al-Bahjah, the educational and dawah foundation in Cirebon, West Java, founded by Buya Yahya (Yahya Zainul Ma'arif) in 2006. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-zainul-maarif-id} Its YouTube channel (@albahjah-tv) counted 6,060,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 15 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} The institution is catalogued here as a group, separately from its founder's person record. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv}

## Work and reach

The YouTube channel was created on 27 May 2015, its channel country is set to Indonesia, and vidIQ records 20,235 videos and 1,208,626,590 total views for it. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} Its most-viewed uploads are lectures by Buya Yahya himself — question-and-answer sessions and practical lessons, the most-viewed a lesson on shrouding the dead from May 2022. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} The person record for Buya Yahya, built from the Indonesian Wikipedia article, states that he founded the Al-Bahjah foundation in 2006 and runs Al-Bahjah TV, and the founder relation is recorded once, on this group record. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-zainul-maarif-id}

## Impact and audience

At 6,060,000 subscribers and more than 1.2 billion views, the channel is one of the largest pesantren media channels in Indonesia. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} The audience is the institution's: it is recorded here, on the group's record, and is not added to the founder's personal reach, which his person record states from its own sources. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} These figures remain dated platform counts, as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv}

## Limits of the record

Two dates belong to two different things and are kept separate: 2006 is the foundation's founding year, from the founder's person record, and 27 May 2015 is the YouTube channel's creation, from vidIQ; neither is written as the other's date. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-zainul-maarif-id} {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv} The channel's day-to-day operator is the institution's media team; no individual channel manager is named in the sources read. {cite:vidiq-al-bahjah-tv}
