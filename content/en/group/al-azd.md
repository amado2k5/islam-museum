## Overview
Al-Azd: a Kahlan clan that emigrated from Yemen under Imran ibn Amr Muzayqiya', whose branches scattered to Medina, Mecca, Oman and Syria.

## Life
The Azd's emigration followed the resolve of their chief and elder Imran ibn Amr Muzayqiya'; they moved about the lands of Yemen sending out scouts, then made north {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Of them: Tha'laba ibn Amr, who went toward the Hijaz then settled Medina — from his sons are the Aws and the Khazraj, sons of Haritha ibn Tha'laba; Haritha ibn Amr — Khuza'a — who settled Marr al-Zahran then took possession of the Sanctuary; Imran ibn Amr, who settled Oman; and Jafna ibn Amr, who went to Syria and became father of the Ghassanid kings — while the tribes of Nasr ibn al-Azd stayed at Tihama, the Azd Shanu'a {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
The Azd is a tribe that journeyed from Yemen; among its descendants are Medina's people — the Aws and Khazraj — and the kings of Ghassan.
