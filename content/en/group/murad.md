## Overview
A Yemeni tribe that Ibn Ishaq counts with Zubayd among [[group/madhhij|Madhhij]], when the Prophet appointed Farwa ibn Musayk "over Murad, Zubayd and all of Madhhij". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
Shortly before Islam, [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] struck them at [[event/battle-of-al-radm|the day of al-Radm]] and inflicted heavy losses on them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/228} From them came [[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk]] to the Prophet, separating himself from the kings of Kinda, and he embraced Islam; see [[event/delegation-of-farwa-ibn-musayk|the arrival of Farwa ibn Musayk]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/228} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229} From them came [[person/qays-ibn-makshuh|Qays ibn Makshuh]], the lord of his people, whom Amr ibn Ma'di Karib invited to go to the Prophet; he refused and mocked his opinion. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230}
