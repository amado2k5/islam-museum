## Summary
The Jordan Academy of Arabic in Amman was first established briefly in 1923 and 1924 by Sa'id al-Karmi and then re-established on a permanent footing by Royal Decree, Provisional Law No. 40, on 1 July 1976, beginning work in October or November of that year, according to the research summary supplied to the museum. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} {cite:jordan-gbd-2024-chapter-2502 research summary} {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-ar research summary} {cite:arabic-jo research summary} This page reports what that summary says; the museum did not open the pages it rests on, with the founder's approval of 21 November 2026.

## Founding and founders
The summary ascribes the first attempt of 1923-1924 to Sa'id al-Karmi, gives no detail of it, and the museum has no record of him. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} {cite:jordan-gbd-2024-chapter-2502 research summary} It says the re-establishment of 1976 came on the initiative of the Jordanian Arabisation, Translation and Publication Committee, set up in 1961. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-ar research summary} It names five active members appointed in 1976: Abdulkareem Khalefah, president from 1976 to 2015, Mahmoud al-Samra, Sa'id al-Tall, Mahmoud Ibrahim and Isa al-Na'uri. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-ar research summary} The museum has no records of these yet, so only their names and posts as the summary gives them are stated; the summary does not say whether the first attempt was an independent academy or part of another body.

## Role
The summary describes the Academy as regulating and preserving Arabic, Arabising technical and university-level scientific terms, and reviving the Arab-Islamic heritage. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} It publishes dictionaries and a biannual journal and oversees Arabic proficiency testing. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} So for this academy Arabisation is direct daily work, finding Arabic words for the terms of university books and technical trades. The summary does not name the Academy's dictionaries or journal or say who sits the tests.

## Arabisation and terminology
The summary suggests the Jordanian Academy is closely tied to Arabisation: its re-establishment rested on the initiative of an Arabisation committee, and its declared work is Arabising university and technical terms. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-ar research summary} {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} Arabic in Jordan has its own page, [[concept/al-arabiyya-fi-jordan|Arabic in Jordan]], and the Academy sits in [[place/amman|Amman]]. It resembles the academies of [[group/majma-al-lugha-al-arabiyya-bi-dimashq|Damascus]], [[group/majma-al-lugha-al-arabiyya-bi-al-qahira|Cairo]] and [[group/al-majma-al-ilmi-al-iraqi|Baghdad]] in purpose; the bond between academies is the [[group/ittihad-al-majami-al-lughawiyya-al-ilmiyya-al-arabiyya|Union of Arab Language Academies]], and the summary does not say when the Jordanian Academy joined it.

## Dates that do not agree
The summary sets side by side 1923-1924, 1961 and 1976, three different moments: a first brief academy, a committee for Arabisation, then the permanent Academy. They must not be fused into one date; the museum records only the last as the founding date and keeps the first in the note. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary} The summary says work began in October or November 1976 without choosing between them. {cite:wikipedia-jordan-academy-of-arabic-en research summary}

## What this page does not reach
The source is a research summary supplied to the museum that rests on English and Arabic Wikipedia and the Academy's official website; the museum did not open those pages, and the founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026. So what happened to the first academy after 1924, the names of the Academy's dictionaries and journal, who followed Abdulkareem Khalefah after 2015 and the date it joined the Union are not known here. All of the above is attributed to the summary.
