## Overview
Alevism is an Anatolian religious-cultural current centred on devotion to Ali and the ahl al-bayt, ocak saint-lineages and the cem assembly {cite:dressler-writing-religion-2013}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni classification:** the currents linked to extremism concerning Ali — the Nusayriyya among them — are classed among the ghulat in Shahrastani's heresiography {cite:milal-nihal-shahrastani}.
- **Academic:** 'Alevism' is a category consolidated in the late-Ottoman and Turkish republican context over Qizilbash loyalty and saint-lineage layers {cite:dressler-writing-religion-2013}.
- **Self-description:** the path of Ali — devotion to the ahl al-bayt through hereditary ocak lineages and the cem, not the mosque {cite:dressler-writing-religion-2013}.
