## Summary
Sözler Köşkü is an Istanbul youth-dawah collective producing short talks, street interviews and animations, and its YouTube channel is @sozlerkosku. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku} The channel counted 4,820,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 11 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku}

## Work and reach
vidIQ recorded 1,263,219,712 total views and 1,373 videos for the channel as of 11 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku} The channel itself is the collective's own YouTube presence, carrying its uploads under its name. {cite:youtube-sozler-kosku} These figures are dated platform counts as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku}

## Limits of the record
No source read for this record names a specific operator for the collective, so no operator, founder or presenter is named here as an operator. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku} No founding date is written for the collective, because no source read documents one. {cite:youtube-sozler-kosku} The collective is catalogued separately from any speaker whose talks appear on its channel, and the channel's carriage of a speaker's talks creates no relation and assigns none of that speaker's audience to it. {cite:vidiq-sozler-kosku}
