## Summary
The Janissary corps (Yeniçeri) — the standing 'new army' that al-Sallabi says Orhan founded on his brother Alaeddin's proposal: captives and prisoners reared on Islamic training and sent to the army's van {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 54-55}. The book describes Orhan organizing the force in tens, hundreds and thousands, reserving a fifth of spoils for it, and building dedicated training centres {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 53-55}.

The author insists that the familiar devşirme picture — Christian boys forcibly levied and converted — is 'a lie planted in Orhan's and Murad's history', and that Orhan never took a single Christian boy from his father's house; this is the author's stated defence, not a consensus of historians {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 53-55}.
