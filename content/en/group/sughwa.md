## Overview
Sughwa is a tribe of the Zaghawa. Al-Idrisi describes them as nomadic travellers with many camels. They weave cloaks and tents from camel hair, and live on their milk and their meat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.111}
