## Summary
A clan from whom Ibn Ishaq names Ka'b ibn Malik; in his account a man of them disparaged Ka'b before the Prophet at Tabuk, and men of them blamed him after his truthfulness. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/177-178}

## In this batch
A man "of Banu Salama" said to the Prophet at Tabuk that Ka'b had been held back by his two striped cloaks ([[event/kaab-ibn-malik-delays-his-preparations|the event]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/177} Then men of Banu Salama rose with Ka'b after the Prophet's answer, blaming him for not having made an excuse ([[event/kaab-tells-the-prophet-the-truth|the event]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/177-178}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- "Banu Salama" appears with kasra on the l in the reading shown on the page image; the text does not say which Khazraj clan they belong to.
