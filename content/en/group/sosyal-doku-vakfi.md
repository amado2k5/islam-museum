## Summary
Sosyal Doku Vakfı is an Istanbul foundation that publishes Nureddin Yıldız's lessons, and its YouTube channel is @sosyaldokudernegi. {cite:vidiq-sosyal-doku-vakfi} The channel counted 822,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as of 9 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-sosyal-doku-vakfi}

## Founder
The Turkish Wikipedia article on Nureddin Yıldız lists his organisation as Sosyal Doku Vakfı and states that he most recently founded the Sosyal Doku association. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr} The founded_by relation is written in this record's data, on the group side, to his person record built in section A of this same wave 202. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr} The foundation is catalogued here as an organisation, separately from its founder's person record. {cite:vidiq-sosyal-doku-vakfi}

## Work and reach
vidIQ recorded 265,338,370 total views and 8,864 videos for the channel as of 9 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-sosyal-doku-vakfi} The channel itself is the foundation's own YouTube presence, carrying its uploads under its name. {cite:youtube-sosyal-doku-vakfi} These figures are dated platform counts as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-sosyal-doku-vakfi} No founding date is written for the foundation, because no source read documents one. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}
