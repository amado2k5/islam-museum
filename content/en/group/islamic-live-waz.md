## Summary
ISLAMIC LIVE WAZ is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@islamiclivewaz) that reposts short, viral clips from waz lectures rather than carrying full lectures of its own. It counted about 462 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026 despite a catalogue of only 22 videos, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-live-waz} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-islamic-live-waz}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records about 65 million total views across those 22 videos, and the count was still rising — 461,000 subscribers at the 7 November 2026 read. {cite:vidiq-islamic-live-waz} This record does not present the channel as continuously active: its most-viewed video, a Mufti Nazrul Islam Kasemi lecture with about 44.6 million views, dates to 14 May 2023, after which the channel went quiet for about 942 days, then revived with a cluster of uploads in August–October 2026, the most recent on 27 October 2026. {cite:vidiq-islamic-live-waz}
