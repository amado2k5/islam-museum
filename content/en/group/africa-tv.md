## Summary
Africa TV3 is a Nigerian Hausa-language Islamic YouTube channel (@AfricaTV3), created on 14 November 2012, carrying religious lectures and Ramadan drama series. It counted about 257 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-africa-tv3}

## Content
vidIQ records 14,332 videos and about 25.6 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Nigeria; its most-viewed videos are Hausa-language items, among them a lecture by Dr Muhammad Sulaiman Adam and episodes of the Ramadan drama series Nagarta and Alhaki, each drawing well over a hundred thousand views. {cite:vidiq-africa-tv3} No operator or parent organisation for the channel is documented in the source read, and none is asserted here. {cite:vidiq-africa-tv3}
