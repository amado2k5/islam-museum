## Overview
Lamtuna are the greatest branch of the veiled Sanhaja of the desert ([[group/sanhaja|Sanhaja]]), and the Almoravid state came out of them, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}

## Their leadership
Their leadership was in Banu Wartantaq, the house of their emir Yahya ibn Ibrahim al-Jaddali. He made the pilgrimage and brought them the jurist Abd Allah ibn Yasin. Among their emirs were Yahya ibn Umar, his brother Abu Bakr, and their cousin [[person/yusuf-ibn-tashfin|Yusuf ibn Tashfin]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}
