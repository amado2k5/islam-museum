## Overview
Banu Hanifa is an Adnanite tribe of Rabi'a. The Sealed Nectar counts them among [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar|Rabi'a]], with Asad ibn Rabi'a, Anaza, Abd al-Qays and the two sons of Wa'il. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} Banu Hanifa ibn Sa'b ibn Ali ibn Bakr went out to [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]] and settled at Hajr, the chief town of al-Yamama. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/15-16}

## Not covered here

## For young visitors
Banu Hanifa lived in al-Yamama, in the centre of the Arabian Peninsula, and their chief town was Hajr.
