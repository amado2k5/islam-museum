## Summary
Waz TV is a Bengali-language YouTube channel specialising in waz mahfil — Bangladeshi Islamic sermon gatherings — and ThoughtLeaders describes it as one of the largest producers of Bangladeshi Islamic lecture content. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv} It counted about 886 thousand subscribers by ThoughtLeaders' page opened on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv} Its exact YouTube handle could not be pinned in this pass, and no named head or operator was identified for it, so this record asserts neither. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv}

## Programmes and platforms
ThoughtLeaders records nearly 4,500 videos and about 138 million total views for the channel, with about 1,000 of the videos uploaded in the last two years and an average of roughly six uploads a week; its first upload dates to 5 February 2017. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv} The channel's country setting is the United States, a platform setting for a channel producing Bengali content for Bangladesh and its diaspora audience; it is not asserted as the channel's place of production. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv} Its content focuses on Islamic teachings, societal issues and lifestyle topics from an Islamic perspective. {cite:thoughtleaders-waz-tv}
