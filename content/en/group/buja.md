## Overview
Al-Baladhuri describes the Buja as a desert people, owners of camels and livestock. Their land is wasteland; between it and the lands of Islam is a month's journey through barren ground and rough mountains, and it has gold mines. It is [[place/bilad-al-buja|the land of the Buja]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.333-335}

They raided the land of Egypt and refused to pay what they owed from the mines. Muhammad al-Qummi fought them in the time of al-Mutawakkil. Their camels were skittish and startled at everything, so al-Qummi hung bells on his horses and their camels bolted. They made peace in the year 241 AH in exchange for a tribute and the bsaq tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.333-335}
