## Summary
Hijjaz is a Malaysian nasyid group, one of the three groups — Rabbani, Raihan and Hijjaz — whose creativity and role in creating Islamic awareness are studied in the Journal of Islam in Asia's case study of Malaysian nashid groups. {cite:iium-hijjaz-nashid-creativity} Its Facebook page counted 590,000 followers by Facebook post metadata on the group's own post dated 10 July 2026. {cite:facebook-hijjaz-channel} Its YouTube channel, HIJJAZ CHANNEL, counted 91,399 subscribers by Mixerno's live counter as crawled on 15 November 2026. {cite:mixerno-hijjaz-channel}

## Work and reach
The academic case study examines the historical background of Rabbani, Raihan and Hijjaz, the role the groups have played in creating Islamic awareness, and the Islamisation of songs and music they promote. {cite:iium-hijjaz-nashid-creativity} The group's own Facebook post of 10 July 2026 shows the page active in 2026 with 590,000 followers recorded in its post metadata. {cite:facebook-hijjaz-channel}

## Limits of the record
The group clears the lane's audience bar on its own Facebook page; its fresh YouTube read of 91,399 subscribers sits just under the ~100,000 line on its own and is carried as corroboration of the group's scale, never rounded up. {cite:facebook-hijjaz-channel} {cite:mixerno-hijjaz-channel} A different channel, Munif Hijjaz Channel, belongs to the group's lead singer Munif as a person; it is not the group's channel and its count is not used here. {cite:mixerno-hijjaz-channel} No source read in this run documents the group's founding year, so no founding date is asserted. {cite:iium-hijjaz-nashid-creativity} Both audience figures are dated platform counts, as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies. {cite:facebook-hijjaz-channel} {cite:mixerno-hijjaz-channel}
