## Overview
Banu al-Muttalib is a branch of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]], descended from [[person/al-muttalib-ibn-abd-manaf|al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf]], brother of [[person/hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] and guardian of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] in his childhood. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.49-50} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/137-138} The Sealed Nectar lists them with [[group/banu-hashim|Banu Hashim]] among the tribes that answered the call to [[event/hilf-al-fudul|the Alliance of the Virtuous]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/133-135}

## In the Alliance of the Virtuous
From Quraysh, Banu Hashim, Banu al-Muttalib, Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza, Zuhra ibn Kilab and Taym ibn Murra answered the call. They gathered in the house of [[person/abdullah-ibn-judan|Abd Allah ibn Judan]] and swore to support the wronged in Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/133-135}

## Not covered here
This section does not cover what is known of the close ties of Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib in the boycott and in the share of the kinsfolk. These are taken up in their proper places in the book.

## For young visitors
Banu al-Muttalib were cousins of the Prophet through al-Muttalib, the uncle of his grandfather. They stood with Banu Hashim in alliances and in hardship.
