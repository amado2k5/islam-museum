## Overview
A household of viziers in Cordoba, known in the way that Ibn Hubayra was known among Fazara. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 338}

## The vizierate and autocracy
They were viziers of the Umayyads. After the caliphate broke apart, Abu al-Hazm Jahwar ibn Muhammad ruled Cordoba in an autocratic way, without going beyond the title of vizier. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 338}

After him, in the year 435 AH, the power passed to his son Abu al-Walid. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 338}
