## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, the Nuba were a people who shot arrows at the Muslims until they put out the eyes, and were called "the archers of the eyes". Their homeland is [[place/nuba|Nubia]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.331-332}

## In the Conquests of the Lands
Al-Baladhuri reports from a shaykh of Himyar who witnessed them his saying that he had never seen a people more keen in war than they were, and that one of them would ask the Muslim where he wished his arrow to land, and would never miss. He mentions that a hundred and fifty eyes put out were counted in a single line. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.331-332}

Abd Allah ibn Sa'd made a truce with them, a truce without poll tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.331-332}
