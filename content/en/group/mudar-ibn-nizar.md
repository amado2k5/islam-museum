## Overview
Mudar is one of the four great tribes descended from the sons of Nizar ibn Ma'add ibn [[person/adnan|Adnan]]. Together with [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar|Rabi'a]], it is the one with the most branches and the widest clans. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15} Among them is [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]], the Prophet's tribe. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21}

## The two divisions
The tribes of Mudar divided into two great divisions: Qays Aylan ibn Mudar, and the branches of [[person/ilyas-ibn-mudar|Ilyas ibn Mudar]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} From Qays Aylan came [[group/banu-sulaym|Banu Sulaym]], [[group/hawazin|Banu Hawazin]] and [[group/ghatafan|Banu Ghatafan]], and from Ghatafan came Abs, Dhubyan, Ashja' and Ghani ibn A'sur. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} From Ilyas ibn Mudar came Tamim ibn Murra, [[group/hudhayl|Hudhayl ibn Mudrika]], [[group/banu-asad-ibn-khuzayma|Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma]], and the branches of [[group/kinana|Kinana ibn Khuzayma]]. From Kinana came Quraysh, the sons of [[person/fihr-ibn-malik|Fihr ibn Malik]] ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21}

## Mudar's three privileges in the pilgrimage
When Khuza'a had sole authority over Mecca, Mudar's tribes held three privileges. The first was to lead the people from Arafat to Muzdalifa and to release them on the day of departure from Mina; this was held by Banu al-Ghawth ibn Murra of the branches of Ilyas, who were called [[group/sufa|Sufa]]. The second was the departure from Jam' on the morning of the sacrifice to Mina, which belonged to Banu [[group/adwan|Adwan]]. The third was the postponement of the sacred months, which was held by Banu Tamim ibn Adi of the Kinana. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.29} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/44} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/119-122} See [[concept/nasi|the postponement of the sacred months]].

## Dwellings
Thaqif lived in al-Ta'if and Hawazin east of Mecca, Banu Sulaym near Medina, Banu Asad east of Tayma and west of Kufa, and Dhubyan near Tayma as far as Hawran. The branches of Kinana remained in Tihama, and the branches of Quraysh in Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/15-16}

## For young visitors
Mudar is a large branch of the Adnanite tribes. From it came Quraysh, Kinana, Hawazin, Sulaym, Ghatafan and Tamim.
