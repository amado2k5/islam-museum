## Summary
Poetic Pilgrimage is a British Islamic hip-hop duo from London, founded in 2002 by Muneera Rashida and Sukina Abdul Noor. {cite:wikipedia-poetic-pilgrimage-en} {cite:wikidata-poetic-pilgrimage}

## Hip Hop Hijabis
The duo is the subject of Hip Hop Hijabis, a 2015 Al Jazeera documentary directed by Mette Reitzel about the constraints on women in hip-hop, which Al Jazeera called "a universal story about friendship, love and idealism". {cite:wikipedia-poetic-pilgrimage-en}

## Works and coverage
It released Freedom Times (2009), the Starwomen Mixtape (2010) and Silence is Consent with Mohammed Yahya (2011), and was covered by the New Statesman (2008), Al-Ahram Weekly (2006), The Independent (2008) and Norway's NRK. {cite:wikipedia-poetic-pilgrimage-en} It was among the artists whose tours [[group/mecca2medina|Mecca2Medina]] organised. {cite:wikipedia-mecca2medina-en}
