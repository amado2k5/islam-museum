## Summary
Youth Club is the YouTube channel (@youthclubpk) of Youth Club Pakistan, a non-profit youth organisation based in Islamabad, Pakistan. {cite:wikipedia-youth-club-pakistan-en} {cite:youtube-youth-club} The channel was created on 6 September 2011 and counted about 1.71 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-youth-club}

## The organisation's channel
Youth Club Pakistan was founded in 2011 and organises community events, religious and educational conferences and youth engagement programmes. {cite:wikipedia-youth-club-pakistan-en} Its channel publishes the talks of the organisation's speakers and its conferences. {cite:youtube-youth-club} vidIQ records 1,896 videos and about 117.2 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Pakistan; its most-viewed video is the 2022 Dil Ki Batein episode "The Love of Allah", at about 1.15 million views, and the series' other episodes and an organisation short film, "The Barber Shop" (2023), follow it. {cite:vidiq-youth-club}
