## Overview
The Shafi'i school is the school of [[person/al-shafii|Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i]]. Ahmad Amin counts it the second widest of the schools in its use of opinion, after the Hanafi. {cite:duha-islam p.502}

## Istihsan
Ahmad Amin reports that the Shafi'is rejected the istihsan that the Hanafis upheld, and transmits a saying of al-Shafi'i in disapproval: "Whoever practises istihsan has made a law." {cite:duha-islam p.504}
