## Overview
Banu Shakir the astrologers are Muhammad, Ahmad and al-Hasan. They took care to bring books out of the land of the Romans, paid generously, and sent Hunayn ibn Ishaq and others, who brought back rare books and unusual works in philosophy, geometry, music, arithmetic and medicine. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.339-340}
