## Overview
According to Ibn Khurradadhbih, the Rus are a people of the Slavs. Their merchants carry brocade, black fox pelts and swords from the furthest Slavic lands to the Roman sea, where the Roman ruler collects a tithe on them. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih pp. 154-155}

If they travel along the river of the Slavs, they pass by the Khamlij city of the Khazars, where its ruler collects a tithe on them, and then reach the sea of Jurjan. Sometimes they carry their goods by camel from Jurjan to Baghdad. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih pp. 154-155}

Slave interpreters from the Slavs translate for them. He records that they claim to be Christians, and so they pay the poll tax. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih pp. 154-155}
