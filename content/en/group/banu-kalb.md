## Overview
A tribe of Qudaa. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.131}

## Islam offered to them
The Prophet offered Islam to them at the pilgrimage season, and they did not accept. He went to a branch of them called Banu Abd Allah and called them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.131} This is recorded within [[event/offering-islam-to-the-tribes|the offering of Islam to the tribes]].
