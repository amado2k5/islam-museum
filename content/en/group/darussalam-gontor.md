## Overview
Darussalam Gontor, an Islamic institute and university in Indonesia marking ninety years since its founding per the book {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 136}. The Grand Imam visited on 25 February 2016, announcing fifty additional scholarships for its students to study at al-Azhar and inaugurating the university's graduate-studies building {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 140}.
