## Overview
The Zaydiyya, as Ibn al-Nadim defines them, held to the imamate of Zayd ibn Ali. After him they held the imamate to be among the descendants of Fatima, whoever he might be, once the conditions of the imamate were fully met in him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.253}

## The people of hadith
Ibn al-Nadim says that most of the hadith scholars followed this school, and gives as examples Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Sufyan al-Thawri, Salih ibn Hayy and his son. He reports from him that most of the scholars of hadith were Zaydi, and likewise some of the jurist-traditionists and the leading traditionists. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.253}
