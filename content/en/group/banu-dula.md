## Overview
A branch of [[group/judham|Judham]], among them [[person/al-hunayd-ibn-us|al-Hunayd ibn Aws]] and his son Aws, who raided [[person/dihya-al-kalbi|Dihya al-Kalbi]] in the valley of Shinar, and Qurra ibn Ashqar al-Dulay'i, who struck al-Nu'man ibn Abi Jial in the knee when the Dubayb rushed to recover Dihya's goods. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/260} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261} Among them was the raid of [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|Zayd ibn Haritha]], in which al-Hunayd and his son were killed. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261}

## Source flags
- The vocalisation of the branch's name differs between the manuscripts, and the editors note this in the margin. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/260}
