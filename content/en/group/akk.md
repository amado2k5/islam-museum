## Overview
A Yemeni tribe that settled in the lower valley when Ghassan settled above it, at the water known as Ghassan's, between the valleys of Zabid and Ramma. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 441, 498, 500}

Al-Mas'udi mentions wars between them and Ghassan that are recorded among the days of the Arabs, including the story of the ostrich and the tree. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 441, 498, 500}
