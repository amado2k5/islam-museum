## Overview
Ghatafan is a tribe of Qays Aylan, of [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]]. The Sealed Nectar says that among the Qays Aylan were Banu Sulaym, Banu Hawazin and Banu Ghatafan, and among Ghatafan were Abs, Dhubyan, Ashja' and Ghani ibn A'sur. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}

## Dwellings
In describing how the Adnanite tribes were spread out, the author says that Dhubyan lived near [[place/tayma|Tayma]] as far as Hawran. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/15-16}

## Wars between kin
The author says that rivalry for honour and leadership often led to wars between tribes that share one father. He gives as examples the Aws and al-Khazraj, Abs and Dhubyan, and Bakr and Taghlib. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.45}

## Not covered here
The raids of Ghatafan, their position in the Confederates' war, and the expeditions of Dhu Amarr and Dhat al-Riqa' are presented in the entries on those expeditions (see [[event/expedition-of-dhu-amarr|the expedition of Dhu Amarr]]).

## For young visitors
Ghatafan is a large tribe of Najd. Its branches Abs and Dhubyan fought each other for a long time in the pre-Islamic period, although they shared one father.
