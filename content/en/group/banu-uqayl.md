## Overview
Banu Uqayl ibn Ka'b ibn Amir ibn Sa'sa'a, of Qays. According to Ibn al-Jawzi they were the largest Arab tribal groups in al-Jazira. They ruled Mosul and its surroundings from the end of the fourth century AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.489-490}

Among them was Qiyrawash ibn al-Muqallad, Mu'tamid al-Dawla, who held Mosul, Kufa and the bank of the Euphrates. He was followed by Quraysh ibn Badran. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.489-490}
