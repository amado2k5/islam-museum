## Summary
Islamic Waz Narsingdi is a Narsingdi-based regional YouTube aggregator channel in Bangladesh publishing Bengali waz mahfils, prominently recordings of Mizanur Rahman Azhari's mahfils. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-narsingdi} Its channel (@islamicwaznarsingdi) counted about 2.35 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-narsingdi} No named head or operator for the channel was identified in the pass this record is built from, so no individual is asserted for it here. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-narsingdi}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 4,539 videos and about 301 million total views for the channel, which ranks #264 among Bangladesh's channels by subscribers, and its January 2025 mahfil videos drew between 0.7 and 1.2 million views. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-narsingdi} Its catalogue also carries clips of women preachers, who in the Bengali waz scene appear mainly as clips on aggregator channels such as this one; no preacher is attributed to the channel and none is credited with its audience, nor is the channel credited with any speaker's. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-narsingdi}
