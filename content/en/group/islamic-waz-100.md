## Summary
ISLAMIC WAZ 100 is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@islamicwaz100) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures and publishes them to a wide audience. It counted about 665 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-100} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass, so this record attributes the channel to no particular person or institution. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-100}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 793 videos and about 146 million total views for the channel. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-100} The channel carries lectures by a range of Bangladeshi preachers, among them Qari Ruhul Amin Siddiqui; its carriage of a preacher's lecture makes none of its audience that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-islamic-waz-100}
