## Overview
Miknasa is a branch of the Zanata, one of the Berber tribes. Ibn Khaldun says that they were nomads in the lands of Mulayya, Kirsif, Sijilmasa and al-Habt. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In Ibn Khaldun
The people of Sijilmasa from among them professed at the beginning of Islam the Safriyya doctrine of the Kharijites, and founded the city of Sijilmasa in the year 140 AH. They had two states, the Banu Wasul and the Banu Abi al-Afiya, kings of Tasul and Fez. Then their leadership was divided during the strife of the Maghrib, until the power of the Maghrawa grew over them, and then the Almoravid state came. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
