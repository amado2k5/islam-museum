## Overview
Lawata are a Berber tribe, people of a covenant in the land of Barqa, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/sulh-barqa|the treaty of Barqa]].

## The condition of Amr ibn al-As
[[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] wrote into their terms: "It is upon you to sell your sons and your women in payment of the poll tax owed by you." Al-Layth ibn Sa'd said: "Even if they were slaves, that would not be lawful for them." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the time of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz
Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote concerning the Lawati women that whoever had a Lawati woman should ask for her from her father, or return her to her people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
