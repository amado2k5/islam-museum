## Summary
The People of the Town in Surah Ya-Sin are the people of a town to whom God sent messengers; they denied them, so He strengthened them with a third, until a man came running from the farthest part of the town, was killed, and a single Cry destroyed them.

## In the texts
Surah Ya-Sin: «strike for them a likeness — the People of the Town, when the messengers came to it; when We sent to them two and they denied them, so We reinforced with a third, and they said 'we are sent to you'» — the story of the three messengers, the man of Ya-Sin and the single Cry {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01} {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Sira and history
The famous view of the commentators makes the town Antioch and the messengers envoys of the Messiah — Sadiq, Saduq and Sham'un on one report — and the man Habib al-Najjar, who said «follow the messengers» and whose people killed him {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## For younger visitors
The People of the Town denied God's messengers and killed the man who counselled them; Surah Ya-Sin tells their story.

## Notes for researchers
The Qur'an names neither the town, the messengers nor the man; the identifications — Antioch, the three envoys and Habib al-Najjar — are the commentators' reports, and they differ.
