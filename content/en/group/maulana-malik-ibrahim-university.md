## Overview
A state Islamic university in Malang, East Java; it conferred an honorary doctorate on the Grand Imam on 24 February 2016 in recognition of his efforts for a centrist Islam and spreading peace {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 112} — its rector saying its students study al-Azhar's curricula and walk its path {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 115}.
