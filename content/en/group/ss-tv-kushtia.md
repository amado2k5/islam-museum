## Summary
SS TV Kushtia is a YouTube channel from Kushtia, Bangladesh (@sstvkushtia), publishing Bengali waz mahfil recordings. The channel was created on 20 January 2019 and counted about 573 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 8 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-ss-tv-kushtia} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-ss-tv-kushtia}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 1,893 videos and about 73.9 million total views for the channel; it had recorded 1,884 videos on 30 October 2026, so nine videos were added in nine days and the channel is actively publishing. {cite:vidiq-ss-tv-kushtia} YouTube's own category tag for the channel is Lifestyle, while its most-viewed content sample is waz-led: a full Amir Hamza waz of 28 September 2024 with about 2.04 million views, a Tarek Monowar mahfil of 27 November 2022 and a Mizanur Rahman Azhari live broadcast from Pekua on 27 December 2024, so this record does not present its mix as purely sermons. {cite:vidiq-ss-tv-kushtia} The channel's carriage of these preachers' lectures makes none of them its members and none of its audience their followers. {cite:vidiq-ss-tv-kushtia}
