## Overview
The Sabri Brothers are a Pakistani qawwali group led by Ghulam Farid Sabri and Maqbool Ahmed Sabri from the 1950s. They were known for the qawwali pieces 'Bahar Do Jahan' and 'Taj Dar-e-Haram', and were among the names that introduced this art to the world before Nusrat. {cite:wikidata}
