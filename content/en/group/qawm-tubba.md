## Summary
The people of Tubba' in the Qur'an are a nation that denied the messengers and was destroyed: the Himyarite kings of Yemen who bore the title Tubba' and whose rule spread wide centuries before Islam.

## In the texts
The Qur'an names them in two places: «are they better, or the people of Tubba' and those before them? We destroyed them, for they were guilty» in al-Dukhan, and in Qaf: «before them the people of Nuh denied, and the Companions of al-Rass, and Thamud, and 'Ad, and Pharaoh, and the brothers of Lut, and the Companions of the Ayka, and the people of Tubba' — each denied the messengers, so My threat came true» {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01} {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Sira and history
For the chroniclers the Tubba's were the Himyarite kings who ruled Yemen and marched in the earth: the first Tubba' who came to Medina and honoured the Prophet's grave before the Call on one report; Tubba' As'ad Abu Karib who restored Himyar's rule; and the last of them Dhu Nuwas who persecuted Najran's Christians {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## For younger visitors
The people of Tubba' were Himyar's kings in Yemen before Islam; the Qur'an counts them among those who denied the messengers.

## Notes for researchers
The Tubba' kings are known to the chroniclers by name and reign and story; 'Tubba'' is a royal title like Caesar and Khosrow, not a personal name — see al-Tabari's and the Sira's reports before merging persons.
