## Overview
Banu Hilal ibn Amir ibn Sa'sa'a. Ibn Khaldun counts them among the greatest Arab tribal groups in Islam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Their movement
In the pre-Islamic period they were in Najd. Then they moved to Egypt during the wars of the Qaramita. The minister al-Barizi, in the caliphate of al-Mustansir the Fatimid, sent them across to fight al-Mu'izz ibn Badis, and they took the outskirts of Ifriqiya. Ibn Hazm, as Ibn Khaldun reports, said that they corrupted it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Then Banu Sulaym competed with them, and they moved west, between Bona and Constantine to the Atlantic. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Lineages
Hilal had five sons: Shu'ba, Nashira, Nuhayk, Abd Manaf and Abd Allah. Their tribes go back to Athbaj, Riyah, Zughba and Qari'. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
