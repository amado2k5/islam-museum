## Overview
The Qadiri order is among the oldest and most widespread Sufi orders of the Islamic world; traced to Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani (d. 561 AH/1166 CE), it expanded from Baghdad east and west {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
Academic study describes the Qadiriyya as the first broad organization of the Sufi path, extending from Iraq to India and North Africa {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.
