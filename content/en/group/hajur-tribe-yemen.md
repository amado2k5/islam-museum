## Overview
Hajur is a people the book names beside [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan|Banu Rabi'a]]: "a party of Banu Rabi'a and of Hajur talking and whispering about those who fight al-Hadi ila al-Haqq". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} Among them is [[person/ibrahim-ibn-sulayman-al-hajuri|Ibrahim ibn Sulayman]], who told the report of the seal. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The book's index lists "batnat Hajur" at pp. 92, 93, 139 and 160 and "Hajur" at p. 296, places not yet read, so the museum gives neither their descent nor their homes here. See [[event/reports-of-al-hadis-seal-and-two-cures-told-at-khiwan-286]].
