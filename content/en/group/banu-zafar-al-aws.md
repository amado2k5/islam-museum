## Overview
Banu Zafar, a branch of [[group/al-aws|al-Aws]]. According to al-Samhudi their lineage is Zafar, who is Ka'b al-Khazraj al-Asghar ibn Amr ibn Malik ibn al-Aws ibn Haritha. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}

They settled their homes east of al-Baqi' in [[place/medina|Medina]], near their mosque known as the Mosque of the Mule, beside [[group/banu-abd-al-ashhal|Banu Abd al-Ashhal]]. They sided with Banu Abd al-Ashhal in their war with Banu Haritha. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-204}
