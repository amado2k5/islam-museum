## Overview
A branch of [[group/al-khazraj|al-Khazraj]]: Banu Sa'ida ibn Ka'b ibn al-Khazraj al-Akbar, the people of the well-known [[event/saqifa|Saqifa]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.220-222}

## Their homes
They were spread across four dwellings in Medina. Banu al-Harith and Banu Tha'laba lived between the market and Banu Damra, east of the market on the side of Syria. There was a village by the well of Bid'a in the middle of their houses. Banu Abi Khuzayma, the kin of [[person/sad-ibn-ubada|Sa'd ibn Ubada]], lived at the Jarar of Sa'd. Banu Waqash and Banu Anan lived in the house of "Banu Tarif", between al-Madda and the Jarar of Sa'd. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.220-222}

Al-Samhudi considers it likely that [[place/saqifat-bani-saida|their Saqifa]] was at the Jarar of Sa'd. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.220-222}

## The fortress of Mu'arrith
They built the fortress "Mu'arrith", the last fortified house built in Medina. When the Prophet arrived they were building it, and they asked his leave to finish it, and he gave it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.220-222}

## Their rank among the Ansar houses
In the excellence of the sound report, they are fourth among the houses of the Ansar. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3807}
