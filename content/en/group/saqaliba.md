## Overview
The Saqaliba are peoples whom al-Mas'udi says those who know the lore make descendants of Mar ibn Yafith ibn Nuh. Their dwellings lie in the north until they reach the Maghrib. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.355-356}

## Their kinds and kings
They are different kinds with wars between them, and they have kings. Some of them follow Christianity according to the Jacobite view, and some are pagans who know nothing of the laws. Among their kinds he names Walinana, Istabrana, Dalawna, Namjin, Manabin, Sartin and others. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.355-356}

Among their kings he names the king of al-Dayr, whose cities are visited by Muslim merchants, and the king of the Awanij, who fights the Romans, the Franks and [[group/nukaburd|the Nukaburd]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.355-356}

He describes houses they revered in their lands and what was in them of idols. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.543}
