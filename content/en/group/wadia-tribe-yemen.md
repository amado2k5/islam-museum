## Overview
Wadi'a is a Yemeni tribe that Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq lists among the delegation of [[place/najran|Najran]] to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at Sa'da: Shakir, Thaqif, Wadi'a, Yam, the Ahlaf and a group of Banu al-Harith. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.66} When he reached upper Najran Wadi'a, Shakir, Thaqif, Yam and the Ahlaf met him, were glad of his coming, pledged to him, showed a liking for the truth and told him what had passed between them and Banu al-Harith. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The editor's footnote says Wadi'a is "a tribe of Yemen", citing al-Himyari's Muntakhabat fi akhbar al-Yaman. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The text gives neither its descent nor exact territory, and this entry is not to be confused with other tribes of the same name in other sources. See [[event/al-hadi-at-najran-oath-between-hamdan-and-banu-al-harith-284]].
