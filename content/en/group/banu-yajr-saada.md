## Overview
The Kulaybis drove [[group/al-akiliyyun-saada|the Akilis]] out of their country to the land of Banu Yajr, where they stayed, with [[person/sulayman-ibn-hajar-al-kulaybi|Sulayman ibn Hajar al-Kulaybi]] among them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.197} The name is read as printed, with no coordinates and no tie to a known site. See [[event/the-kulaybis-swear-obedience-and-the-akilis-are-sent-to-banu-yajr-287]].
