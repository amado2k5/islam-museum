## Overview
The people of al-Subay', with the people of [[group/khirfan-arhab|Khirfan]], are a people of Arhab and cousins of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], whom he used to strengthen himself in his war on [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], by the narrator. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} Al-Hadi came to them after the horse of [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] reached him, found the villages empty, gave them safe-conduct, and they came down and swore to the right, to stand with him, to hear and obey. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} The book names al-Subay' and Khirfan together throughout, so the museum does not separate them in the events. See [[event/al-hadi-at-khirfan-and-al-subay-and-the-march-to-hawth-286]]. The book's index also lists a "Shu'ayb al-Subay'i"; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.448} he is not tied to this people without a text that says so.
