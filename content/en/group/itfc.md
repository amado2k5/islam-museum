## Summary
The International Islamic Trade Finance Corporation (ITFC) is a member of the Islamic Development Bank Group, headquartered in Jeddah and founded in 1429 AH / 2008, which aims to develop trade among the member states of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation. {cite:wikipedia-itfc-en} Its trade finance arm provides Shariah-compliant financing to public and private entities. {cite:wikipedia-itfc-en}

## Origins
The article recounts, in an uncited section, that the formal proposal came at the 10th OIC Summit in Putrajaya in 1424 AH, that the IDB Board of Governors approved it in Jumada al-Awwal 1426 AH, and that it began operating on 1 Muharram 1429 AH (10 January 2008), bringing together the group's scattered trade-finance programmes. {cite:wikipedia-itfc-en}

## Its work
In July 2012 it signed a US$40 million financing agreement with the Government of Tajikistan for imports of gasoline and diesel. {cite:wikipedia-itfc-en}
