## Overview
Maghrawa are of the Zanata, among the greatest tribes of the first rank in the Maghrib, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1813} See [[group/zanata|the Zanata]].

## The family of Khazar
Their head was the family of Khazar: Muhammad ibn Khazar, the propagandist of al-Nasir, his brother Ma'bad, and his son al-Khayr. They were the clients of the Umayyad state in the Maghrib, until Buluggin ibn Ziri finished off al-Khayr in the year 361 AH and subdued the Zanata. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1813}

## Afterwards
Their king allied himself with the family of Ziri ibn Atiyya, and he established the state of Fez under obedience to al-Mansur ibn Abi Amir, until the Almoravid state brought it to an end. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1816}
