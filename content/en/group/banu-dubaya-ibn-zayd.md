## Summary
A clan to which Ibn Ishaq traces five of the twelve builders of the mosque of Dirar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## The members named
[[person/muattib-ibn-qushayr|Mu'attib ibn Qushayr]], [[person/abu-habiba-ibn-al-azar|Abu Habiba ibn al-Az'ar]], [[person/nabtal-ibn-al-harith|Nabtal ibn al-Harith]], [[person/bahzaj|Bahzaj]], and [[person/bijad-ibn-uthman|Bijad ibn Uthman]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Some appear as "of Banu Dubay'a ibn Zayd" and some as "of Banu Dubay'a" alone; they are gathered here, and distinguishing the two forms is the museum's reading (needs_source).
