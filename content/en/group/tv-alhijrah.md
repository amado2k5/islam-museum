## Summary
TV AlHijrah is a state-owned Malaysian free-to-air Islamic television channel, owned and operated by Alhijrah Media Corporation, a company under the purview of the Department of Islamic Development Malaysia of the Prime Minister's Department. {cite:wikipedia-tv-alhijrah-en} It is Malaysia's first full-fledged and sharia-compliance free-to-air Islamic television network, and it broadcasts from its headquarters in the Islamic Centre Complex, Kuala Lumpur. {cite:wikipedia-tv-alhijrah-en} Its TikTok account (@tvalhijrah) counted 265,200 followers by Kolr's count as crawled about 13 November 2026. {cite:kolr-tv-alhijrah}

## Launch
The station began its operations on 1 November 2010 on a trial basis, as a government initiative to establish a channel based on Islam and to provide an Islamic perspective through its programmes. {cite:wikipedia-tv-alhijrah-en} It was officially launched on 1 Muharram 1432H, 7 December 2010. {cite:wikipedia-tv-alhijrah-en}
