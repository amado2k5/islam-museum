## Overview
[[group/banu-kulayb-saada|Banu Kulayb]] are, in the narrator's words, among the best archers of Yemen, hard in war and fine in shooting; twenty arrows struck [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s armour in his charge on them and the Akilis. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} They then sought safety under al-Zubayr ibn Muhammad, were given it on condition they put the Akilis out, and some days later a party apologised at Sa'da and swore hearing and obedience and drove the Akilis to the land of [[group/banu-yajr-saada|Banu Yajr]], al-Hadi sending collectors with them for the alms. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.197} See [[event/the-kulaybis-swear-obedience-and-the-akilis-are-sent-to-banu-yajr-287]]. This record does not mean every Banu Kulayb in Yemen is this body.
