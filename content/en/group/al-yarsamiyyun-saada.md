## Overview
The Yarsamis are a body in the army of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], whom he set on the left wing. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} On the first sortie some went on and closed with the enemy while a party held back whom the narrator calls not loyal advisers to al-Hadi, and al-Hadi rode up close to the enemy force, charged and drove it off. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.192} That is the narrator's description, not the museum's judgement. One of them is [[person/muhammad-ibn-musbih-al-yarsami|Muhammad ibn Musbih al-Yarsami]], who came to al-Hadi with a warning on the second sortie. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} See [[event/the-night-march-of-mid-shaban-and-the-fight-at-allaf-287]].
