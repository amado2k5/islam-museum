## In the introduction to The Sealed Nectar
Abd Shams is one of the four branches of Abd Manaf, along with Nawfal, al-Muttalib and Hashim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21}. When Abd al-Muttalib's arkah was returned to him, [[person/nawfal-ibn-abd-manaf|Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf]] allied with Banu Abd Shams against Banu Hashim. Then Khuza'a allied with Banu Hashim against Banu Abd Shams and Nawfal {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}. The 'uqab, that is, the carrying of the national banner, was among the offices of Quraysh held by the Umayyads of Abd Shams {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.32}.
