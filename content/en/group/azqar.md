## Overview
Azqar is a Berber tribe of desert travellers, with many camels and abundant milk. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112}

The author says that the people of the Maghrib are the most knowledgeable about the sand-divination script attributed to Daniel, and that they recover lost things by means of this script in the sand. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 113}
