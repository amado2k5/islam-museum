## Overview
The Ashrafiyya was a dervish order, according to d'Ohsson, which takes its name from 'Abd Allah Ashraf (Eshref) Rumi, who died in 899 AH (1493) at Cin Iznik, as the entry prints it. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 753}

## Note
The entry is short and rests on d'Ohsson's account; it gives no further history of the order. The entry is signed by M. Meyerhof. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 753}
