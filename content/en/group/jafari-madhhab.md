## Overview
The Ja'fari school is the Twelver school of fiqh traced to Ja'far al-Sadiq; the heresiographies treat it within the Imamiyya and academic study presents it as Twelver jurisprudence {cite:milal-nihal-shahrastani} {cite:momen-1985}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni classification:** the heresiographies list the Imamiyya and its branches — including those who stopped at Ja'far — among the community's sects {cite:milal-nihal-shahrastani}.
- **Academic:** the legal school of Twelver Shi'ism with its own usul and ijma' rules {cite:momen-1985}.
- **Twelver self-view:** the fiqh of the ahl al-bayt school transmitted from al-Baqir and Ja'far {cite:momen-1985}.
