## Summary
Nejashi TV is an Ethiopian Islamic television platform that describes itself as "a united platform born from the coalition of three esteemed Islamic media outlets: Bilal TV, Jeilu TV, and Merkeb Media", broadcasting on Ethiosat to audiences across Ethiopia and beyond. {cite:nejashi-tv-website} Its programmes span news, dawah and lectures, entertainment, series and drama, with a daily news programme, Nejashi News. {cite:nejashi-tv-website} Its YouTube channel (@nejashitva) counted about 526 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-nejashi-tv}

## The channel
The YouTube channel was created on 11 June 2009, and vidIQ records 3,504 videos and about 43.1 million total views for it; its most-viewed videos are Ethiopian Amharic dawah programmes by teachers such as Ustaz Nuru Turki and Ustaz Bedru Hussein, one of them still branded Bilal TV, one of the three outlets the platform says it unites. {cite:vidiq-nejashi-tv} {cite:nejashi-tv-website} The platform's website states no date for the coalition itself, and none is asserted here. {cite:nejashi-tv-website}
