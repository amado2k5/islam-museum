## Overview
Al-Athbaj is a branch of [[group/banu-hilal|Banu Hilal]] ibn Amir. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Some of them settled in Barqa, and Iyad settled at the Mountain of the Fortress. Their Ifriqiya branches settled the villages of al-Zab, among those who were there before them from the Zanata and the forerunners of the conquest, and the people of al-Zab became known through them. Among them were the Banu Muzni, the emirs of Biskra. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

They were the first of the Hilali peoples to settle in Ifriqiya, and the most settled in villages. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
