## Overview
The Khushaymis are a people to whom the book attaches three of those [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put out of Najran in 286 AH: [[person/marzuq-ibn-muhammad-al-khushaymi|Marzuq ibn Muhammad]], [[person/nukhayl-ibn-muhajir-al-khushaymi|Nukhayl ibn Muhajir]] and [[person/abu-al-wajih-ibn-musa-al-khushaymi|Abu al-Wajih ibn Musa]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The book gives neither their homes nor their descent here; the name is read as printed, while a re-reading of the page by optical character recognition gives "al-Khunsaymiyyun", which is not relied on. The museum asserts no descent. See [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]].
