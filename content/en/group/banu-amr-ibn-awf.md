## Summary
A clan of Medina's people from whom Ibn Ishaq names Murara ibn al-Rabi', Abbad ibn Hunayf and Khidham ibn Khalid (of the branch Banu Ubayd ibn Zayd). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174, 178}

## In this batch
Ibn Ishaq attaches to it [[person/khidham-ibn-khalid|Khidham ibn Khalid]] of the branch [[group/banu-ubayd-ibn-zayd|Banu Ubayd ibn Zayd]], [[person/abbad-ibn-hunayf|Abbad ibn Hunayf]], and [[person/murara-ibn-al-rabi|Murara ibn al-Rabi']]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174, 178}
