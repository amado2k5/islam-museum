## Summary
A clan of the people of Medina, from which Ibn Ishaq names Murara ibn al-Rabi', Abbad ibn Hunayf and Khidham ibn Khalid (of the branch Banu Ubayd ibn Zayd) {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174, 178}.

## In this batch
Ibn Ishaq attributes to it [[person/khidham-ibn-khalid|Khidham ibn Khalid]], of the branch [[group/banu-ubayd-ibn-zayd|Banu Ubayd ibn Zayd]], [[person/abbad-ibn-hunayf|Abbad ibn Hunayf]], and [[person/murara-ibn-al-rabi|Murara ibn al-Rabi']] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174, 178}.

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text on these pages does not name all of its members. The list is partial, drawn from the names that are mentioned.

## Abu Afak and Salim ibn Umayr
Also from them, and from Banu Ubayda, is [[person/abu-afak|Abu Afak]]. Ibn Ishaq says his hypocrisy came to light, and that he composed verses disparaging the Prophet. Also from them is his killer, [[person/salim-ibn-umayr|Salim ibn Umayr]], one of the "weepers". See [[event/killing-of-abu-afak|the account]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285}.
