## Overview
A branch of [[group/banu-tamim|Tamim]], as Ibn Hisham gives the lineage: Minqar ibn Ubayd ibn al-Harith ibn Amr ibn Ka'b ibn Sa'd ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
Among them were [[person/amr-ibn-al-ahtam|Amr ibn al-Ahtam]] and [[person/qays-ibn-asim|Qays ibn Asim]] in [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|the delegation of Banu Tamim]], and between them was Qays's belittling of Amr and Amr's satire against him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/212}
