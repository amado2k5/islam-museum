## Overview
Zawawa are among the branches of Kutama, and Kutama is one of the greatest branches of the Berbers. Ibn Khaldun says that their homelands were the towering mountains and the rough country around Bejaia as far as Tadlis. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Their history
They have many branches. Ibn Khaldun describes them as people of fervour and fierce might in the strife of the Maghrib. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

They contended with the kings of Banu Hammad at al-Qal'a, with the Zirids, and with Banu Abd al-Wad. Their affairs were settled in their mountains in the manner of mountain peoples until Ibn Khaldun's time. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
