## Summary

Sabyan Gambus is a Jakarta gambus and sholawat performing group whose official YouTube channel (@sabyanofficial) carries the group's sholawat covers and original religious songs. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus} The channel counted 6,160,000 subscribers by SPEAKRJ's snapshot dated 18 September 2026. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus} The group is catalogued here as a group; its vocalist is catalogued separately as a person, and no relation is written between the two records in either direction. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus}

## Work and reach

The channel was created on 2 January 2018, and SPEAKRJ records 672 videos and 800,080,025 total views for it in its snapshot of 18 September 2026. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus} The channel's own description, as quoted by SPEAKRJ, insists that this is not a compilation channel but the group's official channel of original Sabyan content — sholawat covers, original sholawat and religious songs. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus}

## Impact and audience

At 6,160,000 subscribers the channel is among the largest Indonesian religious-music channels in this museum's modern-figures lane. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus} Its audience belongs to the group as a performing ensemble; under the lane's entity-separation rule it is not credited to any individual member, and no member's own audience is credited to the group. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus} These figures remain dated platform counts, as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies. {cite:speakrj-sabyan-gambus}
