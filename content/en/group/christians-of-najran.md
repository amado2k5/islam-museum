## Overview
The Christians of [[place/najran|Najran]], the people who embraced Christianity at the hands of the ascetic [[person/faymiyun|Faymiyun]], and the people of [[event/massacre-of-najran|the trench]], whom [[person/dhu-nuwas|Dhu Nuwas]] burned, according to The Sealed Nectar. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.41}

## Their conversion
Near the time of the first Abyssinian occupation of Yemen, a man, an ascetic whose prayers were answered and who had miracles, called Faymiyun, came to Najran and invited them to the Christian faith. The people of Najran saw signs of his truthfulness and of the truth of his religion, and so they answered and embraced Christianity. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.41} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/31-34}

## The trench
When Yusuf Dhu Nuwas ruled Yemen, he attacked the Christians of Najran and invited them to adopt Judaism. When they refused, he dug a trench for them and burned them in it. He did not spare man, woman, small children or old men. The number killed is said to have been between twenty thousand and forty thousand. It happened in October 523 CE. The Qur'an mentions part of this story in Surat al-Buruj ([[passage/quran-85-4|Al-Buruj 4]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 6/297-298}

## For young visitors
The people of Najran were Christians. The king of Yemen burned them in a trench of fire because they refused to leave their religion, and the Qur'an mentions their story.
