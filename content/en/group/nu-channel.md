## Summary
NU Channel (@nuchannel) is the official YouTube channel of Nahdlatul Ulama, Indonesia's largest Muslim mass organisation. {cite:setia-iqbal-nu-channel-2022} {cite:vidiq-nu-channel} It counted 1,230,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as updated on 6 November 2026, with 201,200,000 views and 7,672 videos. {cite:vidiq-nu-channel}

## Two channels, kept distinct
Nahdlatul Ulama runs a second, larger YouTube channel, NU Online (@nuonlineid), which counted 1,580,000 subscribers by vidIQ's count as updated on 7 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-nu-online} NU Channel and NU Online are distinct channels, and this record covers NU Channel only: neither channel's count is presented as the other's, and the two are not merged into one record. {cite:vidiq-nu-channel} {cite:vidiq-nu-online}

## Work and orientation
A 2022 journal study of Indonesian religious organisations' social-media adaptation takes NU Channel as its case: Nahdlatul Ulama adopted YouTube as a new medium for conveying its religious views, with religious studies conducted online on NU Channel. {cite:setia-iqbal-nu-channel-2022} The study argues that the assumption that Nahdlatul Ulama tends to be traditionalist and conservative towards technological developments is incorrect, since the organisation proved adaptive to social media. {cite:setia-iqbal-nu-channel-2022}

## Limits of the record
No channel creation date is documented in the sources read, so no founding date is asserted for the channel. {cite:vidiq-nu-channel} {cite:setia-iqbal-nu-channel-2022} The audience figures are dated platform counts, as read on their day, not facts the museum verifies. {cite:vidiq-nu-channel}
