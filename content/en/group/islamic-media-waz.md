## Summary
Islamic Media Waz is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@islamicmediawaz19) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures and publishes them to its audience. It counted about 291 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-islamic-media-waz} No named head or operator was identified for it in this pass. {cite:vidiq-islamic-media-waz}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 653 videos and about 46.9 million total views for the channel. {cite:vidiq-islamic-media-waz} The channel carries lectures by a range of Bangladeshi preachers, among them Dr Enayetullah Abbasi; its carriage of a preacher's lectures makes none of its audience that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-islamic-media-waz}
