## Overview
Qahtan: father of the Pure Arabs; his people's cradle is the land of Yemen, and their most famous clans are Himyar and Kahlan.

## Life
The Pure Arabs are those descending from Ya'rub ibn Yashjub ibn Qahtan, called the Qahtan Arabs; their cradle is the land of Yemen {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Their tribes and clans branched out, and two tribes became famous: Himyar — whose best-known clans are Zayd al-Jumhur, Quda'a and al-Sakasik — and Kahlan, whose best-known are Hamdan, Anmar, Tayy', Madhhij, Kinda, Lakhm, Judham, the Azd, the Aws, the Khazraj and Jafna's sons, kings of Syria {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

## For young visitors
Qahtan is the name gathering Yemen's ancient tribes; from him came famous tribes that spread through the whole peninsula.
