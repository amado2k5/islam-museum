## Overview
The school of [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik ibn Anas]], author of the Muwatta'. Ahmad Amin counts it the third of the schools in its use of opinion, after the Hanbalis and the Zahiris. {cite:duha-islam p.502}

## Istislah
Among the tools of the school is "istislah", or "unrestricted public interest" (al-maslaha al-mursala), which rests on the principle that the lawgiver's legislation turns on preserving five things: religion, life, reason, progeny and property. {cite:duha-islam p.504}
