## Overview
A clan of [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe|Shakir]] called Wa'ila, whom the narrator describes as having "made trouble on the road to Najran" without saying what it was; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] marched on them, the army plundered their land and cut their vines, and they then came in hearing and obedience and asked safety, which he gave, and they stood surety for one another {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.244} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.245} ([[event/al-hadi-marches-on-wail-of-shakir-and-takes-sureties-290]]). The book names no chiefs and gives no numbers, and the museum passes no judgement on what is charged against them.
