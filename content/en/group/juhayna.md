## Overview
Juhayna is a tribe of [[group/qudaa|Qudaa]] living between Medina and the sea coast. Ibn Ishaq does not give it a biography in the fourth volume, but it appears in the accounts of the expeditions through its men. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/257} Among them is [[person/jundab-ibn-makith|Jundab ibn Makith al-Juhani]], who related [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-al-mulawwah|the expedition of Ghalib ibn Abd Allah against Banu al-Mulawwah]], in which he was the lookout of the people. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/257} Among them too is the branch of al-Huraqa, to whom [[person/mirdas-ibn-nahik|Mirdas ibn Nahik]] belonged, the ally of Banu Murra, who was killed in [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-murra|the expedition of Ghalib on the land of Banu Murra]] after he pronounced the testimony of faith. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## Source flags
- Ibn Hisham gives the attribution of al-Huraqa to Juhayna on the authority of Abu Ubayda, and the wording is confused in some manuscripts, as the editors note. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}
- The lineage of Juhayna within Qudaa and its dwellings are well known from the genealogy books, but are not read in these pages (needs_source).
