## Summary
A leading clan of the [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]] tribes in the Hijaz; from it rose the young commander [[person/malik-ibn-awf|Malik ibn Awf al-Nasri]], who held general command of Hawazin and Thaqif's armies in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn campaign]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80}.

## At Hunayn
All of Nasr gathered with Malik ibn Awf, fighting hard around the wealth and families they had driven along with them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-81}. After the defeat Malik ibn Awf became Muslim, his Islam proved good, and the Prophet set him over his people {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/137}.

## For young visitors
A branch of the Hawazin tribe; their chief Malik ibn Awf led the fighters in the battle of Hunayn, then became Muslim and one of the Muslims' leaders.
