## Overview
Banu Salama, lords of the fortress of Tawghzut in the mountain of Wanshrish. Their chiefs were Banu Yadallatan, from the branches of Tujin, and they were the most powerful and most numerous of them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Dwellings
They settled al-Ja'abat and Tawghzut after Banu Qadi and Banu Madun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Their history
Some of them sympathised with the Kharijites in their call. Their chief Nasr ibn Umar ibn Uthman al-Mas'ud ibn Bu Zayd ibn Khalid ibn Abd al-Rahman pledged allegiance to him when he took Tlemcen. They fought Adi ibn Sultan and his people, and the outcome was theirs. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Their rule over Tawghzut continued until the days of Abu Zayyan ibn al-Sultan Abu Sa'id and the strife of the Banu Marin, and in the year 783 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
