## Overview
Kahlan: the second of Qahtan's two great clans beside Himyar; its branches emigrated from Yemen and spread through the peninsula — the Azd, Lakhm, Judham, Tayy' and Kinda.

## Life
A rivalry between Kahlan's clans and Himyar's led to Kahlan's expulsion {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}. Most of them emigrated before the flood of al-Arim, when their trade failed under Roman pressure and Roman control of the sea trade-route {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Al-Mubarakpuri divides the emigrants into four: the Azd — from them the Aws, the Khazraj, Khuza'a, the Azd of Oman, the Azd Shanu'a, and Jafna father of the Ghassanids; Lakhm and Judham — of Lakhm, Nasr ibn Rabi'a father of the Manadhira at al-Hira; Banu Tayy' who settled the two mountains Aja and Salma; and Kinda who settled Bahrayn, then Hadramawt, then Najd {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Kahlan is a great tribe of Yemen; its sons emigrated and from them came many tribes at Medina, Mecca and Syria.
