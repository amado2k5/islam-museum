## Overview
The Mahaniyya are a sect of the Marqiyuniyya. They agree with them in all matters except marriage and slaughter, according to Ibn al-Nadim. They claim that the mediator between light and darkness is Christ. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.475}
