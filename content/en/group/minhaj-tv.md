## Summary
Minhaj TV is the YouTube channel (@minhajtvofficial) of Minhaj-ul-Quran International (MQI), presented as the organisation's official TV channel. {cite:vidiq-minhaj-tv} {cite:wikipedia-minhaj-ul-quran-en} The channel was created on 18 February 2012 and counted about 146 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-minhaj-tv}

## The organisation's channel
Minhaj-ul-Quran International is a global non-governmental organisation founded by Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri in 1980 in Lahore, Pakistan, headquartered in Lahore and expanded to over 100 countries. {cite:wikipedia-minhaj-ul-quran-en} vidIQ records 1,754 videos and about 23.7 million total views for the channel, whose country is set to Pakistan; its most-viewed video is Rahat Fateh Ali Khan's "Pukaro Shah e Jilan ko Pukaro" (2023), at about 684 thousand views, followed by a Surah Taha recitation from the 2025 International Milad Conference. {cite:vidiq-minhaj-tv}
