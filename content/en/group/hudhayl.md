## Summary
An Arab tribe. Ibn Ishaq says that "Utba ibn Mas'ud" was one of them, an ally of Banu Zuhra {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}.

## Of this tribe in the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
- [[person/utba-ibn-masoud|Utba ibn Mas'ud]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the introduction to al-Raheeq al-Makhtum
Hudhayl ibn Mudrika is among the clans of [[person/ilyas-ibn-mudar|Ilyas ibn Mudar]], along with Tamim ibn Murra, Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma and the clans of Kinana {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}.
