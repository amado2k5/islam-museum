## Summary
Ibn Ishaq presents this clan in the lists of the emigrants to Abyssinia under its lineage, "Adi ibn Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}.

## Members of this clan in the chapter on the emigrants to Abyssinia
**Those carried in the two ships:**
- [[person/mamar-ibn-abdullah|Ma'mar ibn Abdullah ibn Nadla]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

**Among the rest of the emigrants to Abyssinia:**
- [[person/urwa-ibn-abd-al-uzza|Urwa ibn Abd al-Uzza]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}
- [[person/adi-ibn-nadla|Adi ibn Nadla]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}
- [[person/al-numan-ibn-adi|al-Nu'man ibn Adi]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

**Women emigrants to Abyssinia:**
- [[person/layla-bint-abi-hathma|Layla bint Abi Hathma]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## On the alliances and the Alliance of al-Fudul (The Sealed Nectar, p.60)
The Sealed Nectar counts this clan among the "alliances" against whom the wronged al-Zubaydi appealed when [[person/al-as-ibn-wail|al-As ibn Wa'il]] withheld his right. They were Abd al-Dar, Makhzum, Jumah, Sahm and Adi, and they paid him no heed. That was the cause of the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Alliance of al-Fudul]], which Banu Hashim, Banu al-Muttalib, Asad, Zuhra and Taym formed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}.
