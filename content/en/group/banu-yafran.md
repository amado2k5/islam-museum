## Overview
Banu Yafran are of the Zanata, and Ibn Khaldun describes them as among the greatest Berber tribes of the Maghrib. They have many branches scattered across their homelands. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Branches
Among them in Ifriqiya are Banu Warkaw and Marnajisa. Among them in the regions of Tlemcen and Tahart came Abu Yazid, the man of the donkey, and Abu Qurra, who besieged Umar ibn Hafs at Tabna. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The two states
They founded two states. The first was in the central Maghrib under Ya'la ibn Muhammad, who was the planner of Ifkan, and who preached in the name of al-Nasir from Tahart to Tangier, until Jawhar the secretary killed him in the year 347 AH. The second was at Sala in the far Maghrib after him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
