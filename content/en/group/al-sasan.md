## Summary
The Sasanian house is the ruling family of the Sasanian Empire, which the record takes from Ardashir Babakan to Yazdegerd III {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}. The state itself has its own page, [[polity/sasanian-empire|the Sasanian Empire]]; this entry is for the family.
## In Nomisma
The Nomisma numismatic vocabulary lists the house as the family concept «sassanid_dynasty», with the preferred English label «Sassanid Dynasty» and «House of Sasan» as an alternative label, and gives it a start year of 224 and an end year of 651 in the Common Era. A query of 14 November 2026 found no coin or coin type in its linked collections that names the family itself as authority or issuer. {cite:nomisma sassanid_dynasty}
