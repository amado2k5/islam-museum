## Overview
Al-Idrisi says the people of India are seven kinds. For him the Brahmins are the worshippers of India who do not drink wine. They are followed by the Kastriyun, then the Shudhariyun, who are the farmers, then the people of crafts, then the people of songs and instruments. Most of the people of India belong to forty-two creeds in belief. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.96}
