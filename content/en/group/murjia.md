## Overview
The Murji'a, according to the book's description, claim that faith is speech without deed, that faith neither increases nor decreases, and that people are not distinguished in it. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96} The book reports from their view that whoever believes with his tongue and does no deed is truly a believer, and is judged to be in error. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.96}
