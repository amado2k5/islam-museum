## Summary
An Arab tribe to which Ibn Ishaq traces Asma bint Umays, calling her "al-Khath'amiyya" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}.

## In the raid of Surad on Jurash
Khath'am took refuge in [[place/jurash|Jurash]]. They entered it with its people when they heard of the Muslims' march. [[person/surad-ibn-abdullah-al-azdi|Surad ibn Abdullah]] besieged them, then struck them at Mount Shukr. See [[event/surad-campaign-against-jurash|the Surad campaign against Jurash]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}. Ibn Ishaq says that Khath'am used to raid the Azd in the Jahiliyya, and that they transgressed in the sacred month {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/235}.

## According to al-Mas'udi
Their lineage is disputed. Some attach them to Yemen, and others make them descendants of Anmar ibn Nizar {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.431}.
