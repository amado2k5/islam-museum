## Summary
Rahma Islamic Organisation is an Ethiopian street-dawah organisation publishing public-preaching videos in Ethiopian cities in Amharic. {cite:facebook-rahma-islamic-organisation} Its Facebook page — named ራህማ እስላማዊ ድርጅት የጎዳና ደዕዋ (Rahma Islamic Organisation — Street Dawah) — counted about 240 thousand followers by Facebook post metadata on a post dated 19 January 2026, read by the wave harvest on 7 November 2026. {cite:facebook-rahma-islamic-organisation} Its TikTok account (@rahma_street_daewa_eth) counted 576,400 followers when read on 7 November 2026. {cite:tiktok-rahma-islamic-organisation}
