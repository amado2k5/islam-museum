## Summary
Al Amin Islamic Media is a Bangladeshi YouTube channel (@alaminislamicmedia) that aggregates Bengali waz lectures and publishes them at high volume. It counted about 606 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 7 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-al-amin-islamic-media} The channel's name is a generic one in common use, and no named head or operator was identified for it in this pass; this record concerns this channel only and extends to no institution or publisher of a similar name. {cite:vidiq-al-amin-islamic-media}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 6,415 videos and about 78 million total views for the channel, an upload volume above most channels in this scene. {cite:vidiq-al-amin-islamic-media} The channel carries Abdullah Al Maruf heavily, alongside other Bangladeshi preachers; its carriage of a preacher's lectures makes none of its audience that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-al-amin-islamic-media}
