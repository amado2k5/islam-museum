## Summary
Semerkand TV is a Turkish religious television broadcaster of the Semerkand Yayın Grubu (Semerkand Media Group), a media company the Turkish Wikipedia article on the group describes as of Sufi type and headquartered in Türkiye. {cite:wikipedia-semerkand-yayin-grubu-tr} The broadcaster was launched on 29 May 2010. {cite:wikipedia-semerkand-tv-diq}

## The group and the launch
The Turkish Wikipedia article states that the group's foundations were laid in 1999 with the start of the Semerkand magazine, and that its television channel Semerkand TV began broadcasting in 2010. {cite:wikipedia-semerkand-yayin-grubu-tr} The infobox of the Zazaki Wikipedia article on Semerkand TV gives the founding date precisely as 29 May 2010 and its language as Turkish. {cite:wikipedia-semerkand-tv-diq} The group is the broadcaster's institutional background as the article states it, and no faith value is written in this record for the group or the channel. {cite:wikipedia-semerkand-yayin-grubu-tr}
