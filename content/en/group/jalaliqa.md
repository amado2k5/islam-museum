## Overview
According to al-Mas'udi, the Jalaliqa (Galicians) are more fierce than the [[group/ifranja|Franks]] and more damaging. One of them can hold off a number of Franks. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.357}

## Their kings and religion
He says they were the peoples most at war with the people of al-Andalus. Their king up to the year 332 was Radmir; before him was Ardun, and before him Adhbushan. They profess Christianity according to the Monophysite view, like the Franks. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.360}
