## Summary
Qtv Natore is a regional YouTube channel from Natore district, Bangladesh (@qtvnatoreofficial), run by a local media house, publishing recordings of Bengali waz mahfils. It counted about 374 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 3 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-qtv-natore} No individual head of the channel was named in this pass. {cite:vidiq-qtv-natore}

## Programmes and platforms
vidIQ records 2,161 videos and about 157 million total views for the channel. {cite:vidiq-qtv-natore} The channel carries gatherings by a range of Bangladeshi preachers, among them Mufti Amir Hamza, and streams some mahfils live — a live stream of 19 October 2026 appeared in the vidIQ read — so it is active in the current season; its carriage of a preacher's gatherings makes none of its audience that preacher's followers and creates no membership link between the channel and him. {cite:vidiq-qtv-natore}
