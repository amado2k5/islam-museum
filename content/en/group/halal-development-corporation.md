## Summary
The Halal Development Corporation (HDC), formerly the Halal Industry Development Corporation, is a Malaysian federal government agency mandated as custodian of Malaysia's halal economy. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en}

## Founding and mission
It was founded on 18 September 2006, with government backing, to bring together the clusters of Malaysia's halal sector and raise its economic contribution. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en} It designs programmes for small, medium and large firms and works with federal and state bodies on halal standards and trade. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en} It drew up the Halal Industry Masterplan 2008–2020 and, later, the Master Plan 2030. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en}

## Role and partnerships
It is secretariat of Malaysia's Halal Industry Development Council, chaired by the deputy prime minister, and coordinates four aims: policy and monitoring, certification enhancement and enforcement, standards and traceability, and industry and entrepreneurship. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en} In October 2013 it signed a memorandum for a RM280 million Halal Development Fund, and since 2019 it has expanded into Japan, Korea and Taiwan. {cite:wikipedia-halal-development-corporation-en}
