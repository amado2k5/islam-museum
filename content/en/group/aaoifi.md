## Summary
The Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI) is a Bahrain-based not-for-profit organisation established to maintain and promote Shariah standards for Islamic financial institutions, participants and the industry as a whole. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en} It runs professional development programmes, among them the Islamic legal accountant programme and a forensic auditing programme. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en}

## Founding
It was established under an Agreement of Association signed by Islamic financial institutions in Algiers on 26 February 1990, and registered in Bahrain on 27 March 1991. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en} It has members from more than 45 countries, including central banks and Islamic financial institutions. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en}

## Reach of its standards
Its standards are applied in Bahrain, the Dubai International Financial Centre, Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar, Sudan and Syria, and the authorities in Australia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and South Africa have issued guidelines derived from them. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en}

## Structure
It has a general assembly, a board of trustees and an accounting and auditing standards board of fifteen part-time members each, a Shari'ah committee, an executive committee, and a full-time secretary-general heading the general secretariat. {cite:wikipedia-aaoifi-en}
