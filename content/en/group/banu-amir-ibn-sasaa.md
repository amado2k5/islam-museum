## Overview
A large Arab tribe of Qays in Najd, distinct from Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy of Quraysh. Among its branches are [[group/banu-kilab|Banu Kilab]] and Banu Ja'far ibn Kilab, from whom came the chiefs of its delegation. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/213}

## Their delegation in Ibn Hisham's biography
The delegation of Banu Amir came to the Prophet in the year 9 AH. It included [[person/amir-ibn-al-tufayl|Amir ibn al-Tufayl]], [[person/arbad-ibn-qays|Arbad ibn Qays]] ibn Juz ibn Khalid ibn Ja'far, and [[person/jabbar-ibn-salma|Jabbar ibn Salma]] ibn Malik ibn Ja'far. Ibn Ishaq says these three were the chiefs of the people, and he describes them in harsh terms, which are his own and not the museum's. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/213} During the delegation came [[event/assassination-attempt-of-amir-and-arbad|the plot of Amir and Arbad]]. Amir then died in the house of a woman of [[group/banu-salul|Banu Salul]], and Arbad died by a thunderbolt. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/214} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215} Arbad's maternal brother, [[person/labid-ibn-rabia|Labid ibn Rabi'a]], was the poet of the tribe. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}
