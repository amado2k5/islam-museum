## Overview
A body gathering senior sages and scholars of the Muslim world, headed by Grand Imam Ahmad al-Tayyib as its first elected head; the book dates its founding to 21 Ramadan 1435 (19 July 2014) and its aim to fostering peace in societies {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 17}. It held its seventh periodic session in Jakarta during the visit, resolving to send peace convoys to Burma {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar}.
