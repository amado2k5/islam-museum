## Overview
A tribe of [[group/ghatafan|Ghatafan]] at Fadak. In his list of expeditions, Ibn Ishaq mentions the raid of Bashir ibn Sa'd on Banu Murra at Fadak. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/260} He then relates [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-murra|the raid of Ghalib ibn Abd Allah on the land of Banu Murra]], in which [[person/mirdas-ibn-nahik|Mirdas ibn Nahik]], their ally from al-Huraqa of Juhayna, was struck. Usama ibn Zayd and a man of the Ansar killed him after he said "There is no god but God". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}
