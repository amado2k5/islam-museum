## Overview
The Crusades were Latin military campaigns against al-Sham and Jerusalem. The Crusaders took Antioch by force in 1098, and then Jerusalem in June 1099, where a great massacre followed {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.238}.

## The Muslim response
Saladin defeated the Crusaders at Hattin in 1187, and entered Jerusalem on 27 Rajab 583 AH after the Crusaders asked for terms {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.110} {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.111}.

## The end
The Crusades ended with the expulsion of the Crusaders from the East, after rivalries among them and the rise of national interests had worn down their unity {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.243}.
