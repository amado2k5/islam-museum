## Overview
The battle of Hunayn, 8 AH / 630 CE, followed the conquest of Mecca. The Muslims faced Hawazin and Thaqif. At first they were thrown into confusion by their numbers, then God granted them victory, and they took the spoils and captives as far as al-Ta'if. {cite:wikidata}
