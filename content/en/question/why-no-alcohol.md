## Short answer
Wine is forbidden in Islam. This ruling is one of the best-known rulings that people know about the law, and its evidence and rulings are set out in the entry on wine.

## Detailed answer
Islam forbids wine and all intoxicants, and it is one of the best-known rulings that people ask about. The ruling, its evidence and its meanings are set out in the entry [[concept/khamr|Wine]].
The entry presents what is reported about wine in the texts and in tradition. It notes that the jurists regarded all intoxicants as having the same ruling as wine, as the entry [[concept/muskir|Intoxicant]] presents this as a description of the ruling.
This question joins jurisprudence and daily life, since it is linked to the lawful and the unlawful in food and drink, which the entry [[concept/halal-haram|The lawful and the unlawful]] sets out.
