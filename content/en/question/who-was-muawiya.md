## Overview
Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan was the founder of the Umayyad state. He was a scribe of revelation and governor of al-Sham, then caliph for twenty years. He is a figure on whom Sunnis agree and on whom Shi'a differ. {cite:wikidata}
