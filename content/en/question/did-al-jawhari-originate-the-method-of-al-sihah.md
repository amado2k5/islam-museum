## Summary
Al-Jawhari's [[work/al-sihah-jawhari|al-Sihah]] arranges Arabic words in chapters by the last radical of the root, then by the first, second and third letters, and al-Jawhari was long regarded as the pioneer of this method until researchers of the fourteenth Islamic century disputed it. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.17} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.36} The editor of al-Sihah, [[person/ahmad-abd-al-ghafur-attar|Ahmad Abd al-Ghafur Attar]], sets out the dispute in an essay, "al-Jawhari, Originator of the Method of al-Sihah", appended to his second edition. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.5}

## The case for al-Bandaniji's priority
In 1394 AH Dr Bakri Shaykh Amin published an article, "Al-Jasir and Attar Expose a Scholarly Error", reporting that [[person/hamad-al-jasir|Hamad al-Jasir]] had shown in his journal al-Arab that [[person/al-bandaniji|Abu Bishr al-Yaman ibn Abi al-Yaman al-Bandaniji]], who died in 284 AH, preceded al-Jawhari in the rhyme arrangement by more than a hundred years, producing photographs of the manuscript of [[work/kitab-al-taqfiya|Kitab al-Taqfiya]], and noting that the book was obscure and seldom mentioned. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.17} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.18} Al-Jasir was followed by the editor of Kitab al-Taqfiya, Dr Khalil Ibrahim al-Atiyya of the University of Basra, who credited al-Bandaniji with the invention. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.24}

## The claim that he took from al-Farabi
Attar notes that the German orientalist [[person/fritz-krenkow|Fritz Krenkow]] had preceded al-Jasir in denying al-Jawhari's originality, claiming that he took into al-Sihah the material of his uncle [[person/ishaq-ibn-ibrahim-al-farabi|al-Farabi]]'s [[work/diwan-al-adab|Diwan al-Adab]] — perhaps prompted by Yaqut's statement that he saw a copy of Diwan al-Adab in al-Jawhari's hand, read with Abu Ibrahim at Farab. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.6} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.7} Another researcher, as Bakri Shaykh Amin reports, held that al-Farabi (d. 350 AH) preceded al-Jawhari in the system of chapter and section. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.18}

## Attar's reply
Attar holds al-Jawhari the originator of his method, arguing: al-Bandaniji wrote not a dictionary but a book on rhyming, ordering words by their spoken surface form rather than their roots and without regard to their first letters, whereas al-Jawhari used only the final radical and then ordered by the first, second and third letters. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.32} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.35} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.36} Al-Bandaniji and his book were obscure, so neither al-Jawhari nor his contemporaries saw it, nor do later dictionaries mention it; priority in time does not cancel originality when the later author never saw the earlier work. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.6} Arranging words by their endings, moreover, had been done before by the poets themselves through the single rhyme. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.35} As for al-Farabi, Attar grants that he "laid some of the foundations of the method of al-Sihah", but holds that al-Jawhari far surpassed him and perfected the system, and that the two dictionaries differ so much in method, arrangement and number of entries that no charge of plagiarism stands. {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.6} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.7}

## Views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Ahmad Abd al-Ghafur Attar**: al-Jawhari originated his method; al-Bandaniji wrote a rhyme book, not a dictionary; al-Farabi laid some foundations {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.36} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.6}.
- **Sunni: Hamad al-Jasir and Khalil Ibrahim al-Atiyya**: al-Bandaniji preceded al-Jawhari in arranging by word endings by about a century {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.18} {cite:sihah-attar-1979-v1 p.24}.
- **Academic: Fritz Krenkow, as reported by Attar**: al-Jawhari took the material of al-Farabi's Diwan al-Adab {cite:sihah-attar-1979-scan-b p.6}.
- **Not yet given here**: the articles of al-Jasir and Krenkow have not yet been checked at first hand, nor al-Atiyya's introduction to Kitab al-Taqfiya.
:::
