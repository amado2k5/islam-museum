## Short answer
Taraweeh is the night prayer in Ramadan performed in congregation after the 'isha prayer. It is a strongly recommended sunna according to the people of the Sunna, and it becomes more emphasised in the last ten nights.

## Detailed answer
Taraweeh has been known in the mosques of Muslims since the first age, and it is one of the signs of the month of Ramadan {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/taraweeh|taraweeh]] sets out its form, and it is linked to [[practice/sawm-ramadan|fasting in Ramadan]] and the holy month.
