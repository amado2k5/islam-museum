## Short answer
The miswak is cleaning the mouth with a twig of the arak tree. It is a sunna that was on the Prophet's mouth, and it has remained a daily practice of hygiene in many homes to this day.

## Detailed answer
The recommendation of the miswak appears in sound hadiths on ablution and night prayer {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/miswak|the miswak]] sets out its place in the sunna and in custom.
