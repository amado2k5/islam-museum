## Overview
The treaty of Hudaybiyya took place in Dhu al-Qa'da 6 AH / 628 CE. It was a peace between the Prophet and Quraysh whose outward aspect was hardship and whose inward aspect was victory. The Qur'an called it a clear victory. {cite:wikidata}
