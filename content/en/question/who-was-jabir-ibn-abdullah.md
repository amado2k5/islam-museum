## Overview
Jabir ibn Abd Allah al-Ansari (d. 697 CE) was the last of the Companions to die in Medina. He witnessed the pledge of al-'Aqaba and seventeen campaigns, and he narrated 1,540 hadiths. {cite:wikidata}
