## Short answer
In the [[event/farewell-sermon-at-arafat|Farewell Sermon]], the Prophet said that [[concept/nasi|intercalation (al-nasi')]] is an increase in disbelief, by which those who disbelieve are led astray: they make it lawful one year and unlawful another year, to match the number of what God has made unlawful. He said that time has returned to its form from the day God created the heavens and the earth, and that the number of months with God is twelve months, of which four are sacred {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/251}.

## Detailed answer
The four sacred months in the sermon are three in succession, and Rajab of Mudar, which falls between Jumada and Sha'ban {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/251}. The editors of the 1936 edition explain that the Prophet said "Rajab of Mudar" because Rabi'a used to make Ramadan unlawful and call it Rajab. He made clear that it was Rajab of Mudar, not Rajab of Rabi'a {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/251}. The meaning of "time has returned to its form" is that the pilgrimage fell in the true Dhu al-Hijja, after intercalation had shifted the months. The passage is close to [[passage/quran-9-36|At-Tawba 36]] and [[passage/quran-9-37|At-Tawba 37]].

## Follow-up questions
