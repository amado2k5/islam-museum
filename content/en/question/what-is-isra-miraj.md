## Short answer
The Isra and Mi'raj is a night journey of the Prophet Muhammad from the Sacred Mosque to al-Aqsa Mosque, and then his ascent to the heavens, as tradition narrates it. In it the five daily prayers were prescribed.

## Detailed answer
The Isra and Mi'raj was a night journey of the Prophet Muhammad. It began at the Sacred Mosque in Mecca and went to al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem, followed by his ascent to the heavens, as the entry on [[event/isra-and-miraj|the Isra and Mi'raj]] presents.
Tradition reports that the five prayers were prescribed on this journey, and they are linked to [[practice/salah|prayer]].
The entry presents the account as a narration from tradition. It mentions what scholars have differed about in its details and its timing.
