## Overview
Taqi al-Din Ibn Taymiyya (1263–1328) was a combative Hanbali jurist. He was among the deepest theorists of the Sunni heritage and an inspiration for modern reform. He was imprisoned many times and died in the citadel. {cite:wikidata}
