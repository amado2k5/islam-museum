## Short answer
Tawaf is circling the Kaaba seven times, starting and ending at the Black Stone. It is a pillar of the hajj and umrah, and it is recommended on entering the Masjid al-Haram.

## Detailed answer
Tawaf is one of the visible rites, and the Qur'an mentions the House and those who circle it in Surat al-Baqara {cite:tanzil-quran}.
The entry on [[practice/tawaf|tawaf]] sets out its form, and it is linked to the [[building/kaaba|Kaaba]] and the [[practice/hajj|hajj]].
