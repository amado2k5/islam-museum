## Summary

On [[passage/quran-2-7|al-Baqara 7]] [[work/al-kashshaf|al-Kashshaf]] stops at the meaning of the seal on hearts and hearing and the covering on sight; al-Zamakhshari says sealing and concealing are twins, since securing a thing by stamping a seal on it conceals and covers it. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.41} In the edition's notes Ibn al-Munayyir remarks that this is the 'first' place in the book where al-Zamakhshari departs from the plain sense of the text. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.42} The question is a branch of the question of the creation of human acts; see [[question/what-is-qadar|What is qadar?]].

## Disagreement and views

:::perspectives
- **Sunni (Maliki): Ibn al-Munayyir in al-Intisaf**: the seal is truly God's act; nothing happens but by His power, and the verse ascribes it to Him explicitly {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.42}.
- **Other view (Mu'tazili): al-Zamakhshari in al-Kashshaf**: the seal is figurative; it is ascribed to God to show entrenchment or because He gave the power, the real sealer being Satan or the disbeliever {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.41} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.42} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.43}.
:::
