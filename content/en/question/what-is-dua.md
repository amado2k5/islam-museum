## Short answer
Dua is a servant's humble asking of God. The Qur'an commands it and promises an answer, and scholars regard it as the essence of worship.

## Detailed answer
Dua is an act of worship in itself, and the Sunna records its etiquette, such as facing the qibla and raising the hands {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/dua|dua]] sets out its meaning and etiquette, and it is linked to [[practice/dhikr|remembrance of God]] and to [[practice/salah|prayer]].
