## Short answer
Taqwa is when a servant places between himself and what he fears and dreads a shield that protects him from it. It is a meaning the Qur'an repeats when it commands worship.

## Detailed answer
Taqwa, in language and in the Islamic law, is when a person places a shield between himself and what he dreads. In Islam it is the fear of God and obedience to Him, as the entry [[concept/taqwa|Taqwa]] sets out.
The Qur'an links fasting to taqwa in [[passage/quran-2-183|Al-Baqara 183]], so fasting counts among the means to taqwa, as the entry [[practice/sawm-ramadan|Fasting in Ramadan]] sets out.
It is a meaning from which many moral qualities and acts of worship branch out. The entry [[concept/iman|Iman]] explains its connection to belief.
