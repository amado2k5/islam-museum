## Short answer
The aqiqa is the slaughter of two sheep for a boy and one for a girl on the seventh day after birth, with shaving of the head and naming. According to the majority it is a sunna.

## Detailed answer
The aqiqa is one of the birth sunnas that the Prophet guided to, and it is a season of family joy in most countries {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/aqiqa|the aqiqa]] sets out its form and timing.
