## Short answer
Sa'y is walking between Safa and Marwa seven times during the hajj and umrah, commemorating Hajar searching for water for her son Isma'il.

## Detailed answer
The Qur'an names Safa and Marwa as among the symbols of God in Surat al-Baqara {cite:tanzil-quran}.
The entry on [[practice/sai|sa'y]] sets out its form, and it is linked to the [[practice/hajj|hajj]] and to [[practice/tawaf|circumambulation]] among the rites.
