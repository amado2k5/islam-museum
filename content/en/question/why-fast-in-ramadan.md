## Short answer
Fasting in the month of Ramadan is obligatory, and it is one of the [[concept/five-pillars|five pillars of Islam]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. The Qur'an links it to taqwa in [[passage/quran-2-183|Al-Baqara 183]], and mentions in [[passage/quran-2-185|Al-Baqara 185]] that the month is the one in which the Qur'an was revealed.

## Detailed answer
Fasting is abstaining from food, drink and all other things that break the fast, from dawn until sunset. This act of worship has social and spiritual dimensions, such as charity and night prayer, which the entry [[practice/sawm-ramadan|Fasting in Ramadan]] explains.

## Common questions
- Scholars differ over how the beginning of the month is established. This is one of the points of jurisprudential disagreement that this entry does not rule on.

## Not covered here
