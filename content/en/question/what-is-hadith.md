## Short answer
The Prophetic hadith is what was transmitted from the Prophet as saying, action or approval. For Muslims it is the second source of legislation after the Qur'an.

## Detailed answer
Hadith was gathered into great collections such as the [[work/sahih-al-bukhari|Sahih of al-Bukhari]] and Sahih Muslim, and the science of [[concept/mustalah-al-hadith|the terminology of hadith]] arose to serve it.
The entry on [[concept/al-hadith-al-nabawi|the Prophetic hadith]] sets out its meaning and collections, and it is linked to [[work/quran|the Qur'an]] among the roots of legislation.
