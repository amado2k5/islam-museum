## Summary
The question concerns the relation between the theory of evolution and what the Qur'an relates about the creation of humans, and people hold different views on it. So far this entry presents the view of the writer [[person/mostafa-mahmoud|Mostafa Mahmoud]] in his book [[work/hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid|Hiwar ma'a Sadiqi al-Mulhid]]; other views are not yet documented with a defined source. {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 124}

## The objection as he frames it
Mostafa Mahmoud has his atheist friend say that the sciences of evolution contradict Adam's being made at once from clay, and see humans as the last of a chain of animal stages, with apes as their "cousins", on the evidence of anatomical resemblance. {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 124}

:::perspectives
- **Ahl al-Sunna: Mostafa Mahmoud (a modern writer's view).** He holds that the Qur'an tells not of instant creation but of creation "in stages and phases over a long divine time": from clay, then an extract, then fashioning and forming, then the breathing of the spirit; he holds that "then" in divine time may mean millions of years, citing [[passage/quran-23-12|al-Mu'minun 12]], [[passage/quran-71-14|Nuh 14]], [[passage/quran-71-17|Nuh 17]] and [[passage/quran-32-7|al-Sajda 7]]. {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 124} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 125} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 126} He accepts anatomical resemblance as evidence of one Creator, but not as necessary evidence that all species descend from one ancestor. {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 126} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 127} He says Darwin was right in his anatomical premise and in affirming evolution, and wrong to explain ascent by the struggle for survival alone, which cannot explain the beauty of peacock feathers and butterfly wings; that random mutation cannot explain the design he sees in creation; and that each species' distinct chromosome map is a third obstacle. {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 129} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 130} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 131} {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 132} He concludes that evolution is a fact, "stated in explicit words in the Qur'an", but its stages and manner "are still a riddle". {cite:mm-hiwar-ma-sadiqi-al-mulhid-awda-1986 p. 132}
