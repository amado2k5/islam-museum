## Short answer
Muslims face the Ka'ba in Mecca when they pray. The first direction of prayer was toward Jerusalem, before it was changed to the Ka'ba.

## Detailed answer
Muslims face the Ka'ba in the Sacred Mosque in Mecca when they pray. This direction is called the qibla, and it is known in [[place/mecca|Mecca]].
Tradition reports that the first qibla was toward Jerusalem, and that it was then changed to the Ka'ba, as the entry [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] sets out.
The rules on the qibla in prayer and how to determine it are set out in the entry [[practice/salah|Prayer]].
