## Short answer
Hajj is the intention to visit the Sacred House in Mecca to perform specific rites. It is one of the [[concept/five-pillars|five pillars of Islam]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. It is a duty owed to God by whoever is able to make the journey, as in [[passage/quran-3-97|Al 'Imran 97]].

## Detailed answer
[[passage/quran-22-27|Surat al-Hajj 27]] calls for proclaiming the pilgrimage among the people. The rites are performed on specific days, and Hajj is distinguished from the umra. Its details are in the entry on [[practice/hajj|Hajj]].

## Follow-up questions
- Hajj is obligatory once in a lifetime for whoever is able. Ability includes physical capacity and financial capacity.
