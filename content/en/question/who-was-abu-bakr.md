## Overview
Abu Bakr al-Siddiq was the first caliph. He was among the earliest men to embrace Islam and was the companion of the Hijra. He ruled for two years, unified the Muslims against the apostates, and carried the conquests into Iraq and Syria. {cite:wikidata}
