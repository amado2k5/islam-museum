## Short answer
The Kaaba is a structure in the Sacred Mosque in Mecca. Muslims face it in their prayers, pilgrims circle it, and it is the site of the Sacred House.

## Detailed answer
The Kaaba is a structure in the Sacred Mosque in Mecca. It is the site of the Sacred House, which Muslims face in their prayers and circle during Hajj and the umra. [[place/mecca|Mecca]] sets its location in the wider context.
It is linked to the duty of [[practice/hajj|Hajj]], since the rites are performed around it.
The entry presents the sources that mention its construction, and the historical disagreements about its origin, without issuing a ruling.
