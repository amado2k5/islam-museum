## Overview
Damascus, al-Sham, is the oldest capital in continuous existence in history. It was the capital of the Umayyads for ninety-six years, and it holds the Umayyad Mosque, the shrines of the Prophet's family, and the madrasas of Ibn Taymiyya and Ibn Asakir. {cite:wikidata}
