## Summary
"The Praiseworthy Station" is what the Prophet is promised in [[passage/quran-17-79|al-Isra' 79]]. In [[work/kitab-al-sunnah-khallal|Kitab al-Sunna]], [[person/abu-bakr-al-khallal|al-Khallal]] gives it a long chapter, "on the Praiseworthy Station", gathering the versions of [[person/mujahid-ibn-jabr|Mujahid ibn Jabr]]'s explanation of the verse, "He will seat him with Him on the Throne", and the sayings of [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]]'s followers accepting it. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 145} {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 160} The question is disputed, and the book and its editor argue for one side.

## The chapter in Kitab al-Sunna
- Al-Khallal gives the report through chains such as Ahmad ibn Asram, from al-'Ala' ibn 'Amr, from Ibn Fudayl, from Layth, from Mujahid: "He will seat him with Him on the Throne." {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 160}
- He records that [[person/abu-bakr-al-marrudhi|Abu Bakr al-Marrudhi]] read them his book "The Praiseworthy Station" once in the congregational mosque; the editor says the book is lost and that al-Marrudhi wrote it when a man he calls "al-Tirmidhi the Jahmi" publicly rejected Mujahid's report. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 160}
- Al-Khallal relates that he read Kitab al-Sunna in [[place/tarsus|Tarsus]] for years, and when he read this chapter in the year ninety-two some followers of "al-Tirmidhi" rejected it; he shunned them and wrote to his teachers in Baghdad, who sent him a letter affirming it, which he read to his companions and which people copied. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 171}
- That letter says the seating is at one time and the intercession at another, and that they do not deny the Muslims' view that the Station is intercession. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 188-189}

## The authorities the editor cites
The editor, 'Adil Al Hamdan, introduces the chapter as al-Khallal's record of the early authorities' agreement on this merit, and quotes among others: {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 145-147}
- Ahmad ibn Hanbal, asked about Mujahid's report: scholars accepted it; we accept the report as it came; and it is not from Mujahid alone but was also related from Ibn 'Abbas. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 145} {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 171}
- [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]], author of the Sunan: whoever denies this is suspect in our eyes; people have always related it. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 146}
- 'Abd Allah ibn Ahmad, al-Saghani, [[person/ahmad-ibn-salman-al-najjad|al-Najjad]], [[person/al-ajurri|al-Ajurri]], [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni]] in his ode, and [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]]. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 146-147}
- He quotes harsh judgements on those who rejected the report, calling them Jahmis and innovators, ascribed to Ishaq ibn Rahwayh, Harun ibn Ma'ruf and others. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 147} He quotes [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] in al-'Uluw that Mujahid would hardly have said this without having been taught it. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 171}

:::perspectives
**Sunni (Hanbali): al-Khallal, al-Marrudhi and those they quote.** The Praiseworthy Station includes the Prophet's seating on the Throne, as Mujahid explained it, a report scholars accepted; this does not deny that it is intercession — the seating is at one time and the intercession at another. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 145-147} {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 188-189}

**Sunni (Shafi'i): Ibn Hajar as the editor quotes him.** All the views can be reduced to the general intercession: the banner of praise, his praise of his Lord, his speech before Him and his sitting on His Kursi are features of the Station in which he intercedes for the judgement of creation; the editor also quotes al-Qassab that the Prophet's own explanation as intercession does not rule out Mujahid's, and Muhammad ibn Ibrahim Al al-Shaykh that the two can be reconciled. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 188-189}

**Another view, in its opponents' words: those who rejected Mujahid's report.** Al-Khallal says followers of a man he calls "al-Tirmidhi the innovator" rejected the chapter in Tarsus, and the book and its editor describe them in harsh terms; their own arguments have not yet been taken from their books. {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 171} {cite:kitab-al-sunna-khallal-al-hamdan-2018 p. 147}
:::

## Not covered here
The views of exegetes who limited the Praiseworthy Station to intercession, criticism of the chain of Mujahid's report by those who weakened it, and the identity of the "al-Tirmidhi" mentioned are to be added from their sources.
