## Overview
Mu'adh ibn Jabal was the most knowledgeable of the community in the lawful and the unlawful. The Prophet sent him as judge and teacher to Yemen. He died young in the plague of Amwas in 639, and the scholars of his time agreed on his jurisprudence. {cite:wikidata}
