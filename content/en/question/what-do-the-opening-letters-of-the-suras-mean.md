## Summary

On the opening of al-Baqara, [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] says in [[work/zad-al-masir|Zad al-Masir]] that scholars differed over 'Alif Lam Mim' and the other [[concept/al-muqattaat|separated letters]] at the start of suras in six views, and then gives five views on 'Alif Lam Mim' in particular. {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.24} {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25} {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.26}

## Views on 'Alif Lam Mim' in particular

Ibn al-Jawzi gives five views on 'Alif Lam Mim': that it is among the ambiguous; that it means 'I am God, I know best', reported by Abu al-Duha from Ibn Abbas and held by Ibn Mas'ud and Sa'id ibn Jubayr; that it is an oath, reported by Abu Salih from Ibn Abbas and by Khalid al-Hadhdha' from Ikrima; and that it is letters of names — among them that alif is from 'Allah', lam from 'Jibril' and mim from 'Muhammad', from Ibn Abbas. {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.26}

## The editor's note

The editor of the Dar al-Kitab al-Arabi edition cites Ibn Kathir: without repetition the letters are fourteen; they were repeated to sharpen the challenge; whoever claims they encode numbers from which the times of events can be extracted claims what is not his, and the hadith cited for it is weak. {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25}

## Disagreement and views

:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Sha'bi and those who agreed, and al-Baghawi**: their knowledge is left to God {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25} {cite:tahdhib-tafsir-baghawi-tayba-2009 p.11}.
- **Sunni: Ali, Ibn Abbas, Abu al-Aliya and al-Rabi'**: letters of God's names {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25}.
- **Sunni: Ibn Abbas, Ikrima and Ibn Qutayba**: letters God swears by {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25}.
- **Sunni: al-Farra and Qutrub**: a pointer to inimitability: the Qur'an is made of their letters {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.25} {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.119}.
- **Sunni: Zayd ibn Aslam and his son**: names of the suras {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.26}.
- **Sunni: al-Akhfash, al-Zajjaj and Ibn al-Anbari**: Arab shorthand, one letter for a word {cite:zad-al-masir-dar-al-kitab-al-arabi-2001-v1 p.26}.
:::

## Notes for researchers
