## Short answer
The afterlife is the belief in life after death, which includes resurrection, judgement, Paradise and Hell. It is one of the articles of belief set out in the works of theology and hadith.

## Detailed answer
In Islam, the afterlife is life after death. It includes the punishment of the grave, resurrection, judgement, the bridge (al-sirat), the scales, Paradise and Hell, as the articles of belief in Al-Tahawi's creed set out in the entry [[concept/akhira|The afterlife]].
The afterlife is part of faith, since it is one of its pillars, as the entry [[concept/iman|Iman]] sets out.
Accounts of the afterlife differ among the transmitted reports and commentaries. The entry [[concept/akhira|The afterlife]] presents the basic principles without detailing the disagreements.
