## Short answer
The Masjid al-Haram is the mosque of Mecca in which the Kaaba stands. It is the qibla of Muslims and the place where the rites of the hajj and umrah are centred.

## Detailed answer
The Kaaba stands in the courtyard of the [[building/masjid-al-haram|Masjid al-Haram]] in [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. Congregational prayers are held there and [[practice/tawaf|circumambulation]] is performed around it.
The entry sets out the construction of the mosque and the position of the [[building/kaaba|Kaaba]] within it.
