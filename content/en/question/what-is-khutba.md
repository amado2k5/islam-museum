## Short answer
The khutba is a sermon given before the Friday prayer and in the two festivals. It contains praise of God, admonition and supplication for the Muslims.

## Detailed answer
The sermon is a condition for the validity of the Friday prayer according to most jurists, and the text of Surat al-Jumu'a relates to it {cite:tanzil-quran}.
The entry on [[practice/khutba|the sermon]] sets out its place and form, and it is linked to the [[practice/adhan|adhan]] and to [[practice/salah|prayer]].
