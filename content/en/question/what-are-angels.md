## Overview
The angels are God's servants of light who carry out His commands; among them Jibril with revelation, Mika'il with provision, Israfil with the trumpet, the Angel of Death, and the guardians of the Fire and Paradise and the noble recorders. They are a pillar of faith for the Sunnis and the Community {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §72} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14974}.

## In the creed
- The Tahawiyya creed states: "we believe in the angels and the prophets and the Books sent down upon the messengers, and we bear witness that they were upon manifest truth" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §72}.
- Ibn Abi al-Izz's commentary elaborates the meaning of belief in them and their attributes {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.
- "Malak" in the lexicon is a subtle luminous body, and "mala'ika" is its plural, and their dwelling is the heavens {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14974}.

## In the Qur'an
- "And when your Lord said to the angels: I am placing a vicegerent on earth" [[passage/quran-2-30|Al-Baqara 30]] — in it God's address to the angels {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "So when I had fashioned him and breathed into him of My spirit, fall prostrate to him * so the angels prostrated, all of them together" [[passage/quran-15-29|Al-Hijr 29]] — in it their prostration to Adam {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "We glorify You with praise" [[passage/quran-38-69|Sad 69]] — in it their glorification {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "Those who bear the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord with praise and believe in Him and seek forgiveness for those who believe" [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]] — in it their bearing of the Throne {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "Every soul has a guardian over it" [[passage/quran-82-11|Al-Infitar 11]] — in it their guarding of souls {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith
- "God created the angels of light before He created Adam of clay" — in a narration of al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- "The angels neither sleep nor flag" in glorification {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- "Upon every person there is a watcher and a recorder" — in it the recorders of deeds {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.

## Their names and works
- Jibril: charged with revelation, God's trustworthy {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- Mika'il: charged with provision and rain in the exegetes' view {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- Israfil: charged with blowing the trumpet {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- The Angel of Death: charged with taking souls {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- The keeper of Paradise: Ridwan, and the keeper of the Fire: Malik {cite:sahih-ibn-hibban} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- Munkar and Nakir: charged with questioning the dead in their grave {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- The noble recorders: charged with recording the deeds {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## Notes for researchers
- "The angels" in the Qur'an are "messengers" in places because they carry His command {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- Their number is uncountable; counting them is of the unseen {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
