## Overview
Angels are servants of God made of light who carry out His commands. They include Jibril, in charge of revelation; Mika'il, in charge of provision; Isra'fil, who will sound the trumpet; the Angel of Death; and the guardians of Hell and Paradise. {cite:wikidata}
