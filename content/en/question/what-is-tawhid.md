## Short answer
Tawhid is affirming God's oneness in worship and denying that He has any partner or equal. It is the root of Islamic belief.

## Detailed answer
In Islam, tawhid is affirming God's oneness in divinity and denying that He has any partner or equal. Al-Tahawi begins his account of belief by saying that God is one with no partner, as the entry [[concept/tawhid|Tawhid]] sets out.
Tawhid is linked to the shahada, since the statement "there is no god but God" carries this meaning, as the entry [[concept/shahada|The shahada]] sets out.
It is a subject that branches into topics of theology and creed, where the schools differ in the details, so it is presented here only in its basic form.
