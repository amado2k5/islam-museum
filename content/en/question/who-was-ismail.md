## Overview
Isma'il ibn Ibrahim, the one who was ransomed, was left as an infant in Mecca by his father. God caused the well of Zamzam to gush forth, he helped his father build the Kaaba, and from his line came the Arabized Arabs and Muhammad. {cite:wikidata}
