## Short answer
Seclusion (itikaf) is staying in the mosque for worship and withdrawal from distractions. It is best known in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

## Detailed answer
Seclusion is mentioned in Surat al-Baqara, and the Prophet kept seclusion in the last ten nights until he died {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/itikaf|seclusion]] sets out its form and timing, and it is linked to [[practice/sawm-ramadan|fasting]] and the month of Ramadan.
