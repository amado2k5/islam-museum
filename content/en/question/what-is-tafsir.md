## Overview
Tafsir is the explanation of the meanings of the Qur'an, its occasions of revelation and its rulings. Its literature has been recorded from al-Tabari, al-Zamakhshari, al-Razi and Ibn Kathir down to the modern age. {cite:wikidata}
