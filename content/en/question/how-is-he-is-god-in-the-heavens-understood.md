## Summary
The third verse of Surat al-An'am describes God as being in the heavens and on earth; see [[passage/quran-6-3|al-An'am 3]]. In [[work/tafsir-ibn-juzayy|al-Tashil]], [[person/ibn-juzayy-al-kalbi-mufassir|Ibn Juzayy]] gives three ways of attaching this phrase and understanding it, and weighs them. {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5} This entry sets them out as he gives them.

## The three readings
- 'In the heavens' attaches to the meaning of the name 'God', so the sense is like the verse saying He is the One who is God in heaven and God on earth (see [[passage/quran-43-84|al-Zukhruf 84]]), as one says 'the Commander of the Faithful is caliph in the east and the west'. Ibn Juzayy says this is preferable and more eloquent, because the name 'God' gathers all the attributes — knowledge, power, wisdom and the rest — and so combines them with brevity. {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}
- The phrase is the predicate, attached to an omitted participle, with a sense close to the first; or, it is said, the meaning is that He is in the heavens and the earth by His knowledge, like the verse saying He is with you wherever you are (see [[passage/quran-57-4|al-Hadid 4]]). Ibn Juzayy says this is supported by the context, which is about God's awareness and knowledge, since the verse goes on to say He knows what you conceal and what you reveal. {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}
- It attaches to an omitted word meaning 'the One worshipped in the heavens and on earth', an epithet of God. {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}

## Disagreement and views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Ibn Juzayy's preferred reading**: it attaches to the meaning of the name 'God' — He is the God in both; preferable and more eloquent {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}.
- **Sunni: a reading Ibn Juzayy reports and supports from the context**: He is in them by His knowledge {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}.
- **Sunni: a third reading Ibn Juzayy reports**: it attaches to an omitted 'the One worshipped' in them {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.5}.
- **Not yet given here**: other exegetes' views and the creedal discussion of the verse have not yet been added; later books will supply them.
:::
