## Short answer
Istikhara is two rak'as and a supplication by which a Muslim asks God for what is best in a matter about which he is undecided. The Prophet taught it to his companions as he taught them a surah.

## Detailed answer
Istikhara is a sound sunna, and its supplication is in Sahih al-Bukhari from Jabir {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/istikhara|istikhara]] sets out its form and timing.
