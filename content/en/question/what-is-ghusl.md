## Short answer
Ghusl is the major purification with water, washing the whole body after major impurity, sexual intercourse, menstruation and postpartum bleeding. It is a condition for valid prayer for someone in a state of major impurity.

## Detailed answer
The Qur'an made ghusl obligatory after major impurity in Surat al-Ma'ida {cite:tanzil-quran}.
The entry on [[practice/ghusl-al-janaba|ghusl]] sets out its manner and what requires it, and it is linked to [[practice/wudu|ablution]] in the chapters on purification.
