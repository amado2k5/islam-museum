## Summary
Much has been said about the creed of [[person/mahmud-al-alusi|Mahmud al-Alusi]], author of [[work/ruh-al-maani|Ruh al-Ma'ani]], because he read so widely in Islamic books of every school, as the thesis Juhud al-Alusi al-Balaghiyya notes. {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.54} This entry sets out his words on reinterpretation, al-Zirikli's description of him and the thesis's conclusion, without a verdict.

## In his own words
The thesis quotes Ruh al-Ma'ani: the safest course is to leave reinterpretation, because it is speaking about God without knowledge; he reinterprets only what the early generations reinterpreted — if they reinterpreted, he does, and if they consigned the meaning to God, he does; everything that comes from the Messenger of God with an acceptable chain he accepts wholeheartedly, and what goes against that he casts aside even if the ignorant accept it; and "if the hadith is sound, it is my school". {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.56}

## His link to the Naqshbandi path
The thesis's biography records that he read the question of the attributes with [[person/khalid-al-naqshbandi|Diya' al-Din Khalid al-Naqshbandi]] and took the Naqshbandi path from him. {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.30}

## The thesis's conclusion
The thesis opens the question with a warning about the gravity of judging Muslims', and especially scholars', beliefs, insisting on reading their words in the best light and not holding them to the implications of what they said; it then states that al-Alusi was "Salafi in creed on the whole", quotes Ibn Taymiyya, al-Dhahabi and Ibn al-Qayyim on dealing with scholars who combined error and right, and pauses with him on some questions of creed. {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.52} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.53} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.54} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.55} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.56}

## Disagreement and views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Alusi on himself**: the safest is to leave reinterpretation; he follows the early generations in reinterpreting or consigning; "if the hadith is sound, it is my school" {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.56}.
- **Sunni: al-Zirikli**: "Salafi in belief, a mujtahid" {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.44}.
- **Academic: the thesis Juhud al-Alusi al-Balaghiyya**: Salafi in creed on the whole, with reservations on some points and with his Naqshbandi link noted {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.54} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.56} {cite:juhud-al-alusi-al-balaghiyya-thesis p.30}.
- **Not yet given here**: studies that place him with the Ash'aris or Maturidis, or that stress the Sufi side of his allusive (ishari) exegesis, have not yet been added; later books will supply them.
