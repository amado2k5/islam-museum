## Summary
This is one of the questions on what breaks [[practice/wudu|ablution]]; [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] gives it two facing chapters, "ablution after touching the private part" and "the permission in this", and [[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|al-Khattabi]] relates the views of the Companions and jurists on it. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 94-96}

## Busra's report
Malik relates from 'Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr that he heard [[person/urwa-ibn-al-zubayr|'Urwa]] say: I went in to [[person/marwan-ibn-al-hakam|Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] and we spoke of what requires ablution; Marwan said, "And touching the private part"; 'Urwa said, "I did not know that"; Marwan said, "[[person/busra-bint-safwan|Busra bint Safwan]] told me she heard the Prophet say: whoever touches his private part, let him perform ablution." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 94-95} The annotator identifies Busra as the daughter of Safwan ibn Nawfal ibn Asad ibn 'Abd al-'Uzza, one of the women who pledged allegiance and emigrated, niece of Waraqa ibn Nawfal and maternal grandmother of 'Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and quotes al-Tirmidhi declaring the report sound. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

## Talq's report
Abu Dawud relates from Mulazim ibn 'Amr al-Hanafi, from 'Abd Allah ibn Badr, from Qays ibn Talq, from his father: we came to the Prophet, and a man who looked like a Bedouin asked about a man touching his private part after ablution; he said, "Is it anything but a morsel of him?" or "a piece of him?" {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 96} In Muhammad ibn Jabir's version: "in prayer". {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 96}

## How the two were reconciled
Al-Khattabi relates the argument of those who require ablution: Busra's report is the later, since [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]], a late convert, also related it, while Talq came at the beginning of the Hijra when the Prophet was building the mosque of Medina, and the later of two rulings is followed. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95} They took Talq's report to mean touching through a barrier, citing the words "in prayer", since one praying does not touch his private part without a barrier. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

## The discussion between Ahmad and Yahya ibn Ma'in
Al-Khattabi relates from [[person/abu-bakr-ibn-al-mundhir-al-nisaburi|Abu Bakr ibn al-Mundhir]] the report that [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] and [[person/yahya-ibn-main|Yahya ibn Ma'in]] met and discussed the question, Ahmad requiring ablution and Yahya not; they examined the reports until they agreed to set aside both, Busra's and Talq's, then turned to the Companions' practice, and Ahmad argued from the practice of [[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Ibn 'Umar]], which Yahya could not answer. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

:::perspectives
**Sunni (Shafi'i): al-Shafi'i, with the early authorities 'Umar, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Ibn 'Umar, Ibn 'Abbas and Abu Hurayra.** Ablution is required after touching the private part; for [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] purity is broken only by touching with the inside of the palm. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

**Sunni (Hanbali): Ahmad, with al-Awza'i and Ishaq.** Purity is broken by touching with the forearm or the back of the hand just as with the palm. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

**Sunni (Hanafi): Abu Hanifa, his companions and Sufyan al-Thawri, with the Companions 'Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, 'Ammar, Hudhayfa and Abu al-Darda'.** Touching does not break purity, on the basis of Talq's report. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}

**Sunni (Maliki): Malik ibn Anas.** The command to perform ablution after it is a recommendation, not an obligation. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 95}
:::
