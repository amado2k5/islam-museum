## Summary
In the introduction to [[work/zad-al-masir|Zad al-Masir]], [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] has a section on the last part of the Qur'an to be revealed, gathering differing reports from the Companions. {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6} This entry sets them out as he gives them, without a verdict.

## The reports
- From [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]], in a report only al-Bukhari has: the last verse revealed to the Prophet was the verse of usury. In one only Muslim has: the last sura revealed whole was al-Nasr; see [[passage/quran-110-1|al-Nasr 1]]. {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}
- Through [[person/al-dahhak-ibn-muzahim|al-Dahhak]] from Ibn Abbas: the last verse revealed was the verse of al-Baqara on the Day when people are returned to God; see [[passage/quran-2-281|al-Baqara 281]]; this was the view of [[person/said-ibn-jubayr|Sa'id ibn Jubayr]] and Abu Salih, and the editor says al-Tabari relates it with a sound chain. {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}
- From [[person/al-bara-ibn-azib|al-Bara']]: the last verse revealed was the verse of kalala at the end of Surat al-Nisa'; see [[passage/quran-4-176|al-Nisa' 176]]; and the last sura revealed was Bara'a. {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}
- From [[person/ubayy-ibn-kab|Ubayy ibn Ka'b]]: the last revealed was the close of Surat al-Tawba to the end of the sura; see [[passage/quran-9-128|al-Tawba 128]]. {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}

## Disagreement and views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Ibn Abbas in al-Bukhari's report**: the last verse was the verse of usury {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}.
- **Sunni: Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak, with Sa'id ibn Jubayr and Abu Salih**: the verse of al-Baqara on the Day of return to God {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}.
- **Sunni: al-Bara' ibn Azib**: the verse of kalala; Bara'a the last sura {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}.
- **Sunni: Ubayy ibn Ka'b**: the close of Surat al-Tawba {cite:zad-al-masir-maktab-islami-v1 p.6}.
- **Not yet given here**: how the scholars of the Qur'anic sciences reconcile these reports, and academic studies, have not yet been added; later books will supply them.
:::
