## Summary
Commentators differ over where [[passage/quran-1|Surat al-Fatiha]] was revealed: some say Mecca, some Medina, and some that it came down twice. In [[work/al-tafsir-al-wasit|al-Tafsir al-Wasit]], [[person/muhammad-sayyid-tantawi|Tantawi]] says that the preferred view among careful scholars is that it came down in Mecca, indeed among the very first revelations there. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.11} In [[work/fath-al-qadir|Fath al-Qadir]], [[person/al-shawkani|al-Shawkani]] lists the reports on which each view relied. {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12} The question belongs to the study of [[concept/makki-wa-madani|Meccan and Medinan revelation]].

## The case for Mecca
- Tantawi quotes [[person/al-qurtubi|al-Qurtubi]], who prefers this view because Surat al-Hijr, Meccan by consensus, mentions "the seven oft-repeated", because there is no disagreement that prayer was prescribed in Mecca, and because no prayer without al-Fatiha is known ever to have existed in Islam. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.11} {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.12}
- Al-Shawkani cites the report of al-Wahidi in *Asbab al-Nuzul* and al-Tha'labi in his commentary from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] that al-Fatiha came down in Mecca "from a treasure beneath the Throne". {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}
- He cites the report of [[person/amr-ibn-shurahbil|Amr ibn Shurahbil]], in Ibn Abi Shayba, Abu Nu'aym, al-Bayhaqi's *Dala'il al-Nubuwwa* and others, that the Prophet told Khadija what he experienced in the first days of revelation, she took him to [[person/waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa]], who advised him to stand firm when he heard the call, and when he was alone he was called with al-Fatiha to its end. {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}
- He cites Abu Nu'aym's report from a man of Banu Salima that the son of [[person/amr-ibn-al-jamuh|Amr ibn al-Jamuh]] recited the opening of al-Fatiha to his father before the Hijra, and Ibn al-Anbari's report in *al-Masahif* from [[person/ubada-ibn-al-samit|Ubada]] that al-Fatiha came down in Mecca. {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}

## The case for Medina
Al-Shawkani notes that those who held it Medinan relied on the report, in Ibn Abi Shayba, Abu Sa'id ibn al-A'rabi and al-Tabarani's *al-Awsat*, through [[person/mujahid-ibn-jabr|Mujahid]] from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]], that Iblis cried out when al-Fatiha was revealed and that it was revealed in Medina, and on what several compilers report through various chains from Mujahid himself, that it came down in Medina. {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}

## The reconciliation: revealed twice
Tantawi reports a third view, that it came down once in Mecca when prayer was prescribed and once in Medina when the qibla was changed, and al-Shawkani gives it as a way of reconciling the conflicting reports. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.11} {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}

## Views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Qurtubi, preferred by Tantawi**: Meccan, among the earliest revelations {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.11}.
- **Sunni: Mujahid, as reported by al-Shawkani**: Medinan {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}.
- **Sunni: a view given by Tantawi and al-Shawkani**: revealed twice, reconciling the reports {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.11} {cite:fath-al-qadir-dar-al-marifa p.12}.
- **Not yet given here**: these two sources give no verdict on the chains of these reports, and academic studies of the order of revelation have not yet been added.
:::

## In al-Baghawi
At the start of his commentary on al-Fatiha, [[person/al-baghawi-al-husayn-ibn-muhammad|al-Baghawi]] says it is Meccan according to most, that Mujahid said Medinan, and that it is said to have come down twice, once in Mecca and once in Medina; he explains its name "the Seven Oft-Repeated" by its being seven verses by the scholars' agreement and repeated in prayer. {cite:tahdhib-tafsir-baghawi-tayba-2009 p.7}

## In al-Baydawi
[[person/al-baydawi-abd-allah-ibn-umar|Al-Baydawi]] reports that it is said to have come down in Mecca when prayer was prescribed and in Medina when the qibla was changed, and holds that it is Meccan, citing the verse of Surat al-Hijr on the Seven Oft-Repeated; see [[passage/quran-15-87|al-Hijr 87]]. {cite:anwar-al-tanzil-fleischer-v1 p.6}
