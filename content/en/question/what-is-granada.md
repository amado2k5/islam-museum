## Overview
Granada was the last of the Andalusian kingdoms, the emirate of the Banu al-Ahmar from 1238 to 1492, and it holds the Alhambra, a jewel of Islamic architecture. Its fall closed eight Islamic centuries in Iberia. {cite:wikidata}
