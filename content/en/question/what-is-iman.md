## Short answer
Iman is the affirmation, by the heart, of the articles of belief: belief in God, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day and divine decree, as the Prophet explained them in the hadith of Gabriel.

## Detailed answer
In Islam, iman is the heart's affirmation of the articles of belief, as the Prophet explained them in the hadith of Gabriel: that a person believe in God, His angels, His books, His meeting, His messengers, and resurrection {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}. The entry on [[concept/iman|iman]] presents its details.
Iman is linked to Islam. Islam is the outward acts, while iman is the root of belief, as the entry on [[concept/islam-din|Islam]] presents.
The root of belief is tawhid, the entry on [[concept/tawhid|tawhid]], from which the articles of iman branch.
