## Overview
Yahya ibn Sharaf al-Nawawi (1233–1277) was a Shafi'i of Damascus. He wrote the Forty Hadiths and Riyad al-Salihin, and a commentary on Sahih Muslim. He was among the most beloved jurists of the Muslims in his life. {cite:wikidata}
