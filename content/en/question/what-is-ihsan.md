## Short answer
Ihsan is the third of the levels of religion: that a person worships God as if he sees Him, and if he does not see Him, then He sees him.

## Detailed answer
Ihsan is the third level in the hadith of Gabriel, after Islam and iman. The Prophet defined it as worshipping God as if you see Him, and if you do not see Him, then He sees you {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}. The entry on [[concept/ihsan|ihsan]] presents its meaning in the hadith and in ethics.
Ihsan is linked to iman, the entry on [[concept/iman|iman]], since in this hadith it is the highest of the levels of religion.
Scholars have expanded on its meaning between worship and ethics, and the ways of attaining it differ between scholars and Sufis. The entry presents the basic meaning without preferring one view.
