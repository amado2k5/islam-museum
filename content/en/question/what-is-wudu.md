## Short answer
Wudu is the minor purification with water required before prayer, which the Qur'an made obligatory: washing the face and the hands up to the elbows, wiping the head and washing the feet.

## Detailed answer
Ablution is one of the conditions for valid prayer according to the jurists, and its text is in Surat al-Ma'ida {cite:tanzil-quran}.
The entry on [[practice/wudu|wudu]] sets out its manner and acts, and it is linked to the entry on [[practice/salah|prayer]] that requires it.
