## Overview
Mecca is the mother of the cities: the birthplace of the Prophet, the home of the Kaaba and the sacred precinct, and the qibla of Muslims, the ancient house of God. It lies in a valley without crops, beside the spring of Zamzam. {cite:wikidata}
