## Overview
A fatwa is an account of a legal ruling for a particular case. It is issued by a qualified mufti. It does not bind as a judgment does, but it binds whoever has committed to it. {cite:wikidata}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)
The Sheikh recalls that in the late 1990s, at the start of a new satellite channel, a woman phoned in on air after the interview to ask about a country where taxes take a large part of the national income (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:53:30}.

She asked whether evading taxes is forbidden or permitted. The reply was that what the answerer had studied in jurisprudence is that evading zakat is forbidden, while she wanted a specific answer about taxes, and the Sheikh comments in amazement (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:54:00}.

He cites a saying to the effect that among the worst of God's servants is one who asks about something and it is forbidden because of his asking, and he mentions young people who live in the West (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:55:00}.

He describes someone who halts the breakfast table in the morning with a question about how a cheese was made, and holds that there is no need to press so hard in asking; he reminds listeners of God's favour in the night, the day and the heavens built above us {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:55:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh criticises that people discuss medicine only with doctors, engineering only with engineers and law only with lawyers, but in religion everyone is a mufti, joking that a family of fifty has fifty-five muftis; he says that after more than forty-five years in preaching he hopes, before he dies, to hear a Muslim say 'God knows best' or 'I do not know', and recalls that the boldest in giving fatwas are the boldest toward the Fire; this is his view and criticism. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:08:30}
