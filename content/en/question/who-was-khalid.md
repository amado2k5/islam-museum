## Overview
Khalid ibn al-Walid was the Drawn Sword of God, among the greatest military commanders of Islam. He won at Mu'ta, embraced Islam and then opened Syria and Iraq, and he won at Yarmuk. He was never defeated. {cite:wikidata}
