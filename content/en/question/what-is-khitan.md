## Short answer
Khitan is the removal of the foreskin of a male. Jurists count it among the natural practices (fitra), and it is a well-established custom in law and common usage. Its forms vary between countries.

## Detailed answer
Circumcision is among the traits of fitra in a sound hadith, and its timing and forms of celebration vary between Muslim lands {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/khitan|circumcision]] sets out its place in law and custom.
