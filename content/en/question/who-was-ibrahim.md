## Short answer
Ibrahim is the father of the prophets in Islam. The biography reports that he settled Hajar and his son Isma'il in the valley of Mecca, and he is known, according to the familiar report, for building the Ka'ba together with his son.

## Detailed answer
Ibrahim is a prophet of God, whom Muslims regard as the father of the prophets, as the entry [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]] sets out. The biographical account reports that he moved from Iraq to Harran and then to Palestine, and that he settled Hajar and Isma'il in the valley of Mecca.
In tradition, Ibrahim is linked to the Ka'ba, since tradition reports that he built it together with his son, as the entry on [[place/mecca|Mecca]] sets out.
His standing is agreed upon among Muslims, and the details of his story are presented according to the narrations of the tradition.
