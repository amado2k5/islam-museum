## Summary
In "the proof that faith increases and decreases" [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] relates a report in which the Prophet distinguishes the description "believer" from "Muslim", and [[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|al-Khattabi]] pauses on it to say, "How often people go wrong on this question!" {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42}

## Sa'd's report
[[person/muhammad-ibn-shihab-al-zuhri|Al-Zuhri]] relates from 'Amir ibn Sa'd, from his father [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]], that the Prophet gave to some men and left one of them out; Sa'd said, "You gave so-and-so and so-and-so and did not give so-and-so, though he is a believer," and he answered, "Or a Muslim," until Sa'd had repeated it three times; then he said, "I give to some men and leave one dearer to me than they, giving him nothing, for fear that they be thrown on their faces into the Fire." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42-43} The annotator, quoting Muhammad Muhyi al-Din 'Abd al-Hamid, explains "or" here as a correction — say rather that he is a Muslim, and do not assert his faith, since what hearts conceal is known only to God. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 43}

## Al-Zuhri's saying
Ma'mar relates from al-Zuhri on [[passage/quran-49-14|al-Hujurat 14]]: "We hold that islam is the word and iman the deed." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 43}

## Al-Khattabi's account of the question
Al-Khattabi says that al-Zuhri held what Ma'mar reports, citing the verse, while others held that faith and islam are one thing, citing [[passage/quran-51-35|al-Dhariyat 35]]–[[passage/quran-51-36|36]]: God promised to bring the believers out of Lot's people, then said He found there only one household of Muslims, so that islam indicates faith. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42} He notes that two leading scholars discussed this, each adopting one view, and that the later one wrote against the earlier a book of some two hundred leaves. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42}

:::perspectives
**Sunni: al-Zuhri.** Islam is the word and iman the deed, on the basis of the verse of al-Hujurat. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 43}

**Sunni: another view, related by al-Khattabi without naming its holder.** Islam and iman are one thing, and the Muslims are the believers, on the basis of the verses of al-Dhariyat. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42}

**Sunni (Shafi'i): al-Khattabi.** The right course is to qualify rather than generalise: iman is at root assent, islam at root surrender and obedience; a Muslim may be a believer in some states and not others, while a believer is a Muslim in every state; one may submit outwardly without obeying inwardly, but one who is truthful within is never disobedient without. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42-43}
:::

## Not covered here
Al-Khattabi names neither the two scholars who disputed the question nor the book; the views of the theologians and the Maturidis on the difference between the two names are not given here.
