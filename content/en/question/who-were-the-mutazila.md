## Overview
The Mu'tazila were an ancient theological movement that held to divine justice, the oneness of God, the intermediate position between two positions, and the createdness of the Qur'an. Their power reached its height under al-Ma'mun, and then Ahl al-Sunna defeated them. {cite:wikidata}
