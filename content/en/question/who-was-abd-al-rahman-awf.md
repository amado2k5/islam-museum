## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf was one of the ten promised Paradise, and among the richest and most pious merchants. The choice of Uthman as caliph was made through him in the consultation. He spent generously, and is hoped to enter Paradise by God's permission. {cite:wikidata}
