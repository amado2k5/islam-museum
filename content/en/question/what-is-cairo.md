## Overview
Cairo, al-Qahira al-Mu'izziyya, is the city of al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah the Fatimid, founded by Jawhar in 969. It holds al-Azhar, one of the greatest surviving universities of Islam, the Citadel of Saladin and a museum of Mamluk architecture. {cite:wikidata}
