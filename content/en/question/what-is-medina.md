## Overview
Medina, formerly Yathrib, was the city of the Hijra and the first state of Islam. It holds the Mosque of the Prophet and his grave. It was the capital of the caliphs until 'Ali, and the knowledge of the Companions and the Successors remained there. {cite:wikidata}
