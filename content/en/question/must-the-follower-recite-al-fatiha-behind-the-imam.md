## Summary

In the sixth to ninth questions of his chapter on the rulings of al-Fatiha, [[person/al-qurtubi|al-Qurtubi]] treats the follower's recitation. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.182} {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.183} He begins with a point of agreement: if one joins the imam while he is bowing, the imam carries the recitation for him, since all agree that he says the takbir and bows without reciting; if he joins him standing, he recites. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.182}

## Disagreement and views

:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Shafi'i and Ahmad; al-Qurtubi's choice**: the follower recites al-Fatiha in every rak'a, loud or silent {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.183}.
- **Sunni: Malik's well-known view**: he recites when the imam is silent, not when he is loud {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.182}.
- **Sunni: Ibn Wahb, Ashhab, Ibn Abd al-Hakam, Ibn Habib and the Kufans**: the follower recites nothing; the imam's recitation counts for him {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 p.183}.
:::

## Notes for researchers

- 'The Kufans' in al-Qurtubi's wording are Abu Hanifa, his companions and those of Kufa who agreed with them; the pages read do not name them here, so only Abu Hanifa is linked.
- The verse is not quoted; it is referred to by number as al-Qurtubi names it.
