## Overview
Ahmad ibn Hajar al-Asqalani (1372–1449) was the hafiz of Egypt. He was chief judge, and the author of Fath al-Bari, the greatest commentary on al-Bukhari, Lisan al-Mizan and Taqrib al-Tahdhib. {cite:wikidata}
