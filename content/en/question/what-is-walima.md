## Short answer
The walima is the food of the wedding feast. The Prophet commanded it, saying 'Hold a feast, even if it is with a sheep'. Its customs vary between countries, from a household table to the gathering of a people.

## Detailed answer
The walima is a sunna confirmed in hadith, and it is one of the customs of joy whose forms vary among Muslims {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/walima|the walima]] sets out its place in law and custom.
