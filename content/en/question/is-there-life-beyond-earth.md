## Summary

Many people ask today whether the universe holds living beings other than humans, jinn and angels. In the Qur'an and sound hadith the museum has read, there is no plain sentence that answers, either by affirming or denying. What it finds is verses about creatures God dispersed in the heavens and the earth and about a creation people do not know, and commentaries by major scholars who read them in different ways. This card sets out their positions without ruling between them: [[passage/quran-42-29|Ash-Shura 42:29]], [[passage/quran-16-8|An-Nahl 16:8]], [[passage/quran-36-36|Ya-Sin 36:36]], [[passage/quran-55-29|Ar-Rahman 55:29]].

## First position: creatures in the heaven, and the door left open to other kinds

Al-Tabari reports from Mujahid that the creature of the Ash-Shura verse is people and angels. {cite:tafsir-tabari-quran-com on 42:29} Ibn Kathir says the word covers angels, jinn, people and all animals in their different shapes, colours and languages, and that God dispersed them across the regions of the earth and the heavens. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir on 42:29} Al-Razi asks how the word creature can be applied to angels and answers in several ways, among them that it is not far-fetched that God created in the heavens kinds of animal that walk as people walk on the earth. {cite:mafatih-al-ghayb-quran-tafsir-net on 42:29}

In his commentary on al-Fatiha al-Razi says that, since in his view there is an unending void outside the world and God has power over everything possible, God is able to create a thousand thousand worlds outside this world, and the philosophers' arguments that the world is one are weak. {cite:mafatih-al-ghayb-quran-tafsir-net on 1:2} This is about power and possibility, not about whether such worlds have occurred.

## Second position: restraint, and keeping to what is soundly transmitted

Al-Qurtubi reports from al-Farra' that what is meant by what was dispersed is what is on the earth, not in the heaven. {cite:tafsir-qurtubi-quran-com on 42:29} On the seven earths al-Qurtubi gives the majority view that they hold inhabitants from God's creation, and then closes: God knows best what He kept to Himself. {cite:tafsir-qurtubi-quran-com on 65:12} Ibn Kathir says in al-Bidaya wa-l-Nihaya that what many of the People of the Book relate about the earths, and a group of scholars took from them, is rejected from whoever says it unless the chain reaches an infallible; and he takes Ibn Abbas's report of a prophet in each earth, if it is sound from him, to be from the Isra'iliyyat. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya vol. 1, PDF p. 25} In his commentary he calls Ibn Abi al-Dunya's report of a white earth in the west, with creatures who never disobeyed God, mursal and very objectionable. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir on 65:12}

## Third position: the modern literature

Academic studies of the subject have appeared in recent years. Those whose details the museum has checked are Jörg Matthias Determann's study of Islam, science fiction and extraterrestrial life (2021), David Weintraub's chapter (2014), Shoaib Ahmed Malik and Determann's *Islamic Theology and Extraterrestrial Life* (2024) and Parandis Tajbakhsh's study (2026). {cite:determann-islam-science-fiction-extraterrestrial-life-2021} {cite:weintraub-religions-and-extraterrestrial-life-2014} {cite:malik-determann-islamic-theology-extraterrestrial-life-2024} {cite:tajbakhsh-islam-and-extraterrestrial-life-2026} The publishers' abstracts say that Tajbakhsh's begins with the history of the plurality-of-worlds debate in Christendom and then turns to the Qur'an and hadith, and that Malik and Determann's gathers Sunni and Shi'i positions. The museum has not read these works' arguments, and they are **modern**.

## What the sources do not say

- No text read by the museum reports rational beings on another planet, describes them or places them.
- Al-Razi's remarks on other worlds outside this world belong to the discussion of power and possibility, not to a report of what has occurred.
- The museum has not read a sound hadith traced to the Prophet that describes the inhabitants of the other earths; of Ibn Abbas's report of a prophet in each earth, al-Bayhaqi said its chain to him is sound but shadhdh.
