## Summary
[[passage/quran-9|Surat al-Tawba]], also called "Bara'a", is the only sura of the mushaf at whose head [[concept/al-basmala|the basmala]] is not written. In his introduction to the sura in [[work/al-tafsir-al-wasit|al-Tafsir al-Wasit]], [[person/muhammad-sayyid-tantawi|Muhammad Sayyid Tantawi]] sets out the scholars' explanations as [[person/al-qurtubi|al-Qurtubi]] summarised them, and then states which he relies on. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501} {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502}

## Al-Qurtubi's five views
- **The custom of revoking treaties:** it was said that when the pre-Islamic Arabs wished to revoke a treaty they wrote a letter without the basmala; so when Bara'a came down revoking the treaties, the Prophet sent [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], who read it at the pilgrimage season without the basmala, following their custom. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}
- **The report from Uthman:** al-Nasa'i reports, and al-Tirmidhi has something similar, through Yazid al-Farisi, that [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] asked [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] why they had joined al-Anfal, one of the mathani, to Bara'a, one of the mi'un, without writing the basmala between them; he answered that al-Anfal was among the first revelations at Medina and Bara'a among the last of the Qur'an, that their stories were alike, and that the Prophet died without making clear that it belonged to it. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}
- **Its opening fell away:** Ibn Wahb, Ibn al-Qasim and Ibn Abd al-Hakam report from [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik]] that when the beginning of the sura fell away the basmala fell away with it; Ibn Ajlan heard that it had been as long as al-Baqara or nearly so, and [[person/said-ibn-jubayr|Sa'id ibn Jubayr]] said it was like Surat al-Baqara. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}
- **One sura or two:** Kharija, Abu Isma and others said that when the mushaf was written under Uthman the Companions differed over whether the two were one sura or two; so a gap was left for those who said two, and the basmala omitted for those who said one. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}
- **The basmala is a safe-conduct:** it is reported that Ibn Abbas asked Ali, who said: the basmala is a safe-conduct, and Bara'a came down with the sword and holds no safe-conduct; [[person/al-mubarrad|al-Mubarrad]] said much the same, that the basmala opens what is good while the start of this sura is a warning and a revoking of treaties. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502}

## The view al-Qurtubi holds correct and Tantawi adopts
After these views al-Qurtubi said: the correct one is that the basmala was not written because Gabriel did not bring it down in this sura. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502} Tantawi says that this is the view he relies on and that careful scholars preferred it, and quotes in support [[person/fakhr-al-din-al-razi|Fakhr al-Din al-Razi]], who after listing six explanations said: the correct one is that the Prophet ordered this sura placed after al-Anfal by revelation, and the basmala dropped from its head by revelation. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502} He quotes the author of al-Jalalayn that it was not written because the Prophet did not order it, and al-Jamal in his gloss that no one's opinion has any say in including or omitting it, since "the Lawgiver's silence where a statement was due is a statement that it is absent". {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502}

## Tantawi's criticism of the other views
Tantawi holds that the first and fifth views are reasoned explanations of the wisdom involved, not a basis for stating the cause. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502} On the report from Uthman he quotes a verifying scholar — [[person/ahmad-muhammad-shakir|Ahmad Shakir]], in his edition of the Musnad of Ahmad — that its chain is "very doubtful", in his judgement "very weak" and indeed "baseless", since it turns on Yazid al-Farisi, about whom it was disputed whether he was Yazid ibn Hurmuz or someone else, whom al-Bukhari listed among weak narrators, and since it casts doubt on what is established by mass transmission about the suras and the basmala at their heads. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2503} He rejects the third view as having no evidence or chain and as touching the sanctity of the Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2503} He judges the fourth weak, because al-Anfal and al-Tawba have been known as separate suras since the Prophet's time and each has its own subject: al-Anfal speaks at length of [[event/battle-of-badr|the battle of Badr]], al-Tawba of [[event/expedition-to-tabuk|the expedition to Tabuk]] in year 9. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2503}

## Views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Qurtubi and Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, adopted by Tantawi**: fixed by revelation; Gabriel did not bring it and the Prophet did not order it written {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502}.
- **Sunni: a view reported by al-Qurtubi**: its wisdom is the Arab custom of omitting the basmala when revoking treaties {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}.
- **Sunni: the report of Yazid al-Farisi from Uthman**: the Prophet did not make clear that it belonged to al-Anfal, so they were joined; Ahmad Shakir judged its chain weak {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2503}.
- **Sunni: a report from Malik**: its opening fell away and the basmala with it; Tantawi rejects it {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501} {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2503}.
- **Sunni: Kharija and Abu Isma**: the Companions differed whether they were one sura or two {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2501}.
- **Sunni: a saying reported from Ali, and similarly from al-Mubarrad**: the basmala is a safe-conduct and Bara'a came down with the sword {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.2502}.
- **Not yet given here**: academic studies of the history of the mushaf on this question have not yet been added.
