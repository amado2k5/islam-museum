## Overview
Samarra was the capital of the Abbasids for half a century, 836 to 892. Al-Mu'tasim built it. It holds the Mosque of al-Mutawakkil, the spiral minaret of al-Malwiya and the wall of the two imams al-Hasan al-'Askari and his son. {cite:wikidata}
