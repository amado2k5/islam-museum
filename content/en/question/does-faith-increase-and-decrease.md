## Summary
The Book of the Sunna in the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan of Abu Dawud]] has two consecutive chapters on this question, "refuting irja'" and "the proof that faith increases and decreases", in which [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] gathers reports that count deeds as part of [[concept/iman|faith]], and [[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|al-Khattabi]] explains them as showing that faith has degrees. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 41}

## The reports in the chapter
- [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]]: "Faith has seventy-odd branches, the highest the saying 'there is no god but God', the lowest removing a bone from the road; and modesty is a branch of faith." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39} Al-Khattabi quotes Tha'lab that *bid'* ("-odd") means from three up to ten. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39}
- [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn 'Abbas]] on the delegation of 'Abd al-Qays: he told them to believe in God and said, "Do you know what faith in God is? Bearing witness that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, performing the prayer, paying zakat, fasting Ramadan, and giving the fifth of the spoils." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 40}
- [[person/abu-umama-al-bahili|Abu Umama]]: "Whoever loves for God, hates for God, gives for God and withholds for God has completed faith"; and Abu Hurayra: "The most complete of believers in faith are the best of them in character." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 42}
- The chapter also holds the report on the deficiency in religion of a menstruating woman who leaves prayer and fasting, and Ibn 'Abbas's report on the change of qibla and al-Baqara 143. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 41-42}

## Al-Khattabi's explanation
- **Branches**: faith in the religious sense names a reality with branches and parts, higher and lower; the name attaches to some of them as to all, while its full reality requires all, just as the canonical prayer has parts and the name attaches to some of them. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39}
- **Degrees**: the chapter establishes degrees in faith and differences of rank among believers. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39}
- **Modesty**: it is a branch of faith because it cuts its owner off from sins and holds him back from them, faith as a whole being doing what God commands and refraining from what He forbids. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39}
- **Deeds as faith**: in the report of 'Abd al-Qays the Prophet taught that prayer, zakat, the Ramadan fast and the fifth are part of faith, as is the testimony; this does not conflict with "I was commanded to fight people until they say there is no god but God", since that phrase is a badge by which entry is marked, while this is a detailed account, "and the detail does not contradict the summary but fits it". {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 40}

:::perspectives
**Sunni: Abu Dawud in his chapter headings and al-Khattabi in his commentary.** Faith has branches, the highest the testimony and the lowest removing harm from the road; deeds belong to it; it increases and decreases, and believers differ in it. Abu Dawud's own heading, "refuting irja'", frames the question as an answer to [[group/murjia|the Murji'a]]. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 39-41}
:::

## Not covered here
The chapter does not give the Murji'a view in their own words, nor the Hanafi and Maturidi position that faith is assent and deeds are not part of what it names; these are to be added from their sources.
