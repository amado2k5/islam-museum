## Short answer
Dhikr is remembrance of God with the heart and the tongue. It is one of the acts of worship that a Muslim engages in often, and scholars have written many books on it.

## Detailed answer
In Islam, dhikr is remembrance of God with the heart and the tongue. It is one of the acts of worship. The entry on [[concept/dhikr|dhikr]] presents its meaning and its forms.
Dhikr is linked to prayer, and includes what is said in it and after it. The entry on [[practice/salah|prayer]] presents its times and its pillars.
Methods and litanies of dhikr differ among people, schools and orders, so this entry presents the general principle without detailing the litanies.
