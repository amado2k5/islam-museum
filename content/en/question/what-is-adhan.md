## Short answer
The adhan is the public call to prayer in a fixed form: Allahu akbar, the two testimonies, 'come to prayer' and 'come to success'.

## Detailed answer
The adhan is a visible rite of Islam. It announces the start of the prayer time and calls people to it {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/adhan|the adhan]] sets out its wording and timing, and it is linked to [[practice/salah|the prayer]] to which it calls.
