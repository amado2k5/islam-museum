## Short answer
Arab historians divided the Arabs by lineage into the extinct peoples, whose histories are lost, such as 'Ad and Thamud; the original Arabs (al-'Arab al-'Aariba) descended from Qahtan, whose home was Yemen; and the Arabized Arabs (al-'Arab al-Musta'riba) descended from Isma'il, called the Adnanites, among whom were the Quraysh {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

## Detailed answer
Historians divided the peoples of the Arabs into three groups according to the lineages they descend from. The first are the [[group/al-arab-al-baida|extinct Arabs]], the earliest peoples whose history is not known in enough detail, such as 'Ad, Thamud, Tasm, Jadis and 'Amaliq. The second are the [[group/qahtan|original Arabs]], descended from Ya'rub ibn Yashjub ibn Qahtan, and called the Qahtanites. The third are the [[group/adnan|Arabized Arabs]], descended from [[person/ismail|Isma'il]], and called the Adnanites {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

The home of the original Arabs was the land of [[place/yemen|Yemen]]. Their best-known tribes were [[group/himyar|Himyar]] and [[group/kahlan|Kahlan]]. Clans of Kahlan migrated from Yemen and spread across the peninsula: the Azd toward the Hijaz, Oman and Syria; Tayy toward the two mountains; and Kinda toward Hadramawt and Najd {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17}.

The Arabized Arabs descend from the offspring of Isma'il, whom his father [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]] settled in Mecca. From his son [[person/qaydar|Qaydar]] came [[person/adnan|Adnan]], then [[person/maadd-ibn-adnan|Ma'add]]. From his son Nizar branched Iyad, Anmar, [[group/rabia-ibn-nizar|Rabi'a]] and [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]]. From Mudar came Kinana, and from Kinana came the [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21}. For the general entry, see [[concept/arab-peoples-before-islam|The Arabs and their peoples before Islam]].

## Not covered here
This division is the one that Arab genealogists set out, as al-Raheeq al-Makhtum relays it from its sources. Recent studies have a different view of the origins of the Arabs, which has not yet been added.
