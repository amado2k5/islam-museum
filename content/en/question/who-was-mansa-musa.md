## Overview
Mansa Musa (reigned 1312–1337) was the sultan of Mali. He is regarded as the richest ruler in history according to the well-known account of him. His pilgrimage in 1324 flooded Cairo with gold, and he built Timbuktu. {cite:wikidata}
