## Overview
The Seljuk Empire (1037–1194) was a Turkish Sunni sultanate that protected the Abbasid Caliphate. Under it were Nizam al-Mulk and the Nizamiyya madrasas, and the battle of Manzikert took place in 1071. {cite:wikidata}
