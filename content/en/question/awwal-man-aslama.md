## The question
In the thirty-ninth type of Tadrib al-Rawi, commenting on al-Nawawi, al-Suyuti takes up the dispute over which Companion was first to accept Islam. He sets out the scholars' views and evidence and judges the chains of some of them. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 211}

## The views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Abu Bakr**: held by Ibn Abbas, Hassan, al-Sha'bi and al-Nakha'i among others. In support is the hadith of Amr ibn Abasa in Sahih Muslim: he asked the Prophet who was with him in this, and he answered "a free man and a slave"; with him that day were Abu Bakr and Bilal. The editor Awwama notes that this view has been ascribed to the majority and even claimed as consensus. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 211} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 212}
- **Sunni: Ali**: reported by al-Tabarani with a sound chain from Ibn Abbas, and from Zayd ibn Arqam, al-Miqdad, Abu Ayyub, Anas, Jabir, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri and others. Al-Hakim reports from Muslim al-Mula'i that the Prophet received his mission on a Monday and Ali accepted Islam on the Tuesday. Al-Hakim claimed that the historians agreed on it, and this was disputed. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 214} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 216} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 217}
- **Sunni: Khadija**: al-Nawawi, adding to Ibn al-Salah, says this is correct according to a group of verifiers. It is the view of Qatada and Ibn Ishaq. Al-Tha'labi claimed consensus on it, the dispute being over who came after her. Ibn Abd al-Barr said they agreed that Khadija was first to believe, then Ali, and that the sound view is that Abu Bakr was the first to make his Islam public. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 217} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 218}
- **Sunni: Zayd ibn Haritha and other views**: al-Zuhri said Zayd. Umar ibn Shabba said Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As accepted Islam before Ali. Al-Mas'udi reported one view naming Khabbab and another naming Bilal. Al-Iraqi said one should say that the first man to believe was Waraqa ibn Nawfal. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 217} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 219} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 221}
- **Sunni: the reconciliation (Ibn al-Salah and al-Nawawi)**: the most scrupulous course is to say: of free men Abu Bakr, of boys Ali, of women Khadija, of freedmen Zayd, of slaves Bilal. This reconciliation is also reported from Abu Hanifa. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 222}
:::

## Ibn Ishaq's order of the first believers
Al-Suyuti quotes Ibn Ishaq's order of the first believers:
1. Khadija;
2. Ali;
3. Zayd ibn Haritha;
4. Abu Bakr, who made his Islam public and called others to God.

Through Abu Bakr's call, Uthman ibn Affan, al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and Talha ibn Ubayd Allah accepted Islam. These were the eight who led the way into Islam.

He also quotes Ibn Khalawayh: the first woman to accept Islam after Khadija was Lubaba bint al-Harith, wife of al-Abbas. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 219} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 223}
