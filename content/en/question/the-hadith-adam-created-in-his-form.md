## The report
The report forbids striking the face and saying "God make ugly your face and the face of whoever resembles you", giving as the reason that God created Adam in his form; one wording [[person/ibn-khuzayma|Ibn Khuzayma]] narrates is: "When one of you fights, let him avoid the face, for God created Adam in his form." {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 83-84} He also narrates from Ibn 'Umar a wording he calls "more obscure": "Do not make the face ugly, for the son of Adam was created in the form of the All-Merciful", and al-Thawri's version of it, broken at 'Ata'. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 85-86} The question is: to whom does the pronoun refer?

## The views
:::perspectives
- **Ibn Khuzayma (Sunni, Shafi'i): the pronoun refers to the one struck.** He says that some who did not seek knowledge carefully imagined "in his form" to mean the form of the All-Merciful, whereas the pronoun stands for the person struck and cursed: Adam's face resembles the faces of his children, so whoever says to someone "God make ugly your face and the face of whoever resembles you" has made ugly the face of Adam. He warns against reading the report in a way that leads to likening God to creation. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 84-85} He faults Ibn 'Umar's wording on three counts: al-Thawri contradicts al-A'mash by transmitting it without the Companion, al-A'mash conceals his sources (tadlis), and so does Habib ibn Abi Thabit; then he says that, if it is sound, ascribing the form to the All-Merciful is the ascription of a created thing to its Creator. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 87-88}
- **Affirmation (Sunni, as the editor gathers it from Ahmad, Ishaq and Ibn Qutayba): the report is sound, and the form is affirmed of God as befits Him.** The editor quotes [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] that Ibn Abi 'Asim and al-Tabarani transmitted the wording "the form of the All-Merciful" with trustworthy men, and that a wording from Abu Hurayra rules out referring the pronoun to the person struck; Harb al-Kirmani that [[person/ishaq-ibn-rahwayh|Ishaq ibn Rahwayh]] said it is sound that God created Adam in the form of the All-Merciful, that [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]] declared it sound, and that Ahmad called the reading "in the form of the man" false and the view of the Jahmiyya; and [[person/ibn-qutayba|Ibn Qutayba]] that the form is no stranger than the Hands, the Fingers and the Eye, all of which he believes in without modality or limit. The editor holds that the defects Ibn Khuzayma names do not touch the report's soundness, and that his interpretation is "very far-fetched". {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 85-90}
- **Referring the pronoun to Adam (Sunni, as Ibn Hajar sets it out).** Among the views Ibn Hajar lists: that Adam was created in the form he kept until he was sent down and died, or that he was created complete from the start, against the eternalists, the naturalists and the Qadariyya; and that [[person/al-maziri|al-Maziri]] and those who followed him denied the soundness of the addition "in the form of the All-Merciful", saying that, if it is sound, it is to be taken in a way befitting the Creator. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 88-89}
:::

## How Ibn Khuzayma's error was regarded
The editor counts this interpretation among the book's substantive faults. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988-study p. 69} From a marginal note in the Taymuriyya manuscript he quotes [[person/ismail-al-taymi|Qiwam al-Sunna Isma'il al-Taymi]]: "Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Khuzayma erred on the hadith of the form, and he is not to be faulted for that"; and Abu Musa al-Madini's comment that there is hardly an imam without a slip, and that if an imam were abandoned for his slip, many imams would be abandoned. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 90} The editor also mentions an article by Hammad ibn Muhammad al-Ansari, "Ta'rif Ahl al-Iman bi-Sihhat Hadith Surat al-Rahman", published in 1396 AH, which authenticates the report. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-khuzayma-shahwan-1988 p. 90}
