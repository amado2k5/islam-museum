## Short answer
In Islamic belief, qadar means that God created creation with His knowledge, decreed for them measures and terms, and that everything happens by His will. This is one of the pillars of belief in qadar.

## Detailed answer
Qadar is the belief that God created everything and knew what would be before it was, and that He decreed things and their terms by His will, as the Tahawi creed presents in [[concept/qadar|qadar]].
Qadar is linked to iman, the entry on [[concept/iman|iman]], since it is one of its articles in the hadith of Gabriel {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.
Views differ on explaining the relation between God's decree and human action, and books of kalam present them. This entry therefore presents the agreed principle without detailing the disagreement.
