## Overview
The Battle of Mu'ta took place in Jumada al-Ula 8 AH / 629 CE. It was the first encounter of Muslims with the Byzantines. Three commanders were killed in it: Zayd, Ja'far and Abd Allah ibn Rawaha. Khalid led the army to safety. {cite:wikidata}
