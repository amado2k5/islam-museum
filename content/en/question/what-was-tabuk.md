## Overview
The Expedition of Tabuk, Rajab 9 AH / 630 CE, was the last campaign of the Prophet. Thirty thousand men set out in hardship and heat to meet the Byzantines, but they met no army, and the expedition exposed the hypocrites. {cite:wikidata}
