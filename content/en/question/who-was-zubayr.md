## Overview
Al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam was the disciple of the Prophet and the son of his aunt Safiyya. He was the first to draw a sword in God's path and one of the ten promised Paradise, and he was killed in the Valley of the Beasts after the Battle of the Camel. {cite:wikidata}
