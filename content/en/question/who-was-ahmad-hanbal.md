## Overview
Ahmad ibn Hanbal (780–855) was the imam of the Sunna. He compiled his Musnad from about forty thousand hadiths, and he held firm to the view that the Qur'an is uncreated during the inquisition, for which he was beaten. {cite:wikidata}
