## Short answer
The funeral prayer is a supplication for the deceased. It is performed standing, without bowing or prostration, with four takbirs, the Fatiha, prayers on the Prophet and supplication. It is a collective obligation.

## Detailed answer
The funeral prayer is among the rights of the dead in the law, and its description is in the two Sahihs {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
The entry on [[practice/salat-al-janaza|the funeral prayer]] sets out its manner and ruling.
