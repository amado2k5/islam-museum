## Short answer
The five pillars of Islam, as the authentic hadith lists them: the testimony that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God; establishing the prayer; giving zakat; fasting Ramadan; and pilgrimage to the House for whoever is able.

## Detailed answer
The five pillars of Islam are the acts on which Islam is built, as the authentic hadith states {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. Their details are in the entry on [[concept/five-pillars|the five pillars of Islam]].
They are: the testimony, then [[practice/salah|the prayer]], then [[practice/zakat|zakat]], then [[practice/sawm-ramadan|fasting Ramadan]], then [[practice/hajj|the pilgrimage]] for whoever is able to make the journey.
The first pillar is [[concept/shahada|the testimony]], the gate through which a Muslim enters Islam. It is linked to [[concept/tawhid|tawhid]], the root of belief.
