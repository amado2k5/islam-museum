## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhyi al-Din al-Jaza'iri (1808–1883) was the leader of Algerian resistance. He led fifteen years of struggle against the French occupation, and then lived in Damascus, where he protected Christians. {cite:wikidata}
