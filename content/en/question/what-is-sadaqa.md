## Short answer
Sadaqa in Islam is broader than giving money. It covers every act of good. It is distinct from the obligatory zakat, which has specific conditions and recipients.

## Detailed answer
Sadaqa in Islam is broader than giving money. The Prophet pointed the poor among the Companions to other kinds of charity when they thought there was no charity but money, as the entry on [[concept/sadaqa|sadaqa]] presents {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.
As for [[practice/zakat|zakat]], it is prescribed with specific conditions and recipients. Sadaqa is voluntary, and its giver is rewarded for it.
This topic is linked to ihsan, the entry on [[concept/ihsan|ihsan]], since charity is one form of ihsan toward people.
