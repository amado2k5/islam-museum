## Overview
Quraysh was the tribe of Mecca and the custodians of the Kaaba. The Prophet and the four rightly guided caliphs came from it, as did most of the leading figures of Islam. Most jurists hold that the caliphs will come from it until the Day of Resurrection. {cite:wikidata}
