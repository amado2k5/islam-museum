## Short answer
Knowledge in Islam is a wide door of merit. Seeking it is an obligation in the well-known report, and the Qur'an honoured its people by saying that God raises those who have knowledge in rank.

## Detailed answer
The entry on [[concept/ilm|knowledge]] sets out its place in belief and jurisprudence, and it is linked to the entry on [[concept/adab|good manners]] that accompanies it.
Knowledge is religious and worldly, and the Sharia urged seeking benefit from both {cite:tanzil-quran}.
