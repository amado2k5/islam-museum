## Overview
Tamadir bint Amr al-Khansa (c. 575–645) was a poet of Mecca and al-Yamama. She was the mistress of elegy in Arabic poetry. She embraced Islam with her sons, and they were martyred at al-Qadisiyya. {cite:wikidata}
