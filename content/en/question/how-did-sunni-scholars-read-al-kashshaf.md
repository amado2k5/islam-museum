## Summary
[[work/al-kashshaf|Al-Kashshaf]] by [[person/al-zamakhshari|al-Zamakhshari]] is a commentary its Mu'tazili author wrote at the request of brethren of his school and finished in Mecca in 528 AH. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12} Among Sunni scholars it drew two responses at once: wide praise for its rhetoric, its language and its mastery of ma'ani and bayan, and close answers to its Mu'tazili readings. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.13} This entry sets out those positions as Khalil Ma'mun Shiha reports them in the introduction to the Dar al-Ma'rifa edition, without a verdict.

## Praise for its rhetoric
The editor quotes Ibn Khaldun as saying that among the best commentaries in this art is al-Kashshaf, and urging readers to make the most of it for the rarity of its linguistic arts; the editor offers this as testimony to its excellence in language, grammar and rhetoric. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.13} He quotes Taj al-Din al-Subki calling it a great book in its field and its author a master of his art. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.13} The editor himself says most later commentaries drew on it and ranks it among the best references for rhetoric. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12}

## Answers on creed
[[person/ibn-al-munayyir|Ibn al-Munayyir]] the Maliki, judge of Alexandria, wrote *al-Intisaf fima tadammanahu al-Kashshaf min al-i'tizal*, debating and answering al-Zamakhshari; Dar al-Ma'rifa prints it in the margin of al-Kashshaf. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.16} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.3} The editor gives examples of what critics answer: the denial of the reality of magic in explaining Surat al-Falaq, the view that human beings create their own acts, and the naming of opponents as determinists and hashwiyya. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.14} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.15} He quotes verses by Shaykh Haydar al-Harawi, one of its annotators, faulting the bending of Qur'anic words to the school. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.16} Many later glosses combined using it with flagging it, such as those of al-Tibi, al-Taftazani and al-Sayyid al-Sharif al-Jurjani. {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.17}

## Disagreement and views
:::perspectives
- **Sunni (Maliki): Ibn al-Munayyir in al-Intisaf**: answer al-Kashshaf on creed and value it on rhetoric; he refutes its Mu'tazili readings while acknowledging its rhetorical insight {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.13} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.16}.
- **Sunni: Ibn Khaldun, Taj al-Din al-Subki and the editor**: among the best commentaries for language, grammar and rhetoric, to be used with caution about its Mu'tazilism {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.13}.
- **Other view (Mu'tazili): al-Zamakhshari on his own school**: he describes his school as "the saved, justice-upholding party" and "the scholars of justice and divine unity" {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.12} {cite:kashshaf-dar-al-marifa-shiha-2009 p.15}.
- **Not yet given here**: academic studies of the influence of al-Kashshaf and its glosses have not yet been added; later books will supply them.
:::
