## Overview
Uclés is a town in Spain. This page draws on the German gazetteer and on Yaqut.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2756492) records this place under the preferred name «Uclés» [Spanish], typed as a populated place. Further names include «Uclés (Cuenca)» [English]; «Uclès» [French]; «أقليش» [Arabic]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 2510106. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2756492}

In Yaqut al-Hamawi's Mu'jam al-buldan (volume 1), Uclés is a city in al-Andalus belonging to the district of Shantabariyya (Santaver), held in his day by the Franks. He quotes al-Humaydi, who calls it a small town of the district of Toledo. Yaqut lists scholars known by the nisba al-Aqlishi, among them Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Ma'ruf al-Tujibi, who came to Alexandria in 546 AH and then set out for the Hijaz, and Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Yahya al-Tujibi, who heard hadith at Toledo and died in 502 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut 1 p.317}
