## Overview
Baykand: a city beyond the river, two farsakhs from its dependency Ramdin, according to al-Baladhuri. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad came to it with twenty-four thousand men, defeated the hosts of Khatun the Turk, made peace with it for a million, and entered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
