## Overview
A place in the land of [[group/tayy|Tayy]], which the Prophet granted to [[person/zayd-al-khayl|Zayd al-Khayl]] together with lands with him, and he was given a document to that effect in the [[event/delegation-of-tayy|delegation of Tayy]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## What the editors say
The editors of the 1936 edition are content to say that Fayd is a place name. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## What is not covered here
The location of Fayd on the Iraqi pilgrim road, and its coordinates, will be added from Yaqut's dictionary of places or from Wikidata.
