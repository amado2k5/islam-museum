## Summary
An oasis north of Medina, which was home to Jewish communities and the target of the Islamic campaign of 628 CE.

## Location

## In the introduction to "The Sealed Nectar"
Many Jewish tribes settled in Yathrib, Khaybar and Tayma after the Romans occupied Palestine in 70 CE, and built villages, towers and fortresses there. Khaybar was one of the well-known Jewish tribes when Islam came {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.151}. The Banu Sulaym lived from the Wadi al-Qura to Khaybar and east of Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22}. See [[concept/judaism-in-arabia|Judaism in the Arabian Peninsula]].
