## Summary
Khaygun is a populous city of the land of China, eight stages from Kashghar, much visited and rich in commerce. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.203}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Kashghar to Khaygun is eight stages; Khaygun is a populous city among the cities of China, with many travellers bound for it and much trade {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.203}. In its land are found the musk animal and the civet; the musk animal is a kind of goat like the gazelle but small and reddish in colour, bearing glands in which its blood gathers and from which musk is taken {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.204}. Kashghar, where its road begins, is a populous town of many benefits with goods and merchandise, standing on a small river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.203}.
