## Overview
A great city in the districts of Ahvaz. Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] made peace with it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The peace was on the condition that none of its people be killed or enslaved, and that their property not be touched, except their weapons. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
