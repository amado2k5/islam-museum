## Overview
al-Sibayn: two rivers dug by Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik. He granted the low lands where the water remained, in return for his spending three million dirhams on the dam of breaches in the days of al-Hajjaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.413}

Then the two rivers were granted to Dawud ibn Ali in the Abbasid state, and they were bought from his heirs and became estates of the caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.413}
