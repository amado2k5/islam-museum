## Overview
Rahbat Malik ibn Tawq is a city on the Euphrates, on its eastern bank, thirty miles from al-Daliya. Al-Idrisi calls it fertile and populous, with buildings and many fruits. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 656} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 657}

## On the Euphrates
Al-Idrisi counts the Euphrates among the six great rivers and follows its course through Qarqisiya, the mouth of the Khabur, Rahbat Malik, al-Daliya, Ana, Hit and al-Anbar. Beyond Rahba the rest of the river runs along the back of the desert, with canals branching off to Sarsar and other places. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 650}

## Neighbours
From al-Daliya to Rahbat Malik ibn Tawq is thirty miles; al-Daliya is a small town on the western bank of the Euphrates. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 656} From Rahba along the Euphrates to al-Khabur is two stages; al-Khabur is a pleasant town on the bank with gardens and much fruit, and from it to [[place/qarqisiya|Qarqisiya]] is two stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 657}
