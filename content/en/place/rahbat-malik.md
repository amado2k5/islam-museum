## Overview
Rahbat Malik is a fertile, populous city on the Euphrates, on the desert road. It lies thirty miles from al-Dalliya, and Malik ibn Tawq is attributed to it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.656}
