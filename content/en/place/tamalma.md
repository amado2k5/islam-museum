## Overview
Tamalma is a small city in the land of Kuwar, on a small hill in an impregnable mountain. It has palms and livestock, and it has no wall. Its people drink from deep wells, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.29}
