## Overview
Tamalma is a small town in the land of Kawar, fourteen stages east of the city of Kuku. It is populous and has no wall; it stands on a small but strong hill, ringed with cliffs on all sides. It has palms and livestock, and its people drink from wells whose water lies deep below the surface. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 29}

## Alum
It has an alum mine of no great quality; they sell it in Kawar, and merchants mix it with good alum and carry it in all directions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 29}

## Neighbours
From the city of Kuku to the city of Ghana is a month and a half. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 29} The land of Kawar lies in the bed of a valley that comes from the south and runs north, and its towns include Qasr Umm Isa, Inklas and Tilmila. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 117} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 118}
