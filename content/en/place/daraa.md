## Overview
Draa: a land of connected villages that is not enclosed by a wall or a ditch. Its people grow henna and carry it to all parts. From Draa to the land of Sus al-Aqsa is four stages, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.227}
