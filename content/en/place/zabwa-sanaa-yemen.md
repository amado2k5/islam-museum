## Overview
Zabwa (printed Zabwa with either ending) is a place where the men had a camp which al-Hadi's army fell on in the night of Friday, taking spoil from it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236} ([[event/the-night-raid-on-zabwa-and-the-death-of-abu-al-qasim-al-jafari-289]]); it is the "mountain of Zabwa" where [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and Abu al-Qasim met the men and fought and the men broke {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239} ([[event/the-keeper-of-the-fort-at-adudan-feigns-surrender-and-the-ambush-is-found-289]]). The book gives neither its distance from Sana'a nor coordinates, and the page image confirms the reading.
