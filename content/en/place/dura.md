## Summary
Dura is a town in the Hebron Governorate of the southern West Bank, one of Palestine's "Throne Villages," on UNESCO's World Heritage Tentative List since 2013. Its old town preserves a traditional stone fabric and functional houses gathered around the [[building/al-omari-mosque-dura|Omari Mosque]]. {cite:unesco-tl-5717-throne-villages} {cite:dura-municipality}

## Heritage and register
RIWAQ's register of historic buildings in Palestine records several of its old structures, including the Omari Mosque built over earlier remains. The municipality today leads old-town revival initiatives and markets under the "Heritage in Your Hands" programme. {cite:riwaq-register} {cite:dura-municipality}
