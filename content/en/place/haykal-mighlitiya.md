## Overview
The Temple of Mighlitiya is the great house that remains to the Sabians of Harran, in the city of [[place/harran|Harran]] at the Raqqa gate, in 332 AH, according to al-Mas'udi. For them it is the temple of Azar, the father of Abraham. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.545-546}

Al-Mas'udi reports from the poem of Ibn Aysun the judge the mention of four cellars beneath it, made for images of the idols representing the heavenly bodies, and what appears from them of speech, by tricks of bellows and attendants from behind the walls. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.545-546}
