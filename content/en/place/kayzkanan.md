## Summary
Kayzkanan was a town of the land of al-Tuwayran in Sind, lying to the west of the city of al-Tuwayran, where that province's governor resided. The city of al-Tuwayran itself was a large, flourishing town of good standing with markets, trade and prosperous conditions, having settled districts and villages, and Kayzkanan lay to its west. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175}

## Details
Al-Idrisi describes it as a populous, well-settled town with cheap prices, having orchards, gardens, grapes and fruits, though no date-palms. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175} He counts it among the towns of al-Tuwayran together with Mahyak, Sura, Qazdar, Kashdan and Masurjan, and notes that between the towns of al-Tuwayran and the lands of al-Mansura stretched continuous deserts and wastelands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.179}
