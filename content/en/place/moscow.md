## Overview
Moscow is the capital city of Russia, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place under its German name Moskau. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2091741}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2091741 with the preferred name «Moskau», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Russia. Among the names it records are «Moscow» (English), «Moskva», «Moscou» (French), «Moscú» (Spanish), «Mosca» (Italian), «Москва» (Ukrainian) and «موسكو» (Arabic). It links the place to GeoNames no. 524901, to a GND record and to Arachne and Zenon thesaurus identifiers. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2091741}
