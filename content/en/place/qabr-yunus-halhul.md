## Overview
A tomb attributed to [[person/yunus|Yunus]] ibn Matta, in the village of Halhul, on the left of one going from [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] to Hebron. It has a building and a dome, and it has an attendant; visitors make a detour to it. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari mentions that he visited it several times, the last in Dhu al-Hijja 745 AH. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 176}

The attribution to Yunus appears in the source in the form "attributed to" (mansub), and it has not been documented in the data by a second source. An additional source is needed.
