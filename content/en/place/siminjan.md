## Overview
Siminjan is a land in Tukharistan. Its people refused to submit to Qays ibn al-Haytham on his march through it, so he besieged it until he conquered it by force, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[place/tukharistan|Tukharistan]].
