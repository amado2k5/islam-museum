## Overview
Nevşehir is a city recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place in Nevşehir Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2194610}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2194610 with the preferred name «Nevşehir», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Nevşehir Province. It records the Arabic form «نوشهر» and names in Turkish, English, German, French and other languages, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 303831. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2194610}
