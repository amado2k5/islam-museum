## Overview
A place called Alab where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] met the men in Sha'ban 288 AH ([[event/al-hadi-fights-at-alab-and-al-jud-under-bayt-bus-288]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}; it has a fort in which [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] spent the night before Tuesday in Shawwal ([[event/abu-al-atahiya-holds-the-fort-of-alab-and-the-fight-at-hidayn-288]]), and al-Hadi rode to it with his horse on the second Friday after the Fitr to face the people of [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]] ([[event/the-raid-on-the-gate-of-sanaa-during-the-fitr-sermon-and-the-sortie-to-alab-288]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}.

## Detail
The book gives neither its distance from Sana'a nor coordinates; it says that at the first fight al-Hadi met the men there without having drawn up his army.
