## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Washington, D.C. as a populated place, with the preferred name «Washington», under the United States of America in its hierarchy. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2097797}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2097797 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Washington». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «United States of America». It records names in Latin («Vasingtonia»), Polish, English («Washington D. C.», «Washington DC»), French, Italian, Greek, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Russian, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 4140963, to the Arachne identifiers 1210230 and 8157 and to the German National Library authority record 4064682-8. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2097797}
