## Overview
Dongola was the seat and residence of the king of Nubia, on the western bank of the Nile, and its people drink from it. Al-Idrisi describes them as the finest-faced and most handsome of the Sudanese, and says that their food is barley and maize. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.37}
