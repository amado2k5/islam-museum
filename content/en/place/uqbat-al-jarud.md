## Overview
The Pass of al-Jarud is in Fars, between Jarra and [[place/shiraz|Shiraz]]. [[person/al-jarud-al-abdi|Al-Jarud al-Abdi]] fell behind his companions there before dawn for a need, carrying a water-skin. A group of Kurds surrounded him and killed him, so the pass was named after him, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
