## Overview
Nakhla al-Yamaniya is a place between al-Ta'if and [[place/mecca|Mecca]], on the road of the Prophet's return from Hunayn. Yaqut mentioned it in a passage on passing through Nakhla al-Yamaniya, then Qarn, then al-Mayh, then Bahrat al-Raghba'. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut} Its location has not yet been fixed with coordinates.
