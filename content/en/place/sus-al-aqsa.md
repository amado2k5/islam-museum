## Overview
Upper Sus is the farthest of the Sus, in the farthest Maghrib, according to al-Baladhuri. Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib ibn Abi Ubayda ibn Uqba ibn Nafi sent a raid to it in the governorship of Abd Allah ibn al-Habhab, together with the land of the Sudan. He mentions that he gained a victory such as no one had seen the like of. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.324}
