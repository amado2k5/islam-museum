## Overview
al-Hama: one of the fortresses in the districts of Granada in [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. The lord of Cadiz seized it by treachery at the last days of the treaty, in the year 887 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 516-517}

The people of Granada besieged it, trying to recover it, and they cut off its water until a host of Christians came to its relief. Then al-Hama entered it and it was fortified. The attempt of the people of Baza and Guadix to recover it failed, and people gave up hope of it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 516-517}
