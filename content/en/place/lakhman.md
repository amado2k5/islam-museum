## Overview
Lakhman is a large, fine, populous town, one of the cities of the Samriqi Kharlukh, and it has crafts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.932}

Between its people and the Aghzaz there is constant war, according to what al-Idrisi reports. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.933}
