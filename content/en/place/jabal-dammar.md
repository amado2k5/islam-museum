## Overview
Mount Dammar is a mountain three stages from Mount Nafusa. On its slopes live a group of Berbers who ride swift horses and raid the camels of the neighbouring Arabs. Al-Idrisi says that the Arabs complain of their harm. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.299}
