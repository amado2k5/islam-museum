## Overview
Tizanabadh is an estate in the Sawad. According to al-Baladhuri, Uthman granted it to al-Ash'ath ibn Qays al-Kindi. It was called Dayr Nabadh, and they changed its name, and it was attributed to al-Dayzan ibn Mu'awiya ibn al-Ubayd al-Sulayhi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
