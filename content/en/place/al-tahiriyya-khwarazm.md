## Overview
al-Tahiriyya: a city with income and outgoings, farms and blessings, and whose fruits are plentiful. It is the first district of Khwarazm on the west side of the river, and it lies two stages from Rasht. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.696}
