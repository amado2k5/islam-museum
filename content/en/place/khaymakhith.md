## Overview
Khaymakhith is two towns on the river Shuran. The southern of them has many crops and trees, and a great deal of timber is brought to them from the mountains. Al-Idrisi says it lies five days from Dadmi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.930} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.931}
