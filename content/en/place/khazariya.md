## Summary
Al-Khazariyya is a large, populous, spacious city rich in waters, standing on a river; the Khazars are named after it, and it lies forty-five miles from the city of the Alans. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from the city of the Alans to the city of Khazariyya, after which the Khazars are named, is forty-five miles; it is a large, populous, spacious city abounding in waters and standing on a river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}. From Khazariyya to the city of Kira is twenty-five miles, and thence to Qumaniyya, after which the Qumans are named and which is called Qumaniyya al-Sawda, twenty-five miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}. Between Qumaniyya and Kira is a great mountain hard to cross and long to traverse {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}. It was called the Black Qumaniyya because a river in its land dives beneath some ravines of that mountain and emerges into the sea, its water black as smoke — a well-known and uncontested fact {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}. As for the city of the Alans, it is of ancient construction whose builder is unknown, and after it the Alan peoples are named {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}.
