## Overview
A water where a gathering of Banu Tha'laba and Muharib used to assemble. The army of Medina reached it in Muharram in the year 3 AH, stayed there, and then returned to [[place/medina|Medina]] without fighting, according to al-Mubarakfuri. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Its location is not certain in the sources loaded.
