## Overview
Aswan: a small, populous city rich in wheat, vegetables and meat, according to al-Idrisi. It is the furthest point reached upstream on the Nile from the land of Egypt. It lies near the mountain of the cataracts, about twelve stages from it, and he counts it among the frontier posts of Nubia. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.39-40}
