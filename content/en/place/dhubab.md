## Overview
A mountain in Medina according to the editors. [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abd Allah ibn Ubayy]] pitched his camp near Dhubab, lower than the camp of the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

No coordinates are fixed for its location.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut transmits the name from al-Hazimi as Dhibab with a kasra on its first letter, and says of it: a mountain at Medina mentioned in the maghazi and the reports; and from al-'Umrani: Dhibab, like the fly of the same form, a mountain at Medina. He then separates it from Rawdat al-Dhibab, which is another place, and from al-Dhubaba, a place at Aja. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.3}

## The museum's identification
The record vowels the name Dhubab with a damma, following the editors of Ibn Hisham, and makes it a mountain at Medina near which 'Abd Allah ibn Ubayy pitched his camp below the Prophet's camp. Yaqut's entry (vol 3 p.3) transmits it with a kasra from al-Hazimi and in the form of the fly from al-'Umrani, so the two vowellings are both on record; it confirms a mountain at Medina noted in the maghazi as the record says, and it mentions neither Ibn Ubayy's camp nor Ibn Hisham. The record's point (24.4784, 39.6053) lies in the Medina oasis. See [[place/medina|Medina]].
