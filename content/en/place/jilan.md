## Overview
The land of Gilan lies south of the Khazar Sea. Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-bara-ibn-azib|al-Bara ibn Azib]] raided it from Qazvin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He also reports that [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]] raided it in the days of Uthman with the Daylam, Muqan, al-Babar and al-Taylasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
