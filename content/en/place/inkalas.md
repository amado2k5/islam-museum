## Overview
Inkalas is one of the largest cities of Kawar in area and the busiest in trade, and it has pure alum mines, according to al-Idrisi. He says its people travel eastward as far as Egypt and westward as far as Wargalan and the land of the Far Maghrib. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.117} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.118}
