## Overview
Lorca is a fortified town on the back of a mountain, with markets, and with a suburb at its foot enclosed by a wall. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}

## What al-Idrisi reports
Al-Idrisi says that in it are minerals of yellow earth and ochre, and he puts forty miles between it and [[place/murcia|Murcia]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives "Lurqa", with a damma, a sukun, a fatha on the ra and then the qaf, says it is also said "Lirqa", with a silent ra and no waw, and that it is mentioned in its place, and that it is a city in al-Andalus of the districts (a'mal) of Tudmir, with a fortress and a strong stronghold; its land is barren and is watered only by standing water, as in Egypt. He adds that it has grapes whose cluster comes to fifty Iraqi ratls, as an elder of its people told him, saying "God knows", and that it has many fruits. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.25-26} His entry for Tudmir calls it a kura of al-Andalus adjoining the districts of the kura of Jayyan, east of Cordoba, seven days from Cordoba for a rider going straight. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.19}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut and al-Idrisi agree that it is a fortified city with a stronghold: al-Idrisi describes it on the back of a mountain and puts it forty miles from [[place/murcia|Murcia]], while Yaqut puts it in the districts of Tudmir without saying that Tudmir is Murcia, so the museum does not treat the two as one. Yaqut adds that its land is barren and that its grape clusters weigh fifty ratls, which al-Idrisi does not mention, while the yellow earth and ochre are in al-Idrisi alone. The point on the record (37.6712, -1.7017) is modern Lorca by the Wikidata identifier recorded for it; Yaqut gives no coordinates and the distance to Tudmir or Murcia is not taken from his text. Yaqut also has a short separate entry "Lirqa" (damma, sukun, qaf), to which he himself points from the Lurqa entry ("mentioned in its place"): a fortress in the east of al-Andalus, west of Murcia and east of al-Mariyya (Almeria), three days between them, with the nisba of Khalaf ibn Hashim al-Lirqi, Abu al-Qasim, who narrated from Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Utbi. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.16} The museum reads it as the same place under a variant spelling; that is its reading, not Yaqut's statement, and he does not say the two are one.
