## Overview
Lorca is a fortified town on the back of a mountain, with markets, and with a suburb at its foot enclosed by a wall. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}

## What al-Idrisi reports
Al-Idrisi says that in it are minerals of yellow earth and ochre, and he puts forty miles between it and [[place/murcia|Murcia]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}
