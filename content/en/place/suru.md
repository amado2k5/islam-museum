## Overview
Suruç is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «Şanlıurfa (Provinz)». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769216}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2769216 with the preferred name «Suruc», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Şanlıurfa (Provinz)». Among the names it records are «Суруч», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 300075. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769216}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Suruj as a town near Harran in the lands of Mudar. He gives its longitude as sixty-two and a half and a third degrees, and its latitude as thirty-six degrees. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.216}

He records that Iyad ibn Ghanm overran its land, then took the town by agreement on the same terms as the agreement of Edessa (al-Ruha), in 17 AH in the days of Umar. Al-Hariri mentions it again in his Maqamat. Yaqut also gives part of an anecdote about the poet Abu Hayya al-Numayri and the rhyme of the letter jim. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.216}
