## Overview
Tilmila is a small city among the cities of Kuwar, a day from Ibzar, according to al-Idrisi. It has little water, few palms and good dates. Its alum mine is of little benefit, because it is mixed with veins of earth. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.118}
