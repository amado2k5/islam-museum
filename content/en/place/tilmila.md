## Overview
Tilmila is a small town of Kawar, a day from Abzar, with little water and few palms, though its dates are fine. It has an alum mine of little value, since its ore is mixed with many veins of earth, so it is mixed with other alum and sold to merchants. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 118}

## Kawar
Kawar lies in the bed of a valley that comes from the south and runs north, with no water on the surface, though water is found in plenty when one digs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 117} Among its towns is Inklas, one of the largest in extent and the busiest in trade, with mines of pure alum of the highest quality. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 117}

## Alum
Al-Idrisi distinguishes the alum of Kawar by quality: the pure alum in the hills of Inklas has no equal, while that of Tilmila is mixed with earthy veins and is sold only blended with other alum. He notes that he has already mentioned the town of Tilmila in the first clime. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 118}
