## Overview
Al-Ahsa: a town on the Persian Gulf, pleasant but small, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 386}

## Location and history
Al-Idrisi says that the Qarmatians were in it. It lies two stages from al-Qatif. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 386}
