## Overview
Inqiltara is a very large island in the great dark sea. It is entered from the Jun of Sanqala, according to al-Idrisi. It has lands, fortresses, villages, farms, valleys, rivers and mountains, and there is uninhabited land in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.880}
