## Summary
Athafit is a town in the land of Yemen which al-Idrisi places on the road from Khaywan; it has many vineyards and its people drink from a great pool fed by springs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Khaywan one comes to Athafit, a town with many vineyards and few palms, whose people drink from a great pool containing springs of water, and thence to Rida, a small fortress-like town girded by many vineyards and continuous crops {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}. Just before, he had described Khaywan with its villages, buildings, farms and well-watered lands inhabited by clans of Ghassan and a body of Arab tribes, noting that the country of the Ibadis lies near Khaywan, prosperous and with strong fortresses {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}. Athafit is thus a stage on this Yemeni road between Khaywan and Rida.
