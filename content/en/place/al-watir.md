## Summary
A water of Khuza'a in lower [[place/mecca|Mecca]]; at it [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] of [[group/banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] ambushed Khuza'a by night during the Hudaybiyya truce — the [[event/khuzaa-bakr-clash-al-watir|breach]] upon which the conquest of Mecca followed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31-32}.

## The location
Ibn Ishaq describes it as "at a water called al-Watir in lower Mecca" — in the lower part of Mecca's valley {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31}. Its exact spot is not fixed in the source {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/31}.

## For young visitors
A water-well in lower Mecca near which safe people were attacked by night in truce-time — and from that night the road to the conquest began.
