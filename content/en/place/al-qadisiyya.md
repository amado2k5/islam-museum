## Overview
al-Qadisiyya: a place in Iraq. The account of Ibn Ishaq in the Sira of Ibn Hisham reports that Amr ibn Uthman al-Taymi was killed there together with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

An additional source is needed to fix the location precisely; its recorded coordinates are approximate.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives al-Qadisiyya the coordinates that the astronomers assign it: 69 degrees of longitude and 31 and two-thirds degrees of latitude. He puts it fifteen farsakhs from Kufa and four miles from al-Udhayb. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.291}

He gives several accounts of its name: that it was named after the Qadis of Herat; that al-Madaini said it was once called Qadis; and that Ibn Uyayna reported that Abraham passed by it, and an old woman washed his head, and he said it was made holy land, so it was called al-Qadisiyya. He dates the battle of al-Qadisiyya to the year 16 AH in the time of Umar, when Sa'd watched the fighting from the palace and was taunted for cowardice, and a Muslim poet answered the taunt. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 pp.291-292}
