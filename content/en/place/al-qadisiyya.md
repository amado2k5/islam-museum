## Overview
al-Qadisiyya: a place in Iraq. The account of Ibn Ishaq in the Sira of Ibn Hisham reports that Amr ibn Uthman al-Taymi was killed there together with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

An additional source is needed to fix the location precisely; its recorded coordinates are approximate.
