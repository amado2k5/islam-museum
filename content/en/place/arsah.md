## Overview
Arsah: a populous city with many houses, waters and rivers, and with vineyards and farms, on the great mountain of Rashnan. al-Idrisi reports that four stages lie between it and Khaymakhith. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.931}
