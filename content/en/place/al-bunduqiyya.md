## Overview
al-Bunduqiyya: the land of the Venetians, which lies in the middle of their country, at the sea known as the Western Sea. It has inhabited islands, six of them: three in a row, and three in the row after it. It has a large fleet. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.771}
