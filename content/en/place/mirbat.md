## Overview
Mirbat is a city on the coast of the land of [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]]. Its mountains grow frankincense trees, and frankincense is shipped from it to other lands. Its people are a mixture of Yemeni tribes and others, and according to al-Idrisi it lies four days by land from Hasik. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.56}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Mirbat as the port of the city of Zafar, about five farsakhs away. Since Zafar had no harbour where ships could anchor, Mirbat's good anchorage became well known to merchants. He describes it as a city apart, between Hadramawt and Oman on the coast, with its own ruler who owed obedience to no one. Near the city is a mountain that grows frankincense; the harvest is the king's revenue, and those who gather it share in it. The people are Arabs who keep an old dress, he says, and combine goodness with a harsh temper. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.97}
