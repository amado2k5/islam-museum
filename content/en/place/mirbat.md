## Overview
Mirbat is a city on the coast of the land of [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]]. Its mountains grow frankincense trees, and frankincense is shipped from it to other lands. Its people are a mixture of Yemeni tribes and others, and according to al-Idrisi it lies four days by land from Hasik. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.56}
