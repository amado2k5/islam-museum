## Overview
Nawaba is a town six days from Kusha al-Waghila, a little distance from the Nile. It lies above the equator, with few inhabitants and little trade, in a hot, dry and extremely parched land. Its people drink from springs that feed the Nile there. Al-Idrisi counts its land among the lands of Nubia mentioned earlier; he fixes its place by Kusha al-Waghila and the Nile and does not describe its buildings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 37}

## Rule
It is under the king of Nubia, called Kasil, a title inherited by the kings of Nubia, whose seat and royal house are in the city of Dongola. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 37}

## Dongola
The city of Dongola stands on the west of the Nile, on its bank, from which its people drink; their food is barley, sorghum and dates brought to them from neighbouring lands, and their drink is the beer made from sorghum. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 37}
