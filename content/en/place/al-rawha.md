## Summary
Al-Rawha, an estate (qati'a) in the Sawad which, in al-Baladhuri, Uthman ibn Affan granted to Adi ibn Hatim al-Ta'i. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 382}

## Details
Musa ibn Talha, quoted by al-Baladhuri, says that the first to grant estates in Iraq was Uthman ibn Affan, who granted qata'i' from the crown lands of Kisra and from the land of the Jalia: he granted Talha al-Nashastaj, granted Wathil ibn Hajar al-Hadrami what adjoined Arara, granted Khabbab ibn al-Aratt Sabina, granted Adi ibn Hatim al-Ta'i al-Rawha, granted Khalid ibn Urfuta land at Hammam A'yan, granted al-Ash'ath ibn Qays al-Kindi Tiznabadh, and granted Jarir ibn Abdallah al-Bajali his land on the bank of the Euphrates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 382} Al-Rawha is thus, in his account, one of the crown-land estates Uthman distributed among leading Companions in Iraq, allotted to Adi ibn Hatim, the chief of Tayyi'. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 382}
