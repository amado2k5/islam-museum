## Overview
Tirmidh is a city on the Jayhun (Oxus). Khatun carried the peace treaty to Sa'id ibn Uthman, and he stayed on until he conquered it by treaty. Then its king, Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim, sought protection, and he drove him out of it and prevailed over it. Then it passed to the governors, and its people rebelled, and [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] conquered it, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
