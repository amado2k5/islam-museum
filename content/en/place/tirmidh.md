## Overview
Tirmidh is a fortress in Transoxiana. Governors held it after a rebel party had seized it, and Qutayba ibn Muslim later took it after its people broke their treaty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 579}

## Qutayba's conquest
Al-Baladhuri reports that when Qutayba reached Tirmidh, its lady (khatun) sent him an offer of peace, and he stayed before the town until he took it by treaty. After the man who had seized it was killed, it passed into the hands of the governors. Then its people broke their covenant, and Qutayba took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 579}

## Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim
When Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami was killed, his son Musa, the lord of Tirmidh, went to it and was given protection there. Later the rebels drove him out of the fortress and took control of it. Musa had stayed in the house of the dihqan (landlord) of Tirmidh, and when he had eaten and lay down to rest, the dihqan told him to leave. Musa replied that he knew of no dwelling like this one. He fought the people of Tirmidh until he overcame them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 584}

## Appeals to the Turks
The dihqan of Tirmidh and its people then went out to the Turks to seek their help, but the Turks did not help them. They told them that God curse them, for a single man with a hundred men had come and driven them from their own town. The people of Tirmidh went on seeking the help of the Turks until they gave it to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 584}
