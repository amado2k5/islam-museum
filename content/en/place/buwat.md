## Overview
Buwat: a mountain of the mountains of Juhayna, on the side of the road to al-Sham, about four postal stages from Medina. The Prophet went out to it to intercept a caravan of Quraysh in Rabi al-Awwal in the year 2 AH. It is one of two branch mountains, together with Radwa. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}
