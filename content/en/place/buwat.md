## Overview
Buwat: a mountain of the mountains of Juhayna, on the side of the road to al-Sham, about four postal stages from Medina. The Prophet went out to it to intercept a caravan of Quraysh in Rabi al-Awwal in the year 2 AH. It is one of two branch mountains, together with Radwa. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

No coordinates are given for it in the source; an additional source is needed.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives Buwat a short entry. He gives the spelling with a damma on the first letter and an unpointed final ṭā', calls it a valley among the valleys of al-Qibliyya on the authority of al-Zamakhshari, who had it from Ali al-Alawi, and adds that the Maghribi teachers al-Asili, al-Udhri and al-Mustamli transmit it with a fatha on the first letter, though the damma form is the better known. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.503} Then, on the word of unnamed informants, he says it is a mountain of Juhayna in the region of Radwa, which the Prophet raided in Rabi' al-Awwal of the second year after the hijra, going out against Quraysh, and that he came back without a clash. The entry ends with a line of verse by a poet whom Yaqut does not name. No other entry in the book mentions Buwat: the museum searched all the volumes for the spelling without vowel marks and found only this entry, the remaining hits being other words such as "batin".

## The museum's identification
The record takes its text from al-Mubarakfuri: a mountain of Juhayna on the side of the Syria road, about four barids from Medina, a branch mountain with Radwa, to which the Prophet went out in Rabi' al-Awwal of 2 AH. Yaqut agrees on Juhayna, on Radwa, and on the month and the year, and adds the vowelling and the disagreement of the transmitters. He leaves open whether Buwat is a valley or a mountain and offers both descriptions. Its distance from Medina and the Syria road are not in his entry, so the figure of four barids rests on al-Mubarakfuri alone, and the museum does not convert it to kilometres here. The record's point (24.4, 38.3) was set by a geocoding pass and lies about 15 km from the museum's Radwa point and about 133 km from its Medina point. The first fits Yaqut's "in the region of Radwa"; the second is a straight-line figure that neither source tests. See also [[event/expedition-of-buwat|the expedition of Buwat]].
