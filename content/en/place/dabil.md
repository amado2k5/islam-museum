## Overview
Dabil: one of the third-rank cities of Armenia. Anushirvan built it and fortified it. In the account of al-Waqidi, Habib ibn Maslama besieged its people, and al-Murayan the Byzantine met him, attacked him by night and killed him, and took booty from his camp. The firmer account among them is that Salman met al-Murayan at Qaliqala. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
