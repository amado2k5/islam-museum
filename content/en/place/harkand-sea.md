## Overview
The Sea of Harkand is the sea in which lie the islands famous for the great ships carved from wood. Al-Idrisi says that the great island in it is well known, and that it has a mountain on which Adam is said to have landed. The author reports this. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.72-73}
