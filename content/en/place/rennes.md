## Overview
Rennes is a large city, extensive in its regions and fortified, according to al-Idrisi. It has brisk markets and continuous industries. It lies on the salt sea, and about eighty miles from Shant Mujayl. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}
