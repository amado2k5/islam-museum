## Overview
Rennes is a large city in the land of Britannia. Its surroundings are settled and its produce is plentiful. It has a strong wall, busy markets and lasting crafts, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}

## Details
Al-Idrisi describes Nantes as a city on the shore, at the bottom of a gulf that runs out of the sea. It is the first land of Britannia, a large, populous town with farming, travel and ships, and it is well fortified. From Nantes to Shant Majyal, the last land of Britannia, the land distance is a hundred and ten miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}

By land from Nantes to Rennes is eighty miles. Rennes is a large city whose surroundings are settled and whose produce is plentiful. It has a strong wall, busy markets and lasting crafts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}

From Rennes by land to Shant Majyal is forty miles. Shant Majyal is a civilised town on the salt sea, pleasant in its region and well built. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}
