## Overview
Qasr Abd al-Karim is a small, populous town lived in by a mixed company of Berbers called Danhaja, on the river Ulks, about three miles from the sea in land that is mostly sand. It has farms and fertility, game of land and sea, a busy market and a number of crafts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 246}

## Distances
From the lands of Miknasa westward to Qasr Abd al-Karim is three stages. From the fort to the city of Sala on the Salt Sea is two stages, by way of al-Ma'mura. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 246} Elsewhere the text runs from Tashmis to Qasr Abd al-Karim, which is near the sea and two days from [[place/tanja|Tangier]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 530}

## River and market
The Ulks is a great river of the Maghrib that runs south from the fort. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 246} Qasr Abd al-Karim is a small town on the bank of the Lukks river with markets to its size where people buy and sell; provisions are plentiful and prosperity general. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 530}
