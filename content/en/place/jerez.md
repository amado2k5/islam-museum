## Overview
A great, populous town with large markets, from the district of Sherish in the west of [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}

Al-Maqqari reports from al-Hajari that it was built by Seville, and that its valley is a son of its valley, and that its people have high spirits and elegance in dress and in displaying luxury. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}

It is distinguished by the fine making of cheeses and the goodness of its cheese. The people of al-Andalus say that whoever enters Sherish and does not eat its cheeses there is deprived. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}
