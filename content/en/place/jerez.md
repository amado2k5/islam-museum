## Overview
A great, populous town with large markets, from the district of Sherish in the west of [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}

Al-Maqqari reports from al-Hajari that it was built by Seville, and that its valley is a son of its valley, and that its people have high spirits and elegance in dress and in displaying luxury. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}

It is distinguished by the fine making of cheeses and the goodness of its cheese. The people of al-Andalus say that whoever enters Sherish and does not eat its cheeses there is deprived. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.199}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records Sharish, first letter opened and second broken, then a dotted ya': a large city in the district of Shadhuna (Sidonia), the capital of that district; in his day they called it Sharsh. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.340} He has a second entry under the same name, spelled with a nun, two dotted shins and a ra' between them then a ya': a mountain between Milyana and Tlemcen. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.355}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut's Sharish, city of the Shadhuna district, is the record's Sharish in western al-Andalus; the Sharish mountain between Milyana and Tlemcen is another, Maghrebi, place with no bearing on the city, and Yaqut's single line on the city is briefer than al-Maqqari's description quoted in the overview.
