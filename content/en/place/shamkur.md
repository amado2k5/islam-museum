## Overview
Shamkur (al-Mutawakkiliyya) is an ancient city in Arran, which belongs to [[place/armenia|Armenia]] according to the book Futuh al-Buldan. A man of the men of Salman ibn Rabi'a conquered it, and then the Sawardiyya ruined it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Bugha, client of al-Mu'tasim, rebuilt it in the year 240 AH, settled in it a group of Khazars who had sought safety and desired Islam, and transferred to it merchants of Barda'a. He named it al-Mutawakkiliyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
