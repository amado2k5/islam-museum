## Overview
Malanda is a large city on the coast of the sea in the land of the Zanj. Its people live by hunting on land and sea, and they extract iron and work it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.59}

Al-Idrisi says that they claim to be able to bewitch harmful animals, so that the animals harm only those they wish to harm. He adds that their sorcerer is called in their language "al-Muqanqa". {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.59}
