## Overview
Atrasa: a city on the river Ganges (al-Ghanj), and one of the frontier posts of Qanuj, according to al-Idrisi. It borders other cities, and lies five stages from Niyast. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.194}
