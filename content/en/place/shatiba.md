## Overview
Shatiba is a city whose beauty and strength are proverbial, according to al-Idrisi. Good paper is made there, which reaches East and West, and it lies thirty-two miles from [[place/valencia|Valencia]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.556}
