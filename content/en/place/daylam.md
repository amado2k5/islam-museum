## Overview
A mountainous land south of the Caspian Sea and west of [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]]. al-Baladhuri reports that Urwa ibn Zayd broke the Daylam together with the people of [[place/rayy|al-Rayy]] on the day of the conquest, and that Kathir ibn Shihab raided them from al-Rayy and struck them. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas also raided them in his second governorship, and the people of al-Rayy continued to raid them from Dastabi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
