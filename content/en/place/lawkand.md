## Summary
Lawkand is a city of Transoxiana equal to Halaward in size, markets and commerce, a populous and fine town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.488}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that Halaward is a fine populous city with markets, trade and movement in and out, and that Lawkand is its like in size, markets and commerce {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.488}. From the small town of Ma'bar Arhan to Halaward is two stages, and to Halbak likewise; Halbak is a town of fine ground and lovely extent with many gardens and promenades, built of clay and gypsum, with numerous markets and well-off people, where the sultan stays {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.488}. Lawkand thus stands in the Halaward neighbourhood on the road toward Mink.
