## Overview
Isnakhwa is a town in an extended land where nothing grows except saffron. From it, saffron is shipped to all the regions of China, according to al-Idrisi. It lies eight stages from Chinese territory. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.211}
