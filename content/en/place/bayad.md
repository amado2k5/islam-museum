## Overview
Bayad is a village in the [[place/al-fayyum|Fayyum]] {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.

## Water and ponds
The great canal of the Fayyum ends at Bayad and fills its ponds and the other ponds. The ponds have distribution outlets, and each receives the water it needs for its share {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.
