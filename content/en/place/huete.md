## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Wabdha is a city of al-Andalus in the district of Shant Barriyya, a short entry in which Yaqut separates it from Wabdha near Toledo. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.359}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the name with a fatha on the waw, a sukun on the ba and a dotted dhal, and says it is a city of the districts of Shant Barriyya in al-Andalus. Directly after it he enters Wabdha, with a different final letter, a city of al-Andalus near Toledo. He treats them as two places and says no more about either. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.359}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record calls Huete a municipality in the province of Cuenca, Spain. Yaqut says only that Wabdha belongs to Shant Barriyya; he does not name Cuenca or the present town. The museum's link between the Andalusi Wabdha and this municipality is its own reading, based on the Arabic label matching the Spanish name and on Shant Barriyya lying in that country, and it has not been confirmed from a second source. Yaqut also enters a place near Toledo, and the museum has not settled which of the two the municipality continues. Cuenca has its record, [[place/cuenca|Cuenca]].
