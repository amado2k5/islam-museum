## Overview
Bish: a famous city among the bases of the land of the Romans, with many markets and houses, and with ships, horses and provisions. It lies on a river that comes to it from a mountain, and it lies about forty miles from Genoa. The museum's record gives it as Pisa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.750}
