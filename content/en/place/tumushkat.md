## Overview
Tumushkat is a land beyond the river. [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] raided it with Karminiyya in the year 88 AH and made peace with them, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
