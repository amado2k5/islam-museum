## Overview
Caesarea is a coastal city in the district (jund) of Palestine, which according to the source belongs administratively to [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/conquest-of-caesarea|The conquest of Caesarea]].

## The conquest
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] was the first to besiege it, in the year 13 AH, and that [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] carried out its conquest in Shawwal of the year 19 AH, though some say the beginning of the year 20. This came after a siege of about seven years and the guidance of Yusuf the Jew to the tunnel (sarb). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He relays from al-Waqidi large figures for those found in it, the number of its markets, and the guard of its wall. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} These are figures each of which needs an additional source.

## After the conquest
The source mentions that the Byzantines raided it in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr, ravaged it and demolished its mosque. When matters were settled for [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]], he restored it, rebuilt its mosque and filled it with men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Muqaddasi's description
Al-Muqaddasi said around the year 375 AH that there is no city on the Roman Sea more glorious or more abundant in goods than it. It has an impregnable fortress, and around it a thriving suburb. Its people drink from wells and cisterns. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.142}
