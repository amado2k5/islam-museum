## Summary
Fuwwa is an Egyptian town on the Rosetta branch of the Nile. Its record states that Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his way to Damietta, a passage not yet verified in the scanned 1958 edition. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

## Details
Fuwwa lies in the Egyptian Delta on the Rosetta branch; the coordinates recorded for it in the museum's card come from the GeoNames entry for the modern town of the same name. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The card also records it as a place Ibn Battuta passed on his way to Damietta; a review of the Arabic text of the Maktaba Tijariyya al-Kubra edition (Cairo, 1958) has not surfaced the name in the passages read, so its mention in the rihla still needs verification on the page, or an additional supporting source. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}
