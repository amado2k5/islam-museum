## Overview
Sanitriyya is a small city at the edge of the desert, according to al-Idrisi. It has a pulpit (minbar), and it is inhabited by Berbers and civilised Arabs. Its water comes from wells and a few springs. It lies four stages from Bahrayn. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.123} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}
