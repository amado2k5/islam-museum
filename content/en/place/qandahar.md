## Overview
Qandahar is a city on the road from Sijistan to India. [[person/abbad-ibn-ziyad|Abbad ibn Ziyad]] conquered it after fighting, and some Muslim men were wounded there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The report says that he modelled it on the tall caps (qalansiyy) of its people, and so it was called "al-Ibadiyya". It also transmits the saying of Ibn Mufarragh about it: "In Qandahar, and whoever's death is written in Qandahar, news of him is kept from reaching him." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
