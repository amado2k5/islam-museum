## Overview
The Diyala is a large river near Baghdad that Yaqut identifies in the Mu'jam al-buldan with the great river of Baʿquba and with the river Tamarra. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.495}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut fixes the name as Dayālā, with a fatḥa on the first letter and imāla on the lām, and calls it a large river near Baghdad, the great river of Baʿquba, which flows beside that town. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.495}

He says it is the boundary between the Khurasan road and al-Khalis, and that it is the river Tamarra itself, so for him the two names are one river. The entry says nothing more about its course, source or history. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.495}
