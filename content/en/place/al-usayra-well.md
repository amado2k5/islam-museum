## Summary
A well belonging to the Ansari Banu Umayya called al-'Usayra — "the hard" — or, in another transmission, al-'Abira; the Prophet renamed it al-Yasira, "the easy," and used to drink from it. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 536}

## Details
Al-Waqidi, as al-Baladhuri relates in Ansab al-Ashraf, said: "The Prophet used to drink from a well of the Ansari Banu Umayya called al-'Abira, and he renamed it al-Yasira; in some reports it was called al-'Usayra and he renamed it al-Yasira — the first is the sounder." {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 536}

Al-Usayra stands in al-Baladhuri's running list of the wells of Medina, which includes the well of Malik ibn al-Nadr known as Bi'r Anas, and the well of Ruma at al-Aqiq — into which the Prophet spat so that its water turned sweet, named after a Muzayna woman who used to draw for the people, and bought by Uthman ibn Affan for four hundred dinars to be given in charity. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 536}
