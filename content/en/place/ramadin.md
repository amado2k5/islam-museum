## Overview
Ramadin is a town attributed to [[place/baykand|Baykand]], two farsakhs from it, according to the Futuh al-Buldan. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad conquered it together with Baykand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
