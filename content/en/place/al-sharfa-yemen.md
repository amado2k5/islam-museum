## Overview
Al-Sharfa is a place "near Sana'a" that [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached when he went to Yemen in 280 AH; the people submitted to him and obeyed, and he stayed among them a short while. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} See [[event/al-hadi-first-journey-to-yemen-280]]. The text locates it no more closely, so no coordinates are given here.
