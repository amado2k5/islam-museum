## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, the land of the Beja is a land between it and the lands of Islam a month's march through waste ground, mountains that are hard to cross, and in it are gold mines. Muhammad al-Qummi raided it in the time of [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] until he reached the fortress of its king. In the year 241 AH they made peace with him on tribute and a levy, and on condition that the Muslims should not be prevented from working in the gold mine. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.333}
