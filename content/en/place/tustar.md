## Overview
Tustar is a town mentioned by al-Baladhuri, where Jaban gathered a great host. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] sent to him [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] and Hanzala the scribe. When they arrived at it, he fled. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
