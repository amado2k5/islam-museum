## Overview
Shimshat is one of the frontier posts of [[place/jazira|al-Jazira]], and according to al-Baladhuri it is counted as the fourth Armenia. Habib ibn Maslama al-Fihri and Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal al-Sulami conquered it under the command of Mu'awiya in the days of Uthman, on terms like the peace of al-Ruha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.259}

Safwan stayed in it and died there. Constantine besieged it in the year 133 AH and achieved nothing against it. It remained subject to kharaj until al-Mutawakkil made it a tithe district (ushriyya), like the other frontier posts. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.269}

From it came Malik ibn Abd Allah al-Khath'ami, known as Malik al-Sawa'if (of the summer raids), campaigning in the year 46 AH. Around it revolve the conquests of Kamakh, Yaghra and Khilat. The details of the conquest are at [[event/conquest-of-shimshat|The conquest of Shimshat]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.269}
