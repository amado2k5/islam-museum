## Overview
Abar Qubadh: a town in the districts of the Tigris. The conquest accounts say that [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan]] turned to it at once, and God granted him its conquest. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
