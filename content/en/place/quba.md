## Summary
A village at Medina's southern edge where the Prophet alighted, coming from the Hijra, on Monday 8 Rabi' al-Awwal 1 AH — 23 September 622 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}.

## The stay
The Prophet stayed at Quba four days on Ibn Ishaq's account, which the author chose — al-Bukhari has fourteen nights and also thirteen — then rode to Medina on Friday {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}.

He founded the mosque of Quba there and prayed in it — the first mosque founded on piety after the prophethood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}. The Prophet lodged with [[person/kulthum-ibn-al-hidm|Kulthum ibn al-Hidm]] — the sounder account — though it was said Sa'd ibn Khaythama {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 171}.
