## Overview
Umm Dunayn is a place [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] reached after [[place/bilbays|Bilbays]]. The Romans fought him hard there and victory was slow, so he wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] for help, and Umar sent four thousand, bringing his force to eight thousand. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 87}

## Horsemen behind the hill
Ibn Wahb reports from Abd al-Rahman ibn Shurayh that a man of Lakhm asked Amr for horsemen to come at the Romans from behind during the battle; Amr sent five hundred riders with him, who went round behind the hill and entered the caves of Banu Wa'il before dawn. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 87} Another report says the five hundred were led by [[person/kharija-ibn-hudhafa-al-adawi|Kharija ibn Hudhafa]], and that when battle was joined in the morning they came out behind the Romans, who fled into the fortress. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 88}
