## Overview
Jayyan is a town with low prices, abundant meat and honey, where silkworms are raised. It has a congregational mosque and a strong citadel with a way up to it, and Mount Kur joins it. It lies twenty miles from Bayyasa, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.568}
