## Summary
Shuzas is a large, populous city in the land of Ifriziya. Its markets are fine, its houses are well settled, and it has many vineyards and trees. Al-Idrisi gives its distance from Sikla as eighty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879}

## Details
Al-Idrisi places Shuzas in the land of Ifriziya, which lies north of the land of Lumaniya. It is bounded on the west by the land of Lubban, on the east by the land of Shusuniya, and on the north by the Dark Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879} He counts Sikla, Shuzas, Akronangja and Berna among its cities, and says that these lands have similar sizes and equal settlement. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879}

The distances he gives link these cities by road. From Sikla to Shuzas is eighty miles; from Sikla to Akronangja, seventy; and from Akronangja to Berna on the sea, seven miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879} Shuzas therefore sits within a group of cities that Idrisi describes as close in size and settlement.

He describes Shuzas itself as a large city, with markets and houses well kept, and with many vineyards and trees. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879} From Shuzas to Dhulbarla is sixty miles to the south. Dhulbarla is a good town at the foot of a mountain, with running water, gardens and crops, and from Dhulbarla back to Sikla is seventy miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.880}
