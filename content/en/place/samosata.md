## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalises the name with a damma and a fatha, and says it is a city on the bank of the Euphrates at the edge of Byzantine territory on the Euphrates' western side, with a citadel on one flank where Armenians live; in his day its holder was al-Malik al-Afdal Ali, son of al-Malik al-Nasir Yusuf ibn Ayyub (Saladin), and al-Mutanabbi mentions it in his verse. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.258} He gives two sets of coordinates: longitude 54 degrees 40 minutes and latitude 36 degrees 20 minutes, and, from the astronomical tables of Abu Awn, the fourth climate with longitude 32 degrees 40 minutes and latitude 36 degrees 20 minutes. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.258} Among those named for it he lists Abu al-Qasim Ali ibn Muhammad al-Sumaysati al-Sulami, known as al-Jumaysh, who died in Damascus in Rabi II 453 and was buried in his house at the Bab al-Natifaniyyin; he had made the house a waqf for poor Muslims and Sufis, its upper part for the mosque, and gave his birth as Ramadan 377. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.258}
