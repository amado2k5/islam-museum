## Overview
Sivas is a city in Turkey, the centre of the province of the same name, known in antiquity as Sebastia. In the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan it has no entry of its own and is only named in passing, in the entry for Tawfat, as lying two days away, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090495}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2090495 with the preferred name «Sivas», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2181765. Among the names it records are «Sevas», «Siwas», «Sebastia» and «Megalopolis». It links the place to GeoNames no. 300619, to Pleiades no. 629075 and to Arachne entries. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090495}
