## Overview
Nitram is a civilised city of wide extent, on level ground, with running water, farms and abundance. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies forty miles north of Aqra. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}
