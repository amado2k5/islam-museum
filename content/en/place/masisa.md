## Overview
Al-Masisa is the Syrian frontier fortress on the Jayhan river. Al-Baladhuri mentions that the Muslims did not settle there until Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Malik built its fortress in the year 84 AH on its old foundations. He settled three hundred selected men there and built a mosque on its hill. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/building-of-masisa|The building of al-Masisa]].

It had a church which Byzantine vanguards would visit. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wished to demolish it, but held back when it became known that it defended Antioch. Then Hisham, Marwan and Abu al-Abbas succeeded one another in building it, followed by al-Mansur, al-Mahdi and Harun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
