## Summary
A valley in [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]]'s territory between Mecca and Ta'if; Malik ibn Awf encamped there with the tribes' hosts and wealth before the battle of Hunayn, Hawazin's fugitives rallied there with [[person/durayd-ibn-al-simma|Durayd ibn al-Simma]] after the rout, and there the [[event/expedition-to-awtas|Awtas detachment]] took place {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80-81, 97-99}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Durayd ibn al-Simma describes the land of Awtas when they camped at it: "A fine field for cavalry — no rugged ground to break hooves, no soft flat to sink in" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/81}. There the decisive battle of Abu Amir al-Ash'ari's detachment turned, and Durayd ibn al-Simma was killed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/93-98}.

## For young visitors
A mountain valley suited to cavalry movement where Hawazin's tribes camped with their wealth — and where the second battle after Hunayn turned.
