## Overview
Nadkhu is a city on a lake of sweet water, where there is much bird life resembling the hoopoe. It lies four stages from Khaqan, and many Turks go to it because of its abundant spring and grass, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.521}
