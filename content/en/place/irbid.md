## Overview
Irbid is a city in Jordan, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, with the name «Arbēla» among its names. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281649}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2281649 with the preferred name «Irbid» (German), classed as a populated place, with a parent place above it in the hierarchy. Among the names it records are «إربد» (Arabic), «Arbēla», «İrbid» (Turkish) and «Ирбид» (Russian), and it links the place to GeoNames no. 248946 and Pleiades no. 678028. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281649}
