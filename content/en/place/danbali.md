## Summary
Danbali was a small flourishing town of the land of Rumaniyya, lying in low ground with vineyards and sound buildings, a day's journey from Misyunis. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.899}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from the city of Aghrizinus eastward to the city of Misyunis is forty miles, that is a day; Misyunis is a populous city where the Rus have a trading post, civilised, with busy markets and abundant goods, and its site is on a mountain between which and Danbali is a day {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.899}. The city of Danbali is a small populous city in low ground, with vineyards and sound buildings {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.899}. From Danbali to the city of Framinyak eastward is half a day; it is a town in low ground near a small mountain rich in trees and plantations, with continuous crops and buildings, and game is plentiful there {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.899}. The author lists Danbali among the towns of the land of Rumaniyya comprised in the fourth part of the sixth climate, together with Farwi, Liglighu, Aqrnus, Istinus and other cities {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. In his account it is a small lowland but inhabited town, neighbouring populous mountain-top Misyunis, on the Rumaniyya road eastward to Framinyak.
