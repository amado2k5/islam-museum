## Summary
Fasa, a town in Fars; its great man made peace on the same terms as the sulh of Darabjird, and it is said the Hirbadh himself settled it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Uthman ibn Abi al-As came to Darabjird — where the Shadhrawan was the banner of their faith, presided over by the Hirbadh — and the Hirbadh made peace with him for money he gave him, on the condition that all the people of Darabjird be treated on a par with those of Fars whose lands had been taken. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547} A host then massed against him near Jahram, so he scattered them and took the land of Jahram; Uthman then came to Fasa, whose great man made peace on the same terms as the sulh of Darabjird — it is also said that the Hirbadh settled on its behalf. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547} Uthman ibn Abi al-As went on to the city of Sabur in year 23 — some say 24 — before Abu Musa's governorship of Basra on behalf of Uthman ibn Affan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547} Fasa is thus, in his telling, a town in the Darabjird region that received a settlement modelled on Darabjird's.
