## Overview
Al-Fur' is a place near [[place/medina|Medina]] that the editor's footnote, citing Mu'jam al-Buldan, calls a village in its environs. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] and Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn Yahya went there when they left Medina at the new moon of Dhu al-Hijja, and came to [[person/al-husayn-ibn-al-qasim-al-rassi|al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim]], the father of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], who was glad of their coming, emptied a house for them near his own and honoured them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37}
