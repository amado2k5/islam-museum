## Overview
Zaila is a city on the coast of the sea connected to the Qulzum, in the land of [[place/abyssinia|Abyssinia]], according to al-Idrisi. Its people are numerous, and ships come to it with kinds of trades. Slaves and silver are exported from it, and gold is scarce in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.44}
