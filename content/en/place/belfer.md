## Overview
Belfer: a city on the salt sea. al-Idrisi describes it as civilised and capable, with fish-catching grounds, livelihoods and plenty of food. Its ships sail to Shant Ya'qub, and it lies about seventy miles from Shant Juan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.855}
