## Summary
Rūdhistū is a fine city sixty miles from Salūnī. Al-Idrisi places the great city of Constantinople about twelve miles east of it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}

## Details
Al-Idrisi describes Rūdhistū in the chain of towns in the land of the Rūm. From Aqrinus to Fārūy is forty miles, and Fārūy is a city on the river Akhīlū. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}

From Fārūy eastwards is Salūnī, fifty miles away; he calls Salūnī a city set in a plain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}

From Salūnī to Rūdhistū is sixty miles, and he calls Rūdhistū "a fine city." {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}

From Rūdhistū eastwards is the great city of Constantinople, at twelve miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}

Al-Idrisi does not describe the people, markets or farming of Rūdhistū in this passage. The entry therefore records its position between Salūnī and Constantinople. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.894}
