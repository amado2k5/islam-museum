## Overview
Al-Idrisi describes Nevers as a noble and distinguished town, with men of courage, one of the finest towns of the lands, with flourishing villages and abundant revenues. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742}

## Setting and distances
From Lyon to Nevers is one hundred and thirty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 741} From Nevers to Limoges is sixty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 741} From Nevers eastward, Dujun is thirty miles away, Lanka sixty and Itruwish sixty. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742} From Dujun to Lakka is seventy miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742} From Mashkun to Lyon is ninety miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742}

## The surrounding land
From Burjush to Nevers is eighty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742} Burjush is among the largest lands of the Franks, and its region stands apart on its own. It is bounded on the south by the land of Clermont, on the north by the region of Tarash, on the west by the land of Bitarsh and on the east by the land of the Burgundians of the Franks. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742}

## A note on names
The page does not give a modern identification for Nevers, Dujun, Lanka or Itruwish, so these names are kept as the text gives them. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 742}
