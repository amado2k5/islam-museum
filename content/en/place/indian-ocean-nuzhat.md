## Overview
Al-Idrisi describes this sea as beginning thirteen degrees north of the equator, and extending westward along the line past China, India, Sind and the south of Yemen as far as Bab al-Mandab. He estimates its length at about four thousand five hundred farsakhs, and mentions about three hundred islands in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.9}
