## Overview
Lughus is one of the eternal islands in the western sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.104}

## What al-Idrisi reports
Al-Idrisi reports, from the author of the book of wonders, that on the island is a tall idol of firm construction with no road to it. He reports that its builder, Tubba' Dhu al-Marahid, was buried in it, in a temple of marble and coloured glass. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.104}

The account is transmitted from the book of wonders, not from direct observation. It needs an additional source.
