## Overview
The Dam of Ma'rib is a great dam in the course of water near the city of Ma'rib. Al-Idrisi mentions that its people perfected its construction, that their homes were built on it, and that they watered their farms from it. He mentions that a great flood destroyed the dam, and the people of the city and the Arabs scattered across the land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.153}
