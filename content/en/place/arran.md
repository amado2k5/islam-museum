## Overview
Aran: the first land of [[place/armenia|Armenia]], which was in the hands of the Khazars, according to al-Baladhuri. The commander of Qubadh ibn Firuz set foot in it with twelve thousand men, and he conquered what lay between the river al-Ras and Shirwan. Qubadh built in it al-Bailaqan, Barada'a, Qibla and Sadd al-Laban. Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili had been ordered by Uthman to raid it, after the order concerning the spoils at Qaliqala. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says Arran is a foreign name for a wide province with many lands, among them Janza, which common speech calls Kanja, Barda'a, Shamkur and Bilqan. The river Rass divides Arran from Adharbayjan: the land on its western and northern side belongs to Arran, and the land to the east belongs to Adharbayjan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.136}

Al-Nasr, whom Yaqut quotes, places Arran among the regions of Armenia, and mentions it with Sijistan. The same name is also borne by Harran, the well-known town in the land of Mudar, where a fine cloth called khazz was once made. Yaqut adds that a fortress named Arran stands in the districts of Qazvin. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.136}
