## Overview
Aran: the first land of [[place/armenia|Armenia]], which was in the hands of the Khazars, according to al-Baladhuri. The commander of Qubadh ibn Firuz set foot in it with twelve thousand men, and he conquered what lay between the river al-Ras and Shirwan. Qubadh built in it al-Bailaqan, Barada'a, Qibla and Sadd al-Laban. Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili had been ordered by Uthman to raid it, after the order concerning the spoils at Qaliqala. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
