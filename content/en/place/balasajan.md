## Overview
al-Balasajan: a mountain in Azerbaijan in which are the Kurds of al-Balasajan, according to al-Baladhuri. The peace of Hudhayfa stipulated with the lord of the land that he should not molest the Kurds of al-Balasajan, Sabalan and Satrudan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
