## Summary
Abura was a large town in the land of Finland in the far north, which al-Idrisi names with Kalmar as the only two settled cities of that land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.953}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that the land of Finland is a country of many villages, settlements and flocks, yet it holds no populated cities except Abura and Kalmar, both large towns {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.953}. Nevertheless a rough pastoral way of life prevails over them, lawlessness weighs on their people, they have less stored provisions than they need, and rain falls on them almost without ceasing {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.953}. Thus his Abura appears as a sizable city in a remote, cold, rainy clime whose inhabitants lived on whatever herding and sparse husbandry provided.
