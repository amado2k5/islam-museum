## Overview
Batn Marr: a place on the road from Mecca to al-Juhfa, about sixteen miles from Mecca. In it is a water spring in a sandy channel, and around it are small palm groves, to which a people of the Arabs resort. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.141}
