## Overview
Ubda is a small city near the great river, between Jayyan and Basta. Its land is fertile and its crops are beneficial, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.569} It may be the same city mentioned as [[place/ubeda|Ubeda]], which needs an additional source.
