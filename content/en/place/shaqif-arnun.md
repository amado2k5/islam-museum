## Overview
The castle of al-Shaqif — the Franks' Belfort — above the Litani river in Tiberias's district; 'Izz al-Din Farrukhshah took it in 578 AH, and al-Malik al-Salih Isma'il surrendered it to the Franks in 638 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 110-111} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 189}.
