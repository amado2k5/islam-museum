## Summary
A city in the Hijaz to which the Prophet migrated in 622 CE, and where the Prophet's Mosque and the first Muslim community were established.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 24.4686 N and 39.6142 E {cite:geonames-gazetteer}. It was known as "Yathrib" until 622 CE.
