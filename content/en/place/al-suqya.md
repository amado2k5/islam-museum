## Overview
al-Suqya: a place on the pilgrimage road, about twenty-seven miles from al-Abwa. It has an orchard and palm gardens, and in it are people of Tayy and other tribes of the Arabs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records several places of this name. The first Suqya is a large village of the district of al-Far', nineteen miles from it on the al-Juhfa side — twenty-nine miles in al-Khwarizmi's book; Ibn al-Faqih counts it among the lowest wadis of Tihama; Ibn al-Kalbi says Tubba' camped at it thirsty and rain overtook him there, so he named it al-Suqya; al-Khwarizmi calls it a great village near the sea at a day and a night's journey; and in Abu 'Ubayd al-Sukuni's book it is a pool and thick lava tracts below Samira' for those ascending to Mecca, four miles between it and Samira'. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.228} Under the entry 'Anid he puts a wadi between Mecca and Medina one mile before al-Suqya, and al-Suqya itself between Mecca and Medina. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.72} A second Suqya is a village at the gate of Manbij with many orchards and running waters, an endowment on the descendants of Abu 'Ubbada al-Buhtari, which Abu Firas named in verse. A third is a well at Medina from which water was drawn for the Prophet, on the authority of Abu Bakr ibn Musa, and the Medina entry names the Buyut al-Suqya. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.228; 5 p.83} A fourth, Suqya al-Jazal of the land of 'Udhra near Wadi al-Qura, is where the singer Tuways died. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.228} The book also has Rawdat al-Suqya in a verse of Aws ibn Maghra'. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.91}

## The museum's identification
The record, from al-Idrisi (p.142), makes al-Suqya a place on the Hajj road about twenty-seven miles from al-Abwa', with an orchard and palm gardens and people of Tayy and other Arab tribes. The match in Yaqut is the first Suqya between Mecca and Medina (vol 3 p.228, vol 4 p.72); the Manbij village, the Medina well and Suqya al-Jazal are other places that the entries themselves keep apart. The entry read does not name its Tayy people or its palms; the distances are given in different figures — nineteen or twenty-nine miles to al-Juhfa in Yaqut, twenty-seven miles to al-Abwa' in the record — and the museum does not convert one into the other. The record's point (23.45, 39.1) is 38.2 km from its al-Abwa' point. See [[place/al-abwa|al-Abwa']].
