## Overview
al-Suqya: a place on the pilgrimage road, about twenty-seven miles from al-Abwa. It has an orchard and palm gardens, and in it are people of Tayy and other tribes of the Arabs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}
