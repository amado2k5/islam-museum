## Overview
Washjird is a town of good size, with many wealthy people, according to al-Idrisi. Much saffron rises from it, and it is one of the most important grain-producing cities in its region. The al-fuwwa, with which wine is made, is gathered there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}
