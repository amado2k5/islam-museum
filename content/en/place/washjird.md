## Overview
Washjird is a town of good size with many buildings and wide trade. It lies on the route of the Wakhshab river in the lands beyond the Oxus, next to Khuttal, and saffron grows there in quantity, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}

## Details
Al-Idrisi gives one day from Shuman to Afdian, and a light day from Afdian to Washjird, a distance of fifteen miles. Washjird is a town of good size with many buildings and wide trade. It has wealthy residents and travellers, its women are handsome, and it has crafts and sound conditions. Saffron grows in quantity around Shuman and Washjird. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}

From Saghanian the road to Washjird goes by Nunlak, nine miles, and then to Hamwaran, a populous, flourishing town on the western bank of the river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.489}

On the Wakhshab river, near Washjird, a bridge crosses the river under a great mountain. It marks the boundary between Khuttal and Washjird. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.482}
