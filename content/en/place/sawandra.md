## Overview
Sawandra is a town in Sind. Its people asked Muhammad ibn al-Qasim for safe conduct, and he granted it to them, on condition of hosting the Muslims and guiding them. Its people today are Muslims, according to the report. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
