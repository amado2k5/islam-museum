## Overview
Nabranta is a ruined city eight stages north of the site of Saghwa. It was one of the well-known cities. Then sand overwhelmed its houses and its wells dried up, so that only the remains of a people were left in it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.111} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.112}
