## Overview
Nabranta is a ruined city lying to the north, eight stages from the place of the Saghwa clan, and was formerly among the famous cities. Al-Idrisi reports that sand overcame its dwellings until they fell into ruin, and overcame its days until they dried up, and that few inhabitants are left. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 111}

## The neighbouring clan
The Saghwa are a clan of the Zaghawa, a nomadic folk with camels, plentiful in milk and fine in their young; they weave coarse cloth and the tents they shelter in from the hair, use the milk and ghee, and live on the meat. Vegetables are few among them. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 111}

## The land
What the Zaghawa grow is mostly sorghum, and wheat is sometimes brought to them from the land of Warqlan and elsewhere. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 111} At the foot of their mountain are springs that run a short way and then stop, and the people of the district avoid a cave in this mountain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 111}
