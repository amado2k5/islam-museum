## Overview
Abarqawayh: a strong and fertile town with many trees. Al-Idrisi describes it as having a wall of earth, most of its buildings of mud brick, and cheap prices. It lies one hundred and twenty-three miles from Kutha. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.429}
