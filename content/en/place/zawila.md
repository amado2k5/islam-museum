## Overview
Zawila is one of the cities of the Maghrib, between Barqa and Fezzan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Uqba ibn Nafi' al-Fihri reached it in his first governorship of the Maghrib, appointed by Amr ibn al-As. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 314-315}

## What Uqba imposed on its people
Uqba imposed on its people, and on those between it and Barqa, what he saw they could bear. For the detail, see [[event/uqba-zawila-appointment|The appointment of Uqba and Zawila]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 314-315}
