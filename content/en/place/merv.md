## Overview
Merv is an ancient city in present-day Turkmenistan, which was the capital of Khurasan. The Abbasid call (da'wa) set out from it, and al-Ma'mun stayed there before moving to Baghdad. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

Al-Maqdisi lists among its features the great melon of Merv, which has no equal in the regions of Persia. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.247}
