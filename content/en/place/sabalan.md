## Overview
Sabalan is a great mountain in Azerbaijan, inhabited by the Kurds of Sabalan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that the peace treaty of Hudhayfa stipulated that the Kurds of al-Balasajan, Sabalan and Satrudhan should not be molested. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name with fathas on the first two letters and a final nun, and calls it a great mountain overlooking the city of Ardabil in Azerbaijan. He says it has several villages and many shrines of the righteous, snow on its summit in summer and winter, and that the people of the region hold it to be one of the landmarks of the righteous and a blessed place of visitation. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.186}
