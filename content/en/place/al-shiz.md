## Overview
al-Shiz: a town in Azerbaijan, in which is the greatest fire-temple, Adharbadh Kushnasp. Its people were among the fighters of the governor of Ardabil. The peace of Hudhayfa granted them specially that they should not be prevented from their fire-temple. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
