## Overview
al-Najraniyya: a village in the district of [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. The Christians of Najran and those who followed them from its Jews settled in it when Umar ibn al-Khattab expelled them from Yemen in his caliphate, and it was named after them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.89-90}

## Collecting the garments
The lord of al-Najraniyya used to send his envoys to those of them who had scattered in al-Sham and the surrounding districts. They collected money from them, which he divided among them to provide the garments imposed on them. They complained of their dispersal, the deaths of those who died, and the conversions of those who converted, until Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz found them at a tenth of their original number. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.89-90}
