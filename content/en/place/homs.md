## Overview
Homs is a city in Syria on the Orontes river, one of the districts of [[place/damascus|Damascus]] according to the entity data. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

After the conquest of Damascus, its people made a peace agreement with Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah for safety for their persons, property, city wall, churches and mills. A quarter of the Church of John was excepted for the mosque, in return for one hundred and seventy thousand dinars. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When Heraclius gathered his army for Yarmuk, the Muslims returned to its people the tribute they had taken from them. Its people chose to be governed by the Muslims, and they shut its gates against the army of Heraclius; its Jews did the same. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then it and Qinnasrin became one military district. Marwan ibn Muhammad demolished its wall, because they had disagreed with him. Al-Mu'tasim ordered its stone paving removed in the days of al-Fadl ibn Qarin, then its people rebelled, killed him and crucified him. Musa ibn Bugha al-Kabir took it by force in the year 250 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
