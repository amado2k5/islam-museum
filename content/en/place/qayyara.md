## Overview
Qayyara is a town on the river Tigris, south of Mosul. Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his journey. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

The coordinates come from GeoNames, for a modern town of the same name. Matching them to the ancient site needs an additional source.
