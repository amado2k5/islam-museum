## Overview
Patras is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place and an archaeological site, within the regional unit of Achaea. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2307594}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2307594 with the preferred name «Patras», classed as a populated place and an archaeological site; its parent in the hierarchy is the regional unit of Achaea. Among the names it records are «Patrae», «Πάτρα», «Patrás», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 255683. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2307594}
