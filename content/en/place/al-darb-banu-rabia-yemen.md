## Overview
Al-Darb is a place of Banu Rabi'a, "strong and mighty" in the book's words, where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] camped after [[place/karawi-banu-rabia-yemen|Karawi]] and stayed days until his horses were rested. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} He returned to it after the second battle at [[place/athafit|Athafit]]; it is a place of little crops and little water, with a ghayl whose water increased, by the account of the people of the place. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.104} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.105} The text gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-al-darb-and-abu-al-qasim-joins-285]] and [[event/end-of-the-battle-and-the-camp-at-al-darb-285]].
