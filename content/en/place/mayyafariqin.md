## Overview
Mayyafariqin is a large, fine and fertile city at the foot of a mountain. Broad cloths, sabaniyyat and the well-known takak are made there. According to al-Idrisi it lies two stages from Amid. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.663}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut calls Mayyafariqin the best-known city of Diyar Bakr. He gives two accounts of its name: that the first to build it was a woman called Maya, and that Farqin means dispute in Persian; the Persians called it Barjin because its moat was dug well. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 pp.235-236}

He records that the parts built of stone were the work of Anushirvan ibn Qubadh, and the parts built of brick were the work of Abarwiz. Ptolemy, he says, placed it in the fifth climate, at 74 degrees and 40 minutes of longitude and 37 degrees and 30 minutes of latitude. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 pp.235-236}
