## Overview
Mayyafariqin is a large, fine and fertile city at the foot of a mountain. Broad cloths, sabaniyyat and the well-known takak are made there. According to al-Idrisi it lies two stages from Amid. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.663}
