## Overview
Hormuz is a large coastal city of the cities of Kirman. Cumin and sesame grow in abundance around it, and they are shipped from it to the cities. It lies on a gulf called al-Jir, which runs from the sea up to it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.436}
