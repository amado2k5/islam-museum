## Overview
Mount Ghazwan is a mountain on whose back are the dwellings of the Banu Sa'd, and in which are a number of the tribes of Hudhayl. Al-Idrisi says its summit is among the coldest places, so cold that water sometimes freezes on it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.145}
