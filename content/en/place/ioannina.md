## Overview
Ioannina is recorded in two gazetteers, Pleiades (as a late-antique settlement) and the German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (as a populated place), which agree on a position at about 39.67° N, 20.86° E. {cite:pleiades 530900} {cite:idai-gazetteer 2070205}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ioannina» (Pleiades no. 530900), classed as a settlement, with a location attested from 300 CE to 640 CE. {cite:pleiades 530900} Recorded names include «Yanya» [Ottoman Turkish, 1500 CE – 1599 CE]. {cite:pleiades 530900} Pleiades describes it as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 54, grid C2. {cite:pleiades 530900}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2070205) records this place under the preferred name «Ioannina», typed as a populated place, with the further names «Yanya» [Turkish], «Giannina» [Italian], «Ιωάννινα» [Greek]. Its parent unit is «Ioannina (Regionalbezirk)», an administrative unit. The record carries the identifiers GeoNames 261779 and GND 4197925-4. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2070205}
