## Overview
al-Kalbaniyya: a place in al-Ahwaz. Siyah al-Asawari and his army stopped there after Yazdajird sent him from Istakhr towards Sus, while Abu Musa was besieging it. At this place the Asawira broke away from the army of the Persians. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
