## Overview
The well of Rumah: an ancient well at al-Aqiq that had become blocked. Some men of Muzayna of the Ansar formed an alliance and repaired it, and it was named after Rumah, a woman of theirs who used to draw water from it for the people. The Prophet passed by it and saw a man of Muzayna drawing water from it for a wage, and he said words to the effect that it is a charity for the believer. [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] bought it for four hundred dinars and gave it in charity. Then the Prophet passed by it again and asked about it; he was told its story, and he prayed for Uthman to enter Paradise. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.536}

## Bi'r Ruma in the history of sabils
[[person/muhammad-hamza-ismail-al-haddad|Al-Haddad]] opens his history of sabils with the well of Ruma at al-Aqiq, citing Ibn Shabba that Uthman ibn Affan bought it and the Prophet told him to make it a siqaya for the Muslims, with its reward his. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.14} Another report says he bought it as caliph for thirty thousand dirhams, or forty thousand, when people drank from it only for a price, and gave it to rich and poor and wayfarer alike; and that when he was besieged in his house in 35 AH he asked to drink from it and was refused. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.15} In his conclusion the author treats it as the origin of endowing water in the way of God. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.49}
