## Overview
al-Munakkab: a fine town of medium size, with many fish-traps. al-Idrisi mentions in it a square building standing like an idol, and a large basin into which water comes from about a mile away. It lies forty miles from [[place/granada|Granada]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.564}
