## Summary
A city in western Iran, where Ibn Sina served as vizier and where he died in 1037 CE.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 34.7983 north and 48.5148 east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## What this page covers
This page gathers what the museum links to the place: events, people and buildings. It is not a full historical description of the place. The description and history of the place, from the original sources, will be added in the next batches.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ecbatana/Hagmatana/Achmeta/Epiphaneia/Ahmadan» (Pleiades no. 903021), classed as a settlement and an archaeological site, with activity attested from 1200 BCE to 2100 CE. It describes the place as the capital of Astyages, captured by Cyrus the Great in 549 BCE, and a summer residence of Persian and Parthian kings. {cite:pleiades 903021}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2043471, typed as populated place, with the preferred name «Hamadan» under «Hamadān (Ostan)» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including Hamedān, Хамадан, همدان. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2043471}
