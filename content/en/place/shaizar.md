## Overview
Shaizar is an ancient settlement on the Orontes river, south-east of Apamea, which scholars identify with modern Shaizar in Syria, according to the Pleiades gazetteer. {cite:pleiades 668292}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Larissa/Sizara» (Pleiades no. 668292), classed as a settlement and a modern settlement, with a time span from 1344 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «Larissa» and «Sizara» in Greek and «شيزر» in Arabic. It describes the place as an ancient settlement on the Orontes river to the south-east of Apamea, which scholars identify with modern Shaizar in Syria. {cite:pleiades 668292}
