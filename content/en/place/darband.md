## Summary
Darband is a small but civilized city that al-Idrisi mentions on the road to the land of al-Rasht, one stage from the city of Washjird. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that whoever wishes to reach the land of al-Rasht turns slightly eastward and travels from the city of Washjird to the city of Darband in one stage; it is a small but civilized city with markets, buildings and prosperous people {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}. From Darband to the city of Jarkan is one stage, a city close to Darband in size with the same craftsmen, labourers and trade; and from Jarkan to the city of al-Qal'a is one stage, a populous and impregnable city at the far end of al-Rashist on the side of al-Arak, set on a high mountain whose people keep guard against al-Arak {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}. He adds that al-Rasht is the farthest point of Khurasan {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}. Darband is thus a flourishing halt on the road to the far borders of Khurasan, lying between Washjird and Jarkan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}
