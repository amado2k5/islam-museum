## Overview
Sirwah was the capital of the Mukarribs of [[polity/kingdom-of-saba|Saba]] in the centuries before 650 BCE, by the estimate of al-Rahiq al-Makhtum. Its ruins lie a day's journey to the west of [[place/marib|Ma'rib]], and are known as Khuraybah. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.23} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 4/195-198}.

## The dam
In the time of the Mukarribs, construction began on the dam known as [[building/marib-dam|the Dam of Ma'rib]], which has a great place in the history of [[place/yemen|Yemen]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.23} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24} Then the kings of Saba gave up the title "Mukarrib" and took Ma'rib as their capital instead of Sirwah. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24}

## For young visitors
Sirwah was the first capital of the kingdom of Saba in Yemen, before Ma'rib, and old ruins of it remain.
