## Summary
Masyunus is a large, populous city of ancient foundation in the land of Maqduniyya (Macedonia), five stages north-east of the city of Lafisa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Masyunus among the towns of the land of Maqduniyya in the sixth climate, together with Nisu, Atrubi, Nuqastru, Bidani, Banuwi, Lafisa, Afranisfa and Aghrizinus {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. He says: from the city of Lafisa, in the direction of north with east, to the city of Masyunus are five stages, and it is a large, populous city of ancient foundation {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. Lafisa stands on a mountain back, and facing it to the south is the city of Aqrunus with four stages between them {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. From Lafisa also to the city of Bidani are five stages; it is a large city between west and north on the river Danu {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. From Buliya Bismusqus to the city of Bizawi are seventy miles; Bizawi is a large, populous city of many markets whose people are well supplied {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}.
