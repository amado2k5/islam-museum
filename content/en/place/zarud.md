## Overview
Zarud and Ramal Zarud are two places on the Iraqi road, where the lava-field (al-harra) ends, on the left side of the road. Al-Hamdani mentions them in his description of the stations on this road. {cite:hamdani-akwa-hawali p. 265}

An additional source is needed: the location of the two names is taken from a single sentence, and no coordinates have been recorded.
