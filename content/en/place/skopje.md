## Overview
Skopje is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «Skopje (Region)». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2096277}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2096277 with the preferred name «Skopje», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Skopje (Region)». Among the names it records are «Scupi», «Üsküp», «سكوبيه», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 785842 and to Pleiades no. 481987. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2096277}
