## Overview
Akhisar is a city in Manisa Province, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, with the name «Thyatira» among its names. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283106}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2283106 with the preferred name «Akhisar» (German), classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Manisa Province. Among the names it records are «Thyatira», «Axari», «Azarion» and «Aq Hisar», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 324698. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283106}
