## Overview
Jayshan is a place in Yemen. In the account of the fight at Sana'a the narrator says the army with Ibrahim ibn Khalaf and Abu Ziyad was made up of men who had been plundering Jayshan and other places {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.208}. Its people then met [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], who entered and stayed three days, told them to fear God, sent [[person/ali-ibn-dirr-governor-of-aden-288|Ali ibn Dirr]] from it to [[place/aden|Aden]] and left [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-razi-of-jayshan|Abu Abd Allah al-Razi]] in it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. The book gives no coordinates for it and the museum does not guess them. See [[event/al-hadi-jails-the-jaafir-at-mankath-and-sends-governors-from-jayshan-288|the jailing of the Ja'afir and the governors sent from Jayshan]].
