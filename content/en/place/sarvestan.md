## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Sarwistan is a district of Fars between Shiraz and Fasa, with villages, orchards and farmland. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.217}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut fixes the vowelling with a kasra on the waw, calls the place a country of Fars containing villages, orchards and farmland, and puts it between Shiraz and Fasa. That is the whole entry: it holds no history, no people and no buildings, but it shows that the name was known on the road between those two cities in Yaqut's time. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.217}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record lists Sarvestan as a city in Fars Province, Iran, which agrees in general with Yaqut's placing of it between Shiraz and Fasa. Yaqut adds nothing beyond that.
