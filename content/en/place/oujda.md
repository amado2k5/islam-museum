## Overview
Oujda is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Morocco. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2765527}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2765527 with the preferred name «Oujda», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Morocco. Among the names it records are «Wadżda», «Ucda» and «وجدة», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 2540483. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2765527}
