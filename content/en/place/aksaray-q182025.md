## Overview
Aksaray is a city in Turkey, which the gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute places in Aksaray Province. The open sources read for this page give no historical account of it, so the page keeps to what the gazetteers record.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2101512 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Aksaray». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Aksaray (Provinz)». It records names in German, English, French, Polish, Spanish, Turkish, Russian, Greek and Chinese, and in Arabic the form «آق سراي», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 324496, to the Arachne identifiers 6128 and 1208222 and to the German National Library authority record 4206312-7. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2101512}
