## Summary
Dazza was a small but strong fortress on the road of the land of Fars between Isfahan and Kashan; the name also occurs among the towns and routes of Sijistan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.452}

## Details
On the road to the city of Rayy from Isfahan, al-Idrisi says that from the ribat of Abu Ali ibn Rustam to the fortress of Dazza is one stage; it is a strong though small fortress, and from the fortress of Dazza to Kashan is two stages through wastes and abandoned ruins, Kashan being a city {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.452}. From the city of Kashan to the village of the Magians is a stage, and from there to the city of Qum a stage {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.452}. In the land of Sijistan the author counts among its famous towns Zaranj, al-Taq, al-Qarnayn, Khwash, Sarwan, Bust, al-Zaliqan, Banjway, Isfinjay, Siwi, Bashlink, Baghnin, Jizza, Farrah, Dazza, Dazaq and others {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.428}. In the routes of that region he notes that from Farrah to Hizza is a stage, and from Dazza to Kuwisan to Khashan a stage, the latter belonging to al-Isfazar, the first of the lands of Herat {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.445}. Dazza in his book thus falls on a small strong fortress of the Fars road and on a locality of the Sijistan districts in the itineraries of Khurasan.
