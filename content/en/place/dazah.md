## Overview
A city and fortress on a hill, with a pulpit, buildings, farms and running water. It lies one stage from [[place/dayr-al-jis|Dayr al-Jiss]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.452}
