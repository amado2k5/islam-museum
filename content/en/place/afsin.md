## Overview
Afsin: a small town which al-Idrisi says is the town of the People of the Cave. Near it is a cave in a mountain with a climb from the surface of the earth like steps, in which dead people lie on their left sides. It lies between Amorium and Nicaea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.802}
