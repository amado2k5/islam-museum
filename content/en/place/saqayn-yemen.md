## Overview
Saqayn is a place where [[person/husayn-ibn-al-hasan-governor-of-saqayn|Husayn ibn al-Hasan]] was governor for al-Hadi when [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] passed it on his way to [[place/saada|Sa'da]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116} The editor's footnote refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab, p. 69 {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116}; that book was not read here, so no coordinates are given. See [[event/ali-ibn-muhammad-reaches-saada-and-the-tabari-emigrants-285]].
