## Overview
Lankabalus is a large, populous island, ten days from Serendib. Al-Idrisi describes its people as black-skinned and naked. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.77-78}

Merchants go to them in small boats, and buy ambergris and coconut in exchange for iron. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.77-78}
