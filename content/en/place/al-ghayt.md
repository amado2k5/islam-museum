## Overview
al-Ghayt: a good harbour, sheltered somewhat from the winds. al-Idrisi reports that wheat and barley are exported from it, and that cheap goods reach it from the Masmuda tribe. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.241}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
