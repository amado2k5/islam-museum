## Summary
Basitirinis was a fine city of abundant waters, peopled courtyards and sound size, lying half a day east of Karnabi, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that from the town of Karnabi — a place near a mountain — eastward to the city of Basitirinis is half a day, and that it is a fine city with many waters, inhabited open spaces and a sound extent {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}. From Basitirinis eastward to the city of Rusu Qastru is another half day — a city of good size with prosperous districts, ample goods and cheap prices, its site in level ground {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}. Basitirinis thus lay in his scheme on a road of continuous half-day stages between Karnabi and Rusu Qastru to the east; its site was marked by plentiful water, peopled courtyards and a sound extent, making it one of the well-proportioned pleasant cities of that region.
