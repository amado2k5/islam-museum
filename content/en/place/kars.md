## Overview
Kars is a city in Turkey; the museum's record places it at about 40.6 degrees north and 43.1 degrees east. All that was read about it here is one line in Lothrop Stoddard's Hadir al-Alam al-Islami, in the Arabic of Ajjaj Nuwayhid with the commentary of Shakib Arslan, a survey of the Muslim world in the 1920s.

## In Hadir al-Alam al-Islami
In its account of the losses the Ottoman state suffered in the nineteenth century, the book says that after the war of 1877 and 1878 with Russia and the Treaty of Berlin the Sublime Porte gave up to Russia in Asia Batum, Kars and Ardahan {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923 vol. 3 scan p.325 (archive.org item hadir.al.aalm.al.islami.03)}. This connects to the records [[place/ardahan|Ardahan]], [[place/ottoman-empire|the Ottoman Empire]] and [[place/russia|Russia]].
