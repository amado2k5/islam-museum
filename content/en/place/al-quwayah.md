## Overview
al-Quwayah: a populous harbour on the coast of the Qulzum (the Red Sea). al-Idrisi reports that its water comes to it from a distant place, and that it is carried to it by the road of al-Juhfa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}
