## Overview
Ansina: an ancient city on the east bank of the Nile, with gardens and many fruits. al-Idrisi reports that it is known as the city of the sorcerers, and that Pharaoh brought them from it for the appointed day of their meeting with [[person/musa|Musa]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}
