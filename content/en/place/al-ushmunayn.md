## Overview
al-Ushmunayn: a small, populous, well-kept town with gardens, palm trees and crops, known for the cloths that are made in it. al-Idrisi reports that it lies about half a day or a little more from Munyat Ibn al-Khasib. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 124} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 125}
