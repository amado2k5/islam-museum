## Overview
Jayy is the city of [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]]. Al-Baladhuri reports that Abd Allah ibn Budayl conquered it by a peace agreement after fighting, on tax and poll tax, with safety for lives and property, except for the weapons in people's hands. Fadusfan handed it to him after a duel with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
