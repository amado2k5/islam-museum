## Overview
Arthal is a place where Jaftam stayed six days, held there, they not allowing him to go to Sana'a; on the seventh day they went out to him in battle order and seized him, his son and his nephew {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.253} ([[event/jaftam-is-seized-at-arthal-and-jailed-at-bayt-bus-290]]). The book does not give its location; the OCR and the page image agree on the reading, and the museum links it to no other place.
