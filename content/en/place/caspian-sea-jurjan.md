## Overview
The Sea of Jurjan and Daylam, which is the Caspian Sea: a closed sea that does not join other seas, into which many rivers and springs flow. It is bounded on the east by the Ghuzz, on the north by the Khazars, on the west by Azerbaijan and Daylam, and on the south by Tabaristan. Its length is about a thousand miles and its width about six hundred and fifty miles, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.12}
