## Overview
Rukhkhaj is a region of Sijistan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura prevailed by way of Rukhkhaj over the land between it and the land of al-Dawar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
