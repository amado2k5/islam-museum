## Overview
al-Sunh: a quarter on the upper side of [[place/medina|Medina]], named after the fortified tower "al-Sunh" that the Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj built. It is one of the quarters of Medina, as Yaqut has it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.210-211}

The house of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] was in it when he married Mulayka, or Habiba, the daughter of Kharija ibn Zayd. It lies about a mile from the house of the Prophet. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.210-211}
