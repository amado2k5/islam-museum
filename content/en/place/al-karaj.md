## Overview
al-Karaj: a city in the mountains. al-Baladhuri reports that Isa ibn Idris settled there, prevailed over it and built its fortress. The position of Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa grew stronger, and his standing with the ruler became great, so the fortress was enlarged and al-Karaj was developed. It came to be called "Karaj of Abu Dulaf", and it is one of the cities of the land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
