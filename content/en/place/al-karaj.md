## Overview
al-Karaj: a city in the mountains. al-Baladhuri reports that Isa ibn Idris settled there, prevailed over it and built its fortress. The position of Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa grew stronger, and his standing with the ruler became great, so the fortress was enlarged and al-Karaj was developed. It came to be called "Karaj of Abu Dulaf", and it is one of the cities of the land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes al-Karaj as a city between Hamadhan and Isfahan, halfway along the road and nearer Hamadhan, with a district attached to it. He says the first to make it a city was Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa al-Ijli, who took it for his home, and that poets sought him there and mentioned it in their verse. Among those named after Karaj of Abu Dulaf he lists the qadi Abu Sa'd Sulayman ibn Muhammad al-Qassari, known as al-Kafi al-Karaji, a jurist who served as judge at Karaj and died in 538. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.446}

He gives the distances: ten farsakhs from Burujird to Karaj, twelve from Karaj to al-Burj, ten from al-Burj to Nubanjan, thirty from Nubanjan to Isfahan, and about thirty farsakhs between Karaj and Hamadhan. He describes it as a scattered city without the compactness of a town, its buildings those of kings, wide palaces set apart, with crops and livestock; it had no gardens or pleasure grounds, its fruit came from Burujird and elsewhere, and its buildings were of mud. It was about a farsakh long, with two markets at the gate of the congregational mosque and another with open ground between. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.446}

## Other places of the name
Yaqut lists other places of the same name in the same entry: a Karaj that is a village of Rayy; another al-Karaj, the largest town in the district of Rudhrawar near Hamadhan, between Hamadhan and Nihavand and seven farsakhs from each; and al-Karaj as a Christian people who lived in the Caucasus mountains and the land of al-Sarir and took the city of Tiflis. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.446}

## The museum's identification
This record is Karaj of Abu Dulaf in the Jibal, Yaqut's first place of the name and the one al-Baladhuri describes, where Isa ibn Idris is said to have built the fortress and Abu Dulaf enlarged it. Yaqut makes Abu Dulaf the first to turn it into a city, so the two reports complement each other without a plain conflict. The Karaj of Rudhrawar, the Karaj of Rayy and the Karaj people of the Caucasus are other places and are not what this record means. The record's coordinates are approximate and come from the geocoding pass, not from Yaqut.
