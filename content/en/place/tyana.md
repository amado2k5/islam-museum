## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan al-Tuwana is a town in the frontier lands of al-Massisa, mentioned in verse and reports from the wars with the Byzantines, and with the report that al-Ma'mun ordered it walled as a city but died before it was done. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.45}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the vowelling with a damma on the ta and a nun after the alif, and says it is a land in the thughur of al-Massisa. He quotes a line of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya that mentions the day of al-Tuwana and says he does not care what their forces met there of fever and illness. He then gives Ptolemy's position of the city in degrees in the fifth clime, with astrological details that Yaqut does not explain. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.45} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.46}

## The wall and the verse
Yaqut reports that when al-Ma'mun came to the frontier on campaign he ordered a wall about a mile by a mile around al-Tuwana and designated it a city, and prepared men and money; he died soon after beginning, and al-Mu'tasim cancelled the work. He quotes verses of Adi ibn al-Riqa praising a ruler for a matter he had made firm by God's leave. On the authority of al-Zubayr he reports that Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik, campaigning at Constantinople, wrote to his brother al-Walid in verse that mentions the desert of al-Tuwana, and he gives the verses of al-Qa'qa ibn Khalid al-Absi that describe the hunger around al-Tuwana, when the men ate horseflesh. The verse is described here, not quoted. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.46}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record calls Tyana an ancient city of Cappadocia in modern Turkey. Linking it to the Arabic name al-Tuwana is the museum's reading. Yaqut does not say that al-Tuwana is Tyana; he places it only in the frontier lands of al-Massisa, which does not contradict the link but does not confirm it.
