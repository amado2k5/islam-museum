## Overview
Karawi is a place of Banu Rabi'a where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] had his camp after the first day of the war at [[place/athafit|Athafit]]; water was scarce, and when it fell he moved to [[place/al-darb-banu-rabia-yemen|al-Darb]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} Re-reading with Apple Vision confirmed the spelling of the name. The text gives no coordinates. See [[event/first-day-of-the-battle-of-athafit-285]].
