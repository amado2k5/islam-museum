## Overview
Ashkala: a small, civilised town in the land of al-Lanniya, near the sea, according to al-Idrisi, who mentions its distance from [[place/ashkshiya|Ashkshiya]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}
