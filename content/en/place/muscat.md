## Overview
Muscat is the capital of the Sultanate of [[place/oman|Oman]], on the Gulf of Oman, and an ancient commercial port. Oman is a centre of the Ibadi school. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut notes that Muscat is the name of several places. The one known to us is a city in the Oman region, at the far end of its borders facing Yemen, on the coast. He also lists Maskat al-Raml on the Basra road, and a district of the same name on the Caspian shore beyond the Gate of Gates, which he says Khusraw Anushirwan founded when he built that gate. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.127}
