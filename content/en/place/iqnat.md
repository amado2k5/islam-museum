## Overview
Iqnat is a town that is neither large nor populous, and most of it is in ruins. Its few people live on maize, barley, fish and meat, according to al-Idrisi. It lies four days' journey by land from Manqunah. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.45}
