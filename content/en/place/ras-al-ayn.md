## Overview
Ras al-Ayn, which is the Spring of the Rose, is one of the cities of al-Jazira on the Khabur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} It is part of [[place/jazira|al-Jazira]]. See [[event/conquest-of-ras-al-ayn|The conquest of Ras al-Ayn]].

## The conquest
The city held out against Iyad ibn Ghanm. [[person/umayr-ibn-sad|Umayr ibn Sa'd]] conquered it by force after fierce fighting with catapults (aradat). Then he made peace with its people on the terms that the land was theirs, with the poll-tax (jizya) on heads at four dinars. Their women and children were not taken captive, and he called out to them, "No harm, come to me, come to me," as a guarantee of safety. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
A number of its people departed, and the Muslims worked their lands and farmed them through land grants. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
