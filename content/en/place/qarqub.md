## Overview
A city where a particular gold-threaded brocade is made, which is carried from it to all horizons and known as qarqubi brocade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.396}

It lies four stages from al-Sus, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.396}
