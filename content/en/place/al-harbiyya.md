## Overview
al-Harbiyya: a district of [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. al-Baladhuri reports that it was named after Harb ibn Abd Allah al-Balkhi, who was in charge of the police of Jafar ibn al-Mansur at Mosul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 pp.414-417}
