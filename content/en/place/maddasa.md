## Overview
Maddasa is a mid-sized, densely built city on the Nile, and its water is drawn from the river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.25}

## Conditions
Rice and maize are abundant there. Its people trade in gold dust and catch fish. It lies six days' journey from Tirqa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.25}
