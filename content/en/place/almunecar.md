## Overview
Al-Munakkab: one of the fortresses of the coast, east of Malaga, in [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. Sultan Abu al-Hasan, the ruler of Granada, went down to it after he had deposed himself in favour of his brother al-Zaghal, and he stayed in it until he died. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 519}
