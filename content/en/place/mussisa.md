## Overview
Mussisa (al-Masisa) is a city on the river Jayhan. Between it and the city of al-Mullawan there is a stone bridge, and its name in Greek is Mamistra. Around it are continuous gardens and crops, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.646}
