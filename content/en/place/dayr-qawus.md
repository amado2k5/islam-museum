## Overview
A place on the Tigris four farsakhs above al-Ubulla. al-Baladhuri reports that the people of [[place/basra|Basra]] used to drink from it before the digging of the river al-Ubulla. It flows through salt marshes with no buildings on its banks, and there is a story told about it that spirits dwell in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
