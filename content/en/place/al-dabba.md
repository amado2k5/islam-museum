## Overview
al-Dabba: a site on the road to Badr, between Dhu Afran and Badr. The army of Medina took this road on its way to Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.210}

No coordinates are recorded for it yet. An additional source is needed.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name al-Dabba with a fatha on the first letter and a light second letter, and says it is a place between al-Asafir and Badr, along which the Prophet travelled when he marched to Badr, citing Ibn Ishaq; he adds that Ibn al-Furat fixed the spelling in several places. He notes that some placed it between al-Rawha and al-Safra. He reports Nasr's remark that the hadith transmitters say it with a light second letter, while the correct form is al-Dabba with a doubled letter, meaning a gathering of sand, and that other place names of the type Dabab and Dabbab exist. For his own part he adds al-Jawhari's meanings: a vessel in which oil is kept, a mound of sand, and, with a damma, a road. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.438}
