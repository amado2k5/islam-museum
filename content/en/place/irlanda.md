## Overview
Ireland is a very large island. Al-Idrisi reports, from the author of the book of wonders, that it had three cities where ships would land, and that its people were wiped out in internal wars. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.947} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.948}
