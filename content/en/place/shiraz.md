## Overview
Shiraz is a city in southern Iran, birthplace of the two poets Sa'di and Hafiz, and a centre of learning and literature. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} It is located at approximate coordinates of 29.6103 N and 52.5311 E, according to the same source. An additional source is needed for the city's Islamic history.
