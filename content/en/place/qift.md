## Overview
Qift is a city on the eastern bank of the Nile, half a day from Akhmim. Al-Idrisi describes its people as mixed, and says that they are Shi'a. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.128}

## Agriculture and industry
According to al-Idrisi, vegetable seeds such as turnip and lettuce are grown there, and oil is extracted from them. Soap is made which is carried to the whole land of Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.128}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Coptos» (Pleiades no. 786010), classed as settlement and port and archaeological site, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 640 CE. Its recorded names include Coptos, Qufṭ and Hormucopto. It describes an ancient Nile settlement, modern Quft or Qift in Egypt, that flourished from predynastic times through late antiquity. {cite:pleiades 786010}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute records this place as no. 2277744, of type archaeological site and populated place, with the preferred name «Koptos», placed in its hierarchy under «Genā (Muhāfazat)». Recorded names include Kbt, Kuft, Qifţ and Justinianopolis. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2277744}
