## Overview
Qift is a city on the eastern bank of the Nile, half a day from Akhmim. Al-Idrisi describes its people as mixed, and says that they are Shi'a. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.128}

## Agriculture and industry
According to al-Idrisi, vegetable seeds such as turnip and lettuce are grown there, and oil is extracted from them. Soap is made which is carried to the whole land of Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.128}
