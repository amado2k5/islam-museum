## Overview
Khiwan is a strong fortress with two water basins. It has vineyards whose grapes are large and sweet, from which raisins are made and carried to the neighbouring lands. It is on the road to [[place/saada|Saada]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}
