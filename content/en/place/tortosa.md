## Overview
Tortosa is a fortified city on the slope of a mountain, with a strong wall, according to al-Idrisi. Large ships are built there from the wood of the city's mountains, and pine wood there is not harmed by termites. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.555}
