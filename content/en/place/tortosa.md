## Overview
Tortosa is a well-fortified city on the slope of a mountain, with a strong wall, markets, buildings, craftsmen and workmen. From Mikhnasa to it is two stages, fifty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555}

## Shipbuilding
Large ships are built there from the timber of its mountains, where grows a pine unmatched in length and thickness, from which masts and spars are made. This wood is red, clear-skinned and oily, does not alter quickly and is not attacked by woodworm as other wood is; it is well known and named for the city. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555}

## Setting
From Tortosa to the place where the Nabr (Ebro) meets the sea is twelve miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555} Tortosa is counted in the province of al-Burtat, with Tarragona and Barcelona. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538} In the description of Saragossa, the great river called Ibra comes partly from the land of the Rum and partly from the mountains of Qal'at Ayyub. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 554}
