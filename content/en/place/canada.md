## Summary
Canada is a country in North America. The Oxford Dictionary of Islam has a short entry, Islam in Canada, describing the Muslim community, its institutions and its challenges. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e407}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The entry says that most Canadian Muslims are immigrants who arrived after 1965, that the largest group is of Indo-Pakistani origin, followed by West Asians and North African Arabs, and that the community is mostly young, well educated and professional. Every large Canadian city, it says, has mosques or other public places for worship. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e407}

Among important Canadian Muslim institutions it names the Council of Muslim Communities of Canada, the Canadian Council of Muslim Women, the Ismaili National Council for Canada, the Canadian Muslim Education and Research Institute and the Muslim Research Foundation. The challenges it lists are bias, stereotyping, unity among Muslims, the difficulty of practising Islam in a mainly Christian setting, gender issues, and keeping Islamic cultural and spiritual heritage while becoming part of the Canadian secular mainstream. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e407}
