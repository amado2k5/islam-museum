## Overview
Angers: a large city with many people, having a wide district, vineyards and tilled land. al-Idrisi reports that it is in the province of Nayi, and that it lies seventy miles from Shant Mujyal, which is on the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.861}
