## Overview
A revered house on the top of a mountain in Isfahan, three farsakhs from [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]]. Al-Mas'udi relates that it contained idols which the king Yastasf brought out when he became a Magian, and that he made it his fire temple. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.538-539}

He mentions that it remained revered among the Magians up to his time, and he counts it among the revered houses built after the names of the stars. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.538-539}
