## Summary
Al-Khadra was a small fortified town in the Maghrib on a little river, with continuous buildings, vineyards and a market that drew the people of its district. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.253}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from Bani Wazalfan to al-Khadra is one stage; it is a small fortified town on a little river with connected buildings and vineyards, fine produce of every kind, a market and a bathhouse, and its market gathers the people of that whole district {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.253}. From al-Khadra to the city of Milyana is another stage, an old, well-sited town of rich farmland watered by its own river for gardens and groves {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.253}. Al-Khadra in his telling is a modest place yet furnished with a fort, a market and a bath for its people.
