## Overview
Madsuna is a large city with a congregational mosque, prosperous and densely populated. Al-Idrisi says that its people are Magians who worship fire. It lies three hundred miles from the fortress of Falmus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.954}
