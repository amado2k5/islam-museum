## Overview
Al-Numays is a fort in [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]] that [[group/al-akiliyyun-saada|the Akilis]] and Rabi'a did not think anyone could master, for its difficulty and narrow approaches. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} After [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and his men drove the enemy back to it the enemy left, and al-Hadi and his men entered the village of al-Numays and he ordered it pulled down, and it was pulled down and burned. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} It has no coordinates. See [[event/the-ascent-of-allaf-and-the-fall-of-al-numays-287]].
