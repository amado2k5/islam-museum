## Overview
Hadaqan is a place near [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] that [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached after Kharaqa. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} There [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] ordered the Jafatim and Al Tarif to al-Sirr, and al-Hadi drew up his army in right, left and centre. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} The name is read as printed, with no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-draws-up-his-ranks-at-hadaqan-and-two-men-ask-about-the-odds-288]].
