## Overview
Qastalun is a fortress of high ground, very secure, populous. It lies about twenty miles from al-Randaj, and from it to the village of Musaqqala is twenty miles, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.621}
