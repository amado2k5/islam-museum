## Overview
al-Bahrayn (an oasis): the second populated town with al-Jafar in the oases. It is a village like a fortress surrounded by palm trees, and from al-Bahrayn to al-Jafar are two days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.123}.
