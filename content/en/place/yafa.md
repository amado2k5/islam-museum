## Overview
Jaffa is a city on the shore of the salt sea, about twenty miles from [[place/ramla|Ramla]], and it has a harbour for ships, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.356}

Al-Muqaddasi named it the treasury of Palestine and the port of Ramla. He describes it as small, with a strong fort, its whole sea gate of iron, its congregational mosque overlooking the sea, and its harbour good. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.142}
