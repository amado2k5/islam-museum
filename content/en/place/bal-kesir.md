## Overview
Bal-Kesir is a city in Turkey, written «Balıkesir» in Turkish. The two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum contain no entry for it, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2223108 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Balıkesir». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Balıkesir (Provinz)». It records names in German, English, Turkish and Greek, and in Arabic the form «بالِق أسير», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 322165 and to Pleiades no. 550560. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2223108}
