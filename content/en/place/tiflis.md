## Overview
Tiflis is a town in the district of Jarzan al-Qarmaz, in Armenia. Al-Baladhuri lists it among the first Armenia, with Sisajan and Arran. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273}

When Habib ibn Maslama arrived at Tiflis, he wrote its people a letter of safety and peace, in return for acknowledging their lowly status and paying jizya at one dinar on every house. He made it a condition that they should not gather families of rank together to lighten the jizya, and that they should not be separated to increase it. They were to give counsel to the Muslims and help them against their enemies as far as they could, to host a needy Muslim for a night in a fitting way, and to pass on any Muslim left stranded among them to the nearest group of believers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.283}

Al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami then wrote a letter to the people of Tiflis confirming the safety that Habib had granted them. He made peace with them over their land, their vineyards and their mills, on condition that they pay one hundred dirhams a year for them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.284}

It is part of [[place/armenia|Armenia]].
