## Overview
Tiflis is on the river Kur, in the district of Jarzan al-Qarmaz. Habib ibn Maslama wrote to its people a treaty granting safety for their persons, their sales, their monasteries and their religion, on a dinar per house as jizya, with conditions concerning villages, counsel and delivery. Then al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah confirmed their safety for their mills and vineyards at a hundred dirhams a year. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

It is part of [[place/armenia|Armenia]].
