## Summary
Al-Laniya was a land and an ancient city in the Caucasus country whose founder is unknown; the Alan nation was named after it, and the river Roshio crossed its territory. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says the river Roshio is a great river issuing from behind Mount Qafq, running north to cut through the land of the Alans, with no famous city on it but populated villages and many farms on both banks, navigated by boats {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.914}. He states that from Istibriya to the city of al-Laniya is twenty-four miles, and the Alan peoples were named after this city, a very old foundation whose builder is unknown {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}. From al-Laniya to Khazariyya, the city after which the Khazars are named, is forty-five miles, a large, spacious, water-abundant city on a river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915}.
