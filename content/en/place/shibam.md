## Overview
Shibam is a city in the land of [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]]. Another city lies about a stage from it, and the two stand on the ascending road from [[place/aden|Aden]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.56}

Al-Hamdani derives its name from the people of [[place/shabwa|Shabwa]], who left and settled in Hadramawt when Himyar and Madhhij fought each other. He holds that the original form was "shabah", and then the mim replaced the ha'. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p.70}
