## Summary
Banzart was a fortified town on the sea, smaller than Susa, with amenities and markets; a long day's journey by land from Tunis, with its famous lake to its east, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.288}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that the district of Matfura contains three towns — Ishluna, Tinja and Banzart — and that Banzart is a fortified city on the sea, smaller than the city of Susa; between Tunis and Banzart is a long day by land, and it is small but well-peopled, with amenities and self-standing markets {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.288}. To its east lies the lake known by it and named after it, sixteen miles long by eight wide, its mouth joining the sea; in it are twelve kinds of fish, only one kind appearing in each month {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.288} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.289}. Adjoining it to the south is the lake of Tinja, whose water is sweet while Banzart's lake is salt; each pours into the other for six months and then the flow reverses {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.289}. From Banzart to the city of Tabarqa is seventy miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.289}.
