## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Qannuj, with a fatha on the first letter, a doubled second letter and a final jim, and says, from al-Azhari, that it is a place in the land of India; some said it is a thicket. He says no more. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.409}
