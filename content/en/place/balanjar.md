## Overview
Balanjar: a city in the land of the Khazars, behind the Gate of Gates. Abd al-Rahman ibn Rabi'a raided it in the year 32 AH and was killed before it, and its people took his body and placed it in a coffin, by which they asked for rain. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 503 v.2}.
