## Overview
Balanjar: a city in the land of the Khazars, behind the Gate of Gates. Abd al-Rahman ibn Rabi'a raided it in the year 32 AH and was killed before it, and its people took his body and placed it in a coffin, by which they asked for rain. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 503 v.2}.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Balanjar as a city in the land of the Khazars, behind the Gate of Gates. He says its conqueror was Abd al-Rahman ibn Rabi'a, while al-Baladhuri says it was Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili. The army went past it, and at a place behind Balanjar the Khaqan met it with his host. Abd al-Rahman and his companions, about four thousand men, were killed. The Turks had thought the Muslims were angels on whom weapons had no effect, until a Turk shot Abd al-Rahman with an arrow from a thicket, and the rest of the Turks grew bold and fought until the Muslims fell. The survivors went back by the road through Jilan. Abd al-Rahman ibn Jumana al-Bahili's verses speak of two of their graves, one at Balanjar and one in China, and he means by the second Qutayba ibn Muslim. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 pp.489-490}
