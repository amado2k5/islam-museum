## Overview
Suq Yusuf is a market in al-Hira. Al-Baladhuri attributes it to Yusuf ibn Umar al-Thaqafi, paternal cousin of al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, who was the governor of Hisham over Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.395}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut records Suq Yusuf as a market in Kufa, named for Yusuf ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hakam ibn Abi Aqil al-Thaqafi, and says nothing more under this headword. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.285}
