## Overview
Suq Yusuf is a market in al-Hira. Al-Baladhuri attributes it to Yusuf ibn Umar al-Thaqafi, paternal cousin of al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, who was the governor of Hisham over Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.395}
