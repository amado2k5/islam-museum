## Overview
Dumrunakh is a small town at the foot of a mountain, with a strong fortress. It lies about four stages east of Lalan. Al-Idrisi reports that its people worship an idol on the top of a high mountain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.712}
