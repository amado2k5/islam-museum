## Overview
Ur, written "Ar" in al-Rahiq al-Makhtum, is a town on the western bank of the Euphrates, near [[place/kufa|Kufa]] in the land of [[place/iraq|Iraq]]. From it comes the origin of [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]], the progenitor of the Arabized Arabs. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 1/553-556}

## Excavations
The author mentions that excavations and digs brought broad details about this town, about the family of Ibrahim, and about its religious and social conditions. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} In the chapter on the king in Yemen, he mentions that the mention of the people of [[polity/kingdom-of-saba|Saba]] was found in the excavations of Ur, twenty-five centuries before the common era. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.23}

## Ibrahim's migration
From it Ibrahim migrated to [[place/harran|Harran]], and from there to [[place/palestine|Palestine]], where he made it the base for his call. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 1/108}

## For young visitors
Ur is a very ancient city in Iraq on the Euphrates, where the Prophet Ibrahim was born before he migrated to Palestine.
