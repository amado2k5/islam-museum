## Overview
"Qarqar" is a place where al-Hadi camped with his army "near the village of al-Hajar" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.292}, and in the description of where Banu al-Harith gathered they are said to be "in the village of al-Hajar and at Minas, with Qarqar between them" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.292}.

## Detail
From it parties of the army rode out each day to skirmish with the people of al-Hajar, and to it was brought the girl taken from the load coming from al-Jawf before she was sent back {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.292} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. The book gives no coordinates here and the editor adds no note on it, so no position is guessed; the ledger records the need for a gazetteer.
