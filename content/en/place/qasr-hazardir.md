## Overview
Hazardar is a palace in [[place/basra|Basra]] with many doors. It was built by Shirawayh al-Asawari for Murjana, mother of [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Why it was named
The source gives two accounts of the name. It is said that a thousand doors were made in it. It is also said that a thousand Asawira (cavalrymen) settled the place in a thousand houses which Kisra had assigned to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
