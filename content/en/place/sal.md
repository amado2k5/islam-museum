## Summary
Sala is a city at the far end of the Maghrib, which Yaqut describes as a middle-sized town set on a corner of land where the sea and a river meet. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.231} The sea lies to its north and the river to its west, flowing from the south, and a great river on which ships sail lies nearer to the sea. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.231}

On the western side of this river Abd al-Mu'min laid out a town which he named al-Mahdiyya; he stayed there when he wished to settle a matter or prepare an army, and it lies ten stages from Marrakesh. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.231}
