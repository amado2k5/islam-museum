## Overview
Ush is a city on the bank of a river, which al-Idrisi describes as fine in its location. It has a strong wall joined to the city's wall, and a fine citadel (qahandiz). It lies on the border of Farghana on the side of the land of the Turks. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.508}
