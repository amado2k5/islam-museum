## Overview
Ush is a city on the bank of a river, which al-Idrisi describes as fine in its location. It has a strong wall joined to the city's wall, and a fine citadel (qahandiz). It lies on the border of Farghana on the side of the land of the Turks. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.508}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Ush as a large town in the district of Fergana, near Qaba. It has a wall with four gates and a citadel, and it lies against the mountain on which watchposts were set to guard against the Turks. The land is very fertile. Several scholars took their nisba from it: Umar ibn Musa al-Awshi; the jurist Imran and his brother Mas'ud, sons of Mansur al-Awshi, of whom one died in Dhu al-Hijja 519 AH; and Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Awshi, who settled in Bukhara, visited Baghdad on pilgrimage, and died in Bukhara in 613 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.281}
