## Overview
al-Shaykhan: a place between Medina and the mountain of [[place/uhud|Uhud]], on the eastern road beside al-Harra. It has two fortified towers that were named because an old man and an old woman used to talk there, in one report. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-203}

## The night of Uhud
The Prophet spent the night there on the night before Uhud and reviewed the people. He allowed those whom he saw fit and sent back those whom he saw fit to send back. Abu Sa'id al-Khudri was among those sent back. The houses of Banu Haritha were in its neighbourhood, and in it is the mosque in which he prayed when he set out for Uhud. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.202-203}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Shaykhan as the dual of shaykh and says it is a place at Medina where the camp of the Messenger of God stood on the night he set out to fight the polytheists at Uhud; there the men were reviewed, some let through and others sent back. He quotes Abu Sa'id al-Khudri as saying he was among those turned back at the two Shaykhs on the day of Uhud. He adds another view, that they are two fortified houses named because an old man and an old woman used to talk there. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.380}
