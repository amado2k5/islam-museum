## Overview
Tripoli: a city of the cities of the Maghrib on the sea, nine days from Ifriqiya, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.316}

## The conquest
al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] came down at it in the year 22 AH, was fought, and then conquered it by force. He took there many loads of brocade, which he sold and divided the price among the Muslims. It is reported that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] forbade him to march on Ifriqiya. There is an account of it in [[event/conquest-of-tripoli-libya|the conquest of Tripoli]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.316}
