## Summary
Maqduniyya (Macedonia) is a land in the sixth climate which al-Idrisi says contains such towns as Nisu, Atrubi, Nuqastru, Bidani, Banuwi, Lafisa, Afranisfa, Aghrizinus and Masyunus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}

## Details
Al-Idrisi opens the fourth section of the sixth climate by stating that it comprises the region of Rumaniyya, the land of Maqduniyya and part of the furthest lands of the Rus {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. He says: as for the land of Maqduniyya, among its towns are Nisu, Atrubi, Nuqastru, Bidani, Banuwi, Lafisa, Afranisfa, Aghrizinus and Masyunus {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. Among the towns of Rumaniyya he lists Faruwi, Lighlaghu, Aqrunus, Istinus, Danbili, Framinyak, Almas, Karnabi, Mighali Tharma, Ghuluwi, Bistrans, Afli, Alaksiyubli, Disina, Tamtana, Barans, Akhilu, Ayman, Barfatu, Malisiyya, Barsiklafsa, Mighali Busklafa, Aghramini, Qurbasiyus, Madhinus, Qalimalaya, Buliya Bismusqus, Bizawi, Constantinople, Idrastar, Aylughis, Aymidiya, Aghatublis, Basiliku and Sazubli {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. From the city of Lafisa, north-eastward, to the city of Masyunus are five stages; it is a large, populous city of ancient foundation {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}.
