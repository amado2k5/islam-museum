## Overview
Buhran: a place in the Hijaz in the district of al-Far'. The text reports that it is a mine. The Prophet marched to it in Rabi al-Akhir in the year 3 AH, and then returned to Medina without war. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut reports that al-Waqidi put eight barids between al-Fara' and Medina. He quotes Ibn Ishaq that Buhran is a mine in the Hijaz in the district of al-Fara', which belonged to al-Hajjaj ibn Ilat al-Bahzi. In the expedition of Abdallah ibn Jahsh, a camel that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and Utba ibn Ghazwan were taking turns to ride was lost at this place. Yaqut notes that Ibn al-Furat vocalised the first letter with a fatha here, although elsewhere he gave it a damma, which is the better-known form. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.341}
