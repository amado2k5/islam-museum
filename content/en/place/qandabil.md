## Overview
Qandabil is a land between Makran and Sind. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Mujja'a ibn Sa'r conquered groups of its people, and then Muhammad ibn al-Qasim completed its conquest. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
