## Overview
Astrik: a fine city with prominent buildings on the west bank of the river Rhine, with vineyards, trees and livestock, bordering the land of Ifrizia, according to al-Idrisi. He describes its people as people of pride and endurance. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.873}
