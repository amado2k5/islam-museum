## Overview
Bitlis is, in Yaqut's words, a town in the districts of Armenia near Khilat, with many orchards; its apples were proverbial for their quality, abundance and cheapness and were carried to many countries. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.494} Today it is a city in Turkey, the centre of a province of the same name. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2221292}

[[person/yaqut-al-hamawi|Yaqut]] fixes the spelling letter by letter, with a fatha on the first letter, a sukun on the second, a kasra on the lam, a silent ya and an unpointed sin, and remarks that he knows no other word of this pattern in Arabic apart from «Wahbil», the name of a man of the Nakha', while among non-Arabic names it resembles Tiflis and Tabriz. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.494} He gives its coordinates as longitude 65 degrees and latitude 38 degrees. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.494}

He also reports from Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir that when Iyad ibn Ghanm had finished with the Jazira he entered the mountain pass and reached Bitlis, went past it to Khilat and made terms with its patrikios, and that the lord of Bitlis undertook the land tax and poll tax of Khilat; Iyad then went back to al-Raqqa and on to Homs, where he died. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.494}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2221292 with the preferred name «Bitlis», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2181661. Among the names it records are «Badlis» and «Bitliss», «Bitlis» in Turkish, English, French, German and Polish, and «Битлис» in Russian. It links the place to GeoNames no. 321025, to Pleiades no. 874438 and to a German Authority File (GND) identifier. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2221292}
