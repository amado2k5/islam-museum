## Overview
Kom Sharik is a place on [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]'s road from al-Fustat to Alexandria, where he met the whole Roman force; they fought for three days until the Romans broke. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 107} Ibn Abd al-Hakam says it is the mound called Kom Sharik after [[person/sharik-ibn-sumayy|Sharik ibn Sumayy]], who either caught the Romans there or was driven onto it and held out. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 108} See [[event/battle-of-kom-sharik|the battle of Kom Sharik]].
