## Overview
Susat al-Sin is a famous city in eastern China, with continuous buildings. Chinese porcelain (al-ghada al-sini) is made there, along with the famous embroideries that match Chinese silk work, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.210}
