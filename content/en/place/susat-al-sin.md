## Overview
Susat al-Sin is a famous, well-known city, with much trade and continuous settlement, combining good things; its people are wealthy and their trade blessed and abundant, and their capital is scattered across the horizons and joined with every city. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 210}

## Crafts
Chinese pottery is made there, unequalled for quality among the pottery of China, and there are many tiraz workshops famous for the making of Chinese silk of high value and sound craft. Susat al-Sin stands on the east of the great river Khamdan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 210} Boats go up this river to the city from Khanfu, Khanku and other famous ports of China. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 213}

## Distances
From Susa to the city of Qabtu is fourteen stages, to the city of Siniyyat al-Sin sixteen stages, and to the city of Sa'la eight days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211} Al-Idrisi opens with it the tenth part of the second clime, which takes in from the eastern lands of China Susat al-Sin, Sa'la, Tughma and Siniyyat al-Sin. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 210}
