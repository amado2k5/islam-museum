## Overview
Wadi Nakhla is the valley between Mecca and al-Ta'if where Samura, the tree of al-Uzza, the greatest idol of Quraysh, stood. It is also the place where the Prophet met the jinn of Nakhla.

## The place
Quraysh revered al-Uzza, a tree (samura) in Wadi Nakhla. Khalid ibn al-Walid destroyed it after the conquest of Mecca, on the Prophet's orders {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

In this valley the jinn heard the Qur'an from the Prophet on the night of Nakhla, and the context of Surat al-Jinn was revealed there {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Wadi Nakhla lies between Mecca and al-Ta'if. The tree of al-Uzza stood there, and there the jinn heard the Qur'an from the Prophet at night.
