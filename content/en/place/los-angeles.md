## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Los Angeles as a populated place, under the state of California in its hierarchy. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2091057}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2091057 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Los Angeles». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «California (State)». It records names in Spanish, English («LA»), Russian, Albanian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Greek and Portuguese, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 5368361, to the Arachne identifiers 1516 and 1205264 and to the German National Library authority record 4036361-2. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2091057}
