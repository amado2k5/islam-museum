## Overview
Qarqarat al-Kudr: a water of the Banu Sulaym in Najd, on the eastern trade road between Mecca and al-Sham. The expedition against Banu Sulaym took place there in the year 2 AH. The Prophet stayed there three days and then returned to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
