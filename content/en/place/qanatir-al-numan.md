## Overview
Qanatir al-Numan are ancient bridges. Al-Baladhuri, quoting Ibn Hisham al-Kalbi, mentions that they are attributed to al-Nu'man ibn Amr ibn Muqarrin al-Muzani, because his army halted there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
