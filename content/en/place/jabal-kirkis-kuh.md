## Overview
Mount Kirkis Kuh is a mountain not large, surrounded by deserts, with something like a courtyard in its middle. It is said that its valleys are far from habitation, and that robbers have their money in them, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.443}
