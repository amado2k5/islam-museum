## Overview
Marseille is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within the Bouches-du-Rhône department. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2080498}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2080498 with the preferred name «Marseille», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the Bouches-du-Rhône department. Among the names it records are «Massilia», «Massalia» and «مارسيليا», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 2995469 and to Pleiades no. 148127. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2080498}
