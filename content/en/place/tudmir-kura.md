## Overview
The district of Tudmir in al-Andalus was named after its lord (alj) Tudmir, and its capital is Orihuela. It was spared the raiding of the Muslims through the management of its lord, so that all of it was made by treaty, with no force, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.300}
