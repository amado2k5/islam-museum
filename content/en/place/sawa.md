## Overview
Sawa is a city in the mountains. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Layla al-Akhyaliyya came to al-Hajjaj, and he gave her a gift, and she asked him to write to his governor in al-Rayy. When she arrived at Sawa she died, and she was buried there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
