## Overview
Hulay is a small town on the coast of the sea. It is a port that ships coming from Yemen and sailing up from Qulzum make for. Goods are brought to it and carried from it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.138}
