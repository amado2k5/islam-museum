## Overview
Al-Ghayl is a village near [[place/saada|Sa'da]], "about half a mile" from it, "a village of Banu Hamza and Banu Sa'd, in which are the Futaymis, who are among his trusted and loving men". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] jailed there those he had put out of Najran in [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]] and charged Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah to look after them in [[event/al-hadi-leaves-saada-for-khiwan-and-the-dry-journey-286]]. The book's index lists "al-Ghayl" at pp. 133, 163, 274, 413 and 484; the book gives no coordinates.
