## Overview
The Palace of al-Qashib is a palace in Ma'rib. Al-Idrisi mentions that it is the palace of Bilqis. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.153}

Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari counts it among the famous monuments of Yemen, together with the dam of Ma'rib. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}
