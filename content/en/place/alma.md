## Summary
Alma was a fortified town of the land of Swaba (Swabia) in the Germans' country; al-Idrisi says it had a strong wall, villages, buildings, vineyards and fruits, and much fertility. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.746}

## Details
Al-Idrisi lists Alma among the towns of Swaba together with Askanja, Akriza and Uzbark, noting that the land of Swaba is bounded by mountains to the south, the land of Babir to the east and the Germans' land to the west. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.745} Descending with the river eastward and northward from Askanja to the town of Alma was sixty miles; from Alma to the town of Bazla in the land of Burgundy one hundred and seventy miles; and from Alma to the town of Uzbark thirty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.746}
