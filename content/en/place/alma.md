## Overview
Alma: a large, civilised city with flourishing markets, lying sixty miles south of Bazla. The author reports that it is one of the cities of Sawaba. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 874} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 875}
