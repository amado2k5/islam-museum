## Overview
Safru is a small settled town with few markets, whose people are mostly farmers with plentiful crops, large herds and flocks, and sweet, abundant water. It is one stage from [[place/fes|Fez]] and two stages from Qal'at Mahdi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243}

## On the road to Sijilmasa
Al-Idrisi gives the road to [[place/sijilmasa|Sijilmasa]] as thirteen stages, passing Safru and Qal'at Mahdi and then the stations beyond, until it cuts through the great mountain to its south and from there reaches Sijilmasa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243} In the road from Sijilmasa to Tlemcen, caravans go from Tlemcen to Fez, then from Fez to Safru, then to Tadla, Aghmat, the Bani Dar'a and Sijilmasa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 249}

## In the list of Maghrib towns
Safru appears in a list of towns of the Maghrib that includes Tlemcen, Tatan, Mughayla, Aqrasif, Wajda, Melilla, Oran, Tahert and Ashir, followed by al-Idrisi's note on the Berber tribes. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 222}
