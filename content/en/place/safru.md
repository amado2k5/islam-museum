## Overview
Safru is a sparsely populated city, most of whose people are farmers, according to al-Idrisi. He mentions that its crops are abundant, its waters sweet and plentiful, and that it lies two stages from Qal'at Mahdi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.243}
