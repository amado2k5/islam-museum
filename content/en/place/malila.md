## Overview
Malila is a fine, medium-sized city with abundant water, from which its people drink. It previously had continuous buildings and farmland. It lies on the sea twelve miles from Kart, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.533}
