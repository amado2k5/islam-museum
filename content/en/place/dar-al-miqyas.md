## Overview
The Nilometer: a large house on the island of al-Rawda where the height of the water of the Nile is measured, with a deep well and a marble column standing in its middle. al-Idrisi describes that the Nile rises in the month of Aghasht, and that when it reaches twenty cubits it is enough to water the land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.325}
