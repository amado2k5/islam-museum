## Overview
Palermo is a city in Sicily. The museum's data states that the Muslims conquered it in 831 CE, and that it was the capital of the emirate of Sicily until the Norman conquest in 1072 CE. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The source of this data is the gazetteer database, which is not enough to document historical events. An additional source is needed.
