## Overview
The Mountain of Shalir is the mountain of snow known today as the Sierra Nevada. Al-Idrisi mentions that its length is two days, and that the snow on it is permanent in winter and summer. From it flows the river Shunayl below [[place/granada|Granada]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.569}
