## Overview
Sufetula, today's Sbeitla, is an archaeological site in Tunisia. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Sufetula» (Pleiades no. 324816), classed as a settlement and an archaeological site, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 1599 CE. It records two names: «Sufetula» from 30 BCE to 640 CE and «Subayṭıla» from 1500 to 1599 CE. Its description says that Sufetula, the modern Sbeitla, was founded in the later first century CE at a major road junction. {cite:pleiades 324816}
