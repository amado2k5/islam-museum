## Overview
Rize is a city on the Black Sea coast of Turkey and the seat of Rize Province, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2238326}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2238326 with the preferred name «Rize», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Rize Province. Among the names it records are «Rizeh», «Rhizus», «Ριζούντα» (Greek) and «ريزه» (Arabic). It links the place to GeoNames no. 740483 and to Pleiades no. 857297. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2238326}
