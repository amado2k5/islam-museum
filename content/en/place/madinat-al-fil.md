## Overview
Madinat al-Fil is the most fortified of the cities of Khwarazm, according to the source. The source quotes Ali ibn Mujahid as saying that it is Samarkand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
The source says that Umayya ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Usayd conquered it, and then its people broke their agreement. Yazid ibn al-Muhallab wanted to take it but could not, and then Qutayba ibn Muslim conquered it. The source quotes Ka'b al-Ashari on this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
