## Overview
Riha is one of the most beautiful places of the Ghawr, together with 'Amta and Baysan, and most of the Ghawr's produce is indigo, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 356}

On the lake of Zughar there are small boats that carry grain and kinds of dates from Zughar and al-Darra to Riha and the other districts of the Ghawr. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 355}

From Riha to Zughar is two days, and from Riha to [[place/jerusalem|Bayt al-Maqdis]] is one stage. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 355}

Riha lies three miles from the Jordan valley. The road from Bayt al-Maqdis to the valley takes one day, and it passes Riha before reaching the valley. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 361}

Al-Idrisi lists the districts of the Jordan province, whose greatest city is Tiberias. Among its districts he names the district of Samaria, which is Nablus, together with Baysan, Riha and Zughar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 377}

Yaqut al-Hamawi describes Arihā as the city of the giants in the Ghawr of the Jordan land in Syria, a day's journey from Bayt al-Maqdis. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 214}

Yaqut also says that the lake of Zughar, which he calls the foul lake, lies west of the Jordan near Arihā. He describes it as a lake from which nothing benefits and in which no living creature arises. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 485}
