## Overview
al-Khalil: a town with a mosque, in which the grave of Ibrahim is mentioned, and with graves facing one another around it. It has dense mountains with trees like the olive. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.363}

## Al-Umari's visit
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari visited it in the year 745 AH. He described its sanctuary and its daily meal, which distributed thousands of loaves to those who came. At the place of the trust's administrator, Nasir al-Din al-Tamimi al-Dari, he saw the Prophetic document concerning the grant of Hebron and Aynun. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.168-176}
