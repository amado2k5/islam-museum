## Overview
Lora is a town on the bank of the great river, with a strong fortress on it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.573}

From it one goes to the village of Sadaf and to other places on the river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.573}
