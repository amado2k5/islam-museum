## Overview
Nicaea is an ancient, well-known city. In earlier times it was the seat of the kingdom of the Romans, before Constantinople was built, when the kingship moved there, according to al-Idrisi. It lies on a lake about twelve miles long, and three miles from the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.805}
