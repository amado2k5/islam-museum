## Overview
Granada is a Spanish city, the capital of the Nasrid kingdom, the last Muslim state in [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]], from the thirteenth century CE until 1492 CE. The Alhambra palace is in it. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
