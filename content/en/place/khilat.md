## Overview
Khilat is a town on Lake Van, which al-Baladhuri counts among the towns of [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. On its road [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] reached Maryala, with the letter of Iyad ibn Ghanm. Iyad had granted him safety for himself, his property and his lands in return for a tribute, and Habib carried this out for him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Khilat as a populous and well-known city, rich in produce and ripe fruit, in the fifth climate, at 64 and a half and a third degrees of longitude and 39 and two-thirds of latitude. It was among the conquests of Iyad ibn Ghanm, who made peace with its people for a tribute. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 pp.380-381}

He says it is the capital of Central Armenia, with abundant fruit and water. Its winter cold is proverbial, and it has a lake without equal in the world, from which the fish known as tarikh is brought to other lands. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 pp.380-381}
