## Overview
Khilat is a town on Lake Van, which al-Baladhuri counts among the towns of [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. On its road [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] reached Maryala, with the letter of Iyad ibn Ghanm. Iyad had granted him safety for himself, his property and his lands in return for a tribute, and Habib carried this out for him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
