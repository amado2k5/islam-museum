## Overview
Al-Jazira al-Khadra is an island joined to the mainland, facing the city of [[place/ceuta|Ceuta]] across the strait. Al-Idrisi says that the sea between it and Ceuta is eight miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.528}
