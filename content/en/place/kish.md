## Overview
Kish is a district of [[place/sijistan|Sistan]] on the side of India. According to al-Baladhuri, [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] prevailed over the land between [[place/zaranj|Zaranj]] and Kish. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
