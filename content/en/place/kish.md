## Summary
Kish was a district of Sijistan on the side toward India; Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura conquered the stretch between Zaranj and Kish and what lay between it and the land of al-Dawar by way of al-Rukhaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.555}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ibn Amir appointed Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura ibn Habib ibn Abd Shams over Sijistan; he came to Zaranj and besieged its marzban in his fortress on a feast day of theirs, and the marzban made peace for two million dirhams and two thousand slaves. Ibn Samura then conquered the land between Zaranj and Kish on the India side and, by the road of al-Rukhaj, the stretch up to the land of al-Dawar; reaching it, he shut its people up in Mount al-Zur and then made terms, so that eight thousand Muslims with him each gained four thousand in spoils, and he entered upon al-Zur, an idol of gold with two ruby eyes, and cut off its hand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.555}
