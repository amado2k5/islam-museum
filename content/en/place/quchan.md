## Overview
Quchan is a city in Razavi Khorasan Province, Iran. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733913}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2733913 with the preferred name «Qučan», classed as a populated place and an archaeological site; its parent in the hierarchy is «Chorāsān-e Razavi (Ostan)». Among the names it records are «قوچان», «Qūchān» and «Quchan» (all Persian). It links the place to GeoNames no. 119115. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733913}
