## Overview
Shant Mariyya is a city of middling size on the coast of the Great Sea, according to al-Idrisi. It has a harbour which the tide enters, and ships arrive at and depart from it. It lies twenty-eight miles from Shalab. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.543}
