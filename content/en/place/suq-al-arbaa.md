## Overview
Suq al-Arbaa is a fine town with a famous market, to which the people of the surrounding regions gather. It has many blessings and shops, and it lies one stage from Askar Mukram, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.398}
