## Overview
Naroa is a splendid halting place and a well-filled station, with busy markets and active crafts, a market on a well-attended day, joined farms and flourishing buildings. It is twelve miles from Karkant (Agrigento), which lies west of it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 610}

## Distances
From Naroa to al-Qata' is ten miles north, and from Naroa to al-Sabuqa twelve miles east. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 610} Al-Sabuqa is a high, populous stronghold with much cultivation and rich produce, and from it to Qal'at al-Nisa is twelve miles on the Karkant road; from Naroa to Qal'at al-Nisa is twenty-one miles between east and north. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 611}

## On the roads
From Karkant to Qarqudhi, eastward, is a hundred and eighteen miles, and from Qarqudhi to Naroa twenty-four miles; between Qal'at al-Nisa and Qarqudhi to the south is fifteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 612}
