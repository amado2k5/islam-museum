## Overview
Unaizah is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as both an administrative unit and a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2770740}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2770740 with the preferred name «Unaiza», classed as both an administrative unit and a populated place; the record gives no parent. Among the names it records are «Unaizah» and «عنيزة». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2770740}
