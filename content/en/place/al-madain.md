## Overview
al-Madain: the city of Kisra on the Tigris, which is Ctesiphon. al-Baladhuri reports that the Muslims besieged Bahurasir for nine or eighteen months. Yazdajird then fled to Hulwan, and the Muslims crossed the Tigris by wading and swimming. They conquered the eastern city in the year 16 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
