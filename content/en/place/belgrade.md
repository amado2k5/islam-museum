## Overview
Belgrade is a fortress city at the confluence of the Sava and the Danube; the museum places it at 44.804 degrees north and 20.465 degrees east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} The museum's records include Mehmed II's siege of it in 1456, which the city withstood, set out on the card [[event/siege-of-belgrade-1456|Siege of Belgrade, 1456]]; later sieges and captures have cards of their own. No source on the city itself was read for this page beyond what those cards cite.
