## Overview
The Fortress of al-Na'ima is a fortress that resembles a small town. Al-Idrisi describes it as fine. Most of its land's plants are carob trees, and carob is shipped from it to the lands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.371}
