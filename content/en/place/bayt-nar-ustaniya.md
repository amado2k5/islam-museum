## Summary
A fire temple near Baghdad, the City of Peace, built by queen Buran daughter of Khusraw Parviz at the place known as Ustaniya, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 554}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi records in Muruj al-Dhahab, in the section listing fire temples, that in the land of Iraq there was a fire temple near the City of Peace built by Buran, daughter of the king Khusraw Parviz, at the place known as Ustaniya. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 554} It stood among the many fire temples the Magians built in Iraq, the land of Fars, Kirman, Sijistan, Khurasan, Tabaristan, the Jibal, Azerbaijan and al-Ran, and in India, Sind and China — most of which al-Mas'udi passes over, mentioning only the famous ones. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 554} Buran daughter of Khusraw Parviz was the Persian queen who ruled after her father, and the fire temple ascribed to her near Baghdad — the City of Peace — shows, in al-Mas'udi's account, the antiquity of the site of Ustaniya and its link to the monuments of the Persian state before Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 554}
