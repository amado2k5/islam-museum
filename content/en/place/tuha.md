## Overview
Tuha is a famous city below al-Ashmunayn, according to al-Idrisi. Wool carpets (sutur) and woollen cloaks attributed to it are made there. He mentions that the crocodile harms the bank opposite al-Ashmunayn, and does not harm the bank of Anasna. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.125}
