## Overview
al-Fayyum: one of the districts of Upper Egypt. Amr ibn al-As sent to it Kharija ibn Hudhafa al-Adawi, together with al-Ashmunayn, Ikhmim, al-Bushrudat and the villages of Upper Egypt. The people of its villages made peace on the same terms as the judgement of Fustat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.304}

It is a site in the account of [[event/four-lieutenants-egypt|the four commanders in Egypt]]. See also [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]].

## Fayyum in al-Maqrizi
Al-Maqrizi says that the site of al-Fayyum was where the Nile's water drained away, and that when Joseph, peace be upon him, took charge of Egypt's affairs, he made it habitable. {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.711}

Citing Ibn Abd al-Hakam, al-Maqrizi reports that once the conquest was complete, Amr ibn al-As sent cavalry raids to the villages around it, and al-Fayyum remained for a year without the Muslims knowing where it was, until a man came and told them of it. {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.733}
