## Overview
Shikla is a fortress which al-Idrisi describes as among the finest fortresses, populous and thriving. In it are gardens bearing every kind of fruit, and a spring known as the Spring of Times (ayn al-awqat). {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.598}
