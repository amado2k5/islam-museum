## Summary
Al-Saylahin was the place where the Muslims spent the night after al-Muthanna ibn Haritha's raid on the market of Baghdad, before they rejoined Khalid at [[place/al-anbar|al-Anbar]], as al-Baladhuri reports. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Khalid ibn al-Walid marched to al-Anbar whose people fortified themselves; then a guide told him of the market of Baghdad — the old market near Qarn al-Sarat — so he sent al-Muthanna ibn Haritha to raid it. The Muslims filled their hands with gold and silver and whatever light goods they could carry, then spent the night at al-Saylahin and came back to al-Anbar where Khalid was {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344}. They besieged its people and burned its outskirts — al-Anbar was so named because the Persians kept their granaries there and the companions and stewards of al-Nu'man drew their provisions from it; seeing what had befallen them, the townspeople made terms that Khalid accepted and confirmed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344}. It is also said that Khalid sent al-Muthanna ahead to Baghdad and followed to conduct the raid himself before returning to al-Anbar, though that account is not firmly established {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344}.
