## Overview
Tomislavgrad is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2066409 with the preferred name «Tomislavgrad», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Bosnien-Herzegowina» (an administrative unit). Among its recorded names are «Duvno», «Dumnum» and «Delminio». It links the place to GeoNames no. 3200930 and GND no. 4585353-8. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2066409}
