## Overview
Sawl is a large village with markets, groups of people, palms and fruits, according to al-Idrisi. It lies at the mouth of the gulf called the Gulf of al-Munha. The author mentions that the canals of al-Fayyum were dug from it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.125}
