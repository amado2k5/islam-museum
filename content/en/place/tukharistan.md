## Overview
Tukharistan is a region south of [[place/balkh|Balkh]], according to al-Idrisi. Its cities include Khulm, Samangan, Baghlan, Warwalin and al-Taliqan, and it connects with the lands of Badakhshan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.483}
