## Overview
Tukharistan is a region south of [[place/balkh|Balkh]], according to al-Idrisi. It is linked with the land of Badakhshan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 483}
Its towns include Khulm, Samangan, Baghlan, Warwaliz, Taliqan and Andarabah. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 483}
Khulm is a fine town rich in good things, with orchards and running water, and two days from Balkh. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
Warwaliz is also a fine town with walls and markets, two days from Balkh. Taliqan is a populous town about a quarter the size of Balkh, with an earthen wall and a large river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
Baghlan is a populous town with rivers, trees, many villages and abundant trade, and lies two stages from Samangan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 485}
The road from Balkh into Upper Tukharistan runs through Walari to Khulm, five farsakhs for each stage. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p. 42} Badakhshan is a town in Upper Tukharistan bordering the lands of the Turks, thirteen stages from Balkh, as Yaqut reports from al-Bashari and al-Istakhri. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 496} In the assessment of Khurasan's revenues made on Abdallah ibn Tahir for the years 211 and 212 AH, the kharaj of Taliqan is given as 21,400 dirhams. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p. 42} {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p. 44}
