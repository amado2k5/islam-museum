## Overview
Qal'at Isfandiyar is a fortress in the district of Istakhr, on the top of a mountain. Al-Idrisi mentions that the climb to it is by a difficult path of about three miles, that no one can reach it on a mount, and that its water comes from rain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.420}
