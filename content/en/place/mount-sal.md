## Summary
A mountain in Medina mentioned by Ka'b ibn Malik: he built a tent on its back, and upon it climbed the crier who brought him word of the repentance's acceptance. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/180}

## The event
Ka'b heard the voice of a crier upon Sal' calling at the top of his voice: "Ka'b ibn Malik, good tidings!" ([[event/repentance-of-the-three-accepted|the repentance's acceptance]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/180}
