## Overview
Silves is one of the cities of western [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. Its districts are called the Kura of Akshunuba, joined to the Kura of Ashbuna. According to al-Maqqari it is a great capital, with cities, strongholds and the house of its ruler. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.200}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Silves as a city in western al-Andalus, three days from Baja and west of Cordova. It is the capital of the province of Ushbuna and ten days from Cordova, according to al-Faris al-Majd, and five days from Shantarin. He gives its first letter a kasra, though he found the name written with a fatha in the hand of one of its men of letters. He was told that in al-Andalus after Seville there was no city like it, and that there were rarely people there who did not compose poetry or pursue letters. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.357}
