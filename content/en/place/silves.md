## Overview
Silves is one of the cities of western [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. Its districts are called the Kura of Akshunuba, joined to the Kura of Ashbuna. According to al-Maqqari it is a great capital, with cities, strongholds and the house of its ruler. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.200}
