## Overview
Qatania is a handsome town known as the town of the elephant, proud in rank and high in fame, on the sea coast in Sicily, six miles from Liyaj. It has thriving markets, splendid houses, mosques and congregational mosques, baths, lodgings and inns and a good anchorage; people travel to it from every region and goods are shipped from it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 596}

## Rivers and buildings
It has many gardens and plentiful water from its rivers and springs. One of its rivers is a marvel: in some years it floods heavily so that mills are set up on it and the wadis fill, and in others it dries up and nothing to drink is found in it. Its built-up area is wide, its fields good and productive and its walls strong. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 596} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 597}

## The elephant and the Wadi Musa
The elephant for which it is famed is a talisman of stone in the shape of an elephant, once set on a tall building in earlier times and now moved inside the city to the monks' church. West of Qatania flows the great river Wadi Musa into its sea, and its fish are of every kind, huge and fine in taste. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 597} In the coastal description, from the Wadi Musa to Qatania is three miles and from there to Liyaj six miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 624}
