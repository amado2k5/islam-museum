## Overview
Catania is a city in Sicily, which al-Idrisi describes as lofty in rank and high in renown. It has splendid houses, mosques and congregational mosques, and travellers come to it from every horizon. In it is the famous erected elephant, and it lies six miles from Liyaj. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.596}
