## Overview
Ardabil: one of the possessions of Azerbaijan, according to al-Baladhuri. The Khazars withdrew to it after the battle of the Desert of Warthan, and al-Jarrah fought them four farsakhs from the side of [[place/armenia|Armenia]], three days, until he and those with him were martyred, as the account reports. Its river was named the river of al-Jarrah, and a bridge was attributed to him. It is also reported that a traveller, Mussafir the butcher, came to it in disguise, and a group of the Shurat went out with him. See [[event/death-of-al-jarrah|the killing of al-Jarrah]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ardabil» (Pleiades no. 884119), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 2000 BCE to 2100 CE. Names recorded there include Ardabil, Adurbadagan, Badhan Firuz. {cite:pleiades 884119}
