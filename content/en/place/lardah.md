## Overview
Lardah is a town of middling size with many benefits, on a river that comes from the Mountain of al-Bartab and passes by Jaqa to Meknes. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}

It lies about seventy miles from Meknes, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}
