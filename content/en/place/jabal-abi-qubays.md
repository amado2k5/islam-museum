## Overview
Mount Abi Qubays is a mountain in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] overlooking the Sacred Precinct. "The Sealed Nectar" mentions it in the account of the cause of the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Alliance of the Virtuous]]: a man from [[group/banu-zubayd|Zubayd]] climbed it after his right had been withheld from him, and he called out in verses describing his wrong, raising his voice, until [[person/al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Zubayr ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] was stirred on his behalf. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}.

## What is not covered here
The book does not describe the mountain, its place in the Sacred Mosque, or what is told about the reason for its name; those belong in geographical books.

## For young visitors
Abu Qubays is a mountain in Mecca that overlooks the Ka'ba. A wronged merchant climbed it and called out loudly for his right, and the people of Mecca heard him and moved to support him.
