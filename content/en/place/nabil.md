## Overview
Nabil is a palace on the sea that stood near a populous city, which was later ruined. Al-Idrisi mentions that the palace of Tusihan is near it, and that there is a trace of a city that once stood there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.293}
