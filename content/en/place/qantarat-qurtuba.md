## Overview
A bridge in [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] with seventeen arches. Between one arch and the next there are about fifty spans (shibr), and its back rises above the valley about thirty cubits. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.579}

Beneath it there is a dam made of stone, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.579}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Roman bridge, Córdoba» (Pleiades no. 729234307), classed as bridge, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 300 CE. Its recorded names include Roman bridge, Córdoba. It describes a Roman bridge of the first century over the Guadalquivir river. {cite:pleiades 729234307}
