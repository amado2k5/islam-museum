## Overview
Al-Rudhan is a large city abounding in good things, with connected profits, according to al-Idrisi. Its people are well off, and it has a populous rustaq (district). In its likeness it is close to Abarquwayh. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.431}
