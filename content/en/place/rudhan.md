## Overview
Rudhan is a large town, rich in goods and joined by buildings. It has markets and profits, its people are well off, and its wide, settled district has many pulpits. It resembles Abarqu in appearance, size and fine buildings, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.431}

## Details
Al-Idrisi gives fifty-four miles from Anar to Rudhan. Anar is a small town with no wall. From Kath to al-Farhaj in the south is fifteen miles, and from al-Farhaj to Anar is seventy-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.431}

Rudhan itself is close to Abarqu in its look, its size and its fine buildings, and it is counted among the districts of Fars. Al-Idrisi lists it among the towns of Fars, alongside Sinis, Jahram, Sabur and Fasa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.403}

It is also a district of the province of Istakhr, and its people carry on their trade and their markets, and its settled villages and buildings join the town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.404}
