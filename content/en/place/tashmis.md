## Overview
Tashmis is a large city which once had markets and benefits, on the river Sufdad, and between it and the sea there is about a mile. Al-Idrisi mentions that strife destroyed it, and remains of it survived. It lies two days from Tangier. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.530}
