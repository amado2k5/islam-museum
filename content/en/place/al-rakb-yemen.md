## Overview
Al-Rakb is a place where al-Hadi spent a night on the road to Najran after al-Batna. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} The editor refers in a footnote to the "Mu'jam al-Buldan", entry al-Rakb, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} which has not been read here, so it has no coordinates. The index lists it at pp. 160 and 89. See [[event/al-hadi-marches-from-saada-to-najran-and-camps-at-the-fortress-286]].
