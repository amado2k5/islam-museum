## Overview
Rayshahr is from the land of Sabur, near Tawwaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As and Shahrak, marzban of Fars, fought each other there in a fierce battle. Sawwar ibn Hammam al-Abdi killed Shahrak, and then the son of Shahrak killed Sawwar. It was conquered by force, and its day was like the day of al-Qadisiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut cites Hamza that Rayshahr is a short form of Rev-Ardashir, a district of the Kura of Arrajan, where the Persian scribes known as kashta-dafteran lived. These were the scribes who wrote medical, astronomical and philosophical books in the Jastaq script, and in Yaqut's time no one there wrote in Persian or in Arabic. He also records the account of Sahrak, marzban of Fars, who gathered a great force and marched to Rayshahr from the land of Sabur near Tawwaj, where he fought a fierce battle with al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As, with a guard posted at a valley to kill any fugitive from his own side. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.112}
