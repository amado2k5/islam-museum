## Overview
Rayshahr is from the land of Sabur, near Tawwaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As and Shahrak, marzban of Fars, fought each other there in a fierce battle. Sawwar ibn Hammam al-Abdi killed Shahrak, and then the son of Shahrak killed Sawwar. It was conquered by force, and its day was like the day of al-Qadisiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
