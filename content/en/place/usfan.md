## Overview
Usfan is a village on the road from Medina to Mecca, near Khulays, according to what our data states. Ibn Battuta and Ibn Jubayr mentioned it on their journeys. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

The coordinates come from the modern village of Usfan, and the ancient site may be near it. An additional source is needed.
