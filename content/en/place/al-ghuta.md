## Overview
al-Ghuta: a wide area around [[place/damascus|Damascus]], extending about two stages in length and one stage in width. It has many villages and estates. Among its names al-Idrisi mentions Darya, Barza and Kawkab. It has rivers that cut through its orchards and monasteries. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.366}

al-Maqdisi counted the Ghuta of Damascus among the four gardens of the world, together with the river al-Ublah, the meadow of al-Sughd and the ravine of Bawan. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.32}
