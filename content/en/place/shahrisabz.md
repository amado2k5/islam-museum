## Overview
Shahrisabz is a city in Uzbekistan; the Pleiades gazetteer treats it as an ancient settlement of Central Asia whose settlement nucleus has moved over time. The first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan hold no entry that can be tied to it. {cite:pleiades 59945}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Shahrisabz» (Pleiades no. 59945), classed as a settlement and a modern settlement, with its recorded locations dated from 330 BCE to 2099 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. It records the names «Shahrisabz» and «Shahr-i-Sabz», attested in the twentieth and twenty-first centuries CE. Its description says that this is an ancient settlement of Central Asia, now Shakhrisabz in Uzbekistan, and that the settlement nucleus has moved over time. {cite:pleiades 59945}
