## Overview
al-Falalij: a place mentioned by al-Baladhuri. Khalid came to it on his way back from Baniqya; a band of non-Arabs was there and they scattered, and Khalid met no resistance from them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.343}
