## Summary
Batn Nu'man was a place containing palm groves on the Aqiq road from Mecca to al-Ta'if, reached by the traveller after Arafat at three miles, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.144}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that whoever goes from Mecca to al-Ta'if by the Aqiq road comes to Arafat at three miles, then to Batn Nu'man, a place containing palm trees, then ascends the pass of Kura and comes in sight of al-Ta'if {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.144}. In the same passage he describes another road from Mecca to al-Ta'if: the traveller proceeds to Bithr ibn al-Murtafi', a peopled village with nomadic Arabs, then to Qarn al-Manazil, a fortress full of inhabitants on the highway, and from there to al-Ta'if {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.144}. Batn Nu'man thus appears in his account as one of the stages of the Aqiq road between Arafat and al-Ta'if, marked by palm groves that showed the traveller his place in the valley.
