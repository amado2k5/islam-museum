## Overview
Lahun is a town in the [[place/al-fayyum|Fayyum]]. The Khitat tells that Yusuf built it to share out the water among the Fayyum's lands {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.720}.

## The canal
Al-Maqrizi says that Yusuf dug the Khalij al-Manhi from upper Ashmun to Lahun, and ordered the builders to dig Lahun {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.723}.

## Entry into the Fayyum
In 307 AH Abu al-Qasim ibn al-Mahdi went by his ships to Lahun, and the troops followed him and entered the Fayyum in Safar {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.531}.
