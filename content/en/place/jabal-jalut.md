## Overview
Mount Jalut is a Berber mountain around which the Banu Hilal live. Al-Idrisi says it was named so because Goliath's army was defeated at it, and he and his horsemen took refuge in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.121}
