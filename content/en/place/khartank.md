## Summary
Khartank: a village of Samarkand about two parasangs away — roughly three miles — where Imam al-Bukhari lodged with relatives at the end of his life; he died there and was buried there on the night of the festival, the first night of Shawwal 256 {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 14}.

## At his grave
When rain failed at Samarkand in the year 464, the qadi and the people went out to al-Bukhari's grave and prayed for rain there; the sky was released with heavy water, and the people stayed at Khartank seven days unable to reach Samarkand for the abundance of rain {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 15}.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut says Khartank is a village three farsakhs from Samarkand, where lies the grave of Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Bukhari, imam of the hadith scholars. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.356} He names Abu Mansur Ghalib ibn Jibra'il al-Khartanki after it, the man with whom al-Bukhari stayed and in whose house he died, and says he related anecdotes about al-Bukhari. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.356}
