## Overview
The Garden of Death was an orchard in al-Yamama called Abad. The polytheists took refuge in it on the day of the battle of al-Yamama in 12 AH, and Musaylima was killed in it. It was then named the Garden of Death because of the number killed in it. It belongs to [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.121}

## After the battle
Al-Baladhuri reports that Ishaq ibn Abi Khumaysa, a client of Qays, built a congregational mosque in it in the days of al-Ma'mun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.127}

Among the fortresses of al-Yamama is the one he calls the Palace of the Rose, attributed to al-Ward ibn Sumayn ibn Ubayd al-Hanafi, and the fortress of Mu'taq, named for its strength. Among its springs is al-Riyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
