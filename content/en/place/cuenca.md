## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Qunka is a city of al-Andalus in the district of Shantabariyya, to which is attributed a hafiz of hadith who died in 517. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.415}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says the name has the pattern of the word before it, but with a kaf, and calls it a city of al-Andalus in the districts of Shantabariyya. He attributes to it Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Khayra, Abu Ishaq al-Qunki, who in his own town narrated from its judge Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Khalaf ibn al-Saqqat and heard from him the Sahih of al-Bukhari, then settled in Cordoba and took much from Abu Ali al-Asali and from Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Karaj, among others. He calls him a hafiz of hadith and says he died in Shawwal 517, giving Ibn Bashkuwal as his authority. The year is Hijri. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.415}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record lists Cuenca as the municipality and capital of the province of Cuenca in Spain. That link is the museum's: Yaqut places the city only in the district of Shantabariyya in al-Andalus and does not give the Spanish name.
