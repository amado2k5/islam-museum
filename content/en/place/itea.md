## Overview
Pleiades says only that this is an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas, map 55, square C4, under the name «Itea». {cite:pleiades 540840}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, it lists this place under the title «Itea» (Pleiades no. 540840), classed as a church, with activity attested from 300 CE to 640 CE. It records no separate names. {cite:pleiades 540840}
