## Summary
Alib was a populated village in Tihama on the road from Mecca to the land of Khawlan, paired by al-Idrisi with the village of Dawqa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}

## Details
Al-Idrisi traces the road from Mecca to Dhu Suhaym in Khawlan: leaving Mecca one reaches Malkan, a water where travellers halt, then one stage to Yalamlam, a mountain barring east to west where the people of Tihama assume ihram, then a stage to a desert halt with a spring, then to Qayna, a small village with two wells, then a stage to Dawqa and Alib, two populated villages, and from them to al-Hasaba {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.147}. Alib thus stands as one of the staging villages on that mountain road rising from Tihama into Khawlan.
