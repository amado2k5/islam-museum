## Overview
Mount al-Tilmun is a mountain on the west bank of the Nile, about five miles from Zamakhir. Al-Idrisi says that it blocks the course of the Nile, pressing its flow and driving it forcefully. The people of Zamakhir tell that a sorceress lived in a palace on its top; al-Idrisi reports this from them and does not adopt it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.127}
