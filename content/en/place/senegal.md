## Summary
A country in which, according to The Oxford Dictionary of Islam, 92 percent of the people are Muslim. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2133}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam gives the population of Senegal as 92 percent Muslim, 2 percent Christian and 6 percent other. It says that Sufi brotherhoods of the nineteenth century, among them the Tijani and the Muridi, fought the French and British colonization, that the French colonists set up a secular state, and that the government of independent Senegal, from 1960, has maintained it. It describes Touba, the base of the Muridi tariqah, as a state within a state, without a governor, an administration or a police force, where veiling is not required and crime is rare, and where alcohol, cigarettes, loud music, sport and politics are banned. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2133}
