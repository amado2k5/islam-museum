## Overview
Tutus is one of the cities of Ifriqiya. [[person/zuhayr-ibn-qays-al-balawi|Zuhayr ibn Qays al-Balawi]] conquered it in his governorship of Ifriqiya, on the appointment of Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, and then departed for [[place/barqa|Barqa]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The place of martyrdom
Zuhayr was martyred in his encounter with Romans who had come down from their ships. Their graves are called the graves of the martyrs, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/zuhayr-martyrdom-barqa|The death of Zuhayr in Barqa]].
