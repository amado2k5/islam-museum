## Summary
Aqrunus was a dignified city on the back of a high mountain in the Romanian lands, six stages from the city of Istubuni. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.893}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Aqrunus among the towns of the land of Rumania together with Faruy, Lighlghu, Istinus and Danbali {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. In his ordering of the routes he says that from the city of Istubuni to the city of Aqrunus is six stages, and Aqrunus is a noble city on the back of a high mountain {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.893}. He includes it in his description of the chief towns of Macedonia and the adjoining countries from Nisu, Atrubi and Atralisa, making it a mountain station of strong position in that clime.
