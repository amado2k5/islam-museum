## Overview
Aydhab: a coast in the land of the Beja. There the supply ships came to it, which Muhammad al-Qummi carried from al-Qulzum in his campaign against the land of the Beja in the time of al-Mutawakkil, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.333}
