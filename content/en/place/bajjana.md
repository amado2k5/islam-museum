## Overview
Bajjana: this was the famous city before al-Mariyya. It was later ruined, and nothing remains of it but traces. Around it are gardens, orchards, pleasure grounds and vineyards. al-Idrisi reports that its people are treated with its baths, and that the sick come to it from all directions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.566}
