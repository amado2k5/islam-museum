## Overview
Udhayb is a stage on the road from Jeddah to [[place/taif|al-Ta'if]], at the foot of a mountain, and it has a spring-fed well (bi'r mu'ina), according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.144}
