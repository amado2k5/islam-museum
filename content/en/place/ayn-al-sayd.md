## Overview
Ayn al-Sayd: a spring among the springs of al-Tuff.

## Its name and its story
al-Baladhuri reports that it was so named because fish gathered in it. It used to be blocked up, and the legs of a Muslim man's horse sank into it, so he dug and water appeared to him. He gathered a group of people who helped him uncover and clean it until it was restored. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.419}

## Its ownership
The spring passed to Isa ibn Ali, who had bought it from the descendants of al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib. It was in the hands of al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan, Umm Kulthum daughter of al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan. al-Baladhuri reports that Mu'awiya granted al-Hasan ibn Ali this Ayn al-Sayd, together with other things, in exchange for the caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.420}
