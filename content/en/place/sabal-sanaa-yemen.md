## Overview
A place called Sabal where [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and [[person/rabi-ibn-al-ruwayya|Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya]] settled in Safar 289 AH facing the men's camp at Ghayman {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236} ([[event/the-tabari-reinforcements-arrive-and-abdullah-and-ibn-al-ruwayya-camp-at-sabal-289]]); al-Hadi later ordered an army out to it, which stayed some days and fought on the "road of Sabal", and the people of Sabal stayed there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239} ([[event/the-fight-on-the-road-of-sabal-and-the-raid-on-the-camp-at-tanhim-289]]); an army of al-Hadi's that had been at Sabal went down from it to settle beside [[place/ghayman-sanaa-yemen|Ghayman]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240} ([[event/the-sortie-to-hadda-and-safa-and-abu-al-ushayra-razes-the-fort-of-ziyad-289]]). The book gives neither its distance from Sana'a nor coordinates, so the museum puts none.
