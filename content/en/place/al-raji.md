## Overview
al-Raji: a place between Usfan and Mecca, also called al-Hada'a. al-Bakri reports that Adal and al-Qara betrayed Khubayb ibn Adi and his companions there. {cite:mujam-ma-istaajam-bakri}

An additional source is needed to fix the location precisely; no coordinates have yet been recorded for it.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives al-Raji' as the place where 'Adal and al-Qara broke faith with the seven men the Prophet had sent with them, among them [[person/asim-ibn-thabit|Asim ibn Thabit]], [[person/khubayb-ibn-adi|Khubayb ibn Adi]] and Marthad ibn Abi Marthad al-Ghanawi, and calls it a water of Hudhayl. He adds that Ibn Ishaq and al-Waqidi place it near al-Hada'a, between Mecca and al-Ta'if, that Abu Dhu'ayb names it in a line of verse, and that the well of Mu'awiya is there, which he says is not the well of Ma'una. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.29} The same entry gives a second al-Raji': Ibn Ishaq, on the march to Khaybar, has the Prophet halt in a valley of that name between Khaybar and Ghatafan, to keep Ghatafan from reinforcing Khaybar, and leave the baggage, the women and the wounded there. Yaqut judges this a different place from the first, because the first is near al-Ta'if while Khaybar is five days from Medina on the Syria side, so that the two would lie more than fifteen days apart. The entry ends with two pieces of verse by [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]] on Khubayb and the men of al-Raji'; Yaqut says he included the second though it is a weak piece, because it names all of them. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.29}

Two other entries bear on it. Under al-Hada'a he says that the place al-Bukhari mentions in the killing of Asim lies between Usfan and Mecca, that al-Bakri vowels the name the same way, and that Abu Hatim calls a place between Mecca and al-Ta'if al-Hadda, without the alif, which is a different place named there to prevent a mix-up. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.395} Under Ghuran he gives, from Ibn Ishaq, the Prophet's route "in the raid of al-Raji'" ending at Ghuran, the dwellings of the Banu Lihyan, and defines Ghuran as a valley between Amaj and Usfan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.191}

## The museum's identification
The record's text, from al-Bakri, is the first al-Raji': a place between Usfan and Mecca, also called al-Hada'a, where 'Adal and al-Qara betrayed Khubayb ibn Adi and his companions. It is not Yaqut's second al-Raji' near Khaybar. Yaqut agrees on the story and on the water of Hudhayl, but he does not say that al-Raji' is "also called al-Hada'a"; he says it is near al-Hada'a, and he gives al-Hada'a two placings, between Mecca and al-Ta'if in the al-Raji' entry (on Ibn Ishaq and al-Waqidi) and between Usfan and Mecca in the al-Hada'a entry (on al-Bukhari and al-Bakri). The museum does not settle between them. The record's point (22.15, 39.65) is about 39 km from the museum's Usfan point and about 83 km from its Mecca point, though Usfan and Mecca are only about 74 km apart, so the point lies north-east of the road between them, not on it; the record's own note calls it approximate and describes only a place between Rabigh and Mecca, and Yaqut gives no coordinates to test it. The museum's record [[place/ghuran]] is a different place (al-Idrisi's town four stages from Dandara) and is not Yaqut's Ghuran. See also [[event/incident-of-al-raji|the incident of al-Raji']].
