## Overview
Firuzabad is a town in the province of Fars in the vicinity of Shiraz; the birthplace of Imam Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi in 393/1003, from which he took the nisba al-Firuzabadi. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 494}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Firuzabad» (Pleiades no. 922643), classed as a bridge, a settlement, a modern settlement and a monument, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. It records no names and one location, dated 1700 to 2100 CE. The Barrington Atlas Directory note in it reads: two rock reliefs and a bridge. {cite:pleiades 922643}
