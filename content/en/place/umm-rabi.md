## Overview
Umm Rabi' is a large river flowing between rocks and boulders, whose waters are crossed by boats. On its bank is the village of Umm Rabi', which al-Idrisi describes as abounding in milk, fish and vegetables. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.237}
