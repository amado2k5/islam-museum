## Overview
Sharshal is an ancient city facing Miliana in the middle west, on the sea shore. It is said that it was the city of the king who seized ships. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.244-245}

Al-Umari holds that it surpasses its description in the breadth of its courtyards, the height of its buildings, the greatness of its bridges, its vaulted arches and its thick walls. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.244-245}
