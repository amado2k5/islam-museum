## Overview
A place called Ghayman where the men's camp stood facing [[place/sabal-sanaa-yemen|Sabal]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236} ([[event/the-tabari-reinforcements-arrive-and-abdullah-and-ibn-al-ruwayya-camp-at-sabal-289]]); al-Hadi's army later settled beside it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240} ([[event/the-sortie-to-hadda-and-safa-and-abu-al-ushayra-razes-the-fort-of-ziyad-289]]), and Abdullah ibn al-Husayn and Abu al-Qasim stayed two days watching the men to cut the supply from the people of Ghayman before the fight on the evening of a Tuesday in Jumada II {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240} ([[event/the-fight-near-ghayman-and-the-rout-of-ibn-al-ruwayya-289]]). The book gives neither coordinates nor its distance from Sana'a.
