## Summary
Mutsa is a populous village on the coast of the Maghreb, about twelve miles from Bijaya within the bay. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.274}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Bijaya to Mutsa is twelve miles by estimate and eight by Rus measure; from Mutsa to al-Mansuriyya in the middle of the bay is ten miles by estimate; from al-Mansuriyya to Fajj al-Zurzur is twelve miles and thence to Mazghitan, a point jutting into the sea, eleven miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.274}. From Mutsa to Fajj al-Zurzur is twenty-five miles by Rus measure, and from Fajj al-Zurzur to Jijel twenty miles by estimate {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.274}. Mutsa is thus a station on the coast near Bijaya within its bay.
