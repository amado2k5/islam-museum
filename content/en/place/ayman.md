## Summary
Ayman is a coastal city which al-Idrisi places twenty-five miles from the city of Akhilu. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Akhilu to the city of Ayman on the sea is twenty-five miles, and from Ayman to Barnas fifty miles, Barnas lying close to the sea, and from Barnas to the city of Armuqistru twenty-five miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. Just before, he notes that to the city of Akhilu is twenty-five miles with a sea inlet between them twelve miles wide and twenty miles long {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. Ayman is thus one link in the chain of coastal cities that al-Idrisi traced along that shore together with Akhilu, Barnas and Armuqistru.
