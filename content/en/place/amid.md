## Overview
A fine and fertile city, with a wall of black stones. Inside its wall there is running water, mills and gardens. al-Idrisi describes it as one of the highest cities of the Jazira. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 663}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut suspects that Amid is a Roman word, though it has a good Arabic root: the amad is the limit or goal. He says its strength together with its freshness angers anyone who wants it. It is the greatest city of Diyar Bakr, the most honoured and the most famous. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.56}

The astronomers, he reports, place Amid in the fifth climate, at 75 degrees and 40 minutes of longitude and 35 degrees and 15 minutes of latitude. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.56}
