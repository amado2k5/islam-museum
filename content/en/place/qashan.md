## Overview
Qashan is a city in the mountains. Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]], who is al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Tamimi, conquered it by force as an envoy of Abu Musa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
