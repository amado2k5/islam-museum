## Overview
Mahdiyya is a city built by al-Mahdi Ubayd Allah, first ruler of the Ubaydid dynasty, on the coast of Ifriqiya. He built it when the Maghrib came under his control in the days of al-Muqtadir. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.587}
