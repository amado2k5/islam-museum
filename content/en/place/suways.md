## Overview
Suways are two cities which were on the Red Sea, most of them now in ruin. Al-Idrisi mentions that their people used to drink from the spring of Suways, a brackish spring which the drinker cannot swallow. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.348}
