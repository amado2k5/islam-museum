## Overview
Siniyyat al-Sin lies at the far end of China, and no city matches it in size, in the amount of building, in the breadth of trade, in the quantity of goods or in the gathering of merchants to it from all regions and from some Indian cities near China. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211}

## Rule
There is in this city a king of China from the ruling house, but under the hand of the Baghbugh, the greatest king, as al-Idrisi has told. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211}

## Distances
From Susa to Siniyyat al-Sin is sixteen stages, from Sa'la seventeen, and from Tughma eight. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211} From Siniyyat al-Sin to the city of Istankhua is eight stages; it lies on a wide plain where nothing grows but saffron, planted and also wild, from which saffron is sent to all parts of China, and silk and pottery are also made there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211}
