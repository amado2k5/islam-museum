## Overview
Kazarun is a land of the district of Shapur in Fars. Al-Baladhuri reports that Uthman ibn Abi al-As conquered it and prevailed over its land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Kazarun as a city in Fars between the sea and Shiraz. He cites al-Bashari that it was a large, populous town, the Damietta of the Persians, because its linen cloth was woven and sold there. Most of its houses and its main mosque stood on a hill, with the markets and merchants' mansions below it. Adud al-Dawla ibn Buya built a house there where the brokers gathered. Al-Istakhri says that Kazarun and Nawbandajan were the largest towns of the Kura of Sabur, that Kazarun had the sturdier building and healthier soil, and that its water came from wells. In the passage he cites from al-Bashari, the town lies three days, eighteen farsakhs, from Shiraz. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.429}
