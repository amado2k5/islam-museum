## Overview
Lubayban is a village in [[place/najran|Najran]], of the [[group/yam-tribe|Yam]], to which [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went without dismounting at the main village; its "troublemakers" feared him and climbed the hills until their clans brought them down. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} The editor notes that Sifat Jazirat al-Arab, p. 169, writes it "Lubaynan", {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} and a re-reading of the page gives "Lubayban". The book gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-third-march-to-najran-and-the-troublemakers-of-lubayban-286]].
