## Overview
Ala Dagh, "mountain of various colours", is a name often given to mountains in Turkish-speaking lands. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 370}

## Examples
One Ala Dagh lies south-west of Konya. Another is a spur of the Kara Dagh in the south-east of Kars, near which the Russians defeated the Turks on 16 October 1877. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 370}
