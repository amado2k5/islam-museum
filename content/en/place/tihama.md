## Overview
Tihama is a low coastal land between the Red Sea and the mountains of Yemen. It extends from the coast of the Qulzum to [[place/aden|Aden]] for about twelve stages, with the mountains to its east and the sea to its west, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.148}
