## Summary
Damavand is a towering mountain near al-Rayy and Tabaristan whose summit is covered in snow, and popular legends about al-Dahhak and Solomon are attached to it. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 441}

Golius describes the mountain as the highest peak in that region, noting that its summit is never free of snow in winter or summer, and quotes Yaqut that no one has ever ascended to its peak. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 441}

The commentator relates from popular lore that al-Dahhak, whom the text calls Biwarasf, was imprisoned in the mountain, and that smoke issues from a cave in it called "the breath of al-Dahhak". He adds that he himself saw a sulfurous spring at its foot that ignites when the sun shines upon it. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 441}

Popular accounts also say that Solomon, son of David, imprisoned a rebellious demon there called Sakhr al-Marid, while other accounts state that Afridun imprisoned al-Dahhak in it. These are popular tales recounted by the commentator without endorsing them. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 442}
