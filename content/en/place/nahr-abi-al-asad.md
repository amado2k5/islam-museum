## Overview
The River of Abu al-Asad is a river at al-Butayha, the marshes. Al-Baladhuri relates that Abu al-Asad, client of the Commander of the Faithful and commander of [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]], dug it. He had been dispatched to [[place/basra|Basra]] during the stay there of Abd Allah ibn Ali, and he was the one who brought Abd Allah ibn Ali into [[place/kufa|Kufa]] when he camped between his army and that of Isa ibn Musa, who was fighting Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan.

It is said that he stayed at the river's mouth because ships could not enter it on account of its narrowness, so he widened it, and it was named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
