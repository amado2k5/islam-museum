## Overview
Farghana is a region beyond the river (Transoxiana), on the river Syr Darya. Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili conquered from it Kasan, Awrasht, Sukhr and Akhsikat, its old city. Nasr ibn Sayyar was in his army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
