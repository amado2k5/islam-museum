## Overview
A region in the centre of the Arabian Peninsula. Abu Qays ibn al-Harith al-Sahmi was killed there on the day of al-Yamama in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. According to Ibn Ishaq, Hawdha ibn Ali al-Hanafi also appears there, in the account of the Prophet's envoys.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut opens the entry al-Yamama with its etymology: the name is transferred from a bird called al-yamam, singular yamama, and his transmitters differ — al-Kisa'i makes the yamam the pigeons that live in houses and the wild ones, al-Asma'i a wild kind of pigeon and allows derivation from amma ya'ummu "to aim", Ibn al-Anbari derives it from al-yamm, a bird, or from al-amam, and al-Zajjaji rejects the last. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.441; 5 p.442} For him it lies in the second clime, longitude 71 degrees 45 minutes, latitude 21 degrees 30 minutes; in al-'Azizi's book the third clime at latitude 35 degrees. Its conquest and the killing of Musaylima were in Abu Bakr's days in year 12 of the Hijra: Khalid ibn al-Walid took it by force and then its people were treated with; between it and al-Bahrayn is ten days; it is counted in Najd and its seat is Hajar; it is called Jaww and al-'Arud (with a fatha on the 'ayn) and its ancient name was Jaww, renamed for al-Yamama bint Sahim ibn Tasm. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.442} On the authority of the siyar people he reports that Tasm and Jadis had their dwellings in al-Yamama when it was called Jaww and what lies around it to al-Bahrayn; that a Tasmid king 'Amliq ibn Habash ruled them as a tyrant; that the Jadis betrayed the Tasm at a feast to which they had invited them; that Riyah ibn Murra of Tasm escaped to a Tubba' — said to be As'ad Tubban ibn Kaylakarib, or Hassan ibn Tubba' — and begged his aid; and that in Jadis was his sister al-Yamama, called Zarqa' al-Yamama, said to see a man a day's march away, who warned her people of the army and was not believed until Himyar fell on them at dawn. He records verses of al-A'sha and of the Yamama herself in the story (he notes some of them are weak verse), that the Tubba' took the fortresses of al-Yamama, had her eyes put out and found their veins packed with ithmid kohl — she is said to be the first of the Arabs to use it — and crucified her on the gate of Jaww, so the land was named for her ever since, and al-Yamama has been ruined from that day as they tell it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.443; 5 p.444; 5 p.445; 5 p.446; 5 p.447}

## The museum's identification
The record's al-Yamama is the central-Arabian region, and it is Yaqut's district: counted in Najd, seated at Hajar, conquered by Khalid ibn al-Walid in year 12 with Musaylima killed there — which matches the record's day of al-Yamama. The long Tasm-and-Jadis and Zarqa' al-Yamama material is given by him on the authority of the siyar people and is transmitted legend, and he himself marks some of its verse as weak; the museum records it as such, not as settled history.
