## Overview
A region in the centre of the Arabian Peninsula. Abu Qays ibn al-Harith al-Sahmi was killed there on the day of al-Yamama in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. According to Ibn Ishaq, Hawdha ibn Ali al-Hanafi also appears there, in the account of the Prophet's envoys.

## Location
