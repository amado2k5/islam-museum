## Summary
Bardsir is a fine, flourishing city of the Kirman country with a wall, a moat and gates and many markets, two stages from al-Sirjan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.439}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that the road from the city of al-Sirjan to Zarand is four stages: from al-Sirjan to the city of Bardsir two stages, it being a fine, flourishing city of great fertility with a wall, a moat and gates, many markets and thriving manufactures, and from it to Janzarudh a long stage, Janzarudh a large flourishing city with markets and craftsmen, and thence to the city of Zarand one stage {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.439}. Bardsir is thus a fortified station on the great road of Kirman between al-Sirjan and Zarand, combining strength, fertility and a busy industry.
