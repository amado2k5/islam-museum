## Overview
Nairun is a city that is neither large nor densely populated. It is fortified, its people are well off, and it has few trees. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies about three stages from [[place/daybul|Daybul]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.168}
