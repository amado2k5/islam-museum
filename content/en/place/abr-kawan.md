## Overview
Abr Kawan: an island in the sea off [[place/fars|Fars]]. al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As conquered it with an army from Abd al-Qays, al-Azd, Tamim and Banu Najiyya, and it was the first part of the Fars coast to be conquered. Common people know it as "Banu Kawan". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
