## Overview
The Lower Fortress of Yahsub is a fortress in the lowest part of the land of Yahsub, sixteen miles from the [[place/hisn-yahsub|Fortress of Yahsub]]. It has running water from sweet springs, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.153}
