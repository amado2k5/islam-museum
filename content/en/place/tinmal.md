## Overview
Tinmal is a fortress in the mountains of Daran, in the land of Hargha. Ibn Tumart took it, and the Masmuda pledged allegiance to him there on monotheism (al-tawhid) in the year 515 AH, and it became his capital. From it the Almohad state set out, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1669}
