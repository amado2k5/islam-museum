## Overview
The land of Qamnuriyya is a land of the Sudan south of the desert, where there were once famous cities. Al-Idrisi mentions that the pressure of the Zaghawa and the Lamtuna exhausted it, until its people scattered into the mountains and deserts, and a small group remained, living on milk and fish. He relates from merchants a confused description of their belief. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.105} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.106}
