## Overview
Hawth is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] camped in the days past of Safar 286 AH with a great army and the brother of [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] and his horse, meaning to confront [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} The editor's footnote refers to al-Hamdani's "Sifat al-Jazira", p. 83, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} which has not been read here, so the place is given no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-khirfan-and-al-subay-and-the-march-to-hawth-286]].
