## Summary
A town that, according to Ibn Ishaq, was ruled by Ukaydir ibn Abd al-Malik al-Kindi, a Christian. The Prophet sent Khalid ibn al-Walid to it from Tabuk.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 29.8114 north and 39.8683 east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
