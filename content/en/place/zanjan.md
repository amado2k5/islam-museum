## Overview
Zanjan is a city in the regions of Qazwin. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Bara' ibn Azib conquered it by force after the peace of Qazwin, and after his raids in al-Daylam, Jilan, al-Babr and al-Taylasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
