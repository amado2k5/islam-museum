## Overview
Zanjan is a city in the regions of Qazwin. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Bara' ibn Azib conquered it by force after the peace of Qazwin, and after his raids in al-Daylam, Jilan, al-Babr and al-Taylasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Zanjan as a large and well-known city in the mountain lands between Azerbaijan and the surrounding region, near Abhar and Qazvin, and notes that the Persians say Zankan with a k. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.152}

Several scholars of learning, literature and hadith came from it. Among the earlier ones is Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Sakin al-Zanjani, who transmitted from Isma'il ibn Musa. Another is Umar ibn Ali Abu Hafs al-Zanjani, a jurist who went to Damascus, heard hadith there, and was transmitted from by Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn Jarida al-Maliki; he wrote a book he called al-Mu'tamad. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.152}
