## Overview
Qiqan is a land of Sind on the side bordering Khurasan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra raided it in the year 44 AH, and that al-Harith ibn Murra was killed there in the year 42 AH, along with Abd Allah ibn Sawwar and Rashid ibn Amr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

An additional source is needed: the data gives no page number, and the dates of the two events are taken from the summary alone.
