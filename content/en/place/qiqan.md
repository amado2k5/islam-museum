## Overview
Qiqan is a land of Sind on the side bordering Khurasan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra raided it in the year 44 AH, and that al-Harith ibn Murra was killed there in the year 42 AH, along with Abd Allah ibn Sawwar and Rashid ibn Amr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

An additional source is needed: the data gives no page number, and the dates of the two events are taken from the summary alone.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives two entries. The first is a place near the city of Aleppo, known to people there. The second is a land near Tabaristan, and he reports from a conquest history that in the caliphate of Ali, al-Harith ibn Murra al-Abdi went as a volunteer to the Sind frontier and took spoils. Yaqut places al-Qiqan in Sind, on the side that faces Khurasan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.423}

According to the same account, al-Harith and those with him were killed in al-Qiqan, except a few, in year 42. Al-Muhallab campaigned there in year 44 and met eighteen Turkish horsemen, who fought and died. In year 45, under Muawiya, Abd Allah ibn Sawwar al-Abdi raided al-Qiqan, and later returned and was killed there after the Turks were called in; Khalifa ibn Khayyat records that the people of al-Qiqan then overcame the Muslims. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.423}
