## Overview
Jarba is a place whose people came to the Prophet at [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]] and gave him the poll tax, according to Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

No coordinates are given for this place, because its identification is not certain.
