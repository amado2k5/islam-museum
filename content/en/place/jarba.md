## Overview
Jarba is a place whose people came to the Prophet at [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]] and gave him the poll tax, according to Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

No coordinates are given for this place, because its identification is not certain.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut says al-Jarba reads like the feminine of al-ajrab, and that it is a place in the district of 'Amman in al-Balqa' in Syria, near the Sarat mountains on the Hijaz side, a village belonging to Adhruh. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.118} He adds that between the two places took place the matter of the two arbiters, 'Amr ibn al-'As and Abu Musa al-Ash'ari. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.118} He also lists a second al-Jarba, a well of Banu Sa'd ibn Zayd Manat of Tamim between Basra and al-Yamama. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.118}
