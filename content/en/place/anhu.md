## Summary
Anhu was a fine, sizable, populous city of Estland in the far north, linked by land and sea routes to the towns of those parts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.954}

## Details
Al-Idrisi notes that the land of Estland has many villages and settlements though few cities, and is colder than Finland, with frost and rain hardly ever leaving its people {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.954}. He says from the city of Anhu to the city of Ragholda is two hundred miles, and Anhu is a fine, great, populous city of Estland {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.954}. From Anhu to the city of Qaluri southward with an easterly bearing is six stages, and from it along the coastal road to the mouth of the Birnu river fifty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.954}.
