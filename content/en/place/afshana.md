## Overview
Afshana: a village near [[place/bukhara|Bukhara]], where [[person/ibn-sina|Ibn Sina]] was born.

An additional source is needed: the place data does not carry a source for this information.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut gives the name with a fatha on the hamza, a sukun on the fa, a fatha on the shin with a dot, and a final nun and ha. He places Afshana among the villages of Bukhara. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.231}

Yaqut's entry is this short. He gives no further description of the village, so the detail about Ibn Sina's birth comes from the existing record and its own sources, not from Yaqut. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.231}
