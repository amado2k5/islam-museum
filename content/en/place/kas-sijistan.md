## Overview
Kas is a small town with a wall in [[place/sijistan|Sistan]]. Its people are wanderers who turn aside, most of them weavers and merchants. It lies on the edge of the desert, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.458}
