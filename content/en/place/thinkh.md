## Overview
Thinkh is a medium-sized city at the top of a strong mountain in the land of Tibet, with a strong stone wall and a single gate. It has crafts of the Turks, works and much trade with their neighbours. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 513}

## Trade
People travel to it from the land of Kabul, the land of Wakhan, Khuttal and Wakhsh and the country of Rasht, and from it come the iron attributed to Tibet and musk. It is said that spikenard grows abundantly on the mountain joined to Thinkh and that musk animals are plentiful in its thickets. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 513}

## Mountain and river
Al-Idrisi mentions in this mountain a cave of great depth in which the murmur of running water is heard at the bottom, though the depth of the chasm cannot be reached; Chinese rhubarb grows on the mountain in quantity and is exported to many regions; and the river of Thinkh is called the river of Sharmakh. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 514} It is among the chief towns of Tibet listed in the ninth part of the third clime. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 510}
