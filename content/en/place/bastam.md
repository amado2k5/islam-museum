## Overview
Bastam: a fine city with a wall, with markets and established dues, according to al-Idrisi. It lies one stage from Jurjan, on the road to Damghan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.689}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Bastam as a large town in Qumis, on the main road to Nishapur, two stages beyond Damghan. He quotes Mas'ar ibn Muhalhal, who called it a large village resembling a small city. Abu Yazid al-Bistami, the ascetic, came from it, and it grows apples of fine colour that were carried to Iraq. Mas'ar reports two properties of the place: that no lover from among its people was ever seen, and that whoever came into it in love and drank its water lost the passion; and that no case of eye inflammation was ever seen there. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.421}
