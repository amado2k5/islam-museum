## Overview
Al-Kharba is a place so called in the [[place/najran|Najran]] account; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went out to it one day after Adha, ordered its palms cut and went back to his camp. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} The book does not fix its place and the editor has no note on it, so it has no coordinates here. See [[event/al-hadi-cuts-the-palms-of-minas-and-al-kharba-after-adha-286]].
