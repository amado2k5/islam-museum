## Overview
Jasta is a small town on a large creek that ships enter. Gold is found there in abundance, and it is the produce and occupation of its people. They eat sea turtles and the flesh of shellfish. It lies two days from Dughwata, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.79}
