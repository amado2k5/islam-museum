## Overview
Ubulla is the port of [[place/basra|Basra]] on the Tigris. Suwayd ibn Qutba al-Dhuhli aided [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] in fighting its people when he came to Basra in the year twelve AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
