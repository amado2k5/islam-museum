## Overview
Kawar is a famous land that people travel to, joined on the east to the land of [[place/kawkaw|Kawkaw]]. From it comes the alum known as Kawari alum, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.116}
