## Overview
Jabay is a town with buildings closely packed among palms and sugar cane, and it has fertility and produce. It lies twelve miles from Basyan and fifteen miles from Sulaymanan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.399}
