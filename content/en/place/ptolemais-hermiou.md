## Overview
Ptolemais Hermiou was a city founded by Ptolemy I Soter as the capital of Upper Egypt, also known by the name Psoi, according to Pleiades. {cite:pleiades 756635}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ptolemais Hermiou/Psoi» (Pleiades no. 756635), classed as a settlement, with a time span from 330 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «Ptolemais Hermiou», «Psoi» and «El-Mansha». It describes the place as a city founded by Ptolemy I Soter as the capital of Upper Egypt. {cite:pleiades 756635}
