## Overview
Jazah is a town whose buildings join the buildings of [[place/farah|Farah]]. It has many villages, buildings and rivers that enter it. It lies about one stage from Sistan on the road to Khurasan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.456}
