## Overview
al-Qatif: a coastal oasis on the Arabian Gulf in Bahrain. Ibn al-Jawzi reports that Abu Sa'id al-Janabi al-Qarmati went to it in the year 287 AH after his affair had grown strong and he had killed those around him from among the people of the villages. It is also said that he intended Basra. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.310}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut calls al-Qatif a city in Bahrayn, now its capital and largest town. The name was once that of a wider district, which the city later took over. He says al-Qatif was earlier a settlement of Jadhima of Abd al-Qays. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.378}

He reports the arrival of the delegation of Abd al-Qays to the Prophet, whose two leaders told him they had entered Hajar and taken its key. He also records that Najda al-Haruri sent his son al-Mutarrih with horsemen to give alms to Abd al-Qays at al-Qatif; the son was killed in the fighting, and the Kharijites later prevailed over the people of the town. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.378}
