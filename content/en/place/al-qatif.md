## Overview
al-Qatif: a coastal oasis on the Arabian Gulf in Bahrain. Ibn al-Jawzi reports that Abu Sa'id al-Janabi al-Qarmati went to it in the year 287 AH after his affair had grown strong and he had killed those around him from among the people of the villages. It is also said that he intended Basra. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.310}
