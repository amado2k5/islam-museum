## Overview
A town on the sea with a wall of strong stone, and a wide district with trees. It lies five miles from the River Ibrahim, and its people drink from wells, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.372}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Byblos» (Pleiades no. 668216), classed as a settlement, a port and an archaeological site, with activity attested from 6000 BCE to 2100 CE. It describes a place occupied since the Neolithic period, historically a major Punic centre called Gebal. {cite:pleiades 668216}
