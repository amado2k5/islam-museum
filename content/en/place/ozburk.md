## Overview
Ozburk is one of the cities of the province of Suwaba. Al-Idrisi mentions that between it and Bansa there are a hundred miles, and between it and a city on the river Muwayn seventy miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 876}
