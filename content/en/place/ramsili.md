## Overview
Ramsili is a city in the land of the Russians, on the river Dnester and to its north. Al-Idrisi lists it among the cities of this part of the world. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904}

## Setting
It stands on the Dnester and to its north. The river runs east until it reaches the city of Zaqa, twelve stages away. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904}

## Distances
From Ratsalaba to Ramsili is one hundred miles, in the land of Sadmara. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890} From Ramsili to Zaqa is twelve stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.903} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904} From Murtori, which stands at the mouth of the Dnester, Ramsili lies four days to the south. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955}

## The land of the Russians
Al-Idrisi describes the land of the Russians as large, with few towns. Its settlements are scattered and the distances between one town and the next are long. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904} The people have constant wars and skirmishes with their own kind and with their neighbours. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904}

## Neighbouring places
Al-Idrisi names Ramsili, Zaqa, Turubiya and Ghalisiya as the cities of the Russians in this section. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904} He gives the road from Ramsili to Zaqa as twelve stages, along the same river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904}
