## Overview
Jazirat al-'Arabiyya is a small island in the Persian Gulf, at about 27 degrees 46 minutes north and 50 degrees 10 minutes east. It is less than a mile square and lies about 50 miles from the Saudi Arabian mainland and about 60 miles from the Iranian coast. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 628}

## Position and claims
The island is one of a group of five, the Kurayn, on the Arabian side of the Gulf. It is normally uninhabited, and three Gulf states, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Iran, each claim it. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 628}
