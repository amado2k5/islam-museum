## Overview
Azerbaijan: a region whose capital is Ardabil, where its governor is and to which its land-tax is collected. Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman conquered it under a governorship from Umar, which al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba later transferred to him. Its governor made peace with him for eight hundred thousand dirhams, of the weight of eight. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
