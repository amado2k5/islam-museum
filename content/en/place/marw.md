## Overview
Marw is the great city of Khurasan. Its marzban, al-Shahjan, sent asking for peace, and Ibn Amir sent Hatim ibn al-Nu'man al-Bahili to it. He made peace with it for a sum whose amount and rank the reports disagree on, and the terms included making room in their houses for the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

All of it was made by treaty except a village called al-Sanj, which was taken by force, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Establishing the amounts of the treaty needs an additional source.
