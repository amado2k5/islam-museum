## Overview
Hadramawt is a region in the south of the Arabian Peninsula, and it is part of [[place/yemen|Yemen]]. Al-Baladhuri reports that the Prophet appointed over it Ziyad ibn Labid al-Bayadi al-Ansari, and that his transmitters agree on this. Among the land the Prophet granted in it was a piece to Alqama ibn Wa'il al-Hadrami. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.93}
