## Overview
Ávila is held in the museum as a settlement site known to the Pleiades gazetteer, which places it at about 40.66° N, 4.70° W. {cite:pleiades 236361}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Avila/Obila?» (Pleiades no. 236361), classed as a settlement, with a location attested from 330 BCE to 640 CE. {cite:pleiades 236361} Recorded names include «Avila» [330 BCE – 640 CE]; «Obila» [30 BCE – 300 CE]. {cite:pleiades 236361} Pleiades describes it as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 24, grid G4. {cite:pleiades 236361}
