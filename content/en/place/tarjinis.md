## Overview
Tarjinis is a commanding fortress overlooking the regions, with continuous farms and building. On it is a populous settlement (ruqqa) overlooking the area, and it lies eighteen miles from Jarami, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.619}
