## Overview
Jarma is a town in the land of Fezzan. Al-Idrisi says the road from it to Awdaghast is about twenty-five stages, that its water comes from wells, and that its people grow maize and barley. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.108} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.112}
