## Overview
The island of Sahilan is a large sandy island, with no plants and no fertility, in the sea of the Khazars (the sea of Tabaristan, the Caspian), north of the island of Hadhu al-Kur. A great many black birds gather there, coloured like the crow but cleaner, and this kind of bird is found only on this island. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 832} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 833}

## The sea
Al-Idrisi describes the sea of the Khazars as a salt sea without tide or ebb, dark in its depths because the soil of its bed is hot mud. It holds four uninhabited islands, two of which have water and trees but no inhabitants. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 832}

## Neighbouring islands
One is Siyah Kuh, facing Abaskun, the large one, with springs, trees, thickets, animals and wild beasts. Next to it is Hadhu al-Kur, a large island with thickets, trees and water, which yields madder; people who seek madder go out to it from the district of Barda'a, and animals are brought and let loose there to fatten. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 832}

The earlier data summary said people catch the birds on the island; the page says only that the birds gather there, and the summary has been corrected.
