## Summary
Al-Mu'alla is the name of a place in the upper part of Mecca, toward the Hajun hill, and is the burial ground Meccans know as Jannat al-Mu'alla. This page sets out what Yaqut al-Hamawi's *Mu'jam al-buldan* says about the place and about a well in it, and it states what the part of the book read here does not say. The name al-Mu'alla in the *Mu'jam* is used for more than one place, so the places are kept apart below.

## The upper part of Mecca
Yaqut distinguishes between what lies below the Sacred Mosque and what lies above it in Mecca. The lower part they call al-Musaffala, and the upper part they call al-Mu'alla. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.187} In this passage the name describes the high ground of the town toward al-Hajun, not a particular burial ground.

## The well of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari
Yaqut quotes Abu Abdallah al-Fakihi, in his book on Mecca, that Salqan, the agent of Bugha, freedman of the caliph al-Mutawakkil, built the well of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari at al-Mu'alla in 242 AH, after it had been filled in, and that it still stood in al-Fakihi's time on the entrance of the Shi'b Abi Dubb at al-Hajun. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.302} This is an account Yaqut passes on from al-Fakihi; the passage does not confirm the detail from the place itself.

## The name in other places
Yaqut describes a second al-Mu'alla between Mecca and Badr, a short distance from Badr al-Uthayl, and a village of al-Kharj in Yamama that bears the same name. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.158} On the road from Badr to Medina he also lists a post station called the post of al-Mu'alla. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.358} These places are not the Meccan burial ground and nothing here is drawn from them for that ground.

## Note on the source
The part of the *Mu'jam* read for this page places al-Mu'alla in the upper town of Mecca and mentions a well there. It does not describe graves, a cemetery, or the people buried there. The description of the place as a cemetery on the museum's record was therefore not confirmed from this source, and no other Meccan source on the burial ground was read in this pass. The page stops there until such a source is read.
