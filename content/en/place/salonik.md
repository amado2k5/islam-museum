## Overview
Salonik (Thessalonica) is a fine, imposing city, thickly built up on land and sea, at the end of a small gulf. From it to Randhina is twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 800}

## Roads
The road from the city of Daras to Salonik on the gulf coast runs from Daras to Batrala in two days, from Batrala to Akhrida in four days, and from Akhrida to Butli two days eastward; Butli is a fine, imposing and pleasant town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 793} From Budhyana, a town on a mountain, to Salonik, near the gulf of Constantinople, is two days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 794}

## Neighbours
Randhina is a large city with walls and thriving markets, and from it to Akhrasubli on the sea is twenty-five miles; that is an imposing city with noble markets and much trade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 800} Between Zaghoriya and coastal Salonik is a hundred and forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 797}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Thessalonica» (Pleiades no. 491741), classed as settlement, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 640 CE. Among its recorded names are «Thessaloniki», «Selānik» and «Saloniki». Pleiades describes a city of northern Greece founded by Cassander of Macedon around 315 BCE and an important centre of the Roman and Late Antique worlds. {cite:pleiades 491741}
