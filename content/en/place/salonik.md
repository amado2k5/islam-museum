## Overview
Salonica is a large, populous city, with many buildings by land and sea, on the edge of a small gulf. Al-Idrisi describes its markets as thriving. From Salonica to Rindhina it is twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.800}
