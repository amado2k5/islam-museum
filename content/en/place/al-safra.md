## Summary
Al-Safra' is a populated valley between al-Rawha' and Badr, from which the Prophet sent Basbas ibn Amr al-Juhani and Adi ibn Abi al-Zaghba' al-Juhani to Badr to gather news of the Quraysh caravan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}

## Details
On the march to Badr, after passing the well of al-Rawha', the Prophet left the Mecca road to the left and took the Iraq road, passing Rahqan between al-Naziya and the defile of al-Safra', then through the defile and down until he drew near al-Safra'. From there he sent Basbas ibn Amr al-Juhani and Adi ibn Abi al-Zaghba' al-Juhani ahead to Badr to spy out news of the Quraysh caravan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}

Al-Safra' appears again in the Badr narrative: Ubayda ibn al-Harith died there some days after the battle — his leg had been cut in the duel and he remained enfeebled until he died at al-Safra'. On the return journey the Prophet camped on a dune between the defile and al-Naziya and divided the spoils among the Muslims, and there al-Nadr ibn al-Harith, who had carried the idolaters' banner on the day of Badr, was executed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
