## Overview
Arsa: a fine city on a strong mountain. al-Idrisi reports, on the authority of al-Hawqali, that strangers used to be killed on entering it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.917} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.918}
