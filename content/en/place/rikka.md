## Overview
Rikka is a city at the foot of the Darn mountain, near [[place/aghmat|Aghmat]], according to al-Idrisi. Its soil is good and its plants are abundant. A river, not large, cuts through the city, and gardens surround it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.226}
