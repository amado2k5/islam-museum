## Overview
Kastamonu is a city in Turkey, the centre of the province of the same name. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090159}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2090159 with the preferred name «Kastamonu», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2225037. Among the names it records are «Kastamonu» in Turkish, English, German and French, «Κασταμονή» in Greek and «قسطموني» in Arabic. It links the place to GeoNames no. 743882, to Pleiades no. 844974, to Arachne entries and to a German Authority File (GND) identifier. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090159}
