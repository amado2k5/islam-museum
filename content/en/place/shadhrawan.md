## Overview
Al-Shadhrawan is a great dam built by the Sasanian kings in front of [[place/tustar|Tustar]] in the course of the water, and it is fastened with stone. Al-Idrisi mentions that the water was held back by it until it rose above the land around it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.393}
