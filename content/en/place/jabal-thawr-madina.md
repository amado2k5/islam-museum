## Overview
Mount Thawr in Medina is a small mountain in the land of [[place/medina|Medina]], different from Mount Thawr in Mecca. It is mentioned in the report on the sanctity of Medina, "what lies between Ayr and Thawr is sacred", as the northern boundary of the [[concept/haram-al-madina|sanctuary of Medina]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.105-106}

## The name's obscurity
The mountain was obscure to some later transmitters. Al-Samhudi reports Mus'ab al-Zubayri saying: "Neither Ayr nor Thawr is known in Medina." Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Salam and al-Hazimi held that the original reading was "from Ayr to Uhud". Others held that the obscurity of the name does not cast doubt on the transmitters. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.105-106}
