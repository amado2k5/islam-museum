## Overview
Awlil: an island near the coast, with the famous salt-works, according to al-Idrisi. Salt is carried from it to the lands of the Sudan: ships come and load the salt, then sail with it to the Nile. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.17}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut quotes Ibn Hawqal that Awlil lies in line with Awdaghust, on the edge of the sea, at the end of the inhabited land. He calls it the salt mine of the Maghrib lands, a month from Awdaghust, and says it is twenty-five miles from Lamta, the source of leather shields. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.283}
