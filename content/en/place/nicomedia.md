## Overview
Pleiades says Nicomedia was founded in 712/711 BCE as a Megarian colony named Astacus, was rebuilt by Nicomedes I of Bithynia in 264 BCE, and served as an important administrative centre of the Roman Empire. {cite:pleiades 511337}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, it lists this place under the title «Nicomedia» (Pleiades no. 511337), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 640 CE. Names recorded there include «Nikomēdeia, Nikomedeia, Nikomedia» (1200 BCE to 1918 CE) and «Kocaeli, Kodjaeli, Kodja-Eli, Koja-Eli» (1918 to 2000 CE). {cite:pleiades 511337}
