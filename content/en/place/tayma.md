## Overview
Tayma is an oasis in northern al-Hijaz, one of the places where the Jewish tribes that migrated from Palestine settled, in the account of al-Rahiq al-Makhtum. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40}

## From the introduction to al-Rahiq al-Makhtum
Jewish tribes settled in Yathrib, Khaybar and Tayma after the Romans occupied Palestine in the year 70 CE, and established villages, forts and castles in them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.151} The Banu Asad settled east of Tayma, and Dhubyan near it, as far as Hawran. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.22} See [[concept/judaism-in-arabia|Judaism in the Arabian Peninsula]].
