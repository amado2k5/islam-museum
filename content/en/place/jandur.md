## Overview
Jandur is a set of palaces joined together, well built and high-peaked, through which fresh water flows. The governor stays in them in the spring. Al-Idrisi says that in an earlier period the governor used to ride an elephant from them every Friday. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.177}
