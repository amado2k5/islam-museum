## Summary
Batrani was a fine, mid-sized urbanized town, thirty miles by land west of Brins, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.898}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that from Brins by land westward to Batrani is thirty miles, and that it is a fine urbanized city of middling size {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.898}. From it westward to the city of Barfuntu is thirty miles; Barfuntu lies on the slope of a pleasant mountain, and between Batrani and Barfuntu runs a river that flows from Barsaklafa and passes south until it enters the sea {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.898}. Batrani thus stood in his scheme on a westward overland route from Brins to Barfuntu, with a river descending from the land of Barsaklafa between it and its neighbour, making it a link in the chain of small urbanized towns along that road.
