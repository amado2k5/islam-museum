## Overview
Lake Tiberias: its waters come from the rivers of Baysan, al-Yarmuk and al-Hadd, and its water flows until it falls into Lake Zughar. al-Idrisi describes it as a place between two mountains. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.355}
