## Overview
The Fortress of Yahsub is a fortress in the land of Yahsub, where a group of ancient Arabs lives, and a few palms. Al-Idrisi says that sixteen miles lie between it and the [[place/hisn-sufl-yahsub|Lower Fortress of Yahsub]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.153}
