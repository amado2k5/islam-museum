## Overview
Khakhit is a town in the districts of Armenia. Al-Baladhuri reports that Marwan ibn Muhammad settled in it ten thousand households of Slavs he had taken captive from the land of the Khazars. Then they killed their commander and fled, and he caught up with them and killed them. It is connected with the account of [[event/marwan-caucasus-expedition|Marwan's campaign in the Caucasus]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292}
