## Overview
The district of Barza: a district of Azerbaijan belonging to the Awd, according to al-Baladhuri. A man of theirs gathered people to it and built a fortress there, and in the year 239 AH a pulpit was made there against the wish of the Awdiyyun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
