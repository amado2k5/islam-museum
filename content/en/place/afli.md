## Overview
Afli: a town on open, level ground, with spacious places and connected buildings. Al-Idrisi says that high mountains lie to its north, that the river Dhanu runs behind it, and that it has an iron industry. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.901}
