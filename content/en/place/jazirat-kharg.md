## Summary
Kharg Island is a fine, grassy and fertile island in the sea of Fars, three miles by three miles, bearing crops, much rice, vineyards and palms. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.387}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Awal Island to Kharg Island is two hundred and forty miles; Kharg measures three miles by three and has crops, much rice, vineyards and palms, being a fine grassy fertile island {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.387}. Its neighbour Awal is likewise a fine island containing a large city called al-Bahrayn, populous, handsome and fertile, rich in crops and palms, with many springs of sweet water {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.387}. Kharg thus lies within the chain of inhabited islands of that sea described in his book.
