## Summary
Mushla is a fine city rich in farms and fruit, wide in its districts, of continuous buildings, with strong walls and busy markets. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.885}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Basu to Mushla is one hundred and fifty miles; it is a fine city with many farms and fruit, wide districts and quarters, connected buildings, strong walls and flourishing markets {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.885}. Basu, from which it is measured, is a city of the land of Buwamiya, and from the city of Aqra to Basu westward with a southern bearing is eighty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.885}. Mushla is thus among the populous walled cities of that region.
