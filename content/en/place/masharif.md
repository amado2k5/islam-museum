## Overview
Masharif is a village in the land of al-Balqa in al-Sham. It was the first place where the army of the Muslims met the army of Heraclius and the Arab allies in [[event/battle-of-mutah|the Battle of Mu'ta]], before the Muslims withdrew to the village of Mu'ta. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

No definite site is known for it, so it has no coordinates in the museum.
