## Overview
Rashid is a civilised city on the Nile, according to al-Idrisi. It has farms and crops of wheat and barley, fine vegetables and fresh fruits. Much Nile fish is caught there, including al-Dulays, and it is carried to the surrounding regions. It lies sixty miles from [[place/alexandria|Alexandria]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.343}
