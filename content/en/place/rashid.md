## Overview
Rashid (Rosetta) is a civilised town on a branch of the Nile near the sea. It has a market and merchants, fields that grow wheat and barley, and abundant fish. Its dalins, a small shellfish, are caught there, salted and carried to all of Egypt, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.343}

## Details
Al-Idrisi describes Rashid as a civilised town with a market, merchants and provisions, fields of wheat and barley, plenty of vegetables and palm trees, and many kinds of fresh fruit. Its waters hold many sea fish and Nile fish. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.343}

The dalins are a small shell that forms where the Nile's fresh water meets the salt sea. The people of Rashid salt them and carry them to every region, and this is one of their trades. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.343}

A lake runs along the coast about six miles from Rashid. From Rashid to Alexandria is sixty miles, travelled from Rashid across the sands to Buqayr and then on to Alexandria. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.344}
