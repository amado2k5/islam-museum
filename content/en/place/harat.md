## Overview
Herat is a city of Khurasan. Al-Baladhuri reports that Firuz exiled to it a group of people from Fars who practised sodomy, and they became helpers to the people of Quhistan alongside the Hephthalite Turks. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
