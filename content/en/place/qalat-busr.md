## Overview
Qal'at Busr is a fortress near a city called Mujjana, at the silver mine, in the districts of al-Qayrawan. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Busr ibn Abi Artat conquered it in the governorship of Uqba ibn Nafi. It is said that Musa ibn Nusayr sent him to it when he was eighty-two years old, and it has been named after him to this day.
