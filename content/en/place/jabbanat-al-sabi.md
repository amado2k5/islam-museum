## Overview
The Cemetery of al-Sabi' is the great graveyard of [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. Al-Baladhuri, quoting Hisham ibn Muhammad al-Kalbi, reports that it was named after the descendants of al-Sabi' ibn Sab' ibn Sa'b al-Hamdani. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.395}
