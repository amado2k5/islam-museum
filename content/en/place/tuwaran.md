## Overview
Al-Tuwaran is a fortified city with open spaces (faraj) and gardens. It is the chief town of a valley that takes its name from it, a valley of fields and continuous buildings, and it lies near al-Fahraj in the land of Kirman, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175}

## Details
Al-Idrisi gives fifteen stages from al-Mansura to Tuwaran. Four stages from Tuwaran lies Qazdar, a large, populous town with markets, trade, good conditions and villages. West of Qazdar is Kizkanan, where the governor of Tuwaran resides: a cultured town of many people and cheap prices, with gardens, vineyards and fruit, but no palm trees. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175}

From Tuwaran, three stages across the middle of the desert bring the traveller to Mustanj, a small town with few fruits and many camels and sheep. From there ten stages reach Multan, at the far end of the land of Sind. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175}
