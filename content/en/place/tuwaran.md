## Overview
Al-Tuwaran is a fortified city with a gap (faraj) and a park. It is the chief town of a valley with farms and buildings, and it lies near al-Fahraj in the land of Kirman, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.175}
