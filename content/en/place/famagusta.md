## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Famagusta as a populated place on the island of Cyprus, under the Latin form «Fama Augusta» among its names and the Turkish form «Gazimağusa». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2044191}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2044191 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Famagosta» in Italian. Its parent in the hierarchy is «Cyprus», an administrative unit and island. It records names in Turkish («Gazimağusa»), Latin («Fama Augusta», «Nova Iustiniana»), English («Famagusta»), Greek («Ammóchostos»), Serbian, Chinese, and the ancient forms «Arsinoë» in Egyptian and «Αρσινόη» in Greek, and in Arabic the form «فاماغوستا». It links the place to GeoNames no. 146617, to Pleiades nos. 707617 and 707485, to the Arachne identifiers 6525 and 1208613 and to the German National Library authority record 4250986-5. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2044191}
