## Overview
al-Kiryawn: a place before Alexandria. There Amr ibn al-As met the armies of the Byzantines and the Copts, who had gathered to attack him at Fustat; he defeated them and killed a great number of them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

There [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr]] said to the Muslims: "Go forth in God's blessing; whoever plants a spear in a house, the house is his and his father's family's." So the house between two or three persons was a dwelling for the days of their garrison duty, not to be sold or inherited. See [[event/conquest-of-alexandria|the conquest of Alexandria]].

## According to al-Maqrizi
Al-Maqrizi says, quoting Ibn Abd al-Hakam, that the canal that brought water into Alexandria was led from the village of Kasa, opposite al-Kiryawn {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.246}.

He says that the fighting at al-Kiryawn went on for some ten days, and that Abdallah ibn Amr commanded the vanguard {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.499}.

He says that when the Muslims left al-Kiryawn, the houses where their spears had been planted were occupied by the Byzantines, who held their repair {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.509}.
