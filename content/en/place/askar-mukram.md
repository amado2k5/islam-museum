## Overview
Askar Mukram: a city in al-Ahwaz, according to al-Baladhuri. It is said that it was named after Mukram ibn Mutarrif ibn Saydan al-Bahili, whom Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr sent in pursuit of the killer of his father, and he marched until he encamped there. It is also said that it was named after Mukram ibn al-Farz. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
