## Overview
al-Marghab: a place in [[place/basra|Basra]]. The source reports that Bashir ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra dug there the marghab, the water channels and the crossing canals by al-Taghlub, and named it "Marghab Marw". It was part of the estate of Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini, which was eight thousand jaribs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
