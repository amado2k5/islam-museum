## Overview
Hispania is the Roman province of the Iberian Peninsula, 218 BC to 472 AD; the museum records a representative point for it at about 40.21 degrees north and 4.35 degrees west. {cite:wikidata} It is the land that Muslims came to call [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]; this page keeps the Roman name because the numismatic vocabulary uses it. The page does not yet carry an account of the province from a source read for this run.
