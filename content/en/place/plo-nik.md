## Overview
Pločnik is held in the museum as a Roman and late-antique settlement site known to the Pleiades gazetteer, which places it at about 43.20° N, 21.36° E. {cite:pleiades 207350}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Pločnik» (Pleiades no. 207350), classed as a settlement, with a location attested from 30 BCE to 640 CE. {cite:pleiades 207350} Pleiades describes it as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 21, grid D6. {cite:pleiades 207350}
