## Overview
Quanzhou is a port in south-east China, where Muslim merchants lived in the Middle Ages. It has an old mosque dating from 1009 CE. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}

The data notes that it may be meant as Khanqu, the city of merchants and wealth, forty farsakhs long, in the reports of Abu Dulaf on China. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.492}
