## Overview
The River Maqil is one of the rivers of [[place/basra|Basra]]. Al-Baladhuri mentions that it was named after [[person/maqil-ibn-yasar|Maqil ibn Yasar al-Muzani]]. [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]] sent him, and he opened it, seeking blessing through his companionship, and people said "the River of Maqil". No one called it the River of Ziyad, although Ziyad dug it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
