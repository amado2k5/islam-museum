## Summary
Misrata is a city on the coast of Libya west of Tripoli. Its record states that Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his way from Tripoli, a mention that still needs verification on the page. {cite:wikidata}

## Details
Misrata lies on the Mediterranean coast of western Libya; the coordinates recorded for it in the museum's card come from the Wikidata P625 value for the city of the name. {cite:wikidata}

The card also records it as a place Ibn Battuta passed on his way from Tripoli; a review of the scanned Arabic text of the 1958 edition of the rihla has not surfaced the name in the passages read, so the identification of the modern city with the traveller's place remains in need of a source verified on the page. {cite:wikidata}
