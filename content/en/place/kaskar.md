## Overview
Kaskar is a land in al-Sawad. Al-Baladhuri reports that Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud defeated the army of Darna to Kaskar, and that the Muslims kept raiding between al-Hira and Kaskar, and between Kaskar and Sura, Buzaqsama and the Sirat of Jamasb. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
