## Overview
Suq al-Ahad is a site in Ifriqiya. According to al-Baladhuri, Huraysh and his companions were killed there after their defeat from [[place/qayrawan|Qayrawan]] of Ifriqiya, following the death of al-Aghlab ibn Salim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.327}
