## Overview
Bamm: a land in Kirman. Its people made peace with al-Rabi ibn Ziyad al-Harithi, then they disbelieved and broke the covenant, and Mujashi ibn Mas'ud conquered it, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
