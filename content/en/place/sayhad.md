## Overview
Sayhad is the desert of Yemen called al-Ghaʾit. According to al-Hamdani, it branches from al-Dahna on the side of al-Yamama and al-'Ulj, and extends between Najran, Bayhan and Hadramawt for four or five stages. Most of it lies beyond Najran to the north. It is a desert with no water except at the watering-places, like al-'Abr. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p.67}
