## Overview
Erzincan is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Erzincan Province. Yaqut al-Hamawi, in his geographical dictionary, describes Arzanjan as a pleasant and well-known town, rich in goods and people, in the lands of Armenia between the Byzantine lands and Khilat and close to Arzan al-Rum. He says most of its inhabitants were Armenians, that Muslims lived there too and were its notables, and that open wine-drinking and licentiousness were common there; he adds that he knew of no one with a nisba to it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut 1 p.194}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2212380 with the preferred name «Erzincan», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Erzincan Province. Among the names it records are «Aziris», «Arzanjan» and «Yerznka», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 315373 and to Pleiades no. 874467. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2212380}
