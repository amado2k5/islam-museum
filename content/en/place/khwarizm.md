## Overview
Khwarazm is a province that includes all of the land beyond the river, and its city is eastern. [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] marched to it after [[place/balkh|Balkh]] but could not take it, and withdrew, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
