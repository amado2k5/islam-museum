## Overview
Khwarazm is a province that includes all of the land beyond the river, and its city is eastern. [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] marched to it after [[place/balkh|Balkh]] but could not take it, and withdrew, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says Khwarizm names the whole region, not only a city. Its chief city is sometimes called al-Jurjaniyya, and its people call it Kirkanj. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.395}

He gives two sets of coordinates: Ptolemy's Malhama places Khwarizm in the sixth climate at 117 degrees and 30 minutes of longitude and 45 of latitude, while Abu Awn's table puts it at the end of the fifth climate, at 91 degrees and 50 minutes of longitude and 44 of latitude. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.395}

For the name he records genealogical opinions from Ibn al-Kalbi about the sons of Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, and a mocking verse about the people of Khwarizm attributed to al-Lahham. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.395}
