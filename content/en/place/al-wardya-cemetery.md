## Summary
Al-Wardiya is a cemetery in Baghdad. Yaqut al-Hamawi, in the Mu'jam al-Buldan, places it on the eastern side of the city, after Bab Abraz and close to Bab al-Zafariyya. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.371}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Al-Wardiya a single short entry as a cemetery in Baghdad. He places it on the eastern side, beyond Bab Abraz, near Bab al-Zafariyya. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.371}

The entry says nothing about the cemetery's origin, the burials in it, or any building inside it. What it fixes is its position in eastern Baghdad. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.371}
