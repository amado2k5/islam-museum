## Overview
Kisal is a town built by [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]] when he was given charge of the frontier, and he lodged in it. It lies forty farsakhs from [[place/bardhaa|Bardhaa]] and twenty farsakhs from [[place/tiflis|Tiflis]]. From it he set out and entered the land of the Khazars on the side of the [[place/bab-al-lan|Gate of al-Lan]], according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292} See [[event/marwan-caucasus-expedition|Marwan's campaign in the Caucasus]].
