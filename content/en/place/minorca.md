## Overview
Minorca is an island facing the city of Denia on the other shore. Al-Idrisi mentions that it has a small, fine city, and that it lies a sea-passage from the coast of [[place/barcelona|Barcelona]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}
