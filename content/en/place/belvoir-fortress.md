## Overview
Belvoir Fortress (Kawkab al-Hawa) is a Crusader castle in northern Israel.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Belvoir Fortress» (Pleiades no. 802931429), classed as a castle, with activity attested from 640 CE to 2100 CE. {cite:pleiades 802931429} Recorded names include «Kawkab al-Hawa» [Arabic] and «Kochav HaYarden» [Hebrew]. {cite:pleiades 802931429} Pleiades describes it as a Crusader fortress in northern Israel whose construction was begun in 1168 by Gilbert of Assailly, Grand Master of the Knights Hospitaller. {cite:pleiades 802931429}
