## Overview
al-Mughitha: a dam that Sulayman ibn Ali built at [[place/basra|Basra]] on al-Bitiha. It held back the water from the river al-Dayr and diverted it to the river of Ibn Umar, and he spent a million dirhams on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
