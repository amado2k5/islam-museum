## Overview
Adra: a small town on the sea with no wall. It has a bath-house, an inn and abundant water, as al-Idrisi describes it. On its western side a large river empties into the sea, which al-Idrisi says rises in Mount Shalir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.564}
