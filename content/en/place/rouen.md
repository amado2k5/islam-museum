## Overview
Rutumaghsh (Rouen) is a large, well-known city on the river, on its eastern bank, one of the chief towns of the land of Nurmandiya (Normandy). From it to Dyaba on the sea is twenty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 865} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 864}

## Distances
Between Rutumaghsh and Lazawdash is forty miles, and there the river of Rutumaghsh falls into the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 865} From Rutumaghsh to the city of Bentiz is thirty miles; this is the last of the lands of Nurmandiya on the south, a populous city on the river, with fine houses, a wide territory and abundant building. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 866}

## Neighbours
Dyaba is a populous city on the sea with moorings, sailing and building of ships for voyages, and from Hanfalat to it eastward is forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 865} Rutumaghsh is listed in the land of Zamandiya (as the text has it) with Biyawsh, Irawsh, Bentiz, Dyaba, Qam and Qastans, a land with many villages and continuous settlements. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 864}
