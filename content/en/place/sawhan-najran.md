## Overview
Sawhan is a place at Najran with a fort, where some of Banu al-Harith held one of three camps along with Minas and the village of al-Hajar. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} Al-Hadi went for it and the fight in which he held his ground alone took place there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} The index lists it at pp. 160, 161, 166 and 175. It has no coordinates here. See [[event/first-fighting-at-the-fortress-of-najran-and-the-three-camps-286]] and [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]].
