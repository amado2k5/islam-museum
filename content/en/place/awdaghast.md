## Overview
Awdaghast: a small city in a desert with little water, between two mountains, according to al-Idrisi. He likens its shape to Mecca, its trade is weak, and its people live on camels. He reports that the road from it to Ghana is twelve stages, to Warqalan thirty-one, and to Jarma about twenty-five. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.108}
