## Overview
The mountains of Shirwin are in Tabaristan. In al-Baladhuri's description they are the most secure and the hardest of its mountains, and the richest in thickets and forests. Muhammad ibn Musa ibn Hafs ibn Umar ibn al-Ala' and Mazyar ibn Qarin conquered them in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See also [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]].
