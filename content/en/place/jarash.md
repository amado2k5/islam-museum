## Overview
Jarash is a town in the land of Hawran. The Hawl reports wonders of its remains. In the eighth century AH, Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari described it as ruined on its foundations, empty of its people and inhabitants. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.231}
