## Overview
Barisi: a small city on the Nile with no wall, more like a settled village, according to al-Idrisi. Its people are travelling merchants, and it is under the obedience of the Takruri. It lies twelve stages east of Takrur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.19}
