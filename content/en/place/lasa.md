## Overview
Lasa is a very small town on the bank of the salt sea, one day from Sharma. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.55}

It has a hot spring in which the people of the region purify themselves, and the sick are brought to it and recover, as al-Idrisi relates. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.55}
