## Overview
Bajjana (Vera) was a city famous before [[place/almeria|Almeria]], according to al-Idrisi. Its people moved to Almeria, which flourished, and Bajjana was ruined. Nothing remains of it but traces of building and its standing congregational mosque, and around it are gardens and orchards. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.566}
