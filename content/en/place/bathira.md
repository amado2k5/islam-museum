## Overview
Bathira: a fortress that is impregnable in its walls and modest in extent, overlooking the sea. It lies one stage from the island of Sicily, with its surrounding land. al-Idrisi reports that its valley is great and turns around it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.599}
