## Overview
Tahirt is a city in the Maghrib, according to al-Baladhuri. Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab ibn Ibrahim founded a city near it in the year 239 AH, which he named al-Abbasiyya. Then Aflah ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Ibadi ruined it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}
