## Overview
Tarsus is one of the Syrian frontier cities, which al-Baladhuri describes as the greatest of them. It remained in ruins after its people departed in the days of the conquest, until al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba described it to al-Mahdi in the year 162 AH, and he ordered its construction. In the year 171 AH it reached al-Rashid that the Romans had conspired to fortify it, but Harthama ibn A'yan forestalled them and rebuilt it through Faraj ibn Sulaym. The troops of Khurasan and al-Sham settled in it in the year 172 AH. Its plots were surveyed, four thousand plots, each twenty cubits long and as wide. It became a launching point for the summer raids and a gateway for jihad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The event of the rebuilding of Tarsus is connected with it: [[event/founding-of-tarsus|The rebuilding of Tarsus]].
