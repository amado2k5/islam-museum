## Overview
Amlah is a village that the book places in the land of [[group/wailat-shakir|Wa'ila]]; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went toward it after the Eid al-Adha in 290 AH and was met by the armies of Wa'ila in a narrow place of the land before it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.250} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.251} ([[event/al-hadi-marches-on-amlah-after-the-eid-and-wail-seeks-safety-290]]). A marginal heading of the original on a juristic matter concerning its wrecking is recorded by the museum without addition. The book does not give its location further, and the page image confirms the reading of the name.
