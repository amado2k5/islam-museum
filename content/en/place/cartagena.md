## Overview
Cartagena: a coastal city in eastern Spain. Ibn Jubayr mentioned it on his way to Granada. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

An additional source is needed for the journey itself.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute holds this place as record no. 2365651, typed as an archaeological site and a populated place, under the preferred name «Cartagena (Municipio)»; its parent in the hierarchy is «Murcia (Comunidad autónoma, Provincia)».
