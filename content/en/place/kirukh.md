## Overview
Kirukh is a town with a strong wall of earth, and its gardens are many and populous. It has the redcurrant that is shipped to all horizons, and it lies three stages from Herat, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.472}
