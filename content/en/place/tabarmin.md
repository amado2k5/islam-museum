## Overview
Tabarmin is a lofty, elevated land, which al-Idrisi counts among the choicest of the ancient fortresses. It has many tuna-fishing traps, and it lies one stage from Messina on the coast. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vowels the name with a fatha on the first two letters, a sukun on the r, a kasra on the m, then ya and nun, and says only that it is a strongly fortified castle in Sicily. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.17}
