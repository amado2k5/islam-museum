## Overview
Tabarmin is a lofty, elevated land, which al-Idrisi counts among the choicest of the ancient fortresses. It has many tuna-fishing traps, and it lies one stage from Messina on the coast. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}
