## Overview
Mount Radwa is a high mountain with ravines and springs. Its greenery is like young herbage and it has much water. Whetstones are cut from it and carried to the horizons. It lies near Khaybar, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.352}
