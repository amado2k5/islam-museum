## Overview
The council of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] before and after Islam. Qusayy ibn Kilab built it near the Kaaba, and the notables of Quraysh met there to decide matters of war, peace and marriage alliances.

## Significance
From it came the consultations of Quraysh about the Prophet and the war against him; in Arabic memory it became the name of any aristocratic consultative council. {cite:wikidata}
