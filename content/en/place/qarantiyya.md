## Overview
The first of the cities of the Kimakiyya, a great city with a strong wall, horses and horsemen. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.708}

It lies on a great lake which is said to be six days long and a day and a half wide. It lies seven days from Bakhuan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.708}
