## Overview
Qarantiyya is the first city of the Kimak lands, a large one nine miles long by three wide, on a great lake called the lake of Ghaghan, which is said to be six days long and a day and a half wide. Its king is fierce and hardy, with many men, horses and archers who all shoot with bows. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 708}

## The way to it
From the city of Atraqana to Qarantiyya is ten stages across a wilderness in which live nomadic Turks with herds and camels and a good condition. From Qarantiyya to the city of the king of the Kimak is twenty-four stages, and to Bakhwan seven days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 708} Travellers who prefer water go from Banjar to the village of Dahra on the lake shore and sail six stages along the lake to Qarantiyya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 709} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 710}

## Neighbours
West of the lake stands the city of Ghaghan, six stages from Qarantiyya, a fine and prosperous place with a tiraz workshop for silk garments, from which Turkish merchants carry goods out in quantity. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 708}
