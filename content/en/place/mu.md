## Overview
Muş is a city; Pleiades records the place as the findspot of a fragment of an Urartian stele. {cite:pleiades 875298457}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Muş» (Pleiades no. 875298457), classed as a findspot, with activity attested from 1500 CE to 1599 CE. Its description says that it is the findspot of a fragment of an Urartian stele. It records one name, «Mūş» (in Arabic script «موش»), in Ottoman Turkish, from 1500 to 1599 CE. {cite:pleiades 875298457}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name as Mush, with a damma on the mim as he found it written, and says that on this vocalisation it has no root in Arabic; with a fatha it would be the verbal noun of a verb meaning to gather what is left of the grape clusters in a vineyard. He lists two places of the name: one non-Arabic, a town in the district of Khilat in Armenia, and the other a mountain in the land of Tayy. The first is the one this page describes. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.223}
