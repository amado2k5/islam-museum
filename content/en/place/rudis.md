## Overview
Rhodes is an island which, according to al-Baladhuri, is among the most fertile of the islands, about sixty miles in extent, with olives, vines, fruits and fresh water. He mentions that Junada ibn Abi Umayya conquered it in the year 53 AH, and that the Muslims stayed in it for seven years in a fortress, until Yazid ordered it demolished and they returned. Mujahid ibn Jabr was residing there, teaching the recitation of the Qur'an. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330}
