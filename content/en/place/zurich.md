## Overview
Zurich is a city recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place in the canton of Zurich. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090501}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists Zurich under no. 2090501 as a populated place with the preferred name «Zürich»; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit canton of Zurich. It records names in many languages, among them the Latin «Turicum» and the Arabic «زيورخ», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 2657896 and to Pleiades no. 187596. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090501}
