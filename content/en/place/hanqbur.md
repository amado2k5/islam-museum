## Summary
Hanqbur was a large populous city of the land of Ankiriyya adjoining the land of Baluniyya, five stages west of the city of Walba. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from the city of Walba to the city of Hanqbur in the western direction is five stages; the city of Hanqbur is large and populous and borders the land of Baluniyya {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. From the city of Walba to the city of Tanisir southward is four great stages amid buildings, farms and plenty, these stages lying between the two rivers together {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. The author counts among the towns of the land of Ankiriyya comprised in this part the cities of Hanqbur, Shant, Baqsin, Jarnaghrana, Qawnwar, Balghrdun, Afrnisfa, Tanisir and Walba {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.882}. Whoever wishes travels from the city of Jarnaghrata to the city of Walba in five stages: leaving the city for the mouth of the river Tisya a great stage, then ascending along the river Shant four stages to the city of Walba, a self-sufficient populous city on the river Shant on its north {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. The land of Baluniyya is the land of scholars, and many of the Romans come to it seeking learning from all quarters, its lands populous and rich in people {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. Hanqbur is thus for him a populous frontier city on the bounds of Baluniyya in the land of Ankiriyya.
