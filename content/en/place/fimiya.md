## Summary
Fimiya is a city that al-Idrisi mentions in the east of the Frankish lands, with a hundred miles between it and the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.951}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Dunst eastward to the city of Fimiya is one hundred miles, that between Fimiya and the sea is one hundred miles, and that facing it on the north, across the Sea of Darkness, lies the city of Lond Shawdhan {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.951}. He adds that from the city of Lond Shawdhan to the mouth of the river Qatlu — also read Qatralu — on which stands the city of Saqtun, is one hundred and ninety miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.951}. Fimiya is thus an inland city in the farthest reaches of the northern lands al-Idrisi described, about a hundred miles from the shore of the Sea of Darkness, facing across that sea the city of Lond Shawdhan among the northern island lands whose people dry their grain in houses where fires are lit because the sun's rays are so faint there {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.951}.
