## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
[[person/yaqut-al-hamawi|Yaqut]] vocalizes the name Bilbis, with *i* on both *b*s and a quiescent *l*, following Nasr al-Iskandari, and notes that common people say "Lilbis". {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.666} For him it is a town ten parasangs from Fustat on the road to Syria, inhabited by the tribe of Abs ibn Baghid, taken in 18 or 19 AH by [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]; he quotes two verses of al-Mutanabbi on Arabs who "spent the night at Bilbis". {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.666}
