## Overview
Ashura Island: a lightly built-up island, with rough, thicket-covered land of many thorn-bushes and trees, according to al-Idrisi. It lies four days from the island of Shamil, and from it to the island of Mallay is a light day's journey. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.85}
