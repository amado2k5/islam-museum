## Overview
Al-Akhtut is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted after [[place/dhamar|Dhamar]], where its people gathered to him and he admonished them; the narrator says it was corrupt because Ibrahim ibn Khalaf had been living there, and many left it when al-Hadi arrived before he went on to Mankath {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.213}. Two days after he reached Mankath he was told that some of the corrupt had shown themselves there and sent after them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.213}. The book gives no coordinates for it and the museum does not guess them. See [[event/al-hadi-at-dhamar-hilan-and-al-akhtut-288|al-Hadi at Dhamar and al-Akhtut]].
