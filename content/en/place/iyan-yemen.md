## Overview
Iyan is a place the text calls a land of [[group/banu-salman-hamdan|Banu Salman]], where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] met [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] with Banu Salman, who asked safe-conduct for them; al-Hadi gave it and they swore hearing and obedience. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The editor refers in a footnote to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab p. 73 {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}; that source was not read here, so no coordinates are given. See [[event/al-hadi-letter-from-najran-and-the-meeting-with-al-daam-at-iyan-286]].
