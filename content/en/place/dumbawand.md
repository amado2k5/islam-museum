## Overview
Mount Damavand is the greatest mountain of Tabaristan. Al-Baladhuri reports that Sa'id ibn al-As conquered it, together with the plain of Tabaristan and Ruyan, in his campaign of 29 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
