## Overview
Maskin is a village in the Sawad of Iraq. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Nusayr raided villages in Maskin and Qutrabbul on behalf of Khalid ibn al-Walid, and took a fine booty from them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

A poet mentioned it in the market of Baghdad, together with al-Anbar, Qutrabbul and Badurya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
