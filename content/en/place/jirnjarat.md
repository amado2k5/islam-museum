## Summary
Jarnaghrata was a large populous city of the land of Ankiriyya with markets and ample goods; from it to Walba are five stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from there along the river to the city of Jarnaghrata is three stages; it is a large populous city with markets and ample goods {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. From there likewise along the river to the city of Tanisir is four stages, that is a hundred and twenty miles; Tanisir is a fine city of many goods on the south of the river Tisya {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. Whoever wishes travels from the city of Jarnaghrata to the city of Walba in five stages: leaving the city for the mouth of the river Tisya a great stage, then ascending along the river Shant four stages to the city of Walba, a self-sufficient populous city on the river Shant on its north {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}. The author counts among the towns of the land of Ankiriyya in this part the cities of Hanqbur, Shant, Baqsin, Jarnaghrana, Qawnwar, Balghrdun, Afrnisfa, Tanisir and Walba {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.882}. Jarnaghrata thus appears in his scheme as a large populous river city of markets in the land of Ankiriyya, joined by the roads of the rivers Tisya and Shant to Walba and Tanisir.
