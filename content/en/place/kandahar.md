## Overview
Kandahar is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as both an administrative unit and a populated place, within Kandahar Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2783275}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2783275 with the preferred name «Kandahar», classed as both an administrative unit and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Kandahar Province. Among the names it records are «قندهار». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2783275}
