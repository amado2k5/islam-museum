## Overview
Kandahar is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as both an administrative unit and a populated place, within Kandahar Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2783275}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2783275 with the preferred name «Kandahar», classed as both an administrative unit and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Kandahar Province. Among the names it records are «قندهار». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2783275}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Kandahar as a city in the third climate, at 100 and ten degrees of longitude and 30 degrees of latitude, in the lands of Sind or India, and well known in the accounts of the conquests. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 pp.402-403}

He reports that Abbad ibn Ziyad raided the frontier of Sind and Sijistan, reached Kandahar, fought its people, defeated them and took the city, after some Muslim men were killed. Seeing the tall caps the people wore, he made a fashion of them, and the style was called the Abbadiyya. Yaqut also quotes verses by Yazid ibn Mufarrigh about men who died at Kandahar. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 pp.402-403}
