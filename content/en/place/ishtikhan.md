## Summary
Ishtikhan, a town of Sughd, which in al-Baladhuri became the seat of Sughd's kings after they had long resided at [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 592}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Qutayba ibn Muslim campaigned against Samarqand — which the kings of Sughd had resided at in ancient times before moving to Ishtikhan — besieged its people and fought them several times; the king of Sughd wrote to the king of al-Shash, then stationed at Tarband, who came with a body of his fighters, but the Muslims met them in the fiercest battle and Qutayba routed and broke them, whereupon Ghurak made peace on two hundred and twenty myriad dirhams each year. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 592} He also relates that Sa'id, the governor of Khurasan, crossed to Transoxiana and camped at Ishtikhan, to which the Turks had come; he fought and routed them and restrained his men from pursuing for a while, then met the Turks again and was defeated with heavy loss among his companions. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 600} Ishtikhan is thus, for him, a royal seat of Sughd and a staging ground in those wars.
