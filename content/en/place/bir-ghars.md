## Overview
The well of Ghars: a well in [[place/medina|Medina]] from whose water the Prophet liked to drink sweet water. Rabah, the freedman of the Messenger of God, once drew water from it. al-Baladhuri reports that the Prophet said it is one of the springs of Paradise. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.536}
