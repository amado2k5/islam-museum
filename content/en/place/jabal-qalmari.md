## Summary
Jabal Qalmari is a mountain in the lands of the Maghrib containing a mine of good iron, four days from Santariya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Santariya to Jabal Qalmari is four days, and that in this mountain there is a mine of good iron {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}. He notes that from Santariya sets out whoever wishes to enter the land of Kuwar and travel the lands of the Sudan, that from Santariya to Awjila westward is ten days, and that in this district stands the red mountain of Birim {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}. Jabal Qalmari is thus one of the mountains of the Maghrib known for the good iron ore within their rock, which al-Idrisi places four days from Santariya in a region from which the roads branch toward the lands of the Sudan and the land of Kuwar — evidence of the mineral wealth those mountains held, extracted by the people of those parts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}
