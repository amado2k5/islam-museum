## Overview
Meknes is a fine city raised on high ground, with water flowing to its east, full of markets, baths and houses, and joined by gardens and crops. Al-Idrisi mentions that it was named after the Meknassa tribe. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.244}
