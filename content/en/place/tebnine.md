## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Tibnin is a town in the mountains of Banu Amir overlooking Banias, between Damascus and Tyre. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.14}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut fixes the vowelling of the name, with a kasra on the first letter, a sukun on the second and a kasra on the nun, then a silent ya and a second nun. He calls it a town in the mountains of Banu Amir that look down on the country of Banias, between Damascus and Tyre. The entry is short and names no buildings, people or events, so it keeps only the spelling and the position. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.14}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record places Tebnine in the Nabatieh Governorate of Lebanon. That is the museum's reading of Yaqut's position; Yaqut does not mention Nabatieh.
