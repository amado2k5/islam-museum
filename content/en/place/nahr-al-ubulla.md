## Overview
The River of Ubulla is the great canal of [[place/basra|Basra]], running from al-Ijana to Ubulla, and its length is four farsakhs. Digging it was begun by [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] on the orders of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], and it was completed by [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan]] from the point where it had become silted up. Al-Baladhuri mentions that this was the cause of his enmity with Abd Allah ibn Amir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
