## Overview
Wazan is a land on the road to [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]]. [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] marched to it after [[place/khawash|Khawash]], and its people fled, and he prevailed over it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
