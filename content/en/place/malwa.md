## Overview
Malwa is a fine city with much trade, both imports and exports, and it has many villages. Among them are the two cities of Dadda and Tatta. According to al-Idrisi, there are four stages between Malwa and Dadda. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.194}
