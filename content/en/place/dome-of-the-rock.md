## Overview
The Dome of the Rock mosque, in the middle of the Haram al-Sharif in Jerusalem above the sacred rock; 'Abd al-Malik b. Marwan built it on an octagon of stone — begun 66 AH (685) and finished 72 AH (691) {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 43}. The book records its restoration with its dome in 1964 under King Husayn's patronage {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 93}.
