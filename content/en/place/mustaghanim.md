## Summary
Mustaghanim is a small coastal town of the Maghreb by a bay, with markets, baths, gardens, orchards, abundant water and a wall on a hill facing west. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Ras Mashana to the anchorage of Arzaw is eighteen miles, a large village to which wheat is brought, thence to Mustaghanim on the sea by the bay, a small town with markets, baths, gardens, orchards and much water, its wall on a hill overlooking the west; the bay measures thirty-four miles by estimate and twenty-four by Rus measure {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271}. From Mustaghanim to Hawd Farruh is twenty-four miles by estimate, a fine anchorage with a populous village, followed by the town of Mazuna six miles from the sea {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271}.
