## Overview
Ani is a strongly fortified castle and city in the land of Armenia, between Khilat and Kanja, according to Yaqut al-Hamawi in the Mu'jam al-buldan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.69}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ani» (Pleiades no. 863739), classed as a settlement and a fortified settlement, with a time span from 330 BCE to 2100 CE. It records two names, «Anisi» in Georgian and «Ani» in Ottoman Turkish. It describes the place as a ruined Armenian city in the extreme east of Turkey, and a note in the record adds that between 961 and 1045 CE it was the capital of the medieval Armenian kingdom. {cite:pleiades 863739}
