## Summary
Gabes is a city on the coast of Tunisia. Ibn Battuta reached it and stayed ten days because of continuous rain, then left it bound for Tripoli. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p. 11}

## Details
On his first journey Ibn Battuta came to the city of Gabes and lodged inside it, staying ten days because the rain kept falling. Ibn Juzay quotes rajaz verses about it: "Extinguished for the sweetness of nights gone by / beside the batha' of Gabes — / my heart at its memory / was like a brand of fire in the hand of Gabes." He then left Gabes bound for Tripoli. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p. 11}

The city appears a second time in the rihla: in Safar 750 he sailed in a Tunisian qarqura to Jerba, then travelled in a small boat to Gabes, where he stayed as the guest of the two brothers Abu Marwan and Abu al-Mabbas, sons of Makki, the amirs of Jerba and Faris, and attended the Prophet's birthday celebration with them; then he took ship to Sfax and continued overland to Tunis. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p. 438} Its recorded coordinates come from GeoNames for the modern city of the name. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
