## Overview
Al-Jawzjan is the name of a district with farmlands in the mountains. Tanned hides are brought to it, and luggage is made from them. Among its towns are al-Faryat, Ashburqan and Anbar, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.478}
