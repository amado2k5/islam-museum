## Overview
al-Tayrahan: a town next to Mosul. [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad]] conquered it together with Tikrit, then travelled through the district of Bajarmi, and then came to Shahrazur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
