## Overview
A city among the great cities between Malaga and Cordoba, thirty-five miles from both of them, according to al-Idrisi. He reports that it and the city of Antequera were evacuated by the rebels in al-Andalus after the rule of Ibn Abi Amir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the vowels and letters of the name (Arshadhuna) and describes it as a city in al-Andalus counted among the districts of Rayya, south of Cordoba, with twenty farsakhs between it and Cordoba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.152}
