## Overview
Sari is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Mazandaran Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2379573}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2379573 with the preferred name «Sari», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Mazandaran Province. Among the names it records are «Sārī», «ساری» and «ساري», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 116996. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2379573}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut lists the city under the name Sariya, calling it a city of Tabaristan in the fourth climate, at a longitude of seventy-seven degrees fifty minutes and a latitude of thirty-eight degrees. He cites al-Baladhuri that Tabaristan had eight districts, that the governor resided at Sariya in the Tahirid period after previously residing at Amul, and that the Alid rulers al-Hasan ibn Zayd and Muhammad ibn Zayd made it their seat. He gives three farsakhs from Sariya to the sea and eighteen to Amul, the relational form as Sari, and notes that Tabaristan is Mazandaran. In a separate entry, Sari, he says it is the same Sariya mentioned before. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.170} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.171}
