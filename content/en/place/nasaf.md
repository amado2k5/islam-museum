## Overview
Nasaf, which is Nakhshab, is a town beyond the river (Transoxiana). The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that its people came with the Sughd, the Turks and Kush to Sa'id ibn Uthman, with a hundred and twenty thousand men, and that they met at Bukhara and were defeated. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
