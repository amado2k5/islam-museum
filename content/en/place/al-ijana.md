## Overview
al-Ijana: a wide hollow on the edge of the Awra branch of the Tigris, on the side facing [[place/basra|Basra]], three farsakhs from it. The pre-Islamic Arabs called it al-Ijana, and the Arabs in Islam called it al-Jazzara. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

From it [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] began digging the river of al-Ubulla. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
