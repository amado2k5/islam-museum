## Overview
al-Qays: an ancient, well-built town on the west bank of the Nile. al-Idrisi mentions in it sugar cane and kinds of dates, and says that it lies about eighteen miles from Dahrut. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}
