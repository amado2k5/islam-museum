## Overview
Arzan al-Rum (Erzurum), an Islamic Armenian city on the border of the land of Rum; in 605 the Georgians besieged it and took it by the sword and burned it — per the Shadharat copy where its name appears as "Arhalas" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 22}.
