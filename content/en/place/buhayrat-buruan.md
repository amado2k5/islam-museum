## Overview
Lake Buruan: a large lake in the land of Tibet, about forty farsakhs long, with castles and fortresses on it. The people of Buruan and Awj catch fish from it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.513}
