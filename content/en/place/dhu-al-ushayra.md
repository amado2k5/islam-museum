## Overview
A place in the district of [[place/yanbu|Yanbu]]. The Prophet reached it in Jumada al-Ula in the year 2 AH to intercept the caravan of Quraysh returning from al-Sham, but it had passed him by some days, according to al-Mubarakfuri. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
