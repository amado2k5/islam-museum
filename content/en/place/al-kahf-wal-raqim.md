## Overview
al-Kahf wal-Raqim: the place where the People of the Cave slept. al-Balqa claims that it is there, and al-Umari rejects that. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.217-218}

al-Umari reports on the authority of al-Harawi that he visited it in the land of the Romans, near a ruined city called Abbasus, close to Ablastin, that is, Ephesus. It is also said that the city of Daqyanus is Toledo. al-Umari holds that what is in the land of the Romans is correct. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.217-218}
