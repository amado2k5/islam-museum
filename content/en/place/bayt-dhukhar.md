## Overview
Bayt Dhukhar is a mountain named in the account of 288 AH: [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] had climbed Jabal Bayt Dhukhar and left Muhammad ibn Abbad as his deputy at Shibam, and the opponents entered Shibam and killed Ibn Abbad. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19}

## Identifying the place
In a footnote the editor says he did not find the name in the Arab geographers he consulted, that a passage in al-Barq al-Yamani suggests it is close to Shibam, and that al-Hamdani, in Sifat Jazirat al-Arab, says Jabal Dhukhar is a large mountain in Wadi Mawr. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} Fixing it on the map needs another reference.
