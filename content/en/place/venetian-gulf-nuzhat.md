## Overview
The Venetian Gulf, which is the Adriatic, is one of two gulfs branching from the Syrian Sea, according to al-Idrisi. It begins east of Calabria at Adhrant, passes north, and then along the coast of the Venetians. Its length is about eleven hundred miles, and it contains about fifteen islands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.11}
