## Overview
Bunjikath: the greatest city of Ushrusana, a large city with a wall and a suburb, in which are a congregational mosque and a citadel. A great river with mills runs through it, and it has gardens, crops and vineyards, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.504}
