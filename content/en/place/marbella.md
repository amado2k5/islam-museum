## Summary
Marbella is a small cultivated town of al-Andalus with buildings and many fig trees, with the formidable fortress of Bishtar to its north. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Malaga to Marbella on the road to the island is forty miles; Marbella is a small cultivated town with buildings and many fig trees, and to its north stands the fortress of Bishtar, a castle of the utmost strength and fortification reached by a difficult road {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}. Its neighbour Malaga is a fine fortified city overlooked by a mountain called Jabal Fara, with an impregnable citadel and two unwalled suburbs containing inns and baths, and fig trees such as are found nowhere else {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}.
