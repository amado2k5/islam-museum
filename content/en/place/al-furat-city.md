## Overview
al-Furat city: one of the districts of the Tigris. Mujashi ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami conquered it by force, and its people came out to the Muslims with their hoes, and the Muslims gained the upper hand over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In it, Arda bint al-Harith spoke her well-known verse urging the fight. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

An additional source is needed for the date of the conquest.
