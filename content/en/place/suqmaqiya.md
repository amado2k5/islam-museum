## Overview
Suqmaqiya is a large, populous city at the northern end of the "Stinking Land". It has no king or chief; its elders and headmen manage its affairs. It stands at the top of a strong mountain and a lofty stronghold, and its people have fields and amenities at the foot of the mountain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 929} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 930}

## The mountain
Its mountain is called Tughura, and six stages from Suqmaqiya lies the city of Tughura, from which the mountain takes its name. Tughura is a medium-sized city with abundant building and a growing economy; the two cities lie east of the Stinking Land and west of the land of Sisyan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 930}

## The Stinking Land
The Stinking Land is a long, rough, black stretch ten days long and bare of plants, desolate on its sides and far-reaching, with deep water and no refuge for any inhabitant. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 929} The rivers that rise in the Asqasqa mountain, among them the river of Shahdhuruj, run on its edge. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 929}
