## Overview
Suqmaqiya is a city on top of a mountain, with farms and amenities, and its affairs are managed by its shaykhs and chiefs. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies east of the stinking land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.930}
