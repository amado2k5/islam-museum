## Overview
Bruges: a medium-sized city with many facilities and cheap prices, joined to vineyards, crops and good produce. al-Idrisi reports that it lies fifteen miles west of Qanat, and thirty miles north of Sanqalla on the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.871}
