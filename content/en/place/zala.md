## Overview
Zala is a city with a strong fortress, according to al-Idrisi. It lies nine days from the land of Mirt, eight days from the land of [[place/wadan|Wadan]], and ten days from [[place/zuwayla|Zuwayla]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.120}
