## Overview
al-Baqi al-Gharqad: the great cemetery of the city, east of the Prophet's mosque, in [[place/medina|Medina]]. Asad ibn Zurara was the first of the Muslims buried in it, according to the report of Abu Hatim transmitted by Razin, and Uthman ibn Maz'un was the first of the emigrants. Al-Samhudi mentions among its virtues that seventy thousand of its dead are sent to Paradise without reckoning, and that prayers are answered at the corner of the house of Aqil beside it. Inside it there was a mosque of Banu Hudayla on the right of whoever enters by its gate. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.91} {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.271}
