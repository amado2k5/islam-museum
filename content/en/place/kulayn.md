## Summary
A village 38 km south-west of [[place/rayy|Rayy]] in the Pashapuya district; its Persian name Kolen was arabicised with imala as Kulayn and Kulin {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400-401}.

## The nisba
It gave the nisba of [[person/al-kulayni|Muhammad ibn Ya'qub al-Kulayni]], which appears in the sources as al-Kulayni, al-Kulini, and erroneously al-Kalini {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400-401}.
