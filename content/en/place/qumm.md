## Overview
Qumm is a city in the mountains. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Abu Musa al-Ash'ari and Abd Allah ibn Qays came to it on their way back from Nihawand, stayed on it for days, and then conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the fourth century
Al-Muqaddasi relates, around the year 375 AH, that the people of Qumm were extreme Shi'a who abandoned the congregational prayers and left the congregational mosque idle, until Rukn al-Dawla obliged them to maintain it and attend it. From it they export chairs, bridles, saddles, wheat and saffron. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.292}
