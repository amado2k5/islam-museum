## Overview
It is Zahr, a place with a prison in which [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] put Abu al-Ghasham and a group of Al Ya'fur {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216}, under its governor [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-muhammad-al-hasani|Ubaydallah ibn Muhammad al-Hasani]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. Reinforcements came from its district to al-Hadi at Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}, and then its men shouted at the gate of Zahr, opened the prison and stayed there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}; from it Ibn Ya'fur was taken to Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.220}. The text writes it without vowels in most places and with them in some, and does not say where it is, so the museum puts down no coordinates. See [[event/the-men-of-zahr-break-the-prison-and-al-hadi-warns-ali-ibn-sulayman-288]].
