## Overview
al-Hawra: a village with a mine from which they cut millstones, which are carried to all parts of the earth. Its people drink from sweet wells. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
