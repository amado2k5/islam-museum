## Overview
Chamchamal is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Iraq. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281921}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2281921 with the preferred name «Chamchamal», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Iraq. Among the names it records are «جمجمال», «Shamshamāl», «Chemchemāl», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 97417. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281921}
