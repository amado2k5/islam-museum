## Overview
al-Muhammadiyya: a city that al-Mahdi built at al-Rayy in the year 158 AH, and surrounded with a trench. He made for it a suburb (faṣīl) built of fired brick, and named it al-Muhammadiyya. The people of al-Rayy came to call the inner city and its suburb the inner city and the outer city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Its surroundings
The fortress of al-Zunbadi lies within it, and after it was built the family of Lahrish settled there. The old al-Rayy was called Araziy in the pre-Islamic period. It is said that it was swallowed by the earth; it lies six farsakhs from al-Muhammadiyya, and al-Rayy is named after it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
