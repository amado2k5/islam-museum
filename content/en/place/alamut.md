## Overview
Alamut: the Ismaili fortress of the Batiniyya in the mountains near Qazvin, the home of their leader al-Hasan ibn al-Sabbah. The sources report that the armies withdrew from the siege of the Batiniyya at Alamut in the year 511 AH, when news reached them of the death of the sultan Muhammad ibn Malikshah. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 505}
