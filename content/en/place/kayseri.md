## Overview
Kayseri is a city in Turkey, ancient Caesarea in Cappadocia. The entries under the name Qaysariyya in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum are not about this city, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2285308 as both an archaeological site and a populated place, with the preferred name «Caesarea in Cappadocia» in Latin. Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Kayseri (Provinz)». It records names in German and Turkish («Kayseri»), in Greek («Καισάρεια») and in Arabic ⁦(«قيصرية»)⁩, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 308464, to Pleiades no. 629035 and to the Arachne place identifier 5943. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2285308}
