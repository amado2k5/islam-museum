## Overview
Desert oases that were once populated with crops and fruit, and whose roads were later effaced. The author mentions in them a great serpent that its people know, and a mountain whose ore is lapis lazuli, which is carried to Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 121} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 122}
