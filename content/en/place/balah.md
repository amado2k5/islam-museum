## Summary
Balah is a fine city of the land of Qarantara on the river Darwa, which al-Idrisi describes as rich in waters and rivers. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that the city of Balah belongs to the land of Qarantara on the river Darwa, a fine city with many districts and territories, abounding in waters and rivers, and he notes that he had already described it before {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883}. He adds that from it northward to the city of Balgharata is seventy miles, Balgharata lying at a distance from the said river, being handsome, eminent and flourishing, with a strong wall, busy markets, established crafts, brisk trade, many facilities and good farms rich in grains and pulses {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883}. Balah is thus one of the cities of the land of Qarantara lying between the rivers Danube and Darwa.
