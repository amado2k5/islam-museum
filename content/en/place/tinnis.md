## Overview
Tinnis is one of the cities of the Lower Egypt region. Amr ibn al-As sent to it Umayr ibn Wahb al-Jumahi, with Damietta, Tuna, Dumayra, Shatta, Daqahliyya, Bana and Buwsir. It made peace with the people of its villages on the terms of Fustat, according to al-Baladhuri. It is connected with the event of [[event/four-lieutenants-egypt|the four lieutenants in Egypt]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.304}
