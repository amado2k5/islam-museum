## Overview
Shabruna is a large, splendid city with plentiful water and farmland, thriving markets and streets, tall houses and lofty promenades, on level fertile ground, twenty miles from the river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 877}

## Distances
From Shabruna to Astarkuna, the capital of the land of Buwamiya (Bohemia), is eighty miles to the south, and the same from Astarkuna back to Shabruna. From the city of Jik Lbuqa to Shabruna is sixty miles east. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 877} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 878} From Shabruna south to Wiyana is forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 877} The next page gives eighty miles from Basu to Shabruna, between east and south. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 878}

## Note
The name is written differently in the manuscripts (Shabruna, Shiruna, Shruta, Shrufa), and the pages give no modern identification, so this entry offers none.
