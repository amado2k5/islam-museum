## Summary
A water between [[place/asfan|Asfan]] and Amaj on the road from [[place/medina|Medina]] to [[place/mecca|Mecca]]; the Prophet and the Muslim army camped at it on the conquest march in Ramadan 8 AH, where he broke his fast and the people broke theirs with him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42}.

## Its importance
The spot is bound to the traveller's licence to break the fast in Ramadan, after exertion had reached its limit with the people: the Prophet called for a vessel and drank while the people watched, so they would follow him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42}.

## For young visitors
A water-well between Asfan and Mecca where the army camped while fasting; the Prophet drank the water to teach people that long travel carries a licence to break the fast.
