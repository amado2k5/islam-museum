## Overview
Al-Mahjam is a small city, like a fortress, where its people gather. It lies on the border between the land of Dahis and the land of Yemen, eight stages from Aden and seven stages from Sana'a, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.55}
