## Overview
Khosrowshahr is a town in East Azerbaijan Province, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place and an archaeological site. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733884}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2733884 with the preferred name «Khosrowshahr» (Persian, in Latin transliteration), classed as a populated place and an archaeological site; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit East Azerbaijan Province. It records the Persian form «خسرو شهر» and links the place to GeoNames no. 127219. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733884}
