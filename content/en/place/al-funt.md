## Overview
al-Funt: two large, populous towns with established markets and abundant fruit. al-Idrisi reports that in Islam they were stopping places for caravans. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.553}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
