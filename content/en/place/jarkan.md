## Summary
Jarkan is a town of the Rashat district on the edge of Khurasan, close to Darband in size and possessing the same craftsmen, labourers and commerce. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that whoever wishes to reach the land of al-Rashat turns slightly east and travels from Washjird to Darband, a small cultivated town with markets, buildings and well-off people, and thence one stage to Jarkan, which approaches Darband in size and has the same craftsmen, workmen and trade {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}. From Jarkan to the City of the Fortress is another stage: a populous, impregnable town at the end of al-Rashat toward the Atrak country, set on a high mountain whose people are on guard against the Atrak, al-Rashat being the farthest part of Khurasan {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.490}.
