## Overview
Igriza is a middling, civilised town with markets, trade, vineyards and sheep. It neighbours Mount Mant Jun on the river Drua, according to al-Idrisi. He says it lies fifty miles north of Alma. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.874} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.875}
