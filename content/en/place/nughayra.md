## Overview
Nughayra is a city in the land of Qamnuriya in West Africa. Al-Idrisi mentions that it was once one of two populous cities there, and that civil strife and raids later destroyed it. He mentions that the road from it to Sula is about twelve days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 105} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 106}
