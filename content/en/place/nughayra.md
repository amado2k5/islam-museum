## Overview
Nughayra is one of two populous cities that once belonged to the people of Qamnuriyya, the name of one being Qanuri and of the other Nughayra. Both held nations of the Qamnuriyya and many people, with chiefs and elders who ran their affairs and judged their disputes, until the days wore them out and strife and raids came on them from every side. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 105}

## The land of Qamnuriyya
The land of Qamnuriyya lies in the north, joined on the west to the Dark Sea and on the east to the Nisr desert; across this desert runs the road of the merchants of Aghmat, Sijilmasa, Dar'a and the far Nul to the land of Ghana and the Wanqara gold-dust country joined to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 105}

## Its history
There were famous towns of the blacks there and noted capitals, but the Zaghawa and the Lamtuna of the desert who live on its two sides sought this land until they wiped out most of its people, cut off their line and scattered them through the country. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 105}
