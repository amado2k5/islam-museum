## Overview
It is a village in al-Bawn called al-Ghayl, a strong fort, where the camp of Abu Ziyad and [[person/sasaa-ibn-jafar-al-hamdani|Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far]] was when they were routed {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] fought them there hard until evening and then went back to Raydah {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. It is not [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl near Sa'da]] where the men put out of Najran were jailed in 286 AH, though the name is the same. See [[event/al-hadi-fights-abu-ziyad-and-sasaa-at-al-ghayl-in-al-bawn-288]].
