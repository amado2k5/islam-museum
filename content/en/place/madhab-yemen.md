## Overview
Madhab is, in the text's words, "a place of open land without shade or trees", where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] met [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] on a fiercely hot day {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.128}, and where the narrator reports the cloud that shaded al-Hadi and his companions. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.128} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} It is overlooked by Naqil al-Ajala, where the Khawlanis were halted. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.128} Neither text nor footnote gives its site, so no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-reconciles-khawlan-and-banu-salman-at-madhab-and-the-cloud-286]].
