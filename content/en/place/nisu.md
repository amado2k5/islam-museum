## Overview
Nisu belongs to the lands of Maqduniya (Macedonia). It is a splendid town and noble country, rich in meat, fish, honey and milk, with prices always cheap and plenty of fruit. It lies on the bank of the river Morava, which comes from the mountains of Sarbiya (Serbia), and the town has a large fine bridge over the river by which one crosses in and out. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 893}

## Neighbours
From Nisu east to the city of Atrubi is forty miles. Atrubi stands on a small river that comes from the mountains of Sarbiya and passes it on its east before falling into the Morava, so that the two run as one; that river then flows into the Danube near the town of Afrnisfa, and on it are grinding mills, orchards and vineyards. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 893}

## Setting
Elsewhere al-Idrisi says that the Morava comes out of the mountains of Sarbiya, and he puts a day between Nisu and a neighbouring city whose name is garbled in the OCR. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 795}
