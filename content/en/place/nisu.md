## Overview
Nisu is a land which al-Idrisi describes as great and a noble region, abounding in goods, honey and milk, and cheap in price. On its river there is a large, fine bridge. It is one of the cities of Macedonia. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.893}
