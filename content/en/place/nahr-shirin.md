## Overview
The River Shirin comes out of Mount Bazarang and waters the rustaq (district) of Furzuk and al-Jaladjan. It then runs on until it empties into the sea near Janaba, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.421}
