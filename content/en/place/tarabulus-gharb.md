## Overview
Tarabulus (the West) is a fortified city with a wall of white stone, with fine streets and well-made markets. It has olives, figs, palms and many fruits. According to al-Idrisi, King Roger conquered it, and it is under his obedience. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.297}
