## Overview
al-Kiraj: a city in Sind. Duhar ibn Dahir went out to it, but Muhammad ibn al-Qasim defeated him, and its people came down under his judgement; he killed and took captives. The people of Sind made an image of Muhammad there when he was brought in chains. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
