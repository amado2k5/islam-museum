## Summary
Aghriyimis was a sea-side city in the northern island lands, named by al-Idrisi among the towns of England and the islands adjoining it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.946}

## Details
Al-Idrisi lists among the chief towns of the southern side of that island Sihstar, Grimsby-like names, Hintuna, Shirham and Aghriyimis and Lundris among others {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.880}. He then gives the distances: from Nirghiq to Aghriyimis on the sea eighty miles, and from Jarnmudha to Aghriyimis on the sea a hundred and fifty miles, where the coast turns northward {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.946}. He adds that from Aghriyimis to Afruik is eighty miles, and that a river cuts through that region and empties into the sea just south of Aghriyimis {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.947}.
