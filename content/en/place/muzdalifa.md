## Overview
Muzdalifa is a site between 'Arafa and [[place/mina|Mina]]. Pilgrims spend the night there on the night of sacrifice. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## For Sprouts
Muzdalifa is the place between Arafa and Mina where the pilgrims spend the eve of the sacrifice night.

## For Explorers
Muzdalifa is the place between Arafa and Mina where the pilgrims sleep on the eve of the sacrifice, after standing at Arafa and before stoning the pillars at Mina.

## For Scholars
Muzdalifa is the place between Arafa and Mina where the pilgrims sleep on the eve of the sacrifice — the station between the daytime standing at Arafa and the stoning of Jamrat al-Aqaba at Mina on the morning of the tenth.
