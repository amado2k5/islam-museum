## Overview
al-Dimas: a building in Antioch, on the right of its congregational mosque. al-Mas'udi describes it as built of brick and stone, and great in its construction. On some summer nights the moon enters through one of its doors as it rises. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}

al-Mas'udi also reports that it was built by the Persians when they ruled Antioch, and that it was a fire-temple for them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}
