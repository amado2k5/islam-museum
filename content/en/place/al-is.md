## Overview
al-Is: a place between Yanbu and al-Marwa near the Red Sea coast. The expedition of Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib reached it in Ramadan in the year 1 AH, and there it met a caravan of Quraysh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}

The book does not fix its coordinates, so its location remains approximate.
