## Overview
al-Is: a place between Yanbu and al-Marwa near the Red Sea coast. The expedition of Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib reached it in Ramadan in the year 1 AH, and there it met a caravan of Quraysh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}

The book does not fix its coordinates, so its location remains approximate.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes al-Is as a place in the land of Banu Sulaym with a water source called Dhanban al-Is. He cites Ibn Ishaq's account of Abu Basir, who halted at al-Is in the area of Dhu al-Marwa on the sea shore, on the road Quraysh used to take from there to Syria. He also records the dual form, al-Isan, as a mine of Banu Numayr ibn Ka'b near Adakh al-Barm, where people of Banu Hanifa lived. The name belongs to more than one place, which he distinguishes. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.173}
