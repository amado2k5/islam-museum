## Overview
Sanbat is a populous city with trade and profits, on the western bank of the Nile. Al-Idrisi describes it as a city of extended wealth and blessings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.335}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut says the common people pronounce the name Sanbat, and that it is also called Sunbutiyya and Sunmutiyya; he describes it as a pleasant small town on the island of Qusniya, in the districts of Egypt. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.261}
