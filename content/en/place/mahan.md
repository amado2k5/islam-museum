## Overview
Mahan is a place with the tomb of Shah Nematollah Vali; Pleiades records the place as a tomb, a monument and the findplace of a cuneiform inscription. {cite:pleiades 878177648}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Māhān» (Pleiades no. 878177648), classed as a tomb and a monument, with activity attested from 1700 CE to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. Its description says that it holds the tomb of Shah Nematollah Vali and is the findplace of a cuneiform inscription. It records one name, «Māhān» (in Arabic script «ماهان»), in Persian, from 1700 to 2100 CE. {cite:pleiades 878177648}
