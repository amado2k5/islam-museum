## Overview
Asal is a place in the land of [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]] where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}, pitched his tents, was met by [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] and his son [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali]] with the Khawlan force, and consulted the guardians of the slain by night. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.128} The editor refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab p. 82 {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}; the source was not read, so no coordinates are given. See [[event/al-hadi-reconciles-khawlan-and-banu-salman-at-madhab-and-the-cloud-286]].
