## Overview
al-Salsal: a water in the land of [[group/judham|Judham]]. Ibn Ishaq says that when [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] was in charge of it he was afraid, so he sent to the Prophet asking for reinforcements. The expedition of [[event/expedition-of-dhat-al-salasil|Dhat al-Salasil]] was named after this water. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/272}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- The position of the water is not specified in the text, and it has no coordinates; the naming of the expedition by sources other than Ibn Ishaq is explained differently (needs_source).
