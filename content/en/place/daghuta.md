## Overview
Daghuta: a city on a great creek, according to al-Idrisi. He describes its people as naked, covering their hands when they meet merchants, and its women as veiled. Gold is there as it is in other places, and it lies one channel from the Island of the Moon. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.79}
