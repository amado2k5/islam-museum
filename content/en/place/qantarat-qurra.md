## Overview
The Bridge of Qurra is in [[place/basra|Basra]], and is attributed to [[person/qurra-ibn-habban|Qurra ibn Habban]] al-Bahili. Beside it there was an old river, which the mother of Abd Allah ibn Amir bought and gave in charity as a reservoir for the people of Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abd Allah ibn Amir bought the market and likewise gave it in charity. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
