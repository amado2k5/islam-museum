## Overview
Safwan is a valley in the area of [[place/badr|Badr]]. The Prophet reached it in Rabi' al-Awwal in the year 2 AH, while he was pursuing Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri, who had raided the pastures of [[place/medina|Medina]]. He did not catch him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

Its site has not yet been fixed with coordinates, as the book does not give them.
