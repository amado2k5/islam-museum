## Overview
Safwan is a valley in the area of [[place/badr|Badr]]. The Prophet reached it in Rabi' al-Awwal in the year 2 AH, while he was pursuing Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri, who had raided the pastures of [[place/medina|Medina]]. He did not catch him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

Its site has not yet been fixed with coordinates, as the book does not give them.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Safwan first as a water a stage away from the Mirbad gate of Basra, where the wind blows up a great deal of dust, which gives it its name. He adds a verse about a girl at Safwan of Basra, and notes that the Basran Safwan is the one al-Nabigha al-Ja'di had in mind. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.225}

As a valley near Badr, Safwan is the place where, according to Ibn Ishaq, the Prophet went out after Kurz ibn Jabir raided his herds and the herds of Medina. The Prophet reached the valley, but Kurz escaped. Yaqut says this was the first Badr expedition, in Jumada I of the second year after the Hijra. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.225}
