## Overview
al-Madhar: a town in the districts of the Tigris. Utba ibn Ghazwan came to it, and its governor came out to fight him. God defeated him, most of those with him were drowned, and he was taken prisoner, and Utba struck off his neck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
