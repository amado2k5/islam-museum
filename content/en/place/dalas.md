## Overview
Dalas: a city on the east bank of most of the Nile. al-Idrisi reports that the polished coats of mail attributed to its craft were known by it, and that the Berbers and the worst of the Arabs spoiled its building, so that few of its people remained. This is the author's account of its history. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.131}
