## Overview
Malal is a small city without a wall, more like a large village. It stands on a mound of red earth that protects it, and its people fortify themselves there against the raids of other Sudanese peoples. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.22}

Its people drink from a spring that flows from a mountain to its south, and its water is brackish. Al-Idrisi mentions that some of its people are taken captive and sold, and that all of them are marked with fire on their faces. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.22}
