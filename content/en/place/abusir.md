## Overview
Abusir, in Giza in Egypt, is an archaeological site, an Old Kingdom necropolis that served the ancient city of Memphis. The first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan hold a Busir entry for four villages of Egypt, but it cannot be tied to this site, so this page keeps to what the Pleiades gazetteer records. {cite:pleiades 195443086}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Abu Sir» (Pleiades no. 195443086), classed as a cemetery, with its recorded location dated from 2670 BCE to 2168 BCE, the Old Kingdom. It records one name in the forms «Abu Sir», «Abusir», «Abū Şīr» and «ابوصير», from 1700 to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. Its description says that this is an Old Kingdom necropolis serving the ancient Egyptian city of Memphis. {cite:pleiades 195443086}
