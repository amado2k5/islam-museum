## Summary
Ghulwi was a town of the land of Rumaniyya — a pleasant, settled district which travellers sought out, bringing it goods and trade that circulated continually, says al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}

## Details
Al-Idrisi lists Ghulwi among the towns of the clime of Rumaniyya together with Mighali Tharma, Bistarnas, Afli, Aleksyubli, Disina and others. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892} From the city of Mighali Tharma to Aytu-qastru was half a day, from it to Ghulwi half a day, and from Ghulwi to the town of Basiqa half a day — a small town with busy markets, populous and inhabited; from Basiqa to the town of Aqli was half a day, and from Aqli to Istilifnus one day. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}
