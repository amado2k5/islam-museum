## Overview
al-Rumiyya: a place on the Tigris near al-Mada'in. al-Baladhuri reports that Sa'd remained there until its people made peace with him: whoever wished to leave might leave, and whoever stayed in obedience, paying the kharaj and guiding the Muslims, would remain. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.366-370}
