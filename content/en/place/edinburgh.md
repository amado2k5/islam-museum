## Overview
Edinburgh is the capital city of Scotland in the United Kingdom. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2088774}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2088774 with the preferred name «Edinburgh», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2084695. Among the names it records are «إدنبرة» in Arabic, «Edimburgo» in Spanish and Italian, «Édimbourg» in French and «Edimburgum» in Latin. It links the place to GeoNames no. 2650225, to Arachne entries and to a German Authority File (GND) identifier. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2088774}
