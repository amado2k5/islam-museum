## Overview
Abaskun: a fine and good town on the border of [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]]. Al-Idrisi notes that roads run from it to al-Abwab, al-Jil and the Daylam. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.688}
