## Overview
Sirt is a city in sandy land two miles from the sea. It has remnants of palms but no olives, and many figs. Its wells are few, so its people depend on rainwater stored in cisterns (mawajil), according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.298}
