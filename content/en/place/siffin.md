## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut places Siffin near Raqqa on the western bank of the Euphrates, between Raqqa and Balis, and says that the battle of Siffin was fought between Ali and Muawiya in the month of Safar in the year 37 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.414}

He records the disagreement over the size of the two armies: one account has Muawiya with one hundred and twenty thousand men and Ali with ninety thousand, and another reverses the figures. He considers the second the sounder, with Ali at one hundred and twenty thousand and Muawiya at ninety thousand. He reports seventy thousand dead, a stay at Siffin of one hundred and ten days, and ninety engagements. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.414}
