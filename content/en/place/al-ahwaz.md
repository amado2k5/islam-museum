## Overview
Al-Ahwaz: a town known as Hormuz Shahr, and the capital of the land of Khuzistan. Al-Idrisi describes its site as spacious and its air as healthy. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 392}

## Its people
Al-Idrisi says that it has abundant livelihoods and many good things, and that its people are wealthy and civilised Arabs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 392}
