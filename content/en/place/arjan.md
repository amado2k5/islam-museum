## Overview
Arjan: a city in the land of Persia, according to al-Idrisi. From Arjan to the village of [[place/asak-khuzistan|Asak]] are two stages, to al-Dawraq one stage, and then to al-Basyan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.401}
