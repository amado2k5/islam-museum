## Overview
Al-Bara is a town and district in the region of Aleppo. Yaqut records it in the Mu'jam al-buldan and distinguishes it from another al-Bara in the district of Algeciras in al-Andalus. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.320}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says al-Bara is a small town and a district in the region of Aleppo, with a fortress, rich in gardens, and that it is called Zawiyat al-Bara. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.320}

He then says al-Bara is also the name of a district of the province of al-Jazira al-Khadra' in al-Andalus, with lofty mountains, whose people have risen in revolt from long ago to his day, a land of fruit rather than of grain. That district is not the Aleppo town; Yaqut puts both under one heading because the name is shared. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.320}
