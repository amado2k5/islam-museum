## Summary
Miqra is a small town of the Maghreb with fields and grain whose people grow flax in plenty, one stage from al-Masila. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.263}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that one leaves al-Masila for Miqra, one stage, a small town with fields and grain whose people cultivate flax, which is abundant among them; from Miqra to Tabna is one stage, between Tabna and Bijaya are six stages, and from Tabna to Baghay four stages {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.263}. Tabna, linked to it, is the city of the Zab: a fine town with much water, gardens, crops, cotton, wheat and barley, walled with earth, inhabited by mixed peoples and possessing crafts, trade and wealth {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.263}.
