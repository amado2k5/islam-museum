## Overview
Atwa is a place to which the people advised [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] to move from [[place/madar-yemen|Madar]]; he halted there, stayed some days and sent criers from it among Hamdan {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.247}, and the encounter of Tuesday, the first of Rajab, took place there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.247} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248} ([[event/the-fights-near-bayt-hadir-and-the-withdrawal-by-matara-and-madar-to-atwa-290]], [[event/al-tarif-attack-al-hadi-at-atwa-on-1-rajab-and-the-hamdani-wing-gives-way-290]]). The book gives neither its distance from Madar nor coordinates, and the OCR and the page image agree on the reading of the name.
