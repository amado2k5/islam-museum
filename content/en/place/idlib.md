## Overview
Idlib is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2280353 with the preferred name «Idlib», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Nahiyat Idlib». Among its recorded names are «Idleb» and the Arabic-script form «إدلب». It links the place to GeoNames no. 169389 and GND no. 4256813-4. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2280353}
