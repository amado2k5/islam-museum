## Summary
Bahrain, officially the Kingdom of Bahrain, is a sovereign island country and a constitutional monarchy. It became independent on 14 August 1971 and has been a kingdom since 14 February 2002. It is a member of the United Nations, the Arab League and the Gulf Cooperation Council, and its official language is Arabic. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en} {cite:wikidata-bahrain}

## Name
The official name is "Kingdom of Bahrain"; in Arabic مملكة البحرين, transliterated Mamlakat al-Baḥrayn, and in French Royaume de Bahreïn. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en} {cite:wikidata-bahrain} This record is for the modern state. The museum has a separate record for the historic region of the same name in eastern Arabia, [[place/bahrayn|Bahrayn, the historic region]]. The two sources summarised here were not read for any link between the two, so nothing is said here about how they relate.

## Location
Wikidata gives the coordinate location of the state as 26°4′3″ N, 50°33′4″ E, about 26.0675 N, 50.5511 E, and its geographic centre as 26°2′22.9″ N, 50°33′33.5″ E {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. English Wikipedia places Manama, the capital, at 26.217 N, 50.583 E {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en}. Manama has its own record, [[place/manama|Manama]]. The coordinates are recorded as approximate, because they come from a research summary and were not checked against the pages or a gazetteer.

## Political status and dates
Wikidata describes Bahrain as a sovereign state, an island country and an archipelago, and a constitutional monarchy {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. English Wikipedia describes it as a sovereign island country and unitary constitutional monarchy, independent on 14 August 1971 and a kingdom since 14 February 2002 {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en}. Wikidata gives its United Nations membership as beginning on 21 September 1971 {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. These are Gregorian dates; the two sources, as summarised, give no Hijri equivalents, so none is stated.

## Memberships
English Wikipedia says Bahrain is a member of the United Nations, the Arab League and the Gulf Cooperation Council {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en}. Wikidata gives United Nations membership from 21 September 1971 {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. The summary gives no dates of joining the League or the Council, so none is stated here. Bahrain's joining the Arab League in 1971 is stated in another record, on language: see [[concept/al-arabiyya-fi-bahrain|Arabic in Bahrain]].

## Languages
The official language is Arabic, and Wikidata cites Article 2 of the Constitution {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. English Wikipedia says English is widely used and that Bahrani Arabic is the most widely spoken dialect {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-en}. Wikidata lists the languages used as Modern Standard Arabic, Bahrani Arabic, English and Malay {cite:wikidata-bahrain}. The museum has a record on the subject, [[concept/al-arabiyya-fi-bahrain|Arabic in Bahrain]], and records for [[language/arabic|Arabic]] and [[language/malay|Malay]]. Each statement is ascribed to its source, and no number of speakers is estimated.
