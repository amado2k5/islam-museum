## Overview
Jahram is a land in Fars. According to al-Baladhuri, a group gathered against [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] in its district, and he defeated them and conquered the land of Jahram. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
