## Summary
Jahram, a town of Fars, where a host massed against [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]]; he scattered them and took the land of Jahram, in al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Uthman ibn Abi al-As came to Darabjird — where the Shadhrawan was the banner of their faith under the Hirbadh — and the Hirbadh made peace with him for money and on condition that the people of Darabjird be treated like the rest of Fars's conquered folk. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547} A host then massed against him in the Jahram area, so he scattered them and took the land of Jahram, after which Uthman came to Fasa, whose great man made peace on the same terms as Darabjird's sulh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547} Jahram is thus, in his account, a district of Fars whose people rose to fight Uthman after the Darabjird settlement; he dispersed them and opened its land before moving on to Fasa and then to the city of Sabur in year 23 — or 24 by another report. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 547}
