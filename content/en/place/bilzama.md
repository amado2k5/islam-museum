## Overview
Bilzama: a fortress near [[place/constantine|Constantine]], with a suburb, a market and wells. It stands in the middle of a wide plain, and its building is of great stones. al-Idrisi describes its people as having honour and strength. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.270}
