## Overview
Istijja is the name of an old district of al-Andalus south-west of Cordoba, the same as present-day Écija in the province of Seville. Yaqut gives it a short article in the Mu'jam al-buldan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.174}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut spells the name with a kasra on the first letter, a sukun on the second, a kasra on the ta, then jim and ha, and says it names a district of al-Andalus joined to the lands of Rayya, to the south and west of Cordoba. He describes it as an old district with broad countryside and farmland on the river Sanjil, which is the river of Granada, ten farsakhs from Cordoba, its lands joining those of Cordoba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.174}

He names one man connected with it, Muhammad ibn Layth al-Istijji, a hadith transmitter mentioned by Abu Sa'id ibn Yunus in his history, who died in 328. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.174}
