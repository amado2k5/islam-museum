## Summary
Bi'r al-Rawha' is a well on the Medina–Mecca road. The Prophet reached it on the march to Badr and, departing from it, left the Mecca road to the left; it is also the point from which he sent Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir back to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}

## Details
On the expedition of the great battle of Badr, the Prophet left Ibn Umm Maktum in charge of Medina and of the prayer; when he reached al-Rawha' he sent Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir back to the city. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}

On the road to Badr he took the main highway leading to Mecca until he reached the well of al-Rawha'; on leaving it he abandoned the Mecca road to the left and took the Iraq road, continuing to Rahqan, between al-Naziya and the defile of al-Safra'. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205} Bi'r al-Rawha' is thus a junction in the story of Badr: the point where the march turned from the road to Mecca onto the road to Badr, and where a second deputy was assigned over Medina after Ibn Umm Maktum. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}
