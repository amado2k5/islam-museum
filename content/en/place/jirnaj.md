## Summary
Jirnaj is a small town of the Marw district with markets, well-off merchants and a pulpit, standing nine miles from Marw on the riverbank. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Jirnaj among the towns of Marw: a small town with markets, prosperous merchants and a pulpit, nine miles from Marw, three miles short of Zaraq, on the bank of the river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}. Of that district's towns he also names Hurmuzfarrah, one stage west of Marw with a pulpit, gardens and crops, and al-Dandanaqan, two stages from Marw on the Marakhas road, a fine town with wall, keep, markets, baths, inns and a congregational mosque with pulpit {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}. Jirnaj thus lies in the web of Khurasanian towns around Marw.
