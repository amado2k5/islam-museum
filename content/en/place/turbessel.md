## Overview
Turbessel, known in Arabic as Tell Bashir, is a place that Pleiades identifies with the unwalled town named in Neo-Assyrian sources, Til-Bašerê. {cite:pleiades 404687440}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Til-Bašerê» (Pleiades no. 404687440), classed as a settlement, with a time span from 3000 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «تل باشر» in Arabic, «Turbessel» in English and «Til-bašerê» in Akkadian. It describes the place as an unwalled town mentioned in Neo-Assyrian sources that is identified with Tell Bašir. {cite:pleiades 404687440}
