## Overview
al-Tha'labiyya: a place on the road to Iraq. al-Baladhuri reports that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas stayed there for three months until the people caught up with him, and then came to al-Mudhayyib in the year 15 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}

It is also said that Jarir ibn Abd Allah took the road by Fayd and al-Tha'labiyya to al-Mudhayyib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.356}
