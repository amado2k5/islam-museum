## Overview
Nakhchivan is a city in Azerbaijan, the capital of the Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic, and this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 1008147) records this place under the preferred name «Nachitschewan» [German], typed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Aserbaidschan», an administrative unit. Further names include «Naxçıvan» [Turkish]; «نخجوان» [Persian]; «Naxuana» [Latin]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 147429, GND 4314454-8. {cite:idai-gazetteer 1008147}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the spelling of the name letter by letter, says that some pronounce it Naqjuwan, and that the adjective derived from it, nashwi, departs from the name's original form. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.276} He describes it as a land at the far end of Azerbaijan and refers the reader to another place in his book where it is treated. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.276}
