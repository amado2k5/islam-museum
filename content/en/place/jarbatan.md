## Summary
Jarbatan is a populous town on a small creek in the land of India, a country of much rice and grain, lying five stages from Fandarina. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.192}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the town of Fandarina to the station of Jarbatan is five stages; it is a populous town on a small creek, a land of abundant rice and grain, and he reports that it supplies provisions to Sarandib, while pepper trees grow in plenty on its mountains {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.192}. From Jarbatan to Danja and Kalkasar is a two-day journey, two populous neighbouring towns on the sea also rich in rice and grain, and from them to Kalkiyan one day {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.192}. It thus stands among the fertile trading lands along that coast.
