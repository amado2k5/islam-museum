## Overview
Cambridge is a town in England, in the United Kingdom, which the iDAI.gazetteer records as a populated place and lists under the Latin name Cantabrigia. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087878}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2087878 with the preferred name «Cambridge», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Cantabrigia» in Latin, «كامبريدج» in Arabic and «Cambridge» in English, German, French and other languages. It links the place to GeoNames no. 2653941, to Arachne place 518 and to a German National Library authority record (4009351-7). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087878}
