## Overview
Lower Sus is a region behind Tangier, and between it and Upper Sus there are a little over twenty days. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Uqba ibn Nafi toured in it during his second governorship of the Maghrib, and no one confronted or fought him. The horses of Musa ibn Nusayr reached it, and he settled its people by force and took captives from them. The place is connected with [[event/conquest-of-tanja|the conquest of Tangier]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.320}
