## Overview
Al-Sughd is the land beyond the river (Transoxiana), and its greatest city is [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]]. Its people came with the Turks and the people of Kush and Nasaf to Sa'id ibn Uthman, with a hundred and twenty thousand men. They met near [[place/bukhara|Bukhara]] and were defeated, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
