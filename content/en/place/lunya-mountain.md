## Overview
Mount Lunya is a high mountain in the land of the Zaghawa, whose soil is white and soft. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.111}

## What al-Idrisi reports
On the mountain is a cave that people avoid, because of a report of a great serpent in it. At the foot of the mountain is water that flows a little and then stops. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.116}

The report of the serpent is a story that people tell, and it needs an additional source.
