## Overview
Qalari is a town in Sind, on the western bank of the river Mihran. Al-Idrisi describes it as a good, prudent town, fine in its features, rich in its produce and profitable in its trade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171}

## Setting
Qalari lies to the west of the Mihran, one stage from al-Mansura. Before the river reaches the town, it divides into two branches. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.168} The river runs west of al-Mansura, and its second branch rejoins the first about twelve miles below al-Mansura. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171}

## Distances
Two stages separate Qalari from Ari. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171} From Qalari to al-Mansura is a long stage of forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171} From Qalari to Shirusan is three stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171}

## The people and the road
Qalari is set back from the main road, but many travellers seek it because its people deal fairly. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171} Al-Idrisi lists Qalari among the towns of Sind in this part of the seventh section, with al-Daybul, al-Nairun and al-Mansura. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.166} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.169}
