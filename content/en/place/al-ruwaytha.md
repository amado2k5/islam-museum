## Summary
Al-Ruwaytha was a watering place in the Arabian desert on the Hijaz road, having four rain pools but no settled population. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}

## Details
Al-Idrisi records on the Hijaz routes that from al-Saqya to al-Ruwaytha is thirty-six miles, and there it has four pools of water but no inhabitants {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}. For him it is a desert watering station where travellers draw on its ponds, not a settled village; it appears in his itinerary of the road's halts and waters between Mecca and the lands beyond.  The recording of such places shows the old travellers' care in fixing water points along the roads, for pools and wells were the true landmarks of the desert; al-Idrisi therefore entered it among the road's halts and watering places even though it held neither buildings nor dwellers.
