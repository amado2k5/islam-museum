## Overview
Isabadh is a district in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. According to al-Baladhuri it was named after Isa ibn al-Mahdi, and it was in the care of the Turk Manazil, who was the son of al-Khayzuran. Al-Mahdi stayed most often in Isabadh, in buildings he built there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
