## Overview
Zafar, the chief town of Yakhsub, is a city in Yemen. Al-Idrisi mentions it in a passage on the distances from the market of [[place/ukaz|Ukaz]] to [[place/najran|Najran]] and to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]]. {cite:idrisi-nuzhat-1592 p.63}

Its location is not fixed by coordinates, and it is not known whether it is the coastal Zafar of Ibn Battuta. The page is taken from a scan and needs matching against the image. An additional source is needed.
