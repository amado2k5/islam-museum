## Overview
Tuz Khormato is a city in Iraq. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the Pleiades gazetteer records. {cite:pleiades 320150993}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ṭūz Khūrmātū» (Pleiades no. 320150993), classed as a findspot. It records one name in the forms «Ṭūz Khūrmātū» and «طوز خورماتو», from 1700 to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. Its description says only that this is the find place of a cuneiform inscription. Its location rests on two sources, OpenStreetMap and CIGS data. {cite:pleiades 320150993}
