## Overview
The River of al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir, ruler of al-Hira, at al-Qandal, is an inlet of the Tigris which Sulayman ibn Ali dammed. It was granted (as an iqta') to al-Nu'man in the days of Kisra, and there was a palace of his there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
