## Overview
Andarab: a city on two rivers, one of which is called the Andarab. It has gardens, pleasure grounds, many trees and vineyards, and it lies seven stages from Badakhshan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.484}
