## Summary
Djibouti, officially the Republic of Djibouti, is a sovereign state and a unitary presidential republic. It became independent from France on 27 June 1977, its constitution dates from 4 September 1992, and it is a member of the United Nations. Its official languages are Arabic and French, and its national languages are Somali and Afar. {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en} {cite:wikidata-djibouti}

## Name
The official name in English is "Republic of Djibouti"; in Arabic جمهورية جيبوتي, in French République de Djibouti, in Afar Gabuutih Ummuuno and in Somali Jamhuuriyadda Jabuuti. {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en} Wikidata gives the native or official names as جمهورية جيبوتي, République de Djibouti, Jabuuti and Gabuuti {cite:wikidata-djibouti}.

## Location
Wikidata gives the coordinate location of the state as 11°48′0.00″ N, 42°25′59.99″ E, about 11.8 N, 42.4333 E, and its geographic centre as 11°49′30.360″ N, 42°35′25.080″ E {cite:wikidata-djibouti}. English Wikipedia places Djibouti City, the capital, at 11.600 N, 43.167 E {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en}. The coordinates are recorded as approximate, because they come from a research summary and were not checked against the pages or a gazetteer. The museum has no separate record for Djibouti City.

## Political status and dates
English Wikipedia describes Djibouti as a sovereign country and a unitary presidential republic, independent from France on 27 June 1977, with a constitution dated 4 September 1992 {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en}. Wikidata describes it as a sovereign state and gives its United Nations membership as beginning on 20 September 1977 {cite:wikidata-djibouti}. These are Gregorian dates; the two sources, as summarised, give no Hijri equivalents, so none is stated.

## Memberships
Wikidata says Djibouti has been a member of the United Nations since 20 September 1977 and is a member of the Arab League, the African Union and the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie {cite:wikidata-djibouti}. English Wikipedia gives United Nations membership {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en}. The summary gives no dates of joining the League, the Union or the Organisation, so none is stated here. For the League see [[concept/al-alam-al-arabi|The Arab states and the League of Arab States]].

## Languages
The official languages are Arabic and French, and the national languages are Somali and Afar {cite:wikipedia-djibouti-en}. Wikidata lists the official languages as French and Arabic and the languages used as Modern Standard Arabic, Somali, French and Afar {cite:wikidata-djibouti}. The museum has a record on the subject, [[concept/al-arabiyya-fi-djibouti|Arabic in Djibouti]], and records for [[language/arabic|Arabic]] and [[language/somali|Somali]]. Each statement is ascribed to its source and no number of speakers is estimated; the summary says no more of Afar than its name.
