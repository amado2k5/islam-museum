## Overview
Laqant (Alicante) is a small, populous town with a market and a mosque. Esparto grass is carried from it to all the lands of the sea, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.558}

On it is a fine, lofty, impregnable fortress on the top of a mountain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.558}
