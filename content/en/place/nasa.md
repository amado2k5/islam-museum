## Overview
Nasa is a town in Khurasan. The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that Ibn Khazim conquered its district of Humranduz, and that the lord of Nasa came to him and made peace for three hundred thousand dirhams, said also to be on the basis of what the land could bear in kharaj, on condition that he kill no one and take no one captive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says that Nasa is pronounced with a short first vowel and is the same word as the sciatic vein, 'irq al-nasa. Ibn al-Sikkit says the vein is called al-nasa, not 'irq al-nisa. The name of the town, he thinks, is foreign. Abu Sa'd gives a story about its name: when the Muslims reached Khurasan and made for it, its people fled, and only the women stayed behind. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.281}
