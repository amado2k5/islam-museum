## Overview
Nasa is a town in Khurasan. The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that Ibn Khazim conquered its district of Humranduz, and that the lord of Nasa came to him and made peace for three hundred thousand dirhams, said also to be on the basis of what the land could bear in kharaj, on condition that he kill no one and take no one captive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
