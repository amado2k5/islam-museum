## Overview
Al-Hilla is a city in Iraq on the Euphrates, near Babylon. Ibn Jubayr passed through it on his way to Syria. {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}

The coordinates match a modern town of the same name; an additional source is needed to confirm the identification.
