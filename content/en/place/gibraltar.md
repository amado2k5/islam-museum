## Overview
The Strait is the sea passage between al-Andalus and the Maghrib. Gibraltar is named after Tariq ibn Ziyad, because he landed there on a Saturday in Sha'ban 92 AH; the common people call it the Mountain of the Conquest. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.171-172}

## The account of Alexander
Al-Maqqari reports that some historians said Alexander ordered engineers to weigh the surface of the Atlantic and the Syrian Sea, and they found the Atlantic slightly higher. He dug between Tangier and al-Andalus and built two quays between them, six miles apart, and then let the water in between, so it entered the Syrian Sea. The people of the two islands call the quay that lies by the land of al-Andalus the causeway. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.171-172}

This is the account of the historians as al-Maqqari gives it. The museum does not vouch for its truth.
