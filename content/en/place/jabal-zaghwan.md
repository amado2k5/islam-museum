## Overview
Mount Zaghwan is a high mountain between [[place/tunis|Tunis]] and [[place/qayrawan|al-Qayrawan]], visible from the back of the sea because of its height. It has fertility, farms and settlement, and it is inhabited by scattered tribes, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.294}
