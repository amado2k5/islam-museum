## Overview
Al-Hajar is a village of Banu al-Harith at Najran, one of their three camps. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} Al-Hadi made for it once the reinforcements came; the narrator says his lodging was next to the village, and he opened a breach in the wall of the fort and entered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.164} The index lists it at pp. 161 and 178 among others. It has no coordinates, and the museum does not confuse it with [[place/hajr-yamama|Hajr of al-Yamama]] or the coast. See [[event/al-hadi-breaches-the-wall-of-al-hajar-286]].
