## Overview
Tus is a city in Khurasan. Its marzban Kanazbak made peace with [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]] over it for six hundred thousand dirhams, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2110058) records this place under the preferred name «Tus», typed as a populated place and an archaeological site. It sits under «Chorāsān-e Razavi (Ostan)» in the gazetteer's hierarchy, and among the names it records are «Shahr-e Ţūs», «Shahr-e Ţūs-e Bālā». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2110058}
