## Overview
It is the land of Qudum to which Al Ya'fur and Al Tarif agreed to send two men of Al Ya'fur; the two went out by night and its people gathered to them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216}. Qudum is said to have joined the two sons of Abu al-Khayr {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}, and the Qudumis who broke the prison of Shibam came from it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}. The text gives neither a place nor a distance, so the museum puts down no coordinates and does not mix it with [[place/qadam-sharif]], a different place. See [[group/qudumis]] and [[event/al-yafur-and-al-tarif-send-two-men-to-qudum-and-sasaa-seizes-the-horses-at-al-bawn-288]].
