## Summary
In Yaqut's entry, Sina is a place in Sham to which the word Tur is added, so that it is called Tur Sina, the mountain on which God spoke to Musa ibn Imran; it is rich in trees. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.300} Yaqut reports al-Jawhari's statement that it is a mountain in Sham, {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.48} and he places a mountain near Aila where a village was opened by a treaty in the ninth year of the Hijra. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.48}

## Identification
The record identifies the peninsula from its Wikidata item. Yaqut's text describes the mountain and its place, not the whole peninsula.
