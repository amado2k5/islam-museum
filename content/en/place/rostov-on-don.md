## Overview
Rostov-on-Don is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Russia. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2095672}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2095672 with the preferred name «Rostov-na-Donu», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Russia. Among the names it records are «Ростов», «Ростів», «顿河畔罗斯托夫», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 501175. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2095672}
