## Overview
Erzurum is a city in eastern Turkey. Pleiades traces its antecedents to at least the Urartian period, notes that in late Roman times it was known as Theodosiopolis, and records a medieval fortress on a prominent hill in the modern city centre. {cite:pleiades 874373}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Erzurum» (Pleiades no. 874373), classed as a fort and a settlement, with a time span from 1200 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «Theodosiopolis» in Greek, «Arzan ar-Rūm» in transliterated Arabic and «Erzurum» in Turkish. It describes the place as a modern city in eastern Turkey with antecedents at least as early as the Urartian period, known in late Roman times as Theodosiopolis, with a medieval fortress on a prominent hill in the modern city centre. {cite:pleiades 874373}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2101814 with the preferred name «Erzurum», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Erzurum (Provinz)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «أرضروم» in Arabic, «Arzan-ar-Rum», «Ard-ar-Rum», «Karin» and «Erzurum» in Turkish and English. It links the place to GeoNames no. 315368, to Pleiades no. 874373 and to a German National Library authority record (4070958-9). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2101814}
