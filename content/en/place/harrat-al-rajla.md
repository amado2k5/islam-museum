## Overview
The lava field to which the people of [[person/rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|Rifa'a ibn Zayd al-Judhami]] went, and where they settled after they answered and became Muslims. See [[event/islam-of-rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|the Islam of Rifa'a and his people]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243}
