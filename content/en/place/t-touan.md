## Overview
Tétouan is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2770734 with the preferred name «Tatuan», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Marokko» (an administrative unit). Among its recorded names are «Tétouan» and «tiṭwān» and the Arabic-script form «تطوان». It records no external identifiers for this entry. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2770734}
