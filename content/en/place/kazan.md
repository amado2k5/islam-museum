## Overview
Kazan is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Tatarstan. The gazetteer's comment, in German, describes it as the capital of the Republic of Tatarstan in Russia and notes that the word kazan means a cauldron in Tatar and Turkish. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2068865}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2068865 with the preferred name «Kasan», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Tatarstan. Among the names it records are «Казань», «Kazan'» and «Казан», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 551487. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2068865}
