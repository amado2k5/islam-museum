## Overview
al-Taylasan: a region in the mountains of Daylam. Kathir ibn Shihab invaded it together with al-Babar; it was one of the places of raiding that the governor of al-Rayy could reach. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the spelling of the name letter by letter, quotes al-Layth on the vowelling of its form, and quotes al-Asma'i that it is an Arabized Persian word whose original is talashan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.56} He says that Taylasan is a wide region with many towns and inhabitants, among the districts of the Daylam and the Khazar, conquered by al-Walid ibn Uqba in the year 35 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.56}
