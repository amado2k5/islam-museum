## Summary
A spot between Mecca and Ta'if where the Prophet held the captives and spoils of [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn]]; where he received [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]]'s delegation and returned to them six thousand captives; where he divided the fay' among Quraysh's nobles and Najd's chiefs to reconcile their hearts; and from which he entered ihram for his umra in Dhu al-Qa'da 8 AH {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/131-143}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
The Prophet camped at al-Ji'rana after returning from the siege of Ta'if, where more than a hundred thousand head of livestock and captives had gathered {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/131, 135}. There he gave the Ansar his famous sermon in the enclosure, and entered ihram for umra at night — so it became the miqat for Meccans' umra {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/142-143}.

## For young visitors
A historical place between Mecca and Ta'if where the Prophet gathered the spoils and captives after the battle, pardoned thousands of captives there, and entered ihram from it to perform umra.
