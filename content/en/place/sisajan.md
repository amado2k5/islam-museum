## Overview
Sisajan is one of the first lands of [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. Its people engaged Habib ibn Maslama and he defeated them, prevailed over Waws, and made peace with the people of its fortresses for a kharaj they would pay. Umm Yazid bint Usayd, daughter of its patrician, was taken captive from it in the raid of Muhammad ibn Marwan, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
