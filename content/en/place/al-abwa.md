## Overview
Al-Abwa: a site between Mecca and Medina, where [[person/amina-bint-wahb|Amina bint Wahb]], the mother of the Prophet, died on her way back from Yathrib to Mecca ([[event/death-of-amina|the death of Amina]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.57} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/168}. Al-Idrisi mentions it as a station on the pilgrimage road, about twenty-seven miles from al-Juhfa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book says that after a month Amina set out from Yathrib to return to Mecca, that illness overtook her at the start of the road and pressed upon her, and that she died at al-Abwa between Mecca and Medina. [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] then took the boy back to Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.57} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.7} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/63}.

## What is not mentioned here
The book does not mention her grave or a later visit by the Prophet to it. What the hadith books report of his visiting his mother's grave needs a source that can be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Al-Abwa is a small village on the road between Mecca and Medina. The Prophet's mother, Amina, died there when he was a boy of six.
