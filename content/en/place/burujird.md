## Overview
Burujird: a city larger than Ramman and in a better state, according to al-Idrisi. It is densely built, and goods are carried from it to Karaj and elsewhere. It lies about twenty-four miles from Ramman. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.675}
