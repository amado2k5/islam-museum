## Overview
Burujird: a city larger than Ramman and in a better state, according to al-Idrisi. It is densely built, and goods are carried from it to Karaj and elsewhere. It lies about twenty-four miles from Ramman. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.675}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Burujird as a town between Hamadhan and Karaj, eighteen farsakhs from Hamadhan and ten from Karaj. It was counted as a village until Hamula, vizier of the house of Abu Dulaf, set up a pulpit there and made it his residence when his power grew in the mountains. It is a fertile town rich in produce, and its fruit was carried to Karaj and elsewhere; saffron grows there. He records verses by poets who mocked its people for meanness, which he does not quote here. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.404}
