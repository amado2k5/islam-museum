## Overview
Suraws is a city on the river Shariya, which is very beneficial. The river rises from Mount Ushlub and empties into Lake Ghaghan. It is one of the cities of the Kimak, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.709}
