## Summary
Kutha, an old town of the Sawad; in al-Baladhuri some of the Muslims crossed Nahr Subudhan and seized Kutha, Nahr al-Malik and Baduraya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 356}

## Details
In the reports on al-Muthanna ibn Haritha and the raids into the Sawad before al-Qadisiyya, al-Baladhuri records that the Muslims found the Persians routed, their rule weakened and their affairs in disarray; some Muslims crossed the river Subudha and seized Kutha, Nahr al-Malik and Baduraya, while others reached Kilwadha, and they lived off what the raids brought them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 356} Kutha is thus an ancient town of the Iraqi Sawad that fell to the Muslim raiders in the first incursions into the Sawad, at a time when Persian rule was weak — before the regular armies under Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas were sent to al-Qadisiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 356}
