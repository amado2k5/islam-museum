## Overview
New York City is a city in the United States. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090485}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2090485 with the preferred name «New York», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «United States of America». Among the names it records are «NYC» (English), «New York» (French) and «Novum Eboracum» (Latin). It links the place to GeoNames no. 5128581 and to Arachne place no. 1839. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090485}
