## Overview
Barah: a city on the seashore in the great land of the Maghrib, about fifteen days from Barqa, according to al-Baladhuri. Its people were Christians, but not Romans. Khalfun the Berber conquered it at the beginning of the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}
