## Overview
Tarabulus (al-Sham) is a great city with a strong wall. It has olives, fruits and kinds of crops. Al-Idrisi describes it as a bastion of the bastions of Islam on the coast of al-Sham. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.372}
