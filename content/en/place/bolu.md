## Overview
Bolu is a city in Turkey, and this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2283200) records this place under the preferred name «Bolu», typed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Bolu (Provinz)», an administrative unit. Further names include «Claudiopolis»; «Boli»; «بولو» [Arabic]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 750516, GND 4196781-1. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283200}
