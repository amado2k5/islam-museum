## Overview
Darabjird: a city in Fars. al-Baladhuri reports that it was the great fire-temple of the people of Fars, and of their religion, and that the Herbad was over it. Uthman ibn Abi al-As made peace with it for money, and on the condition that its people should be equal to those whose lands had been conquered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut spells the name Darabjird and calls it a province in Fars, with many scholars named after it; he gives as an example the preacher Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Darabjirdi. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.419} In the same entry he lists two other places of the same name, a village in the district of Istakhr with a mercury mine and a quarter in Nishapur, which he keeps distinct from the province of Fars. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.419}
