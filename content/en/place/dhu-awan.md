## Summary
A spot where the Prophet camped on his return from Tabuk, an hour's daylight between it and Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173}

## The name
In the editors' footnote: Abu Dharr said it stands in the original with fatha on the hamza (Awan), while al-Khashni transmits it with damma. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173}

## The event
The Prophet camped at it when news of the mosque reached him, and he ordered its demolition ([[event/demolition-of-masjid-al-dirar|the demolition event]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173-174}
