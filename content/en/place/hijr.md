## Overview
Al-Hijr is a site on the Syrian road with remains of the people of Thamud. Ibn Battuta mentions it in his travels. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

The coordinates match a modern town of the same name; an additional source is needed to confirm the identification.
