## Overview
The Palace of Masmuda is a palace on the bank of the sea, where ships and fire-ships are built. It stands at the head of the strait nearest to al-Andalus, and lies about twenty miles from [[place/ceuta|Ceuta]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.529}
