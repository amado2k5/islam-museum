## Overview
A city and island on the coast of the Far Maghrib. al-Idrisi relates the story of its naming, from the deceivers who brought their ships down to it. He also tells the story of Ahmad ibn Umar, known as Duqm al-Iwaz, who was among a part of the fleet of Yusuf ibn Tashfin. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.220}
