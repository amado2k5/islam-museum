## Overview
Adhruh: a place mentioned by Ibn Ishaq in the account of the expedition to [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]]. Its people came to the Prophet there and gave him the poll-tax. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The place data gives its approximate location as the present-day town of Adhruh in southern Jordan near Ma'an. This identification is at the level of the district, not the exact site.
