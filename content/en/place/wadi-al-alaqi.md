## Overview
Wadi al-Alaqi is a valley in the land of the Beja, with many people and a gathering of people, according to al-Idrisi. The people of Upper Egypt and the Beja come to it, and it has sweet wells. Its people seek gold in the sand at night. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.46}
