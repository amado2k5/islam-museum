## Overview
Al-Alaqi is in itself like a large village, with water from sweet wells, and the famous mine of Nubia lies in the middle of its land in a desert with no mountain around, only soft sands and open plains. Those from the Sa'id and the Bujja come to it, and it is a valley with many people and a large crowd. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 46}

## Seeking the gold
On the first and last nights of the Arab month the seekers go out into those sands at night, each looking at the ground near him; when one sees gold dust shining in the dark he marks the place with a sign he knows and spends the night there. In the morning each goes to his mark in the heap of sand, takes it and carries it on his riding camel to wells nearby. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 46}

## Extraction
He then washes it with water in a wooden bowl, extracts the gold, combines it with mercury and smelts it. What they gather they trade among themselves, and the merchants then carry it to all regions; this is their constant occupation and from it come their livelihood and the start of their earnings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 46}
