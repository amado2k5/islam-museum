## Overview
Zamakhir is a city on the west bank of the Nile, above the mouth of the Gulf of al-Munha. Al-Idrisi describes it as fine in construction, abundant in orchards, and rich in fruits and kinds of grain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.127}
