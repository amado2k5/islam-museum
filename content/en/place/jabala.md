## Overview
Jabala is a coastal town on the coasts of Syria. According to al-Baladhuri it was a fortress of the Romans, who left it when Homs was conquered. Then the Muslims took by force a town two farsakhs from it, and it was ruined and its people left it. [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] founded Jabala and garrisoned it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.182}
