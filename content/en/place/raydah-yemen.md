## Overview
Raydah is a place named in the account of the fighting between [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far: al-Hadi routed them there, killed a great many, and razed the palace of Raydah at the end of Jumada II 288 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} In a footnote the editor says it is a town a day's journey from Sana'a, citing Mu'jam al-Buldan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19}

See [[event/battle-of-raydah-288]].

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name with a fatha on the first letter, a sukun on the second and the plain dal. He says «rih ridah» is a soft-blowing wind and cites a verse for it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.112} He calls it a city in Yemen a day's journey from Sanaa, with springs and vineyards, and cites Tarafa's verse and lines by Abu Talib ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, in his elegy for Abu Umayya ibn al-Mughira, that mention Raydah. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.112} He reports al-Hamdani as placing it beyond Sanaa among the villages of Hamdan in the highlands, with the disused well and the lofty palace called Talfum, and, speaking of the towns of Hadramawt, as naming «Raydat al-'Ibad» and «Raydat al-Harmiya». {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.112}
