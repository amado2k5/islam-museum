## Overview
Mahra is a sandy, barren land which only the camels named after it inhabit, famous for their speed. Al-Idrisi says its length is about nine hundred miles, and that its people live on dried fish, dates and milk, and know little wheat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.155}

## In al-Hamdani
Al-Hamdani named them "the people of the wastelands", and mentioned that they used to venerate the sun and prostrate to it. He also related of their camels on the shores of the Yemeni sea that when a camel smells ambergris it kneels over it and does not leave until its owner comes to pick it up, and that the camel sometimes dies of hunger. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab pp.20-21}
