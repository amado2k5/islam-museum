## Overview
Qal'at Ayyub (Calatayud) is a city of pleasing site, strongly fortified and very hard to take, with many trees and fruits, running springs and gushing sources, great fertility and low prices. Gilded pottery is made there and sent out in all directions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 554}

## Distances
It lies fifty miles east of the city of Salim. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 553} From Qal'at Ayyub south to Qal'at Daruqa is eighteen miles; Daruqa is a small, settled town with plenty of water, many gardens and vineyards, and everything there is plentiful and cheap. From Daruqa to the city of Saragossa is fifty miles, and the same from Qal'at Ayyub to Saragossa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 554}

## The river
The great river Ibra (Ebro) comes in part from the land of the Rum, in part from the mountains of Qal'at Ayyub and in part from the districts of Qalhra, and these streams join above the city of Tutila and run on to Saragossa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 554} Qal'at Ayyub is listed in the province of Arnit with Qal'at Daruqa, Saragossa, Wasqa and Tutila. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538}
