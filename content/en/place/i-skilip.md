## Overview
İskilip is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Çorum Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2089957}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2089957 with the preferred name «Iskelib», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Çorum Province. Among the names it records are «Neoclaudiopolis», «Andrapa», «Neapolis» and «İskilip», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 745076. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2089957}
