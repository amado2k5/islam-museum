## Overview
Thaniyyat al-Mirar is a pass on the road from Medina to Mecca, near Mount al-Mirar, which the Muslims took on their way to Hudaybiyya. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq} The delineation on the map is approximate, and its coordinates come from {cite:geonames-gazetteer}.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the pass under Thaniyyat al-Murar, with a damma on the mim and a light ra', and says murar is a bitter herb that makes camels' lips draw back when they eat it; he notes that Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj mentions the pass in his Sahih in the hadith of Abu Mu'adh with a damma, and that in another hadith (of Ibn Habib al-Harithi) there is doubt between damma and kasra, the entry not saying whose; the entry does not say where it is. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.85} In his entry on al-Murar he gives Ibn Ishaq on the year of al-Hudaybiyya: the Prophet set out and, when he took Thaniyyat al-Murar, his camel knelt; people said she had balked; he said she had not, that it was not her nature, and that the one who held back the elephant had held her; the entry then adds, in words that run on from Ibn Ishaq's, that Thaniyyat al-Murar is the descent to al-Hudaybiyya. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.92}

He also has Thaniyyat al-Marra, with a fatha on the mim, a close-looking name, and ties two things to it: the Hijra tradition in which the guide took the Prophet and Abu Bakr by Amaj, then al-Kharrar, then Thaniyyat al-Marra, then Laqfa, and the party of 'Ubayda ibn al-Harith that reached a water in the Hijaz below the pass. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.85} His entry on al-Ahya' calls that water the one 'Ubayda raided and puts it below Thaniyyat al-Marra. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.118} For him they are two neighbouring entries, and he does not join them.

## The museum's identification
The record, from al-Mubarakfuri, has a pass on the road to Mecca from Medina, near Mount al-Marar, taken by the Muslims on the way to al-Hudaybiyya; its coordinates are from GeoNames and its map position is approximate. Yaqut agrees, from Ibn Ishaq, that the pass the Prophet took in the year of al-Hudaybiyya was Thaniyyat al-Murar and that it is the descent to al-Hudaybiyya; but that link is in his entry on al-Murar, not in his entry on Thaniyyat al-Murar, which cites Muslim's Sahih and gives no place. He derives the name from a herb and has no mountain of that name, so "near Mount al-Marar" comes from GeoNames, not Yaqut. He also has Thaniyyat al-Marra, with a fatha, on the Hijra road near al-Kharrar, which the museum does not merge with this pass. The record's point (21.62459, 40.02331) is marked approximate and lies 30.3 km from the museum's Mecca point, 38.7 km from its Hudaybiyya point and 319.0 km from its Medina point; Yaqut gives no distance or coordinates, and the record's phrase "on the road to Mecca from Medina" is not in either of his entries.
