## Overview
Thaniyyat al-Mirar is a pass on the road from Medina to Mecca, near Mount al-Mirar, which the Muslims took on their way to Hudaybiyya. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq} The delineation on the map is approximate, and its coordinates come from {cite:geonames-gazetteer}.
