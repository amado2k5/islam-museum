## Overview
Ramij is a large island among the islands of the Zanj, whose circumference is four hundred miles or more, according to al-Idrisi. In it is a high, impregnable mountain to which those cut off from others take refuge. Some of its people are known as al-Sumra (the dark-complexioned). Rice, sugar and bananas grow there, and merchants come to it every year. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.62}
