## Summary
Bushanj was a town in Khurasan. It fell under the great peace of [[place/herat|Herat]] which its lord concluded with 'Abdallah ibn 'Amir, together with Badghis; Taghun and Baghun alone had been taken by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 570}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that the lord of Herat, hearing what had befallen Ibn 'Amir's progress, came to him and made peace for Herat, Badghis and Bushanj — except Taghun and Baghun, which had been conquered by force {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 570}. Ibn 'Amir wrote him a treaty whose text runs: "In the name of God the Merciful: this is what 'Abdallah ibn 'Amir enjoins upon the lord of Herat, Bushanj and Badghis — he enjoins him to fear God, to deal loyally with the Muslims, and to set right the lands under his hand; and he made peace with him for Herat, its plain and its mountain, on condition that he render the jizya agreed upon and divide it justly among the lands; whoever withholds what is due has neither pact nor protection" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 570}. Rabi' ibn Nahshal wrote the document and Ibn 'Amir sealed it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 570}. Bushanj thus entered the first treaty of peace with Ibn 'Amir in the days of 'Uthman.
