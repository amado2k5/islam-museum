## Overview
The River of Ibn Umar is a river at [[place/basra|Basra]]. Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, governor for Yazid ibn al-Walid, dug it so that its people could drink from it, after complaints about the brackishness of their water. The source mentions in this connection the two water-flasks of Basra and al-Butayha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
