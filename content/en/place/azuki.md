## Summary
Azuki is a settled town of the Masufa country at the first ascent of the desert, which al-Idrisi makes the gateway to the lands of the Sudan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.225}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that the city of Azuki belongs to the lands of Masufa and Lamta and is the first of the desert ascents; from it to Sijilmasa is thirteen stages and to Nul seven stages, and the town is not large but is civilized, its people wearing short woollen garments they call al-Qadawir in their tongue {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.225}. He adds that this city is called Azqi in Berber and Quqdam in the Janawi tongue, and that whoever wishes to enter the lands of Say, Takrur and Ghana among the Sudan countries must pass through it {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.225}. It is thus the outlet of the great desert caravans toward the kingdom of Ghana and its neighbours.
