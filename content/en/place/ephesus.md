## Overview
Ephesus is an ancient Greek city in Anatolia, near the Aegean coast. No entry for it was found in the volumes of the Mu'jam al-buldan read here, so this page rests on what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ephesus/Arsinoe(ia)» (Pleiades no. 599612), classed as a settlement and an archaeological site, with a time span from 1200 BCE to 2000 CE. Among the names it records are «Ephesos» in Greek, «Arsinoe» and «Selçuk» in Turkish, and it also gives Arabic names, including «Ufsus» and «Afsis». It describes the place as the most important ancient metropolis of Ionian Asia Minor. {cite:pleiades 599612}
