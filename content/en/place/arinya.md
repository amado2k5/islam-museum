## Summary
Arinya is a small, flourishing town that al-Idrisi lists in the Nuzhat al-Mushtaq on the road east of the city of Aqra in the land of Bwamiya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from the city of Aqra eastward to the town of Arinya is eighty miles, and that it is a small but flourishing town; from Arinya southward to the city of Baqsin on the river Danube is sixty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}. He describes Baqsin as a famous city counted among the ancient foundations, with markets, shops, craftsmen and Greek scholars, whose people have farms and prosperous districts and whose prices are always low because of the abundance of wheat {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}. Arinya thus lies on the road linking Aqra with Baqsin in those northern reaches of the land of Bwamiya.
