## Overview
Sayda is a city on the shore of the salt sea, cheap in price and surrounded by gardens and trees, according to al-Idrisi. It is attributed to a woman who lived in the pre-Islamic era. A kind of salted fish is made there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.371}
