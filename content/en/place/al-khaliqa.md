## Summary
The barren land of Banu Abi Ahmad near [[place/medina|Medina]]; there [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr]] caught the woman carrying [[work/hatib-letter-to-quraysh|Hatib's letter]] to Quraysh and drew the letter from the plaits of her head {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/41}.

## The spot's name
The edition has three readings of the name: "al-Khaliqa" with damma on the silent kha; with the dotted jim in both; and "Dhu al-Khulayfa" in Ibn Ishaq's book, as in the edition's footnote {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/41}.

## For young visitors
A place near Medina where the Prophet's men stopped the woman carrying a secret letter — and found it hidden in her hair's plaits.
