## Overview
Al-Haffah is a name Yaqut records in the Mu'jam al-buldan as a district west of Aleppo, pausing on the origin of the cloth called hafiyya. The museum's record is the town of Al-Haffah in Latakia governorate; Yaqut's description does not name it outright. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.276}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says al-Haffah, with a fatha and a doubled fa', is a district west of Aleppo that contains several villages. He then relays a saying that the hafiyya cloths are named for it, and differs with it from what he himself knows, which is that al-haff is a part of the weaver's equipment used in making these cloths, and is not used for every kind of cloth. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.276}

The passage shows that Yaqut did not commit to the link between the cloth and the place; he sets out both views and puts forward what he knows. The museum's identification of the district with the town of Al-Haffah in Latakia rests on the name occurring west of Aleppo, and the text does not mention Latakia, so the link is a probable but unconfirmed one that needs another geographical source. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.276}
