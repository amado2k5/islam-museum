## Overview
Qalhat is a populous city on the sea shore in Oman. Its people drink from wells, and fish is caught there. According to al-Idrisi it is a great stage overland from Sur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.155}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Qalhat» (Pleiades no. 431950550), classed as a settlement and an archaeological site, with activity attested from 1200 CE to 1599 CE. {cite:pleiades 431950550}
