## Overview
Quhistan is the city nearest to al-Tabasayn at the gate of Khurasan. Abd Allah ibn Amir and al-Ahnaf ibn Qays came to it, and they met the Hephthalites (al-Hayatila), who were Turks. It is also said that they were a people of Persia whom Firuz exiled to Herat. They became helpers of the Turks against the people of Quhistan, and the Muslims defeated them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
