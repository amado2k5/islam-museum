## Overview
The Yarmuk is a river between Palestine and Jordan. On it took place the great Battle of Yarmuk, in Rajab of the year 15 AH, between the Muslims and the hosts of Heraclius, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.184}
