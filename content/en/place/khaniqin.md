## Overview
Khaniqin is a land east of the Tigris. Al-Baladhuri reports that the Persians placed their dependants and baggage in it on the day of [[place/jalula|Jalula]]. Then Jarir ibn Abd Allah came to Khaniqin, where some of them remained, and he killed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
