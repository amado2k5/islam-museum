## Overview
al-Mansura: a city in Sind, below the lake. al-Baladhuri reports that Amr ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim built it when his affair prevailed, and it is the one in which the governors settle today. Before that its site had been a thicket near old Brahmanabad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
