## Overview
Sisar is a town in the mountains between Hamadhan, al-Dinawar and Azerbaijan. According to al-Baladhuri, Sulayman ibn Qayrat and Salam al-Tayfuri built it by order of al-Mahdi, as a refuge for his animals and sheep from the destitute (sa'alik). It was named after thirty mounds and a hundred springs. Districts of al-Dinawar and Azerbaijan were attached to it, and it became a district (kura). Al-Rashid stationed a thousand men in it. It was taken from Asim ibn Murra in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun and became one of the estates of the caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
