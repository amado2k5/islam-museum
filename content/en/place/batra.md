## Summary
Jabal Batra was a mountain in the land of the Khazars, running across from north to south; al-Idrisi says it held mines of silver and good lead, from which much was extracted and exported to every region. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.920}

## Details
Al-Idrisi mentions it in his description of the Khazar land, saying that the Khazars had wrested the country from the peoples before them — the Burjan, the Bartas and the Bulghar — so that nothing remained to the other nations but a name upon the land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.920} Describing the mountain he says it contains mines of silver and mines of good lead, of which much is extracted and sent out to every direction and region, marking it as a known source of metals in the Khazar country. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.920}
