## Overview
Tamdafus is a good anchorage with a small ruined town above it; most of its wall has fallen and few people remain. There are remains of old buildings, temples and stone idols, and al-Idrisi says it was once among the greatest and widest of lands. It lies eighteen miles east of al-Jazair (Algiers). {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 258}

## Neighbours
From Tamdafus to Marsa al-Dajaj is twenty miles. Marsa al-Dajaj is a large place with a fort round it and few inhabitants, many of whom flee in summer for fear of the fleets; it has a safe anchorage, wide fields and continuous farmland, blessed wheat and, above all, figs carried as dried slices to every region. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 259}

## On the coast
In the coastal description, from the Jazair of Bani Mazghanna to Tamdafus is eighteen miles, and it is an anchorage with buildings and continuous farms. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 273}
