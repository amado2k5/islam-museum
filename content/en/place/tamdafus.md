## Overview
Tamdafus is a small, ruined city, of which part of the wall remains. It has remnants of ancient building, temples and idols of stone. It lies eighteen miles east of Algiers, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.258}
