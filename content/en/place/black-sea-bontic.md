## Overview
The Pontic Sea: a large gulf-like sea connected to the Sea of Darkness. The lands of Heraclius, the Bontim, the Khazariyya, the Qumaniyya and the Russians overlook it, and its source is near Constantinople. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.905}
