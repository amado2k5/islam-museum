## Overview
Marash is one of the frontier posts of al-Sham on the Jazira side, near al-A'maq. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] conquered its fortress by sending Abu Ubayda from Manbij, on condition that its people leave, and then it was laid to ruin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.265-267} See [[event/conquest-of-marash|The conquest of Marash]].

[[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] then built it as a city and settled a garrison in it. Al-Abbas ibn al-Walid developed it, fortified it and built its mosque. Building and ruin alternated in it between Marwan ibn Muhammad and the Byzantines, until Salih ibn Ali built it in the caliphate of al-Mansur, and al-Mahdi increased its garrison. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.265-267}

The raid of Michael in the year 161 AH struck its hinterland. See [[event/michael-incursion-161|The raid of Michael]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.265-267}
