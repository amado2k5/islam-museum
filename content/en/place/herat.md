## Summary
Herat is a city in western Khurasan which Golius identifies as ancient "Aria"; Yaqut visited it in 614 AH, and the Tatars struck it in 618 AH. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 423}

Golius notes that Herat is the ancient "Aria" mentioned by Strabo in Bactria, and connects the name with the river that waters it. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 423}

Yaqut reports that he passed through it in 614 AH, describing it as the finest and most populous city of Khurasan, and quoted two Persian verses likening the city to a pearl. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 423}

The Tatars struck it four years later; Golius transmits the figure as it appears in the text, and it requires verification. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 423}

Abu al-Fida' mentions that it lies about eleven stages from Sijistan, Nishapur, and Khwarazm, and that its position is at longitude 94 degrees 20 minutes and latitude 34 degrees 30 minutes; these figures are transmitted as given and need to be verified against the page image. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 422}
