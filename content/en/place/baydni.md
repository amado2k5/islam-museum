## Summary
Baydni is a large city of the country of Lafisa which al-Idrisi places on the river Danube between west and north. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Lafisa to the city of Baydni is five stages, that it is a large city belonging to Lafisa, lying between west and north on the aforesaid river Danube {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. Just before, he notes that the city of Lafisa stands on the back of a mountain, and that from Lafisa north-eastward to the city of Masyunis is five stages, Masyunis being a large, flourishing, ancient city {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. Baydni is thus one of the large cities dependent on the region of Lafisa, lying along the Danube to the north-west.
