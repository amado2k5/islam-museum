## Overview
Ushrusana is a region beyond the river, not a single town. According to al-Istakhri, as Yaqut reports him, it lies between Sayhun and [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]], its land is mostly mountainous, and no place or town in it bears its name. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 261}

Its neighbours are Fargana to the east, the borders of Samarqand to the west and al-Shash to the north. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 261}

Al-Idrisi says Bunjakat, one stage from Samarqand, belongs to Ushrusana and is its seat. He calls it a large town with a wide suburb and a wall, its buildings are of mud, and it has a congregational mosque and busy markets. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 503} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 702} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 504}

Yaqut, quoting al-Istakhri, calls Bunjakat the greatest town of Ushrusana, where its governors live. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p. 695}

Other towns al-Idrisi names in the region are Fakkat, Ghazq, Kharqana, Zamin, Dizak and Arsaniyakat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 702} Dizak lies north of Ushrusana, and people of Samarqand kept a frontier garrison there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 505}

Al-Idrisi also notes that a river reaching Samarqand grows when snow falls on the mountains of Bitm and Ushrusana. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 499}

According to al-Baladhuri, Qutayba ibn Muslim took Yakand, Kish, Nasaf and al-Shash, raided Fargana, and then raided Sughd and Ushrusana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 429}

Nasr ibn Sayyar raided Ushrusana in the days of Marwan ibn Muhammad and took nothing from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 436}

In al-Ma'mun's time his forces raided Sughd and Ushrusana. Its ruler Kawus had asked al-Fadl ibn Sahl for peace in return for tribute, but refused to keep it when al-Ma'mun reached Madinat al-Salam. When al-Ma'mun's army encamped before his city, Kawus submitted, declared Islam, and was confirmed by al-Ma'mun in his lands; his son Haydar, known as al-Afshin, succeeded him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 437} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 438}
