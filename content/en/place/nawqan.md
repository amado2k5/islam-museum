## Overview
Nawqan is a city which al-Idrisi describes as one of the jewels of the lands. It has a wall of earth, and in it are well-off people, merchants and craftsmen. In it is the tomb of Ali ibn Musa al-Rida, and turquoise is extracted from its mountain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.693}
