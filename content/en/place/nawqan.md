## Overview
Nawqan is one of the finest towns of the region of Nishapur, with a market and a strong earthen wall; it has wealthy men, merchants, craftsmen and workmen, a strong fort, and the tomb of Ali ibn Musa al-Rida. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 692}

## Mountain and minerals
In the mountain of Nawqan is the quarry of stone from which cooking pots are cut for all Khurasan, and in its hills are mines of silver, copper and iron; turquoise, and other stones such as al-hamahin, al-dahanj and rock crystal, are found there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 692} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 693}

## History and road
Nawqan was the seat of government of Khurasan until the Tahirid period, when authority moved to Nishapur, and most of it fell into ruin and lost its charms. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 693} From Nishapur to Baghnash is fifteen miles, from there to al-Hamra eighteen miles, then to Nawqan eighteen miles, then to Marw al-Shahjan twelve parasangs. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 694}
