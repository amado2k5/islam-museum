## Overview
Wadi al-Hijara is a strong, handsome city rich in provisions and good things, combining the means of benefit and produce, with strong walls and flowing springs. It lies fifty miles east of [[place/tulaytula|Toledo]], a distance of two stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 553}

## River and farming
A small river runs on its west, on which it has gardens, orchards, vineyards and fields, and much saffron is grown there, which is sent out from it to all the districts. The river flows south and falls into the greater Tagus, which it swells. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 553}

## The province
Wadi al-Hijara is listed in the province of al-Sharat with Talabira, Toledo, Madrid, al-Fahmin, Aqlish and Ubda. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538} The Tagus rises in the mountains joined to al-Qal'a and al-Funt and flows west to Toledo, then Talabira, al-Makhada, al-Qantara, Santarem and Lisbon, where it falls into the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 553}
