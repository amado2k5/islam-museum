## Overview
Wadi al-Hijara is a fortified city, fine in its markets and buildings, according to al-Idrisi. It has a congregational mosque, and to its west a small river with gardens. Much saffron is brought from it. It lies two stages from Toledo. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.553}
