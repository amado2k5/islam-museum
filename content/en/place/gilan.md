## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Gilan as the name of many lands beyond Tabaristan, with no large city, only villages in meadows between mountains. He cites Abu al-Mundhir Hisham ibn Muhammad, who made Gilan and Muqan sons of Kashij, son of Yafith, son of Noah, and notes that the Persians say Kilan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.201}

Many scholars, especially jurists, are called after it. Among them is Abu Mansur Bay ibn Ja'far al-Gili, a Shafi'i jurist who studied fiqh under Ibn al-Bayda'i and heard hadith, held the judgeship at Bab al-Taq, and died at the start of Muharram in the year 452 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.201}
