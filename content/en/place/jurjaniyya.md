## Overview
Jurjaniyya — Gurgandj to the Persians and, around 600/1200, simply 'Khwarazm' like the surrounding country — was the economic centre of Khwarazm and for long its political capital, lying west of the lowest reaches of the Oxus (Amu Darya). Captured by the Arabs in 93/712, it outstripped Kath in importance after the Seljuks overthrew the Ma'munid shahs in 434/1043, becoming the real centre of the territory and its commercial intermediary with the northern Turkish tribes. The Mongols attacked it in 618/1221 and razed it after a siege of many months {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 1192-1193}. Al-Zamakhshari moved here from Zamakhshar and it became his permanent home; he died there in 538/1144 and was buried near the town {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 917-918}.
