## Overview
Wadi Barhut is in the land of Hadramawt and the land of al-Shihr. Al-Mas'udi mentions it among the fire-springs of the earth, and the fire-springs are the fire that wells up from the ground. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.357}

According to Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari, the well of Barhut in the land of Hadramawt has an unknown depth, and it is not known that any person has descended into it. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.233}
