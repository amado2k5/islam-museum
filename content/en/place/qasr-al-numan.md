## Overview
The Palace of al-Nu'man is a palace in [[place/basra|Basra]], which belonged to al-Nu'man ibn Suhban al-Rasibi, who judged between Mudar and Rabi'a when [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Mu'awiya]] died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The addition and the report
[[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] added to it. The source relays al-Nu'man's saying that this is bad wealth: if the water is abundant it drowns its owner, and if it is scarce he thirsts. Then the water became scarce, and everyone who was there died, according to the report. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
