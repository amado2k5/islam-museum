## Overview
Budapest is the capital and largest city of Hungary. It does not appear in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2061547 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Budapest». Its parent in the hierarchy is a record named «Ostungarn». It records names in English, German, Latin («Aquincum») and Greek, and in Arabic the form «بودابست», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 3054643, to the Arachne place identifier 474 and to the German National Library authority record 4008684-7. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2061547}
