## Overview
Turshiz is one of the cities of Nishapur, populous, with a wall, a ditch, gardens and trade. It lies two stages from Yanabidh, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464} It is among the districts of [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]].
