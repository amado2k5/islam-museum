## Overview
Turshiz is a town in the district of Nishapur in Khurasan. Al-Idrisi calls it a populous and civilised city, with a wall, a moat, gardens and trade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.690}

## Distances
Turshiz lies four stages from Nishapur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464} From Nishapur to Turshiz is also four stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692} From Istishit to Turshiz is one stage, and Turshiz lies in the district of Nishapur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.449}

## Roads and stations
From Turshiz to Khaskin is four stages, and from Turshiz to Qain is five stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.465} Khaskin is a small town with a mosque and a pulpit. It has farms and close-built houses, and it lies sixty-six miles from Nishapur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.465} From Turshiz to the village of Salm is six stages. Salm is in the desert. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.465}

## Nishapur and its neighbours
Al-Idrisi says the district of Nishapur has wide borders, settled villages and many known towns. Turshiz is among them, with al-Buzjan, Malin, Khaymand, Salumak, Sankan and Zawzan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.690}

## The road to Yanabadh
Al-Idrisi gives the distance between Turshiz and Yanabadh as two stages in one place, and as three stages from Turshiz to Yanabadh in another. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.465} The text does not settle the difference, so both figures are given as they stand.
