## Overview
Burgish: a walled city, and its districts are named after it. Its water is abundant and its farms are connected. al-Idrisi reports that it lies a hundred miles from Anqlazima, and forty miles from Shant Juan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.732}
