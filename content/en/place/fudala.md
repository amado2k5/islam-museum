## Overview
The port of Fudala is a harbour on the coast of the Atlantic near Sala, about twelve miles to its south. Wheat, barley, broad beans and chickpeas are shipped from it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.239}
