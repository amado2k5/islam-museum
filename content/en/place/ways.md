## Overview
Ways is a fortress in Sisajan, built by Anushirwan, and it lies within [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.281}

[[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] prevailed over it after the defeat of the people of the place. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.281}
