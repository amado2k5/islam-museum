## Overview
Ruzrawar is a fine province and a noble district where grows saffron of which there is none like on the face of the earth. Its city is the city of Karaj Ruzrawar, a small one. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 675} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 676}

## Location
Ruzrawar is listed among the districts of al-Jabal with Hamadhan, Burujird, al-Karaj and others. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 674} From Hamadhan to Ruzrawar is twenty-seven miles on the road to Khuzistan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 675}

## Neighbours
From Ruzrawar to Nihawand is twenty-one miles; Nihawand is a splendid city on a mountain with a clay wall and clay buildings, with orchards, gardens, fruit, pleasure grounds and plentiful water, and much trade, countryside and building. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 676} From Nihawand to Lashtar is thirty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 676} From Hamadhan to Sawa is ninety miles, and from Sawa to Qum thirty-six miles, covered in two days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 676}
