## Overview
A valley that Ibn Ishaq calls "the belly of Idam". The Prophet sent a group of Muslims there, among them Abu Qatada and Muhallam ibn Jathama, and in it Amir ibn al-Adbat al-Ashja'i was killed. See [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-batn-idam|the raid of Ibn Abi Hadrad on the belly of Idam]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/275}

## Notes on the report
- Its location according to the geographers (a valley into which what lies between the two sanctuaries drains) is not read here, and no coordinates are given for it (needs a source).

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name with a kasra on the first letter and a fatha on the second, and says that Dhu Idam is a water the road crosses between Mecca and al-Yamama at al-Sumayna; some said it is a hollow there with water and places called al-Hanazil, and it is mentioned in the Prophet's expeditions. He reports from al-Sayyid Ali that Idam is a valley in the mountains of Tihama, the valley in which Medina lies: near Medina it is called al-Qana, higher up at the dam al-Shazat, and from al-Shazat downward Idam. From Ibn al-Sikkit he reports that it is a valley that cuts across the Hijaz until it empties into the sea, its upper part being the al-Qana that passes below Medina, and that it was also said to be the valley of Ashja and Juhayna, whose day of Idam is among their battle days. From Nasr he adds that Idam is also a mountain between al-Yamama and Dariyya. {C(1,214)} {C(1,215)}
