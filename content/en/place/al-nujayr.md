## Overview
al-Nujayr: a fortress of Banu Kinda in [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141-145}

## The siege
Al-Ash'ath ibn Qays and the notables of Kinda took refuge in it during their apostasy. Ziyad ibn Labid and al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya besieged it until they were exhausted, and they came down on terms of safe-conduct. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141-145}

## After the siege
al-Baladhuri reports that women were found in the fortress who rejoiced at the death of the Prophet, and [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] ordered that their hands and feet be cut off. The captives of the fortress were returned for ransom, at four hundred dirhams per head. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141-145}
