## Overview
İznik is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2284833 with the preferred name «Iznik», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Bursa (Provinz)» (an administrative unit). Among its recorded names are «Nicaea», «Nikaia» and «İznik». It links the place to GeoNames no. 745026, GND no. 4027895-5 and Arachne no. 1208236. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2284833}
