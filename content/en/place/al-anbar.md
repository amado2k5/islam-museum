## Overview
Al-Anbar: a town. According to al-Baladhuri it was so named because the granaries of the Persians were there, and the clients and dependants of al-Nu'man used to be given their provisions from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest and the treaty
[[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] besieged it after a raid on the market of Baghdad and the burning of its surroundings, and its people made peace with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] its district was made subject to a treaty for four hundred thousand dirhams and a thousand Qatwani cloaks a year, through the treaty of Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali. al-Baladhuri gives another report, introduced with "it is said", of eighty thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
