## Overview
Tadmur is a city in the desert between Iraq and al-Sham, six days from al-Sham. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari counted it among the famous monuments, for its wonders of construction and great works. He mentions that there is a people of Arabs of Quda'a in it. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.231}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Palmyra» (Pleiades no. 668331), classed as an urban site, oasis, station, archaeological site and settlement, with activity attested from 1600 BCE to 2100 CE. Recorded names include Palmyra, Thadamora and Tadmur. It describes an ancient oasis and trading city with monumental remains in Syria, capital of the breakaway Palmyrene Empire under Zenobia until Aurelian razed it, and inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1980. {cite:pleiades 668331}
