## Overview
A great building in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] known as Jayrun. Al-Mas'udi reports that Jayrun ibn Sa'd al-Adi built it and brought marble columns to it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554-555}

It is said that it is Iram of the Pillars. Al-Mas'udi gives a report from [[person/kab-al-ahbar|Ka'b al-Ahbar]], who came to [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] and asked him about it; he described its building in gold, silver, musk and saffron. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554-555}

Al-Mas'udi then judges the report, saying that corruption enters it through the means of transmission and that it is a storyteller's composition. He notes that people disputed about this city and about its site. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554-555}
