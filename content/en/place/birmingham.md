## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Birmingham as a populated place, under the county of Warwickshire in its hierarchy. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087496}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2087496 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Birmingham». Its parent in the hierarchy is «Warwickshire (county)». It records names in English, Chinese, Polish, Turkish, Greek, Portuguese, Russian and Spanish, and in Arabic the form «برمينغهام», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 2655603, to the Arachne identifiers 394, 9450, 1211515 and 1204246 and to the German National Library authority record 4006934-5. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087496}
