## Overview
Baylaqan in Aran: a city built by Qubadh ibn Firuz. Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili conquered it by treaty, on terms of safe-conduct for lives, property and walls, on condition of poll-tax and kharaj. Abd al-Aziz ibn Hatim renewed its building, and a truce and two Imarins were witnessed there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
