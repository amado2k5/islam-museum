## Summary
Laqf (Liqf) is a place on the Hijra road between Qudayd and al-Arj, named by Ibn Ishaq among the stages of the guide's route with the Prophet and his companion. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 167}

## Details
Describing the Hijra route, Ibn Ishaq relates that the guide Abd Allah ibn Urayqit took the Prophet and his companion along the foot of Mecca, then along the coast until he crossed the road below Usfan, then along the foot of Amaj; he led them past Qudayd, then through al-Kharrar, then through Thaniyyat al-Marra, then through Liqf, then across the depression of Mudlajat Liqf. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 167}

In this itinerary Liqf is thus both a place and a valley floor bearing its name in the coastal stretch of the Hijra road — after Qudayd and before Mudlajat Mijaj, Marjih Mijah, the valley of Dhi Kashr and al-Jada'id on the way to Quba'. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 167}
