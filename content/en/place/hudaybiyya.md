## Overview
Al-Hudaybiyya is a place on the boundary of the sacred precinct of Mecca, where the peace agreement between the Muslims and Quraysh was concluded in 628 CE. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

Its location is approximate: it is identified with the Shumaysi area west of Mecca, and the nearest match in the gazetteer is about 14 kilometres away. An additional source is needed for the historical account.
