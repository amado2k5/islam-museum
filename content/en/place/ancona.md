## Overview
Ancona: a large city, one of the capitals of its region, on the river Azmum, and nine miles from the sea. It has a market and ships, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.753}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ancon(a)» (Pleiades no. 413014), classed as an urban place and a settlement and a port, with activity attested from 550 BCE to 640 CE. Names recorded there include colonia Ancona, Ancon, Ἀγκών. {cite:pleiades 413014}
