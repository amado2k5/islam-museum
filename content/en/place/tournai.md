## Overview
Tournai is a civilised, fine city in the land of Liban, with districts, villages and buildings, according to al-Idrisi. He mentions that it lies thirty miles from Qanat, between the west and the south, {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.870} and fifteen miles from Shant Mir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.871}
