## Overview
An impregnable fortress on the Euphrates neighbor to Qal'at al-Bira, seat of the Armenian catholicos under Tatar suzerainty; Sultan al-Malik al-Ashraf Khalil besieged it, set mangonels on it, and it was taken after twenty-five days in Rajab 691 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 729}.
