## Overview
Urmia, written Urmiya by Yaqut al-Hamawi, is a great and ancient city in Azerbaijan, three or four miles from the lake, which people claim was the city of Zoroaster, prophet of the Magians. Yaqut saw it in 617 AH and found it a fine city, rich in goods, with abundant fruit, gardens and water and a healthy climate, though the sultan, in his weakness, did not look after it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.206} He puts it three days from Tabriz and seven days from Irbil, gives its adjectives of origin as Armawi and Armi, and names several scholars from the city. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.207}
