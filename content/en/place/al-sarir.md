## Overview
al-Sarir: a land in the high Caucasus, whose king was titled "Lord of the Throne". Marwan ibn Muhammad entered it and took its fortresses, and then its king submitted to him and obeyed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292-293}

## The agreement
The king made peace with him for a thousand heads each year: five hundred boys and five hundred girls, with black hair and eyebrows and long eyelashes. He also made peace with him for a hundred thousand measures to be poured into the granary of the gate, and a pledge was taken from him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292-293}

The place is connected with [[event/marwan-caucasus-expedition|the expedition of Marwan to the Caucasus]], which belongs to the Umayyad period. An additional source is needed for the date.
