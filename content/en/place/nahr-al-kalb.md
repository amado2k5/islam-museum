## Overview
Yaqut gives Nahr al-Kalb a single short sentence in the Mu'jam al-buldan, fixing its place on the Syrian coast between Beirut and Sidon. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.323}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut writes the name as Nahr al-Kalb with a sukun on the lam, and says al-Hazimi fixed the vowelling in this way. He places it between Beirut and Sidon on the coasts of the 'awasim of al-Sham. The entry says no more: it gives no length for the river, no derivation of the name and no event connected to it, so all Yaqut keeps here is the name, its vowelling and its position. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.323}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record places Nahr al-Kalb in Lebanon, which does not contradict Yaqut's position between Beirut and Sidon. That the entry describes this very river is the museum's reading, not a statement in Yaqut's text.
