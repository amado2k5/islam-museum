## Overview
Wahran is a city on the sea shore, according to al-Idrisi. It has a fine earthen wall, a few markets and crafts. It faces [[place/almeria|Almeria]] on the Andalusian coast across the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.252}
