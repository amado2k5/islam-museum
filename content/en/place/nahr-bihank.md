## Overview
The River Bihank is a great river in northern India. It passes east of Atragha and joins the sea. Al-Idrisi mentions that on its bank there are kings and armies, and that a tribe of India bathes in it, claiming that its sins are washed away. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.208}
