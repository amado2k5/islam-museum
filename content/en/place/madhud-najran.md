## Overview
Madhud is a water of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] that the narrator says [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached while pursuing them from lower [[place/najran|Najran]], and then turned back, not wishing to kill them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} The book does not fix its place and the editor gives it no footnote; the page image bears the reading, so it carries no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-spares-the-routed-and-burns-the-stores-at-thila-286]].
