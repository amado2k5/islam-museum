## Overview
al-Qatul: a place on a river that draws from the Tigris. There was a village in it inhabited by a people of the Jaramiqa and some people of the Nabat. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.444-445}

## Al-Mu'tasim at al-Qatul
al-Mas'udi reports that al-Mu'tasim first settled at al-Baradhan, but did not find its air healthy, and so he moved until he reached al-Qatul. He built a palace there, and people moved to it from Madinat al-Salam. Then he was harmed by it and resolved on Samarra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.444-445}

al-Mas'udi quotes the verse of Ayyar in satire of that move: "O dweller of al-Qatul among the Jaramiqa, you left the patriarchs of Baghdad the rams." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.444-445}
