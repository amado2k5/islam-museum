## Overview
Madinat Musa is a city built by Musa al-Hadi in the land of [[place/qazwin|Qazwin]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Endowment and administration
Al-Hadi endowed to it the land of Rustambadh, which he had bought for the city's needs. It was administered by his client Amr al-Rumi, and then by Amr's son Muhammad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut places Madinat Musa at Qazwin. He relates that Musa al-Hadi went to Rayy in his father al-Mahdi's lifetime and came from there to Qazwin, where he ordered a city built facing Qazwin; it was built and is called the city of Musa al-Hadi. He adds that al-Hadi bought a tract called Rustamabadh and endowed it for the city's needs. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.80}
