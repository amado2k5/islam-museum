## Overview
al-Sirin: a strong fortress on the coast, about five days from Hili. It has much water, and it has a governor and a harbour. Fixed dues are taken from it on ships going up and coming down. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.138}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the name as Sirrayn, with a kasra on the sin and a doubled ra', in the form of the dual of sirr (secrecy), and says it is a small town near Mecca on the sea coast, four or five days from Mecca "near Jidda"; his wording does not show whether "near Jidda" belongs to the town or to the count of days. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.219} He gives as its nisba Abu Harun Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Kathir al-Sirrini, who reported from 'Abd al-Malik ibn Ibrahim al-Juddi and from whom al-Tabarani and others reported. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.219} He adds that there is also a village called al-Sirrayn in the districts of Sana'a. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.219}

The place turns up in other entries: Hali, on 'Umara al-Yamani's word, is one day from it; Hilya is, on one view, a wadi between A'yar and 'Ulayb that empties into it; al-Hasaba is a wadi a night's journey from it on the Yemen side; and Rakhkha' is a place between Adakh and al-Sirrayn where animals' feet sink. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.297} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.258} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.37} The entry on al-Sarir, with a single ra', comes just before it on the same page and names a valley in the Hijaz, a place near al-Jar and a valley at Khaybar; they are not this place. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.219}

## The museum's identification
The record, from al-Idrisi, has a strong coastal fortress about five days from Hulay, with abundant water, a governor and a harbour from which fixed dues are taken on ships going up and coming down. Yaqut agrees that it is on the sea shore on the Mecca side, but calls it a small town, not a fortress, and mentions no water, governor or dues; he puts four or five days between it and Mecca, and, on 'Umara's word, one day between it and Hali, not the five of the record; the difference stays open. The record's point (19.6167, 40.6333) is marked as a site and lies 217.7 km from the museum's Mecca point, 257.1 km from its Jeddah point and 129.7 km from its Hulay point; these are straight-line figures and are not set against days. Yaqut's "near Jidda" has an open meaning, as shown above, so the museum builds nothing on it, though the point is 257.1 km from Jeddah. See [[place/hulay|Hulay]] and [[place/jeddah|Jeddah]].
