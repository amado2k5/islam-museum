## Overview
Mabanisa is one of the strongholds of the land of al-Lamaniyya. Al-Idrisi describes it as a noble, prosperous city with abundant harvests, and places it thirty miles south of Firmiza. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872}
