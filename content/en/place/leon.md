## Overview
León is a town with warrior men, whose people are high-minded and noble, and it was one of the towns of Castile. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.732}

It lies four days from Zamora and three days from Sant Yakub, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.732}
