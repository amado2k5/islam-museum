## Summary
Ak Saray, "White Palace", was a palace near Gurgandj (Urgench) in Khwarazm, still mentioned in the Shaybaniade.

## Encyclopaedia of Islam account
The Encyclopaedia of Islam (2nd ed.) entry AK SARAY, unsigned, describes a palace near Gurgandj, the town now called Urgench, which is still mentioned in the Shaybaniade, the Turkish-language account of the Shaybanids. The article refers the reader for the palace of the same name built for Timur in Shahr-i Sabz to the entry KASH. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 334}
