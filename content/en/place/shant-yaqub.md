## Overview
Shant Yaqub is a famous church on the shore of the sea called al-Anqlishin, visited by pilgrims of the Romans (Christians) from every region. After the church of Jerusalem there is no greater church, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.728}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says the church of Shant Yaqub is built of stone and mortar in a cast, is famous, and is visited on pilgrimage. Romans come to it from all regions. He compares it with the Holy Sepulchre (Qumama) for beauty of building and width of courtyard, and says that after the church of Jerusalem there is none greater. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.728}

It holds about three hundred crosses of gold and silver set with rubies and chrysolites, and about two hundred icons cast in gold and silver. A hundred priests serve it, besides their attendants and servants. Houses surround it where priests, monks and deacons live, with markets for buying and selling, and large villages like towns lie around it, with uncounted numbers of people. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.728}

The road from Shant Yaqub to Bayona runs along the coast, past the wadi of Tamarka and a great river where ships anchor. From Toledo to Shant Yaqub on the Anqlishin sea is nine stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.536}
