## Overview
Shant Yaqub is a great church to which the Romans come from every direction, fine in construction and wide in its courtyard, according to al-Idrisi. In it are large sums of gold and silver studded with rubies, about three hundred wrought crosses, and icons of gold and silver. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.728}

Al-Idrisi describes it as among the greatest churches after Bayt al-Maqdis. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.728}
