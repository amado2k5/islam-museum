## Overview
Khulays is a town on the coast between [[place/rabigh|Rabigh]] and [[place/mecca|Mecca]], on which the pilgrimage road passes. Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his journey from [[place/badr|Badr]] to Mecca. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958} The coordinates are from GeoNames. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
