## Overview
The River of Adi is a river at [[place/basra|Basra]]. It was an inlet (khawr) of the river of Basra until Adi ibn Artat al-Fazari, governor of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]], opened it up from the breach of Shirin, after its people complained and after a letter from Waki' ibn Abi Sawd. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
