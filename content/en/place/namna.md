## Overview
Namna is a village in [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]]. Al-Baladhuri mentions that [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]] conquered it together with Tumaysa in the expedition of Tabaristan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
