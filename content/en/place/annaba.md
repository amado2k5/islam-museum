## Overview
Annaba: a city on the coast of Algeria, and it is the site of Bona in the journey of Ibn Battuta. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

The coordinates are taken from GeoNames for the modern city that bears the same name; matching it with the old site needs an additional source.
