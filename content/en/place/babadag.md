## Overview
Babadag is a place at which Pleiades records an ancient settlement. {cite:pleiades 216724}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Babadag» (Pleiades no. 216724), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 640 CE. Its description says only that it is an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas, map 22, grid square F4. {cite:pleiades 216724}
