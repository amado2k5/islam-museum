## Overview
The abandoned well and the lofty palace: two remains that are joined in a well-known proverb. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari reports that they are near the present-day mountain pass in the east of Yemen. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 232}

## Location
He explains this as the Empty Quarter in the south-east of the Arabian Peninsula. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 232}
