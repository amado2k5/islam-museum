## Overview
Bab Idris: a city between mountains and connected ravines, and the way into it is difficult. It is fertile, with abundant water and fruits. It lies about eighteen miles from Qart, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.531}
