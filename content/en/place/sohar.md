## Overview
Sohar is a city on the shore of the Persian Sea, one of the wealthiest cities of Oman in both ancient and modern times, according to al-Idrisi. Merchants come to it, and goods of Yemen are brought to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.156}
