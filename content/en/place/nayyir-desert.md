## Overview
The Nayyir desert is sparsely inhabited. According to al-Idrisi, merchants cross it to Awdaghust and Ghana in the autumn. Water in it is scarce, and it has known water-points. There is a stretch of about fourteen days without water. Caravans travel through it by night and rest at midday. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.107} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.108}
