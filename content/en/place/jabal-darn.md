## Overview
The Great Mount Darn is a long mountain range that stretches from the sea in the west. Al-Idrisi describes it as having fortresses and castles on its summit, and as having olives, figs, grapes, walnuts and almonds. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.229}
