## Summary
Malay is the town where the king of the long Island of the Moon (Jazirat al-Qamar) resides, among the islands of the Indian Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.71}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that adjoining the islands called al-Dabihat lies the Island of the Moon, seven days' sailing away, a long island whose king dwells in the town of Malay; its people say its length eastward is four months' journey, its beginning among the Dabihat and its end opposite the islands of China to the south {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.71}. In this island grow crops, coconut palms, sugar cane and betel, which is the most abundant of its plants {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.71}. Malay is thus the royal seat of this distant island.
