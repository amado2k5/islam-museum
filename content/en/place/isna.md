## Overview
Esna is a town on the west bank of the Nile, one of the ancient towns built by the first Copts, according to al-Idrisi. It has farms, orchards, safety and calm, and many grapes, carried from it to all the land of Egypt. Remains of building and traces of the Copts are still there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.129}
