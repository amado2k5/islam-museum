## Overview
The Shi'b Abi Talib is a place in Mecca where [[group/banu-hashim|Banu Hashim]] and [[group/banu-al-muttalib|Banu al-Muttalib]] were confined, believers and unbelievers alike, along with [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], for three years in the boycott imposed by [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.109} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.110}

## In al-Rahiq al-Makhtum
The book mentions that they withdrew into the shi'b and were confined in it on the night of the crescent of Muharram in the seventh year of the mission. It mentions that Abu Lahab separated from Banu Hashim and remained with Quraysh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.109} The siege was severe, to the point that they ate leaves and hides, and the weeping of their women and children from hunger could be heard from behind the shi'b. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.110}

## Not covered here
The book does not describe the site of the shi'b or its geographical details. What the regional books say about it needs its own source.

## For young visitors
The shi'b is a narrow place between the mountains of Mecca, where Quraysh confined the Prophet's family for three years until they nearly died of hunger.
