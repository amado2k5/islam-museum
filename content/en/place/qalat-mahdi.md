## Overview
Qal'at Mahdi is a strong fortress above a lofty mountain. It has markets, buildings, farms and crops, and its people keep cattle, sheep and large herds. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243}

## Setting and distances
From Safru to Qal'at Mahdi is two stages, and from Safru to Fez is one stage. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243} From Qal'at Mahdi to the town of Tadla is two stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243}

## The surrounding peoples
South of Qal'at Mahdi live tribes of the Zanata: the Banu Sumjun, the Banu Ajlan, the Banu Taskdalt, the Banu Abd Allah, the Banu Musa and the Banu Marawa, among others. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243}

## The road to Sijilmasa
The road to Sijilmasa is given as thirteen stages. It passes Safru, then Qal'at Mahdi, then Nadla, then Dai, and then the Shi'b al-Safa, and it crosses the great mountain on its south side. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 243}

## Its place in the Maghrib
Al-Idrisi, describing the lands of the Maghrib, names it Qal'at Mahdi b. Tawala, together with Tadla, Fez, Meknes, Sala and the other harbours on the Great Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 221}
