## Overview
Rayo is a small city in the land of Calabria on the sea shore, according to al-Idrisi. It has many fruits and vegetables. It lies seven miles from [[place/messina|Messina]] on the island of Sicily. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.627}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name as Rayu, with a fatha on the first letter, a damma on the second and a silent waw, and says it is a city of the Rum (Byzantines) facing the island of Sicily on the east, on the mainland of the Constantinople side. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.116} He says no more about it there. The headword just before it, with a kasra, is a quarter of Bukhara, a different place.
