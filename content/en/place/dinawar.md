## Summary
Al-Dinawar is a city in the Jibal which Jarir ibn Abd Allah reached; its people agreed to the jizya after a day of fighting, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates in Futuh al-Buldan that al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin advanced on al-Dinawar and stayed before it five days, fighting on one of them, whereupon its people consented to pay the jizya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} In another of his reports Jarir ibn Abd Allah marched toward it — within the conquests made by the men of Kufa in the Jibal lands, where al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin was governor reinforced by men of Kufa and Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} In his account al-Dinawar is one of the Jibal cities in which fighting lasted briefly before it passed to its people upon their undertaking the jizya, as was the custom in the conquest of those lands. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
