## Overview
San Francisco is a city in the United States, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2095870}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists San Francisco under no. 2095870 as a populated place with the preferred name «San Francisco»; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit United States of America. It records names in many languages, among them the English «Frisco» and the Arabic «سان فرانسيسكو», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 5391959. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2095870}
