## Overview
Al-Qamashiyya is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] halted and spent the night heading for [[place/najran|Najran]]; from it they went about the peace between the Salmanis and the Khawlanis. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The editor's footnote refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab p. 83 {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}; the source was not read, so no coordinates are given. See [[event/al-hadi-letter-from-najran-and-the-meeting-with-al-daam-at-iyan-286]].
