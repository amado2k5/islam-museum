## Overview
Kasan is a land of Farghana; common people say Kashnan. Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili conquered it, then its people broke their word. The last to conquer it was [[person/nuh-ibn-asad|Nuh ibn Asad]], in the caliphate of al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Kasan as a once-prosperous city beyond the river, in the marches of the Turkish lands, rich in gardens, wide squares and good country, which was ruined when the Turks took control of it. Several jurists and scholars took their nisba from it. He adds that al-Hazimi knew a different Qasan district near Isfahan, and that a scholar he asked about his own nisba thought his family came from this village. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.295}
