## Overview
Kasan is a land of Farghana; common people say Kashnan. Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili conquered it, then its people broke their word. The last to conquer it was [[person/nuh-ibn-asad|Nuh ibn Asad]], in the caliphate of al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
