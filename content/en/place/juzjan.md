## Overview
Juzjan is a region of Khurasan. The routed survivors of the battle of al-Ahnaf reached it, in Tukharistan, and [[person/al-aqra-ibn-habis|al-Aqra ibn Habis al-Tamimi]] was sent to them with horsemen. The Muslims had a setback, then they charged again, routed their enemy, and conquered it by force. From it comes the district of Huf, with the two castles. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
