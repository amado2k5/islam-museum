## Overview
Al-Batna is a land of Banu Sa'd of Khawlan where al-Hadi halted on leaving Sa'da for Najran and sent to them, so that a great army gathered to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} The book's index puts "Batnat Hujur" against p. 160 among other pages, and the museum does not decide that this is the Batnat Hujur of pp. 92-93; it has no coordinates here. See [[event/al-hadi-marches-from-saada-to-najran-and-camps-at-the-fortress-286]].
