## Overview
Qatarrabul is a village in the Sawad of Baghdad. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Nusayr raided it on behalf of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], together with the villages of Maskin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

An additional source is needed.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut vowels the name in two ways, calls it a foreign word, and gives it as a village between Baghdad and 'Ukbara to which wine is attributed, long a resort of idlers and a place of wine-sellers, much mentioned by poets. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.371} It is said, with no speaker named, that it is also the name of a tassuj of Baghdad: what lies east of the Sarat is Badawriya and what lies west of it is Qatarrabul. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.371} Introducing verses by al-Babbagha, he notes that Qatarrabul is north of Baghdad and Kalwadha south of it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.371} He gives verses of Jahza al-Barmaki and a report, through al-Suli, of Abu Nuwas staying there on his way back from Egypt until his money was gone, and adds that a village of the same name faces the city of Amid in Diyar Bakr, where wine is also sold. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.371-372}

Other entries place it in its district. Al-Istan al-'Ali is a kura west of Baghdad with four tassujes: al-Anbar, Badawriya, Qatarrabul and Maskin. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.174} Under al-'Al he quotes al-Baladhuri that the word in a verse means al-Anbar, Qatarrabul, Maskin and Badawriya. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.71} The dihqan's advice to al-Mansur puts Qatarrabul and Badawriya on the west side of Baghdad. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.458}

## The museum's identification
The record (al-Baladhuri) says that al-Nusayr raided Qatarrabul for Khalid ibn al-Walid together with the villages of Maskin. The entries read (vol 4 p.371-372, vol 1 p.174, vol 4 p.71) do not mention the raid or al-Nusayr. They do pair Qatarrabul with Maskin in one district, which fits the record's pairing; that the record's village is this Baghdad tassuj, not the village near Amid, is the museum's reading. The record's point is 9.3 km from the museum's Baghdad point, to its north, as Yaqut notes for the place; the entry's "between Baghdad and 'Ukbara" is not tied to a distance.
