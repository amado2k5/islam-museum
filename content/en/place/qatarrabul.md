## Overview
Qatarrabul is a village in the Sawad of Baghdad. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Nusayr raided it on behalf of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], together with the villages of Maskin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
