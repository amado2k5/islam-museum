## Overview
Bayt Yathi' is a town al-Hadi set out for from [[place/athafit|Athafit]] one morning in Jumada I 286 AH; its notables had written to him and shown their affection, and it was in the hand of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]]; as he drew near its people came out to him, he entered and they pledged. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} He left it on a Friday and reached Athafit the same day. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The heading in the text is "al-Hadi ila al-Haqq's march to Bayt Yathi'" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125}; the body prints the name with different dotting and the heading's spelling is taken; the editor gives no footnote on its site here, so no location or coordinates are stated. See [[event/al-hadi-and-the-people-of-al-asum-at-bayt-yathi-286]].
