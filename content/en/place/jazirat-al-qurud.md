## Overview
Monkey Island is a small island near Ra'ij Island, with thickets, trees and kinds of fruit. Monkeys are so numerous there that it is said they have a leader whom they obey. The people of Yemen catch them to sell in their shops, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.63}
