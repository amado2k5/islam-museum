## Overview
Qumis is a region. Al-Baladhuri mentions that its people corresponded with the Persians in support of Yazdegerd in the year 20 AH, and that it was among the conquests of Urwa ibn Zayd al-Khayl al-Ta'i after Nihawand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
