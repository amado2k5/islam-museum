## Overview
Rafah is a sound town on the road from [[place/gaza|Gaza]] to al-Arish. It lies one stage from Gaza, al-Arish is another stage beyond it, and from al-Arish to al-Warada is a third stage, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 357}

Al-Idrisi describes Gaza as the last city of Palestine on the side facing al-Jifar and the road to Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 356}

Al-Idrisi also describes al-Arish, the town beyond Rafah, as a city that once had two congregational mosques. Most of its land is sand, it has fruit and fruit trees, and it lies close to the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 357}

In his description of the boundaries of Palestine, al-Idrisi says that its length, from Rafah to Lajjun, is about four days, and that its width, from Jaffa to Riha, is two days' journey. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 354}

In his description of Syria, al-Idrisi says that its length begins at Malatya and runs to Rafah, and that the road from Ramla to Rafah takes two days. The whole route, he states, comes to thirty-five stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 378}
