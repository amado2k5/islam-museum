## Overview
Gharghun is a large lake about a hundred miles long. Its water is sweet and it has a great deal of fish, and on it is a mountain of snow. It lies entirely within Margar, and al-Idrisi says that its people fortify themselves. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.840}
