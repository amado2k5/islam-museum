## Overview
The Palace of al-Nawashiq is a palace in [[place/basra|Basra]], the palace of [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan]]. The source mentions that the bandits (al-shattar) were the ones who gave it this name. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
