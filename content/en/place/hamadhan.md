## Overview
Hamadhan is a city of the mountain region. Al-Baladhuri reports that Jarir ibn Abd Allah conquered it in 23 AH, or 24 AH by another account, on the same terms as Nihawand, after an arrow struck his eye and he said he offered it to God. Its people then broke faith, and al-Ala' ibn Wahb fought them until they came down to his judgement, for a land tax, a poll tax on heads, and a hundred thousand dirhams for the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.430-433}
