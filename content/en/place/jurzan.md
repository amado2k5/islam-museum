## Overview
Jurzan is from the second [[place/armenia|Armenia]], and was in the hands of the Khazars. Anushirvan built in it a city called Sughdabil, and settled in it a group of Sogdians and sons of Persia, and made it a frontier garrison. On the side facing the Romans he built the Gate of Firuz-Qubadh, the Gate of Ladhiqa and the Gate of Barqa on the Sea of Trabzon. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
