## Overview
Jurzan is from the second [[place/armenia|Armenia]], and was in the hands of the Khazars. Anushirvan built in it a city called Sughdabil, and settled in it a group of Sogdians and sons of Persia, and made it a frontier garrison. On the side facing the Romans he built the Gate of Firuz-Qubadh, the Gate of Ladhiqa and the Gate of Barqa on the Sea of Trabzon. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Jurzan with a damma, a sukun, a za, an alif and a nun, and says it is a general name for a district of Armenia whose chief town is Tiflis. He quotes Ibn al-Kalbi, from al-Sharqi ibn Qutami, that Jurzan and Aran lie next to the gates of Armenia and were sons of Kaslukhim son of Lanti son of Yunan son of Japheth son of Noah, Aran being the land of Barda'a next to the Daylam. He cites Ali ibn al-Husayn in his Muruj that the kingdom of the Abkhaz is followed by that of the Jurziyya, whom Yaqut takes to be the Kurj (Georgians) in Arabic form, a great nation with a king in his own day called al-Tanbaghi, whose realm is a place called the mosque of Dhu al-Qarnayn, and who follow Christianity. He relays that the Abkhaz and the Jurziyya paid tribute to the lord of the frontier at Tiflis from its conquest and settlement by Muslims until al-Mutawakkil's day, when Ishaq ibn Isma'il took control of it; al-Mutawakkil sent Bugha the Turk, who took it by the sword and killed Ishaq, after which the sultan's authority on the frontier faded and usurpers coveted it until the Georgians took Tiflis in 515. He adds that in the days of al-Mu'tamid, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Tamimi al-Yamami prevailed over this district and Aran. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.125}
