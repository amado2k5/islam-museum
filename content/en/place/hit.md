## Overview
Hit is a town on the lower Euphrates, and the site of the [[event/conquest-of-hit|conquest of Hit and the fortresses of the Euphrates]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The army of Kufa, sent by Ammar ibn Yasir under the command of Sa'd ibn Amr ibn Haram, conquered it; it is also said under the command of Mudhlaj ibn Amr al-Sulami. Its people were granted safe conduct, and half of their church was excepted from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

It is the last place where the conquest of the Jazira from the side of Umayr ibn Sa'd met the conquest of Iraq from the side of Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Idu/Diacira/Hit» (Pleiades no. 894008), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 2000 BCE to 2000 CE. Names recorded there include Idu, Diacira, Hit. {cite:pleiades 894008}
