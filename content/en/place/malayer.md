## Overview
Malayer is a city in Hamadan Province, Iran. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2762240 with the preferred name «Malayer», classed both as an administrative unit and as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the populated place «Hamadan». It records the Persian name in Latin transliteration («Malāyer») and in Persian script («ملایر»), and it links the place to GeoNames no. 125185. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2762240}
