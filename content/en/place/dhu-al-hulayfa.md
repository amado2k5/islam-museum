## Overview
The miqat of the people of [[place/medina|Medina]], where the pilgrims coming from it enter the state of ihram at its mosque. Its site is known today as Abyar Ali. It was mentioned by Ibn Battuta on his way to Mecca. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}
