## Overview
The miqat of the people of [[place/medina|Medina]], where the pilgrims coming from it enter the state of ihram at its mosque. Its site is known today as Abyar Ali. It was mentioned by Ibn Battuta on his way to Mecca. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Dhu al-Hulayfa as a village six or seven miles from Medina, the miqat of the people of Medina. He says it is one of the waters of Jusham, lying between them and Banu Khafaja of Uqayl. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.295} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.296}

He also lists another Dhu al-Hulayfa, the one in the hadith of Rafi ibn Khadij, who said: we were with the Messenger of God at Dhu al-Hulayfa in Tihama and we took a share of captured sheep. Yaqut says this is a place between Hadhat and Dhat Irq in the land of Tihama and is not the place near Medina. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.296}

## The museum's identification
This record is the Dhu al-Hulayfa that is the miqat of the people of Medina, Yaqut's first place of the name. The Dhu al-Hulayfa of Tihama between Hadhat and Dhat Irq is a different place and is not what this record means. The record's statement that its site is known today as Abyar Ali comes from the gazetteer, not from Yaqut.
