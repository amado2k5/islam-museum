## Overview
al-Mukharram: a village near Ayn al-Tamr. al-Baladhuri reports that it was not then called al-Mukharram, but that some of the descendants of Mukharram ibn Hazn ibn Ziyad ibn Anas al-Dayyan al-Harithi settled in it, and it was named after him, according to Hisham ibn Muhammad al-Kalbi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.348}
