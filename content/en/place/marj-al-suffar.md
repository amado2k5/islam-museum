## Overview
A place in Damascus, according to the editors' footnote. Ibn Ishaq mentions that Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As was killed there in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} See [[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As]] and [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. An additional source is needed for the page number.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut lists Marj al-Suffar as a place at Damascus, and in his entry on al-Suffar he describes it as a place between Damascus and the Golan, a desert where a famous battle took place in the days of the Banu Marwan, which they mention in their reports and poems. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.413} In its own entry he gives a verse whose speaker is not named and which mentions the day of Marj al-Suffar, and then a verse by Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As, of whom the entry says that he was killed at Marj al-Suffar, asking whether any horseman who dislikes fighting will lend him a spear when they dismount there (described here, not quoted). {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.101} He places al-Jabiya near it in the north of Hawran, {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.91} and Harib in the district of Damascus in Hawran, {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.204} and names a palace of Umm Hakim at Marj al-Suffar. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.355}

## The museum's identification
The record, from the editors' note to Ibn Hisham and from Ibn Ishaq, is a place at Damascus where Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As was killed in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. Yaqut agrees on Damascus, on the name of Khalid and on his having been killed at Marj al-Suffar, but in the entries on Marj al-Suffar gives no caliph and no date; the famous battle in his entry on Suffar is set in the days of the Banu Marwan and is not linked there to Khalid, and the museum reads it as a different event from the record's. In his entry on al-Hamqatan he quotes Sayf that Abu Bakr commissioned Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As and sent him to the two Hamqatan on the Syrian frontier, without tying this to Marj al-Suffar. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.305} That the Khalid of Yaqut's verse is the record's Khalid is the museum's reading, since the entry gives only the name. The record's point, taken from Wikidata and approximate, is 25.4 km south of the museum's Damascus point, while Yaqut says only between Damascus and the Golan and near Harib and al-Jabiya.
