## Overview
Shuman is a place named by al-Baladhuri in the account of Qutayba's campaign beyond the river. Its ruler had pressed the king of al-Saghaniyan and attacked him, so the king submitted to Qutayba. The text does not record a conquest by al-Mufaddal ibn al-Muhallab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.590}
