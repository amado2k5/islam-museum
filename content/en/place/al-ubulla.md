## Overview
al-Ubulla: a town near Basra. al-Baladhuri reports that its people gathered against Suwayd ibn Qutba, and Khalid ibn al-Walid deceived them by withdrawing by day and returning by night. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.338}

They were broken, a number of them were killed, and a group was drowned in the Tigris of Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.338}
