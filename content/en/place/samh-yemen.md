## Overview
Samh is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted after Yakla, ordered its people to gather to him, stayed some days until Rabi I began and gave the people their stipends; he gave judgment there for a woman against Abu al-Atahiya over an estate, and then put Zayd ibn Abi al-Abbas al-Futayimi over it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.212}. The book gives no coordinates for it and the museum does not guess them. See [[event/al-hadi-leaves-sanaa-by-bir-al-khawlani-and-yakla-for-samh-288|the march to Samh]] and [[event/a-woman-claims-an-estate-from-abu-al-atahiya-and-al-hadi-gives-judgment-for-her-288|the case of the woman and the estate]].
