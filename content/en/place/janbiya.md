## Overview
Janbiya is a town in the inner desert of the land of Abyssinia, far from habitation. Al-Idrisi says its water from the wells often dries up, and that its people seek silver and gold ores. It lies eight stages from al-Najjaga. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.43-44}
