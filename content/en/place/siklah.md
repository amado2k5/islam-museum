## Overview
Siklah is a large city in a level depression of the land, fine in its regions, with continuous buildings and great markets, according to al-Idrisi. To him it is in the middle of the land of Ifriqiya, and it lies eighty miles west of Astarik. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879}
