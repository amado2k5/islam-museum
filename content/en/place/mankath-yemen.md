## Overview
Mankath, which the book also writes Mankit, is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted after [[place/al-akhtut-yemen|al-Akhtut]], where the case of the two men turned on testimony, and where he stayed some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.213}; Abu al-Ushayra then came to him there and he put [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-futayimi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn al-Futayimi]] over Mankath {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. The book gives no coordinates for it and the museum does not guess them. See [[event/al-hadi-at-mankath-the-witnessed-case-and-the-man-put-to-death-288|al-Hadi at Mankath]].
