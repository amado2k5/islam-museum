## Overview
Al-Qayrawan is the capital (misr) of Ifriqiya, founded by [[person/uqba-ibn-nafi|Uqba ibn Nafi]] al-Fihri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Founding
Its site was a thicket of trees and tamarisk, which wild beasts, snakes and deadly scorpions made impassable. The source relates that Uqba prayed to his Lord, and all of that vanished. Then he built, and the people built houses and dwellings with him, and he built its congregational mosque. He had seen in a dream a man calling the adhan in the place of the minaret, so he built the minarets on the spot where the man had stood. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/founding-of-qayrawan|The founding of al-Qayrawan]].

## Restoration
Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath al-Khuza'i repaired the city and its mosque during his governorship for Abu al-Abbas. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Muqaddasi's description
Al-Muqaddasi saw its congregational mosque around the year 375 AH, and counted it greater than the mosque of Ibn Tulun, paved with marble, with its gutters of lead. The city has fifteen lanes (darb), among them the Lane of al-Rabi', the Lane of Tunis and the Lane of Nafi'. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim pp.180-181}
