## Summary
Manh is a small town in the land of Oman at the foot of Jabal Sharm, with palms and springs, on the bank of the al-Falaj river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.158}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that Manh is a small town at the foot of a mountain called Jabal Sharm, with palms and springs, standing on the bank of the al-Falaj river; from Manh to Sohar of Oman westward is two stages, and Sohar also lies at the foot of Jabal Sharm where the al-Falaj river rises, a great river along which villages and buildings run unbroken until it empties into the sea near the village of Julfara {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.158}. He adds that a little snow falls on the top of Jabal Sharm, though the land of Oman is on the whole a hot country {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.158}.
