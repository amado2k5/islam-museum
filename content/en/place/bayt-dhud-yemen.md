## Overview
Bayt Dhu'd is a place near [[place/athafit|Athafit]] that was in the hand of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim]]; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.94} its people appealed to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] against al-Da'am's troops and he sent a governor with them, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.94} and he later made it his base at the time of the confrontation with al-Da'am at Najd al-Dayr and for three days after the settlement. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.96} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.97} The name also appears on p. 65 in the Yemenis' testimony about the rain, and it is not certain that it is the same place. The text gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-athafit-and-the-people-of-bayt-dhud-285]].
