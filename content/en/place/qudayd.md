## Overview
Qudayd: a town on the seacoast between Mecca and Medina; in its quarter stood Manat, the idol of the Aws and Khazraj, and there the Prophet concluded the peace of Banu Damra.

## The place
Manat was erected on the seashore in Qudayd's quarter between Mecca and Medina; the people of Yathrib and Mecca and the pilgrims used to venerate it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

In the year of the brotherhood the Prophet stayed at Qudayd and concluded a peace with Banu Damra ibn Bakr ibn Abd Manat ibn Kinana, that they would not aid an enemy against him {cite:al-waqidi-maghazi}.

## For young visitors
Qudayd is a town on the coast road between Mecca and Medina; near it stood the idol Manat, and there the Prophet made peace with the tribe of Damra.
