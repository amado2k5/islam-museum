## Overview
Qudayd is a town on the coast between Mecca and Medina. In its district stood Manat, the idol of the Aws and Khazraj, and there the Prophet concluded the truce with Banu Dhamra.

## The place
Manat stood on the sea shore in the Qudayd district between Mecca and Medina. The people of Yathrib, the people of Mecca and those who came on pilgrimage all venerated it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

In the year of the brotherhood, the Prophet stayed at Qudayd and concluded a truce with Banu Dhamra ibn Bakr ibn Abd Manat ibn Kinana, on condition that they would not help any enemy against him {cite:al-waqidi-maghazi}.
