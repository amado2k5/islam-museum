## Overview
Nantes is a large, populous, inhabited city with farmland and outlying territory. It is fortified and fine, and merchants equip themselves from it. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies at the bottom of a gulf that extends out from the sea, and about fifty miles from Balfir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.856}
