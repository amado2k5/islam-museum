## Overview
Qafsa is a fine city with a wall and a running river, according to al-Idrisi. Its produce includes henna, cotton and cumin, it has a standing market and crafts, and it lies four stages from Qastiliya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 278}
