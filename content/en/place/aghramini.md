## Summary
Aghramini was an old settled town of the land of Rumaniyya; it had lain in ruins until Heraclius II rebuilt and restored it, and it had tillage and crops, says al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.898}

## Details
Al-Idrisi lists Aghramini among the towns of the clime of Rumaniyya alongside Farwi, Liglagho, Aqranus, Istinus, Mighali Tharma, Ghulwi, Disina and others. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892} From the city of Barsaklafa to Farbasius was one day — Farbasius being an ancient, famous and prosperous town rich in goods — and from there to Aghramini half a day westward, and from Aghramini to Istinus one day westward — Istinus being a fine, prosperous town of many houses and considerable size — and from Istinus southward to the city of Bulya Bisyqus three days, a town near a mountain from which a river emerges, passing through the city until it joins the river Masyunus, both emptying into the Danube. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.898}
