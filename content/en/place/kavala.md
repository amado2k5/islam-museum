## Overview
Kavala is a city in northern Greece. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090168}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2090168 with the preferred name «Kavala», classed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Kavala (Regionalbezirk)». Among the names it records are «Καβάλα» (Greek), «Χριστούπολις» (Greek) and «Christopolis» (Latin). It links the place to Pleiades no. 501523. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2090168}
