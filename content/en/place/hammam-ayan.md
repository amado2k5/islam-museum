## Overview
The Bath of Ayan is a bath in Kufa, named after Ayan, a client of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. It is the one that al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf sent to Abd Allah ibn al-Jarud al-Abdi when he rebelled. The bath had belonged before to a worshipper called Jabir, and Ayan bought it from his heirs. According to al-Baladhuri, Uthman granted Khalid ibn Arfata land beside the bath. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.382} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.395-396}
