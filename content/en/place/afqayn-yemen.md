## Overview
Afqayn is a place where the Akilis and their cousins [[group/al-mahadhir-saada|the Mahadhir]] had vines. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] went out to it and ordered the vines of [[group/al-akiliyyun-saada|the Akilis]] cut, leaving those of the Mahadhir. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} Some days later word reached him that the Mahadhir's animals had been put out there with a party of them, and he sent men to raid it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.193} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} It has no coordinates and the book gives no direction to it. See [[event/al-hadi-razes-akili-houses-and-cuts-vines-at-saada-and-afqayn-287]] and [[event/raid-on-afqayn-and-the-return-of-the-fifth-to-the-mahadhir-287]].
