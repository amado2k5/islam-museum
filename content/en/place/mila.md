## Overview
Mila is a fine city with many trees and abundant water. Its people are a mixture of Berber peoples, and it was under the authority of the ruler of [[place/bejaia|Bejaia]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.265}
