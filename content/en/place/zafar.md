## Overview
Zafar was a city which was the capital of Yahsib, and in it was the famous palace of Raydan. Most of it is now in ruin, and remnants of its people remain in it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.152}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ẓafār» (Pleiades no. 39423), classed as a settlement and a modern settlement, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 2100 CE. Names recorded there include Sapphar, Saphar, Tafra. {cite:pleiades 39423}
