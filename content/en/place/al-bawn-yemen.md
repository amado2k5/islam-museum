## Overview
It is al-Bawn, whose people Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far wronged, in the narrator's account {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216}; when it passed into the hand of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] he took it from Sa'sa'a's rule and put [[person/muhammad-ibn-isa-al-tamimi|Muhammad ibn Isa al-Tamimi]] over it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. In it is a place called Najr to which Ali ibn al-Abbas al-Alawi was sent with a force {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}, and the village of al-Ghayl, a strong fort {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. The people of al-Bawn stood across al-Hadi's way as he passed, and his son Abu al-Qasim charged them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}. The book gives no distance and no coordinates, and the museum puts none. See [[event/al-yafur-and-al-tarif-send-two-men-to-qudum-and-sasaa-seizes-the-horses-at-al-bawn-288]] and [[place/al-ghayl-al-bawn-yemen]].
