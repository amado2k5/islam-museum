## Overview
Qalimriyya is a city on a round mountain, with a strong wall and three gates, in the extreme of fortification. In another passage al-Idrisi calls it a small, settled and populous town with many vineyards and fruits such as apples and cherries, on the top of an earthen mountain so strong that it cannot be fought. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 547} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 726}

## River and land
It stands on a river called Mundiq, with mills grinding on it, many vineyards and gardens along it, and much ploughland running on toward the sea, with sheep and livestock. The two pages disagree on which side the river runs, one saying to its west and the other to its east. At its mouth stands the fort of Munt Mayur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 547} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 726}

## Road and setting
From Qurya to Qalimriyya is four days, and its people are a force among the Rum. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 547} Some distance to its north lies the great mountain range known as al-Sharat, which runs on until it comes near Qalimriyya at the far west; it holds a great many fat sheep and cattle. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 552}

The data summary gave the river as "Bugu"; both pages read Mundiq, and the summary has been corrected.
