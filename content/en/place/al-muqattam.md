## Overview
al-Muqattam: a mountain that extends from the first land of Egypt in the desert to near Aswan. al-Idrisi reports that ochre and lime are extracted from some of its places. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.132}

The author reports that the mountain has ancient remains and treasures that kings hid, and he ascribes to a widely told account the story of a priest with whom the mountain was associated. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.133}
