## Overview
Baduraya: a place in the Sawad of Baghdad. According to al-Baladhuri, the poet mentioned it in the market of Baghdad together with al-Anbar, Qatarbal and Maskin. Some Muslims crossed the river Subudha and came upon Kuthā, the river of the king and Baduraya.
