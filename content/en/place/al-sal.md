## Summary
Al-Sal was a small village of al-Yamama on Wadi Afnan, home to a few Arab families with wells and a flowing spring, on the road from al-Yamama to Basra. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.161}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says Wadi Afnan cuts through al-Yamama from top to bottom, lined with populated villages, adjoining farms, palms and gardens, among them Manfukha, Wabra, al-Qurfa, Abra, Bahisha, al-Sal, al-Amiriyya, Nisan, Barqa Dahik, Salmiyya, Tawdih, al-Maqra'a and al-Majaza {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.161}. He adds that between Salmiyya and al-Sal is one stage and between al-Sal and Hadramat al-Yamama one stage, and that al-Sal is a small village with a handful of humble Arabs, wells and a gushing spring {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.161}. Whoever travels from al-Yamama to Basra goes one stage from Hadrama to al-Sal then one to Salmiyya {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.161}.
