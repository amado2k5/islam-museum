## Overview
Lisbon is one of the cities of western [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]] on the sea, the city now called Lisbon, capital of Portugal. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.186-187}

Its countryside, joined to Shantarin, has a gold mine, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.186-187}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut calls Ushbuna, also called Lishbuna, a city in al-Andalus, joined to Shantarin and near the Atlantic Ocean, on whose shore fine ambergris is found. Ibn Hawqal says it lies at the mouth of the river of Shantarin, and that it is two days from Shantarin along the river to the mine on the coast. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.195}

Yaqut names a scholar from it, Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Harun al-Musmudi, known as the ascetic of Ushbuna, a Berber who heard from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Ayman and Qasim ibn Asbagh. He was reliable in what he wrote and died in year 360. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.195}
