## Overview
Lisbon is one of the cities of western [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]] on the sea, the city now called Lisbon, capital of Portugal. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.186-187}

Its countryside, joined to Shantarin, has a gold mine, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.186-187}
