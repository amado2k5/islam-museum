## Overview
Banjar: a large city in extent, with troops, numbers and wealth, and in it are kinds of tigers and other animals, according to al-Idrisi. Its people carry furs out to the regions of the land. It lies about three days from Sarawas. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.709}
