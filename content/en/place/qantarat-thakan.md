## Overview
A bridge on the river Masan, near the Gate of Arjan. Al-Idrisi mentions that it rivals the [[place/qantarat-qurtuba|Bridge of Cordoba]] in al-Andalus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.421}
