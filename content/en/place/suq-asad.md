## Overview
Suq Asad is a village and market in Kufa. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Asad ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri, brother of Khalid, took it and moved people to it, and it was named after him. His camp was beside it when he went out to Khurasan as its governor. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.402}
