## Overview
Tarfa is an estate and palaces far from the main Nile, populous with people. It has sugar cane farms, from which sugar and fanid are made. Al-Idrisi mentions that it suffices most of the houses of Egypt. It is close to Samasta and to the Nile. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.131}
