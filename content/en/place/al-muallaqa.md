## Overview
al-Muallaqa: a ruined city in Ifriqiya on the sea coast, about sixteen miles from [[place/tunis|Tunis]]. It is said that it belonged to the daughter of the king who seized every ship by force. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.244}

It has great remains, large stones, deep pits and deep cisterns. Among its wonders is what al-Umari relates: that its stones are carried away to what is near it, that their supply never runs out, and that no shortage in their abundance appears. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.244}
