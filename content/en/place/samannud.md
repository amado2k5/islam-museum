## Overview
Samannud is a town in the Nile Delta of Egypt, on the Nile. Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his way to Cairo. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

The coordinates are taken from the GeoNames gazetteer for a modern place bearing the same name. An additional source is needed to verify its match with the town Ibn Battuta mentioned. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
