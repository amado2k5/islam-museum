## Overview
Tibet is a land which joins Khutal and Wakhsh. Al-Idrisi mentions that its people are supplied with iron, silver and Tibetan musk, and that among its cities are Thinkh, Buruan and Awj. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.512}
