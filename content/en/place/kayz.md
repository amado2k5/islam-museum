## Overview
Kayz is the largest city of [[place/makran|Makran]], according to al-Idrisi, and close to [[place/multan|Multan]] in size. It has many gardens, joined farms and agreeable prices. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.172}
