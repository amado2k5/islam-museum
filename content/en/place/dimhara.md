## Overview
Dimhara: a kingdom on the islands of the Indian Ocean, ruled by a queen whom her husband appoints to manage affairs. The queen judges between the people herself, is carried on a chair, wears woven silk, and places on her head a crown of gold. al-Idrisi reports that no one walks in golden sandals except her, and that she gives alms from known sums to the poor of her land in full view of the people. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.69}
