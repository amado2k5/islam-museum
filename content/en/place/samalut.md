## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Samalut as a populated place, under the governorate of al-Minya in its hierarchy. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2777626}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2777626 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Samalut» given as a transliteration of the Arabic. Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «al-Minyā (Muhāfazat)». It records no further names, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 349717. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2777626}
