## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam describes the Byzantine Empire as the Greek-speaking eastern half of the Roman Empire and dates it from 330 to 1453. It says that at its height it reached across Greece and the Balkans, Anatolia, the Levant and part of North Africa. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e396}

It states that the Fourth Crusade conquered [[place/constantinople|Constantinople]], that is [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]], in 1204, and that the empire, severely weakened, fell to the [[place/ottoman-empire|Ottoman Turks]] in 1453. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e396} The entry prints the starting date as 330 B.C.E.; this record, from Nomisma, gives the year 330 with no era marker, so the era label stays the Dictionary's own wording, neither adopted nor ruled on here. It is a short general entry and this record does not rest on it for anything alone.
