## Overview
Siraf is a land on the coast of Fars. According to one report, its fort named Suryanj is the one from which Abd Muslim wrote a safe conduct to its people and threw it to them on an arrow, and Umar ordered it carried out. It is also said that the fort was Shahriyaj, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «[Siraf]» (Pleiades no. 29762), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 1599 CE. Names recorded there include Siraf, سيراف. {cite:pleiades 29762}
