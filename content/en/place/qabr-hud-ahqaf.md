## Overview
The tomb of Hud in al-Ahqaf is a tomb which the people of [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]] and Mahra visit at all times. Al-Hamdani describes it as lying in the red dune, in an elevated cave, at the foot of the valley of al-Ahqaf. That valley runs from the land of Hadramawt to the land of Mahra, a journey of days. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p. 70}
