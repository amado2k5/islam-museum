## Overview
The River of al-Sila is a river in al-Sawad. Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Mahdi ordered it dug. They dug it and revived the lands along it, and its revenue was assigned to the allowances (silat) of the people of the two sanctuaries. The expenditure there was on the condition of fifty years, followed by sharing of half. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.409}
