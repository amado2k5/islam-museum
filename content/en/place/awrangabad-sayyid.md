## Foundation
Awrangabad Sayyid is a small town in the Bulandshahr district of Uttar Pradesh. It was founded in 1704 by Sayyid 'Abd al-'Aziz, a descendant of Sayyid Jalal al-Husayn of Bukhara. The entry notes no further history. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 817}
