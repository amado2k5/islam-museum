## Overview
Yaqut describes Bayburt as a large village and a fine town among the lands of Arzan al-Rum, in the Armenian region. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.307}

## Yaqut's entry
Yaqut lists the place under its headword and says that a learned man of its people told him about it. The entry gives nothing on its history or condition. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.307}

The entry is very short, so this page says no more than it does. The identification with the modern town in Turkey is the record's own, not Yaqut's.
