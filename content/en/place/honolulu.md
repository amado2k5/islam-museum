## Overview
Honolulu is the capital of the state of Hawaii in the United States and the seat of its county. It is a modern place that the classical Arabic geographical works do not mention, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2089856 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Honolulu». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Hawaii (State)». It records names in German, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, Vietnamese, Greek, Russian and Chinese, and in Arabic the form «هونولولو», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 5856195 and to the German National Library authority record 4123398-0. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2089856}
