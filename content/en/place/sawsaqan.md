## Overview
Al-Sawsaqan is an open place with much pleasure, with running water and gardens, according to al-Idrisi. Between it and Merv there are twelve miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut spells the name with a qaf after the second sin and a final nun, and says it is a village four farsakhs from Marw, by the sands at the edge of the desert. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.281} He names Talha ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Ghanim ibn Khayr al-Sawsaqani, who heard Abu al-Fadl Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Razzaq al-Makhwani and died in 527 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.281}
