## Overview
A famous city and fortress south of the Balqa'; it was the seat of kings of the Ayyubid house, then a refuge for Mamluk amirs — taken in 685 from al-Malik al-Mas'ud Khidr b. Baybars — and al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad b. Qalawun was exiled to it, then brought from it to the sultanate in 698 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 661, 769}.
