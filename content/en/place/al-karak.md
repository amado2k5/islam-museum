## Overview
A famous city and fortress south of the Balqa'; it was the seat of kings of the Ayyubid house, then a refuge for Mamluk amirs — taken in 685 from al-Malik al-Mas'ud Khidr b. Baybars — and al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad b. Qalawun was exiled to it, then brought from it to the sultanate in 698 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 661, 769}.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalises the name with a fatha on the first two letters and a second kaf, and calls it a foreign (non-Arabic) word. For him it names a very strong fortress at the edge of al-Sham, in the hill country of the district of al-Balqa', between Ayla, the Red Sea (Bahr al-Qulzum) and Jerusalem. It stands on the crest of a high mountain ringed by valleys, except on the side of the outer quarter. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.453} He adds that the same name belongs to a separate large village near Baalbek, which is a different place. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.453}
