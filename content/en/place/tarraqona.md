## Overview
Tarraqona (Tarragona) is a city on the shore of the sea, with a wall of black and white marble of a kind seldom found, strong buildings and stout towers; it is strongly defended, and a few of the Rum live in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 734}

## Condition
Al-Idrisi says that in his time the city is inhabited, though in ancient times it was empty because it lay between the frontier of the Muslims and the Rum. It is a handsome city with many harmful snakes, a good anchorage and available water. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 734}

## Distances
From [[place/tortosa|Tortosa]] to Tarraqona is fifty miles on one page and forty-five on another, and from Tarraqona east to Barcelona is sixty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 734} It is listed in the province of al-Burtat with Tortosa and Barcelona. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538} It is also called the city of the Jews, which the text does not explain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 555}
