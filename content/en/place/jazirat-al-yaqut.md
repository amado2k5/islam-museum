## Overview
An island near a town that a road reaches. A round mountain surrounds it, hard to climb, and the people of the region catch rubies in it by various tricks, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.520}

What is not covered here: the name of the neighbouring town. An additional source is needed.
