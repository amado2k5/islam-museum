## Overview
The editor says in a footnote: a village near Sana'a in Yemen (Mu'jam al-buldan) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}. The army of [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] lay there with [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}; they fled to it after [[place/alab-sanaa-yemen|Alab]] ({cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}) and the men came out from it to the parade ground in Dhu al-Hijja ([[event/the-sorties-of-17-dhu-al-hijja-and-the-night-fight-at-sanaa-288]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.231}.

## Detail
The museum does not join it with [[place/bayt-dhukhar]] or with [[place/bayt-dhud-yemen]]; Bayt Dhu'd near Athafit was noted in earlier records.
