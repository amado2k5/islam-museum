## Overview
Lamta in the Maghrib is a town in the Far Maghrib, known in the medieval Islamic period for the making of paper. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

It is not the Lamta that lies between Multan and Kabul. An additional source is needed to fix its location and the page number.
