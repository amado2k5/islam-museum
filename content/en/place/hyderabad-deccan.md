## Overview
Hyderabad in the Deccan is a princely state in central India, ruled by a Muslim sultan in alliance with the English, according to Shakib Arslan's commentary on the book "The New World of Islam". {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

He reports that its people number about 13 million, most of them Hindus, and that its capital has a Muslim majority. It has an army of about 30,000 soldiers, most of them Arabs of Hadramawt. He says that the internal rule of the state is limited, and that the English resident minister has limited influence over it. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

The figures come from one source, Arslan in the 1920s; an additional source is needed.
