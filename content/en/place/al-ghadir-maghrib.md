## Overview
al-Ghadir (al-Musayla): a recently founded town, which al-Idrisi describes as pleasant. Its people are Bedouins who have farms and lands. It lies eighteen miles from [[place/al-musayla|al-Musayla]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.261}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
