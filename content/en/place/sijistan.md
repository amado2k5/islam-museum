## Overview
Sijistan is a region which al-Baladhuri mentions in the context of the conquests. He relates that Yazdegerd went to it, and its king honoured and magnified him, but when Yazdegerd asked him for kharaj, the king became hostile to him, so he went on to Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.440}
