## Overview
Urusht is a land of Farghana. Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili conquered it, then its people rebelled. The last to conquer it was Nuh ibn Asad, in the caliphate of al-Mansur, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
