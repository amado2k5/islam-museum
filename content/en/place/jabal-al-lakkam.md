## Overview
Mount al-Lakkam is a mountain that begins at the Red Sea and extends to the regions of Syria, where it is called the Mountain of the Banu al-Lakkam. It ends at Homs, then bends as far as Marash and Shamshat, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.353}
