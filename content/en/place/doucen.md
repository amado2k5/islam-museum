## Overview
Pleiades says only that this is an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas, map 34, square D3, under the name «Doucen». {cite:pleiades 334540}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, it lists this place under the title «Doucen» (Pleiades no. 334540), classed as a fort, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 640 CE. It records no separate names. {cite:pleiades 334540}
