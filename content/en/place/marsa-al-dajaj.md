## Summary
Marsa al-Dajaj is a large coastal town of the Maghreb ringed by a fortification, with a safe anchorage, twenty miles from Tamdafus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.259}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Tamdafus to Marsa al-Dajaj is twenty miles; it is a town of wide extent with a fortification around it, few people, most of whom would flee in the sailing season for fear of fleets, a safe anchorage, extensive lands and continuous fields; its people's harvests are broad, their wheat blessed, and all fruit and meat is abundant and cheap, while its figs especially are carried sliced and dried to every land, for which it is famous {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.259}. From it to the town of Tadallas is twenty-four miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.259}.
