## Overview
Plovdiv is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place and an archaeological site, within the Plovdiv municipality. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2066626}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2066626 with the preferred name «Philippopel», classed as a populated place and an archaeological site; its parent in the hierarchy is the Plovdiv municipality. Among the names it records are «Filibe», «Φιλιππούπολη», «Plovdiv», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 728193. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2066626}
