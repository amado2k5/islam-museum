## Overview
Tarifa is a city on the strait. The report found in al-Maqqari attributes it to Tarif al-Barbari, client of Musa ibn Nusayr, who raided it with the help of Yulyan, lord of Ceuta. It is also said that it was Tarif ibn Malik al-Nakha'i, who settled there within the conquest of Tariq ibn Ziyad. It was the first of the Andalusian frontier posts that the Muslims trod, before the great conquest. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
