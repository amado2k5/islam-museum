## Overview
Warthan is a town in Azerbaijan, which has a bridge like the two bridges of Wahsh and Arshaf that were built in the days of Babak. Marwan ibn Muhammad built it, revived its land and fortress, and it became his estate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the Umayyads
Al-Baladhuri relates that it was confiscated along with the estates of the Banu Umayya for Zubayda, mother of Ja'far. Her agents demolished its wall, and then it was repaired and renewed soon after. In it al-Afshin Haydar ibn Kawus camped during the fighting with Babak, and he fortified it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
