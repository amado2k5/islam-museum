## Summary
Firbuz is a city populous with people and merchants in the land of Makran; al-Idrisi praises its people for their fine dealings, honesty and avoidance of suspicion. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.172}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that the city of Firbuz is populous with people and merchants, that its people are wealthy folk marked by fair dealing, trustworthiness and the shunning of doubtful matters — noble and pure in themselves — and that Firbuz belongs to the land of Makran {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.172}. He notes that between Firbuz and Manjabari lies a city called al-Hur, a small populous town, and that the road from al-Daybul to Firbuz runs by way of Manjabari {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.172}. He also lists it among the towns of the seventh section of the second clime together with Kih, Kiz, Armabil, Band, Qasr Qand, al-Daybul, al-Mansura and others {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.166}. It is thus one of the flourishing markets of Makran, whose people were renowned for integrity in their transactions and generosity of character. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.172}
