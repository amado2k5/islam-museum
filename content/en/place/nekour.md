## Overview
Nekour is an ancient city on the coast of al-Rif in the Far Maghrib, between two rivers, one of which is also called Nekour and whose source is in the land of Kazanaya, according to Ibn Khaldun. It was the seat of the kingdom of the Banu Salih ibn Mansur of Ghumara. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1662-1663}

## Its ruin
Ibn Khaldun mentions that the Magians attacked it with their fleets in the year 244 AH and sacked it for eight days. He also says that Musa ibn Abi al-Afiya destroyed it in the year 317 AH. It remained empty and was never resettled after that. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1662-1663}
