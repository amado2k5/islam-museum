## Overview
Rawar is a city of the cities of Sind. [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] conquered it by force. The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that a woman of [[person/dahir|Dahir]] burned herself in it, together with her maids and her wealth, for fear of capture. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
