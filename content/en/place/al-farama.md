## Overview
al-Farama: the eastern frontier port of Egypt. After Amr ibn al-As reached [[place/al-arish|al-Arish]], he came to al-Farama, where a people were ready to fight. He fought them, defeated them and took their camp, and then went on to Fustat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.298}

It is a site in the events of [[event/conquest-of-egypt|the conquest of Egypt]]. See also [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]].
