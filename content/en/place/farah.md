## Overview
Farah is a city larger than Qarnayn, with about sixty villages and much palm and abundant fruit. It lies three stages from Jazza, and a river runs through it that waters most of its districts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.457}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalizes the name Farah with fatha on both the fa and the ra and a pure ha at the end, and describes a large city in the districts of Sijistan. It has a rustaq of more than sixty villages and a great river crossed by a bridge. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.259} He places it on the right-hand side of a traveller going from Sijistan towards Khurasan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.259}
