## Summary
A mountain overlooking [[place/mecca|Mecca]], at whose foot the [[event/khandama-skirmish|Khandama skirmish]] took place — the only clash in which weapons were used on the morning of the conquest day, between [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]]'s column and a body of Quraysh and Banu Bakr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49-50}.

## The battle
At the mountain gathered Safwan ibn Umayya, Ikrima ibn Abi Jahl and Suhayl ibn Amr among fighters of Quraysh and Banu Bakr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49}. When they met Khalid's cavalry they stood only briefly; some twelve polytheists were killed, then they fled, and three Muslims were martyred {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## For young visitors
A rocky mountain at Mecca where some men of Quraysh tried to fight to keep the Muslims from entering, but the fighting ended quickly and all surrendered.
