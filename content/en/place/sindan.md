## Overview
Sindan is a district on the coast of India. Al-Fadl ibn Mahan, client of Banu Sama, conquered it and prevailed over it, and built there a congregational mosque in which he invoked blessings for the caliph. Then his two sons disputed it. One of them was killed and crucified, and afterwards the Indians prevailed over it, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
