## Overview
Tangier is a city of the far Maghrib on the sea. Musa ibn Nusayr conquered it in his governorship of Ifriqiya. He settled in it, and was the first to settle there and lay out the Muslims' plots of land in it, according to al-Baladhuri. The authority of the Byzantine sultan in Ifriqiya extended from Tripoli to it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.318} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.322}
