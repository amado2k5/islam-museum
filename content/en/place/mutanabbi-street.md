## Overview
The historic book street of [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] on the bank of the Tigris. It is named after the poet al-Mutanabbi, and since the twentieth century it has been the centre of book, manuscript and literary café trade.

## Significance
The heart of Iraqi cultural life; the bombing of 2007 that targeted it became a symbol of Iraq's resistance to death by the word. {cite:wikidata}
