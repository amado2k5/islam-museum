## Overview
Manadhir, the Greater and the Lesser, are districts of Ahwaz. Al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Musa al-Ash'ari besieged them, and that al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad al-Harithi conquered them by force. He killed the fighting men, took the children captive, and appointed Asim ibn Qays al-Sulami as its governor. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In it al-Muhajir ibn Ziyad was martyred, and according to the same report, they set up his head between two battlements. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
