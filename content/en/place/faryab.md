## Summary
Al-Faryab is a town in Khurasan conquered by al-Ahnaf ibn Qays among his conquests — or, as some say, by Amir ibn Ahmar — according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates in Futuh al-Buldan that al-Ahnaf ibn Qays took al-Taliqan by treaty and took al-Faryab, though it is also said that Amir ibn Ahmar was its conqueror. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} Al-Ahnaf then marched on Balkh, the city of Tukhara, whose people made peace for four hundred thousand — some say seven hundred thousand — and he appointed Asid ibn al-Mutashammis over Balkh before moving on to Khwarizm in the river's watering lands, as he narrates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} In his account al-Faryab is one of the Tukharistan towns reached by al-Ahnaf's conquests in the days of Uthman ibn Affan, and the report of who conquered it differs between al-Ahnaf and Amir ibn Ahmar; al-Baladhuri cites both versions, as is his way with conflicting reports without deciding between them here. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
