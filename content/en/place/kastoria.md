## Overview
Kastoria is a city in Greece, and this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2319602) records this place under the preferred name «Kastoria» [German], typed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Kastoria (Regionalbezirk)», an administrative unit. Further names include «Kesriye» [Turkish]; «Καστοριά» [Greek]; «Kosturi» [Albanian]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 735927, GND 4110039-6. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2319602}
