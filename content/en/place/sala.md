## Overview
Sala is a large city on the northern bank of the Nile, and a gathering place for Sudanese trade, according to al-Idrisi. Its people are men of might, and it belongs to the district (amala) of [[place/takrur|Takrur]]. It is sixteen stages from [[place/awlil|Awlil]], and forty days' caravan journey to [[place/sijilmasa|Sijilmasa]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.18-19}
