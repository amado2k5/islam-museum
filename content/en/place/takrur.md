## Overview
Takrur is a city south of the Nile, about two days from Sala by river and by land. It is larger than Sala and busier in trade, according to al-Idrisi. Merchants of the far Maghrib come to it with wool, copper and beads, and take out gold and slaves from it. Its ruler, the Takruri, was known for his troops and his justice. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.18}
