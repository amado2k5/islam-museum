## Overview
Timbuktu is a city in Mali on the edges of the desert. It was a centre of Islamic learning in the Middle Ages, and it has the Sankore Mosque and the Djinguereber Mosque. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} The description needs an additional source from specialist references.

## For Sprouts
A historic city in Mali famous for its earthen mosques and libraries rich with valuable manuscripts.

## For Explorers
Timbuktu is an ancient city in Mali situated at the southern edge of the Sahara, connected to trans-Saharan caravan routes and celebrated as an African center of Islamic scholarship. Renowned for the historic Sankore Mosque and universities, its family libraries have preserved hundreds of thousands of manuscripts in astronomy, medicine, law, and literature.

## For Scholars
A historic city in Mali near the Niger River, thriving since the 5th century AH (11th century CE) as a paramount trade crossroads and educational center in West Africa. Reaching its zenith under the Mali and Songhai empires, it became celebrated for the Sankore madrasa and Djinguereber Mosque, attracting scholars across the Islamic world. Its private repositories preserve an estimated 400,000 manuscripts covering jurisprudence, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, and regional history, documenting the depth of African scholarly traditions.
