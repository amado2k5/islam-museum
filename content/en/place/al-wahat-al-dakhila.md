## Overview
Oases inhabited by Berbers and civilised Arabs, who cultivate wherever water is plentiful. The indigo known for its quality is attributed to them, and the author reports that their donkeys are small. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 122} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 123}
