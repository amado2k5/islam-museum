## Overview
Barzan: a place in Sijistan. Ratbil sent to Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra asking for peace over his land and the land of Kabul, for two million two hundred thousand. He gave him two hundred thousand back, and the peace was concluded at one million dirhams, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
