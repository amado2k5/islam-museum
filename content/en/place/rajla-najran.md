## Overview
Rajla is a village at [[place/najran|Najran]] where lived a man who had made disorder and fled; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] rode to it, pulled down his house, divided his crop and palms and cut his share. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} The editor says Sifat Jazirat al-Arab, p. 124, writes "Rajla" without the final letter, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} and the book's index lists "Rajla" at pp. 130, 200 and 340. The book gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-rajla-the-division-of-a-wrongdoers-crop-and-the-felling-of-his-palms-286]].
