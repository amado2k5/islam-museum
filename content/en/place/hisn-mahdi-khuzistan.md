## Overview
The Fortress of al-Mahdi in Khuzistan is an impregnable fortress of fine construction, on the sea where the mouth of the water gathers. It is about three miles wide, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.398}
