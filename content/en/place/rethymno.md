## Overview
Rethymno is a city on the island of Crete, Greece, and this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2764408) records this place under the preferred name «Rhethymnon» [German], typed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Rethymno (Regionalbezirk)», an administrative unit. Further names include «Resmo» [Turkish]; «Ρέθυμνο» [Greek]; «Rhithymna» [Latin]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 254352, Pleiades 590028, GND 4274296-1. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2764408}
