## Overview
Rada'a is a city in Yemen. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2752099}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2752099 with the preferred name «Radā‘», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2402334. Among the names it records are «Redā‘» and «Ridā‘», «رداع» in Arabic and «Rada'a» in German. It links the place to GeoNames no. 71491. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2752099}
