## Overview
Rada'a is a city in Yemen. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2752099}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2752099 with the preferred name «Radā‘», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2402334. Among the names it records are «Redā‘» and «Ridā‘», «رداع» in Arabic and «Rada'a» in German. It links the place to GeoNames no. 71491. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2752099}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records Rada' with a fatha: a city which, together with Wuthat, was one of the two cities of the Persians of Yemen, on Nasr's authority. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.39} He also records a Rada' that is a mikhlaf of the mikhlafs of Yemen, the mikhlaf of Khawlan, lying between the Najd of Himyar on which the masani' of Ra'in stand and the Najd of Madhhij on which Radman and Qarn stand. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.39} The name has other places for him: al-Rida' with a kasra, the name of a water and a wadi that runs into Dhat al-Ri'al on Abu 'Ubayda's authority, where 'Awf ibn al-Ahwas ibn Ja'far ibn Kilab died, and under the entry Malhub the Rada' is given as the place where Shurayh ibn al-Ahwas died, as printed. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.39; 5 p.191}

## The museum's identification
The record makes Rada'a a city of Yemen and states that no entry for it was found in the first two volumes of the Mu'jam al-buldan. Its entry is in fact in the third volume (p.39), which now attests both a city of the Persians of Yemen and the neighbouring mikhlaf of Khawlan; the text does not say whether city and district are one, and the museum leaves that open. The other Rada' places (vol 3 p.39, vol 5 p.191) are waters, not the city, and are not to be conflated with it. See [[place/yemen|Yemen]].
