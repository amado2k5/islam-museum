## Overview
Jakarta, capital of Indonesia, on the island of Java {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 24}. Its presidential palace hosted President Joko Widodo's reception of the Grand Imam on 22 February 2016 {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 56}; here also stands the Syarif Hidayatullah State Islamic University where the Imam addressed the Muslims {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 79}, and the al-Azhar mosque named by Shaykh Shaltut on an earlier visit.
