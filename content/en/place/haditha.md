## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
The Mu'jam has several entries named al-Haditha; the one that fits this place is «Haditha of the Euphrates», also known as Haditha al-Nura. Yaqut says it lies a few leagues from al-Anbar and has a strong fortress in the middle of the Euphrates, with water all around it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.230} He relays Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir that 'Ammar ibn Yasir, while governor of Kufa for 'Umar ibn al-Khattab, sent an army under Abu Mudlaj al-Tamimi to survey the lands above the Euphrates; Abu Mudlaj conquered them and built the Haditha on the Euphrates. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.230} Among its attributed people he names Suwayd ibn Sa'id ibn Sahl al-Harawi al-Hadathani, who settled in Haditha al-Nura a league from al-Anbar and heard from Malik ibn Anas, Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna and others; Muslim related from him in his Sahih. Yaqut reports the differing assessments of the critics about him, and that he died in Shawwal 240 AH at a hundred years old, blind. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.230} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.231}
