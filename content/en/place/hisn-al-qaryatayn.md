## Summary
Hisn al-Qaryatayn was a fort on the Basra road toward Mecca, where, in al-Idrisi's words, the routes converged. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.160}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts it among the noted lands of the Arabs together with al-Yamama, Hajar, Salmiyya and Barqa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.150} Describing the road from al-Yamama to Mecca he says: from al-Yamama to al-Ard one stage, then to al-Hadhiqa, al-Shaniyya, al-Safra and Sada' one stage each, then to Hisn al-Qaryatayn, which lies on the Basra road, one stage; at al-Qaryatayn the roads meet, and from it to Rama one stage, then on through Taqja, Sarba, Jadila, Qalja, al-Raqiba, Quba', Maran, Wajra, Utas and Dhat Irq in Tihama, to Bustan Ibn Amir and thence to Mecca — stages which, he says, will be described fully under each of these forts, villages and places in their proper positions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.160}
