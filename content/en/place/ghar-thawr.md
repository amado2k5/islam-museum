## Summary
A cave at the summit of Mount Thawr some five miles south of Mecca; the Prophet and [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] took refuge in it at the start of the [[event/hijra|Hijra]], hiding there three nights {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 164}.

## The location
Mount Thawr is a towering mountain, its path rugged, hard to climb, full of stones; it lies south of Mecca toward Yemen. The Prophet took this route because it is the opposite of the Medina road, toward which eyes first turn {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 164}.

## In the Hijra
The Prophet's feet were worn climbing the mountain — it was said he walked on his toes to hide his trace — so Abu Bakr carried him until he reached the cave at the mountain's summit {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 164}. They stayed in it three nights — Friday, Saturday and Sunday — and the pursuers reached its door and turned back {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 165-166}.

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] relates in her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']] that on the way Abu Bakr walked now in front of the Prophet and now behind him, fearing for him, until they entered the cave. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:00:30} He feared, she says, that something might come out at him from the holes of a cave no one had entered for years, while the Prophet reassured him that God was with them, and then slept with his head on his companion's leg. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:01:30} She tells how Abu Bakr, every time he saw a hole, tore a piece from his garment and stopped it up, until only one hole was left, under his foot, and he put his heel into it so that the Prophet could rest. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:02:00} Something struck his heel, she says, and he was in pain but could not speak, and his tears fell on the Prophet's face; the Prophet woke, saw his companion's clothes torn, looked at him with gratitude and love and prayed that he would be with him in Paradise. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:03:00}
