## Overview
The River Bugu is a great river which boats and warships enter, with cities and fortresses along it, and which empties into the Western Sea. Al-Idrisi mentions that its site lies seventy miles from the fortress of Mant Miyur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.726}
