## Overview
The River of al-Mubarak is a river in al-Sawad. Al-Baladhuri relates that Khalid ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri dug it, that al-Farazdaq composed a poem praising him, and that it waters the tilled land of al-Sawad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.408}
