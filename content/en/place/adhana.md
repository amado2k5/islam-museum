## Overview
Adhana (Adana): a noble and populous town with markets, crafts and wide livelihoods, on the river Seyhan. Al-Idrisi says that the distance between it and [[place/tarsus|Tarsus]] is twelve miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.647}
