## Summary
A wide valley of Tihama's valleys between Mecca and Ta'if near Dhu al-Majaz; its gullies and mountain narrows hid the ambush [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]]'s tribes laid for the Muslim army in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn expedition]] in Shawwal 8 AH {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/84-86}.

## Its terrain
The Companion Jabir ibn Abdullah describes it as "a valley of Tihama's valleys, hollow and steep — we kept descending into it" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/85}. Its rough terrain and narrow entries made a fit site for the surprise ambush Hawazin's archers executed in the early morning {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/85-86}.

## For young visitors
A descending desert valley among the mountains where Hawazin's fighters hid among the rocks to surprise the army — before the Muslims rallied and prevailed.
