## Overview
al-Nahrawan: a small town, cut through its middle by the river al-Nahrawan. It lies on the road from Baghdad to the east. al-Idrisi mentions it among the places of al-Sawad. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.668}
