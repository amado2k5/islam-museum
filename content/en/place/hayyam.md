## Overview
Hayyam is a town on the river Ruda in the land of the Aghzaz, seven days' journey from the lake, with the city of Jajan to its north. Al-Idrisi says it is small and at the far end of fortification. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.838}
