## Overview
Karaman is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Karaman Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2102352}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2102352 with the preferred name «Karaman», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Karaman Province. Among the names it records are «Laranda», «Ak Yokuş», «Darende» and «Caramanie», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 309527 and to Pleiades no. 648693. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2102352}
