## Summary
Hisn Milas is a great fortress on a headland jutting into the sea, comely of appearance and solid of building, a lofty town and impregnable castle among the finest and fairest of lands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.594}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from Libiri to the fortress of Milas are twelve miles; it is a broad fortress beside a headland thrust into the sea, comely of appearance and solid of structure, a lofty town and impregnable castle among the best, fairest, most splendid and finest of lands, most like the great capitals in its buildings, its activity, its markets and the goods and amenities it contains {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.594} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}. It stands on the seashore, the sea surrounding it on every side except a single approach on the north, and it is reached by land and by sea {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}. Fine flax in abundance is exported from it; it has excellent, fertile farms, copious running waters and fishing grounds where the great tuna is caught {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}. From Milas to the city of Basina is a light stage {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.595}.
