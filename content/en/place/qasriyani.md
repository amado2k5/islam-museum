## Overview
Qasriyani is a town with a strong fortress and a sturdy stronghold, wide in extent, well built, with broad cultivation, and its air is cold. It lies eighteen miles from Qal'at al-Nisa, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.611}
