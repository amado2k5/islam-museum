## Overview
Qasriyani is a city on top of a mountain with a strong fortress and a firm stronghold, wide in extent and broadly built, with markets in good order, well-built houses, crafts, goods and trade. It is eighteen miles from Qal'at al-Nisa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 611}

## Land and air
It has a wide district and provinces in good condition, with fertile fields, pleasing crops and a cool air. Al-Idrisi calls it the most impregnable place in God's lands and the most solidly built; its mountain holds fields and running water, so that it needs nothing from the plain, and it cannot be overcome or fought. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 611}

## Neighbours
The Salt River (al-Wadi al-Malih) is close to it, on its east. It rises in the groves of Nizar above Jafla, runs over salt marshes so that its water turns salty, then touches the west of Qasriyani's land and passes five miles east of Qal'at al-Nisa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 612} From al-Hajar al-Muthaqqab to Qasriyani is about twelve miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 613}
