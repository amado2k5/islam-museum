## Overview
Dayr al-Jiss: a strong fortress with a wall of gypsum plaster and fired brick, which is a lodging for travellers. It has no crops or trees, and a desert surrounds it on both sides. Its people drink from rainwater, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.683}
