## Overview
Dandama: a small city in the land of Sufala. Between it and Jantama there are two channels at sea and seven days by land, and its people are like the people of Jantama in poverty and humility, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.67-68}
