## Overview
Jaba Island is one of a cluster of close islands, about two farsakhs between one and the next, all belonging to one king named Jaba. Al-Idrisi describes him as wearing gold and adorning himself with pearls and rubies, and says that his coins are stamped with his image. He reports that these islands have good camphor and bamboo. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.81}
