## Overview
Genoa is an old city with fine surroundings, abundant in fruit, with many farms and villages, according to al-Idrisi. Its people are prosperous merchants, and they have a formidable fleet and knowledge of military tricks. He describes it by the pride of its people. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.749}
