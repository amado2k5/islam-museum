## Overview
Marda was the seat of a kingdom. It has remarkable ruins of building, an arch and a water channel, and a palace called the House of Cooking. Al-Idrisi describes it as speaking of kingship and power. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.545}
