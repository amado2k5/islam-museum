## Overview
Al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem, which the book counts the Prophet's night-journey destination and the second mosque to which journeys are made. It holds the name al-Aqsa once covered the whole walled enclosure — its application to today's building a later usage {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 74} — that 'Abd al-Malik b. Marwan built both mosques known as the Dome and al-Aqsa {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 17-18}, and that the esplanade measures some 140 dunams and nine hundred meters.
