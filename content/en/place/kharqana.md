## Summary
Kharqana was a town of the Ushrusana region on the border of Farghana; al-Idrisi describes it as a small but distinguished and settled town with a market and building activity. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.504}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Kharqana among the towns of Ushrusana, a land of orchards, vineyards and abundant crops, watered by a river that drove mills, and containing many towns. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.504} He lists it among the well-known towns on the Farghana road together with Dizak, Marsamanda and Zamin, calling it a fine small urban centre. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.504} From Kharqana to Zamin was twenty-seven miles, and likewise twenty-seven miles to Samarkand, and fifteen miles to Dizak. Zamin itself lies on the Farghana road from Samarkand — a populous, well-settled and fortified town with a wall, a moat and ample provisions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505}
