## Overview
al-Manhi: a branch that leaves most of the Nile near Asyut, where it is called al-Manhi, and flows towards the west. Zamakhir lies at its mouth on the west bank, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.130}
