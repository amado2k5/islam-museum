## Overview
Amwas: a village in the land of Palestine. The plague of Amwas, which occurred in the year 18 AH, is named after it, and in it died [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]] and [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]] and a host of Muslims, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Amwas as a district of Palestine near Bayt al-Maqdis. Al-Bashari says that it was once the chief town, and that its people moved down to the plain and the coast because of the wells, since the town stood on the edge of the hills. Al-Muhallabi says it was a large village six miles from Ramla on the road to Jerusalem, and that the plague began there in the days of Umar ibn al-Khattab. From there it spread through the land of al-Sham, and countless Companions died in it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.157}
