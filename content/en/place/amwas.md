## Overview
Amwas: a village in the land of Palestine. The plague of Amwas, which occurred in the year 18 AH, is named after it, and in it died [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]] and [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]] and a host of Muslims, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
