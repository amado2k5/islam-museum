## Overview
Ulays is a town in the Sawad. In one copy it is written "al-Layth", according to al-Baladhuri. Jaban went out to it with a great host, and [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] met him at the River of Blood. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] made peace with its people, on condition that they be spies for the Muslims against the Persians, and guides and helpers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
