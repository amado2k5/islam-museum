## Overview
Kuwayfa Ibn Umar is a place that the Muslims scouted before [[place/kufa|Kufa]], and found that water surrounded it. It is also said that [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd]] turned toward it the troops of Aspanbar and the Kurds of Bandadh, before Kufa, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
