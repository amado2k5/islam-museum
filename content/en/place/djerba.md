## Overview
Djerba is an island, the largest of North Africa, according to the Pleiades gazetteer. {cite:pleiades 344440}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Meninx/Lotophagitis/Girba (island)» (Pleiades no. 344440), classed as an island, with a time span from 330 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «Meninx» in Greek, «Djerba» in English and «Lotophagitis insula» in Latin. It notes that modern Djerba is the largest island of North Africa. {cite:pleiades 344440}
