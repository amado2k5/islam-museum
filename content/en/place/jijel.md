## Overview
A town on the coast of the sea with two harbours, one of them the Harbour of the Poets, a fine harbour sheltered by the mountain from the north. It is hard to enter except with a guide. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.268}

It lies seventy miles from al-Qall, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.268}
