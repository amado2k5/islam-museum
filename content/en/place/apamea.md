## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut describes Apamea as a fortified city on the Syrian coast and a district of Homs, quotes a line of Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri about it, and notes that some call it Famiya, without the initial hamza. He cites the book of the physician Yahya ibn Jarir that Seleucus, in the sixth year after Alexander's death, built Latakia, Seleucia, Apamea and Baroa, which is Aleppo. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.227}
