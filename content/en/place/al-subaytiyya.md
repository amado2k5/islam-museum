## Overview
al-Subaytiyya: an estate that al-Mansur brought out of the marsh of Basra against the wish of its people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The reason for the name
There was a thicket in it belonging to a landowner called Subayt. The agent held back part of its price from him and beat him, so he stayed at the gate of al-Mansur demanding what was still owed to him until he died. The estate was then named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
