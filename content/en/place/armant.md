## Overview
Armant: a city on the east bank of the Nile, a day's journey from Isna, according to al-Idrisi. It has palm trees and trees that bear many kinds of fruit. Its building is of good Coptic work, and he reports that its like is rare in the lands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.129}
