## Overview
Armant: a city on the east bank of the Nile, a day's journey from Isna, according to al-Idrisi. It has palm trees and trees that bear many kinds of fruit. Its building is of good Coptic work, and he reports that its like is rare in the lands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.129}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Armant as a kura (district) of Upper Egypt, not a city. Two stages lie between it and Qus to the south, and two stages from it to the city of Aswan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.158}

This is the description Yaqut gives for the district, and it adds a point of place to the description given above, which follows al-Idrisi's account of the town on the east bank of the Nile. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.158}
