## Overview
Samasta is an estate with palaces about two miles from the main Nile, populous with people, according to al-Idrisi. It has sugar cane farms, from which sugar and fanid are made. He counts it together with Tarfa among the inhabited estates of Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.131}
