## Overview
Tawara was a settled, populous and thriving city with many markets and flourishing crafts, where no need went unmet in blessings and fruits. It lies about the same short distance, near a quarter of a mile, from Taqarart, that is Miknasa (Meknes), and is also called Bani Tawara. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 244}

## Water
Its water comes from a great river to the south, which divides at its upper end, and the divided waters pass through all its lanes, streets and most of its houses. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 244} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 245}

## Neighbours
Between Tawara and Bani Ziyad are two small towns. One is al-Qasr, a small town on the road from Taqarart to the old market, built by an emir of the Almoravids, who gave it a strong wall and a fine palace; it had few markets, and the emir lived there with the chief men of his cousins. The other, east of it, is called Bani Atush, with houses, gardens, olives, figs, grapes and fruit, all available there and cheap. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 245}
