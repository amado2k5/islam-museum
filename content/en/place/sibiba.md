## Overview
Sibiba is an ancient city with a strong wall of stone, according to al-Idrisi. It has a suburb in which are the markets, and a large flowing spring, on which are their gardens and orchards. It lies one stage from al-Juhaniyyin. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.294}
