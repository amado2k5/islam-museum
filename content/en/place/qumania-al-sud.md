## Overview
Qumania al-Sud is a city to which the Qumaniyyun are attributed. Al-Idrisi explains the name by a river connected to its land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.915} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.916}
