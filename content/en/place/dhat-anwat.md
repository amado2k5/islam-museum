## Overview
A great green tree on which Quraysh and the Arabs used to hang their weapons and slaughter animals beside it. The Companions passed by it on their way to Hunayn, and the account reports that the Prophet said: "This is what the people of Moses did." {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka}

Its location has not been fixed by coordinates. An additional source is needed.
