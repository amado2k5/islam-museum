## Overview
Bayda (in Fars): a large city with a wide rustaq (district). It was named "the white" because of the whiteness of its palaces. Its people are well-to-do, and their dress is the dress of the Iraqis. It lies twenty-four miles from Sahik. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.432}
