## Overview
Al-Najagha is a small town on the bank of the river. Its people are farmers who grow sorghum and barley, which they live on and trade. The trade of the town is small, its useful crafts are few, fish is plentiful and easy to take, and milk abundant. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 43}

## The river road
Between this town and the town of Marqata mentioned before is six days downstream, and more than ten days upstream, as far as it can be managed; their boats are small. Al-Idrisi names no wall or ruler for it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 43}

## Neighbours
Among the coastal cities of Abyssinia are Zalagh, Manquna, Aqnat and Baqti, with the settlements of the villages of Barbara joined to them; the supplies of these villages are the fish their people catch, milk and the grain they bring from their villages on the bank of the river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 43}
