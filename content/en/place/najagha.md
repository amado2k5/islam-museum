## Overview
Najagha is a small city on the bank of the river that cuts through the land of Abyssinia. Al-Idrisi mentions that its people are farmers who grow maize and barley and supply their livelihood from it, and that it lies six days from Markata. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.43}
