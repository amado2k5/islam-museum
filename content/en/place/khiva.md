## Overview
Khiva is a town in Khwarazm in present-day Uzbekistan, and it was the capital of the Khanate of Khiva. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} What is said here about its history needs an additional source.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2733465, typed as populated place, with the preferred name «Chiwa» under «Usbekistan» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including Chiva, Хива, Jiva. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733465}
