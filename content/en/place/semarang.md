## Overview
Semarang is a city on the island of Java in Indonesia, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769270}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2769270 with the preferred name «Semarang» (English), classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the island of Java. Among the names it records are «Семаранг» (Ukrainian), «Σεμαράνγκ» (Greek), «スマラン» (Japanese) and «سمارانگ» (Persian). It links the place to GeoNames no. 1627896. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769270}
