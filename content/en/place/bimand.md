## Overview
Bimand: a land in [[place/kirman|Kirman]]. The army of [[person/mujashi-ibn-masud-al-sulami|Mujashi ibn Mas'ud]] was destroyed there first, then he conquered it by force, left its people, and gave them safe-conduct. In it is a palace known as the palace of Mujashi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
