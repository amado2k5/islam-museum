## Overview
Nastasiyya is a stronghold and a small city, which al-Idrisi describes as fertile and fortified. It lies twelve miles from the sea, and the valley of Musa joins it from the waters of several rivers. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.617}
