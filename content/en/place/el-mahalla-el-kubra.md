## Overview
El Mahalla El Kubra is recorded in the German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer as a populated place at about 30.97° N, 31.17° E, at an altitude of 17 metres. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769263}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2769263) records this place under the preferred name «Mehalla el-Kobra», typed as a populated place, with the further names «al-Mahalla al-Kubra» [Arabic], «المحلة الكبرى» [Arabic]. Its parent unit is «al-Gharbiyya (Muhafazat)», an administrative unit. The record carries the identifiers GeoNames 360829. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769263}
