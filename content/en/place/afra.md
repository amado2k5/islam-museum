## Overview
Afra: a water-place in Palestine, where the Romans agreed to crucify [[person/farwa-ibn-amr-al-judhami|Farwa ibn Amr al-Judhami]]; they struck off his neck and crucified him there. See [[event/martyrdom-of-farwa-al-judhami|the martyrdom of Farwa]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}

## Its name
Farwa mentions it in his two verses: "at the water of Afra, above one of the mounts". The editors, citing the commentary on al-Mawahib by al-Zurqani, record it as "Afra", with a fatha on the ayn, a sukun on the fa and a long alif, and note that the shortening of it in the verse is a poetic necessity, and that the originals write it as "Afra". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the name in two neighbouring entries and does not merge them. The first is "Afra'", with a fatha on the ayn, a sukun on the fa and the long ending, the feminine of "a'far"; he explains "'ufra" as a whiteness that is not bright but close to the colour of earth, and says that Afra' is a fortress in the districts of Filastin near Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem). {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.131}

The second is "Ifra", with a kasra on the ayn and the short ending, which he calls a water in the region of Filastin. Here he relays Ibn Ishaq's report of Farwa ibn Amr ibn al-Nafira al-Judhami, of the Nufatha: the Romans' governor over the Arabs near them, whose home was Ma'an and the country around it in al-Sham, who sent a messenger to the Messenger of God with news of his Islam and a gift of a white mule. When the Romans learned of it they sought him, imprisoned him, and then led him out to crucify him at a water called Ifra in Filastin. Yaqut gives the verses Farwa spoke at that moment, which name the water, and says they struck off his neck and crucified him at that water. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.131} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.132}

He also quotes a line of Adi ibn al-Riqa' al-Amili that names Ifra and its rijla; the line speaks of recognising there the site of a dwelling reduced to ash and stones. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.131} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.132}

## The museum's identification
This record is the water of Farwa al-Judhami. Yaqut gives it under "Ifra" (kasra, short ending), not under the long-ended "Afra'", which is a fortress near Jerusalem, and he does not join the two. The museum puts the spelling "Afra'" that the editors of Ibn Hisham take from al-Zurqani together with Yaqut's "Ifra" because both stand for the same water in the story of Farwa and in Filastin; that joining is the museum's reading, not Yaqut's wording. Yaqut places the water no more closely than "in the region of Filastin", whereas the record's present coordinates lie in Najd, by the record's own coordinate note, not in Filastin; they do not fit Yaqut's placing and remain unconfirmed.
