## Overview
Masqat is a city built by Anushirwan, according to al-Baladhuri. Its people made peace with Salman ibn Rabi'a, among the kings of the mountains, after he crossed the Kur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The king of Masqat was one of the well-known kings of Anushirwan, and his kingdom had ended by al-Baladhuri's time. In our data it is attributed to [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
