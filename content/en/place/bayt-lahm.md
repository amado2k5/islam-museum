## Overview
Bethlehem: a place with a finely built church. al-Idrisi reports that it is the place where Christ was born, that the tomb of Rachel is on the road there, and that it lies about six miles from Jerusalem. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.362}
