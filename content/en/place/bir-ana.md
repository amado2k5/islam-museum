## Overview
The well of Ana: one of the wells of [[group/banu-qurayza|Banu Qurayza]], in their property area in [[place/medina|Medina]]. The Prophet stopped by it when he came to Banu Qurayza. {cite:ibn-hisham-wustenfeld-1858 p.149}

No coordinates have yet been fixed for its location; according to the report it lies in the property of Banu Qurayza north of Medina.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives Bi'r Anna, with a fatha on the hamza, a doubled nun and a shortened alif, as Ibn Ishaq has it, and says that Ibn Hisham the grammarian held it to be Bi'r Anni, with the nun and the ya both doubled. He then gives Ibn Ishaq's sentence that when the Prophet came to Banu Qurayza he alighted at one of their wells and the people caught up with him. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.298-299} In his entry on Una he adds, on the authority of Nasr, that a well of that name in Medina is among the wells of Banu Qurayza and that the Prophet alighted there when he had finished the Trench expedition and set out for Banu al-Nadir; the name is written there without vowels. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.257} The two entries therefore differ on the destination, Banu Qurayza in the first and Banu al-Nadir in the second, and Yaqut does not reconcile them. He gives Bi'r Abi 'Inaba as a separate well about a mile from Medina, where the Prophet reviewed his companions on the way to Badr. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.301} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.161}

## The museum's identification
The record, from Ibn Hisham, is a well of Banu Qurayza by which the Prophet halted when he came to them, which agrees with Yaqut's entry from Ibn Ishaq. The record's coordinate note adds the name Bir Abi 'Inaba; Yaqut gives each well its own entry and, in the entries read, does not say they are one, so the museum leaves that question open. The record places the well north of Medina; Yaqut gives no direction or distance for Bi'r Anna. The record's point is 0.8 km from the museum's Medina point.
