## Overview
Shahdhuruj is a notable, populous city in the middle of the island of Shahdhuruj, a large island ringed by standing water. It has markets, standing crafts, lasting produce and much traffic, and it is also strongly placed, on the edge of the Stinking Land on its east. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 928}

## The way to it
From the city of Dadmi to Shahdhuruj, northward, is five days, over rough mountains and hard paths. The traveller leaves Dadmi and crosses the mountain, up and down, in one stage, then goes to the side of the Shuran river in another, then from the river to a desert halting place, and then to the pond of Shahdhuruj. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 928}

## River and the Stinking Land
The river of Shahdhuruj rises in the Asqasqa mountain, from which five rivers come out, one of them the river of Shahdhuruj. Three of them are sources of the Itil, which run west and then join. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 928} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 929} The Stinking Land is a long, rough, black stretch ten days long and bare of plants, and at its northern end lies the city of Suqmaqiya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 929}
