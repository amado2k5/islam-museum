## Overview
It is Madar, to which [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] went with six Tabari horsemen when he was put out of Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.220}. [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] later halted there when Abu al-Atahiya came with an army of Hamdan, and stayed some days until his brother Abdullah ibn al-Husayn came to him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. The book gives no coordinates and no distance, and the museum puts none. See [[event/the-rising-of-ibn-mahfuz-at-sanaa-and-ali-ibn-sulayman-leaves-for-madar-288]] and [[event/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-comes-from-the-hijaz-and-al-daam-declines-to-march-288]].
