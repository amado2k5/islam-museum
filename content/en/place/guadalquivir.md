## Overview
The Guadalquivir is the great river of al-Andalus, which passes through [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]]. Al-Idrisi reports that it emerges from a mountain, plunges under the earth and then reappears. It passes [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], [[place/seville|Seville]] and Cadiz, then flows into the Sea of Darkness. He says its source is one with that of the river of Murcia. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}
