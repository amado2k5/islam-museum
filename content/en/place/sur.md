## Overview
Sur is a populous city on the bank of the Persian Sea in the land of Oman. Fish is caught there, and its people drink from wells, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.155}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut places Tyre in the fourth climate, at 59 and a quarter degrees of longitude and 33 and two-thirds of latitude. He notes that the word means a horn in the language, and that the commentators gave this sense to the word in the Qur'anic passage about the trumpet. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.433}

He describes it as a famous city where ascetics and scholars lived, from which several imams came, and which was a frontier post of the Muslims. It juts into the sea like a hand on a forearm, and its only land approach is through its gate. Muslims took it in the days of Umar ibn al-Khattab and held it until 518 AH, when the Franks camped before it and besieged it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.433}
