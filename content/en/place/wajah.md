## Overview
Wajah is a fortress in Tabaristan. The marzban of Jurjan took refuge in it when they rebelled against [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]], and around it are thickets and brushwood. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
Al-Baladhuri relates that Yazid camped before it for seven months and set up a mangonel. Then a man showed them a path with leather ladders, and Jahm ibn Zahr climbed it and conquered it. He killed the captives in the Valley of Jurjan until the blood flowed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
