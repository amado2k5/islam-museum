## Overview
al-Ukhdud: the trench dug for the People of the Trench mentioned in the Holy Qur'an. According to Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari, it is in [[place/najran|Najran]] in Yemen. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}

It is connected with [[event/massacre-of-najran|the massacre of Najran]], being the site of the trench. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Anagrana/Negram» (Pleiades no. 39286), classed as ruin and settlement, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 2099 CE. Recorded names include «Anagrana», «Negram» and «Negrana». It describes the place as a large archaeological site in the Najran oasis of present-day Saudi Arabia, known as al-Ukhdud. {cite:pleiades 39286}
