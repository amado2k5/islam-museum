## Overview
al-Ukhdud: the trench dug for the People of the Trench mentioned in the Holy Qur'an. According to Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari, it is in [[place/najran|Najran]] in Yemen. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}

It is connected with [[event/massacre-of-najran|the massacre of Najran]], being the site of the trench. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}
