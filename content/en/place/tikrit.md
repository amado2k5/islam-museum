## Overview
Tikrit is a city on a river. The Arabic text in our data says that the river is the Euphrates, while the English version says it is the Tigris; an additional source is needed. According to al-Baladhuri, al-Nusayr and Hudhayfa ibn Mihsan raided it after the Day of the Bridge and took plunder of cattle and sheep. It is related from Abu Mas'ud that the two gave its people safe conduct and wrote them a letter, which Utba ibn Farqad al-Sulami carried out when he conquered Tirhan and [[place/mosul|Mosul]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.348}
