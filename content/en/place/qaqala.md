## Overview
A city where silk is abundant, and its people raise silkworms. Qaqali garments and Qaqali silk are made there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.207}

It lies ten stages from [[place/qashmir|Qashmir]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.207}
