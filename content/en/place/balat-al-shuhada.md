## Overview
Balat al-Shuhada (Poitiers): a place in the land of the Franks. The army of Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah al-Ghafiqi, the commander of al-Andalus, was struck there in Ramadan in the year 114 AH, and the battle is known by it, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.272}
