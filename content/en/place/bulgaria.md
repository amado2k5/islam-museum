## Summary
Bulgaria is a Balkan state with an old Muslim minority. The Oxford Dictionary of Islam has a short entry, Islam in Bulgaria. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e381}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The entry says that about thirteen percent of Bulgaria's people are Muslim, mostly Hanafi Sunnis, and that [[place/ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] rule lasted from the fourteenth century to 1878 in the north and 1908 in the south. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e381}

It explains that Bulgarian Muslims form four distinct ethnic groups, Pomaks, Turks, Tatars and Roma (the entry uses an older word for the last), and that how far each group identifies with Islam and practises it varies considerably. It closes by pointing to its entry on Islam in Europe. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e381}

The percentage and the grouping are the Dictionary's own wording and have not been compared here with a census.
