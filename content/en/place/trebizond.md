## Overview
Trebizond is a fine city on the bank of the sea, which was a market and a destination for merchants, according to al-Idrisi. He mentions that between it and Constantinople there are nine and a half sea passages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.907} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.908}
