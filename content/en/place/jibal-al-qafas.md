## Overview
Mountains bordering the Persian Sea and joined to the deserts of Makran. In them live nomadic Kurdish tribes called the Baluṣ, who live on livestock and palms, and whom people fear, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.441}
