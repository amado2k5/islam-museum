## Overview
Majanna was one of the famous markets of the Arabs. It was held in the sacred months, together with [[place/ukaz|Ukaz]], [[place/dhu-al-majaz|Dhu al-Majaz]] and others, since the security that trade needed was absent in the peninsula except in these months. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46} See [[concept/economic-condition-jahiliyya|Economic conditions]].

## Not covered here
The site of the market at Marr al-Zahran and the accounts of it held by the historians have not yet been added.

## For young visitors
Majanna was an old market near Mecca, where Arabs gathered in the season of the Hajj.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut vowels the name Majanna with a fatha and a doubled nun, and makes it the name of an Arab market of the Jahiliyya, counting 'Ukaz, Majanna and Dhu al-Majaz as the markets of that time. From al-Asma'i he says Majanna was at Marr al-Zahran near a mountain called al-Asfar, below Mecca at a barid's distance, and that it was held for ten days at the end of Dhu al-Qa'da, with the 'Ukaz market twenty days before it and the Dhu al-Majaz market eight days of Dhu al-Hijja after it, then the people moved to 'Arafa on the ninth, the day of al-Tarwiya. From al-Dawudi he says it is at 'Arafa; it is also said to be a place some miles from Mecca belonging to Banu al-Di'l, and al-Asma'i also makes it a mountain of Banu al-Di'l in Tihama beside Tufayl. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.58-59}

## The museum's identification
The record makes Majanna one of the Arab markets held in the sacred months with 'Ukaz and Dhu al-Majaz, and its "not yet included" note says the market's site at Marr al-Zahran had not yet been added; Yaqut's entry (vol 5 p.58-59) confirms Marr al-Zahran from al-Asma'i and adds the order and lengths of the three markets and the disagreement over the site. The record's point (35.845, 8.536), however, lies in North Africa, 3,427.7 km from the museum's Mecca point, and matches none of the places the entry names; this is a remark on the coordinates, not on the text, and this packet does not change them.
