## Overview
Majanna was one of the famous markets of the Arabs. It was held in the sacred months, together with [[place/ukaz|Ukaz]], [[place/dhu-al-majaz|Dhu al-Majaz]] and others, since the security that trade needed was absent in the peninsula except in these months. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46} See [[concept/economic-condition-jahiliyya|Economic conditions]].

## For young visitors
Majanna was an old market near Mecca, where Arabs gathered in the season of the Hajj.
