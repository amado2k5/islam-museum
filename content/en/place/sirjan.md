## Overview
Sirjan is a fortified city, its buildings of stone because of the scarcity of wood, where officials and governors stay. It has wealthy people with money, and it is the greatest city of Kirman. Al-Idrisi describes its people as chaste and good in their dealings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.433}
