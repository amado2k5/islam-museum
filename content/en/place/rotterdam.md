## Overview
Rotterdam is a city in South Holland in the Netherlands, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2086056}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2086056 with the preferred name «Rotterdam» (German), classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the province of Zuid-Holland. Among the names it records are «Roterodamum» (Latin), «Ρότερνταμ» (Greek), «Роттердам» (Russian) and «روتردام» (Arabic). It links the place to GeoNames no. 2747891, to a GND record and to two Zenon identifiers. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2086056}
