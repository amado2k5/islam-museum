## Overview
Shadhkhu is a city on a small river that falls into the Eastern Sea, four stages from the sea. From the city of Bajah to Shadhkhu is four stages, and from Shadhkhu to the city of Bishhiyar nine stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 213}

## Bishhiyar
Bishhiyar is a city with a chief appointed by the Baghbugh, with horses, men, retinue, servants and great rule, who fights the Turks entering his land from those who border him, the Turks called the Khaqaniyya and the Khazlajiyya. This ruler has horses detailed to guard gates there in the great mountain that divides them from the Turks, and their dress and the Turks' dress are the same, with no difference between them. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 213}

## In the region
From Bishhiyar to the city of Qasha is eight stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 213} Shadhkhu is named in the tenth part of the second clime with Bajah, Bishhiyar, Qasha and Sarkha. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 210}
