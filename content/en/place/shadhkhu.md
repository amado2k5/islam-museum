## Overview
Shadhkhu is a city on the eastern sea, according to al-Idrisi. It lies four stages from Bajah, the sea separates it from there by another four stages, and it lies nine stages from Bashahyar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.213}
