## Overview
Minas is a place named in the account of Abu Muhammad and Ibn Bustam: [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] brought out the people of Minas and drew them up with Banu al-Harith before a fort that lay below Minas. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} Abu Muhammad fought them with an ambush, drove them off and razed the fort. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} The book does not fix its location and the editor gives no footnote on it, so it carries no coordinates here. See [[event/pledge-at-najran-and-the-fight-below-minas-286]].
