## Summary
Antartus was a small coastal town with a strong wall; it served as the port of Homs, two stages from that city, with the island of Arwad lying offshore opposite it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.375}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says the road from Arqa to Antartus along the coast leaves the town of Arqa for the fort called Shanj and then reaches Antartus, which lies at the end of a great bay most of which is skirted by stretching mountains; crossing this bay takes fifteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.375} Opposite the town in the sea lies the large island of Arwad, containing a great, well-built church, lofty, impregnable and furnished with iron doors like a fortress. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.375} From Antartus southward overland to the fortress of al-Khawabi on the mountaintop was fifteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.375}
