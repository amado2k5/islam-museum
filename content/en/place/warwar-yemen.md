## Overview
Warwar (printed with the doubled w and r) is a place to which [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] withdrew after getting out wounded, where news kept reaching him and he stayed rousing Hamdan, writing to al-Ja'fari, while [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] wrote to Ibn Khalaf about peace {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.250} ([[event/al-hadi-at-warwar-treats-with-ibn-khalaf-and-returns-to-saada-on-news-of-jaftam-290]]). The book gives neither its distance from Sana'a or Sa'da nor coordinates, and the page image confirms the reading of the name.
