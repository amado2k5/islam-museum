## Overview
Bausa: an island, one of two islands belonging to the king of Qamrun. Its people are fair-skinned, its women are beautiful, and they have courage and strength. The author reports that they used to cut the road on people with swift ships. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.90}
