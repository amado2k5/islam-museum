## Overview
Damuna is one of the two well-known villages of the [[place/al-fayyum|Fayyum]], together with Lahun {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.

## Water
Water reaches it by flow from the great canal of the Fayyum, and its vines are watered from it by waterwheels turned by oxen {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.
