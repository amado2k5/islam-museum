## Overview
Yaqut describes Jilliqiyya as a region near the Atlantic coast, on the northern side of al-Andalus at its far western end. Musa ibn Nusayr reached it when he conquered al-Andalus. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.157}

## Yaqut's entry
Yaqut says it is a land that is not pleasant for anyone except its own people. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.157} He cites Ibn Makula that the nisba al-Jilliqi goes back to a town of the Rum lands bordering al-Andalus called Jilliqiyya. From the region came Abd al-Rahman ibn Marwan al-Jilliqi, one of the rebels in al-Andalus in the days of the Umayyads, about whom a history was written. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.157}

The identification with the modern Galicia in north-west Spain is the record's own; the text places the region in the north of al-Andalus.
