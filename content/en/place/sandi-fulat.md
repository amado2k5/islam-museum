## Overview
The island of Sandi Fulat is a great island with sweet water, crops, rice and coconuts. Their king is called Zunbad, and the people wear loincloths, one wrapped round the waist and one over the shoulders. It lies ten days from the island of [[place/sanf-island|Sanf]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 89}

## A gate of China
It is ringed on the China side by rough mountains, and the winds there are fierce; it is one of the gates of China. From it one sails to the city of Khanfu in four days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 89}

## The gates of China
The gates of China number twelve, being rocky heights in the sea with a gap between each two, through which one passes to a particular place among the Chinese cities on the coast. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 89} Before it al-Idrisi mentions the island of al-Mayid, with several towns, larger than the island of al-Mawja in length, wider and more fertile, where the ships of the Chinese gather. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 89}
