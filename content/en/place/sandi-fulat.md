## Overview
Sandi Fulat is a great island with fresh water, crops and rice, according to al-Idrisi. It is ruled by a king called Zanbad, and its people wear loincloths (futa). Rugged mountains surround it on the side of China, and the author considers it a gate of China. It lies ten days from [[place/sanf-island|Sanf Island]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.89}
