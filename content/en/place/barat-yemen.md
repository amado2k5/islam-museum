## Overview
Barat is a great, towering, rugged mountain in the land of [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe|Shakir]] of Hamdan which no ruler had ever entered, with only three ways up, its people a Hamdani group called "Dahma"; the editor's footnote refers to Sifat jazirat al-Arab. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.82} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.83} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] halted before it at "al-Hajar" and then "Udla" below the mountain, climbed it and fought its people in Safar 285 AH, then gave them security and appointed Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan over them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.83} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.84} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.85} The text gives no coordinates and none is supplied. See [[event/al-hadi-expedition-to-barat-285]].
