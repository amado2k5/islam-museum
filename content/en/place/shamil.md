## Overview
Shamil is a populous island at the far end of the Sanfi sea, four days from the coastal city of al-Sanf, according to al-Idrisi. It has many people, sugar cane, and large fish which suffice its people instead of eating meat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.85}
