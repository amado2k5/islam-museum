## Overview
The island of Shamil lies four days from the coastal city of Sanf, at the end of the Sea of Sanf. It is a populous island with a thickly settled people, with wheat, rice, plenty of bananas and sugar cane, and large, fine-tasting fish whose eating spares one from eating meat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 85}

## Neighbouring islands
From Shamil to the island of Ashura is four days; it is thinly peopled, its land rough with many scorpions and vipers, and its mountains continuous. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 85}

## Malay
From Ashura to the island of Malay is a light day. It is a large island running from west to east, with a city where the king of al-Jarz lives; his dirhams are silver, called Tatari dirhams, and he has soldiers, elephants and many ships. It has bananas, coconuts, rice and cane. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 85}
