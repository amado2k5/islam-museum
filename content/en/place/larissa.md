## Overview
Larissa is a city in Greece and the capital of the region of Thessaly; it is the ancient capital of Thessalian Pelasgiotis. The German gazetteer also records a Turkish name for it, «Yenişehr-i Fenar». It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to the two gazetteers.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Larissa» (Pleiades no. 540905), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 2099 CE. Among its recorded names are «Larissa» from 750 BCE to 640 CE, «Larisa Pelasgis» without dates, and «Larissa» in the twenty-first century. Its description calls it the ancient capital of the Thessalian Pelasgiotis, the modern Larisa in Greece. {cite:pleiades 540905}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2298511 with the preferred name «Larisa» (German, transliterated), classed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Larisa (Regionalbezirk)». It records the ancient Greek «Larissa», «Λάρισα» and «Λάρισσα», the Russian «Лариса» and the Turkish «Yenişehr-i Fenar», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 258576, Pleiades no. 540905, the Arachne identifier 8027 and the German National Library authority record 4034588-9. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2298511}
