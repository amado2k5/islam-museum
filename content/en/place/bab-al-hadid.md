## Overview
Bab al-Hadid (the Iron Gate): a fortress and an iron gate in a mountain cut through by a valley. The author quotes Salam the interpreter's description of it as a gate with two leaves, with locks and bolts on it. The guards strike on it so that they can hear what is behind it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.936} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.937}
