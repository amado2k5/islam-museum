## Overview
Arbus: a city on level land, with running springs that never dry up, the best known of which is the Spring of Rabah. al-Idrisi reports that its saffron rivals the Andalusian, and that it lies three stages from Qayrawan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.292}
