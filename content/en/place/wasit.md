## Overview
Wasit is a city built by [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] in the year 83 or 84 AH. According to al-Baladhuri, he built its mosque, its palace and the green dome in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.407}

It was a land of reeds, and so it was called Wasit of the Reeds (qasab). It is at an equal distance from al-Ahwaz, [[place/basra|Basra]] and [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.407}

Al-Hajjaj wrote to [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik]] that he had built a city in a hollow (karsh) of the land between the mountain and the two garrison cities, and so the people of Wasit were called the Karshiyyun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.407}

The founding date is given in two versions (83 or 84 AH); an additional source is needed.
