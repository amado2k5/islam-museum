## Overview
Kalkisar is a populous town on the sea. It is one of two neighbouring towns that lie two days from [[place/jarbatan|Jarbatan]], and it has rice and grain, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.192}
