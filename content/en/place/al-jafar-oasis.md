## Overview
al-Jafar: one of two populated towns at the bottom of the oases. In the past its land was cultivated with saffron, safflower, indigo and sugar cane. The author describes it as a village like a fortress, surrounded by palm trees, with abundant water. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.123}
