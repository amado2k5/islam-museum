## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Bajjana is a city of al-Andalus in the district of Elvira, ruined by Yaqut's day, its people having moved to Almeria; he names two scholars connected with it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.339}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the name with a fatha on the ba, a doubled jim, an alif and a nun, and says it is a city of al-Andalus among the districts of the kura of Elvira, now ruined, its people having moved to al-Mariyya. It is two farsakhs from al-Mariyya and a hundred miles from Granada, which he reckons as thirty-three farsakhs. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.339}

## People of Bajjana
Yaqut names Abu al-Fadl Masud ibn Ali ibn al-Fadl al-Bajjani, who transmitted from Abu al-Qasim Ahmad ibn Ubayda, and Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muadh ibn Saman al-Ruayni al-Bajjani, who heard from several teachers at Bajjana and Cordoba and is described as eloquent, a poet and learned in genealogy, people in both cities hearing from him. He reports from Ibn al-Faradi that he heard from him but found that he lied, and that the man told him he was born in 307. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.339}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record calls Pechina a municipality of Spain, with coordinates near Almeria, which agrees with Yaqut's two farsakhs from al-Mariyya. Yaqut describes a city already ruined in his time and does not say it is the present municipality; the link rests on the name and on the distance from Almeria. Almeria has its record, [[place/almeria|Almeria]], and Granada [[place/granada|Granada]].
