## Overview
The Nizamiyya madrasa in Baghdad is a madrasa built by Nizam al-Mulk, vizier of Alp Arslan, and completed in 459 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 645-646}

[[person/abu-ishaq-al-shirazi|Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi]] was appointed to teach in it. Al-Suyuti relates that it was "the first madrasa built for jurists", introducing this with the form "it is said" (wa-qila), so the matter is attributed to a saying rather than stated as certain. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 645-646}

Al-Suyuti names Nizam al-Mulk as vizier. For an introduction to him, the museum has a page: [[person/nizam-al-mulk|Nizam al-Mulk]]. The madrasa stood in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]].
