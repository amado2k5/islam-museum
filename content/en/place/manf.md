## Overview
Two villages, one of them populous, lie at the foot of Mount al-Muqattam, and most of them are now in ruins, according to al-Idrisi. He mentions a place known as the Oven of Pharaoh, where there is a mirror in which a person's image can be seen, and a talisman associated with it is related. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.326}
