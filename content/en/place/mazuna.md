## Summary
Mazuna is a city among hills about six miles from the sea, lying below a ravine, with rivers, farms, orchards, busy markets and pleasant dwellings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.272}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: next to Hawd Faruh inland to the east is the city of Mazuna, six miles from the sea; it is a city among hills lying below a ravine, with rivers, farms, orchards, busy markets and pleasant dwellings {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.272}. Its market has a known day on which kinds of Berbers gather with varieties of fruits, milk and butter; honey is abundant there, and it is among the finest lands in character, the richest of them in fruits and fertility {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.272}. From Mustaghanim to Hawd Faruh is twenty-four miles by ship's measure and fifteen miles by horse's measure; it is a fair anchorage with a populous village upon it {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.271}. From Hawd Faruh to the point of Juj, a nose jutting into the sea, is twenty-four miles by sea and twelve miles overland {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.272}.
