## Overview
The lava field of the Banu Harithah is a place the Prophet took on his way to Uhud. Abu Khaythama al-Harithi guided him there by a road far from the polytheists, near the property of the Banu Harithah, according to Ibn Hisham and al-Samhudi. {cite:ibn-hisham-wustenfeld-1858} {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa}
