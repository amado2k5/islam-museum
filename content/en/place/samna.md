## Overview
Samna is a city in the land of al-Tajawin on the borders of Nubia. Al-Idrisi relates from some travellers that an emir acting on behalf of the king of Nubia burned and demolished it and scattered its people, so it became a ruin. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.30}
