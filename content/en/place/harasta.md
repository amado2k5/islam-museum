## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name with fathas on the ha and ra, a sukun on the sin and a ta with two dots. He describes it as a large, flourishing village amid the orchards of Damascus on the road to Homs, more than a league from Damascus. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.241} He names as its most notable son his own teacher, the judge 'Abd al-Samad ibn Muhammad ibn Abi al-Fadl al-Ansari al-Harastani, a learned Shafi'i imam and teacher who took the judgeship of Damascus in middle age, left it, and took it again after turning ninety. Yaqut calls him trustworthy and careful; he was born in 520 AH and died on 5 Dhu al-Hijja 614 AH at the age of 94. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.241} Of earlier figures he names Abu Malik Hammad ibn Malik al-Ashja'i al-Harastani, who related from al-Awza'i and others. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.241}
