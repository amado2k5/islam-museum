## Overview
Dayr al-Jathaliq (the Catholicos's Monastery) is an old monastery near Baghdad west of the Tigris, which Yaqut names as the place of the fight between ʿAbd al-Malik ibn Marwan and Musʿab ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.503}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes it as an ancient monastery with a spacious court, in the tassuj of Maskin near Baghdad on the west of the Tigris, in the Harbi breadth, at the head of the boundary between the Sawad and the land of Tikrit. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.503}

He says the war between ʿAbd al-Malik ibn Marwan and Musʿab ibn al-Zubayr took place there, with the two armies on the bank of the Tigris, and that Musʿab was killed at it. This entry does not give the year. He mentions an elegy for him by ʿUbayd Allah ibn Qays al-Ruqayyat, which says the man killed at Dayr al-Jathaliq left the two garrison cities in grief and humiliation and blames those who held back from his support. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.503}

He quotes al-Shabushti that the monastery lies by Bab al-Hadid near Dayr al-Tha'alib, in the middle of the built-up area west of Baghdad, and gives verses by Muhammad ibn Abi Umayya recalling days of pleasure, wine and company there. For Yaqut the place is at once a battlefield and a site poets remembered fondly. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.503}
