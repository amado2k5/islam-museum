## Summary
Thara Island is a small island off the port city of Subara on the Indian Ocean coast. According to al-Idrisi it has a little coconut and costus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.182}

## Details
Al-Idrisi describes Subara as a civilized, populous city with markets and facilities, a port on the Indian Ocean, and a place of pearl fisheries and diving grounds. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.182} Off the city lies Thara Island, which he calls small, with a little coconut and costus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.182}

Elsewhere, in his account of the islands of the same sea, he lists Thara with the mountains of Kasir and Awir, the Dardur, and the island of Dibal. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.167} That passage gives neither its size nor its produce, so this entry adds nothing beyond what the account of Subara says.
