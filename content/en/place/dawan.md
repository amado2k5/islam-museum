## Overview
Dawan: a city in Hadramawt. According to al-Hamdani, in the fourth century it was "the place of the imam who orders and forbids in the matter of the Ibadiyya", that is, the seat of the Ibadi imamate of Yemen in the valley. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p.70}

It lies in [[place/hadramawt|Hadramawt]], and it is connected with [[group/ibadiyya|the Ibadiyya]].
