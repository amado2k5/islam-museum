## Overview
Mount Banbuwan: a mountain east of the city of Nughayra, slightly to the south. al-Idrisi reports that it is among the highest mountains on earth, with white soil, where nothing grows but wormwood and saltwort. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.106}
