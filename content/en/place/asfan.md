## Summary
A spot two stages from [[place/mecca|Mecca]] on the [[place/medina|Medina]] road; there [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] met [[person/budayl-ibn-warqa|Budayl ibn Warqa']] returning from Medina in a mutual exchange of deception {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37-38}. The conquest army passed it on the Ramadan march {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42}.

## For young visitors
A station on the road between Mecca and Medina where two men met, each hiding his true news from the other.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Asfan as a watering place on the road between Juhfa and Mecca, named after the way a flood sweeps through it. He cites al-Sukkari that it lies two stages from Mecca on the road to Medina, and that Juhfa is three stages away. He records that the Prophet fought the Banu Lihyan there five years, two months and eleven days after the Hijra by his count. Others said it was a village with a pulpit, palms and fields, thirty-six miles from Mecca, and the boundary of Tihama. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.121}
