## Summary
A spot two stages from [[place/mecca|Mecca]] on the [[place/medina|Medina]] road; there [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] met [[person/budayl-ibn-warqa|Budayl ibn Warqa']] returning from Medina in a mutual exchange of deception {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37-38}. The conquest army passed it on the Ramadan march {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42}.

## For young visitors
A station on the road between Mecca and Medina where two men met, each hiding his true news from the other.
