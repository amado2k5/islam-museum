## Summary
Aghathublis was a coastal city in the Byzantine lands, listed by al-Idrisi in the chain of seaboard towns between Aimidia and Basilico. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}

## Details
Al-Idrisi orders the distances along this coast: from Ailughis to the city of Aimidia twenty-five miles, a fine, populous town near the sea; thence to Aghathublis twenty-five miles; thence to Basilico on the seashore twenty-five miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. He repeats the same table elsewhere, confirming twenty-five miles from Aimidia to Aghathublis and twenty-five from Aghathublis to Basilico {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.908}. In his geography it stands as an intermediate coastal station between two named cities of that shore.
