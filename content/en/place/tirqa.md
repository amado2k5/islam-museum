## Overview
Tirqa is a large city with many people, without a wall or enclosure, according to al-Idrisi. It lies under the obedience of the lord of [[place/ghana|Ghana]], to whom its people appeal for judgment and in whose name they pronounce the khutba. It lies six days from Ghana on the road of the Nile. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.25}
