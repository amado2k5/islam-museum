## Summary
Murturi is a city in the land of the Rus at the outlet of the river Dunast, four days' journey south of the city of Ramsali. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that in the land of the Rus lies the city of Murturi on the outlet of the river Dunast; from Murturi to the city of Ramsali is four days southward, Ramsali being called Tawiya in the Rus tongue, and Ramsali and Murturi belong to the lands of the Rus, which are extensive in length and breadth {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955}. Of the lands of the Majus far from the sea in that quarter he names the city of Qabi, six stages from the sea, and from it to the city of Qaluri four days {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955}.
