## Overview
al-Mudhayyib: a place on the road to Iraq. al-Baladhuri reports that Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud came to it. He learned that Jaban the Persian was at Tustar with a large host, so he met him and defeated him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.350}

It is also said that Jarir ibn Abd Allah took the road by Fayd and al-Tha'labiyya to al-Mudhayyib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}
