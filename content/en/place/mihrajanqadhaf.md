## Overview
A district in the mountains (al-Jabal). Al-Baladhuri mentions that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] conquered all of it, and that al-Saymara is its city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
