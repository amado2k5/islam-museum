## Summary
A district of the Jibal; al-Baladhuri says [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] conquered all of it, with al-Saymara as its city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 431}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Abu Musa sent Abdallah ibn Qays al-Ash'ari, that is, al-Sa'ib ibn al-Aqra' al-Thaqafi — his son-in-law through his daughter Umm Muhammad bint al-Sa'ib — to al-Saymara, the city of Mihrajanqadhaf, and al-Sa'ib took it by treaty on condition of sparing blood, releasing the captives, pardoning the yellow and the white, and paying jizya and the land-tax; he then took all the districts of Mihrajanqadhaf, the firmest report being that Abu Musa dispatched al-Sa'ib from al-Ahwaz to take it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 431} This belongs to Abu Musa's conquests in the Jibal lands, where the people of al-Sirwan made peace on the terms of al-Dinawar's sulh — to pay jizya and kharaj — while he sent detachments among them and overran their land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 431}
