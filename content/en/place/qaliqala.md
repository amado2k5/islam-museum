## Overview
Qaliqala is one of the cities of Armenia. Al-Baladhuri mentions that it is third in the transmitted division, and some say second, and that it was the centre of the conquest of Armenia. It is part of [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The name and the conquest
Al-Baladhuri relates that a woman, named Quli, ruled the land of Armeniakos after her husband. She built the city and named it Qaliqala, meaning "the goodness of Quli". Her image was carved on one of its gates, and the Arabs arabized it as Qaliqala. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Habib ibn Maslama conquered it in the caliphate of Uthman, on terms of departure for whoever wished and payment of jizya for those who remained. Mu'awiya settled there two thousand men on garrison duty (murabata), with land grants (qata'i'). The conquest has a page: [[event/conquest-of-qaliqala|The conquest of Qaliqala]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Fall and restoration
The city remained defended until Kusan the Armenian took it in the year 133 AH, through a breach dug by two brothers of its people, and he demolished it. Then al-Mansur restored it in 139 AH, ransomed its captives, settled it and placed troops in it. Al-Mu'tasim spent five hundred thousand dirhams on fortifying it. For the detail, see [[event/fall-and-rebuilding-of-qaliqala|The fall and rebuilding of Qaliqala]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
