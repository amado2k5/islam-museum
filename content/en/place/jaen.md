## Overview
Jaen is a fine town rich in fertility, honey and meat, according to al-Idrisi. It has running springs below its wall and a strong citadel. He says that around it are more than three thousand villages where silkworms are raised. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.568}
