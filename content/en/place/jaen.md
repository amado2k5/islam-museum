## Overview
Jaen is a fine town rich in fertility, honey and meat, according to al-Idrisi. It has running springs below its wall and a strong citadel. He says that around it are more than three thousand villages where silkworms are raised. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.568}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Jaen as a town in al-Andalus with a wide district that joins the district of al-Bira. The district tends from al-Bira towards al-Jawf, east of Cordova, and Jaen is seventeen farsakh from Cordova. Its district is joined to the district of Tudmir and the district of Toledo. Among the men he names is al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Ghassani, known as al-Jayyani. He was not from Jaen itself; his father settled there in the troubles. He was the leading transmitter of hadith in Cordova and wrote a book on the men of the two Sahihs. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.195}
