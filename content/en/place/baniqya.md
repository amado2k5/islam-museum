## Overview
Baniqya: a town in the Sawad, according to al-Baladhuri. Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali made peace with it for a thousand dirhams and a cloak. It is one of three towns of the people of the Sawad that had a covenant, together with al-Hira and Alis, according to the saying of Ibn Mughaffal al-Muzani. Dirar ibn al-Azur said a poem about it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
