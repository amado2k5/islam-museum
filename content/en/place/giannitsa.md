## Overview
Giannitsa is a city in the Pella regional unit of Greek Macedonia, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as both a populated place and an archaeological site. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2299219}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2299219 with the preferred name «Giannitsa» (German), classed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Pella. Among the names it records are «Γιαννιτσά» (Greek), «Яница» (Russian), «Yiannitsá» and «Yenitsá». It links the place to GeoNames no. 733776 and to Arachne and Zenon identifiers. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2299219}
