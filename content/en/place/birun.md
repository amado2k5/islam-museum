## Overview
Birun: a city in Sind. Its people sent two envoys to [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]], and they made peace with him. When [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] came to it, they prepared fodder for him, let him into their city, and kept to the peace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
