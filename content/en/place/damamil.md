## Overview
Damamil: a recently built city on the east bank of the Nile, about seven miles from Qus. al-Idrisi describes its air as good, and says that it has grain and cultivation. Its people are mixed, and he describes them as generous and faithful. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.129}
