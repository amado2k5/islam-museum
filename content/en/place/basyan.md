## Summary
Basyan was a mid-sized, prosperous town cleft by a river into two halves, pleasant within and without, two stages from Askar Mukram, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.395}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that west of the city of al-Dawraq lies the city of Basyan at two stages from it, al-Dawraq being a town peopled by its inhabitants and called the city of the rustaq {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.395}. Basyan is a mid-sized prosperous town which a river cuts into two halves; it is fair both inside and out, and between Basyan and Hisn Mahdi are two stages to the west {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.395}. He also notes that from the city of Askar Mukram to al-Dawraq is about four stages, so that Basyan stood in his map between Askar Mukram, al-Dawraq and Hisn Mahdi in the district of Khuzistan {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.395}. He thus depicted it as a flourishing town of moderate size, divided by a river into two parts and pleasing within and without.
