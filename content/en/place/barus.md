## Summary
Barus was a town in the lands of [[place/sind|Sind]] and India. Al-Hakam ibn Abi al-'As al-Thaqafi sent an expedition to it during his brother 'Uthman's governorship over Bahrayn and 'Uman in the year fifteen, as al-Baladhuri relates in the conquests of Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 607}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that 'Umar ibn al-Khattab appointed 'Uthman ibn Abi al-'As al-Thaqafi over Bahrayn and 'Uman in year fifteen; 'Uthman sent his brother al-Hakam to Bahrayn while he himself went to 'Uman and dispatched a force to Tana. When the army returned he informed 'Umar, who wrote back: "Abu Thaqif, you have set grubs upon a stick, and I swear by God that if harm had befallen them I would have taken the like from your own people" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 607}. Al-Hakam also sent an expedition to Barus, and 'Uthman sent his brother al-Mughira ibn Abi al-'As to Khawr al-Daybul, where he met the enemy and prevailed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 607}. Later, when 'Uthman ibn 'Affan became caliph and appointed 'Abdallah ibn 'Amir ibn Kurayz over Iraq, he ordered him to send someone to reconnoitre the Indian frontier and report back, so 'Abdallah sent Hakim ibn Jabala al-'Abdi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 607}.
