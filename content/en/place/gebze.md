## Overview
Gebze is a town in Kocaeli province, Turkey, which the iDAI.gazetteer records as a populated place and lists under the ancient Latin name Libyssa. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283266}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2283266 with the preferred name «Gebze», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Kocaeli (Provinz)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Libyssa» in Latin, «Gebze» in Turkish, English and German, «Guebze» and «Гебзе» in Russian. It links the place to GeoNames no. 747014, to Pleiades no. 511305, to Arachne entity 1208989 and to a German National Library authority record (4237110-7). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283266}
