## Overview
Gaza is a town in the land of Palestine, where [[person/hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim ibn Abd Manaf]], the grandfather of the Prophet, died on a trading journey to Syria. His wife [[person/salma-bint-amr|Salma]] was pregnant in Yathrib with Abd al-Muttalib. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.49} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/137}.
