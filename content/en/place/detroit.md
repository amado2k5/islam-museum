## Overview
The iDAI.gazetteer lists Detroit as a populated place, under the United States of America in its hierarchy. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2088667}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2088667 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Detroit / Michigan». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «United States of America». It records names in Greek, Chinese, Latin («Detroitum»), Vietnamese, Spanish, English, German, French and Italian, and in Arabic the form «ديترويت، ميشيغان», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 4990729 and to the German National Library authority record 4011559-8. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2088667}
