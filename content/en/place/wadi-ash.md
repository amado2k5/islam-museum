## Overview
Wadi Ash is a city of medium size, with encircling walls, pleasing sources of income and abundant flowing water, and a small river that runs all year. It belongs to the province of Elvira, which also holds [[place/granada|Granada]] and al-Munakkab. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 567} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 537}

## The road
The road runs from the end of the plain of Abla to Khandaq Ash and then to the city of Wadi Ash. From it one goes to the village of Dashma, where there is a halting place, then to al-Rutba, the village of Afrafrida and the village of Wad, a run of connected villages, and from there to Granada, eight miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 567}

## A meeting of roads
Al-Idrisi calls Wadi Ash a causeway where many roads meet. For [[place/basta|Basta]] one leaves Wadi Ash for Jabal Asim, then the village of Yura, then Basta, thirty miles in all. Basta is a medium-sized, well-sited and populous town, and near it stands the fort of Tashkar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 568}
