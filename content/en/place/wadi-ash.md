## Overview
Wadi Ash is a city of medium size, according to al-Idrisi. It has encircling walls, and in its middle is a small river that flows constantly. It lies about eight miles from [[place/granada|Granada]] on the road. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.567}
