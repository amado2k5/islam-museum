## Overview
al-Azhar Mosque: a mosque in [[place/cairo|Cairo]]. al-Suyuti reports that the Fatimids began building it in Rabi al-Akhir in the year 359 AH and finished it in Ramadan in the year 361 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 621}

An additional source is needed: any report of its history after that.
