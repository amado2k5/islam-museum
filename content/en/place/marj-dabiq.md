## Overview
A place in the districts of the military province (jund) of [[place/qinnasrin|Qinnasrin]]. [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]] died there on Friday, ten days before the end of Safar in the year 99 AH. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.252}

He had stayed in the meadow with his army, on campaign, and there he wrote his will naming [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] as his successor to the caliphate. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.261-262}
