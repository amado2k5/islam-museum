## Overview
Zaghawa is a city populous with people, around which are tribes of Zaghawiyyun who graze their camels, according to al-Idrisi. They drink from wells, and their food is maize, dried meat (muqaddat) and fish. It lies eight stages from [[place/manan|Manan]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.29}
