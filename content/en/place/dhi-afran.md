## Summary
Dhi Afran is a valley on the Badr road where the Prophet received the news of the caravan (al-'ayr) and of the relief force (al-nafir), and where the consultative council was held before the encounter. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 208-210}

## Details
On the march to Badr, as the Prophet drew near the wells, word reached him at Dhi Afran both of the Quraysh caravan and of the army that had set out from Mecca to its rescue. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 208-210}

There he held a consultative assembly: Abu Bakr rose and spoke well, then Umar rose and spoke well; then al-Miqdad ibn Amr stood and declared that they would go with him to whatever God had commanded, even to Bark al-Ghimad; and finally Sa'd ibn Mu'adh answered on behalf of the Ansar in words that pleased the Prophet. The council sealed the army's resolve before meeting the caravan and the relief force on the day of Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 208-210}
