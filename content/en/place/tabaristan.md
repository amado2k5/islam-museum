## Overview
Tabaristan is a region. Al-Baladhuri mentions that its marzban proposed to Yazdegerd, who was in Isfahan, that he come to it, and informed him of its impregnability. Then Yazdegerd changed his mind and fled to Kirman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.440}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut explains the name by taking "-stan" to mean place or region, so that Tabaristan is the region of the Tabar. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.13}

Its notable towns include Dahistan, Jurjan, Astarabad, Amul, which is its capital, Sari and Shalus. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.13}

The land is mountainous and rich in water, trees and fruit, but Yaqut calls it dangerous and unhealthy. It lies between Rayy, Qumis and the sea, next to Jilan and Daylam. He notes that the name Mazandaran is used by its people but is not found in older books. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.13}
