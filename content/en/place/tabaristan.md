## Overview
Tabaristan is a region. Al-Baladhuri mentions that its marzban proposed to Yazdegerd, who was in Isfahan, that he come to it, and informed him of its impregnability. Then Yazdegerd changed his mind and fled to Kirman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.440}
