## Overview
Tafresh is a city in Markazi Province in Iran, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place and an administrative unit. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2768410}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2768410 with the preferred name «Tafresh», classed as an administrative unit and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Markazi Province. Among the names it records are «تفرش» (Persian) and «Тафреш» (Russian). It links the place to GeoNames no. 113636. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2768410}
