## Summary
Basliko is a city on the seashore which al-Idrisi places twenty-five miles from the city of Aghathublis. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that to the city of Aghathublis is twenty-five miles, thence to Basliko on the seashore twenty-five miles, and from Basliko to the city of Szubli on the sea twenty-five miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. He follows this with the cities of Akhilu, Ayman, Barnas and Armuqistru at equal or similar distances along that same coast {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. Basliko is thus a link in the chain of coastal cities al-Idrisi drew along that shore at regular intervals.  Beyond this bare mention and the distances given, al-Idrisi adds no further description of it here.
