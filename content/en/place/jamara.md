## Summary
Jamara is a large populous city on the Italian coast, whose name in the Roman tongue is Badhantu, forty miles from Batrantu. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Labluna to Jamara is one hundred miles around the curve of the headland, that Jamara's name in the Roman tongue is Badhantu, that from it to Batrantu is forty miles — Batrantu being a small civilized city with markets and buildings — and that Jamara is a large populous city {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}. He adds that from Jamara along the coast to the city of Faskiu is sixty miles, Faskiu being a middling city with a small arm-shaped bay on its eastern side which boats enter through a narrow mouth {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}. He lists it among the coastal and inland lands together with Labluna, Badhrantu, Faskiu, Bandasa, Adharnubli and Yannina {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.627}. It is thus one of the chief cities of that coast described in the Nuzhat al-Mushtaq. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}
