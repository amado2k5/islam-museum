## Overview
al-Shawt: a place near Medina, between it and Uhud, on the road out to [[place/uhud|Uhud]]. People of the Ansar lived there, and in it is a fortified tower known as al-Shar'i. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.177}

## The day of Uhud
At al-Shawt, [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abd Allah ibn Ubayy]] withdrew with about a third of the army on the day of Uhud, according to al-Samhudi and Ibn Hisham. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.211} {cite:ibn-hisham-wustenfeld-1858 p.23}
