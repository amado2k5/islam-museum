## Overview
Qal'at Fayma is an ancient fortress and stronghold in the west of al-Jazira, whose water is scarce in the area around it. Al-Idrisi mentions distances between it and neighbouring places, including al-Hamma and Tarazi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 608}
