## Overview
The Isle of Sheep is a large island in the western sea, followed by small islands beyond counting. Al-Idrisi says that the Mughrurin who visited it know it, and that there are birds on it that sea animals eat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.220}
