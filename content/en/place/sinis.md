## Overview
Sinis is a city near the sea, neighbouring [[place/jannaba|Janaba]]. Al-Idrisi mentions that there is in it what is known as sinizi, a thing whose quality nothing else matches. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.412}
