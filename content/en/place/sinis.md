## Overview
Sinis is a town near the sea in the land of Fars. It has a pulpit, and it is known for Sinizi linen, which al-Idrisi says has no equal in all the lands for fineness and strength. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.412}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says Sinis is a town near the sea with a pulpit. Its linen, the Sinizi cloth, is by common consent the finest in the world, with nothing matching its softness and strength. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.412}

Before it on the coast lies Janaba, a large, flourishing town with busy markets. It has a weaving centre where fine linen clothes are made in many kinds, and it has a rustaq and an administrative district (amala). One of Sinis's flourishing districts is Islajan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.412}

It lies on the Persian coast. Al-Idrisi lists it among the Fars ports, between Abadan, al-Ubulla, Mahrubaan, Janaba, Najiram, Sohar and Siraf, all in the land of Fars. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.379}
