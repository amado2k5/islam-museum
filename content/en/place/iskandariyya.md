## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] conquered Alexandria by force, without a covenant or a pact, in the account of his letter to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]]. Yazid ibn Abi Habib says that all of it was a peace agreement. This happened after the Roman king rejected the peace of al-Muqawqis, so they closed its gate and declared war. Al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam laid out a plot in it, and al-Muqawqis asked Amr that he be buried in one of its churches, which he named. See [[event/conquest-of-alexandria|the conquest of Alexandria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Muqaddasi's description
Al-Muqaddasi, around 375 AH, describes it as a metropolis on the Roman Sea with a strong fortress, and with many righteous and devout people. They drink from the Nile, which enters them through a canal in its flood season and fills their cisterns. Their buildings are of sea stone, and their lanes have gates that are closed at night. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.162}
