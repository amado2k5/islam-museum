## Overview
Fadak is a Jewish oasis in the north of the Hijaz, near [[place/khaybar|Khaybar]]. On the Prophet's return from Khaybar, its people made a settlement with him to give up half the land and its produce, so the pure half became a share of the Prophet's, which he spent on wayfarers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.41-42}

Its people remained there until [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]], in his caliphate, expelled them to Syria, along with the Jews of Khaybar, after valuing their share of the palms and land at a fair price. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.41-42}

In Sahih al-Bukhari, within Umar's account of the Prophet's properties, he says that Fadak was for wayfarers. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4033}
