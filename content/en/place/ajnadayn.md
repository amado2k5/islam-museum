## Overview
A place in the land of al-Sham where Amr ibn Said ibn al-As, Habbar ibn Sufyan and Said ibn Amr al-Tamimi were killed in the caliphate of Abu Bakr, according to Ibn Ishaq.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 31.6833 N and 34.95 E {cite:geonames-gazetteer}.
