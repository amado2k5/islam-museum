## Overview
A place in the land of al-Sham where Amr ibn Said ibn al-As, Habbar ibn Sufyan and Said ibn Amr al-Tamimi were killed in the caliphate of Abu Bakr, according to Ibn Ishaq.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says that Ajnadayn is a place in the land of al-Sham, in the region of Palestine, in the district of Ramla in the province of Bayt Jibrin. A famous battle between the Muslims and the Byzantines was fought there. He reports from the scholars of the conquests that a hundred thousand Byzantines took part. Heraclius had sent away most of them, and the rest were gathered from the provinces, while Heraclius himself was at Homs. God defeated them and scattered them, and many were killed. Among the Muslim dead there were Ikrima ibn Abi Jahl and al-Harith ibn Hisham, and Khalid ibn al-Walid fought there with great distinction. When news of the battle reached Heraclius, he was struck with dread and fled from Homs to Antioch. The battle was twelve nights before the end of Jumada al-Ula in the year 13 AH, about a month before the death of Abu Bakr. Yaqut quotes verses on it by Ziyad ibn Hanzala. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 pp.103-104}
