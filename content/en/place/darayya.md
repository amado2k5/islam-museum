## Overview
Darayya was a large and well-known village among the villages of Damascus in the Ghuta. It is the place of the grave of Abu Sulayman al-Darani, Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Atiyya the ascetic, who died there in 235 AH; some say his origin was Wasit. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.431}

## Yaqut's entry
Yaqut describes Darayya as a large and well-known village of the Ghuta, and notes that the nisba al-Darani is formed to it irregularly. He says the grave of Abu Sulayman is well known and visited, and that his son Sulayman, also a worshipper and ascetic, died two years and a month after his father, in 237 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.431}

Yaqut gives a report from Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari, who says that he and Abu Sulayman went to the mosque and spoke of worldly desires. Abu Sulayman's son Sulayman, who had stayed silent, then said that a man whose heart holds nothing of the next world to keep him from desires gains nothing by merely giving them up. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.431} Yaqut also names Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid ibn Jabir, Abu Utba al-Azdi al-Darani, from whom Abdallah ibn al-Mubarak narrated. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.431}
