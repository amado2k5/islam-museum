## Overview
Allaf is a place of the Akilis, a mountain in which they fortify themselves, and [[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]] went out to it when he did not come to al-Hadi after the safe-conduct, and stayed there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} The index lists "Allaf" at pp. 195, 196, 198, 224, 413 and 414 among others and does not count p. 157. It has no coordinates. See [[event/safe-conduct-for-the-akilis-and-al-hadis-verses-from-saada-286]].
