## Overview
Samandar on the Khazar Sea is a city that was great in past times, built by Anushirwan, according to al-Idrisi. In it were trees, vines and crops beyond description. Then the Rus tribe changed it and destroyed it. From Samandar to Bab al-Abwab it is four days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.835}
