## Overview
An island with no city; its people live in huts (akhsas). It has vineyards, and crops of cumin and anise. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.304}

Al-Idrisi mentions that King Roger conquered it in the year 548 AH. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.304}
