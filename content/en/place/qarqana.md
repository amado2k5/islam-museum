## Overview
Qarqana is a handsome island, populous with its own people, who have no town and live instead in reed huts. It is fertile, with many vines and grapes and crops of cumin and anise, called the hot seed. King Roger conquered it in the year 548 (AH). {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 304}

## Location
It lies opposite Qasr Ziyad in the sea, toward the east, between Qasr Ziyad and [[place/sfax|Sfax]]; from Qarqana to Qabudhiya is twenty miles and to Sfax about fifteen miles. The island is sixteen miles long and six wide. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 304}

## Caves
On the western end of the island are caves and hollows in which people take refuge from anyone who attacks them, known as al-Qarbadi; from al-Qarbadi to Bayt al-Qasir is thirty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 304} Elsewhere, from the end of the island of Jerba called Antijan to the bridge on Qarqana is sixty-two miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 306}
