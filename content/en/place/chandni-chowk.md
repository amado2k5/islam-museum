## Overview
The most famous market in old [[place/delhi|Delhi]], cut by Shah Jahan in the seventeenth century beside the Jama Masjid. Its lane is still narrow and crowded with goldsmiths, cloth sellers and food vendors.

## Significance
For three and a half centuries it has been the popular trading centre of India. Next to it stand the oldest Mughal mosque and the Red Fort. {cite:wikidata}
