## Overview
Naples (Napoli) is a fine, ancient, populous city with goods and merchandise. Al-Idrisi describes it as famous and as having many markets. He mentions that from it to the harbour of Astaba there is much water, and that between it and the Mountain of Fire there is a distance. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.757}
