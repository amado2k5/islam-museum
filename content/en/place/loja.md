## Overview
Loja, called Lawsha in the Arabic sources, is a city in al-Andalus west of Granada. Yaqut records it in the Mu'jam al-buldan with its position on the river Sinjil and its distance from Granada and Cordoba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.26}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says Lawsha is a city in al-Andalus, west of Elvira and before Cordoba, bending slightly away, a good city on the river Sinjil, which is the river of Granada. He gives the name as a fatha on the first letter, a sukun, and then the letter shin. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.26}

He estimates the distances: twenty farsakhs between it and Cordoba and ten farsakhs between it and Granada. The description fits the site of Loja on the river now called the Genil, near Granada, although Yaqut does not use the modern name of the river. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.26}
