## Overview
Tanuma is a populous island with fresh water, rice, coconuts and a pearl bed, and aloes-wood and camphor grow in it. It lies five days from the island of Qumar, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.82}
