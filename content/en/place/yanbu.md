## Overview
Yanbu is a port on the Hijaz coast of the Red Sea, and it was the port of [[place/medina|Medina]] from ancient times. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The cited source is a reference for coordinates; the description needs an additional source.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut's entry on Yanbu is brief: it is a place near the city of the Messenger, which is Medina. In other entries he speaks of the valley of Yanbu. In the entry on Yalil he says that Yalil lies between the valley of Yanbu and al-Udhayba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.248} In the entry on Ayla he says that the mountain of Yanbu lies between Mecca and Medina. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.293}
