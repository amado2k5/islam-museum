## Overview
Shaqubiyya is a group of gathered villages rather than a city, according to al-Idrisi. Its people ride horses, its buildings are interlaced, there are many people in it, and its people possess produce and grazing herds. It lies about fifty miles from Toledo. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}
