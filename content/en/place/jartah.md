## Summary
Jartah was a small settled coastal town with markets and buildings; it lay one hundred miles along the coast from the mouth of the island, and one hundred miles east of the city of Zawada. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.950}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the island's mouth along the coast to the town of Jartah is one hundred miles; thence to the city of Lind Shwdhan two hundred miles — a large flourishing town — and on to the site of the river Qatlu, where stands a town called Saqtun, one hundred and ninety miles, and thence to the town of Qalmar two hundred miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.950} From coastal Jartah eastward to the city of Zawada is one hundred miles; Zawada is a large town after which its land is named, a country little built up and very cold and frozen, and between Zawada and the town of Alba lay one hundred miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.950}
