## Overview
Antioch: the city of the Syrian frontier. Its people made peace with [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] on poll-tax and evacuation; then they broke it, and Iyad ibn Ghanm and Habib ibn Maslama conquered it again, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/conquest-of-antioch|the conquest of Antioch]]. It belongs to the district of [[place/qinnasrin|Qinnasrin]] and the frontier cities.

## After the conquest
al-Baladhuri reports that Umar ordered a garrison of Muslims to be arranged there, that Uthman granted estates in it, and that Mu'awiya moved to it in the year 42 AH people from Ba'labakk, Homs and other places. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Landmarks mentioned by the sources
al-Mas'udi mentions in it a shrine known as al-Dimas, on the right of the congregational mosque, which the moon enters as it rises in some summer months. It is said that it was built by the Persians and that it was a fire-temple. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}
