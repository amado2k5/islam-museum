## Overview
The valley of Lyya, which its people pronounce with a short l and a light y, as al-Hamdani mentions. In his account it is among the valleys that flow into the sea from the land of Hakam. {cite:hamdani-akwa-hawali}

## The editor's note
The editor's note says that "Layya" with a doubled y is a valley east of al-Ta'if. The data indicates that the name may refer to two different valleys that have not yet been separated. {cite:hamdani-akwa-hawali}

For that reason no coordinates have been recorded. An additional source is needed.
