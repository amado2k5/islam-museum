A small cave on Jabal al-Nur some two miles from [[place/mecca|Mecca]]; four cubits long and a cubit and three-quarters wide by the iron cubit. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Prophet used to make i'tikaf in it in the month of Ramadan three years before the prophethood, feeding the poor who came to him and spending his time in worship and reflection on the scenes of the universe, his family near him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

There the angel came to him with the opening of Surat al-'Alaq, making it the scene of [[event/first-revelation|revelation's beginning]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
