## Overview
Subara is a civilised city on the sea, about a mile and a half from it, and it is a port (furda) among the ports of India. It has pearl fisheries and beds, and on it is the island of Thara, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.182}
