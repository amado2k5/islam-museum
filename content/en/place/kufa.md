## Summary
A garrison city in Iraq, founded around 638 CE. It was the seat of the caliphate of Ali ibn Abi Talib and an early centre of learning.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 32.0511 north and 44.4402 east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
