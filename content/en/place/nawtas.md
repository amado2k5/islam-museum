## Overview
Nawtas, which is Nawto, is a castle and town which al-Idrisi describes as among the most exalted of castles for its strength. It has well-ordered markets, running water and many mills, and its industry is extensive. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.598}
