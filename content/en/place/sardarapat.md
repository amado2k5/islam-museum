## Overview
Sardarapat is a village in Armavir Province in Armenia, recorded by Pleiades as a findspot and archaeological site. The first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan hold no entry for it. {cite:pleiades 308320790}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Sardarapat» (Pleiades no. 308320790), classed as a findspot and an archaeological site, with its recorded location dated from 1700 to 2100 CE, an open range for the modern period. It records one name, «Sardarapat», attested in the modern period. Its description says that this is the find place of a cuneiform inscription. {cite:pleiades 308320790}
