## Overview
Fars is a large region named after its people. The Muslims conquered it from the sea side in the caliphate of Umar, beginning with the island of Abr Kawan, then Tawwaj and Rishahr, where its marzban Shahrak was killed, then Arjan, Shiraz, Sabur, Darabjird and others. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
