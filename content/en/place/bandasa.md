## Summary
Bandasa is a mid-sized, flourishing walled city with a market which al-Idrisi places on the coast east of the city of Faskyu. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Faskyu to the city of Bandasa is thirty miles and to al-Takhliya twenty miles, that Bandasa is a mid-sized flourishing city with a wall, a market, buying and selling, and that from it to Nabghtu is a hundred and fifty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}. Before it he mentions that Jammara is a large flourishing city, and that from Jammara along the coast to Faskyu is sixty miles, Faskyu being a mid-sized city with a small arm-like gulf on its eastern side which ships enter through a narrow mouth, and that off Jammara lies the island of Thanuw thirty miles away, a fine island with an anchorage and firewood {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.633}. Bandasa is thus one of the cities of that coast between Faskyu and Nabghtu.
