## Overview
Lake al-Basafriya: a lake with a monastery of the Basafriyyun on it. Its water is salt, its fish are plentiful, and around its shores grow reeds and esparto grass, which the people of those districts make use of. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.424}
