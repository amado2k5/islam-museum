## Summary
Murcia is a city of al-Andalus on the river that bears its name; al-Idrisi gives forty miles from the fortress of Lorca to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that the river flowing past Murcia rises from the root of the mountain, it being related that its source and that of Cordoba's river are one, then the river of Murcia passes in the southern part to the fortress of Afrad and then to the fortress of Mawla {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.561}. From the fortress of Lorca to Murcia is forty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.562}. It thus stands among the cities of al-Andalus that he describes in this section, with their markets, buildings and surrounding strongholds.
