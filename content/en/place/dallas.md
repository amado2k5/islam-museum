## Overview
Dallas is a city in the state of Texas, United States. It is a modern place that the classical Arabic geographical works do not mention, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2116286 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Dallas». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit United States of America. It records names in German, English, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, Albanian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Latin («Dallasium»), Greek, Russian and Chinese, and in Arabic the form «دالاس، تكساس», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 4684888 and to the German National Library authority record 4085382-2. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2116286}
