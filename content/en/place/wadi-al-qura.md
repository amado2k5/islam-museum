## Overview
A valley north of Medina, where [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] met [[group/banu-fazara|Banu Fazara]] twice. See [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-banu-fazara-and-umm-qirfa|The raid of Zayd on Banu Fazara]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/265}

## On the differing counts of expeditions
Al-Mas'udi mentions that whoever counted the expeditions at twenty-seven distinguished the Prophet's return from Khaybar to Wadi al-Qura as another expedition. See [[concept/khilaf-adad-al-maghazi|The disagreement over the number of expeditions]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.579}
