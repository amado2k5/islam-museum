## Overview
Zamakhshar: a small town of mediaeval Khwarazm lying between Nuzwar and Gurgandj (Jurjaniyya), the later capital of the province. Al-Muqaddasi records that in the later fourth/tenth century it had a citadel, a prison, a congregational mosque, walls with iron gates and a protective ditch with drawbridges raised at night. Its fame came from being the birthplace in 467/1075 of the Qur'an commentator and grammarian Abu al-Qasim Mahmud al-Zamakhshari {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 449}.
