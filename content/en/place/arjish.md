## Overview
Arjish: one of the northern cities of [[place/armenia|Armenia]], on Lake Van, according to al-Baladhuri. The agents of Habib ibn Maslama collected the poll-tax from the heads of its villages, and its notables made peace on the kharaj of their land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
