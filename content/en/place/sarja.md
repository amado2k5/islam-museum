## Overview
Al-Sarja is a small town on the coast of Yemen. A group of the sons of al-Halbi live in it, a sect of Yemeni merchants, most of whom reside in Sana'a. They are known for feeding and supporting pilgrims and provisioning them. Ibn Battuta stayed in it one night as their guest. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.156}
