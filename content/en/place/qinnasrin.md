## Overview
Qinnasrin is the chief town of the military province (jund) of Qinnasrin and of its Awasim. Its people made peace with Abu Ubayda on the same terms as the peace of Homs. Then they broke the covenant, and al-Simt ibn al-Aswad al-Kindi conquered it, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.197} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.198}

## Tanukh and the people of the city
The Tanukh were present there from their first settling in al-Sham, in tents of hair, and then they built houses. Some of them embraced Islam. The Banu Salih ibn Hulwan of Quda'a remained upon Christianity, and some of them embraced Islam in the days of al-Mahdi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.198}

## The jund and its districts
Qinnasrin and Homs became a single jund. Among its districts are Aleppo, Manbij, Harran and what surrounds them. It is one of the military provinces of al-Sham ([[place/damascus|Damascus]]), and the site of [[event/conquest-of-qinnasrin|the conquest of Qinnasrin]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.197}
