## Overview
Yerevan is recorded in Pleiades as an ancient settlement site at about 40.19° N, 44.52° E, with an Ottoman-period name Revān. {cite:pleiades 863922}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Yerevan» (Pleiades no. 863922), classed as a settlement, with a location attested from 550 BCE to 30 BCE. {cite:pleiades 863922} Recorded names include «Revān» [Ottoman Turkish, 1500 CE – 1599 CE]. {cite:pleiades 863922} Pleiades describes it as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 88, grid C4. {cite:pleiades 863922}
