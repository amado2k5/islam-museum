## Summary
A town in the north-west of the Arabian Peninsula. It was the furthest point reached by the Prophet's expedition in 630 CE, near the Byzantine frontier.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 28.38 N and 36.57 E {cite:geonames-gazetteer}.
