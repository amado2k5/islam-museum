## Overview
al-Shajara: a place on the road to Medina. al-Idrisi reports that it is the miqat of the people of Medina, that a few Arab people live there, and that the distance from it to [[place/medina|Medina]] is short. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.142}
