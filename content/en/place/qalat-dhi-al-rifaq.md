## Overview
Qal'at Dhi al-Rifaq is a fortress in al-Ahwaz, which Thabit ibn Dhi al-Harra al-Himyari conquered, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} An additional source is needed.
