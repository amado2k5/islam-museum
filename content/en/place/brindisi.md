## Overview
Brindisi: a city surrounded by the sea on several sides, fine in its building and spacious in its quarters, with facilities and a harbour. It lies twelve miles from Gushit, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.763}
