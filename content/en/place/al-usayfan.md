## Overview
al-Usayfan: a country between Qashmir, Multan and Kabul. The sources report that its king became a Muslim in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim after the death of his son. He broke and destroyed the house of his idol and killed its keepers, for they had claimed that his prayer was answered. The merchants of the Muslims offered him the oneness of God, and he professed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
