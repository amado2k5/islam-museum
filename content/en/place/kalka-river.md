## Overview
A river flowing into the Sea of Azov near Mariupol in the southern Wallachian lands; by it was fought the great slaughter of 620 between the Tatars and the Cumans and Rus — the two hosts held fast for days, then the Tatars won and rode them down with the sword {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 87}.
