## Overview
Jasim is an old village in the district of Damascus. Yaqut records it in the Mu'jam al-buldan with its eponym, the verse composed about it, and the scholars and poets attributed to it, the best known being Abu Tammam. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.94}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says Jasim is the name of a village eight farsakhs from Damascus, on the right of the main road to Tiberias. He says it was named for Jasim ibn Iram ibn Sam ibn Nuh, who settled there when the tongues were confused at Babel, and then gives a second genealogy placing Jasim with Tasm, Imliq and Umaym. He sets out both as reports and does not choose between them. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.94}

He quotes a line of Hassan ibn Thabit that names Jasim, the valleys of al-Sufr and the dwelling of Qanabil and Hajan, and verses of Adi ibn al-Riqa al-Amili al-Ta'i that compare the eyes of a beloved to those of a gazelle of Jasim, which attaches the poet's image to the village. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.94}

Among those from Jasim he names the poet Abu Tammam Habib ibn Aws al-Ta'i. On his death he gives differing reports: Niftawayh says he died in 228, his son is quoted as saying his father was born in 188 and died in 231 at Mosul, and another report puts his death at the start of 232. Yaqut adds that al-Hasan ibn Wahb appointed him postmaster of Mosul, that he stayed less than two years, died there and was buried there. The years are Hijri, as Yaqut gives them. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.95}

Yaqut also names the jurist Abu al-Khayr Ni'mat Allah ibn Hibat Allah ibn Muhammad al-Jasimi, who, on the authority of Abu al-Qasim, was from the village of Jasim and heard in Damascus from Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Hannai and from Sa'id ibn Abd Allah al-Nawa'i of the village of Nawa. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.95}
