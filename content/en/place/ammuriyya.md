## Overview
Ammuriyya (Amorium): a city in the land of the Romans. al-Mu'tasim camped before it in the year 223 AH, after the conquest of many fortresses, and Lawi the patrician came out to him and handed it over, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}

al-Mas'udi reports that al-Mu'tasim stayed before it for four days, demolishing and burning, and that he wanted to march on Constantinople, but the revolt of al-Abbas ibn al-Ma'mun turned him away. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}
