## Summary
Bashan is one of the cities of Marw in Khurasan which al-Idrisi describes as flourishing, finely built, containing inns, baths and a congregational mosque. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}

## Details
Enumerating the cities of Marw, al-Idrisi says that among them is Bashan, a flourishing city handsomely built, broad in its quarters and well-furnished in its markets, containing inns, baths and a congregational mosque with a minbar that is walked to, and it lies three miles from Hurmuzfarra {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}. In the same passage he mentions the other cities of Marw such as Dandanqan, two stages from Marw on the Markhas road, with a wall, a fortress, markets, baths, inns and a congregational mosque and minbar, and al-Susqan, a large flourishing city with spacious dwellings, running water and many orchards {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.477}. Bashan is thus one of the flourishing towns of the province of Marw.
