## Overview
al-Safa: a small hill on the right of someone entering [[place/mecca|Mecca]] from the direction of the Ka'ba. [[person/muhammad|The Prophet]] climbed it, gathered the clans of Quraysh, and called out to them a warning ([[event/safa-proclamation|the proclamation from al-Safa]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.79}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book mentions it in two places: when the Prophet climbed it and called out "O Banu Fihr" and "O Banu Adi", and the tribes gathered to him; and when the house of [[building/dar-al-arqam|al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam]] was on al-Safa, and the Prophet made it the centre of his call and of his gatherings with the Muslims. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.79} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.92}. There [[person/hamza|Hamza]] struck Abu Jahl with his bow when he insulted the Prophet in its presence, and he became a Muslim. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.100}.

## What is not mentioned here
The book does not describe the hill or the places of circling and running around it; the rulings of jurisprudence about them need their own sources.

## For young visitors
Al-Safa is a small hill near the Ka'ba. The Prophet climbed it and called the people of Mecca to hear his message.
