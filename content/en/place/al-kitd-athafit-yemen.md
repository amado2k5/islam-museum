## Overview
Al-Kitd is a place near [[place/athafit|Athafit]], "smooth on top and hard to descend" on its side, which [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] took in the battle of Friday, having a ramp built to come down from it to Athafit and then bringing his horse up to it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.102} The editor's footnote says he found the name in no geographical book, that one manuscript reads "al-Kabd", and that he prefers "al-Kitd" because "kitd" is where the shoulders meet and high places are called by it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.102} The reading therefore rests on the editor's preference. The text gives no coordinates. See [[event/second-battle-of-athafit-al-kitd-and-the-ambush-285]].
