## Overview
Batočina is an ancient settlement in Serbia, attested in Pleiades from the Roman and late antique periods and cited there from the Barrington Atlas. {cite:pleiades 206961}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Batočina» (Pleiades no. 206961), classed as a settlement, with a time span from 30 BCE to 640 CE, covering the Roman and late antique periods. It records no separate names for it. It describes the place as an ancient place, citing the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 21, grid D5. {cite:pleiades 206961}
