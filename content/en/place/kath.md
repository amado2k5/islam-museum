## Overview
Kath is a populous, inhabited town with closely joined buildings. Markets and standing buildings are attached to it, and it lies thirty miles from [[place/yazd|Yazd]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.430}
