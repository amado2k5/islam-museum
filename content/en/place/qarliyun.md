## Overview
Qarliyun is a strong, impregnable fortress with a high, well-built stronghold and connected buildings. The river named after it joins it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.605}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says Qarliyun is a strong, impregnable fortress with a high stronghold and buildings joined together. Its river, named after the fortress, runs by it. From Qarliyun to Raya is eight Arab miles, and from Qarliyun to Jatwa is five Frankish miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.605}

From Jatwa to Tarazi is nine miles. Tarazi is a fortress of ancient foundation, very secure, with farmland, its land meets Jatwa's to the north and Qarliyun's to the south, about eight miles from Qarliyun. Between Qarliyun and the road fortress to the north is nine Arab miles, which are three Frankish miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.605}
