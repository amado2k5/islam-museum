## Overview
al-Khattal: a land beyond the Oxus river. Its people made peace with Sa'id ibn Uthman, and then broke it. Umayya ibn Abd Allah conquered it, and then it broke away again, and al-Muhallab ibn Abi Safra conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
