## Overview
Zaragoza is a city in the province of Arnit in al-Andalus. Al-Idrisi mentions it together with Washqa and Tatila, and it lies in the north-east of the provinces of al-Andalus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.538}
