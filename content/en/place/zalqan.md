## Overview
Zalqan is a city at a distance from [[place/bust|Bust]], with a wall, markets, running water, palms and vines, according to al-Idrisi. It is as large as Qarnayn, and most of its people are weavers and merchants. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.457}
