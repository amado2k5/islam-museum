## Overview
al-Musayla: a newly founded, populous town on a level plain. al-Idrisi reports that it was founded in the time of the rule of Idris ibn Abd Allah, and that it has extensive farms, horses, sheep and cattle. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.254}
