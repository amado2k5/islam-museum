## Overview
al-Sus: a great city in the districts of al-Ahwaz. Abu Musa besieged it until its people ran out of food, and he conquered it on terms of safe-conduct for eighty men. Its governor was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The body of Daniel
al-Baladhuri reports that the body of the Prophet Daniel was found in its fortress, and that Abu Musa buried it in the river by the order of Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes al-Sus as a town in Khuzistan, where the tomb of the prophet Daniel is said to be. Hamza says that al-Sus is an Arabized form of al-Shush, meaning good, pleasant and fine. Ptolemy's coordinates place it at thirty-four degrees of latitude. Yaqut reports Ibn al-Muqaffa' saying that the walls of Sus and Tustar were the first set up on earth after the Flood, and records a Persian account that Ardashir ibn Bahman first built the districts of Sus and dug their river. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.280}

He also names two other places called al-Sus: a region in the Maghrib whose chief city was Tangier, and a farther al-Sus in the Maghrib whose city was Tarqula. Between the lower and the farther Sus, he says, is a journey of two months. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.280}
