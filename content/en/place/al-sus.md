## Overview
al-Sus: a great city in the districts of al-Ahwaz. Abu Musa besieged it until its people ran out of food, and he conquered it on terms of safe-conduct for eighty men. Its governor was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The body of Daniel
al-Baladhuri reports that the body of the Prophet Daniel was found in its fortress, and that Abu Musa buried it in the river by the order of Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
