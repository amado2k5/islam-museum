## Overview
The Palace of al-Musayyirin is a palace in Basra which belonged to Abd al-Rahman ibn Ziyad. It is a palace inside a palace. Next to it comes the palace of [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]], and beside it a jawsaq (pavilion). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source mentions that [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] sent to it the dependants of those who went out with Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath, and imprisoned them there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
