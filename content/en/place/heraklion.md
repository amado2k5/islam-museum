## Overview
Heraklion is the port city of Crete. Pleiades describes it as the harbour city of ancient Knossos, later re-founded by the Arab conquerors. {cite:pleiades 589802}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Heraklion» (Pleiades no. 589802), classed as a settlement, a port, a modern settlement and an archaeological site, with a time span from 330 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «Ἡράκλειον» in Greek, «Candia» in Italian, «ربض الخندق» in Arabic and «قندیه» in Ottoman Turkish. It describes the place as the harbour city of ancient Knossos, later re-founded by the Arab conquerors. {cite:pleiades 589802}
