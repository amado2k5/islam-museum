## Summary
Belgrabah is a city of the land of Qarantara which al-Idrisi places thirty miles south of the city of Astarkuna. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.878}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Astarkuna to the city of Belgrabah is thirty miles southward, and that Belgrabah is one of the cities of the land of Qarantara {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.878}. He explains that Qarantara neighbours the land of Anklaya on the west, is bounded by the Venetians' lands on the south, by the land of Ankiriya on the east and by the land of Bwamiya on the north, lying between the river Danube and the Wadi Dharwa; and among its lands and chief cities he lists Bazwara, Buzana, Nitram, Belgrabah, Siq Law and Buza, all of them lands adjoining the Venetians between whom severe wars were continually fought {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.878}. Belgrabah is thus one of the chief towns of Qarantara on its frontier with the Venetians.
