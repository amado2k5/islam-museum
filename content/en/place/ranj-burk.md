## Overview
Ranj Burk is a city in the lands of Babir, according to al-Idrisi. It has many dwellings, is extensive in area, and its gardens and vineyards are contiguous. It lies south of the river Danu. He mentions that between it and Bataswa there are seventy miles to the east. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.876}
