## Overview
Ranj Burk is one of the cities of the land of Babir, with many houses, a populous territory, and continuous plantations and vineyards, lying south of the river Danube (Dunaw). It is sixty miles from Uzburk. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 876}

## The land of Babir
Babir is a large land with villages, settlements and many forts; its famous towns include Ranj Burk (also called Rinsh Burk), the city of Batsuwa, the city of Anzz Qarta and Gharmaysha. It is bordered by the land of Buwamiya (Bohemia) on the east, Sawaba on the west, Qarantara on the south and al-Lamaniya on the north, and it is a splendid land, rich in good things and famed for its blessings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 876}

## Distances
From Ranj Burk to Batsuwa is seventy miles east, and from Batsuwa to Anzz Qarta sixty miles; the latter is a large, populous city with much traffic, joined buildings, and abundant waters, rivers and gardens. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 876} From Ifrizak to Ranj Burk is a hundred miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 887}
