## Summary
Al-Bawazij is a city on the Lesser Zab at its mouth into the Tigris, which al-Idrisi reckons within the district of al-Jazira and Diyar Mudar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.658}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from al-Sinn to the city of al-Bawazij is twelve miles, that it is a city on the Lesser Zab on its western side and on the mouth of the Lesser Zab river, and a short distance up it stands the city of al-Sinn, which is assigned to the district of al-Jazira and Diyar Mudar {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.658}. Just before, he notes that al-Sinn is a city with a strong wall and a market where the Lesser Zab empties, that between al-Sinn and Tikrit is forty miles, and that from al-Sinn to al-Haditha is thirty-six miles, al-Haditha being a flourishing city east of the Tigris where the Greater Zab empties {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.658}. Al-Bawazij is thus a Tigris-side city at the mouth of the Lesser Zab near al-Sinn and al-Haditha.
