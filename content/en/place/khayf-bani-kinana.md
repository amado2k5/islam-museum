## Overview
The Khayf of the Banu Kinana is the people's valley of al-Safi in [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. There the Prophet said to Quraysh: "Our appointment is at the Khayf of the Banu Kinana." It is the al-Muhassab where pilgrims stop after [[place/mina|Mina]], according to al-Azraqi. {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka}

Its location has not yet been fixed with coordinates. An additional source is needed.
