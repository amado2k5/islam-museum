## Overview
The Khayf of the Banu Kinana is the people's valley of al-Safi in [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. There the Prophet said to Quraysh: "Our appointment is at the Khayf of the Banu Kinana." It is the al-Muhassab where pilgrims stop after [[place/mina|Mina]], according to al-Azraqi. {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka}

Its location has not yet been fixed with coordinates. An additional source is needed.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut mentions Khayf Banu Kinana in several entries, with differing descriptions. Under Kinana he calls it the mosque of Mina in Mecca and the ravine of Banu Kinana between al-Hujun and Safi al-Sibab. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.481} Under khayf he defines the word as what slopes down from the hard ground of a mountain and lies above the water channel, names the Mosque of al-Khayf at Mina after it, and quotes al-Qadi 'Iyad that Khayf Banu Kinana is al-Muhassab, as explained in the hadith of 'Abd al-Razzaq, the open ground of Mecca, which some make the beginning of al-Abtah; he quotes al-Zuhri that khayf is a valley and al-Hazimi that Khayf Banu Kinana is at Mina, where the Prophet halted. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.412} Under al-Muhassab he places it between Mecca and Mina and nearer Mina, calls it the open ground of Mecca and Khayf Banu Kinana, gives its limit as from al-Hujun going toward Mina, and quotes al-Asma'i that its limit lies between the ravine of 'Amr and the ravine of Banu Kinana. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.62} Under al-Abtah he says it is also Khayf Banu Kinana, and notes a saying that it is Dhu Tuwa, which the entry rejects. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.74}

## The museum's identification
The record, from al-Azraqi, is the ravine of al-Safi in Mecca, called Khayf Banu Kinana, where the Prophet told Quraysh that their meeting place was Khayf Banu Kinana, and which is al-Muhassab, where pilgrims halt after Mina. Yaqut agrees that Khayf Banu Kinana is al-Muhassab and that it lies near al-Hujun; he does not give the meeting-place report and, in his entries on khayf, Kinana, al-Muhassab and al-Abtah, does not name "al-Safi" alone, the nearest name there being Safi al-Sibab; he does name the ravine of al-Safi (Shi'b al-Safi) at Mecca in two other entries, Shawa {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.369} and Nuza'at al-Shawa, from al-Hazimi {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.281}, without linking it to Khayf, so that the identification of the record's al-Safi with Khayf Banu Kinana is the museum's reading, which Yaqut does not state. His placements differ: the mosque of Mina in Mecca, at Mina (al-Hazimi), and between Mecca and Mina but nearer Mina. The record's point is 3.4 km from the museum's Mecca point and 4.8 km from its Mina point, that is, nearer Mecca, which differs from Yaqut's "nearer Mina"; the museum leaves the point unchanged and sends the question to outside research.
