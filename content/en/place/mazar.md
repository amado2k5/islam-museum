## Overview
Mazar is a large, fine city with wide lanes, streets and busy markets, and wide inns and gardens. Goods are dispatched from it to all horizons. It lies on the sea, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.600}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives "Mazar", with a fatha on the za and a final ra, and says it is a city in Sicily to which some commentators on the Sahih are attributed. He then gives "Mazar" a second time, with the za written first, as a city in Sicily on the authority of al-Silafi, and after it another "Mazar", a village of Lurestan between Isfahan and Khuzistan, which is a different place with no tie to this record. The entry says no more. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.40} {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.41}

## The museum's identification
The record agrees with Yaqut that Mazar is a city in Sicily, which suits al-Idrisi's city on the sea in the record. Yaqut adds nothing to that; he does not describe its buildings or markets. The link from the record to the modern Mazara rests on the Wikidata and al-Thurayya matches recorded for the place and on the museum's reading.
