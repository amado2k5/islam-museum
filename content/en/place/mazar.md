## Overview
Mazar is a large, fine city with wide lanes, streets and busy markets, and wide inns and gardens. Goods are dispatched from it to all horizons. It lies on the sea, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.600}
