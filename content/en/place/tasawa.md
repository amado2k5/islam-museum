## Overview
Tasawa is a town in the land of Fazzan, which the blacks call Little Jarma. It and the town of Jarma are near each other, about a stage apart or less, and equal in size and in the amount of building. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112}

## Water and crops
Their water comes from wells, they have some palms, and they grow sorghum and barley, which they water by hauling with devices they call anjaqa, known in the Maghrib as khattara. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112}

## Mine and road
They have a silver mine in a mountain called Jabal Jujis, of little yield, and the seekers have given up working and extracting it, leaving it to whoever wants it. From Tasawa to this mine is about three stages. From Tasawa to a Berber clan to the east is about twelve days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112} Fazzan adjoins the land of Zaghawa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112} The page names no wall or ruler for either town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 112}
