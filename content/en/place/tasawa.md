## Overview
Tasawa is a city in Fezzan near Jarma, with wells and palms. Al-Idrisi mentions near it a silver mine in a mountain, and places a tribe of Berbers about twelve days from it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.112}
