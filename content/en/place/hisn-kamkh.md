## Overview
The Fortress of Kamkh is one of the frontier posts of Armenia, near Shamshat. Muslims and Romans took turns holding it, according to al-Baladhuri in "Futuh al-Buldan". It is the site of the [[event/conquest-of-kamkh|conquest of the Fortress of Kamkh]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What the source reports
It was opened to Umayr ibn al-Hubab in the year 59, when he climbed its wall alone. Then Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

It was besieged with catapults and siege engines in 149 under the command of al-Abbas ibn Muhammad, and it was taken after two hundred Muslims were killed by stones. The governor of Shamshat took it in 177. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Abd Allah ibn Tahir returned it in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. Afterwards it was handed to the Romans by the arrangement of the Christians of Shamshat with Yuqalla and Buqrat ibn Ashut. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The figures and dates here come from one source; an additional source is needed.
