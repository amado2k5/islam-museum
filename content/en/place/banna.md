## Overview
Banna: a town between Multan and Kabul, near the marshes, according to al-Baladhuri. al-Muhallab ibn Abi Safra came to it in the year 44 AH, and the Azd spent the night in it. Poetry praising the Azd was said about that. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalises the name with a fatha on the first letter and a doubled nun, and says it is a city in Kabul. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.500} From a book of conquests he reports that al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra raided the frontier of Sind in the year 44, in the days of Mu'awiya, and came to Banna and Lahur, which lie between Multan and Kabul; the enemy met him and al-Muhallab killed them with his men, and he cites a line by one of the Azd on it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.501} He then lists a village of the Baghdad district and a fortress in al-Andalus of the same name, which are other places.
