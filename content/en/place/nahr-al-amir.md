## Overview
The River of the Amir is a river at [[place/basra|Basra]]. [[person/al-mansur|Al-Mansur]] dug it, then gave it to his son Jafar. It used to be called the River of the Commander of the Faithful, and then the River of the Amir. Later al-Rashid bought it, granted land from it, and sold parts of it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
