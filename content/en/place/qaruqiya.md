## Overview
Qaruqiya is a fine city whose buildings are of wood and felt tents, among the towns of Bashjirt al-Kharija with Namjan and Gharkhan. The people of Bulghar raid them at all times, sixteen stages away, and their wars are always under way. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 922} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 924}

## The way there
From the city of Gharkhan to Qaruqiya is eight days down the river, or sixteen stages westward by land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 924} Between Qaruqiya and Bashjirt al-Dakhila is twelve stages through rough mountains and hard routes, and from the centre of Bashjirt al-Dakhila to the centre of Bashjirt al-Kharija is eleven stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 925}

## The country
Al-Idrisi describes the country of Bashjirt al-Kharija as populous and self-sufficient, with enough trade and crafts for its needs; the peoples live inside one another's lands and get their requirements from neighbouring countries; it is fertile, with much pasture and livestock. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 922} Most of these lands are continuous deserts and wastes, with few towns far apart. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 922}
