## Overview
Russia is a large land with few cities, whose buildings are widely spaced. Al-Idrisi mentions that its people have constant wars and raids with their neighbours, and he names among its cities Zaqa, Tarubiya and Ghalisiya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.904}
