## Overview
Al-Ghubayb is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sought [[person/ibn-al-dahhak-rebel-at-khiwan-286|Ibn al-Dahhak]] and his companions, found the people gone and ordered their vines cut and their houses pulled down. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.144} After he gave them safe-conduct and they swore to him he spent the night there and next day went back to [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.145} The book's index lists "al-Ghubayb" at p. 145 only, and the museum does not assert that it is the [[place/al-ghubayyib-yemen|al-Ghubayyib]] of p. 136, though the names are close. See [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-al-ghubayb-and-the-return-to-khiwan-on-1-dhu-al-qada-286]].
