## Overview
al-Munarij: a place in Maysan. al-Baladhuri reports that al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba met there the landowner of Maysan, who disbelieved and turned back from Islam. He killed him and wrote to Umar with news of the conquest. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
