## Overview
Ardakan is a city in Yazd Province in Iran, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2110655}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2110655 with the preferred name «Ardakān» (Persian), classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit Yazd Province. Among the names it records are «Ardakan», «اردکان» and «Ardekan». A German note adds «bei Schiraz», near Shiraz, and the entry links the place to GeoNames no. 143073 and to two identifiers of the Arachne database. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2110655}
