## Overview
Ghayara is a town on the bank of the Nile, with a ditch around it, and many people live there. Al-Idrisi describes its people as valiant and knowledgeable, and says that they raid the land of Lamlam, take its people captive and sell them to the merchants of Ghana. It lies eleven stages from Ghana and thirteen from Lamlam. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.26}
