## Overview
Hama is a Syrian city on the Orontes river, famous for its water-wheels. A branch of the Ayyubid family ruled it, and it was the home of the historian Abu al-Fida. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Amathe/Epiphaneia» (Pleiades no. 668182), classed as a settlement and a modern settlement, with activity attested from 2000 BCE to 2100 CE. {cite:pleiades 668182}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2281529, typed as archaeological site and populated place, with the preferred name «Hama» under «Nahiyat Hama» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including حماه, حماة, Hamath. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281529}
