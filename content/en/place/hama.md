## Overview
Hama is a Syrian city on the Orontes river, famous for its water-wheels. A branch of the Ayyubid family ruled it, and it was the home of the historian Abu al-Fida. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records Hamat with fatha, the word of a man's mother-in-law, four readings allowed, hamat also the sinew of the leg; then Hamat the great city, rich in goods, cheap of prices, broad of extent, busy of markets, ringed by a stout wall with a very large suburb outside it holding many markets and a congregational mosque looking over its river known as al-Asi — the Orontes — on which norias draw water for its gardens and pour into the mosque's pool. This suburb they call the Lower Market because it lies below the town, the walled part the Upper; at the town's edge stands a great fortress, its ditch cut a hundred cubits and more, for al-Malik al-Mansur Muhammad ibn Taqi al-Din Umar ibn Shahanshah ibn Ayyub. It is an ancient Jahili city which Imru' al-Qays named in verse, though it was not always the great place it is today: it belonged to the workings of Homs, and Ahmad ibn al-Tayyib called it a village when he passed it in the year 271. The astronomers give it longitude 62 degrees and two-thirds and latitude 35 degrees and two-thirds and a quarter; Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir records that when Abu Ubayda conquered Homs in year 17 he left Ubada ibn al-Samit there and marched on Hamat, whose people submitted, so he truced them for jizya on their heads and kharaj on their land before going on to Shayzar. Between it and Homs, al-Ma'arra and Salamiyya is a day each, to Shayzar half a day, to Damascus five days for caravans, to Aleppo four. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.300-301}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut's Hamat is the record's Orontes city (by the two records' points about 44.8 km from Homs, against the printed day's march); the norias, the twin markets and al-Mansur's Ayyubid citadel are a portrait of Yaqut's own day, and the year-17 conquest and the linguistic notes are reported as printed.
