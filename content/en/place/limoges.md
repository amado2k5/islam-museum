## Overview
Limoges is a fine, fortified town with wealth, conspicuous buildings and crops. The Nuzha describes it as the capital of the province of Anjou. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.741}

From it to the town of Mont Lechon is sixty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.741}
