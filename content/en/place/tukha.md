## Overview
Tukha is a city populous with people, according to al-Idrisi. Tukhi silk garments are made there, and it lies six days from Isqiriya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.206}

## Details
Al-Idrisi places Tukha on the Kalahi, the great river of China. It is a city full of people, with merchants, goods and stores. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.206} Here the Tukhi garments are made from silk called qassi. They are valued highly and are sent out to other lands. They are of the kind called muṭarrafa, similar to the ʿatābī cloths, and there are also garments called murisha, which last a long time when worn. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.206}

His distances link Tukha to its neighbours. From Isqiriya to Tukha is six days. From Tukha to Bura is two days downstream on the Kalahi, or four days by land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.206} From Qarnabul to Tukha is six stages, and Tukha is already described in the entry on Qarnabul. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.207}
