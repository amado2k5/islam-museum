## Overview
Prešov is a city in Slovakia. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the Pleiades gazetteer records. {cite:pleiades 128495}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Prešov» (Pleiades no. 128495), classed as a settlement, with its recorded location dated from 30 BCE to 640 CE, that is the Roman and late-antique periods. It records no names. Its description says only that this is an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas, map 13, square G4, and that the Atlas directory gives it as «Prešov, SVK». {cite:pleiades 128495}
