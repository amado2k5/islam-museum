## Summary
Alamas was a small urbanized town in the Romanian lands, rich in fruits and goods, with spreading buildings and wide districts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Alamas among the named towns of the land of Rumania {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.892}. Giving the distances he says: from Framnak to the city of Alamas eastward half a day, and it is a small urban town abounding in fruits and goods, with spreading buildings and wide territories {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}. From Alamas to the city of Karnabi is half a day, a country place near a mountain {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.900}. In his account Alamas is a built-up township half a day's march along the caravan road of that region.
