## Overview
Qanat, which is today Ghent, is the greatest city in the land of Lubban, according to al-Idrisi. It lies on the west bank of a river he calls Arin. It has many houses, broad buildings, vineyards and standing crops. He gives its distance north from the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.870}
