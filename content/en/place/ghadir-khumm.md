## Overview
Ghadir Khumm is a pool and a stopping place on the pilgrims' road between Mecca and Medina, near al-Juhfa. The Prophet stopped there in 632 CE. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The location is approximate, and the description of the stop needs an additional source.
