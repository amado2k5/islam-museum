## Overview
Tajwa is the capital of the land of the Tajawin, a people whom al-Idrisi describes as Magians. Their land borders the land of Nubia, and it lies thirteen stages from the Zaghawa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.30}
