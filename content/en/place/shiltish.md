## Overview
Shiltish is a city with no wall or enclosure, only buildings joined one to another. It has a market and an iron trade that the people of the country cannot match, the making of the anchors by which ships and heavy boats are moored. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 542}

## The island and the river
It stands on the island of Shiltish, surrounded by the sea on every side and joined to the mainland at one end on the west across about half a stone's throw, from where they cross to fetch water. The island is a little over a mile long, and the city is on its southern side. An arm of the sea there joins the mouth of the river of Labla and widens to more than a mile, then narrows as one goes up it by boat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 542}

## Raids and distances
Al-Idrisi says that the Majus overcame it several times, and that its people flee and leave it empty when they hear of them. From Shiltish to the island of Cadiz is a hundred miles, and northward along the sea to the fort of Qastala eighteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 542} It is listed in the province of al-Sharaf between Seville, Labla and the Dark Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 537}
