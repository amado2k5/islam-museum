## Overview
Shiltish is a city on an island about a mile long, with no wall or enclosure, and it has a market. Iron and the anchors with which ships are moored are made there. It lies a cubit away from Labla, on the sea, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.542}
