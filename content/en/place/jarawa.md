## Summary
Jarawa is a town of the Maghreb which al-Idrisi places one stage from the city of Sa'a, with six miles between it and the sea; it was once populous. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.248}

## Details
Jarawa appears on the road linking Sa'a and Tlemcen: from Sa'a to Jarawa is one stage, six miles separate Jarawa from the sea, and it was inhabited {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.248}. From it to Burqana is one stage, Burqana being a fortress bearing a strong keep with a busy market, abundant water, gardens and vineyards; then one stage to al-'Alawiyyin, a large village on a river reaching it from the south, whose fruit is excellent and whose benefits are all-embracing; and then one short stage to Tlemcen {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.248}. Sa'a, its neighbour in the itinerary, is a small pleasant town at the foot of a hill overlooking a great river that cuts through its outskirts {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.247}.
