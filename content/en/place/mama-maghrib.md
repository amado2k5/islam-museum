## Summary
Mama was a small town of the Maghreb with a mud-brick wall, a dug moat running around it, and a sweet-water wadi bearing farms and crops; its yield of wheat was great. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.256}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the village of Darast to the town of Mama was two stages, and from Mama to the village of An Mujbir one stage — a large village of many crops and sweet water whose people drank from springs and belonged to the Zanata — and from there to Ashir Ziri one stage; from Ashir Ziri to the village of Satit one stage, where a spring of running water rises; thence to the village of Han in a sandy tract one stage — spring waters reach it, though it is now ruined — and from it to al-Msila one stage. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.256}
