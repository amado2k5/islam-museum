## Summary
Afrunisfa was a large, flourishing city in the land of Hungary, standing on a hill overlooking the Danube, noted for cheap prices and constant abundance and trade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says Afrunisfa is a prosperous city on a hill overlooking the river Danube, three days from Belgrade which also lies on that river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.795}. Elsewhere he describes it as a city on level ground, populous, with many comings and goings, cheap prices, lasting abundance, running waters, wide districts, continuous commerce and plentiful goods {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}. He reckons two long stages, said to be a hundred miles, from Ribna to Afrunisfa {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}.
