## Overview
Marw al-Rudh is a land in Tukharistan, one of the districts of Khurasan. [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] besieged it after a fierce battle, and its people took refuge in their fortress. Its marzban made peace with him for sixty thousand, or, it is said, six hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Among its fortresses is the palace of al-Ahnaf. He made peace with its people for three hundred thousand, on condition that a man of the Muslims enter the palace, call the adhan within it, and stay with them until he leaves. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
