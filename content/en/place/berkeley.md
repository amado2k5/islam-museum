## Overview
Berkeley is a populated place in California, in the United States, according to the iDAI.gazetteer, which gives its preferred name as «Berkeley / Kalifornien». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087380}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2087380 with the preferred name «Berkeley / Kalifornien», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «United States of America», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Berkeley» (English), «Беркли» (Russian) and «بيركيلي، كاليفورنيا» (Arabic). It links the place to GeoNames no. 5327684. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087380}
