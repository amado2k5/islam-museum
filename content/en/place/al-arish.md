## Overview
Al-Arish: the first stopping place of [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] on his march to Egypt in the year 19 AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The march
He stayed there, then came to Farama. A letter from [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] reached him at a place near it, ordering him to return to his position if he had arrived short of Egypt; he had already crossed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The place is connected with [[event/conquest-of-egypt|the conquest of Egypt]].
