## Overview
Qalunia is a city on the western bank of the river Rin, large in extent, populous on its sides, with continuous crops. Al-Idrisi mentions that between it and Mubanisa there are sixty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.873}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Col. Clunia (Sulpicia)» (Pleiades no. 246345), classed as settlement and archaeological site, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 1999 CE. Its recorded names include Col. Clunia Sulpicia, Clunia and Peñalba de Castro. It describes a Roman city of Hispania Tarraconensis, besieged by Pompey during the war against Sertorius. {cite:pleiades 246345}
