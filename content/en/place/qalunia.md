## Overview
Qalunia is a city on the western bank of the river Rin, large in extent, populous on its sides, with continuous crops. Al-Idrisi mentions that between it and Mubanisa there are sixty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.873}
