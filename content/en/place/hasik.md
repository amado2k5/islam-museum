## Overview
Hasik is a small, civilised village on the sea. Two islands lie in front of it and a great mountain above it. On a creek called the Creek of Grass, which is hard to get out of except with a particular wind, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.56-57}
