## Overview
Alsh, now Elche, is a small town in the Spanish Levant, about twelve miles south-west of Alicante. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 444}

## Palm groves
The town is noted for its palm groves, which still exist, and which Muslim authors such as Ibn Sa'id and al-Qazwini described. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 444}
