## Overview
Peshawar is a major city of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in Pakistan, near the Khyber Pass. Its name does not appear in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum, so this page rests only on what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Peshawar» (Pleiades no. 569531631), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 1700 CE to 2100 CE. It records two names, «Peshawar» in English and «Purushapura» in Sanskrit. Its description says that ancient Peshawar was a capital of the Gandharan kingdom from the second century CE, when it was known as Purushapura. {cite:pleiades 569531631}
