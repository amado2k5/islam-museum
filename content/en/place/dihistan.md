## Overview
A city behind [[place/jurjan|Jurjan]] in the land of the Turks. Sul the Turk came down in it, and [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] besieged it and cut off supplies from its people until they made peace with him on safe-conduct and the surrender of the city. He appointed over it, and over al-Bayasan, Abd Allah ibn Ma'mar al-Yashkuri, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
