## Overview
Rughusa is a fine, populous, inhabited city, whose people are Slavs (saqaliba). Al-Idrisi describes them as people of raids and ships. In his account it is the last land of the Slavs on the western side, and it lies thirty miles from Shi'ghnu. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.769}
