## Overview
al-Khurayba: a place that Khalid passed after the fighting at al-Ubulla. He conquered it and took captive those in it. al-Baladhuri reports that he appointed as its governor, according to al-Kalbi, Shurayh ibn Amir ibn Qayn of Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr ibn Hawazin. It was a garrison post against the Persians. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
