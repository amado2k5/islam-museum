## Overview
Jantama is a small town like a gathering village in the land of Sufala, according to al-Idrisi. Its people are poor and have nothing to dispose of. Its minerals are iron, and their iron is carried to the land of India. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.67-68}
