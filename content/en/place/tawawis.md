## Overview
Al-Tawawis is a populous city inside the wall of Bukhara. Al-Idrisi says its pulpit is the greatest pulpit of the region, and that people and merchants come to it from every place, and much goods are brought to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.495}
