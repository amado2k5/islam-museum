## Overview
Al-Tawawis is a town inside the wall of [[place/bukhara|Bukhara]], and the one with the greatest pulpit in that district. It is populous and holds a market at a known time of year to which people and merchants come from all Khurasan to buy and sell, and much merchandise is brought to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 495}

## Trade and buildings
Cotton cloth is carried from it to the rest of Iraq in quantity, and it has workshops and tiraz for making these garments, fruits of many kinds and many gardens, with water running through its streets. It is well fortified, with a citadel (qohandiz) and a wall around it, and its congregational mosque stands inside the town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 495} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 496}

## Neighbours
From al-Tawawis to Bukhara is one stage, twenty-seven miles. Within the same wall al-Idrisi names Bumjakath, smaller than al-Tawawis, populous, with an earthen wall, crafts and gardens, as well as Zandana, Mafkan and Khujada. From al-Tawawis to Karminiya is one stage on the road to Samarkand; Karminiya is larger, more populous and more fertile than al-Tawawis. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 496}
