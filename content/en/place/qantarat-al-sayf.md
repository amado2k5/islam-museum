## Overview
Al-Idrisi says that it is two days from Mardah to the bridge of al-Sayf. He calls it one of the wonders of the earth: a strong fortress stands on the bridge itself, and its people shelter within it. The bridge cannot be taken by fighting except through its gate. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.546}

From the bridge to Qurriya is two light stages. Qurriya is a city in Roman territory with a strong wall, one of the most secure strongholds and finest dwellings, with good gardens and estates. Most of its fruit is grapes and fig trees. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.547}
