## Overview
Suq al-Ahwaz is a city in the land of al-Ahwaz. [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|Al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] conquered it during his governorship. Its dihqan, Biruz, made peace with him for money, and then broke the terms. [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] conquered it by force in the year 17 AH, and appointed Samura ibn Jundub al-Fazari over it, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
