## Overview
Philadelphia is a populated place in Pennsylvania, in the United States, according to the iDAI.gazetteer. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2092977}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2092977 with the preferred name «Philadelphia», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Pennsylvania (State)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Philly» (English), «Filadelfia» (Italian) and «Филадельфия» (Russian). It links the place to GeoNames no. 4560349. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2092977}
