## Overview
Qal'at al-Balut (the Oak Fortress) is an impregnable stronghold on a high peak, in a fertile valley surrounded by valleys with many mills. It lies about twelve miles from al-Shaqqa, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.600}
