## Overview
Kirman is a province. In the account of al-Baladhuri, Yazdegerd fled to it after Istakhr. Mujashi ibn Mas'ud alighted at Bayamand in Kirman, and his army was struck by plague and perished. The marzban of the province came to Yazdegerd, and he did not speak to him out of pride, and ordered his leg dragged; then Yazdegerd went on to Sistan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[person/mujashi-ibn-masud-al-sulami|Mujashi ibn Mas'ud]].

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut says that Kirman, with a fatha on the first letter, is a famous province and a large inhabited region between Fars, Makran, Sijistan and Khurasan, rich in palms, crops and livestock. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.454} He quotes al-Bashshari that it is a region resembling Fars, like Basra in some respects and close to Khurasan, names among its towns Jiruft, Bamm, al-Sirjan and Bardsir, and says its people are Sunni. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.454} On the name he gives Ibn al-Kalbi's view that it was named for Kirman ibn Falluj ibn Lanti ibn Yafith, and another view that it was named for Kirman ibn Farik ibn Sam. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.454} He relates that Uthman ibn Abi al-As killed the marzban of Kirman on the island of Barkawan, and that Ibn Amir sent Mujashi ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami after Yazdegerd, whose army perished at Bimand; Mujashi was then made governor of Kirman and took al-Sirjan and Jiruft. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.455}
