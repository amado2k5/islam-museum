## Summary
Awnastra is an inland town which al-Idrisi places in the middle of an island, with a river of the same name flowing past the coastal city of Hantuna. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.945}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Gharhum to the cape of Hantuna, a headland jutting into the sea, is twenty-five miles, and at its eastern edge stands the flourishing city of Hantuna, where the river of Awnastra empties on its eastern side; Awnastra is an inland town, and between Hantuna and Awnastra is eighty miles, and from Awnastra westward to Salabris is forty miles, and the river of Awnastra rises in a mountain that crosses the middle of the island {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.945}. The town is thus interior rather than coastal, lending its name to the river that runs down to Hantuna on the shore.
