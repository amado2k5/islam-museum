## Overview
Pisidia is a historical region of Asia Minor; the museum records a representative point for it at about 38.3 degrees north and 31.02 degrees east. {cite:wikidata} This page does not yet carry an account of the region itself from a source read for this run.

## In Nomisma
The Nomisma numismatic vocabulary links this entry to the concept «pisidia», and its endpoint records 141 coins or coin types that name it as their region, that is, an area of issue and not a mint or issuing authority; 38 other Nomisma concepts name it as their broader term. The dated types run from 500 BC to 25 BC. {cite:nomisma pisidia}
