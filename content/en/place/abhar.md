## Overview
Abhar: a town between [[place/hamadhan|Hamadhan]] and [[place/qazwin|Qazvin]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fortress and safe-conduct
[[person/al-bara-ibn-azib|al-Bara ibn Azib]] came to it on his way to Qazvin, and its people fought him from their fortress. The fortress had been built by some Persians over springs that were sealed with cowhide and wool; a platform was made over them, and the fortress was then raised on top of it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then its people asked for safe-conduct, and he granted it to them on the same terms that [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa]] had granted the people of [[place/nihawand|Nihawand]], and he took possession of its lands. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
