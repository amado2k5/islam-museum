## Overview
Almaligh was the capital of a Muslim kingdom in the upper Ili valley. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 440}

## Foundation and rulers
The kingdom was founded in the 7th/13th century by Uzar (in Juwayni) or Buzar (in Jamal Karshi), who was said to have been a brigand and horse-thief before. According to Jamal, he took the title Toghril Khan as ruler. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 440}

## The city
Almaligh is first mentioned as the capital of this kingdom and later as a great and wealthy commercial city. Its site is known mainly from Chinese sources; it lay south of Lake Sayram and the Talki pass, north of the Ili, probably north-west of modern Kuldja. Its king had dealings with Chinggis Khan, whose hunting-ground lay near Almaligh; the king was surprised and killed while hunting by Kuchluk. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 441}
