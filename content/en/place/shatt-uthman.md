## Overview
Shatt Uthman is a site in Basra. The author of Futuh al-Buldan mentions that Uthman ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi bought it from Uthman ibn Affan with money he had at al-Ta'if. It is said that he bought it in exchange for a house he had in Medina, which Uthman ibn Affan added to the mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The site has a connection with [[place/basra|Basra]].
