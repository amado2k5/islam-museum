## Overview
Lake Zughar: the Dead Sea, which al-Idrisi describes as a place where nothing lives. Two cities of the people of Lot were in it. It lies away from Rihā, and it is called the Dead Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.355}
