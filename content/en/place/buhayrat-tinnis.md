## Overview
Lake Tinnis: the Nile flows into it. Its water is fresh in summer when the Nile spreads out, and in winter the water of the sea prevails over it, and there are kinds of fish in it. al-Idrisi mentions in it the story of the two gardens of the two sons of Atrib. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.338}
