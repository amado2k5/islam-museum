## Overview
Shirwan is a region of the Caucasus lands beyond Arran, according to al-Baladhuri. The commander of Qubadh trod the land between the river al-Ras and Shirwan, and Qubadh built the Sadd al-Laban between the land of Shirwan and the Gate of al-Lan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273}

Among the kings of the mountains whom Anushirwan confirmed in their kingdoms was the king of Shirwan, called Shirwanshah. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.276} The area is connected with [[place/armenia|Armenia]] in the museum's data.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Shirwan as a town in the districts of Bab al-Abwab, which the Persians call Darband. Anushirvan built it and named it after himself, and the name was later shortened by dropping part of it. It is a hundred farsakh from Bab al-Abwab. Some scholars, he says, hold that a rock near the town is the rock where the fish was forgotten in the well-known story of Moses. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.339}

He records that the province of Shirwan has Shamakhi as its seat, near the Caspian sea. Among the transmitters from the town he names Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ushayr ibn Maruf al-Sharwani, a pious jurist who studied with al-Kiya al-Harrasi. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.339}
