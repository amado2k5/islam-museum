## Overview
Shirwan is a region of the Caucasus lands beyond Arran, according to al-Baladhuri. The commander of Qubadh trod the land between the river al-Ras and Shirwan, and Qubadh built the Sadd al-Laban between the land of Shirwan and the Gate of al-Lan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273}

Among the kings of the mountains whom Anushirwan confirmed in their kingdoms was the king of Shirwan, called Shirwanshah. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.276} The area is connected with [[place/armenia|Armenia]] in the museum's data.
