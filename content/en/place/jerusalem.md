## Summary
A city holy to Jews, Christians and Muslims; the first of the two qiblas, and the end of the Night Journey in the report. It was handed over to the Muslims in 637 CE.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 31.78 north and 35.24 east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
