## Overview
Sisiyan is one of the cities of the sixteen kings of the Kimakiyya. Al-Idrisi mentions it together with Buragh, Manan and Mustanah, and says it lies twelve days from Asturur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.716}
