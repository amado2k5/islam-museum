## Overview
Sinn Sumayra is an eastern fortress in al-Jabal. According to al-Baladhuri, the Muslims paused there when they campaigned in the mountains, and it was named after Sumayra, a woman of Dabba among the emigrant women, who had a tooth (sinn). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
