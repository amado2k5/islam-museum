## Overview
Bilaja: a strong, well-protected fortress and a towering stronghold, near which runs the river al-Qarib. It lies fifteen miles from the palace of Ibn Mankud, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.609}
