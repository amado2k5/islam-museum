## Overview
al-Qasaba: a small town, well built, in the Wadi Kawar, surrounded by palm trees. al-Idrisi reports that its people wear wool cloths and garments, drink from sweet wells, and travel through the lands for trade. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.117}
