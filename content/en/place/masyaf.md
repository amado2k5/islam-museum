## Overview
Masyaf is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within the sub-district of Masyaf. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2298647}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2298647 with the preferred name «Masyaf», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is the sub-district of Masyaf. Among the names it records are «Maşyāf», «Massiâf», «Masyāb» and «مصياف», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 167046 and to Pleiades no. 668309. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2298647}
