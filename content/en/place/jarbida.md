## Overview
Jarbida is a town of the Franks. Its bishop, Armaz, was there, and in 328 AH he sent to al-Hakam ibn Abd al-Rahman a book listing the names of the kings of the Franks, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358}
