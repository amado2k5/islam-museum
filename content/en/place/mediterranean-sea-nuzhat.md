## Overview
Al-Idrisi describes the Syrian Sea, which is the Mediterranean, as issuing from the Dark Sea in the west at the strait, where its width is about eighteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.11}

It runs eastward past the coasts of the Maghrib, Ifriqiya, Egypt, al-Sham, Antioch, Constantinople, Sicily, the lands of the Romans and al-Andalus. Its length is estimated at about one thousand one hundred and thirty-six farsakhs, and it contains about a hundred islands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.11}
