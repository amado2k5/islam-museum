## Overview
Samura is a city in the kingdom of the Galicians (al-Jalaliqa). Al-Mas'udi described its fine construction and walls. Abd al-Rahman al-Nasir, ruler of al-Andalus, besieged it with a hundred thousand men or more. The battle between him and Radhmir, king of the Galicians, took place in Shawwal of the year 327 AH, three days after the eclipse. The advantage was first with the Muslims, then, after being besieged, they gave way, and about fifty thousand of them were killed, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.359-360}
