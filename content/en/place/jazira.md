## Overview
The Euphrates Jazira is the region between the Euphrates and the Tigris. The report in "Futuh al-Buldan" says that Iyad ibn Ghanm conquered it after the death of Abu Ubayda. Maymun ibn Mihran is quoted as saying that all of the Jazira is among the conquests of Iyad. He set out for it on the middle of Sha'ban in 18 AH with five thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/conquest-of-jazira|the conquest of the Jazira]].

Its towns include al-Ruha, [[place/harran|Harran]], al-Raqqa, Nasibin, Sinjar and [[place/mosul|Mosul]], and the Tigris district. Together with Syria it became the field of summer and winter raids against the Romans. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
