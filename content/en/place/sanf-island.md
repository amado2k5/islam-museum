## Overview
The island of Sanf adjoins the island of Qamar on the coast side, three miles from it. Sanfi aloeswood is found there and is better than the Qamari because it sinks in water through its quality and weight. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 83}

## Animals and crops
The island has cattle and buffaloes without tails, coconuts, bananas, sugar cane and rice. Its people do not slaughter any four-footed animal or any other creature, and cattle are eaten among them only when they have died. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 83}

## King and neighbours
The island has a king named Zunbad; the people wear two loincloths each, one to wrap round the waist and one to cover themselves, and their water is sweet. From it to the island of Sandi Fulat is ten days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 83} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 84} From Sanf to the city of Luqin is three stages, the first of the ports of China. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 84}
