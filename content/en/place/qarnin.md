## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name with fatha on the first letter, sukun on the second, kasra on the nun and a final nun, and calls it a village of the rustaq of Nishak in the districts of Sijistan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.333} He quotes Ahmad ibn Sahl al-Balkhi that Qarnin is a small city with villages and rural districts, one stage from Sijistan on the left of the road to Bust and two farsakhs from Sarur. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.333} He says the Saffarids, who came to dominate Fars, Khurasan, Sijistan and Kirman, came from it: four brothers, sons of al-Layth, namely Ya'qub, Amr, Tahir and Ali, and he gives the fate of each. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.333} He relates that Ya'qub began as a coppersmith's apprentice working in brass, and that the brothers joined Duraym ibn Nasr and fought the Shurat as volunteers, with Ya'qub's standing rising until leadership passed to him. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.333} Yaqut adds that he set down their story, though he usually avoids such material, because it is rarely found in any book. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.334}
