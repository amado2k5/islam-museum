## Overview
Shubra Khit is a town in Beheira Governorate, Egypt. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2773018}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2773018 with the preferred name «Shubrakhit», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2408712. It records one further name, «Shubra Khit», also a transliterated Arabic form. It links the place to GeoNames no. 349058. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2773018}
