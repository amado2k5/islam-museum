## Overview
A place in al-Sham. Ibn Ishaq dates the killing of Abd Allah ibn Sufyan al-Makhzumi and Sa'id ibn al-Harith al-Sahmi by saying "the year of al-Yarmuk" in the caliphate of Umar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

al-Mas'udi reports that the abodes of the kings of Ghassan were at al-Yarmuk and al-Jawlan and other parts of the Ghuta of Damascus and its districts. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 427}
