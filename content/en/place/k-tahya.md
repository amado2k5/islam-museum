## Overview
Kütahya is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place and an archaeological site, within Kütahya Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283104}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2283104 with the preferred name «Kütahya», classed as a populated place and an archaeological site; its parent in the hierarchy is Kütahya Province. Among the names it records are «كوتاهيا», «Κιουτάχεια», «Kotiaion», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 305268. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2283104}
