## Overview
Qabala is a city in Arran, which in the museum's classification is in [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Qubadh ibn Firuz built it, and that the Khazars are associated with it. Salman ibn Rabi'a conquered it after crossing the Kur, toward the confluence of al-Ras and the Kur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 281-287}
