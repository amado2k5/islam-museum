## Overview
A place in the area of [[place/medina|Medina]], mentioned by Ibn Ishaq in [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-al-ghaba|the raid of Ibn Abi Hadrad on al-Ghaba]]: Rifa'a ibn Qays al-Jushami stopped there with a large clan of his people, and Ibn Abi Hadrad and his two companions lay in ambush around him and killed him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/278} It is also known by its name in [[event/expedition-of-dhu-qarad|the expedition of Dhu Qarad]], in which the Prophet's herd of milch camels was raided. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/289}
