## Overview
A land in [[place/basra|Basra]] that was bought from some of the Arabs, according to al-Baladhuri. The Isfahanis were a people who had become Muslims and emigrated to Basra, and it is said that they were with the cavalry who settled in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
