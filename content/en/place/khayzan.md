## Overview
Khayzan is a town in the district of Armenia. Al-Baladhuri reports that al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami made peace with the people of Hamzin on their removal to it, and gave them two villages from it. Later Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik made peace with it, and ordered its fortress demolished. He took for himself estates there, known today as the Hawz of Khayzan. It is connected with the account of [[event/maslama-khazar-campaign|Maslama's campaign against the Khazars]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.290} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.291}
