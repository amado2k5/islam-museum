## Overview
Shamakhi was a populous city and the capital of the land of Shirvan, on the edge of Arran. Yaqut counts it among the districts of Bab al-Abwab, the pass at Derbent, and says its ruler was the Shirvanshah, brother of the lord of Derbent. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.361}

## Distances and the minbar
Yaqut quotes al-Istakhri's road from Barda'a to Barzanj, eighteen farsakh, and from there across the river Kur to Shamakhi, fourteen farsakh, where there was no minbar (the pulpit used for the Friday sermon). From Shamakhi to Shabran, a small town with a minbar, the road took three days. Yaqut reads these details as showing that Shamakhi had recently been made into a city. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.361} In the Barzanj entry, the crossing of the Kur at Barzanj is the way to Shamakhi, "city of Shirvan". {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.382}
