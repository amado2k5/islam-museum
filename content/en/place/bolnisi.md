## Overview
Bolnisi is a place in Georgia. It has no entry in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum and none in the iDAI.gazetteer, so this page rests only on what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Bolnisi» (Pleiades no. 863760), classed as a monument, with activity attested from 300 CE to 1999 CE. It records one name, «Bolnisi» in English, attested from about 1900 CE to 1999 CE. Its description says only that this is an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas, map 88, grid square C3, and it tags the place with an inscription and a temple. {cite:pleiades 863760}
