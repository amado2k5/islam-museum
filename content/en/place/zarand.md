## Overview
Zarand is a medium-sized city with a mud wall, on the edge of the great desert, with fertility, farming, gains and many benefits. It has tanneries, and the well-known Zarandi linings are made there; merchants carry them to Iraq and sometimes to Egypt. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 439}

## The road from al-Sirjan
From the city of al-Sirjan to Zarand is four stages. From al-Sirjan to the city of Bardsir is two stages; it is a fine, populous and fertile city with a wall, a ditch and gates, with many markets and thriving crafts. From it to Janzarud is one long stage, a large populous city with markets and craftsmen, and from it to Zarand one stage. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 439}

## Setting
Zarand lies at the edge of the great desert that adjoins the land of Kirman and Fars on the east, a desert of which al-Idrisi says there is none like it in the inhabited world. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 428} The page fixes its place on the road and gives no ruler or history for it.
