## Overview
Zarand is a city on the bank of the great desert, with fertility, crops and tanneries for leather. Zarandi linings (bata'in) are made there and carried to Iraq and perhaps to Egypt, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.439}
