## Overview
A town on the Tigris, which today lies at the border between northern Iraq and southern Turkey, and is known as Cizre. Ibn Battuta mentions it as "Jazirat Ibn Umar". {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958} {cite:geonames-gazetteer}
