## Summary
Gharhum is a city on the coast of the island of England, which al-Idrisi places sixty miles along the shore from the city mentioned before it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.944}

## Details
Al-Idrisi counts Gharhum among the inhabited chief towns on the southern coast of the island called England, together with the cities of Sahstar, Huntuna, Sharham, Hastinkash, Dabrus, Jarnamuda, Targhiq, Aghrims, Lundras and others {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.880}. He says that from the fine populous city standing on a great river that comes to it from the north and flows into the sea at its east, to the city of Gharhum on the coast is sixty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.944}. He further relates that from the city of Gharhum to the point of Huntuna — a headland running into the sea — is twenty-five miles, and at its end on the eastern side stands the city of Huntuna, a populous place {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.945}. Gharhum is thus one of the coastal halts of that island's southern shore on the Sea of Darkness. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.944}
