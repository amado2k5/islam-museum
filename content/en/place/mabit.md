## Overview
Mabit is an island near the island of Jaba. It has coconuts, bananas, sugar cane and rice. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.82}

## The volcano
Al-Idrisi mentions that at the far end of the island there is a volcano, from which smoke rises by day and fire blazes by night. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.82}
