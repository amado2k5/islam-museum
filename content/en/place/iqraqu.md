## Overview
Iqraqu is a fine, populous town, one of the towns of the land of Balunya. Al-Idrisi says that mountains surround it and separate it from the lands of the Rus, and that in it is the river Shant. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.889}
