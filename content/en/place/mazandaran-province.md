## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut spells the name with a quiet nun after the za, then dal, ra and a final nun, and says it is a name for the province of Tabaristan, which he has already treated. He adds that he thinks it only a recent name for it, since he has not seen it mentioned in the books of the early authors. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.41}
