## Overview
Qusdar is a town in Makran. Sinan ibn Salama conquered it, then its people broke their covenant, and he died there. Then al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud conquered it and took captives there, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
