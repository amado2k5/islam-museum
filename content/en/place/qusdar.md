## Overview
Qusdar is a town in Makran. Sinan ibn Salama conquered it, then its people broke their covenant, and he died there. Then al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud conquered it and took captives there, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Qusdar as a well-known district near Ghazna, which some place in India; he holds the correct view to be that it lies in the Sind. It is the capital of a district called Tawran and a small town with its own rustaq and towns. He cites al-Istakhri that its dominant figure was a man who gave the khutba only for the caliph, residing in a town called Kirkaban; it is a fertile district with high prices, grapes and pomegranates, but no palms. He cites the author of the Futuh that Sinan ibn Salama conquered it, then its people broke the treaty and he died there, and that al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud took it in the governorship of Ziyad over the Indian frontier. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.353}
