## Overview
al-Kharrar: a place near al-Juhfa. The expedition of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas reached it in Dhu al-Qa'da in the year 1 AH, and they did not find the caravan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

The book does not fix its coordinates.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives the name as al-Kharrar, with a fatha on the kha' and a doubled ra', and explains that kharir is the sound of water. He calls it a place in the Hijaz, said to be near al-Juhfa, and adds three other views without choosing among them: a wadi among those of Medina, a water in Medina, a place at Khaybar. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.350} He then reports from Ibn Ishaq, in the accounts of the raiding parties, that the Prophet sent Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas with eight men of the Muhajirun, in the first year or, some said, the second; he reached al-Kharrar in the land of the Hijaz and came back having met no harm. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.350} The next entry, al-Kharrara, is a place near al-Sayluhin in the region of Kufa and is a different one. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.350} In his entry on Thaniyyat al-Marra he gives the Hijra tradition in which the guide took the Prophet and Abu Bakr by Amaj, then al-Kharrar, then Thaniyyat al-Marra, then Laqfa. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.85}

## The museum's identification
The record, from al-Mubarakfuri, has a place near al-Juhfa reached by the expedition of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in Dhu al-Qa'da of the first year AH, where they did not find the caravan. Yaqut agrees that it is near al-Juhfa, but this is one of four views he lists, and he agrees on Sa'd's expedition from Ibn Ishaq and on the first or second year; his wording is that the party came back having met no harm, and he has nothing on a caravan or on the month, which come from the record alone. The record's point (22.85, 39.25) is marked approximate, 18.3 km from the museum's al-Juhfa point and 169.5 km from Mecca; Yaqut gives no distance. He also places al-Kharrar on the Hijra route between Amaj and Thaniyyat al-Marra, which the record does not say.
