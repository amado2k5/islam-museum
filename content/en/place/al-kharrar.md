## Overview
al-Kharrar: a place near al-Juhfa. The expedition of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas reached it in Dhu al-Qa'da in the year 1 AH, and they did not find the caravan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

The book does not fix its coordinates.
