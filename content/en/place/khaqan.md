## Overview
Khaqan is a large city in the north, on a great river fertile on both banks. It has trade, crafts and the livestock of the people of those regions. It lies six stages from Nadhkhu, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.521}
