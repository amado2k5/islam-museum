## Overview
Sinop, known in older literature as Sinope, is a city in Turkey. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2280255}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2280255 with the preferred name «Sinope», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is gazetteer no. 2232771. Among the names it records are «Sanub», «Sinub» and «Sınob», «Σινώπη» in Greek and «Sinop» in English and French. It links the place to GeoNames no. 739600, to an Arachne place and to Zenon records. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2280255}
