## Overview
Qal'at Ja'bar is a fortress on the Euphrates in northern Syria. Yaqut identifies it by its position opposite Siffin and by the man who took it and gave it his name. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.390}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says Qal'at Ja'bar is on the Euphrates opposite Siffin, where the battle between Mu'awiya and Ali ibn Abi Talib took place. He says it was first known as Dawsar, until a man of the Banu Numayr called Ja'bar ibn Malik took possession of it and overcame it, and it was named after him. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.390}

Under the entry "Dayr Hafir" Yaqut quotes verses by al-Qaysarani in praise of Ali ibn Malik ibn Salim al-Uqayli, lord of Qal'at Ja'bar, that mention Balis, Dayr Hafir and the Euphrates, which shows that at that time the fortress was the seat of a ruler of the Banu Uqayl. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.504}
