## Overview
Aja and Salma: two mountains in northern Najd. After the [[group/al-azd|Azd]] had moved north, [[group/tayy|Banu Tayy]] set out for them, settled there, and stayed until the two mountains came to be known as the Mountains of Tayy. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.231-235}.

## What is not mentioned here
The Sealed Nectar does not describe the two mountains or fix their location; fixing their coordinates awaits a check against a geographical dictionary.

## For young visitors
Two large mountains in the north of the Arabian Peninsula, where the tribe of Tayy lived; people called them "the Mountains of Tayy".
