## Overview
al-Idrisi reports that the China Sea, together with the sea of al-Sanf that lies beside it, the sea of Darlarwi, the sea of Harkand and the sea of Oman, knows the ebb and flow of the tide. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.92}
