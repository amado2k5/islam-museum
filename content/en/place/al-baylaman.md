## Overview
al-Baylaman: a land in Sind, according to al-Baladhuri. [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] sent an army to it; they did not fight and submitted. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Peace
The people of Sarsanat also made peace with him in the same way. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
