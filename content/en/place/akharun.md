## Overview
Akharun: a place in Transoxiana. al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] set out for it at the start of his governorship. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
The king of Akharun and the king of Shuman had pressed hard on the king of Saghaniyan and raided him. So the king of Saghaniyan handed Qutayba a golden key and gave him his obedience. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
