## Overview
al-Shu'aybiyya: a place in Basra. Its people carried water from the Euphrates to Ali, the Commander of the Faithful, in the time of al-Rashid, on condition that they be his cultivators there and that their share of the division be lightened for them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Accordingly the tithe was imposed on them as alms, and they were divided in the way they agreed to. Its affairs were managed by Nu'ayb ibn Ziyad al-Wasiti. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
