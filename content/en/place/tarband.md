## Overview
Tarband is the city of al-Shash. The king of al-Shash was residing there when the king of al-Sughd wrote to him, and he came to him with a company of his fighters for war against Qutayba. Qutayba struck them and broke them, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
