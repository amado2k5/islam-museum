## Overview
Raqqa is a city on the Euphrates, one of the districts of al-Jazira. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} It is part of [[place/jazira|al-Jazira]]. See [[event/conquest-of-raqqa|The conquest of Raqqa]].

## The conquest
Iyad ibn Ghanm conquered it by treaty after five days of siege, on terms of their persons, property and city. He confirmed the land in their hands in exchange for kharaj. A covenant of security was written for them with conditions: that they not establish a church, and that they not display a bell (naqus), a horn (ba'uth) or a cross. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Rafiqa
Beside Raqqa is al-Rafiqa, which was built in the year 155 AH on the model of the City of Peace, and in which troops from Khurasan were settled. Al-Rashid built its palaces, and Ali ibn Sulayman transferred to it the markets of Raqqa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
