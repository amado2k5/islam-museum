## Overview
Tadlis is a fortified city on a height, with a strong wall, houses and places of recreation. Prices, restaurants and drinking places there are very cheap. It lies four stages from the harbour of al-Dajaj, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.259}
