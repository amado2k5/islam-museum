## Overview
Tadlis is a city on a height, well fortified, with a strong wall, houses and places of recreation. Al-Idrisi says that the cheapness of its fruit, prices, food and drink is not found elsewhere. Sheep and cattle are abundant there and sold for small prices, and goods go out from its land to many regions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 259}

## Distances
It is twenty-four miles from Marsa al-Dajaj, and twelve miles from Tarf Bani Jinad, a headland that runs out into the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 259} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 273}

## In al-Idrisi's lists
Al-Idrisi counts Tadlis in the Middle Maghrib together with Tenes, Barshk, the Jazair of Bani Mazghanna, Bijaya, Jijel, Miliana, al-Qal'a, al-Msila and others. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 222}
