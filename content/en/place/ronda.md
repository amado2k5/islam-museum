## Overview
Ronda is one of the fortified cities west of Malaga, in [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. Al-Maqqari mentions that the lord of Castile besieged it in Jumada I of the year 890 AH, when its people had gone out to support Dhakwan. He demolished its walls, and its people came out under safe conduct. All that land obeyed him, and nothing remained west of [[place/malaga|Malaga]] except whoever had entered his obedience. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.520}
