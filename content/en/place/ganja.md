## Overview
Yaqut describes Kanja as a great city and the capital of the land of Arran; men of letters called it Janza. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.482}

## Yaqut's entry
Yaqut states that Kanja was the chief city of Arran and that the literati named it Janza, with j, n and z. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.482} He separates it from another Kanja in the region of Lurestan, between Khuzistan and Isfahan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.482}

The entry is brief and gives no history of the city. The identification with the modern city in Azerbaijan is the record's own, not Yaqut's.
