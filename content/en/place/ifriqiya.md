## Overview
Ifriqiya is a region of the Maghrib between Tripoli and Tangier. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri reports from Ibn al-Kalbi that Ifriqis ibn Qays ibn Sayfi al-Himyari overcame it in the days of ignorance, and it was named after him. He killed its king Jurjir, and said to the Berbers: "How many of you are there in barbarism!" So they were named Berbers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh raided it in the caliphate of Uthman, and killed its patrician at Subaytila, and its notables made peace with him for three hundred qintars of gold. Then Uqba ibn Nafi conquered it and laid out its city, al-Qayrawan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri quotes Ibn al-Kalbi that Ifriqis ibn Qays ibn Sayfi al-Himyari ruled it in the Jahiliyya and it was named for him; he killed its king Jarjir, and told the Berbers 'how much you babble' — whence they were called Barabira.
