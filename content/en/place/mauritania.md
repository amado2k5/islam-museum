## Summary
A country in which, according to The Oxford Dictionary of Islam, virtually the whole population is Sunni Muslim. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1471}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam says that in the eighth century merchants and traders brought Islam into West Africa, and that nearly the whole of Mauritania is Sunni Muslim, its dominant Sufi brotherhoods being the Tijani and the Qadiri. Once the country gained independence from France in 1960, its founders encouraged one shared Islamic culture in place of ethnic notions of national identity. Internal tensions rose, the Dictionary adds, as the Sahara war, a drought that worsened for more than twenty years and the urban growth that followed all took their toll. It names rising foreign debt as the most pressing domestic worry and says that ethnic or racial tension lies behind most threats to internal security. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1471}
