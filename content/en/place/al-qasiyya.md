## Overview
al-Qasiyya: a place in [[place/basra|Basra]] whose water dried up. al-Baladhuri reports that al-Qasim ibn Sulayman, a client of Ziyad, brought it out by a forged letter that he claimed came from Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
