## Overview
Florence is a civilised town with markets, craftsmen and wealth. It lies seventy miles from Lucca and fifteen miles from Sanqalilia, on Mount Anwat, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.754}
