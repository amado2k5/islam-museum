## Overview
A port on the Red Sea in the Hijaz. It has been the main gateway for pilgrims to [[place/mecca|Mecca]] since the time of the caliph [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The coordinates are from the GeoNames gazetteer, to the precision of the site. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2751578, typed as populated place, with the preferred name «Jeddah» under «Saudi-Arabien» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including جدة, Dschidda. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2751578}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Jeddah as a town on the coast of the Red Sea and the port of Mecca. He gives the distance to Mecca as three nights according to al-Zamakhshari, and as a day and a night according to al-Hazimi. He places it in the second climate, at a longitude of sixty-four degrees and thirty minutes and a latitude of twenty-one degrees and forty-five minutes. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.114}

He also records a view from Abu al-Mundhir about the name: that Jadda son of Hazm son of Rayyan was born there, and the town was named after the place. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.114}
