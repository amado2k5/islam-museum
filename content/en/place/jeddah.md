## Overview
A port on the Red Sea in the Hijaz. It has been the main gateway for pilgrims to [[place/mecca|Mecca]] since the time of the caliph [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

The coordinates are from the GeoNames gazetteer, to the precision of the site. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2751578, typed as populated place, with the preferred name «Jeddah» under «Saudi-Arabien» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including جدة, Dschidda. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2751578}
