## Overview
Samangan is a town of Tukharistan, two days from Khulm and two stages west of Taliqan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
It is a fine, populous town, full of merchants, respectable people and notables, and it has an earthen wall. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
Andarabah lies five days from Samangan. It is a town at the foot of a mountain, where silver from the mines of Jarbaya and Banjhir is gathered. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
Baghlan is two stages from Samangan, and six stages from Balkh. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 485} Samangan is named among the towns of Tukharistan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 483}
In the assessment of Khurasan's revenues made on Abdallah ibn Tahir for the years 211 and 212 AH, Samangan's kharaj is given as 12,600 dirhams. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p. 42} {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p. 45}
Andarabah has two rivers, the Andarab and the Kasan, and orchards, pleasure grounds, many trees and vineyards. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 484}
