## Overview
Thila is a fort of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] with food stores around it; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went to it after turning back from the water of [[place/madhud-najran|Madhud]] and ordered the army to plunder it and to pull down and burn the fort. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.173} The editor's footnote refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab p. 107, which was not read here, so the place carries no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-spares-the-routed-and-burns-the-stores-at-thila-286]].
