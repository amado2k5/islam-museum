## Overview
Tunkat is a large city, the chief town of Iyalaq, according to al-Idrisi. It has two fortified walls, several gates and markets, and water runs through its lanes and waters its districts. It is less than half the size of Bankath, and nine miles separate it from Bankath. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.704}
