## Overview
Orissin Island is a large island on the coast of India. Al-Idrisi mentions that elephants are hunted there and their tusks are exported. He also mentions that it has iron ore, and that rhubarb (rawand) grows in its mountains. He describes a poisonous tree there called al-Shahkir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 199}
