## Overview
Daramarsha: a round, sandy island with many bases and villages, and sheltered, populated harbours. al-Idrisi reports that its first part, from the river mouth to the city of al-Sayla on the left of whoever enters, is twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.949}
