## Overview
Balaq: a city of the towns of Nubia between two arms of the Nile, according to al-Idrisi. The merchants of Nubia, Abyssinia and the land of Egypt gather there at the time of peace and truce. It is seven days by land from Balaq to the mountain of the cataracts. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.38-39}
