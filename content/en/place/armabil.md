## Overview
Armabil: a city in Makran, according to al-Baladhuri. Muhammad ibn al-Qasim conquered it after Qanrabur, and Muhammad ibn Harun al-Namari was buried near it, at Qanbal. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
