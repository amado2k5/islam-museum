## Summary
Arma'il, a city in Makran according to al-Baladhuri, was taken by Muhammad ibn al-Qasim after Qanirbur; Muhammad ibn Harun died near it and was buried at Qanbal. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 613}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Muhammad ibn al-Qasim marched into Makran and stayed there some days, then came to Qanirbur and took it, and then came to Arma'il and took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 613} Muhammad ibn Harun ibn Dhira' had met Muhammad ibn al-Qasim, joined him and marched with him; he died near Arma'il and was buried at Qanbal. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 613} Muhammad ibn al-Qasim then set out from Arma'il with Jahm ibn Zahr al-Ju'fi and reached al-Daybul on a Friday, where ships carrying the men, arms and equipment met him; he dug a trench on reaching al-Daybul and spears were planted along it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 613} In his account Arma'il is thus a station on the road of conquest through Makran, between Qanirbur and al-Daybul.
