## Overview
Mount Tantana is a mountain around the land of Wadan and Azqar. At its foot are springs and pastures, and in it is good iron ore, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.113} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.116}
