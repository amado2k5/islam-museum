## Overview
Batlyus: a great city on a level plain, with a suburb larger than the city itself, and on the river Yana, which sinks under the earth before it reaches the fortress of Martala. It lies one day from Merida. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.545}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut calls Batlyus (Badajoz) a large city in al-Andalus, in the district of Merida, on the river Anah west of Cordoba, with a wide district that he describes in its place. Many people were named after it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.447}

Among them, Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Sayyid al-Batlyusi, a grammarian, lexicographer and poet with many books, died in year 521. Abu al-Walid Hisham ibn Yahya ibn Hajjaj al-Batlyusi heard in Cordoba, travelled to the East, heard in Mecca, Syria, Egypt and Ifriqiya, came back to al-Andalus, and was tried in his own town after a denunciation. He then lived in Cordoba and taught there a great deal; Ibn al-Faradi says he heard from him both before and after the trial. Hisham died in Shawwal 385. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.447}
