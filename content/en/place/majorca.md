## Summary
Mayurqa (Majorca) is an island east of the island of Yabisa (Ibiza) with one channel between them; it contains a large city with a ruler and a garrison of men, numbers, weapons and wealth. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}

## Details
Al-Idrisi relates that the island of Yabisa is a fair island rich in vines and grapes, containing a handsome little civilized city, and the nearest point of al-Andalus to it is the city of Daniya, one channel away {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}. East of Yabisa lies the island of Mayurqa, one channel from it, and in it is a large city with a ruler and a garrison possessing men, numbers, weapons and wealth {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}. East of it again is the island of Manurqa (Minorca), facing the city of Barcelona, with one channel between them {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}. From Manurqa to the island of Sardinia are four channels {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}. The city of Daniya faces the city of Tinnis across the water, three channels apart, while Barcelona faces Bejaia on the middle western crossing, four channels across the open sea, each channel being a hundred miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.582}.
