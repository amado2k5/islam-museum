## Overview
Adranto (Otranto): an ancient town with many monuments and much good produce. Al-Idrisi says that the sea surrounds its wall on several sides, that a valley comes down to it from the north, and that it lies about twenty miles from Adrasht. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.632} See also [[place/adrasht|Adrasht]].
