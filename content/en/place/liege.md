## Overview
Liège is a fine town on an island in the river, which surrounds it on every side. It has enough markets and crafts, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.868}

Al-Idrisi says its distance from Mass is a hundred miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.868}
