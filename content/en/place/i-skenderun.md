## Overview
Yaqut al-Hamawi, quoting Ahmad ibn al-Tayyib, describes Iskandaruna as a city east of Antioch on the coast of the Syrian sea, four farsakhs from Baghras and eight from Antioch. He adds that he found in some histories of Syria that an Iskandaruna lies between Acre and Tyre. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut p.238}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2118559 with the preferred name «Iskenderun», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Hatay (Provinz)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Alexandria ad Issum» (Latin), «İskenderun» (German) and «Aleksandretta» (Polish). It links the place to Pleiades no. 658371 and GeoNames no. 311111. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2118559}
