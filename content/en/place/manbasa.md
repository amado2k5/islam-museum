## Overview
Manbasa is a small city in the land of the Zanj, on the sea, and its people work in iron extraction. According to al-Idrisi it lies two days' journey from Malanda. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.59}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the vowels of the name (Manbasa) and calls it a large city in the land of the Zanj, to which ships put in. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.207}
