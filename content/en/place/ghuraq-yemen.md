## Overview
Ghuraq is the place to which [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] went from [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], some days of Safar 286 AH having passed, when he resolved not to fight al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} The name is printed with the vowels "Ghuraq"; the editor adds no footnote on the place, so no location or coordinates are given. See [[event/al-daam-gives-up-the-fight-and-leaves-khiwan-for-ghuraq-286]].

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalises the name with a damma on the first letter and a fatha on the second, on the pattern of Zufar, and says it seems to be a form turned from {ghariq}, drowning, or possibly from the horse's outstripping the others after mixing with them. He then says that Ghuraq is a city in Yemen belonging to Hamdan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.195}
