## Overview
Rawand is a town in the district of [[place/isbahan|Isfahan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The graves of the boon companions
The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that there are there the graves of three boon companions (nudama), who were from the people of Kufa and among those in al-Hajjaj's army sent to al-Daylam. They used to drink together, and they died one after another, and each was buried beside his companion. One of them recited a verse in which he says that he remained alone in Rawand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
