## Overview
A water in Najd, at which [[person/zayd-al-khayl|Zayd al-Khayl]] stopped on his way back from Medina. He caught a fever there and died. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## In poetry
Zayd mentioned it in two lines when he felt death coming: his people set out eastward at dawn, and he is left alone in a house at Farda in Najd. The editors explain that the word "munjad" means "in Najd". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vowels the name with a fatha on the first letter, a sukun on the r and an undotted d, and says it is the feminine of fard, meaning what is single; he notes that Nasr transmitted it with a q and a fatha on the r. He says it is the name of a mountain in the desert, so called because it stands apart from other mountains, and that al-Farda is also a water at al-Thalbut belonging to Banu Nu'ama; he cites two verses of al-Ra'i al-Numayri that mention it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.248}

From Nasr he reports that Farda is a mountain in the lands of Tayyi called Fardat al-Shumus, and that another account makes it a water of Jarm in Tayyi country, where the tomb of Zayd al-Khayl lies. He relays from Abu Ubayda that when Zayd al-Khayl and his companions were returning from the Messenger, Zayd said he had left marks on this branch of Qays, felt sure they would fight him if he passed, and gave God a pledge never to fight a Muslim. They therefore turned aside from their land and took a stretch of the Tayyi road until they reached Farda, a water of Jarm; fever seized him for three days and he died. Yaqut gives verses he said before dying, among them the line that he is left in a house at Farda. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.248}

Yaqut adds that this is what a group of language scholars report, but that he found the word, in Ibn al-Furat's hand, written in other passages with a q, Qarda. He reports that al-Waqidi places Dhu al-Qarada in Najd; that Ibn Ishaq has the raid of Zayd ibn Haritha, which took a Quraysh caravan with Abu Sufyan ibn Harb in it, at al-Farda, a water of Najd, vowelled by Ibn al-Furat with a fatha on f and a kasra on r; that others than Ibn Ishaq call it a place between Medina and Syria; and that Musa ibn Uqba has Zayd's raid at Thaniyyat al-Qarada, with a q as Abu Nu'aym vowelled it. He ends by saying this chapter needs scrutiny and nothing in it has been established so far. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.249}
