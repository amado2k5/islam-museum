## Overview
The narrator describes it as a mountain overlooking [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. Al-Hadi's opponents came down to it and camped there in Sha'ban 288 AH, and [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] fought them at its foot on Sunday, fourteen days of Sha'ban gone ([[event/battle-of-nuqm-shaban-288|the battle of Nuqm]]), then again in Ramadan ([[event/second-fight-at-nuqm-in-ramadan-288]]) and in Dhu al-Qa'da ([[event/fight-at-nuqm-in-dhu-al-qada-and-the-hakamis-commander-is-killed-288]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. The narrator mentions the village below it and its gates (the Darb al-Qutay', the Darb al-Jabbana, the Darb Ibn Zamarid) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}.

## Detail
The book gives no coordinates or distance, and the museum does not guess its position.
