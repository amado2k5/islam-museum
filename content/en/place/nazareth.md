## Overview
Nazareth is a town in the north of Israel, recorded by Pleiades as a settlement of the ancient world. The first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan hold no entry for it. {cite:pleiades 678299}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Nazareth» (Pleiades no. 678299), classed as a settlement, with its recorded location dated from 330 BCE to 640 CE. It records one name, «Nazareth», attested in the Hellenistic and Republican, Roman and Late Antique periods. Its description cites the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 69, grid B4. {cite:pleiades 678299}
