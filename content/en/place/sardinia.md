## Overview
Sardinia is a large island with many mountains and minerals, according to al-Idrisi. It has cities, among them a populous city in its south, and silver mines. He lists it between the island of Minorca and the island of Qarshaqa (Corsica). {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.584}
