## Overview
Sardinia is a large island, mountainous and short of water, two hundred and eighty miles long and a hundred and eighty miles wide from west to east. The island has good silver mines, and silver goes out from it to many lands of the Rum. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 584}

## Towns and people
It has three towns: al-Qaytana, on the southern side, a spacious and populous city; Qalmara, the headland for the crossing to the island of Corsica; and Qashiliya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 584} Its people are by origin African Rum who have become barbarised and wild, among the peoples of the Rum, brave and careful men who never put down their weapons. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 584}

## Crossings and neighbours
From the island of Minorca to Sardinia is four crossings east, and between Sardinia and Corsica the crossing is twenty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 584} Corsica is a fertile and well-peopled island a hundred and fifty miles long and twenty-seven wide, whose people travel more than any other of the Rum. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 584}
