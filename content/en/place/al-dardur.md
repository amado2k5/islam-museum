## Overview
al-Dardur: a place in the sea where the water turns constantly and never stops. al-Idrisi reports that any ship that falls into it keeps turning until it is destroyed. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.164}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
