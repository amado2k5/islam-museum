## Overview
A mountain that begins in the land of Egypt and extends into the desert as far as the area near [[place/aswan|Aswan]]. It has minerals such as ochre, lime and gold. Al-Idrisi mentions reports of treasures and priests attributed to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.132-133}
