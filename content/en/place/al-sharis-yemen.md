## Overview
"al-Sharis" is a place where al-Hadi halted with his army on his way out to Tihama, and where many men of Hujur came to him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}.

## Detail
The book gives no coordinates for this place and the editor adds no note on it, so the museum guesses no position; the question is recorded in the batch ledger. See [[event/al-hadi-goes-out-to-tihama-and-camps-at-al-sharis-and-al-uyayn-292|al-Hadi's going out to Tihama]].
