## Summary
Antaliya was a pair of cities on the Anatolian coast: Antaliya the Burned and New Antaliya standing on a high mountain, two days apart. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.647}

## Details
In the coastal itineraries of the land of Rum al-Idrisi states that from Awlash to Seluqiyya is two days, from Seluqiyya to Antaliya the Burned four miles, and to New Antaliya, between the two cities being two days, the new one set on a high mountain, and from it to a harbour {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.647}. He thus distinguishes the old, ruined Antaliya from the newer city standing on the commanding hill, separated by a two days' journey along that shore.
