## Overview
Wadi Ash is a great city in the districts of Granada, surrounded by gardens and rivers. Al-Maqqari says its people were distinguished in letters and in love of poetry. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.185}

## The fortress and the apples
Among its districts is the fortress of Jaliana, which is large and rivals cities. Its Jaliana apples are famous. Twelve miles lie between the fortress and Wadi Ash. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.185}

## The call of the sultan's sons
Al-Maqqari reports that the call of the sultan's two sons, Abu al-Hasan's sons Abu Abd Allah Muhammad and Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf, was established there when they fled from their father. Almeria, Baza and Granada gave them their allegiance. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.518}
