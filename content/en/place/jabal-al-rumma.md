## Overview
The Mount of the Archers (Mount Ayn) is a small mountain on the south bank of the Qanat valley, south-east of the Muslim camp at [[place/uhud|Uhud]], about a hundred and fifty metres from the army's headquarters. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}

## Its part in the battle
The Prophet appointed Abd Allah ibn Jubayr commander of fifty archers on the mountain, to protect the left flank of the Muslims from the polytheist cavalry. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255} The site is linked with two events: [[event/uhud-deployment|the deployment of the army at Uhud]] and [[event/uhud-archers-mistake|the archers' mistake]].

## The name
In the Prophet's time the mountain was known as Mount Ayn, and it was later called the Mount of the Archers. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}
