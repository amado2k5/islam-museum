## Overview
Lanbudushah is an island with a safe harbour that shelters from every wind, and to which the fleet is brought. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.588}

The text gives its distance from the nearest shore, but the figure recorded here is not established by the data; an additional source is needed.
