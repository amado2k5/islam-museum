## Overview
Al-Abbasiyya: a city that Umar ibn Hafs al-Ataki built at the farthest edge of the land of the Berbers and named al-Abbasiyya, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Its destruction
Abu Hatim al-Ibadi fought him, and Umar was martyred, so the city he had built was torn down. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
