## Summary
Yaqut describes two places called Qaysariyya. The first is on the coast of the Syrian sea within the districts of Palestine, three days from Tiberias; it was once one of the great cities, but in Yaqut's time it was more like a set of villages. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.421}

The second is "a large and great city in the land of Rum," the seat of the Seljuk rulers of Rum. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.421} Yaqut records a site there that people say was the prison of Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya, and a bath that is said to have been built by the sage Balinas for the emperor. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.421} Quoting a report in the Damascus history, he states that Mu'awiya besieged it for seven years less a few months. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.421}
