## Overview
Nahr Tiri is a populous city with houses and markets, abounding in good things. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies one stage from [[place/al-ahwaz|al-Ahwaz]], and that from it to Bayan is two stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.398}
