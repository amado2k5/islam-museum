## Overview
A place in the districts of [[place/kabul|Kabul]]. Al-Baladhuri reports that Yazid ibn Ziyad fought the people of Kabul there when they broke their word and took Abu Ubayda ibn Ziyad captive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The account says that Abu Ubayda was killed with many of those with him, and the rest of the people were routed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
