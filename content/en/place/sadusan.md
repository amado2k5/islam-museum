## Overview
Sadusan is a city in Sind. Al-Baladhuri mentions that its people asked Muhammad ibn Mus'ab al-Thaqafi for safe conduct, and that the Samaniyya were an intermediary between them. He granted them safe conduct, imposed a tax on them and took a pledge, and withdrew with four thousand of the Zutt to Muhammad ibn al-Qasim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
