## Overview
The plains of Muqan in Azerbaijan, on the Aras river. Al-Baladhuri mentions that [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]], governor of Kufa for Uthman, raided it in the course of his northern campaigns. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
