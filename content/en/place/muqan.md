## Summary
Muqan is the steppe of Adharbayjan on the river Aras. Al-Baladhuri records that [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn 'Uqba]], 'Uthman's governor of Kufa, campaigned against Daylam, Adharbayjan, Gilan, Muqan, al-Bir and al-Taylasan before returning. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 450}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Walid ibn 'Uqba, 'Uthman ibn 'Affan's governor over Kufa, raided Daylam in the direction of Qazwin, then Adharbayjan, Gilan, Muqan, al-Bir and al-Taylasan, and then withdrew {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 450}. After him Sa'id ibn al-'As ibn Sa'id ibn al-'As ibn Umayya took office; he too raided Daylam and founded Qazwin as a garrison town, which became the frontier post of the Kufans with their buildings in it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 450}. He also records that Sa'id ibn al-'As campaigned against the people of Adharbayjan and fell upon the people of Muqan and Gilan; a host of Armenians and Adharbayjanis gathered against him near Armenia, and he sent Jarir ibn 'Abdallah al-Bajali against them, who routed them, captured their leader and crucified him on the fortress of Bajrawan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 459}. In Marwan ibn Muhammad's time the governor of Armenia and Adharbayjan camped near al-Maraghah on his return from the raid on Muqan and Gilan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 461}.
