## Summary
A place near Medina where the rebels from Egypt and Iraq encamped about a month and a half before the assassination of 'Uthman, and where 'Uthman reached a settlement with them on five matters after sending 'Ali to them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 163}.

## The Event at the Site
The rebels encamped at Dhu al-Marwah, so 'Uthman sent 'Ali and another man to them; 'Ali presented the Book of God to them and they accepted, and 'Uthman then concluded a settlement with them on five matters {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 163-164}. See the entry "Sulh 'Uthman ma'a Wufud al-Amsar fi Dhi al-Marwah" ('Uthman's Settlement with the Provincial Delegations at Dhu al-Marwah).
