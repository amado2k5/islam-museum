## Summary
Al-Yahudiyya was a town in the land of [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]]. 'Abdallah ibn Budayl ibn Warqa' of al-Ahnaf ibn Qays's force was sent against it, and its people made peace on the same terms as the peace of Jayy, as al-Baladhuri reports in the conquest of Isfahan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 438}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Musa al-Ash'ari and 'Abdallah ibn Budayl together conquered al-Yahudiyya as described — al-Ahnaf ibn Qays leading Abu Musa's vanguard — after which Ibn Budayl took Jayy and the two ranged through the land of Isfahan and mastered it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 438}. In another report he adds that Abu Musa conquered Qumm and Qashan while 'Abdallah ibn Budayl conquered Jayy and al-Yahudiyya {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 439}. The fall of al-Yahudiyya and Jayy belonged to the conquests of Isfahan after [[event/battle-of-nahawand|Nahavand]], in the sweep 'Umar authorised for the men of Kufa and Basra {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 438}. The people of Isfahan also mention a shrine (mashhad) there belonging to 'Uthman ibn Abi al-'As al-Thaqafi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 439}.
