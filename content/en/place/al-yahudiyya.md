## Overview
al-Yahudiyya: a city in Isfahan. al-Baladhuri reports that Abd Allah ibn Badil sent al-Ahnaf ibn Qays to it, and its people made peace with him on the same terms as the peace of Jay. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
