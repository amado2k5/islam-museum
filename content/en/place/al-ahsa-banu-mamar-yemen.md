## Overview
Al-Ahsa is a place of [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]] where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went on a Saturday after his stay at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.134} because of a group of them, "a dozen or more men", who made disorder and sat on the roads; he then camped at [[place/hawth-yemen|Hawth]] and sent messengers to them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.134} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.135} The book's index lists "al-Ahsa" at p. 134 and the book gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-and-the-wrongdoers-of-banu-mamar-at-al-ahsa-and-the-first-days-at-hawth-286]].
