## Summary
Basmad is a town of the land of Sind which al-Idrisi places three stages from the city of al-Rur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from al-Rur to Basmad is three stages, and likewise from al-Rur to Atri four stages {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.171}. Earlier he had counted it among the cities of Sind together with Baniya, al-Sandur, Manjabri and al-Multan, saying that all these cities belong to and are reckoned within Sind {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}. He describes al-Rur, from which these stages set out, as a fine, populous and wealthy city with busy markets and brisk trade, fortified with two walls, the river passing it on the west, its people in ease and plenty, rivalling al-Multan in size {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}. Basmad is thus one of the dependencies of al-Rur in the province of Sind.
