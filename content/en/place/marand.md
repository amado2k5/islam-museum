## Overview
Marand is a city in Azerbaijan that was once a small village, settled by Halbas, father of al-Bu'ayth. Then al-Bu'ayth fortified it, followed by his son Muhammad ibn al-Bu'ayth, who built palaces there. When he rebelled in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil, Bugha al-Saghir fought him until he prevailed over him, and he demolished its wall and his palace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
