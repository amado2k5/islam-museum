## Overview
Qal'at Rabah is a fortress on the bank of the Yana river. The river comes down from meadows above it, passes the village of Yana, and then reaches the fortress, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 550} He places it in the district of al-Wulja, with Sarta and Fatta. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538}

From Merida to the fortress of Karkuwi is three stages; from Karkuwi to Qal'at Rabah the road runs on the bank of the Yana river. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 550}

From the fortress the river runs on to Arinda, then Merida, then passes Badajoz, and near the fortress of Martila it empties into what al-Idrisi calls the Dark Sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 550}

From Qal'at Rabah to Arliya is two days; Arliya is, in his words, an impregnable fortress, and from there to Toledo is one stage. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 550}

Going north from Qal'at Rabah, Hisn al-Balat is two stages away, and from Hisn al-Balat to Talavera is two days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 550}

On another route, from the fortress of Ghafiq to Jabal Afur is one stage, then Dar al-Baqar one stage, then Qal'at Rabah, which he calls a fine town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 580}
