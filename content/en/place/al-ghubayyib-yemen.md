## Overview
Al-Ghubayyib is a place "called al-Ghubayyib" to which [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] told the people to go by the road in their units after the group's messenger came, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.136} and, from the course of the account, the place where he met [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]] and pardoned them in [[event/al-hadi-pardons-banu-mamar-at-al-ghubayyib-and-returns-to-khiwan-286]]. The editor's note reads: "See Sifat al-Jazira, p. 139." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.136} The book's index lists "al-Ghubayb" at p. 145 and not at p. 136, so it is not asserted that one place is meant. The book gives no coordinates.
