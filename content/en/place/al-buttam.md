## Overview
al-Buttam: a mountainous region beyond the Oxus river. Mukhallad ibn Yazid ibn al-Muhallab conquered it during his father's governorship of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

An additional source is needed for the date of the conquest, since the record does not give a year.
