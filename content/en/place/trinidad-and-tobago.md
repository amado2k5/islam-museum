## Summary
Two-island state in the Caribbean; about eight percent of its people are Muslim. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2400}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
Muslims make up, by the Dictionary's count, eight percent of Trinidad and Tobago's people, largely of East Indian descent, yet they carry weight in politics, business and society: numerous Muslims hold elected office and numerous enterprises are Muslim-owned. The entry recalls 1990, when a small armed Muslim group briefly attempted to topple the elected government. Some eighty-five mosques serve Trinidad while Tobago has merely one or two; the state recognizes several Muslim holidays and funds a yearly Id al-Fitr celebration; and Muslim leaders have begun joining Christian and Hindu clergy in warning against alcoholism, drugs, violent crime and AIDS. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2400}
