## Overview
Dastaba: a city. According to al-Baladhuri, by the order of Umar, he sent to it and to al-Rayy Urwa ibn Zayd with eight thousand men, while Kathir ibn Shihab was the governor over it and al-Rayy on behalf of Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
