## Overview
Old Brahmanabad: an ancient city two farsakhs from the site of al-Mansura. The army of Dahir gathered there and fought [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]], and he conquered it by force. al-Baladhuri reports that eight thousand were killed there, or it is said twenty-six thousand, and that it was in ruins in his time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
