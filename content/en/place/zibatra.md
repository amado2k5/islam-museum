## Overview
Zibatra is an ancient Roman fortress among the frontier posts. Al-Baladhuri relates its history across several periods. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 270} See [[event/sack-of-zibatra|The raid on Zibatra]].

## The succession of conquest, ruin and building
Habib ibn Maslama conquered it together with the ancient fortress of al-Hadath in the days of Umar. The Byzantines ruined it in the days of al-Walid ibn Yazid, and then in the strife of Marwan ibn Muhammad. Al-Mansur built it, and the Byzantines damaged it again. Al-Rashid restored it through Muhammad ibn Ibrahim and garrisoned it. Al-Ma'mun repaired and fortified it after a raid on the herds of its people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 270}

## In the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim
Al-Baladhuri mentions that the Byzantines came out against it in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim, killed the men, took the women captive, and ruined it. That was the reason for al-Mu'tasim's expedition to Amorium. Then it was rebuilt, fortified and garrisoned, and the Byzantines sought to attack it but could not. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 270}
