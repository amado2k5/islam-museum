## Overview
Khuttal is the first region of the districts of the Oxus, beyond the river. It has towns, among them Halawarid, Lawkand and Manak, and the largest of them is Manak, where there is a silver mine, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.487}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records al-Khuttal with damma on the first letter and a doubled, opened second; from al-Bishari he reports a wide kura of many towns, that attaching it to Balkh is a mistake since it lies beyond the Jayhun in Transoxiana, that it is grander than al-Saghaniyan, broader of compass, larger of towns and richer in goods, standing on the marches of Sind; its qasaba is Halbak, and among its towns Banjara'ah, Halaward, Lawakand, Kawand, Tamliyyat, Iskandara and Mank. From al-Istakhri he reports that the first kura on the Jayhun beyond the river is al-Khuttal together with al-Wakhsh, two kuras joined in a single administration between Jaryab and Wakshab. Scholars are named after it, among them Ibad ibn Musa al-Khuttali and his son Ishaq, and Imran ibn al-Hasan al-Khuttali al-Khaffaf who died in the year 400. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.346}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut's Khuttal is the record's Transoxanian region (Khatlan, today's Khatlon province of Tajikistan), on the far side of the Jayhun as he himself states. The neighbouring headwords are other places kept apart: Khatlan, an aggregate district near Samarkand; al-Sam'ani's reading of al-Khuttal as a village on the Khurasan road by al-Daskara, on which Yaqut himself remarks "and there is room for doubt"; Khutan, a town short of Kashgar; and Khutta, one of the towns of Bab al-Abwab.
