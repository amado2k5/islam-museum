## Overview
Khuttal is the first region of the districts of the Oxus, beyond the river. It has towns, among them Halawarid, Lawkand and Manak, and the largest of them is Manak, where there is a silver mine, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.487}
