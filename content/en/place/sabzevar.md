## Overview
Sabzevar is a city in Razavi Khorasan Province, Iran. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733406}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2733406 with the preferred name «Sabzevar», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Chorāsān-e Razavi (Ostan)». Among the names it records are «Sabzawār» and «سبزوار» (Persian) and «Sabzevar» (English). It links the place to GeoNames no. 118063. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2733406}
