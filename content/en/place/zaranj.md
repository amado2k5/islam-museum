## Overview
Zaranj is the great city of Sijistan according to al-Baladhuri's report. It is mentioned in two successive sieges. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege of al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad
Al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad besieged it, and its marzban Abruwiz made peace with him for a thousand servants, with each servant a golden cup (jam). Al-Baladhuri mentions that al-Rabi' had him sit on the corpse of a slain man. Al-Rabi's governorship there lasted two and a half years, and he took forty thousand heads as captives. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege of Ibn Samura
Then Ibn Samura besieged its marzban on their festival day, and he made peace with him for two million dirhams and two thousand servants. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
