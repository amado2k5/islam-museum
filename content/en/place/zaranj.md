## Overview
Zaranj was the chief city of Sijistan. Its conquest history follows the governors who held it under Uthman ibn Affan and then under Ali ibn Abi Talib. Some took money and captives from it, and once its people drove out an appointed deputy and shut the gates against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.555}

## Details
Al-Rabi' governed Zaranj for two years and a half, and in that office he took forty thousand captives. Ibn Amir then appointed Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura to Sijistan. He came to Zaranj and besieged its marzban in his palace on one of their feast days, and the marzban agreed to pay two million dirhams and two thousand servants. Ibn Amir had left a man of the Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b as deputy, but the people drove him out and shut the gates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.555}

After the Battle of the Camel, Hasaka ibn Attab and Imran ibn al-Fasil went out with bands of Arab fighters. They camped at Zaliq and then reached Zaranj. Its marzban feared them, made peace, and they entered the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.556}
