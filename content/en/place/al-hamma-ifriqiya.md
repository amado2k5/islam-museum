## Overview
al-Hamma: a place with waters that do not taste good, but are fit for drinking, and whose people are content with them. Settlements adjoin it to the south and east. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}

This is al-Idrisi's description alone, and it needs an additional source.
