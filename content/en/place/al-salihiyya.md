## Summary
Al-Salihiyya, a quarter of Baghdad which, in al-Baladhuri, was named for Salih ibn al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 415}

## Details
Shaykhs of the people of Baghdad told al-Baladhuri that al-Salihiyya of Baghdad was named for Salih ibn al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 415} It is mentioned among the Baghdad quarters known by men's names: al-Harbiyya was named for Harb ibn Abdallah al-Balkhi, who commanded the police of Ja'far ibn Abi Ja'far at Mosul; al-Zuhayriyya, known by Bab al-Tibn, was named for Zuhayr ibn Muhammad of the people of Abiward; and Isa Abadh was named for Isa ibn al-Mahdi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 415} Al-Salihiyya is thus a district of Abbasid Baghdad that carried the name of Salih ibn al-Mansur, just as the neighbouring quarters carried the names of Abbasid princes and commanders. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 415}
