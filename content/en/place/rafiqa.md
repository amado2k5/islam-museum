## Overview
Al-Rafiqa is a city of al-Abbas beside [[place/raqqa|Raqqa]]. It had no ancient trace. Al-Mansur built it in the year 155 AH on the model of his city in Baghdad, and stationed in it troops from the people of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.247}

## Building after al-Mansur
The works there were carried out through al-Mahdi while he was heir apparent. Then al-Rashid built its palaces and transferred the markets of Raqqa to it. See [[event/founding-of-rafiqa|The founding of al-Rafiqa]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.247}
