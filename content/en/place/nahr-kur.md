## Overview
The River Kur comes out of Kuruwan at the edge of the Kurds, passes through the valley of Buwan, waters the districts of Fars, and empties into the lake known as Bakhtakan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.422}
