## Overview
Stuttgart is the capital of the German federal state of Baden-Württemberg, on the Neckar. It does not appear in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer

Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Stuttgart (Regierungsbezirk)».
