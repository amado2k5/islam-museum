## Overview
Ain Zara is a town in Libya, and this page keeps to what Pleiades records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Ain Zara» (Pleiades no. 344291), classed as a cemetery, with activity attested from 300 CE to 640 CE, the late antique period. {cite:pleiades 344291} It describes the place only as an ancient place cited at map 35, grid F2 of the Barrington Atlas. {cite:pleiades 344291}
