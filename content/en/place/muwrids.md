## Summary
Muwrids is a well-fortified, populous city on the edge of the lands of India, containing merchants and armies guarding the frontier of Kabul; it lies three stages from Dada. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.195}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from Muwrids to Dada are three stages; the city of Muwrids is strong of fortress and populous of inhabitants, containing merchants and armies that guard the frontier of Kabul {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.195}. It stands at the foot of a great mountain hard to ascend, on which grow many spears of cane and bamboo {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.195}. From Muwrids to the city of Qandahar are eight stages; it lies on the same mountain already mentioned, and the road between them follows its tail {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.195}. Qandahar is a city of great compass and many people, who are distinguished from others by their beards, letting them grow until they reach the knees or beyond; their dress is that of the Turks, and they have in their town and land wheat, rice, grains, sheep and cattle {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.195}.
