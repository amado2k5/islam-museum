## Overview
Mayraf is a large city on the sea coast, with wealthy merchants. Al-Idrisi mentions that its people are fond of travel and wandering, and that some of them roam for a year or two without returning to their families. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.410}
