## Overview
Labla is a fine, ancient town of middling size, which a river reaches from the direction of the mountain and which is crossed by a bridge. It has trade and many benefits, and its people drink from springs in it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.541}
