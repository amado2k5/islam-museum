## Overview
Daquqa: a place in the districts of Shahrazur. According to al-Baladhuri, Hashim ibn Utba and al-Ash'ath ibn Qays fled to al-Radhanat and came to Daquqa and Khanijar, prevailed over what was there, and conquered the whole district of Bajarma. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
