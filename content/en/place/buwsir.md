## Overview
Buwsir: a small city on the north bank of the Nile, according to al-Idrisi. It is told that most of the sorcerers of Pharaoh were from it, and the author reports that a remnant of students of magic still remains in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}
