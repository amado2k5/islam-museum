## Summary
Awjila is a town in the Sudan country which al-Idrisi places ten days' journey west of Sitriya, near the red mountain of Barim. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Sitriya sets out whoever wishes to enter the land of Kawar and travel the Sudan country, and that from Sitriya westward to Awjila is ten days, and that in that region is the red mountain of Barim from which, it is said, the two obelisks of Alexandria were hewn {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}. In the same passage he notes that the mountain of Qalmari is four days away and contains a mine of good iron {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.124}. Awjila is thus one of the stages of the desert road leading from Sitriya into the Sudan lands, and al-Idrisi made it a marker of that western region with its neighbouring mineral mountains.
