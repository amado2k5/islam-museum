## Summary
Anzalan was a busy anchorage on the far Maghrib coast, the first of the lands of Ghumara, with the fortress of Tiqsas nearby on the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.532}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says from Sabta to Anzalan, an anchorage with buildings, is about fifteen miles, and Anzalan is a populated anchorage, the first of the lands of Ghumara — a country of continuous mountains rich in trees and thickets about three days long {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.532}. Between Sabta and Fes by the Zajjan road is eight days, and to the anchorage of Anzalan; near Anzalan stands the fortress of Tiqsas on the sea, half a day away, an inhabited fort of Ghumara though its people wage constant war with the Ghumara {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.532}. From Tiqsas to Qasr Tazka is fifteen miles and it too has an anchorage {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.532}.
