## Overview
Stara Zagora is a town in Bulgaria which the iDAI.gazetteer records as a populated place and an archaeological site, known in antiquity as Augusta Traiana and also as Beroe. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2066662}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2066662 with the preferred name «Stara Zagora», classed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Stara Zagora (Obština)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are the ancient names «Augusta Traiana» and «Beroe» in Latin, «Βέρεια» and «Ειρηνούπολις» in Greek and «Железник» in Bulgarian, and «Стара Загора» in Bulgarian and Serbian. It links the place to GeoNames no. 726848 and to a German National Library authority record (4349944-2). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2066662}
