## Overview
Pamplona: a strong city with markets, merchants, numbers and wealth. It is the seat of the king of the Franks on the border of the Franks, according to al-Idrisi. It has a suburb, and a strong wall surrounds it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.732}
