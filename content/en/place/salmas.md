## Overview
Salmas is a city at a distance from Lake Kabudhan, with many boats crossing the lake, according to al-Idrisi. It is surrounded by cities and pastures, and between it and [[place/tabriz|Tabriz]] and Dakhraqan there is a distance. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.682}
