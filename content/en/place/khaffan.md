## Overview
Khaffan is a place that Al-Baladhuri mentions in "Futuh al-Buldan". Al-Muthanna ibn Haritha stopped in it after the covenant of Abu Bakr, and called his people to Islam, and they became Muslims. Then Khalid ibn al-Walid stopped in it on his march to [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.337} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut gives Khaffan, with a doubled fa, as a place near Kufa that pilgrims sometimes take, and a lion haunt; one report puts it above al-Qadisiyya. He cites Abu Ubayda al-Sakuni that Khaffan lies beyond al-Nusukh, two or three miles away, at a spring with a village of the descendants of Isa ibn Musa al-Hashimi that is also called Khaffan, the two being villages of the Sawad on the edge of the Hijaz side; the route from there to Wasit in the Taff runs by Najran, Abdiniya, Janbala, the bridges of Banu Dara and Tall Fakhkhar. He cites al-Sukkari that Khaffan and Khafiyya are two thickets near the mosque of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in Kufa, with a verse naming the lair of Khafiyya, which is not quoted here. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.379}
