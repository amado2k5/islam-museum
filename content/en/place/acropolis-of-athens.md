## Overview
The Acropolis of Athens is the ancient citadel above the city of Athens in Greece. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Acropolis of Athens» (Pleiades no. 638356144), classed as an acropolis and an architectural complex, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. It records two names: «Akropolis» from 1900 to 1999 CE and «Acropolis of Athens» from 1700 to 2100 CE. Its description says only that this is the acropolis of the ancient city of Athens in Greece. {cite:pleiades 638356144}
