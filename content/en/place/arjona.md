## Overview
Yaqut describes Arjuna as a town in the district of Jayyan in al-Andalus. Shu'ayb ibn Suhayl ibn Shu'ayb al-Arjuni, known as Abu Muhammad, came from it; he took up hadith and opinion, travelled east, and met a number of leading scholars. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.144}

## Yaqut's entry
Yaqut states that Arjuna is a town in the district of Jayyan in al-Andalus, and says that Shu'ayb was a man of understanding in jurisprudence and legal opinion. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.144}

The entry is short and this page rests on those two reports. The identification with the modern town in the Jaen province is the record's own, not Yaqut's.
