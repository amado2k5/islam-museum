## Overview
A place in the district of al-Ta'if. According to Ibn Ishaq, Sa'id ibn al-As, Abu Uhayha, died there from his property. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Its location has not been fixed historically with certainty, so no coordinates are given here.
