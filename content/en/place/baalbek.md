## Overview
Baalbek: a city among the districts of Damascus, in the province of Sanir. al-Mas'udi reports that in it is the house of Ba'l, the idol mentioned in the Qur'an. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554}

## The temple
al-Mas'udi reports that the Greeks chose for this temple a piece of land between Mount Lebanon and Mount Sanir, and made it a place for idols. There are two great buildings, one older than the other. In them are wonderful carvings cut in the stone, together with the height of their structure, the greatness of their stones and the length of their columns. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554}

## In the books of antiquities
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari counted "the playground of Baalbek" among the famous monuments, from the remains of the buildings of the ancients in al-Sham. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.231}
