## Overview
Zaliq is a fortress in Sijistan, five farsakhs from the city of [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad raided it on the day of Mihrajan and took its dihqan. The dihqan ransomed himself by planting a spear (anza) and then covering it with gold and silver. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
