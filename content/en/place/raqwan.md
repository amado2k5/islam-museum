## Overview
Raqwan is the middle one of the ruined towns of the land of Sisyan, and the only inhabited city there. All the lands of Sisyan are in ruins since before the days of Alexander and his building of the barrier, and nothing of its country has been built up except the city of Raqwan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 930}

## The land
This land measures twenty-seven days to the continuous ruin beside it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 930} Sisyan is a country mostly of deserts, with not many towns; among its noted towns are Shuqmaqiya and Tughura, and the ruined lands join it, with nothing inhabited now except the city of Raqwan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 926}

## The region
Al-Idrisi describes these lands in the eighth part of the sixth clime with the Stinking Land and the land of Samriqi as desolate, far-flung and far to travel, entered only seldom by merchants and wanderers. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 926} Suqmaqiya and Tughura lie east of the Stinking Land and west of the land of Sisyan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 930}
