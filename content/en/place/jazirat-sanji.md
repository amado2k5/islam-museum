## Overview
A populous island with fertility and fresh water. Al-Idrisi describes its people as between fair and dark, and says they wear copper earrings in their ears. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.92}

It has gold mines. On its shore are idols with a raised hand, which the author says point to whoever sees them to turn back. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.92}
