## Summary
The principal town of Upper Galilee on a commanding hill. The Franks built its first fortress in 495/1101-2 and later handed it to the Templars; Saladin took it in Shawwal 584/December 1187 after a six-week siege; al-Mu'azzam Isa razed the fortress in 617/1219-20, and al-Malik al-Salih Isma'il returned it to the Franks in 638/1240; under the Mamluks it became the capital of a province (mamlaka) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 757-758}.
