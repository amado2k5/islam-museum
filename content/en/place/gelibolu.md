## Overview
Gelibolu, known in English as Gallipoli, is a populated place according to the iDAI.gazetteer. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2102584}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2102584 with the preferred name «Gallipoli», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Çanakkale (Vilâyeti)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Gelibolu» (Turkish), «Callipolis» (Latin) and «Καλλίπολις» (Greek). It links the place to Pleiades no. 501450 and GeoNames no. 746983. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2102584}
