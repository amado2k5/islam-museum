## Overview
Thath is a place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted after a place in the districts of Jayshan; he gathered its people, stayed some days, put [[person/muhammad-ibn-abi-al-zubayr-al-yarsami|Muhammad ibn Abi al-Zubayr al-Yarsami]] over it and let Abu al-Ushayra stay in it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. The editor's footnote says it is now known as Thah, near Rada', which is his statement and the museum adds nothing to it; the book gives no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-thath-and-bashar-and-the-return-to-sanaa-and-shibam-288|al-Hadi at Thath and Bashar and the return to Sana'a]].
