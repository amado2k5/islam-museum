## Summary
Al-Kashaniyya was a city of the dependencies of Samarkand on the north of the river Soghd, a stage from Ishtikhan and a stage to Arbinjan on the Bukhara road. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.502}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from Samarkand to Ishtikhan is twenty-one miles north of Samarkand, from Ishtikhan to al-Kashaniyya a stage, and from al-Kashaniyya to Arbinjan a stage; Arbinjan is on the north of the river Soghd on the road of Samarkand below Bukhara, and al-Kashaniyya is also on the north of the river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.502}. The city of Samarkand has several estates, and among the cities attributed to it are al-Dabusiyya, Arbinjan, Kish, Nasaf, Abarakt, Widhar, Ishtikhan, al-Kashaniyya, Banjikat, Kharghanikt, Firankath and Kabudhnjakt {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.499}. He notes that the river Kinjikt branches below the river and passes villages and hamlets, watering the estate of Kinjikt and the estate of the Marzban and others until it ends at al-Kashaniyya and passes it toward the bounds of the wall of Bukhara {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.499}. Al-Kashaniyya thus appears in his scheme among the dependent cities of Samarkand north of the river Soghd, at which the river Kinjikt ends on its way to the wall of Bukhara, on the road between Ishtikhan and Arbinjan.
