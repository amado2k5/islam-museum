## Overview
al-Rami: an island joined to Serendib. al-Idrisi reports that it has crops, minerals and perfume. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.75-76}

## The rhinoceros
The author reports that there is an animal in it called the rhinoceros, and that a picture appears in its horn when it is split. He denies what is told about that. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.75-76}
