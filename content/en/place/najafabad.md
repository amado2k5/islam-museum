## Overview
Najafabad is a city in Isfahan Province, Iran. Its name does not appear in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2753512 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Najafābād» in English. Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Isfahan (Ostan)». It records two Persian names, one in Latin transliteration («Najafābād») and one in Persian script ⁦(«نجف آباد»)⁩, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 418606. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2753512}
