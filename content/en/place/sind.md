## Overview
Sind is the land of India on the coast. Al-Baladhuri relates that Hakim ibn Jabala described it to Uthman: its water is a trickle, its fruit is inferior dates, and its thief is a champion. If the army in it is small they are lost, and if it is large they go hungry. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
