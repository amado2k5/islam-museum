## Overview
Radman is a site in the land of [[place/yemen|Yemen]] where [[person/al-muttalib-ibn-abd-manaf|al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf]] died. After him, [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] took over the offices of watering (siqaya) and provisioning (rifada). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/137-138}

## For young visitors
Radman is a place in Yemen where al-Muttalib, uncle of the Prophet's grandfather, died.
