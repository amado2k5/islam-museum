## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, a place more than ten days from Qayrawan. Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab and twelve men fleeing from Egypt caught up with him there, and he established their leadership over the army of that district and others. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
