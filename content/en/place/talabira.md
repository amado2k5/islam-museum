## Overview
Talabira is a city on the bank of the river Tajah. Al-Idrisi describes it as the most noble of lands in beauty, with beautifully arranged markets and fine houses. It is old in its buildings and monuments. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.551}
