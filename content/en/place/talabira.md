## Overview
Talabira is a large city on the bank of the river Tajah (the Tagus). Its citadel is the highest of fortresses, its city among the noblest of lands in beauty, and it covers a wide area with valuable resources, with well-ordered markets and fine houses. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 551}

## Buildings and land
There are many mills on the river Tajah, and it has a wide district and a province in good condition; its fields are fertile and its surroundings pleasing. It is ancient in settlement and old in its remains. It lies seventy miles from [[place/tulaytula|Toledo]], which is east of it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 551}

## The river and the road
The Tajah rises in the mountains joined to al-Qal'a and al-Funt and flows west to Toledo, then Talabira, then al-Makhada, al-Qantara, Qunaytirat Mahmud, Santarem and Lisbon, where it falls into the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 553} Talabira stands in the province of al-Sharat with Toledo, Madrid and Wadi al-Hijara. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538} From al-Balat to Talabira is two days. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 551}
