## Overview
Sa'la is a city on the bank of a river, populous, with fine dwellings, much trade and abundant buildings; merchants make for it from all the lands near and far with all kinds of goods and wares. It is not large, but compact and settled, and much silk clothing and pottery is made there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211}

## Distances
From Susa to Sa'la is eight days, from it to the city of Siniyyat al-Sin seventeen stages, and from Sa'la to Tughma eight stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211}

## Neighbours
Tughma is a large city with no fortress but populous, with many goods from which varied merchandise is exported; from it to Siniyyat al-Sin is eight stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 211} Sa'la is named in the tenth part of the second clime among the cities of eastern China. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 210}
