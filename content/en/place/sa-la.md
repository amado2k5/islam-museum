## Overview
Sa'la is a city populous with inhabitants on the bank of a river. Al-Idrisi mentions that it has abundant trade and continuous buildings, and that it lies fourteen stages from Qabtawa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.211}
