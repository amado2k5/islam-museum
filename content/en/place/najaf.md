## Overview
Najaf is a city in Iraq, where the shrine of Ali ibn Abi Talib is. Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his way to Iraq. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958} The coordinates recorded for it come from GeoNames, for a modern town of the same name {cite:geonames-gazetteer}. Matching it to the ancient site needs an additional source.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute records this place as no. 2104369, of type populated place, with the preferred name «Nadschaf», placed in its hierarchy under «Irak». Recorded names include Najaf, Meshed ’Ali and Nedjef. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2104369}
