## Overview
Yozgat is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2098191 with the preferred name «Yozgat», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Yozgat (Provinz)» (an administrative unit). Among its recorded names are «Saralus», «Yozgad» and «Yuzgat» and the Arabic-script form «يوزغات». It links the place to GeoNames no. 296562, GND no. 4240543-9 and Arachne no. 1208585. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2098191}
