## Overview
Al-A'sum is a place a journey from [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], as the text says, of whose people the narrator reports a way of honouring a guest that he calls ugly; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent to it from [[place/bayt-yathi-yemen|Bayt Yathi']], and its men and elders came, and he admonished and warned them and took an oath. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The manuscript margin gives its heading with a printed form that differs. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} The editor gives no reference for its site, so no location or coordinates are stated. See [[event/al-hadi-and-the-people-of-al-asum-at-bayt-yathi-286]].
