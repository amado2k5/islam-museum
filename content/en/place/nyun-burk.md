## Summary
Nyun Burk is a city in the land of Shusuniya. Al-Idrisi gives its distance from Iqraqu as one hundred miles, from Hala as forty miles, and from Wazra on the river as twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.891}

## Details
Nyun Burk is a city in the land of Shusuniya, which al-Idrisi places east of the land of Ifriziya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.879} From Iqraqu to Nyun Burk is one hundred miles, and it too lies in the land of Shusuniya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890} Between Hala and Nyun Burk is forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890}

From Nyun Burk westward to the city of Zunburka is sixty miles, and he calls Zunburka a noble city. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890} From Nyun Burk southward to the city of Qizlara is one hundred miles, which he gives as four stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890}

From Qizlara westward to Hirbird, in the land of Lumaniya, is sixty miles. From Qizlara eastward to Mashla is one hundred miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890} These distances show Idrisi drawing a network of nearby places, linked by one or more roads, rather than a single isolated city.

From Hala to Mashla is eighty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.891} From Nyun Burk to Wazra on the river is twenty-five miles, and from Wazra to the Dark Sea is twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.891} Nyun Burk therefore lies on a road that reaches the Dark Sea by way of Wazra.
