## Overview
Askanja: a large city bordering the foot of Mount Mont Jun, on the bank of the river on the east side, according to al-Idrisi. It is a fine place with many springs and rivers. He reports that it lies thirty-five miles from Ikriza. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.875}
