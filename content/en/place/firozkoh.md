## Overview
Firozkoh is the great fortress in the Ghur mountains between Herat and Ghazna that Yaqut names as the seat of the kingdom of the Ghurid rulers. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.284}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut says the name means the blue mountain, that most people pronounce it with a b, and that biruza in the language of Khurasan means blue-green. He describes it as a great, strongly defended fortress in the mountains of Ghurshistan between Herat and Ghazna, the capital of whoever holds those lands. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.284}

He calls it the home town of Shihab al-Din ibn Sam, who ruled Ghazna, Khurasan and India, describes him as a pious man, and adds that his brother Ghiyath al-Din was the elder. These are Yaqut's own statements about the two men; for detail they need other sources. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.284}

Yaqut then names a second Firozkoh, a fortress in Tabaristan near Damavand overlooking a town called Wayma, which he says he saw himself. It is a different place with the same name and does not belong to this record. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.284}
