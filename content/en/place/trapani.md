## Overview
Trapani is an ancient town of long standing on the seashore, with the sea around it on every side. Al-Idrisi says it is the nearest land to the Island of the Monk, and ten miles from the Sicilian coast. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.587}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says Trapani is the nearest land on Sicily to the Island of the Monk, ten miles away. The island has a harbour, a resting place and wells of water, and it lies fifteen miles beyond Trapani. North of the Island of the Monk is a small island called al-Yabisa, with no water and no harbour. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.587}

Trapani is an old, long-established town on the shore, with the sea around it on all sides. Its land is among the finest for farming, rich in produce and crops, and the town has wide markets and fertile livelihoods. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.601}

From Trapani, Mount Hamid is about ten miles away, a great, high peak. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.602}
