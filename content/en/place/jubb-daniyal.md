## Overview
The pit into which Nebuchadnezzar threw Daniel, and to which he had two lions brought. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}

In the story that Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari transmits, a prophet of the Children of Israel came to it by God's command and brought him out. The two lions walked at his right and left until he ordered them to go back. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.232}
