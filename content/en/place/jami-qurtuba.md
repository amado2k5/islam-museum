## Overview
The Great Mosque of Cordoba is a great mosque. Al-Idrisi says that no mosque of the Muslims resembles it. It is a hundred cubits long and about eighty wide, with a thousand columns and more. Its qibla is decorated with gilded and coloured glass, and it has a pulpit, a prayer niche and a dome, which he describes in detail. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.575}

It lies in [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]].
