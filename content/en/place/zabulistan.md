## Overview
Zabulistan is a region south of [[place/kabul|Kabul]] and [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]]. [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] conquered it. Its people broke the covenant, and he took captives from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] prevailed over it until he reached [[place/bust|Bust]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name as Zabulistan and describes it as a wide district standing on its own south of Balkh and Tokharistan, the same as Zabul. He notes that the Persians add the syllables sin-tan to place names, much like a relational ending, and says it is named for Zabul, grandfather of Rustam ibn Dastan, with Ghazna as its chief town. In the following entry, Zabul, he says it is the very same place, that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura ibn Habib conquered it by treaty, and that Muhammad ibn Sirin disliked the enslavement of its people, saying Uthman ibn Affan had made with them an agreement short of a full covenant. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.125}
