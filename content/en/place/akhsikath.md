## Overview
Akhsikath, which al-Balkhi gives as Akhsiket, was the old town of Ferghana, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
[[person/salih-ibn-muslim|Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili]] conquered it together with Kasan, Urshat and Sukhar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
