## Overview
Yaqut places the grave of Ali ibn Musa al-Rida at Tus, a city in Khurasan about ten farsakh from Nishapur. He says Tus comprised two towns, Tabaran and Nawqan, with more than a thousand villages, and that in it is the grave of Ali ibn Musa al-Rida and also the grave of Harun al-Rashid. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.49} He adds that in some of the gardens of Tus lie the graves of Ali al-Rida and of al-Rashid. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.49}

## Sanabadh
Yaqut's entry on Sanabadh describes a village in Tus, about a mile from the town of Tus, with the grave of Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Rida and the grave of al-Rashid. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.259} In a biographical note in the same entry he calls a man from Sanabadh one of "the people of the Rida shrine (al-mashhad al-Ridawi)" there, so the entry itself uses the word shrine. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.259}

## What the text does not give
Yaqut records where the graves lie. He does not describe the shrine's buildings, its custodians, or how Shia and other Muslims venerated it, and this packet does not cite a source for those points. The modern shrine's significance needs a cited second source before it is written here.
