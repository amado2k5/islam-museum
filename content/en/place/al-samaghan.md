## Overview
al-Samaghan: a district in the Sawad of Iraq. Its people made peace with [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad]], together with the people of Dirabadh, on the condition of poll-tax and kharaj, that they should not be killed or taken captive, and that they should not be prevented from using any road. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
