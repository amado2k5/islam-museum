## Overview
Mount Ayr is a large, well-known mountain to the south-west of [[place/medina|Medina]], near Dhu al-Hulayfa, the station of pilgrimage for its people. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.105}

## The sanctuary boundary
In the hadith on the sanctity of Medina, "what lies between Ayr and Thawr is sacred", so Ayr is the southern boundary of the sanctuary, as al-Samhudi reports. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.105} See [[concept/haram-al-madina|the sanctuary of Medina]].
