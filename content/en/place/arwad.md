## Overview
An island near Constantinople, according to al-Baladhuri. Junada ibn Abi Umayya conquered it in the year 54 AH, and Mu'awiya settled Muslims in it. Among its conquerors were Mujahid and Tabi', son of the wife of Ka'b al-Ahbar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
