## Overview
al-Jar: the port of the Hijaz. From the year 21 AH the sea-borne loads of food from the kharaj of Egypt used to arrive there. Sa'd al-Jar took charge of collecting them, and they were carried to Medina, where they were divided among the people in a house by measure. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The loads continued into the caliphate of Abu Ja'far and after it, with interruptions during the first strife and the time of the Umayyads. See [[event/umar-food-transport-egypt|the transport of food from Egypt in the time of Umar]].
