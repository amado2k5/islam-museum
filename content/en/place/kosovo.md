## Summary
Kosovo is a region of the Balkans. The Oxford Dictionary of Islam has two entries on it, Kosovo, Battle of, and Kosovo, Islam in. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1318} {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1319}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The entry on the battle describes a fight in 1389 between the [[place/ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] and Serbian empires. It says the Ottoman sultan Murad was killed but the Serbian force was destroyed, which opened the road to Ottoman expansion into the western Balkans, and that in Serbian national consciousness the battle stands for choosing death over compromise. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1318}

The entry on Islam in Kosovo says Muslims there are largely of [[place/albania|Albanian]] descent, with a few Turks who stayed after Yugoslav independence, and that Albanians began arriving in Kosovo in the late seventeenth century under Ottoman rule, which is the entry's own dating. It speaks of an old enmity between Christian Serbs and Muslim Albanians that was raised to fever pitch under Communism and again in the 1990s, and says the Communist regime forbade public religious observance and outlawed many of the Sufi brotherhoods. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1319}

The entry admits that the present religious situation of the Albanian community is hard to judge because Albanian nationalist assertion, especially since the violence of the 1990s, has taken over public discussion, and that the real influence of mosques and Sufi brotherhoods is unknown. It gives about ninety percent of the population in 2001 as ethnic Albanians, most of whom call themselves Muslims. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1319} The description of enmity and the figures are the Dictionary's own wording and have not been compared with another source.
