## Overview
Damanhur: a city in the Delta of Egypt. Ibn Battuta passed through it on his way from Alexandria to Damietta. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

An additional source is needed: the identification of the old place with the modern city of the same name has not yet been confirmed.
