## Overview
Damanhur: a city in the Delta of Egypt. Ibn Battuta passed through it on his way from Alexandria to Damietta. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

An additional source is needed: the identification of the old place with the modern city of the same name has not yet been confirmed.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Damanhur as a town one day's journey from Alexandria on the road to Misr, medium-sized, which he says he saw himself. He records a defeat there in which Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mazyad was routed by a force, a defeat that a poem by Mu'alla al-Ta'i describes. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.472}

He also names a village called Damanhur al-Shahid, a number of miles from al-Fustat, and a village in Upper Egypt west of the Nile with a great church that Christians visit. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.472}
