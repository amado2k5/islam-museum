## Overview
al-Ha'it al-Muhit: an ancient wall to the south of the land of al-Ghurba and Asbijab. It is also known as the Wall of Abd Allah ibn Humayd and the Wall of al-Qallas. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari counted it among the famous monuments. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.229}

An additional source is needed for the date of its construction.
