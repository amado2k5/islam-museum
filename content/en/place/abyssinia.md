## Overview
Abyssinia (Axum): the kingdom beyond the Red Sea; Yemen's occupier twice, author of the Elephant campaign, and later shelter of the Muslim emigrants.

## The place
The Abyssinians first occupied Yemen in 340 CE, exploiting the rivalry between Hamdan and Himyar, and their occupation lasted until 378 CE {cite:tafhim-al-quran}.

Then they occupied it again in 525 CE with seventy thousand soldiers under Aryat and a Roman fleet; among their governors was Abraha, author of the Elephant campaign against Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Abyssinia is a great African kingdom facing Yemen; it ruled Yemen for a time, and from it came the famous Elephant campaign.
