## Overview
Wargla is a town which al-Idrisi says lies thirty-one stages from Awdaghust. He mentions that the people of the Zaghawa carry wheat to it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.108} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.111}
