## Overview
A city in Palestine's Jordan district at the foot of Mount Gilboa west of the Jordan river; near it was fought the battle of 'Ayn Jalut in 658 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 299}.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Scythopolis/Nysa» (Pleiades no. 678378), classed as a settlement, an archaeological site, a theatre and a findspot, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 2100 CE. It describes it as a settlement at the junction of the Jordan and Jezreel valleys, with important phases from Egyptian to Roman times. {cite:pleiades 678378}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records Baysan: a city of the Jordan in the Syrian Ghor, said to be the tongue of the land, lying between Hawran and Filastin; in it is 'Ayn al-Fulus, said to be of Paradise — a spring with a slight brackishness whose mention comes in the hadith of al-Jassasa, given in full under the entry Tayba. It is described as rich in palms, yet Yaqut says he saw it repeatedly and saw only two date palms in it; he records it as one of the signs of the Dajjal's emergence. It is a hot, unhealthy town whose people are dark-skinned and curly-haired from the heat, and to it, he supposes, the wine is ascribed — Layla al-Akhyaliyya has verses on Tawba mentioning it. Among those ascribed to it are Sariya al-Baysani, 'Abd al-Warith ibn al-Hasan al-Qurashi known as al-Tarjuman al-Baysani, and al-Qadi al-Fadil 'Abd al-Rahim ibn 'Ali al-Baysani, minister of Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.527} He also has a Baysan among the villages of Mosul with a large farm, and one among the villages of Marw al-Shahijan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.528}

## The museum's identification
The record's Baysan is the Ghor city between Hawran and Filastin, and it is Yaqut's city and the Pleiades place (Scythopolis/Nysa); his note that it is among the signs of the Dajjal and that 'Ayn al-Fulus is of Paradise is transmitted material of the al-Jassasa hadith, recorded with its ascription. The Mosul and Marw al-Shahijan Baysans are other villages in his book.
