## Overview
A city in Palestine's Jordan district at the foot of Mount Gilboa west of the Jordan river; near it was fought the battle of 'Ayn Jalut in 658 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 299}.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Scythopolis/Nysa» (Pleiades no. 678378), classed as a settlement, an archaeological site, a theatre and a findspot, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 2100 CE. It describes it as a settlement at the junction of the Jordan and Jezreel valleys, with important phases from Egyptian to Roman times. {cite:pleiades 678378}
