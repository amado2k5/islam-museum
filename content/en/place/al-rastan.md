## Overview
Al-Rastan is a city in Syria, recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, with the ancient Greek name «Arethousa» among its names. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281548}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2281548 with the preferred name «ar-Rastan» (Arabic, transliterated), classed as a populated place, with a parent place above it in the hierarchy. Among the names it records are «Arethousa» (Greek), «الرستن» (Arabic) and «Rastan», and it links the place to Pleiades no. 668199. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2281548}
