## Overview
The Sea of Darkness (the ocean that surrounds the west of al-Andalus): the ocean lying west of al-Andalus and the land of Britain. The author reports that its ebb and flow follow fixed times of the day and night, and he explains this by the stirring of the wind together with the sun, and that the people of the Maghrib witnessed it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.93}.
