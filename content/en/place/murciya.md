## Overview
A city that is the capital of the land of Tudmir, with a thriving, populous suburb and a finely built wall. Water runs through its suburb. It lies on the bank of the White River and, according to al-Idrisi, five stages from [[place/valencia|Valencia]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.559}
