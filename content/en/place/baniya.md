## Summary
Baniya is a small city of the Sind which al-Idrisi describes as rich in comforts, cheap in prices, its people living in ease. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that Baniya is a small city rich in blessings and cheap in prices, its people a mixture who enjoy a life of ease, great plenty over themselves, and most of them well-to-do {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}. He notes that from this city to al-Mansuriya is three stages, to Mamhal six stages, and from al-Daybul to this city two stages, and that the city of Mamhal lies between India and Sind {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}. He counts it among the cities of Sind together with al-Sandur, Manjabri, Basmad and al-Multan, all of them belonging to and reckoned within Sind {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.170}. Baniya is thus one of the prosperous, low-priced towns of the province of Sind.
