## Overview
A place called Hidayn to which the men went out and to which [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] marched with all his army in Shawwal 288 AH; the fighting went on there ([[event/abu-al-atahiya-holds-the-fort-of-alab-and-the-fight-at-hidayn-288]]) and [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] and [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] fell there ([[event/the-deaths-of-ali-ibn-sulayman-and-abu-al-atahiya-at-hidayn-288]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}.

## Detail
The editor gives: Ghayat al-amani 1:183, Tabr Hidayn; from al-Hamdani that the Tabr is a mountain near Sana'a; and from a modern writer that the mountains of Hidayn lie south of Sana'a about seven kilometres away {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}. The museum passes this on as the editor's and has not checked it.
