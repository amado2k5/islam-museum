## Overview
The Two Obelisks: two square stones standing upright along their length, the upper part narrower than the lower, with writing on them in an ancient script. al-Idrisi reports, on the authority of the people of the account, that they were hewn from mountain stone and set up by the order of a king. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.321}
