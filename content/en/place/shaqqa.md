## Overview
Al-Shaqqa is a town on the sea shore, overlooking and spacious in its expanse. Al-Idrisi says that in his time it was the mother of the regions to which people go, and travellers come to it from Ifriqiya and the edges of al-Jazira. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.600}
