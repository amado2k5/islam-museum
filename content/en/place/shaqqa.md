## Overview
Al-Shaqqa is a town on the sea coast of Sicily, with a pleasing outlook, with buildings, markets and many houses. Al-Idrisi says that in his time it was the mother of the regions next to it and of the districts round about, that its anchorage is always busy, and that travel to it from Ifriqiya and Tripoli is always heavy. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 600}

## Distances
From Karkant (Agrigento) to al-Shaqqa is one stage along the sea, twenty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 600} From al-Shaqqa to Mazar is two light stages, with a large halting place called al-Asnam on the sea between them. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 600}

## Qal'at al-Ballut
Its district is that of Qal'at al-Ballut, a strong fort and a lofty stronghold, hard to climb, with fertile country, good estates and fruits of many kinds, springs and streams with many mills on them. It once held many people, who have moved in this period to al-Shaqqa, leaving only a few men to guard it. It is twelve miles from the fort to the sea and nine from there to al-Shaqqa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 600} Elsewhere the distance from the fort to al-Shaqqa is given as four Frankish miles, said to be twelve, so the two pages do not agree exactly. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 607}
