## Overview
Two neighbouring landmarks in the land of Safad. The Pit of Joseph is near the village of Shura, and close to it is the Bridge of Jacob, well known and famous. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari counts them among the monuments of Syria. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.231}
