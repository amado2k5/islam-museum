## Summary
Barnas is a city near the sea which al-Idrisi places fifty miles from the city of Ayman. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Akhilu to the city of Ayman on the sea is twenty-five miles, from Ayman to Barnas fifty miles, Barnas lying close to the sea as mentioned before, and from Barnas to the city of Armuqistru twenty-five miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.896}. It is thus one of the consecutive coastal cities he lists on that road, between Ayman and Armuqistru, and in describing it he refers back to what preceded in his book since he had already mentioned it elsewhere.  Beyond this bare mention and the distances given, al-Idrisi adds no further description of it here.
