## Overview
Mallawi is a town in Middle Egypt which the iDAI.gazetteer records as both a populated place and an archaeological site, within al-Minya governorate. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2751371}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2751371 with the preferred name «Mallawi», classed as an archaeological site and a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «al-Minyā (Muhāfazat)», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «el-Arish» and «Mallaui» in transliterated Arabic, «Manlau» in transliterated Coptic and the English gloss «place of fabric». It links the place to GeoNames no. 352951 and to a German National Library authority record (5182726-8). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2751371}
