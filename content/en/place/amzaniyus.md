## Overview
Amzaniyus: an island in the dark sea. al-Idrisi relays a custom of its people, which he mentions as a report he heard, without vouching for its truth. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.956}
