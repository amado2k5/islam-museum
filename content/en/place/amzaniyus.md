## Summary
The Amzanius islands were two populated islands in the Dark Sea which al-Idrisi attributes to the Magians, one inhabited by men and the other by women. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says the Dark Sea holds many uninhabited islands, but among the populated ones are two called the islands of the Amzanius Magians: the western island is peopled by men only with no woman there, the second holds the women with no man among them, and each year they cross the strait between them in small boats in springtime, the man staying with his wife about a month before returning to his island until the next year — a known custom and standing practice among them {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.955} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.956}. He adds that the nearest approach to them is from the city of Anhu, three crossings away, though one may also come from Kalmar and Ragholda, yet hardly anyone reaches these islands for the heavy mist and darkness of that sea {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.956}.
