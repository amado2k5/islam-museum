## Summary
Mawargha is a fine village of modest size in the Maghreb with crops, fertile soil and running water, one stage from Raygha. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.254}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the village of Raygha to Mawargha is one stage; Mawargha is a fine village though small in extent, with crops, fertile land and running water {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.254}. Raygha, its neighbour, has broad land, extensive ploughlands, fruit and gardens, a good market held on a fixed day each week, and abundant water with gushing springs {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.254}. From Mawargha to Ashir Ziri is two stages, a fortress of fine position with many benefits and a famous market day drawing every fine thing, thence one stage to Tamzakida and two to al-Masila {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.254}.
