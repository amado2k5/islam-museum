## Overview
Sharma is a city on the coast of the land of Yemen, according to al-Idrisi. Between it and Lasa'a there is a day's journey, and in it is a hot spring like the hot spring of Lasa'a. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.55}
