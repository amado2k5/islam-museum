## Overview
Al-Khanuqa is a populous, inhabited town with a busy market and trade on the roadside. It lies two stages from [[place/qarqisiya|Qarqisiya]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}
