## Summary
Al-Khanuqa is a small, populous and inhabited town near Qarqisiya, with a busy market and commerce in proportion, two days from Qarqisiya and two stages from al-Raqqa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from Qarqisiya to al-Khanuqa — also read al-Khaluqa — is two days; al-Khanuqa is a small, populous, inhabited town with a busy market and commerce in proportion {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}. From al-Khanuqa to al-Raqqa is two stages, and he has already described al-Raqqa in its place {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}. The road from Baghdad to al-Raqqa is fifteen stages {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}. Qarqisiya itself is a town on the eastern side of the Euphrates, below which flows the river al-Hirmas called al-Khabur, and it has many fruits {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}. There is another road from Baghdad to al-Raqqa by way of Mosul, and a route across the steppe in ten stages or thereabouts totalling three hundred and seventy-two miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.657}.
