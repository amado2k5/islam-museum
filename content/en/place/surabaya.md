## Overview
Surabaya is Indonesia's second-largest city and main port, in East Java. It is a modern place that does not appear in the two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan read for this museum, so this page rests only on what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2769271 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Surabaya» in English. Its parent in the hierarchy is the island «Java». It records names in German, English and Greek, in Persian ⁦(«سورابایا»)⁩ and in Arabic ⁦(«سورابايا»)⁩, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 1625822. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2769271}
