## Overview
Trabzon is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within Trabzon Province. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2287752}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2287752 with the preferred name «Trapezunt», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is Trabzon Province. Among the names it records are «Trabzon», «Trapezus», «Τραπεζούντα», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 738648. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2287752}
