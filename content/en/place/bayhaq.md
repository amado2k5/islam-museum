## Overview
Bayhaq: a rustaq of Nishapur. al-Aswad ibn Kulthum al-Adawi, the rival of the Rabab, entered it through a breach in its walls, and he fought until he and those with him were killed. Then Adham ibn Kathum took charge of the people's affair, was victorious, and conquered Bayhaq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
