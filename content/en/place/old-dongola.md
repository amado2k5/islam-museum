## Overview
Old Dongola is a site; Pleiades records a settlement there, the late antique capital of the Nubian state of Makuria. {cite:pleiades 865674318}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Dongola» (Pleiades no. 865674318), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 300 CE to 2100 CE, an open upper bound for the modern period. Its description says that it was the late antique capital of the Nubian state of Makuria and remained an important centre of trade through the medieval period. Names recorded there include «Dunqulah al Qadīmah» and «Dunqulā al-ʿAjūz» (in Arabic script «دنقلا العجوز»), both from 1700 to 2100 CE, and «Dunḳula» in Ottoman Turkish from 1500 to 1599 CE. {cite:pleiades 865674318}
