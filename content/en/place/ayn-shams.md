## Overview
Ayn Shams: one of the villages of Egypt. After the conquest of Fustat, Amr ibn al-As sent Abd Allah ibn Hudhafa al-Sahmi to it. He settled on its land, and made peace with the people of its villages on the same terms as the judgement of Fustat, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.304}

It is a site in the accounts of [[event/four-lieutenants-egypt|the four commanders of Amr in Egypt]].
