## Summary
Itil was the capital of the Khazar realm and the name of its river which flows into the Khazar sea; al-Idrisi makes it the centre of distances to Samandar, Burtas, Bulgar and Bajnak. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from Bab al-Abwab to Samandar is four days, between Bab al-Abwab and the kingdom of al-Sarir eight days, and from Itil to Samandar eight days {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}. From Itil to the first bound of Burtas is twenty days, Burtas being a land of about fifteen days from first to last {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}. From Burtas to Bajnak is ten days, and from Itil to Bajnak a month's journey {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}. From Itil to Bulgar by the desert road is about a month, and by water two months, the journey being upstream, while the descent down the river is about twenty days {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}. Al-Khazar is the name of the realm and its capital is Itil, and Itil is the name of the river that runs to it from the Rus and Bulgar lands and pours into the Khazar sea {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.919}. Itil is thus for him the seat of the Khazar king on the river bearing its name, which flows from the lands of the Rus and Bulgar until it empties into the Khazar sea, and to it are referred the land and river distances of those regions.
