## Overview
Sijilmasa is a city in the desert near the land of the Lamtuna, and it is the caravan route to the Sudan, according to al-Idrisi. It lies about forty days' caravan journey from Sala and Takrur, and between it and Awlil there are about forty stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.18}
