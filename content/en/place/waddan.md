## Overview
Waddan is a site between [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and [[place/medina|Medina]], near [[place/al-abwa|al-Abwa']]. Its distance from [[place/rabigh|Rabigh]] on the Medina side is twenty-nine miles. The Prophet made his first raid to it in the year 2 AH. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

The book gives no coordinates for it, so none have been set yet.
