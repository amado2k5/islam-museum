## Overview
Al-Sabra is a mountain of the Akilis among the mountains of [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.192} Ibn Abbad posted about a hundred and fifty picked riders there, who had wounded many of the men, and [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] charged them with seven riders. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.192} On the second sortie al-Hadi looked from near al-Sabra at their array on the flank of the mountain. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} It is not Sabra of Ifriqiya. It has no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-charges-the-akilis-at-al-sabra-and-the-fourteen-arrows-287]].
