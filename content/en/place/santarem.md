## Overview
Shantarin, the Arabic name this record uses for Santarém, was a fortified city of al-Andalus on the Tagus, near the point where the river runs into the Atlantic. Yaqut writes that it was fifteen days' journey from Cordoba and four from Baja, and that it had been held by the Franks since 543 AH. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.367} The identification of Yaqut's Shantarin with the modern town is the record's (Wikidata), not Yaqut's.

## Shantarin on the coast
Yaqut places Shantarin among the towns of the Atlantic coast of the Iberian peninsula. In one route he gives the coast from Shalb as a line of places: Ushbuna (Lisbon), Shantara and Shantarin lie on the right of the route. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.263} In the Lisbon entry he says that Ushbuna, which is also called Lisbon, is joined to Shantarin near the Atlantic, and quotes Ibn Hawqal that the river of Shantarin reaches the sea at Ushbuna. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.195}
