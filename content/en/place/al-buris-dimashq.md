## Overview
al-Buris: a remarkable structure in the middle of Damascus. al-Mas'udi reports that it was still standing in his time, and that wine used to flow through it in ancient times. He reports that poets mentioned it in praising the kings of the Ghassanids. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.555}

An additional source is needed for the date of its construction and the reason for its name.
