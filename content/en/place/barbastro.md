## Overview
Barbastro: the capital of a district of the land of Britain, near Saragossa, according to al-Maqqari. The army of the Ardumaniyyin overcame it in the year 456 AH, after a siege of forty days and the collapse of its water channel. al-Maqqari gives, on the authority of Ibn Hayyan, a description of the killing and captivity that took place in it, and his saying that the like of it had not been witnessed by the Muslims. Then Ahmad al-Muqtadir ibn Hud took it back in Jumada al-Ula in the year 457 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.449-454}
