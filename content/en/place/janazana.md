## Summary
Janazana is a fine populous city of the land of Baluniya, which al-Idrisi places one hundred miles east of the city of Aqraqu. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Aqraqu to the city of Janazana is one hundred miles — it lying east of Aqraqu — that it is a fine populous city, that from Janazana to the city of Ratsalaba is sixty miles, and from Ratsalaba to Ramasali in the land of Sadmara one hundred miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.890}. He counts it among the lands of Baluniya together with Zamataq, Aqraqu, Bartislaba and Ramasali {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.882}. He also mentions that among the cities of that land are Aqraqu, Janazana, Ratsalaba, Saradih, Naghrada and Shatnu, and that all these lands are famous chief towns and firmly established metropolises combining the good things of far-flung countries, possessing scholars versed in the sciences and the Roman faiths, and craftsmen of skill and knowledge in their crafts {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.903}.
