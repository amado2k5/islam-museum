## Overview
Qarqisiya, at the confluence of the Khabur with the Euphrates, is one of the districts of [[place/jazira|al-Jazira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri relates that Iyad sent [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] al-Fihri to it, and he conquered it by treaty on the terms of the treaty of [[place/raqqa|Raqqa]]. Then its people broke the covenant, and [[person/umayr-ibn-sad|Umayr ibn Sa'd]] made peace with them on the same terms as their first treaty, when he took the Khabur route to the fortresses of the Euphrates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He mentions that the rahba (courtyard) below it had no ancient trace, and was built in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
