## Overview
Kuyaba is a town in the land of Bulghar. Al-Idrisi says it lies eight stages from Salaw, and that Muslim merchants head to it from [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.917}
