## Overview
Beaufort Castle, called Qal'at al-Shaqif in Arabic, is a castle in southern Lebanon, placed by Pleiades at about 33.32° N, 35.53° E. {cite:pleiades 812828781}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Beaufort Castle, Lebanon» (Pleiades no. 812828781), classed as a castle, with a location attested from 1081 CE to 1204 CE. {cite:pleiades 812828781} Recorded names include «Qal'at al-Shaqif» [Arabic, 2000 CE – 2099 CE]. {cite:pleiades 812828781} Pleiades describes it as a castle dating from 1139 that served as a Crusader fortress in the twelfth century, standing about 700 metres above the gorge of the Litani river in southern Lebanon. {cite:pleiades 812828781}
