## Overview
Sicily is an island in the sea. According to al-Baladhuri, Mu'awiya ibn Hudayj al-Kindi was the first to raid it, in the days of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan, and it has been raided ever since. The family of al-Aghlab ibn Salim took more than twenty cities from it, which were in Muslim hands at the time al-Baladhuri wrote. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}
