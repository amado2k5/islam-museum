## Summary
Al-Marqab is a fortress of the Syrian coast set on a mountain cut off on every side, eight miles from Antartus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.644}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that Antartus on the sea-shore is small in extent with busy markets and thriving trade; from it is the fortress of al-Marqab, standing on a mountain cut off on every side, eight miles between them; from al-Marqab to the town of Balanyas is eight miles, and Balanyas, four miles from the sea, is a small cultivated town where every fine kind of fruit and grain is found in plenty; from Balanyas to the coastal town of Jabala is ten miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.644}. Al-Marqab is thus a link in the chain of strongholds along that Syrian shore.
