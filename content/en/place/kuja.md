## Summary
Kuja was a town of the land of China lying beyond the mountains enclosing China; al-Idrisi calls it a prosperous town, not large, with much trade and building. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.515}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Awj to Kuja was ten stages by camel-march, and that east of Kuja lies the town of Darkhun, a mid-sized town of the land of China and the last district of China in the north; the settled country of the Tughuzghuz Arak lands adjoins it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.515} As for the town of Atbash, it stands on an impregnable mountain, on guard against the Turks; from it to Tibet is ten stages, and from Atbash to Upper Barskhan seven days through the Turks' land. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.515}
