## Overview
Burs: a place on the road to al-Mada'in. al-Baladhuri reports that Khalid ibn Arfata reached it in pursuit of the fleeing Persians, and stopped at the house of a man called Bastam, who honoured him. For that reason a river there was named the river of Bastam. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.362}
