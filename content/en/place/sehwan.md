## Overview
In the Mu'jam al-buldan Siwistan is a large district of Sind and the first of the lands of India, on the Indus, with a large and prosperous city. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.301}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut spells out the vowelling of the name letter by letter. He calls it a great district of Sind, at the beginning of India, on the river Indus, and a large city with a wide income, many lands and villages. He joins the district and the city and does not separate them, and he names none of its people and tells no story. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.301}

## The museum's identification
The museum's record uses the name Siwistan for the city known today as Sehwan in Pakistan. That identification is the museum's reading, not Yaqut's: his entry describes a district on the Indus and does not mention modern Sindh or Sehwan.
