## Overview
Nubia is a region south of Egypt. Al-Baladhuri mentions that the horses of the Muslims entered it after the conquest of Egypt. They met fierce fighting and a volley of arrows that struck the eyes, so they were named "Dammat al-Hadaq" (those who wound the eyes). Then Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh made a truce with them without jizya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 331}
