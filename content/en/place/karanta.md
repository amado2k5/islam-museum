## Summary
Karanta is a town of the Maghreb which in earlier times had many vineyards, fruit and irrigated fields, one stage from Tamalta. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.247}

## Details
Al-Idrisi traces the road from the Sabu river to Tamalta, one stage, a village with buildings on a river called Wadi Inawun coming from the south, then one stage to Karanta, which in former times was a town of many vineyards, fruit and fields watered by irrigation {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.247}. From it to Bab Zanata is about ten miles, a valley with ploughland watered by it, rich in sheep, cattle and crops near the Inawun river; thence one stage to the fortress of Karmata, which has a market, crops and livestock and overlooks the Inawun river {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.247}.
