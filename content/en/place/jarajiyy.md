## Summary
Jarajiyy was a town on the valley of Jarajiyy, described by al-Idrisi as a fine large town possessed of buildings, crops and vineyards. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.628}

## Details
Detailing the roads of that region, al-Idrisi says that from Barsana to the valley of Jarajiyy is twelve miles, and on this valley stands the town of Jarajiyy; from the valley of Jarajiyy to the valley of al-Anna — a wadi running from Istilu — is twenty-four miles, and from al-Anna to Bahlaya, a valley bearing mills, is twelve miles, and from it to Asjalasa — a wadi that ships enter — is sixteen miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.628} Barsana, from which this road begins, is a halting-place on a mountain with an administration and fertile districts; its people have flocks of sheep and cattle, connected fields and standing revenues. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.627}
