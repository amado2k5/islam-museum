## Summary
A district beside Shahrazur; its people made peace with Utba ibn Farqad on jizya and kharaj among his conquests, in al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 466}

## Details
Ishaq ibn Sulayman al-Shahrazuri told al-Baladhuri, from his father, from Muhammad ibn Marwan, from al-Kalbi, from one of the family of Ezra al-Bajali, that Ezra ibn Qays tried to take Shahrazur while governing Hulwan in Umar's caliphate and failed; Utba ibn Farqad then campaigned against it and took it after fighting, on terms like the peace of Hulwan, at a time when the heavy fighting was striking down Muslim men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 466} Ishaq also reported from his father, from their shaykhs, that the people of al-Samaghan and Darabadh made peace with Utba on jizya and kharaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 466} Darabadh is thus a district neighbouring Shahrazur in the Jibal lands whose people entered the settlement Utba ibn Farqad received after conquering Shahrazur, owing jizya and land-tax like the rest of that region's sulh.
