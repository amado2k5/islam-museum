## Overview
Arafat: a wide plain outside [[place/mecca|Mecca]], where pilgrims stand on the day of Arafat. al-Azraqi reports that it was so named because Gabriel said to [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]]: "Have you come to know your rites?" In its middle is the mount of al-Mashash, and behind it is the mount of Kabkab. {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka}
