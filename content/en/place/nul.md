## Overview
Nul is a large, populous city at the edge of the desert, according to al-Idrisi. The people of that land have no other city to shelter in, and in it are the tribes of Lamtuna and Lamta. From it to the sea is thirteen stages. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 224}
