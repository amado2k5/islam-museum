## Summary
Bajirwan, a district of Azerbaijan, whose men stood among the fighters the marzban of Ardabil mustered against Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 455}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that when Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman came to Ardabil — the city of Azerbaijan and seat of its marzban — the marzban had gathered fighters from the people of Bajirwan, Mimadh, al-Narir, Sarah, al-Shiz and al-Miyanj among others, who fought the Muslims hard for days; the marzban then made peace with Hudhayfa for all the people of Azerbaijan on eight hundred thousand dirhams of eight weight, on condition that none of them be killed or taken captive and no fire temple destroyed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 455} From Abu Makhnaf he reports that al-Ash'ath ibn Qays al-Kindi campaigned against Azerbaijan after they apostatized, took the fortress of Bajirwan and made peace with them on al-Mughira's terms, a pact which held to his own day. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 456} Later, Jarir ibn Abdallah al-Bajali took the leader of an Armenian host and crucified him on the fortress of Bajirwan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 459}
