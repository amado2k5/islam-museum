## Overview
Burgundy of the Franks: a land with many villages and farms. al-Idrisi describes its people as men of war and of high spirit. It is joined by the district of Burgundy of the Lombards, and it is surrounded by the mountain of Mont Jun. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.743}
