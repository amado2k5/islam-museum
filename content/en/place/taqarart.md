## Overview
Taqarart is the city of [[place/meknes|Miknasa (Meknes)]]; al-Idrisi writes "the city of Miknasa is the one called Taqarart", and says it remains in his time as it was, little changed. It is a handsome city raised above the ground, with a small river on its east with mills on it, and buildings, gardens and crops joining it; the soil is good for farming and it has ample earnings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 244}

## The name and the tribe
Miknasa was named for Miknas the Berber, who settled there with his sons when they came to the Maghrib and gave each son a plot to build up with his children; these places lie close together. Among the lands of Miknasa is the one called Bani Ziyad, a populous city with markets, baths and fine houses and water running through its lanes; it is about a quarter of a mile from Taqarart. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 244}

## Neighbours
It is about the same distance from Taqarart to Bani Tawara. Tawara was a settled, populous place with many markets, its water coming from a great river to the south that divides at its upper end and runs through its lanes. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 244} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 245} Between Tawara and Bani Ziyad are two small towns, one of them al-Qasr, built by a Almoravid emir, and the other at Bani Atush, with gardens, olives, figs and grapes. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 245}

The earlier data summary put Taqarart ten miles from Miknasa; the page makes it Miknasa itself, and the summary has been corrected.
