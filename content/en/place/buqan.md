## Overview
al-Buqan: a land near the frontier post of India. al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud and Ibn Hari al-Bahili raided it, and al-Baladhuri reports that its people are Muslims. Imran ibn Musa al-Barmaki built in it the city of al-Bayda in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
