## Overview
Fahl is a place in Syria where the Muslims fought the Romans, one year after the conquest of Damascus, according to the editors' note. According to Ibn Ishaq, al-Sa'ib ibn al-Harith al-Sahmi was killed there in the caliphate of Umar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Wikidata matches it to Pella (the Fahl level) in Jordan for locating the site, and this is an approximate placement at the level of the place.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut vocalises the name with a kasra, a quiet ha and a final lam, and says it names a place in al-Sham where the Muslims fought the Byzantines; the day of Fihl is recorded in the conquest accounts, and he thinks the name foreign, since he has not seen it in Arabic speech. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.237} He says eighty thousand Byzantines were killed there, a year after the conquest of Damascus, and gives verses by al-Qa'qa' ibn Amr al-Tamimi on the battle. He adds that the day of Fihl was also called the day of al-Radgha and the day of Baysan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.237}
