## Overview
Nihawand is a city in the mountains (al-Jibal). Al-Baladhuri mentions the day of Nihawand in the year 20 AH, when the Persians, the people of al-Rayy, Qumis, Isfahan, Hamadhan and al-Mahin gathered to Yazdegerd after his flight from Hulwan. He appointed Mardanshah dhu al-hajib over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
