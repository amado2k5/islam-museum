## Overview
Nihawand is a city in the mountains (al-Jibal). Al-Baladhuri mentions the day of Nihawand in the year 20 AH, when the Persians, the people of al-Rayy, Qumis, Isfahan, Hamadhan and al-Mahin gathered to Yazdegerd after his flight from Hulwan. He appointed Mardanshah dhu al-hajib over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Nihawand as a great city in the south of Hamadhan, three days away. He says that Abu al-Mundhir Hisham held that it was named Nihawand because the people found it as it was. It is also said to have been built by Noah, and that its name was Nuh-awand, which was shortened. Hamza says that its original form was Banu-hawand, meaning doubled goodness. Ptolemy placed it in the fourth clime, at longitude seventy-two and latitude thirty-six degrees, and Yaqut says it is the oldest city in the mountains. He records that it was conquered in the year 19 AH, though some say 20. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.313}
