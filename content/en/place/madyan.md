## Overview
Madyan is a large city on the shore of the Red Sea (the Qulzum Sea). Al-Idrisi says that it is the city of Shu'ayb. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}

## What al-Idrisi reports
It has a house and water from a flowing spring. Its people's livelihood is narrow. It lies five stages from [[place/ayla|Ayla]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut quotes Abu Zayd that Madyan is on the Qulzum sea, opposite Tabuk at about six stages, and is larger than Tabuk. In it is the well from which Musa watered the flocks of Shu'ayb. Abu Zayd saw the well covered, with a building raised over it, and the people draw their water from a flowing spring. Madyan lies in the third clime. Madyan is also the name of the tribe, and the city is the city of the people of Shu'ayb, named after Madyan ibn Ibrahim. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.77}
