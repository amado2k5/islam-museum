## Overview
Madyan is a large city on the shore of the Red Sea (the Qulzum Sea). Al-Idrisi says that it is the city of Shu'ayb. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}

## What al-Idrisi reports
It has a house and water from a flowing spring. Its people's livelihood is narrow. It lies five stages from [[place/ayla|Ayla]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.350}
