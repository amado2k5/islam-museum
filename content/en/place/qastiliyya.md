## Overview
Qastiliya is a city called Tawzar. It has a strong wall, abundant palms and large, fine citron trees. Among its best vegetables are those common throughout the land of Ifriqiya, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}
