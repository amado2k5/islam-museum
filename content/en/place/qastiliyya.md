## Overview
Qastiliyya is a town in the land of Ifriqiya, also called Tuzar. Al-Idrisi describes it as having a strong wall, as one of the places with the most palms, and as having plentiful dates that supply the whole of Ifriqiya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}

## Fruit and produce
It has large, fine citron trees, and most of its fruit is in a moderate condition. Its vegetables are abundant and very good. Its water, however, is not good to drink. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}

## Food and farming
Food prices there are high for most of the year, because food is brought in from outside, and the wheat and barley grown locally are few and small in amount. The town adjoins al-Hamma to its south and east, a short stage away. Al-Hamma's water is not pleasant, but it is drinkable, and its people are content with it. The area has many palms and abundant dates. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}

## Route
From Bagay it is four stages to Qastiliyya, three stages to Qasantina, and four stages to Tubna of the Zab. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.277}
