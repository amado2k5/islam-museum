## Summary
Khur is a small town on the edge of the desert, smaller than Tabas, built of clay, with neither fortress nor citadel, lying two days from Qayin. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.463}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from Tabas to Khur are two long stages, and Khur is a small town on the brink of the waste, with two stages between Khur and Khusab {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.463}. Likewise from Qayin to Khur are two days; Khur is a town smaller than Tabas, built of clay, possessing neither fortress nor citadel, its orchards few and its palms scant, its water entering the town through covered stone conduits {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.463} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464}. Khusab is a town larger than Khur, with a mosque and minbar, fine markets and circulating trade {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464}. From Khusab to the town of Karin is about three stages; Karin is a large, well-watered village without a wall, possessing a great rustaq, and between Karin and Tabas are twelve miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.464}.
