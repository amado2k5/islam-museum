## Overview
Nahrawara is a city with a great king who rides elephants, and elephants are abundant in his army. Al-Idrisi mentions that many Muslim merchants go to it, and that its king honours them and gives them justice regarding what is in their hands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.188}
