## Overview
Astur: a city full of Turks, with a spread-out carpet of land, having wheat, rice and iron mines, according to al-Idrisi. It lies on the river Ghamash, and he describes its people as the most courageous and valiant of the Turks. It lies six stages from Banjar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.716}
