## Overview
Ayadhaj: one of the districts of al-Ahwaz, according to al-Baladhuri. [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]] conquered it after a fierce fight, and Kurds gathered at it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
