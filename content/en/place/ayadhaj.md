## Summary
Ayadhaj, of the districts of al-Ahwaz in al-Baladhuri, was taken by [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abdallah ibn Amir]] after heavy fighting, with Kurds rallying to its defenders. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 538}

## Details
In the conquests of al-Ahwaz, al-Baladhuri reports that Abdallah ibn Amir took Sanbil and al-Zut, whose people had apostatized, so that Kurds from those parts rallied to them, and that he took Ayadhaj after severe fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 538} In the same report Abu Musa took al-Sus, Tustar and Dawraq by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 538} He also relates that Kharazad ibn Bas, when he rebelled, fled to Ayadhaj and fortified himself in a fortress named for him; when the siege dragged on he slipped out by night to join Abd al-Malik, but Makram caught him and sent him to al-Hajjaj, who beheaded him. Beside Makram's camp stood an old village to which building was later joined until it grew large. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 540}
