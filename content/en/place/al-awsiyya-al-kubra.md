## Overview
Al-Awsiyya al-Kubra is a village in the [[place/al-fayyum|Fayyum]] {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.

## Water and the gate
Water reaches it from two outlets, and it has a gate that controls the water. Its palms and trees are watered from it {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.731}.
