## Overview
Bansa: a large, populous, fertile and fortified city with much cultivation, and its people are a people of courage in wars, according to al-Idrisi. In his account it is the seat of the kingdom of the Lamaniyya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.874}
