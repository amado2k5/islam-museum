## Summary
Al-Jund was a populated fortress in Yemen on a great hill, with water wells and people of Khawlan, on the road between al-Thaja and Sanaa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.152}

## Details
Al-Idrisi writes on the Yemen routes that from the heights of Yahsub to the fortress of al-Thaja is thirty-six miles, and from al-Thaja to al-Jund twenty-seven miles; al-Jund is a populated fortress housing people of Khawlan, with water wells, set on a great hill, and from al-Jund to Sanaa is a hundred and forty miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.152}. In his account it is a fortified staging post on a rise overlooking the march between upper Yemen and Sanaa, supplying caravans with well water.
