## Summary
Al-Nawaqir is the name al-Idrisi gives to three towering white mountains overlooking the seashore, some eighteen miles from the fortress of al-Zib on the coast of Syria. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.365}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from Tiberias to Acre is two days, from Acre to the fortress of al-Zib twelve miles — a fine fortress on the shore of the salt sea — and from it to al-Nawaqir, three lofty white mountains looking over the seashore; and from the midst of al-Nawaqir to the city of al-Iskandariyya five miles, and from it to the city of Tyre fifteen miles — a fine town on the seashore where ships anchor and set sail, an old fortified place girt by the sea on three sides, with a large suburb. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.365}
