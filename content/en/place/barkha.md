## Overview
Barkha: a city of medium size on the coast of the land of Sufala, according to al-Idrisi. It is inhabited by a mixture of people of India and others, and the ruler of that land resides in it. It lies three days' sailing from Dandama. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.68}
