## Overview
The island of al-Sahab (the Clouds) lies four crossings or more from the island of al-Mawja. It is named so because a white cloud sometimes rises from its side and shades ships, and from the cloud a thin, long tongue comes down with the raging wind until it touches the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 90}

## The phenomenon
The sea boils and churns at that tongue like a terrible whirlwind, and if it reaches a ship it swallows it; then the cloud rises and rains a rain with sea debris in it. Al-Idrisi says he does not know whether the cloud drew that up from the sea, or how it comes about. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 90}

## The land
It is an island with hills of sand. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 90} Before it he mentions two islands counted to China, one called Busa and the other Lasiya, with a pale-complexioned people who sometimes raid travellers in fast-sailing ships when they are at odds with the Chinese. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 90}
