## Overview
Mirka is a city on the coast in the land of Barbara, where its people grow maize on the bank of the Nile. It lies at varying distances by land and sea from al-Naja and from Khaqun, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.48}
