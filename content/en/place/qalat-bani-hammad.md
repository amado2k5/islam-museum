## Overview
The fortress, known as the Fortress of the Banu Hammad, is among the largest lands in extent and the richest in wealth, according to al-Idrisi. Its wall encircles the mountain of Tiqart, and on its summit stands a high fortress, in which there are palaces and fine dwellings. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.255}
