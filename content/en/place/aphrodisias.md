## Overview
Pleiades records Aphrodisias as an ancient Greek city in Caria, noted above all for the state of its archaeological preservation; its directory entry places it near Geyre in Turkey. {cite:pleiades 638753}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Aphrodisias» (Pleiades no. 638753), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 550 BCE to 2099 CE. Among the names it records are «Aphrodisias» (Ἀφροδισιάς), «Ninoe» and «Stauropolis». Its description calls it a Greek city of Caria, notable for the state of its archaeological preservation, and notes that the site was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in July 2017. {cite:pleiades 638753}
