## Overview
Tlemcen is an Algerian city, which was the capital of the Banu Zayyan kingdom (1235–1554 CE) and a centre of learning in the Maghrib. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} This description needs an additional source.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Pomaria» (Pleiades no. 285501), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 1999 CE. Recorded names include Pomaria, Tilimsan and Tlemcen. Its description only points to the Barrington Atlas map 29 D2 for the ancient place. {cite:pleiades 285501}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer holds this place as record 2764518, typed as an administrative unit and a populated place, with the preferred name «Tlemcen» and the parent «Tlemcen (Wilayat)» in its hierarchy. Recorded names include Pomaria (Latin), Tilimsan (Polish) and Tlemcen (Norwegian). {cite:idai-gazetteer 2764518}
