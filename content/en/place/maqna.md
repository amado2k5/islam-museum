## Overview
Maqna is a place on the coast of the Syrian desert, inhabited by the Jewish Banu Habiba. Its people hunt with al-'uruk, and its women spin. The Prophet made peace with its people during the expedition to [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]], on a quarter of the catch of al-'uruk, a quarter of the spinning and a quarter of the fruit. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.80-81}

A letter of security and protection was written for them, said to be in the hand of Ali in the year 9 AH. The two editors of the edition doubt its attribution, because Ali was not with the Prophet on the expedition of Tabuk. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.80-81}
