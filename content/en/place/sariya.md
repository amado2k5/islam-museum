## Summary
Sariya is a town in Tabaristan. Yazid ibn al-Muhallab raided it along with Jilan on his way to Dihistan, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri places Sariya in the account of Yazid ibn al-Muhallab's governorship of Khurasan. When Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik appointed Yazid over Iraq, Yazid went to Khurasan because of the dispute between Qutayba ibn Muslim and Sulayman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469}

On the way he met Sawl the Turk, and wrote to Sulayman asking leave to campaign against him; Sulayman agreed. Then, in al-Baladhuri's words, he "raided Jilan and Sariya, and then came to Dihistan, where Sawl was." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469} He says that the large army besieging Dihistan included men from the two garrison cities, Basra and Kufa, and from Syria and Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469}

Sawl then offered terms: safety for himself, his property and his household, in return for the city, its people and its goods. Yazid accepted and kept the terms. Al-Baladhuri adds that Yazid killed fourteen thousand Turks and left a deputy in the town. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469}

The passage does not describe Sariya's markets, fields or buildings. It records the town only as a place Yazid raided on his march. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.469}
