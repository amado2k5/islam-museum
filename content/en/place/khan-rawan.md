## Summary
Khan Rawan is a flourishing, inhabited city with briskly selling goods, fine buildings, spacious thoroughfares and well-made forms, lying between Nishapur and Tus, one stage north of Nishapur. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says: from Nishapur to Khan Rawan is one stage northward, and from Khan Rawan to Mihrajan one stage {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}. He counts it among the towns of Tus that possess minbars, together with al-Raykan, Tabaran and Targhuz {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}. In his description: the city of Khan Rawan is a flourishing, inhabited city with briskly selling goods, handsome buildings, spacious thoroughfares and well-proportioned forms {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}. Tus itself is a large city of fine buildings, many markets, ample sustenance, populous quarters and fresh surroundings {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}. From Khan Rawan to Mihrajan is one stage; Mihrajan is a populous city of dwellings and markets abounding in good things, with an earthen wall and a thriving suburb, its people drinking from water brought to it from afar {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}.
