## Overview
Khan Rawan is a populous, established city with busy markets, between [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]] and [[place/tus|Tus]]. It lies one stage north of Nishapur, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.692}
