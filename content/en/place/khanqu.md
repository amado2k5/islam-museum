## Overview
Khanqu is a city of merchants and wealth in China, according to Abu Dulaf al-Yanbu'i, and it is forty farsakhs long. It is said that China has three hundred cities, all flourishing, and over every fifty cities a king appointed by the Baghbur. Among its cities are Waranas and Banasa, which join the land of Tibet, the Turks and the Taghzghaz. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.492}
