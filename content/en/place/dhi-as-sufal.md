## Overview
Dhi as Sufal is recorded in the German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer as a populated place at about 13.83° N, 44.11° E, at an altitude of 1,857 metres. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2773916}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2773916) records this place under the preferred name «Dhī as Sufāl», typed as a populated place, with the further names «Dhī Sufāl» [Arabic], «Dhi as-Sufal» [Arabic], «ذي السفال» [Arabic], «ذي سفال» [Arabic]. Its parent unit is «ʾIbb (muḥāfaẓāt)», an administrative unit. The record carries the identifiers GeoNames 76154. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2773916}
