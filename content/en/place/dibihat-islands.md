## Overview
A group of islands with many people, where coconut palms and sugar cane are abundant. They trade in cowrie shells, make clothes and tools with their hands, and turtles lay their eggs on their land. al-Idrisi reports that its people are Magians. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.70}
