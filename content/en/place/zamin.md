## Overview
Zamin is a city which al-Idrisi describes as populous in its region and full of people, fortified and with a wall. It lies twenty-seven miles from Kharqana. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505}

## Details
Al-Idrisi places Zamin on the road to Farghana from Samarqand. It is a city of full land and many people, strong, with a wall and a ditch, and it has abundant livelihoods. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505}

His distances place it among the towns of Transoxiana. From Kharqana to Zamin is twenty-seven miles, and Kharqana is also twenty-seven miles from Samarqand. From Kharqana to Dizak is fifteen miles, and Dizak lies in the plain. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505} From Zamin to Kirkit is thirty-nine miles, on the left of the traveller going to Farghana. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505}

The passage therefore shows Zamin as a stopping point on the road that runs from Samarqand towards Farghana. Idrisi gives its position through these distances and adds no further description. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.505}
