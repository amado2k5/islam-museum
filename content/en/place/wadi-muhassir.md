## Overview
Wadi Muhassir is a valley between [[place/mina|Mina]] and [[place/muzdalifa|Muzdalifa]]. Al-Azraqi mentions that the distance from the Jamrat al-Aqaba to Wadi Muhassir is seven thousand two hundred cubits. {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka p.789}

He relates that [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] said: Pass beyond Muhassir, meaning do not descend to Muhassir without reaching it. He mentions that the horn (qarn) next to its wall ends at Muhassir. {cite:azraqi-akhbar-makka p.802}
