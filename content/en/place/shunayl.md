## Overview
Shunayl is the river of snow, flowing south of [[place/granada|Granada]], and it begins in Jabal Shulayr, the snow mountain. That mountain is two days long and very high, with snow on it always, in winter and in summer. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 569}

## Course and towns
Granada itself is divided by another river called Hadarru, with the Shunayl to its south. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 569} [[place/istija|Istija]] stands on the river of Granada called Shunayl, a fine city with a bridge of wonderful construction in hewn rock, thriving markets and standing trade, tangled orchards and gardens and bright pleasure grounds. From Istija to [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] is thirty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 572}

## The mountain
Al-Idrisi says that Granada and Wadi Ash lie north of the mountain, and that the mountain's southern face looks out on the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 570}

The earlier data summary described Shunayl as a town with a bridge, which is al-Idrisi's description of Istija; the page makes Shunayl a river, and the summary has been corrected.
