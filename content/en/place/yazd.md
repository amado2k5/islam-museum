## Overview
Yazd is a populous, cheap city, thirty miles east of [[place/kath|Kath]], at the head of a desert connected to the road of Khurasan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.430}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Yazd» (Pleiades no. 29811), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 1200 BCE to 2000 CE. Names recorded there include Yazd, Yazd, Yezd, Yazd, Yezd. {cite:pleiades 29811}
