## Overview
Shakar is a mountain belonging to the people of [[place/jurash|Jarash]]. At it [[person/surad-ibn-abdullah-al-azdi|Surad ibn Abd Allah]] turned upon those among them who sought him, and killed them severely. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}

## Its name
After the afternoon prayer, the Prophet asked the two envoys of Jarash: In which of God's lands is Shukr? They said: In our land there is a mountain called Kashr, and the people of Jarash call it that too. He said: It is not Kashr, but Shukr. He also said that the sacrificial camels (budn) of God were being slaughtered there at that very moment. The two envoys found that their people had been struck on the very day and hour he had mentioned. See [[event/surad-campaign-against-jurash|The campaign of Surad against Jarash]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Shakar as a mountain in Yemen near Jurash, mentioned in the accounts of the campaigns. Surad ibn Abdullah al-Azdi struck the people of Jurash there, after he had come to the Prophet, who sent him to them and was not obeyed. Citing Nasr, Yaqut relates that the Prophet once asked in which of God's lands Shakar lay; told it was at such a place, he said that God's sacrificial camels were being slaughtered there at that moment. Men from that place were present, and when they returned they found their people killed that same day, which Yaqut takes to be the day of Surad's attack. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.357}
