## Overview
Nafta is a small city with markets, trade, palms, crops and running water. It lies two stages from [[place/qafsa|Qafsa]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.278}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute records this place as no. 2771890, of type populated place, with the preferred name «Nefta», placed in its hierarchy under «Tozeur (Gouvernorat)». Recorded names include Nafṭa, Nafţah and Netta. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2771890}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut describes Nafta as a city in Ifriqiya in the districts of the Great Zab, whose people were Ibadi and Wahbi Kharijites in revolt. He puts it one stage from the city of Tozeur, one stage from the city of Nafzawa and two stages from Qafsa, which agrees with al-Idrisi's statement that it is two stages from Qafsa. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.296}

Among its natives he names Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Qasim al-Nafti, known as Ibn al-Sa'igh. He heard hadith in the Maghrib from the hafiz Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Sadafi and from the jurist and qadi Abu Abd Allah ibn Shirin, among others, then travelled to Iraq and heard from Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Marzuq al-Za'farani and from Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Tarkhan al-Turki. Yaqut reports that the hafiz Abu al-Qasim said he stayed in Damascus for a time and then set out for Egypt, heading for his home, and that he gave him a licence for everything he had heard, in Rabi I of 518. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.296}

## The museum's identification
This record is the Nafta of Ifriqiya, near Tozeur, the one Yaqut describes in volume 5; al-Idrisi, the record's main source, also puts it two stages from Qafsa, so the distance is the same in both. In volume 2, in the entry for al-Jubba, the name Abu Bakr ibn Nafta appears as a narrator; it is a personal name, not a place, and has no bearing on this record.
