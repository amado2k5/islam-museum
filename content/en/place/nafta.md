## Overview
Nafta is a small city with markets, trade, palms, crops and running water. It lies two stages from [[place/qafsa|Qafsa]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.278}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute records this place as no. 2771890, of type populated place, with the preferred name «Nefta», placed in its hierarchy under «Tozeur (Gouvernorat)». Recorded names include Nafṭa, Nafţah and Netta. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2771890}
