## Summary
Aylughis is a town set on a mountain which al-Idrisi places twelve miles from the Pontic gulf, east of the city of Bizwi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Bizwi eastward to the town of Aylughis is fifty miles, and that Aylughis is a town on a mountain twelve miles from the Pontic gulf {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. He describes Bizwi before it as a large, flourishing city with many markets whose people keep horses and have continuous farms, adjoining districts and numerous domains; and he notes that from Bizwi to the city of Qalimalaya is fifty-five miles and to the city of Faruy westward ninety miles, with pasture, fertility, perpetual springtime and sweet running water between them {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.895}. Aylughis is thus a mountain town close to the shore of the Pontic gulf.
