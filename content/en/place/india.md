## Overview
India, al-Hind to the Muslim geographers, is the Indian subcontinent; the museum records it as a region with an approximate central point at about 21 degrees north and 78 degrees east. {cite:geonames-gazetteer} {cite:wikidata} This page does not yet carry an account of India itself from a source read for this run, so the approximate geographic entry is all the museum's records support at present.
