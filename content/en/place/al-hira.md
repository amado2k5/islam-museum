## Overview
Al-Hira: the capital of the Manadhira near Kufa; seat of the Arab kings who were Persia's wardens on the desert marches for three and a half centuries.

## The place
In Ardashir's era al-Hira was the seat of the Arab kings subject to Persia; its king kept a battalion of Persian soldiers with him to aid him against desert Arabs who rebelled against his authority {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

Lakhm's kings succeeded one another there from Amr ibn Adi until the last of them, al-Nu'man called al-Ma'rur, in 632 CE {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

## For young visitors
Al-Hira is an old Arab city near Kufa; kings ruled it in service of Persia's kings, guarding their frontier.
