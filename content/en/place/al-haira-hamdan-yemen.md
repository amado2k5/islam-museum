## Overview
Al-Ha'ira is a town of Hamdan where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed two days with hardly any water or fodder, until the offenders were brought, bound in iron and taken to [[place/khiwan|Khaywan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} The narrator says God put blessing in the town's small supply of water while al-Hadi stayed, a marvel he ascribes to the imam and the museum does not affirm. It has no coordinates. See [[event/al-hadi-at-al-haira-seizes-the-offenders-and-goes-by-khiwan-to-athafit-288]].
