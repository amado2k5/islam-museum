## Overview
Shama is a small city in the land of the Zaghawa, resembling a large village, according to al-Idrisi. Most of its people moved to Kuku, sixteen stages away. They drink milk, and he mentions that skin disease is widespread there. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.110}
