## Overview
Pleiades describes Thermopylae as a narrow pass leading from Thessaly into Locris, the site of a pivotal battle of the Graeco-Persian wars in 480 BCE. {cite:pleiades 541140}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, it lists this place under the title «Thermopylae» (Pleiades no. 541140), classed as a fort and a pass. Names recorded there include «Thermopylae» (30 BCE to 300 CE), «Thermopýlai» in Greek script «Θερμοπύλαι» (550 BCE to 300 CE) and «Thermopilai» (1700 to 2100 CE). {cite:pleiades 541140}
