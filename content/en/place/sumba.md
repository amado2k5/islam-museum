## Overview
Sumba is an island rich in crops and grain, with many birds and coconuts. It is connected to another populated island ruled by a king named Qamrun, and between the two islands there are two channels, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.88}
