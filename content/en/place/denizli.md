## Overview
Denizli is a city; Pleiades records the place as both an ancient and a modern settlement. {cite:pleiades 755967974}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Denizli» (Pleiades no. 755967974), classed as a settlement and a modern settlement, with activity attested from 1750 BCE to 2099 CE. Its description says only that it is a place from the TAVO Index, the index of the Tübingen Atlas of the Near East. Names recorded there include «Denizli» in Turkish, «Ladiḳ» in Ottoman Turkish from 1300 to 1453 CE, and «Dar-i Ma» from 1453 to 1683 CE. {cite:pleiades 755967974}
