## Overview
Thénia is held in the museum as a Roman and late-antique estate site known to the Pleiades gazetteer, which places it at about 36.72° N, 3.56° E. {cite:pleiades 295355}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Thénia» (Pleiades no. 295355), classed as a villa, that is a Roman rural estate, with a location attested from 30 BCE to 640 CE. {cite:pleiades 295355} Pleiades describes it as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas of the Greek and Roman World, map 30, grid G3. {cite:pleiades 295355}
