## Overview
Jabal al-Nur is a mountain about two miles from [[place/mecca|Mecca]], on which is the [[place/ghar-hira|Cave of Hira]], where the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] used to spend the month of Ramadan in seclusion before his mission. The first revelation came down to him there ([[event/first-revelation|the beginning of the revelation]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.65} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 1/47}.

## What is not covered here
The book does not describe the mountain, its height or the reason for its name. What the books of places say, that its old name was Hira, needs a source that can be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Jabal al-Nur is a mountain near Mecca, with the Cave of Hira at its top, where the Prophet used to worship.
