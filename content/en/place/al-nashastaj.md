## Overview
al-Nashastaj: an estate in the countryside of Iraq. al-Baladhuri reports that Uthman ibn Affan granted it to Talha ibn Ubayd Allah from the lands seized from Kisra and the land of the exiles. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}
