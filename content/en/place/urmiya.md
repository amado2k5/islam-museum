## Overview
Urmiya is an ancient city in Azerbaijan. The Magians claim that Zardusht, their companion, was from it, according to al-Baladhuri. Its people fought Sadaqa ibn Ali ibn Sadaqa ibn Dinar, client of the Azd, until he entered it and prevailed over it. He and his brothers built palaces in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
