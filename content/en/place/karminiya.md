## Overview
Karminiya is a land beyond the river (Transoxiana). [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] raided it with Tumashkat in 88 AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
