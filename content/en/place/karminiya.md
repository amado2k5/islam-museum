## Summary
Karminiya, a town beyond the river, campaigned by [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] together with Tumushkat in 88 AH, in al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 591}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Qutayba ibn Muslim campaigned against Paykand in 87 AH with Nizak, crossing the river from Zamm to Paykand, the nearest of Bukhara's towns to the river; its people broke faith and called on Sughd, so he fought them, raided them and besieged them until they asked for terms and he took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 591} In 88 AH Qutayba campaigned against Tumushkat and Karminiya, leaving his brother Bishr ibn Muslim over Marw, and he dealt with them and took small fortresses. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 591} Karminiya is thus, in his account, one of the Transoxanian lands Qutayba campaigned in during 88 AH, among the conquests between Paykand and Bukhara, while his brother Salih would succeed him over Marw whenever he set out on campaign.
