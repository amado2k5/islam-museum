## Overview
Anfa: a harbour on the coast of the Maghrib, about forty miles from Fadala. Wheat, barley and sheep are exported from it, and the Berbers of Banu Yafarn and Dakkala come to it, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.240}
