## Overview
Lake Khwarazm: a lake surrounded by wastelands. al-Idrisi reports that its circuit is about three hundred miles, that rivers flow into it, and that it lies about eighteen stages from the sea. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.699}
