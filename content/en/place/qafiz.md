## Overview
Qafiz is a palace in the middle of a plain (wata'), and to its east is a lake about half a mile long. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies four miles from the sea, and that there are the tribes of Ruwaha in it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 315}
