## Overview
Ubeda is a small city near the great river, lying seven miles east of Baiyasa. It has many farms and abundant wheat and barley crops, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.569} It may be the same city mentioned as [[place/ubda|Ubda]], which needs an additional source.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Salaria?» (Pleiades no. 266022), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 2100 CE; recorded names include «Salaria», «Col. Salariensis», «Úbeda». In its description, Pleiades describes it only as an ancient place cited in the Barrington Atlas (map 27, B4) under the query-marked name Salaria. {cite:pleiades 266022}
