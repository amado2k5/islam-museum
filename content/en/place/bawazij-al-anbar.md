## Summary
In al-Baladhuri: villages taken by Jarir ibn Abdallah al-Bajali, housing people of Banu Taghlib and of Shayban. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344}

## Details
Shaykhs of the people of al-Anbar told al-Baladhuri that they made peace in the caliphate of Umar ibn al-Khattab for their tassujs, on four hundred thousand dirhams and a thousand Qatwani cloaks each year, and that the peace was conducted by Jarir ibn Abdallah al-Bajali — some say he settled with them on eighty thousand, and God knows best. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344} They added that Jarir took Bawazij al-Anbar, where there were people of Banu Taghlib and of Shayban. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 344} In their report, then, Bawazij al-Anbar is a cluster of villages in the outskirts of al-Anbar that fell within Jarir's conquest in Umar's days; its inhabitants were a mix of Taghlibi and Shaybani clans, in the wider account of the villages and waterways Jarir opened around the Euphrates.
