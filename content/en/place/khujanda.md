## Overview
Khujanda is a town beyond the river, on the Syr Darya. Salm ibn Ziyad, while he was in Sughd, sent an army to it with al-A'sha of Hamadan in it, and the army was routed there, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
