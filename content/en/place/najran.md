## Summary
A great town seven stages from Mecca toward Yemen; the sira sources know it for its seventy-three villages and a Christian community whose delegation came to Medina in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}.

## The place's account
Al-Mubarakpuri describes Najran as a great town comprising seventy-three villages — a day's journey for a swift rider — able to field a hundred thousand fighters on the Christian religion {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}. Its people came as a delegation in 9 AH, sixty men among them twenty-four nobles led by the 'Aqib, the Sayyid and the Bishop {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}. The Prophet made peace with them for the jizya, under God's covenant and His messenger's covenant, with full freedom in their religion; Islam thereafter spread among them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 451}.

## For young visitors
An ancient city in the peninsula's south whose people were Christians; a delegation of them came to the Prophet and made peace with him.
