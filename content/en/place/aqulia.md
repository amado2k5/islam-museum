## Summary
Aqulia was a city in the land of Isqaluniya which al-Idrisi places southwest of the city of Ifrank Bila in the Hungarian country. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.886}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from the city of Titlus southward to the city of Ifrank Bila, and from Ifrank Bila to the city of Qaun a hundred miles, and from Ifrank Bila again west-southwest to the city of Aqulia in the land of Isqaluniya {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.886}. He notes that the people of this whole region, the land of Hungary, are folk of tillage, earning and building, with districts and populated villages, bordering the land of Isqaluniya most of which in his day the Venetians had overpowered and ruled {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.886}.
