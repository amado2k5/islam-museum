## Overview
al-Hijr: a place where the Prophet and the Muslims stopped on their way to Tabuk. They drew water from its well, and then they were forbidden to drink from it.

## Location
Its approximate coordinates are 26.8142 N and 37.9475 E {cite:geonames-gazetteer}.
