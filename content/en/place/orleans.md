## Overview
Arliyansh, which is Orléans, is a city on a river. Al-Idrisi mentions that it is among the cities of France: populous and civilised, with houses, farms and crops. It lies seventy miles from Tarash. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 862}
