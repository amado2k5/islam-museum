## Summary
Marib was the second capital of [[polity/kingdom-of-saba|Saba]], after [[place/sirwah|Sirwah]]. The kings of Saba made it their capital in the period that The Sealed Nectar dates from 650 BCE to 115 BCE. Its ruins lie sixty miles from [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]], on its eastern side {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 4/195-198}.

## The dam
Beside it is [[building/marib-dam|the Marib Dam]]. Its construction began in the time of the Mukarrib kings of Sirwah, and it has great importance in the history of Yemen. It burst in 450 or 451 CE, which became [[event/flood-of-al-aram|the Flood of al-Arim]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.23} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24}. Sirwah lies a day's journey to the west of Marib {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.23}.

## After Saba
When [[group/himyar|Himyar]] prevailed over the kingdom of Saba, it made Raydan its capital instead of Marib {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24}.

## For young visitors
Marib is an ancient city of Yemen. Beside it was a famous dam that broke; the water flowed out and scattered the people.
