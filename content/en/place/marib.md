## Overview
Ma'rib: Saba''s second capital after Sirwah, and centre of the dam whose bursting was the flood of al-Arim.

## The place
Saba''s kings made Ma'rib their capital in place of Sirwah in the era from 650 BCE to 115 BCE; its ruins lie sixty miles from Sanaa on its eastern side {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

The building of the [[building/marib-dam|Ma'rib dam]] began in the Mukarribs' time at Sirwah, and it has a great place in Yemen's history — until it burst in a mighty breach around 450 CE, which was the flood of al-Arim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Ma'rib is Yemen's ancient city; beside it stood a famous dam that broke, the water flooded, and the people scattered.
