## Overview
Zaghul is a town of [[place/marw-al-rudh|Marw al-Rudh]] in al-Shusha. [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|Al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] died there, returning from the raid on [[place/kish|Kish]] and [[place/nasaf|Nasaf]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The beginning of his illness was grief for his son [[person/al-mughira-ibn-al-muhallab|al-Mughira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
