## Overview
Zaghul is a town of [[place/marw-al-rudh|Marw al-Rudh]] in al-Shusha. [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|Al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] died there, returning from the raid on [[place/kish|Kish]] and [[place/nasaf|Nasaf]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The beginning of his illness was grief for his son [[person/al-mughira-ibn-al-muhallab|al-Mughira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Zaghul as a village in the district of Marw al-Rudh, where al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra, governor of Khurasan, is buried. Al-Muhallab had finished with the Azariqa when Abd al-Malik gave him command of Khurasan, and he returned there in the year 76 AH and stayed until he died at the village of Zaghul; he had set out on a campaign in Dhu al-Hijja 82 AH, aged seventy-six. His term in Khurasan, together with that of his son Habib, lasted seven years. He also mentions a different village called Zaghuni, which he thinks was near Baghdad, from which Ahmad ibn al-Hajjaj ibn Asim al-Zaghuni, a transmitter from Ahmad ibn Hanbal, took his nisba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.126}
