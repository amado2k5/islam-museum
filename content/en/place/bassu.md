## Summary
Bassu is a city of the land of Bwamiya which al-Idrisi places eighty miles west-southwest of the city of Aqra. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.885}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Aqra westward with a southern bearing to the city of Bassu is eighty miles, that Bassu belongs to the land of Bwamiya, and that from Bassu to the city of Mishla, also read Mashla, is a hundred and fifty miles, Mishla being a fine city rich in farms and fruits, broad in districts and quarters, with continuous buildings, strong walls and busy markets {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.885}. He also says elsewhere that from Astarkuna eastward to Bassu is forty miles and that Bassu is a large city he would describe in its place {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.878}. Bassu is thus one of the greater cities of Bwamiya on its branching roads toward Aqra, Mishla and Astarkuna.
