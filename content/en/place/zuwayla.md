## Overview
Zuwayla is a city north of Wadan. Al-Idrisi attributes it to a man who built it and settled in it with his cousins. He mentions that this was in the year 306 AH, and that in his time it was populous. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 115} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 116}
