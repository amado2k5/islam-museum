## Overview
Ayn al-Warda is the name Yaqut gives to Ra's al-Ayn, the well-known city of the Jazira. He records it in the Mu'jam al-buldan and mentions a fight of the Arabs there. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.180}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut explains the name as the singular of "ward" (the scented flower), noting that "ward" is also a colour of animals tending to a pleasing yellow. He then says it is Ra's al-Ayn, the well-known city in the Jazira. He says a battle of the Arabs and one of their "days" took place there, with Rifa'a ibn Shaddad ibn Abdullah ibn Qays ibn Ju'al ibn Badda ibn Futyan as one of their chiefs at the time. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.180}

Yaqut gives neither the date of the fight nor the parties in this entry, so the entry alone does not settle anything about the battle this record relates elsewhere. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.180}
