## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives Tripoli in the Levant a short entry that records only its place in the astronomical tables: it lies in the fourth climate, at a longitude of sixty degrees thirty-five minutes and a latitude of thirty-four degrees. The longer entry just before it, on the western Tripoli, treats the other city of the name. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.26}
