## Overview
Madinat al-Mubarak is a fortress built by al-Mubarak al-Turki, client of al-Hadi, in [[place/qazwin|Qazwin]]. Some of his clients lived there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut says that Madinat al-Mubarak is at Qazwin. It was founded by al-Mubarak al-Turki, and a group of his clients (mawali) lived there. Yaqut thinks that al-Mubarak was a client of al-Mu'tasim or al-Ma'mun. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.79}

The town gives its nisba to Abu Ya'qub Yusuf ibn Hamdan al-Zamin al-Madini. Yaqut quotes al-Khalil ibn Abd Allah al-Qazwini, who reported through his son Waqid that Abu Ya'qub lived in Madinat al-Mubarak and died in 303 AH. The history of Qazwin, Yaqut adds, gives 299 AH. He heard from Abu Hajar and Muhammad ibn Humayd al-Razi, and Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Mahruyah related from him. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.79}
