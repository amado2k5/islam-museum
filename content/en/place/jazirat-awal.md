## Overview
Awal is a fine island with a large town, palms and farmland, and many springs, among them the spring of Bu Zaydan and the spring of Ghadhar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.387}

Al-Idrisi says that it has an independent amir of its own, and that the chiefs of the divers come to it for pearls from every region. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.387}
