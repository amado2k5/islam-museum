## Overview
Marrakesh is a Moroccan city founded by the Almoravids as their capital around 1070 CE. Ibn Khaldun mentions that [[person/yusuf-ibn-tashfin|Yusuf ibn Tashfin]] laid out Marrakesh in 454 AH and settled there, making it the seat of his residence and his army. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1646}
