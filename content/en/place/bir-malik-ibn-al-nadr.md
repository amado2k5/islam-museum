## Overview
The well of Malik ibn al-Nadr: a well in [[place/medina|Medina]], known as the well of Anas. [[person/abu-ayyub-al-ansari|Abu Ayyub]] used to draw sweet water from it for the Prophet when he stayed with him. When the Prophet went to his house, Anas, Hind and Haritha ibn Asma al-Aslami carried pots of water from it to the houses of his wives, from the houses of al-Suqya. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.536}
