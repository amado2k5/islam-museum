## Overview
Sabur is a city in Fars. Uthman ibn Abi al-As reached it in the year 23 AH, or it is said 24 AH, before Abu Musa al-Ash'ari took over Basra from Uthman ibn Affan. Al-Baladhuri reports that its people stood in awe of the Muslims, and that a brother of Shahrak saw in a dream an Arab man strip him of his shirt. The people of the city then made peace with Uthman in exchange for safety and immediate tribute. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.547} They later broke the covenant, and Abu Musa took the city by force in the year 26 AH, with Uthman in the vanguard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.547}

In the neighbouring accounts, Shahrak was killed at the battle of Rashahr, in the land of Sabur near Tawwaj, by Sawwar ibn Hammam al-Abdi, and the day was a great one for the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.545} After that Uthman took Kazarun from Sabur and gained control of its land, and he did the same with Nubandajan, also from Sabur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.546}
