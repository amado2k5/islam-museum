## Overview
Qudayd is a place in the Hijaz where episodes of the Prophet's biography occurred, according to al-Mubarakfuri. The water of al-Muraysi', which belonged to the Banu al-Mustaliq, lay in the district of Qudayd toward the coast. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 217}

The battle of the Banu al-Mustaliq took place in Sha'ban of the sixth year after the Hijra. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 216} When the Prophet reached al-Muraysi', the two sides prepared for battle. The Muslims then charged as one man and the enemy fled. Women, children, herds and flocks were taken as captives, and only one Muslim was killed, by a man of the Ansar who mistook him for an enemy. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 217}

In the seventh year after the Hijra the Prophet sent the expedition of Ghalib ibn 'Abd Allah al-Laythi to the Banu al-Mulawwah at Qudayd, in Safar or Rabi' I, to avenge the companions of Bashir ibn Suwayd whom the Banu al-Mulawwah had killed. The Muslims raided by night and drove off the camels, but a large enemy force pursued them, and a heavy rain and flood came between the two sides, so the Muslims withdrew successfully. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 254}

Among the expeditions after the conquest of Mecca, Sa'd ibn Zayd al-Ashhali was sent with twenty horsemen to Manat, which stood at al-Mushallal near Qudayd and was held by the Aws, the Khazraj, Ghassan and others. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 274}

The geographer al-Bakri describes the mountain of Jumdan, in the Hijaz between Qudayd and 'Usfan, among the dwelling places of the Banu Aslam. {cite:mujam-ma-istaajam-bakri p. 256}
