## Overview
Ghaghan is a city on a lake that bears its name. It has a workshop for making silk garments, and Turkish merchants come from it with much merchandise. It lies six stages from Qarantiya, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.708}
