## Overview
A fine town at the top of a mountain, which al-Idrisi counts among the most secure peaks of al-Jazira. It has luxuriant crops, and near it is the salt valley. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.612}

It lies about a hundred miles from Karkanit. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.612}
