## Overview
Dania: a city with a populous suburb and a strong wall, whose wall is built on the sea with skill. Ships are built in it and the fleet sails out from it, and to its south lies a great mountain, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.557}
