## Overview
Fazzan is a land next to the land of the Zaghawa, with towns such as Jurma and Tasawa. Al-Idrisi reports that its people rely on wells and palms, and that travellers cross it on the merchants' road. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.112} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.109}
