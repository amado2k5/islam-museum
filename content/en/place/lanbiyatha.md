## Overview
Lanbiyatha is a fortress on the top of a rock, surrounded by the sea on three sides. It has a harbour from which ships sail, and in it are buildings and a market, and its land is fine. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.599}

It lies twenty-five miles from Kirkant, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.599}
