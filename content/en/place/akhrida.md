## Overview
Akhrida (Ohrid): a noble town with many buildings, spacious and pleasant, as al-Idrisi describes it. Near it is a large lake where fish are caught. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 792}

## Location
It lies one day's journey from Bulgo. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 792}
