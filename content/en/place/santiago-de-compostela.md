## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name Shant Yaqub and spells out its letters, a ya, then after the alif a qaf with a damma and a ba, and says only that it is a strongly fortified castle in al-Andalus. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.368}
