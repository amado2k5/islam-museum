## Overview
Baghah: a city of great fineness because of its many waters. Water runs through its gardens, and it has vineyards and trees beyond compare. It lies eighteen miles from the fortress known as al-Qabdhaq. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.571}
