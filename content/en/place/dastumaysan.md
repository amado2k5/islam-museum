## Overview
Dastumaysan: a district of the districts of the Tigris. al-Baladhuri reports that its people gathered against the Muslims, but Utba ibn Ghazwan anticipated them with a raid, God defeated them, and he killed their landowners. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
