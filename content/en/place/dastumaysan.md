## Summary
Dastumaysan was a district (kura) of the Tigris districts in the Basra region. Al-Baladhuri reports that its people mustered against the Muslims, so 'Utba ibn Ghazwan struck them first; God routed them and their dihqans were killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 478}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that 'Utba ibn Ghazwan, after conquering al-Ubulla, al-Furat and Abar Qubadh, marched on Dastumaysan whose people had gathered intending to move against the Muslims; he resolved to attack first to break their strength and take heart, met them, and God defeated them and their dihqans were slain {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 478}. Listing 'Utba's conquests — al-Ubulla, al-Furat, Abar Qubadh and Dastumaysan — he cites al-Mada'ini that people used to call Maysan, Dastumaysan, al-Furat and Abar Qubadh all together "Maysan" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 480}. Among the captives of Maysan were Abu al-Hasan al-Basri and his brother Sa'id ibn Yasar {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 480}. He also notes that the post-road once ran to Maysan, Dastumaysan and al-Ahwaz before the Bata'ih marshes formed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 411}. Al-Mughira later conquered Maysan after its dihqan apostatised, meeting and killing him at al-Mana'rij {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 479}.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the name as Dastumisan and calls it a notable district between Wasit, Basra and Ahwaz, nearer to Ahwaz, with Basamta as its chief town. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.455} He adds that it is not Maysan but adjoins it. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.455} He also records another view, that its chief town was al-Ubulla, which would make Basra part of this district. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.455}
