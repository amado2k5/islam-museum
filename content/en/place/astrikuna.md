## Overview
Astrikuna: the capital of the land of Bawamiyya, the greatest and widest of its cities in building and the most populous, according to al-Idrisi. He reports that it is the seat of the kingdom and the residence of the kings of Bawamiyya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.877}
