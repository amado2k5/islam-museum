## Summary
Astrikuna was the capital of the land of Bwamiyya (Bohemia), its largest and most prosperous city, and the seat of rule for its kings, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.877}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says the land of Bwamiyya was extensive and its bounty wide; its towns were subject to the authority of the Lamani (Germans), and its most famous city was Astrikuna, described as the greatest in extent, the widest in building, the most populous and the richest in good. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.877} The Lamani collected its taxes and undertook its protection, and in appointing its kings acted as he willed, installing and deposing with none to oppose his command, while Astrikuna was the seat of the kingdom and the pivot of rule for the kings of Bamiyya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.877} From Astrikuna — also read Astraghuna with a ghayn — to the city of Jik Labuka was eighty miles west-northwest, from Shabruna to Astrikuna eighty miles southward, and from Wyana to Astrikuna fifty miles to the south. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.877}
