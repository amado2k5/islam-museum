## Overview
Mihran is the great river of the Sind and the land around it. Al-Baladhuri relates that Muhammad ibn al-Qasim halted in its midst, while [[person/dahir|Dahir]] prepared himself for battle against him. Muhammad devised a way to cross the Mahwan by a bridge he built, and Dahir was killed at evening. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
