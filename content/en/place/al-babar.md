## Overview
al-Babar: a region in the mountains of Daylam, near the Caspian Sea, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The invasion
Kathir ibn Shihab al-Harithi, the governor of al-Rayy, invaded it with al-Tayalisan in the time of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
