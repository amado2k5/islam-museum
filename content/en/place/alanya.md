## Overview
Alanya is a town and district in Antalya Province, Turkey, and this page keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's iDAI.gazetteer (no. 2280263) records this place under the preferred name «Alanya» [German], typed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Antalya (Provinz)», an administrative unit. Further names include «Coracesium»; «Alaia»; «ألانيا» [Arabic]. It carries the identifiers GeoNames 324190, GND 4206264-0. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2280263}
