## Summary
Damghan is the capital of Qumis according to Golius, who quotes Yaqut that it has many springs irrigating about a hundred villages, and that its three towns were famous for apples. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 435}

Golius quotes Sa'id ibn 'Ali al-Jurjani that Damghan is the capital of Qumis and that its longitude is 89 degrees 30 minutes and its latitude 36 degrees 30 minutes, figures that require verification against the page image. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 435}

The city lies in a plain surrounded by mountains, and Yaqut mentions that from one of the mountains issue nearly a hundred springs that irrigate a hundred villages. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 435}

Qumis was famous for its apples, which were exported to Parthia, and it is noted that the apples of Qumis, Damghan, and Baltam (Bastam) are considered among the finest in that region. {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 436}
