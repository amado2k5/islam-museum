## Overview
Ardestan is a city in Isfahan Province, Iran, and the classical Arabic geographers know it. Yaqut al-Hamawi, in the Mu'jam al-buldan, gathers what al-Istakhri says of it: a city between Qashan and Isfahan, eighteen farsakhs from Isfahan, two farsakhs from Azwara and on the edge of the desert of Karkaskuh. It is built with vaulted buildings (azaj) and has large houses and pleasure gardens; it is walled, with a fort in each quarter, and in the middle of one fort stands a fire temple, and Anushirwan is said to have been born in the city; there are also buildings of Anushirwan ibn Qubadh's making. Its people are all followers of ra'y, it has many large districts, and fine cloth is carried from it to the far regions. Yaqut adds that many scholars are named after it, among them the judge Abu Tahir Zayd ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Ardastani, a man of letters and a poet who came to Nishapur. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-yaqut 1/184}
