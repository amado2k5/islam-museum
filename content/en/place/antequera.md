## Overview
Antequera: a city near Archidona, with thirty-five miles between it and Malaga. al-Idrisi reports that the rebels in al-Andalus evacuated it after the rule of Ibn Abi Amir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Antequera» (Pleiades no. 265798), classed as a quarry, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 300 CE. {cite:pleiades 265798}
