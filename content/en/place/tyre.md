## Overview
Tyre is described by Pleiades as the ancient city of Tyre, today es-Sur on the coast of Lebanon, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in part for its important archaeological remains, mainly from Roman times. {cite:pleiades 678437}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Tyrus/Col. Septimia Severa» (Pleiades no. 678437), classed as a settlement, a modern settlement and a findspot, with a time span from 10000 BCE to 2100 CE. Among the names it records are «صور» in Arabic, «Τύρος» in Greek and «Ṣurru» in Akkadian. It describes the place as the ancient city of Tyre, modern es-Sur on the coast of Lebanon. {cite:pleiades 678437}

## In Nomisma
The Nomisma numismatic vocabulary links this entry to the concept «tyre», and its linked collections record 737 coins or coin types that name it as issuer, authority or mint. {cite:nomisma tyre}
