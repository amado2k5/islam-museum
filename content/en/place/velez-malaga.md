## Overview
Velez-Malaga, or Balash Malaqa, is one of the cities east of [[place/malaga|Malaga]]. It joined the call of the captive sultan Abu Abd Allah, and peace was proclaimed in it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.520}

## The last siege
Al-Maqqari relates that the lord of Castile besieged it by land and sea in the second Rabi' of the year 892 AH. The relief army was defeated before engagement, on news of the revolt in Granada. Its people came out under safe conduct on Friday, the tenth of Jumada I, and the lands east of Malaga and the fortress of Qamarish obeyed. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.523}
