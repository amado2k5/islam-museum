## Overview
Hamra al-Asad is a place eight miles south of [[place/medina|Medina]]. The Prophet reached it the day after the battle of Uhud, Sunday 8 Shawwal 3 AH, in pursuit of the retreating army of Quraysh. He camped there on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, then returned to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.285}

Al-Mubarakfuri counts the expedition of [[event/hamra-al-asad|Hamra al-Asad]] as a continuation of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]], not as an independent expedition. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.285}

The coordinates are approximate, based on the distance given in the source, and need an additional source.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut's entry for Hamra' al-Asad is a single sentence: a place eight miles from Medina, to which the Messenger of God came on the day of Uhud, following the polytheists. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.301} The rest of the entry lists other places called al-Hamra', which are not this place: the old city of Labla in al-Andalus on the river Tantis, with springs of alum and vitriol; a fortress in the district of Jerusalem; a spot in what he calls the Fustat of Mudar; several villages of Egypt, among them Hamra' al-Sunbulawayn; and a village of Sanhan in Yemen. His entry on Khakh adds that Rawdat Khakh, a place between the two Harams, lies near Hamra' al-Asad on the Medina side. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.335} He gives no direction from Medina, no day of the month and no length of stay.

## The museum's identification
The record, drawn from al-Mubarakfuri, says eight miles south of Medina, reached the day after Uhud, Sunday 8 Shawwal 3 AH, with three nights' stay. Yaqut agrees on the eight miles and on the pursuit; the direction, the date and the three nights are al-Mubarakfuri's. His "on the day of Uhud" is loose, and he does not say whether he means the following day. The record's point (24.38, 39.56) was itself set from the eight miles, as its coordinate note says, so its distance of about 11 km from the museum's Medina point is no independent test. Eight miles come to about 12 km if the mile is taken as about 1.5 km and about 15 km if it is about 1.9 km; neither source says which mile it means. Yaqut's Khakh note places the same neighbourhood without a number. See also [[event/hamra-al-asad|the expedition of Hamra al-Asad]].
