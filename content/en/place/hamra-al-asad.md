## Overview
Hamra al-Asad is a place eight miles south of [[place/medina|Medina]]. The Prophet reached it the day after the battle of Uhud, Sunday 8 Shawwal 3 AH, in pursuit of the retreating army of Quraysh. He camped there on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, then returned to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.285}

Al-Mubarakfuri counts the expedition of [[event/hamra-al-asad|Hamra al-Asad]] as a continuation of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]], not as an independent expedition. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.285}
