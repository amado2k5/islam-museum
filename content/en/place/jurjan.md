## Overview
Gurgan is a region at the corner of the Khazar Sea. Its king made a peace agreement with [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Said ibn al-As]] for two hundred thousand dirhams in the campaign of Tabaristan in 29 AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut quotes the astronomers' table, which gives Jurjan a longitude of 80 and three-quarter degrees and a latitude of 38 degrees and 15 minutes, and notes that some place it in the fourth climate. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.119}

He calls it a famous and large city between Tabaristan and Khurasan, which writers disagree on counting as one or the other. He reports that Yazid ibn al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra is said to have first built it, that many scholars, men of letters, jurists and traditionists came from it, and that Hamza ibn Yazid al-Sahmi wrote a history of the city. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.119}

Quoting al-Istakhri, he says Jurjan was the largest city of its region, with less rain and dew than Tabaristan and people of dignity and wealth. It had two parts, the town and Kurabadh, divided by a river on which boats could sail, and its silk was carried to every region. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.119}
