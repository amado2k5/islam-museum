## Overview
Gurgan is a region at the corner of the Khazar Sea. Its king made a peace agreement with [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Said ibn al-As]] for two hundred thousand dirhams in the campaign of Tabaristan in 29 AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
