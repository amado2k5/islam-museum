## Overview
A medium-sized city with a wall. Al-Idrisi mentions that in ancient times it was the house of Rustam and the seat of his rule. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.456}

It lies one stage from [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]], on the left of one going to Khurasan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.456}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut records Qarnayn as a village in the district of Nishak in Sijistan. Citing Ahmad ibn Sahl al-Balkhi, he calls it a small city with villages and districts, one stage from Sijistan on the left of the road to Bust and two farsakhs from Sarur. He adds that the Saffarids came from it, the four brothers Ya'qub, Amr, Tahir and Ali, sons of al-Layth, who came to dominate Fars, Khurasan, Sijistan and Kirman. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.333} He relates that Ya'qub began as a coppersmith's servant and rose through fighting the Kharijites, then held Khurasan, Fars, Kirman, Khuzistan and part of Iraq; power passed to his brother Amr, who was captured by Isma'il the Samanid in a war between them. Yaqut says he told their story here, though he usually avoids such accounts, because it is rarely found in a book. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 4 p.334}
