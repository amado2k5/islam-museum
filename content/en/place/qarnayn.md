## Overview
A medium-sized city with a wall. Al-Idrisi mentions that in ancient times it was the house of Rustam and the seat of his rule. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.456}

It lies one stage from [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]], on the left of one going to Khurasan. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.456}
