## Summary
Aqra was a city of the land of Bohemia on one of two rivers that empty into the Danube, listed by al-Idrisi in the Hungarian country. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from the city of Nitrm to the city of Aqra northward is forty miles, and Aqra belongs to the land of Bohemia {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}. From the Bohemian land issue two rivers running westward that empty into the Danube near Buzana; they rise in a mountain called Balwak which divides Bohemia from Polonia, and on these two rivers stand the cities of Aqra and Batsh {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}. From Aqra to the city of Batsh is forty miles between east and north, and from Aqra eastward to the city of Ariniya eighty miles, a small populous city {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.884}.
