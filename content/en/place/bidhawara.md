## Summary
Bidhawara is a city of the land of Qarantara, which al-Idrisi counts among its lands together with Siq Lawsh, Balghrana, Astarkuna, Shabruna, Gharmasiya, Titlus, Nitram, Afrank Bila and Ariniya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.882}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that from the city of Buzana to the city of Bidhawara is sixty miles, that Bidhawara stands on the river Danube, and that between Buzana and Bidhawara the river Darwa flows into the Danube; Buzana is the last of the lands of Qarantara {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883}. He also states that from this same Bidhawara to the city of Nitram northward is seventy miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.883}. Bidhawara is thus a river city of Qarantara, a land adjoining the Venetians' territory and lying between the river Danube and the Wadi Darwa, and al-Idrisi places it among the famous chief towns of that country in the northern part of Europe which he describes in his book {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.882}.
