## Overview
Tokat is a place recorded in the German gazetteer. It did not turn up in the first two volumes of Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan, so this page keeps to what the iDAI.gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2097021 with the preferred name «Tokat», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Tokat (Provinz)» (an administrative unit). Among its recorded names are «Toqat», «Dazimon» and «Tukat» and the Arabic-script form «توقات». It links the place to GeoNames no. 738743, GND no. 4273162-8 and Pleiades no. 857105. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2097021}
