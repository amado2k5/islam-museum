## Overview
Çanakkale is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «Çanakkale (Vilâyeti)». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087930}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2087930 with the preferred name «Çanakkale», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Çanakkale (Vilâyeti)». Among the names it records are «Abydus», «Dardanelles», «شنق قلعة», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 749780 and to Pleiades no. 769548551. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087930}
