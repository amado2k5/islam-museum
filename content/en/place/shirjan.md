## Overview
Shirjan is a city in Kirman. Al-Baladhuri mentions that Mujashi' ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami conquered it by force after days of siege and the sortie of its people's horses. He left a man in it, and then many of its people departed from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} Kirman is covered on [[place/kirman|its own page]].

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut gives the headword Shirjan, with a kasra on the first letter and a jim after the ra, but identifies it only tentatively: he says he takes it to be none other than Sirjan, the chief town of Kirman, and that if it is another place, its identity is hidden from him. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.381} He quotes al-Umrani saying only that Shirjan is a place, and explains that {shir} in Persian means both fresh milk and a lion. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.381}
