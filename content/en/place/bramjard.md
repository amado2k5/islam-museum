## Overview
Bramjard: a place near [[place/istakhr|Istakhr]]. Its people received there Ubayd Allah ibn Mu'tar al-Taymi, the commander of the vanguard of [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Ibn Amir]]. They fought him and killed him, and he was buried in the orchard of Bramjard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
