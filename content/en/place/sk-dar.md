## Overview
Üsküdar is a district of Istanbul on the Asian shore of the Bosporus; older and European sources call it Chrysopolis or Scutari. No Arabic source on it was read for this page, so it keeps to what the German gazetteer records.

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2234942 as a populated place, with the preferred name «Üsküdar». Its parent in the hierarchy is the administrative unit «Istanbul (Provinz)». Among its recorded names are «Chrysopolis», «Scutari» and «Skutari», and a Russian form, and it links the place to GeoNames no. 738329, to Pleiades no. 520995 and to the German National Library authority record 4122696-3. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2234942}
