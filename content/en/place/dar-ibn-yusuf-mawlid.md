## Overview
The house in Mecca where the Prophet was born on the eighth of Rabi al-Awwal, according to al-Mas'udi. He reports that his birth came fifty days after the people of the elephant came to Mecca. Later al-Khayzuran, mother of al-Hadi and al-Rashid, built it as a mosque. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.571}
