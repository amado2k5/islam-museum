## Overview
Salerno is a noble city with populous markets, on the sea shore. Al-Idrisi mentions that it lies about twelve miles from the harbour of Salerno, in the valley of Silsa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.758}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Salernum» (Pleiades no. 433075), classed as settlement, with activity attested from 330 BCE to 640 CE. Among its recorded names are «Castrum Salerni», «Salernon» and «Salerno». Pleiades describes a city of Campania with Osco-Etruscan origins that became a Roman colony in 197 BCE. {cite:pleiades 433075}
