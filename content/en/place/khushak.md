## Overview
Khushak is a land on the road of [[place/sijistan|Sistan]]. [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] made peace with its people after Wazan, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
