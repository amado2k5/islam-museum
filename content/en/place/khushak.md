## Summary
Khushak, a town on the road of [[place/sijistan|Sijistan]], whose people made peace with [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] after Wazan, in al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 558}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri reports that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura set out, cut through Wadi Nasl, then came to Khawash and Quzan Bust and took it by force, marched on to Wazan whose people fled and whom he overran, then marched to Khushak whose people made peace with him, then came to al-Rukhkhaj whose people fought him so he overcame them and took it, then marched to Dabilistan whose people fought him after breaking their pact — he took it and took captives — and came to Kabul whose people had also broken faith, so he took it too. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 558} Khushak is thus, in his telling, a station on Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura's march through the Sijistan lands whose people settled with him without fighting, between towns taken by force like Quzan Bust and lands whose people broke their treaties and had to be retaken. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 558}
