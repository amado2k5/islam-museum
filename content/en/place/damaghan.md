## Overview
Damaghan: the chief city of Qumis. After the peace of Ibn al-Zaynabi, Sulayman ibn Amr al-Dabbi was sent to Qumis. Its people did not refuse, and they opened the gates of Damaghan, meaning that it was opened by peace without fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
