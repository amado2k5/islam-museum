## Overview
Jalula is a place east of the Tigris. Al-Baladhuri reports that the army of Yazdegerd, led by Kharzad the brother of Rustam, took refuge in it, and Hashim ibn Utba defeated them with twelve thousand men at the end of the year 16 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.366-370}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan

Yaqut describes Jalula as a district of the Sawad on the road to Khurasan, seven farsakhs from Khanaqin, with a great river that runs to Ba'qubha and carries ships to Bajisra. There, in the year 16 AH, the Muslims won the well-known battle over the Persians, and it was called Jalula al-Wa'iqa because the dead covered it. He cites Sayf ibn Umar for the very large number of Persian dead that day. He also records a different Jalula, a famous town in Ifriqiya twenty-four miles from Qayrawan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.156}
