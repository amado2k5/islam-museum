## Overview
Jalula is a place east of the Tigris. Al-Baladhuri reports that the army of Yazdegerd, led by Kharzad the brother of Rustam, took refuge in it, and Hashim ibn Utba defeated them with twelve thousand men at the end of the year 16 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.366-370}
