## Overview
Uriyula (Orihuela) is a city on the bank of the White River, which is both its river and that of Murcia, with its wall on the west along the river's course and a bridge. It has a citadel of extreme strength on a mountain top, gardens, orchards and nearby meadows, fruit past counting, general cheapness, markets and estates. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 557} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 558}

## Distances
Between Uriyula and the sea is twenty miles, between it and the city of [[place/murcia|Murcia]] twelve miles, and from Uriyula to Cartagena forty-five miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 558} From the city of Elche to Uriyula is twenty-eight miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 557}

## The province
Uriyula is listed in the province of Tudmir with Murcia, Cartagena, Lorca, Mula and Jinjala. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 538} From Denia on the coast west to the city of Lacant (Alicante) on the sea is seventy miles; Lacant is a small, populous town with a market, a congregational mosque and a pulpit. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 558}
