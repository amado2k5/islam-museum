## Overview
Orihuela is a city on the bank of the river, which boats enter. It has a chief town at the end of its fortifications, markets and estates. It lies twenty miles from the sea and twelve miles from [[place/murcia|Murcia]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.558}
