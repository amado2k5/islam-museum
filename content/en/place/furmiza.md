## Summary
Furmiza was a fine city of the German lands on the bank of the river called Rabn, noble of dwellings, wide of confines, rich in buildings and steady in revenues. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872}

## Details
Al-Idrisi states that from Ishbira to the city of Furmiza is thirty miles; Furmiza is a fine city on the bank of the river named Rabn, noble of dwellings, wide of confines, rich in buildings and steady in revenues {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872}. Among the chief towns of the German lands is the city of Mabansa, a noble populous city of much cultivation and abundant produce, and from it to the city of Furmiza is thirty miles southward; there the river Murin falls into the river Win {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872}. The author counts among the famous towns of the land of the German Burgundians and the lands of Sawaba and Babir adjoining it, and of the land of Shasuniyya and part of the lands of Ifrziyya to the east, the cities of Bazla, Ishbira, Furmiza, Mabansa, Irnkbirda, Qasila, Qaluniyya, Astrik, Harbrd and Bansa {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.872}. Furmiza thus appears in his scheme as a populous river city of the German lands, joined to Ishbira to the north and to Mabansa to the south, where the rivers Murin and Win meet.
