## Overview
Al-Rahba is the place where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] fought [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf ibn Tarif]] and his dense forces; Abd al-Hakim ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur was then in [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]], and they fought in al-Rahba and al-Hadi prevailed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} A footnote says the "rahba of Sana'a" is mentioned in Mu'jam al-Buldan, but the wording about its distance is unclear in the machine-read text and is not recorded here. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}

See [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288]].
