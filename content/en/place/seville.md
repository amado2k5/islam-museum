## Overview
Seville is a Spanish city, one of the greatest cities of al-Andalus, and was a capital in the time of the Almohads. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

Not covered here: the historical events of the city, which need an additional source.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Hispalis/Col. Romula» (Pleiades no. 256210), classed as settlement, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 640 CE. Among its recorded names are «Hispalis», «Ishbiliya» and «Seville». Pleiades identifies it with modern Seville in Spain. {cite:pleiades 256210}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2361218 with the preferred name «Sevilla (Municipio, ciudad)», classed as archaeological site and populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Sevilla (Provincia)», classed as administrative unit and archaeological site. Recorded names include «Sevilha», «Ispal» and «Spal». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2361218}
