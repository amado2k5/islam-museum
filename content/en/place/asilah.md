## Overview
Asilah is a place in Morocco, recorded by Pleiades as a settlement and a port, and by the iDAI.gazetteer under the ancient name Zilil. {cite:pleiades 275594} {cite:idai-gazetteer 2765533}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Asilah» (Pleiades no. 275594), classed as a settlement and a port, with a time span from 30 BCE to 2099 CE. It records one name, «أصيلة» in Arabic, and its note only refers to map 28, square B3, of the Barrington Atlas. {cite:pleiades 275594}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2765533 with the preferred name «Zilil», classed as an archaeological site and a populated place and marked as ancient; its parent in the hierarchy is «Marokko», an administrative unit. Among the names it records are «Asilah» and «أصيلة» (Arabic), «Colonia Augusti Iulia Constantia Zilil» (Latin) and «Arzila» (Portuguese). It links the place to GeoNames no. 2557055. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2765533}
