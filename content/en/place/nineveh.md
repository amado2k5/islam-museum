## Overview
The two fortresses of Nineveh lie opposite Mosul on the Tigris. The book Futuh al-Buldan mentions that their people fought Utba ibn Farqad. He took the eastern fortress by force and crossed the Tigris, and the people of the other fortress made peace with him on payment of jizya. Those who wished to leave were permitted to depart. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Nineveh/Ninos» (Pleiades no. 874621), classed as a settlement and an archaeological site, with activity attested from 2335 BCE to 2100 CE. Names recorded there include نينوى, Νῖνος, Nineveh. {cite:pleiades 874621}
