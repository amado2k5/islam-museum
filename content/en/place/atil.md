## Overview
Atil: the city of the Khazars and their capital, according to al-Idrisi. It is two cities on the two banks of the river: the king lives in the western city and the people in the eastern city, about two miles apart, and a strong wall surrounds both. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.834}
