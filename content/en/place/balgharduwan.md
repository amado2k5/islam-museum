## Overview
Balgharduwan: a large, populous city with notable churches, according to al-Idrisi. He reports that it lies seventy miles from Qawn. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.887} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}
