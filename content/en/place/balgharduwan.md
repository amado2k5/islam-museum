## Summary
Balghardun was a flourishing city with a large population and splendid churches, lying seventy miles from the city of Qaun, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.887}

## Details
Al-Idrisi reports that from the city of Qaun to Balghardun is seventy miles, amounting by land to two very long stages and by river to less; the city of Balghardun is prosperous, with many inhabitants and splendid churches {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.887}. From the city of Baniya northward to Balghardun is five stages, and from Baniya to Qaun a hundred miles {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.887}. From Balghardun to the city of Afranisfa is seventy-five miles, or two days by river, and Afranisfa is a large prosperous city {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}. From Balghardun also to the city of Rabna is one hundred and fifty miles through the steppe {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.888}. In his account it thus stood as a large inhabited city of the northern lands where land and river routes met, with measured roads radiating to the neighbouring towns.
