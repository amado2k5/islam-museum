## Overview
Akka (Acre): a town on the coast of al-Sham, in the district of Jordan. Ibn al-Athir describes it as the mother of the coastal frontier and the impregnable fortress of Islam in the age of the Crusades. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 149}

## The conquest and the siege
[[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub]] conquered it on a Wednesday after the battle of Hattin, in the year 583 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 149}

The Franks then besieged it for two years and seized it on Friday, the seventeenth of Jumada al-Akhira in the year 587 AH. Ibn al-Athir reports that they killed the Muslim prisoners there on Tuesday, the twenty-seventh of Rajab. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 205-207}
