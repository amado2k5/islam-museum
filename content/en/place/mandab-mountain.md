## Overview
A long mountain at the entrance of the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab, extending along the coast of Yemen, which ships sailing up to Yemen pass by. Al-Idrisi reports, from the author of the Book of Wonders, that it draws iron. This is a saying attributed to that author, which the museum has not verified. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.50}
