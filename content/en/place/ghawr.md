## Overview
Al-Ghawr is a region of populous mountains with springs, gardens and rivers. Its people are farmers and herders, and their language is not the language of the people of Khurasan. It is a fertile land lying between Herat and Sistan, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.468}
