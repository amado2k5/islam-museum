## Overview
Al-Buqa is a place in the Akili account to which [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went out from [[place/saada|Sa'da]] on the Tuesday, ordered the Akilis' sorghum pulled up and went on to [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} The name occurs earlier as a halt where he drew up his army in the earlier march. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} It has no coordinates. See [[event/banu-kulayb-ask-al-hadi-for-safety-and-the-akili-crops-are-cut-287]].
