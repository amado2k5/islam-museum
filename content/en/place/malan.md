## Summary
Malan is a handsome town of the Herat district with countless gardens, orchards and vineyards, one stage from Herat. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.472}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that Malan of Herat is a fine town with innumerable gardens, orchards and vineyards, one stage between it and Herat {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.472}. Most of the excellent raisins carried to those parts come from Malan of Herat, just as from Karukh comes the famous sultana exported abroad, the good-tasting raisin carried to Iraq and beyond {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.472}. Also among Herat's towns is Bashan, a large city with many markets and crafts whose people are well off, though it has few trees and little water and is smaller than Malan {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.472}.
