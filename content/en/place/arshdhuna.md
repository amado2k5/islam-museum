## Overview
Arshdhuna: a city among the cities between Malaga and Cordoba, according to al-Idrisi. He reports that it and Antequera were cities evacuated by the troubles, together with Antequera, and that it had standing in the time of Ibn Abi Amir. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.570}

An additional source is needed to tell these names apart. It may be [[place/archidona|Archidona]].
