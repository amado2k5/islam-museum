## Overview
Dhu al-Majaz was one of the famous markets of the Arabs, held in the sacred months together with [[place/ukaz|Ukaz]], [[place/majanna|Majanna]] and others, because the security needed for trade was lacking in the peninsula except in these months. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46}. See [[concept/economic-condition-jahiliyya|the economic condition of the pre-Islamic period]].

## What is not mentioned here
The site of the market near Arafat, and what the chroniclers report of its events, has not yet been added.

## For young visitors
Dhu al-Majaz was an old market near Mecca, where the Arabs gathered in the season of the pilgrimage.
