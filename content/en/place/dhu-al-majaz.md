## Overview
Dhu al-Majaz was one of the famous markets of the Arabs, held in the sacred months together with [[place/ukaz|Ukaz]], [[place/majanna|Majanna]] and others, because the security needed for trade was lacking in the peninsula except in these months. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46}. See [[concept/economic-condition-jahiliyya|the economic condition of the pre-Islamic period]].

## What is not mentioned here
The site of the market near Arafat, and what the chroniclers report of its events, has not yet been added.

## For young visitors
Dhu al-Majaz was an old market near Mecca, where the Arabs gathered in the season of the pilgrimage.

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records al-Majaz with a fatha and a final zay: one says jaztu al-tariq — jawaz, mijaz, jawz — and al-Majaz means the place, as does al-Majaza. Dhu al-Majaz is a market site at 'Arafa on the side of Kabkab, to the imam's right, a farsakh from 'Arafa, held in the Jahiliyya for eight days; al-Asma'i makes Dhu al-Majaz a water at the base of Kabkab belonging to Hudhayl, behind 'Arafa. He quotes Hassan ibn Thabit's verses addressing Abu Sufyan in the affair of Abu Uzayhir, and al-Mutawakkil al-Laythi's lines "for the beauties at Dhu al-Majaz are traces in Mecca's valley, their covenant old". Al-Majaz is also a place near Yanbu and al-Qusayba. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 5 p.55}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut's Dhu al-Majaz is the Jahiliyya fair by 'Arafa a farsakh away, held eight days — the same fair the record counts with 'Ukaz and Majanna; his placing of it and the Hudhayl water at the base of Kabkab fit the market itself, while his second Majaz near Yanbu is another place with no bearing on the record.
