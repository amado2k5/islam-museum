## Summary
Martula (Mertola) is a populous fortress of al-Andalus famous for its strength and defences, on the river Yana of Merida and Badajoz, with gardens, crops and many fig trees. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.542}

## Details
Al-Idrisi says that sailing north from the island of Shaltish to the fortress of Qastala on the sea is eighteen miles, and between them lies the river Yana, the river of Merida and Badajoz, on which stands the fortress of Martula, famed for its impregnability and fortification; it is populous and inhabited, with gardens, crops and many fig trees {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.542}. The fortress of Qastala also stands on the shore and is likewise inhabited, and from it one reaches the village of Tabira {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.542}. Martula is thus among the strongest strongholds of that bank.
