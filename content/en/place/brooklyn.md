## Overview
Brooklyn is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «United States of America». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087716}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2087716 with the preferred name «Brooklyn», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «United States of America». Among the names it records are «Comitatus Bruclinum», «بروكلين», «Borough of Brooklyn», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 5110302. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2087716}
