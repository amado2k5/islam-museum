## Overview
Taranda is a fortress in the land of the Romans, three stages from [[place/malatya|Malatya]]. The Muslims settled in it after Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Malik raided it in the year 83 AH, and built houses there, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.262}

## Malatya and Taranda
Malatya was then in ruins. In summer the Muslims would come out to it from the troops of al-Jazira, and they would stay at Taranda until the snow fell, and then return. Then Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz moved its people away from it, though they were unwilling, out of concern for them from the enemy. They broke the jars of vinegar and oil, and he settled them in Malatya and ruined Taranda. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.262}
