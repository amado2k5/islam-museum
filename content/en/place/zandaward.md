## Overview
Zandaward is a town of Kaskar; in one copy of the book it is written "Zandarud". Al-Baladhuri mentions that Khalid ibn al-Walid passed by it on his march to al-Hira. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339}

## In the conquests
According to al-Baladhuri, Khalid conquered Zandaward, and conquered Darna and its surroundings by safe conduct, as they had been subject to the people of Zandaward. Then he went on to Hurmuzjird, and its people gave him safe conduct, and he conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339}
