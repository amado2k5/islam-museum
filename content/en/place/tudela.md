## Overview
Tudela is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «Navarra (Comunidad autonóma/Provincia)». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2756485}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2756485 with the preferred name «Tudela», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Navarra (Comunidad autonóma/Provincia)». Among the names it records are «Tudela (Navarra)», «Tudela», «Тудела», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 3107418. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2756485}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut records Tutila, with damma then kasra, a still ya' and a lam: a city in al-Andalus east of Cordoba adjoining the workings of Ishqa (Ecija); in his day it was in the hands of the Rum, a noble site with abundant water, many trees and rivers, laid out in the days of al-Hakam ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Mu'awiya. He relates from Abu Ubayd al-Bakri that around the year 400 there was at Tutila a woman with a full beard like a man's who travelled about as men do, until the district judge had the midwives examine her by his order, and he ordered her beard shaved and that she travel only with a mahram. Between Tutila and Saraqusta are seventeen farsakhs, and he ascribes to it a number of men, among them Abu Marwan Isma'il ibn Abdallah al-Tutili al-Yahsubi. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.33}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut's Tutila is the record's Tudela in Navarre; his placing of it "east of Cordoba adjoining the workings of Ecija" is his own map of al-Andalus — in modern geography the city lies west of Zaragoza. The museum's points for Tudela and Saraqusta stand about 75.2 km apart, and his "seventeen farsakhs" is left as printed. "In the hands of the Rum today" describes Yaqut's own time, not the record's.
