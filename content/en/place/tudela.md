## Overview
Tudela is recorded by the German Archaeological Institute as a populated place, within «Navarra (Comunidad autonóma/Provincia)». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2756485}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer of the German Archaeological Institute lists this place under no. 2756485 with the preferred name «Tudela», classed as a populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Navarra (Comunidad autonóma/Provincia)». Among the names it records are «Tudela (Navarra)», «Tudela», «Тудела», and it links the place to GeoNames no. 3107418. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2756485}
