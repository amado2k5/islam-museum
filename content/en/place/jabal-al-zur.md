## Overview
Mount al-Zur is a mountain in the land of the Dawar, in the districts of Kabul. The sources report that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura besieged its people in it, and they made peace with him. He entered al-Zur, which was an idol of gold with two rubies for eyes, cut off its hand and took the two rubies. He told its keeper that he wanted to show him that it neither harms nor benefits. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
