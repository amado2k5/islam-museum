## Overview
Ajdabiya: a town on level stony ground in the lands of the Maghrib. Al-Idrisi describes it as having kept only two castles in the desert, with no running water in it or around it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 311}

## Location
According to al-Idrisi, it lies six stages from [[place/barqa|Barqa]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 311}
