## Overview
Daybul: the port of Sind at its creek. According to the conquests of the lands (Futuh al-Buldan), Uthman ibn Abi al-As sent his brother al-Mughira to it, who met the enemy and was victorious. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
