## Overview
Khawash and Quzan are two towns on the road to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]]. Al-Baladhuri reports that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura came to them after crossing the valley of Nasl, and conquered them by force. See also [[place/bust|Bust]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan
Yaqut says Khawash is a city in Sijistan whose own people call it Khash. It lies on the left of the traveller heading to Bust, one stage from Sijistan, and he describes it as having palms, trees, underground water channels and springs. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.398}
