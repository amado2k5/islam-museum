## Overview
Shabwa is an ancient city of Himyar, between Bayhan and Hadramawt. In it is the Mountain of Salt, and its second mountain is shared with the people of Ma'rib, according to al-Hamdani. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p.70}

Al-Hamdani relates that when Himyar and Madhhij fought each other, the people of Shabwa left it and settled in Hadramawt, and by them [[place/shibam|Shibam]] was named. He says that the original form was "shabah", and the mim was substituted for the ha'. {cite:sifat-jazirat-al-arab p.70}
