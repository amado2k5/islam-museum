## Overview
Taranto (Idrisi's Tarnit) is an ancient, fine, large city, with many houses, merchants and travellers. Ships are loaded there, and caravans and companies seek it. It has markets and great wealth. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.630}

## Harbour and lake
The city has a harbour on its west side, with a living sea, and on its east and north a lake surrounds the city on every side. Three valleys empty into the lake, and its depth runs from thirty fathoms to ten. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.631}

On the causeway that carries the water between sea and lake there are openings through which the sea flows into the lake and back again, twice by night and twice by day. The causeway is about three hundred cubits long and fifteen cubits wide. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.631}

## The road to it
The road to Tarnit runs from the Ibratana valley to the Akra valley, then the Muwajjan river, the Latus and Lamna valleys, the crooked valley, and the valley of the Tarnit mills. Pine trees grow in abundance in these valleys and the mountains around them, and pitch and tar are made from them and carried to many regions. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.630}
