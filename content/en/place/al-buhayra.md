## Overview
al-Buhayra: a town in Jurjan, according to al-Baladhuri. The Turk Sul stayed in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
[[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] besieged him in it for six months and fought him many times. Then he made peace with him: safe-conduct for his own person, his property and three hundred of his household, and the surrender of al-Buhayra with all that was in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
