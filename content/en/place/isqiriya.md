## Overview
Isqiriya is a Chinese town on a river that feeds the river Khamdan. It lies four stages from Khaygun, according to al-Idrisi. Chinese money is gathered there after being sorted, and deposited in the treasuries of the great king. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.205}
