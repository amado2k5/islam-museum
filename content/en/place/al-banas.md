## Overview
al-Banas: a populous village on the coast in the land of the Zanj, the last of the lands of the Zanj, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 60}

## Its description
There is a large drum in it which, when struck, can be heard from a great distance. It lies six days' journey by land from Manbasa. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 60}
