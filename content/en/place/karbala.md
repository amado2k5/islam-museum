## Overview
Karbala is a city in Iraq where the shrine of al-Husayn is, and Ibn Battuta mentions it on his journey through Iraq. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

An additional source is needed: the coordinates come from the modern settlement that bears the same name, and their match with the ancient site has not been verified. {cite:geonames-gazetteer}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The German Archaeological Institute's gazetteer, iDAI.gazetteer, records this place as no. 2102125, typed as populated place, with the preferred name «Kerbela» under «Irak» in its hierarchy, and recorded names including Karbala, Kerbala, 卡爾巴拉. {cite:idai-gazetteer 2102125}
