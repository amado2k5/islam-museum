## Overview
Mdina is a walled city in the Western Region of Malta. It is not Medina in Arabia; the two share only the Arabic spelling of the name. No entry for it was found in the volumes of the Mu'jam al-buldan read here, so this page rests on what the Pleiades gazetteer records.

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Mdina» (Pleiades no. 399911221), classed as a city wall and a settlement, with a time span from 750 BCE to 1453 CE. It describes the place as having ancient origins in Malta, perhaps occupied as early as 4000 BCE and first fortified by the Phoenicians around 700 BCE. {cite:pleiades 399911221}
