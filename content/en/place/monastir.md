## Overview
A site with three palaces, inhabited by a group of devotees. The people of [[place/al-mahdiyya|al-Mahdiyya]] used to carry their dead to it in boats and bury them in its cemetery, which is near al-Mahdiyya, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.282}
