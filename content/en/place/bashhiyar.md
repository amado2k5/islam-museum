## Overview
Bashhiyar: a city in which there is a chief appointed by the Baghbugh, with retinue, servants and slaves, according to al-Idrisi. It fights the Turks who guard the gates of the Hajiz mountain, and it lies eight stages from Qasha. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.213}
