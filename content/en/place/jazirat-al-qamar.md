## Overview
The Island of the Moon is a long island joined to the islands of Dubayhat. A king who lives in the city of Mulay rules it, and he appears to people only to his servants. Al-Idrisi says that its length with the east is four months, and that it is joined to the islands of China from the south. It has crops, coconut and sugar cane. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.71}
