## Overview
Sakawand is a city populous with its people, seven days from Bayt and from [[place/kabul|Kabul]]. Al-Idrisi mentions that snow falls in its land at certain times, and that it lies in the zone of the Surud, that is, the cold lands. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.469}
