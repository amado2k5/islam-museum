## Overview
Tukra is a great palace on the road, according to al-Idrisi. Around it is a cultivated, populous land, and it lies two stages from Qafiz. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.315}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Arsinoe/Taucheira» (Pleiades no. 373736), classed as a settlement, with activity attested from 750 BCE to 2099 CE; recorded names include «Arsinoe», «Tocra», «Tūkrah». In its description, Arsinoe/Taucheira (modern Tocra) may have originally been a Greek foundation that was part of the Pentapolis of Cyrenaica; it became a Roman colony and was fortified by the time of Justinian. {cite:pleiades 373736}
