## Overview
Wasaha, also written "Washaha", is a town in rough, harsh mountains, near Harad according to the editor's footnote, which refers to Sifat jazirat al-Arab, Ghayat al-amani and al-Yaman al-kubra. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} No ruler had entered it but a sultan of Al Ya'fur whose force perished there; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] entered it in Shawwal 284 AH, stayed eight days and left Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah in charge, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.81} and it was the ground of the fighting with Abu al-Dughaysh al-Shihabi in 285 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.85} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.86} The text gives no coordinates and none is supplied.
