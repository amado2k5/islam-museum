## Overview
The running waters of the Ghuta of Damascus come out of the spring of al-Fijah, a spring high on a mountain whose water pours down from the top like a great river, with a tremendous roar heard from afar; one sees the water come down on the village of Abil until it reaches the city. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 367}

## The branches
From this water branch the rivers known there: the river of Yazid, the river of Thawra, the river Barada, the river of the Qanat al-Mazza, the river of Banas, the river of Saqt, the river of Yashkur and the river of Adiya. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 367}

## In the city
This river runs through the middle of [[place/damascus|Damascus]], with a bridge on it for crossing, and it is not drunk from, because the city's refuse, its washers' dirt and small channels run into it. From the other valleys named, channels also come out and pass through the city to its houses, baths, gardens and markets. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 367}
