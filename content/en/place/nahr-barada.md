## Overview
The River Barada is a river that branches within the city into rivers and channels, which run to its houses, baths and gardens. Al-Idrisi describes it as audible from afar because of the force of its flow, and as not fit for drinking because washing outlets empty into it. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.367}
