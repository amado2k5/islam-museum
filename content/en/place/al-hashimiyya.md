## Overview
al-Hashimiyya: a city on the Euphrates near [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. al-Baladhuri reports that Yazid ibn Umar ibn Hubayra began it, and then left it on a letter from Marwan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}

Abu al-Abbas settled there, completed it and named it al-Hashimiyya. He later left it, because the name of Ibn Hubayra remained on it, and built another Hashimiyya facing it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}

[[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] settled there, completed it and added to it, then moved to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. Abd Allah ibn Hasan was imprisoned there, and his grave is there. al-Mansur built al-Rusafa in Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}
