## Overview
Kirkant is a civilised city, among the noblest and most populous of the capitals. Its citadel is strong and lofty, ships and travellers gather in it, and it lies one stage from al-Shaqa, according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.599}
