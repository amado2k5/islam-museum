## Overview
Karkh Fayruz is a district of Samarra. Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] lodged his client Ashnas in it, with the commanders who joined him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
