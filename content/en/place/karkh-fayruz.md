## Summary
Karkh Fayruz was a quarter of Surra Man Ra'a (Samarra). Al-Baladhuri records that [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] settled his mawla Ashinas there along with the commanders he had gathered around him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 417}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Mu'tasim, after distributing the lands as stipends for his soldiers, built a palace at al-Qatul and made it his residence, assigning that palace to Ashinas the Turk, his mawla; but then he resolved to found a proper city, left it, and began building another {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 417}. He then decided to establish Surra Man Ra'a, moved the people into it and lived there, building a congregational mosque at the edge of the markets and naming the city Surra Man Ra'a; and he settled his mawla Ashinas, together with the commanders he had gathered, in the quarter called Karkh Fayruz {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 417}. Karkh Fayruz was thus one of the quarters of the new capital reserved for Ashinas and his officers, part of al-Mu'tasim's arrangement of settling his troops and clients in the city's districts {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 417}. In this way the palace went to Ashinas and Karkh Fayruz to the commanders attached to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 417}.
