## Overview
Bilalabadh: a place in [[place/basra|Basra]] where there was the bath of Muslim ibn Abi Bakra, the third of the baths of Basra. Afterwards the bath passed to Amr ibn Muslim al-Bahili. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
