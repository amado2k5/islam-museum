## Overview
Kharpoot (Harput) is a town in eastern Anatolia. Under its older name, Hisn Ziyad, Yaqut records it in the Mu'jam al-buldan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.264}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut lists Hisn Ziyad in the land of Armenia and says that it is known in his day as Khartbirt, between Amid and Malatya and nearer to Malatya. He quotes a verse of al-Nami addressed to Nasir al-Dawla ibn Hamdan that mentions Hisn Ziyad. He gives no more than this in the entry. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 2 p.264}
