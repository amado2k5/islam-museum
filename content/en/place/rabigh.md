## Overview
Rabigh is a coastal city on the pilgrimage road from Medina and Mecca. Caravans pass through it between Badr and Khulays. Ibn Battuta mentioned it on his way to Mecca. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958}

## In Yaqut's Mu'jam al-buldan
Yaqut gives "Rabigh", spelling it with a ba after the alif and a ghayn at the end, and calls it a wadi that the pilgrim crosses between "al-Buzwa'" (as the printed text reads) and al-Juhfa, short of 'Azur. He quotes verses he ascribes to Kuthayyir that name Rabigh and the departure of Salma's caravan there. He reports Ibn al-Sikkit as placing Rabigh between al-Juhfa and Waddan and, elsewhere, as calling it a wadi short of al-Juhfa crossed by the pilgrim road short of 'Azur. From al-Hazimi he gives "Batn Rabigh" as a wadi from al-Juhfa that is mentioned in the campaign narratives and in the battle-days of the Arabs, and from al-Waqidi that it is ten miles from al-Juhfa, between al-Abwa' and al-Juhfa; he also quotes a line he gives to Kuthayyir on a day at Marr and Rabigh. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 3 p.11} Elsewhere in the book he notes that al-Burud is near Rabigh and that Rabigh lies between al-Juhfa and Waddan. {cite:mujam-al-buldan-sader-1995 1 p.405}

## The museum's identification
Yaqut does not describe Rabigh as a coastal town but as a wadi on the pilgrim road between al-Abwa' and al-Juhfa, and he names neither Badr nor Khulays nor Ibn Battuta, which the record does. The museum reads the present coastal town (22.7986, 39.0349) as the settlement that took the wadi's name; that is its reading, not Yaqut's text. The museum measured the distances between the points of its own records: about 14.7 km to its al-Juhfa point, close to the ten miles that Yaqut passes on from al-Waqidi if the mile is about 1.6 km, though Yaqut does not give the length of his mile, and about 34.7 km to its al-Abwa' point; the order of latitudes in those records (23.11, 22.80, 22.72) agrees with his saying that Rabigh lies between al-Abwa' and al-Juhfa. The record's point lies about 111 km from the museum's Badr point and about 77 km from its Khulays point, and Yaqut places Rabigh by neither. The first words of the entry give the spelling "al-Buzwa'", and a few lines later al-Waqidi's quotation gives "al-Abwa'"; the museum does not decide which reading is right.
