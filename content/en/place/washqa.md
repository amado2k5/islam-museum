## Overview
Washqa (Huesca) is a fine, civilised city with a wall and markets, standing and well governed. It lies fifty miles from [[place/sarqusta|Zaragoza]], according to al-Idrisi. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}
