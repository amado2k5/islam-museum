## Overview
Washqa is a town in the district of Arnit in al-Andalus, near Saragossa. Al-Idrisi names it with Lardha and Maknasa and calls it a good, civilised town. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.538} {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}

## Distances
From Saragossa to Washqa is fifty miles. From Washqa to Lardha is seventy miles, and from Washqa to Maknasa is seventy miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733} In another passage, Al-Idrisi gives the distance from Saragossa to Washqa as forty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.554} The two figures do not agree, and both are given here as the text states them.

## The town and its markets
Al-Idrisi says Washqa is a good, civilised town with merchants, full markets, and workshops that are well established and busy. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}

## Neighbouring towns
Lardha is a town of medium size, rich in produce, on the river al-Zaytun. From it to Ifraghah is a walled fortress with markets and craftsmen. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733} Maknasa is small, like a fortress, and is one of the frontier towns of al-Andalus. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733} Between Lardha and Maknasa are fifty miles. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.733}

## Saragossa
Al-Idrisi describes Saragossa as the White City, named for the plenty of its plaster and lime. A great bridge is crossed to enter it, and it has strong walls. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.554}
