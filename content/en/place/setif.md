## Overview
Setif is a large, populous fortress, lying two stages south of Bejaia, according to al-Idrisi. It has fruit trees of many kinds, and perfume is sold there at a cheap price. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.269}

## In the Pleiades gazetteer
Pleiades, the open gazetteer of ancient places, lists this place under the title «Sitifis» (Pleiades no. 305153), classed as settlement and archaeological site, with activity attested from 30 BCE to 640 CE. Among its recorded names are «Sétif», «Sitifi colonia» and «Saṭīf». Pleiades describes an ancient site at modern Sétif in Algeria, founded as a colonial settlement for Roman veterans in the reign of the emperor Nerva. {cite:pleiades 305153}

## In the iDAI.gazetteer
The iDAI.gazetteer lists this place under no. 2096154 with the preferred name «Setif», classed as archaeological site and populated place; its parent in the hierarchy is «Algerien», classed as administrative unit and populated place. Recorded names include «Sétif» and «Setif». {cite:idai-gazetteer 2096154}
