## Summary
Bust, a city of Sijistan, taken by Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura by compact together with Zabil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 555}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura, on reaching the land of al-Dawar, besieged its people at the mountain of al-Zur and then made peace with them; he entered before al-Zur, an idol of gold whose eyes were two rubies, cut off its hand and took the rubies, telling the marzban: the gold and jewels are yours — I only wished to show you that it can neither harm nor help; and he took Bust and Zabil by compact. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 555} In another report he came to Khawash and Quzan Bust and took it by force, then marched on to Wazan, whose people fled. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 558} When Rutbil prevailed as far as Bust, al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad marched out, fought him at Bust and routed him; later Abdallah ibn Umayya camped at Bust and Rutbil settled with him for a million; and in the desert of Bust many men perished of thirst and hunger, Ubaydallah ibn Abi Bakra dying of grief. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 561}
