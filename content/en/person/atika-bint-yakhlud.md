## Overview
Atika daughter of Yakhlud ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana, one of the grandmothers of the Prophet in the series of the "Atikas". Al-Baladhuri says that she was the highest in rank of them, and that she was one of three Atikas among the descendants of al-Nadr ibn Kinana. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.534}
