## Overview
Salim ibn Muhammad 'Izz al-Din ibn Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Sanhuri al-Misri was a jurist and mufti of the Malikis, born at Sanhur, educated and died at Cairo in 1015 AH (1606 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.72}

He wrote a gloss on Shaykh Khalil's Mukhtasar in nine volumes called Taysir al-Malik al-Jalil li-Jam' al-Shuruh wa-Hawashi Khalil; a treatise on the night of mid-Sha'ban; and a commentary on Risalat al-Wad'. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.72}
