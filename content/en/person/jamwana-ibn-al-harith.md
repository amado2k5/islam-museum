## Overview
Jamwana ibn al-Harith, of the Banu Amir ibn Sa'sa'a. Al-Baladhuri relates that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz appointed him over Malatya when the people of Tarandah settled there after their removal from it, and that he laid Tarandah waste. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 262}
