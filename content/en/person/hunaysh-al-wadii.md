## Overview
Hunaysh was of [[group/wadia-tribe-yemen|Wadi'a]], with many palms, vines and houses at [[place/najran|Najran]], in the village of "Shawkan"; another manuscript reads "Hubaysh". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.89} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.90} He followed the agents of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at "al-Rakb" with a band of his clan, took about a thousand dinars and killed a man, and after warning refused to return it, so his palms and vines were cut and his house pulled down; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.90} according to the narrator he died two days later of a stab wound in the hand. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.90} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.91} The account is Zaydi and the museum shows what it tells without confirming it.
