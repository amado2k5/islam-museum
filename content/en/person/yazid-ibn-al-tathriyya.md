## Summary
Abu'l-Muhallab Yazid ibn al-Muntashshir ibn Salama al-Khayr ibn Qushayr ibn Ka'b ibn Rabi'a ibn 'Amir ibn Sa'sa'a, known as Ibn al-Tathriyya, the famous poet. He was a born poet, complete in adab, rich in manliness — unblamed and unassailed — brave, rooted, and eminent among his people of Qushayr; among the Umayyads' poets he was foremost in their regard. He was nicknamed "muwaddiq" for his handsome face and sweet verse — they said that when he sat among the women he was "muwaddiq." He is among the great poets: Abu Tammam al-Ta'i mentioned him in several places in the Hamasa, and Abu'l-Faraj al-Isfahani, author of the Aghani, collected his verse into a diwan. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
