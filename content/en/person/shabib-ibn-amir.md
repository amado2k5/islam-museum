## Overview
Shabib ibn Amir, governor of al-Jazira under Ali ibn Abi Talib, according to al-Sallabi's list of Ali's governors {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.358}.

## Governorship
Al-Sallabi places Shabib twelfth in the list of Ali's governors, with al-Jazira as his province {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.358}. The book gives neither the dates of his office nor any further reports about him in this passage.
