## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur Umar ibn Ali ibn Rasul, the second of the [[polity/rasulid-dynasty|Banu Rasul]] rulers of Yemen, and son of [[person/ali-ibn-rasul|Ali ibn Rasul]]. He succeeded his father in 629 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
Ibn Khaldun says that when al-Kamil died in Egypt in 635 AH, and the Ayyubids were occupied with their strife among themselves, his power grew strong in Yemen. He took the title al-Mansur, and stopped the tribute that he had been sending to Egypt. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His end
The Zaydi imam besieged Ahmad ibn Husayn al-Mutawakkil in the fortress of Mala when he intended to revolt. Then a group of his mamluks rose against him in collusion with his nephews, Hasan's sons, and killed him in 648 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
