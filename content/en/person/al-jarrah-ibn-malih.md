## Overview
Al-Jarrah ibn Malih, father of the hadith scholar Waki' ibn al-Jarrah, held the treasury in Baghdad and was in charge of the mint at Rayy in the reign of al-Rashid; his family is said to come from the district of Rayy, from the small town of Ustuwa {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. The signs printed with his entry mark the compilers who recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. Hanash ibn Harb reports from Waki' that his father was born in Sughd {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Ziyad ibn Ilaqa, Abu Ishaq, Simak ibn Harb, Mansur ibn al-Mu'tamir and others; his son Waki', Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi, Qabisa, Musaddad, Yahya al-Himmani, Uthman ibn Abi Shayba and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}.

## What the critics said
Al-Dhahabi quotes Ibn Sa'd that he held the treasury in Madinat al-Salam and was weak in hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. Ja'far ibn Abi Uthman reports that Yahya ibn Ma'in never wrote anything from Waki' from his father; Uthman al-Darimi reports Ibn Ma'in as saying there is no harm in al-Jarrah, Abbas reports him as saying trustworthy, and Ahmad ibn Abi Khaythama reports him as saying weak in hadith yet better than Abu Yahya al-Himmani {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. Ibn Ammar said weak, Abu Dawud said trustworthy, and al-Nasa'i said there is no harm in him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. Ibn Adi said his hadith is not harmful, that he is truthful, and that he found nothing rejected in it to mention {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1283}. Al-Burqani asked al-Daraqutni about Waki's father and he said he was nothing and greatly prone to error; asked whether he could be used for corroboration he said no {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1284}.

## His death
Khalifa ibn Khayyat said he died in 175 AH and Ibn Qani' said 176 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1284}. The heading of his entry in the book carries 175 AH.
