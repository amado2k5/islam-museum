## Summary
Abu 'Ali Yahya ibn 'Isa ibn Jazla the physician, of Asyut in Upper Egypt; a Christian who converted and wrote an epistle refuting the Christians and proving Islam the true religion — the cause of his conversion was keeping company with Abu 'Ali ibn al-Walid until God guided him by his hand; he trained under Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali ibn Ridwan and practised medicine with him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
He composed for the imam al-Muqtadi bi-amr Allah the Taqwim al-Abdan, the Minhaj al-Bayan on what man uses, the Ishara on abridged expression, and an epistle praising medicine and its accord with the shari'a. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
