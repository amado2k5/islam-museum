# Mishal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah

Mishal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah, born at Kuwait City on 27 September 1940, is the Emir of Kuwait. He came to the throne on 16 December 2023, succeeding his half-brother Nawaf Al-Ahmad, after a career spent almost entirely in Kuwait's security services and three years as crown prince.{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Early life and formation

He is the seventh son of Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah, and the paternal half-brother of three earlier emirs — Jaber, Sabah and Nawaf. He was educated at the Al Mubarakiya School and at Hendon Police College in the United Kingdom, graduating in 1960.{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikidata-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah}

## The security career

He joined the Ministry of Interior and rose to head its intelligence and state security service from 1967 to 1980, becoming the first director of Kuwait State Security. From 13 April 2004 he served, at ministerial rank, as deputy chief of the Kuwait National Guard — its de facto head — until 2020.{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Crown prince and accession

Emir Nawaf nominated him crown prince in 2020; at eighty he was the oldest crown prince in the world. On Nawaf's death on 16 December 2023 he became Emir at the age of eighty-three.{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Reign

On 15 February 2024 he dissolved the National Assembly, and on 10 May 2024 he dissolved parliament again and suspended selected articles of the constitution for a period of up to four years. In June 2024 he appointed his nephew Sabah Al-Khalid Al-Sabah crown prince, a choice made without the parliamentary vetting the constitution envisages.{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-mishal-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-ar}
