## Overview
Alfonso, king of Castile, whom the Maghribi historians call "the tyrant", and who is Alfonso XI according to the museum's data. He took part in [[event/battle-of-tarifa-741|the battle of Tarifa]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Ibn Khaldun's account
Ibn Khaldun relates that he met Sultan Abu al-Hasan al-Marini in the battle of Tarifa. His fleet took control of the Strait of Gibraltar and cut off the supplies to the camp of the Muslims. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The nations of Christendom gathered to him, and the king of Portugal backed him. He sent an ambush force to Tarifa, and when the fighting broke out the camp of the Maghrib and al-Andalus was defeated in 741 AH, and he seized the sultan's concubines and treasures. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
