## Overview
Hagar, mother of Isma'il, whom Ibrahim settled with her child in the valley of Mecca; Zamzam sprang for her, and she granted Jurhum leave to settle.

## Life
It is commonly said Hagar was a slave woman, but Judge Muhammad Sulayman al-Mansur Furi verified that she was free — daughter of Egypt's Pharaoh, whom Ibrahim's wife Sara gave him in service, and he married her {cite:rahma-lil-alamin}.

Ibrahim settled her with her suckling Isma'il in a barren valley by the Sacred House, set down beside them a skin of dates and a water-skin, and went back {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. When the provision and water ran out the well of Zamzam burst forth and became their sustenance {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. And she gave Jurhum the Yemenites leave to settle at Mecca {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
Hagar is Isma'il's mother; she stayed in Mecca with her suckling child, and God gave them water from the well of Zamzam, which stands to this day.
