## Overview
Abu al-Ma'ali Badr Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad al-Irbili al-Mawsili, a scholar from Mosul in the eighth century AH, among the people of religious learning and letters. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates of life
The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 686 AH (1287 CE) and his death as 755 AH (1354 CE). The two dates come from one source, so they need a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
