## Overview
Mahwiya, marzban of Marw. Al-Baladhuri says that he came to [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] in his caliphate while he was in Kufa. Ali wrote for him to the landed notables (dahaqin), the cavalry-men (asawira) and the dihshalaran, that they should pay him the poll tax, and then Khurasan rebelled against them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

This report is from al-Baladhuri alone and needs an additional source.
