## Overview
Muhammad ibn Hamuda ibn Ahmad ibn Uthman Ju'ayt, Abu Abd Allah, was mufti of Tunis among the Maliki jurists; he held the fatwa office from 1331 AH until he died in 1337 AH (1918 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.110}

His books include a two-volume gloss on al-Tanqih in fiqh, a work on the biographies of the scholars of Tunis, and a diwan of verse mostly in praise of the Prophet. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.110}
