## Overview
Yahya ibn Yamur al-Layti al-Basri, Abu Sulayman, a scholar among the Successors (d. 129 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3496}

## Transmission and office
He narrated from Uthman, Ali, Ammar, Abu Dharr and Abu Hurayra. He is said to be the first to dot the copies of the Qur'an, and al-Hajjaj appointed him judge of Marw. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3496}
