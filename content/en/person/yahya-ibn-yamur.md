## Overview
Yahya ibn Yamur al-Layti al-Basri, Abu Sulayman, a scholar among the Successors (d. 129 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3496}

## Transmission and office
He narrated from Uthman, Ali, Ammar, Abu Dharr and Abu Hurayra. He is said to be the first to dot the copies of the Qur'an, and al-Hajjaj appointed him judge of Marw. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3496}

## On the dotting of the mushaf and in the chain of the reading

Al-Zarkashi reports, within the accounts of who first dotted the mushaf, that Ibn Sirin had a mushaf which Yahya ibn Ya'mar dotted for him. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p. 177} Al-Suyuti names him among those said to have first set the vowel signs and dots, with Abu al-Aswad, al-Hasan al-Basri and Nasr ibn 'Asim. {cite:itqan-suyuti-calcutta-1852 p. 868} In al-Nashr he read on Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali, and Abu 'Amr ibn al-'Ala' and 'Abd Allah ibn Abi Ishaq al-Hadrami read on him. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 143}
