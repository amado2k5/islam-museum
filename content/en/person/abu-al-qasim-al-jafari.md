## Overview
The book says Abu al-Qasim al-Ja'fari, "of the line of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib", was killed on Friday, the seventeenth of Safar, when the horse and foot of [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]] confronted those of al-Hadi's army who had entered [[place/zabwa-sanaa-yemen|Zabwa]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236} ([[event/the-night-raid-on-zabwa-and-the-death-of-abu-al-qasim-al-jafari-289]]). The narrator asks mercy for him. The book gives no father's name or home, and it is not certain that he is connected with the "Ja'fari" from whom aid came in the following pages, nor with Abu al-Qasim, al-Hadi's son. For the preceding days see [[event/the-tabari-reinforcements-arrive-and-abdullah-and-ibn-al-ruwayya-camp-at-sabal-289]].
