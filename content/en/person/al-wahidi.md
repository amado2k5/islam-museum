## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ahmad al-Wahidi, a Qur'an commentator, who died in 468 AH. His book "Asbab al-Nuzul" ([[work/asbab-al-nuzul-wahidi|Asbab al-Nuzul]]) is, in Haji Khalifa's account, the most famous of what has been composed on that subject, and Burhan al-Din Ibrahim ibn Umar al-Ju'bari abridged it. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.381}

The conversion of the year of death to the Gregorian calendar is derived (about 1075 CE).
