## Overview
Yazid ibn Umar ibn Hubayra al-Fazari, the last of the Umayyad agents over Iraq. al-Baladhuri reports that he built a city in Kufa on the Euphrates and settled in it, and a small part of it remained unfinished. Then a letter from Marwan came to him ordering him to avoid living beside the people of Kufa, and he left it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He built the palace of Ibn Hubayra near the bridge of Sura. After him Abu al-Abbas settled in it and named it "al-Hashimiyya", and he repaired the bridge of Kufa {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
