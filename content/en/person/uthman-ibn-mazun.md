## Summary
Uthman ibn Maz'un al-Jumahi al-Qurashi, one of the early Muslims during the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]] in Mecca, according to the count in *The Sealed Nectar*. He became Muslim together with his two brothers, Qudama and Abdullah. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book mentions him among those who followed the first vanguard: Uthman ibn Maz'un and his two brothers [[person/qudama-ibn-mazun|Qudama]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-mazun|Abdullah]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. He was from the [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76}.

## Not covered here
This chapter of the book mentions only his early Islam. The rest of his story is found in its proper places in the book and in other sources.

## For young visitors
Uthman ibn Maz'un became Muslim at the very beginning of Islam, together with his two brothers, when the Muslims were few and hid their faith.
