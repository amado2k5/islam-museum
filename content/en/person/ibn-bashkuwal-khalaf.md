## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Khalaf ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Mas'ud ibn Musa ibn Bashkuwal, an Andalusian historian and hadith scholar, author of *al-Sila* (The Connection), a collection of biographies of the scholars of al-Andalus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born in 494 AH (1101 CE) and died in 578 AH (1183 CE), according to report. The sources attribute to him [[work/qudat-qurtuba-ibn-bashkuwal|Judges of Córdoba]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
