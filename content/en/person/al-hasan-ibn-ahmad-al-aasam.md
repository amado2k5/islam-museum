## Overview
Al-Hasan b. Ahmad the Qarmatian, known as al-A'sam; he overpowered Damascus before 360. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 29}

## Taking Damascus
There Ja'far b. Fallah al-Kutami, the Fatimids' lieutenant, had gone down to the dakka above the Yazid river outside Damascus; al-A'sam marched out against him though ill, defeated him, captured and killed him, and killed multitudes of his men on Thursday 6 Dhu'l-Qa'da. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 29}
