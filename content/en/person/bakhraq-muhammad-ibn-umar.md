## Overview
Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Abd Allah al-Himyari al-Shafi'i al-Bahraqi, a Shafi'i jurist from Yemen. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 869 AH and his death as 930 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
