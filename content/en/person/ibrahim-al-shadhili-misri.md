## Summary

Shaykh Ibrahim al-Shadhili al-Misri, one of the well-known Sufi shaykhs of Egypt. Sultan Qansuh al-Ghuri had complete faith in him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.

He often provided for people from the unseen, and he was trained at home by Shaykh Abu al-Mawahib al-Tunisi, author of the "Qawanin". He combined the two forms of training. He appeared to many people who were pious and compassionate, and no harm escaped his prayers {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.

He died in the year 914 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.
