## Overview
Wahb ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh, a Qurashi Companion of the Banu Malik ibn Hisl. He was killed as a martyr on the day of Mu'ta {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.

## Mu'ta
The data records that he was killed as a martyr at [[event/battle-of-mutah|the battle of Mu'ta]] in the year 8 AH, in Jumada al-Ula, on a date that is approximate and based on the report {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.
