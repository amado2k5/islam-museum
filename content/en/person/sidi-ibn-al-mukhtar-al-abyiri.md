## Overview
Sidi ibn al-Mukhtar ibn al-Haybah al-Intishai al-Abyiri, a Mauritanian scholar from a family of learning, and a teacher. He was born around 1760 CE in the region of Bitlamt. He read the Qur'an, grammar, rhetoric, logic and creed, and then travelled in pursuit of knowledge {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
He died in 1284 AH / 1868 CE, as recorded in the encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
