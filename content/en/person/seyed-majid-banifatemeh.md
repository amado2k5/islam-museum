## Summary
Seyed Majid Banifatemeh is an Iranian maddah (eulogist) from Tehran, and Iran's most-followed maddah on Instagram. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} {cite:instagram-banifatemeh} Persian Wikipedia describes him as a maddah from Iran who began maddahi in earnest in 1371 SH (1992–93) at the age of fifteen. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} His Instagram account counted 1,176,156 followers in November 2026 per an on-platform profile read — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:instagram-banifatemeh}

## Beginnings and education
Seyed Majid Banifatemeh was born in Tehran in Bahman 1356 SH, at the start of 1978, and continued his schooling to diploma level before turning to maddahi. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} He began professional maddahi in 1371 SH at the age of fifteen. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} He credits his meeting with the maddah Saeed Haddadian at a martyrs' funeral procession with his great progress in the art. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa}

## Works and eulogy
Banifatemeh now performs his maddahi at the Heyat Reyhanat al-Hossein in Tehran's Bagh Feyz district. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} He became known to the public through his noheh "Amoo Abbas" ("Uncle Abbas"). {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} His recorded genres are maddahi, noheh-khani and shur, in the noheh, rowzeh and marsiyeh styles. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa} On Eid al-Ghadir in 1395 SH he appeared as a guest on Rambod Javan's television programme Khandevaneh, an acceptance that drew some abuse on his social page, as the article reports. {cite:wikipedia-seyed-majid-banifatemeh-fa}
