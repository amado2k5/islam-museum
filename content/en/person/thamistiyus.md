## Overview
Themistius, an interpreter of the books of Aristotle. He was the secretary of Julian the Apostate, who turned from Christianity to the doctrine of the philosophers, after Galen {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.355}.

Among his books are a letter to Julian on management, a book "On the Soul" in two treatises, and a letter to King Julian {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.355}.
