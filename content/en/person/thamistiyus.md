## Overview
Themistius, commentator on Aristotle's books and secretary to Julian — who had apostatized from Christianity to the philosophers' school after Galen — author of an epistle to Julian on governance, a two-discourse book on the soul, and letters. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 413}
