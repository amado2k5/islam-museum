Abdullah ibn Jud'an al-Taymi, a shaykh of Taym ibn Murra; his house hosted the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Fudul pact]] for his age and standing among his people, and he was among Mecca's notables in the Jahiliyya. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
