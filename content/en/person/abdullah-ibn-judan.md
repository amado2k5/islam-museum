## Summary
Abdullah ibn Judan al-Taymi, of Taym ibn Murra of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]]. The parties to the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Hilf al-Fudul]] gathered in his house, for his age and standing {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/133-135} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}.

## His house and the pact
The book says that the tribes which responded to the Hilf al-Fudul gathered in the house of Abdullah ibn Judan al-Taymi, for his age and standing, and there they agreed to support the wronged {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/133-135}. The pact is mentioned in this house in the Prophet's saying, after his mission, that he had witnessed a pact in the house of Abdullah ibn Judan, and that if he were called to it in Islam, he would answer {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}.

## From another source
Al-Mas'udi says that he was a slave-dealer in the Jahiliyya, who bought and sold slave-girls, and that he commanded Quraysh on the day of the War of al-Fijar {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.584}.

## Notes for researchers
- On who led Quraysh on the day of al-Fijar, the two sources differ: Harb ibn Umayya in al-Raheeq, and Ibn Judan in al-Mas'udi. Both accounts are recorded.

## For young visitors
Abdullah ibn Judan was an elderly, well-known man in Mecca, famous for generosity. In his house, the clans that agreed to support the oppressed gathered.
