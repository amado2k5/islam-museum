## Overview
Amr ibn al-Nu'man al-Bayadi, the chief of the [[group/al-khazraj|Khazraj]] on the day of [[event/yawm-buath|Bu'ath]], and he was killed in it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.229}

He swore that he would not touch his head with a washing until the Jews of Yathrib had come down to the homes of Qurayza and al-Nadir and he had killed their hostages. When the two sides met, Abd Allah ibn Ubayy told him that he would be killed and four men would carry him. He disobeyed, was killed, and was brought in carried on four men. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.229}

Ibn Hazm says that the chief of the Khazraj that day was the father of al-Nu'man, Ruhayla ibn Tha'laba al-Bayadi. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.229}
