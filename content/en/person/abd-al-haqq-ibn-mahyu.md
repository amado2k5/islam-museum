## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Mahyu ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hamama al-Zanati al-Marini, the eldest son of Mahyu, who took charge of the Banu Marin after him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
Ibn Khaldun describes him as the best ruler they had in attending to their affairs, in refraining from what was in other people's hands, and in his attention to consequences. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar} The Banu Marin used to reach the areas from Kursif to Wattat as they climbed down from the wastelands. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Confrontation with the Almohads
When relations with the Almohad state deteriorated, they met the troops of al-Rashid in the plains of Nukur, and the victory went to the Banu Marin in the «Year of the Busy» (ʿAm al-mashghala). {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death
Abd al-Haqq died in a battle in 614 AH (about 1217 CE, the conversion is derived) with the Banu Muhammad, together with his eldest son Idris. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar} He left ten children, nine of them sons, including Uthman, Muhammad and Abu Yahya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
