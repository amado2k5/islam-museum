## Overview
Muhammad ibn Umar Nawawi al-Jawi, al-Bantani by province and al-Tanari by town, was an exegete and Sufi and one of the Shafi'i jurists. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1316 AH, 1898 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 318}.

## Life
He emigrated to Mecca and died there. Taymur called him the scholar of the Hijaz {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 318}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his many writings: Marah labid li-kashf ma'na al-Qur'an al-majid, printed in two volumes, which is his exegesis; Maraqi al-ubudiyya, a commentary on al-Ghazali's Bidayat al-hidaya, which he finished composing in 1289 AH; Qami' al-tughyan, on the poem Shu'ab al-iman; Qatr al-ghayth fi sharh masa'il Abi al-Layth; Uqud al-lujayn fi bayan huquq al-zawjayn; Nihayat al-zayn bi-sharh Qurrat al-ayn, on law; a commentary on Fath al-Rahman, on recitation; Nur al-zalam, a commentary on the poem Aqidat al-awamm by Ahmad al-Marzuqi; Murqat su'ud al-tasdiq, on Sufism, a commentary on Sullam al-tawfiq by Ibn Tahir, who died in 1272; and Kashifat al-saja, a commentary on Safinat al-naja, in theology and law. Al-Zirikli marks all of these as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 318}.
