## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Khubayq narrated much and had reports to himself; he relates from Yusuf ibn Asbat, Hudhayfa al-Mar'ashi and al-Fadl. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 545, sequence 546, on pages 168 to 171. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/168} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/171}

## Sayings on truth and obedience
Al-Husayn ibn Muhammad relates from Muhammad, from Abd Allah ibn Khubayq, many sayings. Whoever disciplines himself in God's pleasure, God keeps from His hatred. Truthfulness needs none of the states, and it is independent of all of them. The loneliness of servants from the truth has made their hearts lonely; if they were at home with their Lord and held fast to the truth, every creature would be at home with them. Long listening to falsehood puts out the sweetness of obedience in the heart. He said: grieve only over what will harm you tomorrow, rejoice only in what will please you tomorrow, and the most useful fear is what holds you back from disobedience. From Musa ibn Tarif, from Yusuf ibn Asbat: for forty years nothing troubled my heart that I did not leave. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/169}

## Hudhayfa, al-Fadl and a saying about Moses
From Hudhayfa al-Mar'ashi: how will you prosper while the world is dearer to you than the dearest of people? If you do not fear God will punish you for your best deed, you are lost. A man is tried by the hardness of his heart, and a man's matters are four: his eyes, his tongue, his desire and his heart. Al-Fadl said: the head of manners among us is that a man knows his station. A report says God revealed to Moses not to be angry at creation, lest his grief multiply; and that a rabbi of the Children of Israel complained that God did not punish him, and was answered that God punishes him in ways he does not perceive. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/169}

## From Yusuf ibn Asbat
Abd Allah relates from Yusuf ibn Asbat: when you see a man proud and insolent, do not admonish him, for admonition has no place in him. Yusuf saw a man with a register in his hand and said: adorn yourselves as you wish, God will only increase you in humility. The truthful is given three things: sweetness, comeliness and sustenance. When a physician came to Yusuf and said there was no harm, he said: I wish the thing you fear for me were now. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/170}

## Reports he alone transmits
Abd Allah transmitted reports from Yusuf ibn Asbat on various chains, among them reports about the Prophet's wives and his ritual bathing, the creation of a person in the womb for forty days, a report of Abu Dharr on the measure of food, a report on the trials of the last hour that a person may be faithful in the morning and unfaithful by evening, a report on the man who asked about the hour, a report of Abu Hurayra on the warrior who seeks worldly gain, and a report of Ali ibn al-Husayn that a good part of a man's Islam is leaving what does not concern him. The wording of these reports is not given here; they are referred to by their place in the text. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/170} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/171}

## Closing remark
Abu Nu'aym closes with a passage on the friends of God whom the Truth hid from notice, whose names and lineages were erased from fame and remembrance, and whom He made a safety for the dwellers of the realms; by their shares He averts ruin. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/171}
