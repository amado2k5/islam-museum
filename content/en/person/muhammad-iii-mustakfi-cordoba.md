## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Ubaydallah ibn al-Nasir al-Umawi, Abu Abd al-Rahman, al-Mustakfi bi-llah, was lord of Cordoba and one of the Umayyad rulers of al-Andalus. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 366 AH, 976 CE, and his death as 416 AH, 1025 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 190}.

## Reign
He rose with a band of rabble against his predecessor al-Mustazhir bi-llah, Abd al-Rahman ibn Hisham, and they killed him; he took power after him in 414 AH. Al-Zirikli says his policy went badly and that he lasted seventeen months {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 190}.

When the people of Cordoba learned that Yahya ibn Ali al-Hammudi was advancing on them from Malaga, they went in to al-Mustakfi, deposed him and put him out of the city. He made for the frontier districts and died, killed or poisoned according to the report, in the village of Shamnat near Madinat Salim; another report says Uclés. Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Hazm's verdict that al-Mustakfi was at the extreme of baseness, decline, weakness and backwardness {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 190–191}.
