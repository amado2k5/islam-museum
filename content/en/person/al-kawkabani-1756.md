## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Ahmad al-Kawkabani, whose lineage joins the Mahdi Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Hasani, was a Zaydi jurist; his origin was Kawkaban in Yemen, and he was born and died at San'a in 1223 AH (1808 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.47}

He wrote tender verse and composed juristic books and treatises, among them Kashf al-Mahjub on the validity of pilgrimage with usurped money, Inbah al-Anbah on divorce conditioned on “in sha' Allah,” and al-Tanbih on the duty of removing the Jews from the Arabian Peninsula. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.47}
