## Overview
[[person/bujayr-ibn-zuhayr|Bujayr ibn Zuhayr]] ibn Abi Sulma al-Muzani, the poet, brother of the famous poet [[person/kab-ibn-zuhayr|Ka'b ibn Zuhayr]]. Ibn Hajar says he "embraced Islam before his brother"; the details are in Ka'b's entry. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.132}

## The day of the conquest
Ibn Ishaq recites for him verses on [[event/conquest-of-mecca|the conquest of Mecca]], in which he mentions the fighting of his people and that they gave the Prophet covenants of sincere goodwill, and that they came upon Mecca with a thousand of Sulaym and a thousand of the Banu Uthman. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.132} (We do not reproduce the text of the verses here; see the printed text.)
