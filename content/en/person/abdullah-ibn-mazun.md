## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Maz'un al-Jumahi, brother of [[person/uthman-ibn-mazun|Uthman ibn Maz'un]]. The "Sealed Nectar" counts him among the first Muslims who accepted Islam in [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|the secret call]] together with his brother. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}

## In the Sealed Nectar
After the book counts the first cohort, it mentions among the early Muslims Uthman ibn Maz'un and his two brothers Qudama and Abd Allah, among those whom Ibn Hisham counts, more than forty men from all the clans of Quraysh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}

## What is not recorded here
This section does not give any account of him after his acceptance of Islam; what the Companion books say of his emigration, his battles and his offices needs a source to be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Abd Allah accepted Islam at the beginning of Islam together with his two brothers, when the call was still secret.
