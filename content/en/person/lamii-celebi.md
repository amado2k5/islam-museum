## Summary
Lamii Celebi: Ottoman poet, linked in the museum's record to Bursa. The Biblissima record places his life between 1473 and 1532. {cite:biblissima Q10897}
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Mahmud Çelebi Lami'î» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q10897}
