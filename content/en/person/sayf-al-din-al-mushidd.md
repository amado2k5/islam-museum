## Overview
Ali ibn Umar ibn Qizil al-Turkmani al-Yaruqi al-Misri, Sayf al-Din, known as al-Mushidd, was a poet and one of the Turkmen amirs. Al-Zirikli gives his dates as 602 to 656 AH, that is 1205 to 1258 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 315}.

## Life
He was the mushidd of the diwan, its overseer, in Damascus. He was born in Egypt, moved between the chancery bureaux, and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 315}.

## His works
He left a diwan of poetry, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 315}.
