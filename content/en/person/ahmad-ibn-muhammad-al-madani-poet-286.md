## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Madani is a poet whose poem the book gives, spoken when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] entered the village, killed [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] and took Ibn Humayd, faulting Banu al-Harith and describing al-Hadi as an imam of guidance. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.179} The book gives him no lineage or office, and his printed nisba is "al-Madani", not "al-Hamdani", so the museum does not assert that he is [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-hamdani-poet-286]] or [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran]]. See [[event/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-madanis-poem-faulting-banu-al-harith-286]].
