## Overview
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. 'Abdallah b. Mas'ud b. Ahmad al-Mas'udi, the Shafi'i jurist; an excellent, outstanding, scrupulous imam of the people of Marw — he read fiqh under Abu 'Uthman al-Marwazi and commented al-Muzani's Mukhtasar well, transmitted a little hadith from his teacher al-Qaffal, and al-Ghazali cited him in the Wasit on oaths. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
