## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan ibn [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], Abu Muhammad, the father of "the Pure Soul" and of Ibrahim. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur imprisoned him on account of his two sons in the Hashimiyya at Kufa, and his grave is there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The call and his meeting with Ja'far al-Sadiq
Al-Mas'udi relates that a letter from Abu Salama came to him calling him to the summons, and he rejoiced at it and rode to [[person/jafar-al-sadiq|Ja'far al-Sadiq]]. He said that the people wanted his son Muhammad, because he was the Mahdi of this community; Ja'far answered him that he was not its Mahdi, and that if he drew his sword he would be killed. So he went away angry. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.240-241}

## His imprisonment
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Mansur seized him in 144 AH on his return from pilgrimage, and he was carried with his household from Medina to al-Rabadha, then to Kufa, and they were imprisoned in an underground vault, where they died. When the head of his son Ibrahim reached him, he said some words to al-Rabi' that al-Mas'udi quotes. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.287-288}
