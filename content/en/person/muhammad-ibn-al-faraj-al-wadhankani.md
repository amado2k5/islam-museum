## Summary
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Faraj al-Wadhankani, counted among the substitutes and steadfast in spiritual states, whose supplication was answered. [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]] (volume 10, printed number 685, page 401) gives him a notice. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/401}

## Companionship and asceticism
He was a companion of Abu Uthman Sa'id ibn al-Abbas al-Razi, and hunger and frontier-guard duty were made easy for him. He used to pray: O God, take me in the place most beloved to You. He went to Tarsus three times and died there in the year 284 AH, as the scan reads it (the figures are blurred in the scan, and not confirmed). {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/401}

## Reports in the notice
The notice carries a report from Ibn Umar on the deeds most beloved to God, which the wording does not give here; Abu Nu'aym judges it gharib from Nafi', transmitted from him by Uthman ibn Muqsim alone. It also carries a report from Ibn Mas'ud on the deeds nearest to Paradise, and a report from Jabir about the illness of Ubayy ibn Ka'b, for whom the Prophet sent a physician who cauterized his vein. None of these reports is quoted here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/401}

## Sayings of Abu Uthman
He relates from Abu Uthman Sa'id ibn al-Abbas, through his grandfather Mahmud, that Abu Uthman said: when you humble yourself you have attained all virtues; when you guard your tongue you have guarded your limbs; when you make your deeds sincere you have completed what is sufficient for you. One word at the end of the third saying is unclear in the scan. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/401}
