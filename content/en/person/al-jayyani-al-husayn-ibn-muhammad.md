## Overview
Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Ghassani al-Andalusi al-Jayyani, a memorising imam and hadith scholar from al-Andalus. He was born in 427 AH (1035 CE) and died in 498 AH (1105 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His nisba
He is attributed to Jayyan, where his father lived for a time, and his origin was in al-Zahra. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
