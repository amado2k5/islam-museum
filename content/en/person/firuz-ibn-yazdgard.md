## Overview
Firuz ibn Yazdegerd III, son of the last of the Persian kings. Al-Baladhuri reports that, as they claim, he fell into the hands of the Turks, who married him to one of their women, and he remained with them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
