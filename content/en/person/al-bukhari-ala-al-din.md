## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Bukhari, known as Ala al-Din, a Hanafi jurist from Bukhara and one of the Hanafi scholars of his age. He died in 1331 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The Hijri year printed in the source does not match the Gregorian year, so the museum's data do not record it.
