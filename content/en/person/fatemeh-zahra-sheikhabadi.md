## Summary
Fatemeh Zahra Sheikhabadi is an Iranian schoolgirl from the city of Minab in Hormozgan province, in southern Iran. She is known to the world not through office, scholarship or wealth but as a survivor and a witness: the Royal Islamic Strategic Studies Centre in Amman named her the Woman of the Year in the 2027 edition of its annual publication, The Muslim 500, honouring her public testimony about the missile strike that hit her school. {cite:muslim500-fatemeh-zahra-sheikhabadi}

## The strike on her school
On 28 February 2026 a missile struck the Shajareh Tayyebeh primary school in Minab, where Sheikhabadi was a pupil. Classmates, friends and teachers were killed; she survived. In the aftermath she spoke publicly about what had happened to her school and to the people she lost, and according to the reports of the award she carried that testimony to a United Nations forum. {cite:muslim500-fatemeh-zahra-sheikhabadi}

## The honour
The Muslim 500 is an annual publication that profiles influential figures across the Muslim world; its editors measure influence of every kind, positive or negative, and traditionally chose heads of state, generals and senior scholars as its Persons of the Year. In the 2027 edition the editors broke that pattern: they dedicated the Woman of the Year title to Sheikhabadi and, through her, to the classmates and teachers who did not survive. The editors' citation describes her as someone who holds no power over anyone, but whose witness allows the world to learn the fate of those who were killed. The publication named the Palestinian doctor Hussam Abu Safiya its Man of the Year in the same edition. {cite:muslim500-fatemeh-zahra-sheikhabadi}

## Reading the record
This record describes an event and an honour, not a career. Nothing published about Sheikhabadi names a religious school or denomination for her, and the museum records none. The attribution of the missile strike and the reporting around it belong to the sources cited; the museum reports the honour as it was announced by the awarding body, without endorsing or adjudicating the wider claims around the strike itself.
