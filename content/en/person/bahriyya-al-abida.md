## Summary
Bahriyya al-Abida is among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 601); she was strenuous in devotion and held a gathering in which she admonished, and when she spoke she trembled and shivered. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## Her prayer and her hunger
From Rabah ibn Abi al-Jarrah: he saw her weeping and telling her Lord that she had left Him when she was a fresh date and come to Him a dry one, asking Him to accept the dry date as it was. She had some beauty, hunger had worn her down, and she once went forty days eating nothing but a little chickpea. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## From her words
[[person/ahmad-ibn-abi-al-hawari|Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari]] reports from an old woman of Basra that she heard Bahriyya say: "When the heart leaves its desires it becomes familiar with knowledge and follows it, and bears all that comes upon it." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## For young visitors
Bahriyya was a woman of Basra who taught people in her own gathering, and said that a heart which leaves its cravings comes to love knowledge.
