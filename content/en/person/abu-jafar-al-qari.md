## Overview

Abu Ja'far Yazid ibn al-Qa'qa' al-Makhzumi of Medina was a successor of high standing to whom the leadership of the reading in Medina passed; his reading is the eighth of the ten in the order of al-Nashr. He died in 130 AH on the soundest view. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189}

## Chain

He read on his patron 'Abd Allah ibn 'Ayyash ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi, on [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|'Abd Allah ibn 'Abbas]] and on [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]]; those three read on [[person/ubayy-ibn-kab|Ubayy ibn Ka'b]], Abu Hurayra and Ibn 'Abbas also read on [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]], and Zayd and Ubayy read on the Prophet. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 188} Ibn al-Jazari adds that some said he read on Zayd himself, which he finds possible since it is sound that he was brought to [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]], who stroked his head and prayed for him, that he led Ibn 'Umar in prayer, and that he taught people to read before the battle of al-Harra in 63 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189}

## Standing and those who took from him

Ibn al-Jazari quotes Yahya ibn Ma'in that he was the imam of the people of Medina in the reading and reliable, and Malik that he was a righteous man. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189} [[person/nafi-al-madani|Nafi' al-Madani]] read on Abu Ja'far among his seventy teachers from the successors. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 121} His reading is carried by Isa ibn Wardan ([[person/isa-ibn-wardan-al-hadhdha|Ibn Wardan]]) and Sulayman ibn Jammaz ([[person/sulayman-ibn-jammaz|Ibn Jammaz]]), to whom the books return it. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189}
