## Overview
Al-Musta'li billah Ahmad ibn al-Mustansir, the ninth of the rulers of the Ubaydid state in al-Suyuti's phrasing, that is the Fatimid state. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.655}

## Accession
He rose after his father when he died in 487 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.655}

## His death
He died in 495 AH, and after him his son al-Amir bi-Ahkam Allah Mansur was set up, who was a child of five. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.658}
