## Overview
Abu Bakkar al-Hakam ibn Farrukh al-Ghazzal of Basra, a transmitter of hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10351}

## Transmission and assessment
He narrated from Abu al-Malih ibn Usama and Ikrima; Shuba, Muhammad ibn Sawwar, Hammad ibn Zayd and Muslim ibn Ibrahim narrated from him. al-Nasa'i called him trustworthy, Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy, and Ahmad called him sound in hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10351}
