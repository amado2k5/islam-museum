## Overview
Al-Malik al-Muzaffar Ghazi b. al-Malik al-'Adil; a brave horseman, a formidable and awe-inspiring man, a generous king — lord of Mayyafariqin, Khilat, Hisn Mansur and more. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 241}

## His death
He made the hajj from Baghdad and died that same year; his son the martyr al-Malik al-Kamil Nasir al-Din ruled after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 241}
