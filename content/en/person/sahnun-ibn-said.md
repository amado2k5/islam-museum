## Overview
Abd al-Salam ibn Sa'id ibn Habib al-Tanukhi, called Sahnun, was a judge and jurist to whom the leadership of learning in the Maghrib passed. Al-Zirikli describes him as an ascetic who feared no ruler when he spoke what was right. His family was of Syrian origin, from Homs, and he was born in Qayrawan. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 160 to 240 AH, 777 to 854 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 354}.

## The judgeship
He took the judgeship of Qayrawan in 234 AH and held it until he died. Al-Zirikli adds that reports about him are very many and that he was of high standing, chaste and proud of spirit {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 354}.

## The Mudawwana
He narrated the Mudawwana, which is printed, on the branches of Maliki law, from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from Imam Malik. Al-Zirikli notes that Abu al-Arab Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Tamim wrote a book on his merits, life and manners, Manaqib Sahnun wa-Sirat-hu wa-Adab-hu {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 354}.
