## Overview

Ali al-Akbar ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, the eldest of Husayn's sons killed with him at Karbala; Ibn Kathir lists him among them in 61 AH {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya}.

He rode out to fight before his father before falling, and his memory stands out in the Karbala elegies.
