## Overview
Abu 'Abd al-Rahman Muhammad b. al-Husayn al-Sulami al-Nisaburi, shaykh of the Sufis — author of Tabaqat al-sufiyya, Haqa'iq al-tafsir and others. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.348}

## His death
Ibn al-Jawzi records him among the deaths of Sha'ban 412. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.348}
