## Summary
Babdeo Miladu is a Tanzanian preacher and the media owner behind the channel that carries his name, working under the Babdeo Online TV and Miladu TV brands. His main channel on YouTube (@babdeomiladu) counted 330,000 subscribers by vidIQ as of 6 November 2026 — a fresh figure, read on 8 November 2026, that supersedes the stale 300,000-subscriber milestone graphic the channel itself posted on 6 November 2025. {cite:vidiq-babdeo-miladu} {cite:instagram-miladu-tv} His Instagram account (@miladu_tv) counted 110,000 followers by the metadata of that same milestone post dated 6 November 2025, as read by the wave harvest on 8 November 2026. {cite:instagram-miladu-tv}
