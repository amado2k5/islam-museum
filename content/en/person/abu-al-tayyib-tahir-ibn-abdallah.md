## Overview
The judge Abu al-Tayyib Tahir ibn Abd Allah al-Shafi'i. Al-Mas'udi says that he answered the poem of Imran ibn Hittan in praise of Ibn Muljam, and appended to it the last verses of the answer praising Ali. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.704-705}
