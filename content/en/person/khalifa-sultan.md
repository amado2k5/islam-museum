## Overview
Husayn ibn Muhammad, al-Mirza Rafi' al-Din, son of the Amir Shuja' al-Din Mahmud, a Husayni by descent, a Mar'ashi of Amul by origin and an Isfahani by upbringing and residence, was known as Sultan al-Ulama {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}. Al-Zirikli counts him among the leading figures and scholars of the Imamiyya; this is an Imami description, and the museum's default frame is Sunni {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}. He gives his birth as 1001 AH, 1593 CE, and his death as 1064 AH, 1654 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}.

## Life
He held the vizierate for Shah [[person/abbas-i-safavi|Abbas the Safavid]] for about five years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}. He then held it after him for Shah [[person/safi-i-safavi|Safi]] for two years, until Shah Safi dismissed him and exiled him to the land of Qum; after a time he was brought back to Isfahan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}. When Safi died and Shah [[person/shah-abbas-ii|Abbas II]] succeeded, he restored him to the vizierate and kept him close, and he remained in it for eight years and six months {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists Anmudhaj al-Ulum, in manuscript, also called al-Risala al-Jalila {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}. He wrote glosses and commentaries, among them a gloss on the commentary on al-Luma', a gloss on Ma'alim al-Usul, printed, on legal theory, and a gloss on al-Adud's commentary on the Mukhtasar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}.

## Death
He died in the town of al-Ashraf in the lands of Mazandaran, and his bier was carried to al-Najaf {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 256}.
