## Summary
Adem Güneş is a Turkish pedagogue and author on child and family education, born in 1969 in Ankara, who graduated from Rotterdam University's pedagogy faculty and has written 22 books in Turkish and 2 in English on child education. {cite:wikipedia-adem-gunes-az}

His YouTube channel counted 317,000 subscribers as of 15 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-adem-gunes}

## Early life and education
Güneş was born in 1969 in Ankara and graduated from Rotterdam University's pedagogy faculty. {cite:wikipedia-adem-gunes-az}

## Work and project
His official website presents his works under the name "Pedagog Adem Güneş", among them Cezasız Eğitim (Education Without Punishment), in which he examines personality development from childhood to adulthood and how pressure, coercion and punishment reflect on a child's personality. {cite:official-site-adem-gunes}

In the second volume of Cezasız Eğitim, Güneş describes "acquiring" instead of "learning", and explains the importance of the curiosity–enthusiasm–desire triad in a child's learning. {cite:official-site-adem-gunes}

## Works
Among his Turkish books the article lists Tatil Sürecinde Çocuk Eğitimi (2012), Çocukluk Sırrı (2012), Çocuk Deyip Geçmeyin (2013), Doğal Ebeveynlik (2013), Güvenli Bağlanma (2014), Mahremiyet Eğitimi (2015), Cezasız Eğitim (2017) and the two volumes of Çocuk Neyi Neden Yapar (2018). {cite:wikipedia-adem-gunes-az}

His two English books named in the article are The Wonder Of Childhood and The Education Of Privacy Children. {cite:wikipedia-adem-gunes-az}
