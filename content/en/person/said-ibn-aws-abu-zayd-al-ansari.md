## Overview
Abu Zayd Sa'id ibn Aws ibn Thabit al-Ansari al-Khazraji, a philologist, grammarian and transmitter. He took language, grammar, recitation and hadith from the scholars of his age, and he wrote works on language {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
He was born around 122 AH (740 CE) and died around 215 AH (830 CE). The encyclopedia notes that the sources differ on the year of his birth. Both dates need an additional source {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
