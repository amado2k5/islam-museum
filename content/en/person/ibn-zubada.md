## Summary
Qawam al-Din Ibn Zubada, Yahya ibn Sa'id ibn Hibatallah of Wasit then Baghdad; head of the chancery at Baghdad and the last word in the epistolary craft, with knowledge of fiqh, usul, kalam, grammar and poetry; he took from Ibn al-Jawaliqi and transmitted from 'Ali ibn al-Sabbagh and the qadi al-Arjani; he held the oversight of Wasit, then the grand chamberlainship and other posts; he died in Dhu'l-Hijja 594. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 798}
