## Overview
Qaraqush, mamluk of Taqi al-Din Umar and nephew of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]]. Ibn al-Athir reports that he marched with a band of Turks from Egypt to the Nafusa mountains {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}.

## Tripoli and Ifriqiya
He agreed with [[person/masud-ibn-zammam|Mas'ud ibn Zammam al-Balat]], who had revolted against Abd al-Mu'min, and the two of them conquered Tripoli of the West in the year 568 AH ([[event/qaraqush-takes-tripoli-568|the conquest of Tripoli]]). He settled his family in its palace, and he ruled much of the land of Ifriqiya except al-Mahdiyya, Sfax, Gafsa, Tunis and their surroundings {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}.

He ruled those lands with the help of the Arabs, and collected great wealth at Gabes. He aspired to seize all of Ifriqiya, because its ruler, Abu Ya'qub Yusuf ibn Abd al-Mu'min, was far away from it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}.
