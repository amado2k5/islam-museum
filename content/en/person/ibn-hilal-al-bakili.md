## Overview
Abu Nasr Ahmad ibn Hilal al-Bakili ibn Wasif, whom Ibn al-Nadim counts among the scholars of hadith. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.431}

## As Ibn al-Nadim relates
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was the first to open the matter of spiritual resolve (al-'aza'im) in Islam, and that he had wonderful acts and tested endings. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.431}

His books, according to Ibn al-Nadim, include *al-Rooh al-Mutalashiya* (The Dissolving Spirit), *al-Mafakhir fi al-A'mal* (Boasts in Deeds), and *Tafsir ma Qalathu al-Shayatin li-Sulayman ibn Dawud wa ma Ukhidha 'Alayhim min al-'Uhud* (Commentary on what the devils said to Solomon son of David and the covenants taken from them). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.431}
