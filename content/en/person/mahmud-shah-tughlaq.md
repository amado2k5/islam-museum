Nasir al-Din Mahmud Shah ibn Muhammad Shah ibn Firuz Shah, known as Mahmud Shah Tughlaq — the English biography titles him Mahmud Shah II — was the last sultan of the Tughlaq dynasty at Delhi. He succeeded his brother Ala al-Din Sikandar Shah on 20 Jumada I 796 AH / 1394 CE; his father [[person/muhammad-shah-iii-tughluq|Nasir al-Din Muhammad Shah III]] had reigned before them both. His relative Nusrat Shah, a grandson of Firuz Shah, contested the throne, and the war of succession lasted three years, Mahmud ruling from Delhi and Nusrat from Firozabad.{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-ar}{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-en}

In 801/1398 Timur invaded India. Mahmud met him before Delhi and was defeated; Timur entered the city, massacred its people and carried off the treasures accumulated by two centuries of sultans. Thereafter the sultanate fell apart: Khwaja Jahan made himself independent at Jaunpur and founded the Sharqi kingdom, Gujarat and Bengal broke away, and the sultan of Delhi was left ruling a small territory around his capital.{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-en}{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-ar}

Mahmud died in Dhu al-Qa'da 815 AH — February 1413 in the English biography — after some twenty years on the throne, and with his death the Tughlaq dynasty came to an end; Khizr Khan took Delhi and the Sayyid period began. No birth year is recorded for him in any source reached: the two biographies give none, and Wikidata's two values are a century-precision "1300s" and 1394, his accession year misfiled as a birth, so the record writes no born block and says so plainly.{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-ar}{cite:wikipedia-mahmud-shah-tughlaq-en}{cite:wikidata-mahmud-shah-tughlaq}
