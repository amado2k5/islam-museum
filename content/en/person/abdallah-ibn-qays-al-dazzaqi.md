## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Qays ibn Makhlad al-Dazzaqi, mentioned by al-Baladhuri on the authority of al-Waqidi in the account of the [[event/first-raid-on-sicily|first raid on Sicily]]. He reports that he took captives in Sicily and got idols of gold and silver inlaid with jewels, and sent them to Mu'awiya, who sent them to Basra to be carried to India and sold there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}
