## Summary
Najm al-Din Ilghazi, the Artuqid emir and lord of Mardin, succeeded his brother Suqman; both had been emirs of Tutush, lord of Syria. After Tutush's sons died, Ilghazi seized Aleppo and also took Mayyafariqin. He was a brave horseman, much given to raiding and to largesse. He died in 516 and his son Husam al-Din Timurtash succeeded him at Mardin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 528}
