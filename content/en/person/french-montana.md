## Summary
French Montana, born Karim Kharbouch in Casablanca, Morocco, on 9 November 1984, is a Moroccan-American rapper. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} Born and raised in Morocco, he immigrated to New York City with his family at the age of 13 and began his career as a battle rapper under the name Young French. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} His 2017 single "Unforgettable" received diamond certification, and he is the most-streamed African-born musical artist. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en}

## Career
He first gained recognition hosting the DVD series Cocaine City in the 2000s, and founded the record label Coke Boys Records in 2008. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} In 2012 he signed with Sean Combs's Bad Boy Records and Rick Ross's Maybach Music Group in a joint venture with Interscope Records, and his debut studio album Excuse My French (2013) peaked at number four on the Billboard 200. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} His second album, Jungle Rules (2017), peaked at number three, preceded by "Unforgettable" (featuring Swae Lee), which peaked in the Hot 100 top ten and received diamond certification from the RIAA; in 2024 it became the most-streamed song from New York. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} His later albums are Montana (2019) and They Got Amnesia (2021), the latter his final release on a major label. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} In 2003 he survived being shot in the head as he left a New York recording studio. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en}

## Works
His studio albums are Excuse My French (2013), Jungle Rules (2017), Montana (2019) and They Got Amnesia (2021), and his best-known single is the diamond-certified "Unforgettable" (2017). {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en} He is the most-streamed African-born musical artist and the first African-born artist to earn a diamond-certified song from the RIAA. {cite:wikipedia-french-montana-en}
