## Overview
Imran ibn Musa ibn Yahya ibn Khalid al-Barmaki. Al-Baladhuri relates that in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim he built a town in the lands of al-Buqan, which he named "al-Bayda'" (the White). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
