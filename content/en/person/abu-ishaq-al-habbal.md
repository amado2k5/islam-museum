## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Sa'id al-Nu'mani by clientage, al-Misri, Abu Ishaq al-Habbal, was one of the memorisers of hadith. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 391 to 482 AH, 1001 to 1089 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 40}.

## Career
He traded in books {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 40}.

## Works
He wrote Wafayat al-shuyukh, in manuscript, and a part of it deals with the deaths of Egyptians {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 40}.
