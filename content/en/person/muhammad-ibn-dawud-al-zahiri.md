## Overview
Muhammad ibn Dawud ibn Ali ibn Khalaf al-Zahiri, Abu Bakr, was a man of letters, a debater and a poet. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 255 AH and his death to 297 AH, that is 869 and 910 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}. Al-Zirikli quotes al-Safadi as calling him the imam, son of an imam, one of the intelligent people of the world {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}.

## Life
His family came from Isfahan; he was born and lived in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}. He was nicknamed the thorn sparrow for his slight build and the yellowness of his colour {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}. He was the son of the imam Dawud al-Zahiri, after whom the Zahiri school is named {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}.

## Death
According to al-Zirikli he died in Baghdad, killed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Zahra, printed, the first part, on literature; Awraq min Diwanihi, printed; al-Wusul ila Ma'rifat al-Usul; al-Intisar ala Muhammad ibn Jarir wa-Abd Allah ibn Shirshir wa-Isa ibn Ibrahim al-Darir; and Ikhtilaf Masa'il al-Sahaba {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 120}.
