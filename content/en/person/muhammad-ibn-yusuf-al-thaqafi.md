## Overview
Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Thaqafi, brother of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]]. Al-Hajjaj appointed him over Yemen. Al-Baladhuri says that he behaved badly, wronged the subjects, took people's lands without right, among them al-Hurjah, and imposed on the people of Yemen a land tax that he made into an office over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.99-100}

## The reversal
When [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] came to power he ordered his governor to cancel the office and keep to the tithe. The report says that he said that a handful of the Kutham not arriving from Yemen to him was dearer to him than the establishment of this office. When Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik came to power, he ordered it restored. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.99-100}
