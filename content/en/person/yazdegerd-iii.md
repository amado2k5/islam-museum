## Overview
The last of the Sasanian kings. When affairs grew hard for the Persians, Rustam and al-Firuzan searched for the sons of Khusraw. It is said that a boy named Yazdegerd remained, of the line of Shahriyar ibn Khusraw, whose mother was from Badurya, and they took her with him {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.477}.

## His flight and death
He fled to Khurasan in the year thirty AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.286}. The index of al-Tabari records his death in the year 31 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.581}.

al-Baladhuri reports that he fled from al-Mada'in to Hulwan, then to Isfahan, then Istakhr, Kirman, Sistan and Khurasan. Then Mahawayh, lord of Marw, with Nizak Tarkhan, combined against him, and he was killed at Bayt Tahhan on al-Raghab. It is also said that he was strangled with a bowstring and thrown into the water {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
