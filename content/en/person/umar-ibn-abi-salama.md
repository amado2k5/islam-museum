## Overview
[[person/umar-ibn-abi-salama|Umar ibn Abi Salama]], stepson of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], whose mother was [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Stipend
al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] assigned him four thousand, and Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Jahsh objected. Umar said that he gave him more because of his place with the Prophet, and that whoever sought help from a mother like Umm Salama was helped {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
