## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Isa ibn Yusuf al-Ashmuni al-Qahiri, a Shafi'i jurist of Cairo. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His learning
The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" says that he memorised al-Nawawi's "al-Minhaj", al-Subki's "Jam' al-Jawami'", and Ibn Malik's "al-Khulasa al-Alfiyya". He took from a number of the shaykhs of his age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His dates
The encyclopaedia estimates his birth around 1435 CE and his death around 1524 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Notes for researchers
- The encyclopaedia is a secondary reference and the dates are approximate; they need an additional source.
