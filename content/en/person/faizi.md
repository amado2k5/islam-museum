## Overview
Fayd Allah, known as Faizi (Faydi), son of Mubarak, of Akbarabad, is given the kunya Abu al-Fadl in al-Zirikli's heading. Al-Zirikli calls him an exegete versed in both Arabic and Persian letters, from India, born in 954 AH (1547 CE) and died in 1004 AH (1595 CE), his birth and death both at Akbarabad, that is Agra {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 168}.

## Career
Al-Zirikli says he followed the way of the sages (hukama'), that he attached himself to Sultan Akbar, king of India, and that he was given the title king of poets {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 168}.

## Works
Among his Arabic books al-Zirikli lists Sawati' al-Ilham (printed), a Qur'anic commentary written entirely in undotted letters, and Mawarid al-Kalim (manuscript), a treatise on ethics also in undotted letters {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 168}. In Persian he left a diwan of fifteen thousand verses, and he translated from Sanskrit into Persian the book Lilavati on geometry and arithmetic {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 168}.
