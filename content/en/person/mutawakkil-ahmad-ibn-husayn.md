## Overview
Al-Mutawakkil Ahmad ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi, nicknamed al-Muwatti', a Zaydi imam who rose against the Banu Rasul in Yemen. He was given the oath of allegiance in 645 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His character
Ibn Khaldun describes him as a jurist, a man of letters, a scholar of the Zaydi school, and diligent in worship. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The conflict with the Banu Rasul
[[person/mansur-umar-ibn-ali-rasuli|Al-Mansur Umar ibn Ali ibn Rasul]] besieged him for a time in the fortress of Mala, then released him and besieged him from the neighbouring fortresses. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

In the days of [[person/muzaffar-yusuf-rasulid|al-Muzaffar Yusuf]] he held twenty Zaydi fortresses, and advanced on Safad and took it from the Sulaymanis. He killed whoever opposed him, until he was killed. The sons of al-Rassi continued to call him imam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The year of his death is not confirmed in the data (around 648 AH); it needs documentation, and it is a matter of disagreement.
