## Summary
Abd al-Wahid ibn al-Husayn ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Qasim al-Saymari, a Shafi'i jurist, theorist and judge, who died in 386 AH as the book gives. He lived in Basra, attended the court of al-Qadi Abu Hamid al-Marwarudhi, studied under Abu al-Fayyad al-Basri, and al-Mawardi studied under him. His works include al-Idah on Shafi'i law, Adab al-mufti wa-al-mustafti, al-Irshad fi sharh al-hidaya, and al-Qiyas wa-al-'ilal. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15411}
