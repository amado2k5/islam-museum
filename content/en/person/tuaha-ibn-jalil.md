## Summary
Tuaha Ibn Jalil is a Pakistani Islamic public speaker, trainer and life coach who works with Youth Club Pakistan. {cite:oms-tuaha-ibn-jalil} {cite:speakrj-tuaha-ibn-jalil} His YouTube channel counted 1,910,000 subscribers in November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:vidiq-tuaha-ibn-jalil}

## Role at Youth Club
A peer-reviewed study of religious podcasting in Pakistan, published in Online Media & Society in March 2025, describes him in its prose: "Ibn Jalil is an Islamic public speaker. He is an instructor and country head of Youth Club Pakistan." {cite:oms-tuaha-ibn-jalil} The same article describes Youth Club as a non-profit Islamic organisation that provides a platform for the training of youth according to Islamic values. {cite:oms-tuaha-ibn-jalil} Youth Club Pakistan itself is a non-profit youth organisation founded in 2011 and headquartered in Islamabad, which organises community events, religious and educational conferences and youth engagement programmes. {cite:wikipedia-youth-club-pakistan-en}

## Talks and programmes
His channel's own description, quoted by the SPEAKRJ audit, calls him a "Youth Mentor and Life coach" and records talks delivered at TEDx and tech conferences and at Pakistani institutes including LUMS, GIKI, NUST and BNU. {cite:speakrj-tuaha-ibn-jalil} The Online Media & Society study discusses his appearance on Hafiz Ahmed's podcast, where his story of a simple marriage — a wedding to a girl he had never met, kept deliberately free of extravagance — is presented as a counter-narrative to consumerist wedding culture. {cite:oms-tuaha-ibn-jalil} The study observes that figures such as Tuaha Ibn Jalil resonate with urban Muslim youth, offering contemporary explanations and realistic stories of Islamic teachings. {cite:oms-tuaha-ibn-jalil} His best-known recurring format is the Wednesday Night Exclusive, whose episodes fill his channel's most-viewed list. {cite:vidiq-tuaha-ibn-jalil}

## Channel and audience
His YouTube channel was created on 12 September 2019, with its channel country set to Pakistan. {cite:vidiq-tuaha-ibn-jalil} {cite:speakrj-tuaha-ibn-jalil} It stood at 1,910,000 subscribers, 175,764,688 total views and 1,168 videos at vidIQ's update of 5 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-tuaha-ibn-jalil} SPEAKRJ's snapshot of 3 November 2026 independently gives the same 1,910,000 subscribers, with 175,702,492 views and 1,167 videos. {cite:speakrj-tuaha-ibn-jalil} His most-viewed video, "Don't be Sad!" from the Wednesday Night Exclusive series of August 2022, had about 5.11 million views in vidIQ's listing, followed by a July 2022 video on porn addiction at about 3.16 million. {cite:vidiq-tuaha-ibn-jalil}
