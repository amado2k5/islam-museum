## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Bulaydi, an Algerian-born scholar attributed to the town of Bulayda. He moved between the Maghrib, al-Andalus and Tunisia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 1096 AH (1685 CE) and his death as 1176 AH (1763 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
