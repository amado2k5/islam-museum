## Overview
Yazdegerd the Sinful, a king of the Sasanian Persians {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.63}.

## In al-Tabari's history
al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The Reign of Yazdegerd the Sinful", and reports at this point what was said about the years of his reign and his affairs {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.63}.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] describes Yazdagird the Sinner as harsh, punishing small faults like great ones, though his viziers were upright, and says he entrusted his son Bahram to al-Mundhir. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 51} “They say” he died after twenty-one and a half years, and the nobles swore to crown none of his sons, choosing instead Khusraw of Ardashir's line. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 55}
