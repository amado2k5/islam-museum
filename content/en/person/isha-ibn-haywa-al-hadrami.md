## Overview
Ishaq ibn Hayawa al-Hadrami. Al-Mas'udi says that he was among a group who were appointed when Amr ibn Sa'd ordered his companions to trample Husayn with their horses, and they trampled him with their horses on the day of al-Taff. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.61}

The data available go no further than this report. It needs an additional source.
