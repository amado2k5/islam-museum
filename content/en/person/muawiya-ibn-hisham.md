## Overview
Mu'awiya ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, one of the sons of the caliph Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik who held command of the raids. The book says that Hisham's sons had a large share of the raids, and that Mu'awiya had the greatest share of them. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.470}

Al-Zuhri had taken charge of the upbringing and teaching of Hisham's sons from their childhood. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.470}
