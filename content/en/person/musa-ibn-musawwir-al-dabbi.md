## Summary
Musa ibn Musawwir Abu al-Haytham al-Dabbi transmitted from Sufyan ibn Uyayna and Waki'; a good and excellent man. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 665, sequence 666, on page 390. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}

## His scrupulousness and works
He left what he inherited from his father to his brothers out of scrupulousness, because his father had served the ruler, and he took nothing of it. He has well-known works in building caravanserais for travellers and mending roads. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}

## A dream after his death
Abu Muhammad ibn Hayyan relates that he was told Musa was seen in a dream after his death. Asked what God had done with him, he said he had been forgiven: once he passed a woman carrying a sack whose load was heavy for her, and he carried it with her, and God thanked him for it and forgave him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}
