## Overview
John Brookes is recorded in the museum as a garden designer and the author of *Gardens of Paradise: The History and Design of the Great Islamic Gardens*, published in London by Weidenfeld and Nicolson in 1987. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987}

## The book
The book is an illustrated history of the Islamic garden. It moves from al-Andalus to Persia, Mughal India, North Africa and the Ottomans, and closes with notes on garden design today. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987} The museum's scan covers the title page, the contents, parts of the opening chapters and most of the chapter on Mughal India, which is the part used for the [[building/taj-mahal|Taj Mahal]] and its garden. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987}

## How the museum reads him
The source record describes the work as a designer's survey for general readers and notes that its judgements on rulers and religions are the author's own. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987} For that reason the museum uses him for the layout and plants of gardens and attributes his views to him by name.

## What is not known
No birth or death date, place of origin, training or career is given in the sources the museum has read, and none is invented here. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987}
