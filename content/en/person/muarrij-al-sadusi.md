## Summary
Mu'arrij ibn 'Amr ibn al-Harith ibn Thawr al-Sadusi, Basran grammarian; he took Arabic from al-Khalil ibn Ahmad and transmitted hadith from Shababa ibn al-Hajjaj and Abu 'Amr ibn al-'Ala'; he used to say: I came in from the desert with no knowledge of analogy in Arabic — my knowledge was instinct, and the first analogy I learned was in Abu Zayd al-Ansari's circle at Basra; language and poetry dominated him; among his works are the Anwa', Gharib al-Qur'an, Jamahir al-Qaba'il and al-Ma'ani, and a neat one-volume epitome of Quraysh's genealogy. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
