## Summary
Qutb al-Din Muhammad ibn 'Imad al-Din Zangi, lord of Sinjar, ruled after his father while the state's management rested with his father's mamluk Mujahid al-Din Qaranqush. He was pious, virtuous, just, of good conduct, much given to kindness and charity to the poor — except that he was fiercely partisan on behalf of Imam al-Shafi'i's madhhab and much given to denouncing the Shafi'is... he was, in fact, a Hanbali: out of partisanship he built a Hanafi madrasa at Sinjar and stipulated that supervision be for the Hanafis of his own descendants, not the Shafi'is, and that the muezzin and keeper follow Abu Hanifa's madhhab. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
