## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Bahkali al-Damdi and then al-Sabiyi al-Tihami al-Yamani was a historian, born in the town of Sabya, who moved between it and Sanaa {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-Mansur Ali ibn al-Abbas appointed him ruler in Bayt al-Faqih, where his conduct in the judgeship was praised {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Works
He wrote «Nafh al-Ud bi-Dhikr Dawlat al-Sharif Hamud», a manuscript, which records events in the Tihama of Yemen up to 1225 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. He also wrote «al-Afawiq bi-Tarajim al-Bukhari wal-Ta'aliq», «al-Thiqat bi-Ma'rifat Tabaqat Rijal al-Ummahat», and «Taysir al-Yusra bi-Sharh al-Mujtaba min al-Sunan al-Kubra», a commentary on al-Nasa'i in several volumes {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Death
He died from the effects of poison secretly given to him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-A'lam gives his birth as 1182 AH, equal to 1768 CE, and his death as 1248 AH, equal to 1832 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
