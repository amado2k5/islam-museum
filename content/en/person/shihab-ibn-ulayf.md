## Summary

Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Ibn al-Ulayf al-Mahalli al-Shafii. He worked in literature and composed smooth verse on every theme. Fortune brought him the best of his age, and he was given blessings of usefulness, a comfortable life and contentment {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 554}.

A salary was arranged for him, on the model of Ibn al-Ulayf, at the end of the reign of Sultan al-Ashraf Qaytbay, his cousin. When Sultan Qansuh al-Ghuri took power, he granted him a land allotment, and in his days his condition was good. He died in the year 926 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 554}.
