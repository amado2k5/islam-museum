## Overview
Shah Sulayman I was the eighth Safavid shah; he reigned from 1077/1666 to 1105/1694 and was the father of Shah Sultan Husayn. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 635} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 826}

## His reign in the dynasty's story
The article on the Safavids places Sulayman and his son Sultan Husayn as the last two Safavid shahs, calls both weak and pliable products of the harem system, and says Sulayman, an alcoholic, had little interest in affairs of state; the pace of decline accelerated under them. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 826} The article on Husayn says the son was brought up in the harem on the practice begun under Shah Abbas I. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 635} These are the assessments of the articles' authors, not the museum's description.
