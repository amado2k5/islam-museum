## Summary
Nurdiyana binti Amir Hashim, known as Diana Amir, is a Malaysian former actress and host turned dawah figure, born in Birmingham, England, on 22 June 1986. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

She worked on screen from 2008 to 2022, and in December 2022 she announced her retirement from the entertainment industry after fourteen years. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

YADIM, the Islamic Dawah Foundation of Malaysia, lists her among its Da'wah Icons on its achievements page. {cite:yadim-diana-amir}

Her verified Instagram account counted 1,347,190 followers in a direct read on 15 November 2026. {cite:instagram-dianaamir}

## Early life and education
She was born in Birmingham, England, the fourth of seven siblings, and was schooled at Sekolah Menengah Kebangsaan (P) Sultan Ibrahim in Johor Bahru. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

She took a bachelor's degree in human resource management at Universiti Sains Malaysia in Penang. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

## Career and turn to dawah
Her filmography, as the article lists it, runs from 2008 and includes the films 3 Temujanji and Salam Cinta and hosting work, including on TV AlHijrah. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

She began wearing the hijab in 2011, a step the article records on the reporting of Sinar Harian. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms}

In December 2022 she announced her retirement from the industry she had worked in for fourteen years, and her public role since has been that of a dawah figure, including the Da'wah Icon listing by YADIM. {cite:wikipedia-diana-amir-ms} {cite:yadim-diana-amir}
