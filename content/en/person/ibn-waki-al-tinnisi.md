## Summary
Abu Muhammad al-Hasan ibn 'Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn Waki'; of Baghdad origin, born at Tinnis; Abu Mansur al-Tha'alibi mentions him in the Yatimat al-Dahr and says of him: a brilliant poet and all-round scholar who surpassed the people of his time — none outran him in his hour; he has wonderful books and a diwan, and he compiled a work in which he exposed the plagiarisms of Abu'l-Tayyib al-Mutanabbi. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
