## Summary
Habiba al-Adawiyya was a devout woman of [[place/basra|Basra]] recorded by Ibn al-Jawzi in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710}

## Her night prayer
He relates from Abd Allah al-Makki that after the night prayer she would go up on a roof, fasten her shift and veil, and say: "My God, the stars have set, eyes sleep, kings have locked their doors, and Your door is open; every lover is alone with his beloved, and this is my place before You." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710} At the pre-dawn she would pray: "O God, this night has turned away and the day has dawned; would that I knew whether You accepted my night, so that I may rejoice, or rejected it, so that I may be consoled." She swore that this would be her way as long as she lived, and that even if He drove her away she would not leave His door. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710}

## For young visitors
A woman of Basra who went up on the roof at night and spoke to her Lord while everyone else slept.
