## Summary
Sherif Mostafa is an Egyptian Quran reciter who recites in the murattal style in the riwaya of Hafs 'an 'Asim. {cite:surahquran-sherif-mostafa}

He publishes his recitations through his official YouTube channel (@sherifmostafaofficial); the descriptions of uploads of his recitations direct listeners to "the official channel of reciter Sharif Mustafa". {cite:youtube-sherifmostafaofficial}

## His recitations
Among his widely circulated recitations are those of Maryam, Yusuf, al-Baqarah, Taha and al-Kahf, and his recitation of the whole of Juz' Qad Sami'. {cite:youtube-sherifmostafaofficial}

His reciter page on the SurahQuran site presents his complete murattal recitation in the riwaya of Hafs from 'Asim. {cite:surahquran-sherif-mostafa}

## Reach and audience
His reputation rests on the digital spread of his recitations through his channel, Quran listening platforms and phone apps carrying his recitations; no dated subscriber figure for his channel could be verified when checked on 25 October 2026, so none is recorded. {cite:youtube-sherifmostafaofficial} {cite:surahquran-sherif-mostafa}
