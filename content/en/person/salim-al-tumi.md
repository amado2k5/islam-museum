## Summary
Ruler of Algiers at the start of the sixteenth century whose power, in al-Salabi's words, rested on Spanish backing {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 210}.

## The end
He besieged the brothers [[person/uruj-barbarossa|Aruj]] and [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din]] at Jijel; then Aruj entered Algiers with Ottoman support and killed him on proof of his appeals for Spanish troops, in 1516 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 210}.
