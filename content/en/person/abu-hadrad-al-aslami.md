## Summary

Abu Hadrad al-Aslami, father of Abdallah. His name is given variously as Salama ibn Umayr, Abd or Ubayd. He narrated from the Prophet and was narrated from by his son. Ibn Sa'd dates his death to 71 AH; Mughlatai holds that the dated entry belongs to his son Abdallah ibn Abi Hadrad.
