## Overview
Rahat Fateh Ali Khan (born 1974 in Faisalabad) is a Pakistani qawwali singer. He is the nephew and student of Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and brought qawwali to Bollywood cinema and to world film soundtracks. {cite:wikidata}
