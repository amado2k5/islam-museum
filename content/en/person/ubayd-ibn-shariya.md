## Overview
Ubayd ibn Shariya al-Jurhumi, a narrator of reports, who came as a delegate to Mu'awiya. He was one of the people of knowledge of the reports of the past and the histories of the ancient Arabs {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.549-550}.

al-Mas'udi reports that his book was in circulation in his time, and that a group of people of knowledge said his reports were fabricated from invented fables {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.549-550}.

## In Muhammad Qabbani, The Umayyad State from Birth to Fall (Damascus: Dar Wahy al-Qalam, 2006)
Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan ordered that what Ubayd ibn Sharya used to narrate to him in his assembly, of the histories of the ancient kings of Yemen and others, be written down {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.168}.

The book reports that Mu'awiya was keen to know the histories of the earlier nations, in the context of the beginnings of codification {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.168}.
