## Overview
Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, a well-known imam in theology and Qur'anic exegesis, as Ibn al-Athir describes him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.304}

## After the killing of al-Ghuri
When Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri was killed in 602 AH, some people of Ghazna came to his mamluks and told them that al-Razi had killed their master, because he had brought about the death of the man who killed him and had brought down the Khwarazm Shah. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.304}

They rose against him to kill him, but he fled and went to the vizier Mu'ayyid al-Mulk, who secretly sent him to a place of safety. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.304}

This is Ibn al-Athir's report of what the people of Ghazna said, not a judgement of the museum.
