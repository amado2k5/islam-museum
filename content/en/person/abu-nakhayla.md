## Overview
Abu Nakhayla, a poet whose verses praising Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik al-Mas'udi reports. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 250-251}

When he entered upon Abu al-Abbas, he recited verses in which he said that everything he had said about anyone else was falsehood. The caliph was pleased with him, gave him a gift and rewarded him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 250-251}
