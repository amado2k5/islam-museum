## Overview
[[person/abu-malik-al-ashari|Abu Malik al-Ash'ari]], a Companion who came to the Prophet by ship with the Ash'aris, and is counted among the Syrians. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1391}

## His coming
He came to the Prophet by ship with the Ash'aris, and he had companionship. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1391}

## His name
His name is disputed: it is said to be Ka'b ibn Malik, Ka'b ibn Asim, Ubayd, Amr, or al-Harith. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1391}

## Transmission from him
Yazid ibn al-Had transmitted from him, from Tha'laba ibn Abi Malik. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1391}

## For young visitors
Abu Malik al-Ash'ari was a Companion who came to the Prophet with his people, the Ash'aris, by ship. Scholars differ on his name, and he is known by his kunya.

## Notes for researchers
- The entry in "Usd al-Ghaba" is brief, and it does not connect him with the well-known hadith on purification in the Jami'; we have not linked it here, and it should be checked before relying on it.
- "Usd al-Ghaba" on the same page mentions Abu Malik al-Ash'ja'i, Abu Malik al-Ghifari, Abu Malik al-Qurazi and al-Nakha'i. These are different people, and this material concerns al-Ash'ari alone.
- Ibn al-Athir says he is "counted among the Syrians"; this has not been checked in a second source.
