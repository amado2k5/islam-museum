## Overview
Zur'a ibn al-Nu'man, or it is said al-Nu'man ibn Zur'a, of the [[group/banu-taghlib|Banu Taghlib]]. al-Baladhuri reports that he appealed to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] on behalf of the Banu Taghlib, saying that they were a people of the Arabs who disdained the poll tax and that its severity would harm them. Umar brought them back after they had fled, and made peace with them at double the alms {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He appears in [[event/treaty-with-taghlib|the treaty with Taghlib]] as an intercessor with Umar {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
