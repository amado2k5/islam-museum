## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Musa al-Hashimi, Abu Ali, was a judge and one of the Hanbali scholars, of Baghdad by birth and by death. The entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 345 AH, 957 CE, to 428 AH, 1037 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His standing and books
He was favoured by the two imams, the Abbasid caliphs al-Qadir bi-llah and al-Qaim bi-amr Allah, and held a teaching circle in the Mosque of al-Mansur. He wrote books; al-Zirikli names al-Irshad, on jurisprudence, and a commentary on the book of al-Khiraqi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
