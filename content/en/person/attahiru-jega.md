# Attahiru Jega

Attahiru Muhammadu Jega (born 11 January 1957 at Jega, Northern Region, Nigeria) is a Nigerian academic and Hausa political scientist who taught at Bayero University Kano {cite:wikipedia-attahiru-jega-en}. He graduated from Bayero and took his doctorate at Northwestern University in 1985, and he first came to national prominence as president of the Academic Staff Union of Universities (ASUU) under Nigeria's military governments {cite:wikipedia-attahiru-jega-en}.

## From the university to the elections

Jega became vice-chancellor of Bayero University in 2005 {cite:wikipedia-attahiru-jega-en}. On 30 June 2010 President Goodluck Jonathan appointed him chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC); he conducted the 2011 general elections and the fiercely contested 2015 elections in which former head of state Muhammadu Buhari achieved Nigeria's first democratic transfer of power to an opposition party {cite:wikipedia-attahiru-jega-en}. His term ended on 30 June 2015 and he returned to Bayero University; in March 2025 President Bola Tinubu appointed him Special Adviser and Coordinator of the Presidential Livestock Reform Initiative {cite:wikipedia-attahiru-jega-en}.
