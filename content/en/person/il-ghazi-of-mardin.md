## Overview
Najm al-Din Il-Ghazi ibn Artuq al-Turkmani, lord of Mardin, who succeeded his brother Sukman there. The two brothers were among the amirs of Tutush, lord of Syria. He died in 516 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.529}

## His career
He seized Aleppo after the sons of Tutush died, and took Mayyafariqin. Ibn al-Imad describes him as a brave and valiant knight, given to raiding and to generosity. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.529}

## After him
His son Husam al-Din Tumurtash succeeded him in Mardin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.529}
