## Overview
Abd Allah al-Ma'mun ibn [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]], an Abbasid caliph. He was given allegiance in Baghdad after the deposition of his brother al-Amin in 196 AH, and died in 218 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.428} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.646} Al-Tabari mentions the Mihna of the Qur'an in his reign. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.631}

## His upbringing and affair
Al-Suyuti says that he was born on the night of the death of his uncle al-Hadi and the allegiance of his father, in 170 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 456} Al-Mas'udi says that al-Rashid said of him that he recognised in him the firmness of al-Mansur and the asceticism of al-Mahdi, and that his mother was a Badhghisi woman called Maraji. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 337-340}

Al-Mas'udi says that at the start of his affair, when al-Fadl ibn Sahl had the upper hand over him, he leaned toward considering astrological judgements. When he came to Iraq he gave that up, sat with the theologians, favoured the disputants, and kept the jurists and men of letters in his session, paying them their stipends. He describes him as the most forgiving of people and the most generous with money. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 672}

## His caliphate
Al-Suyuti says that he marched in 204 AH and entered Baghdad in Safar, and that in 218 AH he tested the people with the doctrine that the Qur'an was created. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 487-489} He says that among the caliphs only [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] and al-Ma'mun had memorised the Qur'an. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 496-497}

Al-Mas'udi says that he died at al-Badidun, at the spring of al-Qushayra, in 218 AH at the age of forty-nine, and was buried in Tarsus. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 399-402}
