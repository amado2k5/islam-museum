## Summary
Sayyar al-Nabbaji wept and cried out to God, and had wept for God for sixty years. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 541, sequence 542, on page 166. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}

## A vision of Paradise
Ahmad ibn Masruq relates that Sayyar al-Nabbaji, who had wept for God for sixty years, said: one night I missed my litany, and as I was in that state I saw myself enter Paradise: a river ran over pearls and jewels, musk on its banks, domes of pearl and castles of gold and jewels by the water, and maidens on the shore saying: glory to the One glorified in every place. I asked who they were, and they said: we are a voice of the creation of the Merciful. I asked for whom they were, and they answered in verse: for a people who stand at night on their feet, speaking intimately with the Lord of the worlds, while their cares run and people sleep. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}
