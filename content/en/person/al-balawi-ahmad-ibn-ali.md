## Overview
Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Dawud al-Balawi al-Wadi Ashi, a philologist, man of letters and poet from Wadi Ash, who belongs to the Bali tribe. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 866 AH and his death as 938 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.84}
