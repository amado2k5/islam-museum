## Overview
Umayya ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf ibn Qusayy, the ancestor from whom the Banu Umayya trace their descent {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.61-62}.

## The rivalry with Hashim
al-Baladhuri reports that Umayya was wealthy, and attempted to do what [[person/hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] did in feeding Quraysh, and failed. Some people mocked him and blamed him {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.61-62}.

Umayya grew angry and challenged Hashim to a contest for fifty black-eyed she-camels to be slaughtered in Mecca, and for ten years of exile. They appointed as arbiter a Khuza'i soothsayer whose home was at Usfan {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.61-62}.

The soothsayer ruled for Hashim. Hashim slaughtered the camels and fed their meat, and Umayya went out to Syria and stayed there for ten years. al-Baladhuri calls this the first enmity to arise between Hashim and Umayya {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.61-62}.
