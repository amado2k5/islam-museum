## Overview
Salih al-Naji, of the Banu Naji, and among the leading Ibadis according to Ibn al-Nadim. He wrote the book "al-Tawhid" and the book "al-Radd ala al-Mukhalifin" (A Refutation of Opponents) {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.259}.

The museum's data carries an English description that places him in the Najiyya sect of the Kharijites. This differs from the description of the Ibadis in the Arabic source, and it needs an additional source.
