## Overview
Isa ibn Idris ibn Ma'qil al-Ajali. Al-Baladhuri says that he settled in Karaj, gained control of it, and built its fortress. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Karaj
His brother al-Qasim ibn Isa was Abu Dulaf. He widened the fortress and established the towns of Karaj, which came to be called "Karaj of Abu Dulaf". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
