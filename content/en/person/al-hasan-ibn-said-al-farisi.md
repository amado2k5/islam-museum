## Overview
Abu Ahmad al-Farisi the bazzar, known as Ibn al-Bastaban; he heard Sufyan b. 'Uyayna, Ibn 'Uluyya and Dawud b. al-Habbar, and al-Mahamili and Ibn Makhlad transmitted from him; Ibn Abi Hatim said he was truthful. He died in Rabi' I 263 {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 133}.
