## Overview
Al-Sayyid Ahmad al-Husayni al-Shirazi al-Abiji, the imam-scholar; born at Shiraz, took from the shaykhs, entered Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem and Syria and received from some of their scholars and transmitted — the kings of his age favoured him; he died at Mecca in Jumada I 895 at seventy-one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 357}
