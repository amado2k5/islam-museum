## Overview
Atsiz ibn Uwaq al-Khwarazmi, a Turkish commander in the army of Sultan Alp Arslan. Al-Salabi says that the sultan sent him to the south of Syria, where he took al-Ramla and Jerusalem from the Fatimids, but he could not take Ascalon, the gate to Egypt. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.30}

Damascus fell at his hand in 468 AH (1075 CE), and the khutba for the Abbasid caliph was given. He then failed in his raid on Egypt, for he was defeated by a force of Arabs before he met the army of Badr al-Jamali in Rajab 469 AH (1076 CE). {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.33-34}

His affair ended with his killing around 1078 CE, a date that the source gives as approximate. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.34}
