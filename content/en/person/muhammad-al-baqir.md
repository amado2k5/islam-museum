## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, al-Baqir. One of the jurist Successors in [[place/medina|Medina]], whom al-Shirazi mentions in the second class. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

Mus'ab al-Zubayri, as al-Shirazi relays, says that he died in 114 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
