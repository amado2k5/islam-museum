## Overview
Abu Yahya al-Ghafiqi, the muqri'; he took the readings from his father, Abu'l-Hasan Shurayh and a company, and taught Qur'an at Alexandria and Cairo; Sultan Salah al-Din had him dictate to him, drew him near and honoured him — jurist, mufti, muhaddith, muqri', genealogist, chronicler and fine calligrapher, and it is said he was the first to preach the 'Abbasid da'wa in Egypt. He died in Rajab 575. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 730}
