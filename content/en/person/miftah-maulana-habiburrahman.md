## Summary

Miftah Maulana Habiburrahman, known as Gus Miftah, is an Indonesian preacher born on 5 August 1981 in Adilihur, Jabung, East Lampung{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}. He leads the Ora Aji Islamic boarding school in Sleman and became known for preaching to marginalised communities, in nightclubs and in red-light districts{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}. In October 2024 he was appointed President Prabowo Subianto's Special Envoy for Religious Harmony and the Development of Religious Facilities, and he resigned in December of the same year{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}.

## Upbringing and education

Miftah descends from Kyai Muhammad Ageng Besari, founder of the Tegalsari pesantren in Ponorogo{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}. He studied at UIN Sunan Kalijaga in Yogyakarta without completing the degree, was active in the Nahdlatul Ulama-affiliated student movement PMII, and completed a bachelor's degree in Islamic Religious Education in 2023 at Sultan Agung Islamic University in Semarang{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}.

## Preaching to the margins

Miftah built his fame on a preaching style that goes to those the pulpits do not usually reach: nightclub audiences, red-light districts and the socially marginalised{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}; a recording of a sermon he gave in a Bali nightclub spread widely in 2019{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}. He preaches in colloquial Javanese and Indonesian with direct dialogue, and runs his Ora Aji school in Sleman near Yogyakarta{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}.

## The special envoy post and the resignation

Positions are reported here as the sources give them; they are their holders' statements, not the museum's verdict. On 20 November 2024, at a mass preaching gathering in Mungkid, Miftah addressed an iced-tea vendor with an insulting word; the clip spread and a petition of more than 215,000 signatures demanded his resignation{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}. Miftah apologised to the vendor in person on 4 December, then tendered his resignation as special envoy on 6 December 2024{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}.

## Impact and audience

Miftah is among Indonesia's most followed digital preachers, with about 2.2 million Instagram followers and 1.14 million YouTube subscribers in platform figures of November 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:wikipedia-miftah-maulana-habiburrahman-en}.
