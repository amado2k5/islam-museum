## Summary
Afonso de Albuquerque — 'al-Buqarq' in al-Salabi's text — the Portuguese commander in the Indian Ocean {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 182}.

## His letter to the shah
After seizing Hormuz, the book records, he wrote Shah [[person/ismail-i-safavi|Ismail]]: "I honour your esteem for the Christians in your lands, and offer you the fleet, the soldiers and the weapons to use against the Turkish forts in India; and should you wish to strike the lands of the Arabs or attack Mecca you will find me beside you in the Red Sea before Jeddah, or in Aden, Bahrain, Qatif or Basra — the shah will find me at his side along the Persian coast, and I shall do all he wishes" {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 182}.

## Death and the alliance
His death, al-Salabi says, obstructed completion of the Portuguese-Safavid alliance {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 182}; elsewhere the book records Portugal's declared aims as reaching the storming of the Prophet's Mosque to take the Prophet's remains and bargain them for Jerusalem {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 193}.
