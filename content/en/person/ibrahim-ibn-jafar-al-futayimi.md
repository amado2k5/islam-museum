## Overview
He is Ibrahim ibn Jafar al-Futayimi, whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put over [[place/dhamar|Dhamar]] when he left it in Rabi I 288 AH {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.213}. The narrator says nothing more about him; the Futayimis are as the book spells them and the museum does not tie them to a group record. See [[event/al-hadi-at-dhamar-hilan-and-al-akhtut-288|al-Hadi at Dhamar and al-Akhtut]].
