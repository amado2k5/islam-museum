## Shihab al-Din Ibn Hajji al-Hisbani

Judge Shihab al-Din Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Hajji al-Hisbani al-Dimashqi al-Atrush al-Shafii was born on the night of Wednesday, the fifth of Dhu al-Hijja in the year 818. Before he became deaf, he heard hadith from the hadith master Ibn Hajar and from Ala al-Din ibn Bardas al-Ba'li, among others {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 450}.

He gave al-Nu'aymi permission to transmit hadith on his authority, and licensed him to transmit everything he was allowed to transmit. He died on Wednesday, the seventh of Ramadan in the year 907 and was buried in the cemetery of Bab al-Faradis {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 450}.
