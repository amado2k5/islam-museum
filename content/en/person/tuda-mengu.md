## Overview
Tuda Mengu ibn Mongke Temur, king of the Dasht in the north. Ibn Khaldun reports that he inherited rule from his father [[person/mengu-timur-dashti|Mongke Temur]] in the year 681 AH, and sat on the throne of their kingdom at Sarai for five years {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## Withdrawal
He then took the monastic life and gave up rule in the year 686 AH, and attached himself to the company of the poor shaykhs. Qalbagha ruled in his place {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
