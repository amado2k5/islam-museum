## Overview
Inal, brother of Tughril Beg. Al-Suyuti says that al-Basasiri was his secretary and that he tempted him with the post of his brother, so he rose against his brother. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 643}

Tughril Beg occupied himself with him, then prevailed over him and killed him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 643}
