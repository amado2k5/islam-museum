## Overview
Muhyi al-Din Abu Muhammad Mustafa ibn Kamal al-Din ibn Ali al-Bakri al-Siddiqi al-Dimashqi, a Sufi scholar from Damascus. He followed the Khalwati order and was one of the scholars of the al-Bakri al-Siddiqi family. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates
He was born in 1099 AH (1688 CE) and died in 1162 AH (1749 CE), according to the account reported. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
