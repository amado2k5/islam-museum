Abu Muslim Salama ibn Amr ibn al-Akwa' al-Aslami, an eminent Companion famed for surpassing valour and speed of foot — it was said he could outrun horses at a run on his own two feet. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He witnessed the Pledge of Ridwan and pledged to the Prophet three times: among the first of the people, their middle, and their last. His rare heroism shone in the Dhu Qarad expedition when he chased Ghatafan's raiders alone on foot until he recovered the Prophet's milch-camels; he witnessed the conquest of Khaybar and the battles after it and was among the people of fatwa in Medina. {cite:sahih-muslim}

He settled at al-Rabadha late in life after Uthman's killing, then came to Medina and died there in year 74 of the hijra at about eighty years. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
