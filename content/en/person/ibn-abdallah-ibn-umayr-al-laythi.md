## Overview
Ibn Abdallah ibn Umayr al-Laythi, a brother of Abdallah ibn Amir on his mother's side. Qutayba ibn Muslim appointed him as his deputy over Sistan when he left for Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
