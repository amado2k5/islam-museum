## Overview
He is As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur of [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]], who was in the prison of Shibam; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] called him with those of Al Ya'fur and Al Tarif who were in the prison, the text naming him as an example of them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}. Al-Hadi told them he had given them their lives and urged them to fear God, and freed them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}. The record of [[group/banu-yafur]] already named him among those who fought al-Hadi after he entered Sana'a. The text gives neither his father nor his age. See [[event/al-hadi-frees-asad-ibn-abi-yafur-and-ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-from-the-prison-of-shibam-288]].
