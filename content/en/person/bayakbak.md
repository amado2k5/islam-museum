## Overview
Bayakbak, a Turkish amir. He went out with [[person/musa-ibn-bugha|Musa ibn Bugha]] to Sinn in pursuit of Musawir. [[person/al-muhtadi|Al-Muhtadi]] wrote to him to kill Musa and Muflih, and to be the amir over all the Turks. Bayakbak showed Musa the letter and said that this action would befall them all, so they agreed to kill al-Muhtadi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.563}

Al-Mas'udi says that Bayakbak fell into the hands of al-Muhtadi, who struck off his neck and threw him to his companions. It is also said that he was killed in the battle of the bridge of Samarra. Al-Mas'udi says the Turks' anger at al-Muhtadi was because of this killing. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.559}

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]], in his account of al-Mu'tamid's caliphate, records the death of the Turk Bayakbak and says al-Mu'tamid passed the provinces he had held, Egypt among them, to the Turk Yarjukh, who wrote confirming Ahmad ibn Tulun as governor of Egypt. He does not say how Bayakbak died. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 621}
