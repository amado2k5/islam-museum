## Overview
Sultan al-Dawla Abu Shuja' b. Baha' al-Dawla b. 'Adud al-Dawla b. Rukn al-Dawla b. Buwayh al-Daylami — the last Buyid amir of Iraq; he was acclaimed after his father's death in 403 and held the realm for twelve years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 166}

## His end
In Ibn al-Jawzi's account he died at Shiraz in 415 at thirty-two years and five months, and the khutba at Baghdad passed to his brother Musharrif al-Dawla. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.359}
