## Overview
Kamal al-Din al-Muzaffar ibn Ali ibn Nasir al-Qurashi, Abu Mansur al-Himsi, a physician and man of letters born in Homs. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## What the data say
The data record his death in 612 AH (about 1215 CE) as reported. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} He was a Sunni, and his role as a physician was in the Ayyubid and Mamluk age.
