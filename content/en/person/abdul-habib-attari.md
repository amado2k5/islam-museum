## Summary
Abdul Habib Attari, known as Haji Abdul Habib Attari, is a Pakistani Islamic scholar and preacher from Karachi, a member of the Central Executive Committee (Rukn-e-Shura) of Dawat-e-Islami. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} His YouTube channel counted 2,300,000 subscribers in November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:vidiq-abdul-habib-attari}

## Beginnings and education
His official website states that he was born on 14 October 1974 — 27 Ramadan 1394 in its Hijri dating — in Karachi, Pakistan. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} He graduated in the Dars-e-Nizami curriculum from Jamia Amjadiya in Karachi. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} The same site says that, alongside his scholarly achievements, the most important factor of his life is the company and spiritual upbringing of Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri, the founder of Dawat-e-Islami. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari}

## Responsibilities in Dawat-e-Islami
He is a member of the Central Executive Committee of Dawat-e-Islami. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} His official website names him head of Madani Channel, the movement's round-the-clock Islamic television channel, and head of Jamia-tul-Madinah, Dawat-e-Islami's institutions producing Islamic scholars. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} It also places him in charge of the movement's departments for Human Resources, Foreign Affairs and Business Communities. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari}

## Programmes and works
His best-known Madani Channel programme is Zehni Azmaish, which his website says has attracted worldwide viewership; his other named programmes include Aik Qissa hai Quran se and Ziarat-e-Maqamat-e-Muqaddasa. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} He recorded the Urdu translation of the Qur'an, Kanzul Iman, which his website says was acclaimed by thousands of Muslims around the world. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari} His website describes him as a soft-spoken, eloquent speaker who delivers lectures across the world on the Sunnah, the teachings of the Qur'an and the sayings of Abdul Qadir Jilani. {cite:abdulhabibattari-abdul-habib-attari}

## Impact and audience
His YouTube channel began on 27 May 2017, with its channel country set to Pakistan. {cite:vidiq-abdul-habib-attari} It stood at 2,300,000 subscribers, 430,551,020 total views and 10,838 videos at vidIQ's update of 5 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-abdul-habib-attari} The channel's most-viewed uploads are from his Islah e Aamaal (reform of deeds) series — on the virtue of patience, on the supplication God answers, and on deeds that waste one's good works — together with a heart-touching supplication video of 2021. {cite:vidiq-abdul-habib-attari}
