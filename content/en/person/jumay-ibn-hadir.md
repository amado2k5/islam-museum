## Overview
Jumay ibn Hadir al-Bajji, a judge appointed by the agent of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz to hear the claim of the people of Samarqand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The case of Samarqand
The people of Samarqand claimed that Qutayba had entered their city and settled Muslims in it by treachery. He ruled that the Muslims should leave, on terms that they would then be treated on an equal footing in war. The people of the city disliked war, and so they acknowledged the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
