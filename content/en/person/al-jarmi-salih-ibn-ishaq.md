## Overview
Abu Umar Salih ibn Ishaq al-Jarmi al-Basri, a philologist and grammarian, client of the Banu Jarm ibn Zaban. The source counts him among the leading grammarians of Basra. He died in 225 AH (840 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
