## Summary
Hasan ibn 'Uman ibn al-'Adil the Ayyubid, lord of Hisn Kayfa, died in 859 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 294}.

## Details
Among the dead of 859 Ibn al-'Imad lists "the lord of Hisn Kayfa, Hasan ibn 'Uman ibn al-'Adil al-Ayyubi" — one of the Ayyubid princes who ruled Hisn Kayfa on the Tigris, the fortress-city that served as seat of an Ayyubid branch in the Jazira and Diyar Bakr {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 294}. The source adds nothing beyond his descent from al-Malik al-'Adil, brother of Saladin, whose line held this province for generations, and from the previous lord of the fortress {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 294}. Recorded among the same year's dead are the Meccan amir al-Zayn Barakat ibn 'Ajlan and 'Izz al-Din al-Qaylawi of Cairo, showing the breadth of the year's losses from the Hijaz to the Jazira to Egypt {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 294}.
