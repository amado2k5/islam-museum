## Summary
[[person/abu-al-darda|Abu al-Darda]] — "famed by his kunya and his name alike", as Ibn Hajar says; his name is Uwaymir ibn Qays al-Ansari al-Khazraji {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1161}. His name is disputed — some say it is Amir and Uwaymir a nickname, as al-Asma'i asserted — and his father's name is disputed: Amir, Malik, Tha'laba, Abdullah or Zayd {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1161}.

## His conversion and Uhud
Abu Shahr said from Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz: he became Muslim on the day of Badr, witnessed [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] and fought gallantly there {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba pp. 1161-1162}. Ibn Hajar relates from Shurayh ibn Ubayd that the Prophet said on the day of Uhud: "what a fine horseman Uwaymir is", and said "he is the sage of my community" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1162}.

## Trade and Islam
Al-A'mash said from Khaythama from him: I was a merchant before the mission, then tried trade after Islam and the two could not be joined {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1162}. Ibn Hibban said Mu'awiya appointed him judge of [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in Umar's caliphate {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1162}.

## Transmission from him
He related from the Prophet and from Zayd ibn Thabit, A'isha, Abu Umama and Fadala ibn Ubayd; from him relate his son Bilal, his wife Umm al-Darda', Abu Idris al-Khawlani, Suwayd ibn Ghafla, Jubayr ibn Nufayr, Zayd ibn Wahb, Alqama ibn Qays and others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1162}.

## His death: the differing views
Abu Shahr said from Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz: Abu al-Darda' and Ka'b al-Ahbar died in the last two years of Uthman's caliphate; al-Waqidi and a group said he died in year thirty-two; Ibn Abd al-Barr said he died after Siffin — and the soundest view among the hadith scholars is that he died in Uthman's caliphate {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1162}.
