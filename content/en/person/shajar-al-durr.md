## Overview
Shajar al-Durr, slave-girl of al-Malik al-Salih Ayyub and mother of Khalil. She was known as the sultana of Egypt {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709-710}.

## After the death of al-Salih Ayyub
She concealed his death in the year 647 AH, when the Franks took Damietta, until his son Turan Shah arrived. When Turan Shah was killed by his father's mamluks, they made her their ruler. The Turks swore allegiance to her and to her deputy Izz al-Din Aybak. She began to issue appointments to the amirs and grants {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709-710}.

## Aybak
Aybak then made himself sultan on his own, took the title al-Malik al-Mu'izz, and the sermon was given in the names of both of them, and coins were struck in their names. In the year 655 AH she killed him {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709-710}.
