## Overview
Mu'nis al-Muzaffar, a military commander of the Abbasid state. He left Mosul in Shawwal 320 AH heading for Baghdad, and the encounter took place at al-Shammasiyya, where the caliph [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]] was killed. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.148}

Al-Tabari says that Mu'nis was greatly distressed when the body was carried to him. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.152}

The data do not record his birth or death, nor his other reports, and need an additional source.
