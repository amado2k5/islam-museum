## Overview
Waqid ibn Amr al-Tamimi, an author mentioned by Ibn al-Nadim in the Fihrist {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.480}.

## His work
The Fihrist reports that he composed the reports of Babak al-Khurrami. In them he related the beginning of his affair, his upbringing, his rising, his wars and his killing {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.480}.

His life and the date of his death need an additional source.
