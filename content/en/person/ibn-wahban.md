## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ahmad ibn Wahban al-Harithi al-Dimashqi, Amin al-Din, was a Hanafi jurist and man of letters, called Ibn Wahban in the heading of the entry. The entry dates his death to 768 AH, equal to 1367 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## Career
He held the judgeship of Hama and died at about forty years of age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Qayd al-Shara'id, in manuscript, a poem of a thousand lines containing rare questions of jurisprudence; Iqd al-Qa'id, in manuscript, a commentary on Qayd al-Shara'id in two volumes, with copies at the Chester Beatty library under number 4536 and at al-Sadiqiyya; Ahasin al-Akhbar fi Mahasin al-Sab'a al-Akhyar, in manuscript, meaning the seven readers; and a manuscript poem of 127 lines on the reading of Abu Amr, whose title the entry prints as al-Mtithal al-Amr fi Qira'at Abi Amr {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## Death
He died in 768 AH, equal to 1367 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.
