## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Maymun the Maghribi Sufi, who succeeded Abu Madyan in his path; of the Maghreb, settled at Marrakesh, a man of scrupulous piety. It was thought odd that a treatise be attributed to him — being so scrupulous and a teacher — yet it was attributed because he alone ordered it and many profited by it; he died at Azammur in the Marrakesh province in 607 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 34-35}.
