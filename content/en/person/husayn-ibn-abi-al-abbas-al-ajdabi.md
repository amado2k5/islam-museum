## Overview
Husayn ibn Abi al-Abbas Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Ajdabi, Abu Abd Allah. A Maliki jurist, reader of the Qur'an and transmitter of hadith from Kairouan, whose origin was in Ajdabiya in Libya. He took from the leading figures of the Kairouan school. He died in 432 AH (1040 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.289-291}
