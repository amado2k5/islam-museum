## Overview
Sati' ibn Muhammad Hilal ibn al-Sayyid Mustafa al-Husri, from an Arab family of merchants in Aleppo. The encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' counts him among the most prominent theorists of Arab nationalism, and he wrote on education and politics {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## His dates
The encyclopedia gives his birth as 1880 CE (1298 AH) and his death as 1968 CE (1388 AH) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
