## Overview
Isma'il ibn Ahmad al-Samani. Al-Tabari relates that he captured Amr ibn al-Layth in 287 AH, and that al-Mu'tadid praised him for it. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.76} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.77}

## After his brother
Al-Mas'udi says that the rule passed to him after the death of his brother Nasr ibn Ahmad in 289 AH. Al-Mu'tadid sent him rich gifts and three hundred thousand dinars, to distribute among his followers and send them to Sistan to fight Tahir ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn al-Layth. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.628-632}

## The Turkish campaign
Ibn al-Jawzi says that he raided the lands of the Turks in 280 AH, conquered the city of their king, and captured the king and Khatun. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.146}
