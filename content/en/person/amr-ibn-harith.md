## Overview
Amr ibn al-Harith, a young man of Quraysh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.428}

Al-Baladhuri relates that he bought the two chests of jewels from the spoils of Nahavand from al-Sa'ib ibn al-Aqra' with the stipends of the dependants and the fighters. He sold one of them at al-Hira for the price he had paid for both, and the other remained to him; this was the first luxury of wealth he took up. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.428}
