# Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud

Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud is the seventh King of Saudi Arabia, reigning since 23 January 2015. He was born at Riyadh on 31 December 1935, a son of King Abdulaziz by Hassa bint Ahmed Al Sudairi, and one of the Sudairi Seven. {cite:wikipedia-salman-bin-abdulaziz-en} {cite:wikidata-salman-bin-abdulaziz}

## Early life and education

He was educated at the Princes' School in Riyadh, where he studied Islam and Arabic, focusing on sharia in the Wahhabi interpretation under the Hanbali school of jurisprudence, and learned the Quran under a cleric in the Wahhabi tradition. As the note below explains, that passage attests the school of his education — his formation — not a declared personal affiliation. {cite:wikipedia-salman-bin-abdulaziz-en}

## Offices before the throne

He was deputy governor and then governor of Riyadh for some 48 years (1963–2011), then Minister of Defense, and from 2012 Crown Prince. {cite:wikipedia-salman-bin-abdulaziz-en}

## Reign

He succeeded his brother King Abdullah on 23 January 2015. In his reign his son Mohammed bin Salman became Crown Prince in 2017 and Prime Minister in 2022, leading wide-ranging reforms in the country. {cite:wikipedia-salman-bin-abdulaziz-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-salman-en}
