## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Zakariyya al-Zuhri al-Qurtubi, known as al-Ifliili. A vizier and one of the imams of language and literature in al-Andalus, from Cordoba. His nisba refers to a village in Syria where his forebears are said to have lived. He was born in 352 AH (963 CE) and died in 441 AH (1049 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.215}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
