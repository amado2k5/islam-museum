## Overview
David Wharnsby Ali (born 1972 in Ontario, Canada) is a Canadian singer who embraced Islam. He is known for children's songs such as "The Islamic Song for Children" and the album "The Prophet and the Sufi". He belongs to the early generation of English-language Islamic nasheed. {cite:wikidata}
