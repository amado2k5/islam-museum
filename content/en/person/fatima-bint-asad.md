## Summary
[[person/fatima-bint-asad|Fatima bint Asad]] ibn Hashim ibn Abd Manaf, mother of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and his brothers {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

## Her death
Some say she died before the hijra; the sound view at Ibn Hajar is that she emigrated and died in Medina — al-Sha'bi asserted it: she became Muslim, emigrated, and died in Medina {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

Ibn Abi Asim relates from Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib from his father that the Prophet shrouded Fatima bint Asad in his own shirt, saying: "we met none more kind to me after Abu Talib than her" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

## Other reports
Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar says she was the first Hashimi woman to bear a successor, then after her Fatima al-Zahra' {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}. Ibn Sa'd says she was a righteous woman; the Prophet would visit her and take his midday rest in her house {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}. The books of reports relate that Ali told his mother: let Fatima be spared the water-carrying and going out on errands, and I will spare you the grinding and kneading {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.
