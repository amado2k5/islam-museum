## Summary
[[person/fatima-bint-asad|Fatima bint Asad]] ibn Hashim ibn Abd Manaf, mother of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and his brothers {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

## Her death
Some say she died before the hijra; the sound view at Ibn Hajar is that she emigrated and died in Medina — al-Sha'bi asserted it: she became Muslim, emigrated, and died in Medina {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

Ibn Abi Asim relates from Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib from his father that the Prophet shrouded Fatima bint Asad in his own shirt, saying: "we met none more kind to me after Abu Talib than her" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

## Other reports
Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar says she was the first Hashimi woman to bear a successor, then after her Fatima al-Zahra' {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}. Ibn Sa'd says she was a righteous woman; the Prophet would visit her and take his midday rest in her house {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}. The books of reports relate that Ali told his mother: let Fatima be spared the water-carrying and going out on errands, and I will spare you the grinding and kneading {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}.

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] makes Fatima bint Asad, wife of his uncle Abu Talib, the last of the Prophet's mothers she speaks of in her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']]. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep07 00:08:30} He moved to Abu Talib's house, she says, at the bequest of his grandfather Abd al-Muttalib when he died, and found with her a warm and tender embrace; she never made any difference between him and her own children, and so he never forgot her kindness. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep07 00:08:30} She says Fatima became Muslim and emigrated to Medina, and that the Prophet prayed for her at her death. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep07 00:09:00} What she says of her conversion and emigration agrees with what Ibn Hajar reports above. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1934}

In episode 15 she says that the Prophet was raised with Fatima bint Asad, Ali's mother, and his uncle Abu Talib, and that when he grew up, married Khadija and times were hard, he took Ali to be raised in his own house, while another uncle took Ja'far; she sees in this a picture of kinship and of families supporting one another. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep15 00:04:30}
