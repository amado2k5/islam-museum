## Summary
Abu 'Abd Allah Ja'far ibn 'Abd al-Wahid ibn Ja'far ibn Sulayman ibn 'Ali, the qadi. When al-Ma'mun set out for Egypt in 217 he appointed him qadi of Egypt — he judged there three days, then left with him — and Ibn Zulaq counted him among Egypt's qadis for that. He died in 258, also said 268, also said 269. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
