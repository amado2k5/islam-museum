## Overview
Atika daughter of Zayd ibn Amr, sister of Sa'id ibn Zayd ibn Nufayl and wife of al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam. Al-Mas'udi quotes verses she composed in lament for al-Zubayr, which ascribe the treachery to Ibn Jurmuz. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.657}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ish al-Lahza'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] mentions Atika bint Zayd in the twenty-eighth episode of his programme [[work/ich-allahda|'Ish al-Lahza']], on the moment of jealousy: he says [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] was very jealous over his wife Atika and did not like her to leave the house, while she loved to pray the dawn and night prayers in the mosque, and he did not forbid her because of the Prophet's hadith forbidding men to keep women from the mosques. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:00} He says she used to say she would not stop going unless Umar forbade her, and that on the day Umar was stabbed she was in the mosque praying behind him. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:30} He makes this an example of jealousy governed by reason: Umar was jealous, but never let his feelings overpower his mind. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:22:00}

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the twenty-fourth and twenty-fifth episodes of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] to Atika bint Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl, whom he calls the beauty of her age and a gifted poet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:01:30} He begins with her father Zayd, who died five years before the mission after searching for the religion of Abraham. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:04:30} She became Muslim through her brother Sa'id ibn Zayd. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:06:30}

Her first husband was Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr, and he says their love kept Abd Allah from fighting and trade, until one Friday they missed the prayer and Abu Bakr ordered his son to divorce her. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:10:30} Hearing his son's verses of longing at night, Abu Bakr allowed him to take her back. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:13:30} Abd Allah fought at the conquest of Mecca, Hunayn and al-Ta'if, where an arrow wounded him; later the wound reopened, she nursed him, and dying he left her an orchard on condition that she not remarry. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:18:00} She mourned him in verse and refused all suitors until Umar, then caliph, proposed and told her she had forbidden herself what God allowed. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep24 00:23:00}

He says Ali advised her to return the orchard to Abd Allah's family and marry Umar, and that she set him conditions: not to strike her, not to withhold her due, and not to stop her going to the mosque. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:05:30} He draws from it that a woman may refuse a match and set terms. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:06:00} She was at the dawn prayer when Umar was stabbed, and she mourned him too in verse. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:10:30} Her next husband was al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, who accepted the same conditions; he tells how al-Zubayr, jealous, stopped her going to the mosque at night by a ruse. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:17:30} He says that after al-Zubayr's death Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr paid her share of the estate, eighty thousand, and that she asked herself whether she brought misfortune on her husbands. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:21:30} He calls her "companion of martyrs" and holds her up to young women as a model. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep25 00:25:30}
