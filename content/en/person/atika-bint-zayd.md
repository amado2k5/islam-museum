## Overview
Atika daughter of Zayd ibn Amr, sister of Sa'id ibn Zayd ibn Nufayl and wife of al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam. Al-Mas'udi quotes verses she composed in lament for al-Zubayr, which ascribe the treachery to Ibn Jurmuz. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.657}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ish al-Lahza'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] mentions Atika bint Zayd in the twenty-eighth episode of his programme [[work/ich-allahda|'Ish al-Lahza']], on the moment of jealousy: he says [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] was very jealous over his wife Atika and did not like her to leave the house, while she loved to pray the dawn and night prayers in the mosque, and he did not forbid her because of the Prophet's hadith forbidding men to keep women from the mosques. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:00} He says she used to say she would not stop going unless Umar forbade her, and that on the day Umar was stabbed she was in the mosque praying behind him. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:30} He makes this an example of jealousy governed by reason: Umar was jealous, but never let his feelings overpower his mind. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep28 00:22:00}
