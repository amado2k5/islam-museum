# Fazal Rahman

## Summary
Fazal Rahman is a British dawah trainer, the author resolved from the "Dawah Is Easy" channel and brand. His Dawah Training manual — a practical course on dialogue with non-Muslims and dawah method — closes by directing readers to the website DawahIsEasy.com over his own signature, and IslamHouse catalogues the manual as a dawah training manual written by Dr. Fazal Rahman. {cite:dawah-training-manual-fazal-rahman}

## The channel and the training
The DawahIsEasy YouTube channel carries his multi-part "Da'wah Training" series alongside street-dawah clips and filmed conversions. {cite:youtube-dawah-is-easy} In 2025 the UK Islamic Mission's Dawah & Outreach Office ran "Dawah is Easy" training events in London, Glasgow, Manchester and Birmingham, presenting Dr Fazal Rahman — described as having over 150,000 subscribers on his YouTube channel — as the trainer presenting his approach: how to share Islam with friends, neighbours and colleagues, how to handle difficult questions, and how to work with young people. {cite:ukim-dawah-is-easy}
