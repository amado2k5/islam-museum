## Summary
Talha ibn Musarrif ibn Amr al-Hamdani al-Yami of [[place/kufa|Kufa]] is described by al-Zirikli as the best reciter of the people of Kufa in his day, called "the master of the reciters". {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 51}

## Standing
Al-Zirikli counts him among the trustworthy transmitters of hadith. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 51}
