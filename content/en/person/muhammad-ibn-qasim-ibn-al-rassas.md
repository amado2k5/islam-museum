## Overview
Muhammad ibn Qasim, Abu Abd Allah al-Ansari al-Tilimsani then al-Tunisi al-Maghribi, a Maliki, known as Ibn al-Rassas, the surname being the trade of one of his forebears. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17697} He studied with the two Qal-shanis, Ahmad and Umar, with Ibn 'Uqab and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17697} He took up the imamate, preaching, giving fatwas and teaching fiqh, the principles of religion, Arabic and other subjects. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17697} He compiled a commentary on the names of the Prophet, another on a book on prayer over the Prophet, a commentary on Ibn 'Arafa's definitions, and a large book on fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17697} The Mawsu'a cites al-Daw' al-lami' and Shajarat al-Nur al-Zakiyya for him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17697} The heading gives 894 AH with a second figure the scan does not make clear, so the date is stated with that caution.
