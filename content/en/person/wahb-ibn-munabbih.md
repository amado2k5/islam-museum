## Overview
Wahb ibn Munabbih, Abu Abd Allah, of the sons (abna'). al-Mas'udi and Ibn Khaldun mention him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191}.

## Death
He died in Sana'a at the age of ninety, in the year 104 AH, and it is said in the year 110 AH. al-Mas'udi indicates that the writers of histories differed on his death {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191}.

## In Ibn Khaldun
Ibn Khaldun counts him among those who brought into the exegesis the false reports of the Children of Israel, against which the Messenger warned in a hadith that Ibn Khaldun transmits {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.27}.

This is Ibn Khaldun's judgement. The assessments of other scholars of him need an additional source.
