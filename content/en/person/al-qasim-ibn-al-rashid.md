## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Harun al-Rashid, the third of his sons whom he appointed heirs. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 341}

## The heirship
Al-Rashid gave him the allegiance as heir after al-Ma'mun in 187 AH. If the caliphate came to al-Ma'mun, the affair of al-Qasim would be for him, to confirm it or depose him. Al-Ma'mun deposed him from the heirship in 198 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 341} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 419}

## The frontiers
Al-Baladhuri says he was staying at Dabiq when the Byzantines raided Ayn Zarba and drove off the flocks of its people and took some men captive. The people of al-Massisa and its volunteers rose to them and recovered what they had taken. Al-Qasim then sent from the city and repaired its defences and increased its garrison. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 235}
