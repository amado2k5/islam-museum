## Overview
Al-Qa'nabi, Abd Allah ibn Maslama ibn Qa'nab al-Harithi, kunya Abu Abd al-Rahman. He related from Malik his principles, his jurisprudence and his Muwatta'. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 281}

## His death and description
He died in 221 AH. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as a trustworthy and righteous man. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 281}

## Notes for researchers
- The single source is the "Fihrist"; his birth date is not recorded and needs an additional source.
