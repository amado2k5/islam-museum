## Overview
Al-Qa'nabi Abd Allah ibn Maslama ibn Qa'nab al-Harithi, Abu Abd al-Rahman; he transmitted Malik's principles, fiqh and Muwatta, was truthful and pious, and died in 221 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 281}
