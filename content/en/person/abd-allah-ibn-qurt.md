## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Qurt al-Thumali al-Azdi, a Companion who was governor of Homs under Abu Ubayda. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet and from Khalid ibn al-Walid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}
