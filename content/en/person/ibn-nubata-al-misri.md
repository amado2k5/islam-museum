## Overview
Jamal al-Din Ibn Nubata al-Misri, a well-known man of letters and a writer of chancery documents. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.403-404}

## From his chancery writing
Al-Maqqari quotes from his chancery writing a dispatch for the Marinid sultan Abu al-Hasan, in the three copies of the Qur'an endowed to three mosques. He also described the sultan as the one who extended his right hand with the sword and the pen. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.403-404}
