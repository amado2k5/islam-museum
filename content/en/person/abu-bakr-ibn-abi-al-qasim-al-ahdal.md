## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Abi al-Qasim ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Husayni al-Ahdal al-Tihami, a Sufi. He was born in 983 AH and died in 1035 AH, and he wrote books on hadith, history and biographies. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

A second source is needed for his birth and death dates, for the titles of his books, and for the description of him as a saint.
