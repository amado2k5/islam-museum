## Overview
A mufassir whom Rashid Rida cites in his commentary on the divine word "the fools will say": that the fools are those whose reason is slighted and who slight tradition and turn away from inquiry. The second part of that tafsir names neither his full name nor his date, so no date or nisba is recorded here. {cite:tafsir-manar-v02 p.3}
