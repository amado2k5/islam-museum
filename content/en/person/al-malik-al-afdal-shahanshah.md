## Overview
Abu'l-Qasim Shahanshah, titled al-Malik al-Afdal, son of Amir al-Juyush Badr al-Jamali; he took the vizierate in his father's place during the latter's illness, installed al-Musta'li Ahmad in his father al-Mustansir's stead, and remained vizier — a man of fine management and decisive judgment. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His assassination
It was he who set al-Amir b. al-Musta'li in his father's place, ran his state and confined him, barring him from indulgence — which moved al-Amir to work for his killing; a band leapt upon him and killed him. He used to reside in Egypt at Dar al-Malik on the neck of the Nile. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
