## Overview
Al-Mustawrid al-Ijli embraced Islam and then apostatised. Ali killed him and then burned his body {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.303}.

## Burning after death
In the section on the punishment of apostates, al-Sallabi reports that al-Mustawrid al-Ijli accepted Islam, then apostatised, and that Ali killed him and then burned him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.303}. The author suggests that Ali may have burned him out of fear that his people would dig up the body after Ali refused to hand it over for a sum of money they offered {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.303}. This is the author's suggestion; the page gives no source report that states the motive.
