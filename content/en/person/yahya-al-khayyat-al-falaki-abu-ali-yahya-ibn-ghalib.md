## Overview
Abu Ali Yahya ibn Ghalib ibn Muhammad al-Baghdadi al-Khayyat, known as Yahya al-Khayyat al-Falaki, an astronomer and astrologer of Baghdad from the third century AH. He was one of the students of Masha'allah. The encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' gives his death as 250 AH (864 CE), while the biographers differ on the date of his death {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
