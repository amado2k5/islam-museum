## Summary
Abdolkarim Soroush (Hossein Haj Faraj Dastgheib) is an Iranian philosopher counted among the foremost theorists of religious reform in contemporary Iran. He has lived in exile, moving among Western universities, for years. {cite:wikipedia-abdolkarim-soroush}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Tehran in 1945, he studied pharmacy then history and philosophy of science in London, returning to Iran around the Islamic Revolution to serve in its early educational institutions before breaking with them. {cite:wikipedia-abdolkarim-soroush}

## Career and works
He developed the theory of the “contraction and expansion” of religious knowledge: human religious understanding expands and contracts with the human sciences while the text remains fixed. In The Expansion of Prophetic Experience he examined the nature of revelation and religious experience, and his political writings defended religious democracy against absolute guardianship of the jurist. {cite:wikipedia-abdolkarim-soroush}

## Method and contribution
His analytic-epistemological method distinguishes religion from religious knowledge, opening interpretation to scientific criticism. Supporters call him the founder of Iranian reformism, while conservative opponents say he weakens juristic authority; the museum attributes these descriptions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-abdolkarim-soroush}
