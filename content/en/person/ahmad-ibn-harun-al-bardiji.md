## Overview
Ahmad ibn Harun ibn Ruh, Abu Bakr al-Bardiji, was counted among the trustworthy men of hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 265-266}. Al-Zirikli prints his death as 301 AH, 914 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 265-266}.

## Life
His family came from Bardij in the far reaches of Azerbaijan; he settled in Baghdad and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 265-266}.

## Works
He left several books, among them al-Asma' al-Mufrada, in manuscript, on the names of some Companions, Successors and men of hadith, their countries and those who narrated from them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 265-266}.
