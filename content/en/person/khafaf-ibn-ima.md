## Summary
Khafaf ibn Ima', son of Rahdah, of the Banu Ghifar, their chief. He and his father were Companions; he was at al-Hudaybiya with the Prophet and gave the pledge of al-Ridwan. Ibn Ishaq reports Abu Sufyan saying the chief of Kinana had changed religion when Khafaf embraced Islam. Al-Baghawi says he died in Umar's time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13354}
