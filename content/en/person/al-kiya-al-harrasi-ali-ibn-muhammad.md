## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Tabarstani al-Harrasi, from Tabaristan. He was among the leading Shafi'is in jurisprudence, the principles of law and disputation, and was known for memorising the texts of the hadiths of legal rulings. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The museum's data give his birth as about 450 AH (1058 CE) and his death as about 504 AH (1110 CE). Both are given as reported dates and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
