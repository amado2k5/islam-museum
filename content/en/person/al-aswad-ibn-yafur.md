## Overview
Al-Aswad ibn Ya'fur, a pre-Islamic poet from Najran, author of the well-known ode lamenting the family of Muharraq. In it he mentions the people of al-Khawarnaq and al-Sadir, Ma'rib and the palace of Sindad. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.229}
