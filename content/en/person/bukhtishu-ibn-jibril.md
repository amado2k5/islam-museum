## Overview
Bukhtishu ibn Jibril, a physician of the Bukhtishu medical family in the Abbasid age. He was close to the caliph al-Muhtadi, and left a son, Ubayd Allah. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Ibn al-Nadim says that his kunya was Abu Jibril, and that he was well known and eminent with kings. He served al-Rashid, al-Amin, al-Ma'mun, al-Mu'tasim, al-Wathiq and al-Mutawakkil, and earned by medicine what no one like him had earned. The caliphs trusted him with their mothers of children. He wrote the book "al-Tadhkira" for his son Jibril. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.413}

He died around 869 CE, according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
