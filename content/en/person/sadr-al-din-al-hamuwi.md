## Overview
Sadr al-Din al-Hamuwi, shaykh of shaykhs; he came to al-Dhahabi in Syria in 695 seeking hadith and heard much, transmitting from the companions of al-Mu'ayyad al-Tusi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}

## Ghazan's conversion
He related that Ghazan b. Arghun, king of the Tatars, embraced Islam at his hand through his lieutenant Nawruz — it was a day to be witnessed. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}
