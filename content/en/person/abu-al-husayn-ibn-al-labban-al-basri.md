## Overview
Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Laban al-Faradi al-Basri, whose name is Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Laban according to the editor's note quoting al-Subki. He was an imam in jurisprudence and inheritance, and wrote many books on them, which al-Shirazi said no one else had equalled; from him people took inheritance. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his death.
