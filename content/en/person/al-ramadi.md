## Overview
Yusuf ibn Harun al-Ramadi, a poet of Cordoba, prolific in verse and quick in utterance. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 40-41}

## His standing
Al-Maqqari relates that many of the masters of literature in his time said that poetry had been opened with Kinda and closed with Kinda, meaning Imru' al-Qays, al-Mutanabbi and Yusuf ibn Harun. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 40-41}

## His training and last years
He acquired the craft of literature from his teacher Abu Bakr Yahya ibn Hudhayl the blind. Abu Umar ibn Abd al-Barr took from him a piece of his poetry and included some of it in his own work. He lived long enough to see the civil strife, and in it he was overtaken by a poverty that destroyed him. He died around 403 AH (about 1012 CE). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 40-41}
