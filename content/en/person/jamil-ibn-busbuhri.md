## Overview
Jamil ibn Busbuhri, a landed notable (dihqan) of al-Falalij and the two rivers. He became Muslim after the conquest of the Sawad. Umar did not trouble him, did not take the land out of his hands, and removed the poll tax from his neck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The account is taken from al-Baladhuri's *Futuh al-Buldan*, and needs a second source.
