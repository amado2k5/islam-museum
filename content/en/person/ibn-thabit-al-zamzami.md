## Summary
'Abdallah ibn Thabit al-Zamzami al-Makki; one of the Zamzam water-bearers at the Meccan Haram; of scholars' sons he became a scholar himself and a lover of Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti's works, collecting them until he obtained what he did; he drew much learning from his maternal uncles the scholars; he died in 921. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 470}
