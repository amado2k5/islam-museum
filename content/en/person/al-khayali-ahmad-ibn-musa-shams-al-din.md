## Overview
Ahmad ibn Musa Shams al-Din al-Rumi al-Hanafi, known as al-Khayali, a scholar of doctrine. He was a pupil of Shaykh Khidr Bik, and taught at the Sultaniyya madrasa in Bursa and then in Iznik. He was born in 829 AH (1425 CE) and died at Iznik in 862 AH (1458 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
