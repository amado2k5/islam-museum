## Overview
"Abdullah" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen. He is named with his brother [[person/al-qasim-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-horseman-292|al-Qasim]], and the book does not say whether their father is Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Rassi, so the museum draws no link. He is not [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] of al-Hadi's other reports. He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
