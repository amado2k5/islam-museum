## Summary
Baldwin, king of the Franks who took Jerusalem; a valiant tyrant, he resolved to take Egypt and marched with his hosts until he arrived, then turned back ill and died in 511. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}

## After him
They slit him open, salted him, cast out his entrails and buried him at Jerusalem; the priest lord of Edessa took Jerusalem after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}
