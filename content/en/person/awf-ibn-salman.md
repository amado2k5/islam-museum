## Summary
Awf ibn Salman — link number 5 of the disputed part of the Prophet's lineage, counting up from Adnan toward Ismail; his son is سلمان بن الحميسع and his father سلمان in the well-known transmission of Ibn Ishaq and al-Kalbi {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## In the chain
He sits between سلمان بن الحميسع and سلمان in the chain running «Adnan ibn Udd ibn Adad ibn al-Humaysa ibn Salman ibn Awf ibn Buz ibn Qamwal ibn Ubayy ibn al-Awwam ibn Nashid ibn Haza ibn Bildas ibn Yadlaf ibn Tabikh ibn Jahim ibn Nahish ibn Makhi ibn Ays ibn Abaq ibn Da'id ibn Nabt ibn Mashih ibn al-Nashtan ibn Udr ibn al-Dhara' ibn Abd ibn Ahmar ibn Nabit ibn Qaydar ibn Ismail ibn Ibrahim», as transmitted by Ibn Sa'd and al-Tabari with variant names at several points {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## What the genealogists say
The sira and genealogy scholars divide the Prophet's lineage in three: a part agreed as sound down to Adnan, a part disputed between those who suspend judgement and those who affirm it from Adnan up to Ibrahim, and a part above Ibrahim to Adam that certainly contains unsound matter {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48-49}. This link belongs to the second part, and many genealogists stop at Ma'add ibn Adnan on the strength of the report «the genealogists lie» — see [[concept/waqf-al-nasab-inda-maadd|Stopping the genealogy at Ma'add]].

## For younger visitors
Awf ibn Salman is a name in the chain of the Prophet's ancestors between Adnan and Ismail; scholars do not confirm this part of the lineage because the reports disagree.
