## Overview
Ali ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Hasan, al-Ansari al-Sijilmasi al-Jazairi, of the line of Sa'd ibn Ubada al-Khazraji, was a Maliki jurist, counted among the scholars. He was born at Tafilalt, raised at Sijilmasa and lived for a time in Egypt; he then settled at Fes and was appointed mufti in the Green Mountain (al-Jabal al-Akhdar). He died in al-Jazair. The printed text gives his death as 1057 AH, 1647 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'al-Minah al-Ihsaniyya fi al-Ajwiba al-Tilimsaniyya'; 'al-Yawaqit al-Thamina', in manuscript, a poem on Maliki jurisprudence, held at al-Azhariyya and at Rabat; 'Masalik al-Wasl', on the principles of jurisprudence; and many poems, among them 'al-Durra al-Munifa', in manuscript, a poem on the life of the Prophet held at the Zahiriyya, and 'Jami'at al-Asrar', in which he versified the five pillars of Islam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.
