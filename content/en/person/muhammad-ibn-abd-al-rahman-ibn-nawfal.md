## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Nawfal ibn al-Aswad ibn Nawfal ibn Khuwaylid ibn Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza al-Qurashi al-Asadi, Abu al-Aswad al-Madani, known as "Urwa's orphan" (the foster-ward of Urwa ibn al-Zubayr), a trustworthy Successor and one of the leading hadith transmitters in Egypt, where he arrived in 136 AH {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.408}.

## Lineage and upbringing
His grandfather al-Aswad ibn Nawfal was among the emigrants to Abyssinia and died there; his father bequeathed him to Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, which is why he was called "Urwa's orphan" {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.408}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Bukayr ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Ashaj, Habib (Urwa ibn al-Zubayr's freedman), Salim ibn Abd Allah ibn Umar, Sulayman ibn Yasar, Amir ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, Abd Allah ibn Kaysan, the son of al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, Nafi' (Ibn Umar's freedman), and al-Nu'man ibn Abi Ayyash al-Zuraqi {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.409}. Those narrating from him included Haywa ibn Shurayh, Sa'id ibn Abi Ayyub, Shu'ba ibn al-Hajjaj, Abd Allah ibn Lahiy'a, Amr ibn al-Harith, al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Malik ibn Anas, Muhammad ibn Ishaq, Muhammad ibn Muslim ibn Shihab al-Zuhri and Hisham ibn Urwa — both his contemporaries — Mus'ab ibn Thabit and Yahya ibn Ayyub al-Misri {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.409}.

## Accreditation and death
Asked about Abu al-Aswad, Abu Hatim al-Razi said "trustworthy"; pressed "does he rank with al-Zuhri and Hisham ibn Urwa?" he again said "trustworthy." Al-Nasa'i declared him trustworthy, Ibn Hibban listed him in al-Thiqat, and all the major collections narrate from him {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.409}. Al-Waqidi says he died at the end of Umayyad rule {cite:tahdhib-al-kamal-mizzi p.409}.

## For younger visitors
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Nawfal was raised by the scholar Urwa ibn al-Zubayr after losing his father, and grew to be compared in reliability to the two greats of his age, al-Zuhri and Hisham ibn Urwa; he carried hadith to Egypt.

## Notes for researchers
- Text from Tahdhib al-Kamal, Muhammad-names volume, entry 6002, pp. 408-409.
