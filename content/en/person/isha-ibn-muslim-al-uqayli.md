## Overview
Ishaq ibn Muslim al-Uqayli, who was at the head of Sa'id ibn Amr al-Harashi's force to Armenia, with his brothers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.290}

## Armenia
Marwan ibn Muhammad later appointed him over Armenia, and he kept fighting Musafir al-Qassab. Musafir was at the fortress of al-Kilab in al-Sisajan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

He is connected with two events: [[event/maslama-khazar-campaign|Maslama's campaign against the Khazars]], in the first, and [[event/musafir-revolt-armenia|the revolt of Musafir in Armenia]], against which he fought as governor.
