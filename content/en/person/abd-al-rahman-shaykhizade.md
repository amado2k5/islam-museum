## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Sulayman, known as Shaykhizade and also called al-Damad, was a Hanafi jurist from Gallipoli (Kelibolu) in Turkey, one of the army judges. The printed text gives his death as 1078 AH, 1667 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'Majma' al-Anhur fi Sharh Multaqa al-Abhur', printed in two volumes, which he finished at the town of Edirne, and 'Nazm al-Fara'id', printed, on the points of difference between the Maturidiyya and the Ash'ariyya. The entry is short and says nothing more {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
