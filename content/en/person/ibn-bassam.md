## Overview
Ali ibn Bassam al-Shantarini al-Andalusi, Abu al-Hasan, was a man of letters, one of the writers who became viziers {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. His nisba is from Shantarin, called today Santarem in Portugal {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. He died in 542 AH (1147 CE); al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## Al-Dhakhira
He was famous for his book "al-Dhakhira fi Mahasin Ahl al-Jazira", of which three parts were printed and the rest was prepared for printing; it runs to eight volumes and holds 154 detailed biographies of notables of literature and politics, men who were his contemporaries or lived a little before him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.
