## Overview
'Abd al-Razzaq ibn Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani the Hanbali, Abu Bakr; born at Baghdad on Monday 18 Dhu'l-Qa'da 528, his father had him hear Abu'l-Fadl al-Armawi and his generation, then he heard for himself. Al-Diya' said "I saw no one at Baghdad like him in wakefulness and exactness"; Ibn Nuqta called him a trustworthy, faithful hafiz; Ibn al-Najjar "a hafiz, exact, a jurist of Ahmad's school, devout, secluded at home going out only on Fridays, loving transmission, honouring students, proud of soul on the early way"; and Abu Shama "an ascetic worshipper — there was none like him among the Shaykh's sons, and he never entered what his brothers entered." {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Death
He died on Saturday night, 6 Shawwal 603; prayers were said over him at several places in a witnessed day, and he was buried in the Shawiniziyya cemetery. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
