## Overview
Junada ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Harawi al-Azdi, Abu Usama, a philologist and grammarian from Herat. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He died around 399 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
