## Overview
Sulayman Sa'd al-Din ibn Abd al-Rahman Amin Allah ibn Muhammad Mustaqim, known as Mustakimzade, was an inquiring faqih and Sufi among the scholars of the Ottoman state, of Istanbul; he died in 1202 AH (1788 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.127}

He left more than fifty books and treatises, many in Arabic; the best known is Majallat al-Nisab on lineages, kunyas and titles. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.127}
