## Overview
Porphyry the Wise (Furfuris), author of the "Isagoge", the introduction to logic, a book known by its name. al-Mas'udi mentions that his tomb is in the towers known as the towers of Sicily. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.357}

The years of his life are not recorded in the museum's data; an additional source is needed.
