## Overview
Abd al-Masih ibn Amr ibn Qays ibn Hayyan ibn Buqayla. Buqayla's name was al-Harith. He was of the Azd. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339}

## Negotiating for the people of al-Hira
Al-Baladhuri says that he was advanced in age when he went out to Khalid ibn al-Walid to negotiate on behalf of the people of al-Hira, and a well-known exchange took place between them. They agreed on a hundred thousand to be paid every year, and it was the first money carried from Iraq to Medina, in 12 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339} See [[event/conquest-of-al-hira|the conquest of al-Hira]].
