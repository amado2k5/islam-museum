## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abbas al-San'ani is named in Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq as one of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s companions; his nisba points to Sanaa. He was struck by an illness while out in the open country with al-Hadi and fell to the ground; al-Hadi dismounted, wiped his face, recited over him, had him taken to [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and peeled pomegranates for him by hand until he recovered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.55} The text says nothing else of him and gives no dates. See [[event/al-hadi-tends-a-fallen-companion-and-a-fallen-rider]].
