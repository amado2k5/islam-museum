## Overview
Bistam ibn Tarsi, a landlord of Babil and Khatranniya. He embraced Islam after the conquest of al-Sawad. Al-Baladhuri relates that Umar did not interfere with him, did not take the land from his hand, and removed the poll tax from his neck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
