## Summary
Zuhayr ibn Surad, a chief of [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]]'s chiefs; he headed their [[event/hawazin-delegation-and-amnesty|delegation to the Prophet at al-Ji'rana]] in Dhu al-Qa'da of year 8 AH after the division of Hunayn's spoils {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 421}.

## Heading the Hawazin delegation
Hawazin's delegation came to the Prophet as Muslims, fourteen men headed by Zuhayr ibn Surad, among them Abu Burqan, the Prophet's milk-uncle {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 421}. They asked him to bestow the captives and wealth on them, presenting words that melt hearts; the Prophet gave them the choice between the wealth and the sons and women — they chose their kin, saying: we would not exchange our kin for anything {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 421}.

The Prophet taught them the plea-formula after the noon prayer; he and Banu Abd al-Muttalib waived their shares, the Muhajirun and Ansar followed, and six thousand of Hawazin's women and children were returned — the Prophet clothed them each in a Coptic garment {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 422}.

## For young visitors
Zuhayr led a delegation of his people asking the Prophet to free their families' captives; the Prophet freed them all, clothed them in new garments, and they returned home safe.
