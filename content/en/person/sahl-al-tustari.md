## Overview
Sahl ibn Abd Allah ibn Yunus ibn Isa ibn Abd Allah ibn Rafi al-Tustari, a Sufi {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.263}.

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim mentions his book "Daqa'iq al-Muhibbin" (The Subtleties of Lovers) and his book "Mawa'iz al-Arifin" (The Admonitions of the Knowers) {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.263}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy names Sahl ibn Abdullah al-Tustari and reports that four principles made him watchful before God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep05 00:49:00}.

Among Sahl's sayings as the speaker gives them: work for God in proportion to your need of Him, work for this world in proportion to your stay in it, work for the Hereafter in proportion to your remaining in it, and work for the Fire in proportion to your endurance of it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep05 00:49:30}.

Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds, as his own opinion, that a believer who prays at night and makes supplication is answered by God, and he names Sahl ibn Abdullah in this context (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 00:39:30}.

## In Hilyat al-Awliya
Sahl al-Tustari's notice in *Hilyat al-Awliya* (volume 10, no. 546) opens: "Abu Abd Allah Sahl ibn Abd Allah ibn Yunus ibn Isa ibn Abd Allah ibn Rafi' al-Tustari — he was the nephew of Muhammad ibn Sawwar, and met Abu al-Fayd Dhu al-Nun al-Misri at the Sanctuary; his speech's station is the refinement of deeds and the purification of states from defects." {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/190}

Of his transmitted speech: "Our roots are six things: holding fast to the Book of God, following the Sunnah of the Messenger of God, withholding harm, avoiding sins, repentance, and fulfilling rights." And he said: "Whoever's example is the Prophet has no choosing among things in his heart, nor does his heart wander except to what God and His Messenger love." {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/190}

Of what he says on servanthood's modes: "Know, my brothers, that the servants have served God in three ways..."; and of his sayings: "The mark of certainty is sincerity of counsel, the mark of contentment is leaving disagreement." {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/191-192}

He is mentioned as keeping night vigil on a single stirrup, conversing with his Lord until morning. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/211}
