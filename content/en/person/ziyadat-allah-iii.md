## Overview
Ziyadat Allah ibn al-Aghlab, known as Abu Mudar, the last of the Aghlabid amirs in Ifriqiya. He fled before al-Mahdi al-Ubaydi to Egypt and then came to Iraq, and from this date the Maghrib came out of the authority of the Abbasids {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.587}.

## His leaving Ifriqiya
al-Mas'udi reports that Abu Abd Allah al-Muhtasib, the missionary who appeared among the Kutama calling people to Ubayd Allah, drove him out of Ifriqiya {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.647-648}. Ibn Khaldun reports that he fled from Raqqada to Egypt when Abu Abd Allah al-Shi'i took the country in the year 296 AH, and the people of Raqqada plundered the palaces of the Banu al-Aghlab {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.803}.

## His last days
Ibn al-Athir reports that he stayed for a time after he left, that he returned to Egypt and went to Jerusalem, and died at Ramla and was buried there. He put the duration of the Aghlabid rule at a hundred and twelve years {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.17-18}.
