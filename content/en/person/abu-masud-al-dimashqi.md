## Overview
Ibrahim b. Muhammad b. 'Ubayd, Abu Mas'ud al-Dimashqi the hafiz; he roamed the lands, heard hadith and wrote at Baghdad, Kufa, Basra, Wasit, Ahwaz, Isfahan and the lands of Khurasan, and had surpassing care for the two Sahihs, composing Kitab atraf al-Sahihayn — among the first to compile atraf. Trustworthy, devout, scrupulous, discerning; he died at Baghdad in 401, bequeathed to Shaykh Abu Hamid al-Isfarayini who prayed over him, and was buried in the cemetery of the Mansur mosque. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.252}
