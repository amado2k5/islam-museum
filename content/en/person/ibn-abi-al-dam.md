## Summary
Ibn Abi'l-Dam, the Shafi'i qadi and historian; born at Hama, he journeyed to Baghdad for fiqh, heard at Cairo, and transmitted there and in many Syrian lands; he held the judgeship of his town Hamadan and of Hama too — an imam of the Shafi'i madhhab versed in history. His works include a commentary on *Mushkil al-Wasit*, *Adab al-qadi*, a book on history and the Islamic sects, and the great *al-Tarikh al-Muzaffari*. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 221}
