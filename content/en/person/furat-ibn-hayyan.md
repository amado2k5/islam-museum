## Overview
[[person/furat-ibn-hayyan|Furat ibn Hayyan al-Ijli]], an ally of the [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]]. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}. Al-Bukhari reports from Abu Hatim that he had emigrated to the Prophet, and Abu Hatim says that he was from Kufa. Al-Baghawi says that he lived in [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and built a house there, that he has descendants there, and that the Prophet granted him land in Bahrain. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}. Ibn al-Sakan says that he had companionship with the Prophet, and Ibn Sa'd lists him in the class of the people of the Trench and his settling in Kufa. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}.

## What is reported of him and about him
Abu Dawud and al-Bukhari in his History report that the Prophet said: "Among you are men whom I leave to their faith, and among them is Furat ibn Hayyan." {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}. Haritha ibn Mudrib, Qays ibn Zuhayr and al-Hasan al-Basri reported from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}. Ibn Hajar says that he "was a spy for Abu Sufyan in his wars, then embraced Islam and his Islam was good", and al-Marzubani said that he was among those who satirised the Messenger of God and then praised him, and his praise was accepted. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187}. Ibn al-Sakan transmitted from Adi ibn Hatim that he embraced Islam, gained understanding in religion, and that the Prophet granted him land in Yamama yielding four thousand two hundred. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1187} {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1188}.

## With Abu Hurayra and al-Rijal
Sayf reports in "The Conquests", from Ahmad ibn Furat ibn Hayyan, that Abu Hurayra, Furat ibn Hayyan and al-Rijal ibn Unfuwa went out from the Prophet, and he said: "A single tooth of one of them in the Fire is greater than Mount Uhud, and with him is a treacherous nape." They did not feel safe until al-Rijal did what he did and was then killed, and Abu Hurayra and Furat fell in prostration in thanks to God. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1188}. Ibn Hajar says that al-Rijal apostatised, was seduced by Musaylima, and was killed with him as an unbeliever. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1188}, and for the detail see [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]].
