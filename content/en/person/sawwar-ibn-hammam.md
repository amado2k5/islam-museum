## Overview
Sawwar ibn Hammam al-Abdi was at the head of the vanguard of al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As at the [[event/battle-of-rayshahr|Battle of Rayshahr]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The day of Rayshahr
He charged at Shahrak, the marzban of Fars, and struck him, knocking him from his horse, and struck him with his sword until he died. Then Ibn Shahrak charged at Sawwar and killed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
