## Overview
Sahama ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Asamm al-Ghadani, muezzin of al-Hajjaj. He belongs to the Umayyad period {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The report
Al-Baladhuri reports that he was among those who fought with Yazid. The house of Abu Ya'qub al-Khattabi in Basra belonged to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
