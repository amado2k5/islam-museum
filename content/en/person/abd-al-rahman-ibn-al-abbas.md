## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Abbas ibn Rabi'a ibn al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib al-Hashimi. He rose with Ibn al-Ash'ath, and when the army was defeated he went among its remnants to Herat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Herat
Al-Baladhuri relates that he killed al-Ruqad al-Mutakki and collected the land tax. [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] marched against him and defeated him, and ordered his men not to be pursued; the Hashimi went to Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
