# Salah Bukhatir

**Salah Bukhatir** is a Qur'an reciter and preacher native to the United Arab Emirates. The biography published by Muslim Central, the audio platform that distributes his recordings, describes his voice as comparable to that of Sheikh Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais {cite:muslim-central-salah-bukhatir}.

He serves as imam at the Sheikh Saoud Al Qassimi Mosque, and he has also led prayers at the Prophet's Mosque in Medina {cite:muslim-central-salah-bukhatir}.

His complete recorded recitation of the Qur'an has been published through Muslim Central's channels since December 2013 {cite:muslim-central-salah-bukhatir}.
