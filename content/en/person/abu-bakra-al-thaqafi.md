## Summary
A notable Companion, jurist and hadith scholar; he had been a slave of Thaqif in al-Ta'if's fortress, and when the Prophet proclaimed safety and freedom to whoever came down from the fortress, he lowered himself on a pulley-wheel — by which he became known — and the Prophet freed him, naming him among "God's freed" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/128}.

## Coming down from the fortress
During the [[event/siege-of-taif|siege of al-Ta'if]], Abu Bakra answered the Prophet's call and came down from atop the fortress wall using a rope's pulley; the Prophet nicknamed him "Abu Bakra" (father of the pulley) and freed him, refusing to return him to Thaqif after their Islam {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/128}. He afterwards became one of the great hadith transmitters and settled in Basra {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/128}.

## For young visitors
He was a man held in slavery inside al-Ta'if's fortress; he lowered himself by rope and pulley to win his freedom with the Prophet, who freed him — and he became a learned Companion and transmitter of the noble hadith.
