## Summary

Achmad Zaky Syaifudin (born 24 August 1986) is an Indonesian internet entrepreneur and investor, a co-founder of the e-commerce company Bukalapak and its chief executive until he resigned in January 2020; he then started Init6, a venture fund supporting early-stage startups {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en} {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id}.

## Life

Zaky was born in Sragen Regency, Central Java, on 24 August 1986, to parents who both worked as junior-high-school teachers {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id} {cite:wikidata-achmad-zaky}. He began learning computer and programming sciences in 1997 through programming books and a computer given by his uncle, represented his school at the National Science Olympiad in computer science, and won the competition while attending Public High School 1 in Solo {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en} {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id}. He studied informatics engineering at the Bandung Institute of Technology, earned a perfect GPA in his first year and graduated with honours, and in 2008 took a short exchange scholarship to Oregon State University {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id}. In October 2010 he married Diajeng Lestari, the founder of the fashion company HijUp; they have one child {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en}.

## Work

Zaky began in business as a student, building quick-count election software for a television station for IDR 1.5 million and trying a noodle-making business that failed {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en}. After graduating he formed the technology consulting firm Suitmedia, inside which the Bukalapak marketplace was born in 2010 as an internal project; he launched it with his institute colleagues Muhammad Fajrin Rasyid and Nugroho Herucahyono to help micro-businesses and traditional kiosks compete online, and led Bukalapak as CEO until resigning in January 2020 {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id} {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en}. After his departure he founded the early-stage venture fund Init6 with Bukalapak's former chief technology officer Nugroho Herucahyono {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en}. In July 2016 he received the Satyalancana Wira Karya award from President Joko Widodo for his exemplary contributions, and he was named EY Entrepreneur of the Year 2017 in the Technology & Digital category {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-en} {cite:wikipedia-achmad-zaky-id}.
