## Summary
A Companion of Hudhayl, ally of Banu Zuhra; he emigrated to Abyssinia and came in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him with Amir ibn Abi Waqqas under Banu Zuhra, describing him as "their ally of Hudhayl". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
