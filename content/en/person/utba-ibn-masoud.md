## Overview
A Companion of Hudhayl, an ally of Banu Zuhra, who emigrated to Abyssinia and arrived in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him with Amir ibn Abi Waqqas among Banu Zuhra, describing him as "their ally of Hudhayl". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 5873: "Utba ibn Mas'ud al-Hudhali, full brother of Abd Allah"; from al-Zuhri: Abd Allah was no earlier in emigration than Utba, but Utba died before him — Abd al-Razzaq transmits it in the wording "he was no keener than him" — and he emigrated to Abyssinia where he stayed until he came with Ja'far ibn Abi Talib; it was also said he came earlier and witnessed Uhud and what followed. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1000}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
