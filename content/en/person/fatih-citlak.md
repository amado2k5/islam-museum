## Summary
Fatih Çıtlak is a Turkish author and television presenter on Sufi topics, born in 1966 in Istanbul's Fatih district; he has written books on Islam and Sufism and presented radio and television programmes for many years. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en} {cite:official-site-fatih-citlak}

His YouTube channel counted 141,000 subscribers as of 16 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-fatih-citlak}

## Early life and education
Çıtlak was born in 1966 in Istanbul's Fatih district and completed his secondary education at Fatih Imam Hatip High School in Istanbul. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en} {cite:official-site-fatih-citlak}

While studying at Marmara University's Department of Turkology, he studied Arabic and Islamic sciences, and he received his spiritual education from the masters whose lectures and conversation circles he attended. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

## Work and programmes
He is the head of the dervishes of the Istanbul Historical Turkish Music Ensemble affiliated with the Turkish Ministry of Culture. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

He has taken part in many media productions on Sufism and Islamic history on private radio stations for many years, and has served as music director, art consultant and copywriter in many domestic and foreign audio and visual productions, and he continues his radio and television programmes. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

He gave talks on tafsir, sīrah and mysticism in private conversation circles, and participates as a speaker in many conferences, symposiums and panels on mysticism. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

His Masnavi talks at the Yunus Emre Cultural Center, organised by Pendik Municipality, were the cultural event with the highest participation and the longest duration organised by local governments in the history of the Republic of Turkey, as the article records. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

He writes in the journal Keşkul, which he founded, in addition to his contributions to many academic studies. {cite:wikipedia-fatih-citlak-en}

His official website states that he has written fifteen works and that he takes part as postnişin in the sema and devran programmes of the Istanbul Turkish Music ensemble. {cite:official-site-fatih-citlak}
