## Overview
Al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym al-Thawri, one of the ascetic Successors. Ali sent him against the Daylam and appointed him over four thousand Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His mosque in Qazwin
Al-Baladhuri says there is a well-known mosque in Qazwin attributed to al-Rabi'. There was a tree in it that the common people touched for blessing; it is said that he planted his toothstick in the ground and it sprouted until it became that tree. The agent of Tahir ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir cut it down in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil, fearing that people would be tempted by it. The report of the toothstick is attributed to "it is said". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
