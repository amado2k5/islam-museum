## Overview
Abu al-Mahasin Jamal al-Din Yusuf ibn Taghribirdi al-Yashbughawi al-Zahiri al-Hanafi, a historian in Egypt and the author of *al-Nujum al-Zahira* (The Shining Stars). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.491}

## Life
According to the *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'*, he was born in 812 AH (1409 CE) and died in 874 AH (1469 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.491} These dates come from one source and need an additional source.
