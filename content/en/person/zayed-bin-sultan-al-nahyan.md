# Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan

Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan was the founding President of the United Arab Emirates and the ruler of Abu Dhabi from 1966 until his death. He was born at Abu Dhabi on 6 May 1918, a son of Sultan bin Zayed Al Nahyan by Salama bint Butti Al Qubaisi; his kunya was Abu Khalifa. {cite:wikipedia-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan-en} {cite:wikipedia-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan-ar} {cite:wikidata-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan}

## Governor of Al Ain, then ruler of Abu Dhabi

His brother, the ruler Shakhbut, appointed him governor of the Eastern Region (Al Ain) in 1946, and he administered it for twenty years. On 6 August 1966 Shakhbut was deposed and Zayed succeeded him as ruler of Abu Dhabi. {cite:wikipedia-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan-en}

## The federation and his presidency

Zayed led the negotiations that created the United Arab Emirates: the federation was proclaimed on 2 December 1971 and he was elected its first president, re-elected for successive terms until his death. Under him the oil wealth was directed to the development of the new state, and his well-known philanthropy reached beyond the Emirates. {cite:wikipedia-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan-en}

## Death

Sheikh Zayed died at Abu Dhabi on 2 November 2004 and was buried in the courtyard of the new Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi; his eldest son Khalifa bin Zayed succeeded him in Abu Dhabi and in the presidency. {cite:wikipedia-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan-en} {cite:wikidata-zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan}
