## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Nufayl al-Azdi, one of the five leaders to whom the Shi'a turned when they stirred in 65 AH, in remorse for having stayed away from al-Husayn. They camped at al-Nukhayla, then marched to Ayn al-Warda. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.87}
