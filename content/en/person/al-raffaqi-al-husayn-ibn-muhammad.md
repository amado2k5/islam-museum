## Overview
Abu Abd Allah al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Raffaqi, a man of letters from al-Raffaqa. He was born in 373 AH (983 CE) and died in 422 AH (1031 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His poetry, works and teachers are not recorded in the data and need an additional source.
