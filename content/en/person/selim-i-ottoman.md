## Overview
Selim I, sultan of the Ottoman state, son of [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 198-199}.

## His reign
Jarmardi al-Nasiri, a student of al-Suyuti, described him as "the trial of the ninth century (AH)", because of his wars with his brothers and their sons, then his fight against the lord of the East, and his entry into Egypt after Marj Dabiq, where its sultan Qansuh al-Ghuri was killed. He said that Egypt had never been struck by the like of it since it became a land of Islam {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 798}.

## His death
He died on the ninth of Shawwal 926 AH, aged fifty-four. His reign lasted nine years and eight months {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 198-199}.
