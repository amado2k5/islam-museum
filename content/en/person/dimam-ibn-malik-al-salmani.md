## Overview
Dimam ibn Malik al-Salmani, from the delegation of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] who met the Prophet on his return from Tabuk. Ibn Hisham names him on the authority of Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}

## In the delegation of Hamdan
The delegation came with Malik ibn al-Namat Dhu al-Mish'ar. He is mentioned in the [[event/delegation-of-hamdan|delegation of Hamdan]] among those Ibn Hisham names. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}

The date of his meeting with the Prophet is approximate in the data (around 631 CE) and is disputed in the reports; an additional source is needed.
