## Overview
Ibrahim Adil Shah II was a ruler of the Adil Shahi dynasty of Bijapur in the Deccan. The Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Adil Shahs lists his reign as 987 to 1035 AH, 1579 to 1626 CE, and he is treated there within the entry on the dynasty {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 218}.

## The Adil Shahi dynasty
The article says the Adil Shahs were a Muslim dynasty that ruled Bijapur, one of the successor kingdoms to the Bahmani kingdom of the Deccan, from 895 AH, 1489 CE, until 1097 AH, 1686 CE, when it was absorbed by the Mughal empire, and that its founder was Yusuf Adil Khan {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 218}.

## His age
The article's author says Bijapur's power and prosperity reached their height under Ibrahim II, though the kingdom was never free of turbulence among the nobles {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 218}. It says the Adil Shahs were great builders who made Bijapur one of the most magnificent monuments of Islamic architecture in India and were patrons of letters, and that an important historian of the dynasty wrote under Ibrahim II's patronage {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 218}.
