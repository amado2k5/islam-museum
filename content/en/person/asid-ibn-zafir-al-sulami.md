## Overview
Asid ibn Zafir al-Sulami, whose kunya is Abu Yazid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292}

## Marwan's guide
Marwan ibn Muhammad entered the land of the Khazars from the side of the Gate of Lan, with the kings of the mountains from the side of the Gate and the Gates. He took part in that as a guide at the Gate of Lan in [[event/marwan-caucasus-expedition|Marwan's expedition to the Caucasus]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.292}
