## Summary

Al-Kharit ibn Rashid al-Shami, chief of his people, who met the Prophet. He fought alongside Ali, then parted from him when the arbitration was agreed; Ali sent against them Ma'qil al-Riyahi of Banu Yarbu', who defeated them.
