## Summary
Muhammad Nuzul Dzikri is an Indonesian preacher (ustaz and da'i) from Jakarta, born in 1983. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} He is known for long-running thematic study sessions (kajian) on the Qur'an and Sunnah, held in Jakarta mosques and carried to a large online audience; his YouTube channel, started on 2 November 2017, counted 913,000 subscribers, 133,772,560 views and 5,582 videos as read by vidIQ on 13 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:youtube-muhammadnuzuldzikri} {cite:vidiq-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri}

## Early life and education
He spent his childhood in Jakarta, from kindergarten through senior high school. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} He took his bachelor's degree at the Institute of Islamic and Arabic Sciences (LIPIA), in the Sharia sciences programme, graduating in 2009 with a thesis titled "A Comparative Study between Marriage Law and Islamic Fiqh". {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} In 2010 he continued at the same institute in its language-preparation (i'dad lughawi) programme. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id}

## Teaching and sessions
His best-known regular session is the study of Ibn Jama'ah's *Tadzkiratus Saami' wal Mutakallim fii Adabil 'Alim wal Muta'allim* at the Nurul Iman Mosque, Blok M Square, Jakarta. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} Online, he runs regular studies of al-Nawawi's *Riyadhus Shalihin* and Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah's *Al-Wabilush Shayyib*. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} He also teaches a weekly study for inmates at the Pondok Bambu correctional institution. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id}

Alongside these, the article records guest teaching at the Institute of Home Affairs Governance (IPDN) in Cilandak, sessions for medical students at the University of Indonesia in 2013–2014, for staff of PT Aneka Tambang in 2014, and at the Police Science College, a regular Ramadan slot on Radio Elshinta, and regular appearances at The Rabbaanians study and the Youth Islamic Club Study (YISC) at Jakarta's Al-Azhar Grand Mosque. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} From 2010 to 2015 he headed the Sharia sciences section of the Al Barkah Takhassus education institute (LPTA). {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id} He has also given study sessions outside Indonesia, in the United Arab Emirates, Tunisia and Oman. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-nuzul-dzikri-id}
