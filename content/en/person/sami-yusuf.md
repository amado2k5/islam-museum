## Overview
Sami Yusuf (born 1980 in Tehran to an Azerbaijani family) is a British singer and composer who grew up in London. He is known for his first album "The Teacher" (2003), which sold millions of copies across the Muslim world. {cite:wikidata}

## Influence
He is one of the leaders in renewing contemporary Islamic song by combining Western and Eastern instruments. His nasheeds "Hasbi Rabbi" and "Ya Rasool Allah" became well known. In 2006 he was named a goodwill ambassador for the United Nations World Food Programme. {cite:wikidata}
