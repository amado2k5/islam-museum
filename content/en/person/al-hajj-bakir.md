## Summary
Algiers' interim governor after Hasan Agha al-Tushi's death {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 229}.

## The succession
When Hasan Agha died in 951/1544, the men of Algiers' diwan unanimously made al-Hajj Bakir governor until the Sublime Porte's appointee arrived — [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]], that same year {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 229}.
