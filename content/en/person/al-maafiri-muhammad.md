## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad b. 'Abdallah b. Muhammad al-Ma'afiri of al-Andalus, of Seville, the famous hafiz; his teacher Ibn Bashkuwal says of him in the Sila: "the deep-diving hafiz, seal of al-Andalus's scholars, last of its notables and memorisers." {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His journey
He travelled to the Mashriq with his father in 485 — read fiqh under al-Tartushi, entered Baghdad and kept company with Abu Bakr al-Shashi and Abu Hamid al-Ghazali; he made the hajj, entered Egypt and wrote down its scholars' teachings, then returned to al-Andalus in 493; made qadi of his town he benefited its people, then removed he turned to spreading knowledge; among his works 'Aridat al-ahwadhi, a commentary on al-Tirmidhi. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
