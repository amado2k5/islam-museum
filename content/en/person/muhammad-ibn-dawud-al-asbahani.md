## Summary
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Dawud al-Asbahani, author of the Kitab al-Zahra, transmitted from his father and was a scholar, man of letters, dialectical jurist and eloquent poet. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}

## The Thorn-Sparrow
Ja'far al-Khalidi related that Ruwaym said: we were with Dawud ibn 'Ali al-Asbahani when his son Muhammad came in weeping. His father drew him close and asked what grieved him; he said: the boys have nicknamed me "thorn-sparrow." Dawud laughed, and his son said: you are worse to me than the boys! Dawud said: there is no god but God — nicknames come only from heaven; you, my son, are nothing but the thorn-sparrow. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}
