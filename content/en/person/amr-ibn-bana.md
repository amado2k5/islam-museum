## Overview
Amr ibn Bana, that is Amr ibn Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Rashid, a client of Yusuf ibn Umar al-Thaqafi. Bana was his mother, the daughter of Ruh, the secretary of Salama the servant. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.447-448}

He was especially close to al-Mutawakkil and his companion, and he took from Ishaq al-Mawsili. He had a craft in singing, and lived until the days of al-Mu'tadid. He has a book "Mujarrad Amr ibn Bana". He died in 278 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.447-448}
