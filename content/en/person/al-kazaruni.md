## Summary
Jamal al-Din al-Kazaruni al-Madani, shaykh, imam and scholar in whom the headship of learning at Medina the Radiant ended: he held its judgeship and khatibate, then was dismissed, and entered Cairo several times. He left none at Medina like him. He died in 843. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 247}
