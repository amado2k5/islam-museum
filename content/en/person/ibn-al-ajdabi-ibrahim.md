## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Isma'il ibn Ahmad ibn Abdallah al-Lawati al-Maghribi al-Ifriqi, a jurist from Tripoli in the west, attributed to Ajdabiya. The sources give him a brief biography. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.284-288}

## Death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as around 470 AH (around 1077 CE). The date is approximate and comes from a single source, so it needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.284-288}
