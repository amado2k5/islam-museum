## Overview
Zuhayr ibn Sulaym al-Azdi. On the day of al-Mada'in he embraced an al-Nakhirajan, and both fell to the ground. He took a dagger that was at his waist and split his belly, and killed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
