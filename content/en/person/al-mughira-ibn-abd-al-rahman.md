## Overview
al-Mughira ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham ibn al-Mughira, Abu Hashim, of Madina. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10362}

## Transmission and assessment
He narrated from his father, Hisham ibn 'Urwa, 'Abd Allah ibn Sa'id and others; his son 'Ayyash, Mahraz ibn Salama of Madina and Ya'qub ibn Muhammad al-Zuhri narrated from him. Ya'qub ibn Shayba called him one of the jurists of Madina who gave fatwas there, and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10362}
