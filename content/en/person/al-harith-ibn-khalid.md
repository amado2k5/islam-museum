## Summary
A Companion of Banu Taym ibn Murra; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Rayta and arrived in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Taym, with his wife Rayta bint al-Harith ibn Jubayla. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} He names his son Musa ibn al-Harith ibn Khalid ibn Sakhr ibn Amir among the children who perished. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-tahm|Banu Taym ibn Murra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- A conflict in the book: Rayta is said on p. 5 to have "perished in the land of Abyssinia" and on p. 11 to have "perished on the road"; see Rayta.
