## Overview
Muhammad al-Mutahhar, Fakhr al-Din, son of Yahya Sharaf al-Din ibn Ahmad Shams al-Din ibn al-Murtada Yahya, was one of the Zaydi imams of Yemen. Zaydism is a Shi'i school distinct from the Sunni frame the museum takes as its default; what follows rests on al-Zirikli's account. He gives his death as 980 AH, 1572 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 141}.

## Leadership
Al-Zirikli says he administered districts, led the army and had coins struck in his name while his father was still alive. He then besieged his father at al-Jiraf and moved him with some of his sons to the fortress of Kawkaban. He received the pledge of allegiance in the mountains of Sanaa after his father's death in 964 AH, his power grew and he ruled a wide territory in the highlands of Yemen {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 141}.

## War with the Ottomans
The Ottoman Turks fought him in long wars that ended in a settlement leaving him Sa'da, Kawkaban and their districts; he remained so until his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 141}.
