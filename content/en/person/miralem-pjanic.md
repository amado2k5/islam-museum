# Miralem Pjanić

## Summary

Miralem Pjanić is a Bosnian former professional footballer who played as a midfielder for clubs including Roma, Juventus and Barcelona and for the Bosnia and Herzegovina national team. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} Born in Tuzla in 1990 and raised in Luxembourg, he was recognised at Roma as one of Serie A's best midfielders, won four league titles at Juventus, and was widely cited as one of the greatest free-kick takers of all time. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} The English article describes him as a Muslim and an ethnic Bosniak; it names no school for him, so his data record leaves his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en}{cite:wikidata-miralem-pjanic}

## Early life

Pjanić was born on 2 April 1990 in Tuzla, in what was then SFR Yugoslavia, to Fahrudin, a former third-division footballer, and Fatima. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} His family fled to Luxembourg shortly before the outbreak of the Bosnian War; there he joined local club FC Schifflange 95 at the age of seven and moved to Metz in France in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} He represented Luxembourg at under-17 and under-19 level before switching to Bosnia and Herzegovina at senior level. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en}

## Metz, Lyon and Roma

Pjanić began his professional career at Metz, playing one season before signing for Lyon in 2008, and joined Roma in 2011. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} During his time in Rome he came to be recognised as one of the best midfielders in Serie A. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} The Guardian ranked him 55th in its 2015 list of the world's hundred best footballers, and 50th in 2019. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en}

## Juventus and afterwards

In 2016 Pjanić joined Juventus, where he won four league titles and two Coppa Italias and was named in the Serie A Team of the Year for four consecutive seasons, as well as in the 2016–17 UEFA Champions League Squad of the Season. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} He joined Barcelona in September 2020, spent a year on loan at Beşiktaş, signed for Sharjah in the UAE Pro League in 2022 and for CSKA Moscow in 2024, and retired in 2025 after leaving CSKA. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en} For Bosnia and Herzegovina he made his debut in 2008, earned 115 caps and scored 18 goals before his international retirement in 2024, and represented the nation at its first major tournament, the 2014 FIFA World Cup. {cite:wikipedia-miralem-pjanic-en}
