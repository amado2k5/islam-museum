## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Malik al-Khuza'i was in charge of the household and the police of [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.333}

Al-Mas'udi relates from him that a messenger of al-Rashid came to him at a time when he had never come before. He found al-Rashid sitting on his bed, and informed him of the dream of the Abyssinian and the spear, and ordered the release of Musa ibn Ja'far. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.333}
