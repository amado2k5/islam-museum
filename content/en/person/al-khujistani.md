## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Khujistani. Al-Suyuti says that he seized Khurasan, Kirman and Sistan in 267 AH, and resolved to march on Iraq, and struck coins in his name, and on the other face the name of al-Mu'tamid. He was then killed by his servants at the end of the year. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 566}
