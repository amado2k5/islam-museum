## Summary
Imran ibn Milhan, also called Ibn Abd Allah, Abu Raja al-Utaridi of Basra, one of the senior mukhadramun. He lived in the pre-Islamic period, accepted Islam after the conquest of Mecca, narrated from Umar, Ali and Ibn Abbas and others, and was held reliable with some heedlessness. Death 105 or 107 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13345}
