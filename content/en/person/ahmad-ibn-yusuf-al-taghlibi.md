## Summary
Abu Abd Allah Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Taghlibi of Baghdad was one of the great reciters of his time. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.28} He studied with [[person/abu-ubayd-al-qasim-ibn-sallam|Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam]] and with Musa ibn Hizam al-Tirmidhi (d. 251 AH). {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.28} Ibn al-Jazari lists him in Ghayat al-Nihaya without a death date, as does Abd al-Ghani Abd al-Khaliq in al-Tabari al-Faqih, according to Ahmad Khalid Babakr. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.28}

## With al-Tabari
When [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] moved from Kufa to Baghdad he met a number of scholars there, among them Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Taghlibi, from whom he learned the readings. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.28}

## Notes for researchers
- The thesis's footnote gives Abu Ubayd's death as read by the OCR as "214 AH", most likely a misreading; it is not carried over.
