## Summary
An Arab commander from Judham, serving the Romans over Ma'an. He embraced Islam after Mu'ta, and when the Romans gave him the choice between apostasy and death, he chose death and was crucified at Afra' {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 445-446}.

## His account
Farwa ibn Amr al-Judhami was an Arab commander among the Roman commanders, and their agent over the Arabs around him. His residence was Ma'an and the lands of Syria around it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445}. He embraced Islam after seeing the steadfastness, courage and sincere readiness of the Muslims to meet the enemy at the battle of Mu'ta in 8 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445} {cite:zad-al-maad}. He sent a messenger to the Prophet announcing his Islam, and gave him a white mule {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445}. When the Romans learned of his Islam, they seized and imprisoned him, then gave him the choice between apostasy and death. He chose death, so they crucified him in Palestine at a water called Afra' and beheaded him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 446}.

## For young visitors
An Arab commander who became a Muslim and sent the Prophet a gift. The Romans imprisoned him for not giving up his religion, and then killed him.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Ibn Ishaq traces his lineage as Farwa ibn Amr ibn al-Nafira al-Judhami, then al-Nafathi. He sent a messenger to the Prophet announcing his Islam and gave him a white mule. He was an agent of the Romans over the Arabs near them, with his residence at [[place/maan|Ma'an]] and the lands of Syria around it {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/237} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}. When the Romans learned of his Islam, they sought him until they seized and imprisoned him, and he composed verses in his prison. They then agreed to crucify him at a water called [[place/afra|Afra']] in Palestine, and he said two verses there. Al-Zuhri claimed that when he was brought forward to be killed, he said a verse conveying to the leading men of the Muslims that he had submitted to his Lord. Then they beheaded him and crucified him; see [[work/farwa-al-judhami-prison-verses|his verses]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}.
