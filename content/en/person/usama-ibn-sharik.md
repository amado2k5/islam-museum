## Summary
A Companion of the Banu Tha'laba. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5144}

## His report
Authors of the Sunan collections, Ahmad, Ibn Khuzayma, Ibn Hibban and al-Hakim narrated his report, which includes the instruction to seek treatment, since God has not sent a disease without sending a remedy for it, except old age. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5144}
