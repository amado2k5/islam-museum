## Summary
Of Banu Jumah, brother of Hatib ibn al-Harith; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Fakiha bint Yasar and died there a Muslim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him as the brother of Hatib ibn al-Harith, with his wife Fakiha bint Yasar, and says he died there a Muslim while his wife came in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} He lists him again among those who died in Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 1926: "Hattab ibn al-Harith ibn Mu'ammar ibn Habib ibn Wahb ibn Hudhafa ibn Jumah, the Qurashi of Jumah"; he records that Musa ibn Uqba listed him among the emigrants to Abyssinia, as did Ibn Ishaq and al-Tabari in the appendix. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.329}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
