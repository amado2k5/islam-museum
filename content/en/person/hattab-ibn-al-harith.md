## Summary
Of Banu Jumah, brother of Hatib ibn al-Harith; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Fukayha bint Yasar and perished there a Muslim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him brother of Hatib ibn al-Harith, with him his wife Fukayha bint Yasar, saying he perished there a Muslim and his wife came in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} He names him again among those who perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
