## Summary
Anas Action is a Saudi content creator who publishes under this name, presenting long-form YouTube videos on religion, society and general knowledge. {cite:thoughtleaders-anas-action}

Analytics for his channel describe it as an Arabic channel hosted by a single young male creator who produces his content independently; its first videos appeared on 18 October 2016, and the channel profile places it in Saudi Arabia. {cite:thoughtleaders-anas-action}

Anas Action publishes under this published name; none of the sources checked publishes a fuller legal name for him, so this entry carries the published name. {cite:youtube-anas-action} {cite:thoughtleaders-anas-action}

## His content
His video descriptions link his playlists — among them "Sirat Rasul Allah" (the Prophet's biography), "Al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya" and "Qasas Mukhtalifa" (assorted stories) — and his Snapchat, Instagram and Twitter accounts. {cite:youtube-anas-action}

His videos treat extended religious and social topics, such as the "Depression Trade" video, in which he discusses addiction and depression and urges stepping back from distractions and turning to worship. {cite:youtube-anas-action}

His videos average about forty minutes according to the channel analytics, and his style is built on his own on-camera narration and storytelling treatment of historical and religious events. {cite:thoughtleaders-anas-action} {cite:youtube-anas-action}

## Reach and audience
His channel's analytics page records in its FAQ 5 million subscribers and 511 million total views, while its descriptive copy gives the earlier rounded figures of over 4.1 million subscribers, over 400 million views and 132 videos; all are platform figures dated to the check day (25 October 2026), not fixed facts. {cite:thoughtleaders-anas-action}
