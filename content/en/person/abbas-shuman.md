## Overview
Deputy of al-Azhar al-Sharif and author of Nazarat fi'l-Tajdid (Islamic Research Academy, 1439/2018); the book presents itself as articles he published in the press and sets out his vision of renewal in contemporary fiqh. The date he took the deputyship and his other dates have not been checked against a second source.
