## Summary
Abu Ali al-Faraj ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ikhwa, a noted Baghdad tutor (mu'addib) and poet, who died on Friday 14 Jumada al-Akhira 546 AH. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Details
Ibn Khallikan mentions him in Wafayat al-A'yan in an incidental note inside the entry on Sayf al-Dawla: "The aforementioned Abu Ali Ibn al-Ikhwa died on Friday 14 Jumada al-Akhira of the year 546, and he was an accomplished poet." {cite:wafayat-ayan}

The editor's note identifies him as Abu Ali al-Faraj ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ikhwa, a Baghdad tutor counted among the well-known poets, and refers the reader to his biography and verses in Kharidat al-Qasr, the Iraq section — one of those fifth-century Baghdad literati whose poetry was preserved in the anthologies even when the biographical dictionaries had little else to tell. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
