## Overview
Yusuf ibn Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Hasan Ibn Abd al-Hadi al-Salihi, Jamal al-Din, known as Ibn al-Mibrad, was a versatile scholar and one of the Hanbali jurists, from al-Salihiyya in Damascus; the entry gives 840 to 909 AH, 1436 to 1503 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}.

## Books on jurisprudence, hadith and legal theory
On Hanbali jurisprudence he wrote "Mughni Dhawi al-Afham 'an al-Kutub al-Kathira fi al-Ahkam," printed; al-Zirikli says he saw it in the Saudi Library in Riyadh under no. 28 / 86 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. On hadith and transmission, all in manuscript: "al-Nihaya fi Ittisal al-Riwaya," "Dabt man Ghabara fiman Qayyadahu Ibn Hajar," "Tadhkirat al-Huffaz wa-Tabsirat al-Ayqaz," "al-Ikhtilaf bayna Ruwat al-Bukhari" and "Balghat al-Hathith ila 'Ilm al-Hadith"; also leaves of "al-Dabt wa-al-Tabyin li-Dhawi al-'Ilal wa-al-'Ahat min al-Muhaddithin," which he began and did not finish {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. On legal theory and dialectic, all in manuscript: "Tuhfat al-Wusul ila 'Ilm al-Usul," "Ghayat al-Sul ila 'Ilm al-Usul" and "Maqbul al-Manqul min 'Ilmay al-Jadal wa-al-Usul" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. His printed treatise on divorce is "Sayr al-Hathth," and in manuscript he wrote "al-Radd 'ala man Shaddada wa-'Assara fi Jawaz al-Udhiya bi-ma Tayassara" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}.

## Biography and the Sira
Al-Zirikli names "al-Durar al-Kabir," a part of it in manuscript, on biographies and lives; a manuscript fragment of "Tarikh al-Islam"; a manuscript on Shafi'i biographies lacking its beginning, which may be part of "al-Durar al-Kabir"; and "al-'Ata' al-Mu'ajjal," manuscript leaves from its start, on Hanbali biographies {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. On the Sira he wrote, in manuscript, "al-Iqtibas," comments and explanations on the Sira of Ibn Sayyid al-Nas, and "al-Mira fi Hall Mushkil al-Sira," its second part, explaining what is left vague in the Sira of Ibn Hisham {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. His manuscript treatises on merits are "al-'Aqd al-Tamm fiman Zawwajahu al-Nabi," "Mahd al-Shayd fi Manaqib Sa'id ibn Zayd," "Mahd al-Khalas fi Manaqib Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas," "Mahd al-Sawab fi Fada'il Amir al-Mu'minin Umar ibn al-Khattab," "Irshad al-Salik ila Manaqib Malik," "Bahr al-Dam fiman Takallama fihi Ahmad ibn Hanbal bi-Madh aw Dhamm" and "Maraqi al-Jinan bi-Qada' Hawa'ij al-Ikhwan" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. He also wrote "Ta'rif al-Ghadi," four manuscript leaves on the life of a brother named Ahmad, and "Tarikh al-Salihiyya" in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}.

## Treatises on public life and letters
His printed treatises include "al-Tabbakha" on the qualities of some foods, "'Iddat al-Mulimmat fi Ta'dad al-Hammamat," "al-I'anat 'ala Ma'rifat al-Khanat," "Thimar al-Maqasid fi Dhikr al-Masajid," "al-Hisba," "Nuzhat al-Rifaq" on the names of the markets of Damascus in his days, and "al-Durra al-Mudiyya" on the Prophetic family tree {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. In manuscript are "Adab al-Hammam wa-Ahkamuh," "Nuzhat al-Musamir fi Akhbar Majnun Bani 'Amir" and "Ghiras al-Athar wa-Thimar al-Akhbar wa-Ra'iq al-Hikayat wa-al-Ash'ar" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. He also wrote "al-Itqan fi Adwiyat al-Litha wa-al-Asnan" and "al-Itqan li-Adwiyat al-Yaraqan," which the entry prints without a mark for printed or manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}.

## His library and manuscripts
He compiled a manuscript "Fihrisa" of 58 leaves listing the books in his library {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. Al-Zirikli quotes Shadharat al-Dhahab that his pupil Shams al-Din Ibn Tulun wrote a large work on his life {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}. He adds that most of the manuscripts he named are kept, in Ibn al-Mibrad's own hand, in the Zahiriyya in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 219}.
