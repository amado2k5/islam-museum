Bojan Bogdanović (born 18 April 1989) is a Croatian former basketball player from Mostar. Starting at his hometown club Zrinjski and passing through Real Madrid's system, Cibona Zagreb and Fenerbahçe, he spent eleven seasons in the NBA (2014–2025) with Brooklyn, Washington, Indiana, Utah, Detroit and New York, and for Croatia he led all scorers at the 2016 Rio Olympics with 25.3 points per game.{cite:wikipedia-bojan-bogdanovic-en}{cite:wikipedia-bojan-bogdanovic-hr}{cite:wikidata-bojan-bogdanovic}

Recurring foot injuries ended his career: after a final, injury-hit return to Brooklyn, he announced his retirement on 29 June 2025.{cite:wikipedia-bojan-bogdanovic-en}
