## Summary
Hilal ibn Yahya ibn Muslim al-Ra'y al-Basri, called al-Ra'y for the breadth of his learning and his abundant fiqh; the source calls him a teacher of Malik. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15828} He learned fiqh from Abu Yusuf and Zufar and related hadith from Abu Awana and Ibn Mahdi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15828} His works include a book on contracts and Ahkam al-Waqf, which scholars passed among themselves.
