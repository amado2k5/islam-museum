## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Khabbab was the agent of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] over al-Mada'in. Al-Mas'udi says that the [[group/khawarij|Kharijites]] slaughtered him when they gathered around Abd Allah ibn Wahb al-Rasibi, ripped open the belly of his pregnant wife, and killed other women. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.695}

This was among the causes of Ali's march to Nahrawan ([[event/battle-of-nahrawan|the Battle of Nahrawan]]). {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.695}
