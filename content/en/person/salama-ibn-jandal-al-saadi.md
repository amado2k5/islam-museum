## Overview

Salama ibn Jandal ibn Abd Amr, of Banu Ka'b ibn Sa'd of Tamim, known as Abu Malik: a pre-Islamic poet and horseman from the Hijaz. Al-Zirikli's entry estimates his death at about 23 years before the Hijra (about 600 CE).{cite:al-aalam vol. 3 p. 106}

## His poetry

His verse shows wisdom and polish. He is placed in the class of al-Mutalammis and was a describer of horses. A small *diwan* of his poems was transmitted by al-Asma'i. Most historians treat him as an early pre-Islamic poet, though they note that he was a contemporary of Amr ibn Kulthum.{cite:al-aalam vol. 3 p. 106}
