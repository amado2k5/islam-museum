## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Husayni al-Sharif al-Tilimsani al-Alawi, a Maliki jurist. The source describes him as an imam of the Maghrib and a scholar of the Maliki school. He was born in Tlemcen in 710 AH (1310 CE) and grew up there, and died in 771 AH (1370 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.568}
