## Overview
Abu Uthman al-Mazini, a grammarian of Basra. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.143}

## On Sibawayh's book
Ibn al-Nadim quotes him as saying that whoever wants to write a large book on grammar after the book of Sibawayh should be ashamed. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.143}

His full name and his death are not set out here and need an additional source.
