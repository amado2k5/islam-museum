## Overview
Sari al-Saqati, an ascetic Sufi, and the teacher of al-Junayd. He died in the days of al-Mu'tazz {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.559}.

## His chain
In the chain of Sufism that Ibn al-Nadim read in the handwriting of Ja'far al-Khaldi: Sari took from Ma'ruf al-Karkhi, Ma'ruf took from Farqad al-Sanji, Farqad took from al-Hasan al-Basri, and al-Hasan took from Anas ibn Malik. It is reported that al-Hasan met seventy of the people of Badr {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.260}.
