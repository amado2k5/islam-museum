## Summary

Bekir Develi is a Turkish-German television presenter and writer, born on 4 June 1975 in Bielefeld, West Germany{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}. He presented the Ramadan programme Ramazan Sevinci on TRT 1 for seven years and some 600 episodes of travel, quiz and documentary programmes, and then became one of the best-known talk-show hosts on YouTube with his programme Peynir Gemisi{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}.

## Upbringing and education

Develi attended primary school in Germany; his family then returned to Turkey after his father's heart illness, and he completed middle and high school in Adana{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}. He took a degree in German-language teaching at 19 Mayıs University in Samsun, and worked in radio, theatre and trade before television{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}.

## Television career

Develi presented travel, quiz and documentary programmes amounting to some 600 episodes, and his name became attached to Ramazan Sevinci on TRT 1, which he hosted for seven years{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}. He moved to YouTube, launching the talk programme Peynir Gemisi in 2020, and his channel ranked second in YouTube Türkiye's 2021 list of Shorts creators{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}. In September 2025 he joined the Sumud flotilla sailing for Gaza{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}.

## Books

Develi's books include Allah Cümle'mizi Korusun (2018), Fabrika Ayarı (2020, with Hayati İnanç), Herkes İçin Siyer (2021, two volumes, with Muhammed Emin Yıldırım) and Aile İçinde (2021){cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}.

## Impact and audience

Develi is among Turkey's best-known hosts of religious and social talk programmes, with about 1.51 million YouTube subscribers in platform lists of November 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:wikipedia-bekir-develi-tr}.
