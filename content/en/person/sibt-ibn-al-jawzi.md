## Overview
Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi, preacher and historian; in 606 he sat in the Damascus mosque exhorting to jihad — the women cut their braids, made into three hundred bridles for the mujahidin's mounts — and the governor and crowds rode with him toward Nablus to fight the Franks. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.26}

## Lineage and works
His descent from Ibn al-Jawzi is disputed though he is famed by it; his father's name was Farghali per an old copy of al-Wafi bi'l-Wafayat, later corrupted to Qaz'ughli. His grandfather transmitted to him; he came to Damascus around 60x AH and won great acceptance for the sweetness of his preaching, though al-Malik al-Mu'azzam's moving him to the Hanafi school drew criticism. He left a tafsir in twenty-nine volumes, a sharh of the Jami' al-kabir, Mir'at al-zaman — begun from the start of time to the year of his death — and a volume on Abu Hanifa's virtues; he died on Tuesday night, 20 Dhu'l-Hijja, at his house on Mount Salihiyya, and al-Malik al-Nasir attended the burial. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.274-275}

## In his own words in Mir'at al-zaman
In the events of his own time in [[work/mirat-al-zaman|Mir'at al-zaman]], Sibt ibn al-Jawzi reports on himself under "the author said":
- In Damascus in 604 AH he read al-Jawaliqi's al-Mu'arrab with his teacher [[person/al-kindi-abu-al-yumn|Abu al-Yumn al-Kindi]]. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v1 p.9}
- He wrote a commentary on the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah's Ruh al-arifin, kept in the endowment of the Ashrafiyya Dar al-Hadith in Damascus. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.171}
- He preached in the Damascus mosque on Saturday 5 Rabi' I 607, to a crowd estimated at thirty thousand, had three hundred hobbles made of hair carried in for the fighters' horses, and led the people to Nablus, where al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Isa met him; he says he had not met him before that day. This differs from Shadharat al-dhahab, which places the episode in 606. See [[event/sibt-jihad-sermon-607|the session of 607 AH]]. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.172} {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.173} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 26}
- In 613 he travelled to Khilat, preached in the mosque of Harran at al-Malik al-Ashraf's request in the presence of al-Fakhr ibn Taymiyya, made a retreat at al-Raqqa, and was then equipped by al-Ashraf for the hajj by way of Baghdad. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.207}
- In 626 he preached in the Damascus mosque at al-Nasir Dawud's suggestion after Jerusalem was handed to the emperor, saying among other things: "the delegations of visitors to the Holy House are cut off; alas for the loneliness of those who dwell by it". See [[event/jerusalem-cession-626|the cession of Jerusalem]]. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.296}
