## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Shakir ibn Abd al-Ghani Ibn al-Ji'an is recorded by Ibn al-Imad among those who died in 889 AH; he says he died in Sha'ban at the age of forty {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 522}.

## What Ibn al-Imad records
Ibn al-Imad gives nothing beyond the name, the date of death and the age, so the notice names no occupation, teachers or place of death {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 522}. A birth around 849 AH is a derived calculation from the age given.

## The editor's note
The editor of the printed edition points in a footnote to his entry in al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami (volume 11, page 3), to al-Dhayl al-tamm, and to Bada'i al-zuhur {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 522}.
