# Zidane Iqbal

## Summary

Zidane Aamar Iqbal is a midfielder born in Manchester on 27 April 2003 to a Pakistani father and an Iraqi mother. A Manchester United academy product, he became the first British South Asian to play in the Champions League, then moved to Utrecht in the Netherlands and to Dundee United in Scotland. He represents Iraq at senior level. {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-en} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ar} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ur} {cite:wikidata-zidane-iqbal}

## Career

Iqbal played for Sale United from the age of four and joined Manchester United at nine. He signed his first professional contract in April 2021 and made his first-team debut on 8 December 2021, as a late substitute against Young Boys in the Champions League — the first British South Asian to play for the club, and the first to appear in the Champions League. In 2023 he moved to Utrecht, where he was named in the Eredivisie team of the month in December 2024 and March 2025, and in 2025 he joined Dundee United. {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-en} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ar} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ur} {cite:wikidata-zidane-iqbal}

Eligible for England, Iraq and Pakistan, Iqbal chose Iraq, his mother's country, and made his senior debut in 2022. He appeared for Iraq at the 2026 World Cup; an Al Jazeera profile in June 2026 called him the footballer who gave Pakistan its first World Cup moment, as the first player of Pakistani heritage to play at a World Cup. {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-en} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ar} {cite:wikipedia-zidane-iqbal-ur} {cite:wikidata-zidane-iqbal}
