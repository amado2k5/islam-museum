## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abdallah al-Akrawi al-Qalqashandi, known as Muhammad Hijazi al-Wa'iz, was a jurist learned in exegesis and hadith. He was born at Akra, one of the stations of the Egyptian pilgrim caravan on its way to the Hijaz, lived at Qalqashanda and died in Cairo. The entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 957 AH, 1550 CE, to 1035 AH, which it renders as 1625 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names among his books Fath al-mawla al-nasir, a commentary on al-Suyuti's al-Jami al-saghir in twelve volumes; Sawa al-sirat, on the signs of the Hour; al-Qawl al-mashruh fi al-nafs wa-l-ruh; and al-Burhan fi awqat al-sultan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
