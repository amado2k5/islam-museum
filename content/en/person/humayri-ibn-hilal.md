## Overview
Humayri ibn Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini, son of [[person/hilal-ibn-ahwaz|Hilal ibn Ahwaz]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The dispute over al-Mirghab
Al-Baladhuri reports that he disputed with Bishr ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra over al-Mirghab, and Bishr prevailed over him. Amr ibn Yazid al-Usaydi held out hopes for him and helped him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
