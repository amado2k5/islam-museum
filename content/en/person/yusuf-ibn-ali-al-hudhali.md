## Overview

Abu al-Qasim Yusuf ibn 'Ali ibn Jabbara al-Hudhali travelled from the Maghrib to the East and toured its lands. He died in 465 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 45}

## Al-Kamil

Ibn al-Jazari says he took from the masters of the readings as far as Transoxiana, read in Ghazna and elsewhere, and wrote al-Kamil, gathering fifty readings from the imams with fourteen hundred and fifty-nine riwayas and lines of transmission; he quotes al-Hudhali that those he met in this science numbered three hundred and sixty-five teachers from the far Maghrib to the gate of Farghana. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 44} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 45}
