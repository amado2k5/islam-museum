## Summary
Fatima bint [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and [[person/fatima|Fatima]], the Prophet's daughter, bearing her grandmother's name; Ibn Sa'd's Tabaqat and Ibn Hajar's al-Isaba list her among the children of Ali ibn Abi Talib.
