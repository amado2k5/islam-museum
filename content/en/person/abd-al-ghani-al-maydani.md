## Overview
Abd al-Ghani ibn Talib ibn Hamada ibn Ibrahim al-Ghunaymi al-Dimashqi al-Maydani: a distinguished scholar of the Hanafi school, named after the Maydan quarter of Damascus. He lived from 1222 to 1298 AH (1807–1881 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.33}

His works include al-Lubab, a Hanafi commentary on al-Quduri; Kashf al-Iltibas on Sahih al-Bukhari; a printed commentary on the Tahawiyya creed; and further commentaries and treatises on morphology and tawhid. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.33}
