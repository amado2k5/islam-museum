## Overview
Abu al-Husayn al-Jallabi al-Tabari, a jurist and teacher. He was trained in his own country, attended the session of al-Daraki, and then taught during his life. Al-Shirazi describes him as an excellent jurist who knew the hadith. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64}

Al-Shirazi says that he died seventeen days before al-Daraki, and al-Daraki died on the thirteenth of Shawwal 375 AH according to the editor's note. His date of death is therefore inferred and needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64}
