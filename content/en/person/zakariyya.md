## Summary
Zakariyya is a prophet who asked his Lord for a sign that his barren wife had conceived, and was told that his sign would be that he would not speak to people for three days except by gesture, as Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy relates (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:09:00}. The Sheikh notes that the Qur'an calls his wife an imra'a (woman) while she was barren and then a zawj (spouse) once God permitted her to conceive {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:07:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 03)

The Sheikh mentions that Zakariyya asked his Lord for a sign that his barren wife, who was about ninety years old, had conceived, and was told that his sign would be that he would not speak to people for three days except by gesture (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:09:00}.

He says that Surat Maryam mentions three nights while the other surah mentions three days, and he replies to anyone who thinks there is a mistake in this that there is none {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:09:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 09)

The Sheikh notes that the Qur'an calls Zakariyya's wife an imra'a (woman) while she was barren, and when God permitted her to conceive it calls her his zawj (spouse), and then God gave him the good news of a boy {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:07:30}.

He raises a question: if the boy's name is Yahya, meaning the living one, how does this name agree with the texts that establish that every soul will taste death and that all will die? {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:08:00}.

The Sheikh holds that Yahya was granted martyrdom so that he would stay alive, so that God's promise to Zakariyya would not fail, and he then returns to Surat al-Nazi'at, saying that those who pull out with force are angels who draw out the souls of the disbelievers {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:08:30}.
