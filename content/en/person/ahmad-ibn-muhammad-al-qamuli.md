## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abi al-Hazm, Najm al-Din Abu al-Abbas al-Qurashi al-Makhzumi al-Qamuli al-Shafi'i, named after Qamula in Upper Egypt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14964} He was an imam in fiqh, knowledgeable in usul and Arabic, deputised in judgement in Egypt, and held the hisba, teaching and judgeship in several cities. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14964} His works include al-Bahr al-muhit, a commentary on al-Ghazali's al-Wasit, Jawahir al-bahr, al-Rawd al-zahir, Mawdih al-tariq, a commentary on Ibn al-Hajib's al-Kafiya, and a completion of Ibn al-Khatib's tafsir. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14964}
