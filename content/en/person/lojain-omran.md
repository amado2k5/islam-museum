## Summary
Lojain Omran is one of the most-watched television faces in the Arab world — a Saudi presenter whose morning programmes and social-media presence reach tens of millions — and one of the region's few celebrity advocates for refugees. {cite:muslim500-lojain-omran} {cite:wikipedia-lojain-omran}

## Formation
Born in 1977 in al-Jubayl in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, she began her media career in Bahrain on the programme Good Morning, Bahrain ⁦(2004)⁩, having earlier worked in banking. {cite:wikipedia-lojain-omran}

## Career
She became a household name presenting Sabah al-Khair Ya Arab ⁦(Good Morning, Arabs)⁩ on MBC and has hosted talk and entertainment formats across Arab satellite channels, building a following reported at over twelve million across platforms. In 2022 she was appointed a UNHCR high-profile supporter, undertaking field visits and fundraising appeals for displaced people. {cite:muslim500-lojain-omran} {cite:wikipedia-lojain-omran}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists her under Media as a presenter whose humanitarian advocacy distinguishes her influence — celebrity reach turned toward refugee causes. {cite:muslim500-lojain-omran}
