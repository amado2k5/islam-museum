## Summary
Kurdawiyya bint Amr al-Basriyya is among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 608); Abu Abd al-Rahman Muhammad ibn al-Husayn records that she served [[person/shawana|Sha'wana]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## The blessing of service
She was asked what blessing she had gained from serving Sha'wana, and said: "I have not loved the world since I served her, nor worried about my provision, nor has any of the world's great grown large in my eyes out of hope of gain from him, and I have never looked down on any Muslim." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## For young visitors
Kurdawiyya served a devout woman named Sha'wana and learned from her not to love the world too much and never to look down on anyone.
