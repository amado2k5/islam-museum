## Summary
Burhan al-Din Ibn Abi'l-Wafa al-Husayni al-'Iraqi, the Shafi'i imam and scholar; he read in the seven readings, studied fiqh and composed, entered Cairo, and died at his zawiya in Damascus in Jumada I 787, aged sixty-six. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 346}
