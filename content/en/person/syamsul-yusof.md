## Summary
Mohd Syamsul bin Mohd Yusof, known as Syamsul Yusof, is a Malaysian actor, film director, writer, producer, rapper and singer born in Kuala Lumpur on 21 May 1984, the son of the producer and director Yusof Haslam. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

He is the youngest director to win the Malaysian Film Festival's Best Director award, doing so at the age of twenty-five, and he is known for the religious horror film Munafik (2016) and its sequel Munafik 2 (2018). {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

He is catalogued in this collection under the lane's genre ruling of 9 November 2026, which brings creators and directors of Islamic moral dramas and scripted films into scope. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

His verified Instagram account counted 1,826,612 followers in a direct read on 15 November 2026. {cite:instagram-syamsulyusoffilm}

## Education and beginnings
He grew up in Kuala Lumpur and studied at the Malaysia Institute of Integrative Media, also known as Academy TV3, where he took a diploma in film, TV broadcasting and journalism. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

He directed his first feature film, Evolusi KL Drift, in 2007, from a script he co-wrote with his cousin Rizal. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

## Munafik and the record films
After an absence of three years he returned with Munafik, released in Malaysian cinemas in February 2016, and he was named Best Director at the 57th Asia Pacific Film Festival in Cambodia for his work on that horror and religious film. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

Its sequel, Munafik 2 (2018), set the record as the highest-grossing Malaysian film, which it held until his own 2022 epic Mat Kilau surpassed it. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}

In October 2020 he confirmed on his Instagram account that he was working on a screenplay for Munafik 3, later put on hold while he directed a further horror film, Khawarij, for Astro Shaw. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-yusof-en}
