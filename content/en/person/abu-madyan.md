## Overview
The singular gnostic Abu Madyan Shu'ayb b. al-Husayn al-Andalusi, shaykh of the people of the Maghrib; he settled at Tlemcen, a man of works and striving, unmatched in worship and devotion, far-famed — Shaykh Muhyi al-Din Ibn 'Arabi calls him the shaykh of shaykhs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 783}

## His death
Men of merit took their formation from him, such as Abu 'Abdallah al-Qurashi, and many verifying scholars and virtuous men of excellence reached him, Ibn 'Arabi among them. The sultan of the Maghrib summoned him; reaching Tlemcen he dismounted, faced the qibla, recited the tashahhud and said "Here I come, here I come — I hasten to You, Lord, to please You," and died. He was buried in the cemetery of the worshippers nearing eighty; his grave there is a well-known shrine. He died in 590. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 783}
