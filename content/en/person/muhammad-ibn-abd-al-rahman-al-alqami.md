## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Ali ibn Abi Bakr al-Alqami, Shams al-Din, was a Shafi'i jurist learned in hadith, from one of the houses of learning in Cairo. He was born in 897 AH, 1491 CE, and died in 969 AH, 1561 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. He was a pupil of Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti and taught at al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "al-Kawkab al-Munir bi-Sharh al-Jami' al-Saghir", in manuscript in three volumes, of which the first volume has been printed, finished in 968 AH; "Qabas al-Nayyirayn 'ala Tafsir al-Jalalayn", in manuscript in Damascus; "Mukhtasar Ithaf al-Mahara bi-Atraf al-'Ashara", in manuscript in the Dar al-Kutub; "Multaqa al-Bahrayn fi al-Jam' bayna Kalam al-Shaykhayn"; and "al-Tuhaf al-Zurraf fi Talkhis al-Atraf" on hadith, in manuscript, photographed from the Arif Hikmat library for the University of Riyadh, 395 leaves {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
