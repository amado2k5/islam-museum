## Summary
Sohail Nakhooda is a British Muslim publisher, writer and designer, founder and editor-in-chief of the magazine Islamica; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

## Islamica
In the 1990s he founded and edited Islamica, an independent English-language Muslim magazine published in London and Amman — known for literary long-form writing on Islam, history, science and culture — which is remembered as a high point of Muslim magazine publishing in English. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

## Books
His written and designed titles include Islam: An Illustrated History, a visual narrative of Islamic civilisation, and he has served as editor and designer for Islamic publishing projects in Britain and Jordan, including work associated with the Royal Aal al-Bayt Institute in Amman. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

## Practice
He works as a design and publishing consultant for Muslim institutions and publishers, combining typesetting, cartography and editorial craft — a rare pairing of scholarship-facing content and professional book design. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

## Assessment
He is regarded as a quiet institution-builder of English-language Muslim publishing; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}


## Islamica Magazine
Islamica — founded by him and published from Amman — ran for years as a thoughtful English-language Muslim magazine of culture, faith and ideas, notable for refusing sectarian alignment. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

## Christian-Muslim engagement
His scholarly work on Christian-Muslim relations — and editorial collaborations across traditions — places him within the Amman-centred tradition of measured interfaith publishing. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}

Born in Malaysia and educated at the International Islamic University Malaysia and elsewhere, he moved to Jordan in the 1990s and built a career in Islamic publishing — founding and editing Islamica and working with the Royal Aal al-Bayt Institute. {cite:muslim500-sohail-nakhooda}
