## Overview
Ala al-Dawla ibn Kakuya, a Daylami ruler over Isfahan, one of the rulers of the [[polity/kakuyid-dynasty|Kakuyid state]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
He obeyed Sultan Mahmud ibn Sabuktakin, and the khutba was given for him in 420 AH. Then Mas'ud ibn Mahmud came against Isfahan and took it from him, and he left some of his companions to govern it, and its people rose against his agent and killed him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In al-Rayy
He was amir of al-Rayy in 429 AH when the Ghuzz stormed it, and they committed excess in plunder and killing, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
