## Overview
Ali ibn al-Junayd al-Iskafi, al-Mu'tasim's jester and boon companion; of his drolleries — he broke wind in Ibn Hammad's sleeve as the man drew near and said 'I see nothing, but I did not know your clothes held a privy'; and to al-Mu'tasim, when his groom Sindan was barred from him, 'if you see Sindan the Turk, give him my greeting — his case is that you set between us a man whom I saw you before I saw him, and I miss him'.
