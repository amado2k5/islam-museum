## Overview
Ali ibn al-Junayd al-Iskafi, a boon-companion and jester of al-Mu'tasim. Al-Mas'udi relates some witticisms from his sessions, among them that he sent his greetings to the caliph's slave-boy Sandan and said that he had seen al-Mu'tasim before the boy saw him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.441-442}
