## Summary
Mus'ab ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, a Kufan Successor (tabi'i) for whom the Six Books narrate, who remained in Kufa until the year 103 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p. 3859}

## Details
Al-Dhahabi's entry in Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' reads in full: "Mus'ab ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas; he stayed in Kufa until the year 103; they narrated for him in the Six Books." {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p. 3859}

He is the son of the Companion Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, and his notices run through the biographical literature by way of Ibn Sa'd's Tabaqat and Tahdhib al-Tahdhib as al-Dhahabi indicates; in the Siyar his name falls between the entries of Mus'ab ibn Abdallah al-Zubayri of Baghdad and Mus'ab ibn Umayr the Badri — one of the sons of the Companions whose transmissions from their fathers reached the canonical collections. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p. 3859-3860}
