## Summary
[[person/busra-bint-safwan|Busra bint Safwan]] ibn Nawfal al-Qurashiyya al-Asadiyya, daughter of Waraqa ibn Nawfal's brother — or, it is said, daughter of Safwan ibn Umayya ibn Muharrith of Banu Malik ibn Kinana; Ibn al-Athir holds the first sounder. Her mother Salima bint Umayya al-Sulamiyya; she was sister of Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt by her mother {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1829}.

## Her marriage
She was wife of al-Mughira ibn Abi al-As and bore him A'isha, whom Marwan ibn al-Hakam married and who bore him Abd al-Malik — Ibn Hajar calls this an "error", Abd al-Malik's mother being a daughter of Mu'awiya, al-Mughira's brother, as al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar said, the most knowing of his people's lineage {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1829}.

## Hijra and pledge
Al-Shafi'i said she has an early precedence and a hijra; Ibn Hibban said she was among the emigrant women; Mus'ab said she was among the women who pledged {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1830}.

## Transmission
She related from the Prophet; from her relate Marwan ibn al-Hakam, Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, Umm Kulthum bint Uqba and Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1829}. Ishaq recorded in his Musnad from Amr ibn Shu'ayb: I was with Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab who said "Busra bint Safwan is one of my maternal aunts" — then mentioned the hadith on touching the private part {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1830}.

## Her work
Ibn al-Kalbi mentions she was a hairdresser who tended the women of Mecca {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1830}.
