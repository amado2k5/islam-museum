## Overview
Khalil Thabit, Abu Karim, was a Lebanese journalist, one of the ablest writers on foreign politics. Born at Dayr al-Qamar, he graduated from the American school, ran the Sudan press and library at Khartoum, then settled in Cairo as editor-in-chief of al-Muqattam, one of the largest and strongest Arabic newspapers, and headed the committee for publishing the Taymur works, prefacing them with introductory chapters. He died at Beirut under treatment in 1383 AH (1964 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.316}

He translated Masarrat al-Haya from English and had no bent for original writing. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.316}
