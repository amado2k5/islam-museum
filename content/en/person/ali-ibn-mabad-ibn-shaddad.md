## Overview
'Ali ibn Ma'bad ibn Shaddad, the imam, hafiz and jurist Abu al-Hasan and Abu Muhammad al-'Abdi al-Raqqi, resident of Egypt, one of the great imams; he died in 218 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2860}

## Transmission
He narrated from Isma'il ibn Ja'far, al-Layth ibn Sa'd, 'Ubaydallah ibn 'Amr al-Raqqi, Musa ibn A'yun, Isma'il ibn 'Ayyash, al-Thawri, Hushaym, al-Mu'afa ibn 'Imran, Ibn Wahb, Abu Bakr ibn 'Ayyash, al-Shafi'i and many others, and transmitted al-Jami' al-Kabir and al-Jami' al-Saghir from Muhammad ibn al-Hasan. Yahya ibn Ma'in, Abu 'Ubayd, Ishaq al-Kawsaj, Khashish ibn Asram, Abu Hatim and many others narrated from him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2860}
