## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Yusuf al-Jurjani, Abu Muhammad, is called by al-Dhahabi the judge, the imam, the hadith scholar and the hafiz {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. He was born in 409 AH, which is 1018 to 1019 CE {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. He should not be confused with Abd Allah ibn Yusuf al-Tinnisi, the transmitter of the Muwatta' who died in 218 AH, nor with Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Yusuf al-Juwayni, the father of Imam al-Haramayn; the museum keeps three separate records.

## Teachers and students
He heard from Hamza ibn Yusuf al-Sahmi, Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Khandaqi and the pupils of Ibn Adi and of al-Isma'ili {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. In Nishapur he heard from Abu Hafs ibn Masrur and Abd al-Ghafir ibn Muhammad al-Farisi, and from others of their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. Among those who narrated from him al-Dhahabi names his sister's son Tamim ibn Abi Sa'id al-Mu'addib, al-Junayd ibn Muhammad al-Qayini, Ali ibn Hamza al-Musawi, Wajih al-Shahhami and Abu al-As'ad Hibat al-Rahman ibn al-Qushayri {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. The entry also names, among his teachers, Abu Nu'aym Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad al-Astarabadhi the younger, a companion of Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili, and Abu Ma'mar al-Mufaddal ibn Isma'il al-Isma'ili {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 159}.

## His books and his death
Al-Dhahabi says he compiled and wrote, and had memory and understanding {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. He put together a book on the merits of al-Shafi'i and another on the merits of Ahmad ibn Hanbal {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. He lived eighty years and died in Dhu al-Qa'da 489 AH, which is October to November 1096 CE {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 19 p. 158}. The entry gives no place of birth or death.
