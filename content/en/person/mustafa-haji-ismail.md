## Summary

Mustafa Haji Ismail Harun is a Somali scholar and preacher whom Somali Guardian described in January 2026 as "Somalia's most prominent religious scholar".{cite:somaliguardian-mustafe-2026} His lectures and lessons in the Somali language are heard in Somalia and across the diaspora: his own YouTube channel, Sheekh Mustafe Official Channel, counted 452,000 subscribers, and its 295 videos had been viewed 38,987,658 times, as the channel's statistics were read on 4 November 2026.{cite:youtubers-me-sheekh-mustafe} In Somali he is named "Shiikh Mustafe Xaaji Ismaaciil Xaaji Haaruun", and his Wikidata item (Q124099593) carries the label "Mustafe Haji Ismail Harun" and the description "Norwegian-Somali ulama and philosopher".{cite:wikidata-mustafa-haji-ismail}

## Early life and education

The only narrative source available for his life is a biography published on Medium in December 2024 with no sources cited; its material is therefore related here as its account, and relied on only where it agrees with the description on the Wikidata item, which records his education at Somali National University.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography}{cite:wikidata-mustafa-haji-ismail} According to the sketch, he was born in the countryside of Burao to a family that lived for a time in Aden; his father and several of his grandparents were sheikhs; he received his first Qur'anic schooling in a dugsi, then attended primary school in Burao and a technical institute there.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography} The sketch relates that he earned a BSc in electrical engineering at Somali National University in 1980 and studied Islamic sciences with sheikhs in the country.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography} The sketch also gives 1956 as his birth year, but no other, trusted source was found confirming any birth date, so no birth date is recorded in this record's data.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography}

## Beginnings and career

After graduating he worked, according to the sketch, as a teacher at Burao Technical School, and about a year and a half after he began teaching the Somali military regime imprisoned him repeatedly and he remained in prison for years.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography} He later settled in Norway, which agrees with the Wikidata item's description of him as Norwegian-Somali and with the Somali and Norwegian citizenships recorded in the linked Wikimedia Commons category.{cite:wikidata-mustafa-haji-ismail} The sketch relates that he earned the respect of Somali Muslim communities in Somalia and Norway, supported the building of mosques and educational centres, and delivered hundreds of lectures on religious, social and cultural topics.{cite:medium-mustafa-biography}

## Channel and distribution

His own YouTube channel, Sheekh Mustafe Official Channel, carries his sermons, lectures and question-and-answer sessions, and held 295 videos by the statistics of November 2026.{cite:youtubers-me-sheekh-mustafe} His lectures are not confined to his own channel: the Kenya-based Somali production house Hadaf Production — 521,000 subscribers by the statistics of 3 November 2026 — lists lectures of his in its catalogue, among them "Taariikhda iyo Burburkii Falastiin" (The History and Destruction of Palestine) of December 2023.{cite:vidiq-hadaf-production} That house is an external distribution channel, not his property and not part of his channel.{cite:vidiq-hadaf-production}

## Positions and criticism

The following positions are related as their sources attribute them; they are not the museum's verdict on their subject: in January 2026, speaking in Somaliland, he warned that Somaliland was betraying its own history by engaging Israel, and drew a parallel with the 1988 crackdown that displaced the people of Hargeisa and Burao and left the country's reputation scarred.{cite:somaliguardian-mustafe-2026} A second, independent report of the same occasion adds that he spoke at a mosque in Hargeisa and describes him as one of Somalia's leading religious scholars.{cite:axadle-mustafe-2026}

## Impact and audience

His impact is measured by the audience for his Somali-language lectures: 452,000 subscribers on his own channel and about 39 million views of its videos, with Somali production houses carrying his lectures beyond it.{cite:youtubers-me-sheekh-mustafe}{cite:vidiq-hadaf-production} These figures remain dated platform counts, as read in November 2026, not facts the museum verifies.{cite:youtubers-me-sheekh-mustafe}
