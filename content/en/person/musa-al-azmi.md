## Summary
Musa ibn Rashid al-Azmi is a Kuwaiti researcher in the Prophetic biography (Seerah), known for his book "Al-Lu'lu' al-Maknun fi Sirat al-Nabi al-Ma'mun", published by Dar al-Sumay'i in Riyadh in its first edition in 2013. {cite:makkahnewspaper-musa-al-azmi}

No source consulted publishes a birth date for him, so none is recorded here. {cite:saaid-musa-al-azmi} {cite:makkahnewspaper-musa-al-azmi}

## Career
Al-Azmi spent years composing his book on the Seerah; the Saaid page collecting his tweets records that the research for the book took ten years and its review two years. {cite:makkahnewspaper-musa-al-azmi} {cite:saaid-musa-al-azmi}

Between 23 May and 7 August 2013 (29 Ramadan 1434 AH) he published the whole Prophetic biography as a series of 770 tweets on his Twitter account (@Musa_al3azmi), from the birth to the death, and the Saaid site collected them on a single page. {cite:saaid-musa-al-azmi}

## Works
His book "Al-Lu'lu' al-Maknun fi Sirat al-Nabi al-Ma'mun" runs to four volumes; it was endorsed by Sheikh Othman al-Khamees and published by Dar al-Sumay'i in Riyadh (first edition, 2013). {cite:saaid-musa-al-azmi} {cite:makkahnewspaper-musa-al-azmi}

Makkah Newspaper quotes the book in an article on the graves of Khadija and Maymuna, among it his report that Khadija died before the prayer was prescribed at the Isra and Mi'raj and was buried at al-Hajun in the cemeteries of Makkah. {cite:makkahnewspaper-musa-al-azmi}
