## Overview
[[person/muhammad-vi-uthmani|Sultan Wahid al-Din Muhammad VI]], the last sultan of the Ottoman state, who was in Istanbul under occupation after the First World War. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.468}

## His stance on Mustafa Kemal
Al-Salabi describes him as remaining in Istanbul, submissive to the humiliation that was imposed on him during the occupation. The newspapers relayed from him a call for the blood of Mustafa Kemal to be shed and for him to be treated as a rebellious rebel, which increased people's anger at him in comparison with the struggle of Mustafa Kemal. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.468}

## Notes for researchers
- The entry is based on al-Salabi's summary of the Ottoman state, and his evaluation of the sultan's stance is the author's own. We have not yet gone back to the newspapers he relays from.
