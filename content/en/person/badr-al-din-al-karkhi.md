## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Karkhi, titled Badr al-Din, was a jurist learned in Qur'anic exegesis; he became well known in Egypt and died there. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 910 AH, 1504 CE, and his death as 1006 AH, 1598 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 61}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names Majma' al-Bahrayn, a gloss on Tafsir al-Jalalayn in four manuscript volumes, and al-Manhaj al-Asna fi Ayat al-Kursi wa-l-Asma' al-Husna, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 61}.
