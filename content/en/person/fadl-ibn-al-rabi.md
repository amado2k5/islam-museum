## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn al-Rabi, vizier of [[person/muhammad-al-amin|al-Amin]] and chamberlain of [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.459}

## With al-Amin
Al-Jahiz described him as the most intelligent and greatest of men. Al-Suyuti reports that he knew that if the caliphate passed to al-Ma'mun, al-Ma'mun would spare him nothing. He therefore incited al-Amin to depose him, and urged him to appoint his own son Musa as heir. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.459} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.473}

This is al-Suyuti's explanation of his position, not the museum's judgement.
