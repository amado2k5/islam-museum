## Overview
The amir Shams al-Din Abu'l-Fada'il Batikin the Armenian (d. 640 Baghdad); a mamluk of the mother of Caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah, he served as deputy of Irbil under al-Mustansir for a long stretch; when the Tatars took Irbil in late 634 he returned to Baghdad and died there {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
