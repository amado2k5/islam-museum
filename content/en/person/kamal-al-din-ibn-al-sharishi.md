## Summary
Kamal al-Din Ibn al-Sharishi was a Damascene scholar and contemporary of [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]]. Muhammad Rashad Salim, in a note to his introduction to [[work/dar-taarud-al-aql-wal-naql|Dar' Ta'arud al-'Aql wa'l-Naql]], identifies him as shaykh of the Dar al-Hadith and of the Ribat al-Nasiri, born in 653 AH and died in Shawwal 718 AH on his way to the pilgrimage, referring for his biography to *Shadharat al-Dhahab*, *al-Durar al-Kamina* and *Fawat al-Wafayat*. {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 8}

## Offices in Damascus
The editor quotes Ibn Kathir that Ibn al-Sharishi became superintendent of the [[building/umayyad-mosque|Umayyad Mosque]] in Safar 703 AH and resigned in Rajab of the same year, and that when al-Afram, the sultan's deputy, came to [[place/damascus|Damascus]], the Shamiyya Barraniyya college and the Dar al-Hadith were assigned to him "on the advice of Shaykh Taqi al-Din Ibn Taymiyya". {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 9} He also quotes him that in 708 AH Ibn al-Sharishi resigned as agent of the treasury and refused to return, remaining out of office until Ashura of 709 AH, when his appointment was renewed under the new regime. {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 10}

## His reply to the Dar'
The editor quotes Ibn Abd al-Hadi in *al-'Uqud al-Durriyya* that Ibn Taymiyya had "a book of about a volume in which he answered what Kamal al-Din Ibn al-Sharishi raised against this book", that is, the Dar'. {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 4} The editor takes Ibn al-Sharishi's death in Shawwal 718 AH as the latest possible date for the Dar', and judges that Ibn al-Sharishi wrote his reply in Damascus after Ibn Taymiyya's return there. {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 8} {cite:dar-taarud-salim-v1-1411 p. 10}

## Note
These sources give neither the text nor the title of his reply. He is not to be confused with [[person/al-sharishi|al-Sharishi]], the commentator on the Maqamat (d. 685 AH).
