## Summary
A Companion of Zubayd, an ally of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia, came in the two ships, and the Prophet set him over the Muslims' fifth. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him of Banu Sahm, describing him as "an ally of theirs from Banu Zubayd", and says the Prophet set him over the Muslims' fifth. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} The editors' note remarks that his father's name is related with a doubled zay without hamza, and the correct form has the hamza, as al-Daraqutni fixed it (p. 5).

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The reading of "al-Jaz'": related with doubled zay without hamza; the correct form has hamza per al-Daraqutni (editors' note p. 5).
