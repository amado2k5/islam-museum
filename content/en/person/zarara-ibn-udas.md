## Overview
Zarara ibn Udas al-Tamimi, a lord of the Banu Darim, of the [[group/banu-darim|Banu Darim]] {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 412}.

## His story
As-ad ibn Amr ibn al-Mundhir al-Lakhmi was suckled by him, and Suwayd ibn Abd Allah ibn Darim killed him. He raided with Amr ibn al-Mundhir and failed. He advised him to pay money to Tayy, and he was taken captive, killed and his property seized, and this remained in the hearts of Tayy against him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 412}.

Amr ibn Multaqit al-Ta'i incited the king against him. Then Zarara set out by night to his people and fell ill, and at his death he charged his son Hajib to take his boys into the Banu Nahshal, and charged his nephew with Amr ibn Multaqit {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 412}.
