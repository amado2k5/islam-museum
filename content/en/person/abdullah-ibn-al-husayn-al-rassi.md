## Overview
Abdullah ibn al-Husayn was the brother of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], who left him as deputy at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] when he went out to Yahsub, Ra'in and those districts to set them in order, going as far as Jayshan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} The text says nothing else about Abdullah in these pages.
