## Summary
Habib Ali Zaenal Abidin Al-Hamid is a freelance preacher from Bondowoso, East Java, Indonesia, who became one of the best-known habib preachers in Malaysia through the Darul Murtadza majlis. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021} A peer-reviewed study of his preaching in Tawau, Sabah, is the principal written source for his profile. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021}

## Formation
He comes from Bondowoso in East Java and travelled to Hadhramaut in Yemen to study, learning usuluddin, sharia and tasawwuf among other sciences. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021} The study notes a gift for public speaking evident since he was small. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021}

## Preaching in Malaysia
The habib group around him has been active in spreading dakwah since about 2012. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021} He runs a weekly programme at Masjid Mu'az bin Jabal. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021} At the Malam Cinta Rasul programme at the same mosque, held under Majlis Ta'lim Darul Murtadza in Tawau, Sabah, some 4,000 participants attended, with Habib Ali giving the tazkirah address. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021} His Instagram profile names him chairman of Yayasan Ar-Riayah and guide (pembimbing) of Darul Murtadza. {cite:instagram-habib-ali-zaenal-abidin} The study describes his Darul Murtadza and Ba'Alawi majlis milieu; that description is a picture of his tradition and circle, and this record does not treat it as an attestation of a school. {cite:anida-habib-ali-2021}

## Reach
His Instagram account, @habibalizaenalalhamid, counted 1,432,708 followers when the platform profile was read directly on 15 November 2026. {cite:instagram-habib-ali-zaenal-abidin} His Facebook page counted 224,308 followers on the same day's direct read. {cite:facebook-habib-ali-zaenal-abidin} A tracker result for a "Habib Ali" YouTube channel could not be verified as this preacher and is not counted here. {cite:instagram-habib-ali-zaenal-abidin} Audience figures are platform figures, recorded with their dates; they change constantly. {cite:instagram-habib-ali-zaenal-abidin}
