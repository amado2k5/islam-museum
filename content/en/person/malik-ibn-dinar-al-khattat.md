## Overview
Malik ibn Dinar, a client of a woman of the Banu Sama ibn Lu'ayy ibn Ghalib, known by the kunya Abu Yahya. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.16}

## Copying
He used to copy out Qur'an codices for a wage. Ibn al-Nadim says that he died in 130 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.16}
