## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Utba ibn Mas'ud al-Hudhali, nephew of Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud, the emigrant. Al-Mas'udi says that he died in 86 AH in the days of al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, and that his son Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah was among the great men of knowledge. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.154}
