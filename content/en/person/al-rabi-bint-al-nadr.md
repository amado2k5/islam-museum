## Overview
Al-Rabi' daughter of al-Nadr, the paternal aunt of Anas ibn Malik. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report about al-Hasan al-Basri
Al-Baladhuri says it is said that al-Hasan al-Basri and his brother Sa'id came to her from the captives of Maysan, and she set them free, so their clientage became hers. It is also said that their companion was Jamila, the wife of Anas, of the Banu Salama. The report differs about her identity. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data need an additional source to choose between the two accounts, and for her biography.
