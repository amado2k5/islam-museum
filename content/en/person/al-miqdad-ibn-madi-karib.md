## Overview
Al-Miqdad ibn Madi Karib al-Kindi, Abu Karima, a Companion who came from Yemen as a youth with the Kinda delegation (d. 87 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet, Khalid ibn al-Walid, Mu'adh ibn Jabal and Abu Ayyub al-Ansari; he has forty hadith, one of which al-Bukhari alone reports. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}
