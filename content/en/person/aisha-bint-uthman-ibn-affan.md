## Summary
Aisha bint [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]], counted among the mistresses of eloquence and rhetoric {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 162}.

## Her address after her father's killing
When her father was killed and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] was given allegiance, she delivered an extended address denouncing the killers for shedding unlawful blood, saying "To God we belong and to Him we return; he gave his soul and shed his blood in the sanctuary of God's Messenger, and had He willed He could have prevented it" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 162-163}. She dwelt on what God had granted Uthman of proof and of aid to the Muslims, among it her words "you took vengeance on him for less than he had done to you" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 163}.

She then turned to what came after: she mentioned the caliphate of Umar ibn al-Khattab as "the best of counsel," seconded by the judiciary and thirded by consultation, then said "he went to Samarra with his staff laid across his shoulder and you bowed to him as a ring bows to its rod" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 163-164}.
