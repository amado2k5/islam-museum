## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Ibn al-Qaysarani Muhammad b. Nasr the adib, bearer of poetry's banner in his age; born at Acre in 487, he administered Damascus's water-clocks for a time, then settled at Aleppo — versed in astronomy, the stars, geometry and reckoning, praising kings and grandees, exchanging letters, answers and flytings with Ibn al-Munir; he died the night of Wednesday the eleventh of Sha'ban at Damascus and was buried at the Bab al-Faradis cemetery. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 630-631}
