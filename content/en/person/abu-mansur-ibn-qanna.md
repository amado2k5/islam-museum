## Overview
Abu Mansur Ibn Qanna, vizier of the Buyid 'Imad al-Dawla Abu Kalijar; a virtuous man, among whose monuments: he endowed a library for students of knowledge gathering nineteen thousand volumes — none in it but an ascribed original — including four thousand sheets in the hand of the Banu Muqla. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.406}
