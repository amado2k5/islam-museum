## Overview
Madyan ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Qusuni was the chief physician of Egypt in his time, accomplished in letters and history. He died in Egypt after 1044 AH (1634 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.197}

His books include Rayhan al-Albab wa-Ray'an al-Shabab fi Maratib al-Adab; Qamus al-Atibba' wa-Namus al-Alibba' on medical simples, finished in 1044 AH; Tayyibat al-Anba' fi Tabaqat al-Atibba'; and a substantial history which al-Muhibbi cites without naming and al-Baghdadi calls Tarikh Misr. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.197}
