## Summary
Umayya ibn al-Samit was a devout worshipper, steadfast in the stations, who held his own self in contempt. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 530 in the scan, which is read as 520, with sequence 521, on page 154. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/154}

## A look at a youth
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Muhammad relates from his brother Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Sufi that he heard Khayr al-Nassaj al-Sufi say: I was with Umayya ibn al-Samit when he looked at a youth. He then recited a verse on God being with them wherever they are and seeing what they do, and spoke of the angels by whom God guards His prison. He said: blessed is God; how great is the trial He set me with this look at the youth! I could compare my look at him only to a fire that falls on reeds on a windy day, leaving nothing standing. He asked God's forgiveness for the trial his eyes had brought to his heart and his inner parts, saying: I feared I would not be saved from its harm nor cleared of its sin, even if I came to the Resurrection with the deeds of seventy truthful ones. Then he wept until he nearly died. A phrase in his weeping is unclear in the scan and is not given here. The Qur'an verses are referred to by sense only. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/154}
