## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath al-Khuza'i. Abu al-Abbas appointed him over Ifriqiya, and he repaired the city of Kairouan and its mosque. Then al-Mansur removed him and appointed in his place Umar ibn Hafs Hazarmard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.322}
