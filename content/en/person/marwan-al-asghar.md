## Summary
Abu'l-Samt Marwan ibn Abi'l-Junub ibn Marwan al-Akbar ibn Abi Hafsa; among the famous foremost poets of his age; al-Mubarrad in the Kamil gives some of his people's reports then says: the most deep-rooted folk in poetry were the Al Hassan — six of them in a row, all poets — but in the present time it is the Al Abi Hafsa, a house each of whom is a poet inheriting it father from son; Yahya ibn Abi Hafsa, kunya Abu Jamil, whose mother Tuhya bint Maymun is said to descend from al-Nabigha al-Ja'di — and that is how poetry came to Abu Hafsa. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
