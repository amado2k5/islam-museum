## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahid, Abu Mansur ibn al-Sabbagh of Baghdad, nephew and son-in-law of Abu Nasr ibn al-Sabbagh, author of al-Shamil. A Shafi'i jurist, he studied with his uncle and with al-Qadi Abu al-Tayyib, heard hadith and transmitted to Muhammad ibn Tahir al-Maqdisi and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16636} Ibn al-Najjar called him an excellent jurist who knew the school by heart and left good works; al-Subki says his fatwas were collected from his uncle's words. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16636}
