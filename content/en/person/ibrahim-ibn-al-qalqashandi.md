## Summary

Shaykh of shaykhs Burhan al-Din Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ahmad ibn Ali ibn al-Qalqashandi, a Shafii. He was a traveller and a memoriser of hadith {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 473}.

He heard hadith from Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Hajar, Jalal al-Din al-Balqini, al-Badr al-Damiri and al-Sakhawi, and he was the last person to transmit from that generation {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 473}.

He died on Tuesday, the tenth of Jumada al-Akhira in the year 922, at the age of ninety-one {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 473}.
