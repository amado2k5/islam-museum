## Overview
Abd Kulal ibn Muthawwib, a Himyarite Tubba'. Al-Baladhuri says that he set out from Yemen and came upon Tasm and Jadis at al-Yamama, who had been weakened by their wars with each other, and killed them. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 8}

He crucified a woman of Jadis called al-Yamama, daughter of Marr, at the gate of Jaww, and Jaww was named after her. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 8}
