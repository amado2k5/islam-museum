## Summary
Al-Rabi' ibn Anas al-Bakri of Basra and Khurasan was one of the exegetes among the Successors; he transmitted from [[person/abu-al-aliya-al-riyahi|Abu al-Aliya]] and [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]] and died in 139 AH, as Ahmad Khalid Babakr notes from al-Jarh wa-l-Ta'dil and Tahdhib al-Tahdhib. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.49}

## His commentary in al-Tabari's Jami' al-Bayan
The thesis says al-Rabi' ibn Anas's commentary left a clear mark on [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]]'s, and that al-Tabari used it through Ammar ibn al-Hasan, from Abd Allah ibn Abi Ja'far, from his father, from al-Rabi' ibn Anas. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.49}
