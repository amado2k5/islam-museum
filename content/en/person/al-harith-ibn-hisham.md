## Summary
A noble and chief among the lords of [[group/quraysh|Banu Makhzum]], brother of Abu Jahl ibn Hisham; he fought the Muslims at Badr and Uhud, then became Muslim on the day of Mecca's conquest and his Islam was good. The Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]], and he was martyred striving in the battle of the Yarmuk in Syria {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## His Islam and jihad
Al-Harith fled on the day of Badr after his brother Abu Jahl's killing, and remained hostile until the conquest of Mecca, when Umm Hani bint Abi Talib granted him protection; the Prophet gave him security and he became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/53-54}. The Prophet gave him a hundred camels at al-Ji'rana in the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won over]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}. Then he went out with his family and wealth as a striver to Syria in Abu Bakr's and Umar's caliphates, and was martyred in the Syrian conquests {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
Abu Jahl's brother, who fought the Muslims at first, then became Muslim after the conquest of Mecca and his Islam was good; he went out striving to bring peace to the lands of Syria until he was martyred.
