## Summary
Najm al-Din Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Mun'im ibn 'Ali al-Nahri al-Harrani, Hanbali jurist and preacher, travelled to Baghdad in his youth in 478 and studied fiqh under Abu'l-Fath ibn al-Muni. He returned to Harran, then came to Baghdad a second time in 596 with his two sons al-Najib 'Abd al-Latif and al-'Izz 'Abd al-'Aziz, settled there and held a preaching assembly; he was sweet-spoken, nimble of phrase, and proud-spirited despite severe poverty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 11-12}

## On him
Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi said: he was very shy, visited my grandfather, and heard hadith with us; and he mentioned that al-Nahri settled at Baghdad because of a rift between him and Harran's khatib Ibn Taymiyya, fearing the man would move against him. He died on Thursday, 16 Rabi' I 601. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 11-12}
