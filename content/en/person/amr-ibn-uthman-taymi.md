## Summary
Of Banu Taym ibn Murra; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed at al-Qadisiyya with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants" of Banu Taym, saying he was killed at al-Qadisiyya with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-tahm|Banu Taym ibn Murra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}
