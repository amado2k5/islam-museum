## Overview
Harun ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali, known as al-Sari, a Kharijite leader among the members of the [[group/khawarij|Kharijites]]. Ibn Khaldun in "Al-Ibar" reports that the Kharijites in al-Jazira gathered around him after Musawir. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Seizure and capture
Ibn Khaldun reports that he seized [[place/mosul|Mosul]] and spread corruption in al-Jazira and Azerbaijan. Al-Husayn ibn Hamdan pursued him on the order of [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]], captured him and brought him to the caliph. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar} Al-Mu'tadid then bestowed a robe of honour on al-Husayn and his brothers, and ordered the demolition of the fortress of Hamdan in Mardin. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
