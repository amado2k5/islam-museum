## Overview
Kamal al-Din Ibn al-Adim, a historian and companion, who came to Egypt as an envoy seeking help against the Tatars when they descended on Amid in 657 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 719-720}

## The effect of his arrival
Al-Suyuti says that [[person/qutuz|Qutuz]] gathered the amirs and notables after his arrival. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 719-720}
