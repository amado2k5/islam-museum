## Overview
A singer from among the singers of the days of al-Walid ibn Yazid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.201}

## At the gathering of al-Walid
Al-Mas'udi relates that al-Walid brought singers to him from the lands and sat with the diverting ones among them, and Ibn Muhriz was among them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.201}

His full name and the dates of his life need an additional source.
