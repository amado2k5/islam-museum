## Overview
Abd al-Razzaq, titled Jamal al-Din, ibn Ahmad, Kamal al-Din, ibn Abi al-Ghana'im Muhammad al-Kashi (also al-Kashani or al-Qashani), was according to al-Zirikli a Sufi, exegete and scholar. He gives his death as 730 AH, 1330 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 349}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names "Kashf al-Wujuh al-Ghurr", printed, a commentary on the Ta'iyya of [[person/ibn-al-farid|Ibn al-Farid]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 349}. He names "Istilahat al-Sufiyya", in manuscript, a film of which is in Damascus, also called "Lata'if al-I'lam fi isharat ahl al-afham" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 349}. He lists a printed commentary on "Manazil al-Sa'irin" of al-Harawi the Hanbali, "al-Siraj al-Wahhaj" on Qur'anic exegesis, and a printed commentary on "Fusus al-Hikam" of [[person/ibn-arabi-muhyi-al-din|Ibn Arabi]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 349}. He adds "Ta'wilat al-Qur'an", in manuscript, whose first part is in Riyadh (no. 2437) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 350}, a printed treatise on decree and destiny, and "Rashh al-Zulal fi sharh al-alfaz al-mutadawila bayna arbab al-adhwaq wa-l-ahwal" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 350}.
