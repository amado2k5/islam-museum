## Overview
Al-Tusayr ibn Daysam ibn Thawr, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the report of Khalid ibn al-Walid at Ayn al-Tamr. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] sent him to a water of the Banu Taghlib, and he attacked them at night, killing and taking captives. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.347}

The data hold no dates of his birth or death, which need an additional source.
