## Overview
Yahya ibn Ibrahim al-Guddali, chief of the Guddala of the Lamtuna. He set out on pilgrimage with leaders of his people in the year 440 AH, approximately, and on their return through Qayrawan they met Abu Imran al-Fasi, shaykh of the Maliki school. Yahya asked him to send with them some of his pupils, so that they could refer to him on their cases, according to Ibn Khaldun {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}.
