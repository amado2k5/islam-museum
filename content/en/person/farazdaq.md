## Overview
Hammam ibn Ghalib al-Farazdaq, a poet of the Umayyad period. Al-Baladhuri reports that he praised the river al-Mubarak, which Khalid ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri dug. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Muruj al-Dhahab
Al-Mas'udi reports that the king of the Romans wrote to al-Walid when he demolished the church, and al-Farazdaq answered him with a verse of the Qur'an. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.157} He also reports verses of his in elegy for Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.184}

The data ascribes to him a work, [[work/farazdaq-verses-on-al-aqra-ibn-habis|his verses on al-Aqra ibn Habis]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
