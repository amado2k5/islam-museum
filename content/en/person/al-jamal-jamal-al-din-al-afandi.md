## Overview
Abu al-Fuyudat Muhammad ibn Hamid al-Din ibn Mahmud ibn Muhammad ibn Jamal al-Din al-Afandi, a Sufi shaykh from a family of scholars. He followed the Khalwati order and was known as al-Afandi. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Death
The data of the encyclopaedia give his death as 899 AH (1494 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
