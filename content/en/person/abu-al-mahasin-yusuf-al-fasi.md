## Summary
Chief of the Shadhili-Jazuli order {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 262-263}.

## Steadiness and the wing
When the people of al-Qasr al-Kabir meant to flee to the mountains he steadied them, tireless in raising morale; he led one wing of the Moroccan army at [[event/battle-of-wadi-al-makhazin-1578|Wadi al-Makhazin]], held fast until victory, then abstained from all the booty {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 262-263}.
