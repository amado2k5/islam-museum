## Overview
Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan ordered that what Ubayd ibn Sharya used to narrate to him in his assembly, of the histories of the ancient kings of Yemen and others, be written down {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.168}.

The book reports that Mu'awiya was keen to know the histories of the earlier nations, in the context of the beginnings of codification {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.168}.
