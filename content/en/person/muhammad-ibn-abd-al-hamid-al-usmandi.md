## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan ibn Hamza al-Usmandi al-Samarqandi, Abu al-Fath, Ala al-Din, is described by al-Zirikli as a jurist, one of the great Hanafis. The entry gives his years as 488 to 552 AH, 1095 to 1157 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His life
He was from Samarqand, and his name points to Usmand, one of its villages. He was a disputant, one of the horsemen of theology, and travelled to Baghdad and debated its scholars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «Mukhtalaf al-riwaya», in manuscript, on jurisprudence; «al-Ta'liqa», in several volumes; «Badhl al-nazar», on the principles of jurisprudence; «Sharh manzumat al-khilafiyyat li-l-Nasafi», in manuscript in the municipal library; and «al-Hidaya», on the principles of belief. He also dictated a book on Qur'an exegesis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
