## Summary
Wahb ibn Masarra, Abu al-Hazm, was a Maliki jurist of al-Andalus, named after Wadi al-Hijara, a town there. He was a jurist, a hadith transmitter, a hafiz and an expert in the men who transmitted hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6457}

## Teachers and students
In Cordova he heard from Ibn Wadah, Ubayd Allah, Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Faradi, Ahmad ibn Khalid, Muhammad ibn Qasim and Qasim ibn Asbagh, among others. Abu Muhammad al-Qali'i and Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Ajuz transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6457}

## Standing
Ibn Farhun described him as a reliable and trustworthy imam, and in his lifetime people travelled to him for learning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6457}

## Work and death
His listed work is «The Sunna and the Affirmation of Qadar and the Vision». The book gives his death as 346 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6457}
