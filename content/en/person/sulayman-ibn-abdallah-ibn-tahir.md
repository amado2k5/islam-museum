## Overview
Sulayman ibn Abdallah ibn Tahir ibn al-Husayn, Abu Ayyub al-Khuza'i, belonged to the Tahirid family. Al-Safadi says he died in 266 AH, which converts to 879 CE (derived) {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 p. 242}.

## Office
Al-Safadi reports from al-Tabari that he was appointed to the police of Baghdad and the Sawad on behalf of al-Mu'tazz on the sixth of Rabi' al-awwal of the year 205, as the printed edition gives it {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 p. 242}.

## Letters and transmission
Al-Safadi describes him as a man of letters and a poet, and says al-Mubarrad, Abu Malik al-Darir and others narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 p. 242}. Among his verses are lines he wrote to a friend while ill, saying that each of his brothers bears the pain according to his capacity, and that he himself will carry what they cannot {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 p. 242}. Al-Akhfash Ali ibn Sulayman also narrated lines of his, in the munsarih metre, about a singing girl at whose song every limb of the listener wishes it were an ear {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 pp. 242-243}. Al-Safadi comments that the poetry is good {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 15 p. 243}.
