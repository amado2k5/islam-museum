## Overview
Abdallah ibn Hunayn ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Malik al-Kalbi al-Qurtubi, Abu Muhammad, known as Ibn Akhi Rabi' al-Sabbagh. One of the scholars of [[place/cordoba|Córdoba]], a client (mawla) of the Umayyads, who took his learning in al-Andalus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.393}

## Death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 318 AH (930 CE). The date comes from a single source and needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.393}
