## Overview
Al-Sibt al-Tarabulsi, grandson of the elder hafiz al-Silafi (d. 4 Shawwal 651); he came to Egypt, heard and gave ijazas there — and with him Egypt's lofty isnad finally broke, the last through whom it ran {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 260}.
