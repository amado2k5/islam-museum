## Summary
Al-Zayn al-Hafizi the physician; he treated al-Malik al-Hafiz of Ja'bar — hence the nisba — then served al-Malik al-Nasir Yusuf, who raised him high and sent him as envoy to the Tatars. He ingratiated himself with them and counselled them, so Hulagu employed him and he became a Tatar man, a traitor to the Muslims; God set his own master upon him and he was slain before him — he had been al-Malik al-Zahir's secretary — and with him were killed his kinsmen and intimates, fifty in all. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 535}
