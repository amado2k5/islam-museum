## Overview
Ma'ruf al-Karkhi, a link in the chain of Sufism that Ja'far al-Khuldi relates, as Ibn al-Nadim gives it. Al-Sarri took from him, and he took from Farqad al-Sanji, and Farqad took from al-Hasan al-Basri. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.260}
