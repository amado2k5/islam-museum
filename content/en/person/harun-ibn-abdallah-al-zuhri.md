## Overview
Abu Yahya al-Zuhri of Medina, of the line of 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf; he heard Malik b. Anas, 'Abd al-Rahman b. Zayd b. Aslam, Hadama al-Zuhri, 'Abd al-'Aziz al-Darawardi and 'Abd Allah b. Salama al-Zubayri, and Yahya b. Bukayr and others transmitted from him; his nasab connects to the house of al-Zuhra, the Banu 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.11 p.13}.
