## Overview
Zuhra ibn Hawiyya ibn Abd Allah ibn Qatada al-Tamimi, then al-Sa'di. He was in charge of the fodder of the Muslims on the day of al-Qadisiyya, and Persian horsemen met him, and that was the cause of the battle {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Ibn al-Kalbi said that he fought Galenus, and al-Baladhuri describes this report as the most firmly established {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
