## Overview
Amid al-Mulk al-Kunduri, the first vizier of the Seljuk state, according to Ibn al-Imad in "Shadharat al-Dhahab". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310-311}

## His zeal
Ibn al-Imad relates that he was extremely zealous against the Shafi'is. The sultan Alp Arslan permitted him to curse the Rafidis on the pulpits of Khurasan, and he ordered them cursed and added the Ash'aris to them, so that some imams of them, among them al-Qushayri and the Imam al-Haramayn, left Khurasan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310-311}

Then Alp Arslan sent him to the Khwarazm-shah to ask for his daughter in marriage for him. His opponents spread the word that he had asked for her for himself, so he shaved his beard. Ibn al-Imad mentions a report that the sultan had him castrated. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310-311}

The sultan dismissed him and moved him to Merv al-Rudh and imprisoned him. When he felt that he was to be killed, he brought out his shroud, bade farewell to his family and washed. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310-311}

The source of these reports is one, and the dates of the events are not recorded in the data, and need an additional source.
