## Summary
Khofifah Indar Parawansa is an Indonesian politician of the National Awakening Party (PKB) and, since February 2019, governor of East Java — the first woman to hold that office in the country's most populous province after Java's west — and a senior figure in Muslimat NU, the women's wing of Nahdlatul Ulama. {cite:wikipedia-khofifah-indar-parawansa}

## Life and formation
Born on 19 May 1965 in Surabaya into a family rooted in Nahdlatul Ulama — her father served as a local regent — she studied education and law at Surabaya institutions and rose through NU's women's organisation and student bodies into politics. {cite:wikipedia-khofifah-indar-parawansa}

## Career
Elected to parliament in the 1990s, Khofifah became state minister for women's empowerment in President Abdurrahman Wahid's cabinet (1999–2001), then served as a deputy speaker of the People's Consultative Assembly and led PKB in parliament. She twice ran for governor of East Java (2008, 2013) and lost narrowly — the 2013 defeat was contested before the Constitutional Court. Appointed Minister of Social Affairs in President Joko Widodo's cabinet in 2014, she resigned in January 2018 to run again, and won the 2018 election, taking office in February 2019; she was re-elected in November 2024 for a second term. In 2018 she was also elected general chair of Muslimat NU. {cite:wikipedia-khofifah-indar-parawansa}

## Legacy
Khofifah's career threads the Nahdlatul Ulama world's mass women's organisation into elected executive office at provincial scale — governing tens of millions in Indonesia's second-largest province by GDP contribution — making her one of the most prominent Muslim women in democratic politics anywhere. She has also been mentioned in commentary as a possible national figure. {cite:wikipedia-khofifah-indar-parawansa}
