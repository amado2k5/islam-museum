## Overview
Turayfa ibn Hajiza, brother of Mamman ibn Hajiza. al-Baladhuri reports that Abu Bakr wrote to him ordering him to fight al-Fujaa'a Jubayr ibn Iyas al-Sulami, who used to kill the Muslims and the apostates. He fought him, captured him, and sent him to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136}.

al-Baladhuri gives another report: that Abu Bakr wrote to Ma'n about the matter of al-Fujaa'a, and Ma'n sent his brother Turayfa, who captured him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136}.

This took place in the context of [[event/ridda-wars|the Ridda wars]].
