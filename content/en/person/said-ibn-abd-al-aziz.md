## Overview
Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Harith ibn al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As ibn Umayya, nicknamed "Hudhayfa". He was the son-in-law of [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] through his daughter {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The nickname
Al-Baladhuri reports that a dihqan saw him wearing a saffron-dyed garment with his hair combed, and said: "This is Hudhayfa", meaning "dihqana" (a feminine form of dihqan). The nickname stuck {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His governorship of Khurasan
Maslama appointed him over Khurasan, and he sent Surah ibn al-Hurr beyond the Oxus. He defeated the Turks at Ishtikhan, and they later defeated him a second time. He then appointed Nasr ibn Sayyar. The notables of Khurasan complained about him to Maslama, who dismissed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
