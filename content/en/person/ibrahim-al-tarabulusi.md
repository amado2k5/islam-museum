## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Musa ibn Abi Bakr, Burhan al-Din al-Tarabulusi, a Hanafi jurist. Born in Tripoli of the Levant, he studied at Damascus with several masters, then moved to Cairo and died there. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7508} His works include al-Isʿaf on the rules of endowments, Mawahib al-Rahman in the school of al-Nu'man, and its commentary called al-Burhan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7508}
