## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mujahid Asad al-Din Shirkuh b. Muhammad, lord of Homs, nephew of Asad al-Din Shirkuh, uncle by name of Salah al-Din's master; his were also al-Rahba, Palmyra and whatever was taken of the Khabur lands. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 184}

## His successor
He left a body of sons, and his son al-Malik al-Mansur Nasir al-Din Ibrahim took his place in the kingdom. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 184}
