## Summary
A Companion of Banu Muharib ibn Fihr; he was in the cavalry of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] on the day Mecca was conquered and strayed with [[person/khunays-ibn-khalid|Khunays ibn Khalid]] from the main road — they met the body of resisters at [[place/al-khandama|al-Khandama]], and he was martyred fighting for him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## His martyrdom
When Khalid's cavalry entered Mecca, Kurz and Khunays took a side road and met the resistance of Safwan and Ikrama at al-Khandama; Khunays was struck first, and Kurz stood defending him, chanting:
"A fair one of Banu Fihr knows — pure of face, pure of breast — today I will surely strike in defence of Abu Sakhr"
until he was killed a martyr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## For young visitors
A brave knight who lost his way with his companion on the day Mecca was entered; when the two were encircled he stood defending his wounded friend with utter loyalty until he was martyred.
