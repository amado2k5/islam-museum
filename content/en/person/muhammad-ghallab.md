## Summary
Muhammad Ghallab, professor of philosophy at al-Azhar University and author of studies in Oriental and Islamic philosophy and comparative literature; his book al-Islam kama Yarahu al-Urubiyyun presents orientalist views and discusses some of them; Muhammad Rajab al-Bayyumi's foreword says he was born at the start of the twentieth century, studied at al-Azhar and the Egyptian University, took his doctorate from France and taught at the faculty of usul al-din; among his works are a series on Oriental philosophy and books on the history of philosophy and literature. {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 11} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 12} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 20}
