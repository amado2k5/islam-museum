## Summary
Ebubekir Sifil is a Turkish hadith scholar, academic and writer, born on 25 December 1960 in Sarıkamış, Kars, and a faculty member at Yalova University's theology faculty. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

His YouTube channel counted 112,000 subscribers as of 16 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-ebubekir-sifil}

## Early life and education
Sifil was born on 25 December 1960 in Sarıkamış, Kars, completed his primary and middle schooling there, and attended Kars Imam Hatip High School. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

He began university in 1978 at the Kâzım Karabekir Education Institute in Erzurum and left of his own accord, then graduated in 1985 from the Journalism and Public Relations department of Gazi University in Ankara. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

He completed his master's in 1996 and his doctorate in 2006 in hadith studies at Selçuk University's Institute of Social Sciences in Konya; his thesis concerned Umar's understanding of the Sunna. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

He knows Arabic and English. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

## Academic career
He worked as a research assistant at Van Yüzüncü Yıl University's theology faculty (1993–1996) and at Eskişehir Osmangazi University's theology faculty (1998–1999), leaving both posts of his own accord. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

He currently serves as a faculty member at Yalova University's Faculty of Islamic Sciences; he received the associate-professor title in 2017, and the article records that he received the professor title in 2024. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}

## Works and journalism
Among the works the article lists are Modern İslam Düşüncesinin Tenkidi, Hz. Ömer ve Nebevi Sünnet, a concise commentary on the Tahawi creed, Modern Fetvalar Çağdaş Hurafeler and İslam ve Modern Çağ in three volumes, and he translated works including al-Kawthari's articles. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr} {cite:official-site-ebubekir-sifil}

He wrote a column in Millî Gazete from 2001 to 2013, then stopped writing for it over his disagreement with the newspaper's Syria policy, as he declared on various occasions, and wrote for Vahdet newspaper from December 2014 to May 2015. {cite:wikipedia-ebubekir-sifil-tr}
