## Summary
Muwaffaq al-Din Abu'l-Ma'ali Ahmad ibn Hibat Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Husayn ibn Abi'l-Hadid al-Mada'ini — also called al-Qasim — was the brother of 'Izz al-Din Ibn Abi'l-Hadid; both were jurists, accomplished men of letters, with fine poetry. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
He wrote *al-Washi al-marqum fi hall al-manzum* — for all its brevity, exceedingly fine and useful — and *al-Ma'ani al-mukhtara'a fi sina'at al-insha'*, likewise unsurpassed in its field; a large single-volume anthology of selected verse by Abu Tammam, al-Buhturi, Dik al-Jinn and al-Mutanabbi; and a diwan of letters in several volumes. He died at Baghdad shortly after the Tatars took it. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
