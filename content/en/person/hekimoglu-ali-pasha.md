## Summary
Ali Pasha, known as 'Hekimoğlu' — son of the physician — an Ottoman grand vizier {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Ali Paşa (Hekimzâde)'}.

Son of the palace chief physician Nuh Efendi, born on the night of mid-Sha'ban 1110/16 February 1699, 'the most excellent of his brothers'. Under Mahmud I he governed Anatolia, Tabriz and Diyarbekir, retaking Tabriz and Rumiyya; as governor of Bosnia in 1149/1736–37 he 'showed outstanding valour in the war'. He reached the grand vizierate and chronicles title him 'Şeyhü'l-vüzera' — sheikh of the viziers {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Ali Paşa (Hekimzâde)'}.
