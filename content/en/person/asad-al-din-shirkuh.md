## Overview
Asad al-Din Shirkuh, amir of Nur al-Din. He equipped him with two thousand horsemen for Egypt in 562 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-680}

## His two campaigns in Egypt
He fought the Egyptians and the Franks and was victorious despite the smallness of his army, and killed thousands of the Franks, and collected the land-tax of Upper Egypt. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-680}

He then came with his armies in 564 AH, and al-Adid appointed him vizier. He died sixty-five days later, and his nephew Salah al-Din succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-680}
