## Overview
Abu al-Khattab Nasr ibn Ahmad ibn Abdallah al-Gharbi al-Baghdadi al-Bazzaz, from Baghdad. He is attributed to the quarter of al-Gharaba and is known as al-Bazzaz (the cloth merchant). He was born in 398 AH (1007 CE) and died in 494 AH (1100 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
