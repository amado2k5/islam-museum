# Siraj Wahhaj

## Summary
Siraj Wahhaj is an African-American imam, born Jeffrey Kearse in Brooklyn, New York, on 11 March 1950, and raised in a Baptist family. He joined the Nation of Islam in 1969, taking the name Jeffrey12x, and after Elijah Muhammad's death in 1975 he became a Sunni Muslim as Warith Deen Mohammed reorganised the movement towards orthodox Islam; he was chosen to study at Umm al-Qura University in Mecca in 1978. {cite:wikipedia-siraj-wahhaj-en}

## Masjid At-Taqwa and national roles
In 1981 he founded the community that became Masjid At-Taqwa in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn, and remains its imam; in 1988 he led anti-drug patrols that were praised by the media and the New York Police Department. In 1991 he became the first Muslim to deliver an opening prayer in the United States House of Representatives. He is a former vice-president of the Islamic Society of North America and the leader of the Muslim Alliance in North America (MANA). {cite:wikipedia-siraj-wahhaj-en} {cite:influencewatch-siraj-wahhaj}
