## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Hakam, an Egyptian transmitter. Ibn al-Nadim in the Fihrist says that he transmitted from Malik the book "al-Sunna" in jurisprudence. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.281}

The information available here goes no further than that; his dates and the details of his life need an additional source.
