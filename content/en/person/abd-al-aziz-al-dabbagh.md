## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Mas'ud, Abu Faris, al-Dabbagh, was a Sufi of the Hasanid sharifs, born and died at Fez. He was born in 1095 AH, 1684 CE, and died in 1132 AH, 1720 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## What al-Zirikli says of him
He was unlettered and could neither read nor write, and his followers went far in praising him and in relating wonders about him. Ahmad ibn Mubarak al-Lamti composed "al-Ibriz min Kalam Sidi Abd al-Aziz", printed in two parts, on his character and the conversations that passed between them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
