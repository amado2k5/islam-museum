## Summary
Mehmet Okuyan is a Turkish Qur'an-commentary (tafsir) scholar, professor and writer, born in 1965 in Çaykara, Trabzon, whose whole education and career ran at Ondokuz Mayıs University's theology faculty, where he became professor in the tafsir department in 2008; he is known for his religious television programmes and his books on tafsir. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr} {cite:omu-avesis-mehmet-okuyan}

His YouTube channel counted 485,000 subscribers as of 15 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-mehmet-okuyan}

## Early life and education
Okuyan was born in 1965 in Çaykara, Trabzon, completed his primary schooling in Çaykara and his secondary schooling in the Hayrat district of Trabzon. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

He graduated from Ondokuz Mayıs University's theology faculty in 1987 and began work there as a research assistant in the tafsir department in 1988. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

He took his master's in 1990 with the thesis "Turkish Commentators of the First Half of the Twentieth Century", and his doctorate in 1994 with the thesis "Necmuddin Daye and His Sufi Tafsir". {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

He became assistant professor in 1994, associate professor in 2002 and professor in 2008, all in the same faculty. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

## Offices and work
At Ondokuz Mayıs University Okuyan served as a member of the faculty administrative board, as head of the Tafsir Method and History division, as deputy head of the Department of Basic Islamic Sciences, and as vice-dean of the faculty. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

His articles were published in the journals İlmî Araştırmalar and İslâmî Araştırmalar, and he wrote entries for the TDV Encyclopedia of Islam. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

He remains a faculty member in the faculty's tafsir department, as his current university profile records. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr} {cite:omu-avesis-mehmet-okuyan}

Okuyan prepares and presents religious programmes on various television channels, mainly in Ramadan. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}

## Works
Among the books the article lists are Necmuddin Daye ve Tasavvufî Tefsiri, Kur'an Verilerine Göre "Öteki"nin Konumu, Kur'an'da Vücuh ve Nezâir, Kısa Sûrelerin Tefsîri in five volumes, Kur'ân Sözlüğü and Kur'an Meal-Tefsir: Geniş Açıklamalı. {cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okuyan-tr}
