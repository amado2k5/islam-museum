## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Sarjis al-Muzani, a long-lived Companion who lived in Basra. He narrated from the Prophet, Umar, Abu Hurayra and others; Asim al-Ahwal, Qatada and Uthman ibn Hakim narrated from him. Al-Bukhari and Ibn Hibban mention him, and he transmitted seventeen reports from the Prophet, three of them in Muslim. Death given as "over eighty" H. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13361}
