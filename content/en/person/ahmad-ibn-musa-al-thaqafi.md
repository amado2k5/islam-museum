## Summary
Ahmad ibn Musa al-Thaqafi was a poet and man of letters who became patient and discerning. He turned away from the world after having been devoted to it, and turned toward the hereafter. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 490 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 138. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/138}

## Verses against the world
Abu al-Hasan al-Fihri recited to Abu Bakr al-Qurashi, who recited to Ahmad ibn Musa al-Thaqafi, verses against the world and those deceived by it. They describe a fool whom no prohibition restrains, who spends his day in play and amusement and does not know what comes after. The poet passes a king's palace and sees in it a warning: black slave-women wailing and breaking the instruments of pleasure. He tells the one who lives in such a house to know which house he is in and not to rest in it. Some words in the verses are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/138}
