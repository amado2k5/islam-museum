## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Qasim was an uncle of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and a son of [[person/al-qasim-ibn-ibrahim-al-rassi|al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim]]. In Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah's account to his son Ali, when the night prayer fell due at [[place/al-fur-medina|al-Fur']] al-Hadi asked his uncle Muhammad to go forward and lead them; the uncle excused himself, saying it was not permissible for him to go before al-Hadi, and al-Hadi said he had put the matter in his hands, so he led the night prayer. Afterwards he asked al-Hadi to ask forgiveness for him for having gone before him, saying that al-Hadi was the more entitled to go first, and al-Hadi prayed that God forgive him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37}

Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah says that this increased his desire for al-Hadi and his love for him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} The account is from the Zaydi narrator's side.
