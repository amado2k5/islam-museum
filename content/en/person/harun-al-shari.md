## Overview
Harun al-Shari, a person whom al-Tabari mentions in the events of the year 283 AH. He says that he was captured after [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] sent al-Husayn ibn Hamdan in pursuit of him. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.43}

He seems to be the person mentioned in [[person/harun-al-sari|Harun al-Sari]], but the data does not explicitly link the two pages, and this needs an additional source.
