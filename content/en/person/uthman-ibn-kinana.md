## Summary
A jurist of Medina who studied with Imam Malik and was known for reasoning by opinion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5140}

## His place with Malik
Malik gave him leave to enter first when people crowded at his door, calling him by name, and he sat at Malik's right hand without leaving him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5140} Ibn Bukayr said no one was more exact or more instructive than him in Malik's circle, and after Malik's death he took his seat in it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5140}
