## Overview
Qutba ibn Qatada al-Udhri, of Banu Udhra. He held command of the right wing in the army of Mu'ta. Ibn Hisham (edited by al-Saqqa) reports that he charged at Malik ibn Zafila, commander of the Arab allies of the Romans, and killed him, and sang of this in his poetry. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} See [[event/battle-of-mutah|the battle of Mu'ta]]. More sources are needed to confirm the details.
