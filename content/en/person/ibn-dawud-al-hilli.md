## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Dawud, Taqi al-Din, known as Ibn Dawud al-Hilli, was the author of a Kitab al-Rijal on the scholars of the Imamiyya. He died in 740 AH (1338 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.203}

Al-Zirikli calls his book unreliable for its many errors; the author closed it with a self-biography listing some thirty of his own works, of which al-Khwansari said they could obtain only one, called al-Jawhara. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.203}
