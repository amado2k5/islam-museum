## Overview
A poet. Al-Baladhuri relays from al-Mada'ini that she came to [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]], who gave her a gift, and she asked him to write for her to his governor in al-Rayy. When she reached Sawa, she died and was buried there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}

Al-Mas'udi says that al-Hajjaj never showed his boon companions a cheerfulness like the day Layla al-Akhyaliyya entered upon him, when she apologised to him as she passed the grave of Tawba ibn al-Humayyir. He liked her words and fulfilled her needs. Hammad the transmitter relates that she went down into his grave and died and was buried beside him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.131-132}

The two accounts differ on the place of her death, and the two sources are reported here without a preference.
