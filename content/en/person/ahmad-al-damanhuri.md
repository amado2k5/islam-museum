## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Mun'im ibn Yusuf ibn Siyam al-Damanhuri was shaykh of al-Azhar and one of the Egyptian scholars who wrote most, on jurisprudence and other subjects. He was born in 1101 AH, 1690 CE, and died in 1192 AH, 1778 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## His life
He was known as "al-Madhahibi" for his knowledge of the four schools. He was born in Damanhur, studied at al-Azhar and held its shaykhdom. He spoke the truth, the emirs stood in awe of him and kings sought him out. He died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names "Nihayat al-Ta'rif bi-Aqsam al-Hadith al-Da'if", in manuscript; "al-Fayd al-'Amim fi Ma'na al-Qur'an al-'Azim", in manuscript; "Idah al-Mubham min Ma'ani al-Sullam", printed, on logic; "Hilyat al-Lubb al-Masun bi-Sharh al-Jawhar al-Maknun", printed, on rhetoric; "Muntaha al-Iradat fi Tahqiq al-Isti'arat"; "Sabil al-Rashad ila Naf' al-'Ibad", printed, sermons; "al-Fath al-Rabbani bi-Mufradat Ibn Hanbal al-Shaybani", in manuscript; "'Ayn al-Hayat fi Istinbat al-Miyah", a treatise in manuscript; "al-Qawl al-Sarih fi 'Ilm al-Tashrih"; "Manhaj al-Suluk fi Nasihat al-Muluk"; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.
