## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Hadi al-Tattawi, Abu al-Hasan, Nur al-Din al-Sindi, was a Hanafi jurist learned in hadith, Qur'anic exegesis and Arabic. His family came from al-Sind and he was born there; he settled in Medina and stayed until he died. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1138 AH, 1726 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists his glosses: on the Sunan of Ibn Majah, printed; on the Sunan of Abu Dawud, in manuscript; on the Sahih of al-Bukhari, printed; on the Musnad of Imam Ahmad; on the Sahih of Muslim, in manuscript; on the Sunan of al-Nasa'i, printed; and on al-Baydawi; he adds that there are others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
