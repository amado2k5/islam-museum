## Summary

Wahbah al-Zuhayli (1932–2015) was a Syrian Shafi'i jurist and decades-long professor of Islamic jurisprudence and legal theory at the University of Damascus; his encyclopedia Al-Fiqh al-Islami wa Adillatuhu became a university reference across the Muslim world.{cite:wikipedia-wahbah-al-zuhayli}

## Life

Born in Deir Atiyah near Damascus, he studied at Damascus's Faculty of Sharia then at al-Azhar where he took his doctorate, and returned to teach, becoming professor and head of the jurisprudence department.{cite:wikipedia-wahbah-al-zuhayli}

## Works

Al-Fiqh al-Islami wa Adillatuhu (10 volumes) is a comparative fiqh encyclopedia presenting Sunni, Shia and Zahiri positions with their evidence. He also wrote the tafsir Al-Munir fi al-'Aqida wal-Shari'a wal-Manhaj, Usul al-Fiqh al-Islami and Al-Fiqh al-Hanbali al-Muyassar.{cite:wikipedia-wahbah-al-zuhayli}

## Standing

His fiqh is taught in sharia faculties across the Muslim world, noted for an organised style presenting the schools in balance. He died in 2015 leaving a reference library of jurisprudence.{cite:wikipedia-wahbah-al-zuhayli}
