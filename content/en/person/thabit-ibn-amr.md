## Summary

Thabit ibn Amr ibn Zayd ibn Adi ibn Sawad al-Ansari, of Banu al-Najjar. He fought at Badr and was killed at Uhud; Ibn Ishaq names him among the Badr veterans and among those killed at Uhud. Urwa's report makes him a man of Ashja' who became an ally of the Ansar and took their lineage, but Ibn Hajar argues that his line back to al-Najjar shows he was an Ansari by birth. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.179}
