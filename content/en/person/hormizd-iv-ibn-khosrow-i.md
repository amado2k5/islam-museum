## Overview
Hormizd ibn Khosrow Anushirwan, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Hormizd ibn Khosrow Anushirwan". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.172}

Al-Tabari, at this point, gives what is said about the years of his rule and his affairs. The details of his government need an additional source.
