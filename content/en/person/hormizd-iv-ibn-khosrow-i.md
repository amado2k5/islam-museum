## Overview
Hormizd ibn Khosrow Anushirwan, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Hormizd ibn Khosrow Anushirwan". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.172}

Al-Tabari, at this point, gives what is said about the years of his rule and his affairs. The details of his government need an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] says Hormizd son of Khusraw was the son of a daughter of the Turkish Khaqan, and that his father's designation of him was written and deposited with the chief priest. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 74} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 75} As an instance of his justice he relates that Hormizd fined his own son Parviz because his horse had damaged a crop. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 77} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 78} In the eleventh year of his reign enemies surrounded him: the Turkish shah reached Herat and the Roman emperor advanced on Nisibis. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 78}
