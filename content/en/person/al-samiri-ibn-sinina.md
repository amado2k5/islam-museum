## Overview
Abu 'Abdallah Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn 'Abdallah ibn al-Husayn al-Samiri, the Hanbali jurist and specialist in inheritance law known as Ibn Sinina; Ibn al-Najjar says he was born at Samarra in 535, heard Ibn al-Batti and Abu Hakim al-Nahrawani and others at Baghdad, studied fiqh under Abu Hakim and attached himself to him until he excelled in fiqh and inheritance, writing celebrated works — the Mustaw'ib in fiqh, the Furuq and the Bayan in inheritance law. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Offices and death
He held the qada' of Samarra and its district, then the qada' and hisba of Baghdad; dismissed from the qada' he kept the hisba, lost that too, supervised the Diwan al-Zimam and was dismissed from that as well — in his days of office he was styled Mu'azzam al-Din; dismissed, he kept to his house, then was allowed back to his town, and in his last years returned to Baghdad where he died on Tuesday night, 17 Rajab 616. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
