## Overview
Isma'il ibn Hammad, a judge. Al-Mas'udi says that he entered the presence of al-Mu'tadid wearing the black garb, and greeted him as caliph, so that he was the first to greet him so on the day al-Mu'tamid died. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.595}
