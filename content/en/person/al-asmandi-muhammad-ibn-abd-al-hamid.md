## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Hamid al-Asmandi, a scholar whose biographical entries are recorded in the books of biographies. The sources differ about his kunya and origins. He was born around 488 AH (1095 CE) and died around 553 AH (1158 CE). Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
