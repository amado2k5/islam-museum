## Summary
A virtuous Companion woman of Banu Sulaym, wife of the eminent Companion Uthman ibn Maz'un; she was present at the Muslim camp in the [[event/siege-of-taif|siege of Ta'if]], and she is the one who offered herself to the Prophet — verses of the Quran descended in her affair {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/127}.

## Her stand at Ta'if
Ibn Ishaq relates that Khuwayla bint Hakim was with the Muslims at the siege of Ta'if and said: "Messenger of God, if God opens Ta'if for you, give me the ornaments of Badiya bint Ghaylan ibn Salama, or the ornaments of al-Fari'a bint Aqil" — the two being among Thaqif's wealthiest women. The Prophet answered her gently, signifying that its conquest had not then been decreed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/127}.

## For young visitors
A righteous Companion among the earliest women to Islam, wife of Uthman ibn Maz'un; she accompanied the army in the siege of Ta'if and witnessed its events.
