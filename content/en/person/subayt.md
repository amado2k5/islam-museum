## Overview
Subayt, a landholder (dihqan) of Basra, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in Futuh al-Buldan. He had a thicket on the land that al-Mansur reclaimed for the Subaytiyya estate {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His story
The source reports that the agent withheld part of its price from him and beat him. He kept at the door of al-Mansur demanding what remained owed to him until he died. The estate was then named the Subaytiyya {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
