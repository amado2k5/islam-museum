## Summary
Izudin Alwy Ahmed is a Kenyan sheikh who teaches in Swahili. His YouTube channel (@izudinalwy), open since 31 August 2012, counted 193,000 subscribers by vidIQ as of 7 November 2026 — read on 8 November 2026 and matching ThoughtLeaders' FAQ figure of 193K from its 6 November 2026 crawl. {cite:vidiq-izudin-alwy-ahmed} {cite:thoughtleaders-izudin-alwy-ahmed} Kenya's Al Huda TV names him among the sheikhs of its "Mimbar ya Al Huda" programming. {cite:youtube-alhuda-tv-izudin}

## Platforms and work
The channel held 6,067 videos and 31,335,230 total views at the read, and ThoughtLeaders describes it as a Swahili channel on Islamic teachings and lifestyle publishing about four videos a week. {cite:vidiq-izudin-alwy-ahmed} {cite:thoughtleaders-izudin-alwy-ahmed} A recent video on his own channel, a Swahili lesson on what is done for the deceased before washing whose description reads "SHEIKH IZUDIN ALWY", was updated three days before the read, so the channel is still uploading. {cite:youtube-izudin-alwy-ahmed} Al Huda TV's programme videos list "Shk. Izudin Alwy Ahmed" in their keywords among the channel's sheikhs, on both "Mimbar ya Al Huda" and "Mawlid ya Mtume" programmes, alongside other Kenyan sheikhs. {cite:youtube-alhuda-tv-izudin}
