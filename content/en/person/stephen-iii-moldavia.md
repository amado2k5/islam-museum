## Summary
Stephen, lord of Moldavia (Bogdan) in the reign of [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 135}.

## The ambush in the woods
Al-Salabi recounts his ambush of Mehmed's army in the Balkans: hidden with his men behind dense woods, his cannon fire threw the Muslim ranks into near-disorder until the sultan cried "be God's soldiers," took up shield and sword and led the charge into the forest. Sword-fighting ran from morning to evening; the Moldavian troops were torn apart and Stephen fell from his horse, escaping only with difficulty {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 135}.
