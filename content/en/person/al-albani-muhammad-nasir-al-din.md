## Overview
Muhammad Nasir al-Din ibn Nuh ibn Adam Najati al-Albani, a well-known hadith scholar of Albanian origin and Syrian nationality. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His learning
He became known for authenticating and weakening hadiths, and for the jurisprudence of hadith. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 1333 AH (1914 CE) and died in 1420 AH (1999 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
