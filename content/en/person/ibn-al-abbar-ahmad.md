## Summary
Abu Ja'far Ahmad b. Muhammad al-Khawlani al-Andalusi of Seville, known as Ibn al-Abar; the famous poet, among the gifted poets of al-Mu'tadid b. 'Abbad al-Lakhmi, lord of Seville — a scholar and jurist who composed and left a poetry diwan; Ibn Bassam mentions him in the *Dhakhira*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
