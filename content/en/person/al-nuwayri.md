## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Ahmad ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Nuwayri, Shihab al-Din, saying he copied Sahih al-Bukhari eight times, collating each copy and selling it for a thousand, and compiled a large history in thirty volumes, a copy of which in his own hand he sold for two thousand dirhams. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/197} He was in favour with al-Malik al-Nasir, supervised the army office at Tripoli, and died on 21 Ramadan 733 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/197}
