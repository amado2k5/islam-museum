## Overview
Jarir ibn Atiya ibn al-Khatafi al-Tamimi al-Basri, a poet who praised the caliphs of the Umayyads. His poetry is preserved in writing. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 pp.40-41}

## With the caliphs
He praised Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and the caliph gave him a hundred she-camels. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 pp.40-41}

## Among the poets of his age
The book says that with al-Farazdaq and al-Akhtal he was among the most prominent poets of the Umayyad age, and that there was disagreement over which of them was the best poet. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.41}
