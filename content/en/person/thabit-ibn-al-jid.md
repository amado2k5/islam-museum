## Summary
A Badri Companion of the Ansar, of Banu Salama of [[group/khazraj|the Khazraj]]; he witnessed the second pledge of Aqaba, Badr, Uhud and the Trench, and won martyrdom at the [[event/siege-of-taif|siege of Ta'if]] under Thaqif's arrows {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/129}.

## His jihad and martyrdom
His name is Tha'laba, and he was known by the epithet "al-Jidh'" — the Trunk — for his steadfastness and courage like a palm's trunk. He was in the vanguard of Banu Salama's heroes in the battles of Badr, Uhud, the Trench and all the campaigns, until he was hit by a hail of arrows fighting at Ta'if's fortress and martyred in Shawwal of 8 AH {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/129}.

## For young visitors
A hero of Medina who witnessed all of Islam's early battles with the Prophet, and was martyred defending his faith at the walls of Ta'if.
