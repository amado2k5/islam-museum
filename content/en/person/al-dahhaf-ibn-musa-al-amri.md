## Overview
A man of Banu al-Harith who raided Yadr of Banu Mazin in Najran on the night of 15 Ramadan 294 AH. Wanted by Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, he was taken with companions at al-Muwaqqiha and jailed, as the book reports.

## Detail
The book says al-Dahhaf ibn Musa al-Amri of Banu al-Harith was sought by Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah after raiding Yadr of Banu Mazin. He escaped the force on the night of 15 Ramadan, and was taken with some companions at al-Muwaqqiha, a village of Najran, and jailed. The book gives no dates of birth or death and does not say what became of him after prison.
