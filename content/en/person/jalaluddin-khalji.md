## Summary

Jalal al-Din Firuz Khalji (c. 1220 - 19 July 1296) was the founder of the Khalji dynasty of Delhi {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. The English article gives his birth as circa 1220 {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. He ruled the Delhi Sultanate from 1290 until his assassination in 1296 by his nephew Alauddin Khalji {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. No prose text read names a legal school or an order for the sultan himself, so this record leaves his faith unattributed {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}.

## From regent to sultan

He had served the Mamluk dynasty as an officer and rose to become regent for the child sultan Kayumars {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. In 1290 he deposed Kayumars, took the regnal name Firuz Shah, and founded the Khalji dynasty {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. The Urdu article notes that he was about seventy years old at his accession {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-ur}.

## His reign

Contemporaries described him as mild-mannered and reluctant to shed blood, a temper that also let rebellions gather strength in his reign {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-ur}. He repelled a Mongol invasion, after which a body of Mongols who had accepted Islam settled near Delhi {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. He captured Mandawar and Jhain, while his attempt on Ranthambore failed {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. He appointed his nephew and son-in-law Alauddin governor of Kara in Awadh {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-ur}.

## Death

Alauddin's rich raid on Devagiri in 1296 raised the sultan's suspicions and his hopes at once {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. On 19 July 1296, at Kara, Alauddin had his uncle assassinated and took the throne {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-en}. The Arabic article converts the death to 4 Ramadan 694 AH / 18 July 1295, a year earlier than the English article and Wikidata give; that year is not followed in this record {cite:wikipedia-jalaluddin-khalji-ar}.
