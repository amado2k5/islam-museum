## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Muslim al-Bahili, brother of Qutayba. He was appointed over Khwarazm after the people of the kingdom killed their king, whom they had restored despite his weakness, according to al-Baladhuri {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
