## Overview
Mu'izz al-Din Abu al-Ma'ani Sa'd ibn Ali, known as Ibn Hadid, vizier of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah. Ibn al-Athir describes his conduct in the vizierate as good. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 361}

## His last days
Ibn al-Athir says that he kept to his house, and then died in Jumada al-Ula 610 AH. His coffin was carried to the shrine of the Commander of the Faithful Ali at Kufa. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 361}
