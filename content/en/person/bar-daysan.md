## Overview
Ibn Daysan, so called because he was born on a river called Daysan. Ibn al-Nadim places him among the Thanawiyya doctrine, and says that he appeared about thirty years after Marcion. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.458}

Among his books are "The Book of Light and Darkness", "The Book of the Spirituality of Truth", "The Book of the Moving and the Inanimate", and many other books. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.474}
