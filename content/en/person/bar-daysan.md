## Overview
Ibn Daysan (Bardaisan), so named because he was born on a river called Daysan; he appeared about thirty years after Marcion and is listed by Ibn al-Nadim among the dualist schools, with books and epistles to his name. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 458}
