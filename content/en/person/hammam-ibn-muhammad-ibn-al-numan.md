## Summary
Abu Amr Hammam ibn Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam, one of two brothers who inherited learning and devotion from their renowned forebears; the dominant traits in him were worship and care. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], volume 10, printed number 684, page 400, alongside his brother Abu Bakr and their father [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-numan-ibn-abd-al-salam|Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man]]. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## The brothers described together
Abu Nu'aym treats the two together: their standing in learning and asceticism is well known, and their merit among people is widely spread. Abu Amr was marked by devotion and care. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## A report from Aisha
The report from Aisha on refraining from sins as the way to outstrip the diligent is carried in the chain through his brother Abu Bakr, not through Hammam himself. The wording is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## A report from Abu Hurayra
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Qurazi relates from Hammam ibn Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man, from al-Abbas ibn Yazid ibn Fudayl, from Hammad ibn al-Qa'qa', from Abu Zur'a, from Abu Hurayra, a report of two phrases light on the tongue, heavy in the scale, and beloved to the Merciful. The wording is not given here. The father's name in the first link is garbled in the OCR ⁦(the text reads "ابن حمر")⁩; it is read here as Hamdan, not confirmed. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}
