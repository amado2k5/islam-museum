## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ali ibn Muhammad Abi al-Fahm al-Tanukhi, of the Tanukh tribe, and father of Abu Ali al-Muhsin ibn Ali al-Tanukhi. His father's name was Dawud ibn Ibrahim ibn Tamim. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi calls him Abu al-Qasim Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Dawud ibn Fahm al-Tanukhi al-Antaki. In 332 AH he was in Basra among the Buhayris, and he opposed Ibn Durayd in the latter's "Maqsura" ode. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.674}

## Dates
The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his birth as 278 AH and his death as 342 AH (about 891 and 953 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Notes for researchers
- The two sources differ on the name of his grandfather, and the choice needs an additional source.
