## Overview
Iskandar ibn Niqula ibn Sam'an al-Barudi, a physician, man of letters, journalist and educator from Lebanon. He worked in education and teaching, and in the judiciary. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born in 1856 CE (1273 AH) and died in 1921 CE (1340 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
