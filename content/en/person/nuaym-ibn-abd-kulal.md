## Overview
Nu'aym ibn Abd Kulal, one of the kings and crowned lords of Himyar. He wrote to the Prophet to declare his Islam when the Prophet returned from Tabuk {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235}.

## The letter of the kings of Himyar
He was among those named in the [[event/letter-of-himyar-kings|letter of the kings of Himyar]], to whom the Prophet wrote his letter {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235}. He belongs to [[group/himyar|Himyar]].
