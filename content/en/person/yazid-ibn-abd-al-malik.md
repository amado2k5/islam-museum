## Overview
The ninth Umayyad caliph, known by the kunya Abu Khalid, son of [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]]. He was born in the year 71 AH according to al-Suyuti, and in the year 72 AH, which is more probable, according to al-Salabi. He succeeded [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] by a bequest from his brother Sulayman, in Rajab of the year 101 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399-400} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.425-429}.

## His rule
al-Suyuti reports that when he took office he said: "Proceed in the manner of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz." He transmits from Ibn al-Majishun that he kept to this for forty days and then deviated {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399-400}. al-Salabi reports that he was unable to continue this course, that his policy returned to the course of those Umayyads who had preceded him, and that he quelled internal movements {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.433-436}.

In the year 102 AH [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] rose against him, and [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] defeated him at al-Aqr {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399-400}.

## His death
He died at the end of Sha'ban in the year 105 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399-400}. al-Mas'udi reports that he died at Ardad in the land of al-Balqa', aged thirty-seven, so that his rule was four years, one month and two days {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 185}. al-Mas'udi has another report on the cause of his death, linked with a slave-girl of his, and it has been said that her owner was Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 487-488}.

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 332–335}

- **Medina:** Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Fihri (101 AH, then dismissed); then Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd Allah al-Nasri (104 AH until he died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 332}
- **Mecca:** Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Asid (dismissed); then Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Dahhak ibn Qays (with Ta'if, 103 AH, then dismissed); then Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd Allah al-Nasri (with Ta'if, 104 AH until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 332}
- **Yemen:** Urwa ibn Muhammad (confirmed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 332}
- **Basra:** [[person/adi-ibn-artat|Adi ibn Artat]] (imprisoned by Yazid ibn al-Muhallab when he seized Basra in 101 AH); then Shabib al-Mazini, father of Isa ibn Shabib (agreed on by the Basrans after Yazid ibn al-Muhallab was killed in 102 AH); then Abd al-Rahman ibn Sulaym al-Kalbi (sent ahead by Maslama as a garrison commander); then Abd al-Malik ibn Bishr ibn Marwan (appointed by Maslama); then [[person/umar-ibn-hubayra|Umar ibn Hubayra al-Fazari]] (governor of Iraq, arrived 103 AH); then Sa'id ibn Amr al-Harashi (appointed by Ibn Hubayra); then Hassan ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Mas'ud al-Fazari (of Damascus); then Firas ibn Sumayy al-Fazari (husband of Umar ibn Hubayra's mother, until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 332} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Kufa:** [[person/abd-al-hamid-ibn-abd-al-rahman-ibn-zayd-ibn-al-khattab|Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn al-Khattab]] (confirmed, then dismissed by Maslama); then Muhammad ibn Amr ibn al-Walid ibn Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt (dismissed by Ibn Hubayra in 103 AH); then al-Sa'r ibn Abd Allah (of Murra Ghatafan, until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Khurasan:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Nu'aym al-Ghamidi; then Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Harith ibn al-Hakam (sent by Maslama, confirmed by Ibn Hubayra, then dismissed); then Sa'id ibn Amr al-Harashi (103 AH, then dismissed); then Muslim ibn Sa'id ibn Aslam ibn Zur'a al-Kilabi (104 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Sijistan:** al-Qa'qa' ibn Suwayd (of Banu Minqar; dismissed by Ibn Hubayra); then al-Sayyal ibn al-Mundhir ibn Awf ibn al-Nu'man {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Sind:** [[person/amr-ibn-muslim|Amr ibn Muslim]] (in office when Umar died); then Yazid ibn Wada' ibn Humayd al-Azdi (when Yazid ibn al-Muhallab seized Basra); then Hilal ibn Ahwaz (for Maslama, 102 AH); then Ubayd Allah ibn Ali al-Sulami (appointed by Ibn Hubayra in 103 AH, then dismissed); then Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd al-Rahman (of Murra Ghatafan, until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Bahrayn and Yamama:** Ibrahim ibn Arabi (restored) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Armenia:** Mu'allaq ibn Saffar al-Bahrani (of Homs; 103 AH, dismissed 104 AH); then [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 333}
- **Jazira:** Fayid ibn Muhammad al-Kindi; then al-Urs ibn Qays ibn Shu'ba ibn al-Arqam al-Kindi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Ifriqiya:** [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-muslim|Yazid ibn Abi Muslim]] (Dhu al-Qa'da 101 AH; killed there; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Bishr ibn Safwan (102 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Yahya ibn Ma'isa al-Kalbi (Bishr's deputy, 105 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Egypt:** [[person/bishr-ibn-safwan-al-kalbi|Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi]] (then appointed to Ifriqiya; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Judgeship of Basra:** al-Nadr ibn Anas ibn Malik (appointed by Abd al-Malik ibn Bishr); then Musa ibn Anas ibn Malik (appointed by Maslama in 102 AH); then Abd al-Malik ibn Ya'la (appointed by Ibn Hubayra in 103 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** al-Qasim ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud (confirmed, then dismissed in 103 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Kindi (in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Judgeship of Medina:** Maslama ibn Abd Allah ibn Salama al-Makhzumi (appointed by Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Dahhak; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Sa'd ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf (appointed by Abd al-Wahid al-Nasri, then dismissed); then Sa'id ibn Sulayman ibn Zayd ibn Thabit (until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 334}
- **Summer campaign:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Sulaym al-Kalbi (until Yazid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
- **Police:** Ka'b ibn Hamid al-Absi (until he died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
- **Land tax, army and chancery:** Salih ibn Jubayr al-Ghudani (then dismissed; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Usama ibn Zayd, a mawla of the people of Yemen (in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
- **Seal, storehouses and treasuries:** Mutayr, his mawla; then Usama ibn Zayd (over the seal, per Hatim ibn Muslim) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
- **Guard:** Ghaylan, son-in-law of Abu Ma'n; then Abu Malik al-Saksaki (per Hatim) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
- **Chamberlain:** Khalid, his mawla (written in the margin of the manuscript) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 335}
