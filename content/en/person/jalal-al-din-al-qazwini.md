## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Qazwini, Jalal al-Din Abu al-Ma'ali: born in 666, he lived in Anatolia with his father, later became preacher of the Umayyad Mosque, and was appointed by al-Nasir judge of Syria and then chief judge of Egypt in Jumada al-Akhira 727. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/3} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/4} His works include Talkhis al-Miftah. He died in mid-Jumada al-Ula 739. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/5}
