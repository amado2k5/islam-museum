## Overview
Mu'adha al-Adawiyya, a devout woman of the women of Basra. Her husband was Silat ibn Ashyam Abu al-Sahba' al-Adawi, who was martyred with Yazid ibn Ziyad at Janza in the battle of Kabul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
