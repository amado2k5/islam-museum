## Summary
Wife of Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As; she emigrated with him to Abyssinia and bore him there Sa'id and Ama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her wife of Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As, ascribing her to Khalf ibn As'ad, and says she bore him Sa'id and Ama in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Hisham said: "and it is said: Humayna bint Khalf" (pp. 3-4) — the name stands between Amina and Humayna.
