## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muslim al-Bahili, brother of [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]]. Qutayba sent him against Khurrazad, brother of the king of Khwarazm; he fought him and killed him, and took four thousand captives and killed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
