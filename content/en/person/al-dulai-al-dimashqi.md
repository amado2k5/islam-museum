## Overview
Diya' al-Din Abu'l-Qasim 'Abd al-Malik b. Zayd b. Yasin b. Zayd b. Qa'id b. Jamil al-Taghlibi al-Arqami al-Dula'i al-Shafi'i, khatib of Damascus's congregational mosque; a throng the like of which was not known attended his death — so dense that the shrouds were thrown onto him for the press of the crowd. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
