## Overview
A foreigner who came to Egypt, whom al-Suyuti mentions in the account of the ending of the Fatimid khutba. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 683}

## The khutba
When he saw Saladin and his amirs reluctant to end the Fatimid khutba, he said: "I will begin it." He mounted the pulpit before the preacher on the first Friday of Muharram 567 AH, and invoked blessings on al-Mustadi, and no one objected. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 683}

His name is not recorded in the data, which needs an additional source.
