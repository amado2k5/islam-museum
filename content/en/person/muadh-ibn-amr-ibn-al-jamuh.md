## Summary
Mu'adh ibn Amr ibn al-Jamuh al-Khazraji, one of the two Ansari youths who struck [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]] on the day of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## The blow on Abu Jahl
Ibn Ishaq relates from him that he heard the people saying "Abu al-Hakam cannot be reached", so he made that his affair; he charged him and struck a blow that sent his foot flying off with half his shin. Then his son [[person/ikrima-ibn-abi-jahl|Ikrama]] struck him on the shoulder and threw his hand off — it hung by a strip of his side's skin, and he fought that day dragging it until he stretched it free and cast it aside {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 221}. Then his brother [[person/muawwidh-ibn-afra|Mu'awwidh ibn Afra']] passed by Abu Jahl while he lay hamstrung and finished him; afterwards [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abdullah ibn Mas'ud]] took off his head {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 220-221}.

When the Prophet asked the two youths which of them had killed him and each said "I", he looked at the two swords and said: "you both killed him", and awarded Abu Jahl's spoil to Mu'adh since Mu'awwidh was killed in the same battle {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:mishkat-al-masabih}. Mu'adh remained until the time of Uthman ibn Affan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 221}.
