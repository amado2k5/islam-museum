## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Ansari al-Harawi, a scholar from Herat. The source describes him as a memoriser, historian, hadith scholar, Sufi and Qur'an commentator, a Hanbali, who traces his descent to Abu Ayyub al-Ansari. He was born around 396 AH (1005 CE) and died around 481 AH (1088 CE). Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.649}
