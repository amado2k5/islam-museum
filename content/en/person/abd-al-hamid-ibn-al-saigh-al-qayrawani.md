## Summary
A Maliki jurist of Qayrawan who studied with Abu Hafs al-Attar, Ibn Mahriz, Abu Ishaq al-Tunisi and Abu al-Tayyib al-Kindi, among others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5138}

## Students
Al-Mazari al-Mahdawi, Abu Ali Hassan al-Barbari, Abu al-Hasan al-Huwfi and Abu Bakr Ibn Atiyya were among those who studied with him. He is known for a commentary on the Mudawwana. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5138}
