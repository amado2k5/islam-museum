## Overview
Abu Nasr Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Ali b. Rustam al-Kalabadhi, exacting hafiz; one of the imams of hadith, trustworthy — Kalabadh is a quarter of Bukhara. Ibn Khallikan says the hafiz Abu Sa'd al-Sam'ani placed his birth-date after his death-date and corrupted it in several respects. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
