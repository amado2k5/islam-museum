## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Aban ibn Uthman ibn Affan al-Qurashi al-Umawi is called by al-Dhahabi one of those fit for the caliphate {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated a little from his father {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}. Umar ibn Sulayman, Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm and Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Taymi narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}.

## What is said of his piety
Musa al-Taymi said he had never seen anyone who combined religion, rank and honour as he did {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}. It is said he used to buy members of a household, clothe them and free them, saying he sought their help against the throes of death {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}. It is also said he was much given to worship and devotion, and that Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn Abbas, seeing him, admired his piety and bearing and followed his example in good {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}. Al-Dhahabi gives Tahdhib al-Tahdhib as his source {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}.

## His death
It is said he died asleep in prostration {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2161}. Al-Dhahabi gives no date for his death in this entry, so the card keeps the date it already carries, attributed to Tahdhib al-Kamal.
