## Overview
Ibn Hamdan, whom Ibn al-Nadim saw in Mosul. He was a propagandist (da'i) for the imams of the Banu Hammad, and wrote many books, among them *The Seventh Philosophy*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.269}
