## Summary
Taj al-Din Ibn Hamuwayh, sufi shaykh of shaykhs, shaykh of the Sumaysatiyya. Born at Damascus in 566, he heard from Shahda and the hafiz Abu'l-Qasim ibn 'Asakir; he entered the Maghreb before 600 and stayed six years. He has collected dictations and useful notes, and he composed history. He was clean-living, chaste, little-greedy, turning to none of God's creatures for the sake of the world; he was uncle of the sons of Shaykh al-Shuyukh Sadr al-Din al-Juwayni. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222}
