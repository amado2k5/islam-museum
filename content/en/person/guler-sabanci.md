## Summary
Güler Sabancı is a Turkish businesswoman who chaired Sabancı Holding — one of Turkey's two great family conglomerates — for nearly two decades and remains its best-known philanthropic face. For years she was regularly listed as the most powerful woman in Turkish business. {cite:wikipedia-guler-sabanci}

## The Sabancı group
Born in 1955 into the family founded by her grandfather Hacı Ömer Sabancı, she joined the group in the 1970s and worked up through its tire and rubber businesses before succeeding her uncle Sakıp Sabancı as chairperson in 2004. Under her chairmanship the holding — with interests in banking (Akbank), energy, cement, retail and industry — was restructured and professionalised, and she became the public voice of Turkish corporate modernisation. {cite:wikipedia-guler-sabanci}

## Philanthropy
The family's Sabancı Foundation, on whose board she sits, is among Turkey's largest private grant-makers, funding universities (Sabancı University was founded under her aegis), women's programmes and arts institutions. She has served on international councils and is frequently ranked by Forbes and Fortune among the world's most powerful women. {cite:muslim500-guler-sabanci}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as the matriarch of modern Turkish business: the woman who took a patriarchal conglomerate's chair and held it through financial crises and the country's economic transformation. {cite:muslim500-guler-sabanci}
