## Overview
[[person/wabisa-ibn-mabad|Wabisa ibn Ma'bad ibn Malik]] al-Asadi, of Asad ibn Khuzayma, known by the kunya Abu Salim. He was a Companion who settled in Kufa, then moved to al-Raqqa and remained there until he died {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1233}.

## Lineage and kunya
Ibn al-Athir reports that Abu Umar mentioned him with a lineage, and that Ibn Manda and Abu Nu'aym mentioned him with a longer lineage that rises to the Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1233}.

## His narration
He had companionship and narrated hadith from the Prophet. His two sons Amr and Salim narrated from him, as did al-Sha'bi and Ziyad ibn Abi al-Ja'd {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.1233}.

## For young visitors
Wabisa ibn Ma'bad was of the Banu Asad. He was a Companion of the Prophet, then moved from Kufa to al-Raqqa, where he died. His two sons and some of the Successors narrated from him.
