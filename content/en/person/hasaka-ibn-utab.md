## Overview
Hasaka ibn Utab al-Habati, a man of the Rashidun period. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that after the battle of the Camel he went out with Imran ibn al-Fudayl al-Barjumi, with a group of the vagabond Arabs, to Sistan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Sistan
They took money from Zalaq, seized the grandfather of al-Bukhtari, and entered Zaranj. Hasaka killed Abd al-Rahman ibn Juz al-Ta'i, the envoy of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] to them. Ali sent Rab'i ibn al-Kas with four thousand men, and they killed Hasaka. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
