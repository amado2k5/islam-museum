## Overview
Sulayman ibn Addu, a commander of the Almoravids. He succeeded [[person/abdallah-ibn-yasin|Abd Allah ibn Yasin]] in command after his death in the year 450 AH, in the war against the Barghawata {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

Ibn Khaldun reports that he uprooted them and erased the trace of their call in the Maghrib. He then died in the struggle against them in the year 451 AH {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
