## Overview
Aristotle, son of Nicomachus, who took from Plato and succeeded him. Ibn al-Nadim lists his pupils, and says that his books on logic, natural science and theology were translated into Arabic. They were interpreted by Alexander of Aphrodisias, Themistius, Yahya al-Nahwi, al-Farabi, al-Kindi and others. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.343-357}
