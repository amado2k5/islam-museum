## Overview
Al-Afshin, an Abbasid commander who fought Babak al-Khurrami. He is mentioned in the battle of his companions with Adhin, Babak's commander, in 222 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.670}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi relates that he met the king of the Romans in 223 AH, fought him and defeated him, and killed most of his patricians. He quotes him as saying that he was king, and that kings keep some of one another. Al-Mu'tasim crowned him with a jewelled golden crown. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.449-451}

He relates that al-Mazyar confessed before him at Samarra that he had sent him to rebel. He was arrested and died in prison. He was crucified at the Gate of the Commons and burned with idols that had been brought to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.449-451}
