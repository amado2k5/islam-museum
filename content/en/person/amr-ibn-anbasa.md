## Overview
Amr ibn Anbasa, a Companion among the early converts. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.575}

Some counted him the third to embrace Islam after [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]], which is one of the scholars' views on who was first to embrace Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.575}
