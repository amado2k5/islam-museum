## Overview
Abu al-Hayyaj al-Asadi, that is Amr ibn Malik ibn Ju'ada al-Asadi. Al-Baladhuri says that Sa'd appointed him to lay out the plots for the people on the day Kufa was founded as a garrison city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
