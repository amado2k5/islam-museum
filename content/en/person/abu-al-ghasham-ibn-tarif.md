## Overview
He is Abu al-Ghasham ibn Tarif, who was in the prison of [[place/zahr-yemen|Zahr]] with a group of Al Ya'fur after [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] had scattered the prisoners among Sana'a, Zahr and Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.216} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. When Ibn Abi Abbad was killed and the men of the reinforcement shouted for arms at Zahr and its governor fled, they freed him and the men of Al Ya'fur with him, and they stayed at Zahr {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. The text writes his name Abu al-Ghasham on p. 216 and Abu al-Ghasham ibn Tarif on p. 219, and the museum reads one man. See [[event/the-men-of-zahr-break-the-prison-and-al-hadi-warns-ali-ibn-sulayman-288]].
