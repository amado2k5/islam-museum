## Summary
Shihab al-Din al-Milkawi, the most learned of Damascus's qadis and one of its esteemed imam-scholars. Al-Zuhri said: no one in the city took the sciences in their proper form except him; he was sought for fatwas from every region. He leaned strongly toward Ibn Taymiyya and held many of his positions sound; there was sharpness in him and he had a squeamishness toward people. He came away from Timur's calamity ailing and weak of body, and died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 32}
