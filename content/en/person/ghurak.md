## Overview
Ghurak, king of Sughd. He sought help from the king of Tarabanda against Qutayba, and the king of Shash was defeated, and Qutayba struck them and broke them. Ghurak then made a treaty with [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] for two thousand two hundred thousand dirhams each year, and that he would pray in Medina. He prepared a meal for him; Qutayba ate and prayed, and he built a mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
