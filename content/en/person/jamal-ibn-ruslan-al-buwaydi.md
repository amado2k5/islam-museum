## Summary

Jamal al-Din Abd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Ruslan al-Buwaydi, from the village of al-Buwayda in the district of Damascus, later Damascene, a Shafii. He was born in the year 851. He was a close companion of Shaykh Taqi al-Din al-Baltansi in his studies with their shaykhs. Shaykh Musa al-Kannawi read al-Bukhari with him, among other works {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560}.

He died at the Nuri hospital on a Thursday, around the sixth or seventh of Dhu al-Qada in the year 923. His companion al-Baltansi led the funeral prayer over him, and he was buried in the cemetery of Bab al-Saghir beside Shaykh Hammad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560}.
