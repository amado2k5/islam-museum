## Summary
Akib Ahmed (আকিব আহমেদ) is a Bangladeshi actor, director and writer, and the primary figure behind Akib Islamic TV, a channel publishing Islamic moral short drama — the form known in Bangladesh as natok. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} {cite:thoughtleaders-akib-islamic-tv} His channel's About text presents him as a content creator and influencer, and his videos' credits show him writing, starring in and most often directing his own productions. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} His main channel (@akibislamictv) stood at 5,380,000 subscribers and 2,145,289,055 views across 3,500 videos as counted on 8 November 2026 — dated platform figures, not facts the museum measures itself. {cite:vidiq-akib-islamic-tv}

## Career and work
His main channel started on 9 December 2019, has its country set to Bangladesh, and gained 90,000 subscribers in the four weeks to 8 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-akib-islamic-tv} His productions are credited to Creative Media, and the credits of his 2025–2026 videos repeat a pattern: on "Vikkhar Babsa", "Chacha M.P." and "Onnayer Poton" he is named Cast, Director and Script Writer / Playwright, while on other titles such as "Kormocharir Koshto" and "Jora Kobor" another man directs and he remains script writer and cast — the credits vary by title, and no single title should be generalised. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} The ThoughtLeaders platform describes his channel as a Bengali-language channel hosted by Akib Ahmed, focusing on entertainment with Islamic and societal themes, its library including dramas, thrillers and religious-themed productions. {cite:thoughtleaders-akib-islamic-tv} He remains active: his most recent uploads read on 9 November 2026 date from about two weeks earlier and gathered about 2.3 million views. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv}

## Platforms and audience
His main channel's most-viewed uploads are natok titles — "Lalosha" at about 14.5 million views, a 2024 short drama at about 13.2 million and "Ayna" at about 10.4 million — and vidIQ ranks the channel #71 in Bangladesh. {cite:vidiq-akib-islamic-tv} His second, older channel (@akibahmed) started on 13 November 2017 and stood at 5,130,000 subscribers and 665,750,466 views across 243 videos, its subscriber count unchanged in the four weeks to 8 November 2026; its most-viewed uploads mix cycle-race challenge videos with natok titles also carried on the main channel. {cite:vidiq-akib-ahmed} His videos' descriptions pin his own social links — Instagram @akib_ahemed and a Facebook Page named "Stunt Akib Ahemed" — but no dated follower count for either could be read, so no figure is carried for them here. {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv} This record is explicit about its limits: no press coverage, no Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item could be found for him, so it rests on his channels plus the ThoughtLeaders profile; no source read gives a birth date, birthplace or education; and no text read attests a school of law for the man himself, so his faith is recorded as unknown. {cite:thoughtleaders-akib-islamic-tv} {cite:youtube-akib-islamic-tv}
