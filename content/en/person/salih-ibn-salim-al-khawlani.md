## Summary
Salih ibn Salim al-Khawlani, a freedman of his tribe, known as Abu Muhammad, a Maliki scholar who knew the fiqh by heart. He studied under al-Shafi'i and then turned to the Maliki school, and he transmitted from Ibn Wahb and al-Shafi'i. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1630}
