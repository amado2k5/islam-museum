## Summary
A commander whose exploits shone in Algiers' defence against Charles V's 948/1541 expedition {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 225}.

## In the battle
Al-Salabi names him among those whose supreme courage and rare gallantry reaped the attackers, listing wise leadership and the tribesmen flooding to the field among the causes of the emperor's defeat {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 225}.
