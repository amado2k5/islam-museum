## Summary
'Izz al-Din al-Maqdisi, the Hanbali muhaddith; he attended Abu Hafs Ibn Tabarzad, heard from al-Kindi and his generation, travelled to Baghdad and heard from al-Fath ibn 'Abd al-Salam and others, then on to Egypt; he wrote much, devoted himself to hadith and took fiqh from Shaykh al-Muwaffaq — accomplished, righteous, reliable, benefiting many; he died mid-Dhu'l-Hijja and was buried at the foot of Qasyun. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 530}
