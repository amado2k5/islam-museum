## Summary
Lala Şahin Pasha, Murad I's commander — in al-Sallabi's telling he led the Ottoman force at the Maritsa, striking the Balkan coalition's camp in the dark before dawn with a small body of men and destroying an army of near a hundred thousand, opening the Balkan road {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 58-59}.
