## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Samad ibn Bashir, Abu al-Tahir al-Tanukhi al-Mahdawi, a hadith scholar, philologist and Maliki jurist, one of the leading figures of the school who moved from following tradition to independent choice and weighing of opinions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14951} He studied under Abu al-Hasan al-Lakhmi, al-Suyuri and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14951} His works include al-Anwar al-badi'a ila asrar al-shari'a, al-Tanbih, al-Tadhhib 'ala al-Tahdhib and an abridgement, which he said he completed in 526 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14951} The scan notes he was alive in 526 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14951}
