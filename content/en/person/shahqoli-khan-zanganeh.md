## Summary

Shahqoli Khan Zanganeh (died 1716) was a Kurdish nobleman of the Zanganeh tribe who served as grand vizier to the Safavid shah Soltan Hoseyn from 1707 until his death. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} He was the third son of the vizier Shaykh Ali Khan Zanganeh, who had held the grand vizierate a generation before him. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} No article read gives him a birth year — the Persian article prints a question mark in its place — and the Wikidata item's birth value of 1650 is recorded at century precision rather than year precision. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-fa} {cite:wikidata-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh}

## Early offices

Shahqoli began his public career as governor of Kermanshah in the 1680s. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} In 1690 Saru Khan Sahandlu, the qurchi-bashi (head of the royal bodyguard), killed forty men of the Zanganeh tribe, and Shahqoli protested against the killings. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} Saru Khan was executed in 1691, and Shahqoli succeeded him as qurchi-bashi. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en}

## The grand vizierate

Shahqoli became grand vizier in 1707 and held the office until his death in 1716. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-ar} On his death he was succeeded by his son-in-law Fath-Ali Khan Daghestani. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-ar} His son, named Shaykh Ali Khan like his grandfather, afterwards held high office in the state. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} The English and Persian articles describe the Zanganeh as a Shia Kurdish tribe, which is the background of his family and tribe rather than a school named for the man himself. {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahqoli-khan-zanganeh-fa}
