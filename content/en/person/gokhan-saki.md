# Gökhan Saki

## Summary

Gökhan Saki is a Turkish-Dutch kickboxer and mixed martial artist, born in Schiedam on 18 October 1983 to Turkish parents from Kars. Nicknamed "The Rebel", he won the K-1 World Grand Prix 2008 tournament in Hawaii, became the inaugural Glory Light Heavyweight Champion in Istanbul in 2014, and later fought twice in the UFC. {cite:wikipedia-gokhan-saki-en} {cite:wikidata-gokhan-saki}

## Career

Saki began kickboxing at ten and gave up football for it at sixteen. Under Jan Pasztjerik he became Dutch, European and World Muay Thai champion within three years, then moved to Golden Glory. He reached the K-1 World Grand Prix tournament final in Amsterdam in 2006 and won the Hawaii tournament in 2008. At Glory 15 in Istanbul on 12 April 2014 he beat Nathan Corbett and then Tyrone Spong — whose leg fractured as Saki checked a kick — to become Glory's first light heavyweight champion. He signed with the UFC in 2017, knocked out Henrique da Silva on his debut, and lost to Khalil Rountree in 2018. The Turkish article records that he competes for Turkey. {cite:wikipedia-gokhan-saki-en} {cite:wikipedia-gokhan-saki-tr}
