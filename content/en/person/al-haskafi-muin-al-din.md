## Overview
Mu'in al-Din al-Haskafi Yahya b. Salama al-Shafi'i, known as al-Khatib, of the strong citadel of Hisn Kayfa; raised at Hisn Kayfa, he came to Baghdad and read fiqh until he excelled, read adab under al-Khatib Abu Zakariyya al-Tibrizi, commentator of the Maqamat, then returned home, settled at Mayyafariqin, held its khitaba and sat for fatwa and study — people profited from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 648-649}

## Praise and death
Al-'Imad says in the Kharida: he was the age's sign in knowledge and the era's wonder in prose and verse; Ibn Shahba said: he has rare tarsi', precious tajnis, mighty-tender wording and easy-deep meaning. He died in 551; he leaned to Shi'ism. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 648-649}
