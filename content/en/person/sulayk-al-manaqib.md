## Overview
Sulayk al-Manaqib, one of the slaves and brave men of the Arabs. al-Mas'udi mentions him in Muruj al-Dhahab {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.621}.

## His mention
Amr ibn Ma'd Yakrib counted him before Umar among those whom one would not fear to meet, together with Antara al-Absi, and said that they were "the two slaves": Antara al-Absi and Sulayk al-Manaqib {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.621}.
