## Overview
Abu 'Ata' Marzuq al-Sindi the poet — also called Abu'l-'Anas, some say he was al-Hasan — a mawla of Asad b. Khuzayma; his tongue carried an accent, turning jim to zay and shin to sin, and Hammad al-Rawiya, Hammad 'Ajrad, Hammad b. al-Zibriqan and Bakr b. Mus'ab al-Muzani told how they invited him one night and contrived to make him say "jarada, zajj, shaytan" to expose his lisp; he inclined to Shi'ism and traded satires with al-Buhturi. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
