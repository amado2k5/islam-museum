## Overview
Abu al-Bashar, the father of humankind. Al-Bukhari relates that God created him, that he was sixty cubits tall, and that God ordered him to greet the angels. What he heard of their greeting was his own greeting and the greeting of his descendants. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.3326}

In the hadith of the Night Journey, the Prophet saw him in the lowest heaven, and said to him: "Welcome, son and prophet." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.3207}

The rest of his story is not set out here and needs an additional source.
