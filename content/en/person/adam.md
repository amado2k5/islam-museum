## Overview
Abu al-Bashar, the father of humankind. Al-Bukhari relates that God created him, that he was sixty cubits tall, and that God ordered him to greet the angels. What he heard of their greeting was his own greeting and the greeting of his descendants. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari-bugha 3148}

In the hadith of the Night Journey, the Prophet saw him in the lowest heaven, and said to him: "Welcome, son and prophet." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari-bugha 3035}

The rest of his story is not set out here and needs an additional source.

## For Sprouts
Adam was the first human created by God, who taught him all the names and placed him in the Garden, and he is the father of all humanity.

## For Explorers
The Qur'an relates that Adam was the first human created by God from clay, honored by divine breath and endowed with the knowledge of all names. Placed with his spouse in the Garden before dwelling on Earth to cultivate it with goodness, he was the first prophet and the father of humankind, honored with moral agency and stewardship.

## For Scholars
The father of humanity and the first prophet in Islamic theology. The Qur'an chronicles his creation from clay and dust, the breathing of life into him, the prostration of angels in honor of divine command, and his instruction in the names of all things, signifying the primacy of knowledge in the human calling of stewardship (khilafa). Residing with his spouse in the Garden, they descended to Earth following their repentance, which God accepted. Reflected throughout the Qur'an and canonical Hadith corpora, Adam represents the ontological unity of the human race and the genesis of moral agency grounded in divine guidance.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)
The Sheikh says that Adam repented in Paradise and came down to earth seeking forgiveness, cleansed of his sin; he did not descend with his sin, but after God had forgiven him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:43:30}. He holds that the Paradise Adam lived in, even if it was a garden in this world on his account, is one a person will return to if he has planted good in it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:43:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
Omar Abdelkafy says that Adam was forbidden a single tree among very many, that the devil whispered to him and Eve that it was the tree of eternity, that they ate of it and were sent out of the Garden, and that God then turned to him and chose him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:06:00}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
**Created upon the form.** In episode 6 [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|al-Sha'rawi]] says the pronoun to which the hadith's mention of the form refers may point to God or to Adam. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:19:30} If it refers to God, it means for him that Adam's limbs answer the will of his self without intermediary, as the universe submits to God when He wills, not the reality of the divine essence. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:20:30} If it refers to Adam, it means he was not created an infant who then grew but a complete man of sixty cubits, because God made him with His hands and not by the word "Be". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:21:00} He holds that stature began to decrease once secondary causes, marriage and procreation, came in. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:21:00}

**The greeting to the angels.** He says Adam was told to greet a particular group of angels, not all of them, because among the angels are "the exalted" ('alin), enraptured ones who know only God and were not included in the command to bow. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:22:30} He holds that the angels Adam greeted are those connected with the human being, the guardians and those who manage affairs, and that the greeting sets between server and served a bond in which neither looks down on the other. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:24:30} He says the mission of the religions is to establish peace within the self, with people, with the universe and with one's Lord. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:26:00} He holds that people enter the Garden in Adam's form so that they enjoy it with the original gift with which they were created. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep06 00:29:30}

**The garden of trial.** In episode 27 he says that the garden Adam lived in was not the Garden of eternity, which has no exit and which Satan does not enter, but a period of training in which slips were not punished, with only one prohibition, a tree. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep27 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep27 00:23:30} He holds that Adam was not yet a prophet in that period, and that his being chosen came after it. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep27 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep27 00:28:30} This is his view, close to what is described under [[concept/jannat-al-tajriba|the garden of trial]].
