## Summary
Shaykh Zayn al-Din Abu'l-Husayn Yahya ibn 'Abd al-Mu'ti ibn 'Abd al-Nur al-Zawawi, of the great Zawawa tribe of Ifriqiya; Hanafi jurist and grammarian, born 564, who taught Arabic for a time at Egypt and Damascus and transmitted from al-Qasim ibn 'Asakir and others; the most eminent of al-Jazuli's pupils, singular in Arabic, author of the famous Alfiyya and more; he died at Egypt in Dhu'l-Qa'da 628 — his grave near Imam al-Shafi'i's tomb. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 129}
