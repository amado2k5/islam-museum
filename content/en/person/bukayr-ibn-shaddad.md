## Overview
Bukayr ibn Shaddad ibn Amir of the Banu Kinana, a horseman of Atlal, which was also the name of his horse. He was with Sa'id ibn al-As in [[event/conquest-of-azerbaijan|the raid on Azerbaijan]]. In it al-Shammakh ibn Dirar said a verse: "Bukayr of the Banu al-Shaddakh, a horseman of Atlal." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri relates that in the caliphate of Umar he heard a Jew reciting verses and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
