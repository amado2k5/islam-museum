## Overview
Abu al-Fath Mahmud ibn al-Husayn al-Sindi ibn Shahak the secretary, known as Kushajim, a man of learning, transmission and letters. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.680}

## His verses
He wrote to a friend verses condemning backgammon, for which he was well known. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.680} Al-Mas'udi relates that at the table of al-Mustakfi he recited some of his poetry describing food, among it asparagus, *al-juzabiyya* and *qatayif*. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.714-717}
