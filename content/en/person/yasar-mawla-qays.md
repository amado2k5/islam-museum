## Overview
Yasar, grandfather of Muhammad ibn Ishaq, the author of the biography. He was a client of Qays ibn Makhrama ibn al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf, according to al-Baladhuri {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 345}.

## The report
Yasar was among the captives of the church of Ayn al-Tamr {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 345}. He is linked with [[event/siege-of-ayn-al-tamr|the siege of Ayn al-Tamr]].
