## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah ibn Hatim al-Harawi, Abu Ishaq, was a hadith hafiz who settled in Baghdad; al-Safadi gives his entry the heading "al-Hafiz al-Harawi" {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 22}.

## Life
Al-Safadi says that al-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, Ibn Abi al-Dunya and a group of others narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 22}. He describes him as upright, ascetic and abstinent, fasting continually unless someone invited him, in which case he broke his fast {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 22}. He died in the month of Ramadan in 244 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 22}, which corresponds to 858 CE by the museum's conversion (derived).
