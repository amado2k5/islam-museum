## Ishaq ibn Dawud, king of Abyssinia

Ishaq ibn Dawud ibn Sayf Ar'ad al-Hashi, king of Abyssinia. He died in Dhu al-Qaʿda 833 AH, after a reign of twenty-one years. His son Andraws was set up after him and ruled for four months and then died. Then his paternal uncle Kharnabay was set up and ruled briefly. After him a small child was installed, until he died in the plague of 839 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 201}.
