## Overview
Abd Allah Pasha ibn Ibrahim al-Husayni al-Jarmaki al-Shatji was an Ottoman governor with a knowledge of Qur'anic exegesis. Al-Zirikli gives his dates as 1115 to 1174 AH, that is 1703 to 1761 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}.

## Life
He was born in Jarmak, in the district of Diyarbakir. He gained learning in Arabic and composed Anhar al-Jinan fi Yanabi' Ayat al-Qur'an, which is printed. He moved through the great provinces and was at Edirne, Van, Diyarbakir and elsewhere {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}.

## Offices and campaigns
Al-Zirikli says he took part in the war against Nadir Shah and the siege of Belgrade, and that he held the grand vizierate; the last posts he held were Aleppo and then Damascus, in 1172 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}. He performed the pilgrimage and fought the Harb tribes between the two Holy Sanctuaries, killing their shaykh. Sayyid Ja'far al-Barzanji wrote a book on this, titled al-Nafh al-Farji fi al-Fath al-Jatha ji (spelled as printed), in manuscript in the Zahiriyya library, no. 8724 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}. Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Wakil, who was in his service, wrote Tarwih al-Qalb al-Shaji fi Ma'athir Abd Allah Pasha al-Shatji, in manuscript in the public library in Vienna, no. 1196. Dr. Izzat, editor of Hawadith Dimashq, saw it, and it says he was a man of presence and dignity who honoured writers and poets {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}.

## His writings
Al-Zirikli records a treatise by him on the Mi'raj and another on prosody, and mentions some of his poetry {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}.

## Last years
His term in Damascus was short. He was transferred to Diyarbakir after being dismissed, then it was rumoured that he had been killed, and the state seized his property {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 64}.
