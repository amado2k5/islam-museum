As'ad al-Shuqairi (1860–1940) was a Palestinian scholar and politician from Acre who trained at al-Azhar and rose through the Ottoman sharia judiciary.{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-ar}{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-en} Elected for Acre to the Ottoman parliament in 1908 and 1912, he was appointed qadi of Acre in 1914 and mufti of the Fourth Ottoman Army during the First World War.{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-en}

A committed Ottomanist, al-Shuqairi opposed Arab separatism before the war and, under the Mandate, led the Palestinian opposition to the Supreme Muslim Council of Amin al-Husayni.{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-ar}{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-en} He died at Acre in February 1940; his son Ahmad Shukeiri later became the first chairman of the Palestine Liberation Organization.{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-ar}{cite:wikipedia-asad-al-shuqairi-en}
