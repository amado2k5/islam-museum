## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ahmad, known as Ibn Sida al-Mursi, a jurist and imam in language and grammar. Among his best-known books is *al-Muhkam* (The Firm). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.31}

## At the court of Denia
Al-Maqqari relays from *al-Mutmah* that he devoted himself to al-Muwaffaq, lord of Denia. There he found the leisure for learning and wrote *al-Muhkam*, which al-Maqqari describes as the most original and the most sound of books. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.31} See [[work/al-muhkam-ibn-sida|al-Muhkam]].

## After al-Muwaffaq's death
When al-Muwaffaq died, Ibn Sida feared his son Iqbal al-Dawla and fled to one of the neighbouring districts. From there he wrote to him a poem asking for his favour, beginning: "Is there any way to kiss your right hand?" {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.31}
