## Overview
Umm Habib al-Sahba bint Habib ibn Bujayr al-Taghlabiyya, who was the mother of Umar ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-153}.

## The captivity
She was among the captives of the Banu Taghlib, who had turned against obedience under the leadership of Rabi'a ibn Bujayr at al-Mudayq and al-Musayyad, according to al-Baladhuri. Khalid ibn al-Walid defeated them on his way to Syria, and sent the captives to Abu Bakr {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-153}. See [[event/khalid-march-to-syria|Khalid's march to Syria]].
