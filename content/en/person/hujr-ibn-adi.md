## Overview
Hujr ibn Adi, killed in 51 AH with his companions. In al-Mas'udi's account, Ibn Muljam passed al-Ash'ath in the mosque reciting, and Hujr said 'You have killed him, one-eyed — may God kill you', and so it was heard. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
