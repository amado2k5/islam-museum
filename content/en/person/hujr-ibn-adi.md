## Overview
Hujr ibn Adi al-Kindi, who was killed in 51 AH together with his companions, as al-Tabari mentions. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.253}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi reports that Hujr heard Ibn Muljam pass by al-Ash'ath while he was in the mosque, and Hujr spoke harshly to him, and then what he had heard came to pass. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.702}

An additional source is needed: the data does not detail the cause of his killing or the fate of his companions.
