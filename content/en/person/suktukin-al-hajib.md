## Overview
Suktukin al-Hajib, chamberlain of Mu'izz al-Dawla. In the year 340 AH he sent him with a huge army of Turks, Daylamites and Arabs to repel the armies of Khurasan from the regions of Iraq {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.365}.
