## Summary
Al-Zawzani, shaykh of the Sufis of Baghdad; born in 366 to an elderly mother, he kept company with Abu'l-Hasan al-Husri and transmitted from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, becoming shaykh of the Sufis — the ribat facing the Mansur mosque was named for him, though it had been built for al-Husri and al-Zawzani his companion. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.555}

## His saying and death
He used to say: 'I kept company with a thousand shaykhs, al-Husri first among them; from every shaykh I preserve one saying.' He died in Ramadan 451 and was buried in the ribat. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.555}
