## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan al-Zawzani, a makhira whose son Abu'l-Hasan al-Husri was a Zoroastrian-born; he transmitted from Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami and became shaykh of the Sufis; the ribat facing the Mansur mosque bears his name. He said: 'I kept company with a thousand shaykhs, al-Husri among them, preserving a saying from each.' He died in Ramadan and was buried in the ribat. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 16 p. 58}
