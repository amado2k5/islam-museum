## Overview
Mari Jata, founder of the [[polity/mali-empire|Mali empire]] and, according to Ibn Khaldun, the greatest of the kings of the Western Sudan. In their language "Jata" means lion, and "Mari" means king. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His conquests
He overcame the people of Sosso and wrested from their hands the old kingdom of the people of Ghana. He ruled from the Nile to the Atlantic Ocean. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death and succession
Ibn Khaldun did not fix the year of his death. The approximate date in the data is derived and needs an additional source. He was later succeeded in rule by [[person/mansa-musa|Mansa Musa]].
