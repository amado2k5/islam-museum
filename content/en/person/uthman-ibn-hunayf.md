## Summary
[[person/uthman-ibn-hunayf|Uthman ibn Hunayf]] al-Ansari; for his lineage Ibn Hajar refers to his brother Sahl's entry {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1005}. Al-Tirmidhi alone said he witnessed Badr; the majority said his first battle was Uhud {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1005}.

## Measuring the land
Ibn Abi Shayba relates from Abu Mijlaz: Umar sent Uthman ibn Hunayf over the land survey — after [[place/kufa|Kufa]] was conquered — and in Bukhari, Umar said to him and to Ammar: "do you fear you have loaded the land with more than it can bear?" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.

## The governorship of Basra
[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] had appointed him over [[place/basra|Basra]] before he came to it; [[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha]] and [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr]] overpowered him there — "and there was the famous affair of the battle of the Camel", as Ibn Hajar says {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}. His nephew Abu Umama ibn Sahl and a number related from him; they said he settled at Kufa and died in Mu'awiya's caliphate {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.
