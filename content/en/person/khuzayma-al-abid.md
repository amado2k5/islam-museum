## Summary
Khuzayma Abu Muhammad al-Abid. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 473 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 130–131; the state that prevailed on him was leaving off his own choice and constant helplessness and need. The nisba in the heading is garbled in the scan and is not recorded. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/130-131}

## The man abandoned in affliction
Khuzayma ibn Muhammad al-Abid relates that a prophet passed by a man whom his family had abandoned in affliction, and said: my Lord, this is Your servant, and if You were to move him from his state. God revealed to him: ask him whether he would like Me to move him. The prophet said: sir, would you like God to move you from this state of yours to another? The man said: do you choose over God? That is for Him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/131}
