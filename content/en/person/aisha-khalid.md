## Overview
Aisha Khalid is a Qur'an teacher who runs the YouTube channel Islam with Aisha, a learning channel for women only, presenting word-by-word Urdu translation and tafseer of the Qur'an. Her channel's own descriptions call her "a very successful business woman and also a teacher of the Holy Quran for many years", with "a large number of students all around the world and from different walks of life". {cite:youtube-islam-with-aisha}

Her channel had about 663,000 subscribers by platform figures in October 2026, while the Feedspot directory of Islamic YouTube channels lists it at about 650,000; both are dated platform figures that change constantly. {cite:youtube-islam-with-aisha} {cite:feedspot-islam-with-aisha}

## Career and work
The channel's stated purpose is to spread the teachings of Islam through easy word-by-word translation and tafseer of the Qur'an in Urdu, and it runs regular live classes working through the Qur'an's parts and surahs, including a Dawrah-e-Quran series covering the parts in full and lessons on individual surahs such as al-An'am. {cite:youtube-islam-with-aisha}

Feedspot's directory describes the channel as "A women only Channel For Islamic Learning", attributes the tafseer to Aisha Khalid, and invites viewers to watch Qur'an videos with tafseer in Urdu. {cite:feedspot-islam-with-aisha}

Linked Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts carry her lessons and live-class announcements. {cite:youtube-islam-with-aisha}

Alongside teaching, the Urdu podcast AL-Hikmah Talks, which featured her as "Ustadah Aisha Khalid" discussing her journey with the Qur'an, names her business as Ashes Skin Care. {cite:al-hikmah-talks-aisha-khalid}
