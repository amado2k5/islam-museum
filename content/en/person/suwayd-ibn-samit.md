Suwayd ibn al-Samit al-Ansari al-Awsi, one of Yathrib's poets and leading men, so distinguished for vigour and nobility his people called him "the Accomplished". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He came to Mecca as pilgrim or 'umra-visitor, and the Prophet called him to Islam. Suwayd said: perhaps what you have is like what I have? The Prophet said: "and what do you have?" He said: Luqman's scroll — meaning his wisdom. The Prophet said: "that is fine speech; what I have is better than this — a Qur'an God sent down, guidance and light", and he recited to him and called him to Islam, and Suwayd thought it good. Not long after, Suwayd was killed at Yathrib in the battle of Bu'ath between the Aws and the Khazraj. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
