## Summary
A Companion of the Prophet from Quraysh, of Banu Abd al-Dar; his name is given as Amr or Ubayd Rabbih; he transmitted from the Prophet and was transmitted by Zufar ibn Aws and al-Aswad ibn Yazid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7834}
