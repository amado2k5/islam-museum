Saba Qamar Zaman (born 5 April 1984) is a Pakistani actress who works predominantly in Urdu films and television. She first drew attention in the historical drama Jinnah Ke Naam (2009) and built a leading career with series including Dastaan, Maat, Bunty I Love You and Digest Writer.{cite:wikipedia-saba-qamar-en} Her performance in the Indian film Hindi Medium (2017) earned a Filmfare nomination, and the Government of Pakistan honoured her with the Tamgha-e-Imtiaz in 2012 and the Pride of Performance in 2016.{cite:wikipedia-saba-qamar-en}{cite:wikidata-saba-qamar}
