## Overview
Qudaa al-Asadi, a poet who swore allegiance to Ibn al-Zubayr and then broke it. al-Mas'udi quotes his verses, in which he says that Ibn Mut'i invited him to the allegiance, and he came to it while his heart was not at ease with it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.79}
