## Overview
Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, Abd Allah ibn Qays al-Ash'ari, a Companion of the Ash'aris and an ally of the Banu Utba ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd Shams. He was among those whom the two ships brought from Abyssinia to the Prophet at Khaybar. See [[event/return-of-abyssinian-emigrants|the return of the emigrants from Abyssinia]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

He became known for his jurisprudence and his recitation of the Qur'an, and he was appointed over Basra and Kufa in the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Before Siffin
Al-Mas'udi relates that before Siffin he used to say that this community would not stop facing trials until they appointed two arbiters. Suwayd ibn Ghafla advised him not to be one of them. He then accepted an arbiter on behalf of the people of Iraq, and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] did not accept him, but al-Ash'ath and his group insisted. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 682-683}
