## Overview
Zayn al-Din Abu al-Faraj Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad, the Hanbali hafiz known as Ibn Rajab after the title of his grandfather Rajab. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.7}

## Early life
He was born in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in Rabi' al-Awwal 736 AH, according to the sources the editor consulted. Ibn Hajar dated his birth to 706 AH, and the editor considers that a slip of the pen. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.7} He came to [[place/damascus|Damascus]] with his father in 744 AH. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.8}

## Travels and teachers
He travelled in search of knowledge to Egypt, Nablus, the Hijaz, Jerusalem, Mecca and Medina. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.9} The editor counts [[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya]] among his most prominent teachers. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.11}

## Death
He died in Damascus in 795 AH, with no disagreement about the year. The sources differ on the month: Ramadan according to al-Sakhawi and Ibn al-Imad, and Rajab according to Ibn Hajar and al-Suyuti. He was buried in the cemetery of Bab al-Saghir. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.9} These dates come from one source and need an additional source.

## His work
One of his books is [[work/jami-ulum-hikam|Jami' al-Ulum wa al-Hikam]] (The Compendium of Knowledge and Wisdom). {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad Ibn Rajab of Baghdad and Damascus, the Hanbali hafiz Zayn al-Din; the scan gives his birth as Baghdad, Rabi' al-Awwal 706, which differs from the 736 given elsewhere and needs checking against the print. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/321} His works include a commentary on al-Tirmidhi, part of a commentary on al-Bukhari, Dhayl Tabaqat al-Hanabila and al-Qawa'id al-Fiqhiyya. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/322} He died in Rajab 795. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/322}
