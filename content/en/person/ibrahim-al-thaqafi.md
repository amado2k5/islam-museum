## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Sa'id ibn Hilal al-Thaqafi was a scholar who first held the Zaydi view and then moved to the Imami position; both are Shi'i schools. He was from Kufa, moved to Isfahan and died there. The printed text gives his death as 283 AH, 896 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'al-Maghazi', 'al-Ridda', 'al-Shura', 'Maqtal Uthman', 'Siffin', 'al-Nahrawan', 'al-Gharat', 'Rasa'il Ali ibn Abi Talib wa Akhbaruhu wa Hurubuh', 'al-Jami' al-Kabir' on Imami jurisprudence, a book on the imamate ('al-Imama'), 'Man Qubila min Al Muhammad', 'al-Siyar', a book on history, two books on drinks (al-ashriba), a book of sermons, 'Akhbar al-Mukhtar', and 'Fadl al-Kufa wa man Nazalaha min al-Sahaba' {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.
