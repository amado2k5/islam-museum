## Overview
Al-Mu'afa ibn Zakariya ibn Yahya al-Jariri al-Nahrawani, Abu'l-Faraj known as Ibn Tarrar (303–390 AH / 916–1000 CE), was a judge counted among the literary jurists, a fine poet; born and died at Nahrawan in Iraq, he served as deputy judge in Baghdad and was called al-Jariri because he followed the school of Ibn Jarir al-Tabari. {cite:al-aalam}

He left enjoyable works in literature and other fields, including a six-volume tafsir — likely the Bayan al-Mujiz fi Ulum al-Qur'an al-Mu'jiz — and the book al-Jalis wa'l-Anis. {cite:al-aalam}
