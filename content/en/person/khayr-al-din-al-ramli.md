## Overview
Khayr al-Din ibn Ahmad ibn Ali, al-Ayyubi, al-Alimi, al-Faruqi, was a jurist and researcher who also composed verse, of Ramla in Palestine. The entry dates his birth to 993 AH, equal to 1585 CE, and his death to 1081 AH, equal to 1671 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 325}.

## Life
He was born in Ramla and died there. In 1007 AH he travelled to Egypt and stayed six years at al-Azhar, then returned to his town, where he gave legal opinions and taught until his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 325}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names as his best-known book al-Fatawa al-Khayriyya, printed in two volumes; then Mazhar al-Haqa'iq, in manuscript, a gloss on al-Bahr al-Ra'iq in Hanafi jurisprudence; a collection of verse, in manuscript; and other works {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 325}.

## Death
He died in Ramla in 1081 AH, equal to 1671 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 325}.
