## Summary
Asaduddin Owaisi is an Indian barrister and politician, president of the All India Majlis-e-Ittehadul Muslimeen (AIMIM) and member of parliament for Hyderabad since 2004 — the country's most prominent Muslim elected voice, known for courtroom-trained oratory on minority rights and the constitution. {cite:wikipedia-asaduddin-owaisi}

## Life and formation
He was born on 13 May 1969 in Hyderabad into the city's best-known Muslim political family: his grandfather Abdul Wahed Owaisi rebuilt the Majlis after 1948, and his father Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi held the Hyderabad seat for two decades. Educated at Hyderabad's elite schools and in law at Lincoln's Inn, London, he was called to the Bar before entering politics. {cite:wikipedia-asaduddin-owaisi}

## Political career
Elected to the Andhra Pradesh assembly in 1994, he moved to the Lok Sabha in 2004 and has held Hyderabad ever since. As AIMIM president he expanded the party beyond its old city base — winning seats in Maharashtra, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh contests, entering municipal politics across states, and building the party's image as an independent Muslim platform outside the larger secular parties. {cite:wikipedia-asaduddin-owaisi}

## Positions and debate
In parliament he has championed affirmative action for Muslims, questioned citizenship legislation as discriminatory, and defended a constitutional secularism while attacking both the BJP and, frequently, secular parties for taking Muslim votes for granted. Supporters call him the most articulate elected Muslim voice in India; critics accuse AIMIM of splitting opposition votes and of rhetoric that sharpens communal boundaries.
