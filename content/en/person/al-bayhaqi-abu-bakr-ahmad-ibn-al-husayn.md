## Overview
The imam, the memoriser, the hadith scholar, the expert in jurisprudence and principles, Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Musa al-Bayhaqi, from Khusrawjird in Khurasan. He was born in 384 AH (994 CE) and died in 458 AH (1066 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
