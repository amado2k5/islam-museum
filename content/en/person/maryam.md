## Overview
Maryam daughter of Imran, mother of Isa. The museum's data record two hadiths from the Sahih of al-Bukhari about her. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3411}

## In the hadith
The Prophet counted Maryam among the perfect women, together with Asiya, the wife of Pharaoh. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3411}

He also excepted her and her son from the touch of Satan that every newborn experiences. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3431}

The rest of her life and everything else said about her need an additional source.

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] relates that the wife of Imran lost her husband while carrying a child she thought was a boy, and vowed it to the service of the temple; when she bore a girl she named her Maryam after Moses' sister, as is said, and sought refuge for her and her offspring from Satan ([[passage/quran-3-35|Al Imran 35]], [[passage/quran-3-36|36]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:07:00} He says God accepted her and raised her well, that [[person/zakariyya|Zakariyya]], the senior of the rabbis, took charge of her, and that the provision he found with her was a marvel from God ([[passage/quran-3-37|Al Imran 37]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:11:00} He points out that a sura of the Qur'an bears her name, while none bears the name of the Prophet's mother, of Khadija or of Fatima. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep01 00:03:00}
