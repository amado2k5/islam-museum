## Overview
Maryam daughter of Imran, mother of Isa. The museum's data record two hadiths from the Sahih of al-Bukhari about her. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3411}

## In the hadith
The Prophet counted Maryam among the perfect women, together with Asiya, the wife of Pharaoh. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3411}

He also excepted her and her son from the touch of Satan that every newborn experiences. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3431}

The rest of her life and everything else said about her need an additional source.

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] relates that the wife of Imran lost her husband while carrying a child she thought was a boy, and vowed it to the service of the temple; when she bore a girl she named her Maryam after Moses' sister, as is said, and sought refuge for her and her offspring from Satan ([[passage/quran-3-35|Al Imran 35]], [[passage/quran-3-36|36]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:07:00} He says God accepted her and raised her well, that [[person/zakariyya|Zakariyya]], the senior of the rabbis, took charge of her, and that the provision he found with her was a marvel from God ([[passage/quran-3-37|Al Imran 37]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep02 00:11:00} He points out that a sura of the Qur'an bears her name, while none bears the name of the Prophet's mother, of Khadija or of Fatima. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep01 00:03:00}

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the fourteenth and fifteenth episodes of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] to Maryam bint Imran, "perhaps the greatest woman in history". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:01:00} He cites the hadith of the four perfect women and the reports that make her the best of them, and counts her among the truthful ones, the rank after the prophets. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:02:00} He says prophets were sent only from among men, while these women reached the human perfection the prophets reached. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:01:30} Her mother, a devout worshipper at Jerusalem in Zakariyya's time, vowed her unborn child to the service of the temple and was surprised to bear a girl, whom she named Maryam and placed under God's protection. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:05:30} He sees in God's acceptance of her that nothing prevents a woman from serving religion as a man does. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:07:30}

He relates that the guardians cast their pens into a river, "it is said the Jordan", to decide who would raise her, and that only Zakariyya's pen went against the current. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:09:30} Zakariyya found fruit of the wrong season with her again and again, and she told him it came from God. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:12:00} She withdrew east of Jerusalem to worship until she was fifteen, which is why she is called al-Batul. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:15:00} Gabriel came to her in the form of a man, and she conceived. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep14 00:22:00}

In the second episode he relates a report, through Ibn al-Athir, of a conversation with her cousin Yusuf the carpenter about a child without a father, adding "God knows best whether it is sound". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:06:30} She withdrew to a far place, gave birth alone at the trunk of a palm, and in her distress wished she had died before; he lists her names al-Tahira, al-Batul and al-Adhra'. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:10:30} He draws from her shaking the palm trunk, weak as she was, that one must act, even a little, and not only pray. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:12:30} She carried the child to her people and pointed to him, and the infant spoke. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:20:00} Fearing for him, "it is said" she took him to Bethlehem, "and it is said" to Egypt. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:24:00} He says she lived until Jesus was raised up and then died a natural death, a model of the devoted worshipper. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep15 00:27:00}
