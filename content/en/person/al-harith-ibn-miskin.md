## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Miskin, a companion of Malik. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.539}

## The judgeship of Egypt
[[person/al-mutawakkil|Al-Mutawakkil]] appointed him judge of Egypt in 237 AH, after he refused for a time, replacing Ibn Abi al-Layth. He humiliated the judge who had been dismissed, having him beaten twenty lashes every day so that grievances might be returned to their owners. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.539}
