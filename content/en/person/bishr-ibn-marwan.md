## Overview
Bishr ibn Marwan, brother of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. His brother appointed him over Kufa in 73 AH. Al-Mas'udi describes him as an elegant, witty man who loved poetry, evening conversation, singing and drinking. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.102-104}

## The story of Rawh ibn Zinba'
His brother told him that Rawh ibn Zinba' was his uncle, whom he should not cut off any affair without. Bishr devised a plan against Rawh, and wrote on the wall of his room that the death of ibn Marwan had come and that he should devise a way for himself. Rawh left for Abd al-Malik. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.102-104}
