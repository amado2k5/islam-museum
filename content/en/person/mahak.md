## Overview
Mahak, lord of Istakhr. Al-Baladhuri says that he made peace with Abdallah ibn Amir on behalf of its people in 28 AH. After him they broke the covenant and killed his agent, and Ibn Amir came back on them and took it by storm. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
