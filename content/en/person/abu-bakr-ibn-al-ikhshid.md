## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn al-Ikhshid, a scholar whose view on the origin of the name of the Mu'tazila Ibn al-Nadim records. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.557}

## His view on the origin of the name
He said that the name "Mu'tazila" came to them in the days of [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan]], as some people reported, and that this was not established in his view. The well-known view among his scholars is that the name arose after al-Hasan. Its cause was that when al-Hasan died and [[person/qatada-ibn-diama|Qatada]] took his session, [[person/amr-ibn-ubayd|Amr ibn Ubayd]] withdrew from it with a group, and Qatada called them the Mu'tazila. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.557}
