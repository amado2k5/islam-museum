## Summary
Ibn al-Sammak Ahmad b. al-Husayn b. Ahmad, Abu'l-Husayn the preacher; born 330, he preached at the Mansur and Mahdi mosques on the Sufis' way — among his rarities, asked about the Ababil he said 'a thousand fits of rage' (?). {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.418}

## Accusation and death
He was named among those accused of lying in hadith with Abu'l-Fath al-Misri; he died in Dhu'l-Hijja 424 and was buried at Bab Harb. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.418}
