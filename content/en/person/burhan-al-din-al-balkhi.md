## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. al-Hasan al-Balkhi, the Hanafi preacher and ascetic styled Burhan al-Din; he taught at the Sadiriyya, then Amir Tankhan's house was made a madrasa for him — and the Hanbalis rose against him for speaking of them. An ascetic who shunned the world, he led the abolishing of 'hayya 'ala khayr al-'amal' from Aleppo, was mighty and splendid in the state, and taught also at Khatun's mosque; his biography falls under the year 548. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 628}
