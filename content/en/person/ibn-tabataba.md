## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Ibrahim al-Tabataba, the Husayni sharif of Raqqa origin, Egyptian; naqib of the Talibids in Egypt and among its greatest chiefs, with fine poetry in zuhd and ghazal — Abu Mansur al-Tha'alibi mentions him in the Yatima and quotes selections. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
