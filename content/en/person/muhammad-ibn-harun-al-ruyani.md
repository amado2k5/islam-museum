## Overview
Muhammad ibn Harun al-Ruyani, Abu Bakr, is described by al-Dhahabi as the imam, the memoriser and the trustworthy, author of the famous Musnad {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}. Al-Dhahabi gives, with his own chain, a hadith from his Musnad on the authority of Abu Bakra about whoever disgraces the authority God has set on earth, and notes that the Abu Bilal named in the report is Mirdas ibn Udayya, a Kharijite {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Abu al-Rabi' al-Zahrani, Ishaq ibn Shahin, Abu Kurayb Muhammad ibn al-Ala', Muhammad ibn Humayd al-Razi, Amr ibn Ali al-Fallas, Yahya ibn Hakim al-Muqawwim, Abu Zur'a al-Razi, Ibn Wara and a multitude beyond them; he travelled widely and was thoroughly learned {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}. Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili, Ibrahim ibn Ahmad al-Qirmisini, Ja'far ibn Abd Allah ibn Fannaki and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}.

## What the critics said
Abu Ya'la al-Khalili held him trustworthy and recorded that he wrote works on jurisprudence {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}.

## A report from his travels
Al-Dhahabi relays a report that the memoriser Ahmad ibn Mansur al-Shirazi had from Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Sahhaf, from Abu al-Abbas al-Bakri: in Egypt the journey for hadith brought together Muhammad ibn Jarir, Ibn Khuzayma, Muhammad ibn Nasr and al-Ruyani, their provisions ran out and they went hungry, and they drew lots over who should ask for them; the lot fell on Ibn Khuzayma, who stood to pray, and a servant sent by the governor of Egypt arrived with purses of fifty dinars each, one for every man, saying that the governor had seen in a dream that the scholars were hungry and had sworn that they must tell him when it ran out {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}. It is a report passed on with its chain, which al-Dhahabi relays without passing a judgement on it.

## His death
Al-Khalili recorded that he died in 307 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3745}.
