## Summary
Dr Akram Diya' al-Umari is the author of [[work/al-sira-al-nabawiyya-al-sahiha-umari|al-Sira al-Nabawiyya al-Sahiha]], subtitled "an attempt to apply the hadith scholars' rules to the criticism of the sira reports". {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.2}

## His approach to history
He calls for combining the hadith scholars' criticism of chains with the findings of modern historical criticism, provided it is governed by the standards of the Islamic worldview, and considers modern Islamic historical studies still at the start of the road. {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.12} {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.13} {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.14} Among his own works he mentions an article on Musa ibn Uqba among modern studies of the pioneers of the campaign literature. {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.54}
