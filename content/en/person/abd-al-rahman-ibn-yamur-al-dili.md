## Abd al-Rahman ibn Yamur al-Dili

Abd al-Rahman ibn Ya'mur al-Dili, known as Abu al-Aswad, a Companion. He related from the Prophet the report that the Hajj is Arafat and the report forbidding the gourd and the pitched vessel; Bukayr ibn Ata al-Laythi related from him. Ibn Hajar said Ibn Hibban counted him among the Companions and that he was a Meccan who settled in Kufa. The book gives no date. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5503}
