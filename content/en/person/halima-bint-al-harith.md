## Overview
Halima bint al-Harith al-Akbar ibn Abi Shamir al-Ghassani, king of the Arabs of Syria, was one of the kings' daughters of the Jahiliyya; to her are ascribed 'the day of Halima' among the days of the Arabs and 'the meadow of Halima' in the Syrian steppe where the battle was fought. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.270}

They are named for her because she stirred her father's men to fight that day in the meadow, or because she brought out for them a mortar of perfume with which she scented them. She is the proverb 'the day of Halima is no secret', and among their sayings is 'more glorious than Halima'. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.270}
