## Summary
Khalid Muhammad Khalid was an Egyptian writer, author of [[work/rijal-hawl-al-rasul|Rijal Hawl al-Rasul]], first published in Cairo in 1384/1964. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.8}

## Works
The list of his works in the Dar al-Muqattam edition gives thirty-four titles, among them *Min Huna Nabda'*, *Muwatinun la Ra'aya*, *al-Dimuqratiyya Abadan*, *al-Din li-al-Sha'b*, *Allah wa-al-Hurriyya*, *Insaniyyat Muhammad*, *Kama Tahaddatha al-Qur'an*, *Kama Tahaddatha al-Rasul*, *Khulafa' al-Rasul*, *Abna' al-Rasul fi Karbala'*, *Mu'jizat al-Islam Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz* and *Qissati ma'a al-Hayat*, besides his books on the four caliphs. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.643} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.13}
