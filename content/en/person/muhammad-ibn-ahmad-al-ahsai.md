## Overview

Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Ahsa'i, a learned Hanafi jurist from al-Ahsa in Najd, who settled in Baghdad and died there in 1083 AH / 1672 CE.{cite:al-aalam vol. 6 p. 11}

## Works

Al-Zirikli's entry lists a supercommentary (*hashiya*) on al-Suyuti's commentary on the *Alfiya*, a commentary on *Tahdhib al-Mantiq*, a commentary on al-Qaduri's Hanafi *fiqh* manual, and a book on definitions (*al-Ta'rifat*). It marks the supercommentary as a manuscript.{cite:al-aalam vol. 6 p. 11}
