## Summary

Muhyi al-Din Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Bardai al-Hanafi, one of the mawlas of Anatolia and the son of scholars. He studied with his father and others, then went to Shiraz and Herat, read with their scholars and gained a great deal of learning. He then travelled to Anatolia, where he became a teacher at the madrasa of Ahmad Pasha in Bursa, and then at one of the two adjoining madrasas in Edirne, where he died while still teaching {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}.

He wrote glosses on al-Baydawi's commentary on the Qur'an, glosses on the commentary of al-Sharif al-Sayyid on al-Tajrid, glosses on al-Jalawi, and a commentary on Adab al-Bahth by al-Adud. He had good manners, a gentle character, and was humble and reverent. He wrote a fine hand, even when writing quickly {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}.

He died in Edirne in the year 923 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}.
