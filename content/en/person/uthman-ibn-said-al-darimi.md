## Overview
Al-Darimi — 'Uthman ibn Sa'id ibn Khalid ibn Sa'id, the imam, erudite hafiz and critic, shaykh of his region Abu Sa'id al-Tamimi al-Darimi al-Sijistani, author of the great Musnad and other works; born shortly before 200 AH, he roamed the lands in quest of hadith. He died in 280 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2651}
