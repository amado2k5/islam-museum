## Overview
Al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf: Hashim's brother and heir of the water-supply and the feeding after him; he brought his nephew Shayba from Yathrib, whence the boy was nicknamed "Abd al-Muttalib".

## Life
The siqaya and rifada passed after Hashim to his brother al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf, an obeyed nobleman of merit among his people whom Quraysh called al-Fayyad ("the overflowing") for his generosity {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

When Shayba grew to be a young man in Yathrib, al-Muttalib heard of him and journeyed in search of him; he mounted him behind him on his mount and told his mother: he is only going to his father's kingdom and to God's sanctuary. He entered Mecca with him riding behind on his camel and people said: this is Abd al-Muttalib ("al-Muttalib's servant") — he answered: he is but my nephew Hashim's son {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Al-Muttalib perished at Radman in the land of Yemen, and Abd al-Muttalib took over after him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Al-Muttalib was the Prophet's grandfather's uncle; he brought the boy Shayba from Medina and people named him "Abd al-Muttalib" — servant of his uncle — and the name prevailed.
