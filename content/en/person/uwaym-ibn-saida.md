## Overview
Uwaym ibn Sa'ida, of the Ansar. He met Abu Bakr, Umar and Abu Ubayda on their way to the Saqifa with Ma'n ibn Adi, and they told them what the people had gathered upon. In Ibn Ishaq's account the verse about men who love to be purified is revealed concerning him, and the Prophet said of him: "He is an excellent man among them" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/307}. See [[event/saqifa|the Saqifa]].

## Notes on the report
- Ibn Ishaq transmits this from al-Zuhri, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/307}.
