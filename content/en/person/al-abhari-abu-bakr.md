## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Salih ibn Umar al-Tamimi al-Abhari, a Maliki jurist from Abhar, between Qazvin and Zanjan. He combined the sciences of readings, high chains of transmission, and fine jurisprudence. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.202-206}

## Dates
He was born in 289 AH (901 CE) and died in 375 AH (985 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.202-206}
