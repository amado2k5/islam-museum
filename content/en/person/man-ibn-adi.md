## Summary
Brother of Banu al-Ajlan; the Prophet called him with Malik ibn al-Dukhshum to demolish and burn the mosque of Dirar — Ibn Ishaq wavers, saying "or his brother Asim ibn Adi". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In the demolition of the mosque of Dirar
He went out quickly with Malik ibn al-Dukhshum to Banu Salim ibn Awf; he waited until Malik brought him the fire, then the two entered the mosque, burnt it and demolished it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- "and Ma'n ibn Adi — or his brother Asim ibn Adi" (p. 174): the narrator is not certain which of the two brothers.
