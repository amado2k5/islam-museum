## Summary

Abdulrahman Mosad is a young Egyptian Qur'an reciter who began memorising the Qur'an at the age of five and completed its memorisation at twelve{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}{cite:mawdoo3-abdulrahman-mosad}; his recitations spread through YouTube and social media until he became one of the best-known young Qur'anic voices in the Arab world, with a channel of about 2.35 million subscribers in platform figures of November 2026{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}. The available profiles disagree on whether he was born in Egypt or in Riyadh, and agree that he was raised in Riyadh and holds Egyptian nationality{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}{cite:mawdoo3-abdulrahman-mosad}; his birth year is not officially published in any source the editors read.

## Upbringing and memorisation

Mosad grew up in a devout family in Riyadh and began memorising the Qur'an as a child of five, encouraged by his parents, completing the memorisation at twelve{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}{cite:mawdoo3-abdulrahman-mosad}. He took part in school and mosque Qur'an competitions and drew attention for the sweetness of his voice from an early age{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}.

## Digital reach

Mosad reached his largest audience through YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, where his short and long recitations circulate widely, and profiles present him as one of the leading reciters of the new generation{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}.

## Books

In 2022 Mosad published the book Inna Rabbi Latif ("Indeed my Lord is Subtle"), launched at the Cairo International Book Fair{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}.

## Impact and audience

Mosad is among the most famous young reciters in the Arab digital sphere, with about 2.35 million YouTube subscribers in platform figures of November 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:almrj3-abdulrahman-mosad}.
