# Sardar Azmoun

## Summary

Sardar Azmoun is an Iranian professional footballer who was born in Gonbad-e Kavus on 1 January 1995.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-ar}
A striker of Turkmen origin, he became Iran's youngest player in the Russian Premier League and starred for Zenit Saint Petersburg, finishing as the league's co-top scorer and Player of the Season in 2020–21.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
He has played for Iran since 2014, scoring more than fifty international goals and appearing at two World Cups.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}

## Early life

Azmoun was introduced to football on a family trip to Turkmenistan at the age of nine and began his career at Oghab Gonbad in his home city, also playing volleyball to a standard that brought an invitation to Iran's national under-15 team.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
He joined Sepahan's youth ranks at fifteen, and at seventeen moved to Rubin Kazan, becoming Iran's youngest legionnaire and its first player in the Russian Premier League.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
He is the son of Khalil Azmoun, a former Iranian national volleyball player and coach.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}

## Club career

At Rubin Kazan Azmoun made his senior debut in the Europa League in July 2013, and in February 2015 he joined Rostov on loan, a move that became permanent after a contract dispute between the two clubs was settled in Rostov's favour.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
With Rostov he scored in the Champions League against Ajax, Atlético Madrid and Bayern Munich as the club reached the group stage for the first time, and in February 2019 he signed for Zenit Saint Petersburg.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
At Zenit he won three league titles, finished the 2020–21 season as the league's co-top scorer with seventeen goals, and was voted the Russian Premier League's Player of the Season.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
He joined Bayer Leverkusen in January 2022, spent the 2023–24 season on loan at Roma, and moved to Shabab Al Ahli of the United Arab Emirates in July 2024.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}

## International career

Azmoun made his Iran debut in 2014 at the age of nineteen and played at the Asian Cups of 2015, 2019 and 2023 and the World Cups of 2018 and 2022.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
At the article's last statistical update he had scored 57 goals in 93 internationals.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
British media have called him the "Iranian Messi", and he has been touted as the heir of the Iranian striker Ali Daei.{cite:wikipedia-sardar-azmoun-en}
