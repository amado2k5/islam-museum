## Overview
Al-Afshin, lord of Ushrusana. Muslim ibn Sa'id raided him, and he made peace in return for six thousand heads and handed over his fortress. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His personal name, the date of the event, and the page number are not recorded and need an additional source.
