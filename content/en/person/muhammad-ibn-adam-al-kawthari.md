# Muhammad ibn Adam Al-Kawthari

Muhammad ibn Adam Al-Kawthari (born 1976) is a British jurist and mufti from Leicester. He graduated from Darul Uloom Bury, studied with Taqi Usmani at Darul Uloom Karachi, and in 2000 studied in Damascus under Abd al-Razzaq al-Halabi and Abd al-Latif Farfur al-Hasani.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-adam-al-kawthari-en}

He is the founder of Darul Ifta Leicester and its Chief Mufti, and teaches at Jamiah Uloom-ul-Quran in Leicester. His books include Islamic Guide to Sexual Relations, Birth Control & Abortion in Islam, and an Arabic commentary on the creed text Al-Muhannad ala al-Mufannad.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-adam-al-kawthari-en}
