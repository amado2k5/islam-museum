## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahim al-Faruqi al-Dahlawi al-Hindi, Abu Abd al-Aziz, called Shah Waliullah, was a Hanafi jurist among the hadith scholars, from Delhi in India. He visited the Hijaz in 1143 to 1145 AH. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 1110 to 1176 AH, 1699 to 1762 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## What was said of him
Al-Zirikli quotes the author of Fihris al-Faharis to the effect that God revived hadith and the sunna in India, after their decline, through him, his children, his daughter's children and their students, and that in those lands reliance rests on his books and chains of transmission. He adds that the author of al-Yani' al-Jani named him Wali Allah ibn Abd al-Rahim, and that a death in 1179 AH has also been reported {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists these books, all printed unless noted: al-Fawz al-Kabir fi Usul al-Tafsir, written in Persian and translated after his death into Arabic and Urdu and published in both; Fath al-Khabir bi-ma la Budda min Hifzihi fi Ilm al-Tafsir; Hujjat Allah al-Baligha, two volumes; Izalat al-Khafa an Khilafat al-Khulafa; al-Irshad ila Muhimmat al-Asnad; al-Insaf fi Asbab al-Khilaf; Iqd al-Jid fi Ahkam al-Ijtihad wa-l-Taqlid; al-Musawwa min Ahadith al-Muwatta, two volumes; Sharh Tarajim Abwab al-Bukhari; Ta'wil al-Ahadith; al-Khayr al-Kathir, on wisdom; al-I'tiqad al-Sahih; al-Budur al-Bazigha, on Sufism and wisdom; and al-Qawl al-Jamil fi Bayan Sawa' al-Sabil, on Sufism. He also translated the Qur'an into Persian in the manner of Arabic verse and called the book Fath al-Rahman fi Tarjamat al-Qur'an {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.
