## Summary
Abdurrahman Önül is a Turkish ilahi (devotional song) performer, writer and composer, born on 1 December 1973 in Bayburt and active in the genre since 1990. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az} {cite:meb-tuzla-abdurrahman-onul}

His official YouTube channel counted 372,000 subscribers as of 12 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-abdurrahman-onul}

## Early life and education
Önül was born in Bayburt on 1 December 1973 and graduated from the Fatih Imam Hatip High School in Istanbul. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az} {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-ar} {cite:meb-tuzla-abdurrahman-onul}

The Azerbaijani Wikipedia article says his interest in music began in childhood, that he took private music lessons and learned to play several instruments. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az}

The Ministry of Education page records that he resides in Istanbul. {cite:meb-tuzla-abdurrahman-onul}

## Career and work
Önül entered the music market in 1990 with his first album, Yalvar Allah'a, and has since released dozens of albums, among them Söyle Dostum, Yusuf'un Öyküsü, Arafat, Son Peygamber, Hac Mevsimi and Dosta Doğru. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az} {cite:meb-tuzla-abdurrahman-onul}

He has presented many programmes on Turkish local, regional and national radio and television channels, including Ramadan programmes and weekly programmes on Turkish religious channels. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az}

The Arabic Wikipedia article describes him as a Turkish munshid known for a well-known nasheed he performed with Celal Karaturi. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-ar}

The Azerbaijani article and his standard publicity biography report that he long held the title of the best-selling ilahi artist in Turkey and Europe; this museum carries that as the article's and his publicity's framing, not as a verified fact. {cite:wikipedia-abdurrahman-onul-az}
