## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Uthman al-Marini, sultan of the Maghrib, who styled himself Commander of the Muslims, Sultan of the Two Shores and Protector of the Two Edges. Al-Maqqari says that he wrote three copies of the Qur'an in his own hand and sent them to the three mosques to which journeys are made, and endowed endowments upon them. He sent to [[person/al-nasir-muhammad-ibn-qalawun|al-Malik al-Nasir ibn Qalawun]] a gift of precious stones, and wrote him a letter composed by [[person/ibn-nubata-al-misri|Ibn Nubata al-Misri]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.403-404}

Al-Maqqari says that he saw one of these copies, the one in Jerusalem. He says that he ruled for ten years and seven days, and was then replaced by his son [[person/abu-inan-faris|Abu Inan]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.404}
