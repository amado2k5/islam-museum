## Overview
Sultan Orhan ibn Osman ibn Ertugrul, who ruled from 726 to 761 AH (1327–1360 CE), and who was the second sultan of the House of Osman {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.52}.

## His policy
al-Salabi reports that he followed the policy of his father [[person/uthman-i-uthmani|Osman]] in rule and conquest. Nicomedia fell into his hands in the year 727 AH, and he founded there the first Ottoman university, and entrusted its administration to Dawud al-Qaysari {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.52}.

He concerned himself with building the army on modern foundations, and made it a regular army {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.52}.

## The plan for Constantinople
He devised a plan to besiege Constantinople from the west and the east. He sent his son and heir Suleyman to cross the Dardanelles strait and seize positions on the western side {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.52}.

## Notes for researchers
- The date of death (1360 CE) is recorded in the data with the status of "commonly transmitted report".
