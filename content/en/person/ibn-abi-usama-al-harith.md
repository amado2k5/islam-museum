## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Usama al-Tamimi al-Baghdadi, a hadith scholar and memoriser and a transmitter of reports from Baghdad, knowledgeable in the Qur'anic readings. He is described as trustworthy (thiqa). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.534}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 186 AH (802 CE) and died in 282 AH (895 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.534} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
