## Overview
Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, counted as the grandfather of the Abbasid caliphs. Al-Mas'udi relates in "Muruj al-Dhahab" that his maternal uncles from Kinda prevented him on the day of al-Harra from giving the allegiance to the army of Yazid under Musrif, and that people of Rabi'a prevented him likewise, and he mentioned that in verses he composed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.68}

His dates of birth and death need an additional source.

## In Futuh al-Buldan — al-Baladhuri (Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition)
Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas. Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that Muhalhil, Mujalid al-Sarawi and Maymun Abu Bishr were among his clients, and that his son Muhammad named the client Yahya "Muhallil" and set him free. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}
