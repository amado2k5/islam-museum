## Summary
Abda, sister of [[person/abu-sulayman-al-darani|Abu Sulayman al-Darani]], is among the women ascetics of Syria in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 822). {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.858} Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami records that Abu Sulayman had two sisters, Abda and Amina, who both stood very high in intelligence and religion. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.858}

## A bridge over Hell
Ibn al-Jawzi relates from Abu Sulayman that he described to his sister Abda one of the bridges over Hell, and she remained a day and a night in a single cry that did not stop; it then left her, but whenever it was mentioned she cried out. Asked what her cry was, he said she pictured herself on the bridge as it tipped her over. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.858}

## Poverty and contentment
[[person/ahmad-ibn-abi-al-hawari|Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari]] relates Abu Sulayman's words: "I heard my sister say: the poor are all dead, except those whom God brings to life with the dignity of contentment and acceptance of their poverty." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.858}

## For young visitors
Abda was the sister of the ascetic scholar Abu Sulayman; she used to say that a poor person content with what he has lives an honourable life.
