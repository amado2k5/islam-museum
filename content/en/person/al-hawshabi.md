## Overview
Ali ibn Mani' al-Hawshabi was the sultan of the Hawashib, one of the Yemeni protectorates, whose people were Sunnis. He resided in a village called al-Musaymir, drew a subsidy from the Aden government, and was charged with keeping the caravan routes safe. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.321}

Amin al-Rihani saw him on his journey to Yemen in 1340 AH and described him as thin as a shadow, nervous in temper, sharp in disposition and free of tongue. He died in 1340 AH (1922 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.321}
