## Summary
Ali ibn Musa ibn Harun, Abu al-Hasan, known as Ibn Harun, a Maliki jurist, mufti and expert in inheritance. He studied with Ibn Ghazi for about twenty-nine years, and with al-Wansharisi and al-Miknasi. Abd al-Wahid al-Wansharisi and Abd al-Wahhab al-Zaqqaq studied with him. The book gives his death as 951 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7147}
