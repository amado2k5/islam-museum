## Overview
Sayf al-Din Qutuz al-Mu'izzi. Al-Maqrizi says he was the deputy of al-Mu'izz Aybak, managed the affairs of Aybak's son al-Mansur Ali, and then deposed him on Saturday, 14 Dhu al-Qa'da 657 AH. He took the sultanate after that under the title al-Malik al-Muzaffar {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1916}.

Al-Maqrizi says that he met Hulagu's army at Ayn Jalut and defeated it on Friday, 15 Ramadan 658 AH {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1916}.

Al-Suyuti relates that when the Tatars camped at Amid in 657 AH and Ibn al-Adim asked for help, Qutuz gathered the emirs and notables, and Shaykh Izz al-Din ibn Abd al-Salam ruled that fighting them was obligatory, on conditions he set out {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.719-721}.

Al-Maqrizi says that Baybars al-Bunduqdari killed him near al-Manzila al-Salihiyya on a Saturday in the middle of Dhu al-Qa'da {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1916}. Ibn al-Imad gives the sixteenth of Dhu al-Qa'da for his death, so the two accounts differ on the day {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.299}. See [[person/baybars|Baybars]].
