## Overview
Sayf al-Din Qutuz al-Mu'izzi, a mamluk of al-Mu'izz Aybak and atabeg of his son al-Malik al-Mansur Ali. al-Suyuti relates that when the Tatars camped at Amid in 657 AH and Ibn al-Adim asked for help, Qutuz gathered the emirs and notables, and Shaykh Izz al-Din ibn Abd al-Salam issued a ruling that it was obligatory to fight them, on conditions he set out {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.719-721}.

He then took the sultanate and was titled al-Malik al-Muzaffar. He led the Egyptians into Syria and defeated the Tatars at Ayn Jalut on Friday, 25 Ramadan 658 AH. Baybars and a group of emirs killed him on the road on 16 Dhu al-Qa'da {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.719-721}. See [[person/baybars|Baybars]].
