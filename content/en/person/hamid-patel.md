## Summary
Sir Mufti Hamid Patel is a British educationist, Chief Executive of Star Academies, a multi-academy trust running dozens of state schools across northern England and beyond. Unusually for the head of a major state school chain, he is also a classically trained Muslim scholar — a graduate of Darul Uloom in Bury who carries the title Mufti — and he was knighted in 2021 for services to education. {cite:wikipedia-hamid-patel}

## Formation
Born in the late 1970s, Patel studied the Islamic sciences at Darul Uloom al-Arabiyyah al-Islamiyyah in Bury, Lancashire, qualifying in the issuing of legal responsa, and also trained in mainstream education leadership. That double formation — seminary scholar and state-school executive — defines his public profile. {cite:wikipedia-hamid-patel}

## Star Academies
He led Tauheedul Islam Girls' High School in Blackburn — a Muslim faith school whose pupils, many from deprived neighbourhoods, achieved some of the best results in England — and then built it into Star Academies, the trust that now runs several dozen primary and secondary schools. Under his leadership the trust has repeatedly topped national league tables for pupil progress, and its model of high-expectation schooling in poor communities has been studied by policymakers. {cite:muslim500-hamid-patel}

## Public roles and honours
Patel has served on national education bodies and was appointed interim chair of the Ofsted board in 2024. Knighted in the 2021 Birthday Honours, he is frequently listed among the most influential figures in English education. His dual identity invites two readings: admirers see proof that Muslim institutions can lead in the mainstream; some secularist critics question the place of faith schools in the state system at all. The museum records both attributed. {cite:wikipedia-hamid-patel}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as the most prominent Muslim in British school education — a seminary-trained executive whose trust's results made the case, in data, for faith-ethos schooling in deprived areas. {cite:muslim500-hamid-patel}
