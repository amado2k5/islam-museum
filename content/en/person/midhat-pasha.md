## Overview
Midhat Pasha, an Ottoman minister, author of the constitution of 1876 CE. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.377}

## The constitution
Al-Salabi says that this constitution was the first to be put into effect in the history of Islam and its states, and that it was taken from the French and Belgian constitutions. He adds that the constitutionalists fought against him afterwards, and he was dismissed. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.377}
