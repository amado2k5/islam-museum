## Overview
Utba, a slave-girl of al-Khayzuran. al-Mas'udi reports that Abu al-Atahiya wrote much of love poetry about her, until she complained of him to her mistress, and al-Mahdi had him flogged {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.305-306}.

## The report
When she saw him flogged, her tears flowed. When al-Mahdi was about to hand her over to him, she said that she would not be content to be handed to a jar-seller who earns a living by poetry. She told him that if he were in love with Utba, he would have been occupied away from gold and silver {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.305-306}.
