# Ali Maâloul

## Summary

Ali Maâloul is a Tunisian professional footballer, born in Sfax on 1 January 1990. A left-back with an unusual scoring record for his position, he played nine seasons for Egypt's Al Ahly between his two spells at CS Sfaxien, and has won around ninety caps for Tunisia. {cite:wikipedia-ali-maaloul-en} {cite:wikipedia-ali-maaloul-ar} {cite:wikidata-ali-maaloul}

## Career

Maâloul came through CS Sfaxien and played 157 league matches for the club from 2009 to 2016, scoring 31 goals from defence. In 2016 he crossed to Al Ahly in Cairo, where he spent nine seasons and played 163 league matches, scoring 35 goals, before returning to CS Sfaxien in 2025. {cite:wikipedia-ali-maaloul-en} {cite:wikipedia-ali-maaloul-ar}

He has played for Tunisia since 2013, winning 89 caps and scoring 3 goals by the recorded revision. {cite:wikipedia-ali-maaloul-en}
