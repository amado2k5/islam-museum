## In brief
Kathir b. Murra al-Hadrami of Homs, the jurist and scholar of the people of Homs; he met seventy Badri Companions and narrated from Mu'adh, Abu al-Darda', Ubada b. al-Samit, and their layer, while the Al al-Zahriyya, Khalid b. Ma'dan, Makhul, Salim b. Amir, Abd al-Rahman b. Jubayr, and others narrated from him. Al-Nasa'i said "there is no harm in him". {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.53}
