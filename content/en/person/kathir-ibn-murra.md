## Overview
Kathir ibn Murra al-Hadrami. [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan]], governor of Egypt, asked him to write down for him what he had heard from the Companions of the Prophet, except the hadiths of Abu Hurayra, which were with someone else. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 168}

## Context
The book gives this report in the context of the beginnings of recording hadith in the Umayyad period. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 168}
