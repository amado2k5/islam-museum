## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Ya'la, Abu Abd Allah, titled Badr al-Din al-Ba'li, was the shaykh of the Hanbalis in Baalbek, and the giving of legal opinions there turned on him. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 778 AH, 1376 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 286}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names his Mukhtasar al-Fatawa al-Misriyya, printed, which he called al-Tashil; it abridges al-Durar al-Mudiyya min al-Fatawa al-Misriyya, a book al-Zirikli attributes to [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 286}.
