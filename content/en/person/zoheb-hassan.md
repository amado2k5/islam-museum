Zoheb Hassan (born 18 November 1966) is a Pakistani pop singer, songwriter and producer, born Syed Zoheb-u-Din Hassan.{cite:wikipedia-zoheb-hassan-en}{cite:wikidata-zoheb-hassan} With his elder sister Nazia he formed the duo Nazia and Zoheb, whose albums — beginning in the early 1980s — sold over 65 million records worldwide and made the siblings the first pop stars of the subcontinent's new pop era.{cite:wikipedia-zoheb-hassan-en}{cite:wikipedia-zoheb-hassan-ur}

After Nazia's death in 2000 he largely withdrew from singing and worked in business; he later returned with solo releases, including work dedicated to his sister's memory.{cite:wikipedia-zoheb-hassan-en}
