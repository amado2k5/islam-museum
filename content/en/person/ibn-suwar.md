## Summary
Ibn Suwar, muqri' of Iraq Abu Tahir Ahmad ibn 'Ali ibn 'Ubaydallah ibn 'Umar ibn Suwar, author of the Mustanir on the readings; reliable and proficient, he gave iqra' to multitudes, heard much and transmitted from Ibn Ghaylan and his generation; he died in 496. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 403}
