## Summary
Kulthum ibn al-Hidm al-Ansari, the Prophet's host at [[place/quba|Quba]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.171}.

## Hospitality
When the Prophet alighted at Quba, he stayed with Kulthum ibn al-Hidm. In the author's view this is the better-established account, though it is also said that he stayed with Sa'd ibn Khaythama {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.171} {cite:zad-al-maad}.

[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] also stayed with Kulthum when he caught up with the Prophet, having emigrated on foot after returning the trusts he held {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.171-172}.

## The Prophet's stay at Quba (*The Sealed Nectar*, pp. 171–172)
The Prophet stayed with him at Quba. It is also said that he stayed with Sa'd ibn Khaythama, but the first account is the more firmly established in the book. He remained there four days ([[event/hijra-journey-to-medina|the Hijra journey and entry into Medina]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.171} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.172}.
