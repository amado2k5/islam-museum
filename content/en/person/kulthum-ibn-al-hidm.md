## Summary
Kulthum ibn al-Hidm al-Ansari, the Prophet's host in [[place/quba|Quba]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 171}.

## The hospitality
When the Prophet alighted at Quba he lodged with Kulthum ibn al-Hidm — the sounder view to the author — or it is said with Sa'd ibn Khaythama {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 171} {cite:zad-al-maad}.

[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] also lodged with Kulthum when he caught up with the Prophet, emigrating on foot after rendering the trusts {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 171-172}.
