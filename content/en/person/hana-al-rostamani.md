## Summary
Hana Al Rostamani is the Group Chief Executive of First Abu Dhabi Bank — the largest bank in the United Arab Emirates and one of the largest in the Middle East — and the first woman to lead it. She was appointed in January 2021. {cite:wikipedia-hana-al-rostamani}

## Career
With a master's in information management and public administration from Western institutions, Al Rostamani built her career across the UAE's financial and development sectors — including the private-banking arm of FAB's predecessors, leadership of its consumer-banking business, and earlier service at institutions such as Mubadala-linked entities and the Abu Dhabi education sector. She came to the group CEO role as head of personal banking. {cite:wikipedia-hana-al-rostamani}

## FAB
FAB was formed by the 2017 merger of National Bank of Abu Dhabi and First Gulf Bank. As group CEO, Al Rostamani leads an institution with assets in the hundreds of billions of dollars and operations across some twenty markets — steering its digital transformation, its sustainable-finance commitments (a major Gulf-bank pledge made under her tenure) and its regional expansion. {cite:muslim500-hana-al-rostamani}

## Standing
Regularly listed by Forbes and Fortune among the world's most powerful women in business, she is profiled in the Muslim 500 as the most powerful woman in Arab banking — the executive who took command of the Gulf's largest lender and made Emirati women's ascent in finance visible at the very top. {cite:muslim500-hana-al-rostamani}
