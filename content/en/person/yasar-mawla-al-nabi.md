## Overview
A slave whom the Prophet took as spoil in the expedition against Muharib and Banu Tha'laba, and he placed him with his milch camels, which grazed in the area of al-Jamma' in the outskirts of Medina {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/290}. Some sick men of [[group/bajila|Bajila]] came, and they were allowed to drink the milk of the camels and their urine until they recovered. Then they attacked him, slaughtered him, drove thorns into his eyes, and drove off the camels. [[person/kurz-ibn-jabir-al-fihri|Kurz ibn Jabir]] pursued them, and they were brought back and punished. See [[event/sariyya-of-kurz-ibn-jabir-against-the-killers-of-yasar|the report and its parallels]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/290}.

## Notes on the report
- Nothing is known of Yasar apart from this report. In the two Sahihs he is mentioned as "the Prophet's herdsman" without a name, in the report of the 'Uraniyyun (needs_source).
