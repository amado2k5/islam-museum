## Overview
Abu al-Jawa'iz al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Bari, the secretary of Wasit, was one of the accomplished; he lived long at Baghdad, where al-Khatib recorded in his history that he took down reports, anecdotes, poems and dictations from Ibn Shikra al-Hashimi and others, though he was not reliable — an adib and poet fine of verse in praise and description. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.2 p.110}

He wrote goodly works in a good hand and delightful verse; Ibn Khallikan says he saw many quotations of his but never a diwan, and does not know whether his poetry was ever collected; among his current poems are lines of description and wisdom. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.2 p.110}
