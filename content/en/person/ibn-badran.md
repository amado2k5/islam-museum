## Summary
Abd al-Qadir ibn Mustafa ibn Abd al-Rahim ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn Badran, author of al-Madkhal ila madhhab al-Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 13}. He wrote the book's draft in Jumada al-Ula 1338 AH in Damascus, at the school of Abdullah Pasha al-Azem {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 13}. He died in Damascus in Rabi' al-Thani 1346 AH, and the book was printed after his death {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 13}.
