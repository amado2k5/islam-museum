# Waleed Basyouni

## Summary
Waleed Basyouni is an Islamic scholar and educator based in Texas. He is the President of AlMaghrib Institute and Head of its Islamic Theology and Ethics department, and he holds a PhD in theology. {cite:almaghrib-waleed-basyouni}

## Posts
He is the imam of Clear Lake Islamic Center and the Director of the Texas Dawah Convention. {cite:almaghrib-waleed-basyouni} He is a member of the North American Imam Federation and serves on the Assembly of Muslim Jurists of America's Fatwa and Research Committee, and he advises Islamic societies and organisations across the United States. {cite:listennotes-waleed-basyouni} His recorded lectures are archived in Muslim Central's podcast series under his name. {cite:listennotes-waleed-basyouni}
