## Overview
The caliph al-Mu'tadid appointed him judge of the City of Peace (Baghdad) in the year 283 AH. He invested him with robes and assigned him to the eastern side {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 620,636}.

His kunya was Abu Hazim, and the poet satirised his sons, as al-Mas'udi reports {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 620,636}.
