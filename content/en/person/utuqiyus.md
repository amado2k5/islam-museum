## Overview
Apollonius (Utuqiyus) of Ascalon, skilled in the science of geometry, mentioned by Ibn al-Nadim in the Fihrist {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.373}.

## His works
The Fihrist reports that he gathered what he could of the book of Apollonius on conics, and corrected four treatises from it. He also has a commentary on the first treatise of the book of Archimedes on the sphere and the cylinder {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.373}.

The Fihrist also transmits from the Banu Musa that he had good books on geometry, of which nothing reached them {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.374}.
