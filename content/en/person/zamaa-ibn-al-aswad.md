## Overview
Zam'a ibn al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib ibn Asad al-Qurashi al-Asadi, one of the leaders of Quraysh. He stood with Zuhayr, al-Mut'im and Abu al-Bakhtari in annulling the document of the boycott, and said that they would not accept what was written in it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.111}.

## What is not covered here
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum does not, at this point, give his full lineage or his death. They will be completed from its sources when he is mentioned.

## For young visitors
Zam'a ibn al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib ibn Asad was one of the men of Quraysh who objected to the injustice done to the Banu Hashim in the valley.
