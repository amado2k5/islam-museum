## Summary
Nuh ibn Abi Maryam Yazid ibn Abi Ju'una, called al-Jami', either because he was the first to gather the fiqh of Abu Hanifa or because he combined several sciences. He studied law with Abu Hanifa and Ibn Abi Layla, transmitted hadith from al-Zuhri and others, and was appointed judge of Merv. Ahmad described him as severe against the Jahmiyya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1611}
