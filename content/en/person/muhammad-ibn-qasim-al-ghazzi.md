## Overview
Muhammad ibn Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad, Abu Abd Allah, Shams al-Din al-Ghazzi, known as Ibn Qasim and as Ibn al-Gharabili, was a Shafi'i jurist. He was born and raised in Gaza, studied there and in Cairo, settled in Cairo and held posts at al-Azhar and elsewhere {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 859 to 918 AH, that is 1455 to 1512 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «Fath al-Qarib al-Mujib fi Sharh Alfaz al-Taqrib», printed, known as Ibn Qasim's commentary on the text of Abu Shuja'; a gloss on the commentary on morphology (tasrif), in manuscript at the Azhar library, in which he commented on al-Sa'd al-Taftazani's commentary on Arabic morphology; and glosses on al-Khayali's gloss on the commentary on the creed of al-Nasafi, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
