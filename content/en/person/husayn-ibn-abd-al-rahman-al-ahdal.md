## Overview
Husayn ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Ahdal al-Husayni, Abu Abd al-Rahman, known by the title Badr al-Din. A historian and scholar from among the scholars of Yemen. He was known for his learning, and people came to him to study. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.521}

## His works
He wrote books on history and biography, among them "Tuhfat al-Zaman fi Tarikh Sadat al-Yaman". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.521}

## The dates
He was born around 779 AH and died in 855 AH. The printed encyclopaedia gives 779 AH / 1387 CE, and the two dates do not match, so the Hijri year has been recorded and the Gregorian year derived from it. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.521} This is one source; an additional source is needed.
