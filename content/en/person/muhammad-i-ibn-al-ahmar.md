## Overview
Muhammad I ibn al-Ahmar, founder of the state of the [[group/bani-al-ahmar|Banu al-Ahmar]] in al-Andalus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1008-1009}

## Origins
Ibn Khaldun says that their origin is from Arjuna, one of the fortresses of Córdoba, and that they had an ancestor there among the sons of the soldiery known as the Banu Nasr, who trace their lineage to Sa'd ibn Ubada, lord of the Khazraj. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1008-1009}

## Rise
When the power of the Almohads weakened, he rose against Ibn Hud, and was given the oath of allegiance in 629 AH, with prayers for the amir Abu Zakariyya, lord of Ifriqiya. He was known as al-Shaykh and was titled Abu Dabbus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1008-1009}

Hayyan and Sharish obeyed him in 630 AH, and he entered Seville in 632 AH. Then he overcame Granada in 635 AH through the collusion of its people, and settled there after Ibn Hud's death. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1008-1009}
