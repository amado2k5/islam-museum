## Summary
al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Nizam al-Din al-Qummi al-Nisaburi, known as al-A'raj: a Qur'an commentator and jurist with interests in philosophy and mathematics. Ibrahim Atwa, in the introduction to his Gharayib al-Qur'an, places him among the scholars of the eighth century AH. His works include Gharayib al-Qur'an wa Raghaib al-Furqan, known as the Tafsir of al-Nisaburi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7165}
