## Overview
Maymun Abu Bishr, a client of Ali ibn Abdallah. Al-Baladhuri says that the caliph al-Mansur granted him an estate beside the Garden of the Priest, in the district of Bab al-Sham. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}

## The estate
According to al-Baladhuri, the "Tâqāt Bishr" are attributed to his son Bishr ibn Maymun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}
