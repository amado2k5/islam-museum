## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad Abu Zayd al-Tajuri, a scholar and jurist from Tajura near Tripoli in Libya, to which he is attributed. He died in 960 AH (1553 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
