## Summary
Ali ibn Abd al-Hamid al-Ghada'iri was one of the worshippers of [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]]. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 644 of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], describes him as tireless in worship and a man of high states and works, and says that he was counted among the abdal (the hidden saints). {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/366}

## With al-Sari al-Saqati
He relates that he knocked at the door of [[person/sari-al-saqati|al-Sari al-Saqati]] and heard him praying that God would occupy with Himself whoever distracted him from God. He says that by the blessing of that prayer he made forty pilgrimages on foot from Aleppo. The same report appears in al-Sari's own notice in the book. He is also the transmitter of al-Sari's saying that whoever claims an inner knowledge that overturns an outward ruling is mistaken. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/366} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/117} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/121}

## Transmission
Abu Nu'aym transmits through him a hadith on the merit of understanding in religion, which Ali relates from Sawwar ibn Abd Allah with his chain back to Mu'awiya. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/366}
