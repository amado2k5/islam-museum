## Overview
Muhammad ibn Bihar al-Hamdani is described as a man renowned for horsemanship and courage; the narrator heard him say, after [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] drew up his army outside [[place/saada|Sa'da]], described fighting and showed them how to thrust with a lance: "I never saw the like of Abu al-Husayn, and no one can find fault with him", meaning in his horsemanship. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} See [[event/al-hadi-march-from-saada-to-najran-284]]. The name stands as printed, a reading confirmed by re-reading the page image.
