## Overview
Ali ibn Baliq, one of the men of Mu'nis al-Muzaffar. Ibn al-Athir relates that al-Muqtadir met him on the day he was killed, when he was in flight; Ali dismounted, kissed the ground and asked him to turn back. It is also said that some men ordered him killed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.180}

Ibn al-Athir says that al-Qahir arrested him with his father and Mu'nis at the start of Sha'ban 321 AH, then he was slaughtered and his head was taken to his father. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.190-195}
