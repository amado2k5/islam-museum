## Overview
Sadaqa ibn Ali ibn Sadaqa ibn Dinar, a client (mawla) of the Azd. al-Baladhuri mentions him in Futuh al-Buldan in the account of Azerbaijan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The report
The book describes his conduct in Azerbaijan, together with al-Wajna' ibn al-Rawad, as mischief and corruption. He then fought the people of Armiya until he entered it and gained control of it. He and his brothers built palaces there {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
