## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ghalib, Abu Bakr al-Burqani: a hadith scholar of Khwarazm who settled in Baghdad and died there, between 336 and 425 AH (948–1034 CE). He never stopped writing until his death. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.212}

He compiled a Musnad containing what al-Bukhari and Muslim had included, gathering the hadith of Sufyan al-Thawri, Shu'ba, Ayyub and others; he also wrote al-Takhrij li-Sahih al-Hadith, a copy of which survives in the Chester Beatty library under number 3890. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.212}
