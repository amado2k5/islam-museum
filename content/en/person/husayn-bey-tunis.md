## Overview
Abu Muhammad Husayn ibn Mahmud ibn Muhammad al-Rashid ibn Husayn ibn Ali Turki (1778–1835 CE), Bey of Tunis. He was born there. {cite:al-aalam}

## His rule
Al-Zirikli reports that his father left him the management of its affairs, and his conduct was good. When his father died he assumed the rule alone in 1239 AH. He built a fleet and raised an army from the people of the realm. The robe of honour was sent to him from the Ottoman state in 1247 AH. Al-Zirikli describes him as a lover of good, with firmness, courage and forbearance. He died in office. {cite:al-aalam}
