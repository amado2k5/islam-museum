## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad b. Kamil b. Khalaf b. Shajara, the Baghdadi qadi — pupil of Muhammad b. Jarir al-Tabari and author of works in the arts; he held Kufa's judgeship, transmitted from Muhammad b. Sa'd al-'Awfi and a company, and lived ninety years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 2}

## The critics on him
Al-Daraqutni: "he would sometimes relate from memory what is not in his book — pride ruined him; he chose for himself and followed no one"; Ibn Razquya: "my eyes never saw his like." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 2}

## Student and biographer of al-Tabari
Ahmad Khalid Babakr counts Qadi Abu Bakr ibn Kamil (d. 350 AH) among the most prominent students of [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]], citing Ibn al-Nadim that he was well known in the Qur'anic sciences and wrote Gharib al-Qur'an, al-Qira'at and Mujaz al-Ta'wil fi Mu'jiz al-Tanzil, as well as a biography of al-Tabari most of which Yaqut reproduced in Mu'jam al-Udaba'. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.34}
