## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Isma'il ibn Abi Bakr al-Abshitay al-Qahiri, a jurist who was Shafi'i and then Hanbali, from Cairo. He was born in Abshit in al-Gharbiyya in Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.179-182}

## Dates
He was born in 802 AH (1399 CE) and died in 883 AH (1478 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.179-182}
