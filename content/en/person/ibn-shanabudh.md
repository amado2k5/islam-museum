## Overview
Ibn Shanabudh, a reciter of the Qur'an who is known for an important incident in 323 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.604}

## The incident
Al-Suyuti says that he was made to repent of reciting the irregular readings, and a record was written against him in the presence of the vizier [[person/ibn-muqla|Ibn Muqla]]. He relates that Ibn Shanabudh prayed against the vizier that God should cut off his hand, and the vizier's hand was cut off, and he tasted humiliation. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.604} He died in the days of al-Radi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.607}
