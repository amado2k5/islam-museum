## Overview
Qutlumush ibn Isra'il ibn Saljuq ibn Duqaq, the king Shihab al-Dawla the Turkmen Seljuk; father of Sulayman ibn Qutlumush, lord of Rum, whose line held the kingdom of Rum until Hulagu took it from them. He died in 456 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3086}
