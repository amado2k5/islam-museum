## Overview
Yahya ibn Zakariyya ibn Abi Za'ida, known by the kunya Abu Sa'id. He was a judge in al-Mada'in and died there in the year 183 AH, and he has the "Book of the Sunan" {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 316}.
