## Overview
Abu Qilaba Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn Amr al-Jarmi al-Azdi, one of the jurists among the Successors named in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha". He died in Syria in 106 or 107 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}

## What is reported of him
Al-Shirazi quotes Muslim ibn Yasar as saying: "If Abu Qilaba had been a non-Arab, he would have been the chief of chiefs of the Magians." He also relates that Abu Qilaba attended Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz and asked them about the qasama. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}

## Date of death
The date is disputed: the source gives two possible years, 106 or 107 AH, so it is recorded here with low confidence. It needs an additional source.
