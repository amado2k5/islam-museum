## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Bitriq, of al-Ma'mun's mission to the king of the Byzantines to seek the ancient sciences stored in Byzantine lands, together with al-Hajjaj ibn Matar and Salm, head of the House of Wisdom. He was among those who translated Greek books into Arabic {cite:duha-islam}.
