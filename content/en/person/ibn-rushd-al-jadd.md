## Summary
Ibn Rushd the Elder, qadi al-jama'a of Cordoba and imam of the Malikis in his time; of lofty isnad in al-Andalus, a vessel of knowledge with famous works; he lived seventy years and died about 520 — he is the grandfather of the jurist Ibn Rushd the Younger. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 540}
