## Overview
[[person/anas-ibn-al-nadr|Anas ibn al-Nadr al-Ansari al-Khazraji]], one of the steadfast fighters at [[event/battle-of-uhud|the battle of Uhud]], and known for his stand when the Muslims were thrown into confusion by the report that the Prophet had been killed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.262}

## His stand at Uhud
When the morale of some Muslims collapsed and some said the Prophet had been killed, Anas ibn al-Nadr passed by them as they had laid down their arms. He said: "What will you do with life after him? Stand up and die as the Messenger of God died." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.265}

## Notes for researchers
- The date of his death and the place of his martyrdom are not yet read in the local sources, so they are not set out here.
- The report is from "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum", which refers in it to the hadiths of al-Bukhari; we have not yet compared it with the text of al-Bukhari itself.
