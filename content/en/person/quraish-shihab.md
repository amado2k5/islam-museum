## Summary
Muhammad Quraish Shihab is an Indonesian exegete and one of Southeast Asia’s leading contemporary scholars, joining Azharite training to service of the Quran for Indonesia’s vast public. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-shihab}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1944 in Sulawesi, Indonesia, to a scholarly family of Hadhrami descent, he studied in Indonesian institutes then at al-Azhar in Cairo, taking a doctorate in Quranic sciences and tafsir. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-shihab}

## Career and works
He taught in Indonesia’s higher institutes and served as minister of religious affairs in 1998. His fifteen-volume Tafsir al-Mishbah is the largest contemporary Indonesian exegesis; his other works introduce Quranic sciences and apply verses to contemporary life. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-shihab}

## Method and contribution
His thematic, linguistic exegesis pairs verses with modern context in accessible language. He is celebrated as a bridge between the exegetical heritage and the contemporary Indonesian Muslim, with occasional debates over his social positions; the museum attributes these descriptions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-shihab}
