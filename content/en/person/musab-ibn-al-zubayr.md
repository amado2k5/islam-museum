## Overview
Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr, governor of Iraq for his brother Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr. A few dirhams were struck in his time, and they were broken afterwards.

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that Mus'ab gave security to a group and then killed them. He counted seven thousand men among those killed by him along with al-Mukhtar, whom he called the Khashabiyya. He brought before him the women of al-Mukhtar and called them to disown him; the daughter of Samura ibn Jundab disowned him, while the daughter of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir refused, and he killed her by execution. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.93}
