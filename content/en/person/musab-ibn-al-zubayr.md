## Overview
Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr, his brother Abd Allah's governor of Iraq; al-Mas'udi records that he gave them safe-conduct then killed them — seven thousand counted among those slain with al-Mukhtar, whom he called the wooden ones — and that he brought al-Mukhtar's womenfolk and bade them disavow him; Samra ibn Jundub's daughter did, while al-Nu'man ibn Bashir's daughter refused and was put to death.
