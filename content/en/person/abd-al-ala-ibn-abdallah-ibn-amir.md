## Overview
Abd al-Ala ibn Abdallah ibn Amir disputed a river with Harb ibn Salm ibn Ziyad, and the judge ruled in his favour. He then gave it to Harb and said that he would never take back what he had granted him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
