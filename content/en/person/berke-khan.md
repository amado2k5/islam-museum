## Overview
Berke Khan, a Tatar king, who became a Muslim and corresponded with the Mamluk sultan al-Zahir [[person/baybars|Baybars]]. Ibn al-Imad says that he defeated his cousin [[person/hulagu|Hulagu]] in a great battle. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.305}

Ibn Khaldun says that after he returned to Bukhara he met the shaykh al-Bakhuri of the companions of Najm al-Din Kubra, and became a Muslim at his hand. He asked his brother Mangu-Timur for leave to take over the affairs of the caliphate, and was given it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1536}
