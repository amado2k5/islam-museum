## Summary
Bakr ibn Muhammad, Abu Ahmad al-Nasa'i al-Baghdadi. Abu Bakr al-Khallal records that Ahmad ibn Hanbal honoured him, and that he kept many questions heard from Ahmad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8197}
