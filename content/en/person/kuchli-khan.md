## Overview
King of a nation of Tatars who settled on the borders of China, between it and Turkestan. There was enmity and war between them and the Khitai. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1530}

## With the Khitai and the Khwarazm-shah
He marched with his peoples against the Khitai to seize the opportunity against them, and sent to the Khwarazm-shah asking for victory over them. The Khwarazm-shah prepared, making each side think that he was on its side, and stayed apart from both. When they clashed, the Khitai were defeated and he turned with the Khwarazm-shah against them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1530}

## Afterwards
He seized Kashgar, the lands of Turkestan and Sagun. Then a group of the Mughal rose against him, led by their king [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]], and he was occupied with fighting them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1530}
