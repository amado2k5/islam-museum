## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Husayn al-Qurashi al-Nawfali al-Makki. He transmitted from al-Harith ibn Jumayla, al-Hasan al-Basri, Shahr ibn Hawshab, Tawus ibn Kaysan and Ata ibn Abi Rabah; Ahmad, al-Nasa'i and Abu Zur'a called him trustworthy; Ibn Abd al-Barr called him trustworthy by consensus, a jurist learned in the rites of pilgrimage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15395}
