## Overview
Musa ibn Ishaq al-Ansari, an Ansari judge. Al-Mas'udi says that he died in the caliphate of al-Muqtadir in 297 AH, and was buried on the eastern side. He was among the scholars of hadith and the great transmitters. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 juz' 4 p. 243}
