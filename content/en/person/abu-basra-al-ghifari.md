## Overview
Humayl ibn Basra ibn Waqqas ibn Hajib ibn Ghifar, Abu Basra al-Ghifari, a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949} He narrated from the Prophet and from Abu Dhar al-Ghifari. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949} Those who narrated from him include Tamim ibn Fara' al-Mahri, Abu al-Haytham Sulayman ibn Amr al-Utwari, Ubayd ibn Jabr, Amr ibn al-As and Abu Hurayra. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949} Al-Bukhari in al-Adab al-mufrad, Muslim, Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i included reports from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949}
