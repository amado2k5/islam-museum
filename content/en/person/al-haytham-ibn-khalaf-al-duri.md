## Overview
The hafiz Abu Muhammad al-Haytham ibn Khalaf al-Duri died in Baghdad in 307 AH, about 919 CE (derived conversion), and Ibn al-Imad lists him among the deaths of that year {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 37}.

## Life
Ibn al-Imad says he narrated from Ubaydallah ibn Umar al-Qawariri and others of his generation, compiled and wrote works, and was trustworthy {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 37}.
