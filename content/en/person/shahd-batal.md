# Shahd Batal

## Summary
Shahd Batal is a Sudanese-American content creator based in Los Angeles. She is known for modest fashion, beauty and lifestyle content, and for merging her modest styling with sneaker culture. {cite:hypebae-shahd-batal} {cite:refinery29-shahd-batal}

## Background
She began on YouTube making natural-hair tutorials. Around 2016 she decided to start wearing hijab — a choice she has described as turning back to her religion during a difficult period — and reworked her channel around skincare, makeup and lifestyle content. She moved from Minnesota to Los Angeles. {cite:refinery29-shahd-batal} {cite:hypebae-shahd-batal}

## Sneakers and style
She got into sneakers in her senior year of high school in 2014 and rebuilt her collection after adopting hijab, reasoning that streetwear is inherently modest; by 2021 her collection stood at around fifty pairs. Her channel continues with fashion, beauty and Ramadan lifestyle vlogs. {cite:hypebae-shahd-batal} {cite:youtube-shahdbatal}
