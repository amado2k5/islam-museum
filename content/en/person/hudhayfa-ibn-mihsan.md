## Overview
Hudhayfa ibn Mihsan, a commander among the commanders of the conquests. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## To Tikrit
Al-Baladhuri reports that al-Muthanna ibn Haritha sent him with al-Nasir after the day of the Bridge to Tikrit. The two attacked the Banu Taghlib and crossed over to Tikrit. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

From Utab ibn Ibrahim it is reported that the two of them granted safe conduct to the people of Tikrit and wrote them a letter, which Utba ibn Farqad sent when he conquered al-Tayrhan and Mosul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
