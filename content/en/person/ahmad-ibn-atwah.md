## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Atwa ibn Zayd al-Tamimi was one of the scholars of Najd, born in al-Uyayna in the land of al-Yamama, to which he is attributed; the heading of the entry calls him al-Uyayni. The entry dates his death to 948 AH, equal to 1541 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 265}.

## Career
He travelled to Damascus and stayed there for a time acquiring knowledge, then returned and died in his own town {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 265}. He gave many fatwas {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 265}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Rawda, al-Tuhfa, and Durar al-Fawa'id wa Uqyan al-Qala'id {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 265}.

## Death
He died in 948 AH, equal to 1541 CE, in his own town after returning from Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 265}.
