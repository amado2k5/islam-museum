## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur II, lord of Hama — Nasir al-Din Muhammad b. al-Malik al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din Mahmud, of the Ayyubid house; he took the kingdom after his father in 642 at ten years old, under the regency of his mother al-Sahiba, daughter of al-Kamil, and his rule lasted some forty years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 655}

## In al-'Ibar
Ibn al-Athir says in al-'Ibar: "he was a game-player, insistent on his affairs — God forgive him." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 655}
