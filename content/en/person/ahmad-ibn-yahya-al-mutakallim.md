## Overview
Abu Abd al-Rahman Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Mutakallim, one of the leading companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], who later became one of the companions of Ibn Abi Du'ad. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}

Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death. His birth date needs an additional source.
