## Summary

Shaykh of shaykhs Burhan al-Din Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Imad al-Din Umar ibn Muflih al-Hanbali al-Rumayni al-Dimashqi, the Hanbali mufti of Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 468}.

Ibn al-Shihna said of him: "He was a master of learning in Iraq, and he was prolific in it." He died at Maydi'a on Sunday, the sixteenth of Sha'ban in the year 917 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 468}.
