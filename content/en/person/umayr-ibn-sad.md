## Overview
[[person/umayr-ibn-sad|Umayr ibn Sa'd al-Ansari]], a Companion of the Ansar, whom [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] sent to the lands of the Byzantines in the year 21 AH {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.186}.

## The summer raid
Umar appointed him over the summer raid (al-sa'ifa), which was the first summer raid. He instructed him to deal gently with [[person/jabala-ibn-al-ayham|Jabala ibn al-Ayham]], and to invite him to return to the lands of Islam, on condition of paying the alms and remaining in his religion. Jabala refused to do anything but remain. Umayr reached a place known as al-Himar, a valley, and fell upon its people and laid it waste {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.186}.
