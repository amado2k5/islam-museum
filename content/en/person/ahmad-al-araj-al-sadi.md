## Summary
The Sa'di sultan in [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]]'s day {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 219}.

## Against Portugal and the Wattasids
He besieged Safi and Azemmour in 941/1534, and the cities nearly fell to him but for Portuguese relief; then he defeated his Wattasid rivals at [[event/battle-bir-uqba-943|Bi'r 'Uqba]] in 943/1536 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 219-221}.

## The truce
Answering a Portuguese overture, the two sides agreed a truce on 25 Dhu'l-Qa'da 944/25 April 1537 for three years with reciprocal trade — in al-Salabi's reading, the Portuguese aim was to prevent real cooperation between the Ottomans and the Sa'dis {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 222}.
