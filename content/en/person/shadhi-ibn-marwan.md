## Overview
Shadhi ibn Marwan, grandfather of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]]. He came from Dawin, at the far end of the district of Azerbaijan, and was of the Ruwadiyya Kurds, of the Hadhbaniyya branch {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 140}.

## His family
He took his two sons, Shirkuh and Ayyub, to Baghdad. Then they settled in Tikrit, and he died there, and there is a dome over his grave inside the town {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 140}.

Ibn Khallikan says that he traced their lineage at length and found no one who gave Shadhi another father. He cited a genealogy arranged by al-Harashi that links them to Adnan, and took it to be fabricated {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 140}.
