## Summary
Ed Husic (Edham Nurredin Husic) is an Australian Labor politician, the member for Chifley in western Sydney since 2010 and the first Muslim to serve in an Australian federal cabinet, appointed Minister for Industry and Science in June 2022. {cite:wikipedia-ed-husic} {cite:muslim500-ed-husic}

## Life and formation
He was born on 3 February 1970 in Sydney to Bosnian Muslim migrant parents, grew up in the city's west and studied applied science at the University of Technology Sydney. He worked as a union organiser for the Communication Workers Union and later for Integral Energy before entering parliament. {cite:wikipedia-ed-husic}

## Parliamentary career
Elected for Chifley at the 2010 federal election, he was one of the first two Muslims elected to the Australian parliament — sworn in on the Qur'an, a moment that drew both celebration and hostile commentary, which he answered with restraint. He chaired the house economics committee and developed a profile on cost-of-living scrutiny and technology policy before Labor returned to government in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-ed-husic}

## Minister for industry and science
In the Albanese government he took the industry and science portfolio, launching the National Reconstruction Fund, quantum and critical technologies strategies and a review of artificial intelligence regulation — positioning himself as a spokesman for manufacturing revival and responsible tech adoption. {cite:wikipedia-ed-husic} {cite:muslim500-ed-husic}

## Assessments
Admirers describe a measured, policy-minded minister who widened the space for Muslims in Australian public life; critics on the left have at times faulted his positioning within Labor's internal debates. The museum records both readings attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ed-husic}
