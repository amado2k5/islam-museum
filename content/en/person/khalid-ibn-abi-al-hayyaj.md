## Overview
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was the first to copy out Qur'an codices in the early period, and that he was praised for beautiful calligraphy. Ibn al-Nadim saw a codex in his hand. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 15-16}

## A codex for Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz
He wrote for Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz a codex that he perfected. Umar admired it, but thought its price too high, and returned it to him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 15-16}
