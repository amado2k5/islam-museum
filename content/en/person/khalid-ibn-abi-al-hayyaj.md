## Overview
Khalid ibn Abi al-Hayyaj, the first to write out Qur'an codices in the early period, famed for fine calligraphy; Ibn al-Nadim saw a codex in his hand, and one he wrote for Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz — so ornate that Umar admired it but sent it back, judging its price excessive. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 15-16}
