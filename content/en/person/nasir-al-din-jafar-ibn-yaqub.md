## Summary
Abu Muhammad Ja'far ibn Ya'qub al-Hamadhani, styled Nasir al-Din, was deputy of 'Imad al-Din Zangi, lord of the Jazira, Mosul and Syria. Zangi appointed him his lieutenant at Mosul, where he solidly rebuilt the city wall and dug its moats. In his tenure the imam al-Mustarshid came to besiege Mosul and pressed it hard for a time, then the caliph withdrew without exacting vengeance — in Ramadan 527. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
