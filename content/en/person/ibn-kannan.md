## Overview
Muhammad ibn 'Isa ibn Mahmud ibn Kannan, called Ibn Zayn al-Tuqat, was a historian, a Hanbali of the scholars of Damascus, born and died there in 1153 AH (1740 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.323}

His books include al-Hawadith al-Yawmiyya, a chronicle covering twenty-three years; al-Muruj al-Sundusiyya on the history of the Salihiyya quarter of Damascus; Hada'iq al-Yasamin on the morals of kings and caliphs; al-Iktifa' on the terminology of kings and caliphs; al-Mawakib al-Islamiyya describing Syria; a history of the madrasas of Damascus; an abridgement of Hayat al-Hayawan; and al-Durar al-Mundid fi Ashab al-Imam Ahmad, an abridgement of al-'Ulaymi's al-Minhaj al-Ahmad. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.323}
