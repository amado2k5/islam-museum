## Overview
Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Jisr al-Tarabulusi (d. 1909 CE), a man of learning from Tripoli in Syria, where he was born and died. {cite:al-aalam}

## His works
Al-Zirikli mentions his "Riyad Tarabulus" in ten volumes, in which he collected his articles; "Asira Muhadhdhab al-Din", which is a narrative; "Al-Kawakib al-Durriyya" in literature; and "Al-Risala al-Hamidiyya on the reality of the Islamic religion". {cite:al-aalam}
