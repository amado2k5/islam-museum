## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim 'Ali b. Ja'far b. 'Ali b. Muhammad b. 'Abdallah b. al-Husayn al-Shantarini al-Sa'di, known as Ibn al-Qatta' — Sicilian by birth, Egyptian by home and death, the philologist; one of the imams of adab, especially lugha. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Works and departure
His works: the *Kitab al-Af'al* — finer than Ibn al-Qutiyya's — *Abniyat al-asma'*, *al-Durra al-khatira* on the island's chosen poets, and *Lamh al-malh* gathering Andalusi poets, with a fine arud too; he left Sicily when the Franks neared its mastery and reached Egypt around 450. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
