# Salim Ismail

Salim Ismail (born 17 May 1965) is an Indian-born Canadian technology entrepreneur and author, best known as the founding executive director of Singularity University and the lead author of Exponential Organizations (2014). The English article and Wikidata give his birth on 17 May 1965 at Hyderabad, India; the Urdu article is a stub that files him under 1965 births. No Arabic edition of his article exists; this file's companion Arabic text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-salim-ismail-en} {cite:wikipedia-salim-ismail-ur} {cite:wikidata-salim-ismail}

## Early life and education

Ismail was born in Hyderabad and moved to Toronto at the age of ten. He studied at the University of Waterloo. {cite:wikipedia-salim-ismail-en}

## Career

Ismail co-founded the early social and technology companies PubSub Concepts and Confabb, and the location-aware social network Ångströ, which Google acquired in 2010. He led Brickhouse, Yahoo's internal incubator for new products. In 2008 he became the founding executive director of Singularity University, the Silicon Valley education venture. His book Exponential Organizations (2014), written with Michael S. Malone and Yuri van Geest, set out how organisations scale at the pace of information technology; he later founded ExO Works and the OpenExO platform to apply the model, and has served on the XPRIZE board and as chairman of Genius Group. {cite:wikipedia-salim-ismail-en}
