## Overview
Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan. Ibn al-Nadim says that he took care to bring out the ancient books on the craft, and that he was an eloquent poetic orator. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 497-498}

## The craft
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was the first for whom books of medicine, the stars and alchemy were translated. He is also reported to have said that he had desired the caliphate, but it was turned away from him, so he sought the craft so as to make his companions and family independent of the sultan's door. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 497-498}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim saw some five hundred leaves of his books and poems. Among his books are *Kitab al-Haraharat* (The Book of Heats), *al-Sahifa al-Kabir* (The Great Page), *al-Sahifa al-Saghir* (The Little Page), and his book of counsel to his son on the craft. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 497-498}
