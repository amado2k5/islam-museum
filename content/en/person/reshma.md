Reshma (c. 1947 – 3 November 2013) was a Pakistani folk singer born at the village of Loha near Bikaner in Rajasthan, into a nomadic Banjara household; her family moved to Karachi shortly after the Partition of India, when she was an infant.{cite:wikipedia-reshma-en} She received no formal education and sang as a child at the shrines of Sindh; at the age of twelve she was heard singing at the shrine of Lal Shahbaz Qalandar in Sehwan by the radio and television producer Saleem Gilani, who arranged her recording of Lal Meri Pat Rakhio for Radio Pakistan in 1968.{cite:wikipedia-reshma-en}{cite:wikipedia-reshma-ur}

Her powerful, untrained voice made her one of the most popular folk singers of Pakistan, with fame across the border in India: her songs include Laal Meri, Dama Dam Mast Qalandar, Hai O Rabba Nahion Lagda Dil Mera, Ankhiyan Nu Rehen De and Lambi Judai, the last used by Subhash Ghai in the film Hero (1983).{cite:wikipedia-reshma-en} She recorded for both the Pakistani and Indian film industries and performed abroad. She received the Pride of Performance in 1982 and the Sitara-i-Imtiaz in 2008.{cite:wikipedia-reshma-en}{cite:wikidata-reshma} Diagnosed with throat cancer in the 1980s, she died at Lahore on 3 November 2013.{cite:wikipedia-reshma-en}{cite:wikipedia-reshma-ur}
