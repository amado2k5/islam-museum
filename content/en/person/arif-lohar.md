Arif Lohar (born 18 April 1966) is a Pakistani Punjabi folk singer, the son of the renowned folk singer Alam Lohar, born in the village of Achh in Gujrat district.{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-en}{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-ur} Like his father, he sings accompanied by the chimta, and his music represents the traditional folk heritage of Punjab; with his seven brothers he began singing at a young age in a household formed by his father's art.{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-en}

He has recorded more than 3,000 songs, released over 150 albums and performed on more than fifty foreign tours, including in Britain, the United States and the Emirates.{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-en} His 2006 album 21st Century Jugni, and the song Jugni sung with Nooran Lal, made headlines in the Punjabi music world, and in 2010 his Coke Studio performance of Alif Allah (Jugni) with Meesha Shafi became an international success.{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-en} He has also acted in some forty-five Punjabi films. He received the Pride of Performance in 2005 and a Lifetime Achievement Award at the Brit Asia TV Music Awards in 2018.{cite:wikipedia-arif-lohar-en}{cite:wikidata-arif-lohar}
