## Overview
Letters came to [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] from al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Futaymi telling him what [[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]] had agreed and that some of the Yarsamis helped him, and Muhammad then went to al-Hasan ibn Ali with the narrator and two slaves. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} The text reads "al-Futaymi" and a second optical reading gives "al-Futayi"; the index lists "al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Futayma" at pp. 163, 78 and 61, without settling that the man is one in every page. See [[event/night-of-adha-at-saada-ibn-abbads-plan-and-the-prisoners-freed-286]].
