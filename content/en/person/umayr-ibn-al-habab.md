## Overview
Umayr ibn al-Habab al-Sulami, of the [[group/banu-sulaym|Banu Sulaym]]. al-Baladhuri reports his account of the conquest of the fortress of Kamakh {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}.

## Kamakh
He went out to the fortress with Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal in the year 59 AH. He climbed its wall and kept fighting on it alone until the Byzantines retreated and the Muslims ascended. It was opened to him, and he took pride in that. See [[event/conquest-of-kamkh|the conquest of Kamakh]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}.
