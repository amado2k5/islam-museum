## Overview
Abu Said al-Qarmati, leader of the Qarmatians in Bahrain, and the father of [[person/abu-tahir-al-qarmati|Abu Tahir Sulayman]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.575}

## In al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti says that he appeared in Bahrain in 286 AH, his power grew strong, and he raided Basra and its surroundings and defeated the caliph's army several times. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.575}

## In Ibn al-Jawzi
Ibn al-Jawzi relates that a Qarmatian known as Abu Said appeared in Jumada al-Akhira 287 AH. A group gathered to him, including Bedouins, and his followers multiplied. He killed the people of the villages around him, then went on to al-Qatif. It was said he meant Basra. Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Wathiqi wrote to the sultan, and a wall was built around Basra at a cost estimated at fourteen thousand dinars. Abu Said took control of Hajar and gave its people safe conduct. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.310}

## Notes for researchers
- The two sources differ on the year of his appearance (286 in al-Suyuti and 287 in Ibn al-Jawzi). The difference is not settled here and needs an additional source.
