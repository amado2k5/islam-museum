## Summary

Karim al-Din Abd al-Karim ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Umar ibn Muhammad al-Ju'bari, a reader of the Qur'an, an imam and a great scholar. He wrote the commentary on the Shatibiyya and well-known books on the readings of the Qur'an {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 586}.

He came to Damascus in the year 932, and the shaykh Shihab al-Din al-Tibi studied hadith with him and the works of Ibn al-Jazari. He died around the year 939 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 586}.
