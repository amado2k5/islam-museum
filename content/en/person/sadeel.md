## Overview
Sadeel, known by the handle SidSity, is a gaming streamer and content creator on YouTube and Twitch. Her profile on the stream-tracking site StreamRecorder describes her as a South Korean and Palestinian creator, a Muslim, who first went live in December 2021. {cite:streamrecorder-sidsity}

Her YouTube channel had about 179,000 subscribers by platform figures in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-sidsity}

## Career and work
Her YouTube content is gaming and livestreams, led by horror games such as Five Nights at Freddy's, Granny, Poppy Playtime and Roblox, while her Twitch streaming centres on Valorant alongside chat and IRL streams from Seoul. {cite:youtube-sidsity} {cite:streamrecorder-sidsity}

The StreamRecorder profile puts her Twitch following at over 86,000 — the platform figure as that profile presents it — and ranks her 437th among the platform's Valorant streamers. {cite:streamrecorder-sidsity}
