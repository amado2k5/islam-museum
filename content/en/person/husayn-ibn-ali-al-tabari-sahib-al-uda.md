## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn, Abu Abd Allah, al-Tabari, a Shafi'i jurist who settled in Mecca and was its hadith scholar and jurist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15820} He studied in Khurasan under Nasir al-Umari, in Baghdad under Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari, and with Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi until he excelled in the school and the disputes; he taught at the Nizamiyya in Baghdad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15820} His book al-Uda is a commentary on al-Ibana of al-Furani.
