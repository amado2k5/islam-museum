## Overview
Max Herz, known in the Encyclopaedia of Islam as Herz Bey, was an architect of the Comité de Conservation des Monuments de l'Art Arabe in Cairo, whose works on the Mamluk monuments of the city are still cited. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 693} Wikidata describes him as a Hungarian architect who lived from 1856 to 1919. {cite:wikidata}

This record is a short stub. The Encyclopaedia of Islam has no biographical article on him; it names him only in bibliographies and in a few remarks, and no other book read for the museum so far tells his life.

## Work on Cairo's monuments
The article on Cairo calls Max Herz Bey's monograph on the Sultan Hasan mosque the main account of that building's architectural history, and says that its interpretation had not significantly advanced since. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 452} The monograph is La mosquée du Sultan Hassan au Caire, Cairo 1899, published by the Comité de Conservation des Monuments de l'Art Arabe. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 693} He is also credited with the monuments map of Cairo issued by the Egyptian Department of Antiquities in 1914, which later maps superseded. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 459}

## Other publications named in the Encyclopaedia
The bibliographies list his study of the mosque of the emir Ghanim al-Bahlawan (Cairo 1908), an article on the mosque of Ezbek al-Yusufi in the Revue Egyptienne of 1899, critical observations on the basins in mosque courtyards (Bulletin de l'Institut d'Egypte, 1896), and an article on Arab arms and armour in the Bulletin de l'Institut francais d'archeologie orientale in 1910. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 460} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 648} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 308} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 817} He also edited the index to the Comité's reports up to 1914, published in Cairo in 1915. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 460}
