## Overview
Usama ibn Zayd al-Tanukhi, in charge of the land tax of Egypt in the time of Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik. He is not the Companion Usama ibn Zayd {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.202}.

## Dismissal and return
The book describes him as tyrannical and oppressive, cutting off hands in punishments without the conditions for amputation being met. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz dismissed him and ordered him imprisoned, one year in Egypt and one year in Palestine. Then Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik restored him to his office after Umar's death {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.202}.

The book also mentions him among the governors whose financial authority was separated from their administrative authority {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.435-436}.
