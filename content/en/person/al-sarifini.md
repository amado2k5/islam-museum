## Overview
Al-Sarifini, the Hanbali hafiz and jurist resident at Damascus; he heard Ibn al-Akhdar and Ibn Tabrazad at Baghdad, then travelled the lands, hearing at Isfahan, Nishapur, Herat, Bushanj, Dinawar, Nahawand, Tustar, Tus, Mosul, Damascus, Jerusalem and Harran, and read fiqh at Baghdad under Ibn al-Tarikhi and Abu'l-Baqa' al-'Ukbari. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 218}

## His standing
Shaykh of the Dar al-Hadith of Manbij, then holder of the Dar al-Hadith endowed by the Sahib Ibn Shaddad at Aleppo; al-Birzali called him "a pious trustworthy hafiz," Abu Shama "learned in hadith, devout, humble." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 218}
