## Overview
Dawud ibn Abi Hind al-Basri, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the delegation of Sulayman ibn Ali to al-Mansur concerning the matter of the Subaybiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His teachers
The book Tabaqat al-fuqaha says that he studied under [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], [[person/muhammad-ibn-sirin|Ibn Sirin]] and [[person/said-ibn-al-musayyab|Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
