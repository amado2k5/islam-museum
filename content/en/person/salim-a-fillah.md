## Summary
Salim Akhukum Fillah, whose birth name is Arif Nursalim and who writes as Salim A. Fillah (born 21 March 1984), is an Indonesian Islamic book writer and preacher from Yogyakarta. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} He became widely known after publishing Nikmatnya Pacaran Setelah Pernikahan ("The Pleasure of Courtship After Marriage", 2003), and he is an activist of Masjid Jogokariyan in Yogyakarta, where he leads its Majelis Jejak Nabi study circle on the Prophet's biography. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} {cite:cerdika-salim-a-fillah-2025}

## Early life and education
He has loved books since childhood, and his writing life began while he was a student at SMA Teladan in Yogyakarta. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} He studied electrical engineering at Gadjah Mada University and psychology at the State Islamic University Sunan Kalijaga, but did not complete either course. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} The Indonesian article's infobox places his birth at Kulon Progo, in the Special Region of Yogyakarta, while the 2025 journal study and his Wikidata item give Yogyakarta; the date, 21 March 1984, is the same in all three. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} {cite:cerdika-salim-a-fillah-2025} {cite:wikidata-salim-a-fillah}

## Books and work
His first book, Nikmatnya Pacaran Setelah Pernikahan (2003), was also the first book published by Pro-U Media, the publishing house he founded. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} The article lists his works: Agar Bidadari Cemburu Padamu (2004), Gue Never Die (2005), Barakallahu Laka: Bahagianya Merayakan Cinta (2005), Saksikan Bahwa Aku Seorang Muslim (2007), Jalan Cinta Para Pejuang (2008), Dalam Dekapan Ukhuwah (2010), Menyimak Kicau Merajut Makna (2012), Lapis-lapis Keberkahan (2014), Menggali ke Puncak Hati (2015), Rihlah Dakwah (2016), Bersamamu di Jalan Dakwah Berliku (2016), Sunnah Sedirham Surga (2017), #Mncrgknskl (2018), the historical novel Sang Pangeran dan Janissary Terakhir (2019), Ketika Aku dan Kamu menjadi Kita (2022) and Kisah-Kisah Pahlawan Nusantara (2023). {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id}

At Masjid Jogokariyan he serves on the mosque's management and leads the Majelis Jejak Nabi, its study circle on the Prophet's biography (sira). {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id} {cite:cerdika-salim-a-fillah-2025} His dawah specialises in Islamic history: a 2025 journal study examines how he transmits Islamic-history material on YouTube to a wide audience, including young people. {cite:cerdika-salim-a-fillah-2025} He has twice been widely reported for open letters: the first to Prabowo Subianto, the Gerindra candidate in the 2014 presidential election, and the second to Vice President Jusuf Kalla. {cite:wikipedia-salim-a-fillah-id}
