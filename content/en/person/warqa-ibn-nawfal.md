## Overview
Warqa b. Nawfal, commander of al-Muqtadir bi'llah; in 303 he fell upon the Arabs of the al-Ajfar district. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 418}

## The attack
He killed a body of them, captured another and brought them into Baghdad — where the mob fell on the prisoners and killed them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 418}
