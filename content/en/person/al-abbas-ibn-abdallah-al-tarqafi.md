## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Isa, Abu Muhammad al-Tarqafi, was a narrator of hadith. Al-Safadi fixes the spelling of his nisba: a ta with fatha, then ra, then a qaf with damma, then fa; he adds the further nisba al-Baksa'i {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 16 p. 375}.

## His reputation and death
Al-Safadi quotes al-Khatib al-Baghdadi as saying he was reliable, righteous and devout, and notes that Ibn Majah narrated from him. He died in 267 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 16 p. 375}; the Gregorian equivalent is derived, about 880 CE.
