## Summary
Amir Burhan al-Din al-Husayni al-'Alawi, naqib of the ashraf at Damascus; advanced in the Prophetic house as was the government's custom. A man wronged him when he sought to imprison him — Sultan Qaytbay released him and struck off the iron collar from his neck, such collars being for the People of the House; he made the hajj, held the awqaf, judgeship and regency several times, and pilgrimaged with the mahmal in 819. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}
