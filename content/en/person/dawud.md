## Overview
Dawud, a prophet of the Children of Israel and the father of [[person/sulayman|Sulayman]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## In hadith
Al-Bukhari reports that the Prophet said to [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]], for the beauty of his recitation of the Qur'an: "You have been given a flute of the flutes of the family of Dawud." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5048}
