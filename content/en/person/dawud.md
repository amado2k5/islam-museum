## Overview
Dawud, a prophet of the Children of Israel and the father of [[person/sulayman|Sulayman]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## In hadith
Al-Bukhari reports that the Prophet said to [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]], for the beauty of his recitation of the Qur'an: "You have been given a flute of the flutes of the family of Dawud." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5048}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh holds that Dawud was a prophet, messenger, king and rich man, that his voice in the Psalms was so beautiful that water stopped flowing in the river and the birds and mountains echoed him, that God softened iron for him, and yet the family of Dawud were told 'work in gratitude'; he cites this for the dignity of work (needs_source: the water stopping as the Sheikh tells it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:06:30}
