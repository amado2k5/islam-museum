## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Zayd al-Sakuni by clientage (bi-l-wala'), Abu Ja'far al-Bazanti, was a man of learning from Kufa. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 221 AH, 836 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 203}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he met the two Imams al-Rida and Abu Ja'far and held a great standing with them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 203}. The museum notes, outside the text of the entry, that the word imam here follows the Imami (Twelver Shi'i) usage and not the Sunni usage.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Jami' and al-Nawadir {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 203}.
