## Overview
Ata ibn Yasar, a client of Maymuna the wife of the Prophet, kunya Abu Muhammad. Al-Mas'udi says that he died in the year 103 AH in the days of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, at the age of eighty-four. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191}

Ibn al-Nadim's "Fihrist" lists under his name the number of the surahs, verses, words and letters of the Qur'an. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.68-69}

The attribution of this counting to him needs an additional source.
