## Overview
Al-Aswad ibn Sari al-Tamimi, said to have laid out the mosque of Basra, and the first to judge in it. Al-Baladhuri relates that Mujashi and Mujalid, the two sons of Mas'ud, told him that he had brought disgrace upon himself, and he answered them: "I will not return." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Hakim's Mustadrak
In the Book of Knowing the Companions, [[person/al-hakim-al-nisaburi|al-Hakim]] quotes [[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] for his lineage, al-Aswad ibn Sari' ibn Himyar ibn 'Ubada ibn al-Nazzal ibn Murra ibn 'Ubayd; for his house in Basra by the congregational mosque, on the side of Banu Tamim; and for his death in the time of Mu'awiya. Al-Dhahabi agrees. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 712}

Al-Hakim also gives, through al-Hasan, al-Aswad's words to the Prophet: "Shall I not recite to you praises with which I have praised my Lord?" The Prophet said: "Your Lord loves praise", and asked for no more. Al-Hakim calls it sound in chain, and al-Dhahabi agrees. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 712} In another version, through 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakra, he said: "I have composed a poem praising God and praising you"; its chain includes Ma'mar ibn Bakkar, of whom al-Dhahabi says: "he has objectionable reports". {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 712}
