## Overview
Al-Aswad ibn Sari al-Tamimi, said to have laid out the mosque of Basra, and the first to judge in it. Al-Baladhuri relates that Mujashi and Mujalid, the two sons of Mas'ud, told him that he had brought disgrace upon himself, and he answered them: "I will not return." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
