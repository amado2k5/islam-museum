## Overview
Ali ibn Sulayman ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas, of the Abbasid family. Al-Baladhuri mentions him as governor of al-Jazira and Qinnasrin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The city of al-Hadath
He built the city of al-Hadath from mud-brick, and it was named al-Muhammadiyya. Al-Mahdi died in 169 AH, when they had just finished building it, so it is also called al-Mahdiyya and al-Hamdiyya. The data link this with [[event/founding-of-al-hadath|the founding of al-Hadath]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri relates from Abu al-Khattab that he assigned four thousand people to it and settled them there, and that two thousand men were moved to it from Malatya, Shamshat, Samisat, Kaysum, Duluk and Ra'ban. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Raqqa
He had come before that as governor of al-Jazira, and he moved the markets of al-Raqqa to the open space between al-Raqqa and al-Rafiqa (see [[event/founding-of-rafiqa|the founding of al-Rafiqa]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His dismissal
Al-Hadi dismissed him and appointed Muhammad ibn Ibrahim in his place. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
