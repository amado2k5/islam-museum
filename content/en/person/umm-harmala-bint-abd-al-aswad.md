## Summary
Wife of Jahm ibn Qays; she emigrated with him to Abyssinia and perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her as the wife of Jahm ibn Qays and says she died in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives her her own entry in al-Isaba, no. 12015: "Umm Harmala bint Abd al-Aswad ibn Khuzayma ibn Aqish ibn Amir ibn Bayada al-Khuza'iyya", referring back to her earlier mention in the entry on Khalid. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1990}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- She was not named in the list of the sixteen women (pp.10-11) though named here; the count of women does not line up with everyone covered in the earlier sections.
- Al-Isaba gives her a Khuza'i lineage, while Ibn Ishaq places her among Banu Abd al-Dar; both attributions stand as reported.
