## Summary
Wife of Jahm ibn Qays; she emigrated with him to Abyssinia and perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her as Jahm ibn Qays's wife and says she perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- She is not named in the list of the sixteen women (pp. 10-11) despite being mentioned here; the count of women does not reconcile neatly with everyone listed in the earlier sections.
