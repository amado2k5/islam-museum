## Overview
Abu Ali Isma'il ibn al-Qasim al-Qali, the philologist, who died in 356 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 623}

## His "Amali"
He composed his "Amali" in Cordoba after 330 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 623} See [[work/amali-al-qali|the Amali of al-Qali]].

## Notes for researchers
- The Gregorian date of death is derived and approximate. The year of birth is not recorded and needs an additional source.
