## Overview
Muhammad ibn Nafi' al-Khuza'i, a transmitter mentioned in the report of the uprooting of the Black Stone. Al-Suyuti, in the *History of the Caliphs*, relates that he said: "I looked at the Black Stone while it was uprooted, and the blackness was only on its head, and the rest was white, and its length was the size of a forearm bone." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.616}

The data do not record his full lineage or his year of death, and need an additional source.
