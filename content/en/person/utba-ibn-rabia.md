## Brief
Utba ibn Rabi'a was an elder of Banu Abd Shams who argued for buying off the Prophet rather than fighting him — the opposition's negotiator, killed with the rest of the elite at Badr.

## Life
The sira gives Utba its most interesting scene of attempted settlement: sent by the elders to offer Muhammad wealth, kingship and a physician if only he would stop, he sat and listened, returned to the council, and — the sources say — warned them this man was no madman and they would regret fighting him {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}.

At Badr the tradition has him counsel retreat even as the army marched; he died there with the elite of Quraysh {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}. His daughter was [[person/hind-bint-utba|Hind]], later the wife of [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] — the family's own history carrying the whole arc of the war.

## Legacy
Utba stands for the opposition that talked: the path not taken at Badr, preserved by the sources as a real alternative, argued and lost.

## For young visitors
Utba tried to make a deal instead of a war. The deal failed, the war happened, and he died in it — with his daughter's family on both sides of the story.
