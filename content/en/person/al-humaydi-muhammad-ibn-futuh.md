## Overview
Muhammad ibn Futuh ibn Abd Allah al-Azdi, known as the memoriser al-Humaydi, a historian, man of letters, jurist and hadith scholar. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.288}

## His life
He followed the Zahiri school, and he was devoted to learning and ascetic. He was born in 420 AH (1029 CE) and died in 488 AH (1095 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.288}
