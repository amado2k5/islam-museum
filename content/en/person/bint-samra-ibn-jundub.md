## Overview
The daughter of Samura ibn Jundab, one of the wives of [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd]]. [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Musab ibn al-Zubayr]] offered her to the sword so that she would disown him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.93}

Al-Mas'udi relates that she withdrew from his position and cursed him. What happened to her afterwards is not in the entity's data and needs an additional source. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.93}
