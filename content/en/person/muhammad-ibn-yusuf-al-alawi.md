## Overview
Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Alawi. He succeeded his brother Yusuf ibn Isma'il over Mecca after his death in 252 AH, and was twenty years older than him. Al-Mas'udi says that people suffered great hardship because of him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.551}

## Al-Mu'tazz's response
Al-Mu'tazz sent Abu al-Saj al-Ashrusani to the Hijaz, and Muhammad ibn Yusuf fled, and a multitude of his companions were killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.551}
