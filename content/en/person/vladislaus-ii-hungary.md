## Summary
Vladislaus II Jagiellon, king of Hungary in [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]]'s reign {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201-202}.

## The slain envoy
Al-Salabi records that he resolved to annul every undertaking his forebears had given the Ottoman sultans, going so far as to kill Süleyman's envoy sent to claim the yearly tribute; Süleyman answered in 1521 with the great campaign that ended in the victory of [[event/battle-mohacs-932|Mohács]] in 1526 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201-202}.
