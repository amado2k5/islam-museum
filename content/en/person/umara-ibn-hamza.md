## Overview
Umara ibn Hamza, secretary and client of Abu Ja'far [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.171}.

## His description
Ibn al-Nadim describes him as proud, generous, eloquent and one-eyed. al-Mansur and al-Mahdi advanced him and bore with his manners for his excellence and eloquence, and he held great offices for them both {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.171}.

## His letters
He has collected letters, among them the letter of the army which upholds the Banu al-Abbas {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.171}.
