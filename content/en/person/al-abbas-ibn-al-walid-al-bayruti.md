## Summary
Abu al-Fadl al-Abbas ibn al-Walid al-Bayruti al-Udhri of Syria was an imam of Qur'an recitation and a companion of Abd al-Hamid ibn Bakkar. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.30} Ahmad Khalid Babakr cites Ibn Hajar that he was born in 169 AH and died in 270 AH, so that he passed a hundred years. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.30} He is not to be confused with the Umayyad commander al-Abbas ibn al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik.

## With al-Tabari
When [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] left Baghdad for Syria he settled for a time in Beirut and met al-Abbas ibn al-Walid; Ibn Asakir says al-Tabari read the Qur'an with him and took the readings (huruf) from him. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.30} From Beirut al-Tabari went on to Egypt. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.31}
