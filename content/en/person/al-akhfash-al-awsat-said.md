## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Sa'id ibn Mas'ada, a grammarian of Basra of Khwarazmian origin, and a pupil of Sibawayh. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His title
He is called al-Awsat to distinguish him from the other Akhfashes. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 215 AH (830 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} The date rests on tradition and needs an additional source.
