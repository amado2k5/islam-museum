## Summary
al-Farrar al-Jar is an epithet, meaning the wanderer and the neighbour who never settles; the notice does not give his name. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 518, sequence 519, on page 153. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}

## Never settling
Amr ibn Uthman al-Makki relates that he met a man wandering among the villages of Egypt, and asked: why do I see you never settle in one place? He answered: how can one settle in one place who is sought? I said: are you not in His grip everywhere? He said: yes, but I fear to make a home of the lands, lest He take me unawares in the security of settling, among the deluded. A word describing the fear is unclear in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}
