## Summary
Abu'l-Mutawwaj Muqallad ibn Nasr ibn Munqidh al-Kinani, styled Mukhlis al-Dawla — father of Amir Sadiq al-Dawla Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali, lord of Shayzar castle, and from him came the Banu Munqidh lords of Shayzar. He was a man modest in standing and modest in fame. He and his family lived near Shayzar castle, moving between Aleppo and Hama; theirs were noble souls and prized estates — Syria's kings honored and venerated them, and their age's poets sought them out and praised them. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
