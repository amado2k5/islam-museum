## Overview
Yusuf ibn Yusuf ibn Salama ibn Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Hashimi al-Abbasi, Abu al-Mahasin Muhyi al-Din al-Mawsili, known as Ibn Zaylaq, was an accomplished poet and learned man, the chancery secretary of Mosul, where the Tatars killed him when they took the city in 660 AH (1262 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.259}

Ibn Shakir quoted fine selections of his verse in al-Fawat, and Ibn al-Fuwati noted that he left epistles and poems. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.259}
