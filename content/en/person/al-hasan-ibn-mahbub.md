## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Mahbub al-Sarrad, or al-Zarid, Abu Ali, was an Imami jurist, that is of the Imami Shi'a, as the entry describes him, and a native of Kufa. The entry gives his birth as 149 AH, equal to 766 CE, and his death as 224 AH, equal to 839 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Nawadir, of about a thousand leaves; al-Tafsir; al-Fara'id; al-Mashyakha; and al-Hudud {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## Death
He died in 224 AH, equal to 839 CE, according to the entry, which does not name the place of death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.
