## Overview
A man of the people of hadith, known by this nickname ("Sajjada", the prayer mat). al-Suyuti reports that he refused during the [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|Mihna of the createdness of the Qur'an]], was put in shackles, and then gave the answer required {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.490,492-493}.

## The caliph's criticism
In al-Ma'mun's letter he is faulted for being so occupied with preparing date pits for his prayer mat that it distracted him from monotheism. This is al-Ma'mun's own words, as al-Suyuti transmits them {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.490,492-493}.
