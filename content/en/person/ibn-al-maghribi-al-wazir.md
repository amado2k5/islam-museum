## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim Husayn b. 'Ali al-Shaybi, known as Ibn al-Maghribi the vizier; when al-Hakim of Egypt killed his father, uncle and brothers he fled to Hassan b. Mufarrij al-Ta'i, praised him and was honoured — then became vizier to Ahmad b. Marwan al-Kurdi, lord of Mayyafariqin; fine poetry and several works, he lived forty-eight years and was among the craftiest and cleverest of men. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 210}
