## Summary
Naseem Shah is a Pakistani international cricketer, a right-arm fast bowler who debuted in Test cricket at sixteen and became one of the leading pace talents of his generation, known for late swing, pace and a match-winning temperament forged early in Australia's hardest conditions. {cite:wikipedia-naseem-shah}

## Life and formation
He was born on 15 February 2003 in Mayar, in the Lower Dir district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Spotted young, he moved to Lahore for coaching and was fast-tracked through Pakistan's age-group and domestic cricket, making his first-class debut for Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited in 2018 barely into his teens. {cite:wikipedia-naseem-shah}

## International career
Picked for the 2019 tour of Australia, he made his Test debut at Brisbane aged sixteen years and nine months, and in the same tour at Karachi against Sri Lanka took a Test hat-trick — then the youngest bowler to do so. He added one-day and T20 international debuts, was instrumental in Pakistan's home Test series wins, and bowled the closing overs of the 2022 Asia Cup win over Afghanistan. Injuries — notably a shoulder problem — interrupted him through 2023–24, costing him a World Cup. {cite:wikipedia-naseem-shah}

## Franchise and domestic cricket
He has played in the Pakistan Super League for Quetta Gladiators and Islamabad United, and in overseas leagues including county and Caribbean cricket, returning to international duty as fitness allowed. {cite:wikipedia-naseem-shah}

## Assessments
Coaches and commentators praise his natural wrist position and his appetite for hard spells; critics note the workload management issues that come with teenage pace. The museum records the assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-naseem-shah}
