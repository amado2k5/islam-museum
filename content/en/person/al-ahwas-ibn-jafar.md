## Overview
Al-Ahwas ibn Ja'far ibn Kilab, a poet of the Banu Amir. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40}

## The day of Rahrahan
Al-Harith ibn Zalim killed his brother Khalid. So al-Ahwas went out with the Banu Amir, heading for the Banu Darim, seeking from al-Harith the blood of his brother. They met at Rahrahan and fought. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40}
