## Overview
Al-Simt ibn al-Aswad al-Kindi, a commander in the army of Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah in the conquest of Syria. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the conquests
Abu Ubayda appointed him over [[event/conquest-of-hims|Homs]] after it was conquered, though it is said the governor was Sharahbil ibn al-Simt. Then he sent him to Qinnasrin when its people broke the covenant; he besieged them and took it, [[event/conquest-of-qinnasrin|the conquest of Qinnasrin]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ubada ibn Nusayy related from Abd al-Rahman ibn Ghanm that he garrisoned at Qinnasrin with al-Simt, and that al-Simt divided among them a share of the cattle and sheep he had gained, and put the rest in the spoils. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
