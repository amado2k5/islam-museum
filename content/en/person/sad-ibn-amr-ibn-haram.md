## Overview
Sa'd ibn Amr ibn Haram al-Ansari was sent by Ammar ibn Yasir, governor of Kufa for Umar, at the head of an army to raid the territory beyond al-Anbar, according to al-Baladhuri {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.246}.

## Hit
The people of the fortresses of the Euphrates came to him as far as Hit and asked for safe conduct, and he granted it to them. He made an exception for the people of Hit, in that half of their church was to remain {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.246}. See [[event/conquest-of-hit|the conquest of Hit]].

It is also said that Madhlaj ibn Amr al-Sulami had been there before him, and that he was the one who took charge of its conquest and built al-Hadithah {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.246}.
