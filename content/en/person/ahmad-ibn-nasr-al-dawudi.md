## Summary
Ahmad ibn Nasr al-Dawudi al-Asadi, Abu Ja'far, a Maliki jurist among the leading masters of the school in the Maghrib, known for learning and for good writing; he studied largely on his own and did not follow a famous master in most of his learning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16644} Abu Abd Allah al-Buni, Abu Bakr ibn al-Shaykh Ibn Abi Zayd and Abu Ali ibn al-Wafa transmitted from him; his works include al-Nami fi sharh al-Muwatta', al-Wa'i in fiqh, al-Nasiha on the Sahih of al-Bukhari, and al-Idah refuting the Qadariyya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16644}
