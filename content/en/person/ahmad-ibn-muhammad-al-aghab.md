## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab, whom al-Baladhuri mentions in "Futuh Sicily". In the caliphate of [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] he conquered the palace of Yanna and the fortress of Ghaliyana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

He is linked with the event of [[event/aghabid-conquests-sicily|the Aghlabid conquests in Sicily]].

The dates of his life need an additional source.
