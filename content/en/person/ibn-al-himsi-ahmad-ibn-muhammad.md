## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Umar, known as Ibn al-Himsi. He was of Homs origin, and was born there in 841 AH (1437 CE) and died there in 934 AH (1527 CE). The encyclopaedia says he took part in the sciences of his age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
