## Overview
Zayn al-Din ibn Ali ibn Ahmad al-Amili al-Juba'i was an Imami, that is, a scholar of the Twelver Shi'a, described by al-Zirikli as a scholar of hadith and a tireless researcher. His entry in al-A'lam is headed al-Shahid al-Thani. He was born in 911 AH, 1505 CE, and died in 966 AH, 1559 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 63}.

## His travels
He was born at Jaba in Lebanon and went to Mays, from there to Karak Nuh, and then made for Egypt, the Hijaz, Iraq and the lands of Rum. He stayed some months in Istanbul and was appointed teacher at the Nuriyya school in Baalbek, and went there; an informer denounced him to the sultan, who summoned him, and he returned to Istanbul under guard. The man guarding him killed him and brought his head to the sultan, who had the killer put to death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 63}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Munyat al-Murid fi Adab al-Mufid wa-l-Mustafid, printed; al-Iqtisad fi Ma'rifat al-Mabda' wa-l-Ma'ad, in manuscript; al-Iman wa-l-Islam wa-Bayan Haqiqatihima, printed; Ghunyat al-Qasidin fi Istilah al-Muhaddithin; Manar al-Qasidin fi Asrar Ma'alim al-Din; al-Rijal wa-l-Nasab; a verse composition on grammar; a commentary on al-Shara'i' in seven volumes; and a commentary on the Alfiyya in grammar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 63}. In jurisprudence he lists Rawd al-Jinan, al-Rawda al-Bahiyya, Masalik al-Afham ila Shara'i' al-Islam and Kashf al-Riba an Ahkam al-Gheeba, all printed, and many treatises on jurisprudence, some of them printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 63}.
