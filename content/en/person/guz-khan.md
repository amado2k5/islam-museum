## Summary
Guz Khan (born Ghulam Dastgir Khan) is a British comedian and actor from Coventry. A humanities teacher before comedy, he went viral in 2015 as his character Mobeen, then created and starred in the BBC comedy series Man Like Mobeen, which ran from 2017 to 2025, and has acted in Taskmaster, Our Flag Means Death and The Gentlemen. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en}

## Life
Khan was born on 24 January 1986 in Coventry, in the West Midlands, to Pakistani Punjabi immigrant parents; the English article gives the date in full and Wikidata agrees on the year. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} {cite:wikidata-guz-khan} He is the youngest of three children, with two sisters ten and eleven years older, and his father died when he was three. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-ar} He grew up on a housing estate in Hillfields, Coventry, attended Stoke Park School and graduated from Coventry University, then taught humanities at Grace Academy in Coventry. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} The English article records that he is a Muslim, and the Arabic article likewise describes him as a Muslim of Pakistani Punjabi origin; he lives with his wife and three children in the West Midlands. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-ar}

## Work
Khan uploaded his first video to Facebook in 2014 and first performed on stage the same year, opening for Aamer Rahman at Birmingham Repertory Theatre, performing at first under the stage name "Guzzy Bear". {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} In June 2015, performing as Mobeen, a Muslim living in Small Heath, Birmingham, he made the "Pakisaurus" video, mock-protesting the film Jurassic World because the dinosaurs' cry sounded like a racial slur; the video was watched hundreds of thousands of times within days and made his name. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} The same month he made the short film Roadman Ramadan for the BBC's British Muslim Comedy series, in which Mobeen guides a newly converted friend through his first Ramadan, and in July 2015 he left teaching to pursue comedy full-time. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} His four-part BBC comedy Man Like Mobeen launched on BBC iPlayer in December 2017 and ran to five series by 2025. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en} He has since performed on Live at the Apollo, appeared in series 12 of Taskmaster in 2021, played Ivan in the first season of Our Flag Means Death in 2022, and played Chucky "The Cobra" Kubra in the Netflix series The Gentlemen in 2024. {cite:wikipedia-guz-khan-en}
