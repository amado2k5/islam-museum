## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Wazir al-Hafiz al-Dimashqi, a poet. Al-Mas'udi says that he recited at the table of the caliph al-Mustakfi verses describing *al-Arziyya*, a dish of rice. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 714-715}
