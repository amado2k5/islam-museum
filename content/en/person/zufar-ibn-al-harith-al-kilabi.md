## Overview
Zufar ibn al-Harith al-Kilabi, a horseman and poet of the Banu Kilab. He was with al-Dahhak ibn Qays at Marj Rahit. When the sword went deep among his people, he turned and left his two companions of the Banu Sulaym, who were killed, and he composed verses on that {cite:muruj-mari-2005 p.77}.
