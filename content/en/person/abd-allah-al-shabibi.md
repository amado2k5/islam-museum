## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf, Abu Muhammad al-Balawi al-Shabibi of Qayrawan, a jurist. He studied with Abu al-Hasan al-Awwani, Abu Imran al-Manawi, Abu Abd Allah al-Ghallal and Muhammad al-Haskuri; Abu al-Qasim ibn Najji, al-Barzali and Abu Hafs al-Masrati heard him. He taught for about thirty-five years. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8200}
