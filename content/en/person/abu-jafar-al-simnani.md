## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Abu Ja'far al-Simnani, the qadi. Ibn al-Jawzi lists him in al-Muntazam among those who died in 444 AH {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 15 p. 337}. He was born in 361 AH and settled in Baghdad {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 15 p. 337}.

## Teachers and learning
He transmitted from Ali ibn Umar al-Sukkari, al-Daraqutni, Ibn Habbaba and others, and Ibn al-Jawzi calls him learned, virtuous and generous {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 15 p. 337}.

## His school and his gathering
Ibn al-Jawzi says that in the fundamentals he held the Ash'ari position, and that he kept a gathering for debate in his house {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 15 p. 337}.

## Death
He died in Rabi' I of 444 AH in Mosul, where he was qadi, after he lost his sight {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 15 p. 337}.
