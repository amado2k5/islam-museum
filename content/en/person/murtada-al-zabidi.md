## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Razzaq al-Husayni al-Zabidi, Abu al-Fayd, surnamed Murtada, is described by al-Zirikli as a great scholar of language, hadith, the study of transmitters and genealogy, and one of the great authors. The entry gives his years as 1145 to 1205 AH, 1732 to 1790 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His life
His family came from Wasit in Iraq, he was born in India at Bilgram and raised in Zabid in Yemen. He travelled to the Hijaz and settled in Egypt, where his learning became famous and gifts poured in on him, and the rulers of the Hijaz, India, Yemen, Syria, Iraq, the far Maghrib, the Turks, the Sudan and Algeria wrote to him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. Al-Zirikli says people's regard for him grew so far that many in the Maghrib claimed a pilgrim who did not visit al-Zabidi and give him something had not completed his pilgrimage {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He was good in Turkish and Persian and knew some of the Georgian tongue {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He died of the plague in Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «Taj al-arus fi sharh al-Qamus», printed in ten volumes; «Ithaf al-sada al-muttaqin», printed, a commentary on al-Ghazali's Ihya ulum al-din, ten volumes, Egyptian edition; «Asanid al-kutub al-sitta», in manuscript; «Uqud al-jawahir al-munifa fi adillat madhhab al-Imam Abi Hanifa», printed in two volumes; «Kashf al-litham an adab al-iman wa-l-Islam»; «Raf al-shakwa wa-tarwih al-qulub fi dhikr muluk Bani Ayyub», printed; and «Mu'jam shuyukhih», in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. Also «Alfiyyat al-sanad», in manuscript, on hadith, 1,500 lines, with its commentary; «Mukhtasar al-Ayn», in manuscript, an abridgement of the Book of al-Ayn attributed to al-Khalil ibn Ahmad; and «al-Takmila wa-l-sila wa-l-dhayl li-l-Qamus», in manuscript, two large volumes {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. Also «Idah al-madarik bi-l-ifsah an al-awatik» and «Iqd al-juman fi bayan shu'ab al-iman», two manuscript treatises; «Tuhfat al-qama'il fi madh Shaykh al-Arab Isma'il», in manuscript in his own hand; «Tahqiq al-wasa'il li-ma'rifat al-mukataba wa-l-rasa'il», in manuscript; «Judhwat al-iqtibas fi nasab Bani al-Abbas», in manuscript; and «Hikmat al-ishraq ila kitab al-afaq», printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. Also «al-Rawd al-mi'tar fi nasab al-sada Al Ja'far al-Tayyar», in manuscript; and «Muzil niqab al-khafa an kuna sadatina Bani al-Wafa», in manuscript, which al-Zirikli thinks may be the one also called «Raf niqab al-khafa amman intama ila Wafa wa-Abi al-Wafa»; he says he owns it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. Also «Balghat al-gharib fi mustalah athar al-habib», printed; «Tanbih al-arif al-basir ala asrar al-Hizb al-Kabir»; «Safinat al-naja»; «Ghayat al-ibtihaj li-muqtafi asanid Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj»; «Iqd al-la'ali al-mutanathira fi hifz al-ahadith al-mutawatira»; and «Nashwat al-irtiyah fi bayan haqiqat al-maysir wa-l-qidah», all in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He also names «al-Ara'is al-majluwwa fi dhikr awliya' Fuwwa», in manuscript at Rabat, number 2371 K {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
