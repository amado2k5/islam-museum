## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Qasim ibn Sa'id, Abu Abd Allah al-Uqbani al-Tilimsani: a jurist of Tlemcen who held the judgeship of the congregation there. His book Tuhfat al-Nazir wa Ghunyat al-Dhakir on guarding the rites and changing what is wrong treats enjoining good and forbidding evil. The book gives his death as 871 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7164}
