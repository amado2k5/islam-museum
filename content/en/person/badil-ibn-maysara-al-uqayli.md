## Overview
Badil ibn Maysara al-Uqayli al-Basri was a transmitter of hadith; al-Safadi gives his death as 126 AH and no year of birth {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 10 p. 63}.

## Transmission
Al-Safadi says he narrated from Anas, from Abu al-Jawza' Aws al-Rab'i, from Abd Allah ibn Shaqiq and from Ata' ibn Abi Rabah; that Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah transmitted reports from him; and that Ibn Ma'in declared him reliable {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 10 p. 63}.
