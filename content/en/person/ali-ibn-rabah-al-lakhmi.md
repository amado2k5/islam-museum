## Overview
Ali ibn Rabah al-Lakhmi, a Basran Successor, kunya Abu Abd Allah. "Nafh al-Tib" gives his birth in the year of the battle of al-Yarmuk, 15 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314-315}

## His life
He had standing with Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, and it was he who escorted Umm al-Banin to her husband al-Walid. Abd al-Aziz later rebuked him and sent him to raid Ifriqiya. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314-315}

Ibn Habib counts him as one of the three Successors who entered al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314-315}
