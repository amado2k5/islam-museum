## Overview
'Ali ibn Rabah ibn Qusayr ibn Qushayb ibn Yashi' — the trustworthy scholar whose name 'Ali was diminutivised to 'Ulay; Abu 'Abd al-Rahman the reciter explained that when the Umayyads heard of a newborn named 'Ali they killed him, and when this reached Rabah he said the boy was 'Ulay. He was born at the start of 'Uthman's caliphate and died in 114 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2779}
