## Overview
Jalinus here is a leader of a band of Persians who fought Abu Ubayda at the Euphrates in the battle of the Bridge. The Muslims were defeated in that battle after the bridge was cut. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 604}

Al-Mas'udi attributes to Jalinus a saying about love: that it occurs between two sensible people because they resemble each other in reason, and does not occur between two fools. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 360}

Note: in the sources the name itself points to the Greek physician, and the museum's data bring the two reports together under one name. Telling them apart needs an additional source.
