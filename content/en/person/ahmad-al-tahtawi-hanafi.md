## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ismail, a Hanafi jurist born in Tahta near Asyut, who studied at al-Azhar and was appointed head of the Hanafis, was dismissed and then reinstated. His works include a gloss on Maraqi al-falah, a gloss on al-Durr al-mukhtar, and Kashf al-rayn on wiping over the socks. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1631}
