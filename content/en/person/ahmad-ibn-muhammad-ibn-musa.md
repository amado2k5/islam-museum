## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Musa ibn al-Abbas, Abu Muhammad, devoted to the pursuit of historical reports and chronologies; he held the hisba of the slave market and the market of Egypt, and material was compiled from him. He died in Muharram of that year, per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 13 p. 358}
