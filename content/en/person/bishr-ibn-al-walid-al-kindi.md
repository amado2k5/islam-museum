## Overview
Abu al-Walid Bishr ibn al-Walid al-Kindi, one of the leading figures of opinion. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an elderly man of sound lineage and chastity, and says he was appointed judge by al-Ma'mun. He related the dictations of Abu Yusuf, which contain thirty-six books. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.287}

## In the Mihna
He was among those whom Ishaq ibn Ibrahim tested in [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|the Mihna over the Qur'an]]. He refused to say anything about the Qur'an except that it is the speech of God. Al-Suyuti relates the answer of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] about his case: that he should be summoned, and if he repented his case would be publicised, but if he persisted in what al-Ma'mun called polytheism, unbelief and heresy by his denial of the createdness of the Qur'an, his neck should be struck. This is the judgement of al-Ma'mun as the source relates it, not the judgement of the museum. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.491}
