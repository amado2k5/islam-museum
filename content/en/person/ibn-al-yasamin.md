## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Hajjaj, Abu Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Yasamin, was a calculator and secretary, one of the sultan's men in the Maghrib, Berber-born of Marrakesh, where he was found slain in his house in 601 AH (1204 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.124}

He wrote an urjuza on algebra (al-jabr wa-l-muqabala) which the grandson of al-Mardini commented, and an urjuza on the operations of roots. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.124}
