## Summary
Zakariya ibn al-Salt, of firm scrupulousness and a gentle heart, known for devotion, striving, longing and solitude. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 683, sequence 684, on page 400. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## His sayings on innovators
He said: nothing intercedes better for a sinner than service to the Lord of the worlds. He said: whoever looks at an innovator with favour helps the word read in the scan as "al-mi" (probably "destruction"; not confirmed), so one should close one's eyelids against the gaze of innovators. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## A report from Abu Hurayra
Ahmad ibn Ishaq relates from Muhammad ibn al-Abbas ibn Ayyub, from Zakariya ibn al-Salt, from Abd al-Salam ibn Salih, from Abbad ibn al-Awwam, from Abd al-Ghaffar, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, a report that for every innovation that harms Islam and its people, God raises one who defends it; one should seize such gatherings to defend the weak and rely on God. The wording is not given here. Abu Nu'aym notes that Abd al-Ghaffar alone transmits it from Sa'id, and Abbad from him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}
