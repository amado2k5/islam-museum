## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn al-Qasim al-Tabari, one of the authors among the companions of al-Shafi'i. He wrote glosses on [[person/abu-ali-ibn-abi-hurayra|Abu Ali ibn Abi Hurayra]], and taught in Baghdad after his teacher. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 60}

He wrote "al-Muharrir" on disputation, which al-Shirazi says was the first book composed on pure disagreement, and "al-Ifsah" on the school. He died in 350 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 60}

The editor's note says that al-Subki calls him "al-Husayn". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 60}
