## Overview
Abu Bakr Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Abi al-Dunya al-Qurashi, tutor of al-Muktafi billah and author of books on asceticism and other subjects. Al-Mas'udi says he died in Muharram 281 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.631-632}

## His death according to Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim says he died on a Tuesday, fourteen nights into Jumada al-Akhira 281 AH. The two sources agree on the year and differ on the month. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.262}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim lists among his books: The Devices of Satan, Forbearance (al-Hilm), The Jurisprudence of the Prophet, Condemnation of Entertainments, Pardon (al-Afw), Sincerity and Intention, Plagues (al-Tawa'in), Patience (al-Sabr), Reports of Quraysh, Condemnation of the World, Noble Character, Remembrance of Death and the Graves, The Asceticism of Malik ibn Dinar, and others. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.262}
