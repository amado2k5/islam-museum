## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim ibn Ridwan al-Ba'li al-Shafi'i, a Shafi'i by school, born and raised in Ba'labakk. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.599}

## Identification
He lived in the eighth century AH. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as around 699 AH and his death as around 774 AH. These dates come from one source and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.599}
