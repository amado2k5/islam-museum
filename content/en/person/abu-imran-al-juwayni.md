## Summary
Musa ibn al-Abbas ibn Muhammad, Abu Imran al-Juwayni al-Nishapuri, a great hadith scholar and memoriser. He heard from Abd Allah ibn Hashim, Ahmad ibn al-Azhar and others; al-Hasan ibn Sufyan, Abu Ali al-Hafiz and al-Hakim narrated from him. Al-Hakim called him good in hadith. He wrote a work on Sahih Muslim. Death 323 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13347}
