## Overview
Abu Ibrahim Isma'il ibn Yahya al-Muzani, author of the "Mukhtasar", one of the companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] and of those who took knowledge from him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 576-577}

## His death
He died in Egypt on Thursday, six nights before the end of Rabi al-Awwal 264 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 576-577} Al-Shirazi agrees that he died in Egypt in 264 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## His relation with al-Shafi'i
The data cite al-Shirazi that he was one of the companions of al-Shafi'i, and that al-Shafi'i said of him: "the champion of my school". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
