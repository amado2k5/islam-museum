## Overview
Abu Ibrahim Isma'il ibn Yahya al-Muzani, author of the Mukhtasar, among the companions of Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i from whom he took knowledge; he died in Egypt on Thursday the twenty-fifth of Rabi' I, 264 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
