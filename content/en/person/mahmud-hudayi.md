## Overview
Mahmud, also called Aziz Mahmud, ibn Fadlallah ibn Mahmud al-Rumi al-Qustantini al-Hudai al-Uskudari, was a Hanafi preacher of Anatolia and one of the shaykhs of the Jalwatiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1038 AH, 1628 CE, and prints no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}. He wrote in Arabic and in Turkish, and the Jalwatiyya lodge in Uskudar is attributed to him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists Khulasat al-Akhbar fi Ahwal al-Nabi al-Mukhtar, an abridgement in manuscript in the Chester Beatty collection {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}. He lists Hayat al-Arwah wa-Najat al-Ashbah, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}. He lists Jami' al-Fada'il wa-Qami' al-Radha'il, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}. He lists Kashf al-Qina' 'an Wajh al-Sama', in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 180}.
