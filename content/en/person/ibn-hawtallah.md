## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Sulayman ibn Dawud ibn Umar ibn Hawtallah al-Ansari al-Harithi, a jurist of the principles of law, secretary, man of letters and poet. He was born in Muharram 541 AH and died in Granada on 2 Rabi' al-Awwal 612 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338}

## His learning and judgeship
Al-Maqqari describes him as scrupulous, a memoriser and excellent. He studied the *Kitab* of Sibawayh and the *Mustasfa* of al-Ghazali, and used to preach in the gatherings of princes and assemblies. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338}

He held the judgeship in Seville, Córdoba, Murcia, Ceuta, Salé and Majorca. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338}

## His teachers
He heard hadith by oral transmission in a way that no one else in the West shared, and read more than sixty works, most of them with Ibn Habish, Ibn al-Fakhkhar and al-Suhayli. He was among the pupils of [[person/ibn-hubaysh-hafiz|Ibn Habish]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338}

His body was carried to Málaga, where he was buried. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338}

The date of death in the Gregorian calendar is derived from the Hijri date.
