## Overview
Ma'bad the singer, the singer of Medina. Al-Mas'udi relates that Hababa sent him with al-Ahwas, and he sang verses of al-Ahwas before Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, beginning "What is life but what one enjoys and desires", until Yazid repeated them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.186}
