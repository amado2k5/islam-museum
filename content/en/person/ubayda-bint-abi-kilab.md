## Summary
Ubayda bint Abi Kilab was a devout woman of [[place/basra|Basra]] recorded by Ibn al-Jawzi in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.711} {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## With Malik ibn Dinar
He relates from Abd al-Aziz ibn Salman that she attended Malik ibn Dinar for twenty years and asked him something only once: "When does the God-fearing person reach the highest degree, above which there is none?" He said, "When he reaches it, nothing is dearer to him than coming to God" — and she cried out and fell in a faint. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## Her contemporaries' praise
Al-Bara' al-Ghanawi said on the day she died, "Basra has left no one better than her", and Abd Allah ibn Rashid al-Sa'di, a companion of [[person/abd-al-wahid-ibn-zayd|Abd al-Wahid ibn Zayd]], said he had seen no woman or man more excellent or of sounder mind. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712} Abda bint Abi Shawwal, the servant of [[person/rabia-al-adawiyya|Rabi'a al-Adawiyya]], relates that she saw Rabi'a in a dream and asked about Ubayda; Rabi'a said Ubayda had outstripped them to the highest degrees, because she never cared in what state of the world she rose or retired. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## For young visitors
A devout woman of Basra who attended the lessons of the scholar Malik ibn Dinar for twenty years; people said there was no one better in Basra.
