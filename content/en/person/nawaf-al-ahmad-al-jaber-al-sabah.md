# Nawaf Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah

Nawaf Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah (25 June 1937 – 16 December 2023) was Emir of Kuwait from 29 September 2020 until his death, succeeding his half-brother Sabah Al-Ahmad. He had spent his whole career in Kuwait's security establishment before fourteen years as crown prince.{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Early life and education

He was born at Kuwait City, a son of Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah, and studied at the Hamada, Sharq, Al-Naqra, Eastern and Mubarakiya schools.{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikidata-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah}

## The security career

He was governor of Hawalli from 21 February 1962 to 19 March 1978, then minister of interior from 1978 to 26 January 1988 and minister of defence from 1988 to 1991. After the liberation of 1991 he acted as minister of labour and social affairs, later served as deputy chief of the Kuwait National Guard, and returned to the Interior Ministry from 2003 to 2006. On 7 February 2006 Emir Sabah named him crown prince.{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Reign

He became Emir on 29 September 2020, on Sabah's death. His reign of just over three years grappled with internal political disputes — repeated stand-offs between the government and the elected National Assembly — until his death.{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-ar}

## Death and succession

He died on 16 December 2023, aged 86, and was buried the next day at Sulaibikhat Cemetery. He was succeeded by his half-brother, Crown Prince Mishal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah.{cite:source/wikipedia-nawaf-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}
