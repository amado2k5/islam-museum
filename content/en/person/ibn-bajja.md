## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Yahya, known as Ibn Bajja, of the Tujibi family of Zaragoza, one of the philosophers of Islam. The Europeans call him "Avempace". {cite:al-aalam p. 868}

The source says that he was a skilled poet and knew genealogies as well as philosophy, and he wrote many books on philosophy, medicine and natural science. He died young in [[place/fez|Fez]]. {cite:al-aalam p. 868}
