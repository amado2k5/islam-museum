## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Hasan, known as Ibn al-Zarkashi, Shihab al-Din, a jurist with a share in several sciences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10349}

## Teaching and works
He taught at the Hasamiyya madrasa; his works include a commentary on al-Hidaya. He died in 733 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10349}
