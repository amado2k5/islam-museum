## Overview
Sharaf al-Din 'Abdallah b. al-qadi Shams al-Din Muhammad b. Muflih, of Ramina then Damascus, the Hanbali — the imam, scholar of the age, shaykh of the Hanbalis. He died in 834. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 208}
