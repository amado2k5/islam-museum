## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan al-Umawi, son of [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]], of the [[group/banu-umayya|Banu Umayya]]. Al-Baladhuri relates that he raided in the summer campaign in 84 AH, entered by the pass of Antioch and came to al-Massisa, rebuilt its fortress on its old foundation, which is linked to [[event/building-of-masisa|the building of Massisa]]. He placed there a garrison of three hundred men selected from the valiant, and built a mosque on the hill of the fortress. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225}

He then marched and raided and took the fortress of Sinan, and sent Yazid ibn Hunayn al-Ta'i al-Antaki, who raided. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225}
