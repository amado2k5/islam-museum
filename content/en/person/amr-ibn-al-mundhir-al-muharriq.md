## Overview
Amr ibn al-Mundhir al-Lakhmi, a prince, the son of [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-imri-al-qays|al-Mundhir ibn Imri al-Qays]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}

It is said that he was called "al-Muharriq" (the burner) because he burned ninety-nine men of the Banu Darim on the second day of Awara, and completed the hundred with a deputation of the Baraji, so his saying "the wretched one is the deputation of the Baraji" became a proverb. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.412-413}
