## Overview
The imam and memoriser Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Khalaf ibn Battal al-Maliki, author of *al-I'tisam* in hadith. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 469}

The book [[work/al-itisam-ibn-battal|al-I'tisam]] is attributed to him. The dates of his life are not documented in the museum's data, and need an additional source. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 469}
