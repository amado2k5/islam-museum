# Lamine Diack

Lamine Diack (7 June 1933 – 3 December 2021) was a Senegalese athlete, sports administrator and politician from Dakar who presided over world athletics as president of the IAAF from 1999 to 2015.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-fr}

## Athlete, mayor, parliamentarian

Diack competed in athletics and played and coached football in Dakar, introducing the flat back-four defensive system as a coach, before moving into sports administration: he led the Senegalese Olympic committee and served as secretary of state for youth and sports in the early 1970s.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-fr}{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en} In politics he was mayor of Dakar from 1978 to 1980 and a member of Senegal's National Assembly from 1978 to 1993, ending as its senior vice-president.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en}

## The IAAF presidency and its fall

Diack became president of the IAAF in 1999 and a member of the International Olympic Committee the same year.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en} His tenure ended in scandal: investigators found that he had negotiated a payment of 1.5 million dollars with Russian officials to fund Macky Sall's 2012 presidential campaign in Senegal in exchange for delaying anti-doping procedures against Russian athletes.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en} Under house arrest in Paris from November 2015, he was tried in France and on 16 September 2020 the Paris Criminal Court sentenced him to four years in prison, two of them suspended, with the maximum fine of 500,000 euros; his son Papa Massata Diack was convicted with him.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-en} He returned to Senegal in May 2021, when the Dakar club Jaraaf, which he had twice presided over, paid his bail, and he died at home in Dakar on 3 December 2021, aged 88.{cite:source/wikipedia-lamine-diack-fr}
