## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Mukarram al-Tabari al-Husayni was a learned man among the scholars of the Hijaz, born and died in Mecca, a fine writer who also composed verse {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 137}.

## His works
Among his books are Uyun al-Masa'il min A'yan al-Rasa'il, in which he gathered the essence of forty branches of knowledge, a commentary on al-Maqsura al-Duraydiyya, and further commentaries and treatises {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 137}.
