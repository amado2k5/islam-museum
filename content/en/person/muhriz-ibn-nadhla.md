## Overview
Muhriz ibn Nadhla ibn Abd Allah ibn Murra, of the Banu Ghanm of Asad ibn Khuzayma, known as al-Akhram al-Asadi with the kunya Abu Nadhla, was a Companion and one of their valiant, present at Badr. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.284}

He was killed by Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Uyayna al-Fazari at the raid of Dhu Qarad in 6 AH (627 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.284}
