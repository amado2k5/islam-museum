## Overview
Muhammad ibn Musafir al-Tarmi, one of the great Daylamites, lord of the fortress of al-Tarm. He married into some of the Kurdish chiefs, and his son Rustam was born of this. He had many children, among them Sallar, Sa'luk and [[person/marzuban-ibn-muhammad|al-Marzuban]]. From his house the state of the Banu Musafir arose in Azerbaijan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226}
