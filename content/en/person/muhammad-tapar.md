## Overview
The Seljuq sultan Ghiyath al-Din Abu Shuja' Muhammad ibn Malikshah. He became independent in the kingdom after his brother Barkiyaruq died, and died in Dhu al-Hijja 511 AH in Isfahan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 505}

## His reign
Ibn al-Imad describes him as a brave knight. There were many wars between him and his brother Barkiyaruq, and then he became independent in the kingdom after his brother died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 505}

## His death and successors
He died in Isfahan and was buried in a great madrasa for the Hanafis. His son Mahmud was set up after him, aged fourteen. He left four sons who became sultans: Mahmud, Mas'ud, Tughrulbek and Sulayman Shah. When news of his death reached the armies, they moved away from the siege of Alamut. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 505}
