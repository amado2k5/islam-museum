## Overview
Asiya al-Maqdisiyya (d. 640) — mother of al-Sayf b. al-Majd and sister of the hafiz Diya' al-Din al-Maqdisi; her brother al-Diya' said: "There is none like her in our time — she hardly ever leaves the night vigil" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 207}.
