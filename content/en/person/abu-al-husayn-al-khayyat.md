## Overview
Abu al-Husayn al-Khayyat, a Mu'tazili who wrote refutations of Ibn al-Rawandi. Ibn al-Nadim lists among these refutations: the book of "Argument against the Messengers", the book "Description of Wisdom", the book "Attack on the Composition of the Qur'an", the book "the Golden Rod" and the book "al-Furand". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.529}

The available data includes neither his biography nor his date of death, and needs an additional source.
