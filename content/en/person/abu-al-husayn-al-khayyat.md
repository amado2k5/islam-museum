## Overview
Abu'l-Husayn al-Khayyat the Mu'tazili, who refuted Ibn al-Rawandi's books — his Refutation of the Prophets, Qualifying Wisdom, Attack on the Composition of the Qur'an, al-Qadib al-Dhahab and al-Farand. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 529}
