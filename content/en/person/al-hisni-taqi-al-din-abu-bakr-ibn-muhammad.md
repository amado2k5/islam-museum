## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Mu'min ibn Huraz ibn Mu'alla al-Husni al-Dimashqi, a Shafi'i jurist of Damascus, titled Taqi al-Din and known as al-Husni. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.35}

## His life
He was born in 752 AH (1351 CE) and died in 829 AH (1426 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.35}
