## Summary
Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq; his son 'Abdallah ibn Muhammad left descendants called the Al Abi 'Atiq among Abu Bakr's offspring — because several of Abu Bakr's sons were boasting: one said 'I am the son of al-Siddiq', another 'I am the son of the second of two', another 'I am the son of the cave's companion' — and Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Rahman said: 'I am the son of 'Atiq' — and so he and his children were named for it. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
