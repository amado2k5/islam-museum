## Summary
Umama bint Dhi al-Isba', a pre-Islamic poetess of the Arabs who elegized her people after their destruction {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 80}.

## Elegy
The entry transmits verses opening "How many a youth had a shining, moon-like appointment," naming Fahm and her foes "slain and perished," and recalling the days when "they were kings, masters over the nations" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 80}.
