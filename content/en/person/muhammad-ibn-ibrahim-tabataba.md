## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Tabataba rose in Kufa in 199 AH calling to "the contentment of the family of Muhammad". Al-Tabari gives various reasons for his rising. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.528}

The details of those reasons need an additional source.
