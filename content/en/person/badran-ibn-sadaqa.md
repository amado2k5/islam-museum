## Overview
Taj al-Muluk Badran b. Sadaqa (d. 530, Egypt) — son of Sayf al-Dawla Sadaqa b. Mansur, lord of al-Hilla; when his father was killed he fled Baghdad for Syria, stayed a while, then went on to Egypt and died there; he composed verse and Dubays satirized him in lines {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
