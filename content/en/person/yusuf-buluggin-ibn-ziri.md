## Overview
Yusuf Buluggin ibn Ziri ibn Manad al-Sanhaji. al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah appointed him over the lands of the Maghrib when he set out for Egypt, and he took Ifriqiya, Constantine, the basin of Tlemcen and others {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488-489}.

## After al-Mu'izz
When al-Mu'izz died in the year 365 AH, al-Aziz confirmed him in the governorship of Ifriqiya. He became independent in its rule and his affair grew great {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488-489}.
