# Shaikhzada Babich

Shaikhzada Babich (Şəyexzada Möxəmmətzəkir ulı Babiç; 14 January 1895 – 28 March 1919) was a Bashkir poet, writer and playwright, a classic author of Bashkir national literature. He was born in the village of Asyanovo in Ufa Governorate; his father Mukhametzakir was the mullah of the Asyanovo mahalla and directed the village madrasa where the boy received his primary education. In 1910 he travelled the Kazakh steppe teaching Kazakh children, and between 1911 and 1916 he studied at the Ğəliyə madrasa in Ufa, where his interest in literature took hold.{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-en}{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-ru}

Most of his work was written in Old Bashkir and published in Tatar magazines and newspapers; he began writing poetry in Bashkir in 1917. From the autumn of 1917 he was drawn into the Bashkir liberation movement: secretary of the Bashkir Central Shuro, editor of the newspaper "Bashkort", head of the Bashkir youth organisation Tulqın, and a member of the Bashkir government. In 1918 he published in Orenburg his only lifetime book, the poem collection "Blue Songs, Young Bashkortostan", and in 1918–1919 he followed Bashkir troops as a war correspondent.{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-en}{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-ru}

On 28 March 1919, during the transition of the Bashkir army to the Red Army, Babich was brutally murdered by a member of the Red Army in the village of Zilair; he was twenty-four. He was buried in a grave on the outskirts of Zilair, where a bust of him now stands.{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-en}{cite:wikipedia-shaikhzada-babich-ru}
