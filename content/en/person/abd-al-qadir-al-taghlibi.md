## Summary
Abd al-Qadir ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Qadir al-Taghlibi al-Shaybani al-Hanbali al-Dimashqi, Abu al-Tuqa, a Hanbali jurist and expert in inheritance. He studied with Abd al-Baqi al-Hanbali and his son Abu al-Mawahib and read with several scholars of his time. He earned his living binding books and taught at the Umayyad mosque. His work Nayl al-Ma'arib is a commentary on Dalil al-Talib. The book gives no death date here. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13360}
