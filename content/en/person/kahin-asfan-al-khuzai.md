## Overview
A soothsayer (kahin) of Khuza'a whose home was in Usfan, and the grandfather of Amr ibn al-Hamiq. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 61-62}

## Judging between Hashim and Umayya
Hashim and Umayya ibn Abd Shams made him arbiter between them in a contest of boasting. Al-Baladhuri relays his judgement in rhymed prose, in which Hashim preceded Umayya in glories, first and last. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 61-62}
