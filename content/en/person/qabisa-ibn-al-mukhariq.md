## Summary
Qabisa ibn al-Mukhariq ibn Abd Allah ibn Shaddad ibn Mu'awiya, Abu Bishr al-Hilali of Basra. He came to the Prophet as a delegate. Al-Bukhari, Ibn Abi Hatim and Ibn Hibban say that he had companionship. His son Qatn, Kinana ibn Na'im and Abu Uthman al-Nahdi transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15416}
