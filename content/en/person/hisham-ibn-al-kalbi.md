## Overview
Hisham ibn Muhammad ibn al-Sa'ib al-Kalbi, a genealogist, who died in 204 AH, the year of al-Shafi'i's death according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.418}

## His account of the origin of script
Ibn al-Nadim reports from him that the first to devise the Arabic script were people of the ancient Arab tribes who settled among Adnan ibn Udd, and that he named them: Abu Jad, Hawwaz, Hutti, Kalamun, Sa'fas and Qurashat. They wrote the script after their names. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.9-10}
