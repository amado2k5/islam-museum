## Overview
Musa ibn Yahya ibn Khalid ibn Barmak, an Abbasid governor of the frontier of Sind, who succeeded Ghassan ibn Abbad, and died in 215 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## On the frontier of Sind
He killed Balla, king of al-Sharqi, who had offered him five hundred thousand dirhams for his release. Al-Baladhuri says that he acted well. He was succeeded by his son Imran. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
