## Summary
Reham Nayyar Khan (born 3 April 1973) is a British-Pakistani journalist, author and filmmaker. A BBC weather presenter from 2008, she moved to Pakistani television in 2013, hosted The Reham Khan Show, produced the film Janaan (2016), published a memoir in 2018, and in 2025 launched the Pakistan Republic Party. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en}

## Life
Khan was born in Ajdabiya, Libya, on 3 April 1973, to a family from Baffa, in the Hazara country of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan; her father was Nayyar Ramzan. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-ur} {cite:wikidata-reham-khan} She took a bachelor's degree in education from Jinnah College for Women, Peshawar. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} She married Ejaz Rehman in 1993, with whom she has three children, and after their divorce married the cricketer and politician Imran Khan in a ceremony on 6 January 2015; that marriage ended in divorce on 30 October 2015. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} On 23 December 2022 she married Mirza Bilal. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en}

## Work
Khan began presenting in 2006 on Legal TV and in 2007 on Sunshine Radio in Hereford and Worcester, and in 2008 she joined the BBC as a weather presenter. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} In 2013 she returned to Pakistan, working for News One, Aaj TV and PTV before joining Dawn News, where she presented the current-affairs programme In Focus and, from May 2015, The Reham Khan Show, a programme celebrating Pakistani heroes; she then hosted Tabdeeli on Neo TV until June 2016. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} She produced the romantic comedy film Janaan, set in Swat, which premiered on 13 September 2016. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} Her memoir, Reham Khan, appeared in 2018 and drew wide attention in Pakistan ahead of that year's general election. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en} On 15 July 2025 she launched the Pakistan Republic Party at the Karachi Press Club, saying it would represent marginalised groups and challenge dynastic politics. {cite:wikipedia-reham-khan-en}
