## Summary
Brother of the Hafsid sultan [[person/al-hasan-ibn-muhammad-hafsi|al-Hasan b. Muhammad]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 215}.

## Flight
Fleeing his brother's killing he took refuge among the Arabs of the steppe, then went to Khayr al-Din in Algiers asking protection and aid against his brother — which was granted {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 215}.

## Over Tunis
When Khayr al-Din entered Tunis in 1534 he installed al-Rashid over it and proclaimed its annexation to the Ottoman dominions {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 218}.
