## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Yusuf, Abu al-Abbas al-Buni, was the author of works on the science of letters and a Sufi of Maghribi origin; his nisba refers to Bona, on the coast of Ifriqiya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}. He died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## Works
His best-known book is «Shams al-Ma'arif al-Kubra», printed, also called «Shams al-Ma'arif wa Lata'if al-Awarif fi Ilm al-Huruf wal-Khawass», in four parts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}. The entry also names, as manuscripts, «al-Lum'a al-Nuraniyya», «al-Misk al-Zahir» on the science of letters, and «Mawaqif al-Ghayat fi Asrar al-Riyadiyyat», a treatise in the Azhar library {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}. Al-Zirikli mentions «Shams al-Ma'arif al-Wusta» and «al-Sughra» on the authority of Ubayd's notes, a printed treatise on explaining the Greatest Name of God, and another printed one on the merit of the basmala {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## Death
Al-A'lam places his death in Cairo in 622 AH, equal to 1225 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.
