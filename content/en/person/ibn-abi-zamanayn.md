## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn 'Isa al-Murri, Abu Abd Allah, known as Ibn Abi Zamanayn, was a Maliki jurist among the preaching littérateurs, of Elvira. He lived in Cordoba then returned to Elvira and died there in 399 AH (1008 CE); asked why his family were called the Banu Abi Zamanayn he said he did not know. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.226}

His many books on fiqh and exhortation include Usul al-Sunna; Muntakhab al-Ahkam; a Tafsir al-Qur'an abridged from the tafsir of Yahya ibn Sallam al-Taymi; al-Mughrib abridging the Mudawwana and explaining its difficulties; Hayat al-Qulub on asceticism; al-Nasa'ih al-Manzuma, his verse; Adab al-Islam; al-Muhadhdhab abridging Ibn Muzayn's commentary on the Muwatta'; and al-Mushtamil on the science of deeds. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.226}
