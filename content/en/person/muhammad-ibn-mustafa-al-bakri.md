## Overview
Muhammad ibn Mustafa ibn Kamal al-Din ibn Ali al-Bakri al-Siddiqi, Kamal al-Din, Abu al-Futuh, was a man of letters and one of the Hanafi jurists of Palestine. He was born in Bayt al-Maqdis, Jerusalem, and died in Gaza. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1143 AH, 1731 CE, and his death as 1196 AH, 1782 CE, and says he left verse and compositions {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 100}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists Khulasat Tahqiq al-Zunun fi al-Shuruh wa-l-Mutun, in manuscript, in which he stripped Kashf al-Zunun of its repetitions and added supplementary material; al-Rawd al-Ra'id fi Ilm al-Fara'id; Tashnif al-Sam' fi Tafdil al-Basar ala al-Sam'; al-Minah al-Ilahiyya fi Madh Khayr al-Bariyya, a commentary on a badi'iyya poem of his own; and Nibras al-Afkar, his diwan of verse {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 100}.
