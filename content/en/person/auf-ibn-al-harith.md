## Overview
'Awf ibn al-Harith ibn Rifa'a ibn 'Afra'; he witnessed al-'Aqaba — some count him among the six men who first met the Messenger of God — witnessed Badr and was martyred in 2 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2979}
