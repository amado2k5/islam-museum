## Overview
Ibn al-Athir names him al-Malik Mas'ud ibn Qilij Arslan ibn Sulayman ibn Qutlumush, lord of Konya and the neighbouring parts of the land of Rum {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir vol. 9 p. 407}.

## Death
Ibn al-Athir records his death among the events of 551 AH, which corresponds to 1156 CE by the museum's conversion (derived) {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir vol. 9 p. 407}. He adds that his son Qilij Arslan took the throne after him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir vol. 9 p. 407}.
