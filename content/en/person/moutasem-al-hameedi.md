# Moutasem Al-Hameedi

## Summary
Moutasem Al-Hameedi is an Islamic lecturer and author whose recorded lectures circulate widely in English. Muslim Central's podcast series under his name has carried his lectures since 2015 and runs to several hundred episodes, and Islamway catalogues a further body of his recorded lessons. {cite:listennotes-moutasem-al-hameedi} {cite:islamway-moutasem-al-hameedi}

## Studies and posts
His published biography says he studied under prominent students of al-Albani: Mashhoor Hasan (hadith and the principles of jurisprudence), Hussain al-Awaaysha (jurisprudence and manners), Abu Suhaib, an imam at the Wise Islamic Centre in the UK, Ayman as-Saadiq (Arabic grammar and morphology), and Malik Sha'ban (creed and Qur'an commentary). His dawah work, as the biography lists it, includes serving as imam and lecturer at the Ibn Taymiyyah Mosque in London and at the Call to Islam Islamic Center in Luton, lecturing on Islam at British universities and schools, and hosting television shows and lecture series on Huda TV. {cite:listennotes-moutasem-al-hameedi}

## Writings
He is the author of Reach the Sweetness of Prayer, published by Tertib Publishing in Malaysia in 2019. {cite:tadabburbooks-moutasem-al-hameedi}
