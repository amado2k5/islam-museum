## Overview
Umayr ibn Wahb al-Jumahi, who was in the army of al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, sent to Egypt {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.299}.

## Egypt
After the conquest of Fustat, [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] sent him to Tinnis, Damietta, Tuna, Dumayra, Shata, Daqahliyya, Bana and Busir. The people of their villages made peace with him on the same terms as the people of Fustat. See [[event/conquest-of-egypt|the conquest of Egypt]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.304}.
