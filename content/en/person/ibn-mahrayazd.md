## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad, Abu Muslim, known as Ibn Mihrayazd (or Mihrizad, the spelling is disputed), was the hadith scholar of Isfahan in his time, a Mu'tazili versed in tafsir and letters. He wrote a Tafsir al-Qur'an in twenty volumes and died at Isfahan in 459 AH (1067 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.276}
