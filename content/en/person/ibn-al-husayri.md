## Summary
Ibn al-Husayri, shaykh of the Hanafis at Damascus; he transmitted Sahih Muslim from al-Farawi's companions, taught at Damascus's Nuriyya for twenty-five years, and wrote fine books including a commentary on *al-Jami' al-kabir*; among the working scholars, much in charity, copious in tears — the headship of Abu Hanifa's followers ended with him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 190}
