## Summary
[[person/arwa-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Arwa]] bint [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] al-Hashimiyya, aunt of [[person/muhammad|the Messenger of God]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1807}. Abu Umar said she was under Umayr ibn Wahb ibn Abd ibn Qusayy and bore him Tulayb; then Kulda ibn Abd Manaf ibn Abd al-Dar ibn Qusayy succeeded him over her and she bore him Arwa {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1807}.

## Did she become Muslim?

## Her stand the day Abu Jahl assaulted the Prophet
Ibn Sa'd records from al-Waqidi with his chain to Barra bint Abi Tajra: Abu Jahl and several with him fell upon the Prophet and hurt him, whereupon Tulayb ibn Umayr went for Abu Jahl, struck him and gashed him — they seized him, and Abu Lahab rose to his aid. Word reached Arwa and she said: "his best day is the day he aided his aunt's son." Abu Lahab was told "Arwa has turned to the new faith"; he went in to her reproaching her, and she said: "rise for your nephew's sake — if he prevails you will have the choice, and if not you will have your excuse in your nephew." Abu Lahab said: "do we have strength against all the Arabs? He has come with a new religion" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1807}.

## Her elegy for the Prophet
Ibn Sa'd mentions that this Arwa elegised the Prophet, and verses of hers are recited {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1807}.
