## Overview
Yusuf ibn Musa ibn Rashid, the trustworthy traditionist-imam Abu Ya'qub of Kufa, al-Qattan, resident of Baghdad; born in 269 AH, he narrated from Jarir ibn 'Abd al-Hamid, Abu Khalid al-Ahmar, Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna, 'Abdallah ibn Idris, Abu Bakr ibn 'Ayyash, Waki', 'Abdallah ibn Numayr, Hakkam ibn Salm, Ahmad ibn Yunus and their peers. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4286}
