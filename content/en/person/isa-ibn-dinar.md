## Overview
Isa ibn Dinar ibn Waqid, also given as Ibn Wahb, Abu Muhammad al-Qurtubi, a Maliki jurist and the leading jurist of al-Andalus in his time. He was appointed qadi of Toledo under al-Hakam and head of the consultative council in Cordoba, travelled in search of hadith, and wrote the ten-part Kitab al-Hidaya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3135}

## Dates
He died in 212 AH, as the entry heading gives it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3135}
