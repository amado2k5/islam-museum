## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Mahraz b. Muhammad al-Wahrani, styled Rukn al-Din — some say Jamal al-Din; one of the wits and virtuosi, he came from his homeland to Egypt in Salah al-Din's day, his craft being the art of insha'. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## The road of jest
When he arrived and saw al-Qadi al-Fadil and 'Imad al-Din al-Isfahani, he knew he was not of their rank, so he abandoned the path of earnest for the path of jest, composing the famous dream-visions and epistles ascribed to him — abundant and betraying his lightness of spirit and perfect wit, the most famous being *al-Manam al-kabir*. He wandered the lands, settled at Damascus and held the preaching post at Darayya in the Ghuta. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
