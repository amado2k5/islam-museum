## Overview
Ibn al-Nahhas, "Hujjat al-'Arab" — shaykh of Arabic in the lands of Egypt; he transmitted from Muwaffaq b. Ya'ish and Ibn al-Latti, and was among the cleverest of his age {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 773}.
