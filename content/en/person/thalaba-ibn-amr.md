## Overview
Tha'laba ibn [[person/amr-ibn-amir-muzayqiya|Amr Muzayqiya]], of the [[group/al-azd|Azd]]. He branched off in the migration of the Azd toward the Hijaz and settled between al-Tha'labiyya and [[place/dhu-qar|Dhu Qar]]. When his son grew up and his strength increased, he set out for [[place/medina|Medina]], settled there and made it his home {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.231-235}.

## The Aws and the Khazraj
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum says that among the sons of this Tha'laba were [[group/al-aws|the Aws]] and [[group/al-khazraj|the Khazraj]], the two sons of Haritha ibn Tha'laba {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17}. In another place the author cites the preference of Sulayman al-Nadawi that the Ansar, of the Aws and the Khazraj, were not of the family of Qahtan but of the family of [[person/nabit|Nabit ibn Isma'il]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:tarikh-ard-al-quran 2/78-86}. Their lineage is therefore a matter of dispute among genealogists.

## For young visitors
Tha'laba was the grandfather of the Aws and the Khazraj, the two tribes who lived in Medina and supported the Prophet after the Hijra.
