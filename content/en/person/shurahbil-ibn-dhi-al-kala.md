## Overview
Shurahbil ibn Dhi al-Kala' al-Himyari, one of the five commanders of the vanguard of [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] in his war with the Tawwabin at Ayn al-Warda, as al-Mas'udi reports {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.88}.
