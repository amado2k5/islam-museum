## Summary

Badi' al-Din Shah al-Rashidi (1926–1996) was a Pakistani Sindhi hadith scholar of the Ahl-i Hadith, nicknamed 'Musnid of the Age' for the height of his isnads; he taught the Sunna collections in the Meccan Sanctuary for over forty years.{cite:wikipedia-badi-al-din-shah-al-rashidi}

## Life

Born in Sindh, Pakistan, he learned under Ahl-i Hadith scholars in India and the Hijaz, read under senior hadith scholars and received high isnads, and settled in Mecca teaching in the Sanctuary and the institutes.{cite:wikipedia-badi-al-din-shah-al-rashidi}

## Works and teaching

His books include Al-Ijaba al-Badi'a fi Sharh al-'Aqida al-Tahawiyya, Ghayat al-Maram fi al-Ta'arudat and Al-Fatawa al-Badi'iyya. Many hadith scholars trained under him and received ijazas in the Six Books and others.{cite:wikipedia-badi-al-din-shah-al-rashidi}

## Standing

He is remembered as one of the last masters of the high isnad in hadith, joining the Pakistani Ahl-i Hadith school to the Meccan ijaza tradition; his recorded lessons are still studied. He died in Mecca in 1996.{cite:wikipedia-badi-al-din-shah-al-rashidi}
