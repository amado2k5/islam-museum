## Overview
Ma'bad al-Juhani, who followed Abu Yunus al-Aswari — known as Sinsawayh — in the discourse on free will under al-Balkhi; Abu Yunus was a man of the Asawira and the first to discourse on qadar and i'tizal. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 558}
