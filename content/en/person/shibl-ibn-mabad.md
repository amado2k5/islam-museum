## Overview
Shibl ibn Ma'bad ibn Ubayd al-Bajali. He was one of the four witnesses against al-Mughira in the story of Umm Jamil, and took part in [[event/mughira-adultery-affair|this affair]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] flogged him, and he said, according to the transmitted account: "Will you flog the witnesses of the truth and void the punishment?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
