## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Makhrama al-Kindi, a man mentioned in al-Mas'udi's *Muruj al-Dhahab* in an account involving Abu al-Abbas. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.251}

## The account
When he wanted to ask Abu al-Abbas for something, he postponed it until his food was brought, because at that time his face was most open. When Abu al-Abbas asked him about this, he said that he was seeking success. Abu al-Abbas told him that he was worthy of leadership because of this shrewdness. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.251}

The data do not specify his post or the year of his death. It needs an additional source.
