## Summary
Nashwa Al Ruwaini is an Egyptian-Qatari media producer and entrepreneur, founder and CEO of the Pyramedia Group; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-nashwa-al-ruwaini} {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Career
She worked in Egyptian and Gulf television before founding Pyramedia in the 1990s, building it into one of the Middle East's leading production and media-consultancy companies, headquartered in Dubai. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Productions
Pyramedia produced and packaged some of Arab television's most-watched formats — including the talent contests Arab Idol-associated programming, the Million's Poet and Prince of Poets poetry competitions on Abu Dhabi TV — bringing large-scale entertainment formats to pan-Arab audiences. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Profile
Regularly ranked among the most powerful women in Arab media and business by regional business press, she sits on media industry juries and speaks on Arab content industries. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Assessment
She is credited with professionalising Arab television production at regional scale and with a model of the female media mogul in the Gulf; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini} {cite:muslim500-nashwa-al-ruwaini}


## Pyramedia
Her company Pyramedia produces large-scale Arabic formats — the early pan-Arab talent shows and branded reality formats — and she has led it as chief executive since its founding, becoming a reference point for Arabic television production. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Nashwa show
Her self-titled talk show Nashwa on Dubai TV ran for years as a high-rating vehicle for social and cultural issues, extending her influence from production into fronting. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Recognition
She is regularly listed among the most powerful Arab women in media — including Forbes Middle East rankings — and serves on juries and advisory boards across regional broadcasting. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini} {cite:muslim500-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## Origins
Born in Egypt and raised between Cairo and the Gulf, she studied communication and moved through regional television production before founding Pyramedia in Abu Dhabi in 1998 — one of the first independent Arab production companies of scale. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## The format pioneer
Her productions — early pan-Arab talent and reality formats — are credited with building the regional version of global formats, making her a central figure in the commercialisation of Arabic television content. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## The Nashwa show
Her self-titled talk show ran on Dubai TV as a vehicle for social debate, extending her public role from producer to presenter and making her a recognisable face alongside her executive reputation. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini} {cite:muslim500-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## The production pioneer
Nashwa Al-Ruwaini's founding of Pyramedia in Abu Dhabi in 1998 created one of the first independent Arab production companies of scale: a vehicle for the early pan-Arab talent shows and branded reality formats that built the regional version of global formats. Her self-titled talk show Nashwa on Dubai TV ran for years as a vehicle for social debate, extending her public role from producer to presenter and making her a recognisable face alongside her executive reputation. {cite:wikipedia-nashwa-al-ruwaini}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists her among the most influential Muslims in media, citing her role as a pioneer of Arabic television production and her position among the most powerful women in Arab media across Forbes Middle East rankings. {cite:muslim500-nashwa-al-ruwaini}
