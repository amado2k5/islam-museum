## Overview
Mas'ud ibn al-Aswad ibn Haritha ibn Nadhla, a Qurashi Companion from the Banu Adi ibn Ka'b. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Martyrdom
He was martyred on the day of [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta]] in Jumada al-Ula 8 AH, as the biography records. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
