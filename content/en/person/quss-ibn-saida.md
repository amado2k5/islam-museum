## Overview
Qass ibn Sa'ida al-Iyadi, one of those who called to God and drew the attention of their people to His signs in the period between Jesus and Muhammad. al-Mas'udi counts him among the monotheists who affirmed the Creator and believed in the Resurrection. al-Rahiq al-Makhtum mentions him among those who spoke of divinity before Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.240}

## In al-Rahiq al-Makhtum (p. 77)
al-Rahiq al-Makhtum relays from Muhammad al-Ghazali that when news of the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret mission]] reached Quraysh, they paid it no attention. They may have taken Muhammad for one of the religious speakers who discussed divinity and its rights, as Umayya ibn Abi al-Salt, Qass ibn Sa'ida, Amr ibn Nufayl and their like had done {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.77} {cite:fiqh-al-sira-al-ghazali p.76}.
