## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Halabi was a Hanafi jurist of Aleppo; he studied there and in Egypt, then settled at Constantinople, where he died past ninety years of age in 956 AH (1549 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.66}

His best-known book is Multaqa al-Abhur on fiqh; also Ghunyat al-Mutamalli, a commentary on Munyat al-Musalli; and he wrote Mukhtasar Tabaqat al-Hanabila, an abridgement of al-Qamus al-Muhit, Talkhis al-Fatawa al-Tatarkhaniyya, and Talkhis al-Jawahir al-Mudiyya on the classes of the Hanafis. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.66}
