## Overview
'Abd al-Wahid b. Yusuf b. 'Abd al-Mu'min the Almohad, uncle of the caliph al-Nasir, known as al-Makhlu' (the Deposed); the state swore allegiance to him after al-Mustansir for his age and sound mind, but he neither managed well nor indulged the men of his realm — so they deposed and strangled him nine months into his rule. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
