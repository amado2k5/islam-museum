## Overview
Chief of the Awraba Berbers of the Maghrib at the time of the conquest. He was an amir over his people, and Hassan ibn al-Nu'man at first made light of him. Then he showed Islam and was spared. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1607-1608}

## Later
He encamped at the springs of Tlemcen and gathered all the Berbers, met the Muslims with an army, and ruled Ifriqiya for five years; he settled in Kairouan. Then Zuhayr ibn Qays al-Baluwi defeated him in 79 AH, killed him and countless Berbers, and pursued them to Marmahanna and Malwiya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1607-1608}

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
Ibn Abd al-Hakam says Kusayla ibn Lamzam killed Uqba ibn Nafi and Abu al-Muhajir at Tahudha with a host of Rum and Berbers, then occupied the place where Uqba had camped. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 267} Zuhayr ibn Qays al-Balawi then marched against him and killed him; others say it was Hassan ibn al-Nu'man who sent Zuhayr. Al-Layth dates Kusayla's death to the year 64. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 269} See [[event/battle-of-tahudha|the battle of Tahudha]].
