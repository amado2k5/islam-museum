## Overview
Suraqa ibn Mirdas ibn Asma' ibn Khalid al-Bariqi al-Azdi was an Iraqi poet of Yemeni origin who died in 79 AH (698 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.80}

He fought al-Mukhtar al-Thaqafi at Kufa in 66 AH and lampooned him in verse; al-Mukhtar's men captured and brought him, and he ordered his release, whereupon Suraqa went to Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr at Basra and on to Damascus, returning to Iraq with the governor Bishr ibn Marwan after al-Mukhtar's fall. When al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf took Iraq, Suraqa lampooned him too and fled to Syria when sought, dying there. He was witty, a fine reciter and sweet of talk, brought near and loved by the amirs; he exchanged flytings with Jarir, and Ibn 'Asakir's history has him reaching the prophetic era and witnessing al-Yarmuk. He left a small diwan, edited by Husayn Nassar. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.80}
