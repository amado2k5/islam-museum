## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Ahtham, Abu Hajan, was [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]]'s deputy over Merv when he went out on raids. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Informing and hiding
The report says that he wrote to [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] to speak ill of Qutayba; al-Hajjaj folded his letter back to him, so he sensed evil and fled to Syria. There he sold wine and linen goods, then disguised himself as "Abu Tina", a dealer in oil, and put a rag and cotton over one eye. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the death of al-Walid
When al-Walid died he stood up and preached to Sulayman, offering congratulations and attacking al-Hajjaj and Qutayba, and people said that Abu Tina the oil-dealer was the most eloquent of the people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} This report is from a single source and needs a second source.
