## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Sahl, Abu Bakr, known as Shams al-A'imma al-Sarakhsi, was a judge, one of the great Hanafis and a mujtahid, from Sarakhs in Khurasan. Al-Zirikli dates his death to 483 AH, that is 1090 CE as printed, and prints no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 315}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says the cause of his imprisonment was a word of counsel he gave the Khaqan, and that when he was released he settled in Farghana and lived there until he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 315}.

## Works
His best-known book is al-Mabsut, on law and legislation, printed, in thirty parts, which he dictated while a prisoner in the well at Uzjand in Farghana. He also wrote a commentary on al-Jami' al-Kabir of Imam Muhammad, of which one manuscript volume is noted; a commentary on al-Siyar al-Kabir of Imam Muhammad, printed, which al-Zirikli describes as a commentary on al-Shaybani's Ziyadat al-Ziyadat; al-Usul on the principles of law, in manuscript; and a commentary on the Mukhtasar of al-Tahawi, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 315}.
