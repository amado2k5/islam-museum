## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur, lord of Hama — Muhammad b. al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din 'Umar b. Shahanshah b. Ayyub; he heard from Abu'l-Zahir Ibn 'Awf and compiled a year-by-year history in volumes known as his Tarikh; his son al-Nasir Qilij Arslan ruled Hama after him until al-Kamil took it, imprisoned him, and gave it to his brother al-Malik al-Muzaffar; al-Mansur died in 617. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 77-78}
