## Overview
Shams al-Din Ibn Nuh, mudarris of the Rawahiyya (d. Rabi' II 654) — the greatest of Ibn al-Salah's pupils and the most madrasa-learned in the madhhab; a company took fiqh from him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 273}.
