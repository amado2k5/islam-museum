## Overview
Tayhan ibn Abjar al-Taymi, who spoke about deposing Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, in the account of Ibn al-Ash'ath in the year 81 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.338}.

al-Tabari reports that the people deposed him along with the rest of them, and that only a few remained loyal to his allegiance {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.338}.
