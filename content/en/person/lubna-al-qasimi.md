## Summary
Sheikha Lubna bint Khalid bin Sultan Al Qasimi is an Emirati royal, technologist and politician who in 2004 became the first woman appointed to a ministerial post in the United Arab Emirates. {cite:wikipedia-lubna-al-qasimi}

## Life and formation
Born on 4 February 1962 in Dubai into the Al Qasimi family that rules Sharjah — and kin to the emirate's ruler Sheikh Sultan bin Muhammad Al Qasimi — she was educated partly in the United States, taking a bachelor's degree in computer science from California State University, Chico, and an executive MBA from the American University of Sharjah. She returned to the Gulf and built a technology career, including senior IT roles in Dubai's ports authority, where she worked on the systems that helped make Dubai a logistics hub. {cite:wikipedia-lubna-al-qasimi}

## Career
In 2000 she headed Tejari, an early Middle Eastern business-to-business e-marketplace. Appointed Minister of Economy and Planning in November 2004 — the first woman minister in UAE history — she managed trade policy and economic diversification; she later served as Minister of Foreign Trade and then, from 2013 to 2016, as Minister of International Cooperation and Development, directing the UAE's large overseas aid programme. She subsequently became president of Zayed University. {cite:wikipedia-lubna-al-qasimi}

## Legacy
Lubna al-Qasimi repeatedly appeared high on Forbes' list of the world's most powerful women — a marker less of royal status than of a technocratic portfolio — and is cited as opening the federal cabinet to Emirati women. Her career illustrates the Gulf's pattern of royal women entering public office through technology and development roles. {cite:wikipedia-lubna-al-qasimi}
