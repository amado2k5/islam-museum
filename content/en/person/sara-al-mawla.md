## Summary
A freedwoman of Banu Abd al-Muttalib; she appears in this version as one of two accounts of the identity of the bearer of [[work/hatib-letter-to-quraysh|Hatib's letter]] to Quraysh {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/40}. She also appears in the list of those the Prophet ordered killed on the conquest day for harming him in Mecca; she was granted safety, then horses trampled her at al-Abtah and killed her in Umar's time {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52-53}.

## The letter's bearer
On the identity of the woman who carried Hatib's letter there are two accounts: Ibn Ishaq said from Ibn Ja'far from Urwa that she was a woman of Muzayna; in another account she is Sara, freedwoman of Banu Abd al-Muttalib {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/40}.

## The conquest day
She was ordered killed among those the Prophet ordered killed on the conquest day — it is said she used to harm him in Mecca {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52-53}. She was granted safety, then horses trampled her at al-Abtah and killed her in Umar's time {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/53}.

## For young visitors
A freedwoman of Banu Abd al-Muttalib whose name appears in two different accounts: some say she carried the secret letter to Mecca, others say it was another woman of Muzayna. She survived the conquest day, then years later a mount trampled her dead.
