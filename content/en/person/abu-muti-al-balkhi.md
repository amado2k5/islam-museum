## Overview
Al-Hakam ibn Abd Allah, Abu Muti al-Balkhi, was a jurist and the author of the Fiqh al-akbar. Al-Safadi writes out his death year in words, which gives 199 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 pp. 70–71}.

## Career
He studied law under Abu Hanifa and became judge of Balkh; al-Safadi calls him gifted in reasoned opinion, and says Ibn al-Mubarak held him in high regard {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 pp. 70–71}. Al-Safadi relays from al-Nadr ibn Shumayl that Abu Muti said faith and Islam come down in the Qur'an in two senses, while for him they are one; al-Nadr asked whether he thought the mistake lay in himself, in the Messenger, in Jibril or in God, and he was left speechless {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 pp. 70–71}.

Al-Safadi also reports a saying that he was among the leaders of the Murji'a, quotes Ibn Ma'in calling him weak, and quotes Abu Dawud that his hadith was abandoned because he was a Jahmi; these are judgements al-Safadi attributes to others, not the museum's {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 pp. 70–71}.
