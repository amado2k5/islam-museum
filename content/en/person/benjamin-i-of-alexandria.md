## Overview
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports, from elders of Egypt other than Sa'id ibn Ufayr, that there was at Alexandria a bishop of the Copts called Abu Binyamin. When he heard that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] had come to Egypt he wrote to the Copts that the Romans would have no more rule and their kingdom was ended, and told them to go out to meet Amr. It is said that the Copts at [[place/al-farama|al-Farama]] were Amr's helpers that day. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 86}

## In the editor's note
The editor identifies him as the chief bishop of the Copts at Alexandria and cites John of Nikiu for his flight from Alexandria and his return under a safe-conduct written by Amr. This is the editor's note, not Ibn Abd al-Hakam's text. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 86}
