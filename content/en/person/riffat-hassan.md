## Summary
Riffat Hassan is a Pakistani theologian based in the United States, a pioneer of Islamic feminist theology rereading foundational texts from the angle of women’s dignity. {cite:wikipedia-riffat-hassan}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Lahore, Pakistan, in 1943, she studied philosophy in Lahore and Britain and settled in the United States, teaching religious and Islamic studies. {cite:wikipedia-riffat-hassan}

## Career and works
She taught at the University of Louisville in Kentucky. Her research dismantles the traditional reading of Eve as the cause of the Fall, arguing the Quranic text does not lay greater blame on women. She founded mosque education programmes for women and campaigned against honour killings and domestic violence. {cite:wikipedia-riffat-hassan}

## Method and contribution
Her method is internal: she reads Quran and Sunna with historical tools to dismantle accumulated readings, not to attack the text. She is celebrated in Islamic-feminist literature and opposed by conservative currents; the museum attributes these positions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-riffat-hassan}
