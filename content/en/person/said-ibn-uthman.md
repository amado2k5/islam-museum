## Overview
Sa'id ibn Uthman ibn Affan, appointed over Khurasan by Mu'awiya. al-Baladhuri reports that he was the first to cross the river with his army, and that he then fought the people of Bukhara and Samarqand.

## Samarqand and Termez
At Samarqand he lost an eye three days after the fighting began. He made peace with them for seven hundred thousand dirhams and hostages from the sons of their notables. He stayed at Termez until he took it by peaceful agreement {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
