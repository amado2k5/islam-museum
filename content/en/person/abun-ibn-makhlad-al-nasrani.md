## Overview
Abdun ibn Makhlad, a Christian of Baghdad, brother of Sa'id ibn Makhlad. Ibn al-Jawzi says that he rebuilt the old monastery by his own strength after the common people had demolished it in 271 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.82}

The museum's data hold nothing else about his life beyond this report; anything more needs an additional source.
