# Ernesto Hoost

## Summary

Ernesto Frits Hoost is a Dutch retired kickboxer, born in Heemskerk on 11 July 1965 to Surinamese parents. Known as "Mr. Perfect", he won the K-1 World Grand Prix four times — in 1997, 1999, 2000 and 2002 — and is considered one of the greatest kickboxers of all time. {cite:wikipedia-ernesto-hoost-en} {cite:wikidata-ernesto-hoost}

## Career

Hoost played football until he was fifteen, then started kickboxing at Sokudo Gym, winning his first match by knockout in 1983. He collected European and world titles in savate and Muay Thai before K-1 began, and gave up his job as a sports teacher to fight professionally. At the first K-1 Grand Prix in 1993 he reached the final and lost to Branko Cikatić; he won the one-off K-2 Grand Prix the same year. After losing the 1995 final to Peter Aerts and the 1996 final to Andy Hug, he beat Hug in 1997 to become K-1 World Champion, and won the tournament again in 1999, 2000 and 2002, accumulating victories over Aerts, Cro Cop, Le Banner, Hug, Sefo and Mark Hunt. He announced his retirement after the K-1 World Grand Prix Final in Tokyo on 2 December 2006, and lives in Hoorn with his wife and children. {cite:wikipedia-ernesto-hoost-en}
