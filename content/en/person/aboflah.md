## Summary
Hassan Suleiman, known online as AboFlah, is a Kuwaiti content creator of Somali descent born in 1998 — one of the Arab world's most-subscribed YouTubers and a record-breaking charity livestreamer; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-aboflah} {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Career
He began uploading gaming and comedy content in 2016, growing into one of the most-subscribed Arabic-language channels on YouTube with tens of millions of followers across platforms, centred on games, challenges and stunts. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Charity records
His marathon livestreams for refugee relief — including a 2021 campaign with the UNHCR that raised millions of dollars and set a Guinness record for longest continuous livestream ⁦(over 11 days)⁩ — made him a byword for creator-led philanthropy in the region. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Profile
He has been featured at Dubai's gaming and creator events, received regional influencer honours, and is frequently cited as the most influential young Arab digital creator. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Assessment
He is credited with converting entertainment reach into organised charity at unprecedented scale in Arab social media; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah} {cite:muslim500-aboflah}

His gaming and charity livestreams — including marathon Guinness-recognised streams — made him the most-subscribed Arabic-speaking YouTuber, with tens of millions of subscribers and billions of views. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Charity campaigns
He has run large livestream fundraisers for refugee causes — campaigns with UNHCR and NGOs raising millions of dollars — and built his brand around turning his audience's attention toward aid. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}

## Origins
Born Hassan Suleiman in Kuwait in 1998 to a Palestinian-Iraqi family, he began streaming games in the early 2010s — building a channel that grew into the largest Arabic-language presence on YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-aboflah}
