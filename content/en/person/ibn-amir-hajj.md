## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn Amir Hajj and also called Ibn al-Mawqit, Abu Abd Allah Shams al-Din, was a jurist of the Hanafi scholars, of Aleppo. He died in 879 AH (1474 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.48}

His books include al-Taqrir wa-l-Tahbir in three volumes, a commentary on Ibn al-Humam's al-Tahrir in usul al-fiqh; Dhakhirat al-Qasr, a commentary on the sura “Wa-l-'Asr”; and Hilyat al-Majalli in fiqh. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.48}
