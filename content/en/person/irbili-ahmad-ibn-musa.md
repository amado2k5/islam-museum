## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Sharaf al-Din Ahmad ibn Musa ibn Yunus al-Irbili al-Mawsili al-Shafi'i, a Shafi'i jurist from Mosul. He developed his knowledge of the religious sciences in the time of the Zangids. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates of life
The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 575 AH (1179 CE) and his death as 622 AH (1225 CE). The two dates come from one source, so they need a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
