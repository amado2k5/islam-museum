## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Alawi al-Hammani, a poet, and the half-brother of Isma'il al-Alawi on his mother's side. He settled in Kufa at Hammana, and was added to them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.532-533}

## Al-Mas'udi's report
He lamented Yahya ibn Umar, and he refused to meet him when al-Hasan ibn Isma'il entered Kufa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.532-533}
