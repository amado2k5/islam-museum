## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Jurayj, a client of the family of Usayd ibn Abi al-Is ibn Umayya, known by the kunya Abu al-Walid. He died in 150 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.316}

## His book
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the *Kitab al-Sunan*, which contains chapters such as purity, fasting, prayer, zakat and others. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.316}
