## Overview
Suhaym ibn al-Muhajir, one of the men of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. al-Baladhuri mentions him in the account of the Jarajima, and he belongs to [[event/treaty-with-jarajima|the peace with the Jarajima]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.219}.

## The report
al-Baladhuri reports that Byzantine horsemen went out to Jabal al-Lukam and then to Lebanon in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr. The Jarajima, the Anbat and runaway slaves joined them. Abd al-Malik was forced to make peace with them and with the tyrant of the Byzantines for a sum of money. Abd al-Malik sent Suhaym to the Byzantine. He entered upon him in disguise, showing that he was an ally and disparaging Abd al-Malik, until he was trusted. Then he turned on him with a group of Abd al-Malik's clients and soldiers whom he had prepared. He killed him and the Byzantines with him, and proclaimed safe conduct. The Jarajima scattered, and the slaves returned to their masters {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.219}.
