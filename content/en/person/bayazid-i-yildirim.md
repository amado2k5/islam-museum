## Overview
Sultan Bayezid I, son of [[person/murad-i-uthmani|Murad I]], who ruled from 791 to 805 AH (1389–1402 CE). He took the throne after his father was killed. Al-Salabi describes him as brave and eager for conquests. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.60}

He moved quickly between the Balkan and Anatolian fronts, so they called him "the Thunderbolt". He made the Christian principalities of Anatolia subject to the state. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.60}

He established friendly relations with the Serbs, and agreed that the two sons of King Lazar should rule them on condition that they gave him allegiance, tribute and a number of soldiers. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.60}

His rule ended with his capture at Ankara in 805 AH (1402 CE); the Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi}
