## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Ali, Abu al-Khayr al-Baydawi, a jurist, Qur'an commentator, theologian and expert in the principles of jurisprudence. He was born in 568 AH (1172 CE) and died in 685 AH (1286 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is the author of the commentary "Anwar al-Tanzil", which is [[work/anwar-al-tanzil|Anwar al-Tanzil]] in the museum's records. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

The dates come from one source and need a second.

## On the Halabi title page and in his preface
The cover of part 4 of the Dar al-Kutub al-Arabiyya al-Kubra printing (Cairo, 1330 AH) gives his name as al-Qadi Nasir al-Din Abu Sa'id Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Muhammad al-Shirazi al-Baydawi, after al-Bayda', a village in the district of Shiraz; it says he died in 791 AH, a date that differs from the biographical works (685 or 691 AH). {cite:anwar-al-tanzil-halabi-1330-v4 p.1} In the preface to [[work/anwar-al-tanzil|Anwar al-Tanzil]] he calls tafsir the chief of the religious sciences and says he meant to gather in it the best of what reached him from the Companions, the Successors and the early generations, with later scholars' fine points and the well-known readings. {cite:anwar-al-tanzil-fleischer-v1 p.5}
