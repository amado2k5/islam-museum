## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Ali, Abu al-Khayr al-Baydawi, a jurist, Qur'an commentator, theologian and expert in the principles of jurisprudence. He was born in 568 AH (1172 CE) and died in 685 AH (1286 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is the author of the commentary "Anwar al-Tanzil", which is [[work/anwar-al-tanzil|Anwar al-Tanzil]] in the museum's records. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
