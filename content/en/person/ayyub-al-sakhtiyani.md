## Overview
Abu Bakr Ayyub ibn Abi Tamima al-Sakhtiyani, a client, a jurist and scholar from Basra. Al-Shirazi says that he died in 131 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}

Al-Shirazi relates from al-Hasan that he was the master of the youths of Basra, and from Shu'ba that he was the master of the jurists. Malik and Sufyan al-Thawri, among others, took from him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}
