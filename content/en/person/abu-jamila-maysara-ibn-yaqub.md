## Summary
Maysara ibn Ya'qub, Abu Jamila al-Tuhawi al-Kufi, who carried the banner of Ali ibn Abi Talib. He narrated from Ali, al-Hasan ibn Ali and Uthman ibn Affan; Husayn ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, Ata ibn al-Sa'ib and Abu Khabbab al-Kalbi narrated from him. Ibn Hibban mentions him among the trustworthy, and Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah narrated from him {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13747}. The book gives no dates for him.
