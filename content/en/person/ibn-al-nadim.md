## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Muhammad ibn Ishaq al-Nadim al-Warraq al-Baghdadi, author of the *Fihrist*, a catalogue of the books of the nations and their sciences, with the titles of the books and their authors. He lived in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in the fourth century AH, and was both a boon companion and a bookseller. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 1-49}

In the introduction to his catalogue he records that his account reaches to the year 377 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 3}
