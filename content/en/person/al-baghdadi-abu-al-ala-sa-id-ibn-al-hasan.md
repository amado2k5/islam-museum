## Overview
Abu al-Ala Sa'id ibn al-Hasan ibn Isa al-Rab'i al-Baghdadi, a Baghdadi philologist whose origins go back to the lands of Rabi'a in Mosul. He narrated in the east from al-Sirafi and al-Farisi, and died in 417 AH (1026 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.627-628}
