## Overview
Ahmad al-Saghir ibn Hamallah al-Tishitti, a Sufi scholar from the Maghrib al-Kabir. The encyclopaedia describes him as an excellent, righteous scholar whose lineage ends with al-Muslimi, and he became known as al-Saghir. He was born in 1809 CE (1224 AH) and died in 1855 CE (1272 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are recorded as reported dates and need an additional source. The rest of his life needs an additional source.
