## Overview
Dawud ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas, of the Abbasid family, mentioned by al-Baladhuri and al-Mas'udi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.413} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.224}

## In al-Baladhuri
All of the Subayyin were granted as an iqta' when the Umayyad properties were seized in the early Abbasid state. They were later bought back from his heirs with their rights and boundaries, and became part of the caliphate's estates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.413}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi reports that on the day allegiance was given to Abu al-Abbas, he said from the pulpit of Kufa that the imam who had stood among them after the Messenger of God was Ali ibn Abi Talib, and that this was the one who stood now. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.224}

He also reports that he met them at Dumat al-Jandal on his return from al-Shurat, and feared to make a move in Kufa. Abu al-Abbas appointed him governor of Kufa and its territory. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.239}
