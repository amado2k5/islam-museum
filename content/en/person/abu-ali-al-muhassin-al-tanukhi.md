## Overview
The judge Abu Ali al-Muhassin ibn Abi al-Qasim Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Abi al-Fahm al-Tanukhi, a judge and man of letters of the Tanukh. He wrote on the reports of the judges and of those who did good. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The same encyclopaedia gives his birth as 327 AH and death as 384 AH; both dates need an additional source.
