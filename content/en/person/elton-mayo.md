## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Elton Mayo as the physician who rebutted Taylor's claim that money is man's only goal, arguing instead that man has social needs — appreciation, security and belonging — in what became known as Theory Y {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:31:00}.

## Man as a social being
Al-Suwaidan explains that Mayo held that things more important than money exist for man, so the principle of 'man as a social being' arose against Taylor's 'man as a material being', and factory managers began to care for the human side, motivation and loyalty {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:31:30}.
In his view, Mayo is the one to whom the social turn in management science can be traced, before Maslow {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:32:00}.
