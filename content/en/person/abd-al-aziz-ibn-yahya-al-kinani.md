## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Yahya al-Kinani al-Makki, the theologian who debated Bishr al-Marisi before al-Ma'mun on denying that the Qur'an was created. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}

Dawud ibn Ali said that he was one of the companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]]: he studied with him, kept his company for a long time, and went with him to Yemen. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}
