## Summary
Chief of the Ahlaf allies of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]] and commander of their banner in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn campaign]]; he stood firm in the battle until Hawazin were routed, then drew off with his people and his banner, falling back toward Ta'if with the least losses {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80, 90-92}.

## In the Hunayn campaign
Thaqif had two chiefs: Qarib ibn al-Aswad over the Ahlaf, and Subay' ibn al-Harith over Banu Malik {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80}. When the polytheists' ranks broke, Qarib planted his banner and fought around it until he was sure of his people's withdrawal, then leaned the banner on a tree and followed to Ta'if with his men — of the Ahlaf only two men were lost {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/91-92}.

## For young visitors
A commander of the tribe of Thaqif who led his men bravely in the battle of Hunayn; when he saw the rout he led them wisely and withdrew with them to Ta'if to keep them from being killed.
