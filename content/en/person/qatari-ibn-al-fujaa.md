## Overview
Qatari ibn al-Fuja'a, the amir Abu Nu'ama al-Tamimi al-Mazini, the renowned champion and head of the Kharijites; he rose in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr, defeated armies and his scourge grew overwhelming. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3098}

## Campaigns
Al-Hajjaj sent army after army against him and he broke them, prevailing over the land of Fars; his exploits were legendary, his bravery unheard of, and his verse famous. He was killed in 79 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3098}
