## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Ali ibn Abdallah ibn al-Abbas. He governed [[place/basra|Basra]]. In his days al-Mahdi added to the mosque the houses of Nafi' ibn al-Harith, Umm Jamil al-Hilaliyya and Amr ibn Wahb al-Thaqafi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Review note: this register used to attribute to him the conquest of Fustat in 292 AH. That report concerns [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-katib|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Katib]], a different person who came more than a century later.
