## Overview
[[person/ibn-al-athir-al-jazri|Ibn al-Athir al-Jazari]], Abu al-Sa'adat al-Mubarak ibn Muhammad, a scholar of hadith and of language, author of *al-Nihaya fi Gharib al-Hadith wa al-Athar* (The Final Word on Obscure Hadith and Report), which is his most important work, and of *Jami' al-Usul fi Ahadith al-Rasul* (Compendium of the Roots in the Hadiths of the Messenger). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.268} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.269}

## His brother
His brother was Izz al-Din Ibn al-Athir the historian, author of *al-Kamil fi al-Tarikh* (The Complete History). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.268} The book states that Ibn al-Athir al-Jazari himself was a Shafi'i. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.268}

## His work and illness
He held the diwan of al-Jazira, and then became one of the companions of Izz al-Din Mas'ud ibn Mawdud in Mosul, who appointed him head of the chancery (diwan al-insha'). After Mas'ud's death the diwan passed to his son Nur al-Din Arslan Shah. Gout kept him from writing, so he stayed at home, visited by scholars and students. It is said that he completed most of his works while ill, dictating them to his pupils. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.268}
