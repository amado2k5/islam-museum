## Summary
Father of Khuzayma, Hudhayl, Tabikha and Qam'a; a link in the agreed lineage between Ilyas ibn Mudar and Khuzayma {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## His place in the lineage
His four sons branch into large tribes: Khuzayma, ancestor of Quraysh and Banu Kinana; Hudhayl, ancestor of Banu Hudhayl; Tabikha, through whom Banu Tamim descend; and Qam'a {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16}.

## For younger visitors
Mudrika ibn Ilyas is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
