## Overview
Amr ibn Ilyas ibn Mudar, who is Mudrika, brother of Tabikha and Qam'a. The clan of Kinana ibn Khuzayma ibn Mudrika, in the lineage of the Prophet, came from him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.567}
