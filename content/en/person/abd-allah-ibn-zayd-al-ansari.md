## Overview
'Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn Tha'laba ibn 'Abd Rabbih ibn Zayd, of Banu Jusham ibn al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj — al-Ansari al-Khazraji al-Harithi, of [[group/banu-al-harith-al-khazraj|Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj]] — was known as Abu Muhammad, and is the Companion who was shown [[practice/adhan|the call to prayer]] in a dream. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 404}

## Lineage
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr quotes 'Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Ansari that Tha'laba is not among his forefathers: he is 'Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn 'Abd Rabbih ibn Zayd ibn al-Harith, while Tha'laba ibn 'Abd Rabbih was 'Abd Allah's uncle, Zayd's brother, who was wrongly inserted into his lineage. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 404} He is distinct from [[person/abdullah-ibn-zayd-ibn-asim|'Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn 'Asim al-Mazini]], son of Umm 'Umara, whose entry follows his in the book. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 405}

## Campaigns
He was present at [[event/second-pledge-of-aqaba|al-'Aqaba]], at [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] and at all the other campaigns with the Prophet, and he carried the banner of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj on the day of [[event/conquest-of-mecca|the conquest]]. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 404}

## The dream of the call to prayer
He was shown the call to prayer in his sleep, and the Prophet told [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal]] to call the adhan as 'Abd Allah ibn Zayd had seen it; the dream took place in year 1, after the Prophet had built his mosque. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 404} The editor notes that the hadith of the dream is in Ahmad, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah and al-Tirmidhi, who called it hasan sahih. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 404}

## Death and students
He died in Medina in 32 AH aged sixty-four, and [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|'Uthman]] led his funeral prayer. [[person/said-ibn-al-musayyab|Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab]], 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla and his son Muhammad ibn 'Abd Allah ibn Zayd narrated from him. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 405}
