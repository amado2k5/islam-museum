## Summary
Shaykh Abu'l-Barakat 'Abdallah b. al-Hadrami b. al-Husayn, known as Ibn al-Shirzaji; famed in the twin sciences of hadith and fiqh, he held Basra's judgeship and taught at Mosul's old Atabakiyya — Ibn Khallikan heard part of al-Tha'labi's tafsir under him, who licensed him to transmit all his narrations and wrote it for him in his audition register in 567. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
