## Overview
Salih ibn Madrak, amir of the Tayy. He and his people rose up in the year nine AH and plundered the Iraqi caravan, and, according to al-Suyuti, the property taken from al-Hajjaj was worth a million mithqal {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.576}.

## The account of al-Mas'udi
al-Mas'udi reports that Nabhan, Sanbas and others of Tayy fell upon the pilgrims at the plain of al-Ajfar in the year 285 AH. The pilgrims were thrown into confusion, many people died of thirst and killing, and some two million dinars were taken {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.622}.

## Notes for researchers
- The two accounts differ in date and in amount. The "nine" year in al-Suyuti corresponds, in the museum's data, to 289 AH, and it needs an additional source to confirm.
