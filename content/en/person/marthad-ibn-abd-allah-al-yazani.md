## Overview
Abu al-Khayr Marthad ibn Abdallah al-Yazani, judge of [[place/alexandria|Alexandria]] and a Sunni jurist. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

## Transmission
Abu Raja' Yazid ibn Abi Habib took from him, and Bukayr ibn Abdallah ibn al-Ashajj and Amr ibn al-Harith transferred to him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}
