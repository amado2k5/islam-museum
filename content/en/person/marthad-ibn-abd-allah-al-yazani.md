## Overview
Marthad ibn 'Abdallah, the imam Abu al-Khayr al-Yazani of Egypt, the scholar and mufti of the Egyptian lands — Yazan being a clan of Himyar. He narrated from Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, Zayd ibn Thabit, Abu Basra al-Ghifari, 'Uqba ibn 'Amir, 'Amr ibn al-'As and his son, and attached himself to 'Uqba for a time, learning fiqh from him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3812}

## Standing in Egypt
Abu Sa'id ibn Yunus says he was the mufti of the people of Egypt in his day, and 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Marwan, the governor of Egypt, would bring him into his council for fatwa. Ibn 'Awn dates his death to 90 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3812}
