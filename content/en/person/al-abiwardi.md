## Overview
Abu'l-Muzaffar Muhammad b. Abi'l-'Abbas al-Umawi al-Abiwardi, the famous poet; among the celebrated litterateurs — transmitter, genealogist, poet — his lineage joining Mu'awiya b. Abi Sufyan. Most famous of his verse is the alfiyya opening "draw near — if you will not grant a visit, at least a greeting," in which he has rare meanings. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
