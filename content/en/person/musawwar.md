## Overview
Musawwar, a rebel against Abbasid authority. Al-Suyuti says that Musa ibn Bugha and Bakbak set out for al-Sinn in search of him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.563}

## The effect of his rising
Their rising was the cause of the exposure of the affair of al-Muhtadi's letter and of his killing. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.563}
