## Overview
Ubayd ibn Jabr, Abu Ja'far al-Ghifari, a client of the Ghifar and Egyptian, a Successor. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14961} He narrated from his master Abu Basra al-Ghifari, and Kulayb ibn Dhahl al-Hadrami narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14961} Abu Dawud has one hadith from him, and al-Fasawi listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14961} Ibn Yunus reports that he was said to be a Copt among those sent by al-Muqawqis with Maria. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14961}
