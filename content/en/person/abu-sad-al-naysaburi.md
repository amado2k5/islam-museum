## Overview
Muhammad b. Yahya, the scholar Abu Sa'd al-Naysaburi, Muhyi'l-Din — shaykh of the Shafi'is and companion of al-Ghazali; to him came the headship of the madhhab in Khurasan, jurists sought him from the lands, and he composed works including al-Muhit, a commentary on al-Wasit. He died in Ramadan a martyr at the hands of the Ghuzz at seventy-two, mourned by a company including 'Ali al-Bayhaqi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 631}
