## Overview
al-Hasan ibn Abd Allah ibn Hamdan was given the title "Nasir al-Dawla" by the caliph when Ibn Ra'iq was killed by treachery at Tikrit in 330 AH, and he took his place {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.609-610}.

## Confrontation with Tuzun
He set out with a large army of Bedouins and Kurds to fight Tuzun, but he and al-Muttaqi were defeated and fled from Akbara to Mosul and then to Nasibin {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.609-610}.

al-Mas'udi reports that al-Muttaqi and his brother Sayf al-Dawla corresponded with him to help him and rescue him from Tuzun's dominance, on condition that rule be delegated to them {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.691-692}.
