## Summary
Abd al-Khaliq ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd Allah, Abu al-Fadl, al-Wabari al-Khwarazmi, a blind Hanafi jurist, legal theorist and man of letters. Ibn al-Shi'ar, quoted in the book, describes him as one of the leaders and imams of the companions of Abu Hanifa, responsible for fatwas and teaching in Khwarazm. Dates are not given. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7165}
