## Summary
Shaddad al-Majdhum was a famous worshipper named among those content with God. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 509, sequence 510, on pages 145 and 146. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/145} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146}

## Visited by companions of al-Hasan
Mukhallad ibn al-Husayn relates that in Basra there was a man called Shaddad, whom leprosy struck without end. Companions of al-Hasan came to visit him and asked how he was. He said: well; I have not missed my night portion since I fell ill, and my only trouble is that I cannot attend the congregational prayer. Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146}
