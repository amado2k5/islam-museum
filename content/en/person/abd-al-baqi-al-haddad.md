## Overview
'Abd al-Baqi b. Hamza b. al-Husayn al-Haddad, the Hanbali faradi, Abu'l-Fath; born 425, a righteous, good, near shaykh who read fiqh and had a hand in inheritance-law and arithmetic, hearing Abu Muhammad al-Jawhari. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 399-400}

## His work
His Kitab al-idah fi'l-fara'id he composed on Ahmad's madhhab, rendering the school's transmission with a fine edition, and in the chapter on inheriting by distant kin he set down the case of the three aunts dividing an estate in fifths, saying: "this is the text transmitted from Ahmad." He died Saturday 14 Sha'ban 493. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 399-400}
