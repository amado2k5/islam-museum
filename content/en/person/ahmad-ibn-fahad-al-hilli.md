## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Fahd al-Asadi al-Hilli was an Imami jurist, that is a scholar of the Imamiyya, a Shi'i school. He was born at al-Hilla al-Sayfiyya, to which his name refers, and he died and is buried at Karbala. The printed text gives his birth as 757 AH and his death as 841 AH, that is 1356 and 1437 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 220}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'al-Muhadhdhab al-Bari' ila Sharh al-Nafi'', 'al-Mujaz al-Hawi' and 'al-Muharrar', all on jurisprudence, and 'Iddat al-Da'i', printed, and 'al-Tahsin fi Sifat al-Arifin', printed. The entry is short and says nothing more {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 220}.
