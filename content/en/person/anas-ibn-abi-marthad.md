## Summary
Anas ibn Abi Marthad al-Ghanawi, a Companion of Banu Ghani; he volunteered to guard the Muslim camp on the night before the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn expedition]] in Shawwal, year 8 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 415}.

## Guarding the night of Hunayn
When the Prophet marched from Mecca to Hunayn and his scout brought word that Hawazin and Thaqif had massed with their wealth, women and flocks, he smiled and said: "that is the Muslims' booty tomorrow, God willing" — and Anas ibn Abi Marthad al-Ghanawi volunteered to stand guard that night {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 415; sunan-abi-dawud}.

## For young visitors
Anas was a brave Companion who stayed awake all night guarding the Muslims' camp before a great battle; the army slept safe thanks to his vigil.
