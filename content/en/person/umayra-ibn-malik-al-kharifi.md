## Overview
Umayra ibn Malik al-Kharifi of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]]. Ibn Hisham counts him among their delegation to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}.

## The delegation
He was in the [[event/delegation-of-hamdan|delegation of Hamdan]] who met the Prophet on his return from Tabuk, together with Malik ibn al-Namat Dhu al-Mash'ar. Ibn Hisham named them on the authority of Abu Ishaq al-Subay'i {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}.
