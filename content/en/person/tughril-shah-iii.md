## Overview
Tughril Shah ibn Arslan, last of the Seljuks of Iraq. al-Suyuti reports that he died in the year 590 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.693-694}.

al-Suyuti transmits from al-Dhahabi that the number of Seljuk kings was more than twenty, the first of them Tughrul Beg, who restored al-Qa'im to Baghdad, and that the duration of their state was a hundred and sixty years {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.693-694}.
