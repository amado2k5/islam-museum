## Overview
Tughril Shah ibn Arslan ibn Tughril ibn Muhammad ibn Malikshah the Turk, last of the Malikshahid Seljuk kings; he rose against the caliph al-Nasir, his army was routed and his vizier Ibn Yunus captured, and al-Nasir then dispatched Khwarazmshah against him. He died in 590 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2061}
