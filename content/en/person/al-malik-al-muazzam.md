## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Isa ibn al-Adil, son of al-Malik al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub. He ruled Damascus from his father's death in 615 AH until his own death. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 474}

Ibn al-Athir describes him as a scholar of several sciences, among them jurisprudence according to the school of Abu Hanifa, grammar and philology. He ordered a dictionary compiled that would gather the "Sihah" of al-Jawhari, what al-Azhari had missed of the "Tahdhib", and the "Jamhara" of Ibn Duraid, and ordered the Musnad of Ahmad ibn Hanbal arranged by chapters. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 474}

He is said to have received scholars from every region, honoured them and paid them stipends. Ibn al-Athir reports his saying that his belief in the principles was what Abu Ja'far al-Tahawi had written down. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 474}

He directed that he be shrouded in white and buried in a niche with no building over it, and during his illness he used to say that he had from God, in the matter of Damietta, something he hoped would bring him mercy. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 474}

He died on Friday at the end of Dhu al-Qa'da 624 AH at Dusunturiya, after about ten years and five months in power, and his son al-Nasir Dawud succeeded him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 474}
