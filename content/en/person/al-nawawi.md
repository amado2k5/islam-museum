## Overview
The imam Abu Zakariya Yahya al-Nawawi, a Sunni jurist and hadith scholar, whom Ibn Rajab describes as the imam jurist, ascetic and model. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 26}

## The Forty
He compiled "al-Arba'in" of comprehensive hadiths ([[work/arbaun-nawawi|the Forty Hadiths of al-Nawawi]]). He began from the twenty-six hadiths in the session of "comprehensive hadiths" of Ibn al-Salah, and added to them until they reached forty-two hadiths. It became well known, its memorising spread, and commentators took care of it. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 26}

## His commentary on Sahih Muslim
The data attribute to him the book "Sharh Sahih Muslim", which appears in the references of the book's editor. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
