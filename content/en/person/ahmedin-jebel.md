## Summary
Ahmedin Jebel — in Amharic አሕመዲን ጀበል — is an Ethiopian dawah preacher and author who works in Amharic and Afaan Oromo. {cite:tiktok-ahmedin-jebel} {cite:youtube-ifa-rahmataa-tv} His main channel is his Facebook page (facebook.com/ahmedinjebel99), which counted 1.4 million followers by the wave harvest's read of his posts' metadata on 7 November 2026 — a dated figure that rests on that harvest read, because Facebook profile fetch is blocked in the build environment, and not on a direct read by the museum. {cite:facebook-ahmedin-jebel} His TikTok account (@ahmedinjebelm99), whose Amharic bio states that it is his official TikTok account, counted 263,700 followers when read directly on 7 November 2026. {cite:tiktok-ahmedin-jebel}

## Platforms and work
His TikTok account held 148 videos and 1,400,000 likes in total when its profile was read directly on 7 November 2026, and it carries no platform verification badge, its standing as his official account resting on the statement in its own bio. {cite:tiktok-ahmedin-jebel} An Afaan Oromo lecture of his, "Islaamummaa Sabaa Fi Sabboonummaa", is hosted on the Ifa Rahmataa TV YouTube channel, alongside the Amharic output on his own accounts. {cite:youtube-ifa-rahmataa-tv} The Mediterranean Politics article "Salafism and the local" names him among the new ustaz actors of Ethiopian dawah and as the leader of Impact Makers, and records that he had over 1 million followers in its 2021–2023 interview period. {cite:tandfonline-salafism-local-ostebo}

## Public voice
The African Studies Review article of 2023 describes him as a Muslim journalist and activist who in 2011 published the book Ethiopian Muslim: A History of Domination and Resistance, and quotes his statement in the May 2021 Grand Iftar dispute that "Muslims refuse anything less than equality and will not accept being second class citizens". {cite:asr-ostebo-religious-dynamics-2023} The Religions article of 2024 records that he works as an ustaz (teacher) and activist and as social affairs advisor to the president of the Ethiopian Islamic Affairs Supreme Council, and that he wrote three books critical of the history of Ethiopian Muslims and their relationship with Christian kings and two other books on Islam and contemporary issues. {cite:mdpi-ahmedin-jebel-religious-other-2024} His video speech of 3 June 2023 was streamed live from his own official Facebook page and attracted over half a million viewers, 47,000 reactions and 6,100 comments. {cite:mdpi-ahmedin-jebel-religious-other-2024} The Rift Valley Institute report frames the ustazes he belongs to as a new category of actor who have become influential religious role models in Ethiopia. {cite:rift-valley-being-muslim-ethiopian}
