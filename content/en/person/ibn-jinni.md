## Summary
Uthman ibn Jinni al-Mawsili, the grammarian and philologist, one of the leading masters of grammar and Arabic, who settled in Baghdad and taught there until his death in the year 392 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.219-221}

## His origin and upbringing
His father Jinni was a Byzantine slave owned by Sulayman ibn Fahd ibn Ahmad al-Azdi al-Mawsili. His son Uthman, Abu al-Fath, grew up in a house of learning in Mosul, then settled in Baghdad and taught there. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.220}

## His works
He wrote books on grammar in which he excelled and did well, among them "al-Lumaʿ," "al-Taʿaqub fi al-ʿArabiyya," a commentary on rhyme (Sharh al-Qawafi), "al-Mudhakkar wa al-Muannath," "Sirr al-Sinaʿa," "al-Khasaʾis," and other works. He also composed poetry and did it well. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.219-220}

## His death
He died in Baghdad on a Friday, two nights before the end of Safar in the year 392 AH, and was buried at al-Khayzuraniyya. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.221}
