## Overview
Arhab, a son of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim]], entered [[place/athafit|Athafit]] with [[group/banu-salman-hamdan|Banu Salman]] after the settlement between his father and [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.98} The narrator says Banu Salman disliked the settlement, took Arhab up with them and brought him to [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], where [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn al-Hadi]] met him and offered him single combat, which he did not take up, from fear in the narrator's words. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.98} He then entered Athafit, where al-Hadi's governor Abu Umar was killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} After the war at Athafit Banu Rabi'a and Banu Sarim asked al-Hadi to release them against Arhab's villages in al-Subay' and Khurfan, and he refused. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.105} See [[event/arhab-and-banu-salman-attack-on-khiwan-285]] and [[event/arhab-takes-athafit-and-abu-umar-is-killed-285]]. The book's unflattering descriptions of him are the narrator's words.
