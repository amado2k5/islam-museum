## Overview
Abu Mansur Mawhub ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Jawaliqi al-Baghdadi, a philologist and man of letters. He was born in Baghdad in 465 AH (1073 CE), grew up there, and died in 540 AH (1145 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His nisba
He is attributed to "al-Jawaliq", a Persian word which the source says means justice. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
