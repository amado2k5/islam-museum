## Summary
Rum Mehmed Pasha, a vizier of [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 139}.

## The Karaman deportations
Al-Salabi records that when Mehmed annexed the Karamanid emirate and ordered its craftsmen transferred to Constantinople, Rum Mehmed Pasha wronged the deportees — among them scholars and men of merit such as Ahmad Çelebi ibn Amir Ali; when the sultan learned of it he apologised to the scholar and returned him and his companions home in honour {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 139}.
