## Overview
The hafiz Abu Musa Muhammad b. Abi Bakr 'Umar b. Abi 'Isa al-Isfahani al-Madini; imam of his age in memorisation and knowledge — he travelled from Isfahan seeking hadith then returned. Useful works: Kitab al-mughith in one volume completing and supplementing al-Barawi's al-Gharibayn, Kitab al-ziyadat appended to his shaykh Abu'l-Fadl Muhammad b. Tahir al-Maqdisi's al-Ansab, and a dhayl on Ibn Manda's Ma'rifat al-Sahaba. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
