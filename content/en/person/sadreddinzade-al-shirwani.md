## Overview
Muhammad Amin ibn Sadr al-Din al-Shirwani was an exegete of the Qur'an. His nisba is to Shirwan, which al-Zirikli places in the districts of Bukhara {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He lived in Amid (Diyar Bakr) and stayed for a time in al-Astana {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. Al-Zirikli prints the year of his death as 1036 AH and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. The Turkish encyclopaedia of Islam (TDV) is more exact: he died on 3 Dhu al-Hijja 1036 AH, 15 August 1627 CE, and was buried at Uskudar at his own request {cite:tdv-sirvani-sadreddinzade vol. 39 p. 208}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists for him, all in manuscript, a gloss on al-Baydawi's commentary on the Qur'an, left unfinished, a commentary on Surat al-Fath, and «al-Fawa'id al-Khaqaniyya», which covers 53 sciences {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. The entry is short and names no teachers or students.
