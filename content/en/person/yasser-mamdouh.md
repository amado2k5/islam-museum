## Summary
Yasser Mamdouh is an Egyptian preacher and content creator who presents short videos on religious and social topics in a simplified style addressed to young people. {cite:tawthiq-yasser-mamdouh}

He began uploading to his YouTube channel on 16 July 2015, and his video descriptions give his official accounts: his Facebook page and his Instagram account. {cite:youtube-yassermamdouh}

## His work
He became known for his weekly "Friday video" series; among his topics are "Why did God create us?", repentance and prayer. {cite:youtube-yassermamdouh}

He takes part in the Eh El-Moshkla? ("What's the problem?") podcast in the role of the questioner who puts young people's common questions, alongside Amir Mounir and Mohamed Elghaleez. {cite:tawthiq-yasser-mamdouh}

He founded Awtad, a training platform offering intensive courses for young people in digital content-making skills, from scriptwriting to filming, design and editing. {cite:tawthiq-yasser-mamdouh}

## Reach and audience
ThoughtLeaders' analytics page for his channel, checked on 25 October 2026, recorded over 1.29 million subscribers and more than 85 million total views; these are dated platform figures, not fixed facts. {cite:youtube-yassermamdouh}
