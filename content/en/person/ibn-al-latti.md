## Summary
Ibn al-Latti, the musnid of the age, a blessed man; he heard from Abu'l-Waqt, Sa'id b. al-Banna and others, and Mas'ud al-Thaqafi and the Isfahanis gave him ijaza — the last to transmit al-Baghawi's hadith with lofty isnad; he spread his hadith through Syria, returned at the end of 434, and died at Baghdad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 171}
