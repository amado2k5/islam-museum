## Summary
Of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his son al-Nu'man and perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Adi with Urwa ibn Abd al-Uzza, saying he perished in the land of Abyssinia, and that his son al-Nu'man ibn Adi was with him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi ibn Ka'b]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- His printed lineage (ibn Nadla ibn Abd al-Uzza) meets Urwa ibn Abd al-Uzza's lineage, which would make Urwa his uncle by descent — the text does not mention the kinship (p. 9).
