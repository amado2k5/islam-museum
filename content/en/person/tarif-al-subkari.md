## Overview
Tarif al-Subkari, an Abbasid commander. He was among the sultan's companions with al-Hajjaj in the year 312 AH, and Abu Tahir al-Qarmati captured him on the day of Kufa {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.115}.

al-Qahir billah then imprisoned him in a pit. When al-Qahir was deposed in the year 322 AH, they opened his place, took him out, and imprisoned al-Qahir in his stead {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.210}.

Ibn al-Athir reports that al-Qahir sent him to Mu'nis, calling him to the palace, and that Mu'nis was afterwards seized {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.190}.
