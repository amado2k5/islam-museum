## Overview
A great man of [[place/bahrayn|Bahrain]] from [[group/abd-al-qays|Abd al-Qays]]. The Prophet sent [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to him before the conquest of Mecca, and he embraced Islam and his Islam was good. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
Ibn Ishaq ends the account of the delegation of Abd al-Qays by saying that the Prophet had sent al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami before the conquest of Mecca to al-Mundhir ibn Sawa al-Abdi, and that he embraced Islam and his Islam was good. Then he died after the Messenger of God, before the apostasy of the people of Bahrain, and al-Ala was with him as the Messenger of God's governor over Bahrain. See [[event/islam-of-al-mundhir-ibn-sawa|the Islam of al-Mundhir ibn Sawa]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222} The Sealed Nectar counts him among the kings of the Arabs to whom the Prophet wrote after al-Hudaybiyya. See [[event/letters-to-rulers-and-kings|the letters to the rulers and kings]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.358}
