## Overview
Al-Malik al-Rahim Abu Nasr Khusraw Firuz, son of Abu Kalijar ibn Sultan al-Dawla ibn Baha al-Dawla ibn Adud al-Dawla. He was the last of the [[polity/buyid-dynasty|Buyid]] kings in Iraq, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

When his father died he marched on Basra, hoping for it, and he was victorious. Then he fought his brothers over the kingdom, and seized Basra and al-Ahwaz. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

His war was linked with al-Basasiri and Dubays ibn Mazyad until Tughril Beg entered Baghdad in 447 AH, and the Buyid state came to an end. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
