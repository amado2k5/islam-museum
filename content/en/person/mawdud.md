## Overview
Mawdud, lord of Mosul. He came with an army in 507 AH to fight the king of the Franks in Jerusalem, and a great battle took place between them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.661}

## His death
He then returned to Damascus and prayed the Friday prayer in the mosque, when an Isma'ili (batini) leapt upon him and wounded him, and he died the same day. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.661}
