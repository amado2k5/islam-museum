Mehdi Hassan Khan (18 July 1927 – 13 June 2012), known as Mehdi Hassan, was a Pakistani ghazal and playback singer born at Luna, in the Jaipur State of British India, into a family of hereditary Kalawant musicians.{cite:wikipedia-mehdi-hassan-en}{cite:wikidata-mehdi-hassan} Known as Shahenshah-e-Ghazal, 'Emperor of Ghazal', he migrated with his family to Pakistan at partition in 1947 and sang for more than 300 films.{cite:wikipedia-mehdi-hassan-en}

He received the Pride of Performance (1985), the Nishan-e-Imtiaz (2006) and the Hilal-e-Imtiaz (2010), Pakistan's highest civilian honours.{cite:wikipedia-mehdi-hassan-en}{cite:wikipedia-mehdi-hassan-ur} After a long chronic lung illness he died at Karachi on 13 June 2012.{cite:wikipedia-mehdi-hassan-en}{cite:wikidata-mehdi-hassan}
