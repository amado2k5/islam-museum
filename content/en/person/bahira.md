Bahira — his name in the author being Jurjis — a Christian monk of the people of [[place/busra|Busra]] in Syria. When [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]]'s trading caravan alighted at his dwelling he came out to them, made them food and honoured them; then looking at the boy [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]] he recognised in him a description from their books and the seal of prophethood between his shoulders, so he counselled returning him to Mecca out of fear for him of the Jews. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
