## Overview
Umm al-Sharif, paternal aunt of Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Isa ibn al-Shaykh, lord of Amid {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}.

## Her letter to al-Mu'tadid
In the year 286 AH she corresponded with Shu'ba ibn Shihab al-Yashkuri, the envoy of al-Mu'tadid to her. She gave generous counsel in her letter, and added verses to it, among them: "Accept the counsel of a mother whose heart is fearful and compassionate for you" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}.

Shu'ba presented the letter to al-Mu'tadid, who admired her verse and her intelligence. He said that he hoped to accept her intercession for many of the people {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}.

## Her nephew's reply
Her nephew said to her envoy: "States are not governed by the opinions of women, nor is kingship ruled by their minds" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}.

## After the conquest of Amid
When Amid was conquered, and her nephew came down under safe conduct, she unveiled her face and composed verse, the first line of which is: "By the vicissitudes of time and its turns" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}.
