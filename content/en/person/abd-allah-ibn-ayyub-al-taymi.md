## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Ayyub al-Taymi, a hadith scholar of Basra, a client of the Banu Taym Allah ibn Tha'laba. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his death as 209 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
