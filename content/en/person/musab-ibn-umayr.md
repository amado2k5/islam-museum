## Overview
[[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr al-Abdari al-Qurashi]], one of the first to embrace Islam, the first envoy the Prophet sent to Yathrib. He then carried the banner of the Emigrants at [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] and [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] and was martyred at Uhud. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.144, p.205, p.252, p.282}

## In Mecca
When his mother learned of his Islam, she starved him and expelled him from her house, though he had been among the most comfortable of people, until his skin became rough. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.89}

## Envoy to Yathrib
After the Pledge of al-Aqaba, the Prophet sent Mus'ab with those who had pledged to teach the Muslims of Yathrib the laws of Islam. He alighted at As'ad ibn Zurara, and they spread Islam with great zeal and energy; he was known as "the reciter". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.144} No house of the Ansar remained without Muslim men and women, except the Banu Umayya ibn Zayd, Khatma and Wa'il, until the year of the Trench. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.146}

## Badr and Uhud
At Badr the Prophet gave him the general banner of the army. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.205} At Uhud the Prophet made him the bearer of the banner of the Emigrants' battalion. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.252}

## His martyrdom
He was killed on the day of Uhud, and shrouded in a cloak which, if his head was covered, his feet showed, and if his feet were covered, his head showed. Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf said: "Mus'ab ibn Umayr was killed, and he was better than me." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.282}

## For young visitors
Mus'ab ibn Umayr was a young man who was wealthy in Mecca. When he embraced Islam, his family abandoned him and he went hungry. The Prophet then sent him to Medina to teach people the laws of Islam, and Islam entered the houses of the Ansar. He was killed at Uhud while carrying the banner.

## Notes for researchers
- *al-Rahiq al-Makhtum* relays from Ibn Hisham, Ibn Ishaq and *Rahmah lil-Alamin*; their texts have not yet been checked, and they should be before the entry is relied on.
- The wording "he starved him and expelled him", and the length of his mission in Medina and its date, are not given here by year; a second source for the dates should be added.

## The leadership of the army at Badr (*al-Rahiq al-Makhtum*, p. 205)
The Prophet gave him the banner of the general command, which was white, when the army went out to Badr ([[event/badr-expedition-setup|the preparations before Badr]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.205}
