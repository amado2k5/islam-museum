## Overview
Husayn ibn Ali ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib. He rose in the days of al-Hadi and was killed at Fakhkh, six miles from Mecca, on the day of Tarwiya. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.316}
