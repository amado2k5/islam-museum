## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Harith al-Sayigh, a companion of Ahmad ibn Hanbal, from whom he transmitted most of the recorded questions. Abu Bakr al-Khallal says that Ahmad liked him and honoured him, and that he related many questions from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15400}
