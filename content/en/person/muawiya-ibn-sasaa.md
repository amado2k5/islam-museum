## Overview
Mu'awiya ibn Sa'sa'a ibn Mu'awiya, a paternal cousin of al-Ahnaf ibn Qays. He helped Humayri ibn Hilal in the dispute over al-Marghab. When Bashir complained that al-Marghab was the pasture of their camels and cattle, Mu'awiya said that they did not want to overcome them in defence of their pasture over a small matter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
