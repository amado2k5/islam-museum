## Overview
The memoriser Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahim ibn Sa'id al-Zuhri al-Barqi al-Misri, a hadith memoriser, historian and jurist. He died in 270 AH (883 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His birth date, his teachers and his works are not recorded in the museum's data, and need an additional source.
