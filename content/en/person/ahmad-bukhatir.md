## Overview
Ahmad Bukhatir (born 1975 in Sharjah) is an Emirati nasheed singer and imam of a mosque. He has been known for his nasheeds since the 1990s, such as "Kami al-Awsaiti" and "Ya Binti". He belongs to a generation that renewed the Arabic nasheed without music, keeping to the mawwal and the vocal rhythm. {cite:wikidata}
