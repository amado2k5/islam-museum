## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn 'Abbad ibn Quraysh al-Lakhmi, qadi of Seville and founder of the 'Abbadid state; its people rose against Yahya and killed him, the matter settled for Muhammad — he ruled Seville, then ruled Cordoba and other cities after that; when he died his son al-Mu'tadid-billah 'Abbad took his place. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 253}
