## Overview
Abu Shajara Amr ibn Abd al-Uzza al-Sulami, a poet and horseman of the Banu Sulaym, and the son of [[person/al-khansa|al-Khansa]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136}

## In the Ridda wars
Al-Baladhuri says he commanded a force of the Banu Sulaym at Jaw Qaraqir, or it is said at al-Nuqra, and fought [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] in the [[event/ridda-wars|Ridda]]. One Muslim was killed, and then their force scattered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136}

## After his conversion
He then embraced Islam and came to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] while he was giving to the poor, and asked him for a gift. Umar said to him: "Did you not say: 'I was struck by the wind from Khalid's host, and I hope to live after it'?" and struck him with the whip. He answered: "Islam has erased that, Commander of the Faithful." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136}
