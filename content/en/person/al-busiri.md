## The author of the Burda
Sharaf al-Din al-Busiri, Muhammad ibn Saʿid ibn Hammad, born in Dalas, originally of Maghribi descent and raised in Busir. He was born on Tuesday, the first of Shawwal 608 AH in the region of Dalas, and excelled in verse {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 752}.

## His standing among his contemporaries
The hafiz Ibn Sayyid al-Nas, as al-Suyuti reports it in "Husn al-Muhadara," said that he was better than al-Jazzar and al-Warraq, two of the best-known poets of his age in Egypt {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 752}.
