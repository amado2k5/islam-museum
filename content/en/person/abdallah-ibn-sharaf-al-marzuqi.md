## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Sharaf ibn Najda al-Marzuqi, a Shafi'i jurist who attended the lectures of Taqi al-Din ibn Razin, and who wrote much poetry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} He was a repeater at the Husayni shrine in Cairo. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} His work includes a commentary on al-Tanbih. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} He died before 700 AH or shortly after, according to the scan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966}
