## Summary
Youssef Mansour is an Egyptian billionaire businessman, born on 21 May 1944, who is a part owner of the Mansour Group alongside his younger brothers Mohamed and Yasseen. Forbes estimated his fortune at US$1.9 billion in March 2020. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-en}

## Life
Mansour took a bachelor's degree in engineering at North Carolina State University and a master's in business administration at Auburn University. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-en} He is married with five children and lives in Cairo. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-en} The two Wikipedia articles agree on his birth date of 21 May 1944, while Wikidata gives only the year 1940 and the museum's commissioning queue gave 1945; the built date follows the articles and is marked debated in the data note. {cite:wikidata-youssef-mansour} No source consulted for this record states a religion for Mansour himself. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-ar}

## Work
Mansour shares ownership of the Mansour Group, the family conglomerate founded by his father Loutfy Mansour, whose businesses include the Egyptian distribution of General Motors vehicles and Caterpillar equipment. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-en} Unlike his brother Mohamed, he has held no government office and keeps a low public profile; his recorded public footprint is largely the Forbes billionaire listings. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-mansour-en}
