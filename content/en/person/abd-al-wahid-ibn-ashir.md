## Overview
Abd al-Wahid ibn Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Ashir al-Ansari was a jurist who also composed verse. He was of Andalusian origin, grew up in Fez and died there at the age of fifty {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}. The entry gives his years as 990 to 1040 AH, 1582 to 1631 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left several works, among them 'al-Murshid al-Mu'in ala al-Daruri min Ulum al-Din', printed, a versified text of Maliki jurisprudence; a rhymed poem on 'Amal al-Rub' al-Mujib'; 'Tanbih al-Khillan', printed, on the science of the orthography of the Qur'an; 'Fath al-Mannan', in manuscript, a commentary on 'Mawrid al-Zam'an' on Qur'anic orthography; and 'Shifa' al-Qalb al-Jarih bi-Sharh Burdat al-Madih', in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}.
