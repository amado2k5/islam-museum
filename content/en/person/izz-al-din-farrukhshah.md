## Overview
Izz al-Din Farrukhshah, nephew of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]]. Ibn al-Athir says that most of the action fell to him on the day of the Ford of Sorrows in 575 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.95-96}

## The verse
Ibn al-Athir relates his own account that, in that situation, he recalled two verses of al-Mutanabbi, so death became small in his eyes and he threw himself at it, and that this was the cause of victory. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.95-96}

He took part in [[event/battle-ford-of-sorrows-575|the Battle of the Ford of Sorrows]] and [[event/conquest-shaqif-578|the conquest of Shaqif]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}
