## Summary
Saliha Erdim is a Turkish family counsellor and author, born in Havza, Samsun, in 1956. She works on marriage, parenting and family communication, co-founded the Yeryüzü Eğitim Danışmanlık consultancy, and has presented family-education programmes on Turkish radio since 1997. Her books on family life are widely read, and her talks reach a large online audience — about 1.4 million followers on Instagram by Heepsy's count for the November 2026 harvest, and about 336,000 subscribers on her YouTube channel by ThoughtLeaders' count (dated platform figures, not museum-verified facts). {cite:kitapyurdu-saliha-erdim}

## Early life and education
Erdim was born in 1956 in Havza, in the province of Samsun. She attended primary school in Vezirköprü, middle school at the Çorum Health School, and high school at the Zeynep Kâmil Health Vocational High School in Istanbul. She began nursing studies at the Hacettepe Higher School of Nursing but left because of the unrest of the period, and later left an Open Education social-sciences programme over the headscarf restrictions then in force. She completed a bachelor's and then a master's degree in behavioural sciences at International Newport University, writing her theses on childhood jealousy and adult jealousy. In 2012 she completed the Cognitive and Behavioural Therapies Association (KDTD) training programme, run to the standards of the European Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Therapies, qualifying as a cognitive and behavioural therapist. {cite:kitapyurdu-saliha-erdim}

## Career
Erdim has worked for some twenty-five years in family education and counselling, giving courses, conferences and seminars in Turkey and abroad. With her husband Zekeriya Erdim she has presented the "Aile Okulu" (Family School) programme on Marmara FM since 1997, and later on Moral FM; between 2003 and 2006 she presented the weekday "Hanımefendi" (The Lady) programme alone. She wrote for magazines including Mektup, Ferzan, Beyan, Yenidünya and Hanımefendi, and served as editor-in-chief of Hanımefendi magazine. She is a founding partner of Yeryüzü Eğitim Danışmanlık, through which she continues her professional work as an educator and consultant. An education association named her "Mother of the Year" in 2005. She is married and the mother of six children. {cite:kitapyurdu-saliha-erdim}

## Books
Her first book, Sıradan Konulara Sıradışı Yaklaşımlar (Extraordinary Approaches to Ordinary Topics), was later expanded and republished in two volumes as Hayat Filminden Kurtarıcı Kareler (Life-Saving Frames from the Film of Life). Her other books include Ailemde Huzur İstiyorum (I Want Peace in My Family), Eşimi Anlamak İstiyorum (I Want to Understand My Spouse), Kendime Yardım Etmek İstiyorum (I Want to Help Myself), Kendime Düşeni Yaparak Mutlu Olmak İstiyorum, Sorusu Olmayanın Cevabı Yoktur, a book on sibling jealousy in parent-child relations, and İnsanca Yaşamak (Living Humanely, 2025); Kitapyurdu lists eleven works under her name. {cite:kitapyurdu-saliha-erdim}
