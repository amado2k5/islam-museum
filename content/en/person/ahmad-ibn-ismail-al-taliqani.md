## Overview
Ahmad ibn Isma'il ibn Yusuf al-Taliqani, Radi al-Din al-Qazwini, was a preacher and scholar of hadith, from Qazvin by birth and death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}. He stayed for a time in Baghdad and taught at the Nizamiyya, and he was an imam in Shafi'i jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}. Al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 512 AH (1118 CE) and 590 AH (1194 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.

## His works
He wrote "al-Tibyan fi Masa'il al-Qur'an", in which he replied to the Hululiyya and the Jahmiyya, and "Ta'rif al-Ashab Sawa' al-Sabil", in manuscript, at Chester Beatty under number 3557 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.
