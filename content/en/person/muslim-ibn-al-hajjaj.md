## Overview
Abu al-Husayn Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj al-Qushayri al-Nisaburi, a scholar of hadith and jurisprudence. Ibn al-Nadim lists books by him, among them the *Sahih*, *al-Asma' wa al-Kuna*, *al-Awhad*, *al-Mufrad*, *al-Tarikh* and *al-Tabaqat*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.322}
