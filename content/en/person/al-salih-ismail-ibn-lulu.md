## Overview
Al-Salih Isma'il b. Badr al-Din Lu'lu', lord of Mosul after his father; his father had sent with him a detachment to Hulagu in 655 when he made for Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 278}; the Tatars took Mosul by deception in 660 after months' siege, gave the sword free rein nine days, spared him a while, then killed him and killed his son 'Ala' al-Din the king {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 308}.
