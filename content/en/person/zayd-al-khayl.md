## Summary
Chief of Tayy' who came in his people's delegation in 9 AH, became Muslim and proved good in Islam; the Prophet named him Zayd al-Khayr — "Goodness" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}.

## His story
Tayy''s delegation came, Zayd al-Khayl among them; they spoke with the Prophet, he offered them Islam and they became Muslim and their Islam proved good {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}. The Prophet said of Zayd: no Arab man was praised to me who then came to me but I found him less than what was said of him — except Zayd al-Khayl, for not all that is in him was told; and he named him Zayd al-Khayr {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}.

## For young visitors
Zayd was a chief famous among his people for generosity and courage; when he came, the Prophet was so impressed he named him "Zayd the Good".
