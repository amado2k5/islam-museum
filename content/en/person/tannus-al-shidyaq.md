## Summary
Tannus ibn Yusuf al-Shidyaq of al-Hadath was a Maronite historian, according to his entry in the first edition of al-A'lam. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he was born in al-Hadath in Lebanon, served the Shihabi emirs, and then became judge of the Christians of Lebanon. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}

## Works
His books include Akhbar al-A'yan fi Jabal Lubnan, which is printed, and an abridgement of the history of Patriarch Istifan al-Duwayhi of Ehden, in manuscript; al-Zirikli cites Zaydan's Adab al-Lugha. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}
