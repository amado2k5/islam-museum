## Summary
Taha ibn Muhanna, of Jibrin origin, of [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]], lived from 1084 to 1178 AH (1673-1764) according to the head of his entry in the first edition of al-A'lam. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}

## Learning and works
Al-Zirikli calls him a learned man who wrote on part of Sahih al-Bukhari and a book on "the lives of the people of Badr", and composed verse, citing Silk al-Durar. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}
