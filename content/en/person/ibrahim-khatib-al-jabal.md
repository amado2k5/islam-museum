## Overview
Ibrahim, khatib of al-Jabal, the ascetic; he heard from al-'Imad, Shaykh Muwaffaq al-Din, Abu'l-Yumn al-Kindi, Abu'l-Qasim Ibn al-Hurastani and many — an imam in knowledge and works, versed in the madhhab, righteous, worshipful, ascetic, sincere, a man of states and wonders, commanding the right and speaking truth. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560-561}

## His life and death
The muhaddith Abu'l-Fida' Ibn al-Khabbaz gathered his sira in one volume; a company transmitted and heard from him, among them Abu'l-'Abbas al-Hayri, his last companion. He died on 19 Rabi' I and was buried at the foot of Qasyun — father of the two imams 'Izz al-Din the fara'idi and 'Izz al-Din Muhammad, khatib of the Muzaffari mosque. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560-561}
