## Overview
Isa ibn Ja'far ibn al-Mansur al-Abbasi. He governed [[place/basra|Basra]] in the days of al-Rashid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governor's house
Harun ordered him to bring the governor's house into the mosque, and he did so. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
