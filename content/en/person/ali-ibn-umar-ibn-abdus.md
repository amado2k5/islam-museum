## Summary
Ali ibn Umar ibn Abdus al-Harrani, Abu al-Hasan, a Hanbali jurist who heard hadith in Baghdad from Ibn Nasir and his circle and excelled in fiqh, exegesis and preaching. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15809} Nasr Allah ibn Abd al-Aziz, Fakhr al-Din Ibn Taymiyya (his maternal nephew) and Umar ibn Ali al-Qurashi studied with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15809} His works include a large exegesis, al-Mudhhab fi al-madhhab and sermons.
