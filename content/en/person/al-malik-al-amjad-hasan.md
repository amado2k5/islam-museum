## Overview
Al-Malik al-Amjad Hasan b. al-Malik al-Nasir Dawud, lord of Karak; among the excellent — well-versed in many sciences with perfect knowledge of adab — he turned ascetic and kept company with the shaykhs, withholding nothing from them; rich in muruwwa and forbearance. He died at Damascus and was buried in his grandfather al-Malik al-Mu'azzam's mausoleum at Qasyun's foot. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 577}
