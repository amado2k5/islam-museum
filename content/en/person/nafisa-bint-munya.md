Nafisa bint Munya, friend and confidante of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]; Khadija confided to her her wish to marry Muhammad, so she went and sounded him out — he answered yes, spoke to his uncles, and they asked for her from her uncle; she managed the matter between the two sides until the marriage was done. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
