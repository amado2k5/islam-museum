## Summary
Nafisa bint Munya, a friend of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]. Khadija confided in her, and she went to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] to propose that he marry Khadija. He agreed, and she was the intermediary in [[event/marriage-to-khadija|the marriage]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/189-190}.

## Her role in the marriage
*The Sealed Nectar* relates that when Khadija found in the Prophet what she had been seeking, she told her friend Nafisa bint Munya what was in her heart. Nafisa went to him to propose that he marry Khadija. He agreed, and spoke with his uncles. They went to Khadija's uncle and asked for her hand, and the marriage was then concluded {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/189-190} {cite:fiqh-al-sira-al-ghazali p.59}.

## Not covered here
The book does not give her lineage or her religion, nor anything about her life after this. What the books of the Companions say about her would need a source that is read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Nafisa was Khadija's friend. She went to the Prophet and asked whether he would be pleased to marry Khadija, and he said yes.
