## Summary
Ahmad al-Maymuni, one of the worshippers of Syria, a sworn brother of Ahmad al-Mawsili. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 479 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 134. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}

## Abu al-Aliya's report
Ahmad al-Maymuni said: I went to Ahmad al-Mawsili and said: I have brought you a report. He said: tell it; either more from God will come to me and I will act on it, or I will sigh a sigh and die. I said: I was told by Abu al-Aliya that he read in one of the books a report that drove sleep from him and took away his desire: O scholars of the Muhammadan community, hasten to the abode whose soil is yellow chrysolite, its trees hanging with the fruit of Paradise. When he heard the words "O scholars", he turned yellow, then black, and fainted. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}
