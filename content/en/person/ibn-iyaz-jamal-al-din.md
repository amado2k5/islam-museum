## Overview
Jamal al-Din Abu Muhammad al-Husayn ibn Badr ibn Iyaz al-Baghdadi, one of the grammarians of Baghdad. He died in 681 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.591-593}

## Teaching and works
He taught at the Mustansiriyya madrasa and wrote on grammar and morphology. His books include *al-Mahsul fi Sharh al-Fusul* (The Harvest: A Commentary on the Chapters) and *Qawa'id al-Mutarahah* (Rules of Debate). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.591-593}
