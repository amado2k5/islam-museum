## Summary
Zafir al-Haddad, the famous poet Abu'l-Mansur Zafir ibn al-Qasim al-Judhami al-Iskandari, was among the accomplished poets with a mostly fine diwan; he praised a circle of Egypt's emirs. The hafiz Abu Tahir al-Salafi and other notables transmitted from him; Ibn Khallikan and Ibn Shihba mentioned him, and 'Ali ibn Zafir praised him in *Bada'i' al-bada'ih*. He died in Egypt in Muharram 529. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 571-573}
