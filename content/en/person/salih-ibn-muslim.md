## Overview
Salih ibn Muslim al-Bahili, brother of [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]]. He succeeded him over Transoxiana, and conquered Kashan and Awrasht in Farghana {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

[[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] was with him in his army. He conquered Sakhr and Akhsakath, the old city of Farghana {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
