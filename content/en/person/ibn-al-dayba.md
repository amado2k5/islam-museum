## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Shaybani al-Zabidi al-Shafi'i, Wajih al-Din, known as Ibn al-Dayba, is described by al-Zirikli as a historian and hadith scholar of Zabid in Yemen, born and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. The entry gives 866 and 944 AH (1461 and 1537 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. His father died in India without his ever seeing him, and his maternal grandfather brought him up {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His works
Among his books are "Bughyat al-mustafid fi akhbar madinat Zabid", part of it printed, and "al-Fadl al-mazid fi tarikh Zabid", in manuscript, a continuation of the first {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. "Qurrat al-uyun fi akhbar al-Yaman" is printed; he abridged it from al-Khazraji's "al-Asjad al-masbuk" and took it down to the events of 923 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. "Taysir al-wusul ila jami' al-usul min hadith al-rasul" is printed in three parts, "Ahsan al-suluk fi man waliya Zabid min al-muluk" is a verse work in rajaz in manuscript, and "Tamyiz al-tayyib min al-khabith" on hadith is printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His byname
Al-Zirikli notes that "al-Dayba" means "the white" in the language of the Sudan, and was the byname of his remote ancestor Ali ibn Yusuf {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
