## Overview
Mu'adh ibn Muslim al-Harra', Abu Muslim, was a long-lived man of letters who also wrote verse, from the people of Kufa. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 187 AH, 803 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 258}.

## His name and writings
He was called al-Harra' because he sold the Harawi cloths that came from the city of Herat. He wrote books on grammar, which are lost, and many reports about him with his contemporaries are recorded {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 258}.

## What was said of him
Al-Zirikli records that Sahl ibn Abi Ghalib al-Khazraji composed verses about him, among them a line telling anyone who passes Mu'adh to say that time itself has grown weary of the length of his life {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 258}.
