## Overview
Khalid ibn Barmak, the forefather of the [[group/barmakids|Barmakid]] family of viziers. The family traces its name to Barmak, which was the title of the keeper of the Nawbahar temple in Balkh. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p. 97}

## His career
He came forward early in the Abbasid period through his administrative and financial ability. Al-Saffah entrusted him with the diwan of land tax and the diwan of the army, and after the death of Abu Salama al-Khallal he held the vizierate of execution. He served as adviser to al-Mansur and supervisor of financial affairs, and his name became prominent in the building of Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p. 97}

He kept his standing in the time of al-Mahdi, who appointed him over Fars in 163 AH. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p. 97}
