## Overview
A delegate from the [[group/al-azd|Azd]] who embraced Islam and whose Islam was good. The Prophet appointed him over those of his people who had embraced Islam, and ordered him to fight those polytheists of the Yemeni tribes who lived near him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/233} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}.

## In the Sira of Ibn Hisham
He came with a delegation of the Azd to the Prophet, embraced Islam, and his Islam was good. See [[event/delegation-of-surad-ibn-abdullah|the coming of Surad ibn Abd Allah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/233}. He then set out, at the Prophet's command, until he camped at [[place/jurash|Jurash]]. He besieged its people for nearly a month, then turned back from them. When he reached a mountain called [[place/shakar|Shakar]], they thought he had been defeated and pursued him. He turned on them and killed them with great slaughter. See [[event/surad-campaign-against-jurash|the expedition of Surad against Jurash]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}.
