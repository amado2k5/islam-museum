## Overview
Dawud ibn Isa, brother of the prince [[person/mukaththir-ibn-isa|Mukaththir]], who held the emirate of Mecca in 571 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77}

## The emirate
Ibn al-Athir reports that the caliph ordered him to take his brother's place when Mukaththir built a fortress on Mount Abu Qubays. He assumed the emirate after the strife of Dhu al-Hijja 571 AH and Mukaththir's flight from the city. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77}
