## Overview
Jibril ibn al-Fadl, Abu Hatim al-Samarqandi, is recorded by Ibn al-Jawzi among those who died in 306 AH, which corresponds to 918 CE by the museum's conversion (derived) {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 13 p. 184}.

## Life
Ibn al-Jawzi says that he came to Baghdad on pilgrimage in 292 AH, transmitted from Qutayba and others, and that Abd al-Baqi ibn Qani' narrated from him {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 13 p. 184}. He describes him as reliable, and adds that he died in that year, 306 AH {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 13 p. 184}.
