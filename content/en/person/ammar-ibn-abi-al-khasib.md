## Overview
Ammar ibn Abi al-Khasib, through whose hands the building of the congregational mosque of the city of al-Mahdi, Muhammadiyya, at al-Rayy, was done. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The mosque
His name was written on the mosque's wall, and the building was dated 158 AH. The mosque is connected with [[event/founding-of-muhammadiyya-rayy|the founding of al-Muhammadiyya at al-Rayy]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
