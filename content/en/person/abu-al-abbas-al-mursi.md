## Overview
Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Abbas al-Ansari al-Mursi, one of the great saints. He was a companion of Shaykh Abu al-Hasan al-Shadhili and succeeded him after him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 190-191}

He came from Murcia in al-Andalus and settled in Alexandria. His tomb was known for the answering of supplications, and al-Maqqari visited it many times. Ibn Ata Allah made him known in "Lata'if al-Minan". {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 190-191}

Al-Maqqari says that he honoured people according to their standing with God, and was very averse to whisperings in the prayer and the ablution, and had words on the interpretation of the Qur'an. He died in Alexandria in 686 AH; the Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 190-191}
