## Overview
Taqi al-Din Shabib — the skilled adib, brilliant poet, physician and oculist, brother of Najm al-Din Ahmad b. Hamdan b. Shabib al-Harrani; he answered "Banat Su'ad" with a great ode opening: "A glory imagination fails to reach its utmost — and renders men's reason a thing intelligible" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 749}.
