## Overview
Qutb al-Din Sanjar, a mamluk of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah. He was appointed over Khuzistan after Tashtakin, emir of the pilgrimage. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.352}

He showed signs of departing from obedience in 606 AH. When the caliph's army marched against him, he left the land and joined the lord of Shiraz, Atabeg Sa'd ibn Dukla. The lord of Shiraz handed him over under a covenant, and he entered Baghdad in Muharram 608 AH with two chains on his leg. He confessed to matters attributed to him, and was imprisoned. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.352}
