## Overview
Abu Muhammad Hibat Allah ibn Ahmad al-Dimashqi, known as Ibn al-Akfani, a memorising hadith scholar, Shafi'i jurist and historian. He was born in Damascus in 444 AH and died in 524 AH, according to the encyclopaedia "Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama wa al-Udaba". He became known for his memorisation and Shafi'i jurisprudence. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
