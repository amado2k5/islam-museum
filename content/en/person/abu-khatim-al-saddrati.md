## Overview
Abu Khatim al-Saddrati, given by al-Baladhuri as Abu Hatim al-Saddrati. It is said that his original nisba was al-Sadrani, after Sadrana. He was an Ibadi from the people of Sadrata and a client of Kinda. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 326}

## His fighting
He fought Umar ibn Hafs al-Atki, who was killed, and his Abbasid city was destroyed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 326} See [[event/martyrdom-of-umar-ibn-hafs|the killing of Umar ibn Hafs]].

## His killing
Yazid ibn Hatim then killed him at Batir Iblis. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 326} See [[event/yazid-ibn-hatim-ifriqiya|Yazid ibn Hatim in Ifriqiya]].

## Notes for researchers
- The spelling of the name, Saddrati or Sadrani, needs an additional source.
