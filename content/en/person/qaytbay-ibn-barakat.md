## Overview
Qaytbay b. Muhammad b. Barakat, the sharif sultan of Mecca; news of his death reached Damascus the selfsame day as Bayezid II's and Yemen's Sultan 'Amir b. Muhammad's — Sunday 18 Rabi' I 918 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 485}.
