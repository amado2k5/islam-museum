## Overview
Abu'l-Futuh al-Hasan ibn Ja'far al-Alawi, amir of Mecca; Abu'l-Qasim ibn al-Mugharibi tempted him with kingship and the Hasani shaykhs paid him allegiance, as Ibn al-Jawzi narrates. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 14 p. 355}
