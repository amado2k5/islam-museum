## Overview
The sharif Abu Ja'far Mas'ud b. 'Abd al-'Aziz b. al-Muhsin b. al-Hasan b. 'Abd al-Razzaq al-Bayyadi, famous poet; he was called al-Bayyadi because one of his forebears sat in some caliph's majlis among the 'Abbasis distinguished by a white garment; he has verse on the history of the Seljuk state. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
