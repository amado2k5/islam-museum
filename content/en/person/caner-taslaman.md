## Summary
Caner Taslaman is a Turkish academic, professor of philosophy of religion at Yıldız Technical University, Qur'an researcher and writer, born in 1968 in Istanbul and known for his works on the relation of science and religion. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-en} {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-tr}

His YouTube channel counted 244,000 subscribers as of 16 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-caner-taslaman}

## Early life and education
Taslaman was born in 1968 in Istanbul; his birth name is Osman Caner Uygun. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-en} {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-tr}

He completed his undergraduate education in sociology at Boğaziçi University, earned his master's and doctorate at Marmara University with theses on the Big Bang theory and the evolution theory in relation to philosophy and theology, and completed a second doctorate at Istanbul University. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-tr}

He carried out postdoctoral studies at the University of Tokyo and the University of Oxford, and was a visiting scholar at Harvard University and the University of Cambridge. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-tr}

## Academic career
He received the title of professor in 2014 in Yıldız Technical University's philosophy department, and he still serves there as professor of philosophy. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-tr} {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-en}

His research interests cover the philosophy of science, the philosophy of religion, the philosophy of physics, the philosophy of biology and Islamic attitudes towards science. {cite:wikipedia-caner-taslaman-en}
