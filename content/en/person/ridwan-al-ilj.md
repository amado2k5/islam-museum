## Summary
Chamberlain of Sultan [[person/abd-al-malik-al-sadi|Abd al-Malik]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 265}.

## The concealment
Only he and Ahmad al-Mansur knew of his master's death at Wadi al-Makhazin; he kept telling the troops, 'the sultan orders so-and-so to that spot, so-and-so to hold the banner, so-and-so forward, so-and-so back' — until victory was complete {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 265}.
