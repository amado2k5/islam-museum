## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Ya'qub ibn Yusuf ibn Ibrahim ibn Killis, vizier of al-Aziz Nizar ibn al-Mu'izz, lord of Egypt {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 27-28}.

## Origins
He was a Jew in origin, and it is said that he claimed to be of the descendants of Harun ibn Imran. He was born in Baghdad by the gate of al-Qazz in the year 318 AH {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 27-28} {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 34}.

His father sent him to Egypt in the year 331 AH, and he attached himself to the intimates of Kafur al-Ikhshidi, then Kafur seated him in his chancery. He became a Muslim in the year 356 AH {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 27-28}.

## The vizierate
When Kafur died, he was seized, and he paid money until he was released, then he joined al-Mu'izz. Ibn Khallikan transmits from Ibn Zulaq that he was the first to serve as vizier of the Fatimid state in Egypt. He was appointed vizier of al-Aziz in the year 368 AH {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 28-29}.

He loved the people of learning, and arranged a council every Friday night where he read his works to the people. In his house were men who copied Qur'ans, books of hadith, jurisprudence and literature {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 29-30}.

## Death
He died on Sunday night, the fifth of Dhu al-Hijja in the year 380 AH. al-Aziz went out, prayed over him, wept, and attended his burial {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 33-34}.
