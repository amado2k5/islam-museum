## Overview
Shurahbil ibn al-Harith ibn Amr al-Kindi, of [[group/kinda|Kinda]], son of [[person/al-harith-ibn-amr-al-kindi|al-Harith ibn Amr al-Kindi]] and brother of [[person/hujr-ibn-al-harith-al-kindi|Hujr ibn al-Harith]]. His father made him ruler over Bakr ibn Wa'il, Banu Hanzala, Usayd and al-Rabab {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.410-411}.

## The first day of al-Kilab
When al-Harith died and his sons' affairs were scattered, Shurahbil marched with his forces to the water of al-Kilab between Basra and Kufa, and fought with his brother Salama. Abu Hanash al-Taghlibi stabbed him and unhorsed him, and took his head and sent it to Salama {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.410-411}.
