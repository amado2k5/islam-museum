## Overview
Maslama ibn Ahmad ibn Qasim ibn Abd Allah al-Majriti, Abu al-Qasim, was a philosopher, mathematician and astronomer. He was the leading mathematician of al-Andalus and the widest in his grasp of the science of the spheres and the motions of the stars. He was born and died at Majrit, which is Madrid. Al-A'lam gives his years as 338 AH, 950 CE, to 398 AH, 1007 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 223}.

## The Epistles of the Brethren of Purity
Al-Zirikli notes that some historians held him to be the author of the Rasa'il Ikhwan al-Safa, and says that this was not established {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 223}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names: Thimar al-adad on arithmetic, known as al-Mu'amalat; Ikhtisar ta'dil al-kawakib min zij al-Battani; Rutbat al-hakim (manuscript); Ghayat al-hakim (printed); Kitab al-ahjar (manuscript); and Rawdat al-hada'iq, a small treatise (manuscript) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 223}.

## His work on al-Khwarizmi's zij
He took up the astronomical tables (zij) of Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi, converted their Persian-calendar date into the Arabic calendar, and added good tables. But, as al-Qifti says, he followed al-Khwarizmi in his error and did not point out where the mistakes lay {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 223}.
