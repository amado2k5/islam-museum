## Summary
Qalender Çelebi, leader of the rising in Konya and Maraş under [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 200-201}.

## The rising
Al-Salabi puts thirty thousand followers at his back, and quotes 'some historians' that he made killing a Sunni Muslim and assaulting a Sunni woman a claim to the greatest reward — a saying the book attributes to the historians rather than asserts itself {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 200-201}.

## The end
Bahram Pasha, sent to suppress him, was killed by the rebels; then Grand Vizier [[person/ibrahim-pasha-franji|Ibrahim Pasha]]'s stratagem won over some of Qalender's men, his forces dwindled, and he was defeated and killed {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201}.
