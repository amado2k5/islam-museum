## Summary

Amr ibn Suraqa ibn al-Mu'tamir al-Qurashi al-Adawi, of Umar ibn al-Khattab's circle and brother of Abdallah ibn Suraqa. He joined Abd Allah ibn Jahsh's expedition to Nakhla, witnessed Badr, and received from Umar a share of the Khaybar land. He died in Uthman's caliphate.
