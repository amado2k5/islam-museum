## Summary
Umm Hisham, daughter of [[person/haritha-ibn-al-numan|Haritha ibn al-Nu'man]] al-Ansari, was a Companion present at the [[event/bayat-al-ridwan|Pledge of Ridwan]]; [[person/mahmood-ahmad-ghadanfar|Ghadanfar]] describes her as loyal, learned in religion, devout, careful about purity, ablution and prayer, and a neighbour of the Prophet in Medina. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.226}

## Her household
Her father Haritha ibn al-Nu'man, a wealthy notable of Medina, gave the Prophet several houses as each marriage needed one, until the Prophet said he felt ashamed before him; while the Prophet stayed some six months with Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, Haritha was the next-door neighbour, and Umm Hisham would cook and send food to the Prophet. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.226} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.227} Her mother was Umm Khalid bint Khalid ibn Ya'ish of Banu Malik, and her siblings Abd Allah, Abd al-Rahman, Sawda and Amra all embraced Islam. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.227}

## Memory and transmission
Ghadanfar says the Prophet used to recite Surat Qaf at Friday and Eid prayers and that Umm Hisham learned it by heart from hearing him; she had a sharp mind and strong memory, knew many hadiths, and her sister Amra, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn As'ad ibn Zurara, Yahya ibn Abd Allah and Habib ibn Abd al-Rahman narrated from her. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.228}

## At al-Hudaybiya
She was among the 1,400 who set out with the Prophet for the lesser pilgrimage in 6 AH and were stopped by Quraysh at al-Hudaybiya, and she pledged allegiance under the tree when it was rumoured that Uthman had been killed — so she was one of the "Companions of the Tree" of whom, Ghadanfar quotes, none would enter the Fire. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.228} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.229} See [[passage/quran-48-18|al-Fath 48:18]].

## For young visitors
Umm Hisham lived in Medina next door to the Prophet. She cooked food for him, and learned Surat Qaf by heart from hearing him recite it on Fridays.
