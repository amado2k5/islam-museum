## Overview
Mahdi ibn Husayn ibn Aziz al-Khalisi al-Kazimi was an Imami jurist and one of the leaders of the rising against the British occupation of Iraq; exiled afterwards to Iran, he died at Mashhad in Khurasan in 1343 AH (1924 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.312}

His works include a gloss on Muhammad Kazim al-Khurasani's Kifayat al-Usul. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.312}
