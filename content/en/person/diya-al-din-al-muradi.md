## Summary
Diya' al-Din al-Muradi, the imam, exacting hafiz, verifier, master, ascetic — al-Nawawi's shaykh; al-Nawawi mentioned him in his additions to Ibn al-Salah's Tabaqat: 'my eyes never saw his like in his time; he was brilliant in knowing hadith and its sciences and in verifying its wording, especially the two Sahihs, with utmost care — likewise grammar, fiqh and the sufis' lore, a fine discusser in them.' {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Al-Nawawi's company
'To me he was among the great travellers on the paths of realities, an excellent teacher; I kept his company some ten years and never saw of him anything disliked. In gentleness he held a high station as far as he could, and in compassion for the Muslims and sincere counsel to them he had few peers.' {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
