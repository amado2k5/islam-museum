## Overview
Sasa ibn Dahir fled, intending to go to Iraq to complain of the treachery of al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman. al-Junayd kept reassuring him until he placed his hand in his, and then killed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
