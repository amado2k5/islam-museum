## Overview
Abu Ya'qub al-Sijistani, successor of the Imam al-Muqim, based at al-Rayy; before him the Banu Hammad had directed the mission in al-Jazira and its environs. On hearing something he disavowed Ibn Nafis and sent Persian men who killed him by treachery. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287}
