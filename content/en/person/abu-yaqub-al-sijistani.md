## Overview
Abu Ya'qub al-Sijistani, deputy of the "Resident Imam", who was in al-Rayy according to Ibn al-Nadim. The Banu Hammad were the missionaries of the call in al-Jazira and the surrounding regions on his behalf. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.268}

## The report on Ibn Nafis
Ibn al-Nadim says that he denounced something Ibn Nafis had done that had reached him, and sent some Persians who killed him secretly in his house. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.269}
