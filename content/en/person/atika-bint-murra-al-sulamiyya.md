## Overview
Atika daughter of Murra al-Sulamiyya, mother of Hashim ibn Abd Manaf. Al-Baladhuri gives her lineage as of the Banu Sulaym: Atika daughter of Murra ibn Hilal ibn Falij ibn Dhakwan ibn Tha'laba ibn Bahtha ibn Sulaym ibn Mansur. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.534}

It is also said that she was Atika daughter of Jabir ibn Qunfudh of the Banu Sulaym, and al-Baladhuri considers this the more established of the two views. She is one of three "Atikas" of the Banu Sulaym among the grandmothers of the Prophet. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.534}
