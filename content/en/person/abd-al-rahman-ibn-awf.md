## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf al-Zuhri al-Qurashi was among the earliest Muslims during the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]] in Mecca, according to al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. He was one of the eight of the first vanguard who embraced Islam through the call of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## In al-Raheeq al-Makhtum
After his own conversion, Abu Bakr was active in the call. He began calling those among his people whom he trusted, who visited him and sat with him. Through his call, Uthman ibn Affan, al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, both of Banu Zuhra, and Talha ibn Ubaydullah embraced Islam. These eight, together with the first four, formed the first vanguard and the forerunners of Islam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. He belongs to [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76}.

## Not covered here
This chapter of the book mentions nothing beyond his early conversion. The rest of his story is completed in its own places in the book and in other sources.

## For young visitors
Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf was among the first to believe in the Prophet. His friend Abu Bakr called him, and he embraced Islam. He was one of the eight who went ahead of others into Islam.
