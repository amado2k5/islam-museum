## Overview
Hashim ibn Abd Manaf: to whom the siqaya and rifada fell in Banu Abd Manaf's drawing of lots, holding them all his life; to him the Prophet's house is ascribed.

## Life
Banu Abd Manaf cast lots over the offices that had come to them, and the siqaya and rifada fell to Hashim ibn Abd Manaf, who held them all his life {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. On his death his brother al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf succeeded him, then [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] ibn Hashim took them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

And it is from Hashim's house that God chose the Prophet Muhammad ibn Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Hashim is the Prophet's great ancestor, after whom his clan Banu Hashim is named; he fed and watered the pilgrims.
