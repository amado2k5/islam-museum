## Summary
Tahmina Kohistani is an Afghan runner in the 100 metres, born in Kapisa Province in 1989. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-en} {cite:wikidata-tahmina-kohistani} Her birth date is given differently across the sources: the Persian article gives 18 June 1989, the English lead and the Urdu article print 8 June 1989, and Wikidata carries both 18 June 1989 and 10 June 1990. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-fa} {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-ur} {cite:wikidata-tahmina-kohistani}

## London 2012
Kohistani was Afghanistan's only female athlete at the 2012 Summer Olympics in London. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-fa} In the heats of the 100 metres she set a new personal best of 14.42 seconds; she finished last in her heat and did not advance. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-en} Her previous personal best was 15.00 seconds, set at a competition in Bydgoszcz, Poland, in 2008. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-en}

## Recognition
In 2015 she was listed among the BBC's 100 Women. {cite:wikipedia-tahmina-kohistani-en}
