## Summary
Abd al-Khaliq ibn Abd al-Warith al-Siyuri, Abu al-Qasim, a Maliki jurist, the last of the scholars of Ifriqiya and of the last imams of Qayrawan, a memoriser who upheld the school and knew the disputes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15819} He studied with Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman and Abu Imran al-Fasi, and Abd al-Hamid al-Sa'igh, al-Lakhmi, Hassan al-Barbari and Abd al-Haqq al-Saqli studied with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15819} His work is a gloss on notes from al-Mudawwana, taken down by his companions; it is said he later leaned towards the Shafi'i school.
