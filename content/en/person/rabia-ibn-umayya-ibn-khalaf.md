## Summary
A Qurashi Companion who called out the Prophet's words to the people at Arafat during the Farewell Sermon, relaying his words to the great crowd {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460}.

## His account
The one who called out the Messenger of God's words to the people while he stood at Arafat was Rabi'a ibn Umayya ibn Khalaf {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460; ibn-hisham-sira}. The crowd that day reached a hundred and twenty-four or a hundred and forty-four thousand, and his voice carried the sermon section by section {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 459-460}.

## For young visitors
A strong-voiced man who stood beside the Prophet repeating the words of his sermon in a loud voice so that all the people at Arafat could hear.
