## Summary

Abd al-Razzaq ibn Abd al-Muhsin Al-Badr (born 22 Dhu al-Qa'da 1382 AH / 16 April 1963 CE) is a Saudi scholar born in al-Zulfi, a professor in the Aqidah department of the Islamic University of Madinah who also teaches in the Prophet's Mosque, son of the scholar Abd al-Muhsin Al-Abbad, and author of many works on the names of God, supplication and remembrance. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-ar} {cite:wikidata-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr} His YouTube channel (@sheikhalbadr) carries his lessons; the English article recorded 226,000 subscribers for it at a June 2026 update, a dated platform figure. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-en} {cite:youtube-sheikhalbadr}

## Career

Al-Badr took all his degrees at the Islamic University of Madinah: a bachelor's in sharia, a master's in creed with a thesis on Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'di's efforts in establishing the creed, and a doctorate in creed with a thesis on the increase and decrease of faith and the ruling on the exception clause in it. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-ar} He studied under his father Abd al-Muhsin Al-Abbad, and under Abd Allah al-Ghunayman and Ali Nasir Faqihi, as the article reports from Midad and Al-Shamela. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-ar} He teaches in the university's Aqidah department and in the Prophet's Mosque, and his lessons and sermons are published through his official website al-badr.net and his YouTube channel. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-ar} {cite:youtube-sheikhalbadr}

## Works

His works include Fiqh of the Most Beautiful Names of God, Fiqh of Supplications and Remembrances, Hajj and the Refinement of Souls, A Study of Malik's Report on Istiwa, Al-Hawqala: Its Meaning, Virtues and Doctrinal Significance, Studies in the Everlasting Good Deeds, Al-Tuhfa al-Saniyya (a commentary on Ibn Abi Dawud's Ha'iyya), Remembrances of Purity and Prayer, A Commentary on Abu Dawud's Hashiya, Hadiths on Reforming Hearts, Hadiths on Faith, Hadiths on Character, Tadhkirat al-Mu'tasi (a commentary on Abd al-Ghani al-Maqdisi's creed) and a commentary on The Six Principles. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-razzaq-al-badr-ar}
