## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Hadrami, known as Ibn Kharruf the grammarian of Seville, was accomplished in the science of Arabic and left works that attest his merit and breadth of knowledge. He died in 610 AH (1213 CE) — some say 609 — at Seville. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.3 p.334}

He made a good commentary on Sibawayh's Book, and also commented Abu al-Qasim al-Zajjaji's al-Jumal without falling short; he had trained under Ibn Tahir the Andalusi grammarian known as al-Khudb; he is not the poet Ibn Kharruf. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.3 p.334}
