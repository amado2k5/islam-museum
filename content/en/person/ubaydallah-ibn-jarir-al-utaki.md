## Summary
'Ubayd Allah ibn Jarir ibn Jubayla ibn Abi Dawud al-'Utaki al-Basri transmitted from Musaddad and others; Ibn Abi'l-Dunya and Ibn Sa'id transmitted from him. He recited of himself: "What is not to be will never be, by any device — ever; and what is to be shall be — what is to be shall come in its time; and worry is brother to weariness." He died at Wasit in Rajab 162. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 129}
