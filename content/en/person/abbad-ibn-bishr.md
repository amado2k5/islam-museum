## Summary
[[person/abbad-ibn-bishr|Abbad ibn Bishr]] ibn Waqsh ibn Zaghba al-Ansari, of Banu Abd al-Ashhal {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## Badr and Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf
Musa ibn Uqba counts him among those who witnessed [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], and says he took part in the [[event/killing-of-kab-ibn-al-ashraf|killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf]] and spoke verse about it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## His merit
[[person/aisha|A'isha]] said: "three of the Ansar — none is preferred in merit over them, all of Banu Abd al-Ashhal: [[person/usayd-ibn-hudayr|Usayd ibn Hudayr]], [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]] and Abbad ibn Bishr" — and Ibn Hajar authenticated it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}. In the Sahih from A'isha: the Prophet heard Abbad ibn Bishr's voice and said: "O God, have mercy on Abbad" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## The luminous staff
In the Sahih from Anas's hadith: Abbad ibn Bishr and Usayd ibn Hudayr went out from the Prophet on a dark night, and the staff of one of them gave light; when they parted, each man's staff gave light {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## Reports from him
Abu Dawud recorded a hadith for him in "The Merits of the Ansar" through Ibn Ishaq; al-Tabarani and Ibn Shahin also recorded a hadith of his — and Isma'il the Judge said of Ibn al-Madini: I know no other for him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 765}.
