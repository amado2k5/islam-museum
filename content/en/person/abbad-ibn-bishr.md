## Summary
[[person/abbad-ibn-bishr|Abbad ibn Bishr]] ibn Waqsh ibn Zaghba al-Ansari, of Banu Abd al-Ashhal {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## Badr and Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf
Musa ibn Uqba counts him among those who witnessed [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], and says he took part in the [[event/killing-of-kab-ibn-al-ashraf|killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf]] and spoke verse about it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## His merit
[[person/aisha|A'isha]] said: "three of the Ansar — none is preferred in merit over them, all of Banu Abd al-Ashhal: [[person/usayd-ibn-hudayr|Usayd ibn Hudayr]], [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]] and Abbad ibn Bishr" — and Ibn Hajar authenticated it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}. In the Sahih from A'isha: the Prophet heard Abbad ibn Bishr's voice and said: "O God, have mercy on Abbad" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## The luminous staff
In the Sahih from Anas's hadith: Abbad ibn Bishr and Usayd ibn Hudayr went out from the Prophet on a dark night, and the staff of one of them gave light; when they parted, each man's staff gave light {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 764}.

## Reports from him
Abu Dawud recorded a hadith for him in "The Merits of the Ansar" through Ibn Ishaq; al-Tabarani and Ibn Shahin also recorded a hadith of his — and Isma'il the Judge said of Ibn al-Madini: I know no other for him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 765}.

## In Khalid Muhammad Khalid's Rijal Hawl al-Rasul
[[person/khalid-muhammad-khalid|Khalid Muhammad Khalid]] titles his chapter on him in [[work/rijal-hawl-al-rasul|Rijal Hawl al-Rasul]] "With him was a light from God", and says he became Muslim through [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr]] when Mus'ab came to Medina, and fought in the front ranks of the campaigns. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.579} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.580} He tells of his watch with [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]] after the [[event/expedition-of-dhat-al-riqa|campaign of Dhat al-Riqa']]: he let Ammar sleep and stood in prayer, was struck by three arrows while reciting, pulled them out and completed his bowing and prostration before waking his companion; he said he had been reciting verses he did not wish to break off, and but for fear of leaving unguarded a post the Prophet had entrusted to him, he would have preferred death to interrupting them. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.580} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.581}
He quotes Aisha: "Three of the Ansar were surpassed in merit by no one: [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]], [[person/usayd-ibn-hudayr|Usayd ibn Hudayr]] and Abbad ibn Bishr," and mentions the well-known report that when he walked in the dark a light went out from him that lit his way. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.582} At the [[event/battle-of-yamama|Battle of al-Yamama]] he relates from [[person/abu-said-al-khudri|Abu Sa'id al-Khudri]] Abbad's dream that the sky opened for him and then closed over him, which he read as martyrdom; then his call to the Ansar to break their scabbards and stand apart from the others, and how he led them with [[person/abu-dujana|Abu Dujana]] and [[person/al-bara-ibn-malik|al-Bara' ibn Malik]] to the walled garden, where he was martyred and could be recognised only by a mark on his body. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.583} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.584}
