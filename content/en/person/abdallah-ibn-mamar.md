## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Ma'mar al-Yashkuri, appointed by [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] over Dihastan and al-Bayasan with four thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
The report says that the isbahbadh wrote to the marzban that he should kill the Arabs among his people, so they killed Abd Allah and his men while they were surprised in their dwellings ([[event/hayyan-ispahbadh-treaty|the treaty of Hayyan the isbahbadh]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
