## Summary
Al-'Ala' Ibn al-Taj al-Baghdadi, the Shafi'i imam, muhaddith, exact and abundant — musnid of Baghdad in his time; born 793, he heard in it from al-Hasani and al-Qasri, in his youth from Abu Hurayra Ibn al-Dhahabi, and at Cairo and Damascus from a company. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## His standing
A righteous, good man preferring the matters of the hereafter, abundant in transmission, constant in devotions, attached to the iqra' session, greatly respected for teaching and fatwa, heading a company in many arts — they called him the Muqri'; al-Dhahabi said he was illustrious; he died in 867 and was buried at the Bab Nasr cemetery. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
