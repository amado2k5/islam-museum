## Summary
Khalid Al Ameri is an Emirati content creator born in 1983, one of the most followed social-media personalities in the Arab world; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-khalid-al-ameri} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Background
Born in Abu Dhabi to an Emirati father and Scottish mother, he studied in the United States and worked in finance before turning to content creation full-time around 2016. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Content
His videos — largely family sketches with his wife Salama Mohamed and their children — mix comedy with portrayals of Emirati and Muslim family life, disability awareness and intercultural exchange, presented in Arabic and English. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Reach
With more than twenty million followers across platforms, he is among the most-watched Gulf creators; he has represented the UAE at creator events, won regional digital awards and spoken on cross-cultural understanding. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Assessment
He is credited with a family-friendly content model that made Emirati life legible to a global audience; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri} {cite:muslim500-khalid-al-ameri}


## Content
His family-and-travel videos — often with wife Salama Mohamed and their children — build a distinctly Emirati-inflected family-comedy genre; the couple's joint channels reach audiences well beyond the Gulf. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Career turn
He moved from a corporate career into full-time creation after his early videos went viral, a trajectory often cited in Gulf creator-economy coverage as a model shift. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}

## Scale and recognition
His combined following — tens of millions across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok — and mainstream-media profiles make him among the best-known Gulf creators globally. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri} {cite:muslim500-khalid-al-ameri}

## The Emirati creator model
Khalid Al-Ameri's career is the leading example of the Gulf creator model: a Stanford-educated professional who moved from a corporate career into full-time creation after his family videos went viral, building a distinctly Emirati-inflected family-comedy genre with wife Salama Mohamed and their children. His combined following — tens of millions across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok — made him the best-known Emirati creator globally and a reference point for the region's creator economy. {cite:wikipedia-khalid-al-ameri}
