## Summary
Prince Ahmad ibn [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]], brother of Sultan [[person/selim-i-ottoman|Selim I]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 184-185}.

## Flight to the Mamluks
Al-Salabi places him foremost among the Ottoman princes who fled Selim to the Mamluk state, whose court meant to use these princes as instruments of trouble against Selim — one of the reasons the book lists for the Ottoman turn to annexing Syria and Egypt {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 184-185}.
