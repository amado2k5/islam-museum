## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali al-Azdi al-Zaghuli was a hadith hafiz and one of the Shafi'i jurists, learned in language and exegesis. His nisba is to Zaghul, a village of Panj Dih in Marw al-Rudh, where he was born; he settled in Marw and became famous there. The entry gives his years as 472 to 559 AH, 1080 to 1164 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
He wrote 'Qayd al-Awabid', in more than four hundred volumes, on exegesis, hadith, jurisprudence and language {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
