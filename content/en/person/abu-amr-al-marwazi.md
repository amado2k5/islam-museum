## Summary
Abu Amr al-Marwazi was a wise man who committed his affair to the All-Hearing, All-Knowing. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 523, sequence 524, on page 155. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}

## Three attributes of the friends of God
Muhammad ibn Ahmad relates that he heard Abu al-Abbas al-Thaqafi say that he heard Abu Amr al-Marwazi say: the friends of God have three attributes: returning to God in all things, poverty before God in all things, and trust in God in all things. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}
