## Overview
Zakariyya ibn Yahya ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Adi al-Dabbi al-Basri al-Saji, Abu Yahya, was, in al-Zirikli's words, the hadith scholar of Basra in his time, one of the trusted hafiz {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 pp. 46-47}. Ibn al-Imad calls him Abu Yahya Zakariyya ibn Yahya al-Saji al-Basri, the hafiz and muhaddith of Basra {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}. Al-Safadi describes him as one of the trustworthy imams {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 14 pp. 136-137}.

## Transmission and learning
Ibn al-Imad says he transmitted from Hudba ibn Khalid and his generation {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}. He quotes al-Isnawi that the nisba al-Saji refers to al-saj, a kind of wood, and that he was one of the imams among the jurists, the hafiz and the trustworthy {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}. He also reports al-Isnawi's statement that Abu Ishaq mentioned him in his Tabaqat and said that he took jurisprudence from al-Rabi' and al-Muzani and compiled Ikhtilaf al-Fuqaha' and Ilal al-Hadith {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote a weighty book on the defects of hadith (Ilal al-Hadith), which shows his depth of learning, and that another of his books is Ikhtilaf al-Fuqaha' {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 pp. 46-47}. Ibn al-Imad likewise names his book on the defects of hadith {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}.

## Death
He died at Basra {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 pp. 46-47} {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36}. Al-Zirikli prints his years as 220 and 307 AH, matching 835 and 920 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 pp. 46-47}. Ibn al-Imad {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 36} and al-Safadi {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 14 pp. 136-137} both date his death to 307 AH.
