## Summary
A nobleman and physician among the notables of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]], son of the famous physician al-Harith ibn Kalada al-Thaqafi and ally of Banu Abd al-Dar; he became Muslim in the year of the conquest, and the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]] in the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won over]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## His lineage and Islam
He is ascribed to Banu Ilaj of Thaqif; his father al-Harith ibn Kalada was the Arabs' famed physician. Al-Nadir entered Islam after the conquest of Mecca, and the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at al-Ji'rana to win him and his people over {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}. Historians distinguish him from al-Nadr ibn al-Harith ibn Alqama al-Abdari, who was killed a disbeliever after Badr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
A physician and chief of Thaqif who became Muslim after the conquest of Mecca; the Prophet gave him from the al-Ji'rana spoils to win his heart and honour his standing among his people.
