Džanan Musa (born 8 May 1999) is a Bosnian basketball player from Bihać. A prodigy at Cedevita in Croatia — where he debuted in the EuroLeague at sixteen and won the EuroCup Rising Star Trophy in 2018 — he was drafted 29th overall by the Brooklyn Nets that year, and after spells in the NBA and Turkey he rebuilt his career in Spain: Liga ACB MVP with Breogán in 2022, then a EuroLeague title with Real Madrid in 2023, before joining Dubai Basketball in 2025. He also leads the senior Bosnia and Herzegovina national team.{cite:wikipedia-dzanan-musa-en}{cite:wikipedia-dzanan-musa-bs}{cite:wikidata-dzanan-musa}

His faith is part of his public story in his own account: a Sports Illustrated profile calls him "a devout Muslim" and records how, as a sixteen-year-old in Croatia, he was targeted with anti-Muslim insults by fans: "I was a boy at 16 years old and I heard the insults."{cite:sports-illustrated-musa-2018}
