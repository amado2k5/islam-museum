## Summary
Sulayman ibn Muhammad ibn Umar al-Bujayrimi, a Shafi'i jurist from Bujayram, a village in Gharbiyya in Egypt. He came to Cairo as a child, studied and taught at al-Azhar, and lost his sight. His works include al-Tajrid, a commentary on al-Minhaj, and Tuhfat al-habib, a gloss on al-Khatib's commentary Iqna' fi hall alfaz Abi Shuja'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1615}
