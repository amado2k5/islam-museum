## Overview
Mansur ibn Yunus ibn Salah al-Din al-Buhuti, a Hanbali jurist, attributed to Buhut in the western part of Egypt. He was the shaykh of the Hanbalis in Egypt in his time, wrote much on Hanbali jurisprudence, and combined with it exegesis, principles and inheritance. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 1000 AH (1591 CE) and his death as 1051 AH (1641 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
