## Overview
Abu al-Arab Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Tamim al-Darimi al-Tamimi, a Maliki jurist, transmitter of hadith and historian from Qayrawan. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" says that he was born in 251 AH and died in 333 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The study here is short because the only source in the data is a summary; a second source is needed for the details of his teachers and books.
