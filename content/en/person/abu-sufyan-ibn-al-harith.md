Abu Sufyan ibn al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib al-Hashimi, the Prophet's cousin and foster-brother; he was nursed with him in the land of [[group/banu-sad-ibn-bakr|Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr]] at [[person/halima-al-sadiyya|Halima al-Sa'diyya]]'s and she tended them both, and after his Islam he became one of Islam's poets. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
