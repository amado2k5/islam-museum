# Abdur Raheem Green

## Summary
Abdur Raheem Green is a British dawah preacher, and the founder and chairman of iERA, the Islamic Education and Research Academy. He was born Anthony Waclaw Gavin Green in Dar es Salaam in September 1964, to a father who was a colonial administrator in the British Empire and a Polish mother; Wikidata's item for him agrees on the September 1964 birth and also carries a rival 1962 value, which is not followed. {cite:wikipedia-abdur-raheem-green-en} {cite:wikidata-abdur-raheem-green}

## Dawah and iERA
Raised a Roman Catholic and educated at Ampleforth College, Green embraced Islam in 1988 and became known for dawah at Speakers' Corner in Hyde Park and in televised formal settings. {cite:wikipedia-abdur-raheem-green-en} In 2009 he founded iERA in the United Kingdom as a missionary organisation for the propagation of Islam, and he chairs the charity. {cite:wikipedia-iera-en} {cite:wikipedia-abdur-raheem-green-en}
