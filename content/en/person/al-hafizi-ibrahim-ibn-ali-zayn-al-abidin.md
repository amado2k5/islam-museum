## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ali Zayn al-Abidin al-Hafizi, of the family of the Al Bakri al-Ujayli among the notables of Lemah in Asir. He was born in 1305 AH (1888 CE) and died in 1372 AH (1952 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.76}

The encyclopaedia lists him among the scholars. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His studies, teachers and works are not set out here and need an additional source.
