## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Yazid ibn Hisn ibn Amr ibn al-Harith, Abu Musa, al-Awsi al-Ansari, a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8518}
He witnessed al-Hudaybiya at seventeen and the pledge of Ridwan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8518}

## Battles and office
He fought at the Camel and at Siffin with Ali, and was Ali's governor at Kufa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8518}
He narrated from the Prophet, Abu Ayyub, Qays ibn Sa'd, Zayd ibn Thabit and al-Bara' ibn Azib; his son Musa, his grandson Adi ibn Thabit and Muhammad ibn Sirin narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8518}
