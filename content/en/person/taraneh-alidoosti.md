## Summary
Taraneh Alidoosti is an Iranian actress regarded by critics' polls as one of the finest of her generation — and an artist whose public stands have twice made headlines beyond cinema. The Muslim 500 lists her in Arts & Culture as a Crystal Simorgh-winning actress of Iranian cinema; Wikipedia records her role in Asghar Farhadi's The Salesman, which won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film in 2017. {cite:muslim500-taraneh-alidoosti} {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti}

## Career
Born in Tehran in 1984, Alidoosti began acting in her teens — her father is the well-known Iranian actor and footballer Hamid Alidoosti — and won the Crystal Simorgh for best actress at the Fajr Film Festival while still in her early twenties for I'm Taraneh, 15. A run of acclaimed performances followed: Fireworks Wednesday, About Elly, Modest Reception, Shahrzad — work that made her a fixture of Iranian art cinema's golden era under directors including Asghar Farhadi. {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti}

## The Salesman and the world
Her role as Rana in Farhadi's The Salesman ⁦(2016)⁩ carried her to international attention; the film won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, and critics' polls in Iran voted her the best actress of the decade. She also translates from English and German and works as a producer. {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti} {cite:muslim500-taraneh-alidoosti}

## Public stands
Alidoosti is among the Iranian artists who have taken public positions on political questions — including her visible support for the protests that followed the death of Mahsa Amini, for which she was arrested in December 2022 and held for weeks before release. The museum reports these facts attributed to the sources read, without verdict on the politics involved. {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti}

## Significance
In the museum's record, Alidoosti stands for the artist whose craft and conscience are read together: the leading actress of Iran's celebrated national cinema whose roles and public stands alike have made her among the most watched Iranian artists of her generation. {cite:muslim500-taraneh-alidoosti} {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti}

## The actress and the record
Alidoosti's public identity is built on two pillars the sources agree on: her filmography, which includes the central role in Asghar Farhadi's Oscar-winning The Salesman, and her public stands — her support for Iranian protest movements, her refusal to appear in a headscarf in solidarity during the Mahsa Aini protests, her arrest and brief detention in 2022, and her eventual departure from Iran — all documented in international press coverage. The museum's record describes the career and the stands as reported, attributing the political framing to the press that covers her; the listing records her reach as the most prominent Iranian actress of her generation. {cite:wikipedia-taraneh-alidoosti} {cite:muslim500-taraneh-alidoosti}
