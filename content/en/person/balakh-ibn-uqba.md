## Overview
Balakh ibn Uqba al-Azdi, a Kharijite of Yemen. He entered Mecca and Medina with Abu Hamza al-Mukhtar ibn Awf in 129 AH, calling for Abd Allah ibn Yahya al-Kindi. He was killed at Wadi al-Qura when he met the army of Marwan under Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Atiyya. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 228-229}
