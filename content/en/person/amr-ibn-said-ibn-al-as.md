## Summary
A Companion of Banu Abd Shams; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Fatima bint Safwan, arrived in the two ships, and was killed at Ajnadayn in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him brother of Khalid ibn Sa'id, saying his wife Fatima bint Safwan ibn Umayya ibn Muharrith al-Kinani perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He says he was killed at Ajnadayn in the land of Syria in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} Verses addressed to him are ascribed to his father Sa'id ibn al-As. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}
