## Summary
King of Portugal in [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]]'s day {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 219}.

## The consultation
When word reached him that the Ottoman fleet under Khayr al-Din had reached North Africa on 3 Rabi' I 941/13 September 1534, he sent a consultation to the nobles, notables and bishops of his realm: should Safi and Azemmour be left to the Moroccans, or held as fortresses? The clergy's answers were near-unanimous in advising abandonment of the southern posts — Santa Cruz, Safi, Azemmour — as worth far less than their cost, and the concentration of force on the northern positions and Ceuta's defences against a feared attack by Khayr al-Din {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 219}.
