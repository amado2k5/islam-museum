## Overview
Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf: Abd al-Muttalib's uncle who seized his arkah lands after al-Muttalib's death, until he was compelled to restore them by Abu Sa'd al-Najjari's sword and the pact of Banu Hashim and Khuza'a.

## Life
When al-Muttalib died, Nawfal leapt upon Abd al-Muttalib's arkah lands and seized them from him, while Quraysh's elders refused to intervene between the two sons of the brother {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

When Abu Sa'd ibn Adi came with eighty riders and drew his sword at the sanctuary-stone, Nawfal returned the lands, with Quraysh's shaykhs bearing witness over him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Nawfal is Abd al-Muttalib's uncle; he took his nephew's right, then returned it when his mother's people came to aid him.
