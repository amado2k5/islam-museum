## Summary
Nawfal ibn [[person/abd-manaf-ibn-qusayy|Abd Manaf]], one of the four branches of Abd Manaf, and uncle of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]]. After the death of [[person/al-muttalib-ibn-abd-manaf|al-Muttalib]], he leapt upon Abd al-Muttalib's *arkah* (inherited estate) and seized it from him, until it was returned by the sword of [[person/abu-sad-ibn-adi|Abu Sa'd ibn Adi]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}.

## The seizure and its reversal
When al-Muttalib died, Nawfal leapt upon Abd al-Muttalib's *arkah* and seized it from him. Abd al-Muttalib asked men of Quraysh for support against his uncle, but they said: "We will not come between you and your uncle" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50}. When Abu Sa'd ibn Adi came with eighty riders and drew his sword on Nawfal, who was sitting in the Hijr with the elders of Quraysh, Nawfal said: "I have returned it to him," and he had the elders of Quraysh bear witness against him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50}. See [[event/recovery-of-abd-al-muttalib-rights|Abd al-Muttalib's recovery of his rights]].

## The alliances
When this happened, Nawfal allied with [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf]] against [[group/banu-hashim|Banu Hashim]]. [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] allied with Banu Hashim against Banu Abd Shams and Nawfal. This alliance later became the cause of the conquest of Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}.

## For young visitors
Nawfal was the uncle of Abd al-Muttalib. He took his nephew's right, and then gave it back when his mother's kin came to support his nephew.
