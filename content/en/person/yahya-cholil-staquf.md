## Summary
Yahya Cholil Staquf is an Indonesian Islamic scholar and the general chairman of the central executive board of Nahdlatul Ulama (NU), Indonesia's largest Muslim organisation and one of the largest in the world. He was elected to the post in December 2021. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-cholil-staquf}

## Life and background
He was born on 16 February 1966 in Rembang, Central Java, into a prominent NU scholarly family: his father, K.H. Cholil Bisri, was a respected kiai, and Staquf was educated in the pesantren tradition of Java's traditionalist Islamic boarding schools. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-cholil-staquf}

## Career
Staquf served as presidential spokesman under President Abdurrahman Wahid (Gus Dur) in 1999–2001, and later sat on the Presidential Advisory Council (Watimpres) under President Joko Widodo. In December 2021 the NU national congress elected him general chairman of the Tanfidziyah, the organisation's executive body, succeeding Said Aqil Siradj. Within NU he is associated with the Humanitarian Islam (Islam Nusantara's outward-facing strand) initiative, which argues for recontextualising Islamic law for a pluralist world and has been carried into NU's engagement with the G20 and international religious forums. Observers read the project as an attempt to give Indonesia's traditionalist Sunni Islam a global voice; critics inside and outside Indonesia question how far classical jurisprudence can be revised. The museum records both readings as attributed. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-cholil-staquf}

## Standing
He is listed in The Muslim 500 among the world's most influential Muslims, chiefly in the category of administration of religious affairs, reflecting the scale of NU's institutions: tens of millions of members, thousands of pesantren, and a major university network. {cite:wikipedia-yahya-cholil-staquf}
