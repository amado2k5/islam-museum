## Summary
A traditionist and memorizer from Marw and later Baghdad, called Abu Ahmad (also Abu Yahya); he transmitted from Malik, al-Layth, Hafs ibn Maysara, Isma'il ibn Ayyash and Muhammad ibn Ayyub ibn Maysara; Ahmad ibn Hanbal, his son Abd Allah, Isma'il ibn Abi al-Harith and Abu Zur'a transmitted from him; Ibn Ma'in called him reliable, Ibn Hibban listed him among the reliable, and al-Khalili called him agreed upon as reliable. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7845}
