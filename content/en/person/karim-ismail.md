# Karim Ismail

## Summary

Karim Ismail — who brands himself in Latin letters as Kareem Esmail — is an Egyptian trainer and lecturer in mental health and awareness-building, the founder of the Mental Health Hub platform and the author of the book "Istiraha Nafsiyya" (A Psychological Break). His content joins modern psychology to practical experience and spiritual values, and his courses, lectures and videos reach millions of followers across the Arab world. {cite:mental-health-hub-karim-ismail} {cite:manjmy-karim-ismail}

## His work and platform

His own platform describes Karim Ismail as a trainer and lecturer in mental health and awareness-building with more than ten years of experience in public speaking, training and coaching. It lists his professional accreditations: Associated Certified Coach (ACC) and Certified Life, Relationship, Parenting, Business and Career Coach from the International Coaching Federation (ICF); Certified CliftonStrengths Coach from Gallup; and Positive Discipline Educator from the PDA. He founded the Mental Health Hub platform, which specialises in building psychological awareness, and has cooperated with universities and institutions in designing training programmes. {cite:mental-health-hub-karim-ismail}

The platform states that his online courses have had more than forty thousand subscribers since 2018, that his educational content has passed seventy million interactions, that his digital community exceeds six million followers, and that his lectures and workshops average over a hundred thousand attendees. Among his recorded courses is "Litama'in Nafsi" (That My Soul May Be Reassured), on self-esteem, building boundaries, treating perfectionism and recovering from self-blame. {cite:mental-health-hub-karim-ismail}

He is the author of the book "Istiraha Nafsiyya" (A Psychological Break), which his platform describes as one of the most prominent Arabic books in its field. {cite:mental-health-hub-karim-ismail}

## His content and channel

The Manjmy Islamic profile presents Karim Ismail as a content creator and trainer specialising in positive psychology and self-development who has built a large community of followers seeking a deeper understanding of life, relationships and mental health, relying on a mix of scientific knowledge, real-life experience and spiritual values. {cite:manjmy-karim-ismail}

His audio work includes the podcast "Behudoo" (Calmly), on managing anxiety and pressure, building relationships and self-esteem; the podcast "Sukoon", on inner peace and psychological tranquillity; and the "Wa'i" (Awareness) series, which the profile presents as an intellectual project for reshaping how a person understands himself and the world around him. {cite:manjmy-karim-ismail}

The spiritual side holds an important place in his channel's content: among his series are "The Real Influencer", a contemporary reading of the Prophet's character as the supreme model of positive influence and humane leadership, and "Rasayel Rabbana", which draws psychological and educational meanings from verses of the Qur'an. He also treats youth questions — among them emotional relationships, attachment and self-esteem — from a psychological and behavioural perspective. {cite:manjmy-karim-ismail}
