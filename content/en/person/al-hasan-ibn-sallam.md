## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Sallam al-Sawwaq, Abu Ali of Baghdad, is called by al-Dhahabi the imam, the reliable hadith scholar {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1407}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Ubayd Allah ibn Musa, Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Muqri, Amr ibn Hakkam, Abu Nu'aym, Affan ibn Muslim and others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1407}. Ibn Sa'id al-Hafiz, Isma'il al-Saffar, Uthman ibn al-Sammak, Abu Bakr al-Najjad, Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i and many others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1407}.

## What the scholars said
Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi called him reliable and truthful {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1407}.

## His death
Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i said he died in Safar 277 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1407}.
