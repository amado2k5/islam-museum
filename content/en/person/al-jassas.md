## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Ali, known as al-Jassas al-Razi al-Hanafi, whom Haji Khalifa describes as "the shaykh and imam". He died in 370 AH, and is the author of "Ahkam al-Qur'an". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.212}

## His works
His name is linked with the book [[work/ahkam-al-quran-jassas|Ahkam al-Qur'an]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

## Notes for researchers
- The Gregorian death date (about 980 CE) is converted from the Hijri year and is derived.
- The data do not give his teachers or the place where he grew up. It needs an additional source.
