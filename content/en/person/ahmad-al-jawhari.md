## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Abd al-Karim al-Khalidi al-Jawhari was an Egyptian scholar of al-Azhar. He was born in 1096 AH, 1685 CE, and died in 1182 AH, 1768 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. His father sold jewels, and the name came to him from that {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names "Munqidhat al-'Abid min Ribqat al-Taqlid", on the oneness of God; a treatise on "al-Gharaniq"; and a thabat in manuscript, a list of the names of his teachers {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. The entry in al-A'lam is short and says nothing more.
