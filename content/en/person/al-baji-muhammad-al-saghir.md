## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Saghir ibn Yusuf al-Baji, a Tunisian historian. The encyclopaedia says that his full name is often misquoted, because he is famous under the kunya "al-Saghir al-Baji". He was born in 1109 AH (1698 CE) and died in 1185 AH (1772 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.34}
