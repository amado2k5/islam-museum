## Overview
Rabah ibn Murra, brother of al-Yamama bint Murra, known as Zarqa' al-Yamama. al-Mas'udi reports that he was the guide of King Hassan to Jadis. He warned the king that his sister could see a rider at a distance of three nights' journey, so the king ordered his companions to uproot the trees and march behind him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.455}.
