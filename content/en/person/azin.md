## Summary
Azin was a commander of Babak al-Khurrami; al-Tabari records the engagement between al-Afshin's men and Azin's men under the events of 222 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.670}

## Details
Under the events of 222 AH — the year Babak al-Khurrami, who had risen in Azerbaijan, was finally overcome — al-Tabari mentions the engagement between the troops of al-Afshin (Khaydhar ibn Kawus, dispatched by al-Mu'tasim against Babak) and the troops of Azin, one of Babak's commanders. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.670}

The war between al-Afshin and Babak was among the longest and hardest campaigns of al-Mu'tasim's reign; it ended with Babak's capture and his being carried to Samarra, where he was executed. Al-Tabari narrates its many engagements under that year, among them this clash between al-Afshin's soldiers and Azin's. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.670}
