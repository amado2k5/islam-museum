Wael Al-Dahdouh (born 30 April 1970) is a Palestinian journalist from Gaza City and the bureau chief of Al Jazeera in Gaza. He began in journalism in 1998 with the Al-Quds newspaper, worked for Voice of Palestine and Al Arabiya, and joined Al Jazeera in 2004; before that he had spent seven years in Israeli prisons after an intifada-era arrest, taking his journalism degree from the Islamic University of Gaza in 1998 and a master's from Al-Quds University in 2007.{cite:wikipedia-wael-al-dahdouh-en}{cite:wikipedia-wael-al-dahdouh-ar}{cite:wikidata-wael-al-dahdouh}

During the Gaza war he became one of its best-known reporters. An airstrike on the Nuseirat camp on 25 October 2023 killed his wife Amna, his son Mahmoud, his daughter Sham and a grandson; he buried them and returned to the air. Wounded in the December 2023 strike that killed his cameraman Samer Abu Daqqa, he reported again within days, and on 7 January 2024 his eldest son Hamza, also a journalist, was killed. He received the Peace Through Media Award in 2013 and the National Press Club's International John Aubuchon Press Freedom Award in 2024.{cite:wikipedia-wael-al-dahdouh-en}
