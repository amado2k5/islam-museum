## Overview
Asim ibn Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Hilali, whom Hisham appointed over Khurasan after the death of al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman in Merv. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His governorship
Al-Salabi says that [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] appointed him governor over Khurasan after its affairs were disturbed between the Qays and the Yemenis. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.483}
