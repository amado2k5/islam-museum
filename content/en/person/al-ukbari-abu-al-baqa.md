## Overview
Abu al-Baqa Abd Allah ibn al-Husayn ibn Abd Allah al-Ukbari, a blind grammarian, the leading grammarian of Basra in his age. His books include "al-Tibyan fi I'rab al-Qur'an" and "al-Lubab fi Ilal al-Bina' wa al-I'rab". He died in 616 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.399}
