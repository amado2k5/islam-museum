## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi is called by the opening page the companion and trusted man of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi ila al-Haqq]], compiler of his book al-Muntakhab and author of a book on the merits of the Commander of the Faithful Ali, and the narration of [[work/sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq|the biography]] is attributed to him together with Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.15} These descriptions are the book's own wording; it is a Zaydi source.

## In al-Hadi's service
The editor says he found no biography of him in the sources available to him, but that the biography itself shows he was one of al-Hadi's companions before the imam went to Yemen, perhaps joining him from Kufa after hearing of him, and that he set out for Yemen some fifty days before al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.7} In Yemen he stayed close to al-Hadi in what resembled the office of vizier: al-Hadi gave him missions, had him carry out orders and put some provincial and tax-collecting duties in his hands. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.7}

## The editor's view of his reports
The editor thinks his reports are documentary in character, though they relate events from one side. He judges that al-Kufi was not one of the compilers of the book, although the narration is attributed to him on its first page, and that the author is [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]], who relates part of the material from him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.7} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.8}
