## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Sayf al-Din al-Dihlawi was a Hanafi jurist from Delhi in India, and according to al-Zirikli the muhaddith of India in his time. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 959 AH and his death to 1052 AH, that is 1552 and 1642 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 280}.

## Life
He stayed for four years near the two Noble Sanctuaries and took from their scholars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says it was said that his writings reached one hundred volumes, in Arabic and Persian. Among them are a Muqaddima on the terminology of hadith, printed, in Arabic, and a Thabat, in manuscript, listing his teachers and his chains of transmission from them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}.
