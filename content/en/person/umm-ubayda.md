## Overview
Umm Ubayda, nurse and client of Muhammad ibn Ali {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The grant
al-Baladhuri reports that al-Mansur granted her Qatma, and that the Tawaqat of Umm Ubayda near the bridge are attributed to her {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
