## Overview
[[person/kuayba-bint-sad|Kuayba bint Sa'd al-Aslamiyya]], a Companion known for treating the wounded at the [[event/battle-of-uhud|Battle of Uhud]]. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.363}

## At Uhud
Her tent was near the field of battle. She grieved over the death of many Muslims, and then bent to treating some of the wounded and watching over their comfort. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.363}

## The lesson of Uhud
The book *Nisa' Hawl al-Rasul* says that from Uhud she learned to set her tent close to the field of battle, so that she could help the wounded and save them. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.363}

## Notes for researchers
- The events here are taken from *Nisa' Hawl al-Rasul*, a modern reference used for events and references only. They have not yet been checked against the reports of Ibn Sa'd and Ibn Ishaq.
- Her dates of birth and death, her husband and her detailed lineage are not given here, as they have not been read in the local sources yet.
