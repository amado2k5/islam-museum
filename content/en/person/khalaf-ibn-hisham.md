## Overview
Khalaf ibn Hisham, from Baghdad, one of the ten readers of the Qur'an. The sources say that his reading is accepted. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His birth is reported around 150 AH and his death around 848 CE, approximately. These dates need a second source, and the rest needs an additional source.

## Chain and reading in Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr

He is Abu Muhammad Khalaf ibn Hisham ibn Tha'lab al-Bazzar, the owner of the choice. Ibn al-Jazari treats him both as a transmitter of the reading of [[person/hamza-al-zayyat|Hamza]] and as an imam with a choice of his own, which is the tenth reading of the ten in the order of al-Nashr. He read on [[person/sulaym-ibn-isa-al-hanafi|Sulaym]], the companion of Hamza, on Ya'qub ibn Khalifa al-A'sha, a companion of Abu Bakr ibn 'Ayyash, and on Abu Zayd Sa'id ibn Aws al-Ansari, a companion of al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi and Aban al-'Attar, all of whom read on 'Asim; he narrated letters from Ishaq al-Musayyabi, the companion of Nafi', from Yahya ibn Adam from Abu Bakr, and from al-Kisa'i, without reading the whole to him. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 201} He died in Jumada II 229 AH and was born in 150; he memorised the Qur'an at ten, began seeking knowledge at thirteen, and was a great imam, learned, reliable, ascetic and devout. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 201} Ibn al-Jazari records Ibn Ashta's claim that his choice differed from Hamza's in a hundred and twenty letters, and then says he traced the choice and found it leaves the reading of the Kufans in a single letter, in the sura of al-Anbiya', and that Abu al-'Izz al-Qalanisi reports from him a silent pause between suras, which differs from the Kufans. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 201} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 202} His transmitters are [[person/ishaq-ibn-ibrahim-al-warraq|Ishaq al-Warraq]] and [[person/idris-ibn-abd-al-karim-al-haddad|Idris al-Haddad]], and Ibn al-Jazari counts thirty-one lines of transmission. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 201}
