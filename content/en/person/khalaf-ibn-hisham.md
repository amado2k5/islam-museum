## Overview
Khalaf ibn Hisham, from Baghdad, one of the ten readers of the Qur'an. The sources say that his reading is accepted. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His birth is reported around 150 AH and his death around 848 CE, approximately. These dates need a second source, and the rest needs an additional source.
