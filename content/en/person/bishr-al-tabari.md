## Summary
Bishr al-Tabari lived in Syria, one of God's lovers who were tested and submitted to what was tried upon them. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 472 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 130. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/130}

## The buffalo and the servants
Abu Amr al-Kindi relates: the Byzantines raided some four hundred buffalo of Bishr al-Tabari. Abu Amr rode with him, with a son of Bishr. They met the servants who had been with the buffalo, carrying their staves, and they said: Master, the buffalo have gone! He said: and you too, go with them; you are free for the sake of God. His son said: Father, you have impoverished us! He said: be quiet, my son; my Lord has tested me, and I wished to increase it. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/130}
