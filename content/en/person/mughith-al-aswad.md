## Summary
Mughith al-Aswad was a preacher of the finest counsel and of the most binding reminders. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 502, sequence 503, on page 143. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143}

## Reminders of graves, Paradise and Fire
A man of the Quraysh relates that Mughith al-Aswad used to say: visit the graves each day in your thought, picture each day the comprehensive good of Paradise with your minds, look at where the two groups go, to Paradise or to Hell, with your resolve, and let your hearts and bodies feel the mention of the Fire, its hooks and its layers. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143}
