## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Abi Dawud al-Sijistani, Sulayman ibn al-Ash'ath ibn Ishaq ibn Bashir ibn Shaddad. Ibn al-Nadim counts him among the great hadith scholars and jurists, trustworthy, and gives his death as 316 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.324-325}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the book "al-Tafsir", which he composed when al-Tabari composed his book, and "al-Masabih fi al-Hadith", "al-Masahif", "Nazm al-Qur'an", "Fada'il al-Qur'an", "Shari'at al-Tafsir", "al-Nasikh wa al-Mansukh", and "al-Ba'th wa al-Nushur". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.324-325}

## An account with the vizier
Al-Suyuti relates that the vizier [[person/ali-ibn-isa|Ali ibn Isa]] wished to reconcile him with Ibn Sa'id, and they exchanged sharp words. Then Ibn Abi Dawud said that he would never take anything from the vizier's hand. When al-Muqtadir heard of it, he began to weigh his stipend with his own hand and send it to him on a tray with the servant. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.595}

The report is from al-Suyuti alone and needs an additional source.
