## Summary
Mawla Hamid Afandi, mufti of the imperial throne after Mawlana Abu'l-Su'ud; the Kawakib says: among the virtuous men of letters, much given in charity, a fierce champion of truth; he was ailing until he died at the start of Sha'ban. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 408}
