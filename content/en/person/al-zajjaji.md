## Overview
Abu Ishaq al-Zajjaji, a grammarian and companion of Abu al-Abbas al-Mubarrad. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.594}

Al-Mas'udi counts him among those who collected the sayings of the Prophet in their books, together with Ibn Durayd, Nafṭawayh and Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Mawsili. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.594}
