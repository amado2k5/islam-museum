## Overview
Al-Mas'ud Atsiz ibn al-Malik al-Kamil, an Ayyubid, lord of Yemen. He is the son of [[person/al-malik-al-kamil|al-Malik al-Kamil]] Muhammad ibn al-Adil. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435-436}

## Mecca in 620 AH
He marched to Mecca in 620 AH, and his army plundered it until the afternoon, to the point of taking clothes from people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435-436} See [[event/yemeni-sack-mecca-620|the event]].

He ordered the grave of the Sharif Qatada ibn Idris dug up and burned. They dug it up and the coffin was empty. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435-436}
