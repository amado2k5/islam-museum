## Overview
Yahya ibn Yusuf ibn Yahya al-Ansari, Abu Zakariyya, Jamal al-Din al-Sarsari, was a poet from Sarsar near Baghdad who settled in Baghdad and was blind {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 588 AH, 1192 CE, and his death as 656 AH, 1258 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}.

## Works
He left a small diwan of verse, in manuscript, and versified works on jurisprudence and other subjects {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}. One is al-Durra al-Yatima wa-l-Mahajja al-Mustaqima, in manuscript, a poem rhymed in dal on Hanbali jurisprudence of 2,774 verses, which Muhammad ibn Ayyub al-Tadhifi commented on in two volumes {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}. Others are al-Muntaqa min Madaih al-Rasul, in manuscript, which al-Zirikli thinks may be the work called al-Mukhtar min Madaih al-Mukhtar, a creed in manuscript, and al-Wasiyya al-Sarsariyya, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}. He also composed a poem each verse of which contains all the letters of the alphabet, beginning with the line «abat ghayra thajji al-dam'i muqlatu dhi huzni» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}.

## Death
Al-Zirikli reports that the Tatars killed him on the day they entered Baghdad, and that it was said he struck one of them with his crutch before he was killed as a martyr {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}. His body was carried to Sarsar and buried there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 177}.
