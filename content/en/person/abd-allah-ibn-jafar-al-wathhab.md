## Overview
'Abdallah b. Ja'far, known as Ibn al-Waththab; he was attached to al-Ta'i' li'llah, and when al-Ta'i' was seized and deposed he fled, wandering the lands until he reached al-Batiha, where he stayed with Muhadhdhib al-Dawla before moving on. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.206-207}

## Claiming the caliphate
Al-Qadir gave money to whoever brought him in; he was jailed, then escaped to Gilan and claimed to be al-Ta'i' li'llah himself, naming marks he knew from his intimacy with the caliphal palace — they accepted and honoured him; Ibn al-'Abbas, one of their amirs, married him to his daughter, he established the da'wa in his country, people of other districts obeyed him and paid him the tithe — until a party of them reached Baghdad, his affair was exposed, and they turned from him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.206-207}
