## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas Malik, Abu al-Qasim al-Qurashi al-Zuhri of Medina, is called by al-Dhahabi the reliable imam; he was brother of the commander Umar ibn Sa'd, of Amir ibn Sa'd and of Aisha bint Sa'd {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3436}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from his father, from Uthman ibn Affan, from Abu al-Darda and others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3436}. His sons Ibrahim and Ismail, Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i, Yunus ibn Jubayr, Ismail ibn Abi Khalid and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3436}. By the codes at the head of the entry, al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Maja recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3436}.

## Rising with Ibn al-Ash'ath
He narrated a fair amount of knowledge, then was among those who rose against al-Hajjaj with Ibn al-Ash'ath; he was taken prisoner on the day of Dayr al-Jamajim and al-Hajjaj killed him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3436}. It is also said that he fled to al-Mada'in, where many people gathered to him, and that he then went on to Basra {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3437}.

## His death
He met his end in 82 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3437}.
