## Summary
Ibn Salam, the pure muhaddith Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Salim ibn Salam al-Dimashqi; he heard from Dawud ibn Mala'ib, Ibn al-Ban and their generation — an accomplished imam, alert, meticulous, righteous and ascetic despite his youth, who wrote much and memorised the sciences of hadith for the Hakim; he died in Safar 630 at twenty-one, and his father was stricken by his loss. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 140}
