## Overview
Lord of Mosul — the sultan al-Malik al-Qahir 'Izz al-Din Abu'l-Fath Mas'ud b. Sultan Nur al-Din Arslan Shah b. Mas'ud the Atabegid Zangid; born in 590, he ruled after his father at seventeen, described as handsome, just and generous. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 62}

## His death
It was said he was poisoned; he died in Rabi' II 615 at twenty-five, and the people felt his loss grievously. After him ruled, by his own appointment, his son Nur al-Din Arslan Shah — also called 'Ali — aged ten, who died at the year's end likewise. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 62}
