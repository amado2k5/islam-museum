Hidayet "Hedo" Türkoğlu (born 19 March 1979 in Istanbul) is a Turkish basketball executive and former player. The first Turkish-born player in NBA history, he played fifteen seasons for six teams, won the league's Most Improved Player award in 2007–08, reached the 2009 NBA Finals with Orlando, and has served as president of the Turkish Basketball Federation since October 2016.{cite:wikipedia-hidayet-turkoglu-en}{cite:wikidata-hidayet-turkoglu}

His English biography states of him that he is a Muslim, and Wikidata carries the same structured value; no source names a school for him, so his faith is recorded only to that extent.{cite:wikipedia-hidayet-turkoglu-en}
