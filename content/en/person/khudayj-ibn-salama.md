## Summary

Khudayj ibn Salama ibn Aws al-Balawi, an ally of Banu Haram of the Ansar; his father's name is also given as Salim, and his kunya was Abu Shibath. Musa ibn Uqba, al-Tabari and others list him at the second pledge of Aqaba and say he fought at neither Badr nor Uhud. Abu Musa, following Ibn Makula, made him two men because of the disagreement over his father's name. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.388}
