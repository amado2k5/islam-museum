## Summary

Khuzayma ibn Aws ibn Yazid ibn Asram al-Ansari, of Banu al-Najjar. Musa ibn Uqba lists him among those at Badr, and Salama ibn al-Fadl, from Ibn Ishaq, lists him among those killed at the Battle of the Bridge. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.392}
