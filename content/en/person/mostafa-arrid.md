# Mostafa Arrid

## Summary
Mostafa Arrid is the author of the YouTube channel "Ishraqat Nafsiyya" (Psychological Illuminations), a psychological-guidance channel mixing counselling with religious subjects, whose videos he presents himself. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

The channel (@Dr.Arrid_Mostafa) describes itself as "the channel of purposeful psychological guidance", and lists among its keywords "the objective school in social psychology". {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

## Career
Arrid publishes his videos on his channel himself, and its titles describe him as "the promising preacher" and "the promising jurist". {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

The videos shown on the channel span about a decade, the most recent about two years before the channel was checked on 26 October 2026. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

No independent biographical source for him could be verified, so nothing is recorded here about his life beyond his announced work on his channel. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

## Works
Among his channel's videos is "The relation of istighfar (seeking forgiveness) to the human body", its most viewed at about 147,000 views when checked. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

Other videos treat religious and jurisprudential subjects, among them sacred hadiths (ahadith qudsiyya), the rulings of the festival sacrifice and the meanings of verses, alongside videos on calming the anxious soul. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}

## Positions and criticism
This section presents what the sources report about his work; it is not the museum's verdict on him or on his opinions.

His announced approach on his channel is to offer psychological guidance within an Islamic frame, pairing subjects of the soul and anxiety with religious material from the Qur'an, the Sunna and jurisprudence. {cite:youtube-mostafa-arrid}
