## Overview
Bujayr ibn Waraqa al-Sarami of the Banu Tamim. He was among a group who deposed Ibn Khazim and went to Abrashahr. They followed him while he was heading to Tirmidh, and he fought them near Merv and stabbed him with Wakî ibn al-Dawraqiyya. Al-Baladhuri relates that he rose against [[person/bukayr-ibn-wishah|Bukayr ibn Washah]] in his prison and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
