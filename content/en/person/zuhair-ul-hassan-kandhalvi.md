## Summary
Maulana Zuhair ul-Hassan Kandhalvi is a leader of the Tablighi Jamaat, the global Sunni preaching movement centred at Nizamuddin in Delhi, and a member of the international consultative council (alami shura) that has governed the movement since its amirate gave way to collective leadership. {cite:muslim500-zuhair-ul-hassan-kandhalvi}

## Life and formation
He was born and raised in Nizamuddin, Delhi, into the movement's first family: his father Maulana Zubair ul-Hasan Kandhlavi (1950–2014) was the fourth amir of the Tablighi Jamaat and a member of its founding-era shura, and his maternal line descends from Sheikh Zakariyya Kandhlawi. He completed his Islamic studies at Mazahir Uloom in Saharanpur, the Deobandi seminary of the Kandhalvi family. {cite:muslim500-zuhair-ul-hassan-kandhalvi} {cite:wikipedia-zubairul-hasan-kandhlavi}

## The leadership question
After Zubair ul-Hasan's death in 2014, the shura expanded at the Raiwind centre in Pakistan while Maulana Saad Kandhalvi asserted leadership at Nizamuddin — a dispute that split the movement's elders, mosques and international chapters between the "shura" side and Saad's followers. Zuhair ul-Hassan, as his father's son, is among the figures on the shura side around whom loyalties have coalesced, including in Pakistan where the Raiwind centre backs the council. {cite:muslim500-zuhair-ul-hassan-kandhalvi} {cite:wikipedia-zubairul-hasan-kandhlavi}

## Assessments
To supporters of the shura he represents continuity with the founders' collective model; to Saad's followers his side abandoned an agreed amir. The museum records the positions attributed, without verdict, and notes that the Tablighi Jamaat is a quietist preaching movement with no organisational tie to militancy. {cite:muslim500-zuhair-ul-hassan-kandhalvi} {cite:wikipedia-zubairul-hasan-kandhlavi}
