## Overview
Abd al-Salam ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Rijal al-Lakhmi al-Ifriqi, known as Ibn Barrajan, a theologian and ascetic Sufi from the Maghrib. He was known for preaching, Sufism and kalam. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He died in 536 AH (1141 CE), according to report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
