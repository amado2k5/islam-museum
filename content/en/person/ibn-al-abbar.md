## Summary
Ibn al-Abbar, the katib-adib, one of the imams of hadith; he mastered the readings, devoted himself to the tradition, excelled in rhetoric, verse and prose, and possessed majesty and standing. The lord of Tunis killed him unjustly on the twentieth of Muharram, aged sixty-three. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 303}
