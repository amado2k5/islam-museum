## Overview
Judai' al-Kirmani, leader of the Yemeni and Rabi'a forces in Khurasan during the Abbasid revolution. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.24-25}

## The arena of Khurasan
Taqqush says that four forces were in the arena: the Umayyad state led by Nasr ibn Sayyar; the Yemeni and Rabi'a under Judai'; the Harurite Kharijites under Shayban al-Harurî; and the Abbasid revolution led by Abu Muslim. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.24-25}

## With Abu Muslim
Abu Muslim cooperated with Judai', then with his son Ali, and with Shayban al-Harurî, to overthrow the Umayyads. Then he sowed discord between the Umayyad governor and the tribal chiefs, and got rid of Shayban and the two sons of Judai', Ali and Uthman. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.24-25}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] says Juday' was called al-Kirmani because he was born in Kirman while his father was with al-Muhallab fighting the Azariqa. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 340} He reports that al-Kirmani went by night to Abu Muslim's camp and obtained safe-conduct for his men, and that on his return Nasr had him killed by stealth; others say Nasr sent a commander with three hundred horse who ambushed him by night. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 362}
