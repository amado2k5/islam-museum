## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Hayyan al-Jama'i went in to al-Hadi at Sa'da with [[person/al-zubayr-al-kulaybi|al-Zubayr al-Kulaybi]] and some Yarsamis and they asked safe-conduct for the Akilis, which he granted. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} The index lists him at p. 157 only. See [[event/safe-conduct-for-the-akilis-and-al-hadis-verses-from-saada-286]].
