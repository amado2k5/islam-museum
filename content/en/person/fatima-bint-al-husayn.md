## Overview
Fatima bint al-Husayn wrote to the caliph Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz thanking him for what he had done. He had divided one thousand dinars from the share of the kinsfolk among the Banu Hashim, and made no distinction in it between male and female, or between the grown and the young. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.358}

An additional source is needed: the book does not state her lineage precisely, and the name given here is the one that appears in the text.
