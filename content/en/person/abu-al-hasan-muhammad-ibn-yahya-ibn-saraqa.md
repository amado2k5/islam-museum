## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Saraqa, a jurist and expert in inheritance, who took the science of inheritance from [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-labban-al-basri|Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Laban]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his death.
