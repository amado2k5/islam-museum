## Overview
Malik ibn Murra al-Rahawi, envoy of the kings of Himyar to the Prophet. He brought news of their Islam and of their abandoning of polytheism, and Zar'a Dhu Yazan had sent him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235-237}

## The message
He met the Prophet in Medina as he returned from Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235-237} See [[event/letter-of-himyar-kings|the letter of the kings of Himyar]].

## The Prophet's letter to Himyar
The Prophet mentioned him in his letter to Himyar among his envoys, whose commander was Mu'adh ibn Jabal. The letter says that he conveyed the news and kept the unseen, and that he reported that Zar'a had become Muslim first among the Himyar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235-237} See [[event/mission-of-muadh-to-yemen|the mission of Mu'adh to Yemen]].
