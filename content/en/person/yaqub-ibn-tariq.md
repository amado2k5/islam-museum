## Overview
Ya'qub ibn Tariq, among the best of the astrologers; author of the cutting of sine tables, a book on what rises of the meridian arc, and the Zij computed in the Sindhind degree by degree — one of the books being on the science of the sphere. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 411}
