## Overview
Imran ibn Muslim al-Qasir of Basra, who saw Anas and narrated from al-Hasan and Ata ibn Abi Rabah. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3492}

## Assessment
Al-Qattan called his narrations sound and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy, while Ibn Ma'in said he was worth nothing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3492}
