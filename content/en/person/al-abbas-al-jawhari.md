## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Sa'id al-Jawhari, one of the astronomers of the observatory whose field was chiefly geometry; author of a commentary on Euclid's book and a book on the propositions he added to Euclid's first book. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 412}
