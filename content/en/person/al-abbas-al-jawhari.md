## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Sa'id al-Jawhari, one of the astronomers, whose main study was geometry, according to Ibn al-Nadim's "Fihrist". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.375}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim mentions two of his books: a commentary on the book of Euclid, and the book of "Figures" that he added to the first treatise of Euclid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.375}

His birth and death dates need an additional source; they are not recorded.
