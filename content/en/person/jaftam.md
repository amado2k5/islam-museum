## Overview
Jaftam is a name that occurs in the account of [[person/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-qadi-of-hamdan|Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak]] and Murawwi al-Sa'idi of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s prayer about him: "O God, drive him out of Yemen in the worst of states; and the year had not turned before he left Yemen, driven out, wandering and afraid." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The book gives neither his lineage nor his place in the story here, and the name is as printed and as in the book's index, p. 444, which lists it at pp. 140, 250, 253, 272 and 273, pages not yet read, so nothing beyond p. 140 is ascribed to him. It is an attributed account. See [[event/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-and-murawwi-on-al-hadis-prayers-against-abu-mihjan-and-jaftam-286]].
