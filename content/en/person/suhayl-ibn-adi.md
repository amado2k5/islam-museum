## Overview
Suhayl ibn Adi went to Kirman in the year 23 AH, and he had a vanguard in which was al-Nasir ibn Amr al-Ijli. al-Tabari reports it {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.180}.
