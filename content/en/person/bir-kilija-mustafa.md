## Summary
Börklüce Mustafa — in al-Sallabi's account the best-known preacher and field commander of Sheikh Badr al-Din's movement. He killed Mehmed's commander Shishman and routed his army, then fell to a second host under the vizier Bayezid Pasha at Qara Burnu and was executed under the hiraba ruling {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 75}.
