## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Umm Shayban al-Hashimi, the judge. Al-Muti' appointed him judge in 363 AH after he had been reluctant, and he stipulated certain conditions for himself: that he would not draw a salary from the judgeship, would not be clothed in robes of honour, and would not be interceded with in anything that contradicted the Sharia. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.622-623}

A long letter of appointment was written for him, naming him chief judge over all the provinces to which the call reached. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.622-623}
