## Overview

Abu al-Harith al-Layth ibn Khalid of Baghdad is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/al-kisai|al-Kisa'i]], with [[person/abu-umar-al-duri|al-Duri]]. He died in 240 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 183}

## His standing

Ibn al-Jazari calls him reliable, a firm upholder of the reading, exact and a verifier, and quotes al-Dani that he was among the leading companions of al-Kisa'i. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 183} From him Muhammad ibn Yahya, who died in 288 AH, took the reading; al-Dani called him the most distinguished of Abu al-Harith's pupils. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 183}
