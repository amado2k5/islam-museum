## Summary
Da'laj b. Ahmad b. Da'laj b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sijistani; an amir of the 'Abbasid state and a man of generosity and honour — when word reached him of a needy man he would grant him and verify his account for fear of error, and he gave money to those who stood before his gate. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
