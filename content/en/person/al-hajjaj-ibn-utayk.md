## Overview
Al-Hajjaj ibn Utayk, a man of the Thaqif. He was the husband of Umm Jamil daughter of Yahjan, whom the witnesses saw al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba going in to. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He was over the Euphrates, and is the al-Hajjaj whom Abu al-Mukhtar mentions in his ode on the governors of al-Ahwaz. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
