## Overview
Ayyub ibn Razah, also called Yutab. Al-Mas'udi says that he was the second of those from Yemen to rule Syria after Faligh ibn Yaghur. He says that God mentioned his story through his prophet, and that then the Byzantines overcame their lands and they scattered through the lands. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.425}
