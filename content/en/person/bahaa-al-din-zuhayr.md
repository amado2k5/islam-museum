## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Zuhayr b. Muhammad b. 'Ali b. Yahya, styled Baha' al-Din; the poet-katib, high in his master's esteem, interceding only for good and profiting many by his gracious mediation; his first panegyric was at Damietta's port in 605, and he went to Mosul as envoy from his master al-Malik al-Salih. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## At Ibn Khallikan's
Ibn Khallikan, who met him at Cairo, described him for noble character, great modesty and gentle nature, and licensed him to transmit his diwan — abundant in people's hands. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
