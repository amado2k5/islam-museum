## Overview
Abd al-Jabbar ibn Muhammad Shit al-Jumurd, an Iraqi historian. He was born in Mosul in 1909 CE and grew up in a family of scholars, and he died in 1971 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
