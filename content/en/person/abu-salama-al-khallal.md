## Overview
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that [[person/bukayr-ibn-mahan|Bukayr ibn Mahan]] on his deathbed appointed Abu Salama al-Khallal, one of the leading partisans, and that the imam Muhammad ibn Ali wrote confirming him in charge. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 334}

## At Kufa
When Ibrahim al-Imam was killed, Abu al-Abbas, Abu Ja'far and their uncles fled to Kufa and stayed as guests of Abu Salama. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 358} Al-Dinawari says Abu Salama was a vinegar-seller, and that their evening meals were made of the butcher Musawir's meat, Abu Salama's vinegar and Yaqtin's spices. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 359}

## Vizierate and death
Abu al-Abbas then made him vizier over all affairs beyond his door; he was called “the vizier of the family of Muhammad” and acted without consultation. Abu Muslim sent his commander Marwan al-Dabbi to Kufa, who took him from the caliph's presence, beheaded him and left. Al-Dinawari does not say here that the caliph consented. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 370}
