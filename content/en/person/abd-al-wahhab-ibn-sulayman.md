## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Sulayman ibn Ali ibn Mushrif al-Tamimi al-Najdi was a Hanbali jurist, whom al-Zirikli lists in al-A'lam under the heading Ibn Mushrif. He gives his death as 1153 AH, 1740 CE, and prints a zero for the year of birth, meaning it is unknown {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 182}.

## Life
He came from al-Uyayna in Najd and held its judgeship, then moved from there to Huraymila {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 182}. Al-Zirikli names him as the father of Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, whom he calls the imam of the Hanbalis of Najd {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 182}.

## Writings
He left writings on some questions of jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 182}.
