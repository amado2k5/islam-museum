## Summary

Mahmoud Xaliike (Somali: Sheekh Maxamuud Xaliike; full form as seen in the wave harvest: Maxamuud Maxamed Maxamuud Xaliike) is a Somali scholar and preacher based in the Laascaanood area whose lectures are among the most carried in the Somali scene, borne by aggregator channels across it.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} His work is published officially through the Rashaad Media operation, whose YouTube videos call their channel "the official channel page of Sheikh Mahmoud Xaliike"; the operation's TikTok account counted 181,400 followers, as its profile was indexed in web search at about 20 October 2026.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media}{cite:tiktok-rashaad-media}

## His preaching and lectures

He is based in the Laascaanood area and keeps a recurring lecture series at Al Huda Mosque in Laascaanood, whose fourth session was announced on 8 January 2026.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} Lectures of his in Laascaanood and Garoowe are dated November 2026, among them the Laascaanood lecture on "The Danger of Hypocrisy", whose video description was read as indexed on 27 October 2026.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} His documented topics of October–November 2026 include the importance of patience and prayer, a multi-part series on Muslim character — whose credits style him "Sheikh Mahmoud Muhammad Haliki" — and blessings on the Prophet.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} His lectures do not stay on his official channel: aggregator channels of the scene carry them, among them WARBAHINTA NOLOSHA ISLAAMKA, Dacwa TV, Iftiin Diineed and Tasbiix Production (the last reached by reels of February–March 2026), but those channels' audiences are their own and are not credited to him.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media}

## His official channel

Rashaad Media is his official channel operation: its YouTube videos (@RashaadMedia1) state outright that the channel is his official channel page and promote his Telegram channels, and the brand is run by Rashaad Cabdilaahi in Laascaanood, whose Facebook post of 8 January 2026 announced the Al Huda Mosque session while cross-promoting the channel and the TikTok account together.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} No separate personal YouTube channel under his own name was found in either research pass; his YouTube presence runs entirely through the operation's channel.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media} The operation's TikTok account (@rashaadmedia1) counted 181,400 followers and 4,500,000 likes, as the profile was indexed at about 20 October 2026, up from 180,700 followers at about 15 October 2026, and fresh videos from the account were still being posted up to 8 November 2026 — a growing, active account.{cite:tiktok-rashaad-media} The operation's YouTube subscriber count could not be pinned from any public tracker in either research pass, so no YouTube figure is stated for him here.{cite:youtube-rashaad-media}
