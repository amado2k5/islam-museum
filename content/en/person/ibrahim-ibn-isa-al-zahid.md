## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Isa al-Zahid kept company with Ma'ruf al-Karkhi and heard from Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi and Muhammad ibn al-Muqri'. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 672, sequence 673, on page 393. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}

## His prayer for the other communities and his sayings
Abu Ja'far al-Dani relates that he was in Ibrahim's house, and that after his dawn prayer Ibrahim would pray for the Jews, Christians and Magians to be guided, then raise his hands and say: O God, if You are to put me in the Fire, enlarge my body until there is no room in it for the community of Muhammad. Among his sayings: the believer thinks well of God and trusts His promise; he takes piety as a register and the Qur'an as a guide; fear is the road, longing the mount, anxious shame the emblem, prayer a treasure, patience a minister and shame the commander. The more one grows in righteousness toward God, the more God makes one fear Him; think well of God and you will do well. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}

## A report on a man of Paradise
Ibrahim relates through a chain to Ibn Umar a report about a man of the people of Paradise who would appear to the people, and each day Mu'awiya appeared. The wording is not reproduced here, and its judgement is left to the scholars of hadith. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}
