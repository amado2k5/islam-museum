## Overview
Nafisa bint Abi Muhammad al-Hasan b. Zayd b. al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. Abi Talib, known as al-Sayyida Nafisa; among the righteous, pious women of the Prophet's house; she dwelt in Egypt at the site of her shrine on Darb al-Siba' between Cairo and Fustat. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Al-Shafi'i with her; her burial
It is related that when Imam al-Shafi'i entered Egypt he attended her and heard hadith on her, and when he died his bier was brought into her house and she prayed over him in her home; her husband the trusted Ishaq b. Ja'far al-Sadiq resolved to carry her to Medina for burial, but the Egyptians begged him to keep her — so she was buried in Egypt, and her grave is famed for answered prayer and is visited. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme "Liqa' al-Iman"
In episode 6 of [[work/likaa-al-iman|Liqa' al-Iman]] [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|al-Sha'rawi]] is asked why al-Sayyida Nafisa is called "Nafisat al-'Ilm" (the precious one of knowledge). {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:00:00} He divides knowledge in two: knowledge of the universe, on which people's interests in building up the earth depend, and heavenly knowledge that teaches the lawful and the forbidden. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:01:30} He says she went to the more precious of the two, the knowledge of the lawful and forbidden, because knowledge of the universe serves a person's happiness in a life of limited span, while knowledge of the lawful leads to a life that does not end and whose bliss is not lost. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:09:30} He links nafs (self), nafas (breath) and nafis (precious) as one root, saying a person can go a month without food and ten days without drink, but cannot go without air, breathing in or out. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:10:00} He holds that however much a person enjoys this world, two things threaten him there: that he dies and loses the bliss, or that the bliss leaves him while he lives. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:10:30} He describes the wonders reported of her as personal experiences. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep06 00:11:30}
