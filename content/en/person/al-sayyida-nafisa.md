## Overview
Nafisa bint Abi Muhammad al-Hasan b. Zayd b. al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. Abi Talib, known as al-Sayyida Nafisa; among the righteous, pious women of the Prophet's house; she dwelt in Egypt at the site of her shrine on Darb al-Siba' between Cairo and Fustat. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Al-Shafi'i with her; her burial
It is related that when Imam al-Shafi'i entered Egypt he attended her and heard hadith on her, and when he died his bier was brought into her house and she prayed over him in her home; her husband the trusted Ishaq b. Ja'far al-Sadiq resolved to carry her to Medina for burial, but the Egyptians begged him to keep her — so she was buried in Egypt, and her grave is famed for answered prayer and is visited. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
