## Summary
Ottoman fleet commander under Süleyman the Magnificent {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249-252}.

## Djerba and Tajura
He attacked Djerba in Ramadan 967/May 1560, achieving his aims against Spain — which did not scruple to call in France — then, persuaded by [[person/turgut-reis|Dragut]], turned to Tripoli to punish the rebels near Tajura, entering like a conqueror with enemy flags drooping from his masts {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249}.

## Malta
He commanded the fleet at the [[event/siege-of-malta-1565|Great Siege of Malta]] in 973/1565 by Süleyman's order {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.
