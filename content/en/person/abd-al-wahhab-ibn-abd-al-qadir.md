## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Wahhab b. Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, the Baghdadi Hanbali preacher; born 2 Sha'ban 522, his father had him audit while still a boy, and he read fiqh under him until he excelled, lecturing as his deputy at the madrasa while his father lived — he was then past twenty — and independently after him; he held the mazalim office for al-Nasir in 583. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 794}

## His wit
Clever and graceful, one of Baghdad's wits; none of his father's sons was more versed in fiqh — eloquent in preaching, apt with choice anecdotes, humorous, good-natured and shrewd. Asked one day in his session, "what say you of the People of the House?" he answered, "they have blinded me" — he was weak-eyed himself — answering for his own house. He died on the night of Wednesday 15 Shawwal 593. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 794}
