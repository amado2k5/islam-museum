## Overview
Waki' ibn Hassan al-Tamimi, of the Banu Ghudana ibn Yarbu' ibn Handhala, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in Futuh al-Buldan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Against Qutayba
al-Baladhuri reports that al-Hudayn ibn al-Mundhir recommended him to the people of Khurasan to fight Qutayba. He thought that he alone could manage the matter, because he was a rough Bedouin whom his tribe obeyed, and Qutayba had killed the Banu al-Ahtham, whose blood they were seeking {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He gave them his hand and they pledged allegiance to him. The envoy between him and them was Hayyan, client of Mustasal {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
