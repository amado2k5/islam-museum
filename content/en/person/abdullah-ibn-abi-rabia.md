Abdullah ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi, one of Quraysh's two hardened envoys alongside [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]; before he became Muslim, Quraysh sent him to [[person/negus-of-aksum|the Negus]] with gifts and arguments to recover the [[event/second-migration-to-abyssinia|emigrants to Abyssinia]] — but the Negus refused and returned their gifts. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
