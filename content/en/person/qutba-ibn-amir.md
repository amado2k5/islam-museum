## Summary
Qutba ibn Amir ibn Hadida al-Ansari al-Khazraji; one of the six Khazraj who met the Prophet at the first Aqaba meeting, and one of its twelve deputies — he witnessed Badr and the pledges {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## The Kha'tham detachment
In Safar of year 9 AH the Prophet placed him over [[event/sariyya-of-qutba-to-khatham|a detachment of twenty men]] on ten camels they rode in turns, to a tribe of [[group/khathum|Kha'tham]] in the region of Tabala; he launched the raid, they fought a hard fight, and the Muslims drove off the cattle, women and sheep to Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
Qutba was among the first of Medina's people to believe in the Prophet at Aqaba before the hijra; he witnessed Badr and later led a detachment in the south of the Peninsula.
