## Summary
Abida Parveen (born 1954 in Larkana, Sindh) is a Pakistani Sufi singer, one of the greatest voices of Sindh and the Punjab. She sings the verse of Shah Abd al-Latif Bhittai, Sultan Bahu and the Sufi poetic heritage at the gatherings of the darbar and the adraj. {cite:wikidata}
