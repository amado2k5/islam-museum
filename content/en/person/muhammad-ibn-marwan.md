## Overview
Muhammad ibn Marwan ibn al-Hakam. Al-Baladhuri relates that he governed Mosul, al-Jazira, Armenia and Azerbaijan for his father Marwan. His chief of police was Ibn Tulayd, who paved Mosul with stones. From al-Waqidi, al-Baladhuri relates that Abd al-Malik appointed him over al-Jazira and Armenia, and appointed his brother Sa'id over Mosul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
