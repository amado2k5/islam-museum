## Overview
Abu Sa'id 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi'l-Hasan Ahmad b. Abi Musa Yunus b. 'Abd al-A'la al-Sadafi, the Egyptian muhaddith-historian — grandson of Yunus b. 'Abd al-A'la, companion of Imam al-Shafi'i and transmitter of his new doctrine; he assembled for Egypt two histories: the larger devoted to the Egyptians, the smaller covering strangers who came to Egypt; Abu'l-Qasim Yahya b. 'Ali al-Hadrami continued and built on both. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
