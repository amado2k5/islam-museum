## Overview
Habib ibn Zayd ibn Asim, an Ansari of the Banu Mabdhul of the Banu al-Najjar. The Prophet sent him with Abd Allah ibn Wahb al-Aslami to [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]]. Musaylima did not touch Abd Allah, but he cut off Habib's hands and feet. Al-Baladhuri gives the words of al-Waqidi that the two had come with Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri from Oman, and that Musaylima took them captive, and that Amr and those with him escaped except these two. Their mother was Nusayba bint Ka'b, who fought on the day of al-Yamama. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His mutilation came before the battle of al-Yamama. His death is estimated at around 11 AH; the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Khalid Muhammad Khalid's Rijal Hawl al-Rasul
[[person/khalid-muhammad-khalid|Khalid Muhammad Khalid]] calls his chapter "A legend of sacrifice and love". He says Habib and his father Zayd ibn Asim were among the seventy who pledged at the Second Aqaba, and that his mother [[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b]] was the first of the two women who pledged that night, the other being his maternal aunt. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.492} He relates that after the Prophet answered Musaylima's letter claiming half the earth, he chose Habib to carry a letter forbidding him his follies. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.493} Brought before Musaylima after being tortured by his men, Habib was asked, "Do you testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of God?" — "Yes." — "And do you testify that I am the Messenger of God?" — "I hear nothing." He was killed; the author notes that he could have saved himself by outward compliance with his heart firm in faith, but chose steadfastness. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.494} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.495} He ends with his mother Nusayba at al-Yamama: when Musaylima was killed she stood, her body full of wounds, seeing her martyred son's face everywhere. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.496}
