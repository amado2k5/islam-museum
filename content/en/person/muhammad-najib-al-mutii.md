## Summary
Shaykh Muhammad Najib al-Muti'i was a Shafi'i jurist and teacher in Cairo, whom [[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] names as the first of his teachers and introduces as the author of «Takmilat al-Majmu'», the completion of al-Nawawi's «al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab». {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:11:30}

## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
Al-Heweny relates that a friend at the Faculty of Engineering told him of Shaykh al-Muti'i's lesson near the faculty, in the Malaysian students' house next to the Faculty of Engineering on Abduh Pasha Square in Abbasiyya, and that he began attending his circles as a university student; he calls this the "first phase" of his studies. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep13 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep13 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:10:00} He says al-Muti'i taught four books: Sahih al-Bukhari, «Ihya' Ulum al-Din», «al-Ashbah wa-l-Naza'ir» and «al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab». {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:10:00} He mentions that al-Muti'i used to say that whoever read the completion beside al-Nawawi's text would find no difference, as if al-Nawawi had written it; that al-Subki had written a portion and no one had continued it until al-Muti'i finished it; and that he read «al-Majmu'» until al-Nawawi's style entered his blood. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:12:00} He relates that a dispute arose over the authorship of the completion and that al-Muti'i established it as his own after lawsuits. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:10:30}

He tells how al-Muti'i taught, following al-Nawawi, that someone who had abandoned the prayer for years must make it up; al-Heweny said that some scholars held it is not made up, and when asked who, he said: al-Albani; the shaykh's reply made the room laugh, and after that the shaykh ignored his raised hand. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:17:30} He relates that he asked him whether to go down to prostration on the hands or on the knees, which was then a mark among the young, and al-Muti'i said he had gone down on his knees as a young man and went down on his hands as an old one. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:20:00} He says al-Muti'i explained al-Bukhari with discussion of the chains, that he took a fair share of al-Bukhari and «al-Majmu'» from him, and that he did not complete the four books because al-Muti'i emigrated to Sudan after the events of Sadat's time. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep14 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep08 00:14:30}
