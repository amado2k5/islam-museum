## Overview
Abu Bakr Yahya ibn Hudhayl al-Kafif, scholar of the men of letters of al-Andalus. He was the teacher of al-Ramadi, from whom he acquired the craft of literature. al-Maqqari quotes one of his verses about standing at the house of its people {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.40}.
