# Mustafa Suleyman

Mustafa Suleyman (born August 1984) is a British artificial-intelligence entrepreneur, co-founder of DeepMind and Inflection AI and chief executive of Microsoft AI. The English article and Wikidata give his birth, at month precision, in August 1984 at London; the Arabic article gives the day as 1 August 1984, a precision no other read source carries. No Urdu edition of his article exists; this file's companion Urdu text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-suleyman-en} {cite:wikipedia-suleyman-ar} {cite:wikidata-suleyman}

## Early life and education

Suleyman was born in London, the son of a Syrian father who drove a taxi and an English mother who worked as a nurse. He was educated at Queen Elizabeth's School, Barnet, and enrolled at Oxford to study philosophy and theology, dropping out at nineteen. {cite:wikipedia-suleyman-en}

## Career

In 2001, as a teenager, Suleyman helped Mohammed Mamdani establish the Muslim Youth Helpline, a telephone counselling service; he later worked in human-rights policy. In 2010 he co-founded the artificial-intelligence company DeepMind, where he led applied AI and later its health division. After leaving DeepMind's orbit for a policy role at Google in 2019, he co-founded Inflection AI in March 2022 and launched its chatbot Pi in 2023. In March 2024 he became chief executive of Microsoft AI. He was appointed CBE in 2019, and his book The Coming Wave (2023), written with Michael Bhaskar, argues for the containment of transformative technologies. {cite:wikipedia-suleyman-en}
