## Summary
Abu Salih Mansur ibn Nuh ibn Nasr ibn Isma'il ibn Ahmad ibn Asad ibn Saman, one of the Samanid kings; Abu Bakr al-Razi composed for him in his youth the book of medicine known as al-Mansuri for him to work at; Ibn Juljul relates that al-Razi wrote him a book proving the art of alchemy — he was given a thousand dinars for it, then was unable to bring what he promised into being; he died in Shawwal 365. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
