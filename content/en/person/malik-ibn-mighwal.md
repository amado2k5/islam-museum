## Overview
Malik ibn Mighwal ibn Asim al-Bajali al-Kufi, Abu Abd Allah, was a hadith transmitter of Kufa whom al-Dhahabi calls the imam, the trustworthy, the orderly muhaddith, and counts among the masters of the scholars {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. The sign printed with his entry shows that his hadith is recorded in the six canonical books {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from al-Sha'bi, Abd Allah ibn Buraida, Nafi' the client of Ibn Umar, Ata ibn Abi Rabah, Talha ibn Musarrif, al-Hakam, Awn ibn Abi Juhayfa, Qays ibn Muslim, Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Aswad, Abu Ishaq, Muhammad ibn Suqa, Simak, Zubayd al-Yami and many others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. Those who narrated from him include Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i, who was also his teacher, Shu'ba, al-Thawri, Mis'ar, Isma'il ibn Zakariyya, Ibn Uyayna, Ibn al-Mubarak, Ibn Numayr, Waki', Abu Mu'awiya, Yahya ibn Sa'id, Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi and Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Firyabi {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}.

## What the critics said
Ahmad ibn Hanbal called him reliable and firm in hadith; Ibn Ma'in, Abu Hatim and others also called him reliable, and al-Ijli described him as a righteous man who excelled in virtue {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. Ahmad reports from Ibn Uyayna that when a man told Malik ibn Mighwal to fear God, he laid his cheek on the ground {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. Al-Khatib notes that his students Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i and al-Rabi' ibn Yahya al-Ashnani died some ninety-seven or ninety-eight years apart, and that his hadith comes to about a hundred reports {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}.

## His death
Abu Nu'aym and Abu Bakr ibn Abi Shayba say he died in 159 AH, while Muhammad ibn Sa'd gives 158 {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. The museum records both without choosing, as the heading of al-Dhahabi's entry itself gives 158 or 159 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3169}. Al-Dhahabi also gives, with his own chain, the report of A'isha about the gleam of perfume in the parting of the Prophet's hair while he was in ihram, and notes that al-Bukhari, Muslim and al-Nasa'i record it through Abd Allah ibn Numayr from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3170}.
