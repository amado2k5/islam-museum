## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan Ali ibn Isa al-Rummani; al-Sari al-Raffa was his neighbour in the Perfumers' Market and often passed by him as he sat at his door, sitting with him and talking until al-Sari adopted Mu'tazilism in his wake. He wrote a book on the inimitability of the Qur'an and other works. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 189}
