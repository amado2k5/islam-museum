## Overview
Abu al-Awar al-Sulami, one of the commanders of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] at Siffin. Al-Mas'udi says that he was entrusted with the bank of the Euphrates with forty thousand men, and was at its front, and he stood between the army of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and the water. Al-Mas'udi quotes an exchange between [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] and Mu'awiya about it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.667-668}
