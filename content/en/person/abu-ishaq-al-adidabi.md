## Summary
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim b. Isma'il al-Adidabi was a philologist and calligrapher of the Luwata Berbers. He lived in the second half of the 5th/11th century and died in Tripoli, where his tomb is still venerated.

## Encyclopaedia of Islam account
The Encyclopaedia of Islam (2nd ed.), signed H. H. Abdul-Wahab, describes him as Abu Ishaq Ibrahim b. Isma'il b. Ahmad al-Luwari, the author of various works on philology, especially a lexicographical work. Al-Tidjani possessed several of them in autograph copies, since al-Adidabi was famous for his calligraphy. He lived in the second half of the 5th/11th century in Tripoli, where he also died, and his tomb is still venerated there. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 226}
