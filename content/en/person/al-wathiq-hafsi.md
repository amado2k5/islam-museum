## Overview
Al-Wathiq Yahya ibn al-Mustansir al-Hafsi, the son of [[person/al-mustansir-hafsi|al-Mustansir]] and the holder of power in the [[polity/hafsid-dynasty|Hafsid state]]. He became known by the title "the Deposed". {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Beginning of his rule
He was given the allegiance on the night his father died in 675 AH. He opened his rule by lifting injustices, releasing the prisoners, giving generously, and repairing the mosques. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Domination by his vizier
His Andalusian vizier Ibn al-Habbar then took over the rule. Ibn Khaldun says that this vizier tortured the vizier Ibn Abi al-Husayn until he died, in pursuit of a store of his wealth. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His abdication
His uncle Abu Ishaq entered Bejaia and called the troops to him for the caliphate, and al-Wathiq realised that his kingdom had gone. He yielded the affair to his uncle on the first of Rabi al-Awwal 678 AH, and moved from the palaces of the Qasaba to the house of al-Aqwari. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
