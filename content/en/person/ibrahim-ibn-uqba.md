## Summary
Ibrahim b. 'Uqba b. Abi 'Ayyash was a Successor of [[place/medina|Medina]], and the first man [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] profiles in the "chapter alif" of [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik's]] teachers in [[work/al-tamhid-ibn-abd-al-barr|al-Tamhid]]. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 258}

## Clientage and brothers
He was a client of the family of al-Zubayr b. al-'Awwam, one of three Medinan brothers: Ibrahim, Muhammad and [[person/musa-ibn-uqba|Musa]], sons of 'Uqba b. Abi 'Ayyash. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 258} Yahya b. Ma'in said they were clients of Umm Khalid bint Khalid b. Sa'id b. al-'As, but no one followed him in this; Ibn 'Abd al-Barr holds that they were clients of the family of al-Zubayr, as Malik, others and al-Bukhari said. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 258}

## Teachers and students
He heard Umm Khalid bint Khalid b. Sa'id b. al-'As, one of the women who gave the oath of allegiance, which makes him a Successor; his brother Musa heard from her her report on the punishment of the grave. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 258-259} He also heard [[person/said-ibn-al-musayyab|Sa'id b. al-Musayyab]], [[person/urwa-ibn-al-zubayr|'Urwa b. al-Zubayr]], [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz]], 'Amir b. Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, Abu 'Abd Allah al-Qarraz and [[person/kurayb-mawla-ibn-abbas|Kurayb, client of Ibn 'Abbas]]. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259} Malik b. Anas, Ma'mar, [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|al-Thawri]], [[person/hammad-ibn-zayd|Hammad b. Zayd]], [[person/ibn-ishaq|Muhammad b. Ishaq]], [[person/sufyan-ibn-uyayna|Ibn 'Uyayna]], Muhammad b. Ja'far b. Abi Kathir and [[person/abd-al-aziz-al-darawardi|al-Darawardi]] transmitted from him. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259}

## His standing with the critics
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says he was trustworthy and an authority in what he transmitted; he was older than Musa, Muhammad older than him, Musa had the most hadith, and all of them were trustworthy. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259} He quotes Abu Dawud from Yahya b. Ma'in on the sons of 'Uqba: Musa had the most hadith, Muhammad was the eldest, and Muhammad and Ibrahim were more reliable than Musa. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259}

## His report in the Muwatta'
Malik has one report from him in the Muwatta', mursal in most recensions: from Kurayb, that the Prophet passed a woman in a litter, who lifted up a child with her and asked whether he had a pilgrimage, and he said, "Yes, and you have a reward." {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259} Ibn 'Abd al-Barr notes that Ibn Wahb, al-Shafi'i, Muhammad b. Khalid b. 'Athma, Abu Mus'ab and 'Abd Allah b. Yusuf transmitted it from Malik with a full chain — from Kurayb from Ibn 'Abbas. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260} He glosses the *mihaffa* as something like a howdah, or, it is said, one without a cover, and the *dab'* as the inner forearm. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260}
