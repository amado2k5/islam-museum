## Summary
Ali ibn Musa ibn Yazdad, from Qum and later Nishapur, the Hanafi imam of his time, who wrote refutations of the Shafi'is. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2066}

## Works
His 'Kitab al-dahaya' is on sacrifices; he died in 305 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2066}
