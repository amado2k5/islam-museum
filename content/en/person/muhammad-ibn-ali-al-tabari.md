## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Tabari is a narrator the book cites at p. 135 as "Muhammad ibn Ali al-Tabari told me"; he relates the consultation of the people at Hawth about fighting the group and a man's warning against fighting their cousins and his subsequent illness. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.135} From his nisba he seems to belong to [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabari emigrants to al-Hadi]]; the book adds nothing here to his lineage or home, and its index lists him at p. 135 only. See [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-the-array-at-al-damriyya-286]]. He is a Zaydi narrator; the museum attributes the report to him and neither confirms nor denies it.
