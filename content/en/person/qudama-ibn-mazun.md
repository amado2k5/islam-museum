## Overview
Qudama ibn Maz'un al-Jumahi, brother of [[person/uthman-ibn-mazun|Uthman ibn Maz'un]]. al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts him among the earliest Muslims, who became Muslim with his brother during the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret mission]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## In al-Rahiq al-Makhtum
After counting the first generation, the book names among the earliest Muslims Uthman ibn Maz'un and his brothers Qudama and Abd Allah, among the more than forty men of all the clans of Quraysh whom Ibn Hisham counts {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## What is not covered here
This chapter does not mention anything about his life after Islam. What the books of the Companions say about his migration, his battles and his offices needs a source that can be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Qudama became Muslim in the early days of Islam with his brothers, at a time when the mission was still secret.
