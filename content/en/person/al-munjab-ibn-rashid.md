## Overview
Al-Munjab ibn Rashid al-Dabbi. Al-Baladhuri relates that he asked to build a bath in Basra and was granted it. In it is a verse said by the dying man, meaning: "How many a noon-time traveller who was exhausted, how is the road to the bath of al-Munjab?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
