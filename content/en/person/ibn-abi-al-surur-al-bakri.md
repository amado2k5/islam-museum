## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad Abi al-Surur Zayn al-Abidin ibn Muhammad Abi al-Makarim al-Bakri al-Siddiqi, known as Ibn Abi al-Surur, is described by al-Zirikli as an Egyptian historian, exegete and researcher {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. The headship of the house of al-Bakri in Egypt passed to him; he was born and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. The entry gives 1005 and 1087 AH (1596 and 1676 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Works on biography and history
Among his books are "al-Nur al-mubin fi tawdih ma fi Ihya' ulum al-din", "al-Durra al-asma' fi tabaqat al-fuqaha'", "al-Rawda al-nadiyya fi tabaqat al-sufiyya", "Ayn al-yaqin fi tarikh al-mu'allifin" in several volumes, and "al-Durar fi al-akhbar wa al-siyar" in thirty parts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. The entry also lists, in manuscript, "al-Rawda al-zahiyya fi wulat Misr al-Qahira al-Mu'izziyya", "Uyun al-akhbar wa nuzhat al-absar", a history that begins with the creation and runs to the end of the Circassian state, and "Qatf al-azhar min al-khitat wa al-athar", an abridgment of al-Maqrizi's Khitat {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Other works
He wrote a large exegesis known as the Tafsir of Ibn Abi al-Surur, a book on the Nile, its source and what has been said of it, and verse and muwashshahat in a diwan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. "Fayd al-mannan bi dhikr dawlat Al Uthman" survives in manuscript in the Rabat library, and "al-Qawl al-muqtadab fima wafaqa lughat ahl Misr min lughat al-Arab" is printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
