## Summary
Badr al-Habashi, the great amir; commander of a hundred horse at Damascus, in the emirate some forty years, famed for valour, mind and counsel. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 770-771}

## Abolishing the levy
Ibn Shahba said: he was first to abolish what was yearly levied on the pilgrims for the bedouin — ten dirhams on every camel — paying it from his own money; he abolished the tax in 581 and it stayed abolished to Ibn al-'Imad's day. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 770-771}
