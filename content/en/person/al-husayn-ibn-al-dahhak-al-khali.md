## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Dahhak ibn Yasir al-Bahili, a poet of Basra by birth and upbringing, of Khurasani origin, titled al-Khali' al-Ashqar. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.164}

## His life
The encyclopaedia estimates his birth at about 773 CE, and his death at 251 AH (864 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.164}
