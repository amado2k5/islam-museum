## Overview
Umar ibn Hafs ibn Uthman ibn Qabisa ibn Abi Safra al-Atiki. [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] appointed him over Ifriqiya after the dismissal of Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.322}.

## Ifriqiya
According to al-Baladhuri, he entered Ifriqiya and campaigned from it as far as the farthest lands of the Berbers, and built al-Abbasiyya. al-Mansur was full of admiration for him. Then Abu Khatim the Ibadi fought him, and he was martyred with a group of his household {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325-326}. See [[event/martyrdom-of-umar-ibn-hafs|the death of Umar ibn Hafs]]. His year of death is derived and approximate, around 150 AH {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.326}.
