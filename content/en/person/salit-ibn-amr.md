## Summary
Of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; he emigrated to Abyssinia — Ibn Ishaq describes him as the Messenger of God's envoy to Hawdha ibn Ali al-Hanafi at al-Yamama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Amir in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", describing him as having been "the Messenger of God's envoy to Hawdha ibn Ali al-Hanafi at al-Yamama". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9} The book gives no details of his mission here; that is reviewed in the chapter on the Prophet's envoys to the kings (a later batch).

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 3561: "Salit ibn Amr, the Qurashi of the Amiri clan, son of the brother of Suhayl ibn Amr"; Ibn Ishaq named him among the envoys to the kings — he was sent to Hawdha ibn Ali, the chief of al-Yamama — and among the emigrants to Abyssinia, where he emigrated with his wife Umm Yaqza bint Alqama and she bore him there Salit ibn Salit; Ibn Ishaq also listed him among those martyred at al-Yamama. The entry concludes that the accounts are of one man: he was the envoy to Hawdha ibn Ali. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.604}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}
