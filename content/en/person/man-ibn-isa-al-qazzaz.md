## Summary
Ma'n ibn Isa al-Qazzaz was among the senior students of Malik ibn Anas; he took from him and transmitted his books and compilations, according to the Fihrist. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim records in the Fihrist, among Malik's companions, that Ma'n ibn Isa al-Qazzaz was one of Malik's leading students who took from him and transmitted his books and compilations. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277} He lists him with Abd Allah ibn Wahb, who transmitted Malik's books, sunan and Muwatta and was upright and trustworthy, with Dawud ibn Abi Hind and his son Sa'id who transmitted from Malik — Dawud being among the reliable — and other senior students of Malik. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277} In his account Ma'n thus stands among the chief transmitters of Malik's works and teaching in the generation of his students. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277}
