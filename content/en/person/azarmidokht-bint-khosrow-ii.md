## Overview
Azarmidokht daughter of Khosrow Parviz, one of the Sasanian kings of Persia. Al-Tabari gives her report under the heading "The reign of Azarmidokht daughter of Khosrow Parviz", and relates what was said of the years of her rule and her affairs. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.232}
