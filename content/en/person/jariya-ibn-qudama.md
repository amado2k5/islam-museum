## Overview
Jariya ibn Qudama al-Sa'di al-Tamimi, one of the commanders of Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.732-733}

## In pursuit of Busr
Ali sent him with two thousand men, together with Wahb ibn Mas'ud with two thousand, in pursuit of Busr ibn Abi Artat, who had plundered the Hijaz and Yemen in 40 AH. Busr fled, and Jariya followed him to Mecca, where he took the oath of allegiance from its people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.732-733}

## In Medina
He came to Medina while [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] was leading the people in prayer, and Abu Hurayra fled from him. Jariya said: "If I found Abu Sanwar, I would kill him." When news of Ali's death reached them, the people of Medina gave their oath to al-Hasan ibn Ali. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.732-733}

## In al-Hakim's Mustadrak
In the Book of Knowing the Companions, [[person/al-hakim-al-nisaburi|al-Hakim]] gives his lineage, Jariya ibn Qudama ibn Zuhayr ibn Husayn ibn Rabah ibn Sa'd ibn Yahya ibn Rabi'a ibn Ka'b; his kunyas, Abu al-Walid and Abu Yazid; and his house in Basra in the lane of al-Bahariyya. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 713}

He also gives, through [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]], Jariya's report that he asked the Prophet: "Tell me something that will benefit me, and keep it short so that I may remember it." The Prophet said, "Do not become angry", and repeated it to him several times. Al-Dhahabi passes over it without comment in the *Talkhis*. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 713}
