## Overview
Abd al-Salam ibn Ibrahim al-Laqani of Egypt was, according to al-Zirikli, the shaykh of the Malikis in his time at Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 119}. For him he refers to Khulasat al-Athar {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 119}.
