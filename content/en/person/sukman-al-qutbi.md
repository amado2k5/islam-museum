## Overview
Sukman al-Qutbi, founder of the state of Akhlat, one of the Shahs of Armenia, in the year 493 AH. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan says that his amirate was the strongest of the Seljuk principalities that arose in Armenia, although most of its people were Armenians {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.83}.

## The dynasty
His family lasted until the year 581 AH, when rule passed to his mamluk Bek Temur and then to the Ayyubids. See [[polity/shah-arman-akhlat|the Shahs of Armenia (Akhlat)]] {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.88-89}.
