## Summary
Abu'l-Walid Yunus b. 'Abdallah b. Muhammad b. Mughith, known as Ibn al-Saffar, qadi al-jama'a of Cordoba; he studied fiqh under Abu Bakr Ibn Darb and held the judgeship with the preaching and the vizierate, attaining headship in religion and world — a righteous, just jurist, authoritative signpost in lugha, Arabic and poetry, eloquent and articulate, rich in merits, with works on asceticism and more; he died in Rajab aged seventy-one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 244}
