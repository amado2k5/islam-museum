## Summary
Isma'il ibn Yahya ibn Isma'il ibn 'Umar ibn Muslim al-Taymi, companion of al-Shafi'i; a skilled faqih, reliable in hadith, of worship and merit — among the best of God's creation, constant at the ribat; he died on Wednesday with four nights left of Rabi' I 264, and al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman prayed over him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 136}
