## Overview
Ziyad ibn Hudayr al-Asadi, an agent of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] over one-half of the tithe. al-Baladhuri reports, from Ibrahim ibn al-Muhajir from him, that Umar sent him to the Christian [[group/banu-taghlib|Banu Taghlib]] to take from their property one-half of the tithe, and forbade him to tithe a Muslim or a protected person who paid the land tax {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He appears in [[event/treaty-with-taghlib|the treaty with Taghlib]] in this role {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
