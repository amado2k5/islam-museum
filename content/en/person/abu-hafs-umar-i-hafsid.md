## Overview
Umar ibn Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Hafsi al-Hintati, Abu Hafs, al-Mustansir the Second, was the ruler of Tunis of the Hafsid dynasty. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 642 to 694 AH, 1244 to 1295 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 69}.

## Career
He was with his brother Ibrahim ibn Yahya when the pretender Ibn Abi Umara overcame Ifriqiya, and escaped after the killing of Ibrahim and his sons; he went to Qal'at Sinan near Tunis, the Arabs heard of him and came to swear allegiance in 683 AH, and with them he fought the usurper Ibn Abi Umara and retook Tunis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 69}. The usurper was killed the same year and the country rallied to him; al-Zirikli notes that he was the second Hafsid to bear the title al-Mustansir bi-llah, and calls him sensible and brave {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 69}. He died in Tunis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 69}.
