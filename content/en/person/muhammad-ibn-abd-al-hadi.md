## Summary
Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Hadi, the last to transmit by ijaza from Shahda; he heard from Muhammad ibn Hamza ibn Abi'l-Saqr, 'Abd al-Razzaq al-Najjar, Yahya al-Thaqafi and others — a righteous, chaste old man; one day he was reciting God's Book at the Sawiya mosque in Nablus's district when he was martyred by the Tatars in Jumada I, past ninety — so says al-Dhahabi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 303}
