## Overview
Abu Imran Sharaf al-Din Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Uthman al-Khalili, an astronomer who grew up in Damascus and wrote a treatise on astronomy. He died in 1402 CE, according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
