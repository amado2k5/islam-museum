# Shazia Ahmad

## Summary
Shazia Ahmad is an Islamic teacher based in Mississauga, Canada. She lived in Damascus, Syria for two years, where she studied aqidah, fiqh, tajweed, tafsir and Arabic. {cite:seekersguidance-shazia-ahmad}

## Study and teaching
After Damascus she attended the University of Texas at Austin, where she completed her Master's in Arabic, and then moved to Amman, Jordan, where she studied fiqh, Arabic and other sciences. She later moved back to Mississauga, where she lives with her family, and she answers religious questions for SeekersGuidance. {cite:seekersguidance-shazia-ahmad}
