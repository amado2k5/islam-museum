## Overview
Zinnira: a slave-woman who embraced Islam in Mecca, and whom the polytheists tortured, and then Abu Bakr bought and freed her {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91}.

## What is not covered here
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum does not, at this point, give her lineage or her later reports. They will be completed from other sources when she is mentioned.

## For young visitors
Zinnira was among the early Muslims in Mecca, and the polytheists tortured her when she embraced Islam.
