## Overview
Hababa, a singing slave-girl. Umm Sa'id al-Uthmaniyya, grandmother of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, bought her by a trick, because he had long held some feeling towards her. [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid]] gave Salama the priest to Umm Sa'id. Hababa then sent to al-Ahwas and Ma'bad, and they sang to him a verse beginning "Life is nothing but what delights and what one desires." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 185-186}
