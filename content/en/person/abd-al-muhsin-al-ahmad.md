## Summary

Abd al-Muhsin ibn Muhammad Al-Ahmad is a Saudi preacher, styled on his official website "the Islamic preacher Sheikh Dr", known for exhortatory lectures and short, affecting sermon-stories that his website publishes in audio and video. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad} He has no article in Arabic or English Wikipedia and no Wikidata item, and this pass found no birth date for him in any trusted source, so none is recorded. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad}

## Career

His official website, abdelmohsen.com, publishes his lectures in series — among them "Reflections on Surat Yusuf", "A Night in the Prophet's House" and "The Ten Causes of God's Love" — along with short clips under "Stories and Exhortations"; the oldest items in its archive date to 2009 by the site's own posting dates. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad} His television appearances, as his site records them, include the Nasamat programme on the Al-Majd channel and studio interviews on Gaza. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad}

## Works

His published output is the lecture and the sermon-story rather than the printed book: his site collects his video and audio lectures, his sermons and his short story-exhortation clips, and adds to them as they appear. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad} His lectures also circulate on other uploaders' YouTube channels; no channel of his own could be verified in this pass, so none is registered for him. {cite:website-abdelmohsen-al-ahmad}
