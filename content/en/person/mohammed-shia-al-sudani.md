# Mohammed Shia' Al Sudani

Mohammed Shia' Sabbar al-Sudani (born 4 March 1970) is an Iraqi politician who served as prime minister of Iraq from 27 October 2022 to 14 May 2026, in succession to Mustafa Al-Kadhimi. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-ar}

## Early life
Al-Sudani was born in Baghdad on 4 March 1970 to a middle-class family hailing from Maysan province in the south; his father worked at the Agricultural Cooperative Bank. When he was about ten, his father and five other relatives were executed for membership of the then-banned Islamic Dawa Party. He graduated from the University of Baghdad with a bachelor's degree in agricultural science and a master's in project management, and from 1997 worked in the Maysan agriculture administration, rising to head its agricultural production department and supervising an FAO research programme. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-en}

## Political career
After the 2003 invasion al-Sudani joined the Islamic Dawa Party, was appointed mayor of Amarah in 2004, sat on the Maysan provincial council, and became governor of Maysan in 2009. He was Minister of Human Rights from 2010 to 2014 and Minister of Labour and Social Affairs from 2014 to 2018, holding several other portfolios in an acting capacity, including industry, trade, finance and agriculture. He left the Dawa Party in 2019 and founded the Furatayn Movement. The element 'Shia'' in his name is his father's given name, Shayya', not a sect label. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-ar}

## Premiership
Designated by the Coordination Framework and appointed by President Abdul Latif Rashid, al-Sudani took office on 27 October 2022. His government pursued development and services programmes and confronted the fallout of the 'theft of the century', the disappearance of some $2.5 billion of tax deposits under his predecessor. In 2025 The Muslim 500 listed him among the most influential Muslim politicians. His term ended on 14 May 2026, when Ali al-Zaidi succeeded him. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-shia-al-sudani-en}
