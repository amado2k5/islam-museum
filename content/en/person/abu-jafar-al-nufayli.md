## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Nufayl, Abu Ja'far al-Nufayli, was in al-Zirikli's words one of the great memorisers of hadith and among the most reliable. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 234 AH, 848 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 117}.

## Career
He was from the people of Harran {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 117}.

## Works
He wrote Kitab al-Maghazi, in manuscript; its third part, sixteen leaves, is in the Zahiriyya, in the hand of Tahir ibn Barakat al-Khushu'i, dated 454 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 117}.
