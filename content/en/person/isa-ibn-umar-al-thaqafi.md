## Overview
Isa ibn Umar al-Thaqafi al-Basri, one of the early grammarians of Basra. He is not Isa ibn Umar al-Hamdani al-Kufi. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 110-111}

## Teachers and pupils
He took from Abdallah ibn Abi Ishaq al-Hadrami, and al-Khalil ibn Ahmad took from him. He was blind, and Ibn al-Nadim counts him among the readers of Basra. He died in 149 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 110-111}

## His books
He wrote *al-Jami'* (The Compendium) and *al-Mukammal* (The Completed). Ibn al-Nadim says that both were lost long ago, and that no one who had seen them has reached anyone. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 110-111}
