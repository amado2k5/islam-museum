## Overview
Aws ibn Hudhayfa al-Thaqafi, a Companion who came to the Prophet with the delegation of Thaqif from Banu Malik. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14953} He narrated from the Prophet and from Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14953} Those who narrated from him include his son Amr, his grandson Uthman ibn Abd Allah, al-Nu'man ibn Salim and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14953}
