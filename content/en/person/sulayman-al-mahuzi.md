## Overview
Sulayman ibn Abd Allah ibn Ali ibn Ammar al-Bahrani al-Mahuzi was an Imami jurist, that is, of the Twelver Shi'a, and one of the preachers and poets. He came from al-Mahuz, a village of Bahrain, and excelled in hadith and history. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 1075 to 1121 AH, 1665 to 1709 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 120}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his writings Azhar al-Riyad, on literature, in four volumes on the pattern of al-Kashkul of al-Amili; its fourth volume survives in manuscript in his own hand at the University of Tehran, and its printing has begun. Others are al-Bulgha, in manuscript, on the men of hadith among the Shi'a; Ta'rikh Ulama al-Bahrayn, in manuscript; al-Fawa'id al-Najafiyya; al-Shifa', on theoretical philosophy; and many treatises on various subjects {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 120}.
