## Overview
Mujja'a ibn Sir al-Tamimi, whom al-Hajjaj appointed over the frontier of Makran after the Ulafiyyin. Al-Baladhuri says that he raided, took booty, and conquered groups of Qandabil; then Muhammad ibn al-Qasim completed its conquest. He died a year later in Makran. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
