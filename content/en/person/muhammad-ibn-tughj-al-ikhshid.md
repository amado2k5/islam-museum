## Overview
Ibn Tughj, ruler of Egypt, titled al-Ikhshid. Al-Suyuti relates that he came to al-Muttaqi at Raqqa, when news had reached him of al-Muttaqi's peace with Tuzun, and said to him: "For God's sake, for your own sake; come with me to Egypt, for it is yours." Al-Muttaqi did not accept, and al-Ikhshid returned to his lands. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.610}

He died in 334 AH; he is Muhammad ibn Tughj al-Farghani. Al-Ikhshid means "king of kings", a title for anyone who ruled Farghana. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
