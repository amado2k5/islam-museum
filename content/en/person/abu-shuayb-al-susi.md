## Overview

Abu Shu'ayb Salih ibn Ziyad ibn 'Abd Allah ibn Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Jarud al-Susi al-Raqqi is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/abu-amr-ibn-al-ala|Abu 'Amr ibn al-'Ala']], beside [[person/abu-umar-al-duri|al-Duri]]. He died at the start of 261 AH, close to ninety years old. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 144}

## Chain and lines of transmission

Al-Susi read on Abu Muhammad [[person/yahya-ibn-al-mubarak-al-yazidi|Yahya ibn al-Mubarak al-Yazidi]] from Abu 'Amr; Ibn al-Jazari calls him an exact, careful and reliable reader, one of the most distinguished and senior companions of al-Yazidi. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 144} Two great lines of transmission descend from him in the books of the readings, that of Ibn Jarir and that of Ibn Jumhur; from the latter come the paths of al-Shadha'i and of al-Shannabudhi, which Ibn al-Jazari lays out in detail. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 142}
