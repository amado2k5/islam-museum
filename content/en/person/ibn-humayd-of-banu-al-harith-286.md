## Overview
The narrator names Ibn Humayd among those who pledged with Ibn Abbad, Ibn Bustam and Ibn al-Dahhak that each would make trouble for al-Hadi in his own land. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155} At Najran he appears among Banu al-Harith, having sworn to them that he would go for al-Hadi if he saw him; when al-Hadi saw him at Sawhan he called out his oaths, and he fled with his men into an enclosure at the fortress gate and told them to pull down the wall. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.162} The narrative says he ordered the death of a man whom al-Hadi struck to be kept quiet so that people would not see the blow. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} The book gives no fuller name. Its index lists "Ibn Humayd" at pp. 146, 149, 162, 163, 166 and 167 among others, and [[person/al-tahir-ibn-al-tahir|al-Tahir ibn al-Tahir]] is described as his nephew; the museum does not assert that every mention is one man.
