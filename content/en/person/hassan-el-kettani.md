# Hassan El Kettani

**Mohamed Hassan Echerif El Kettani** is a Moroccan Muslim scholar and jurist. He was born at Salé on 16 August 1972 into the Kettani family, long rooted in Morocco's religious scholarship, and spent parts of his childhood in Boston, Dhahran and the Hijaz, where his father, the engineer and historian Ali Kettani, worked. He studied with Moroccan scholars including Muhammad Abu Khubza, Abdullah al-Ghumari and Abd al-Aziz al-Ghumari, and with scholars outside Morocco, among them Muhammad ibn al-Uthaymin and Abdul Qader Arnaoot; he took a master's degree in fiqh and its principles at Al al-Bayt University in Jordan and a doctorate in usul al-fiqh {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-en} {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-ar}.

He taught and preached in Salé and Rabat. On 18 February 2003 he was arrested, and in September 2003 he was sentenced to twenty years in prison in connection with the Casablanca bombings, accused of being an ideological leader of the Salafia Jihadia current. He denied the charges and maintained that his conviction was politically motivated; human rights groups criticised the trial. In prison he joined hunger strikes in 2005 and 2010, continuing the 2010 strike longer than the other detainees {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-en}.

On 5 February 2012 King Mohammed VI pardoned Kettani together with other Islamist prisoners. Since his release he has denounced extremism and violence and distanced himself from the Moroccan branch of Ansar al-Sharia; in May 2012 he was deported from Tunisia on arrival, together with another Islamist, despite having been invited by Tunisian Islamists {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-en} {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-fr}.

The Arabic article lists many works by him, among them a comparative study of the fiqh of the hadith master Ahmad ibn al-Siddiq al-Ghumari (the subject of his master's thesis), a description of Ibn Hazm's al-Muhalla, works on usury, on the excuse of ignorance, and on the Najdi da'wa, together with long recorded commentaries on works of fiqh and creed {cite:wikipedia-hassan-el-kettani-ar}.
