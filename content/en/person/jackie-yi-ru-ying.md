## Summary
Jackie Yi-Ru Ying is a chemical engineer counted among the pioneers of nanotechnology applications in medicine. She led Singapore’s Institute of Bioengineering and Nanotechnology from its founding and made it a world-class research centre. {cite:wikipedia-jackie-yi-ru-ying}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1966, she grew up between Singapore and the United States, where she studied chemical engineering to the doctorate and taught at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology before moving to lead research in Singapore. {cite:wikipedia-jackie-yi-ru-ying}

## Career and works
In 2003 she founded the Institute of Bioengineering and Nanotechnology (IBN) under Singapore’s Agency for Science, Technology and Research and remained its executive director for many years. Her inventions include SmartCells glucose-responsive insulin release, and research on nanoparticles for diagnostics and therapy. {cite:wikipedia-jackie-yi-ru-ying}

## Legacy
She has won international prizes in engineering and materials science and is counted among Singapore’s most prominent contemporary scientific figures; her laboratory is cited in the nano-bioengineering literature. {cite:wikipedia-jackie-yi-ru-ying}
