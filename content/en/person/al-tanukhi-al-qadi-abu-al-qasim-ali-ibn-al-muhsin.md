## Overview
The judge Abu al-Qasim Ali ibn al-Muhsin al-Tanukhi, a jurist. He was a companion of [[person/abu-l-ala-al-maarri|Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri]] and took much from him. He was born in 365 AH (975 CE) and died in 474 AH (1081 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.668}

The notables in this source are described as Sunni. His works and teachers are not in the museum's data and need an additional source.
