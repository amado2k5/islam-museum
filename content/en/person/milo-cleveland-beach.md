## Summary
Milo Cleveland Beach is the author of [[work/the-grand-mogul-beach|The Grand Mogul: Imperial Painting in India 1600–1660]], the catalogue of the exhibition organised by the Sterling and Francine Clark Art Institute, Williamstown, in 1978, with contributions by Stuart Cary Welch and Glenn D. Lowry. {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 3} {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 4}

## Approach
His preface records a grant to examine paintings in London and Tehran and help in reading inscriptions, and acknowledges Stuart Cary Welch's information and visual judgement. {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 9} He sees the exploration of naturalism between about 1600 and 1660 as the central achievement of Mughal painting, and closes the book with "a personal view" of it. {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 5} {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 18}
