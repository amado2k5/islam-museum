## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Yahya ibn Abi'l-Qasim 'Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ma'mur ibn Ja'far, styled Za'im al-Din, took over supervision of the arsenal in Jumada II 542 until 567, when he deputized in the vizierate after Abu'l-Faraj ibn al-Muzaffar's dismissal — and remained at it until he died. He was thanked and praiseworthy in conduct among the people of learning. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
