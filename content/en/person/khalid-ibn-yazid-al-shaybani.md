## Overview
Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mazyad ibn Za'ida, Abu Yazid al-Shaybani, was one of the generous amir-governors of the Abbasid age, and a patron praised by Abu Tammam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 293}. Al-Ma'mun appointed him over Egypt in 206 AH; he entered it, but Ubayd Allah ibn al-Sari fought him and he did not settle there. He was then given Mosul and later all of the lands of Rabi'a (Diyar Rabi'a), where he stayed into the days of al-Wathiq {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 293}. When Armenia rebelled, al-Wathiq sent him; he equipped a great army and marched toward it, but fell ill on the way and died before reaching it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 293}. In peace he was called Abu Yazid and in war Abu al-Zubayr {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 293}. Al-A'lam gives his death as 230 AH, that is 845 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 293}.
