## Overview
Maslama ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, one of the sons of the caliph [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]]. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.470}

## The pilgrimage
The book says that he led the people in the pilgrimage in 119 AH, since Hisham had assigned raiding and the pilgrimage to his sons. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.470}
