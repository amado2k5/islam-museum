## Overview
Ahmad ibn Hamallah al-Tishitti, a Mauritanian Sufi scholar, known by his nisba to his homeland of Tishit. His lineage goes back to the Sharif Ahmad. He was born in 1695 CE (1107 AH) and died in 1755 CE (1169 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are recorded as reported dates and need an additional source. The rest of his life needs an additional source.
