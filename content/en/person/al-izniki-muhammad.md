## Overview
Mulla Muhammad b. Qutb al-Din al-Izniki the Hanafi, imam, scholar, doer; he read the revealed and rational sciences under Mulla al-Fanari, mastered them and outstripped his peers, then took the sufi path — joining shari'a, tariqa and haqiqa. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 343}

## His works
He wrote a commentary on Miftah al-ghayb of Shaykh Sadr al-Din al-Qunawi — exceedingly fine — and also commented al-Qunawi's Fusus; he died in 886. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 343}
