## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sirin, one of the Successors. Al-Baladhuri says that he is the son of [[person/sirin-father-of-ibn-sirin|Sirin]], who was taken captive in the church of Ayn al-Tamr with his brothers Yahya, Anas and Ma'bad, and that he was the eldest of them. They were clients of [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]] al-Ansari. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Mas'udi says that he died a hundred nights after [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], in 110 AH, aged eighty-one. The children of Sirin were five brothers, all of whom transmitted the Sunan. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.192}

Al-Shirazi says that he died in 110 AH at the age of seventy-seven. The disagreement about his age is recorded here and not decided. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}

Al-Salabi says that al-Hasan al-Basri preferred him over others. He relates that Amr ibn Dinar said he had not seen the like of Tawus, and Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani replied that if Ibn Sirin had seen him he would not have said so. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.282-283}
