## Overview
A king of the Sasanian Persians. al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The Reign of Yazdegerd son of Bahram Gur" {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.81}.

al-Tabari reports at this point what was said about the years of his reign and his affairs. The details of his rule have not yet been read in the source, and they need an additional source.
