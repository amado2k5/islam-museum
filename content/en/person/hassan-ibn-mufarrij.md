## Overview
Al-Hasan b. al-Mufarrij b. al-Jarrah al-Ta'i, amir of the Banu Jarrah of Tayy in Palestine in al-Qadir bi'llah's days; Abu'l-Qasim b. al-Maghribi attended him to turn him against al-'Aziz lord of Egypt — then went on to Mecca and tempted its amir Abu'l-Futuh al-Hasan b. Ja'far with kingship {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.166}.
