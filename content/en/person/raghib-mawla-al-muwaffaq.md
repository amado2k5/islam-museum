## Overview
Raghib, servant and client of Abu Ahmad al-Muwaffaq; in 285 he raided by sea and God granted him many Byzantine vessels with all they carried. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 295}

## The raid
Three thousand of them were beheaded, the ships were burned, and many fortresses were taken. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 295}
