## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ali ibn Nasr al-Baghdadi, known as Ibn Nasr, a Maliki jurist, theologian, transmitter of hadith and poet from Baghdad. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his birth as 362 AH and his death as 421 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The data goes no further than this; a second source is needed for both dates and for the details of his biography.
