## Overview
Al-Fuja'a, whose name was Bujayr ibn Iyas ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His story in al-Baladhuri
He came to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and asked him to carry and strengthen him so he could fight the apostates. Abu Bakr gave him a mount and weapons. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He then went out intercepting people, killed Muslims and apostates, and gathered a force. Abu Bakr wrote to Tarifa ibn Hajiza ordering him to fight him, and Tarifa captured him and sent him to Abu Bakr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu Bakr ordered him burned in the area of the musalla. In another report, Abu Bakr wrote to Ma'n, and Tarifa sent his brother to capture him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri says that Abu Bakr regretted this, and said he wished he had killed him without burning him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His story is connected with [[event/ridda-wars|the Ridda wars]]. The date in the museum's data, about 11 AH, is a derived conversion.
