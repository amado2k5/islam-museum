## Overview
The hafiz of Yalda, a village of Damascus (568–655 AH); he took up hadith late, traveled, heard Ibn Kulayb, Ibn Bush and their tier and wrote much; he related that the Prophet told him in a dream "you are a good man." He died in his village, whose khatib he was, on the eighth of Rabi' I {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 277}.
