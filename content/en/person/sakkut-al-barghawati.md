## Overview
Sakkut al-Barghawati, the chamberlain (hajib). He had been a slave of Shaykh Haddad, a client of the Banu Hammud, whom Haddad bought from the captives of the Barghawata. He then passed to Ali ibn Hammud, until Ali became independent in their affairs. He made Tangier and Ceuta the seat of his rule, and the Ghumara tribes obeyed him {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## The end of his rule
His time extended into the period of the Almoravids. Yusuf ibn Tashfin sent troops under the command of Salih ibn Imran, and they met near Tangier in the year 471 AH, and Sakkut's forces were routed. Later al-Mu'izz ibn Yusuf stormed Ceuta in the year 476 AH, and Sakkut was killed. According to Ibn Khaldun, this ended the state of the Banu Hammud {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
