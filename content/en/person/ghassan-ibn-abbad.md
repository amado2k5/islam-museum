## Overview
Ghassan ibn Abbad, from the people of the Sawad of Kufa. Al-Ma'mun sent him to Bishr ibn Dawud, who brought him out under safe conduct and brought him to the City of Peace. He left Musa ibn Yahya al-Barmaki in charge of the frontier of Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
