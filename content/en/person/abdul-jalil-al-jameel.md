## Overview
Abd al-Jalil ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Razzaq, of the family of Al Jamil, was a learned man and one of the notables of Baghdad, where he was born and died. Al-Zirikli lists him in al-A'lam under the heading Ibn Jamil and gives his years as 1287 to 1376 AH, 1870 to 1957 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 275}.

## Life
He worked in teaching, and in 1328 AH was appointed mufti in al-Kazimiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 275}. When the British occupied Baghdad in 1335 AH they arrested him and sent him to India; he was later brought back to Baghdad and returned to teaching {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 275}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Ujala on grammar; Tanwir al-Adhhan on logic, printed; al-Muhadarat on the principles of jurisprudence and exegesis; and Zubdat al-Afkar, a commentary on Mukhtasar al-Manar in the principles {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 275}.
