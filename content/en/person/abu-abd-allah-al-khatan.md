## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Ibrahim, known as al-Khatan, that is the son-in-law of Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili. He was a virtuous jurist and wrote a commentary on "al-Talkhis" by Ibn al-Qass. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 63}

The editor's note, quoting al-Subki and Ibn Khallikan, says that he died in 386 AH; this date needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 63}
