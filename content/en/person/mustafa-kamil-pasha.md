## Overview
Mustafa Kamil Pasha ibn Ali Muhammad (1874–1908 CE), an Egyptian journalist and orator. Al-Zirikli counts him among the outstanding figures of Egypt in his age and one of the founders of its national movement. He was born and died in Cairo. {cite:al-aalam}

## His life
His father was an engineer, who took care of his education and upbringing, and he obtained a law degree before the age of twenty. {cite:al-aalam}

Al-Zirikli describes him as eloquent and enchanting in speech. He devoted himself to resisting the English occupation through his speeches, articles and books, and he went to Paris, where he spread his political call in its newspapers and societies. {cite:al-aalam}

He then returned to Egypt and founded the daily newspaper *al-Liwa'* in 1899 CE. He moved between Egypt, France and England in pursuit of his country's independence. {cite:al-aalam}
