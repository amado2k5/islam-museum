## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn Abi Tahir al-Marwazi, a secretary whom al-Suyuti describes as one of the masters of poetry. He is the author of "Tarikh al-Khulafa", and his death is recorded as 280 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

The page number and the rest of his life are not set out here and need an additional source.
