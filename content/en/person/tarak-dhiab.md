# Tarak Dhiab

## Summary

Tarak Dhiab is a Tunisian former footballer and politician, born in Tunis in 1954 — the English and Arabic articles give 15 January, the French article and Wikidata 15 July, and the date is recorded as debated. A playmaker for Espérance de Tunis, he was named African Footballer of the Year in 1977 and later served as Tunisia's Minister of Youth and Sports. {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-en} {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-ar} {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-fr} {cite:wikidata-tarak-dhiab}

## Career

Dhiab learned football at the school founded in Tunis by Abdel Rahman Ben Ezzedine in 1966, and the Arabic article records his byname, "Emperor of football". He played for Espérance de Tunis from 1972 and for Saudi Arabia's Al-Ahli, and in 1977 he was chosen African Footballer of the Year. At the 1978 World Cup in Argentina he was part of the Tunisia team that beat Mexico to become the first African side to win a World Cup match; he also played at the 1988 Olympics. The Tunisian federation credits him with 107 caps, a figure FIFA has not ratified, and he was chosen as the Tunisian footballer of the twentieth century. {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-en} {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-ar}

After the 2011 revolution he entered government as an independent, serving as Minister of Youth and Sports under prime ministers Hamadi Jebali and Ali Larayedh from 24 December 2011 to 29 January 2014, and later worked as a television pundit. {cite:wikipedia-tarak-dhiab-en}
