## Overview
Mansur ibn Jumhur al-Kalbi, who killed Mughallis al-Abdi, agent of Abu Muslim over Sind, and defeated his army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His end
Musa ibn Ka'b al-Tamimi then met him and defeated him, and killed his brother Manzur. Mansur fled until he reached the sands and died of thirst. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
