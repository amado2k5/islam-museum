# Bayramzade Zekeriya Efendi

Bayramzade Zekeriya Efendi (1514–1593) was an Ottoman scholar and jurist who climbed the whole ladder of the learned hierarchy — medrese, kadiship and kazaskerlik — to the Shaykh al-Islamate, the empire's chief muftiate, in the last year of his life.

## Life and career

He was born at Ankara in 920/1514, the son of Bayram Efendi. He studied under Arapzade Abdülbaki Efendi and Malul Emir Efendi, spent time in Cairo in 950/1543, and then taught in the medreses of Bursa. In 973/1565 he won the post at Istanbul's Atik Ali Paşa medrese by examination, and in 977/1570 reached the Sahn-ı Seman, the summit of Ottoman teaching. The judicial career followed: kadi of Aleppo in 981/1573, of Bursa in 985/1577 and of Istanbul in 988/1581; kazasker of Anatolia in 989/1582; retirement in 991/1583 and the hajj in 994/1586; then kazasker of Rumelia in 997/1589, dismissal in 998/1590 and reappointment in 999/1591.

On 27–28 Rajab 1000 / 9–10 May 1592, aged seventy-eight, he succeeded Bostanzade Mehmed Efendi as Shaykh al-Islam. Fourteen months later, on 11 Shawwal 1001 / 11 July 1593, while waiting at the palace to be received by the sultan, he recounted a dream in which he had seen the Prophet — and died suddenly on the spot. The funeral prayer was held at Fatih Mosque, and he was buried beside the complex he had built near the Sultan Selim Mosque — a medrese, a dar al-hadith and a dar al-qurra completed in Muharram 1001 / October 1592; his son Yahya arranged the endowment deed in 1594. His elder son was Zekeriyazade Yahya Efendi, who would hold the Shaykh al-Islamate three times; the younger was Şeyhi Lutfullah.

## Works

His surviving scholarly work is in the gloss form of his milieu: a Ḥāshiya on Ṣadr al-sharīʿa and a Ḥāshiya on the Sharḥ al-Miftāḥ, together with a Divan of poetry.

## Faith

No text read for this record names a madhhab for Zekeriya Efendi personally, and his faith is recorded unknown. He headed the Shaykh al-Islamate, the chief mufti's office of a Hanafi establishment, and spent his career inside that institution — but office and milieu are not attestation, any more than they were for his son Yahya, whose record is unknown on the same ground.
