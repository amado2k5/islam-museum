# Muhammad Taqi Amini

**Muhammad Taqi Amini** (5 May 1926 – 21 January 1991) was an Indian Sunni Islamic scholar and Urdu author, known for his studies of Islamic jurisprudence and for his deanship of the Faculty of Theology at Aligarh Muslim University.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-en}{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-ar}

## Early life and formation

Amini was born at Subeha in Barabanki district, Uttar Pradesh, and studied at Jami'ul Ulum in Kanpur and at Madrasa Aminia in Delhi, where he completed the dars-e-nizami curriculum under Kifayatullah Dihlawi.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-en}{cite:wikidata-muhammad-taqi-amini}

## Teaching career

Amini taught at Nadwatul Ulama in Lucknow, served as principal of Darul Uloom Muiniya in Ajmer, and became professor and then dean of the Faculty of Theology at Aligarh Muslim University, retiring in 1986. He died at Aligarh on 21 January 1991.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-en}

## Works

Amini wrote some forty books in Urdu, among them a study of the jurist Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani and a study of Shah Waliullah Dehlavi.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-en}

## Affiliation

The English and Urdu articles call Amini a Sunni scholar in their text; the Hanafi school and the Maturidi creed appear only in the religious-biography infobox module shared by both editions, and the editions' categories file him among the Deobandis without any text naming the movement as his school.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-en}{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-ur}{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-amini-ar}
