## Summary
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Khushani, the worshipper, for whom worship was his trade and weeping his delight. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]] (printed number 689, pages 406 to 408). The notice does not say where he lived or when he died. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/406}

## His eloquence in discipline
Abu Nu'aym describes his speech as eloquent in disciplining ascetics and worshippers, and says that many forerunners came out of his teaching. Among his students and their circle he names Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ahmad ibn al-Marzuban al-Aswari, Salim ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Marzuban (Abu Bakr, the preacher), Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Salih, and Abu Uthman ibn Abi Hurayra. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/406}

## His sayings
He said that the life of the truthful lies in watchfulness, that the spirit of their life is the following of the commands and states of the prophets, and the life of their spirits is in obedience and in tasting the correction of the path of the leaders. He also said that whoever keeps to service inherits the stations of nearness, and that these stations bring the sweetness of intimacy. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/406}

## A report on fear and hope
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Wa'iz relates from him, through Ja'far ibn Umayya, Muhammad ibn Ayyub al-Razi, al-Asmai, and Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash, from Asim ibn Abi al-Najud, a saying that a believer must have two things, fear and hope. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/406}

## A report on the diviner
Abu Muslim Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazzal relates from him, through Husayn ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan and Abu Bakr ibn Khallad, a report from Safiyya, one of the wives of the Prophet, about one who goes to a diviner to ask about something, whose prayer is not accepted for forty nights. The wording is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/406}

## The ascetics of Syria
On page 407 Abu Nu'aym lists the ascetics of Syria, among them Amir ibn Najiya, al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn Mazid, Zayd ibn Bandar, Yasar ibn Mushir and Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari. He notes that a group of them were trained by Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Banna, and calls al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Sa'id al-Sunbulani one of the substitutes. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/407}

## What Ibn Hayyan reports
Abu Nu'aym says that he heard Abu Muhammad ibn Hayyan relate stories of al-Khushani, that he visited him with his father on Fridays, and that he reported from several men, among them Sulayman ibn Shabib, Ubayd Allah ibn Yazid, and Abu Mas'ud, from whom Abu Muhammad did not write. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/408}

## The close of the notice
The notice ends with the names of those trained by Ali ibn Sahl and by Abu Abd Allah al-Salihani, then the leading figures of established standing, then Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Mashadhah, who closes the chain of inquiry. It ends with a dictation date, the last Friday of Dhu al-Hijja in the year 422 AH; the name of the speaker is not clearly read in the scan. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/408}
