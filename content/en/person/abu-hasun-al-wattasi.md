## Summary
Last of the Wattasid kings, whom al-Salabi styles 'the Ottoman pasha Bu Hasun' {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 238-244}.

## Ottoman support
Having lost hope of Spanish and Portuguese backing, he was joined by Ottoman forces under [[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]] against the Sa'dis; after their defeat near Badis he entered Fez on 3 Safar 964/8 January 1554 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 238}.

## Deposed and restored
Salih Reis tried replacing him with the Idrisi sharif Mawlay Bu Bakr at the murabitun's suggestion, but the people's rising forced Bu Hasun's restoration on terms: the khutba in the Ottoman sultan's name and a garrison at Balata {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239}.
