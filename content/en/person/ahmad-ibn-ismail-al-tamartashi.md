## Ahmad ibn Ismail al-Tamartashi

Ahmad ibn Ismail ibn Muhammad, Zahir al-Din Abu Muhammad, or Abu al-Abbas, al-Tamartashi, a Hanafi of Khwarazm, named after the village of Tamartash in Khwarazm, and mufti of Khwarazm. His works include the Fatawa al-Tamartashi, a commentary on the Jami' al-Saghir, and a book on the Tarawih. The heading places his death around 600 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5496}
