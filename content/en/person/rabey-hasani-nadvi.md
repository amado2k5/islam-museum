## Summary
Rabey Hasani Nadvi was one of the most senior figures of the Indian Sunni scholarly establishment — for over two decades rector of Nadwatul Ulama in Lucknow, and president of the All India Muslim Personal Law Board, the body that speaks for Indian Muslims on sharia-based personal law. {cite:muslim500-rabey-hasani-nadvi} {cite:wikipedia-rabey-hasani-nadvi}

## Formation
Born in 1929 in Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, a grand-nephew of Nadwa's founder Abul Hasan Ali Hasani Nadwi, he was educated at Nadwatul Ulama and at institutions in Saudi Arabia, and spent his career teaching and writing within the seminary. {cite:wikipedia-rabey-hasani-nadvi}

## Career
He became rector ⁦(mohtamim)⁩ of Darul Uloom Nadwatul Ulama in 2000 and president of the All India Muslim Personal Law Board in 2002, holding both until death. He authored around thirty works in Arabic and Urdu — Qur'anic studies, hadith, jurisprudence and biography — and presided over the board through disputes including the triple-talaq litigation. He died in Lucknow on 13 April 2023. {cite:muslim500-rabey-hasani-nadvi} {cite:wikipedia-rabey-hasani-nadvi}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 records him in its obituaries as a leading voice of Indian traditional scholarship — head of one of the subcontinent's great seminaries and custodian of the Nadwa legacy. {cite:muslim500-rabey-hasani-nadvi}
