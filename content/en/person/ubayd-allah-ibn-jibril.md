## Overview
Abu Sa'id Ubayd Allah ibn Jibril ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Bakhtishu', a physician, researcher and historian of medicine, of the Bakhtishu' family of physicians {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Learning
The encyclopedia describes him as a scholar of the knowledge of the Christians and their sects, and gives his death as 451 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
