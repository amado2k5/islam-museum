## Overview
Khalifa ibn Khayyat, known as Shabib al-Usfuri, from Basra. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 324}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim lists among his books *al-Tabaqat* (The Generations), *al-Tarikh* (The History), *Tabaqat al-Qurra'* (The Generations of the Readers), *Tarikh al-Zamnaa, al-'Urjan, al-Mardha and al-'Umyan* (The History of the Lame, the Crippled, the Sick and the Blind), and sections of the Qur'an, its tenths, sevenths and verses. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 324}
