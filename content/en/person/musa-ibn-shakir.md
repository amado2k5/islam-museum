## Overview
Musa ibn Shakir was the father of the three engineers known as the Banu Musa. Al-Zirikli dates his death to about 200 AH, about 815 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 323}.

## His life
In his youth he was a highway robber; he repented and entered the service of al-Ma'mun. He learned astrology and the form of the celestial spheres. He then died while his sons were small, and they were placed in the House of Wisdom and became distinguished; al-Zirikli refers the reader for them to the entry on the eldest, Muhammad ibn Musa, who died in 259 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 323}. His sons were [[person/muhammad-ibn-musa-ibn-shakir|Muhammad]], [[person/ahmad-ibn-musa-ibn-shakir|Ahmad]] and [[person/al-hasan-ibn-musa-ibn-shakir|al-Hasan]], and the institution is the [[group/bayt-al-hikma|House of Wisdom]].

## His works
Al-Zirikli attributes to him a book titled al-Darajat, in manuscript, on the natures of the seven planets {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 323}.
