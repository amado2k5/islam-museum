## Summary
Al-Miqdad ibn Amr al-Bahrani, adopted by al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghuth al-Zuhri so that he became known as al-Miqdad ibn al-Aswad; among the earliest to Islam {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## His role at Badr
He was one of the Islamic army's two horsemen and was given command of the left wing {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}. At the [[event/badr-council|council]] in the valley of Dha Afran he rose after Abu Bakr and Umar and spoke his famous words: we will not tell you as the Children of Israel told Musa "go, you and your Lord, and fight" — but "were you to march with us to Bark al-Ghimad we would fight beside you until you reach it"; the Prophet prayed for good for him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 209}.

## After Badr
He lived into Uthman's caliphate and died at al-Jurf near Medina in year thirty-three, on the well-known account {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.
