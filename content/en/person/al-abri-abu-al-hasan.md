## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Ibrahim ibn Asim al-Abri al-Sijistani, a hadith scholar, memoriser and historian from Sistan. He travelled to Syria, Khurasan and al-Jazira and related from a group of hadith scholars. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.21-22}

## Death
He died in 363 AH (974 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.21-22}
