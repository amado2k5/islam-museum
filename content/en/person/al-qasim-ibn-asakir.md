## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ali ibn al-Hasan ibn Hibat Allah, Abu Muhammad, Ibn Asakir, was a hadith scholar from Damascus. He visited Egypt and its people took from him. He was the son of the author of the great history. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 527 to 600 AH, 1133 to 1203 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Fadl al-Madina; al-Jami' al-Mustaqsa fi Fada'il al-Aqsa, in manuscript; al-Jihad; sessions (majalis) that he dictated; and the work on the merit of visiting al-Khalil, the place of his grave and the graves of his sons, in manuscript. Al-Zirikli says he acquired a carefully written copy of that last work, written in the year 725 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}.
