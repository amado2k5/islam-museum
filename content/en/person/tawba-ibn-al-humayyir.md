## Overview
Tawba ibn al-Humayyir, beloved of Layla al-Akhiliyya; at his grave al-Hajjaj told her 'you were not true to him — were he in your place he would not have turned from you', and Layla said of him she heard him say 'if only Layla al-Akhiliyya would greet me though rocks and boulders lay upon me, she would greet me gladly'. Hammad al-Rawiya related that she came to his grave, which opened to a white dove that struck its breast, fell dead, and was buried beside him.
