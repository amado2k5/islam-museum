## Overview
Abu Sa'id al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Yazid ibn Isa al-Istakhri, one of the imams of the Shafi'is in Baghdad and judge of Qum. He is attributed to Istakhr in Fars. He was born in 244 AH (858 CE) and died in 328 AH (940 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.111}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
