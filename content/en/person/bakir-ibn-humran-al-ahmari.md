## Overview
Bukayr ibn Humran al-Ahmari, the killer of Muslim ibn Aqil. Al-Mas'udi relates that they exchanged two blows, and Muslim's sword cut through his upper lip, and Muslim struck his head. Ibn Ziyad had him taken up to the top of the palace together with Muslim, so that he might take revenge, and he struck off his neck. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 57-58}
