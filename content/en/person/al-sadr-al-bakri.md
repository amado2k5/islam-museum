## Overview
Al-Sadr al-Bakri, the Sufi hafiz of Nishapur then Damascus (574–656); he heard at Mecca from 'Amaz al-Miyanishi, at Damascus from Ibn Tabarzad, in Khurasan from Abu Ruh, and at Isfahan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 282}.
