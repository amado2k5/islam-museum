## Overview
He is Ali ibn Dirr, as the name is printed in the edition, a man of [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] whom the narrator describes as chaste, Muslim, devout and learned in the lawful and the forbidden; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent him from [[place/jayshan-yemen|Jayshan]] and put him over [[place/aden|Aden]] with a charge to fear God {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. The name is read as printed, and Apple Vision reads it the same; the museum knows nothing of his descent and nothing else about him. See [[event/al-hadi-jails-the-jaafir-at-mankath-and-sends-governors-from-jayshan-288|the jailing of the Ja'afir and the governors sent from Jayshan]].
