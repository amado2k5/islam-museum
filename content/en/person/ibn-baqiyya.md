## Summary
The vizier Abu'l-Tahir Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Baqiyya ibn 'Ali, surnamed Nasir al-Dawla; of the people of Awana in Baghdad's district, vizier of 'Izz al-Dawla Bakhtiyar ibn Mu'izz al-Dawla ibn Buya, and among the greatest chiefs and noblest viziers and most generous — the monthly quota of his candles was a thousand mann; when his master turned on him he ordered him thrown to the lions, which killed him; he was then crucified by his house at Bab al-Taq, and the poet Ibn 'Anbari elegised him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
