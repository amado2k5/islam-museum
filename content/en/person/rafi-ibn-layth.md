## Overview
Rafi' ibn Layth ibn Nasr ibn Sayyar. al-Tabari reports that he appeared in Samarqand in 190 AH, opposing Harun al-Rashid and renouncing his obedience {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.319}. See [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]].
