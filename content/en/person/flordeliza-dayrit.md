## Summary
Flordeliza Dayrit is a Canadian Muslim entrepreneur, co-founder of MuslimKids.TV, a subscription streaming platform built to give Muslim children screen content that reflects their own lives and values. {cite:muslim500-flordeliza-dayrit}

## MuslimKids.TV
Launched in Canada, MuslimKids.TV assembles thousands of episodes, e-books, songs and games for children aged roughly two to twelve, mixing licensed international animation with original productions in which Muslim characters, festivals and manners appear as ordinary parts of the story rather than as lessons. The platform's premise, as its founders describe it, is that Muslim parents worldwide face the same gap: mainstream children's media either ignores their children or flattens them into stereotypes. {cite:muslim500-flordeliza-dayrit}

## Reach and model
The service distributes through its own apps and through smart-TV platforms, reaching families across North America, Europe, the Gulf and Southeast Asia, and it has partnered with children's-content producers to commission shows featuring Muslim leads. Dayrit's role has covered content strategy and community building, and the venture has been cited in the Muslim 500 as part of a broader wave of Muslim-owned digital media built for the community's own audience rather than for outsiders' perceptions of it. {cite:muslim500-flordeliza-dayrit}

## Standing
Her inclusion in the Muslim 500 reflects the growing weight the list gives to Muslim media entrepreneurs: figures whose influence is measured in the daily screen habits of children rather than in pulpits or parliaments. {cite:muslim500-flordeliza-dayrit}
