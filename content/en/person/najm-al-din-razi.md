## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad, Abu Bakr, Najm al-Din al-Asadi al-Razi, was an exegete and Sufi who died in Baghdad. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 654 AH, 1256 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 125}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Bahr al-haqa'iq wa-l-ma'ani fi tafsir al-sab' al-mathani, in manuscript, the first part of which is in the collection he calls Sofia; and Kashf al-haqa'iq wa-sharh al-daqa'iq, on Sufism {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 125}.
