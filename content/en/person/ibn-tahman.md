## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Tahman ibn Shu'ayb al-Harawi al-Khurasani, Abu Sa'id, was a hafiz, one of the great ones of Khurasan. Born at Herat, he lived at Nishapur and Baghdad, and died at Nishapur — it is also said at Mecca — in 168 AH (784 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.44}

Al-Fayruzabadi counted him among the imams of Islam, though with irja' in him, and it is also said he recanted the irja'. Al-Zirikli transmits from Abu Zur'a that he was with Ahmad ibn Hanbal when Ibrahim ibn Tahman was mentioned; Ahmad, reclining from illness, sat up and said: the righteous should not be mentioned while one reclines. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.44}
