# Asim Khan

## Summary
Asim Khan is a British Muslim scholar, author and lecturer known as Shaykh Asim Khan. His Islam21c author biography records five books, including the Simple Seerah and the best-selling "The Heart of the Qur'ān", a commentary on Surat Yāsīn. {cite:islam21c-asim-khan}

## Education and career
By the same biography, he is a Hafidh of the Qur'ān, gained a Masters in Pharmacy from University College London, and studied Arabic and Qur'anic Sciences in Cairo, Egypt; his true passion is stated to lie in tafsir studies, with numerous online lectures on Qur'ān-related topics. {cite:islam21c-asim-khan} The Muslim Research and Development Foundation announced his book Elevate Your Salah, on the prayer. {cite:mrdf-asim-khan} Muslim Central distributes a podcast of his lectures, described there as a UK scholar known for his tafsir sessions, with 144 episodes released since September 2017. {cite:muslim-central-asim-khan}
