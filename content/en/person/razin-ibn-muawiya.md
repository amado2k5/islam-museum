## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan Razin ibn Mu'awiya al-'Abdari al-Andalusi al-Saraqasti, author of the *Tajrid al-Sihah*, transmitted al-Bukhari's book from Abu Maktum ibn Abi Dharr and Muslim's from al-Husayn al-Qatri. He sojourned at Mecca for a long while and died in Muharram 535. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 586}
