## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan Razin ibn Mu'awiya al-'Abdari al-Andalusi al-Saraqasti, author of the *Tajrid al-Sihah*, transmitted al-Bukhari's book from Abu Maktum ibn Abi Dharr and Muslim's from al-Husayn al-Qatri. He sojourned at Mecca for a long while and died in Muharram 535. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 586}

## His book in Ibn al-Athir's account
[[person/ibn-al-athir-al-jazri|Ibn al-Athir]] describes Razin's [[work/tajrid-al-sihah|Tajrid al-Sihah]] as the largest and most comprehensive of the compilations combining the source-books, since it contained all six, while faulting it for misplaced hadiths, repetition and omission, and for including Malik's fiqh and al-Bukhari's chapter headings; his [[work/jami-al-usul|Jami' al-Usul]] grew out of that critique. {cite:jami-al-usul-arnaut-2016 p. 57} {cite:jami-al-usul-arnaut-2016 p. 61}
