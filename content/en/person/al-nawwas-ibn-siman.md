## Overview
[[person/al-nawwas-ibn-siman|Al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an al-Ansari al-Kilabi]], counted among the Syrians. His hadith is related through Jubayr ibn Nufayr, Abu Idris al-Khawlani, Yahya ibn Jabir al-Ta'i, Raja' ibn Haywa, Bishr ibn Ubayd Allah and Makhul. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2735}

## On righteousness and sin
He related that the Prophet was asked about righteousness and sin, and he said: "Righteousness is good character, and sin is what stirs in your breast and you dislike that people should see it." {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2735}

## The hadith on betrayal
Jubayr ibn Nufayr related from him a hadith in which the Prophet said: "It is a great betrayal to tell your brother a story that you believe to be true while you are lying to him." {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2735}

## For young visitors
Al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an was one of the Ansar. He related from the Prophet that righteousness is good character, and sin is what you feel in your chest and dislike that people should see.

## Notes for researchers
- The text of the hadith on righteousness and sin is given in summary here; it should be checked in Sahih Muslim before it is relied on.
- The attribution to the Kilab follows Abu Nu'aym; it should be checked.
