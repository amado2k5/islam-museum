## Summary
Amra was the wife of the Basran ascetic Habib Abu Muhammad al-Ajami; Ibn al-Jawzi gives her entry among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 595). {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## Before dawn
From al-Husayn ibn Abd al-Rahman, from one of his companions: one night she woke while her husband slept, and roused him before dawn, saying: "Get up, man — the night has gone and the day has come; before you is a long road and little provision, the caravans of the righteous have set out ahead of us, and we have stayed behind." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## The pain of the heart
From Muslim ibn Ibrahim, from Suhayl the brother of Hazm: when her eye was ailing she was asked how she felt, and said, "The pain of my heart is worse than the pain of my eye." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.712}

## For young visitors
Amra woke her husband before dawn to pray, telling him that the road to the next life is long and that good people had already gone ahead.
