## Summary
A jurist who studied with Wahb ibn Isa, Ibn Abi Tamam and Ibn Lubaba, and who narrated, with several people hearing from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5141}

## Learning and works
He knew the Maliki school and its masters by heart. He wrote the Book of Counsels and a work on the marks of purity and prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5141}
