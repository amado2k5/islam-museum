## Overview
Ishaq ibn al-Furat, the great imam, jurist and qadi of the Egyptian lands Abu Nu'aym al-Tujibi, their Egyptian client, pupil of Imam Malik and not inferior to Ibn al-Qasim; he narrated from Humayd ibn Hani', his earliest shaykh, Yahya ibn Ayyub, al-Layth, Malik and a group. He died in 204 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1072}
