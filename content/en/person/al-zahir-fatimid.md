## Overview
Al-Zahir li-I'zaz Din Allah Ali ibn al-Hakim, the seventh of the Fatimid rulers in al-Suyuti's count, who calls the state "Ubaydid". He succeeded his father, who was killed at Hulwan in 411 AH, and was titled al-Zahir li-I'zaz Din Allah. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.639,644}

Al-Suyuti says that their state weakened in his days, and Aleppo and most of Syria left them. He died in 428 AH, and his son al-Mustansir Ma'add, aged seven, was set up. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.639,644}
