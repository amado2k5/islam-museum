## Overview
A judge from Harran, whom al-Mas'udi describes as having understanding and knowledge. He died after the year 300 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 545-546}

## His poem
He wrote a long poem setting out the doctrines of the Harranians, who are known as the Sabians. According to al-Mas'udi, in it he described their temple, which he called "Mughliya", and the four crypts beneath it that held images of idols, made as a likeness of the heavenly bodies. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 545-546}

This report is attributed to al-Mas'udi and needs an additional source.
