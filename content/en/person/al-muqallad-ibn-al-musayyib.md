## Overview
Al-Muqallad ibn al-Musayyib al-Uqayli, titled Husam al-Dawla, an amir from the [[group/banu-uqayl|Banu Uqayl]] who ruled Mosul within the [[polity/uqaylid-dynasty|Uqaylid state]]. He succeeded [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-musayyib|Muhammad ibn al-Musayyib]] and was the father of [[person/qirwash-ibn-al-muqallad|Qirwash]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Mosul
Ibn Khaldun relates that he won the Daylam over at Mosul, and wrote to Baha al-Dawla undertaking to guarantee the city for two million dirhams a year, and so he took possession of it. Then the caliph al-Qadir billah sat for him and gave him the title Husam al-Dawla, and assigned him Mosul, Kufa, al-Qasr and the two mosques. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Daquqa and his brother Ali
He took Daquqa after it had belonged to Christians. He seized his brother Ali and imprisoned him, and then a reconciliation took place between them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
