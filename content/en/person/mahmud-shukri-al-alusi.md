## Overview
Mahmud Shukri ibn Abdallah al-Alusi al-Baghdadi, of a Baghdadi scholarly family. He worked to revive the heritage of the early scholars and to publish their books. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.322-326}

He was born in 1273 AH (1856 CE) and died in 1342 AH (1924 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.322-326}

The encyclopaedia says that his grandfather was Abu al-Thana' al-Alusi, the author of the commentary *Ruh al-Ma'ani*. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.322-326}
