## Overview
Qutlugh Khatun, widow of Qutb al-Din Muhammad ibn Khanat Qutlugh. She ruled Kirman from 655 to 681 AH, in obedience to the Ilkhanids, and her son Hajjaj was the official ruler between 660 and 700 AH. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan counts her among the most famous queens who ruled independently in the Mongol era. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim} See [[polity/qutlughkhanid-kirman|the Qutlughkhanids of Kirman]].
