## Overview
Turkan, wife of Sultan [[person/malikshah|Malik-Shah]]. al-Suyuti reports that when he died she concealed his death, and secretly sent to the amirs, and made them swear allegiance to her son Mahmud, who was five years old {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.653}.

She then sent to al-Muqtadi asking him to make the boy sultan, and he agreed, and gave him the title Nasir al-Dunya wa al-Din {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.653}.
