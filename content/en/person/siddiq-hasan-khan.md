## Summary
Muhammad Siddiq Khan ibn Hasan ibn Ali, Abu al-Tayyib, of the Husayni family, al-Bukhari, al-Qannawji; a scholar and prince who worked in many fields and wrote some sixty works in Arabic, Persian and Hindi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8517}

## Life
He studied at Delhi and moved to Bhopal to earn a living, grew wealthy, married the ruler of Bhopal and held the title of Nawwab. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8517}

## Works
His works include Husn al-Uswa, Fath al-Bayan fi maqasid al-Qur'an, Nayl al-Maram fi tafsir ayat al-ahkam, al-Rawda al-nadiyya, Husul al-ma'mul min 'ilm al-usul and 'Awn al-Bari. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8517} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8518}
