## Overview
Nasif ibn Abd Allah ibn Nasif ibn Janbalat, known as al-Yaziji, was a poet and one of the leading men of letters of his time {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}. His family came from Homs in Syria, he was born at Kafarshima in Lebanon and died in Beirut; the entry gives 1214 to 1287 AH, 1800 to 1871 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}.

## Work
The Emir Bashir al-Shihabi employed him in his secretarial work for about twelve years, after which he devoted himself to writing and to teaching in some Beirut schools, and he died in Beirut {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}.

## His books
His books include "Majma' al-Bahrayn," printed, maqamas; "Fasl al-Khitab," printed, on the rules of the Arabic language; "al-Jawhar al-Fard," printed, on morphology; "Nar al-Qira fi Sharh Jawf al-Fara'," printed, on grammar; and "Mukhtarat al-Lugha," a manuscript in his own hand {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}. He also wrote "al-'Urf al-Tayyib fi Sharh Diwan Abi al-Tayyib," printed, which Ibrahim refined and completed, and three volumes of verse which he named "al-Nubdha al-Ula," "Nafhat al-Rayhan" and "Thalith al-Qamarayn," all printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}. Isa Mikhail Saba wrote a printed book on his literature and life, "al-Shaykh Nasif al-Yaziji" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 346}.
