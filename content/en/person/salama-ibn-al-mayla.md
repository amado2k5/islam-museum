## Summary
A fighter of Juhayna who was in the cavalry of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] on the day Mecca was conquered; he was martyred in the skirmishes of the entry into Mecca on the conquest morning {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## His martyrdom
Salama ibn al-Mayla' was struck down in the al-Khandama clash and the skirmishes at Mecca's lower end, and was among the three Muslim martyrs of the conquest day, with Kurz ibn Jabir and Khunays ibn Khalid {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## For young visitors
A Muslim fighter of the tribe of Juhayna who was among the horsemen who entered Mecca on the conquest morning and was martyred there.
