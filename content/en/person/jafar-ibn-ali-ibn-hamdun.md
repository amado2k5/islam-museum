## Overview
Ja'far ibn Ali ibn Hamdun, lord of Musayla and leader of the Shi'a, the subject of Ibn Hani's well-known qasida rhyming in *fa'*. Al-Maqqari says that at the beginning of the state the one who aspired to rule was with those who were with him from the Zanata and the Berbers. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.433}

## Alliances
Ibn Abi Amir later enlisted his help against Ghalib, and then Ibn Abi Amir had him killed, through the collusion of Ibn Abd al-Wadud, Ibn Jahwar and Ibn Dhi al-Nun. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.433}
