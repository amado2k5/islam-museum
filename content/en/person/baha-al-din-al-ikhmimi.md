## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd al-Wali ibn Abd al-Salam, known as Baha' al-Din al-Ikhmimi al-Maraghi, was an Egyptian Shafi'i jurist and legal theorist. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 700 AH, 1300 CE, and his death as 764 AH, 1363 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 183}.

## Career
He studied in Cairo, then settled in Damascus and died there in the plague {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 183}.

## Works
He was best known for a book of theology, al-Munqidh min al-Zalal fi al-Ilm wa-l-Amal; al-Zirikli records a manuscript in the Egyptian Dar al-Kutub, photographed from the Feyzullah copy no. 1216 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 183}. Al-Zirikli says the author followed a path of his own in the book, and that scholars have remarks on a few points in it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 183}.
