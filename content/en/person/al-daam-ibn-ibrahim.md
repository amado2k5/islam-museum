## Overview
Al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim is described as one of "the Yemeni rulers who had taken power"; he wrote to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] asking him to appoint him over the land he held, and al-Hadi required of him ruling by the Book of God and the sunna of His Messenger and following what they follow; many letters passed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.91} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.92} The text names neither his land nor the outcome. See [[event/al-hadi-at-khiwan-and-the-letter-of-al-daam-285]].
