## Summary
Dato' Seri Dr Zulkifli Mohamad Al-Bakri (born 16 January 1969) is a Malaysian Islamic scholar who served as the seventh Mufti of the Federal Territories and then as Minister in the Prime Minister's Department in charge of Religious Affairs. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en}

## Early life and education
He was born at Kampung Gong Pauh, Kemaman, in the state of Terengganu, on 16 January 1969. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en} His education is listed as the Islamic University of Madinah, Al-Ulum Al-Islamiah Wa Al-Arabiah University in Damascus, and a PhD from Universiti Sains Malaysia. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en}

## Offices
He served as the seventh Mufti of the Federal Territories from 23 June 2014 to 9 March 2020. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en} He then served as Minister in the Prime Minister's Department (Religious Affairs) in the Perikatan Nasional administration under Prime Minister Muhyiddin Yassin, from 10 March 2020 to 16 August 2021. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en} He was also a Senator, from March 2020 to March 2023. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en} These offices are recorded here as dated facts of his career; all of them are past at the time of writing. {cite:wikipedia-zulkifli-mohamad-al-bakri-en}
