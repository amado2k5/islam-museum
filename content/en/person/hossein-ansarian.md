## Summary
Hossein Ansarian is an Iranian cleric, Qur'an commentator and preacher, among contemporary Iran's best-known Qur'an interpreters and pulpit speakers. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa} English Wikipedia describes him as an Iranian Shia cleric, and Persian Wikipedia describes him as a Shia cleric and Qur'an commentator from Iran. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa} His lectures reach their widest audience today through his official Instagram page, run by the Dar al-Erfan institute, which counted 853,136 followers in November 2026 per an on-platform profile read — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:instagram-ansarian-ir}

## Beginnings and education
Hossein Ansarian was born in Khansar, Isfahan province, on 9 November 1944, to a father from the Haj Sheikh family, a well-known family of the town, and a mother from its Sayyid Mostafavi family. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} His family moved to Tehran when he was three years old; he attended the Borhan school, completed high school in 1962, and continued his higher education at the Qom and Tehran seminaries. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} His teachers included Ali-Akbar Borhan, Mohammad Fazel Lankarani, Sayyid Muhammad Muhaqqiq Damad, Mirza Hashem Amoli and Hussein-Ali Montazeri. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa}

## Career
Ansarian taught interpretation of the Qur'an and Nahj al-Balagha at the Qom seminary, and fiqh there, and mysticism and ethics at the Tehran seminary. {cite:wikihussain-hossein-ansarian} In 1998 he founded the Dar al-Irfan Scientific Research Center in Qom. {cite:wikihussain-hossein-ansarian} He preaches traditionally at the Hosseinieh Hamadaniha in Tehran, and the Persian article describes his audience as mostly the traditional people of Tehran's old bazaar streets. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa} Four thousand lecture tapes and more than forty book titles in about eighty volumes are recorded for him. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa}

## Works and exegesis
His works include a translation of the Qur'an, a translation of Nahj al-Balagha, a translation of the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, and a translation and paraphrase of Mafatih al-Jinan. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa} He wrote a commentary on the Kumayl supplication, the fifteen-volume Erfan-e Eslami (commentary on Misbah al-Shari'a) and the fifteen-volume Diyar-e Asheqan (commentary on the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya). {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-fa} His books also include The Family System in Islam, Tawbe (Repentance, the Bosom of Mercy), Ahl-e Bayt and Luqman al-Hakim, and a collected book of poems. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en} His works have been translated into English, Urdu, Russian, Turkish and Arabic. {cite:wikipedia-hossein-ansarian-en}
