## Summary
Ibn Shayth al-Qurashi — God gathered for him excellence, manliness, generosity and gallantry; he gave much in charity and al-Qadi al-Fadil needed him for the art of letters; Abu'l-Muzaffar al-Nadanji wrote him a letter of longing; he held the vizierate for al-Malik al-Mu'azzam in Syria; raised at Qus, he died at Damascus 625 and was buried in his turbah at Qasyun. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 117}
