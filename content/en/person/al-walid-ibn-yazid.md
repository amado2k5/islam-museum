## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, the eleventh Umayyad caliph. He was the son of [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik]], and succeeded [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] by his designation. His cousin [[person/yazid-ibn-al-walid|Yazid ibn al-Walid]] killed him in 126 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.407-408}

## Reports about him
Al-Suyuti says that the Musnad of Ahmad contains a hadith about a man called al-Walid who would be harder on the community than Pharaoh was on his people. Ibn Fadl Allah describes him as an arrogant tyrant, and says that he shot the Qur'an with arrows. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.407-408}

Al-Mas'udi says that he was given the allegiance on the day Hisham died in 125 AH, and was killed at al-Bakhra' near Damascus. He took singers and showed wine and entertainments, and was called the debauched one of the Marwanids. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.200-204}

The length of his caliphate and his age at his killing differ between the two sources: six months and an age of thirty-five or forty-six according to al-Suyuti, and a year and two months, aged forty, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.407-408} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.200-204}

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366–368}

- **Medina:** Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm (Muhammad ibn Hisham's deputy, dismissed by al-Walid); then Yusuf ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf (with Mecca and Ta'if until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Yemen:** al-Dahhak ibn Zaml (until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Basra:** al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim (confirmed until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Kufa:** Ubayd Allah ibn al-Abbas al-Kindi (then dismissed by Yusuf); then Abu Umayya ibn al-Mughira ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Aqil al-Thaqafi (lasted a week until Yusuf fled) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Khurasan:** [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar al-Laythi]] (confirmed until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Sijistan:** Harb ibn Qatan ibn Qabisa al-Hilali {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Sind:** Amr ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Thaqafi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Bahrayn:** Muhammad ibn Hassan ibn Sa'id al-Asadi (until al-Walid was killed); then Bishr ibn Sallam al-Abdi (some say he killed al-Musayyab ibn Fadala and stayed until Ibn Hubayra came) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 366}
- **Yamama:** al-Muhajir ibn Abd Allah al-Kilabi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Ifriqiya:** Hanzala ibn Safwan (until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Oman:** al-Fayd ibn Muhammad ibn Kardam ibn Bayhas (appointed by Yusuf ibn Umar) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Jazira, Armenia and Azerbaijan:** [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad ibn Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] (until al-Walid was killed); then Asim ibn Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Hilali (Marwan's deputy over Armenia and Azerbaijan) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Judgeship of Basra:** Amir ibn Ubayda (withdrew during the civil strife) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** [[person/muhammad-ibn-abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-layla|Ibn Abi Layla]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Judgeship of Medina:** Sa'd ibn Ibrahim (appointed by Yusuf ibn Muhammad, then dismissed); then Yahya ibn Sa'id (until al-Walid was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Summer campaign:** al-Ghamr ibn Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Police:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Hanbal al-Kalbi (then dismissed); then Abd Allah ibn Amir al-Kala'i {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Chancery (letters):** Salim, mawla of Sa'id ibn Abd al-Malik; then Abd Allah ibn Salim (his son) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Land tax and army:** Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf; then al-Hajjaj ibn Umayr {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Seal, storehouses and treasury:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Hanbal al-Kalbi (together with the police) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Small seal:** Rabah ibn Abi Umara {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 367}
- **Chamberlain:** Isa ibn Miqsam {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 368}
- **Guard:** Ghaylan, son-in-law of Abu Ma'n {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 368}
