## Overview
Shurayh the judge, the well-known judge of Kufa, and a pupil of [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud]] {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.

## Judgement on Ali's armour
The sources report that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] had a dispute with a Jew over a coat of mail. Shurayh ruled for the Jew, because a son's testimony is not admissible for his father. The Jew then testified to the truth, and became a Muslim, admiring this judgement against the Commander of the Faithful {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.306-307}.

## Death
al-Shirazi transmits from al-Mada'ini that he died in the year 82 AH, and from al-Ash'ath that he lived to be a hundred and twenty. The date is disputed {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.
