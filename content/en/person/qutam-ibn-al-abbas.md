## Overview
Qutham ibn al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib was with Sa'id ibn Uthman in Khurasan. He died in Samarqand, and it is said that he was martyred there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He refused five shares of the spoils and was content with one share for himself and one for his horse. When the news of his death reached Ibn Abbas, he said: "How far apart are his birth and his grave." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
