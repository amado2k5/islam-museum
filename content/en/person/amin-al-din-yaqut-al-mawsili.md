## Overview
Amin al-Din Yaqut, the Mosul scribe, friend of Ibn al-Athir and the calligrapher of his age. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.426}

## Ibn al-Athir's praise
Ibn al-Athir says that in his time no one wrote anything close to him, and no one reproduced the method of Ibn al-Bawwab as he did. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.426}

He describes him as possessing many excellences of literature and other learning, abundant in goodness, well known in the world, and that people agree in praising him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.426}

## His death
He died in 618 AH (about 1221 CE). {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.426}

## Notes for researchers
- The information is from Ibn al-Athir alone, who was his friend, and needs an additional source.
