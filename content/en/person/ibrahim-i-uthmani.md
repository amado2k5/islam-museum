## Overview
Sultan Ibrahim I ibn Ahmad, an Ottoman sultan. His reign lasted eight years and nine months, and he was killed in 1058 AH (1648 CE) at the age of thirty-four. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.307}

## The war over Crete
He came to power while the affairs of the state were unsettled. In his reign the Ottomans mustered armies and a fleet and declared war on the Venetians, who dominated the island of Crete and the trade of the Aegean Sea. The Venetians were arrested in various parts of the land and their property confiscated. A campaign was sent to Crete in 1055 AH (1645 CE) that took parts of it. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.307}

## Deposition
The soldiers in Istanbul rebelled and decided to depose him, and to install in his place his son [[person/mehmed-iv-uthmani|Mehmed IV]], who had not yet reached the age of seven. Ibrahim was killed. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.307}
