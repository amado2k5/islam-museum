## Overview
Ahmad Mukhtar Pasha al-Ghazi was a Turkish mathematician and one of the great Ottoman commanders {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}. Al-Zirikli prints his birth as 1253 AH, marked with a question mark, 1837 CE, and his death as 1337 AH, 1919 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}.

## Life
He studied in Istanbul and moved between posts in the Hijaz, Yemen, Crete, Albania and Egypt, where he served as high commissioner {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}. He returned home, became a member of the Senate in 1908 and grand vizier in 1913, and died in Istanbul {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}. He received the title al-Ghazi for his good service in the Russo-Turkish war {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}.

## Works
He read Arabic well but wrote his books in Turkish, and Shafiq Yakan translated some of them into Arabic {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}. Chief among them are Riyad al-Mukhtar, Mir'at al-Miqat wa-l-Adwar, Islah al-Taqwim and al-Taqwim al-Mali, all printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 255}.
