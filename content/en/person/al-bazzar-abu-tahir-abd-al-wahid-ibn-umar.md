## Overview
Abu al-Tahir Abd al-Wahid ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Hashim al-Baghdadi al-Bazzar, a Baghdadi reciter and grammarian, among the notables of the second half of the fourth century AH. He died in 349 AH (961 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The sources differ on his date of birth, and the museum's data record 278 AH (891 CE). That year needs an additional source.
