## Overview
Mansur al-Daylami, amir of the pilgrimage in 317 AH. He led the pilgrims from Baghdad to Mecca. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.154}

## The pilgrimage of 317
Ibn al-Athir relates that Abu Tahir al-Qarmati met them at Mecca on the day of Tarwiya. He and his companions plundered the wealth of the pilgrims and killed them, even in the Sacred Mosque and in the House itself, and they tore out the Black Stone. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.154} See [[person/abu-tahir-al-qarmati|Abu Tahir al-Qarmati]].

The data hold no other report about Mansur, and his biography needs an additional source.
