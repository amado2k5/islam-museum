## Summary
Faris al-Din Aqtay, the renowned amir; master of seven hundred mamluks, his estate feared — a peerless sword and a valiant slayer. Shajar al-Durr, al-Malik al-Mu'izz and their party agreed his intent was ill, saying only guile could remove him from Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## His killing
Al-Mu'izz called him to the Citadel, took him aside, beat him and returned him bruised with the marks of ill on his face — poison was slipped him and he died in agony; or, it was said, run through with a lance, he witnessed his fay' assigned to 'Abda b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, qadi of his town. His death was reckoned the herald of their state's fall; he has an ode praising the Prophet. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
