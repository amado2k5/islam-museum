## Summary
Badal al-Tabrizi, the muhaddith, hafiz, trustworthy traveller; he heard from Abu Sa'd Ibn Abi 'Asrun and others, journeyed and transmitted extensively from al-Labban and al-Saydalani, heard at Nishapur, Egypt and Iraq, wrote, toiled and taught, and held the shaykhdom of Irbil's dar al-hadith; when the Tatars took it in 634 he came to Aleppo and there died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 188}
