## Overview
Muhalhil ibn Safwan, whose name was Yahya, a client of Ali ibn Abdallah. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur granted him an estate in Medina, and the Street of Muhalhil is attributed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}

## The naming
Al-Baladhuri relates that his name was Yahya, until Muhammad ibn Ali asked him to recite some verse, and he recited a line of Muhalhil, and so he named him Muhalhil, and then freed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}
