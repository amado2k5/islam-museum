## Overview
Najib ibn Sulayman al-Haddad (1867–1899 CE) was a journalist and writer. He was born in Beirut and died in Cairo. {cite:al-aalam}

## His work
He moved to Cairo and wrote for about ten years for the newspaper «al-Ahram». {cite:al-aalam}

He published many novels, most of them translated from French, among them «Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi», «Shuhada al-Gharam», «Hadan», «al-Sayyid» and «Ghusn al-Ban». {cite:al-aalam}
