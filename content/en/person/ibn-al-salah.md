## Overview
The imam and memoriser Abu Amr Ibn al-Salah. He dictated a session that he called *al-Ahadith al-Kulliyya* (The Comprehensive Hadiths), in which he gathered the comprehensive hadiths on which, it is said, the religion turns. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}

The session contained twenty-six hadiths. [[person/al-nawawi|Al-Nawawi]] took them and built his *Forty* on them. See [[work/ahadith-kulliyya-ibn-al-salah|The Comprehensive Hadiths]]. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
