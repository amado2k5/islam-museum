## Overview
Abu Umar Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Kindi al-Misri. "Kashf al-Zunun" says that he was the first to gather the reports of [[work/qudat-misr|the judges of Egypt]] down to 246 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}

Ibn Zulaq continued his book, and then Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani added a large volume to it, titled "Raf' al-Isr an Qudat Misr". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}

He should not be confused with [[person/al-kindi|Ya'qub ibn Ishaq al-Kindi]] the philosopher; the museum's data treat them as two different people.
