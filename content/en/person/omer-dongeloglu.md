## Summary
Ömer Döngeloğlu was a Turkish theologian, writer, producer and television presenter, born in 1968 in Zile, Tokat, who died of COVID-19 on 3 May 2020 in Istanbul; he was known for his religious programmes on Kanal 7 and for his research on the sirah. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

His YouTube channel is a posthumous legacy channel, created after his death, which counted 339,000 subscribers as of 15 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-omer-dongeloglu}

## Early life and education
Döngeloğlu was born in Zile, Tokat, in 1968; the Turkish and Dutch editions disagree with the English edition on his birth day, so no day is recorded here. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en}

He completed his primary education at Zile Altunyurt Primary School and his secondary education at Zile İmam Hatip High School, and later graduated from Sakarya University's theology faculty. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

He was fluent in Arabic and English. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

## Career and work
Döngeloğlu worked as an imam-hatip in his hometown in Tokat between 1986 and 1996, and worked in various administrative positions in the public sector for many years. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

He spent years researching, studying, reading and travelling on Islamic history and the Siyer-i Nebi. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr} {cite:official-site-omer-dongeloglu}

He was known for the religious programmes he hosted on Kanal 7, among them Önden Gidenler and the Sahur Vakti programme. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-simple}

## Works
Among the books the articles list are Peygamberin İzinde, Allah'a Adanmış Hayatlar, Yeryüzünün Yıldızları, Sözün Miracı; DUA, Allah Resulünü Görenler, Peygamberimizin Dostları and Mus'ab bin Umeyr. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

## Death
Döngeloğlu died on 3 May 2020 in a hospital in Başakşehir, Istanbul, where he was being treated for COVID-19, and was buried in Edirnekapı Martyr's Cemetery in Istanbul. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-en} {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

## Personal life
The Turkish article states that he was married and the father of five children; this record adds no further family detail. {cite:wikipedia-omer-dongeloglu-tr}

## Reach
His audience figure is the legacy YouTube channel (@omerdongelogluondengidenler): 339,000 subscribers, 183,631,719 views and 5,083 videos as of 15 November 2026 per vidIQ. {cite:vidiq-omer-dongeloglu} {cite:youtube-omer-dongeloglu}

This channel was created after his death — vidIQ dates its start to 28 October 2021 — and carries recordings of his Kanal 7 programmes, so it is not a channel he ran in life. {cite:vidiq-omer-dongeloglu} {cite:youtube-omer-dongeloglu}
