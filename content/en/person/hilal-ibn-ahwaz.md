## Summary
Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini was one of the landowners of [[place/basra|Basra]]. [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn 'Abd al-Malik]] granted him the estate in which the Marghab canal lay at Basra — eight thousand jaribs — as al-Baladhuri records. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 507}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Bashir ibn 'Ubaydallah ibn Abi Bakra dug the Marghab and called it Marghab Marw, and that the estate containing the Marghab belonged to Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini, to whom Yazid ibn 'Abd al-Malik had granted it — eight thousand jaribs {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 507}. Bashir dug the Marghab, its feeders and cross-channels by encroachment and claimed it as his own estate; Humayri ibn Hilal disputed with him, so Khalid ibn 'Abdallah al-Qasri wrote to Malik ibn al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud — then over the ahdath of Basra — to separate Ajayri from the Marghab and its land, for Bashir had presented himself before Khalid and his complaint was accepted {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 507}. 'Amr ibn Yazid al-Asidi backed Humayri and told Malik ibn al-Mundhir: "This is no clearance — it is only an obstruction between Humayri and the Marghab" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 508}.
