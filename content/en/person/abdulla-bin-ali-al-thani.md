## Summary
Sheikh Dr Abdulla bin Ali Al-Thani is a Qatari academic and official among the chief engineers of Qatar's higher-education landscape: he was the founding president of Hamad Bin Khalifa University (2010–2015) and vice-president for education at the Qatar Foundation for a decade, and today serves as advisor at the Amiri Diwan, chairman of Media City Qatar and vice-chairman of the board of trustees of Qatar University. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani}

## Education
He took an MSc at Colorado State University and a PhD at the University of Southampton, then served on the faculty of Qatar University (1993–2006), spending 2004–2005 at the RAND-Qatar Policy Institute researching higher-education questions. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani}

## Building the higher-education system
At the Qatar Foundation he was vice-president for education (2005–2015), developing the foundation's higher-education strategy, then became founding president of Hamad Bin Khalifa University (2010–2015), the national research university inside Education City. He founded and chaired the World Innovation Summit for Education (WISE) (2009–2015), which became an annual international platform for education reform and launched the WISE Prize, WISE Awards and WISE Learners' Voice, and he launched Stars of Science, the reality-TV programme encouraging innovation among Arab youth with millions of followers. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani} {cite:muslim500-abdulla-bin-ali-al-thani}

## Current public roles
He serves as advisor at the Amiri Diwan, chairman of Media City Qatar — the government body attracting global media to Qatar — chair of the Executive Board and vice-chairman of the Board of Trustees of Qatar University, and managing director and board member of Qatar Leadership Centre. He also sits on international boards including the board of directors of Georgetown University, the board of trustees of the American University of Beirut and the advisory board of Rice University's Baker Institute, and previously served on the executive council of the World Digital Library at the Library of Congress and the governing board of the UNESCO Institute for Lifelong Learning in Hamburg. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani}

## Recognition
The World Economic Forum named him a Young Global Leader in 2011 for his regional role in education reform; in 2015 France made him Commander of the Order of Arts and Letters for contributions to education and culture, and in 2012 he accepted the Magisterio Prize for Education Leaders on behalf of WISE. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani}

## Legacy
His role reads as the institutional execution of Qatar's education vision: while Sheikha Moza built Education City as an umbrella, he built within it the national research university and the international mechanisms (the Summit and Stars of Science) that turned education reform from government policy into permanent institutions with a global presence. {cite:aia-abdulla-al-thani}
