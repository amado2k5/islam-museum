## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Hasan ibn Muhammad al-Hasani al-Alawi, Abu Faris, was sultan of Morocco and the son of its sultan, and brother of the legitimate sultan, Mawlay Yusuf ibn al-Hasan. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1298 AH, 1881 CE, and his death as 1363 AH, printed as 1944 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His works
He was given allegiance after his father's death in 1311 AH and set up a house of antiquities in Fez; he was the first to bring electric light into the city {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}. He gave up the throne in 1326 AH; the French exiled him in 1333 AH to Pau, where he stayed for a time, then his freedom was restored and he lived in Tangier and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
