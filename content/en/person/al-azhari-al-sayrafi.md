## Overview
Abu'l-Qasim 'Ubaydallah b. Ahmad b. 'Uthman al-Baghdadi al-Sayrafi al-Azhari — hafiz, writer of much, devoted to hadith, transmitting from al-Qati'i and his tier. He died in Safar 435 at eighty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 255}
