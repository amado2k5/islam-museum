## Overview
The amir Rukn al-Din Baybars ibn Abd Allah al-Mansuri, the Dawadar. He was taken as a child slave to Egypt in 659 AH, became one of the Mamluk amirs, and died in 725 AH (1325 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia describes him as a commander and historian. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
