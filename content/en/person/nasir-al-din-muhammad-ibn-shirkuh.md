## Summary
Lord of Homs — al-Malik Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn al-Malik Asad al-Din Shirkuh, Salah al-Din's cousin. He was a brave, daring horseman with ambitions for the sultanate; some said wine killed him, others that he was fed poison. He died on the day of 'Arafa 581. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 753}
