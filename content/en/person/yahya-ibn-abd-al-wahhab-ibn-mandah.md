## Overview
Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya al-Abdi al-Isbahani, Abu Zakariyya, Ibn Mandah, was a historian and hafiz of hadith, from a family of well-known learning and merit in Isfahan. He was born and died in Isfahan, in 434 AH, 1043 CE, and 511 AH, 1118 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.

## His works
He came to Baghdad on pilgrimage, related hadith there and dictated at the mosque of al-Mansur {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}. Al-Zirikli names among his books a History of Isfahan; a work on the two Sahihs in hadith; the merits of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal; al-Tanbih ala Ahwal al-Juhhal wa-l-Munafiqin, of which Ibn Nasir al-Din had a copy in his hand; and a work on those of the Companions who lived a hundred and twenty years, in manuscript, a few leaves of it photographed at the Institute of Manuscripts, number 840 history {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.
