## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn al-Azhar ibn Talha al-Azhari al-Harawi, a philologist and man of letters, a Shafi'i in school, from Herat. He first took interest in jurisprudence, then language came to dominate him, and he became known for it. His best-known book is "Tahdhib al-Lugha". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He died in 370 AH / 981 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
