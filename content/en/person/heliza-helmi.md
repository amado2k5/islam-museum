## Summary
Nur Heliza binti Helmi, known as Heliza Helmi, is a Malaysian singer and actress turned dawah and humanitarian figure, born in Kuala Terengganu on 3 February 1986, and known to the public as a contestant on season 5 of Akademi Fantasia in 2007, where she finished fourth. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

YADIM (Yayasan Dakwah Islamiah Malaysia) lists her among its Da'wah Icons on its achievements page, and she is active in Islamic humanitarian work, often with her younger sister Hazwani Helmi. {cite:yadim-heliza-helmi} {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

Her Instagram account counted 2,300,000 followers by the metadata of her own post dated 16 April 2026 — the platform's own post metadata, not a statistics-service read, and the figure is recorded on exactly that basis. {cite:instagram-heliza-helmi}

## Beginnings and career
Heliza was accepted into Akademi Fantasia season five as one of sixteen student candidates selected to enter the academy, and sang assigned songs through the weekly concerts until she placed fourth in the final week. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

A recording and screen career followed: the English article lists her album *Jom Zikir* (2015), acting roles including the film *Syurga Cinta* (2009) and *Bukan Cinta Malaikat* (2017), and television appearances running to 2025. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

The article's infobox gives her education as UMS (Universiti Malaysia Sabah), Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

## Dawah and humanitarian work
The article describes her as one of the artists active in the humanitarian field of the Islamic community, often working with her younger sister Hazwani Helmi, citing Harian Metro. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

It also records, citing the same newspaper, that her passion for the ummah's cause grew stronger after she went and saw for herself the situation in Gaza, Palestine, in 2012, and that the lyrics of her song "La Tahzan" were inspired by her first experience of setting foot there. {cite:wikipedia-heliza-helmi-en}

YADIM's achievements page lists her name among its Da'wah Icons. {cite:yadim-heliza-helmi}
