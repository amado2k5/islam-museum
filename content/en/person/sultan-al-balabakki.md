## Summary
Sultan al-Ba'labakki, the ascetic, a companion of Shaykh 'Abd Allah al-Yunini; al-Sakhawi counted him among God's greatest saints in his Tabaqat. His servant related that he asked him how many times he had reached Mecca in a night — thirteen times; his son related that the gates of Baalbek would open for him at night. He conferred the state upon a Kurdish groom, then ordered him to bury him after three days — and the groom died after three. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 219}
