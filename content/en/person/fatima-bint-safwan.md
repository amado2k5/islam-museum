## Summary
Wife of Amr ibn Sa'id ibn al-As, of Kinana; she emigrated with him to Abyssinia and perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her wife of Amr ibn Sa'id ibn al-As, saying she perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He names her again among "the rarities of the Arabs" of the Abyssinian emigrants, she being of Kinana. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/kinana|Kinana]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}
