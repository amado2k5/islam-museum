## Summary
A [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] chief in the truce years: he sheltered the Khuza'a fugitives in his house, led their delegation to Medina, met [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] at [[place/asfan|Asfan]] and concealed his news, spied on the army with him at Marr al-Zahran then slipped away and told the Prophet their number {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa pp. 33, 37-38, 46}. Under the ascription "Budayl ibn Umm Asram" he answered the verse of [[person/al-akhzar-ibn-lut|al-Akhzar ibn Lu't]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/34}.

## Sheltering the fugitives
When [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] surprised Khuza'a at [[place/al-watir|al-Watir]], they fortified themselves in the house of Budayl ibn Warqa' and the house of [[person/rafi-mawla-khuzaa|Rafi']] their client {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/33}.

## The delegation to Medina
Budayl came in a party of Khuza'a and told the Prophet of Quraysh's backing Banu Bakr and breaking the pact {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}. On his way back he met Abu Sufyan at Asfan and denied having seen anything or anyone come to him — though he had been to Medina {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37-38}.

## Spying at Marr al-Zahran
Abu Sufyan spied on the army at Marr al-Zahran with [[person/hakim-ibn-hizam|Hakim ibn Hizam]] and Budayl; when they saw the fires each turned back with his news — in one account Budayl told Abu Sufyan he feared Muhammad had made ready, then slipped away, came to the Prophet and told him their condition and number {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa pp. 42, 46}.

## The verse
Under the ascription "Budayl ibn Umm Asram" he answered al-Akhzar ibn Lu't al-Dili's poem with a long poem restoring the merit to Khuza'a {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/34-35}. Al-Isti'ab's genealogists differed on his ascription, as in the note {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/34}.

## For young visitors
A chief of Khuza'a who sheltered his fleeing people in his house, then went to the Muslims' prophet to tell him what had happened, then stood on a mountain watching the conquest army beside his enemy while hiding his intent.
