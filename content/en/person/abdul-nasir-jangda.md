# Abdul Nasir Jangda

Abdul Nasir Jangda is an American scholar and teacher from Dallas, Texas. Born and raised in Dallas, he memorised the Qur'an in Karachi as a boy and returned there after high school for Jamia Binoria's alim programme — dated 2001 in Qalam's biography and 2002 in AlMaghrib's — before taking degrees in Arabic from Karachi University and in Islamic studies from the University of Sindh.{cite:qalam-abdul-nasir-jangda}{cite:almaghrib-abdul-nasir-jangda}

He founded the Qalam Institute and its seminary, where he teaches hadith, tafsir and jurisprudence, and recorded the complete Seerah Podcast and the Tafsir Podcast.{cite:qalam-abdul-nasir-jangda}
