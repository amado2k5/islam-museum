## Summary

Sa'd ibn Ubayd ibn al-Nu'man, an Awsi Ansari, Abu Zayd al-Qari who gathered the Qur'an. A Badri who led prayers at the Quba mosque in the times of the Prophet, Abu Bakr and Umar. Ibn Numayr names him among those martyred at al-Qadisiyya in 16 AH; al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar has him die at Medina in Umar's time.
