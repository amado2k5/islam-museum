## Overview
Abu Talib 'Abd al-Rahman Ibn al-'Ajami al-Hilli al-Shafi'i; he transmitted from Yahya al-Thaqafi and Ibn Tabarzad and taught; the Tatars tortured him for the money-hoard until he died, on 24 Safar 658 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 301}.
