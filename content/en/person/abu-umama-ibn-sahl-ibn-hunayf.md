## Overview
Abu Umama ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf al-Ansari, of Banu Bayada; al-Waqidi said he had companionship, and Khalifa and al-Baghawi counted him among the Companions. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1620}

## His transmission
Ibn Hajar records through Muhammad ibn Ishaq from Ma'bad ibn Malik from his brother Abdallah ibn Ka'b from him that he heard the Messenger of God say: no man cuts off a Muslim's right by his oath but God forbids him Paradise and makes the Fire his due. He also transmitted from Umar, and Muslim and al-Baghawi transmitted through al-Ala' ibn Abd al-Rahman from Ma'bad from his brother from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1620-1621}
