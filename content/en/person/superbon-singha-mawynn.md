# Superbon Singha Mawynn

## Summary

Superbon Singha Mawynn is a Thai Muay Thai fighter and kickboxer, born Suppachai Muensang on 16 August 1990 in Phatthalung province, Thailand. He fought for years under the name Superbon Banchamek, after the camp of his trainer Buakaw Banchamek. He won the Kunlun Fight tournament in 2016 and the ONE Featherweight Kickboxing World Championship in 2021, returned to the title in 2024, and compiled 117 wins, 29 of them by knockout. {cite:wikipedia-superbon-singha-mawynn-en} {cite:wikidata-superbon-singha-mawynn}

## Beginnings and first titles

Superbon began his kickboxing career in 2015 in China's Kunlun Fight promotion, reaching the semi-finals of its 64-man tournament at the first attempt, then won the tournament in 2016, knocking out Jomthong Chuwattana in the final. He won the Enfusion eight-man tournament in 2018, and took IFMA world championship gold medals in 2015, 2016 and 2017 and World Games gold in 2017. {cite:wikipedia-superbon-singha-mawynn-en}

## ONE Championship

On his ONE Championship debut on 31 July 2020 he defeated Sitthichai Sitsongpeenong by unanimous decision. On 15 October 2021 he faced Giorgio Petrosyan for the inaugural ONE Featherweight Kickboxing World Championship and stopped him with a right high kick in the second round, becoming only the second fighter to knock Petrosyan out and the first champion of the division. He defended the title against Marat Grigorian on 25 March 2022, lost it to Chingiz Allazov by knockout on 14 January 2023, and on 5 April 2024 beat Grigorian again to take the interim title; the article lists him as the current and two-time champion. {cite:wikipedia-superbon-singha-mawynn-en}

## Style and teacher

Superbon trained early on under Buakaw Banchamek and carried his camp's name for years, and is more recently a student of the trainer Ajarn Gae. He is one of only two fighters to have knocked out Giorgio Petrosyan. {cite:wikipedia-superbon-singha-mawynn-en} {cite:wikipedia-buakaw-banchamek-en}
