## Overview
Shurayh ibn Hani al-Harithi. al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-abi-bakra|Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra]] made peace with Ratbil for five hundred thousand dirhams and three of his sons as hostages. Shurayh said to him: "Fear God and fight these people, for if you do what you want, you will weaken Islam on this frontier." Then they fought, and Shurayh charged and was killed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
