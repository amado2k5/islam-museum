## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Mas'ud al-Ansari al-Burshani, a jurist, philologist, and expert in inheritance and arithmetic, from Wadi Ash in al-Andalus. He took knowledge from the leading scholars of his age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 806 AH (1403 CE) and his death as 888 AH (1483 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
