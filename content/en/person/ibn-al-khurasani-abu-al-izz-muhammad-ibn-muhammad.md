## Overview
Abu al-Izz Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Mawahib, known as Ibn al-Khurasani, a grammarian, prosodist, poet and writer. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* says that he was born in 494 AH (1100 CE) and died in 576 AH (1180 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The sources mention only some of his teachers. The rest needs an additional source.
