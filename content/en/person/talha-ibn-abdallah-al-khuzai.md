## Overview
Talha ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalaf al-Khuza'i, known as "Talhat al-Talhat", who was governor of Sistan on behalf of Salm ibn Ziyad {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The ransom of Abu Ubayda
Salm ibn Ziyad sent him, and he ransomed Abu Ubayda ibn Ziyad from captivity in Kabul for five hundred thousand dirhams. Then he set out from Kabul to Sistan as its governor {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He collected the land tax and gave to his visitors, and he died in Sistan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
