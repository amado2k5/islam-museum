## Summary
Abu 'Umar Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Abdallah b. 'Isa al-Mu'afiri al-Andalusi al-Talamanki, muqri', muhaddith, hafiz, scholar of Cordoba and author, ninety years old; he made the hajj and studied in Egypt under Abu Bakr al-Adfuwi and Abu Bakr the engineer among many, expert in the sciences of the Qur'an — tafsir, readings, i'rab, rulings and meanings. Ibn Bashkuwal said: he was a drawn sword against the people of whims and innovations, their suppressor, jealous for the shari'a. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 243-244}
