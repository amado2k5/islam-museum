## Overview
Mas'ada ibn Hakama ibn Malik ibn Hudhayfa ibn Badr, of the Banu Badr, lords of [[group/banu-fazara|Fazara]]. [[person/qays-ibn-al-musakhkhar|Qays ibn al-Musakhkhar al-Ya'muri]] killed him in [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-banu-fazara-and-umm-qirfa|the second raid of Zayd ibn Haritha on the Banu Fazara]] in the Wadi al-Qura, and Qays composed verses on his killing, in which he recalls his overpowering of the son of Badr and his flight. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/265} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266} His son Abdallah ibn Mas'ada was taken captive and brought to the Prophet with the daughter of Umm Qirfa. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}

## Notes on the report
- Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage in full, and does not say what became of his son after his arrival. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}
