## Summary
[[person/umm-ziyad-al-ashjaiyya|Umm Ziyad]] al-Ashja'iyya. Rafi' ibn Salama ibn Ziyad al-Ashja'i related her hadith from Hashraj ibn Ziyad al-Ashja'i from his grandmother, his father's mother {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2000}.

## Going out to Khaybar
She went out with the Prophet on the [[event/battle-of-khaybar|Khaybar campaign]], the sixth of six women; it reached the Prophet and he sent for them and said: "by whose leave did you women go out?" — they saw anger in his face — they said: "we went out with medicine to treat the wounded, handing arrows and pouring sawiq" — and in the hadith he allotted them shares of the dates. Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Abi Asim narrate it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2000}.
