## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Muktafi, son of the caliph [[person/al-muktafi-billah|al-Muktafi]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 194}

Ibn al-Athir relates that the caliph [[person/al-qahir-billah|al-Qahir]] sought him after the arrest of Mu'nis in 321 AH. He was caught, and a wall was built around him while he was still alive, until he died. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 194}
