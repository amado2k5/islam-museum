## Overview
Osman III was Ottoman sultan from 1168 to 1171 AH (1754–1757 CE). He came to the throne at fifty-eight years of age, received the oath of allegiance in the mosque of Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, and was congratulated by the ambassadors of Europe. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.313}

He reigned only three years, in which no foreign wars or disputes occurred. He attended to internal reform, issued orders forbidding what contravened the sharia, and put down the revolts and uprisings across the state, especially the Kurdish revolts; historians relate that he would go about in disguise at night to see to the condition of his subjects. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.313-314}
