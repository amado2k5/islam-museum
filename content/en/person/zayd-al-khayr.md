## Summary
Zayd al-Khayr is a figure related by Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy in his lesson: the Prophet asked him what he felt and he said that he loves good and the people of good. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:24:30} The Sheikh sees in this a sign that God wills good for the servant in the hereafter. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:25:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 9)
The Sheikh relates that Zayd al-Khayr, when the Prophet asked him what he felt, said that he loves good and loves the people of good, and that when good passes him by he yearns for it (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:24:30}. The Sheikh holds that loving good and its people is a sign that God wills good for the servant in the hereafter: whoever loves good and its people, seeks to see them, feels that Islam is still well, in the circles of knowledge, with scholars and with those who remind him of God, and yearns for good when it passes him, has in these some of the signs of submission to God (his view) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:25:00}.
