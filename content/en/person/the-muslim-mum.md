## Overview
"The Muslim Mum" is a content creator who publishes under that name alone on her YouTube channel. Her channel description presents her, in her own words, as "a marriage & parenting expert with a Masters degree in psychology" who "specialise[s] in providing solutions and advice for western Muslims". {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

Her channel had about 162,000 subscribers by platform figures in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

## Career and work
The channel's published titles show three strands of content: advice on marriage and parenting drawing on her stated expertise; reaction and commentary clips on public debates and media discussions about Islam; and live interview streams in which she hosts Muslim women to discuss their experiences and issues. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

Her recent live streams include interviews with women who moved from atheism, Christianity or Mormonism to Islam, and discussions of safeguarding Muslim women, women's rights, hijab and family questions; a guest's own upload title ("I Was Interviewed By Muslim Mum On Muslim Women's Rights, Dawah, and MORE!") shows her in the interviewer's role. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

She does not publish a legal name in any available source, so this record carries the name she publishes under; the museum does not attempt to uncover an identity she has not published. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of positions reported in the sources, not as the museum's judgement on them.

The channel addresses western Muslims generally and presents itself as defending traditional Islamic understandings of marriage and family against critics of Islam in western media, as its video titles show. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}

No available source names a school for her, so the museum records none; a channel addressed to Muslims generally is not an attestation of a legal or theological school. {cite:youtube-the-muslim-mum}
