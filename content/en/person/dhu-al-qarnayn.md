## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy cites Dhu al-Qarnayn in his teaching on empowerment, as an example of one whom God empowered and who was given the means and followed them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:12:00}

## What the Sheikh says of him
The Sheikh says that Dhu al-Qarnayn was given a means for everything and followed a means, and describes him as one who took the means, applied them and acted on them; the Sheikh points to [[passage/quran-18-84|the passage in Surat al-Kahf]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:18:30}

## His knowledge and empowerment
The Sheikh holds that Dhu al-Qarnayn had knowledge, languages and wide learning, so that he travelled to the east and the west of the earth, and that this was empowerment from God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:19:30}

## The barrier
The Sheikh says that a people complained to him of corruption in the land and asked him to set a barrier between them and those who were corrupting it, and that he built the barrier for them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:19:00}

## From the series Qissa wa 'Ibar
In the sixth episode Omar Abdelkafy tells the story of Dhu al-Qarnayn, whom God established in the land and gave a means to everything, and who followed the means; he reports the scholars' saying that the world was ruled by two believers, Dhu al-Qarnayn and Sulayman, and two unbelievers, Nimrod and Nebuchadnezzar. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:01:30} He says that the people who could hardly understand speech waited for him to build their barrier although the materials and the manpower were theirs, so he used their raw materials and set them to work; he draws from this a lesson for Arabs and Muslims today not to wait for foreign experts when they have the minds and the resources. This is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:07:30}
