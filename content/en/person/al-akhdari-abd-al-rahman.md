## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Saghir ibn Muhammad ibn Amir al-Akhdari, a Maliki jurist and versifier from the Maghrib. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His learning
He became known for verse works on jurisprudence and worship, which are taught in the traditional schools. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 920 AH (1514 CE) and died in 953 AH (1546 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates need an additional source to confirm them; they rest on the tradition alone.
