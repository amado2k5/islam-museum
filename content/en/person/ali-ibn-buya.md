## Overview
Imad al-Dawla Ali ibn Buya, the eldest of the Buyid brothers. Al-Suyuti relates that he was the son of a poor fisherman, and became a commander under Mardawij. Then he became independent, defeated the caliph's deputy Muhammad ibn Yaqut, and seized Fars in 322 AH. He agreed with al-Radi to hold what he held for eight hundred million dirhams a year, and then delayed paying the money. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.598,603}

## Title and death
Al-Mustakfi gave him the title "Imad al-Dawla" when his brother Ahmad entered Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.613} In 338 AH Mu'izz al-Dawla asked al-Muti' to add his brother Imad al-Dawla to him, and Imad al-Dawla died the same year. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.616}

Ibn al-Athir gives his death at Shiraz in Jumada al-Akhira 338 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.366}
