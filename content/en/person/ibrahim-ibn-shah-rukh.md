## Overview
Amirzada Ibrahim b. Shah Rukh, lord of Shiraz; he had also ruled Basra, a man of excellence and a very fine hand in calligraphy. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229}

## His death
He died in Ramadan 839. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229}
