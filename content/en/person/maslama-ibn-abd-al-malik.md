## Overview
Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, of the [[group/banu-umayya|Banu Umayya]], and son of [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His lands and campaigns
Al-Baladhuri says that the land of Baghras was his, and he endowed it for the way of goodness. The spring of al-Sulur and its lake were also his. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He set out as a raider against the Romans from the Jazira frontiers, and encamped at Balis. Its people, and the people of Nuwaylis, Qasirin, Ayidin and Safin, asked him to dig them a canal from the Euphrates, on condition that he should have a third of their produce after the sultan's tenth. He did so, and the canal was called "the Canal of Maslama". He also repaired the city wall. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Balis and its villages passed to his heirs until Abu al-Abbas Sulayman ibn Ali granted them out. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege of Constantinople
He took part as a commander in [[event/siege-of-constantinople-717|the siege of Constantinople]]. The book *The Umayyad State* says that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote to him to return after a siege that lasted about two years, and sent him horses and food. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.332}

## Governorships
Iraq was gathered to him in the time of [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik]], and then the governorships of the East were added to him along with Ibn Hubayra. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.435}

Al-Mas'udi relates that he reproached his brother Yazid when he secluded himself and turned to drinking and pleasure, and Yazid refrained for a time. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.185}
