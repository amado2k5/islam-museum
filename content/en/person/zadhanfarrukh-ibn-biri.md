## Overview
Zadhan Farrukh ibn Biri, secretary of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]]. al-Baladhuri reports that al-Hajjaj made him secretary when he was appointed over Iraq {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His relationship with Salih
Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman was with him, writing before him in Arabic and Persian, and Zadhan introduced him to al-Hajjaj. Then he told him that he was close to the amir, and that the amir had come to favour him and he could not be sure that the amir would not put him before him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
