## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Jalal al-Din Abu Abd Allah al-Mahalli the Shafi'i: born in Cairo on 1 Shawwal 791, he studied with al-Bulqini, Wali al-Din al-Iraqi, Ibn Hajar and others. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/39} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/40} His works include commentaries on Jam' al-Jawami', al-Waraqat and al-Minhaj, and an unfinished exegesis. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/40} He taught at the Barquqiyya and the Mu'ayyadiyya, declined the judgeship, and died on the morning of Saturday 1 Muharram 864. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/41}
