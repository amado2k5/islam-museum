## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Jalal al-Din Abu Abd Allah al-Mahalli the Shafi'i: born in Cairo on 1 Shawwal 791, he studied with al-Bulqini, Wali al-Din al-Iraqi, Ibn Hajar and others. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/39} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/40} His works include commentaries on Jam' al-Jawami', al-Waraqat and al-Minhaj, and an unfinished exegesis. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/40} He taught at the Barquqiyya and the Mu'ayyadiyya, declined the judgeship, and died on the morning of Saturday 1 Muharram 864. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 7/41}

## In the introduction to the Maktabat al-Iman edition of Tafsir al-Jalalayn
Muhammad Muhammad Tamir gives al-Mahalli's name as Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Mahalli the Shafi'i, titled Jalal al-Din, legal theorist, theologian, jurist and exegete, born in Egypt in 791 AH. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.4} He describes him as skilled in many disciplines, outstanding in intelligence and understanding, one who spoke the truth without fear of blame; he declined a judgeship when it was offered and taught at the Mu'ayyadiyya and Barquqiyya colleges. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.4} The works he names include commentaries on al-Waraqat in legal theory, on al-Minhaj in law and on Jam' al-Jawami' in legal theory, and a book on jihad. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.4} He died in 864 AH. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.4} He explained the second half of the Qur'an together with al-Fatiha, and [[person/al-suyuti|al-Suyuti]] completed what he had left, making the book [[work/tafsir-al-jalalayn|Tafsir al-Jalalayn]]; in the closing note of his completion al-Suyuti calls him "the shaykh, the imam, the learned, the very learned, the verifier". {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369}
