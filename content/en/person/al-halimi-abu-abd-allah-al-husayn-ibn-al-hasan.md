## Overview
Abu Abd Allah al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Halim al-Halimi, one of the notables of the fourth century AH. He was born in 338 AH (949 CE) and died in 403 AH (1012 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.182}
