## Overview
Yazid ibn al-Qa'qa' al-Makhzumi al-Madani, one of the ten well-known readers of the Qur'an, from the people of Medina {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
He was born in 40 AH and died in 130 AH, according to the encyclopedia {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
