## Overview
Ahmad ibn Salih ibn Abi al-Rijal al-Yamani, Safi al-Din, was a historian and man of letters of wide reading, and one of the scholars of the Zaydiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 133}. He was born in al-Ahnum in Yemen, grew up in Sanaa and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 133}. Al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 1029 AH (1620 CE) and 1092 AH (1681 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 133}.

## His works
Among his books, in manuscript, is "Matla' al-Budur wa-Majma' al-Buhur"; al-Zirikli notes that Ibn al-Muhibbi mentioned it and described it as a rich history in seven volumes, in which he recorded most of the scholars, imams and leaders of Yemen {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 133}. His other manuscript books include "I'lam al-Mawali bi-Kalam Sadatihi al-A'lam al-Mawali", "Taysir al-Shari'a" and "al-Riyad al-Nadiyya" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 133}.
