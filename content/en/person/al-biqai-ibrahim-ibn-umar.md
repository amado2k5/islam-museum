## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Umar ibn Hasan, Abu al-Hasan al-Biqai al-Ribati, a Shafi'i scholar and hadith scholar from al-Biqa. He was born in 809 AH (1406 CE) and died in 885 AH (1480 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His best-known book is "Nazm al-Durar fi Tanasub al-Ayat wa al-Suwar". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
