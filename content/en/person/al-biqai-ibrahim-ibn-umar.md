## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Umar ibn Hasan, Abu al-Hasan al-Biqai al-Ribati, a Shafi'i scholar and hadith scholar from al-Biqa. He was born in 809 AH (1406 CE) and died in 885 AH (1480 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His best-known book is "Nazm al-Durar fi Tanasub al-Ayat wa al-Suwar". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The dates come from one source and need a second.

## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Ibrahim ibn Umar ibn Hasan al-Rubat, Burhan al-Din Abu al-Hasan al-Biqa'i: born, by his own account, around 809 at Khirbat Ruha in the Biqa', he studied the readings with Ibn al-Jazari and attached himself to Ibn Hajar. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/101} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/102} His works include al-Munasabat on the coherence of the Qur'an, whose citations of the Torah and Gospel stirred a controversy that ended in a ruling affirming his Islam. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/106} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/107} He died in Damascus on the eve of Saturday 18 Rajab 885. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/107} The entry is severe towards its subject, and the edition's editor notes in a footnote that al-Sakhawi's pen wavers in his entries on some contemporaries. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 1/101}
