## Overview
Jabir ibn Yazid ibn al-Harith al-Ju'fi, Abu Abd Allah, was a tabi'i and one of the jurists of the Shi'a, a native of Kufa; he is placed here in the Shi'i frame as the entry itself places him. The entry dates his death to 128 AH, equal to 745 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 99}.

## Career
He transmitted widely and had abundant knowledge of religion {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 99}.

## What was said of him
Some men of hadith praised him, and others accused him of holding the doctrine of the return, the raj'a; al-Zirikli reports both without naming those who said them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 99}.

## Death
He died in Kufa in 128 AH, equal to 745 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 99}.
