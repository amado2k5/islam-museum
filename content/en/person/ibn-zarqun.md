## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'id ibn Ahmad al-Ansari, Abu Abd Allah, known as Ibn Zarqun, was a Maliki jurist versed in hadith, Andalusi, born at Jerez and settled and died at Seville in 586 AH (1190 CE); al-Dhahabi said he was the musnid of al-Andalus in his time. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.138}

He held the judgeships of Silves and Ceuta, praised for his conduct and probity. He wrote Jawami' Anwar al-Muntaqa wa-l-Istidhkar of Ibn Abd al-Barr, a commentary on the Muwatta', and another work combining the collections of al-Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud al-Sijistani. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.138}
