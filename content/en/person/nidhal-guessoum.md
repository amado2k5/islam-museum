## Summary
Nidhal Guessoum is an Algerian astrophysicist holding a double position: observational astronomer and writer on the philosophy of Islam-science relations. He lives in the UAE, teaching at the American University of Sharjah. {cite:wikipedia-nidhal-guessoum}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Algeria in 1960, he studied physics then astronomy in the United States to a doctorate, working with space observatories including the INTEGRAL gamma-ray mission. {cite:wikipedia-nidhal-guessoum}

## Career and works
He has published research in gamma-ray astrophysics and cosmic sources, and worked on Islamic crescent-sighting and calendar questions using astronomical calculation. His book Islam’s Quantum Question examines the relation between the Islamic worldview and modern science, alongside wide-ranging science-communication work. {cite:wikipedia-nidhal-guessoum}

## Method and contribution
He defends a middle position accepting the results of modern science (including evolution in his public discussions) while preserving a religious meaning for the cosmos, earning him welcome from conciliationists and criticism from some conservatives; the museum attributes these positions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-nidhal-guessoum}
