## Overview
Abu Hashim Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib. Al-Suyuti relates a report of his meeting with [[person/muhammad-ibn-ali-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.418}

## The report
He went out to Syria and met Muhammad ibn Ali al-Abbasi. He said to him: "O cousin, I have a knowledge that I wish to pass to you, and you are not to let anyone know of it: this matter the people hope for is in you." Muhammad answered that he knew it, and asked that no one hear it from him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.418}

## Its significance for al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti holds that in this report lies the origin of the claim that the imamate was bequeathed from the family of Ali to the family of al-Abbas. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.418}

## Notes for researchers
- The report is from al-Suyuti alone; a second source is needed. The other views on this claim are not set out here.
