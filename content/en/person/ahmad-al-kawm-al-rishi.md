## Summary
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Ghulam Allah ibn Ahmad al-Hasib al-Kawm al-Rishi (786–836/1384–1432) took his name from Kawm al-Rish, a suburb of Cairo (today al-Zawiya al-Hamra), and served as timekeeper at the mosque of al-Mu'ayyad Shaykh near Bab Zuwayla. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 30}

## What the book reports
The book cites Ibn Hajar: he worked in astronomy, knew much of judicial astrology, solved *zij* tables and wrote almanacs, and was well known for this; he was past fifty when he died in 836 AH. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 30} His works include *Nuzhat al-nazir fi talkhis zij Ibn al-Shatir* and *Kifayat al-ta'lim fi wad' al-taqwim*. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 30}
