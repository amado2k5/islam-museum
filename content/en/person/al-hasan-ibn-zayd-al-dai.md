## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Zayd al-Da'i ila al-Haqq, the leader of the Alid call in Tabaristan. He carried on the call in 250 AH in al-Rayy and among the Daylam, and he was the first of the three of the Banu al-Hasan in the Tabaristan state. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.795}

## His story
He was succeeded by his brother al-Qa'im bi-al-Haqq Muhammad, who was killed in 288 AH, and then his grandson al-Mahdi al-Hasan ibn Zayd ibn al-Qa'im rose. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.795}

Al-Mas'udi says that in 253 AH he defeated Sulayman ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir and drove him out of Tabaristan. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.552}
