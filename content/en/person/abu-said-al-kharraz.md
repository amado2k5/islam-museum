## Overview
Abu Sa'id al-Kharraz, counted among those famed for spiritual striving, scrupulousness and watchfulness; he transmitted from Ibrahim ibn Bashshar the companion of Adham and others, and Ali ibn Muhammad al-Misri related from him. Of his sayings: 'when the eyes of the fearful weep, they have signed a covenant with God in their tears' — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 12 p. 280}
