## Overview
Abu Said al-Kharraz, Ahmad ibn Isa, one of the leading Sufis. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His Sufism
The encyclopaedia says that his Sufism was at the station of trust in God (tawakkul). It records his death in 299 AH (about 912 CE). This is an inherited report and needs a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
