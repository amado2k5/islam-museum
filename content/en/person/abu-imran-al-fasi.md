## Overview
Abu Imran al-Fasi, the shaykh of the Maliki school in Qayrawan in the fifth century, as Ibn Khaldun mentions in "al-Ibar". {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1645}

## The delegation of Kadala
He met the delegation of Kadala returning from the pilgrimage, who asked him to send them one of his pupils. He directed them to someone suitable, and wrote for them to Wajjaj ibn Zallu al-Lamti in Sijilmasa. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1645}

He charged Wajjaj to seek for them someone whose religion and jurisprudence they trusted. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1645}
