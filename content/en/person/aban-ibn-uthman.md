## Overview
Aban ibn Uthman ibn Affan, one of the jurists of Medina, who once served as its governor. He punished a man who clipped dirhams with thirty lashes and had him paraded among the people, because he had clipped them and mixed in hollow and counterfeit coins. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
