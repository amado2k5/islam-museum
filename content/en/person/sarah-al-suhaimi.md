## Summary
Sarah Al-Suhaimi is a Saudi investment banker, chair of the Saudi Tadawul Group — the holding company of the kingdom's stock exchange, the largest in the Middle East — and the first woman to lead both Saudi investment banking and the Saudi bourse. {cite:wikipedia-sarah-al-suhaimi}

## Career
A graduate of King Saud University in Riyadh with later executive education at Harvard, Al-Suhaimi spent her early career in asset management at Samba and JPMorgan's Saudi operations. In 2014 she became the first woman to head a Saudi investment bank as chief executive of NCB Capital — the securities arm of the National Commercial Bank (now SNB Capital) — running the kingdom's largest asset manager. {cite:wikipedia-sarah-al-suhaimi}

## Tadawul
In 2021 she was appointed chair of the Saudi Tadawul Group — parent of the exchange that lists Aramco and the market through which most regional equity value trades — the first woman to chair the Saudi bourse. The role placed her at the centre of the kingdom's capital-market opening under Vision 2030: foreign investor access, IPO pipelines and the shift to a holding-company structure for the exchange. {cite:muslim500-sarah-al-suhaimi}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as the most senior woman in Saudi finance: a career that broke two ceilings — the top job in Saudi investment banking and the chair of its exchange — inside a decade of rapid market reform. {cite:muslim500-sarah-al-suhaimi}
