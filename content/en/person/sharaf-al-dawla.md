## Overview
Son of Rukn al-Dawla. al-Suyuti reports that in the year 376 AH he marched against his brother Samsam al-Dawla, defeated him and blinded him, and the army inclined to him {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.631}.

## Baghdad
He came to Baghdad, and al-Ta'i rode out to him to congratulate him on the lands. He was given the sultanate by investiture, and then departed. His investiture was read out while al-Ta'i listened. In the year 378 AH he ordered the observation of the seven planets in their courses, as al-Ma'mun had done. He died in the year 379 AH and left the succession to his brother Abu Nasr {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.631}.
