## Overview
Ibrahim ibn al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, known by the kunya Abu Ishaq, son of [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] and brother of [[person/yazid-ibn-al-walid|Yazid ibn al-Walid]]. He was given the oath of allegiance to the caliphate after his brother's death, and was deposed about seventy nights later. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.410-411}

## The oath
Al-Suyuti says it was said that Yazid had designated him as successor, and it was said that he did not. He relays from Bard ibn Sinan that Qutn fabricated a document of designation in Yazid's name, had people witness it, and that Yazid designated no one. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.410-411}

## The deposition
He remained in the caliphate for seventy nights. He was deposed on Monday, fourteen nights into Safar 127 AH, when Marwan ibn Muhammad rose against him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.410-411}

Al-Suyuti relays from al-Mada'ini that his position was never settled: some greeted him as caliph, some as amir, and some refused to give him their allegiance. The motto of his seal was "Ibrahim trusts in God." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.410-411}

## Last years
He lived until 132 AH and was killed among those of the Umayyads who were killed in the battle of al-Saffah. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.410}
