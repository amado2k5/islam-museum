## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Ardabili al-Gharawi al-Ha'iri was a scholar of biographical literature. Al-Zirikli describes him as Imami (Twelver Shi'i) and as a native of Ardabil in Iran {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. He stayed for a time in Isfahan, took from al-Majlisi and read with him, and al-Majlisi gave him an ijaza in 1098 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. No year of birth is given; the last date in the entry is 1100 AH, and his death is placed after it, that is after 1689 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His works
His book "Jami' al-Ruwat" survives in his own hand in Tehran, written in 1100 AH. It is a large volume of biographies in which he arranged the names of narrators and of their fathers by letter; after the letter ya he gave the kunyas in order, and then the titles in the same way. He closed it with ten benefits, and in the eighth he mentions another book of his called "Tashih al-Asanid", an abridgement of which was printed at the end of the third volume of al-Mamaqani's "al-Rijal" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
