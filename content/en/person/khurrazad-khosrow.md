## Overview
A king of the Sasanian Persian kings, whom al-Tabari mentions under the heading "The Mention of the Reign of Khurrazad Khosrow". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.233}

The details of the years of his reign and his affairs have not yet been read in the source, and the page remains recorded under its title only. It needs an additional source.
