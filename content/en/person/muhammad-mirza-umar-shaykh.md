## Muhammad Mirza Umar Shaykh al-Timuri

Muhammad Mirza Umar Shaykh, a cousin of Timur Lang and ruler of Fars. His brother Iskandar Shah rose against him and defeated him. Muhammad was said to be very just and generous. Some of his close men inclined against him, and one of them killed him to win the favour of his brother Iskandar, who took over his brother's kingdom, so that his realm grew large. This was in 812 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 105}.
