## Overview
Quraysh ibn Badran ibn al-Muqallad al-Uqayli, emir of [[group/banu-uqayl|Banu Uqayl]] and lord of Mosul after [[person/qirwash-ibn-al-muqallad|Qirwash ibn al-Muqallad]] in 444 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.489-490}

Ibn al-Jawzi reports that he protected the lands of the Jazira from the Ghuzz, and opposed al-Basasiri when he rose against the caliph al-Qa'im. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.489-490}
