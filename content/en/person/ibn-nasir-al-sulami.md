## Overview
The trustworthy hafiz Abu'l-Fadl Ibn Nasir Muhammad ibn Nasir al-Salami of Baghdad, muhaddith of Iraq; born in 467, he took to hadith after mastering fiqh, and switched from the Shafi'i to the Hanbali school because of a dream in which the Prophet told him "keep to the school of Shaykh Abu Mansur al-Khayyat"; people used to say Ibn Nasir would come out Baghdad's philologist and Ibn al-Jawaliqi its muhaddith — it turned out the reverse. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Testimonies
Ibn al-Jawzi said: "he is the one who gave me my hadith auditions and from him I took all I took of the science — I read to him thirty years and profited from no one as I did from him"; Abu Musa al-Madini called him the leading hadith scholar of Baghdad in his time; he endowed his books as waqf, left worn clothes and three dinars, and died childless. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
