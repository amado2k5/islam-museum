# Leena Snoubar

## Summary
Leena Snoubar is an American modest-fashion and lifestyle creator based in Texas, known as With Love, Leena. Her YouTube channel, which carries her full name, publishes modest-fashion, beauty and family-life videos, and she also writes a blog of the same name. {cite:youtube-withloveleena} {cite:famousbirthdays-with-love-leena}

## Background
Her father is Palestinian and her mother is American. In a podcast interview she spoke about her journey in wearing hijab, dressing modestly and finding inner confidence, noting that she is the only woman in her family who wears a headscarf. Her first YouTube video, in November 2016, was a Q&A with her sisters. {cite:famousbirthdays-with-love-leena} {cite:mintarrow-leena-snoubar} {cite:youtube-withloveleena}

## Work and family
Her everyday hijab tutorials have been viewed millions of times, and a 2017 vlog documented her honeymoon in the Maldives. She is married to Omar Snoubar; they welcomed a daughter, Alaina Noor, in 2020 and a son, Jonah, in 2023, and the family lives in Texas. {cite:famousbirthdays-with-love-leena} {cite:youtube-withloveleena}
