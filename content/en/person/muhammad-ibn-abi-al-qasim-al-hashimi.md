## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim al-Hashimi, their client, one of those intimate with God's remembrance and known for answered prayer; he came near a hundred years. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 484 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 135 and the start of page 136. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135-136}

## His admonition to a tyrant
Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim, who had come near a hundred years, related that he admonished a tyrant, who had him seized and his hands and feet cut off, and he was carried to his place of worship. His brothers came to blame him; he said: do not console me, but congratulate me on what God has brought me. Then he said: my God, I have come to the station of desires and I look at wonders. My God, You show affection with Your favours to the one who harms You; how much more is Your affection toward one who is harmed for You. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135-136}
