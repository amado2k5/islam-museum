## Overview
Abd al-Rahim ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Shaybani, Abu al-Fadl Ibn al-Ukhuwwa, was a copyist of the Shafi'i jurists of Baghdad who died in 548 AH (1153 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.343}

He travelled to Khurasan, Nishapur and Tabaristan seeking hadith, lived forty years at Isfahan, and was swift in reading and writing; Ibn Shakir says he copied beyond counting, and he used to say 'I wrote a thousand volumes in my own hand'. He also had verse. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.343}
