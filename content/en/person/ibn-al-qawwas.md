## Overview
Ibn al-Qawwas, musnid of the age at Damascus; he heard in person from Ibn al-Huristani and Abu Ya'la b. Abi Luqma — their last transmitter — and a mashyakha was compiled for him; devout, good, humble, loving the transmission {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 773}.
