## Overview
Abd Ali ibn Nasir ibn Rahmah al-Huwayzi was counted by al-Zirikli among the leading poets of his age. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1053 AH, 1643 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 31}.

## Life
He attached himself to the rulers and governors of Basra and lived under their protection until he died. He composed well in Turkish and Persian, and was skilled in music and in fine songs {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 31}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists: a diwan of poetry; al-Mu'awwal fi sharh shawahid al-Mutawwal; Qatr al-ghamam; al-Uqud al-rafi'iyya fi l-sana'i' al-badi'iyya, in his own hand, in the library of the University of Tehran; and al-Sira al-mardiyya, a manuscript acquired by Muhammad al-Khal, judge of Sulaymaniyya in Iraq, who drew from it a treatise on the reports of Ali Pasha ibn Afrasiyab, described as amir of Basra between 1033 and 1053 AH. The treatise is printed under the title Tarikh al-imara al-Afrasiyabiyya aw halqa min tarikh al-Basra, as given in Majallat al-Majma' {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 31}.
