## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Dimyati, known as al-Banna, a linguist from Damietta in Egypt. Biographical works describe him as having grasped the Arabic language and mastered the Islamic sciences. They say his works did not exceed five books in different sciences. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His dates of life and the titles of his books are not recorded in the museum's data and need an additional source.
