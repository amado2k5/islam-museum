## Summary
Muhammad ibn Mustafa ibn Muhammad ibn Rahmat Allah, al-Ayyubi, al-Ansari, al-Dimashqi, Abu al-Barakat, known as al-Rahmati — a Hanafi jurist, a polymath of precise discernment between the explicit wording and its implication. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16247} He travelled to Medina and studied under its learned men, among them the scholar Ahmad Efendi, mufti of Medina, and Muhammad Efendi Mirghani, mufti of Mecca. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16247} He taught the qadi Iyad's Kitab al-Shifa in the Prophet's Mosque by order of the sultan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16247} His works include a gloss on al-Ala'i's Mukhtasar Sharh al-Tanwir, a gloss on al-Minh, and Sharh al-Tariq al-Salik ala Zubdat al-Manasik. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16247} The encyclopedia dates him 1176–1250 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16247}
