## Overview
Abu Abd al-Rahman Abd Allah ibn Maslama ibn Qa'nab al-Qa'nabi al-Madani, a memoriser, devout, proof, and a great transmitter of hadith from the people of Medina. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his death as 216 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
