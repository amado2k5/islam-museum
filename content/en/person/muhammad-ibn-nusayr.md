## Overview

Muhammad ibn Nusayr al-Namiri, eponym of the Nusayriyya. Al-Shahrastani transmits from al-Nawbakhti in the Milal that he claimed prophethood as sent by al-Hasan al-Askari, professing metempsychosis and the deification of Abu'l-Hasan {cite:milal-nihal-shahrastani}.

He is recorded there among the ghulat sects that grew within the followers of the ahl al-bayt.
