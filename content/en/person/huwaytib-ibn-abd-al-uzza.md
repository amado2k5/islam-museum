## Summary
A Qurashi notable of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]]; he witnessed the Hudaybiyya peace on his people's religion, then became Muslim in the year of Mecca's conquest — the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rrana]] in the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won over]] — and lived very long {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## In the Prophet's Sira
Huwaytib was among Quraysh's wise notables, present at the Hudaybiyya peace; then God opened his heart to Islam on the conquest day, and the Prophet gave him a hundred she-camels at al-Ji'rrana to gladden him and steady his faith {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}. He lived past a hundred twenty years and died in Medina in the caliphate of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
One of Quraysh's great wise men; he became Muslim at the conquest of Mecca and the Prophet honoured him with gifts at al-Ji'rrana — and he lived a long life in obedience to God and Islam.
