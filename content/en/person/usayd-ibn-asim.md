## Summary
Abu al-Husayn Usayd ibn Asim ibn Muhammad; he and his brother Muhammad ibn Ali followed the path of the companions of Sufyan al-Thawri in knowledge, worship and noble conduct. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 675, sequence 676, on page 394. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394}

## Their prayers and the answer
When care and sickness came down, people turned to their prayers and found an answer at once; they came to them from distant lands and asked for their prayers in their troubles, and saw the answer. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394}

## Their reports
He narrates through Husayn ibn Hafs, from Sufyan, from Yunus ibn Ubayd, from Shu'ayb, from Anas, a report about freeing Safiyya and making her freedom her dowry. He also narrates from Ismail ibn Umar, from Qays ibn Hammar al-Dhuhli (the name is unclear in the scan), from Atiyya, from Abu Sa'id a report that there is no religion for one who follows what is not the Book of God, or who obeys one who disobeys God. The wording is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394}
