## Summary
Jurd Bak, one of the greatest amirs of the Nuri and Salahi states; he was present at all the conquests — it was he who killed Shawar in Egypt and Ibn al-Khashshab at Aleppo — and he was the cavalier of Islam; he died in 594. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 796}
