## Overview
Amr ibn Mas'ada, one of the viziers of al-Ma'mun, who was greeted as vizier after the killing of al-Fadl ibn Sahl. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.399}

He died in 217 AH, and al-Ma'mun went after his wealth, though he did not go after the wealth of any other vizier. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.399}
