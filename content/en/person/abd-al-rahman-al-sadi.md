## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdallah ibn Imran al-Sa'di is described by al-Zirikli as a historian. He was born in Timbuktu in 1004 AH, 1595 CE, and died in 1066 AH, 1656 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His life
He travelled to Jenne, on the Niger river, and took the post of imam at the Sankore mosque. He travelled widely and held a number of posts, settled in the kingdom of Songhai, and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His work
He wrote Tarikh al-Sudan, which has been printed and translated into French {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
