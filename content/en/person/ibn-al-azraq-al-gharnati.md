## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad, Abu Abd Allah Shams al-Din al-Gharnati, a Maliki jurist and judge. He held the judgeship at Granada until the Christians took it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8505}
He then moved to Tlemcen and to the East, seeking help from the rulers for the king of Granada, and became chief judge at Jerusalem. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8505}

## Teachers and pupils
He studied with Ibn Fatuh, Abu Abd Allah al-Sarqusti, Abu al-Faraj Abd Allah al-Biqni, Ahmad ibn Abi Yahya al-Sharif al-Tilimsani and Abu Ishaq al-Abdusi; al-Hafiz Ibn Dawud was among those who studied with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8505}

## Works
His works include Shifa' al-Ghalil fi sharh Mukhtasar Khalil, Bada'i' al-Silk fi tabai' al-mulk, Rawdat al-A'lam bi-manzilat al-'arabiyya min 'ulum al-Islam, and al-Ibriz al-masbuk fi kayfiyat adab al-muluk. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8505}
