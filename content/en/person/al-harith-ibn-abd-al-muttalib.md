## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib, an uncle of the Prophet and one of the sons of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim]] whom al-Mubarakfuri's "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" counts in its section on the Prophet's family. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## Counting the sons of Abd al-Muttalib
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts ten sons for Abd al-Muttalib: al-Harith, al-Zubayr, Abu Talib, Abd Allah, Hamza, Abu Lahab, al-Ghaydaq, al-Muqawwim, Dirar and al-Abbas. It mentions other views that make them eleven by adding Qutham, or thirteen by adding Abd al-Ka'ba and Hajl. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.8-9} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/56-66}. He is thus a brother of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah]], the Prophet's father. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## What is not recorded here
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum says nothing here of his life, his religion or his death. Only what the book says is recorded here; what the biographical and genealogical books say needs a source to be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Abd al-Muttalib, the grandfather of the Prophet, had many sons and daughters, and al-Harith was one of them, so he was one of the uncles and aunts of the Prophet.
