## Overview
Ma'qil ibn Yasar al-Muzani of Basra, one of the people of the Pledge of Ridwan; Muhammad ibn Sa'd notes that no Companion bore the kunya Abu 'Ali and he was also titled Hisn al-Dawla. He died toward the end of Mu'awiya's caliphate. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3905-3906}
