## Overview
Ma'qil ibn Yasar, a transmitter from whom al-Baladhuri relates two reports. In the first, Umar consulted al-Hurmuzan on whether to begin with Isfahan or with Azerbaijan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.425}

## Nahavand
In the second, he witnessed the day of Nahavand, and came to al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin while he was at his last breath. He washed his face and gave him the good news of the victory. Al-Nu'man said: "Praise be to God; write to Umar." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.425}
