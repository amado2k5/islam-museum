## Overview
Ma'qil ibn Yasar al-Muzani of Basra, one of the people of the Pledge of Ridwan; no Companion is known to have borne the kunya Abu Ali other than him, and Ibn Sa'd also records the honorific "Fortress of the State" (hisn al-dawla). He died late in the caliphate of Mu'awiya. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3905-3906}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 8468: "Ma'qil ibn Yasar ibn Abd Allah ibn Ma'bar ibn Hiraq ibn Abi ibn Ka'b ibn Abd Thawr ibn Hudma ibn Latim ibn Uthman ibn Amr al-Muzani" — Muzayna being the mother of Uthman ibn Amr, so the sons were traced to her — and records that he became Muslim before al-Hudaybiyya and witnessed the Pledge of Ridwan; Ibn Hibban counts him a Companion. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1447}

## The canal of Ma'qil
Ibn Hajar cites al-Baghawi: it was he who dug the Canal of Ma'qil at Basra on Umar's orders, so it was named after him; he settled in Basra, built a house there and died there in the caliphate of Mu'awiya. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1447}
