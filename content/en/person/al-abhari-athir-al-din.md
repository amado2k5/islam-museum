## Overview
Al-Mufaddal ibn Umar ibn al-Mufaddal al-Samarqandi al-Abhari, one of the best-known scholars in Islamic thought in logic, astronomy and mathematics. His nisba "al-Abhari" refers to the town of Abhar, and the place of his birth is disputed. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.187-201}

## Death
He died in 663 AH (1265 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.187-201}
