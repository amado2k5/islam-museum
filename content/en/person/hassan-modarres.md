# Sayyid Hassan Modarres

Sayyid Hassan Modarres (1870–1937) was an Iranian Twelver Shia cleric and parliamentarian who led the Majlis opposition to Reza Shah. {cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-en}

## Formation

He was born at Sarabeh near Ardestan in about 1870, studied at the Isfahan seminary, and then spent seven years at Najaf under Akhund Khorasani and Sayyid Muhammad Kazim Yazdi, reaching the rank of ijtihad, before returning to teach in Isfahan. {cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-en}{cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-fa}

## Politics

He entered the second Majlis as a clerical deputy, took part in the wartime defence of Tehran, and became the most prominent opponent of the 1919 Anglo-Persian Agreement. When Reza Khan proposed a republic in 1924, Modarres organised the clerical opposition that defeated the proposal; he survived an assassination attempt in 1926, was kept out of the seventh Majlis, and was arrested and exiled to Khaf and then Kashmar. On 1 December 1937 he was killed there on Reza Shah's orders. {cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-en}

## Affiliation

His affiliation is recorded as Twelver Shia, documented: the English article's lead names him "an Iranian Twelver Shi'a cleric" and the Persian lead calls him a Shia mojtahed — two independent article texts — with his Isfahan and Najaf Usuli formation supplying the branch context. {cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-en}{cite:wikipedia-hassan-modarres-fa}
