## Summary
Mu'in al-Din Ibn Juwayni, the great sahib, son of Shaykh al-Shuyukh Sadr al-Din al-Juwayni. He held several offices and rose in the Egyptian ruler's regard, who put him over the army that besieged and took Damascus; he was appointed and dismissed, worked as deputy sultan — then his appointed end came suddenly after four months. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 226}
