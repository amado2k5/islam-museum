## Overview
Taj al-Dawla Tutush ibn [[person/alp-arslan|Alp Arslan]], brother of Sultan [[person/malikshah|Malik-Shah]]. al-Salabi reports that Malik-Shah assigned him the regions the Seljuks had taken in Syria in the year 470 AH, to pursue the conquest, and so he founded the state of the Seljuks of Syria {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.33}.
