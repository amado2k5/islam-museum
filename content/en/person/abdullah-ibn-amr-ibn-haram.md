Abu Jabir Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Haram al-Sulami al-Khazraji, chief of Banu Salama of the Ansar and father of the notable Companion Jabir ibn Abdullah. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He came to Mecca in the pilgrimage season with his people's delegation; the Muslim elders of the Ansar called him to Islam before the great Pledge of al-Aqaba and told him of the Prophet's affair, so he became Muslim, witnessed the second Pledge of al-Aqaba, and the Prophet chose him as one of the twelve leaders. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He witnessed Badr, then was martyred on the day of Uhud — the first Muslim casualty in the battle. Of his merit it is told that God gave him life and spoke to him face to face with no veil between, saying: "my servant, ask of me and I will give you"; he said: Lord, give me life again to be killed for You a second time. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy relates that Abdullah ibn Amr al-Ansari, the father of Jabir, spoke to his son on the day of battle about going before him; after he was martyred the Prophet comforted Jabir that God had never spoken to anyone except from behind a veil save his father, to whom He spoke face to face, and that he had wished to be sent back to the world to be killed again, and was told that it had already been decreed that the dead do not return to it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:08:30}
