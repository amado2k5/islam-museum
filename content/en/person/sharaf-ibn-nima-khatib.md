## Summary
Sharaf ibn Ni'ma, khatib and mufti of Damascus and shaykh of its Shafi'is, heard from al-Sakhawi and Ibn al-Salah and studied fiqh under Ibn 'Abd al-Salam, excelling in fiqh, usul and Arabic. He deputized in the judgeship for a time, taught at the Shamiyya and Ghazaliyya, wrote an unmatched proportioned script and authored a book on usul. He was among the ornaments of the age, with famous riddles — among them his riddle on the noria. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 742}
