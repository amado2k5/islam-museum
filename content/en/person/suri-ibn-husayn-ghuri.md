## Overview
Shuri ibn Husayn, king of Ghur. He came to the throne after his brother Muhammad ibn Husayn was killed, and he was crucified at the gate of [[place/ghazna|Ghazna]] in the year 544 AH {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## Revenge and Ghazna
Ibn Khaldun reports that his brother Muhammad had sided with Arslan in his strife with Bahram Shah, and Bahram imprisoned him and killed him. Shuri resolved on revenge, and marched to Ghazna in the year 543 AH {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

The army of Ghazna deserted him for Bahram Shah. He was defeated, and Bahram captured him. Bahram entered Ghazna in Muharram of the year 544 AH, and Shuri was crucified at its gate {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

His other brother, [[person/ala-al-din-husayn-ghuri|Ala' al-Din Husayn]], then took revenge for both of them. He is the lord of the [[polity/ghurid-dynasty|Ghurid state]] in the museum's data. The Common Era date of death is derived, and needs an additional source.
