## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Mustafa ibn Muhammad al-Thalith ibn Muhammad al-Thani Bayram, a Tunisian scholar of the Bayram family, who lived in the early period of the French protectorate. The encyclopaedia says that he was born around 1840 CE (1256 AH) and died around 1889 CE (1307 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The two dates are recorded on the basis of the encyclopaedia alone, and need an additional source.
