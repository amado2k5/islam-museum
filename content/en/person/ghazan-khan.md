## Overview
Ghazan ibn Arghun ibn Abaqa ibn Hulagu, king of the Tatars. Al-Suyuti mentions that he entered Islam in 694 AH, and that people rejoiced at this and Islam spread in his army. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 731}
