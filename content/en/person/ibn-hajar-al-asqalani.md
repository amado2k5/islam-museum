## Overview
The memoriser and shaykh of Islam Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, author of *Inba' al-Ghumr* (Informing the Ignorant of the Age). {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.765,774,781}

## In the History of the Caliphs
Al-Suyuti says that he composed his well-known poem in praise of the caliph al-Musta'in when he was given the oath of allegiance to the sultanate. Al-Suyuti relied on him for events up to 850 AH. He died in the days of al-Mustakfi II. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.765,774,781}

## His works
Haji Khalifa attributes to him [[work/ilam-bi-man-wala-misr|Al-I'lam bi-man Wali Misr]] (Informing about Those Who Governed Egypt), [[work/raf-al-isr-qudat-misr|Raf' al-Isr 'an Qudat Misr]] (Lifting the Burden from the Judges of Egypt) and [[work/al-isaba-fi-tamyiz-al-sahaba|al-Isaba fi Tamyiz al-Sahaba]] (The Hit: Distinguishing the Companions). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
