## Summary
The Spanish governor of Oran under Charles V {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 241-243}.

## The Sa'di negotiations
He received [[person/al-muzawwar-ibn-bu-ghannam|al-Muzawwar Bu Ghannam]]'s mediation letter early Rabi' I 963/January 1555 and sent a three-man embassy to Fez to agree with [[person/muhammad-al-shaykh-al-sadi|Muhammad al-Shaykh]] on a joint anti-Ottoman campaign; he then forwarded the report to [[person/philip-ii-spain|Prince Philip]] with a letter offering himself and his whole household as the sharif's hostages if the bargain required it {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 241-243}.
