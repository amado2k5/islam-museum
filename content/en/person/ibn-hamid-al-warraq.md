## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn Hamid ibn Ali ibn Marwan, Abu Abd Allah al-Warraq, head, imam and teacher of the Hanbalis in Baghdad, and author of major works in jurisprudence and its foundations. He died in the year 403 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.263-264}

## His imamate and his works
Leadership of the Hanbali school in teaching and in legal opinions came to him, and senior adherents were trained under him, foremost among them [[person/abu-yala-ibn-al-farra|al-Qadi Abu Yaʿla Ibn al-Farra]]. His large works include the "al-Jamiʿ," of about four hundred parts, "Tahdhib al-Ajwiba," and a commentary on the principles of religion. He was sound and accurate in giving legal opinions. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.263}

## His death on the road to the pilgrimage
He set out for the pilgrimage in the year 403 AH. When he had completed the rites and was returning with the pilgrims' caravan, death overtook him at Waqisa on the night of Friday, the eighteenth of Jumada al-Ula, and he was buried there, on the road to Mecca. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.264}
