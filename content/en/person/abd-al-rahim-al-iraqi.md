## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Husayn, Zayn al-Din Abu al-Fadl al-Iraqi, of Kurdish origin from Raznan: born on 21 Jumada al-Ula 725 at al-Manshiyya between Fustat and Cairo. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 4/171} He studied the Qur'an readings first and then turned to hadith on Ibn Jama'a's advice. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 4/172} His works include the Alfiyya on the hadith sciences with its commentary and al-Mughni on the hadiths of the Ihya'. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 4/173} He became judge and preacher of Medina on 12 Jumada al-Ula 788 and began his dictation sessions in Cairo in 795. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 4/174} He died on the eve of a Wednesday in Sha'ban 806 in Cairo. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 4/177}
