## Overview

Armaghan Khan is an American content creator of Pakistani descent, known as Captain Halal, who presents short comedy videos about the Muslim experience in which he himself plays multiple characters{cite:source/famous-birthdays-armaghan-khan}. He was born and raised in Ohio according to the Famous Birthdays database, and his TheCityCeleb biography gives his birth as 4 April 2000 in Canton, Ohio{cite:source/famous-birthdays-armaghan-khan}{cite:source/thecityceleb-armaghan-khan}. His YouTube channel introduces him in one line: "I Am A Halal Content Creator"{cite:source/youtube-captain-halal}.

## Early life and education

Famous Birthdays says he is of Pakistani descent, was born and raised in Ohio, and graduated from college with a bachelor of business administration in finance{cite:source/famous-birthdays-armaghan-khan}. TheCityCeleb's biography adds that his business education enabled him to handle digital marketing and brand collaborations{cite:source/thecityceleb-armaghan-khan}.

## Career and work

He began on TikTok and Instagram in May 2020 with short comedic videos centred on Muslim experiences, cultural insights and light-hearted skits{cite:source/famous-birthdays-armaghan-khan}{cite:source/thecityceleb-armaghan-khan}. His TikTok account (@captain.halal) passed one million followers and 54 million likes according to Famous Birthdays{cite:source/famous-birthdays-armaghan-khan}.

On YouTube he expanded his channel (@CaptainHalal) into longer formats: religious guessing games through Akinator, such as "Can Akinator Guess The Prophets", with millions of views; playing games "the halal way" in Fortnite, FIFA 23 and Among Us; reactions; Q&A sessions; and a travel vlog about a hair transplant in Turkey{cite:source/youtube-captain-halal}. The channel had about 1,190,000 subscribers in a platform count taken in October 2026; platform figures change constantly{cite:source/youtube-captain-halal}. His channel's keywords include his signature phrase "keep it halal"{cite:source/youtube-captain-halal}.
