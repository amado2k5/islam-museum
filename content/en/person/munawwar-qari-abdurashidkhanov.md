# Munawwar Qari Abdurashidkhanov

Munawwar Qari Abdurashidkhanov (1878–1931) was an Uzbek teacher, writer and Jadid leader in Turkestan, remembered as the founder of the new-method schools in Tashkent {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en}.

## Life and education

He was born at Tashkent in 1878, the youngest child in a family of Islamic scholars; the Uzbek article's infobox alone gives a day, 4 November 1878, carried by no other edition {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en} {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-uz}. He studied at the Yunuskhan madrasa in Tashkent and at the Mir-i-Arab madrasa in Bukhara (1885–1890), memorised the Qur'an with tajwid under Osman Domla, and worked as an imam and teacher {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en} {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-ru}.

## The new-method schools

In 1901, after the example of Ismail Gasprinsky, he converted his home into Tashkent's first new-method (usul-i jadid) school, and within two years a second school opened under his supervision {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en}. He wrote textbooks for the new schools, among them Adibi avval, Adibi soniy, Tazvid al-Qur'an, Yer yuzi and Khavoratnoma, and he translated works from Russian and Ottoman Turkish {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-uz}.

## Press and politics

He edited the newspapers Khurshid (1906) and Shuhrat (1907–1908) and worked on Tujjor, Sadoyi Turkiston and Najot; in February 1917 he chaired the Turkestan Muslim congress that founded Shuroi Islomiya, which he led in Tashkent {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en} {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-ru}. Under Soviet rule he worked in the education commissariat's academic centre and, from 1924, at the Samarkand museum {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-en}.

## Repression and death

Arrested in 1925 as an alleged leader of a pan-Turkist organisation, he was deported for three years, amnestied in 1927, rearrested in November 1929, and shot in Moscow — the Russian article and Wikidata give 23 May 1931, the Uzbek article 23 April 1931, a direct conflict carried in the record {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-ru} {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-uz} {cite:wikidata-munawwar-qari}. He was buried in the Vagankovo cemetery and rehabilitated in 1965 {cite:wikipedia-munawwar-qari-ru}.
