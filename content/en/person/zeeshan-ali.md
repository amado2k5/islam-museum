# Zeeshan Ali

## Summary
Zeeshan Ali is a British Muslim debater and presenter known as Smile2Jannah, the host of the Smile2Jannah YouTube channel. {cite:youtube-smile2jannah} The 5Pillars Blood Brothers podcast introduces him as "Zeeshan Ali aka Smile to Jannah", a Muslim YouTuber, influencer and debater. {cite:blood-brothers-zeeshan-ali} iERA's ReRooted podcast likewise describes him as the host of Smile2Jannah. {cite:rerooted-zeeshan-ali}

## Channel and debates
The channel began with light-hearted dawah sketches and later moved into news and current affairs alongside his Speakers Corner debates in Hyde Park, London; he also runs the S2J News website. {cite:blood-brothers-zeeshan-ali} His debate appearances, filmed at Speakers Corner and circulated widely by dawah channels, made him one of the recognisable faces of street dawah in Britain, and the channel approached two million subscribers in 2026. {cite:youtube-smile2jannah}
