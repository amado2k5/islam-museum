## Summary
Abu al-Abyad, a worshipper and ascetic, withdrawn from creatures and turned away from them. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 478 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 133 and the start of page 134. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/133-134}

## His letter to a brother
Abu al-Abyad wrote to one of his brothers: peace be upon you and the mercy of God; I praise God to you, there is no god but He. To proceed: you were charged from this world with only one soul; if you set it right, the corruption of others will not harm you. Know that you will not be safe from this world until you no longer care who eats from red and black. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}
