## Summary
Farajallah Ahmad Yusuf is a scholar of Islamic archaeology and numismatics who submitted a doctoral thesis to the Department of Islamic Archaeology of Cairo University's Faculty of Archaeology in 1418/1997, supervised by Hasan al-Basha and Ra'fat al-Nabarawi; the King Faisal Center in Riyadh published it as [[work/al-ayat-al-quraniyya-ala-al-maskukat|"Qur'anic Verses on Islamic Coins"]]. {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 1} {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 15}

## His work
He relied on libraries including the King Abdulaziz Foundation and King Abdulaziz Public Library in Riyadh, the Museum of Islamic Art library and Dar al-Kutub in Cairo, and the American Numismatic Society library in New York, and signed his preface in Riyadh on 19 Rajab 1422 (6 October 2001). {cite:yusuf-ayat-quraniyya-maskukat-2003 p. 16}
