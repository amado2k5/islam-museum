## Overview
Abu Muhammad Amr ibn Dinar al-Jumahi al-Makki al-Athram, a Successor and the leading shaykh of the Holy Sanctuary in his age, one of the great transmitters of hadith. He was born in 46 AH and died in 126 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The book says that he said he had not seen anyone like Tawus, in the context of the scholars of Basra and Mecca in the time of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.282}
