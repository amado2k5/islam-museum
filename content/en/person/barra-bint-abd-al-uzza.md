## Overview
Amina daughter of Wahb, the mother of the Prophet, so she is his maternal grandmother. She is Barra daughter of Abd al-Uzza ibn Uthman ibn Abd al-Dar ibn Qusayy, a Qurashi. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.533}
