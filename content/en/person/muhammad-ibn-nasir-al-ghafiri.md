## Overview
Muhammad ibn Nasir ibn Amir ibn Ramtha ibn Khamis al-Ghafiri was one of the imams of Oman. Al-Zirikli describes him as brave, of strong tribal following, and obeyed by his people both before and after his imamate. He gives his death as 1140 AH, 1727 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 121}.

## Life
He fought many engagements in the days of the imamate of Ya'rub ibn Bil'arab and others. Agreement on his own imamate was reached at Nizwa in 1137 AH; he then threw himself into the task and fought rebels and opponents, nomad and settled alike, and came close to securing the whole Omani realm {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 121}.

## Death
Al-Zirikli says that a bullet struck him in one of the battles at Sohar and that he died of it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 121}.
