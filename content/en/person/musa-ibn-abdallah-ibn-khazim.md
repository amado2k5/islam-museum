## Overview
Musa ibn Abdallah ibn Khazim al-Sulami. Al-Baladhuri says that when his father was killed, he came to the king of Tirmidh, who granted him protection and sheltered him and his company. Then he expelled the king from the city, seized it, and was in opposition to the authority. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When he was killed, the city passed to the governors; then its people rebelled, and [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
