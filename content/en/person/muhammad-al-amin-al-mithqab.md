## Overview
Muhammad al-Amin al-Mithqab, whose full name is Muhammad al-Amin al-Mithqab Hamin ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammadi al-Alawi. A Mauritanian scholar and teacher from a family of learning. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.304}

He was born in 1875 CE (1292 AH) and died in 1962 CE (1382 AH). These dates are traditional, from one source, and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.304}
