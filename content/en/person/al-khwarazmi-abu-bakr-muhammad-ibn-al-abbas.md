## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Abbas al-Khwarazmi. He was born in 323 AH (935 CE) in Khwarazm, travelled the lands seeking knowledge and wealth, and died in 382 AH (993 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
