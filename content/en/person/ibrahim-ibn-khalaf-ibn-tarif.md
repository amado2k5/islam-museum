## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Khalaf ibn Tarif, of [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]], was the man who led the fighting against [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] among the Banu Tarif and other Yemenis who backed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} He met al-Hadi at [[place/al-rahba-sanaa|al-Rahba]] with dense forces while Abd al-Hakim ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur was in Sana'a; they fought, al-Hadi prevailed, routed them and killed a number, and the last of them reached a place the text reads as Zahr, to which the editor's footnote refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} See [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288]].
