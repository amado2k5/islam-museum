## Overview
Al-Qa'qa' ibn Yazid, one of those who sat at night with Ibn Shubruma, Fadl, al-Harith al-Ukli and al-Mughira discussing jurisprudence. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 45}

## A question of the name
The editor of the edition notes that some copies and another edition read "al-Qam'aqa' ibn Hakim", and that al-Qam'aqa' ibn Hakim was from the people of Medina. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 44}

## Notes for researchers
- The identity of the man, whether "al-Qa'qa' ibn Yazid" or "al-Qam'aqa' ibn Hakim", is not settled. The page gives no dates and it needs an additional source.
