## Overview
Najm al-Din Ahmad ibn Isma'il ibn Sa'id al-Shaybani al-Jazari, a secretary from the lineage of Ibn al-Athir. He was known for the art of epistolary composition, and worked in the chanceries of his age in [[place/mosul|Mosul]]. He died in 737 AH (1336 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
