## Overview
Al-Hasan b. 'Isa b. al-Muqtadir bi'llah b. Ja'far al-Mu'tadid, the amir Abu Muhammad al-'Abbasi; he transmitted from his tutor Ahmad al-Yashkuri and was a chief, pious, keeper of the caliphs' annals. He died in Sha'ban 440 at over ninety. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264}
