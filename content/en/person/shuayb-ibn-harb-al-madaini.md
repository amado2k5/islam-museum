## Summary
Abu Salih Shu'ayb ibn Harb al-Mada'ini, of the sons of Khurasan, heard Shu'ba, Sufyan al-Thawri and Zuhayr ibn Mu'awiya; Musa ibn Dawud, Yahya ibn Ayyub al-Muqabri and Ahmad ibn Hanbal transmitted from him. He was among those renowned for worship, righteousness and commanding right. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## The lawful morsel
Sari al-Saqati said: four in the world worked themselves over seeking the lawful so that nothing entered their bellies but the lawful — Wahb ibn al-Ward, Shu'ayb ibn Harb, Yusuf ibn Asbat and Sulayman al-Khawwas. He once called out to Harun al-Rashid: O Harun! You have wearied the community and wearied the favorites! Harun asked: what kept you from calling me by my name? He said: I call God by His names — O God, O Merciful; I do not call you by yours. He ordered him put out, and he was. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
