## Overview
Majd al-Din Ibn Taymiyya, the Hanbali shaykh al-islam, grandfather of Shaykh al-Islam Taqi al-Din Ibn Taymiyya; born at Harran where he memorised the Qur'an, he heard his uncle the khatib Fakhr al-Din and the hafiz 'Abd al-Qadir al-Ruhawi, journeyed to Baghdad in 603 with his cousin Sayf al-Din 'Abd al-Ghani and stayed six years, studied fiqh under Abu Bakr ibn Ghunayma and al-Fakhr Isma'il, and mastered Arabic, arithmetic and algebra. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Testimonies and works
Al-Dhahabi reports of his grandson that "his grandfather raised orphans"; Jamal al-Din ibn Malik told him "fiqh was made pliant for you as iron was made pliant for Dawud"; Ibn Rajab notes he sometimes gave the fatwa that a triple divorce uttered at once counts as one only; his works include al-Muntaqa of the legal hadiths, al-Muharrar in fiqh, Muntaha'l-ghaya, a sharh of the Hidaya, and the atraf of the hadiths of tafsir and ahkam. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
