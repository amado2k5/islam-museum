## Overview
Sa'id ibn Jubayr was killed on the orders of al-Hajjaj. al-Tabari reports his death in the year ninety-four AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.487}. Al-Shirazi, in Tabaqat al-Fuqaha', says that he died in the year ninety-five {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}. The difference is recorded in the museum's data and has not been settled.

## His last words
Al-Mas'udi narrates a dialogue between him and al-Hajjaj when al-Hajjaj took him captive. He laughed as he was turned to be killed, and prayed that God would not give al-Hajjaj power over anyone after him. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Hajjaj lived after him only fifteen nights {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.154-155}.
