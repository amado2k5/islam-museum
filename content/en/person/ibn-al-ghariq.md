## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abd al-Samad ibn the caliph al-Muhtadi bi-llah Muhammad ibn al-Wathiq al-Abbasi, Abu al-Husayn al-Khatib, known as Ibn al-Ghariq and Ibn al-Muhtadi bi-llah, was the sayyid and shaykh of the Abbasids in his time. He was called the monk of the Banu Hashim for his piety and devotion, and he is one of the trustworthy transmitters of hadith. He died at Baghdad in 465 AH (1074 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.276}

He wrote Kitab al-Fawa'id, also called al-Fawa'id al-Mukharraja min al-Usul, on hadith. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.276}
