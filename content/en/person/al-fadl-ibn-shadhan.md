## Overview
This is an Imami, that is Shi'i, scholar, and the entry is given as al-Zirikli states it: al-Fadl ibn Shadhan ibn al-Khalil, Abu Muhammad al-Azdi al-Nisaburi, a scholar of theology and one of the jurists of the Imamiyya; he died in 260 AH, 874 CE, and no birth year is given {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 145}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he has about 180 books, among them "al-Radd ala Ibn Karram", "al-Iman", "Mihnat al-Islam", "al-Radd ala al-Damigha al-Thanawiyya", "al-Radd ala al-Ghulat", "al-Tawhid" and "al-Radd ala al-Batiniyya wa al-Qaramita" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 145}.
