## Summary
Dr Zaidul Akbar (born 30 November 1977 in Jambi) is an Indonesian physician, Islamic preacher, consultant and practitioner of prophetic medicine. {cite:wikipedia-zaidul-akbar-id} He is known as the founder of Jurus Sehat Rasulullah, abbreviated JSR — "the Messenger's healthy method" — a system of health teaching drawn from prophetic medicine. {cite:wikipedia-zaidul-akbar-id}

## Education and work
He took his medical degree at the Faculty of Medicine of Universitas Diponegoro, graduating in 2003. {cite:wikipedia-zaidul-akbar-id} He was one of the founders of the Indonesian Cupping Association (PBI) and served as its general chairman for three terms, from 2012 to 2025, and he sits on the central board of the Indonesian Traditional Healers' Association (ASPETRI). {cite:wikipedia-zaidul-akbar-id} He has written a book titled Jurus Sehat Rasulullah. {cite:wikipedia-zaidul-akbar-id}
