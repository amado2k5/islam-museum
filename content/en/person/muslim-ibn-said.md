## Overview
Muslim ibn Said, an Umayyad governor whom Umar ibn Hubayra appointed over Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Khurasan
He made peace with Afshin over six thousand heads and handed over his fortress to him. When he heard of the governorship of Asad ibn Abdallah, he raided Farghana, cut down its trees and ruined its buildings. Khaqan of the Turks came down on him, and he moved on, marching three stages in a day until his animals tired, and the Turks snatched at the edges of his army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
