## Summary
Miskin ibn Ubayd al-Sufi kept company with the followers of Ibrahim ibn Adham and took his path in tawhid and asceticism. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 487 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 136 and 137. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/136}

## Three kinds of asceticism
Miskin relates from al-Mutawakkil ibn al-Husayn al-Abid that Ibrahim ibn Adham said: asceticism has three kinds, obligatory asceticism, supererogatory asceticism and asceticism of safety. Obligatory asceticism is abstention from the forbidden; supererogatory asceticism is abstention from the permissible; asceticism of safety is abstention from doubtful matters. The report reaches Abu Nu'aym through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Barjalani, and it is transmitted onward by Abu Bakr ibn Abi al-Dunya. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/137}
