## Overview
Sulayman ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Sulayman al-Tamimi al-Najdi was a brother of Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, the leader of the reform movement. Al-Zirikli gives his death as about 1210 AH, about 1795 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 130}.

## His stand on his brother's call
Al-Zirikli says he opposed his brother in the call and wrote treatises on the matter, among them al-Radd ala man Kaffara al-Muslimin bi-Sabab al-Nadhr li-Ghayr Allah, in manuscript among the endowment libraries of Baghdad under number 6805 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 130}. He then says that Sulayman returned and expressed regret, and reports from Ali Jawad al-Tahir that he has a printed treatise on this {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 130}.
