## Overview
A Persian commander charged with the palace of Shapur, which in al-Baladhuri's time was known as the Palace of Isa ibn Ali. He went out against al-Nasir and the Muslims, who fought him and defeated him, and his companions fled to Ayn al-Tamr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.348}
