## Overview
Al-'Anbari al-Ifriqi, transmitter from Anas b. 'Iyad; he died at 'Aqsa in 259 AH. Abu Sa'id b. Yunus said: it is said this city never rains, its provisions coming from elsewhere, and there is great harshness in its people {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 106}.
