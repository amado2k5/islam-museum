## Overview
Abu Bakr Ibn Quzman, the zajjal (vernacular poet) of Córdoba. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.28}

## Ibn al-Khatib's view
Al-Maqqari relays the words of Lisan al-Din Ibn al-Khatib about him: he was unique in literature, wit and renown. In his view Ibn Quzman reached a level in zajal that no one else reached, so that he was its miraculous sign and its master horseman. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.28}

## Patronage
The source says that al-Mutawakkil ala Allah took him into his company and clothed him in his garments. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.28} The name also appears with [[place/cordoba|Córdoba]].
