## Overview
Al-Hakam ibn Utayba al-Kindi, a jurist of Kufa. Al-Mas'udi says he died in 115 AH, and adds that it was said Ata ibn Abi Rabah died in the same year. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.192}

Al-Shirazi, in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha", says he died in 115 AH. The editor notes that the name is printed as "Utayba", and that another edition prints it "Uyayna", which he takes to be an error. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
