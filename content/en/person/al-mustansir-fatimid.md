## Overview
Al-Mustansir billah Ma'add ibn al-Zahir, the eighth of the rulers of the Ubaydid state in al-Suyuti's phrasing, that is the Fatimid state. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.643-644}

## Length of his rule
He was installed in 428 AH at the age of seven, and remained in the caliphate sixty years and four months. Al-Dhahabi, as al-Suyuti quotes him, says he knew of no one in Islam, caliph or sultan, who remained that long. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.643-644}

## The famine and al-Basasiri
In his days there was famine in Egypt, the like of which had not been known since the time of Joseph. It lasted seven years, until people ate one another. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.643-644}

He was prayed for in the Mansur mosque in Baghdad in 450 AH, when al-Basasiri took it with his money. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.643-644}
