## Summary
Umar ibn Muhammad, Abu Ali and Abu Hafs, al-Rajraji al-Fasi, a Maliki jurist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15817} Ibn Ghazi called him a righteous shaykh whose learning and piety were agreed; al-Sakhawi called him the imam of the Andalusian mosque in Fes, known for asceticism and scrupulousness and foremost in fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15817} He studied with Abu Imran al-Abdusi, al-Qabbab and al-Wanghili, and Ibn al-Khatib al-Qasantini and Ibn Allal al-Masmudi studied with him.
