## Overview
Abu'l-Mahasin al-Ruyani, shaykh of the Shafi'is and the Shafi'i of his time; Ibn Qadi Shuhba said he held eminence, leadership and full acceptance with kings, and mastered the madhhab until he would say: "were al-Shafi'i's books to burn I would dictate them from memory"; he held the judgeship of Tabaristan and built a madrasa at Amol. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 484}

## His martyrdom
Among his works: al-Bahr, al-Kafi, al-Hilya, al-Mubtadi and Haqiqat al-qawlayn wa'l-wajhayn; he was martyred in Amol's congregational mosque just after finishing a dictation, on Friday 11 Muharram 502 — a Batini killed him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 484}
