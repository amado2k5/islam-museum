## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Hasan ibn Shihab al-Din al-Shahrani al-Shahrazuri al-Kurani, Burhan al-Din, is described by al-Zirikli as a mujtahid among the Shafi'i jurists and a scholar of hadith. He was born in 1025 AH, 1616 CE, and died in 1101 AH, 1690 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## His life
He was born at Shahran, in the district of Shahrazur in the Kurdish mountains, heard hadith in Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz, settled in Medina, died there and was buried at al-Baqi'. Besides his knowledge of Arabic he was fluent in Persian and Turkish {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## His works
It is said that his books number more than eighty {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. They include Ithaf al-Khalaf bi-Tahqiq Madhhab al-Salaf, a manuscript treatise in the Aydarus al-Habashi library at al-Ghurfa in Hadramawt, with two other manuscripts of his in the same place, al-Ta'rif bi-Tahqiq al-Ta'lif and Jala' al-Anzar bi-Tahrir al-Jabr wa-l-Ikhtiyar; Imdad Dhawi al-Isti'dad li-Suluk Maslak al-Sadad, a manuscript in al-Zirikli's own possession; al-Amam li-Iqaz al-Himam, printed; and Lawami' al-La'al fi al-Arba'in al-'Awali {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.
