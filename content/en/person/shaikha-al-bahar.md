## Summary
Shaikha Al-Bahar is a Kuwaiti banker, Deputy Group CEO of the National Bank of Kuwait — the country's oldest and largest bank — and one of the most powerful women in Gulf finance. {cite:wikipedia-shaikha-al-bahar}

## Career
Educated at Kuwait University and in executive programmes at Harvard, Stanford and other institutions, Al-Bahar joined NBK and rose through private banking and investment management, leading the bank's investment arm (Watani Investment Company) before being named Deputy Group CEO in 2014 — responsible for strategy across NBK's private banking, investment and international operations. {cite:wikipedia-shaikha-al-bahar}

## NBK RISE
She founded NBK RISE, the bank's programme to develop women leaders inside its own ranks — widely cited as a first of its kind for a Gulf bank — and has argued in public for women's advancement in the region's financial sector, where Kuwaiti women have historically reached senior management earlier than elsewhere in the Gulf. {cite:muslim500-shaikha-al-bahar}

## Standing
Repeatedly ranked among the most powerful Arab businesswomen by Forbes Middle East and similar lists, she is profiled in the Muslim 500 as a leading figure of Gulf banking and of the region's slow but real opening of financial leadership to women. {cite:muslim500-shaikha-al-bahar}
