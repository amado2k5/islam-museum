## Overview
The sharif Abu Nasr al-Zaynabi al-Abbasi, the naqib of the Hashimites. Ibn al-Athir lists him in al-Kamil fi l-Ta'rikh among those who died in 479 AH, and gives no fuller name than the kunya and the nisba {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir vol. 8 p. 450}.

## His office and hadith
Ibn al-Athir calls him the naqib of the Hashimites and a well-known hadith transmitter with a high chain of transmission {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir vol. 8 p. 450}.
