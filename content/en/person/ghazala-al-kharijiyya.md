## Overview
Ghazala, wife of Shabib the Kharijite, a woman of outstanding courage and horsemanship; she vowed to enter the mosque of Kufa and pray two rak'as there reciting al-Baqara and Al Imran — she came to the mosque with seventy men and fulfilled her vow.
