## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn Yahya ibn Abdallah ibn al-Hasan, one of the Alids. Al-Mas'udi says that he went to Egypt and was sought, so he entered the Maghrib and joined the lands of Tahart al-Sufla. A multitude gathered to him, and he appeared among them with justice and uprightness, then died there, poisoned. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 330}
