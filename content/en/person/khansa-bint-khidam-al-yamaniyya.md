## Summary
Khansa' bint Khidam was an Arab woman of Yemen whom [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] places first among the devout women of [[place/yemen|Yemen]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]], stating that she is not the Companion of the same name ([[person/al-khansa-bint-khidham|Khansa' bint Khidham al-Ansariyya]]). {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}

## In Sifat al-Safwa
He reports from Hafs ibn Amr al-Ju'fi that she was a stately woman of great beauty who fasted for forty years until her skin clung to her bones, wept until she lost her sight, and stood in prayer until her legs gave way. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
[[person/tawus-ibn-kaysan|Tawus]] and [[person/wahb-ibn-munabbih|Wahb ibn Munabbih]] held her in high esteem; when eyes had closed she would call out in a mournful voice asking God to raise the obedient and fulfil His promise to them, and weeping was heard from the houses around her. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
The editor notes that *Taqrib al-Tahdhib* and *Tahdhib al-Tahdhib* know Khansa' bint Khidam as a well-known Companion, so the distinction between the two women rests on Ibn al-Jawzi's statement. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
