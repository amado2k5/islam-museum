## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Abd Allah ibn Nu'aym al-Nu'aymi al-Dimashqi al-Shafi'i was a learned travelling shaykh, the historian of Damascus and one of its muhaddiths, born Friday 12 Shawwal 845 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.10 p.209}

He kept the company of shaykh Ibrahim al-Naji, the scholar Zayn al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Khalil, Zayn al-Din Khattab al-Ghazzawi and Zayn al-Din Muflih ibn Abd Allah al-Habashi — from whom he took the Sufi cloak — transmitted from al-Badr Ibn Qadi Shuhbah and al-Shihab Ibn Qara, and read Burhan al-Biqa'i's work al-Aydhan under him with licence; his shaykhs were many, as he records in his histories. He wrote many books, among them al-Daris fi Tawarikh al-Madaris. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.10 p.209}
