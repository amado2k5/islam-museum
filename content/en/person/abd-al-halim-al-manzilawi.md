## Overview
Shaykh 'Abd al-Halim al-Manzilawi; he would beat the murids hard until some fell into death, then he would restore life to them and accept their repentance — generous, no one was denied his bounty. He died shortly after Sultan Qaytbay. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 592}
