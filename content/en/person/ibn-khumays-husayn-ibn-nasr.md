## Overview
Abu Abdallah al-Husayn ibn Nasr ibn Muhammad ibn Khumays al-Mawsili al-Juhani al-Ka'bi, a hadith scholar and jurist from Mosul, of the Banu Juhayna. He was born in 466 AH (1073 CE) and died in 552 AH (1157 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
