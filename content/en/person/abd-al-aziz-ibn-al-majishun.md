## Overview
'Abd al-'Aziz b. 'Abdallah b. Abi Salama al-Majishun, kunyat Abu 'Abdallah; nephew of the Medinan jurist al-Majishun. He died at Baghdad — al-Mahdi prayed over him — and was buried in the graves of Quraysh. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
