## Summary
Sa'id al-Shahid, veiled in iron and longing to see the glorious Benefactor. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 540, sequence 541, on pages 165 and 166. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/165} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}

## The raid and the verses
Abbas ibn Yusuf relates from Maysara the servant, who said: on one raid we made for the enemy, and beside me was a youth veiled in iron. He charged the enemy's right wing and broke it, charged their left and broke it, and charged the centre and broke it. Then he recited verses of longing for the gardens, asking the maidens of Paradise to stand aside, since they had no power over his killing or his speech, and that he longed for their Lord who knows what is hidden and what is public. He charged and fought and killed a number of them, and returned to his line. The enemy closed on him; he charged a second time and recited verses hoping that his striving and his quest would not be lost that day. He charged a third time and spoke to the everlasting maiden, asking her to listen and turn back, for she had no claim on them. He fought on until he was killed. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/165} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}
