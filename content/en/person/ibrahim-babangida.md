# Ibrahim Babangida

Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida (born 17 August 1941) is a Nigerian general and politician from Minna who ruled Nigeria as military president from 1985 to 1993 after his coup against Muhammadu Buhari, his rule ending in the crisis over the annulled June 1993 election. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en} {cite:wikidata-ibrahim-babangida}

## Early life and military career

Babangida was born in Minna on 17 August 1941 and received early Islamic education before attending primary school from 1950 to 1956, as the English article records. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en} He joined the army in 1962, fought in the Nigerian Civil War and rose through the ranks to become Chief of Army Staff (1984–1985). {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en}

## Military presidency

On 27 August 1985 Babangida led the coup that overthrew Buhari and became Nigeria's military president. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en} In 1986 Nigeria under him joined the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, a move welcomed in the Muslim-majority north and criticised by non-Muslims as an Islamisation agenda for a secular country; his deputy Ebitu Ukiwe, who opposed the decision, was removed. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en}

## The 1993 crisis and stepping aside

Babangida's regime supervised the election of 12 June 1993, which Moshood Abiola appeared to have won, and then annulled its results; the ensuing Third Republic crisis ended with Babangida stepping aside on 26 August 1993, handing power to an interim government under Ernest Shonekan. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-babangida-en}
