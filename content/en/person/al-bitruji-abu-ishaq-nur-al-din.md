## Overview
Abu Ishaq Nur al-Din al-Bitruji, one of the greatest Arab astronomers. He was of Andalusian origin, from the town of Bedroche near Cordoba, and was alive in the sixth century AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
