## Summary
'Ali al-Muttaqi b. Husam al-Din al-Hindi, then of Mecca; one of the working scholars and righteous servants of God, of great scrupulosity, piety and striving in worship and renunciation; he left many works and many graces, and died at Mecca after a long sojourn there. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 379}
