## Summary
Mohammed Abu-Ghazaleh is a Jordanian-Palestinian businessman best known as the chairman and chief executive of Fresh Del Monte Produce, the global fruit company he has led since 1996 — and as chairman of major Jordanian financial institutions. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-abu-ghazaleh}

## Formation
Born in 1942 in Palestine, Abu-Ghazaleh built his early career in banking and shipping-linked finance in the Middle East before moving into the fresh-produce business. He acquired control of Del Monte Fresh Produce in the 1990s — in a buyout that made him owner of one of the world's best-known fruit brands — and has run it since as chairman and CEO. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-abu-ghazaleh}

## Fresh Del Monte
Under his ownership the company, headquartered in Jordan-linked offices and listed in New York, grew into one of the world's largest vertically integrated producers and marketers of fresh fruit and vegetables — bananas, pineapples, melons and prepared foods — with farms, ships and distribution across Latin America, Asia, Europe and the Middle East. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-abu-ghazaleh}

## Jordanian roles
In Jordan he chairs or has chaired flagship institutions — reported in profiles to include banking and investment concerns — placing him among the most senior figures in the kingdom's private sector. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-abu-ghazaleh}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as a rare example of a Middle Eastern entrepreneur owning and running a global consumer brand — a Palestinian refugee's son who became the steward of one of the world's most recognised names in food. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-abu-ghazaleh}
