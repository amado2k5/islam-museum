## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Khwarazmi al-Bafi, a jurist, man of letters, poet, graceful writer and generous man. He was the companion of [[person/abd-al-aziz-al-daraki|al-Daraki]]. He taught in Baghdad after al-Daraki. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 64}

## Death
Al-Shirazi says he died in 398 AH. The Gregorian date is derived. The date needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 64}

## Note
He is not Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Abbas al-Khwarazmi; the museum's records treat these as two different people.
