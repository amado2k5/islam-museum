## Summary
Asim Ijaz Khwaja is a British-born Pakistani-American economist who serves as the Sumitomo-FASID Professor of International Finance and Development at Harvard Kennedy School, and as director of the Center for International Development (CID) at Harvard University. His research covers development economics, corporate finance, education, political economy, institutions, mechanism design and contract theory, and industrial organisation. {cite:wikipedia-asim-khwaja-en} {cite:wikidata-asim-khwaja}

## Early life and education
Khwaja was born in London, United Kingdom, to Ijaz Ahmed Khawaja, an ophthalmologist, and Zahida Khawaja, a gynaecologist, both of Kashmiri descent. At the age of two he moved with his parents to Kano, Nigeria, where he lived for eight years; the family then moved to Pakistan, where he attended Aitchison College. He obtained a bachelor's degree in economics and mathematics with computer science from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 1995, and his PhD in economics from Harvard University in 2001. {cite:wikipedia-asim-khwaja-en}

## Career
Khwaja joined Harvard University in June 2001 as an assistant professor, was promoted to associate professor in June 2006 and to professor in January 2010, and has been a visiting faculty member at Yale University's Economic Growth Center and at the Haas School of Business of the University of California, Berkeley. In October 2005 he released RISE-PAK, a searchable database for earthquake relief in Pakistan. He is a co-founder of the Center for Economic Research in Pakistan (CERP) and co-director of Harvard Evidence for Policy Design. {cite:wikipedia-asim-khwaja-en}

In 2018 Khwaja was appointed to Pakistan's Economic Advisory Council. He resigned shortly afterwards, in protest, when his colleague Atif Mian was forced off the council because of Mian's Ahmadiyya faith. {cite:wikipedia-asim-khwaja-en}

## Personal life
Khwaja is a citizen of Pakistan, the United Kingdom and the United States. He is married to Sehr Jalal and has three children. {cite:wikipedia-asim-khwaja-en}
