## Summary
Ragheb al-Sirjani is an Egyptian physician and writer who combined medicine with popular history. He founded the Qissat al-Islam platform, now among the most widely used Arabic sites for accessible Islamic history. {cite:wikipedia-ragheb-al-sirjani}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Egypt in 1964, he graduated from Cairo University’s Faculty of Medicine (Kasr Al-Ainy), specialised in urological surgery and became a professor, alongside deep self-directed study of Islamic history. {cite:wikipedia-ragheb-al-sirjani}

## Career and works
He founded the Qissat al-Islam website in 2008 to publish readable and audio materials on Islamic history. His books include The Story of Andalusia and works on the Prophet’s biography and Ottoman history, plus widely viewed lectures and programmes. {cite:wikipedia-ragheb-al-sirjani}

## Method and contribution
His narrative, simplifying style addresses the general reader without academic apparatus, making his books an entry point for millions, though specialists criticise some of his historical generalisations; the museum attributes these judgements to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-ragheb-al-sirjani}
