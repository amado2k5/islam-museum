## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir ibn Dawud al-Baghdadi al-Baladhuri, an Abbasid historian and poet, author of "Futuh al-Buldan" and "Ansab al-Ashraf". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.3-7}

## His kunya and death
The edition by the Ma'arif foundation gives his kunya in disagreement: some say Abu Bakr, some Abu Ja'far, some Abu al-Hasan. His date of birth is not known precisely. Narrators differ on his death: some say he died in the caliphate of al-Mu'tamid, others insist he lived to see al-Mu'tadid and lived in his days, and some scholars put his death in 279 AH. The Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.3-7}

## His teachers and transmitters
The preface to the edition says he heard from Abd Allah ibn Salih al-Ijli, Abu al-Hasan al-Mada'ini, Hisham ibn Ammar, Abu Ubayda, Ali ibn al-Madini, and Muhammad ibn Sa'd, secretary of al-Waqidi, and others. Among those who transmitted from him were Muhammad ibn al-Nadim, Ahmad ibn Ammar, Ja'far ibn Qudama and Ya'qub ibn Nu'aym. Yaqut included him in "Mu'jam al-Udaba" and Ibn Asakir in "Tarikh Dimashq". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.3-7}

## An opinion of him
The preface quotes Ubayd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Tahir as saying that he had good books, and that he was the author of "Kitab al-Buldan", which he composed and composed well. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.3-7}
