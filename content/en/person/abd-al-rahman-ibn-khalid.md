## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Khalid ibn al-Walid al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi is described by al-Zirikli as a fierce commander among the Followers {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 93}.

## His life
He took part in the conquests of al-Sham and settled there, was at Siffin with Mu'awiya, and campaigned against the Byzantines in his days, fighting battles there; he returned to al-Sham, grew in standing, and moved to Homs {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 93}.

## His death
Al-Zirikli reports that Mu'awiya feared him and instructed Ibn Uthal the physician, of the people of Homs, to slip poison into a drink for him, from which he died at Homs {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 93}. This is the account of al-A'lam, with its own references; the museum gives it as his and does not affirm it {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 93}.
