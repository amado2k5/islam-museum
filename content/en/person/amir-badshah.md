## Summary
Muhammad Amin ibn Mahmud al-Bukhari, known as Amir Badshah, an exegete and a scholar of usul al-fiqh; the entry gives his death as 987 AH. His works include Taysir al-Tahrir, a commentary on the sura of Victory, a treatise that a pilgrimage accepted removes sins, and a treatise on the particle qad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7513}
