## Summary
Hisham ibn Amir ibn Umayya al-Ansari, a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8523}
It is said that his name was Shihab, and the Prophet changed it to Hisham. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8523}
He narrated from the Prophet; his son Sa'd and Humayd ibn Hilal narrated from him. The source is truncated at this point. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8523}
