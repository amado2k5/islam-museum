## Overview
Al-Mundhir, titled al-Ma'rur, the last of the kings of the [[group/banu-lakhm|Banu Lakhm]] over [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]]. Kisra appointed a Persian governor over al-Hira after [[person/iyas-ibn-qabisa|Iyas ibn Qabisa]], and in 632 CE the kingship returned to the Banu Lakhm, and al-Mundhir al-Ma'rur was appointed from among them. His rule did not exceed eight months before Khalid ibn al-Walid came against him with the armies of the Muslims. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/29-32}

The conquest of al-Hira and Khalid's agreement with its people are in [[event/conquest-of-al-hira|the conquest of al-Hira]].
