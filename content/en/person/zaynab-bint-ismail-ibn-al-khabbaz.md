## Summary
Zaynab bint Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Khabbaz, also called Amat al-Aziz, was an accomplished hadith transmitter born on the last day of Jumada al-Ula 659 AH; [[person/umar-rida-kahhala|Kahhala]] gives her entry in [[work/alam-al-nisa-kahhala|A'lam al-Nisa']] from Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina and manuscript copies of the parts she heard. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.58} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.60}

## What she heard
She learned from the great scholars of her day; she heard the hadith of Sabur, al-Ajurri's Forty, the hadith of Ayyub, parts of the hadith of al-Baghawi, Ibn Sa'id, Ibn Abi Shayba and al-Mukhallis, al-Khatib's al-Rihla, al-Khara'iti's al-Qana'a, "The Merit of Horses" from al-Kamal ibn Abd, a selection from Musa ibn Uqba's Maghazi, the Majalis of al-Makhladi and Abu Khaythama's al-Ilm. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.58} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.59}

## What was read to her
Works read to her included the hadith of Ibn al-Akfani, the Hundred of al-Furawi, "The Merits of Ramadan" heard from Ahmad ibn Abd al-Da'im, two parts of al-Hasan al-Jawhari's Amali heard from Ibn al-Bukhari, "The Killing of Uthman" in Ahmad ibn Kamil's transmission, a part of "The Merits of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq", al-Mahamili's al-Du'a', the first part of Muhammad ibn al-Ma'mun's Fawa'id heard from [[person/zaynab-bint-makki|Zaynab bint Makki]] and others, the hadith of Abu Tahir [[person/al-silafi|al-Silafi]], and hadiths selected by [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni]]. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.59}

## For young visitors
Zaynab was a hadith scholar of Syria who heard many books from scholars, and later students came to read them to her.
