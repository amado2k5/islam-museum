## Overview
Udhayna ibn al-Sumayda' al-Amaliqi, one of the Amalaqa whom the Byzantines made rulers over the eastern and western parts of Syria and al-Jazira. al-A'sha mentions him in a line of verse {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}.

## Succession
He was succeeded by Hassan ibn Udhayna, then by Amr ibn Tarab, who is said to be the one known by his mother, al-Zaba'. There were wars between him and Jadhima al-Abrash, and Jadhima killed him, according to al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}.
