## Summary
Talha, son of [[person/tahir-ibn-al-husayn|Tahir ibn al-Husayn]] al-Khuza'i, was emir of [[place/khurasan|Khurasan]] and son of its emir, according to his entry in the first edition of al-A'lam. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 50}

## His governorship
Al-Zirikli says the Abbasid caliph [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] appointed him over Khurasan after his father Tahir died in 207 AH, and that he held it until his death; he describes him as generous and sensible. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 50}
