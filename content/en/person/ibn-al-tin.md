## Overview
Abd al-Wahid ibn al-Tin al-Safaqusi, a Maliki jurist, hadith scholar and exegete of the Maghrib (d. 611 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3478}

## Works
His work is al-Khabar al-fasih fi sharh al-Bukhari al-sahih, on which Ibn Hajar relied. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3478}
