## Summary
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahim, Abu Zur'a Wali al-Din Ibn al-Iraqi, one of the leading Shafi'i scholars in Egypt in his time, born and died in Cairo. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2050}

## Works
His works include 'al-Bayan wa-al-tawdih' on those whom the Sahih includes but who have been touched by some criticism, and 'Akhbar al-mudallisin'; he died in 826 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2050}
