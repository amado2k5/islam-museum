## Overview
The amir of Mecca al-Zayn Abu Zuhayr Barakat ibn al-Badr Abi al-Ma'ali Hasan ibn Ajlan ibn Rumaythah is listed by Ibn al-Imad among the deaths of 859 AH, about 1455 CE (derived conversion), and he says Barakat did not complete sixty years {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 430}.

## The year of his death
Ibn al-Imad records in the same year that a great flood struck Mecca and entered the Haram until it came near the Black Stone {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 430}.
