## Overview
Husayn al-Kurdi, commander of the Mamluk fleet sent by Sultan Qansuh al-Ghawri to repel Portuguese aggression in the Arabian Sea and the Indian Ocean. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.195}

## The expedition
He led thirteen ships carrying one thousand five hundred men. He reached the island of Diu, then Chaul. He met the Portuguese fleet under Lourenço de Almeida in 914 AH / 1508 CE, and victory was his, as al-Salabi reports. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.195}

## Diu
The Portuguese reinforced their forces and came back to the attack, and the Mamluk fleet was defeated in the battle of Diu in 915 AH / 1509 CE. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.195}
