## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim ibn Muhammad al-Maghili al-Tilimsani was an exegete and jurist from Tlemcen. Al-Zirikli places his death in Tuwat and prints it as 909 AH, 1503 CE, with no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 216}. His data card carries 1505 CE; this text alone does not explain the difference between the two dates.

## Travels and activity
Al-Zirikli says he became known for his opposition to the Jews and for pulling down their places of worship in Tuwat, near Tlemcen, and that he travelled to the Sudan and the land of Takrur to spread the rulings and rules of the shari'a; he died in Tuwat {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 216}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books: al-Badr al-munir fi 'ulum al-tafsir; al-Ta'rif fima yajib 'ala al-muluk, in manuscript, which he thinks may be the treatise called Taj al-din fima yajib 'ala al-muluk wa-l-salatin, which is printed; Ahkam ahl al-dhimma, in manuscript; a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Khalil in Maliki law; Miftah al-nazar on the science of hadith; and Minah al-Wahhab, in manuscript, a versified work on logic on which he wrote the commentary Imnah al-ahbab min Minah al-Wahhab, held in Dar al-Kutub {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 216}. He also wrote verse, including a poem in answer to the Burda {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 216}.
