## Overview
Sulayman, son of [[person/dawud|Dawud]]. al-Bukhari records a hadith in which he said that he would go round a hundred women, each of whom would bear a boy who would fight in the way of God {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5242}.

## In the hadith
al-Bukhari reports that the king said to him: "Say, if God wills", but he did not say it. The Prophet said in the hadith: "If he had said, if God wills, he would not have failed, and it would have been more hopeful for his need" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5242}.

The data classes him under the role of "prophetic figure", with a source in the Qur'an and al-Bukhari. Other details of his life need an additional source.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
Omar Abdelkafy holds, 'by his little knowledge' as he puts it, that Sulayman was among the most severely tested by office, standing, glory and wealth, and that in the end he said: this is of my Lord's bounty, to test me whether I give thanks or am ungrateful. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:02:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh says Sulayman was a prophet, messenger, king and 'billionaire', the wind blowing gently at his command, devils working for him as builders and divers, birds shading him, and yet the family of Dawud were commanded to work in gratitude; he cites this for the dignity of work. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep20 00:06:30}

## From the series Qissa wa 'Ibar
In the eighth episode Omar Abdelkafy says that God gave Dawud and Sulayman knowledge, and that this favour is greater than the wind, the jinn and the birds made subject to Sulayman, provided the knowledge is beneficial and acted upon. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:02:00} He tells how Sulayman inspected the birds in his court and missed the hoopoe, and draws the lesson that one in charge must stay alert to those under him; Sulayman then did not accept the hoopoe's report about Saba' until he had checked it, sending the bird with a letter to its queen. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:06:00} He relates that Sulayman refused Bilqis's gift because he had called her to guidance and she sought to satisfy him with wealth, and that he asked for her throne: an 'ifrit of the jinn offered to bring it before he rose from his seat, and the one with knowledge of the Book offered to bring it before his glance returned to him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:09:30} In the fifth episode he tells the story of Sulayman and the ant he asked how much food it needed in a year. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep05 00:07:00}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
In episode 10 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|al-Sha'rawi]] says that the story of the ant and Sulayman shows the ant colony to be a kingdom in which each group has a task, sentries among them, and that the ant excused Sulayman's army because they might tread on the ants without knowing, at which Sulayman smiled and thanked God for a knowledge He had taught him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep10 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep10 00:20:00} The presenter relates al-Sha'bi's view that the ant Sulayman heard had wings, since God had taught him the speech of birds, and calls it correct because only the queen ant has wings. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep10 00:29:00} (needs_source: the presenter's reading.)

In episode 17 al-Sha'rawi says that the hoopoe brought sure news from Saba, not worldly news but news bearing on Sulayman's mission: it objected that a people ruled by a woman prostrated to the sun and not to God, which shows that it knew what the creature owes the Creator. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:16:30} He takes this as evidence that the glorification of God by all things is real. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:15:00}
