## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Ghani, Abu al-Abbas Shams al-Din al-Saruji, a Hanafi jurist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14957} He studied under Abu al-Rabi' Sulayman ibn Abi al-Izz, Abu al-Zahir Ishaq ibn Ali ibn Yahya and Shaykh Najm al-Din. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14957} He was active in many sciences and also compiled, wrote, issued legal opinions and taught. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14957} His works include Objections to Ibn Taymiyya in kalam, and a commentary on al-Hidaya which he titled al-Ghaya and did not complete. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14958}
