## Overview
Mansa Sulayman ibn Abi Bakr, king of [[polity/mali-empire|Mali]]. He is the brother of [[person/mansa-musa|Mansa Musa]], and succeeded him after his son Mansa Maghan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
He ruled for twenty-four years, and the material Ibn Khaldun quotes describes his rule as firm, and under him the bounds of the kingdom widened. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The year of his death is approximately 761 AH, reckoned from the sequence Ibn Khaldun gives, and needs an additional source.
