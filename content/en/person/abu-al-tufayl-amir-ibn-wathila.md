## Summary
Amir ibn Wathila ibn Abd Allah ibn Amr, Abu al-Tufayl al-Laythi, a Companion known by his kunya. He related from the Prophet and from Abu Bakr, Umar, Ali and others, and he attended all of Ali's campaigns. The book gives his death as 100 AH and calls him the last Companion to die. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7149}
