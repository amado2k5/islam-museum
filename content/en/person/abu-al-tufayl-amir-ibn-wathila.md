## Summary
Amir ibn Wathila ibn Abd Allah ibn Amr, Abu al-Tufayl al-Laythi, a Companion known by his kunya. He related from the Prophet and from Abu Bakr, Umar, Ali and others, and he attended all of Ali's campaigns. The book gives his death as 100 AH and calls him the last Companion to die. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7149}

## The last Companion to die
In al-Taqrib, al-Nawawi names Abu al-Tufayl Amir ibn Wathila al-Laythi as the last Companion to die, and says he died in 100 AH. Al-Suyuti sets out the dispute over the year:
- **100**: Muslim in his Sahih; al-Hakim reports the same from Khalifa ibn Khayyat.
- **After 100**: Khalifa elsewhere said he lived on past 100.
- **102**: Mus'ab al-Zubayri.
- **107**: Ibn Hibban, Ibn Qani' and Abu Zakariyya ibn Manda state it firmly.
- **110**: Wahb ibn Jarir reports from his father that he saw his funeral at Mecca that year. Al-Dhahabi judged this the correct date.
{cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 223} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 224} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 225}

That he was the very last Companion to die was stated firmly by Muslim, Mus'ab al-Zubayri, Ibn Manda and al-Mizzi, among others. Sahih Muslim records his words: I saw the Messenger of God, and there is no man on the face of the earth who saw him except me. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 225}
