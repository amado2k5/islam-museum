## Summary
A Successor from Kufa, Abu al-Jazl; he transmitted from Umar ibn al-Khattab and Aisha; al-Sha'bi and Khaythama ibn Abd al-Rahman transmitted from him; Ibn Khaldun rated him reliable; he died before 75 AH as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7841}
