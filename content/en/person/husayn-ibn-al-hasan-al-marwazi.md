## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan ibn Harb al-Sulami al-Marwazi, a resident of Mecca, is the narrator through whom the text of [[person/abd-allah-ibn-al-mubarak|Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak]]'s [[work/kitab-al-zuhd-ibn-al-mubarak|Kitab al-Zuhd wa-l-Raqa'iq]] reached us in its best-known recension. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 99} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 47}

## Teachers and students
[[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] quotes Ibn Hajar: he transmitted from Ibn al-Mubarak, Hushaym, Yazid ibn Zuray', Ibn Ulayya, Ibn Uyayna, Abu Mu'awiya, al-Walid ibn Muslim, al-Fadl ibn Musa al-Sinani and others; and from him transmitted al-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, Baqi ibn Makhlad, Ibn Abi Asim, Dawud ibn Ali ibn Khalaf, [[person/yahya-ibn-muhammad-ibn-saaid|Ibn Sa'id]] and others. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 99}

## Standing
Ibn Abi Hatim reports that his father heard from him in Mecca and, asked about him, called him truthful; Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy and said he died in 246; Maslama called him trustworthy. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 99}

## His transmission of Kitab al-Zuhd
At the start of al-A'zami's text, Ibn Sa'id narrates the book from him, and the chain gives the year 245 for his hearing from Ibn al-Mubarak. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 1} He added reports not through Ibn al-Mubarak, which Ahmad Farid counts among the students' "additions", and sometimes paired another teacher's report with Ibn al-Mubarak's as corroboration, as in the first report, from al-Fadl ibn Musa. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 56} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 1}

## For young visitors
Al-Marwazi learnt Kitab al-Zuhd from his teacher Ibn al-Mubarak and taught it to his own students, and so it reached us through him.

## Notes for researchers
- The year 245 in the chain is read from the scan, where the editor notes a disorder in the chain in his footnote.
