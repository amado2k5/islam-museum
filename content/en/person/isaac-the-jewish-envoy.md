## Overview
Isaac, a Jewish man who accompanied the embassy of Charlemagne to [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] in 181 AH / 797 CE, as an interpreter with two Franks. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.106-107}

## The journey
The journey took three years. The two Franks died along the way, so Isaac returned alone, with an elephant that al-Rashid had given as a gift to Charlemagne. He reached Aachen in 183 AH / 799 CE, at the time when al-Rashid's embassy arrived. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.106-107}

According to Taqqush, these details are reported only in Latin sources, such as Einhard, St. Gall and the Royal Annals. He takes part in [[event/rashid-charlemagne-embassies|the embassies of al-Rashid and Charlemagne]] in the role of "interpreter of the first embassy". {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.106-107}
