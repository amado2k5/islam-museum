## Summary
Ali ibn Abd al-Salam, Abu al-Hasan al-Tasuli, a Maliki jurist; the entry gives his death as 1258 AH. His works include al-Bahja, a commentary on the Tuhfa in Maliki furu', a gloss on al-Zaqaqiya, and a commentary on al-Shamil. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7514} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7515}
