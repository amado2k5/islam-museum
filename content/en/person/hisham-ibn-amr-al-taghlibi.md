## Overview
Hisham ibn Amr al-Taghlibi, a governor whom al-Mansur appointed over Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His governorship of Sind
Al-Baladhuri reports that he opened what had been closed, conquered Qashir, and took captives and many slaves. He conquered Multan, drove the Mutaghallaba out of Qandabil, and took Qandahar by ships. He demolished "Budha" and built a mosque on its site. He says that the land grew fertile in his governorship. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
