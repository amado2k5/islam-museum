## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Sulayman, Abu Ghalib al-Sunsuni al-Zurari. Al-Zirikli describes him as the shaykh of the Imamiyya of his time; the Imamiyya are a Shi'i group, and this is a label of affiliation that al-Zirikli reports, not one the museum adopts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 209}. He was of the people of Kufa and settled in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 209}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 285 AH, 898 CE, and his death as 368 AH, 978 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 209}.

## His nisba
His nisba is to Zurara ibn A'yan al-Shaybani; al-Zirikli says that one of his forebears was among the clients of that family {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 209-210}.

## His book
Among his books is a History (al-Ta'rikh) that he did not complete; al-Zirikli says he wrote about a thousand leaves of it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 210}.
