## Summary
Ibn al-Battar, the brilliant physician and herbalist, author of the *Kitab al-Mufradat* on simples; the knowledge of plants, their properties, benefits and habitats ended with him. He was in the service of al-Malik al-Kamil then his son al-Salih, and was chief in Egypt; he died at Damascus in Sha'ban. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 242}
