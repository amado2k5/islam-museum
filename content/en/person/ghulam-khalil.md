## Overview
Ghulam Khalil — his name Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ghullab ibn Khalid ibn Firas al-Bahili; author of books on supplication, on cutting oneself off unto God, on prayer, and on exhortations. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 268}

## In al-Sarraj's al-Luma'
In [[work/kitab-al-luma-fi-al-tasawwuf|al-Luma']], [[person/abu-nasr-al-sarraj|Abu Nasr al-Sarraj]] says that Ghulam Khalil "used to denounce" the Sufis of Baghdad, and that he reported [[person/abu-al-husayn-al-nuri|Abu al-Husayn al-Nuri]] to [[person/abu-ahmad-al-muwaffaq|al-Muwaffaq]] as a zindiq whose blood was lawful, testifying against him for his talk of loving God passionately. {cite:luma-sarraj-jadir-dar-al-fath p. 546} He also relates that, on a woman's claim, Ghulam Khalil testified to immorality against a group of Sufis including Samnun "the Lover", calling them zindiqs whose blood was on his neck, until the ruler ordered them beheaded and they were then saved. {cite:luma-sarraj-jadir-dar-al-fath p. 552} {cite:luma-sarraj-jadir-dar-al-fath p. 553} This is al-Sarraj's account, given in defence of the Sufis and against their opponents.
