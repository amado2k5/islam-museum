## Overview
A Mamluk sultan, son of al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 742}

## The council of the caliphate
He held a council on Thursday the eleventh of Dhu al-Hijja 741 AH, and called for the caliph Ibrahim, the heir apparent Ahmad, and the judges. He asked: who rightfully deserves the caliphate? {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 742}

Ibn Jama'a said that al-Mustakfi had bequeathed the caliphate to his son Ahmad. Ibrahim was deposed, and Ahmad was given the allegiance. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 742}

## Notes for researchers
