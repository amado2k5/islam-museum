## Overview
Al-'Abbas b. 'Abdallah, Abu'l-Fadl al-Tartushi; he dwelt at Baghdad and transmitted from a company — Ibn Abi'l-Dunya, Ibn Sa'id and Ibn Makhlad transmitted from him — trustworthy, truthful, righteous. Ibn Makhlad said: "I never saw him laugh, nor even smile." He died at Samarra in 267, said also 268. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 61}
