## Overview
Ta'abbata Sharran, a formidable poet, known for his line about the ghoul: "So I awoke, with the ghoul as my neighbour" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.469}.

## In al-Mas'udi
al-Mas'udi cites this line in the section on the reports of the Arabs about ghouls and their transformations {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.469}.

He is also cited in the section on the pre-Islamic beliefs about souls. His maternal uncle al-Shanfara asked him about a man he had killed, and he said: "I bridled him with a sword, and his soul flowed like water" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.468}.
