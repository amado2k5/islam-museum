## Overview
Ta'abbata Sharran, the poet and daredevil author of the famous line on the ghoul — 'and in the morning the ghoul was my neighbour' — which al-Mas'udi cites in his chapter on the Arabs' tales of ghouls; to his uncle al-Shanfara, who asked how the killing went, he answered 'I bitted him with a blade and his soul poured out'.
