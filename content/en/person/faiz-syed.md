## Summary
Adv. Faiz Syed — full name Faiz Ali Syed — is an Indian Islamic preacher and lecturer from Aurangabad, Maharashtra, and the founder of the Islamic Research Centre Education & Welfare Trust (IRC). {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} His lectures reach their largest audience through IRC TV, the IRC's YouTube channel, which counted 4,170,000 subscribers in November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:vidiq-irc-tv}

## Beginnings and education
Faiz Syed was born on 12 December 1979 in Aurangabad, Maharashtra, to Azam Ali Syed and Aliya Fatima, according to his official website. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} The same site lists an unusually long string of degrees: a B.Sc. in Computers, a B.A in Islamic Studies, a B.G.L and an LL.B, graduation from the International Islamic Academy Online, an M.C.A, and an M.A in Islamic Studies from Maulana Azad National Urdu University. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} The "Adv." in his public name reflects his legal training, though his public work is preaching and education rather than courtroom practice. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed}

## Career and work
In 2001 he founded the Islamic Research Centre Education & Welfare Trust, a registered non-profit, and he remains its founder and president. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} He is also president of the Al Kitab Education & Welfare Society and a director of the Janseva Co-operative Credit Society. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} His official website credits him with more than 2,000 lectures and talks, followed by question-and-answer sessions, in Urdu and Hindi in more than 200 cities in India, on more than 950 topics, besides speaking tours abroad that the site lists across Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Nepal and the United Kingdom. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} Those lecture totals are his own counts, reported here as such. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed}

## Programmes and media
IRC TV began on YouTube on 4 December 2009, with its channel country set to India. {cite:vidiq-irc-tv} The channel's five most-viewed videos are all his lectures — on the signs of the last age, on how to perform Umrah, on whether one's provision is fixed, on travelling to Hajj on foot, and on dreams of a coming death in the house. {cite:vidiq-irc-tv} His own website describes IRC TV as one of the most followed Urdu Islamic channels globally, and also lists his personal channel, Adv. Faiz Syed Official, at more than 220,000 subscribers. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed}

## Impact and audience
IRC TV stood at 4,170,000 subscribers, 1,071,425,329 total views and 34,207 videos at vidIQ's update of 5 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-irc-tv} His official website gives a slightly different figure for the same channel — 4.23 million subscribers with 33,000+ videos and 1.05 billion views — and dates those figures to September 2026. {cite:advfaizsyed-faiz-syed} The later, independently aggregated vidIQ figure is the one this record carries. {cite:vidiq-irc-tv}
