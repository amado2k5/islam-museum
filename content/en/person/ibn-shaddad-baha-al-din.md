## Overview
Baha' al-Din Abu al-Mahasin Yusuf ibn Rafi' ibn Tamim al-Asadi, judge of Aleppo and a Shafi'i jurist, known as Ibn Shaddad. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.77-78}

## Upbringing
Ibn Khallikan relates that he was born in Mosul on the night of 10 Ramadan 539 AH. He was raised by his maternal uncles, the Banu Shaddad, and was attributed to them. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.77}

## Study
He attended Abu Bakr Yahya ibn Sa'dun al-Qurtubi for eleven years, reading with him the seven readings, hadith and its commentaries, and exegesis. Ibn Khallikan reports that he testified that no one had read with him more than Ibn Shaddad had. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.77-78}

## His work
Haji Khalifa attributes to him [[work/al-alaq-al-khatira|al-A'laq al-Khatira]] (The Precious Possessions). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
