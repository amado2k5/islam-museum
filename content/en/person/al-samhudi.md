## Overview
Nur al-Din Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Hasani al-Samhudi al-Shafi'i, historian and mufti and teacher of [[place/medina|Medina]]. He was born in Safar 844 AH in the town of Samhud in the Egyptian districts and died in 911 AH. He came to Medina in 873 AH. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.4}

## His works on Medina
He wrote three works on the history of the abode of emigration: the large "Iqtifa' al-Wafa", which burned in the fire of the Prophet's Mosque and which he did not finish; the middle "Wafa' al-Wafa"; and the abridgement "Khulasat al-Wafa". {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.4-8}

## His observation
He took part in person in the rebuilding of the noble chamber, so accounts of his own observation appear in his book, which the editor says no other historian reaches. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.4-8}

## His biography in the edition of Wafa' al-Wafa
Muhammad Muhyi al-Din Abd al-Hamid gives al-Samhudi's biography at the start of his edition, drawing on *Shadharat al-dhahab* and *al-Daw' al-lami'*: he was Nur al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali, son of the judge Afif al-Din Abd Allah ibn Ahmad, whose lineage he traces to al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib; a Hasani, known as al-Samhudi, resident, scholar, mufti, teacher and historian of Medina, a Shafi'i. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.8}

He was born in Safar 844 AH at Samhud and grew up there; he memorized the Qur'an and the *Minhaj* in law, studied with his father, reading the *Minhaj* with al-Jalal al-Mahalli's commentary, and went to Cairo with him several times; he studied law, legal theory and Arabic with al-Shams al-Jawjari, read with al-Jalal al-Mahalli, was a close pupil of al-Sharif al-Munawi, who invested him with the Sufi cloak, read law and inheritance with Shaykh Zakariyya, and was licensed to teach by several scholars. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.8} He settled in Medina from 873 AH, studying there with al-Shihab al-Abshiti, and heard hadith in Mecca from Kamaliyya bint al-Najm al-Marjani, her brother al-Kamal and al-Najm Umar ibn Fahd. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.8}

His works include *Jawahir al-iqdayn fi fadl al-sharafayn*, the three books on Medina, [[work/iqtifa-al-wafa|Iqtifa' al-Wafa]], [[work/wafa-al-wafa|Wafa' al-Wafa]] and [[work/khulasat-al-wafa|Khulasat al-Wafa]], a gloss on al-Nawawi's *Idah* called *al-Ifsah*, a gloss on the *Rawda* called *Umniyyat al-mu'tanin* that reached the chapter on usury, and a volume of his fatwas; precious books of his all burned while he was in Mecca in 886 AH. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.9} He visited Jerusalem and returned to settle in Medina; the editor quotes al-Sakhawi: "there is hardly anyone in Medina who has not studied with him". {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.9} He died in Medina on Thursday 18 Dhu al-Qa'da 911 AH. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.9}
