## Overview
Al-'Izz Muhammad ibn al-Hafiz 'Abd al-Ghani ibn 'Abd al-Wahid al-Maqdisi — Abu'l-Fath, hafiz son of a hafiz; born in 566, he travelled to Baghdad as a youth hearing Ibn Shamil and his generation, heard at Damascus and journeyed to Isfahan, and was noted for fine recitation, sound memorisation and understanding. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Testimonies and death
Al-Diya' called him "a hafiz, Hanbali jurist, man of many disciplines" and attested his firm religion and complete manliness; Abu Shama said he accompanied al-Malik al-Mu'azzam 'Isa and heard much from him, being devout, ascetic and scrupulous; Taqi al-Din's sons Ahmad and 'Izz al-Din 'Abd al-Rahman, al-Diya', al-Shihab al-Qawsi, Shams al-Din 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi 'Umar and Ibn al-Bukhari transmitted from him; he died on Monday night 19 Shawwal 613 and was buried at the foot of Qasyun. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
