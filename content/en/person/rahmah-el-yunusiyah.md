# Rahmah el Yunusiyah

## Summary
Rahmah el Yunusiyah (26 October 1900 – 26 February 1969) was an Indonesian educator from Padang Panjang, West Sumatra, who founded Diniyah Putri in 1923 and made it the reference point for modern Islamic schooling for girls in Indonesia. She also built further schools and a teacher-training institute, was imprisoned in the struggle before independence, and later sat in the People's Consultative Assembly for the Masyumi Party. {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-en} {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-id} {cite:wikidata-rahmah-el-yunusiyah}

## Family and formation
She was born at Bukit Surungan, Padang Panjang, into a family of scholars: her father, Muhammad Yunus, was a qadi, and her grandfather Muhammad Rasyad was a scholar who led the local Naqshbandi branch. That lineage is part of her biography, not evidence of her own school; no source names one for her, and the record keeps her faith unknown with the evidence stated. She was educated in the reforming school world of Padang Panjang and resolved early that girls should receive the same structured religious and general education as boys. {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-en} {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-id} {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-ar}

## Diniyah Putri
In 1923 she opened Diniyah Putri, a school for girls that joined Qur'anic and Islamic studies to general subjects under a graded modern curriculum. It grew from a single school into a small system — kindergarten, primary and secondary levels, teacher training and a women's college — and drew students from across the Malay-Indonesian world. The school has no museum group entity, so its founding is carried in prose rather than as a founder_of relation. {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-en} {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-id}

## Politics and death
In the years around independence she was active in the nationalist movement and was imprisoned by the Dutch; after independence she represented Masyumi in the People's Consultative Assembly. Party membership is affiliation, not a school. She died at Padang Panjang on 26 February 1969. The lane queue's death year of 1964 is contradicted by the English, Indonesian and Arabic articles and by Wikidata, all of which give 1969. {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-en} {cite:wikipedia-rahmah-el-yunusiyah-id} {cite:wikidata-rahmah-el-yunusiyah}
