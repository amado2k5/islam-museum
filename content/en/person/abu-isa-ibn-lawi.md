## Overview
Abu Isa ibn Lawi, a Jew from al-Ahwaz, whom Ibn al-Nadim mentions in the "Fihrist". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 529}

## His relation to Ibn al-Rawandi
Ibn al-Nadim says that [[person/ibn-al-rawandi|Ibn al-Rawandi]] composed for him most of his books which he called "al-Kufriyyat", and that Ibn al-Rawandi died in his house. This is Ibn al-Nadim's naming. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 529}
