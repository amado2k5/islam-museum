## Summary
Abubaker Ahmed — in Amharic ኡስታዝ አቡበከር አሕመድ, and on his own platform Ustaz Abubeker Ahmed — is an Ethiopian dawah preacher working in Amharic, who reaches his audience chiefly through his TikTok account. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} The account's Amharic bio states that it is the official TikTok account of Ustaz Abubeker Ahmed. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} The account counted 240,300 followers when read on 7 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed}

## Platforms and work
His TikTok account (@ustaz.abubekerahmed) held 509 videos and 1,900,000 likes in total when its profile was read on 7 November 2026. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} The videos it publishes are Amharic dawah lectures and sermons, and the account remains active with videos posted through 2026. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} The account carries no platform verification badge, and its standing as his official account rests on the statement in its own bio. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed}

## Public voice
The Ethiopian News Agency, in its report of 13 May 2021, described him as a prominent Islamic preacher and quoted his remarks at the Eid al-Fitr celebrations of 1442 AH on the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, where he said the Muslim community had condemned the unfair use of Nile waters. {cite:ena-abubeker-ahmed-gerd-2021} His remarks appeared in the same report alongside those of another preacher, Ustaz Yassin Nuru, and of Sheikh Ali Ahmed, Deputy President of the Addis Ababa Supreme Islamic Affairs Council. {cite:ena-abubeker-ahmed-gerd-2021}

## Impact and audience
Abubeker Ahmed's TikTok account stood at 240,300 followers, 509 videos and 1,900,000 likes when read on 7 November 2026, placing him among the largest individual Amharic dawah accounts on the short-video platform. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} This record is deliberately minimal and honest: no Wikipedia article in any language and no independent source documenting his biography could be found, so no birth date and no school of law are recorded for him here. {cite:tiktok-abubaker-ahmed} {cite:ena-abubeker-ahmed-gerd-2021}
