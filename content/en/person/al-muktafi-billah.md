## Overview
Al-Muktafi billah, an Abbasid caliph whose rule lasted six years and nineteen days. He died in 295 AH and was buried in the house of Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.27}

## The allegiance and conduct
Al-Suyuti says that he was born on the first of Rabi al-Akhir 264 AH, and that his mother was a Turkish woman called Jijak. His father made him heir, and he was given the allegiance in 289 AH while at al-Raqqa, and the vizier al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah took charge of the allegiance. He demolished the underground dungeons his father had made and made them mosques, and returned the gardens and shops that his father had taken to their owners, and the people loved him. Al-Suyuti relates his saying in his illness that he grieved only for seven hundred thousand dinars that he had spent from the money of the Muslims on buildings he had no need of. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.581-583}

Ibn al-Jawzi says that when he succeeded in 295 AH he went down into the palace and ordered the underground dungeons demolished, so that a congregational mosque could be built on their site. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.341}

## Other reports
Al-Mas'udi says that he left large sums in the treasuries, and was nevertheless very strict about the expense of his table. He says that he built a palace at al-Shammasiyya and took estates without payment, so that many prayed against him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.638-639} He says that when his illness grew severe he had the judge Muhammad ibn Yusuf and another witness to his will to appoint his brother Ja'far as heir. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.645-648}

Ibn al-Nadim relates that the queen of the Franks wrote to him in a letter on white silk asking for his friendship. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.45}
