## Overview
Qadi Abu Muhammad al-Istakhri studied jurisprudence under the judge [[person/abu-hamid-al-marwarudhi|Abu Hamid al-Marwarudhi]]. He was judge of Fasa and jurist of Fars. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 62}

He wrote a commentary on Mansur's "al-Musta'mal", and the book describes him as a refined jurist. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 62}

His full name is not given in the text, and he is not Abu Sa'id al-Istakhri. His death date is not in the data and needs an additional source.
