## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Yusuf, known as Qadri Efendi, is the author of Waqi'at al-Muftin, famous as the Fatawa Qadri or al-Fatawa al-Qadiriyya. He died in 1083 AH (1672 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.48}

He was the fatwa-distributor of the mufti Yahya ibn Zakariya at Istanbul, his work confined to collecting the questions answered by the fatwa office and delivering them to their owners on a set day each week; he served two later muftis, then rose to hold the judgeship of the army and of Istanbul, where he died. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.48}
