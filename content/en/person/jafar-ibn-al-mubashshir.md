## Overview
Ja'far ibn al-Mubashshir, one of the scholars of the Mu'tazila, their adept and their ascetic, according to al-Mas'udi. His brother [[person/hanash-ibn-al-mubashshir|Hanash ibn al-Mubashshir]], a scholar among the people of hadith and a leader of the Hashwiyya, was the opposite of him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}

## The dispute
The debate and mutual hostility between them went on for a long time, and each swore that he would not address the other until death. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}
