## Summary
Muhammad ibn Mu'awiya al-Sufi kept to the call of the wise and was cleansed and made whole. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 501, sequence 502, on pages 142 and 143. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}

## A wise man at the meadow
Muhammad ibn Mu'awiya al-Sufi relates that a wise man passed young men sitting on a grassy meadow and said: O company of the living, what holds you back on the Lord's road? They said: we sit to take heed. He said: I warn you, by the One who gave you life in a time of the dead, not to incline to what He who gave you life has abandoned. Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143}
