## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn Ma'ali al-Irbili, a scholar from Irbil, one of the men of learning of the seventh century AH. He was born in 589 AH (1193 CE) and died in 639 AH (1241 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.108}
