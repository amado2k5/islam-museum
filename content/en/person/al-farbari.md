## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Matr ibn Salih ibn Bishr al-Farbari is the transmitter of al-Bukhari's Sahih from its author — people travelled to him to hear the book, and he was the last to transmit the Sahih directly from al-Bukhari. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.4 p.289}

He was born in 231 AH and died on 3 Shawwal 320 AH (831 CE); his nisba goes to Farbar, a town at the edge of the Jayhun beyond Bukhara. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.4 p.289}
