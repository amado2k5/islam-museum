## Overview
Yazid ibn al-Husayn ibn Numayr al-Sakuni, son of [[person/al-husayn-ibn-numayr|al-Husayn ibn Numayr]]. He shot [[person/sulayman-ibn-surad|Sulayman ibn Surad al-Khuza'i]], chief of the Tawwabin, with an arrow and killed him at Ayn al-Warda {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 88}.
