# Khan Zaman Ali Quli Khan

## Summary

Ali Quli Khan Zaman (d. 1567), known by his title Khan Zaman, was an Uzbek noble who served the Mughal Empire under Humayun and Akbar and was killed leading the Uzbek nobles' rebellion against Akbar.{cite:wikipedia-khan-zaman-ali-quli-khan-en}

## In Mughal service

Ali Quli Khan came from the Shaibanid Uzbeks and rose in Mughal service until he received the title Khan-i Zaman, "Khan of the Age".{cite:wikipedia-khan-zaman-ali-quli-khan-en} Akbar placed him at Jaunpur in the east, which became his base for campaigns against the Afghans holding out in and around the Jaunpur region.{cite:wikipedia-khan-zaman-ali-quli-khan-en}{cite:chandra-medieval-india-2005} His victories over the Afghans made him one of the leading Uzbek commanders at the Mughal court.{cite:chandra-medieval-india-2005}

## Rebellion and death

His relations with Akbar deteriorated, and in the mid-1560s he joined the rebellion of the Uzbek nobles against the emperor.{cite:wikipedia-khan-zaman-ali-quli-khan-en}{cite:chandra-medieval-india-2005} The rebellion ended with his defeat and death at the Battle of Kara in 1567.{cite:wikipedia-khan-zaman-ali-quli-khan-en}{cite:chandra-medieval-india-2005}
