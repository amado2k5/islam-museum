## Summary
Abu'l-Husayn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Isma'il the preacher of Baghdad, known as Ibn Sam'un; unique of his age in discourse on spiritual intimations, fineness of preaching, majesty of allusion and delicacy of expression; he reached many of the great shaykhs including Abu Bakr al-Shibli; the people of Iraq held him in great esteem, and it was he whom al-Hariri of the Maqamat meant in the Razi maqama where he says: and they bring down Ibn Sam'un for him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
