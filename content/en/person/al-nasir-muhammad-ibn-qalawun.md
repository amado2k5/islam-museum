## Overview
Al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun, a Mamluk sultan who held the sultanate three times. The amirs gave him the allegiance after his brother al-Ashraf, when he was nine years old, in 693 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 730-731}

## Between deposition and return
He was deposed in 694 AH, and Kitbugha al-Mansuri took the sultanate, then Lajin in 696 AH. When Lajin was killed in 698 AH, al-Nasir was brought back from his exile at al-Karak. The caliph invested him, and sent al-Adil to Hama as deputy. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 730-731}

## Karak and his third return
Al-Suyuti says that in 708 AH he set out for the pilgrimage, but the bridge broke under him at al-Karak, and he escaped, while four others died. He stayed at al-Karak and wrote to depose himself from the kingdom, so Baybars al-Jashnakir was given the allegiance. Then he returned in Rajab 709 AH and entered Egypt on the day of the Feast of Breaking the Fast. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 734}
