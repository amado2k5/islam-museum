## Overview
Abu Abdallah Ibn Rizam, author of a book refuting the Isma'ilis and exposing their doctrines. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.264}

## His book
Ibn al-Nadim quotes the book in its own words in the section on the Isma'ili doctrine. He says that he is free of responsibility for it, both in truthfully reporting it and in not lying about it. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.264}
