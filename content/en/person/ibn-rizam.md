## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Ibn Rizam, author of the book that refuted the Ismailis and laid bare their doctrines; Ibn al-Nadim reproduces the book in its own words in his discourse on the Ismaili school, declaring his disavowal of it. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 288}
