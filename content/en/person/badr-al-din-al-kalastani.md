## Summary
Badr al-Din al-Kalastani, katib al-sirr of Egypt; he worked in his homeland, then Baghdad, came to Damascus obscure, then Egypt where closeness to al-Juybani brought him modest ease; when al-Juybani held Syria's regency he came with him and was given the Zahiriyya teaching, the Asadiyya shaykhdom and the headship of the Umayyad Mosque. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## The Turkish letter
When the sultan marched to Aleppo a letter arrived from the Lesser Armenia in Turkish that none could read; he was summoned, read it, drafted an excellent reply, and was kept in the sultan's company until given the secretariat — he used to tell that he rose owning not a dirham and by evening had horses and wealth beyond description. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
