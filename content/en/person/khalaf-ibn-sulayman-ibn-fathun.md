## Summary
Khalaf ibn Sulayman ibn Khalaf ibn Muhammad ibn Fathun, Abu al-Qasim, of Orihuela in al-Andalus, a Maliki jurist, writer and poet who was judge of Shatiba and then Denia. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15810} He related from his father, Abu al-Walid al-Baji and Tahir ibn Mawfaz, and was related from by his son Muhammad and Ziyad ibn Muhammad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15810} His book on contracts was the first of its kind.
