## Overview
The title of his book names him "Ahmad ibn Abi Ya'qub ibn Ja'far ibn Wahb ibn Wadih al-Katib al-Abbasi, known as al-Ya'qubi", and the copy's colophon calls him "Ibn Wadih al-Katib al-Abbasi". {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. scan 3} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 625}
## His book
He wrote a [[work/tarikh-al-yaqubi|history]] in two parts, the first on the early nations and the second on Islamic history to the events of 258 AH, and said in its preface that he gathered the reports of the scholars and transmitters before him because they disagreed. The book is marked by its care for the length of each caliphate, the caliphs' ages and the leaders of the pilgrimage year by year. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. scan 4} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 487}
## Notes for researchers
The book itself gives neither the author's birth nor his death, so both are left unstated here until they are read in another source.
