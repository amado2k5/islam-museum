## Overview
Abu Ghalib al-Hasan b. Mansur, Dhu'l-Sa'adatayn; born at Siraf in 352, he served Fakhr al-Mulk, then Sultan al-Dawla gave him the vizierate's title "Najah al-Muluk" and he was steward at Baghdad. When the khutba passed to Musharrif al-Dawla he was sent down with the Daylamis to Khuzistan, where the Daylamis leapt on him and killed him at Ahwaz — a vizierate of eighteen months and three days, at sixty years and five months. His son was mulcted of eighty thousand dinars — Sultan al-Dawla breathed easy; al-Mutarraz mourned him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.345}
