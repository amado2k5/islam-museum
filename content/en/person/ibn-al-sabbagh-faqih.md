## Summary
Ibn al-Sabbagh, the Shafi'i jurist, author of *al-Shamil*; when Baghdad's Nizamiyya madrasa was finished in 459 and Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi was assigned its teaching, he hid — saying the site was usurped — so Ibn al-Sabbagh taught there twenty days until al-Shirazi relented. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.246; shadharat-dhahab p. 315}

## The outcry
When some spoke of the lawfulness of Ibn al-Sabbagh's sitting in the madrasa — he who said 'were it not that I performed my duty in it, I would have been of the lost' — it reached Nizam al-Mulk and heaven rose against him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.598}
