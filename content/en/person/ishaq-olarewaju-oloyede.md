## Summary
Ishaq Olanrewaju Oloyede is a Nigerian professor of Islamic studies and public administrator: former vice-chancellor of the University of Ilorin, secretary-general of the Nigerian Supreme Council for Islamic Affairs since 2013, and registrar of the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) — the body conducting Nigeria's university entrance examinations — from 2016 to 2026. {cite:wikipedia-ishaq-olarewaju-oloyede}

## Life and formation
He was born on 10 October 1954 in Abeokuta, Ogun State, studied Arabic and Islamic studies in Lagos and at the University of Ibadan, took a first-class degree in Arabic at the University of Ilorin in 1981, and earned his doctorate in Islamic studies, specialising in Islamic law and jurisprudence, there in 1991. {cite:wikipedia-ishaq-olarewaju-oloyede}

## Academic career
Appointed assistant lecturer at Ilorin in 1982, he rose to professor, serving as director of academic planning, deputy vice-chancellor and then vice-chancellor from 2007 to 2012 — a tenure remembered in Nigerian higher-education commentary for raising the university's continental standing. He also led the Nigerian Inter-Religious Council and co-secretaried the 2005 political reform conference. {cite:wikipedia-ishaq-olarewaju-oloyede}

## JAMB and public service
Appointed JAMB registrar in 2016, he cut examination fees while multiplying the board's remittances to the federal treasury — figures in the tens of billions of naira against a few million in previous decades — and computerised testing and fraud controls. Admirers cite the remittances as rare proof of integrity in Nigerian administration; critics have faulted examination glitches and centre accessibility. The museum records both readings attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ishaq-olarewaju-oloyede}

## Muslim leadership
As secretary-general of the Supreme Council for Islamic Affairs — Nigeria's umbrella Muslim body — he is among the country's most visible Muslim public voices, bridging scholarship and state service. {cite:wikipedia-ishaq-olarewaju-oloyede}
