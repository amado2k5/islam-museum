## Summary
Abu Uthman Sa'id ibn al-Abbas al-Razi. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 464 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], directly after Yahya ibn Mu'adh, describing him as one firmly sure of arrival, eloquent in principles, and averse to idle matters, with a sufficient eloquence. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/70}

## His warning
He tells his brother that he warns him against the devils of humankind and of jinn, as the Prophet warned Abu Dharr; their leader is Iblis; a person should know in his heart who calls him to ruin and who calls him to salvation, and seek help from God. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/70}

## The world and love of the world
He warns against the world, its people and those who call to it. He says that love of the world claims with its tongue to worship its Lord, while in its heart, intention, going and coming, and pleasure and anger it serves its own desire and its world. He recalls that in the Prophet's time scholars were wary of the world's lawful things and feared them more than the wary man fears its unlawful things. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/70-71}

## The scholars as heirs of the prophets
He says the scholars are the trustees of the Messenger and heirs of the prophets, that the scholar who fears God demolishes by God's right the falsehood of those who desire the world, and that a deceived scholar puts out the light of truth with the darkness of falsehood. He describes two kinds of imam: a guiding imam who guides by God's command, and an imam who calls to the Fire, that is, who calls to disobedience of God. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/71}

## The open door
He says the door of the next world is open, and that between God and His servants there is no means except obedience to Him. On page 72 the admonition on asceticism continues, and warns that a person may know the truth without acting on it. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/72}
