Husein Smajić, born in 1952, is a Bosnian scholar-official of the Islamic Community in Bosnia and Herzegovina who held its second office for most of a decade. As Mufti of Sarajevo he stood for the Community's headship in the election of September 2012: in the council's ballot he placed third with 36 votes, behind Husein Kavazović of Tuzla and Nusret Abdibegović of Travnik, and Kavazović was elected Reis-ul-ulema{cite:kuna-bosnian-clerics-mufti-race-2012}. Smajić had set out his own programme for the 2012–2019 term in a document published by the Community that August, arguing for a stronger religious authority for the Community and for guarding the Bosnian tradition of Islam{cite:islamskazajednica-smajic-program-2012}.

He then served as deputy Reis-ul-ulema — the naibu-reis — from 2012 to 2019, the ninth holder of an office created in 1947{cite:wikipedia-deputy-reis-bih-sr}. In those years he also represented the Islamic Community on the Interreligious Council of Bosnia and Herzegovina, sitting alongside the Catholic cardinal Vinko Puljić, the Orthodox bishop Grigorije Durić and the Jewish community president Jakob Finci{cite:islamicpluralism-bosnian-religious-leaders-2013}.
