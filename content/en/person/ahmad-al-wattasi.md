## Summary
The Wattasid king defeated at [[event/battle-bir-uqba-943|Bi'r 'Uqba]] in 943/1536 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221}.

## The defeat
The Khalut tribes who formed his army's vanguard deserted and spread chaos through it; he fell before the Sa'dis near Wadi al-'Abid {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221}.

## Toward Portugal
The defeat pushed him to an eleven-year treaty with Portugal, placing Moroccans around Asilah, Tangier and Qasr al-Saghir under the jurisdiction of the king of Fas, permitting Wattasid subjects' trade there barring arms and contraband, and forbidding purchase of booty taken from Moroccans from Ottoman or French ships {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221-222}.
