## Overview
Lord of the Maghrib, Abu Muhammad Ibn al-Ma'mun — that is 'Abd al-Wahid b. Idris al-Mu'mini, master of Marrakesh; he took power in 630 and restored the name of Ibn Umrat in the khutba to win the hearts of the Almohads. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 208}

## His death
He died drowned in the tank of his garden, and his brother al-Mu'tadid ruled after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 208}
