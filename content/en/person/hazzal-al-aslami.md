## Overview
Hazzal al-Aslami was Hazzal ibn Dhi'ab ibn Yazid ibn Kulayb ibn 'Amir ibn Khuzayma ibn Mazin ibn al-Harith ibn Salaman ibn [[group/aslam|Aslam]] ibn Afsa ibn Du'mi. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 745}

## His hadith
His son and [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-munkadir|Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir]] narrated from him a single hadith — Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says, "I do not think he has any other" — namely the Prophet's words: "O Hazzal, had you covered him with your cloak." Some say that Nu'aym ibn Hazzal stands between Ibn al-Munkadir and this Hazzal. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 745} The editor notes that Ahmad and al-Nasa'i in *al-Sunan al-Kubra* report it, and that it is sound from him. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 745} On the idea of covering others' faults that the hadith touches, see [[concept/satr|covering a Muslim's faults (satr)]].

## Others of the same name
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr distinguishes him from two other men: Hazzal "of the tree", of whom he says "I know no more of him than this", whose hadith is with the Basrans — Mu'awiya ibn Qurra narrated from him: "You commit sins that look finer than a hair in your eyes, which in the Prophet's time we counted among the ruinous ones"; and Hazzal ibn Murra al-Ashja'i, whom Ibn al-Azraq lists among the Companions. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 745}
