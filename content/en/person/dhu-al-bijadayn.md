## Summary
Abdullah Dhu al-Bijadayn al-Muzani; he died on the Tabuk expedition and the Prophet buried him with his own hands in his grave by night, in Abu Bakr's and Umar's presence, per Ibn Mas'ud's account. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/171}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "Abdullah Dhu al-Bijadayn al-Muzani", saying he died on the Tabuk expedition, a grave was dug for him and the Prophet descended into it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/171} Ibn Hisham explains the reason for his being called Dhu al-Bijadayn ("he of the two ragged garments"). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: of Muzayna. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/171}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The naming's explanation comes from Ibn Hisham, not Ibn Ishaq (p. 172).
- The text does not give his full lineage.
