## Overview
Najm al-Din Abu 'Abdallah Ahmad b. Hamdan b. Shabib al-Nimri al-Harrani, the Hanbali jurist, usuli and qadi resident in Cairo; the last to transmit from the hafiz 'Abd al-Qadir al-Ruhawi, he heard from the khatib Abu 'Abdallah Ibn Taymiyya, and kept company with his cousin Majd al-Din Ibn Taymiyya, debating with him at length until knowledge of the madhhab — its subtleties and its depths — ended with him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748-749}

## His works
His writings include al-Ri'aya al-sughra and al-Ri'aya al-kubra in fiqh, Kitab al-Wafi, a Muqaddima in usul al-din, and Sifat al-mufti wa'l-mustafti; he held the deputy judgeship at Cairo, and al-Dimyati, al-Mizzi and al-Birzali transmitted from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748-749}
