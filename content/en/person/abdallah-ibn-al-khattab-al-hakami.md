## Overview
Of the Hakam, he came to al-Hadi in 294 AH with a band of horsemen and men, fleeing for fear of his cousin al-Futayf ibn Muhammad al-Ashajj, and went with him to Najran.

## Detail
The book says that Abdallah ibn al-Khattab al-Hakami came to al-Hadi with horsemen and men, fleeing for fear of his cousin al-Futayf ibn Muhammad al-Ashajj, and went out with al-Hadi to Najran on the march of Rajab 294 AH. The book calls the cousin corrupt and a tyrant; that is the narrator's description. Nothing more is said of Abdallah in these pages.
