## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Abi Salim al-Marini, nicknamed al-Musta'in, [[person/abu-salim-ibrahim-marinid|son of Abu Salim]] of [[polity/marinid-dynasty|the Marinids]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He met his cousin the amir Abd al-Rahman at Wadi al-Naja and they swore to hold their hands together. Then he marched on the new city and besieged it until he entered it on the seventh of Muharram 776 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Abd al-Rahman marched to Marrakesh and became independent there, and a peace was made between them in which Azemmour was the boundary. He appointed Muhammad ibn Uthman as vizier, and his relation with the sultan of al-Andalus, Ibn al-Ahmar, grew close. In his days [[person/ibn-al-khatib|Ibn al-Khatib]] was killed in Fez. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
