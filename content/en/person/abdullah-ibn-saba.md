## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam calls Abd Allah ibn Saba an enigmatic figure and the alleged founder of Shii Islam, a movement it describes as urging that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] should rule. Early sources, it says, disagree about his origins and his political role. It lists ideas credited to him that it labels extremist: that Ali was divine, that Ali had been divinely appointed, and that Ali had not died and would reappear to establish righteousness on earth. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e10}
