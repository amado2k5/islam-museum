## Overview
Barra daughter of Abd al-Muttalib, aunt of the Prophet, one of the daughters of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim]] whom al-Mubarakfuri's "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" counts in its section on the Prophet's family. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## Counting the daughters of Abd al-Muttalib
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts six daughters for Abd al-Muttalib: Umm Hakim, who was al-Bayda', Barra, Atika, Safiyya, Arwa and Umayma. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.8-9} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/56-66}. She is thus a sister of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah]], the Prophet's father. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## What is not recorded here
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum says nothing here of her life, her marriage or her religion. Only what the book says is recorded here; what the biographical and genealogical books say needs a source to be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Abd al-Muttalib, the grandfather of the Prophet, had many sons and daughters, and Barra was one of them, so she was one of the uncles and aunts of the Prophet.
