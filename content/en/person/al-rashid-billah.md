## Overview
Al-Rashid billah Abu Ja'far Mansur ibn al-Mustarshid, an Abbasid caliph who ruled from 529 to 530 AH. He was born in 502 AH, and his mother was a concubine. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 669-670}

## The caliphate
His father had him named heir apparent in 513 AH, and he was given the allegiance when his father was killed. Al-Suyuti describes him as eloquent, a man of letters, a poet, brave and generous. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 669-670}

## His deposition and death
Sultan Mas'ud deposed him in 530 AH in a session of jurists. He went out to Azerbaijan and ravaged Maragha, Hamadhan and Isfahan, and was killed by his Persian bodyguards in 532 AH. Al-Suyuti relates from al-Imad that he had "the beauty of Yusuf and the generosity of Hatim". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 669-670}

Al-Suyuti also says that it is said he was born closed up, and the doctors opened an outlet for him with a golden instrument. The report is attributed to a common saying, not to a specific witness. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 669-670}
