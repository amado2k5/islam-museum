## Summary
al-Khadim al-Makhdum is an epithet, meaning the servant who is served; the notice does not give his name. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 517, sequence 518, on pages 152 and 153. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/152} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}

## The young man who copied the notebook
Adam ibn Abi Iyas relates that a young man who wrote from him took a notebook to copy it. He copied it, and Adam suspected him of wrongdoing; then the young man came back in tattered clothes. Adam was gentle with him and offered him dirhams, which he refused, and though Adam pressed, he would not take them. He took Adam by the hand, led him to the sea, drew a cup from his sleeve, scooped sea water, and said: drink. Adam drank, and it was sweeter than honey. He said: one who serves One of such power, what does he do with your dirhams? Then he went out of sight and was not seen again. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/152} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}
