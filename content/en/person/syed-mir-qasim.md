Syed Mir Qasim (17 March 1921 – 12 December 2004) was a Kashmiri politician who served as chief minister of Jammu and Kashmir from 1971 to 1975.{cite:wikipedia-syed-mir-qasim-en} Born at Dooru Shahabad in Anantnag district, he took part in the Quit Kashmir movement against Dogra rule, helped draft the constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, and founded the state unit of the Indian National Congress.{cite:wikipedia-syed-mir-qasim-en}

In 1975 he resigned the chief ministership so that Sheikh Abdullah could return to power under the Indira–Sheikh accord.{cite:wikipedia-syed-mir-qasim-en} His autobiography, My Life and Times, appeared in 1992, and he was awarded the Padma Bhushan posthumously in 2005.{cite:wikipedia-syed-mir-qasim-en} He died in New Delhi on 12 December 2004 and was buried at his home town of Dooru Shahabad.{cite:wikipedia-syed-mir-qasim-en}{cite:wikidata-syed-mir-qasim}
