## Overview
The polymath Taj al-Din Abu'l-Yumn Zayd ibn al-Hasan ibn Zayd ibn al-Hasan al-Baghdadi, Qur'an-teacher, grammarian and philologist; shaykh of the reciters and grammarians of Syria and musnid of the age — born in 520, he had completed the ten readings at ten years old, something not known to have been granted anyone else; Sibt al-Khayyat cared for him, taught him to recite and sent him on to Abu'l-Qasim Hibat Allah ibn al-Tabar, Abu Mansur ibn Khayrun, Abu Bakr khatib of Mosul and Abu'l-Fadl ibn al-Muhtadi bi'llah. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Standing and death
He attained ample standing — al-Malik al-Mu'azzam constantly studied under him and would come down from the citadel to him; he died on 6 Shawwal 613, and the people stepped down a degree with his death in readings and hadith, for he was the last who had heard Judge Abu Bakr, the last who had heard al-Jawhari, the last to transmit from al-Qati'i. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
