## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Hasan ibn Yusuf al-Jabali, one of the scholars and writers of al-Azhar. He was born in al-Rahmaniyya in the Shibrakhit district of al-Buhayra in 1295 AH (1878 CE), and died in 1370 AH (1951 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.79}
