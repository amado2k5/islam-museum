## Overview
Abu al-Husayn al-Ardabili, a jurist who taught in Baghdad. Al-Shirazi says that he died in 381 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64} The date of death rests on a single source and needs an additional source. He is not Faraj ibn Muhammad al-Ardabili, nor Yusuf ibn Ibrahim al-Ardabili.
