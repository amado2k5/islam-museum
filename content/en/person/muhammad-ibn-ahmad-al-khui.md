## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Khalil ibn Sa'ada al-Khu'i, Shihab al-Din, Abu Abdallah, was the qadi of Damascus and the son of one of its qadis. He was born and died in Damascus. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 626 AH, 1229 CE, and his death as 693 AH, 1294 CE; the name al-Khu'i points to Khoy, in the districts of Azerbaijan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 324}.

## Career
He became qadi of Jerusalem in 657 AH, then of Aleppo, then of the Egyptian lands, and was afterwards transferred to the judgeship of Syria. Al-Zirikli describes him as a Shafi'i jurist and researcher {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 324}.

## Works and teachers
Among his writings al-Zirikli lists Aqalim al-Ta'lim, on the enumeration of the sciences, a manuscript of 84 leaves; a manuscript commentary, held at Dar al-Kutub, on Ibn Mu'ti's grammar al-Fusul al-Khamsun; al-Jabr wa-l-Muqabala; al-Hay'a; verse on rhetoric, inheritance law and prosody; a large one-volume book covering twenty arts; a versification of Ibn al-Salah's hadith sciences; a versification of Tha'lab's al-Fasih; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 324}.

Ubayd ibn Muhammad al-Is'ardi compiled for him a mashyakha in alphabetical order covering 236 teachers, and al-Zirikli adds that he had about 300 further teachers not named in it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 324}.
