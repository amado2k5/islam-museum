# Hani Sidow

## Summary
Hani Sidow is a British-Somali beauty blogger and author, known on Instagram as @hanihanns, where Stylist magazine counted 102,000 followers at the release of her first book. She was 22 at the time of that interview. {cite:stylist-hani-sidow}

## Insta-glam and representation
She joined social media because creating an Instagram account was part of her university degree in advertising and public relations, and her makeup posts began to draw attention. {cite:stylist-hani-sidow} "There weren't many people who looked like me", she told Stylist, describing why she began speaking about representation and styling modest fashion for hijab-wearing women; Somali women, she said, are her main audience. {cite:stylist-hani-sidow} Her first book, Insta-glam, was published by Octopus, and Hachette UK lists her among its contributors. {cite:stylist-hani-sidow} {cite:hachette-hani-sidow}
