## Summary
Habib ibn Bahriz was one of the early translators who rendered works of Greek science for al-Ma'mun, named by Ibn al-Nadim among the translators of the Barmakid era. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 337}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim lists in the Fihrist, among the early translators of the Barmakid days who translated Greek books for al-Ma'mun: Yahya ibn al-Bitriq; al-Hajjaj ibn Matar, who translated for al-Ma'mun and rendered the Almagest and Euclid; Ibn Na'ima, whose name was Abd al-Masih ibn Abd Allah al-Himsi al-Na'imi; Salam al-Abrash; and Habib ibn Bahriz — through whose hands many Greek books were transmitted. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 337} These translators, as Ibn al-Nadim presents them in this section, worked at rendering books of medicine, philosophy and the sciences of the heavens from Greek into Arabic in the age of al-Ma'mun, forming the link by which the ancient sciences passed to the people of Islam. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 337}
