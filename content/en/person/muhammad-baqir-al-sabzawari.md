## Overview
Muhammad Baqir ibn Muhammad Mu'min al-Khurasani al-Sabzawari was an Imami jurist, that is, from the Twelver Shi'a, so what follows is read within that frame. His family came from Sabzawar, the chief town of Bayhaq in Khurasan; he travelled to Iraq and then settled in Isfahan, where he was shaykh al-Islam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. Al-A'lam gives his death as 1090 AH, that is 1679 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «Dhakhirat al-Ma'ad fi Sharh al-Irshad», in manuscript at the Chester Beatty library under number 3217, and «Kifayat al-Ahkam», in manuscript, both extensive works of jurisprudence, the first left unfinished; «Rawdat al-Anwar», on ethics, printed; and a treatise on the direction of the qibla («Samt al-Qibla»), in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
