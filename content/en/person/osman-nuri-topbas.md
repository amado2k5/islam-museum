## Summary
Osman Nuri Topbaş is a Turkish Sufi master and author, born in Istanbul on 21 January 1942. He is the spiritual leader of the Naqshbandi Sufi community based in Kadıköy, Istanbul — the community known as the Erenköy community — and the author of religious books that are published free of charge online in 43 languages. {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-en}

## Family and early life
Topbaş was born in Erenköy, in Istanbul's Kadıköy district. His father was Musa Topbaş, who led the Erenköy community before him, and his mother was Fatma Feride, a daughter of H. Fahri Kığılı; the family traces its roots to Kadınhanı in Konya province. {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-tr} {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-en}

## The Erenköy community and his work
The Turkish article describes him as a cleric, an Islamic scholar and a Sufi (mutasavvıf), and names the Erenköy community as the circle his teaching guides; the English article likewise introduces him as the spiritual leader of the Naqshbandi order based in Kadıköy. {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-tr} {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-en} His talks and books circulate from Istanbul across the Turkish-speaking world and in translation; his recorded sermons reach a wide online audience, and his YouTube channel counted about 179 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 1 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-en}

## Books
Topbaş is a prolific author of works on Islamic belief, worship and spiritual discipline. His books are offered free of charge online in 43 languages through his publishing house's website, a reach the English article notes from the publisher's own catalogue. {cite:wikipedia-osman-nuri-topbas-en}
