## Overview
[[person/suraqa-ibn-amr-mutah|Suraqa ibn Amr ibn Atiyya ibn Khansa']] al-Ansari, of the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar, who was killed as a martyr on the day of Mu'ta {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.1457}.

## Mu'ta
Ibn Lahi'a reports, from Abu al-Aswad, from Urwa, the naming of those of the Ansar who were killed as martyrs on the day of Mu'ta, and of the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar: Suraqa ibn Amr ibn Atiyya {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.1457}. Ibn Ishaq also mentions him, saying: "Of the Ansar, Suraqa ibn Amr ibn Atiyya was killed as a martyr on the day of Mu'ta" {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.1457}.

## For young visitors
Suraqa ibn Amr, of the Banu Mazin among the Ansar, was killed as a martyr in the expedition of Mu'ta.

## Notes for researchers
- The names of those killed at Mu'ta in this text are transmitted by Abu Nu'aym from Urwa and from Ibn Ishaq. We have not yet compared them with Ibn Ishaq's own text.
- Do not confuse this entry with "Suraqa ibn Malik al-Mudlaji", the companion of the Hijra, nor with "Suraqa ibn Umayr", mentioned on the same page.
