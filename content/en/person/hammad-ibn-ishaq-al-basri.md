## Overview
Hammad ibn Ishaq ibn Isma'il ibn Hammad ibn Sa'd, client of al-Hasan ibn 'Ali; born around 120 AH, he narrated from Hisham ibn 'Urwa, al-A'mash, Ibn Abi Khalid, Idris ibn Yazid al-Awdi, Ajlah al-Kindi, Akhwas ibn Hakim al-Shami, Usama ibn Zayd al-Laythi, Burayd ibn 'Abdallah ibn Abi Burda, Muhriz ibn Hakim, Hatim ibn Abi Saghira, Habib ibn al-Shahid, al-Hasan ibn al-Hakam al-Nakha'i, Sa'd ibn Sa'id al-Ansari and others. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1551}
