## Overview
Abu Sa'id Uthman ibn Abi al-Ala' Idris ibn Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Haqq, shaykh of the holy warriors in Granada in the time of the Banu al-Ahmar. He died on Sunday, the second of Dhu al-Hijja in the year 730 AH {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.488-489}.

## The shaykhate of the holy warriors
al-Maqqari reports that the Banu al-Ahmar agreed to give the shaykhate of the holy warriors to one of the relatives of the Banu Marin, because they were the first to rule al-Andalus when their cousins took the kingdom of the Maghrib, so there was no rivalry between them {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.488-489}.

## The inscription on his grave
Ibn Khaldun transmits what was written on his grave: that he lived eighty-eight years, which he spent in the way of God, and that he had completed, according to what is well known, seven hundred and thirty-two raids. The inscription described him as the shaykh of the defenders and the sword of jihad {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.488-489}.
