Asadullah Yate is a British translator of classical Islamic texts. He studied at the University of Cambridge and received his doctorate there in 1987 with a dissertation on Ibn Rushd as a jurist. He studied with Shaykh Abdalqadir as-Sufi and translated books on his behalf, among them Mahmud Shabistari's Secret Garden, and his translation of al-Mawardi's Al-Ahkam as-Sultaniyyah: The Laws of Islamic Governance was published by Ta-Ha Publishers in 1996.{cite:taha-alahkam-yate-bio-1996}

For Diwan Press he translated Ibn Juzayy's Al-Qawanin al-Fiqhiyyah, working from Arabic, Persian, German and French, and with others from Turkish; the same publisher records that he teaches Arabic and fiqh at the Weimar Institute and is imam al-khateeb at the mosque of Stralsund, Germany.{cite:diwan-press-qawanin-yate-bio-2021} No source reached gives a birth date for him — the queue's 1952 is unattested and is not recorded — and no source names a legal school or an order membership for the man himself, so his record leaves his faith unknown, with his study and his public teaching and prayer role stated in full.{cite:taha-alahkam-yate-bio-1996}{cite:diwan-press-qawanin-yate-bio-2021}
