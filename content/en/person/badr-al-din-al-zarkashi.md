## Overview
Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Zarkashi, a Shafi'i jurist who died in 794 AH. He is known for his book [[work/ilam-al-sajid|I'lam al-Sajid bi-Ahkam al-Masajid]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.518}

The year of death is converted to the Gregorian calendar, and the conversion is derived from the Hijri year.

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Bahadur ibn Abd Allah, Badr al-Din al-Zarkashi, of Turkish origin and Egyptian: born in 745, he studied with al-Isnawi and al-Bulqini, and with Ibn Kathir in Damascus. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/397} His works include al-Bahr in legal theory, al-Khadim and al-Tanqih on al-Bukhari. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/397} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/398} He kept to his house, going out only to the book market where he read all day, and died in Cairo on 3 Rajab 794 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/398}

## In the biography of the Dar al-Hadith edition of al-Burhan
Abu al-Fadl al-Dimyati's biography names him Muhammad ibn Bahadur ibn Abd Allah al-Zarkashi, Badr al-Din Abu Abd Allah, and the title page of his edition gives his dates as 745–794 AH. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.9} {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.2} Among his teachers it lists Jamal al-Din [[person/al-isnawi-abd-al-rahim|al-Isnawi]], [[person/siraj-al-din-al-bulqini|Siraj al-Din al-Bulqini]], Shihab al-Din al-Adhra'i and Salah al-Din Umar ibn Umayla. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.9} It lists thirty-two of his works, among them al-Bahr al-Muhit, [[work/al-burhan-fi-ulum-al-quran-zarkashi|al-Burhan fi Ulum al-Qur'an]], Tashnif al-Masami' bi-Jam' al-Jawami', al-Dibaj fi Tawdih al-Minhaj, al-Ijaba li-Irad ma Istadrakathu A'isha 'ala al-Sahaba, I'lam al-Sajid bi-Ahkam al-Masajid, Zahr al-Arish fi Ahkam al-Hashish and al-Nukat 'ala Ibn al-Salah. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.9} {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.10} It says he died on Sunday 3 Rajab 794 AH. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.10}
