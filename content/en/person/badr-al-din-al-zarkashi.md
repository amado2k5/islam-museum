## Overview
Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Zarkashi, a Shafi'i jurist who died in 794 AH. He is known for his book [[work/ilam-al-sajid|I'lam al-Sajid bi-Ahkam al-Masajid]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.518}

The year of death is converted to the Gregorian calendar, and the conversion is derived from the Hijri year.

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Bahadur ibn Abd Allah, Badr al-Din al-Zarkashi, of Turkish origin and Egyptian: born in 745, he studied with al-Isnawi and al-Bulqini, and with Ibn Kathir in Damascus. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/397} His works include al-Bahr in legal theory, al-Khadim and al-Tanqih on al-Bukhari. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/397} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/398} He kept to his house, going out only to the book market where he read all day, and died in Cairo on 3 Rajab 794 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/398}
