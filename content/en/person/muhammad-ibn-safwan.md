## Overview
Muhammad ibn Safwan, Abu Marhab, al-Ansari, a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3137}

## Name
Sources give the name both as Muhammad ibn Safwan and as Safwan ibn Muhammad. al-Tabari prefers the first; Ibn Abd al-Barr says the second is more common. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3137}

## Lineage
Ibn Abi Khaythama did not know his Ansari clan; al-Askari places him among Banu Malik of the Aws. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3137}
