## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Husam al-Din Muhammad al-Akhsikathi, a scholar from Akhsikath in the Ferghana valley. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Death
He died in 644 AH (1247 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} The date rests on tradition and needs an additional source.
