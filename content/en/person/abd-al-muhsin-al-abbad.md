## Summary
Abd al-Muhsin al-Abbad is among the senior Saudi hadith shaykhs, his name bound to Madinah’s university and Prophet’s Mosque for over half a century. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-muhsin-al-abbad}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1934 in Zulfi, Saudi Arabia, he studied at the College of Sharia at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud University then joined the Islamic University of Madinah as teacher, dean and vice president. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-muhsin-al-abbad}

## Career and works
He taught the Six Books in the Prophet’s Mosque for decades, graduating generations of hadith students. His works include Fath al-Qawi al-Matin, a commentary on the Forty Hadith, a multi-volume unfinished commentary on Sunan Abi Dawud, and the epistle Rifqan Ahl al-Sunna bi Ahl al-Sunna on conduct among students of knowledge. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-muhsin-al-abbad}

## Method and contribution
His style is a calm isnadic narration reading hadith through fiqh. He is honoured as one of the last pioneering generation in Madinah, while debates surround some of his pronouncements on others (such as Sururi-method shaykhs); the museum attributes these descriptions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-muhsin-al-abbad}
