## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Yahya al-Suli, the chess-player and historian, author of "al-Awraq fi Akhbar al-Abbasiyyin", who died in 335 AH in the caliphate of al-Muti'. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.463}

## His books
He gathered a book on the reports and poetry of Abu Tammam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.463} Al-Suyuti relied on him in "History of the Caliphs". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## Reports in al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi relates that he had a promise from al-Mu'tadid, so he composed an ode in which he mentioned Badr, and al-Mu'tadid laughed and ordered him given what he had promised. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.636-637}

He also relates that al-Suli played chess with al-Mawardi in the presence of al-Muktafi, and beat him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.677}
