## Overview
One of the sons of the caliph [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], who took charge of the raids and the command of the pilgrimage. al-Salabi reports that he appointed the people to the pilgrimage in the year 123 AH {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.470}.

al-Zuhri used to accompany Hisham's sons on the pilgrimage and guide them.
