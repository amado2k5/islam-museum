## Summary
Mahmud ibn Maslama ibn Salama, al-Ansari, brother of Muhammad ibn Maslama — a Companion who witnessed Uhud, the Trench, al-Hudaybiyya and Khaybar, and was martyred at Khaybar {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1374}.

## His entry in al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar says: the authorities list him among the Companions, martyred in the Prophet's lifetime — so Musa ibn Uqba in al-Maghazi from Ibn Shihab, likewise Abu al-Aswad from Urwa, and Muhammad ibn Ishaq and others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1374}. Ibn Ishaq says the first fortress of Khaybar to be conquered was Na'im's fort, and it was there that Mahmud ibn Maslama was killed: a millstone was dropped on him and killed him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1374}. Ibn al-Kalbi says Muhammad ibn Maslama was struck from the fortress by a stone that damaged his eyes — Marhab threw it — so the Prophet turned to his brother and said "tomorrow your brother's killer will be killed", and so it was {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1374}.

## His martyrdom on Ibn Sa'd's account
Ibn Sa'd says Mahmud witnessed Uhud, the Trench, al-Hudaybiyya and Khaybar, and was killed that day a martyr: Marhab let down a millstone on him that struck his head, shattered the helmet onto his skull and made the skin of his brow fall over his face; he was brought to the Prophet, who set the skin back so that it returned as it was and bound it with a cloth; Mahmud lingered three days then died, and Muhammad killed Marhab that very day, with Ali ibn Abi Talib standing over Marhab after Muhammad had struck him down; Mahmud and Amir ibn al-Akwa' were buried in a single grave {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1374}.

## For younger visitors
Mahmud ibn Maslama was an Ansari Companion who fought in the campaigns from Uhud to Khaybar; he was killed beneath Na'im's fort by a millstone Marhab dropped on him, and it was his brother Muhammad ibn Maslama who killed Marhab that day, as the Prophet had foretold.
