## Overview
"Ibrahim al-Hajuri" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen. The name points to Hajur, whose men came in numbers to al-Hadi at [[event/al-hadi-goes-out-to-tihama-and-camps-at-al-sharis-and-al-uyayn-292|his halt at al-Sharis]]; the text does not say that he is the [[person/ibrahim-ibn-sulayman-al-hajuri|Ibrahim ibn Sulayman al-Hajuri]] of other reports on al-Hadi, so the museum does not merge the two records. He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
