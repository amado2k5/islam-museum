## Overview
Al-Malik al-Nasir Salah al-Din Dawud b. al-Malik al-Mu'azzam 'Isa; lord of Damascus after his father. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## The poet's praise
For his birth a Baghdad poet named Ibn 'Unayn praised him in verse, and al-Malik al-Nasir sent him three hundred dinars as a gift. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
