## Overview
Nu'aym ibn Yazid al-Tamimi was among the men of the [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|delegation of Banu Tamim]] that came to the Prophet in 9 AH, as Ibn Ishaq names him. Nothing more is recorded about him than his name {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.212}.

## Islam
The whole delegation became Muslim after the boasting contest {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.212}. He was of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]].
