## Overview
Fatima bint al-Kharshub al-Anmariyya, of Ghatafan, was a woman of Jahiliyya proverbial for bearing perfect sons — 'more fertile than Fatima' — the wife of Ziyad ibn Sufyan al-'Absi. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.130}

She bore him four sons described as the 'complete ones': al-Rabi' al-Kamil, Qays al-Haffaz, 'Umara al-Wahhab and Anas al-Fawaris. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.130}
