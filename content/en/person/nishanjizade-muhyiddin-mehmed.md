## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad Muhyi al-Din, known as Nishanjizade, was a Hanafi jurist of Rum (Ottoman lands). Al-Zirikli says he served as judge in Edirne and died there. He gives his birth as 962 AH, 1555 CE, and his death as 1031 AH, 1622 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 8}.

## Works
Among his Arabic works al-Zirikli lists: al-Fatawa al-Rumiyya; Nur al-aynayn, in manuscript at al-Azhar, on law, in which he abridged Jami al-fusulayn; Mir'at al-ayyam fi mirqat al-a'lam; and Maqsad al-umma min musnad al-a'imma {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 8}.
