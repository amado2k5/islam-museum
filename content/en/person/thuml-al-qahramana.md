## Overview
Thumal al-Qahramana, the housekeeper (qahramana) of the mother of al-Muqtadir, that is, the supervisor of her household affairs. al-Suyuti reports that the giving of orders and prohibitions passed to the caliph's harem and its women, and that the mother of al-Muqtadir ordered her to sit for hearing grievances and to examine people's petitions each Friday {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.589}.

Judges and notables would attend her council, and the decrees would be issued bearing her handwriting {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.589}.

Her lineage and the year of her death are not given here. The entry needs an additional source.
