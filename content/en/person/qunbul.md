## Overview
Qunbul — Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muhammad b. Khalid b. Sa'id b. Jurji al-Makki al-Makhzumi — the transmitter of Ibn Kathir the muqri'; he died in 291 aged ninety-six {cite:wafayat-ayan}.

## Chain and reading in Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr

Ibn al-Jazari gives him as Abu 'Umar Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn Jurja al-Makhzumi al-Makki, who died in 291 AH, born in 195, and was a careful and exact imam of the reading to whom the headship of teaching in the Hijaz came; people travelled to him from every region. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 129} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 130} His chain reaches [[person/abdallah-ibn-kathir|Ibn Kathir]] through al-Qist, Ma'ruf ibn Mushkan and Shibl ibn 'Abbad. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 130} [[person/ibn-mujahid|Ibn Mujahid]] and Ibn Shannabudh read on him; Ibn Mujahid was the first to fix the number of the readers at seven, and Ibn Shannabudh held that reading by a sound chain was permitted even where it differed from the written text, a view over which a council was held, as Ibn al-Jazari relates. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 129} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 131} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 132}
