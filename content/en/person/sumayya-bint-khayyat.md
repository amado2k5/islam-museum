## Overview
[[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya bint Khayyat]], mother of [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]], the first martyr in Islam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}.

## Torture in Mecca
Ammar was a client of the Banu Makhzum. He and his father and his mother embraced Islam, and the polytheists, led by [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]], used to bring them out to al-Abtah when the sand grew hot, and torture them with its heat {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}. The Prophet passed by them while they were being tortured, and said: "Be patient, family of Yasir, for your appointment is Paradise" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}.

## Her death
Yasir, Ammar's father, died under torture, and "Abu Jahl stabbed Sumayya, the mother of Ammar, in her private parts with a spear, and she died, and she was the first martyr in Islam" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}.

## For young visitors
Sumayya, mother of Ammar, was one of the earliest Muslims. The polytheists in Mecca tortured her because she had embraced Islam, and she endured until she died. For this reason her name is always remembered among the first martyrs.
