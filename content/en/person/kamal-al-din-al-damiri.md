## Overview
Muhammad ibn Musa ibn Isa ibn Ali al-Damiri, Abu al-Baqa', Kamal al-Din, was a researcher and man of letters among the Shafi'i jurists {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He came from Damira in Egypt and was born, raised and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 742 AH, 1341 CE, and his death as 808 AH, 1405 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}.

## Life
He earned his living by tailoring, then turned to learning, issued fatwas and taught {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He had a circle of his own at al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He lived for some time in Mecca and Medina {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists Hayat al-Hayawan, printed in two volumes, and Hawi al-Hisan min Hayat al-Hayawan, in manuscript, which he abridged himself from his book; al-Zirikli takes this from Ali al-Khaqani in the journal of the Iraqi Academy {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He lists al-Dibaja, a commentary on the hadith work of [[person/ibn-majah|Ibn Majah]] in five volumes {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He lists al-Najm al-Wahhaj, in manuscript, a part of it, a commentary on the Minhaj of [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]], and an urjuza on jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}. He also lists an abridgement, in manuscript, of the commentary on Lamiyyat al-Ajam by [[person/al-safadi|al-Safadi]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 118}.
