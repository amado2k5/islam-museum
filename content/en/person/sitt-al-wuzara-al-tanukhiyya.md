## Overview
Sitt al-Wuzara' bint Umar ibn As'ad ibn al-Munajja al-Tanukhiyya, a Hanbali, Umm Muhammad, called Wazira, was a woman jurist and hadith transmitter. Al-Zirikli gives her birth as 624 AH, 1227 CE, and her death as 716 AH, 1316 CE, and says she was born and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 78}.

## Her transmission of hadith
She took the Sahih of al-Bukhari from Abu Abdallah al-Zubaydi and transmitted it, together with the Musnad of al-Shafi'i, in Damascus and then in Egypt in 705 AH, several times {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 78}.

## What was said of her
Al-Zirikli reports that al-Maqrizi knew her as the long-lived musnida; that Ibn Taghribirdi said she became the destination of travel of her time, with people journeying to her from many regions; and that Ibn al-Imad called her the musnida of her age and said she was of great goodness {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 78}.
