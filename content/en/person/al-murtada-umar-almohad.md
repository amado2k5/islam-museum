## Overview
Abu Hafs Umar ibn Abi Ibrahim ibn Yusuf, titled al-Murtada, an Almohad caliph. He was killed around 665 AH. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.15-16}

## His end
Ibn Khallikan relates that he was installed in Rabi al-Akhir, before al-Wathiq Abu Dabbus entered Marrakesh in 665 AH. He fled to Azmur, where its governor seized him. Al-Wathiq ordered him killed, and he was killed in the last ten days of Rabi al-Akhir that year, at a place called Kutama, three days from Marrakesh. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.15-16}
