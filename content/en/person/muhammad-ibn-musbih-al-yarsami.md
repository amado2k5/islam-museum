## Overview
Muhammad ibn Musbih al-Yarsami is a man of [[group/al-yarsamiyyun-saada|the Yarsamis]]. On the second sortie he told [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] that he was leading his men into peril while the enemy had the upper hand, and that he feared they would be struck; al-Hadi answered that he would soon gladden him, God willing, and he left saying, according to the narrator, that al-Hadi's men would be disgraced and killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} After the enemy's defeat and the pulling down of [[place/al-numays-allaf-yemen|al-Numays]] he came to al-Hadi, and the account breaks off at the foot of the page. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} Apple Vision reads the name as Musbih; the print gives no clear vowels. See [[event/al-hadi-musters-at-saada-on-2-ramadan-and-scouts-al-sabra-287]]. What he said to al-Hadi on arriving is on the next page, in [[event/ibn-musbih-congratulates-al-hadi-and-the-spoils-of-al-numays-287]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196}
