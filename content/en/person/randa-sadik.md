## Summary
Randa Sadik is the chief executive of Arab Bank, one of the largest financial institutions in the Arab world — and the first woman to lead it in its nine-decade history. She was appointed in February 2022 after more than thirty years inside the bank. {cite:wikipedia-randa-sadik}

## Career at Arab Bank
Educated in economics and finance, Sadik joined Arab Bank in the late 1980s and spent her career in its treasury and investment operations, rising to deputy chief executive officer before the board named her CEO. The appointment made her, at the time, one of the most senior women in Middle Eastern banking. {cite:wikipedia-randa-sadik}

## Arab Bank under her leadership
Arab Bank, founded in Jerusalem in 1930 and headquartered in Amman, operates hundreds of branches across dozens of countries on five continents. Leading it puts Sadik at the head of an institution central to the region's corporate, trade and private banking — and frequently among the most profitable banks of the Arab world. Profiles of her tenure note both continuity in the bank's conservative culture and pressure to modernise digital services. {cite:muslim500-randa-sadik}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as the most powerful woman in Arab banking — a lifelong insider who reached the top of one of the region's oldest and largest financial institutions. {cite:muslim500-randa-sadik}
