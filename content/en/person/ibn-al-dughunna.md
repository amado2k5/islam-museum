## Overview
Ibn al-Dughunna, a noble of the Qara tribe, who gave protection to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] when he resolved to emigrate to Abyssinia, and brought him back with him to Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.117} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/548}

## What is not stated here
The book does not give his full lineage or his death; these are to be filled in from its sources when he is mentioned again.

## For young visitors
Ibn al-Dughunna was a generous man who gave protection to Abu Bakr, so Abu Bakr returned with him to Mecca, because a protected person has a right to that protection.
