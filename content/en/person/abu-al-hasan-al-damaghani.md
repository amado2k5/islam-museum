## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan al-Damaghani, the Hanafi chief qadi; he held the judgeship one year and twenty, a man of firmness, judgment, mastery, abundant awe and manifest piety — he read fiqh under his father and transmitted from Abu Muhammad al-Sarifini and a company. He died in 513 at the Haram at sixty-four. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 520}
