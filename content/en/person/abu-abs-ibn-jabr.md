## Overview
[[person/abu-abs-ibn-jabr|Abu Abs ibn Jabr]] ibn Amr ibn Zayd al-Ansari al-Awsi of the Banu Haritha, known by his kunya. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} It is said that his name in the Jahiliyya was Abd al-'Uzza, and it is said Ma'bad, and the Prophet named him Abd al-Rahman. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724}

## What is related of him
Ibn Hajar relates from Ibn al-Kalbi that he was "one of those who killed Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} Ibn Mandah gives a report of this with a chain reaching his grandson, in which [[person/kab-ibn-al-ashraf|Ka'b]] used to make poetry and disparage the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} See [[event/killing-of-kab-ibn-al-ashraf|the killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf]]. Musa ibn Uqba and others mention him among those who attended [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]]. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} It is said that he was forty-eight years old at the time, and that he and Abu Burda were breaking the idols of the Banu Haritha when they accepted Islam. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724}

## The lamp that gave light
Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar relates in "al-Muwaffaqiyyat" that the Prophet gave him a staff after his sight had gone, and told him to light his way by it, and it used to shine for him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724}

## His death and transmission of hadith
Al-Madaini says that he died in 34 AH at the age of seventy, and [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] prayed over him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} His hadith on the merit of walking in the way of God is in al-Bukhari through Abaya ibn Rifa'a from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} His son Zayd and his grandson Abu Abs ibn Muhammad transmitted from him, and Uthman visited him, and he had been at Badr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724} Ibn Sa'd says that the Prophet made brothers between him and [[person/khunays-ibn-hudhafa|Khunays ibn Hudhafa]]. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1724}

## A note
In the next entry Ibn Hajar mentions another man with the kunya Abu Abs, Ibn Amir ibn Adi al-Sulami, who attended Badr according to Ibn al-Kalbi. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1725} The two should not be confused.
