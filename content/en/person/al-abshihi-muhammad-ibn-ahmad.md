## Overview
Abu al-Fath Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Mansur al-Abshihi, an Egyptian man of letters and author in the Mamluk period. He gathered reports and anecdotes in his books. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.183}

## Dates
He was born in 790 AH (1388 CE) and died in 852 AH (1448 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.183}
