## Summary
Al-Qadi al-Muhadhdhab Abu 'Ali Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn 'Ali, brother of al-Qadi al-Rashid ibn al-Zubayr; both were accomplished in verse and prose, and he has a diwan. He first composed verse in 526; he was the better poet of the two, while al-Rashid was more learned in the other sciences. Al-'Imad al-Katib mentioned him in *al-Sall wa'l-dhayl*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
