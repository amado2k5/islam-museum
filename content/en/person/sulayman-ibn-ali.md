## Overview
Sulayman ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas. Abu al-Abbas appointed him over [[place/basra|Basra]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

al-Baladhuri reports that he built, on the walls of the rooms that Adi ibn Artat had raised, a structure of mud brick. He then abandoned it and moved to al-Mirbad, and settled there {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
