## Summary
Omar Hisham Al-Arabi is an Egyptian Qur'an reciter who recites in the Hafs 'an Asim narration. A complete recorded recitation of his is carried by the Muslim Central and Quran Central platforms as a podcast running since 8 July 2019. He is known worldwide through his official YouTube channel, which has published his recitations for about nine years; individual recitations have reached tens of millions of views, among them Surah Ar-Rahman at about 59 million. {cite:muslim-central-omar-hisham-al-arabi} {cite:youtube-omar-hisham-al-arabi}

## Limits of documentation
Omar Hisham Al-Arabi has no Wikipedia article in any language, and the profiles available do not document his birth date, home town, teachers or any public role as imam or teacher; his Muslim Central profile itself notes that his education, teachers and significant life events are not widely documented. This record is therefore limited to what the sources establish: his Egyptian origin, his Hafs 'an Asim narration, his complete recorded recitation and his official channel. {cite:muslim-central-omar-hisham-al-arabi}
