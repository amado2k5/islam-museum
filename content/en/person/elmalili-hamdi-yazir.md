## Summary
Ottoman-Turkish scholar, jurist and exegete (1878–1942), surnamed Elmalili after his birthplace. The Turkish government commissioned him to write a Qur'an translation and commentary in Turkish, producing the nine-volume Hak Dini Kur'an Dili — the most famous modern Turkish tafsir; he also sat in parliament and served as minister of pious foundations. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

## Life and career

Muhammad Hamdi Yazir was born in 1878 in Elmali, Antalya, in the Ottoman state; schooled there and then in Istanbul's religious colleges, he took his ijaza and taught in the dars institutes and higher schools. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

He took part in late-Ottoman intellectual and political life: he entered the Chamber of Deputies as member for Antalya, served as minister of pious foundations (awqaf), and worked on the legal committees drafting the Mecelle's Islamic provisions. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

## His tafsir

Under the Republic, the Presidency of Religious Affairs commissioned him to write a Qur'an translation and commentary in Turkish; over nine years he produced Hak Dini Kur'an Dili ('the Language of the Religion of Truth') in nine volumes, combining linguistic, juristic, kalam and spiritual exegesis. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

The tafsir became the first Turkish reference of its kind, written in polished Turkish on the basis of wide reading in the great commentaries and the Hanafi fiqh to which he belonged; it is still printed and read. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

## Other works and legacy

Among his other works are Irshad al-Ahlaf on Arabic rhetoric and Metalib ve Mezahib on moral philosophy — described as the first Islamic-philosophy book in Turkish — plus translations and treatises on logic and jurisprudence. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}

He died in Istanbul in 1942, remembered in Turkey as one of the last great Ottoman scholars straddling the medrese school and the republican modern age; his tafsir continues to be esteemed and taught. {cite:wikipedia-elmalili-hamdi-yazir}
