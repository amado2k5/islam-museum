## Summary
Najla al-Awadhi broke two ceilings in the United Arab Emirates: one of the first women elected to the Federal National Council and the first Arab woman to run a major broadcaster's television portfolio as CEO of channels at Dubai Media Incorporated. {cite:muslim500-najla-al-awadhi} {cite:wikipedia-najla-al-awadhi}

## Formation
An Emirati of Dubai, she studied mass communication in the United States before entering television management in the 2000s, when Dubai was consolidating itself as the region's media hub. {cite:wikipedia-najla-al-awadhi}

## Career
Elected to the Federal National Council in 2006 — among the body's first female members and its youngest parliamentarian — she served on parliamentary committees while managing Dubai Media Incorporated's television channels, overseeing news and entertainment output. She later worked in media strategy and became a frequent commentator on Arab media development. {cite:muslim500-najla-al-awadhi} {cite:wikipedia-najla-al-awadhi}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists her under Media as a pioneer of women's leadership in Gulf broadcasting and a symbol of the UAE's managed political opening to elected bodies. {cite:muslim500-najla-al-awadhi}
