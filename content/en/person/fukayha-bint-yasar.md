## Summary
Wife of Hattab ibn al-Harith al-Jumahi; she emigrated to Abyssinia and returned in one of the two ships after her husband perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her wife of Hattab ibn al-Harith, saying she came in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} He counts her in the women's list among "the rarities of the Arabs" with Baraka bint Yasar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The women's list on p. 11 gives "Fukayha bint Yasar" and "Baraka bint Yasar" side by side; the text does not say they were sisters, and Baraka is on p. 6 "freedwoman of Abu Sufyan ibn Harb" — so we assert no kinship between them.
