## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Ghaffar al-Iji, Abu al-Fadl 'Adud al-Din, was a scholar of the principles, rhetoric and Arabic, of Ij in Fars; he held the judgeship and produced great pupils, dying in 756 AH (1355 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.294}

He fell into a trial with the lord of Kirman, who imprisoned him in the citadel, where he died. His works include al-Mawaqif on kalam; al-'Aqa'id al-'Adudiyya; al-Risala al-'Adudiyya on the science of wad'; Jawahir al-Kalam, an abridgement of the Mawaqif; a commentary on Ibn al-Hajib's Mukhtasar on usul al-fiqh; al-Fawa'id al-Ghiyathiyya on ma'ani and bayan; Ashraf al-Tawarikh; and al-Madkhal on the sciences of ma'ani, bayan and badi'. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.294}
