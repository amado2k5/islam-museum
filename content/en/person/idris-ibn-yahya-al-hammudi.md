## Overview
Idris ibn Yahya al-Hammudi, a Hammudid caliph in Málaga, of the line of Idris al-Alawi. Al-Maqqari mentions him in *Nafh al-Tib* in an account involving the poet Ibn Muqana al-Ishbuni. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.250}

## The poem
Ibn Muqana came before his chamberlain and recited to him his well-known *nuniyya* ode. When he reached the description of his face, the caliph raised the curtain himself and said: "Look as you wish." He relaxed with the poet and treated him well. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.250}
