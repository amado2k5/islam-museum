## Overview
Secretary-general of the Islamic Research Academy at al-Azhar al-Sharif; he wrote the foreword to the edition of Usul al-Tarbiya wa'l-Ta'lim fi'l-Qur'an al-Karim wa'l-Sunna al-Nabawiyya dated 18 Jumada I 1447, corresponding to 9 November 2025 {cite:usul-al-tarbiya-azhar p. 10}.
