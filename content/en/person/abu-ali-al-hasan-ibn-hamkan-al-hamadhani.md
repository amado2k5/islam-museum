## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn ibn Hamkan al-Hamadhani, one of the companions of [[person/abu-hamid-al-marwarudhi|Abu Hamid al-Marwarudhi]]. He settled in Baghdad and taught there. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 62}

The editor's note gives his death as 405 AH; the date needs a second source.
