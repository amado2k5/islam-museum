## Overview
Ibn al-Kalbi — the unique erudite historian and genealogist Abu al-Mundhir Hisham, son of the brilliant historian Muhammad ibn al-Sa'ib ibn Bishr al-Kalbi, Kufan Shi'i; he narrated much from his father and from Mujalid, Abu Mikhnaf and a group, and like his father was among the abandoned narrators. He died in 204 or 206 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4095}
