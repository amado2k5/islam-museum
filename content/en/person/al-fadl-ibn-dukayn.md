## Overview
Abu Nu'aym al-Fadl ibn Dukayn, a client of the family of Talha ibn Ubayd Allah in Kufa, a man of hadith. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.442}

Al-Mas'udi says he died in 219 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.442}

His teachers, his students and his standing among the hadith scholars need an additional source.
