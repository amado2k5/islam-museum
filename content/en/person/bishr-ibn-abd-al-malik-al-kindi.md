## Overview
Bishr ibn Abd al-Malik, brother of Ukaydir ibn Abd al-Malik al-Kindi, then al-Sukuni, lord of Dumat al-Jandal. He was a Christian living in al-Hira, where he learned the Arabic script. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The sources say that he taught writing in Mecca, al-Ta'if, the lands of Mudar and Syria. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
