## Overview
Al-Ruqad al-Mutakki was killed at Herat by Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Abbas al-Hashimi, when he arrived there in the army of Ibn al-Ash'ath. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data hold no details of al-Ruqad or the date of the event, which need an additional source.
