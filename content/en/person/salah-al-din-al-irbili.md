## Overview
Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Sayyid ibn Sha'ban al-Irbili, styled Salah al-Din; cupper to al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Muzaffar al-Din ibn Zayn al-Din, lord of Irbil, who turned against him and imprisoned him for a time; on release he left for Syria in 603 in the company of al-Malik al-Qahir Baha' al-Din Ayyub son of al-Malik al-'Adil, and entered the service of al-Malik al-Mughith; when al-Mughith died he moved to Egypt and served al-Malik al-Kamil, rising in his favour until he became master of the pigeon-post — a man of complete virtue and fair participation. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Ordeal and release
In the Shadharat: al-Kamil turned on him and imprisoned him, but being a fine composer he made a dubayt and dictated it to a songstress who sang it; asked whose it was she said "Salah al-Din al-Irbili's" — so he freed him and restored his standing better than before; it was he who settled the affair of al-Kamil's brother al-Fa'iz Ibrahim and wrote to him about it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
