Hassam Ali Khan (born 16 January 1990), known as Hassam Khan, is a Pakistani actor who began as a theatre actor in 2011 while completing his bachelor's degree, appearing in stage productions including Anwar Maqsood's Pawnay 14 August.{cite:wikipedia-hassam-khan-en} He moved to television and became known for the lead role of Salman in Geo TV's drama serial Noor Jahan (2016), followed by roles as Azher in ARY Digital's Khasara (2018) and Akmal in Dil Mom Ka Diya (2018).{cite:wikipedia-hassam-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-hassam-khan}
