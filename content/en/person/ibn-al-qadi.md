## Overview
Shaykh and man of letters Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Maghribi al-Maqqari, a resident of Egypt. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 380}

He is the author of [[work/azhar-al-riyad|Azhar al-Riyad fi Akhbar Iyad]] (The Flowers of the Gardens: On the Reports of Iyad). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 380}
