## Summary
Sa'd al-Din Ibn Hamuwayh, the sufi shaykh of the Juwayni house, accompanied his uncle Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Hamuwayh and travelled with him to Egypt in the retinue of Fakhr al-Din Ibn al-Shaykh. He described the captives on the day of the victory of al-Mansura as more than twenty thousand, kings and notables among them, the slain numbering seven thousand. He was among the shaykhs of the Path, with states and sayings. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248, 259}
