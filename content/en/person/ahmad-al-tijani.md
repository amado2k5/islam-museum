## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Mukhtar al-Tijani, the shaykh and founder of the Tijaniyya order. He was born at Ain Madi in Algeria. The encyclopaedia says he was born around 1737 CE (1150 AH) and died around 1815 CE (1230 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are recorded as reported dates and need an additional source. The details of the order and its spread need an additional source.
