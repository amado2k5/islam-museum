## Summary
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Qasim, al-Halabi al-Hanafi al-Simnani, named after Saman, a town in Qumis between Damghan and Khuwar al-Rayy. A Hanafi jurist and historian, he studied law with the chief judge Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ali al-Damghani and studied theology and legal theory with Abu Ali Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn al-Walid. His works include Rawdat al-qudat wa-tariq al-najat on the duties of judges, and al-Urwa al-wuthqa on contracts and conditions. The book gives his death as 499 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6804}
