## Summary
Abdul Hai Muhammad Saifullah is a Bangladeshi scholar, preacher and author, and the khatib of Masjidul Juma Complex in Pallabi, Dhaka. {cite:youtube-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah} {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026} He graduated in Al-Hadith from Islamic University, Kushtia with the President's Gold Medal, is a student of Dr Khandaker Abdullah Jahangir, and has published books since 2021. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} His lectures and sermons reach a mass audience through his YouTube channel and his verified Facebook page. {cite:vidiq-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah} {cite:facebook-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah}

## Early life and education
Saifullah was born and raised in Naogaon town, where he also received his primary schooling, according to the author biography carried by his publishers and booksellers. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} He completed his honours and masters in the Al-Hadith department of Islamic University, Kushtia, receiving the university's President's Gold Medal for standing faculty-first. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} He took his MPhil from the same institution, where he is currently a PhD researcher. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} He is a student of the noted Bangladeshi scholar Dr Khandaker Abdullah Jahangir. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} No source read in this run gives his birth date, so none is recorded here; the number in his channel's legacy YouTube username is not a birth year and is not used as one. {cite:youtube-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah}

## Career and the khatib post
Saifullah serves as khatib of Masjidul Juma Complex in Pallabi, Dhaka, as his channel's own About text states in Bengali and as the October 2026 press reports confirm. {cite:youtube-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah} {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026} In parallel he works as religious director at EBS, a private telecom services company, according to his publisher biography. {cite:publisher-bio-saifullah} Bangladeshi press presents him as an Islamic speaker and researcher, and he is a fixture of the country's national waz circuit. {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026}

## Reported positions
This section sets out a position as reported in the sources. It is not a verdict by the museum.

**The madrasa child-safety statement, October 2026.** On Friday 23 October 2026 Saifullah published a statement on his verified page, against the background of a child-abuse case at a madrasa in Tongi that had drawn nationwide discussion, writing that covering up wrongdoing inside religious educational institutions while speaking out against others' wrongdoing is a grave moral offence and doubly harmful. {cite:facebook-abdul-hai-muhammad-saifullah} {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026} He said that discipline, affection and care toward students have a definite limit, that the moral standard for a religious teacher must be stricter still, that such allegations require impartial investigation and exemplary punishment where guilt is proven, and that there is no compromise over the safety of one's own children. {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026} The statement was covered on 24 October 2026 by several Bangladeshi outlets, led by Azadir Dak. {cite:press-saifullah-madrasa-statement-2026}
