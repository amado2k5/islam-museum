## Summary
A Companion of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Amra and came in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him of Banu Amir, with his wife Amra bint al-Sa'di ibn Waqdan ibn Abd Shams. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
