## Summary
Abu 'Ali Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn 'Umar al-Lu'lu'i was a student of [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud al-Sijistani]] and one of the two transmitters through whom the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan]] is handed down, the other being [[person/ibn-dasa|Ibn Dasa]]. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 10}

## Not to be confused
He shares the nisba with [[person/al-lului|al-Hasan ibn Ziyad al-Lu'lu'i]], the companion of Abu Hanifa; they are two different men, the one an early Hanafi jurist, the other the transmitter of Abu Dawud's Sunan. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 10}
