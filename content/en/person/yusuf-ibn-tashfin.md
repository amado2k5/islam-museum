## Overview
Yusuf ibn Tashfin, commander of the Muslims, lord of Ceuta and Marrakesh. In the year 479 AH he sent to al-Muqtadi asking that he be granted the sultanate, and that the lands in his hand be confirmed to him. The caliph sent him the robes, banners and the investiture, and gave him the title "Commander of the Muslims". The jurists of the Maghrib rejoiced at this, and he founded the city of Marrakesh {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.652}.

## Ibn Khaldun's account
Ibn Khaldun reports that he laid out Marrakesh in the year 454 AH, and that al-Mu'tamid ibn Abbad and Ibn al-Aftas met him at al-Zallaqa near Badajoz. Then he returned, seized al-Mu'tamid and led him as a captive to Marrakesh {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1646-1648}.

## In the 'Al-Mubdi'un' programme (part 1 of 2)
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Yusuf ibn Tashfin as 'the sixth rightly-guided caliph' in some historians' estimate — after the Rashidun and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:02:00}.
He sketches the context: the Maghreb shattered with the caliphate's weakness into petty states — Aghlabids, Idrisids, Rustamids, Fatimids — and the Amazigh tribes (a name he prefers over 'Berbers', a European coinage) sank into ignorance until Jahiliyya customs returned {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:05:30}.
The Almoravid movement: the ascetic jurist Abdallah ibn Yasin founded it in 443 AH at a ribat on an island in the Senegal river, supported by Yahya ibn Umar, amir of Lamtuna, becoming a jihad movement to reunify the Maghreb and uproot idolatry {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:10:00}.
In this milieu rose Yusuf ibn Tashfin al-Lamtuni; when Yahya died in 447 AH his brother Abu Bakr ibn Umar took over and made Yusuf, then forty-eight, commander of his armies; and Abdallah ibn Yasin was martyred in 451 AH fighting pagan Barghawata {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:11:30}.
Returning from reconciling the tribes, Abu Bakr found the state ordered in two months and abdicated to his nephew — 'you are worthier in religion, learning and administration' — a rare surrender of power in the history of rulers {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:13:00}.
Of his building: he turned the movement into a complete state, dividing the land into sectors and his forty-thousand-strong army accordingly, founding Marrakesh as capital in 454 AH, joined by the great Masmuda tribe, building a state from Tunis to the Atlantic and from the Mediterranean to the bounds of Mali, Chad, Niger and Senegal {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:19:00}.
Of his asceticism: 'he wore only wool all his life and ate only barley, camel meat and its milk' despite his vast realm, ever inspecting the frontiers and his subjects' affairs, ruling only by God's law {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:18:00}.
He refused the caliphal title though stronger than the Abbasid caliph, seeking from him instead the investiture of the Maghreb to preserve the caliphate's unity — so he was named 'amir of the Muslims and Nasir al-Din' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:20:30}.
On al-Andalus: the age of the taifa kings — twenty-two weak petty states sunk in pleasures paying tribute to Alfonso VI of the Reconquista — and the fall of Toledo in 476 AH that shook the Muslim world and turned every eye toward the deliverer {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep23 00:24:30}.

## In the 'Al-Mubdi'un' programme (part 2)
Al-Suwaidan continues: when al-Andalus' cries for help poured in he gathered the jurists and Almoravid shaykhs who unanimously ruled aid obligatory, and crossed the strait in early 479 AH; when a storm struck he prayed 'O God, if our crossing holds good for the Muslims ease it, and if not, make it hard' and the sea calmed — whence his title 'the one whose prayer is answered' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:06:00}.
His reply to Alfonso VI's long threatening letter: three words on its back, 'you will see what you will see' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:08:00}.
At al-Zallaqa on Friday 12 Rajab 479 AH, aged eighty: he fought at the front sword in hand, his plan a drawing retreat then a strike from the army's rear with the 'Black Guard' Amazigh battalion — the immortal victory {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:10:30}.
On the day of victory came word of the death of his eldest son and heir Abu Bakr, amir of the Maghreb, so he returned to order the realm, leaving a three-thousand-man garrison in al-Andalus under al-Mu'tamid ibn Abbad {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:12:00}.
When eastern al-Andalus — Valencia, Murcia, Mallorca — cried out and the taifa kings refused, al-Andalus' scholars led by Imam al-Baji issued a fatwa deposing the taifa rulers, and a delegation under him crossed and pledged to Yusuf ibn Tashfin {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:15:30}.
He crossed again in Rabi' I 481 AH inviting the taifa kings to join a united jihad keeping their thrones; but some secretly conspired with Alfonso to foil his campaign, fearing for their seats — so he returned harbouring the greater resolve {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:17:30}.
In 483 AH, past eighty-three: he brought down the taifa statelets one by one and united al-Andalus under the Almoravid banner — after consulting scholars as far as al-Ghazali and al-Tartushi in Baghdad, 'I wish to act by religious ruling, not by my own whim' — and the Andalusian people received him {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:22:00}.
He remained an ascetic unaltered by greatness until his death in Muharram 500 AH at a full hundred years of jihad; the speaker distils 'a clear goal, a mighty will, persistence', wishing Yusuf had been in the east when Jerusalem fell {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep24 00:24:30}.
