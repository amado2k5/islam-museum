## Overview
Atarid ibn Muhammad, calculator and astrologer, a learned and excellent man; author of an interpretation of the Indian jafr, books on the use of the astrolabe and of the armillary sphere, a book on the composition of the spheres and one on burning mirrors. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 411}
