## Overview
Atarid ibn Muhammad, a computer and astrologer. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an excellent and learned man. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.387}

Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him a number of books, among them his commentary on the "Hindi Jafr", "The Work with the Astrolabe", "The Work with the Ring-instrument", "The Composition of the Spheres", and "The Burning Mirrors". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.387}
