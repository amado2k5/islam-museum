## Summary
A chief of Banu Amir and brother of the poet Labid by his mother; he drew his sword behind the Prophet in the delegation of year 9 AH but his hand was stayed, as the Sira relates, and a thunderbolt struck him on his return {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}.

## His account
Arbad ibn Qays was Labid's brother by his mother, among Banu Amir's chiefs in their delegation of year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}. He plotted with Amir ibn al-Tufayl to assassinate the Prophet: he circled behind him and drew his sword a span, then God stayed his hand so he could not unsheathe it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}. The Prophet prayed against them both; on their way back God sent a thunderbolt upon Arbad and his camel that burned them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 453}.

## For young visitors
A man of Banu Amir who meant to harm the Prophet with his sword but could not draw it; the Sira relates that a thunderbolt struck him on his way back.
