## Summary
Mohamed El-Sawy is an Egyptian preacher who publishes his sermons and religious talks on his YouTube channel (mohamedelsawyofficial) and his official accounts, known for his moving stories delivered in an emotional narrative style. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial}

He is not the Egyptian actor of the same name, a film and television actor of an older generation; this El-Sawy is a preacher who publishes his own dawah content on social platforms. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial}

No source consulted publishes a birth date for him, so none is recorded here. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial} {cite:facebook-sheikhmuhammadelsawy}

## Career
El-Sawy publishes his sermons and lectures on his YouTube channel, and his uploads' descriptions list his official accounts: Facebook (/sheikhmuhammadelsawy), Twitter (/muhammadalsawy), Instagram (/muhammadelsawyofficial) and SoundCloud (/muhammadelsawy); his Facebook page also publishes his sermons and clips. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial} {cite:facebook-sheikhmuhammadelsawy}

His television appearances include an interview with the presenter Tony Khalifa, one of the topics his channel highlights in its uploads. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial}

## Works
His channel's videos are organised in playlists including "100% True Stories", "Moving Clips", "Featured Clips", "Masterpieces of Sermons and Lectures", "Moving Poems" and "Humble Recitations", and his content turns on affecting sermon-stories, sermons and short religious talks. {cite:youtube-mohamedelsawyofficial}
