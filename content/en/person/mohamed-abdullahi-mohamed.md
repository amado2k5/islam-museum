# Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed

Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed (born 11 March 1962), known as Farmajo, is a Somali politician who served as president of Somalia from 2017 to 2022 and earlier as prime minister from 2010 to 2011.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en}{cite:source/wikidata-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed}

## Mogadishu, Buffalo and public service abroad

Mohamed was born in Mogadishu, of the Marehan clan; his parents were activists of the Somali Youth League, and his father worked as a civil servant in the national Department of Transportation.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en} When the civil war began in 1991 he claimed asylum in Canada and later in the United States, earning bachelor's and master's degrees at the University at Buffalo and working for the New York State Department of Transportation.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en}

## Prime minister, and a popular resignation

In October 2010 President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed appointed him prime minister; he took office on 1 November 2010 and won a wide following for anti-corruption and salary reforms.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en} Under the Kampala Accord between the president and the parliament speaker he was asked to step aside, and he resigned on 19 June 2011 — a departure that drew public protests in Mogadishu.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en} He founded the Tayo party in 2012.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en}

## President

On 8 February 2017 the Federal Parliament elected Mohamed president, defeating the incumbent Hassan Sheikh Mohamud; he took office on 16 February 2017.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en} His administration's final phase was consumed by an electoral dispute: in April 2021 the lower house voted to extend his term by two years, the move provoked armed clashes in Mogadishu and was reversed, and elections were repeatedly delayed.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en} On 15 May 2022 Hassan Sheikh Mohamud won the presidency again, and Mohamed handed over power on 23 May 2022 — each man having succeeded the other once.{cite:source/wikipedia-mohamed-abdullahi-mohamed-en}
