## Overview
Musafir al-Qassab, a leader who rose against authority in Armenia and Azerbaijan, and is the [[event/musafir-revolt-armenia|leader of the uprising]] in this event. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

## The uprising
Al-Dahhak the Kharijite installed him at the gate and made him governor of Armenia and Azerbaijan. He came to Ardabil in hiding, and a group of the Shurat went out with him, and crowds gathered around him until he came down at Yunan. He fortified himself in the Castle of Dogs in al-Sisjan, and Ishaq ibn Muslim fought him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

## His end
When the Abbasid state came, Abu Ja'far al-Mansur, who had been over al-Jazira and Armenia in the caliphate of al-Saffah, sent a commander from the people of Khurasan. He fought him until he defeated him and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}
