## Summary
A Maliki jurist learned in several sciences, of al-Fayyum, known as al-Gharqawi; works include a treatise on the question of khullu in endowments, Kashf al-Niqab on questions in some surahs, al-Qawl al-Tamm on the stages of Adam, and Husn al-Suluk on the manners of kings. The printed death year is 1101 or 1069 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7842}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7843}
