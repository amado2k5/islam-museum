## Summary
Francisco de Almeida — 'Alunzu di Almida' in al-Salabi — commander of the Portuguese fleet in the Indian Ocean {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 195}.

## Diu
His fleet met [[person/husayn-al-kurdi|Husayn al-Kurdi]]'s Mamluk expedition in 914/1508 and victory went to the Muslims; reinforced, the Portuguese returned and destroyed the Mamluk fleet at the famous [[event/battle-of-diu-915|battle of Diu]] in 915/1509 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 195}.
