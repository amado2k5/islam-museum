## Overview
Sultan (the third) ibn Murshid ibn Adi al-Ya'rubi was the tenth, and last, of the Ya'ariba imams of the Ibadis in Oman. The Ibadis are not Sunnis, and al-Zirikli describes him in those terms. The entry gives his death as 1155 AH, 1742 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}.

## His imamate and wars
He was given the pledge of allegiance after the deposition of Sayf ibn Sultan, in 1154 AH. Sayf fought him and Sultan prevailed, and the forts and the land came to him alone. But Sayf came back with an army from Iran, and wars broke out between them in which Sultan was wounded, and he died of the wounds {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}. The entry is brief and says no more.
