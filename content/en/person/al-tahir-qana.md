## Summary

al-Tahir Qana is an Algerian researcher in Islamic economics who wrote a dissertation for the MA in Islamic economics in the Department of Shari'a, Faculty of Social Sciences and Islamic Sciences, University of El Hadj Lakhdar, Batna: [[work/al-dawr-al-tawzii-li-l-milkiyya|al-Dawr al-Tawzi'i li-l-Milkiyya fi al-Iqtisad al-Islami: Dirasa Muqarana]], in the academic year 2006–2007, supervised by Dr Jamal Laamara. {cite:dawr-tawzii-milkiyya-qana-2007 p.1} {cite:dawr-tawzii-milkiyya-qana-2007 p.2}

## The defence

The dissertation was defended on Wednesday 27 February 2008 at the University of Batna, before a committee chaired by Dr Hasan Ramadan Fahla, with the supervisor, Dr al-Tayyib Dawudi and Dr Izz al-Din Kayhal; for the occasion the author wrote a poem naming the committee's members. {cite:dawr-tawzii-milkiyya-qana-2007 p.1} {cite:dawr-tawzii-milkiyya-qana-2007 p.220}
