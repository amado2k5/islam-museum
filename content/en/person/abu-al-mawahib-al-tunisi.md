## Overview
Abu'l-Mawahib Muhammad b. Ahmad, the exacting Tunisian Shadhili shaykh and imam resident in Egypt — the one who sat facing the Maghribis' riwaq at al-Azhar, a man of litanies and states; Burhan al-Din al-Aqsara'i took tasawwuf from him and was called "al-Mawahibi" after him. He died in 901. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 426, 454}
