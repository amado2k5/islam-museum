## Overview
Humayd ibn Ma'yuf al-Hamadani, according to al-Baladhuri, campaigned against Crete in the caliphate of al-Rashid and conquered part of it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He is connected with the event of [[event/conquest-of-iqritish|the conquest of Crete]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
