## Summary
Ismail ibn Musa Menk, known as Mufti Menk, is a Zimbabwean scholar and preacher who serves as Grand Mufti of his country. He combined traditional Islamic training with a vast social-media presence, bringing his sermons to a global audience few contemporary scholars reach. {cite:wikipedia-mufti-menk}

## Life and upbringing
He was born in 1975 in Zimbabwe to a family of Indian origin, studied Islamic sciences in Madinah, Saudi Arabia, then returned home to take up ifta and da’wa. {cite:wikipedia-mufti-menk}

## Career and works
He became mufti of Zimbabwe and worked as an imam and preacher; his reach then widened through recorded lectures and short post series such as Motivational Moments and Ramadan Reflections. His style simplifies moral exhortation and ties it to daily life and human relations. {cite:wikipedia-mufti-menk}

## Method and contribution
His accessible preaching avoids juristic complexity when addressing general audiences, stressing ethics, hope and unity. Admirers say this broadened Sunni religious discourse for English-speaking youth in Africa and beyond, while some critics prefer a more specialised discourse; the museum reports these descriptions attributed to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-mufti-menk}
