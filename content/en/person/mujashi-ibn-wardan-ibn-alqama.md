## Overview
Mujashi' ibn Wardan ibn Alqama, the third of the Kharijites who were appointed to kill Ali, together with Ibn Muljam and Shabib ibn Bajra. Al-Mas'udi says that Qatam bound them with silk while she was in seclusion in the Great Mosque, and they sat at the gate of the sudda. When the blow fell, Mujashi' fled and saved himself. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 702}
