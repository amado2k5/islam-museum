## Overview
Barukh ibn Nariya — Baruch — the scribe of the prophet Jeremiah; al-Mas'udi found in the scroll he cites a genealogy of Ma'add ibn Adnan to Qaydar ibn Isma'il ibn Ibrahim in some thirty ancestors, differing from the Arabic versions; Jeremiah's dealings with Ma'add ibn Adnan make a long account which al-Mas'udi cites so the people's dispute about it may be known.
