Salman Khan (born 27 December 1965) is an Indian actor, producer and television presenter, born at Indore, the son of the screenwriter Salim Khan.{cite:wikipedia-salman-khan-en} He debuted in Biwi Ho To Aisi (1988) and broke through with Maine Pyar Kiya (1989), becoming one of Hindi cinema's most bankable stars across the following three decades; he founded the Being Human charitable foundation and has hosted the reality show Bigg Boss since 2010.{cite:wikipedia-salman-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-salman-khan}

Born to a Muslim father and a Hindu mother and brought up in both faiths, he told a Jodhpur court in 2015, when asked his religion: "I'm Hindu and Muslim both. I'm Bharatiya."{cite:hindustan-times-salman-khan-2015} That dual self-description is preserved in full in this record's faith note.{cite:wikipedia-salman-khan-en}
