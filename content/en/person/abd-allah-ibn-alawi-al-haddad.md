## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Alawi ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Muhajir ibn Isa al-Husayni al-Hadrami, known as al-Haddad or al-Haddadi Ba Alawi, was a man of learning from Tarim in Hadramawt. He was born at al-Subayr, one of its outskirts, died at al-Hawi and was buried in Tarim {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. He was blind, having lost his sight to smallpox as a child, and the Yafi'is, who ruled Tarim, persecuted him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1044 to 1132 AH, that is 1634 to 1720 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «Aqidat al-Tawhid»; «al-Da'wa al-Tamma wa'l-Tadhkira al-Amma», printed; «Tabsirat al-Wali bi-Tariqat al-Sada Bani Alawi»; «al-Masa'il al-Sufiyya»; «al-Durr al-Manzum», a poetry collection he composed, printed; «al-Mu'awana wa'l-Muzahara li'l-Raghibin fi Tariq al-Akhira», in manuscript at the end of collection 1170 K in Rabat; «Ithaf al-Sa'il bi-Ajwibat al-Masa'il», printed; «al-Fusul al-Ilmiyya wa'l-Usul al-Hikamiyya», in manuscript, which he says he owns, with a copy in the Ambrosiana; «al-Nasa'ih al-Diniyya»; «Fatawa»; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. His pupil Ahmad ibn Abd al-Karim al-Shajjar al-Ahsa'i gathered a selection of his words in a book he called «Tathbit al-Fu'ad», printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.
