## Summary
Sa'da bint Kurayz ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd Shams al-Abshamiyya, a soothsayer of the Arabs' soothsayers and a poetess of their poets, maternal aunt of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 197}.

## The prophecy to Uthman
Uthman related that he was in the court of the Kaaba when word came that Muhammad had married Ruqayya, daughter of Utba ibn Abi Lahab, and grief entered him; he went home and found his aunt Sa'da sitting with her family, having foretold for her people. She recited to him her verses: "Rejoice — thrice odd, then thrice, then three more… you are a firstborn and you have met a firstborn; by God you have married a blossoming steed" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 197}. She then told him that Muhammad ibn Abd Allah had come to him with the revelation and the criterion {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 197}.

Uthman then went to Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and told him what his aunt had said; Abu Bakr answered "By God your aunt spoke true — this Muhammad ibn Abd Allah has been sent by God with His message" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 197-198}. Uthman married Ruqayya, and Sa'da composed verses on his Islam and marriage opening "He was guided — and by God He guides to the truth" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 198}. Ibn Hajar records her in al-Isaba and Ibn Abd al-Hadi in al-Iqd al-Tamm {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 198}.
