## Summary
Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf al-Zuhri, a senior Successor in Medina, trustworthy, learned and a prolific transmitter. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2053}

## Works
He held the judgeship of Medina and died in 94 AH; his given name is given as Abd Allah or Isma'il. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2053}
