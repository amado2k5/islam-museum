## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Atiyya al-Sa'di, a commander whom Marwan ibn Muhammad equipped with an army in 130 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.229}

## His campaign
Al-Mas'udi reports that he killed Balakh ibn Uqba at Wadi al-Qura, then caught up with Abu Hamza and killed him and most of those with him, then met Abdallah ibn Yahya al-Kindi at Jarash and killed him and most of his companions, and then camped at Sana'a. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.229}
