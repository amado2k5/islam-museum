## Summary
Muhyi al-Din Abu l-Fada'il 'Abd Allah ibn Rashid al-Din 'Abd al-Zahir ibn Nashwan al-Sa'di al-Misri was a chancery scribe and historian. Biblissima records 1223 to 1292 CE {cite:biblissima Q12222}. He is the author of al-Rawd al-Zahir.
## In the sources
The museum's card links his birth, death and burial to Cairo at medium confidence, pending a second source {cite:biblissima Q12222}.
