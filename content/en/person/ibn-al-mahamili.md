## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn al-Qasim al-Dabbi, Abu al-Hasan, known as Ibn al-Mahamili, was a Shafi'i jurist, born and died at Baghdad in 415 AH (1024 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.211}

His works include Tahrir al-Adilla, al-Majmu', Lubab al-Fiqh in some 776 pages, and al-Muqni' on Shafi'i jurisprudence. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.211}
