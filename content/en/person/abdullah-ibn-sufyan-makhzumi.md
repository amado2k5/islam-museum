## Summary
Of Banu Makhzum, brother of Habbar ibn Sufyan; he emigrated to Abyssinia. Ibn Ishaq says he was killed at the Yarmuk — with a note of doubt. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him the brother of Habbar ibn Sufyan, says he was killed at the Yarmuk in Syria under Umar ibn al-Khattab's caliphate, then adds that it is doubted: was he killed there or not? {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-makhzum|Banu Makhzum]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq said of Abdullah ibn Sufyan: "it is doubted whether he was killed there or not" (p. 7); unsettled, so his end is recorded as uncertain.
