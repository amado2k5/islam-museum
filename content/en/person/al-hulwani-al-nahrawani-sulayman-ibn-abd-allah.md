## Overview
Sulayman ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Talib al-Hulwani al-Nahrawani, a scholar who came to Baghdad as a young man. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.152}

## His learning and death
He read grammar under al-Thamanini and philology under Ibn al-Dahhan, and he heard hadith. He died in 493 AH (1100 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.152}
