## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ma'ruf al-Mattar, known as Mu'ammala, was one of those famous for asceticism, worship and scrupulousness, and was imam of the main mosque. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 680, sequence 681, on page 398. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}

## His teachers and the mosque
He heard from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Qattan and Yazid ibn Harun. The mosque of Mu'ammala ibn Ma'ruf is named after him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}

## A report from Abu al-Darda
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ma'ruf relates from his father, from Yahya ibn Sa'id, from al-Haytham ibn Hakim, from Abu al-Darda, a report that whoever dies bearing witness that there is no god but God enters Paradise. The wording of the report is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}
