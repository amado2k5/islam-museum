## Overview
Afdal al-Din Abu al-Fada'il Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Namawar ibn Abd al-Malik al-Khunaji, a scholar of medicine, wisdom and logic, of Persian origin. He was born in 590 AH (1194 CE) and died in 646 AH (1248 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
