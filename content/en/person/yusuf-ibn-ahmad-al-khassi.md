## Summary
Yusuf ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Bakr, Najm al-Din al-Khassi, from the village of al-Khass in Khwarazm. A Hanafi jurist and a distinguished imam, he studied with Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abd Allah and with al-Sadr al-Shahid Husam al-Din Umar and al-Hasan Qadikhan. His works include al-Fatawa and Mukhtasar al-Fusul. Birth 634 H as printed; death not given. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13353}
