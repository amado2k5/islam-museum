## Overview
Husayn ibn Hubaysh was a man of the Duhmis of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] whom the book calls "a troublemaker"; [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] took him and jailed him in the prison of [[place/saada|Sa'da]], "and some of al-Akilis worked at it until they got him out". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} That is why those [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put out of Najran were jailed at [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl]] rather than at Sa'da. The book does not say here what became of him. See [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]]. The account is Zaydi.
