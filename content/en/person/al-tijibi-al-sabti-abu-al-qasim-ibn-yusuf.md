## Overview
Abu al-Qasim ibn Yusuf al-Tujibi al-Sabti, a scholar of Valencian origin. He was born in Ceuta in 666 AH (1267 CE) and grew up there. He died in 730 AH (1329 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.424}
