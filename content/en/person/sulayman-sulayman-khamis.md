## Overview
Sulayman Sulayman Khamis was an Egyptian Azhari scholar and a professor at the Faculty of Usul al-Din (Fundamentals of Religion), and he is the author of the book "Min al-Falsafah al-Yunaniyyah ila al-Falsafah al-Hadithah", published by the Islamic Research Academy in 1447 AH / 2025 CE {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 11}.

## Life
He was born in Idku in Beheira Governorate in 1913 CE, memorized the Quran in the traditional schools (katatib) of Idku, then joined the Alexandria Institute and obtained the primary and secondary certificates in 1936 CE. He subsequently enrolled in the Faculty of Usul al-Din and earned his degree in 1940 CE, ranking third out of one hundred and forty students {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 11}.

He obtained the higher degree (al-ustadhiyyah) in 1947 CE, with a thesis on the philosophy of al-Jahiz. He was appointed lecturer at the Faculty of Usul al-Din, later became a professor there, and assumed the chairmanship of the Department of Theology and Philosophy in 1971 CE {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 11}.

He died on 13 Jumada al-Ula 1391 AH, corresponding to 7 July 1971 CE, and was buried in Idku {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 12}.

## Works
Among his works, as mentioned in the book, are: "Nahwa 'Aqidah Qur'aniyyah", "Tawdih al-Sam'iyyat", "Nihayat al-Falsafah al-Islamiyyah", "Min al-Falsafah al-Yunaniyyah ila al-Falsafah al-Hadithah", "Tawdih al-'Aqa'id al-Nasafiyyah", "Sharh al-Maqulat al-'Ashr", "Tawdih al-Ilahiyyat fi Sharh al-Sayyid al-Sharif 'ala al-Mawaqif li-l-Imam al-'Adud", and "Tuhfat al-Muhibbin fi Adillat Najat Abaway Khatam al-Mursalin". It also mentions that he has other works in manuscript form held by his heirs {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 12}.

## The Book
He wrote the book to present the course of human thought from ancient Eastern and Greek philosophy, through Islamic philosophy, to modern philosophy in Bacon and Descartes {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 6}. The 1447 AH edition was based on an earlier edition by Dar al-Tiba'ah al-Muhammadiyyah published in Sha'ban 1390 AH, that is, October 1970 CE {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 12}.

## For Young Visitors
He was a teacher who made sure his students understood the lesson before leaving class, asking them about the Hadith and Quranic verses until he was confident they had memorized and understood them {cite:min-al-falsafa-al-yunaniyya-azhar p. 11}.
