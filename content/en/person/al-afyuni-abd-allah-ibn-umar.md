## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Muhammad al-Hanafi al-Tarabulsi al-Afyuni, a man of letters and poet from Tripoli in Syria. He was born and raised there, and worked in Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.226}

## Death
He died in 1154 AH (1741 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.226}
