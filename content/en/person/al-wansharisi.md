## Summary
Maliki jurist of Tlemcen, died 1508, compiler of al-Mi'yar on the fatwas of the Maghreb and al-Andalus. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2483}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
Al-Wansharisi (died 1508), born in Tlemcen in today's Algeria, is presented by The Oxford Dictionary of Islam as the Maliki jurist chiefly remembered for his many-volumed al-Mi'yar al-mu'rib, which gathers the legal opinions of the Maghreb and al-Andalus. Within a century of his death it had entered the North African curriculum; today it is quarried for the social, cultural, economic and legal life of the medieval Maghreb and Andalus. Some fourteen further works of his survive, the entry says, almost all on jurisprudence. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2483}
