## Overview
Al-Hasan b. 'Isa b. al-Muqtadir — an 'Alawi 'Abbasi of the caliphs' sons; born Muharram 303, he heard from his tutor Ahmad b. Mansur al-Yashkuri and Abu'l-Azhar 'Abd al-Wahhab b. 'Abd al-Rahman the katib; virtuous, devout, a memoriser of the caliphs' chronicles, versed in the days of men, righteous and ascetic. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.479}

## His funeral
He renounced the caliphate while able to take it, preferring al-Qahir bi'llah; he died in 440, bequeathing that the qadi Abu'l-Husayn Ibn al-Ghuriq wash him and pray over him, that he be carried to Bab Harb by day and buried without a coffin. The viziers Kamal al-Mulk and Za'im al-Mulk attended his funeral, and al-Basasiri walked behind it from his house to his grave; he was buried near Ahmad b. Hanbal's grave, and the ra'is al-ru'asa Abu'l-Qasim sat for condolences next day. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.479}
