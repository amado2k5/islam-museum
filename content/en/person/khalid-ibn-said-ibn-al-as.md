## Summary
A Companion of Banu Abd Shams. He emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife and two sons, came in the two ships, and was killed at Marj al-Saffar in the caliphate of Abu Bakr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}.

## In the chapter on the emigration to Abyssinia
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Abd Shams, of those whom the Negus carried in the two ships, together with his wife Amina bint Khalaf and his sons Sa'id and Umma, who had been born in the land of Abyssinia {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}. He says that Khalid was killed at Marj al-Saffar in the land of Syria in the caliphate of Abu Bakr {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}. His father Sa'id ibn al-As died at al-Zariba, on the outskirts of Ta'if. His brother Aban ibn Sa'id addressed Khalid and Amr in verses when they became Muslims, and Khalid answered him in verses {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}.

## Tribe
According to Ibn Ishaq, he belongs to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}.

## Source notes and weak reports
- Ibn Hisham, on the name of his wife: "and it is said: Humayna bint Khalaf" (pp. 3–4).

## On the alms of Murad and Madhhij
When the Prophet appointed [[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk]] over Murad, Zubayd and Madhhij, he sent Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As with him to collect the alms. Khalid stayed with him in their lands until the Prophet died {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}.

## In the delegation of Thaqif
He walked between the delegation and the Prophet, and he wrote their document with his own hand. They would not eat any food that came to them from the Prophet until Khalid had eaten from it {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/184}. The document given to Thaqif was written by the Prophet's command: its thorn trees at Wajj and its game are not to be cut {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/187}.
