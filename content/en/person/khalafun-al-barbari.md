## Overview
Khalfun the Berber, a Berber man, said to be a client of Rabi'a. Al-Baladhuri says that he raided Bara in the great land and conquered it at the beginning of the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil, after Jabala, client of al-Aghlab, had failed to take it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He took part in [[event/conquest-of-barah|the conquest of Bara]]. The date of the event and his lineage need an additional source.
