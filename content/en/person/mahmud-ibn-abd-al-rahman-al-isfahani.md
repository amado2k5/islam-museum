## Overview
Mahmud ibn Abd al-Rahman (Abu al-Qasim) ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Thana', Shams al-Din al-Isfahani, was an exegete learned in the rational sciences {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 674 AH, 1276 CE, and his death as 749 AH, 1349 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}.

## Life
He was born and educated in Isfahan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He then travelled to Damascus, where the people honoured him and [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] admired him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He moved to Cairo, where the Amir Qawsun built a khanqah for him in al-Qarafa and appointed him its shaykh; he remained until he died of the plague in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists his Tafsir, in manuscript, in the Sufi collection, a complete and precious copy of 843 folios, and its fourth part, in manuscript, is called Anwar al-Haqa'iq al-Rabbaniyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. Al-Zirikli quotes [[person/al-safadi|al-Safadi]] saying that he saw him writing in his commentary from memory without consulting any book {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He lists Tashyid al-Qawa'id, in manuscript, a commentary on Tajrid al-'Aqa'id by [[person/nasir-al-din-al-tusi|Nasir al-Din al-Tusi]], and a commentary on Fusul al-Nasafi, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He lists Matali' al-Anzar, printed, a commentary on Tawali' al-Anwar by [[person/al-baydawi-abd-allah-ibn-umar|al-Baydawi]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He lists Nazirat al-'Ayn, in manuscript, on logic, with its commentary Nadirat al-'Ayn by Ahmad ibn Umar al-Maliki {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He lists al-Bayan, in manuscript, a commentary on the Mukhtasar of [[person/ibn-al-hajib|Ibn al-Hajib]] on legal theory, and Bayan Ma'ani al-Badi', a commentary on al-Badi' of Ibn al-Sa'ati on legal theory {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}. He lists a commentary on Matali' al-Anwar by al-Armawi on logic, a commentary on the Kafiya of Ibn al-Hajib, in manuscript, and a commentary on the Minhaj of al-Baydawi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 176}.
