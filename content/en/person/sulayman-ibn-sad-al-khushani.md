## Overview
Sulayman ibn Sa'd al-Khushani, an official over Jordan in the time of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. al-Baladhuri reports that Abd al-Malik ordered him in the year 81 AH to transfer the Syrian register from Greek, and he belongs to [[event/arabization-of-syrian-diwan|the Arabization of the Syrian register]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The land tax
He asked Abd al-Malik to put him in charge of the land tax of Jordan for a year, and Abd al-Malik did so and appointed him over Jordan. Before the year was out he had completed the transfer, and brought it to Abd al-Malik. The office of Jordan that year was worth a hundred and eighty thousand dinars {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
