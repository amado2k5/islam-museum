## Summary
Khoirunnisa, known as Nissa Sabyan, is an Indonesian singer born on 23 May 1999 and the vocalist of Sabyan Gambus, the Jakarta gambus group the public knows for singing shalawat and re-singing Islamic songs; its video clips have been watched millions of times and often trend on YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en} {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-ar}

Her own YouTube channel, "NISSA SABYAN", counted 1,180,000 subscribers, 340,284,268 views and 979 videos as read by vidIQ on 13 November 2026 — dated platform figures, not facts the museum verifies itself. {cite:vidiq-nissa-sabyan} {cite:youtube-nissasabyanofficialchannel}

## Early life and education
The sources disagree about her birthplace: the infoboxes of the English and Arabic articles give Lumajang, East Java, while the English article's biography text says she was born in Bandung, West Java, was raised there until the sixth grade of primary school, then moved to Jakarta, and entered a vocational high school after junior high school. {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en} {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-ar}

The article says that at nineteen, in 2018, she hoped to go on to study music at university, but wanted to focus on her group first. {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en}

## Career with Sabyan Gambus
Sabyan Gambus consists of several former Islamic boarding school (pesantren) students based in Jakarta, and was made originally for weddings; it became known for performing Islamic songs and the Prophet's salawat, both as covers and as new works. {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en}

Among the best-known pieces Nissa has sung with the group are "Ya Maulana", "Deen Assalam", "Ya Jamalu", "Law Kana Bainanal Habib", "Ya Habibal Qolbi", "Rahman ya Rahman", "Ya Asyiqol Musthofa", "Ahmad Ya Habibi", "Ya Taiba", "Qomarun" and "Assalamualaika ya Rasulullah". {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en}

The group won the 2018 Anugerah Syiar Ramadan 1439 award in the music band / singer category, and Nissa played herself in the 2019 film *Sabyan: Menjemput Mimpi*. {cite:wikipedia-nissa-sabyan-en}
