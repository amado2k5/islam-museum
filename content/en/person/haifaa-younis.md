## Overview
Haifaa Younis is an American board-certified obstetrician-gynaecologist of Iraqi roots, and the founder and chairman of Jannah Institute, an educational nonprofit based in Irvine, California. {cite:islamicinfocenter-haifaa-younis-bio} She has no Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item, and no source publishes a birth date for her, so this record gives none. {cite:islamicinfocenter-haifaa-younis-bio}

## Career and education
She graduated from the Mecca Institute of Islamic Studies and from Al-Huda Qur'an Memorization School in Jeddah, memorised the Qur'an, and her biography reports that she holds an ijaza in the Hafs 'an 'Asim reading. {cite:islamicinfocenter-haifaa-younis-bio}

She is a resident scholar at the Islamic Center of Irvine (ICOI), where she teaches Qur'an, tafsir and tazkiyah; her programmes include the "Year of Knowledge", and she is profiled in The Muslim 500. {cite:islamicinfocenter-haifaa-younis-bio}

## The institute and its channel
She founded Jannah Institute in 2013 as an educational nonprofit in Irvine offering courses in Qur'an, tafsir and tazkiyah, addressed especially to women. {cite:islamicinfocenter-haifaa-younis-bio} Her lectures and courses appear on the institute's YouTube channel, @jannahinstitute, which had about 974,000 subscribers in October 2026, a dated platform figure that changes constantly; the channel's video descriptions link the institute's website and her official accounts. {cite:youtube-jannahinstitute}
