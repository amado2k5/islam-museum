## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Yunus ibn Ubayd, client of the Abd al-Qays, originally from Kufa. al-Shirazi mentions him among the jurists of Basra {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}.

## Death
He died in the year 139 AH, and it is said in 140 AH. The Common Era date is calculated from the Hijri date {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}.
