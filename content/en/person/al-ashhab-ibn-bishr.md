## Overview
Al-Ashhab ibn Bishr al-Kalbi, a governor whom al-Hajjaj appointed over Sistan when the years of the truce with Ratbil had ended. Al-Baladhuri relates that he pressed Ratbil hard in the tribute he paid, so Ratbil wrote to al-Hajjaj to complain of him, and al-Hajjaj dismissed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
