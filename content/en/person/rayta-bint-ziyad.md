## Overview
Rayta bint Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan. al-Baladhuri reports that she asked permission to build a bath in Basra. She was granted it within a general permission that came after the revenue of the bath of Muslim ibn Abi Bakra had become widely known {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}. An additional source is needed to identify the page of the report.
