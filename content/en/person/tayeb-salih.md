## Summary

Tayeb Salih (1929–2009) was a Sudanese novelist translated into the world's languages, who put the Sudanese village on the map of world fiction with Season of Migration to the North.{cite:wikipedia-tayeb-salih}

## Life

Born in Karmakol in northern Sudan, he studied in Khartoum and London, worked at the BBC Arabic Service, then in Qatar's Ministry of Information and UNESCO, living most of his life outside Sudan.{cite:wikipedia-tayeb-salih}

## Works

Season of Migration to the North (1966) is the novel of Mustafa Sa'eed and the educated colonised subject's return home, its theme the centre-periphery of colonialism; his other books include The Wedding of Zein, Maryud, Dou al-Beit and Bandarshah, set in the fictional village of Wad Hamid.{cite:wikipedia-tayeb-salih}

## Standing

Arab critics often rank him the greatest Arab novelist of the twentieth century; he received awards and wide translation and died in London in 2009.{cite:wikipedia-tayeb-salih}
