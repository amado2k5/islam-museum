## Summary
The bishop of [[place/najran|Najran]] and holder of its religious leadership, one of the three who led its sixty-man delegation to Medina in year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}.

## His account
Abu Haritha ibn Alqama was Najran's bishop, holding the religious leadership and spiritual guidance within its people's sixty-man delegation to the Prophet in year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}. The delegation was led by three: al-Aqib Abd al-Masih for command and government, al-Sayyid al-Ayham for cultural and political affairs, and Abu Haritha for religion {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}. The delegation asked the Prophet what he said of Jesus and held back from Islam; he called them to mutual imprecation, they asked instead for peace, and he made peace with them on jizya and dhimma {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 451}.

## For young visitors
Abu Haritha was the greatest of Najran's Christian scholars; he came in their delegation asking the Prophet and debating him, and they made peace with him.
