## Summary
Salah al-Din Ibn al-Faris al-Misri, the celebrated adib-poet; a witty, excellent littérateur who wore the dress of the Turks' sons, began with Hanafi fiqh then adab mastered him till he stood among the accomplished poets — huge in body yet delicate, adroit, ever-ready in jest and quick of wit; he died in Sha'ban 843, past fifty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248}
