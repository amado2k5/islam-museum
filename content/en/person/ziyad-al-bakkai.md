## Overview
Ziyad ibn Abd Allah al-Bakka'i, a Kufan of the third generation of writers of the sira, who died in the year 183 AH according to Ahmad Amin's table {cite:duha-islam p.639}.

He is a transmitter of the sira of [[person/ibn-ishaq|Ibn Ishaq]], by which he became known; his relation to him is that of a pupil to a shaykh {cite:duha-islam}.
