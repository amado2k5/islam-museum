# Malaz Majanni

## Summary
Malaz Majanni is an Australian Muslim media producer based in Sydney, the founder of OnePath Network, a not-for-profit Islamic media network established in March 2014 to present Islam positively in mainstream media. {cite:wikipedia-onepath-network-en} A 2017 industry profile lists him as the network's founder and chief executive. {cite:salaam-gateway-onepath-network}

## OnePath Network
The network began as a community-funded studio in western Sydney, filming interviews and educational videos for its YouTube channel, and grew into one of the largest English-language dawah media operations, with about two million channel subscribers in 2026. {cite:youtube-onepath-network} Contemporary coverage quoted Majanni as the network's head explaining its purpose: to answer mainstream misrepresentation of Muslims with a clear message of their own. {cite:wikipedia-onepath-network-en} The network's productions span Islamic history, current news, short films and daily inspiration, and it is supported by community donations. {cite:salaam-gateway-onepath-network}
