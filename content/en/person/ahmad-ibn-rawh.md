## Summary
Ahmad ibn Rawh cried to his Lord for relief when trials fell. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 542, sequence 543, on page 166. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}

## Crying to the Master
al-Husayn ibn Abd al-Rahman, the judge, relates from his father that he heard Ahmad ibn Rawh recite verses: when trials fall, I cry to a master by whom affliction is driven off and harm is lifted; I hope in a Lord who does not disappoint His servant, to whom belong the glory, the praise, the creation and the command. He also recited verses of a companion of his: I seek the door of the One I call alone, and I hope to draw near to my beloved; when the eyes of all people sleep, I knock on the door with a sorrowful heart. The name of the transmitter from Uthman ibn Muhammad is unclear in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/166}
