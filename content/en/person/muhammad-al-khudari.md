## Summary
Shaykh Muhammad al-Khudari was the author of [[work/nur-al-yaqin|Nur al-Yaqin fi Sirat Sayyid al-Mursalin]]; the title page of its fourth edition (1344/1926) presents him as an inspector at the Ministry of Education and former teacher of Islamic history at the Egyptian University. {cite:nur-al-yaqin-tijariyya-1926 p.1} He also wrote lectures on the history of the Islamic nations (*Muhadarat Tarikh al-Umam al-Islamiyya*). {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}

## From the preface of Nur al-Yaqin
In the preface of Nur al-Yaqin he calls himself "Muhammad al-Khudari, son of the late Shaykh Afifi al-Bajuri", and says that from his youth he enjoyed reading histories and tales of past peoples, finding them "the best refiner and the most sincere teacher" of the mind. {cite:nur-al-yaqin-tijariyya-1926 p.3} He relates that he wrote the sira after coming to al-Mansura and meeting the judge Mahmud Bek Salim, who wanted an accessible biography for ordinary Muslims. {cite:nur-al-yaqin-tijariyya-1926 p.3} {cite:nur-al-yaqin-tijariyya-1926 p.4}
