## Overview
Mid'am, servant of the Prophet. He was struck by an arrow and killed at the expedition of Wadi al-Qura. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.580}

## Reports
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Waqidi, because of it, increased on the saying of Ibn Ishaq that the Prophet fought in eleven expeditions. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.580}
