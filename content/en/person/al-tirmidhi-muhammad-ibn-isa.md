## Overview
Muhammad ibn Isa ibn Sawra al-Sulami al-Bughi, a hadith scholar from Tirmidh on the river Jayhun. He wrote the "al-Jami' al-Sahih", known as the Sunan of al-Tirmidhi. He was born in 209 AH (824 CE) and died in 279 AH (892 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.450}
