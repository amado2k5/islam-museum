## Overview
Muhammad Taqi ibn Maqsud Ali al-Isfahani al-Majlisi was an Imami jurist, that is, a Twelver Shi'i and not a Sunni, who busied himself with reviving the legacy of the Ahl al-Bayt. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1070 AH, 1660 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His life
He was a pupil of Baha' al-Din al-Amili, author of al-Kashkul. He was born and died at Isfahan. He was the father of the scholar Muhammad Baqir al-Majlisi, whose entry comes earlier in al-A'lam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names: a manuscript anthology of verse and prose, in Arabic and Persian, in the library of al-Sayyid Nasiri in Tehran, with notes in his own hand on some pages; Rawdat al-muttaqin; and Ihya' al-ahadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
