## Summary
Enes Karic is among the most productive Muslim scholars of the Balkans: a professor of Qur'anic studies in Sarajevo, a wartime education minister, a translator of classical Islamic texts into Bosnian, and a prize-winning novelist. {cite:muslim500-enes-karic} {cite:wikipedia-enes-karic}

## Formation
Born in 1958 in Travnik, he studied at the Faculty of Islamic Studies in Sarajevo and earned his doctorate at the University of Belgrade, then returned to Sarajevo to teach — apart from wartime service in government he has spent his career in its faculty. {cite:wikipedia-enes-karic}

## Career
During the siege of Sarajevo he served in the Bosnian government as Minister of Education, Science, Culture and Sports (1994–1996). As an academic he has written extensively on the Qur'an and Qur'anic interpretation, translated works of Islamic scholarship into Bosnian, and published essays and novels that have won Bosnian literary awards; he also served as dean of the Sarajevo Faculty of Islamic Studies. {cite:muslim500-enes-karic} {cite:wikipedia-enes-karic}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as one of Bosnia's leading intellectuals, bridging Qur'anic scholarship and literature. {cite:muslim500-enes-karic}
