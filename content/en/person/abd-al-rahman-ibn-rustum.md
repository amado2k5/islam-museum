## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Rustam, imam of the Ibadiyya and founder of the Rustamid dynasty. He governed [[place/qayrawan|Qayrawan]], then fled from it to Tahart, and founded it in 144 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1613-1614}

## His legacy
Ibn Khaldun says that the city flourished, that the Berber Ibadis are attributed to him, and that his state continued after him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1613-1614}
