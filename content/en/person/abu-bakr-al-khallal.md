## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Harun, Abu Bakr al-Khallal, who collected the dicta related from Imam Ahmad with their chains and travelled the lands to meet the Imam's companions {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 124}. He authored the book al-Jami'; Ibn al-Jawzi reckoned it at about two hundred parts, Ibn Qayyim at about twenty volumes or more {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 124}. The book became a root of Ahmad's school — the companions looked into it and composed the books of fiqh from it {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 124}. He died in 311 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 124}.
