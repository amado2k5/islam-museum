## Overview
Muhammad Ridha ibn Hadi ibn Abbas, of the Kashif al-Ghita family, was a man of learning from Najaf who also wrote verse. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1310 AH, 1893 CE, to 1366 AH, 1947 CE. In this entry al-Zirikli does not state his school {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 122}.

## His books
Among his printed books al-Zirikli names a work on al-Sharif al-Radi, in the form of his biography; al-Ghayb wa al-shahada, a treatise on the difference between the letters dad and za'; and Naqd al-ara' al-mantiqiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 122}.

## His poetry
Al-Zirikli notes that al-Khaqani's Shu'ara' al-Ghari contains samples of his verse {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 122}. The entry is brief and says no more.
