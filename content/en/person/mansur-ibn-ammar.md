## Overview
Mansur ibn Ammar, known by the kunya Abu al-Sari. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an ascetic author. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.261}

## His sessions
Ibn al-Nadim counts several sessions of his that he did not name as books, among them a session on the remembrance of death, a session on good opinion of God, a session on being presented before God, and the session of al-Taqfuriyya on raiding. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.261}

The data hold no other information about him, and his biography needs an additional source.
