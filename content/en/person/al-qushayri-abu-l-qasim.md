## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Abd al-Karim ibn Hawazin al-Qushayri, author of "al-Risala al-Qushayriyya". A scholar and theologian from Khurasan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}

## Leaving Khurasan
Ibn al-Imad relates that Amid al-Mulk al-Kunduri added al-Qushayri and men like him to the Rafidis in the curse from the pulpits of Khurasan, so he left Khurasan with its imams. Then Nizam al-Mulk honoured him when his state came. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}
