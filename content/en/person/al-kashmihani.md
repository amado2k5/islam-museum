## Overview
Abu'l-Fath 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Kashmihani of Marw al-Rudh, the khatib, shaykh of his town's Sufis and the last to transmit Sahih al-Bukhari from Muhammad b. Abi 'Imran; he lived eighty-eight years, his notice falling under year 548 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 630}.
