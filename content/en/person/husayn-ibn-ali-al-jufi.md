## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn al-Walid al-Ju'fi, a client of the Ju'fi tribe, was a Kufan Qur'an reciter and ascetic who transmitted hadith. Al-Safadi lists him in al-Wafi bi-l-Wafayat and gives his death as 203 AH, about 818 CE by derived conversion; he gives no year of birth {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 p. 14}.

## Life
Al-Safadi calls him the Kufan reciter and ascetic. He reports that Ibn Ma'in said he was reliable, and that Ibn Hanbal said he had seen no one better {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 p. 14}.

Al-Safadi also relays a dream told by Humayd ibn al-Rabi'. In it al-Husayn al-Ju'fi saw the Resurrection and heard a caller ask the scholars to rise and enter Paradise; he stood with them, but was told to sit down because he did not transmit hadith. After that he transmitted constantly, though he had not done so before, until people had written down more than ten thousand hadith from him. Al-Safadi adds that al-Jama'a transmitted from him; the local text reads al-Jami'a, probably a misreading {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 p. 14}.

## Death
Al-Safadi gives his death in 203 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 13 p. 14}.
