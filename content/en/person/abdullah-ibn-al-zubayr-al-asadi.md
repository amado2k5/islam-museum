## Overview
A man named Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr al-Asadi, whom the sources mention in a single report: he went out in panic when [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] killed Umayr ibn Dabi' of the force of al-Muhallab. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 122}

A man of his people called Ibrahim met him and told him of the killing of Umayr, and recited a verse to the effect that al-Hajjaj would never sheathe his sword as long as time lasted. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 122}

This is not [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]], the son of al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam; the link between the two names may need an additional source.
