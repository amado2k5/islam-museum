## Overview
Abu Amr ibn al-Ala' was a philologist, and in the Fihrist's index he is the first of the seven readers. Ahmad ibn Zayd al-Halwani, Isma ibn Abi Isma and al-Yazidi transmitted his reading from him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 71-72}

Al-Mas'udi says that he died in 154 AH in the days of al-Mansur. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 293}

Al-Baladhuri quotes from him a reason for naming the land "al-Sawad": when the Arabs saw the abundance of villages, palms and trees, they said "we have not seen a greater sawad", and "al-sawad" means the mass of a person. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
