## Overview
Abu Amr ibn al-Ala' was a philologist, and in the Fihrist's index he is the first of the seven readers. Ahmad ibn Zayd al-Halwani, Isma ibn Abi Isma and al-Yazidi transmitted his reading from him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 71-72}

Al-Mas'udi says that he died in 154 AH in the days of al-Mansur. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 293}

Al-Baladhuri quotes from him a reason for naming the land "al-Sawad": when the Arabs saw the abundance of villages, palms and trees, they said "we have not seen a greater sawad", and "al-sawad" means the mass of a person. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Chain and reading in Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr

Ibn al-Jazari says Abu 'Amr read on a great many teachers of the Hijaz, Basra and Kufa, among them Abu Ja'far, Yazid ibn Ruman, Shayba ibn Nisah, [[person/abdallah-ibn-kathir|'Abd Allah ibn Kathir]], Mujahid ibn Jabr, [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], Abu al-'Aliya, Humayd ibn Qays al-A'raj, 'Abd Allah ibn Abi Ishaq al-Hadrami, 'Ata' ibn Abi Rabah, 'Ikrima the client of Ibn 'Abbas, [[person/ibn-muhaysin|Ibn Muhaysin]], [[person/asim-ibn-abi-al-najud|'Asim ibn Abi al-Najud]], [[person/nasr-ibn-asim|Nasr ibn 'Asim]] and [[person/yahya-ibn-yamur|Yahya ibn Ya'mar]]. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 143} The chain of Nasr and Yahya ibn Ya'mar passes through Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali from 'Uthman and 'Ali, and that of al-Hasan through Hattan ibn 'Abd Allah al-Raqashi from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari and through Abu al-'Aliya from 'Umar, Ubayy, Zayd and Ibn 'Abbas. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 143} He died, on most accounts, in 154 AH, others saying 155 or 157, and was born in 68 or 70; Ibn al-Jazari calls him the most knowledgeable of men in the Qur'an and in Arabic, with truthfulness, reliability, honesty and religion. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 143} His transmitter is al-Yazidi, and through al-Yazidi [[person/abu-umar-al-duri|al-Duri]] and [[person/abu-shuayb-al-susi|al-Susi]]; Ibn al-Jazari counts a hundred and fifty-four lines of transmission. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 144}
