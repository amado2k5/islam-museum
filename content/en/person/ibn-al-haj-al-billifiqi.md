## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Hajj al-Sulami al-Ballifiqi, Abu al-Barakat, of the progeny of 'Abbas ibn Mirdas al-Sulami, was a judge and historian, one of the notables of al-Andalus in hadith and letters. Of Ballafiq in the district of Almeria, he studied there and at Bougie and Marrakesh and settled at Ceuta, then held the judgeship of Malaga in 735 AH, the judgeship and khitaba of Almeria, then Granada, then Almeria again, and was employed as envoy between kings. He died in 771 AH (1370 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.38}

His books include Asma' al-Kutub wa-l-Ta'rif bi-Mu'allifiha arranged alphabetically; al-Afsah on those famed for piety in al-Andalus; Mushtabihat Mustalahat al-'Ulum; al-Mu'taman, biographies of his contemporaries; al-'Adhb al-Ajaj, his diwan; Tarikh al-Mariyya, left unfinished; al-'Ulan fi Anba' Abna' al-Zaman; Salwat al-Khatir; and Shi'r Man La Shi'r Lahu on poets unknown for verse, among others. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.38}
