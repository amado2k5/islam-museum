## Overview
A Successor from the Ansar named Abd Allah ibn Yazid. It is said that he was the fourth of the Successors who entered al-Andalus, and he transmitted from al-Munayzir al-Ifriqi. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 314}

The word "it is said" comes from the source, so this is a report not a certainty.
