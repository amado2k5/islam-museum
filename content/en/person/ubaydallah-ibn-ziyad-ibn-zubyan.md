## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad ibn Zubyan, of the Banu Aisha ibn Malik ibn Taym Allah ibn Tha'laba ibn Ukaba. He avenged his brother al-Nabi, and stabbed Mutarrif ibn Sidan and killed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He then joined Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and fought with him, and Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr was killed by him, and he cut off his head {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
