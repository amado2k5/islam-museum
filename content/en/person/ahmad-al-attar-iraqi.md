## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Hasani al-Baghdadi al-Attar, an Imami jurist; the Imamiyya are a Shi'i group, and this is a label al-Zirikli reports. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1127 AH, 1715 CE, and his death as 1215 AH, 1800 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 244}.

## His city
Al-Zirikli says he was of the people of Baghdad and moved to al-Najaf, and then gives another possibility, that he was Baghdadi by origin but Najafi by birth and death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 244}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists al-Tahqiq, a manuscript in the library of the Al Haydari in al-Kazimiyya running to 12 volumes; a rijal poem (Urjuza fi l-Rijal), in manuscript in his own hand; Riyad al-Jinan fi A'mal Shahr Ramadan, printed; and a verse diwan in praise of the Imams {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 244}. He also lists al-Ra'iq, a manuscript in the library of Imam al-Sadiq in al-Kazimiyya, an anthology of Arab poetry {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 245}.
