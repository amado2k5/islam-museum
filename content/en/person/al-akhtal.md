## Overview
Ghiyath ibn Ghawth al-Taghlibi, a Christian, the poet of the Umayyad state. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 pp.37-38}

## Under the Umayyads
Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan gave him generous gifts and preferred him over other poets. For about a quarter of a century he praised the Umayyad caliphs and promoted them. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 pp.37-38}

## His standing
Al-Salabi counts him among the great poets of his age, together with al-Farazdaq and Jarir. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 pp.37-38}
