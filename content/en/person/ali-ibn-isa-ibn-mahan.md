## Overview
Ali ibn Isa ibn Mahan, a commander of al-Amin. Al-Suyuti says al-Amin appointed him over the mountain lands in 195 AH, and he set out with forty thousand men to fight al-Ma'mun. Tahir ibn al-Husayn defeated him with fewer than four thousand men, and he was killed and his head carried to al-Ma'mun. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.475} He took part in [[event/battle-of-rayy-195|the battle of al-Rayy in 195 AH]] as one of the defeated.

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that he said of Tahir that he was a thorn from its branches and a spark from its fire. When his troops came near al-Rayy he turned aside to a district, where Tahir defeated him, and a man known as Tahir ibn al-Raji slaughtered him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.375-376}
