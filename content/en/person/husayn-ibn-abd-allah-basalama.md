## Overview
Husayn ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Salam Basalama, a scholar from Mecca. He was born there in 1299 AH (1881 CE), memorised the Qur'an in one of its kuttabs (Qur'anic schools), and died in 1364 AH (1944 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.94}
