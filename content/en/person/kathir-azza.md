## Overview
Kuthayyir Azza, a poet who died in 105 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 377}

## The day of his death
Al-Mas'udi says that he died on the day that [[person/ikrima-mawla-ibn-abbas|Ikrima]], the client of Ibn Abbas, died, and people said: "The greatest of the jurists and the chief of the poets have died." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 377}
