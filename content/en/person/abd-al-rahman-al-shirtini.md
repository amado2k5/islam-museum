## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Sharbini, an Egyptian Shafi'i jurist who was Shaykh of al-Azhar from 1322 to 1324 AH and died in Cairo. His works include a gloss on the commentary on Bahjat al-tullab in Shafi'i law, a report on the commentary on Jam' al-jawami' in usul, and a report on the commentary on Talkhis al-miftah in rhetoric. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1628}
