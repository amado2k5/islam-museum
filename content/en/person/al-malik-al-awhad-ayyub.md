## Overview
Al-Malik al-Awhad Ayyub b. al-Malik al-'Adil Abi Bakr b. Ayyub; he took the city of Akhlat in 604 after a war with its lord Balyan, whom he later killed, and ruled it some five years — a tyrant, shedder of the amirs' blood; he died in Rabi' I 609. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 20, 45}
