## Overview
Shihab al-Din Abu'l-Khayr Ni'mat Allah, son of Shaykh Sharaf al-Din Muhammad al-Jarihi; he and his brother returned bound for Mecca — and Ni'mat Allah drowned in the river of al-Hasa at Wajb in 840 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}.
