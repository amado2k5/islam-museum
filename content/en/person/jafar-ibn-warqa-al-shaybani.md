## Overview
Ja'far ibn Warqa al-Shaybani, who was in charge of the affairs of Kufa and the road to Mecca. Ibn al-Athir says that he marched ahead of the pilgrims with a thousand men of the Banu Shayban for fear of Abu Tahir al-Qarmati. He met him and fought him, and when a group of Qarmatis came up on his right, he was defeated. Abu Tahir entered Kufa in 312 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 115-116}
