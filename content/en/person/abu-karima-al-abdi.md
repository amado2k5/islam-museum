## Summary
Abu Karima al-Abdi guarded his hours closely and grieved over their loss. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 498, sequence 499, on pages 141 and 142. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/141}

## What he said to the son of Adam
Isa ibn al-Hudhayl relates from Abu Karima that he was one of the worshippers of Syria, and that he used to say: O son of Adam, what remains of your life has no price. The name of the transmitter who reports from Isa ibn al-Hudhayl is unclear in the scan; it is given here as the context reads it. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/141} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}
