## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Furan, Abu al-Qasim al-Furani: a jurist counted among the scholars of legal theory and positive law, the foremost Shafi'i at Merv, where he was born and died between 388 and 461 AH (998–1069 CE). He wrote on usul, disputed questions (khilaf), dialectic, and sects. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.326}

His works include al-Ibana an Ahkam Furu' al-Diyana — its first volume held at Dar al-Kutb in Shafi'i fiqh — and Tatimmat al-Ibana in ten parts. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.326}
