Ahmad Shuqairi (1 January 1908 – 26 February 1980) was a Palestinian politician and diplomat, born at Tebnine in south Lebanon to As'ad al-Shuqairi, the Ottoman scholar and parliamentarian, and raised at Tulkarm.{cite:wikipedia-ahmad-shuqairi-en}{cite:wikipedia-ahmad-shuqairi-ar} Trained as a lawyer in Jerusalem, he joined the Istiqlal Party, represented Palestine in Washington from 1945, and then served at the United Nations with the Syrian and Saudi delegations and as assistant secretary-general of the Arab League.{cite:wikipedia-ahmad-shuqairi-en}

In 1964 the Arab League charged him with preparing a Palestinian entity, and the first Palestinian National Council at Jerusalem elected him the first chairman of the Palestine Liberation Organization.{cite:wikipedia-ahmad-shuqairi-en}{cite:wikidata-ahmad-shuqairi} He resigned in December 1967, after the Six-Day War, and spent his last years writing on the Palestinian cause; he died at Amman in February 1980, the day itself given as the 25th by one Arabic account and the 26th by the English biography.{cite:wikipedia-ahmad-shuqairi-ar}{cite:wikidata-ahmad-shuqairi}
