## Summary
Shihab al-Din Ibn al-Hayyim, styled al-Qa'im; the poet of his age, born 799, who studied and grasped something of the sciences, excelled in poetry and its arts, and stood alone in his last years — a large diwan all of surpassing beauty; he died in Jumada II 787. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 346}
