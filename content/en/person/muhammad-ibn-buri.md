## Summary
Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Buri, emir of Damascus, took the emirate after his brother Mahmud was killed in 533; his rule lasted ten months. He died in Sha'ban 534, and Abaq succeeded him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 583, 586}
