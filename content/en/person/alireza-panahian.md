## Summary
Alireza Panahian is an Iranian cleric and lecturer from Tehran, known for his lectures and books on religious education and ethics. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} English Wikipedia describes him as an Iranian Twelver Shia scholar and Hojatoleslam, and Persian Wikipedia describes him as an Iranian Shia cleric and religious speaker. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} His voice reaches its largest audience through his official Instagram account, which counted 1,352,604 followers in November 2026 per an on-platform profile read — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:instagram-alireza-panahian}

## Beginnings and education
Alireza Panahian was born in Tehran on 28 Farvardin 1344 SH, corresponding to 17 April 1965; his father was one of Tehran's clerics and preachers. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} He studied at the Qom seminary up to the kharij level, and holds university studies in psychology alongside his seminary education. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} His seminary teachers included Ali Khamenei, Hossein Wahid Khorasani and Javadi Amoli. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa}

## Career
Panahian has held several cultural offices, among them membership of the board of trustees of the University of Arts and the headship of the Supreme Leader's think tank for universities. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} In February 2011 he was among some seventy high-ranking officials who established the Ammar Headquarters, of which he was a founding member and deputy head. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} In 1392 SH (2013) he founded the Dar al-Hikmah seminary in Tehran, which he still runs. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} He is one of the speakers at official ceremonies held in the Supreme Leader's presence. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa}

## Works and lectures
He lectures extensively on the apocalypse and Mahdism, on family and raising children, and on publishing Shiism. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} He holds that religiosity must be accompanied by inner passion and happiness. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} His books include Shahr-e Khoda, Entezar (Expectation: Popular, Scholarly, Mystical), Chegune yek Namaz-e Khub Bekhanim (How to Pray a Good Prayer), Rahayi az Gonah (Freedom from Sin) and Takabbor-e Penhan (Hidden Pride). {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} In 2010 he addressed the Islamic Unity Seminar in Lahore, Pakistan, on the Prophet Muhammad's character and Islamic unity. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en}

## Views and criticism
His political positions and statements have repeatedly caused controversy in Iranian news circles. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa} In July 2010 he issued a statement calling for the purge of "liberal-minded and clean-shaven" individuals from government offices, as English Wikipedia reports. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-en} In September 2020 Iranian press published documents about a construction partnership and property of his; he replied that he owned only one unit of the house, received as a gift from friends, and the published debate over those documents and his replies ran in the Iranian press. {cite:wikipedia-alireza-panahian-fa}
