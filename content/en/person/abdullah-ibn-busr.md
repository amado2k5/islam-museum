## Overview
A Companion from whom the hadith is transmitted: "Let your tongue never cease to be moist with the remembrance of God, Mighty and Majestic." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 927}

It is the fiftieth hadith of the additions of Ibn Rajab to the Forty, and the last of them. Ahmad, al-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah and Ibn Hibban transmitted it through Amr ibn Qays al-Kindi from him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 927}

Al-Tirmidhi said: "Good, strange." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 927}
