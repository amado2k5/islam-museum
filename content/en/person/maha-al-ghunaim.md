## Summary
Maha Al-Ghunaim is a Kuwaiti financier, co-founder in 1998 of Global Investment House — for a time one of the region's flagship investment banks — and one of the first women anywhere to found and chair a publicly listed investment company in the Arab world. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-ghunaim}

## Global Investment House
A graduate of San Francisco State University in mathematics, Al-Ghunaim began in Kuwaiti banking and, with partners, founded Global Investment House in 1998 as an asset-management and investment-banking house. Under her chairmanship it grew into a multi-billion-dollar institution investing across real estate, private equity and funds, listed on the Kuwaiti exchange and then, in 2008, became the first Kuwaiti company to list its shares — as GDRs — on the London Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-ghunaim}

## Crisis and after
The 2008 financial crisis struck Gulf investment companies hard; Global was forced into debt restructuring in 2009, and Al-Ghunaim later stepped back from its management. Profiles treat both parts of the arc: the build-up that put a Kuwaiti woman at the head of a London-listed investment house, and the crash that tested a whole model of Gulf investment banking. {cite:muslim500-maha-al-ghunaim}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as a pivotal figure of Arab finance — the woman who built one of the first great post-oil investment houses of the Gulf and carried it to London. {cite:muslim500-maha-al-ghunaim}
