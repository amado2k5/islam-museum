## Overview
Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, whose name was al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Tamimi. Al-Baladhuri mentions him in "Futuh Fars", and al-Mas'udi in the account of his death. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}

## In the conquests
In al-Baladhuri, Abu Musa sent him to Qashan, which he took by force. Abd Allah ibn Budayl sent him to the Jews, and their people made peace with him on terms like the peace of Jay. In one report he was at the head of Abu Musa's vanguard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi relates that he lived apart from his people of Banu Tamim in the valley of al-Sibaʿ. When al-Zubayr passed by him on his way back, he said something about him, and a group of his people caught up with him and killed him. Al-Mas'udi adds that it was also said he was killed on the sending of someone among his own people. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.657}
