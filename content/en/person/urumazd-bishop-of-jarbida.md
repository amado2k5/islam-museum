## Overview
Urumazd, bishop of the city of Jarbida, one of the cities of the Franks {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358-359}.

## The book he gave
In the year 328 AH he gave to al-Hakam ibn Abd al-Rahman, heir apparent of his father, the ruler of al-Andalus, a book containing the names of the kings of the Franks from Qaludiyya to Ludharic ibn Qarla {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358-359}.

al-Mas'udi reports that he found the book in Fustat in Egypt in the year 336 AH {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358-359}.
