## Overview
Amr ibn Utba ibn Nawfal ibn Uhayb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra, whose mother was Atika daughter of Abi Waqqas. Al-Baladhuri relates from Hisham ibn al-Kalbi that he was over the people on the day of Jalula on behalf of Sa'd. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
