## Summary
The commander who carried out Salih Reis's plan on Oran after his death {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.

## The sailing
He sailed on Oran in 963/1556; en route came the sultanic order naming [[person/hasan-corso|Hasan Corso]] beylerbey, and the city was besieged but not taken {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.
