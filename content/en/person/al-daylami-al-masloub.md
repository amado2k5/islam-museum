## Summary
al-Daylami, the captive crucified, is a laqab for a person whose name the report does not give. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 519, sequence 520, on page 153. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}

## Captured, crucified, brought down
al-Walid ibn Muslim relates that the Muslims made a raid, and among them was al-Daylami. The Romans captured him and crucified him on a mast. When the Muslims saw this they charged the Romans, took the ship that carried him, and brought him down from the mast. He said: give me water to pour over me. They asked: do you need it? He said: I am in a state requiring ritual purification. When they crucified me, a soul appeared to me, and I saw myself beside a river. The detail of that vision is not rendered here; it is what made him need washing. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/153}
