## Overview
Rustam ibn Farrukhzad, a Persian commander, who was with Firuzan over the people of Fars {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.477}.

al-Tabari reports that as the battle drew near, the Persians asked the two of them to meet, and said that if they continued to disagree, the Persians would perish {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.477}.
