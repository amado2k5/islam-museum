## Summary
Abd al-Salam ibn Muhammad ibn Sa'd al-Shuwair is a Saudi Sunni scholar, associate professor in the Department of Sharia and Legal Sciences at King Fahd Security College and a teacher in the Two Holy Mosques, with many printed books, articles and published papers. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar} {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv}

He studied under Sheikh Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz; his father, Muhammad ibn Sa'd al-Shuwair, was the compiler of Ibn Baz's fatwas and the manager of his office. {cite:shamela-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair}

No source consulted publishes a birth date for him, so no birth date is recorded here. {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv} {cite:shamela-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair}

## Career
He took his bachelor's degree from the College of Sharia in Riyadh in 1415/1416 AH, his master's in comparative fiqh from the Higher Judicial Institute of Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University in 1418 AH, and his doctorate in comparative fiqh from the same institute in 1421 AH with distinction and first-class honours; his doctoral thesis was an edition of Al-Bayan fi'l-Fiqh al-Shafi'i by al-Umrani. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar} {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv}

He was appointed assistant professor in the Department of Sharia and Legal Sciences at King Fahd Security College from 1421 to 1426 AH, served as the department's vice-dean and head between 1422 and 1425 AH and as vice-dean for civil studies between 1425 and 1427 AH, and became associate professor in 1426 AH; he is a faculty member at the college and at the Higher Institute for Security Studies. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar} {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv}

He teaches and supervises and examines master's and doctoral theses at several institutions, among them the Higher Judicial Institute, the Islamic University of Madinah and the College of Sharia in Riyadh, and his official website describes him as a teacher in the Two Holy Mosques. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar} {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv}

His lessons and lectures are published on his YouTube channel, linked with his other service platforms such as Telegram, Twitter and the Turath al-Shuwayer website. {cite:youtube-alshuwayer9}

## Works
His books include Aqd al-Kafala wa-Tatbiqatuh al-Haditha, Al-Bitaqat al-I'timaniyya al-Qardiyya, Ahkam al-Dima' fi'l-Hajj, Fiqh Sawm al-Marid, Fiqh Hajj al-Marid, Sharh Taharat al-Marid wa-Salatih, Al-Sulh fi'l-Uqud, Mudhakkira fi'l-Bunuk wa'l-Mu'amalat al-Masrifiyya al-Mu'asira and Shakhsiyyat al-Sharikat al-Haditha fi'l-Fiqh al-Islami, a comparative study. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar}

His editions and commentaries include Al-Tatabbu' li-Awham Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi on his transmission of Malik's Muwatta, a commentary on Dalil al-Talib, Fath al-Wahhab on Al-Hajjawi's Manzumat al-Adab, Al-Faradiyya fi'l-Fara'id ala Madhhab al-Hanabila by Shams al-Din al-Faradi, Al-I'tidad bi-Khilaf al-Zahiriyya and Al-Tawqit al-Hawli fi'l-Zakah; his official website hosts a library of his books and lessons, among them commentaries on the Forty Hadith, Bulugh al-Maram and Zad al-Mustaqni. {cite:wikipedia-abd-al-salam-al-shuwair-ar} {cite:shuwaier-abd-al-salam-cv}
