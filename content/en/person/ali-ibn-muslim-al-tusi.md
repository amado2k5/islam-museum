## Summary
Ali ibn Muslim ibn Sa'id, Abu al-Hasan al-Tusi, settled in Baghdad and related hadith there from Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn Aslam, Yusuf ibn al-Majishun, Hushaym, Abdallah ibn al-Mubarak, Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Jarir ibn Abd al-Hamid, Abbad ibn al-Awwam, Ismail ibn Ulayya, Muhammad ibn Bakr al-Barsani, Abdallah ibn Ghurar, Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi, Hibban ibn Hilal, Abd al-Samad ibn Abd al-Warith, Wahb ibn Jarir and Rawh ibn Ubada. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.108}

Muhammad ibn Ishaq al-Saghani, Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Bukhari (in his Sahih), Abu Bakr al-Athram, Mu'adh ibn al-Muthanna, Abdallah ibn Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu al-Abbas al-Baghawi, Ibrahim ibn Hammad al-Qadi, Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Nuri, Yahya ibn Ṣā'id, the judge al-Muhamili, Ibn Ayyash al-Qattan and others transmitted from him. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.108}
