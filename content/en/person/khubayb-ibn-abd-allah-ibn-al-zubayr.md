## Overview
Khubayb ibn Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr, a descendant of Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr. He narrated a weak hadith condemning the Umayyads, as al-Salabi mentions. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.174-175}

## His ordeal
The book says that al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik ordered his governor Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz to imprison him and flog him a hundred lashes. Umar flogged him and poured cold water over him, and he died. Umar regretted this, and released him from prison when his pain grew severe. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.174-175}
