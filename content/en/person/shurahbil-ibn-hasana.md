## Overview
Shurahbil ibn Hasana — Shurahbil ibn 'Abd Allah ibn al-Muta' ibn 'Abd Allah of [[group/kinda|Kinda]], an ally of [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] — was known as Abu 'Abd Allah and named after his mother Hasana. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330} Ibn Ishaq names him among the emigrants to Abyssinia as the half-brother through their mother of [[person/junada-ibn-sufyan|Junada]] and [[person/jabir-ibn-sufyan|Jabir]], sons of Sufyan, and traces him to his mother [[person/hasana-umm-shurahbil|Hasana]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Lineage and mother
*Al-Isti'ab* gives two accounts of his link to Hasana. Ibn 'Abd al-Barr calls her his mother, but quotes al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar that Hasana, wife of Sufyan ibn Ma'mar ibn Habib al-Jumahi, adopted him and was not his mother, so that he was named after her; and that she was a client of Ma'mar ibn Habib from 'Adawla, near Bahrain, after which the "'Adawli" ships are named. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330}

He also quotes Ibn Ishaq: his mother Hasana was an 'Adawli woman whose clientage belonged to Ma'mar ibn Habib of Jumah. Sufyan, a man of the Ansar from Banu Zurayq, married her; he was called Sufyan ibn Ma'mar because Ma'mar ibn Habib al-Jumahi made him his ally, adopted him and married him to Hasana, who already had Shurahbil by another man, and she bore Sufyan Jabir and Junada. When they came back from Abyssinia they settled with their people of [[group/banu-zurayq|Banu Zurayq]], and Shurahbil with his two half-brothers; then Sufyan and his two sons died in 'Umar's caliphate without descendants, and Shurahbil moved to Banu Zuhra and became their ally. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330}

## Emigration and standing
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says that Shurahbil ibn Hasana was one of the emigrants to Abyssinia and was counted among the leading men of Quraysh. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330} Ibn Ishaq's *Sira* names him with the household of Sufyan ibn Ma'mar among the emigrants to [[place/aksum|Abyssinia]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Command in Syria and death
He governed one of the four districts of [[place/al-sham|Syria]] for [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|'Umar ibn al-Khattab]], and died in [[event/plague-of-amwas|the plague of 'Amwas]] in the year 18 at the age of sixty-seven. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330}

## Other accounts of his lineage
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr also quotes Ibn Hisham: he is Shurahbil ibn 'Abd Allah of Banu al-Ghawth ibn Murr, brother of Taym ibn Murr; and Musa ibn 'Uqba from Ibn Shihab: he is Shurahbil ibn 'Abd Allah of Banu Jumah, and his mother is Hasana. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 330}
