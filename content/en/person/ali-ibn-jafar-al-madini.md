## Overview
Ali ibn Ja'far al-Madini, one of the memorisers of hadith. Al-Mas'udi says that he died in Samarra on Monday, three nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 234 AH, at the age of seventy-two years and some months. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.512}

## His books and teachers
Ibn al-Nadim calls him Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn Ja'far al-Madini, and lists among his books "al-Musnad with its defects", "al-Mudallisin", "al-Du'afa'", "al-'Ilal" and "al-Asma' wa al-Kuna". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.322} The "Classes of the Jurists" says that he wrote from [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

The two sources differ on the month of his death: Dhu al-Hijja according to al-Mas'udi, and Dhu al-Qa'da according to Ibn al-Nadim.
