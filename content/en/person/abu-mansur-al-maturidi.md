## Summary
Abu Mansur Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Maturidi al-Samarqandi, imam of the mutakallimun and eponym of the Maturidi school of doctrine; his nisba refers to Maturid, a quarter of Samarqand. He died in 333 AH {cite:al-aalam}.

## In al-A'lam
Al-Zirikli calls al-Maturidi the imam of the scholars of kalam, named after Maturid, a quarter of Samarqand, lists among his works the *Kitab al-Tawhid* and *Awham al-Mu'tazila*, and dates his death to 333 AH {cite:al-aalam}. He also has a notice in Ibn Khallikan's *Wafayat al-A'yan* {cite:wafayat-ayan}.

## For young visitors
Al-Maturidi was a theologian of Samarqand whose school of doctrine became one of the widest Sunni creeds in Transoxiana and the eastern Islamic lands.
