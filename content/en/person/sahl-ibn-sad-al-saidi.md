## Overview
A Companion of the Banu Sa'ida {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.

## His hadith
A hadith is narrated from him: "Renounce the world and God will love you; renounce what is in people's hands and people will love you." It is the thirty-first hadith of al-Nawawi's Forty Hadiths. Ibn Majah narrates it (4102) through Abu Hazim from him {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.

Another hadith from him, in al-Tirmidhi (2320), concerns the worth of this world in the sight of God {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.
