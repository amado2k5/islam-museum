## Overview
A Companion of the Banu Sa'ida {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.

## His entry in al-Isaba
He is Sahl ibn Sa'd ibn Malik ibn Khalid ibn Tha'laba ibn Haritha ibn Amr ibn al-Khazraj ibn Sa'ida, al-Ansari al-Sa'idi, among the best-known Companions; Ibn Hibban reports that his name had been Hazn and the Prophet changed it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.624-625}. He narrated from the Prophet and from Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Asim ibn Adi and Amr ibn Abasa; his son al-Abbas, Abu Hazim, al-Zuhri and others narrated from him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.625}.

## His age and death
Al-Zuhri said the Prophet died when Sahl was fifteen years old, and he was the last of the Companions to die in Medina; he died in 91 AH, some say earlier, and al-Waqidi said he lived a hundred years {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.625}.

## His hadith
A hadith is narrated from him: "Renounce the world and God will love you; renounce what is in people's hands and people will love you." It is the thirty-first hadith of al-Nawawi's Forty Hadiths. Ibn Majah narrates it (4102) through Abu Hazim from him {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.

Another hadith from him, in al-Tirmidhi (2320), concerns the worth of this world in the sight of God {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.

## For younger visitors
Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi was a Companion from Medina who lived so long that he was the last Companion to die there; the scholars narrated a group of hadiths from him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.625}.
