## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Isma'il ibn Muhammad al-Talhi al-Taymi al-Qurashi al-Asfahani, known as Qawwam al-Sunna. He was from Isfahan and traced his descent to Talha ibn Ubayd Allah. He was a leading authority in tafsir, hadith, jurisprudence, the readings of the Qur'an, language and literature. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

According to "Mawsu'at a'lam al-ulama wa al-udaba", he was born in 457 AH and died in 535 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
