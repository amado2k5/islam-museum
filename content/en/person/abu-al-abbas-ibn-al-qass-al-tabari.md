## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Abi Ahmad, known as Ibn al-Qass al-Tabari, a jurist of the companions of [[person/ahmad-ibn-umar-ibn-surayj|Abu al-Abbas ibn Surayj]]. He died in Tarsus in 335 AH, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}

Al-Shirazi counts him among the imams of his school, and mentions among his works "al-Miftah", "Adab al-Qadi", "al-Mawaqit" and "al-Talkhis". Jurisprudence was taken from him by the people of Tabaristan. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58} The date of death here comes from a single source and needs an additional source.
