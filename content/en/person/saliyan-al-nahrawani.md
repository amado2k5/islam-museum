## Summary
Sulayyan ibn 'Abd Allah ibn al-Fata, Abu 'Abd Allah al-Nahrawani, grammarian and philologist and author of compositions — among them the *Qanun* on language in ten volumes and a tafsir which the people of Isfahan transmitted. He related from Abu Talib ibn Ghaylan and others, and was father of al-Hasan, professor of the Nizamiyya. He died in 493. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 399}
