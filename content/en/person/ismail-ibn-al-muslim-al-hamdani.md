## Overview
Ismail ibn al-Muslim al-Hamdani, in the text "ibn al-Muslim ibn Zayda" in a reading that is not certain, was one of the four who stood by Abu al-Qasim [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Muhammad ibn al-Hadi]] in the charge at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] when he called his companions to attack. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The narrator also names him among the willing elders of Khiwan, with Yusuf ibn Mu'adh, Ibrahim and Ghutayf, who advised Abu al-Qasim to leave. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} See [[event/arhab-and-banu-salman-attack-on-khiwan-285]] and [[event/al-hadi-at-al-darb-and-abu-al-qasim-joins-285]]. No records are made for the other names, as the book adds nothing to them.
