## Summary
The Ghamidi woman, a woman of the tribe of Ghamid who came to the Prophet in year 9 AH and confessed herself guilty of the grave offence {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## Her confession and [[event/stoning-of-al-ghamidiyya|stoning]]
She came and confessed before the Prophet, who turned her back repeatedly, then deferred her until she bore her son and weaned him; she was stoned after his weaning {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}. The Prophet said of her that she had repented a repentance which, divided among seventy of Medina's people, would have sufficed them {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## For young visitors
A woman confessed a grave sin before the Prophet and insisted on bearing its punishment after her child had grown; the Prophet described her repentance as truly immense.
