## Overview
Zaynab bint Ali Fawwaz al-Amili, a historian and writer, who died in 1914 CE. She was born in Jabal Amil, from a family known in the village of Tibnin, grew up in Cairo, stayed briefly in Damascus where she married, then parted from her husband and returned to Egypt, and died in Cairo {cite:al-aalam}.

## Her works
She wrote "al-Durr al-Manthur fi Tabaqat Rabbat al-Khudur" (The Scattered Pearls on the Ranks of the Women of the Veiled Chambers) in one large volume, which al-Zirikli considers the best work in its field. She has a "Collection of Letters" and studies she published in newspapers and magazines {cite:al-aalam}.
