al-Furay'a bint Malik ibn Sinan al-Khudriyya al-Ansariyya, sister of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri; she is also called al-Fari'a. She took part in the Pledge of al-Ridwan with the Prophet, and the four books of the Sunan transmitted for her. The heading gives her death date as unclear (? - 2), which is not read as a date here. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17457}

## In Ghadanfar's Great Women of Islam
[[person/mahmood-ahmad-ghadanfar|Ghadanfar]] presents her as the daughter of [[person/malik-ibn-sinan|Malik ibn Sinan]] al-Khudri and sister of [[person/abu-said-al-khudri|Abu Sa'id al-Khudri]], with Qatada ibn al-Nu'man as her brother on her mother's side. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.220} He relates that her father set out for Uhud with his son Sa'd (Abu Sa'id), whom the Prophet sent back as too young; the boy wept, and al-Furay'a hugged him, wiped his tears and told him to be patient. Malik was killed at Uhud defending the Prophet, and al-Furay'a received the news with the patience the Prophet had urged. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.220} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.221} She was married to Sahl ibn Rafi' ibn Bashir al-Khazraji, who was killed by some of his slaves near Medina; she asked the Prophet's leave to return to her family, and he told her to stay in her house until her waiting period was complete, which she did. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.222} Ghadanfar says that Uthman ibn Affan, as caliph, summoned her when there was disagreement over where a widow should spend her waiting period and ruled by what she told him, that jurists of Medina, Syria, Iraq and Egypt based their rulings on 'idda on her report, and that Ibn al-Qayyim cites a related case from Muhammad ibn Sirin in *Zad al-Ma'ad*. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.222} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.223} She was among those who took the [[event/bayat-al-ridwan|Pledge of Ridwan]]. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.222} {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.223}
