# Ahmet Cevdet Oran

Ahmet Cevdet Oran (1862–1935) was an Ottoman-Turkish journalist who founded the newspaper İkdam and was known by its name as "İkdamcı". He is a different man from Ahmed Cevdet Pasha, the statesman and historian, whom the English article itself distinguishes him from. {cite:wikipedia-ahmet-cevdet-oran-en}

## Career

Born at Istanbul in 1862, the son of the tobacco merchant Hacı Ahmed Efendi, he was educated at the Mülkiye and the law school, began journalism at Tercümân-ı Hakîkat at twenty-one, and after editorial posts at Sabah, Tarik and Saadet launched İkdam in 1894, editing it until 1928 and using it to champion a purified Turkish. As a publisher he issued Evliya Çelebi's Seyahatnâme and Şemseddin Sâmî's Kamus-ı Türki. {cite:wikipedia-ahmet-cevdet-oran-en}{cite:wikipedia-ahmet-cevdet-oran-tr}

## Exile and death

His opposition to the Committee of Union and Progress drove him into exile in France and Switzerland after 1909; he supported the Turkish War of Independence from abroad and returned after the Republic was proclaimed. He died of a heart attack at the Press Congress in Ankara on 27 May 1935 and was buried at Eyüp Cemetery in Istanbul. {cite:wikipedia-ahmet-cevdet-oran-en}{cite:wikipedia-ahmet-cevdet-oran-tr}
