## Summary

Sameera Moussa (3 March 1917 – August 1952) was an Egyptian physicist, Egypt's first woman nuclear physicist and the first woman to work at Cairo University, a pioneer of ionising-radiation research and its medical uses; she organised the Atomic Energy for Peace conference and was killed in a car crash in the United States in 1952, amid unproven assassination claims {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-en} {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-ar}.

## Life

Moussa was born in the village of Sanbo El-Kobra in the Zefta district of Gharbia Governorate, the daughter of a prominent political activist; the Arabic article records that she memorised parts of the Qur'an as a child. She moved with her father to Cairo, excelled at school, took a BSc in radiology at Fouad I University in 1939 and a doctorate on X-ray radiation in Britain, and became the Faculty of Sciences' first woman lecturer {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-ar} {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-en}. No text read names a faith or school for her: the Qur'an memorisation is upbringing, and the earlier Muslim scientists she admired are her field's history, so her faith is recorded as unknown {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-ar} {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-en} {cite:wikidata-sameera-moussa}.

## Work

She researched ionising radiation and pressed for atomic energy to be used peacefully, above all for affordable cancer treatment, organised the Atomic Energy for Peace conference in Cairo, and called for international oversight of atomic energy {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-en} {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-ar}. In August 1952 she travelled to the United States on a scientific mission and was killed when the car she was riding in left the road near Sheridan, Wyoming; the detailed narratives of both articles date the crash to 15 August 1952, while the Arabic article's lead and Wikidata give 5 August, so her death is built on the narrative date with the day marked debated; claims attributing her death to a Mossad assassination are reported as claims, not fact {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-en} {cite:wikipedia-sameera-moussa-ar} {cite:wikidata-sameera-moussa}.
