## Overview
Abu Hashim al-Mughira ibn Miqsam al-Dabbi, a client of the Banu Dabba and transmitter of Ibrahim al-Nakha'i. A Sunni jurist. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}

## His death
He died in 133 AH or after it, according to "al-Tahdhib", as given in the editor's note and not in the text of the book. His name is sometimes printed in some copies as "Abu Hisham". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}
