## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Dawud al-Sanhaji, known as Ibn Ajrum, a Sanhaja jurist and grammarian from [[place/fez|Fez]]. He is known for the science of grammar, and his biography notes an inclination toward Sufism. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.33}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 672 AH (1273 CE) and died in 723 AH (1323 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.33} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
