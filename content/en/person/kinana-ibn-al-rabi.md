## Summary
Kinana ibn al-Rabi' in the Sira is one of the lords of Khaybar's Jews and a keeper of Banu al-Nadir's treasure: he was killed at the taking of Khaybar, and his wife Safiyya bint Huyayy was among the captives whom the Prophet freed and married.

## In the texts
Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Sa'd name him in the affair of Khaybar: one of its chiefs and a keeper of Banu al-Nadir's treasure, which was buried and whose place was unknown; he was killed in the siege after being asked for the treasure on the famous account, and his wife Safiyya the Prophet freed and married {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.

## In the Sira and history
Khaybar fell in year seven; Kinana was one of its lords and was besieged in the forts of al-Qamus and others; when it was taken he was killed, and his wife Safiyya bint Huyayy of Banu al-Nadir was captured — the Prophet gave her the choice, she became Muslim, he freed her and married her and she became a Mother of the Believers {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq} {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.

## For younger visitors
Kinana ibn al-Rabi' was a Jewish lord of Khaybar; he was killed in its taking, and his wife Safiyya became Muslim and the Prophet married her.

## Notes for researchers
He is named with his brother Abu Rafi' and Ibn Abi al-Huqayq in the matter of Banu al-Nadir's treasure; the story of the treasure and the questioning is in Ibn Ishaq's Sira — read it in its place and do not merge differing reports.
