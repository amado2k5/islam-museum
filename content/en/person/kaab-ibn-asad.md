Ka'b ibn Asad al-Qurazi, chief of Banu Qurayza and the holder of their shared covenant with the Prophet in Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Huyayy ibn Akhtab came to him at the outset of the Confederates' campaign calling him to break the pact; Ka'b shut his door in his face and said: "woe to you, Huyayy! You are a man of ill omen — I have covenanted with Muhammad and I shall not break what is between me and him; I have seen nothing from him but truthfulness and faithfulness." But Huyayy kept deluding and deceiving him, pledging to enter the fortress with him if Quraysh should retreat, until Ka'b consented and tore up the pact's document. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When Qurayza were besieged and he was certain of doom he offered his people terms, which they refused; they came down on the judgement of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh — and Ka'b ibn Asad was killed with their fighting men, in recompense for the treachery and the great betrayal at the hour of the Trench. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
