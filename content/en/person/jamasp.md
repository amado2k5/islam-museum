## Overview
Jamasp, a sage mentioned together with Zoroaster in a report of Abu Sahl ibn Nawbakht, as Ibn al-Nadim relays it. The report says that they warned the kings of Persia against the deed of Alexander, and so the books were copied and secured in the land of India and China. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 333}
