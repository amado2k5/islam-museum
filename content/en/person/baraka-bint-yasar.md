## Summary
Freedwoman of Abu Sufyan ibn Harb, wife of Qays ibn Abdullah al-Asadi; she was wet-nurse to Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh and Umm Habiba, and emigrated with them to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her "Baraka bint Yasar, freedwoman of Abu Sufyan ibn Harb" and wife of Qays ibn Abdullah, saying she went out with Umm Habiba and Ubaydullah to the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6} He lists her among the women in "the rarities of the Arabs". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The edition's editors say al-Isti'ab's report stands alone on "she was wet-nurse"; her nursing is not asserted (note p. 6).
