## Overview
Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Hamza al-Fanari, an Ottoman scholar, author of "Unmudhaj al-Ulum". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.665}

## What Haji Khalifa relates
He composed a hundred questions on a hundred arts, and posed riddles about the names of the arts as a test for the excellent scholars of his age. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.665}

He travelled to Qaramān and wrote them there as a test for its scholars, who had been denying his merit. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.665}

He died in 834 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.665}
