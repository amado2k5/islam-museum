## Summary
Mu'izz al-Dawla Thamal ibn Salih al-Kilabi al-Mirdasi, lord of Aleppo, met the Byzantine king at Artah in Aleppo's territory in 454; the Muslims won, plundered and took captives — until a beautiful slave-woman sold for a hundred dirhams. Shortly after, Thamal died at Aleppo. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 301}
