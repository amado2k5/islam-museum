## Overview
Ardashir ibn Babak, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Ardashir ibn Babak". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.37}

Al-Mas'udi says, in his account of backgammon, that he was the first to play it. He said that he made its houses twelve, to the number of the months, and its dogs thirty, to the number of the days. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.681}

The details of his reign still need an additional source; they have not yet been read.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Ardashir son of Babak rose at Istakhr after the Petty Kings had ruled for 266 years, killed Farrukhan, king of al-Jibal and last of Ardawan's line, at Hurmuzdjan, and then marched through the lands as far as Iraq. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 43} He says Ardashir founded six cities, among them Ardashir-Khurra, Ram-Ardashir, Hurmuz-Ardashir (the chief town of al-Ahwaz) and Astadh-Ardashir (Karkh Maysan), and left a covenant for kings which they learned by heart. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 43}
