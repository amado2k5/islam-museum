## Overview
Yazid ibn Rabi'a ibn Mufarrigh al-Himyari, a poet of Himyar, who has poetry about the Umayyads. The lineage of his father is disputed. He died in the year 69 AH according to the encyclopedia {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
