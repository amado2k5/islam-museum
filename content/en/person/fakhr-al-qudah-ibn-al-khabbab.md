## Overview
Fakhr al-Qudah Ibn al-Khabbab, the muqri' and overseer of the awqaf (d. 648); he alone transmitted from al-Ma'muni, so that Sahih Muslim lived on in him after his peers had gone; he was among the precise, trustworthy ones {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 249}.
