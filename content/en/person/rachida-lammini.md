## Overview
Rachida Lammini is a Moroccan teacher and preacher known as Ustadha Rachida Lammini, who gives short recorded religious lessons, many of them addressed to women, in Moroccan Darija and in Arabic, on Qur'an reflection and themes close to daily life: inner peace and tranquillity, the kind word, self-reform and family matters. Clips of her lessons circulate widely under the tag "words of gold" (kalam min dhahab). {cite:tiktok-rachida-lammini} {cite:youtube-rachida-lammini}

No Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item exist for her, and no press or institutional biography was found as of the check date (24 October 2026); this record therefore rests only on her own channel and official account, is deliberately short, attributes to her nothing those sources do not support, and records no birth date or place for her.

## Lessons and reach
The bio of her official TikTok account calls it "the official channel of Ustadha Rachida Lammini" and links her YouTube channel (@rachidalammini) and her Instagram account; the founder's harvest recorded about 302,000 subscribers for her YouTube channel in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. Her TikTok account showed about 104,500 followers and 36 videos at the check. {cite:tiktok-rachida-lammini} {cite:youtube-rachida-lammini}

Among the titles of her circulating lessons are a reflection on Ayat al-Kursi titled "How to live in peace with Ayat al-Kursi?", a lesson on "true happiness" in Surah Ta-Ha, "A plan to live in peace and tranquillity", an exhortation "Return to yourself, O human being", and a filmed supplication for her followers from al-Rawda in Madinah. {cite:tiktok-rachida-lammini}

Her clips also spread through fan accounts that repost her lessons, which makes her total reach hard to delimit; the museum records the figures of her official accounts only. {cite:tiktok-rachida-lammini}
