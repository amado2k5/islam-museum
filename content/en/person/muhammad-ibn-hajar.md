## Overview
Muhammad ibn Hajar ibn Sulayman. Ibn al-Nadim says that Hajar was from Harran and was eloquent, that he wrote on his own behalf to the governors of Armenia and Syria, and that he has books that were compiled. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 172}

The original wording of the entry is ambiguous between Muhammad and his father Hajar; this needs review of the source.
