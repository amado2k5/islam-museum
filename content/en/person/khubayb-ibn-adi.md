Khubayb ibn Adi ibn Malik al-Ansari al-Awsi, a Badri Companion famed for worship and asceticism; he was captured at the affair of al-Raji' and carried to Mecca, where the sons of al-Harith ibn Amir ibn Nawfal bought him to kill him in revenge for their father, whom Khubayb had slain on the day of Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

They imprisoned him in Mecca for months until the sacred months had passed, then brought him out to al-Tan'im to crucify him. He asked them to leave him be until he prayed two rak'as — he prayed them light and complete — then turned to them and said: "by God, were it not that you would think it fear of death, I would have made the prayer longer" — so he was the first to establish the two rak'as at execution for every patient captive. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

When they raised him on the timber they asked: would you wish Muhammad were in your place while you sat safe among your family? He said: "by God, I would not wish Muhammad now, in the place where he is, to be hurt by even a thorn while I sit among my family" — then he prayed against them and recited his famous verses on steadfastness until he was martyred. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh relates that when the polytheists crucified Khubayb ibn Adi in Mecca to kill him slowly, Abu Sufyan, then still an unbeliever, asked him: would you like to be safe with your family and Muhammad in your place? He said: by God, that you cut me to pieces is dearer to me than that the Messenger of God be pricked by a thorn; he tells it as an example of steadfastness. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:06:30}

## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's lessons "Madrasat al-Hayat"
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] tells Khubayb's story in the lesson "The reality of beneficial knowledge" as a witness that whoever trusts God in his heart, God subjects the means of the world to him. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:34:00} In his telling the Prophet sent ten scouts under Asim ibn Thabit al-Ansari; about two hundred archers pursued them; Asim refused to come down under an unbeliever's guarantee; seven were killed and three came down, and one of these, when they were bound, called it the first treachery and was killed. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:34:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:35:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:35:30} The Banu al-Harith bought Khubayb because he had killed al-Harith at Badr, and held him until the sacred months were over, since they honoured them even in the Jahiliyya. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:36:00}

He relates that Khubayb borrowed a razor to shave, that a small boy of the woman of the house sat on his knee and she was alarmed, and he said he would not do it; she said she had never seen a better captive, having seen him eat grapes in chains when there was no fruit in Mecca. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:37:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:38:30} He says Khubayb was the first to establish the two rak'as for one killed sabran, which he explains as being killed while bound; that he prayed two short rak'as and said he would have lengthened them were it not that they might think him afraid; and that he then invoked against them. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:41:00} He adds that the story became known through the woman, who later became Muslim. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:41:30} That Khubayb was first to establish the two rak'as before execution is also stated in al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
