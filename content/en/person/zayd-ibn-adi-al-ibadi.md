## Overview
Zayd ibn Adi al-Ibadi, author of the denunciation that he arranged before Khusraw against [[person/al-numan-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir]], king of [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]]. Khusraw became angry with al-Nu'man and sent for him. al-Nu'man deposited his family and property with [[person/hani-ibn-masud|Hani ibn Mas'ud]], then went to Khusraw, who imprisoned him until he died {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/29-32}. That was the first link in the chain that ended in [[event/battle-of-dhu-qar|the day of Dhu Qar]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26}.

## What is not covered here
al-Raheeq does not give the content of the denunciation or its motive (the killing of al-Nu'man's father Adi ibn Zayd, in the account of the narrators). Only what the book says is recorded here.

## For young visitors
Zayd was a man of the court of Khusraw who denounced the king of al-Hira, and Khusraw imprisoned him until he died.
