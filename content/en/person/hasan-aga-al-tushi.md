## Summary
[[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]]'s deputy over Algiers {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 216}.

## The appointment
When Khayr al-Din resolved to travel to Istanbul in 940/1533 he appointed Hasan Agha al-Tushi in his place — a man al-Salabi describes as wise, pious and broadly learned — and he remained in office as deputy after Khayr al-Din was made Kapudan Pasha {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 216-217}.
