## Summary
Abu Abd Allah al-Barani was one of the well-known worshippers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 489 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 137 and 138. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/137}

## His sayings on contentment and deeds
Hakim ibn Jumu'a relates from Abu Abd Allah al-Barani: the highest rank on the Day of Resurrection will belong to those who are content with God in every state, and whoever is granted contentment has reached the best ranks. Whoever renounces by understanding the reality has a light burden, and whoever does not know the reward of deeds finds every state heavy. The scan of the transmitter's name is unclear; it is given here as the context reads it. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/138}
