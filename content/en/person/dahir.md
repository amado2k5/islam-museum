## Overview
Dahir, king of Sind, who met Muhammad ibn al-Qasim in the battle at the river Mihran. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.104}

## Cause of the campaign
Al-Baladhuri mentions that he refused to free the women taken by pirates from Daybul, and said that they were robbers beyond his reach. So [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] sent [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] on the campaign against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The battle
He was on an elephant, with the war elephants and the Kakara around him, and they fought a fierce battle. Then he dismounted and fought until he was killed at evening, and his companions were routed. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.104}

Al-Mada'ini mentions that a man from Banu Kilab killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
