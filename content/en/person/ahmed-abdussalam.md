## Summary
Ahmed Abdussalam is a preacher whose recorded lessons are kept by the Assabile archive, which lists him among the speakers of Egypt; it holds only one series by him, the programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']], twenty-two video episodes on invoking blessings on the Prophet, loving him and following him. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep14 00:00:30} No Wikipedia article or Wikidata item about him was found (the Arabic article under this name is about another man, a Tunisian historian), so everything on this page comes from his recordings and is attributed to him. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam}

## What the recordings show
He presents the programme alone in a formal Arabic mixed with Egyptian colloquial, addresses his viewers, men and women, as 'O Muslim man, O Muslim woman', asks them questions and asks them to say amen to his prayers. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:06:00} In the second episode he prays for the crew of the channel that broadcasts the programme, and he describes the episodes as short in form and great in meaning. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:00:30} His examples include the Egyptian pound, people's rivalry over the size of their flats, and a telephone call from an unknown number. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:09:30} In the twentieth episode he tells his viewers he loves them for God's sake, with no trade or anything else binding him to them. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep20 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep20 00:08:30}

## His method in the programme
He builds the programme on a principle he repeats: that testifying to the Prophet's messengerhood entails five practical duties, the first obedience and the last worshipping God only as the Prophet laid down. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:07:30} He explains words before urging them, saying God is not worshipped with speech one does not understand. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:08:00} He cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Ahmad's Musnad and al-Albani's grading, and of one report he does not vouch for says God knows best whether it is sound. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep19 00:03:30} Stories and word-pictures dominate his style; he often asks the viewer to imagine the scene with him. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep22 00:01:00}
