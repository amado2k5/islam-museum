## Overview
Al-Malik al-'Aziz 'Uthman b. al-'Adil Abi Bakr b. Ayyub, full brother of al-Mu'azzam; lord of Banias, Tibnin and Hunin — he built al-Subayba castle between those towns — sensible and tranquil; his death occurred at al-Na'ima, an orchard of his where he slept at Salihiyya of Damascus, on 10 Ramadan 630. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 136}
