## Overview
Yahya, titled al-Wathiq billah, son of Muhammad al-Mustansir billah ibn Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Abi Hafs, was one of the kings of the Hafsid state at Tunis. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 679 AH, 1280 CE, and prints zero for the year of birth, meaning it is not known {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 165}.

## Life
The oath was taken to him after his father's death in 675 AH; he removed grievances and wrongs, released prisoners and was lavish in payments to the army {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 165}. His uncle Ibrahim ibn Yahya rose against him and he stood down in 678 AH; his uncle then had him arrested and slaughtered him together with his sons {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 165}. Al-Zirikli adds that he came to be known afterwards as al-Makhlu, the deposed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 165}.
