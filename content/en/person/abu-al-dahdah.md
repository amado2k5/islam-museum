## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy relates that when Abu al-Dahdah heard God's words about lending to Him, he lent his orchard to God, then called to his wife: Umm al-Dahdah, bring your children out, for we have lent this orchard to God, Lord of the worlds. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:11:00}

## High resolve
The Sheikh comments on the account, 'look at this resolve', and contrasts it with those raised on love of the world rather than love of religion; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:12:00}
