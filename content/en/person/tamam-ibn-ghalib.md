## Overview
Tamam ibn Ghalib ibn Umar al-Qurtubi al-Mursi, Abu Ghalib, an Andalusian philologist from Cordoba and Murcia, who died in the year 433 AH (1041 CE) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## His nisba
The encyclopedia says that his nisba to "al-Tiyani" indicates a seller of tiyani {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

His works and the details of his life need an additional source. The date of death rests on a single source.
