## Overview
Roger, lord of Sicily, a contemporary of the state of al-Mu'izz ibn Badis and his son, as Ibn Khaldun mentions {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1635-1636}.

## Mahdiyya
His fleets came down on Mahdiyya in 543 AH with three hundred ships, commanded by Jirjis ibn Mikha'il al-Antaki. He took the whole coastal region and imposed on its people the tax called "al-jari" {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1635-1636}.

The situation remained so until 'Abd al-Mu'min, shaykh of the Muwahhidun, rescued them {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1635-1636}.
