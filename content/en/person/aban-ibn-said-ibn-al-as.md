## Summary
Of Banu Abd Shams, brother of Khalid and Amr, the sons of Sa'id; Ibn Ishaq attributes to him verses addressed to his two brothers when they became Muslim, which Khalid answered. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq mentions that Aban ibn Sa'id ibn al-As addressed his brothers Khalid and Amr in verses when they became Muslim, and that Khalid answered him in verses. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} The book does not state here what Aban's own state was when he spoke the verses, nor does it state that he emigrated to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}
