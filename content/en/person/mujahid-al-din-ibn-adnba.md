## Overview
Mujahid al-Din Ibn Adnba, wali of Damascus (d. 654) — a sensible, virtuous governor; he built the Khanqah al-Mujahidiyya at Damascus on the southern ridge, where he lies buried; among his verses: "I likened you to the bough in grace of form and pliancy — but the bough betrays only what it bears, and you betray" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 272-273}.
