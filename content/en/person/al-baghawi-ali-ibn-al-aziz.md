## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn al-Aziz al-Baghawi, a hadith scholar from Baghdad. He transmitted from al-Nasa'i, Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Musa ibn Harun and others, and al-Husayn al-Naysaburi, the memoriser known as al-Qabbani, took from him. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.650-651}

His birth and death years are not recorded and need an additional source.
