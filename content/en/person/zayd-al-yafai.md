## Summary
Zayd al-Yafa'i, the Yemeni imam-jurist, studied fiqh under Abu Ishaq al-Marhi, who married him to his daughter; he then moved to Mecca and read under al-Husayn ibn 'Ali al-Shashi, pupil of Shaykh Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi. Yemen's people gathered round him — Imam Yahya, author of *al-Bayan*, and 'Abd Allah al-Hamdhani among others read to him. He was small of body, great of awe. He died at al-Jund in 514. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 523-524}
