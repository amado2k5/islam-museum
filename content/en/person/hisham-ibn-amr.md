Hisham ibn Amr al-Amiri al-Qurashi, a Meccan notable known for gallantry and keeping kinship-ties; he would come to Abu Talib's defile at night with his camel laden with food and clothing, loose its halter at the pass-mouth and strike it — it would enter the pass and Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib would take it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He was the first mover in annulling the boycott document: he walked to [[person/zuhayr-ibn-abi-umayya|Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya]] and reminded him of his maternal uncles, then to [[person/mutim-ibn-adi|al-Mut'im ibn Adi]], then to [[person/abu-al-bakhtari-ibn-hisham|Abu al-Bakhtari ibn Hisham]], then to [[person/zamaa-ibn-al-aswad|Zam'a ibn al-Aswad]], until they gathered at al-Hajun and covenanted to tear the document and annul the pact. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
