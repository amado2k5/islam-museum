Hisham ibn Amr al-Amiri al-Qurashi, a Meccan notable known for his chivalry and his ties of kinship. He would come to the Shi'b of Abu Talib by night with a camel loaded with food and clothing. When he reached the mouth of the valley he would loosen its halter and strike it, so that it entered the valley, and Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib would take it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He was the prime mover in annulling the boycott document. He went to [[person/zuhayr-ibn-abi-umayya|Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya]] and reminded him of his maternal kin, then to [[person/mutim-ibn-adi|al-Mut'im ibn Adi]], then to [[person/abu-al-bakhtari-ibn-hisham|Abu al-Bakhtari ibn Hisham]], then to [[person/zamaa-ibn-al-aswad|Zam'a ibn al-Aswad], until they gathered at al-Hajun and agreed to tear the document and break the pact. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}


## On the annulment of the boycott document (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum p.110)
He used to take food secretly by night to the Banu Hashim in the Shi'b. He was the one who began the effort to annul the document, going first to [[person/zuhayr-ibn-abi-umayya|Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya]] ([[event/nullification-of-boycott-covenant|the annulment of the document]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.110}.
