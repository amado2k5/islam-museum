## Overview
The qutb, knower of God, Ahmad b. al-Shaykh Husayn b. al-Shaykh 'Abdallah al-'Aydarus; al-Nur says he was among the masters of the Path's shaykhs, the unveiled by the lights of the Real — granted perfection of character and creation, breadth of gnosis, sound intent and true dealing; many virtues, famous states. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 352}

## His death
He died on 7 Jumada I at Tarim, and my father mourned him in a great ode opening: "the fates spent him and moved on — after clear water comes the turbid." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 352}
