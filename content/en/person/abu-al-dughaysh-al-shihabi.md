## Overview
Abu al-Dughaysh al-Shihabi, of the Shihabis, was among those who used to levy the town of [[place/wasaha-yemen|Wasaha]] before [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] entered it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.81} In Rabi' II 285 AH he gathered a large force and withheld the alms, and al-Hadi's governor and his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] fought him, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.85} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.86} he was routed at Afara, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.87} and then sought security through [[person/yusuf-ibn-muhammad-al-hasani|Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Hasani]] and was given it on an oath to hear and obey. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.88} The narrator's descriptions of him are hostile and are his words, not the museum's. See [[event/battle-of-afara-285]].
