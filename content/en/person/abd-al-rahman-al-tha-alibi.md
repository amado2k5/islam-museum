## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Makhluf al-Tha'alibi al-Jaza'iri (786–875 AH, around 1385–1470 CE) was an Algerian scholar, attributed to the Tha'aliba tribe that settled the plain of Mitija. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
