# Abdoulaye Wade

Abdoulaye Wade (born 29 May 1926) is a Senegalese politician from Kébémer, founder and long-time leader of the Senegalese Democratic Party (PDS), who served as the third president of Senegal from 2000 to 2012.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-fr}

## Lawyer, teacher, opposition leader

Wade was born at Kébémer into a Wolof family from Gandiol and Waalo. He studied and taught law in France, took two doctorates in law and economics, and became dean of the law and economics faculty at the University of Dakar.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en} At the 1974 Mogadishu summit of the Organization of African Unity he told President Léopold Sédar Senghor that he wanted to found a new party, and Senghor agreed: the PDS was founded on 31 July 1974, and Wade led it for the rest of his career.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en} He ran for president four times from 1978 onward, a quarter-century as the country's leading opposition figure, before finally winning in 2000 — taking office at 73, the oldest person to occupy the post.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en}

## President

In the second round of the 2000 election, on 19 March, Wade defeated the incumbent Abdou Diouf by 58.5 per cent to 41.5 per cent, and he took office on 1 April 2000.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdou-diouf-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en} He won re-election in 2007 with a majority in the first round. His bid for a third term in 2012, allowed by the Constitutional Council on the argument that his first term predated the 2001 constitution, was fiercely contested; he was defeated in the second round by his former prime minister Macky Sall.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdoulaye-wade-en}
