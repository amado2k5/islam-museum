## Overview
Al-Kamal al-Mahalli, blind shaykh of the readers at Cairo; a company profited from him — he was among those salaried to teach hadith at the western iwan of the Zahiriyya madrasa between the Palaces at its completion in 662; he died in Rabi' II at fifty-one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 585, 533}
