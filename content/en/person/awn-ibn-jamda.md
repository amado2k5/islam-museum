## Overview
Awn ibn Jamda ibn Hubayra al-Makhzumi. Al-Baladhuri, on the authority of Abu Mikhnaf, says that Ali sent him to Sistan, and that the Tayyi robber Bihdali killed him on the road to Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
