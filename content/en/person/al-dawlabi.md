## Overview
Ali ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Dawlabi, a jurist of al-Tabari's school; his works include a Refutation of Ibn al-Mughallas, books on the basmala, the readings, the principles of kalam, the Tabsira, an epistle to Nasr al-Qushuri, and the greater, lesser and middle Usul.
