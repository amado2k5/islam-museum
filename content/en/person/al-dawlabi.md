## Overview
Ali ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Dawlabi, one of those who studied jurisprudence according to the school of al-Tabari, according to Ibn al-Nadim. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.327-328}

## His books
The "Fihrist" lists among his works: a refutation of Ibn al-Mughallis, the readings, the principles of theology, the actions of the Prophet, "al-Tabsir", the greater, middle and lesser "Usul", the interpretation of dreams, and proof of the mission. He also has two letters, to Nasr al-Qushuri and to Ali ibn Isa. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.327-328}
