## Overview
Abdallah ibn Rashiq al-Maghribi was a copyist from Damascus; the entry dates him to 749 AH (1349 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. The entry is a single sentence and says nothing more of his life.

## What was said of him
Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Kathir on him: he was the copyist of the works of "our shaykh the great scholar Ibn Taymiyya", and when something escaped the shaykh, this Abdallah brought it out {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.
