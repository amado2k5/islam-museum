## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Abd al-Rahman al-Sumayt as the model of a man who made himself a great life project: a Kuwaiti physician who left medicine and turned his whole life to serving Islam and development in Africa, spending six months in Africa and six in Kuwait, and who founded the 'Direct Aid' society (jam'iyyat al-'awn) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:48:30}.
He says of his projects that six million people embraced Islam through the committee he founded, and that he built three hundred schools and thirty hospitals besides clinics — adding that no one in the whole Islamic world serves the cause of Africa's Muslims like him (the speaker's figures and comparison; needs_source) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:49:00}.
