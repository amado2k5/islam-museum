## Overview
Abu al-Qasim ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Barzali al-Balawi, the jurist and imam of Tunis according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". He was born in Kairouan and settled in Tunis. He was born in 741 AH (1339 CE) and died in 844 AH (1439 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He wrote the "Great Diwan" on jurisprudence. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The dates come from one source and need a second.
