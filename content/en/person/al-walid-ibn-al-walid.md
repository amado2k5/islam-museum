## Overview
Al-Walid ibn al-Walid ibn al-Mughira was the brother of Khalid ibn al-Walid; Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy mentions him in his lesson and says that his brother's fame has overshadowed him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:53:30}. The Sheikh calls him a student of the Messenger of God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:54:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 10)

The Sheikh mentions al-Walid ibn al-Walid ibn al-Mughira, the brother of Khalid, and says Khalid's fame has overshadowed his brother; he says al-Walid was the thirteenth Muslim in the order of the Companions, Umar the fortieth, and that Khalid became Muslim 21 years after the revelation of 'Iqra' (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:53:30}. He calls him a student of the Messenger of God, and relates that the Prophet told the Companions that Makkah would cast its liver-pieces to them, which took the Companions by surprise {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:54:30}.
