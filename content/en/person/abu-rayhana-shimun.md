## Overview
Abu Rayhana Shimun, a Companion who witnessed the conquest of Damascus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3482}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet; Abu al-Husayn al-Haytham ibn Shafi, Mujahid ibn Jabr and Shahr ibn Hawshab narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3482}

## In Ibn al-Mubarak's Kitab al-Zuhd
Part 7 of Ibn al-Mubarak's [[work/kitab-al-zuhd-ibn-al-mubarak|Kitab al-Zuhd]] opens with reports about Abu Rayhana, whom the narrator calls "one of the Companions of the Prophet". {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 304} One relates that he returned from a campaign, had supper, made ablution and went to his place of prayer, reciting one chapter after another until the caller sounded before dawn; when his wife reproached him, he said his heart had been carried away by what God describes of paradise. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 304} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 305} Another relates that he asked his commander for one night's leave to visit his family in Jerusalem, went first to the mosque and prayed until morning, then returned to his outpost without seeing his family, saying, "I do not lie and I do not break my word." {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 305}

Another relates that while garrisoned at Mayyafariqin he bought a rope from a Nabataean for a few coins and forgot to pay until he reached the pass of al-Rastan near Hims, and then went all the way back to pay the man. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 306} And that passing through Hims he heard its people quarrelling over the division of their dwellings, and raised his hands praying that it would not become a trial for them. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 306} The editor notes that Ibn Hajar gives some of these reports in the entry on Sham'un Abu Rayhana in al-Isaba, as does Abu Nu'aym in the Hilya. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 305} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 306}
