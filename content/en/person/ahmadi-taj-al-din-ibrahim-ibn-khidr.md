## Overview
Taj al-Din Ibrahim ibn Khidr Ahmadi, one of the best-known Ottoman poets of the fourteenth century CE. His name is Ibrahim, his title Taj al-Din, and his father Khidr. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he died in 815 AH (1412 CE). The same source estimates his birth at about 735 AH (1334 CE). Both come from one source and need a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
