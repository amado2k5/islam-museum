## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas, uncle of the two caliphs al-Saffah and al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}

## The siege of Kamkh
Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Mansur put him in charge of the raiders in 149 AH from Hadithat al-Mawsil, and ordered him to raid Kamkh with them. He went with al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba to Melitene, and they took provisions from there, then camped at Kamkh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}

Catapults were set up, and the Byzantines killed two hundred Muslims with stones. The Muslims made shelters until they took the fortress. Matar al-Warraq was with him on this raid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}
