## Summary
Of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b; Ibn Ishaq names her among the Abyssinian emigrant women. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her among the emigrant women to Abyssinia of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b, tracing her to Abu Hathma ibn Ghanim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} The text does not state her husband at this point.

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi ibn Ka'b]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The book gives "Layla bint Abi Hathma"; the text names neither her husband nor her fate on p. 11.
