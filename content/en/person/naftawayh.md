## Overview
Naftawayh, a grammarian and author of the two-volume book "Tarikh al-Khulafa", who lived into the days of al-Qahir. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.594}

## al-Mas'udi's mention of him
al-Mas'udi counts him among those who collected the Prophet's sayings in their books, alongside Ibn Duraid, al-Zajjaji, and Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Mawsili. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.594}
