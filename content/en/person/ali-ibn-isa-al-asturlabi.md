## Overview
Ali ibn Isa al-Asturlabi, an astrologer. Al-Mas'udi mentions him in "Muruj al-Dhahab" among those whom al-Mansur brought near for their knowledge of the rules of the stars. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.669}
