## Overview
Arslan Arghun b. Sultan Alp Arslan the Seljuk — lord of Marw, Balkh, Nishapur and Tirmidh; a tyrannical and stubborn man. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 394}

## His murder
A mamluk of his killed him at Damghan in 490, and his nephew Barkiyaruq took Khurasan without a fight. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 394}
