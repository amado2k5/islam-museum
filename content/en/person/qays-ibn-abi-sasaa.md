## Summary

Qays ibn Abi Sa'sa'a al-Ansari, named Amr, of Banu Ghanm of Banu al-Najjar. He witnessed al-Aqaba and Badr, where the Prophet placed him over the rear-guard. His hadith includes asking the Prophet how often the Qur'an should be read through, answered: every fifteen days.
