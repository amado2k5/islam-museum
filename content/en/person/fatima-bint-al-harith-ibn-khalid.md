## Summary
Daughter of al-Harith ibn Khalid and Rayta, born in the land of Abyssinia — the only one of Rayta's children to survive. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her a "daughter" Rayta bore there who came back with her — none other of her children survived — and she is called Fatima. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} He counts her among the five females born in Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-tahm|Banu Taym ibn Murra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The phrase "she is called Fatima" is a marker of doubt in her naming.
