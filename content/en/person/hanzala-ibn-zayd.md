## Overview
Hanzala ibn Zayd, brother of Urwa ibn Zayd al-Khayl al-Ta'i. Al-Baladhuri mentions him in "The Conquests of the Lands" in the account of al-Rayy, saying that Urwa left him in al-Rayy when he came to Ammar and then to Umar with the good news. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

The data does not give a wider lineage or a date of death; an additional source is needed.
