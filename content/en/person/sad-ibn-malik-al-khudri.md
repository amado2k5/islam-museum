## Overview
A Companion of the Prophet, of the Banu Khudra of the Ansar, who narrated hadith. His full name in the encyclopedia is Sa'd ibn Malik ibn Sinan al-Khazraji al-Ansari al-Khudri {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
The encyclopedia gives his birth as around 612 CE and his death in the year 74 AH (693 CE). These dates are given on the basis of transmitted report and need a second source {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Notes for researchers
- The entry rests on the encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' alone, with no page numbers in the data. It needs an additional source.
