## Overview
Mustafa ibn Fathallah al-Shafi'i, al-Hamawi and later al-Makki, is described by al-Zirikli as a historian and one of the literary men of his age. His family came from Hama, and he died in 1123 AH, 1711 CE, at about eighty years of age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.

## His travels
He left Hama for Damascus and read with some of its scholars, then travelled to Yemen and took widely from its people, settled in Mecca, and died at Dhamar in Yemen {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.

## His work
He wrote Fawa'id al-Irtihal wa-Natayij al-Safar fi Akhbar Ahl al-Qarn al-Hadi Ashar, a manuscript in three large volumes held in Dar al-Kutub under number 1093. Al-Zirikli says he acquired a photographic copy of its second half {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.
