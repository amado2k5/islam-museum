## Overview
Ziyad ibn al-Habula, one of the most famous kings of the [[group/dajaima|Dajaima]], the Banu Dajam ibn Sulayh of [[group/qudaa|Qudaa']], whom the Romans set up as kings over the Arabs of [[place/al-sham|Syria]] before the Ghassanids {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/34} {cite:tarikh-ard-al-quran 2/80-82}.

## Time
The author estimates the period of the Dajaima from the early second century CE to about its end, and their rule ended after the coming of the Ghassanids, who overcame them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27}.

## What is not covered here
al-Raheeq does not go beyond naming him. His reports in the hands of the historians have not been added.

## For young visitors
Ziyad was an ancient Arab king in Syria whom the Romans appointed before the kings of the Ghassanids.
