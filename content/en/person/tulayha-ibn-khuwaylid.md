## Overview
Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid, war-chief of Banu Asad who claimed prophethood at the Prophet's death; [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] broke him at Buzakha and he fled to Syria — then returned a Muslim and fought in the conquests.

## Life
When the Prophet died Tulayha rose among Banu Asad claiming revelation; clans of Ghatafan and Tayy' gathered to him, and his was the Ridda trouble closest to Medina {cite:tabari-donner-1993}.

Khalid ibn al-Walid marched on him and broke him at [[event/battle-of-buzakha|Buzakha]] in 632 CE, and Tulayha fled to Syria beyond the caliphate's reach {cite:tabari-donner-1993}.

Then he returned a Muslim; the tradition says he witnessed the engagements in Iraq among the Muslims' soldiery and died in Umar's caliphate {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

## For young visitors
Tulayha claimed prophethood, was defeated and fled — then returned a Muslim and fought in the ranks of the side that had beaten him.
