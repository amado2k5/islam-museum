## Summary
Shaykh Najm al-Din Abu 'Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn 'Ali, known as Ibn al-Habbaz al-Mawsili, imam-jurist and professor of the Sayfiyya madrasa at Aleppo — Ibn Khallikan read to him the whole of al-Ghazali's *al-Wajiz* through to the ijaza. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
