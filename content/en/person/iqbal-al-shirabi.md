## Summary
Iqbal al-Shirabi built a madrasa at Wasit with a mosque beside it, built a madrasa at Baghdad in the Sultan's Market, renewed at Mecca the ribat that bore his fame and the 'Arafa spring at the station and ran its water for the pilgrims' benefit, and made fine endowments for all of it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 269}
