## Overview
Salama ibn Hisham ibn al-As ibn al-Mughira al-Makhzumi, brother of Abu Jahl, counted among the Companions and the fighters {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Mu'ta and his death
He escaped from Mu'ta, and it is reported that he withdrew from prayer out of shame at what was said about his army {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

al-Baladhuri lists him among the martyrs of the [[event/battle-of-ajnadayn|Battle of Ajnadayn]] in Jumada al-Ula of the year 13 AH. It is also said that he was killed at Marj al-Safar in Muharram of the year 14 AH. The year of his death is disputed in the source, and the conversion to the Common Era is derived, approximately 634 CE {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.156}.
