## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn al-Bitriq, Abu al-Husayn al-Asadi al-Hilli, was a researcher (bahith). Al-Zirikli counts him among the jurists of the Imamiyya, the Twelver Shi'a, a community distinct from the Sunni frame that is this museum's main reference, so what follows is al-Zirikli's description of an Imami author. He gives his dates as 523 to 600 AH, that is 1129 to 1204 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 141}.

## Movements
He was from Hilla in Iraq, lived in Baghdad for a time, stayed in Wasit, and was in Aleppo in 596 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 141}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Umda fi uyun sihah al-akhbar (manuscript), on the merits of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib; Ittifaq sihah al-athar fi imamat al-a'imma al-ithna ashar; and al-Radd ala ahl al-nazar fi tasaffuh adillat al-qada' wa-l-qadar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 141}.
