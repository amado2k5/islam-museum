## Summary
Idris ibn Sultan Ya'qub ibn Yusuf, Abu'l-'Ala' al-Ma'mun; they gave him the bay'a in al-Andalus, then he came to Marrakesh, ruled it and his sultanate grew mighty — a brave hero of great awe and much bloodshed; he cut Ibn Tumart's mention from the khutba, and died a ghazi in 630. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 136}
