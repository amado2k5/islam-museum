## Overview
Muhammad al-Amin ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammadi al-Shinqiti al-Alawi. A Mauritanian scholar from a family of learning, known for learning and teaching in the lands of Shinqit. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.264}

He was born in 1875 CE (1292 AH) and died in 1963 CE (1383 AH). These dates are traditional, from one source, and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.264}
