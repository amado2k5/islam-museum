## Summary
Marwan al-Asfar, Abu Khalaf of Basra; some say his name was Marwan ibn Khaqan, and some say there were two men. He narrated from Anas ibn Malik, Amir al-Sha'bi, Abd Allah ibn Umar, Masruq and Abu Hurayra; Ja'far ibn Burqan and Harb ibn Thabit narrated from him. Al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi record him. Abu Dawud called him reliable. Dates not given. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13367}
