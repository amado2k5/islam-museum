## Summary
Ammar ibn Sayf was the man to whom Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak bequeathed his books; he erased and burned them, and they left no issue, according to the Fihrist. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 311}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim records in the Fihrist, in the entry on Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak, that he bequeathed his books to Ammar ibn Sayf, who erased and burned them leaving nothing behind. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 311} This occurred in the year 181 AH, when Ibn al-Mubarak was sixty-three, born in 118 AH; he left nothing to al-Mubarak ibn Sa'id, as the account states. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 311} By this act Ibn al-Mubarak's manuscript library — his compilations and transmissions — was lost, and of his works only those transmitted by others survived, such as the Great and Lesser Jami', the Fara'id and his epistles. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 311}
