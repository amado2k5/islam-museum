## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn Imri al-Qays al-Lakhmi, king of al-Hira, lord of the first day of Awara. Ibn al-Athir relates that he swore to slaughter the Banu Bakr ibn Wa'il on the summit of the mountain until the blood reached the lowland. He slaughtered their captives and burned their women with fire. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.412}
