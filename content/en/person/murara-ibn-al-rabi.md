## Summary
One of the three left behind, of Banu Amr ibn Awf; Ka'b ibn Malik names him with Hilal ibn Umayya when asked whether anyone else had met this fate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175, 178}

## In the story
His name is mentioned at the opening of the chapter with Ka'b and Hilal; he stayed behind without doubt or hypocrisy per Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175} Ka'b says people mentioned to him "two righteous men in whom is a model", and that his two companions stayed meekly in their houses during the boycott. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/178}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- "al-Amri" is the ascription to Banu Amr ibn Awf, read on the page image.
- The text says no more than the name, the tribe, and his state in the boycott.
