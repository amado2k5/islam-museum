## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Yasin al-Jazuli, a jurist, and the founding missionary of the call of the Almoravids. His death is mentioned as around 451 AH, a derived estimate from the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## The call
Ibn Khaldun relates that the jurist Wajjaj ibn Zallu al-Lamti sent him from Sijilmasa with a delegation of Lamtuna returning from the pilgrimage, to teach them the Qur'an and establish the religion for them. They found his teaching hard and cast him off. He turned away from them and took to seclusion on an island in the Nile delta with Yahya ibn Umar al-Lamtuni and those who followed them, until they reached a thousand. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}

He told them that a thousand would not be defeated for lack of numbers, and that standing for the truth and calling to it was incumbent on them. They went out and fought those among the tribes of Lamtuna, Kadala and Masufa who had resisted, until they returned to the truth. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}
