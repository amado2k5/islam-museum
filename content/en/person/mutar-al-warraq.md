## Summary
Mutar ibn Tahman, Abu Raja al-Khurasani, who settled in Basra; a learned worker who wrote out Qur'an copies and was skilled at it. He narrated from Anas ibn Malik, al-Hasan, Ibn Buraydah, Shahr ibn Hawshab and Bakr ibn Abd Allah; Shu'ba, al-Husayn ibn Waqid, Ibrahim ibn Tahman, Hammad ibn Salama and Hammad ibn Zayd narrated from him. Al-Ijli calls him a truthful Basran, and Ibn Sa'd says there was weakness in his hadith {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13762-13763}. The book gives his death as 129 AH.
