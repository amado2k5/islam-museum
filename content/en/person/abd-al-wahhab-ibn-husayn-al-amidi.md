## Summary
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Husayn ibn Wali al-Din al-Amidi. The book says he was alive in 1190 AH; among his works is a commentary on the treatise al-Walidiyya on the manners of inquiry and debate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15395}
