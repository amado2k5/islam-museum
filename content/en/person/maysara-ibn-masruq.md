## Overview
Maysara ibn Masruq al-Absi, one of the commanders of the detachments of [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]]. Al-Baladhuri says that some counted him as the first to cross the road, that is, [[event/first-crossing-of-darb-baghras|the Darb Baghras]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.224-225}

## The detachment
Abu Ubayda sent him, and he met a band of Romans with Arabized Ghassanids, Tanukh and Iyad, who wanted to join Heraclius. He struck them and killed a great slaughter among them. It is said that he reached Zanda. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.224-225}

## Disagreement among reports
This view is contradicted by those who make the first to cut the road 'Umayr ibn Sa'd in the affair of Jabala, or Abu Ubayda himself. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.224-225}
