## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Jami, Abu al-Barakat Nur al-Din, a Persian poet and Sunni Sufi from Jam, to which he is attributed. He wrote on poetry and Sufism. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 817 AH (1414 CE) and his death as 898 AH (1492 CE). These dates need a second source.
