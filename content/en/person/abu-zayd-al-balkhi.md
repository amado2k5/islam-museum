## Overview
Abu Zayd Ahmad ibn Sahl al-Balkhi, a sage of many sciences. Yaqut relates, and Ahmad Amin repeats, that in his youth he felt a desire to travel from Balkh to Iraq to sit among the scholars. {cite:duha-islam p.434-435}

## His journey and learning
He went to Iraq on foot with the pilgrims and stayed there eight years. He travelled the neighbouring lands and met their great men and notables. {cite:duha-islam p.434-435}

He was a pupil of Abu Yusuf Ya'qub ibn Ishaq [[person/al-kindi|al-Kindi]]. He went deep into philosophy, took up the secrets of astrology and astronomy, excelled in medicine and natural science, and examined the principles of religion. {cite:duha-islam p.434-435}
