## Summary
Nadir al-Islam Abu'l-Tahir Isma'il ibn Makki ibn Isma'il ibn 'Awf al-Zuhri, Maliki shaykh of Alexandria; he took fiqh from Abu Bakr al-Turtushi and heard from him and from Abu 'Abdallah al-Razi, excelled in the madhhab and produced students, and Sultan Salah al-Din sought him out and heard the Muwatta' from him; he died in Sha'ban 581 at ninety-six — on the very day the imam al-Suhayli died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748, 752}
