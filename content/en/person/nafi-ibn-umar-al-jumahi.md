## Overview
Nafi' ibn Umar al-Qurashi al-Jumahi al-Makki was a hafiz of hadith and the muhaddith of Mecca in his time. Al-Zirikli says he died in Mecca in 169 AH, 785 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 5}.

## Judgement on his transmission
Al-Zirikli reports that Ahmad ibn Hanbal said of him that he was firmly reliable, and that his book was sound {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 5}.
