## Summary
Ubayd ibn Umayr ibn Qatada al-Laythi al-Jundai al-Makki, a preacher and exegete born in the Prophet's lifetime. He narrated from his father, Umar, Ali, Abu Dharr, Aisha and Ibn Abbas; his son Abd Allah, Ata ibn Abi Rabah and Amr ibn Dinar narrated from him. He was among the reliable Successors and leaders of Mecca; Ibn Ma'in and Abu Zur'a called him reliable. Death 74 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13362}
