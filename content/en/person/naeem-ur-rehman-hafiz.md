## Summary
Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman is a Pakistani engineer and politician, elected in April 2024 as the sixth emir of Jamaat-e-Islami Pakistan, the country's oldest Islamist party, after a decade leading its Karachi chapter. A hafiz of the Qur'an by training, he built his profile on municipal advocacy in Karachi before taking the party's national leadership. {cite:wikipedia-naeem-ur-rehman-hafiz} {cite:muslim500-naeem-ur-rehman-hafiz}

## Life and formation
He was born on 1 December 1972 in Hyderabad, Sindh, and moved to Karachi, where he took a civil engineering degree at the NED University and a master's in Islamic history at Karachi University. His politics began in the Islami Jamiat-e-Talaba student federation — elected its national nazim in 1998 — before he formally joined Jamaat-e-Islami in 2000. {cite:wikipedia-naeem-ur-rehman-hafiz}

## Karachi leadership
Elected emir of Jamaat-e-Islami's Karachi chapter in 2013, he campaigned on the city's civic grievances — power shortages, water, transport, local-government funds — leading protests against the K-Electric utility and reviving the party's street presence in a city long dominated by other parties. In 2023 he narrowly lost the Karachi mayoral contest amid disputed party-position arithmetic. {cite:wikipedia-naeem-ur-rehman-hafiz}

## National leadership
In April 2024 the party's members elected him emir, succeeding Siraj-ul-Haq, at a moment when Jamaat-e-Islami sought distance from both government and the PTI opposition. His stated programme combines Islamic governance rhetoric with anti-corruption and cost-of-living campaigning. Supporters credit him with energising a shrinking party; critics question whether municipal protest scales to national leadership.
