## Overview
Nu'ayb ibn Ziyad al-Wasiti took charge of the Shu'aybiyya for Ali ibn al-Rashid. In Wasit, on the Tigris, there is a house belonging to one of his descendants that is named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
