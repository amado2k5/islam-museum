## Overview
Kharija ibn Hudhafa. Umar wrote to Amr ibn al-As that he should assign him a share in the honour of the stipend, for his courage. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He may be Kharija ibn Hudhafa al-Adawi, but the data do not settle this. It needs an additional source.
