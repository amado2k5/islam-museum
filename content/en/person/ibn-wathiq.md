## Overview
Ibn Wathiq, shaykh of the qurra', the accomplished master reciter; he read the seven qira'at at Gaza and elsewhere in 595 under several of Abu'l-Hasan Shurayh's pupils, and Abu 'Abd Allah Ibn Zarqun licensed him, whence he transmitted the *Taysir* by ijaza. He taught recitation at Mosul, Syria and Egypt on a towering isnad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 272}.
