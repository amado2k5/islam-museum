## Overview
'Ikrima ibn Abd Allah al-Barbari al-Madani, Abu Abd Allah, the client of Abd Allah ibn 'Abbas, was a Follower reckoned among the most learned in tafsir and the maghazi. He toured the lands and about three hundred men transmitted from him, among them more than seventy Followers. He died at Medina in 105 AH (723 CE) on the same day as the poet Kathir 'Izza, so that it was said: the most learned and the most poetic of men died that day. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.244}

Al-Zirikli relates that he joined Najda al-Haruri and stayed with him six months, after which he used to transmit Najda's doctrine, and that he went to the Maghrib whose people took the Sufriyya doctrine from him; he then returned to Medina and hid from its amir until he died. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.244}
