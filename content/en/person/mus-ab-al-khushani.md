## Overview
Mus'ab ibn Muhammad ibn Mas'ud al-Khushani, a scholar from the Maghrib, who learned from Abd al-Haqq al-Ishbili in Bejaia. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* says that he was born in 535 AH (1139 CE) and died in 604 AH (1208 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
