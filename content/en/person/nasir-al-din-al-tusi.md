## Overview
Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Tusi, who died in 672 AH (about 1274 CE). Haji Khalifa describes him as "the learned verifier" {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.268}.

## Akhlaq-i Nasiri
He wrote his Persian work "Akhlaq-i Nasiri" in Quhistan for its ruler, Nasir al-Din Abd al-Rahim al-Muhtasham {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.268}. See [[work/akhlaq-i-nasiri|Akhlaq-i Nasiri]].
