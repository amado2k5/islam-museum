## Overview
Al-Ashraf Abu Hafs Umar ibn al-Malik al-Muzaffar al-Ghassani, a Yemeni king of the Rasulid dynasty. He wrote on the craft of the astrolabe, the science of veterinary medicine, the treatment of animals, and simple drugs {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.34-35}.

## His works
The encyclopedia reports that he benefited from [[person/ibn-al-baytar|Ibn al-Baytar]] and al-Zahrawi {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.34-35}.
