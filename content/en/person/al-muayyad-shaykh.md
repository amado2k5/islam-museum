## Overview
A Circassian Mamluk sultan, titled al-Mu'ayyad and given the kunya Abu Nasr. He came to the sultanate in 815 AH after al-Nasir Faraj was deposed. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 108}

## Rise to the sultanate
He defeated al-Nasir Faraj and killed him. The caliph al-Musta'in entrusted him with managing the kingdom and gave him the title "Order of the Kingdom". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 767-770} Then he asked the caliph to entrust the sultanate to him, and the caliph made a condition that he come down from the citadel, to which he did not agree. He prevailed over the sultanate, deposed al-Musta'in in Sha'ban 815 AH, and gave the allegiance to Dawud al-Mu'tadid. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 767-770}

The judges of Syria ruled that it was not lawful to depose the caliph, and Nawruz fought him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 767-770}

## The Mu'ayyadiyya madrasa
The Mu'ayyadiyya madrasa was completed in his days in 822 AH, and he appointed as its shaykh al-Shams ibn al-Diyari. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 767-770}
