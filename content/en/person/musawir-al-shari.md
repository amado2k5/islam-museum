## Overview
Musawir al-Shari'i, a leader of the [[group/khawarij|Kharijites]] who rose against Mosul in the days of the caliph al-Mansur, according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The rising against Mosul
He defeated the people of Mosul in the governorship of Uqba ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath, and prevailed over the city, and his followers grew in number. According to Ibn Khaldun he died in his war with the armies in the year 263 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

A second source is needed: Ibn Khaldun mentions al-Mansur and the year 263 AH together, and the gap between these two dates is clear in the data.
