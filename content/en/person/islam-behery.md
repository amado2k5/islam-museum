## Summary
Islam Behery (also written Islam El-Behery) is an Egyptian researcher, writer and television presenter who describes himself as an "Islamic researcher". He holds a master's degree in "methods of dealing with heritage" from the University of Wales in the United Kingdom, worked at the Youm7 institution, and presented *Ma'a Islam* (With Islam) on Al-Qahera wal-Nas — the programme that built both his fame and his dispute with the religious establishment over the inherited tradition. {cite:cnn-arabic-islam-behery-2016}

## The programme and the trial
*Ma'a Islam* stirred wide controversy in religious and cultural circles; Al-Azhar criticised it and the channel suspended it. In May 2015 an Egyptian court convicted him of "contempt of religion" and sentenced him to five years, reduced in December of the same year to one. On 17 November 2016 President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi issued decree 515 pardoning 82 prisoners, Behery among them, the first batch of pardons under Article 155 of the constitution. {cite:ahram-online-sisi-pardons-2016} {cite:cnn-arabic-islam-behery-2016}

## His project in his own words
In his CNN Arabic interview after the pardon he said prison had confirmed to him that he stood on solid ground and had "done something that mattered", and he read the pardon as an apology for a sentence he called unjust and as a defeat for the other side before "the enlightened side and the intellectuals". The museum presents his words and positions as the sources carry them, with no ruling of its own. {cite:cnn-arabic-islam-behery-2016}
