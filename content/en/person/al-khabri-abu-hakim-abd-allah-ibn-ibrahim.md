## Overview
The imam Abu Hakim Abd Allah ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah al-Khabri, a Shafi'i jurist attributed to Khubr. He died in 476 AH (1083 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
