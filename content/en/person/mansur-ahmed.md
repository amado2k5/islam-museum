## Overview
Mansur Ahmed is a British street preacher and debater, and a co-founder and co-host of the DawahWise YouTube channel with Hashim; the project's official website states that "DawahWise is a YouTube channel run by Mansur and Hashim, dedicated to providing education and addressing issues relevant to the Muslim community" through debates, live streams and informative videos. {cite:dawahwise-website} {cite:youtube-dawahwise}

Mansur is known for his public debates at Speakers' Corner in London's Hyde Park, taking up subjects such as the Trinity, the Incarnation, the reliability of the Bible, salvation and divine election, and women's rights in Islam, and he has appeared in Speakers' Corner channels' videos for years, including the SC Dawah channel and Smile2Jannah. {cite:youtube-dawahwise}

No Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item exist for him as of the check date (24 October 2026); this record therefore rests on the channel and the project's website, is deliberately short, and attributes to him nothing those sources do not support. The channel bills him by his first name alone, "Mansur"; the fuller name Mansur Ahmed rests on his social-media handle @mansurxahmed, linked as "Follow Mansur here" by a fellow Speakers' Corner channel (Smile2Jannah), and is not confirmed by an independent biography.

## Career and work
The founder's harvest recorded about 469,000 subscribers for the DawahWise channel in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly, and the project's website links sister channels in several languages and a DawahWise Gold channel. {cite:youtube-dawahwise} {cite:dawahwise-website}

Mansur co-hosts the channel's "Demystifying Hinduism" dialogue series with Hashim and Mohammad, which has hosted Hindu academics and religious leaders, among them Dr Howard Resnick, alongside Muslim guests such as the historian Adnan Rashid; the series description states DawahWise's goal "to educate, inform and inspire you, so that you may be balanced and articulate in your interactions during interfaith discussions", and that building bridges and improving interfaith understanding is a common interest. {cite:youtube-dawahwise}

The project's website states its mission as empowering people with a deeper understanding of Islam, fostering meaningful conversations and exploring Islamic teachings and their application in daily life. {cite:dawahwise-website}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of positions reported by the sources, not as a verdict of the museum on them.

Speakers' Corner debates are by nature adversarial and heated, and clips of Mansur's debates circulate under celebratory titles on fan channels, while DawahWise's own video descriptions publish the project's ground rules: it has never advocated or encouraged violence or illegal activity, completely disavows anyone who commits them, and expects its audience to obey the laws of their localities. In its live streams the channel stresses that the views of guests and callers are their own and do not necessarily reflect those of DawahWise. {cite:youtube-dawahwise} {cite:dawahwise-website}
