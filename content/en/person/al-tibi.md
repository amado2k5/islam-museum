## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Tibi, Sharaf al-Din, a scholar of hadith, exegesis and rhetoric (d. 743 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3490}

## Works
His works include al-Tibyan fi al-ma'ani wa al-bayan, al-Khulasa fi usul al-hadith and a commentary on Mishkat al-masabih. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3490}
