## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry, written in his subject's lifetime, for Zakariyya ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Ansari al-Sunayki, of Cairo and al-Azhar, the Shafi'i: born in 826 at Sunayka in al-Sharqiyya, he moved to Cairo in 841. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 3/234} He studied with al-Qayati, al-Bulqini, Ibn Hajar, al-Kafiyaji and Ibn al-Humam. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 3/234} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 3/235} His works include al-Ghurar al-Bahiyya, a commentary on al-Bahja al-Wardiyya, and Ghayat al-Wusul. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 3/236} He accepted the chief judgeship, after declining, on Tuesday 3 Rajab 886. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 3/238}
