## Overview
Abu Khalifa al-Fadl ibn al-Habbab al-Jumahi, a client of the Banu Jumah of Quraysh, and the orator of the people of Basra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 602-604}

## His eloquence
Al-Mas'udi describes him as their eloquent companion who did not strain over grammar, which had become second nature to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 602-604}

## The delegation of Basra
He came in the delegation of the people of Basra to [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] in white greased boats, complaining of drought and the injustice of the governors. He addressed them in his well-known rhymed prose. The vizier al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah answered a question on the shares of inheritance for camels. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 602-604}

Al-Mu'tadid said of him: "This is a devil the sea has cast up", and that men like him should go to the kings. He then wrote to them what they asked for. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 602-604}

## His later life
He afterwards held the judgeship, and died in Basra in 305 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 602-604}
