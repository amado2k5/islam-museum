## Overview
Muhammad, known as Sa'd al-Din, son of the Shaykh Muhyi al-Din Ibn al-Arabi, and the son of [[person/ibn-arabi-muhyi-al-din|Ibn Arabi]], according to al-Maqqari {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.172}.

## His life
He was born in Malatya in Ramadan of the year 618 AH. He heard hadith, studied, and composed good poetry. He had a well-known collection of poems, visited Cairo, and settled in Aleppo {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.172}.

## His death
He died in Damascus in the year 656 AH, the year Hulagu entered Baghdad and killed the Caliph al-Musta'sim. He was buried beside his father at the foot of Qasyun {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.172}.
