## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Husayn ibn Hasnun, Abu Ahmad al-Samiri, was the authority of the Qur'an readers in his time and a scholar of language. Of Samarra, he grew up at Baghdad, moved to Egypt and died there in 386 AH (996 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.79}

He wrote Kitab al-Lughat fi-l-Qur'an, transmitted with his isnad back to Ibn 'Abbas. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.79}
