## Overview
Malik ibn Sinan, an Ansari of the Banu Khudra ibn Awf ibn al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, and grandfather of [[person/abu-said-al-khudri|Abu Sa'id al-Khudri]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.211}

## The fort
He had a fort called "al-Ajrad", and its well was called "al-Bussa". It was in the house of the Banu Khudra, known by their name. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.211} He was of the [[group/al-khazraj|Khazraj]].
