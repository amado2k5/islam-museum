## Overview
Tammam ibn al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, a cousin of Ali ibn Abi Talib. Al-Sallabi lists him as Ali's governor of Medina {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.357}.

## Governorship
Al-Sallabi says Ali appointed Tammam and Qutham, sons of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, among his kinsmen to provinces {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.357}. In the book's list of Ali's governors, Tammam is second, with Medina as his province {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.357}. The book gives no dates for his office on this page.
