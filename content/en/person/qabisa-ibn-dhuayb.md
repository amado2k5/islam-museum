## Overview
Qabisa ibn Dhu'ayb ibn Amr al-Khuza'i, of Medina. al-Shirazi says that Abu al-Zinad counted him among the four jurists of Medina, together with Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan and Urwa ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.33-36}

The book describes him as the vizier, counsellor and close companion of Abd al-Malik, on his travels and at home. He opposed removing Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan from the succession, interceded on behalf of Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab after he was beaten, and brought al-Zuhri close to Abd al-Malik. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.33-36}

## Death
al-Shirazi reports that Yahya said he died in 87 AH, and al-Waqidi said 86 AH in Syria. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981} al-Sallabi gives his death as 86 AH, or 87, or 88. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.33-36}
