## Overview
Muhammad al-Tardi ibn al-Talib Abdallah ibn Sawda al-Mari al-Fasi, a jurist from a family of learning in Fez. He was also a hadith scholar, a Sufi and a shaykh. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.376}

## Dates
He was born in 1118 AH (around 1707 CE) and died in 1209 AH (around 1795 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.376}
