## Overview
Ibn Muhanna — Malik al-'Arab Abu al-Fadl 'Isa ibn the amir Muhanna ibn Mani' ibn Haditha ibn Fadl ibn the amir Rabi'a al-Ta'i, leader of the Al Fadl Arabs of Syria; a brave, swift and obeyed chieftain with sons and flocks, a complete man of his kind. He died in 683 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2995}
