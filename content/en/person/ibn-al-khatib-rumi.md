## Summary
Mulla Muhyi al-Din Muhammad b. Ibrahim al-Rumi al-Hanafi, famed as Ibn al-Khatib, among the renowned scholars of Rum; he read under his father Mulla Taj al-Din, the 'allama 'Ali al-Tusi and Mulla Khidr Bey, held offices and rose in them until Sultan Muhammad b. 'Uthman made him his own teacher; he wrote glosses on al-Sayyid al-Sharif's commentary on the Tajrid and on the Kashshaf's gloss, and more; he died in 901. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 425}
