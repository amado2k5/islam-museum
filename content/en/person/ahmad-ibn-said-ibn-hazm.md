## Overview
Abu Amr Ahmad ibn Said ibn Hazm al-Andalusi, a vizier and father of the scholar Abu Muhammad [[person/ibn-hazm|Ibn Hazm]]. He was a secretary and a linguist, learned in the science of the language. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.171}

He died in 393 AH. The Gregorian date is approximate and derived from the Hijri date. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.171}

The date of death comes from one source and needs a second.
