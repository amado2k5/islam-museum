## Summary
Abu Bakr Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam, one of two brothers who inherited learning and devotion from their renowned forebears; the dominant traits in him were example and transmission. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], volume 10, printed number 684, page 400, alongside his brother Hammam and their father [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-numan-ibn-abd-al-salam|Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man]]. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## The brothers described together
Abu Nu'aym treats the two together: their standing in learning and asceticism is well known, and their merit among people is widely spread. Abu Bakr was marked by example and transmission, Abu Amr by devotion and care. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}

## A report from Aisha
He relates from Ja'far ibn Ma'bad, from Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man, from Farwa (the rest of the nisba is unclear in the scan), from Ali ibn Mushir, from Yusuf ibn Maymun, from Ata, from Aisha, a report that whoever wishes to outstrip the diligent should refrain from sins. The wording is not given here. Abu Nu'aym judges it gharib: Yusuf alone transmits it from Ata. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/400}
