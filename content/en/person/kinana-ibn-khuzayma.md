## Summary
Father of al-Nadr, the ancestor of Quraysh; the genealogies of Quraysh, Banu Kinana, Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma and Banu Amr meet at him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}; through his grandson Kinana ibn al-Nadr come the Banu Kinana clans.

## His place in the lineage
Four great clans of the Hijaz and Najd descend from Kinana ibn Khuzayma: al-Nadr the ancestor of Quraysh, the Banu Kinana, Banu Asad and Banu Amr ibn Kinana — so 'Banu Kinana' names all these tribes in some genealogists' usage {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:ansab-al-ashraf}.

## For younger visitors
Kinana ibn Khuzayma is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
