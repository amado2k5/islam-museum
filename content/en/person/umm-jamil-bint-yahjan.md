## Overview
Umm Jamil bint Yahjan ibn al-Afqam ibn Sha'ithah ibn al-Mazim, of the Banu Hilal {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The affair
She had a husband from Thaqif, [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-utayk|al-Hajjaj ibn Utayk]]. al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba used to visit her while he was governor of Basra. The witnesses lay in wait for him until he entered her house, and they testified against him before Umar {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

For details, see [[event/mughira-adultery-affair|the affair of al-Mughira]]. The book's page is not given in the data; the entry needs an additional source.
