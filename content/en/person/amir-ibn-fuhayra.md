## Summary
Amir ibn Fuhayra, Abu Bakr's client, the third companion of the hijra {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## At the cave
He pastured for the Prophet and Abu Bakr a gift-flock of sheep, bringing it to them as evening fell so they spent the night on its milk; each of the three nights he drove his sheep over [[person/abdullah-ibn-abi-bakr|Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr]]'s tracks after he left for Mecca, effacing them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 165}.

## On the journey
He travelled with the Prophet and Abu Bakr in the company of the [[journey/hijra-route|hijra]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 167}. When [[person/suraqa-ibn-malik|Suraqa ibn Malik]] asked their safety he sought a writ of security; the Prophet had Amir write it for him on a scrap of leather {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 169} {cite:zad-al-maad}.

He was killed at the Well of Ma'una on the well-known account {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.
