## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Jaza al-Ta'i was sent by [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]], and Hasaka ibn Utab al-Habati killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri relates that Ali said: "I will kill four thousand of the Habata"; it was said to him that the Habata did not number five hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
