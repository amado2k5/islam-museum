## Overview
Yazid ibn Ziyad went out against the people of Kabul when they broke the covenant and took his brother Abu Ubayda captive. He fought them at Janza, and he was killed, with many of those who were with him, and the rest of the people were routed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
