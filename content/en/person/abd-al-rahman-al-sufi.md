## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Umar Abu l-Husayn al-Sufi al-Razi was an astronomer of Persian lands. The Biblissima authority record gives his years as 903 to 986 CE {cite:biblissima Q16843}. He is known for his book on the fixed stars.
## In the sources
The museum's card records the nisba al-Razi as a sign of an association with Rayy, not as proof that he lived there. This page holds no detailed life story, because his entry in a biographical dictionary has not yet been read on the page {cite:biblissima Q16843}.
