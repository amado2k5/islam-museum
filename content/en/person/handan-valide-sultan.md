## Summary
Handan Valide Sultan; mother of Sultan Ahmed I and a consort of Sultan Mehmed III {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Handan Vâlide Sultan'}.

She died on 15 Rajab 1014/26 November 1605 and was buried in her husband's mausoleum {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Handan Vâlide Sultan'}.
