## Overview
Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Himyari, a Basran jurist. Al-Shirazi reports from Muhammad ibn Sirin that he was the most learned jurist of Basra ten years before he died. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.47}

An additional source is needed: the data does not give the year of his death.
