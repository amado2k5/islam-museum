## Overview
Abu'l-Makaram Hibatallah b. Wazir b. Muqallid, katib and poet of Egypt; it reached the qadi al-Sa'id that he had lampooned him — he brought him in, chastised and reviled him, so Najm al-Mulk 'Ali b. Mifraj al-Ma'arri, known as Ibn al-Munajjim, wrote him interceding for the man. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
