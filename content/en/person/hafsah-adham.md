# Hafsah Adham

## Summary
Hafsah Adham is a British certified spiritual confidence coach and public speaker, and the founder of the Awakened Hearts Academy and The Confident Muslimah, through which she coaches Muslims — largely women — in self-awareness, purpose and spiritual confidence. She is based in the UK with her three children. {cite:awakenedheartsacademy-hafsah-adham-about}

## Background
She comes from an entrepreneurial family and was born and raised in the UK. After her degree she travelled and settled in the Far East for eight years, raising her young family and taking part in charity projects, networking events and community initiatives, before returning to the UK. {cite:humansoffuzia-hafsah-adham}

## Work
Her coaching is rooted in Islamic principles and blends them with coaching frameworks and psychology. She holds a diploma in transformational coaching accredited by the International Coaching Federation, along with training in leadership, positive psychology and confidence. She is a masterclass instructor with The Productive Muslim Co, a lead coach at Muslim Mastery, runs one-to-one and group programmes including The Travellers, and is a volunteer lecturer at a revert centre; alongside her coaching she continues her own studies in the Islamic sciences under various teachers. {cite:awakenedheartsacademy-hafsah-adham-about} {cite:humansoffuzia-hafsah-adham}
