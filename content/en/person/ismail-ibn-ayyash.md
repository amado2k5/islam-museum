## Overview
Isma'il ibn Ayyash al-Ansi al-Himsi, a hadith scholar of the Levant in the second century AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.213}

## A ruling on Cyprus
Al-Baladhuri says that Abd al-Malik ibn Salih wrote to him asking for a legal opinion on the people of Cyprus. He replied that they were humbled and subdued, whom the Romans overpowered in their persons and their women; that it was incumbent on the Muslims to protect and defend them, and to maintain them in their covenant and protection. He cited the letter of Habib ibn Maslama to the people of Tiflis, and the fact that al-Walid ibn Yazid had exiled them to Syria, which the Muslims found repugnant and the jurists found grave, so Yazid ibn al-Walid returned them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.213}
