## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Isma'il ibn Sa'd ibn Isma'il al-Khashshab al-Shafi'i al-Misri, an Egyptian Shafi'i and a friend of the historian Abd al-Rahman al-Jabarti. He died in 1230 AH (1815 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
