Abu Bishr al-Bara' ibn Ma'rur ibn Sakhr al-Salami al-Khazraji, chief of Banu Salama and elder of the Ansar delegation at the [[event/pledges-of-aqaba|second Aqaba pledge]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Al-Bara' was the people's speaker and spokesman before the Prophet and his uncle [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] on the night of al-Aqaba; he took the Prophet's hand and said: "yes — by Him who sent you a prophet with the truth — we will shield you from what we shield our own from; pledge to us, Messenger of God, for by God we are men of war and men of the blade, inherited father from father." The Prophet chose him chief over his people Banu Salama. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Al-Bara' died in Medina in Safar, a month before the Prophet arrived as an emigrant; he had willed that his grave face the Ka'ba and that a third of his wealth go to the Messenger of God — and on arriving the Prophet prayed over him and faced his grave as he had willed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
