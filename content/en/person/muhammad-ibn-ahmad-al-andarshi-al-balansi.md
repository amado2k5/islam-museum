## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Andarshi al-Balansi, a jurist and physician. He died in Tunis in 674 AH. He wrote a poem on medicine and a versified work on simple drugs. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.413-414}
