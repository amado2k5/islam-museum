## Overview
Al-Mughira ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi, brother of Uthman ibn Abi al-As. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The estuary of Debal
His brother sent him to the estuary of Debal, where he met the enemy and was victorious. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
