## Overview
Hani ibn Qabisa ibn Mas'ud al-Shaybani, of the [[group/banu-shayban|Banu Shayban]], mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the account of the [[event/conquest-of-al-hira|conquest of al-Hira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The negotiation
Al-Baladhuri reports that he went out with Abd al-Masih ibn Buqayla and Ayas ibn Qabisa as negotiators on behalf of al-Hira to Khalid ibn al-Walid, and they made peace with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
