## Overview
Fatima bint 'Abbas ibn Abi'l-Fath, the shaykha, muftiya, jurist, scholar, ascetic and worshipper Umm Zaynab al-Baghdadiyya al-Hanbaliyya the preacher; the women of Damascus were reformed through her, by her sincere admonition and contentment with little. She knew fiqh well and asked after it, and Shaykh Taqi al-Din marvelled at her knowledge and intelligence and praised her often. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3015}

## Later years
She moved to Egypt after 700 AH, where the women of Cairo benefited from her; she had studied fiqh under the Maqadisa and Shaykh Shams al-Din among others. She died on the night of 'Arafa, 704 AH, aged over eighty. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3015}
