## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Khurasani, Abu Ja'far al-Khazin, was one of the great astronomers of Islam, serving Abu al-Fadl Ibn al-'Amid, vizier of the Buyid Rukn al-Dawla, with his observations; he was versed in mathematics and geometry and died about 400 AH (1010 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.98}

His works include Zij al-Safa'ih — al-Qifti called it the noblest and finest book of its kind — al-Masa'il al-'Adadiyya, and a commentary on Euclid. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.98}
