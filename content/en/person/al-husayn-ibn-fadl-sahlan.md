## Overview
Al-Husayn b. Fadl b. Sahlan, Abu 'Ali of Ramhurmuz, vizier of Sultan al-Dawla; Ibn al-Jawzi records in his tarjama that he built the wall of the Ha'ir at Karbala in 403 — though elsewhere in the book the building is credited to his brother Abu Muhammad al-Hasan b. al-Fadl b. Sahlan — he was killed in Sha'ban 414 at fifty-three. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.355}
