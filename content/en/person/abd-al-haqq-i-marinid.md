## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Mahyu ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hamama ibn Muhammad al-Marini, Abu Muhammad, is called by al-Zirikli the founder of the Marinid state in the far Maghreb. He gives his birth in 542 AH, 1147 CE, and his death in 614 AH, 1217 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}.

## Lineage and people
Al-Zirikli says the Banu Marin are Berbers of the Maghreb from the Zanata tribe; they lived in the southern lands from the Zab of Ifriqiya to Sijilmasa, moving through the deserts under no sultan and paying no tax, occupied with hunting and raiding the edges of the settled lands {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. Leadership among them belonged to the forebears of his subject. One was al-Mukhaddab ibn Askar ibn Muhammad, killed in one of the wars between [[person/abd-al-mumin-ibn-ali|Abd al-Mu'min al-Kumi]] and the [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|Almoravids]] in 540 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. Leadership passed to his cousin Abu Bakr ibn Hamama and then to the latter's son Abu Khalid Mahyu, who was killed in the Zab desert in 592 AH; his son Abd al-Haqq took charge of the tribe after him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}.

## Life
Abd al-Haqq was born in the Zab. In 610 AH he moved his people into the far [[place/maghreb|Maghreb]], seeking abundant water and fertile land, and they held the fortress of Tazuta and the Rif country around it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. The [[polity/almohad-caliphate|Almohads]] of Marrakesh and Fez fought them, and the Marinids prevailed in 613 AH; the amir Abd al-Haqq advanced on the ribat of [[place/taza|Taza]], killed its governor and routed the Almohads and their supporters who were with him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. Some of his men from the Banu Askar then rose against him, went to the Banu Riyah, the strongest Arab tribes of those deserts, and returned with large forces to fight him; he held firm and his men swore to die before him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. The battle was near Wadi Sabu, some miles from Tafartast; the Marinids won but lost their amir, whom they buried outside the village of Tafartast near [[place/meknes|Meknes]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}. Al-Zirikli calls this their first appearance as a body of strength and unity {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 282}.
