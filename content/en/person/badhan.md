## Overview
Badhan: the last Persian governor over Yemen; he embraced Islam in 638 CE, and Persian rule over the land of Yemen ended with his Islam.

## Life
Kisra made Yemen a Persian governorship after the assassination of Ma'dikarib, and Persian governors succeeded one another over it until the last of them, Badhan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Badhan embraced Islam in 638 CE, and with his Islam Persian rule over the land of Yemen ended {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Badhan was Yemen's last Persian governor; he entered Islam, and sixty years of Persian rule ended with him.
