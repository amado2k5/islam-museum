## Summary
Badhan was the last of the Persian governors of [[place/yemen|Yemen]]. He embraced Islam in 638 CE, in the chronology of The Sealed Nectar, and with his Islam Persian authority over the land of Yemen came to an end {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 25} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 4/195-198}.

## Persian rule
After the assassination of [[person/sayf-ibn-dhi-yazan|Ma'dikarib ibn Sayf Dhi Yazan]], Kisra appointed a Persian governor over [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]] and made Yemen a Persian province. Persian governors continued to succeed one another over Yemen until the last of them, Badhan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 25}. See [[event/persian-rule-of-yemen|Persian rule over Yemen]].

## Notes for researchers
The year 638 CE is as the author gives it. Biographical works mention Badhan's Islam during the Prophet's lifetime, after the letter to Kisra, so the date needs checking before it is relied on.
