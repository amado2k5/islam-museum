## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam lists Mir Damad (died 1630), also known as Mir Muhammad Baqir ibn Shams al-Din Muhammad Astarabadi, as a Safavid-era philosopher and theologian from Persia and one of the main figures of the School of Isfahan. It singles out his striking blend of Ibn Sina's peripatetic philosophy with the illuminationist philosophy of Hikmat al-Ishraq, and his work to bring Shiism and Sufism together. The Dictionary's death year, 1630, differs from the 1631 on the draft card above; both are reported and neither is settled. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1522}
