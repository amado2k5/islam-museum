## Overview
Salama ibn Aslam ibn Harish al-Khazraji al-Ansari, Abu Sa'd, was a Companion and one of the valiant, present at Badr, Uhud, the Trench and all the battles. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.112}

He went out in the army of Usama ibn Zayd against the Byzantines to avenge those slain at Mu'ta, the campaign that led to the conquest of Syria, and was martyred at the battle of the Bridge of Abu 'Ubayd in 14 AH (635 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.112}
