## Overview
Abu al-Muthanna Ahmad ibn Ya'qub, a judge. Ibn al-Mu'tazz appointed him judge when he was given the caliphate in 296 AH, then he fled when the deposers were defeated. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.585-586}
