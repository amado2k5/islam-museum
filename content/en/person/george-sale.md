## Overview
The orientalist and English translator of the meanings of the Qur'an. Zaqzuq records that the introduction to his English translation — dated by the book to 1736 — asserted the Prophet authored the Qur'an, that this introduction had a long effect on European writing, and that Sale was described as half a Muslim. These are judgements of the book, attributed to it.
