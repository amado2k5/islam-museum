## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy mentions Qarun in several places in his lessons: as a man who credited his wealth to his own knowledge and forgot God's favour, and as a man who oppressed his people and was not fit to lead them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:17:30}

## Qarun and wealth
The Sheikh says that Qarun claimed his wealth came from his own knowledge when Moses asked him for zakat, and he says that this forgetting of God's favour was the cause of Qarun's ruin. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:17:30} In another lesson he says that Qarun's people told him not to rejoice in arrogance, and that God does not love such rejoicing. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep10 00:11:30} He then recalls Qarun's claim that his wealth came from his knowledge, and says a person should not rejoice as he did. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep10 00:13:30}

## Qarun and responsibility
The Sheikh speaks of responsibility and says it is not frowning, raising the voice or belittling people. He cites Qarun as an example of a man who oppressed his people and so was not fit to take charge of them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:07:00}

## The speaker's view: repeating proud words
The Sheikh refers to what the Qur'an records of the claims of Iblis to superiority, of Pharaoh to the kingdom and of Qarun to wealth, and warns against echoing such words. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:22:30} He says such statements are repeated without awareness of what they mean, and recalls Qarun's claim that his wealth came from his knowledge. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:23:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Explaining the maxim that created things are outwardly a lure and inwardly a lesson, Omar Abdelkafy mentions those who wished for Qarun's place when he went out in his finery, and who said after the earth swallowed him that God gives provision abundantly to whom He wills. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:21:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh lists Qarun among the schemers on whom their scheming recoiled, swallowed by the earth with his house. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep09 00:08:30} See: [[concept/al-makr-al-sayyi|evil scheming]].

## From the series Qissa wa 'Ibar
Omar Abdelkafy devotes the seventh episode to Qarun, presenting his story as that of corruption through wealth and the use of a favour to disobey the One who gave it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:02:00} He mentions that the keys of his treasuries weighed down a band of men, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:03:30} and that when asked for the zakat of his wealth he said he had been given it through knowledge of his own; he relates that Qarun turned base metals into gold so that wealth piled up with him (his account; needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:05:30} He pauses at those who wanted this world wishing for the like of what Qarun had, to distinguish envy from ghibta, and at the earth swallowing him and his house, to say that ample provision is no sign of God's pleasure nor scant provision of His anger. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:10:30} He holds that “hoarding Qarunism” joined to “tyrannical Pharaonism” in a country is corruption itself. This is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep07 00:02:30}
