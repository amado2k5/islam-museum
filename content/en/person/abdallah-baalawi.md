## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Ja'far ibn Alawi al-Ba'Alawi: a Sufi born in al-Shihr, who lived in India for about twenty years and then settled in Mecca, where he remained until his death in 1160 AH (1747 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.76}

His works include Kashf Asrar Ulum al-Muqarrabin, a commentary on the diwan of Shaykh ibn Isma'il al-Shihri, and a diwan of poetry and correspondence among other writings. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.76}
