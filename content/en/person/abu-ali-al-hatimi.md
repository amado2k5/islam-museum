## Overview
Abu Ali Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Muzaffar al-Hatimi al-Baghdadi, a philologist and writer. He took literature from Abu Umar al-Zahid, the client of Tha'lab, and a group took from him, among them the judge al-Tanukhi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 137}

He wrote the "Hatimiyya Epistle", explaining what passed between him and al-Mutanabbi about the plagiarisms and the faults of his verse. "Shadharat" gives his death as about 390 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 137}
