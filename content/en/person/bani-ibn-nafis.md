## Overview
A commander of the companions of Mu'nis al-Muzaffar, who was with him in the deposition of al-Muqtadir in 317 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.150}

Ibn al-Athir relates that he went to the tomb of al-Muqtadir's father, dug from a grave in it six hundred thousand dinars, and carried them to the caliph's house. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.150}
