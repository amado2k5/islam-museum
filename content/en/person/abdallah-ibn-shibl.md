## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Shibl al-Antamisi was in charge of the vanguard of al-Walid ibn Uqba in the raid on Azerbaijan in 25 AH, which is part of [[event/conquest-of-azerbaijan|the conquest of Azerbaijan]]. He raided the people of Muqan, al-Babr and al-Taylasan, and took booty and captives. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
