## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Darraj, client of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]]. Al-Baladhuri says that in the days of his governorship he extracted the land tax of Kufa, along with al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, from estates of waste land that had been abandoned, and from breaches of water, marshes and thickets on which he imposed embankments. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.408}

[[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj]] then took possession of them for Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan and settled them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.408}
