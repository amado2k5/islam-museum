## Overview
Abu Hamid al-Barawi al-Tusi, Muhammad b. Muhammad the Shafi'i jurist, pupil of Muhammad b. Yahya and author of the famous Ta'liqa on khilaf; the end-point in kalam, speculation, rhetoric and disputation, brilliant in the Ash'ari madhhab. He came to Baghdad, made trouble against the Hanbalis, stirred fitna and preached at the Nizamiyya — and after his fame he was found dead one morning: it is said the Hanbalis sent him with a woman a poisoned dish of sweets, and it is said al-Barawi had said "were it mine to command I would impose the jizya on the Hanbalis." His tarjama is in the annals of 567. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 704}
