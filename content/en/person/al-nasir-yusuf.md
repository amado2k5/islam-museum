## Overview
Al-Nasir, lord of Damascus. After the fall of Baghdad, Hulagu sent him three letters threatening him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 718-719}

## Hulagu's letters
Al-Suyuti quotes from these letters a phrase asking him to answer "the king of the earth", and forbidding him to say that his fortresses were impregnable and his men were fighters, saying that flight is upon them and pursuit is upon us. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 718-719}
