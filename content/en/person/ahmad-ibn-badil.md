## Overview
Abu Ja'far al-Yami al-Kufi, Ahmad b. Badil; he heard Abu Bakr Ibn 'Ayyash, 'Abdallah b. Idris, Hafs b. Ghiyath, 'Abd b. Fudayl, Waki', Abu Mu'awiya and others — a man of learning and excellence. He held Kufa's judgeship, saying when invested "I have been betrayed in my old age," and also held Hamadhan's; he came to Baghdad and related there. He died in 258. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 101}
