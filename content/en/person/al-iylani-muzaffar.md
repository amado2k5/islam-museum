## Overview
Abu'l-'Izz Muwaffaq al-Din Muzaffar b. Ibrahim b. Jama'a b. 'Ali b. Shami b. Ahmad b. Nahid b. 'Abd al-Razzaq al-'Iylani — the nisba to Qays 'Aylan — Hanbali littérateur, poet and prosodist of Egypt, blind; born with five nights left of Jumada II 544 in Egypt, he heard hadith from Abu'l-Qasim Ibn al-Basti, Ibn al-Sabuni, Abu Tahir al-Silafi, al-Busiri and others, and met many men of letters. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 111-112}

## His verse
He composed fine verse, mastered prosody and wrote a celebrated work in it proving his skill; asked how he could love though blind, he answered with his famous lines, "I love with the limb of hearing, and I have not seen the named one." He died in Egypt at dawn on Saturday in Muharram 623 and was buried at the foot of al-Muqattam. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 111-112}
