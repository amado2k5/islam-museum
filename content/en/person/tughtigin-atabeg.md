## Overview
The Atabeg Zahir al-Din Tughtigin; one of the amirs of the Seljuk Tutush at Damascus — Tutush married him to the mother of his son Duqaq; he then became Duqaq's atabeg, and afterwards king of Damascus. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 545-546}

## His career
A master, brave, wise, managing and ruling well, with celebrated stands against the Franks; he died in Safar 522 and was buried in his mausoleum before the musalla, and his son Taj al-Muluk Buri ruled after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 545-546}
