## Summary
Abbas Shakir al-Baghdadi was a master of classical Islamic calligraphy, born in Baghdad in 1951, whose thuluth and diwani works were prized for their balance and clarity across Iraq and the Arab world. {cite:muslim500-abbas-shakir-al-baghdadi}

## Career
Trained in the Baghdad school of Ottoman-Arabic calligraphy, he taught generations of calligraphers, produced illuminated panels and Qur'anic compositions exhibited internationally, and was recognised as one of Iraq's leading custodians of the classical canon. He died on 2 May 2023. {cite:muslim500-abbas-shakir-al-baghdadi}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 records him in its obituaries as a guardian of the Arabic calligraphic tradition. Published sources on him are thin; the museum notes this limited sourcing on the record itself. His name shares only the nisba 'al-Baghdadi' with unrelated public figures — he was an artist, not a political or militant actor. {cite:muslim500-abbas-shakir-al-baghdadi}
