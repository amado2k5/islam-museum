## Summary
Abu Hashim Fadim. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 467 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], directly after Ali al-Jurjani; it lies on page 112 alone. He says Abu Hashim was among the early Baghdadis known for ascetic practice and for the realisation of Sufism. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/112}

## With Sufyan al-Thawri
[[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]] sat with him, praised his way and his steady adherence to purity and loyalty. Nothing of his speech survives except what al-Thawri related: "I kept showing off without noticing, until I sat with Abu Hashim, and from him I took leaving off showing off." {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/112}

## His saying on knowledge that breeds pride
It was told to him that he had seen Sharik ibn Abd Allah the judge coming out of the house of Yahya al-Barmaki, with people bowing before him. He said: I seek refuge in God from knowledge that produces this, and leads its owner to what I see. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/112}

## The Bedouin woman and the blind man
A chain through Muhammad ibn al-Mu'ammil gives Abu Hashim Muhammad ibn Sa'id (Abu Ali), who heard his father relate that he was circling the Kaaba at night when he saw a Bedouin woman leading a blind Bedouin man, who recited verses calling on God: that He is near in the far place, that He hears the voice where the dead cannot hear, that He is the cure of sicknesses; that every union apart from His union is false, and every love apart from His love is sinful. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/112}
