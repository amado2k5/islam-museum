## Summary
Khalid ibn Zayd ibn Kulayb al-Najjari, Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, the Prophet's host in Medina {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Taking the baggage
When the Prophet's camel knelt at the site of today's Prophet's Mosque — among Banu al-Najjar, his maternal kin — Abu Ayyub was first to his saddle and carried it into his house, the Prophet saying: "a man stays with his baggage" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 173}.

In Anas's account in Bukhari, the Prophet asked: which of our people's houses is nearest? Abu Ayyub said: mine, Messenger of God — this is my house and this is my door. He said: go then, prepare us a resting-place {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Afterward
The Prophet lodged with him until the mosque and its apartments were built; Abu Ayyub was killed at Constantinople's walls and is famously buried there {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.
