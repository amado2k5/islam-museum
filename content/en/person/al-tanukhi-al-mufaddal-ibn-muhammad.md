## Overview
Abu al-Mahasin al-Mufaddal ibn Muhammad ibn Mash'ar al-Tanukhi al-Ma'arri, from the people of Ma'arra in Syria. He came to Baghdad in 390 AH and took jurisprudence from Abu al-Husayn, and died in 442 AH (1050 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The data also give his birth as 390 AH, the same year he came to Baghdad; this date needs an additional source.
