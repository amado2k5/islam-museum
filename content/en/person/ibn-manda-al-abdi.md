## Summary
The hafiz Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Manda al-'Abdi, the famous hafiz and author of Tarikh Isbahan; one of the trustworthy hafizes of a great house from which many scholars came — the mother of this hafiz and her mother Barra bint Muhammad were of the Banu 'Abdallah, so he was named for his maternal uncles. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
