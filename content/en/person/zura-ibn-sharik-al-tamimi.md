## Overview
Zur'a ibn Sharik al-Tamimi. al-Mas'udi reports that on the day of al-Taff he struck al-Husayn's left palm before Sinan ibn Anas stabbed him and cut off his head {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.60}.
