## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Wathiq, an Abbasid prince. Al-Mas'udi relates that when the caliphate was offered to al-Mu'tazz after the deposition of al-Muhtadi, Muhammad ibn al-Wathiq embraced him in welcome, and they sat together on the couch. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 juz' 4 p. 143}
