## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Kufi, known as Ibn A'tham, a historian and author of the book *al-Futuh* (The Conquests). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He arranged in his book the reports of Islamic history beginning with the death of the Prophet and the Saqifa of the Banu Sa'ida, and mentioned its men. His death is dated, on the basis of report, to 314 AH (926 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
