## Overview
Lutfallah ibn Abd al-Karim ibn Ibrahim ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Ali al-Maysi was an Imami jurist from the people of Mays in Jabal Amil {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 242}. Al-Zirikli describes him as Imami; this is a non-Sunni school, named here as a label and not as a judgement {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 242}. He died in Isfahan; al-Zirikli gives his death as 1032 AH, 1623 CE, and prints no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 242}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists al-I'tikafiyya, in manuscript, on jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 242}.
