## Overview
Qays ibn al-Husayn, Dhu al-Ghussa, of the delegation of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] that came with Khalid ibn al-Walid in 10 AH. The Prophet appointed him over his people. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The title
The editors of Ibn Hisham say that he was called "Dhu al-Ghussa" because whenever he spoke, he suffered something like choking. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The delegation
He became Muslim with Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b at the hand of Khalid in 10 AH, and came in their delegation. See [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|Khalid's mission to Banu al-Harith]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
