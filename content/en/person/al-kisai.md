## Overview
Ali ibn Hamza al-Kisa'i, the shaykh of the Qur'an readers and grammarians of Kufa, and the teacher of al-Hadi, al-Amin and al-Rashid. He was buried in al-Rayy. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 331}

Al-Mas'udi says he died in 189 AH after travelling with al-Rashid to al-Rayy. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 331}

Ibn al-Nadim says he was one of the seven readers of Kufa, of foreign origin, and that he moved between the lands. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 76}

Ibn al-Nadim relates the story of his debate with Sibawayh, in which they referred to eloquent Bedouins and the correct answer was with al-Kisa'i. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 143}
