## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Hakam al-Ghazal, an Andalusian poet. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.259}

## The embassy
The amir sent him as an envoy to the lands of the Magians when he was nearing fifty. The queen of the king asked him his age, and he said jokingly: "Twenty." He gave her an answer that pleased her. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.259}

He was also sent to the king of the Romans, who liked his conversation, and he recited verses on the beauty of the queen. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.259}

## The exile
Al-Maqqari says he wrote a bitter satire against [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]], and that Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Hakam ordered him exiled, so he went to Iraq. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.262}

He lived ninety-four years and died around the year 250 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.262}
