## Summary
Ubayd Allah ibn Adi ibn al-Khiyar ibn Adi ibn Nawfal, al-Nawfali al-Qurashi al-Madani. He was born in the Prophet's lifetime, and his father was among the freed Meccans. He narrated from Umar, Ali, Uthman and others; Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Ata ibn Yazid narrated from him. He was reliable, a man of few reports, and one of the jurists and scholars of Quraysh. The printed text gives no death date. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13362}
