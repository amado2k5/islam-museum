## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Fadl al-Tabarsi, Amin al-Din, Abu Ali, was an Imami (Twelver Shi'i) scholar. Al-Zirikli calls him a meticulous exegete and linguist and one of the eminent Imamis, and says his nisba is to Tabaristan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 145}. He died in Sabzevar in 548 AH (1153 CE) and was carried to the shrine city of Mashhad of al-Ridha {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 145}. The entry gives no year of birth.

## His works
His books include "Majma' al-Bayan fi Tafsir al-Qur'an wa-al-Furqan", printed in two volumes; "Jawami' al-Jami'", also on exegesis and printed; "Taj al-Mawalid"; "Ghunyat al-'Abid"; "Mukhtasar al-Kashshaf"; and "I'lam al-Wara bi-A'lam al-Huda", printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 145}.
