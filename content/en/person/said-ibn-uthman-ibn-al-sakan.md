## Summary
Sa'id ibn Uthman ibn al-Sakan, Abu Ali al-Baghdadi, who settled in Egypt and died there; a leading memoriser and compiler of hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2048}

## Works
His work includes "al-Sahih al-Muntaqa" on hadith. He died in 353 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2048}
