## Overview
Ruzbihan ibn Abi al-Nasr al-Baqli al-Fasawi al-Shirazi, a Sufi scholar from Shiraz, titled Sadr al-Din, according to "Mawsu'at a'lam al-ulama wa al-udaba" {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Standing
The encyclopedia reports that he had a share in several sciences, and that he is among the great authors of Persian Sufism {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
He died in 606 AH (1209 CE), according to the encyclopedia {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
