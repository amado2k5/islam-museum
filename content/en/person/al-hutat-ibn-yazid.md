## Overview
One of the notables of the [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]] who came in their [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|delegation]] to the Prophet in 9 AH, from the [[group/banu-darim|Banu Darim]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207}

## His name
Ibn Ishaq calls him "al-Hubhab ibn Yazid", and Ibn Hisham says he is al-Hutat. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} The editors note that "al-Hubhab" is so given in the Isaba and in the Usul further on, and that two copies make him "Zayd". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206}

## Brotherhood with Mu'awiya
Ibn Hisham relates that the Prophet made brothers among some of his Companions of the Muhajirun: between [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], between [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] and [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], between [[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha ibn Ubayd Allah]] and [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]], between [[person/abu-dharr-al-ghifari|Abu Dharr al-Ghifari]] and [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr al-Bahrani]], and between [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] and al-Hutat ibn Yazid al-Mujashi'i. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} When al-Hutat died in the caliphate of Mu'awiya, Mu'awiya took what he left as an inheritance by that brotherhood. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} [[person/farazdaq|Al-Farazdaq]] then said to Mu'awiya two verses of his rebuking him: "Your father and my uncle left an inheritance that its kin possess, so why was the inheritance of al-Hutat eaten, while the inheritance of Harb is firmly yours and melts?" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206}

## Notes
This brotherhood between Muhajirun is distinct from [[event/brotherhood-of-muhajirun-and-ansar|the well-known brotherhood between the Muhajirun and the Ansar]]; Ibn Hisham does not give its date here.
