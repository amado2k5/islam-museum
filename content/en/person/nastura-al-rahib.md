## Overview
Nastura the monk saw the Prophet on Khadija's trading journey to Syria with her servant Maysara, according to al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.569-570}.

## The report
The monk was in his hermitage when he saw a cloud shading the Prophet. He said: "This is a prophet, and this is the last of the prophets." This happened between the Prophet's presence in the fourth war of al-Fijar and the building of the Ka'ba, as al-Mas'udi mentions {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.569-570}.
