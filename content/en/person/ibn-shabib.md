## Overview
Isma'il ibn Umar ibn Nu'ma, Abu al-Tahir al-'Attar, known as Ibn Shabib, was an Egyptian littérateur of Byzantine origin, skilled in the knowledge of simples (materia medica). He died at Cairo in 606 AH (1209 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.319}

His literary works include Mi'a Jariya wa-Mi'a Ghulam. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.319}
