## Summary
The sixth of the Weepers, brother of Banu Waqif; Ibn Ishaq names him among those who asked the Prophet for mounts on the Tabuk expedition when he found nothing to carry them on. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him sixth of the Weepers, brother of Banu Waqif, among the seven men who came asking the Prophet for mounts — he said he found nothing to carry them on, and they turned back with eyes overflowing with tears. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: brother of Banu Waqif. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}
