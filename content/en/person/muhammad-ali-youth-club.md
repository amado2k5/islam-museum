## Overview
Muhammad Ali is a Pakistani teacher, trainer, educator and motivational speaker associated with Youth Club, the Lahore-based youth dawah organisation. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel} {cite:listennotes-muhammad-ali-podcast}

He is not the boxer Muhammad Ali, the engineer and debater Muhammad Ali Mirza, or Muhammed Ali of The Muslim Lantern channel; those are separate people with their own records in this museum. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel}

His YouTube channel had about 1.14 million subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel}

No source consulted gives a birth date or birthplace for him. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel} {cite:feedspot-islamic-youtubers}

## Career and work
He holds a B.Sc in Mechanical Engineering from the GIK Institute, and his own biography gives a Masters in Applied Psychology and a Masters in Islamic Sciences in progress at IOU, the International Open University. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel} {cite:listennotes-muhammad-ali-podcast}

The Feedspot directory's version of his biography instead describes a Post Graduate Diploma in Applied Psychology; the two wordings conflict and the museum records the conflict rather than choosing between them. {cite:feedspot-islamic-youtubers} {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel}

His stated mission is to inspire positive change in society and help people live a meaningful life. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel}

On his channel he publishes Urdu reminders and lectures for young people, including his long-running "MA Podcast" series, and his talks also appear as an audio podcast on Youth Club's "AmrBilMarof" feed, with 160 episodes listed since June 2024. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel} {cite:listennotes-muhammad-ali-podcast}

His channel's video descriptions link to Youth Club's own pages, including its Lahore chapter. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ali-channel}

A channel directory listed him at about 1.1 million YouTube subscribers and about 930,000 Instagram followers. {cite:feedspot-islamic-youtubers}
