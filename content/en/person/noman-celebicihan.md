# Noman Çelebicihan

Noman Çelebicihan (1885 – 23 February 1918) was a Crimean Tatar politician, writer and poet, the first head of government of the Crimean People's Republic. He was born in 1885 at Büyük Sonaq village near Dzhankoy in Crimea; the Russian and Ukrainian accounts record that his father, İbrahim Çelebi, was the imam of the village mosque. He was educated at village and madrasa schools in Crimea, then from 1908 in Istanbul, where he studied at the Vefa high school and read law and theology at Istanbul University.{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-ru}{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-uk}

In Istanbul he co-founded the Vatan society in 1909 and helped organise the Young Tatar writers, returned to Crimea in 1912, and went on to study law in St Petersburg in 1913. After the February Revolution he was elected, in March 1917, chairman of the Provisional Crimean Muslim Executive Committee and temporary mufti of the Muslims of Crimea, Lithuania, Poland and Belarus; the Ukrainian account notes reform calls he issued in that office, including on women's dress and joint worship.{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-ru}{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-uk}

He organised the first Crimean Tatar Qurultay, and on 13 December 1917 the Directory of the newly proclaimed Crimean People's Republic elected him its chairman and head of its justice administration. His best-known poem, "Ant etkenmen" ("I Swear"), became the Crimean Tatar national anthem.{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-en}{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-uk}

When the Bolsheviks took Crimea in January 1918 he was arrested, held in the Sevastopol city prison, and on 23 February 1918 shot by Black Sea Fleet sailors; his body was thrown into the Black Sea.{cite:wikipedia-noman-celebicihan-en}{cite:wikidata-noman-celebicihan}
