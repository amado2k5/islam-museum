## Summary
Umar ibn Muhammad al-Laythi al-Baghdadi, Abu al-Faraj, a Maliki jurist who studied fiqh under the qadi Isma'il and other Malikis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16243} Abu Bakr al-Abhari, Abu Ali ibn al-Sakan, Abu al-Qasim al-Shafi'i, Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Bandar and Umar ibn al-Mu'ammal al-Tarsusi transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16243} He held the judgeship of Tarsus, Antioch, al-Massisa and other towns. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16243} His works include al-Hawi on the madhhab of Malik and al-Luma' on usul al-fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16243} The encyclopedia dates his death to 331 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16243}
