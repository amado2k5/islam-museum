## Overview
Jabbar ibn Salma ibn Malik ibn Ja'far al-Amiri, of the Banu Amir ([[group/banu-amir-ibn-sasaa|Banu Amir ibn Sa'sa'a]]). Ibn Ishaq names him as one of the three chiefs of the delegation of the Banu Amir to the Prophet in 9 AH, together with [[person/amir-ibn-al-tufayl|Amir ibn al-Tufayl]] and [[person/arbad-ibn-qays|Arbad ibn Qays]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The book does not give him a role in [[event/assassination-attempt-of-amir-and-arbad|the plot of Amir and Arbad]], nor any report after it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
