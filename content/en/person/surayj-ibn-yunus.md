## Overview
Surayj ibn Yunus ibn Ibrahim, the imam, exemplar and hafiz Abu al-Harith al-Marwazi then of Baghdad; he narrated from Isma'il ibn Ja'far, Hushaym ibn Bashir, 'Abbad ibn 'Abbad, Yusuf ibn al-Majishun and their peers, while Muslim, al-Bukhari through intermediaries, al-Nasa'i, Baqi ibn Makhlad, Abu Zur'a, Abu'l-Qasim al-Baghawi and many others narrated from him. Ahmad ibn Hanbal called him "a man of good," and Salih Jazara "most trustworthy, a worshipper." {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1771}

## Standing
Al-Dhahabi says Surayj was one of the worshipful imams, a man of spiritual states and a head of the Sunna. Al-Bukhari dates his death to Rabi' I, 235 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1771}
