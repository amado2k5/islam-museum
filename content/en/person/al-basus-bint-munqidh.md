## Overview
Al-Basus bint Munqidh of the [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]], the maternal aunt of Jassas ibn Murra. Sa'd ibn Shumays al-Jirmi stayed with her, and his she-camel Sarab grazed with the camels of Jassas. When Kulayb ibn Rabi'a struck her udder, al-Basus heard the cry of her neighbour, placed her hand on her head and cried "Oh, my humiliation!" That was the spark of the War of al-Basus. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 391}

Her name became a proverb: "More ill-omened than al-Basus" and "More ill-omened than Sarab". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 391}
