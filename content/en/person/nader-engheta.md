# Nader Engheta

Nader Engheta (born 8 October 1955) is an Iranian-American scientist and electrical engineer at the University of Pennsylvania, a pioneer of metamaterials and nano-optics. The Persian article gives his birth on 16 Mehr 1334, the same day as the English article's 8 October 1955. No Arabic edition of his article exists; this file's companion Arabic text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-engheta-en} {cite:wikipedia-engheta-fa} {cite:wikidata-engheta}

## Early life and education

Born in Tehran, he took his bachelor's degree at the University of Tehran and moved to the United States for graduate study, completing his doctorate at the California Institute of Technology in 1982 under Charles H. Papas, with a dissertation on the radiation patterns of interfacial antennas. {cite:wikipedia-engheta-en} {cite:wikipedia-engheta-fa}

## Career and research

Engheta is the H. Nedwill Ramsey Professor at the University of Pennsylvania. He founded much of the modern field of metamaterials and optical nanocircuits, and his work spans plasmonic optics, nanophotonics, graphene photonics, nano-antennas and bio-inspired imaging of polarisation vision. His honours include the Guggenheim Fellowship (1999), the IEEE Electromagnetics Award (2012), the SPIE Gold Medal (2015), the Isaac Newton Medal (2020) and the Franklin Medal (2023). {cite:wikipedia-engheta-en}
