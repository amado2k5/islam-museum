## Summary
Muhibb Allah ibn Abd al-Shakur, of Bihar in India, a Hanafi jurist and usuli who was a verifier. The emperor Aurangzeb appointed him judge of Lucknow and then of Hyderabad, and later chief of the Indian realms. His work Musallam al-thubut is on usul al-fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1642}
