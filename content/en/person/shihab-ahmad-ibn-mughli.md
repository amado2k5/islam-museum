## Shihab al-Din Ahmad Ibn Muflih

Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muflih al-Ramini, later of Damascus and Salihiyya, a Hanbali, brother of Shaykh Taqi al-Din Ibn Muflih. He was born in 754 AH and died in 814 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 114}.
