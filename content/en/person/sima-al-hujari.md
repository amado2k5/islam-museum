## Overview
Sima, leader of the Harjiyya. He advised [[person/al-radi-billah|al-Radi]] to blind [[person/al-qahir-billah|al-Qahir]] when he refused to abdicate. He blinded him with a heated nail, until his eyes ran down his cheeks {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.599}.
