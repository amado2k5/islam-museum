# Mustafa Benhamza

**Mustafa Benhamza** is a Moroccan Muslim scholar and jurist, born at Oujda on 21 Ramadan 1368 (17 July 1949). He studied under the jurist Bensaid Mahdawi al-Washani, took a licence in Sharia from the Faculty of Sharia at Fez and another in Arabic literature from the Faculty of Letters at Fez, graduated from Dar al-Hadith al-Hassania in Rabat, and earned a postgraduate diploma from Mohammed V University on Ibn Mada al-Qurtubi's theory of the grammatical operator, and a state doctorate from Mohammed I University at Oujda for his edition of Ibn Antara's manuscript on Arabic verb morphology. He taught at Mohammed V University and then as a professor of Islamic studies at Mohammed I University {cite:wikipedia-mustafa-benhamza-ar} {cite:wikidata-mustafa-benhamza}.

Before Morocco's official fatwa body was created, he became known for his contemporary fatwas, given in a mufti's slot on Moroccan television's first channel and on IslamOnline. He sat on the scientific committee that studied the reform of the Moroccan family code (the Mudawwana), where he was among the strongest opponents of changes that did not respect the Sharia controls on family law, and he answered his critics in a long press interview in the summer of 2003 after the king approved the compromise text {cite:wikipedia-mustafa-benhamza-ar}.

He presided over the local scientific council of Oujda and, in the 2023 restructuring, over the regional scientific council for the Oriental region; he is a member of the Supreme Scientific Council, directs the Baath Islamic Institute for Sharia sciences at Oujda, and is a member of the senior council of the Mohammed VI Foundation of African Scholars. He delivered three Ramadan Hassania lectures before King Mohammed VI, in 1421, 1423 and 1427 AH, and received royal decorations, the last in 2007 {cite:wikipedia-mustafa-benhamza-ar}.

His books include works on the rights of the disabled in Islam (1993), on cultural obstacles to applying the Sharia (2001), an analytical study of Arabic grammar theory (2004), on the preacher and the state of the umma (2001), on the dignity of women in their legislative particularities (2002), and a study of the character of Maliki jurisprudence (2004) {cite:wikipedia-mustafa-benhamza-ar}.
