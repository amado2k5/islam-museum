## Summary
Mada' ibn Isa al-Shami is one of the ascetics treated by [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] in the ninth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]] (notice 454). He describes him as a man of practice, drawn by love and seized by fear. All his sayings in the notice are related by [[person/ahmad-ibn-abi-al-hawari|Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari]], who heard them from him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 9/324}

## Sayings
He said: fear God and He will inspire you; work for Him and He will not drive you to anyone base. He said that the day's work is drawn out by the night, and the night's work by the day. He and Abu Safwan said that whoever loves a man for God's sake and then falls short in what he owes him is lying in his love, and that when God wills good for a young man He gives him a righteous man as a companion. He related from Hudhayfa al-Mar'ashi that hearts are of two kinds: a heart that presses its request, and a heart that waits for what will come to it. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 9/324-325}

## With Abu Sulayman al-Darani
Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari tells that he visited him together with [[person/abu-sulayman-al-darani|Abu Sulayman]]. Mada' brought them eggs; he and Abu Sulayman were fasting, and Ahmad had meant to fast, but Mada' told him to eat and he ate. [[person/al-qasim-ibn-uthman-al-jui|Al-Qasim ibn Uthman]] reports that Mada' and a group of ascetics agreed that giving up one mouthful is better than standing a night in prayer. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 9/325}

## Transmission
Abu Nu'aym transmits one hadith through him, which Mada' related in [[place/kufa|Kufa]] from Shu'ba with his chain to Ibn Mas'ud, promising Paradise to whoever controls his tongue and his belly. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 9/325}
