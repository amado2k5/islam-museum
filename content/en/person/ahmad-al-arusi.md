## Overview
Ahmad ibn Musa ibn Dawud al-Arusi, Shihab al-Din, was, in al-Zirikli's words, an accomplished Egyptian scholar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 262}. Al-Zirikli prints his death as 1208 AH, 1793 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 262}.

## Life
He was born at Minyat Arus, an outlying place of al-Minufiyya in Egypt, and was educated at al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 262}. Al-Zirikli's entry does not mention a headship of al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 262}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books a commentary on a versification of al-Tanwir on the omission of planning, and a gloss on al-Malawi's work on al-Samarqandiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 262}.
