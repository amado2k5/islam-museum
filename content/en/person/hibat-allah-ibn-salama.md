## Summary
Hibat Allah b. Salama b. Nasr, Abu'l-Qasim al-Baghdadi, the blind exegete; among the foremost memorisers of Qur'an exegesis, with a famous circle in the Mansur mosque; he authored the renowned book of *al-Nasikh wa'l-mansukh*. He heard hadith from Abu Bakr Ahmad b. Ja'far b. Malik al-Qati'i and others, and died at Baghdad in Rajab 410. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.295}
