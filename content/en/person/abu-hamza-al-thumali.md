## Overview
Thabit ibn Dinar al-Thumali al-Azdi by clientage, Abu Hamza: a transmitter counted trustworthy by the Imami school and cited by some Sunni scholars as well; he was of Kufa and died in 150 AH (767 CE). Three of his sons were killed alongside Zayd ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn, and al-Rida Ali ibn Musa used to say of him that he was the Luqman of his age; his father had been a client of al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.97}

He left a book on Qur'an commentary, a book of asceticism (al-Zuhd), and a book of rare reports (al-Nawadir). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.97}
