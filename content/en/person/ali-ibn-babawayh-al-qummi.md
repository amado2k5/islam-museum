## Overview
Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Musa ibn Babawayh, Abu al-Hasan al-Qummi, is described by al-Zirikli as the shaykh of the Imamis in Qum in his time. The Imami (Twelver Shia) scholarly tradition is not the museum's main frame, so that description is attributed to al-Zirikli. He was born and died in Qum; al-Zirikli gives his death as 329 AH, 941 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 277}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists books on divine unity, on the imamate and on Qur'anic exegesis, and a treatise on the laws, al-Shara'i', in manuscript; he adds that there are others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 277}.
