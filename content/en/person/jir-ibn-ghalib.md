## Overview
Jir ibn Ghalib, Abu Firas, one of the early jurists of the Shurat (Kharijites); he was a jurist, poet, preacher and master of eloquence, author of al-Sunan wa'l-Ahkam, Ahkam al-Qur'an, the Mukhtasar in fiqh and al-Jami'. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 278}
