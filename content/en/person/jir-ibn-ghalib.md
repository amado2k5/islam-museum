## Overview
Jir ibn Ghalib, known by the kunya Abu Firas, one of the early jurists of the Shurat. He was a jurist, a poet, a preacher and an eloquent speaker. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.330}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim lists for him the *Book of the Sunan and the Rulings*, the *Book of the Rulings of the Qur'an*, the *Book of the Abridgement in Jurisprudence*, the *Great Compendium in Jurisprudence*, and his letter to Malik ibn Anas. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.330}
