## Overview
Ishaq ibn Yasar, father of Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Yasar Abu Bakr al-Makhrami of Medina, one of the great imams {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p.468}.

## Lineage
Al-Dhahabi's Mizan al-I'tidal gives the lineage of Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Yasar, and says that Yasar was among the captives of Ayn al-Tamr and a client of Qays ibn Makhrama ibn Abd al-Muttalib ibn Abd Manaf {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p.468}. The page thus links Ishaq to Yasar in the line of descent. It gives no separate biography of Ishaq himself.
