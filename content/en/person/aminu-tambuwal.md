# Aminu Tambuwal

Aminu Waziri Tambuwal is a Nigerian lawyer and politician, born on 10 January 1966 in Tambuwal town, Sokoto State. He studied law at Usmanu Danfodiyo University in Sokoto, then attended the Nigerian Law School in Lagos and was called to the Bar in 1992 {cite:wikipedia-aminu-tambuwal-en} {cite:wikidata-aminu-tambuwal}.

## The House of Representatives and the Speakership

He represented the Tambuwal/Kebbe constituency in the House of Representatives from 2003 to 2015, serving as Deputy Chief Whip (2007–2011) and then as the twelfth Speaker of the House (2011–2015). His party allegiance moved repeatedly: from the Peoples Democratic Party to the Democratic People's Party and back, then to the ruling All Progressives Congress in 2014, and back to the PDP in 2018 {cite:wikipedia-aminu-tambuwal-en}.

## Governor of Sokoto, then senator

He governed Sokoto State from 29 May 2015 to 29 May 2023, and contested his party's presidential primaries more than once; in 2018 he was among the top four candidates in the PDP race. Since 13 June 2023 he has sat in the Senate for Sokoto South {cite:wikipedia-aminu-tambuwal-en}.
