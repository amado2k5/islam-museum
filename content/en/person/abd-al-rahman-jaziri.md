## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad Awad al-Jaziri was a jurist among the scholars of al-Azhar, born on the island of Shandawil in the district of Sohag in Egypt, and he died at Helwan. The entry gives his birth as 1299 AH, equal to 1882 CE, and his death as 1360 AH, equal to 1941 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Career
He studied at al-Azhar between 1313 and 1326 AH and taught there; he was appointed inspector of the mosques section in the Ministry of Endowments in 1330 AH, then chief of the inspectors, then professor in the Faculty of Usul al-Din, and he was a member of the Council of Senior Scholars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Fiqh ala al-Madhahib al-Arba'a, printed in four volumes, whose first volume a committee of scholars helped him write and whose remainder he wrote alone; Tawdih al-Aqa'id, printed, on the science of tawhid; al-Akhlaq al-Diniyya wa al-Hikam al-Shar'iyya, of which the first part is printed; Adillat al-Yaqin, printed, in reply to some missionaries; and a Diwan Khutab, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Death
He died at Helwan, in 1360 AH, equal to 1941 CE, as the entry gives it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
