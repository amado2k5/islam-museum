## Summary
Arfajah al-Kufi, known among the devout and among the worshippers, who kept vigil in prayer through the night. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 482 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 135. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135}

## His visit to his brothers and his mother's dream
A young man of Kufa who was devoted in worship, called Arfajah, kept the night in prayer. One night some of his brothers invited him to visit them, and he asked his mother's leave, and she gave it. The old woman said: when it was night and I was asleep, men stood over me and said: mother of Arfajah, why did you give permission to our imam tonight? {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135}
