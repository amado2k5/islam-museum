## Overview
Underrated Hijabi is a British content creator who publishes on YouTube under her channel's name; she does not publish a legal name. {cite:youtube-underrated-hijabi}

She became known for preparing and reviewing food from inside her car, including making breakfast, reviewing the sourest candies and making baklava from behind the steering wheel, as documented in her interview with the official YouTube Blog. {cite:youtube-blog-underrated-hijabi}

Her channel had about 2.06 million subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-underrated-hijabi}

## Career and work
She began on TikTok during the quarantine period and moved to YouTube at her followers' urging, despite initial hesitation, as she told the YouTube Blog. {cite:youtube-blog-underrated-hijabi}

Her channel passed one million subscribers in early 2023, and the YouTube Blog marked the milestone with an interview about her beginnings and motivations. {cite:youtube-blog-underrated-hijabi}

Her output later widened from in-car food reviews to large food challenges, such as eating only £1 Iceland meals for a full day and a 10,000-calorie challenge, and to travel videos, including street food in Malaysia and five days in the Sahara desert, alongside a filmed collaboration with chef Jamie Oliver. {cite:youtube-underrated-hijabi}

Her channel's entire description is the line "Alhamdulilah Always". {cite:youtube-underrated-hijabi}
