## Overview
Maysara al-Abdi, a client of Ali ibn Abdallah ibn al-Abbas. He was one of three missionaries who organised the Abbasid call in Iraq, together with [[person/bukayr-ibn-mahan|Bukayr ibn Mahan]] and Abu Salama al-Khallal. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22}
