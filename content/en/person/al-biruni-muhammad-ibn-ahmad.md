## Overview
Abu al-Rayhan Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Biruni, one of the leading scholars of his age in astronomy, mathematics, geography and history. He was born in the outskirts of the city of Khwarazm (Kath). The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 362 AH (973 CE) and his death as 442 AH (1051 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

The date of death comes from one source and needs a second.
