## Overview
Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar, the historian and genealogist of Quraysh, and the author of [[work/nasab-quraysh-zubayr|"Ansab Quraysh"]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi cites him in the lineage of Talha ibn Ubayd Allah, and in the matter that his mother al-Sa'ba was married to Abu Sufyan Sakhr ibn Harb. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.658}
