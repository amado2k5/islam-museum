## Overview
Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar, Abu 'Abdallah al-Qurashi al-Asadi of Mecca, was the memorising imam and genealogist who served as judge of Mecca. He died in Dhu al-Qa'da 257 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.475}

## Transmission and learning
He narrated from Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna, Anas ibn 'Iyad, al-Nadr ibn Shumayl, 'Abdallah ibn Nafi' al-Sa'igh and many others; al-Bukhari, Ibn Abi al-Dunya, Isma'il al-Warraq, the judge al-Mahamili, Yusuf al-Azraq and others narrated from him. Al-Daraqutni accredited him, and al-Khatib described him as trustworthy, learned in genealogy and the reports of the early generations, with a compiled work on the lineage of Quraysh. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.475}
