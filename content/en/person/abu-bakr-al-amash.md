## Summary
Muhammad ibn Sa'id, Abu Bakr, known as al-A'mash, a Hanafi jurist. He studied under Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Iskaf; his son Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah and the jurist Abu Ja'far al-Hindawani studied under him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15400}
