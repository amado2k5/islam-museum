## Overview
Abu Ma'shar Ziyad ibn Kulayb al-Tamimi al-Hanzali, a jurist mentioned in the same class as al-Harith al-Ukli, al-Mughira ibn Miqsam and al-Qa'qa' ibn Yazid {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}.

The editor of the edition says that he died in the year 120 AH most probably. This is from the editor's note, not from the text of the book {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}.
