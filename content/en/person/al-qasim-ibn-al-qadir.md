## Overview
Abu'l-Qasim, son of the caliph al-Qadir bi'llah; he died on the eve of Saturday, the last night of Jumada II 418 — his brother Abu Ja'far (afterwards al-Qa'im) prayed over him, the people walked with his bier from the bridge's head to the Rusafa mausoleum, Abu'l-Hasan b. 'Isa b. al-Muqtadir repeated the prayer over him, and the state's drums fell silent for twenty days. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.376}
