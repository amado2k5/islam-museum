## Overview
Ri'ab al-Shanni. al-Mas'udi counts him among those who called to God and drew their people's attention to His signs in the period of the interval (al-fatra), and counts him among the monotheists who affirmed the Creator and believed in the Resurrection {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.442}.
