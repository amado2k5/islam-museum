# Sabah Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah

Sabah Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah (16 June 1929 – 29 September 2020) was Emir of Kuwait from January 2006 until his death. Before the throne he held the Foreign Ministry for some forty years, from 1963 to 2003, and the premiership from 2003 to 2006, becoming the region's senior diplomat and a mediator in its disputes.{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Early life

He was the fourth son of Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah. The sources disagree about his birthplace: the English article's infobox gives Jahra, while Wikidata gives Kuwait City, and the record notes both. He was educated at the Al Mubarakya School in the 1930s and completed his education under private tutors.{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikidata-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah}

## Foreign minister and prime minister

Appointed foreign minister in 1963, he held the post until 2003 — four decades in which he became the dean of Kuwaiti diplomacy and a standing mediator in Gulf and Arab disputes. In July 2003 his half-brother Emir Jaber appointed him prime minister, taking that office from Crown Prince Saad Al-Abdullah, and he held it until January 2006.{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}

## Accession and reign

Jaber died on 15 January 2006. Crown Prince Saad succeeded him but abdicated within days on health grounds, and after parliament's vote Sabah was sworn in as Emir on 29 January 2006. His fourteen-year reign was marked by the mediation diplomacy he had practised as foreign minister: the United Nations honoured his humanitarian leadership on 9 September 2014, Kuwait hosted donor conferences for Syria under him, and in the Gulf crisis of 2017 he shuttled between the parties as mediator.{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-ar}

## Death and succession

After months of hospitalisation he died on 29 September 2020 at the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota, aged 91. Kuwait declared forty days of mourning, and he was buried on 30 September 2020 at Sulaibikhat Cemetery. He was succeeded by his half-brother, Crown Prince Nawaf Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah.{cite:source/wikipedia-sabah-al-ahmad-al-jaber-al-sabah-en}
