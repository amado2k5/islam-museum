## Summary
Ali ibn Yahya ibn al-Qasim al-Sinhaji, Abu al-Hasan, al-Jaziri, a Maliki jurist who settled in al-Jazira al-Khadra in al-Andalus and became its judge. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15814} He taught fiqh and drew up contracts; his book al-Maqsid al-mahmud fi talkhis al-uqud is on contracts.
