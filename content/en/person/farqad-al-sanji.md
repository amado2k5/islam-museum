## Summary
Farqad al-Sanji was among the worshippers and ascetic Sufis; he took from al-Hasan al-Basri and Ma'ruf al-Karkhi took from him, according to the Fihrist. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 256}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim records in the Fihrist, among the names of the worshippers, ascetics and Sufis, that Ma'ruf al-Karkhi took from Farqad al-Sanji, that Farqad took from al-Hasan al-Basri, and that al-Hasan took from Anas ibn Malik. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 256} He lists him among those who transmitted from al-Hasan ibn Abi'l-Hasan al-Basri together with Muhammad ibn Sirin, Harm ibn Hayyan, Alqama al-Aswad, Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, al-Sha'bi, Malik ibn Dinar, Muhammad ibn Wasi', Ata al-Salami, Malik ibn Anas, Sufyan al-Thawri, al-Awza'i, Thabit al-Bunani, Ibrahim al-Taymi, Sulayman al-Taymi, Ibn al-Sammak, Utba al-Ghulam, Salih al-Murri, Ibrahim ibn Adham, Abd al-Wahid ibn Zayd, Ibn al-Munkadir, Muhammad ibn Habib al-Farisi, al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym, Abu Umama al-Aswad, Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani, Yusuf ibn Asbat, Abu Sulayman al-Darani, Ibn Abi'l-Hawari, Dawud al-Ta'i, Fath al-Mawsili, Shayban al-Ra'i, al-Mu'afa ibn Imran and al-Fudayl ibn Iyad. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 256}
