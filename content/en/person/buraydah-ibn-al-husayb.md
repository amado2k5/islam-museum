## Summary
[[person/buraydah-ibn-al-husayb|Burayda ibn al-Husayb]] al-Aslami {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}.

## His Islam
Ibn al-Sakan said he became Muslim when the Prophet passed him emigrating at al-Ghamim, and stayed in his place until Badr and Uhud had passed, then came; it is also said he became Muslim after the Prophet's return from Badr {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}.

## His campaigns and dwelling
He settled in [[place/basra|Basra]] when it was conquered; in the two Sahihs from him he campaigned with the Prophet on sixteen expeditions {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}. He campaigned in Khurasan in Uthman's time, then moved to Marw and dwelt there until he died in Yazid ibn Mu'awiya's caliphate {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}.

## His name
Abu Ali al-Tusi, Ibn al-Mubarak's companion, said his name was Amir and Burayda a nickname; reports of Burayda are many and his merits famous {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}.

## His death
Ibn Sa'd said he died in 63 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 141}.
