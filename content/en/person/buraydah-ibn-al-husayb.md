## Summary
Buraydah ibn al-Husayb al-Aslami was a Companion of the tribe of Aslam; Ibn Hajar in al-Isaba gives him a long lineage ending in Afsa al-Aslami and records that he settled in [[place/basra|Basra]], then Khurasan, and died at Merv. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141}

## His Islam
Ibn al-Sakan said he became Muslim when the Prophet passed him on the Hijra at al-Ghamim, and stayed in his place until Badr and Uhud had passed, then came; it is also said he became Muslim after the Prophet's return from Badr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141}

## His campaigns
The two Sahihs report from him that he campaigned with the Prophet on sixteen expeditions. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141}

## Basra and Khurasan
He settled in Basra when it was conquered, campaigned in Khurasan in Uthman's time, then moved to Merv and lived there until he died. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141} Al-Baladhuri in Futuh al-Buldan gives his kunya as Abu Abd Allah and names him among those whom [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad]] moved to Khurasan with their families from the two garrison towns in 51 AH, in the [[event/rabi-khurasan-transplant|transplant of the two towns' people to Khurasan]], and says he died at Merv in the days of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His name
Abu Ali al-Tusi, a companion of Ibn al-Mubarak, said his name was Amir and Buraydah a nickname; reports of Buraydah are many and his merits well known. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141}

## His death
Ibn Sa'd said he died in 63 AH, which agrees with al-Baladhuri's placing of his death in Yazid's days. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.141} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## For young visitors
Buraydah was a man of the tribe of Aslam who became Muslim on the road of the Hijra, went out with the Prophet on many campaigns, and later travelled far east to Khurasan, living in the city of Merv until he died.
