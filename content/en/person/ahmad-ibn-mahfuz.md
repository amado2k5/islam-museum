## Overview
Ahmad ibn Mahfuz is a man the narrator names in the account of a crowd of the people of Sana'a who broke the prison there and drove [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] out, after which [[person/abd-al-qahir-ibn-ahmad-ibn-naim|Abd al-Qahir ibn Ahmad ibn Na'im]] took the city. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} The narrator describes him in contemptuous terms; these are his own words and not a judgement of the museum, so they are not repeated here. See [[event/sanaa-rising-ahmad-ibn-mahfuz-288]].
