## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Mahmud ibn al-Mubarak al-Janabadhi — of Janabadh, a village near Nishapur — the Hanbali then of Baghdad; musnid of Iraq and its hafiz in his time, born Thursday 18 Rajab 524 at Baghdad, his first audition in 503; he attached himself to Abu'l-Hasan ibn Bakrus and Ibn Nasir, and Ibn al-Najjar said none of his peers heard more or had finer principles — he transmitted for some sixty years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Works and death
His works include al-Maqsad al-arshad on those who transmitted from Ahmad in two volumes, Tanbih al-labib wa-talqih fahm al-murib on the Kitab's slips, and Talkhis wasf al-asma'; Ibn al-Jawzi, Ibn al-Dabithi, Ibn Nuqta, Ibn al-Najjar, al-Diya' al-Maqdisi, al-Barzali and Ibn Khalil transmitted from him; he died on Saturday night between the two evening prayers, 6 Shawwal 611. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
