## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Qasim is an Alawi of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent with Ali ibn Muhammad, with a party of horse and foot, to raid [[place/afqayn-yemen|Afqayn]] and take its animals. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.193} When some of the troops wished to plunder the spoils the two took the animals out of their hands, and Muhammad ibn al-Qasim caught a man of al-Mahra riding a mount from the spoils, struck him with a whip and took the camel from him; the man appealed to al-Hadi against him, and al-Hadi ordered Muhammad brought so that he might be struck in turn after he had admitted striking, but when the man saw this he pardoned him and gave up his claim. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.193} No link is made here between this man and [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-al-rassi|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Rassi]].
