## Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Dawudi al-Busanji

Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar ibn Muhammad ibn Dawud, Abu al-Hasan al-Dawudi al-Busanji, a jurist and traditionist. He studied law with Abu Bakr al-Qaffal, Abu al-Tayyib al-Su'luki, Abu Hamid al-Isfara'ini and Abu al-Hasan al-Tulaysi, and heard hadith from Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Hamawayh al-Sarakhsi, Abu Muhammad ibn Abi Sarij and Abu Tahir al-Ziyadi and others. Abu al-Waqt, Musafir ibn Muhammad, Aisha bint Abd Allah al-Busanjiyya and Abu al-Mahasin Asad ibn Ziyad al-Malini related from him. Abd Allah ibn Yusuf al-Jurjani said he settled in Busanj to write, teach, issue fatwas and preach until his death, and he had a share of verse and prose. The heading gives death 467 AH and birth 374 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5498}
