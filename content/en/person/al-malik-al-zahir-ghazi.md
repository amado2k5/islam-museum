## Summary
Al-Malik al-Zahir Ghiyath al-Din Ghazi ibn Salah al-Din — Aleppo fell to his lot when his father died in 589 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}

## Details
In the year 589 the sultan Salah al-Din died; the messenger reached Baghdad carrying his war equipment, his horse, a single dinar and thirty-six dirhams — all the money he left — and Egypt settled for his son Imad al-Din Uthman, al-Malik al-Aziz, Damascus for his son al-Malik al-Afdal Nur al-Din Ali, and Aleppo for his son al-Malik al-Zahir Ghiyath al-Din Ghazi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}

Aleppo was thus al-Zahir's share of his father's inheritance among the Ayyubid sons, and he held the city until his death in 613 AH; he had been born in Egypt in 568 AH, as given in Shadharat al-Dhahab. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 55}
