## Summary
Hanan Barakat is a Qur'an and tajweed teacher who runs a YouTube channel under her own name. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}

Her channel is dedicated to women: in its own words it is concerned with memorising the Qur'an and teaching the rules of tajweed in a simplified way. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}

## Career
She presents the channel's lessons herself and links her official Facebook page (hananbarakattajwid) and her Instagram account from her video descriptions. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}

No biographical source for her could be verified, so nothing is recorded here about her life beyond the channel she runs. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}

## Works
Her channel carries a complete Surah al-Baqarah series in the Warsh recitation, presented as for women only. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}

It also carries the morning adhkar and religious talks on various topics, among them reminders on raising children. {cite:youtube-hanan-barakat}
