## Overview
The Hanbali muhaddith of Damascus; he heard much on the authority of his maternal grandfather from Majd b. Abi'l-Nayib, 'A'isha bint al-Muslim al-Harraniyya, al-Mizzi and a company of Ibn 'Abd al-Da'im's pupils. Ibn Hajar says: "I heard much on him; he held unique transmissions and was slow at dictation" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 23}.
