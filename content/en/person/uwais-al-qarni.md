## Overview
Uways ibn 'Amir ibn Jaz' ibn Malik al-Qarni, of Banu Qarn ibn Radman ibn Najiya ibn Murad, was one of the foremost ascetics and worshippers and a chief of the Successors; his origin was Yemen, dwelling in the wastes and sands, and he died in 37 AH (657 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.32}

He lived through the Prophet's lifetime without seeing him; he came as a delegate to 'Umar ibn al-Khattab, then settled at Kufa, was present at the battle of Siffin with 'Ali, and many hold he was killed in it. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.32}
