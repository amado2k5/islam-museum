## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Umayya ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Usayd. His father appointed him over Sistan while he was in Kirman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He made peace with Rutbil the second when he came down at Bust, and then went deep into the country, and Rutbil seized the passes against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The matter ended in a peace for three hundred thousand dirhams and a writ not to raid, after which [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik]] removed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
