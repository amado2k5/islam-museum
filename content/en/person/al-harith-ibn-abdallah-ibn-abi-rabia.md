## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi, known as "al-Qabba'". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sistan
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd Allah ibn Amir appointed him over Sistan in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr. The people let him into Zaranj and fought Ratbil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} He is one of the men of the Umayyad period.
