## Summary
A transmitter named after the Darim branch of Tamim, who narrated from his father, and from whom Hammad ibn Salama narrated. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5142}

## The critics' verdicts
Fifteen reports from the Prophet are attributed to him. Ibn Hajar said their chains are dark, Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy, Ibn Sa'd called him unknown, and al-Bukhari said his name, his report and his hearing from his father are open to doubt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5142}

He is listed here in the appendix of jurists; the verdicts are recorded as given and are not a judgement by the museum.
