## Summary
A Thaqafi Companion famed for cunning; he struck al-Lat's door with the iron mallet on the day of its demolition in year 9 AH, after terrifying al-Ta'if's people with his feigned fall {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}.

## His account
In the mission to demolish al-Lat, al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba took the mallet and told his companions: "by God I will make you laugh at Thaqif" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}. He struck with the mallet, then fell and rolled; al-Ta'if's people shuddered and cried: God keep al-Mughira far — the goddess has slain him! He sprang up saying: God confound you — she is naught but a lowly heap of stones and clay {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}. Then he struck the door and broke it, climbed her highest wall with the men, and al-Lat was demolished and razed to the ground until her foundation was dug up and her jewellery and covering brought out {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}.

## For young visitors
A clever man who pretended to fall to show al-Ta'if's people that an idol neither harms nor helps — then broke its door, and the building was torn down.
