## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Marzuban, Abu al-Qasim al-Baghawi, known as Ibn Qani', was a hadith hafiz and scholar; his origin was Baghshur between Herat and Marw al-Rudh, he was born and died at Baghdad in 317 AH (929 CE), and he was the muhaddith of Iraq in his time. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.119}

He wrote Mu'jam al-Sahaba; al-Ja'diyyat on hadith; and Hikayat Shu'ba 'Amr ibn Murra. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.119}
