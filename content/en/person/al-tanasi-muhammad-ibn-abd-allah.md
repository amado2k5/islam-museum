## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Jalil al-Tanasi al-Tilimsani, a memoriser from Tlemcen, probably born in the city of Madin. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 820 AH (1417 CE) and his death as 899 AH (1494 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His works and teachers need an additional source.
