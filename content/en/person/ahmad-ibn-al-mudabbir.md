## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Mudabbir, appointed by the caliph [[person/al-muhtadi|al-Muhtadi]] over the land-tax of Palestine. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 561-563}

Al-Mas'udi says he had seven boon companions whom he found no pleasure without, each of whom was expert in a kind of learning. He relates his well-known episode with Ibn Duraj al-Tufayli, who entered his session dressed as one of his boon companions and out-did them at chess, backgammon, the lute and the tanbur, until he struck his chamberlain with fifty lead pellets fired from a pellet-bow. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 561-563}

His birth and death are not set out here and need an additional source.
