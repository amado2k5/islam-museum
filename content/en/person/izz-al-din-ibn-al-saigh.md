## Summary
'Izz al-Din Ibn al-Sa'igh, chief qadi of Damascus; he stayed with the qadi Kamal al-Din al-Tiflisi until he ranked among his noblest companions, taught at the Shamiyya, then held the trusteeship of the bayt al-mal and the judgeship of Syria; dismissed in early 677 he showed mettle, gallantry and standing for the right; restored to office in early 680, he was then tried in Rajab 682 — they produced against him a writ of about a hundred thousand dinars until God delivered him; he withdrew to his orchard until he died; when death came he gathered his family, made wudu', led them in prayer and said: say la ilaha illa Allah with me — and kept saying it with them until he died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 654}
