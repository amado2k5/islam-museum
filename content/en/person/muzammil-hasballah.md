## Summary
Muzammil Hasballah is an Indonesian Qur'an reciter (qari), muezzin and architect from Aceh, born in Sigli on 21 September 1993. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id} His murottal recitations, first known from videos of his imamate as a student, are among the most widely shared Indonesian recitation content of the past decade; his YouTube channel counted 4,120,000 subscribers, 608,396,619 views and 1,042 videos as read by vidIQ on 12 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:youtube-muzammilhb} {cite:vidiq-muzammil-hasballah}

## Early life and education
He is the youngest of three siblings, and he learned the Qur'an from childhood in his home town of Sigli, Aceh, under his parents' supervision. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id} He completed his bachelor's degree in architectural engineering at the Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB) in 2015. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id}

## Imamate and recitation
While still a student, he regularly led prayers as imam at the Salman Mosque of ITB in Bandung, and it was there that his name first became known to the public, after recordings of his recitation were uploaded to YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id} Profiles of him also describe him as an imam at the Al Lathif Mosque in Bandung. {cite:muzammil-hasballah-app-profile}

His recordings — of Qur'an chapters, dhikr and shalawat — are released through YouTube, Spotify and other streaming platforms. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id}

## Projects
He founded Ngajilah.id, an education medium whose stated mission is to eradicate Qur'an illiteracy in Indonesia and to empower Qur'an teachers. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id} As an entrepreneur he also founded Millahtour.id, a Hajj and Umrah travel venture. {cite:wikipedia-muzammil-hasballah-id}
