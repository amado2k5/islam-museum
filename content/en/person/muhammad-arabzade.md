## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad, known as Arabzade, was a Hanafi jurist of Rum (the Ottoman lands) who wrote verse and books in Arabic. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 919 AH, 1513 CE, and his death as 969 AH, 1562 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 59}.

## Life
He taught in Bursa and then in Istanbul. The shaykh al-Islam became angry with him, and he was beaten and banished to Bursa for two years; he was then pardoned and restored to teaching {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 59}. He was next appointed judge in Cairo and went to sea; when the ship passed Rhodes some of its passengers drowned, and he was among them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 59}.

## Works
He wrote glosses on several books, among them a gloss on al-Hidaya in law, in manuscript in the library of Ashir, and a gloss on Anwar al-tanzil, in manuscript in Baghdad, of 353 folios {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 59}.
