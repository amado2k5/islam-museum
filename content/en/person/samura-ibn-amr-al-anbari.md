## Overview
Samura ibn Amr al-Anbari was appointed by [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] over al-Yamama after its conquest in the year 12 AH. This happened when Khalid marched to Bahrain in response to a letter from [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] asking him to come to the aid of al-Ala' ibn al-Hadrami {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

The source of the report is al-Baladhuri (p.123).
