## Overview
Abu Nasr ibn al-Qushayri, an Ash'ari preacher and the son of [[person/al-qushayri-abu-l-qasim|al-Qushayri]]. He arrived in Baghdad in 469 AH and sat to teach at the Nizamiyya. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

## The disturbance
Ibn al-Jawzi says that he denounced the Hanbalis and attributed anthropomorphism to them. Because of him a disturbance broke out between the Hanbalis and the Ash'aris, until a tailor was killed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

The minister al-Sharif summoned Abu Ja'far for reconciliation. Ibn al-Jawzi quotes al-Sharif as saying: "If one could be thanked for his innovation, this young man would be the one." The description is attributed to al-Sharif, and the museum does not judge it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}
