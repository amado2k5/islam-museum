## Overview
Uthman ibn Abi Nas'a al-Khath'ami, governor of al-Andalus appointed by Ubayda ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, lord of Ifriqiya. He came in the year 110 AH, and was dismissed for five months by Hudhayfa ibn al-Ahwas al-Qaysi, then returned {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.272}.
