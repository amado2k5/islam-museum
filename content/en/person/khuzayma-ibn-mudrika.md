## Summary
Father of Kinana, Asad, Amr and Awn; from Kinana descend the prophetic Quraysh and Banu Kinana, and from Asad ibn Khuzayma the tribe of Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## His place in the lineage
His son Kinana is the ancestor at whom the Quraysh genealogies meet; Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma are famous clans for poetry and generosity, and Banu Amr ibn Khuzayma another Qaysi clan {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:ansab-al-ashraf}.

## For younger visitors
Khuzayma ibn Mudrika is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
