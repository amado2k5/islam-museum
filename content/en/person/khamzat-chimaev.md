# Khamzat Chimaev

## Summary

Khamzat Khizarovich Chimaev is a Russian-Emirati professional mixed martial artist and freestyle wrestler, born in Gvardeyskoye, Chechnya, on 1 May 1994.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}{cite:wikidata-khamzat-chimaev}
He is a three-time Swedish national champion in freestyle wrestling, and in the UFC he won the middleweight championship in August 2025 before losing it in his first defence in May 2026.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
He is the first Chechen-born fighter to win a UFC championship and, after taking Emirati citizenship in January 2025, the first athlete representing the United Arab Emirates to win one.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}

## Early life and wrestling

Chimaev was born in Gvardeyskoye to a Chechen family the English article describes as poor and conservative Muslim, and he started wrestling at the age of five; it has been reported that he won a bronze medal at the Russian junior national championships.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
In 2013, at eighteen, he emigrated to Sweden with his mother, joining his older brother.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
In Sweden he won gold at the Swedish freestyle national championships in 2016 and 2017 at 86 kilograms and in 2018 at 92 kilograms, going 12–0 across the three tournaments.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
Before turning professional in fighting he worked at a poultry factory in Kalmar and did security work.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}

## Mixed martial arts career

Chimaev entered the UFC in July 2020 and set a modern UFC record by winning twice in ten days, then a third time within 66 days of his debut.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
He holds wins over three former UFC champions: Kamaru Usman, Robert Whittaker and Dricus du Plessis.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
On 16 August 2025, at UFC 319, he beat du Plessis by unanimous decision to win the UFC middleweight championship.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
On 9 May 2026 he lost the title to former champion Sean Strickland by split decision in his first defence, the first loss of his MMA career.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
In freestyle wrestling he later signed with Real American Freestyle and, at RAF 10 on 13 June 2026, pinned Dillon Danis in 44 seconds, the fastest pin in the promotion's history.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}

## Association with Ramzan Kadyrov

The sources report that Chimaev is close to Chechen leader Ramzan Kadyrov, who gifted him a Mercedes-Benz and, reportedly, persuaded him in March 2021 not to retire and to return to Chechnya, which led to media speculation about whether he was forced to do so.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-ar}
In 2022 he began training Kadyrov's two teenage sons and was in the elder son's corner at his professional MMA debut at ACA 150.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
Chechen dissidents criticised his continued association with Kadyrov in March 2022 because of Kadyrov's repressive rule of the Chechen Republic.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
He married in Chechnya on 21 May 2022, and the wedding was attended by Kadyrov.{cite:wikipedia-khamzat-chimaev-en}
