## Overview
Khidr ibn Ali ibn Marwan ibn Ali, Husam al-Din al-Aydini, known as Haji Pasha and called al-Khattab, was a physician-theologian of the Hanafi scholars; his origin was Konya, born and raised at Aydin, he settled in Egypt and died there in 820 AH (1417 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.306}

His books in medicine and beyond include al-Tashil; al-Farida on beneficial foods; Shifa' al-Asqam wa-Dawa' al-Alam, a huge medical volume; Ikhtiyarat al-Shifa', its abridgement; a gloss on al-Armawi's Sharh Matali' al-Anwar on logic and wisdom; Majma' al-Anwar on tafsir; al-Sa'ada wa-l-Iqbal; and a commentary on al-Baydawi's Tawali' al-Anwar on kalam. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.306}
