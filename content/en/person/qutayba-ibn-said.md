## In brief
Qutayba b. Sa'id, the shaykh and memoriser, hadith-master of Khurasan, Abu Raja' al-Thaqafi their client, al-Baghlani of Baghdad; born in 149 AH, he heard Malik, al-Layth, Ibn Lahi'a, Sharik, and their layer. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.406}

## Transmission and standing
The whole community of collectors except Ibn Majah narrated from him, along with Musa b. Harun, al-Hasan b. Sufyan, al-Faryabi, Abu al-Abbas al-Sarraj, and many; he was reliable, learned, a man of hadith and travels, and wealthy; Ahmad b. Sayyar said "would that Qutayba stayed with me this winter until I bring you a hundred thousand hadiths from five", Ibn Sayyar said "Qutayba was a man of the sunna who wrote hadith from three generations", Ibn Ma'in called him reliable, and al-Nasa'i "reliable and trustworthy". {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.406}

## His death
He died in Sha'ban of 240 AH at ninety-one. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.407}
