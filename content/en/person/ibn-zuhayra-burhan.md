## Overview
Burhan al-Din Ibn Zuhayra al-Makki al-Qurashi al-Shafi'i — "the scholar of the Hijaz," imam and polymath of Mecca's Zuhayra learned house; he died Friday night 6 Dhu'l-Qa'da 891, near sixty-five {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 350}.
