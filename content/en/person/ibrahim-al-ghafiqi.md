## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ahmad ibn Isa ibn Ya'qub al-Ghafiqi al-Ishbili, a reciter and grammarian. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.17-18}

## Study
His family moved from Seville to Ceuta, where he memorised the Qur'an in the transmitted readings. He took Arabic and grammar from Ibn Abi al-Rabi'. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.17-18}

## Death
The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* gives his death as 1344 CE. This date comes from one source and needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.17-18}
