## Overview
Musa ibn Husayn ibn Musa ibn Imran al-Qaysi, Abu Imran, the ascetic known as al-Mirtuli, was an Andalusi poet with knowledge of Qur'anic exegesis, jurisprudence and hadith. Al-A'lam gives his years as 522 AH, 1128 CE, to 604 AH, 1207 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}.

## Origin and residence
His family came from the frontier town of Mertola, in the district of Beja, and he lived in Seville. He died in the city of Fes and was buried outside Bab al-Futuh {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}.

## Verse and prose
He left a diwan of poetry, mostly on asceticism and warning of the Hereafter. Al-Zirikli quotes two sayings of his prose: the one who gains you something wins your heart, and whoever is light of tongue and foot is heavy with regret later {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}. The entry is brief and says no more.
