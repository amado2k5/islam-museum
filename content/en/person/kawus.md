## Overview
Kawus, king of Ushrusana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His treaty and its breaking
Al-Fadl ibn Sahl made peace with him in return for a tribute he would pay, on condition that the Muslims should not raid his land. He broke the treaty when al-Ma'mun came to the City of Peace (Baghdad), and his son Haydar killed his steward al-Athir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His surrender
When Ahmad ibn Abi Khalid pressed him on the shorter road, he surrendered and came to the City of Peace, where he professed Islam. Al-Ma'mun gave him back his lands, and after him his son Haydar ruled them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
