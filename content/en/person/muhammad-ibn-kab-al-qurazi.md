Muhammad ibn Ka'b ibn Hayyan ibn Sulaym, Abu Hamza and Abu Abd Allah al-Qurazi, of Medina: a Successor and one of the leading imams of Qur'an exegesis. Ibn Sa'd called him trustworthy, learned, a frequent transmitter and pious. He transmitted from a number of Companions, among them Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, Abu Hurayra, Zayd ibn Arqam, Ibn Abbas, Jabir, Anas and Ibn Umar. His brother Uthman, Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir, Zayd ibn Aslam, al-Hakam ibn Utayba and Yazid ibn al-Had transmitted from him, and many others did too. His death is given as 117, 119 or 120 AH. The compilers of the canonical books transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17468}
