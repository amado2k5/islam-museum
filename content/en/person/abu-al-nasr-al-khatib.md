## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Salih ibn Abd al-Rahim al-Khatib al-Shafi'i, Abu al-Nasr: a hadith scholar. He was born in Damascus and died at al-Tall in its countryside, and is buried in Damascus; he lived from 1253 to 1324 AH (1837–1906 CE). He travelled to the Hijaz and Egypt and held judgeships in various districts. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.213}

Al-Kattani says of him: he is the only person I ever saw narrate many hadiths from memory, text and chain alike, among all the people of East and West I met. He was one of the eloquent preachers of the mosques and a teacher of the Umayyad Mosque in his old age. He left a thabat of his teachers and transmissions and a small abridged treatise. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.213}
