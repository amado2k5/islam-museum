## Overview
Musa ibn Ka'b. Al-Baladhuri says that he was the governor of al-Jazira at Harran when Constantine besieged the people of Malatya in 133 AH, and al-Jazira was then in turmoil. The people of Malatya sent an envoy to him, but he could not come to their aid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.263}
