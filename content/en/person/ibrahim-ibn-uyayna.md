## Overview
Ibrahim ibn 'Uyayna ibn Abi 'Imran al-Hilali Abu Ishaq, a traditionist and imam of good standing; born around 120 AH, Yahya ibn Ma'in, al-Fallas, al-'Adani, 'Ali ibn Muhammad al-Tanafisi and a group narrated from him, the last to die being al-Hasan ibn 'Ali ibn 'Affan. Ibn Ma'in said he was a truthful Muslim, not one of the profuse or most accurate narrators. He died in 199 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.686}
