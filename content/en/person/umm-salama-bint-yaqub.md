## Overview
Umm Salama bint Ya'qub ibn Salama ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Walid ibn al-Mughira al-Makhzumi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.247-250}.

## Her marriages
She was married to Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Walid and he died and left her; then to Hisham, and he died and left her. She offered herself to Abu al-Abbas for seven hundred dinars, and he married her and gave her a dowry of five hundred {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.247-250}.

## With Abu al-Abbas
al-Mas'udi reports that she found favour with him, and he swore not to marry anyone over her nor take a concubine. She bore him Muhammad and Rayta, and he did not decide any matter except on her advice {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.247-250}.

It was because of her that he came to Khalid ibn Safwan, when he described women to him, until he began describing their harm {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.247-250}.
