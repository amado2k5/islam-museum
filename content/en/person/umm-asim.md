## Overview
Umm Asim, wife of Ruderic, king of the Goths {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.317}.

## After the conquest
At the time of the conquest she made peace for herself and her property, accepted the poll tax, and remained in her religion {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.317}.

## Her marriage
The amir [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-musa|Abd al-Aziz ibn Musa]] married her, and she found favour with him. al-Maqqari reports that this marriage was among the things the soldiers resented in him {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.317}.
