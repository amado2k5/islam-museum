## Overview
A poet whom al-Mas'udi describes as one of the most natural of the people of his age, bringing the rare line and the proverb that passes into use; he was above his peers in his time and below al-Buhturi. He wrote satire against Sa'id ibn Humayd. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 529-530}
