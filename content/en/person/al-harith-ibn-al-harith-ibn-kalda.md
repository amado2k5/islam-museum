## Overview
[[person/al-harith-ibn-al-harith-ibn-kalda|Al-Harith ibn al-Harith ibn Kalda]] ibn Amr ibn Ilaj al-Thaqafi. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.252} Ibn Abd al-Barr says he was among "those whose hearts are to be reconciled". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.252} As for his father, his Islam is not sound in Ibn Abd al-Barr's view. Ibn Hajar says the reply to this will come in the entry on al-Harith ibn Kalda. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.252}

## Notes for researchers
- The entry in the Isaba is brief. We do not add to it until we have read his father's entry and checked the disagreement over his Islam.
