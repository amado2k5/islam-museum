## Summary
A Maliki jurist and hadith memorizer of Meknes; judge at Sijilmasa; heard from Ibn al-Fakhkhar, Ibn al-Baqqal, Abu Dharr al-Khushani and others; Ibn Mudaa and al-Tadili corresponded with him. Works include al-Nazar fi Ahkam al-Nazar and Bayan al-Wahm wal-Iham. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7832}
