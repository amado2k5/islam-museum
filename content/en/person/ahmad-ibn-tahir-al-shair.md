## Overview
Ahmad ibn Tahir, a poet whom al-Mas'udi mentions. He lamented Abu al-Husayn Yahya ibn Umar in a long poem, and al-Mas'udi quotes some of its verses. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.531}

The dates and lineage of his life need an additional source.
