## Overview
Sa'id ibn Ahmad ibn Sa'id ibn Salm al-Bahili, the foremost of the Bahila {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.100}.

## The report
According to the account, the Bahila had become greedy for the marshes after the Zanj left them, and they spread corruption there. He was seized and taken to Baghdad. al-Mu'tamid ordered him to be given seven hundred lashes; he was flogged and crucified in Rabi' al-Akhir of the year 258 AH. The rest of their leaders then joined the Zanj {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.100}.

## Notes for researchers
- The account comes from al-Muntazam alone. It needs an additional source.
