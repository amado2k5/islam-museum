## Overview
Abdallah ibn Mahmud ibn Mawdud al-Mawsili al-Baldahi, Majd al-Din Abu al-Fadl, was a Hanafi jurist, one of their great figures. He was born at Mosul, travelled to Damascus, held the judgeship of Kufa for a time and then settled at Baghdad as a teacher, where he died. The printed text gives his birth as 599 AH and his death as 683 AH, that is 1203 and 1284 CE; the card keeps 1202 for his birth because 599 AH runs from late 1202 into 1203 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli says he had books, among them 'al-Ikhtiyar li-Ta'lil al-Mukhtar', printed, on jurisprudence, a commentary on his own book 'al-Mukhtar' on the branches of Hanafi law; the latter is in manuscript at Chester Beatty (no. 4360) and at Riyadh University (no. 1446) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}.
