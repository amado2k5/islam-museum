## Overview
Ayyub, a prophet known for patience. Al-Bukhari relates that he bathed naked, and locusts of gold fell upon him, and he began to gather them in his garment. His Lord called to him: "Did I not enrich you from what you see?" He said: "Yes, by Your might, but I have no need of Your blessing." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3211}

The rest of his story is not set out here and needs an additional source.
