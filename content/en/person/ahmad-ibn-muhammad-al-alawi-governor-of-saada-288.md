## Overview
He is Ahmad ibn Muhammad of the descendants of al-Abbas ibn Ali. When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] gathered a great army from Khawlan and set out for Khaywan, he left him as governor at Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202} The page does not say which al-Abbas ibn Ali is meant, so he is not tied to the record of al-Abbas ibn Ali nor to that of Ahmad ibn Muhammad, governor of Najran. See [[event/al-hadi-leaves-saada-for-khiwan-and-meets-al-daam-at-al-amshiyya-288]].
