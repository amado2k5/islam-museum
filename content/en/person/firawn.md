## Summary
Pharaoh as Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy mentions him in the series "Al Fawzu Al Moubeen": his dream and what his counsellors made of it, his arrogance in what he said, and a comparison between the sending of Musa to him and the sending of the prophets to their peoples. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:26:30}

## The dream and the fear of a child
The Sheikh says that Pharaoh saw a dream, which his counsellors interpreted to mean that a boy from the Children of Israel would grow up and remove him from his kingdom. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:26:30}

## Pharaoh's story in the Qur'an
The Sheikh says that the Qur'an relates the stories of Qarun, Pharaoh, Musa and Shu'ayb in brief, and that its verses present images of tyranny, hoarding and injustice. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:40:30}

## Musa sent to Pharaoh
The Sheikh compares the sending of the prophets to their peoples with the sending of Musa to Pharaoh, and some sentences at this point are unclear (needs_source: the sentences are garbled in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:27:30}

## The speaker's view: Pharaoh's arrogance
The Sheikh criticises Pharaoh's arrogance and describes his saying that he sees only what he himself sees, and he notes that the prophets were sent to their peoples. [[passage/quran-40-29|The verse the Sheikh cites on Pharaoh's words]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:27:00}

## The speaker's view: the gentle word to Pharaoh
The Sheikh reports al-Shafi'i's explanation of God's command to Musa and Harun to speak gently, and he wonders why they were commanded to be gentle with one who claimed supreme lordship [[passage/quran-20-44|The verse on the command to speak gently]] (needs_source: the work of al-Shafi'i is not named in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep09 00:28:00}

## The speaker's view: what Pharaoh said to Musa about his upbringing
The Sheikh presents Pharaoh's words to Musa reminding him of how he was raised, then applies the same point to someone who holds over his nephew the cost of what he spent on him. He speaks of treating orphans gently and cites a verse on that [[passage/quran-93-9|The verse on the treatment of the orphan]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:30:30}
