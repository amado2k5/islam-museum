## Summary
Abu Muhammad 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Fadl b. Bahram b. 'Abd al-Samad al-Tamimi al-Darimi was a hadith master of [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]], compiler of [[work/sunan-al-darimi|the Musnad]] known as Sunan al-Darimi. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 20} Al-Suyuti's *Tarikh al-Khulafa'* places his death in the days of al-Mu'tazz. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 559}

## Lineage
"Al-Tamimi" refers to Tamim, "al-Darimi" to Banu Darim b. Malik b. Hanzala b. Zayd Manat b. Tamim, as al-Sam'ani explains. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 21} Mughaltay reports from Yahya b. Badr al-Samarqandi that he was "a client of theirs". {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 21}

## Birth, upbringing and travels
Ishaq b. Ibrahim al-Warraq reports him saying: "I was born in the year Ibn al-Mubarak died, 181." {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 23} He had a brother, Ibrahim b. 'Abd al-Rahman, knowledgeable in hadith, who was buried beside him. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 23-24} Al-Khatib calls him "one of those who travelled for hadith, known for collecting and memorising it". {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 24} The editors quote Ibn al-Jawzi: he heard in Khurasan from 'Uthman b. Jabala and Muhammad b. Sallam, in Iraq from 'Ubayd Allah b. Musa, Abu Nu'aym and Rawh, in Egypt from Sa'id b. Abi Maryam, in the Hijaz from al-Humaydi and Ibn Abi Uways, and in Syria from Muhammad b. Yusuf al-Firyabi, Abu al-Yaman and Abu Mushir. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 25}

## Teachers and students
The Dar al-Ta'sil editors counted 211 teachers from whom he narrates in the Musnad, most often Muhammad b. Yusuf al-Firyabi (269 hadiths) and Yazid b. Harun (194). {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 26} Those who transmitted from him include [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] outside his Sahih, [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]], [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]], [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]], [[person/baqi-ibn-makhlad|Baqi b. Makhlad]] of al-Andalus, [[person/abu-zura-al-razi|Abu Zur'a al-Razi]], and 'Isa b. 'Umar al-Samarqandi, the transmitter of the Musnad from him. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 28-29}

## What scholars said of him
- [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad b. Hanbal]]: "trustworthy and more"; he said memorisation had come to rest with four men of Khurasan, al-Darimi among them, and urged a visitor: "Hold to that master." {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 29-30}
- Muhammad b. Bashshar: the world's memorisers are four — Abu Zur'a in Rayy, Muslim in Nishapur, al-Darimi in Samarqand and al-Bukhari in Bukhara. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 31}
- Abu Hatim al-Razi: "the imam of the people of his time", and the most reliable of the scholars of Khurasan. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 31}
- Abu Zur'a al-Razi: every man described to him proved less than the description when he met him — except al-Darimi, who proved more. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 31}
- Ibn Hibban: one of the precise masters and people of scruple, who made the Sunna known in his city and called people to it. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 32}

## Creed and school
Al-Lalika'i lists him among the imams of the Sunna and among those who held the Qur'an to be God's uncreated speech and faith to increase and decrease, and al-Dhahabi counts him among those who did not interpret away the divine attributes and affirmed God's elevation. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 33} Al-Dhahabi says "he was a vessel of knowledge; he reasoned for himself and did not follow another", and the editors hold that Ibn Abi Ya'la's listing him in the Tabaqat of the Hanbalis means only that he transmitted from Ahmad, not that he followed him. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 33-34}

## As judge
He was asked to be judge of Samarqand and refused; the ruler insisted until he accepted, judged a single case, then asked to be released and was. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 34}

## Works
Few works are attributed to him: the *Musnad*, also called *al-Sunan*; a *Tafsir* attributed by Ahmad b. Sayyar and al-Khatib but not found; and *al-Jami'*, attributed by al-Khatib; the editors judge the attribution of *Sawm al-Mustahada wa-al-Mutahayyira* to him a mistake — it is by Abu al-Faraj al-Darimi, the Shafi'i (d. 449 AH). {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 35}

## Death
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: Ahmad b. Sayyar, endorsed by al-Khatib, and similarly Makki b. Mahan and Ibn Hibban**: he died in 255 AH on the day of Tarwiya after the afternoon prayer and was buried on the day of 'Arafa, a Friday, aged seventy-five. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 36}
- **Sunni: Yahya b. Badr al-Samarqandi**: he prayed over him after the Friday prayer on the day of 'Arafa, 254 AH. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 36}
:::
Ishaq b. Ahmad b. Khalaf relates that when news of his death reached [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]], he bowed his head, uttered the istirja', wept, and recited a line of verse on the loss of loved ones. {cite:musnad-darimi-tasil-2015 p. 36}
