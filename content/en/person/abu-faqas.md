## Overview
Abu Faqas, a man who was beaten in Baghdad on Thursday, seven nights into Ramadan 258 AH, after evidence was established that he abused the early generations. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.100}

## The event
He was beaten fifty lashes and died. This is what Ibn al-Jawzi relates in "al-Muntazam". {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.100}
