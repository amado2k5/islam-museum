## Overview
Ibn al-Salus the merchant-scribe; he held the hisba of Damascus and conducted himself well, though people held the office beneath him — and soon he held the vizierate, entering Damascus in a procession the like of which was not known. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 740}

## His end
Before that he had fasted and made much dhikr; once vizier he grew arrogant toward people, the amirs above all, and wronged the man who had raised him to the sultan. When al-Malik al-Ashraf was killed he was seized and his wealth and kin uprooted; a warning letter had been written to him against the "Shuja'i's bite," and it afflicted him until he died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 740}
