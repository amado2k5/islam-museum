# Kamaru Usman

## Summary

Kamarudeen Usman is a Nigerian-American professional mixed martial artist and former freestyle wrestler, born in Auchi, Nigeria, on 11 May 1987.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}{cite:wikidata-kamaru-usman}
He won the UFC welterweight championship in 2019, defended it five times in a division-record 15-fight win streak, and lost it in 2022; he is widely considered one of the greatest welterweights in MMA history.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
Before MMA he was an NCAA Division II national champion wrestler.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}

## Wrestling and road to the UFC

Usman was born in Auchi, Edo State, and emigrated with his family to Dallas, Texas, when he was eight.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
At the University of Nebraska at Kearney he became NCAA Division II national champion at 174 pounds in 2010 and a three-time NCAA Division II All-American, and he was a member of the 2010 US University World Team.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
In MMA he won The Ultimate Fighter 21 tournament in 2015.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}

## UFC welterweight championship

On 2 March 2019, at UFC 235, Usman beat Tyron Woodley by unanimous decision to win the UFC welterweight championship.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
He defended the title five times — Colby Covington, Jorge Masvidal, Gilbert Burns, Masvidal again and Covington again — building a 15-fight win streak, the longest in the division's history.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
On 20 August 2022, at UFC 278, Leon Edwards knocked him out in the fifth round to take the title; he also lost the rematch in March 2023.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
He took his nickname "The Nigerian Nightmare" from American football player Christian Okoye, whose nickname it was, with Okoye's blessing.{cite:wikipedia-kamaru-usman-en}
