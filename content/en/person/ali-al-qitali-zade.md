## Summary
Mawla 'Ali Qitali-zade; one of the qadis who were accomplished poets and wits, born in 918. He dissented on the question of beating with the bastinado and wrote a book on it, and enjoyed a fine renown in Abu'l-Su'ud's lifetime; a tasteful poet of lovely adab whose ode 'the outcast seeking refuge in God's sanctuary' encompassed the arts. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 388-389}

## His killing
He was killed by sorcery at Antioch — or, it was said, his wife gave him something in his last illness; he died on the seventh of Ramadan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 388-389}
