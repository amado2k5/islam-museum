## Overview
Najm al-Din Ayyub ibn Shadhi, father of Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub, and brother of [[person/asad-al-din-shirkuh|Asad al-Din Shirkuh]]. He was the son of [[person/shadhi-ibn-marwan|Shadhi ibn Marwan]]. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.139-140}

## Origins
Ibn Khallikan reports that historians agree that his father and family were from Dawin, a town at the far edge of the province of Azerbaijan on the side of Arran and the lands of the Kurj. They were Kurds of the Rawadiyya, a branch of the Hadhbaniyya. Ayyub was born there. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.139-140}

## Migration
His father Shadhi set out with his two sons, Asad al-Din Shirkuh and Najm al-Din Ayyub, to Baghdad. They then settled in Tikrit, where Shadhi died. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.139-140}
