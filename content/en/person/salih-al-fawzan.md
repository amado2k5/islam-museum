## Summary
Salih ibn Fawzan al-Fawzan is a Saudi Hanbali jurist appointed Grand Mufti of the Kingdom in 2025 after decades of teaching and fatwa within the official religious establishment. {cite:wikipedia-salih-al-fawzan}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1935 in al-Shamasiyya, Qassim, he studied in Saudi religious institutes to a doctorate in fiqh, learning under the leading shaykhs of Qassim and Riyadh. {cite:wikipedia-salih-al-fawzan}

## Career and works
He taught in religious institutes and universities, joined the Permanent Committee for Fatwa, the Council of Senior Scholars and the Islamic Fiqh Academy, and appeared on fatwa radio programmes. His works include the widely taught al-Mulakhkhas al-Fiqhi and Kitab al-Tawhid on creed. In 2025 he was appointed Grand Mufti. {cite:wikipedia-salih-al-fawzan}

## Legacy and debate
Al-Fawzan represents the traditionalist Hanbali wing of official Saudi fatwa. Some of his rulings on social and legal questions draw wide media controversy; supporters defend their textual fidelity while opponents object. The museum reports the disagreement attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-salih-al-fawzan}
