## Overview
Muhammad Baqir ibn Muhammad Akmal al-Bihbahani is described by al-Zirikli as an Imami scholar, that is a scholar of the Twelver Shia. This is a non-Sunni description from outside the Sunni frame that governs this museum, and it is given here as al-Zirikli's. He gives his birth as 1118 AH, 1706 CE, and his death as 1206 AH, 1791 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 49}.

## Life
He was born in Isfahan, lived for a time in Behbahan, then settled in Karbala and died at al-Ha'ir {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 49}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books: glosses on Minhaj al-maqal, printed in its margin; a gloss on Mafatih al-ahkam in law, in manuscript; Fawa'id 'atiqa, in manuscript; Fawa'id jadida, in manuscript; and many glosses and treatises {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 49}.
