## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Izz al-Din ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn al-Mu'ayyad al-Hasani, al-Nasir li-Din Allah, entered in al-A'lam as al-Nasir al-Zaydi, was one of the Zaydi imams of Yemen. Zaydism is a Shi'i school distinct from the Sunni frame the museum takes as its default; what follows rests on al-Zirikli's account. He gives his birth as 862 AH, 1458 CE, and his death as 929 AH, 1523 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 199}.

## His imamate
Al-Zirikli says he called people to his own imamate in the fortress of Kuhlan after his father's death in 900 AH, and the Friday sermon was given in his name in the city of Sa'da. Opponents rose against him and fabricated a story on which a judicial ruling annulled his imamate; people turned from him and he carried on with a small following {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 199}.

## Death and writings
He died in the town of Fallala, north of Sanaa, and al-Zirikli calls him a distinguished jurist. His works include al-Qistas al-Maqbul, a commentary on Mi'yar al-Uqul in legal theory, and treatises of literary and rhetorical quality {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 199}.
