## Overview
Baha al-Din Sam ibn Muhammad ibn Mas'ud, nephew of the Ghurid brothers Ghiyath al-Din and Shihab al-Din, and lord of Bamiyan. In 594 AH he took the city of Balkh when its Turkish lord Azih died, and it had been paying tribute every year to the Khata. He cut off the tribute to them and gave the khutba for Ghiyath al-Din. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.252}
