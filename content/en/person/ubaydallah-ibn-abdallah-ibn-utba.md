## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Utba, one of the senior people of learning. al-Mas'udi transmits the saying of al-Zuhri: "I used to think that I had attained knowledge, until I sat with Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah, and he was like the sea" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.154}.
