## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Harun al-Tawzi, a philologist. Books of classes give his biography and mention writings of his, most of which were lost. He died in 230 AH (844 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.694}
