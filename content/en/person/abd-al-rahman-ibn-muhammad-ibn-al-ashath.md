## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath led the rising against [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] in 81 AH; people gathered to him, and he was the first to be given the allegiance. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.338}

## According to al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that he fought from Sistan against kinds of Turks called the Uz and the Khalaj, and the kings of India who were near them, such as Rutbil. After he threw off al-Hajjaj's obedience, the people of Basra and the mountains next to Kufa and Basra submitted to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 122-123}
