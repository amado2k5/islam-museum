## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Isma'il al-Qaffal al-Shashi, a Shafi'i jurist. He studied under Abu al-Abbas [[person/ahmad-ibn-umar-ibn-surayj|Ibn Surayj]], and was an imam with many works. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

## His learning
Al-Shirazi says that he was the first of the jurists to write fine dialectic. He has a book on the principles of jurisprudence and a "Commentary on the Risala". The jurisprudence of al-Shafi'i spread from him beyond the river. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

## His death
Al-Shirazi says that he died in 336 AH. Ibn Khallikan relates a difference about his death, including that he died at al-Shash in 365 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

## Notes for researchers
- The year of death is disputed and needs a second source.
