## Overview
Allan al-Shu'ubi, a Persian-origin transmitter of genealogies and reports. Ibn al-Nadim says that he used to copy in the House of Wisdom for al-Rashid, al-Ma'mun and the Barmakids. {cite:duha-islam}

## Notes for researchers
- The recorded source is Ahmad Amin's "Duha al-Islam" (vol. 3, p. 80), citing the "Fihrist"; the "Fihrist" itself has not been checked. This needs an additional source.
