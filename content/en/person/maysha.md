## Overview
Maysha, daughter of Zadhawayh, marzban of Sarakhs. She fell to the share of Abdallah ibn Khazim al-Sulami as part of the booty of the peace of Sarakhs, and he took her and named her Maysha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
