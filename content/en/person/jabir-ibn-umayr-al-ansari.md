## Summary
Jabir ibn Umayr al-Ansari al-Madani, a companion. He narrated from the Prophet on the excellence of archery, and Ata ibn Abi Rabah narrated from him. The book reports a story Ata gives about Jabir ibn Abd Allah and Jabir ibn Umayr shooting together and talking about lawful play {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13751}. The book gives no dates for him.
