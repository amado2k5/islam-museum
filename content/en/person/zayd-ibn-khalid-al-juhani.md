## Overview
Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, a Companion whose kunya is variously given as Abu Zur'a, Abu Abd al-Rahman and Abu Talha. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.515}

## His transmission and campaigns
He transmitted from the Prophet and from Uthman, Abu Talha and A'isha; his sons Khalid and Abu Harb, his freedman Abu Umra, Ubaydallah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba, Abu Salama and others transmitted from him. He witnessed al-Hudaybiyya, carried the banner of Juhayna on the day of the Conquest, and his hadith is in the two Sahihs and elsewhere. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.515}

## His death
Ibn al-Barqi and others said he died in 78 AH in Medina at the age of eighty-five; it was also said in 68, and it was said he died earlier in Mu'awiya's caliphate in Medina. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.515}
