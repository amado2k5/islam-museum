## Overview
Mansur ibn Talha ibn Tahir ibn al-Husayn. Ibn al-Nadim says that Abdallah ibn Tahir called him the judge of the house of Tahir and admired him, and that he governed Marw, Amul and Khwarazm. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.170}

## His books
He has well-known books on philosophy, among them *al-Mu'nis* on music, which al-Kindi read and said that it was indeed a companion, as its author called it. He also wrote *al-Ibana 'an Af'al al-Falak* (Clarification on the Actions of the Sphere), *al-Wujud* (Existence), a treatise on number and the numbered, and *al-Dalil wa al-Istidlal* (The Proof and the Inference). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.170}
