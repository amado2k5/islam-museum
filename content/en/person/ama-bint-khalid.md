## Summary
Daughter of Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As and Amina bint Khalf, born in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her a daughter of Khalid ibn Sa'id, born in the land of Abyssinia with her brother Sa'id. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He counts her among the five females born there, of Banu Abd Shams. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}
