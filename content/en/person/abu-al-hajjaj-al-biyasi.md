## Overview
Abu'l-Hajjaj al-Biyasi, one of the excellent memorisers of al-Andalus — of Biasa, a large town in Kurjiyan; a brilliant littérateur versed in every branch of Arab speech, verse and prose, and a transmitter of their campaigns, wars and days. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 270-271}

## His memory and works
Ibn Khallikan reports that he had memorised the Kitab al-Hamasa, the diwan of al-Mutanabbi and the aphorisms of al-Zand; he moved through al-Andalus roaming most of it, and composed for the lord of Ifriqiya Kitab al-i'lam bi'l-hurub al-waqi'a fi sadr al-Islam in two volumes — beginning with the killing of 'Umar b. al-Khattab and ending with the revolt of al-Walid b. Tarif al-Shari against Harun al-Rashid; his Kitab al-hamasa is likewise in two volumes. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 270-271}
