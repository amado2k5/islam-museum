## Overview
Al-Hakam ibn Amr al-Ghifari, whom the entry describes as chaste and a Companion. Ziyad appointed him over Khurasan when al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As wanted it, and al-Ghifari was brought in instead. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri says that he died in Khurasan in 50 AH. He was the first to pray across the river (Transoxiana), and he conquered the lands of al-Saghaniyan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
