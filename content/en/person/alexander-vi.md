## Summary
Alexander VI, pope after [[person/innocent-viii|Innocent VIII]], in the reign of [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.

## Cem's death and the league
Al-Salabi relays that Prince [[person/jem-sultan|Cem]] passed to Alexander's custody on Innocent's death and was killed soon after — the chronicles, as the book reports rather than asserts, accusing Bayezid II of ridding himself of his brother {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}. At Venice's request Alexander VI raised a league of France and Spain against the Ottomans after Lepanto fell {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 173}.
