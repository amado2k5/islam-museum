## Overview
Muhammad Tawfiq ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Bakri al-Siddiqi, an Egyptian scholar and writer of the Siddiqi Bakri family. He is credited with the *Mu'jam al-Matbu'at* (A Dictionary of Printed Works). He was born in 1870 CE (1287 AH) and died in 1932 CE (1351 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
