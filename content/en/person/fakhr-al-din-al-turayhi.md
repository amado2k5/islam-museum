## Overview
Fakhr al-Din ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Turayh al-Rammahi al-Najafi was one of the scholars of the Imamis. He was born in 979 AH, 1571 CE, and died in 1085 AH, 1674 CE, at al-Rammahiyya, and was carried to Najaf {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 136}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Majma' al-Bahrayn wa-Matla' al-Nayyirayn", printed, on the rare words of the Qur'an and of hadith; "al-Muntakhab fi Jam' al-Marathi wa-al-Khutab", printed; "Gharib al-Hadith"; "Jami' al-Maqal fima Yata'allaq bi-Ahwal al-Hadith wa-al-Rijal", printed; "Kashf Ghawamid al-Qur'an"; "Jawahir al-Matalib fi Fada'il Ali ibn Abi Talib"; "Marathi al-Husayn"; and "Nuzhat al-Khatir wa-Surur al-Nazir", in manuscript, photographed in the library of the University of Riyadh, 211 leaves, on the words of the Qur'an; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 136}.
