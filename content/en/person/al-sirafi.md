## Overview
Abu Sa'id al-Hasan ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Marzuban al-Sirafi, a famous grammarian and philologist, who lived in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.72-73}

## His works
He wrote a commentary on Sibawayh's book, and Ibn Khallikan says that he came to see in research what was not seen by others. He then wrote "al-Iqna'", and died before completing it, and his son Abu Muhammad Yusuf completed it. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.72-73}

## His assembly
He had a session in Baghdad at which scholars read to him, and among his companions was the keeper of the House of Knowledge, Abd al-Salam al-Basri. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.72-73}
