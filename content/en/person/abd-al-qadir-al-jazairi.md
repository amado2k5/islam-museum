## Overview
Prince Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhyi al-Din al-Hasani al-Jaza'iri was a scholar and poet who led the struggle against the French in Algeria. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141}

## Early life
He was born at al-Qaytana, one of the villages of the province of [[place/wahran|Oran]], and studied in Oran. He performed the pilgrimage with his father in 1241 AH, and visited Medina, Damascus and Baghdad. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141}

## The struggle
When the French entered Algiers in 1246 AH, the Algerians gave him their allegiance and entrusted him with the command of the holy war ([[event/french-occupation-algiers-1246|the French entry into Algiers]]), and he fought them for fifteen years. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141} He struck coins which he called "al-Hamdiyya", and set up workshops for weapons, military equipment and soldiers' clothing. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141}

## Exile and death
He surrendered on terms in 1263 AH, and was exiled to Toulon and then Amboise, where Napoleon III visited him and released him on condition that he never return to Algeria. He then visited Paris and Istanbul, settled in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in 1271 AH, and died there in 1883 CE. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141}

## Works
His works include "Dhikra al-Aqil", a volume of poetry, and "al-Mawaqif" on Sufism. {cite:al-aalam p.140-141}
