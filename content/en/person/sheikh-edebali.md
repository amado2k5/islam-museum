## Summary
Shaykh Edebali was one of the famous scholars of Konya, as [[person/mehmed-sureyya|Mehmed Süreyya]] describes him in the [[work/sicill-i-osmani|Sicill-i Osmani]], and Osman Gazi was among his devotees. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 106} He also had standing in the Sufi path. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 106}

## His bond with Osman Gazi
Süreyya records that Osman Gazi, as a young prince, frequented the shaykh's lodge, won his favour, and married his daughter Malhun Hatun. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 106}

## Death and standing
He died in 726 AH (1326), four months before the sultan; Süreyya calls him a Qur'an commentator, traditionist and jurist, and one of those counted among the saints. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 106} The entry refers to the first edition, vol. I p. 312. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 106}
