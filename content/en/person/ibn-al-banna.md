## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Abdullah ibn al-Banna' al-Baghdadi, a jurist, Quran-teacher, hadith-scholar and preacher who composed about five hundred works, among them al-Mujarrad in fiqh and Sharh al-Kharaqi {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 412}. He died in 471 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 412}.
