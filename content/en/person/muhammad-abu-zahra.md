## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad Abu Zahra is called by al-Zirikli the greatest scholar of Islamic law of his age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He was born in the city of al-Mahalla al-Kubra, was brought up at the Ahmadi mosque, and studied at the School of Shari'a Judiciary from 1916 to 1925 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. The entry gives his years as 1316 to 1394 AH, 1898 to 1974 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## Academic career
He taught the Islamic and Arabic sciences for three years and taught in secondary schools for two and a half {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. His turn to scholarly research began at the Faculty of Usul al-Din in 1933; in 1935 he was appointed lecturer for higher studies at the university and a member of the Supreme Council for Scientific Research {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He served as deputy dean of the Faculty of Law at Cairo University and deputy head of the Institute of Islamic Studies {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he published more than forty books, of which the printed ones include «al-Khitaba» (Oratory), «Tarikh al-Jadal fi al-Islam» (History of Disputation in Islam), «Usul al-Fiqh», «al-Milkiyya wa Nazariyyat al-Aqd fi al-Shari'a al-Islamiyya» (Ownership and the Theory of Contract in Islamic Law) and «Mudhakkarat fi al-Waqf» (Notes on Endowment) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He also wrote detailed historical, juristic and usuli studies of the four imams, a large book for each: Abu Hanifa, Malik, al-Shafi'i and Ibn Hanbal {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. His printed works further include «al-Ahwal al-Shakhsiyya» (Personal Status), «Ahkam al-Tarikat wa al-Mawarith» (Rulings on Estates and Inheritance) and «Khulasat Ahkam al-Ahwal al-Shakhsiyya wa al-Wasaya wa al-Mawarith» (Summary of the Rulings on Personal Status, Bequests and Inheritance), written in answer to a request from the Institute of International Law in Washington and translated into English {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He also wrote «al-Wahda al-Islamiyya», «Tanzim al-Islam li al-Mujtama'», «al-Hurriyya wa al-Uquba fi al-Shari'a al-Islamiyya», «Muhadarat fi Muqaranat al-Adyan» and «Muhadarat fi al-Mujtama' al-Islami» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## Death
He died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
