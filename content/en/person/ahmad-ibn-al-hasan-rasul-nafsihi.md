## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan al-Kufi Ahmad b. al-Hasan, known as Rasul Nafsihi; he transmitted from Ibn 'Uyayna and others. Al-Daraqutni said his hadith is abandoned; Ibn Hibban said he fabricates hadith upon the trustworthy. He died in Egypt in 262. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 120}
