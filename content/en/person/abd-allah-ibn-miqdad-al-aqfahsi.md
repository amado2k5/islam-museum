## Overview
Jamal al-Din Abd Allah ibn Miqdad ibn Isma'il al-Aqfahsi al-Qahiri al-Maliki, a Maliki jurist. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" says that the leadership of the Maliki school and the giving of fatwas in Egypt ended with him in his time, and that he held the judgeship. It gives his birth as 745 AH and his death as 823 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
