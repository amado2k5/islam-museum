## Summary
Jezima Ismail is a veteran Sri Lankan educationist and human-rights defender, and one of the most senior Muslim women in the country's public life. Over a career spanning schools, universities and civil society, she became among the first women to lead a Sri Lankan university and built the Muslim Women's Research and Action Forum into a durable platform for Muslim women's rights. {cite:wikipedia-jezima-ismail}

## Education and teaching
Born in Batticaloa on Sri Lanka's eastern coast, she trained and worked as a teacher and rose through the school system as a principal and administrator at a time when few women — and fewer Muslim women — held senior educational posts. She later moved into higher education, serving as Vice-Chancellor of the South Eastern University of Sri Lanka, an institution serving the island's eastern province and its large Muslim population. {cite:wikipedia-jezima-ismail}

## Muslim Women's Research and Action Forum
In the 1980s she founded the Muslim Women's Research and Action Forum (MWRAF), which for decades has studied and campaigned on the questions where Muslim personal law, customary practice and women's rights meet in Sri Lanka — marriage age, divorce, maintenance, testimony and the education of girls. The forum's approach, research before advocacy, made it a reference point for reform-minded Muslim women across South Asia. {cite:muslim500-jezima-ismail}

## Public service and honours
Beyond the community, she has served on national bodies and in interfaith and women's networks, working through Sri Lanka's civil war years on behalf of communities caught between the state and the Liberation Tigers — including Muslims expelled from the north in 1990. Sri Lankan honours conferred on her include the national title Deshamanya, awarded for distinguished service. {cite:wikipedia-jezima-ismail}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as a pioneer: an educator who opened senior academic office to Sri Lankan Muslim women and an activist who insisted their questions be argued with evidence rather than slogans. {cite:muslim500-jezima-ismail}
