## Overview
Sarah, wife of [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]]. The introduction of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum mentions her in the origin of the Arabized Arabs {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18}.

## In Egypt
Ibrahim once came to [[place/misr|Egypt]], and the Pharaoh of Egypt attempted a scheme and an evil against his wife Sarah. But God turned his scheme back upon his own throat. The Pharaoh came to know of Sarah's strong bond with God, and out of recognition of her merit he gave her [[person/hajar|Hajar]] to serve her. Sarah then married her to Ibrahim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/474} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/34}. In a footnote the author notes that it is commonly held that Hajar was a slave-woman, but the judge Muhammad Sulayman al-Mansurfuri established that she was free and was the daughter of the Pharaoh. The matter is among those on which opinions differ {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/36-37}.

## Her jealousy of Hajar
Ibrahim returned to [[place/palestine|Palestine]], and God granted him [[person/ismail|Isma'il]] by Hajar. Sarah became jealous, to the point that she compelled Ibrahim to take Hajar and her small son out to the Hijaz, and he settled them beside the Sacred House {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/474-475}. The details are in [[event/settlement-of-hajar-and-ismail|the settlement of Hajar and Isma'il in Mecca]].

## Not covered here
These pages mention the birth of Isaac and the good tidings of him only in passing, with a reference to the Book of Genesis for the order of the story {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.19}.

## For young visitors
Sarah was the wife of the Prophet Ibrahim. She lived with him in Palestine, and Hajar, the mother of Isma'il, had been her servant before she became Ibrahim's wife.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh says that Sara, 'the noble Syrian lady of good lineage', bore Ibrahim no child until both were old, and that after Hajar bore Isma'il, Sara then bore Ishaq, which he sees as part of God's decrees. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:03:00}
