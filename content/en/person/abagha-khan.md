## Overview
Abagha Khan, king of the Tatars, son of their king Hulagu; he died near Hamadan between the two festivals in 680, at about fifty years of age. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 643, 653}

## His successor
The sultanate passed after him to his brother Ahmad the Muslim. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 643, 653}
