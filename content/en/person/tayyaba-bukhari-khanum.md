
## Themes and honours
Her recorded lectures cover a wide range — prayer, patience, the philosophy of worship and fasting and pilgrimage, women's rights, ethics and child-rearing — some preserved as ten-lecture series; her honours include the Quran-e-Hakim Award of 2007 and a Wonder Women Award of 2012 per reports. {cite:prideofpakistan-tayyaba-bukhari} {cite:pakistantoday-tayyaba-bukhari}

## A woman in a male field
The Muslim 500 describes her as one of very few female religious scholars in Pakistan with significant influence — a rare description in a country where the majlis pulpits were long reserved for men; threats drove her abroad in 2014, whence she continued her discourse on television and among the diaspora. {cite:muslim500-tayyaba-bukhari} {cite:pakistantoday-tayyaba-bukhari}
