## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Ali, Abu Zayd al-Fasi, was a researching jurist of many accomplishments from Fez in the far Maghrib; the historian Ibn Zaydan called him the al-Suyuti of his age. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1040 AH, 1631 CE, and his death as 1096 AH, 1685 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. He was a close companion of Mawlay al-Rashid ibn Ali, on whom he wrote much verse {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His works
He wrote more than seventy books {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-Zirikli names Miftah al-Shifa, a continuation of the book al-Shifa, in two volumes; Azhar al-Basatin, with lives of some shaykhs of his time; al-Uqnum fi Mabadi al-Ulum, a manuscript didactic poem, two parts of which he saw in the Rabat library; Tuhfat al-Akabir fi Akhbar al-Shaykh Abd al-Qadir, on the life of his father; Ibtihaj al-Basair fiman Qara ala al-Shaykh Abd al-Qadir, lives of those who took from his father; verse treatises on medicine, the astrolabe and timekeeping; and Ibtihaj al-Qulub bi-Khabar al-Shaykh Abi al-Mahasin wa-Shaykhihi al-Majdhub, in manuscript, in Rabat {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. He also names Dhikr Baed Mashahir Ahl Fas fi al-Qadim, printed, a tract the printer ascribed to his brother Muhammad, and Zahr al-Shamarikh fi Ilm al-Tarikh, printed, a poem in three quires {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
