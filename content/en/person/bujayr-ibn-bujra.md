## Summary
A man of Tayy' whom Ibn Ishaq says spoke verses on the cow story that pointed to Ukaydir, confirming the Prophet's word to Khalid. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "a man of Tayy' called Bujayr ibn Bujra" who spoke verses mentioning the Prophet's word to Khalid: you will find him hunting cows. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: a man of Tayy'. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The name is read in the page image p. 170 "Bujayr ibn Bujra"; the OCR scan confuses ba and mim.
- He appears in the book in two forms; the text names no chain for the verses.
