## Overview
Marut is one of the two angels the Qur'an names in 2:102: God sent them to Babylon to teach people sorcery as a trial and a test, teaching what would come between a man and his wife until they parted every man from his wife.

The available data go no further than this Qur'anic report; an additional source is needed.
