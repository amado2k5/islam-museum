Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Thaqafi al-Naysaburi — the master Abu 'Ali — was the imam who joined fiqh, kalam, preaching, scrupulosity and intellect, and the shaykh of al-Hakim of Nishapur. He took up learning late in life, displayed Sufism openly at Nishapur, and spoke of the Sufis in the best of terms.

Abu'l-Qasim al-Shirwani said: "no one more intelligent than Abu 'Ali al-Thaqafi was born in Islam." Al-Shibli sent a man to Nishapur for a full year to carry back Abu 'Ali's morning and evening sessions, and judged his morning discourses "miraculous in the science of realities." When Ibn Khuzayma set him questions and watched him write the answers, he told him: "no one of us may issue fatwa while you live." Ibn Surayj said: "no one more learned in fiqh ever came to us from Khurasan."

He died on a Thursday night and was buried on Friday 23 Jumada I 328, aged eighty-nine; al-Hakim, who attended his funeral, said he never saw such a gathering at Nishapur after him.
