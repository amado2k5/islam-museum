## Overview
Ali ibn Umar al-Aswad, titled Ala al-Din al-Qarahisari, was a Hanafi jurist, one of the scholars of Rum, the Anatolian lands. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 800 AH, 1398 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 316}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names his Sharh al-Mughni on the principles of jurisprudence, a large commentary in manuscript in the Chester Beatty library (no. 3590); the Mughni it explains is by al-Khabbazi, Umar ibn Muhammad, d. 691 AH, who has his own entry later in al-A'lam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 316}.
