## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Attab ibn Usayd was among the dead of the Battle of the Camel. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 663-664}

## After the battle
Al-Mas'udi relates that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] described him as "the chief of Quraysh", and said words standing over him in which he lamented the dead of the Banu Abd Manaf. Al-Ashtar said to him: how intense is your grief over them; and Ali answered him with his reply. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 663-664}

He is said to have had a hand struck at Mina, or it is said at al-Yamama, where an eagle dropped it with a ring engraved with his name, three days after the Camel. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 663-664}
