## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Shaykh ibn Abi Zakariyya al-Wattasi, known as Abu Abdallah al-Burtuqali, is described by al-Zirikli as the second king of the Wattasid state at Fez. The entry gives his death as 932 AH, 1525 CE, and gives no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His reign
He was pledged to after his father's death in 910 AH, and was active in trying to recover Asila from the Portuguese; he fought them and ruined it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. The Portuguese took the ports of Azemmour and al-Mamura and began to rebuild the town of Anfa, which came to be called al-Dar al-Bayda {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. In his days the Sa'did state appeared in the Sus country and then at Marrakesh; he attacked Marrakesh and could not take it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His death
He remained in power until he died at Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
