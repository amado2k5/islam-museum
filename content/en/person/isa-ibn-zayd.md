## Overview
Isa ibn Zayd ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib. Al-Mas'udi says that he was with Ibrahim ibn Abdallah when he went out from Basra until he was killed at Bakhmara. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 285-286}
