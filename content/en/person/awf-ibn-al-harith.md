## Overview
[[person/awf-ibn-al-harith|Awf ibn al-Harith]], that is Awf ibn Afra', brother of Mu'adh and Mu'awwidh. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1156} Abu Umar said that some named him "Awdh", but "Awf" is sounder. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1156}

## Badr
Ibn Ishaq counts among those who witnessed [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] Mu'adh, Mu'awwidh and Awf, the sons of al-Harith ibn Rifa'a of the Banu al-Najjar. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1156} Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada relates that Awf ibn Afra' said on the day of Badr: "O Messenger of God, what makes the Lord laugh at his servant?" He said: "That he sees him plunge his hand into the battle bare-headed." Awf took off his armour and advanced and fought until he was killed as a martyr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1156} See his brother [[person/muawwidh-ibn-afra|Mu'awwidh ibn Afra']].
