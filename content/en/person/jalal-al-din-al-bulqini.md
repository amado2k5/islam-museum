## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Umar ibn Ruslan, Jalal al-Din al-Bulqini, a Shafi'i jurist who grew up in Cairo and studied fiqh under his father and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15814} He excelled in fiqh, usul, grammar and exegesis, gave fatwas, taught in Egypt and Damascus, and served as judge. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15814} His works include glosses on al-Rawda, unfinished notes on al-Minhaj, versified rules of fiqh and notes on al-Hawi al-Saghir.
