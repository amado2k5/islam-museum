## Summary
Zuber Issa is a British billionaire entrepreneur, the younger half of the partnership that built EG Group from one Blackburn petrol station into a global fuel-and-convenience empire and then bought the Asda supermarket chain. {cite:wikipedia-zuber-issa}

## Formation
Born in 1972 in Blackburn, Lancashire, to Indian-Gujarati Muslim parents, Zuber joined his brother Mohsin in the purchase of a single filling station in 2001. The pair's insight — that a petrol station earns its money in the shop, not the pump — became the formula behind two decades of acquisition-driven expansion. {cite:wikipedia-zuber-issa}

## EG Group
As Euro Garages and then EG Group, the brothers acquired thousands of forecourt and roadside sites across Britain, continental Europe, the United States and Australia, filling them with branded food and retail partnerships. Zuber handled much of the group's operational expansion; at its height the business ran around six thousand sites in ten countries. {cite:muslim500-zuber-issa}

## Asda and after
In 2021 the brothers, with TDR Capital, acquired Asda for about £6.8 billion. In subsequent restructuring Zuber has taken a more independent path — buying the group's UK standalone petrol forecourts to run under his own venture while retaining a stake in Asda — and he remains a major Lancashire-based employer and donor to Muslim community causes. {cite:wikipedia-zuber-issa}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as the operational half of Britain's best-known Muslim business partnership: the brothers who turned the humble petrol station into a multinational enterprise. {cite:muslim500-zuber-issa}
