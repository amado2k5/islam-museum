## Summary
Khawar Qureshi KC is a British barrister of Pakistani origin who has built one of the more international practices at the English Bar — spanning commercial arbitration, public international law and cases between states — and who leads McNair International, a Doha-based chambers of international arbitration specialists. {cite:wikipedia-khawar-qureshi}

## Formation
Called to the Bar of England and Wales, Qureshi took silk — the rank now styled King's Counsel — relatively early in his career, and later served as a deputy High Court judge. He has also taught, holding academic posts including at the University of Cambridge's law faculty, where he lectured on international law. {cite:wikipedia-khawar-qureshi}

## Practice
His casework has ranged across investor-state and commercial arbitration, boundary and treaty disputes, and appearances before international tribunals. From Qatar he built McNair International into a chambers focused on high-value cross-border disputes, working across jurisdictions from London to the Gulf and South Asia. The Muslim 500 profiles him as among the most successful Muslim advocates in the English legal system — a silk who argued, it notes, at the highest levels of international litigation. {cite:muslim500-khawar-qureshi}

## Standing
He is frequently listed among the leading Muslim lawyers in the common-law world: a figure whose career joined the traditions of the English Bar to the arbitration markets of the Gulf and Asia. {cite:muslim500-khawar-qureshi}
