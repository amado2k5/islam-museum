## Overview
Abu al-Fath Nasr ibn Ibrahim al-Maqdisi, a Shafi'i jurist and ascetic who settled in Damascus. He wrote the book "al-Hujja ala Tarik al-Mahajja", which contains the principles of religion on the rules of the people of hadith and the Sunna. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 824}

## His link with al-Nawawi
Ibn Rajab, in his takhrij of the forty-first hadith, relates that [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]] said: "We have related it in the book of "al-Hujja" with a sound chain." This shaykh is the one meant by the book "al-Hujja". The book refers to "Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'" 19/136. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 824}
