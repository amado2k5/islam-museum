## Overview
Utban ibn Malik, an Ansari from the Banu Salim ibn Awf of the Khazraj, of the [[group/banu-salim-ibn-awf|Banu Salim ibn Awf]]. He built the fortress of "al-Muzdalif", and afterwards the fortress passed to Malik ibn al-Mujalan al-Salami {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.211}.

Malik said of it: "I built al-Muzdalif for the wars" {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.211}.
