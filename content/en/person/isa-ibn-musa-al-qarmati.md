## Overview
Isa ibn Musa al-Qarmati, one of the Qarmatians who appeared in the Sawad of Iraq. A large crowd gathered around him at Ayn al-Tamr and its surroundings. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 137}

## Kufa
He marched to Kufa, encamped outside it, collected the land tax, and removed the officials from the Sawad. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 137}
