## Summary
Shazrina binti Azman, known as Mizz Nina, is a Malaysian former singer and television host turned dawah and humanitarian figure, born in Kuala Lumpur on 1 July 1980, and a co-founder of Dopstv, an Islamic lifestyle channel. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

She first gained renown as a hip-hop musician, singer-songwriter, rapper and dancer, but retired from singing after her hajj in 2013 to focus on her fashion and production work and her humanitarian and dawah work. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

## Early life
She was born in Kuala Lumpur, the daughter of Azman Hashim, chairman of the AmBank Group, as the English article records. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

## Beginnings and singing career
In 1997 she formed First Born Troopz with Fiquetional, with whom she co-founded the Malaysian hip-hop group the Teh Tarik Crew in 1999; the group's first album received two AIM nominations in 2002 and its second two more in 2004, and the group disbanded in 2007. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

She released her first solo album, *What You Waiting For*, in July 2010, and her second, *Takeover*, in 2012, collaborating with the American rapper Flo Rida on its title song, and in 2013 she released the single "Around The World" featuring Jay Park. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

She hosted television programmes on channels including 8TV and Channel V, and in 2012 hosted an episode of The MTV Iggy Show in Times Square, New York. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

## The hajj and retirement
She performed the hajj in 2013, and on her return appeared with a more modest image that surprised reporters and netizens, and announced her retirement from singing; The Star reported in January 2014 her words that she would only sing for a female audience now, and that she wanted to focus on her boutique and production company. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

## Projects and initiatives
In 2016 she and two partners founded DOPS (Deen of Peace Studios) TV — Dopstv — which has produced a number of TV series and talk shows. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

She is a fashion designer for her own women's labels Mizz Demeanor and Madeena Clothing, and through her production company Big Fish Entertainment produced a 13-episode children's programme, *The Cici & Caca Show*. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}

She developed the QalbyApp, a Muslim lifestyle app providing readings, spiritual video lessons and prayer collections, launched after about two years of development just before Covid broke, as British Muslim Magazine reported in 2023. {cite:british-muslim-magazine-qalbyapp-2023}

She chairs the Min Qalby Foundation, a humanitarian organisation; her signed message on the foundation's site says she has been involved with humanitarian causes since 2014, in the midst of her spiritual change, that the work led her from Gaza to Jordan, Turkey, Syria, Lebanon, Thailand, Bangladesh, Indonesia and Africa, and that the idea of Min Qalby was born after a conversation with an NGO in Palu, Indonesia, during the 2019 earthquake and tsunami; the foundation describes its focus as social and business empowerment, education and humanitarian efforts. {cite:minqalby-foundation-about}

The English article also records her involvement with organisations assisting the underprivileged, including Cinta Syria Malaysia, 1moment4them and Feeding The Needy. {cite:wikipedia-mizz-nina-en}
