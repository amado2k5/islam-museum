## Summary
The fifth of the Weepers, of Muzayna; Ibn Ishaq names him among those who asked the Prophet for mounts on the Tabuk expedition when he found nothing to carry them on. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him fifth of the Weepers, of Muzayna, among the seven men who came asking the Prophet for mounts — he said he found nothing to carry them on, and they turned back with eyes overflowing with tears. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: of Muzayna. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq said: "some people say: rather he is Abdullah ibn Amr al-Muzani" (p. 161).
- The editors note a disagreement on some of the Weepers' names (note p. 161).
