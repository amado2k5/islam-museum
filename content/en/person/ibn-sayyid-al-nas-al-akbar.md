## Overview
Ibn Sayyid al-Nas the Elder, the Sevillian khatib-hafiz (597–659 AH); he devoted himself to hadith abundantly and obtained the source-books for himself — "with him the knowledge of hadith in the Maghreb was sealed." He died at Tunis in Rajab {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 306-307}.
