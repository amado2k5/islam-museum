## Overview
Abu Tammam al-Ta'i, a great poet. Al-Suyuti mentions his famous ode on al-Mu'tasim's conquest of Amorium in 223 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.523}

## The Amorium ode
Al-Suyuti relates that when [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] prepared, the astrologers judged to the caliph that the omen was unlucky and that he would be defeated, but the victory came beyond all expectation. One of the lines of Abu Tammam's ode on that conquest begins: "The sword is truer in its reports than books." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.523}

## His works
Al-Mas'udi attributes to him the [[work/hamasa-abu-tammam|Hamasa]]. He says some people called it "the book of al-Khubayya". He also says that Abu Bakr al-Suli wrote a book on his reports and poetry. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.463} The museum's data link the Hamasa to him on the authority of {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
