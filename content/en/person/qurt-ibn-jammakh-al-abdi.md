## Overview
Qurt ibn Jammakh al-Abdi, a fighter in the conquest of Iraq. al-Baladhuri reports that on the day of Mihran he struck until his sword bent. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He was among those who advanced on Rustam on the day of al-Qadisiyya, and it is not known who killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
