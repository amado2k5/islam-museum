## Overview
He is Yazid ibn Ali ibn Jamil al-Shakiri. When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] drew up his army at [[place/hadaqan-sanaa-yemen|Hadaqan]] he and a man of Khath'am called Abu Rifa'a came to him and said to him, O son of, and the account breaks off at the foot of printed p. 205 and continues on p. 206. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} Apple Vision reads the name as printed.
