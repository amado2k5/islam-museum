## Overview
Abu Bakr Ibn al-Labbana of Denia, the poet; Ibn Khallikan gives no death-date nor do the books — but al-Khassa of Abu'l-Hajjaj Yusuf al-Biyasi records Ibn al-Labbana's arrival at Marrakesh in Dhu'l-Hijja 439, praising its king with verses opening "A king whose splendor in his subjects' adornment — shone with the crafts of his age"; Ibn al-Abbar ascribes two verses of an ode to him {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
