## Overview
Ziyad ibn Khalfun, a physician of Qayrawan, a client of the Aghlabids. He died in the year 308 AH according to the encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' (the Common Era conversion is derived) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
