## Summary
A freedman of the Prophet. His name is given variously as Ibrahim, Sinan or Yasar, though the best-known report calls him Aslam. He died in the time of Ali ibn Abi Talib {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 70}.

## Narration from Ali
Al-Sallabi lists Abu Rafi among the Companions who narrated from Ali ibn Abi Talib, citing Ibn Abd al-Barr for the best-known form of his name — Aslam — and for his death in Ali's time {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 70}.

## Note
His name is disputed and the source does not prefer one report over the others; it should be checked before relying on any single form.
