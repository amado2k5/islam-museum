## Summary
Abu Hakim Zafir ibn Jabir ibn Mansur al-Sukkari was a physician of [[place/mosul|Mosul]], according to his entry in the first edition of al-A'lam. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 53}

## Life and work
Al-Zirikli says he moved to [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]] and stayed there until the end of his life, and that he wrote an epistle "on why the animal dies although food replaces what dissolves from it", citing Tabaqat al-Atibba'. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 54}
