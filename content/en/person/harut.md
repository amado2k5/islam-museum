## Who is Harut?
Harut and Marut are the two names in the Babylon magic narrative of surah al-Baqarah, in the verse on those who followed what the devils recited about the kingdom of Solomon, and then 'Solomon did not disbelieve, but the devils disbelieved, teaching people sorcery' [[passage/quran-2-102|al-Baqarah 102]].

## The exegetical difference over their nature
The exegetes differed over who Harut and his companion were: Ibn Kathir and others held them to be two angels sent to Babylon to test the people, tradition adds that they taught sorcery until God punished them, while others said they were jinn or humans; the tafsir literature details 'what the devils recited about the kingdom of Solomon' and the magic books attributed to Solomon's age {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:tanzil-quran}.
