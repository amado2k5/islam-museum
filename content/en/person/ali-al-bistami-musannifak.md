## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad (Majd al-Din) ibn Mas'ud al-Shahrudi al-Bistami, titled Ala al-Din wa-l-Milla and known as Musannifak, was a researcher who wrote in Arabic and Persian, mostly glosses and commentaries. He was born in Khurasan, raised in Herat, then moved to Konya as a teacher and on to Istanbul, where he died; he descended from [[person/fakhr-al-din-al-razi|Fakhr al-Din al-Razi]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 9}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 803 AH and his death as 875 AH, and prints two Common Era years beside them, 1400 and 1370; the second is an evident misprint that does not fit 875 AH, which corresponds to 1470 CE, the year the card carries (derived) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 9}. He explains the nickname Musannifak as coming from his devotion to writing from childhood, the kaf being the Persian diminutive {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 9}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli names: al-Irshad and Sharh al-Misbah, on grammar; Sharh Adab al-Bahth; Hall al-Rumuz, in manuscript, a commentary on an abridgement by al-Suhrawardi on Sufism; al-Hudud wa-l-Ahkam, in manuscript, on Hanafi law; a gloss on al-Mutawwal, in manuscript; Sharh al-Hidaya; a commentary on al-Baghawi's al-Masabih; a gloss on al-Kashshaf; and Mukhtasar al-Muntazam wa-Multaqat al-Multazam, in manuscript, an abridgement of Ibn al-Jawzi's al-Muntazam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 9}.
