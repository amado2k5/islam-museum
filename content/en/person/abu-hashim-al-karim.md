## Summary
Abu Hashim al-Karim divided wealth, broke stinginess and restrained anger. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 511, sequence 512, on pages 146 and 147. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/147}

## Spending by means and by good opinion of God
Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari relates: I heard Abu Hashim say: we looked into this matter, and those who reached its ends were the solitary ones. Abu Hashim also said: some worshippers spend according to what they have, and others spend in good opinion of God; those are the ones. Many names in the chain are garbled in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146}
