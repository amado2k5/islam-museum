## Summary
Son of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]] by her husband Abu Hala; the Prophet's stepson, from whom one of the most famous descriptions of his bearing and sittings was transmitted {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 486}.

## His account
Hind ibn Abi Hala was Khadija's son before the Prophet, and was raised in his house {cite:zad-al-maad}. He related a long description of the Prophet's character and sittings that the books of shama'il preserved: ever joined to cares, always in thought; he had no ease, nor spoke without need; long in silence, he opened speech and closed it with the fullest words, and spoke comprehensive speech, exact, with nothing idle nor lacking {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 486; jami-al-tirmidhi}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet's stepson, who described for people how he sat and spoke — through him history kept the finest details of his bearing and character.
