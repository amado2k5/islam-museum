## Overview
Abu'l-'As al-Hakam b. Muhammad b. Hakam al-Judh'ami — of Judh'am, a Yemen tribe — the Cordoban, musnid of al-Andalus; he made the hajj and heard from Abu Muhammad Ibn Abi Zayd, Ibrahim b. 'Ali al-Tammar and Abu Bakr al-Muhandis, and read on 'Abd al-Mun'im b. Ghulbun; righteous and trustworthy — he would sometimes pray a thousand rak'as a year — abstemious. He died in Rabi' II 447 at over ninety. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 276}
