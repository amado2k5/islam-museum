## Summary
Fatima bint Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir was a devout woman of [[place/medina|Medina]], daughter of the Successor [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-munkadir|Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir]], whom [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] lists second among the known devout women of Medina in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370-371}

## In Sifat al-Safwa
He reports from Ibrahim ibn Muslim al-Qurashi that she spent her days fasting, and when night fell she called out in a mournful voice: the night has stilled, darkness has mingled, every lover has gone to his beloved — asking God in her solitude to free her from the Fire. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370-371}
The editor notes that al-Bayhaqi mentions her in *Shu'ab al-Iman*. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370-371}
