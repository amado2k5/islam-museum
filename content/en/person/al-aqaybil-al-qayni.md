## Overview
Al-Aqaybil ibn Nabhan ibn Khanaf al-Qayni, of Banu al-Qayn ibn Jasr of Quda'a: an Islamic-era poet who died about 85 AH (704 CE). He became known in the days of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya, then was with al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf when the latter marched against Ibn al-Zubayr; he lampooned al-Hajjaj, who sought him, so he fled until he reached the tomb of Marwan ibn al-Hakam and sought refuge there, whereupon Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan granted him safety, wrote to al-Hajjaj not to touch him, and took him under his protection. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.5}

Al-Amidi says he left fine qasidas and short pieces counted among the poems of Banu al-Qayn. His she-camel threw him on one of his journeys and he died; he was dark-skinned. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.5}
