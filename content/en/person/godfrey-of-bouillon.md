## Overview
Godfrey the Frank — commander of the campaign that took Jerusalem in 491. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 396, 400}

## His death
He besieged Acre in 494, where an arrow struck and killed him — as related by the Islamic chroniclers. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 396, 400}
