## Overview
Zaki ibn Najib ibn Ibrahim al-Arsuzi, a Syrian thinker and teacher from Latakia, born around 1900 CE and died in 1968 CE. He is ascribed to the village of Arsuz in the district of Alexandretta {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Writings
He wrote on philosophy, education and Arab national thought {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
