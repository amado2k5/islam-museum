## Summary
Abd al-Rahman al-Haik, Abu Zayd, of Tetouan, a careful jurist. He studied with Shaykh al-Tawdi, Shaykh al-Banani and Shaykh Jasus, and taught Shaykh al-Rahuni and Shaykh al-Mamun Ijlal al-Hasani, judge of Tetouan. The entry says he was alive in 1220 AH and attributes to him Fatawa Ghayat fi al-Tahrir. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7519}
