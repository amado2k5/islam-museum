## Overview
Fatima bint Sa'd ibn Sayl, of the Hadhara of the Azd Shanu'a, the mother of [[person/qusayy-ibn-kilab|Qusayy ibn Kilab]]. Her mother was Huyayy bint Hulayl ibn Habshiyya al-Khuza'iyya. She is thus, among the grandmothers of the Prophet, one of the Yemeni line. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.533}

It is reported from the Prophet that he said: "I am the son of the Fawatim and the Atawik", and she is one of the Fawatim. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.533}
