## Summary

Amir Marjan ibn Abd Allah al-Zafiri, who built the dome of al-Idarus in Aden, where he is buried beside it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}.

The scholar Ba-Harq said of him: "The amir supported by God's grace and care, whose inner sight God opened with the light of faith, whose inner heart God purified from bad belief, who came to be counted among the saints and who had a large share, among rulers and governors, of humility before God and kindness to the poor and needy." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}

He died around the year 923 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 561}.
