## Overview
Al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami of Madhhij. He governed Armenia and Azerbaijan, and al-Baladhuri describes him as a governor who set up measures and weights in Barda'a. Other references say that he was killed in 112 AH in the war with the Khazars. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.386}

## In Armenia
Al-Baladhuri relates that he came to Barda'a, where the differences in its measures and weights were raised to him, and he established them on justice, and made a measure called "al-Jarrahi", by which its people traded until the time of the writer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} Al-Salabi says that he was appointed over Armenia and Azerbaijan in 104 AH to repel the Khazars, that he crossed the river Kur, that the battle of al-Ran took place in 104 AH, and that he took Balanjar. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.440-443} See [[event/jarrah-governorship-armenia|his governorship of Armenia]].

## Tiflis and the Khazars
He sent the safe conduct of Habib ibn Maslama to the people of Tiflis, by a letter in which he imposed on them a hundred dirhams a year from their mills and vineyards. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/treaty-of-tiflis|the treaty of Tiflis]]. Al-Salabi says that he governed Khurasan and Sistan for Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, that Umar wrote to him not to go on raids, and that some four thousand dhimmis embraced Islam at his hand in Khurasan. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.333-343}

## Notes for researchers
- The accounts of the place and dates of his governorship differ between al-Baladhuri and al-Salabi; they have not yet been compared.
