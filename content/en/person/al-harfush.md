## Overview
Al-Harfush, author of the Kitab al-harfush on preaching; a learned, abstemious sufi preacher famed for goodness — the people held exceeding faith in him and he would foretell things that came to pass as he said; he lived beside the Haram at Mecca over thirty years and died at the start of 801. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 15}
