## Overview
Qays ibn Hubayra ibn al-Makshuh al-Muradi, lord of [[group/murad|Murad]] in the time of the Prophet, and a commander in the conquest of Iraq {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230} {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.357}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
When the Prophet's command reached them that the two should set out together to the Qurashi man who says he is a prophet, in order to learn his teaching, [[person/amr-ibn-madikarib|Amr ibn Ma'd Yakrib]] invited Qays to go. Qays refused and mocked his opinion, so Amr rode alone and became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230}. When this reached Qays, he threatened Amr and was harsh with him, saying: "You opposed me and abandoned my opinion." Amr then satirised him in [[work/amr-ibn-madikarib-poem-to-qays-ibn-makshuh|a poem]]; see [[event/delegation-of-zubayd|the arrival of Amr ibn Ma'd Yakrib]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/231}.

## In al-Baladhuri
Umar wrote to Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah, who sent Qays to Sa'd with seven hundred men. It is disputed whether he took part in al-Qadisiyya or came after the Muslims had finished the battle. Umar ruled that if he arrived before the dead were buried, his share would be given to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.357}.

## In Futuh al-Buldan — al-Baladhuri (Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition)
Qays ibn Hubayra of Murad, nicknamed al-Maksuh because he was cauterised on his flank for an ailment he had. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Futuh al-Buldan — al-Baladhuri (Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition): To Yemen
The Prophet sent him to Yemen to fight al-Aswad al-Ansi. He instructed him to win over the Abna', and sent Farwa ibn Musayk with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Futuh al-Buldan — al-Baladhuri (Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition): The killing of al-Aswad
News of the Prophet's death reached him in Yemen. He showed al-Aswad that he was of his opinion, until al-Aswad let him enter Sana'a. Then he won over Fayruz al-Daylami and Dadhawayh, and they agreed to kill al-Aswad and entered on him at dawn. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The report says that Qays slaughtered him, though it is also said that Fayruz killed him and Qays finished him off. See [[event/killing-of-al-aswad-al-ansi|the killing of al-Aswad al-Ansi]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Futuh al-Buldan — al-Baladhuri (Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition): Afterward
He was accused of killing Dadhawayh. When word reached Abu Bakr that he wanted to drive the Abna' out of Sana'a, al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya brought him to Abu Bakr. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] made him swear fifty oaths at the Prophet's pulpit that he had not killed him. Then he released him and sent him to Syria with those who had volunteered for the raid on the Byzantines. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
