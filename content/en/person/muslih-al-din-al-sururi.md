## Overview
Mustafa ibn Sha'ban, a Hanafi of Rum (Anatolia), Muslih al-Din, known as Sururi, was a Turkish scholar who wrote in Arabic, Turkish and Persian. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 897 AH, 1492 CE, and his death as 969 AH, 1562 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 235}.

## Life
He was born in the town of Kelibolu (Gallipoli) and studied under Tashkoprizade and others. He died and was buried in the town of Qasim Pasha in Istanbul {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 235}.

## Works
Among his Arabic books al-Zirikli lists: al-Hawashi al-kubra, in manuscript, and al-Hawashi al-sughra, both glosses on the exegesis of al-Baydawi; a gloss on al-Talwih; a commentary on al-Bukhari that reached nearly its half; a commentary on the Qur'anic story of Joseph (Surat Yusuf), in manuscript; a commentary on the Gulistan, in manuscript; a commentary on al-Amthila al-mukhtalifa, in manuscript; a gloss on the opening of al-Hidaya; and a commentary on al-Misbah in grammar, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 235}.

In Turkish he wrote several books, among them Bahr al-ma'arif, a translation of Aja'ib al-makhluqat, and a translation of Rawd al-rayahin fi hikayat al-salihin {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 235}.
