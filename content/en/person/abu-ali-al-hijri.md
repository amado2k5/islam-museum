## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah b. Muhammad b. 'Ali al-Hijri, of al-Andalus and Murcia — hafiz, ascetic, exemplar, one of the great figures; born 505, he read Sahih al-Bukhari under Shurayh, heard much from Abu'l-Hasan Ibn Mughith, Ibn al-'Arabi and the elders, mastered many sciences, excelled in hadith, lived long and his fame spread. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 787}

## Ceuta
He held Ceuta firm, and the sultan called him to Marrakesh to hear from him — the utmost in probity in this matter. He died in early Safar 591. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 787}
