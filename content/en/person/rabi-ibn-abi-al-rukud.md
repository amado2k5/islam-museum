## Overview
When [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] resolved to leave the village of [[place/al-hajar-najran|al-Hajar]] by night, he ordered his womenfolk to be handed to Rabi' ibn Abi al-Rukud {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. He later sent to him from al-Hisn, and he brought him his womenfolk and children {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.231}.

## Detail
The text gives nothing beyond his name, no lineage and no tribe, so the museum says nothing of them. He appears in [[event/banu-al-harith-besiege-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-at-al-hajar-288]] and [[event/muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-leaves-al-hajar-for-al-hisn-and-the-strife-of-banu-al-harith-288]].
