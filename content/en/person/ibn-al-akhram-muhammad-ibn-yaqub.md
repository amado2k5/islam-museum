## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Yaqub ibn Yusuf al-Shaybani al-Nisaburi, known as Ibn al-Akhram, a hadith scholar from Nishapur and one of the scholars of hadith in the fourth century AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.363}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 250 AH (864 CE) and died in 344 AH (955 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.363} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
