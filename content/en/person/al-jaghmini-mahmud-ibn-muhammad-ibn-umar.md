## Overview
Mahmud ibn Muhammad ibn Umar al-Jaghmini, an astronomer, one of the leading figures of scientific progress in Asia Minor within Islamic civilisation. He is attributed to the village of Jaghmin. He was born in 549 AH (1154 CE) and died in 618 AH (1220 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.332}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
