## Overview
Al-Muhallab ibn Abi Safra al-Azdi, the amir of the people of Basra and the commander of the wars against the Azariqa. He granted his wife Khayra daughter of Damra al-Qushayriyya, the mother of his son Abu Uyayna, the two places Muhallabān and Khayrakān in Basra. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.85-86}

## Governorship of Khurasan
He was appointed over Khurasan in 78 AH by the recommendation of the caliph Abd al-Malik. He sent armies to the lands of the Turkmen in 80 AH, crossed the river Balkh, and raided Samarqand, Bukhara and Tokharistan. He took a fifth of the booty for the treasury and divided the rest among his companions. He died in 82 AH while governor of Khurasan. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.85-86}

Al-Baladhuri has a mention of the grant described above. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
