## Summary

Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i (1933–2001) was a Yemeni hadith scholar, builder of Dar al-Hadith at Dammaj in Sa'da province, among the most famous teachers of hadith sciences in Yemen and the contemporary Salafi world.{cite:wikipedia-muqbil-bin-hadi-al-wadii}

## Life

Born in the Wadi 'Am area of Sa'da, he received early learning in hadith at Zabid and Sa'da, studied in Medina and travelled for scholarship. He founded Dar al-Hadith at Dammaj in 1979 and students came from across the world.{cite:wikipedia-muqbil-bin-hadi-al-wadii}

## Works and method

His books include Al-Sahih al-Musnad mimma laysa fi al-Sahihayn, Al-Jami' al-Sahih and Asbab al-Nuzul; thousands of Yemen's and the world's hadith students graduated under him. His method rests on isnad, authentication and verification against the hadith books.{cite:wikipedia-muqbil-bin-hadi-al-wadii}

## Standing and debate

He is remembered as a renewer of hadith science in Yemen and a teacher of hadith scholars; Dar al-Hadith at Dammaj — closed after the 2014 crisis — is his best-known institute. The museum records the standing without sectarian evaluation.{cite:wikipedia-muqbil-bin-hadi-al-wadii}
