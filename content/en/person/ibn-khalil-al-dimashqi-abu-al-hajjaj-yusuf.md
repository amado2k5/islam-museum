## Overview
Shams al-Din Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf ibn Khalil ibn Qaraja ibn Abdallah, of Damascus and then of Aleppo. He was born in 555 AH (1160 CE) and died in 648 AH (1250 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
