## Overview
Abd al-Karim ibn Ahmad ibn Musa, Ibn Tawus al-Alawi al-Hasani, was an Imami (Twelver Shi'i) jurist and genealogist, and al-Zirikli labels him so. He was born in al-Ha'ir, grew up in Baghdad and died in al-Kazimiyya. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 648 AH, 1250 CE, and his death as 693 AH, 1294 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote books, among them al-Shaml al-Manzum fi Musannafi al-Ulum and Farhat al-Ghari, in manuscript, in the Dar al-Kutub {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}. The entry is short and says nothing more.
