## Overview
Tarrad ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Hashimi al-Abbasi al-Zaynabi, Abu al-Fawaris, was the chief of the nuqaba' and the musnid of Iraq in his time, the highest-ranked man at the caliph's court. He died in 491 AH (1098 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.224}

He dictated many majalis and held the niqaba of the Abbasids at Basra. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.224}
