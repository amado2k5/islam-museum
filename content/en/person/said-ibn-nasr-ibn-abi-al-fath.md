## Summary
Abu 'Uthman Sa'id b. Nasr, known as Ibn Abi al-Fath, was one of the Cordoban teachers from whom [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] transmitted most, and the source of the Muwatta' recension on which he built [[work/al-tamhid-ibn-abd-al-barr|al-Tamhid]]. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 14} {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 201} The Tamhid's editor gives his dates as 315–395 AH, as the copy used reads them. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 14}

## Family
His father was among the senior clients favoured by [[person/abd-al-rahman-iii-al-nasir|'Abd al-Rahman al-Nasir]], as al-Humaydi reports from Ibn 'Abd al-Barr, who mentioned and praised him. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 14}

## What Ibn 'Abd al-Barr transmitted from him
He transmitted from him [[person/qasim-ibn-asbagh|Qasim b. Asbagh's]] *al-Mujtaba*, from Qasim. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 14} Sa'id also gave him Yahya b. Yahya al-Laythi's recension of the Muwatta' by dictation, reading from his own copy while Ibn 'Abd al-Barr followed in his, from Qasim b. Asbagh and Wahb b. Masarra, from [[person/muhammad-ibn-waddah|Muhammad b. Waddah]], from Yahya, from Malik — the first of the routes to the Muwatta' that Ibn 'Abd al-Barr lists in the introduction to the Tamhid. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 201}
