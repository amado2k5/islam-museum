## Summary
One of the grandees of pre-Islamic Quraysh; he gathered his people on Fridays — hence 'the day of Ka'b' — telling them what he had read in the earlier scriptures about a prophet who would come from his loins, and bidding them follow him {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48}.

## His place in the lineage
He would say to the gathered Quraysh: hear, understand, retain and obey, and you shall prosper — for God will honour you with a man who comes from my loins whose affair grows great and whose name is raised high {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## For younger visitors
Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
