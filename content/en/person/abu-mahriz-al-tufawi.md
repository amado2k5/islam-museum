## Summary
Abu Mahriz al-Tufawi was diligent in striving, and reached the earlier generation. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 491 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 138 and 139. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/138}

## The discerning and the world
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Barjalani relates, through Awn ibn Hamara, the saying of Abu Mahriz: the discerning have the highest station in both abodes; they sought height through high deeds, and knew that a thing is reached only by more than it, so they gave the most they had, and for God's sake gave their souls in hope of rest with Him and of relief on a day when the seeker is not disappointed. He then says: people were absorbed in the world and gained from it no more than their share, and turned away from the hereafter and its aim, while servants hope to save their own souls. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/138}

## A second saying: the guardian of thoughts and breaths
[[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a second notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 525 and sequence 526, on pages 158 and 159. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/158} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/159}

Abu Bakr ibn Ubayd relates from Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Barjalani, from Awn ibn Hamara, that Abu Mahriz al-Tufawi said: [the opening is not in the scan; the text resumes with a phrase of warning] blessed are the righteous who obeyed Me among My creatures, who gave Me the sight of their hearts in contentment, and showed Me in their inner selves truthfulness and steadfastness; blessed are they; what they have with Me of reward when they come to Me from their graves: the light runs before them, the angels surround them, until I give them what they hope from My mercy. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/159}
