## Summary
Al-Malik Sanjar Shah ibn Ghazi ibn Mawdud ibn Atabeg Zangi, lord of the 'Umariyya Jazira. His son Ghazi killed him in 605; they had sworn to him, but the next day his father's retainers leapt on the son, killed him and crowned his brother al-Malik al-Mu'azzam. Sanjar Shah was ill-conducted and tyrannical. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 23}
