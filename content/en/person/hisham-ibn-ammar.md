## Overview

Hisham ibn 'Ammar ibn Nusayr al-Sulami of Damascus is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/abdallah-ibn-amir-al-yahsibi|'Abd Allah ibn 'Amir al-Yahsubi]], with [[person/ibn-dhakwan|Ibn Dhakwan]]. He was born in 153 AH and died in 245 AH, or 244 by another report. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 154} Ibn al-Jazari describes him as the scholar of Damascus, its preacher, reader, hadith transmitter and mufti, reliable, careful and just. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 155}

## Chain and what scholars said of him

Hisham read on Abu Sulayman Ayyub ibn Tamim, Abu al-Dahhak 'Irak ibn Khalid, Suwayd ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz and Sadaqa ibn Khalid, all of whom reach Yahya ibn al-Harith al-Dhimari and through him 'Abd Allah ibn 'Amir. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 154} Ibn al-Jazari quotes al-Daraqutni calling him truthful and of high standing, and 'Abdan reporting him saying that in twenty years he had never repeated a sermon. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 155} Among those who transmitted his reading was al-Hulwani, who travelled to Qalun and mastered both Qalun's and Hisham's riwayas. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 123}
