## Overview
Nushajan ibn Jasnasima, given in al-Tabari as Anushajan. He was lord of the castle of Nahr al-Mar'a, and Shurayh ibn Amir made a peace agreement with him. The lady of the castle was the daughter of his paternal uncle, Kaman Dar bint Narsa {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

The date and place of the event are not fixed in the museum's data; an additional source is needed.
