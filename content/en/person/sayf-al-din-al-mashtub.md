## Summary
Sayf al-Din al-Mashtub, father of 'Imad al-Din Ibn al-Mashtub, was stationed by Sultan Salah al-Din at Acre together with Baha' al-Din Qaraqush when he feared the Franks for it; he remained there until the Franks besieged him, took it and captured him. When he escaped them he reached the Sultan at Jerusalem on Thursday at the start of Jumada II 588; Salah al-Din rose to him and embraced him. None among the emirs of the Salahid state equalled him in standing and rank — they called him the Great Emir, emir of the Kurds and their chief. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
