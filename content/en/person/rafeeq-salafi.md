## Overview
Rafeeq Salafi is a dawah speaker who delivers his speeches in the Malayalam language through his official YouTube channel; the channel's description states that it is his official channel and that its purpose is to promote Islamic dawah and "the true message of Islam". {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

His channel had about 218,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

His video descriptions link his Facebook page and his Telegram channel, named "Samakalikam" (contemporary issues), the official outlets through which he publishes his new material. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

## Career and work
His output is headed by replies to atheism and the rationalist current in Kerala: his channel publishes recorded debates and rebuttals aimed at freethinkers such as E. A. Jabbar and M. N. Karassery, with titles addressing the existence of God, prophethood, the waiting period of women and the relation of science and religion. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

Alongside this he lectures on worship and jurisprudence — the rulings of prayer, zakat, the funeral prayer and water used for ablution — and gives lessons on monotheism and the stories of the prophets addressed to a general Malayalam-speaking audience. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

The channel also documents a series of clips recorded while travelling in Saudi Arabia, covering Qassim, Khaybar and Jubba. No trusted source establishes his permanent place of residence, so the museum records none. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of different positions reported by the sources, not as a verdict of the museum on them.

His work presents itself as Salafi-oriented dawah, a description that appears in the name he preaches under and in his channel's self-definition; "Salafi" here is a self-declared orientation and method, not an independent attestation of a legal school, and the museum found no independent source naming a school for him, so no school is recorded. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}

His public argument with rationalists and atheists in Kerala is documented in his channel's titles and published content, including his calls for live debates with opponents; this is the declared material of his preaching, on which the museum passes no judgement. {cite:youtube-rafeeq-salafi}
