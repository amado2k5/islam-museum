## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn 'Abdallah ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Bukayr, Abu 'Abdallah the money-changer; born 327; he heard Isma'il al-Saffar, Abu 'Umar, Ibn al-Sammak, al-Najad, al-Khuldi and Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i; Ibn Shahin, al-Azhari and al-Tanukhi transmitted from him and al-Daraqutni heard on him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}

## His memory
Al-Azhari said: I used to attend him with great ajza' before him and he would say: which do you prefer — name me a matn from these hadiths and I tell you its isnad, or name me an isnad and I tell you its matn? I would name him the texts and he would give the isnads from memory; Ibn Abi'l-Fawaris spoke of him as... {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}
