## Summary
Muhammad ibn Hasan al-Laqani, Nasir al-Din, Abu Abd Allah, of Egypt, a Maliki jurist and usuli. After his brother Shams al-Din died, the leadership of learning in Egypt passed to him, and he was consulted from every region. He wrote glosses on al-Tawdih and a gloss on al-Mahalli's commentary on Jam' al-jawami'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1641}
