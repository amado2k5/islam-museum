## Overview
Abu Haritha al-Nahri was the keeper of the treasuries of al-Mahdi's money. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.302-303}

## The story of the keys
Al-Mas'udi relates that when al-Mahdi distributed his father's money, Abu Haritha threw the keys before him and said: "What is the meaning of keys to treasuries that have been emptied?" When the money came in, and al-Mahdi was busy with checking it, al-Mahdi said to him: "You are a stupid bedouin." He answered that when an event occurs it does not wait until one is directed to bring out the money. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.302-303}

## Notes for researchers
- The story is from al-Mas'udi alone; a second source is needed.
