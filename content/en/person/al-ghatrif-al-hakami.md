## Overview
"Al-Ghatrif" is named after "Ibrahim ibn Ali" in the phrase "Ibrahim ibn Ali and al-Ghatrif, the two Hakamis" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}.

## Detail
He wrote with Ibrahim ibn Ali to al-Hadi, offering money, troops and the district {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}. The text gives his descent only as "al-Hakami" and nothing else, and the museum does not equate him with any man of that name known from other books. See [[event/the-hakami-letters-and-the-oaths-and-abu-al-qasim-sent-to-khaywan-292|the event]] and [[person/ibrahim-ibn-ali-al-hakami|Ibrahim ibn Ali al-Hakami]].
