# Pierre Omidyar

Pierre Morad Omidyar (born Parviz Morad Omidyar, 21 June 1967) is a French-born Iranian-American billionaire, software engineer and philanthropist, the founder of eBay. The English and Persian articles and Wikidata give his birth on 21 June 1967 in Paris; the Arabic article alone gives 12 June, an apparent transposition, so the date is carried as debated with the majority reading followed. {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-en} {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-fa} {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-ar} {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-ur} {cite:wikidata-omidyar}

## Early life and education

Omidyar was born in Paris to Iranian parents who had come to France for higher education — his mother Elahé Mir-Djalali an academic linguist, his father Cyrus Omidyar a surgeon — and his maternal grandfather was General Mahmud Mir-Djalali of the Imperial Iranian Army. The family immigrated to the United States when he was a child; he attended Punahou School in Honolulu and St. Andrew's Episcopal School in Potomac, Maryland, and graduated from Tufts University in computer science in 1988. {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-en} {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-fa}

## Career

After working at Claris, Omidyar co-founded the pen-computing startup Ink Development in 1991, later rebranded as the e-commerce company eShop and acquired by Microsoft in 1996. In 1995, aged 28, he wrote the code for an online person-to-person auction venue and launched it on Labor Day, 4 September 1995, as Auction Web on his own website; the business grew explosively, was renamed eBay in 1997, and went public in September 1998, making him a billionaire. He served as eBay's chairman from 1998 to 2015, stepped down from the board in 2020, and retains the title of director emeritus. In 2010 he launched the investigative news service Honolulu Civil Beat, and in 2013 he created First Look Media, publisher of The Intercept. {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-en} {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-ar}

## Philanthropy

In 2004 Omidyar and his wife Pamela founded Omidyar Network, a philanthropic investment firm that has committed more than $992 million to for-profit and nonprofit organisations working on property rights, governance, education and financial inclusion; the Omidyar Group and, from 2018, Luminate group his further ventures. In 2010 he joined Bill Gates and Warren Buffett as a signatory of the Giving Pledge, declaring his intention to give away most of his wealth during his lifetime, and he has bankrolled organisations campaigning against the power of the large technology companies. {cite:wikipedia-omidyar-en}
