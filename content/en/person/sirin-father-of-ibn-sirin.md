## Overview
Sirin, father of [[person/muhammad-ibn-sirin|Muhammad ibn Sirin]]. He had brothers named Yahya, Anas and Ma'bad, and Ma'bad was the eldest. al-Baladhuri reports that they were clients of Anas ibn Malik al-Ansari, taken captive from the church of Ayn al-Tamr in the time of Khalid ibn al-Walid {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.345-347}.

## Among the captives
al-Baladhuri adds a report that Sirin was from Jarjaraya, and had been visiting some of his relatives when he was taken with them in the church. The account of the event is in [[event/siege-of-ayn-al-tamr|the siege of Ayn al-Tamr]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.345-347}.
