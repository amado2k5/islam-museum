Haram ibn Milhan ibn Khalid al-Ansari al-Najjari al-Khazraji, a brave Badri Companion, uncle of Anas ibn Malik. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He went out with the seventy Reciters in the Bi'r Ma'una detachment in Safar of year four of the hijra, sent ahead carrying the Messenger of God's letter to Amir ibn al-Tufayl calling him and his people to Islam; Amir did not look at the letter but signalled a man of his followers, who speared him in the back until it came out of his chest. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Seeing the blood gush, he kept wiping his face and head with it, crying in full faith: "God is greatest — I have triumphed, by the Lord of the Ka'ba!" — and these words of his moved his killer Jabbar ibn Salama al-Kilabi to Islam after he pondered the meaning of so great a triumph at death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
