## Summary
A Companion from Hadramawt, whose father settled in Mecca where al-Ala was born; the Prophet made him governor of Bahrain with the collection of alms and gave him a writing on the rates of zakat for camels, cattle, sheep, fruit and goods; Abu Bakr and Umar confirmed him; al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid and Abu Hurayra transmitted from him; he is said to be the first Muslim to ride the sea for a raid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7842}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 6204: "al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami, whose name was Abd Allah ibn Imad ibn Akbar ibn Rabi'a ibn Malik ibn Uwayf al-Hadrami; his father had settled in Mecca and allied with Harb ibn Umayya, the father of Abu Sufyan, and al-Ala had several brothers, among them Amr ibn al-Hadrami." {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1051}
