Al-Ala' ibn Abdullah ibn Imad al-Hadrami, a notable Companion, jurist and commander of the Muslims; his lineage goes back to Hadramawt and he was an ally of Harb ibn Umayya at Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Late in the sixth hijri year the Prophet sent him with his letter to al-Mundhir ibn Sawa al-Tamimi, lord of Bahrayn, calling him and his people to Islam; al-Mundhir became Muslim, and with him the Arabs of Bahrayn, while its Magians and Jews entered into jizya and covenant. {cite:zad-al-maad}

The Prophet gave him the emirate of Bahrayn and the collecting of its alms; he was among the Companions' great governors and one of those famed for answered prayer and for a manifest marvel of crossing the sea in the Ridda wars; he remained governor of Bahrayn until he died in year twenty-one of the hijra in Umar's caliphate. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
