## Summary
William Oncken — pronounced 'On' in the lesson — the management thinker who set out the 'monkey laws' theory of delegation in the Harvard Business Review; al-Suwaidan recounts that publishers rejected his book until he turned to Ken Blanchard, who wrote the foreword, and the book came out jointly as [[work/one-minute-manager-meets-the-monkey|The One Minute Manager Meets the Monkey]] and spread and made him famous {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 00:28:30}.
