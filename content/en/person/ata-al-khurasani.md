## Overview
Ata ibn Abi Muslim al-Khurasani, whom al-Shirazi mentions among the jurists of Khurasan. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}

## His birth and death
Al-Shirazi says that he was born in 50 AH and died in 135 AH, and that he was a "wanderer". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}

## Notes
- The only source is al-Shirazi's "Classes of the Jurists" and needs an additional source. The Gregorian conversion is derived.
