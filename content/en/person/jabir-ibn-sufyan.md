## Summary
Son of Sufyan ibn Ma'mar and Hasana; he emigrated with them to Abyssinia and perished in Umar's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him son of Sufyan ibn Ma'mar alongside his brother Janada, saying he perished in Umar's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
