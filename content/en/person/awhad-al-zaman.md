## Summary
Awhad al-Zaman Abu'l-Barakat Hibat Allah b. 'Ali b. Malkan, the famous philosopher; author of *al-Mu'tabar* on wisdom, which al-Qifti counted among his grandest books; between him and Amin al-Dawla Ibn al-Tilmidh there was rivalry and famous sessions. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His conversion and affliction
He had been a Jew, then became Muslim late in life; leprosy struck him, and he treated himself by setting starved vipers upon his body — they bit him thoroughly, he was cured of leprosy but went blind, and his story in that is famous. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
