## Overview
Mardanshah ibn Zadhan Farrukh, the Persian scribe of al-Hajjaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.422}

## The register
Al-Baladhuri relates that when al-Hajjaj appointed Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman, he moved the register into Arabic. Mardanshah asked him what he should do with *dahawiyya* and *shashawiyya*. Salih said that he would write them as "ten and a half a tenth". He asked him about "wayd", and Salih said he would write that too. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.422}

Mardanshah cursed him, saying that God had cut off his root from the world as He had cut off the root of Persian. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.422}
