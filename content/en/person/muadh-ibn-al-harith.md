## Overview
Mu'adh ibn al-Harith ibn Rifa'a ibn al-Harith ibn Sawwad ibn Malik ibn Ghanm ibn Malik ibn al-Najjar, al-Ansari al-Najjari; his brothers were 'Awf, Rafi' and Rifa'a, their mother 'Afra' bint 'Ubayd, and his children were 'Ubaydallah, al-Harith, 'Awf and Salma. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3873}

## Companionship
He witnessed both 'Aqaba pledges, and the Messenger of God paired him in brotherhood with Ma'mar ibn al-Harith al-Jumahi, one of the Badr veterans. He died in 35 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3873}
