## Overview
Giustiniani, commander of Constantinople's defenders in the 857/1453 siege, in al-Salabi's account.

## Holding the breach
He fought to exhaustion over the breach the Ottoman cannon opened at the Lycus valley on 18 April — a narrow gap thick with arrows and missiles {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 96}.

## Wounded, withdrawn
In the final assault he was gravely wounded, left the field and fled aboard a ship; the emperor Constantine took command of the defence himself in his place {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 110}.
