## Summary
Najm al-A'imma al-Bari'i: a learned jurist and preacher, whose title Bari' (excelling) is given to one who excels in a field of learning. He died at Jurjaniyya in Khwarazm in 645 AH, according to the book. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7164}
