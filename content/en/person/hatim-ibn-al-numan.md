## Overview
Hatim ibn al-Numan al-Bahili, whom Ibn Amir sent to Merv. According to al-Baladhuri, he made a settlement with its marzban for two million two hundred thousand dirhams. It is also said one million dirhams and two hundred thousand jarib of wheat and barley, and it is said one million and one hundred thousand ounces. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
