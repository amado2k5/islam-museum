## Summary
Chief of [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] of [[group/banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] in the truce years; he led the [[event/khuzaa-bakr-clash-al-watir|night attack of al-Watir]] on [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] seeking an old vengeance, and refused to let it go when his men appealed the bounds of the sanctuary {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}.

## His leadership of the night attack
Nawfal ibn Mu'awiya al-Dayli went out among Banu al-Dil — not all of Banu Bakr followed him — and surprised Khuza'a by night at [[place/al-watir|al-Watir]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}. When Banu al-Dil pursued Khuza'a into the sanctuary his companions said: Nawfal, you are in the sanctuary — he answered: "your God, your God, my people! Will you take your revenge from the polytheists while the polytheists bar you from taking it? Win your revenge!" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/32}

## For young visitors
A chief who wanted revenge for his old kin; when the time of peace came he would not wait, and led a night assault on his neighbours until they entered the sanctuary — and his error began the end of the whole war.
