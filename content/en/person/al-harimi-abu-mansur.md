## Overview
Abu Mansur al-Harimi, the hafiz-muhaddith of the Hanbalis; he heard much at Baghdad from Ibn al-Akhdar and others, at Harran from the hafiz al-Ruhawi, and at Aleppo and Damascus from Abu'l-Yumn al-Kindi; Ibn Nuqta said he heard in Syria and the Jazira and read much; Ibn Rajab said he has a large history, fawa'id, ajza' and letters to al-Samiri, author of the Mustaw'ab, remonstrating with him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 227}

## The Mustansiriyya
Ibn al-Sa'i records that when al-Mustansir built his madrasa he appointed two shaykhs at its dar al-hadith: one this Abu Mansur, the other Ibn al-Najjar the Shafi'i, author of the history. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 227}
