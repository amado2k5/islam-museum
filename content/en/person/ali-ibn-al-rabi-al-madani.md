## Overview
A man of the nisba al-Madani, whom the Zaydi book describes as one of the people of corruption and sedition against the imam al-Hadi. Al-Hadi ordered him held in irons for twelve days at al-Hijr (294 AH).

## Detail
The book says that on the second day after al-Hadi's arrival at al-Hijr he was thrown into prison and irons and held there twelve days, after which Banu al-Harith came to al-Hadi. A marginal note in the manuscript also calls him the one who caused corruption. All the charges come from the narrator; the museum gives no verdict on the man, and nothing more about him is known from these pages.
