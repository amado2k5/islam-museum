## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Salih ibn Muslim al-Ajli al-Kufi was a Qur'an reciter and the father of the hafiz Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Ajli, author of the Tarikh. Al-Safadi lists him in al-Wafi bi-l-Wafayat; he was born in Kufa in 141 AH and died in 211 AH, and al-Safadi adds that his death is also said to fall around the twenties after the two hundreds. The Common Era year for the death, 826, is derived {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 pp. 112-113}.

## Study and teachers
He recited the Qur'an under Hamza al-Zayyat, and al-Safadi says he was the last to recite to him before Hamza's death {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 pp. 112-113}. He narrated from Hamza, Abu Bakr al-Nahshali, al-Hasan ibn Salih ibn Hayy, Abd al-Rahman ibn Thabit ibn Thawban, Fudayl ibn Marzuq, Zuhayr ibn Mu'awiya, Hammad ibn Salama, Asbat ibn Nasr, Shabib ibn Shayba, Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Majishun and others {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 pp. 112-113}.

## Students
Those who narrated from him include, as it is said, al-Bukhari, his son Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Ajli, Ahmad ibn Abi Uzra, Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Baladhuri the scribe, Bishr ibn Musa, Abu Zur'a al-Razi, Abu Hatim, Muhammad ibn Ghalib Tamtam, Ibrahim al-Harbi and many more {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 pp. 112-113}.

## Judgements of the scholars
Al-Safadi reports that Yahya ibn Ma'in called him reliable, that Abu Hatim called him truthful, and that Ibn Hibban wrote in his book of reliable narrators that his hadith was sound {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 pp. 112-113}.
