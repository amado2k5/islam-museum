## Summary
Abu Zakariya Yahya ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hasan al-Sarraj, an Andalusian of Fez, a jurist, traditionist, memoriser, philologist and poet; the entry gives his death as 805 AH. He studied with Abu al-Barakat ibn al-Hajj al-Balfiqi, 'Abd al-Nur and Ibn Abbad, among others, headed the study of hadith in his time, and wrote a catalogue of his teachers and books. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7511} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7512}
