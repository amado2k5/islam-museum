## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Sabiq ibn Abd Allah ibn Sabiq al-Umawi, also given as Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Sabiq al-Bayri; a Maliki jurist who knew the school by heart. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949} He narrated from Sa'id ibn Tamir, Sulayman ibn Nasr and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14949}
