## Overview
Muhammad Abu al-Wafa al-Ghunaymi al-Taftazani, born around 1930 CE in Kafr al-Ghunaymi in the governorate of al-Sharqiyya in Egypt. He studied Islamic philosophy and Sufism, and died in 1994 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 523}

Both dates rest on a single source and need an additional source.
