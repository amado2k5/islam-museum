## Overview
Diya' al-Din Abdallah ibn Ahmad al-Malaqi al-Shashshab, known as Ibn al-Baytar, author of *al-Mufradat fi al-Adwiya* (On Simple Medicines). Ibn al-Imad in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* says that knowledge of plants, their properties, their uses and their habitats ended with him, and that he entered the service of al-Kamil and then of his son al-Salih, and was a chief in the lands of Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 234}

He died in Damascus in Sha'ban 646 AH, according to the same source. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 234}

For another biography of a scholar of this name, see [[person/ibn-al-bitar-abu-muhammad-abd-allah-ibn-ahmad|Ibn al-Bitar Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Ahmad]]. It needs an additional source to confirm whether the two are one person.
