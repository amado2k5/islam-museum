## Overview
He is Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya, who came to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/mankath-yemen|Mankath]] with a dense army, and al-Hadi set out with Abu al-Ushayra {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}; he then asked leave to stay at [[place/thath-yemen|Thath]] to mend an estate of his there, and was given it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. The record of the entry into Sana'a, built on p. 18, says he met al-Hadi at Jayshan in hearing and obedience, and the museum does not reconcile the two reports. The narrator gives neither his full name nor his descent.
