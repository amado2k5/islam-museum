## Overview
Abu al-Khayr Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Armiyuni, a scholar of mathematics and astronomy in Egypt. He was born in Armant in western Egypt, and the attribution "al-Azhari" is thought likely because he studied at al-Azhar. He died in 1019 AH (around 1610 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
