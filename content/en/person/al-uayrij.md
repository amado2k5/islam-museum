## Overview
Ibn Abd al-Hakam says that the Qasr, the fortress of Babylon, was governed by a Roman called al-U'ayrij, who served under [[person/al-muqawqis|al-Muqawqis]]. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 85} The editor identifies him in a footnote as the commander George. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 85}

## After al-Muqawqis left
When al-Muqawqis and the Coptic notables withdrew to the Island during [[event/siege-of-babylon-fortress-640|the siege]], some elders of Egypt claimed that al-U'ayrij stayed in the fortress and later left in ships moored to it. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 96}
