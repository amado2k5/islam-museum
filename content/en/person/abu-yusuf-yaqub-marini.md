## Overview
Abu Yusuf Ya'qub al-Marini, that is Ya'qub ibn Abd al-Haqq, ruler of the Maghrib and king of Fez. Al-Maqqari says he crossed to al-Andalus to support its people against the Franks, and some said that Muslims had not been helped since al-Uqab until Ya'qub al-Marini came. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.389}

## In al-Andalus
Al-Maqqari relates that he fought a Christian king called "Dhuntuh", and it is said he killed forty thousand of his army. Among the lands of al-Andalus he held Randa, al-Jazira al-Khadra, Tarif, Jabal Tariq and others. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.389}

## After him
When he died, his son Yusuf succeeded him. Al-Adhfunsh, king of the Christians, sought refuge with him, kissed his hand and pledged his crown to him, and Yusuf helped him recover his kingdom, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.389}

The dates of his life and rule that are not given here need an additional source.
