## Overview
Taj al-Din Ishaq ibn Ibrahim ibn Ahmad al-Tadmuri, a scholar and jurist whose biography appears in the books of the Maliki classes. He died in 833 AH (1430 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
