## Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala al-Aslami

Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala ibn Amr ibn Sanna, Abu Harmala, al-Aslami. He related from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, Handhala ibn Ali al-Aslami, Amr ibn Shu'ayb and others; al-Thawri, al-Awza'i, Malik, Sulayman ibn Bilal and Hatim ibn Isma'il related from him. Muhammad ibn Amr said he was trustworthy and prolific in hadith; Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy and said he errs; Ishaq reported from Ibn Ma'in that he was sound, while Abu Hatim said his hadith is written down but not relied on. The heading gives death 145 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5503} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5504}
