## Overview
Urwa ibn Qatiba carried the letter of [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Ibn al-Zubayr]] to Abd Allah ibn Khazim, appointing him over Khurasan, six months after Ibn Khazim's agreement with Sulayman ibn Marthad on arbitration {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
