## Summary
The imam and hafiz Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. al-Husayn al-Zarakani, known as Alb Arslan; an imam, hafiz and trustee on whom al-Silafi selected a hundred parts. He lived 139 years, born 483, and died in 519. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 539}
