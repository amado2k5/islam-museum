## Overview
A Sindhi prince, a paternal cousin of the king [[person/dahir|Dahir]] of Sind. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 112}

## His Islam
The book says that he became Muslim, together with others of the rulers, princes and commanders, during [[event/conquest-of-sind-89|the conquest of Sind]] at the hands of Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Thaqafi. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 112}
