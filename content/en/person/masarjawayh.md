## Overview
Masarjawayh, a physician. The book *The Umayyad State* says that, according to al-Qifti, he translated a book of medicine for the caliph Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.181}

This translation is placed in the context of the beginnings of the movement of translation in the Umayyad period. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.181}
