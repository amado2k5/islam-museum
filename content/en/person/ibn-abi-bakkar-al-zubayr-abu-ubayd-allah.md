## Overview
Abu Ubayd Allah al-Zubayr ibn Abi Bakr, known as Bakkar, from [[place/medina|Medina]]. He was born there in 172 AH (788 CE), took knowledge from his father and from a number of teachers, and died in 256 AH (870 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.703-704}

## Lineage
The book gives his full lineage: al-Zubayr ibn Abi Bakr ibn Abdallah ibn Mus'ab ibn Thabit ibn Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr. His kunya is Abu Ubayd Allah and his title is Bakkar. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.703}

## Standing
The data classify him as a historian and a scholar of the Sunni community, and place him in the early Abbasid period. His birth and death dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
