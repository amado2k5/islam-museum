## Overview
Yathrib ibn Qatiya ibn Muhalhil ibn Iram ibn Ubayl. According to al-Mas'udi, he settled in Medina with his children and those who followed him, and Yathrib was named after him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 458}.

## The report
al-Mas'udi reports that they perished in some of the perils and calamities of time, and that their poet lamented them for it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 458}.
