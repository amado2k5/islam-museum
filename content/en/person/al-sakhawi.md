## Overview
The hafiz Shams al-Din Abu al-Khayr Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sakhawi. His origin is Sakha, he was born in Cairo, he was Shafi'i in school, and he settled in the two holy cities. He was born in Rabi al-Awwal 831 AH and died in Medina in 902 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.431-432}

## Learning
He memorised the Qur'an as a child, and memorised a number of texts, among them "Umdat al-Ahkam", "al-Tanbih", "al-Minhaj" and Ibn Malik's Alfiyya. He excelled in jurisprudence, Arabic, the readings, hadith and history. He took from more than four hundred shaykhs, and attended his teacher [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]] most closely. Ibn al-Imad relates that Ibn Hajar said of him that he was "the best of my group". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.431}

## His works
Among his works are "al-Jawahir wa al-Durar" on the life of his teacher Ibn Hajar, "Fath al-Mughith", "al-Daw' al-Lami' li-Ahl al-Qarn al-Tasi'", "al-Maqasid al-Hasana", and [[work/al-ilan-bil-tawbikh|al-I'lan bil-Tawbikh]]. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.431-432} {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
