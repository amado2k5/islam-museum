## Summary
Zuhayr al-Shawish is named in the book as the owner of al-Maktab al-Islami; he published [[person/al-albani-muhammad-nasir-al-din|Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani]], whom he calls "our teacher"; he supervised the printing of [[work/sahih-al-jami-al-saghir|Sahih al-Jami' al-Saghir wa-Ziyadatuh]]. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 5} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 17}

## His work on Sahih al-Jami'
He says that at his suggestion he began printing the book in Damascus in 1388 AH in small parts, from drafts al-Albani kept for review and had not prepared for print, then reprinted it photographically; renewing it was long impossible because neither of them was settled in one country, until al-Albani left him free to reissue it. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 5-6} For the new edition he corrected errors, moved hadiths between the sound and weak parts according to al-Albani's revised views, added references to al-Albani's books printed since the first edition, arranged the hadiths by fiqh chapters, and added a glossary and notes signed "Zuhayr" or "Z". {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 6-9} He dated the preface 1 Ramadan 1405 AH. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 10}

Al-Albani says they agreed to publish the book in instalments, and that Zuhayr suggested printing an inner number for the *Ziyada* hadiths in place of the letter zay. {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 17} {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988 p. 19}
