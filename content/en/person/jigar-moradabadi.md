# Jigar Moradabadi

**Jigar Moradabadi** (6 April 1890 – 9 September 1960), born **Ali Sikandar**, was an Indian Urdu ghazal poet, among the most popular Urdu poets of his age; he received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1958 for his collection Atish-e-Gul.{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-en}{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-ur}

## Early life and formation

Jigar was born at Moradabad into a literary family: his father Ali Nazar and his uncle Ali Zafar were poets. He was a disciple in his youth of Rasa Rampuri, travelled across India for his work, and formed a close friendship with the poet Asghar Gondvi, at whose home in Gonda he stayed.{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-ur}{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-en}

## Poetry and fame

Jigar became famous for his ghazals and his recitation at mushairas, becoming one of the most sought-after poets of his time, and he was a mentor of the poet Majrooh Sultanpuri. His collections include Dagh-e-Jigar, Shola-e-Toor and Atish-e-Gul, and his verse was gathered in the Kulliyat-e-Jigar.{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-en}{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-ur}

## Death

Jigar died at Gonda on 9 September 1960.{cite:wikipedia-jigar-moradabadi-en}{cite:wikidata-jigar-moradabadi}
