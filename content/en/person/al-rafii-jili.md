## Overview
Al-Rafi'i al-Jili, chief qadi of Damascus; a brilliant jurist-debater versed in kalam, philosophy and the ancients' sciences — he commented Ibn Sina's Isharat well. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222-223}

## His fall
He held Baalbek's judgeship then was moved to Damascus's — where he pursued a corrupt course: false registers, baseless claims, standing false witnesses, devouring bribes, orphans' and endowments' wealth, intoxicants, attending Friday prayer drunk; his agents were seized and tortured — their chief Ibn al-Rawas died under punishment — and he was taken out to a cave on Mount Lebanon: it was said he was strangled, said he was thrown from a height. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222-223}
