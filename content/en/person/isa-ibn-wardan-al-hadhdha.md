## Overview

Abu al-Harith 'Isa ibn Wardan al-Madani al-Hadhdha' is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/abu-jafar-al-qari|Abu Ja'far]], with [[person/sulayman-ibn-jammaz|Ibn Jammaz]]. He died around 160 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189}

## His standing

Ibn al-Jazari calls him a reader of the first rank, exact in the reading and a verifier, one of the early companions of [[person/nafi-al-madani|Nafi']] and of Abu Ja'far's students in the reading. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189} He read on Abu Ja'far. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 188} Isma'il ibn Ja'far al-Ansari read on him and on Sulayman ibn Jammaz, and al-Kisa'i narrated letters of the reading from Isma'il. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 183}
