## Overview
Tarmashirin ibn Dawa Temur ibn Halu, king of the Chaghatayid Mongols in Samarqand and Transoxiana. Their rule was unsettled after their first state, until Jankshi ibn Dawa Temur became king {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1537}.

## Conversion to Islam
Ibn Khaldun reports that when the kingdom passed to Tarmashirin, he became a Muslim. He was the first of the Chaghatayid kings in whom Islam appeared in Samarqand and Transoxiana {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1537}.
