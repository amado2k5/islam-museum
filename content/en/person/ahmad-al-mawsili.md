## Summary
Ahmad al-Mawsili, one of the worshippers of Syria, a sworn brother of Ahmad al-Maymuni, the two having drunk the draught of the longing. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 480 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 134. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}

## On hearing the report
When Ahmad al-Maymuni recounted to him Abu al-Aliya's report about the abode of Paradise, he turned yellow, then black, and fainted; his companion rose and left him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}
