## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Sinbat, a Melkite Christian from Harran, who was known among the Harranian Sabians. Al-Mas'udi in "Meadows of Gold" reports from him accounts of their animal sacrifices, and of the star incense with which they fumigated. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.544}

The museum's data does not give the dates of his life; an additional source is needed.
