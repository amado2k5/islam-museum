## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Abd al-Wahid Khallaf was an Egyptian jurist, one of the scholars; he was professor of Islamic law at the Faculty of Law, an inspector in the shari'a courts, and a member of the Academy of the Arabic Language {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}. He was born at Kafr al-Zayyat and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}. Al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 1305 AH (1888 CE) and 1375 AH (1956 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## Education and posts
He graduated from the School of Shari'a Judiciary in Cairo in 1912, where he was the most eloquent speaker among the students; he taught there in 1915 and then moved into the judiciary {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}. In 1935 he was appointed assistant professor of Islamic law at the Faculty of Law of Cairo University, and then professor there until 1948 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names printed writings of his, among them "Ahkam al-Waqf fi al-Shari'a al-Islamiyya"; "Nur min al-Qur'an al-Karim", on exegesis; "Ilm Usul al-Fiqh"; "al-Siyasa al-Shar'iyya aw Nizam al-Dawla al-Islamiyya fi al-Shu'un al-Dusturiyya wa-al-Kharijiyya wa-al-Maliyya"; "Nur ala Nur"; "Tarikh al-Tashri' al-Islami"; "al-Ijtihad wa-al-Taqlid"; "al-Ahwal al-Shakhsiyya"; and "Ahkam al-Mawarith" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.
