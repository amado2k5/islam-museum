## Overview
Abu al-Saraya was in charge of organising the rising of Ibn Tabataba in Kufa in 199 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.528} He fled from Kufa in 200 AH, and was killed in Rabi al-Awwal of that year. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.534} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.535}
