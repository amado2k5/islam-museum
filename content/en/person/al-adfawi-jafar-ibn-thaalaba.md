## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Kamal al-Din Jafar ibn Tha'laba al-Adfawi, from Adfu in Upper Egypt. He combined jurisprudence and literature. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.414}

## Dates
He was born in 685 AH (1286 CE) and died in 748 AH (1347 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.414}
