## Summary
Abu 'Amir Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan ibn Dhi'l-Wizaratayn Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Malik ibn 'Umar ibn Shahid al-Ashja'i al-Qurtubi, the poet and standard-bearer of eloquence and verse in al-Andalus; Ibn Hazm said: he died in Jumada I, Abu'l-Hasan Jumhur prayed over him, and no peer of his in poetry and eloquence came after; he was open-handed and generous, and lived forty-odd years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 230}
