# Kalidou Koulibaly

## Summary

Kalidou Koulibaly is a Senegalese professional footballer, born in Saint-Dié-des-Vosges, France, on 20 June 1991.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-ar}
A centre-back, he plays for Saudi Pro League club Al-Hilal and captains the Senegal national team.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
He spent eight seasons at Napoli, where he was named Serie A's best defender, and led Senegal to their first Africa Cup of Nations title in 2021.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}

## Early life

Koulibaly was born to Senegalese parents in Saint-Dié-des-Vosges; his father worked seven days a week in a textile mill in Senegal for five years to save enough to bring his mother to France.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
He began playing with his hometown club Saint-Dié and joined Metz's academy, but was released at fifteen, the academy manager later saying he was too introverted and his level a little below expectations.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
He returned to Metz and signed his first professional contract in 2010.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}

## Club career

Koulibaly made his professional debut for Metz on 20 August 2010 and joined Belgian club Genk on 29 June 2012, winning the Belgian Cup in his first season.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-ar}
He joined Napoli on 2 July 2014, won the Supercoppa Italiana that year — scoring the winning penalty in the shootout against Juventus — and the Coppa Italia in 2020.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-ar}
After one season at Chelsea he moved to Al-Hilal in 2023, winning the Saudi Pro League and the King's Cup in 2023–24.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}

## International career

Eligible for both France and Senegal, Koulibaly played for France's under-20 team before making his senior debut for Senegal in 2015.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
As captain he led Senegal at the 2018, 2022 and 2026 World Cups, and to the Africa Cup of Nations title in 2021 after a runner-up finish in 2019; Senegal were runners-up again in 2025.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
He was appointed a Grand Officer of the National Order of the Lion by President Macky Sall after the 2021 title.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}

## Racism in Italian football

During his years at Napoli, Koulibaly spoke out repeatedly about racism in Italian football; in February 2016 racist abuse from Lazio supporters led the referee to suspend a match temporarily, Lazio were fined, and Napoli supporters wore masks of Koulibaly in solidarity at the next home game.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
He was named an honorary citizen of Naples in 2019, and has been involved in charitable work for poor people in Naples and in Senegal.{cite:wikipedia-kalidou-koulibaly-en}
