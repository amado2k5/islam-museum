## Overview
The historian and traveller Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali al-Mas'udi al-Baghdadi, who died around 336 AH, author of "Muruj al-Dhahab". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

## His works and travels
The data record among his books "Muruj al-Dhahab", "Ma'adin al-Jawhar", "al-Tanbih" and "al-Ishraf". He travelled through the lands from Persia and India to the Maghrib and Egypt, and gathered in his books the reports of nations and cities, of creeds and sects. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

## The end of the Muruj
At the end of "Muruj al-Dhahab" he apologised for any error or misreading that might appear because of his many journeys, and he warned that whoever distorts his book or ascribes it to another would incur God's anger. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 750-751}
