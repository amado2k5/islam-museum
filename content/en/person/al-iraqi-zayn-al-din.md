## Overview
The hafiz Zayn al-Din Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Husayn al-Iraqi, a scholar who in 751 AH wrote two books on the takhrij (source-tracing) of the hadiths of "al-Ihya", one of them large. He died in 806 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.222}

The data attribute to him the [[work/alfiyya-al-iraqi|Alfiyya of al-Iraqi]].

The year of death is approximate in the museum's data and needs an additional source.
