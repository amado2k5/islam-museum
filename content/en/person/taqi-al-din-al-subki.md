## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Ali ibn Abd al-Kafi al-Subki, Taqi al-Din Abu al-Hasan the Shafi'i: born at Subk al-Abid on 1 Safar 683, he studied law with his father and then Ibn al-Rif'a, grammar with Abu Hayyan and hadith with al-Dimyati. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/63} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/64} He became judge of Damascus on 19 Jumada al-Akhira 739. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/64} He later resigned the judgeship to his son Taj al-Din and went to Cairo, where he died on 3 Jumada al-Akhira 756 and was buried at Sa'id al-Su'ada'. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/70}
