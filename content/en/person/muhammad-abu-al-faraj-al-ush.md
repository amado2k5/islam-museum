## Summary
Muhammad Abu al-Faraj al-Ush was a specialist in Islamic coins whose name appears on the title page of *Kanz Umm Hajara al-fiddi* (1972) as chief curator of the National Museum of Damascus. {cite:ush-kanz-umm-hajara-1972 p. 1}

## His work on the hoard
He undertook the study of the Umm Hajara hoard, which the security services handed to the Directorate-General of Antiquities and Museums in 1970; he says its Arab-Sasanian group, containing pieces "rare and unique in the world", drew him to it, and that he published it to encourage the officers and citizens who reported it to cooperate with the antiquities authorities in protecting the heritage. {cite:ush-kanz-umm-hajara-1972 p. 5} He ends his foreword hoping to go on publishing what he had gathered in the field and asking Arab and foreign colleagues to point out his errors. {cite:ush-kanz-umm-hajara-1972 p. 2}
