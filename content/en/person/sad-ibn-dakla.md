## Overview
Atabeg Izz al-Din Sa'd ibn Dakla al-Salghuri, lord of Shiraz and Fars. Ibn al-Athir mentions him in the account of Qutb al-Din Sanjar, a mamluk of the Caliph, who rebelled in Khuzistan and then took refuge with him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.352-353}.

## What the source records
Sa'd honoured Qutb al-Din Sanjar and stood in his defence. When the Caliph's army resolved to march on Shiraz, he interceded on Sanjar's behalf and asked for a pledge that he would not be harmed. He then handed Sanjar over, together with his property and his family, to the Caliph's forces {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.352-353}.
