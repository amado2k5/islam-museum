## Overview
Al-Rabi' b. Sulayman b. Dawud al-Jizi, Abu Muhammad al-Azdi al-Misri "the Lame" — an Egyptian companion of al-Shafi'i, "a jurist and a righteous man". He transmitted from al-Shafi'i, 'Abdallah b. Wahb, Ishaq b. Wahb and 'Abdallah b. Yusuf; Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and al-Tahawi transmitted from him, and it is he who reports from al-Shafi'i that reciting the Qur'an with melodies is disapproved. He died in Dhu'l-Hijja 256 AH, or 257 {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.1 p.259}.
