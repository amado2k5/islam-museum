Suhayb ibn Sinan, known as Suhayb al-Rumi, among the first Muslims tried at Mecca; he relates that when [[event/islam-of-umar|Umar became Muslim]] Islam came into the open and was called to openly — the Muslims sat in circles around the House, made tawaf of it, and took their right from those who had been hard on them, returning some of what was done to them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
