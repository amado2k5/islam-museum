## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Usayla al-Sunabihi, Abu Abd Allah, one of the jurists of the Successors in Egypt. Al-Shirazi mentions him with Abu Tamim [[person/abd-allah-ibn-malik-al-jaysh-ani|Abd Allah ibn Malik al-Jaysh-ani]], and says that they were among the companions of Umar. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 41}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 4792: "Abd al-Rahman ibn Usayla — in the diminutive with two unpointed letters — ibn Usal al-Muradi, Abu Ubayd Allah al-Sunabihi the Yemeni, resident in Syria"; he records that he came as an envoy and found that the Prophet had died, so he prayed behind Abu Bakr, and he narrated from him and from Umar, Ali, Bilal, Sa'd ibn Ubada, Mu'adh ibn Jabal and a group. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.811}

## The scholars on him
Aslam the client of Umar, Ata' ibn Yasar, Abd Allah ibn Muhayriz, Abu al-Khayr al-Yazani, Yunus ibn Maysara and others narrated from him. Ibn Sa'd called him trustworthy with little hadith; Ibn Yunus says he witnessed the conquest of Egypt; Ibn Ma'in says he lived on into the time of Abd al-Malik; and al-Bukhari listed him among those who died between seventy and eighty (AH). {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.811}
