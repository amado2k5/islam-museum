## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Umar ibn Salih, Shihab al-Din Abu al-Najah al-Manini, was a man of letters among the scholars of Damascus. The entry dates his birth to 1089 AH, equal to 1678 CE, and his death to 1172 AH, equal to 1759 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## Life
He was born at Manin, one of the villages of Damascus, and was raised and died in Damascus; his family came from one of the villages of Tripoli {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names al-Fath al-Wahbi, printed in two volumes, a commentary on the history of al-Utbi; al-I'lam bi Fada'il al-Sham, printed; Fath al-Qarib, in manuscript, a commentary on a poem on the Prophet's special qualities; and al-Fara'id al-Saniyya fi al-Fawa'id al-Nahwiyya, in manuscript. He also left verse of good quality {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.

## Death
He died in Damascus in 1172 AH, equal to 1759 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 156}.
