## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Hatim ibn al-Nu'man ibn Amr al-Bahili was governor of Armenia after his brother Abdallah ibn Hatim, who died there while serving as governor for Mu'awiya. According to al-Baladhuri, he built the city of Dabil, fortified it and enlarged its mosque; he built al-Nashwa, restored Barda'a (it is also said that he renewed its construction), and renewed al-Bilqan. These cities had fallen into disrepair. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.288}

Al-Waqidi said that Abd al-Malik built Barda'a at the hand of Hatim ibn al-Nu'man al-Bahili or his son. His account is linked with the [[event/reconquest-of-armenia|reconquest of Armenia]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.288}
