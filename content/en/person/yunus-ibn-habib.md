## Overview
Yunus ibn Habib al-Nahwi al-Basri, who knew best the inflections of grammar. His circle in Basra was frequented by students of learning and eloquent Bedouins. He passed a hundred years and died in the year 183 AH {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.111-112}.

It is reported of him that he witnessed an estrangement between the family of Ziyad and the family of Ibn Amir because of the river of al-Ubulla, which Ziyad completed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
