## Overview
Qaraza ibn Ka'b al-Ansari, Abu Muslim. According to al-Baladhuri, he was the last to conquer al-Rayy after its repeated revolts, in the time when Abu Musa al-Ash'ari governed Kufa for Uthman, and it remained quiet after him. His name is linked to the [[event/conquest-of-rayy-qumis|conquest of Rayy and Qumis]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The governors of Rayy used to stay in the fort of al-Zanbadi and gather in a mosque built in its vicinity. He later became governor of [[place/kufa|Kufa]] for Ali and died there, and Ali led his funeral prayer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
