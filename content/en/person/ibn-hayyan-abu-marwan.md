## Overview
Abu Marwan Hayyan ibn Khalaf ibn Husayn ibn Hayyan al-Qurtubi, an Andalusian historian from Córdoba (377–469 AH), author of *al-Muqtabas* (The Borrowed Fire) on the history of al-Andalus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.396}

He may be the same person as [[person/ibn-hayyan-al-qurtubi|Ibn Hayyan the Andalusian historian]] mentioned in *Nafh al-Tib*, but the data do not state this. It needs an additional source.

## In al-Hajji's introduction to al-Muqtabis
- He was Abu Marwan Hayyan ibn Khalaf ibn Husayn ibn Hayyan ibn Muhammad ibn Hayyan al-Qurtubi. He was born in Cordoba in 377 AH/987 CE and died on a Sunday in Rabi' al-Awwal 469 AH/30 October 1076. {cite:al-muqtabis-ibn-hayyan-hajji-dar-al-thaqafa p. 11}
- He was called "the shaykh of letters and of the historians of al-Andalus". The editor praises his fluent style, free of ornament, and notes that he gives the hour of an event, not only its day. {cite:al-muqtabis-ibn-hayyan-hajji-dar-al-thaqafa p. 11-12}
- His works were many; some put them at fifty, and most are lost. They include [[work/al-muqtabis-ibn-hayyan|al-Muqtabis]] in ten parts, al-Matin in sixty parts (surviving only in quotations), al-Batsha al-Kubra on the Amirid state, and Intikhab min Akhbar al-Qudat. {cite:al-muqtabis-ibn-hayyan-hajji-dar-al-thaqafa p. 12-14}
