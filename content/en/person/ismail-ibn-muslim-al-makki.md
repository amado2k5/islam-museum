## Overview
Isma'il ibn Muslim al-Makki, from Basra who settled in Mecca. He was among the companions of [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|Hasan al-Basri]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}

Al-Shirazi does not give the year of his death.
