## Overview
Judge Ibn Ghanaim ibn al-Adim al-Halabi, of the house of Ibn al-Adim, judges and historians of Aleppo. He died in 628 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.439}

## Ibn al-Athir's testimony
Ibn al-Athir describes him as one of those who strove in worship and discipline, and who acted on their knowledge. He says that he was one of his teachers, from whom he heard hadith, and that he benefited from seeing him and hearing him speak. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.439}
