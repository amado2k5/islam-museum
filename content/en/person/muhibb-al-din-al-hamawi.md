## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Dawud ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Alwani al-Hamawi, Abu al-Fadl, known as Muhibb al-Din ibn Taqi al-Din, was among the leading scholars of his age and one of the Hanafi jurists; he was the grandfather of Abu al-Muhibbi, author of «Khulasat al-Athar» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He was born in Hama, travelled to Anatolia (bilad al-Rum), Tabriz and Egypt, settled in Damascus and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 949 to 1016 AH, that is 1542 to 1608 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «Umdat al-Hukkam», a versified work on jurisprudence, printed; «Tanzil al-Ayat» on the proof-verses of the Kashshaf, printed; «al-Durra al-Mudiyya fi'l-Rihla al-Misriyya», in manuscript; «Badi al-Dumu al-Andamiyya bi-Wadi al-Diyar al-Rumiyya», in manuscript; and about twenty treatises gathered in one volume {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
