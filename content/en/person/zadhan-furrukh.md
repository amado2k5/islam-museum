## Overview
Zadhan Farrukh was head of the register of land tax in Iraq in the time of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]], and he used to write in Persian {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.356}.

## The transfer to Arabic
Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman transferred the register to Arabic by the order of al-Hajjaj. Zadhan was killed afterwards in the strife of Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.361}.
