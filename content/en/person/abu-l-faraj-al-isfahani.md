## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Ali ibn al-Husayn al-Isfahani, author of "Kitab al-Aghani". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 527}

## Kitab al-Aghani
Haji Khalifa relates from al-Muhallabi that he compiled the book over fifty years, and that in his lifetime he wrote it out in his own hand only once. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 527}

## His death
He died in 356 AH; the Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 527}
