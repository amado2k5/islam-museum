## Overview
Sulayman ibn al-Hasan, an Abbasid vizier. He was arrested in the year 319 AH after money had become scarce in his time, and Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Kulwadhani was appointed vizier after him {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.138}.
