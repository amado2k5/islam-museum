# Habiba Da Silva

## Summary
Habiba Da Silva is a British beauty and modest-fashion creator based in Birmingham, known online as LifeLongPercussion from her first blog before her move to YouTube, where her videos cover beauty, fashion and relatable subjects. {cite:youtube-habibadasilva} {cite:modishmuslimah-uk-influencers}

## Work and reach
Her own site describes her as an international influencer based in the United Kingdom with over 1.5 million followers across social media, and says she has worked with partners in Dubai, Malaysia, Turkey, Lebanon, Nigeria and Canada. {cite:habibadasilva-about} Her first business venture, a hijab line in a range of skin tones, was launched to represent her love of diversity and inclusivity, and her site credits her collections with shaking the 'hijabi' industry and setting trends in modest culture. {cite:habibadasilva-about} The Modish Muslimah list of top UK modest-fashion influencers likewise notes that she owns a hijab brand. {cite:modishmuslimah-uk-influencers}
