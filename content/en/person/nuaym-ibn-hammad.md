## Overview
Nu'aym ibn Hammad, a narrator in the chain of the hadith "None of you truly believes until his desire follows what I have brought," which is the forty-first hadith of the Forty {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.824-825}.

## Place in the chain
He stands between Abd al-Wahhab al-Thaqafi, Abd al-Rahman ibn Hatim al-Muradi, and Ibn Warra {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.824-825}.

## Dispute over the hadith
The hafiz Abu Musa al-Madini said: this hadith is disputed on the authority of Nu'aym, and it has been said that in it he reported "Some of our shaykhs told us, Hisham or someone else told us." Ibn Rajab said: its authentication is very far-fetched on several grounds, among them that Nu'aym alone transmitted it {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.824-825}.
