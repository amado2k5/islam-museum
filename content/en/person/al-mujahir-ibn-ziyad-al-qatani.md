## Overview
Al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad was a man of [[group/banu-qatan-najran|Banu Qatan]] who came with a party of Banu Bishr and sent, with some of [[group/banu-abd-al-madan-najran|Banu Abd al-Madan]], to Abu Muhammad to meet them with Hamdan, Thaqif and al-Ahlaf so that they might pledge mutual support and loyalty. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} Abu Muhammad rode to them and they pledged support. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} The book's index lists him at p. 147 only, and the book gives neither his lineage nor his death. See [[event/pledge-at-najran-and-the-fight-below-minas-286]].
