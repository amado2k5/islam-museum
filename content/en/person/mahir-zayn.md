## Overview
Maher Zain (born 1981 in Tripoli, Lebanon, and raised in Sweden) is a nasheed singer and international pop artist. He is known for the song "Insha Allah" and his album "Forgive Me" (2009), which was very successful in Malaysia, Indonesia and the Arab world. {cite:wikidata}

## Influence
He sings in Arabic, English, Turkish and Malay, and is among the most listened-to Muslim artists online. Songs such as "Barakallah" and "Ramadan" have become occasion songs in many homes. {cite:wikidata}
