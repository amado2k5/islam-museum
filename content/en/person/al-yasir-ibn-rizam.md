## Overview
A man of [[group/jews-of-khaybar|the Jews of Khaybar]]. The Prophet was told that he was gathering [[group/ghatafan|Ghatafan]] to fight him, so he sent [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abd Allah ibn Rawaha]] with a group of his Companions. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266} When they came to him and promised him that the Prophet would appoint him over Khaybar and honour him, he went out with them and a group of Jews. When they were at al-Qarqara, six miles from Khaybar, he regretted it, turned back on his mount and reached for his sword. [[person/abdullah-ibn-unays|Abd Allah ibn Unays]] struck him, cut off his leg, and struck him a light blow with a cudgel that split his head. Each of the Jews killed his companion, except one man who escaped on foot. See [[event/killing-of-al-yasir-ibn-rizam|the report]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/267}

## Notes for researchers
- Ibn Ishaq calls him "al-Yasir", and in other books "Usayr"; the number of men with him was thirty in one copy (needs a source). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}
- He is described as a "Jew" from the text of Ibn Ishaq, and the report of his gathering Ghatafan is one that Ibn Ishaq does not say who carried.
