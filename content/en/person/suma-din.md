# Suma Din

## Summary
Suma Din is a British author of faith-based non-fiction for adults and children, based in Buckinghamshire, England. Her own website presents her as a published author and freelance writer specialising in non-fiction, and lists her latest book as Dear Mother — Letters from the Heart. {cite:rootedwriter-suma-din}

## Books and teaching
Her best-known book is Turning the Tide, a work on the stages of a woman's life published by the Islamic Foundation, alongside children's educational titles. She is also an editor, researcher and educational consultant, taught for many years in the supplementary and adult education sector, and has co-led a girls' youth group and taken part in interfaith programmes. {cite:ymwa-suma-din}
