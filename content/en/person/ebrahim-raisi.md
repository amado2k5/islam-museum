# Ebrahim Raisi

Ebrahim Raisi (14 December 1960 – 19 May 2024), full name Ebrahim Raisolsadati, was an Iranian jurist and politician who served as president of Iran from 3 August 2021, succeeding Hassan Rouhani, until his death in a helicopter crash near Varzaqan on 19 May 2024. The English article's lead calls him an "Iranian cleric and politician" and a faqih. {cite:wikipedia-raisi-en} {cite:wikipedia-raisi-ar}

## Early life and judicial career

He was born in the Noghan district of Mashhad, began clerical studies there at fifteen, and rose through the revolutionary judiciary: prosecutor in Karaj and Hamadan, deputy prosecutor of Tehran, deputy chief justice, attorney general, and from 2016 custodian of Astan Quds Razavi, the Imam Reza shrine foundation. He was also a member of the Assembly of Experts from 2006 and its first deputy chairman, and in 2019 became chief justice. {cite:wikipedia-raisi-en} {cite:wikipedia-raisi-fa}

## Presidency and death

After a first, unsuccessful candidacy in 2017, he won the presidency in 2021. On 19 May 2024, returning from the Azerbaijan border, his helicopter crashed in mountainous fog near Varzaqan and all aboard were killed; after funeral processions in several cities he was buried at the Imam Reza shrine in Mashhad on 23 May 2024, as the English article recounts. {cite:wikipedia-raisi-en}
