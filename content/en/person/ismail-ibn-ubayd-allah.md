## Overview
Ibn Abi al-Muhajir — Isma'il ibn 'Ubaydallah ibn Abi al-Muhajir, the great imam Abu 'Abd al-Hamid of Damascus, client of Banu Makhzum and tutor of the caliph 'Abd al-Malik's sons, one of the trustworthy scholars; he narrated from al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid, Anas ibn Malik, 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Ghanm, Umm al-Darda' and others. He died in 132 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1114}
