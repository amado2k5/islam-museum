## Overview
[[person/hanzala-ibn-abi-amir|Hanzala ibn Abi Amir al-Ansari al-Awsi]], known as "the Washer", one of those who fought at [[event/battle-of-uhud|the battle of Uhud]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.262}. His father Abu Amir was the monk who was called "the Sinner". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.262}.

## At Uhud
Hanzala had only recently married. When he heard the war cry while he was with his wife, he pulled himself out of her embrace and rose to fight, and he began to cut through the ranks until he reached the field of battle. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.262}.
