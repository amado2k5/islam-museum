## Overview
Ashinas al-Turki, a client of al-Mu'tasim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Baladhuri
Al-Mu'tasim gave him the palace of al-Rashid at al-Qatul. Then he settled him, with the commanders that were attached to him, in Karkh Firuz at Samarra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti says that al-Wathiq appointed him as sultan over the state in 228 AH, and clothed him in two jewelled sashes and a jewelled crown. He says: "I think he was the first caliph to appoint a sultan." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.529}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Mu'tasim and his Turkish companions granted him the place known as Karkh Samarra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.445}

## Notes
- Three sources each give one side of him. Al-Suyuti's report may need review; in the entity's data it is combined with that of al-Baladhuri. It needs an additional source.
