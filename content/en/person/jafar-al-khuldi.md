## Overview
Abu Muhammad Ja'far al-Khuldi, a leading figure among the Sufis and a man of scrupulousness and asceticism, according to Ibn al-Nadim.

## The chain of Sufism
Ibn al-Nadim says that he read in al-Khuldi's own hand the chain of transmission of Sufism from him: from al-Junayd, from al-Sarri, from Ma'ruf, from Farqad, from al-Hasan, from Anas ibn Malik. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.260}
