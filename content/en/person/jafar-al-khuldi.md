## Overview
Abu Muhammad Ja'far al-Khuldi, one of the chiefs of the Sufis, pious and ascetic; Ibn al-Nadim read in his hand the chain of Sufism going back through al-Junayd, al-Sari, Ma'ruf, Fariqad and al-Hasan to Anas. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 269}
