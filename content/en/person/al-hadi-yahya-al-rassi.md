## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim al-Rassi al-Alawi, titled al-Hadi. Ibn Khaldun says that he was the first of the Banu al-Rassi to summon people to himself at Sa'da, and that he was given the allegiance there as imam of the Zaydis. He is one of the founders of the [[polity/rassid-dynasty|Rassid state]] and a member of the [[group/zaydiyya|Zaydiyya]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun describes him as a jurist who exercised independent judgement in the legal rulings and held views on jurisprudence. He relates from Ibn Hazm that he had a view on jurisprudence that he had seen and did not stray from the community altogether. His son Muhammad al-Murtada succeeded him, then his brother al-Nasir Ahmad. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
