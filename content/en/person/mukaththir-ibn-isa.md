## Overview
The amir Mukaththir ibn Isa, amir of Mecca. Ibn al-Athir says that he built a fortress on the mountain of Abu Qubays, and the caliph ordered the amir of the pilgrimage, Tashtakin, to remove him and to install his brother [[person/dawud-ibn-isa|Dawud]] in his place. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 77-78}

## The war of Dhu al-Hijja 571 AH
In Dhu al-Hijja 571 AH there was a fierce war in Mecca, in which the pilgrims attacked the city. Mukaththir fled to his fortress and was besieged there; then he left it and marched away from Mecca, and his brother Dawud took over the command. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 77-78}
