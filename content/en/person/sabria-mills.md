# Sabria Mills

## Summary
Sabria Mills is an American behavioral therapist, coach and public speaker, and the founder and host of The DOPE Muslim Woman Podcast. She holds a master's degree in Educational Leadership and Human Behavior from Georgia Southern University, is a certified behavioral therapist and the CEO of The DOPE Muslim Woman Podcast LLC, and serves on the board of directors of Rabata. {cite:rabata-sabria-mills}

## Her podcast and platform
Sabria Mills specializes in helping her clients overcome heartbreak and negative behavioral patterns so that they can once again live purposeful and God-conscious lives, after nearly two decades of work addressing the needs of individuals living with physical, mental and emotional challenges. {cite:rabata-sabria-mills} Through her platform she amplifies the voices and experiences of Black American Muslims and supports marginalized communities through transformational change and healing; her work spans transformational workshops and podcast content on heartbreak and healing recovery, communal belonging and ancestral trauma. {cite:rabata-sabria-mills} The podcast's YouTube channel carries its episodes and live streams on marriage, divorce, faith, femininity, power, trauma and religious authority. {cite:youtube-dope-muslim-woman} Listen Notes records the podcast's successive seasons since 2020, including episodes on leadership and power, relationships and healing. {cite:listennotes-dope-muslim-woman}
