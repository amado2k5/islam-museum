## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Abi'l-Wafa' the Hanafi; his father wrote his entry: 'My son Ibrahim, the striving, worshipping, ascetic preacher'; he read on the scholars of his age as a mufti, led the people in prayer to three hundred and fifty completions, took up teaching and was engaged with the Sufis and preaching; he died in 925. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 546}
