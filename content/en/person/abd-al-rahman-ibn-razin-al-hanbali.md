## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Razin ibn Abd al-Aziz, a Hanbali jurist from Hawran and later Damascus, who heard hadith in Damascus and Baghdad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15808} His works include al-Tahdhib, an abridgment of al-Mughni, al-Nihaya, an abridgment of al-Hidaya, and a short gloss on the disputed questions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15808} He died in 656 AH as the source gives it.
