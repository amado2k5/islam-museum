## Overview
Ala al-Din Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Husni al-Dimashqi al-Hanafi, known as al-Haskafi, a Hanafi mufti from Damascus of Husni origin. He is the author of "al-Durr al-Mukhtar" on Hanafi jurisprudence. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.32}

## His life
He was born in 1025 AH (1616 CE) and died in 1088 AH (1677 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.32}
