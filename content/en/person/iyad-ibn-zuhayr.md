## Summary
Of Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr; he emigrated to Abyssinia — third of the three men Ibn Ishaq names of Banu al-Harith among the rest of the emigrants. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", tracing his descent to Zuhayr ibn Abi Shaddad. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-al-harith|Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}
