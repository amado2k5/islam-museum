## Overview
Abd al-Muhsin ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ghalib al-Suri, Abu Muhammad, called Ibn Ghalbun, was a poet of fine themes, from Tyre in the Levant, where he was born and where he died. The entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 339 AH, 950 CE, to 419 AH, 1028 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.

## His poetry
Al-Zirikli names a diwan of his poetry, in manuscript, and says he is the author of the two well-known lines, the first beginning with the words "by the One who inspired my torment by your teeth", the second asking what your eyes said to my heart so that it answered {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.
