## Overview
Abu al-Mughira Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ahmad ibn Hazm, an Andalusian vizier and secretary. Al-Maqqari relates that in "al-Matmah" he described himself as unique in writing. He and Abu Amir ibn Shuhayd were close friends who were never apart in the morning or the afternoon. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 652-657}

## The session with al-Mansur
He related that he sat with al-Mansur ibn Abi Amir in the Manyat al-Surur at al-Zahira, when the slave-girl Anas al-Qulub sang to them, and al-Mansur drew his sword against her and against him until he apologised. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 652-657}

## Death
Al-Maqqari says he died in Toledo in 438 AH. The Gregorian date is derived and is approximately 1046 CE. The date needs an additional source. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 657}
