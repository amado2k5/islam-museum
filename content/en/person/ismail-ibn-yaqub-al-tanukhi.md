## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan Isma'il ibn Ya'qub al-Tanukhi al-Anbari, born in 152, came to Baghdad and related there from 'Abd Allah ibn Ahmad and Muhammad ibn 'Uthman ibn Abi Shayba among others. He was a hafiz of the Qur'an, learned in Yemen's genealogies, abundant in hadith, reliable and truthful. He died at al-Anbar. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 209}
