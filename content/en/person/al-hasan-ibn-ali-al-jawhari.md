## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad, Abu Muhammad al-Jawhari, was a hadith scholar. Al-Zirikli reports that they said the height of the chain of transmission in the world came to him. His family was from Shiraz; he lived and died in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## Dictation and his compilation
He dictated many sessions. Among the Chester Beatty manuscripts, number 3524, is al-Muntaqa min Hadith al-Jawhari, a manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## His years
Al-A'lam gives his birth as 362 AH, marked with a question mark, which is 972 CE, and his death as 454 AH, 1062 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.
