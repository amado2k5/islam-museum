## Overview
A Mamluk sultan. He seized power in Damascus in 696 AH while the sultan was there, and the amirs swore allegiance to him, and no two of them disagreed about him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.731}

The caliph invested him with the black robe and wrote him a decree of appointment. He was then killed in Jumada al-Akhira 698 AH, and [[person/al-nasir-muhammad-ibn-qalawun|al-Malik al-Nasir]] was restored. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.731}
