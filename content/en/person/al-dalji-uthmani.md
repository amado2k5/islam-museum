## Overview
Shams al-Din Muhammad al-Dalji al-'Uthmani, the Shafi'i; born 860 at Dalja where he memorised the Qur'an, then entered Cairo and read al-Tanbih and more under its scholars, then travelled to Damascus and stayed some thirty years, studying under al-Burhan al-Biqa'i, the hafiz Burhan al-Din al-Naji, al-Qutb al-Khaydari, the qadi Nasir al-Din Ibn Zurayq al-Hutili and the imam-muhaddith Shams al-Din al-Sakhawi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 686}

## His works
He travelled to the land of Rum and met its sultan Abu Yazid, made the hajj from Syria, then returned to Cairo. He wrote commentaries on the Khazrajiyya, on al-Nawawi's Forty, on al-Qadi 'Iyad's al-Shifa and on al-Munfarija; he abridged al-Minhaj and al-Maqasid under the title Maqasid al-maqasid and commented on it; a company took from him including al-Najm al-Ghayti. He died at Cairo. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 686}
