## Overview
Aqil ibn Abi Talib, one of those whom Umar summoned with Makhrama ibn Nawfal and Jubayr ibn Mut'im, who were among the eloquent men of Quraysh. Umar told them to register the people by their ranks, and they began with the Banu Hashim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
