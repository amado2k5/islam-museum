## Overview
'Aqil ibn Abi Talib al-Hashimi, the eldest of his brothers and the last of them to die, grandfather of the traditionist 'Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn 'Aqil; his sons included Muslim, Yazid — from whom his kunya came — Sa'id, Ja'far, Abu Sa'id al-Ahwal, Muhammad, 'Abd al-Rahman and 'Abdallah. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2701}

## His story
He was present at Badr among the polytheists, taken out to it unwillingly, and captured; having no money he was ransomed by his uncle al-'Abbas. He died in the caliphate of Mu'awiya. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2701}
