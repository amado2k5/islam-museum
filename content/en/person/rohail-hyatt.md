Rohail Hyatt (born 4 December, at Rawalpindi) is a Pakistani record producer, keyboardist and composer. His birth year is read as 1966 by the Urdu biography and Wikidata and as 1964 by the English article's infobox; both readings are preserved in his record.{cite:wikipedia-rohail-hyatt-en}{cite:wikipedia-rohail-hyatt-ur}{cite:wikidata-rohail-hyatt} In 1986 he founded the band Vital Signs, whose first album carried the number-one single "Dil Dil Pakistan", composed by Hyatt, and helped shape Pakistan's rock industry.{cite:wikipedia-rohail-hyatt-en}

After Vital Signs ended in 1998 he founded Pyramid Productions, one of the country's leading music production companies, and composed the soundtrack of the film Khuda Kay Liye (2007).{cite:wikipedia-rohail-hyatt-en} From 2008 he was the founding producer of Coke Studio Pakistan, the televised platform whose fusions of folk, Sufi and popular music reshaped the country's soundscape. He is a recipient of the Hilal-e-Imtiaz.{cite:wikipedia-rohail-hyatt-en}
