## Summary
Haruna (El Hadj) Nkunduwiga is a long-serving leader of the Muslim Community of Burundi (Comibu), the country's umbrella Muslim organisation, and has been its public voice on health, peace and interfaith cooperation. {cite:muslim500-haruna-nkunduwiga}

## Work
As Comibu's secretary-general and later president of one of its branches he mobilised mosques for HIV/AIDS awareness and public-health campaigns, joined Christian leaders in joint appeals during Burundi's civil war — including a Ramadan truce appeal — and spoke for the community during the political crises of the 2010s, when the organisation itself split into rival branches. {cite:muslim500-haruna-nkunduwiga}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists him under Social Issues for building Muslim-Christian cooperation on the pandemic. Published sources on him are thin and mostly Francophone; the museum records what they attest and notes this limited sourcing on the record. {cite:muslim500-haruna-nkunduwiga}
