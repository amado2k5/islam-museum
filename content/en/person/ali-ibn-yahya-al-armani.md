## Overview
Ali ibn Yahya al-Armani was a military commander of the Abbasid state under al-Mutawakkil. He led the summer expeditions (the sa'ifa), and al-Mutawakkil wrote ordering him to march by the winter route against rebel lands; Ibn al-Athir records that his raids brought out five thousand captives along with much livestock as booty. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.5}

He met his death in al-Mutawakkil's reign; Ibn al-Athir gives the event its own heading, "the killing of Ali ibn Yahya al-Armani," among the events of 249 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.5}
