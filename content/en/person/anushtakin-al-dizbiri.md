## Overview
Anushtakin al-Dizbiri, commander of the armies of the Fatimid caliph al-Zahir from Egypt to Syria. He was his governor over the lands as far as Ascalon, and he besieged Hassan ibn Mufarrij and his people, the Banu Kilab. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Al-Zahir sent him in 429 AH to fight [[person/salih-ibn-mirdas|Salih ibn Mirdas]] and Hassan. He met them on the Jordan near Tiberias in [[event/battle-tiberias-429|the battle of Tiberias]], and they were defeated; Salih and his youngest son were killed. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
