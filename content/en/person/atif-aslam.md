Muhammad Atif Aslam (born 12 March 1983) is a Pakistani playback singer, songwriter and actor, born at Wazirabad in Punjab and educated at Lahore and Rawalpindi.{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-en}{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-ur} He co-founded the band Jal and rose to prominence with its song Aadat; after parting from the band he released his solo debut album Jal Pari in 2004 and built a playback career in both Pakistani and Indian films, known for his vocal belting technique.{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-en}

His best-known film songs include Woh Lamhe, Pehli Nazar Mein, Tu Jaane Na, Tera Hone Laga Hoon and Dil Diyan Gallan, and his Coke Studio performances include Tajdar-e-Haram and Wohi Khuda Hai; he sings mainly in Urdu and Punjabi and has also recorded in Hindi, Bengali, Persian and Pashto.{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-en} He made his acting debut in the social drama Bol (2011).{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-en}{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-ar} He received the Tamgha-e-Imtiaz in 2008 and several Lux Style Awards, a star on The Dubai Stars in 2019, and a place in Forbes Asia's 100 Digital Stars list in 2020.{cite:wikipedia-atif-aslam-en}{cite:wikidata-atif-aslam}
