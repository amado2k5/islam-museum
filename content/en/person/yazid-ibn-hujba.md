## Overview
Yazid ibn Hujba ibn Amir ibn Taym Allah ibn Tha'laba ibn Ukaba. [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] appointed him over al-Rayy and Dastaba. al-Baladhuri reports that he broke and reduced the land tax, so Ali imprisoned him. He came out of prison and joined Mu'awiya in Syria {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
