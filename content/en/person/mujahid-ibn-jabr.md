## Overview
Mujahid ibn Jabr, client of Qays ibn al-Sa'ib al-Makhzumi, known by the kunya Abu al-Hajjaj. Al-Mas'udi says he died in 103 AH at the age of eighty-four. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 191}

## In Rhodes and Arwad
Al-Baladhuri says that he lived in Rhodes teaching people the Qur'an, and that Mu'awiya used to alternate governors among the people there. He was among those who conquered Arwad, and there he taught the Qur'an to Tubay', son of the wife of Ka'b al-Ahbar; it is said that he taught him in Rhodes. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} He took part in [[event/conquest-of-rhodes|the conquest of Rhodes]] and [[event/conquest-of-arwad|the conquest of Arwad]].

## With Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz
The book says that he came before Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik and then before Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, and witnessed his death. Umar told him in his illness that he had been given poison to drink, and ordered that the dinars that had been paid as the price of the poison be returned to the treasury. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.280}
