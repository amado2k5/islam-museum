## Overview
Abu Ali ibn Marwan ibn Dushak, nephew of [[person/bad-al-kurdi|Bad al-Kurdi]], attributed to the [[polity/marwanid-dynasty|Marwanid state]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun relates that he escaped from a battle in which his uncle was killed, and reached the fortress of Kifa and took it. Then he possessed what had belonged to his uncle in Diyar Bakr, and took Mayyafariqin in 380 AH. He was then killed by a stratagem, and his brother Abu Mansur succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1104-1105}
