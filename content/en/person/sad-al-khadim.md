## Overview
Sa'd al-Khadim was appointed by al-Mutawakkil to govern the Beja and the road between them and Mecca, according to al-Baladhuri as reported by al-Maqrizi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His appointment
He appointed Muhammad al-Qummi to this post, and al-Qummi returned to the Beja with Ali Baba {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}. See [[event/al-qummi-buja-war|the war of al-Qummi and the Beja]].
