## Overview
Ibn Mujahid, born in Rabi' II 145 AH, shaykh of the Qur'an readers of his time and their foremost; Tha'lab used to say no one in our age knew God's Book better than Abu Bakr ibn Mujahid. He died and was buried in the Bab al-Bustan cemetery, as Ibn al-Jawzi records. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 283}
