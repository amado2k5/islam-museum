## Overview
Abu'l-Azhar Mahlab ibn Abi Isa al-Muhallabi; al-Mansur delivered his uncle Abd Allah ibn Ali into his custody, then ordered him killed — he strangled him to death, strangled a slave girl who was with him, and laid them like embracing lovers on a bed, both dead.
