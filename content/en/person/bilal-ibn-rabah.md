## Brief
Bilal ibn Rabah was an Abyssinian-born slave in Mecca and one of the earliest Muslims. He was tortured for his faith until [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] ransomed him. He became the muezzin of the community, the first voice to call the Muslims to prayer.

## Life
Born in Mecca to an enslaved Abyssinian mother around 580 CE {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}, Bilal embraced Islam while still owned by [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf]], who used to throw him onto the sands of Mecca. Tradition relates that under the torture he kept saying "One, one" until [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] bought him and freed him {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}.

In Medina the Prophet chose him as muezzin. He took part in [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], and after the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest of Mecca]] he is recorded calling the adhan from the roof of the Kaaba itself {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}. After the Prophet's death he served in Syria. The sources differ on whether he died there or in Medina, around 640 CE {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

## Legacy
Bilal stands where three histories meet: slavery and freedom, Africa and Arabia, and the sound of Islam. Every adhan in the world is remembered as descending from his voice.

## For young visitors
Bilal was an enslaved man who was tortured for his faith, and he would not stop saying "One." He became the first person to call to prayer in Islam, and his call can still be heard today.

## In the secret call (The Sealed Nectar p. 76)
The book mentions him among the early Muslims after the first group, and names him Bilal ibn Rabah the Abyssinian ([[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|the secret call]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## His torture in Mecca (The Sealed Nectar p. 90)
Umayya ibn Khalaf would put a rope around his neck and hand him over to boys, who would drag him around the mountains of Mecca. He would throw him onto the sands of Mecca at the height of noon, place a rock on his chest, and say to him: "You will stay like this until you die or disbelieve in Muhammad." Bilal would say: "One, one," until [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] bought him and freed him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 90}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 6)

The Sheikh relates that the Prophet told Bilal he had never entered Paradise without hearing the sound of his sandals there, and asked what he did, and Bilal answered that he never committed a sin without following it with a plea for forgiveness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:21:30}. He adds that Bilal would pray after every ablution, and the Sheikh then recommends reading Surat Yusuf, saying that it brings light and certainty into the chest and calms blood pressure (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:22:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 7)

The Sheikh repeats that the Prophet heard the sound of Bilal's sandals in Paradise, then moves to the day of the conquest of Makkah and says that Ali asked to give the call to prayer from the top of the Kaaba {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:30:00}. He says the Prophet wished to set a rule and did not permit it, saying that only Bilal would give the call, and he notes that Abd Allah ibn Umm Maktum also gave the call to prayer in Madina {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:30:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:31:00}. He says Bilal climbed the Kaaba and gave the call and the mountains of Makkah heard it, and that after the Prophet was taken, Bilal stood to call the adhan and his voice caught at the mention of the Messenger's name as he looked toward the grave {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:31:00}. He says Bilal asked Abu Bakr's leave and set out for Homs, after which the Sheikh prays for the lands of the Muslims, asking God to restore them as they were, to give victory to Islam and to honour the Muslims {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:31:30}.

He relates that Bilal gave the maghrib call in Madina, and as soon as he said Allahu akbar the people of Madina left their work and their market, as though history had returned {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:34:00}. He says that sobbing and weeping were heard in Madina at the mention of the Prophet's name in the call, and that the Companions were deeply attached to him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:34:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy recounts that Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf offered Bilal ibn Rabah marriage into his family by giving him his sister, and that Bilal said he would seek God's guidance (istikhara) before answering (needs_source: the exchange is garbled in the automatic transcript) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep08 00:18:30}.

He recounts that the Companions asked Bilal to give the call to prayer at al-Aqsa; when he did, it moved everyone present to tears and brought the Prophet to their minds (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep08 00:19:30}. He recounts that the Prophet told Bilal that he had heard Bilal's footsteps ahead of him in Paradise (needs_source: parts of the saying are unclear in the transcription) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep08 01:12:30}.

The sheikh says Islam made Khabbab ibn al-Aratt equal, in everything, to the son of Umar ibn al-Khattab (needs_source: the list of names is as the sheikh gives it) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 00:29:00}.
