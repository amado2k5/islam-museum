## Summary
Qadi Nasir al-Din Abu'l-Baqa' Ibn Zurayq al-Salihi al-Hanbali, imam, scholar and muhaddith; born at the Salihiyya of Damascus in Shawwal 812, of Shaykh al-Islam Abu 'Umar's progeny; he read on the scholars of his age, excelled, mastered, taught and was transmitted from by many notables — white-haired, comely, on the way of the righteous salaf; he long held the oversight of his grandfather Abu 'Umar's madrasa and deputised in judgment, then renounced it; he died at the Salihiyya on Saturday eve, 9 Jumada II 899. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 365-366}
