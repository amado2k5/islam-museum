## Summary
Ziyad ibn al-Harith al-Sudai, a Companion who called the prayer on a journey with the Prophet's leave; the Prophet sent an army to his people in Yemen and recalled it when they had embraced Islam, saying that whoever calls the prayer leads it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9963}
