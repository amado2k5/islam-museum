## Summary
Of Banu Zuhra; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Ramla bint Abi Awf and perished there, and Abdullah was born to him there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants" of Banu Zuhra, with his wife Ramla bint Abi Awf ibn Dubayra, saying he perished in the land of Abyssinia and she bore him Abdullah there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He adds that it was said his son Abdullah was the first man to inherit his father in Islam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq said: "and it used to be said: he was the first man to inherit his father in Islam" (p. 7) — a formula of doubt, not ascribed to certainty.
