## Overview
Abu Zakariyya Yahya ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Maymun al-Hammani al-Kufi, one of the travelling memorisers of hadith. He died in 228 AH (842 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
He came to Baghdad and related hadith there from a great many people. He was the first to compile a Musnad in Kufa. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The date of his death is recorded from one source and needs an additional source.
