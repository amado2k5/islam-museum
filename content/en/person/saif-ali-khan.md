Saif Ali Khan (born 16 August 1970) is an Indian actor and producer, born at New Delhi as Sajid Ali Khan Pataudi, the son of the actress Sharmila Tagore and the cricketer Mansur Ali Khan Pataudi, and titular head of the Pataudi family since his father's death in 2011.{cite:wikipedia-saif-ali-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-saif-ali-khan} Educated in India and at Winchester College in England, he made his acting debut in Parampara (1993) and became one of Hindi cinema's leading stars through films such as Yeh Dillagi (1994), Dil Chahta Hai (2001), Kal Ho Naa Ho (2003) and Hum Tum (2004), the last earning him the National Film Award for Best Actor.{cite:wikipedia-saif-ali-khan-en}

He received the Padma Shri in 2010.{cite:wikipedia-saif-ali-khan-en} The biography records that he grew up a Muslim and that religion played a major role in his upbringing; in a 2021 interview he said of his present outlook: "I know there is a higher power, but I can't say for sure what that is."{cite:wikipedia-saif-ali-khan-en}{cite:express-tribune-saif-ali-khan-2021}
