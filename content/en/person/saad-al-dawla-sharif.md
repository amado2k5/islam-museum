## Summary
Sa'd al-Dawla Abu'l-Ma'ali Sharif ibn Sayf al-Dawla al-Hamdani ruled after his father and his reign was long. He was struck by colic, was near death and lost his sight; his physician came in and he ordered aloes-wood and ambergris burned before him. Reviving a little, the physician said: show me your pulse. He offered his left hand; the physician said: I want the right. He answered: my right has left me no right — for he had sworn oaths and betrayed them. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
