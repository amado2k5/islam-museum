## Overview
Ibn Abd al-Hakam says the seat of power in Ifriqiya was a city called [[place/qartajanna|Qartajanna]], ruled by a king called Jurjir whom [[person/heraclius|Heraclius]] had made his deputy; Jurjir renounced him and struck dinars with his own face, and his rule extended from Tripoli to Tangier. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 246}

## His death
When [[person/abdullah-ibn-sad-ibn-abi-sarh|Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh]] marched on Ifriqiya, Jurjir met him in battle and was killed, his killer being, "as they claim", [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]]. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 246} Ibn Abd al-Hakam also tells of Jurjir's daughter, who fell to the share of a man of the Ansar. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 248} See [[event/battle-of-sufetula-647|the conquest of Ifriqiya]].
