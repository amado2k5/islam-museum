## Overview
Qays ibn Mas'ud al-Shaybani was in charge of al-Tuff on behalf of Khosrow, and he built al-Manjashaniyya six miles from Basra. His horses grazed in the meadow above it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
