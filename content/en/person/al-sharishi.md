## Summary
Al-Sharishi, the Maliki jurist, usuli and exegete, named for Sharish in Játiva; he heard at the frontier, Baghdad and Damascus, and was accomplished in Malik's madhhab, exact in Arabic, versed in kalam, devoted to God's Book and its exegesis — a man of asceticism and dignity, who wrote a celebrated commentary on al-Hariri's *Maqamat*. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 663}
