## Overview
Abu Bishr Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Harawi, known as al-Alim. He settled in Baghdad, and the caliph [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir billah]] studied with him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64}

Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64} A second source is needed.
