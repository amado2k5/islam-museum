## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Sun Tzu, in his account of the history of management, as the author of the oldest surviving book on the subject, [[work/fann-al-harb|The Art of War]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:08:00}.

## His book in the context of management
Al-Suwaidan says the estimates place the writing of The Art of War about three thousand years before the Common Era, and that the book is translated and in print (needs_source: the date is the speaker's figure, unchecked) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:08:00}.
He explains that the book is military at its core, teaching the commander how to run his army and deal with opponents, with rules that mix deception and good administration {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:08:30}.
In his view much of human development had a military basis, and modern management books such as The Art of War in Business have built anew on this old foundation {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:09:00}.
