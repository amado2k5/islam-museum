## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] closes his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] with Fatima bint Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, in the second half of the twenty-ninth episode and the thirtieth, the series' last. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:00:30} He says she was the only child of her mother among more than sixteen brothers and two sisters, and that her father raised her himself before entrusting her to scholars for the Qur'an, hadith, poetry, fiqh and history. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:17:00} Abd al-Malik brought his nephew Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz to Damascus to grow up with his sons, and married Fatima to him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:18:00} When al-Walid made Umar governor of Medina at twenty-five, she went with him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:18:30}

He relates that when Umar was recalled from Medina and wept on leaving it, she comforted him, and that she supported him when he refused al-Walid's wish to set aside the heir Sulayman. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:22:00} He tells that al-Walid had Umar shut in a sealed room, and that Fatima searched for him until, with Umm al-Banin's help, he was found near death. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep29 00:23:00}

In the last episode he tells how Umar, made caliph, gave her the choice between a husband with no time for her and her family's house; she chose him, and at his word returned her money and jewels to the treasury, and later refused them when her brother Yazid, as caliph, offered them back. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:09:00} The daughter of a caliph, who had once had dozens of servants, now washed her own clothes, lived through the Damascus cold without a quilt, and told a servant tired of lentils that this was the food of the Commander of the Faithful. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:12:00} He quotes her wish that the distance between them and the caliphate were as great as between the two easts. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:11:30} When she bought grapes from the governor of Baalbek for two dinars, Umar, living on two dirhams a day, rebuked her for tiring the Muslims' animals for his craving and would not eat them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:15:30}

He relates that on his deathbed Umar had no shirt but a patched one, and that when Ata' asked her about him she described him at work on the Muslims' needs into the night, by his own lamp, weeping at the thought of every poor, hungry and captive person he would be asked about. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:18:30} From Ibn al-Jawzi's biography of Umar he says she told her brother that Umar died of the fear of God. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:21:30} He sees in her a woman whose faith and love let her bear poverty after a life of luxury. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep30 00:22:30}
