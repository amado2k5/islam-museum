## Summary
Beylerbey of Algiers from Dhu'l-Hijja 974/June 1567, succeeding Hasan b. Khayr al-Din {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.

## A term of hardship
His governorship met plague, famine, a Janissary mutiny and popular strikes — he spent his time consoling the stricken and calming sedition — then crushed the revolt of Constantine's governor, influenced by Tunis's Hafsid rulers, appointing the commander Ramadan over the city {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.
