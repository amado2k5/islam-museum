## Overview
Sinnimar, a builder whom al-Umari mentions in "Masalik al-Absar". He built [[place/al-khawarnaq|al-Khawarnaq]] and al-Sadir for al-Nu'man ibn Imri' al-Qays, and completed them in twenty years {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.230}.

## The saying of Sinnimar
al-Umari reports that al-Nu'man stood over the building and admired it. Sinnimar said to him: "If I wished, I could make it turn with the sun, I would do it." Al-Nu'man ordered him thrown from the top of its battlements, and so he became a proverb: "He was repaid the reward of Sinnimar". His relation with [[person/al-numan-ibn-imri-al-qays|al-Nu'man ibn Imri' al-Qays]] is mentioned in the same source {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.230}.
