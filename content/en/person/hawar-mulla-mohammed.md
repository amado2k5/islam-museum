# Hawar Mulla Mohammed

## Summary

Hawar Mulla Mohammed Taher Zebari is a Kurdish Iraqi former professional footballer, a winger born in Mosul on 1 June 1981. He played 113 times for Iraq between 2001 and 2012, scoring 20 goals, and was part of the side that won the 2007 AFC Asian Cup. {cite:wikipedia-hawar-mulla-mohammed-en} {cite:wikipedia-hawar-mulla-mohammed-ar} {cite:wikidata-hawar-mulla-mohammed}

## Career

He began his senior career with Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya of Baghdad, then played abroad — Ansar in Lebanon, Apollon Limassol in Cyprus, Al Ain in the Emirates, Al-Khor in Qatar, and Persepolis, Esteghlal and Zob Ahan in Iran — before returning to Iraq with Sulaymaniyah, Erbil and Dohuk. He retired on 11 June 2015. With Iraq he also won gold at the 2002 Asian Games and the 2005 West Asian Games with the under-23 side, and crowned his international career with the 2007 Asian Cup title. {cite:wikipedia-hawar-mulla-mohammed-en} {cite:wikipedia-hawar-mulla-mohammed-ar} {cite:wikidata-hawar-mulla-mohammed}
