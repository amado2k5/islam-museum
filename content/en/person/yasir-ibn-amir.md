Yasir ibn Amir, father of [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar]] and husband of [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya]]; the whole family was tortured at Banu Makhzum's hands, and the Prophet would pass them saying: "patience, family of Yasir — your appointed place is Paradise". Yasir died under the torture. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
