Yasir ibn Amir, father of [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar]] and husband of [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya]]. The whole family was tortured by Banu Makhzum. The Prophet passed by them and said: "Patience, family of Yasir, for your appointment is Paradise." Yasir died under the torture. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## His torment in Mecca (The Sealed Nectar, p.90)
He died under torture together with his wife [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya]]. The Prophet passed by him and said: "Patience, family of Yasir, for your appointment is Paradise" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}.
