# Kamal El-Mekki

## Summary
Kamal El-Mekki is an American dawah trainer and AlMaghrib Institute instructor dubbed the "Black Belt of Dawah". He is best known for his workshop "How to Give Shahadah in 10 Minutes", designed to equip Muslim student associations and dawah organisations for outreach, and he formerly served as a Muslim chaplain and imam at George Mason University in Virginia. {cite:muslimcentral-kamal-el-mekki} {cite:trinitynews-kamal-el-mekki}

## Work
El-Mekki's teaching combines a ground-breaking style with humour, and his lectures and online videos, targeting contemporary challenges, have reached Muslim and non-Muslim audiences alike. {cite:muslimcentral-kamal-el-mekki} In 2015 a lecture visit to Trinity College Dublin, co-hosted by the Muslim Student Association and the Irish branch of the AlMaghrib Institute, drew objections from some students; he responded that the clip cited against him was an excerpt from a 39-hour course, taken out of context, in which he explained that apostasy law is not something Muslims advocate reviving or practising. {cite:trinitynews-kamal-el-mekki}
