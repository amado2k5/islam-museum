## Summary
Ibrahim al-Wa'ili al-Najdi al-Hanbali, a jurist to whom the leadership of the Hanbali school in the Hijaz came; he was born and died in Medina. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2046}

## Works
His work includes "al-Adhb al-fa'id", a commentary on the inheritance poem known as the Alfiyya of the inheritance rules. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2046}
