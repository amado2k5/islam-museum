## Overview
Abu Muhammad Yusuf ibn Abi Sa'id al-Sirafi, a grammarian, philologist and transmitter of reports, the son of the famous [[person/al-sirafi|al-Sirafi]]. He took the lead in his father's assembly after his death, and succeeded him in what he had held. He completed his father's book "al-Iqna'", which had died before he finished it {cite:wafayat-ayan p.72-73}.

## His works
He wrote books on explaining the verses in well-known books, among them "Sharh Abyat Kitab Sibawayh", "Sharh Abyat Islah al-Mantiq" and "Sharh Abyat Gharib al-Musannaf". al-Ma'arri relates that he had been a fat-seller, then left his shop and busied himself with learning until he excelled {cite:wafayat-ayan p.73}.

## Dates
He was born in 330 AH and died in 385 AH, and the two reports differ on the day {cite:wafayat-ayan p.73-74}.
