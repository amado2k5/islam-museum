## Summary
Prince of Spain, son of Emperor [[person/charles-v|Charles V]], later king {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 242}.

## The Oran report
To him the Count of Alcaudete forwarded his embassy's report from Fez on the proposed Spanish-Moroccan campaign against the Ottomans, 963/1555 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 242}.

## The annexation of Portugal
After Prince [[person/don-antonio-portugal|Don António]] failed to resist his forces, Spain annexed Portugal in 988/1580, and Philip entered the capital Lisbon on 27 Jumada II 989/31 July 1581 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 270-271}.

## The near-agreement with al-Mansur
He neared a military agreement with [[person/ahmad-al-mansur-al-sadi|Ahmad al-Mansur]] to provide Morocco military aid against Ottoman intervention, in exchange for the city of Larache and other concessions {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 271}.
