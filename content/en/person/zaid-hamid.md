## Summary
Zaid Hamid is one of Pakistan's most polarising media personalities — a self-described defence and security analyst whose television commentary and online lectures command a loyal following while drawing sustained criticism from journalists, scholars and parts of the state itself. {cite:muslim500-zaid-hamid} {cite:wikipedia-zaid-hamid}

## Career
Born in 1965, he founded the Brass Tacks think tank and rose to prominence on Pakistani television in the late 2000s presenting himself as a strategic analyst. His programmes blend military commentary, conspiracy-framed geopolitics and religious-nationalist rhetoric — critics characterise them as inflammatory, a characterisation he rejects. {cite:muslim500-zaid-hamid} {cite:wikipedia-zaid-hamid}

## Controversy
He has been accused by Pakistani journalists and civil society of incitement and of promoting conspiracy theories, was reportedly detained in Saudi Arabia in 2015 on accusations relating to statements on Saudi affairs, and has faced blasphemy-linked court proceedings in Pakistan. Supporters describe him as a patriotic analyst; detractors as a peddler of disinformation. The museum records these positions attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-zaid-hamid} {cite:wikipedia-zaid-hamid}
