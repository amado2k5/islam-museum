## Overview
The queen of Sheba in the Islamic narrative; the historians name her Bilqis daughter of al-Hadhad — the Qur'an leaves her unnamed — recounting her reign and her encounter with Sulayman {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}.

## In the sources
Al-Mas'udi says she ruled after Tubba' the First, that her reign lasted a hundred and twenty years, and that her affair with Sulayman was as God related {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}; al-Tha'labi recounts her story among the prophets' tales {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition}.

## From the series Qissa wa 'Ibar
In the eighth episode Omar Abdelkafy tells how the hoopoe found in Saba' a woman ruling her people, with a mighty throne, while they worshipped the sun, and how it dropped Sulayman's letter in her lap; she gathered her court and called it a noble letter, and he suggests she recognised its nobility from its appearance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:06:30} He calls her shrewd: her people told her they were men of strength and might and that the matter was hers, so she sent a gift to test Sulayman, and her envoys found the road to his throne paved with gold. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:09:00} From her entry into the palace he draws the lesson that she was a queen and wore long garments, and that civilisation lies in modesty and keeping to values; he then mentions her submission with Sulayman to God, Lord of the worlds. The lesson is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep08 00:10:30}

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the twentieth episode of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] to the queen known as Bilqis, following the Qur'an's account of Solomon and the hoopoe closely. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:01:30} He says the hoopoe found a woman ruling Saba' in Yemen, who had been given of everything and had a great throne, and whose people worshipped the sun. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:04:00} He takes her rule as proof that a woman can lead a state with strength and wisdom. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:03:00} When Solomon's short letter fell on her, she gathered her notables and would decide nothing without them, which he reads as consultation, and when they offered only force she judged that kings who enter a city ruin it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:11:00}

He reads her gift to Solomon as a way of learning his strength and testing whether he wanted only wealth. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:13:00} When he refused it, she decided to go to him with a delegation to spare her state and people. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:15:00} Shown her own throne, brought from Yemen and disguised, she answered that it was "as if it were it", an answer he admires as neither yes nor no. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:19:30} In the palace floored with glass over flowing water she bared her shins, thinking it a pool, and then, seeing what no human could make, submitted with Solomon to the Lord of the worlds. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:23:00} He adds that "many reports" say she married Solomon. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep20 00:23:30}
