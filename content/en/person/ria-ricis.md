## Summary
Ria Yunita, known as Ria Ricis, is an Indonesian content creator, actress and author born in Batang in 1995 — among the most-subscribed female YouTubers in Southeast Asia; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-ria-ricis} {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Career
She began posting comedic videos on Instagram before building one of Indonesia's biggest YouTube channels through vlogs, challenges and family content, reaching tens of millions of subscribers. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Beyond YouTube
She has acted in Indonesian films including Yowis Ben, published young-adult and motivational books for her large teen audience, and launched business ventures around her personal brand. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Profile
Her 2021 marriage to Teuku Ryan was a national media event broadcast on television; she remains among Indonesia's most-followed digital personalities across platforms. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Assessment
She is regarded as part of the first generation of Indonesian YouTube megastars and a reference point for the hijabi creator mainstream; commentary on her public life is reported attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis} {cite:muslim500-ria-ricis}


## YouTube record
She was among the first Indonesian women to pass ten then twenty million YouTube subscribers — at times the most-subscribed female creator in Southeast Asia — built on daily vlogs and family content. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Books and media
She has published bestselling books, appeared in films and television, and launched production and consumer ventures, turning her following into a multi-line business. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## Public figure
Her highly publicised marriage and family life — covered across Indonesian entertainment media — extend her reach well beyond the platform into national celebrity. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis} {cite:muslim500-ria-ricis}

## Origins
Born Ria Yunita in Batam in 1995, she began posting daily vlogs and family content in the early 2010s — building a channel that grew into one of the largest of any Southeast Asian creator. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}

## The YouTube record
Her passing of ten then twenty million subscribers — at times the most-subscribed female creator in Southeast Asia — made her a reference point for the scale the Indonesian creator economy could reach. {cite:wikipedia-ria-ricis}
