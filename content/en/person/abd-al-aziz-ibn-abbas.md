## Summary
Lord of Qal'at Bani Abbas in the Béjaïa country, who declared his independence {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 248-249}.

## The end
He swooped on the Ottoman fort Hasan b. Khayr al-Din had built at M'sila; fighting broke out with the garrison and he met his death, in 966/1559 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249}.
