## Overview
Uthman ibn Ahmad ibn Sa'id ibn Uthman ibn Qa'id al-Najdi was a jurist and one of the most distinguished people of Najd; the entry's description of his books shows him to be a Hanbali. It dates his death to 1097 AH, equal to 1686 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 196}.

## Life
He was born at al-Uyayna in Najd and travelled to Damascus, where he took from its scholars. He then moved to Cairo and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 196}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names Hidayat al-Raghib fi Sharh Umdat al-Talib, in manuscript, on Hanbali jurisprudence; glosses on Muntaha al-Iradat, in manuscript, on jurisprudence; a treatise on fosterage; and Najat al-Khalaf fi I'tiqad al-Salaf, printed. He also abridged Durrat al-Ghawwas, with a few criticisms {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 196}.

## Death
He died in Cairo in 1097 AH, equal to 1686 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 196}.
