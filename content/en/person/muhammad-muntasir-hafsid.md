## Overview
Muhammad al-Muntasir ibn Muhammad al-Mansur ibn Abi Faris Azzuz ibn Ahmad al-Hafsi was one of the rulers of the Hafsid state at Tunis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}.

## Accession and death
He was acknowledged after the death of his grandfather Azzuz in 837 AH, when he was in Tripoli, and then moved to Tunis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}. A grave illness stayed with him until he died at Sanyat Bardo, and his reign lasted one year and 71 days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}. The heading of his entry dates his death to 839 AH, which is 1435 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}.

## Character and works
Al-Zirikli describes his conduct as praiseworthy {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}. Among his works, he began the Muntasiriyya madrasa in the Suq al-Falqa in Tunis, which his brother [[person/uthman-hafsid|Abu Amr Uthman]] completed after him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 46}.
