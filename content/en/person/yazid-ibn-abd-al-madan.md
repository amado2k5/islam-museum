## Overview
Yazid ibn Abd al-Madan, of the delegation of the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] who came to the Prophet in the year 10 AH. He is the one who answered the Prophet on the fourth occasion, saying: "We are those who, when they are driven, advance." He also said to him: "We did not praise you, nor did we praise Khalid; we praised God, who guided us through you" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.

## His Islam
The Sira reports that he embraced Islam with his people at the hands of Khalid in the year 10 AH, and came in their delegation (Ibn Hisham, p.240). He is mentioned among [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|Khalid's mission to the Banu al-Harith]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.240}.
