## Overview
[[person/abu-fakiha|Abu Fakiha al-Jahmi]], client of [[person/safwan-ibn-umayya|Safwan ibn Umayya]]. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1745} It is said that he was a client of the Banu Abd al-Dar, and it is said his origin is of the Azd. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1745}

## Torture and manumission
He accepted Islam early. [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf]] tied a rope to his foot and dragged him until he threw him on the hot sand and began to strangle him. His brother [[person/ubayy-ibn-khalaf|Ubayy ibn Khalaf]] came and said: "Increase it." He kept at it until he thought he had died, then [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr al-Siddiq]] passed by, bought him and freed him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1745}

The views differ on this matter in the Isaba; they are set out in the section of views at the top of the page.

## Torture in Mecca (Sealed Nectar, p. 90)
Abu Fakiha, whose name was Aflah, was a client of the Banu Abd al-Dar. The polytheists used to tie a rope to his foot and drag him along the ground. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 1/57} He was among [[event/persecution-of-mustadafin|the weak who were tortured in Mecca]].
