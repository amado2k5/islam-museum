## Overview
Muhammad ibn Khalifa ibn Hamad ibn Musa al-Nabhani was of the tribe of Tayy by descent, born and raised in Mecca, and Maliki by school; al-Zirikli calls him the historian of the island of Bahrain in the modern period. Like his father he was among the teachers at the Meccan sanctuary. The entry in al-A'lam dates his death to 1369 AH, 1950 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His book on the history of Bahrain and the Peninsula
He travelled to Bahrain at the start of 1332 AH and stayed a short time, gathering what he could of its history and of the lives of its emirs in a book he called al-Nubdha al-latifa fi al-hukkam min Al Khalifa. He then went to Baghdad, where he was advised to make the book general to the Arabian Peninsula; he added to it and called it al-Tuhfa al-Nabhaniyya fi imarat al-jazira al-arabiyya, and published its first part, on Bahrain alone, in 1332 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## Basra and the First World War
He went to Basra in 1333 AH just as the First World War broke out, and the British detained him and took his books and papers, among them the drafts of his history. He was released in 1334 AH through the intercession of Shaykh Isa ibn Ali of the Al Khalifa, but was not allowed to leave Basra. After the war, in 1337 AH, he returned to the work, arranged it on a different plan from the first, added much to it and called it al-Tuhfa al-Nabhaniyya fi tarikh al-jazira al-arabiyya, printed in 1342 AH in three parts bound in one volume {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His other works and his death
At the end of the second part of the Tuhfa are the names of other works of his: Munis al-azab, a supplement to Sabaik al-dhahab fi ansab al-arab; Qatf al-azhar fi marifat al-maadin wa-l-ahjar; al-Nukhba al-Nabhaniyya, a commentary on the Baiqunian poem in hadith terminology; al-Tadhkira al-Nabhaniyya, on the names of some modern inventions and discoveries; and Thamarat al-kharaid fi rasm al-basaid. He died at Basra {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
