## Summary
Qadi of the Hanafis of Egypt, Shams al-Din Ibn al-Shihna al-Halabi — the imam, scholar, poet and marvel, scion of great scholars; of his verse: 'I told him, when he kept our tryst — no treason in my heart for other than he — and he lavished union upon his face till he rose above every beloved and surpassed.' He died in Muharram 890, aged eighty-five. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 349-350}
