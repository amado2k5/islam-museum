## Overview
Ahrun the priest, in the early days of the (Abbasid) state; he composed his book in Syriac and Masarjis translated it — his Kitab al-Kunnash he made thirty discourses, to which Masarjis added two. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 413}
