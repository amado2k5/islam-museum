## Overview
Ahrun al-Qass, mentioned by Ibn al-Nadim at the beginning of the Dawla. He wrote his book in Syriac, and Masarjawayh translated it. He also has the "Kunnash", which he made into thirty treatises, to which Masarjawayh added two. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.413}
