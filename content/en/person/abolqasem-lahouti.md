# Abolqasem Lahouti

Abolqasem Lahouti (1887–1957) was an Iranian-Soviet poet and political activist from Kermanshah who led the 1922 Tabriz uprising and became the founder of Soviet Tajik poetry. {cite:wikipedia-abolqasem-lahouti-en}

## From Kermanshah to Tabriz

Born Abolqasem Elhami on 12 October 1887 to a cobbler father who wrote religious poetry, he took the pen name Lahouti, served as a gendarmerie officer, was sentenced to death at Qom, fled to Turkey, and returned to join Mohammad Khiabani at Tabriz. After a second rising there in 1922 was crushed, he crossed into the Soviet Union and never returned to Iran. {cite:wikipedia-abolqasem-lahouti-en}{cite:iranica-abolqasem-lahouti}

## Soviet Tajikistan

Settling at Dushanbe in 1925, he became the founding figure of Soviet Tajik poetry, wrote the lyrics of the Tajik SSR anthem, founded theatre and opera in Tajikistan, served as its minister of culture, and rose to vice-chairman of the Soviet Writers' Union under Maxim Gorky. He died at Moscow on 16 March 1957 and was buried in Novodevichy Cemetery. {cite:wikipedia-abolqasem-lahouti-fa}{cite:iranica-abolqasem-lahouti}
