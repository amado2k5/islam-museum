## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed on the day of al-Yamama in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Sahm in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", saying he was killed on the day of al-Yamama in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
