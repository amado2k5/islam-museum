## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Haddad al-Zabidi was a Yemeni Hanafi jurist, from al-Ibadiyya, a village of Hazat Wadi Zabid in Tihama; al-Zirikli explains that a hazah is a place near the mountain. He settled in Zabid and died there. The entry gives his death as 800 AH, 1397 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli quotes al-Damadi as saying that he wrote splendid works in the school of Abu Hanifa, and that no Hanafi scholar in Yemen wrote their like in number and benefit. His books come to about twenty volumes. They include al-Siraj al-wahhaj, in manuscript, in eight volumes, a commentary on the Mukhtasar of al-Quduri; al-Jawhara al-nayyira, printed, in two volumes, also a commentary on the Mukhtasar of al-Quduri; Siraj al-zalam, in manuscript, a commentary on the versified al-Hamili text in jurisprudence; and a book of Qur'anic exegesis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.

## What was said of his exegesis
Al-Zirikli quotes al-Shawkani as saying that it is a good exegesis, well known among people today, who call it the Tafsir of al-Haddad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.
