## Overview
Abu'l-Ghayth Munqidh b. Nasr b. Munqidh al-Kinani (d. 539) — of the Banu Munqidh lords of Shayzar, brother of Abu'l-Mutawwaj Muqallad Mukhlis al-Dawla, father of Sadiq al-Dawla 'Ali lord of the castle; at his death the shaykh-adib Abu Muhammad 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad Ibn al-Qaysarani al-Khafaji of Aleppo, the diwan poet, mourned him {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
