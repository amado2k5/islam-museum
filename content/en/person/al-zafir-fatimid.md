## Overview
Al-Zafir bi-Nasr Allah Isma'il ibn al-Hafiz, the twelfth of the Fatimid rulers in al-Suyuti's count, who calls the state "Ubaydid". He was installed after his father al-Hafiz in 544 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.673-674}

He was killed in 549 AH, and they set up his son al-Fa'iz Isa, a small child, and the affair of the Egyptians weakened. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.673-674}
