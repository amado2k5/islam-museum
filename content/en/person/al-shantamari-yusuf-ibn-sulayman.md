## Overview
Yusuf ibn Sulayman ibn Isa, Abu al-Hajjaj, an Andalusian scholar from Shantamariyya al-Gharb. He was called "al-A'lam" for a wide split in his upper lip. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates
He was born in 410 AH (1019 CE) and died in 1084 CE, according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
