## Summary
Beylerbey of Algiers from Safar 960/January 1552 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 235}.

## Appointment
The Ottoman state entrusted him with the beylerbeylik of Algiers in place of the deposed [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]], judging a change of regional policy necessary now that Morocco had become a strong ally of Spain {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 235}.
