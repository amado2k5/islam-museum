## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Sa'ib ibn Bishr al-Kalbi, Abu al-Nadr, was the learned historian and exegete who stood at the head of the genealogists of his day. He took from Abu Salih, Jarir, al-Farazdaq and others, and died in 146 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3432}

## Standing and narration
His son Hisham ibn al-Kalbi narrates from him, as do others; Sufyan al-Thawri narrated from him while disguising his name as "Abu al-Nadr". Al-Dhahabi notes that despite his eminence in learning he was a Shi'i whose hadith was abandoned — yet he remains the foundational authority for reports and genealogies on which his son Hisham built. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3432}
