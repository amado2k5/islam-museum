## Overview
Abu al-Nadr Muhammad ibn al-Sa'ib al-Kalbi, also called Abu Hisham al-Kalbi. A commentator and genealogist, from whom his son Hisham took. Ibn al-Nadim relates from the handwriting of Ibn al-Kufi that he was one of the genealogists. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.299-301}
