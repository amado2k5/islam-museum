## Overview
Abu'l-Nadr Muhammad ibn al-Sa'ib al-Kalbi, father of Hisham al-Kalbi, an exegete and genealogist from whom his son Hisham took; in Ibn al-Kufi's handwriting he is named among the genealogists. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 299-300}
