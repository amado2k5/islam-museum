## Summary
Hazim al-Hanafi was overcome in the presence of remembrance, and his head was bound from wounds. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 494, sequence 495, on page 140. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140}

## What Khalid ibn al-Safar saw
Khalid ibn al-Safar relates that when Hazim al-Hanafi remembered God while beside a wall, he struck his head against the wall until it bled. He saw Hazim's head bound with a cloth, and saw him with Sulaym the reciter. A man came to Sulaym to recite to him, and Sulaym said: get up, let us go, for Hazim beside the wall does not hear the Qur'an, so he strikes his head against the wall. The report gives no further explanation of the practice. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140}
