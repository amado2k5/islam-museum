## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Abd al-Salam al-Jarawi, a poet and scholar from Jarawa in the Maghrib, known for his poetry and learning. The encyclopaedia gives his death as 609 AH (1212 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The date is recorded as reported and needs an additional source. His reports and his poetry also need an additional source.
