## Overview
Dawud ibn Nusayr al-Ta'i, Abu Sulayman, was one of the imams of the Sufis, living in the days of the Abbasid al-Mahdi. His family came from Khurasan and he was born in Kufa. The entry dates his death to 165 AH, equal to 781 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 330}.

## Career
He travelled to Baghdad and took from Abu Hanifa and others, then returned to Kufa, withdrew from people and kept to worship until he died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 330}. There are reports of him with the princes and scholars of his time {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 330}.

## What was said of him
One of his contemporaries, not named in the entry, said that had Dawud lived among the past nations, God would have told something of his story {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 330}.

## Death
He died in Kufa in 165 AH, equal to 781 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 330}.
