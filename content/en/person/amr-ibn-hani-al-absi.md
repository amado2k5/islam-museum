## Overview
Al-Hajjaj sent Amr ibn Hani al-Absi with the people of Damascus against the Kurds who had ravaged in the days of Ibn al-Ash'ath. He struck them and killed a great number. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then he ordered him to raid the Daylam, and he raided them with twelve thousand men. See [[event/hajjaj-daylam-expedition|al-Hajjaj's raid on the Daylam]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
