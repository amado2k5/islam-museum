## Overview
Al-Zirikli describes Aban ibn Taghlib as one of the extreme Shi'a (ghulat al-Shi'a); he was a reciter and linguist of Kufa {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. He was Aban ibn Taghlib ibn Rabah al-Bakri al-Jariri by clientage, Abu Sa'id {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. His grandfather Rabah was a client of Jarir ibn Abbad al-Bakri, of Bakr ibn Wa'il, and so took his nisba from him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. He died in 141 AH (758 CE); al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## His works
Among his books are "Gharib al-Qur'an", of which al-Zirikli says that he was perhaps the first to write on this subject, "al-Qira'at", "Siffin", "al-Fada'il" and "Ma'ani al-Qur'an" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.
