## Overview
Tamam b. Muhammad b. Sulayman Abu Bakr al-Hashimi (269–350 AH), of the 'Abbasid house; he transmitted from 'Abd Allah b. Ahmad and others, and Ibn Razquya transmitted from him; he died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 350 {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.8}.
