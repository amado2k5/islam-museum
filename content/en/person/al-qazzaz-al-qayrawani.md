## Overview
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Ja'far al-Tamimi the grammarian, known as al-Qazzaz of al-Qayrawan; he was attached to the service of al-'Aziz b. al-Mu'izz, lord of Egypt, from 390 onwards — grammar and lugha dominated him, with versatility in compositions. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
Al-'Aziz the 'Ubaydi lord of Egypt ordered him to compose a book on every particle that had come with a meaning in the alphabet's letters — he gathered the scattered of the precious books until the work reached a thousand folios; his also is the Kitab al-ma'arid and naturally-gifted verse; Ibn Rashiq says of him in the Umdhuj that he shamed the earlier men and cut the tongues of the later. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
