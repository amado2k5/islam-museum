## Summary
Abu al-Fadl Ja'far ibn al-Muktafi was a son of the Abbasid caliph [[person/al-muktafi-billah|al-Muktafi bi-llah]], who reigned from 289 to 295 AH, and was well versed in many sciences and in the history of earlier and later sages. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 17} He was born in 294 AH and died in Safar 377, so he was still a young man when he questioned [[person/al-battani|al-Battani]]. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 17}

## His report from al-Battani
On Ja'far's authority [[person/ibn-al-nadim|Ibn al-Nadim]] and [[person/al-qifti|al-Qifti]] relate that al-Battani began observing in 264 AH and continued to 306, and that he fixed the stars in his Zij for 299 — information Ja'far had from al-Battani himself. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 17} Nallino counts these conversations among the signs of the esteem in which the scholars of the age held al-Battani. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 26}

## As a source
Ja'far was one of the sources Ibn al-Nadim used in the Fihrist; Nallino cites him together with his notices in al-Qifti and Bar Hebraeus. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 17}
