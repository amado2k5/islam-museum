## Overview
Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, Abu al-Asbat and father of Yusuf. He is among the prophets mentioned in the Qur'an, as the museum's data records {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}.

## Lineage
He is the son of [[person/ishaq|Ishaq]]. al-Bukhari mentions him in the prophetic chain with the words: "the generous son of the generous son of the generous son of the generous" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}.
