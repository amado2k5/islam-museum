## Overview
Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, Abu al-Asbat and father of Yusuf. He is among the prophets mentioned in the Qur'an, as the museum's data records {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}.

## Lineage
He is the son of [[person/ishaq|Ishaq]]. al-Bukhari mentions him in the prophetic chain with the words: "the generous son of the generous son of the generous son of the generous" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures: The loss of Yusuf and grief

He recounts that Ya'qub lost Yusuf through the plot of his other sons, and that when he lost another son he still did not despair of God's mercy (the verse number is not given). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep08 00:29:30}

He cites a verse in which Ya'qub says that he complains of his grief only to God (the surah is not named). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep13 00:10:00}

He says that Ya'qub's grief was mixed with anger, and he cites the verse in which Ya'qub laments Yusuf (the surah is not named), and says that the brothers' words were not truthful (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep13 00:13:30}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures: Hope and patience

He cites a verse about the patience of Ya'qub, and says that it means patience that is free of complaint (the surah is not named). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep14 00:45:00}

He says that Ya'qub hoped for God's mercy and was answered, and that a person cannot know where good lies, so he should be patient. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep22 00:13:00}

He says that Ya'qub knew God would save Yusuf, and that his hope returned when he lost his second son, until the dream was fulfilled. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep23 00:31:00}

He says that Ya'qub told his sons not to despair of God's mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep08 00:30:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures: From loss to reunion

He says that Ya'qub doubted his sons' account that a wolf had eaten Yusuf, because the shirt was not torn; they then brought a shirt stained with blood, and he said that criminals always leave traces, attributing this to criminologists (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep22 00:08:00}

He reflects that Ya'qub lost his second son, Benjamin, and was left to grieve. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep22 00:10:00}

He recounts that Ya'qub lost Yusuf for years and was then reunited with him, and he takes this as an example of God's wisdom in what He withholds. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep23 00:14:00}

He recounts Yusuf's rise in Egypt and Ya'qub bowing before him, as the fulfilment of an earlier vision (the surah is not named). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep23 00:14:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy speaks of Ya'qub's patience in what befell him through his two sons, mentions the shirt being brought and cast over his face, and asks how Ya'qub dealt with the trial. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:51:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:52:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah (2)
The Sheikh says that Ya'qub showed Yusuf a special love and a distinct standing before his brothers, and draws the lesson that fathers should not favour one child over another. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:19:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh draws an educational lesson from the story of Ya'qub and Yusuf: when Yusuf's brothers saw their father's love for Yusuf and his brother, they tried to kill him and threw him into the well, thinking they would end his love in their father's heart, but it only increased until his eyes went white with grief; he holds that a father whose heart is taken by love for one child should not show it, because it stirs the jealousy of the others. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:07:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
At the opening of 'Bustan al-Amal' the Sheikh cites Ya'qub's words to his sons: go and seek news of Yusuf and his brother, and do not despair of God's mercy, as an example that the believer does not despair. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:06:30}
