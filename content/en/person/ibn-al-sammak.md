## Overview
Ibn al-Sammak, a preacher. He entered the presence of [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] and asked for water, and a cup was brought to him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 468}

Al-Suyuti relates that he asked the caliph how much he would pay for this drink if it were withheld from him. The caliph said half his kingdom. He then asked the price of getting it out of his body if it were withheld, and the caliph said all his kingdom. Ibn al-Sammak said that a kingdom whose worth is a cup of water is worthy not to be competed for, and Harun wept. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 468}
