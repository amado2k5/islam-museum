## Summary
Abd al-Ali Muhammad ibn Nizam al-Din Muhammad al-Ansari, Abu al-Iyyash, of Sahalawi and Lucknow in India, author of Fawatih al-rahamut on the Musallam al-thubut. His works include al-Arkan al-arba'a fi al-'ibada, a Persian translation of Manar al-anwar, glosses on Sullam al-munawraq and on al-Shirazi's commentary on the Hidaya of philosophy, and a commentary on the Tahrir of Ibn al-Humam. The book gives 1225 AH as his death year. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15411}
