## Overview
Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Sulayman al-Tunisi, a Tunisian traveller and scholar. His lineage is of a family scattered between Egypt and the Maghrib. The encyclopaedia says that he was born around 1790 CE (1204 AH) and died around 1857 CE (1274 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The two dates come from the encyclopaedia alone, and need an additional source.
