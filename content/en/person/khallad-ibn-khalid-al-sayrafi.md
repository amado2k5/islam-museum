## Overview

Abu 'Isa Khallad ibn Khalid al-Shaybani al-Kufi al-Sayrafi is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/hamza-al-zayyat|Hamza al-Zayyat]], with [[person/khalaf-ibn-hisham|Khalaf ibn Hisham]]; both took it from [[person/sulaym-ibn-isa-al-hanafi|Sulaym ibn 'Isa]], who read on Hamza. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 175} He died in 220 AH. Ibn al-Jazari calls him an imam of the reading, reliable, knowing, a verifier, a master of recitation and a precise teacher. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177}

## What scholars said and his lines of transmission

Ibn al-Jazari quotes al-Dani: he was the most exact and the most distinguished of Sulaym's companions. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177} He counts sixty-eight lines of transmission for Khallad, among those who took from him being al-Talhi, al-Wazzan, Ibn al-Haytham and Ibn Shadhan. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 175} Ibn al-Haytham is the one whom al-Dani called the most distinguished of Khallad's companions, a man who stood by Hamza's reading and kept it precisely. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177}
