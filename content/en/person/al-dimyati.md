## Overview
Abd al-Mu'min ibn Khalaf al-Dimyati, Abu Muhammad, Sharaf al-Din, was a hadith hafiz and one of the great Shafi'is. He was born at Damietta, travelled in the lands, and died suddenly in Cairo. The entry gives his years as 613 to 705 AH, 1217 to 1306 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli quotes al-Dhahabi as saying that he was of pleasing appearance and good character, smiling, eloquent, a philologist and reciter, with fine expression, a large soul, accurate in his books, and very useful in study sessions; he quotes al-Mizzi as saying that he had seen no one who memorised more {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}. His books include a 'Mu'jam' naming his teachers, about one thousand three hundred, in four volumes; 'Kashf al-Mughatta fi Tabyin al-Salat al-Wusta', printed; 'al-Matjar al-Rabih fi Thawab al-Amal al-Salih', in manuscript; 'Qaba'il al-Khazraj'; 'al-Aqd al-Muthamman fiman Ismuhu Abd al-Mu'min'; 'al-Mukhtasar fi Sirat Sayyid al-Bashar', in manuscript; 'Fadl al-Khayl', printed; and 'al-Tasalli wa-l-Ightibat bi-Thawab man Taqaddama min al-Afrat', in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 167}.
