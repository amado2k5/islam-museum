## Overview
Mukram ibn Mutarrif ibn Sidan al-Bahili. Al-Baladhuri says that Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr sent him in pursuit of Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad ibn Zibyan, the killer of his father. He marched until he encamped at the place that was named "the camp of Mukram", and did not meet him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
