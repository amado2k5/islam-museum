## In brief
Muslim b. Ibrahim, the memoriser and musnad-master Abu Umar al-Azdi al-Farahidi, client of Basra; he heard a single hadith from Ibn Awn and heard from Qurra b. Khalid, Hisham al-Dastuwa'i, Aban b. Yazid, Wahb, Shu'ba, Malik, and Mughira, while Abd al-Dari, Abu Muslim al-Kajji, al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, Abu Khalifa al-Jumahi, and hosts besides narrated from him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.360-361}

## His memory
Ibn Ma'in called him "reliable, trustworthy", Abu Isma'il al-Tirmidhi heard him say "I wrote from a hundred shaykhs without crossing the bridge", and he preserved the hadith of Qurra b. Khalid, Hisham al-Dastuwa'i, and Aban b. Yazid. He died in Safar of 222 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.360-361}
