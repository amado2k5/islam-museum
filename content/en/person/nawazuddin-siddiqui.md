Nawazuddin Siddiqui (born 19 May 1974) is an Indian actor, born at Budhana in Uttar Pradesh and trained at the National School of Drama in New Delhi.{cite:wikipedia-nawazuddin-siddiqui-en} After a decade of small parts he broke through in 2012 with Kahaani and Anurag Kashyap's Gangs of Wasseypur, receiving the National Film Award's Special Jury Award that year, and went on to acclaim in The Lunchbox (2013), Badlapur (2015), Manto (2018) and the series Sacred Games.{cite:wikipedia-nawazuddin-siddiqui-en}{cite:wikidata-nawazuddin-siddiqui}

In April 2017 he posted a silent video, 16.66, whose placards reported a "DNA test" finding him 16.66 percent of each of India's religions, and concluded: "when I discovered my soul I found that I am a 100 percent artist."{cite:better-india-nawazuddin-siddiqui-2017}
