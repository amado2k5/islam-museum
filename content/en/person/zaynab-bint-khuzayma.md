## Summary
[[person/zaynab-bint-khuzayma|Zaynab bint Khuzayma]] al-Hilaliyya, "the Mother of the Believers, wife of the Prophet"; she was called "Mother of the Poor" for feeding them and giving them charity {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1883}. She was sister of [[person/maymuna-bint-al-harith|Maymuna bint al-Harith]] by her mother {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1883}.

## The length of her marriage and her death
The Prophet's consummation with her came after his consummation with [[person/hafsa|Hafsa]] bint Umar; she then stayed with him only two or three months and died {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}. Ibn al-Kalbi mentions she stayed with him eight months and died in Rabi' al-Akhir of year 4 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}. Al-Waqidi mentions her age was thirty {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}. Umm Salama mentions in Ibn Sa'd's account that when the Prophet married her he moved her into Zaynab bint Khuzayma's, Mother of the Poor's, house after she had died {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}.

## Ibn Hajar's correction of the "longest arm" hadith
Ibn al-Athir said Ibn Manda mentioned in her entry the hadith "the first of you to catch up with me is the longest of you in arm" — and that it was already given in [[person/zaynab-bint-jahsh|Zaynab bint Jahsh]]'s entry, to whom it fits better, since what was meant by their catching up with him was their deaths after him, and this one died in his lifetime; Ibn Hajar calls this "a strong correction" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}. He mentions that the black servant-woman's story Ibn Sa'd related of "the Hilali woman who was with the Prophet" is an error — its subject is Maymuna bint al-Harith, who was Hilali {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1884}.
