## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-'Azim al-Mundhiri, hafiz and muhaddith of Egypt; Ibn Khallikan mentions him by way of blessing and reference — he was the one who pursued the deaths of notables and composed upon them the Kitab al-takmila. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Al-Targhib wa-l-Tarhib
The al-Maktaba al-Qayyima edition names him "Zaki al-Din 'Abd al-'Azim b. 'Abd al-Qawi al-Mundhiri, who died in the year 656 of the Hijra". {cite:targhib-tarhib-qayyima j.1 p. 1} In the preface to [[work/al-targhib-wa-al-tarhib-mundhiri|al-Targhib wa-l-Tarhib]] he says he dictated it, at a student's request, after dictating his *Mukhtasar* of Abu Dawud and *al-Khilafiyyat wa-Madhahib al-Salaf*, without chains, citing each hadith to its compilers and indicating its grade. {cite:targhib-tarhib-qayyima j.1 p. 2-3} Among his stated views there: what Abu Dawud is silent on does not fall below good, and those before him were lenient with hadiths of encouragement and warning, many citing fabricated reports without saying so. {cite:targhib-tarhib-qayyima j.1 p. 3} {cite:targhib-tarhib-qayyima j.1 p. 8}
