## Overview
Ali ibn Umar ibn Ali al-Katibi al-Qazwini, Najm al-Din, also called Dabiran, was a sage and logician, and one of the pupils of Nasir al-Din al-Tusi. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 600 AH, 1203 CE, and his death as 675 AH, 1277 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 315}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his writings: al-Shamsiyya, a printed treatise on the rules of logic; Hikmat al-ayn, on logic, natural science and mathematics; and al-Mufassal, a commentary on al-Muhassal of Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, in theology (kalam) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 315}.

He also lists Jami al-daqa'iq fi kashf al-haqa'iq, in manuscript, on logic, and three treatises published in Nafa'is al-makhtutat at Baghdad: al-I'tiraf bi-l-haqq, Ithbat wajib al-wujud, and Munaqashat ta'liqat al-Tusi fi ithbat wajib al-wujud {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 316}.
