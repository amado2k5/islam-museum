## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ya'qub ibn Ishaq al-Sa'di al-Jawzajani al-Bushanji, a hadith scholar from Jawzajan, attributed to Bushanj. He was born in 165 AH (781 CE) and died in 259 AH (873 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
