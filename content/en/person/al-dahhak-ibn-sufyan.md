## Summary
Al-Dahhak ibn Sufyan al-Kilabi, a Companion of [[group/banu-kilab|Banu Kilab]]; the Prophet appointed him over the alms of his people in the alms-collectors' dispatch of year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 424}.

## The Banu Kilab detachment
When his people refused to answer Islam and fought, the Prophet set him over [[event/sariyya-of-al-dahhak-to-banu-kilab|a detachment against them]] in Rabi' al-Awwal of year 9 AH; the Muslims routed them and killed one man {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
Al-Dahhak was a man of Banu Kilab who became Muslim; the Prophet sent him to call his people first, and when they fought he led the detachment and prevailed.
