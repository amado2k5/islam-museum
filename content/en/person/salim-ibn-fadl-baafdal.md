## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Salim ibn Fadl Ba'afdal, a scholar and traditionist from Tarim in Hadramawt. His lineage goes back to Sa'd al-Ashira. He died in the year 581 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.118}.

The year corresponds to about 1185 CE in the museum's data. No other details are available, and the entry needs an additional source.
