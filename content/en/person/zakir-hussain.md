Zakir Hussain (9 March 1951 – 15 December 2024) was an Indian tabla player, born in Bombay the eldest son of the tabla maestro Alla Rakha Qureshi.{cite:wikipedia-zakir-hussain-en}{cite:wikidata-zakir-hussain} He moved to San Francisco in the late 1960s and was based in the Bay Area for much of his career, becoming a central figure of world music through the fusion band Shakti and collaborations across jazz and the classical traditions of east and west.{cite:wikipedia-zakir-hussain-en}

He received the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian honour, in 2023, and won three Grammy Awards in 2024.{cite:wikipedia-zakir-hussain-en} He died in San Francisco on 15 December 2024, of complications from idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, aged 73.{cite:wikipedia-zakir-hussain-en}{cite:wikipedia-zakir-hussain-ur}
