## Overview
Ma'qil ibn Qays al-Riyahi, one of the amirs of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.716}

## The Kharit
When Ziyad ibn Khasafa wrote to Ali with news of al-Kharit, Ma'qil stood up and said that those who sought these men should have had ten men in place of each one of them, because a numerous force holds out against a numerous force. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.716}

Ali sent him with two thousand men of the people of Kufa, and made him commander over an army that joined two thousand men of Basra. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.716}
