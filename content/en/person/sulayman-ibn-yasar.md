## In brief
Sulayman b. Yasar of Medina, mawla of Maymuna Mother of the Believers, one of the Seven Fuqaha, the learned master; his kunya is given as Abu Ayyub, also Abu Abd al-Rahman and Abu Abdallah. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.89}

## Transmission and standing
He narrated from A'isha, Abu Hurayra, Zayd b. Thabit, Ibn Abbas, Maymuna, and a group; from him narrated Amr b. Dinar, al-Zuhri, Salim Abu al-Nadr, Yahya b. Sa'id, Salih b. Kaysan, and others. He was among the imams of ijtihad; Malik said Sulayman was among the scholars of the people, and it is said that when Sa'id b. al-Musayyab was asked for a fatwa he would send the questioner to Sulayman b. Yasar. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.89}

## His death
Mus'ab b. Uthman says he was one of the most handsome of young men. He died in 107 AH, it is said, and it is said in 104 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.89}
