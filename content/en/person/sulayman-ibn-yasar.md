## Overview
Sulayman ibn Yasar, client of Maymuna, wife of the Prophet, and brother of Ata ibn Yasar. He was known by the kunya Abu Ayyub. He died in Medina in the year 107 AH, or it is said 108 AH, aged seventy-three {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191-192}.

al-Salabi reports that he was one of the members of the consultative council that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz set up in Medina when he became its amir {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.173}.
