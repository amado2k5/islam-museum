## Overview
Salih ibn Tarif was one of the people of piety and righteousness whose efforts kept the spread of Islam in the lands beyond the Oxus moving steadily and flourishing in the time of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], according to the book. The governor Ashras ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami had sent preachers and jurists to those lands {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.109}.
