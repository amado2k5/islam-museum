## Overview
An Indian traditionist of the Siddiqi family, author of Awn al-Mabud, a commentary on the Sunan of Abu Dawud. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5155}

## Dates
The source says he was alive before 1323 AH and gives neither his birth nor his death.
