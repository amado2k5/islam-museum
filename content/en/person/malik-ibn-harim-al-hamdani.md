## Overview
Malik ibn Harim al-Hamdani, commander of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] on the day of al-Radm against Murad, according to Ibn Hisham. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.228}

## A difference in the report
Ibn Hisham differs in this from Ibn Ishaq, who named the commander of Hamdan as al-Ajda' ibn Malik. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.228} See [[event/battle-of-al-radm|the day of al-Radm]].
