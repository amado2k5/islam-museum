## Overview
Muti' ibn Iyas, a poet of Persian clientage in the early Abbasid period. Ahmad Amin says that he was among those accused of heresy. {cite:duha-islam p.139}

## The accusation of heresy
Ahmad Amin relates that he supported the Manichaean and Daysanite doctrines, and that he became known for heresy along with the three Hammads, Bashshar ibn Burd, Ibn al-Muqaffa', Ibn Abi al-Awja' and Salih ibn Abd al-Quddus. {cite:duha-islam p.139} {cite:duha-islam p.149}

This is the judgement of the source, not the judgement of the museum.

## An anecdote
Ahmad Amin relates that the poets praised al-Mahdi, and when Muti' had finished, he began a discourse with an unbroken chain of transmission from the Prophet about the Mahdi. {cite:duha-islam p.304}
