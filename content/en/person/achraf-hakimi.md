# Achraf Hakimi

## Summary

Achraf Hakimi Mouh is a Moroccan professional footballer, born in Madrid, Spain, on 4 November 1998.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-ar}
A right-back for Paris Saint-Germain, he captains the Morocco national team.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He has won the Champions League with two clubs — Real Madrid in 2018 and Paris Saint-Germain in 2025 and 2026 — and was named African Player of the Year for 2025.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}

## Early life

Hakimi was born to Moroccan parents: his father, Mohamed, a street vendor from Oued Zem, and his mother, Saida, a housewife from Ksar el-Kebir.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He grew up in Getafe in economic difficulty, and the article quotes him saying he grew up in a household shaped by Arab and Muslim culture, which later influenced his decision to represent Morocco.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He joined Colonia Ofigevi as a child and moved to Real Madrid's academy in 2006.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}

## Club career

Hakimi came through Real Madrid's academy, played for Castilla from 2016 and made his first-team debut in 2017, winning the 2017–18 Champions League — the first Moroccan to win the European Cup.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
A two-year loan at Borussia Dortmund followed, where he won the 2019 DFL-Supercup and set a Bundesliga speed record, before a move to Inter Milan in 2020 brought him the Serie A title.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He signed for Paris Saint-Germain in July 2021, and scored the opening goal of the 2025 Champions League final against his former club Inter as PSG won their first title; PSG retained the trophy in 2026.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}

## International career

Hakimi made his senior debut for Morocco on 11 October 2016, aged seventeen.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He played at the 2018 World Cup and at the 2022 World Cup, where Morocco reached the semi-finals and he scored the winning penalty, a panenka, in the shootout against Spain.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
As captain he won an Olympic bronze medal with Morocco at Paris 2024 and the Africa Cup of Nations in 2025, and reached one hundred caps at the 2026 World Cup.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
He finished sixth in the 2025 Ballon d'Or voting, the highest placing ever achieved by a Moroccan, and was named the 2025 African Player of the Year.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}

## Personal life and legal proceedings

Hakimi was married to the actress Hiba Abouk from 2020 to 2023 and has two sons.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
The article records, in attributed terms, that in March 2023 he was indicted by an investigating judge in Paris over a rape allegation, which his lawyer states he strongly denies, and that in September 2026 France's highest criminal court rejected his appeal, so he will stand trial.{cite:wikipedia-achraf-hakimi-en}
