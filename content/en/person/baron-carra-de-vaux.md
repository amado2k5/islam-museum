## Summary
Baron Carra de Vaux, a French orientalist of the early twentieth century; author on Islamic philosophy of *Les penseurs de l'Islam* in five volumes, two books on Avicenna and al-Ghazali, and *al-Muhammadiyya* treating many Islamic sects. {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 45} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 46}

## In Ghallab's account
Ghallab cites him that the Qur'an is not a book of philosophy yet presents philosophical questions in inspired form, and that it treated the matters of divinity, oneness and power. {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 47}
