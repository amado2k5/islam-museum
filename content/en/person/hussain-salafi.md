## Overview
Hussain Salafi is a preacher who delivers his sermons and lectures in the Malayalam language through his official YouTube channel, whose description presents its content as "authentic Islamic content in the Malayalam language" by "Sheikh Hussain Salafi, a renowned Islamic scholar and educator". {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}

He preaches the Friday sermon at Masjid al-Aziz in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, has recorded sermons at Masjid ur-Rahman in Sharjah as well, and has lectures recorded in the emirate of Ras Al Khaimah dating back to at least 2017. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi} {cite:proelite-hussain-salafi}

His channel had about 301,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}

## Career and work
His output is built on weekly Friday sermons and lecture series, including a series explaining Riyad as-Salihin, lessons on Surat al-Kahf, and a series titled "Paraloka Viswasam" on belief in the hereafter, alongside short daily clips on worship and conduct. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}

The channel's oldest recordings date to about 2015 and include sermons and lectures delivered in Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah and other places in the Emirates. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}

The organisers of the PROFeLITE 2026 professionals' conference presented him as a speaker in his capacity as "Khatheeb, Masjid Azeez, Sharjah", documenting his preaching role outside his own channel. {cite:proelite-hussain-salafi}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of different positions reported by the sources, not as a verdict of the museum on them.

His content is marked by argument within Kerala's Muslim landscape: the published titles of his lectures show exchanges with traditional bodies such as Samastha (the association of Ahl al-Sunna scholars of Kerala, in its successive generations) and criticism of practices he counts as innovations, such as some forms of Mawlid celebration and seeking help from saints — positions he presents as a call to unadulterated monotheism. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}

His channel presents itself with keywords such as "SunniIslam", "AhleSunnahWalJamaah" and "Salafism"; these are self-descriptions of school and orientation, not independent attestation, and the museum therefore records no school for him. {cite:youtube-hussain-salafi}
