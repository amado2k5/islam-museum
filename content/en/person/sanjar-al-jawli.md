## Overview
Yeomans writes that Sanjar and Salar were lifelong friends who held the highest offices of state, and that Sanjar avoided the intrigues that ruined his friend; after Salar's death Sanjar became governor of Gaza, and after a fall from grace in the 1320s resumed his career as governor of Hama, dying in 1344. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.145} He built the [[building/tomb-of-salar-and-sangar|khanqah-madrasa]] near the Mosque of Ibn Tulun (1303-1304) that holds his own tomb and Salar's. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.145}
