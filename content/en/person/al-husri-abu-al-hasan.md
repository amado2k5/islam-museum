## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan al-Husri, muqri' and poet resident of Ceuta — 'Ali b. 'Abd al-Ghani al-Fihri, nephew of Abu Ishaq al-Husri author of Zahr al-adab; an exacting muqri' and brilliant blind poet — he left al-Qayrawan after its ruin and the taifa kings vied for him, then Tangier took him in. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 386-387}

## His readings and verse
Versed in the readings and their paths, he taught them at Ceuta and beyond; his diwan includes the entrancing ode "O night of the lover, when will its morrow come?" He died in 488. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 386-387}
