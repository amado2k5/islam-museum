## Summary
Jamal al-Din Ibn al-Mutahhar al-Hilli was an Imami jurist and theologian. Sunni readers know him above all through his book *[[work/minhaj-al-karamah|Minhaj al-Karama fi Ma'rifat al-Imama]]* and Ibn Taymiyya's reply to it, *[[work/minhaj-al-sunnah|Minhaj al-Sunna al-Nabawiyya]]*. {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 51} Ibn Taymiyya calls him "Ibn al-Mutahhar", and in the course of the reply refers to him as "the Rafidi author". {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 61}

## His book on the imamate and the king it was presented to
Ibn Taymiyya describes him as one of the Imami shaykhs of his own time, who composed his book inviting people to that school for the king named in it as "Khudabanda". {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 17} {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 19} The editor of Minhaj al-Sunna gives the king's name from a manuscript of Minhaj al-Karama as "Aljaytu Khudabanda Muhammad", that is [[person/oljaitu|Öljeitü]]. {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 62} In the treatise he states that the imamate is the most important matter of religion and that the imam is infallible and explicitly designated, and he lists the Twelve Imams from Ali to "the successor, the Proof". {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 61} {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 91}

## In his opponent's eyes
:::perspectives
**Sunni: Ibn Taymiyya (his view, in a work of refutation).** He holds that al-Hilli's book, though among the best arguments his side has, is weak in both transmission and reasoning. He answers its title "Minhaj al-Karama" with "Minhaj al-Nadama" ("The Way of Regret") and accuses al-Hilli of lying and distortion in reporting the Sunni position. {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 20} {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 51} {cite:minhaj-sunna-shamela p. 93}
