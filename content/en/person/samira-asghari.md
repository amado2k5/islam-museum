## Summary
Samira Asghari is an Afghan basketball player and sports administrator, born on 31 March 1994 in the Jalrez District of Afghanistan. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} {cite:wikidata-samira-asghari} In 2018 she became the first Afghan member of the International Olympic Committee (IOC), and one of the youngest members in its history. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en}

## Early life and education
Shortly after Asghari's birth, her family became war refugees and moved to Iran, where she grew up playing football and basketball at school. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} She later studied international relations at Kateb University in Kabul. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en}

## Career
Asghari played for the Afghanistan women's national basketball team and was named its captain. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} Working for the Afghanistan National Olympic Committee during the 2010s, she held one-year posts as finance director and as Deputy Secretary General, after earlier work in women's sports and international relations. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} In 2018 she was elected to the IOC at the age of 24 — the first representative from Afghanistan and one of the youngest members ever. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} In 2019 the Afghanistan Sports Journalists Federation named her Sports Personality of the Year. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en} After the Taliban takeover of Afghanistan, when she was living in Europe, she called on the United States to help evacuate Afghan female athletes. {cite:wikipedia-samira-asghari-en}
