## Overview
Ali ibn Hisham al-Marwazi, a commander of al-Ma'mun. Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that al-Ma'mun sent him to Qum after its people disobeyed and refused the land-tax. He killed their chief Yahya ibn Imran, demolished the wall of their city, and collected from them about seven million dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.440-443}
