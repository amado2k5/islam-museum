# Asma Bawla

## Summary
Asma Bawla is an American Muslim podcast host. Since 2020 she has hosted the podcast Muslimah, which she describes as a space for Muslim women to discuss the struggles women face in the modern age and their dreams as Muslim women. {cite:listennotes-muslimah-podcast} An independent podcast directory likewise lists Muslimah with Asma Bawla as its host. {cite:feedspot-muslim-women-podcasts}

## The podcast
The podcast has run since 21 June 2020 and carries 59 episodes. In its episodes she speaks in her own voice about her personal decisions — getting married, deciding to wear the hijab, accepting her body, and finishing her second year of law school — alongside guest conversations, including one with three young Muslim women on the realities of being Muslim women in the twenty-first century, and episodes giving an honest, unfiltered glimpse into her own life. {cite:listennotes-muslimah-podcast}
