## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Jabir al-Baghdadi, a great scholar of the Zahiri school, which is attributed to Dawud ibn Ali al-Isbahani. He wrote on the disagreements of the jurists. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 235 AH and his death as 310 AH (922 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.613-614}
