## Overview
Al-Hutay'a, whose name is Jarwal ibn Aws ibn Malik al-Absi, a poet of Abs. He was known for satire and praise, and lived into Islam. The encyclopaedia gives his death as 45 AH (665 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.54}

The date rests on one source and needs an additional source.
