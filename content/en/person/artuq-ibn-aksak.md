## Overview
Artuq ibn Aksak, a Mamluk of Sultan [[person/malikshah|Malik Shah]] ibn Alp Arslan. Ibn Khaldun gives him a "praised station" in their state. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His career
He was over Hulwan and its neighbouring lands in Iraq. Malik Shah sent him with an army to besiege Mosul together with Fakhr al-Dawla ibn Jahir in 477 AH, and Muslim ibn Qurayshi defeated him and besieged him at Amid. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He then joined Tutush, who met him and defeated him; in that battle he had the praised station. The ruler of Aleppo, Ibn al-Husayn, sought his protection and he gave it against Tutush. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death
He died in 483 AH at [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]], and his two sons Abu al-Ghazi and Suqman ruled after him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
