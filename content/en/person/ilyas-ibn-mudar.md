## Overview
Ilyas ibn [[group/mudar-ibn-nizar|Mudar]] ibn [[person/nizar-ibn-maadd|Nizar]]. The tribes of Mudar divided into two great branches: Qays Aylan ibn Mudar, and the clans of Ilyas ibn Mudar. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}

## The clans of Ilyas
From Ilyas ibn Mudar descend Tamim ibn Murra, [[group/hudhayl|Hudhayl ibn Mudrika]], [[group/banu-asad-ibn-khuzayma|Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma]], and the clans of [[group/kinana|Kinana ibn Khuzayma]]; from Kinana come [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]], the sons of [[person/fihr-ibn-malik|Fihr ibn Malik]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.21} From the clans of Ilyas also come the Banu al-Ghawth ibn Murra, who were called [[group/sufa|Sufa]], and to whom the authority to send the people out from Mina was given. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.29} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/119-122}

## For young visitors
Ilyas was the forefather of Quraysh, Kinana, Hudhayl and Tamim, who are among the tribes of Mudar.
