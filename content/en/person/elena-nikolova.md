# Elena Nikolova

## Summary
Elena Nikolova is the founder and creator of MuslimTravelGirl.com, a halal-travel blog. Her Travel Massive profile describes her as the first woman to become a Chartered Islamic Marketer in the UK, and notes that she grew up in Corfu, where her family ran a hotel. {cite:travelmassive-elena-nikolova}

## Muslim Travel Girl
On her own website she presents herself as an award-winning travel blogger who has helped Muslims since 2012 to plan halal holidays and DIY Umrah trips for less. She also writes monthly travel pieces for Muslim lifestyle magazines and speaks at halal-travel conferences. {cite:muslimtravelgirl-website} {cite:travelmassive-elena-nikolova}
