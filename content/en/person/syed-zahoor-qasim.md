Syed Zahoor Qasim (31 December 1926 – 20 October 2015) was an Indian marine biologist and oceanographer. Born at Rakswara village near Allahabad, he was educated at Aligarh Muslim University, where he took his B.Sc. (1949) and M.Sc. in zoology (1951) with the university gold medal, and completed his D.Sc. and Ph.D. at the University College of North Wales in 1956.{cite:wikipedia-syed-zahoor-qasim-en} He directed the Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute at Kochi from 1971 to 1974.{cite:wikipedia-syed-zahoor-qasim-en}

Qasim helped lead India's exploration of Antarctica, heading the first Indian expedition in 1981 and guiding the seven expeditions that followed, to 1988.{cite:wikipedia-syed-zahoor-qasim-en} He was vice-chancellor of Jamia Millia Islamia from 1989 to 1991 and a member of the Planning Commission of India from 1991 to 1996, and held honorary professorships at several Indian universities.{cite:wikipedia-syed-zahoor-qasim-en} He received the Padma Shri in 1974 and the Padma Bhushan in 1982, and died in Delhi on 20 October 2015.{cite:wikipedia-syed-zahoor-qasim-en}{cite:wikidata-syed-zahoor-qasim}
