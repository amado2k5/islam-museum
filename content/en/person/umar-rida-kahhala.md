## Summary
Umar Rida Kahhala is the compiler of [[work/alam-al-nisa-kahhala|A'lam al-Nisa' fi Alamay al-Arab wa-al-Islam]]; he signed its preface in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in 1379 AH (1959 CE). {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.5} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.8}

## His work on women's biographies
In his preface he says he searched as hard as he could for the largest possible number of famous women in Arab and Muslim society, arranged them alphabetically, followed each entry with its sources, and in the enlarged edition added women found after the first. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.7} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.8}

## For young visitors
Umar Rida Kahhala was a writer from Damascus who gathered the life stories of thousands of famous women in Arab and Muslim history into one big book.

## Research notes
- The book gives no biography of its author; his birth and death dates need another source before they are added.
