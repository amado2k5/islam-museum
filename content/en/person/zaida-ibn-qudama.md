## Overview
Zaida ibn Qudama al-Thaqafi, known by the kunya Abu al-Salt. He died in the land of the Byzantines on the raid of al-Hasan ibn Atiyya in the year 161 or 162 AH {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 316}.

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the book of Sunan, the book of Readings, the book of Exegesis, the book of Asceticism and the book of Virtues {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 316}.
