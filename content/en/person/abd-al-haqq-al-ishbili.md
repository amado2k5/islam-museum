## Overview
Shaykh Abd al-Haqq al-Ishbili al-Azdi, author of the two books «al-Aqiba» and «al-Ahkam». {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.333}

## Verses on death and the return
Al-Maqqari, in his «Nafh al-tib», quotes verses of his in which he says that death and the return hold concern and reminder for people of understanding, and he advises seizing two things before death comes: bodily health and leisure. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.333}
