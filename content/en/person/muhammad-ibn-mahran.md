## Overview
Muhammad ibn Mihran al-Jammal, the trustworthy hafiz, tireless traveller and transmitter Abu Ja'far al-Razi; he narrated from Fudayl ibn 'Iyad, Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna, Hatim ibn Isma'il, 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Darawardi, Marhum ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz and their peers. He died in 239 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3726}
