## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Husayni al-Naysaburi, Jamal al-Din, called al-Sharif, was a Hanafi scholar of Arabic and of the principles of jurisprudence. The entry dates his birth to 706 AH, equal to 1306 CE, and his death to 776 AH, equal to 1374 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## Life
He took up teaching at Aleppo, stayed for a time at Damascus and for as long again at Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}. Al-Zirikli quotes Tashkoprizade that the meaning of Nuqrahkar is a silversmith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books a commentary on al-Manar, on the principles of jurisprudence; a commentary on al-Tashil, on grammar; a commentary on al-Shafiya, on morphology, in manuscript, written for the emir al-Jami, with a copy at Manisa under number 5870; a commentary on Lubb al-Lubab, on grammar, in manuscript, with another copy at Manisa under number 2471, written in 857 AH, which is entered at the Chester Beatty library under number 4140 as al-Ubab; a commentary on al-Talkhis, on rhetoric, written for the emir Mankali Bugha; a commentary on al-Tanqih of Sadr al-Shari'a, on the principles of jurisprudence, whose composition he completed in Shawwal 771 AH; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## Death
He died in 776 AH, equal to 1374 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.
