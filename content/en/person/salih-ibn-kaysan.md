## Overview
Salih ibn Kaysan, the trustworthy imam and hafiz Abu Muhammad — also given as Abu al-Harith — the Medinese tutor of 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz's sons, said to be client of Banu Ghifar or of Banu 'Amir. He saw 'Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr and 'Abdallah ibn 'Umar, and Yahya ibn Ma'in held that he heard from both. He died after 140 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2016}
