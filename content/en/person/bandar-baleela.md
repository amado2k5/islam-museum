# Bandar Baleela

**Bandar bin Abdul Aziz bin Siraj bin Abdul Malik Baleela** is a Saudi Qur'an reciter and an imam and khatib of Masjid al-Haram in Mecca. He was born in Mecca in 1975, corresponding to 1395 AH, and memorised the Qur'an while young {cite:wikipedia-bandar-baleela-en} {cite:wikipedia-bandar-baleela-ar}.

He studied in his home city and in Medina: a bachelor's degree and a master's in fiqh at Umm al-Qura University, the master's completed in 2001, and a doctorate in fiqh at the Islamic University of Madinah in 2008. He became an assistant professor at Taif University {cite:wikipedia-bandar-baleela-en}.

After serving as imam in several Mecca mosques, he was appointed an official imam of Masjid al-Haram and first led its Tarawih prayers on 15 Ramadan 1434, 23 July 2013. A royal decree in 2019 made him one of its khatibs, and in October 2020 he joined the Council of Senior Scholars. He delivered the sermon of the Day of Arafat and the Eid al-Adha sermon in 2021, and was appointed fatwa commissioner for the Makkah region {cite:wikipedia-bandar-baleela-en} {cite:wikipedia-bandar-baleela-ar}.
