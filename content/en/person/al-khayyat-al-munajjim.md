## Overview
Abu Ali al-Khayyat, Yahya ibn Ghalib, or it is said Isma'il ibn Muhammad, a pupil of Masha'allah and, according to Ibn al-Nadim, one of the excellent astrologers. Among his books are "al-Madkhal", "al-Masa'il", "al-Ma'ani", "al-Duwal", "al-Mawalid", "Transformation of the Years of Nativities", "al-Manthur" which he made for Yahya ibn Khalid, "Qadib al-Dhahab", "Tahawil Sini al-Alam", and "al-Nukat". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 385-386}
