# Akbar Hashemi Rafsanjani

Ali Akbar Hashemi Bahramani, known as Akbar Hashemi Rafsanjani (25 August 1934 – 8 January 2017), was an Iranian Shia cleric and politician who was president of Iran from 1989 to 1997 and one of the founding fathers of the Islamic Republic. {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-en} {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-fa}

## Formation and rise
Born at Bahreman near Rafsanjan, he went to Qom at fourteen and studied at the seminary under teachers who included Hossein Borujerdi and Ruhollah Khomeini; under Khomeini's influence he turned to politics and opposition to Mohammad Reza Pahlavi, and after Khomeini's exile he acted as his representative inside Iran. {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-fa}

## Power broker and president
Across forty years Rafsanjani amassed power as speaker of parliament (1980–1989), acting commander-in-chief during the Iran–Iraq War, and the man who, in the English article's words, chose Ali Khamenei as supreme leader in 1989 — the Arabic and Urdu infoboxes likewise record that he succeeded Khamenei as president that year. He won the 1989 and 1993 presidential elections, lost the 2005 runoff to Mahmoud Ahmadinejad, chaired the Expediency Discernment Council from 1989 until his death and the Assembly of Experts from 2007 to 2011, and faced political isolation after supporting the opposition in 2009. He died of a heart attack at Tajrish in Tehran on 8 January 2017. {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-en} {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-ar} {cite:wikipedia-rafsanjani-ur}
