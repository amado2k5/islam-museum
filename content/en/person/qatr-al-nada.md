## Overview
Qatr al-Nada, daughter of Khumarawayh ibn Ahmad ibn Tulun. She was married to al-Mu'tadid in 282 AH, and he went in to her in Rabi' al-Awwal. According to al-Suyuti, her trousseau included four thousand jewelled belts and ten chests of jewels. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.574}

al-Mas'udi reports that she reached Madinat al-Salam (Baghdad) with Ibn al-Hassas in Dhu al-Hijja 281 AH, and that Ali ibn al-Abbas al-Rumi composed verses for her wedding. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.631}
