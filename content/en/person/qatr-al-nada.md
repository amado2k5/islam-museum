## Overview
Qatr al-Nada bint Khumarawayh, whom al-Mu'tadid bi'llah married; she died on the twenty-third of Rajab that year and was buried inside the Rusafa palace, per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 12 p. 412}
