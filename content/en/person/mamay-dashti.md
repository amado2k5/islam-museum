## Overview
Mamay, a Tatar amir who took control of the Dasht-i Qipchaq. Ibn Khaldun mentions him in the reports of Timur's wars with Toqtamish. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## What Ibn Khaldun reports
He began from the outskirts of the Dasht, and ruled the lands of Haji Sharkas in Manj Tarkhan. He stripped from the usurpers what they held and wiped out their trace. Then he died, and the kingdom was settled at Sarai and its districts for Toqtamish ibn Burdibek. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
