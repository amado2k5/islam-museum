Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Surayhi, known as Ibn Zamrak, was among the greatest poets and secretaries of al-Andalus. He was born in 733 AH / 1333 CE in the Rawd al-Bayyazin quarter of Granada, of humble origin, and studied under the qadi Abu al-Qasim al-Sharif al-Gharnati and under [[person/ibn-al-khatib|Ibn al-Khatib]].{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-es} His teacher introduced him at the Nasrid court; he accompanied Sultan Muhammad V into exile in Morocco, and when Muhammad was restored to the throne in 1361 he made Ibn Zamrak his private secretary and designated him court poet.{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-es}

He rose through the chancery until the sultan put him in charge of his correspondence and his chamberlainship, and when Ibn al-Khatib was dismissed as vizier in 1371, Ibn Zamrak succeeded him — and incited the sultan against his own teacher until Ibn al-Khatib was strangled in his prison at Fez.{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-es} A Nasrid sultan collected Ibn Zamrak's poems and muwashshahat in a great volume, 'The Remnant and the Attainment of the Words of Ibn Zamrak'; many of his verses are still inscribed on the fountains and palaces of the Alhambra, and al-Maqqari quoted him extensively in Nafh al-Tib and Azhar al-Riyad.{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-en}

Ibn Zamrak himself fell from favour and was restored, was imprisoned by Yusuf II for nearly two years, and in the end Muhammad VII sent men who killed him in his house while he was reading the Quran, together with the servants and sons who were with him.{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-es} The sources disagree on the year of his killing: the English biography and Himsi's monograph give 1393 (796 AH in Himsi's pairing), the Spanish biography 1394, the Arabic biography 793 AH / 1392 CE; the record preserves the disagreement in full.{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-en}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-es}{cite:wikipedia-ibn-zamrak-ar}{cite:wikidata-ibn-zamrak}
