## Overview
Lutfullah ibn Hasan al-Tawqani al-Rumi al-Hanafi, known as Mawlana Lutfi. A Hanafi scholar of Turkish origin and upbringing, who studied under the scholars of his age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.701}

## Death
He died in 904 AH, which corresponds to 1499 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.701}
