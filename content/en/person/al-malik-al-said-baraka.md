## Overview
Al-Malik al-Sa'id Baraka Khan, Nasir al-Din Abu'l-Ma'ali Muhammad b. al-Malik al-Zahir Baybars; he succeeded his father in Safar 676 — a handsome, generous youth of fine disposition, just, gentle, beneficent and loving of good, at eighteen years of age. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 632}

## Deposition and death
They deposed him and he resided at Karak some months, then died almost suddenly in mid-Dhu'l-Qa'da at Karak castle; a year and a half later he was moved to his father's mausoleum, and his brother Khidr ruled after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 632}
