## Overview
Umar ibn al-Ala', a butcher of Rayy, who fought Sanbadh when he rose there, and who was appointed over Tabaristan in the caliphate of al-Mahdi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His conduct
He gathered a force and fought Sanbadh and acquitted himself. Jahwar ibn Marrar al-Ijli sent him to [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]], who drew him near and gave him a rank. Then he was appointed over Tabaristan, and was martyred there in the caliphate of [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
