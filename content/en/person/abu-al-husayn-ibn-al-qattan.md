## Overview
Abu al-Husayn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Qattan al-Baghdadi, the last of the companions of [[person/ahmad-ibn-umar-ibn-surayj|Abu al-Abbas ibn Surayj]] whom al-Shirazi knew. He taught in Baghdad and scholars took from him, and he died in 359 AH according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.59} The date needs a second source.
