## Overview
Yazid ibn Mazyad commanded the vanguard of Ma'n ibn Za'ida to al-Rakhkh. When the Kharijites killed Ma'n at Bust, he killed them, and not one of them escaped {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Sijistan
He then took charge of Sijistan, and his grip grew harsh on both the Arabs and the non-Arabs of its people. Some Arabs cunningly obtained, in his name, a letter to [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]], and he was dismissed, imprisoned, and all his property was sold. He was then spoken for, and was brought to the City of Peace, where the Kharijites met him on the bridge and fought him. He then went to Yusuf al-Barm in Khurasan, and remained in good standing {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
