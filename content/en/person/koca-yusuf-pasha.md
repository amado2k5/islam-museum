## Summary
Yusuf Pasha, 'Koca Yusuf', twice Ottoman grand vizier in the late eighteenth century {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 5, 'Yusuf Paşa (Koca)'}.

A Georgian freedman of the harbour reis Hacı Hasan, he sold coffee in Kasımpaşa and sailed with the summer convoys to Egypt. Attached to Cezayirli Hasan Pasha, who made him treasurer on becoming kapudan-ı derya. Made governor of the Morea with vizier rank in 1199/1785 and grand vizier in 1200/1786, serdâr-ı ekrem in the Russo-Austrian war of 1787, grand vizier a second time in 1205/1791, and finally governor of Damascus and amir of the Hajj {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 5, 'Yusuf Paşa (Koca)'}.
