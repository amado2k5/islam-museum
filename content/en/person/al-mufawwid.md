## Overview
Ja'far al-Mufawwid ila Allah ibn [[person/al-mutamid|al-Mu'tamid]], the heir apparent of his father. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 564-565,569}

## The heirship and its loss
His father made him his heir apparent, gave him Egypt and the Maghrib, and titled him al-Mufawwid ila Allah. Then in 279 AH he deposed him in a public session, and gave the allegiance to Abu al-Abbas ibn al-Muwaffaq, who was titled [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 564-565,569}

According to al-Mas'udi, his father installed him as successor when he camped with al-Qa'im for the war with Ya'qub al-Saffar. Then Isma'il ibn Bulbul brought him with al-Mu'tadid to Baghdad in a litter, and al-Mu'tadid deposed him when he took his father's place. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 594-595}

## Notes for researchers
- The two accounts are close; the data do not record his birth or death years.
