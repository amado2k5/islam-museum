## Overview
The great wali Shaykh Abu Bakr b. Salim Ba 'Alawi; al-Nur says he was among the singular shaykhs sought by visitors from the farthest lands — settled and nomad alike profited by his blessing, God's servants were steeped in the breaths of his nearness, his wonders and virtues spread to the horizons borne by caravans and companies, and consensus and agreement fell upon his wilaya. He died on the night of Sunday 27 Dhu'l-Hijja at 'Aynat of Hadramawt's villages, half a march from Tarim. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 426}
