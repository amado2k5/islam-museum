## Summary
Sabba' al-Mawsili, who enjoyed the gardens of intimacy. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 485 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 136. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/136}

## Mada's question
Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari heard Mada' say to Sabba' al-Mawsili: O Abu Muhammad, where does asceticism lead them? He said: to intimacy with God. The scan reads the last word unclearly; it is given here in the sense that fits the answer. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/136}
