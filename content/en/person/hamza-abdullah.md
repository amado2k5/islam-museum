Hamza Abdullah (born 20 August 1983) is an American football safety from Los Angeles. Selected by the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in the seventh round of the 2005 NFL draft out of Washington State, he went on to play for the Denver Broncos, Cleveland Browns and Arizona Cardinals.{cite:wikipedia-hamza-abdullah-en}{cite:wikidata-hamza-abdullah}

Raised in Southern California as a devout Muslim, he and his younger brother Husain, also an NFL safety, together took the 2012 season off to make the pilgrimage to Mecca.{cite:wikipedia-hamza-abdullah-en} No source consulted names a school or tradition for him.
