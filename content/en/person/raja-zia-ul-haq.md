# Raja Zia ul Haq

## Summary
Raja Zia ul Haq is a Pakistani youth preacher and the chief executive of Youth Club Pakistan, a non-profit youth organisation founded in 2011 and based in Islamabad that organises community events, religious and educational conferences, and youth engagement programmes. {cite:wikipedia-youth-club-pakistan-en} His talks and the organisation's conferences circulate through the Youth Club YouTube channel. {cite:youtube-youth-club}

## Youth Club
Hiba Magazine interviewed him as the club's ameer: he described an organisation based in Islamabad and Lahore running street dawah, workshops, weekly halaqahs, school sessions, university lectures and an annual conference, and he said the club was the brainchild of a group of very dedicated sisters — he leads the organisation, he did not found it alone. {cite:hiba-youth-club} In August 2023, independent media reported that his YouTube channel was taken down, drawing attention to his role in the organisation's digital outreach. {cite:wikipedia-youth-club-pakistan-en}
