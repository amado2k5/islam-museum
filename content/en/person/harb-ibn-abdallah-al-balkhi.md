## Overview
Harb ibn Abd Allah al-Balkhi, a man of the early Abbasid period. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that he was in charge of the police of Ja'far ibn al-Mansur in Mosul, and that the quarter of al-Harbiyya in Baghdad was named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

The data holds neither his birth date nor his death date; an additional source is needed.
