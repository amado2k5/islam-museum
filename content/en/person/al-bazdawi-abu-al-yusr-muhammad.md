## Overview
Abu al-Yusr Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Abd al-Karim al-Bazdawi, titled Sadr al-Islam, a Hanafi jurist from Bukhara and judge of Samarqand. The encyclopaedia credits him with leadership of the Hanafis in his age, and says he is attributed to Bazda, beyond the river. He was born in 421 AH (1030 CE) and died in 493 AH (1100 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
