# Akmal Ikramov

Akmal Ikramov (September 1898 – 13 March 1938) was an Uzbek Soviet politician, born in Tashkent in a peasant family — the Russian article gives his birth month as September 1898, the English article the year only. He joined the Communist Party in February 1918, worked as a teacher and education inspector in Namangan, chaired the Muslim Bureau of the Namangan party committee, and in 1921–1922 was secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Turkestan before studying at the Sverdlov Communist University in Moscow. In the struggle over Turkestan's future he sided with those who divided it into national republics, against Turar Ryskulov's pan-Turkic proposal, and in January 1925 became secretary of the Tashkent regional committee of the new Uzbek SSR.{cite:wikipedia-akmal-ikramov-en}{cite:wikipedia-akmal-ikramov-ru}

In December 1929 Ikramov became first secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Uzbekistan — the first ethnic Uzbek in the post — and held it until 1937, driving forced collectivisation and the decision to make Uzbekistan the Soviet Union's main source of cotton, and in 1934 he was elected to the Central Committee of the All-Union party. The English article, citing Shoshana Keller's study of the Soviet campaign against Islam in Central Asia, records that he bore the most responsibility for designing the anti-Islamic measures of the first five-year plan and sometimes personally ordered the arrest of clergymen.{cite:wikipedia-akmal-ikramov-en}

Denounced by Stalin in August 1937 for failing to struggle against anti-Soviet elements, expelled from the party in September and arrested on 20 September 1937, Ikramov was a defendant at the Trial of the Twenty-One, where he confessed to Trotskyism, nationalist conspiracy and espionage. He was sentenced to death and shot at the Kommunarka shooting ground on 13 March 1938 — both articles note that other sources give 15 March — and was fully rehabilitated in June 1957, the first defendant of the show trials to be rehabilitated.{cite:wikipedia-akmal-ikramov-en}{cite:wikipedia-akmal-ikramov-ru}{cite:wikidata-akmal-ikramov}
