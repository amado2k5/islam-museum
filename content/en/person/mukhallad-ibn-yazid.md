## Overview
Mukhallad ibn Yazid al-Harrani, one of the trustworthy imams; he narrated from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari, Ja'far ibn Burqan, Ibn Jurayj, Hanzala ibn Abi Sufyan and al-Awza'i, while Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Ishaq, Ibn Numayr, Abu Bakr ibn Abi Shayba and others narrated from him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3809}

Abu Hatim called him truthful; he is cited as evidence in the Sahih collections, and died in 193 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3809}
