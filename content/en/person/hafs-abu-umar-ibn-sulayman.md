## Overview
Abu Umar Hafs ibn Sulayman ibn al-Mughira al-Asadi al-Kufi, one of the well-known transmitters from Asim ibn Abi al-Najud, one of the Seven Readers, and reader of Kufa. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.62}.

## His life
The encyclopaedia describes him as a trustworthy and precise reciter. It states that he was born in 90 AH (709 CE) and died in 180 AH (796 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.62}.
