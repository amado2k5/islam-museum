## Overview
Abu Umar Hafs ibn Sulayman ibn al-Mughira al-Asadi al-Kufi, one of the well-known transmitters from Asim ibn Abi al-Najud, one of the Seven Readers, and reader of Kufa. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.62}.

## His life
The encyclopaedia describes him as a trustworthy and precise reciter. It states that he was born in 90 AH (709 CE) and died in 180 AH (796 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.62}.

## Chain and reading in Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr

Ibn al-Jazari names him Hafs ibn Sulayman ibn al-Mughira al-Asadi al-Kufi al-Ghadhiri al-Bazzaz, and says he read on [[person/asim-ibn-abi-al-najud|'Asim]] together with [[person/shuba-abu-bakr-ibn-ayyash|Abu Bakr Shu'ba]]. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 165} He died in 180 AH on the sound view and was born in 90; he was the most knowledgeable of 'Asim's companions in 'Asim's reading and his stepson, the son of his wife. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 166} Ibn al-Jazari quotes Yahya ibn Ma'in that the sound narration of 'Asim's reading is Hafs's, Ibn al-Munadi that the early scholars ranked him above Ibn 'Ayyash in memory and described him as exact in the letters he read to 'Asim and as teaching people for a long time, and al-Dhahabi that in reading he is reliable, firm and exact, unlike his standing in hadith. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 167} Among his companions were 'Ubayd ibn al-Sabbah (d. 235), whom al-Dani calls among the most distinguished and exact of Hafs's companions, and 'Amr ibn al-Sabbah (d. 221), said by several to be 'Ubayd's brother. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 167}
