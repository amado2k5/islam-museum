## Summary
Muhammad ibn Salih al-Taymi had a present heart and a full mind. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 503, sequence 504, on page 143. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143}

## Reflection on the signs
Abu Bakr ibn Ubayd relates from Muhammad ibn Salih al-Taymi that a learned man, when he came to the verse on the signs for those who have certainty in the earth, said: I testify that the heavens and the earth and all in them are signs that point to You and bear witness to what You described Yourself by; each one gives Your proof, acknowledges Your lordship, and bears the marks of Your power and the landmarks of Your governance. The hearts are marked with knowledge of You, which comforts them against the loneliness of thought; they bear witness in their acknowledgement that the attributes do not encompass You and the imagination does not grasp You. The share of the one who reflects on You is acknowledgement of You and the affirmation of Your oneness. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143}
