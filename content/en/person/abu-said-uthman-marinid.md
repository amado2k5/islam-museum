## Overview
Sultan Abu Said Uthman ibn Yaqub ibn Abd al-Haqq al-Marini, one of the sultans of the [[polity/marinid-dynasty|Marinid state]], who ruled from 710 to 731 AH. He succeeded [[person/abu-al-rabi-sulayman-marinid|Abu al-Rabi Sulayman]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Coming to power
Ibn Khaldun relates that his sister Aribah worked for his allegiance until he took up the affair. He lifted grievances, removed levies and tolls, and freed the prisoners. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His reign
He invaded Tlemcen in 714 AH and besieged it at al-Mal'ab. In his time his son Abu Ali rebelled, and the revolt ended with his death. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun describes his state as the flower of the Banu Marin in prosperity and building, and says that he was given the khutba in al-Andalus. He died in 731 AH and his son Abu al-Hasan was given the allegiance. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
