## Summary
Nour El Tayeb is a former Egyptian professional squash player, born in Cairo on 8 March 1993. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} {cite:wikidata-nour-el-tayeb} She reached a career-high world ranking of No. 3 in 2018 and retired from the professional tour in July 2024. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en}

## Career
El Tayeb turned professional in 2007. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} The highlight of her junior career was the 2009 World Juniors, where at sixteen she beat top seed Dipika Pallikal before losing the final to her compatriot Nour El Sherbini; she won the world junior title itself in 2011 and was named WISPA Young Player of the Year for 2010. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} With Egypt she won the women's world team championship three times, in 2012, 2018 and 2022, with a bronze medal in 2014. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} In the individual world championship she took silver in the 2018–19 edition in Chicago and bronze in 2017 in Manchester. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} She stepped away from the tour during the 2020–21 season to give birth, then returned and re-entered the top 10. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} Soon after reaching the quarter-finals of the 2024 world championship she announced her retirement. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en}

## Personal life
El Tayeb is married to fellow squash professional Ali Farag; they married in 2016. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en} {cite:wikidata-nour-el-tayeb} At the 2017 US Open the pair became the first married couple to win the same major title on the same day. {cite:wikipedia-nour-el-tayeb-en}
