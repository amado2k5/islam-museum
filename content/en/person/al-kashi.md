## Summary

Ghiyath al-Din Jamshid ibn Mas'ud al-Kashi was a mathematician, astronomer and instrument-maker in Ulugh Beg's Samarkand environment. Precision, fractions, instruments and teaching texts feature prominently in his career. ## Calculation and books

The [[work/risala-muhitiyya-kashi|Muhitiyya]] contains the [[invention/al-kashi-pi-calculation|circumference computation]] dated1424. Okten's reported result approximates twice pi, not pi, and includes sexagesimal output. The Key to Arithmetic, completed1427, serves astronomy, surveying, architecture and practical arithmetic, addressing fractions, roots and architectural mensuration. These topics are not all consequences of the shorter Circumference {cite:kashi-mactutor Circumference and Key} {cite:tdv-kashi-okten mathematics}.

MacTutor qualifies unrestricted invention claims for decimals and roots by discussing al-Karaji's school and al-Samawal. The Chord and Sine treats sine one degree and an approximate cubic solution, with uncertainty about completion and Qadi Zada's role. It is not another title for the Muhitiyya or a separately counted invention in this batch {cite:kashi-mactutor decimals roots and final treatise}.
