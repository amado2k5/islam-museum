## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud al-Abdi, one of the men of the Umayyad period. Al-Baladhuri says that he spoke to [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] about digging the river "Thar", and Mu'awiya wrote to Ziyad, and the river of Ma'qil was dug, "so they say". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
