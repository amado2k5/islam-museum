## Summary
Abu Bakr Muhammad b. al-Sarri b. Sahl, known as Ibn al-Sarraj the grammarian; he took adab from Abu'l-'Abbas al-Mubarrad and others, and a company of notables took from him — Abu Sa'id al-Sirafi and 'Ali b. 'Isa al-Rummani among them — while al-Jawhari transmitted many passages from him in the Sihah. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Works
His famed grammatical works include the *Kitab al-Usul* — among the finest composed in this field and the resort when transmission is disturbed — *Jumal al-usul*, *al-Mujaz*, *al-Ishtiqaq*, a commentary on Sibawayh's book, *Ihtijaj al-qurra'*, *al-Shi'r wa'l-shu'ara'*, and treatises on wind, air, fire, camels and interconnections; he lisped the ra'. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
