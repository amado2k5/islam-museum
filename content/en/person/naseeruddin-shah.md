Naseeruddin Shah (born 20 July 1950) is an Indian actor and director, born at Barabanki in Uttar Pradesh and trained at the National School of Drama and the Film and Television Institute of India.{cite:wikipedia-naseeruddin-shah-en} A central figure of the parallel cinema movement from his debut in Nishant (1975), he won three National Film Awards and the Volpi Cup for Best Actor at Venice for Paar (1984), and co-founded the Motley theatre company.{cite:wikipedia-naseeruddin-shah-en} He received the Padma Shri in 1984 and the Padma Bhushan in 2003.{cite:wikipedia-naseeruddin-shah-en}{cite:wikidata-naseeruddin-shah}

In the concluding essay of his 2017 Hindustan Times series he wrote: "I abandoned religion then and have never felt the need for it" — and in the same essay called himself "no longer a practising Muslim"; both statements are preserved in this record's faith note.{cite:goan-observer-naseeruddin-shah-2017}
