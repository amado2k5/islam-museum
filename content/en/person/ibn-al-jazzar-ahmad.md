## Overview
Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Ibrahim ibn Abi Khalid Ahmad ibn al-Jazzar al-Qayrawani, a physician from Kairouan. The encyclopaedia counts him among the great physicians of his time, and he wrote on medicine and its drugs. He was born in 285 AH (898 CE) and died in 369 AH (980 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
