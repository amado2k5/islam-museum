## Overview
Muna Haydar (born 1988 in Chicago to a Syrian family) is an American rapper and poet. She is known for the song "My Hijab" (2017), which celebrates veiled women and discusses how society views them. She writes about Islam, motherhood and immigrants. {cite:wikidata}
