## Overview
Abu Numayy ibn Abi Sa'd al-Hasani, Sharif of [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and holder of its affairs and of the authority over the pilgrimage season. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1711-1712}

Ibn Khaldun says that he and his brother Idris carried the Abbasid call from the time Saladin turned it over to them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1711-1712}

## Allegiance to the Hafsid al-Mustansir
When the Tatars took Baghdad and erased the trace of the caliphate there, [[person/ibn-sabin|Ibn Sab'in]] came to him about the allegiance to the Hafsid al-Mustansir. He dictated the letter of allegiance and wrote it in his own hand, and he preached for al-Mustansir to the people of Mecca. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1711-1712}

The data do not record the date of his rule or of his death, both of which need an additional source.
