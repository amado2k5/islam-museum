## Overview
Abu Umayya Amr ibn al-Harith, one of those who passed to [[person/marthad-ibn-abd-allah-al-yazani|Marthad ibn Abd Allah al-Yazani]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

Rabi'a said: "There will remain jurisprudence in that west as long as that short man is there", meaning Amr ibn al-Harith. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}
