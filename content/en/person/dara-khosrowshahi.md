# Dara Khosrowshahi

Dara Khosrowshahi (born 28 May 1969) is an Iranian-American business executive, chief executive of Uber since August 2017 and previously chief executive of Expedia. The English, Arabic and Persian articles and Wikidata give his birth on 28 May 1969 at Tehran. No Urdu edition of his article exists; this file's companion Urdu text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-en} {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-ar} {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-fa} {cite:wikidata-khosrowshahi}

## Early life and education

Khosrowshahi was born in Tehran into the prominent Khosrowshahi family, associated with the Alborz Investment Company. After the Iranian revolution began, the family fled Iran in 1978 by way of southern France and settled with relatives in Tarrytown, New York. He was educated at the Hackley School and took a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering at Brown University in 1991. {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-en}

## Career

Khosrowshahi began his career at the investment bank Allen & Company, then moved to IAC, where he rose to chief financial officer. He became chief executive of Expedia in August 2005 and led it for twelve years. In August 2017 he was chosen to succeed Travis Kalanick as chief executive of Uber, the ride-hailing and delivery company, steering it to its 2019 stock-market listing and through its expansion into food delivery. {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-en}

## Personal life

He married Sydney Shapiro in 2012; they have twin sons. {cite:wikipedia-khosrowshahi-en}
