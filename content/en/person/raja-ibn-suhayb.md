## Summary
Abu Ghassan Raja' ibn Suhayb al-Jurwani, one of those who turned away from the base world and left it. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 670, sequence 671, on page 392. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}

## The world as a road
He used to say: how good a house the world is as a road to Paradise; whoever takes the world as a road does not stop at what is in it. The world is the road of the wise, who gained their souls in it and departed from it. The heading gives his father's name as Suhayb; the body of the notice reads it as Husayb in the scan, and the heading reading is used here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}
