## Overview
Salma bint Hafsa, widow of [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha al-Shaybani]]. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas married her after him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.610-612}.

## Abu Mahjan
On the night of al-Qadisiyya she released [[person/abu-mahjan-al-thaqafi|Abu Mahjan al-Thaqafi]] from his prison, on the condition that he would return his leg to the shackle. She lent him al-Balqa'. Then she told Sa'd her story, and he released him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.610-612}.

## Her dispute with Sa'd
A dispute arose between her and Sa'd, which angered him, because she had mentioned al-Muthanna. She remained angry with him until she made peace with him on the morning after the night of al-Harir {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.610-612}.
