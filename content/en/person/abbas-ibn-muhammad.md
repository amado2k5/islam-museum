## Overview
Al-'Abbas ibn Muhammad al-Hashimi, their client, al-Duri al-Baghdadi, Abu al-Fadl, was one of the huffaz of hadith, trustworthy; he died in 271 AH (884 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.264}

He wrote a book on rijal transmitted from Yahya ibn Ma'in. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.264}
