## Overview
Mansur ibn Nasr ibn al-Husayn al-Harrani and then al-Baghdadi, Abu Bakr, Zahir al-Din ibn al-Attar, was a secretary and vizier {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}. He was keeper of the Makhzan for the caliphs and deputy of the vizierate in Egypt, and al-Zirikli says his conduct was not good {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}.

## Vizierate
He took the vizierate for the Abbasid caliph al-Mustadi' in 573 AH in Baghdad after the killing of the vizier Ibn Hubayra, and al-Zirikli says Zahir al-Din was the cause of that killing {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}. Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Khaldun that he seized the state and ruled it at will {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}. He quotes Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi that Zahir al-Din intended to install Abu Mansur as caliph, that the plan failed, and that when the Imam al-Nasir received the pledge of allegiance he did not attend and excused himself by illness {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}.

## End
Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi, as quoted, says al-Nasir seized him, held him some days and brought him out of the prison dead, with marks of beating on him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}. Al-A'lam also quotes «al-Bidaya wal-Nihaya» that on 7 Dhu al-Qa'da 575 the keeper of the Makhzan, Zahir al-Din ibn al-Attar, was dismissed and gravely humiliated together with his companions, many of whom were killed and some paraded through the city {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}. It places his death in 575 AH, equal to 1180 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 296}.
