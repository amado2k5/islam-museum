## Summary
Ibn Hisham names him among those in Suwaylim's house when Talha burned it; he broke out through the house's back and his leg broke, and he spoke verses on it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Hisham names him in the account of the burning of Suwaylim's house: he broke through the back of the house and his leg broke; his companions broke out and escaped. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160} Verses he spoke on it are ascribed to him, saying he would not do the like again. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text does not ascribe him to a tribe, nor state here his Islam or hypocrisy beyond the group's general description as "hypocrites" in the report.
