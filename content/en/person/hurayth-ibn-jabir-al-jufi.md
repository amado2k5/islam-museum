## Overview
Hurayth ibn Jabir al-Ju'fi, one of the companions of Ali at Siffin. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.676}

## On the killing of Ubayd Allah ibn Umar
Al-Mas'udi reports that it was said he was the one who stabbed Ubayd Allah ibn Umar and killed him. It was also said that it was al-Ashtar al-Nakha'i. It was also said that Ali himself struck him a blow that cut through the iron until it reached the lining of his belly. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.676}

The accounts are many in the source, and no preference is given among them here.
