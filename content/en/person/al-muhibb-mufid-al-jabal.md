## Overview
The muhaddith Mufid al-Jabal (d. 12 Jumada II 658, aged forty); he transmitted from al-Shaykh al-Muwaffaq, Ibn al-Bann and Ibn al-Zubaydi, traveled to Baghdad and heard al-Qabiti, Ibn al-Fakhar and their tier, and wrote much — devoting himself to hadith most completely in hearing and writing {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 300}.
