## Overview
Muhammad ibn Dawud ibn al-Jarrah, a Baghdadi vizier from the Banu al-Jarrah family of secretaries. Ibn al-Athir says that he was among the leaders of those who rose to depose al-Muqtadir billah in 296 AH. When Ibn al-Mu'tazz was given the title of caliph, he made him vizier, and when the oath of allegiance collapsed, he hid in his house. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 11-13}
