## Overview
Qays ibn al-Mulawwah, the Majnun of the Banu Amir; Abu'l-Hayaj ibn Sabiq al-Najdi met him in the wilderness of the Banu Amir — he fled from him like a wild thing until he grew tame — and when he recited Qays ibn Dharīḥ's verse to him he wept and said 'by God I am a better poet than he', and recited 'the heart refuses all but her love — an Amiri woman; she has the kunya of Amr but no Amr'.
