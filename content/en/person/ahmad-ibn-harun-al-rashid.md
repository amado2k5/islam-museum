## Overview
Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad b. Harun al-Rashid b. al-Mahdi b. al-Mansur, the Hashimi; a righteous servant who abandoned the world in his father's lifetime though able to have it, preferring withdrawal and seclusion — he was surnamed for working with his hands on Saturday a thing he spent through the week, devoting the rest to worship. Ibn al-Jawzi includes him in Safwat al-safwa. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
