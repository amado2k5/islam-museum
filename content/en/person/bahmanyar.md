## Overview
Bahmanyar ibn al-Marzuban al-Adharbayjani, Abu al-Hasan, was a philosopher and one of the pupils of [[person/ibn-sina|Ibn Sina]]. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 458 AH, 1066 CE, and prints no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 77}.

## Life
He had been a Zoroastrian and became a Muslim {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 77}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names Ma ba'd al-Tabi'a (printed), Maratib al-Mawjudat (printed) and al-Tahsil (manuscript), on philosophy and logic {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 77}. He notes a library copy of al-Tahsil in Damascus of 340 folios, and a part of it in the Vatican (Arabic 1414) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 77}.
