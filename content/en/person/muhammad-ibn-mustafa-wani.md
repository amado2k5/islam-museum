## Overview
Muhammad ibn Mustafa al-Wani, known as Wan Quli, was a Hanafi jurist of Rum, the Ottoman lands. The entry dates his death to 1000 AH, equal to 1592 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Career
He held posts of teaching and judgeship in his country {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Works
He wrote works in Arabic, of which al-Zirikli names Ithbat al-Masmu'at, in manuscript; Ta'liqat on Durar al-Hukkam fi Sharh Ghurar al-Ahkam, in manuscript, on Hanafi jurisprudence, called Naqd al-Durar; and a treatise on the disliked character of dhikr and of the salat al-ragha'ib, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}. He translated into Turkish the Sihah of al-Jawhari and the Kimiya al-Sa'ada of al-Ghazali {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Death
He died in 1000 AH, equal to 1592 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.
