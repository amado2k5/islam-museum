## Overview
Ibn A'isha al-Qurashi, singer of al-Walid ibn Yazid; he sang to him 'verily I saw, on the morning of the sacrifice, houris', and al-Walid bade him repeat it — by Abd Shams, by Umayya, climbing ancestor by ancestor until he reached himself and swore by his own life — then fell upon him, threw his own clothes over him, granted him a thousand dinars and mounted him on his mule.
