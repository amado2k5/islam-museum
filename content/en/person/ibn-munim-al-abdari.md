## From Denia to Marrakech

Abu Jafar Ahmad ibn Ibrahim ibn Ali ibn Munim al-Abdari came from Denia in al-Andalus and settled in Marrakech, teaching at the Mansuriyya dome near the mosque. Birth and arrival years are unknown. Abd al-Aziz al-Naqr reports a researcher's suggested invitation from al-Mansur, not an invitation document we read. He distinguishes the Sicilian Muhammad ibn Isa ibn Abd al-Munim: a similar name does not transfer books between people {cite:arrabita-ibn-munim-naqr sections1,3}.

## Teaching and medicine

The biography reports learning from his father, practising medicine around thirty, teaching and mathematical distinction. Mentally reviewing Euclid in reverse before sleep is an anecdote attributed to an older biography, not our observation or a memory test.
