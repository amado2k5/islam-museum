## Overview

Abu al-Hasan Rawh ibn 'Abd al-Mu'min ibn 'Abda ibn Muslim al-Hudhali al-Basri, a grammarian, is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/yaqub-al-hadrami|Ya'qub al-Hadrami]], with [[person/ruways-muhammad-ibn-al-mutawakkil|Ruways]]. He died in 234 or 235 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 197}

## His standing

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an eminent, reliable, exact and famous reader, among the most distinguished and trustworthy of Ya'qub's companions, and notes that al-Bukhari narrated from him in his Sahih. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 197} He counts forty-four lines of transmission from Rawh, among them those of Ibn Wahb and al-Zubayri. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 196}
