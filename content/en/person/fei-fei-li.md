# Fei-Fei Li

Fei-Fei Li (李飞飞; born 3 July 1976) is a Chinese-American computer scientist, a professor at Stanford University and one of the leading researchers in computer vision and artificial intelligence. Born in Beijing and raised in Chengdu, she emigrated to the United States as a teenager, studied at Princeton and earned her doctorate at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech). {cite:wikipedia-fei-fei-li-en} {cite:wikipedia-fei-fei-li-ar} {cite:wikidata-fei-fei-li}

## ImageNet and Stanford

Li is best known for creating the ImageNet dataset and its associated challenge, which enabled the rapid advances in deep learning and computer vision in the 2010s. A professor of computer science at Stanford, she co-founded the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) and co-founded AI4ALL, a nonprofit working to increase diversity in artificial intelligence. In 2024 she founded World Labs, a company working on "spatial intelligence". She has been elected to the National Academy of Engineering, the National Academy of Medicine and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. {cite:wikipedia-fei-fei-li-en} {cite:wikipedia-fei-fei-li-ur}
