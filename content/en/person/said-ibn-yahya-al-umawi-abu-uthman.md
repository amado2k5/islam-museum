## Overview
Abu Uthman Sa'id ibn Yahya ibn Sa'id ibn Aban al-Umawi, a traditionist and jurist. His origins were in Kufa, and he settled in Baghdad. He narrated hadith from his paternal uncles and from the scholars of his age {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
The encyclopedia gives his death as 249 AH, corresponding to 803 CE. The two dates do not match, so only the printed Common Era year has been recorded. This needs an additional source {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
