## Overview
Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man al-Ahwal, who lodged at Taq al-Mahamil in Kufa. The common people called him "the devil of the vault" (Shaytan al-Taq), and his followers called him "the believer of the vault" (Mu'min al-Taq). Ibn al-Nadim counts him among the companions of [[person/jafar-al-sadiq|Ja'far al-Sadiq]], and relates that he met Zayd ibn Ali Zayn al-Abidin and disputed with him on the imamate. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 532}

## The title
Ibn al-Nadim relates that the title came from his witnessing and examining dinars: some people disputed with him over a dinar, and he judged it counterfeit, and he was right and gave them the proof. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as sound in belief, skilled in kalam, and quick in reply. He reports disputes between him and [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]]. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 532}

## His books
Among his books according to Ibn al-Nadim are: the *Book of the Imamate*, the *Book of Knowledge*, a *Refutation of the Mu'tazila on the Imamate of the Less Excellent*, and a book on the matter of Talha, al-Zubayr and A'isha. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 250}

The date of his death is not recorded in the museum's data. The data cite two different pages of Ibn al-Nadim, which needs review.
