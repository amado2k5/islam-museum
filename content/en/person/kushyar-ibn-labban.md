## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Kushyar ibn Labban al-Jili, an astronomer and mathematician, whose nisba refers to Gilan in northern Iran. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.612}

## Dates
According to the encyclopaedia he was born around 360 AH (969 CE) and died around 420 AH (1029 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.612} His works and biography need an additional source.
