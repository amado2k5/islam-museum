## Summary
Abu Burayda al-Aslami, chief of his people; he set out chasing Quraysh's bounty and became Muslim upon meeting {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}.

## On the hijra road
He went out after the Prophet and Abu Bakr hoping to win the great reward Quraysh had proclaimed; when he faced the Prophet and spoke with him he became Muslim on the spot, with seventy men of his people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}.

Then he took off his turban, bound it to his spear and made it a banner proclaiming that the king of security and peace had come to fill the world with justice and equity {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}.
