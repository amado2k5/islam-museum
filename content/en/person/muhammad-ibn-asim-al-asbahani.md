Muhammad ibn Asim ibn Yahya, Abu 'Abd Allah al-Asbahani, known as "the judge's secretary," journeyed for learning and studied under al-Shafi'i's companions and Ibn Wahb. He heard 'Ali b. Harb and Salama b. Shabib, transmitted to Ahmad b. Bandar and al-Tabarani, and — in Abu'l-Shaykh's words — composed many books. He died in 299.
