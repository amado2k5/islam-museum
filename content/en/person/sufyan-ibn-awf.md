## Summary
Sufyan ibn Awf ibn al-Mughaffal ibn Awf ibn Umayr ibn Kalb ibn Dhuhl ibn Sayyar al-Ghamidi al-Azdi, a Companion and commander of the summer expeditions (al-sawa'if) under Mu'awiya, who held him in honour; he raided deep into Byzantine territory to the gates of Constantinople and died about 53 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.588} {cite:al-aalam}.

## In al-Isaba and al-A'lam
Ibn Hajar records in al-Isaba that Sufyan ibn Awf al-Ghamidi was the commander of the summer expeditions, that Mu'awiya appointed him over them and honoured him, and transmits the report of his raid on Hit and al-Anbar alluded to by Ali in a sermon; Ibn Asakir gives his lineage through al-Mughaffal, Awf, Umayr, Kalb, Dhuhl, Sayyar and Waliba back to Ghamid, styling him sahib al-sawa'if {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.588}. Khalifa dated his death to 53 AH and Abu Ubayd to 54 {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.588}. Al-Zirikli reports that Mu'awiya sent him with an army into Byzantine lands, where he penetrated to the gates of Constantinople and was killed there {cite:al-aalam}.

## For young visitors
Sufyan ibn Awf was a Companion who led the summer campaigns under Mu'awiya; his raids reached the gates of Constantinople, where he was killed.

## Research notes
- The year of death is 53 AH in Khalifa and 54 in Abu Ubayd; al-Zirikli has him killed near Constantinople — the reports are given as transmitted.
- The Isaba notice is read from the Dar al-Faysaliyya facsimile (pp. 588-589); the record's filed source is the 1328 Sa'ada edition — either citation is to Ibn Hajar's text, and the Sa'ada page may be added on checking.
