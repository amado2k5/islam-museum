## Overview
Athir al-Din Abu Hayyan the grammarian, author of "Irtishaf al-Darab min Lisan al-Arab" in grammar, in two volumes. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.305}

## His work
The book is mentioned in [[work/irtishaf-al-darab|Irtishaf al-Darab]], and it appears in the "Kashf al-Zunun" of al-Qustantini. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.305}

## Notes for researchers
- The source is single; a second source is needed. The dates of his birth and death are not in the data.

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Yusuf Ibn Hayyan of Granada, Athir al-Din Abu Hayyan al-Andalusi: born at the end of Shawwal 654, he came to Egypt in 679. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/302} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/304} His works include al-Bahr al-Muhit and a commentary on al-Tashil, and he wrote on the languages of the Turks, Persians and Ethiopians. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/304} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/305} Ibn Hajar quotes al-Safadi calling him the absolute master of grammar and morphology. He died at his home outside Bab al-Bahr in Safar 745, having gone blind shortly before. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/310}
