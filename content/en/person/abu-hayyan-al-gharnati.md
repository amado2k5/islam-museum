## Overview
Athir al-Din Abu Hayyan the grammarian, author of "Irtishaf al-Darab min Lisan al-Arab" in grammar, in two volumes. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.305}

## His work
The book is mentioned in [[work/irtishaf-al-darab|Irtishaf al-Darab]], and it appears in the "Kashf al-Zunun" of al-Qustantini. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.305}
