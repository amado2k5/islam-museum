## Overview
Al-Hirbadh, lord of Darabjird. In al-Baladhuri's wording he was the pillar of the knowledge and religion of the people of Persia. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
He made peace with Uthman ibn Abi al-As for a sum of money he gave him, and on condition that the people of Darabjird should be on a par with those whose lands were opened. It is also said that he made peace over Fasa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
