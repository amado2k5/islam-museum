Urwa ibn Mas'ud ibn Mu'tib al-Thaqafi, chief of Ta'if's people and one of the Arabs' counted notables for wisdom and counsel. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Quraysh sent him as negotiator to the Prophet at al-Hudaybiyya for his experience; he watched the Prophet's companions with a seeing eye and was amazed at the reverence and devotion he found in them — whenever the Prophet spat, it fell in a man's palm and he rubbed his face with it; when he made ablution they near-fought over its water. He returned to Quraysh dazzled and said: "people — by God I have been envoy to Kisra, to Qaysar and to the Negus in their kingdoms, and I never saw a king whose companions honour him as Muhammad's companions honour Muhammad; he has offered you a course of right guidance — accept it". {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

He became Muslim in year nine of the hijra after returning from the siege of Ta'if, went back to his people Thaqif calling them to tawhid, and a man shot him with an arrow while he called the prayer on the roof of his house — so he was martyred. The Prophet said of him: "his likeness among his people is the likeness of Ya-Sin's man among his people". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
