Urwa ibn Masud ibn Muattib al-Thaqafi was the chief of the people of al-Taif and one of the best-known Arab nobles for wisdom and judgement. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Quraysh sent him as a negotiator to the Prophet at Hudaybiyya because of his experience. He observed the Prophet's Companions with a discerning eye, and he saw in them a reverence and devotion that astonished him. Whenever the Prophet spat, it fell into the palm of one of them, who rubbed it on his face. When he performed ablution, they nearly fought over the water he had used. He returned to Quraysh amazed and said, “O people, by God, I have been received by Kisra, Caesar and the Negus in their kingdoms, and I have never seen a king whom his companions honour as the companions of Muhammad honour Muhammad. He has offered you a right course, so accept it.” {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

He became Muslim in the ninth year of the Hijra, after returning from the siege of al-Taif. He went back to his people, Thaqif, to call them to monotheism. A man shot him with an arrow while he was calling to prayer on the upper level of his house, and he was killed as a martyr. The Prophet said about him, “His likeness among his people is like the man of Ya-Sin among his people.” {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Urwa ibn Masud caught up with the Prophet on the road before he reached Medina, and he became Muslim. He asked the Prophet to let him go back to his people with Islam. The Prophet said that they would fight him. He said, “I am dearer to them than their own virgin daughters.” {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/182} When he called them to Islam from a raised room of his house, they shot him with arrows and killed him. His people attributed his killer to two men from the Banu Malik and the Ahlaf, and Ibn Ishaq did not prefer either account. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/182} See [[event/delegation-of-thaqif|the delegation of Thaqif]].
