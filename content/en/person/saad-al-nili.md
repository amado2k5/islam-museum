## Summary
Sa'd ibn Ahmad ibn Makki al-Nili — named for Nil, a town on the Euphrates — tutor and poet whose verse is mostly panegyric of the Prophet's house. Al-'Imad said he was an extreme Shi'i, bound by scrupulosity and learned in adab. He died in 592. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 789}
