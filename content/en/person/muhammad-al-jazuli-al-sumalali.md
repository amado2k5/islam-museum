## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Jazuli al-Sumalali, from the Sus mountains of Morocco, author of the well-known *Dala'il al-Khayrat* (Guides to Goodness) on prayers upon the Prophet. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.272}

He died in 870 AH (around 1465 CE). The encyclopaedia classes him among the Sufis and the scholars, and attributes to him the quality of sainthood. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.272}

His year of birth and the details of his life are not recorded in the museum's data, and need an additional source.
