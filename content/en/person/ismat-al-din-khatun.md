## Summary
'Ismat al-Din Khatun, daughter of the amir Mu'in al-Din Anar — wife of Nur al-Din Mahmud the martyr, then of Sultan Salah al-Din; endower of the Hanafi madrasa at Damascus, she built a khanqah for the Sufis on the southern rise outside Bab al-Nasr and a turbah at Qasyun on the Yazid river facing Jaraks's dome, where she was buried — today within the new mosque at the Salihiyya — and she endowed all these places richly; she died in 581. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 752}
