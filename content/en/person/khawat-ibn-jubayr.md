## Overview
Khawwat ibn Jubayr ibn al-Nu'man ibn Umayya al-Ansari al-Awsi, brother of 'Abdallah ibn Jubayr, the Badr veteran and commander of the archers; his kunya was Abu Salih — some say Abu 'Abdallah. In the Jahiliyya he was the keeper of Dhat al-Nahyayn, then he embraced Islam and excelled. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1639}

## Badr and death
He set out for Badr but at al-Rawha a stone's edge struck and broke his leg; the Messenger of God sent him back to Medina yet allotted him his share and reward, so he counted as one who had attended. He died in Medina in 40 AH aged seventy-four; he dyed his hair and was a stocky man. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1639}
