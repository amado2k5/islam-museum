## Overview
Qahtaba ibn Shabib al-Ta'i. al-Tabari mentions his death in 132 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.412}

The museum's data contain nothing beyond this; his biography needs an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] says Qahtaba was among those who met Abu Muslim in the prison at Wasit, and the second man in Khurasan to wear black. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 337} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 339} He then marched west: Tus, Jurjan, Rayy, Isfahan, Nihawand and Hulwan. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 364} He camped between Baghdad and al-Anbar before Baghdad was built, when it was a village with a monthly market. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 368} Facing Ibn Hubayra on the Euphrates, he ordered the horses into the water with himself in front, and disappeared; some people claim his horse plunged and he drowned. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 369}
