## Overview
Al-Husayn b. 'Ali, Abu 'Abdallah of Basra, known as al-Jall (d. c. 368); he dwelt at Baghdad among the shaykhs of the Mu'tazila, wrote on their doctrines and followed in the branches the madhhab of the people of Iraq; Imam Abu 'Ali al-Farisi prayed over him and he was buried in the mausoleum of his teacher Abu'l-Hasan al-Karkhi on Darb al-Hasan b. Zayd, at nearly eighty. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.106}
