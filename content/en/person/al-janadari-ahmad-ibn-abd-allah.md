## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhsin al-Qahit, known as al-Janadari, a Yemeni scholar attributed to al-Jund. He was born in 1279 AH (1862 CE) and died in 1337 AH (1918 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
