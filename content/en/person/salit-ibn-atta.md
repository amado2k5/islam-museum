## Overview
Salit ibn Atta al-Hanafi, a messenger sent by Wakī' ibn Hassan with the head of Qutayba ibn Muslim to Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik. He belongs to the Umayyad period {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
