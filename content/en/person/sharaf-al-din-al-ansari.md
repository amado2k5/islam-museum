## Overview
Sharaf al-Din ibn Zayn al-'Abidin, grandson of the qadi Zakariyya al-Ansari al-Suniki al-Misri, was a learned man of Egypt who died at Cairo in 1092 AH (1681 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.160}

He wrote works including al-Tabaqat, in which he recorded his shaykhs and the scholars of his age. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.160}
