## Overview
Khalid ibn al-Harith ibn Ghallab of the Banu Dahman. He was in charge of the treasury in Isfahan. He is the Ibn Ghallab whom Abu al-Mukhtar mentions in his qasida. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
