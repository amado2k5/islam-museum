## Overview
Zaganos Pasha — 'Zughnush' in al-Salabi's text — a commander in Mehmed II's army, described by the book as an Albanian-born convert to Islam {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 105}.

## At the war council
On the eve of the general assault on Constantinople the vizier Khalil Pasha counselled withdrawal; Zaganos cried out in his Albanian-accented Turkish: 'God forbid, O Sultan — I will never accept what Khalil Pasha said, for we came here to die, not to return,' arguing that Alexander's army which conquered half of Asia was no larger than theirs {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 105-106}.

## The outcome
Commander Tarkhan and the shaykhs Aq Shams al-Din and al-Kurani backed him; gladness showed on the Conqueror's face, and the council ended with the assault imminent {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 106}.
