# Zainab Hassan

## Summary
Zainab Hassan is a British content creator and the founder of The Documented Life, a wellness brand and community platform centred on intentional living. In October 2026 she won the Content Creator award at the Black Muslim Awards in London; Amaliah's report of the ceremony describes her as much more than a beauty influencer. {cite:amaliah-black-muslim-awards}

## The Documented Life
The brand's signature product is a journal for reflection, intention and honest conversations with yourself, and its products include tote bags printed with Hausa proverbs. {cite:thedocumentedlife} Her YouTube channel, @Zainabhassanxo, carries the same banner: its description reads "My documented life". {cite:youtube-zainabhassanxo}
