## Summary
Abd al-Majid Taliqani was an Iranian calligrapher whom Maryam Ekhtiar names among the great masters of the [[concept/shikasta-script|shikasta]] ("broken") script, with dates from 1737 to about 1771. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 49}

## His letter at The Met
The Met holds a letter in shikasta signed by him and dated 1176 AH/1763 CE: eight lines, six written diagonally and two horizontally at the bottom. Its unusual composition and the upward sloping and stacking of words at the end of each line show the characteristic features of shikasta, and the sweep of the descending letters and the airy flow of the text enhance its composition. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 49}
