## Summary
Hasher Al Maktoum is an Emirati racing driver, born in Dubai on 29 April 1985. {cite:wikipedia-hasher-al-maktoum-de} {cite:wikidata-hasher-al-maktoum}

## Career
A two-time karting champion of the United Arab Emirates, Al Maktoum made his first start in an international motor racing series in the FIA GT Championship in 2004, and raced in the French Formula Renault Campus series in 2006, finishing fifteenth in the standings. {cite:wikipedia-hasher-al-maktoum-de} He reached international attention through the Speedcar Series, where he raced for Union Properties in 2008 and finished thirteenth in the drivers' standings while his team mate David Terrien was runner-up. {cite:wikipedia-hasher-al-maktoum-de} In the following season he scored points in every race he finished and took his first and only podium in the series with third place at the season finale, improving to seventh in the standings. {cite:wikipedia-hasher-al-maktoum-de}
