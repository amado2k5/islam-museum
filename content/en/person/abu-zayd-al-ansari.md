## Overview
Abu Zayd al-Ansari, one of those who collected the Qur'an in the time of the Prophet, according to Abu Mikhnaf. He was killed at the Battle of the Bridge. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.352}

The Battle of the Bridge took place at the end of Ramadan 13 AH according to al-Baladhuri. That date is reported from one source and needs an additional source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.352}
