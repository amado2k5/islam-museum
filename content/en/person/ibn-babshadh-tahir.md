## Overview
Tahir ibn Ahmad ibn Babshadh, Abu al-Hasan al-Jawhari al-Nahwi, from Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He worked in the jewel trade, then taught grammar in the mosque of Amr in Cairo. He wrote a commentary on grammar that became known among the grammarians. The encyclopaedia gives his death as 454 AH (1063 CE), according to report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
