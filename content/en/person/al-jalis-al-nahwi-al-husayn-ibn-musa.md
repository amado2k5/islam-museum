## Overview
Abu Abd Allah al-Husayn ibn Musa ibn Hibat Allah al-Dinawari, known as al-Jalis al-Nahwi, a grammarian and philologist from Dinawar. He died in 490 AH (1097 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
