## Overview
Qadi Abu Bakr b. Muhammad b. 'Abdallah al-Yafa'i; the author of al-Bayan attended his death and said "manliness has died." He took fiqh from Zayd al-Baqa'i, was a learned poet, held the judgeship of Yemen, had great sway with kings, freed Yemen's jurists from the kharaj and the oppressions, and when the qadi al-Rashid came from Egypt to Yemen he honoured him greatly; his tarjama is in the annals of 552. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}
