## Overview
Muhriz al-Mudliji, the physiognomist whose reading the Prophet confirmed, so that a body of jurists of the garrison towns invoked him in ruling on qiyafa — marvelling at his gift and at the confirmation of it; in al-Waqidi's account, as al-Mas'udi relates, he was killed on the day of the Wood among five or six of the polytheists whom the Prophet himself slew in that expedition.
