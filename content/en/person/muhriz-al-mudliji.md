## Overview
Muhriz al-Mudliji, a *qa'if* (one who traces tracks to establish lineage). Al-Mas'udi says that the Prophet confirmed his skill as a *qa'if*, and some jurists of the cities relied on this to rule by *qiyafa* (judgement by the tracker's signs). {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 481}

## The day of al-Ghaba
Al-Mas'udi relates from al-Waqidi that he was killed on the day of al-Ghaba among five or six of the polytheists, whom the Prophet killed with his own hand in that expedition. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 580}
