## Summary
[[person/sulayman-ibn-surad|Sulayman ibn Surad]] ibn al-Jawn al-Khuza'i, Abu al-Mutarrif. It is said his name was Yasar and [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] changed it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}.

## Narration
He related from the Prophet, from Ali, Abu al-Hasan and Jubayr ibn Mut'im; from him related Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i, Yahya ibn Ya'mar, Abdullah ibn Yasar and Abu al-Duha {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}. Ibn Hajar describes him as "good and virtuous" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}.

## After the Prophet
He witnessed Siffin with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and killed Hawshab in single combat {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}. Then he was among those who wrote to [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]], then fell behind him; he and al-Musayyab ibn Najaba came forward last of all and went out seeking his blood, four thousand strong {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}.

## His death
Ubaydullah ibn Ziyad met them at Ayn al-Warda with Marwan's army, and Sulayman and those with him were killed — in Rabi' al-Akhir of year 65 AH, when he was ninety-three on the day he was killed. His killer was Yazid ibn al-Husayn ibn Numayr, who hit him with an arrow; his head and al-Musayyab's were carried to Marwan {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 611}.
