## Overview
Hunnad ibn al-Sari ibn Mus'ab al-Tamimi al-Darimi was a hadith scholar and ascetic, one of the memorisers, and the shaykh of Kufa in his time; he was called the monk of Kufa, for he neither married nor kept a concubine. He died in 243 AH (857 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.95}

He wrote a Kitab al-Zuhd. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.95}
