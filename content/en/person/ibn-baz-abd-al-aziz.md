## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Abdallah Al Baz, a Saudi scholar born in Riyadh in 1330 AH (1912 CE). An illness affected his eyes during his early studies. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He died in 1420 AH (1999 CE), according to report. The data hold no other details about him, and it needs an additional source.
