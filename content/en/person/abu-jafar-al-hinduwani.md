## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Muhammad, Abu Ja'far al-Balkhi al-Hinduwani. Ibn al-Imad lists him in Shadharat al-Dhahab among those who died in 362 AH, a year that corresponds to about 972 and 973 CE (derived conversion) {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 328}.

## His epithet and learning
Ibn al-Imad says that because of his skill in jurisprudence he was called Little Abu Hanifa {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 329}. He transmitted hadith from Muhammad ibn Aqil al-Balkhi and others {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 329}.

## Death and standing
According to Ibn al-Imad he died in Bukhara and was the shaykh of those lands in his time {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 329}.

## His nisba
Ibn al-Imad gives the vowelling of al-Hinduwani (ha with kasra, dal with damma) and says the nisba is to Bab Hinduwan, a quarter of Balkh {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 329}.
