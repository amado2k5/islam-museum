## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn al-Ma'mun, son of the caliph al-Ma'mun. Al-Mas'udi relates that he was secretary to the king of the Romans, and that some people pledged allegiance to him in the army of al-Mu'tasim while he was at Amorium in 223 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}

## His end
Al-Mu'tasim hastened on his march, imprisoned him and his partisans, and al-Abbas died that year. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}
