## Overview
Burtuqiz, whose name is Yusuf ibn Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Abd Allah, kunya Abu al-Mahasin, and nisba al-Zughwani, a Hanafi jurist from Zaghwan in Tunisia. The encyclopaedia says he was learned in the principles of jurisprudence, cared for the restrictions of writing, and composed verse. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.289}

## Life
According to the encyclopaedia he was born around 1092 AH (1681 CE) and died in 1148 AH (1735 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.289}
