## Summary
Amir ibn Hamdawayh al-Zahid settled in Musayla and kept the company of Sufyan al-Thawri. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 663, sequence 664, on page 390. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}

## His transmission from Sufyan
The narrator says he heard Amir ibn Hamdawayh narrate questions from Sufyan al-Thawri. The notice gives nothing further. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}
