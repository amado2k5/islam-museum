## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ali al-Ahdab al-Tarabulusi (1242–1308 AH / 1826–1891 CE), a man of letters, poet, author, and jurist from Tripoli in Syria. He left a large legacy of books and treatises. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.302-304}

## What the encyclopaedia records
The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* counts him among the scholars and among the poets, and attributes him to the People of the Sunna. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.302-304}
