## Overview
Hormuzan, a Persian commander over Ahvaz. Battles took place between him and the Muslims, and then he asked for a settlement and corresponded with Utba ibn Ghazwan about it. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.74}

## His killing
The index of the fourth volume of al-Tabari's history states that Ubayd Allah ibn Umar killed Hormuzan in 24 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.580}

## His counsel
Al-Baladhuri transmits from Ma'qil ibn Yasar that Umar consulted him: should he begin with Isfahan or with Azerbaijan? Hormuzan said that Isfahan is the head and Azerbaijan the two wings, and that if the head is cut off the two wings fall. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
