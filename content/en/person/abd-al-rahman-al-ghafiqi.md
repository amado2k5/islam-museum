## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah al-Ghafiqi was amir of al-Andalus appointed by Ubayd Allah ibn al-Habhab, lord of Ifriqiya. He entered it in 113 AH and raided the Franks, and his army was struck in Ramadan 114 AH at a place known as the Court of the Martyrs, from which the raid takes its name. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 272}

## His governorship
Al-Maqqari says that his governorship lasted one year and eight months. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 272} The study by al-Salabi says that he took command of the army after the killing of al-Samh ibn Malik in the battle of Tuluza, withdrew to Narbonne, and remained amir with the al-Andalus people's presentation of him until the new governor arrived. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.300}

The same book says that he put down some risings in the northern mountain provinces. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.431}

## The Court of the Martyrs
His name is linked in the sources with the battle of [[place/balat-al-shuhada|the Court of the Martyrs]], the place where his army was struck. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 272}
