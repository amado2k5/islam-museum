## Overview
Yazid ibn Mufarrigh al-Himyari the poet, of Rabi'a, settled at Tabala in the Hijaz; a biting satirist with praised panegyric too, his verse widely circulated. He satirised 'Ubaydallah ibn Ziyad, who sought his death from Mu'awiya; his famous line: "The slave is beaten with the stick; the free man — a rebuke suffices." He died in 69 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4231}
