## Summary
Al-Mufaddal ibn Salama ibn Asim, Abu Talib al-Dabbi, a philologist known for works on the arts of literature and the meanings of the Qur'an. He studied with Ibn al-A'rabi and other well-known scholars, and al-Suli and others studied with him. His works include al-Bari' fi ilm al-lugha and Diya' al-qulub on the meanings of the Qur'an {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13763}. The book gives his death as 290 AH.
