## Overview
Anbasa ibn Ishaq al-Dabbi, governor of Egypt in al-Maqrizi's account of al-Baladhuri. Al-Mutawakkil wrote to him to help Muhammad al-Qummi in [[event/al-qummi-buja-war|the war against the Buja]], and he did what was needed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Before that he had been al-Mu'tasim's governor over Sind. He demolished the upper minaret of Daybul and made a prison in it, and began rebuilding the city from its stones, but was dismissed before it was finished. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The report is taken from the editor's footnote, and the attribution to al-Maqrizi is not given in the data; it needs an additional source.
