## Summary
Mawla 'Ata' Allah; teacher at Iznik, then Edirne, then through the ders from the forty to the sixty to the eighty, then qadi'asker, and he became tutor to Sultan Selim b. Suleyman who now sits upon the throne; he died at the start of Safar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 388}
