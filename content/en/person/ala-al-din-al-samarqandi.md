## Summary

Ala' al-Din Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Ahmad al-Samarqandi was a Hanafi jurist and the author of [[work/tuhfat-al-fuqaha|Tuhfat al-Fuqaha']]. {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.77} {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.88} The editors of al-Bada'i' give his kunya in one place as Abu Bakr and in another note as Abu Mansur. {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.77} {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.88}

## In the preface of al-Bada'i'

His student [[person/ala-al-din-al-kasani|al-Kasani]] says in the preface of [[work/badai-al-sanai|Bada'i' al-Sana'i']] that their masters wrote many law books, all useful and excellent, but none attended to systematic order except his teacher, whom he calls "heir and bequeather of the sunna, the shaykh, the imam, the ascetic Ala' al-Din, head of the people of the sunna", and that he followed his example. {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.88}

## His students

Al-Kasani studied law with him and read most of his works, such as al-Tuhfa in law and books of legal theory; his daughter [[person/fatima-al-samarqandi|Fatima]] studied with him and memorised al-Tuhfa, and he married her to al-Kasani. {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.74} {cite:badai-sanai-ilmiyya-v1 p.88}

## Date of death

His date of death is not given on the pages read; the year on the record comes from an earlier card based on al-A'lam.
