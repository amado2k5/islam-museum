## Summary
[[person/hasan-dan-muazu|Hasan dan Mu'azu]] — Hasan dan Mu'azu, Sultan of Sokoto (1931–1938) under British colonial rule; appointed after Muhammadu Tambari's deposition and exile, holding the seat until his death, when Siddiq Abubakar III succeeded. {cite:britannica-usman-dan-fodio} {cite:britannica-usman-dan-fodio}.
