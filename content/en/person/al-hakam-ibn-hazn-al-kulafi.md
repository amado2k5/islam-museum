## Summary
A Companion of the Kulaf, a branch of Tamim, who came as a delegation to the Prophet. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5146}

## His report
Abu Dawud, Abu Ya'la and others narrated his report through Shu'ayb ibn Zurayq al-Ta'ifi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5147}
