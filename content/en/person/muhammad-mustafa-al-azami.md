# Muhammad Mustafa al-Azami

## Summary

Muhammad Mustafa al-Azami (1930 – 20 December 2017) was an Indian-born Saudi hadith scholar, best known for his critical examination of the theories of Ignác Goldziher, David Margoliouth and Joseph Schacht on hadith. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en} {cite:wikidata-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami}

## Early life and study

He was born at Mau in India early in 1930, then in Azamgarh district, hence his nisba; Wikidata gives 1932. He studied at Darul Uloom Deoband (1952), at al-Azhar (MA, 1955) and at the University of Cambridge (PhD, 1966), whose thesis appeared as 'Studies in Early Hadith Literature'. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en} {cite:wikidata-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami}

## Career

He was professor emeritus at King Saud University and chaired its department of Islamic Studies; he was also curator of the National Public Library of Qatar, associate professor at Umm al-Qura University, visiting scholar at Michigan, Princeton and Colorado, and visiting fellow at St Cross College, Oxford. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en}

The article says much of his work challenged Western scholarship on hadith, in particular by showing the written activity in hadith during the lifetime of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. His books include 'Dirasat fi al-Hadith al-Nabawi', 'Kuttab al-Nabi', 'Manhaj al-Naqd 'ind al-Muhaddithin', 'The History of the Qur'anic Text' and 'On Schacht's Origins of Muhammadan Jurisprudence'; he edited Ibn al-Madini's 'al-'Ilal', Muslim's 'al-Tamyiz', 'Urwa ibn al-Zubayr's 'Maghazi', the 'Muwatta', 'Sahih Ibn Khuzayma' and 'Sunan Ibn Maja'. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en}

## Honours and death

He received the [[award/king-faisal-prize|King Faisal Prize]] for Islamic Studies in 1980, for studies on the hadith. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en} {cite:wikipedia-king-faisal-prize-en} He died in Riyadh on 20 December 2017, aged 87. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami-en} {cite:wikidata-muhammad-mustafa-al-azami}
