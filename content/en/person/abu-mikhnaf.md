## Overview
Abu Mikhnaf Lut ibn Yahya al-Azdi al-Ghamidi, a Kufan akhbari. Ibn al-Nadim says that scholars said he surpassed others in knowledge of the reports and conquests of Iraq. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 292-293}

## His books
The "Fihrist" lists for him the "Book of Raids", the "Book of Siffin", the "Book of the Camel", the "Book of the Killing of Hujr ibn Adi", the "Book of the Killing of al-Husayn ibn Ali", and other books on battles and killings. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 292-293}
