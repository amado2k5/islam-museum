## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Sulayman ibn Abd al-Qawi al-Tufi, Najm al-Din the Hanbali, named after Tuf, a village near Baghdad: born in 657, he lived in Damascus and then Egypt. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/154} His works include a commentary on al-Nawawi's Forty and an abridgement of the Rawda in legal theory with its commentary. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/155} Ibn Hajar reports that he was tried in Cairo and banished to Qus, and that he died at Hebron in Rajab; the year in the scan is missing a digit. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/154} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/155}
