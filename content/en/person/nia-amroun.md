# Nia Amroun

## Summary
Nia Amroun is a modest-fashion influencer known for styling outfits around the hijab and the abaya, in a minimalist aesthetic built on monotone palettes and neutral layers. Harper's Bazaar Arabia profiled her in its May 2020 issue as a Riyadh-based modest fashion influencer and a former Londoner. {cite:harpersbazaararabia-nia-amroun}

## Background and audience
She was born and raised in London and also spent part of her upbringing in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, where she is based; her Instagram audience numbers over 220,000 followers. {cite:famousbirthdays-nia-amroun} In the Bazaar feature she shared her entire beauty routine, from double cleansing and vitamin C serum to the products she uses on her brows, lashes and lips. {cite:harpersbazaararabia-nia-amroun}
