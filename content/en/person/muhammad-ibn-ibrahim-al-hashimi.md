## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Hashimi. Al-Baladhuri relates that he was sent to Malatya in 141 AH with troops from Khurasan, with al-Musayyab ibn Zuhayr as his chief of police. He stationed himself there so that the enemy would not covet it, and the remaining people of the town returned to it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His governorship
Caliph al-Hadi appointed him over al-Jazira and Qinnasrin, in succession to Ali ibn Sulayman. He assigned to the city of al-Hadath troops from Syria, al-Jazira and Khurasan, granted them dwellings, and gave each man three hundred dirhams; the work was completed in 169 AH. The building of Zubtara and the settlement of al-Hadath were carried out by him in the caliphate of al-Rashid, and then he was removed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
