## Overview
Musharrif al-Dawla Abu Ali ibn Baha' al-Dawla; a sharp illness struck him and he died on the twenty-second of Rabi' I aged twenty-three years, three months and fourteen days, after a reign of five years, one month and twenty-five days — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 15 p. 173}
