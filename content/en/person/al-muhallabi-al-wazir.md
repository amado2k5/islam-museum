## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Hasan ibn Muhammad al-Muhallabi, vizier of [[person/muizz-al-dawla|Mu'izz al-Dawla]]. Al-Suyuti says that after what the Shi'a had written of cursing on the doors of the mosques was erased, he advised Mu'izz al-Dawla to write in its place: "May God curse the wrongdoers against the family of the Messenger of God", and that they only expressed the curse on Mu'awiya. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.619}

## His coming to Baghdad
Ibn al-Athir relates that he came to Baghdad before Mu'izz al-Dawla, met Ibn Shirzad in the place where he was hiding, and then met al-Mustakfi. Al-Mustakfi showed his pleasure at the coming of Mu'izz al-Dawla, and informed him that he had hidden from the Turks so that they would scatter, and the affair would come to Mu'izz al-Dawla without fighting. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.336}

The year of his death is not set out here and needs an additional source.
