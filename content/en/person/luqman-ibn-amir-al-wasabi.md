## Summary
Luqman ibn Amir al-Wasabi, Abu Amir, of Homs, a trustworthy Successor who transmitted from Abu al-Darda' and Abu Hurayra. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2069}

## Works
Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy, and Abu Hatim said his hadith is to be written. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2069}
