## Overview
Known as Dhu al-Shahadatayn (the one of the two testimonies). When the Qur'an was gathered, Zayd ibn Thabit found the last two verses of Surat al-Tawba with him alone. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 2310: "Khuzayma ibn Thabit ibn al-Fakih ibn La'laba ibn Sa'ida ibn Amir ibn Ghayyan ibn Amir ibn Khatma — his name was Abd Allah ibn Jusham — the Ansari of Aws, later of Khatma; his mother was Kabsha bint Aws al-Sa'idiyya; his kunya was Abu Umara. He was among the earliest forerunners; he witnessed Badr and what came after, though it was also said his first battle was Uhud. He used to break the idols of Banu Khatma, and the banner of Khatma was in his hand on the day of the Conquest." {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.392}

## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's lessons "Madrasat al-Hayat"
In a lesson from the Book of Knowledge of Sahih al-Bukhari, [[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] tells the hadith of Khuzayma ibn Thabit al-Ansari: the Prophet saw a Bedouin's camel and agreed with him to buy it, then the Bedouin sold it to a man who offered more, denied the sale and asked for a witness; Khuzayma testified though he had not been present, because he believed the Prophet in far greater things, and so his testimony was made equal to that of two men. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:27:00} He says that when the mushaf was written they required at least two witnesses for each verse, and found one verse, on the believers who were true to their covenant with God, only with Khuzayma, so the text was completed by his testimony, counted as two. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:27:30} This page's entry from Tarikh al-Khulafa says instead that what Zayd ibn Thabit found with him alone, when he gathered the Qur'an, were the two closing verses of Surat al-Tawba; both reports are given here, each with its source. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434} Al-Heweny sets this rough Bedouin beside the courtesy of Jabir ibn Abd Allah over the sale of his camel, to show that the Prophet bore from the Bedouins what he did not bear from the Companions who lived with him. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep06 00:21:30}
