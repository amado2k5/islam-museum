## Overview
Ali ibn Ali al-Shabramallisi, Abu al-Diya, Nur al-Din, was an Egyptian Shafi'i jurist, a native of Shabramall in al-Gharbiyya in Egypt, who lost his sight in childhood. The entry gives his birth as 997 AH, equal to 1588 CE, and his death as 1087 AH, equal to 1676 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## Career
He studied and taught at al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books a gloss on al-Mawahib al-Laduniyya of al-Qastallani, in manuscript, in four volumes; a gloss on al-Shama'il, in manuscript, under the title Hawashin ala Matn al-Shama'il wa Sharhiha li-Ibn Hajar al-Makki, held in the Rabat library; and a gloss on Nihayat al-Muhtaj, printed, on Shafi'i jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## Death
He died in 1087 AH, equal to 1676 CE, as the entry gives it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.
