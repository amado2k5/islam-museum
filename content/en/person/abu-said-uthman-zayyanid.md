## Overview
Abu Said Uthman ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Yahya ibn Yaghmurasan, of the house of Yaghmurasan, and one of the rulers of the [[polity/zayyanid-tlemcen|kingdom of Tlemcen]]. He regained the kingship with his brother Abu Thabit after the death of Sultan Abu Inan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The course of events in Ibn Khaldun
Ibn Khaldun relates that Sultan Abu al-Hasan al-Marini moved the house of Yaghmurasan from Tlemcen to the Maghrib. They asked his leave to garrison the frontiers of al-Andalus, and he settled them on the island. He then called them out with the Zanata to raid Ifriqiya in 748 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

After the disaster at Kairouan they passed through Tunis and then joined Shalif. Their people gathered to them and gave Uthman the allegiance at the gate of the Eid prayer-ground in Tunis, on the shield. He and his brother made a pact with Ali ibn Rashid, amir of the Maghrawa, and they recovered the kingdom of Tlemcen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
