## Overview
Qabus III ibn Imri' al-Qays ibn al-Nu'man ibn al-Aswad al-Lakhmi was one of the kings of al-Hira, the capital of Iraq in the Jahiliyya; he succeeded after the killing of his brother 'Amr ibn Hind about 45 BCE (582 CE) and his reign was not long. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.170}
