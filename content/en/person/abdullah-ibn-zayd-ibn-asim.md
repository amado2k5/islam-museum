## Overview
An Ansari of the Banu Mabdhul of the Banu al-Najjar. He is the brother of [[person/habib-ibn-zayd|Habib ibn Zayd]], whose hands and feet [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]] cut off, and their mother was [[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He attended [[event/battle-of-yamama|the Battle of al-Yamama]], and his mother was wounded there while fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri says that some transmitters said that he fought Musaylima in the Garden of Death, but this is the view of some, not an agreed one. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The call to prayer at the start of the Hijra (Sealed Nectar, p. 185)
The book says that the call to prayer was prescribed at the start of the Hijra, and that the story of the vision of Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn Abd Rabbih in this is well known, related by al-Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Ahmad and Ibn Khuzayma. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.185}
