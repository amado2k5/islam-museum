## Overview
The amir Abu Bakr ibn al-Pahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan and the last of the Ildenizid atabegs there, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.283-284}

## His rule
Ibn al-Athir says that he was occupied with drinking night and day, and did not look into the affairs of his kingdom or his subjects. The frontiers were neglected until the Georgians took Dawin in 599 AH and killed many of its people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.283-284}
