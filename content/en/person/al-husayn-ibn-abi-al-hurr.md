## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn Abi al-Hurr al-Anbari, mentioned in a report about Basra in al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
He asked permission to build a bath in Basra, and it was granted to him among the general permissions. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
