## Overview
A Successor from Kinda. He transmitted from Abu al-Darda, Ubada ibn al-Samit, Abu Said al-Khudri and Abu Abd Allah al-Sunabihi; Ismail ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abi al-Muhajir, Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Ghassani transmitted from him. Al-Ijli called him a Syrian Successor and trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5157}

## Distinction
He is not Qays ibn al-Harith al-Tamimi of the Tamim delegation, nor Abu Qays ibn al-Harith of Sahm. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5157}
