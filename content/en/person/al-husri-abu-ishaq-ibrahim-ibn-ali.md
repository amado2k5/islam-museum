## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ali ibn Tamim al-Ansari al-Qayrawani, known as al-Husri. He was a poet and secretary from Qayrawan. He is attributed to al-Husur, meaning the craft of making mats, or to a village next to Qayrawan. He is close to the poet al-Husri. He died in 413 AH (1022 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.20}
