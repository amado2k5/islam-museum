## Overview
A woman of the [[group/banu-umayya-ibn-zayd|Banu Umayya ibn Zayd]] of the Aws, who was married to Yazid ibn Zayd of the [[group/banu-khatma|Banu Khatma]]. On the day she was killed she had five sons who were men. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286} Ibn Ishaq says that when Abu Afak was killed she "apostatised" and said [[work/asma-bint-marwan-verses-against-the-ansar|verses]] blaming the Ansar for obeying a stranger who was neither of Murad nor of Madhhij, and wishing for someone to assassinate him. Hassan ibn Thabit answered her. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286} [[person/umayr-ibn-adi-al-khatmi|Umayr ibn Adi]] killed her in her house at night; see [[event/killing-of-asma-bint-marwan|the report and its variants]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/286}

## Notes for researchers
- Her name is "Asma'" in al-Saqqa's edition and "Asma'" in other sources (needs review).
- The description of her apostasy is Ibn Ishaq's own, and the chain goes through Abd Allah ibn al-Harith ibn al-Fudayl from his father. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285}
