## Summary
The first of the seven Weepers, of Banu Amr ibn Awf per Ibn Ishaq's context; Ibn Ishaq names him among those who asked the Prophet for mounts on the Tabuk expedition when he found nothing to carry them on. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him first of the seven Weepers, of Banu Amr ibn Awf per Ibn Ishaq's context, among the seven men who came asking the Prophet for mounts — he said he found nothing to carry them on, and they turned back with eyes overflowing with tears. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: of Banu Amr ibn Awf per Ibn Ishaq's context. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The editors note a disagreement on some of the Weepers' names (note p. 161).
