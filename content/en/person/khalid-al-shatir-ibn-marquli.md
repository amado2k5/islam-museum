## Overview
Khalid al-Shatir, known as Ibn Marquli, of the descendants of the people of Bukhara. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad settled them in Basra, and then al-Hajjaj moved them to Wasit. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
