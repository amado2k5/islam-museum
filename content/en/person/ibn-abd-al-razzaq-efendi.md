## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ibrahim ibn Ahmad, famous as Ibn Abd al-Razzaq, was a Hanafi jurist of Damascus and preacher of the al-Sinaniyya mosque. He died in 1138 AH (1726 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.293}

His works include Qala'id al-Manzum, about four hundred lines on inheritance, with his commentary on it; Mafatih al-Asrar, a commentary on al-Durr al-Mukhtar; a diwan of poetry; a diwan of sermons; and Hada'iq al-In'am on the merits of Damascus. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.293}
