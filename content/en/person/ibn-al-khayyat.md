## Overview
Abu 'Abd Allah Ahmad b. Muhammad al-Taghlibi, known as Ibn al-Khayyat, the Damascene katib-poet; among the accomplished poets, he toured the lands praising patrons and entered the lands of the 'Ajam, meeting Abu'l-Fath Ibn Hayyus at Baalbek — he showed him his verse and Ibn Hayyus pointed him to where his strength lay {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
