## Summary
al-Walid ibn Muslim, Abu al-Abbas of Damascus, a client of the Umayyads, a hadith master and companion of al-Awza'i. He transmitted from al-Awza'i, Sufyan al-Thawri, Malik and al-Layth, and from him al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Baqiyya ibn al-Walid, Abd Allah ibn Wahb and Ahmad ibn Hanbal. Scholars agreed on his standing and reliability. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16652} He is credited with some seventy books of hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16652}
