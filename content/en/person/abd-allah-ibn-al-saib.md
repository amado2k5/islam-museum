## Summary
Abd Allah ibn al-Sa'ib ibn Abi al-Sa'ib Sayfi ibn Abid, Abu Abd al-Rahman, of the Makhzum of Makka, reader of the Qur'an for Makka, a companion who also transmitted reports. He recited the Qur'an to Ubayy ibn Ka'b and transmitted from him and from Umar; Ibn Abi Mulayka and Ata transmitted from him. It is said that he died in the rule of Ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15413}
