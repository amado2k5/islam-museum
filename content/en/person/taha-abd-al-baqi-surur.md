## Overview
Taha ibn Abd al-Baqi Surur, of the Nu'aym family, was an Egyptian researcher. Al-Zirikli says of him that he had a Sufi leaning and wrote much in the magazines concerned with Islamic affairs {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 230}. He died in Cairo in 1382 AH, 1962 CE; the entry gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 230}.

## His works
Among his printed books al-Zirikli names «al-Ghazali» and «Shakhsiyyat Sufiyya» (Sufi Figures), both small, and «Min A'lam al-Tasawwuf al-Islami» (Among the Luminaries of Islamic Sufism), «al-Hallaj», «Rabi'a al-Adawiyya», «Muhyi al-Din Ibn Arabi», «Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah» and «al-Sha'rani wa al-Tasawwuf al-Islami» (al-Sha'rani and Islamic Sufism) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 230}. The entry is short and names no teachers or posts.
