## Summary
Sadia Azmat is an English stand-up comedian, author and broadcaster, born in Leyton, London. She began stand-up in 2010, came to notice at the Edinburgh Fringe, and draws her act from her experience as a British Muslim woman; she made the BBC short Things I Have Been Asked As a British Muslim, hosted the podcast No Country for Young Women, and published the memoir Sex Bomb in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en}

## Life
Azmat was born in Leyton, London, to parents the English article describes as Indian; she started wearing hijab at the age of 19. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} Her birth year is given inconsistently: Wikidata gives 1987 at year precision without a reference, while the English article's infobox gives 1990 and files her under 1990 births, so the museum records 1987 as debated and preserves both readings in her data record. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} {cite:wikidata-sadia-azmat} She identifies generically as a Muslim in her own voice — in her BBC film's title and in her 2019 Metro article "Horny Muslim women like me aren't supposed to exist during Ramadan" — but no source names a school or order for her, so her faith is recorded as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en}

## Work
Azmat had worked in call centres before starting stand-up in 2010. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} Her debut Edinburgh Fringe show, Please Hold — You're Being Transferred to a UK Based Asian Representative (2011), was followed by the full-length show I Am Not Malala (2014). {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} In 2015 she made Things I Have Been Asked As a British Muslim, one of five short films by Muslim comedians commissioned by the BBC for iPlayer, in which she tackled stereotypes about Muslim women, Ramadan and hijab. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} From 2018 to 2020 she co-hosted three series of the BBC Sounds podcast No Country for Young Women. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en} Her first book, the memoir Sex Bomb: The Life and Loves of an Asian Babe, was published in May 2022, with a paperback following in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-sadia-azmat-en}
