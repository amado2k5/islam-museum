## Overview
The name of Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad appears in the accounts of Kufa in the year sixty AH. During his governorship, Hani ibn Urwa, who had given protection to Muslim ibn Aqil, was killed by one of Ibn Ziyad's clients {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.362} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.379}.

al-Baladhuri reports in Futuh al-Buldan that Dawud al-Naqid saw an odd dirham bearing his name {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

al-Mas'udi reports that he entered Kufa veiled with a black turban, riding a mule, and the people thought he was al-Husayn. When al-Nu'man ibn Bashir recognised him, he uncovered his face, and struck Hani ibn Urwa with a rod and broke his nose {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.56}.
