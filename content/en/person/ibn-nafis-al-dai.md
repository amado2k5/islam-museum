## Overview
Ibn Nafis, Abu Abd Allah, one of the chief missionaries, to whom the authority passed in succession to Abu Ya'qub al-Sijistani; on hearing something against him, Abu Ya'qub sent Persian men who killed him treacherously at Karah. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287}
