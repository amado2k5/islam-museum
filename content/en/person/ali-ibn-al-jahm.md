## Overview
Ali ibn al-Jahm, a poet who was associated with the Abbasid caliphs. Al-Suyuti relates that he entered upon [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] holding two pearls, recited an ode, and was given one of them; then he recited other verses and was given the second. He also praised al-Wathiq in an ode. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.534,542}

## His end
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Mutawakkil banished him to Khurasan in 232 AH, though it is said 239 AH, and that when he set out in 249 AH the horsemen of the Kalbiyyun met him near Qinnasrin and killed him. He describes him as hostile to Ali, displaying Sunnism, and sweet in his phrasing. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.497}

Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari relates another report: that he was crucified at the gate of the palace of al-Shadhiyakh in Nishapur. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.233}
