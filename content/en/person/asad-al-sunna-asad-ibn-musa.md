## Overview
Asad ibn Musa ibn Ibrahim al-Umawi, known as Asad al-Sunna, a hadith scholar from Egypt in the second century AH. In the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" he is counted among the transmitters of the Sunna. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
