## Overview
Abu'l-Futuh Fadl b. Salih, al-Qa'id Fadl; among the commanders of the 'Ubaydi state of al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah in Egypt — the town Minyat al-qa'id Fadl in the Giza district opposite Fustat is named for him; strong in al-Hakim's state until al-Hakim turned on him and imprisoned him — he was killed in his cell, no funeral was shown for him and he was wrapped in a mat. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
