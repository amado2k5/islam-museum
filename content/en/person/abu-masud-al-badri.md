## Overview
[[person/abu-masud-al-badri|Abu Mas'ud al-Badri]], whose name was Uqba ibn Amr ibn Tha'laba ibn Usayra al-Ansari, of the Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj. He is known as al-Badri. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2181-2182}

## Lineage
Abu Nu'aym says he was the son of Tha'laba ibn Yusayra; it is also said Usayra ibn Asayra ibn Judara ibn Awf ibn al-Harith. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2181}

## Badr and al-Aqaba
The people of Kufa count him among those of Badr, but the people of Medina did not list him among the men of Badr. He witnessed the pledge of al-Aqaba. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2182} Ibn Lahi'a relates from Urwa, in naming those who witnessed the pledge of al-Aqaba from the Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, Uqba ibn Amr, known as Abu Mas'ud. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2182}

## Kufa
Ali ibn Abi Talib appointed him as deputy over Kufa when he went out to Siffin. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2182}

## Those who transmitted from him
Among those who transmitted from him were Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Khatmi, Abu Wa'il, Alqama, Qays ibn Abi Hazim, Abu Ma'mar, Aws ibn Dama'j, Masruq, Rab'i ibn Hirash, and others. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.2182}

## For young visitors
Abu Mas'ud al-Badri was one of the Ansar. He witnessed the pledge of al-Aqaba, and Ali made him deputy over Kufa. The people of Kufa and of Medina differ about whether he witnessed Badr.

## Notes for researchers
- The disagreement about his presence at Badr is reported by Abu Nu'aym from the people of Kufa and Medina; we have not decided between them.
- The transmitters named in this entry are given in abbreviated form in the scanned text on page 2182, so the names should be checked before relying on them.
