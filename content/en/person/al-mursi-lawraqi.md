## Overview
Al-Mursi al-Lawraqi, shaykh of the readers in Syria; he read the readings on three of Ibn Hudhayl's companions, then on Abu'l-Jud, then on al-Kindi, and heard at Baghdad from Ibn al-Akhdar; versed in kalam, the principles and Arabic — he taught iqra', worked, wrote books, taught by deputyship at the 'Aziziyya and held the mashyakha of iqra' and grammar at the 'Adiliyya; he died on 7 Rajab having commented the Shatibiyya. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 532}
