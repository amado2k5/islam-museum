## Overview
Al-Bahri, of Banu Bahr, is not named further in the text; he appears in the account of [[place/wasaha-yemen|Wasaha]]: he met thousands who had joined an [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] sultan and he and his clan killed them, fewer than a hundred escaping. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] left Wasaha for Sa'da, al-Bahri went before him with Abu al-Dughaysh al-Shihabi and the two sons of Rida. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.81} See [[event/al-hadi-march-to-wasaha-and-governor-appointed-284]].
