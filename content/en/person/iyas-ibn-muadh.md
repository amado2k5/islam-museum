Iyas ibn Mu'adh ibn Anas al-Awsi, a young man from Yathrib, who came to Mecca in a delegation from his people, among them Abu al-Haysar Anas ibn Rafi'. They were seeking an alliance with Quraysh against the Khazraj, shortly before the battle of Bu'ath. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Prophet came to them, sat with them, called them to Islam and recited the Qur'an to them. Iyas said: "My people, by God, this is better than what you came for." Abu al-Haysar took a handful of dust from al-Batha and struck his face with it, saying: "Leave us alone, for by my life we came for something other than this." Iyas fell silent. They returned to Yathrib, and Iyas did not live long. He died while declaring God's oneness, magnifying Him and praising Him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}


## At the season (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum p.132)
He came in a delegation of the Aws seeking an alliance with Quraysh against the Khazraj. The Prophet sat with him and presented Islam to them, shortly before the battle of Bu'ath, in the early part of the eleventh year of the mission ([[event/offering-islam-to-the-tribes|Offering Islam to the tribes]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.132}.
