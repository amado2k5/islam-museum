Iyas ibn Mu'adh ibn Anas al-Awsi, a young lad of Yathrib's people, who came to Mecca in a delegation of his folk — among them Abu al-Haysar Anas ibn Rafi' — seeking an alliance with Quraysh against the Khazraj, shortly before the day of Bu'ath. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Prophet came to them, sat with them, called them to Islam and recited the Quran to them; Iyas said: "my people, by God this is better than what you came for" — whereupon Abu al-Haysar took a handful of valley-dust and struck his face with it, saying: leave us alone, for my life we came for something else; and Iyas fell silent. They returned to Yathrib, and Iyas did not last long before he died — declaring God's oneness, magnifying Him and praising Him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
