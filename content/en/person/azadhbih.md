## Overview
Azadhbih, lord of the Persian frontier posts between Kisra's territory and the Arabs. Al-Baladhuri relates that he met Khalid ibn al-Walid at Majma' al-Anhar, where the Muslims fought him and defeated him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
