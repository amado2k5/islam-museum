## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad Sharif ibn Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Ghazzi al-'Amiri al-Husayni al-Siddiqi, Abu al-Fadl Kamal al-Din, was a historian, genealogist and littérateur, mufti of the Shafi'is at Damascus, born and died there in 1214 AH (1799 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.70}

He wrote good verse and books, among them al-Tadhkira al-Kamaliyya in twenty parts, subtitled al-Durr al-Maknun wa-l-Juman al-Masun min Fara'id al-'Ulum wa-Fawa'id al-Funun; al-Mawrid al-Anasi on the shaykh Abd al-Ghani al-Nabulsi; and al-Na't al-Akmal on the companions of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.70}
