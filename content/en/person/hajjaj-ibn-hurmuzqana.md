## Summary
Al-Hajjaj b. Hurmuzqana, Abu Ja'far the commander; Baha' al-Dawla's deputy over Iraq, tasked with war on the Arabs and Kurds, a leading figure in 'Adud al-Dawla's days and his sons' — versed in war, valiant, of great majesty and sound judgement. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.248}

## Death
When he left Baghdad in Ramadan of the year *92, sedition broke out there and security scattered; he died at al-Ahwaz in Rabi' I 400, aged a hundred and five. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.248}
