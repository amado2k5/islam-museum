## Overview
Babak ibn Bahram, a pupil of Shili. He was of the Mughtasila, then opposed them. Ibn al-Nadim says that he inclined to the doctrine of the Jews. The sayings of Malih al-Khawlani are attributed to him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.477-478}
