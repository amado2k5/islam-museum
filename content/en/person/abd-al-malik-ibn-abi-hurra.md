## Overview
A transmitter who reported from his father, whom the source describes as the one who best informed people about the Sawad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.380}

## What he transmitted
In al-Baladhuri's «Futuh al-buldan» he transmits from his father an enumeration of the choice lands of Umar's tithe, that their tax reached seven million dirhams, and that their register was burned in the skulls. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.380}
