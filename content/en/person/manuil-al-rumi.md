## Overview
Manuel the Roman, a eunuch whom Constantine ibn Heraclius sent in three hundred ships loaded with fighters. He entered [[place/alexandria|Alexandria]] in 25 AH, and it is said 23 AH, and killed those among the Muslim garrison who were there, except those who escaped by flight. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.310}

## Amr's answer
[[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] marched against him with fifteen thousand men and defeated them as far as Alexandria. They took refuge in it and set up catapults, and Amr set up siege engines until he took its walls. He then entered it by the sword and killed Manuel. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.310}
