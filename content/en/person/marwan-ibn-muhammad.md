## Overview
Marwan ibn Muhammad ibn Marwan, the last of the Umayyad caliphs, known by the kunya Abu Abd al-Malik. Al-Tabari relates his caliphate, his defeat at the Zab in 132 AH, and then his killing. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.664} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.432} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.437}

## The oath and his rule
Al-Mas'udi relates that he came from al-Jazira in 127 AH and entered Damascus; Ibrahim ibn al-Walid fled from it, and then Marwan defeated him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.212} He was given the oath of allegiance at Damascus on Monday, fourteen nights into Safar 127 AH; it is said that he called himself caliph at Harran. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.220-221}

## Lineage and title
His mother was an umm walad named Riya, and it is said Tarunat, who had belonged to Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr and then to his father. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.220-221} Ibn al-Nadim attributes him to his tutor al-Ja'd ibn Dirham, so that he was called "Marwan al-Ja'di". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.472}

## His killing
He was killed at Busir, one of the villages of al-Fayyum, at the beginning of 132 AH. His age is disputed, between fifty-eight and seventy-seven years. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.220-221}

Al-Mas'udi says that the people of Harran refused to remove the cursing of Abu Turab from the pulpits, and that Mosul prevented him from entering it when he was defeated. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.232-233}

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 405–408}

- **Basra:** al-Miswar ibn Abbad ibn Husayn (Ibn Hubayra wrote to him in 129 AH to lead the prayer; Banu Sa'd stopped him); then Abbad ibn Mansur (agreed on by the people; led the prayer); then Abd Allah ibn Abi Uthman (some say; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Salm ibn Qutayba ibn Muslim al-Bahili (for Ibn Hubayra); then Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Hashimi (of Banu Nawfal; Salm's deputy) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 405}
- **Kufa:** Milhan al-Shaybani (appointed by al-Dahhak ibn Qays; killed); then Umar ibn Abd al-Hamid (appointed by Ibn Umar, then dismissed); then Isma'il ibn Abd Allah (then dismissed); then Abd al-Samad ibn Aban ibn al-Nu'man ibn Bashir al-Ansari; then Ziyad ibn Salih al-Harithi (appointed by Ibn Hubayra) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 405} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Khurasan:** [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] (until Abu Muslim drove him out) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Sijistan:** Bujayr ibn al-Salhab (seized it); then Amir ibn Dubara al-Murri (appointed by Ibn Hubayra) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Sind:** [[person/mansur-ibn-jawhur|Mansur ibn Jumhur]] (seized it) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Bahrayn:** Bishr ibn Sallam al-Abdi (confirmed by Ibn Hubayra until he died); then Sayyar ibn Bishr (died); then Salm ibn Bishr (until Marwan was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Yamama:** al-Bahiyy, a man of Banu Hanifa (seized it and died); then Abd Allah ibn al-Nu'man al-Hanafi (until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406}
- **Medina:** Abd al-Aziz ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz (confirmed, dismissed 129 AH); then Abd al-Wahid ibn Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik (then withdrew before Abu Hamza); then Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Atiyya (killed Abu Hamza; Mecca added to him); then al-Walid ibn Urwa ibn Muhammad ibn Atiyya (Abd al-Malik's deputy); then Yusuf ibn Urwa ibn Muhammad ibn Atiyya (until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 406} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407}
- **Mecca:** Abd al-Aziz ibn Umar (confirmed with Medina); then Abd al-Wahid ibn Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik; then Rumi ibn Ma'iz al-Kilabi (appointed by Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad, then dismissed); then Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik; then Yusuf ibn Urwa ibn Muhammad (until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407}
- **Ifriqiya:** [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri]] (seized it until killed in 138 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407}
- **Yemen:** al-Dahhak ibn Zaml (expelled by Abd Allah ibn Yahya); then Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad (killed Abd Allah ibn Yahya, later assassinated); then Yusuf ibn Urwa (with Mecca and Medina); then al-Walid ibn Urwa (sent by his brother Yusuf, until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407}
- **Armenia:** Asim ibn Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Hilali (killed by Musafir ibn al-Qassab); then Abd Allah ibn Muslim (died); then Ishaq ibn Muslim {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407}
- **Judgeship of Basra:** Abbad ibn Mansur (under Ibn Suhayl; dismissed by Salm ibn Qutayba); then Mu'awiya ibn Umar al-Ghallabi (judged three days, then resigned); then Amir ibn Ubayda al-Bahili (resigned); then Abbad ibn Mansur (until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 407} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** [[person/muhammad-ibn-abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-layla|Ibn Abi Layla]] (until al-Dahhak ibn Qays entered Kufa); then Ghaylan ibn Jami' al-Muharibi (appointed by al-Dahhak); then al-Hajjaj ibn Asim al-Muharibi (appointed by Ibn Hubayra, then dismissed); then Mansur (until Abu al-Abbas's accession) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Judgeship of Medina:** Uthman ibn Umar al-Taymi (confirmed by Abd al-Wahid ibn Sulayman); then Muhammad ibn Imran al-Taymi (for al-Walid ibn Urwa at the end of Marwan's rule) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Police:** Kawthar ibn al-Aswad al-Ghanawi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Army register, land tax, treasuries and storehouses:** Imran ibn Salih, mawla of Hudhayl {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Chancery (letters):** [[person/abd-al-hamid-ibn-yahya|Abd al-Hamid al-Kabir]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Small seal:** Abd al-A'la ibn Maymun ibn Mihran {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
- **Chamberlain:** Saqlab (a mawla of his); then Miqlas (some say) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 408}
