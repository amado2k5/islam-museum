## Overview
Hamdan ibn Uthman Khoja, from a family of standing, learning and political participation in the regency of the Dey in Morocco, and a writer on politics and administration. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.551}.

## His life
The encyclopaedia states that he was born around 1128 AH (1715 CE) and died in 1258 AH (1842 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.551}.
