## Summary
Dhakhirat al-Din, son of the caliph al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah; in Sha'ban 440 he was circumcised by the caliph's order and named on the minbars as heir apparent. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.479}

## Death
He died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 447, well-bred, and the calamity was great: the ra'is al-ru'asa sat for condolences in a portico of Dar al-Salam's wing, the people were ordered to tear their garments and disorder their turbans, a pavilion was pitched across the portico with the bier behind it — the caliph came out and prayed over him with the people behind the pavilion, pronouncing four takbirs — and the drum was silenced at the caliphal palace through the days of mourning. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.479}
