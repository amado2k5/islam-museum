## Overview
Qara Mustafa Pasha, grand vizier in the reign of Sultan Mehmed IV. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 307}

## His reforms
Al-Salabi says that he was able to stop the interference of women in the affairs of the sultanate, and succeeded in suppressing the attempts of court men to corrupt the state. He put down the mischief-makers, the troublemakers and the highwaymen in various provinces. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 307}
