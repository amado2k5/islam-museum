## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Muhammad al-Farisi al-Karkhi al-Istakhri, a Persian geographer and traveller who visited many lands. In Baghdad he met the geographer Ibn Hawqal. He died in 346 AH (957 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.108}
