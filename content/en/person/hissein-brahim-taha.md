## Summary
Hissein Brahim Taha is a veteran Chadian diplomat who has served since November 2021 as the twelfth Secretary-General of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, the second-largest intergovernmental body after the United Nations — the first Chadian and the first West African to hold the office. Before that he spent decades in the Chadian diplomatic service, including years as foreign minister. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha}

## Early life and education
He was born in 1951 in Am Timan in eastern Chad. He began university studies in medicine before turning to diplomacy, and trained in international relations and Arab affairs at specialised institutes, combining academic formation with a long practice of multilateral diplomacy. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha}

## Diplomatic career
He served in Chadian embassies in several capitals, then held senior posts in the Chadian foreign ministry including chief of staff, before appointment as ambassador to Bahrain, to Egypt and as permanent representative to the OIC. He became Minister of Foreign Affairs, African Integration and Chadians Abroad in 2017 in the government of Albert Pahimi Padacké, serving until 2020, leading Chad's diplomacy on Sahelian and regional neighbourhood files. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha}

## At the OIC
In November 2021 the organisation elected him Secretary-General, succeeding Yousef al-Othaimeen, making him the first representative of sub-Saharan Africa in the post in decades. His tenure has covered coordination on the Palestinian question, mediation in member-state crises, and the organisation's work in education, development and combating Islamophobia — a file the OIC's summits have placed among its priorities. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha} {cite:muslim500-hissein-brahim-taha}

## Language and identity
He speaks Arabic and French and is culturally linked to both the Arab and African spheres, which diplomatic reporting describes as making him a consensus candidate between the organisation's Arab and African blocs. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha}

## Legacy
His accession to the Secretary-Generalship extended the organisation's leadership toward its African member states, which hold a large share of the world's Muslims; his tenure is read as a bridge between the OIC's central Arab file and sub-Saharan Africa's development and security concerns. {cite:wikipedia-hissein-brahim-taha}
