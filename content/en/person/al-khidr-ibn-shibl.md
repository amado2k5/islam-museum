## Overview
Khatib of Damascus Abu'l-Barakat al-Khidr b. Shibl al-Harithi, the Damascene Shafi'i jurist; he taught at the Ghazzaliyya and the Mujahidiyya, and Nur al-Din built him the madrasa by Bab al-Faraj where he taught — now known as the 'Imadiyya because al-'Imad the katib taught there after him and it became famous by him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 685-686}

## His learning
Wide of memory, firm in transmission, a man of visible wealth, versed in the madhhab, discoursing in usul and khilaf; born 486, he died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 562 and was buried at Bab al-Faradis. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 685-686}
