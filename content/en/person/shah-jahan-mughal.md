## Summary
Shah Jahan was the fifth Mughal emperor and the builder of the [[building/taj-mahal|Taj Mahal]] {cite:wikidata}. The museum's record gives his years as 1592 to 1666 {cite:wikidata} and links him to [[place/mughal-empire|the Mughal empire]], which he ruled {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}, and to the [[building/jama-masjid-delhi|Jama Masjid of Delhi]], which he commissioned {cite:asher-mughal-india-1992}. No further detail is added until the sources the record names are read for this page.
