## Overview
Hamza ibn 'Amr al-Aslami was a Companion descended from [[group/aslam|Aslam]] ibn Afsa ibn Haritha ibn 'Amr ibn 'Amir, known as Abu Salih or Abu Muhammad and counted among the people of the Hijaz. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 137}

## Transmission and devotion
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says that the people of [[place/medina|Medina]] narrated from him and that he used to fast continuously. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 137} [[person/al-hakim-al-nisaburi|Al-Hakim]], in the Book of Knowing the Companions of the *Mustadrak*, gives a report of his through Sufyan ibn Hamza al-Aslami, from Kathir ibn Zayd, from his son Muhammad ibn Hamza, about the food of the Prophet's Companions; its wording is garbled in this copy. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 596}

## Kunya and death
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says he died in 61 AH aged seventy-one, or, some say, eighty. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 137} Al-Hakim reports from Sufyan ibn Hamza that he was known as Abu Muhammad and died in 61 aged seventy-one, and from al-Waqidi, with a chain to his son Muhammad ibn Hamza, that he was Abu Muhammad and died in 61; al-Dhahabi repeats this in the *Talkhis*. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 596}
