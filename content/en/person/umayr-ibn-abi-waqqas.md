## Summary

Umayr ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri, brother of Sa'd. An early Muslim who went out with the Prophet to Badr; turned back for his youth, he wept until he was let through, and Sa'd fastened his sword-belt for him. He fought until killed at sixteen; it is said Amr ibn Abd Wudd killed him.
