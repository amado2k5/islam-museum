## Overview
Abu al-Najm Badr ibn Hasanwayh al-Kurdi. After his father died in 369 AH, Adud al-Dawla chose him from among his brothers and strengthened him with men, so he controlled those regions and restrained the aggression of the Kurds in them. Ibn al-Athir describes him as a man whose affair was upright and who was wise. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.518}
