## Overview
Badr ibn Hasanwayh, who sent nine thousand dinars a year to be paid to al-Asafar in place of what he used to take from the pilgrims, making it a standing stipend from his own wealth until 403 AH — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 14 p. 373}
