## Overview
Ala al-Din Tekish, Khwarazm-shah, the father of Ala al-Din Muhammad. He took al-Rayy in 588 AH at the call of Qutlugh Inanj ibn al-Bahlawan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.232-233}

## His succession
His brother Sultan Shah had marched against Khwarazm, but its people refused him, and he died in 589 AH. Tekish took over Merv, its kingdom and its treasuries, and appointed his son Qutb al-Din Muhammad over Nishapur. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.232-233}

In 590 AH his army killed Sultan Tughril, the last of the Seljuks of Iraq, near al-Rayy. He took Hamadhan and the lands of the mountain, handed them to Qutlugh Inanj, and went back to Khwarazm. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.232-233}
