## Summary
Umm Aban bint [[person/utba-ibn-rabia|Utba ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd Shams]], a Qurashi noblewoman who came to Syria and witnessed the conquest, and was widowed when her husband [[person/aban-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Aban ibn Sa'id ibn al-As]] was killed at Ajnadayn {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 24}.

## Proposals and refusals
She was proposed to by [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]], and answered that if he entered he would enter with al-Abbas and if he left he would leave with him — meaning he brought her no good {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 24-25}. Then [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]] sought her, and she refused, saying his wife had nothing from him but the fulfilment of his need {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 25}. Then [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] was proposed, and she said women had no share of him except that he sit among their quarters without touching them {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 25}.

When she later married, Ali congratulated her husband, addressing him as "Abu Muhammad" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 25}.

## Elegy
Verses are transmitted mourning her son Mus'ab and his brother Janah, killed by Banu Taym without weapons, including "do not hope for peace while she lives" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 24}.
