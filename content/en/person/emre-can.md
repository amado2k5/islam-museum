# Emre Can

## Summary

Emre Can is a German professional footballer who was born in Frankfurt on 12 January 1994.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-ar}
A defensive midfielder who came through Bayern Munich, he played for Bayer Leverkusen, Liverpool and Juventus before joining Borussia Dortmund in 2020 and becoming their captain in 2023.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
He won the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup with Germany and has won 48 caps.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}

## Early life

Can joined his hometown club Eintracht Frankfurt at the age of twelve and moved to Bayern Munich's youth academy in 2009 after the family relocated to Munich.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
He made his senior debut for Bayern in the 2012 DFL-Supercup and signed his first professional contract that year.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}

## Club career

Seeking regular football, Can joined Bayer Leverkusen in 2013, playing in the Champions League and scoring four league goals in his single season there.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
He moved to Liverpool in 2014, becoming a regular under Brendan Rodgers and Jürgen Klopp, and scored a bicycle-kick goal against Watford in May 2017 that was voted the Premier League's goal of the season.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
In 2018 he signed for Juventus, winning two Serie A titles, and in January 2020 he joined Borussia Dortmund, where he was named captain in 2023 and, as the English article records, renewed his contract until 2027.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}

## International career

Can played for Germany at every youth level and made his senior debut in a European Championship qualifier against Poland in September 2015.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
He was part of the squad that won the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup in Russia and of Germany's squad at Euro 2020.{cite:wikipedia-emre-can-en}
