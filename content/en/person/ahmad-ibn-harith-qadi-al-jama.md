## Overview
Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Sa'd ibn Harith, Abu al-'Abbas al-Lakhmi al-Jayyani al-Qurtubi, Qadi al-Jama'a of Cordoba. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10360}

## Qualities and works
He was a skilled reciter, a critic of recitation, a transmitter of many hadiths, versed in the points of law and its roots, and skilled in many of the ancient sciences, with a keen mind. His works include al-Mashriq fi Islah al-Mantiq and Tanzih al-Quran 'an ma la yaliq bi-al-bayan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10361}
