## Overview
Al-Muwaffaq Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Latif ibn Yusuf, the polymath Dhu'l-Funun of Baghdad — Shafi'i jurist, grammarian, philologist, physician, philosopher and author of many works; born in 557, he heard a large circle including Ibn al-Batti and Abu Zar'a, studied fiqh under Abu'l-Qasim ibn Fadlan, resided at Aleppo and memorised many books. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Works and death
His works include a sharh of Ibn Babshadh's grammar primer, sharhs of the Maqamat and of Ka'b's Banat Su'ad, the Jami' al-kabir on logic, physics and metaphysics in ten volumes, the Refutation of Jews and Christians, Gharib al-hadith in three volumes (abridged too), and a sharh of Ibn Maja's medical hadiths; al-Dhahabi called him one of the brilliant masters of language, letters, medicine and the ancient sciences though his many claims diminished him — among his sayings: "whoever cannot bear the pain of learning never tastes the sweetness of knowledge." Travelling from Aleppo intending hajj, death overtook him at Baghdad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
