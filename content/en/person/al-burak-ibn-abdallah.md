## Overview
Al-Burak ibn Abd Allah al-Tamimi, a Kharijite from the Banu Tamim. Al-Suyuti says he made a pact in Mecca with Ibn Muljam and Amr ibn Bakr to kill the three amirs on the night of the seventeenth of Ramadan 40 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.295}

## The strike
Al-Burak chose Mu'awiya, and struck him while he was prostrating and hit his buttock, but Mu'awiya did not die. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.295}

## A difference in the name
The museum's data record that his name in al-Tabari is Hajjaj ibn Abd Allah al-Sarimi, and that al-Mas'udi names the third man Zadawayh, a client of the Banu al-Anbar. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
