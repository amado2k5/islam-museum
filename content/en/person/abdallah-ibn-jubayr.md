## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Jubayr, client of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, or it is said client of al-Abbas's client. Al-Mas'udi gives his death as 105 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191} The two reports about his clientage differ visibly in the text and need an additional source.
