## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for al-Qasim ibn Muhammad al-Birzali, Alam al-Din, the hafiz of Damascus: born in Jumada al-Ula 665 AH, he travelled to Aleppo, Baalbek, Egypt and the Hijaz, and his teachers by hearing numbered over two thousand. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/237} He wrote a history continuing Abu Shama from the year of his own birth. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/238} Ibn Hajar quotes al-Dhahabi calling him truthful, reliable and a follower of the Sunna, and the man who made him love hadith. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/238} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/239} He died far from home on the road to Mecca on 4 Dhu al-Hijja 739 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/239}
