## Overview
Abu Qatada ibn Rib'i al-Ansari — it is well known that his name was al-Harith. Iyas ibn Salama reported from his father that the Messenger of God said: the best of our horsemen is Abu Qatada; and Abu Nudra reported from Abu Sa'id that he was better than him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1747}

## His campaigns
Ibn Sa'd said he witnessed Uhud and what came after; Abu Ahmad al-Hakim said it is claimed he was a Badr veteran. One report has him guarding the Prophet on the night of Badr, and the Prophet praying for him; another that when the polytheists broke away toward the Prophet's milch-camels he caught up with them and killed Mas'ada, and the Prophet said on seeing him: the face has prospered. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1747}

## His old age and death
Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar from Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Aqil that when Mu'awiya came to Medina he said to Abu Qatada: everyone came out to meet me except you Ansar. Al-Bukhari counted him in al-Awsat among those who died between fifty and sixty AH, and related that Marwan, as Mu'awiya's governor of Medina, sent for him to show him the places where the Prophet and his Companions had stood, and he went with him and showed him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1747}

## In Ahmed Abdussalam's programme 'Osalli Alayh'
In the twelfth episode of his programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']], [[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] relates from Abu Qatada that they were travelling with the Prophet and the people went off in search of water while he kept close to the Prophet; the Prophet dozed on his mount and leaned, and he propped him until he sat straight, then he leaned a second time and he propped him, then a third time until he nearly fell, and he propped him and the Prophet woke. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:03:30} The Prophet asked who he was and how long he had been riding beside him; he said since the start of the night, and the Prophet prayed that God would protect him as he had protected His Prophet. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:04:30} His lesson is that whoever protects the Prophet's religion, God protects. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:04:30}
