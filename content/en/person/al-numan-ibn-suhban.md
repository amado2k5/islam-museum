## Overview
Al-Nu'man ibn Suhban al-Rasibi, who judged between Mudar and Rabi'a in the days when Yazid ibn Mu'awiya died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The palace of al-Nu'man
He had a palace in Basra known as the palace of al-Nu'man, to which Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad added. Al-Nu'man said: "What a wealth, if the water grows much you drown, and if it grows little you thirst." The source says the matter was as he said. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
