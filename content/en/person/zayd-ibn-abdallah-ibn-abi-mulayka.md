## Overview
Zayd ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Mulayka ibn Abd Allah ibn Judan al-Qurashi. He was killed as a martyr with Yazid ibn Ziyad at Janza in the battle of Kabul {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
