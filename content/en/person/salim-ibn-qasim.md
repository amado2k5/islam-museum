## Overview
Salim ibn Qasim al-Husayni, amir of Medina. He fought Amir Qatada al-Husayni, amir of Mecca, in the year 601 AH. He met him at Dhu al-Hulayfa, after he had prayed at the Prophet's chamber and made supplication. He defeated Qatada, followed him to Mecca and besieged him there {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.245}.

Then Qatada turned some of the amirs who were with Salim against him, and they went over to Qatada. Salim then returned to [[place/medina|Medina]] {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.245}.
