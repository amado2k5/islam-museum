## Overview
Yusuf ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Marwazi. al-Mutawakkil appointed him over Armenia for two years of his caliphate. He seized Baqrad, its patrician, at Khilat and took him to Samarra. His agent al-Mala' ibn Ahmad violated the monastery of the cups in Sisajan, and the patricians corresponded and sent secretly to the Khuwaythiyya {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His death
They rose against him at Batrun, when he had scattered his companions among the villages, and they killed him and took over what was in his army. See [[event/killing-of-yusuf-al-marwazi|the killing of Yusuf al-Marwazi]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
