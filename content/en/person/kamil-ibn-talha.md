## Overview
Kamil ibn Talha al-Jahdari al-Basri, Abu Yahya, is described by al-Dhahabi as the imam, the memoriser, the truthful, the shaykh of Basra in his time; he settled in Baghdad, and he was the paternal uncle of the hadith scholar Abu Kamil Fudayl ibn al-Husayn al-Jahdari {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3109}. He was born in 145 AH and travelled to seek hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3109}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Hammad ibn Salama, Mubarak ibn Fadala, Abu Hilal Muhammad ibn Sulaym, Fudal ibn Jubayr the companion of Abu Umama, Mahdi ibn Maymun, al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Malik ibn Anas, Abd Allah ibn Umar al-Umari, Ibn Lahi'a, Abu Awana, Buhlul ibn Rashid al-Ifriqi and others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3109}. Abu Khaythama, Ibrahim al-Harbi, Abu Dawud, Mutayyin, Hanbal, Abd Allah ibn Ahmad, Muhammad ibn Hibban al-Bahili, Abu Ya'la al-Mawsili, Musa ibn Harun, al-Baghawi and a great many others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3109}.

## What the critics said
Abu al-Hasan al-Maymuni said he asked Ahmad ibn Hanbal about Kamil ibn Talha and Ahmad answered that he was trustworthy in his view {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3109}. Ahmad ibn Asram reports Ahmad as saying he was close in his hadith, and Abu Dawud reports that Ahmad had seen him in Basra with a teaching circle and judged his hadith close to sound {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}. Abbas reports Yahya ibn Ma'in as saying he was nothing, and Ibn Abi Hatim says his father said there was no harm in him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}. Sa'id ibn Amr al-Bardha'i relates from Abu Zur'a that Yahya ibn Aktham had him flogged and stood him before the people over a testimony, so his standing fell {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}. Al-Dhahabi says he is truthful, God willing, that he does not know the reason for Abu Dawud's remark that he threw away his books, and that from Ibn Lahi'a he has material that is rejected and not corroborated {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}. Al-Dhahabi also gives, with his own chains, hadith narrated by Kamil from Hammad ibn Salama, and calls one of them sound in chain but rare, recorded by the four Sunan {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}.

## His death
He died in 231 AH, a date fixed by Musa ibn Harun, and it is recorded that he used to dye his hair {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3110}.
