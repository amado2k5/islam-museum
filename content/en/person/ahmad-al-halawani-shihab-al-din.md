## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Ahmad ibn Isma'il al-Halawani, a scholar from Egypt, Shafi'i in school and Khalwati in the Sufi order. He was born in 1833 CE (1249 AH) and died in 1891 CE (1308 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are from the encyclopaedia of notable scholars and men of letters, and they are recorded as reported dates. They need an additional source.
