## Overview
Al-Zughbi — "a great shaykh, rich in miracles and states, famous for majdhubiyya"; the author's father kept his company and delighted in it, and he remained ever at worship, speaking only from need; he died in the Cairo hospital {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 387}.
