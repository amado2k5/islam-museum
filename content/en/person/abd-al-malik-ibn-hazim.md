## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Amr ibn Hazim al-Ansari, judge to Harun in Baghdad, and author of a book on the campaigns («al-Maghazi»). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.315-316}

## His death
He died in Baghdad in 176 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.315-316}

## Notes for researchers
- His only source is the «Fihrist». A second source is needed for the date of death.
