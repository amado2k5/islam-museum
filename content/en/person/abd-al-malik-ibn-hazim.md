## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Amr ibn Hazim al-Ansari, who died in Baghdad in 176 AH; he served as qadi under Harun (al-Rashid) and wrote Kitab al-Maghazi. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 315-316}
