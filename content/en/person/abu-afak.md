## Overview
A man of the [[group/banu-amr-ibn-awf|Banu Amr ibn Awf]], then of the Banu Ubayda, whom Ibn Ishaq names only by his kunya. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284} He says that his hypocrisy "showed itself" when al-Harith ibn Suwayd ibn al-Samit was killed, and he composed [[work/abu-afak-verses-against-the-prophet|verses]] in which he mentions the fidelity of the sons of Qayla to their covenants, and that a rider came to them and split their gathering. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285} The Prophet said: "Who will rid me of this wicked man?" [[person/salim-ibn-umayr|Salim ibn Umayr]] went out and killed him; see [[event/killing-of-abu-afak|the account and its parallels]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285} In the verses of [[person/umama-al-muzayriyya|Umama al-Muzayriyya]] it is said that he was stabbed at the end of the night "in his great age". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/285}

## Marks of the report
- The description as a hypocrite comes from Ibn Ishaq's words, while the description "wicked" is attributed to the Prophet in the report; there is no chain for the report.
- His old age is inferred from Umama's verse, not from a biography of him.
