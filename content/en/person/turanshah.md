## Overview
Turanshah, al-Malik al-Mu'azzam, son of al-Malik al-Salih Ayyub. al-Suyuti reports that his mother Shajar al-Durr summoned him to take the rule of Egypt when his father died in the year 647 AH, and he came {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709}.

Then his father's mamluks rose against him and killed him in Muharram of the year 648 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709}.
