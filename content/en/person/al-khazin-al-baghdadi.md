## Summary
Ala' al-Din 'Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Baghdadi, known as al-Khazin, was a Qur'an commentator and hadith scholar. The Biblissima record gives 1280 and 1341 CE {cite:biblissima Q16866}. He is the author of the commentary Lubab al-Ta'wil.
## In the sources
The museum's card puts his birth in 1279 and his death in 1341, so its birth year differs by one from the Biblissima record. The nisba al-Baghdadi marks an association with Baghdad, not a proven residence {cite:biblissima Q16866}.
