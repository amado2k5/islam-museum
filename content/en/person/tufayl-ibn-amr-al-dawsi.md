Tufayl ibn Amr al-Dawsi was a noble of the Daws tribe and its outstanding poet, known as Dhu al-Nur. He came to Mecca towards the end of the eleventh year of prophethood. The leaders of Quraysh came to him and warned him about the Prophet, claiming that his speech was like magic that separates a man from his father and his brother. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He stuffed cotton into his ears, out of fear that he would hear anything of his speech. Then he entered the mosque and stood near the Prophet while he prayed at the Kaaba. God willed that he should hear some of his words. He said to himself, “May my mother lose me! By God, I am an intelligent man and a poet; nothing of what is good or bad is hidden from me. Why should I not listen?” He followed the Prophet to his house, heard the Qur'an, became Muslim, and bore witness to the truth. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He went back to his people in Yemen to call them to Islam, and he kept at it until he came to the Prophet in Medina during the expedition of Khaybar, with seventy or eighty households from his people. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Islam in Mecca (The Sealed Nectar pp.133-134)
He came to Mecca in the eleventh year of the mission. The people of Mecca warned him about the Prophet and said that his speech was like magic. He stuffed his ears with cotton, but then he heard some of his words. The Prophet offered him Islam and recited the Qur'an to him, and he became Muslim. He asked that God give him a sign. His sign was a light on his face, which then moved to his whip. Later his father and his wife became Muslim. He emigrated after the Battle of the Trench with seventy or eighty households of his people, and he was martyred on the day of Yamama. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.133} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.134}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy relates that Quraysh warned al-Tufayl ibn Amr against the Prophet, calling him a sorcerer, poet and soothsayer who parts a man from his wife, so that he put cotton in his ears, then lifted it without meaning to and heard wondrous words such as he had never heard; and that after he embraced Islam God gave him a sign, a light that shone for him when the night grew dark. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:37:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:37:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:38:00}
