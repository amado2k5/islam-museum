## Overview
Abu Layla Ishaq ibn Muhammad al-Awrabi, chief of the Awraba tribe in the far Maghrib. Ibn Khaldun relates that Idris ibn Abd Allah came to him in 172 AH and he gave him protection. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1627}

According to the book, he gathered the Berbers to the call of Idris. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1627}

The data do not record his birth or death. Both need an additional source.
