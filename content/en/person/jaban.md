## Overview
Jaban, a Persian commander in al-Baladhuri. He set out with a great host toward Ulays, and al-Muthanna ibn Haritha met him at the River of Blood. Then he gathered at Mustar, and Khalid sent to him al-[[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|Muthanna]] and Hanzala the secretary; when they reached him he fled. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.339,343-344}

He plays the role of the fugitive in [[event/al-falalij-and-tustar-jaban|the account of al-Falalij and Tustar]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
