## Overview
Thawaba ibn Salama al-Judhami, amir of al-Andalus by the appointment of Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib, lord of Ifriqiya, at the end of Rajab in the year 129 AH {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.274}.

al-Maqqari reports that he kept control of al-Andalus. [[person/al-sumayl-ibn-hatim|Al-Sumayl ibn Hatim]] was in charge of his affairs, both parties rallied to him, and he died a year into his governorship {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.274}.
