## Overview
Fakhr al-Din al-Irbili, the Sufi (d. Ramadan 633); he transmitted from Yahya b. Thabit, Abu Bakr Ibn al-Naqur and a great company — his transmissions widespread and prized; he died at Irbil {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 161}.
