## Overview
Jashnasdah, a king of the Sasanian Persian kings, whom al-Tabari mentions under the heading "The Mention of the Reign of Jashnasdah". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.232}

The sayings about the years of his reign and his reports are relayed here. The details of his rule have not yet been read, and need an additional source.
