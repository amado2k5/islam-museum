## Overview
Abu Juhayfa al-Suwa'i was Wahb ibn 'Abd Allah — or Wahb ibn Wahb — known as Wahb al-Khayr ("Wahb the Good"), a descendant of Hurthan ibn Suwa'a ibn 'Amir ibn Sa'sa'a. Ibn 'Abd al-Barr notes that 'Amir ibn Sa'sa'a had five sons, four of whom left descendants — Suwa'a, Hilal, Numayr and Rabi'a — while 'Amr left none, and refers readers to his *al-Inbah 'an Qaba'il al-Ruwat* for the tribes of Qays. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 788}

## Companionship
He was one of the younger Companions: the sources say the Prophet died before Abu Juhayfa reached puberty, but he heard from him and narrated from him. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 788} Al-Hakim gives with his chain, from his son 'Awn ibn Abi Juhayfa, his report: "I was with my uncle in the Prophet's presence when he said, 'The affair of my community will remain sound until twelve caliphs have passed'; then he said something in a low voice, and I asked my uncle, who was in front of me, what he had said; he said, 'All of them from Quraysh.'" {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 716}

Ibn 'Abd al-Barr gives, again through 'Awn from his father, that he ate a dish of wheat bread soaked with meat and came to the Prophet belching, and the Prophet said: "Hold back your belching, Abu Juhayfa, for those most sated in this world will be hungry longest on the Day of Resurrection"; Abu Juhayfa never again ate his fill until he left this world, and if he had supper he took no lunch, and if he had lunch he took no supper. The editor judges the chain very weak because of al-Walid ibn 'Amr ibn Saj. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 788}

## In Kufa with 'Ali
He settled in [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and built a house there; [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|'Ali]] put him in charge of the treasury at Kufa, and he was with 'Ali in all his battles. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 788} The biographical appendix of the Kuwaiti *Mawsu'a fiqhiyya* adds that he held both the treasury and the police for 'Ali, that he narrated from the Prophet, al-Bara' ibn 'Azib and 'Ali, and that Isma'il ibn Abi Khalid, al-Hakam ibn 'Utayba and al-Sha'bi narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13746-13747}

## Death
Khalifa ibn Khayyat, as al-Hakim reports, said that Abu Juhayfa Wahb ibn 'Abd Allah al-Suwa'i died while [[person/bishr-ibn-marwan|Bishr ibn Marwan]] was governor. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 716} The *Mawsu'a* quotes al-Waqidi to the same effect and adds that he was the last Companion to die in Kufa, giving his dates as "?-64 AH". {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13746-13747}
