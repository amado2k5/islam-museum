## Summary
Wahb ibn Abd Allah ibn Muslim ibn Janada, Abu Juhayfa al-Suwa'i, called Wahb al-Khayr, one of the younger companions of the Prophet. He narrated from the Prophet, al-Bara ibn Azib and Ali ibn Abi Talib, and Isma'il ibn Abi Khalid, al-Hakam ibn Utayba and al-Sha'bi narrated from him. He held the treasury and the police for Ali. According to al-Waqidi he died in the governorship of Bishr ibn Marwan and was the last companion to die in Kufa {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13746-13747}. The book gives his death as 64 AH, with the year marked as uncertain.
