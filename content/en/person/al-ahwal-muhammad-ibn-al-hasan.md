## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Dinar al-Ahwal, a poet and philologist from Basra, mentioned in the "Classes of the Great Poets". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His place
The museum's data place him in the early Abbasid age, among the men of letters and language, in the Sunni school. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His birth and death dates, his teachers and his works need an additional source; the data do not record them.
