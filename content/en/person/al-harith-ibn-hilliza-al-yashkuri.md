## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Hilliza al-Yashkuri is a pre-Islamic poet to whom the seventh of the seven Mu'allaqat is ascribed, the ode opening "Asma' announced her parting to us". Al-Zawzani's commentary gives it after the sixth Mu'allaqa {cite:sharh-muallaqat-sab-zawzani-1921 p.163}.

## The Mu'allaqa
The commentary places the ode of al-Harith ibn Hilliza after the sixth Mu'allaqa {cite:sharh-muallaqat-sab-zawzani-1921 p.163}. The commentator glosses the opening words: "al-idhan" means informing, "al-bayn" means parting, and "al-thawa" means staying {cite:sharh-muallaqat-sab-zawzani-1921 p.163}.

## The name
The commentator notes that "Hilliza", with a kasra on the ha and a short, doubled lam, is also said to mean "the stingy one", and that the poet is named from it {cite:sharh-muallaqat-sab-zawzani-1921 p.163}.
