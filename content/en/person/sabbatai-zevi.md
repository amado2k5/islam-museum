## Overview
Sabbatai Zevi (1626-1676 CE) was a Jewish messianic claimant whose movement swept the Ottoman world and ended in his nominal conversion to Islam before the sultan; its remnants persisted in the Dönmeh {cite:scholem-sabbatai-1973}.

## Perspectives
- **Ottoman establishment:** the movement was contained and closed by the claimant's conversion before Mehmed IV {cite:scholem-sabbatai-1973}.
- **Academic:** the largest Jewish messianic movement of the early modern period, as Scholem describes {cite:scholem-sabbatai-1973}.
