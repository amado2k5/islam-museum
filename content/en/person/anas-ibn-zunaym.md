## Overview
Anas ibn Zunaym rode with Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali and Fil, the client of Ziyad, while he was on a pacing hackney. He riddled Abu al-Aswad with a verse about the dove of an elephant and the dove of Kisra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
