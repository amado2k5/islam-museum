## Overview
Umara ibn al-Walid, a young man of Quraysh whom the leaders of Quraysh presented to Abu Talib, so that he would take him as a son in exchange for handing over his nephew, the Prophet, to be killed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.97}.

## What is not covered here
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum does not, at this point, give his lineage or his later reports. They will be completed from other sources when he is mentioned.

## For young visitors
Umara ibn al-Walid was among the early Muslims in Mecca, and the polytheists tortured him when he embraced Islam.
