## Summary
Sawad ibn Ghaziyya al-Ansari, a Companion of the people of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], hero of the "stick" scene — among the gems of faith the book relates {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 215}.

## The story of the stick
While the Prophet was straightening the ranks before the armies met, a qadah — a stick — in his hand, he saw Sawad standing ahead of the line and said: "stand level, Sawad", jabbing him in the belly with the stick. Sawad said: you have hurt me, Messenger of God, and God sent you with truth and justice — so give me retaliation {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 215}.

The Prophet bared his belly and said: "retaliate" — and Sawad embraced him and kissed his belly. He asked: what made you do this? He said: Messenger of God, you see what has come — I wanted my last moment with you to be my skin touching your skin {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 215}.
