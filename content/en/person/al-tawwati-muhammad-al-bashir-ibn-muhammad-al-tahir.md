## Overview
Muhammad al-Bashir ibn Muhammad al-Tahir al-Tawwati, a scholar of Bejaian origin who grew up in Tunis. He was attributed to al-Tawwati because of his connection with a righteous man from the people of Tuwat. He died in 1311 AH (1893 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.679}
