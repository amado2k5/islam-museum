## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Ya'qub ibn Dawud ibn Imran Tahman al-Sulami by clientage, client of Abu Salih Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami, governor of Khurasan. He was secretary to Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 19-20}.

## What Ibn Khallikan reports
Ibrahim and his brother Muhammad rose against al-Mahdi in Basra and its surroundings, and they were killed in the year 145 AH. When he died, Abu Hanash al-Hilali, who lived a hundred years, mourned him with verses in the Hamasa {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 19-20}.
