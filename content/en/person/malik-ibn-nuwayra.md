## Overview
Malik ibn Nuwayra al-Tamimi, chief of the Banu Hanzala ibn Malik of the Banu Yarbu'. They called him al-Jaful because of how much he dismissed his people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136-137}

## Collector of alms
He was the Prophet's agent over the alms of the Banu Hanzala, as in [[event/dispatch-of-sadaqa-collectors|the sending of the alms collectors]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} When the Prophet died, he set aside the shares he had in his hands, and said to them: "The matter of your wealth is yours, O Banu Hanzala." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136-137}

## His death
After Buzakha, a detachment of Dirar ibn al-Azwar al-Asadi took him prisoner and brought him to [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]], who ordered his neck struck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136-137} See [[event/killing-of-malik-ibn-nuwayra|the killing of Malik ibn Nuwayra]].

## The reports about him
According to one report, Malik told Khalid that he had not apostatised. Abu Qatada al-Ansari testified that the Banu Hanzala laid down their arms and gave the call to prayer. His killing was among the things for which [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] rebuked [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136-137}

His brother Mutammim told Umar that he used to keep his fire burning until morning, for fear that a guest would come and not know where it was. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.136-137}
