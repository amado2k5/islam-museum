## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Abd Kulal, one of the kings and crowned chiefs of [[group/himyar|Himyar]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235}

## The letter of the kings of Himyar
He and other kings of Himyar wrote to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] announcing their Islam when he returned from Tabuk, and the Prophet wrote his letter to them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.235} See [[event/letter-of-himyar-kings|the letter of the kings of Himyar]]. The date of the letter is estimated at about 631 CE.

His Islam is recorded here on the basis of the report. The second source, "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum", deals with the same subject, and the data give no specific page for it.
