## Overview
Son of Ali ibn Abi Talib by a woman of the Banu Hanifa. When his father was dying, he charged him to honour his two brothers al-Hasan and al-Husayn, charged them with him, and said that he was their sword and the son of their father. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.703}

## Ibn al-Zubayr
Al-Mas'udi relates that Ibn al-Zubayr spoke against Ali in his sermon, and Ibn al-Hanafiyya came and set a chair for him in front of the pulpit, and defended his father in the presence of Quraysh. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.76-77}
