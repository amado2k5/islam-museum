## Overview
Ahmad Amin, an Egyptian historian of intellectual life in Islam, author of the series "Fajr al-Islam", "Duha al-Islam" and "Zuhr al-Islam". {cite:duha-islam p. 6-7}

## Duha al-Islam
He wrote [[work/duha-al-islam|"Duha al-Islam"]], which deals with social life, cultures, the scientific movement, creeds and religious schools in the early Abbasid age. {cite:duha-islam p. 6-7}
