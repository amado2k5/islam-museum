## Summary
Ali ibn Isa ibn al-Faraj ibn Salih, Abu al-Hasan al-Rabii al-Baghdadi, a leading grammarian. He studied with Abu Sa'id al-Sirafi in Baghdad and with Abu Ali al-Farisi in Shiraz until he reached the highest level. His works include a commentary on Mukhtasar al-Jarmi, a commentary on al-Idah of Abu Ali al-Farisi, and a correction of Ibn Jinni's reading of al-Mutanabbi. The dates printed are 420 and 328 H, given as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13363}
