## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Khasib, a vizier mentioned by al-Suyuti and al-Mas'udi. The two accounts differ about him.

## In al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti relates from al-Suli that he was a vizier of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]]. He reports that al-Mu'tasim said to him that the Umayyads had ruled, that none of the Abbasids had ruled in al-Andalus where the Umayyad rules, and he asked for an estimate of what would be needed to fight them. He began on it, then his illness grew severe and he died. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 524}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says he was the son of al-Dahhak al-Jurjani, and that he was vizier to al-Muntasir. He quotes him, when he pardoned al-Shari, as saying that the pleasure of pardon is sweeter than the pleasure of revenge, and that the ugliest acts of al-Muqtadir were revenge. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 521}

The difference between these reports needs an additional source.
