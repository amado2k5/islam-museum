## Overview
Shaghab, mother of the caliph [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]]. She was of Byzantine origin, or Turkish, it is said. Her name was Gharib, or Shaghab {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 585,589,591}.

## Her affairs
She founded a hospital whose annual cost reached seven thousand dinars. Six hundred thousand dinars were looted from her on the night her son was deposed {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 585,589,591}.

In the caliphate of [[person/al-qahir-billah|al-Qahir]] he confiscated her property and beat her until she died in torment {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 597}.

According to Ibn al-Jawzi, she was tortured in various ways. She grieved when news reached her of her son's killing, and she died about seven months after him {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 545}.
