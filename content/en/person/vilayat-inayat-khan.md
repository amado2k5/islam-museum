# Vilayat Inayat Khan

## Summary

Vilayat Inayat Khan (19 June 1916 – 17 June 2004) was an Indian teacher of meditation and of the traditions of the Chishti Order of Sufism, and a mystic of the Inayati Order.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en} He was a son of Inayat Khan, founder of the Sufi Order in the West, whose teaching he carried on in a form tailored to Western seekers, and a brother of Noor Inayat Khan.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en}

## Before his teaching career

Before teaching, he worked in London in 1949 as an assistant to Ghulam Mohammed, the finance minister of Pakistan, and served as a personal assistant to Prime Minister Liaquat Ali Khan during his visit to England.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en}

## Teaching and foundations

In 1975 he founded The Abode of the Message, the central residential community of the Sufi Order International, a conference and retreat centre and a centre of esoteric study.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en} His own books include The Message in Our Time (1978), on the life and teaching of his father, Awakening: A Sufi Experience (1999), and In Search of the Hidden Treasure (2003).{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en} He chose his eldest son, Zia Inayat Khan, as his successor.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en}

## Death

He died on 17 June 2004, two days before his 88th birthday; his dargah is in Delhi, India.{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-en}{cite:wikipedia-vilayat-inayat-khan-ar}
