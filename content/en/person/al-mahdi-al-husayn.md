## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim ibn Ali al-Iyani, al-Mahdi li-Din Allah, was one of the Zaydi imams of Yemen, that is of the Zaydi school, which is a Shi'i school. He took up the imamate after his father and lived at Sanaa. Some of his opponents fought him and he was killed at al-Bun, north of Sanaa. He was eloquent and a skilled debater. The printed heading gives the years 384 and 404 AH, that is 994 and 1013 CE; the card keeps 1013 for his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 219}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'al-Tahaddi lil-Ulama wa al-Juhhal'; 'Tafsir Gharib al-Qur'an', in manuscript; 'Kitab al-Asrar'; 'al-Sifat'; and others. The entry is short and says nothing more {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 219}.
