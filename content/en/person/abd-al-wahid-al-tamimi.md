## Summary
ʿAbd al-Wahid ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAziz ibn al-Harith, Abu al-Fadl al-Tamimi, leader and notable of the Hanbalis in Baghdad in his age, and one of the great authors on creed and jurisprudence. He died in 410 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.295}

## Transmission and works
He heard from Abu Bakr al-Najjad, Abu al-Qasim al-Baghawi and Ibn al-Jaʿabi, and he wrote on the principles and branches of religion. Among his best-known books is *ʿAqidat al-Imam al-Mubajjal Ahmad ibn Hanbal* (The Creed of the Revered Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal). He was held in high esteem by caliphs and viziers, and he had standing and a high rank in Baghdad. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.295}

## His grand funeral
He died in Baghdad in Dhu al-Qaʿda 410 AH, and some fifty thousand people prayed over him at a crowded gathering. He was buried beside the grave of Imam [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] in the cemetery of Bab Harb. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.295}
