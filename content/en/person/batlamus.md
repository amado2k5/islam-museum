## Overview
Ptolemy, author of the "Almagest" and the "Geography", in which he counted four thousand two hundred cities. Al-Mas'udi mentions him among the first who advanced in the sciences of the heavens before those who came after Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.519}
