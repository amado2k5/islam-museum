## Overview
Al-Zaki b. Rawaha al-Mawsili (d. 622 — or 623 per Abu Shama); of the Ibn Rawaha house that endowed the Rawahiyya madrasas of Damascus and Aleppo, founded by al-Zaki Abu'l-Qasim Hibat Allah b. 'Abd al-Wahid b. Rawaha; buried at maqabir al-Sufiyya {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
