## Overview
Sayf al-Islam Tughtakin, brother of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]]. Salah al-Din sent him to Yemen in the year 578 AH and placed its affairs in his hands, because they had fallen into disorder {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 111-112}.

## In Yemen
Hattan ibn Munqidh had gained strength against Izz al-Din Uthman al-Zanjabili, who held Aden. When Sayf al-Islam reached Zabid, Hattan fortified himself in some of the castles. Sayf al-Islam kept giving him safe conduct and sending him gifts, until Hattan came down to him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 111-112}.

He then arrested Hattan when he wanted to depart with his property and family, recovered all his wealth, and imprisoned him in one of the castles. The report says that he killed him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 111-112}.
