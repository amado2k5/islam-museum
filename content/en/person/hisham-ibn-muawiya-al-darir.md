## Overview
Abu 'Abd Allah Hisham b. Mu'awiya al-Darir, the Kufan grammarian (d. 209); companion of al-Kisa'i from whom he took much grammar, with sayings ascribed to him and works including al-Hudud, al-Mukhtasar and al-Qiyas; when Ishaq b. Ibrahim b. Mus'ab erred in a word before al-Ma'mun he took his grammar from this very Hisham {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
