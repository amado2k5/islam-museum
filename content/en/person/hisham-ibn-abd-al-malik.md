## Overview
[[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], one of the Umayyad caliphs. He was given the oath of allegiance while he was at al-Zaytuna, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195}. In his caliphate he removed many of his governors in Medina and elsewhere. [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] was given charge of Khurasan, and he had a connection with the affair of [[person/zayd-ibn-ali|Zayd ibn Ali]]. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 388}, {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 410}.

## The oath of allegiance
The messenger came to Hisham with the staff and the ring, and greeted him as caliph while he was at al-Zaytuna, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195}.

## Removing governors
Hisham removed Umar ibn Hubayra in the year 105, as printed at the head of the page. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195} He removed Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd Allah al-Nadri from Medina in the year 106, as printed before the paragraph. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 199} He removed Ibrahim ibn Hisham from Medina and appointed Khalid ibn Abd al-Malik ibn al-Harith over it, after the year 114 as printed. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 289} He removed Khalid ibn Abd Allah from all his posts in the year 120, as printed at the head of the page. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 369}

## Khurasan
Hisham appointed Nasr ibn Sayyar over Khurasan, and sent his letter of appointment with Abd al-Karim ibn Sulayt. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 388}

## Learning and the pilgrimage
Hisham asked al-Qasim ibn Muhammad about his condition, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 200} He wrote to Abu al-Zinad asking him to write down for him the rules of the pilgrimage before he entered Medina, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 210}

## Zayd ibn Ali
Hisham wrote to the governor of Medina that he should send the Medinan delegation to him, and he did so. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 396} It is said that he summoned Zayd from Medina on the basis of a letter from Yusuf ibn Umar, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 398} He wrote to Yusuf ibn Umar about the affair of Zayd ibn Ali, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 410}

## Notes for researchers
- Most of the reports are given "according to the report"; al-Tabari transmits them from named transmitters, and they have not yet been compared with other sources.
- The years mentioned are taken from the printed marginal notes as they stand, and have not been reconciled with the dates of the other pages.
- What relates to Zayd ibn Ali (pages 398 and 410) should be read against the scan before it is relied on, because the order of events there is unclear in the OCR.
- The references are to the page number of the scan, and reports with OCR noise have been left out.

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 357–362}

- **Mecca:** Muhammad ibn Hisham ibn Isma'il al-Makhzumi (with Medina and Ta'if from Jumada I 106 AH; kept Mecca until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 357}
- **Medina:** Muhammad ibn Hisham ibn Isma'il (dismissed 114 AH; a marginal note says the Medina governor was his brother Ibrahim ibn Hisham); then Khalid ibn Abd al-Malik ibn al-Harith ibn al-Hakam (dismissed 119 AH); then [[person/abu-bakr-ibn-hazm|Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm]] (led the prayer in the interim); then Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Hisham (119 AH until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 357}
- **Yemen:** [[person/yusuf-ibn-umar-al-thaqafi|Yusuf ibn Umar al-Thaqafi]] (arrived 27 Ramadan 106 AH, until appointed to Iraq in 120 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then al-Salt ibn Yusuf (his father's deputy; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then al-Qasim ibn Umar (Yusuf's brother, until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 357}
- **Basra:** Aban ibn Dubara (appointed by Khalid al-Qasri; of Homs); then [[person/bilal-ibn-abi-burda|Bilal ibn Abi Burda]] (prayer, police and judgeship joined under him until Khalid's dismissal in 120 AH); then al-Nadr ibn Umar al-Muqri' al-Himyari (over the prayer; dismissed late 110 AH); then Kathir ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami, Abu al-Aj (appointed by Yusuf ibn Umar; dismissed 122 AH); then al-Qasim ibn Muhammad (until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 358}
- **Kufa:** Abd al-Malik ibn Jaz' ibn Hadrajan al-Azdi (appointed by Khalid; of Palestine); then Isma'il ibn Awsat al-Bajali; then Abd Allah ibn Amr al-Bajali; then Asim ibn Amr (his brother); then Dubays ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali; then Nawf al-Ash'ari; then Ziyad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Harithi; then al-Hakam ibn al-Salt al-Thaqafi (appointed by Yusuf ibn Umar); then Yusuf ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Thaqafi; then Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Thaqafi; then Ziyad ibn Sakhr al-Lakhmi; then Ubayd Allah ibn al-Abbas al-Kindi; then Abu Umayya ibn al-Mughira ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Aqil al-Thaqafi (lasted a week until Yusuf ibn Umar fled) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 358}
- **Khurasan:** [[person/asad-ibn-abdallah-al-qasri|Asad ibn Abd Allah]] (appointed by his brother Khalid; dismissed by Hisham in 108 AH); then [[person/al-ashras-ibn-abdallah|Ashras ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami]] (dismissed 113 AH); then al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman (of Murra Ghatafan; dismissed 115 AH); then Asim ibn Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Hilali; then [[person/asad-ibn-abdallah-al-qasri|Asad ibn Abd Allah]] (second term; died 120 AH); then Ja'far ibn Hanzala al-Bahrani (Asad's deputy); then [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar al-Laythi]] (until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 358} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Sijistan:** Yazid ibn al-Ghurayf al-Hamdani (appointed by Khalid); then al-Asfah al-Kindi, father of Khalid ibn al-Asfah; then Abd Allah ibn Abi Burda ibn Abi Musa al-Ash'ari (until Khalid was dismissed); then Muhammad ibn Hujr ibn Qays al-Abdi (appointed by Yusuf); then Ibrahim ibn Asim al-Uqayli (died); then Harb ibn Qatan ibn Qabisa ibn Mukhariq al-Hilali (until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Sind:** al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman (confirmed by Khalid for two years, then dismissed); then Tamim ibn Zayd al-Qayni; then al-Hakam ibn Awana (killed by the Med); then Muhammad ibn Arar al-Kalbi (dismissed by Yusuf in 122 AH); then [[person/amr-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Amr ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] (until Hisham died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Bahrayn:** Muhammad ibn Ziyad ibn Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali (among Khalid's appointees); then Hazzan ibn Mu'ayd (among Khalid's appointees); then Yahya ibn Isma'il (among Khalid's appointees); then Yahya ibn Ziyad ibn al-Harith al-Harithi (among Khalid's appointees); then Abd Allah ibn Sharik al-Numayri (among Yusuf's appointees); then Muhammad ibn Hassan ibn Sa'd al-Usaydi (among Yusuf's appointees) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Yamama:** al-Muhajir ibn Abd Allah (of Banu Abi Bakr ibn Kilab; on his death his son took over) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Egypt:** Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan; then Ubayda ibn al-Habhab, mawla of Banu Salul {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359}
- **Ifriqiya:** [[person/bishr-ibn-safwan-al-kalbi|Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi]] (restored by Hisham, arrived 106 AH, died 109 AH); then Na''as ibn Qurt al-Kalbi (Bishr's deputy, dismissed by Hisham); then Ubayda ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami (arrived 110 AH); then Uqba ibn Abd Allah ibn Qudama al-Tujibi (Ubayda's deputy); then Ubayda ibn al-Habhab (joined with Egypt; dismissed 123 AH); then Kulthum ibn Iyad; then [[person/hanzala-ibn-safwan-al-kalbi|Hanzala ibn Safwan al-Kalbi]] (arrived mid-Jumada I 124 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 359} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 360}
- **Armenia:** [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami]] (confirmed, dismissed 107 AH); then [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] (dismissed 109 AH); then [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami]] (second term; killed 112 AH); then Sa'id ibn Amr al-Harashi (dismissed 113 AH); then [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] (second term, then returned); then [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]] (from early 114 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 361}
- **Summer campaign:** [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]]; then Mu'awiya ibn Hisham (his son); then Sulayman ibn Hisham (his son) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 360}
- **Judgeship of Basra:** Thumama ibn Anas ibn Malik (appointed by Khalid; dismissed 109 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then [[person/bilal-ibn-abi-burda|Bilal ibn Abi Burda]] (until Yusuf ibn Umar arrived in 120 AH; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Abd Allah ibn Burayda al-Aslami (died soon after; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa); then Amir ibn Ubayda al-Bahili (until Hisham and al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 361}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Kindi (confirmed by Khalid, then dismissed); then Sa'id ibn Ashwa' al-Hamdani; then Muharib ibn Dithar (113 AH); then al-Hakam ibn Utayba al-Ijli; then Sa'id ibn Ashwa' (reinstated until he died); then Isa ibn al-Musayyab al-Bajali (dismissed by Yusuf ibn Umar); then Abd Allah ibn Shubruma al-Dabbi (then moved to the treasury); then [[person/muhammad-ibn-abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-layla|Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla]] (until Hisham and al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 361}
- **Judgeship of Medina:** Muhammad ibn Safwan al-Jumahi (appointed by Ibrahim ibn Hisham); then al-Salt ibn Zubayd ibn al-Salt; then Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Huwaytib (appointed by Khalid ibn Abd al-Malik, then dismissed); then Muhammad ibn Safwan al-Jumahi (again); then [[person/abu-bakr-ibn-hazm|Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm]] (until he died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 361}
- **Police:** Ka'b ibn Hamid al-Absi (confirmed for thirteen years, then sent to Armenia); then Yazid ibn Ya'la ibn Dakhm al-Absi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 361}
- **Chancery (letters):** Salim, mawla of Sa'id ibn Abd al-Malik {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Land tax and army:** Usama ibn Zayd (then dismissed); then Ubayda ibn al-Habhab, mawla of Banu Salul (then sent to Egypt); then Sa'id ibn Uqba, mawla of Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Seal:** al-Rabi' ibn Shabur, mawla of Banu al-Harish {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Treasuries and storehouses:** Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-Harith {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Guard:** Nusayr, his mawla (then dismissed); then al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad (with the seal) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Small seal and privy affairs:** Istakhr Abu al-Zubayr, his mawla {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
- **Chamberlain:** Ghalib ibn Mas'ud, his mawla {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 362}
