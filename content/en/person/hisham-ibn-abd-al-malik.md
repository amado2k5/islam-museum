## Overview
[[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], one of the Umayyad caliphs. He was given the oath of allegiance while he was at al-Zaytuna, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195}. In his caliphate he removed many of his governors in Medina and elsewhere. [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] was given charge of Khurasan, and he had a connection with the affair of [[person/zayd-ibn-ali|Zayd ibn Ali]]. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 388}, {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 410}.

## The oath of allegiance
The messenger came to Hisham with the staff and the ring, and greeted him as caliph while he was at al-Zaytuna, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195}.

## Removing governors
Hisham removed Umar ibn Hubayra in the year 105, as printed at the head of the page. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 195} He removed Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd Allah al-Nadri from Medina in the year 106, as printed before the paragraph. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 199} He removed Ibrahim ibn Hisham from Medina and appointed Khalid ibn Abd al-Malik ibn al-Harith over it, after the year 114 as printed. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 289} He removed Khalid ibn Abd Allah from all his posts in the year 120, as printed at the head of the page. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 369}

## Khurasan
Hisham appointed Nasr ibn Sayyar over Khurasan, and sent his letter of appointment with Abd al-Karim ibn Sulayt. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 388}

## Learning and the pilgrimage
Hisham asked al-Qasim ibn Muhammad about his condition, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 200} He wrote to Abu al-Zinad asking him to write down for him the rules of the pilgrimage before he entered Medina, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 210}

## Zayd ibn Ali
Hisham wrote to the governor of Medina that he should send the Medinan delegation to him, and he did so. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 396} It is said that he summoned Zayd from Medina on the basis of a letter from Yusuf ibn Umar, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 398} He wrote to Yusuf ibn Umar about the affair of Zayd ibn Ali, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 410}

## Notes for researchers
- Most of the reports are given "according to the report"; al-Tabari transmits them from named transmitters, and they have not yet been compared with other sources.
- The years mentioned are taken from the printed marginal notes as they stand, and have not been reconciled with the dates of the other pages.
- What relates to Zayd ibn Ali (pages 398 and 410) should be read against the scan before it is relied on, because the order of events there is unclear in the OCR.
- The references are to the page number of the scan, and reports with OCR noise have been left out.
