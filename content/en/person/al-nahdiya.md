## Overview
Al-Nahdiya, a slave-woman who embraced Islam in Mecca with her daughter, and the idolaters tortured her. Abu Bakr then freed her. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91}

## What is not set out here
The Sealed Nectar does not give here her lineage or the account of her life after that. It will be completed from other sources when she is mentioned.

## For young visitors
Al-Nahdiya was one of the early Muslims in Mecca. When she embraced Islam the idolaters tortured her.
