## Overview
Ata ibn al-Sa'ib al-Khushl, a client of the Banu Layth. The sources say he undertook the ruin of the Nawbahar of Balkh in the campaign of Qays ibn al-Haytham, and built bridges over three rivers of Balkh at a distance of a league, which were called "the bridges of Ata". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
