## Overview
Abd al-Karim ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim, Abu al-Qasim al-Rafi'i al-Qazwini, was a jurist and one of the great Shafi'is. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 557 AH and his death to 623 AH, that is 1162 and 1226 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 55}.

## Life
He held a session at Qazwin for exegesis and hadith and died there. According to al-Zirikli his nisba goes back to Rafi' ibn Khadij, the Companion {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 55}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Tadwin fi Dhikr Akhbar Qazwin, in manuscript; al-Iyjaz fi Akhtar al-Hijaz, the thoughts that came to him on his journey to the pilgrimage; al-Muharrar on law, in manuscript; Fath al-Aziz fi Sharh al-Wajiz li-l-Ghazali on law, printed; a commentary on the Musnad of al-Shafi'i; al-Amali al-Sharita li-Mufradat al-Fatiha, in manuscript; and Sawad al-Aynayn on the merits of Ahmad al-Rifa'i, printed, adding that the attribution of this book to him is doubtful {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 55}.
