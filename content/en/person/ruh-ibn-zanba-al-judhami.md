## Overview
Ruh ibn Zanba al-Judhami was in the army of the people of Syria with al-Husayn ibn Numayr, and was among the people closest to Abd al-Malik {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.78-79} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.33-34}.

## After al-Harra
al-Mas'udi reports that he climbed the pulpit of the Messenger of God in Medina when its people shouted at the army and recalled their dead at al-Harra. He said that they called the people of Medina to obey Yazid, and that they fought them for obedience to him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.78-79}.

## Abd al-Malik's succession
al-Sallabi reports that he supported the idea of removing Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan from the succession, and advised Qabisa ibn Dhu'ayb otherwise. Then Abd al-Aziz died before the matter was carried out {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.33-34}.
