## Overview
Muadh ibn Anas al-Juhani al-Ansari, a Companion who fought with the Prophet and settled in Egypt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3494}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet, Abu al-Darda' and Ka'b al-Ahbar; only his son Sahl narrated from him, and his hadith is weak. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}
