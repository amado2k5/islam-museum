## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Sawar al-Abdi was governor of the frontier of India in al-Baladhuri's account. He raided al-Qiqan and took booty, and sent horses from al-Qiqan to Mu'awiya. Then he returned, and the people of al-Qiqan asked the Turks for help, and they killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri describes his generosity: no one lit a fire in his camp but his own, and he fed people with khabis three times when it was made for a woman in childbirth. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
