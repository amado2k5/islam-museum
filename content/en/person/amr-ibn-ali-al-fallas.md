## Overview
Amr ibn Ali ibn Bahr, Abu Hafs, the water-carrier al-Fallas, was a scholar from Basra. He settled in Baghdad and died at Surra man Ra'a (Samarra) in 249 AH, 864 CE; al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 79}.

## His standing in hadith and his works
He was among the reliable memorisers of hadith, and al-Zirikli says that among the hadith people there are those who rank him above Ibn al-Madini, without naming any {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 79}. He wrote a "Musnad", a work on defects, "al-'Ilal", a "Ta'rikh" and a book of Qur'anic commentary, "al-Tafsir" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 79}.
