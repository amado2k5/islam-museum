# Yasin al-Tuhami

## Summary

Yasin al-Tuhami is an Egyptian munshid and singer from Upper Egypt, one of the best-known voices of religious song in Egypt. {cite:wikipedia-yasin-al-tuhami-ar} {cite:wikidata-yasin-al-tuhami}

## Early life

He was born on 6 December at al-Hawatka in the Manfalut district of [[place/asyut|Asyut]]; the article's text gives 1948, its infobox and Wikidata 1949. Raised in a religious household, he was sent by his father to the Azhari institutes and reached the second year of Azhari secondary school in 1970, when he left his studies for personal reasons. {cite:wikipedia-yasin-al-tuhami-ar} {cite:wikidata-yasin-al-tuhami}

## Today

The article, citing Youm7, reports a concert he opened at the Cairo Opera House on 28 March 2024 under a 'sold out' banner. {cite:wikipedia-yasin-al-tuhami-ar}
