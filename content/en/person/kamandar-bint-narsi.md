## Overview
Kamandar daughter of Narsi, a paternal cousin of al-Nushajan ibn Jasnasma, and the lady of the Palace of the Woman's River. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Hospitality to Abu Musa
Al-Baladhuri relates that she provided [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] with a sweet pastry (khabisa) when he alighted with her, and the river was named after her. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
