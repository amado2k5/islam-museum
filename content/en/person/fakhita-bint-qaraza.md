## Overview
Fakhita bint Qaraza al-Qurashiyya, a wife of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.208}

## The raid on Cyprus
He took her with him on the [[event/first-raid-on-cyprus|first raid on Cyprus]] in the year 28 AH, in keeping with the condition of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] that he must sail the sea with his wife, or not at all. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.208}

## The account of al-Mas'udi
Al-Mada'ini reports, in al-Mas'udi, that Muawiya went in to her with a eunuch while she was uncovered, and she covered her head. He asked her about this, and she answered that mutilating a man does not make lawful to him what God has forbidden. After that he did not allow a servant to enter upon his womenfolk. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.610}
