## Overview
The book says that when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stood in the main track before [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] he had seven horsemen, among them "Abdullah ibn al-Husayn al-Futayimi" and some of his companions. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.102} The page was re-read with Apple Vision and the name and nisba are printed as given. He is not to be taken for [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]], al-Hadi's brother, who appears in the book with his other nisba; it is not said that they are one man, nor certain that they are two. See [[event/al-hadi-stands-in-the-mahajja-at-athafit-285]].
