## Overview
Jalal al-Din Mingburnu ibn Khwarazm-shah, the eldest son of Khwarazm-shah Muhammad and the last sultan of the Khwarazmian state. He carried its banner after his father. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 460-462}

## Events
Ibn al-Athir relates that he fought Genghis Khan on the Indus and was defeated, after he had caused among the Tatar armies such dismay that Genghis Khan admired him. He then ruled the Jibal lands and Azerbaijan. He took Tiflis from the Georgians on 8 Rabi' al-Awwal 623 AH, and then besieged Khilat and took it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 460-462, 492-495}

He opposed al-Malik al-Ashraf and Kaykubad, lord of the lands of Rum, until he was defeated by them. He was defeated by the Tatars near Amid in 628 AH, and his news was cut off thereafter. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 460-462, 492-495}

## For Sprouts
Jalal al-Din Mankubirti, son of Muhammad Khwarazmshah, was the last sultan of the Khwarazmian state, carrying its banner after his father. He fought Genghis Khan on the Indus and was defeated after astonishing the Tatars; he then ruled the Jibal and Azerbaijan, took Tiflis in 623 AH and won Akhlat.

## For Explorers
Jalal al-Din Mankubirti, the eldest son of Muhammad Khwarazmshah and the last Khwarazmian sultan, carried the state's banner after his father. Ibn al-Athir records that he fought Genghis Khan on the Indus river and was defeated — after doing among the Tatar ranks what amazed them until Genghis Khan admired him; he then ruled the Jibal and Azerbaijan, took Tiflis from the Georgians on 8 Rabi' al-Awwal 623 AH and besieged Akhlat until it was his; he opposed al-Malik al-Ashraf and Kayqubad of Rum until defeated by them, and was beaten by the Tatars near Amid in 628 AH, after which news of him ceased.
