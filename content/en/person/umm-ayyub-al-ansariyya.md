## Summary

Umm Ayyub al-Ansariyya, wife of Abu Ayyub, a daughter of Qays ibn Sa'id of the Khazraj. The Prophet lodged with them; her hadith tells of his dislike of the cooked greens and his word that the angels are hurt by what hurts the sons of Adam.
