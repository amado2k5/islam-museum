## Summary
Firuz Shah Tughluq was a sultan of Delhi. Biblissima records his birth in 1309, with a question mark, and his death in 1388 CE {cite:biblissima Q232279}. The museum's card describes him as a builder of foundations and canals who had Ashokan pillars transported.
## In the sources
He appears in Hasan Ibrahim's Tarikh al-Islam, whose entry has not yet been read on the page, so details of his reign are not given here {cite:biblissima Q232279}.
