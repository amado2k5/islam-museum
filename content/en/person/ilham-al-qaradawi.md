## Summary
Ilham Al-Qaradawi is an Egyptian physicist working in Qatar and a leading figure in Gulf nuclear physics. She established the region’s first positron-beam facility at Qatar University. {cite:wikipedia-ilham-al-qaradawi}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Egypt in 1959, she studied physics to advanced degrees in nuclear and radiation physics, then moved to academic and research work in Qatar. {cite:wikipedia-ilham-al-qaradawi}

## Career and works
She has taught physics at Qatar University for decades and founded its positron-beam facility for materials and crystal-defect research. Her interests cover nuclear and radiation physics and environmental radiation monitoring in Qatar and the region, and she has served on regional and international scientific committees. {cite:wikipedia-ilham-al-qaradawi}

## Legacy
She is presented as a pioneering woman in Arab nuclear physics and has received prizes and honours in Qatar and abroad for her research and university teaching. {cite:wikipedia-ilham-al-qaradawi}
