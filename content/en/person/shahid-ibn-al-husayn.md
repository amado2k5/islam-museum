## Overview
Shahid ibn al-Husayn, known by the kunya Abu al-Hasan. Ibn al-Nadim reports that he followed the philosophy of al-Balkhi, and that he wrote books. There were debates between him and al-Razi, and each of them wrote refutations of the other {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.416}.
