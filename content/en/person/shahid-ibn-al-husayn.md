## Overview
Shahid ibn al-Husayn, Abu'l-Hasan, who ran in the track of al-Balkhi's philosophy in learning and conduct; he composed books, and between him and al-Razi were debates, each writing refutations of the other. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 414}
