## Summary
Pargalı Ibrahim Pasha, grand vizier under [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman the Magnificent]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201}.

## The stratagem against the rising
Al-Salabi records that when Qalender Çelebi's revolt in Konya and Maraş grew strong and his rebels killed Bahram Pasha sent to suppress it, Grand Vizier Ibrahim Pasha's stratagem won over some of Qalender's men — his forces dwindled, and he was defeated and killed: the last of the four risings of Süleyman's early years {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 200-201}.
