## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Rahuni of Morocco, a Maliki jurist and theologian who was the reference for fatwas in Morocco; he studied with al-Tawudi, al-Warzazi and al-Bannani, and wrote a gloss on al-Zurqani's commentary on Khalil and a rajaz poem on menstruation. He died in 1230 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9962}
