## Overview
Burayda ibn al-Husayb al-Aslami, kunya Abu Abd Allah. He was among those whom al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad transferred to Khurasan in 51 AH with their families from the two garrison towns. He died in Merv in the days of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
