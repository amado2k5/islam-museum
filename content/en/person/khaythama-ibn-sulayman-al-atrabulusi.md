## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Khaythama ibn Sulayman ibn Haydara al-Qurashi al-Atrabulusi, a hadith scholar and memoriser, whom the encyclopaedia describes as the chief narrator (musnad) of Syria in his age. He was born in 250 AH and died in 343 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He travelled in search of hadith among the frontier towns, Iraq and the Hijaz, and died in Tripoli in Syria. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
