## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Tarif al-Shari, a Kharijite from al-Jazira in the days of [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]]. He rose in 178 AH and his affair grew great, so al-Rashid sent Yazid ibn Mazyad al-Shaybani against him. He was killed in his rising, and his sister al-Fari'a lamented him with poems. {cite:duha-islam p.951-952}

Ahmad Amin says that Ibn al-Samʿani and Ibn Khallikan call him a Shaybani, and Ibn al-Athir calls him a Taghlibi. {cite:duha-islam p.951-952} See [[event/walid-ibn-tarif-revolt-178|his revolt]].

The death date (about 794 CE) is derived from the Hijri date.
