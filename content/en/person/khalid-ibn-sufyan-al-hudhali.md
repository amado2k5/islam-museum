## Overview
A man of [[group/hudhayl|Hudhayl]], of whom the Prophet told Abdallah ibn Unays that he had heard he was gathering people to attack him when he was at Nakhla or at 'Urana. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/267} [[person/abdullah-ibn-unays|Abdallah ibn Unays]] went out to him alone, and found him in a litter of women seeking a camp for them. He told him that he was of Khuza'a and had heard of his gathering against Muhammad, and had come to join him. He walked with him until he had the chance, then killed him with the sword and went out. When he came to the Prophet, the Prophet gave him a [[object/staff-of-abdullah-ibn-unays|staff]]. See [[event/killing-of-khalid-ibn-sufyan-al-hudhali|the account]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/267} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/268}

## Notes on the report
- "At Nakhla or at 'Urana" is the transmitter's doubt about the place. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/267}
- That he was gathering people against the Prophet is what "reached" the Prophet; Ibn Ishaq does not relay anything about him from his own side.
