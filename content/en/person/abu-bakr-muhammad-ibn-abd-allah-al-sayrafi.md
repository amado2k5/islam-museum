## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Sayrafi, a jurist who died in 330 AH, and who has works in the principles of jurisprudence and other fields, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}

The date of death is from al-Shirazi alone and needs an additional source.
