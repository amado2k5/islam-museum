## Summary
Abu Khalid and Amr and Aban; he perished at al-Zurayba toward Ta'if over wealth of his, and Ibn Ishaq relates verses he spoke to his son Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him father of Amr ibn Sa'id and ascribes to him verses he spoke to his son Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He says he perished at al-Zurayba toward Ta'if over wealth he had there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The place-name is written "al-Zurayba" as in the page image p. 4; its location was not verified in this batch.
