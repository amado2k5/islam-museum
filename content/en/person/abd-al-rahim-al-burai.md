## Overview
Abd al-Rahim ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Yamani al-Bura'i: a Sufi poet from al-Niyabatayn in Yemen, who gave legal opinions and taught. He died in 803 AH (1400 CE); his nisba refers to Bura', a mountain in Yemen. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.343}

He left a printed diwan of poetry, most of it in praise of the Prophet. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.343}
