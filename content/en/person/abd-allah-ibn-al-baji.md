## Summary
Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Baji, a Maliki jurist. He studied with Ibn Lubaba, Aslam ibn Abd al-Aziz, Ahmad ibn Khalid and Qasim ibn Asbagh among others; his son Ahmad, his grandson Muhammad ibn Ahmad, Ibn al-Farudi and al-Asili heard him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8191}
