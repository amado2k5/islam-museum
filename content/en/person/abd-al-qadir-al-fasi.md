## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Maghribi al-Fasi, the Maliki, was one of the eminent shaykhs of his age. He was born and raised in al-Qasr, moved to Fez in 1025 AH, and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}. The entry gives his years as 1007 to 1091 AH, 1599 to 1680 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His works
He did not busy himself with authorship; rather, answers to questions put to him were issued by him and a number of his companions gathered them into one volume. Al-Zirikli quotes the author of 'al-Safwa' as saying of them that they are among the fatwas on which the scholars of the time rely. They include 'al-Ajwiba al-Kubra', printed, and 'al-Ajwiba al-Sughra', printed in its margin; 'Ta'liqat ala Sahih al-Bukhari', printed, gathered by one of his sons; and a booklet on 'al-Fara'id wa-l-Sunan', printed. He also wrote 'Risala fi al-Imama wa-Ahkamiha', in manuscript at Rabat, and a printed 'Aqida' attributed to him became well known after him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}. His son Abd al-Rahman wrote a full-length book on his life, 'Tuhfat al-Akabir bi-Manaqib al-Shaykh Abd al-Qadir', and two others: 'Bustan al-Azhar', on his reports, and 'Ibtihaj al-Basa'ir', on those who read with him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
