## Summary
Abd al-Qadir ibn Ali ibn Yusuf, Abu Muhammad al-Fasi, a Maliki jurist, hadith scholar and exegete whose probity and merit are agreed. He studied with his father, his brother Ahmad, Ibn Ashir and Ibn Abi Nu'aym. He wrote no books; his answers to questions were collected by his students in one volume, and a creed is attributed to him. The dates as printed are 1091 and 1007 H, which do not agree; they are given as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13361}
