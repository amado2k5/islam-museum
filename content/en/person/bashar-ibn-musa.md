## Overview
Bashshar ibn Musa al-Ijli, or al-Shaybani by another report, the Basran al-Khaffaf who settled in Baghdad, Abu Uthman, is called by al-Dhahabi the great hadith scholar {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Sharik, Abu Awana, Yazid ibn Zuray', Ubayd Allah ibn Amr and their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}. Ahmad ibn Hanbal and his son Abd Allah, Salih Jazara, al-Hasan ibn Ulayya, al-Baghawi and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}.

## The disagreement over his reliability
His reliability was disputed {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}. Abu Zur'a weakened him; Ahmad said his hadith may be written and thought well of him; Ibn Ma'in and al-Nasa'i said he was not reliable; Abu Dawud said he did not narrate from him; and al-Bukhari said he had left him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}. Ibn Adi said he saw no objectionable hadith of his and hoped there was no harm in him, having heard that Ibn al-Madini thought well of him, and Ibn al-Madini said there was none in Baghdad firmer in the sunna {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}. Ibn al-Ghallabi reports from Ibn Ma'in that he called him an impostor {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}. The museum gives each judgement to the one who made it and chooses none.

## His death
It is said he died in 228 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1203}.
