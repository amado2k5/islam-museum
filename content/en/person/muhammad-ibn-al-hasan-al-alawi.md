## Overview
An Alid of the Talibid line. He and his brother Ali entered Medina on the first of Safar 271 AH, killed a group of its people and demanded money from them.

Ibn al-Jawzi says that the people of Medina did not pray in the Mosque of the Prophet for four Fridays, neither Friday prayer nor congregational prayer. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.101}
