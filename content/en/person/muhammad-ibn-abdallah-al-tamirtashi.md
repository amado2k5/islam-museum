## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Ahmad, al-Khatib al-Umari al-Tamirtashi al-Ghazzi al-Hanafi, Shams al-Din, is described by al-Zirikli as the leading Hanafi of his time. He came from Gaza, where he was born and where he died. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 939 AH, 1532 CE, and his death as 1004 AH, 1596 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 239}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Tanwir al-Absar, a work of jurisprudence that has been printed, and Minah al-Ghaffar, a commentary on Tanwir al-Absar that survives in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 239}.

He also lists Mus'if al-Hukkam ala al-Ahkam; al-Wusul ila Qawa'id al-Usul; Mu'in al-Mufti ala Jawab al-Mustafti; al-Fatawa; I'anat al-Haqir; Mawahib al-Mannan, on jurisprudence; and Iqd al-Jawahir al-Nayyirat, on the merits of the ten Companions. He adds that there are many treatises, among them one on coinage, and marks most of these works as manuscripts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 239–240}.
