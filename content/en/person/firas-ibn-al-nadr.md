## Summary
Of Banu Abd al-Dar; Ibn Ishaq names him among the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him second after Abu al-Rum of Banu Abd al-Dar, tracing his lineage to Abd Manaf ibn Abd al-Dar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The name is garbled in the OCR scan; it was read "Firas ibn al-Nadr" from the page image of p. 7 (compare: al-Nadr ibn al-Harith ibn Kalada of Abd al-Dar).
