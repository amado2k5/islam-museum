## Summary
Of Banu Abd al-Dar; Ibn Ishaq names him among the rest of the emigrants to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him second after Abu al-Rum of Banu Abd al-Dar, tracing his lineage to Abd Manaf ibn Abd al-Dar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 7015: "Firas ibn al-Nadr ibn al-Harith ibn Alqama ibn Kalda ibn Abd Manaf ibn Abd al-Dar ibn Qusayy al-Abdari, given the kunya Abu al-Harith"; he records that Ibn Ishaq listed him among those who emigrated to Abyssinia and was martyred at the Yarmuk, while his father was killed at Badr as an unbeliever. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1189}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}
