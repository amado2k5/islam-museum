## Overview
Dirar ibn Damra, one of the close companions of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], known for his description of him before [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Muawiya]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.710-711}

## His description of Ali
He entered upon Muawiya as an envoy, and Muawiya pressed him to describe Ali. He said that he was far-reaching in his vision and strong in his power, spoke with decisiveness and judged with justice, and that knowledge gushed forth from all sides of him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.710-711}

He said that Ali honoured the people of religion, showed mercy to the poor, felt estranged from the world and found comfort in the night. He then described his prayer niche, his weeping, and his words: "O world, deceive someone other than me." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.710-711}
