## Overview
Abu al-Ja'd Samira ibn al-Ja'd al-Shaybani of the Banu Shayban, who was one of the boon-companions of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]]. Qatari ibn al-Fuja'a wrote to him in verse inviting him, and he joined the Kharijites {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.127-129}.

He wrote to al-Hajjaj a letter with verses at the bottom in which he declared that he had abandoned every religion except the religion of the Kharijites. He has many poems on asceticism and death {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.127-129}.
