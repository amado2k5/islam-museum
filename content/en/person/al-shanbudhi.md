## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Shanbudhi, a Baghdadi reciter, the servant of Ibn Shanbudh. He read the readings with Ibn Shanbudh and with Ibn Mujahid and a group. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.137}

## His learning
He took the lead in teaching the Qur'an and knew the exegesis. He used to say that he memorised fifty thousand lines of poetry as evidence for the Qur'an. Al-Daraqutni spoke against him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.137}
