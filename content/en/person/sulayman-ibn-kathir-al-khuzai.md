## Overview
Sulayman ibn Kathir al-Khuza'i, chief of the chiefs (naqib al-nuqaba') of the [[group/abbasid-dawa|Abbasid mission]] in Khurasan {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22-25}.

## In the mission
Imam Ibrahim ibn Muhammad won him over with his personal influence, and so restored the cohesion between the Khurasani group and the leadership in al-Humayma. Ibrahim offered him the leadership of the mission, but he excused himself as an elderly man, saying: "I will not swear by two people." Abu Muslim was chosen in his place {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22-25}.

## The declaration of the revolt
The revolt was declared in Khurasan at his hands, on Thursday, the twenty-fifth of Ramadan in the year 129 AH. The supporters adopted black as their emblem, and became known as al-Musawwida (the Black-Clad). They held their first prayer for the Feast of Fitr at Safidhanj {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22-25}.

## His end
When Abu Muslim had the affair to himself, he killed Sulayman ibn Kathir and his son Muhammad without referring the matter to the caliph, according to Taqqush {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22-25}.
