## Summary
Muhammad Jalal is the host of The Thinking Muslim, an English-language podcast of long-form interviews that its show page describes as "a platform for the elevation of thinking and a call to enlightened action." {cite:acast-the-thinking-muslim} The podcast's YouTube channel (@TheThinkingMuslim) had 373,000 subscribers in the founder's channel harvest of October 2026. {cite:youtube-the-thinking-muslim}

## The podcast
The show page on Acast names Muhammad Jalal as host, and its episodes are long conversations with guests; a recent episode listed on the page, published on 23 October 2026, discussed surveillance capitalism and digital privacy with Omar Al Zabir, a former engineering manager at Meta. {cite:acast-the-thinking-muslim} The show's notes invite listeners to support "our Muslim media project" through membership, promote a Thinking Muslim Conference in London, and name Baitulmaal as the show's charity partner. {cite:acast-the-thinking-muslim} The episodes listed on the page address Muslim thought, politics, technology and society. {cite:acast-the-thinking-muslim}
