## Overview
Abd Allah (Diya' al-Din) ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd al-Latif Al Abd al-Salam al-Kuwazi al-Shafi'i al-Basri, known as Abd Allah Bash A'yan: a scholar of the well-known Bash A'yan family of Basra, which claims Abbasid descent. He was raised by his maternal grandfather Ahmad Nuri al-Ansari, the qadi of Basra, and held a succession of posts. He lived from 1263 to 1340 AH (1847–1921 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.101}

He made the pilgrimage in 1290 AH and wrote a short travel account of it titled al-Futuhat al-Kuwaziyya fi al-Siyaha ila al-Ard al-Hijaziyya (printed), and in his last years devoted himself to teaching hadith in his home until he died. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.101}
