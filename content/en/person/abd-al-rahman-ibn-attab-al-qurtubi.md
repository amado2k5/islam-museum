## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Attab ibn Muhsin, Abu Muhammad, of Cordoba, a Maliki jurist who studied readings, exegesis and philology and learned law from his father. He was known for the seven readings and for fatwa work; his book Shifa' al-sadr concerns asceticism. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8192} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8193}
