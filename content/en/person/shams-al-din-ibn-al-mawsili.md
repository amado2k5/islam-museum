## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim ibn Radwan al-Ba'li, Shams al-Din, known as Ibn al-Mawsili, was a littérateur and a scholar of jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He was born in Baalbek, studied there and in Damascus and Hama, and died in Tripoli {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Works
His books include «Bahjat al-Majalis wa Ronaq al-Majalis» in five volumes; «al-Durr al-Muntazam», in which he put al-Tha'alibi's philology into verse; «Lawami' al-Anwar», a manuscript versifying the rare words of the Muwatta' and of Muslim after Ibn Qurqul; and a versification of al-Nawawi's «al-Minhaj» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He also left verse and prose {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Dates
Al-A'lam gives his birth as 699 AH, equal to 1300 CE, and his death as 774 AH, equal to 1372 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
