## Overview
Abu 'Ali al-Hasan al-Daqqaq of Nishapur — shaykh of al-Qushayri and the elder of Nishapur's sufis; Ibn al-Jawzi lists him among the deaths of 412, while others place his death earlier. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.349}

## In the introduction to al-Risala al-Qushayriyya
The editor of [[work/al-risala-al-qushayriyya|al-Risala al-Qushayriyya]] says that al-Qushayri came to Nishapur to learn accounting, but fate brought him to the circle of Abu 'Ali al-Daqqaq, "the imam of his age and the tongue of his time"; in his company he lost his taste for the society of worldly men, and al-Daqqaq advised him to seek knowledge, so he studied law with Abu Bakr al-Tusi and the two sciences of principles with Ibn Furak. {cite:risala-qushayriyya-minhaj-2017 p. 17} In a note the editor says al-Daqqaq studied law with al-Qaffal and Abu 'Abd Allah al-Khidri, was a pupil of the pupils of Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari, heard hadith from al-Kushmihani, and followed the path under Abu al-Qasim al-Nasrabadi, through whom he received the Sufi cloak. {cite:risala-qushayriyya-minhaj-2017 p. 18} He quotes al-Qushayri saying that in his early days he never entered al-Daqqaq's presence except fasting and having bathed, that he would reach the door of his school and turn back out of awe, and that no objection to him ever crossed his mind. {cite:risala-qushayriyya-minhaj-2017 p. 19} The editor says the Risala preserves many of al-Daqqaq's sayings, and that al-Qushayri was buried at his feet in his school in Nishapur. {cite:risala-qushayriyya-minhaj-2017 p. 37} {cite:risala-qushayriyya-minhaj-2017 p. 40}
