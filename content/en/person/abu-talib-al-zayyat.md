## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Zayyat, companion of Ibn Wahshiyya — it is he who transmits Ibn Wahshiyya's books from him; he was alive in Ibn al-Nadim's time and died shortly after. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 527}
