## Overview
Abu Talib al-Zayyat, Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Zayyat, companion of Ibn Wahshiyya. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.433}

## In Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim says that he transmitted the books of Ibn Wahshiyya, and that he was alive in his time and died shortly after. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.433}
