## Overview

Abu al-Rabi' Sulayman ibn Muslim ibn Jammaz al-Zuhri of Medina is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/abu-jafar-al-qari|Abu Ja'far]], with [[person/isa-ibn-wardan-al-hadhdha|Ibn Wardan]]. He died shortly after 170 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189}

## His standing

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an eminent, exact and noble reader, sought out for the readings of Abu Ja'far and Nafi', which he took by reading them to both. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 189} [[person/ismail-ibn-jafar-al-ansari|Isma'il ibn Ja'far al-Ansari]] read on him, and also on Nafi' and Shayba ibn Nisah. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 183} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 188}
