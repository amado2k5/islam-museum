## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn al-Harith ibn Asad al-Khushani. An Andalusian historian from Kairouan, attributed to al-Khushani. He died in 371 AH (981 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
