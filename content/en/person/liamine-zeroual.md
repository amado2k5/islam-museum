# Liamine Zéroual

Liamine Zéroual (1941–2026) was an Algerian officer and politician, the fourth president of the Republic of Algeria. He was born on 3 July 1941 at Batna in eastern Algeria, fought in the National Liberation Army during the war of independence, and rose through the People's National Army to general; he retired from the army in 1989 before being recalled. {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-ar} {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-en}

## Presidency in the Black Decade

Zéroual was appointed minister of defence on 10 July 1993, then head of state on 31 January 1994 in succession to Ali Kafi, chairman of the High Council of State, and was elected president of the republic on 16 November 1995. He governed Algeria through the heart of the civil war known as the Black Decade until 27 April 1999, when Abdelaziz Bouteflika succeeded him. {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-ar} {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-en}

## Death

Liamine Zéroual died at the Aïn Naâdja Military Hospital in Algiers on 28 March 2026 — 9 Shawwal 1447 in the Arabic article's dating — aged eighty-four. President Abdelmadjid Tebboune declared three days of national mourning; his body lay in state at the People's Palace in Algiers, and he was buried in the central cemetery at Bouzourane in Batna. {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-ar} {cite:source/wikipedia-liamine-zeroual-en}
