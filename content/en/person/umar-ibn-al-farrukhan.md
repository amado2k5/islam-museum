## Overview
Umar ibn al-Farrukhan al-Tabari, one of the astrologers, from the land of Fars {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.381}.

## His work
According to Ibn al-Nadim, he translated and explained the meanings of the first treatise, together with Thabit and Ibrahim {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.381}.
