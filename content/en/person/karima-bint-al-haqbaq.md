## Summary
Karima bint al-Haqbaq, musnida of Syria, the Qurashi-Zubayri woman known as Bint al-Haqbaq; she transmitted from Hassan al-Zayyat and many, and Abu'l-Waqt, Ibn al-Baghiyan, Mas'ud al-Thaqafi and others gave her ijaza; she transmitted a great deal; she died in Jumada II at her orchard at al-Mutawwir. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 220}
