## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ibrahim al-Imam, son of the imam of the call Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ali. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur sent him in 140 AH as governor of the Jazira and its frontiers, with [[person/al-hasan-ibn-qahtaba|al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba]] and the troops of the people of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Building Malatya
He gathered the levies from the people of Syria and the Jazira until they reached seventy thousand, and camped at Malatya to build it with workers gathered from every land. See [[event/rebuilding-of-malatya|the rebuilding of Malatya]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba fed the people from his own money, Abd al-Wahhab wrote to al-Mansur complaining of him, and al-Mansur replied with a harsh rebuke belittling his resolve, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
