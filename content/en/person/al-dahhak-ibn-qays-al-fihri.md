## Overview
Al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Fihri, a commander who declared the caliphate of Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr in Syria, and was at the head of his army there. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.202}

## Marj Rahit
Marwan ibn al-Hakam met al-Dahhak at Marj Rahit near Damascus. Marwan was killed there by a man of the Taym al-Lat, and many of the Qays were killed with him. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.202}
