## Overview
Sa'id ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Sa'id, the Andalusi Taghlibi Abu al-Qasim, was a historian of wide inquiry; his origin was Cordoba, born at Almeria, and he held the judgeship of Toledo until he died in 462 AH (1070 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.186}

His books include Jawami' Akhbar al-Umam min al-'Arab wa-l-'Ajam; Siwan al-Hikma on the classes of the wise; Maqalat Ahl al-Milal wa-l-Nihal; Islah Harakat al-Nujum; Tarikh al-Andalus; Tarikh al-Islam; and Tabaqat al-Umam. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.186}
