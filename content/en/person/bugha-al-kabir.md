## Overview
Bugha al-Kabir, a client of al-Mu'tasim. Al-Baladhuri relates from the people of Barda'a that he was governor of Armenia, Azerbaijan and Shamshat. In 240 AH he rebuilt Shamkur after the Sawardiyya had ruined it. He settled there people who had come to him from the Khazars seeking safety and desiring Islam, and moved merchants there from Barda'a, and named it "al-Mutawakkiliyya". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.286} He took part in [[event/founding-of-mutawakkiliyya|the founding of al-Mutawakkiliyya]].

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi relates that a trick was devised against him: notes were thrown into al-Mutawakkil's tent that he had plotted to kill him, and in his tent it was said that boys had resolved to kill the caliph. He rode with his horsemen guarding the places. Al-Mutawakkil believed the notes and left him in Syria, and the clients then did what they did. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.502-503}
