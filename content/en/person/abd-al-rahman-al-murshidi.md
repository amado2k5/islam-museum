## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Isa ibn Murshid, Abu al-Wajaha al-Umari al-Murshidi, was the mufti of the Meccan sanctuary and one of the scholar-poets of the Hejaz. He was born in Mecca. He held the chancery (diwan al-insha') under the Sharif Muhsin ibn al-Husayn ibn Abi Numayy, and the imamate and preaching of the Sacred Mosque and the sultanic ifta' in 1020 AH, as the entry words it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. When Muhsin died, the Sharif Ahmad ibn Abd al-Muttalib succeeded him, seized al-Murshidi and persecuted him, and he died in prison, strangled {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 975 to 1037 AH, that is 1567 to 1628 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «Zahr al-Rawd al-Muqtataf wa-Nahr al-Hawd al-Murtashaf», a history; «al-Tarsif fi Fann al-Tasrif», a poem in rajaz on morphology, printed with its commentary «Fath al-Khabir al-Latif»; «Sharh al-Murshidi ala Uqud al-Juman» of al-Suyuti, on rhetoric (ma'ani, badi' and bayan), printed in two parts; «Ta'mim al-Fa'ida bi-Tatmim Surat al-Ma'ida»; «al-Wafi fi Sharh al-Kafi», on prosody, in manuscript; «Manahil al-Samar fi Manazil al-Qamar», a treatise; «Bara'at al-Istihlal wa-ma Yata'allaq bi'l-Shahr wa'l-Hilal», in manuscript; and «al-Tadhkira», in manuscript in the Rabat library under number 449 Kattani {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
