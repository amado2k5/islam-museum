## Summary
Abu'l-Fath Nasir ibn 'Abd al-Sayyid ibn 'Ali al-Mutarrizi, jurist, grammarian and man of letters of Khwarazm. In the branches he followed Imam Abu Hanifa and was eloquent, and excellent in fiqh; among his useful works are a commentary on al-Hariri's *Maqamat* and the book *al-Mughrib* on the unusual words the jurists use — it stands to the Hanafis as al-Azhari's book does to the Shafi'is. He entered Baghdad as a sojourner in 601, a Mu'tazili in creed, and held disputations there with a group of jurists while the litterateurs took from him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
