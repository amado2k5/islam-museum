## Overview
Mohamed Ali Mowleed is the imam and teacher of Alfurqan Islamic Centre in Manchester, England. {cite:divine-journeys-umrah} {cite:manchester-student-ramadan-guide}

His name is spelled differently across the sources: "Mohamed Ali Mowleed" by Divine Journeys and in the centre channel's video titles, "Mohammed Ali Mowlid" in the University of Manchester's Student Ramadan Guide, and "Muhammed Ali Mowlee" in the Manchester Islamic Society's course listing. {cite:divine-journeys-umrah} {cite:manchester-student-ramadan-guide} {cite:manchester-isoc-courses}

The centre's YouTube channel had about 163,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-alfurqan-islamic-centre}

## Career and work
His lessons and sermons form the core of the centre's YouTube channel, including a multi-part reading of the poem of Abu Ishaq al-Biri and short lessons in an introductory fiqh series. {cite:youtube-alfurqan-islamic-centre}

He teaches a "Kitab At-Tawhid" course at the centre, listed among the in-person courses run by the University of Manchester's Islamic Society with the city's mosques and centres. {cite:manchester-isoc-courses}

He leads the centre's Umrah groups as their imam and religious guide, as in the October 2025 and October 2026 packages run by Divine Journeys from Manchester to Makkah and Madinah. {cite:divine-journeys-umrah}
