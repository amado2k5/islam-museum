## Overview
Abu Awn Abd Allah ibn Awn ibn Artaban, client of the Muzayna, who died in 151 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 48}

Ibn al-Mubarak said of him that he had not seen anyone like him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 48}
