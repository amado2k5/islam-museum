## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Hafizi, of the Hafizi family of notables of Lemah in Asir. He was born in 1345 AH (1926 CE) and died in 1406 AH (1986 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.77}

The encyclopaedia of notable scholars and men of letters lists him among the scholars and traces a long lineage for him back to Abd al-Qadir al-Hafizi. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His studies, teachers and works are not set out here and need an additional source.
