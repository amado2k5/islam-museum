## Summary
Rachid Ghannouchi is a Tunisian Islamic thinker and politician combining theory with political practice, among the leading figures of democratic-Islamist thought in the Arab world. {cite:wikipedia-rachid-ghannouchi}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1941 in the Kairouan region of Tunisia, he studied philosophy at Zitouna and Damascus, then the Sorbonne, influenced by the Brotherhood school before formulating his own project. {cite:wikipedia-rachid-ghannouchi}

## Career and works
He co-founded the Islamic Tendency Movement (later Ennahdha), spent years in prison and exile in London, and returned after the 2011 revolution to serve as speaker of Tunisia’s parliament (2019–2021). His works include Public Liberties in the Islamic State and writings on Islam, democracy and human rights. He was imprisoned in 2023 amid arrests of opponents of President Kais Saied. {cite:wikipedia-rachid-ghannouchi}

## Method and contribution
He argues Islamic shura meets democracy in mechanism while differing in ultimate reference. His project is praised by many as a model for the evolution of Islamic thought and criticised by secularists doubting Ennahdha’s commitment and by Islamists who see compromise; the museum attributes these positions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-rachid-ghannouchi}
