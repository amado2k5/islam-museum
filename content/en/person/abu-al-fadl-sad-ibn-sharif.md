## Overview
Abu'l-Fada'il Sa'd b. Abi'l-Ma'ali Sharif b. Sayf al-Dawla al-Hamdani; he ruled after his father — Ibn Khallikan found no date for his death — and with his death the kingship of the Banu Sayf al-Dawla died out. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
