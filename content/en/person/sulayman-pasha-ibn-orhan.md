## Summary
Prince Suleiman Pasha, son and heir of Orhan, led the first Ottoman crossing into Europe: in al-Sallabi's telling he slipped across the Dardanelles by night with forty horsemen, seized Roman boats, and secured footholds on the western shore including Gallipoli — the base from which Ottoman raids spread into the Balkans {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 52}.

He died before his father, and the succession passed instead to his brother Murad I {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 57-58}.
