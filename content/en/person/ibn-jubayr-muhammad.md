## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Kinani, Ibn Jubayr, an Andalusian traveller from Valencia and Granada. He performed the hajj in 579 AH and wrote an account of his journey, which describes the roads and the cities. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.491}

## The journey
On his first journey he stayed in Damascus, Mosul and Baghdad, and he embarked from Acre with the Franks. His ship was damaged in the narrow gulf of Sicily, and he endured hardships until he reached al-Andalus in 581 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.491}

## Later life
He set out again for the East, and died in Alexandria on Wednesday the twenty-seventh of Sha'ban 614 AH. Al-Maqqari relays from Ibn al-Raqiq that prayers made at his grave are answered. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.491}

He transmitted the *Shifa'* (the Book of Healing) from Muhammad ibn Isa al-Tamimi, from the judge Iyad, and he afterwards held the judgeship of Ceuta. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.491}
