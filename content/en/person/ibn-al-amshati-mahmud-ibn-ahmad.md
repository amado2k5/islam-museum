## Overview
Muzaffar al-Din Abu al-Thana' Mahmud ibn Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Isma'il ibn Ya'qub, known as Ibn al-Amshati, a Hanafi scholar of Cairo. He grew up in Cairo, and his family origin is traced to Ayntab. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.343}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 815 AH (1412 CE) and died in 902 AH (1496 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.343} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
