## Summary

Abd al-Fattah Abu Ghuddah (1917–1997) was a Syrian Hanafi jurist and hadith editor who left Aleppo to settle in Riyadh, becoming a leading figure in manuscript editing and in documenting scholars' manners in the twentieth century.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-fattah-abu-ghuddah}

## Life

Born in Aleppo, he studied under its scholars, emigrated to Saudi Arabia after the 1980 events, lived in Riyadh as teacher and lecturer, and worked in Saudi scholarly institutions and the education institute.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-fattah-abu-ghuddah}

## Works and editions

His books include Qimat al-Zaman 'ind al-'Ulama' (The Value of Time for Scholars), Al-Rasul al-Mu'allim wa Asalibuhu fi al-Ta'lim and Safahat min Sabr al-'Ulama'; his editions include al-Zayla'i's Nasb al-Raya and al-Ghunaymi's Al-Lubab fi Sharh al-Kitab, among others.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-fattah-abu-ghuddah}

## Method and standing

He joined Hanafi fiqh to hadith science and the transmission of high isnads; generations of hadith scholars and editors trained under him, and he is called a teacher of editors on the student's manners. He died in Riyadh in 1997.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-fattah-abu-ghuddah}
