## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Thawban al-Qurashi al-Amiri. He transmitted from Abu Hurayra, Abu Sa'id, Fatima bint Qays, Zayd ibn Thabit, Jabir, Ibn Abbas and Ibn Umar. His brother Sulayman, Yahya ibn Abi Kathir, Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari and al-Zuhri transmitted from him. Abu Hatim calls him a Successor; Ibn Sa'd, Abu Zur'a and al-Nasa'i call him trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15418}
