## Overview
Hajjaj ibn Artah ibn Thawr al-Nakha'i was a judge from Kufa, counted among the transmitters and memorizers of hadith; he was asked for fatwas at sixteen years old and was appointed judge of Basra. {cite:al-aalam v.2}

He died in Khurasan in 145 AH (762 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2}
