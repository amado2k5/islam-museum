## Overview
Hajjaj ibn Artah ibn Thawr ibn Hubayra ibn Sharahil ibn Ka'b, the imam and erudite mufti of Kufa alongside Imam Abu Hanifa and the judge Ibn Abi Layla — Abu Artah al-Nakha'i, the Kufan jurist, one of the luminaries. He died in 149 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1366}
