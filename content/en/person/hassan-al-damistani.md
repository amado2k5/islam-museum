## Overview
Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Khalaf al-Damistani was an Imami scholar, that is, one of the scholars of the Imamiyya. He came from Damistan, one of the villages of Bahrain, moved from there to al-Qatif and died there. The printed text gives his death as 1181 AH, 1767 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 219}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names "Intikhab al-Jayyid min Tanbihat al-Sayyid", in manuscript, on clarifying the narrators of "al-Tahdhib", and a "Diwan shi'r", in manuscript, at Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 219}. The entry in al-A'lam is short and says nothing more.
