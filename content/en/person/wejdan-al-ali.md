# Wejdan Al-Ali

## Summary

Wejdan Al-Ali is a preacher known for his lectures and lessons on the purification of the soul, love of God and His Messenger, and the meanings of the divine names. His work is published through his official YouTube channel, which he describes as a path for travellers coveting the garment of love and the soundness of tranquillity. {cite:youtube-wejdan-al-ali}

## Programmes and series

Al-Ali presented the programme Wajd, in episodes on matters of the heart such as prostration and tranquillity; the series Jamal al-Arkan on the pillars of Islam; and the series Wa-litusna' 'ala Ism Allah ("That you may be fashioned upon God's name"), explaining the divine names, their meanings and their effect in purifying the soul. His recent lectures include talks delivered in Mecca in the Hajj seasons on gentleness, the rites and the sacred feelings. {cite:youtube-wejdan-al-ali}

## Digital presence

His official YouTube channel gathers his lectures, programmes and series, among them the Wa-litusna' 'ala Ism Allah episodes on names such as al-Wadud, al-Wasi', al-Wali, al-Nur, al-Majid and al-Malik, together with his recent lectures from Mecca; it is the platform that continuously publishes his lessons under his name. {cite:youtube-wejdan-al-ali}
