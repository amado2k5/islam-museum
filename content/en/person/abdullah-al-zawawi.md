## Overview
Abdullah ibn Muhammad Salih al-Zawawi, then al-Ahsa'i, al-Makki, al-Hasani, al-Idrisi, was the mufti of the Shafi'is in Mecca {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}. Al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 1266 AH (1850 CE) and 1343 AH (1924 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}.

## Education and teaching
He studied at Mecca in the Sawlatiyya school, then became one of the teachers of the Sacred Mosque, and headed the committee of Ayn Zubayda {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}.

## His work
He wrote "Bughyat al-Raghibin wa-Qurrat Ayn Ahl al-Balad al-Amin", printed, a treatise on the condition of Ayn Zubayda {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 130}. His entry in al-A'lam is short, and this page adds nothing beyond it.
