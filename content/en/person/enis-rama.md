## Summary
Enis Rama (born 12 June 1976 in Mitrovica) is a Kosovar imam and author, imam of the Isa Beg Mosque in Mitrovica and chief editor of Peace TV Shqip, and one of Kosovo's most followed preachers on the platforms: his Facebook page counted 237,000 followers in October 2024, per the peer-reviewed Context study. {cite:context-cupi-2025}

## Early life and education
Rama was born in Mitrovica and completed primary school there, then graduated from the Alauddin madrassa in Pristina and travelled to Amman in Jordan to deepen his Arabic — where, the Albanian article says, he met Muhammad Nasiruddin al-Albani. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq} He then studied hadith at the Islamic University of Medina, and in 2006 completed postgraduate studies in usul al-din at the American Open University in Cairo. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq} He is a former soldier of the Kosovo Liberation Army. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq}

## Beginnings and career
Rama returned to Kosovo and works as imam of the Isa Beg Mosque in Mitrovica, where he lives; he holds regular weekly lectures in Mitrovica and lectures in Pristina as well, drawing Muslims from all the Albanian lands. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq} He is chief editor of Peace TV Shqip, the Albanian service of the Peace TV network, and is known for his deep readings of the sharia texts and his lectures on the healing of hearts and on faith. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq}

## Writings and lectures
Rama's books include "Ta njohim Allahun" ("That We May Know Allah", 3 volumes), "Harta e Kthimit tek Allahu", "Ndikimi i Kuranit" and "Myzhde Agu", and he reviewed the Albanian translation of Ali al-Tantawi's general introduction to Islam. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq} His best-known lecture series include "Rruga drejt Allahut" ("The Path to Allah"), a commentary on the Rikak chapter of Sahih al-Bukhari, a commentary on Ibn Taymiyya's al-Aqida al-Wasitiyya, a commentary on al-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, and a commentary on the thirtieth juz' of the Qur'an. {cite:wikipedia-enis-rama-sq}

## Impact and audience
The Context study, reading the platform pages in October 2024, recorded 237,000 followers on Facebook, 25,000 on Instagram and 8,510 subscribers on YouTube. {cite:context-cupi-2025} The figures are dated platform counts, not facts the museum measures itself.
