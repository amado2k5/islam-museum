## Overview
Al-Fayd ibn Abi Salih Shirawayh al-Farisi, Abu Ja'far, is called by al-Dhahabi the great vizier; he became a Muslim, having been a Christian, and served al-Mahdi as vizier in the last years of his reign {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3053}.

## The vizierate and his character
Al-Dhahabi says he was generous and open-handed, his generosity a proverb, and that he had an excessive pride which made people forget the pride of the vizier Abu Ubayd Allah {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3053}. Al-Suli says he remained vizier until al-Mahdi died, after which al-Fayd held the army bureau until his own death {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3053}. Among the sources al-Dhahabi lists for the entry are al-Tabari's history, al-Jahshiyari's book of viziers and scribes, and Wafayat al-A'yan {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3053}.

## His death
He died in 173 AH, according to al-Suli as reported by al-Dhahabi {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3053}.
