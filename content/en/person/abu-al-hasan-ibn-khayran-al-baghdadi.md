## Overview
Abu al-Hasan ibn Khayran al-Baghdadi, author of the book "al-Latif". Abu Ahmad ibn Ramin, the teacher of al-Shirazi, studied with him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61} The editor's note says that al-Subki did not include him, and that Haji Khalifa mentioned his book in a large volume. He is not Abu Ali ibn Khayran mentioned on page 58.
