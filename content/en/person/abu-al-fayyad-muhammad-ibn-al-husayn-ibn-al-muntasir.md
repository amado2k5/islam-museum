## Overview
Abu al-Fayyad Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Muntasir, one of the companions of [[person/abu-hamid-al-marwarudhi|Abu Hamid al-Marwarudhi]]. He taught in Basra, and its jurists took from him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.62}

He is not the Abbasid caliph [[person/al-muntasir|al-Muntasir]].
