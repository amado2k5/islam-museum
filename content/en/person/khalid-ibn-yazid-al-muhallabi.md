## Overview
Khalid ibn Yazid ibn al-Muhallab. His father sent him with his paternal uncle Abu Uyayna ibn al-Muhallab against the Isbahbadh. The Isbahbadh defeated them both until he drove them back to the army of Yazid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
