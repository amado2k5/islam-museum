## Overview
Najib Baladi (1907–1978 CE) was an Egyptian philosopher and writer. He was among the first cohort at the Egyptian University and one of the pioneers of contemporary Arabic philosophical thought. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.38}

The Hijri dates in the data are 1324 and 1397 AH. No other details are recorded; an additional source is needed.
