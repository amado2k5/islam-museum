## Summary
A Companion of Ghifar, among those who pledged under the Tree per Ibn Ishaq; he relates of the Tabuk expedition the Prophet's question to him about the stragglers of Ghifar and Aslam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him Abu Ruhm Kulthum ibn al-Husayn, one of the Prophet's companions who pledged under the Tree, and says he campaigned at Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172} His brother's son relates from him that one night at al-Akhdar the Prophet asked him about the stragglers of Ghifar, then about a group of Aslam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172-173}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: of Banu Ghifar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- His full name is "Kulthum ibn al-Husayn" per Ibn Ishaq; the chain through his unnamed nephew's son is unknown.
