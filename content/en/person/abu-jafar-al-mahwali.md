## Summary
Abu Ja'far al-Mahwali wept and complained to God. He was one of the early knowers of God from Baghdad, lived at the Bab al-Hawl, and took his nisba from it. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 506, sequence 507, on page 144. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}

## A complaint to God
Ali ibn Abi Maryam relates from Abd Allah ibn Abi Habib: I heard Abu Ja'far al-Mahwali say: to You I complain of a body that You nourished with Your blessing and that then leapt upon Your disobedience. Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}
