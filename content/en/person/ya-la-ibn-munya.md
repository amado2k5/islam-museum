## Overview
Ya'la ibn Munya, of the descendants of Malik ibn Handhala ibn Malik, an ally of the Banu Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf. His mother was of the Banu Mazin ibn Mansur. al-Baladhuri reports that Khawlan in Yemen apostatised in the caliphate of Abu Bakr, and Ya'la marched against them and fought them until they submitted and acknowledged the alms-tax. It is also said that he met no war, and that they returned to Islam. After that Yemen was divided among three: al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya over Sana'a and its districts, Ziyad ibn Labid over what was in his hand, and Ya'la. He is among those who took part in [[event/ridda-wars|the Ridda wars]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
