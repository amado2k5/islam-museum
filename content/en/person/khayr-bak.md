## Overview
Amir Khayr Bak, a great Circassian amir of Qansuh al-Ghawri. Ibn al-Imad says in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* that his intrigue was the cause of the defeat of al-Ghawri's army at Marj Dabiq in 922 AH, since in the battle he leaned toward Sultan Salim. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}

The report rests on one source and needs an additional source.

## Rule over Egypt
Al-Salabi counts Khayr Bak among the Mamluk commanders resolved to join Selim's army and carry rule within the Ottoman frame; after the conquest Selim entrusted him with Egypt {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 188}.
