## Overview
Amir Khayr Bak, a great Circassian amir of Qansuh al-Ghawri. Ibn al-Imad says in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* that his intrigue was the cause of the defeat of al-Ghawri's army at Marj Dabiq in 922 AH, since in the battle he leaned toward Sultan Salim. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}
