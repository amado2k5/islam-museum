# Mohamed Hoblos

## Summary
Mohamed Hoblos — also spelled Muhammad Hoblos — is a preacher originally from Lebanon, now residing in Sydney, Australia. He is renowned for his passionate and emotional style of preaching, which has earned him a significant following, particularly among young Muslims, and he travels extensively to lecture to Muslim communities around the world. {cite:listennotes-muhammad-hoblos} {cite:youtube-lectures-from-mohamed-hoblos}

## Work
Hoblos produces content for One Path Network, the Sydney Muslim media platform; among his best-known videos is his interview with Ali Banat, "Gifted with Cancer", made for Living Muslim, in which the Sydney businessman described his terminal illness as a gift that changed his life. {cite:listennotes-muhammad-hoblos} He was part of the Sydney Deen Team, an Islamic radio programme, and took part in the Islamic short film "Last Chance". {cite:listennotes-muhammad-hoblos} His lectures circulate through the "Lectures From Mohamed Hoblos" channel, whose biography — like the Muslim Central profile — stresses his emotive delivery; the channel also states plainly that he is not a shaykh. {cite:youtube-lectures-from-mohamed-hoblos}
