## Overview
Jamal al-Din al-Sanbati, the Shafi'i preacher; he kept Shaykh Siraj al-Din al-Bulqini's circle reading on him from his words and others', and spoke to the people at al-Azhar mosque for some seventy years — yet still worked at knowledge and commanded fiqh — and deputised in judgment for the qadi Jalal al-Din and others; he died in Ramadan 846 after a long illness, per Ibn Hajar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 258}
