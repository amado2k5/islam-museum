## Summary

Al-Dahhak ibn Haritha ibn Zayd ibn Tha'laba, a Khazraji Ansari. Musa ibn Uqba names him among those who witnessed Badr, and Urwa among those who witnessed al-Aqaba — Abu Hatim calls him Aqabi and Badri.
