## Overview
Abu'l-Rabi' al-Asqalani, the Shafi'i jurist, khatib of the Haram and grandson of 'Umar b. 'Abd al-Majid al-Mayanishi; he transmitted from Zahir b. Rustam and others; he died in Muharram 661 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 529}.
