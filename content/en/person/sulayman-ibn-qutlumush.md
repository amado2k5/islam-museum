## Overview
Sulayman ibn Qutlumush the Seljuk, lord of Konya and Aksaray, one of the Seljuks of Rum. He marched with his armies to Syria in the year 477 AH and took Antioch, which had been in Byzantine hands since the year 358 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.651}.
