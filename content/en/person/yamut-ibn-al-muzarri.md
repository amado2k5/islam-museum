## Overview
Yamut ibn al-Muzarri', traditionist and man of letters, nephew of al-Jahiz; he related of al-Jahiz's last illness that he said 'I suffer a twofold affliction — sickness and debt', that his age was seventy-odd years, and that he would have died rather than fall sick again, fearing the illness's return.
