## Summary
Abu Nasr Mansur ibn Ali ibn Iraq was a mathematician and astronomer of Khwarazm who studied under [[person/abu-al-wafa-al-buzjani|Abu al-Wafa Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Buzjani]]; he was styled "client of the Commander of the Faithful", and the editor of the Qanun identifies the caliph as [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir bi-llah]]. {cite:qanun-masudi-jundi-v1 p. 24}

## Al-Biruni's teacher
[[person/al-biruni-muhammad-ibn-ahmad|Al-Biruni]] studied under Abu Nasr, who showed his pupil great kindness: whenever questions troubled al-Biruni he laid them before Abu Nasr, who guided him with the utmost care. {cite:qanun-masudi-jundi-v1 p. 24} One witness is his epistle to al-Biruni "in answer to questions of geometry", in which he notes that a third of the questions al-Biruni sent were already covered by Abu Sahl's book. {cite:qanun-masudi-jundi-v1 p. 24}

## Al-Biruni's loyalty to him
Al-Biruni's loyalty shows in [[work/al-athar-al-baqiya|al-Athar al-baqiya]], where he writes of "the derivation of my teacher Abu Nasr Mansur ibn Ali ibn Iraq". {cite:qanun-masudi-jundi-v1 p. 25} In the catalogue of his own works al-Biruni says that what others composed in his name are to him like foster children, and that among those who did so was Abu Nasr Mansur ibn Ali ibn Iraq. {cite:qanun-masudi-jundi-v1 p. 25}
