## Summary
Alqama ibn Mujazziz al-Mudlaji, a Companion; the Prophet set him over [[event/sariyya-of-alqama-to-jeddah|a detachment of three hundred]] to Jeddah's shores in Rabi' al-Akhir of year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## The Jeddah-coast detachment
The detachment was sent against men of Abyssinia gathered near Jeddah's shores to raid Mecca's people; he crossed the sea until he reached their island, and when they heard the Muslims marching on them they fled {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
Alqama led a large detachment across the sea to protect Jeddah's coast from pirates who attacked people; they fled as soon as they heard of his coming.
