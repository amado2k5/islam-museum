## Summary
Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Halabi settled in Damascus, was a lover of Sari al-Saqati, and was one of the pillars among the scholarly worshippers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 645, sequence 646, on page 366. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/366}

## A report on repaying a kindness
Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar relates from Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, Abu Uthman, in Damascus, from Abu Umayya Ubayd ibn Hisham, from Hafs ibn Umar al-Wasiti, from Amr ibn Kathir, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Zinad, from his father, from Aban ibn Uthman ibn Affan, from his father, a report that whoever does a kindness to a man of the Banu Abd al-Muttalib in this world, which he cannot repay, God will repay on the Day of Resurrection. The wording of the report is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/366}
