## Summary
Muhammad ibn al-Fadl, Abu Bakr al-Fadli al-Kamari, from Kamar near Bukhara, a jurist and mufti. He learned from Abd Allah al-Sabadhmuni and Abu Hafs al-Saghir, and among his students were Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn al-Khadir al-Nasafi and Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Katib. His fatwas and transmissions fill the books of fatwa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8195}
