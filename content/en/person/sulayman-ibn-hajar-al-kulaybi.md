## Overview
He is one of the chiefs of [[group/banu-kulayb-saada|Banu Kulayb]]. When the Kulaybis returned to al-Hadi and drove the Akilis out of their country to the land of [[group/banu-yajr-saada|Banu Yajr]], he stayed with them, because in the narrator's words he did not feel safe with al-Hadi after the war he had made on him together with the Akilis, while all the other Kulaybis came down to al-Hadi, who gave them safety. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.197} The name is read as printed. See [[event/the-kulaybis-swear-obedience-and-the-akilis-are-sent-to-banu-yajr-287]].
