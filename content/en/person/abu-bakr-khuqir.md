## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Arif ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad Ali Khuqir: a Hanbali jurist of Mecca — born, resident and deceased there — between 1282 and 1349 AH (1865–1930 CE). He was appointed mufti of the Hanbalis in 1327 AH; in the days of Sharif Husayn ibn Ali he suffered imprisonment of eighteen months followed by some seventy months of exile, after which he traded in books and kept a bookshop at Bab al-Salam in Mecca. In the Saudi era he was appointed a teacher in the Meccan sanctuary and remained so until his death. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.70}

His works include Fasl al-Maqal wa-Irshad al-Dall fi Tawassul al-Juhhal (printed), Musamarat al-Dayf fi Rihlat al-Shita' wa-l-Sayf (printed), Ma La Budd Minhu fi Umur al-Din (printed), and al-Tahqiq fi al-Tariq, a critique of the Sufi paths. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.70}
