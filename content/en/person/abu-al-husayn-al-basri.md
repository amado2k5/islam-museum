## Overview
Abu'l-Husayn Muhammad b. 'Ali b. al-Tayyib al-Basri, the mutakallim of the Mu'tazila — one of the foremost figures pointed to in this art; fine of speech, lovely of expression, abundant in output. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
He has surpassing works in usul al-fiqh, among them al-Mu'tamad — a large book from which Fakhr al-Din al-Razi took his Mahsul — Tasaffuh al-adilla in two volumes, Ghurar al-adilla, Sharh al-usul al-khamsa, and a book on the imamate. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
