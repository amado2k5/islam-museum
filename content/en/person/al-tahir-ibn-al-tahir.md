## Overview
Al-Tahir ibn al-Tahir was a nephew of Ibn Humayd; the narrator says that in the fighting on the night of the attack at Najran he threw a stone at [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] that felled him senseless, after which Banu al-Harith, who had feared his place, grew in number and closed in. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} The index lists "al-Tahir ibn al-Tahir" at p. 146 only; the book does not say he was of Banu al-Harith, which is read from the context alone. See [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]].
