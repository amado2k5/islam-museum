Konstantin (Kosta) Hörmann (8 September 1850 – 16 November 1921) was an Austro-Hungarian official of Bavarian descent who became the founding director of the National Museum of Bosnia and Herzegovina in Sarajevo.{cite:wikipedia-kosta-hormann-en}{cite:wikipedia-kosta-hormann-hr} Born at Bjelovar and trained in Vienna for the Military Frontier administration, he entered Bosnia with the occupation forces of 1878 as a civilian commissioner and rose to head the provincial government's political-administrative department.{cite:wikipedia-kosta-hormann-en}

As museum director from 1888 he built up the collections, founded and edited the museum journal Glasnik and in 1895 launched the literary magazine Nada.{cite:wikipedia-kosta-hormann-hr} His most cited work is the two-volume Narodne pjesne Muhamedovaca u Bosni i Hercegovini (1888–89), one of the earliest major collections of Bosnian Muslim oral poetry.{cite:wikipedia-kosta-hormann-en}{cite:wikidata-kosta-hormann}
