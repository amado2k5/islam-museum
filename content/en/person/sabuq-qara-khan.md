## Overview
Sabuq Qara Khan was the ruler of the Turks at Kashgar, and the first of the Turkish kings in Turkestan to embrace Islam. He was named Abd al-Malik, according to Ibn Khaldun {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## His realm
Turkestan was under his rule, with Kashgar as its capital, together with Sagun, Khumu and the lands connected to them, as far as the desert that adjoins China. He and his descendants obeyed the Samanids and called upon them for their wars {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

The records link him to the [[polity/qarakhanid-dynasty|Qarakhanid state]] as one of its rulers, and describe him as the first Muslim king in Turkestan.
