## Overview
Mansur al-Namri, a poet whom al-Mas'udi mentions in a lament for the Barmakids. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.368}

## His lament
Among his verses for them is one calling for weeping over the Banu Barmak, and describing the world as a bride adorned by them for a time, which is now in mourning. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.368}
