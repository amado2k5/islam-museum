## Overview
Sawran, a figure whom al-Baladhuri mentions in the report of [[event/al-mufarraj-fortresses|the fortresses of al-Mufarraj]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His report in al-Baladhuri
He rose after al-Mufarraj ibn Salam in "the great land". He sent his envoy to [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] asking him for a charter and a letter of appointment, but al-Mutawakkil died before the envoy returned. The charter remained pending until Wasif, client of the Commander of the Faithful, granted it to him and enforced it in the time of [[person/al-mustain|al-Musta'in]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

In one of the copies the name appears as "Sudan" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
