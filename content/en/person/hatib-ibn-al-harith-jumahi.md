## Summary
Of Banu Jumah; he emigrated to Abyssinia with his wife Fatima bint al-Mujallal and his sons Muhammad and al-Harith, and perished there a Muslim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants" of Banu Jumah, with his sons Muhammad and al-Harith and his wife Fatima bint al-Mujallal, saying he perished there a Muslim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He says his wife and two sons came in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He names him again among those who perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The spelling of the children's mother's name differs between "al-Mujallal" (jim) and "al-Muhallal" (ha) in the book's copies; editors' note p. 7.
