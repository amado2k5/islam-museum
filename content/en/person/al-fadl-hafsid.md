## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn Abi Bakr al-Mutawakkil ala Allah ibn Yahya al-Hafsi, Abu al-Abbas, was one of the Hafsid kings in Tunis. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 721 AH, 1321 CE, and his death as 751 AH, 1350 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 147}.

## His reign
Al-Zirikli says he took power in 750 AH, in the days when the state was in disorder and coming apart; he held it for five and a half months and was then killed by some of the men who had seized power {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 147}.
