## Overview
Rufay' Abu al-Aliya al-Riyahi, a client of a woman of Banu Riyah, who was with Sa'id ibn Uthman when he crossed the river with his troops {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His death
al-Shirazi reports that he died in 106 AH, and it was also said 93 AH. The phrase "it was said" is a marker of doubt {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.

The disagreement over the year of death stands, and fixing it needs an additional source.
