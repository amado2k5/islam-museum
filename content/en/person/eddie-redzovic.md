# Eddie Redzovic

## Summary
Eddie Redzovic is an American Muslim broadcaster based in Chicago, the founder and host of The Deen Show, a long-running dawah talk show, and the founder and head of the Deen Center. {cite:influencewatch-deen-center} The centre's own biography presents him as a Chicago-raised convert to Islam who established the centre. {cite:deencenter-eddie-redzovic}

## The Deen Show
The show interviews Muslim clerics, activists and academics on religion, politics and culture, and has run for over eighteen years with more than 1,100 episodes; its podcast listing credits the programme to Redzovic. {cite:listennotes-deen-show} InfluenceWatch describes The Deen Show as his project and a project of the Deen Center he founded. {cite:influencewatch-deen-center}
