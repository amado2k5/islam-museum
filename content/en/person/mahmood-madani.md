## Summary
Mahmood As'ad Madani is an Indian Islamic scholar of the Deobandi school and the president of Jamiat Ulama-e-Hind, one of the oldest Muslim organisations in India, founded in 1919. He has combined religious scholarship with public office, serving a term in the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of the Indian parliament. {cite:wikipedia-mahmood-madani}

## Life and education
He was born on 3 March 1964 in Deoband, the town in Uttar Pradesh that gave the Deobandi movement its name. He is a son of As'ad Madani, who led Jamiat Ulama-e-Hind for decades, and grew up inside the world of the organisation and of Darul Uloom Deoband. {cite:wikipedia-mahmood-madani}

## Career
Madani rose through the Jamiat Ulama-e-Hind over many years, serving as its general secretary before the organisation split in 2021 into two rival bodies; he was elected president of the faction recognised as Jamiat Ulama-e-Hind (M). Between 2006 and 2012 he sat as a member of the Rajya Sabha. Under his leadership the Jamiat has run relief and legal-aid programmes for victims of communal violence, campaigned on questions touching Muslim personal law, waqf property and citizenship, and maintained the organisation's stated commitment to Indian secularism and its opposition to violence — positions the museum reports as those of the organisation, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-mahmood-madani}

## Standing
He appears regularly in The Muslim 500, the annual listing of influential Muslims published in Amman, which ranks him among the leading scholars and administrators of religious affairs in South Asia. Supporters see in him the continuer of a century-old institution of Indian Muslim civil society; critics within the Deobandi world point to the 2021 split and to rival claims on the Jamiat's legacy. Both readings are recorded as attributed positions. {cite:wikipedia-mahmood-madani}
