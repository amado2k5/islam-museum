## Overview
Habash ibn Musa al-Sini, author of a Book of Songs arranged by alphabetical letters which he composed for al-Mutawakkil, recording items of song that neither Ishaq nor Amr ibn Bana mentioned. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 449}
