## Summary
Abu al-Walid ibn Jahwar was the son of the vizier Jahwar ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Hazm; rule over Cordoba passed to him on his father's death in 435 AH, making him one of the city's taifa kings. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.339}

## Details
Al-Maqqari says in Nafh al-Tib that the Banu Jahwar were kings and viziers of Cordoba, descended from Kalb ibn Wabara, and that when the caliphate's cord unravelled the vizier Abu al-Hazm ibn Jahwar took sole control of Cordoba without going beyond the title of vizier. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.338} Describing the taifa kings, al-Maqqari notes that they seized power in al-Andalus — Ibn Jahwar in Cordoba, Ibn Abbad in Seville — and that the land had no single ruler until Yusuf ibn Tashfin came from across the strait and destroyed the taifa kings. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.337}
