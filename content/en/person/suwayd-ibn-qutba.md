## Overview
Suwayd ibn Qutba al-Dhuhli, of Bakr ibn Wa'il. He was in Basra with a group of his people when it was conquered {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## In al-Baladhuri
al-Baladhuri reports that he wished to do in Basra what al-Muthanna had done in Kufa. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] relied on him in fighting at [[event/battle-of-ubulla|al-Ubulla]], and then appointed him over its district {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Other than Abu Mikhnaf, the report says that Qutba ibn Qatada al-Dhuhli was the one who was there. The name differs between the reports, and the matter needs an additional source. He is of [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr ibn Wa'il]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
