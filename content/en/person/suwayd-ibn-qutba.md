## Overview
Suwayd ibn Qutba al-Dhahli of Bakr ibn Wa'il; he was at Basra with a band of his people at its conquest. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Baladhuri
Al-Baladhuri says he intended to do at Basra what al-Muthanna did at Kufa. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] sheltered with him during the battle of [[event/battle-of-ubulla|Ubulla]] and then left him in charge of his sector. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He transmits from someone other than Abu Mikhnaf that it was Qutba ibn Qatada al-Dhahli who was there. The report differs on the name, and the matter needs an additional source. He is of [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr ibn Wa'il]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 3812: "Suwayd ibn Qutba al-Wa'ili, mentioned in al-Futuh", and transmits from Abu Isma'il al-Azdi in the conquests of Syria that when Khalid ibn al-Walid arrived at the site of Basra he found there a man called Suwayd ibn Qutba of Banu Bakr ibn Wa'il around whom a band had gathered, and Khalid placed him among his companions. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.639}
