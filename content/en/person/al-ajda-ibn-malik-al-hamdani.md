## Overview
The leader of Hamdan at the day of al-Radm against Murad, shortly before Islam, according to Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Dispute over the leader's name
Ibn Hisham relates the report from Ibn Ishaq, but differs from him and makes the leader Malik ibn Harim al-Hamdani. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## His connections
He belongs to [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], and took part in [[event/battle-of-al-radm|the day of al-Radm]] as their leader according to Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
