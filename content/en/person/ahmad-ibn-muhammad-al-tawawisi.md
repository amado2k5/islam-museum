## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hamid ibn Hashim al-Tawawisi, named after Tawawis, a village eight farsakhs from Bukhara. He related from Muhammad ibn Nasr al-Marwazi and Abd Allah ibn Shirawayh al-Nisaburi, among others, and Nasr ibn Muhammad ibn Gharib al-Shashi and Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn Idris related from him. The book gives his death as 344 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6806}
