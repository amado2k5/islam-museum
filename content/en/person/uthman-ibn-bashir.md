## Overview
Uthman ibn Bashir ibn al-Muhtafiz. Abd Allah ibn Khazim captured him at Herat after his son Muhammad had been killed, and he executed him in cold blood {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}. When Ibn Khazim was killed, they sent his right hand to the sons of Uthman {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
