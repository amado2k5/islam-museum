## Overview
"al-Qasim" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen. He is named with his brother Abdullah, and the book does not say whether their father is the Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Rassi who has [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-al-rassi|a record]], so the museum draws no link. He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
