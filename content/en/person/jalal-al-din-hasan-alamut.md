## Summary
Jalal al-Din Hasan, lord of Alamut and Dardakuha, head of the Isma'ilis; he had openly displayed the shari'a of Islam — the adhan and the rest — so the subjects turned to him after his forefathers had been otherwise; he died in 618 and his eldest son succeeded him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 84}
