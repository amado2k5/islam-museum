## Overview
Rudhmir, king of the Galicians until 332 AH. Before him the kingship was held by Ordoño, and before him by Adefonso, as al-Mas'udi reports {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.359-360}.

## His vizier
He made Umayya ibn Ishaq his vizier, the brother of the vizier of Abd al-Rahman al-Nasir who was killed, and Umayya showed him the weak points of the Muslims {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.359-360}.

## The battle of Simancas
The battle between him and Abd al-Rahman, lord of al-Andalus, took place at Simancas in Shawwal 327 AH. According to al-Mas'udi, about fifty thousand Muslims were killed there after they had crossed the trench {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.359-360}.
