## Summary
Kitbugha al-Mansuri was a Mamluk sultan who took power in 694 AH after the deposition of al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun, taking the title al-Malik al-Adil. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.730}

## Details
Al-Suyuti records in Tarikh al-Khulafa' that the amirs deposed al-Nasir Muhammad ibn al-Mansur and enthroned his brother Muhammad ibn al-Mansur with the title al-Malik al-Nasir — he was then nine years old — and that he too was deposed in Muharram of 694, whereupon Kitbugha al-Mansuri became sultan under the name al-Malik al-Adil. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.730} In that same year Qazan ibn Arghun ibn Abgha ibn Hulagu, king of the Tatars, entered Islam; the people rejoiced and Islam spread through his army. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.731} Then in 696, while the sultan was in Damascus, Lajin seized the sultanate; the amirs swore to him, none disputed his claim, and he took the title al-Malik al-Mansur. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.731}
