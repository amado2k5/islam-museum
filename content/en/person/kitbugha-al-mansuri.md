## Overview
A Mamluk sultan who took power in 694 AH, after the deposition of [[person/al-nasir-muhammad-ibn-qalawun|al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun]], and styled himself al-Malik al-Adil. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.730}
