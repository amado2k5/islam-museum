## Overview
Ibn al-Munayyir, qadi of Alexandria and its famous scholar, a Maliki (620–683); brilliant in fiqh, usul, dialectic, Arabic and rhetoric, with authored works; he died at the start of Rabi' I {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 652}.
