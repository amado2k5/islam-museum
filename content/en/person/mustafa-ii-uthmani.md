## Overview
Sultan Mustafa II ibn Muhammad IV, who ruled from 1106 to 1115 AH (1694–1703 CE). He was the son of [[person/mehmed-iv-uthmani|Muhammad IV]], and succeeded his uncle Ahmed II. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}

## His reign
Al-Salabi says that in his reign the Islamic advance began to retreat from Eastern Europe. The Treaty of Karlowitz was signed in 1110 AH (1699 CE), by which the Ottomans withdrew from Hungary and Transylvania, and the states that had been paying tribute stopped paying it. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}

## Deposition
The Janissaries demanded the removal of the Grand Vizier and the sultan refused, so they decided to depose him. He died four months later at the age of thirty-nine. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}
