## Summary
The Prophet's son by [[person/maria-al-qibtiyya|Maria the Copt]]. He died young in Medina on 28 or 29 Shawwal, 10 AH, corresponding to 27 January 632 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.475}.

## His account
Al-Muqawqis gave Maria the Copt to the Prophet as a gift, and she bore him his son Ibrahim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.475}. Ibrahim died young in Medina while his father was still alive, on 28 or 29 Shawwal, 10 AH, corresponding to 27 January 632 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.475}. When he died, the Prophet said: "The eye weeps and the heart grieves, and we say only what pleases our Lord. Indeed, Ibrahim, we are saddened by you" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
The last of the Prophet's children was named Ibrahim. He died as an infant in Medina, and his father grieved for him deeply.


## In the chapter on the marriage to Khadija (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum p.61)
Al-Raheeq al-Makhtum, in the chapter on the Prophet's marriage to [[person/khadija|Khadija]], mentions that all of the Prophet's children by her were born to him except Ibrahim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/190-191}.
