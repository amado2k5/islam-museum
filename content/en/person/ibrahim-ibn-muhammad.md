## Summary
The Prophet's son by [[person/maria-al-qibtiyya|Maria the Copt]]; he died young in Medina on 28 or 29 Shawwal, year 10 AH — January 27, 632 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 475}.

## His account
Al-Muqawqis gifted Maria the Copt to the Prophet, and she bore him his son Ibrahim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 475}. Ibrahim died young in Medina in his father's lifetime on 28 or 29 Shawwal, year 10 AH — January 27, 632 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 475}. At his death the Prophet said: the eye weeps and the heart grieves, and we say nothing but what pleases our Lord; indeed, Ibrahim, we are grieved for you {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
The last of the Prophet's children was named Ibrahim; he died as a suckling infant in Medina, and his father grieved for him deeply.
