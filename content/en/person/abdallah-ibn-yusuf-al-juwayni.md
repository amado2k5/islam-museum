## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah b. Yusuf b. 'Abdallah b. Yusuf b. Muhammad b. Hayuwayh al-Juwayni, the Shafi'i jurist — father of the Imam al-Haramayn; an imam in tafsir, fiqh, usul, Arabic and adab. He read adab under his father Abu Ya'qub, then worked at fiqh at Nishapur under Abu'l-Tayyib Sahl b. Muhammad al-Sa'luki and at Marw under Abu Bakr al-Qaffal al-Marwazi until he mastered the madhhab and khilaf. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Teaching and works
He returned to Nishapur in 407, sat to teach and give fatwa, and a great company formed on him — among them his son the Imam al-Haramayn. His works: the great Tafsir, al-Tabsira, al-Tadhkira, Mukhtasar al-mukhtasar, and Mawqif al-imam wa'l-ma'mum. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
