## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Thabit, Abu Abd Allah al-Muqri (d. 378 AH), the blind reciter and author of the poem on the readings of the seven. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.146}

## The blind hafiz
He was born blind and was a memoriser. He attended the session of Ibn al-Anbari and memorised what was dictated there. He is the author of the poem on the readings of the seven, which he composed during the lifetime of al-Naqqash; al-Naqqash and the shaykhs of his time were impressed by it. He was witty and handsomely dressed. He died in Ramadan of the year 378 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.146}
