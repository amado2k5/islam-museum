## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Ibrahim b. Sa'id al-Hawfi, author of I'rab al-Qur'an in ten volumes; an imam in Arabic, grammar and adab with many works — pupil of al-Adfawi — Egypt's people profited from him and trained in grammar under him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 247}
