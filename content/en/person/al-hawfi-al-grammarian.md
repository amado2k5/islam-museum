## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Ibrahim b. Sa'id al-Hawfi, author of I'rab al-Qur'an in ten volumes; an imam in Arabic, grammar and adab with many works — pupil of al-Adfawi — Egypt's people profited from him and trained in grammar under him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 247}

## Full name and death date in the Siyar
A research summary supplied to the museum (the museum did not open the page it names; the founder approved its use on 21 November 2026) gives his full name from al-Dhahabi's Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', vol. 17, as Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ibrahim ibn Sa'id al-Hawfi, and his death in 430 AH (1038-1039 CE) {cite:islamweb-siyar-al-hawfi p.521-522}, which agrees with the record above. Ibn Hisham al-Ansari names al-Hawfi in the preface of [[work/mughni-al-labib|Mughni al-Labib]] as the one most thorough in parsing the obvious {cite:mughni-labib-mubarak-1985 p.16}; that the name there is this al-Hawfi is the research summary's identification.
