## Overview
Arghwan, founder of the Hafiziyya madrasa, aunt of al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Turanshah b. al-Malik al-Salih Najm al-Din; a slave-girl of the bustard whom al-Malik al-'Adil freed — she acquired great wealth, endowed it, and built the Hafiziyya madrasa with its great garden; a lady, trustworthy and honoured. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248-249}

## Her death
She died at Cairo on 20 Sha'ban 648 and was buried in her madrasa. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248-249}
