## Summary
Frederick II, the Emperor; son of the daughter of William b. Roger of Sicily — his mother died leaving him a child, so he was crowned and his kingship endured; wise and excellent, he corresponded with al-Malik al-Kamil of Egypt, and it was he who recovered Jerusalem from him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
