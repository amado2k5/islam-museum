## Summary
Son of Hatib ibn al-Harith and Fatima bint al-Mujallal; he arrived with his mother and brother in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him a son of Hatib ibn al-Harith with his brother Muhammad, saying their mother brought them in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}
