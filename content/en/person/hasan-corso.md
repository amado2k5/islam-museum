## Summary
Beylerbey of Algiers by sultanic appointment, 963/1556 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.

## Appointed at sea
The order naming him reached the fleet while the commander Yahya was already sailing on Oran to carry out [[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]]'s plan; Hasan took over the siege, which failed to take the city for Spain's continuous relief {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.
