## Summary
Abu Mansur Muhammad ibn 'Ali ibn Ibrahim ibn Zubraj the grammarian, known as al-'Utabi for the 'Utaba quarter of west Baghdad; versed in grammar, philology and the arts of adab; his hand was a fine, correct script that men of learning competed over — any book in his hand was sought after; he read adab under the sharif Abu'l-Sa'adat Ibn al-Shajari and Abu Mansur Mawhub ibn al-Jawaliqi, heard hadith from the shaykhs of his time and wrote much. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
