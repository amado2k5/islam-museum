## Overview
Sabur ibn Sahl was the head of the hospital of Gundishapur, and a Christian, according to Ibn al-Nadim {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.413}.

## His learning
Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an accomplished and advanced scholar. He wrote the Aqrabadhin, on which hospitals and pharmacists' shops relied, in twenty-two chapters, and a book on the powers of foods, their harms and their benefits {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.413}.

## His death
He died on Monday, nine days before the end of Dhu al-Hijja in the year 255 AH {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.413}.
