## Summary
Umayr ibn al-Humam al-Ansari al-Khazraji, one of the martyrs of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## His scene at Badr
When the Prophet roused the Muslims to fight, saying "rise to a Paradise as wide as the heavens and the earth", Umayr said: bakh, bakh! — and when asked what had moved him to say it he said: no, by God, Messenger of God — only the hope of being one of its people. He said: "then you are of its people" {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 218}.

He took out some dates from his quiver and began to eat them, then said: if I live until I finish these dates of mine, that is a long life indeed — and he threw away the dates he had and fought until he was killed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 218}.
