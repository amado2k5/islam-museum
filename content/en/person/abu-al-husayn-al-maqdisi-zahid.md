## Overview
Abu'l-Husayn al-Maqdisi the ascetic, man of states and wonders; Shaykh al-Diya' wrote down his sira in one part, and his grave at Aleppo is visited. His tarjama is in the annals of 548. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 632}
