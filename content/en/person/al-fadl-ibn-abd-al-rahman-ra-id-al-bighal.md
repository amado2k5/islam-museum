## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Abbas ibn Rabi'a ibn al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib, titled "Breaker of Mules" for his skill at riding them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What al-Baladhuri relates
After Ibn al-Ash'ath fled to Sistan, people followed him, and al-Fadl fled from [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The estate of the river Nafidh was returned to him; Uthman had granted it to al-Abbas ibn Rabi'a together with a hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His birth and death dates, and the details of his story after his flight, need an additional source.
