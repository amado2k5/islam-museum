## Overview
Hashim ibn Utba ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri, known as al-Mirqal. He was of the Banu Abi Waqqas. Al-Baladhuri reports that he lost an eye on the day of [[event/battle-of-yarmuk|Yarmuk]], along with others who lost their eyes there, such as al-Ash'ath ibn Qays and Qays ibn Maksuh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## At al-Qadisiyya
Al-Mas'udi reports that he commanded the reinforcements sent from Syria to al-Qadisiyya: five thousand horsemen from Rabi'a and Mudar, and a thousand from Yemen, with them al-Qa'qa' ibn Amr, a month after the conquest of Damascus. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 609}

## At Siffin
According to al-Mas'udi, he was killed at Siffin together with Dhu al-Kala', the bearer of the standard of Himyar. His son took up the banner when he was killed. Ali stood over his body and the bodies of the Aslamis killed around him; he asked God's mercy on them and recited a verse in their praise. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 674-675}
