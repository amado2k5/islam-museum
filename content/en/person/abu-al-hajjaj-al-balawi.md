## Overview
Yusuf ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Yahya ibn Ghalib, Abu al-Hajjaj al-Balawi of Malaga, Andalusi and Maliki, also called Ibn al-Shaykh: a scholar of language and letters, born and deceased in Malaga between 529 and 604 AH (1135–1207 CE). He held the office of preacher in Malaga and visited Alexandria on his pilgrimage journeys in 561 and 562 AH. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.247}

Al-Mundhiri says he was one of the famous ascetics: he is said to have built some twelve mosques in Malaga with his own hands and never missed a campaign by land or sea. Ibn al-Abbar reports that he built twenty-five mosques in his home town at his own expense, worked on them himself, dug with his own hands more than fifty wells, and campaigned with al-Mansur in the Maghrib and with Saladin in Syria. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.247}
