## Overview
[[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]] chose him as chief of police in Iraq and the East, and the book notes that he stipulated that al-Hajjaj should provide for his dependants and household. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 133}

## In the police
The book relates from [[person/al-shaabi|al-Sha'bi]] reports of his severity in keeping order, among them the cutting off of the hand of a man who drew a weapon on people. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 133}

Al-Hajjaj added the police of Basra to the police of Kufa. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 134}

The data do not give the dates of his birth or death; a second source is needed.
