## Overview
Salim ibn Wabisa ibn Mabad al-Asadi al-Raqqi, an emir and hadith scholar whom the book counts among the Successors, who was also a poet. He governed Raqqa for Muhammad ibn Marwan for about thirty years and died at the end of Hisham's caliphate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3132}

## Dates
He died around 125 AH, a date the book gives as approximate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3132}
