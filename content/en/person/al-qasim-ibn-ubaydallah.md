## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah, vizier of the caliph al-Mukafi. Al-Mas'udi describes him as very awesome and bold in attack, and says people were in fear of him, great and small. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 638-640}

## His reports in al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi attributes to him the killing of Abd al-Wahid ibn al-Muwaffaq, the uncle of al-Mukafi, and the destruction of the poet Ali ibn al-Abbas ibn Jurayj al-Rumi by poison in a pastry "as it was said". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 638-640}

He died on the evening of Wednesday, ten nights into Rabi al-Akhir 291 AH, at about thirty years of age. Al-Mas'udi says that people drank in joy at his death. Then al-Mukafi wanted to dig up his grave and beat and burn him, when it became clear to him that he had killed Abd al-Wahid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 638-640}

## In the Fihrist
Ibn al-Nadim says that Ishaq ibn Hunayn was devoted to him and specially favoured by him, and confided his secrets to him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 415}
