## Overview
He is of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]], a man of loyalty and affection in the narrator's words. He overtook [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] as he followed those who had raided Hamdan, with another Harithi man, and they asked him to go back to his place since he had no army, and swore that he must return. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.200} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.201} The name is printed al-Jahir and was confirmed by Apple Vision. See [[event/banu-al-harith-raid-hamdan-and-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-rides-out-of-najran-287]].
