## Overview
Alqama ibn Marthad al-Hadrami al-Kufi, Abu al-Harith, is called by al-Dhahabi the imam, jurist and authority {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}. The sign printed with his entry shows his hadith is in the six canonical books {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, Tariq ibn Shihab, Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla, Sa'd ibn Ubayda and their like; al-Dhahabi counts him among the younger Followers, though he died early {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}. Ghaylan ibn Jami', Abu Hanifa, al-Awza'i, Shu'ba, Sufyan al-Thawri, Mis'ar ibn Kidam, al-Mas'udi and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}.

## What the scholars said
Ahmad ibn Hanbal said he is firm in hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}.

## His death
Al-Dhahabi says he died in 120 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2718}. The report on the preceding page about Alqama ibn Qays al-Nakha'i concerns a different man and is not attributed to this one {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2717}.
