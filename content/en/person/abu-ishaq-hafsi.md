## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Abi Zakariya al-Hafsi, a Hafsid sultan. He was the son of [[person/abu-zakariya-al-hafsi|Abu Zakariya]] and the brother of [[person/al-mustansir-hafsi|al-Mustansir]]. Ibn Khaldun mentions him in "al-Ibar". {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1723-1724}

## His return from al-Andalus
He had been displaced to al-Andalus in the days of his brother. When al-Mustansir died and his son al-Wathiq was given the allegiance, he crossed from al-Andalus to Tlemcen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1723-1724}

## Taking power
He entered Bejaia at the end of Dhu al-Qa'da 677 AH, and the Almohads and notables gave him the allegiance. He entered the capital in the middle of Dhu al-Hijja at the end of 678 AH, after his nephew was deposed. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1723-1724}

He appointed Abu Bakr ibn Abi al-Hasan ibn Khaldun to the office of public works. Abu Bakr had come with his father al-Hasan to the Amir Abu Zakariya from Seville. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1723-1724}

## His death
He died in 683 AH according to the best-known dates; the Gregorian conversion is derived. He is among the rulers of the [[polity/hafsid-dynasty|Hafsid state]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
