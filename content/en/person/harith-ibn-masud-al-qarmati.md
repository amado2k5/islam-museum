## Overview
Harith ibn Mas'ud al-Qarmati, a leader of the Qarmatians who appeared in the countryside of Iraq. Ibn al-Athir reports in "Al-Kamil" that more than ten thousand men who held the Qarmatian doctrine and had been concealing it gathered around him in the countryside of Wasit, and then revealed it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.137}

## In the lands of al-Muwaffaqiyya
Harith moved to the lands of al-Muwaffaqiyya, built a house there which he named "the House of Emigration", and seized that district. Ibn al-Athir describes his followers as plundering, taking captives and killing, and says that they defeated the Banu Ibn Nufays. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.137}
