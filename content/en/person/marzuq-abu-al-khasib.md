## Overview
Marzuq Abu al-Khasib, a client of Khazim ibn Khuzayma, and the Palace of al-Khasib in Kufa is attributed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The stratagem of Tabaristan
The museum's data say that he devised a stratagem against al-Mansur's conquest of Tabaristan, and this is [[event/marzuq-ispahbadh-ruse|the stratagem of Marzuq with the Ispahbadh]]. Two commanders struck him and shaved his head and beard, and he claimed to the Ispahbadh that they had wronged him, and fled to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The Ispahbadh trusted him, and he guided him to the weak points of the Arabs, and devised a way to the gate until the Muslims took the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
