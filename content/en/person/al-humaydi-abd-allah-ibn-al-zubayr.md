## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr ibn Isa al-Humaydi al-Asadi al-Makki, a Meccan hadith scholar of the Banu Asad, one of the greatest transmitters of hadith in Mecca, and the teacher of [[person/al-bukhari|Imam al-Bukhari]]. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.284}

## His teachers
The "Classes of the Jurists" says that he travelled to Egypt with [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] and kept his company until he died, and that he took from [[person/sufyan-ibn-uyayna|Ibn Uyayna]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## His death
He died in 219 AH (834 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.284}
