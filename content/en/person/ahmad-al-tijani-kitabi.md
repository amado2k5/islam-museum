## Overview
Ahmad al-Tijani ibn Yusuf ibn Bashir al-Kitani, a scholar of the al-Kitani house, known among the tribes of the Maghrib. He was born in 1912 CE (1331 AH) and died in 1937 CE (1356 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are recorded as reported dates and need an additional source. The rest of his life needs an additional source.
