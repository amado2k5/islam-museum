## Overview
Ibrahim b. Muflih, shaykh of the Hanbalis, their head and chief qadi at Damascus; among his works Kitab fadl al-salat 'ala'l-nabi, Kitab al-mala'ika, a commentary on al-Muqni', and Tabaqat ashab al-imam Ahmad — most of them lost in Timur's fitna. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 30-31}

## Timur's fitna
When the Tatar fitna struck he was the last to leave Damascus, then went out to Timur with a company; debates and refutations ran between him and 'Abd al-Jabbar the Mu'tazili, Timur's imam, in Tamerlane's presence — Timur admired him and inclined to him, so he spoke to him of peace and Timur assented, then went back on it; the man of this entry grieved until he died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 30-31}
