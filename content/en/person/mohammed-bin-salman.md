# Mohammed bin Salman

Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud has been Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia since 21 June 2017 and Prime Minister since 27 September 2022. He was born at Riyadh on 31 August 1985, the first child of King Salman and his third wife Fahda bint Falah Al Hithlain, and took a law degree from King Saud University. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-salman-en} {cite:wikidata-mohammed-bin-salman}

## Rise

He worked as an adviser to his father from 2009, became deputy crown prince and defence minister in 2015, and on 21 June 2017 his father appointed him Crown Prince in place of Muhammad bin Nayef; on 27 September 2022 he became Prime Minister. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-salman-en}

## Reforms and positions

In April 2016 he announced the Saudi Vision 2030 programme to diversify his country's economy away from oil, and he has led wide social reforms, among them the lifting of the ban on women driving in 2018 and the curtailing of the Wahhabi religious establishment's role in public life. In a televised interview on 25 April 2021 he criticised devotion to Wahhabi doctrines and said there are "no fixed schools of thought" — a statement of religious policy, not an attestation of personal affiliation. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-salman-en}

## Positions and criticism

A United States ODNI assessment concluded that he ordered the assassination of the journalist Jamal Khashoggi in 2018; Mohammed bin Salman has denied ordering it, while accepting responsibility for it as the country's leader. This is recorded in attributed form, as documented in the source. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-salman-en}
