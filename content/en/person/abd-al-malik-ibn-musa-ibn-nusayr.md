## Overview
Abd al-Malik, son of [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]]. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.322}

## Rule of the Far Maghrib
Al-Maqqari reports from Ibn Sa'id that the descendants of Musa had distinction in rule: Abd al-Aziz took rule over al-Andalus, Abd al-Malik over the Far Maghrib, and Abdallah over Ifriqiya. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.322}
