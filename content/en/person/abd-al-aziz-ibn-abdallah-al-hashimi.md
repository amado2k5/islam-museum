## Summary
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abdallah ibn Ubaydallah ibn al-Abbas ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abdallah ibn al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, Abu al-Qasim al-Hashimi, was a Baghdadi hadith scholar of the descendants of Ibn Abbas. He heard from Yahya ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Muqri, Abu Bakr al-Humaydi and Abdallah ibn Ja'far al-Raqqi. Abdallah ibn Ishaq al-Madaini, the judge al-Muhamili and Ismail ibn Muhammad al-Saffar transmitted from him. Al-Daraqutni said: he was trustworthy. He died eleven nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja in the year two hundred and seventy-five, aged eighty-six. He was handsome and dignified, and he had not dyed his white hair. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.9 p.451-452}
