## Summary
Ibn Rawaha al-Shafi'i al-Hawi was born in Sicily while his parents were in captivity, in 560; his father read him hadith at Alexandria from the elder al-Salafi and a circle. He died on 8 Jumada II at eighty-five. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 242}
