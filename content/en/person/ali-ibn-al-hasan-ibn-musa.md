## Summary
Ali ibn al-Hasan ibn Musa kept wisdom in mind and transmitted from workers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 504, sequence 505, on pages 143 and 144. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/143} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}

## What opens thought
Abu Bakr ibn Ubayd relates from Ali ibn al-Hasan that a learned man was asked what opens thought. He said: the gathering of resolve, for when a servant gathers his resolve he reflects, and when he reflects he looks, and when he looks he sees, and when he sees he acts; he is then one who moves upward in action. He was asked how that ascent works. He said: it is carried by the desire for virtues until he reaches a point at which God brings him near by His kindness and clothes him in kindness. Asked what the garment of kindness is, he said: humility, dignity, tranquillity, piety and modesty. A servant so clothed is raised to honour, and God, being honoured, gives him a draught of His love, then moves him among causes, then follows him with the deed done for Him. Such a person is given the reward of a year for one night's reflection, and the reward of a night for a year's reflection. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}
