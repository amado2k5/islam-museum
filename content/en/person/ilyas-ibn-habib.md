## Overview
Ilyas ibn Habib, son of [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri]]. Al-Baladhuri says that he was appointed to govern the frontier of the Maghrib after his father. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325}

## After him
Habib ibn Abd al-Rahman was appointed after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325}

The data do not record his years in office. It needs an additional source.
