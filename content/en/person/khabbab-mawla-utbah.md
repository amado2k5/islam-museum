## Summary

Khabbab, the freedman of Utba ibn Ghazwan, known as Abu Yahya. Ibn Ishaq lists him among those at Badr, as one of the allies of Banu Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf. Abu Nu'aym says he left no descendants and no hadith, and that he died in 19 AH in the caliphate of Umar, who led his funeral prayer. Ibn Hajar keeps him distinct from Khabbab ibn al-Aratt and faults Ibn Manda for merging the two. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.383}
