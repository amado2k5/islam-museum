## Overview
Abu Uthman Sa'id ibn Harun al-Ashnandani al-Baghdadi, a grammarian and philologist from Baghdad. The encyclopedia counts him among the imams of the language and its transmission, and among the scholars of literature. He settled in Basra, where Ibn Duraid met him {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## His death
The encyclopedia gives his death as 256 AH (870 CE) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
