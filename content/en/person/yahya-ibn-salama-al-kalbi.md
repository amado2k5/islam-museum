## Overview
Yahya ibn Salama al-Kalbi, the first governor of al-Andalus appointed by the amirs of Ifriqiya. Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi, governor of Ifriqiya, sent him when the people of al-Andalus asked for a governor after the killing of Anbasa ibn Suhaym {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.272}.

He arrived at the end of the year 107 AH and remained in his governorship for two and a half years without raiding, according to al-Maqqari.
