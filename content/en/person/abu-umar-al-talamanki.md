## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Isa al-Ma'afiri al-Andalusi al-Talamanki, Abu Umar, was a reciter of the Qur'an and a scholar of exegesis and hadith from al-Andalus. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 340 AH and his death to 429 AH, that is 951 and 1038 CE as printed, and says he was the first to bring the science of the recitations into al-Andalus, and that he was learned in exegesis and hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 212}. Al-Safadi calls him the reciter who settled in Cordoba and says he wrote good, useful books in line with the schools of the Sunna, in which his learning shows {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 23}.

## Life
He came from Talamanca, on the eastern frontier of al-Andalus, settled in Cordoba and travelled to the East {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 213}. Al-Safadi says he took great care over the transmitted reports, began his studies early and held high chains of transmission {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 23}. Al-Safadi also says he was a drawn sword against the people of whims and innovations; this is al-Safadi's description, not the museum's judgement {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 23}.

## Death
Al-Safadi relays from Ibn Bashkuwal, through al-Hijari and his father, that one day al-Talamanki came out to his students while they were reading to him and told them to read much, since he would not live beyond that year; when they asked why, he told of a dream he had seen the night before. He died in that year, in Dhu al-Hijja 429 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 23}. Al-Zirikli says he died in Talamanca {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 213}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Dalil ila Ma'rifat al-Jalil in one hundred parts, a Tafsir of the Qur'an in about one hundred parts, al-Wusul ila Ma'rifat al-Usul, al-Bayan fi I'rab al-Qur'an, Fada'il Malik, Rijal al-Muwatta', al-Rawda on the recitations, and a treatise on the foundations of religion {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 213}.
