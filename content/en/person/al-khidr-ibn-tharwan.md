## Overview
Al-Khidr ibn Tharwan ibn Ahmad al-Tha'labi al-Tumathi al-Fariqi al-Jazari, Abu al-Abbas, was a blind grammarian with knowledge of letters and fine poetry. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.306}

His origin was Tumatha near Barqa'id in the Mosul region; he was born in the Jazira, raised in Mayyafariqin, and died at Bukhara in 580 AH (1184 CE). Yaqut praised him in his two Mu'jams and quoted some of his verse. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.306}
