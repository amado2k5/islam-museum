## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mughith 'Umar, son of al-Malik al-Salih Ayyub (d. 642) — of whom no foul word was ever recorded; imprisoned by al-Malik al-Salih Isma'il and squeezed by the minister "the Samaritan," he died of grief and resentment and was buried in the turbah of his grandfather al-Malik al-Kamil {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 223}.
