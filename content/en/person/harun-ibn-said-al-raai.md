## Summary
Abu Abd al-Rahman Harun ibn Sa'id al-Ra'i was one of the ascetics and wanderers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 681, sequence 682, on page 398. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}

## Syria, his teachers and his transmitters
In Syria he met Abu Sulayman al-Darani, Muhammad ibn al-Mubarak al-Suri and Ahmad ibn Asim al-Antaki. Abu Mas'ud al-Razi transmitted from him in his musnad; Abu Mas'ud heard from Abd al-Rahman ibn Ibrahim ibn Duhaym, Muhammad ibn Abi al-Sari al-Maqlani and their generation. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}

## His reports and an exegesis
He relates from Yahya ibn Abi Khalid, from Ibn Abi Sa'id al-Ansari, from his father, a report that regret is repentance, and that one who repents of a sin is like one who has no sin; the wording is not given here. He also gives Ibn Abbas's explanation of the verse on not advancing before God and His Messenger: it means not to say anything contrary to the Book and the Sunna. The verse itself is not typed here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/398}
