## Summary
Qawam al-Din Abu Hanifa al-Itqani al-Farabi, a leader of the Hanafi school in his time, skilled in law and Arabic; born in Itqan, a town of Farab, and died in Cairo. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2053}

## Works
He held the judgeship of Baghdad, taught at the Zahiriyya house of hadith in Damascus, and wrote 'Ghayat al-bayan', a commentary on the Hidaya in twenty volumes, 'al-Tabyin' on the principles of the school, and a commentary on al-Bazdawi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2054}
