## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Kawwa al-Yashkuri, a man of Bakr ibn Wa'il. Al-Mas'udi says that the Harurites made him their leader in prayer when they withdrew to Harura, while Shabib ibn Rib'i was in command of their affair. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.685}

[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] debated with them, and they returned with him to Kufa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.685} No further details of him are available here; a second source is needed.
