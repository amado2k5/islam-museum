## Overview
Mahmud ibn Ghaylan al-Marwazi, Abu Ahmad al-Adawi by clientage, is described by al-Dhahabi as the imam, the memoriser and the proof, one of the leading men of the transmitted reports {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}. The signs printed with his entry show that al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Sufyan ibn Uyayna, al-Fadl ibn Musa, al-Walid ibn Muslim, Abu Mu'awiya, Waki', Yahya ibn Sulaym al-Ta'ifi, Abd al-Razzaq and their generation, narrating much and well, and al-Dhahabi calls him one of the horsemen of hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}. The compilers of the six books except Abu Dawud narrated from him, as did Abu Zur'a, Abu Hatim, Mutayyin, al-Hasan ibn Sufyan, al-Haytham ibn Khalaf, Abu al-Qasim al-Baghawi, Ibrahim ibn Abi Talib, Abu al-Abbas al-Sarraj, Ja'far ibn Ahmad ibn Nasr, Muhammad ibn Shadhan, Ibn Khuzayma and many others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}.

## What the critics said
Ahmad ibn Hanbal said he knew him for hadith, a man of the sunna, and that he had been imprisoned because of the Qur'an {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}. Al-Nasa'i said he was trustworthy {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}. Al-Dhahabi quotes Mahmud ibn Ghaylan as saying that Ishaq ibn Rahawayh heard two hadith from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}.

## His death
Al-Hakim reports through Abu Raja' Muhammad ibn Hamdawayh that Mahmud ibn Ghaylan set out for the pilgrimage in 246 AH, was sent back to Marw and died ten days before the end of Dhu al-Qa'da in 249 AH; al-Dhahabi holds that the correct date of his death is Ramadan 239 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3801}.
