## Overview
Sa'id ibn Abi Sura was with [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], and the narrator describes him as one of Yemen's counted horsemen. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.103} The book reports from him an account of the war at [[place/athafit|Athafit]]: he chose thirty horsemen who charged [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] as he stood in the main track, their hands stiffened on their lances and fear was cast in their hearts, and he pledged to God never to fight him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.103} The narrator adds that he went over to al-Hadi and served well in his rule. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.103} The account is attributed to its transmitter, and the museum neither affirms nor denies it. See [[event/al-hadi-stands-in-the-mahajja-at-athafit-285]].
