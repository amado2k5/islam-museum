## Summary
Khadija bint Nasr Allah al-Dimashqiyya, a pious woman of Syria, virtuous and beneficent {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 348}.

## Waqf and veneration
She endowed a house she had inherited from her father at the Hanbali congregational mosque in Damascus; people held a belief in her, especially the Rum, until the grand vizier Iyas Pasha sent dirhams from the Rum lands to restore her dwelling in the Dawudiyya zawiya {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 348-349}.

## Death
She died in Muharram 946 AH {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 349}.
