## Overview
Al-Mufaddal ibn al-Muhallab ibn Abi Safra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Khurasan
Al-Hajjaj appointed him over Khurasan after his brother Yazid. He conquered Badghis when it had broken its pact, conquered Shuman and others, and took spoils which he divided among the people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
