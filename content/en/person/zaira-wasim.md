Zaira Wasim (born 23 October 2000) is an Indian former actress, born at Srinagar into a Kashmiri Muslim family.{cite:wikipedia-zaira-wasim-en}{cite:wikidata-zaira-wasim} She made her debut as the young Geeta Phogat in Dangal (2016), the highest-grossing Indian film, winning the National Film Award for Best Supporting Actress, then starred as an aspiring singer in Secret Superstar (2017), which brought her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress.{cite:wikipedia-zaira-wasim-en} She was honoured with the National Child Award for Exceptional Achievement in 2017, and her last film was The Sky Is Pink (2019).{cite:wikipedia-zaira-wasim-en}

On 30 June 2019 she announced that she was leaving acting, publishing a statement that the work conflicted with her religious beliefs and faith — reported under her own words, "my relationship with my religion was threatened."{cite:wikipedia-zaira-wasim-en} In November 2020 she asked fans to take down her pictures from social media as she began a new chapter in her life.{cite:wikipedia-zaira-wasim-en}
