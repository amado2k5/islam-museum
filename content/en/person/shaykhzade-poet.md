## Overview
Abd al-Rahim ibn Ali ibn al-Mu'ayyad al-Amasi, known as Shaykhzade, was a Sufi scholar among the Hanafis of the Ottoman state; he died in 944 AH (1537 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.347}

He composed Nazm al-Fara'id wa-Jam' al-Fawa'id on forty issues between the Maturidis and the Ash'aris. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.347}
