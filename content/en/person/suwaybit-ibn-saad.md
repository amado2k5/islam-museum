## Summary

Suwaybit ibn Sa'd ibn Harmala, a Qurashi Companion of Banu Abd al-Dar whose mother Hunayda was of Khuza'a. He was among the emigrants to Abyssinia and witnessed Badr, and was a cheerful joker. In the famous jest he sold his companion Nu'ayman as a "slave" until Abu Bakr fetched him back — and the Prophet and his companions laughed at the tale for a year.
