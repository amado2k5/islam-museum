## Overview
Al-Mustajir bi'llah b. al-'Isa b. al-Muktafi bi'llah; in Sha'ban 349 word came that he had appeared in the region of Armenia and Muqan under the name al-Mustajir bi'llah, calling to al-Murtada of the Prophet's house — he donned wool and enjoined the right, a company followed him and he marched to Azerbaijan. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 687}
