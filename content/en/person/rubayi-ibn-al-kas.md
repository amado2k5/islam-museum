## Overview
Rab'i ibn al-Kas al-Anbari, appointed over Sistan by Ali, according to al-Baladhuri {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Sistan
Ali ordered Ibn Abbas to appoint him over Sistan, and he sent him out with four thousand men, with al-Husayn ibn Abi al-Hurr and Nayl ibn Dhi al-Hurra in the vanguard. Haska ibn Attab fought them and they killed him, and Rab'i secured the land {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

al-Baladhuri quotes a rajaz verse of their poet, in which they say that they do not befriend the Shi'a of Ibn Affan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
