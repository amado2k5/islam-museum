## Overview
Al-Dayzan ibn Mu'awiya, king of Tanukh, also called al-Dayzan ibn Jabhala, and Jabhala was his mother. He took the lands of al-Hadr after the kings of the Syriacs. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.552-553}

## The fall of al-Hadr
Al-Mas'udi describes him as having many soldiers, a truce-maker with the Romans and inclined to them, and says that his men raided Iraq and the Sawad. He took refuge in al-Hadr when Shapur came against him with troops, and his daughter al-Nadira showed the way to its capture. Shapur killed him and those with him and demolished the fortress. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.552-553}
