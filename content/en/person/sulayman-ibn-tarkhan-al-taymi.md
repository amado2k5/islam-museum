## Overview
Abu al-Mu'tamir Sulayman ibn Tarkhan al-Taymi, a traditionist of Basra. He narrated from the Companion Anas ibn Malik and a number of the Successors {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
He was born in 46 AH (666 CE) and died in 143 AH (760 CE), according to the encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## In al-Umari's al-Sira al-Nabawiyya al-Sahiha
[[person/akram-diya-al-umari|Akram Diya' al-Umari]] lists him among the pioneers of sira-writing: Sulayman ibn Tarkhan al-Taymi (d. 143 AH), "a trustworthy hadith scholar of the Successors", counted among the critics of narrators, author of a book *al-Sira al-Sahiha* lost except for a part; Ibn Hajar saw his sira. {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.56}
