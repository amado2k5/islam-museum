## Overview
Abu al-Faraj al-Mu'afa ibn Zakariyya ibn Yahya ibn Humayd al-Nahrawani, known as al-Jurayri and as Ibn Tarar, a scholar from Nahrawan. He wrote on language and hadith. He was born in 305 AH (917 CE) and died in 390 AH (1000 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
