## Overview
The hafiz Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah b. Ahmad al-Shantarini of Seville; he transmitted the Sahih through Ibn Mansur from Abu Dharr and heard Hatim b. Muhammad and a company; Ibn Bashkuwal said he was a hafiz of hadith and its 'ilal, versed in its men and in jarh and ta'dil — trustworthy, writing much, devoted to Abu 'Ali al-Ghassani, with works in rijal. He died in Safar 522 at seventy-eight. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 546}
