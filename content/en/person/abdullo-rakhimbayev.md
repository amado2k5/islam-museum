# Abdullo Rakhimbayev

Abdullo Rakhimbayev (1 June 1896 – 7 May 1938) was a Soviet politician of an Uzbek family from Khujand, born there in a trader's family on 1 June 1896 (19 May by the Old Style calendar). Educated at the Samarkand land school and the Tashkent teachers' seminary, in 1917 he was a member and one of the leaders of the Khujand organisation Shuro-i-Islamiya ("Council of Islam") and then secretary of the Khujand Council of Muslim Workers; he joined the Russian Communist Party in 1919.{cite:wikipedia-abdullo-rakhimbayev-en}{cite:wikipedia-abdullo-rakhimbayev-ru}

He chaired the executive committees of Khujand uezd and city, then the Central Executive Committee of the Turkestan ASSR in 1920–1921 and again in 1922, sat on the Collegium of the People's Commissariat for Nationalities, and after the national delimitation became responsible secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Uzbekistan (1923–1925). After study in Moscow he chaired the board of the Central Publishing House of the Peoples of the USSR and the Committee of Soviet National Minorities, and from December 1933 he chaired the Council of People's Commissars of the Tajik SSR, sitting also as one of the chairmen of the Central Executive Committee of the USSR.{cite:wikipedia-abdullo-rakhimbayev-ru}

Removed from the Tajik premiership in the summer of 1937 and expelled from the party, Rakhimbayev was arrested in Stalinabad in September 1937, taken to Moscow and held in Lefortovo Prison, and was shot there on 7 May 1938 by sentence of the Military Collegium; the English article gives the Kommunarka shooting ground as his resting place. He was rehabilitated in 1956.{cite:wikipedia-abdullo-rakhimbayev-en}{cite:wikipedia-abdullo-rakhimbayev-ru}{cite:wikidata-abdullo-rakhimbayev}
