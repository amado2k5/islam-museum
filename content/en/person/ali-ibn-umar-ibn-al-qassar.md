## Summary
Ali ibn Umar ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Hasan al-Baghdadi, known as Ibn al-Qassar, a Maliki jurist, usuli and hafiz. He studied with Abu Bakr al-Abhari and Ali ibn al-Fadl al-Suri; Abu Dharr al-Harawi, Abd al-Ghani al-Hafiz and Abd al-Wahhab taught under him. Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi called him reliable. His book on disputed questions is the largest in the school. The printed text gives no date of death here. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13357}
