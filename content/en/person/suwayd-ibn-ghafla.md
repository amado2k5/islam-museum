## Overview
Suwayd ibn Ghafla, a Successor who was a contemporary of [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]]. His report with him appears in al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.683}.

## Abu Musa's report
al-Mas'udi reports that Abu Musa told him, before Siffin, that this community would send forth two arbiters. Suwayd said to him: "If you live to see that time, beware of being one of the two arbiters" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.683}.

Abu Musa used to take off his shirt and say: "God has not made for me an ascent in heaven, nor a seat on earth." Then Suwayd met him again and reminded him of what he had said, and he said to him: "Ask your Lord for well-being" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.683}.
