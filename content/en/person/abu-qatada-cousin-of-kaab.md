## Summary
Ka'b ibn Malik's cousin and dearest person to him; he did not return his greeting in his garden during the boycott, answering after three adjurations: "God and His Messenger know best." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/178}

## In the account
Ka'b climbed his garden wall and greeted him; he did not reply. Ka'b adjured him by God whether he knew that he loved God and His Messenger — he stayed silent twice, then said the third time: God and His Messenger know best. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/178}
