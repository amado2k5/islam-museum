## Overview
Muhammad al-Aziz ibn Yusuf Ja'it, a Tunisian Sunni scholar. He was born in the city of Tunis in 1303 AH (1886 CE), was educated at the Zaytuna Mosque, and died in 1389 AH (1970 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
