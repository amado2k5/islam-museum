## Overview
Khatib-zada al-Rumi al-Qastamuni, a scholar. He was born in Kastamonu and is known by the title "Khatib-zada". The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* gives his death as 901 AH (1496 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
