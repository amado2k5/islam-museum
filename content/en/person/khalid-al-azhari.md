## Overview
Khalid ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr al-Azhari, an Egyptian grammarian and philologist. He took Arabic from the scholars of his age in Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Life and renown
Khalid b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Bakr al-Azhari, an Egyptian grammarian, was born at Jirja in Upper Egypt, whence the ethnic appellation al-Jirjawi is sometimes applied to him. He died at Cairo in 905 AH (1499). He enjoyed great renown in his time, and al-Suyuti is reckoned among his pupils. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 870-871}

## Works
His grammatical treatise is the Muqaddima al-Azhariyya fi 'ilm al-'arabiyya, printed at Bulaq (1252 AH) with his own commentary and in later editions with glosses by various schoolmen. He wrote a number of grammar manuals, a commentary on the commentary of Ibn Hisham on the Alfiyya of Ibn Malik, and commentaries on the Burda of al-Busiri and on the Jarrumiyya. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 870-871}
