## Summary
Karam Ibrahim Gaber is an Egyptian Greco-Roman wrestler, born in Alexandria on 1 September 1979. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-ar} {cite:wikidata-karam-gaber} He won the gold medal in the men's Greco-Roman 96 kg event at the Athens 2004 Olympics and a silver medal at London 2012. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en}

## Career
Gaber won silver at the 2003 World Championships. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} At Athens 2004 he won the final 12-1 against Ramaz Nozadze of Georgia, having beaten Mehmet Özal of Turkey 11-0 in the semi-finals; it was Egypt's second Greco-Roman wrestling gold, after 1928, and the Arabic article frames it as Egypt's first Olympic gold medal of any kind since the weightlifter Ibrahim Shams's title in 1948. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-ar} On 31 December 2004 he fought a mixed martial arts bout against Kazuyuki Fujita at K-1's Dynamite event. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} He was Egypt's flag bearer at the Beijing 2008 opening ceremony but did not reach the quarter-finals. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} At London 2012 he won his second Olympic medal, a silver. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} In 2015 he was given a two-year ban for an anti-doping rule violation relating to whereabouts failures, which was upheld on appeal. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en} In February 2024 he announced his retirement, an injury having kept him out of the African Olympic qualification tournament for Paris 2024. {cite:wikipedia-karam-gaber-en}
