## Overview
Husayn ibn Ali al-Marwarudhi, a Samanid commander. The amir Ahmad ibn Isma'il appointed him over the army marching to Sistan in 298 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.46-47}

## Sistan
He besieged al-Mu'addal ibn Ali ibn al-Layth until he made a settlement with him and gave him safe conduct. He took control of Sistan, and withdrew with al-Mu'addal to Bukhara. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.46-47}
