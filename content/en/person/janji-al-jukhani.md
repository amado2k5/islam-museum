## Overview
Janji al-Jukhani used to worship idols and strike the zanjalij in the house of the idol. He later abandoned that and turned to a doctrine he devised. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.475}

## His doctrine
Ibn al-Nadim says that his doctrine rested on the claim that something existed before the light and the darkness, and that in the darkness there were two forms, a male and a female. He called his followers the Janjiyya. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.475}
