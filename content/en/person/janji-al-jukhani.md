## Overview
Janji al-Jukhani, who worshipped idols and beat the zanjalij in the idol-temple, then left that creed for one of his own devising: that something existed before Light and Darkness, and that in Darkness are two forms, male and female — as Ibn al-Nadim reports. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 473}
