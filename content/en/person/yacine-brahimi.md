# Yacine Brahimi

## Summary

Yacine Nasreddine Brahimi is an Algerian professional footballer, born in Paris, France, on 8 February 1990 to Algerian parents and raised in Montreuil in the eastern suburbs. An attacking midfielder selected for the Clairefontaine academy, he played for Rennes, Granada and Porto before moving to Qatar, first with Al-Rayyan and then Al-Gharafa. For Algeria he has played more than seventy times, winning the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations and the 2021 FIFA Arab Cup. {cite:wikipedia-yacine-brahimi-en} {cite:wikipedia-yacine-brahimi-ar} {cite:wikidata-yacine-brahimi}

## Career

Brahimi passed through the Clairefontaine academy and signed for Rennes, turning professional in 2008 and impressing on loan at Clermont before a move to Spain's Granada in 2013 and then to Porto for €6.5 million a year later. At Porto he scored a Champions League hat-trick against BATE Borisov in September 2014 and spent five seasons as one of the side's creative players; in 2019 he moved to Qatar with Al-Rayyan, joining Al-Gharafa in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-yacine-brahimi-en}

A France youth international at every level, he switched to Algeria in February 2013 and debuted a month later, playing at the 2014 World Cup and four Africa Cup of Nations, winning the 2019 edition. {cite:wikipedia-yacine-brahimi-en}
