## Overview
Al-Mughira ibn al-Muhallab ibn Abi Safra, who died before his father. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
Al-Baladhuri says that the beginning of al-Muhallab's illness was grief over him. Al-Mughira died at Zaghul in Merv al-Rudh, at al-Shusha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
