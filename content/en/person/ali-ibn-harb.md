## Overview
'Ali ibn Harb ibn Muhammad ibn 'Ali ibn Hayyan ibn Mazin ibn al-Ghaduba, the trustworthy imam and muhaddith, man of letters and isnad-master of his time, Abu'l-Hasan al-Ta'i of Mosul; born in Azerbaijan in 175 AH and raised in Mosul. He heard Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna, Hafs ibn Ghiyath, 'Abdallah ibn Idris, Abu Mu'awiya, Waki' ibn al-Jarrah, Yahya ibn Yaman and others. He died in 265 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2753}
