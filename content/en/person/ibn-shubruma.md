## Overview
Abdallah ibn Shubruma, judge of Kufa. He was born in 72 AH and died in 144 AH, according to al-Shirazi in *Tabaqat al-Fuqaha'*. These two dates come from one source and need an additional source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## On the grant of land in Basra
*Futuh al-Buldan* relays a saying of his: "If I were governor of Basra, I would seize their wealth." His reason, as he gave it, was that Umar granted land there to no one except Abu Bakra and Nafi' ibn al-Harith; Uthman granted it only to Imran ibn Husayn; Ibn Amir granted his house, and Uthman's freedman Humran was granted land. Ziyad is said to have granted Imran a parcel. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The page of *Futuh al-Buldan* is not given in the data and needs an additional source.
