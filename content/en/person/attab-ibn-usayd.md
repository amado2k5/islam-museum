## Summary
Attab ibn Usayd ibn Abi al-Is ibn Umayya ibn Abd Shams, a young Umayyad Qurashi Companion; the Prophet made him his deputy over Mecca when he left it for [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn]] in Shawwal, year 8 AH, then confirmed him its governor on his return from [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 414, 422}.

## His appointment over Mecca
The Prophet left Mecca for Hunayn on the sixth of Shawwal, year 8 AH, appointing Attab ibn Usayd over Mecca though he was a youth of tender age {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 414}. The Prophet said of him: "what are a people doing appointing a youth like this?" — they said: Messenger of God, he recites — so he confirmed him. He led Mecca's people in prayer and led their hajj in the Prophet's lifetime {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 422}.

When the Prophet finished dividing the spoils and performed [[event/umrah-of-jirana|his umra from al-Ji'rana]], he departed for Medina having appointed Attab ibn Usayd over Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 422}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet chose a young man to be responsible for the great city of Mecca because he knew and had memorised the Qur'an — proving knowledge and trustworthiness matter more than age.
