## Overview
Sumayra al-Dubbiyya, a woman of the Dabba, of the Banu Mu'awiya ibn Ka'b ibn Tha'laba ibn Sa'd ibn Dabba, and one of the women who emigrated {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The tooth of Sumayra
al-Baladhuri reports that she had a tooth on the mountain, and so it was called "the tooth of Sumayra", after her name {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
