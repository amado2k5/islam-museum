## Summary
The jurist who led the embassy of Algiers to Sultan [[person/selim-i-ottoman|Selim I]] in 925/1519 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 212}.

## The embassy
He carried the letter written at [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din]]'s order in the name of the qadis, khatibs, ulema, imams, merchants, notables and people of Algiers, asking to bind the country to the Ottoman state. Al-Salabi calls him one of Algiers' greatest scholars and a military and political leader able to picture his country's conditions and perils {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 212}.

## The document
The letter is preserved in the Topkapı Palace archive under no. 4656 and was translated by Abd al-Jalil al-Tamimi {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 211}.
