## Overview
Tulayb ibn Umayr, known by the kunya Abu Adi, a Meccan of the Banu Abd Manaf. His mother was Arwa bint al-Muttalib, aunt of the Prophet {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.156}.

## Ajnadayn
He was killed as a martyr at the [[event/battle-of-ajnadayn|Battle of Ajnadayn]]. al-Baladhuri reports that a non-Arab challenged him and struck him with a blow that severed his right hand, so that his sword fell with his hand. Then he came upon him and killed him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.156-157}.

al-Baladhuri reports that this was on Monday, twelve nights before the end of Jumada al-Ula of the year 13 AH. The conversion to the Common Era is derived {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.156-157}.
