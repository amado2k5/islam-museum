## Overview
Muhammad ibn Musab al-Thaqafi. Muhammad ibn al-Qasim sent him to Sudusan with horses and asses. Its people asked for safe conduct, and the Samaniyya intervened on this. He granted them safe conduct, imposed a land tax on them, took hostages from them, and then left with four thousand of the Zutt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
