## Overview
[[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Shalil al-Bajali]], Abu Amr, of the Bajila. He embraced Islam in the year the Prophet died, lived in Kufa until the caliphate of Ali, and then moved to Qarqisiya. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## His beauty
He was at the peak of beauty and height, and Umar ibn al-Khattab called him "the Joseph of this community" for his beauty. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## Al-Qadisiyya
He challenged Mihran on the day of al-Qadisiyya and killed him. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## Dhu al-Khalasa
A tyrant of Dhu al-Khalasa asked him to build a house for the Khath'am called "the Yemeni Ka'ba". He went out to it and burned it, and the Prophet prayed for him to be steadfast and guided. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## The pledge
He pledged allegiance to the Prophet that he would give sincere counsel to every Muslim and would separate himself from the polytheist. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## Death
He died in the year fifty-six, and it is said fifty-four. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.625}

## For young visitors
Jarir ibn Abdallah al-Bajali was handsome and tall. He embraced Islam shortly before the Prophet died, burned a house that was worshipped in Yemen, and fought at al-Qadisiyya.

## Notes for researchers
- The text implies that Umar called him "Joseph of this community"; the statement is reported in summary, and its wording should be checked in the edition relied on.
- The account of Dhu al-Khalasa and the burning of the house is reported from Abu Nu'aym through his chains, and we have not yet checked it against a second source.

## For Explorers
Jarir ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Shalil al-Bajali, Abu Amr, of Bajila, entered Islam in the year the Prophet died, lived at Kufa until Ali's caliphate, then moved to Qarqisiya. He was the utmost in beauty and height — Umar called him 'the Yusuf of this nation'. At al-Qadisiyya he fought Mihran in single combat and brought him down. Tasked with the idol-house of Dhu al-Khalasa — 'the Yemeni Kaaba' of Khat'am — he rode to it and burned it, and the Prophet prayed for his steadfastness and guidance.
