## Overview

Ishaq ibn Ibrahim al-Warraq is one of the two transmitters of the choice of [[person/khalaf-ibn-hisham|Khalaf ibn Hisham]], with [[person/idris-ibn-abd-al-karim-al-haddad|Idris al-Haddad]]. He died in 286 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 202}

## His transmission

Ibn al-Jazari describes him as reliable, a firm upholder and exact master of the reading, singular in transmitting Khalaf's choice and known for no other. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 202} He quotes Ibn Abi 'Umar saying that he read Khalaf's choice to Ishaq, who knew no other, and that when Ishaq's hearing failed his son Muhammad took his place; Ibn al-Jazari corrects a date in some books by saying that the one who died in 286 was Ishaq himself. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 202} He counts twenty-two lines of transmission from Ishaq, and notes that a claim in some books that al-Birsati read to Ishaq's brother Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Marwazi al-Warraq is a mistake, since Ahmad died too early for al-Birsati to meet him. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 200}
