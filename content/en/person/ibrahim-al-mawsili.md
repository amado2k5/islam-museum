## Overview
Ibrahim al-Mawsili, a singer. Al-Mas'udi relates from him the account of Miskin al-Madani, Abu Sadaqa, when [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]] gathered the singers. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.348}

## The gathering
Miskin sang a melody which the chiefs had fallen short of rendering as the caliph wished, and the curtain was raised between him and al-Rashid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.348}

## Learning singing
Ibn al-Nadim relates that it is said that he took singing from Yunus the secretary. The report is given with "it is said", and needs an additional source. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.447}
