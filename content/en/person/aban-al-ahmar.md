## Overview
Aban ibn Uthman ibn Yahya ibn Zakariyya al-Lu'lu'i al-Bajali, by clientship, Abu Abdallah, known as al-Ahmar, was a scholar of reports and genealogies. Al-Zirikli describes him as Imami, that is a Twelver Shi'i; what follows should be read within that frame. The entry dates his death to about 200 AH, about 815 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## Where he lived and who learned from him
He was of Kufan origin and lived at times in Kufa and at times in Basra. Among those who took from him were Abu Ubayda Mamar ibn al-Muthanna and Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Sallam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Maghazi, on the reports of the beginning and the mission, the expeditions of the Messenger, the Saqifa and the apostasy wars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.
