## Overview
Muhammad ibn Khayr ibn Umar ibn Khalifa al-Ishbili, an Andalusian scholar from Seville, client of Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ya'mur. He was a hadith scholar and the author of a well-known index of transmissions. He was born in 502 AH (1108 CE) and died in 575 AH (1179 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
