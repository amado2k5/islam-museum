## Summary
Abu Said ibn al-Mu'alla al-Ansari of Medina, a Companion. His name is given as Rafi ibn Aws, or al-Harith ibn Aws. Hafs ibn Asim and Ubayd ibn Hunayn narrated from him; al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah record him. Death 73 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13345}
