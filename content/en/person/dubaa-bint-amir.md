## Overview
[[person/dubaa-bint-amir|Dubaa bint Amir al-Qushayriyya]] of the Banu Qushayr. She became Muslim after marrying Hisham ibn al-Mughira and bearing him her son Salama, and she was among the women who embraced Islam with the Prophet in Mecca. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.300}.

## Her first marriage
Abd Allah ibn Jud'an set hard conditions for her if she wished to marry Hisham ibn al-Mughira. She asked for time to think, then accepted, and her first marriage ended in divorce. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.298}

## Her husband Hisham and her son
She married Hisham ibn al-Mughira and bore him her son Salama. Hisham died before she became Muslim. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.300}

## Ukaz
The book Women Around the Messenger reports, from elders of her people, that the Prophet came to them while they were at Ukaz and invited them to support him. They refused. A man of the Banu Qushayr called Bajir ibn Faras then struck the Prophet's camel, which reared and threw him. Dubaa hastened to her cousins to rouse them, and each of them seized a man, threw him to the ground, sat on his chest and struck his face. The Prophet prayed for blessing upon them. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.300}, {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.301}.

## Emigration
She never left Mecca for another place until the emigration to Yathrib, when she left with her son Salama. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p.301}

## Notes for researchers
- The events before her conversion (the marriage conditions and the circumambulation) come from Women Around the Messenger; the pre-Islamic details are not repeated here, and only the events the biography needs are kept.
- Women Around the Messenger is used here for events and references only.
- The book's references to Ibn Hisham (the Sira, 164/1) and to the sources of al-Isaba and Usd al-Ghaba have not yet been read in local sources.
- Her birth and death dates are not given in the pages read.
