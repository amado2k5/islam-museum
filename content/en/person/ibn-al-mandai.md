## Overview
Abu'l-Fath Muhammad b. Abi'l-'Abbas Ahmad b. Bakhtiyar b. 'Ali b. Muhammad b. Ibrahim b. Ja'far al-Wasiti, known as Ibn al-Manda'i; a company of notables took from him, among them the hafiz Abu Bakr al-Hazimi, and he transmitted al-Hariri's Malhat al-I'rab {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
