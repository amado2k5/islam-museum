## Overview
Jamal al-Din Ibn Matruh, the katib and poet; born at Asyut in 592, he entered the service of al-Malik al-Kamil then of his son al-Salih, who made him overseer of the treasury; he was in the office of vizier for Damascus in 643, then al-Salih turned on him and he stayed in his house until he died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 255-257}

## His verse
Ibn Khallikan said, "between us were letters and firm affection"; he has a diwan, and between him and Baha' Zuhayr was an old love from boyhood until they were like brothers. He bequeathed that his two verses be written at his head: "At the bottom of my pit I am now held in pawn — of my world I possess nothing but a shroud." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 255-257}
