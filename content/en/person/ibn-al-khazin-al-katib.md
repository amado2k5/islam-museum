## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Ahmad b. Muhammad b. al-Fadl b. 'Abd al-Khaliq, known as Ibn al-Khazin, the katib-poet — Dinawari by origin, Baghdadi by birth and death; an excellent man, a marvel of the pen, singular in his age for it, and father of Abu'l-Fath Nasr Allah the famed katib; he copied out many Maqamat that remained in people's hands. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
