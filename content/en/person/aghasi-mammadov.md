# Aghasi Mammadov

## Summary
Aghasi Aghagul oghlu Mammadov (born 1980) is an Azerbaijani bantamweight boxer who represented two countries at the Olympics, Turkey and then Azerbaijan. He was born in Baku on 1 June 1980. {cite:wikipedia-aghasi-mammadov-en} {cite:wikipedia-aghasi-mammadov-ar} {cite:wikidata-aghasi-mammadov}

## Career
He competed for Turkey for several years under the name Aghasi Aghaguloghlu, winning the European title at Tampere in 2000 and the Mediterranean Games gold in 2001, taking world silver at Belfast in 2001, and boxing at the Sydney 2000 Olympics, where he lost to Cuba's Guillermo Rigondeaux. He returned to Azerbaijan before the 2002 European Championships. {cite:wikipedia-aghasi-mammadov-en}

At the 2003 World Championships in Bangkok he won the world bantamweight title, beating Rigondeaux himself in the second round and Russia's Gennady Kovalev in the final. He qualified for the Athens 2004 Olympics, defeated Joel Brunker, Detelin Dalakliev and Maksym Tretyak, and lost the semi-final to Thailand's Worapoj Petchkoom to take the bronze medal. {cite:wikipedia-aghasi-mammadov-en}
