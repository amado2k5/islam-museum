## Overview
A Companion and poet of Banu Ya'mur. Ibn Ishaq mentions him in two places in the fourth volume. At [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta]] he composed verses apologising for what happened that day and praising Khalid ibn al-Walid {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/25}. In the [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-banu-fazara-and-umm-qirfa|second raid of Zayd ibn Haritha on Banu Fazara]], [[person/masada-ibn-hakama|Mas'ada ibn Hakama]] was killed, and the poet composed verses about it. Zayd ordered [[person/umm-qirfa|Umm Qirfa]] killed, and she was killed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/265} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}.

## Notes on the report
- The name of his father is given as "al-Musahhar" in the edition, and in some manuscripts otherwise, as the editors point out. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}
- His verses on the killing of Mas'ada are rendered by meaning, not by text.
