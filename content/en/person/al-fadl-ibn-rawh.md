## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn Rawh, the son of [[person/rawh-ibn-hatim|Rawh ibn Hatim]]. Al-Baladhuri says that he governed Ifriqiya after his father. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His end
The soldiers attacked him and slaughtered him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His story is linked with [[event/yazid-ibn-hatim-ifriqiya|the report of Yazid ibn Hatim in Ifriqiya]].

The date of his governorship and his killing need an additional source.
