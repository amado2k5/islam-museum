## Summary
Father of Qusayy and Zuhra; he died while Qusayy was an infant, so his mother Fatima bint Sa'd took him to Yemen where he grew up until he returned to Mecca as a young man. From Zuhra ibn Kilab come Banu Zuhra — the clan of Amina bint Wahb the Prophet's mother, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48-49} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:isaba}.

## His place in the lineage
Kilab's progeny split into two houses: the house of Qusayy holding the Prophet's line and the Umayyads, and the house of Zuhra holding the Prophet's mother and great Companions {cite:isaba}.

## For younger visitors
Kilab ibn Murra is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
