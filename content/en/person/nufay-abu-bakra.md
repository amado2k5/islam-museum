## Overview
Nufay, Abu Bakra, one of the two "Nafi'un" named by Abu al-Mukhtar in his poem on the governors of al-Ahwaz, together with his brother Nafi ibn al-Harith ibn Kalada {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
