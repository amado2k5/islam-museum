## Overview
Isma'il ibn Qabiha, a full brother of [[person/al-mutazz|al-Mu'tazz]] on the father's and the mother's side. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Mu'tazz placed him in line for the succession in place of his brother [[person/al-muayyad|al-Mu'ayyad]], after al-Mu'ayyad had been taken out of his prison dead. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.551}
