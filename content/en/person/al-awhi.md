## Overview
Al-Awhi — fathas both — of Awah, a village between Zanjan and Hamadhan; the ascetic Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. Ahmad b. Yusuf, resident of Jerusalem, transmitted abundantly from al-Silafi and a company — a righteous, devout servant, a man of states and struggles, with ajza' he related. He died on 10 Safar 630. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 136}
