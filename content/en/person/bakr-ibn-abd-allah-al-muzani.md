## Overview
Bakr ibn 'Abdallah ibn 'Amr, the exemplary imam and preacher Abu 'Abdallah al-Muzani of Basra, one of the luminaries, mentioned alongside al-Hasan and Ibn Sirin; he narrated from al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn 'Umar, Anas ibn Malik, Abu Rafi' al-Sa'igh and others. He died in 108 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1228}
