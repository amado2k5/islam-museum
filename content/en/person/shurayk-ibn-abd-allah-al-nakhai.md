## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Shurayk ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Shurayk al-Nakha'i, a jurist and scholar. He was born in Bukhara in the year 95 AH, died in Kufa in the year 177 AH, and held the judgeship first in Kufa and then in Ahwaz {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.46}.

al-Shirazi quotes Ibn Uyayna: "I did not meet anyone in Kufa with a readier answer than Shurayk" {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.46}.
