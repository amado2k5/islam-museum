## Summary
'Ali ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Ibrahim ibn Bayan, Abu'l-Hasan; born 340; he was katib for al-Ta'i' then for al-Qadir from Shawwal 386 — writing for two caliphs for forty years; his father had served in Abu 'Umar al-Mahalla's vizierate; he was eloquent. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.393-394}

## His death and successor
He died in Rajab 421 and was buried at Barakat Zalzal, then moved to the Quraysh cemeteries in 425; his son Abu'l-Fadl succeeded him in the writing office eight months, then was dismissed for want of substance. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.393-394}
