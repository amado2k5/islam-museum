## Overview
Amr ibn Hind al-Lakhmi, lord of [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]], of the [[group/banu-lakhm|Banu Lakhm]], and his title was "the stone-breaker". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}

Ibn al-Athir says the title was for the severity of his rule and the strength of his policy. His mother was Hind daughter of al-Harith ibn Amr al-Maqsur, the paternal aunt of the poet Imru' al-Qays. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}

Al-Mundhir appointed him over Bakr and Taghlib in the raid of the Banu Akil al-Murar, and he was eager to humiliate the nobles of the Arabs, until he treacherously dealt with the mother of [[person/amr-ibn-kulthum|Amr ibn Kulthum]], Layla daughter of al-Muhalhil, and Ibn Kulthum rushed at his sword and killed him in the tent. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.409}
