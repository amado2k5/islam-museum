## Summary
[[person/rayhana-bint-zayd|Rayhana]] bint Sham'un ibn Zayd — or it is said Zayd ibn Amr ibn Khanafa — of Banu al-Nadir per Ibn Sa'd, of Banu Amr ibn Qurayza per Ibn Ishaq. She had been married to a man of Banu Qurayza called al-Hakam {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1877}. It is also said her name was "Rabija" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1878}.

## Her death
She died sixteen months before the Messenger of God's death — or it is said, when he returned from the Farewell Pilgrimage {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1877}. In Ibn Sa'd's account from al-Waqidi she died on his return from the hajj and he buried her at al-Baqi' {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1878}.

## What is related of his praying in her house
Ibn Hajar cites Muhammad ibn al-Hasan in the reports of Medina: the Messenger of God prayed in a lodging in the house of Qays ibn Qahd which Rayhana al-Quraziyya, the Prophet's wife, dwelt in {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1878}.

## Did Ibn Manda give her her own entry?
Abu Musa said Ibn Manda mentioned her within Maria's entry without giving her one of her own; Ibn Hajar answers: "rather, he did give her one" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1878}.
