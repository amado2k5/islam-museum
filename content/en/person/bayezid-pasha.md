## Summary
Bayezid Pasha, first vizier of Mehmed I — al-Sallabi credits him with commanding the second army against Börklüce Mustafa and defeating him at Qara Burnu, after Börklüce had killed the commander Shishman and routed the first {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 75}.

On the sultan's deathbed in 824/1421 Mehmed charged him with the succession of Murad; Bayezid and the vizier Ibrahim concealed the sultan's death for forty-one days until the heir arrived and took the reins {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 77-78}.
