## Overview
Abu Hammu Musa ibn Uthman ibn Yaghmurasan, sultan of Tlemcen, the fourth of the kings of the Banu Abd al-Wad and the successor of [[person/abu-ziyan-muhammad-ibn-uthman|Abu Ziyan Muhammad ibn Uthman]] in the [[polity/zayyanid-tlemcen|Zayyanid state of Tlemcen]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The dispute with his son
Ibn Khaldun relates that he drew his cousin Mas'ud ibn Abi Amir close to his private hours. He gave his son Abd al-Rahman Abu Tashfin companions from the Mu'alluj to serve him. He often blamed and reproached him for his cousin's standing, so the Mu'alluj sensed his jealousy and urged him to seize the affair. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His killing
The Mu'alluj waited for the time of the noon rest, when the sultan left his session, and they attacked with their attendants the close men of the palace and killed him with swords. They killed with him Mas'ud ibn Abi Amir and Abu Sarhan, and the viziers of the Banu al-Mallah, chamberlains of the palace, perished. A herald called out in Tlemcen that Abu Sarhan had betrayed the sultan and that his son Abu Tashfin had avenged him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Date of death
Estimated at around 718 AH (1318 CE), an estimate based on the common accounts; the conversion is derived. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
