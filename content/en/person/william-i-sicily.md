## Summary
Ghulaym (William) ibn Rujar (Roger). When Roger, lord of Sicily, died in the last ten days of Dhu'l-Hijja, his son William ruled after him; to him came the poet Abu'l-Futuh Nasr Allah ibn Qalaqis, who praised him and was rewarded by him — in the year 563. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
