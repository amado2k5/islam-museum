## Summary
Commander of Badis under Hasan b. Khayr al-Din, rightly styled lord of the Strait of Gibraltar {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 247-251}.

## Lord of the strait
Appointed in 965/1558, he ravaged the Spanish coast from Cartagena to Cape St Vincent; the Spanish report of Francois de Ibanier credits him with four warships in the strait, two at Badis and a third building — no ship could pass without his leave and fear spread along the coast, until Hasan deposed him in late 971/1563 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 247-251}.
