## Overview
Baghir al-Turki, one of the Turks who murdered al-Mutawakkil. Al-Suyuti says that Wasif and Bugha killed al-Mutawakkil in 251 AH, and the Turks turned against al-Musta'in, and he feared and went down from Samarra to Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.555}

Al-Mas'udi says that Bugha al-Saghir employed him and tested him in the killing of al-Mutawakkil, so he went in to him with ten men with masked faces and struck him with the sword. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.503-506}
