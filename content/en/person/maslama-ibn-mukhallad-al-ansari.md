## Overview
Maslama ibn Mukhallad al-Ansari, a governor among the governors of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Maghrib
Mu'awiya removed Mu'awiya ibn Hudayj and appointed him over Egypt and the Maghrib. He appointed over the Maghrib his client Abu al-Muhajir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
