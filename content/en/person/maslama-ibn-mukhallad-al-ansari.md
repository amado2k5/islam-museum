## Overview
Maslama ibn Mukhallad al-Ansari, a governor among the governors of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Maghrib
Mu'awiya removed Mu'awiya ibn Hudayj and appointed him over Egypt and the Maghrib. He appointed over the Maghrib his client Abu al-Muhajir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

This connects to [[event/founding-of-qayrawan|the founding of Qayrawan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
At the siege of Alexandria he is said to have been the one who advised Amr to give command of the fighting to [[person/ubada-ibn-al-samit|Ubada ibn al-Samit]]. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 116} He laid out [[building/dar-al-raml-fustat|Dar al-Raml]] with Abu Rafi' and Uqba ibn Amir and later gave it to Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 143} He is reported to have been born when the Prophet came to Medina, to have governed Egypt for Mu'awiya and early in Yazid's reign, and to have died in Egypt in the year 62, leaving no son, so that his daughter Umm Sahl, after whom Munyat Umm Sahl is named, inherited. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 136} {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 143} During his governorship six hundred thousand dinars beyond the soldiers' stipends were sent to Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 145}
