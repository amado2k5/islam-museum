## Overview
Sadr al-Din Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Hamuwayh al-Juwayni, Shaykh al-Shuyukh in Egypt and Syria. He was a jurist and Sufi from a great house of Khurasan {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.399}.

## His death
He died in Mosul in the year 617 AH (1220 CE), having arrived there as an envoy. Ibn al-Athir says: "He was an excellent man" {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.399}.
