## Overview
al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Ishaq al-Tusi, Qawam al-Din, known as Nizam al-Mulk. He was vizier to Alp Arslan and then to his son Malikshah {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.645} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.381-382}.

## The vizierate
He reversed what his predecessor, Amid al-Mulk, had done by cursing the Ash'aris. He championed the Shafi'is, honoured the imam al-Haramayn and Abu al-Qasim al-Qushayri, and built the Nizamiyya. It is said to be the first school built for jurists {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.645}.

Ibn al-Sam'ani said: "The Ka'ba of glory and the follower of generosity." Dawud ibn Mikal recommended him to his son Alp Arslan. When Alp Arslan died, he secured the realm for Malikshah, until the whole affair came to rest with Nizam al-Mulk, and he remained so for twenty years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.381-382}.

## His death
He was killed by a young man with the appearance of a Sufi from the Batiniyya on the tenth night of Ramadan 485 AH. The man handed him a petition, then struck him in the chest with a knife. It is said that Malikshah had arranged it, but this report is not one we can affirm {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.381-382}.
