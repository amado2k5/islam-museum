## Summary
A Companion of Banu Jumah; he emigrated to Abyssinia and came in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him alone of Banu Jumah among those in the two ships, calling him "Uthman ibn Rabi'a ibn Ahban". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
