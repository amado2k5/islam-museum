# Youssef Msakni

## Summary

Youssef Msakni is a former Tunisian professional footballer, born in Tunis on 28 October 1990. A winger, he spent a decade in Qatari football after his years at Espérance de Tunis, and won 104 caps for Tunisia. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-msakni-en} {cite:wikipedia-youssef-msakni-ar} {cite:wikidata-youssef-msakni}

## Career

Msakni came through Stade Tunisien and joined Espérance de Tunis in 2008, where he became one of the Tunisian league's leading scorers. In January 2013 he moved to Qatar, playing for Al Duhail until 2023 — with loans to Eupen in Belgium and Al Arabi — then joined Al Arabi permanently until 2025, and returned to Espérance for a final spell. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-msakni-en} {cite:wikipedia-youssef-msakni-ar}

For Tunisia he played from 2010 to 2024, winning 104 caps and scoring 23 goals, appearing at Africa Cup of Nations tournaments from 2010 onward. {cite:wikipedia-youssef-msakni-en}
