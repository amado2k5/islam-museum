## Overview
Muhammad ibn Najm al-Din ibn Muhammad al-Salihi al-Hilali was a poet and one of the secretaries, a man of Damascus. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 956 AH, 1549 CE, and his death as 1012 AH, 1603 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 123}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists his Saj' al-Hamam fi Madh Khayr al-Anam, a printed diwan of poems in praise of the Prophet; Safinat al-Salihi, in manuscript, a collection of literature, lectures and biographies; and Sawanih al-Afkar wa-l-Qara'ih fi Ghurar al-Ash'ar wa-l-Madaih, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 123}.
