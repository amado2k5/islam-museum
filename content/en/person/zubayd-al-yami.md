## Overview
Zubayd ibn al-Harith al-Yami, Abu Abd al-Rahman, a hadith transmitter called reliable by Ibn Ma'in, Abu Hatim and al-Nasa'i. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3487}

## Transmission
He narrated from Murra ibn Sharahil, Sa'd ibn Ubayda and Abd al-Rahman Abi Layla. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3487}
