## Overview
"Muhammad ibn Sa'id" appears on p. 108 as a companion and adviser of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]: after the plunder of [[place/athafit|Athafit]] al-Hadi told him he had resolved to withdraw and speak to no one. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.108} He and [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] kept coaxing him and told him Islam would perish if he did so, then advised him to send for the people and ask them to repent, since they were ignorant bedouins. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.108} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109}

The book's index gathers "Muhammad ibn Sa'id ibn Yusuf" at several places, among them pp. 78, 80 and 108, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.455} and the text on p. 80 calls a Muhammad ibn Sa'id "al-Yarsami"; the museum keeps the name as the index gives it and does not assert more. The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers. See [[event/al-daam-leaves-athafit-for-khiwan-and-the-plunder-of-athafit-285]].
