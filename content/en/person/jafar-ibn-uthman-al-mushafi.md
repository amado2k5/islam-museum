## Overview
Ja'far ibn Uthman al-Mushafi, chamberlain of al-Hakam al-Mustansir. Al-Maqqari says that he helped Ibn Abi Amir kill al-Mughira on the night of the pledge to Hisham al-Mu'ayyad. Then Ibn Abi Amir moved him to expel the Slav servants from the palace, who numbered eight hundred or more. At the end of his career Ibn Abi Amir, with the help of Ghalib, brought him down and erased his trace from the state. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 433}

The dates of his birth and death are not recorded in the data and need an additional source.
