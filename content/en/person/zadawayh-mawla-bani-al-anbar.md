## Overview
Zadawayh, client of the Banu al-Anbar, the third conspirator with Ibn Muljam and al-Baraq. They agreed in Mecca to kill three of the amirs {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 701}.

## The choice
Zadawayh chose [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], but he missed him, because Amr had stayed away from the prayer. In another report the third was Amr ibn Bakr {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 701} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 295}.
