## Overview
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Salama b. Ja'far al-Quda'i, the Shafi'i jurist; Ibn 'Asakir mentions him in Tarikh Dimashq — he held Egypt's judgeship as deputy for al-Zahir b. al-Hakim, lord of Egypt, and journeyed as envoy toward Byzantium; among his works: the Kitab al-shihab on judgeship, Manaqib al-imam al-Shafi'i wa-akhbaruh, al-Inba' 'an al-anba', Tawarikh al-khulafa' and Khitat Misr; Ibn Makula mentions him in the Ikmal: he was versatile in several sciences. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
