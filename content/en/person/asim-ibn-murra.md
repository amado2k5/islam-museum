## Overview
Asim ibn Murra, son of Murra ibn Ali al-Rudhini. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sisar
Al-Baladhuri says that Sisar was taken from him, and it was removed from his hand in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun, and returned to the estates of the caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
