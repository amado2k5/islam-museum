## Overview
Hammad ibn Zayd ibn Dirham al-Azdi al-Jahdami, a hadith scholar of Basra. The encyclopaedia "Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama wa al-Udaba" describes him as among the most pious and most just of the memorisers. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.500}.

## Memorisation
The encyclopaedia states that he memorised four thousand hadiths, and that he was blind. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.500}.

## His dates
According to the encyclopaedia, he was born in 98 AH (717 CE) and died in 179 AH (795 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.500}.
