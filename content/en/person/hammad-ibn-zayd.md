## In brief
Hammad b. Zayd b. Dirham, the imam and memoriser, generous shaykh of Iraq, Abu Isma'il al-Azdi their client of Basra, the blue-eyed blind man; his grandfather Dirham was among the captives of Sijistan of the clients of the family of Jarir b. Hazim. He narrated from Abu Imran al-Jawni, Muhammad b. Ziyad, Abu Qurra al-Dab'i, Anas b. Sirin, Amr b. Dinar, Thabit al-Bunani, and others — he did not reach Qatada. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.215}

## His standing
Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi, Musaddad, Muhammad b. Abi Bakr al-Muqaddimi, Ali b. al-Madini, Ahmad b. Hanbal, and hosts besides narrated from him. Ibn Mahdi said "the imams of the people in their time are four: al-Thawri, Malik, al-Awza'i, and Hammad b. Zayd", Yahya b. Ma'in said "no one is more reliable than Hammad b. Zayd", Ahmad said "he is among the imams of the Muslims of the people of religion, and dearer to me than Hammad b. Salama", Ibn Mahdi said "I never saw anyone more learned than him — not Malik, not Sufyan — nor anyone in Basra of deeper fiqh", Yazid b. Zuray' called him "chief of the Muslims"; blind, he kept all his hadith memorised with no book — it was said he held four thousand hadiths. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.215-216}

## His death
Born in 98 AH, he died in Ramadan of 179 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.216}
