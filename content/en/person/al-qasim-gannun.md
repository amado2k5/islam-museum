## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim ibn Idris, nicknamed Gannun, was one of the last Idrisid amirs of their second state in the countryside of Morocco; he died in 337 AH (948 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.181}

His seat was the fortress of Hajar al-Nasr; he took over the lands of the far Maghrib save the city of Fez, which held out against him, and his da'wa was for the Ubaydids (Fatimids). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.181}
