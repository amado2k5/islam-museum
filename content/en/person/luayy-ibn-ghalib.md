## Summary
Father of Ka'b, Amir, Sama and Awf; from Ka'b descend Quraysh of the hollows and from his brothers Quraysh of the outskirts, including Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy — the clan of Suhayl ibn Amr {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## His place in the lineage
Lu'ayy ibn Ghalib was among the chiefs of Quraysh, a man of stern resolve, who fought for the sanctuary of Mecca when Arab kings meant to drive Quraysh out {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## For younger visitors
Lu'ayy ibn Ghalib is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
