## Overview
Abd al-Wahid ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahid, Abu Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Dallaj, was a Maghribi physician who died in 1099 AH (1688 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.177}

He wrote books, among them Zubdat al-Minha on the sciences of cure and health, al-Rawd al-Ma'nus on the antidote, 'Aqd al-Juman on the duties of a hospital superintendent, and Tuhfat al-Talib on the rules of striking practice. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.177}
