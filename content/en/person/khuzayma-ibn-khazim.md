## Overview
One of the Abbasid governors of Armenia, who came after Ruh ibn Hatim al-Muhallabi and before Yazid ibn Mazyad al-Shaybani. Al-Baladhuri describes him as the most severe of them in governance. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.296}

## The survey
He is the one who instituted the land survey in Dabil and al-Nashwa, which had not been done before him. See [[event/khuzayma-land-survey|the survey of Khuzayma]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.296}
