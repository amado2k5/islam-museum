## Overview
Hubaysh ibn al-Hasan, one of the translators whom the Banu al-Munajjim supported. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.340}

Ibn al-Nadim reports that they used to give him, together with [[person/hunayn-ibn-ishaq|Hunayn ibn Ishaq]] and Thabit ibn Qurra, about five hundred dinars a month, for transcription and for constant attendance. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.340}
