## Overview
Hubaysh ibn al-Hasan, a translator whom the Banu'l-Munajjim paid together with Hunayn ibn Ishaq and Thabit ibn Qurra about five hundred dinars a month for translating and constant service. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 340}
