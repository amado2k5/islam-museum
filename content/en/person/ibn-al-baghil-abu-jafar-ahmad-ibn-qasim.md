## Overview
Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Qasim ibn Abdallah ibn Ibrahim al-Makhuwlani al-Judhami, known as Ibn al-Baghil. A jurist, philologist and historian from Almería in al-Andalus, whose origin was in Murcia. He died in 750 AH (1349 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
