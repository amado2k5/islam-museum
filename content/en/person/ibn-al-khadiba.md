## Summary
Abu Bakr Ibn al-Khadiba Muhammad b. Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Baqi al-Baghdadi, the hafiz, benefactor of Baghdad; he transmitted from Abu Bakr al-Khatib and Ibn al-Muslima and their tier, and journeyed to Syria — of high rank, a critic and learned man, loved by all for his piety, humility and haste in serving their needs, with beautiful recitation. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 393}

## The critics on him
Ibn Tahir said: none in the world recited hadith better than he; al-Fasihi said: I have seen no muhaddith stronger in lugha than Ibn al-Khadiba. He died in Rabi' I 489. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 393}
