## Overview
Al-Mufarraj ibn Sallam, written in one copy "al-Mufarrakh", who rose in the great land after Khalfun the Berber. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328}

## The conquest and the death
Al-Baladhuri says that he took twenty-four fortresses and seized them. He wrote to the postal officer in Egypt asking that the imam confirm his governorship over his district, so that he might leave the limits of the usurpers. He built a congregational mosque, then his companions rioted against him and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328} See [[event/al-mufarraj-fortresses|the fortresses of al-Mufarraj]].
