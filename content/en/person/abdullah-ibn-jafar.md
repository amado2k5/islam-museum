## Summary
Son of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib and Asma' bint Umays, born in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him with Ja'far and his wife Asma' among the Banu Hashim emigrants, and says Asma' bore him in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3} He names him again under "their sons in Abyssinia", first of the five men born there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-hashim|Banu Hashim]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}
