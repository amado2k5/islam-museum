## Overview
Shurayh ibn Hafi' al-Muradi. al-Baladhuri reports that he went to [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] on behalf of Kathir ibn Shihab al-Harithi, when Mu'awiya had imprisoned him in Damascus and beaten him, and he secured his release {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
