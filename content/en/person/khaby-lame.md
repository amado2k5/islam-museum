## Summary
Khaby Lame — born Khabane Lame in Senegal in 2000 and raised in Chivasso, Italy — is a content creator who became the most-followed person on TikTok in the world; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-khaby-lame} {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}

## Rise
Laid off from a factory job during the 2020 pandemic, he began posting TikTok videos reacting wordlessly to absurdly complicated 'life hack' clips — deadpan expressions and the signature open-palm gesture — growing to over 160 million followers, the platform's largest account. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}

## Style
His wordless comedy needs no translation: a global vernacular of exasperated common sense that made him famous simultaneously in Africa, Europe, the Arab world and the Americas. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}

## Career
He has signed global brand partnerships, attended major fashion and film events as a guest of luxury houses, voiced a character in a Black Panther film-related project and appeared in Hollywood productions, while retaining his signature video format. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}

## Assessment
He is credited with proving that silent, universally legible comedy could beat language-bound content at the largest scale in social media; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame} {cite:muslim500-khaby-lame}


## Silent format
His reaction videos — wordless, deadpan demonstrations cutting through over-complicated 'life hack' clips — created a universal-language comedy format; analysts credit their accessibility with the unprecedented speed of his rise. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}

## Scale
Since becoming the most-followed TikTok account in 2022 he has crossed a hundred and fifty million followers, alongside brand partnerships, fashion campaigns and screen roles that made him the platform's defining global figure. {cite:wikipedia-khaby-lame}
