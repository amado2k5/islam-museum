## Overview
Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri, a scholar of Medina. He was with [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]] at Marj Dabiq when death came to him. He wrote his testament and had it witnessed by Raja' ibn Haywa and Makhul. When he was buried, al-Zuhri rose and asked the people: "Are you content with the one whom the Commander of the Faithful named in his testament?" Then he read the document, in which were the name of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] and after him Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.172-173}

Al-Salabi says that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ordered him to collect the Sunan, and he wrote them out, volume by volume, and sent them to every land that had a ruler. He relates a saying of Umar: "Hold to Ibn Shihab, for no one remains who knows a past Sunna better than he." {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.298-300}

Al-Mas'udi says that he died in 123 AH; al-Waqidi mentions that he died in 124 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.192}
