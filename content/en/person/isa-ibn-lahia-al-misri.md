## Overview
Isa ibn Lahia al-Misri, author of *al-Halaʾib wa al-Jalaʾib* (The Races and the Horses). Al-Mas'udi says that in it he recorded every race in which horses ran in the pre-Islamic and Islamic periods. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 529}

## The story of Zad al-Rakib
He transmitted that Solomon son of David provided a horse to some men of the Azd so that they could hunt on it, and it was called "Zad al-Rakib" (the rider's provision). {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 529}
