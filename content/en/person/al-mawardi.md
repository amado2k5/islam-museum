## Overview
The imam Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Mawardi al-Basri al-Shafi'i, who died in 450 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi} He was one of the leading Shafi'i jurists.

## Judgeship and writing
He held the judgeship in many lands, and wrote works on the principles and branches of jurisprudence. Among them are "al-Ahkam al-Sultaniyya", "Qawanin al-Wuzara'", "al-Hukm" and "al-Amthal", and "al-Nukat" on exegesis. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}

The data attribute to him [[work/al-ahkam-al-sultaniyya-mawardi|al-Ahkam al-Sultaniyya]], [[work/adab-al-dunya-wal-din|Adab al-Dunya wal-Din]], [[work/amthal-al-quran-mawardi|Amthal al-Qur'an]], and [[work/alam-al-tubwa|A'lam al-Nubuwwa]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 518} Haji Khalifa says that the abridged "A'lam al-Nubuwwa" contains twenty-one chapters. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 518}

## What he said of his books
It is reported that he expanded jurisprudence over four thousand leaves and then abridged it to forty. The "expanded" is the "al-Hawi" and the "abridged" is "al-Iqna'". {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}

- The year of death, 450 AH, is converted to about 1058 CE, and needs a second source.
