## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Jallab al-Basri al-Maliki, a Maliki jurist from Basra {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
The encyclopedia gives his death as 378 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
