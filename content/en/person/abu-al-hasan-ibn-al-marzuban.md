## Overview
Abu al-Hasan ibn al-Marzuban al-Baghdadi, a jurist from among the companions of [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-qattan|Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Qattan]]. He died in 366 AH, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}

Al-Shirazi describes him as a scrupulous jurist, and reports that he said he knew of no one who had a claim against him. He taught in Baghdad, and Abu Hamid al-Isfara'ini studied with him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}
