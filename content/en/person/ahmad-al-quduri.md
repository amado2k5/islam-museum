## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ja'far ibn Hamdan, Abu al-Husayn al-Quduri, was a Hanafi jurist. He was born and died in Baghdad, and the leadership of the Hanafis in Iraq came to him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 202}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 362 to 428 AH, that is 973 to 1037 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 202}.

## His works
He composed the abridgement known by his name, «al-Quduri», in Hanafi jurisprudence, which is printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 202}. Among his books is «al-Tajrid», in seven parts, which covers the differences between al-Shafi'i and Abu Hanifa and his companions; its first volume survives in manuscript at the Chester Beatty library under number 3523; and a book on marriage («al-Nikah»), printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 202}.
