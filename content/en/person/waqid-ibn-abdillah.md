## Overview
[[person/waqid-ibn-abdillah|Waqid ibn Abd Allah]] al-Tamimi al-Yarbu'i, an ally of the [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi ibn Ka'b]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1562}. Musa ibn Uqba said in the campaigns: Waqid, also said Waqdan, took part at Badr, and Ibn Ishaq similarly mentions him among those who took part at Badr {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1562}.

## The expedition to Nakhla
Ibn Ishaq reports, from Urwa, that the Prophet sent [[person/abdullah-ibn-jahsh|Abd Allah ibn Jahsh]] to Nakhla. In it, Waqid ibn Abd Allah came up to them having shaved his head, and they said: "These are umar (pilgrims); there is no harm to you from him." The Muslims then resolved to fight them, and "Waqid ibn Abd Allah shot Amr ibn al-Hadrami with an arrow and killed him". The verse of [[passage/quran-2-217|al-Baqara (2:217)]] was then revealed {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1562} {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1563}. For details, see [[event/expedition-of-nakhla|the expedition to Nakhla]]. Abu Nu'aym reports the story, and al-Tabari reports it through al-Suddi {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1563}.

## Boasting and after
Abu Ubayda said: the Banu Yarbu' used to boast that among them was the first who killed a polytheist in Islam. [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] composed verses about this, mentioning the spears that gave Ibn al-Hadrami to drink at Nakhla when Waqid kindled the war {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1563}. Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab said, from Umayr: "I named my son Waqid after Waqid ibn Abd Allah al-Yarbu'i" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1563}. Ibn Abi Hatim said, from his father: this Waqid died at the beginning of the caliphate of Umar {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1563}.
