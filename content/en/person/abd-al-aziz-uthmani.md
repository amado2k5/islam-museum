## Overview
Sultan Abd al-Aziz ibn Mahmud II (1277–1293 AH / 1861–1876 CE), son of [[person/mahmud-ii-uthmani|Mahmud II]] and successor of [[person/abd-al-majid-i-uthmani|Abd al-Majid I]]. He was deposed and then killed in 1293 AH / 1876 CE in a coup plotted by state officials. Al-Salabi notes that this is well known from the documents. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.394-395}
