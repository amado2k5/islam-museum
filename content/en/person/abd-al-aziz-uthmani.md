## Overview
Sultan Abd al-Aziz ibn Mahmud II (1277–1293 AH / 1861–1876 CE), son of [[person/mahmud-ii-uthmani|Mahmud II]] and successor of [[person/abd-al-majid-i-uthmani|Abd al-Majid I]]. He was deposed and then killed in 1293 AH / 1876 CE in a coup plotted by state officials. Al-Salabi notes that this is well known from the documents. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.394-395}

## The plot as al-Salabi tells it
Al-Salabi attributes the deposition to a plan by consuls and European diplomats in the capital, carried out through officials, among them Midhat Pasha, who admitted at his trial that he had taken part in the deposition and killing of the sultan. This is the author's attribution and is presented as his. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.394}
