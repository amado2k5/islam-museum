## Summary
Artemidorus is, for Ibn al-Nadim, the author of the dream book: he wrote a book on dream interpretation in five treatises, translated into Arabic by Hunayn ibn Ishaq. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 357}

## Details
In the Fihrist's seventh discourse, in the section "names of the natural philosophers whose dates and ranks are unknown," Ibn al-Nadim lists Artemidorus: "Artemidorus, author of the book on dreams; among his books is a book of dream interpretation in five treatises, translated by Hunayn ibn Ishaq." {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 357}

Ibn al-Nadim names the work a second time in the chapter "books composed on dream interpretation," listing "the book of Artemidorus on dream interpretation, five treatises," alongside Porphyry's On Sleep and Waking, the dream book of Ibn Sirin and others. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 439} It thus belongs to the Greek books that reached Arabic through the translation movement, carried by Hunayn ibn Ishaq, the famous Baghdad translator of the third century AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 357}
