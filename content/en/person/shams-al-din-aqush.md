## Overview
Amir Shams al-Din Aqush, lord of Aleppo. He installed a caliph there, titled him "al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah", gave the sermon in his name, and had his own name struck on the dirhams. So there were two caliphs at one time, in Aleppo and in Cairo {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.724}.
