## Overview
The judge Mujir al-Din Abu al-Yumn Abd al-Rahman al-Ulaymi al-Hanbali, author of [[work/al-uns-al-jalil|al-Uns al-Jalil bi-Tarikh al-Quds wa al-Khalil]] (The Sublime Companion on the History of Jerusalem and Hebron). He died around 927 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 649-650}

## The book
He gathered in it the essence of the histories of Jerusalem, and added to it a summary of events and deaths. He began writing it in Dhu al-Hijja 900 AH, and finished it four months later. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 649-650}

## Date of death
The text of *Kashf al-Zunun* gives 927 AH, while its editors hold 928 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 649}
