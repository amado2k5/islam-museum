## Overview
A commander of the army of Syria. He entered al-Andalus in 124 AH, escaping the battle of Kulthum ibn Iyad with the Berbers at Malwiya. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273}

Al-Maqqari relates that he rose against the amir Abd al-Malik ibn Qatan al-Fihri and killed him, aged seventy, and his affair in al-Andalus was set right for him. The Fihris sided against him and held out against him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273}
