## Overview
Uthman ibn Utba ibn Abi Sufyan was called to give allegiance after the killing of his brother al-Walid, on the day of the prayer of Mu'awiya ibn Yazid. He stipulated that he would not fight nor engage in battle, but they refused him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.70}.

He went to Mecca and joined the company of Ibn al-Zubayr, and the affair passed from the family of Harb, according to al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.70}.
