## Overview
Ratbil, king of the Zabulis, who held Zabulistan and al-Rukhkh until he reached Bust, as al-Baladhuri reports {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## With the Muslims
After Kabul Shah had driven the Muslims out of Kabul, al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad fought him at Bust, defeated him, and pursued him to al-Rukhkh. He then sent to Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra asking for peace. It was concluded for a million dirhams, and Ziyad confirmed it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

al-Sallabi reports that Abd Allah ibn Umayya made peace with him for three hundred thousand dirhams, and was dismissed by Abd al-Malik for it. He reports that Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra made peace with him on paying tribute for a time, and refused it at other times. al-Hajjaj advanced with an army of forty thousand in 80 AH {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.87-88}.
