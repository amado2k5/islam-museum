## Summary
Shihab al-Din al-Turki al-Tarabulusi al-Dimashqi al-Shafi'i led prayers at the congregational mosque of Tripoli in Syria and was among the people of piety and goodness — he used to read the entire mushaf in two rak'as every day and night. He died in 920. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 470}
