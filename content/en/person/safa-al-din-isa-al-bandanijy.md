## Overview
Abu al-Huda Safa' al-Din Isa ibn Jalal al-Din Musa al-Bandanijy al-Qadiri al-Naqshbandi al-Baghdadi. A Sufi scholar of Baghdad, from both the Qadiriyya and Naqshbandiyya orders, and called after Bandanijin {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
The encyclopedia gives his birth as 1203 AH (1788 CE) and his death as 1283 AH (1866 CE). It classes him as a saint on the basis of transmitted report {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
