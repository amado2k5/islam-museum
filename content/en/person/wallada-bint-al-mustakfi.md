## Overview
Wallada bint al-Mustakfi billah Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Ubayd Allah ibn al-Nasir, an Andalusian woman of letters. al-Maqqari mentions her in Nafh al-Tib {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.209}.

## al-Maqqari's description
al-Maqqari describes her as unique in her time, graceful in conversation and praiseworthy in discussion. He says that she was famous for chastity and modesty {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.209}.

## Ibn Zaydun
al-Maqqari reports that the name of Ibn Zaydun was linked with hers. When he inclined toward her slave-girl, she wrote him verses reproaching him, and called him al-Musaddas (the Hexagonal) {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.210}.
