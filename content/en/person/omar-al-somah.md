# Omar Al Somah

## Summary

Omar Jehad Al Somah is a Syrian striker born in Deir ez-Zor on 23 March 1989, nicknamed Al ʿAggied (العكيد, "the Colonel"). He finished as the Saudi Pro League's top scorer in three straight seasons (2015, 2016 and 2017) with Al-Ahli, winning the 2016 league title, and has won 47 caps for Syria, scoring 23 goals. {cite:wikipedia-omar-al-somah-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-al-somah-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-al-somah}

## Career

He began in Syria with Al-Fotuwa, moved to Al-Qadsia in Kuwait, and in 2014 joined Al-Ahli of Jeddah, where his scoring made him one of the Saudi league's defining forwards; he later played for Al-Arabi in Qatar, Al-Hazem and Neom in Saudi Arabia, and in 2026 returned to Syria with Al-Wahda of Damascus, the city the Arabic article records as his residence. He has been married since 2015. {cite:wikipedia-omar-al-somah-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-al-somah-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-al-somah}
