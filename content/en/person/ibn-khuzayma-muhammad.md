## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Khuzayma ibn al-Mughira al-Sulami, described by the source as the imam of the imams and the hadith scholar of Nishapur. He compiled the *Sahih*, known as the Sahih of Ibn Khuzayma. He was born in 223 AH (838 CE) and died in 311 AH (924 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
