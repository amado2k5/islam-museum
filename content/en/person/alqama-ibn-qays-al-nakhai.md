## Overview
Abu Shibl Alqama ibn Qays ibn Abd Allah ibn Alqama al-Nakhai, one of the jurists of Kufa among the Successors. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

## His kinship and connections
He was the paternal uncle of al-Aswad ibn Yazid and Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid, and the maternal uncle of Ibrahim al-Nakha'i. Ibrahim said that he was better than al-Aswad. He took part in the battle of Siffin. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

The book says that he was among the companions of [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Ibn Mas'ud]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## His death
Al-Shirazi relates that he died in 62 AH (about 681 CE; the conversion is derived). {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

## Notes for researchers
- The link with Ibn Mas'ud is taken from the list of his companions in the book; it needs an additional source.
