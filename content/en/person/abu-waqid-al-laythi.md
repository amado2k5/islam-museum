## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Malik, Abu Waqid al-Laythi, a Companion known by his kunya. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.268}

## His companionship and hadith
Al-Sha'bi reported from him that he heard the Messenger of God say on the day of the Conquest: Mecca shall never again be raided, until the Day of Resurrection. Al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Hibban transmitted his hadith and declared it sound, and al-Daraqutni transmitted it through al-Sha'bi from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.268}
