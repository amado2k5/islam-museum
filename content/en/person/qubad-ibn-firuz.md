## Overview
Qubad ibn Firuz, a king of the Sasanian Persians. al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Qubad ibn Firuz", and reports there what was said about the years of his reign and his affairs. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.90}
