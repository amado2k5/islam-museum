## Overview
Qubad ibn Firuz, a king of the Sasanian Persians. al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Qubad ibn Firuz", and reports there what was said about the years of his reign and his affairs. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.90}

No other details are recorded here; an additional source is needed.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] says Kavad reigned forty-three years and on his deathbed passed the rule to Anushirvan, reproaching him for his suspiciousness. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 67} He began the survey of the land to replace crop-sharing with a fixed land tax, but died before completing it. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 71}
