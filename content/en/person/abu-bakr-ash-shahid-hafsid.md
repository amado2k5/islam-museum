## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Yahya al-Wathiq ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid was one of the kings of the Hafsid state in Tunis. The entry dates his death to 709 AH, equal to 1309 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.

## Accession
He took power after his brother al-Mustansir, who is Muhammad ibn Yahya, by an appointment from him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.

## His killing
Khalid the Hafsid, a son of Yahya ibn Ibrahim ibn Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid, rose against him. Abu Bakr wished to fight him, but his troops dispersed from around him, so he surrendered to Khalid, who killed him at Tunis. He was therefore given the epithet al-Shahid. His reign lasted seventeen days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.

## Death
He was killed at Tunis in 709 AH, equal to 1309 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 60}.
