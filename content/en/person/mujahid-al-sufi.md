## Summary
Mujahid al-Sufi found intimacy in the remembrance of God and estrangement from anything else. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 477 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 133. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/133}

## His saying
Abu Turab al-Zahid relates his saying: take God as your companion and leave the people aside. Whoever has the Qur'an as his speaker, supplication as his messenger, the angels as his companions, and God as his intimate, loss will not frighten him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/133}
