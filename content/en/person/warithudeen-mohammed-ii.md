## Summary
Warithudeen Mohammed II is an American imam and president of The Mosque Cares, the charitable organisation founded by his father — Imam W. Deen Mohammed, who moved the Nation of Islam toward mainstream Sunni Islam after 1975 — and from that office he continues his family's line of teaching, worship and community development. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## Raised in the imam's house
He grew up inside one of the great transitions of American Islam: his grandfather Elijah Muhammad led the Nation of Islam, and his father Warith Deen Mohammed succeeded him in 1975 and led the community into Sunni Islam, rejecting the deification of its founder, teaching the Five Pillars and opening worship to Muslims of every race. The son was born around 1964, by a 2015 report describing him as fifty-one years old. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## The Mosque Cares
He presides over The Mosque Cares, the charity his father established, which sponsors classes in Qur'anic Arabic and financial literacy, informational sessions in health and nutrition, and business and entrepreneurial initiatives, working from Chicago's southern suburbs. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## Preaching and community
He preaches in the community's mosques and travels to speak on his family's evolution in Islam and on citizenship and cooperation within one society; his line, like his father's, centres on the Qur'an, education and work. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## Legacy
He embodies the third generation of the Nation's move to Sunni Islam: from a racial movement in 1930s Detroit to an American Sunni community. His record carries the transition in his person, and his role is to maintain his father's charitable institutions rather than to found a new platform. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}
## Three generations
A Parliament of the World's Religions session framed his story as ninety-two years of Islam's evolution in America across three generations of leadership: from Elijah Muhammad, to Warith Deen Mohammed who turned the community to Sunni Islam in 1975-1976 and established The Mosque Cares, to the son appointed before his father's 2008 death as president of the organisation and the Ministry of Imam W. Deen Mohammed. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## The programmes
The Mosque Cares sponsors, per his profile, classes in Qur'anic Arabic and financial literacy, informational sessions in health and nutrition, and business and entrepreneurial initiatives — the charitable and educational arm of the community his father built on the Sunni path. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}
## The community after the imam
After W. Deen Mohammed's death in 2008, his community passed to a generation of successors carrying his institutional legacy, among them his eldest son Warithudeen II, who runs The Mosque Cares from Chicago's suburbs and tours American cities preaching in the community's mosques and lecturing in its centres, carrying the story of the move to Sunni Islam to a new public. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}

## The message of learning and work
His line centres on what his father taught: return to the Qur'an as the source of guidance, education as the foundation of human building, and productive work as the foundation of the community's dignity — in programmes such as the Arabic classes and financial literacy the organisation calls its charitable and educational arm. {cite:muslim500-warithudeen-mohammed-ii}
