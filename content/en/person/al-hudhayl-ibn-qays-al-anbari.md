## Overview
Al-Hudhayl ibn Qays al-Anbari, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the account of the move of the Anbaris to Isfahan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434}

## The report
Al-Baladhuri relates from al-Kalbi and Abu al-Yaqzan that Marwan appointed him over Isfahan, and from that time the Anbaris went there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434}
