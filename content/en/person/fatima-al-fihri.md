## Overview
A wealthy Tunisian woman who endowed her wealth to the construction of the al-Qarawiyyin mosque in Fez around 859; it is described as the oldest continuously operating university in the world. {cite:wikidata}

## The heroic stand
Fatima al-Fihri inherited a wide fortune and spent none of it on house or jewels; she set the whole of it aside for a mosque and a school where people would still be learning centuries later {cite:wikidata}. So one woman's inheritance in Fez became [[building/qarawiyyin|al-Qarawiyyin]], remembered as among the oldest continuously working institutions of learning in the world {cite:wikidata}. A quiet, long-lived heroism: leaving what endures and benefits rather than what is spent and forgotten.
