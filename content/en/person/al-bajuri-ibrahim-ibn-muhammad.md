## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Bajuri, a theologian and Shafi'i jurist. He was born in the town of al-Bajur in the al-Minufiyya district of Egypt, memorised the Qur'an there, and then moved to al-Azhar. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 1198 AH (1784 CE) and his death as 1277 AH (1860 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.29}
