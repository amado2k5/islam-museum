## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Ali al-Rushati al-Mari, a scholar of al-Andalus. He was concerned with hadith, the transmitters, narrators and histories, and wrote "Iqtibas al-Anwar" on the genealogies of the Companions and the transmitters of reports, in the manner of al-Samani's "Ansab". {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.466}

## His life
He was born in 466 AH in the village of Orihuela, in the district of Murcia, and died in Almeria on the morning of Friday, twentieth of Jumada al-Ula 542 AH, when the enemy overcame it. Al-Maqqari describes him as having died a martyr, which is the source's description. The Gregorian date in the museum's data is derived from the Hijri date. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.466}
