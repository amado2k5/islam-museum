## Summary
Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Mahdi ibn Rustam, of firm faith and trustworthy in transmission, who spent much wealth on learning and was generous. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 679, sequence 680, on pages 396 and 397. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/397}

## The woman and the concealment
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim relates that Ahmad ibn Mahdi said: a woman came to him in Baghdad one night, claiming to be of respectable people, and said she had suffered a wrong and asked him to conceal it. When he asked, she said she had been forced while she was pregnant, and asked him to let it be said that he was her husband; he stayed silent and she went. When she gave birth, the imam of the quarter and a group of neighbours came to congratulate him on the child; he showed gladness, and the next day weighed two dinars and gave them to the imam for the woman, as maintenance for the child; he paid two dinars each month through the imam for two years. The child died, and he showed submission and contentment to the people. Later the woman came one night with the dinars and returned them; she said: may God conceal you as you concealed me. He said: these were a gift from me to the child, and they are yours, since you inherit from him; spend them as you wish. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/397}

## Spending on learning
Abu Muhammad ibn Hayyan relates that Ahmad ibn Mahdi had much wealth and spent all of it on learning, about three hundred thousand dirhams; and it was said that he was not known to have had a fixed dwelling for forty years. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/397}

## Traditions
The notice gives a report from Abu Hurayra on the virtue of saying that there is no god but God, and a report from Musa ibn Talha, from his father, on the verse about the men who were true to their covenant with God being revealed concerning the dead of Uhud. The wording of these reports is not given here, and the verse itself is not typed. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/397}
