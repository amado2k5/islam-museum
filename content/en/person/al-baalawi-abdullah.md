## Summary
Ba 'Alawi 'Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn 'Abd al-Rahman al-Yamani, Sufi shaykh of the Hadramawt, died in Ramadan 865 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}.

## Details
Ibn al-'Imad enters him under the dead of 865 as "Ba 'Alawi 'Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn 'Abd al-Rahman al-Yamani, the Sufi," calling him "the shaykh of Hadramawt, its pillar, its Sufi and its ascetic," with followers and servants and "manifest sainthood and resplendent secrets"; he died in the month of Ramadan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}. He thus ranks among the great Sufi masters of ninth-century Hadramawt, of the Ba 'Alawi house whose tariqa spread through those lands and was later carried to India, the Malay world and East Africa {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}. The description pairing outward sainthood with resplendent secrets combines the recognized rank of wilaya with the miracles Sufis attribute to their shaykhs {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}.
