## Who he was
A Jew of Medina known in the books of seerah and lore as the Prophet's sorcerer; named in the sources as Labid ibn al-Asam {cite:sira-ibn-ishaq-zakkar}.

## What he did
He bewitched the Prophet until his condition grew severe; when the two Suras of Refuge were revealed the magic was drawn from a well beneath his bedding {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## His end
He confessed what he had done and was expelled from Medina {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes
His report is the most famous case of human magic in the Prophetic biography {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
