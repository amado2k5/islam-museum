## Summary
A Companion of Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza; he emigrated to Abyssinia and arrived in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him alone of Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-asad|Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
