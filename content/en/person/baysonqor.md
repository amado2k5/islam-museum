## Summary
Baysonqor: Timurid prince, linked in the museum's record to Herat. The Biblissima record places his life between 1397 and 1433. {cite:biblissima Q231821}
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Ḡīāt al-Dīn Bāysonḡor» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q231821}
