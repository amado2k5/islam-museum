## Summary
Of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b; he emigrated to Abyssinia and perished there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Adi in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", saying he perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9} He lists him again among the dead — he and Adi ibn Nadla count among the seven who perished. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi ibn Ka'b]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/9}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The OCR dropped some letters of his lineage; the lineage was established from the page image p. 9 (Abd al-Uzza ibn Hurthan ibn Awf ibn Ubayd ibn Uwayj).
