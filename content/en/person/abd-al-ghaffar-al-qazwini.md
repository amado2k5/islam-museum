## Summary
Abd al-Ghaffar ibn Abd al-Karim ibn Abd al-Ghaffar, Najm al-Din al-Qazwini, a Shafi'i jurist who also knew arithmetic. His works include al-Hawi al-saghir and al-Aja'ib fi sharh al-lubab, both on Shafi'i law, and a book on arithmetic. The book gives 665 AH as his death year. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8202}
