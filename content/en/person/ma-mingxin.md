## Summary

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam presents Ma Mingxin (died 1781) as a leading Muslim figure of eighteenth-century China. Born into the Muslim community of Gansu, in the north-west, he travelled to the Middle East, above all Yemen, Mecca and Medina, and studied there. After being initiated into the Naqshbandi tariqah he came home in 1761 resolved to purify Islamic life. The renewal movement associated with him, known as the "New Teaching", recited its litany aloud instead of silently and urged reform of Muslim life. He came into conflict with both the Muslim and the Chinese imperial establishments, and a rebellion broke out in which imperial forces killed him. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1363}
