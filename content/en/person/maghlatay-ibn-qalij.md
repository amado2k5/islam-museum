## Overview
Mughaltay ibn Qalij ibn Abd Allah al-Bakjari al-Misri al-Hikri al-Hanafi, Abu Abd Allah 'Ala' al-Din, was a historian and hadith hafiz versed in genealogies, of Turkish origin naturalised Arab, of Egypt. He held the chair of hadith at the Muzaffariyya madrasa in Egypt and was a critic with censure for the hadith men and the linguists. He died in 762 AH (1361 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.275}

His works number over a hundred, among them a commentary on al-Bukhari in twenty volumes; an unfinished commentary on Ibn Maja's Sunan; Ikmal Tahdhib al-Kamal on the names of rijal; al-Zahr al-Basim on the sira of Abu al-Qasim; a supplement to Ibn Nuqta's al-Mu'talif wa-l-Mukhtalif; al-Ishara on the Prophet's sira, the abridgement of al-Zahr al-Basim with additions on some caliphs; al-Wadih al-Mubin on lovers martyred; and more. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.275}
