## Summary
Leader of the Christian fleet in the Mediterranean under [[person/charles-v|Charles V]], in al-Salabi's phrase {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 216}.

## The captives' ruse
Hearing that Sultan [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]] meant to invade Spain and had summoned [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din]] from Algiers, Doria spread word among the Christian captives there of an imminent Spanish attack to free them; they rejoiced and rebelled — and Khayr al-Din executed them, which al-Salabi frames as the public-interest precaution before his 940/1533 departure {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 216}.

## In Khayr al-Din's path
When Khayr al-Din reached Preveza its people rejoiced, fearing as they had Doria's attack — which withdrew on hearing of his coming {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 216-217}.
