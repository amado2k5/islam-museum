## Overview
Muhammad ibn As'ad al-Siddiqi al-Dawwani, Jalal al-Din, was a judge and scholar whom al-Zirikli counts among the philosophers. He was born at Dawan, in the land of Kazarun, settled in Shiraz, took the judgeship of Fars and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 32}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 830 AH, 1427 CE, and his death as 918 AH, 1512 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 32}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Unmudhaj al-Ulum, in manuscript; Ta'rif al-Ilm, in manuscript; a thabat on his teachers, in manuscript; Ithbat al-Wajib, a printed treatise; a printed gloss on al-Qushji's commentary on the Tajrid al-Kalam; Af'al al-Ibad, a printed treatise; a printed gloss on the Tahrir al-Qawa'id al-Mantiqiyya of Qutb al-Razi; the printed Sharh al-Aqa'id al-Adudiyya; a manuscript tafsir of the sura named al-Kafirun; al-Arba'un al-Sultaniyya, forty hadiths, in manuscript; a manuscript gloss on Mabahith al-Umur al-Amma; and a manuscript Sharh Tahdhib al-Mantiq {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 32}. He adds that al-Dawwani left Persian treatises, some of which have been translated into English {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 33}.

## Copies al-Zirikli saw
Al-Zirikli says he saw al-Dawwani's manuscript al-As'ila al-Sharifa al-Qur'aniyya in the Vatican Library, number 488 Arabic {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 32}. He also says he found a copy of al-Dawwani's commentary on al-Suhrawardi's Hayakil al-Nur in the hand of Shaykh Muhammad Abduh, mufti of Egypt, and quotes its colophon: the author wrote the book out after the evening prayer in 872 at the Dar al-Muwahhidin in Hormuz, in the blessed Muzaffariyya zawiya; the colophon goes on to the sultan who founded it, his march towards Diyar Bakr early that year, the enemy's attack on him and his assassination on 13 Rabi' I of the same year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 32-33}.
