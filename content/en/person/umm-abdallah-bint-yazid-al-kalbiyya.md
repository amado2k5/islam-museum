## Overview
Umm Abd Allah bint Yazid al-Kalbiyya, the wife of [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama al-Fihri]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278}.

## The night of the raid
al-Baladhuri reports that on the night the Byzantines made a night attack at Qaliqala, she asked her husband: "Where is your appointment?" He said: "The tent of the tyrant, or Paradise" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278}.

When he reached the tent, he found her there. The event has a page of its own: [[event/conquest-of-qaliqala|the conquest of Qaliqala]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278}.
