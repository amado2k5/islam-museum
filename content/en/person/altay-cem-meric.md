## Summary
Altay Cem Meriç is a Turkish physician, author and content creator born in 1991 in Ankara, known for his work on faith, philosophy and the Islamic sciences and for his return from atheism to Islam after a period of questioning. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr} {cite:kitapyurdu-altay-cem-meric}

His YouTube channel counted 539,000 subscribers as of 3 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-altay-cem-meric}

## Early life and education
Meriç was born in Ankara in 1991 and completed his higher education at Necmettin Erbakan University's Faculty of Medicine in Konya, taking the title of medical doctor, and he has continued to practise medicine since graduating. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr} {cite:kitapyurdu-altay-cem-meric}

His academic interest extended to philosophy and the study of religion, and he learned Arabic alongside English so as to read Islamic and philosophical texts in their original language. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr} {cite:kitapyurdu-altay-cem-meric}

## Creedal journey and work
The article and his publishers' biography say that in his youth he held an atheist worldview, pursued deep questioning and philosophical research into faith, existence, science and logic, and that this course ended with his return to Islam. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr} {cite:kitapyurdu-altay-cem-meric}

That experience directed him to focus on the creedal doubts facing modern people and on analytical, kalam-based answers to objections raised against Islam. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr}

## Books and publishing
Among the books his publishers' biography names are Peygamberliğin İspatı (The Proof of Prophethood), the Muhtelif series on objections to Islam and their answers, Öğrenmeyi Öğrenmek (Learning to Learn) and Kötülük Problemi (The Problem of Evil). {cite:kitapyurdu-altay-cem-meric}

He founded the Tin publishing house to publish works in his own intellectual line and to fill a gap in kalam and philosophy publishing, and he conducts his publishing activity through it. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr}

## Programmes and work
On his eponymous channel (@altaycemmeric) he publishes regular videos on faith, philosophy, modern science and the Islamic sciences, and the series in which he tells his own creedal journey is among his channel's most-watched content. {cite:wikipedia-altay-cem-meric-tr} {cite:youtube-altay-cem-meric}

In his channel's own description he presents himself as a medical doctor who opened the channel to examine and comment on the turning points of the history of thought through books. {cite:youtube-altay-cem-meric}

## Reach
His audience figure is his own YouTube channel: 539,000 subscribers, 81,898,783 views and 1,097 videos as of 3 November 2026 per vidIQ; his appearances on other people's channels are not counted here. {cite:vidiq-altay-cem-meric}
