## Overview
Abu Turab Muhammad ibn al-Abbas al-Alawi is an Alawi in the army of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. Al-Hadi put him over a party of the Futaymi horse, a party of the troops and foot of the Hayyiyun and the Hamzis, and ordered him to go by a road he named until he entered [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]] from its top. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} He then came up with his force to al-Hadi at Allaf after [[place/al-numays-allaf-yemen|al-Numays]] was pulled down. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} No further life of him is given in these pages.
