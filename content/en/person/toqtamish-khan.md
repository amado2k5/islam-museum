## Overview
Toqtamish ibn Berdibek, of the Banu Doshi Khan, lord of the throne at Sarai in the [[polity/golden-horde|Golden Horde]]. Ibn Khaldun reports that rule was firmly his at Sarai and its dependencies after Mamai {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## The war with Timur
Ibn Khaldun reports that the Shaykh, lord of Mazandaran, rebelled against [[person/timur-lang|Timur]] at the instigation of Toqtamish, so Timur marched against him. The two armies met, Toqtamish was defeated and his household scattered, but then he returned and recovered Sarai. The war between them did not end {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

At the end of the year 797 AH the news reached that Timur had overcome him, killed him and seized all his lands {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
