## Overview
Nasr al-Qushuri was one of al-Hallaj's scribes. al-Hallaj called him "the righteous shaykh" {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.271-272}.

## The book al-Durra
According to Ibn al-Nadim, the book "al-Durra" among al-Hallaj's writings is attributed to him {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.271-272}.
