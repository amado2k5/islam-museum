## Overview
Bustam ibn Qays ibn Mas'ud al-Shaybani, kunya Abu al-Sahba', called Dhu al-Jaddayn, lord of the [[group/banu-shayban|Banu Shayban]] in the pre-Islamic age. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461}

## His reports
Ibn al-Athir says that he raided the Banu Dabba and took the camels of Malik ibn al-Muntafiq. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461}

He was killed on the day of al-Shaqiqa, when he was at the back of the people protecting his companions on his dark horse called al-Za'faran. Much poetry was composed about him, and the common people said: "On the day of al-Ifaqa, they submitted Bustam." {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.461}
