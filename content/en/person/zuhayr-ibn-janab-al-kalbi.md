## Overview
Zuhayr ibn Janab al-Kalbi, a pre-Islamic horseman of the Kalb. Sa'sa'a ibn Suhan cited him to Ibn Abbas when describing the horseman, in verses in which he laments his son Amr {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.45-46}.
