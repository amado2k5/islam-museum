## Overview
Shams al-Din Sawab al-'Adili (d. Ramadan 632) — al-Kamil's field commander and a byword for courage; among his own mamluks were a hundred servants, several of them amirs; he died at Harran as al-Kamil's governor there {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 149}.
