## Summary
Son of Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As and Amina bint Khalf; he was born in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him son of Khalid ibn Sa'id, saying he was born in the land of Abyssinia with his sister Umma. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He counts him among the five men born there, of Banu Abd Shams. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}
