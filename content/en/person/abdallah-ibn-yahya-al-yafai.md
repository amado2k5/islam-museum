## Overview
The imam-scholar 'Abdallah b. Yahya al-Yafa'i; he died teacher of Ceuta. One had tried to frighten him on a Yemen road — the author of al-Bayan loved him and was present at his agony the day he died. He related that men fell upon him on a road and struck him with swords, and their swords did not cut; asked, he said: "I was reciting Surat Ya-Sin." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 646-647}

## His works
He composed Kitab al-ta'rif in fiqh; he was supported and provided by one of the shaykhs of the Banu Yahya of Yafi'. His tarjama is in the annals of 553. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 646-647}
