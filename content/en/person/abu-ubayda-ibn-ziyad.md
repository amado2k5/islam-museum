## Overview
Abu Ubayda ibn Ziyad, a man of the Umayyad period. The people of Kabul captured him when they broke the covenant. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His ransom
Al-Baladhuri says that Talha ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalaf al-Khuza'i ransomed him for five hundred thousand dirhams, at the request of Salm ibn Ziyad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
