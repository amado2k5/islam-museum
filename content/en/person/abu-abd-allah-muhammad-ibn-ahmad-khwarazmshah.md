## Summary
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ahmad is the last Afrighid in al-Biruni's works, and Eduard Sachau, following al-Biruni, counts him as the twenty-second king of the house. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 50}

## His end
In 385/995 Ma'mun ibn Muhammad, the Samanid-dependent emir of Jurjaniyya, attacked Kath, captured Abu Abd Allah and had him killed in Jurjaniyya, then took the title Khwarazmshah. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 70} Kingship thus passed from Kath to the Ma'munid house. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 10}

## His coins
- Type 1 is a fals with the profession of God's oneness on the obverse as on earlier coins, a margin reading "In the name of God, this fals was struck at Khwarazm", and on the reverse "Muhammad ibn Ahmad Khwarazmshah" with no marginal legend. A specimen was found at the site of Sadvar in southern Khwarazm in the excavations of 1973–1975. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 50} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 51}
- Types 2 and 3 follow almost the same model. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 51}
