## Overview
An Ansari whom the Prophet sent with [[person/amr-ibn-umayya-al-damri|Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri]] on [[event/mission-of-amr-ibn-umayya-against-abu-sufyan|the mission to Mecca to kill Abu Sufyan]]. When they entered Mecca at night, he said to Amr: "If we circled the House and prayed two rak'as." They did so. When Quraysh pursued them after the body of Khubayb had been taken down, Amr told him to flee until he reached his camel and mounted it, since Amr would occupy the people; the Ansari "had no strength in walking" (la rujla lahu), meaning he lacked the strength to walk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/282} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/283}

## Notes on the report
- His whole account is in Ibn Hisham's version, from one he trusted, and not in Ibn Ishaq at this point. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/282}
