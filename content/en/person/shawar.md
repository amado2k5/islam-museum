## Overview
Shawar, lord of Egypt. The source describes him as one who kept seeking help from others {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-680}.

He sought the help of the Franks against Asad al-Din Shirkuh in the year 562 AH, and they entered by way of Damietta. In the year 564 AH he burned Fustat out of fear of the Franks, when they besieged Cairo, and then he wrote to Nur al-Din asking him for help {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-680}.
