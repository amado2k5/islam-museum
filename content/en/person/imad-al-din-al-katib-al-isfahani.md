## Summary
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Safi al-Din Abi al-Faraj Muhammad ibn Nafis al-Din Abi al-Raja Hamid al-Isfahani, known as Ibn Sabiq al-Izz, with the title Imad al-Din al-Katib {cite:wafayat-ayan}.

## With Salah al-Din
He was secretary to Sultan Salah al-Din. His well-known works include al-Barq al-Shami on the history of the Nurid state, al-Fath al-Qussi fi al-Fath al-Qudsi on Salah al-Din's conquests, and Kharidat al-Qasr wa Jaridat al-Asr on the poets of his age {cite:wafayat-ayan}. He kept his position and standing until Sultan Salah al-Din died. Then his affairs were disrupted, and he turned to his home and devoted himself to writing {cite:wafayat-ayan}.

## Death
He was born on Monday, 2 Jumada al-Akhira, or in Sha'ban according to another account, in 519 AH in Isfahan, and he died in Damascus on Monday, the first day of Ramadan {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
