## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Asbahani, to whom the house of Ibn al-Asbahani in Basra is attributed. He had four hundred slaves, and he met al-Mukhtar with [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Mus'ab]] while he was on Mus'ab's right wing, according to the book of al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
