## Overview
Chief qadi Shams al-Din al-Khuyi the Shafi'i — of Khuy, a town in Azerbaijan; he entered Khurasan, read usul under al-Qutb al-Misri, pupil of the imam Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, fiqh under al-Rafi'i and the science of dialectic under 'Ala' al-Din al-Tusi, and held the chief judgeship of Syria. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 183}

## His works and praise
His books include one on usul, one of philosophical symbols, one on grammar and one on prosody; al-Dhahabi called him "a jurist, imam, debater, expert in kalam, a master in medicine and wisdom, devout, much praying and fasting." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 183}
