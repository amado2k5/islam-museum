## Summary

Abdallah ibn Makhrama ibn Abd al-Uzza, a Qurashi Companion of Banu Amir. He emigrated to Abyssinia in the second migration with Ja'far ibn Abi Talib, then on to Medina, and witnessed Badr. He was martyred at al-Yamama aged about thirty; it is related that lying wounded he asked whether the fasting person may break his fast, and called for water.
