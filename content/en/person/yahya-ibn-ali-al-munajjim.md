## Summary
Abu Ahmad Yahya ibn 'Ali ibn Yahya ibn Abi Mansur al-Munajjim, the boon-companion. Early on he was companion to al-Muwaffaq Abu Ahmad Talha ibn al-Mutawakkil, al-Mu'tadid's father, then companion to the caliphs after him, and became the chosen companion of al-Muktafi bi'llah — his rank rose and he took precedence over the caliph's intimates and table-companions. He was a mutakallim of Mu'tazili creed with many books in the field, and held an assembly attended by a group of mutakallims in al-Muktafi's presence. He composed the book *al-Bahir* on the poets astride the two dynasties, beginning with Bashshar ibn Burd; he left it unfinished and his son Abu'l-Hasan Ahmad ibn Yahya completed it. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
