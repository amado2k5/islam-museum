## Summary

Khalid ibn Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt ibn Abi Amr ibn Umayya ibn Abd Shams, of the Umayyads, the brother of al-Walid. He became Muslim at the conquest of Mecca and settled at al-Raqqa, where his descendants lived; the historian of al-Raqqa counts him among the Companions who settled there. He had a part in the siege of Uthman's house, and Azhar ibn Sayhan alludes to him in a verse. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.375}
