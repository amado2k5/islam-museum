## Summary

Khalid bin Ali Al Humaidan (born 1955) is a Saudi intelligence officer who has directed the General Intelligence Directorate since January 2015 {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar}.

## Life

Al Humaidan was born in Ha'il in 1955 - the Arabic article gives the Hijri year 1374 - into a family originating in Unaizah in the Qassim region {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar}. He entered King Fahd Security College in 1402 AH and studied criminal justice at Saginaw Valley State University in Michigan {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en}.

## Work

Al Humaidan served for over twenty years in the security forces - the English article says the army - before becoming deputy head of the criminal investigation division in 2011; the Arabic article specifies the post as deputy director general of the General Investigations Directorate (Mabahith), with promotion to lieutenant general {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar}. A royal decree of 29 January 2015 appointed him head of General Intelligence with the rank of minister and a member of the Council of Political and Security Affairs; the Arabic article names Khalid bin Bandar Al Saud as his predecessor, the English article Youssef bin Ali Al Idrissi {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en}. He also sits on the boards of the Saudi Data and AI Authority and the National Cybersecurity Authority {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar}. No text read for this record names a faith for Al Humaidan himself, so his faith is recorded as unknown {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-en} {cite:wikipedia-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan-ar} {cite:wikidata-khalid-bin-ali-al-humaidan}.
