## Overview
The imam Shihab al-Din 'Abd al-Halim b. 'Abd al-Salam b. Sayf al-Din, khatib of Harran — father of Shaykh al-Islam Taqi al-Din; he read the madhhab under his father until he mastered it, taught, gave fatwa, wrote, and became shaykh of the town after his father, its khatib and its judge. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 564, 645}

## The emigration to Damascus
He had a strong hand in inheritance-law, arithmetic and astronomy; then he emigrated with his family and kin to Damascus in 667, taking the shaykhdom of Dar al-Hadith al-Sukriyya at al-Qassa'in, keeping a chair in the mosque on which he spoke from memory on Fridays; when he died his son Abu'l-'Abbas succeeded him in both. Al-Birzali said he was of the notables of the Hanbalis, and al-Dhahabi said he was eclipsed only by the light of moon and sun — meaning his father and his son. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 564, 645}
