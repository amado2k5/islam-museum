## Overview
Tawus ibn Kaysan, jurist of Yemen. al-Mas'udi reports that when [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] was made caliph, he wrote to him: "If you want your work to be entirely good, appoint people of goodness." Umar said: "That is sufficient as an admonition" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.173}.

He died in the year 104 AH, and it is said in Mecca in the year 106 AH. Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik led the funeral prayer over him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191}.
