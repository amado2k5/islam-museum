## Summary
Nusayb the poet was a black slave belonging to a man of Wadi'l-Qura; he wrote a contract of self-purchase, then praised 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Marwan, who bought his loyalty. His kunya was Abu'l-Hajna', also said Abu Muhjan. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Before Sulayman
He attended with al-Farazdaq before Sulayman ibn 'Abd al-Malik, then caliph. Al-Farazdaq recited in praise of his father and Sulayman turned from him as if displeased; Nusayb then recited a completion in the same rhyme, and Sulayman asked al-Farazdaq: how do you find him? He said: he is the greatest poet of his color. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
