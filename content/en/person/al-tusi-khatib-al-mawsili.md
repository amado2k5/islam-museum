## Overview
Majd al-Din Abu'l-Fadl 'Abd Allah b. Ahmad al-Tusi, khatib of Mosul; famous for transmission — people sought him from the horizons — and Abu'l-Mahasin Ibn Shaddad heard on him the Jami' of Abu 'Isa al-Tirmidhi and many of his audition-collections {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
