## Overview
A'isha bint Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas belonged to the Banu Zuhra and is counted among the trustworthy women narrators of hadith. Al-Zirikli gives her birth as 33 AH, 653 CE, and her death as 117 AH, 735 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 240}.

## Her narration
Al-Zirikli lists her among the reliable women narrators of hadith. She lived in Medina, saw six of the Mothers of the Believers, and a number of scholars took hadith from her {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 240}.
