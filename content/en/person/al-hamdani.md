## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'qub al-Hamdani al-Yamani, who died in 334 AH (about 945 CE; the conversion is derived). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.556}

## His works
He is the author of "al-Iklil" on the genealogies of Himyar and the days of its kings, a great and very useful book, which is completed in ten volumes according to "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.556} See [[work/al-iklil-hamdani|al-Iklil]].

His "Sifat Jazirat al-Arab" is also attributed to him. [[work/sifat-jazirat-al-arab|Sifat Jazirat al-Arab]]
