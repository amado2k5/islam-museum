## Overview
Muhammad b. Yusuf, son of the qadi Yusuf b. Ya'qub; on Thursday with fourteen nights past of Rabi' I 284 he was given the robe of investiture and entrusted with the judgeship of Ja'far's city in place of 'Ali b. Muhammad b. Abi'l-Shawarib, and sat for attendance in the mosque — al-Mansur's city having gone without a qadi five months and four days {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 171}.
