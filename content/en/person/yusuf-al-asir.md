## Overview
Yusuf ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad al-Husayni al-Asir (1817–1889 CE), of a Sidonian family known by the epithet "al-Asir". He grew up in Sidon and memorised the Qur'an, then went to Damascus in the year 1832 CE to seek knowledge {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

The museum classes him as a scholar and writer of the Sunni school. No other details are given in the data, and this needs an additional source.
