## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Khalaf al-Barbahari, a Hanbali jurist and hadith scholar. He is attributed to al-Barbahar, medicines brought from India. He was born in 233 AH (847 CE) and died in 329 AH (941 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
