## Summary
Hüseyin Pasha, nicknamed 'Mezzomorto' — 'half-dead' in Italian — one of the Ottoman sea commanders {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 3, 'Hüseyin Paşa (Mezemorta)'}.

Of the derya beys, he captained the galleons then became beylerbey of Algiers; appointed kapudan-ı derya in 1101/1689–90, dismissed soon after, and restored to the post a second time in Muharram 1107/August 1695, dying in office on 14 Safar 1113/21 July 1701. The Sicill-i Osmani calls him 'courageous and zealous'. His son was Said Bey and a daughter of his lies buried on the Kashgar road {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 3, 'Hüseyin Paşa (Mezemorta)'}.
