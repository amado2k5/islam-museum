## Overview
Qabiha, mother of al-Mu'tazz, a Roman-born umm walad. According to al-Suyuti in "Tarikh al-Khulafa", she withheld money from her son that he meant to spend on the Turks, and they deposed him. She then disappeared, reappeared in Ramadan, and gave Salih ibn Wasif a great sum. He took all of it and banished her to Mecca until al-Mu'tamid came to power and returned her. She died in 264 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.557-559}

## al-Mas'udi's account
al-Mas'udi reports that on the night of al-Mutawakkil's killing she sent him a robe of honour that contained a red shirt and a silk mantle. He tore the mantle and told her servant to keep it, so that it might serve as his shroud. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.506}
