## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Umar ibn al-Husayn ibn Abd Allah ibn Ahmad al-Kharaqi, a Hanbali jurist and author of the well-known "Mukhtasar" on Hanbali jurisprudence. He died in 334 AH (945 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
