## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Ruhuni of Morocco, a Maliki jurist and theologian who was the reference for fatwas in Morocco. His works include a gloss on al-Zurqani's commentary on Khalil's Mukhtasar in law, and al-Tahsin wa-al-mana'a, against those who hold that the sunna is an innovation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1625}
