## Summary

Chief judge Baha al-Din Abd al-Barr ibn Baha al-Din Ibn al-Shihna al-Hanafi al-Halabi. He held the judgeship of Aleppo, and was then moved to the Hanafi judgeship of Cairo {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 470}.

He was among the companions and the wits of the sultan Qansuh al-Ghuri, to the point that some poets mocked him in verse. He loved learning and scholars {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 470}.

He died in the year 921 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 470}.
