## Summary
Dawud al-Qaysari, one of the Ottoman scholars trained in Egypt — in al-Sallabi's account the man to whom Sultan Orhan entrusted the first Ottoman madrasa, founded in Nicomedia after its fall in 727 AH {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 52}.
