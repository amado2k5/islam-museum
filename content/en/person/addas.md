Addas of Nineveh, a Christian servant boy working in the orchard of Utba and Shayba, sons of Rabi'a, in al-Ta'if; his masters sent him with a cluster of grapes to the Prophet when they saw the hardship and harm he had met from Thaqif. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When he set the grapes before the Prophet he said "in God's name" and ate; Addas marvelled and said: this is not a saying the people of this land say. The Prophet asked him about his land and his religion; he said: a Christian of the people of Nineveh. The Prophet said: "from the village of the good man Yunus ibn Matta — that was my brother; he was a prophet and I am a prophet". Addas fell to kissing his head, hands and feet, and became Muslim. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
