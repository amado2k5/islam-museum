# Bahaeddin Şakir

Bahaeddin Şakir (1874–1922) was an Ottoman physician who became one of the most powerful organisers of the Committee of Union and Progress, head of the political section of the wartime Special Organization, and — by the account the articles carry — one of the architects of the 1915 deportations and massacres of Ottoman Armenians, for which an Istanbul court martial sentenced him to death in absentia. {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-en}

## Formation

He was born in 1874 at Sliven (İslimiye) in Ottoman Bulgaria, into a Circassian family; his father was Mehmed Şakir Bey. After the Military Medical Academy in Istanbul, where he specialised in histology, he was exiled to Damascus for Unionist activity, escaped to Paris in 1899 and worked with Ahmed Rıza's Young Turk circle, then returned after the 1908 revolution. The Turkish article names him the founder of forensic medicine in Turkey and the author of its first copyrighted textbook in the field. {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-en} {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-tr}

## Unionist career

Şakir sat on the CUP's central committee, wrote for and directed Şûrâ-yı Ümmet, organised the party's provincial branches, and during the First World War headed the political section of the Teşkilât-ı Mahsusa (Special Organization). The English article quotes his letter of 3 March 1915 to a provincial official and describes, citing Dadrian, Kieser and other scholars, his role in ordering and supervising the deportations in the eastern provinces and his work to keep the Unionist network alive after the 1918 armistice, through the Karakol organisation in Istanbul and exile in Berlin. {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-en}

## Death

On 17 April 1922, on Uhlandstraße in Charlottenburg, Berlin, Armenian operatives Arshavir Shirakian and Aram Yerganian shot him dead, together with Cemal Azmi, under the Armenian Revolutionary Federation's Operation Nemesis. He was buried in the cemetery of Berlin's Şehitlik Mosque. {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-en} {cite:wikipedia-bahaeddin-sakir-tr}
