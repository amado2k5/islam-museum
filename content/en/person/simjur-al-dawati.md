## Overview
Simjur al-Dawati, a Samanid commander, and the father of the Simjurid family, who held Khurasan for the Samanids. Ibn al-Athir mentions him among the commanders of the army that marched to conquer Sistan in the year 298 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.46}.
