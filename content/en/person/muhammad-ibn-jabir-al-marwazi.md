## Summary
Muhammad ibn Jabir ibn Hammad, Abu Abd Allah al-Marwazi, a memorizer and jurist, who heard from Hudba ibn Khalid, Ali ibn al-Madini, Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu Mus'ab al-Zuhri, Ishaq ibn Rahwayh and Ahmad ibn Salih; al-Bukhari narrated from him in his Tarikh, and Ibn Khuzayma and Abu al-Abbas al-Mahbubi narrated from him. Al-Hakim called him one of the imams of his time; al-Dhahabi says he compiled, wrote and excelled {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13762}. The book gives his death as 279 AH.
