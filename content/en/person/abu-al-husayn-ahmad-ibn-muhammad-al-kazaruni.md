## Overview
Abu al-Husayn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Kazaruni took the science of inheritance from [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-labban-al-basri|Ibn al-Laban]]. Al-Shirazi says that in his time there was no more skilled or more calculating man in inheritance. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63} Al-Shirazi does not mention his death.
