## Overview

Abu al-Hasan Tahir ibn 'Abd al-Mun'im ibn 'Ubayd Allah ibn Ghalbun al-Halabi settled in Egypt. His father was the imam and master Abu al-Tayyib [[person/abd-al-munim-ibn-ghulbun|'Abd al-Mun'im ibn Ghalbun]]. He died in Egypt on the tenth of Dhu al-Qa'da 399 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 82}

## His book

He is the author of al-Tadhkira fi al-Qira'at al-Thaman, one of the books on which Ibn al-Jazari drew for al-Nashr; Ibn al-Jazari gives his own chain to it, by recitation from his teacher Ibn al-Sa'igh in Egypt back to the author, and calls it a sound, high chain linked by the exact Egyptian masters. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 82}
