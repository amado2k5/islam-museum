## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Ibrahim al-Ghafiqi al-Mallahi, Abu al-Qasim, was a historian and one of the memorisers of hadith. He was an Andalusian from the village of al-Malaha, a postal stage from Granada. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 549 to 619 AH, 1154 to 1222 CE, and says he died at Granada {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books a history of the scholars of Elvira, their lineages and their news; al-Shajara, on the genealogies of the nations of Arabs and non-Arabs; a book of forty hadiths; a supplement to al-Isti'ab on the Companions; and Fada'il al-Qur'an, in manuscript at Alexandria, where he is known as al-Gharnati. He also wrote al-Majalis, on the merits of the four caliphs {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
