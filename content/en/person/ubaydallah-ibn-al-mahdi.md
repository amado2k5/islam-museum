## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn al-Mahdi al-Abbasi. al-Rashid granted him the sharecropping of land in al-Ahwaz, and a doubt entered into it, so some people referred the matter to al-Ma'mun {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The doubtful land
al-Ma'mun ordered that the matter be examined. What was free of doubt he confirmed, and what was doubted was called "the doubtful" (al-mashkuk fihi), and this is known in al-Ahwaz {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
