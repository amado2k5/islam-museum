## Summary
A Companion of Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr; he emigrated to Abyssinia and arrived in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him alone of Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr in the two ships, tracing him to Abd Qays ibn Luqayt. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} He says some of the Muslim women whose husbands perished there were carried back with them in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-al-harith|Banu al-Harith ibn Fihr]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
