## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Tufayl, an Andalusian philosopher. He died in 581 AH, which corresponds to 1185 CE. {cite:al-aalam p.937}

## His books
Al-Zirikli names among his books the published treatise *Hayy ibn Yaqzan* and the published *Asrar al-Hikma al-Mashriqiyya* (Secrets of Eastern Wisdom). {cite:al-aalam p.937} See [[work/hayy-ibn-yaqzan|Hayy ibn Yaqzan]].

His birth and his teachers are not recorded in the data. They need an additional source.
