## Overview
Ibn al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam and of Asma bint Abi Bakr. The people of Mecca and Iraq gave him their allegiance as caliph; he went through the strife of al-Harra and the rebuilding of the Ka'ba. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## The siege of Mecca
[[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj]] besieged him in Mecca for fifty nights from Dhu al-Qa'da in 72 AH, until he was killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## His speech after the killing of Mus'ab
When his brother [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Mus'ab]] was killed, he went up to the pulpit and said that the news had grieved him and gladdened him at once, and that his family do not die in their beds but by spears and under the shade of swords. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}
