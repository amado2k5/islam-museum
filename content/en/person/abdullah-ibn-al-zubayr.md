## Overview
Ibn al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam and of Asma bint Abi Bakr. The people of Mecca and Iraq gave him their allegiance as caliph; he went through the strife of al-Harra and the rebuilding of the Ka'ba. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## The siege of Mecca
[[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj]] besieged him in Mecca for fifty nights from Dhu al-Qa'da in 72 AH, until he was killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## His speech after the killing of Mus'ab
When his brother [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Mus'ab]] was killed, he went up to the pulpit and said that the news had grieved him and gladdened him at once, and that his family do not die in their beds but by spears and under the shade of swords. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 269–270}

- **Judgeship of Basra:** Hisham ibn Hubayra ibn Fadala al-Laythi {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 269}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** Sa'id ibn Nimran al-Hamdani (appointed by Mus'ab when Shurayh withdrew; then dismissed); then [[person/abdallah-ibn-utba-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Utba ibn Mas'ud]] (until Mus'ab was killed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 269}
- **Chamberlain:** Abd Allah ibn Sa'd, mawla of Hatib ibn Abi Balta'a {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 270}
- **Over all his affairs:** Abd Allah ibn Safwan ibn Umayya ibn Khalaf {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 270}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
