## Overview
Imran ibn al-Husayn, known by the kunya Abu Najid al-Khuza'i, one of the Companions of the Messenger of God. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## To Basra
Al-Baladhuri says that Umar sent him with Abu Musa al-Ash'ari to Basra, among the Ansar who were sent to help him, when al-Mughira was removed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
