## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Yaqut ibn Abd Allah al-Musta'simi al-Rumi, Jamal al-Din al-Hamawi al-Baghdadi. A geographer and lexicographer, author of the "Mu'jam al-Buldan" (Dictionary of Countries) and the "Mu'jam al-Udaba'" (Dictionary of Men of Letters) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Life
The encyclopedia gives his birth as 575 AH and his death as 626 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
