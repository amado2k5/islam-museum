## Summary
Abu Na'ila al-Ansari — his name Silkan ibn Salama ibn Waqsh ibn Zaghba ibn Za'ura' ibn Abd al-Ashhal, the Awsi Ashhali, brother of Salama ibn Waqsh; it was also said his name was Sa'd and Silkan a nickname, though he is famous under his kunya. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1778}

## His record
He is mentioned in the Sahih in the affair of the killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf, being among those sent against him; he witnessed Uhud and other battles, and was a poet and counted among the noted archers. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1778}
