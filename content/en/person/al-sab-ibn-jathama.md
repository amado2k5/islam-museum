## Summary
Al-Sa'b ibn Jathama ibn Qays ibn Rabi'a al-Laythi, a Companion known for his bravery. He took part in the battles of the Prophet's time and was present at the conquests of Istakhr and Fars. The book reports a hadith in which the Prophet praises his courage at Hunayn, and says he has narrations in the Sahih collections. It gives his death as about 25 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6806}
