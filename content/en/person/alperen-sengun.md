Alperen Şengün (born 25 July 2002 in Giresun) is a Turkish basketball player, the Houston Rockets centre drafted 16th overall in 2021 after rising through Banvit and Beşiktaş, and a Turkish international named an NBA All-Star for the first time in 2025.{cite:wikipedia-alperen-sengun-en}{cite:wikidata-alperen-sengun}
