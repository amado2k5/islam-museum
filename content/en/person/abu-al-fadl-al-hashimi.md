## Summary
Abu al-Fadl al-Hashimi. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 537, sequence 538, on page 164. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}

## A bad concern, and hidden graces
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn relates from Abu Ja'far al-Razi that he heard Zakariya ibn Dulawayh say: Abu al-Abbas ibn Masruq al-Tusi entered upon Abu al-Fadl al-Hashimi while he was ill and had a family and no known means of living. When Abu al-Abbas rose to leave, he asked himself how this man would eat. Abu al-Fadl cried out: Abu al-Abbas, return this bad concern, for God has hidden graces. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}
