## Overview
Abu Hudhayfa Ishaq ibn Bishr, among the compilers of the sira and early events; his books include al-Mubtada', the Ridda, the Camel, the Conquests, the Digging of Zamzam, the Standards, and Siffin. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 139}
