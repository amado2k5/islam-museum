## Overview
Abu Hudhayfa Ishaq ibn Bishr, one of the writers of biographies and events. Ibn al-Nadim lists books of his, among them *al-Mubtada'* (The Beginning), *al-Ridda* (The Apostasy), *al-Jamal* (The Camel), *al-Futuh* (The Conquests), *Hafr Zamzam* (The Digging of Zamzam), *al-Alwiya* (The Banners) and *Siffin*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.294}
