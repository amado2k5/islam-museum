## Overview
Sarma ibn Abi Anas, a poet of the Banu Adi ibn al-Najjar, who has a poem on the Prophet's arrival in Medina {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.578}.

## His poem
al-Mas'udi gives its opening line: "He dwelt among Quraysh for some fifteen years". Some of its verses mention the Ansar giving up their wealth and their lives in battle {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.578}.
