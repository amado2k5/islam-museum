## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm al-Ansari, placed in the second generation of writers on the maghazi in the survey of Ahmad Amin. {cite:duha-islam p.642-643}

## Lineage and family
His great-grandfather [[person/amr-ibn-hazm|Amr ibn Hazm]] was one of the great Companions, whom the Prophet sent to the people of Yemen to teach them the religion. His father Abu Bakr was judge of Medina for [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]], and Umar wrote to him asking for the collection of hadith. {cite:duha-islam p.642-643}

## His influence on the maghazi
He married Fatima, who transmitted from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman from Aisha, so that many hadiths connected with the expeditions were made easy for him to collect. Many reports were transmitted from him, which [[person/ibn-ishaq|Ibn Ishaq]], [[person/al-waqidi|al-Waqidi]], Ibn Sa'd and [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] mention, and he had a great influence on the books of biography and the expeditions. {cite:duha-islam p.642-643}

Ahmad Amin's table gives his death as 135 AH. {cite:duha-islam p.639} This date rests on a single source and needs a second source.
