## Overview
Al-Muqtadir billah, an Abbasid caliph. He was given the allegiance in 295 AH, was deposed in 296 AH, was restored to the caliphate in 317 AH, and was killed in 320 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.28} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.152}

## Lineage and allegiance
According to al-Suyuti he was born in Ramadan 282 AH, and his mother was a Byzantine woman called Shaghab. No caliph before him came to the caliphate younger than he, as he took it at the age of thirteen. Al-Dhahabi says that the order of the state was greatly disrupted in his days because of his youth. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.585-589}

According to al-Mas'udi he was Ja'far ibn Ahmad al-Muktafi, with the kunya Abu al-Fadl. He was given the allegiance on Sunday, thirteen nights into Dhu al-Qa'da 295 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.648-649}

## Deposition and return
Ibn al-Athir relates that in the deposition of 317 AH he, his mother, his maternal aunt, the chief of his female attendants and his children were taken out of the caliphal palace to the house of Mu'nis. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.149-150}

Ibn al-Jawzi says that he knows of no caliph who was deposed and then restored except two: al-Amin and al-Muqtadir. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.351}

## Viziers and policy
Al-Mas'udi lists his viziers, among them Ali ibn Muhammad ibn al-Furat, who was appointed vizier three times, Ali ibn Isa ibn al-Jarrah, and Abu Ali ibn Muqla. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.660-661}

Ibn al-Jawzi says that in 302 AH he ordered that no one among the Jews and Christians be employed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.351}

## His killing
He was killed in Baghdad after the afternoon prayer on Wednesday, three nights before the end of Shawwal 320 AH, at the age of thirty-eight. In al-Mas'udi's reckoning his caliphate lasted twenty-four years, eleven months and sixteen days. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.648-649}
