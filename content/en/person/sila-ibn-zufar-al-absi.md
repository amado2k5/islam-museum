## Overview
Silat ibn Zufar al-Absi. al-Baladhuri reports that he was with [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] in Armenia. When [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] ordered in his letter for Hudhayfa's withdrawal that Silat should be left there in his place, he left him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
