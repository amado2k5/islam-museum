## Overview
Muhammad Rashid ibn Ali Rida, a scholar and journalist, one of the pioneers of Islamic reform, a student of Shaykh [[person/muhammad-abduh|Muhammad Abduh]], and author of a two-volume book on the life of his teacher. In his commentary on "Hadir al-Alam al-Islami", Shakib Arslan describes him as the Grand Master, praising the breadth of his learning and his ability to connect the Shari'a to contemporary events {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}.

This description comes from a contemporary and friend, so it is not neutral. An additional source is needed for discussion of his works and dates.
