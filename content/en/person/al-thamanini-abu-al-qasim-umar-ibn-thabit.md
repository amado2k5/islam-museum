## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Umar ibn Thabit al-Thamanini, a blind grammarian and man of letters, from Thamanin, a town in the island of Ibn Umar in the land of Mosul. He died in 442 AH (1051 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.845}
