# Noussair Mazraoui

## Summary

Noussair Mazraoui is a Moroccan professional footballer, born in Leiderdorp, Netherlands, on 14 November 1997 to Moroccan parents from Tétouan. A defender who came through the Ajax academy, he played for Bayern Munich before joining Manchester United in 2024. For Morocco he has made more than fifty appearances, reached the semi-finals of the 2022 World Cup — the first African side to get that far — and won the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. {cite:wikipedia-noussair-mazraoui-en} {cite:wikipedia-noussair-mazraoui-ar} {cite:wikidata-noussair-mazraoui}

## Career

Mazraoui rose through Ajax's academy to the first team, winning Dutch titles before moving to Bayern Munich in 2022. After two seasons in Germany he signed for Manchester United in August 2024, making his Premier League debut days later; that October he underwent a minor heart procedure after experiencing palpitations, and in December 2024 the club dropped a planned show of support involving a rainbow jacket after he declined to wear one for religious reasons. {cite:wikipedia-noussair-mazraoui-en}

Born in the Netherlands, he chose Morocco at senior level and debuted in September 2018. He fell out with coach Vahid Halilhodžić and refused a call-up in March 2022, then returned under Walid Regragui for the 2022 World Cup, where he played a large part in Morocco's run to the semi-finals. {cite:wikipedia-noussair-mazraoui-en}
