## Overview
Sulayman ibn al-Mughira, the imam, hafiz and exemplar Abu Sa'id al-Qaysi of Basra, client of Banu Qays ibn Tha'laba of Bakr ibn Wa'il. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1923}

## Transmission
He narrated from al-Hasan al-Basri, Muhammad ibn Sirin, Humayd ibn Hilal, Thabit ibn Aslam, Abu Musa al-Hilali and his father al-Mughira; al-Thawri, Abu Usama, Abu Dawud, Ibn Mahdi, Yahya ibn Adam, Muslim ibn Ibrahim and others narrated from him. He died in 165 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1923}
