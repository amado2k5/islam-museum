## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhaysin al-Sahmi, Abu Yahya, reader of Mecca after Ibn Kathir and its best-informed Arabist in grammar; the book dates him to the 2nd century of the Hijra (d. 123 H as printed), with no more detail on his birth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13343}
