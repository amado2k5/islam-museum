# Naheed Nenshi

## Summary

Naheed Kurban Nenshi was born in Toronto on 2 February 1972 to parents who had immigrated from Tanzania in 1971, and he was raised in Calgary.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
Nenshi served three consecutive terms as mayor of Calgary, from 2010 to 2021, becoming the first Muslim to lead a major Canadian city.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
The Ismaili, the community's official site, reported his 2010 election under the title "Ismaili Muslim elected mayor of the third-largest city in Canada", and his biography at Toronto Metropolitan University states that his Ismaili Muslim faith instilled in him the ethic of seva, or service to the community.{cite:the-ismaili-nenshi-2010}{cite:tmu-nenshi-bio}
Nenshi became leader of the Alberta New Democratic Party in 2024 and Leader of the Opposition in Alberta's Legislative Assembly in 2025.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}

## Career

Nenshi earned a commerce degree from the University of Calgary, where he served as students' union president, and a Master of Public Policy from Harvard Kennedy School in 1998.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
He worked as a consultant with McKinsey, founded the Ascend Group consultancy, taught non-profit management at Mount Royal University, and wrote a column for the Calgary Herald.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
Nenshi won Calgary's 2010 mayoral election with 39 per cent of the vote in a campaign known as the "Purple Revolution".{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
He was re-elected in 2013 with 74 per cent of the vote and in 2017 with 51 per cent.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
His leadership of Calgary's response to the 2013 southern Alberta floods was widely praised, as the article recounts.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
Nenshi won the 2014 World Mayor Prize from the City Mayors Foundation, the first Canadian to receive it.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
In 2011 he became the first Calgary mayor to serve as grand marshal of the city's Pride parade.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
He did not seek re-election in 2021 and was succeeded as mayor by Jyoti Gondek.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
On 22 June 2024 Nenshi won the leadership of the Alberta NDP with 86 per cent of the vote, 62,746 ballots.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
He won the Edmonton-Strathcona by-election on 23 June 2025 and became Leader of the Opposition in Alberta on 12 July 2025.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}

## Works

Nenshi founded the Ascend Group, a consultancy.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
He taught non-profit management at Mount Royal University's Bissett School of Business in Calgary.{cite:wikipedia-naheed-nenshi-en}
After his mayoralty he joined Toronto Metropolitan University's Bridging Divides programme as an advisor, and his biography there credits his Ismaili Muslim faith with instilling in him "the ethic of seva, or service to the community".{cite:tmu-nenshi-bio}
