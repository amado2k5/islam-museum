## Summary
Yahya ibn Manda, the Isfahani hafiz and Hanbali, author of the *Tarikh Isfahan*. Baghdad heard from him when he came on hajj in his old age — he dictated and related there, and Ibn Nasir, 'Abd al-Wahhab al-Anmati, Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jili, Ibn al-Khashshab and the hafiz al-Salafi heard from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 511-512}

## On him
Ibn al-Sam'ani said: high in rank, rich in merit, wide in transmission, a reliable abundant hafiz, truthful, prolific in composition, of good conduct — his house produced a whole house in its age. He composed the *Manaqib al-'Abbas* and *Manaqib Ahmad*, each in a large volume. He died in Dhu'l-Hijja 511 at seventy-four. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 511-512}
