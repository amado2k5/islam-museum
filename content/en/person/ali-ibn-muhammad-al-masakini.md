## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Khalifa al-Sharif al-Masakini, a jurist and educator from Tunis, who founded a school at the end of the Husaynid state. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His dates
The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his birth as 1080 AH (around 1669 CE) and his death as 1172 AH (around 1758 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
