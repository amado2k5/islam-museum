## Overview
Ahmad b. 'Arus of the Maghrib, of Tunis; al-Manawi says in Tabaqat al-awliya' he was among Tunis's greatest saints of the jadhb folk — manifest wonders, dazzling states: wild birds would alight on him and eat from his hand, and when a large company of the poor was with him he would stretch his hands into the air and bring them provision enough; greatly dreaded — not everyone could meet him, the body shivering at the sight of him — he sat on the roof of a funduq in Tunis night and day, and so remained until he died around 871. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 311}
