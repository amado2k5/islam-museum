## Summary
Ibn al-Najjar, the great hafiz, author of the Tarikh Baghdad; born 578, vast of memory and perfect in knowledge of the art — al-Mustansir appointed him with Abu Mansur al-Harimi the two shaykhs of the Mustansiriyya's dar al-hadith, busied with the science of hadith. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 235}
