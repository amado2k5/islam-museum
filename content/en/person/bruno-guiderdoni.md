## Summary
Bruno Abd al Haqq Guiderdoni is a French astrophysicist, research director at the French National Centre for Scientific Research ⁦(CNRS)⁩ and former director of the Lyon Observatory. The Muslim 500 lists him in Science & Technology for research on galaxy formation and the early universe and for being a prominent voice on science and Islam — he converted to Islam in 1987 and writes under the name Abd al Haqq Guiderdoni. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## Career in astrophysics
Guiderdoni studied at the École Normale Supérieure in Paris and built his career in observational and theoretical cosmology at the Centre de Recherche Astrophysique de Lyon, which he directed from 2005 to 2015. His research concerns the formation and evolution of galaxies — how the diffuse matter of the early universe condensed into the galaxies we observe — including the physics of star formation and dust in distant galaxies observed at infrared and submillimetre wavelengths. {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## A scientist who converted
Guiderdoni embraced Islam in 1987, taking the name Abd al Haqq — "servant of the Truth". He has been open about the conversion in lectures and interviews, describing a journey that passed through study of religious traditions before settling on Islam. The conversion did not interrupt his science; it ran alongside it, and he has spent decades lecturing on the relationship between Islamic thought and modern cosmology. {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni}

## Writing on Islam and science
His public work on Islam includes lectures, essays and television appearances in France and the Muslim world arguing that modern cosmology and Qur'anic themes can be read together, and writing on Islamic spirituality — work that has made him one of the most visible scientist-converts in the francophone Muslim world. The museum reports this as his own attributed position, described, not endorsed. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## Recognition
Within astrophysics his record includes leadership of a major French observatory and contributions to the study of galaxy evolution used by international surveys; within the Muslim world he is a sought-after speaker on science and faith, which is the ground of his Muslim 500 listing. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## Significance
In the museum's record, Guiderdoni joins the line of Western scientists who embraced Islam and made the dialogue between their science and their new faith a public project — an astrophysicist who maps galaxies by day and writes on cosmology and Islam by vocation. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## A convert's scientific Islam
Guiderdoni's public writing — in French journals, at interfaith gatherings, on French television — works a seam few scientists touch: the relation between a spiritual search and a cosmologist's rigour. He joined France's Buddhist-Christian-Taoist dialogue before his conversion, and after 1987 he brought the same comparative curiosity to Islam, becoming one of the few French voices that can speak about Sufi metaphysics and galaxy-formation simulations in the same lecture. The Muslim 500 lists him in science and technology for this combined profile: a working astrophysicist who is also a public intellectual of Islam in a country where that combination is rare. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni} {cite:wikipedia-bruno-guiderdoni}

## The convert's argument
Guiderdoni's record is the museum's example of the convert who brings his scientific training to his faith: the astrophysicist who argues that the universe's order is not evidence for God but evidence for a universe worth worshipping in, that the scientific and the mystical are not competitors but complements. The argument is his; the platform is the French interfaith circuit; and the result — a Muslim scientist who can speak to both the mosque and the academy — is the profile his Muslim 500 entry records. {cite:muslim500-bruno-guiderdoni}
