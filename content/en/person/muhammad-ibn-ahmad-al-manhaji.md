## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Khaliq, Shams al-Din, al-Asyuti then al-Qahiri, a Shafi'i jurist and man of letters, called al-Manhaji. Born in Asyut, he learned the Qur'an by heart under Sa'd al-Din al-Wajiyy, and studied fiqh with al-Zaki al-Maydumi and al-Badr ibn al-Khallal. The dates as printed are 880 to 813 H, given as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13367}
