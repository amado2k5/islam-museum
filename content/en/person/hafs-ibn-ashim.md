## Overview
Hafs ibn Ashim, whom Ibn al-Nadim counts among the Kharijites in the "Fihrist". He has a book on the sects and the refutation of them, which he transmitted from Jubayr ibn Aghab. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.259}.
