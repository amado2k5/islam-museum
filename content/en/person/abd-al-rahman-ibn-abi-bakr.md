## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr, son of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr al-Siddiq]]. He fought on the side of the polytheists at Badr, then accepted Islam and his Islam was sound. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 597}

## His descendants
Al-Mas'udi says that he had many descendants, nomads and settled, in the region of the Hijaz on the side of the road toward Iraq, in the place known as al-Safiniyyat and al-Masih. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 597}
