## Overview
Hisham ibn Amr al-Fuwati, Abu Muhammad, a Kufan and client of the Banu Shayban, was a Mu'tazili theologian. He belongs to the Mu'tazili school of kalam, not to Sunni Islam, and every judgement given here is attributed to the one who made it {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4094}. Al-Dhahabi calls him a man of intelligence, disputation, innovation and ruin {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4094}.

## Students
Abbad ibn Sulayman and others took from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4094}.

## Views reported of him
Al-Dhahabi reports that he forbade saying a well-known formula of trust in God taken from the Qur'an, and that he said God does not punish an unbeliever by the fire, nor give life to land by rain, nor guide nor lead astray; he explained that people are punished in the fire rather than by it, that land lives at the rain rather than by it, and that the sense of "al-wakil" is the one trusted in {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4094}.

## Anecdotes
Al-Mubarrad relates that a man asked him how many years he counted; he answered with a verbal quibble, from one to more than a thousand, and then said he was thirty-two years old, the two going on over the wording of "years" against "life lived" {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4095}. Al-Dhahabi commented that this is the utmost of knowledge these pedants have, and that they bend words from their places with it; he sought refuge in God from kalam and its people {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4095}. That is al-Dhahabi's judgement from the Sunni side; the museum gives it to him and rules on nothing.

## His death
The entry gives no year of death. Its editors refer to Tabaqat al-Mu'tazila and Ibn al-Nadim's al-Fihrist {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4095}.
