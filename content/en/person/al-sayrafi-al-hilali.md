## Overview
Abu Ali ibn Harb al-Sayrafi, one of the theologians of the Kharijites, who was a Hilali of the Banu Hilal, according to Ibn al-Nadim's "Fihrist". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.258}
