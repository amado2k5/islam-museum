## Summary

William Tanuwijaya (born 11 November 1981) is an Indonesian entrepreneur, the co-founder of Tokopedia, an Indonesian technology company with a leading e-commerce business; he represents Indonesia as a Young Global Leader at the World Economic Forum, and in 2019 received the Satyalancana Wira Karya medal from the President of the Republic of Indonesia for his contribution to Indonesia's technology and economic growth {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en} {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id}.

## Life

Tanuwijaya was born in Pematangsiantar, North Sumatra, on 11 November 1981, into a middle-income family whose father was an ordinary worker, and spent his childhood and teens in the city {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en} {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id} {cite:wikidata-william-tanuwijaya}. After high school he moved to Jakarta and studied at BINUS University; when his father was diagnosed with cancer in his second year he became the family's breadwinner, working twelve-hour night shifts as an internet-cafe operator for about three years until graduating in 2003 {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id} {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en}. He then worked as a game and software developer at companies including Telkomsigma and Sqiva Sistem, and as IT and business development manager at Indocom Mediatama from 2006, and later took executive education at the Harvard Kennedy School in 2017 {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en} {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id}. He married Felicia HW in November 2015 {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id}.

## Work

Tanuwijaya began building Tokopedia in 2007 and asked his friend Leontinus Alpha Edison to join him as co-founder; after two years of repeated rejections from traditional investors, seed funding came from his former employer, and Tokopedia officially launched on 6 February 2009, opening to the public on 17 August that year {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en} {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id}. The company received US$100 million from SoftBank and Sequoia Capital in October 2014 and a $1.1 billion investment led by Alibaba Group in August 2017 {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en}. He led Tokopedia as CEO until early 2023, then served as commissioner and co-chairman of the merged GoTo group until mid-2024 {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-id}. He is one of the founders of the Indonesia eCommerce Association (idEA) and sits on its supervisory board {cite:wikipedia-william-tanuwijaya-en}.
