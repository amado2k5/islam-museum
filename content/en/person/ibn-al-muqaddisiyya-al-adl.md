## Overview
Al-'Adl the Maliki, born at Sfax and settled at Alexandria (573–654 AH); his uncle the hafiz Ibn al-Mufaddal brought him the musalsal bil-awwaliyya reading at al-Silafi's and secured his ijaza, then had him hear Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Hadrami and others; Mansur extracted his mushaykha {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}.
