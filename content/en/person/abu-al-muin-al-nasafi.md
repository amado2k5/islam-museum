## Overview
Maymun ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ma'bad ibn Makhul, Abu al-Mu'in al-Nasafi al-Hanafi, was a scholar of legal theory and theology (kalam). Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 418 AH and his death as 508 AH, with 1027 and 1115 CE printed beside them; 508 AH runs across 1114 and 1115 CE on the usual tables (derived) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 341}.

## Life
He lived in Samarqand and settled in Bukhara {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 341}.

## Works
Among his books on kalam al-Zirikli names Bahr al-Kalam (printed) and Tabsirat al-Adilla (manuscript); also al-Tamhid li-Qawa'id al-Tawhid, al-'Umda fi Usul al-Din and al-'Alim wa-l-Muta'allim, all manuscripts, and Idah al-Mahajja li-Kawn al-'Aql Hujja. In Hanafi positive law he lists a commentary on al-Shaybani's al-Jami' al-Kabir, and Manahij al-A'imma on the branches of law {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 341}.
