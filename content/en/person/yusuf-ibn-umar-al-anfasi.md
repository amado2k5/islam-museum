## Overview
A Maliki jurist, imam of the Qarawiyyin mosque in Fez. He studied with Abd al-Rahman ibn Affan al-Jazuli and others, and his son Abu al-Rabi Sulayman studied with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5161}

## Works
His works include a gloss on the Risala of Abu Zayd al-Qayrawani. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5161}

## Dates
The source gives «(761 - 661 AH)», read as death then birth; the Gregorian conversion is derived.
