## Overview

[[person/ibn-bassal|Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Bassal al-Tulaytuli]] was an agronomic author working in Toledo and Seville. His book relates land, water and season to plants, presenting practical observations in an older vocabulary of natures. This is neither invention of farming nor a current laboratory classification {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal biography} {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha chs.1–2}.

## Work, gardens and travel

Ozel connects him with Toledo, al-Mamun's service, Ibn Wafid's milieu and the ruler's garden by the Tagus. The project treats service under Ibn Wafid and succession as director as probable, not an appointment established by a document in our hands. Association is narrower than a reconstructed complete apprenticeship {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal biography} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile biography}.

Through the Anonymous Botanist of Seville, the project reports his pilgrimage to the Hijaz, connecting travel with agricultural experience in Sicily, Egypt and Syria. More distant countries are partly tentative, not a verified itinerary. In Seville, he is associated with a garden for [[person/al-mutamid-ibn-abbad|al-Mutamid ibn Abbad]] and a milieu including Ibn Hajjaj, Abu al-Khayr, al-Tighnari and Ibn al-Lunquh {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile biography} {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal biography}.

The sources supply no certain birth/death dates. Ozel explicitly calls them unknown; the project offers approximations. They also differ about a move before or after1085. These limits are preserved, not replaced with years deduced from a ruler's name or a nearby political event {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal opening-and-biography} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile biography}.

## The book and its reasoning

The larger *Diwan al-filaha* was abridged as [[work/al-qasd-wa-al-bayan|Al-Qasd wa-l-bayan]], with sixteen chapters. Ozel uses its association with al-Mamun and the patron's1075 death to date the original earlier. That does not date every part of the abridgement to one day or establish survival of the entire original {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal work}.

His [[invention/ibn-bassal-soil-water-matching|linked land/water/crop assessment]] appears directly in chapters1–2: four water sources and ten practical land types, with seasonal change, air/water movement and cultivation. He stresses surface/subsurface differences and repeated examination. Hot/cold descriptions are not modern measurements; lack of earlier-author citations does not prove lack of inherited knowledge {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha chs.1–2} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile biography}.

The synopsis also covers planting, grafting, vegetables, flowers, wells and fruit preservation, not an encyclopaedia of all animal husbandry. This is a contents/synopsis map, not a claim that we read every original passage or that historical advice constitutes present-day safety guidance {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha contents} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile chs.5–16-synopsis}.

**Sunni frame:** Individual Muslim identity rests on the specifically reported pilgrimage through the botanist's testimony, not name or pilgrim epithet alone. Sunni is separately the declared museum default for the documented Andalusian milieu, with no contrary personal tradition in the sources read, not a certificate of a particular legal school {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile biography-and-ch.10-synopsis}.

## Reception and evidence limits

Ozel reports many quotations by Ibn al-Awwam, numbering273, a cited count rather than our own audit. Some are absent from the abridgement, making the larger work/later witnesses distinction important. The abridgement was translated into Castilian in the thirteenth century; translations are neither uniformly complete nor identical {cite:tdv-ibn-bassal reception} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile editions}.

An Arabic edition with Spanish translation appeared in1955 and was republished in1995 with study and indexes. The project discusses incomplete witnesses and the need for a broader critical edition. Its old announcement of a project scheduled for2011 is not treated as proof of publication. Our direct Arabic reading covers contents, two chapters and chapter10's opening, alongside biography/synopsis, not all manuscripts {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile editions-and-manuscripts} {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha contents-chs.1–2}.
