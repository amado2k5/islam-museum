## Overview
Yusuf al-Khwarizmi, master of a well-defended fortress on the Jayhun; he was brought as a captive to Sultan Alp Arslan on 6 Rabi' I 465. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.618-619}

## Killing Alp Arslan
The sultan cursed him and ordered four stakes driven for him and his limbs bound to them; Yusuf said "Effeminate one — is a man like me killed this way?" The sultan, enraged, took his bow and shot — and missed; Yusuf ran at him as he sat on the throne; Alp Arslan rose to descend, tripped and fell on his face, and Yusuf leapt on him and stabbed him in the flank — the wound that killed him. The soldiers caught Yusuf and killed him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.618-619}
