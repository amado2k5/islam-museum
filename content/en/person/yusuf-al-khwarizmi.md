## Overview
Yusuf al-Khwarizmi, brought before the sultan who confronted him with his ugly deeds and ordered him bound to four stakes; when the sultan loosed an arrow and missed, Yusuf charged at him — the episode of his arrest and punishment as Ibn al-Jawzi tells it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 16 p. 143}
