## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr al-Tasuli al-Maghribi al-Maliki, a Maliki jurist from the Maghrib, known as Ibn Abi Yahya. He died around 749 AH (1348 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.466}
