## Overview
Abu Bakr al-Mutawwi'i; he heard Ahmad b. Hanbal and 'Ali b. al-Madini, and al-Najjad and al-Khalidi transmitted from him. Ja'far al-Khalidi said: I heard Abu Bakr al-Mutawwi'i say, "my litany in my youth was to recite Qul huwa Allahu ahad thirty-one thousand times a day and night — or forty-one thousand." He died that year. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 187}
