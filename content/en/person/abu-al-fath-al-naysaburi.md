## Summary
Nasir ibn Sulayman ibn Nasir ibn Imran, Abu al-Fath al-Ansari al-Naysaburi, a Shafi'i scholar, hadith imam and skilled disputant in theology. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2053}

## Works
He heard from his father and others; Sa'd al-Sam'ani transmitted from him; his work includes a book in theology. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2053}
