## Overview
Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd al-Da'im al-Bazmawi, a scholar of jurisprudence, Arabic and inheritance. He settled as shaykh of the Fakhriyya madrasa in Cairo, and taught at the Salahiyya madrasa in Jerusalem. He wrote verse works and summaries on the principles of jurisprudence, inheritance and metre, and performed the pilgrimage in 828 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His dates of birth and death are not recorded in the museum's data and need an additional source.
