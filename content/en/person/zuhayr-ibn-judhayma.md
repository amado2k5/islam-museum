## Overview
Zuhayr ibn Judhayma ibn Rawaha al-Absi, lord of the Banu Abs in the pre-Islamic period. He and Khalid ibn Ja'far al-Kilabi struck at each other with their swords, and Zuhayr fell under Khalid, and Khandaj ibn al-Bukka' struck him and killed him {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.40}.

## The aftermath
Ghatafan were angered by his killing, and al-Harith ibn Zalim guaranteed them that he would kill Khalid, and he killed him by treachery {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.40}.
