## Overview
Abu Hafs Harmala ibn Yahya ibn Abd Allah ibn Harmala ibn Imran al-Tujibi, a Shafi'i jurist in Egypt. Al-Shirazi in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha" reports that he was a memoriser of hadith, and that he wrote "Al-Mabsut" and "Al-Mukhtasar". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.52}

He was born in 166 AH, and died in Egypt in 243 AH according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.52} These two dates come from one source, and need an additional source.
