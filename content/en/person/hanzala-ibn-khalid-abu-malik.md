## Overview
Hanzala ibn Khalid al-Tamimi, known as Abu Malik, from Qazvin, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in "The Conquests of the Lands". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The matter of Dastabi
Al-Baladhuri reports that he worked until all of Dastabi was assigned to Qazvin, after it had been divided between al-Rayy and Hamadhan. A man of his own town heard him say: "I gathered it, and I am Abu Malik." He answered him: "Rather, you have ruined it, and you are Abu Halik." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
