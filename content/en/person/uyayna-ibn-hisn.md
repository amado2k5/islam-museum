## Summary
Chief of Banu Fazara and Ghatafan in Najd; he led Ghatafan in the Trench campaign with the Confederates, then became Muslim in the conquest year — the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]] in the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## His positions
Uyayna was a brave man obeyed among his people, nicknamed "the fool obeyed among his people" for the size of his following despite his rashness; the Prophet singled him out for a great gift at al-Ji'rana to win over his people Fazara {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}. He apostatised with Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid in the Ridda wars, then was captured and brought to Abu Bakr at Medina, repented, became Muslim anew — and his Islam proved good — and took part in the conquests {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
A strong tribal chief of Najd whom thousands of horsemen followed; the Prophet gave him many camels to win his heart toward peace, and after the Ridda he repented and became one of the Muslims.
