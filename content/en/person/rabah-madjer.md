# Rabah Madjer

## Summary

Rabah Madjer is an Algerian retired footballer, born in Hussein Dey, Algiers in 1958, and widely regarded as one of the best Algerian players of all time. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en} A striker, he reached stardom with Porto in the 1980s, winning nine major titles including the 1987 European Cup. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en} He won 87 caps for Algeria, played at the 1982 and 1986 World Cups and won the 1990 Africa Cup of Nations, and later coached the national team in several spells. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en} The sources state no religion for him at all, so his data record leaves his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en}{cite:wikidata-rabah-madjer}

## Club career

Born in the Algiers district of Hussein Dey, Madjer began with NA Hussein Dey before moving to Racing Paris in 1983 and then to Porto in 1985. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en} In six years at Porto he won nine major titles, among them three Portuguese championships and the 1987 European Cup, with a loan spell at Valencia in 1988, and he closed his playing career at Qatar SC in 1991–92. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en}

## Algeria and coaching

Madjer played 87 times for Algeria, scoring 28 goals, and helped the team to its first World Cup in 1982 and to a second in 1986, and to the Africa Cup of Nations title on home soil in 1990. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en} After retiring he coached clubs in Portugal and Qatar and managed Algeria in several spells between 1993 and 2018. {cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-en}{cite:wikipedia-rabah-madjer-ar}
