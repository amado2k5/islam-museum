## Overview
Ridwan ibn Abd Allah al-Falaki was an Egyptian engineer and astronomer; the entry gives his death as 1123 AH, 1711 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.

## His life
He made the pilgrimage in 1091 AH and read with some of the scholars of the two Holy Sanctuaries {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names these books of his, all in manuscript: "Dustur Usul Ilm al-Miqat wa Natijat al-Nazar fi Tahrir al-Awqat", in his own hand, in the Azhariyya and in Dar al-Kutub, number 3833 with the letter kaf; "Bughyat al-Tullab fi Istikhraj al-A'mal al-Falakiyya bi al-Hisab", in Dar al-Kutub, number 4024 kaf; an astronomical almanac in his own hand, in Baghdad; a treatise on "Ma'rifat al-Ijtima' wa al-Istiqbal wa al-Kusuf wa al-Khusuf", in Dar al-Kutub, number 4019 kaf; "Zij Ridwan Afandi", in the same library, number 3985 kaf; and "al-Zij al-Mufid ala Usul al-Falak al-Samarqandi Sahib al-Rasad al-Jadid", a commentary on the astronomical principles of Ulugh Beg, also in Dar al-Kutub, number 3771 kaf {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.
