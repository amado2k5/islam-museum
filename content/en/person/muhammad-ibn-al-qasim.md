## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Thaqafi, conqueror of Sind in the caliphate of al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.101-102}

## The campaign
The book says that al-Hajjaj appointed him over the frontier of Sind and added to him six thousand men of the Syrian army. The expedition took Daybul, Nirun, al-Ruwar and Multan, and Dahir was killed in his battle on the River Mihran. It is related that the expenses of the campaign reached sixty million dirhams. Then Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman removed him and imprisoned him at Wasit, and the reports of his death differ. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.102-107}

Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Walid moved to Antioch a group of the Zutt of Sind, whom Muhammad ibn al-Qasim had sent to al-Hajjaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.221-222}
