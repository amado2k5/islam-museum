## Abd al-Jabbar ibn Umar al-Ayli

Abd al-Jabbar ibn Umar, Abu Umar, also called Abu al-Sabbah, al-Ayli of the Umayyad clients. He related from al-Zuhri, Ibn al-Munkadir, Nafi' the client of Ibn Umar, Rabi'a and Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari and others; Rashdin ibn Sa'd, Ibn al-Mubarak, Ibn Wahb and Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Maqri related from him. al-Duri reported from Ibn Ma'in that he was weak and of no account; Ibn Abi Hatim reported that Abu Zur'a called him trustworthy yet weak in hadith, but his legal answers are acceptable. The heading gives death after 260 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5502}
