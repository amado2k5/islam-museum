## Overview
Hanzala ibn al-Rabi' ibn Rabah al-Asadi, of the Banu Tamim, known as Hanzala the Secretary. Al-Baladhuri mentions him in the account of [[event/al-falalij-and-tustar-jaban|al-Falalij and Tustar]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The mission
Al-Baladhuri reports that Khalid sent him with al-Muthanna ibn Haritha to Jaban at Tustar, and Jaban fled when they reached him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
