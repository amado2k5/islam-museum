## Overview
Sultan Sharaf al-Dawla, the Buwayhid amir of Baghdad; he died in 416, his treasuries were plundered, Jalal al-Dawla Abu Tahir son of Baha' al-Dawla took the throne at Basra — then the troops turned to the king Abu Kalijar {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 204}.
