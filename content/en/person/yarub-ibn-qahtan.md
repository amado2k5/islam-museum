## Overview
Ya'rub ibn Qahtan ibn Abir ibn Shalikh ibn Arfakhshadh ibn Sam ibn Nuh, according to his lineage as al-Mas'udi gives it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 448}.

## The report
al-Mas'udi reports that he set out with those of his children and others who followed him, and settled in Yemen. He counts Ya'rub among those who spoke Arabic, together with Jurhum, 'Ad, 'Ubayl, Jadis, Thamud, 'Amaliq, Tasm, Wabar and 'Abd Dakhm {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 448}.

## Notes
- This report is transmitted from al-Mas'udi alone, and needs an additional source.
