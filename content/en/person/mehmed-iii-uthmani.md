## Overview
Sultan Mehmed III, son and successor of [[person/murad-iii-uthmani|Murad III]]. He was born in 974 AH, came to the sultanate in 1003 AH, and died in 1012 AH. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.295-296}

## Rule
He sat on the throne twelve days after his father's death, while he was in Manisa. His mother Safiye was of Italian origin. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.295-296}

Al-Salabi says that weakness began to affect the state. Seeing that sultans no longer led the armies themselves, he went out and took command of them, and marched to Belgrade and then to the battlefields. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.295-296}

Al-Salabi relates that he nearly fell captive in one of the battles after the conquest of the fortress of Igri, and that Shaykh Sa'd al-Din Efendi steadied him, so he took up his sword and victory came down. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.295-296}

## His character
Al-Salabi describes him as having a share of education and literature, very devout, and inclined to Sufism. He died on Sunday, 18 Rajab 1012 AH, after nine years, two months and two days of his rule. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.295-296}
