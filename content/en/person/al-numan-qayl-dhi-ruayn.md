## Overview
Al-Nu'man, the qayl of Dhi Ru'ayn, Mu'afir and Hamdan, one of the qayls of Himyar. The qayl, according to the researchers, is a king below the supreme king. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p. 235}

## The letter of Islam
He wrote with al-Harith and Nu'aym, the two sons of Abd Kulal, to the Prophet announcing their Islam, and the Prophet wrote to them. Ibn Hisham says this was at his coming back from Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p. 235} See [[event/letter-of-himyar-kings|the letter of the kings of Himyar]] and [[group/himyar|Himyar]].

## Notes for researchers
- The estimated date of the Islam is about 631 CE; it is approximate and attributed to the report.
- The pages are 235–237 of the fourth volume of Ibn Hisham's biography.
