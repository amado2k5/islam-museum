## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Jurbadhaqani, a philologist, man of letters and Shafi'i jurist, born in Jurbadhaqan. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.175}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 321 AH (around 933 CE) and his death as 382 AH (around 990 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.175}
