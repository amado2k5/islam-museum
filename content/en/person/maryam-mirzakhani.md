# Maryam Mirzakhani

Maryam Mirzakhani (12 May 1977 – 14 July 2017) was an Iranian mathematician, the first woman and the first Iranian to receive the Fields Medal, awarded in 2014 for her work on the dynamics and geometry of Riemann surfaces and their moduli spaces. The English article gives her birth as 12 May 1977, citing MacTutor, and the Persian article gives 22 Ordibehesht 1356, the same day; the Arabic and Urdu articles give 3 May 1977, and the death day is likewise split, 14 July against 15 July — both days are carried as debated on her data record. {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-fa} {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-ar} {cite:wikidata-mirzakhani}

## Early life and education

Born in Tehran, she was educated at Tehran's Farzanegan school for gifted students and won gold medals at the International Mathematical Olympiad in 1994 and 1995, with a perfect score in 1995. She took her bachelor's at Sharif University of Technology in 1999 and her doctorate at Harvard University in 2004, under Curtis McMullen. After a period at Princeton, she became a professor at Stanford University. {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-fa}

## Work and recognition

Her research concerned Teichmüller theory, hyperbolic geometry, ergodic theory and symplectic geometry; her results on the growth of the number of simple closed geodesics on hyperbolic surfaces are among its landmarks. Beyond the Fields Medal she received the Blumenthal Award, the Satter Prize and the Clay Research Award, and she was elected to the National Academy of Sciences in 2016. Her birthday, 12 May, is now marked as the International Day of Women in Mathematics. {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-en}

## Death and legacy

Diagnosed with breast cancer in 2013, she died at Stanford Hospital in California on 14 July 2017, aged forty. Iranian officials led by President Rouhani sent condolences, and several Iranian newspapers broke the customary rule by printing her photograph with her hair uncovered — a gesture the English article records without drawing any conclusion about her personal belief, and none is drawn here. {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mirzakhani-fa}
