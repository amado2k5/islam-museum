## Summary
Ahmad Zarruq: jurist and Sufi author, linked in the museum's record to the Maghreb. The Biblissima record places his life between 1442 and 1493. {cite:biblissima Q105540} The museum's record card names Qawa'id al-Tasawwuf as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Aḥmad ibn Aḥmad ibn Muḥammad ibn ʿĪsā al-Burunsī al-Fāsī Zarrūq» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q105540}
