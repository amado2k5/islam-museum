## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Isa al-Burnusi al-Fasi, Abu al-Abbas, known as Zarruq, was a jurist, hadith scholar and Sufi, of the people of Fes in the Maghreb. The entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 846 AH, 1442 CE, to 899 AH, 1493 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.

## His journeys and his Sufism
He studied jurisprudence in his own city and read in Egypt and Medina; Sufism came to dominate him, so he gave up worldly ties and travelled, and he died at Takrin, a village of Misrata in the district of Tripoli in the west. Al-Zirikli says he left many works in which he tends to brevity with careful verification, and that he was singular in the quality of his writing on Sufism {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Khalil in Maliki jurisprudence; al-Nasiha al-kafiya li-man khassahu Allah bi-l-afiya and al-Qawaid in Sufism, both printed; Ianat al-mutawajjih al-miskin ala tariq al-fath wa-l-tamkin, in manuscript, of which he says he acquired a copy written in 969 AH; several commentaries on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah, among them a manuscript commentary in the Rabat library; al-Hawadith wa-l-bida, a manuscript tract in the Rabat library; al-Janna li-l-mutasim min al-bida bi-l-sunna; al-Bida allati yafaluha fuqara al-sufiyya, in a hundred chapters; al-Kunnasha; a Rihla; and a printed commentary on the Risala of Abu Zayd al-Qayrawani in jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.

## The year of his death
Al-Zirikli mentions a manuscript by Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Ayyashi, al-Anwar al-saniyya ala al-wazifa al-Zarruqiyya, of which a careful Moroccan copy came into his hands. It introduces Zarruq as Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Isa al-Burnusi al-Fasi al-Shadhili, after his grandfather, and says he died in 896 AH. Al-Zirikli adds that this is a second report of the year of his death, given by some of his biographers too {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 84}.
