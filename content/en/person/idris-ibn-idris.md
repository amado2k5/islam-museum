## Overview
Idris ibn Idris, son of [[person/idris-ibn-abdallah|Idris ibn Abdallah]]. Al-Mas'udi says that he took his father's place after an agent of al-Mansur poisoned him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.285}

## Name and land
The land of the Maghrib became known by them, and it was said "the land of Idris ibn Idris". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.285}
