## Overview
Zain Bhikha (born 1974 in Pretoria, South Africa) is a nasheed singer who sings in English and Arabic. He is known for his nasheeds for children and families, such as "Nasheeds for Children" and "Allah Knows", and has made works with Yusuf Islam. {cite:wikidata}
