## Overview
Al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf al-Thaqafi, a governor for Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, as al-Tabari relates. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.338} He was the commander of the army at the battle of Dayr al-Jamajim with Ibn al-Ash'ath, and Ibn al-Ash'ath was defeated there. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.346} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.357}

## In Iraq
Al-Mas'udi relates his famous sermon on arriving in Iraq, and his address to the people of Iraq in it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.119} He says that he wrote to Abd al-Malik about the report of Ibn al-Ash'ath, and Abd al-Malik sent him armies. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.123} Then he preached in Kufa when the head of Ibn al-Ash'ath was brought to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.123}

He imprisoned al-Ghadban ibn al-Qabathari for three years and then released him after the affair of the green dome at Wasit. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.139}

## Building Wasit and his death
The Umayyad state of al-Salabi says that al-Hajjaj built Wasit as a military base between Ahwaz, Basra and Kufa, and that Abd al-Malik on his deathbed advised al-Walid to honour him. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.116}

Al-Mas'udi says he died in Wasit in 95 AH at the age of fifty-four, and that his governorship lasted twenty years. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.157}

## Accounts of the sources
Al-Mas'udi relates that he counted those he had killed by the sword, apart from those killed in his wars, and found them to be a hundred and twenty thousand. He relates that at his death there were fifty thousand men and thirty thousand women in his prison. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.157} This is an account from al-Mas'udi, and the museum does not adopt the figures. Verifying them needs an additional source.

Al-Salabi says that al-Walid made him commander of the pilgrimage in 92 AH, and that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz asked to be excused from passing through Medina, and al-Walid excused him. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.176}

Modern historians' and others' assessments of him are not set out here and still need to be added.

## For Sprouts
Al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf al-Thaqafi, governor for Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan; he led the army against Ibn al-Ash'ath at Dayr al-Jamajim, built Wasit, and died in 95 AH.

## For Explorers
Al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf al-Thaqafi, governor for Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan per al-Tabari; he led the army at Dayr al-Jamajim where Ibn al-Ash'ath was beaten. Al-Mas'udi relates his famous sermon on coming to Iraq and his address to its people; that he wrote Abd al-Malik about Ibn al-Ash'ath's affair and was reinforced with armies, then preached in Kufa when Ibn al-Ash'ath's head reached him; and that he imprisoned al-Ghadban ibn al-Qaba'thari three years before freeing him after the Green Dome affair at Wasit. The book of the Umayyad state says he built Wasit as a military base between Ahwaz, Basra and Kufa, and that Abd al-Malik, ill, charged al-Walid to honor him; al-Mas'udi has him dying at Wasit in 95 AH aged fifty-four, after twenty years in office.
