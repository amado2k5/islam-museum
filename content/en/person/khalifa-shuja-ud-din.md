Khalifa Shuja-ud-Din (1887–1955) was a Lahore lawyer and politician, honorary professor at Islamia College, Lahore, in 1906–08 and secretary of the Punjab Muslim League from 1919 to 1936.{cite:iqbal-review-malik-survey-1953} In April 1919, after the Amritsar massacre, he read a speech by Gandhi to a protest crowd of some 25,000–35,000 gathered in the courtyard of the Badshahi Mosque.{cite:wikipedia-badshahi-mosque-en}

When the Provincial Assembly of Punjab was re-established in 1951 he became its first Speaker, taking the chair on 7 May 1951.{cite:wikipedia-speaker-punjab-assembly-en} In 1955 he was appointed president of the Commission on Marriage and Family Laws; he chaired its first meeting on 5 October 1955 and died the next day, 6 October 1955, whereupon the retired Chief Justice Abdul Rashid was appointed in his place.{cite:fazle-omer-muslim-family-laws-thesis}
