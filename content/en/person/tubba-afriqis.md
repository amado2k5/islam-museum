## Overview
Afriqis ibn Qays al-Himyari, one of the Tubba' kings. al-Baladhuri, in Ansab al-Ashraf, reports that he raided Ifriqiya and conquered it, and that it was named after him {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.7-8}.

## The Berbers
He is reported to have heard the speech of the Amalaqa and said: "How many of them are barbarians", and so they were called the Barbar. The Banu Sanhaja and Kutama of Himyar remained with them {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.7-8}.

This is the report of a genealogist, and no date accompanies it. The entry needs an additional source.
