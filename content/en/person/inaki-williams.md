# Iñaki Williams

## Summary

Iñaki Williams Arthuer is a professional footballer, born in Bilbao, Spain, on 15 June 1994 to Ghanaian parents. A forward, he has spent his entire senior career at Athletic Bilbao, making more than 400 league appearances and winning the 2023–24 Copa del Rey. He played once for Spain in 2016, then switched his international allegiance to Ghana in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-inaki-williams-en} {cite:wikipedia-inaki-williams-ar} {cite:wikidata-inaki-williams}

## Early life and career

Williams's parents left Ghana and crossed the Sahara on foot before reaching Spain, entering by claiming refugee status; they settled in Bilbao, where Iñaki was born, and later in Pamplona, where he grew up. His Basque first name honours Iñaki, a volunteer with the Catholic charity Caritas in Bilbao who helped his parents after they arrived. He joined Athletic Bilbao's academy in 2012, rose through Basconia and Bilbao Athletic, and made his first-team debut in December 2014; he has remained at the club ever since, one of the most durable players in its modern history. {cite:wikipedia-inaki-williams-en}

He represented Spain at under-21 level and won one senior cap in 2016. In 2022 he switched to represent Ghana at international level, and was selected for the 2022 and 2026 World Cups. His younger brother Nico Williams is also an Athletic Bilbao forward. {cite:wikipedia-inaki-williams-en}
