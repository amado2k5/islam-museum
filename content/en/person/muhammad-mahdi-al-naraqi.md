## Overview
Mahdi ibn Abi Dharr al-Naraqi al-Kashani was, in al-Zirikli's words, an Imami jurist, that is, of the Twelver Shi'i school, which is distinct from Sunni Islam; the entry gives his death as 1209 AH, 1794 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 311}. It gives no year of birth.

## His books
His books include "Anis al-Mujtahidin" on legal theory, in manuscript; "Jami' al-Afkar" on proving the necessary Being, in manuscript; "Jami' al-Sa'adat fi Mujibat al-Naja" on ethics, in manuscript, which his son Ahmad (died 1245 AH) translated into Persian under the title "Mi'raj al-Sa'ada"; and "al-Tajrid," printed, on legal theory {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 311}.
