## Overview
Candarli Halil Pasha the Younger, Mehmed II's vizier during the siege of Constantinople, and the last grand vizier of the Candarli house.

## His stance on the siege
Al-Salabi reports him foremost among the counsellors who tried to persuade the sultan to give up the city and settle with its people {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 98}, urging withdrawal at the war council — no bloodshed, and beware Christian Europe's anger {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 105}.

## The accusation
The book says he stood accused of colluding with the Byzantines and dampening resolve — Zaganos Pasha answered him fiercely in council {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 105}. It is an accusation al-Salabi relays from the chronicles, not a settled verdict.
