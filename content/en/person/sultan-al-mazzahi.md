## Overview
Sultan ibn Ahmad ibn Salama ibn Isma'il al-Mazzahi, the Egyptian Shafi'i, was a scholar who became shaykh of Qur'anic recitation in Cairo. He was born in 985 AH, 1577 CE, and died in 1075 AH, 1664 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}. His name points to Minyat Mazzah, in the Daqahliyya province of Egypt; he studied and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names a gloss on the commentary on "al-Manhaj" by the judge Zakariyya, on jurisprudence, in manuscript at the Azhar library; a commentary on "al-Shama'il"; a work on the four readings beyond the ten, in manuscript at Riyadh, number 2543; "al-Jawhar al-Masun", in manuscript at the Baladiyya library, number 1778; and "Masa'il wa-Ajwibatuha", on tajwid, in manuscript at the Baladiyya, number 5265 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}. He adds that "perhaps" two further works are his: answers to questions on the readings that were put to him, in manuscript of thirty-two leaves at Dar al-Kutub al-Misriyya, and a treatise answering the twenty questions that some reciters had raised, in manuscript at Riyadh, number 2519 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 108}.
