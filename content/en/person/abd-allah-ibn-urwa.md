## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Urwa ibn al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam al-Asadi: a successor-generation orator and brave man, who lived from 30 to 126 AH (650–743 CE). He was likened to his grandfather Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr for his eloquence and boldness, and he composed verse. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.103}
