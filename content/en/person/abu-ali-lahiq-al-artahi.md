## Overview
Abu 'Ali Lahiq al-Artahi, the Hanafi — sole heir of al-Mubarak's ijaza; devout and self-restrained, the younger Zaki 'Abd al-'Azim transmitting from him; he heard from his great-uncle 'Abd Allah al-Artahi and died in Egypt in Jumada II 658 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 304}.
