## Overview
Subay'a bint al-Harith al-Aslamiyya, a woman Companion of [[group/aslam|Aslam]], was the wife of [[person/sad-ibn-khawla|Sa'd ibn Khawla]]. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912}

## Her hadith on the waiting period
Sa'd ibn Khawla died at Mecca, and a few nights after his death — twenty-five nights, or fewer, it is said — she gave birth. [[person/abu-al-sanabil-ibn-bacak|Abu al-Sanabil ibn Ba'kak]] told her that her term was four months and ten days, so she went to the Prophet and told him, and he said: "You are free; marry whom you wish" — or, in some versions, "When someone you approve of comes to you, marry." {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912} Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says that the jurists of Medina and of Kufa among the Successors transmitted this hadith of hers. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912} The editor notes that Muslim reports something similar from Subay'a herself and that al-Bukhari gives it without a full chain. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912} Her hadith is a foundation for the [[concept/iddah|waiting period]] of a pregnant widow.

## Her report on dying in Medina
[[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|'Abd Allah ibn 'Umar]] narrated from her that the Prophet said: "Whoever of you is able to die in Medina, let him do so, for no one dies there but that I shall be an intercessor or a witness for him on the Day of Resurrection." The editor rates its chain good (hasan), and notes that it is also soundly reported from Ibn 'Umar by another route without mention of Subay'a. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912}

## The question of her identity
Al-'Uqayli claimed that the Subay'a from whom 'Abd Allah ibn 'Umar narrated is a different woman; Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says: "That is not established in my view; God knows best." {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 912-913} He follows her with Subay'a bint Habib al-Dubay'iyya, a Basran from whom Thabit al-Bunani narrated her hadith on those who love one another for God. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 913}
