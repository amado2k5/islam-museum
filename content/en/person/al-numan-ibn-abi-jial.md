## Overview
From the [[group/banu-dubayb|Dubayb]] of Judham, one of those who embraced Islam under the writing of [[person/rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|Rifa'a ibn Zayd]]. He went with his people to al-Hunayd ibn Awz to recover the goods of [[person/dihya-al-kalbi|Dihya al-Kalbi]], and Qurra ibn Ashqar al-Dulay'i shot him with an arrow, which struck his knee. Al-Nu'man said: "Take it, I am the son of Lubna", and he was named that way. His father Abu Jiyal is the author of [[work/abu-jial-poem-on-the-judham-raid|the poem]] recited after the Prophet returned what had been taken from Judham. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/260} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/264}
