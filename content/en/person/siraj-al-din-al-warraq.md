## Overview
Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Hasan, Abu Hafs, Siraj al-Din al-Warraq, is called by al-Zirikli the poet of Egypt in his age. He gives his birth as 615 AH, 1219 CE, and his death as 695 AH, 1296 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 63}.

## Life
He served as secretary to the governor of Egypt, the emir Yusuf ibn Sabasalar, and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 63}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left a large diwan of poetry in seven volumes, from which al-Safadi made the selection Lum' al-Siraj, in manuscript. He also composed Nazm Durrat al-Ghawwas, in manuscript, and a commentary on it, in manuscript, held among the endowment libraries of Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 63}.
