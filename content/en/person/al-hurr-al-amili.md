## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Amili, called al-Hurr, was an Imami jurist and historian, that is, a Twelver Shi'i and not a Sunni. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1033 AH, 1623 CE, to 1104 AH, 1692 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His life
He was born in the village of Mashghar in Jabal Amil in Lebanon, moved to Jiba', and from there to Iraq, and ended at Tus in Khurasan, where he stayed and died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names: Amal al-amil fi dhikr ulama' Jabal Amil, of which the first part is printed, while the second, which he called Tadhkirat al-mutabahhirin fi tarjamat sa'ir al-ulama' al-muta'akhkhirin, is in manuscript; al-Jawahir al-saniyya fi al-ahadith al-qudsiyya (printed); Tafsil wasa'il al-Shi'a ila tahsil masa'il al-shari'a (printed), shortened to al-Wasa'il; Hidayat al-umma ila ahkam al-a'imma, in three parts; al-Fusul al-muhimma fi usul al-a'imma (printed); and treatises on various subjects {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His poetry
He wrote much verse. A diwan in his own hand, in manuscript, is in Najaf, with about twenty thousand lines {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## What was said of his works
Al-Zirikli reports al-Khwansari (in Rawdat al-jannat), who after listing the works says that although they are many they lack rigorous verification and need refining, revision and editing {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
