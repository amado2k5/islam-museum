## Overview
Sultan Murad V, son of [[person/abd-al-majid-i-uthmani|Abd al-Majid I]], born on 25 Rajab 1256 AH (1840 CE). He came to the caliphate on 7 Jumada al-Ula 1293 AH after his uncle [[person/abd-al-aziz-uthmani|Abd al-Aziz]] was deposed, but his rule lasted only ninety-three days. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 395}

## Character and deposition
Al-Salabi describes him as intelligent and cultured in both Turkish and Western culture, who visited Europe and inclined toward the constitution. He says that he suffered a nervous disorder after the night of his uncle's deposition, and the Shaykh al-Islam declared him deposed by a fatwa in 1876 CE. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 395}

## Afterwards
He recovered after his deposition, and spent the rest of his life in the palace of Cragan until he died at about sixty-four years of age. The year of his death in the data is approximate (around 1904 CE). {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 395}
