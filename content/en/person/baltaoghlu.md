## Overview
Baltaoglu, the Ottoman fleet commander at the siege of Constantinople in 857/1453.

## The sea fight
When European ships tried to reach the gulf, Mehmed sent him word: 'Take those ships or sink them — and if you fail, do not return to me alive.' His fleet could not stop them despite great efforts {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 97}.

## The dismissal
The angry sultan dismissed and berated him as a coward; Baltaoglu lifted his turban's edge from his wounded eye: 'I face death steadfastly — but it grieves me to die under such an accusation.' Mehmed saw the man had excuse enough, contented himself with the dismissal, and put Hamza Pasha in his place {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 97}.
