## Overview
Faraj ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abi al-Faraj al-Ardabili, a Shafi'i jurist, whom the source describes as combining transmitted and rational knowledge. He is attributed to [[place/ardabil|Ardabil]], and died in 749 AH (1348 CE). The date rests on one source and needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
