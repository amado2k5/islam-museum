## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hasan al-Jabarti (1167–1237 AH, around 1754–1822 CE) was an Egyptian historian who recorded the events of Egypt and the lives of its men in the age of the French expedition and after. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
