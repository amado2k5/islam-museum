## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib ibn Abi Ubayda ibn Uqba ibn Nafi al-Fihri. Al-Baladhuri relates that Abd Allah ibn al-Habhab sent him to raid Sus and the land of the Sudan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The raid on Sus
Al-Baladhuri says that he achieved a victory like which no one had seen, and took two slave-girls of the women of Tarjan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-sus-raid|the raid of Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib on Sus]].
