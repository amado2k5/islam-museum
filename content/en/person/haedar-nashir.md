## Summary
Haedar Nashir is chairman of Muhammadiyah, Indonesia's second-largest Muslim organisation with some thirty million followers, a position he has held since the organisation's 47th congress in Makassar in 2015, re-elected at the 48th congress in 2022. A sociologist by training, he leads a movement of schools, universities, hospitals and charities founded on Islamic modernism. {cite:wikipedia-haedar-nashir}

## Life and formation
He was born on 25 February 1958 in Bandung, West Java, and studied at the Muhammadiyah University of Malang and then sociology at Gadjah Mada University, Yogyakarta, where he earned his doctorate. His scholarship concerns Muhammadiyah itself — its ideology, its modernist reformism and its place in Indonesian civil society — and he has written widely on Islamic movements and modernisation. {cite:wikipedia-haedar-nashir}

## Organisational ascent
A Muhammadiyah cadre from youth, he edited the movement's magazine Suara Muhammadiyah, rose through its central board as secretary, then vice-chairman, before election as chairman in 2015. His leadership has emphasised educational expansion abroad — Muhammadiyah schools and colleges opened in several countries — alongside the movement's domestic network of thousands of institutions. {cite:wikipedia-haedar-nashir}

## Positions and debate
He positions Muhammadiyah as a moderate "middle path" for Indonesian Islam: engaged in welfare and education, formally non-partisan, wary of both religious conservatism and secular neglect. Supporters describe principled distance from power; critics inside and outside debate how far the movement stays neutral in Indonesian politics.
