## Overview
Mehmed Pasha Sokollu, Ottoman grand vizier. al-Salabi describes him as an outstanding vizier, a warrior and a capable statesman. Sultan Selim II entrusted him with the supreme command {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.277-293}.

## His works
According to al-Salabi, he restored the state's prestige and made a peace with Austria in the year 975 AH / 1567 CE. He prepared a great campaign in the years 976-977 AH to seize Astrakhan and to link the Volga and Don rivers by a canal suitable for the passage of ships. A third of it was dug before the winter stopped the work {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.277-293}.

## His death
He was killed in the time of Murad III, around the year 987 AH / 1579 CE, through the intrigues of the sultan's entourage. al-Salabi holds that his death was a severe blow, and that it opened a door to evil in the appointment and dismissal of grand viziers. The year is approximate in the data {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.293}.
