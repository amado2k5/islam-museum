## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Khuzayma al-Naysaburi, the imam of Nishapur in hadith. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.100}

## His stance on al-Tabari
Ibn al-Athir relates that al-Husayn ibn Ali al-Razi asked him why he did not write from Muhammad ibn Jarir al-Tabari, when the Hanbalis prevented people from entering his company. Ibn Khuzayma replied: "I wish you had not written from everyone you wrote from, and had heard from Abu Ja'far." {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.100}

He is also reported to have said, on reading al-Tabari's commentary: "I know of no one on the face of the earth more learned than Abu Ja'far, and the Hanbalis have wronged him." {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.100}
