## Overview
Qutb al-Din Mawdud ibn Zangi ibn Aqsunqur, lord of Mosul and the Jazira, brother of Nur al-Din Mahmud Zangi. In 551 AH his deputy seized Sultan Sulayman Shah ibn Muhammad ibn Malikshah and imprisoned him in Mosul; he later sent him to Hamadan to assume the sultanate under an agreement making Mawdud his atabeg. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.9 p.437}

## His death
He died in Mosul in Dhu'l-Hijja 565 AH at about forty, after a reign of twenty-one years and five and a half months. In his will he passed over his eldest son Imad al-Din Zangi in favour of his other son Sayf al-Din Ghazi, who succeeded him. Ibn al-Athir describes him as among the best of kings in conduct: he refrained from his subjects' wealth, was generous to them, beloved of high and low, while his servant Fakhr al-Din Abd al-Masih directed the affairs of his state. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.10 p.24-25}
