## Summary
Al-Fadl ibn Ziyad al-Qattan was among the closest companions of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal, and was one of those who related from him most often in the questions (masa'il) he answered. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.363}

Ya'qub ibn Sufyan al-Fasawi, al-Hasan ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Abi al-Anbar, Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Adami and Ja'far ibn Muhammad al-Sandali transmitted from him. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.363}

Abu Bakr al-Khallal said: al-Fadl ibn Ziyad was among the senior men in the eyes of Abu Abdallah (Ahmad). Abu Abdallah knew his worth and honoured him, and al-Fadl would lead Abu Abdallah in prayer. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.363}
