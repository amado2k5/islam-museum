## Overview
Usama ibn Murshid al-Kinani, whose title was Mu'ayyid al-Dawla. He died in the year 584 AH (the Common Era conversion is derived). Haji Khalifa attributes to him the book [[work/azhar-al-anhar|Azhar al-Anhar]] {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.372}.
