## Summary
Khwaharzada al-Kurdari, nephew of Shams al-A'imma 'Ala' al-Din al-Kurdari, author of the Bidaya; he read fiqh under his maternal uncle and took from him, and taught at the Balbisiiyya of Damascus; Ibn Kamal Pasha says he is called Badr al-Din Khwaharzada Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Hamid ibn 'Isa — and he transmitted from the author of the Hidaya, his father having been the brother of the Hidaya-author's mother. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 262-263}
