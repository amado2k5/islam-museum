## Overview
Al-Mujir, the imam Abu'l-Qasim Mahmud b. al-Mubarak al-Wasiti, later of Baghdad — Shafi'i jurist, one of the quick-witted debaters; born 517, he read fiqh at the Nizamiyya under Abu Mansur Ibn al-Razzaz among others, took kalam from Abu'l-Futuh Muhammad b. al-Fadl al-Isfara'ini, and became the one pointed out in his time, preferred over his peers. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 791}

## Teaching and embassy
He taught at the Nizamiyya — brilliant, persistent, diving for the heights; he came to Damascus where the Jarukh madrasa was built for him, then went to Shiraz where its king built him a madrasa; then Ibn al-Qassab summoned and advanced him. Ibn al-Dubaythi said, "we never saw anyone more comprehensive of the sciences, with such fine expression." He went out as an envoy to Khwarazmshah at Isfahan and died at Hamadan in Dhu'l-Qa'da 592. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 791}
