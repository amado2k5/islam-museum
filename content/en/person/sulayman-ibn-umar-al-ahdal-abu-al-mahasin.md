## Overview
Abu al-Mahasin Sulayman ibn Umar ibn Yahya ibn Umar ibn Maqbul al-Ahdal al-Zabidi, a Yemeni traditionist, known as the "traditionist of Yemen". He was born in Zabid in 1137 AH (1725 CE) and died in 1197 AH (1783 CE) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Works
Among his works is a commentary on a versified poem on the terminology of hadith {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
