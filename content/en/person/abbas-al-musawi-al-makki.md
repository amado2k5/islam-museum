## Overview
Abbas ibn Ali ibn Nur al-Din ibn Abi al-Hasan al-Makki al-Husayni al-Musawi was a travelling man of letters, rich in knowledge of reports and anecdotes. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1111 AH, 1699 CE, to 1180 AH, 1766 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 256}.

## His travels
He was born and lived in Mecca. Matters arose in which his relatives and companions harmed him, so he set out travelling through Iraq, India and Yemen from 1131 to 1142 AH, returning to perform the pilgrimage in most years. His wanderings ended in going back and forth between the port of al-Mokha and Mecca, and in 1145 AH he settled in al-Mokha {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 256}.

## His book
The governor of al-Mokha, the jurist Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Khazindar, arranged a living for him. He turned to gathering his scattered papers and composed from them Nuzhat al-jalis wa munyat al-adib al-anis, printed in two volumes. He finished it on 4 Shawwal 1148 AH and made it a gift to the governor al-Khazindar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 256}.
