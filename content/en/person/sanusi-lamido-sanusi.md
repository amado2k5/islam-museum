# Sanusi Lamido Sanusi

Sanusi Lamido Sanusi (born 31 July 1961 in Kano) is a Nigerian banker and economist of a Fulani ruling family, grandson of the Emir of Kano Muhammadu Sanusi I {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}. He studied economics and law at Ahmadu Bello University and later took a master's and a graduate diploma in Sharia at the International University of Africa in Khartoum {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}.

## From the central bank to the emirate

Sanusi began his banking career in 1985 and headed risk management at United Bank for Africa and then at First Bank of Nigeria {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}. President Yar'Adua appointed him Governor of the Central Bank on 3 June 2009; he led a sweeping reform that uncovered some ten billion dollars in losses and rescued failing banks, earning international banker-of-the-year awards, before President Jonathan suspended him in February 2014 after he exposed unremitted oil revenues {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}. On 8 June 2014 the Kano kingmakers installed him as emir under the regnal name Muhammadu Sanusi II, succeeding his grand-uncle Ado Bayero; the Kano state government deposed him on 9 March 2020 and exiled him to Nasarawa, and after years of exile and residence the state government reinstated him on 23 May 2024, with the legitimacy of his emirate still contested in court {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}.

## Affiliation, as the sources attest it

The English article's text states personally that Sanusi "is the spiritual leader (khalifa) of the Tijanniyah Sufi order in Nigeria", bears the religious title Khalifa Sanusi II, and records that on 10 May 2021 he was appointed khalifa of the order — a position his grandfather had held — and turbaned in Senegal {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-en}. The French article says that as emir he leads Nigeria's Sufi community gathered in the Tijaniyya branch {cite:wikipedia-sanusi-lamido-sanusi-fr}. This is stronger than descent or office alone — the khalifate was conferred on him personally by the order's leadership, four years after his deposition — yet no text used states his own initiation or discipleship in so many words, and the office reached him within his family's line. Under the museum's headship line the data record therefore leaves his affiliation unknown, flagged for the founder, with the Tijani reading preserved in full in the record's note; Wikidata records Islam only {cite:wikidata-sanusi-lamido-sanusi}.
