## Summary
Said ibn Salim al-Qaddah, Abu Uthman, who settled in Mecca and gave fatwas there, transmitting from al-Thawri and Ibn Jurayj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}

## Works
Ibn Adi called him truthful and acceptable; it is said he held the Murji'a view; he died before 200 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}
