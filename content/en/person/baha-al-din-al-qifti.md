## Summary
Baha' al-Din al-Qifti the qadi; he studied fiqh under al-Majd al-Qushayri and usul under al-Shams al-Isfahani at Qus, and met 'Izz al-Din Ibn 'Abd al-Salam and al-Zaki al-Mundhiri at Cairo. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 768-769}

## His judgeship
He held the judgeship of Isna and the teaching of its Mu'izziyya — Isna being, per the source, packed with Rafida — and stood for the Sunna until the Rafida resolved to kill him; God shielded him. Taqi al-Din Ibn Daqiq al-'Id was among those who profited by him; he wrote many books, his time divided between recitation, teaching and composition. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 768-769}
