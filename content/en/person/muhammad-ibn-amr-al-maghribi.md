## Summary
Muhammad ibn Amr al-Maghribi. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 471 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]]; he describes him as devoted to worship with the sight of his Object of worship, pure of sharing in the meals of eaters, and absent from them. The notice runs from page 128 to the start of page 130. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/128-130}

## Abu Zur'a's report
Ishaq ibn Ahmad al-Farisi relates: I heard Abu Zur'a say that eighteen days would pass over Muhammad ibn Amr al-Maghribi without his tasting food or drink, and that he had not seen anyone in Egypt more upright than him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/128}

## His eating in Ramadan
The report says that Muhammad ibn Amr ate two meals in Ramadan without strain; the sentence that follows is cut off in the scan and is not recorded here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/128}

## The hadith about Moses and the people in God's shade
He transmits a hadith from Abu Idris Ha'idh Allah that Moses said: my Lord, who is in Your shade on a day when there is no shade but Yours? He said: those who remember Me and love one another for My majesty; they are in My shade on a day when there is no shade but Mine. He said: my Lord, who are Your chosen among Your servants? He said: everyone whose heart is God-fearing and whose hands are clean, who does not come to a relative, walks gently and speaks rightly, and whose mountains move while he does not. He said: my Lord, who dwells in Your holy enclosure? He said: those whose eyes do not look at adultery, who do not put usury into their wealth nor take bribes in their judgement, with truth in their hearts and truth on their tongues; they dwell in My holy enclosure. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/129}

## The hadith of Aisha about the light
Muhammad ibn Amr relates from Aisha that the Prophet slept beside her, then woke; she felt lonely for him and heard him pray; she performed ablution and prayed behind him; he prayed as long as God willed; a light came until the whole house was lit, then it went; then a light came stronger still, so that if a mustard seed had been in my house I would have picked it up; then the Prophet turned away. She asked: O Messenger of God, what was this light you saw? He said: did you see it, Aisha? She said: yes. He said: I asked my Lord for my community and He gave me a third of them; I praised and thanked Him; then I asked for the rest and He gave me a second third; I praised and thanked Him; then I asked for the third third and He gave it; I praised and thanked Him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/130}
