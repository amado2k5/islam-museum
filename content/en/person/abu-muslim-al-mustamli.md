## Overview
Abu Muslim, the transcriber of Yazid ibn Harun, that is the one who dictated hadith for him. [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|Al-Ma'mun]] summoned him in 218 AH among the first seven in the Mihna. He answered, and was then sent back to Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 490}
