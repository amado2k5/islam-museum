## Overview
Asid ibn al-Mutashammis, said to be the nephew of al-Ahnaf. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Balkh
Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays appointed him over Balkh after its peace, and he collected its tribute. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
