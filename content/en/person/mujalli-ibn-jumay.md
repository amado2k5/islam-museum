## Summary
Mujalli ibn Jumay ibn Naja, Abu al-Ma'ali al-Qurashi al-Makhzumi, of Arsuf origin and Egyptian residence. He was chief judge of Egypt from 547 H, and fatwas in Egypt were referred to him. Al-Subki called him one of the imams of the school. His works are al-Dhakha'ir, a broad treatise on Shafi'i fiqh, and al-Umda fi adab al-qada'. Death 550 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13358}
