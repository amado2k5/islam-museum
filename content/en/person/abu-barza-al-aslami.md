## Overview
Abu Barza al-Aslami is known chiefly by his kunya, and his name and his father's are given variously: Nadla ibn 'Ubayd ibn al-Harith, Nadla ibn 'Abd Allah ibn al-Harith, 'Abd Allah ibn Nadla, Salama ibn 'Ubayd, or Nadla ibn 'A'idh. Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says the first is correct, quoting Ahmad ibn Zuhayr, who heard his father and Yahya ibn Ma'in say that Abu Barza's name is Nadla ibn 'Ubayd; this is also the view of Ahmad ibn Hanbal. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719, 783} His full lineage runs: Nadla ibn 'Ubayd ibn al-Harith ibn Hibal ibn Di'bil ibn Rabi'a ibn Anas ibn Khuzayma ibn Malik ibn Salaman ibn [[group/aslam|Aslam]] ibn Afsa ibn Haritha ibn 'Amr ibn 'Amir al-Aslami. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 783}

## Conversion and the conquest of Mecca
He accepted Islam early and was present at [[event/conquest-of-mecca|the conquest of Mecca]]. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719} He is reported to have said: "I killed [[person/abdullah-ibn-khatal|Ibn Khatal]] as he clung to the curtains of the Ka'ba." {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719}

## Appearance and students
Al-Azraq ibn Qays said: "I saw Abu Barza al-Aslami, a man of middle height and dark complexion." Those who narrated from him include [[person/abu-al-aliya-al-riyahi|Abu al-'Aliya]], Abu al-Minhal, Abu al-Wadi', [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]] and others. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719}

## Basra and Khurasan
He moved to [[place/basra|Basra]], where his children lived and he had a house, then campaigned in [[place/khurasan|Khurasan]] and settled at [[place/merv|Merv]]. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719, 783} According to al-Baladhuri he commanded the right wing of [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|'Abd Allah ibn 'Amir]] at [[event/conquest-of-istakhr|the conquest of Istakhr]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715} [[person/abu-bakra-al-thaqafi|Abu Bakra al-Thaqafi]], who died in Basra in 51 or 52 AH, asked in his will that Abu Barza lead his funeral prayer, and he did. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 782-783}

## Death
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr gives differing reports in his two notices: under the names, that he died in Khurasan in the days of [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Mu'awiya]] or at the end of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya's]] caliphate; under the kunyas, that he died in Basra after Ibn Ziyad's governorship and before Mu'awiya's death in 60, or else in 64 AH. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 719, 783}
