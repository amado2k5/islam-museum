## Summary
A man of Banu Tamim (named Hurqus ibn Zuhayr al-Sa'di) who faced the Prophet boldly at the [[event/distribution-of-hunayn-spoils|al-Ji'rana division]] saying: "be just, Muhammad — by God you have not been just!"; the Prophet refuted him, denounced his saying, and foretold that the Kharijites would come from his offspring {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/139-140}.

## His affair at al-Ji'rana
Ibn Ishaq relates from Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn al-Baqir and Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Taymi that Dhu al-Khuwaysira came up to the Prophet as he divided the Hunayn spoils and said: "Muhammad, I have seen what you did in this booty — he said: and what did you see? — he said: I have not seen you deal justly!" The Prophet grew angry and said: "woe to you — if justice is not with me, with whom can it be?" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/139-140}

Umar ibn al-Khattab begged leave to kill him; the Prophet forbade it saying: "let him be — he will have a faction who will go deep into the religion until they pass out of it as the arrow passes through the game" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/140}. He afterwards became one of the Kharijite leaders and was killed at al-Nahrawan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/140}.

## For young visitors
A man confronted the Prophet rudely and ill-manneredly while money was being distributed; the Prophet treated him with forbearance and kept his companions from killing him, warning against extremism and conceit.
