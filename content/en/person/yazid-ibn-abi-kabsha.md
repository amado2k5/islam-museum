## Overview
Yazid ibn Abi Kabsha al-Sakasaki, a governor whom Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik appointed over Sind. He brought [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] in shackles, and Muhammad died eighteen days after arriving in the land of Sind {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Another report says that Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman appointed him over Sind, and that he seized Muhammad ibn al-Qasim and sent him to Wasit {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.106}.
