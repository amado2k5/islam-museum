## Overview
Zurara ibn Awfa, the great imam and judge of Basra Abu Hajib al-'Amiri, one of the luminaries; he heard 'Imran ibn Husayn, Abu Hurayra and Ibn 'Abbas, while Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani, Qatada, Muhriz ibn Hakim, the Bedouin 'Arif and others narrated from him. Al-Nasa'i and others declared him trustworthy. He died in 93 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1718}
