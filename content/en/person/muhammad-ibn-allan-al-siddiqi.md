## Overview
Muhammad Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Allan ibn Ibrahim al-Bakri al-Siddiqi al-Shafi'i was an exegete and a scholar of hadith from Mecca. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 996 AH and his death to 1057 AH, that is 1588 and 1647 CE as printed, and says he left many works and treatises {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 293}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Diya' al-Sabil on exegesis; al-Tayf al-Ta'if bi-Tarikh Wajj wa-l-Ta'if, in manuscript, in the library of the Meccan Haram, number 120; a commentary on the poems of Ibn al-Mulayyiq and Abu Madyan, printed; al-Fath al-Mustajad li-Baghdad; al-Manhal al-Adhb al-Mufrad fi al-Fath al-Uthmani li-Misr wa-man Wuliya Niyabat dhalika al-Balad; three histories of the building of the Ka'ba; Dalil al-Falihin li-Turuq Riyad al-Salihin, printed, in eight parts, a commentary on al-Nawawi's Riyad al-Salihin; al-Mawahib al-Fathiya ala al-Tariqa al-Muhammadiya on Sufism, in manuscript; al-Talattuf fi al-Wusul ila al-Ta'arruf on principles, in manuscript; al-Futuhat al-Rabbaniyya ala al-Adhkar al-Nawawiyya, printed; Raf' al-Khasa'is, in manuscript; Muthir Shawq al-Anam ila Hajj Bayt Allah al-Haram, in manuscript; and Ithaf al-Fadil bi-l-Fi'l al-Mabni li-Ghayr al-Fa'il on language, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 293}.
