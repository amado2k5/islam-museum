## Overview
Al-Fath ibn Khaqan, vizier of [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.544}

## His killing
He was killed with al-Mutawakkil on the night of al-Mutawakkil's killing, at the hands of five Turks, on the fifth of Shawwal in 247 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.544}

Al-Mas'udi relates that when Bagir and the Turks attacked al-Mutawakkil, those present scattered, and none remained to defend him except him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.506}

## Another report
Al-Mas'udi says that he and al-Mutawakkil disputed the interpretation of a verse, so al-Mutawakkil sent al-Mubarrad to the governor of Basra, Muhammad ibn al-Qasim al-Hashimi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.476}
