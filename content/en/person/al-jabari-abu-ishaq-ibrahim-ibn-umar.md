## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Umar ibn Ibrahim ibn Khalil al-Rab'i al-Ju'bari, a reciter of the Qur'an. He is titled Taqi al-Din in Baghdad and Burhan al-Din elsewhere, and is known as Abu Ishaq. He was born in 640 AH (1242 CE) and died in 732 AH (1332 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.299}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source. The data write the name "al-Ju'bari" while the page identifier is "jabari"; the difference is only one of Latin transliteration.
