Mirza Teletović (born in September 1985) is a Bosnian former basketball player and executive. He won two Liga ACB titles in six years with Saski Baskonia, played six NBA seasons for Brooklyn, Phoenix and Milwaukee, captained Bosnia and Herzegovina, and presided over its basketball federation from 2018 to 2022; a pulmonary embolism ended his playing career in 2018.{cite:wikipedia-mirza-teletovic-en}{cite:wikidata-mirza-teletovic}

His sources disagree on his birth day and town — 17 September (Turkish article and Wikidata) against the English article's 18 September, and Mostar against its infobox's Jablanica — and both readings are preserved in his record. His English biography describes him as a practicing Muslim; no source names a school for him.{cite:wikipedia-mirza-teletovic-tr}{cite:wikidata-mirza-teletovic}
