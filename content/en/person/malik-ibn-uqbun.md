## Overview
Malik ibn Uqbun, one of the Harranian Sabians. Al-Mas'udi mentions him as the interpreter of a Syriac writing at the door of the Sabians' assembly. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.542-543}

## The writing on the door
Al-Mas'udi saw on the knocker of the door of the Sabians' assembly a writing in Syriac, a saying of Plato: "Whoever knows himself becomes divine." Malik ibn Uqbun and others explained it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.546}

## His exchange with al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi addressed him with some of what had reached him about their conditions. He acknowledged some of it and denied some of what was said about the sacrifices. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.542-543}

Al-Mas'udi mentions the assembly at Jurjan in one place, and the data confuse "Jaran" with "Jurjan"; the source needs review.
