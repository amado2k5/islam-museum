## Summary
'Abd al-Warith b. Sufyan b. Jabrun b. Sulayman of Cordoba (317–395 AH) was one of the teachers from whom [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] transmitted most, and the channel through which he received the learning of [[person/qasim-ibn-asbagh|Qasim b. Asbagh]]. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 13}

## His companionship with Qasim b. Asbagh
He began studying with Qasim b. Asbagh in 333 AH and heard most of his transmissions, becoming the most reliable authority on him and his most constant companion. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 13} Al-Humaydi quotes Ibn 'Abd al-Barr as saying that he was among those who kept closest to Qasim and the best known in Cordoba for his company — it was said that hardly anything read to Qasim escaped him — and that he had seen many of Qasim's original copies and found 'Abd al-Warith's hearing recorded in all of them. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 13}

## What Ibn 'Abd al-Barr took from him
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr read with him Qasim b. Asbagh's *Musannaf* on the Sunan, and Ibn Qutayba's *al-Ma'arif* and *Sharh Gharib al-Hadith*, as al-Humaydi records. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 13}
