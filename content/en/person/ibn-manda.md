## Overview
Abu Bakr Ibn Manda, a hafiz from Isfahan, author of [[work/marifat-al-sahaba-ibn-manda|Knowledge of the Companions]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.398}

## His book
Haji Khalifa says that his book is the oldest of the four works on the knowledge of the Companions that Ibn al-Athir surveyed in *Usd al-Ghaba*, and that Ibn al-Athir marked it with the letter (d). Abu Musa al-Madini added a supplement to it. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.398}
