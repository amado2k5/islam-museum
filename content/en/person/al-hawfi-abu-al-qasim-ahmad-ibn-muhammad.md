## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Khalaf al-Qal'i al-Hawfi al-Ishbili, one of the greatest specialists in the science of inheritance and the drawing up of contracts in al-Andalus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.368}

## Death
He died in 588 AH (1192 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.368}
