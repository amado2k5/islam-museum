## Summary
Ibn al-Dabithi, the hafiz, historian and muqri', named for Dabitha a village of Wasit; he heard from Abu Talib al-Kinani, Ibn Shatil, 'Abd al-Mun'im b. al-Farawi and their tier, studied fiqh under Abu'l-Hasan Ibn al-Buqi, mastered Arabic, rose and led; he excerpted usul and khilaf, devoted himself to hadith and its men, and composed a history of Wasit and a dhayl on Ibn al-Sam'ani's, dictating both. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 185-186}

## Diya' al-Din on him
The hafiz Diya' al-Din al-Maqdisi said: 'He is my shaykh and the last of the abundant hafizes; my eyes never saw his like in memorising the histories, the siras and the days of men.' He was stricken late in life. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 185-186}
