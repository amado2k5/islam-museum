## Overview
Abu Uyayna ibn al-Muhallab, brother of Yazid ibn al-Muhallab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the reports
Al-Baladhuri says that Yazid sent him with his son Khalid against the Isbahbadh, and the Isbahbadh defeated them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
