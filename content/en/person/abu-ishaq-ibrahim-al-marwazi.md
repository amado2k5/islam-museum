## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ahmad al-Marwazi, a Shafi'i jurist and one of the companions of [[person/ahmad-ibn-umar-ibn-surayj|Ibn Surayj]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

## His standing
Al-Shirazi says that leadership of learning in Baghdad came to him, that he wrote a commentary on "al-Mukhtasar", and that he wrote on the principles of jurisprudence. Imams took from him, and the jurisprudence of his students spread through the lands. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

## His death
He went to Egypt and died there in 340 AH. The Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}
