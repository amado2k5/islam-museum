## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Abd Allah ibn Ahmad al-Abyani, a scholar and jurist of Ifriqiya in the fourth century AH. Al-Usayli and Abu al-Hasan al-Qabisi, among others, transmitted from him. He lived through half a century of the Aghlabid period and half of the Fatimid period. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The museum's data record his death in 355 AH, from this source alone, so it needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
