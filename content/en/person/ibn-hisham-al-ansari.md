## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Abd Allah ibn Yusuf Ibn Hisham, Jamal al-Din Abu Muhammad the grammarian: born in Dhu al-Qa'da 708, he was first a Shafi'i and then became a Hanbali, memorising al-Khiraqi's Mukhtasar in under four months. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/308} His works include Mughni al-Labib, Qatr al-Nada and Shudhur al-Dhahab. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/308} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/309} Ibn Hajar reports from Ibn Khaldun that in the Maghrib they heard of an Egyptian grammarian more learned in grammar than Sibawayh. He died on the night of Friday 5 Dhu al-Qa'da 761 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/309}
