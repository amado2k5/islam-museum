## Overview
A man of the Ashja' of whom only his death is known. He passed by the expedition of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abi-hadrad|Ibn Abi Hadrad]] at Batn Idam, sitting on his mount with some goods and a skin of milk. He greeted them with the greeting of Islam, but the people held off from him, and [[person/muhallim-ibn-jaththama|Muhallim ibn Jaththama]] charged at him and killed him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/275} Ibn Abi Hadrad says that the verse of [[passage/quran-4-94|al-Nisa' 94]] was revealed about them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/275} At Hunayn [[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna ibn Hisn]] sought his blood-money as chief of Ghatafan, and the Prophet decided the blood-money was one hundred camels: fifty on the journey and fifty on the return. See [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-batn-idam|the raid]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/276}

## Notes for researchers
- His Islam is inferred from his greeting and from the context of the verse. Ibn Ishaq does not state it outright, except in what he relates from al-Aqra' that his people might bear witness that he "was killed an unbeliever who never prayed", in a threatening context. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/277}
