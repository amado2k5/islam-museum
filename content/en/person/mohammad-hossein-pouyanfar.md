## Summary
Mohammad Hossein Pouyanfar is an Iranian maddah (eulogist) from Shemiranat, Tehran, the founder of the Reyhanat al-Nabi hey'at and of the Hamdardi charity, best known for the piece "Man Iranam o to Araghi". {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar} His Instagram account counted 615,485 followers in November 2026, read on the platform on 7 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:instagram-mhpooyanfar}

## Early life and education
Pouyanfar was born on 24 Ordibehesht 1363 in the Persian calendar (14 May 1984) in Shemiranat, Tehran, according to his Photokade biography; in his own published interview he confirms the birth year 1363 and his bachelor's degree in psychology. {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar} {cite:fardanews-pouyanfar-interview} He grew up in a hey'at family: both his grandfathers kept hey'ats, his maternal grandfather Haj Mirza Hasan Asgarian was a maddah, and after his father's death in 1372 (1993) he turned to Qur'an recitation, which he practised professionally for several years. {cite:fardanews-pouyanfar-interview}

## Beginnings and career
In 1377 (1998), at a gathering in Shemiran, the eulogist Hojjat Kasri heard his recitation and asked him why he did not perform maddahi; he began attending Kasri's classes and started eulogising within about two months, and he still names Kasri as his teacher. {cite:fardanews-pouyanfar-interview} In 1380 (2001) he founded the Reyhanat al-Nabi hey'at for the young people of Shemiran, with his brother, friends and Kasri's counsel — its name was chosen by opening the Qur'an, which brought up the name of Fatimah al-Zahra — and its weekly sessions ran regularly from 1384. {cite:fardanews-pouyanfar-interview} {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar}

## Works and activities
His best-known piece is the eulogy "Man Iranam o to Araghi, ah az feraq" ("I am Iran and you are Iraq — ah, the separation"), which his biography describes as a well-produced piece released at the right moment. {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar} In Mordad 1400 (2021) he presented for the first time, hosting the online programme Mahla, which continued into further seasons with him as presenter. {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar} In 1398 (2019) he founded the Hamdardi charity inside his hey'at, to relieve people in difficulty alongside the religious work, and his biography notes that other hey'ats followed the example. {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar}

## Positions
Pouyanfar is known for social criticism voiced from the eulogist's platform: his biography quotes his jab at officials who want to poll the public — that their cars should also reach Tehran's deprived districts — and his view that one of the country's biggest problems is officials who cannot bear criticism. {cite:photokade-mohammad-hossein-pouyanfar} These positions are reported here as his biography records them.
