## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ya'qub ibn Ishaq al-Sa'di al-Juzajani, Abu Ishaq, was the hadith scholar of Syria and one of the memorisers, authors and transmitters counted reliable. His nisba is to Juzjan, in the districts of Balkh in Khurasan, where he was born {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}. He travelled to Mecca, then Basra, then Ramla, staying a while in each, and settled in Damascus, where he lived until his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}. Al-A'lam gives his death as 259 AH, that is 873 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.

## His works
He has a book on criticism and accreditation (al-jarh wa'l-ta'dil) and a book on the weak narrators (al-du'afa') {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}. Al-Zirikli reports that Ibn Kathir said he has works, among them «al-Mutarjam fihi ulum ghazira wa-fawa'id kathira» (words as printed in the entry) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.
