## Overview
Jamal al-Din Yusuf ibn Ibrahim al-Ardabili al-Shafi'i, a Shafi'i jurist from [[place/ardabil|Ardabil]] in Azerbaijan, who died in 779 AH (1377 CE). The date rests on one source and needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
