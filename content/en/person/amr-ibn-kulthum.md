## Overview
Amr ibn Kulthum ibn Malik al-Taghlibi, of the [[group/banu-taghlib|Banu Taghlib]], a pre-Islamic poet and the author of one of the Mu'allaqat. His mother was Layla daughter of [[person/al-muhalhil|al-Muhalhil]], and his maternal uncle was [[person/kulayb-ibn-rabia|Kulayb]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}

He killed [[person/amr-ibn-hind-al-lakhmi|Amr ibn Hind]], the lord of al-Hira, when he insulted his mother Layla. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.409}

He answered al-Harith ibn Abi Shamir al-Ghassani when he threatened to raid Taghlib with the words: "No people ever woke but their judgement was sharpened and their community was strengthened, so do not wake their sleepers." Al-Harith raided them, was defeated, and his brother was killed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}
