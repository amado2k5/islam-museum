## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad b. Ahmad b. 'Ali b. Shahuya al-Farisi, the Shafi'i jurist; the hafiz Abu 'Abdallah mentions him in Tarikh Naysabur: he resided at Nishapur a while, went out to Bukhara, returned to Nishapur, then journeyed to Fars and held its judgeship, then came back to Nishapur and transmitted there; in the madhhab he held far-reaching positions peculiar to him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
