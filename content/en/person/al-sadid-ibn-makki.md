## Overview
Al-Mu'addil, the last of the hafiz Ibn 'Asakir's companions to die — Safar 652, aged eighty-nine; he also transmitted solely from Abu'l-Fahm 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi'l-'Ajayiz and Abu'l-Ma'ali Ibn Khaldun {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 268}.
