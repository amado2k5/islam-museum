## Overview
Abu al-Mukhtar Yazid ibn Qays ibn Yazid al-Su'aiq, a poet. He composed the well-known ode in which he complained against the agents of al-Ahwaz to Umar ibn al-Khattab, and Umar took half of their property from them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
