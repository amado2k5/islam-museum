## Overview
Hilal ibn Uqqa ibn Qays ibn al-Bishr al-Namiri. Al-Baladhuri reports that he was in charge of the Namir under Ibn Saqit at Ayn al-Tamr, and he gathered men for Khalid and fought him ([[event/siege-of-ayn-al-tamr|the siege of Ayn al-Tamr]]). Khalid overcame him, killed him and crucified him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri, citing Ibn al-Kalbi, reports that the man in charge of them on that day was Uqqa ibn Qays ibn al-Bishr himself. His death is dated to 12 AH; the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
