## Summary
[[person/amr-ibn-hazm|Amr ibn Hazm]] ibn Zayd ibn Lawdhan al-Ansari, kunya Abu al-Dahhak. His brother was [[person/umara-ibn-hazm|Umara]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}. He witnessed [[event/battle-of-the-trench|the Trench]] and what followed it, and the Prophet appointed him over Najran {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.

## The letter
His son Muhammad related from him a letter the Prophet had written for him, containing the fara'id (shares of inheritance), zakat, blood-money and other matters. Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Darimi and others recorded it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.

## His death
Abu Nu'aym said he died in the caliphate of Umar, and Ibrahim ibn al-Mundhir said the same in al-Tabaqat. It is also said that he died after fifty. Ibn Hajar judges this the nearer to the truth, since Musnad Abi Ya'la, through a chain whose narrators are trustworthy, reports that he spoke forcefully to Mu'awiya about the matter of his pledge of allegiance to Yazid {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}. In al-Tabarani and elsewhere, he related to Mu'awiya and to [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] the hadith: "The rebellious group will kill Ammar" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
The Prophet sent him to [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] after their delegation had departed, to instruct them in religion, teach them the Sunna and the markers of Islam, and collect their alms. The Prophet wrote him [[work/charge-to-amr-ibn-hazm|a letter setting out his charge]]: the fear of God, teaching the Qur'an, the times of prayer, the obligatory alms, and the jizya.
