## Summary
[[person/amr-ibn-hazm|Amr ibn Hazm]] ibn Zayd ibn Lawdhan al-Ansari, kunya Abu al-Dahhak; his brother is [[person/umara-ibn-hazm|Umara]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}. He witnessed [[event/battle-of-the-trench|the Trench]] and what followed, and the Prophet appointed him over Najran {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.

## The letter
His son Muhammad relates from him a letter the Prophet wrote him containing the obligations, the zakat, the blood-moneys and other matters; Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Darimi and more than one recorded it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.

## His death
Abu Nu'aym said he died in Umar's caliphate, and so said Ibrahim ibn al-Mundhir in al-Tabaqat; it is also said after fifty — Ibn Hajar says this is nearer the truth, for Musnad Abi Ya'la carries on a chain of trustworthy men that he spoke to Mu'awiya forcefully on the matter of his pledge to Yazid {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}. In al-Tabarani and elsewhere he related to Mu'awiya and to [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] the hadith: "the rebel band will kill Ammar" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1095}.
