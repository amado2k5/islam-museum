## Overview
Anas ibn Iyad al-Laythi al-Madani, Abu Damra, was the hadith scholar of Medina in his time; the highest isnads there ended with him, and Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal and many others transmitted from him. He died in 200 AH (815 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.24}
