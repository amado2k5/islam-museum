## Overview
Kulthum al-Attabi settled in Raqqa, and his origin is in the land of the Jund of Qinnasrin and the Awasim. Al-Mas'udi describes him as learned, cultured and eloquent, and as having a skill in correspondence that few of his contemporaries had. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.408-410}

## With al-Ma'mun
He persuaded Yahya ibn Aktham to let al-Ma'mun know where he was. Al-Mas'udi relates from his words that the alms of prestige is to spend it on al-Musta'in. At al-Ma'mun's assembly a joke about his name passed between him and [[person/ishaq-al-mawsili|Ishaq al-Mawsili]], and according to the report Kulthum answered him with a reply that delighted those present. One of his sayings: "A man's secretary is his tongue, his chamberlain is his face, and his companion is all of him." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.408-410}
