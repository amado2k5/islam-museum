## Summary
Abu Ya'qub Ishaq ibn Ibrahim al-Saffar; he transmitted from 'Abd al-Wahhab and al-Waqidi; Ibn Sa'id and al-Baghandi narrated from him, the last being Ibn Makhlad; he was firm, reliable, meticulous, retentive; he died in 262. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 120}
