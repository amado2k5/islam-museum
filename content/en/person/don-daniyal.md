## Summary
Don Daniyal bin Don Biyajid (born 14 November 1984), better known as Ustaz Don Daniyal or Ustaz Don, is a Malaysian freelance preacher, television presenter and lecturer. {cite:wikipedia-don-daniyal-ms} He is known for his religious programmes, most of them broadcast on TV AlHijrah and Astro Oasis. {cite:wikipedia-don-daniyal-ms}

## Television career
He began his media career hosting the interactive Islamic programme 30 Minit Ustaz Don on TV AlHijrah. {cite:wikipedia-don-daniyal-ms} The programme's title — "Thirty Minutes with Ustaz Don" — became the name by which Malaysian audiences know him. {cite:wikipedia-don-daniyal-ms}

## Reach
His official Facebook page, "Ustaz Don Daniyal Don Biyajid: Solat & Selawat Tour", counted 1,717,376 followers when the platform page was read directly on 15 November 2026. {cite:facebook-don-daniyal} His Instagram account, @ddaniyaldbiyajid, counted 684,944 followers on the same day's direct read. {cite:instagram-don-daniyal} His video descriptions route YouTube viewers to Agami TV, an organisational channel, so this record claims no personal YouTube count for him. {cite:facebook-don-daniyal} Audience figures are platform figures, recorded with their dates; they change constantly. {cite:facebook-don-daniyal}
