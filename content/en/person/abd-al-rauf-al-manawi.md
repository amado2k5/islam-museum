## Overview
A Shafii scholar of Cairo, learned in several fields. He studied with Ali ibn Ghanim al-Maqdisi, Hamdan al-Faqih and Muhammad al-Bakri, and Sulayman al-Babili and Ali al-Ajhuri studied with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5160}

## Works
His works include al-Taysir (a commentary on the Jami al-Saghir), Fayd al-Qadir, Taysir al-Wuquf on the subtleties of the rules of endowments, a commentary on al-Tahrir in Shafii law, and al-Ithafat al-Sunniyya on the sacred hadiths. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5160}

## Distinction
He is not Sharaf al-Din al-Manawi, who has his own entry.
