## Overview
Muhammad Hamza Isma'il al-Haddad is professor of Islamic architecture, archaeology and civilisation at the Faculty of Archaeology, Cairo University, as his book's title page presents him. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 title page} He is the author of the series Islamic Architecture in the Arabian Peninsula, whose fourth part is [[work/al-asbila-bi-makka-wa-al-madina|*The Sabils in the Islamic Architecture of Mecca and Medina*]] (2004); the book's references list another of his works on architecture. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 title page} {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.64}
