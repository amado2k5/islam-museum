## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Alwan is the one who cut off the head of [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]], after the pole of the tent fell on his head and killed him, according to al-Baladhuri in "Futuh al-Buldan". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
