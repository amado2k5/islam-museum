## Overview
Isma'il al-Kurani, the ascetic exemplar of Kuran near Isfarayin — a shaykh of great standing whom people travelled to visit, pious, truthful, probing of his own religion; his term found him at Gaza in Rajab 665, as al-Dhahabi said {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 552}.
