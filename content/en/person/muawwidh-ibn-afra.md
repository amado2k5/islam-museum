## Summary
Mu'awwidh ibn al-Harith ibn Rifa'a al-Khazraji, known by his mother's name "Ibn Afra'"; one of the two youths who struck [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]] on the day of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## At Badr
He went out with his brother [[person/awf-ibn-al-harith|Awf]] and Abdullah ibn Rawaha — their mother being Afra' — among the three Ansaris who stepped out first for the duels, whom the Qurashis turned back asking for equals of their own cousins {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 216}. And when [[person/muadh-ibn-amr-ibn-al-jamuh|Mu'adh ibn Amr ibn al-Jamuh]] struck Abu Jahl's leg, Mu'awwidh passed by him hamstrung and struck him until he finished him — he left him with a last breath {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 221}.

Mu'awwidh fought on until he was killed in that same battle; when the Prophet ruled that both youths had killed Abu Jahl, Mu'adh alone was given his spoil because Mu'awwidh had been martyred {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:mishkat-al-masabih}.
