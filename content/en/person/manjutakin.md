## Overview
Manjutakin was a commander of the Fatimid caliph al-Aziz Billah in Egypt, sent at the head of a large army against Aleppo — held by Abu'l-Fada'il and Lu'lu' — about 382 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.7 p.455}

## The Aleppo and Antioch campaign
The defenders called on Basil, king of the Byzantines; hearing of the enemy's approach Manjutakin marched to meet them before they could join the defenders, crossed the Orontes, defeated them and drove them back to Antioch, then marched on the city and plundered and burned its town and villages. Returning to besiege Aleppo again, he accepted money from Lu'lu' to withdraw that year and went to Damascus, which angered al-Aziz, who ordered the army back to Aleppo and supplied it by sea via Tripoli; they besieged it thirteen months until provisions ran short and the defenders again corresponded with the Byzantine king, who came and camped at Aleppo's gate while Manjutakin withdrew. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.7 p.455-456}
