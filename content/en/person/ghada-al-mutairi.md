## Summary
Ghada Al-Mutairi is a Saudi-American chemist counted among the leading researchers in nanomedicine. She leads the Center of Excellence in Nanomedicine at the University of California, San Diego, where her work designs smart nanomaterials that respond to the body’s disease signals. {cite:wikipedia-ghada-al-mutairi}

## Life and upbringing
She was born in 1976 into a Saudi family, studied chemistry, and continued her research training in the United States, where her academic career settled. {cite:wikipedia-ghada-al-mutairi}

## Career and works
Her research centres on inventing polymeric nanoparticles that break down in response to inflammation or to near-infrared light, opening a route to precise drug delivery at disease sites. She helped found research centres and groups in this field and directs the Center of Excellence in Nanomedicine in San Diego. {cite:wikipedia-ghada-al-mutairi}

## Legacy
Al-Mutairi is cited in the scientific literature as one of the pioneers of nanomedicine from the Arab world and the United States, and her work is referenced in studies of drug delivery and bioimaging. {cite:wikipedia-ghada-al-mutairi}
