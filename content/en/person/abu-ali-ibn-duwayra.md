## Overview
Abu 'Ali Ibn Duwayra, shaykh, chief and teacher of Basra's Hanbalis; nations studied under him and more than a thousand people finished the Qur'an upon him — righteous, ascetic, scrupulous — and he transmitted at al-Tirmidhi's mosque by licence of the hafiz Abu Muhammad Ibn al-Akhdar. He died at Basra; after him his pupil Nur al-Din 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Umar al-Basri held the teaching at his madrasa. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 267}
