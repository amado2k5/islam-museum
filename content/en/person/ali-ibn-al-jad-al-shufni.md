## Summary
Ali ibn al-Ja'd al-Shufni is counted by Ibn al-Nadim among the senior companions of ra'y (opinion-based jurisprudence), named in the Fihrist's section on the jurists of the school of Abu Hanifa. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287}

## Details
In the sixth discourse of the Fihrist, on the reports of scholars and the titles of their works, Ibn al-Nadim devotes a section to the jurists of the school of opinion — the companions of Abu Hanifa and those who learned and wrote on their doctrines, from Abu Yusuf and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and al-Hasan ibn Ziyad al-Lu'lu'i down to the later judges and jurists of Baghdad. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287}

Among these he names Ali ibn al-Ja'd al-Shufni in the ranks of the senior companions of ra'y. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287} Al-Shufni was a jurist of the third century AH trained in the Iraqi school of opinion, and his name survives in the early bibliographical literature that enumerates those who carried the jurisprudence of Abu Hanifa and transmitted its books. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 287}
