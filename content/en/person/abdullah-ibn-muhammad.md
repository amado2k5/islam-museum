## Summary
The Prophet's son by [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]], nicknamed al-Tayyib and al-Tahir; he died young in Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 473}.

## His account
Among the Prophet's sons was Abdullah, nicknamed al-Tayyib and al-Tahir; he died young in Mecca — none of his sons lived {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 473; zad-al-maad}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet had a son named Abdullah whom people nicknamed the Good and the Pure; he died young in Mecca.
