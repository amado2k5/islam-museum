## Brief
The Prophet's son by [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]], known as al-Tayyib and al-Tahir; he died young in Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.473}.

## Life
Among the Prophet's sons was Abdullah, who was called al-Tayyib and al-Tahir. He died young in Mecca, and so none of his male children survived {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.473; zad-al-maad}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet had a son named Abdullah, whom people called al-Tayyib and al-Tahir; he died young in Mecca.

## In the chapter on the marriage to Khadija (The Sealed Nectar p.61)
The Sealed Nectar, in its chapter on the marriage, notes that Abdullah was the last of the Prophet's children by [[person/khadija|Khadija]] in birth order, that he was called al-Tayyib and al-Tahir, and that all the Prophet's sons died in childhood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/190-191} {cite:fath-al-bari 7/507}.
