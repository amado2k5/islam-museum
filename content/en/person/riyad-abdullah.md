## Summary
Riyad Abdullah — spelled Ustaz Riyaad Abdallaa in his scene — is an Ethiopian dawah preacher who works in Afaan Oromo. {cite:youtube-ifa-rahmataa-tv} His main channel is his Facebook page (@riyadabdaallah), which counted 280,000 followers by the wave harvest's read of his posts' metadata on 7 November 2026 — a dated figure that rests on that harvest read, because Facebook profile fetch is blocked in the build environment, and not on a direct read by the museum. {cite:facebook-riyad-abdullah}

## Platforms and work
His Afaan Oromo lectures are published on the Ifa Rahmataa TV ("Light of Mercy TV") YouTube channel, whose videos read on 7 November 2026 include his recent lectures on kindness to parents and on the prayer, the most recent of them updated three days before the read. {cite:youtube-ifa-rahmataa-tv} A milestone post on his own Facebook page on 7 June 2026 marked the channel reaching 80,000 subscribers and more than 670 videos, by the harvest's read of that post. {cite:facebook-riyad-abdullah} The channel also hosts other speakers, among them Sheikh Yasin Ahmed and Sheikh Jamaal Aliyyii, and an Afaan Oromo lecture by Ahmedin Jebel, so it functions as a shared dawah platform for the scene as well as the home of his own lectures. {cite:youtube-ifa-rahmataa-tv}
