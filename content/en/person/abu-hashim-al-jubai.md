## Overview
Abu Hashim 'Abd al-Salam b. Abi 'Ali Muhammad al-Jubba'i b. 'Abd al-Wahhab b. Salam, mawla of 'Uthman b. 'Affan — the celebrated mutakallim, scholar son of a scholar; he and his father were among the great Mu'tazila, and he has treatises on the i'tizal madhhab. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
