# Shoaib Hussain

Shoaib Hussain is a dawah content creator known as The Sunnah Guy. At eighteen, in a period of doubt, he found his way back to practice through lectures by Bilal Assad, whose personal video message — sent through a family connection in Melbourne — he describes as his turning point.{cite:onepath-the-sunnah-guy}

He began with street dawah and community work, produced the UK-based Islamic film Two Sides, and during the COVID-19 pandemic moved to short-form video; by 2025 his reminder reels reached a reported 2.9 million followers, with a growing share of non-Muslim viewers.{cite:onepath-the-sunnah-guy}{cite:youtube-the-sunnah-guy}
