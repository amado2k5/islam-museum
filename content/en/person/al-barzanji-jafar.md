## Overview
Jafar ibn Hasan ibn Abd al-Karim al-Barzanji, a Sufi scholar from the Barzanji family of sayyids in Medina. The encyclopaedia describes this family as known for learning and piety. He was born in 1128 AH (1716 CE) and died in 1177 AH (1763 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
