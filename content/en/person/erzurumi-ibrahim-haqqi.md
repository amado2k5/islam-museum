## Overview
Ibrahim Haqqi al-Erzurumi, an Ottoman scholar from Erzurum. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.467}

## His life
He returned to Erzurum after his father's death and was educated there. He then served one of his teachers in Nillo. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.467}

The data gives his birth as around 1115 AH (1703 CE) and his death as around 1194 AH (1780 CE), according to one report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.467}
