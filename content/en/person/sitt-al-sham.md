## Summary
Sitt al-Sham Zumurrud Khatun bint Ayyub, full sister of Shams al-Dawla Turanshah ibn Ayyub; her husband was Nasir al-Din ibn Asad al-Din Shirkuh, lord of Homs. She built a madrasa inside Damascus south of the Nuri hospital, and built the other madrasa outside Damascus holding her tomb and those of her brother and husband; Ibn al-Salah managed the teaching of her madrasa together with the Nasiriyya at Jerusalem, the Rawahiyya and the dar al-hadith. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
