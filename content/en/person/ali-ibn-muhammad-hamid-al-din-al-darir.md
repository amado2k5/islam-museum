## Summary
Ali ibn Muhammad, Hamid al-Din al-Darir, a Hanafi scholar beyond the river, a jurist, usuli and careful traditionist, who studied with Shams al-A'imma al-Kurdari. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2059}

## Works
His works include 'al-Fawa'id', glosses on the Hidaya, and commentaries on the Nasafi poem and on al-Jami' al-kabir; he died in 667 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2059}
