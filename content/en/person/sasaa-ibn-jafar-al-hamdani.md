## Overview
Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far was a man of Hamdan who left Shibam with a man of the line of Abu al-Khayr ibn Ya'fur, and the two allied against [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] near Bayt Dhukhar with a crowd behind them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} The narrator says they resented that al-Hadi had held their hands back from what was not due, and forbade them wine, corruption and wrongs. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} Sa'sa'a then came to him with people of Qudum and others and fought him at [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]]; al-Hadi routed them and killed a great many. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} See [[event/battle-of-raydah-288]].

The reason given is the Zaydi narrator's own; the book offers no other account here.
