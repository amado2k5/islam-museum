## Summary
Husam al-Din al-Hadhabani, Abu 'Ali Muhammad b. 'Ali al-Kurdi, among the great and lofty of the state; he enjoyed special closeness to al-Malik al-Salih Najm al-Din, deputised for him in the sultanate of Damascus then of Egypt, made the hajj in 449 — then epilepsy struck him late in life and grew until he died; born at Aleppo in 592, he has good poetry. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 304}
