# Munir El Haddadi

## Summary

Munir El Haddadi Mohamed is a Moroccan professional footballer, born in El Escorial, Spain, on 1 September 1995 to a Moroccan father and a mother from the Spanish city of Melilla, and raised in Galapagar near Madrid. A forward who came through Barcelona's academy, he scored on his first-team debut in August 2014 and was part of the squad that won the treble that season; he later won the Europa League with Sevilla in 2020. Capped once by Spain in 2014, he switched his international allegiance to Morocco in 2021. {cite:wikipedia-munir-el-haddadi-en} {cite:wikipedia-munir-el-haddadi-ar} {cite:wikidata-munir-el-haddadi}

## Career

Munir began in the youth ranks of Atlético Madrid before transferring to Barcelona in 2011, where he won the UEFA Youth League in 2014. He scored in his first match for Barcelona's senior team in August 2014, at the start of a season that ended with the treble of league, cup and Champions League; he played in Barcelona's runs in the cup and the Champions League that year. Loans at Valencia and Alavés and a permanent move to Sevilla followed, where he won the Europa League in 2020, before spells at Getafe, Las Palmas and Leganés and a move to Iran's Esteghlal and then Albacete. {cite:wikipedia-munir-el-haddadi-en}

For Spain he made a single senior appearance in 2014. In 2021 he chose to represent Morocco, the country of his father, and played eleven times for them across 2021 and 2022, including at the 2021 Africa Cup of Nations. {cite:wikipedia-munir-el-haddadi-en}
