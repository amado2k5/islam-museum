## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Qadir al-Hafizi, of the Hafizi family of notables of Lemah in Asir, whose lineage links to Shaykh Abu al-Abbas ibn Musa. He was born in 1176 AH (1762 CE) and died in 1237 AH (1821 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.79}

The encyclopaedia lists him among the scholars. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
