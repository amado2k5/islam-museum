## Overview
Ahmad ibn Isma'il Taymur, one of the leading figures of the Arab cultural renaissance in modern Egypt. He comes from a family of Kurdish origin that settled in Egypt {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
He was born in Cairo in 1288 AH (1871 CE) and died in 1348 AH (1930 CE) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
