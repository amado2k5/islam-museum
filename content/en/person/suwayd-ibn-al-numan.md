## Overview
Suwayd ibn al-Nu'man, an Ansari of Medina of the Aws, a Companion who witnessed Uhud and later campaigns and pledged under the tree. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3488}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet on rinsing the mouth after sawiq; Bashir ibn Yasar narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3488}
