## Overview
Isa ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Yulbakht al-Jazuli, a Maghribi jurist from among the Berbers. His name in Berber means "the lord of fortune". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 607 AH (1210 CE). The date comes from one source and needs a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Note: the identifier of the page is "al-Jaziri" and the name in the data is "al-Jazuli"; this needs review.
