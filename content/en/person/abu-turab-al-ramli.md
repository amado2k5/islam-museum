## The kite and the roast in Ramla
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn relates from Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Razi that Abu Turab al-Ramli left Mecca one year and told his companions: you take the main road, and I will take the road to Tabuk. They said: the heat is severe. He said: it must be so; but when you enter Ramla, stay with my friend. They entered Ramla and stayed with him; he roasted four pieces of meat for them, and when they were set before them a kite came and took one piece. They said: it was not our provision; and they ate the rest. Two days later Abu Turab came out of the desert, and they asked whether he had found anything on the road. He said: no, except one day a kite threw down a piece of hot roast. They said: we have eaten from it, for it was from us; the kite took it from our table. Abu Turab said: thus was the truth. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/165}
