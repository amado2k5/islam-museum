## Summary
A Hanbali jurist from Harran. He studied under Majd al-Din Ibn Taymiyya, Abu al-Faraj Ibn Abi al-Fahm and Nasir al-Din al-Baydawi, among others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5137}

## Works
His known Mukhtasar on law reaches the chapter on zakat and shows the learning and understanding of its author. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5137}
