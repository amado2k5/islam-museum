## Overview
Sulayman ibn Qutta al-Taymi, their client, of Basra — a reciter and one of the chief poets; he presented a complete recitation to Ibn 'Abbas and heard from Mu'awiya and 'Amr ibn al-'As, while 'Asim al-Jahdari read under him, and Musa ibn Abi 'A'isha, Humayd al-Tawil, Aban and others narrated from him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1922}
