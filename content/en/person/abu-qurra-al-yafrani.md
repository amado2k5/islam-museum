## Overview
Abu Qurra al-Yafrani al-Zanati, one of the chiefs of the [[group/banu-yafran|Banu Yafran]] in the region of Tlemcen at the start of the Abbasid state. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His account in Ibn Khaldun
Ibn Khaldun says he was king over his people in that region, and he besieged Umar ibn Hafs, the Umayyad governor, at Tubna until he forced him to return to Ifriqiya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun counts him among the notable Zanata of the early period in the central Maghrib, before leadership later settled with the Banu Yafran in Muhammad ibn Salih and his son Ya'la. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
