## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah, son of the vizier al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muqtadir appointed him vizier late in his caliphate, and that he was the one killed at al-Raqqa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.661}

The museum's data do not give the dates of his birth and death, which need an additional source.
