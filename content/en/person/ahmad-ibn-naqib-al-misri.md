## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Ahmad ibn Lu'lu' al-Rumi, Shihab al-Din Ibn al-Naqib: born in 706, his father was a Rum from Antioch freed by an amir whom he served as naqib, and who later became a Sufi at the Baybarsiyya lodge. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/239} He abridged al-Kifaya, wrote notes on al-Minhaj, and abridged al-Tanbih and then his own abridgement. Ibn Hajar quotes al-Isnawi describing him as learned in law, the readings, exegesis, legal theory and grammar, and as never signing a fatwa out of scruple. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/239} He died of plague in mid-Ramadan 769 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/240}
