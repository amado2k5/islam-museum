# Kolo Touré

## Summary

Kolo Touré is an Ivorian retired footballer and coach, born in Bouaké in 1981. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} A defender, he was part of Arsenal's "Invincibles" side that won the Premier League in 2003–04, won the title again with Manchester City in 2012, and played for Liverpool and Celtic; he remains the African player with the most Premier League appearances (353). {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} He won 120 caps for Ivory Coast and lifted the 2015 Africa Cup of Nations. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} The sources identify him generically as a Muslim and name no school for him, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en}{cite:wikidata-kolo-toure}

## Club career

Touré began at ASEC Mimosas and joined Arsenal in 2002, making 326 appearances and forming part of the unbeaten 2003–04 title side. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} He moved to Manchester City in 2009, where his younger brother Yaya joined him a year later, and helped the club to its first league title in 44 years in 2012; he signed for Liverpool in 2013 and later won the Scottish Premiership and League Cup with Celtic. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en}

## Ivory Coast

Touré won 120 caps for Ivory Coast between 2000 and 2015 — the second-highest total for his country — and played at the 2006, 2010 and 2014 World Cups. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} He was in the side that won the 2015 Africa Cup of Nations. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en} He is the elder brother of Yaya Touré. {cite:wikipedia-kolo-toure-en}
