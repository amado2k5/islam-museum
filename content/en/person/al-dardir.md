## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Adawi, Abu al-Barakat, known as al-Dardir, is described by al-Zirikli as a man of merit among the Maliki jurists. The entry gives his years as 1127 to 1201 AH, 1715 to 1786 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 220}.

## His life
He was born at Bani Adi in Egypt, studied at al-Azhar and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 220}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «Aqrab al-masalik li-madhhab al-Imam Malik», printed; «Manj al-taqdir», in two volumes, printed, a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Khalil in jurisprudence (the title is spelled so in the entry); and «Tuhfat al-ikhwan fi ilm al-bayan», printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 220}.
