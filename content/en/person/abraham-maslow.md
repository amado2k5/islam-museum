## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Abraham Maslow as the author of the 'hierarchy of human needs', published in 1943, ordering man's needs from the physiological to self-actualisation {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:32:30}.

## The pyramid
Al-Suwaidan explains that Maslow held that man is a social being with many concerns, but ordered: first the basic physiological needs of food, drink, shelter and reproduction, then belonging, then self-fulfilment {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:33:00}.
He adds that whoever wants to manage properly must make sure the base of the pyramid is complete before moving up, and that the pyramid is found in nearly every management book {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:33:30}.
