## Overview
Rashid ibn Hidaya Ahmad al-Ansari al-Gangohi was a scholar of hadith whose lineage, according to al-Zirikli, goes back to Abu Ayyub al-Ansari; the entry gives his years as 1244 to 1323 AH, that is 1828 to 1905 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.

## His life
He was born in Kankoh, a dependency of Saharanpur in India, and studied jurisprudence in Delhi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}. He took part in the revolt against the English in 1273 AH, 1857 CE, and was imprisoned for six months {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}. He then gave himself to teaching and issuing fatwas, made the pilgrimage three times, and when his sight failed devoted himself to worship until his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left writings; among those in Arabic is "al-Arf al-Shadhi", printed, a gloss on the Sunan of al-Tirmidhi that is made up of his lectures during his lessons, gathered by one of his pupils; the rest of his works are in Urdu {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 22}.
