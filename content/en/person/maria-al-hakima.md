## Overview
Maria the Copt, the Sage, among the philosophers of the Craft (alchemy) in Ibn al-Nadim's listing; attributed to her are the book of Maria the Copt with the sages when they gathered to her, and the Greater Book of Maria. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 536}
