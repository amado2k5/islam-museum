## Overview
Maria the Wise, also called the Copt, one of the philosophers of the craft (alchemy) according to Ibn al-Nadim. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.497}

## Her books
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to her two books: *The Book of Maria the Copt with the Sages when they gathered to her*, and *The Great Book of Maria*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.497}
