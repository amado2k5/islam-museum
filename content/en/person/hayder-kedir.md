## Summary
Hayder Kedir is an Ethiopian dawah preacher also known by the kunya Abu Hayder, and clips of his talks circulate on TikTok under his name and kunya. {cite:tiktok-dire-abuhayder-kedir} {cite:tiktok-abdela-shifa-hayder-kedir} His Facebook author page counted 320,000 followers in the read of a page post's metadata — the post dated August 2026 — made by this wave's harvest on 7 November 2026; Facebook fetch is blocked in the build environment, so that figure is the harvest's same-day read, not this record writer's, and the page's exact URL could not be pinned despite searching. {cite:facebook-hayder-kedir}

## Public voice
An Africa Press report on the sixth annual nationwide Grand Street Iftar stated that Ustaz Hayder Kedir is President of the Ethiopian Muslim Youth Council, and quoted him saying that year's programme was unique as it would take place simultaneously across the country, providing an opportunity for young people to strengthen their role in promoting unity, development, and social progress. {cite:africa-press-hayder-kedir-iftar} His remarks appeared in the same report alongside those of another preacher, Ustaz Abubeker Ahmed of the Ethiopian Islamic Affairs Supreme Council. {cite:africa-press-hayder-kedir-iftar}
