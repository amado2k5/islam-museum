# Hassan Sheikh Mohamud

Hassan Sheikh Mohamud (born 29 November 1955) is a Somali politician serving as president of Somalia since 2022, having previously held the office from 2012 to 2017 — the first Somali president to serve two non-consecutive terms.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}{cite:source/wikidata-hassan-sheikh-mohamud}

## Education and civic career

Mohamud was born at Jalalaqsi in the Hiran region, attended primary and secondary schools in his hometown, and moved to Mogadishu in 1978.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en} He taught at the Lafole technical secondary school and joined the Somali National University-affiliated Technical Teachers' Training College in 1984, becoming its department head in 1986, then completed a master's degree in technical education at Bhopal University in India in 1988.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en} When civil war broke out he remained in Somalia, working as an education officer for UNICEF in the central and southern regions from 1993 to 1995 and as a consultant to NGOs and UN bureaus; in 1999 he co-established the Somali Institute of Management and Administration in Mogadishu, which grew into SIMAD University, and served as its dean until 2010.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}

## First presidency

In 2011 Mohamud founded the Peace and Development Party and was elected its chairman; in August 2012 he entered the newly formed Federal Parliament, and on 10 September 2012 parliament elected him president, defeating the incumbent Sharif Sheikh Ahmed.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en} His first administration governed under the provisional constitution, fought al-Shabaab alongside African Union forces, and saw the partial lifting of the UN arms embargo; he survived assassination attempts and two no-confidence efforts, and left office after losing the February 2017 election to Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}

## Second presidency

On 15 May 2022 Mohamud won the presidential election against the incumbent Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed and took office on 23 May 2022, succeeding the man who had succeeded him.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en} He appointed Hamza Abdi Barre prime minister, and in October 2022 defended his doctoral dissertation in peace, governance and development studies at the University for Peace — the first sitting Somali president to earn a doctorate, according to the presidency.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en} His second administration has pursued an offensive against al-Shabaab and, since May 2026, faced an opposition dispute over his continuation in office: he maintains that constitutional amendments adopted in 2024 set a five-year term running to 2027, while the opposition holds that his term expired on 15 May 2026.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}

## A note on the record

On faith, the sources offer filings only: the English, Arabic and Urdu articles each file him under Somali Muslims, no text in any language discusses his religion or names a school, and Wikidata carries no religion value for him at all.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-ar}{cite:source/wikidata-hassan-sheikh-mohamud} His faith is therefore entered as unknown.

His first accession also explains a gap in the museum's succession chain: he took office in 2012 from an acting president, Mohamed Osman Jawari, who has no record here, so no succession edge is written for 2012; the edge written is his 2022 succession of Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed.{cite:source/wikipedia-hassan-sheikh-mohamud-en}
