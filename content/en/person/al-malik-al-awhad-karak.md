## Overview
Al-Malik al-Awhad, lord of al-Karak of the Ayyubid house, son of al-Nasir Dawud; he heard from Ibn al-Latti, and al-Dimyati transmitted from him in his Mu'jam; he died at Jerusalem in Dhu'l-Hijja aged seventy {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 774}.
