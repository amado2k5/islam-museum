## Summary
İsa Çelebi, son of Bayezid I, escaped the field of Ankara in 804/1402 and reached Bursa, where he proclaimed himself his father's successor — one of the three brothers fighting over the fragments of the state alongside Süleyman at Edirne and Mehmed at Amasya {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 70}.

The fratricidal war lasted ten years until the youngest, Mehmed I, emerged sole victor in 1413 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 70-73}.
