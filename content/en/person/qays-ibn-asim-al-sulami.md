## Overview
Qays ibn Asim al-Sulami killed Sulayman ibn Marthad in his war with Abd Allah ibn Khazim over the governorship of Khurasan, and cut off his head. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The entry notes that he is not Qays ibn Asim al-Minqari al-Tamimi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
