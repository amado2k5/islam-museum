## Summary
Qays ibn Asim al-Minqari al-Tamimi, a chief of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]] famed for his forbearance; he became Muslim and the Prophet put him over the alms of part of Banu Sa'd in year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

## His alms work
Qays ibn Asim was among the alms-collectors the Prophet sent to the tribes in the ninth year, holding the alms over part of Banu Sa'd with [[person/al-zibriqan-ibn-badr|al-Zibriqan ibn Badr]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

## For young visitors
Qays was a chief known for his patience and forbearance; he became Muslim and the Prophet entrusted him with gathering his people's alms.
