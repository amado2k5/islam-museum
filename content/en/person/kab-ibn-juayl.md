## Summary

Ka'b ibn Ju'ayl ibn Qumayr al-Taghlibi, the celebrated poet. Ibn Fathun counts him among the Companions on al-Baghawi's authority; he had a known exchange with Mu'awiya over Khalid ibn al-Walid. He was the poet of the people of Syria, witnessed Siffin on Mu'awiya's side, and lived to join a delegation to al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik.
