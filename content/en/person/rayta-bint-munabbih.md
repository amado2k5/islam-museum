## Summary

Rayta bint Munabbih ibn al-Hajjaj al-Sahmiyya, mother of Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As. She embraced Islam and gave the pledge; Ibn Sa'd reports her conversion on the day of the conquest of Mecca.
