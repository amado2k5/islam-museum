## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Ja'far ibn Durustawayh, a grammarian of Baghdad. Like al-Mubarrad, he held the *Kitab al-Ayn* in high regard and transmitted it. {cite:duha-islam p.594-595}

## His link with al-Zajjaj
Ahmad Amin says that almost all of the linguistic reports of Abu Ishaq al-Zajjaj in Arabic are found only through him. {cite:duha-islam p.594-595}
