## Overview
Abd al-Ghani ibn Jamil was a learned man, a poet and one of the notables of Baghdad. Al-Zirikli lists him in al-A'lam under the heading Ibn Jamil and gives his years as 1194 to 1279 AH, 1780 to 1863 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 33}.

## Life
He held the post of mufti of the Hanafis in Baghdad and was the head of the family known there as Al Jamil {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 33}. Al-Zirikli records that Abdullah Bahauddin al-Alusi wrote a book about him, titled al-Rawd al-Khamil fi Madaih Abd al-Ghani al-Jamil (as the title is printed) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 33}.
