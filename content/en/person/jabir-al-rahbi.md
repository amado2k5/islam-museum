## Summary
Jabir al-Rahbi had delicate states and graceful kindnesses. ## The race across the bridge
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'qub relates that he heard al-Junayd ibn Muhammad say: Abu Ja'far al-Khassaf told me that Jabir al-Rahbi said to him one day as they walked: let us race; you pass this way and I will pass that way. Khassaf crossed the bridge, and when he had gone some distance he turned and saw Jabir walking on the water, the water spraying from under his feet as dust rises under a walker's foot. When they met, Khassaf said: who does well at such a thing, I walk on the bridge and you walk on the water! Jabir said: have you seen me? Khassaf said: yes. Jabir said: you are a righteous man. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/167}
