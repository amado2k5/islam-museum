## Summary
Taj al-Dawla Ja'far ibn Thiqat al-Dawla Yusuf ibn 'Abdallah al-Kalbi, lord of Sicily; an adib and poet, author of the famous verses on two pages, one in red brocade and one in black: 'I see two full moons risen upon two branches / in mother-of-pearl, and in two robes dyed / by the dyer of the limit and the pupil' — he composed them in 527. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
