## Overview
'Ilm al-Din al-Bulqini, the Egyptian Shafi'i, qadi and copyist, shaykh al-Islam — bearer of the Shafi'i banner in his age; born 815, he read fiqh under the scholars of his time such as al-Zayn al-'Iraqi, al-'Ala' al-Tabari, and Shaykh 'Abd al-Rahim, cousin of the author of Shadharat al-dhahab, and others; he memorised much of the madhhab and specialised in fatwa in his town while still under twenty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 308}

## His judgeship
He held the judgeship of Damascus and of Egypt — though he refused much of its perquisites — remaining in the judgeship twenty-six years and some months; he memorised the Qur'an and a foundation of logic, and people describe his excellence — though his time was short. He died Wednesday with five nights left of Rajab 868. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 308}
