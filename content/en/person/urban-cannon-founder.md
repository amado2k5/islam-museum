## Overview
Orban, a Hungarian engineer masterful in cannon-founding, brought in by Mehmed II to prepare the conquest of Constantinople.

## The Sultan's cannon
The sultan received him handsomely and furnished every resource; Orban designed the great guns, foremost the famous Sultan's cannon — the book gives its weight in the hundreds of tons and hundreds of oxen to move it — while Mehmed himself supervised their casting and proving {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 92}.

## His end
Al-Salabi records that the great cannon burst from overuse late in the siege, killing its crew with Orban at their head {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 104}.
