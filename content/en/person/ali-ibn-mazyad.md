## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Mazyad al-Asadi, the chief of the [[group/banu-mazyad|Banu Mazyad]]. Ibn Khaldun says that he was the founder of their affair, and places him in the line of the rulers of the [[polity/mazyadid-dynasty|Mazyadid state]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His conduct
He sought revenge for his brother Abu al-Ghana'im, who was killed in 401 AH. He defeated the Banu Dubays, killed Hassan and Nabhan, and seized the Dubaysi island. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Fakhr al-Dawla entrusted him with its affair, except al-Tayyib and Qarqub. Later Mudar ibn Dubays rose against him and took it from him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Death
He died in 408 AH (about 1017 CE; the conversion is derived). His son Dubays had come to power in his lifetime: Sultan al-Dawla invested him and authorised his governorship. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Notes for researchers
- The information is from Ibn Khaldun alone; it needs an additional source.
