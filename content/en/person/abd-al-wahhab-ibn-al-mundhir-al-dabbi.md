## Summary
Abd al-Wahhab ibn al-Mundhir al-Dabbi was a jurist and worshipper who fasted and prayed much. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 673, sequence 674, on page 393. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}

## Finishing the Qur'an each day
He finished the Qur'an every day, and this was his practice until he died. He narrated from Mu'tamir ibn Sulayman. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}

## His saying: the beginning of good is seeking forgiveness
His father relates from him that everything has a beginning, and the beginning of good is seeking forgiveness; he cited a verse on asking God for forgiveness, which he understood as one that keeps building up those who seek forgiveness. The verse is referred to by sense only. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/393}
