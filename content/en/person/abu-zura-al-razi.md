## Overview
'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd al-Karim b. Yazid b. Farukh Abu Zur'a al-Razi, mawla of al-'Abbas b. Mutarrif al-Qurashi; the ninth century's hafiz, partner of Ibn Abi Hatim in jarh and ta'dil. He died in 264 {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 136}.
