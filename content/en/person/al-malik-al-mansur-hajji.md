## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur Hajji; he held the sultanate after his uncle al-Nasir Hasan in Jumada I 762 — Yalbugha steered his state and travelled with him to Syria, he being about fifteen; back from the journey his commands and prohibitions multiplied, so Yalbugha spread it that he was mad and deposed him in Sha'ban 764 after three years, two months and five days — he was held in the hawsh where al-Malik al-Nasir's offspring dwelt until he died on 9 Muharram 801, leaving ten sons for whom al-Malik al-Zahir fixed a stipend. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 18}
