## Summary
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Umar al-Marrakushi was a Maghribi scholar renowned in astronomy, mathematics, geography and the making of sundials; the Bibliotheca Alexandrina book says he drew a map of the Maghrib correcting Ptolemy's errors and was the first to use, on a map, longitude lines marking equal hours, and gives the date 660/1261 with his name. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 16}

## Works
They include a treatise on sighting the new moon and *Jami' al-mabadi' wal-ghayat fi ilm al-miqat*. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 16}
