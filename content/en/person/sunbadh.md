## Overview
[[person/sunbadh|Sunbadh the Magian]], from the village of Ahrawana on the outskirts of Nishapur, who led a movement that arose in the year 137 AH after the killing of Abu Muslim al-Khurasani {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.53-55}.

## The movement
Sunbadh rose seeking revenge for Abu Muslim against Abu Ja'far al-Mansur. Many followers came after him. He prevailed over Nishapur, Qumis and al-Rayy, took the title "Firuz Asbadh", seized Abu Muslim's treasuries, and announced that he would attack Mecca and destroy the Ka'ba in revenge {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.54}.

## His end
al-Mansur sent an army under Jahwar ibn Marrar al-Ijli against him, and it crushed him in the year 137 AH, seventy days after the movement began {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.55}.

## Notes for researchers
- Taqqush holds that the movement had three motives: political, in avenging Abu Muslim; doctrinal, drawing its principles from the Khurramiyya and the Mazdakiyya; and racial, aiming to revive the Persian state. This is the author's assessment, and we have not yet verified it against primary sources {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.53-54}.
