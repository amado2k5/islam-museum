## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahmud ibn Ubaydan al-Ba'li, Zayn al-Din, Abu al-Faraj, a Hanbali jurist who heard hadith, studied fiqh under Taqi al-Din and others, and gave fatwas. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15809} Ibn Rajab says he was a leading authority in fiqh and its fine points, usul, hadith, grammar and Sufism, and that he trained a circle of students. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15809} His works include al-Mutli fi al-ahkam ala abwab al-Muqni, a partial commentary on al-Muqni, and a collection of the additions of al-Kafi and al-Muharrir to al-Muqni.
