## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad Ba Kathir al-Hadrami, a scholar of the Ba Kathir family in Hadramawt. The family has scholarly achievements and mosques in Tarim and Sayun. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 1283 AH and his death as 1355 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
