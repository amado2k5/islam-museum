## Summary
Maymuna al-Sawda' was a devout woman of [[place/kufa|Kufa]], placed by Ibn al-Jawzi in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] first among "the chosen women among the wise fools, devout women of Kufa". {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.605}

## Abd al-Wahid ibn Zayd's dream
He relates through [[person/al-fudayl-ibn-iyad|al-Fudayl ibn Iyad]] that [[person/abd-al-wahid-ibn-zayd|Abd al-Wahid ibn Zayd]] said: I asked God for three nights to show me my companion in Paradise, and saw someone say, "Your companion in Paradise is Maymuna al-Sawda', among such-and-such a family in Kufa." He went to Kufa and asked about her, and was told: she is a madwoman among us who herds a few sheep. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.605} He found her, and saw wolves among the sheep, the sheep not afraid of them and the wolves not eating them. When he asked her about it she said, "Leave me be — I have set right what is between me and my Master, so He has set right what is between the wolves and the sheep." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.606}

## For young visitors
A poor shepherdess of Kufa whom people thought mad — but a devout man saw in a dream that she was one of the people of Paradise.
