## Summary
Ahmad ibn Said ibn Ibrahim al-Hamdani al-Qurtubi, Abu Umar, known as Ibn al-Hindi, a trustworthy Maliki jurist who was the foremost of his time in the science of legal documents. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2051}

## Works
He studied with Abu Ibrahim Ishaq ibn Ibrahim; his work is 'Diwan al-watha'iq' in six parts. Born 320 AH, died 399 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2051}
