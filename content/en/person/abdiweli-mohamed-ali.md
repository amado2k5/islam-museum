# Abdiweli Mohamed Ali

Abdiweli Mohamed Ali (born 2 July 1965), known as Abdiweli Gaas, is a Somali economist and politician who served as prime minister of Somalia from 2011 to 2012 and as president of Puntland from 2014 to 2019.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en}{cite:source/wikidata-abdiweli-mohamed-ali}

## An economist in America

Ali was born at Dhusamareb in the Galgadud region and educated at the Somali National University, Vanderbilt University, Harvard University and George Mason University.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en} He settled in the United States and joined Niagara University in 2003, where he was an associate professor of economics when Somalia's president called him home.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en}

## Prime minister of Somalia

Ali succeeded Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed as prime minister on 19 June 2011, under President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en} His premiership produced the "Roadmap for the End of Transition", the political process that set benchmarks toward permanent democratic institutions and the end of the transitional period in 2012; he left office on 17 October 2012 and sat briefly in the new Federal Parliament.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en}

## President of Puntland

On 8 January 2014 Ali was elected president of Puntland, defeating the incumbent Abdirahman Mohamud Farole.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en} His five-year administration launched a biometric fishery database, taxation and development programmes, road, airport and seaport projects, and the Galgala campaign against al-Shabaab; he lost the January 2019 election to Said Deni and handed over on 8 January 2019.{cite:source/wikipedia-abdiweli-mohamed-ali-en}
