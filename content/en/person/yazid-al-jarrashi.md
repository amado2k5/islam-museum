## Overview
Yazid al-Jarrashi, Abu Salim, one of the commanders whom Ibn Amir sent in the conquest of Khurasan. He sent him to the district of Zam from Nishapur, and he conquered it by force. Then he conquered Bakharz, a district of Nishapur, and conquered Juwayn, and took captives {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
