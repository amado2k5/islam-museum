## Overview
Hisham al-Dastuwai, one of the companions of [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]] and [[person/muhammad-ibn-sirin|Ibn Sirin]], as al-Shirazi mentions in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 48}

Al-Shirazi does not mention the date of his death.
