## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Abd al-Majid ibn al-Ajami al-Halabi, a friend of Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.439}

## In Aleppo
Ibn al-Athir mentions that he and his household were leading figures among the Sunnis of Aleppo, and that he was a man of abundant generosity and good character. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.439}

## His death
He died on the twelfth of Rabi' al-Awwal 628 AH (around February 1231 CE, the conversion is derived). {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.439}

## Notes for researchers
- The date comes from Ibn al-Athir alone. A second source is needed.
