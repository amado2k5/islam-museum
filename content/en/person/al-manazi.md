## Overview
Abu Nasr Ahmad b. Yusuf al-Sulayki al-Manazi, katib; among the notables of the excellent and the exemplars of poets — vizier to Abu Nasr Ahmad b. Marwan the Kurd, lord of Mayyafariqin and Diyar Bakr — and envoy to Constantinople more than once. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His library; with al-Ma'arri
He gathered many books then endowed them on the congregational mosques of Mayyafariqin and Amid — they remain in the two mosques' treasuries known as the Kutub al-Manazi; and he met Abu'l-'Ala' al-Ma'arri at Ma'arrat al-Nu'man. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
