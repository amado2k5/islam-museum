## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ishaq al-Ibadi, one of the leaders of the Ibadis, and one of those who wrote books. Ibn al-Nadim lists two of his books: *al-Radd 'ala al-Qadariyya* (A Refutation of the Qadariyya) and *al-Imama* (The Imamate). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.259}
