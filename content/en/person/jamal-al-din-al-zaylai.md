## Overview
Abdallah ibn Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Zaylai, Abu Muhammad, Jamal al-Din, was a jurist learned in hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}. Al-Zirikli says his origin was in Zayla in Somalia and that he died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}. He gives his death as 762 AH, 1360 CE, and prints no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli lists Nasb al-Raya fi Takhrij Ahadith al-Hidaya, printed, a work in the Hanafi school, and Takhrij Ahadith al-Kashshaf, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}. Al-Zirikli distinguishes him from [[person/uthman-al-zaylai|Uthman al-Zaylai]], the commentator on al-Kanz {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}. The page also prints a plate of a manuscript page of al-Shama'il, held in the library of Hasan Husni Abd al-Wahhab in Tunis, captioned under the name of Abdallah ibn Yusuf al-Zaylai {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 147}.
