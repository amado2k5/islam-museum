## Summary
Abu Bakr Muhammad b. al-Walid b. Muhammad b. Khalaf al-Fihri al-Andalusi al-Tartushi, the Maliki jurist and ascetic known as Ibn Abi Randaqa; he kept company with Abu'l-Walid al-Baji at Zaragoza, learning khilaf from him, and read adab under Abu Muhammad Ibn Hazm at Seville. In 476 he journeyed east — hajj, Baghdad, Basra — studied fiqh under Abu Bakr al-Shashi al-Mustazhiri and Abu Ahmad al-Jurjani, and settled in Syria to teach. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Preaching and works
An imam, scholar, devout and austere ascetic; he preached to al-Afdal Shahanshah, vizier of al-Musta'li, until he wept. His works include *Siraj al-muluk*, *Birr al-walidayn*, *al-Fitan* and *Siraj al-huda*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
