## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur Nasir al-Din Abu'l-Ma'ali Muhammad b. 'Umar (d. 617, Hama) — son and successor of al-Malik al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din 'Umar as Ayyubid ruler of Hama {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
