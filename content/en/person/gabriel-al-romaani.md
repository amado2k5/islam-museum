# Gabriel Al Romaani

## Summary
Gabriel Al Romaani is a Romanian-born dawah speaker and teacher. Muslim Central's biography of him records that he was born in Romania to a Christian family, migrated to Canada in 1998, and converted to Islam in 2003 after studying it for some years; he co-founded the ICRAA da'wah organisation, studied at Mishkah University, and authored Journal of a New Muslim. {cite:listennotes-gabriel-al-romaani}

## Channel and later work
His lectures circulate through his own YouTube channel, and he dates his online dawah work from January 2007. {cite:youtube-gabriel-al-romaani} A later Deen Show profile describes him as a school principal in Kuala Lumpur and a global educational consultant, where the older Muslim Central biography had placed him teaching in the United Arab Emirates. {cite:thedeenshow-gabriel-al-romaani}

## Positions and faith
Read this section as the report of the sources, not as a museum ruling.

His conversion to Islam in 2003 is documented in his standard biography. {cite:listennotes-gabriel-al-romaani} No source names a school for him personally, so no faith value is recorded; his Christianity belongs to his past before his conversion.
