## Overview
[[person/umm-jamil|Umm Jamil bint Harb ibn Umayya]], sister of [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan ibn Harb]] and wife of [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]]. She was among the people most hostile to the Prophet {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86}.

## Her hostility to the Prophet
She used to carry thorns and place them on the Prophet's path and at his door at night, and she spread her tongue against him with slander and malice, stirring up strife {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86}. For this the surah of al-Masad names her as a carrier of firewood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86}; see [[passage/quran-111|the surah of al-Masad]].

## After she heard what had been revealed about her
When news reached her of what had been revealed about her and her husband, she came to the Prophet while he was sitting in the mosque by the Ka'ba with Abu Bakr, and she had a stone in her hand to throw at him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86}. In another report about Abu Bakr, when she found him struck down after the assault of the polytheists of the Banu Taym, she said: "By God, a people who have done this to you are people of wickedness and disbelief" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.120}.

## For young visitors
Umm Jamil was a woman who was angry at the Prophet and stood in his path with thorns. God revealed in the surah of al-Masad about her and her husband.

## Notes for researchers
- The description of the thorns and the stone is taken from al-Raheeq, which relies on the books of the sira. The original text in Ibn Ishaq in the accepted edition should be checked.
- The citation of Abu Bakr's encounter (p.120) is brief. See [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr al-Siddiq]] when the entry on him is completed.

## In open preaching (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum, pp.86-87)
She carried thorns and placed them on the Prophet's path and at his door at night, and extended her tongue with abuse and slander, so the Qur'an names her as a carrier of firewood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86}. When what had been revealed about her and her husband reached her, she came to the Prophet while he was in the mosque by the Ka'ba with Abu Bakr, with a stone in her hand. God took away her sight from him, so she saw only Abu Bakr. She said that she had composed verse in which she said "We disobeyed Mudhammam", and she went away. Abu Bakr asked the Prophet: "Did she not see you?" He said: "She did not see me; God took away her sight from me" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.86} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.87}.
