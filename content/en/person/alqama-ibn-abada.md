## Overview
Alqama ibn Abada ibn Nashira ibn Qays, of the Banu Tamim, was a pre-Islamic poet of the first rank, known in the heading of the entry as Alqama al-Fahl. The entry dates his death to about 20 years before the Hijra, equal to about 603 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 246}.

## Career
He was a contemporary of Imru' al-Qays and exchanged verse contests with him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 246}. Al-Harith ibn Abi Shamir al-Ghassani took a brother of his, named Shas, captive; Alqama interceded for him and praised al-Harith in verses, and al-Harith set him free {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 246}.

## Works
He has a printed Diwan of poetry, which al-A'lam al-Shantamari explained in a commentary {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 246}.

## Death
The entry gives no birth year and dates his death to about 20 years before the Hijra, equal to about 603 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 246}.
