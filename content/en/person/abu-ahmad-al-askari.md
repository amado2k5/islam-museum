## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Abdallah ibn Sa'id ibn Isma'il al-Askari, Abu Ahmad, is described by al-Zirikli as a jurist and man of letters to whom, in his age, the headship of hadith transmission, dictation and teaching came in the land of Khuzistan. The entry gives his years as 293 to 382 AH, 906 to 993 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## His life
He was born at Askar Mukram, in the districts of al-Ahwaz, from which his name comes, moved to Baghdad and travelled to Basra, Isfahan and other places; his fame rose, and eminent men travelled to him to study under him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}. He was the maternal uncle of Abu Hilal al-Hasan ibn Abdallah ibn Sahl al-Askari, and his teacher {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «al-Zawajir wa-l-mawa'iz»; «al-Tafdil bayna balaghati al-Arab wa-l-Ajam», printed; «al-Hikam wa-l-amthal»; «Rahat al-arwah»; «Tashifat al-muhaddithin», in manuscript, which he thinks may be the book printed as «Sharh ma yaqa' fihi al-tashif wa-l-tahrif»; «Tashih al-wujuh wa-l-naza'ir»; «al-Masun», on literature, printed; and «Sina'at al-shi'r» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.
