## Overview
Aisha bint Talha, distinguished in literature and generosity like her father Talha. She is mentioned together with [[person/sakina-bint-al-husayn|Sakina bint al-Husayn]] in literary gatherings. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.144}

She was married to [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Musab ibn al-Zubayr]], ruler of Iraq during the caliphate of his brother Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr (67–72 AH). The book says he gave her a dower of one million dirhams. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.145}
