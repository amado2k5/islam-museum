## Overview
'A'isha bint Talha ibn 'Ubaydallah al-Taymiyya, daughter of the Mother of the Faithful 'A'isha's sister Umm Kulthum bint al-Siddiq; her cousin 'Abdallah ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr married her, then after him the amir of Iraq Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr, who gave her a dower of a hundred thousand dinars. She was the most beautiful and noblest woman of her time, and her hadith is included in the Sahih collections. She died around 110 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2077}
