# Jawed Karim

Jawed Karim (born 28 October 1979) is a German and American software engineer and internet entrepreneur of Bangladeshi descent, a co-founder of YouTube in 2005 and the first person to upload a video to it. He was born in Merseburg, East Germany, to a Bangladeshi father, a researcher, and a German mother, a biochemistry scientist, and was raised between Germany and the United States. {cite:wikipedia-jawed-karim-en} {cite:wikipedia-jawed-karim-ar} {cite:wikidata-jawed-karim}

## YouTube

Karim worked at PayPal, where he designed core components including its real-time anti-fraud system, and there met his later co-founders Steve Chen and Chad Hurley. In 2005 he co-founded YouTube, and on 23 April 2005 he uploaded the platform's first video, "Me at the zoo", which has since been viewed hundreds of millions of times. He studied computer science at the University of Illinois and took a master's degree at Stanford. {cite:wikipedia-jawed-karim-en} {cite:wikipedia-jawed-karim-ur}
