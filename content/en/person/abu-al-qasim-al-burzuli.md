## Overview
Abu al-Qasim ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Balawi al-Qayrawani, known as al-Burzuli, was one of the Maliki imams of the Maghrib. He made the pilgrimage, passed through Cairo in 800 AH, and settled in Tunis, where the giving of fatwas came to rest with him; he was styled shaykh al-Islam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171} {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 172}. He lived long: al-Zirikli reports that al-Sakhawi said he died in Tunis at the age of a hundred and three {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 172}.

## His works
Among his books al-Zirikli names 'Jami' Masa'il al-Ahkam mimma Nazala min al-Qadaya lil-Muftin wa-l-Hukkam', in manuscript in two volumes, which he says may be an abridgement of al-Burzuli's own 'al-Fatawa', also in manuscript. Al-Zirikli himself acquired a fine copy of it in four volumes divided into six, written in 982 AH, which the copyist titled 'al-Fatawi' at the beginning, in the manner of the easterners, and 'al-Nawazil' at the end, in the manner of the Maghribis. He also names 'al-Diwan al-Kabir' in jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 172}.
