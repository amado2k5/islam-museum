## Summary
Ibn al-Faqih al-Hamadhani was an author on countries whom [[person/al-muqaddasi|al-Muqaddasi]] lists among his predecessors in the preface of the [[work/ahsan-al-taqasim|Ahsan al-taqasim]]. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.14}

## His book according to al-Muqaddasi
Al-Muqaddasi says Ibn al-Faqih took another path than al-Jayhani and al-Balkhi: he mentioned only the great cities, did not order the districts and provinces, and filled his book with branches of learning unsuited to it, "now urging renunciation of the world, now desire for it, now making one weep, now laugh and be amused". {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.14} In the Constantinople manuscript's version, given in the edition's apparatus, he says he saw Ibn al-Faqih's book in five volumes and that, reading it, he would fall into stories and digressions until he forgot which country he was in, which he did not approve, although he himself included stories and debates suited to his subject. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.15} This is al-Muqaddasi's assessment.
