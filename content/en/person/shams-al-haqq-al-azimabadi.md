## Summary
Abu al-Tayyib Muhammad, called Shams al-Haqq, b. Amir b. 'Ali b. Haydar al-Siddiqi al-'Azimabadi — as he names himself in the preface to [[work/al-taliq-al-mughni|al-Ta'liq al-Mughni 'ala al-Daraqutni]]. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 5}

## Teachers and chains
He says he read part of [[work/sunan-al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni's Sunan]] with Sayyid Muhammad Nadhir Husayn al-Dihlawi, whom he calls "the head of the hadith scholars of his age", who licensed him for the whole book; Nadhir Husayn transmitted it from Muhammad Ishaq al-Dihlawi, from [[person/shah-abd-al-aziz-dihlawi|'Abd al-'Aziz b. Wali Allah]], from his father [[person/shah-waliullah-al-dahlawi|Wali Allah al-Dihlawi]]. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 10} He also read portions of it with the judge Husayn b. Muhsin al-Ansari al-Yamani, who licensed him for all of it; Husayn b. Muhsin transmitted it from teachers including Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Ali al-Shawkani. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 11}

## Method
He describes his notes as criticism of some of the Sunan's hadiths, explanation of their defects and clarification of some points, briefly, drawn from the books of the discipline. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 5} He defends [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni]] against al-'Ayni's weakening of him. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9}
