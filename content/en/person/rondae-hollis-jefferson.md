Rondae Hollis-Jefferson (born 3 January 1995) is an American basketball forward from Chester, Pennsylvania. He played at Arizona and in the NBA from 2015 before continuing his career internationally, later representing Jordan after naturalisation.{cite:wikipedia-rondae-hollis-jefferson-en}{cite:wikidata-rondae-hollis-jefferson}

His Islam is on record in his own words: "Me being Muslim, me knowing a lot of Muslims, it's definitely, definitely heartbreaking to see," he said in 2017.{cite:si-hollis-jefferson-2017} In 2025 he was still observing Ramadan with prayers and fasting while playing in the Philippines.{cite:spin-hollis-jefferson-2025} No source names a school or tradition for him.
