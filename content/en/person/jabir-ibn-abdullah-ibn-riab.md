## Overview
[[person/jabir-ibn-abdullah-ibn-riab|Jabir ibn Abdallah ibn Riab]] al-Ansari al-Sulami, "one of the six who witnessed the First Pledge of al-Aqaba". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.195} He is not [[person/jabir-ibn-abdullah|Jabir ibn Abdallah ibn Amr ibn Haram]], the well-known Companion and transmitter.

## Al-Aqaba and Badr
Ibn Ishaq relates from elders of the people of Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada that when the Prophet met the six of the Ansar, they were As'ad ibn Zurara, Jabir ibn Abdallah ibn Riab, Qutba ibn Amir, Rafi' ibn Malik, Uqba ibn Amir ibn Zayd and Awf ibn Malik, and they embraced Islam. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.195} Musa ibn Uqba relays from Ibn Shihab, and Abu al-Aswad from Urwa, that he was among those who witnessed Badr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.195}

## Transmissions
Ibn Abd al-Barr says that he has a hadith in al-Kalbi's version, from Abu Salih, from him, and that he knows no other. Ibn Hajar adds: "Indeed, hadiths have come from Jabir ibn Abdallah ibn Riab by weak chains," and al-Baghawi and Ibn al-Sakan narrate through al-Waza' ibn Nafi' the hadith of the passing of Mikha'il; al-Baghawi says al-Waza' is very weak. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.195} Al-Bukhari in *al-Tarikh* relates, through Ibn Ishaq, from al-Kalbi, from Abu Salih, from Jabir ibn Riab, the story of Abu Yasir ibn Akhtab; Yunus ibn Bukayr relates it in *al-Maghazi* from Ibn Abbas and Jabir ibn Riab, "as if he had ascribed Jabir to his grandfather". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.195}
