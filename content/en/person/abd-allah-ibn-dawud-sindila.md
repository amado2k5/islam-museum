## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Dawud al-Sindila was one of the worshippers, good and excellent, whose prayer was answered; he transmitted much. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 671, sequence 672, on page 392. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}

## Narrating from al-Husayn ibn Hafs
He narrated from al-Husayn ibn Hafs. Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Mandah relates from Abd Allah ibn Dawud: among the signs of truth is hatred of those who follow desire; whoever loves the truth must hate the people of desire, meaning those who turned from the traditions and followed opinions. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}
