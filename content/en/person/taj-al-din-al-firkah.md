## Overview
Taj al-Din al-Firkah, jurist of Syria and shaykh al-Islam; he read fiqh under Ibn al-Salah and Ibn 'Abd al-Salam, sat to teach at twenty-odd and was writing on fatwas at thirty — fatwas reached him from every quarter, and most of the qadis of Syria and its environs were his pupils. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 722-724}

## His rank
Al-Dhahabi said: "the headship of the madhhab in the world ended with him, and he was of those who reached the rank of ijtihad"; Ibn Qadi Shuhba said he was "more juristically profound and of purer talent than al-Nawawi, with more memorised than he" — and between him and al-Nawawi there was estrangement. He taught at the Badara'iyya and was buried in the Bab al-Saghir cemetery. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 722-724}
