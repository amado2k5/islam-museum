## Summary
Muhammad ibn Hasan al-Laqani, Shams al-Din, Abu Abd Allah, of Egypt, a Maliki jurist who knew the school by heart and was a verifier. He studied under Ahmad Zarruq and others, and people benefited from him in fatwas and teaching. He was the brother of Nasir al-Din al-Laqani. He wrote careful glosses (tarar) on Khalil's Mukhtasar. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1641}
