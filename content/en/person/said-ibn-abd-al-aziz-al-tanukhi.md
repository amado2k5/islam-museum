## Overview
Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Tanukhi, Abu Muhammad. A jurist of the people of Syria, after al-Awza'i, according to al-Shirazi {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}.

## His death
He died in Damascus in the year 166 AH {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}. He lived in [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. This corresponds roughly to 782 CE; the conversion is derived {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}.
