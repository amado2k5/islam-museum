## Summary

Al-Harith ibn Uqba ibn Qabus of Muzayna. Al-Waqidi's Maghazi relates that he and his uncle Wahb ibn Qabus came to Medina with their flock and found it empty, went out to the Prophet at Uhud, became Muslims and fought until both were killed. Umar used to say that the death he would most love was the death of the two Muzanis. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.263}
