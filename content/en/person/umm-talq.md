## Summary
Umm Talq was a devout woman of [[place/basra|Basra]] recorded by Ibn al-Jawzi in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.713}

## Her worship and sayings
He relates from Shu'ba ibn Dukhan that she prayed four hundred rak'as every day and night and recited as much of the Qur'an as God willed, and from Asim al-Jahdari her words, "I have not given my soul what it craves since God gave me authority over it", and "The soul is a king if you follow it, and a slave if you tire it." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.713} From Sufyan ibn Uyayna, that she told her son Talq, "How beautiful your voice in the Qur'an is — may it not be a burden on you on the Day of Resurrection", and he wept until he fainted. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.713}

## For young visitors
A devout woman of Basra who prayed a great deal and used to say that the self becomes your master if you obey it in everything it wants.
