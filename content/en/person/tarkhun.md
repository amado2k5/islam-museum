## Overview
Tarkhun, king of Samarqand. He honoured Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim when he came to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Then a man among Musa's companions leapt upon a man of Sughd and killed him, and Tarkhun expelled him and those with him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
