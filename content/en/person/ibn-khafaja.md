## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibn Khafaja, the well-known poet of al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.24}

## Meeting Abu al-Arab al-Tujibi
Al-Maqqari relates that Abu al-Arab al-Tujibi met him and asked after his condition when he had reached eighty-one years, and he recited verses of his own about his age. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.333}

## On al-Andalus
Al-Maqqari quotes verses from his description of al-Andalus, in which he compares it to Paradise. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.24}
