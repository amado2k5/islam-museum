## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdun al-Julluli, a jurist attributed to Jullula, a place near Qayrawan according to the source. The source says he died before 450 AH (1057 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
