# Muhammadu Buhari

Muhammadu Buhari (17 December 1942 – 13 July 2025) was a Nigerian officer and politician from Daura in Katsina State who ruled Nigeria twice: as military head of state from the end of 1983 to 1985, and as elected president from 2015 to 2023. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en} {cite:wikidata-muhammadu-buhari}

## Early life and military career

Buhari was born in the village of Daura in northern Nigeria on 17 December 1942, to a Muslim family as the Arabic article puts it, and was named after the hadith scholar Muhammad al-Bukhari. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-ar} {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en} He joined the army and trained as an officer, holding military and administrative posts including Federal Commissioner for Petroleum and Natural Resources (1976–1978) and briefly governor of Borno State in 1976. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en}

## Military rule and detention

On 31 December 1983 the army overthrew President Shehu Shagari's government and Buhari became military head of state; his rule is remembered for the "War Against Indiscipline". {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en} On 27 August 1985 Ibrahim Babangida overthrew him in turn, and Buhari was detained for about three years, until his release in December 1988. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en}

## Candidacies and civilian presidency

Buhari ran unsuccessfully for the presidency in 2003, 2007 and 2011, then won the 2015 election against the sitting president Goodluck Jonathan — the first opposition candidate to defeat a sitting president in Nigeria — and was re-elected in 2019, handing over to Bola Tinubu on 29 May 2023. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en}

## His religious positions as the sources report them

The English article quotes his 2001 statement of "total commitment to the Sharia movement" and his support for the implementation of sharia in the northern states, alongside his denial of a radical Islamist agenda, and cites his profile in The Muslim 500; the Arabic article attributes to him personally only his birth to a Muslim family. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en} {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-ar} No source used names a school for him, so his faith value is left unclassified (unknown) in his record; Wikidata gives a single value, Islam. {cite:wikidata-muhammadu-buhari}

## Death

Buhari travelled to London for medical treatment in his final weeks and died in a clinic there on 13 July 2025, aged 82; his remains were taken to Daura and buried at his home, and Nigeria declared seven days of national mourning. {cite:wikipedia-muhammadu-buhari-en} {cite:wikidata-muhammadu-buhari}
