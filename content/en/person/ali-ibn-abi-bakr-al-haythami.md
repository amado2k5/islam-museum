## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Ali ibn Abi Bakr ibn Sulayman, Nur al-Din Abu al-Hasan al-Haythami the hafiz: born in Rajab 735, he was the companion of Zayn al-Din al-Iraqi from his youth and married his daughter. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 5/201} He gathered the zawa'id works into Majma' al-Zawa'id. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 5/201} He died on the eve of Tuesday 29 Ramadan 807 in Cairo. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 5/202} Al-Sakhawi judges that he mastered the craft of the zawa'id in particular, while noting the agreement on his piety. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 5/202} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 5/203}
