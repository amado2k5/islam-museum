## Overview
Al-'Irbad ibn Sariya al-Sulami, known as Abu Najih, was a Companion and one of [[concept/ahl-al-suffa|the People of the Bench (ahl al-suffa)]]. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 590}

## Residence and death
He lived in [[place/al-sham|Syria]] and died there in 75 AH; others say he died during the strife of Ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 590}

## Those who narrated from him
Among the Companions, Abu Ruhm and [[person/abu-umama-al-bahili|Abu Umama]] narrated from him, and so did a number of the Successors of Syria. {cite:istiab-alam-2002 p. 590}
