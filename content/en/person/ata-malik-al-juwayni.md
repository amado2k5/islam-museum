## Summary
'Ata Malik al-Juwayni, sahib al-diwan, brother of the great vizier Shams al-Din; he and his brother attained in wealth, retinue and standing what exceeds description in Abaqa's state, and Iraq's affairs rested with 'Ala' al-Din who governed it excellently. He was sought in the year 683, went into hiding and died in hiding; his brother Shams al-Din was killed. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 655}
