## Overview
Jurjis, father of Bakhtishu, who was at the beginning of the state. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an excellent man. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.412}

He wrote the well-known book *al-Kunnash*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.412}
