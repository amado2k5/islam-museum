## Summary
Jurjis Abu Bakhtishu was a physician of the Bukhtishu family who served at the head of the state and was an accomplished man, author of the well-known book al-Kunnash, according to the Fihrist. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 408}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim records in the Fihrist, among the early physicians, that Jurjis Abu Bakhtishu was in the forefront of the state and a man of merit, and that his books include the well-known Kunnash. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 408} He numbers him among the physicians of the Bukhtishu house who excelled in medicine at the court of the Abbasid kings and served them — among them Salmawayh ibn Bunan, also distinguished and senior, who served al-Mu'tasim and enjoyed his special favor, as he relates in the continuation of the Bukhtishu family's story. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 408} Jurjis thus belongs, in his account, to the famous Bukhtishu medical dynasty that practiced at the caliphal court for generations. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 408}
