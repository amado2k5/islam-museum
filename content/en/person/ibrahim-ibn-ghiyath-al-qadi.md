## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ghiyath, whose clientage al-Ma'mun bought, preparing him for the judgeship; the witticisms recorded about salaried judges were aimed at him, in the passage Ibn al-Nadim devotes to condemning judgeship for hire. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 435}
