## Overview
Al-Mundhir al-Ifriqi, a Companion whom Ibn Abd al-Barr mentions among the Companions. He lived in Ifriqiya, and Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Habuli related from him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314-315}

Ibn Habib claimed that he entered al-Andalus, and that he was the one man of the minor Companions who entered it. This is a view attributed to Ibn Habib as al-Maqqari relates it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314-315}
