## Overview
Washmakir ibn Ziyar of the Ziyarids, lord of Tabaristan and Gurgan. Ibn al-Athir reports his affairs in al-Kamil {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.401}.

## War with Rukn al-Dawla
Ibn al-Athir reports that [[person/rukn-al-dawla|Rukn al-Dawla]] ibn Buya marched against him in the year 351 AH, and besieged and took Sari. Washmakir left Tabaristan and made for Gurgan. Rukn al-Dawla remained in Tabaristan until he had taken all of it, then pursued him to Gurgan and seized it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.401}.

Three thousand men from Washmakir's army joined Rukn al-Dawla, who grew stronger, and Washmakir grew weaker. Washmakir entered the land of al-Jil {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.402}.

He may be the same person as [[person/washmakir-ibn-ziyar|Washmakir ibn Ziyar]]. This needs an additional source.
