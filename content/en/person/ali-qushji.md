## Summary
The scholar Ala al-Din Ali ibn Muhammad al-Qushji, among the greatest mathematicians and astronomers of his age {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 344-345}.

## At Mehmed's court
Al-Salabi records that whenever Qushji composed a book in Persian he rendered it into Arabic and presented it to Sultan [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed the Conqueror]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 142}; Ibn al-Imad counts him among the great scholars Mehmed drew from the farthest lands until Istanbul became mother of the world {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 344-345}.
