## Overview
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. 'Ali b. 'Umar b. Muhammad al-Tamimi al-Maziri, Maliki jurist and muhaddith, among the notables pointed to; he commented Sahih Muslim and wrote Idah al-mahsul fi burhan al-usul and several works in adab — excellent and exact; his nisba is to Mazar, a town in the island of Sicily. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
