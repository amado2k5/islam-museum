## Summary
Fakhr al-Nisa' Shahda bint Abi Nasr Ahmad ibn al-Faraj ibn 'Umar al-'Ibri, the copyist — Dinawari by origin, born and deceased at Baghdad — was among the scholars, wrote a fine hand, and a great many people heard hadith on her authority; hers was the lofty isnad. She related from Abu'l-Tahir al-Qasim ibn al-Batr, Abu'l-Ghanim ibn Ahmad ibn Talha al-Nu'ali, Turad ibn Muhammad al-Zayni, Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Ayyub, Abu'l-Husayn Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Qadir ibn Yusuf and Fakhr al-Islam Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Shashi. Her fame spread and her renown reached far. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
