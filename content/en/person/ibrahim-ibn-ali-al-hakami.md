## Overview
"Ibrahim ibn Ali and al-Ghatrif, the two Hakamis" wrote to al-Hadi at Sa'da asking him to come to their country {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}.

## Detail
They said they would hand over what they held of money, troops and the district and gave him their pledges; they sent their trusted men, and oaths were then taken from them by his envoys {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}. The page gives nothing but the nisba "al-Hakami" and does not name their country, and nothing of what follows on later pages is ascribed to him here. The next page speaks of "the Hakami" in the singular, to be read in the next batch. See [[event/the-hakami-letters-and-the-oaths-and-abu-al-qasim-sent-to-khaywan-292|the event]] and [[person/al-ghatrif-al-hakami|al-Ghatrif al-Hakami]].
