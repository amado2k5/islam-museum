## Overview
Abu Attab Mansur ibn al-Mu'tamir, a jurist of the Sunni community. Al-Shirazi mentions him in the same generation as al-A'mash, Abu Hashim, al-Harith al-Ukli and others, who took their learning from al-Sha'bi and al-Nakha'i. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}

## Death
The editor's margin, relaying from *al-Ma'arif*, says that he died in 132 AH; this is not in Shirazi's text. That year needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}
