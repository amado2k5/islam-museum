## Overview
[[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b al-Mazaniyya]], Umm Umara, of the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar of the Ansar {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148}.

## The pledge at al-Aqaba
She was one of the two women who attended the pledge at al-Aqaba. The Muslims at that time were seventy-three men and two women, and she was one of the two {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148}.

## Uhud
After attending Uhud she was among a band of champions around the Prophet, among them Abu Dujana, Mus'ab ibn Umayr and Ali ibn Abi Talib, and "Umm Umara Nusayba bint Ka'b al-Mazaniyya" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.270}.

## For young visitors
Nusayba bint Ka'b was one of the women of the Ansar who pledged to the Prophet at al-Aqaba, and she took part in the defence of the Prophet at Uhud. She was called "Umm Umara" after her son Umara.

## Notes for researchers
- The count of those at al-Aqaba and the number of women is taken from al-Rahiq (p. 148), whose source is Ibn Ishaq; we have not compared it against the original text.
- Her part at Uhud is mentioned in the context of the ranking of champions, and al-Rahiq does not detail exactly what she did; the detail should be added from Ibn Sa'd when it is available.
