## Overview
[[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b al-Mazaniyya]], Umm Umara, of the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar of the Ansar {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148}.

## The pledge at al-Aqaba
She was one of the two women who attended the pledge at al-Aqaba. The Muslims at that time were seventy-three men and two women, and she was one of the two {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148}.

## Uhud
After attending Uhud she was among a band of champions around the Prophet, among them Abu Dujana, Mus'ab ibn Umayr and Ali ibn Abi Talib, and "Umm Umara Nusayba bint Ka'b al-Mazaniyya" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.270}.

## For young visitors
Nusayba bint Ka'b was one of the women of the Ansar who pledged to the Prophet at al-Aqaba, and she took part in the defence of the Prophet at Uhud. She was called "Umm Umara" after her son Umara.

## Notes for researchers
- The count of those at al-Aqaba and the number of women is taken from al-Rahiq (p. 148), whose source is Ibn Ishaq; we have not compared it against the original text.
- Her part at Uhud is mentioned in the context of the ranking of champions, and al-Rahiq does not detail exactly what she did; the detail should be added from Ibn Sa'd when it is available.

## In Mosaad Anwar's series 'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise'
In episode 20 of [[work/the-ten-promised-paradise-mosaad-anwar|'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise']], [[person/mosaad-anwar|Mosaad Anwar]] introduces an Ansari woman for whom, together with her husband and two sons, the Prophet prayed that they be his companions in Paradise. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:01:30} He says she was one of two women who pledged to the Prophet at [[event/second-pledge-of-aqaba|the Second Aqaba]] together with seventy-three men, the same number this record gives from al-Rahiq al-Makhtum. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:02:00} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148} He describes her as the mother of the martyr killed by [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]] and the mother of the man who, with [[person/wahshi-ibn-harb|Wahshi ibn Harb]], killed Musaylima the Liar, and names her Umm Umara Nusayba bint Ka'b al-Maziniyya. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:02:30}

He relates that the Prophet used to present Islam during the pilgrimage seasons; six men of Khazraj embraced it, came back the next year as twelve and asked for a teacher, and he sent [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr]] with them, who stayed a year in Medina calling people with kind words and good counsel and letting them hear the Qur'an in his moving voice, until there was no house that Islam had not entered. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:04:00} He names the two women: Umm Mani' Asma' bint Amr, whom he calls the mother of [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]], and Nusayba bint Ka'b, Umm Umara. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:04:30} He describes how they slipped out at night in Mecca once the polytheists were asleep and met the Prophet, and how the men pledged to him by hand on the oneness of God and on abandoning fornication and killing; when the two women went to shake his hand he said he does not shake hands with women, and took their pledge in words. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:06:30} He draws from this that Muslim women strove for this faith from its first day: the first to believe and the first to pray behind the Prophet was [[person/khadija|Khadija]], the first woman martyr was [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya]], mother of [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]], and the first to dwell at the Sanctuary was [[person/hajar|Hajar]]; in his view the soundness of the community depends on the soundness of its women, who are half of society and bear the other half, and he gives as examples [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]], [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]], [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] and [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]], who grew up fatherless and were formed by their mothers. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:08:30}

He relates that after Badr Quraysh came back seeking revenge; the Prophet consulted his Companions and went out to [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]], placing fifty archers on the hill and ordering them never to leave it whatever happened; victory was the Muslims' early in the day, but most of the archers came down for the spoils despite the order of their commander Ibn Jubayr, and [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], leading the polytheists' cavalry, came round through the gap. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:13:30} In her own words, as he gives them, she went out with a waterskin to give water to the fighters, and when the Muslims broke and no more than about ten remained with the Prophet, she, her son and her husband defended him. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:14:00} At the Prophet's word she took a shield from a man who was fleeing and covered herself with it, then struck the hamstring of an attacking rider's horse so that it fell, and the Prophet called to her son to help his mother, and together they finished the man. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:15:00} A man of the polytheists whom he calls Ibn Qami'a came on intending to kill the Prophet; she and Mus'ab ibn Umayr stood in his way; he killed Mus'ab, who was reciting the verse saying that Muhammad is a messenger before whom messengers had passed away, and struck her a blow on the shoulder that she was treating for a year, while her own blows did not harm him because he was wearing armour. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:16:00} He reports from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sad|Ibn Sa'd]] in al-Tabaqat that she bound the wound of her son, whose upper arm was almost severed, and told him to get up and fight, and the Prophet said: who can bear what you bear, Umm Umara? {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:16:30} He relates the Prophet's saying that on the day of Uhud he did not turn right or left without seeing her fighting in his defence, and that her stand was better than that of so-and-so among the men; and that the Prophet pointed out to her the man who had wounded her son, she struck him on the leg so that he went down and they killed him, and the Prophet told her she had taken her due. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:18:00} As the proof of her glad tidings he gives her request that the Prophet pray for them to be his companions in Paradise, and his prayer for her, her husband and her sons [[person/habib-ibn-zayd|Habib]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-zayd-ibn-asim|Abdallah]]. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:17:30} Her stand around the Prophet at Uhud agrees with what this record gives from al-Rahiq al-Makhtum, that she was in the band that surrounded him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.270}

He says she was with the Prophet at Uhud, [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|al-Hudaybiyya]], [[event/battle-of-khaybar|Khaybar]] and [[event/conquest-of-mecca|the Conquest]], and then at [[event/battle-of-yamama|al-Yamama]] in the time of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]]. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:18:30} He relates that the Prophet sent her son Habib ibn Zayd with another man carrying a letter to Musaylima, the claimant to prophethood; the other man, under threat, affirmed Musaylima and was let go, which in his view was taking the dispensation; Habib, however, affirmed the Prophet's messengership and, asked about Musaylima, said he could not hear, so Musaylima had him cut piece by piece until he stabbed him in the heart and he died a martyr. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:19:30} When the news reached her she said she had raised him for this moment and sought her reward with God, and vowed against Musaylima if God gave her power over him. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:20:30} She went out with Khalid ibn al-Walid's army to al-Yamama in Abu Bakr's time and fought until she heard the cry of "God is greatest" at Musaylima's death; in his account he was killed by Wahshi ibn Harb and her son Abdallah ibn Zayd together, one with the spear and the other with the sword, and he quotes Wahshi's saying that with his spear he had killed the best of people, [[person/hamza|Hamza]], and the worst of people, Musaylima. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:21:30} He says she came out of al-Yamama with more than fifteen wounds and a severed hand, returned to Medina happy, and died after a long life. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep20 00:23:00}

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
Most of the thirteenth episode of [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]'s programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] is about Nusayba bint Ka'b of Banu al-Najjar, Umm Umara. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:09:30} He says she became Muslim at al-Aqaba, one of only two women at the pledge, and went out to Uhud with her husband Ghaziyya and her sons Abd Allah and Habib, sons of her earlier husband Zayd ibn Asim. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:10:00} Women went to tend the wounded and carry water; but when the archers left their post and Khalid ibn al-Walid, then fighting for Quraysh, charged from behind, only a handful stayed by the Prophet, she, her husband and her two sons among them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:11:00}

He relates that she took up the shield a fleeing man threw down, bound her wounded son's wound and sent him back to the fight, and struck the leg of the horse of the man who had wounded him, so that he fell and was killed; the Prophet said that wherever he turned that day he saw her fighting to defend him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:14:00} He says she received thirteen wounds at Uhud, among them a deep sword cut to the shoulder, and that the Prophet asked after her and had her treated. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:16:00} When he led the army out to Hamra' al-Asad she set out too but collapsed from bleeding, and on his return his first question was about her. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:17:00} She was also at al-Hudaybiya, the Pledge of Ridwan and the Farewell Pilgrimage. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:18:00}

Her son Habib, sent as an envoy to Musaylima, refused to testify to Musaylima's prophethood and was cut to pieces; she swore not to wash until Musaylima was killed or she died. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:21:00} He says Abu Bakr allowed her alone among women to go with the army of al-Yamama, with her son Abd Allah, and that after Musaylima was killed her son brought her to see the body. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:23:00} He closes by setting her story against the claim that a woman's only place is the home. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep13 00:23:30}
