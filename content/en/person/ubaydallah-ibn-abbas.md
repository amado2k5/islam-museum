## Overview
Ubaydallah ibn al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, a cousin of Ali ibn Abi Talib. Al-Sallabi lists him among Ali's governors, as governor of Yemen and Bahrain {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.358}.

## Under Ali
In defending Ali's appointment of kinsmen to provinces, al-Sallabi names the sons of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib whom Ali appointed: Abdallah, Ubaydallah, Qutham and Tammam {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.357}. In the book's list of Ali's governors, Ubaydallah is ninth, with Yemen and Bahrain as his provinces {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.358}. The book gives neither the dates of his office nor further reports on these pages.
