## Overview
The imam-scholar al-Khujandi the Hanafi; he transmitted a juz' from 'Izz al-Din Ibn Jama'a, kept the people of Medina busy with him forty years — they profited by him for his faith and learning — and died past eighty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 24}
