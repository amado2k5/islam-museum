## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim ibn Juzayy al-Kalbi, an Andalusian secretary of Granadan origin. He wrote for the sultan Abu Inan of Morocco after he had written for the sultan Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}

## Ibn Battuta's journey
Al-Maqqari says that he was the one who wrote and arranged the journey of Ibn Battuta. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}

## An example of his elegance
Al-Maqqari relates that the scribes and the notables disputed how to describe the down on a youth's cheek. Each side said it resembled only what suited its own craft. Ibn Juzayy combined both views in a single letter, the lam. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}

## Redactor of Ibn Battuta's Rihla
In the preface of the *Tuhfat al-nuzzar*, Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Juzayy al-Kalbi presents himself as a servant devoted to the Marinid sultan [[person/abu-inan-faris|Abu Inan]], ordered to gather what [[person/ibn-battuta-muhammad|Ibn Battuta]] dictated into a book, refining, polishing and clarifying the language. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.14} He says he conveyed the meaning of the shaykh's words in adequate wording, sometimes keeping his exact words, included every story and report he told without investigating or testing them, vocalized difficult names of places and people, and explained foreign names where he could. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.14} {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.15}

Here and there in the Rihla he adds passages beginning "Ibn Juzayy says", among them that Ibn Battuta told him his birth date in Granada, and the story, heard from one of his travelling teachers, of the archer who climbed the Pillar of the Columns at Alexandria. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.15} {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.21} He ends the book by saying that the foregoing is his summary of the shaykh's account and that Ibn Battuta is "the traveller of the age", followed by a long prayer for the sultan. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.566} {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-khayriyya-1904 p.567} See [[work/rihla-ibn-battuta|the Rihla]].
