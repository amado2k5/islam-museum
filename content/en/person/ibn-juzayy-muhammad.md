## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim ibn Juzayy al-Kalbi, an Andalusian secretary of Granadan origin. He wrote for the sultan Abu Inan of Morocco after he had written for the sultan Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}

## Ibn Battuta's journey
Al-Maqqari says that he was the one who wrote and arranged the journey of Ibn Battuta. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}

## An example of his elegance
Al-Maqqari relates that the scribes and the notables disputed how to describe the down on a youth's cheek. Each side said it resembled only what suited its own craft. Ibn Juzayy combined both views in a single letter, the lam. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.173}
