## Overview
Al-Fadhusfan, the marzban (military governor) of Isfahan, an old man. Ibn Badil was his contemporary, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
Al-Baladhuri relates that he was secretary to the people of the city, who let him down. He went out with thirty archers fleeing towards Kerman to join Yazdgerd. Ibn Badil followed him and challenged him to single combat, and his blow fell on the pommel of his saddle and broke it. He then offered him peace on behalf of his people, and he accepted and surrendered the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
