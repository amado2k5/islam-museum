## Overview
Aban ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn Lahiq ibn Ufayr al-Raqqashi, a prolific poet, most of whose poetry is in the mazdawija form. He put the book «Kalila wa-Dimna» into verse. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}
