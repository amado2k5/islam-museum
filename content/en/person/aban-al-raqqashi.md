## Overview
Aban ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn Lahiq ibn Ufayr al-Raqqashi, a prolific poet much of whose verse was in the rajaz-like muzdawij form; he versified the book Kalila wa-Dimna into poetry. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 423}
