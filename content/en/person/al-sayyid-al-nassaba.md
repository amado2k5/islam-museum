## Overview
Al-Sayyid the genealogist Husayn al-Dimashqi al-Hasani al-Shafi'i, shaykh of Damascus's shaykhs; born at Salihiyya in 774 and heard hadith in his father's early age — a genealogist who claimed he had compiled five books on nasab; he died in Sha'ban 866, past a hundred. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}
