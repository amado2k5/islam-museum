## Overview
Muhammad ibn Kathir al-Farghani, author of "al-Fusul al-Thalathin". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.519}

Al-Mas'udi counts him among those who engaged in the sciences of the heavens. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.519}

His death and the content of his book need an additional source.
