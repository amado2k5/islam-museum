## Overview
Janibeg Khan ibn Uzbek, king of the Dasht in the north after his father [[person/uzbek-khan|Uzbek]], and one of the rulers of the [[polity/golden-horde|Golden Horde]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun says that he drove his armies back to Khurasan until he ruled it in 738 AH. He then advanced to Azerbaijan and Tabriz, which the Young Shaykh, son of Dimirdash ibn Jupan, had seized, so he marched with the armies to recover them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

His birth and death dates are not recorded here and need an additional source.
