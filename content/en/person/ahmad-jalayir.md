## Summary
Ghiyath al-Din Ahmad ibn Uways was a Jalayirid sultan of Baghdad and Tabriz, who contested the lands with Timur and Qara Yusuf, and died in 1410 {cite:bosworth-new-islamic-dynasties-1996}. He belongs to [[polity/jalayirids|the Jalayirids]]. The dynastic handbook entry the record names has not yet been read for this page, so no reign dates, battles or relations are added here.
