## Overview
Uthman ibn Suwayd Abu Thari al-Akhmimi, from Akhmim, a village in Egypt. He was a leading master and head of the craft of alchemy. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 505}

## His relation with Ibn Wahshiyya
He had debates and letters with Ibn Wahshiyya. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 505}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim counts among his books: "The Red Sulphur", "Clarification", "Corrections", "Turning Away Suspicion from Dhu al-Nun al-Misri", "Glosses", "Instruments of the Ancients", "Solution and Binding", "Management", "Sublimation and Distillation", "The Great Hell", and "Debates and Discussions of the Scholars". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 505}
