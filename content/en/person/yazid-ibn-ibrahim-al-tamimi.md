## Summary
Yazid ibn Ibrahim al-Tamimi al-Basri, a client of the Banu Tamim, whom the major hadith collectors related from. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15828} He transmitted from Ibn Sirin, al-Hasan, Ata ibn Abi Rabah and others; Ibn al-Mubarak, Waki, Ibn Mahdi, Yazid ibn Harun and many others transmitted from him.
