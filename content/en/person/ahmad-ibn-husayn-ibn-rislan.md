## Overview
Ahmad ibn Husayn ibn Rislan, a Shafi'i jurist of Ramla who died in Jerusalem (d. 844 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3479}

## Works
His works include Safwat al-zubad, a versified fiqh, commentaries on Abu Dawud and al-Bukhari, and a commentary on Minhaj al-wusul in usul. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3479}
