## Summary
Born in the land of Abyssinia to his father al-Muttalib ibn Azhar and his mother Ramla bint Abi Awf. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him a son born to al-Muttalib ibn Azhar in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He counts him among "their sons in Abyssinia" of Banu Zuhra, within the five men born there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq said: it used to be said he was the first man to inherit his father in Islam (p. 7); an uncertain remark.
