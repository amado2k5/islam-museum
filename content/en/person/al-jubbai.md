## Overview
Abu 'Ali Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Wahhab b. Sallam b. Khalid al-Jubba'i, one of the imams of the Mu'tazila; an imam in kalam and chief of the mutakallimun of his era — he took this science from Abu Yusuf Ya'qub b. 'Abdallah al-Shahham of Basra, chief of Basra's Mu'tazila — and from him Shaykh Abu'l-Hasan al-Ash'ari, shaykh of the Sunna, took kalam. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
