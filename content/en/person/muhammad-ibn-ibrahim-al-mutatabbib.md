## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Mutatabbib, private physician to the Ottoman sultan Ahmed I, who ascended the throne in 1603 CE. He died in 1029 AH (1620 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
