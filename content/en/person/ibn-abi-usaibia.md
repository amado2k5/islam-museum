## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Qasim ibn Khalifa ibn Yunus al-Khazraji, Muwaffaq al-Din, Abu al-Abbas, known as Ibn Abi Usaybi'a, was a physician and historian, author of "'Uyun al-Anba' fi Tabaqat al-Atibba'", printed in two volumes. He was born in Damascus in 596 AH, 1200 CE, and died in 668 AH, 1270 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}.

## His residence and his books
He was based in Damascus, where he composed his book in 643 AH. He visited Egypt in 634 AH and stayed there about a year as a physician {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}. His other books include "al-Tajarib wa-al-Fawa'id", "Hikayat al-Atibba' fi 'Ilajat al-Ad'wa'" and "Ma'alim al-Umam", and he wrote a great deal of verse. He died at Sarkhad, in the Hawran country of Syria {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}.
