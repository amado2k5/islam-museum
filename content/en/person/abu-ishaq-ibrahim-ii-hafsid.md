## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abi Bakr al-Mutawakkil ala Allah ibn Yahya al-Hafsi, Abu Ishaq, was one of the Hafsid kings in Tunis. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 770 AH, 1369 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 34}.

## Career
He took power in 751 AH as a boy, with disorder prevailing, and Abu Muhammad ibn Tafrajin, who had been his father's chamberlain, managed his affairs {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 34}. His reign was long and surrounded by strife: the people of Tunis would drive him out and others would bring him back, until he died suddenly {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 34}.
