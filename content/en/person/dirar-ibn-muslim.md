## Overview
Dirar ibn Muslim al-Bahili, brother of Qutayba. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The day Qutayba was killed
He survived the day Qutayba was killed, and the Banu Tamim granted him safe conduct. He was the only one of his brothers to do so. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
