## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Yala al-Laythi al-Basri, qadi of Basra. Ibn Hajar calls him trustworthy, of the fourth tier. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3135}

## Sayings attributed to him
The encyclopedia reports that Iyas ibn Muawiya referred Habib ibn al-Shahid to him for fatwas, and Yazid ibn Harun's report of his view on three Friday prayers missed without excuse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3135}

## Dates
The book gives no year of death, only that he died after the first century AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3135}
