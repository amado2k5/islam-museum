## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Rumi al-Babarti, titled Akmal al-Din, a Hanafi jurist and theologian who lived in Cairo in the age of the Mamluk sultans. The encyclopaedia says he died in 786 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.8-10}

His birth year is not recorded and needs an additional source.
