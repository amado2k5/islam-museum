## Overview
Antara ibn Shaddad al-Absi, the knight and poet of the Banu Abs, author of one of the Mu'allaqat. "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" quotes verses from his ode in praise of the pre-Islamic people's drinking of wine as one of the paths of generosity. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46}

## In the book
The author says that among the results of the generosity of the people of the Jahiliyya was that they praised drinking wine, not as a glory in itself but as a path of generosity and something that makes extravagance easy for the soul. This is a theme of praise and boasting in the Jahili diwans. He gives Antara's verses in his ode, in which he says that when he drinks he squanders his wealth and his honour is abundant, and when he is sober he does not hold back from generosity. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.46} See [[concept/jahili-virtues|the virtues of the people of the Jahiliyya]].

## For young visitors
Antara was a famous knight and poet from the Banu Abs. He composed much verse on courage and generosity.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh relates that Antara, asked the secret of his courage, said it was patience for an hour, and challenged a huge man to put each his finger in the other's mouth and bite; the man cried out first, and Antara said: had you held out a moment longer, I would have cried out (needs_source: the story as the Sheikh tells it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep09 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep09 00:04:30}
