## Overview
Badr al-Din Lulu, lord of Mosul. News of the Tatars reached Mosul after they took Maragha in 618 AH, and its people feared them until some of them meant to leave. Badr al-Din sent a good company of his soldiers as reinforcements to Muzaffar al-Din, lord of Irbil, and resolved to guard the rugged passes on the Tatars' side. Letters and envoys from the caliph reached Mosul ordering them to prepare. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.412}
