## Summary
The hafiz Burhan al-Din Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Khalil al-Hilli, the traditionist, died at Aleppo in 840 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}.

## Details
Known as al-Qawf, grandson of Ibn al-'Ajami, he was born at Hilla on 12 Rajab 753 and studied there: hadith under Kamal al-Din 'Umar ibn al-'Ajami, Sharaf al-Din ibn Habib and al-Zubayr ibn al-'Ajami among others; grammar under the two Andalusian shaykhs Abu Ja'far and Abu 'Abdallah; and he worked in fiqh, the readings, morphology, rhetoric and Sufism {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}. He then travelled, hearing hadith at Hama, Damascus and Cairo from the hafiz Ibn al-Muhibb, Salah al-Din ibn Abi 'Umar, the hafiz Zayn al-Din al-'Iraqi and the hafiz Siraj al-Din ibn al-Mulaqqan, and at Alexandria, Jerusalem and Gaza {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}. Many studied under him, including Ibn Hajar and Ibn Nasir al-Din, the hafiz of Damascus; his works include Nihayat al-Sawl on the transmission of the Six Books, a commentary on Sunan Ibn Majah, and a supplement to al-Dhahabi's Mizan. He died at Aleppo on Monday, 26 Shawwal {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}.
