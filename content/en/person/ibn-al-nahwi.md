## Overview
Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Ansari, known as Ibn al-Nahwi, an Andalusian poet among the jurists of letters. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p. 615-616}

[[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]] cites him as an example of how a linguistic faculty is revealed by the material a person has memorised. He recited to Abu al-Abbas ibn Shu'ayb the opening of a poem, and Ibn Shu'ayb said on the spot that it was the verse of a jurist, because the phrase "ma al-farq" (what is the difference) is a term of the jurists rather than a usage of the Arabs. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p. 615-616}
