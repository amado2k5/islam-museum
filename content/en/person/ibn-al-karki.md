## Summary

Zayn al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Karki, a Hanafi from Egypt and the transmitter of hadith for the land of Egypt. He heard hadith from Shaykh al-Islam Jalal al-Din al-Balqini, and our shaykh Ibn Hajar gave him a licence. He collected many certificates of hearing and sat with the leading figures of his age {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 473}.

He was drowned in the Pond of the Elephant on Tuesday, the fifth of Shaban in the year 922. Sultan Tuman Bay II walked in his funeral procession on foot {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 473}.
