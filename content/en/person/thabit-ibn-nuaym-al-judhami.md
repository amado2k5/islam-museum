## Overview
Thabit ibn Nu'aym al-Judhami rebelled against [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]] when news reached him of the killing of al-Walid ibn Yazid {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}.

Musafir al-Qassab was among those who agreed with his view. al-Dahhak the Kharijite appointed him over Armenia and Azerbaijan. His name is linked with [[event/musafir-revolt-armenia|the revolt of Musafir in Armenia]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}.
