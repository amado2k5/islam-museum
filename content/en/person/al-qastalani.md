## Overview
Shaykh Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad b. 'Ali al-Qastalani; he accompanied Shaykh Abu 'Abd Allah al-Qurashi, profited by him and transmitted from him, and gathered all his utterances and whatever issued from him into a large fascicle which Ibn Khallikan consulted and quoted {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
