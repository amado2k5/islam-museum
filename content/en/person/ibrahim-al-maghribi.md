## Summary
Ibrahim al-Maghribi. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 538, sequence 539, on page 164. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}

## The calamities of the world
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn relates from Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Razi that he heard Ibrahim ibn al-Walid say: I went in to Ibrahim al-Maghribi after a mule had kicked him and broken his leg, and he said: were it not for the calamities of the world, we would have come before God as paupers. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}
