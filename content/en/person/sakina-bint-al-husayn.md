## Overview
Sakina bint al-Husayn al-Shahid; she narrated from her father and was of striking beauty. Her cousin 'Abdallah ibn al-Hasan the Elder married her and was killed with her father. It was said: I came to her and found at her door Jarir, al-Farazdaq, Jamil and Kuthayyir. She died in Rabi' I, 117 AH, and narrated little. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1860}

## In Kahhala's A'lam al-Nisa'
Kahhala says "Sukayna" was a pet name given by her mother al-Rabab bint Imru' al-Qays, and that her name is disputed — Amina, Amina or Umayma. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.206} He describes her as a noble lady of high standing who sat with the leading men of Quraysh, and to whom poets and men of letters came for judgement, which she gave by sorting the lean from the fat; once the transmitters of Jarir, Kuthayyir, Nusayb, Jamil and al-Ahwas met in Medina and agreed to her arbitration, and she criticised the verses of each poet in turn. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.206} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.207} He gives reports from *al-Aghani* on her love of song and of the singers Ibn Surayj and al-Gharid. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.214} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.215} He lists her husbands: her cousin Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali, Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr, Abd Allah ibn Uthman al-Hizami, Zayd ibn Amr ibn Uthman, al-Asbagh ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan (the marriage not consummated), and others. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.220} He relates that leaving Iraq for Medina after Mus'ab's death she told its people that they had killed her father, her grandfather, her uncle and her husband, orphaning her when young and widowing her when grown. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.221} He says she came to Damascus with her family after her father's killing, then went to Medina, and died there on 5 Rabi' I 117 AH, when Khalid ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Harith governed Medina under Hisham; others say she died in Mecca. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.227} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p.228}
