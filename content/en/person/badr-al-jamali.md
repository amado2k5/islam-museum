## Summary
Badr al-Jamali, amir al-juyush; an Armenian by birth bought by Jamal al-Dawla Ibn 'Ammar, raised as his slave and advanced by his master; al-Mustansir of Egypt appointed him his deputy at Tyre — some say Acre. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His coming and monuments
When al-Mustansir's state weakened he summoned him; he braved the winter sea and reached Cairo on the eve of Wednesday, two nights left of Jumada II 466, was given the direction of affairs — and the state stood upright at his coming. He was vizier of sword and pen, over the chief judgeship and the da'is; he built the mosque at the Alexandrian port in the perfumers' market and the Mashhad al-Ra's at Ascalon. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
