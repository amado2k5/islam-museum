## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Kabir ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Fayd and Abu Abd Allah, al-Kettani, was a jurist, philosophically inclined Sufi, of the people of Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. He founded the Kattaniyya order in Morocco and was the brother of Muhammad Abd al-Hayy, author of «Fihris al-Fahaaris» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## What befell him
Scholars of Fez criticised some of his statements and attributed to him a bad creed, complained of him to Sultan Abd al-Aziz in Marrakesh and added the charge that he sought kingship; he went to Marrakesh, showed his innocence of what was ascribed to him, stayed there for a time and was then allowed to return to Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. When the people of Fez wished to pledge allegiance to Sultan Abd al-Hafiz, al-Kettani dictated its conditions, which included binding the sultan to consultation; the sultan resented it, and his circumstances worsened {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. He left Fez in 1327 AH heading for the Berber lands with his whole family; the sultan sent horsemen after him and he was brought back under a guarantee of safety, then arrested and jailed in irons with those who were with him, women and children included {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## Works
His books include «al-Lamahat al-Qudsiyya fi Muta'alliqat al-Ruh bil-Kulliyya», «al-Mawaqif al-Ilahiyya fi al-Tasawwurat al-Muhammadiyya», «Hayat al-Anbiya», the printed collection «Qasa'id al-Kattani», the printed «al-Kamal al-Mutalali wal-Istidlalat al-Awali», and the printed «Lisan al-Hujja al-Burhaniyya fi al-Dhabb an Sha'a'ir al-Tariqa al-Ahmadiyya al-Kattaniyya» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Sarghini wrote a book on his life, «Rawd al-Jinan bima li-Shaykhina Abi Abd Allah al-Kattani min al-Khusustiyya wal-Irfan» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## Death
He was flogged and dragged to Bini'qa in the Mashwar of Abu al-Khasisat in Fes al-Jadid, and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. Al-A'lam gives his birth as 1290 AH, equal to 1873 CE, and his death as 1327 AH, printing 1907 CE beside it; the Hijri year 1327 falls in 1909 and 1910 CE, so the card's 1909 is kept {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
