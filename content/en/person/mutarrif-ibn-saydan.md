## Overview
Mutarrif ibn Saydan al-Bahili of Banu Jana'a, whom Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr appointed as police chief of Basra. He was killed in revenge for a highway robber whom he had killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His killing
Mutarrif killed al-Nabi ibn Ziyad ibn Zabyan, a highway robber. His brother Ubayd Allah sought revenge for him, stabbed Mutarrif and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Ubayd Allah joined Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and fought with him against Mus'ab, killed him and cut off his head. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
