## Summary
[[person/umm-kulthum-bint-muhammad|Umm Kulthum]] bint [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]; [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] married her after her sister [[person/ruqayya-bint-muhammad|Ruqayya]]'s death in his keeping {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2031}.

## Her marriage
Abu Umar mentions that Utba ibn Abi Lahab married her before the mission and did not consummate it; when the Prophet was sent, his father ordered him to leave her, and Uthman then married her after Ruqayya's death in year 3 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2031}. Ibn Sa'd relates that it was in Rabi' al-Awwal of year 3 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}. Ibn Manda relates, in an unusual chain, a hadith that the Prophet married Uthman to Umm Kulthum only by revelation from heaven — he says: "unusual, known only by this chain" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}.

## What is disputed
- Whether she or Fatima was the Prophet's youngest daughter {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2031}.
- The timing of the divorces by Abu Lahab's two sons: Ibn Hajar transmits a saying that Utba and Utayba divorced Ruqayya and Umm Kulthum before consummation when Surat al-Masad came down — preferring it over what Abu Umar said, following Ibn Sa'd, that the marriage was before the mission, since Zaynab, the eldest daughter, was born ten years before the mission {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}.

## Her death
She died with Uthman in Sha'ban of year 9 AH, having borne him no children {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}. Ibn Hajar mentions from Anas that he saw the Prophet at her grave with eyes brimming; he asked: "is there among you one who did not approach his wife tonight?" Abu Talha said: I — he said: "go down into her grave" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}. Al-Waqidi said: Ali, al-Fadl and Usama ibn Zayd went down into her grave {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}.

Ibn Hajar also brings that Asma' bint Umays said she washed Umm Kulthum and Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib; in another account Umm Atiyya was among those who washed her {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2032}.
