## Summary
'Umar al-Fayyumi, the jurist, studied fiqh at Cairo under al-Siraj al-Bulqini, travelled to Aleppo and held the army judgeship there, then was dismissed. He was brilliant in the inheritance-shares, a partner in the other sciences, with prose and verse, and commented on the *Burda*. He was killed in late Muharram at the Khan Ghabaghib outside Damascus while bound for Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 17}
