## Ibrahim ibn Mubarakshah al-Isʿirdi

Ibrahim ibn Mubarakshah al-Isʿirdi, known as al-Khwaja, a well-known merchant and the owner of a college at the White Bridge. He had great wealth, was very generous and gave away much. He died in 826 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 181}.
