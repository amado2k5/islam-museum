# Ziauddin Yousafzai

Ziauddin Yousafzai (born 20 April 1969) is a Pakistani education activist, born in Shangla in the North-West Frontier Province (now Khyber Pakhtunkhwa) into a Pashtun family; his father, Rohul Amin, was an orator, a teacher of theology and imam of the local mosque. He studied at Jahanzeb College in Swat, taking a master's degree in English, and co-founded a chain of low-cost private schools (the Khushal Public Schools) open to girls in the Swat Valley. {cite:wikipedia-ziauddin-yousafzai-en} {cite:wikipedia-ziauddin-yousafzai-ar} {cite:wikidata-ziauddin-yousafzai}

## Malala's father

Yousafzai became known worldwide as the father of Malala Yousafzai, the 2014 Nobel Peace Prize laureate: he encouraged his daughter from childhood to speak out for girls' right to education against the Pakistani Taliban's ban, and stood beside her after the 2012 attempt on her life. He is a co-founder and board member of the Malala Fund, the author of the memoir Let Her Fly (2018), and the recipient of honorary doctorates from Canadian universities for his work for girls' education. {cite:wikipedia-ziauddin-yousafzai-en} {cite:wikipedia-ziauddin-yousafzai-ur}
