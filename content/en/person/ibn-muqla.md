## Overview
An Abbasid vizier, who held the vizierate in 316 AH and was removed from it in 318 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.117}

## Vizierate and removal
After his removal he was imprisoned in the caliph's palace, and Sulayman ibn al-Hasan ibn Makhlad was appointed in his place. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.130} Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muqtadir appointed him vizier after Ali ibn Isa, and that his name was Abu Ali Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muqla. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.661}

## With al-Qahir and al-Radi
Al-Suyuti relates that the vizierate was delegated to him when al-Qahir was given the title in 317 AH, and that he agreed with Mu'nis and others to depose al-Qahir in 321 AH. He went into hiding and his house was burned. Al-Radi ordered him to write a book on the faults of al-Qahir, to be read out to the people. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.591}

Ibn al-Athir says that he plotted the deposition of al-Qahir in 322 AH, meeting with the commanders at night in secret. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.209} He relates that al-Qahir removed him after Mu'nis was arrested, and sealed his house, which was then plundered. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.191} During his vizierate for al-Muqtadir he arrested al-Shalmaghani and imprisoned him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.218}

Ibn al-Jawzi says that he feared al-Qahir and went into hiding, and incited the troops against him; they deposed him and blinded his eyes. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.660} The sources differ on the years (321, 322 and 333 AH), and the order of events needs review.

## Calligraphy
Ibn al-Nadim mentions him in the section on the calligraphy of Qur'an copies and of scribes. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.20-24}

## His son
Ibn al-Athir says that his son al-Husayn ibn Muqla served as vizier to al-Muttaqi. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.312-313}
