## Summary
Fatima Al Jaber is an Emirati architectural engineer and corporate executive who rose to run the operations of the Al Jaber Group — one of the UAE's largest construction and logistics conglomerates — becoming the most senior woman in Gulf construction. {cite:wikipedia-fatima-al-jaber}

## Engineer to executive
A graduate in architectural engineering from UAE University, Al Jaber joined the family group founded by her father, Obaid Al Jaber, and rose through technical and managerial posts — in an industry, construction, where the site workforce in the Gulf is overwhelmingly male — to become chief operating officer of the group, overseeing tens of thousands of employees and projects across the Emirates and the region. {cite:wikipedia-fatima-al-jaber}

## Public roles and recognition
She has served on official and semi-official economic bodies in Abu Dhabi, including the chamber and economic-development councils, and has been ranked repeatedly among the most powerful Arab women by Forbes Middle East and similar publications — cited particularly as proof that women could lead not only banks and ministries but heavy industry. {cite:muslim500-fatima-al-jaber}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as the UAE's pioneering woman industrialist: an engineer who ran one of the region's biggest construction workforces and, in doing so, reset expectations for Emirati women in the private sector. {cite:muslim500-fatima-al-jaber}
