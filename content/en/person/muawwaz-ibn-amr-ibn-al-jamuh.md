## Overview
Mu'awwaz ibn 'Amr ibn al-Jamuh al-Ansari al-Sulami; per Ibn Sa'd he witnessed Badr with his brothers Mu'adh and Khallad — though Ibn Ishaq does not name him, so God knows best — and he witnessed the 'Aqaba with the seventy per Ibn Ishaq alone. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3915}
