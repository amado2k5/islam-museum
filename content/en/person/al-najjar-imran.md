## Overview
Al-Najjar "Sahib al-Musalla" (d. 358, before Jawhar's conquest of Egypt): 'Imran b. Musa al-Najjar, client of Ghafiq — though it is also said the Najjar in question is Abu'l-Tayyib Muhammad b. Ja'far al-Baghdadi al-Najjar, known as Ghundur; Ibn Khallikan records both identifications {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
