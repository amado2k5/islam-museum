## Summary
A jurist, grammarian, lexicographer, traditionist and man of letters of Fez; he studied with Abu al-Abbas al-Hilali, Abu al-Abbas al-Dala'i and Abd al-Rahman al-Manjara; Sultan Abu al-Rabi' Sulayman studied with him; he is praised for precision. Works include a commentary on the second ten of al-Nawawi's Forty Hadiths. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7841}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7842}
