## Summary
Abu Sa'id al-Baraqa'i was one of the great knowers of God in Syria. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 510, sequence 511, on page 146. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146}

## The sweetness in worship
Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari relates from Abu Sa'id al-Baraqa'i, from Ubayd Allah ibn Zahr al-Haddad, from Salih al-Muri, from Hawshab, from al-Hasan: look for the sweetness in prayer, in the Qur'an and in remembrance; if you find it, go on and rejoice, and if you do not find it, know that the door is closed. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/146}
