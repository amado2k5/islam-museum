## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Tahanawi al-Faruqi, from India, a Hanafi and a lexicographer who took part in some sciences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2056}

## Works
His works include 'Kashshaf istilahat al-funun' and 'Sabq al-ghayat'; he was alive in 1158 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2056}
