## Overview
Imad al-Din Isma'il ibn Ahmad ibn Sa'id al-Shaybani al-Jazari, a historian and poet from the scholarly house of Ibn al-Athir. The encyclopaedia says he was born around 1245 CE and died in 699 AH (1299 CE), and that he is the author of *al-Bahir fi Tarikh Dawlat al-Atabakah bi al-Mawsil* (The Brilliant on the History of the Atabeg State in Mosul), combining historical narrative with verse. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He lives in [[place/mosul|Mosul]]. The encyclopaedia records him within the Sunni frame of the museum. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
