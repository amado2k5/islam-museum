## Summary
Ashir ibn Muhammad, Abu Muhammad al-Ansari al-Andalusi al-Fasi, a Maliki scholar with learning in the readings of the Qur'an, grammar, exegesis, legal theory and theology. His works include al-Murshid al-mu'in, a versified Maliki law, a commentary on Khalil's abridgement, and al-Kafi on the readings. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7146}
