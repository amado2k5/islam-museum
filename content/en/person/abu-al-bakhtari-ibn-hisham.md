## Overview
Abu al-Bakhtari ibn Hisham al-Qurashi, of the Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza. He rose with Zuhayr to annul the document of the boycott, and he enabled Hakim ibn Hizam to carry wheat to Khadija. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.111}

## What is not recorded here
The "Sealed Nectar" does not give his full lineage or death at this point; these are completed from its other sources when he is mentioned again.

## For young visitors
Abu al-Bakhtari ibn Hisham was among the men of Quraysh who denounced the injustice done to the Banu Hashim in the ravine.
