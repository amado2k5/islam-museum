## Summary
Abdullah ibn Urayqit al-Laythi, the hired guide of the hijra {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 166}.

## His role
The Prophet and Abu Bakr hired him as their road-guide — a "guide well-versed" in the paths — and though he was on Quraysh's disbelieving religion they trusted him with it, handed him their two mounts and appointed him the [[place/ghar-thawr|cave of Thawr]] after three nights {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 166}.

He came to them on Monday night, the first of Rabi' al-Awwal, with the mounts, and took them by the coastal route — deep south toward Yemen, then west to the coast, then north along the Red Sea shore, on a road scarcely anyone used {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 166-167}.
