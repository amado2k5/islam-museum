## Summary
[[person/al-miqdam-ibn-madikarib|Al-Miqdam ibn Ma'dikarib]] ibn Amr ibn Yazid, kunya Abu Karima — or Abu Yahya, it is said {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1458}. He "kept the Prophet's company" and related hadiths from him, and related from Khalid ibn al-Walid, Mu'adh and Abu Ayyub; he settled in Homs {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1458}.

## His transmitters
From him relate his son Yahya, his grandson Salih ibn Yahya, Khalid ibn Ma'dan, Habib ibn Ubayd, Yahya ibn Jabir al-Ta'i, al-Sha'bi, Shurayh ibn Ubayd, Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Awf and others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1458}.

## Did he see the Prophet?
Al-Baghawi records through Abu Yahya Sulaym al-Kila'i: we said to al-Miqdam, Abu Karima, the people claim you never saw the Prophet — he said: "yes by God, I saw him, and he took hold of my earlobe while I was walking with an uncle of mine" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1459}.

## His death
Ibn Sa'd lists him in the fourth class of the people of Syria, saying he died in year eighty-seven at ninety-one years of age, and mentions other views on the year of his death {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1458}.
