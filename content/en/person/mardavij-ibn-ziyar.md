## Overview
Mardavij ibn Ziyar al-Jili, founder of the Ziyarid state in Tabaristan and Jurjan according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.130}

## His rise
Isfar ibn Shiruya summoned him from Gilan, made him commander of the army, and treated him well. They marched on Tabaristan and seized it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.130}

He was one of the nobles of the Daylam. Some of them claimed to be descendants of Shiruya ibn Qawwad, brother of Khosrow Parviz. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.130}
