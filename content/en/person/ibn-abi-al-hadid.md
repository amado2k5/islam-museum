## Summary
Ibn Abi'l-Hadid, the jurist-adib 'Izz al-Din Abu Hamid 'Abd al-Hamid b. Hibat Allah b. Muhammad b. Husayn al-Mada'ini; he was commissioned to answer Diya' al-Din Ibn al-Athir, author of *al-Mathal al-sa'ir*, and gathered his objections into a book he named *al-Fulk al-da'ir 'ala'l-mathal al-sa'ir*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His standing
A jurist, adib and excellent man with graceful poetry — the author of the celebrated commentary on *Nahj al-balagha*; he died at Baghdad. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
