## Summary
Atabeg Shihab al-Din Abu Sa'id Tughril, atabeg of al-Malik al-'Aziz Muhammad ibn al-Zahir ibn Salah al-Din at Aleppo and manager of the state jointly with Qadi Abu'l-Mahasin, was industrious, upright, of good conduct and praiseworthy way. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
