## Overview
[[person/jahwar-ibn-marrar-al-ijli|Jahwar ibn Marrar al-Ijli]], an Abbasid commander whom Abu Ja'far al-Mansur sent against Sunbadh the Magian. He clashed with him between Hamadhan and Rayy and ended his movement in 137 AH. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.55}

## The battle
Jahwar advanced with his army toward Sunbadh, met him between Hamadhan and Rayy, and the encounter ended with the defeat of the movement. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.55}
