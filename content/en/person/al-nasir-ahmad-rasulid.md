## Overview
Al-Malik al-Nasir Ahmad ibn al-Ashraf Isma'il ibn al-Afdal Abbas, lord of Yemen of the Banu Rasul. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 190}

## His rule
He established himself in the kingdom after his father in 803 AH, and events occurred in his time. Ibn al-Imad describes him as dissolute and oppressive. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 190}

## His death
Ibn al-Imad relates from Ibn Hajar that he died because a thunderbolt fell on his fortress of glass; he was startled by its sound, fell ill and died. This happened on the sixteenth of Jumada al-Akhira 827 AH. Ibn al-Imad comments with a verse about thunderbolts: [[passage/quran-13-13|al-Ra'd 13]]. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 190}
