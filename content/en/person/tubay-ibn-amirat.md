## Overview
Tubay' is the son of a woman of [[person/kab-al-ahbar|Ka'b al-Ahbar]]. al-Baladhuri reports that he was among those who took part in [[event/conquest-of-arwad|the conquest of Arwad]], and that Mujahid ibn Jabr taught him the Qur'an there. He adds that it is said this was at Rhodes {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330}.
