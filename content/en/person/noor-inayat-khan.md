# Noor Inayat Khan

## Summary

Noor-un-Nisa Inayat Khan (1 January 1914 – 13 September 1944), also known as Nora Baker and by her SOE codename Madeleine, was a British-Indian agent of the Special Operations Executive in the Second World War, the first female wireless operator sent from Britain into occupied France.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en} The Arabic article introduces her as an Indian Muslim princess who resisted the Nazis.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-ar}

## Early life

She was born in Moscow, the eldest of four children of Inayat Khan, the musician and teacher of Sufism, and his American wife Ameena Begum.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en} The family left Russia for Britain in 1914 and moved in 1920 to Suresnes near Paris; after her father's death in 1927, the thirteen-year-old Noor helped care for her younger siblings, studied child psychology at the Sorbonne and music at the Paris Conservatory.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en}

## Writer

As a young woman she published poetry and children's stories in English and French and contributed to children's magazines and French radio; her book Twenty Jataka Tales, retelling Buddhist Jataka stories, was published in London in 1939.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en}

## War service and death

The family fled France in June 1940 ahead of the German conquest, reaching Falmouth by sea.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en} She joined the Women's Auxiliary Air Force in November 1940 and trained as a wireless operator, was recruited by the SOE's F Section, and was sent into occupied France, where she was betrayed and captured.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en} She was executed at Dachau concentration camp on 13 September 1944.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en}{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-ar}

## Honours

She was posthumously awarded the George Cross and the French Croix de Guerre.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-en} A memorial to her stands in Gordon Square, London; the Urdu article notes that it was the first memorial in England to a Muslim woman.{cite:wikipedia-noor-inayat-khan-ur}
