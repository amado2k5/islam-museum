## Overview
Shamardal ibn Shurayk ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Tha'laba, a poet of the Banu Yarbu'. The source describes him as one of the Kharijites and one of the poets of valour (hamasa) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
The encyclopedia gives his death as 100 AH (about 718 CE). The date in the data is recorded as a commonly transmitted report {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.196}.
