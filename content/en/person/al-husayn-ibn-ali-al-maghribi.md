## Overview
Abu'l-Qasim al-Husayn b. 'Ali al-Maghribi, katib-vizier and last of the Banu'l-Maghribi; he held the vizierate for Jalal al-Dawla — Ibn al-Jawzi gives him a lengthy tarjama among the deaths of 418. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.373-375}
