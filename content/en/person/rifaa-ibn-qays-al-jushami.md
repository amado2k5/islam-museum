## Overview
A man of [[group/banu-jusham|Banu Jusham ibn Mu'awiya]], "of a name and nobility among Jusham". He camped at al-Ghaba with his people and those with him, intending to rally Qays against the war on the Prophet {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/278}. He set out alone in pursuit of a shepherd of his who had lagged behind, and refused to let anyone accompany him. [[person/abdullah-ibn-abi-hadrad|Ibn Abi Hadrad]] shot him through the heart with an arrow, and he did not speak again. He cut off his head and carried it to the Prophet; see [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-al-ghaba|the raid on al-Ghaba]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/279}.

## Markers of the report
- "Rifa'a ibn Qays or Qays ibn Rifa'a": doubt in the text of Ibn Ishaq {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/278}.
- The intention to make war that is attributed to him is Ibn Ishaq's explanation for sending the detachment, not a report from him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/278}.
