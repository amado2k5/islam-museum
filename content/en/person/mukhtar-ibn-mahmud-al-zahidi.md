## Summary
A Hanafi jurist, usuli and expert in inheritance, of Azmin, a town of Khwarazm; studied under Ala al-Din Sadid ibn Muhammad al-Khayyati, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim al-Turkistani and Nasir al-Din al-Mutarrizi. Works include al-Hawi fi al-Fatawa, al-Mujtaba (a commentary on al-Quduri) and Qunyat al-Munya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7838}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7839}
