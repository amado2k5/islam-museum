## Overview
Abu Hatim Ahmad ibn Hamdan al-Razi, the missionary of al-Rayy among the Isma'ilis. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.673}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the "Book of Adornment", a large work of about four hundred leaves, and the "Book of the Compendium", which includes jurisprudence and other things. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.673} {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.268}

## Notes for researchers
- The source is single. The details attributed to him here are only his works; a second source is needed. The description of him as an Isma'ili comes from Ibn al-Nadim.
