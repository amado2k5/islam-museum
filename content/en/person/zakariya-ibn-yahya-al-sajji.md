## Overview
Abu Yahya Zakariya ibn Yahya al-Sajji al-Basri. He took from [[person/al-rabi-ibn-sulayman-al-muadhdhin|al-Rabi']] and [[person/al-muzani|al-Muzani]], and died in Basra in the year 307 AH {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 55}.

## His works
He has the book "Ikhtilaf al-Fuqaha'" (Differences of the Jurists) and the book "Ilal al-Hadith" (Defects of Hadith) {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 55}.
