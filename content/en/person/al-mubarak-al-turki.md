## Overview
Al-Mubarak al-Turki, a client of Musa al-Hadi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## A fortress in Qazwin
He built in [[place/qazwin|Qazwin]] a fortress called the City of al-Mubarak, and some of his clients live in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
