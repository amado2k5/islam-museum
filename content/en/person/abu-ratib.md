# Abu Ratib

## Summary

Muhammad Mustafa Ali, known as Abu Ratib (born 1962), is a Syrian munshid of Islamic and Arabic song built on classical Arabic poetry and the head of the al-Huda international nasheed ensemble. {cite:wikipedia-abu-ratib-ar} {cite:wikidata-abu-ratib}

## Early life and study

He was born in [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]] and immersed early in the musical traditions of the Arab world; he joined Aleppo's institute of Arabic music, where he studied solfège and the violin, and later studied broadcasting and television directing in Amman, Jordan, gaining experience in broadcast engineering. {cite:wikipedia-abu-ratib-ar}

## Awards

In 2006 he received the World Youth award for Islamic work in the field of religious song. {cite:wikipedia-abu-ratib-ar}

## 2010

The article, citing mlive and the Arab American News, reports that in 2010, after returning from Canada, he was detained in the United States in a matter connected with the Holy Land Foundation, that he admitted making false statements during naturalisation proceedings, and that he then left for a country of his choosing. The museum reports what the sources state, without characterisation. {cite:wikipedia-abu-ratib-ar}

## Family

He is married to Batoul Kawakji and has five daughters. {cite:wikipedia-abu-ratib-ar}
