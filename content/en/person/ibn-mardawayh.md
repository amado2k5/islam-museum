## Summary
Ahmad ibn Musa ibn Mardawayh ibn Furak, Abu Bakr the hafiz of Isfahan — great imam, muhaddith and mufassir, author of the copious Musnad Tafsir, the Musnad and Tarikh Isbahan; among the imams and abundant hafizes of hadith; he died at Isfahan in Ramadan 410. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.294}
