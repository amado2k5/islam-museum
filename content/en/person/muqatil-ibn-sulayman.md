## Overview
Muqatil ibn Sulayman, one of the Zaydis, the transmitters of hadith and the readers. He wrote the great commentary (*al-Tafsir al-Kabir*) and the *Book of the Abrogating and the Abrogated*. The *Sealed Nectar* relays from him what was first made obligatory of prayer. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}

## In the Sealed Nectar (pp. 76–77)
The *Sealed Nectar* relays from him that God made obligatory at the beginning of Islam the prayer of two rak'as in the morning and two in the evening, citing the verse of [[passage/quran-40-55|Ghafir 55]] ([[concept/al-salat-qabl-al-isra|the prayer before the Night Journey]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.77} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.88}
