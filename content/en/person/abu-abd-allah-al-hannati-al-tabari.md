## Overview
One of the imams of Tabaristan, who came to Baghdad in the days of Shaykh Abu Hamid al-Isfara'ini. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 62}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his date of death; the editor estimates it to be just after or just before the year 400 AH. The editor's note attributes his name to al-Subki as al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 62}

He is not [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-hannat-al-shirazi|Abu Abd Allah al-Hannat al-Shirazi]].
