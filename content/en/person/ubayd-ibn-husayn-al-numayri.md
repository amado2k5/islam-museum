## Overview
Ubayd ibn Husayn al-Numayri, whose kunya was Abu Jundal, and who was known as al-Ra'i for his frequent description of camels in his poetry. He was one of the senior poets of his age {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.42}.

## His poetry
He praised Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and joined al-Farazdaq in satirising Jarir {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.42}.

The author holds that he was an Umayyad for the sake of his people, and that he wished Abd al-Malik to lift from them the injustice of the tax-collectors {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.43}.
