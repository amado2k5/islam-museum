# Adnan Rashid

## Summary
Adnan Rashid is a British Muslim historian and lecturer based in London, specialising in the history of Islamic civilisation and Islamic numismatics. He holds a BA with honours in history from Birkbeck College, University of London, and a master's degree from SOAS, and holds ijazas in hadith. {cite:muslim-central-adnan-rashid} He runs his own YouTube channel of history lectures and debates. {cite:youtube-adnan-rashid}

## Work
Rashid served as a senior researcher and lecturer with the dawah organisation iERA and has led dawah missions to Africa; he is a khateeb in a number of West London mosques and has debated high-profile Christian and atheist figures. {cite:muslim-central-adnan-rashid} His official website presents him as a historian, bibliophile and numismatologist, and records his co-authored peer-reviewed research on the medals of Tipu Sultan's Mysore published in the Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society, together with the guided history tours he leads to al-Andalus and Ottoman sites. {cite:website-adnan-rashid}
