## Overview
Muzaffar, lord of Sahyun castle — held it thirty-three years after his father, "resolute, commanding, awe-inspiring"; he lived ninety years, was buried in the castle, and his son Sayf al-Din Muhammad succeeded {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 306}.
