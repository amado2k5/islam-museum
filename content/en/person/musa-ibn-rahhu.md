## Overview
Musa ibn Rahhu ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Haqq al-Marini, a Marinid commander, the first to hold the leadership of the Banu Rahhu over the raiders of al-Andalus. He is [[group/marinid-ghazis-andalus|one of the Marinid raiders in al-Andalus]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## In al-Andalus
He withdrew to the mountains of Ghumara when the princes of the house rose against Sultan Abu Yusuf. Then he crossed into al-Andalus in 670 AH, and Ibn al-Ahmar appointed him over all the holy warrior raiders there, because he was their chief and the one they turned to. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

His leadership of this command passed to his brother Abd al-Haqq, and then to his son Hammu. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
