## Summary
Ibn Shufayn, the Hashimi 'Abbasi sharif, musnid of Iraq. Abu Bakr ibn al-Zaghuni, Nasr ibn Nasr al-'Ukbari, Abu'l-Waqt and Muhammad ibn 'Ubayd Allah al-Ratbi gave him ijazas, and he heard from Yahya ibn al-Sadnak. He was noble and gracious. He died in Rajab at ninety-one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 208-209}
