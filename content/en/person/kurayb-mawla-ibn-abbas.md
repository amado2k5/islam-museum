## Summary
Kurayb b. Abi Muslim, client of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|'Abd Allah b. 'Abbas]], was a Successor of [[place/medina|Medina]]; [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] gives his notice in [[work/al-tamhid-ibn-abd-al-barr|al-Tamhid]] at the report that [[person/ibrahim-ibn-uqba|Ibrahim b. 'Uqba]] transmits from him. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260}

## Teachers and students
He heard [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama b. Zayd]] and 'Abd Allah b. 'Abbas. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260} A number of the leading men of Medina transmitted from him, among them the three sons of 'Uqba — Ibrahim, Muhammad and [[person/musa-ibn-uqba|Musa]] — and Bukayr b. al-Ashajj. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260}

## Standing and death
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr calls him trustworthy and an authority in the religious reports he transmitted. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260} He relays from al-Waqidi, from Ibn Abi al-Zinad, from Musa b. 'Uqba, that Kurayb died in Medina in 98 AH, at the end of the caliphate of Sulayman b. 'Abd al-Malik. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 260}

## His report in the Muwatta'
His report on a child's pilgrimage, which Malik transmits from Ibrahim b. 'Uqba from him, is mursal in most recensions of the Muwatta'; Ibn Wahb, al-Shafi'i, Abu Mus'ab and others transmitted it from Malik with a full chain, from Kurayb from Ibn 'Abbas. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 259-260}
