## Overview
Mundhir ibn Sa'id ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Qasim al-Nafzi al-Qurtubi al-Balluti, chief judge of the community in Córdoba in the Nasirid state. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* says that he was born in 273 AH (886 CE) and died in 355 AH (966 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His sermon
Al-Maqqari relates that al-Nasir was devoted to building until he built the city of Madinat al-Zahra', and he missed attending the Friday prayer in the Great Mosque for three Fridays. Mundhir opened his sermon with a verse denouncing construction, and went on in admonition until those present wept and the caliph wept. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 606-607}

Al-Maqqari says that al-Nasir was angry with Mundhir and swore that he would not pray the Friday prayer behind him, but he refused to dismiss him when his son al-Hakam asked him, saying that he was ashamed before God to lose an intercessor like him in his piety and sincerity. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 606-607}
