# Manal Chinutay

## Summary
Manal Chinutay is a London-based modest-fashion, beauty and lifestyle creator known online as Chinutay A. The Modish Muslimah list of top UK modest-fashion influencers describes her feed as beautiful pictures alongside travel diaries, hijab tutorials and beauty hacks. {cite:modishmuslimah-uk-influencers}
