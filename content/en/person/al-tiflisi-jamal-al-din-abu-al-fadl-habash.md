## Overview
Jamal al-Din Abu al-Fadl Habash ibn Ibrahim al-Tiflisi, a physician, astrologer, philologist and man of letters from Tiflis. He lived in the days of the Seljuks and served them in medicine and astronomy. Most of his works are in Persian. He died in 629 AH (1231 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.527}
