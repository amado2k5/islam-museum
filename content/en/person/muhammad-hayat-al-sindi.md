## Overview
Muhammad Hayat ibn Ibrahim al-Sindi al-Madani was a scholar of hadith. He was born in al-Sind, and lived and died in Medina. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1163 AH, 1750 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 111}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books: a commentary on al-Mundhiri's al-Targhib wa-l-tarhib; a Muqaddima on creed, in manuscript; Tuhfat al-muhibbin, in manuscript, a commentary on al-Nawawi's Forty Hadiths; and a commentary on al-Hikam al-'Ata'iyya, in manuscript; he adds that there are others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 111}.
