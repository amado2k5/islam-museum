## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Sahl al-Tabari, who wrote for al-Hazyar ibn Qarin. When he became a Muslim at the hand of al-Mu'tasim, he drew near, and his excellence became manifest at the court. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.412}

## His life
Al-Mutawakkil included him among his boon companions, and he was of standing in literature. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.412}

## His books
- "Firdaws al-Hikma": seven kinds, containing thirty treatises, and in it three hundred and sixty chapters.
- "Tuhfat al-Muluk".
- "Kunnash al-Hadra".
- "Manafi' al-At'ima wa al-Ashriba wa al-Maqaqir" (on the benefits of foods, drinks and drugs).

{cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.412}
