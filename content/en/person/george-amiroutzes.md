## Summary
George Amiroutzes, "the Greek scholar" of al-Salabi's text, a geographer in the service of [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 141-142}.

## Translating Ptolemy
Al-Salabi records that when Mehmed came upon Ptolemy's Geography and its map, he studied it eagerly with Amiroutzes, then charged him and his son — versed in Greek and Arabic — with translating the book into Arabic and redrawing the map with place-names verified in both tongues, rewarding both generously {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 141-142}.
