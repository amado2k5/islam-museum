## Overview
Ruh ibn Hatim ibn Qabisa ibn al-Muhallab al-Azdi was an amir counted among the praised men of generosity. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 174 AH (791 CE) and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 34}.

## Career
He served as chamberlain to the Abbasid caliph al-Mansur; al-Mahdi, al-Mansur's son, appointed him over Sind and then moved him to Basra and Kufa {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 34}. Harun al-Rashid made him governor of Palestine and then recalled him. On his way to Baghdad the news arrived of the death of his brother Yazid ibn Hatim, the amir of Ifriqiya, and al-Rashid sent Ruh there as governor of Qayrawan in 171 AH. He stayed in the post until he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 34}.

## Death and character
He was buried beside his brother. Al-Zirikli describes him as known for learning, courage and resolve {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 34}.
