## Overview
Abd al-Hamid ibn Muhammad al-Mustafa ibn Badis was an Algerian scholar and leader of the reform movement. He was born in Constantine in 1889 CE (1307 AH) and died in 1940 CE (1359 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba j.3 p.53}
