## Overview
Abu al-Tayyib Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Tafilalti al-Maliki al-Maghribi, a Moroccan mufti from Tafilalt. He was a Maliki who later became a Hanafi. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.366}

## Death
He died in 1191 AH (around 1777 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.366}
