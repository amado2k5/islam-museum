## Overview
Hisham ibn Ubayd Allah al-Razi carries the epithet al-Sunni in the heading al-Dhahabi gives him, who calls him the jurist, one of the imams of the sunna {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Al-Dhahabi says he was one of the seas of knowledge {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. He reports Musa ibn Nusayr hearing him say that he had met one thousand seven hundred teachers, the youngest of them Abd al-Razzaq, and that seven hundred thousand dirhams had gone from him in seeking knowledge {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Ibn Abi Dhi'b, Malik ibn Anas, Hammad ibn Zayd, Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Mukhtar and their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Baqiyya ibn al-Walid, who was also one of his own teachers, narrated from him, as did Muhammad ibn Sa'id al-Attar, al-Hasan ibn Arafa, Hamdan ibn al-Mughira, Abu Hatim al-Razi, Ahmad ibn al-Furat, Abd Allah ibn Yazid and others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}.

## What the critics said
Abu Hatim said he was truthful, and that he never saw anyone of greater standing or eminence than Hisham in Rayy and Abu Mushir al-Ghassani in Damascus {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Ibn Hibban, by contrast, called him weak and cited a report from him that al-Dhahabi says cannot be accepted {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Al-Dhahabi also quotes the shaykh Abu Ishaq in Tabaqat al-Hanafiyya as saying he was lax in transmission and that Muhammad ibn al-Hasan died in his house {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Muhammad ibn Khalaf al-Kharraz reports hearing him say that the Qur'an is the speech of God and uncreated; asked by a man about the description of the reminder as newly arrived in a verse, he answered that it is new to us and not new with God {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. Al-Dhahabi comments that this is because it belongs to God's knowledge, and God's knowledge is not described as originated {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}. The museum gives each judgement to the one who made it and chooses none.

## His death
He died in 221 AH, a date given by Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Abdi {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص4086}.
