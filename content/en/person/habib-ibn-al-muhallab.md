## Overview
Habib ibn al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra. Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik appointed him to the war in Sind. He camped on the shore of the Mihran, and its people gave him their obedience, and he fought a group of people and gained victory over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Sulayman appointed him governor of Sind, but he did not allow the son of Dahir to take back the rule of his fathers. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.108}
