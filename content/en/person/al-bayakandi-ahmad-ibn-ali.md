## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Amr al-Sulaymani al-Bayakandi, a scholar from Bayakand, to which his nisba refers. He was born in 311 AH (923 CE) and died in 404 AH (1013 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
