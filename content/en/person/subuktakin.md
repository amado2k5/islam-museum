## Overview
Subuktakin, chamberlain of Izz al-Dawla Bakhtiyar ibn Mu'izz al-Dawla. al-Suyuti reports that when al-Muti' was struck with paralysis and his tongue grew heavy, he called on him to depose himself and hand over the command to his son al-Ta'i. He did so in the year 363 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.624}.

## After the deposition
The next day al-Ta'i bestowed on him the robes of the sultanate, gave him the banner, and gave him the title Nasr al-Dawla. Then a dispute arose between him and Izz al-Dawla. He called the Turks to himself, they answered him, and wars took place between them {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.626}.
