## Overview
Tushi, also called Dushi, son of [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]], his eldest son. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His domains
His father assigned to him the lands from Filaq to Bulghar, which is the seat of the Qipchaq, and added to him Aran, Hamadhan, Tabriz, Maragha and Irlan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His descendants
He died in his father's lifetime around 624 AH, the year being approximate. He left Nakhu and [[person/berke-khan|Berke]], and the kingdom settled among his descendants in the Dasht-i Qipchaq. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
