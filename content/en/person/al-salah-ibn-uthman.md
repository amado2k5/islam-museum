## Overview
Al-Salah 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Uthman al-Shahrazuri the Kurd (c. 539–618, Aleppo), father of the hafiz Taqi al-Din Ibn al-Salah; among the noted Kurdish shaykhs — his son first read fiqh on him, then he moved him to Mosul to pursue it; he had earlier entered Baghdad and studied there, and also under Sharaf al-Din Ibn Abi 'Asrun {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
