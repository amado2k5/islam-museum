## Summary
Of Banu Sahm, described by Ibn Ishaq as "the poet"; he emigrated to Abyssinia and died there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him first of eleven men of Banu Sahm in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", traces him to Sa'd ibn Sahm, describes him as "the poet", and says he died in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} He repeats him among those who died. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The book's manuscripts have "Sa'id" instead of "Sa'd" in the lineage of Banu Adi ibn Sa'd ibn Sahm — a corruption, per the book's editors; al-Suhayli (quoted in the note on p. 8) says others disagree: it is Sa'd, or Sa'id ibn Sahm the brother of Sa'd.
