## Overview
Mujalid ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami, brother of Mujashi'. Al-Baladhuri says that he said words with his brother to al-Aswad ibn Sari' when the mosque of Basra was being marked out. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The text of their remark and the details of the occasion need an additional source.
