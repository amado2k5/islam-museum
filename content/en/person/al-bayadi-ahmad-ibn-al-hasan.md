## Overview
Kamal al-Din Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Yusuf al-Bayadi al-Rumi al-Hanafi, a Hanafi scholar of Ottoman Rumi origin. He is attributed to Bisna, and is titled Kamal al-Din. He died in 1098 AH (1687 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
