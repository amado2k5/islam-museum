## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Fadl Burhan al-Din al-Nasafi, was a Hanafi scholar of Qur'anic exegesis, legal theory and theology. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 600 AH, 1203 CE, and his death as 687 AH, 1289 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 31}.

## Life
He lived in Baghdad and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 31}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names al-Wadih, a summary of the Qur'anic commentary of [[person/fakhr-al-din-al-razi|al-Fakhr al-Razi]]; al-Muqaddima al-Nasafiyya, also called al-Muqaddima al-Burhaniyya, on the law of disagreement (khilaf); al-Fusul fi Ilm al-Jadal; Mansha' al-Nazar fi Ilm al-Khilaf; al-Qawadih al-Jadaliyya; Daf' al-Nusus wa-l-Nuqud; and a commentary on the Beautiful Names of God. He marks all of these as manuscripts except al-Wadih, for which he gives no status {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 31}.
