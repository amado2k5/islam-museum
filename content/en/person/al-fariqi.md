## Overview
Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. Ibrahim al-Fariqi al-Shafi'i al-Azdi; he first studied under Abu Muhammad al-Majd al-Kazaruni, then moved to Baghdad to work under Shaykh Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi, author of the Muhadhdhab, and Abu Nasr Ibn al-Sabbagh, author of the Shamil; he held the judgeship of Wasit, abstemious and scrupulous, and heard hadith from al-Khatib Abu Bakr; his is the Kitab al-fawa'id on the Muhadhdhab. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
