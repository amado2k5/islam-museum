## Overview
Ahmad ibn Khalil ibn Sa'ada ibn Ja'far ibn Isa, Abu al-Abbas Shams al-Din al-Muhallabi al-Khu'i, was a Shafi'i judge, learned in theology (kalam), who also had knowledge of medicine. He was born in Khuy in Azerbaijan, studied there and in Khurasan, then became chief judge (qadi al-qudat) in Syria and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 583 to 637 AH, that is 1187 to 1240 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. He was the father of the judge Muhammad ibn Ahmad, who died in 693 AH as given in «al-I'lam» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names a book on the principles (usul); a book which Ibn Abi Usaybi'a says contains philosophical allusions, written for the sultan al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Isa ibn Abi Bakr ibn Ayyub; «al-Safina al-Nuhiyya», in manuscript, on the soul and spirit, in whose preface he says he had meant to comment on al-Fakhr al-Razi's book on the soul but turned to this short book instead, putting into it what spares the reader length, the copy being a precious library copy written in 868 AH, 31 leaves, in a collection in Damascus; and a book on prosody, which Abu Shama says he has in the author's hand {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}.
