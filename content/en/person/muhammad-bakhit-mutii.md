## Overview
Muhammad Bakhit ibn Husayn al-Mutii al-Hanafi was the mufti of the Egyptian lands and one of its great jurists; the entry gives his years as 1271 to 1354 AH, 1854 to 1935 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His life
He was born in the town of al-Mutia in the district of Asyut, studied at al-Azhar and taught there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He moved to the sharia judiciary in 1297 AH and became connected with Sayyid Jamal al-Din al-Afghani {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. Al-Zirikli says he was among the fiercest opponents of the reform movement led by Shaykh Muhammad Abduh {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. He was appointed mufti of the Egyptian lands from 1333 to 1339 AH, 1914 to 1921 CE, and then kept to his house giving fatwas and teaching until he died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names these books of his, all printed: "Irshad al-Umma ila Ahkam Ahl al-Dhimma"; "Ahsan al-Kalam fima Yata'allaq bi al-Sunna wa al-Bida' min al-Ahkam"; "Husn al-Bayan fi Daf' ma Warada min al-Shubah ala al-Qur'an"; "Izahat al-Wahm", on the two questions of the phonograph and the "sukurta"; "al-Kalimat al-Hisan fi al-Ahruf al-Sab'a wa Jam' al-Qur'an"; "al-Qawl al-Mufid fi Ilm al-Tawhid"; "al-Ajwiba al-Misriyya an al-As'ila al-Tunisiyya"; "al-Badr al-Sati' ala Jam' al-Jawami'", on the principles of jurisprudence; "Haqiqat al-Islam wa Usul al-Hukm"; "al-Murhafat al-Yamaniyya", on family endowment; "Irshad al-Ibad fi al-Waqf ala al-Awlad"; "al-Qawl al-Jami'", on divorce; "al-Kalimat al-Tayyibat", on the Night Journey and Ascension; and "Raf' al-Aghlaq an Mashru' al-Zawaj wa al-Talaq" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
