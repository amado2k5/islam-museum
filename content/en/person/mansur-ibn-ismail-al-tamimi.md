## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Mansur ibn Isma'il al-Tamimi al-Misri, a Shafi'i jurist of the Sunni community. He was blind, and took jurisprudence from the companions of al-Shafi'i and their companions. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}

## His works
He wrote works on the school, among them *al-Wajib* (The Obligatory), *al-Musta'mal* (The Used), *al-Musafir* (The Traveller) and *al-Hidaya* (The Guidance). {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}

## Death
Al-Shirazi says that he died before the year three hundred and twenty. The editor's margin, relaying from al-Subki and Ibn Khallikan, says that his death was in Jumada al-Ula 306 AH. That needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}
