## Overview
Buran bint al-Hasan ibn Sahl; Ibn al-Jawzi notes al-Ma'mun consummated his marriage to her in 210 AH — she was al-Ma'mun's wife and daughter of his client al-Hasan ibn Sahl. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 10 p. 108}

## In Kahhala's A'lam al-Nisa'
Kahhala describes her as among the most accomplished of women in culture and character; she was born in Safar 192 AH, and al-Ma'mun married her in Ramadan 210 AH, spending vast sums on the wedding. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.163} He cites al-Tabari that al-Ma'mun stayed with her father al-Hasan ibn Sahl for seventeen days, that al-Hasan gave robes and gifts to the commanders to a total of fifty million dirhams, that al-Ma'mun ordered him ten million from the revenue of Fars and granted him a district above Wasit, and that al-Hasan scattered among the commanders and the Hashimites slips bearing the names of estates, each to go to whoever caught it. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.163} He adds that Hamduna bint Afif put the spending on Buran at twenty-five million dirhams, that according to al-Suli al-Hasan spread for her a mat of gold, and that she died in 271 AH. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.163}
