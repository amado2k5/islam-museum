## Overview
Buran daughter of al-Hasan ibn Sahl, a wife of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. He married her in 210 AH with a trousseau of forty jewelled belts and chests of pearls and rubies. Her father scattered a dish of jewels before al-Ma'mun at her wedding. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.487-488}

Ibn al-Jawzi says that she died in Rabi al-Awwal 271 AH at the age of eighty, and that she was of sharp mind and intelligence. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.82}
