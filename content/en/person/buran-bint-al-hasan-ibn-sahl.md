## Overview
Buran bint al-Hasan ibn Sahl; Ibn al-Jawzi notes al-Ma'mun consummated his marriage to her in 210 AH — she was al-Ma'mun's wife and daughter of his client al-Hasan ibn Sahl. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 10 p. 108}
