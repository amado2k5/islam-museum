## Overview
Al-Hasan b. Abi'l-Rabi' — Abu'l-Rabi' being Yahya b. al-Ju'd b. Nashit — transmitted from 'Abd al-Razzaq, Yazid, Shababa, al-'Aqdi and others; al-Baghawi, Ibn Sa'id and al-Mahamili transmitted from him, and Ibn Abi Hatim said "he is truthful." He died in the Karkh of the City of Peace in Jumada I 163 at eighty-five. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 133}
