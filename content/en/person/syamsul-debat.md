## Summary
Syamsul Amri bin Ismail (born 3 August 1977), better known as Syamsul Debat, is a Malaysian freelance preacher, host, motivational expert, politician and social-media personality. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms} He is famous for his religious television programmes, most of them broadcast on TV AlHijrah, among them the programme that bears his name, Syamsul Debat. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms}

## Early life
He was born on 3 August 1977 at Jempol in the state of Negeri Sembilan. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms}

## Politics, as reported
This section sets out his political history exactly as the sources report it. It is past history, not a present position, and it is not the basis of his place in this museum — his preaching reach is.

In April 2018 the national news agency Bernama reported him campaigning as the Barisan Nasional (BN) candidate for the Sikamat state seat in the fourteenth general election (GE14), against the incumbent Aminuddin Harun of PKR; the report names him Seremban UMNO Youth information chief at the time. {cite:mstar-syamsul-debat-2018} The election table records the result: Aminuddin Harun (PH/PKR) 9,832 votes, Syamsul Debat (BN/UMNO) 6,419 — he was not elected. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms} On 18 May 2018 he announced that he was releasing all his UMNO posts with immediate effect, while remaining a member of the party. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms} No source read for this record shows a later candidacy or party office, and none is claimed. {cite:wikipedia-syamsul-debat-ms}
