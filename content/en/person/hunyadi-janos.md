## Summary
John Hunyadi — the Hungarian commander who led the crusader coalition's land forces against Murad II. In al-Sallabi's account he crossed the Danube and inflicted two heavy defeats on the Ottomans in 846/1442, driving them to sue for the ten-year peace sworn at Szeged in 848/1444 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 80}.
