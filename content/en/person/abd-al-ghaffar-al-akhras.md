## Overview
Abd al-Ghaffar ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Wahb al-Akhras was a poet from Mosul who moved to Baghdad and grew up there. He was called al-Akhras (the stammerer) because of a speech impediment. His birth is estimated at around 1805 CE and his death at around 1873 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba j.1 p.360}
