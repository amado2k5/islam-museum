## Summary
Ali ibn Thabit was a worker, and he urged those who sought God to shed heavy burdens and cast aside preoccupations. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 499, sequence 500, on page 142. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}

## What he said of the two lives
Muhammad ibn Mu'awiya al-Azraq relates from Ali ibn Thabit, who was among those who worked for God, that he said: if you can, do not be on the same footing in both lives. The word after the name is unclear in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}
