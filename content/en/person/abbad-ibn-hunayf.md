## Summary
One of the twelve men who built the Dirar mosque, per Ibn Ishaq; his lineage: brother of Sahl ibn Hunayf, of Banu Amr ibn Awf. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In the Dirar mosque
Ibn Ishaq named him in the list of those who built the mosque the Prophet ordered demolished on his return from Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage as brother of Sahl ibn Hunayf. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq mentions nothing more of the man here than his lineage and his name among the twelve.
- Describing the group as "hypocrites" in the book is not attributed here to this man in particular; the verse's descent "among them" is collective (p. 174).
