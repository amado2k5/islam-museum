## Overview
Kharija ibn Hudhafa al-Adawi was in the army of al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, which was sent to reinforce the conquest of Egypt, as part of [[event/conquest-of-egypt|the conquest of Egypt]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

After the conquest of Fustat, Amr ibn al-As sent him to al-Fayyum, al-Ashmunayn, Akhmim, al-Bushrudat and the villages of Upper Egypt. Their people made peace on terms like the terms of Fustat. This is recorded among [[event/four-lieutenants-egypt|the four commanders in Egypt]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
