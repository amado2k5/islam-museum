## Overview
Sufyan ibn Mujib al-Azdi, a commander whom Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan sent to Tripoli in the caliphate of Uthman. His mission is connected to [[event/conquest-of-syria|the conquest of Syria]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.173-174}.

## Tripoli
al-Baladhuri reports that he built a fortress in a meadow a few miles from the city, which was named the Fortress of Sufyan. He cut off supplies to its people and besieged them, until they gathered in one of their three fortresses and wrote to the king of the Byzantines. He sent them ships, in which they sailed by night and fled. In the morning Sufyan found the fortress empty, entered it, and wrote to Mu'awiya announcing the conquest {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.173-174}.

Mu'awiya settled a large number of Jews there, and each year he sent troops to it and appointed an agent over it. Later its people broke the treaty in the days of Abd al-Malik, and a Byzantine patrician settled in it. It is said that he and its agent were killed, and it is said that he was captured and killed and crucified {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.173-174}.
