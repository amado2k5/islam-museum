## Overview
Abu'l-Faraj al-Zaz 'Abd al-Rahman b. Ahmad b. Muhammad b. Zaz b. Humayd, the teacher al-Sarakhsi then al-Marwazi — jurist of Marw and pupil of the qadi Husayn; Ibn al-Sam'ani says he was one of Islam's imams, proverbial through the lands for memorising the Shafi'i madhhab — the imams journeyed to him from every side; pious, scrupulous, cautious in food and dress — he would not eat rice because it needs much water and its grower rarely does not wrong another; born in 431 or 432. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## His works
Among them the Kitab al-amali — al-Asnawi said most of al-Rafi'i's transmission is from six works besides al-Ghazali's words, among them the Amali of Abu'l-Faraj al-Sarakhsi; he died in 494. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
