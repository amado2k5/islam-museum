## Summary
Noura bint Mohammed al-Kaabi is the Emirati minister who built the UAE's culture ministry and its creative-industry policies, moving between government, broadcasting and cultural boards — not to be confused with Qatar's energy minister Saad Sherida al-Kaabi, a different person. {cite:muslim500-noura-al-kaabi} {cite:wikipedia-noura-al-kaabi}

## Formation
Born in 1979 in Abu Dhabi, she studied at the UAE University and worked in Abu Dhabi's state media, rising through twofour54, the emirate's media-zone authority, where she became CEO. {cite:wikipedia-noura-al-kaabi}

## Career
Appointed minister of state for Federal National Council affairs in 2016, she became Minister of Culture and Knowledge Development in 2017 — later renamed Culture and Youth — and in 2023 returned to minister-of-state rank in the foreign ministry, also chairing the UAE's National Media Office-linked cultural bodies and serving on boards including the UAE National Media Council and Abu Dhabi's media and cultural institutions. {cite:muslim500-noura-al-kaabi} {cite:wikipedia-noura-al-kaabi}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists her under Media as the UAE's architect of soft power through culture: museums, film policy and creative-economy strategy. {cite:muslim500-noura-al-kaabi}
