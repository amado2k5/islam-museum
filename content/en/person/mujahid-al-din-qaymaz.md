## Overview
Mujahid al-Din Qaymaz, a ruler in the state of Nur al-Din Mahmud ibn Zangi, to whom affairs were referred. Ibn al-Athir says that his governorship of the citadel of Mosul began in Dhu al-Qa'da 571 AH, that he was appointed over Irbil in 559 AH, and that he died in the citadel of Mosul in Rabi' al-Awwal 595 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 263}
