## Summary
A knight of [[group/banu-sulaym|Banu Sulaym]] known as Ibn al-Dughunna; he caught up with the old man [[person/durayd-ibn-al-simma|Durayd ibn al-Simma]] at Awtas after the rout of Hunayn and killed him after a famous exchange {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/93-94}.

## The killing of Durayd ibn al-Simma
Rabi'a caught up with Durayd ibn al-Simma as he sat in his litter on his she-camel, too old to fight; Rabi'a struck him with his sword but cut nothing. Durayd said to him in mockery: "how poorly your mother armed you! Take this sword of mine from the back of the litter and strike with it — keep clear of the bones — and tell your mother you killed Durayd ibn al-Simma; many a day I defended your women" — and he struck him with his own sword and killed him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/93-94}.

His daughter Umm al-Banin Ruqayya bint Durayd mourned him in famous verses recalling her father's feats and his killer's inadequacy {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/94}.
