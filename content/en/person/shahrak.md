## Overview
Shahrak, marzban and governor of Fars. The transmitted account describes the Arab arrival in Fars as the greatest challenge he faced, and says he gathered a large force and marched until he reached Rayshahr {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Rayshahr
He took part in the [[event/battle-of-rayshahr|Battle of Rayshahr]], where Sawwar ibn Hammam al-Abdi struck him with a spear and killed him from his horse. He posted a man with a group in a valley, and ordered them to kill any fugitive who passed through it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
