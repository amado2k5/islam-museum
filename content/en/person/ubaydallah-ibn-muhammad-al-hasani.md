## Overview
He is Ubaydallah ibn Muhammad al-Hasani, governor of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/zahr-yemen|Zahr]], where Abu al-Ghasham and some of Al Ya'fur were jailed {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. Al-Hadi told him to raise the call in the district and send his men to Shibam, and he sent the men of the district of Zahr to the imam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217}. When the men of his reinforcement shouted for arms and shot at the gate of Zahr he fled to Sana'a and left Zahr empty, and the prison was opened {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. The text calls him Abdullah ibn Muhammad once on p. 217, and a footnote gives another manuscript's reading Ubaydallah ibn Muhammad al-Alawi on p. 219; both readings are kept.
