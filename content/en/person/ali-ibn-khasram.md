## Overview
Ali ibn Khashram ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Ata ibn Hilal al-Marwazi, Abu al-Hasan, is called by al-Dhahabi the imam, the memoriser, the truthful, and was the son of Bishr al-Hafi's sister {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}. The sign printed with his entry shows that Muslim, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}. Abu Raja Muhammad ibn Hamdawayh heard him say he was born in 160 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}.

## Teachers and students
He heard Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Darawardi, Hushaym ibn Bashir, Isa ibn Yunus, Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash, Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Abd Allah ibn Wahb, al-Fadl ibn Musa al-Sinani, Abu Tumayla, Waki' and their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}. Muslim, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma, Abu Bakr ibn Abi Dawud, Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Firabri, Muhammad ibn Mu'adh al-Malini and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}. The highest chains in Transoxiana, Marw and Herat ended with him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}.

## Reports about him
Abu Raja reports him saying he had fasted eighty-eight Ramadans {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}. Al-Dhahabi notes that his narration from him occurs in a gloss on the hadith of Musa and al-Khidr from Ibn Uyayna, and cautions that this is not in every copy of the Sahih {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}.

## His death
He died in Ramadan 257 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2778}.
