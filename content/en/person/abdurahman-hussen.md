## Summary
Abdurahman Hussen is an Ethiopian dawah preacher who addresses his audience in Afaan Oromo and reaches it chiefly through his TikTok account. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen} The account counted 819,900 followers when read directly on 7 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a measurement the museum makes itself. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen}

## Platforms and work
His TikTok account (@abdurahman_hussen) held 1,550 videos, 15,100,000 likes in total and 376 following when its profile was read on 7 November 2026. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen} The account's display name is "Abdurahman hussen", and its Afaan Oromo bio directs viewers to the link of his YouTube channel below it, without that channel's name or count being read in this research. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen} The account carries no platform verification badge. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen} This wave's harvest recorded a celebration of his reaching 700,000 followers on 25 December 2025 in a post by Ustaz Riyad Abdaallah carrying a screenshot of the profile, but that post itself could not be located in a citable form on 7 November 2026, so this record does not rest on it; the direct read carried here is higher than it and consistent with it. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen}

## Impact and audience
Abdurahman Hussen's TikTok account stood at 819,900 followers and 1,550 videos when read on 7 November 2026, placing him among the largest individual Afaan Oromo dawah accounts on the short-video platform. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen} This record is deliberately minimal and honest: no Wikipedia article in any language and no independent source documenting his biography could be found, so no birth date and no school of law are recorded for him here, and no Ethiopic-script form of his name was verified from any page read, so none is recorded. {cite:tiktok-abdurahman-hussen}
