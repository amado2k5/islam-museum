## Summary
Leader of the tribes around Tlemcen; an enemy of Ottoman influence who became the Sa'dis' intermediary to Spain {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230-241}.

## The Spanish mediation
[[person/muhammad-al-shaykh-al-sadi|Muhammad al-Shaykh]] charged him with the Spanish channel; his first letter went to the [[person/conde-de-alcaudete|Count of Alcaudete]], governor of Oran, in early Rabi' I 963/January 1555, announcing receipt of the count's letters and the sharif's and his son Abdallah's pleasure at the coming embassy for the joint campaign {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 241}.
