## Summary
Leader of the tribes neighbouring Tlemcen who stood against Ottoman influence outside the city {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.

## Allegiances
Al-Salabi notes he wished to support his son-in-law, the prince Mulay Ahmad, an ally of Spain; his tribes' harrying kept Ottoman authority outside Tlemcen unsteady {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.
