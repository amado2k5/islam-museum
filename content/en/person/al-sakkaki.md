## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Yusuf b. Abi Bakr b. Muhammad al-Sakkaki, author of Miftah al-'ulum; a Hanafi, great imam, brilliant scholar, deep-diving in grammar, morphology, the sciences of meaning and expression, prosody and verse; he took from Shaykh al-Islam Mahmud b. Sa'id al-Harithi and from Sadid b. Muhammad al-Hannati; Mukhtar b. Mahmud al-Zahid, author of the Qunya, took kalam from him. He died in 626. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 122}
