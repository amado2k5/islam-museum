## Overview
Ahmad Ibn Aqbaras, the Khwarizmian of al-Salihiyya; he heard from Ishaq al-Amidi, Muhammad b. al-Muhibb and Zaynab bint al-Kamal. Ibn Hajar said: "I took much from him at the Salihiyya and he was a good man" — he died in the fitna {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 32}.
