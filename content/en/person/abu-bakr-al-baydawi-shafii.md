## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn al-Abbas, Abu Bakr al-Baydawi al-Farisi, a Shafii judge, jurist and man of letters; the entry gives his death as 468 AH. His works include al-Tabsira on the branches of fiqh, al-Adilla on the causes of the Tabsira's rulings, al-Tadhkira on its problems, and al-Irshad on the Kifaya of al-Sayməri. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7514}
