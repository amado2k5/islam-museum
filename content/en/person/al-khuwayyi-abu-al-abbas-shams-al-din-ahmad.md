## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Shams al-Din Ahmad al-Khuwayyi. He was born in 583 AH (1187 CE) and lived about fifty-five years. He died in Hebron and was buried there in 637 AH (1240 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
