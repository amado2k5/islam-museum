## Overview
Musa ibn Uqba of Medina, from the second generation of writers on the campaigns, who died in 141 AH. {cite:duha-islam p.639}

## His campaigns
The fame of his campaigns spread in the books of later authors, to the point that some scholars of hadith preferred them. {cite:duha-islam p.639}
