## In brief
Musa b. Uqba, the memoriser al-Asadi of Medina, client of the family of al-Zubayr b. al-Awwam; he heard Umm Khalid bint Khalid al-Muhabbiyya, Urwa, Salim, Abu Salama b. Abd al-Rahman, al-A'raj, and a group, and compiled the Maghazi. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.143-144}

## Transmission and the Maghazi
Ibn Jurayj, Malik, Ibn Uyayna, Hammad b. Isma'il, Ibn al-Mubarak, Abu Damra, Muhammad b. Fudayl, and others narrated from him; al-Waqidi said "Musa was a mufti and jurist", Ahmad b. Hanbal said "you must have the maghazi of Musa b. Uqba, for they are reliable", and al-Dhahabi read Musa's Maghazi at Mezza under Abu Nahr al-Farisi. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.144}

## His death
He died in 141 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.144}
