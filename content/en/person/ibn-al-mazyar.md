## Overview
Ibn al-Mazyar, a pupil of Abu Ma'shar the astrologer. Al-Mas'udi attributes to him the book *al-Muntakhab min Kitab al-Alluf* (Selections from the Book of Thousands), in which he mentions the great structures that come about every thousand years. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 556}
