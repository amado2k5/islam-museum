## Summary
Alauddin I, born I Mangngarangi, was the fourteenth ruler of Gowa in South Sulawesi and the first sultan of the Sultanate of Gowa (r. 1593–1639). {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en} {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id} He was the first ruler of Gowa to embrace Islam, which he did in 1605, and he made Islam the religion of the state. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en}

## Early life and conversion
He was the son of Tunijalloq, the twelfth king of Gowa, and was installed as king in 1593 at the age of seven after his elder brother Tunipasuluq's rule ended. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en} {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id} At about nine he was introduced to Islam by Datuk ri Bandang, a Minangkabau preacher from Koto Tangah, and on 22 September 1605 he converted, taking the name Alauddin; the Gowa–Talloq kingdom was then renamed the Sultanate of Gowa. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en} {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id}

## Reign
His reign, shared with the chief minister Karaeng Matoaya, saw large-scale expansion across Sulawesi, continued against Bone, and was driven in large part by the spread of Islam and by the sultanate's commercial ambitions; he built the forts Ana Gowa and Garassi and declared the seas of the sultanate open to traders of every origin. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en} {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id} Islam's adoption as the official state religion under him extended through the population of the realm. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en}

## Death
He fell ill at Cikkoang on 10 June 1639 and died five days later, on 15 June 1639, at Somba Opu. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id} {cite:wikidata-alauddin-of-gowa}

## Religion
Both articles document his conversion to Islam and his establishment of it as the state religion; the Indonesian infobox records his religion as "Animism, then Islam". No source read names a branch or a legal school for him, and his record keeps his faith unattributed beyond the documented conversion. {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-en} {cite:wikipedia-alauddin-of-gowa-id} {cite:wikidata-alauddin-of-gowa}
