## Overview
Yusuf ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hakam ibn Abi Aqil al-Thaqafi, cousin of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]]. al-Baladhuri reports that he was an agent of Hisham over Iraq, and the market of Yusuf in al-Hira is attributed to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Zayd ibn Ali
al-Mas'udi reports that he fought [[person/zayd-ibn-ali|Zayd ibn Ali]] when he rose at Kufa. The companions of Zayd were routed, and Zayd was struck in the forehead by an arrow and died {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 194}.

## The Umayyad state
The book "The Umayyad State" by al-Salabi reports that [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] appointed him over Yemen, then over Iraq and the East. He describes him as generous and bounteous, harsh and tyrannical. He reports that al-Walid ibn Yazid confirmed him over Iraq {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.482-483}. The book reports that he defeated the revolt of Zayd in Kufa, took out his body after his killing and sent his head to Hisham, and crucified him naked by Hisham's order. His strict measures affected the people of Kufa {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.510-513}.
