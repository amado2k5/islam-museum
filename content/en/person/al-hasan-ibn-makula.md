## Overview
Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. Ja'far Ibn Makula, vizier of Jalal al-Dawla after his cousin Abu Sa'd's imprisonment in 417; his mamluk killed him at al-Ahwaz in Dhu'l-Hijja 422 at fifty-six. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.403}
