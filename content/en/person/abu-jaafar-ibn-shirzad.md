## Overview
Abu Jafar ibn Shirzad, secretary to [[person/tuzun|Tuzun]], who ruled Baghdad at the end of Tuzun's time. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 610-613}

## In Baghdad
Tuzun sent him from Wasit to Baghdad, where he ruled and gave orders and prohibitions. He guaranteed the thief Hamdi against robbery in Baghdad for twenty-five thousand dinars a month. Hamdi would raid people's houses with torches and candles. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 610-613}

## After Tuzun's death
Tuzun died in the days of al-Mustakfi, and Ibn Shirzad hoped for the kingdom and swore the troops to himself. The caliph gave him a robe of honour, then Ibn Buyah entered Baghdad and he went into hiding. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 610-613}

Ibn al-Athir says that he hid with al-Mustakfi when Baghdad was in turmoil with the approach of [[person/muizz-al-dawla|Muizz al-Dawla]], and that his rule lasted three months and twenty days. He then reappeared, and Muizz al-Dawla gave him the kharaj and the collection of revenues. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 336}
