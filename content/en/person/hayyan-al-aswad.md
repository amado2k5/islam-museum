## Summary
Hayyan al-Aswad. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 536, sequence 537, on page 164. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}

## A thousand units each day and night
Ja'far ibn Muhammad relates from Hayyan al-Aswad: there was among us a man who stayed thirteen years praying a thousand units of prayer every day and night, until his legs were weakened. When he prayed the afternoon prayer he sat with his arms round his knees facing the qibla, and said: I marvel at creation, how it sought a substitute for You; I marvel at how its hearts were lit by remembering other than You; I marvel at how it found comfort in other than You. Then he kept silent until sunset. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/164}
