## Overview
Ali ibn Abd al-Ghani al-Fihri, whose kunya is Abu al-Hasan, a blind poet and reciter born in al-Andalus. The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" says he was born around 420 AH (1029 CE) and died around 488 AH (1095 CE); these two dates need a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His biographical details and poetry need an additional source.
