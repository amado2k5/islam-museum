## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Isma'il ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Khidr al-Badisi al-Gharnati, a hadith scholar, historian and genealogist. He is the author of "al-Maqsad al-Sharif" on the lives of the righteous in al-Rif, and died in 722 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.52}
