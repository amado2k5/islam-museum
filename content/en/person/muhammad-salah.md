# Muhammad Salah

## Summary
Muhammad Salah is an Egyptian scholar and television presenter — not the footballer of a similar name. He is the religious supervisor of Huda TV and the presenter of Ask Huda, one of the best-known live fatwa programmes on the channel, as well as Correct Your Recitation and Gardens of the Pious. {cite:huda-tv-muhammad-salah} {cite:listennotes-muhammad-salah}

## Studies and work
Salah holds a PhD in Comparative Fiqh, an MBA in Islamic Studies from Cloverdale College in the United States, a BA in Shari'ah and Islamic Law from Al-Azhar University in Egypt and a BA in pharmacology, besides a diploma in Muslim arts and Arabic calligraphy and a pre-master diploma in Islamic studies. {cite:huda-tv-muhammad-salah} {cite:listennotes-muhammad-salah} He led the Islamic Center of Victoria, Texas, and founded two Islamic and Arabic schools, Iman Stems and the Muslim Generation; he has taught at Al-Azhar University, Shariah Academy and a college in London, and has accumulated hundreds of live broadcasting hours on Egyptian, Arabic and American television stations. {cite:listennotes-muhammad-salah}
