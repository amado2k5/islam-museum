# Shareef Abdur-Rahim

## Summary

Shareef Abdur-Rahim was born in Marietta, Georgia on 11 December 1976; his full name is Julius Shareef Abdur-Rahim.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}{cite:wikidata-shareef-abdur-rahim}
The Vancouver Grizzlies drafted him third overall in 1996 after one season at the University of California, Berkeley, and he played twelve seasons in the NBA.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
Abdur-Rahim won the gold medal with the United States team at the Sydney 2000 Olympics.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-ar}
After retiring as a player in 2008 he held front-office posts with the Sacramento Kings and the league office, and became president of the developmental NBA G League in 2018.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}

## Career

Abdur-Rahim was born to Muslim parents of African origin, the third of his family's children, and starred in high-school basketball, earning selection to the McDonald's All-American team.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-ar}{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
In the NBA he moved between Vancouver, Atlanta, Portland and Sacramento, and was selected once to the All-Star team, in 2002, while playing for the Atlanta Hawks.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
After retirement he worked as an assistant coach and then a front-office executive with the Sacramento Kings, as general manager of the Reno Bighorns in 2013, and as the NBA's associate vice president of basketball operations.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
Abdur-Rahim was appointed president of the NBA G League in December 2018, succeeding Malcolm Turner.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}

## Works

Abdur-Rahim founded the Future Foundation, which provides after-school services and support for at-risk youth in Atlanta.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
He returned to the University of California, Berkeley after retirement and earned a degree in sociology in 2012, then an MBA from the University of Southern California in 2016.{cite:wikipedia-shareef-abdur-rahim-en}
