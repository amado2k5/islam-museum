## Overview
Ali ibn Mahdi al-Himyari, of the people of al-Athr on the coast of Zabid. Ibn Khaldun says in "al-Ibar" that he grew up in the righteousness of his father, withdrew and became ascetic, went on the pilgrimage, met the scholars of Iraq and took preaching from their preachers. He was a memoriser and eloquent, so people inclined to him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The founding of his state
After the death of Umm Fatik, the ruler of Zabid, in 545 AH, the people of the mountains came to him and allied with him. He took Tihama and its mountains, and in 554 AH he overcame the Banu Najah in Zabid. He was the founder of [[polity/dawlat-bani-mahdi-yemen|the state of the Banu Mahdi in Yemen]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
