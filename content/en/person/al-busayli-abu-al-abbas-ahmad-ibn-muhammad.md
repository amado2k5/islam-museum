## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Busayli, a scholar from Algeria who settled in Tunis and was buried there. Reports of his life are connected with the Tafsir of Ibn Arafa. He died in 1437 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
