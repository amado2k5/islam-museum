## Overview
Ahmad ibn Mani al-Baghawi, one of the memorisers, who accompanied Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam. He is known as Ibn Bint Mani, after his maternal grandfather. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.651-652}

His birth and death years are not recorded and need an additional source.
