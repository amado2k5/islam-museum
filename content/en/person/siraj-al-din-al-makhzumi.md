## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Muhammad al-Makhzumi al-Rifa'i al-Husayni, Siraj al-Din, is described by al-Zirikli as the shaykh al-Islam of his time. He gives his birth as 793 AH, 1391 CE, and his death as 885 AH, 1480 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 238}.

## Life
He was born in Wasit in Iraq, travelled to Syria and Egypt, and died in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 238}. Al-Zirikli adds that the Baghdad quarter called Mahallat al-Shaykh Siraj al-Din is named after him, and that he also wrote verse {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 238}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists: a Qur'anic exegesis, al-Bayan fi Tafsir al-Qur'an; Sihah al-Akhbar fi Nasab al-Sada al-Fatimiyya al-Akhyar, printed, in which he answers Ibn al-Athir's statement that the line of Khalid ibn al-Walid had died out; Jala' al-Qalb al-Hazin, on Sufism; Rahiq al-Kawthar, a printed treatise drawn from the sayings of Shaykh al-Rifa'i; Silah al-Mu'min, on hadith; and al-Nuskha al-Kubra, on what the people of the science of letters occupy themselves with {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 238}.
