## Overview
Muhammad Mustafa Hilmi, one of the pioneers of philosophical studies in Egypt and the Arab world. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates
He was born in 1322 AH (1904 CE) and died in 1389 AH (1969 CE), according to the account reported. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
