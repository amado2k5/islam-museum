## Overview
Musa ibn Talha ibn 'Ubaydallah, the imam and exemplar Abu 'Isa al-Qurashi al-Taymi of Medina, resident of Kufa; he narrated from his father and from 'Uthman, 'Ali, Abu Dharr, Abu Ayyub, 'A'isha, Abu Hurayra and others, and his son 'Imran, his grandson Sulayman ibn 'Isa and his nephews narrated from him. He died in 103 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3982}
