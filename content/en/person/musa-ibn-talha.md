## Overview
Musa ibn Talha, a transmitter from the family of Talha ibn Ubayd Allah. Al-Baladhuri mentions him in the report about land grants in Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}

## In al-Baladhuri
He relates that Uthman ibn Affan was the first to grant land in Iraq. He also relates that Ibn Mas'ud and Sa'd used to give their land for a third or a quarter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}
