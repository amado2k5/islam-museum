## Overview
Al-Mundhiri, the Hanbali muhaddith of Nablus then Damascus; he travelled for hadith through the amsar — hearing Ibn al-Hasri at Mecca, al-Busiri, al-Artahi and the hafiz 'Abd al-Ghani in Egypt, al-Mubarak b. Kulayb and Ibn al-Jawzi at Baghdad, Abu'l-Makarim al-Labban at Isfahan, 'Abd al-Mun'im al-Farawi and al-Mu'ayyad al-Tusi in Khurasan, Abu Sa'd al-Saffar at Nishapur, and the hafiz 'Abd al-Qadir al-Ruhawi at Harran where he attached himself for a time. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 203-204}

## His condition
'Umar b. al-Hajib called him "a righteous servant, a man of wonders, of manliness amid utter poverty"; al-Diya', al-Mundhiri, al-Birzali and the qadi Sulayman b. Hamza transmitted from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 203-204}
