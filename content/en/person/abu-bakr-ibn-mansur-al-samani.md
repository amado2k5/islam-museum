## Overview
Abu Bakr Ibn Mansur al-Sam'ani, the hafiz of Marw, father of the hafiz Abu Sa'd al-Sam'ani; brilliant in hadith and its knowledge, fiqh and its subtleties — a Shafi'i — adab and its arts, history, genealogy and preaching. He travelled, heard at Baghdad, Nishapur, Isfahan, Kufa and the Hijaz, dictated much and surpassed his peers. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 509}

## Praise
'Abd al-Ghafir called him "the imam, son of the imam, son of the imam, and father of the imam." He died in 510 at forty-three. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 509}
