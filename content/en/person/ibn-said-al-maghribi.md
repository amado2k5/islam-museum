## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Musa ibn Said al-Maghribi, author of *al-Maghrib fi Hula al-Maghrib* (The West in the Ornaments of the West) and *al-Mashriq fi Hula al-Mashriq* (The East in the Ornaments of the East). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.333-335}

## Birth
He was born on Tuesday 22 Ramadan 610 AH, into a house of learning, letters and vizierate, in the fortress of Abd al-Salam. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.335}

## Travel and licence
He travelled to the East, and gave his book *al-Maghrib* as a licence to the judge Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn al-Tifashi and to Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Khatlakh al-Farisi al-Armawi. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.333-335}
