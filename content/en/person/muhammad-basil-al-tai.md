## Summary
Muhammad Basil al-Tai is an Iraqi theoretical physicist based in Jordan, noted for attempting a rigorous bridge between quantum physics and Islamic kalam. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-basil-al-tai}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Iraq in 1952, he studied theoretical physics to advanced degrees and worked as a university professor in Iraq, Jordan and other countries. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-basil-al-tai}

## Career and works
He has published research in quantum field theory in curved spacetime and quantum cosmology. In intellectual work he authored Daqiq al-Kalam, presenting doctrinal questions through the tools of modern physics, and studies on crescent sighting and the Islamic astronomical calendar. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-basil-al-tai}

## Method and contribution
He is one of the few who write on kalam questions in mathematical and physical language, treating problems like the indivisible particle and time in quantum terms. His thesis is discussed in contemporary kalam circles with acceptance and reservation alike; the museum attributes the positions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-basil-al-tai}
