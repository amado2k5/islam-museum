## Summary

The Amir Abu Numay Muhammad ibn Barakat II ibn Muhammad ibn Barakat ibn Hasan ibn Ajlan al-Hasani, Amir of Mecca. He was born in 910 AH and remained Amir of Mecca for many years. He shared rule with his father before the latter's death and then ruled alone after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 424-425}.

## Command and removal

He was removed from office a year after the reign of Selim began. His cousin Idris shared power with him, and then he returned to the amirate of Mecca until he died on the day of Ashura. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 425}.

## His description in al-Nur al-Safir

The author of al-Nur al-Safir said: "I have not seen in Mecca an amir more eloquent than him, nor more skilled, nor more excellent in eloquence, nor finer in composing verse, nor more lasting in prose, nor more tender of heart, nor of lower repute." He was distinguished by many good qualities. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 425}.
