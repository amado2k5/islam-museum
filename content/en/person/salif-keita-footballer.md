# Salif Keïta

## Summary

Salif Keïta — full name Salif Keïta Traoré — was a Malian footballer, born in Bamako in 1946 and died there in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-en} A striker nicknamed the "Black Panther", he became an icon at Saint-Étienne, won the first African Footballer of the Year award in 1970, and played for Marseille, Valencia and Sporting CP. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-en} He is not the musician Salif Keita (born 1949). {cite:wikidata-salif-keita-footballer} No source consulted makes any statement of his religion, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-en}{cite:wikidata-salif-keita-footballer}

## Career

Keïta began in Mali with Real Bamako, winning three Malian Cups, and Stade Malien, reaching the African Cup of Champions Clubs final with both. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-fr} At 23 he joined Saint-Étienne, where he won three French league titles and two French Cups and became one of the club's defining players. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-fr} He later played for Marseille, Valencia and Sporting CP — winning a Portuguese Cup with the last — and finished his career in the United States with the New England Tea Men. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-fr} In 1970 he became the first player to receive the African Footballer of the Year award. {cite:wikipedia-salif-keita-footballer-en}
