## Overview
Rifa'a ibn Shaddad al-Bajali, the fifth of the leaders of the Tawwabun, to whom the Shi'a in Kufa turned in 65 AH, and who camped at al-Nukhayla, as al-Mas'udi reports {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.87}.
