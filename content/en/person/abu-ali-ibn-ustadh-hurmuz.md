## Overview
Al-Hasan b. Abi Ja'far Ustadh Hurmuz, Abu 'Ali, surnamed 'Amid al-Juyush; born 350, his father one of 'Adud al-Dawla's chamberlains — he served Samsam al-Dawla and Baha' al-Dawla. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.220,252}

## Regent of Iraq
Baha' al-Dawla gave him the regency of Iraq; he entered Baghdad in 392 with fitnas ablaze and the 'ayyarun ascendant, held the city with firmness and severity, barred Sunnis and Shi'is alike from displaying madhhabs in the seasons, exiled the Shi'i jurist Ibn al-Mu'allim — until affairs submitted to him and the fitnas quieted. He remained regent of Iraq until his death in Dhu'l-Qa'da 401. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.220,252}
