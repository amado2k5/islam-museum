## Summary
Ahmed Kalaja (born 16 April 1977 in Tirana) is an Albanian imam and public figure, imam-khatib of the Dine Hoxha Mosque in Tirana and one of his country's most followed preachers on the platforms: his Facebook page counted 326,000 followers and his Instagram 248,000 in October 2024, per the peer-reviewed Context study. {cite:context-cupi-2025}

## Early life and education
Kalaja was born in Tirana, and his path to religion began in high school; he studied Islam for over ten years in a Tirana madrassa, then in Damascus from 1993 to 1997, and completed his higher education at the Islamic University of Medina in Islamic law. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru} After returning in August 2001 he devoted some twenty years to preaching across Albania and in the diaspora — courses, camps and translation among the work. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru} He later undertook postgraduate study in the Department of Cultural Anthropology at the University of East Anglia, defending in 2017 a dissertation on "The role of Muslim clergy in interreligious harmony and tolerance in Albania, 1912–1945". {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru}

## Beginnings and career
Kalaja is imam and lecturer-khatib of the Dine Hoxha Mosque, one of Tirana's largest mosques, a member of the Tirana Council of Muftis for over fifteen years, and formerly a member of the Theological Council (the highest religious decision body) of the Muslim Community of Albania; he appears from time to time on television channels. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru} The Russian Wikipedia article introduces him as an imam and blogger with hundreds of thousands of followers on Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru}

## Positions and initiatives
In Kosovo's public sphere Kalaja became known for a petition he initiated calling on the member states of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation to recognise Kosovo's independence; 52,000 citizens signed it within two weeks. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru} In April 2021 the Dine Hoxha Mosque was attacked and five worshippers were wounded with a knife; Kalaja expressed the hope that the attack on the congregation had not been a terrorist act. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-kalaja-ru} These events are recorded as the sources state them; they are not the museum's verdict.

## Impact and audience
The Context study, reading the platform pages in October 2024, recorded 326,000 followers on Facebook, 248,000 on Instagram and 105,000 subscribers on YouTube. {cite:context-cupi-2025} The figures are dated platform counts, not facts the museum measures itself.
