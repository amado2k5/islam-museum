## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Utba ibn Abi Sufyan prayed over [[person/muawiya-ibn-yazid|Mu'awiya ibn Yazid]] so that the affair would be his after him. When he said the second takbir, he was stabbed and fell dead before the prayer was finished. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.70}
