## Summary
Hynd Bouhia is a Moroccan economist and financier whose career has moved between the World Bank, the office of the Moroccan prime minister and the Casablanca Stock Exchange — the first woman to head it — and into sustainable finance. {cite:wikipedia-hynd-bouhia}

## Formation
Born in 1973 in Casablanca, Bouhia studied engineering in Morocco and economics at Harvard, where she earned a doctorate. She worked at the World Bank in Washington on development operations before returning to Morocco, where she served as economic adviser in the prime minister's office. {cite:wikipedia-hynd-bouhia}

## Casablanca Stock Exchange
In the late 2000s she was appointed head of the Bourse de Casablanca — the first woman to run Morocco's stock exchange — steering it through the global financial crisis and the modernisation of its listing and governance rules. {cite:muslim500-hynd-bouhia}

## Sustainable finance and writing
In subsequent years she moved into sustainable and green finance and economic advisory work, and published a memoir-manifesto encouraging women to claim decision-making roles. The Muslim 500 profiles her as one of the Arab world's most accomplished financial executives and a pioneer in bringing environmental, social and governance questions into Moroccan finance. {cite:muslim500-hynd-bouhia}
