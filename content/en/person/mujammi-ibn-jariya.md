## Summary
One of the twelve men who built the mosque of Dirar per Ibn Ishaq; his lineage: son of Jariya ibn Amir. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In the mosque of Dirar
Ibn Ishaq names him in the list of those who built the mosque the Prophet ordered demolished on his return from Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} He is one of the two sons of Jariya whom Ibn Ishaq names among the twelve. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq mentions nothing here of the man beyond his name and descent among the twelve.
- The book's description of the group as "hypocrites" is not attached here to this man in particular; the verse's descent covers them all together (p. 174).
