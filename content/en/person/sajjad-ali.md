Sajjad Ali (born 1966) is a Pakistani semi-classical, pop and rock singer, poet, actor, and film director and producer. The Urdu biography gives his full birth date as 24 August 1966 and his birthplace as Lahore, while the English biography gives the year only and Karachi as his city; the two readings are preserved in his record.{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-ur}{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-en}{cite:wikidata-sajjad-ali} He trained in classical music under his uncle Tassaduq Hussain and first performed on Pakistan Television as a child.{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-ur}

His album Babia (1993) made his name, followed by songs and albums such as "Chief Saab" and "Sohni Lagdi" that remain popular; he also acted in and directed films, and his light, distinctive ghazal singing gave the form a new colour.{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-ur}{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-en} He sang "Din Pareshan Hai" for Shoaib Mansoor's film Bol (2011), and he is a recipient of the Sitara-e-Imtiaz.{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-ur}{cite:wikipedia-sajjad-ali-en}
