## Overview
Uzra ibn Qays ibn Ghazziyya al-Bajali, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in Futuh al-Buldan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.423}.

## In Hulwan
al-Baladhuri reports that Jarir ibn Abd Allah made him his deputy over Hulwan when he went toward al-Dinawar and conquered Qarmasin. Then he appointed him over it again when he marched to Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the year 19 AH {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.423}.

He may be the same person as [[person/uzra-ibn-qays|Uzra ibn Qays]]. This needs an additional source.
