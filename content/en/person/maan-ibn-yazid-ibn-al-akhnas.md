## Summary
Ma'n ibn Yazid ibn al-Akhnas al-Sulami, Abu Yazid, was a Companion from the clan of Banu Malik ibn Khafaf. He narrated from the Prophet, and Abu al-Juwayriya al-Jarmi, Suhayl ibn Dharr and Utba ibn Rafi' narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6455}

## Career
He held a standing with Umar ibn al-Khattab, fought at Badr and at the conquest of Damascus. He lived in Kufa, then entered Egypt and settled in Syria, and fought at Siffin with Mu'awiya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6455}

## Date of death
The encyclopedia gives his death as 54 AH, marked with a question mark. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6455}
