## Overview
Al-Khayzuran, mother of the caliphs. Al-Suyuti says that no woman is known to have borne two caliphs except her: she was the mother of al-Hadi and al-Rashid. He adds that in the time of her son al-Hadi she ruled autocratically over the great affairs, and it is said she planned his killing when he rebuked her for the processions at her door. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 451-453}

## Her works and reports
Al-Baladhuri says that she ordered the digging of the canal known as Mahdud, which she called al-Rayyan. Her agent divided it into sections, and he appointed people to dig each section, so it was called Mahdud. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Mas'udi says that she made into a mosque the house in Mecca in which the Prophet was born. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 571} He also relates the story of the entry of Mazna, the wife of Marwan ibn Muhammad, into her house known as Ashnas, and that she treated her well. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 303-305}

## In Kahhala's A'lam al-Nisa'
[[person/umar-rida-kahhala|Kahhala]] counts her among the women of politics and power, saying she played a great role in her son al-Hadi's caliphate, sharing in affairs of state, so that processions never ceased at her door. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.399} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.400} Citing al-Tabari, he relates that al-Hadi then forbade her interference and threatened any commander or courtier seen at her door, so she withdrew and did not speak to him until he was dying; he also gives the story of a poisoned dish of rice al-Hadi is said to have sent her. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.401} Kahhala then states that she had her slave-girls poison him — his own reading of a disputed report — and quotes her words on the night of his death: we used to say that on this night a caliph would die, a caliph would rule and a caliph would be born — and Musa died, Harun ruled and al-Ma'mun was born. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.402} He records her revenue as 160 million dirhams, that she had the al-Mahdud canal dug near al-Anbar and named it al-Rayyan, and that on pilgrimage Abu Dulama asked her for a slave-girl to keep him company, at which she laughed and promised. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p.402}
