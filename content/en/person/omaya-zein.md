# Omaya Zein

## Summary
Omaya Zein is a modest-fashion and beauty creator. Her YouTube channel (@omayazein) built a large audience with hijab and turban tutorials alongside makeup tutorials and beauty vlogs. {cite:youtube-omayazein}

## Channel and family life
Her turban tutorial has been viewed millions of times, and she has also published hijab tutorials in Spanish, including summer styles and a Spanish-language tutorial series. In recent years her channel has centred on family vlogs with her husband and on her fertility journey, which she documented through treatment to the birth of her son Adam, alongside Ramadan and everyday-life vlogs. {cite:youtube-omayazein}
