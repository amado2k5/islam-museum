## Overview
Son of the imam al-Hadi, who accompanied him to Najran in 294 AH; sent to turn Banu Rabi'a away from Raha, he brought them back to his father.

## Detail
The book says that Ahmad ibn Yahya was with his father on the march to Najran with a group of his cousins, and that al-Hadi sent him to Raha in Sha'ban 294 AH; on arrival he found the two sides in combat, turned Banu Rabi'a and their companions away, and brought them to his father. These pages give no dates of birth or death for him.
