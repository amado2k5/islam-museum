## Overview
He is Ali ibn al-Abbas, a man of the line of al-Hasan ibn Ali, one of those living in Tabaristan in the narrator's words, whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put over the mountain after climbing [[place/bayt-dhukhar|Bayt Dhukhar]] and gathering its people in Safar 288 AH {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.211}. The narrator names no particular mountain, and the context points, probably, to Bayt Dhukhar. His descent is given as the book gives it and the museum adds nothing to it. See [[event/al-hadi-sends-governors-from-sanaa-and-lifts-the-levies-at-shibam-and-bayt-dhukhar-288|al-Hadi sends governors and lifts the levies]].
