## Overview
Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Sa'd Allah ibn Jama'a, a Shafi'i jurist, hadith scholar and grammarian, one of the leading scholars of Cairo and Damascus in his age. He was born in 639 AH (1241 CE) and died in 733 AH (1332 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.392}

## What is reported of him
The source says that he combined jurisprudence, hadith and grammar. The details of his teachers, his works and his posts need an additional source.

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Sa'd Allah Ibn Jama'a al-Kinani of Hama, the Shafi'i: born at Hama in 639, he taught at the Qaymariyya, served as judge of Jerusalem, then chief judge of Egypt from Ramadan 690, then judge of Damascus in 693 with the preachership and the office of shaykh al-shuyukh, and was later restored to the judgeship of Egypt. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/281} He was dismissed in 709, restored in Safar 710, and relieved at his own request in Jumada al-Ula 727 after going blind. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/281} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/282} He died in Jumada al-Akhira 733, aged over ninety-four, and was buried in the Qarafa near al-Shafi'i. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/283}
