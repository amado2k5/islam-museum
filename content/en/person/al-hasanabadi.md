## Overview
Al-Hasanabadhi, whom Ibn al-Nadim saw and visited among his companions; he lived near Bayn al-Qasrayn and was elegant in craft and marvellous in song, in expression and speech and what he would recite. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 448}
