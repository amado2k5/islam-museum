## Overview
Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn al-Mu'tazz ibn Muhammad ibn al-Mustaghfir al-Nasafi, Abu al-Abbas, is described by al-Zirikli as a jurist who also worked on history, and as one of the men of hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 120}. He was the preacher of Nasaf, in the lands beyond the river, and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 120}. The entry gives 350 and 432 AH (961 and 1041 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 120}.

## His works
Among his books are "al-Da'awat" on hadith; "al-Tamhid fi al-tajwid", in manuscript in the Chester Beatty library; "Fada'il al-Qur'an"; "al-Shama'il wa al-dala'il wa ma'rifat al-sahaba al-awa'il"; "al-Musalsalat" on hadith; "Tarikh Kish"; "Tarikh Nasaf"; and "al-Ziyadat", what he added to the book on al-mukhtalif wa al-mu'talif by Abd al-Ghani ibn Sa'id; the entry adds that there are others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 120}.

## What the hadith scholars held against him
Al-Zirikli reports that the men of hadith hold against him that he related fabricated reports without distinguishing them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 120}.
