## Summary
Alaeddin Pasha, son of Osman and brother of Sultan Orhan — a scholar of the shari'a and Islamic letters in al-Sallabi's account, and the originator of the 'new army' idea: a standing corps of captives raised on Islamic training, which Orhan adopted and implemented {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 54-55}.

The book credits the brothers' joint ordering of the young state — its judiciary and the continuing education of its youth — among the reasons the Ottomans could rise again from the ruins of Ankara {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 72}.
