## Overview
Chaghri Beg ibn Seljuk, brother of [[person/tughril-beg|Tughril Beg]] and lord of Khurasan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.644}

## Peace with the Ghaznavids
Al-Suyuti says that a peace was made between him and Sultan Ibrahim ibn Mas'ud ibn Mahmud ibn Sabuktakin, lord of Ghazna, in 451 AH, after many wars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.644}

## His death
He died in the same year, and in his place his son [[person/alp-arslan|Alp Arslan]] was set up. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.644}
