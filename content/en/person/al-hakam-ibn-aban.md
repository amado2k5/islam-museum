## Summary
al-Hakam ibn Aban was diligent in his leadership, and among those who swim he glorified God. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 496, sequence 497, on pages 140 and 141. The scan of the notice breaks off at the foot of page 141. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/141}

## Sleeping in the sea
Ishaq ibn al-Dayf relates from elders of the people of Awf that al-Hakam ibn Aban prayed through the night, and when sleep overcame him he cast himself into the sea, saying: I glorify God with the fish. A word describing his position is unclear in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/141}
