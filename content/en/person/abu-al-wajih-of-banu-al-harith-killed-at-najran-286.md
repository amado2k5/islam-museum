## Overview
Abu al-Wajih is the kunya of a man of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]], one of their men and chiefs, "a chief among their chiefs known for courage and manliness" in the narrator's words. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.166} He caught a low wall while trying to leap into the mosque, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] speared and killed him, and his head was sent to his people, who fled. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.166} The book gives him no name or lineage, and he is not [[person/abu-al-wajih-ibn-musa-al-khushaymi|Abu al-Wajih ibn Musa al-Khushaymi]], whom al-Hadi put out of Najran alive to be jailed at al-Ghayl; the kunya is common. The details are in [[event/al-hadi-kills-abu-al-wajih-and-takes-al-hajar-286]].
