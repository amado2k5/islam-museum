## Summary
Al-Zaki al-Barzali, the travelling hafiz, muhaddith and benefactor of Syria; he heard in the Hijaz, Egypt, Syria, Iraq, Isfahan, Khurasan and the Jazira — much, gathered, and retained. He began his quest in 502; his earliest shaykhs were 'Ayn al-Shams al-Thaqafiyya and Mansur al-Farawi. He dwelt long at Damascus's Fulus Mosque, set out for Aleppo, and death overtook him at Hama; he is the father of Shaykh 'Ilm al-Din al-Barzali. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 190}
