## Overview
Musaylima ibn Habib, chief of Banu Hanifa at al-Yamama — he claimed prophethood parallel to Muhammad's and wrote to him proposing to divide it, then led his people in the bloodiest of the Ridda battles, in which he was killed.

## Life
Banu Hanifa were the greatest of al-Yamama's tribes, and Musaylima claimed prophethood among them while the Prophet was alive. The sources preserve his letter "from Musaylima the messenger of God to Muhammad the messenger of God" proposing that half the affair be his — and the Prophet's reply to him {cite:tabari-donner-1993} {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

When the Prophet died and Abu Bakr sent [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]], the two armies met at [[event/battle-of-yamama|Aqraba' in al-Yamama]] in 632 CE — a battle that ended with Musaylima's killing; the account ascribes the blow to the spear of [[person/wahshi-ibn-harb|Wahshi]]. So many memorisers of the Quran fell that the battle was counted a cause for hastening its compilation {cite:tabari-donner-1993}.
