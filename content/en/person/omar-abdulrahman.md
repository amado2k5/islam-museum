# Omar Abdulrahman

## Summary

Omar Abdulrahman Ahmed Al Raaki Al Amoudi is an Emirati former attacking midfielder born in Riyadh on 20 September 1991 to Yemeni parents, known by the nickname Amoory (عموري). He was the Asian Footballer of the Year in 2016 and won 74 caps for the United Arab Emirates. {cite:wikipedia-omar-abdulrahman-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-abdulrahman-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-abdulrahman}

## Career

Scouted playing football in Riyadh, he joined Al Ain's academy after the club arranged Emirati passports for his whole family, and he spent eleven seasons in Al Ain's first team, reaching the 2016 AFC Champions League final; after a loan season at Al-Hilal he played for Al Jazira, Shabab Al-Ahli and Al Wasl. His brothers Mohammed and Khaled are also professional footballers. {cite:wikipedia-omar-abdulrahman-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-abdulrahman-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-abdulrahman}
