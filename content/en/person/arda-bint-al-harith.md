## Overview
Arda daughter of al-Harith ibn Kalada, the wife of [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan]]. She used to urge people to fight on the day of the city of the Euphrates, which is part of the account of [[event/conquest-of-ubulla|the conquest of Ubulla]]. The report says she said verses on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
