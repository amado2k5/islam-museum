## Overview
Muhyi al-Din Ahmad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Nahhas al-Dimashqi al-Shafi'i. Ibn al-Imad in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* says that he wrote a substantial book on holy war titled *Masari' al-Ushshaq* (The Downfall of Lovers), and abridged it himself. He also wrote *Tanbih al-Ghafilin 'an A'mal al-Jahilin* (Warning the Heedless against the Deeds of the Ignorant), on events and innovations. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 113-114}

## His death
He was killed at [[place/dimyat|Damietta]] when the Franks attacked it. He went out with a group of its people, a great battle took place, and he was killed in it in 814 AH. The conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 113-114}
