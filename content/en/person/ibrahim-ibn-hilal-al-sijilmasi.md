## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Hilal ibn Ali, Abu Ishaq, a Sanhaji by descent, al-Falali al-Sijilmasi, was a Maliki jurist, the mufti and scholar of Sijilmasa in the far Maghrib, and he died there. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 817 AH, 1414 CE, and his death as 903 AH, 1497 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 78}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists: al-Nawazil, printed in two parts and arranged by Ali ibn Ahmad al-Jazuli; al-Durr al-nathir ala ajwibat Abi al-Hasan al-Saghir, printed; al-Ajwiba, printed, in jurisprudence; a commentary on al-Bukhari in four volumes; a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Khalil; a Fihrist of 27 leaves, a manuscript in Rabat under 271 K; and an abridgement of al-Dibaj al-Mudhahhab of Ibn Farhun, a manuscript in the Institute of Manuscripts from the Rabat library {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 78}.
