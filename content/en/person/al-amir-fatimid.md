## Overview
Al-Amir bi-Ahkam Allah Mansur ibn al-Musta'li, the tenth of the rulers of the Fatimid state in al-Suyuti's phrasing. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 658}

## Accession
He was installed after his father al-Musta'li in 495 AH, when he was a child of five. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 658}

## His death
He was killed in 524 AH without an heir. His cousin al-Hafiz Abd al-Majid succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 667}
