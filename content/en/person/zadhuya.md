## Overview
Zadhuya, marzban of Sarakhs. He asked Abd Allah ibn Khazim for peace on the safe conduct of a hundred men, and that the women be handed over to him. His daughter fell to the share of Ibn Khazim, who took her as a concubine and named her Maysha' {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

In another version Ibn Khazim named the hundred to him, and Zadhuya did not name himself, so he killed him and entered Sarakhs by force {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
