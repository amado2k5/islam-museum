Sa'd ibn Zayd ibn Wadi'a al-Ashhali al-Awsi, an eminent Badri Companion, a knight among the Ansar's nobles and chiefs of Banu Abd al-Ashhal. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He witnessed the Aqaba pledge, Badr, Uhud, the Trench and all the battles with the Messenger of God. In Ramadan of year eight of the hijra the Prophet chose him as commander of a detachment of twenty horsemen to demolish the idol of Manat at al-Mushallal on the sea coast; he demolished it and finished off its custodians, and lived honoured under Islam's care. {cite:zad-al-maad}
