## Summary
Muhammad ibn al-Yunini, son of the pious shaykh Asad al-Sham 'Abd Allah al-Yunini, was born in 602, memorized the Qur'an, studied jurisprudence and letters, and surpassed his peers in learning and piety. He settled at Baalbek until his father died, then succeeded him in teaching and preaching. He died in Rajab 651 and was buried at Bab al-Faradis. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 261}
