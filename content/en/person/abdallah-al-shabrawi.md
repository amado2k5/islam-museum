## Overview
Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Amir al-Shabrawi was an Egyptian jurist who also composed verse, and he held the headship of al-Azhar. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1091 AH, 1680 CE, and his death as 1171 AH, 1758 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Sharh al-Sadr fi Ghazwat Badr, printed; a collection of verse he called Manaih al-Altaf fi Madaih al-Ashraf, printed; Unwan al-Bayan, counsels and maxims, printed; and al-Ithaf bi-Hubb al-Ashraf, printed, of which copies in his own hand are in the Rabat library among the Kattani books. A Thabat of his, in manuscript, is also in the Rabat library, in collection 1282 Kattani {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.
