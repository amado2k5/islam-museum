## Overview
Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Salih ibn Khayran, a jurist who died in 320 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 58}

He was offered the judgeship and did not accept it; one of the viziers of al-Muqtadir had a guard set at his house so that he would take it. Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari relates that he rebuked the judge Ibn Surayj for accepting the judgeship. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 58}
