## Overview
'Uthman ibn Sa'id ibn 'Adi al-Misri, known as Warsh, was one of the great Qur'an readers; his nickname overtook his name for his extreme fairness of skin; his origin was Qayrawan and he was born and died in Egypt in 197 AH (812 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.205}
