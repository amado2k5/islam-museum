## Overview
Harb ibn Salm ibn Ziyad, of the Umayyad period, son of [[person/sallam-ibn-ziyad|Salm ibn Ziyad]]. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that a river was named after him, and that he disputed it with Abd al-Ala ibn Abd Allah ibn Amir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account of the river
When the judgement went in favour of Abd al-Ala, Harb came to him and said that he regretted the dispute and that the river was his. Abd al-Ala returned the river to him. Then the clients of Abd al-Ala said to Harb that he had come only after the judgement had gone in favour of their master. Harb answered that he would never go back on what he had given him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
