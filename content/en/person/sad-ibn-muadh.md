## Summary
Sa'd ibn Mu'adh ibn al-Nu'man al-Awsi, chief of the Ansar and bearer of their banner on the day of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.205}.

## His role at Badr
When the Prophet held the consultative council at the valley of Dhu Afran, the leaders of the Muhajirun spoke. Then he wished to hear the view of the Ansar, since they were the majority of the army and the terms of the Aqaba pledge did not oblige them to fight outside their homes. Sa'd grasped this and answered on their behalf: they had believed and given their pledge to hear and obey, and if the Prophet were to plunge with them into the sea, they would plunge in with him. He said: "Proceed with us in God's blessing" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.209}. In another account, he said that he was speaking about the Ansar and answering for them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.209}.

It was he who proposed building the arbour on a rise to the north-east of the battlefield as the command post, and young men of the Ansar took turns guarding it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.212}.

## After Badr
Sa'd later passed judgement on [[group/banu-qurayza|Banu Qurayza]] after their siege, and died from an arrow that struck him in that affair {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.

## An umra in Mecca (*The Sealed Nectar*, p. 195)
He set out for umra and stayed with [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf]] in Mecca. Abu Jahl met him and reproached him for giving shelter to the *sabi'a*, those who had turned away from their ancestral religion. Sa'd replied that his road runs past the people of Medina ([[event/quraysh-letter-to-ibn-ubayy|a threat from Quraysh]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.195}.

## The Ansar's banner at Badr (*The Sealed Nectar*, pp. 205, 209)
The Prophet put him in charge of the Ansar contingent. When the Prophet consulted him, he said: "We have believed in you and affirmed you, so proceed with what you intend. If you were to lead us across this sea, we would plunge into it with you" ([[event/badr-council|the council of Badr]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.205} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.209}.

## For Sprouts
Sa'd ibn Mu'adh was the chief of the Ansar and their banner-bearer at Badr. At the Wadi Dhi Afran council he answered for the Ansar that they had pledged hearing and obedience and would follow the Prophet through the sea — and it was he who proposed the command shelter on the battlefield.
