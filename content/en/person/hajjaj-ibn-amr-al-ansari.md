## Summary
Hajjaj ibn Amr ibn Ghaziyya al-Ansari al-Mazini al-Madani, a Companion, though some count him among the Successors. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2057}

## Works
The compilers of the Sunan transmitted a report from him about the pilgrimage, and he fought at Siffin with Ali. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2057}
