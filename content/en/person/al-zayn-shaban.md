## Al-Zayn Shaʿban ibn Dawud al-Misri

Al-Zayn Shaʿban ibn Muhammad ibn Dawud al-Misri, an imam in literature and its branches. He died in 828 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 192}.
