## Overview
Faraj ibn Sulaym, a eunuch and one of the leading servants and agents of the Abbasid state. Al-Baladhuri reports that al-Rashid put him in charge of building [[event/founding-of-tarsus|Tarsus]]. Abu Sulaym brought him to the City of Peace, and he fixed the two boundary marks and measured the land between the river and the river, four thousand plots, each twenty cubits long by the same width. He then allotted the plots to the people of Tarsus. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
