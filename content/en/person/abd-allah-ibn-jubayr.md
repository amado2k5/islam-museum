## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-jubayr|Abd Allah ibn Jubayr]] of the Aws. He attended the Aqaba with the seventy, and attended Badr and Uhud, and was martyred at Uhud as commander of the archers. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.239}

## Command of the archers at Uhud
The Prophet appointed him over the archers at Uhud, who were fifty men, and charged them to stand on the two hills, a ridge of the mountain of Uhud. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.239} He was killed that day and mutilated, and Ikrima ibn Abi Jahl killed him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.239}

When the archers saw the Muslims gathering the spoils, Ibn Jubayr reminded them of the Prophet's orders and said: "Have you forgotten what the Messenger of God said to you?" Most of them paid no attention, and about forty of them left their positions on the mountain, so that only nine of his companions were left with Ibn Jubayr, who held their positions. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264} Khalid ibn al-Walid seized the chance, turned until he reached the rear of the Muslim army, and destroyed Ibn Jubayr and his companions. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}

## For young visitors
Abd Allah ibn Jubayr was the commander of the archers at Uhud, was martyred that day, and was among the seventy who attended the Aqaba.

## Notes for researchers
- Al-Dhahabi's remark on his order to the archers to hold firm is summarised here. The detail of the archers leaving their positions is taken from al-Mubarakfuri's "Sealed Nectar" (pp. 255 and 264), and has not yet been compared with Ibn Hisham's biography.
