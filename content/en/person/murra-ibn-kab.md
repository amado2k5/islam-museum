## Summary
Father of Kilab, Taym and Yalaq; a link in the agreed lineage between Ka'b and Kilab — from Taym ibn Murra descends the Banu Taym clan of Abu Bakr and Talha ibn Ubayd Allah {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:isaba}.

## His place in the lineage
In Murra's progeny God joined the Prophet's house and the Siddiq's house: Banu Kilab ibn Murra hold the Prophet through Qusayy, and Banu Taym ibn Murra hold Abu Bakr {cite:isaba} {cite:ansab-al-ashraf}.

## For younger visitors
Murra ibn Ka'b is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
