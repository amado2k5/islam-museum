## Summary
A Companion of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; he emigrated to Abyssinia and arrived in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him first of two men of Banu Amir in the two ships, tracing him to Amr ibn Abd Shams. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
