## Overview
The chief qadi Sharaf al-Din Abu al-Qasim Hibat Allah ibn the chief qadi Najm al-Din Abd al-Rahim, son of the qadi Shams al-Din Ibrahim, known as Ibn al-Barizi al-Shafi'i, was qadi of Hama and a prolific author, born in Ramadan 645 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.208}

He heard his father, grandfather, 'Izz al-Din al-Farawthi, Jamal al-Din Ibn Malik and others; he recited by the Seven readings, took fiqh from his father and nahw from Ibn Malik, worked through the sciences, gave fatwa, taught and wrote. He held the judgeship of Hama, went blind at the end of his life, and transmitted at Damascus and Hama — al-Barzali, al-Dhahabi and many others heard him; Ibn Tughril issued him a large mashikha and al-Barzali a juz'. Al-Dhahabi called him in his mu'jam 'shaykh of the scholars, last of the notables', and al-Subki said the headship of the school in Syria ended with him, sought from every quarter. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.208}
