## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abi Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Zafar al-Siqilli, surnamed Hujjat al-Din; one of the accomplished adibs and author of delightful works — among them the Sulwan al-Muta' fi 'Udwan al-Atba' composed for a certain commander in Sicily in 554, Khayr al-Bishar bi-Khayr al-Bashar, the large Manba' on Qur'an exegesis, Nasma' al-Abna', a hashiya on al-Hariri's Durrat al-Ghawwas, and two commentaries on the Maqamat, one long and one short; Shaykh Taj al-Din al-Kindi said of him after debating him at Hama: Taj al-Din knows more grammar than I, and I know more philology than he. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
