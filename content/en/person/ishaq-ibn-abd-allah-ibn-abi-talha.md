## Summary
Ishaq b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Talha al-Ansari was one of the younger Successors of [[place/medina|Medina]]; he met [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas b. Malik]], and Malik counted him among his most reliable teachers. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400-401}

## Kunya and family
He was known as Abu Najih — or, it is said, Abu Muhammad or Abu Yahya. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400} His father [[person/abd-allah-ibn-abi-talha-al-ansari|'Abd Allah b. Abi Talha]] was born in Medina in the Prophet's lifetime; Anas said he took the infant to the Prophet for *tahnik* in the morning and found him branding the camels of the alms with a branding-iron in his hand. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400} His grandfather [[person/abu-talha-al-ansari|Abu Talha Zayd b. Sahl]] was one of the senior Companions, whom Ibn 'Abd al-Barr treats in his book on the Companions. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400} His mother was Nubayta (as the printed text reads the name) bint Rifa'a b. Rafi' b. Malik b. al-'Ajlan al-Zuraqi al-Ansari. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400}

He had brothers — 'Amr, 'Umar, 'Abd Allah, Ya'qub and Isma'il, sons of 'Abd Allah b. Abi Talha — all of whom transmitted knowledge; Ishaq, Ibn 'Abd al-Barr says, was the highest, most learned and most reliable of them. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400}

## Students and standing
A number of imams transmitted from him, among them [[person/yahya-ibn-abi-kathir|Yahya b. Abi Kathir]], Malik, [[person/al-awzai-abd-al-rahman|al-Awza'i]], [[person/hammad-ibn-salama|Hammad b. Salama]] and Hammam b. Yahya. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400} Ibn 'Abd al-Barr calls him trustworthy and an authority in what he transmitted. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 400} Al-Waqidi said: Malik ranked no one above Ishaq b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Talha in hadith. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 401}

## Death
He died in Medina in 132 AH; others say 134. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 401}

## His reports in the Muwatta'
Malik has fifteen of his reports from the Prophet in the Muwatta': ten from Anas, two from Rafi' b. Ishaq, one from Zufar b. Sa'sa'a, one from Abu Murra and one from his wife. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 401} The first is Anas's report on Bayruha': Abu Talha owned more palm groves than any other Ansari in Medina, and Bayruha' was the dearest of his properties to him; when [[passage/quran-3-92|Al 'Imran 92]] was revealed he made it an endowment for God, and the Prophet advised him to give it to his nearest kin, so he divided it among his relatives and cousins. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 402}
