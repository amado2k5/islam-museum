## Qasim ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Shat

Qasim ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Shat, Abu Muhammad and Abu al-Qasim, of the Ansar, from Seville, a Maliki jurist and expert in inheritance shares, who also took part in some other sciences. He studied with Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn al-Rabi' and was licensed by Abu al-Qasim ibn al-Bara' and others, and transmitted from him Abu Zakariyya ibn al-Hudhayl and others. His works include Anwar al-Buruq, a critique of the problems of the Qawa'id and the Furuq, a work on the science of shares, and a treatise on the reward. The book gives his death as 723 AH and his birth as 643 AH in its heading. {C(5489,5490)}
