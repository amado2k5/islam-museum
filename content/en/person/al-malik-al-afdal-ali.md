## Overview
Al-Malik al-Afdal Nur al-Din Ali ibn Salah al-Din. Damascus was settled for him when his father died in 589 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}

Ibn al-Athir describes him as the nephew of [[person/al-malik-al-adil-ayyub|al-Malik al-Adil]], who took from him the kingdoms he held. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 393-394}
