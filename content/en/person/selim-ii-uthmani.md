## Overview
Sultan Selim II, son of [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Suleiman the Magnificent]] and his successor in rule. He came to the throne on the ninth of Rabi' al-Awwal in the year 974 AH {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 277-285}.

## His reign
The source says that he was not qualified to preserve his father's conquests, and that the vizier Muhammad Pasha Sokollu restored the state's prestige. In the year 975 AH he made a peace with Austria, in which Austria kept its possessions in Hungary {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 277-285}.

In his time Cyprus was conquered in the year 978 AH, the battle of Lepanto took place, and Tunis was returned to Ottoman influence in the year 981 AH {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 277-285}.

He died in the year 982 AH (1574 CE) {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi}.
