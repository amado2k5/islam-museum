## Summary
Muhammad Abduh Tuasikal (born 24 January 1984) is an Indonesian preacher (dai) active in writing and teaching, and the head of Pondok Pesantren Darush Sholihin at Panggang, Gunungkidul. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} He is an engineering graduate who came to preaching without formal religious schooling, and he founded the religious website Rumaysho.Com. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id}

## Early life and education
He was born in the city of Ambon on 24 January 1984. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} {cite:wikidata-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal} He received his primary and secondary education at general schools in Jayapura. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} The article states plainly that he has no formal religious education; alongside his university studies he studied Arabic grammar, studied at Ma'had Al-'Ilmi in Yogyakarta in 2004–2006, and spent six years studying under Ustadz Aris Munandar, working through usul and the writings of Ibn Taymiyya and Ibn Qayyim. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id}

He studied chemical engineering at Gadjah Mada University from 2002 to 2007. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} In 2010 he went to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, for a master's degree in chemical (polymer) engineering at King Saud University, which he completed in January 2013. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} In September 2020 he began doctoral studies at Yogyakarta State University and took his doctorate in education management in June 2024. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id}

## Work and teaching
He heads Pondok Pesantren Darush Sholihin at Panggang, Gunungkidul, a pesantren that serves as a place of Qur'an learning for children (TPA) and of religious study circles. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} He is known for managing the Islamic religious website Rumaysho.Com, which took its name from his eldest daughter, Rumaysho; by Similarweb's ranking, it was the fourth most popular religious website in Indonesia in November 2024. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} Alongside his teaching he writes Islamic books and serves as an adviser to the Indonesian Muslim Entrepreneurs Community (KPMI). {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id}

## Reach
His teaching is carried to a wide audience by Rumaysho TV, the YouTube channel of his pesantren and platform, of which he is the principal teacher. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-abduh-tuasikal-id} The channel counted 394,000 subscribers, 45,354,972 views and 12,641 videos by youtubers.me, crawled in November 2026. {cite:youtubers-me-rumaysho-tv} Audience figures are platform figures, recorded with their dates; they change constantly. {cite:youtubers-me-rumaysho-tv}
