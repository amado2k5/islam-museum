## Overview
Al-Fari'a al-Kharijiyya, sister of al-Walid ibn Tarif al-Shari. {cite:duha-islam p.956-957}

## Her lament for her brother
After her brother's death in 178 AH she mourned him in several odes, among them her well-known ode. Ahmad Amin compared her position with that of al-Khansa over her two brothers Sakhr and Mu'awiya. {cite:duha-islam p.956-957}

Ahmad Amin quotes a passage from them in his discussion of the literary heritage of the Kharijites. {cite:duha-islam p.956-957}
