## Overview
A man of the Layth whom [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-al-mulawwah|the raid of Ghalib ibn Abd Allah]] met at Qudayd, and they took him. He said that he had come wanting Islam, and had come out only to the Messenger of God. They said to him: "If you are a Muslim, a night's bond will not harm you; if you are otherwise, we will hold you fast." They bound him, left a black man over him, and then took him along with them on their return. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/258} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/259}

## Notes for researchers
- His story comes from the report of Jundab ibn Makith alone, and what happened to him afterwards is not mentioned by Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/259}
