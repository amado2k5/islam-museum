## Summary
Ibn Hammawayh, qadi of Syria — a Hanafi jurist who then became a Hanbali and was a mujtahid in the Sunnis' madhhab. Appointed qadi on his madhhab, he was asked: why not go out? When told Ja'far was made shaykh, he said: this is nothing but mockery — so be it, and they bound him to it. He judged for some months, then was dismissed. He related much and was an awe-inspiring, dignified shaykh. He died 28 Rabi' II. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 578}
