## Overview
Abu'l-Fath al-Karukhi 'Abd al-Malik b. 'Abdallah al-Harawi, of Karukh a town near Herat — the righteous man, transmitter of Tirmidhi's Jami'; scrupulous and trustworthy, he wrote a copy of the Jami' and endowed it, lived from copying, and transmitted at Baghdad and Mecca; he lived eighty-six years and died in Dhu'l-Hijja 548. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 628}
