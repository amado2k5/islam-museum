## Overview
Al-Muzaffar Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Maslama ibn al-Aftas, king of Badajoz in the western part of al-Andalus, of the Banu al-Aftas. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 478}

## His writings
He is the author of a work called "al-Muzaffari" in about fifty volumes. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 478} See [[work/al-muzaffari|al-Muzaffari]].

## Among the Taifa kings
Ibn Khaldun counts him among the great Taifa kings, and mentions wars between him and Ibn Dhi al-Nun and also with Ibn Abbad. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1001}
