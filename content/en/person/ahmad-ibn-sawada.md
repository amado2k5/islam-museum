## Overview
Ahmad b. Sawada, Abu'l-'Abbas, known as Khashish — of Kufa by origin, settled Baghdad and related from 'Ubayda b. Humayd, Zayd b. al-Hubab and others; Waki' the qadi and Qasim al-Mutarriz transmitted from him. Al-Daraqutni: his hadith is considered but not proof; al-Khatib: "I saw nothing sound in his hadith." {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 102}

## His turn
He recited for himself: "be busy with God's remembrance, withdrawn from all people"; the hammaz said to him, "you are bound to hear and obey, may God mend you — al-Mutawakkil has sent you ten thousand dirhams; take them — what ails you?" And the joy constricted him and he was silent, inwardly gladdened. He died in 258. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 102}
