## Overview
Yusuf Efendi Akhi-zade, an Ottoman scholar of jurisprudence, also known as Akhi Qalbi. He died at the beginning of the tenth century AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he died in 905 AH (1500 CE). It comes from one source and needs a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
