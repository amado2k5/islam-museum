## Summary
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Sa'dan was of Baghdadi origin. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 650 of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], among the gnostics whose days he says he reached. He describes him as eloquent and clear in speech, a leading figure in the sciences of the law who followed the school of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], and a clear voice on the knowledge of those devoted to practice and worship. He records that Ibn Abi Sa'dan lived for a time in [[place/tarsus|Tarsus]] and was sent as an envoy to the Byzantines because of his standing and his eloquence. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/377}

## Sayings
Abu al-Qasim al-Razi relates his saying that whoever acts on the knowledge of transmission inherits the knowledge of understanding, whoever acts on the knowledge of understanding inherits the knowledge of observance, and whoever acts on the knowledge of observance is guided to the way of truth. Muhammad ibn Ibrahim heard him say that one who hears with his ear repeats, one who hears with his heart takes heed, and one who acts on what he knows is guided and guides. He also said that the first good allotted to the self is the spirit, then knowledge to show it its right course, then intellect to direct both knowledge and the self. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/377}
