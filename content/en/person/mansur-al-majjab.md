## Overview
Mansur al-Majjab, whom Ibn al-Nadim himself saw among the conjurers who played sleight-of-hand; he died at a hundred and fifteen and used to say 'I performed before al-Mu'tamad'. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 434}
