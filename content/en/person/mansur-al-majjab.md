## Overview
Mansur al-Majjab, a man whom Ibn al-Nadim counts among those who perform sleight of hand. He died aged one hundred and fifteen. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.434}

Ibn al-Nadim relays his saying that he performed before al-Mu'tamid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.434}
