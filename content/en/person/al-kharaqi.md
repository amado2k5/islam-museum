## Summary
Umar ibn al-Husayn ibn Abdullah ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Qasim al-Kharaqi, author of the famous Mukhtasar in Ahmad's school {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}. The Mukhtasar was explained by Judge Abu Ya'la, his shaykh Ibn Hamid, and Muwaffaq al-Din al-Maqdisi in his book al-Mughni {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}. Abu Ishaq al-Barmaki said the number of al-Kharaqi's questions is two thousand three hundred {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}. He died in Damascus in 334 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}.
