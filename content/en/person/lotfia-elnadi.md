## Summary

Lotfia ElNadi (1907–2002) was a pioneer of Egyptian aviation, the first Egyptian, Arab and African woman to fly with an official licence, and an early symbol of women entering technical fields.{cite:wikipedia-lotfia-elnadi}

## Life

Born in Cairo, she secretly learned to fly at the Misr Airwork school (1932–1933) without her father's knowledge, and received her pilot's licence in 1933 at twenty-six.{cite:wikipedia-lotfia-elnadi}

## Achievement

She flew solo from Cairo to Alexandria in December 1933, joined the Cairo–Alexandria air race, appeared in the international press as a pioneer, and worked in the Egyptian aviation establishment.{cite:wikipedia-lotfia-elnadi}

## Standing

She became a symbol of defying norms in 1930s Egypt; Google honoured her with a Doodle in 2017, and she died in 2002 aged about 95.{cite:wikipedia-lotfia-elnadi}
