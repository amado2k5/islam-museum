## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah al-Tilimsani, Shams al-Din, known as al-Shab al-Zarif and also called Ibn al-'Afif, was a poet of delicate, well-received verse, the son of the poet 'Afif al-Din al-Tilimsani. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.149}

He was born in Cairo while his father lived as a Sufi in the Sa'id al-Su'ada' khanqah, served as keeper of the treasury in Damascus, and died there in 688 AH (1289 CE). He left a diwan of poetry and Maqamat al-'Ushshaq, a two-leaf epistle. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.149}
