## Overview
Abu Umama al-Sudayy ibn Ajlan al-Bahili, a Companion and one of the commanders of the raiding parties in Syria. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151}

## The Battle of al-Araba
Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan sent him against a Byzantine force at al-Araba in the land of Palestine, and he struck them and killed their great man. Abu Mikhnaf has it that six Byzantine commanders camped at al-Araba with three thousand men. He marched against them with a group of Muslims, defeated them and killed one of them. He then pursued them to al-Dabiya, defeated them there, and took good booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151}

The elders of Syria say that the Battle of al-Araba was the first of the Muslims' battles. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152} See [[event/battle-of-al-araba|the Battle of al-Araba]].
