## Summary
Asif Aziz is a British real-estate entrepreneur, founder of Criterion Capital — owner and developer of a multi-billion-dollar London property portfolio — and founder of the Aziz Foundation, a charity funding postgraduate scholarships and community work for British Muslims. {cite:wikipedia-asif-aziz}

## Formation and business
Born in Malawi in the 1960s to a family of Bangladeshi origin and raised in Britain, Aziz built his fortune in London property: through Criterion he assembled a portfolio concentrated in the West End — hotels, serviced apartments, leisure and the redevelopment of landmarks — becoming one of the city's most visible private landlords and one of Britain's wealthiest Muslim businessmen. {cite:wikipedia-asif-aziz}

## Philanthropy
In 2015 he endowed the Aziz Foundation, which funds master's scholarships for British Muslims, community projects and research on British Muslim life — among the largest dedicated grant programmes for the community in the country. {cite:muslim500-asif-aziz}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as a Muslim-owned property fortune turned to community endowment: the entrepreneur behind some of central London's most valuable real estate who channelled part of it into scholarships for the community his family belongs to. {cite:muslim500-asif-aziz}
