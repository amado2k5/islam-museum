## Overview
Nusayr, the father of [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]]. al-Baladhuri says that he was a client (mawla) of the Umayyads, and that according to one report he was among the captives of [[event/siege-of-ayn-al-tamr|Ayn al-Tamr]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Accounts of his origin
al-Baladhuri reports from Ibn al-Kalbi that he was an Arab of the Arasha of Bali, taken captive in the days of Abu Bakr from Mount al-Jalil in Syria. His name was Nasr, which was diminished to Nusayr, and one of the Banu Umayya freed him. He then returned to Syria, and Musa was born to him in a village called Kafr Mari. Musa was lame {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

It is also said that he and Abu Farwa were brothers among the captives of Ayn al-Tamr, and that their clientage was to the Banu Dabba {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
