## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Umar ibn Bayazid ibn al-Hajj Ahmad al-Baghdadi, born in Baghdad in 1030 AH (1621 CE) and died in 1093 AH (1682 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Literature and language
From his youth he had a passion for literature and for knowing and mastering the Arabic language, and he wrote «Khizanat al-adab». {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
