## Overview
Fihr ibn Malik ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana: nicknamed Quraysh, to whom the tribe is ascribed; an ancestor in the agreed-sound portion of the Prophet's lineage.

## Life
In the first part of the Prophet's genealogy whose soundness the biographers and genealogists agree on: Fihr ibn Malik ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana, nicknamed Quraysh, to whom Quraysh is ascribed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Quraysh divided into many tribes, the most famous being Jumah, Sahm, Adi, Makhzum, Taym, Zuhra and the clans of Qusayy ibn Kilab {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Fihr is the man after whom the tribe of Quraysh was named, among the Prophet's ancestors agreed upon by the genealogists.
