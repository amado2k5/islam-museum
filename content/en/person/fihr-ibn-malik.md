## Summary
Fihr ibn Malik ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana, nicknamed Quraysh, and the [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] tribe is ascribed to him. The Sealed Nectar says that Quraysh is of [[group/kinana|Kinana]]: they are the sons of Fihr ibn Malik ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 21} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/14-15}.

## In the genealogy
Fihr is among the branches of [[person/ilyas-ibn-mudar|Ilyas ibn Mudar]]. From Ilyas come Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma and the branches of Kinana ibn Khuzayma, and from Kinana come Quraysh, the sons of Fihr {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 21}. In the first part of the Prophet's lineage, which biographers and genealogists agree is sound, Fihr ibn Malik ibn al-Nadr ibn Kinana appears {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 48}.

## The branches of Quraysh
Quraysh divided into many tribes, the most famous of which are [[group/banu-jumah|Jumah]], [[group/banu-sahm|Sahm]], [[group/banu-adi|Adi]], [[group/banu-makhzum|Makhzum]], Taym and [[group/banu-zuhra|Zuhra]], and the branches of [[person/qusayy-ibn-kilab|Qusayy ibn Kilab]]: Abd al-Dar, Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza and Abd Manaf {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 21}. Abd Manaf had four factions: [[group/banu-abd-shams|Abd Shams]], Nawfal, al-Muttalib and [[group/banu-hashim|Hashim]]. The House of Hashim is the one from which God chose the Prophet {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 21}.

## For young visitors
Fihr is the man after whom the tribe of Quraysh is named, and he is among the Prophet's ancestors agreed upon by the genealogists.
