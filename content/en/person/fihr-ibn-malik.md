## Summary
Fihr ibn Malik, the 'Quraysh' to whom Quraysh of the hollows and the outskirts are confined; it is said he was called Quraysh because he 'quarashed' (crushed) a Yemeni enemy that meant to carry off Quraysh, defending his people and Khuza'a; his sons are Ghalib, Muharram and al-Harith {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## His place in the lineage
In the other famous explanation, qurash is the name of the sea creature that eats and is not eaten and rules the sea — a simile for the tribe Fihr gathered; both sayings are reported by the sira men on the meaning of 'Quraysh' {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## For younger visitors
Fihr ibn Malik is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
