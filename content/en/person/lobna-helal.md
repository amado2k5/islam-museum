## Summary
Lobna Helal was the first woman appointed to the board of the Central Bank of Egypt and served as one of its deputy governors, overseeing banking stability and payments reform through a turbulent economic decade. {cite:muslim500-lobna-helal} {cite:wikipedia-lobna-helal}

## Career
An economist by training, she rose through the central bank's research and banking-supervision departments, joined its board, and in January 2016 became deputy governor — among the most senior posts ever held by a woman in Arab central banking — serving until 2019. She has chaired and served on boards including Telecom Egypt and the Egyptian Banking Institute, where she is deputy chair. {cite:muslim500-lobna-helal} {cite:wikipedia-lobna-helal}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists her under Business as a trailblazer for women in Middle Eastern finance — a technocrat whose significance lies in the offices she broke into rather than in public advocacy. {cite:muslim500-lobna-helal}
