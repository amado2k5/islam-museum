## Overview
The amir Abu al-Hayja al-Samin was among the greatest and bravest of the Salahi amirs, according to Ibn al-Athir. [[person/kanz-al-dawla|Al-Kanz]] killed his brother, who was amir in his fief in Upper Egypt, so Abu al-Hayja marched against him with a group of amirs and troops sent by [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]]. He took the city of Tawd, then overtook al-Kanz and killed him and those with him in 570 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.64-65}
