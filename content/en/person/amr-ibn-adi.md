## Summary
Amr ibn Adi ibn Nasr al-Lakhmi was the first of the Lakhmid kings over [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]]. He governed it after the death of [[person/jadhima-al-abrash|Jadhima]], in the reign of Kisra Sabur ibn Ardashir {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 26} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/29-32}.

## His house
Nasr ibn Rabi'a, his grandfather, was the father of the Mundhir kings of al-Hira among the Lakhm, from the clans of [[group/kahlan|Kahlan]] that migrated from Yemen {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 17}. Lakhmid kings then succeeded one another over al-Hira until the time of Qubadh ibn Firuz {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 26}. See [[polity/lakhmid-kingdom|the Lakhmid kingdom of al-Hira]].

## For young visitors
Amr ibn Adi was the first king of the house of Lakhm in al-Hira. From his line, the Arab kings who ruled for Persia succeeded one another for centuries.
