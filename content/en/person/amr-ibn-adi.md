## Overview
Amr ibn Adi ibn Nasr al-Lakhmi: the first of the Lakhmid kings over al-Hira, in the reign of Sabur ibn Ardashir.

## Life
After Jadhima's death, Amr ibn Adi ibn Nasr al-Lakhmi — first of the Lakhmid kings — was appointed over al-Hira in the reign of Kisra Sabur ibn Ardashir {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Lakhmid kings then succeeded one another over al-Hira until the Persians appointed a Persian governor after Iyas ibn Qubaysa {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

## For young visitors
Amr ibn Adi was the first king of the house of Lakhm in al-Hira; from his line the Arab kings under Persia succeeded one another for three and a half centuries.
