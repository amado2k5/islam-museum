## Summary
Dr. Hamdy Abdel Aal (1934–1990) was a researcher and university professor who taught at the Faculty of Sharia, Kuwait University. He wrote on comparative religion and the sects and on creed and ethics; his four known books were published by Dar al-Qalam, Kuwait, between 1984 and 1989. His family maintains a memorial library at origins.faith that gathers his books and makes them readable and searchable, grounding his biography in what is printed on his book covers, in library records and in the family's knowledge. {cite:origins-faith}

## Life and work
He lived between 1934 and 1990 and was — as printed on his book covers — a professor at the Faculty of Sharia, Kuwait University. The available sources establish nothing more exact about his upbringing and education; his library page notes that the biography, photographs and memories will be added when complete, so the museum confines itself to what the records support. {cite:origins-faith}

## Works
His four known books, all published by Dar al-Qalam, Kuwait, are: *Manhaj al-Salaf fi al-Aqidah* (The Method of the Righteous Predecessors in Creed, 1984; second printing 1986), *al-Saba'iyyun: Manhajan wa-Ghayah* (The Saba'ites: Method and Aim, 1985, 117 pp.), *al-Akhlaq wa-Mi'yaruha bayna al-Wad'iyya wa-al-Din* (Ethics and Its Criterion: Between Man-Made Systems and Religion, 1985; third edition Cairo 2002, 174 pp.), and *al-Milla wa-al-Nihla fi al-Yahudiyya wa-al-Masihiyya wa-al-Islam* (Milla and Nihla in Judaism, Christianity and Islam, 1989, 216 pp.). Copies are held by the University of Toronto, Princeton, Kuwait University and the German union catalogue. {cite:origins-faith}

## Method and project
In *al-Milla wa-al-Nihla* he distinguishes the milla — religion as God revealed it — from the nihla, what people claim for themselves: opinions, schools and personal readings of the texts, and follows that distinction through the three religions with a historical, comparative method. From its closing pages: "People are driven by their beliefs more than by their ideas. Hence religions will keep holding the reins of human history and human conflict." {cite:origins-faith}
