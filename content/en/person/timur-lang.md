## Overview
Timurlane, a Turco-Mongol commander. He began his campaign in the year 773 AH, and died on the night of Wednesday, the seventeenth of Sha'ban in the year 807 AH, as al-Suyuti reports {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.758}.

## His origins
al-Suyuti reports that he was of peasant stock, and grew up as a thief and highwayman. He then joined the service of the master of the sultan's horses, succeeded him after his death, and kept rising. al-Suyuti transmits that he "ruined the lands and destroyed the people". This is the judgement of the historian, not the museum's {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.758}.

## His conquests in the Shadharat
Ibn al-Imad reports in Shadharat al-Dhahab that he invaded India in the year 801 AH and took Delhi, enslaving a great number of people {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.3}.

He lists the stages of his rule: Khwarazm, then Transoxiana, then Samarqand, then Khurasan, then Herat, then Tabaristan and Gurgan, then Tabriz and Azerbaijan, then Isfahan, then Baghdad, then Sivas, Aleppo and Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.71-72}.

He also reports that he defeated Toqtamish Khan, lord of Sarai, in his rule in the year 797 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.71-72}. See [[person/toqtamish-khan|Toqtamish, lord of Sarai]].
