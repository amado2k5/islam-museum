## Overview
Asad Allah Isfahani was a celebrated Persian sword-maker, a shamshirsaz, of the time of Shah Abbas I. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 734}

## The helmet
The story goes that the Ottoman sultan presented a helmet to Shah Abbas and offered a sum of money to anyone who could cut it in two with a sword. Asad made a sword that achieved this, and as a reward Shah Abbas remitted the tax on the sword-makers, who continued to be exempt until Qajar times (A. K. S. Lambton, Islamic Society in Persia, 1954). {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 734}
