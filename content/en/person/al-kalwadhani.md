## Overview
Abu'l-Khattab al-Kalwadhani, shaykh of the Hanbalis and man of works; an imam, scholar, scrupulous, righteous, ocean of knowledge, fine lecturer — he read fiqh under the qadi Abu Ya'la and imams trained under him, and a company read fiqh on him including 'Abd al-Wahid b. Taymiyya, Abu Bakr al-Dinawari and Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jili; al-Silafi said: Abu'l-Khattab is among the imams of Ahmad's companions — he gives fatwa in his madhhab and debates. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 507-508}

## His death
He died on Wednesday 14 Jumada II 510 and was buried beside Imam Ahmad's grave. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 507-508}
