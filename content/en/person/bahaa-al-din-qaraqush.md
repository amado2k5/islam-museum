## Overview
A commander of Salah al-Din. He put him in charge of building the great wall around Egypt and Cairo in 572 AH. Al-Suyuti relates from Ibn al-Athir that its circuit was twenty-nine thousand and three hundred cubits by the Hashimi measure. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 684}
