## Overview

Abd Allah ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, the infant of Karbala; Ibn Kathir lists him as the second of Husayn's sons killed with him in 61 AH {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya}.

In the Twelver tradition this infant is named Ali al-Asghar, and some of the most famous mourning laments attach to him.
