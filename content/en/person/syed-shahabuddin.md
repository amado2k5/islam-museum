Syed Shahabuddin (4 November 1935 – 4 March 2017) was an Indian diplomat and politician, born at Ranchi and educated at Patna University, where he took a physics honours degree and led student politics before joining the Indian Foreign Service.{cite:wikipedia-syed-shahabuddin-en}{cite:wikipedia-syed-shahabuddin-ur} He served as consul general in Jeddah and as ambassador to Venezuela and Algeria, and by his voluntary retirement in 1978 he was the joint secretary in charge of Southeast Asia, the Indian Ocean and the Pacific in the Ministry of External Affairs.{cite:wikipedia-syed-shahabuddin-en}

Entering politics with the Janata Party, he sat in the Rajya Sabha from 1979 and twice represented Kishanganj in the Lok Sabha, becoming one of the most articulate voices of Muslim politics in independent India: he led the Muslim opposition to the Shah Bano judgment, campaigned over the Babri Masjid, founded the Insaf Party in 1989, presided over the All India Muslim Majlis-e-Mushawarat from 2004 to 2011, and edited the monthly journal Muslim India from 1983 to 2006.{cite:wikipedia-syed-shahabuddin-en}{cite:wikidata-syed-shahabuddin} He died on 4 March 2017; the English article places his death in Delhi, the Urdu article at Noida.{cite:wikipedia-syed-shahabuddin-ur}
