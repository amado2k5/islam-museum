## Overview
Khatun, queen of Bukhara. She called on the Turks against Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad, and they were defeated. He made peace with her for a million, and entered Bikand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Breaking the covenant
She broke the covenant in the days of Sa'id ibn Uthman. The people of Sughd, the Turks, Kush and Nasaf came against him with a hundred and twenty thousand and were defeated. She then gave him hostages and renewed the peace, helped him with the people of Bukhara against Samarqand, and sent him a peace offering for Tirmidh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
