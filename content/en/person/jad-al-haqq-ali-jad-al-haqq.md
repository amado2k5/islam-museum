## Overview
Jad al-Haqq Ali Jad al-Haqq, a jurist of the principles of law and a judge, the forty-second shaykh of al-Azhar al-Sharif. He was born in the governorate of Dakahlia in 1335 AH (1917 CE) and died in 1416 AH (1995 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The two dates in the museum's data come from one source, and they need an additional source.
