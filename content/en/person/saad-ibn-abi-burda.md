## Summary
Amir ibn Abi Musa Abd Allah ibn Qays, Sa'id ibn Abi Burda al-Ash'ari of Kufa. He narrated from his father, Anas ibn Malik and others, and Qatada, Abu Ishaq al-Shaybani and Shu'ba narrated from him. Ahmad called him firm in hadith and reliable; Ibn Hibban lists him among the reliable. Death 168 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13356}
