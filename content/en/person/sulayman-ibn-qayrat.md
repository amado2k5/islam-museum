## Overview
Sulayman ibn Qayrat, a client of al-Mahdi, and holder of the Qayrat desert in the City of Peace (Baghdad) {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Sisar
al-Baladhuri reports that he was in charge of al-Mahdi's pastures of beasts and sheep at Sisar. He wrote to al-Mahdi about the report of the vagrants, and al-Mahdi ordered him to build the city of Sisar. He built it with his partner Salam al-Tayfuri, and they fortified it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
