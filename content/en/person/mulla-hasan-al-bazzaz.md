## Overview
Mulla Hasan al-Bazzaz, one of the best-known poets of Mosul, who studied under a number of its scholars. He was born in 1845 CE (1261 AH) and died in 1887 CE (1305 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
