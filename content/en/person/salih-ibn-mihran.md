## Summary
Abu Sufyan Salih ibn Mihran, called al-Hakim, whose words were written down. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 668, sequence 669, on pages 391 and 392. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}

## Scrupulousness and sayings
Sulayman al-Shadhakuni said: I have not seen anyone more scrupulous than Abu Sufyan. Among his sayings: people should be certain they will not see joy in Islam; every craftsman works only with a tool, and the tool of Islam is knowledge; if you see a scholar who is not careful in his knowledge, you should not take from him. He said: the keys of the world were placed on the world and it did not open; then the keys of the hereafter were placed on it and it opened. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}

## Right and foolish scrupulousness
Muhammad ibn Asim relates from Abu Sufyan that scrupulousness is of two kinds, right and foolish. The right kind is to ask a man where he has come from, and he says from the market; the foolish kind is to ask and be told: from the mosque, God willing. He also said: every deed done for other than God is a sin on the one who does it; and sincerity is certainty. The scan breaks off at this point. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}
