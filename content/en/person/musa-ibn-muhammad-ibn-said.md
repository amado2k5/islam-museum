## Overview
Abu Imran Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Said, a scribe and historian, father of Ali ibn Musa, lord of the Maghrib. His son described him as known for memorising history and for his care for letters in his country. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.335-336}

## Reading and writing
He lived sixty-seven years, and never passed a day without reading a book or books, even on feast days. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.335-336}
