## Overview
Uthman ibn Hayyan, governor of the Hijaz in the time of al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, and governor of Medina in the time of Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.177-181}.

## His appointment
The book reports that al-Walid advised him, with Khalid al-Qasri, when Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz dismissed him, and that Umar advised his dismissal. The book describes his office briefly {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.177-181}.
