## Summary
Maiga Aboubakary leads the Burkina Faso Islamic Organisation for Population and Development, a body that carries public-health and family-planning education into mosques and Muslim communities across the country. {cite:muslim500-maiga-aboubakary}

## Work
Under his leadership the organisation has worked with imams and community networks to open discussion of reproductive health, child welfare and development in religious settings, positioning mosques as partners in national health campaigns. {cite:muslim500-maiga-aboubakary}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists him under Social Issues as an example of a grassroots religious-social figure: not a scholar or official but a community organiser whose influence is measured in local health outcomes. The published sources for him are thin — the Muslim 500 entry is the principal record of his work, and the museum notes this limited sourcing on the record itself. {cite:muslim500-maiga-aboubakary}
