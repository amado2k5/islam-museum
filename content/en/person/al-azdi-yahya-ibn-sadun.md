## Overview
Abu Bakr Yahya ibn Sa'dun ibn Tammam ibn al-Nas al-Azdi al-Qurtubi, an Andalusian scholar from Cordoba, from whom students took and heard hadith. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 486 AH and his death as 567 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.519-520}

The museum's data hold no details of his teachers or his works, which need an additional source.
