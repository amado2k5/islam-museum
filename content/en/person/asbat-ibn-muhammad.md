## Overview
Asbat ibn Muhammad, Abu Muhammad al-Kufi, was a hadith transmitter who died in 200 AH, about 815 CE (derived conversion), according to al-Safadi and Ibn al-Imad {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 249} {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 2 p. 474}.

## Transmission and assessment
Al-Safadi says he was the father of Ubayd ibn Asbat, that Ibn Ma'in held him reliable, and that al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 249}. Ibn al-Imad says he was reliable and a man of hadith, who narrated from al-A'mash and others of his generation {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 2 p. 474}. Ibn al-Imad also cites the work al-Mughni as calling him Asbat ibn Muhammad al-Qurashi, reliable and well known, and the quotation goes on to give Ibn Sa'd's view that he was reliable with some weakness {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 2 p. 474-475}.
