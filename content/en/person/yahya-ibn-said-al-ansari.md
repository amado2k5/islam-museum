## Overview
Yahya ibn Sa'id ibn Qays al-Ansari, one of the jurists of Medina of the third generation, who was a judge for Abu Ja'far al-Mansur. al-Shirazi reports, from Hammad ibn Zayd, that Ayyub said to him: "No one more learned in jurisprudence than Yahya ibn Sa'id has been left." He died in the year 143 AH according to al-Shirazi, and the Common Era date is derived {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.

The year of death needs an additional source. He lived in [[place/medina|Medina]].
