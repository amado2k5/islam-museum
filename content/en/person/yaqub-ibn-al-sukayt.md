## Overview
Yaqub ibn al-Sikkit, an imam in Arabic, whom [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] appointed to teach his sons {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 541}.

## His death
al-Suyuti reports that al-Mutawakkil asked him in the year 244 AH: "Which of them do you love more, al-Mu'tazz and al-Mu'ayyad, or al-Hasan and al-Husayn?" He answered: "Qanbar, the client of Ali, is better than both of them." So he ordered the Turks to trample his belly until he died. It is also said that he ordered his tongue cut out, and he died, and he sent his blood-money to his son {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 541}.
