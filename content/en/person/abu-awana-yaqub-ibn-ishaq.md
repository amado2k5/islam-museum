## Overview
Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim ibn Yazid al-Isfara'ini, a hadith scholar of memory and a jurist from Isfara'in. He became famous for his "Mustakhraj" on the Sahih of Muslim, and he was the first to spread the Shafi'i school in Isfara'in. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 230 AH and his death as 316 AH; both dates need an additional source.

## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] says that for his edition of «Mustakhraj Abi Awana» on Sahih Muslim he gathered the manuscripts that exist in the world, about seven, none of them complete; the book will run to fourteen or fifteen volumes, and one volume remains for him to finish. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:21:00} He says his edition contains more than three thousand hadiths that are entirely new, never published before, and that he had thought the previous printed edition good until he numbered it and found tens or hundreds of errors. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:23:00}

Alongside it he is working on two books: one on the biographies of Abu Awana's teachers, studying the hadiths of those on whom no critic has spoken, and «Tarh al-Bahraj bi-Atraf al-Mustakhraj», which arranges the hadiths of the Mustakhraj by their opening phrases on the method of al-Mizzi's «Tuhfat al-Ashraf»; he shows viewers his index cards for it. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep28 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep28 00:01:30} He says the cards are the most accurate tool he has and that he does not use the computer. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep28 00:02:00} Elsewhere he mentions that Abu Awana held [[person/shuba-ibn-al-hajjaj|Shu'ba]] in such awe that he followed him even in his errors. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep23 00:25:30}
