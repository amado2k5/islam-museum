## Overview
Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim ibn Yazid al-Isfara'ini, a hadith scholar of memory and a jurist from Isfara'in. He became famous for his "Mustakhraj" on the Sahih of Muslim, and he was the first to spread the Shafi'i school in Isfara'in. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 230 AH and his death as 316 AH; both dates need an additional source.
