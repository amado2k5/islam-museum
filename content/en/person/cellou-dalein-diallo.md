# Cellou Dalein Diallo

Cellou Dalein Diallo (born 3 February 1952) is a Guinean economist and politician, a member of the Fula people from Labé, who was Prime Minister of Guinea from 2004 to 2006 and has led the opposition Union of Democratic Forces of Guinea (UFDG) since 2007. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} {cite:wikidata-cellou-dalein-diallo}

## Ministerial career

Diallo studied at the University of Conakry and in Paris, became an inspector of trade in 1976, and worked at the Central Bank of the Republic of Guinea from 1985 to 1995. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} He joined President Lansana Conté's government in July 1996 and held a succession of portfolios — transport and tourism, infrastructure, public works, and finally fisheries — before Conté appointed him prime minister on 9 December 2004, after the office had stood vacant since François Lonseny Fall's resignation. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} In April 2006 a decree that would have greatly increased Diallo's power was announced and then reversed within a day, and on 5 April 2006 he was dismissed as prime minister "for serious misconduct". {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en}

## Opposition leader

On 8 November 2007 the UFDG appointed Diallo its president. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} Soldiers searched his home at gunpoint on 1 January 2009 after the military seized power, and on 28 September 2009 he was injured — three ribs reportedly broken — when soldiers fired on the opposition protest in Conakry in which he was taking part. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} He placed first in the first round of the 2010 presidential election but lost the runoff to Alpha Condé, stood again in 2015, and in 2020 received about a third of the vote yet declared himself the victor, a claim followed by riots and a period under house arrest. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en} In September 2021 he supported the putschists who carried out that year's coup. {cite:wikipedia-cellou-dalein-diallo-en}
