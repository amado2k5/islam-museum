## Overview
Amir ibn Sharahil al-Sha'bi, the jurist of Kufa. He died in 104 AH according to one view; it is also said 107 AH, and it is said he was eighty-two years old, according to al-Shirazi's "Classes of the Jurists". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## Reports about him
He entered [[place/rayy|al-Rayy]] with Qutayba ibn Muslim, and Qutayba asked him which drink he liked best. He gave a well-known answer recorded in al-Mas'udi's book. Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan was his boon companion, and taught him the directions of the winds. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.87}

The sources note that he was with Ibn Sirin when he asked Umar ibn Hubayra in 103 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.190}
