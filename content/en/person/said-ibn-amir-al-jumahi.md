## Overview
Sa'id ibn Amir ibn Hudhaym al-Jumahi al-Qurashi, one of the Companions of the Messenger of God and one of the commanders of the army of Iyad ibn Ghanm. al-Baladhuri reports from al-Waqidi that he was on the right wing when the army marched to al-Jazira in mid-Sha'ban of the year 18 AH. The vanguard was under Maysara ibn Masruq, and the left wing under Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.237}. See [[event/conquest-of-jazira|the conquest of al-Jazira]].

## His dealings with Homs
Al-Mas'udi reports that the people of Homs complained of him to Umar. They said that he did not come out to them until the day had risen, and that he did not answer at night. Umar sent him a thousand dinars, and he distributed them among the orphans and the needy until little remained {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.602-603}.

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] recalls Sa'id ibn Amir in her episode on generosity in her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']], as an example of the giving the Companions learned from the Prophet. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:06:30} She relates that Umar ibn al-Khattab made him governor in Syria, a rich land where whoever went might be tempted, when he was newly married; his bride wanted them to buy things with the money he had been given for their living, and he said he would invest it in a profitable trade, then distributed it among the poor of Syria, and whenever she asked about the trade he said it was making a profit, until a relative of hers told her the story and she wept at such giving. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:07:30}

The people of Syria complained of him to Umar, she relates, but every complaint showed what a great man he was: that he did not come out to them at night, because the day was theirs and the night his Lord's; that for two days he came out late, because he had only two garments, one to wear and one to wash; and that fainting fits came over him, because he had seen Khubayb ibn Adi tortured and killed while he himself was still a polytheist and had not defended him, and whenever he remembered it he fainted. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep20 00:09:00} In al-Mas'udi's report cited above, the people of Homs complained of him to Umar that he did not come out to them until the day was well advanced and did not answer at night. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.602-603}
