## Overview
Sa'id ibn Amir ibn Hudhaym al-Jumahi al-Qurashi, one of the Companions of the Messenger of God and one of the commanders of the army of Iyad ibn Ghanm. al-Baladhuri reports from al-Waqidi that he was on the right wing when the army marched to al-Jazira in mid-Sha'ban of the year 18 AH. The vanguard was under Maysara ibn Masruq, and the left wing under Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.237}. See [[event/conquest-of-jazira|the conquest of al-Jazira]].

## His dealings with Homs
Al-Mas'udi reports that the people of Homs complained of him to Umar. They said that he did not come out to them until the day had risen, and that he did not answer at night. Umar sent him a thousand dinars, and he distributed them among the orphans and the needy until little remained {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.602-603}.
