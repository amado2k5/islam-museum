## Overview
Ali ibn al-Furat, an Abbasid vizier in the time of al-Muqtadir. Al-Tabari says that he was appointed vizier a second time in 304 AH, and that he and his son were arrested in 312 AH, and al-Muqtadir ordered them killed. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.59} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.104} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.105}

## Other reports
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muqtadir appointed him vizier three times, and that his son Muhsin dominated affairs in the third, after which he and his son were arrested. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.660-661}

Ibn al-Athir says that al-Muqtadir arrested him in Dhu al-Hijja 299 AH, that three comets appeared a short time before that, and that he was put in charge of his house and his property was plundered. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.48-49}

The discrepancy in the years between al-Tabari and Ibn al-Athir is clear in the sources and needs an additional source to reconcile them.
