## Overview
Munira, client of Muhammad ibn Ali. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur granted her land, and the Lane of Munira and the Khan of Munira on the eastern side are attributed to her. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414-417}

The data do not record the history of her life, and need an additional source.
