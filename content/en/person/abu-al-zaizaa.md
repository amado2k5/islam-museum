## Overview
The guard-captain of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. The report in "Meadows of Gold" says that Abd al-Malik instructed him to kill Amr ibn Sa'id al-Ashdaq, and he struck his neck inside the room. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 95-96}
