## Overview
Al-Shaykh al-'Imad Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn 'Abd al-Wahid al-Maqdisi the Hanbali, brother of the hafiz 'Abd al-Ghani; born at Jamma'il in 543 AH, he emigrated in 551 with his relatives, heard hadith at Baghdad and Mosul, memorised al-Khiraqi and Gharib al-'Azizi, gave lessons, debated, and read the canonical readings under Abu'l-Hasan al-Batayihi. He devoted himself to teaching Qur'an and fiqh and was pious, humble, fasting and night-praying; al-Muwaffaq said "I never left him save to travel, and I never knew him to disobey God." {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## His stations and death
He held the Mountain when al-Muwaffaq was in town and the town when al-Muwaffaq went up, taught at the Umayyad mosque from dawn to night, and was fervent in commanding right and forbidding wrong; his works include the Book of Distinctions in legal questions. He died suddenly on Thursday night, 17 Dhu'l-Qa'da 614, and the mosque could not contain his funeral. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
