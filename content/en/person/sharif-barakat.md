## Overview
Barakat ibn Muhammad, Sharif of Mecca {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.191}.

## His submission
al-Salabi reports that when news reached him of the killing of the Mamluk sultan Qansuh al-Ghuri and his deputy Tuman Bay at the hands of the Ottomans, he hastened to pledge hearing and obedience to Sultan Selim I. He handed him the keys of the Ka'ba and some relics {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.191}.

Selim confirmed him as sharif of the Hijaz, amir over Mecca and the Hijaz, and granted him wide powers {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.191}.
