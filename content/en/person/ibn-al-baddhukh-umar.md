## Overview
Abu Ja'far Umar ibn Ali ibn al-Baddhukh al-Qal'i al-Maghribi, a physician and pharmacist from the Maghrib. He was known for his knowledge of simple and compound medicines and their preparation, and he wrote verse. He died in 576 AH (1180 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
