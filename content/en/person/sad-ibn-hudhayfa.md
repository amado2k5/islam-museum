## Overview
Sa'd, son of [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]]. He was killed as a martyr at Siffin in the year 36 AH, together with his brother [[person/safwan-ibn-hudhayfa|Safwan]] {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.675}.

## The report
Al-Mas'udi reports that their father died seven days after he addressed the people and pledged allegiance to [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]]; it is also said forty days {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.675}.

## Notes for researchers
- The report comes from a single passage in Muruj al-Dhahab. It needs an additional source.
