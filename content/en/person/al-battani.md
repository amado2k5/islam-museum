## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Jabir al-Battani, counted by al-Mas'udi among those who pursued astronomy after the rise of Islam; his Zij is the one al-Mas'udi relied on for chronology of the caliphs' reigns from the hijra to 336 AH.
