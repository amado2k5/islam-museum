## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abra, client of Khuza'a, was the first governor of Ali ibn Abi Talib over Khurasan, according to Abu Ubayda; after him came Ja'da ibn Hubayra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
