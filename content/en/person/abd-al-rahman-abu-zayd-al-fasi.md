## Summary
A Maliki scholar of Fez of the Fihriya, traditionist and man of letters, learned in several sciences; he studied with his father, his uncle Ahmad, Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Fasi, the judge Ibn Sawda and Abd al-Wahhab ibn al-Arabi; his father called him the Suyuti of his age. Works include Miftah al-Shifa and Azhar al-Basatin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7834}
