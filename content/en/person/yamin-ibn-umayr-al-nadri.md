## Summary
Ibn Ishaq names him in a report that reached him: he met Abu Layla and Abdullah ibn Mughaffal weeping, gave them a riding-camel and provisioned them with dates. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq said: it reached me that Ibn Yamin ibn Umayr ibn Ka'b al-Nadri met Abu Layla and Abdullah ibn Mughaffal weeping, asked them, and gave them a riding-camel of his and provisioned them with some dates. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161-162}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: of Banu al-Nadir, per the nisba "al-Nadri". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The name in the book is "Ibn Yamin ibn Umayr"; in al-Zurqani's account (transmitted by the editors) it is "Yamin ibn Amr" (note p. 161) — the name may be "Ibn Yamin" or "Yamin", and is not settled.
- The account "it reached me" carries no chain (p. 161).
