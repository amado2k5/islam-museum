## Summary
Amr ibn al-Aswad Abu Iyad al-Anasi, also called al-Hamdani, from Damascus, a Successor. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8519}
He narrated from Amr ibn Mas'ud, Mu'adh ibn Jabal, Ubada ibn al-Samit, Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As, Abu Hurayra and Aisha. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8519}
Mujahid, Khalid ibn Ma'dan and Shurayh ibn Ubayd narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8519}

## What the scholars said of him
Ibn Hibban called him one of the worshippers and ascetics of Syria, Ibn Sa'd called him trustworthy and of few reports, and Ibn Abd al-Barr said that the scholars agree he was among the trustworthy scholars. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8519} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8520}
