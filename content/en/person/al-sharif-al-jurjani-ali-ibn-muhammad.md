## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Jurjani, known as al-Sharif, a scholar of philosophy, logic, theology and rhetoric. He was born in Taju in 740 AH (1340 CE) and died in 816 AH (1413 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
