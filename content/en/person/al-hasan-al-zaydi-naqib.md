## Overview
Abu Tammam al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. 'Abd al-Wahhab b. Sulayman al-Zaydi (d. c. 384), naqib of the 'Abbasids and sahib al-salat; the first to combine the salat-office and the niqaba in 380 — his son Abu'l-Hasan, surnamed Nizam al-hadratayn, was appointed over the salat and invested for him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.179}
