## Overview
Mulhim ibn Haydar al-Shihabi was a learned man of the Shihabi amirs in Lebanon, born and raised at al-Shuwayfat, where he took up fiqh and letters and composed an urjuza on fiqh. He died in 1296 AH (1879 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.287}

He was imprisoned four months on the charge of taking part in the events of 1860; his innocence was established and he was appointed director of the Shuf district from 1280 to 1289 AH, and died at al-Shuwayfat. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.287}
