## Summary
[[person/al-rubayyi-bint-muawwidh|Al-Rubayyi']] bint Mu'awwidh ibn Afra' al-Ansariyya al-Najjariyya of Banu Adi ibn al-Najjar. Iyas ibn al-Bukayr al-Laythi married her and she bore him Muhammad {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1869}. Ibn Abi Khaythama said she was among the women who pledged at the Tree {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1869}; Abu Umar said she would sometimes go on campaign with the Messenger of God {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## On campaign
Bukhari, al-Nasa'i and Abu Muslim al-Kajji relate from al-Rubayyi': "we went on campaign with the Messenger of God; we gave the men water, served them, and carried the slain and wounded back to Medina" — in Bukhari's account: "we carried water and treated the wounded" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## Describing the Prophet
Abu Ubayda ibn Muhammad ibn Ammar relates that he said to al-Rubayyi': describe the Messenger of God to me. She said: "my son, had you seen him, you would have seen the sun rising" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## Her wedding night
Bukhari and al-Tirmidhi relate from her: "the Prophet came in to me the morning after my wedding-night and sat on my bedding as you sit now; our little girls beat the tambourine and lamented those of my fathers slain at Badr, until one said 'and among us is a prophet who knows what tomorrow holds' — he told her: leave that, and say what you were saying" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## The ablution
Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah record several hadiths on the chain of Ibn Aqil from her describing the Prophet's ablution, among them: "he would come to us and say: pour me water for ablution" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## The khul'
Ibn Sa'd relates from her that she told her husband: may I ransom myself from you for all I own? He said yes; she gave him everything but her dress, so he disputed with her before Uthman, who told him: "her condition stands". Another account says Uthman said: "the stipulation stands — take everything down to her head-ties". This was during Uthman's siege, year 35 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.

## Her transmitters
She related from the Prophet; from her relate her daughter A'isha bint Anas ibn Malik, Sulayman ibn Yasar, Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman, Nafi' client of Ibn Umar, Ubada ibn al-Walid ibn Ubada ibn al-Samit, Khalid ibn Dhakwan and Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Aqil {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1870}.
