## Overview
Abu Abd al-Rahman Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak al-Marwazi, client of the Banu Hanzala. Al-Shirazi says that he studied jurisprudence with Sufyan and Malik, and died at Hit, a town on the Euphrates near al-Anbar, in "around eighty and a hundred". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 50} The Sufyan is not named in the text; for Malik see [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik ibn Anas]].

Al-Shirazi quotes Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi: "The imams are four: Sufyan al-Thawri, Malik, Hammad ibn Zayd and Ibn al-Mubarak." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 50}

## Date of death
The date of death differs: al-Shirazi says "around eighty and a hundred", and the editor's note on Ibn Qutayba makes it 181 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 50}
