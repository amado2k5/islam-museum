## Overview
Shabib ibn Rabi'i al-Tamimi, the first amir of the Harruriyya according to al-Mas'udi. They made him their commander when twelve thousand readers and others withdrew to Harura' after the arbitration at Siffin {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 685}.

[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] went out to them and debated with them, and then they all entered Kufa together {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 685}.
