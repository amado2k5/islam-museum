## Overview
Asim ibn Sulayman al-Ahwal al-Basri, Abu Abd al-Rahman, was one of the memorisers of hadith, trustworthy, of Basra. He held public offices: the hisba at Kufa and the judgeship at al-Mada'in, and was famed for asceticism and worship. He died in 142 AH (760 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.248}
