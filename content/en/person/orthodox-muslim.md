## Overview
The Orthodox Muslim is a debater and preacher who runs a YouTube channel under that name, and is also known as Libyano, a name carried in his own channel's keywords. {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim}

His channel describes him in a single line: "A humble servant of Allah". {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim}

His channel had about 137,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim}

## Career and work
His output is built on livestreams and filmed debates with Christian and Jewish apologists and others, including a debate with Jay Dyer, reaction streams answering Sam Shamoun, street confrontations filmed in New York City, and interactive formats such as "1 Muslim VS 10 Christians". {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim}

His recorded work includes a lecture titled "A COMPLETE Refutation of Hadith Rejection" and a debate titled "Does Islam Need the Hadith?" against Waleed Naeem, in which he defends the centrality of the hadith against those who reject it. {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim}

He teaches as a guest instructor for the Sapience Institute, a dawah education organisation, which presented him in its recording as "our guest instructor" for a seminar titled "A Muslim Critique of Christian Belief", covering the Trinity, the status of the Holy Spirit and Pauline theology. {cite:sapience-orthodox-muslim}

He has also appeared in a joint debate alongside Korra The Taymi against Cliffe and Stuart Knechtle, placing him within the platform's network of young English-language debaters. {cite:youtube-korra-the-taymi}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of different positions reported by the sources, not as a verdict of the museum on them.

His public image rests on a confrontational livestream debate style: his video titles are filled with phrases such as "HEATED DEBATE" and "CRASHES OUT", engagement formulas that market the confrontation as spectacle, while the content of his teaching, as in his Sapience Institute seminar, presents a methodical defence of monotheism and a critique of Christian theology. {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim} {cite:sapience-orthodox-muslim}

His adopted name "The Orthodox Muslim" is a self-description of stance rather than the name of a Muslim school, and the available sources attribute no named school to him, so the museum records no school for him. {cite:youtube-orthodox-muslim} {cite:sapience-orthodox-muslim}
