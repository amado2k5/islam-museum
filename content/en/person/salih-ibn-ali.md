## Overview
Salih ibn Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas, one of the commanders of the Abbasid mission, and its first governor over Syria {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Mount Lebanon
According to al-Baladhuri, news reached him that some people on Mount Lebanon had complained of the tax agent of Baalbek, and that the Byzantines had colluded with them. He sent them men who killed their combatants, confirmed those who remained in their religion and returned them to their villages, and exiled some of the people of Lebanon. al-Awza'i then wrote his well-known letter to him denouncing the seizure of the common people for the sins of the elite. See [[event/al-awzai-letter-to-salih|al-Awza'i's letter to Salih]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The house in Ramla
The house of the dyers in Ramla passed to his heirs, when it was confiscated together with the property of the Umayyads {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The pursuit of Marwan
He set out in pursuit of Marwan ibn Muhammad, together with Abu Awn Abd al-Malik ibn Yazid and Amir ibn Isma'il al-Madhhiji. They caught up with him in Egypt, where he was camped at Busir. They attacked his camp by night, calling out "Vengeance for Ibrahim!" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.233}.
