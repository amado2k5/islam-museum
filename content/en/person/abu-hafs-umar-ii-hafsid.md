## Overview
Umar ibn Abi Bakr al-Mutawakkil ala Allah ibn Yahya ibn Ibrahim al-Hafsi, Abu Hafs, was one of the kings of the Muwahhids in Tunis, in al-Zirikli's wording. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 723 to 748 AH, 1323 to 1347 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 43}.

## Career
He was given the oath of allegiance after his father's death in 747 AH, and his brothers Abu al-Abbas, Khalid and Azzuz rose against him; he killed them all {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 43}. His time was short: some soldiers killed him near Gabes, and al-Zirikli puts his rule at ten months and 13 days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 43}.
