## Summary
Anas Jalhoum is an Egyptian Quran reciter and imam, known for his moving recitations from the night (qiyam) and tarawih prayers, which his video descriptions say moved the imam and the congregation to tears. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum} His recordings circulate through his official website, "Al-Jannah" (the Paradise website), and the Al-Jannah YouTube channel.

No source consulted by the museum publishes a birth date for him or a documented account of his upbringing and education, so this entry is limited to what his official channel and website establish.

## Recitations and reach
The Al-Jannah YouTube channel (@algannah_anas_galhoum) presents itself in its description as "your way to Paradise" and as presenting the masterpieces of recitations by Sheikh Anas Jalhoum, including recitations from prayers such as qiyam and tarawih. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum} Individual recitations on the channel reach into the tens of millions of views — a recitation of Surah Yusuf stood at 19 million views when the channel was checked on 25 October 2026; these are dated platform figures that change constantly. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum}

The descriptions of his recitation videos name the Al-Jannah website (www.algannah.com) as the official website of Sheikh Anas Jalhoum, and link his official Facebook page (anasgolhoum) and his Twitter account (anasgolhoom); they also mention the Al-Jannah audio library on SoundCloud and the Al-Jannah app on the Play Store, through which the morning and evening adhkar can be heard in his voice or read. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum}

Among the channel's most recent uploads when checked were recitations of his recorded in Sharjah in the United Arab Emirates, including recitations from Ramadan nights and from Surah al-Dhariyat. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum}

## Impact and audience
Anas Jalhoum's impact rests on his voice and his recitations recorded from the prayers, which reach a wide audience through the Al-Jannah channel, the Al-Jannah website and its app, and on his recorded adhkar — platforms whose figures are dated by nature. {cite:youtube-algannah-anas-galhoum}
