## Summary
Abu Isma'il Hammad, son of the imam Abu Hanifa al-Nu'man b. Thabit; of his father's madhhab and among the righteous and good in high degree. When his father died he insisted on surrendering the deposits of gold and silver held with him to their owners — the qadi weighed and received them so his father's obligation was acquitted; his son Isma'il was qadi of Basra, removed by the qadi Yahya b. Aktham. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
