## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Ali ibn Abi Sharif al-Maqdisi, Abu al-Ma'ali, Kamal al-Din, son of the Amir Nasir al-Din, was a scholar of legal theory among the Shafi'i jurists {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. He belonged to Jerusalem by birth and by death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 822 AH, 1419 CE, and his death as 906 AH, 1501 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}.

## Life
Ibn al-Imad describes him, in the words al-Zirikli quotes, as «the imam, shaykh al-Islam, king of the notable scholars» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. He taught and issued fatwas in his own city and in Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}.

## Works
Among his writings al-Zirikli lists al-Durar al-Lawami' bi-Tahrir Jam' al-Jawami', printed, on legal theory {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. He lists al-Fara'id fi Hall Sharh al-'Aqa'id in his own hand, which al-Zirikli saw in the al-Laythi library at Markaz al-Saff in Egypt; he finished the draft in 889 at his house in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. He lists al-Musamara 'ala al-Musayara, printed, on the doctrine of divine oneness {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. He lists Sawb al-Ghamama fi Irsal al-'Imama, in manuscript in the Chester Beatty collection {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}. Al-Zirikli says he relied on Brockelmann for the name of his father {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 53}.
