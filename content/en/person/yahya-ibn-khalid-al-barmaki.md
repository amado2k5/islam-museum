## Overview
Yahya ibn Khalid al-Barmaki, vizier of al-Rashid, son of [[person/khalid-ibn-barmak|Khalid ibn Barmak]] and one of the [[group/barmakids|Barmakids]] {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush}.

## His learning
al-Mas'udi reports that when al-Rashid wanted to join the Red Sea with the Mediterranean, Yahya drew his attention to the fact that the Byzantines might seize people from the two holy cities, and al-Rashid refrained {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.558}. He was a man of learning and reflection, and he had a council where the people of theology, and the holders of opinions and sects, gathered {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.357-358}.

Ibn al-Nadim reports that Sibawayh came to Iraq seeking him, and he brought him together with al-Kisa'i and al-Akhfash. He sent a man to India to bring drugs and to write down their religions, and the Barmakids took care of the scholars and physicians of India {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.143} {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.484}.
