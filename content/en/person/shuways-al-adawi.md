## Overview
Shuways al-Adawi, one of al-Baladhuri's narrators. He reports that they went out with the governor of al-Ubulla and won it, then crossed the Euphrates. His people came out to them with their shovels, and they defeated them and opened the Euphrates {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}. He is among those who took part in [[event/conquest-of-ubulla|the conquest of al-Ubulla]].
