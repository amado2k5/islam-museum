## Overview
Abu Zayd 'Abdallah b. 'Umar b. 'Isa al-Dabusi, the Hanafi jurist; one of Imam Abu Hanifa's great companions cited in proverb — the first to lay down the science of khilaf and bring it into being. His works: Kitab al-asrar, a Taqwim of the proofs, and other works and commentaries. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
