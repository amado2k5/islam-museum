## Overview
Shurayh ibn Amir ibn Qayn of the Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr ibn Hawazin. al-Baladhuri reports on the authority of al-Kalbi that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] appointed him as successor at al-Kharayba after its conquest and the capture of its people. It had been a garrison for the non-Arabs {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
