## Overview
Yazid ibn al-Muhajjal, of the delegation of the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] who came with [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] to the Prophet in the year 10 AH, as Ibn Ishaq names him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.240}.

The Sira reports that he embraced Islam with his people at the hands of Khalid, and came in their delegation. He is mentioned in [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|Khalid's mission to the Banu al-Harith]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.240}.
