## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Ahmad al-Halabi al-Ba'li was a Hanbali mathematician and scholar of the law of inheritance {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}. Al-Zirikli prints his birth as 1108 AH, 1697 CE, and his death as 1189 AH, 1775 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}.

## Life
His family came from Aleppo, but he was born, raised and educated in Damascus, and became well known in Baalbek, whence his name al-Ba'li {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}. He took up the post of Hanbali mufti of Damascus in 1182 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}. He earned his living by weaving a kind of cloth called al-aldaja, made the pilgrimage, taught in Medina, and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}.

## Works
He wrote books on arithmetic, inheritance and jurisprudence, among them Munyat al-Ra'id li-Sharh Umdat Kull Farid, in manuscript in the Jawish collection in Beirut {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 162}.
