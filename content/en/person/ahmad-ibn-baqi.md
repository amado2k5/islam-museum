## Overview
Ahmad ibn Baqi ibn Makhlad al-Qurtubi; al-Nasa'i, al-Baghundi, Abu 'Uruba and others narrated from him, and al-Nasa'i said "no harm in him." He refrained from learning under Ya'la ibn al-Ashdaq because he heard him speak obscenely. He died in Safar 244 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.742}
