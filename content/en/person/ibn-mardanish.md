## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'd ibn Mardanish, ruler of eastern al-Andalus in the time of the weakness of the Almoravids and the rise of the Almohads. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.479}

## The struggle with the Almohads
He fought Abd al-Mu'min ibn Ali and sought the help of the Christians against the Almohads. Abd al-Mu'min and his commander Ibn Hamshak defeated him in the plain of Granada in a defeat in which many were killed, and in 557 AH Abd al-Mu'min took Granada out of his hands. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.479}
