## Summary
Taqi al-Din Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Futuhi al-Hanbali; from him and from his book *al-Muntaqa* many took additions to Ahmad's Musnad. Hafiz Ibn Hajar supplied supplements on it in his *al-Matalib al-'aliya*, and supplemented it greatly again in *al-Muntaha* and *al-Insaf*. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 388}
