## Overview
Hudhayfa ibn Badr al-Fazari, lord of the Banu Fazara of Ghatafan. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.15-16}

## His killing
Al-Baladhuri reports that the Banu Abs killed him on the day of al-Haba'a, and that they and the Banu Dhubyan were allies. His killing was one of the causes of the War of Dahis and al-Ghabra'. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.15-16}
