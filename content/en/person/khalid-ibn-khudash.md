## Summary
Abu'l-Haytham Khalid ibn Khudash ibn 'Ajlan al-Muhallabi, freedman of the Al al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra; of Basra's people, he settled in Baghdad and transmitted there from Malik ibn Anas, al-Mufaddal ibn 'Abd al-Rahman, Mahdi ibn Maymun and Hammad ibn Zayd; Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Dawraqi and Ibrahim ibn al-'Ala' al-Jawhari narrated from him; Abu Hatim called him truthful. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
