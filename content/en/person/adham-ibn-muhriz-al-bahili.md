## Overview
Adham ibn Muhriz al-Bahili, one of the five commanders whom Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad sent ahead of his army from al-Raqqa to fight the Tawwabin at Ayn al-Warda. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.88}
