## Overview
Ubayd al-Kayyis. Ibn al-Nadim reports that he was the first to practise sleight of hand (shi'badha) in Islam. Two books are attributed to him: the Book of Sleight of Hand and the Book of Trickery {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.434}.

The entry does not record his period or his life. It needs an additional source.
