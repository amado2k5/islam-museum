## Overview
Al-Husayn b. Sa'id b. Hamdan, brother of Abu Firas al-Hamdani; first of the Banu Hamdan to hold Aleppo, wresting it in Rajab 332 from Ahmad b. Sa'id al-Kulabi, the Ikhshid's officer; renowned for valour — Ibn al-Munhim says of him: "and when they saw him advance they cried: behold, the death-darts are beneath that one's banner." {cite:wafayat-ayan}
