## Overview
One of the two men said to have killed [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]. He struck him on the forehead with a club, and his companion Sa'd ibn Humran al-Muradi struck him with a sword on the strap of his shoulder. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.639}
