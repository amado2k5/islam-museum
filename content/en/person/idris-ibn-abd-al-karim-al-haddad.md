## Overview

Abu al-Hasan Idris ibn 'Abd al-Karim al-Haddad is one of the two transmitters of the own choice of [[person/khalaf-ibn-hisham|Khalaf ibn Hisham]], with [[person/ishaq-ibn-ibrahim-al-warraq|Ishaq al-Warraq]]. He died in 292 AH aged ninety-three. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177}

## His standing and lines of transmission

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an exact, careful and reliable imam who narrated from Khalaf both his riwaya and his choice, and records that al-Daraqutni, asked about him, said he was reliable and a degree above reliable. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177} Al-Qati'i, Ibn Buwayan, al-Mutawwi'i and al-Shatti read on him, and Ibn al-Jazari counts nine lines of transmission for Idris. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 201} Elsewhere he counts fifty-three lines of transmission from Khalaf through Idris in Khalaf's own narration of the reading of Hamza. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 171}
