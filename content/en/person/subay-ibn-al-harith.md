## Summary
Chief of Banu Malik of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]], nicknamed Dhu al-Khimar; he led Banu Malik's banner with his brother Ahmar in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn campaign]] — fighting grew fierce around their banner and about seventy of their men were killed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/80, 90-91}.

## Banu Malik's fight
Banu Malik fought desperately at Hunayn under the banner of Subay' and his brother Ahmar ibn al-Harith; seventy dead fell beneath their banner as they fought the Muslims around it, until they broke and followed on to Ta'if {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/90-91}.

## For young visitors
A chief of Thaqif nicknamed Dhu al-Khimar, who led his clan Banu Malik in the battle; they fought hard defending their banner until they withdrew to their city Ta'if.
