## Overview
A poet mentioned by al-Suyuti and al-Mas'udi. When [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] resolved to settle in Damascus, he composed verses warning him against leaving Iraq. Then he changed his mind and returned. He lamented him afterwards in a poem whose opening line is: "A caliph who did not attain what any man attained" {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 541,544}.

al-Mas'udi reports that he composed verses on the death of al-Muntasir, and that he was one of the Shi'a of the family of Abu Talib {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 519}.
