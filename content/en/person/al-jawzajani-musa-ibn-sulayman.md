## Overview
Abu Sulayman Musa ibn Sulayman al-Jawzajani, from Jawzajan, which the source describes as a great city in Khurasan. He was a transmitter of hadith. He died in 200 AH (816 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
