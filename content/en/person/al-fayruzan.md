## Overview
Al-Fayruzan, a Persian commander with Rustam in the affair of al-Qadisiyya. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.477}

Al-Tabari says that the Persians asked the two of them to agree, and that they ordered the women and concubines of Khosrow to be brought out. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.477}

His life and his fate need an additional source.
