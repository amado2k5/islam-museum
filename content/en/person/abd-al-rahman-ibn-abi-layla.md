## Summary
Abu Isa Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla al-Ansari, one of the senior Successors of Kufa; his father's name is disputed — Yusar or Dawud ibn Bilal ibn Uhtalha ibn al-Jalah al-Ansari — and his father Abu Layla narrated from the Prophet. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Hearing and transmission
He heard Ali ibn Abi Talib, Uthman ibn Affan and Abu Ayyub al-Ansari among others; it is also said he heard Umar, though the hadith masters do not confirm that audition. Al-Sha'bi, Mujahid, Abd al-Malik ibn Umayr and many others heard from him. He was present at the Camel carrying Ali's banner, and was born when eight years remained of Umar's caliphate. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Death
He was killed with Ibn al-Ash'ath — some say killed at Dujayl, some that he drowned in the river of Basra, some that he perished at al-Jajim in 83 AH in Ibn al-Ash'ath's battle, and it was also said in 81 or 82 AH. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
