## Overview
Abu Zaynab ibn Awf al-Azdi, whom al-Mas'udi mentions in the account of al-Walid ibn Uqba. He entered upon al-Walid with Jundab ibn Zuhayr al-Azdi and others. He showed his ring, and they testified before Uthman that he had drunk wine. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.630}

The data hold nothing else about his life beyond this report; anything more needs an additional source.
