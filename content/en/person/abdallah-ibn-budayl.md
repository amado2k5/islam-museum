## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Budayl ibn Warqa al-Khuza'i. Al-Baladhuri says that Umar sent him to Isfahan in 23 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}

## The conquests
He took Jay by peace after fighting, and fought in single combat with its marzban Fadusfan, who honoured him and made peace with him on the poll tax for the people of his land, and he kept his word. He took Judaism, prevailed over the land of Isfahan and its districts, and dealt with them over the land tax in about the manner of the people of Khuzistan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}
