## Overview
Fakhr al-Din Ibn al-Shaykh, son of Shaykh al-Shuyukh Sadr al-Din al-Juwayni, deputy of the sultanate; a dignified chief, a revered master of mind, judgment, cunning, courage and generosity. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 246-247}

## Prison and battles
The sultan jailed him in 640; he bore hardships and stayed in al-Husn three years, then was released, honoured and put over the army. He led the conquest of Ascalon and Tiberias in 644, was at Damietta the day the Franks besieged it in 647, and on the day of Mansura was stabbed — two blows came to his face and he fell. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 246-247}
