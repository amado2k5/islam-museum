## Overview
Nasir al-Din Nasir ibn Mahdi al-Alawi, vizier of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.399}.

## Death
He died in 617 AH. The caliph led the funeral prayer for him at the palace mosque, the people of the state attended, and he was buried at the shrine {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.399}.
