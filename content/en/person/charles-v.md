## Summary
Charles V — 'Sharl Kant' in al-Salabi's text — Holy Roman Emperor in [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]]'s reign {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201-202}.

## On the Ottoman front
The book counts his great war with [[person/francis-i-france|Francis I]] of France among the factors enabling the 1522 Rhodes campaign {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201}; he granted the [[group/knights-of-st-john|Knights of St John]] departing Rhodes rule over Malta {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 201-202}; and his reconciliation with Francis freed him to fight the Ottomans and save Vienna in 1529 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 202}.
