## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Yusuf ibn al-Zaki Abd al-Rahman al-Mizzi, Abu al-Hajjaj Jamal al-Din the hafiz: born in Rabi' al-Akhir 654 at al-Ma'qiliyya outside Aleppo, he had about a thousand teachers. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/457} He held the Dar al-Hadith al-Ashrafiyya and wrote Tahdhib al-Kamal. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/458} Ibn Hajar quotes al-Dhahabi calling him “the seal of the hadith masters”. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/460} He died on Saturday 12 Safar 742; his son-in-law Ibn Kathir says it was of plague; he was buried in the Sufi cemetery. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/461}
