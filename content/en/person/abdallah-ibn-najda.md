## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Najda, a man of the Banu Abi Shabib ibn Bajra, described by al-Mas'udi as a Kharijite. Al-Mas'udi says that Shabib came in upon him fleeing after the battle of Ibn Muljam, pulling the coat of mail from his chest. He told him what had happened, and Abd Allah went away and then came back with his sword and killed him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.702}
