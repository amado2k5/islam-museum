## Overview
Kukhan the Qara-Khitai, king of the Turks in Transoxiana; he dealt Sultan Sanjar a terrible defeat in 536 within a host of three hundred thousand horsemen — the Muslims suffered, a hundred thousand or more of Sanjar's army were killed, and his wife and daughter were captured. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 591, 594}

## His justice and death
Despite the greatness of his state he was just, granting no one an iqta' within his realm; he died in Rajab 537. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 591, 594}
