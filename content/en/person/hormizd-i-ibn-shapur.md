## Overview
Hormizd ibn Shapur, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Hormizd ibn Shapur". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.51}

Al-Tabari, at this point, gives what is said about the years of his rule and his affairs. The details of his government need an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] says Hormizd son of Shapur caught Mani, flayed him, stuffed him with straw and hung him on the gate of Jundaysabur, which is still called the Gate of Mani, hunted down and killed his followers, and reigned thirty years. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 47} The editor notes in a footnote that Mani was put to death under Bahram. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 47}
