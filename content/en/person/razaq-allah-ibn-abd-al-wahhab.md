## Overview
Razzaq Allah ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Tamimi, a Hanbali jurist and historian from Baghdad. The "Mawsu'at a'lam al-ulama wa al-udaba" reports that Ibn al-Jawzi and others wrote his biography {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.620}.

The encyclopedia gives his birth as 1010 CE (401 AH) and his death as 1095 CE (488 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.620} These two dates come from a single source and need an additional source.
