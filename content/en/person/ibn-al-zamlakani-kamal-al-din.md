## Overview
Kamal al-Din Ibn al-Zamlakani, grandfather of the famous qadi al-Kamal Ibn al-Zamlakani; qadi of Sarkhad, he heard Shaykh Baha' al-Din Ibn 'Asakir and a company, transmitted and gave fatwa; he died in 651 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 260}.
