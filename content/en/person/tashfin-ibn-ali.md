## Overview
Tashfin ibn Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin, son of [[person/ali-ibn-yusuf-al-murabiti|Ali ibn Yusuf]] and the last of the strong [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|Almoravid]] amirs {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1649}.

## Against the Almohads
He fought [[person/abd-al-mumin-ibn-ali|Abd al-Mu'min]] and the Almohads very fiercely, until they besieged him at Tlemcen. He fled to Oran, and the Almohads followed him and stormed the town. He died in the year 541 AH (1145 CE) {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1649}.

The Common Era date is converted from the Hijri year.
