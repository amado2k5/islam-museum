# Omar Khribin

## Summary

Omar Maher Khribin is a Syrian forward born in Damascus on 15 January 1994. In 2017 he became the first Syrian to win the Asian Footballer of the Year award, and he has won 70 caps for Syria, scoring 31 goals. {cite:wikipedia-omar-khribin-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-khribin-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-khribin}

## Career

He began with Al-Wahda of Damascus, played in Iraq for Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya and Al-Minaa, and joined Al-Hilal of Riyadh in 2017, winning the 2019 AFC Champions League with the club; after a loan at Pyramids in Egypt he moved to Al-Wahda of Abu Dhabi, where — apart from a loan at Emirates Club — he plays today. His name is also spelled Kharbin and Kh'rbin in English sources. {cite:wikipedia-omar-khribin-en} {cite:wikipedia-omar-khribin-ar} {cite:wikidata-omar-khribin}
