## Overview
Imad al-Din Zangi ibn Aq Sunqur, atabeg, lord of Mosul and Aleppo. He was killed in Rabi' al-Akhir 541 AH. His son Ghazi took Mosul, and his son [[person/nur-al-din-mahmud-ibn-zangi|Nur al-Din Mahmud]] took Aleppo and other places. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.608-609}

## Rise
He held the shihna (governorship) of Baghdad at the end of the reign of al-Mustazhir billah, and then was moved to Mosul. Sultan Mahmud handed over to him his son Farrukhshah to raise, and for this he was called atabeg. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.608-609}

## Character and lands
Ibn al-Imad describes him as a brave and valiant knight, of great might and great awe, and says that there was injustice and harshness in him. This is Ibn al-Imad's description. He traversed Mosul, Aleppo, Hama, Homs, Halabak, Edessa and Ma'arra. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.608-609}

## His death
Some of his mamluks killed him while he slept, and they fled to the fortress of Ja'bar, where its lord Ali ibn Malik al-Uqayli opened it to them. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.608-609}
