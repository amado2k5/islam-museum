## Overview
Jabala ibn Salim, a translator of Persian books into Arabic, according to Ibn al-Nadim. He translated the *Book of Rustam and Isfandiyar* and the *Book of Bahram Chubin*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.424}

## His translations
Ibn al-Nadim says that he translated from Persian books of campaigns and tales the *Book of Rustam and Isfandiyar* and the *Book of Bahram Jubin*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.424}

The second book's title appears in two forms in the museum's data (Bahram Chubin and Bahram Jubin), which needs review against the source.
