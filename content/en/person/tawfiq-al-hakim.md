## Overview
Tawfiq al-Hakim, an Egyptian writer, born in Alexandria in October 1898, and raised there and in Cairo. He was one of the pioneers of the Arabic theatre and novel {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
He died in 1987 CE, according to the same encyclopedia.
