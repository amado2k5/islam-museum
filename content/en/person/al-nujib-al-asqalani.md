## Summary
al-Nujib Abu al-'Asha'ir Firas ibn 'Ali ibn Zayd al-Kinani — of Ascalon, then of Damascus; the merchant-witness who transmitted from al-Khashshu'i and al-Qasim ibn 'Asakir; died 663 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 543}.

## Details
Under the dead of 663 Ibn al-'Imad enters "al-Nujib Abu al-'Asha'ir Firas ibn 'Ali ibn Zayd al-Kinani, of Ascalon then Damascus, the merchant and upright man," who transmitted from al-Khashshu'i, al-Qasim ibn 'Asakir and others {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 543}. He combined trade with hadith audition and transmission, one of the Ascalonites displaced after their town was ruined, who settled in Damascus and Egypt — his nisba preserving the name of a hometown its people no longer inhabited {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 543}. One manuscript reads al-Mu'addil ("the accredited witness") for al-'Adl ("the upright"), both being marks of a reliable transmitter {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 543}.
