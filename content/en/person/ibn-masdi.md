## Summary
Ibn Masdi the hafiz; he transmitted from Muhammad ibn 'Imad and very many others, collecting and compiling; Ibn Nasir al-Din said he was a hafiz and polymath of wide travels and discernment — Shi'ism was reported of him — who resided at Mecca and was murdered there treacherously; al-Dhahabi said he died at Mecca in Shawwal having made himself a mu'jam. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 543}
