## Overview
Nasir al-Din Khalji was sultan of [[place/malwa|Malwa]] from 1500 to 1511, with his capital at Mandu. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 134} Welch's catalogue calls him the "second patron" of the illustrated *Ni'matnama* made at Mandu, meaning that work on it had begun for another and continued for him. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 134}

## Patron of the book
The [[object/mandu-bustan-national-museum-delhi|Bustan of Sa'di]] now in the National Museum, New Delhi, was written for him by Shahsuvar al-Katib; his name and titles are in the shamsa of folio 1r, and an inspection date of AH 908 (1502-3) on the colophon page shows it was finished early in his reign. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 134} Both manuscripts were painted by Haji Mahmud at Mandu, and for Welch the echoes of Bihzad in them show the level of artistic appreciation at his court. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 134}
