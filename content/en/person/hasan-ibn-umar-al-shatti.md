## Overview
Hasan ibn Umar ibn Ma'ruf al-Shatti was a Hanbali jurist and a specialist in the law of inheritance shares (faradi) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}. He was of Baghdadi origin, born and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}. Al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 1205 AH (1790 CE) and 1274 AH (1858 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.

## His works
He has writings, among them "Mukhtasar Sharh Aqidat al-Saffarini", printed, and epistles on "al-Basmala al-Sharifa", "Faskh al-Nikah" and "al-Taqlid wa-al-Talfiq", printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 174}.
