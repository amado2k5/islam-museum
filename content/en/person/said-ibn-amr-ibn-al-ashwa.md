## Overview
Said ibn Amr ibn al-Ashwa al-Hamdani al-Kufi, a judge. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17710} He transmitted from Shurayh ibn Hani', al-Sha'bi, Abu Burda ibn Abi Musa and others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17710} Al-Thawri, Salama ibn Kuhayl and others transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17710} Al-Nasa'i said there was no harm in him, and Ibn Hibban mentioned him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17710} The source cited is Tahdhib al-tahdhib. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17710} The date in the heading is not clear in the scan and is not stated.
