## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Salim al-Saffarini, Shams al-Din, Abu al-Awn, was a scholar of hadith, the principles of jurisprudence and letters, and a verifier; the entry gives his years as 1114 to 1188 AH, 1702 to 1774 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His life
He was born in Saffarin, a village of Nablus, travelled to Damascus and studied with its scholars, then returned to Nablus, where he taught, gave fatwas and died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His works
Among his books al-Zirikli names "al-Darari al-Masnu'at fi Ikhtisar al-Mawdu'at", in manuscript with Yusuf Zakhur in Damascus; "Kashf al-Litham, a commentary on Umdat al-Ahkam", in manuscript in the Zahiriyya in Damascus, whose copy carries an authorisation in his own hand listing his works up to 1169 AH; "al-Qawl al-Ali li-Sharh Athar al-Imam Ali", in manuscript in Rabat; and "al-Milah al-Gharamiyya", a commentary on the poem "Gharami Sahih", in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. Printed are "Ghidha al-Albab, a commentary on Manzumat al-Adab", in two parts, and "Lawa'ih al-Anwar al-Bahiyya wa Sawati' al-Asrar al-Athariyya al-Mudi'a fi Aqd Ahl al-Firqa al-Mardiyya", in two parts, a commentary on his own poem on the creed of the salaf {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. The entry also lists "Tahbir Ulufa fi Sirat al-Mustafa", "al-Tahqiq fi Butlan al-Talfiq", scattered fatwas, some of a quire or less, and a manuscript thabat of his chains of transmission, in the collection numbered 1374 Kattani in the Rabat library {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
