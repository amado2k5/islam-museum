## Summary
A man of Banu Layth who meant to assassinate the Prophet while he circled the Ka'ba during the conquest of Mecca; the Prophet called him by name and laid his hand on his chest — his heart grew calm and he became Muslim on the spot {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/59-60}.

## His affair at the tawaf
Ibn Hisham relates that Fadala ibn Umayr al-Laythi meant to kill the Prophet as he circled the House in the conquest year; when he drew near the Prophet said: "Fadala?" He said: yes, Fadala, Messenger of God. He said: "what were you telling yourself?" He said: nothing — I was remembering God. The Prophet laughed, then said: "ask God's forgiveness", laid his hand on his chest, and his heart grew calm {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/59-60}.

Fadala used to say afterwards: "by God, he had not lifted his hand from my chest before nothing of God's creation was dearer to me than him"; he returned to his family a believing Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/60}.

## For young visitors
A man thought of harming the Prophet as he circled the Ka'ba; the Prophet surprised him by calling him by name with kindness and praying for him — and all anger left his heart, making him among his most devoted companions.
