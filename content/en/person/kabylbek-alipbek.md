## Summary
Kabylbek Alipbek is a Kazakhstani online preacher who headed the five leading online preachers of Kazakhstan in a peer-reviewed study published in 2025 in Millah: Journal of Religious Studies: the researchers counted 986 thousand followers on his Instagram account @kabylbek.ustaz as of 31 January 2025, the largest audience of the five and more than the official account of Kazakhstan's Spiritual Administration of Muslims. {cite:millah-kazakh-preachers-2025} These are dated platform figures measured by the researchers, not museum-verified facts.

## His work and style
The study describes the leading five as imams of the sanctioned clergy, holding advanced theological credentials and active in mosques, three of them connected with the country's Council of Islamic Scholars. {cite:millah-kazakh-preachers-2025} For Kabylbek, the researchers tracked a shift in his content: he began diversifying his official Instagram account with videos of nature, his travels around the world and conversations with business leaders, and has moved strategically toward a new role as a family psychologist and business coach, with a substantial portion of his content focused on family dynamics and the rest on "business" discussions in which he acts as a business mentor. {cite:millah-kazakh-preachers-2025}

## Influence and audience
Kabylbek's audience is chiefly Kazakhstani, and his Instagram following is the largest of the country's online preachers by the study's January 2025 measurement; the researchers also noted that posts from his private life and travels draw higher views than his professional content. {cite:millah-kazakh-preachers-2025}
