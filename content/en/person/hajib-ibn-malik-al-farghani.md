## Overview
Hajib ibn Malik ibn Arkin, the trustworthy muhaddith Abu'l-'Abbas al-Darir al-Farghani the Turk, resident of Damascus; he heard Abu 'Umar al-Duri, 'Ali ibn Harb, Ibn 'Abd al-Hakam and their peers, while al-Tabarani, Abu'l-Shaykh, Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar and others narrated from him. Al-Khatib declared him trustworthy and al-Daraqutni said "no harm in him." He died in 306 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1348}
