## Overview
Abu Muwayhiba, a freedman of the Prophet. The Prophet woke him in the middle of the night and said: "I have been ordered to ask forgiveness for the people of al-Baqi'. Come with me." When he stood among the graves, he said: "Peace be upon you, people of the graves. Trials have come upon you like pieces of the dark night." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/292}

## The choice
Then he turned to Abu Muwayhiba and said: "I have been given the keys of the treasuries of this world and eternity in it, then Paradise. I have been given the choice between that and meeting my Lord and Paradise." Abu Muwayhiba said: "May my father and mother be your ransom, take the keys of this world." He replied: "No, by God, Abu Muwayhiba. I have chosen the meeting with my Lord and Paradise." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/292} See [[event/death-of-the-prophet|the death of the Prophet]].

## Notes for researchers
- The report comes from Ibn Ishaq through the chain of Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As from Abu Muwayhiba. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/291}
