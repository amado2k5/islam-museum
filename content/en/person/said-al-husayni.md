Sa'id al-Husayni (1878–1945) was a Palestinian politician from Jerusalem who became the city's mayor in 1905 and worked to block Jewish immigration and land sales to the Zionist movement.{cite:wikipedia-said-al-husayni-ar} Elected for Jerusalem to the Ottoman parliament in 1908 and again in 1914, he followed the Arab Revolt in its final stages and entered Damascus with the forces of Sharif Husayn.{cite:wikipedia-said-al-husayni-ar}

In March 1920 al-Husayni became the first foreign minister of the Arab government at Damascus under King Faysal I, charged with founding a Syrian foreign ministry from nothing.{cite:wikipedia-said-al-husayni-ar} After the fall of the Faysali government he retired from politics in Jerusalem, where he died in 1945.{cite:wikipedia-said-al-husayni-ar}{cite:wikidata-said-al-husayni}
