## Summary

Erekle I (1642-1709), known by his Muslim name Nazar Ali Khan and at the Russian court as Tsarevich Nicholas Davidovich, was a prince of the Georgian Bagrationi dynasty who reigned as king of Kartli (1688-1703) and then of Kakheti (1703-1709) under Safavid protection. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} He was the son of Prince David and a grandson of King Teimuraz I of Kakheti. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} Raised in Moscow from childhood, he accepted Islam after the shah made it the condition of his receiving a crown, becoming one of the most prominent Muslim kings of Georgia in the Safavid period. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ru}

## Upbringing in Moscow

Erekle was born in 1642 according to the Georgian and Arabic articles, while the English article and the Wikidata item give 1643 and the Russian article gives 1637 or 1642. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ka} {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ar} {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} {cite:wikidata-erekle-i} {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ru} His grandfather Teimuraz I had been exiled and had lost the throne of Kakheti, and Erekle was raised from 1653 or 1654 at the Moscow court under the name Tsarevich Nicholas Davidovich. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} In 1671 he served as best man at the wedding of Tsar Alexis, a place of honour showing his standing at the Russian court. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} In 1662 he attempted to recover Kakheti but failed against his rival Archil. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en}

## Return and conversion to Islam

Erekle returned to the Caucasus in 1674 hoping for the throne of Kartli or Kakheti with Persian support. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} Shah Suleiman demanded that he change his faith as the price of a crown, and he at first refused to become a Muslim, citing the oath of allegiance his ancestor Alexander II of Kakheti had given to Tsar Feodor I of Russia. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} After long hesitation he accepted Islam and took the name Nazar Ali Khan; the English article dates his conversion to 1676. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ka} {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} The Russian article likewise relates that after hesitation he accepted Islam and received the name Nazar Ali Khan before spending fourteen years at the shah's court in Isfahan. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ru}

## King of Kartli

The shah installed him as king of Kartli in 1688 with Persian troops, while his predecessor George XI was driven into exile. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} He fought a long civil war against George XI, who returned from exile, and in 1691 George blockaded Tbilisi without permanently recovering his throne. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} In his reign the city administration of Tbilisi was organised and charters were issued regulating its craft guilds. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ka}

## King of Kakheti and death

In 1703 the shah transferred him to rule Kakheti and appointed him commander of the shah's guard. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-en} He then handed Kakheti to his son David II, known as Imam Quli Khan, who had likewise entered Islam. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ka} According to the Russian article, Erekle married Ana Cholokashvili in 1677. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ru} He died at Isfahan in 1709. {cite:wikipedia-erekle-i-ru}
