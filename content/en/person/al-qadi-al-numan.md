## Overview
Abu Hanifa al-Nu'man b. Abi 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Mansur b. Ahmad b. Hayyun, qadi al-Nu'man the da'i; one of the imams of Isma'ili fiqh — qadi of al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah the Fatimid and his da'i; a Maliki who moved to the Imami madhhab; he kept close to al-Mu'izz in Ifriqiya and entered Egypt with him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
Many works, among them Da'a'im al-Islam, Iftitah al-da'wa, Ikhtilaf usul al-madhahib and Kitab al-akhbar in fiqh; al-Musabbihi in his history describes him with knowledge, fiqh, religion and nobility. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
