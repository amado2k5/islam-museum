## Overview
A prophet and the son of a prophet. In al-Bukhari the Prophet said: "The generous son of the generous son of the generous son of the generous: Yusuf ibn Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}. So he is the son of [[person/yaqub|Ya'qub]].

In al-Bukhari the Prophet also said: "Had I remained in prison as long as Yusuf remained, and then the summoner came to me, I would have answered him" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3387}. And among his supplications against Mudar: "Make them years like the years of Yusuf" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3386}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 06)

The Sheikh summarises Yusuf's ordeal: a dream that stirs envy, his father's counsel not to tell it, then his brothers' envy and throwing him into the well, his sale and becoming a slave in Egypt, and his unjust imprisonment for several years; he says that when God wishes to rescue a servant He brings causes that never crossed his mind {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:19:00}.

He says that when God wishes to settle someone's debt, guide a sinner or soften the heart of a relative toward him, He brings a cause you never thought of, and then mentions that the king slept in his bedroom and in the morning told his council that he had seen seven fat cows eaten by seven lean ones, and seven green ears of grain (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:19:30}.

He mentions that the king's advisers said it was a muddle of dreams and that they had no knowledge of interpreting it, and the Sheikh says that here begins the key to resolving the problem of Yusuf, forgotten in prison, and that it was God who made the king see the dream so as to bring a man out of detention through it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:20:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh gathers Yusuf's hardships into three: the well, slavery and prison. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:01:00} He holds that when Ya'qub forbade him to tell his dream to his brothers, he attributed the plotting to Satan and did not say his brothers envied him, so as not to set his tender heart against them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:02:30} He holds that the brothers came to their father at nightfall weeping because the night hides false tears, and that their lie exposed itself, since they said what their father had feared; he says 'stupidity is one of God's soldiers'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:04:00} Then he mentions the caravan that picked him up and sold him for a paltry price, digressing that people today are sold like goods, giving professional footballers and fashion models as examples; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:06:00} Then the attempted seduction by the governor's wife, then prison, which he calls 'the first remand in custody in history', without charge, until the king saw his dream; Yusuf came out, took charge of Egypt's storehouses and managed the famine. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep10 00:10:00}
The Sheikh relates that when Yusuf's brothers threw him into the well his hand clung to its edge and they beat his hand so that he would fall, and when he fell Jibril's palm was under his feet (needs_source: the detail as the Sheikh tells it); then the situation was reversed and they came before him when he was the Aziz of Egypt saying 'distress has touched us and our family... and give us charity'; he holds that their hands, once raised high in wrongdoing, became the lower hand, and that wrongdoing is darkness on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep28 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep28 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep28 00:09:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
Omar Abdelkafy advises the worried to read surat Yusuf, and recounts what Yusuf went through: his brothers' envy and his being cast into the well, then prison, then rule over Egypt and the coming of his brothers and father. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:20:30} He relates that Yusuf told his fellow prisoner to mention him to his master and so stayed some years in prison, holds that God trained him through trial, and describes him as managing the famine as 'crisis management' is taught today. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:11:30}
