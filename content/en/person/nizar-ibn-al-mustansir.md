## Overview
Nizar b. al-Mustansir — brother of al-Musta'li bi'llah al-'Ubaydi, to whom the Nizari da'wa of Alamut is traced; he fled in his brother's days to Alexandria, where its security chief swore to him, backed by its qadi Ibn 'Ammar and its governor Iftikin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 402}

## His end
Al-Afdal, amir al-juyush, besieged them; Iftikin sallied out and was routed, then on a second siege al-Afdal prevailed and returned to Cairo with Iftikin and Nizar — Iftikin was beheaded, and over Nizar a wall was built up so that he perished, at the opening of 495. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 402}
