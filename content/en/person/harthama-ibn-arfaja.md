## Overview
Harthama ibn Arfaja al-Bariqi, a commander of the Rashidun period. Al-Baladhuri in "The Conquests of the Lands" reports that he was the first to lay out [[place/mosul|Mosul]], settled the Arabs in it, and made it a garrison city, and that Umar appointed him over it after the removal of Utba ibn Farqad. In Ibn Duraid the name appears as "Arfaja ibn Harthama". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The founding of Mosul as a garrison city
Harthama found in Mosul a fortress, a church of the Christians, a few houses and a quarter of the Jews. He settled the Arabs in their houses, laid out the plots for them, and built the congregational mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} It is also said that he first settled al-Hadhitha and laid it out as a garrison city before Mosul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He took part in [[event/conquest-of-mosul|the conquest of Mosul]].
