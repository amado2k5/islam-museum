## Overview
Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, the father of Ya'qub. Al-Bukhari mentions him in the chain "Yusuf ibn Ya'qub ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim". {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3382}

He is the son of [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]]. The data classify him as a prophetic figure mentioned in the sacred text. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
