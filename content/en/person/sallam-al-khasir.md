## Overview
Sallam al-Khasir, a poet who mourned the Barmakids in a verse saying that the seas of generosity dried up after them {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.367}.

## What was said of him
Ibn al-Nadim counts him among the poets who professed Islam while concealing heresy (zandaqa). This judgement is attributed to Ibn al-Nadim and needs an additional source {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.473}.
