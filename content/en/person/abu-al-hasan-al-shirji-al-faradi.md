## Overview
Abu al-Hasan al-Shirji al-Faradi, the reckoner and expert in inheritance, who took the science of inheritance from [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-labban-al-basri|Ibn al-Laban]]. Al-Shirazi mentions him as "our shaykh". It is related from Ibn al-Laban that there is no expert in inheritance on earth except his companions, or the companions of his companions, or someone who knows nothing well. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63} Al-Shirazi does not mention his death.
