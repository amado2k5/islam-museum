## Overview
Mansur ibn Qaratikin, a Samanid commander. Amir Nuh ibn Nasr ordered him, and he marched from Nishapur to al-Rayy in Safar 340 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364}

## Al-Rayy
He entered al-Rayy and seized it, and Ali ibn Kama, deputy of Rukn al-Dawla, evacuated it to Isfahan. He distributed the armies through the lands, and they took the lands of the Jibal as far as Qarmisin. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364}
