## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Karim, commander of the army at Mahdiyya in the Almohad state. Ibn al-Athir describes him as a famous brave man whose blows against the Arabs were great. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.259-260}

## Mahdiyya
He disputed with the vizier Abu Sa'id over the families and wealth of the Banu Awf. He arrested the governor of Mahdiyya and overcame the city, then released him for a ransom of twelve thousand dinars. He made an alliance with [[person/ali-ibn-ghaniya|Ibn Ghaniya]]. He was among those who took part in [[event/mahdiyya-revolt-594|the revolt of Mahdiyya]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.259-260}

When Muhammad al-Nasir came to power and sent armies by land and sea, Ibn Abd al-Karim said that he was obedient to the Commander of the Faithful and would surrender the city only to one whom he sent, and he returned to obedience. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.259-260}
