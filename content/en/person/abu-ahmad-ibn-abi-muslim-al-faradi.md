## Overview
Abu Ahmad ibn Abi Muslim al-Faradi, who took the science of inheritance from [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-labban-al-basri|Ibn al-Laban al-Faradi]], and was the teacher of Shaykh Abu Hamid al-Isfara'ini in inheritance. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 63}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his death. In one edition and in the note of al-Subki he appears as "Ahmad" without a kunya.
