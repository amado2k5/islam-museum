## Summary
[[person/umm-sad-bint-sad-ibn-al-rabi|Umm Sa'd]] bint [[person/sad-ibn-al-rabi|Sa'd ibn al-Rabi']] al-Ansariyya {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}. Ibn Sa'd said: her mother is Khallada bint Anas ibn Sinan of Banu Sa'ida; she bore her months after Sa'd was killed {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}.

## Her upbringing
Abu Dawud narrates from Dawud ibn al-Husayn, who said: I used to recite to Umm Sa'd bint Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' with her grandson Musa ibn Sa'd — she was an orphan in Abu Bakr al-Siddiq's care {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}.

## The inheritance
Ibn Sa'd narrates from al-Waqidi that she said: Zayd ibn Thabit came in to me and said: "if you want to speak about your inheritance from your father, speak — for Umar has today granted the unborn child inheritance" — her father had been killed on the day of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] while she was yet unborn {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}.

## Her marriage and children
[[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]] married her, and she bore him Kharija, Sa'd, Uthman, Sulayman and Umm Zayd {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}. Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit related from her something of Sa'd ibn al-Rabi's virtues from Abu Bakr {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}.

## Disagreement on Kharija's mother's name
Ibn Sa'd said in Kharija's entry: his mother is Umm Sa'd Jamila bint Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' — "so he said" — and Ibn Hajar points out that what is in Umm al-Ala''s entry contradicts this {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 2002}.
