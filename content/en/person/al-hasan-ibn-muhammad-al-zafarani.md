## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn al-Sabbah al-Za'farani al-Baghdadi, one of the Baghdadi companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.53}

## His life
He was one of the pupils of al-Shafi'i. He died in 260 AH (about 874 CE; the conversion is derived). The Za'farani Lane in Baghdad, where there is a mosque, is named after him. The author says it is the mosque where he used to teach. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.53}

## In Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra — Taj al-Din al-Subki (al-Husayniyya edition)
Al-Hasan b. Muhammad b. al-Sabbah al-Baghdadi, Abu 'Ali al-Za'farani (154–260 AH) — jurist, hadith scholar and master of eloquence, counted among the transmitters of al-Shafi'i's "old" corpus. He was the reader in al-Shafi'i's circle: when the imam arrived in Baghdad none dared read to him but al-Za'farani, who read him all the books save two. The Six-save-Muslim transmitted from him, and Baghdad's Darb al-Za'faran took its name from him. He died in Ramadan 260 {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.1 p.250-258}.
