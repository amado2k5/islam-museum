## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn al-Sabbah al-Za'farani al-Baghdadi, one of the Baghdadi companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.53}

## His life
He was one of the pupils of al-Shafi'i. He died in 260 AH (about 874 CE; the conversion is derived). The Za'farani Lane in Baghdad, where there is a mosque, is named after him. The author says it is the mosque where he used to teach. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.53}
