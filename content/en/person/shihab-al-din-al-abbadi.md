## Summary
Shihab al-Din al-'Abbadi, Hanafi jurist, studied fiqh under al-Siraj al-Hindi, distinguished himself, taught and kept working; he then married into the Qayiji family, deputized in the judgeship, signed on the bench and taught at the Madrasa al-Zahir Hasan. He used to gather the students and treat them well; he had a rift with al-Salimi and another with al-Malik al-Zahir. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 11-12}
