## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Zanjani, Izz al-Din, a Shafi'i jurist and Sufi, alive in 655 AH; his works include 'The Essence of al-Aziz in the Branches of the Shafi'is' and a work on morphology. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9963}
