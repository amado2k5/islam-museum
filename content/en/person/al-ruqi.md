## Overview
Maymun ibn Mihran al-Raqqi, Abu Ayyub, was a jurist and judge. The entry dates his birth to 37 AH, equal to 657 CE, and his death to 117 AH, equal to 735 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 340}.

## Life
He was the freedman of a woman at Kufa, who set him free, and he grew up there. He then settled at Raqqa, in the Euphrates Jazira, and became the scholar and the leading man of the Jazira {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 340}.

## Offices
Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz employed him over the land tax and the judiciary of the Jazira. He was with the vanguard of the Syrian army under Mu'awiya ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik when it crossed the sea to raid Cyprus in 108 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 340}.

## What is said of him
Al-Zirikli writes in the entry that he was reliable in hadith and much given to worship {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 340}.

## Death
He died in 117 AH, equal to 735 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 340}.
