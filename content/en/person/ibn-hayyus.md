## Summary
Abu'l-Fityan Muhammad ibn Sultan ibn Muhammad ibn Hayyus al-Ghanawi, surnamed Mustafa al-Dawla; he was called 'the amir' because his father was one of the Arab amirs; one of the handsome Syrian poets and foremost masters, with a large diwan; he met several of Aleppo's Mirdasid kings and praised them — his most famous ode is his ra'iyya on Nasr ibn Salih ibn Mirdas, consoling him for his father while praising him; he was the shaykh of the poet Ibn al-Khayyat. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
