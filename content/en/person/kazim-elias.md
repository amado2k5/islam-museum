## Summary
Dato' Ustaz Mohammad Kazim bin Elias (born 28 November 1972), better known as Ustaz Kazim Elias, is a Malaysian independent preacher, writer, lecturer and Islamic consultant. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-en} He is the founder and principal of Pusat Pendidikan Al Barakah, an Islamic education centre headquartered at Kampung Manjoi, Ipoh, in the state of Perak, which serves as the base of his preaching. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms}

## Early life and education
He was born at Kampung Paya, Batu Kurau, in Mukim Selama, Perak. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms} {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-en} He was educated at the Maahad Tahfiz Al Quran & Qiraat of the Perak State Mosque, where he took his tahfiz diploma. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms}

## Preaching career
His lectures use a relaxed, humorous approach, and their constant themes are family life and motivation, which has drawn listeners from across Malaysian society. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms} He is a familiar presence on Malaysian television and radio, among it the programmes Forum Perdana Ehwal Islam and Tanyalah Ustaz and the station Perak FM. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms} The English article names "Allah Sedang Menguji Kita" ("God Is Testing Us") as his notable work. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-en}

In 2015 he received two Malaysian honours: Knight Commander of the Order of the Territorial Crown (PMW), carrying the title Datuk, and Knight Commander of the Order of the Perak State Crown (DPMP), carrying the title Dato'. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-en}

## Projects and initiatives
The Al Barakah Education Centre in Ipoh is his own foundation and the base from which he preaches and teaches; the Malay article records him as its founder and principal. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-ms}

## Positions and reported matters
This section sets out matters as reported in the sources. It is not a verdict by the museum.

**Party politics.** In December 2013 the national news agency Bernama reported that he had quit PAS, saying he could not stand the party's revile remarks and slander, and the New Straits Times reported an Umno figure's view that he could help Umno in Islamic matters. {cite:wikipedia-kazim-elias-en} Astro Awani reported that in November 2022 he withdrew as the Barisan Nasional parliamentary candidate for Larut, that he was replaced by Mohd Shafiq Fhadly Mahmud, and that he said he had withdrawn for the sake of his family. {cite:astro-awani-kazim-elias}

**A denied allegation.** Malaysian press reporting, including Astro Awani's, recorded that he denied a molestation allegation connected to his tahfiz school. {cite:astro-awani-kazim-elias} The allegation is press-reported and denied by him; the museum states it as neither established nor disproven, and reports no outcome as fact. {cite:astro-awani-kazim-elias}
