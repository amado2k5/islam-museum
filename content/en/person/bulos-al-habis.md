## Summary
Bulus al-Habis, the monk-katib; he dwelt in a cave on Mount Helwan near Cairo — it was said he chanced upon al-Hakim's treasure and shared it with the poor and respectable of every community, till his fame spread; he answered the confiscators for a vast sum, six hundred thousand dinars, apart from what he gave the poor. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His interrogation
The sultan summoned him, treated him kindly and asked for the money; he fenced and evaded, and when he wearied the sultan he was pressed and tortured until he died — confessing nothing. He was carried dead from the citadel and cast at the Qarafa gate; he had eaten nothing of that money nor worn it, and nothing of it appeared in his estate. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
