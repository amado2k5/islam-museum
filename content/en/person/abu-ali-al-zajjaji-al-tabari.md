## Overview
Abu Ali al-Zajjaji al-Tabari, one of the companions of [[person/abu-al-abbas-ibn-al-qass-al-tabari|Abu al-Abbas ibn al-Qass]]. He wrote "Ziyadat al-Miftah", and the jurists of Amul took jurisprudence from him; the judge Abu al-Tayyib, the teacher of al-Shirazi, studied with him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}

Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death.
