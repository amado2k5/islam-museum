## Overview
Al-Zubayr ibn Abd al-Muttalib, an uncle of the Prophet and one of the sons of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim]] whom al-Mubarakfuri's "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" counts in its section on the Prophet's family. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## Counting the sons of Abd al-Muttalib
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts ten sons for Abd al-Muttalib: al-Harith, al-Zubayr, Abu Talib, Abd Allah, Hamza, Abu Lahab, al-Ghaydaq, al-Muqawwim, Dirar and al-Abbas. It mentions other views that make them eleven by adding Qutham, or thirteen by adding Abd al-Ka'ba and Hajl. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.8-9} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/56-66}. He is thus a brother of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah]], the Prophet's father. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}

## Later in the book
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum returns to al-Zubayr ibn Abd al-Muttalib in the section on youth, when it mentions the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Pact of al-Fudul]]; his biography will be completed there.

## What is not recorded here
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum says nothing here of his life, his religion or his death. Only what the book says is recorded here; what the biographical and genealogical books say needs a source to be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
Abd al-Muttalib, the grandfather of the Prophet, had many sons and daughters, and al-Zubayr was one of them, so he was one of the uncles and aunts of the Prophet.

## On the Pact of al-Fudul (Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum p. 60)
The book says that, when the wronged man of Zabid climbed Mount Abu Qubays and called out his grievance, al-Zubayr ibn Abd al-Muttalib took up the matter and said that this affair would not be left alone. The Banu Hashim, the Banu al-Muttalib, Asad, Zuhra and Taym gathered in the house of [[person/abdullah-ibn-judan|Abd Allah ibn Judan]], and they concluded the pact and took from [[person/al-as-ibn-wail|al-As ibn Wa'il]] the right of the wronged man of Zabid. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.30-31}
