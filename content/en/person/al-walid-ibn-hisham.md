## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Hisham ibn al-Mughira, who advised [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] when he consulted about the register. He told him that he had come to Syria and seen its kings had written down a register and enrolled troops, and Umar took his advice. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
