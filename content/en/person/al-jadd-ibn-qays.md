## Summary
Of Banu Salama; the Prophet asked him about fighting Banu al-Asfar and he begged leave to stay behind for fear of temptation. Ibn Ishaq says a verse of Sura al-Tawba descended about him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/159}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him one of Banu Salama, saying the Prophet asked him as he equipped himself: are you for fighting Banu al-Asfar this year? {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/159} He begged leave to stay behind, fearing temptation by the women of Banu al-Asfar; the Prophet turned from him and gave him leave, and Ibn Ishaq says al-Tawba 49 descended about him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/159-160}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: of Banu Salama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/159}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The judgement on his intent comes from Ibn Ishaq's reading, not from the Prophet's words.

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"
In his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] takes al-Jadd ibn Qays as his example of "the intemperate leader" among the types of bad leadership, and then of "the obstinate follower": low leadership ability, high challenge, low effectiveness. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep03 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:30:30} He relates that the Prophet made Bishr ibn al-Bara' ibn Ma'rur chief of Banu Salima in his place. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:09:00} That al-Jadd alone did not take the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|Pledge of Ridwan]], hiding behind a red camel, as he relates from Jabir. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:24:30} And that he excused himself from the campaign to [[event/expedition-to-tabuk|Tabuk]] by his fear of being tempted by Byzantine women, after which a verse of al-Tawba came down ([[passage/quran-9-49|al-Tawba 9:49]]). {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:30:00} Al-Suwaidan says it is not permitted to call a living person a hypocrite, since judging hearts belonged to revelation. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep08 00:10:00}
