## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn Hamid ibn Ali ibn Marwan al-Baghdadi, imam of the Hanbalis in his time, their tutor and teacher, and master of Judge Abu Ya'la {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 412}. His works include al-Jami' fi al-madhhab and Sharh al-Kharaqi {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 412}. He died in 403 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 412}.
