## Husam al-Din al-Abiwardi

Husam al-Din Hasan ibn ʿAli al-Abiwardi, a Shafiʿi resident of Mecca. The nisba refers to Abiward, a town in Khurasan. He studied with al-Saʿd al-Taftazani and others, excelled in the rational sciences, and went to Yemen, where he met al-Nasir and was given charge of teaching in some colleges in Taʿizz, but death overtook him there. He wrote "Rabiʿ al-Janan" on rhetoric and eloquence {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 129}.
