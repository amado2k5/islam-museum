## Overview
Hayyan ibn Shurayh, the agent of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz over Egypt. His secretary al-Salt ibn Abi Asim read to him Umar's letter, in which it was stated that Egypt had been conquered by force without any treaty or covenant ([[event/conquest-of-egypt|the conquest of Egypt]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Salabi reports that Umar wrote to him to remit the poll tax for those of the protected people who had embraced Islam, citing verses of the chapter of Repentance (al-Tawba). {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.203}
