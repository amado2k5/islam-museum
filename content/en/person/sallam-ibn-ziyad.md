## Overview
Sallam ibn Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan, the son of [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan]]. He was governor of Khurasan for Mu'awiya and Yazid, and the Sallam river in Basra is attributed to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## What al-Baladhuri reports
al-Baladhuri reports that Yazid appointed him over Khurasan, and that the people of Khwarazm made peace with him for four hundred thousand. He crossed the river with his wife, who was the first Arab woman to cross it. Then Ibn al-Zubayr fined him four million dirhams and imprisoned him, but he dug through the prison wall. Abd al-Malik appointed him over Khurasan, and he died in Basra {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
