## Overview
[[person/ukasha-ibn-mihsan|Ukasha ibn Mihsan al-Asadi]], an ally of the Banu Abd Shams among the Companions, who was killed at Buzakha in the caliphate of Abu Bakr {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133}.

## Buzakha
[[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] sent him ahead to Buzakha, together with [[person/thabit-ibn-aqram|Thabit ibn Aqram al-Balawi]], an ally of the Ansar. Hubal ibn Khuwaylid met them and killed them both. Then [[person/tulayha-ibn-khuwaylid|Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid]] came out, with his brother Salama, and they met Ukasha and Thabit and killed them. Tulayha composed his well-known verse about them {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133}.

Ukasha took part in [[event/battle-of-buzakha|the battle of Buzakha]]. His year of death is derived from the same report and is approximate, around 11 AH.
