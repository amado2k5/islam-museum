## Summary
Abu Tahir Yahya ibn Tamim ibn al-Mu'izz ibn Badis al-Himyari al-Sanhaji, lord of Ifriqiya and its dependencies. His rule at al-Mahdiyya came in succession to his father Tamim on Friday, four nights remaining of Dhu'l-Hijja 497; his age at independence was forty-three years, six months and twenty days. In *al-Jam' wa'l-bayan fi akhbar al-Qayrawan*, by his nephew 'Izz al-Din 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Shaddad, it is told that his father brought him a book of prophecies containing "al-Malik al-Maghdur." He left thirty sons, was buried in the palace, then moved to Qasr al-Sayyida at al-Mushtiya. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
