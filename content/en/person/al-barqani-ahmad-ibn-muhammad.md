## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ghalib al-Khwarazmi, a Shafi'i jurist. He is attributed to Barqan, a village in the district of Kath in Khwarazm, and so is known as al-Barqani. He was born in 336 AH (948 CE) and died in 425 AH (1034 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is classed among the jurists and hadith scholars. The dates come from one source and need a second.
