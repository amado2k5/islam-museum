## Overview
Sadr al-Din Muhammad ibn al-Wazzan, head of the Shafi'is in Rayy. He rose to great standing with the Khwarazm Shah Ala' al-Din Takish {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.263}.

## His death
Ibn al-Athir reports that the "Batiniyya", meaning the Isma'ilis in his wording, killed him during Takish's siege of the fortress of Alamut in the year 595 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.263}.

## Notes for researchers
- The term used for the Isma'ilis is Ibn al-Athir's own wording, not the museum's. The report needs an additional source.
