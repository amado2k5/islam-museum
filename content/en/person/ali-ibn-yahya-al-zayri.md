## Summary
'Ali b. Yahya b. Tamim al-Sanhaji al-Zayri, lord of Ifriqiya; his father appointed him over Sfax, and when his father died the notables of his realm joined in a letter summoning him to come quickly — he arrived at noon on the second day of the feast and sat for the people on the morning of Friday the thirteenth of Dhu'l-Hijja, and they saluted him with the emirate. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His rule
His affairs ran in good order until he died on a Tuesday, having delegated the succession to his son al-Hasan b. 'Ali. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
