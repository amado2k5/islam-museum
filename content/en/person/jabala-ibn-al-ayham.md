## Overview
Jabala ibn al-Ayham: last of the Ghassanid kings, agents of Byzantium over the Arabs of Syria; he yielded to Islam in Umar's reign.

## Life
The Ghassanids kept ruling Syria in succession as agents of the kings of Byzantium until the battle of Yarmuk in 13 AH, when their last king Jabala ibn al-Ayham yielded to Islam in the reign of Umar ibn al-Khattab {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

## For young visitors
Jabala is the last king of Ghassan; he entered Islam after the defeat at Yarmuk, in the caliph Umar's time.
