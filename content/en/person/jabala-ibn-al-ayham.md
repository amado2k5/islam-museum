## Summary
Jabala ibn al-Ayham, the last of the [[polity/ghassanid-kingdom|Ghassanid]] kings, who were Byzantine agents over the Arabs of [[place/al-sham|al-Sham]]. He submitted to Islam in the time of Umar ibn al-Khattab {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/34}.

## In al-Raheeq al-Makhtum
The Ghassanids continued to rule Syria in succession, as agents of the Byzantine kings, until the battle of Yarmuk in the year 13 AH. Their last king, Jabala ibn al-Ayham, submitted to Islam in the time of the Commander of the Faithful Umar ibn al-Khattab {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27} {cite:tarikh-ard-al-quran 2/80-82}.

## What is not mentioned here
The story of Jabala's apostasy and his flight to the Byzantines after a slap, which the chroniclers relate, is not mentioned in al-Raheeq. Only what the author said is recorded here.

## For young visitors
Jabala was the last king of Ghassan. He entered Islam after the defeat at Yarmuk, in the time of the caliph Umar.
