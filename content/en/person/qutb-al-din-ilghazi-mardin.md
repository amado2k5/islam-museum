## Overview
Qutb al-Din Ilghazi ibn Najm al-Din ibn Alpi ibn Timurtash ibn Ilghazi ibn Artuq, lord of Mardin. He died in 580 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.129}

He was succeeded by his son Husam al-Din Bulq Arslan, who was a child. His mamluk Nizam al-Din al-Baqish took charge of his upbringing and the management of his realm. Shah Armen, lord of Khilat, was the boy's maternal uncle, and he held power in the state and placed al-Baqish alongside his son. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.129}
