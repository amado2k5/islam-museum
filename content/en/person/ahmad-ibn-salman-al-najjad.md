## Summary
Ahmad ibn Salman, Abu Bakr al-Najjad al-Baghdadi, a Hanbali scholar who led in law and transmission. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2072}

## Works
He held a circle for fatwas and another for dictation at the mosque of al-Mansur; his works are 'al-Sunan' and 'al-Fawa'id'; he lived from 253 to 348 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2072}
