## Overview
Mas'ud ibn Mahmud ibn Sebuktigin, sultan of Ghazna after his father [[person/mahmud-ghaznawi|Mahmud of Ghazna]]. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400}

## The Seljuq defeat
Ibn al-Jawzi says that the Seljuqs defeated him in Jumada al-Akhira 430 AH, and took Khurasan and the Jibal. He fled, and his inkwells were taken. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400}

He then withdrew to Ghazna, and Tughrulbek returned to Nishapur, and the Ghuzz seized the whole of Khurasan. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.413}
