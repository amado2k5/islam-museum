# Nuhu Ribadu

Nuhu Ribadu is a Nigerian lawyer, retired police officer and politician, born on 21 November 1960 in Yola. His father, Ahmadu Ribadu, was a First Republic member of parliament and ambassador to Niger. He studied law at Ahmadu Bello University in Zaria, graduating in 1983 and being called to the Bar in 1984, and later earned a Master of Laws from the same university {cite:wikipedia-nuhu-ribadu-en} {cite:wikidata-nuhu-ribadu}.

## The police and the EFCC

He joined the Nigeria Police Force shortly after graduation and rose through its ranks, eventually heading its Legal and Prosecution Department. In 2003 President Olusegun Obasanjo appointed him the pioneer chairman of the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC), and reappointed him in 2007. Under Ribadu the EFCC charged prominent bankers, former state governors, ministers, senators and senior party figures — even the head of the Nigerian police — issued thousands of indictments and achieved about 270 convictions. In October 2006 he told the BBC that over 380 billion dollars had been stolen or wasted by Nigerian governments since independence in 1960 {cite:wikipedia-nuhu-ribadu-en}.

## Dismissal, exile and the presidential run

In December 2007 he was removed from the EFCC, and in 2008 the Police Service Commission dismissed him from the force; he left Nigeria and lived in exile until 2010. He returned to declare for the presidency and ran in 2011 as the candidate of the Action Congress of Nigeria, losing. He then chaired the Petroleum Special Revenue Task Force (2012–2014), ran for governor of Adamawa State in 2015 on the Peoples Democratic Party ticket, later joined the All Progressives Congress to run in Adamawa again, and lost the 2023 primary contest to Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed {cite:wikipedia-nuhu-ribadu-en}.

## National Security Adviser

On 19 June 2023 President Bola Tinubu appointed him National Security Adviser, the office he has held since {cite:wikipedia-nuhu-ribadu-en} {cite:wikidata-nuhu-ribadu}.
