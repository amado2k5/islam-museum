## Overview
Abu al-Walid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Ahmad al-Azraqi, a Meccan historian of the third century AH, who wrote on the reports and monuments of Mecca. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.526-528}

The dates of his birth and death are not recorded in the museum's data, and need an additional source.
