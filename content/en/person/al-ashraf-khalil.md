## Overview
King al-Ashraf Salah al-Din Khalil ibn Qalawun, a Mamluk sultan, who came to power after his father Qalawun in 689 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.730}

## His reign
Al-Suyuti says he brought the caliph's position into the open, after the caliph had been inactive in the days of his father. He had the caliph preach to the people and mention his appointment over the affairs of Islam. Then he gave a martial sermon urging the taking of Baghdad. In 691 AH he besieged the Castle of the Romans. He was killed at Tarja in Muharram 693 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.730}

Shadharat al-Dhahab says he took the sultanate after his father al-Mansur Qalawun in Dhu al-Qa'da 689 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.422}
