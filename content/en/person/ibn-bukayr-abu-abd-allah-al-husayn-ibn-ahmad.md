## Overview
Abu Abdallah al-Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Bukayr al-Sayrafi al-Baghdadi, a memoriser and hadith scholar of Baghdad. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The sources mention only some reports of his transmission of hadith and the names of those from whom he transmitted. He was born in 327 AH (938 CE) and died in 388 AH (998 CE), according to report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
