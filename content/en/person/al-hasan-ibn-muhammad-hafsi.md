## Summary
The Hafsid sultan of Tunis in [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]]'s day {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 215}.

## Misrule
Al-Salabi depicts him as governing badly and killing a number of his brothers until the country fell into disorder and men broke from his obedience; his brother [[person/al-rashid-ibn-muhammad-hafsi|al-Amir al-Rashid]] fled him into Khayr al-Din's protection {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 215}.

## Flight and restoration
When Khayr al-Din entered Tunis in 1534 he fled to Spain and appealed to Charles V, who reinstalled him in 942/1535 bound to surrender Bône and Mahdia, to oppose the Ottomans, and to fund a 2,000-man Spanish garrison at La Goulette — Mahdia he could not deliver, for it remained Ottoman {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 218-220}.
