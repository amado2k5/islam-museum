## Overview
Ali ibn Buya ibn Fanakhusraw al-Daylami, Abu al-Hasan, Imad al-Dawla, is described by al-Zirikli as the first of the Buyids to rule {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. The entry gives 281 and 338 AH (894 and 949 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## His rule and family
He held the land of Fars, with Shiraz as his capital {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. He was the brother of Rukn al-Dawla (al-Hasan) and Mu'izz al-Dawla (Ahmad); al-Zirikli says their father was a fisherman and that their fortunes rose until they ruled and became lords {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. Imad al-Dawla remained in power sixteen years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## His death
He died in Shiraz without children {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.
