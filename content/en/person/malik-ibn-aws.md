## In brief
Malik b. Aws b. al-Hadathan, Abu Sa'id al-Nasri of Medina; a mukhadram who saw Abu Bakr — companionship is also claimed for him — narrating from Umar, Uthman, Ali, Talha, and a group, while Ibn al-Munkadir, Ikrima b. Khalid, al-Zuhri, and a group narrated from him. He was among the firmly reliable scholars and the eloquent Arabs famed for articulateness, and he witnessed the conquest of Jerusalem; he died late in the first century. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.67}
