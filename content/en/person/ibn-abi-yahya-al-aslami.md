## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Yahya Sam'an al-Aslami, Abu Ishaq, was a scholar of hadith from the people of Medina. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 184 AH, 800 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 59}.

## Life
He was among the teachers of Imam al-Shafi'i, who took from him in his youth. He compiled a Muwatta' that al-Zirikli says was many times the size of Malik's {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 59}.

## Criticism
Al-Zirikli reports that the men of hadith criticised him and called him a Qadari, a Mu'tazili and a Jahmi. He also reports al-Rabi' as saying that when al-Shafi'i said "someone I do not suspect told us", he meant Ibrahim ibn Abi Yahya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 59}.
