## Overview
Shahak al-Khadim was a companion of al-Musta'in when he was carried from Wasit {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 545-546}.

## His account
Al-Musta'in told him at al-Qatul to look at the leader of the people, and if it was Sa'id al-Hajib, then he was lost. Shahak carried the jewel of the caliphate, the mantle, the staff and the sword to [[person/al-mutazz|al-Mu'tazz]], together with Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 545-546}.
