## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Jaza'iri, known as Aqujili, was a learned man engaged in hadith; he died in 1080 AH (1669 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.294}

He wrote 'Uqd al-Juman al-Lami', a poem setting out the names of the transmitters through whom the hadiths of al-Bukhari's Sahih come and the number belonging to each. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.294}
