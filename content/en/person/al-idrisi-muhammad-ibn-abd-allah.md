## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Idris, known as al-Idrisi. A geographer and traveller from Ceuta, he worked in Sicily at the court of Roger II. He wrote [[work/nuzhat-al-mushtaq|"Nuzhat al-Mushtaq"]] with a map of the world. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.408} He was born in 493 AH (1100 CE) and died in 560 AH (1164 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.408}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
