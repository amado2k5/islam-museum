## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Idris, known as al-Idrisi, was a geographer who wrote the [[work/nuzhat-al-mushtaq|"Nuzhat al-Mushtaq fi ikhtiraq al-afaq"]] for King Roger II of Sicily. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.4}

## The book and the king
Al-Idrisi opens the book by praising the great king Roger, "the victorious by God's might", whom he calls king of "Sicily and Italy, Ankabarda and Calabria". He says that the king conquered lands to east and west. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.4}

## The date of completion
Al-Idrisi says that the book was finished in the first ten days of the month that corresponds to Shawwal, in the year 548 AH. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.7}

## Sicily in the book
After describing the islands, al-Idrisi says that he will describe "Upper Sicily", which he calls "unique in its age for excellence and virtues". {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.588}
