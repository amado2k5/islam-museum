## Further studies
Beyond his Madinah theology, he holds a bachelor's in history and a master's in social communication from the Universidade Metodista in São Paulo, plus specialisation in addiction prevention and treatment — joining religious formation to the tools of modern social science. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## The institutions
He presides over the Instituto Cinco Pilares and the CALCEE ⁦(Latin Arab Centre for Culture and Strategic Studies)⁩, alongside his leadership in the union of Islamic entities, so that his da'wah becomes a network of institutions serving Latin America's Muslims in education, media and representation. {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}
