## Summary
Jihad Hassan Hammadeh is a Brazil-based sheikh of Arab origin, born in Syria and brought to Brazil as a child, who studied eleven years at the Islamic University of Madinah; active in Brazilian da'wah for decades, he is religious adviser to the National Association of Islamic Jurists and vice-president of the national union of Islamic entities. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## Upbringing and migration
Born in Syria, he migrated with his family to Brazil as a child, settling in São Bernardo do Campo where his father worked as an itinerant peddler. Observant of Islamic law since adolescence and seeking its foundations, he was invited by a figure connected to the Islamic University of Madinah to study theology on scholarship, spending eleven years there on Qur'an, sharia and the tradition. {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## Return and da'wah
He returned to Brazil a sheikh and gave his life to the Muslim community: he presides over two Islamic institutions, is vice-president of the União Nacional das Entidades Islâmicas, religious adviser to ANAJI ⁦(the National Association of Islamic Jurists)⁩, and served seventeen years as WAMY's vice-president for Latin America. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## Further studies
Beyond his Madinah theology, he holds a bachelor's in history and a master's in social communication from the Universidade Metodista in São Paulo, plus specialisation in addiction prevention and treatment — joining religious formation to the tools of modern social science. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## Media and society
He appears regularly on Brazilian national television and media speaking about Islam, worked as consultant for two Brazilian soap operas on Muslim themes, once served as a football interpreter, and lectures against stereotypes of Arabs and Muslims. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## The institutions
He presides over the Instituto Cinco Pilares and the CALCEE ⁦(Latin Arab Centre for Culture and Strategic Studies)⁩, alongside his leadership in the union of Islamic entities, so that his da'wah becomes a network of institutions serving Latin America's Muslims in education, media and representation. {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## Legacy
His path reads as a bridge between worlds: an Arab migrant who returned from Madinah with religious formation to serve a Brazilian congregation of many origins in Portuguese, tying Latin America's Islamic institutions to the centres of da'wah in the Muslim world; The Muslim 500 files him among Preachers & Spiritual Guides. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh}
## The Muslims of Brazil
Brazil hosts a small Muslim community of Arab, African and convert Brazilian origins, which Hammadeh has served since the 1980s as preacher, teacher and representative of national Islamic institutions; his path witnesses the establishment of institutional Islam in South America within a single generation. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh} {cite:museudapessoa-jihad-hammadeh}

## The image of the Brazilian Muslim
He devotes part of his work to correcting stereotypes of Arabs and Muslims in Brazilian media — television producers consult him for drama productions and he appears on national programmes explaining Islam in Portuguese — making him a public face of the community before the wider society. {cite:muslim500-jihad-hammadeh}
