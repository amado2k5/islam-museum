## Overview
Abd Manaf ibn Qusayy: Qusayy's son who grew honoured and ruling in his father's lifetime; the watering and feeding passed to his house in the division of offices between his sons and his brother Abd al-Dar's sons.

## Life
Abd Manaf grew honoured and ruling in his father Qusayy's lifetime, so Qusayy bequeathed to him the Quraysh offices he had held: Dar al-Nadwa, the gatekeeping, the banner, the watering and the feeding {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

When Abd Manaf died his sons disputed these offices with their cousin Abd al-Dar's sons; Quraysh split into two factions and fighting nearly broke out, until they rallied to a settlement and divided them: the watering and feeding went to Banu Abd Manaf, while Dar al-Nadwa, the banner and the gatekeeping remained with Banu Abd al-Dar {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. Then Banu Abd Manaf cast lots over what had fallen to them, and it came out for Hashim ibn Abd Manaf {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Abd Manaf son of Qusayy — from his house came Hashim and Abd al-Muttalib, the men who served the pilgrims generation after generation.
