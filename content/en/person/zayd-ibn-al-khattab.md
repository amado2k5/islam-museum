## Summary
[[person/zayd-ibn-al-khattab|Zayd ibn al-Khattab]] ibn Nufayl al-Adawi, brother of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] — his mother Asma' bint Wahb of Banu Asad. He was older than Umar, became Muslim before him, and witnessed [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] and the campaigns {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 515}.

## Al-Yamama
He was martyred at al-Yamama in 12 AH in Abu Bakr's caliphate — the Muslims' banner was with him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 515}. Umar grieved for him deeply, and when he was killed said: "he beat me to the two best things: he became Muslim before me and was martyred before me" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 515}.

## Narration
He has one hadith in the Sahih, on the prohibition of killing houses' snakes, in Ibn Umar's account from him joined with Abu Lubaba; Salih Jazara judged the correct version to be from Abu Lubaba alone {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 515}.

## In Khalid Muhammad Khalid's Rijal Hawl al-Rasul
[[person/khalid-muhammad-khalid|Khalid Muhammad Khalid]] calls him "the falcon of the Day of al-Yamama". He relates that the Prophet said in a gathering, "There is among you a man whose molar in the Fire will be greater than Mount Uhud", and that of those present only Abu Hurayra and al-Rajjal ibn Unfuwa outlived the rest; al-Rajjal then apostatised, joined Musaylima and testified to his prophethood, making clear whom the saying meant. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.348} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.349} He says Zayd resolved to end this sedition in the person of al-Rajjal; Khalid ibn al-Walid gave him the Muslims' banner at al-Yamama, and when the battle turned against the Muslims he climbed a rise and called on them to clench their teeth and press on, swearing not to speak until God defeated the enemy or he met Him. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.349} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.350} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.351} His brother Umar, seeing that Zayd's mail had fallen from him, said "Take my mail, Zayd", and he replied, "I want of martyrdom what you want, Umar". The author says Zayd killed al-Rajjal, news of which ran through Musaylima's army, and was then martyred. {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.350} {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.352} Umar would say: "May God have mercy on Zayd — he beat me to both good things: he became Muslim before me and was martyred before me", and "Whenever the east wind blows, I find in it the scent of Zayd." {cite:rijal-hawl-al-rasul-muqattam-1994 p.353}
