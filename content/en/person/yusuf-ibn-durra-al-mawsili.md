## Overview
Yusuf ibn Durra, the poet known as Ibn al-Durri, of Mosul origin, a clever young man. Abu Shuja' ibn al-Dahhan mentions in his history that he perished with the pilgrims in the year 545 AH, when the Za'b came out against them. The Za'b were a well-known branch of Sulaym, and many people perished by killing, hunger and thirst {cite:wafayat-ayan p.230-231}.

Imad al-Din al-Isfahani mentions him in "Kharidat al-Qasr", and Abu al-Ma'ali al-Hazmiri in "Zinat al-Dahr". Ibn Khallikan quotes one of his verses about a man with lame legs {cite:wafayat-ayan p.230-231}.
