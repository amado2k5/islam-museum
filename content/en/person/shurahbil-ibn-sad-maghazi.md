## Overview
Shurahbil ibn Sa'd, a client of the Ansar in Medina. Ahmad Amin counts him among the first pillars of writing the campaigns (maghazi), together with Aban ibn Uthman, Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Wahb ibn Munabbih {cite:duha-islam p.639-642}. Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm reported from him in the biography {cite:duha-islam p.639}.

## Death
He died in the year 123 AH, according to Duha al-Islam {cite:duha-islam p.639}.

## In al-Umari's al-Sira al-Nabawiyya al-Sahiha
[[person/akram-diya-al-umari|Akram Diya' al-Umari]] says Shurahbil ibn Sa'd al-Madani (d. 123 AH) was "truthful, but his memory became confused in old age", dying at nearly a hundred; that Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban included his hadith in their Sahihs; and he quotes Ibn Uyayna: no one knew the campaigns and the men of Badr better than he. {cite:sahih-sira-umari-ulum-hikam-1994 p.55}
