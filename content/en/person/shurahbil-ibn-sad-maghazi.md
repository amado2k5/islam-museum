## Overview
Shurahbil ibn Sa'd, a client of the Ansar in Medina. Ahmad Amin counts him among the first pillars of writing the campaigns (maghazi), together with Aban ibn Uthman, Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Wahb ibn Munabbih {cite:duha-islam p.639-642}. Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm reported from him in the biography {cite:duha-islam p.639}.

## Death
He died in the year 123 AH, according to Duha al-Islam {cite:duha-islam p.639}.
