## Summary
Of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; Ibn Ishaq names her among the Abyssinian emigrant women. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her among the Abyssinian emigrant women of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy, tracing her to Suhayl ibn Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text does not state she is Sahla's sister; the two are simply both daughters of Suhayl ibn Amr, so sisterhood is an inference from the shared lineage.
