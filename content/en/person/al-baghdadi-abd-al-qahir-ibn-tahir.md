## Overview
Abu Mansur Abd al-Qahir ibn Tahir al-Baghdadi, a theologian and researcher into the principles of religion and the doctrines of the sects, from Baghdad. He wrote on faith and its principles, on denying the createdness of the Qur'an, and on interpreting the ambiguous reports. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.634-635}
