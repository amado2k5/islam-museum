## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Bannis al-Fasi al-Maghribi, a scholar from Fez in the Maghrib. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 1160 AH (1747 CE) and his death as 1214 AH (1799 CE); the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
