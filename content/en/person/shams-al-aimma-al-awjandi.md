## Summary
Mahmud ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Awjandi, the qadi, a Hanafi jurist bearing the titles Shams al-A'imma and Shaykh al-Islam, and the grandfather of Qadi Khan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16249} He studied fiqh under Shams al-A'imma al-Sarakhsi, who died in 483 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16249} The text gives no dates of birth or death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16249}
