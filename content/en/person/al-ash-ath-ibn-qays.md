## Overview
Al-Ash'ath ibn Qays ibn Ma'di Karib al-Kindi, a chief of Kinda. His kunya was Abu Muhammad and his title "the knower of fire". Al-Baladhuri relates that he apostatised in the caliphate of Abu Bakr, and that he and the notables of Kinda entrenched themselves at al-Nujayr. Ziyad ibn Labid and al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya besieged them until they were exhausted. See [[event/siege-of-al-nujayr|the siege of al-Nujayr]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141}

## Safe conduct and his fate
Al-Ash'ath asked for safe conduct for a number of his companions and left himself out of the number. When he was brought to Abu Bakr, Abu Bakr told him he had no safe conduct because he had put himself outside it. He asked Abu Bakr to show him favour and to marry him, and Abu Bakr did, giving him his sister Umm Farwa daughter of Abu Quhafa, though it is said her name was Qurayba. The report says she bore him Muhammad, Ishaq, Qurayba, Hababa and Ja'da. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141}

He lived in Medina, then went out as a warrior to Syria and Iraq. He died in Kufa, and al-Hasan ibn Ali prayed over him after his peace with Mu'awiya. The estimated date of death, around 661 CE, is derived and approximate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.141}

## What is reported of Abu Bakr
Al-Baladhuri reports Abu Bakr as saying that he wished he had struck off al-Ash'ath's neck on the day he was brought in, because it seemed to him that he saw no evil without striving in it and helping it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
