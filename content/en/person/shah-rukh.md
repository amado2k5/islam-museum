## Shah Rukh ibn Timur

The khan Muʿin al-Din Shah Rukh ibn Timur Lang, ruler of Samarqand, Bukhara and other lands. He succeeded his father Timur in the kingdom, and his rule was established in Khurasan and its regions for more than forty years. His days were full of security and justice and support for learning and scholars, until leadership among the rulers of his time came to him. He died in 851 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 269}.
