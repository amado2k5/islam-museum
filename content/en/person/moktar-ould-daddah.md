# Moktar Ould Daddah

Moktar Ould Daddah (25 December 1924 – 14 October 2003) was a Mauritanian politician who served as the country's first prime minister (1957–1961) and its first president (1960–1978), leading Mauritania to independence from France. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} {cite:wikidata-moktar-ould-daddah}

## Formation

Daddah was born at Boutilimit into an important marabout family of the Ouled Birri tribe, attended elite Islamic academies, and worked for the French colonial administration as a translator. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} As a law student in Paris he graduated as the first Mauritanian to hold a university degree, and he was admitted to the bar at Dakar in 1955. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en}

## First president

Named acting president of the new Islamic Republic at independence on 28 November 1960 and confirmed in the first post-independence election of August 1961, Daddah established an authoritarian one-party state under his Mauritanian People's Party and followed a policy of "Islamic socialism" with many nationalisations. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} His 1975 charter called for Mauritania to become an Islamic, nationalist, centralist and socialist democracy, and his presidency was consumed by the Western Sahara war after Mauritania joined Morocco in dividing the territory. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en}

## Overthrow, exile and death

A military coup deposed Daddah on 10 July 1978. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} After a period of imprisonment he was allowed to go into exile in France in August 1979, where he organised the Alliance for a Democratic Mauritania in 1980; from 1985 he lived with his family at Nice. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} He was allowed to return to Mauritania on 17 July 2001, and he died in Paris on 14 October 2003. {cite:wikipedia-moktar-ould-daddah-en} {cite:wikidata-moktar-ould-daddah}
