## Summary
The Idrisi sharif al-Rashidi whom the Sufi murabitun proposed to rule Fez in the Ottoman sultan's name {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239}.

## The failed attempt
[[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]] tried to replace [[person/abu-hasun-al-wattasi|Bu Hasun]] with him; the people's rising forced Bu Hasun's restoration {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239}.
