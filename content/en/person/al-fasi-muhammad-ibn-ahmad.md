## Overview
Nur al-Din Abu al-Tayyib Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Fasi, later al-Makki al-Maliki, was the benefactor and scholar of the Hijazi lands, born in 775 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.9 p.288}

He was licensed in the ifada of shaykh Najm al-Din al-Marjani Ibn 'Awad, Ibn al-Sallar, Ibn al-Muhibb and a group of Damascenes; he applied himself to hadith, hearing after 790 AH from masters of his town, and travelled repeatedly to Cairo and Syria. He held the Maliki judgeship of his town — the first Maliki to hold it independently — and wrote Akhbar Makka, Akhbar Wulatiha and Akhbar man nubila biha min ahliha wa-ghayrihim, several works long and short, and a sequel to al-'Ibar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.9 p.288}
