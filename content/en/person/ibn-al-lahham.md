## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Abbas ibn Shayban, Abu al-Hasan 'Ala' al-Din, known as Ibn al-Lahham, was a Hanbali jurist originally of Baalbek. He settled in Damascus and served as deputy judge there, then went to Egypt and lived as a teacher at the Mansuriyya until he died aged fifty-odd in 803 AH (1401 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.6}

He composed books, among them al-Qawa'id al-Usuliyya wa-l-Akhbar al-'Ilmiyya fi Ikhtiyarat al-Shaykh Taqi al-Din Ibn Taymiyya. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.6}
