## Overview
Lupe Fiasco (born Wasalu Muhammad Jaco in 1982 in Chicago) is an American Muslim rapper. He is known for his 2006 album "Food & Liquor" and the song "Kick, Push", and his lyrics refer to his conversion to Islam, Chicago and politics. {cite:wikidata}
