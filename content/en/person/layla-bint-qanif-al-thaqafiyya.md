## Summary
Layla bint Qanif al-Thaqafiyya was a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8205}
She was among the women who washed Umm Kulthum, daughter of the Prophet, and she described it accurately. Through Dawud ibn Urwa ibn Masud al-Thaqafi she reported that the first garment handed to them was the waistwrap, then the shift, the veil and the sheet, after which the body was wrapped in the outer cloth, while the Prophet stood behind the door passing the garments to them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8206}
