## Overview
Jamal al-Din Ibn Yaghmur, al-Zahir's man, na'ib of Aleppo; he had been the sultan's deputy at Damascus, passed fifty, and took the Aleppo deputyship — which ended ill; he lived through the days of destruction, a brave and daring man {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 533}.
