## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn Hurayra al-Qaysi, an Andalusian poet and embroiderer. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His nickname
He is called "the blind" because he was blind, and he is attributed to Tutayla and Seville. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 525 AH (1129 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
