## Overview
Shihab al-Din Abu al-Makarim Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Mas'ud al-Shaybani al-Talafari al-Mawsili, a scholar and historian. He was born in Mosul in 593 AH (1196 CE) and died in 675 AH (1276 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
