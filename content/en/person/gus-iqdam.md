## Summary
Gus Iqdam — Agus Muhammad Iqdam Kholid — is a young Indonesian preacher from Blitar, East Java, born on 27 September 1994. {cite:uinsa-proceedings-gus-iqdam-2025} {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024} He is a caretaker of the Mamba'ul Hikam II pesantren in Karanggayam village, Srengat district, Blitar regency, and the founder of the Sabilu Taubah study assembly. {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024} His YouTube channel, Gus Iqdam Official, started on 1 April 2020, counted 1,720,000 subscribers, 231,969,719 views and 1,404 videos as read by vidIQ on 15 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:youtube-gus-iqdam-official} {cite:vidiq-gus-iqdam}

## Early life
He is the son of the cleric of the Mamba'ul Hikam II pesantren in Karanggayam, and was raised in that pesantren environment. {cite:uinsa-proceedings-gus-iqdam-2025} {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024}

## The Sabilu Taubah assembly
He founded the Sabilu Taubah ("path of repentance") study assembly at the end of 2018, beginning with only seven members; the ICONDAC proceedings paper records its growth to 66,000 members. {cite:uinsa-proceedings-gus-iqdam-2025} The assembly is known for welcoming people whom formal religious spaces often exclude — the Al-Hikmah journal describes its openness to marginalised groups such as street children, gang members and youth involved in criminal activities. {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024} Its routine sessions are carried on the Gus Iqdam Official channel, which academic studies of the channel describe as launched on 1 April 2020. {cite:youtube-gus-iqdam-official}

## Style and audience
Academic studies of his preaching describe an informal, humour-led register, substantially in Javanese, addressed in particular to young people; the Al-Hikmah article describes him as a young preacher and leader within the Nahdlatul Ulama community, and the assembly as popular among Nahdliyin communities. {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024} That community description is recorded here as the sources give it; it is not a statement of a legal or theological school. {cite:alhikmah-gus-iqdam-2024}
