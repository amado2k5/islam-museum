## Overview
Faraj ibn Qasim ibn Lubb al-Gharnati al-Andalusi, a scholar of Granada in al-Andalus in the eighth century AH. The encyclopaedia "Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama wa al-Udaba" describes him as a jurist and scholar, and dates his birth to 710 AH and his death to 782 AH, according to one report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
