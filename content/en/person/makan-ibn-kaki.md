## Overview
Makan ibn Kaki al-Daylami, a Daylami commander in Tabaristan. Ibn al-Athir says that Isfar ibn Shiruya was among his companions, and that Makan later expelled him from his army. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.129-130}

## What the sources report
Ibn al-Athir relates that his brother Abu al-Hasan ibn Kaki was killed at Rayy by the Alid captive held by him. Makan then returned to Isfar and fought him, and Isfar was defeated. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.129-130}
