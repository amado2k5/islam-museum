## Overview
Ibn Shu'ayb al-Siqilli, the muqri'-adib (590–664 AH); the Sicilian transplanted to Damascus, companion of al-Sakhawi for a time, master of the readings; he heard al-Qasim b. 'Asakir and a company, and read much on al-Sakhawi and his tier. He died in Jumada I — so said al-Dhahabi in al-'Ibar {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 548}.
