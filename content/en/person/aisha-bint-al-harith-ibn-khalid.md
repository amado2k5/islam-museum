## Summary
Daughter of al-Harith ibn Khalid and Rayta, born in the land of Abyssinia; she perished on the road with her mother, her sister and her brother. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her among two daughters Rayta bore there, saying they perished with their mother and their brother Musa from water they drank on the road. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} He counts her among the five females born in Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-tahm|Banu Taym ibn Murra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}
