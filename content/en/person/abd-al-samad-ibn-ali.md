## Overview
Abd al-Samad ibn Ali of the Abbasids. Al-Mas'udi, in listing the commanders of the pilgrimage, says that he led the people on pilgrimage in 150 AH and in 155 AH. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 juz' 4 p. 320}
