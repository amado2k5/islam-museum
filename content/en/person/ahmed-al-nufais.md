## Summary
Ahmed Al-Nufais is a Kuwaiti Qur'an reciter and imam born in 1987, among the most beloved reciters of the younger Gulf generation; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential Qur'an reciters. {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais} {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Formation
He memorised the Qur'an young, studied recitation under senior Kuwaiti and Arab masters — among them Sheikh Mishary al-Afasy — and obtained a licence ⁦(ijaza)⁩ in the narration of Hafs, later teaching recitation himself. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Ministry
He serves as an imam at the Grand Mosque ⁦(Masjid Al-Kabir)⁩ in Kuwait City, leading daily and Ramadan night prayers, and his recitations there are broadcast and widely shared online. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Style and reach
His recitation is known for a soft, intimate tone and careful tajwid that listeners find moving; recordings of his prayers circulate on streaming and video platforms to tens of millions of listeners. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Assessment
He is regarded as an exemplar of the contemporary Kuwaiti school of emotive recitation alongside his teacher al-Afasy; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais} {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais}


## Style and output
His recitation is noted for an extended melodic range while staying within recognised tajweed; dozens of suras and complete mushafs are in circulation under his name, and his studio sessions on social media draw millions of views. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Haram recordings
Recordings of his recitation inside the Haram in Makkah — particularly long passages in tahajjud hours — are among the most shared recitation clips in the Gulf and Southeast Asia. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Influence
Younger Gulf reciters cite his controlled modulation as a model, and his school of recitation has produced a distinct 'Nufais style' recognised across Arabic recitation communities online. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais} {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Origins
Born in Saudi Arabia in the 1970s and trained in Qur'an recitation from youth, he built his career through recordings rather than mosque imamate — a path that made him a recording-era reciter before the social-media age. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## The cassette-to-streaming shift
His catalogue bridged the cassette and CD era of Gulf recitation into the streaming age: recordings made for cassette labels now circulate as YouTube clips and Quran-app audio heard across continents. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## Devotional listening
His recitation is associated with tahajjud and late-night devotional listening across the Gulf — recordings chosen for personal worship hours rather than broadcast imamate — a niche that defined his reach. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais} {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais}

## The recording reciter
Ahmed Al-Nufais's career is defined by recordings rather than mosque imamate — a path that made him a recording-era reciter before the social-media age. His catalogue bridged the cassette and CD era of Gulf recitation into the streaming age: recordings made for cassette labels now circulate as YouTube clips and Quran-app audio heard across continents, with his tahajjud-hour sessions in the Haram in Makkah among the most-shared recitation content in the Gulf and Southeast Asia. His extended melodic range within recognised tajweed made him a reference voice for younger reciters. {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-al-nufais}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists him among the most influential Muslims in arts, citing his position as one of the Gulf's most-recorded reciters and the global circulation of his recordings across the recording-to-streaming transition. {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais}



## Standing in the field

Within Qur’an recitation, Ahmed Al-Nufais stands as a Gulf reciter whose Haram tahajjud recordings are among the most shared in the genre — a position that explains the scale of public attention the career has attracted. The Muslim 500's listing places Ahmed Al-Nufais among the most influential Muslims of the present generation in this field, reflecting both the size of the audience the work reaches and the extent to which the figure has come to represent it beyond specialist circles. {cite:muslim500-ahmed-al-nufais}
