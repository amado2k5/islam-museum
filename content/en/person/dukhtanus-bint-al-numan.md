## Overview
Dukhtanus, daughter of al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir, ruler of al-Hira, and wife of Laqit ibn Zurara of Tamim. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.30-31}

## The day of Shi'b Jabala
When [[person/laqit-ibn-zurara|Laqit]] was killed on the day of Shi'b Jabala and the Banu Abs struck him after he was dead, she said that they had struck a face that carried awe, and that if they had met Laqit alive they would not have stood against the spear-points. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.30-31}

Al-Baladhuri reports that Laqit, at his death, recited a verse about her in which he wished to know what she would say when news of him reached her. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.30-31}
