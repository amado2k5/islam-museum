## Summary
Musa ibn Harun (214–294 AH), Abu Imran; his father was known as al-Jamal. He heard from Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Yahya ibn Maʿin and others, and leading hadith transmitters and memorisers related from him. He was the imam of the people of his age and a sign of his time in memorisation, in knowledge of the narrators and in precision, extremely scrupulous and of great dignity. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 190}.

Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi said: I heard al-Suri say that he heard Abd al-Ghani ibn Saʿid say: "The best of people in speaking on the hadith of the Messenger of God in their time were Ali ibn al-Madini in his time, Musa ibn Harun in his time, and Ali ibn Umar al-Daraqutni in his time." I also heard most of our shaykhs describe him as having great scrupulousness, asceticism, piety, religiousness, good conduct and an upright way. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 190}.

He died in Shaʿban and was buried in the cemetery of Bab Harb in Baghdad. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 190}.
