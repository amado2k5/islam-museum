## Overview
[[person/ibn-al-athir-izz-al-din|Ibn al-Athir Izz al-Din]], Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Shaybani (555–630 AH), a historian, scholar of hadith and genealogist, author of *al-Kamil fi al-Tarikh* (The Complete History). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.278}

## Early life
He was born in Jazirat ibn Umar. He lived in Mosul with his father and two brothers, then travelled to Syria and Jerusalem, where he heard hadith from a number of scholars. He returned to Mosul and kept to his house for learning and writing. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.278}

## His works
He abridged and supplemented Ibn al-Sam'ani's *al-Ansab* (Genealogies), and brought the result out as *al-Lubab fi Tahdhib al-Ansab* (The Essence in Refining the Genealogies). He also wrote a book on the Companions that gathered their biographies. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.278}

## The Kamil
He relied fundamentally on the history of al-Tabari for his history, and said so in more than one place. He also gave reports that al-Tabari did not mention. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.279} He witnessed some events in Syria in the time of Salah al-Din, and so was close to them in time and place. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.279}
