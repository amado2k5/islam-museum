## Summary
Sohaira Siddiqui is a scholar of Islamic studies and a professor at Georgetown University in Qatar, and the founding Executive Director of Al-Mujadilah Center and Mosque for Women in Doha — a purpose-built women's mosque and research centre that opened in 2024 and has been described as the first institution of its kind in the Gulf. {cite:wikipedia-sohaira-siddiqui}

## Scholarship
Trained in the United States — she earned her doctorate at the University of California, Santa Barbara — Siddiqui works on the intellectual and legal history of classical Islam. Her first book, 'Law and Politics under the Abbasids: An Intellectual Portrait of al-Juwayni' (2019), studied the eleventh-century Shafi'i jurist's thought on authority and law, situating it inside the political theory of the period. {cite:wikipedia-sohaira-siddiqui}

## Al-Mujadilah
At Qatar's Education City she conceived and built Al-Mujadilah, a women-centred mosque and institution combining worship space, research, education and programming — named with a word carrying the sense of 'the woman who reasons or argues', drawn from the Qur'anic story remembered through the title of its fifty-eighth chapter. Under her direction it opened in Doha in 2024 as a research and community centre attached to a mosque led for women. {cite:muslim500-sohaira-siddiqui}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as a scholar-institution-builder: an academic of classical Islamic law who built, in Qatar, a permanent platform for Muslim women's scholarship and religious life — work at the intersection of the classical texts she studies and the contemporary institutions women occupy. {cite:muslim500-sohaira-siddiqui}
