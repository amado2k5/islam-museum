# Taimiyyah Zubair

## Summary
Taimiyyah Zubair is a teacher of Qur'an and Hadith studies at the AlHuda Institute. She is the daughter of Dr Idrees Zubair and Dr Farhat Hashmi, the founders of Al Huda International, and her recorded lectures are carried by Muslim Central in a feed of more than four thousand episodes. {cite:listennotes-taimiyyah-zubair}

## Formation and teaching
She completed her first study of the translation and tafseer of the Qur'an in her mid teens, under the supervision of her parents, and then formally studied Arabic grammar, fiqh, history and tafseer, again under her parents. Her lecture catalogue is organised around the text of the Qur'an, part by part, together with topical series. {cite:podcastapp-taimiyyah-zubair} She is the mother of two children. {cite:listennotes-taimiyyah-zubair}
