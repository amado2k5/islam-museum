## Overview
Khidas ibn Bishr ibn Khalid ibn Khaid al-Mujashi' al-Tamimi, a poet of the Banu Mujashi' of Tamim. His kunya was Abu Malik and Abu Yazid, and his son was Malik. The encyclopaedia gives his death as 134 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
