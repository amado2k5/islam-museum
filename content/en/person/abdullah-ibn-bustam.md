## Overview
The letter of [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|the governor of Najran]] in [[event/al-hadi-letter-from-najran-and-the-meeting-with-al-daam-at-iyan-286]] says that "Ibn Bustam" set about mischief against the imam and offered the Yamis money so that strife would return between them and Banu al-Harith. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The book's index reads "Abdullah ibn Bustam = Ibn Bustam", from which the full name is taken. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.450} When he was sure al-Hadi had left Khiwan he fled to al-Da'am, who sought safe-conduct for him, and al-Hadi gave it, overlooked his slip and went on with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} See [[event/ibn-bustam-seeks-safe-conduct-through-al-daam-and-al-hadi-reaches-saada-286]]. The account is Zaydi, in the imam's followers' voice; the text gives neither his line nor his death here, and the index points to later passages not yet read, so nothing is said of them.
