## Summary
Ousmane Dembélé is a French professional footballer born in Vernon in 1997, a forward for Paris Saint-Germain and the French national team, winner of the 2025 Ballon d'Or; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential figures in sport. {cite:muslim500-ousmane-dembele} {cite:wikipedia-ousmane-dembele}

## Career
Born to a family of Mauritanian-Senegalese descent, he rose through Rennes' academy to Borussia Dortmund, where a single dazzling season earned a 2017 move to Barcelona — then one of the most expensive transfers in history — before returning to France with PSG in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-ousmane-dembele}

## Honours
His trophies include the 2018 World Cup with France, La Liga titles with Barcelona, and PSG's first Champions League title in 2025 — a season in which he was the competition's decisive player, winning the Ballon d'Or and FIFA's The Best award. {cite:wikipedia-ousmane-dembele}

## Game
A two-footed winger of exceptional speed and dribbling, his career was long framed by injury-interrupted promise; the 2024–25 season, recast as a central forward, produced his first fully realised campaign. {cite:wikipedia-ousmane-dembele}
