## Overview
Ala al-Din Abu al-Mahasin Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Shaykhi al-Baghdadi, a Baghdadi scholar of jurisprudence and hadith in the eighth century AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 678 AH (1279 CE) and his death as 741 AH (1339 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
