# Mohammad al-Sammak

## Summary

Mohammad al-Sammak is a Lebanese-born thinker and writer, a Saudi citizen since November 2021, known for his work in Muslim-Christian dialogue and the dialogue of civilisations. {cite:wikipedia-mohammad-al-sammak-ar} {cite:wikidata-mohammad-al-sammak}

## Early life and study

He was born in [[place/bayrut|Beirut]] in 1936 according to the Arabic article and Wikidata, and in 1939 according to the prize's page. He studied in the schools of the Makassed Islamic Philanthropic Association, then at the American University of Beirut and the Lebanese American University, and holds an honorary doctorate in the humanities. {cite:wikipedia-mohammad-al-sammak-ar} {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates mr-mohammad-al-sammak}

## Journalism and writing

He began as a journalist writing on Islamic thought, and has written for al-Mustaqbal (Lebanon), al-Ittihad (UAE) and al-Shorouk (Egypt); he sat on al-Mustaqbal's board and belongs to the Lebanese Press Syndicate, the Lebanese Writers' Union and the Federation of Arab Journalists. His articles deal with politics, Muslim-Christian dialogue and the dialogue of civilisations, and he has written several books on these subjects. {cite:wikipedia-mohammad-al-sammak-ar} {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates mr-mohammad-al-sammak}

## Dialogue and posts

He is secretary-general of the National Islamic-Christian Committee for Dialogue and of the permanent secretariat of the Islamic Spiritual Summit in Lebanon, adviser to the Mufti of the Lebanese Republic, and was an adviser to Prime Minister Rafik Hariri. He sits on the board of the King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz International Centre for Interreligious and Intercultural Dialogue and on the presidential council of Religions for Peace in New York. {cite:wikipedia-mohammad-al-sammak-ar} {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates mr-mohammad-al-sammak}

## The King Faisal Prize

He received the [[award/king-faisal-prize|King Faisal Prize]] for Service to Islam in 2024; the prize's page says it was given for his early and continuing contribution to Muslim-Christian dialogue, his part in dialogue conferences, his leadership of and membership in bodies that call for tolerance and peace, and the depth of his books and research. {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates mr-mohammad-al-sammak} {cite:wikipedia-mohammad-al-sammak-ar}
