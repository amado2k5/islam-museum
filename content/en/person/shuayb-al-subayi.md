## Overview
Shu'ayb al-Subay'i was a man of [[group/al-subay-arhab|al-Subay']] in Arhab, "of al-Da'am's clan and one who supported him in the war against the imam", as the man who tells the report puts it; they were in doubt about [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} A man of Banu Rabi'a or Hajur relates from him that he fell ill in the throat to the edge of death, took the seal of a letter that came from al-Hadi to the people of al-Subay', prayed, ate it and recovered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} The book gives neither his lineage, his father nor the time of his illness, and the report is attributed, which the museum neither confirms nor denies.
