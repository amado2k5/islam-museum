## Summary
Ala' al-Din Ali ibn Uthman al-Mardini, known as Ibn al-Turkumani, a Hanafi jurist from Egypt who held the Hanafi judgeship there and taught, issued fatwas and wrote on law and usul al-fiqh. His works include al-Kifaya fi mukhtasar al-Hidaya, an introduction to usul al-fiqh, and a takhrij of the hadiths of the Hidaya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1599}
