## Summary
Ibn al-Dabbagh, shaykh of al-Andalus in Arabic and the readings; he took the readings from Abu Bakr Ibn Saf and Arabic from Abu Dharr Ibn Abi Rakkab al-Khushani, and led his contemporaries in Arabic. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 244}

## Death
He died at Seville after the Rum took it, in Sha'ban — the ringing of the bell and the silencing of the adhan broke him; he kept grieving, lamenting and restless until he died — or, it was said, he died the day it fell. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 244}
