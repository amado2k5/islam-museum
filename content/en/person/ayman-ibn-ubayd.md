## Summary
Son of [[person/umm-ayman|Umm Ayman]], the Prophet's nurse, and brother of Usama ibn Zayd by his mother; an Abyssinian-Qurashi Companion who stood firm with the Prophet when the people scattered in the narrows of [[place/wadi-hunayn|Hunayn]], fighting before him until martyred {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa pp. 88, 101}.

## His gallantry and martyrdom
When Hawazin's ambush struck and the people fell back at the battle's start, Ayman ibn Ubayd was among the few who held firm around the Prophet, gathering round him at his mount, and kept defending with his sword until he fell a martyr in the valley of Hunayn {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa pp. 88, 101}.

Ibn Ishaq names him at the head of that day's martyrs of Banu Hashim and their allies {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/101}.

## For young visitors
The son of the noble nurse Umm Ayman stood like a shield defending the Prophet bravely on the day of Hunayn until he won martyrdom.
