## Overview
Amir of Wasit and its districts. Al-Suyuti says that he seized the country in 324 AH, so that the office of the vizierate and the registers ceased, and money was carried to him, with [[person/al-radi-billah|al-Radi]] remaining with him in name only. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.605}

Bajkam rose against him in 326 AH, and he went into hiding. He reappeared in the days of al-Muttaqi, fought Kurtakin the Daylami and defeated him, and took the command of the amirs. Then Ali al-Buraydi rose against him, and he fled with the caliph to Mosul, and was killed by treachery at Tikrit. Al-Hasan ibn Hamdan took his place, with the title Nasir al-Dawla. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.605,609}
