## Overview
Harim ibn Hayyan al-Abdi, a commander of the Rashidun period. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that Uthman ibn Abi al-As sent him to the fortress of Shubayr, and that he took it by storm after a siege and fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Another report, which al-Baladhuri transmits from "some", says that he took the fortress of al-Satwaj by storm. The name of the fortress is therefore a point of disagreement in the source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Ahmad Farid's lessons 'The Boat'
In [[work/the-boat-ahmad-farid|'The Boat']] [[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] quotes Harim ibn Hayyan on God's love: when a servant turns his heart towards God, God turns the hearts of the believers towards him until He grants him their affection. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep08 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep08 00:02:00} He cites the saying in his lesson on the love God places for believers among people, after the hadith of Gabriel's call. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep08 00:01:00}
