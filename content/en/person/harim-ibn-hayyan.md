## Overview
Harim ibn Hayyan al-Abdi, a commander of the Rashidun period. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that Uthman ibn Abi al-As sent him to the fortress of Shubayr, and that he took it by storm after a siege and fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Another report, which al-Baladhuri transmits from "some", says that he took the fortress of al-Satwaj by storm. The name of the fortress is therefore a point of disagreement in the source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
