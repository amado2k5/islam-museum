## Summary
Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Hilal, Abu al-Abbas, chief judge, a jurist and judge learned in many fields. He studied law with Fakhr al-Din ibn al-Mukhallada and with others, and his works include a commentary on Ibn al-Hajib's lesser work and a commentary on the Qur'an verse of the Throne. The book gives his death as 795 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7147}
