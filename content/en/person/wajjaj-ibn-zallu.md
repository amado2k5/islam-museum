## Overview
Wajjaj ibn Zallu al-Lamti, a jurist of Sijilmasa, among those who studied with Abu Imran al-Fasi {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}.

## The delegation of Kadala
Abu Imran wrote to him and charged him to seek out for the delegation of Kadala a man whose religion and jurisprudence he trusted, and who would accustom himself to the hardship of life in their land. He sent with them Abd Allah ibn Yasin al-Jazuli {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1645}.
