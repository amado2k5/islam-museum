## Summary
Atiyya ibn Sa'd ibn Junada al-Awfi al-Jadali al-Qaysi of Kufa, Abu al-Hasan, was one of the exegetes among the Successors and died in 111 AH. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.48} He transmitted from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, Abu Hurayra, [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] and Ibn Umar, and Abu Hatim al-Razi said of him: weak, his hadith is written down — as Ahmad Khalid Babakr cites from Tahdhib al-Tahdhib and al-Jarh wa-l-Ta'dil. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.48}

## His commentary in Jami' al-Bayan
Following Fuat Sezgin, the thesis counts it among the written commentaries [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] relied on: he quotes it in some 1,560 places of [[work/jami-al-bayan|Jami' al-Bayan]] through a single chain of Atiyya's descendants — Muhammad ibn Sa'd, from his father, from his uncle, from al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan, from his father, from his grandfather Atiyya ibn Sa'd al-Awfi, from Ibn Abbas. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.48} It is thus one of the chains for Ibn Abbas's exegesis that recur most often in the book.

## Notes for researchers
- Later (p. 49) the thesis mentions "Abu al-Hasan Sa'd ibn Junada al-Awfi" in connection with al-Tabari's History, apparently a confusion of the name; it is not used.
- The death date rests on the thesis's footnote alone.
