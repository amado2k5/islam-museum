## Overview
Yusuf ibn 'Adi ibn Zurayq ibn Isma'il — also given as ibn 'Adi ibn al-Salt — the trustworthy imam and hafiz Abu Ya'qub al-Taymi of Kufa, client of Taym Allah, brother of the precise hafiz Zakariyya ibn 'Adi; he settled in Egypt and narrated there while his brother lived in Baghdad, both being from Kufa. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4275}

## Teachers and standing
He narrated from Sharik, Abu al-Ahwas, 'Amr ibn Abi al-Miqdam, Malik ibn Anas, 'Ubaydallah ibn 'Amr al-Raqqi, 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Zinad and others. He died in 230 AH or after. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4275-4276}
