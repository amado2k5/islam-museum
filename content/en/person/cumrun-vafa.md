# Cumrun Vafa

Cumrun Vafa (born 1 August 1960) is an Iranian-American theoretical physicist at Harvard University, one of the leading figures of string theory. The English article gives his birth on 1 August 1960 in Tehran; the Persian and Arabic articles give the year only. No Urdu edition of his article exists; this file's companion Urdu text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-vafa-en} {cite:wikipedia-vafa-fa} {cite:wikipedia-vafa-ar} {cite:wikidata-vafa}

## Early life and education

Born in Tehran and educated at Alborz High School, he moved to the United States in 1976, took a bachelor's degree in mathematics and physics at MIT, and completed his doctorate at Princeton University under Edward Witten, with a dissertation on symmetries, inequalities and index theorems. {cite:wikipedia-vafa-en}

## Career and research

After his doctorate he joined Harvard as a junior fellow, received a senior faculty position in 1989, and has remained there, holding the Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy and, from October 2025, a Timken University Professorship. His work includes the Strominger–Vafa counting of black hole microstates, the geometric engineering of quantum field theories and the Gopakumar–Vafa invariants; in 1997 he developed F-theory. He shared the 2008 Dirac Medal and the 2017 Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics, and received the Dannie Heineman Prize in 2016 and the Mustafa Prize in 2021. {cite:wikipedia-vafa-en} {cite:wikipedia-vafa-fa}
