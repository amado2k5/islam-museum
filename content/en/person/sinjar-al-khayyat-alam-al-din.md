## Overview
The great amir Alam al-Din Sinjar al-Masruri al-Salihi al-Hajib, known as al-Khayyat, a Mamluk amir who held Cairo {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.606}.

## Death
He died in 695 AH (1296 CE). The sources differ on the year of his death. The encyclopedia records the date as a commonly transmitted report, and the entry needs an additional source {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.606}.
