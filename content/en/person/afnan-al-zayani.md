## Summary
Afnan Al-Zayani is a Bahraini businesswoman, President and Chief Executive of AlZayani Commercial Services, and one of the pioneers of women's corporate leadership in the Gulf — among the first women in the region to run a family trading group. {cite:muslim500-afnan-al-zayani}

## Business career
Trained in the United States, Al-Zayani returned to Bahrain and built AlZayani Commercial Services into a diversified trading and services concern. In a family-business economy where senior management long passed almost exclusively to sons, her rise to head a commercial group made her a reference case for women's advancement in Gulf business, and she has served on boards and in national business bodies. {cite:wikipedia-afnan-al-zayani}

## Advocacy and recognition
Beyond her own firm she has argued publicly for the economic participation of women — entrepreneurship, board service, financial literacy — in Bahrain and across the region, appearing repeatedly in Forbes Middle East and Arabian Business rankings of powerful Arab businesswomen. {cite:muslim500-afnan-al-zayani}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as a trailblazer of Gulf women's business leadership: a CEO whose career demonstrated, a generation ago, that a woman could run a major family enterprise in the region — and who spent that career pressing the argument further. {cite:muslim500-afnan-al-zayani}
