## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Ahmad ibn Nasr ibn Salih al-Hulwani al-Bukhari, Abu Muhammad, known as Shams al-A'imma: a Hanafi jurist named after the trade of halwa-making, so that he was also called al-Hulwa'i. He was the leading scholar of the ra'y school in Bukhara in his time, and died in 448 AH (1056 CE) at Kishsh; he was buried in Bukhara. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.13}

His works include al-Mabsut on jurisprudence, al-Nawadir on the branches of the law, al-Fatawa, and a commentary on Abu Yusuf's Adab al-Qadi. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.13}
