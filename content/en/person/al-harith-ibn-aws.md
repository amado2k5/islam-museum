## Overview
[[person/al-harith-ibn-aws|Al-Harith ibn Aws]] ibn Mu'adh ibn al-Nu'man al-Ansari al-Awsi, nephew of [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]], lord of the Aws. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.250}

## His mention in hadith
His mention is established in a sound hadith that Ahmad relates from Aisha: she went out on the day of the Trench and heard a sound, and it was Sa'd ibn Mu'adh with his nephew al-Harith ibn Aws carrying his shield. Ibn Hibban graded it sound. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.250}

## The killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf
Ibn Hajar considers that he witnessed [[event/killing-of-kab-ibn-al-ashraf|the killing of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf]]. In al-Bukhari, from a hadith of Jabir, [[person/muhammad-ibn-maslama|Muhammad ibn Maslama]] came with two men: Abu Qays ibn Jabir and al-Harith ibn Aws, "this is he, God knows best". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.250} Sa'd ibn Mu'adh said, in the report of Abu Na'ila, that he would go with his nephew al-Harith ibn Aws. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.250}
