## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Musab. Al-Baladhuri relates that the caliph al-Mu'tasim sent him, with the troops of the court he attached to him, to fight Mazyar ibn Qarin together with al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
