## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Amr ibn Abdallah ibn Safwan al-Nasri, Abu Zur'a al-Dimashqi, was, in al-Zirikli's words, one of the leading authorities of his time in hadith and the knowledge of its narrators {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 320}. Al-Zirikli prints his death as 280 AH, 893 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 320}.

## Life
He belonged to Damascus and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 320}.

## Works
He wrote a book on history and the defects of narrators; the first part survives in manuscript in the Fatih collection in Istanbul, no. 4250, and al-Maymani wrote of it that it is fit for publication; he also left questions on hadith and jurisprudence in several parts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 320}.
