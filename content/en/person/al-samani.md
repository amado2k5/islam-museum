## Overview
The imam Abu Sa'd Abd al-Karim ibn Muhammad al-Marwazi al-Samani, a Shafi'i and a memoriser, who died in 562 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}

## "al-Ansab"
His book [[work/al-ansab-samani|al-Ansab]] is great in its field and would run to eight volumes complete, but it is rare to find. Ibn al-Athir al-Jazari abridged it under the name "al-Lubab", and al-Suyuti abridged that under the name "Lubb al-Lubab". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}
