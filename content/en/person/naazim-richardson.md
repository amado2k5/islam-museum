# Naazim Richardson

## Summary

Naazim Richardson (26 November 1965 – 24 July 2020) was an American boxing trainer from Philadelphia, most notable for training Bernard Hopkins and "Sugar" Shane Mosley.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}{cite:wikidata-naazim-richardson}
The English Wikipedia article describes him as a devout Muslim who was often acknowledged as "Brother" Naazim.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}

## Career

Richardson was raised in Philadelphia, left home at fourteen, and was jailed as a teenager; The New York Times wrote that boxing took him from the streets of North Philadelphia and gave him energy and purpose.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
He was a head trainer at the Concrete Jungle gym in Philadelphia and at other gyms across the city, and worked for a time under the trainer Bouie Fisher; his son Rock Allen was a boxer before a car accident.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
In 2007 he suffered a stroke that temporarily left him unable to walk or speak, and he then returned to boxing as a trainer.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
On his return he became Shane Mosley's trainer for three major fights: the win over Antonio Margarito and the losses to Floyd Mayweather Jr. and Manny Pacquiao.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
Before the Mosley–Margarito fight in January 2009, Richardson caught plaster knuckle pads in Margarito's hand wraps, which led to Margarito's one-year suspension from boxing.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
Other fighters he trained or advised include Steve Cunningham, his nephew Karl Dargan, Badr Hari, Yusaf Mack and Sergio Martinez.{cite:wikipedia-naazim-richardson-en}
