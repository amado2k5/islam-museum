## Summary
Shihab al-Din Ahmad Ibn al-Mallah, of Ramla then Damascus, the Shafi'i; born 859, he was of great learning, piety and purity of heart — an imam of the readings who held the shaykhdom of iqra' at the madrasa; he died about 923. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 522}
