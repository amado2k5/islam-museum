## Overview
Jamil ibn Ka'b al-Tha'labi, among the chiefs of Rabi'a and the partisans of Ali; Mu'awiya took him captive, and when he ordered his neck struck he said 'O God, bear witness that Mu'awiya kills me neither for Thy sake nor because my killing pleases Thee — he kills me for the trash of this world'.
