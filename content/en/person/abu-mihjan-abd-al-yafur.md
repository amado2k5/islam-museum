## Overview
Abu Mihjan, "of the slaves of Al Ya'fur", is so named in the book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The book's index, p. 454, lists "Abu Mihjan, of the slaves of Al Ya'fur" at pp. 140 and 133. The account of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s messenger, his prayer against Abu Mihjan and what befell him is in [[event/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-and-murawwi-on-al-hadis-prayers-against-abu-mihjan-and-jaftam-286]], from [[person/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-qadi-of-hamdan|Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak]] and Murawwi al-Sa'idi, and the museum neither confirms nor denies it. P. 133 in the index is where an Abu Mihjan stands among those al-Hadi put out of Najran in [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]]; it is not asserted here that he is the same man, since only the index joins them. The book here gives neither his lineage nor the sense of "slave of Al Ya'fur"; see [[group/banu-yafur]] for the name, with no link asserted.
