## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Mahdi ibn Ahmad al-Khawafi. He is attributed to Khawaf, a district in the region of Nishapur from which a number of scholars came. He died in 450 AH (1058 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
