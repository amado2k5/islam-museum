## Overview
Shams al-Din Ibn al-Samdisi, the Egyptian Shafi'i; a deputy qadi at Cairo who worked and served in the judgeship — his house a refuge for students; he died Monday 16 Jumada I 922 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 472}.
