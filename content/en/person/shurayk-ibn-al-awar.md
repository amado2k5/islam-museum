## Overview
Shurayk ibn al-A'war al-Harithi, who is Shurayk ibn al-Harith. [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ibn Ziyad]] appointed him over Kirman. Yazid ibn Ziyad ibn Rabi'a ibn Mufarri' al-Himyari wrote to him, and he granted him land in Kirman. He sold it after Ibn Ziyad fled from Basra {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
