## Summary
Ghassan Abu-Sittah is a British-Palestinian plastic and reconstructive surgeon whose career joined elite surgical training in London and Glasgow to repeated humanitarian missions in the wars of the Middle East — above all in Gaza, where he has operated in every major round of conflict since the late 2000s. In 2024 the students of the University of Glasgow elected him their Rector. {cite:wikipedia-ghassan-abu-sittah}

## Formation and surgical work
Born in 1969, he studied medicine at the University of Glasgow and trained in plastic surgery in Britain, specialising in reconstructive surgery for war wounds and children's injuries. He worked at the Centre for Blast Injury Studies at Imperial College London and became known for documenting and treating the patterns of wounding produced by modern urban bombardment. {cite:wikipedia-ghassan-abu-sittah}

## War-zone surgery
Abu-Sittah volunteered with medical missions in Iraq, Lebanon, Syria, Yemen and Gaza. During the war that began in Gaza in October 2023 he spent weeks operating at Al-Ahli (Baptist) and Al-Shifa hospitals under siege conditions, and on leaving he became one of the most-heard medical witnesses on the humanitarian situation there, briefing journalists, universities and governments. Supporters describe him as an indispensable documenter of war injuries; critics of his public advocacy have questioned his political statements. The museum records both readings attributed. {cite:wikipedia-ghassan-abu-sittah}

## Public roles
In March 2024 students at his alma mater elected him Rector of the University of Glasgow — a largely honorary but historically prominent office — on a platform centred on divestment and solidarity with Gaza. He has continued to practise and teach surgery in London while campaigning for the reconstruction of Gaza's shattered health system. {cite:muslim500-ghassan-abu-sittah}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as a surgeon whose humanitarian record made him, in wartime, one of the most recognisable Palestinian voices in Western public life. {cite:muslim500-ghassan-abu-sittah}
