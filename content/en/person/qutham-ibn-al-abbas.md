## Overview
Qutham ibn al-Abbas, son of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib. al-Mas'udi reports that he went down into the Prophet's grave at the burial, together with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], his brother [[person/al-fadl-ibn-al-abbas|al-Fadl]], and Shuqran, client of the Messenger of God {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.582}.
