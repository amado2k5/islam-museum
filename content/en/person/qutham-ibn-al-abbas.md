## Overview
Qutham ibn al-'Abbas ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim al-Hashimi, cousin of the Prophet and brother of al-Fadl, 'Abdallah, 'Ubaydallah and Kathir; his mother was Umm al-Fadl Lubaba bint al-Harith al-Hilaliyya, the second woman to embrace Islam after Khadija. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3090}

## Standing and death
Al-Mas'udi records that he went down into the Prophet's grave at the burial with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|'Ali ibn Abi Talib]], his brother al-Fadl and Shuqran, the Prophet's client. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.582} He went out to Khurasan with Sa'id ibn 'Uthman and died at Samarqand in 57 AH — some say martyred — having declined five booty-shares for one share for himself and one for his horse; when the news reached Ibn 'Abbas he said, 'What a distance between his birthplace and his grave.' {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3090}
