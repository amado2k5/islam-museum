## Overview
Al-Rukn 'Abd al-Salam ibn 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani; born on the night of 8 Dhu'l-Hijja 548, he heard from his grandfather, taught at his school, studied fiqh there and held several offices — a clever, gifted litterateur versed in logic, philosophy and astrology, until he was accused of the doctrine of the ancients, and loose of tongue. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.16,53-54}

## His ordeal
In the days of vizier Ibn Yunus he suffered an inquisition: books of philosophy, the Epistles of the Brethren of Purity, works of magic and star-worship including a letter to Saturn in his hand were taken from his house and burned publicly; the qadi ruled him a sinner and his grandfather's school was taken from his father and given to Ibn al-Jawzi — whom he had himself pursued until the man was arrested and carried with him by boat to Wasit. He died on Friday, 8 Rajab 611, and was buried east of Baghdad; Ibn al-Najjar mentions him with harsh censure, noting that he transmitted no hadith. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.16,53-54}
