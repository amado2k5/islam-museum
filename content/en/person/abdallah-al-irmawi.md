## Overview
'Abdallah al-Irmawi, the exemplary ascetic, master of the zawiya on Mount Qasyun; righteous, humble, shedding all affectation — walking alone, buying his own needs — a man of states, struggles and standing in poverty who travelled the lands and met abdal and saints, and in his beginning would lodge nowhere but the waste. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 145-146}

## The tale of fellowship
He told of himself that he fell among Frankish robbers who tortured and bound him; when they slept, Muslim robbers came, woke them and drove them into a cave. When they were far, the Franks asked: "why did you not betray us and escape?" He said: "I kept your company and ate your bread — and in our way companionship is precious; I saw no salvation for myself in your ruin." They thanked him and freed him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 145-146}
