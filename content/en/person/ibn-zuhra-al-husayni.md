## Summary
Shaykh Abu 'Ali al-Hasan ibn Zuhra al-Husayni the naqib; head of the Shi'a of Aleppo — their might, their prestige and their scholar, versed in the readings, Arabic, history and fiqh on the sect's view; he was marked for the vizierate and went as envoy to Iraq and elsewhere; he died in 620 and the Shi'a were shattered by his death. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 87}
