## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, son of Layla bint Mas'ud al-Nahshali. He was killed together with al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd in Kufa, among those of al-Mukhtar's companions whom Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr killed {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.92-93}.

al-Mas'udi has a report of how he escaped al-Mukhtar and went on to Basra {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.92-93}.
