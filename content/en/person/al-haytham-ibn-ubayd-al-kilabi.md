## Overview
Al-Haytham ibn Ubayd al-Kilabi, the amir of al-Andalus on behalf of Ubayda ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, governor of Ifriqiya. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.272}

## His governorship
He came to al-Andalus in Muharram 111 AH, and raided the land of Manusa and conquered it. He died in 113 AH, two years after his governorship began. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.272}
