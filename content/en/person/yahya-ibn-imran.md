## Overview
Yahya ibn Imran, chief of the people of Qom. al-Baladhuri reports that Ali ibn Hisham al-Marwazi killed him when they disobeyed and refused the land tax in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
