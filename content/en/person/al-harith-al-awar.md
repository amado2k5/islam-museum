## Overview
Al-Harith al-A'war al-Hamdani, a jurist of Kufa. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.43}

## His standing among the jurists of Kufa
Abu Ishaq said that there was no one in Kufa more knowledgeable in the law of inheritance than Ubayda and al-Harith al-A'war. Ibn Sirin named four jurists of Kufa in his time: al-Harith, Ubayda, Alqama and Shurayh. The order of al-Harith and Ubayda differs in the transmission. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.43}

According to the same book's established record, he was among the pupils of [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.43} This connection rests on one source and needs an additional source. The page gives no dates.
