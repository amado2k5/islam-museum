## Overview
The daughter of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir, one of the wives of [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd]]. When [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Musab ibn al-Zubayr]] brought the wives of al-Mukhtar and called them to disown him, she refused. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.93}

Al-Mas'udi says that she was killed in cold blood, and quotes a verse of a poet marvelling at her killing. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.93}
