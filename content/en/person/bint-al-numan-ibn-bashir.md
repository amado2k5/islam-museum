## Overview
The daughter of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir, one of al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd's wives; when Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr brought them and bade them disavow him she refused, saying 'a witness provisioned her and I leave her? I follow Ibn Hind and I follow him'.
