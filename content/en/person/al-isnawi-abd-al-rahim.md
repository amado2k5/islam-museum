## Overview
Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Isnawi, a Shafi'i jurist from Isna in Upper Egypt. He memorised the Qur'an and al-Shirazi's "al-Tanbih" in its mosque. He was born in 704 AH (1305 CE) and died in 772 AH (1371 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.681}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
