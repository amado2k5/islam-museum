## Summary
Mahmood Ahmad Ghadanfar is the author of [[work/great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar|Great Women of Islam]], on the women Companions given the good news of Paradise, published by Darussalam, Riyadh, in Jamila Muhammad Qawi's English translation revised by Safiur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.1}

## For young visitors
