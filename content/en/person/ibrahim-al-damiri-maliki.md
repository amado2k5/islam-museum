## Summary

Chief judge and shaykh of shaykhs Burhan al-Din Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Ahmad al-Damiri, a Maliki. He reached his post through his own effort, became chief judge of the Malikis in Cairo, and stayed as a neighbour of Shaykh Ahmad ibn al-Mirghi in al-Za'faran {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.

The author of al-Kawakib reports that, when he was to preach to Sultan al-Ghuri one Monday night in Ramadan, Ibrahim al-Damiri was forced to preach in the sultan's presence. He died on Wednesday, the thirteenth of Ramadan in the year 913, and Sultan al-Ghuri prayed the funeral prayer over him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.
