## Overview
Ahmad ibn Mustafa ibn al-Khawja, a historian and writer who lived in Tunis. His full name in the source is al-Hajj Ahmad ibn Mustafa ibn al-Hajj al-Ma'qun. He was a follower of the Hanafi school in Tunis, and he wrote several works. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he was born in 1074 AH (1664 CE) and died in 1138 AH (1726 CE). Both dates come from one source and need a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
