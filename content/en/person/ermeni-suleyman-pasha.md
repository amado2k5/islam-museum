## Summary
Süleyman Pasha, nicknamed 'the Armenian' and 'Koca' — the Great — a seventeenth-century Ottoman vizier {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 5, 'Süleyman Paşa (Koca)'}.

A kinsman of İsmail Agha of Malatya, whence the byname 'the Armenian'. Palace-bred, he became rikâbdar, was made vizier and agha of the Janissaries in Dhu'l-Hijja 1062/November 1652; dismissed in Rabīʿ II 1063 and sent to Crete with the sancak of Malatya, then recalled in 1064/1654 as a kubbe vizier {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 5, 'Süleyman Paşa (Koca)'}.
