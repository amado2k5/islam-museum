## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Abd Allah ibn Qudama, Jamal al-Islam Sharaf al-Din, known as Ibn Qadi al-Jabal, was the shaykh of the Hanbalis in his time; his origin was Jerusalem and he was born and died at Damascus in 771 AH (1370 CE), knowing twenty thousand lines of verse by heart. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.111}

He went to Egypt and taught at the Sultan Hasan madrasa, then returned to Damascus and held its judgeship from 767 AH until he died in office. His works include al-Fa'iq on the branches of fiqh and an unfinished Usul al-Fiqh. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.111}
