## Hayyan ibn Husayn Abu al-Hayyaj al-Asadi

Hayyan ibn Husayn Abu al-Hayyaj al-Asadi of Kufa, a Successor. He related from Ali and Ammar, and his sons Jarir and Mansur, Abu Wa'il and al-Sha'bi related from him. Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy, al-Ijli called him a trustworthy Successor, and Ibn Abd al-Barr said he was Ammar's secretary. The book gives no date of death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5493} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5494}
