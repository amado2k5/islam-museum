## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Hasan ibn Ali al-Shamunni, of Constantine origin and Alexandrian domicile, Abu al-Abbas Taqi al-Din, was a hadith scholar, Qur'an commentator and grammarian. He was born in Alexandria and studied and died in Cairo in 872 AH (1468 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.229}

His works include a commentary on Ibn Hisham's al-Mughni, Muzil al-Khafa 'an Alfaz al-Shifa, and Kamal al-Diraya fi Sharh al-Niqaya on Hanafi jurisprudence. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.229}
