## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Nafi' ibn al-Harith ibn Kalda al-Thaqafi, the son of [[person/nafi-ibn-al-harith|Nafi' ibn al-Harith]]. Al-Baladhuri relates that he and his brothers refused to sell their father's house, which adjoined the Great Mosque of Basra. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad demolished from it what he needed to square the mosque, while he was in his white palace on al-Bitah. Then he satisfied them with five cubits for every cubit, and opened for them a small door in the wall to the mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
