## Overview
Sadr al-Din 'Abd al-Mun'im b. al-qadi 'Ala' al-Din Ibn Muflih, the Hanbali imam and scholar; he took learning from his father and others, was of the people of knowledge and faith, a jurist who taught and benefited at Aleppo and elsewhere — good and humble, though he had not his father's share of the world. He died at Aleppo in Rabi' II 897. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 359-360}
