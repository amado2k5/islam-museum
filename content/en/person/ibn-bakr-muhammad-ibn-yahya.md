## Overview
Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Ahmad al-Ash'ari al-Malaqi, a Maliki jurist from Málaga in al-Andalus, known as Ibn Bakr. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born in 674 AH (1276 CE) and died in 741 AH (1340 CE), according to report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
