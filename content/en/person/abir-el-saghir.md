## Summary
Abir El Saghir is a Lebanese culinary content creator born in 1997, one of the most-followed food personalities in the Arab world; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Rise
She began posting short cooking videos in the early 2020s and grew at extraordinary speed on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, becoming among the most-followed food creators in the Middle East within a few years. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Style
Her signature videos pair precise, high-production cooking demonstrations — dishes from around the world alongside Lebanese staples — with tablescaping and hosting rituals of Arab hospitality, usually presented in elegant, wordless or minimally narrated formats. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Reach
Her audience numbers in the tens of millions across platforms, with particular strength among Arab and Muslim households; she has received regional digital-creator honours and brand partnerships. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Assessment
She is credited with raising food content to a cinematic standard in Arabic-language social media and with a wholesome brand built around hospitality; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}


## Content model
Her short-form cooking videos — polished, music-driven and under a minute — made her one of the most-followed food creators in Arabic and one of the most-subscribed women creators on TikTok globally. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Brand and businesses
She has built a commercial brand around her reach — product lines, restaurant ventures and media appearances — turning a social following into a food-media business. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Recognition
Her subscriber milestones on TikTok and YouTube — tens of millions combined — have been noted by platform and press rankings as among the largest of any Arab female creator. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir} {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}

## Origins
Born in Lebanon in 1998 and trained in culinary arts, she began posting cooking videos in the early 2020s — short, polished, music-driven clips that quickly made her among the most-followed food creators in Arabic. {cite:muslim500-abir-el-saghir}
