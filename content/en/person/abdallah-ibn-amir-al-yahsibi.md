## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Amir al-Yahsibi, kunya Abu Imran, one of the seven readers. It is said that he took the Qur'an from [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] and recited to him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.75}

He is in the first generation of the Successors from the people of Damascus, and died there in 118 AH. He transmitted from a group of the Companions, among them Wathila ibn al-Asqa, Fadala ibn Ubayd and Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan. Those who transmitted from him include Yahya ibn al-Harith al-Dhamari, Isma'il ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Muhajir, Hisham ibn al-Ghaz and Thawr ibn Yazid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.75}
