## Overview
Sul the Turk, a king whom the Turks made ruler after the wall of Jurjan had been taken. al-Baladhuri mentions him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## With Yazid ibn al-Muhallab
Sul presented himself to Yazid ibn al-Muhallab on the road to Khurasan. Yazid besieged him at Dihistan and then at al-Buhayra for six months. The siege ended in a treaty: his own safety and property, and that of three hundred of his household, in exchange for the surrender of al-Buhayra. He took part in [[event/yazid-jurjan-campaign|Yazid's campaign against Jurjan]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

The book also transmits the saying of Abu Ubayda Ma'mar ibn al-Muthanna that Sul was killed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
