## Overview
Umar ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Ma'mar al-Taymi, who was with Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura, one of the nobles of Sistan, at the conquest of Kabul {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Ibn Samura sent him with [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] to bring the good news of the conquest {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
