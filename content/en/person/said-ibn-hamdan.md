## Overview
Sa'id ibn Hamdan of the Banu Hamdan. Ibn al-Athir reports that al-Muqtadir sent him, together with Safi al-Basri, at the head of a large body of horse to Samarra, when news reached the caliph of Mu'nis's march from Mosul in the year 320 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.179}.
