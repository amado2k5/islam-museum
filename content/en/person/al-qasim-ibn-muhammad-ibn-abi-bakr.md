## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq, jurist of Medina and among the best men of his age; he kept close to his aunt 'A'isha in fatwa and sat with Ibn 'Abbas, Abu Hurayra and Ibn 'Umar. Ibn Shawdhab reported Yahya ibn Sa'id saying: we knew no one in Medina we would prefer over al-Qasim. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3072}

## Standing
Contemporaries held him the most learned of men in the Sunna, so much that it was said a man was not counted a man until he knew the Sunna; Ibn 'Uyayna named the three most learned in 'A'isha's hadith as al-Qasim, 'Urwa and 'Umra. He was among those who transmitted hadith letter for letter, with Ibn Sirin and Raja' ibn Haywa. He died around 110 AH leaving a hundred thousand, lawful wealth, as Ayyub noted. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3072}
