## Overview
Rabi'a ibn Nizar ibn Ma'dd ibn 'Adnan is an ancient Jahiliyya forefather, whose sons dwelt between al-Yamama, Bahrayn and Iraq; he is the one called Rabi'a of the Horse. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.17}

From his line come Banu Asad, 'Anza, Wa'il, Jadila, al-Di'l and others, branching into clans and sections still most numerous today; the talbiya of Rabi'a in the Jahiliyya when they made pilgrimage ran 'at Your service, our Lord, at Your service, for we have set out toward You', and some said 'at Your service on behalf of Rabi'a, hearing and obeying her Lord'. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.17}
