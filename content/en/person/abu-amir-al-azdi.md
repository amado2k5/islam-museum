## Overview
Abu 'Amir al-Azdi, qadi Mahmud b. al-Qasim b. al-qadi Abu Mansur Muhammad b. 'Abdallah al-Muhallabi of Herat, the Shafi'i jurist — transmitter of Tirmidhi's Jami' from al-Jarrahi; Abu Nasr al-Fami called him "peerless in asceticism, righteousness and chastity." Born 400, he died in Jumada II 487. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 383}
