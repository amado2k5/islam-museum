## Overview
Abu'l-Fath al-Karajiki — that is, al-Khayyami — Muhammad b. 'Ali, head of the Shi'a and man of works; comprehensive, a physician-mutakallim of many parts, among the senior companions of al-Sharif al-Murtada; author of Talqin awlad al-mu'minin. He died at Tyre in Rabi' II 449. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 291}
