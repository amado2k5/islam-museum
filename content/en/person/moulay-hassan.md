## Summary

Moulay Hassan (born 2003) is the Crown Prince of Morocco, the elder child of King Mohammed VI and Princess Lalla Salma, named after his grandfather King Hassan II {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en} {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-ar}. He has participated in official engagements with his father since 2013 and currently serves as Coordinator of the Offices and Services of the General Staff of the Royal Armed Forces {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en}.

## Life

Moulay Hassan was born at the Royal Palace of Rabat on 8 May 2003 and educated at the Royal College of Rabat, taking his high school diploma with honours in 2020 {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en} {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-ar}. He enrolled at the Faculty of Governance, Economics and Social Sciences of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University, majoring in international relations, and obtained his master's degree in May 2025 {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en}. He holds the rank of Colonel-Major in the Royal Armed Forces, promoted in 2025 {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en}.

## Work

As crown prince he was the youngest participant at the One Planet Summit in France in 2017, represented his father at the launch of the Tanger Med II port in June 2019 and at the funeral of Jacques Chirac in Paris that September, and in November 2022 inaugurated the International Exhibition and Museum of the Biography of the Prophet and Islamic Civilization at ICESCO in Rabat {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en} {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-ar}. He received France's Grand Cross of the Legion of Honour in October 2024 {cite:wikipedia-moulay-hassan-en}.
