## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Hajj Ahmad ibn Muhammad Dawud al-Tatwani al-Andalusi, a jurist, historian and journalist from Tetouan, who traces his descent to al-Andalus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.475}

## Death
He died in 1405 AH (1984 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.475}
