## Overview
Abu Bakr Zakariya, son of the Hafsid sultan [[person/al-mustansir-hafsi|al-Mustansir]], who titled himself "the chosen one to revive the religion of God". He is known in the museum's records as the lord of Bejaia. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1729}

## Rising to power
After escaping his father's death he went to Tlemcen and stayed with his in-law Uthman ibn Yaghmurasan. When the Arabs gave him their allegiance and the signs of power appeared, he took Constantine in 683 AH, then entered Bejaia in 684 AH and took Algiers. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1729}

## His effect
He cut off the western part of the lands of the capital, and the Hafsid state was divided into two states, as Ibn Khaldun relates. He is the one who laid out the madrasa for learning opposite the house of al-Aqwari in Tunis. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1730}

The dates of his birth and death are not in the museum's data and need an additional source.
