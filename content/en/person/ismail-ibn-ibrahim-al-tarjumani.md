## Overview
Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn Bassam, Abu Ibrahim al-Tarjumani, is described by al-Safadi as a learned and excellent scholar {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 47}.

## Life
Al-Safadi says that a great crowd attended his funeral {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 47}. The imam Ahmad wrote down hadiths from him and remarked on how fine they were {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 47}. He transmitted from Hushaym ibn Bashir and others {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 47}. He died in 236 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 47}, which corresponds to 850 CE by the museum's conversion (derived).
