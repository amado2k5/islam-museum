## Summary
Imam Mohamed Magid heads the All Dulles Area Muslim Society (ADAMS) Centre in Sterling, Virginia — one of the largest mosque communities in the United States — and served as president of the Islamic Society of North America, the country's main Sunni umbrella organisation. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-magid} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-magid}

## Formation
Born in 1965 in Sudan, the son of a leading scholar, he studied at the University of Khartoum and in other Islamic settings before emigrating to the United States in the 1980s, where he worked through American mosque communities toward leadership. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-magid}

## Career
At ADAMS he built a multi-campus congregation known for youth and interfaith programmes, and he chaired ISNA ⁦(2010–2014)⁩. He has advised US officials on counter-extremism — serving on the FBI's Muslim, Sikh and Arab advisory committee and working with the State Department — while publicly defending civil liberties of American Muslims. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-magid} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-magid}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as a model of the American imam who is simultaneously congregational pastor, interfaith partner and government interlocutor. His co-operation with federal agencies has drawn both praise for pragmatism and criticism from some activists; the museum records the positions attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-magid}
