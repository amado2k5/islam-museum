## Overview
Al-Faruthi, shaykh of Iraq, muqri' and sufi; he read the readings on Ibn al-Baqillani's companions, took the khirqa from al-Suhrawardi and lived in retreat a while. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 742}

## Al-Dhahabi's witness
Al-Dhahabi said: he came to us in 691, taught the readings and transmitted much; he held the khitaba after Ibn al-Marhal, then was removed after a year — he travelled with the pilgrims, entered Iraq and died at Wasit. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 742}
