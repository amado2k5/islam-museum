## Overview
Al-Shammakh ibn Dirar al-Thumali, also said al-Taghlabi, a poet. He was with Sa'id ibn al-As in the raid on Azerbaijan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His poetry
He said a verse about Bukayr ibn Shaddad mentioning horses at Mawqan. The text of the verse is in the data, and the source is al-Baladhuri's "Futuh al-Buldan". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
