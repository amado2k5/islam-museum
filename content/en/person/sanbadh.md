## Overview
Sanbadh, a man who rose up in Rayy at the start of the first Abbasid state. Umar ibn al-Ala', the butcher of Rayy, fought him with a force he had gathered, and he acquitted himself and inflicted losses {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
