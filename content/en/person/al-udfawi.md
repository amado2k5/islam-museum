## Overview
Ja'far ibn Tha'lab ibn Ja'far al-Udfawi, Abu al-Fadl Kamal al-Din, was a historian versed in letters, fiqh, the law of inheritance and music. Born at Edfu in Upper Egypt, he studied at Qus and Cairo and died at Cairo in 748 AH (1347 CE) after returning from the pilgrimage. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.122}

His works include al-Tali' al-Sa'id al-Jami' li-Asma' Nujaba' al-Sa'id, biographies of the men of his time; al-Badr al-Safir wa-Tuhfat al-Musafir in two volumes written in 790 AH on men of the seventh Hijri century; al-Imta' bi-Ahkam al-Sama'; and Fara'id al-Fawa'id on inheritance, besides verse and prose. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.122}
