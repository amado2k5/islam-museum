## Overview
Harish, a man of the army of the frontier in Tunisia. Al-Baladhuri reports in "The Conquests of the Lands" that he gathered a host against al-Aghlab ibn Salim, the governor of Musa al-Hadi, and besieged him in Kairouan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The end of the siege
Al-Aghlab was struck by an arrow and died, and Harish and his army were routed. Al-Aghlab's men pursued them for three days and killed them, and they killed Harish at a place known as the Sunday Market. The museum's data notes that the original also has "Nafid" and "Khurayish", so the correct form of the name needs an additional source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He took part in [[event/death-of-al-aghab|the killing of al-Aghlab]] as the defeated besieger who was killed.
