## Overview
A woman who composed verse on the day that [[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna ibn Hisn]] raided the [[group/banu-al-anbar|Banu al-Anbar]]. Ibn Ishaq names her only by her name: "Salma bint Attab then said on that day", and he gives two lines of hers {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270}. In the two lines she speaks of the evil that "Adi ibn Jundab" had met, and that enemies surrounded them on every side and their glory and fortune had left them. Ibn Hisham says that Adi ibn Jundab was of the Banu al-Anbar, and al-Anbar was the son of Amr ibn Tamim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270}. See [[work/salma-bint-attab-verses-on-banu-al-anbar|her verses]] and [[event/sariyya-of-uyayna-to-banu-tamim|the expedition of Uyayna]].

## Notes on the report
- Her attribution to Tamim is an inference from the lament's subject, the Banu al-Anbar, and not a statement in Ibn Ishaq.
- The editors of this edition explain "al-mahwat" as a low place between two mountains, and "al-judud" as good fortune and luck {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/270}.
