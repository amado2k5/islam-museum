## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Harith ibn Asad al-Tamimi al-Hanbali, of the Banu Tamim. The source counts him among the great Hanbali scholars in jurisprudence, the principles and the shares of inheritance. He was born in 317 AH (929 CE) and died in 371 AH (982 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.616}
