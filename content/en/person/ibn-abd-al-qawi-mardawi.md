## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Qawi ibn Badran al-Mardawi al-Maqdisi, Abu Abd Allah, Shams al-Din, was a Hanbali jurist {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. He was born at Marda, a village of Nablus, from which his nisba comes, and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}. The entry gives his years as 630 to 699 AH, 1232 to 1299 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books a «Kunnash» (notebook) on jurisprudence composed wholly in verse, printed under the title «Aqd al-Fara'id wa Kanz al-Fawa'id» in two volumes, which puts the issues of the Hanbali school into verse; a book on «Tabaqat al-Ashab» (generations of the school's scholars); and «Manzumat al-Adab», printed together with al-Saffarini's commentary on it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
