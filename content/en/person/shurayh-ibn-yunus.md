## Summary
Abu al-Harith Shurayh ibn Yunus. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 468 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], the notice runs from page 113 to page 115, and the next notice, al-Sari al-Saqati, begins on page 116. He is described as one known for the perfection of worship and servitude, from whom noble manners were taken, from whom much of the traces of the law was drawn, and from whom noble states were related; he died in 235 AH. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/113}

## A dream
Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak, one of the frequent weepers, relates that he saw Shurayh in a dream and asked how he fared. Shurayh said he had been forgiven and that his palace stood beside the palace of Muhammad ibn Bishr ibn Ata al-Sakandi. Ahmad said: you are greater for us than Muhammad ibn Bishr! Shurayh said: do not say that; God gave Muhammad a share in bearing every believing man and woman, because whenever he prayed he said: O God, forgive the believing men and women, the Muslim men and women, and those among them. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/113}

## Other reports
Shurayh said that he saw the Lord of might in a dream, and He said to him: Shurayh, ask your need. Muhammad ibn Ibrahim relates from Ma'mal ibn Shu'ayb that Shurayh once lay asleep above a water-channel, heard the sound of a frog in the mouth of a snake, and said: I ask you by God to release it; and it was released. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/113}

## Hadiths he transmitted
Shurayh transmitted many hadiths. Among them is a hadith from Jabir about a Bedouin who asked about the lineage of the Lord, in which the surah of sincerity was sent down (the surah is referred to, not quoted); a hadith from Jabir on reciting that surah at the end of a meal; a hadith from Abu Hurayra about a man who left the mosque when the call to the iqama was being given; a hadith from Abu Sa'id that the most severely punished on the Day of Resurrection is an unjust ruler; a hadith from Ali on straightening the rows; and a hadith from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet held a man briefly until he was sure of him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/113-114}

## The admonition of al-Irbad ibn Sariya
Shurayh relates the admonition of al-Irbad ibn Sariya: the Prophet led the dawn prayer and admonished them with an admonition that brought tears and fear to the heart; someone asked whether it was a farewell; he said: I counsel you to fear God and to hear and obey, and whoever lives after me will see much disagreement; hold to my Sunna and that of the rightly guided caliphs, bite onto it with your molars, and beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is error. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/115}

## The story of Zamzam
Shurayh relates from Ibn Abbas that Abd al-Muttalib saw in a dream that he should dig up a thing (the word is garbled in the scan), then was told to dig up Zamzam, and was shown its place between the dung and the blood, where the ants walk and the crow stands. He dug with his son al-Harith, and they brought up golden gazelles, ornaments, and swords; then the water. Then the lots were cast for the gazelle, the ornaments and the swords. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/115}
