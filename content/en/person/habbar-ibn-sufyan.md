## Overview
Of Banu Makhzum; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed at Ajnadayn in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him first of three men of Banu Makhzum among "the rest of the emigrants to Abyssinia", saying he was killed at Ajnadayn in the land of Syria in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 8987: "Habbar ibn Sufyan ibn Abd al-Asad ibn Hilal ibn Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Makhzum al-Makhzumi, nephew of Salama ibn Abd al-Asad"; he records that Musa ibn Uqba — from Ibn Shihab, Abu al-Aswad, Urwa and Muhammad ibn Ishaq — listed him among the emigrants to Abyssinia, and that he was martyred at Ajnadayn; so too said Abu Khalifa in al-Mubtada, Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Qaddami in al-Futuh, and Muhammad ibn Sa'd. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1537}

## His transmission
Al-Isma'ili in his Mu'jam of the Companions and al-Khatib in al-Mu'talif transmit through his son — Yahya ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Habbar, from his father — who said: "The Messenger of God passed…" his hadith. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1537}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-makhzum|Banu Makhzum]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}
