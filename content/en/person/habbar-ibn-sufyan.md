## Summary
Of Banu Makhzum; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed at Ajnadayn in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him first of three men of Banu Makhzum in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", saying he was killed at Ajnadayn in the land of Syria in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-makhzum|Banu Makhzum]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}
