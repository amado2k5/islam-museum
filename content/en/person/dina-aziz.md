# Dina Aziz

## Summary
Dina Aziz is an author, influencer and data analyst. She is the author of the Ramadan Planner, a guided planner for the fasting month published by Century, an imprint of Penguin, in February 2024. {cite:themuslimvibe-dina-aziz} {cite:penguin-ramadan-planner-dina-aziz}

## The Ramadan Planner
In her interview with The Muslim Vibe she said the idea for the planner came from watching a dear friend who had recently reverted to Islam struggle to keep up with everything during Ramadan, and that she designed it as a guide to reflection and growth for Muslims of every background and level of experience, whether observing their first Ramadan or deepening an established practice. The 208-page book combines explanations of the month's essentials with checklists, meal plans, reflection prompts and trackers for prayers, good deeds and goals. Penguin's author biography describes her as an influencer whose modest fashion, lifestyle and artistry content has drawn a vast following, and who shares glimpses of her planner each Ramadan. {cite:themuslimvibe-dina-aziz} {cite:penguin-ramadan-planner-dina-aziz}
