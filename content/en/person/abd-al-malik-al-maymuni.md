## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Hamid, the jurist Abu al-Hasan al-Maymuni al-Raqqi, was a companion of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal. Ibn al-Imad lists him among the deaths of 274 AH, in Rabi' al-Awwal, which corresponds to 887 CE by the museum's conversion {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 310}. Quoting Ibn Nasir al-Din, he gives the fuller name Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Hamid ibn Maymun ibn Mihran al-Maymuni al-Jazari al-Raqqi, Abu al-Hasan, and says that al-Nasa'i, Abu Awana and others held him reliable {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 311}.

## Teachers and closeness to Ahmad
He transmitted from Ishaq al-Azraq, from a Muhammad ibn Abd as the edition prints the name, and from others, and was a man of high standing among Ahmad's companions {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 310}. Ibn al-Imad says he was under a hundred years old when he died, and that Ahmad honoured and esteemed him and treated him as he treated no one else {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 310}.

Ibn al-Imad quotes him as saying he kept constant company with Abu Abd Allah, meaning Ahmad, from 205 AH to 227 AH, and that afterwards he would go away and return to him from time to time {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 pp. 310-311}. He reports that Ahmad likened his questioning to that of Ibn Jurayj with Ata for its frequency, and told him he did for no one what he did for him {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 311}.

## Questions put to Ahmad
Ibn al-Imad cites a question al-Maymuni put to Ahmad: whether the imam prays over a man who has killed himself. According to the report, Ahmad answered that the imam does not pray over one who has killed himself or over one who took from the spoils unlawfully, but that the Muslims do pray over them {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 311}. He also quotes al-Mardawi, near the end of al-Insaf, saying Ahmad honoured him and that he transmitted very many questions from him, in sixteen parts and two large parts {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 311}. From Ibn Nasir al-Din's al-Badi'a he reports that he is called al-Hafiz al-Maymuni {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 311}.
