## Overview
Ibn Yamin al-Basri, a poet. Al-Mas'udi relates that he was the first of the poets to describe the sword al-Samsama on the day al-Hadi placed it before him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323-324}

## The Samsama
He opened his verses with the words: "Amr has taken the Samsama of al-Zubaydi." Al-Hadi gave him the sword and a basket, and he shared out the basket among the poets. Al-Hadi then bought the sword from him for fifty thousand. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323-324}
