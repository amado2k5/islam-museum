## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdun al-Baghdadi, who died in 255 AH. He came from a Baghdadi literary family that flourished in the third century AH, and more than one member of it rose to distinction. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.230}
