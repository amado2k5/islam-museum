## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ismail ibn Ahmad, Abu Ishaq, Rukn al-Islam al-Bukhari al-Saffar, was a Hanafi jurist and ascetic called al-Zahid al-Saffar. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 534 AH, 1139 CE, and leaves the year of birth as a zero {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 32–33}.

## Career
He was from Bukhara and died there; he was severe in curbing sultans, and the sultan Sanjar banished him to Marw {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 32–33}.

## Works
His writings include Kitab al-Sunna wa-l-jama'a and Talkhis al-adilla li-qawa'id al-tawhid, the latter in manuscript in the Awqaf collection of Baghdad, number 5233, and in al-Azhar, number 3316 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 32–33}.
