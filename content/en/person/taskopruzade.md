## Overview
Ahmad ibn Mustafa ibn Khalil, Abu al-Khayr 'Isam al-Din, known as Tashkopruzade, was a historian, Turkish-born and naturalised Arab; born at Bursa and raised at Ankara, he took letters and fiqh and moved about the Turkish lands as teacher of fiqh, hadith and the Arabic sciences. He died in 968 AH (1561 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.257}

He held the judgeship of Istanbul from 958 AH, lost an eye and then his sight in 961 AH. His works include al-Shaqa'iq al-Nu'maniyya on the scholars of the Ottoman state, dictated at Istanbul in 965 AH; Miftah al-Sa'ada; Nawadir al-Akhbar on the merits of the good, a biographical lexicon; al-Shifa' li-Adwa' al-Waba', a treatise; al-Risala al-Jami'a on the useful sciences; and verse. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.257}
