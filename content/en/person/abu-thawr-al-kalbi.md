## Overview
Abu Thawr al-Kalbi, Ibrahim ibn Khalid al-Kalbi, a jurist who died in 240 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.497}

## What is known of him
The museum's data say he took jurisprudence from [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], and that he died in 240 AH according to al-Shirazi in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}

His upbringing, his works and his school are not set out here and need an additional source.

## In Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra — Taj al-Din al-Subki (al-Husayniyya edition)
Ibrahim b. Khalid b. al-Yaman, Abu Thawr al-Kalbi of Baghdad — a leading companion of al-Shafi'i. He began in the ra'y method of the Iraqis, then attached himself to al-Shafi'i on his arrival in Baghdad and turned to hadith. Ahmad b. Hanbal said "I have known him for the Sunna for fifty years; in my view he stands in the skin of Sufyan al-Thawri," and al-Nasa'i called him "one of the jurists, trustworthy". He died in Safar 240 AH {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.1 p.228}.
