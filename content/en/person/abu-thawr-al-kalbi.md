## Overview
Abu Thawr al-Kalbi, Ibrahim ibn Khalid al-Kalbi, a jurist who died in 240 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.497}

## What is known of him
The museum's data say he took jurisprudence from [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], and that he died in 240 AH according to al-Shirazi in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}

His upbringing, his works and his school are not set out here and need an additional source.
