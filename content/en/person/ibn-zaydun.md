## Overview
Abu al-Walid Ahmad ibn Abdallah Ibn Zaydun al-Andalusi al-Makhzumi, a poet and one of the notables of Córdoba. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.209-210}

## Wallada, daughter of al-Mustakfi
Al-Maqqari says that he loved [[person/wallada-bint-al-mustakfi|Wallada, daughter of al-Mustakfi]], and composed poems and fragments for her. When it became clear to her that he inclined toward her black slave-girl, she wrote to him: "If you were fair in love between us, you would not have desired my slave-girl." She gave him the nickname al-Musaddas (the hexagon). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.209-210}

## Among the house of Abbad
He presented the first apple of the season to al-Mu'tadid, the father of al-Mu'tamid. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.209-210}
