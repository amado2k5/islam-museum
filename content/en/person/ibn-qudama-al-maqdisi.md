## Overview
Muwaffaq al-Din Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Ahmad ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi al-Hanbali, a jurist from Jerusalem. {cite:lumat-al-itiqad}

## Works
He wrote [[work/lumat-al-itiqad|Lum'at al-I'tiqad]] (The Gleam of Belief), on the creed of the people of the Sunna in the manner of the early generations. The title page of the edition calls him a Hanbali. {cite:lumat-al-itiqad}

He also wrote [[work/al-mughni|al-Mughni]]. {cite:lumat-al-itiqad-ibn-uraiyir-2022}

## Birth and death
He was born in Jammaʿil in 541 AH, and died in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in 620 AH, according to the editor's introduction to the second edition of the *Lum'a*. It is also said that he died on the day of Eid al-Fitr and was buried on the slope of Qasiyun. These two dates come from one source and need an additional source. {cite:lumat-al-itiqad-ibn-uraiyir-2022 p.40}

## Ibn Taymiyya's praise
The editor relays from [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] that after al-Awza'i no jurist more learned than al-Muwaffaq had entered Syria. {cite:lumat-al-itiqad-ibn-uraiyir-2022}
