## Overview
Khalid ibn Ja'far al-Kilabi, boon companion of al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir, king of al-Hira. He interceded for al-Nabigha al-Dhubyani with the king's chamberlain until he brought him in. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 417-418}

## Other accounts
Al-Baladhuri says that he fought the Banu Abs and was killed, with his companions, when their chief Zuhayr ibn Judhayma was killed. He then went to al-Nu'man and sought his protection, fearing the pursuit of his people for the blood of Zuhayr. Al-Harith ibn Zalim entered and killed him, and his brother al-Ahwas went out with the Banu Amir to seek his killer, and that was the day of Rahrahan. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40}
