## Overview
Ibrahim al-Fazari the astrologer, author of a poem on the stars. Al-Mas'udi counts him among the astrologers of [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]], known for the sciences of the stars and the configuration of the heavens. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.668-669}
