## Overview
Ali ibn Abi Anbasa, in one manuscript "Ali ibn Anbasa", used to buy [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s needs in the market; al-Hadi told him: "Fear God, and look to what is between you and Him in what is bought for me; take nothing from anyone except for a price as people buy, and add nothing to what I pay, lest you sin." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.59} On p. 65 al-Kufi relates from him that al-Hadi asked him to buy a separate paper for writing on himself. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.65} See [[event/al-hadi-scrupulousness-and-refusal-of-revenue]].
