## Overview
Amr al-Rumi, a client of Musa al-Hadi. He ran the city of Musa in Qazwin and the endowment of Rustambadh, and after him his son Muhammad ibn Amr took it over. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He related to al-Rashid the vision of al-Mahdi about the two staves of Musa and Harun. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323}
