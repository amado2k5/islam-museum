## Overview
Mas'ud ibn Haritha, brother of [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] al-Shaybani. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## At Mihran
Al-Baladhuri relates that he was killed on the day of Mihran in 14 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Muthanna told the Muslims not to be terrified by the death of his brother, since the deaths of their best men were like this. They charged as one man. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
