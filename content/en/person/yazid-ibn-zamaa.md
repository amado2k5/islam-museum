## Summary
A Quraysh Companion of Banu al-Muttalib ibn Asad; he fought in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn campaign]] and was martyred when his horse called al-Janah bolted with him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/101}.

## His martyrdom
Ibn Ishaq relates that Yazid ibn Zam'a ibn al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib was martyred on the day of Hunayn after a horse of his called al-Janah bolted with him, threw him and killed him amid the fighting {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/101}.

## For young visitors
A Companion of Quraysh who fought in the battle of Hunayn and was martyred when he fell from his horse during the fighting.
