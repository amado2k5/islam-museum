## Summary
Shaykh 'Abdallah, famed as Hajji Khalifa, his origins in the province of Kastamonu; he mastered the outward sciences first, then attached himself to Shaykh Taj al-Din Ibn Bakhshi, receiving the Sufi path until licensed to guide, and was seated in his place after his death. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Character
He joined learning and gnosis, humble and reverent, of praiseworthy traits and blessed works, a showpiece of goodness, graced, the scholars' resort, raiser of the poor and righteous — a marvel in generosity and chivalry, cheerful and beautiful in character; he died at the end of Jumada II 894. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
