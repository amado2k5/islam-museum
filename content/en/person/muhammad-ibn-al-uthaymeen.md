## Summary

Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (1929–2001) was a Saudi Hanbali jurist, member of the Council of Senior Scholars, among the most influential contemporary Salafi shaykhs in university teaching, mosque lessons and referenced fatwas.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-al-uthaymeen}

## Life and formation

Born in Unaizah, Qassim, he memorised the Quran and studied under scholars of Qassim and Riyadh including Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'di, graduated from Riyadh's Faculty of Sharia, and worked as teacher and reconciliation judge, then professor at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud University.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-al-uthaymeen}

## Works and lessons

His books include Al-Sharh al-Mumti' 'ala Zad al-Mustaqni' (15 volumes), a tafsir of Surat al-Kahf, a commentary on Riyad al-Salihin, a commentary on Kitab al-Tawhid and Fiqh al-'Ibadat; his mosque lessons at the Prophet's Mosque and Unaizah's Grand Mosque were recorded by students and published.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-al-uthaymeen}

## Standing

He is described as among the twentieth century's most beneficial scholars for spreading knowledge in the Salafi world; his fatwas and books are a relied-upon reference for students in Saudi Arabia and abroad. He died in 2001; his library and lessons continue.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-ibn-al-uthaymeen}
