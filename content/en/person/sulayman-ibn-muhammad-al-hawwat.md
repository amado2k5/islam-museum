## Overview
Abu al-Rabi' Sulayman ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Hawwat al-Shafshawni al-Fasi. He came from a noble house of Fez, and was one of the scholars of the Maghrib in the thirteenth century AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Dates
He was born in 1160 AH (1747 CE) and died in 1231 AH (1816 CE), according to the encyclopedia Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
