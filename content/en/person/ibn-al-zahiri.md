## Summary
Ibn al-Zahiri, the hafiz and ascetic; one of those devoted to hadith, who wrote from seven hundred shaykhs in Syria, the Jazira and Egypt, and never ceased seeking, transmitting and tracing hadith to the end of his days; he was among the firm reliables; his father was a freedman of al-Zahir Ghazi ibn Yusuf. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 759-760}
