## Summary
Father of Ilyas; from Mudar descends Qays 'Aylan, from which branch Kinana, Asad, Tamim, Dabba, Hudhayl, Ghatafan and the rest of the Mudar clans {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## His place in the lineage
The Mudari tribes are set against the Rabi'iyya — the sons of his brother Rabi'a; from Mudar came the prophetic Quraysh and the houses of the Umayyad and Abbasid caliphs {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}.

## For younger visitors
Mudar ibn Nizar is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
