## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Ahmad ibn the great shaykh Kamal al-Din Abu'l-Fath Musa ibn Shaykh Radi al-Din Abu'l-Fadl Yunus ibn Man'a — Irbili by origin — Shafi'i jurist. He wrote a fine commentary on the *Tanbih* in fiqh and abridged the *Ihya' 'ulum al-din* twice, in a large and a small epitome. He held the teaching post at al-Malik al-Mu'azzam's madrasa at Irbil from early Shawwal 610, then moved to Mosul in 617 and the Qahiriyya madrasa was entrusted to him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## On him
Ibn Khallikan said: I used to attend his lesson as a boy, and I never saw anyone deliver lessons like him — he was among the ornaments of existence. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
