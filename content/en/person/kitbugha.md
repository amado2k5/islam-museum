## Overview
Kitbugha Noyan, commander of the Tatars over Syria; he took up residence in the citadel of Damascus as Hulagu's lieutenant after its surrender, received Baalbek and its fortress, took Nablus and its districts by the sword, and al-Malik al-Nasir remained in his service for months. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 298-299}

## Ayn Jalut
He was killed in the battle-line on the day of Ayn Jalut west of Baysan, on 15 Ramadan 658, with a company of the Mongol amirs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 299}
