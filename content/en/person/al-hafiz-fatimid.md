## Overview
Al-Hafiz li-Din Allah Abd al-Majid ibn Muhammad ibn al-Mustansir, the eleventh of the rulers of the Ubaydid state according to al-Suyuti. He took power after his nephew al-Amir, who had been killed in 524 AH without an heir. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.667} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.673}

He died in 544 AH, and his son al-Zafir Isma'il was installed after him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.673}
