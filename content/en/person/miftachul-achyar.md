## Summary
Kiai Haji Miftachul Achyar is the rais aam — supreme leader — of Nahdlatul Ulama, the world's largest Muslim organisation, a post he has held since 2018 after succeeding Ma'ruf Amin. A Surabaya pesantren kiai, he also chaired the Indonesian Ulema Council (MUI) from 2020 to 2023. {cite:wikipedia-miftachul-achyar} {cite:muslim500-miftachul-achyar}

## Life and formation
He was born on 30 June 1953 in Surabaya, East Java, the eighth of thirteen children of Kiai Abdul Ghoni, caretaker of the Tahsinul Akhlaq Rangkah pesantren. From the age of eight he moved between Java's great pesantren — Tambakberas and Tebuireng in Jombang, Sidogiri in Pasuruan, Lasem in Rembang, Mranggen in Demak — and sat in the teaching circle of Sayyid Muhammad bin Alawi al-Maliki in Malang. {cite:wikipedia-miftachul-achyar}

## Scholarly leadership
Settling at his grandfather's house in Kedung Tarukan, Surabaya, he opened a teaching circle that grew into the Miftachus Sunnah pesantren he still leads. His organisational ascent ran from rais syuriah of NU's Surabaya branch (2000–2005) to East Java provincial rais (2007–2018), then deputy rais aam, acting leader after Ma'ruf Amin's 2018 vice-presidential candidacy, and confirmed rais aam at the Lampung congress in 2021 — renewed at the 2026 congress. {cite:wikipedia-miftachul-achyar}

## Indonesian Ulema Council
Elected MUI chairman in November 2020, he resigned the post in March 2022 — formally in 2023 — saying one person should not hold both summits of Indonesian Islam, a gesture praised as restraint and criticised by some as creating a vacuum. The museum records both readings attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-miftachul-achyar} {cite:muslim500-miftachul-achyar}
