## Summary
'Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya Abu'l-Hasan, known as Thiqat al-Dawla Ibn al-Anbari, was among the notables and great men, a particular intimate of the imam al-Muqtafi li-amr Allah; he had adab and composed verse. He built a madrasa for the Shafi'is on the Tigris bank at Bab al-Azaj with a ribat for the sufis beside it, and made fine endowments for both. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
