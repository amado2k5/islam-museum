## Summary
Umayma bint [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and [[person/fatima|Fatima]], the Prophet's daughter — one of their daughters listed by Ibn Sa'd in his Tabaqat and by Ibn Hajar in his al-Isaba.

## Her marriage
She married her cousin [[person/abdullah-ibn-jafar|Abdullah ibn Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]], remaining of the Hashimite house of the Prophet's household.
