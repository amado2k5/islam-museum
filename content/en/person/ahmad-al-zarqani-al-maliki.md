## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Zarqani al-Maliki, a grammarian with a commentary on Ibn Hisham's Qawa'id al-I'rab. The book found no full biography in the sources it had, and suggests he may be the grandfather of Abd al-Baqi al-Zarqani. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16641}
