## Overview
Muhammad ibn Hamid ibn Ali, Abu Bakr al-Bukhari, a Hanafi jurist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} He heard from al-Haytham ibn Kulayb al-Shashi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} Al-Hakim called him the leading Hanafi scholar of Bukhara, the most learned in disputation and argument, the most ascetic, and the most committed to seclusion and to avoiding rulers. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966} He died in Bukhara, and the shops were closed for three days in his mourning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14966}
