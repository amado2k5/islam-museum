## Summary
An Aqaba man and Badri who was with the Prophet when his she-camel strayed; he drove Zayd ibn al-Lusayt from his camel's saddle when he learned of his remark. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/166}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "Umara ibn Hazm", describing him as an Aqabi Badri and uncle of Banu Amr ibn Hazm. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/166} He says Umara was with the Prophet when his she-camel strayed, and that when he learned of Zayd ibn al-Lusayt's remark he turned on him and ordered him down off his mount. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/166-167}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: uncle of Banu Amr ibn Hazm. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/166}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq describes him as "an Aqabi Badri"; the museum attributes the description to him.
