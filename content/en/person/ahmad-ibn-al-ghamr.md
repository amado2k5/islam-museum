## Summary
Ahmad ibn al-Ghamr al-Himsi, kept from idle play and music, strengthened with steadiness and patience. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 475 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 131–132. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/131-132}

## The monk's exchange
Ahmad ibn al-Ghamr said: I heard Muhammad ibn al-Mubarak al-Suri say: I asked a monk when a man reaches the reality of intimacy with God. He said: when affection is pure in him and his dealing between him and God is sincere. I asked: when does affection become pure and dealing become sincere? He said: when concern gathers and becomes one in obedience. I asked: when does concern gather into obedience? He said: when the many worries become one concern. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132}

## Little food and the road to rest
I asked: what helps against scant food? He said: scrutiny of earnings, and attention to clothing. I said: admonish me briefly. He said: eat what is lawful, and sleep wherever you like. I asked: where is the road to rest? He said: in opposing desire. I asked: when does a man find rest? He said: at the first step he takes into Paradise. I asked: by what is the road to God cut? He said: by continual vigil and thirst in the midday heat. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132}

## Signs of knowledge, ignorance and scruple
I asked the sign of knowledge: fear and compassion. The sign of ignorance: greed and desire. The sign of scruple: flight from places of doubt. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132}

## What keeps him in this state
I asked what kept him in this pledge. He said: I was told that whoever walks on the earth stumbles, so the fear of the wise hastened me to take refuge in the One in heaven from the trial of those on earth; I feared that the thieves of the intellect would steal my intellect. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132}

## Provision and contentment
On food he gave an answer which is garbled in the scan; it is not recorded here. He said that good opinion of God brings rest to the soul. He then recited verses that no one who clings to the world escapes it, and that death diminished the rank of a great king by removing him from what he had. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132}
