## Summary
Al-Qaysari, a Shafi'i jurist whom Ibn al-Salah counted among the leading scholars of Iraq. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16252} The text gives neither his name nor his dates. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16252}
