## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl al-Duri the merchant, Ja'far ibn Mukram; he heard Abu 'Amir al-'Aqadi, Rawh ibn 'Ubada and Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi among many others; Ibn Sa'id and others narrated from him — reliable and truthful; he died in Jumada I 264. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 136}
