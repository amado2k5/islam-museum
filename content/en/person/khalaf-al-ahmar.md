## Overview
Khalaf ibn Hayyan ibn Mihraz al-Ahmar, a transmitter and philologist from Basra, known for his memorisation and transmission of Arabic poetry. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 338-339}

## Life
He was born around 115 AH (733 CE) and died in 180 AH (796 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 338-339}
