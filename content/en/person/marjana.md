## Overview
Marjana, mother of Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad, who was the son of [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Her marriage and palace
Shirawayh al-Iswari married her, and he built for her the palace of Hazardar with many doors. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
