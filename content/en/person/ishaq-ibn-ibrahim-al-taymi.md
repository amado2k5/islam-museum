## Overview
Abu Ya'qub, known as al-Taymi al-Rayti and nicknamed "pearl"; he heard Isma'il b. 'Uluyya, Waki' b. al-Jarrah and others, and was truthful and trustworthy. He died in Sha'ban 259 {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 105}.
