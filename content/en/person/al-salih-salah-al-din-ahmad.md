## Overview
Al-Malik al-Salih Salah al-Din Ahmad b. al-Malik al-Zahir Ghazi b. Salah al-Din, lord of 'Ayntab; the elder brother of al-Malik al-'Aziz Ghiyath al-Din, lord of Aleppo — but he was kept back from kingship because his mother was a slave-girl while his brother's mother was Safiyya Khatun bint al-Malik al-'Adil; he died at 'Ayntab. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
