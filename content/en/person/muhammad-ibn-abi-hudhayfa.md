## Summary
Born in the land of Abyssinia to his father Abu Hudhayfa; Ibn Ishaq names him in "their children in Abyssinia" of Banu Abd Shams. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him in "their children in Abyssinia" of Banu Abd Shams, counting him among the five men born there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} The text states neither his mother's name nor his father's name beyond "Abu Hudhayfa"; and his father is not mentioned in the count of the emigrants of Banu Abd Shams on pp. 3-4.

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- His father "Abu Hudhayfa" does not appear in the main lists (pp. 3-10); the son's appearance here is evidence of his presence in Abyssinia, but he is uncounted.
