## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, a man of Bahrain with the kunya Abu Umar, had been put over [[place/athafit|Athafit]] after its people pledged to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]; the narrator describes him as one of the best of the Muslims, learned in God's unity and justice, a jurist, scrupulous and virtuous, who enjoined the right and forbade the wrong. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.95} This is the Zaydi narrator's description and is attributed to him. When [[person/arhab-ibn-al-daam|Arhab ibn al-Da'am]] and [[group/banu-salman-hamdan|Banu Salman]] entered Athafit he called the people out, a small group came and the rest let him down, and he was killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The book later says that a group of Banu Rabi'a and Banu Sarim had been with him and the attackers took their weapons and clothes, and that Banu Rabi'a sought redress from Arhab but al-Hadi would not let them raid. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.105} See [[event/arhab-takes-athafit-and-abu-umar-is-killed-285]] and [[event/end-of-the-battle-and-the-camp-at-al-darb-285]].

## Is he Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan of Najran?
On p. 85 the book names an Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan "of Najran" whom al-Hadi left as governor of Barat {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.85}; here it names him "of Bahrain". Both pages were re-read with Apple Vision and are printed as the OCR gave them, and the book's index puts pp. 31 and 95 under one entry "Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan" and does not list p. 85. It is therefore not settled whether they are one man; the two records stay separate, and neither identity nor difference is asserted. See [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan-al-najrani]].
