## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim 'Umar ibn al-Husayn ibn 'Abd Allah al-Khiraqi, jurist of the Hanbalis, author of the *Mukhtasar* on Ahmad ibn Hanbal's madhhab which became one of the two base-texts of the school. He transmitted from a circle; Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i, Ibn al-Sawwaf and 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Ja'far transmitted from him, and he was al-Marwazi's successor. He died on the day of Fitr and was buried at Bab Harb beside Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal's grave. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 207}
