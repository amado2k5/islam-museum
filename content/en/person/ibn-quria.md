## Summary
The qadi Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Rahman, known as Ibn Quri'a al-Baghdadi; he belonged to the entourage of the vizier Abu Muhammad al-Muhallabi, devoted to him; famous for speed of reply and witty answers in the plainest words and sweetest rhymed prose; his recorded questions and answers fill a well-known book — the chiefs and virtuosi of his age would play with him, sending him strange and comic questions to which he answered on the spot, matching the question; al-Sahib Ibn 'Abbad marvelled at him when he attended the vizier al-Muhallabi's majlis. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
