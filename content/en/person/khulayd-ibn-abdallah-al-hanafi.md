## Overview
Khulayd ibn Abdallah al-Hanafi. In a report al-Baladhuri relates, he was in command of the army sent to Herat, in the place of Aws ibn Tha'laba ibn Ruqayy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The report is a single account and needs an additional source.
