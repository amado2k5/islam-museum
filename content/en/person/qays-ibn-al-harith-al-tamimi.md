## Overview
Qays ibn al-Harith al-Tamimi, one of the men of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]] who came to the Prophet in 9 AH, whom Ibn Ishaq named within [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|their delegation]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The sources say nothing more about him than his name. The delegation became Muslim after the boasting contest. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
