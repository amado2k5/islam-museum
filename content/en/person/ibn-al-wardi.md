## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Umar ibn al-Muzaffar Ibn al-Wardi of Ma'arra, Shafi'i jurist and poet, who grew up in Aleppo and studied with the judge Sharaf al-Din al-Barizi at Hama. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/195} His works include al-Bahja al-Wardiyya, a versification of al-Hawi al-Saghir in 5,063 lines; he served as deputy judge and then judge of Manbij. He died in the great plague at the end of 749 AH, after writing a maqama about it. Ibn Hajar rejects al-Safadi's claim that Ibn al-Wardi took his ideas. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/195}
