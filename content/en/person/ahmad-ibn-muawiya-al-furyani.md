## Summary
Ahmad ibn Mu'awiya ibn al-Hudhayl, Abu Ja'far al-Furyani, and his brother al-Hudhayl ibn Mu'awiya were excellent in worship and in following the path of the substitutes and the friends of God; they heard hadith from the companions of al-Thawri and from al-Husayn ibn Hafs and others. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 676, sequence 677, on pages 394 to 396. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## Reports of the followers and the ascetics
He relates from Ibrahim al-Taymi his saying that a month or two would pass over him without his eating anything. He relates from Mutarrif ibn al-Shikhkhir: whoever purifies, purity is made his; whoever mixes, mixing is made his. He relates from Yahya ibn Abi Sa'id that of two brothers in Islam, one who is known and one who is not, the better is the one who is not known, since it is as his state is. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/395} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## Reports on conduct and remembrance
He transmits, through a chain from Ibn Mandah, reports from 'Aisha on the prohibition of insulting the dead and on counsel for one who misbehaved in the mosque, and on much seeking of forgiveness in one's record. The wording of these reports is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/395}
