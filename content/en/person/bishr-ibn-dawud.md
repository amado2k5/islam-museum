## Overview
Bishr ibn Dawud was governor of the frontier of Sind in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. Then he disobeyed and opposed, so al-Ma'mun sent Ghassan ibn Abbad against him. Bishr came out to him under safe conduct and came with him to the City of Peace (Baghdad). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
