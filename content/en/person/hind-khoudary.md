## Summary
Hind Khoudary is a Palestinian journalist born in Gaza in 1995 who became one of the most followed reporters on the war that began in 2023; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-hind-khoudary} {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Career
She worked as a freelance fixer and reporter for international outlets in Gaza, then during the 2023– war built a large audience through direct social-media reporting — video diaries of bombardment, displacement and civilian life — carried also by international broadcasters. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Reporting
Her coverage documented daily survival in Gaza — hospitals, displacement camps, food scarcity — and was cited by international media following the conflict; she was displaced repeatedly while continuing to report. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Recognition and scrutiny
Her work has been recognised by press-freedom organisations and widely shared; she has also faced public criticism and online attacks — including a 2020 controversy over her role in a Hamas-run authorities' arrest of a Gaza activist that press groups debated. The museum reports documented events without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Assessment
She is regarded as part of the generation of Palestinian journalists whose phone-camera reporting defined the global image of the Gaza war; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary} {cite:muslim500-hind-khoudary}


## War reporting
Her on-the-ground reporting from Gaza during the 2023-24 war — for Amnesty International dispatches, Al Jazeera English and other outlets — made her one of the most visible Palestinian field reporters of the conflict, at considerable personal risk. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Freelance model
Working freelance and through social platforms, she typifies a generation of Palestinian journalists whose reach comes through direct publishing and wire pick-up rather than a single employer. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Recognition
Her reporting has been cited in press-freedom discussions of journalists' protection in Gaza, and she has been named in profiles of women changing the frontline of Middle-East reporting. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary} {cite:muslim500-hind-khoudary}

## Origins
Born in Gaza in 1996, she studied media and began reporting as a freelancer in the mid-2010s, building a career inside a territory where journalism is practised under blockade and recurrent war. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## The Gaza press corps
Her work sits inside Gaza's freelance press corps — young reporters whose dispatches, photographs and social posts provide a large share of the world's on-the-ground picture of the territory. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary}

## Risk and visibility
Press-freedom documentation of the 2023-24 war noted the extraordinary casualties among Palestinian journalists; Khoudary's continued reporting through the period made her among the most-recognised of the surviving corps. {cite:wikipedia-hind-khoudary} {cite:muslim500-hind-khoudary}
