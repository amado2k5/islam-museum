## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Ibrahim, Abu Amir al-Marini, styled the sultan al-Mustansir bi-llah, was one of the kings of the Marinid dynasty in the Maghrib. Al-Zirikli lists him in al-A'lam under the heading al-Mustansir al-Marini, gives his death as 800 AH, 1398 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 68}.

## Reign
He was given the pledge of allegiance after the death of his brother Abd al-Aziz in the early part of 799 AH, and the conduct of affairs was in the hands of the viziers {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 68}. Death overtook him while he was still young, and his reign lasted a year and five months less some days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 68}.
