## Summary
Shems Friedlander was an American convert to Islam who joined the Halveti-Jerrahi order of Istanbul, became an award-winning graphic designer and spent four decades at the American University in Cairo teaching design and writing authoritative books on Rumi and the Mevlevi dervish tradition. {cite:muslim500-shems-friedlander} {cite:wikipedia-shems-friedlander}

## Formation
Born in 1940 in New York, he trained in design, converted to Islam in the 1970s within the circle of Muzaffer Ozak, the Halveti-Jerrahi sheikh, and was later authorised as a muqaddam of the order. {cite:muslim500-shems-friedlander} {cite:wikipedia-shems-friedlander}

## Career
A professor of practice at the American University in Cairo, he combined an advertising and design career — with industry awards — with films, photography and books including The Whirling Dervishes and a biography of Rumi that became standard English-language introductions to the subject. He died on 22 November 2022. {cite:muslim500-shems-friedlander} {cite:wikipedia-shems-friedlander}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 records him in its obituaries as the designer-scholar who carried Mevlevi aesthetics to Western readers — convert, artist and teacher in one career. {cite:muslim500-shems-friedlander}
