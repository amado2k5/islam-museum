## Overview
Mihjan ibn al-Adra' al-Aslami was a Companion and one of the great archers; he lived at Medina, then at al-Sabra, whose mosque he laid out, and he lived long, dying in 60 AH (680 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.283}

He transmitted five hadiths. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.283}
