## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Akwa, a scholar of jurisprudence and inheritance, a philologist and a prolific writer. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His learning
He had wide knowledge of the history of Yemen in the Islamic age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 1321 AH (1903 CE) and died in 1419 AH (1998 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
