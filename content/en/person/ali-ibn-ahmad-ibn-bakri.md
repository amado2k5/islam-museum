## Overview
Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Bakri, or, as some say, ibn Umar ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Baqi ibn Bakri, Abu al-Hasan, was the keeper of the book collection at the Nizamiyya, and al-Safadi titles him the scribe. Al-Safadi gives his death as 575 AH and no year of birth {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 20 pp. 103-104}.

## Life
Al-Safadi says he had a good knowledge of literature, studied grammar under Abu al-Sa'adat Ibn al-Shajari, under Abu Mansur al-Jawaliqi and others, and was accomplished, with a fine hand and exact copying {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 20 pp. 103-104}. He adds that he copied many literary books, more than can be counted {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 20 pp. 103-104}.
