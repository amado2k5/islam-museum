## Overview
Umm Muhammad bint Abd Allah ibn Uthman ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi, the wife of Salm ibn Ziyad {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Crossing the river
She crossed the river with her husband into Khurasan. al-Baladhuri reports that she was the first Arab woman to cross it. She bore him a son whom he named al-Sughdi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

She borrowed the jewellery of the wife of the lord of Sughd, then went back and returned it, and she took her away {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
