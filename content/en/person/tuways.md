## Overview
Tuways, the singer. al-Mas'udi reports that he was one of the singers in the days of al-Walid ibn Yazid, when the desire for singing overcame al-Walid and both the elite and the common people {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.201}.

The entry does not record his lineage or his year of death. It needs an additional source.
