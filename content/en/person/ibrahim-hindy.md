# Ibrahim Hindy

## Summary
Ibrahim Hindy is a Canadian imam and scholar based in Mississauga, Ontario. He is the imam of Dar Al-Tawheed Islamic Centre and the Religious Director for Yaqeen Canada at the Yaqeen Institute for Islamic Research, and he chairs the Muslim Council of Peel. {cite:canadadocs-ibrahim-hindy} {cite:nccm-ibrahim-hindy}

## Education and work
He attained a degree in Religious Studies at the University of Toronto and later an MA in Usul al-Fiqh from Al-Madinah International University in Malaysia. {cite:canadadocs-ibrahim-hindy} He hosts The Hindy Project Podcast, in which he interviews imams and scholars, and co-hosts Yaqeen Institute's Sincerely Yours talk show. {cite:listennotes-hindy-project} In April 2017 the National Council of Canadian Muslims named him among the local imams targeted by violent threats linked to a campaign against school prayers in Peel, and quoted his call for all Canadians to stand together against hatred. {cite:nccm-ibrahim-hindy}
