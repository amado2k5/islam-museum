## Overview
The Hanbali jurist and ascetic Abu Khazim Muhammad b. Muhammad b. the judge and imam Abu Ya'la, brother of the judge Abu'l-Husayn; born in Safar 457 — a year before his father's death, who gave him licence — he heard hadith from Ibn al-Muslima, Ibn al-Ma'mun and others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 562}

## His fiqh and his death
He read fiqh under the qadi Ya'qub, kept to him and took dictation from him, excelling in the madhhab, khilaf and usul, and wrote useful works including Kitab al-tabsira fi'l-khilaf, Kitab ru'us al-masa'il and a commentary on Mukhtasar al-Khiraqi; he was of the ascetic jurists, the good and righteous. He died Monday 19 Safar 527 and was buried at his house by Bab al-Azaj, then moved in 534 to Imam Ahmad's cemetery where he was laid beside his father. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 562}
