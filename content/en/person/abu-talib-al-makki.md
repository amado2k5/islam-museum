## Overview
Abu Talib Muhammad b. 'Ali b. 'Atiyya al-Harithi, the preacher of Mecca, author of Qut al-qulub; he was not of Mecca's people but of al-Jabal — he settled at Mecca and was named for it. A righteous man strenuous in worship who used riyada much — it is said he abandoned food so long his skin greened. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His preaching
He entered Basra after Abu'l-Hasan Ibn Salim's death and attached himself to his doctrine; he came to Baghdad and preached, but muddled his speech — he was left and shunned, and kept from speaking thereafter. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
