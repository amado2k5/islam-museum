## Summary

Bujayr ibn Abi Bujayr al-Absi, an ally of the Ansar. Musa ibn Uqba, from Ibn Shihab, lists him among those at Badr, and so does Ibn Ishaq. Ibn Manda says no hadith is known from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.131}
