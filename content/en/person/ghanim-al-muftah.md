## Summary
Ghanim Al-Muftah is a Qatari motivational speaker and entrepreneur born with Caudal Regression Syndrome, a rare condition that left him without the lower half of his body; he made his personal story a global platform for hope and disability awareness, peaking in visibility when he appeared in the opening ceremony of the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar before millions of viewers. {cite:wikipedia-ghanim-al-muftah}

## Upbringing
He was born in 2002 in Doha with a rare diagnosis affecting the lower spine; his mother has recounted in interviews that she and his father refused doctors' suggestions and raised him and his twin without distinction. Al-Muftah learned swimming, skating and climbing despite his mobility impairment, documented ascents of several summits including attempts toward Everest Base Camp and major peaks as a record-bearing hand-climber, and climbed Mount Kilimanjaro. {cite:wikipedia-ghanim-al-muftah}

## Public career
He became known as a teenage motivational speaker in Gulf schools and conferences, gathered millions of followers on his accounts, founded with his mother the social enterprise Gharissa and the platform Ghinima for products, and launched initiatives including free wheelchair distribution at home and abroad. He was named goodwill ambassador for humanitarian agencies and bodies and was the youngest ambassador of the 2022 World Cup; on 20 November 2022 he appeared in the opening ceremony in a dialogue scene with actor Morgan Freeman broadcast worldwide — a scene that became among the most famous images of the tournament. {cite:wikipedia-ghanim-al-muftah} {cite:muslim500-ghanim-al-muftah}

## Recognition and institutional work
He has held the title of GCC youth ambassador, received the Gulf's most beautiful initiative award, been listed among influential Arab youth, and lectures in schools and universities inside and outside Qatar on overcoming disability. {cite:wikipedia-ghanim-al-muftah}

## Legacy
Al-Muftah is credited with turning disability from an object of pity into an object of example: an athlete, speaker and social entrepreneur who stood on the largest sporting stage on earth and became, for families of people with disabilities in the Gulf, a public face of what their children can do. {cite:wikipedia-ghanim-al-muftah}
