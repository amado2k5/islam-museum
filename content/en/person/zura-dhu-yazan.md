## Overview
Zur'a Dhu Yazan, one of the kings and lords of Himyar. Malik ibn Murra al-Rahawi was sent by him to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] with the news of their Islam and their renunciation of idolatry {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.

The Prophet wrote to him, enjoining the good treatment of his envoys and of their amir [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]], and gave him good tidings because he was the first of the [[group/himyar|Himyar]] to embrace Islam. He appears in [[event/letter-of-himyar-kings|the letter of the kings of Himyar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.
