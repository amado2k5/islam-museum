## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Qasim ibn Khalifa ibn Yunus al-Sa'di al-Khazraji, a physician and historian of medicine from a Damascene family. He wrote "Uyun al-Anba' fi Tabaqat al-Atibba'" (Sources of Information on the Classes of Physicians). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.131}

## Death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as around 668 AH (around 1270 CE). The date is approximate and comes from a single source, so it needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.131}
