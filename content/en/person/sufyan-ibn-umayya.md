## Overview
Sufyan ibn Umayya ibn Abd Shams, one of the scribes of Quraysh. al-Baladhuri reports that he learned spelling, then handwriting, from Bishr ibn Abd al-Malik in Mecca {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
