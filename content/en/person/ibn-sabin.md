## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Nasr, known as Ibn Sab'in, a famous Sufi philosopher from al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.203-204}

## Lisan al-Din's view
Al-Maqqari relays from Lisan al-Din Ibn al-Khatib that his fame, his standing in understanding and opinion, his depth in philosophy and his defence of the doctrines of the theologians are things to be wondered at. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.203-204}

## Events
He sent his brother Abu Talib ibn Sab'in as an agent of Ibn Hud to address the Christian bishop of Rome, when the Christian tyrant broke faith with him. He also composed the letter of allegiance of the people of Mecca to the Hafsid sultan al-Mustansir, in which he indicated that al-Mustansir is the Mahdi who was announced. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.203-204}
