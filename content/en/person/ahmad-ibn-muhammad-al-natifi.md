## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Natifi al-Tabari, a Hanafi jurist of Rayy (d. 446 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}

## Works
His works on Hanafi fiqh include al-Waqi'at, al-Ajnas wa al-furuq, al-Hidaya and al-Ahkam. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}
