# Nusratullo Maksum

Nusratullo Maksum (Tajik: Нусратулло Махсум; 1 July 1881 – 1 November 1937), also known as Nusratullo Lutfullayev, was a Tajik Soviet politician. He was born at Chashma-kuzi in the Gharm District, and he was a recipient of the Order of the Red Banner.{cite:wikipedia-nusratullo-maksum-en}

In 1925 Maksum became the first Prime Minister of the Tajik SSR, serving until 1926. From 16 December 1926 to 28 December 1933 he sat on the Presidium of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee as the representative of the Tajik Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic and then of the Tajik Soviet Socialist Republic.{cite:wikipedia-nusratullo-maksum-en}

Maksum was executed by firing squad on 1 November 1937, during the Great Purge, and his resting place is the Kommunarka shooting ground. After the independence of Tajikistan from the Soviet Union, his portrait was placed on the 200-somoni banknote.{cite:wikipedia-nusratullo-maksum-en}
