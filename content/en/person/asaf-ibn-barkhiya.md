## Overview
Asaf ibn Barkhiya, the maternal nephew of Solomon ibn Dawud, a Hebrew. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.430}

## Solomon's scribe
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was among those who wrote for Solomon, together with Yusuf ibn Isu the Hebrew and Hurmuzaz ibn al-Kardul the Persian Hebrew. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.430}
