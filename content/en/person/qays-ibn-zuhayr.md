## Overview
Qays ibn Zuhayr ibn Jadhima ibn Rawaha al-'Absi, Abu Hind, was the amir and strategist of 'Abs and one of the leading chiefs of the Arabs of Iraq, nicknamed Qays al-Ra'y for the quality of his judgement; he died in 10 AH (631 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.205}

He is counted among princes, schemers, valiant men, orators and poets. He inherited the command from his father; his exploits in the wars with Banu Fazara and Dhubyan are famous, his wisdom in transmitted sayings widespread, his sermons many, his verse strong and masterly. In his last years he turned ascetic and journeyed to Oman, abstaining from fine food until he ate colocynth; he stayed in Oman until he died, and his cunning became proverbial. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.205}
