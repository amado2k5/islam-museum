## Overview
Umm Musa al-Hashimiyya was appointed housekeeper (qahramana) of the household of al-Muqtadir billah in the year 298 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47-48}.

## Her role
She carried letters from al-Muqtadir and his mother to the vizier {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47-48}.

Ibn al-Athir says that he mentioned her because she later had such a share of authority in the state that her mention was necessary {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47-48}.
