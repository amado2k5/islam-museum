## Overview
Maymun al-Jurjumani, a commander of the Umayyad period. Al-Baladhuri relates that he was a Roman slave of the Banu Umm al-Hakam, sister of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]], and that he came to be called al-Jurjumani because he mixed with the Jarajima. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.219}

## From slavery to command
He went out with the Jarajima in the Mount Lebanon. When [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] heard of his valour and courage, he asked his masters to free him, and they did. He then made him commander of a group of troops and stationed him at Antioch. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.219}

## Al-Tawana
He raided al-Tawana with Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik at the head of a thousand men of Antioch. Al-Baladhuri says that he was killed as a martyr after good conduct and a notable stand. Abd al-Malik was moved by his loss, and sent a great army to the Romans seeking vengeance for him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.219}
