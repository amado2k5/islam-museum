## Summary
Ahmedi: Ottoman poet, linked in the museum's record to Anatolia. The Biblissima record places his life between 1334 and 1413 (the record marks the dates as uncertain). {cite:biblissima Q540} The museum's record card names the Iskandarname as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Ahmedî» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q540}
