## Overview
Suhaym, client of Utba ibn Farqad. Utba sent him to Umar with two baskets of Khabis, and Umar tasted it and wrote to Utba his well-known letter. See [[event/utba-khabis-letter|Umar's letter to Utba about Khabis]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
