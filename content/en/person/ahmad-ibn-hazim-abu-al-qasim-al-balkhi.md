## Summary
Ahmad ibn Hazim, Abu al-Qasim al-Saffar al-Balkhi, a great Hanafi jurist to whom people travelled in Balkh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2054}

## Works
He said that he differed from Abu Hanifa on a thousand questions, and that the fatwa of his day followed his view on them. He died in 326 AH, or 336 by another report. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2054}
