## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak is, in the narrator's words, "qadi of Hamdan, its jurist and its scholar, to whom they go in that and in all that they need of the lawful and the unlawful". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] relates from him that he and Murawwi ibn Abdullah al-Sa'idi pledged to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], knowing, as they put it, that he was the most upright of men in God's right, and told what they saw of the effect of his prayer on [[person/abu-mihjan-abd-al-yafur|Abu Mihjan]] and [[person/jaftam|Jaftam]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The book's index lists "Ibn al-Dahhak" at many places including pp. 140, 144, 145, 183 to 185, 245 and 249, none of them yet read, so nothing beyond p. 140 is ascribed to him here. See [[event/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-and-murawwi-on-al-hadis-prayers-against-abu-mihjan-and-jaftam-286]].
