## Summary
[[person/jabir-ibn-abdullah|Jabir ibn Abdullah]] ibn Amr ibn Haram al-Ansari al-Sulami, kunya Abu Abdullah — also said Abu Abd al-Rahman, also said Abu Muhammad; one of the prolific narrators from the Prophet {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}. His father is [[person/abdullah-ibn-amr-ibn-haram|Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Haram]]; both he and his father had companionship {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}.

## Aqaba and the campaigns
In the Sahih he was among those who witnessed Aqaba {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}; his name recurs in the [[event/pledges-of-aqaba|pledges of Aqaba]]. Bukhari in his Tarikh relates with a sound chain from Jabir: "I used to pour water for my companions on the day of Badr" — al-Waqidi denied this account from Jabir {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}.

Jabir says the Messenger of God campaigned twenty-one expeditions himself, of which he witnessed nineteen {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}. In Muslim: "I witnessed neither Badr nor Uhud — my father forbade me; when he was killed I never stayed behind again" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}. And from him: "the Messenger of God asked forgiveness for me twenty-five times on the night of the Camel" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}.

## The study circle
In Waki's Musannaf from Hisham ibn Urwa: Jabir ibn Abdullah had a circle in the Mosque — meaning the Prophet's — from whom knowledge was taken {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}.

## His last days and death
Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada relates that Jabir came to them after his sight had gone {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}. Some say he was the last Companion to die in Medina, but al-Baghawi says that is an error and the last was Sahl ibn Sa'd {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}. Yahya ibn Bukayr said he died in year seventy-eight; Ibn Hajar says al-Haytham ibn Adi's view — that he died in year seventy-four — agrees with al-Tabari and Bukhari's Tarikh, which have al-Hajjaj present at his funeral; it is also said year seventy-three, and it is said he lived ninety-four years {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 196}.
