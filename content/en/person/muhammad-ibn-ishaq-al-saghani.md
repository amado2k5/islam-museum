## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ishaq, Abu Bakr al-Saghani and later al-Baghdadi, a hadith hafiz and authority (hujja), is recorded by Ibn al-Imad among those who died in 270 AH, in the month of Safar; the year corresponds to 883 CE by the museum's conversion (derived) {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 301}. The editor notes that the name is also given as al-Sughani {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 301}.

## Studies
Ibn al-Imad says he heard from Yazid ibn Harun and the teachers of his generation {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 3 p. 301}.
