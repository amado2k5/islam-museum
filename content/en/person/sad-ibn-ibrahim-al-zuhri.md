## Overview
Sa'd ibn Ibrahim ibn Sa'd ibn Ibrahim ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, father of 'Abdallah and 'Ubaydallah; he heard his father, Ibn Abi Dhi'b and 'Ubayda ibn Abi Ra'ita, and his two sons, Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Burjulani and Muhammad ibn Sa'd narrated from him. He died in 201 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1771}
