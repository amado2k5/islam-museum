## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Wahb al-Misri was among the leading students of Malik ibn Anas; he transmitted Malik's books, his sunan and the Muwatta, and was upright and trustworthy, according to the Fihrist. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277}

## Details
Ibn al-Nadim records in the Fihrist, among Malik's companions, that Abd Allah ibn Wahb transmitted from Malik his books, sunan and Muwatta and was upright and reliable. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277} He lists him among those who took from Malik together with Dawud ibn Abi Hind and his son Sa'id, Abu Bakr, Abu Mu'il, Abu Uways, al-Mughira ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Hizami, Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Sama al-Majishun, Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Hakam al-Misri, Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim of Egypt, Ashhab ibn Abd al-Aziz of Egypt, and al-Layth ibn Sa'd who was at first of his school then chose his own way. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277} Ibn Wahb is thus, in his account, one of the major transmitters of Malik's teaching. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 277}
