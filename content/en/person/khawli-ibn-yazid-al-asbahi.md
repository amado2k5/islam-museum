## Overview
A man of Himyar. A report in al-Suyuti's *Tarikh al-Khulafa'* says that he finished off al-Husayn on the day of Karbala', cut off his head and brought it to Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 341-342}

The source attributes to him verses that he said about it. The report is a single account in the source and needs an additional source.
