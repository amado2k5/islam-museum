## Summary
Son of Jahm ibn Qays; he accompanied his father to Abyssinia and came with him in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him son of Jahm ibn Qays alongside his brother Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq counted Jahm and those with him as one "man" in the total of sixteen (p. 5); counting the sons separately is not guaranteed.
