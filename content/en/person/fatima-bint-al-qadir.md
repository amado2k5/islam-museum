## Summary
Fatima, daughter of the caliph al-Qadir and sister of the caliph al-Qa'im; she died in 448, and her bier was brought out with the bier of al-Dhakhira Abu'l-'Abbas b. al-Qa'im. The caliph prayed over them in the Court of Peace while the ra'is al-ru'asa sat with the two biers in the pavilion, and they were borne to Rusafa — no more than forty attended the mourning, the town empty and its people perished of death and want. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.517}
