## Overview
Husayn ibn al-Hasan was a man "of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali", a cousin of [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] as the latter's son [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] tells it, and governor for al-Hadi at [[place/saqayn-yemen|Saqayn]] when Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah passed him on his way, in hiding, to Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116} He took him by the hand and the two went out in fear until they reached [[place/saada|Sa'da]], where they stayed some days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.116} The text gives neither his full line nor his death year, and nothing is asserted beyond what is said. The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers. See [[event/ali-ibn-muhammad-reaches-saada-and-the-tabari-emigrants-285]].
