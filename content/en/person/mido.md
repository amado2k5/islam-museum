# Mido

## Summary

Mido — full name Ahmed Hossam Hussein Abdel Hamid Wasfi — is an Egyptian football manager and former striker, born in Cairo in 1983. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en} A Zamalek youth product, he won the Belgian Ebony Shoe with Gent and went on to play for Ajax, Marseille, Roma, Tottenham Hotspur and Middlesbrough among many clubs; he won 51 caps and scored 19 goals for Egypt, and after retiring became a coach, managing Zamalek twice among other clubs. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}{cite:wikipedia-mido-ar} The sources file him generically as a Muslim and name no school for him, so his data record leaves his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}{cite:wikidata-mido}

## A much-travelled playing career

Mido began at Zamalek in 1999 and moved to Belgium's Gent in 2000, where his scoring won him the Ebony Shoe and a 2001 transfer to Ajax. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}{cite:wikipedia-mido-ar} A loan at Celta Vigo was followed by Marseille in 2003 and Roma in 2004; in England he played for Tottenham Hotspur — first on loan, then permanently — and Middlesbrough, with further loans at Wigan Athletic, Zamalek and West Ham United, before returning to Ajax and Zamalek and finishing his playing career at Barnsley in 2012–13. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en} In all he made 219 club appearances and scored 73 goals. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}

## Egypt

Mido played for Egypt's under-20 side and then the senior national team from 2001 to 2009, winning 51 caps and scoring 19 goals. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en} He was a member of the Egypt squad that won the 2006 Africa Cup of Nations on home soil. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}

## Coaching and television

Mido moved into coaching in 2014 with Zamalek, and has since managed Ismaily (twice), Zamalek again, Wadi Degla, Saudi club Al Wehda and Misr Lel Makkasa, alongside work as a television presenter and pundit. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}{cite:wikipedia-mido-ar} His father, Hossam Wasfi, had also played for Zamalek; Mido married at 19 and has three sons. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en} At 34 his weight reached 150 kg and put him at risk of diabetes; he lost 37 kg in five months. {cite:wikipedia-mido-en}
