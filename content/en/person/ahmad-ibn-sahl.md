## Overview
Ahmad ibn Sahl al-Samani, one of the leading commanders of the Amir Ahmad ibn Isma'il al-Samani. He was among those sent with the army that conquered Sistan in 298 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.46}
