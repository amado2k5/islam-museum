## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali, known as Sipahizade al-Brusawi, was a learned man from Bursa, in Turkey. The entry in al-A'lam dates his death to 997 AH, 1589 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names Awdah al-masalik ila marifat al-buldan wa-l-mamalik, in manuscript, in which he arranged Abu al-Fida's Taqwim al-buldan by letter and added what he gathered from other works, and Unmudhaj al-funun, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
