## Overview
Yahya ibn Hassan, Abu Zakariyya, al-Shami and later al-Misri al-Tinnisi, was a scholar of hadith from Damascus. He moved from there to Egypt, settled at Tinnis, became well known and died in Egypt. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 144 to 208 AH, 761 to 823 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.

## Who narrated from him, and what was said of him
Imam al-Shafi'i narrated from him and died before him. Al-Zirikli reports from Zakariyya al-Ansari that wherever al-Shafi'i narrates from 'the trustworthy one' from al-Layth ibn Sa'd, he means Yahya ibn Hassan. Ibn Yunus said that he was trustworthy and good in hadith, and that he composed books {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.
