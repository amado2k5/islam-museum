## Overview
Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Hasani, once read "al-Husayni" in the machine-read text, is one of the few whom Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah names as accompanying [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] on his journey to Yemen. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.38} When the people met them in large numbers at Zubayd and surrounded them, Yusuf went to al-Hadi to tell him that the people had closed in on him and his companions and that he should shorten his prayer; al-Hadi paid no heed and completed his prayer as it should be. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.40} See [[event/al-hadi-road-to-saada-well-and-zubayd]].
