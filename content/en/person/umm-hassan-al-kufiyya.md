## Summary
Umm Hassan al-Kufiyya was a devout woman of [[place/kufa|Kufa]], placed by Ibn al-Jawzi in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] first among the named women ascetics of Kufa; he says [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]], [[person/abd-allah-ibn-al-mubarak|Ibn al-Mubarak]] and others used to visit her. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.602}

## Sufyan's visit
He relates from Ibn al-Mubarak that al-Thawri mentioned a woman of Kufa given to striving and worship; they entered her house and found nothing in it but a worn piece of matting. Al-Thawri said, "If you wrote a note to one of your cousins, they would improve your condition." She replied that he had been greater in her eyes before this moment: she did not ask the world of the One who has power over it and owns it, so how could she ask it of those who have no power over it? — and she did not want any moment to come in which she was busied with other than God. Sufyan wept. Ibn al-Mubarak said: I heard that Sufyan married her. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.602}

## For young visitors
A poor woman of Kufa who worshipped God a great deal. When the scholar Sufyan al-Thawri suggested she ask her relatives for help, she said she asked only God.
