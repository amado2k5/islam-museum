## Overview
Humay, daughter of Bahman ibn Isfandiyar, one of the kings of the Persians. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.550}

## Building at Istakhr
Al-Mas'udi reports that she brought out the great building at Istakhr and made it a fire temple. The fire was later moved away from it and it fell into ruin, and it came to be known among the people as the Mosque of Solomon. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.550}
