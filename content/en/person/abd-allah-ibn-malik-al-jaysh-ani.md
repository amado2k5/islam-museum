## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Malik al-Jaysh-ani, Abu Tamim, one of the jurists of the Successors in Egypt. Al-Shirazi mentions him with [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-usayla-al-sunabihi|Abd al-Rahman ibn Usayla al-Sunabihi]], and says that they were among the companions of Umar. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 41}

Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death in these pages; a second source is needed.
