Husain Abdullah (born 27 July 1985) is an American football safety from Los Angeles. Undrafted out of Washington State, he signed with the Minnesota Vikings in 2008, became a starter, and finished his career with the Kansas City Chiefs, retiring on 28 March 2016 after five concussions.{cite:wikipedia-husain-abdullah-en}{cite:wikidata-husain-abdullah}

A practising Muslim, Abdullah fasted Ramadan even during the season and sat out the whole of 2012 to perform the Hajj with his brother Hamza.{cite:wikipedia-husain-abdullah-en} In September 2014 his prostration after an interception return for a touchdown drew a penalty that the NFL admitted the next day should not have been assessed.{cite:sports-illustrated-abdullah-2014} No source consulted names a school or tradition for him.
