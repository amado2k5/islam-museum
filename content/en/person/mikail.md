## Overview
Mika'il is the angel named in the Qur'an alongside Jibril; the commentators cited by Ibn Kathir say he descends with mercy, rain and vegetation {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir details the reports about Jibril and Mika'il under the verse naming them {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
