## Summary
A Successor from the Abd al-Qays tribe. He narrated from Ali, Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, Abu Dharr, Abu Hurayra, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Umar, Imran ibn Husayn and Samura ibn Jundab, among others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5143}

## Who narrated from him and the critics
Sulayman al-Taymi, Abd al-Aziz ibn Suhayb and Yahya ibn Abi Kathir narrated from him. Ibn Ma'in and Ibn Sa'd called him trustworthy, and Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5143}
