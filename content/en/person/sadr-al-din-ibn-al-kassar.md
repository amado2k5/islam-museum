## Overview
Sadr al-Din Ibn al-Kassar, the hafiz of al-Jazira; he heard from Ibn al-Bukhari and a company and wrote much hadith in a fine hand — wearing rags and no visitor of note; in his last days the Dirja school was opened for him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 771-772}.
