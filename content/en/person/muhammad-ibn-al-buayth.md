## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Buayth ibn Halabas, lord of Marand in Azerbaijan. He fortified it and built palaces there, then rebelled in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil. Bugha al-Saghir, client of the Commander of the Faithful, fought him until he overcame him and took him to Samarra, and demolished the wall of Marand and its palace. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.164}

Al-Baladhuri says that his grandfather Halabas settled in Marand, then al-Buayth fortified it, then his son Muhammad. Al-Tabari relates the account of his flight in 234 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.164}
