## Summary
Lamine Yamal is a Spanish professional footballer born in 2007 in Esplugues de Llobregat, Catalonia, a winger for FC Barcelona and Spain, widely regarded as the leading young talent in world football; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential figures in sport. {cite:muslim500-lamine-yamal} {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Rise
A La Masia graduate of Moroccan and Equatorial Guinean descent, he debuted for Barcelona at 15 — the club's youngest-ever league player — and for Spain at 16, breaking youth records at every step. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Euro 2024
At Euro 2024 he became the tournament's youngest player and youngest scorer — a stunning semi-final goal against France — as Spain won the title; he finished as its best young player. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Career
He has since become Barcelona's decisive attacker, winning La Liga titles and the Ballon d'Or young-player awards ⁦(Kopa Trophy)⁩ while drawing comparisons to the game's greats at the same age. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Assessment
Analysts debate only how high his ceiling is; his visibility as a footballer of Muslim heritage at the summit of the sport is widely noted. The museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal} {cite:muslim500-lamine-yamal}


## Records
He became the youngest scorer in La Liga, the youngest scorer at a European Championship ⁦(at Euro 2024, which Spain won)⁩ and the youngest Ballon d'Or podium finisher — a record-setter at an age when most players are still youth prospects. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Barcelona role
Installed as a first-team winger at sixteen, he became central to Barcelona's attack and its 2024-25 La Liga title, inheriting the number ten shirt and comparison with the club's all-time greats in Spanish press. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## Identity
His celebrations — waving the Morocco flag, the '304' sign for his Rocafonda neighbourhood — publicly mark his Moroccan-Equatoguinean heritage and Catalan upbringing, discussed in profiles of the player's background. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal} {cite:muslim500-lamine-yamal}

## Origins
Born in Esplugues de Llobregat, Catalonia, in 2007 to a Moroccan father and Equatoguinean mother, he rose through Barcelona's La Masia academy to become the youngest first-team player of his generation. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}

## The record run
His 2023-24 and 2024-25 seasons produced a succession of youngest-ever records — in La Liga, at Euro 2024 and in the Ballon d'Or voting — that press coverage treats as unprecedented for a player of his age. {cite:wikipedia-lamine-yamal}
