## Overview
Sufyan ibn al-Abrad al-Kalbi, a commander whom Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan sent from Syria with large forces to fight the Kharijite Shabib, when al-Hajjaj had taken refuge in the governor's house in Kufa {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.130}.

## Against Shabib
According to al-Mas'udi, Sufyan defeated Shabib, and Ghazala and his mother were killed. He then pursued him with the people of Syria, and caught up with him at al-Ahwaz {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.130}.
