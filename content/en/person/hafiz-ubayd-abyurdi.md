## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Muhammad, called Hafiz Ubayd al-Abyurdi, takes his nisba from Abyurd. Ibn al-Imad counts him among those who died in 895 AH and describes him as the imam and 'allama {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 537}.

## What Ibn al-Imad records
His notice stands in Shadharat al-Dhahab among the events of the year 895 AH, between the learned imam Sayyid Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Husayni al-Shirazi al-Ayji and the chief judge Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Kazaruni al-Hanbali. Ibn al-Imad gives no more than his name, his nisba and the description imam and 'allama; he names no teachers, no works and no place of death {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 537}.

## The editor's note
The editor of the printed edition points in a footnote to his entry in al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami, volume 5, page 116, and notes that the name there reads Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 9 p. 537}.
