## Summary
Manuel II Palaiologos, Byzantine emperor through the reigns of Mehmed I and Murad II — the book has him appealing fruitlessly to Europe when Bayezid I demanded Constantinople's surrender {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 68}, and standing behind the intrigues of Murad's reign: funding Prince Mustafa's rising to the siege of Gallipoli, then harbouring another brother of Murad who seized Nicomedia before Murad marched on him and destroyed his force {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 79}.
