## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn al-Khannat al-Qurtubi, a poet of Córdoba. Al-Maqqari in *Nafh al-Tib* says that his father sold wheat in Córdoba, and that the Banu Dhakwan took responsibility for educating his sons. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 519}

He was connected with the Banu Hammud dynasty, and was one of the poets devoted to Ali ibn Hammud. He died in 437 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 519}
