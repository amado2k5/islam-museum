## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abdallah ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, brother of [[person/muhammad-al-nafs-al-zakiyya|Muhammad al-Nafs al-Zakiyya]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.285-288}

## In al-Baladhuri
Al-Baladhuri says that Isa ibn Musa fought him, and that Abu al-Asad, a freedman of [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]], encamped between him and his army at al-Bataih. He also says that he and Muhammad were the cause of their father Abdallah being imprisoned in the Hashimite city of Medina. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that he was killed at Bakhmara, sixteen farsakhs from Kufa, with four hundred Zaydis with him, and it is said five hundred. His head was brought, and al-Mansur sent it to his father Abdallah in prison. Abdallah placed it before them while he was praying; he took it and placed it in his lap and said: "Welcome, O Abu al-Qasim." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.285-288}
