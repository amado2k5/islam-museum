Ymer Prizreni (c. 1820 – 12 June 1887), also known as Ymer Drini, was an Ottoman-Albanian 'alim and hoxha born at Zgatar in Opojë into a family of müderrises and landowners — his grandfather Ali Nuhi had founded the first mosque of Zgatar in 1814, and his father Sylejman served as imam of the Bajrakli Mosque in Prizren.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-en} Educated in Prizren and then in Istanbul, where he studied Islamic theology and jurisdiction, he taught in the madrasa of Prizren and won a reputation as a defender of the lower classes against the corrupt Ottoman administration.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-en}{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-sq}

In 1877 he was elected to the first Ottoman Parliament and helped found the Central Committee for Defending Albanian Rights in Istanbul; in the spring of 1878 he chaired the committee that organized the Prizren assembly which founded the League of Prizren.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-en} He was elected chairman of the League in October 1879 and, in January 1881, head of its Autonomous Provisional Government, directing the resistance until Ottoman armies suppressed the League that spring.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-en} Refusing submission, he settled at Ulcinj, where he died on 12 June 1887 and where his tomb still stands; the English biography records that he was allegedly murdered and his archive stolen.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-en}{cite:wikidata-ymer-prizreni} The Albanian biography's lead also counts him a member of the Naqshbandi tariqa — a reading the museum preserves unclassified, as it rests on that single attestation.{cite:wikipedia-ymer-prizreni-sq}
