## Summary
Faymiyun was the ascetic of answered prayers and wonders who entered [[place/najran|Najran]] close to the time of the first Abyssinian occupation of Yemen, and called its people to the Christian faith. The people of Najran saw such signs of his truthfulness, and of the truth of his religion, that they answered the call to Christianity and embraced it for his sake {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 40} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 41} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/31-34}.

## Context
Christianity came to the lands of the Arabs by way of the Abyssinian and Roman occupations. The first Abyssinian occupation of Yemen lasted from 340 CE to 378 CE. In that time Christian missionary work entered the regions of Yemen, and close to it Faymiyun entered Najran {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 40} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 6/297}. See [[concept/christianity-in-arabia|Christianity in the Arabian Peninsula]] and [[group/christians-of-najran|the Christians of Najran]].
