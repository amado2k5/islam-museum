## Overview
Faymiyun: the ascetic of answered prayers who entered Najran and called it to Christianity before the second Abyssinian occupation.

## Life
Near the time of the first Abyssinian occupation, an ascetic man of answered prayers and marvels — named Faymiyun — entered Najran and called its people to the Christian religion; Najran's people saw such signs of his truthfulness and his religion's truth that they answered to Christianity and embraced it {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## For young visitors
Faymiyun was a good man who came to Najran long ago and called its people to Christianity; they professed it at his hand.
