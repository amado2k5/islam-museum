## Summary
Mehmed ibn Suleyman Fuzuli of Baghdad was a poet who wrote in Turkish, Persian and Arabic. Biblissima records his birth in 1494 and death in 1556 CE, the latter with a question mark {cite:biblissima Q11663}. The record points to his entry in the Turkish Islamic Encyclopaedia, volume 13, pp. 240-246.
## In the sources
The museum's card links his burial to Karbala and his birth to the Aq Qoyunlu realm at medium confidence, pending a second source {cite:biblissima Q11663}.
