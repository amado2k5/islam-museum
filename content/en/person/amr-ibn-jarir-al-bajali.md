## Summary
Amr ibn Jarir al-Bajali was first drawn in and then became beloved. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 483 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 135. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135}

## The cause of his repentance
Amr ibn Jarir said to Raja' ibn Haywa: do you know what caused my repentance? I went out with youths in Kufa, and when I was about to commit the sin, a voice called out to me with a verse whose sense is that every soul is held in pledge for what it has earned; the verse is referred to, not quoted. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/135}
