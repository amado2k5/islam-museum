## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ahmad b. Muhammad b. Ibrahim al-Nisaburi al-Tha'labi, the exegete, of the companions of al-Siraj; a hafiz and preacher, a head in tafsir and Arabic, solid in religion. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 230}

## His works
Ibn Khallikan said he was unique in his age in the science of tafsir; he composed the great commentary that surpassed other tafsirs, and his Kitab al-'Ara'is on the stories of the prophets, among others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 230}
