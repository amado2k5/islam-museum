# Subhi Alshaik

## Summary
Subhi Alshaik is an Australian Muslim media producer based in Sydney. He is the founder and lead producer of One 4 Kids, the studio behind the children's character Zaky, and the producer and director credited on the One Islam Productions channel's own videos. {cite:edunesia-one4kids-nasyid} {cite:youtube-one-islam-productions}

## Career and work
In 2016 the Australian company One4Kids launched a cartoon channel for Muslim children, built around Zaky, a bear who gives Islamic education in a form children can digest; a journal study of the company's songs names Subhi Alshaik as "the producer and director of One4Kids" and says he created the animated film as an alternative to Peppa Pig. {cite:edunesia-one4kids-nasyid}

In a 2020 podcast interview he is introduced as the creator of Zaky; he speaks of his past in the music industry, his religious awakening, and how he started One4Kids. {cite:kalamcast-subhi-alshaik}

His company's second major platform is the One Islam Productions channel, which publishes lectures, reminders and series such as the lives of the prophets in several languages. Its own "How To Pray In Islam" video closes its description with the credit "Produced & Directed by Subhi Alshaik" and the address of his company's site. {cite:youtube-one-islam-productions} The channel had about 2.66 million subscribers in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-one-islam-productions}

The channel's streaming app, One Islam TV, is developed and published by One 4 Kids, and its store description presents the app as the channel's own platform, telling subscribers that their fee directly supports One Islam Productions. {cite:appbrain-one-islam-tv}
