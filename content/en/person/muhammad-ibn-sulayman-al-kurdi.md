## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kurdi was the jurist of the Shafi'is in the Hijaz lands of his time, born in Damascus and raised in Medina. The entry gives his birth as 1127 AH, equal to 1715 CE, and his death as 1194 AH, equal to 1780 CE, and its heading calls him al-Shaykh Muhammad al-Kurdi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.

## Career
He held the office of mufti of the Shafi'is in Medina until he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Fatawa, printed; Jaliyat al-Hamm wa al-Tawani an al-Sa'i li-Qada' Hawa'ij al-Insan, in manuscript, forty hadiths; Fath al-Qadir bi-Ikhtisar Muta'alliqat Nusk al-Ajir, printed, a short treatise; al-Hawashi al-Madaniyya ala Sharh Ibn Hajar li-l-Muqaddima al-Hadramiyya, printed, two volumes; Sharh Fara'id al-Tuhfa; Uqud al-Durar fi Mustalahat Tuhfat Ibn Hajar; a gloss on Sharh al-Ghaya by al-Khatib; al-Fawa'id al-Madaniyya fiman Yuftu bi-Qawlihi min A'immat al-Shafi'iyya; Fath al-Fattah on the conditions of the hajj; Kashif al-Litham an Hukm al-Tajarrud qabl al-Miqat bila Ihram; al-Thaghr al-Basim an Ma'ani al-Suwar allati Yuzawwij fiha al-Hukkam; and Zahr al-Ruba fi Bayan Ahkam al-Riba {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.

## Death
He died in 1194 AH, equal to 1780 CE, still serving as mufti of the Shafi'is in Medina {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.
