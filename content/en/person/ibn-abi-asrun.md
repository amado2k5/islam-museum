## Summary
Abu Sa'd 'Abdallah b. Abi'l-Sari Muhammad b. Hibat Allah b. Muthir b. 'Ali b. Abi 'Asrun al-Tamimi, Hadathi then of Mosul — Sharaf al-Din Ibn Abi 'Asrun; the Shafi'i jurist among the notables of jurists and the virtuous of his age, he studied under Qadi al-Murtada al-Shahrazuri, As'ad al-Mayhani and Abu'l-Fath Ibn Burhan, and taught at Mosul, Aleppo and the western zawiya of Damascus's Umayyad Mosque. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Offices
He held the judgeship at Sinjar, Nisibis and Harran, then Damascus in 573; appointed over Syria he rose in Nur al-Din's esteem and built him madrasas at Aleppo, Hama, Homs and Baalbek; he authored *Safwat al-madhhab* from *Nihayat al-matlab*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
