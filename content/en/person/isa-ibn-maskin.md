## Overview
Isa ibn Miskin al-Ifriqi, Abu Muhammad, is described by al-Dhahabi as the shaykh of the Maliki school in the Maghrib and a companion of Sahnun {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2994}.

## Teachers and students
Al-Dhahabi names Tamim ibn Muhammad, Hamdun ibn Mujahid, Luqman the jurist and Abd Allah ibn Masrur ibn al-Hajjam among those who took knowledge from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2994}.

## Character and work
Al-Dhahabi says he was trustworthy, scrupulous and devout, and that his prayers were answered; he took up the office of judge unwillingly, drew water himself with a jar and avoided ceremony, and he left writings {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2994}.

## His death
He died in 295 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2994}.
