## Overview
Dhu al-Janahayn, a king who gathered the Persians at Nihawand, mentioned by al-Mas'udi in the account of the embassy of [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.618}

## The embassy
Al-Mas'udi reports that he had them cross their river to receive al-Mughira as an envoy. He seated him on his throne with his crown in his splendour, with the sons of kings in two rows. Al-Mughira kept piercing their carpets with his spear to anger them, then leapt up and sat with the king on his throne. They pulled him down, and he said: "We do not treat your envoys in this way." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.618}

## The battle
According to al-Mas'udi, the Muslims crept up to them five or six at a time. Dhu al-Janahayn fell from a grey mule during the battle and his belly was split open. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.619}
