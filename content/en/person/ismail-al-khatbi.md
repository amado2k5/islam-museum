## Summary
Isma'il ibn Isma'il ibn Bunan, Abu 'Umar al-Khatibi (269–350), muhaddith and historian of Baghdad; reliable, accomplished, noble and discerning, versed in the days of men and the reports of the caliphs, he compiled a great history ordered by years; he died in Jumada II 350. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.8}
