## Summary
Shaykh al-Shuyukh Abu'l-Fath 'Umar ibn 'Ali ibn Hamuwayh al-Juwayni, the sufi, transmitted from his grandfather and al-Farawi; Nur al-Din invested him with the shaykhate of shaykhs of Syria, and he was full of gravity. His obituary is under 577, at the age of sixty-four. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 739}
