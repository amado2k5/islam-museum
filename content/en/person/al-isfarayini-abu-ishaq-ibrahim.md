## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Muhammad al-Isfarayini, a theologian and Shafi'i jurist from Isfara'in, between Nishapur and Jurjan. He took theology and the principles of jurisprudence in Baghdad. He was born around 337 AH (949 CE) and died in 418 AH (1027 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.612}

Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source.
