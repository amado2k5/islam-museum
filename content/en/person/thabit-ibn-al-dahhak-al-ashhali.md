## Summary
Thabit ibn al-Dahhak ibn Khalifa ibn Tha'laba ibn Adi ibn Ka'b ibn Abd al-Ashhal, al-Ansari al-Ashhali, a Companion who witnessed the Pledge of Ridwan under the tree at al-Hudaybiyya, as established in Sahih Muslim on the transmission of Abu Qilaba from him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.178}.

## His entry in the rijal books
Ibn Hajar gives his full nasab in al-Isaba back to Abd al-Ashhal ibn Jusham and records that he witnessed the Pledge of Ridwan, citing what is established in Sahih Muslim through Abu Qilaba, whom Thabit himself told of it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.178}. In Taqrib al-Tahdhib Ibn Hajar writes: "Thabit ibn al-Dahhak ibn Khalifa al-Ashhali: a Companion" {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p.108}.

## For younger visitors
Thabit ibn al-Dahhak was an Ansari Companion of Banu Abd al-Ashhal who was among those who pledged allegiance to the Prophet under the tree at al-Hudaybiyya.

## Notes for researchers
- Al-Isaba entry no. 902 (al-Maktaba al-Asriyya scan, pp. 178–179); Taqrib al-Tahdhib entry t. 819 (Majmu'at al-Kutub scan, p. 108).
- Ibn Hajar distinguishes him from Thabit ibn al-Dahhak ibn Umayya ibn Tha'laba (no. 900) and Thabit ibn al-Dahhak ibn Tha'laba (no. 901); al-Isaba itself notes the error of dropping "Khalifa" from his nasab.
