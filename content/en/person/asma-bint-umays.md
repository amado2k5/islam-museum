## Overview
[[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma daughter of Umays]] of the Khath'am, Umm Abd Allah, one of the early women emigrants. She emigrated to Abyssinia with her husband [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]], then after him she was married to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr al-Siddiq]], and then to [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.204-205}

## Emigration to Abyssinia and Medina
It is said that she embraced Islam before the Prophet entered the House of al-Arqam, and her husband Ja'far emigrated with her to Abyssinia, where she bore him Abd Allah, Muhammad and Awn. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.204} When she emigrated to Medina in the seventh year, Ja'far had already been martyred on the day of Mu'ta. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.205}

## What the Prophet said to her
She said to Umar ibn al-Khattab, when he said to her: "O Abyssinian woman, we preceded you in the emigration", that the emigrants to Abyssinia had been with the Prophet, feeding his hungry and teaching his ignorant. She told the Prophet this, and he said: "The people have one emigration, and you have two." {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.205}

## The birth of Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr
She gave birth to Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr at Dhu al-Hulayfa when they were setting out for the Farewell Pilgrimage. Abu Bakr told her to wash and then to enter the state of pilgrimage, after asking the Prophet, who ordered her to do so. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.206}

## The death of Abu Bakr
Abu Bakr directed that Asma wash him, and she washed him, being his wife. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.206}

## For young visitors
Asma daughter of Umays was among the early Muslim women. She emigrated with her husband to Abyssinia and then to Medina, and Abu Bakr married her afterwards, and then Ali. When Abu Bakr died she washed him with her own hands.

## Notes for researchers
- The primary source here is al-Dhahabi's "Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'" (vol. 2), edited by al-Abyari, of which a photographed copy is in the local repository.
- The statement "it is said that she embraced Islam before the House of al-Arqam" is given by al-Dhahabi in the hedged form; it is recorded as a report.
- Ibn Sa'd, al-Isti'ab and al-Isaba are mentioned in al-Dhahabi's footnote to the entry; their texts have not yet been compared.
- The date of the emigration to Medina as "the seventh year" is given on al-Dhahabi's authority; a second source is needed before it is relied on.

She told Umar ibn al-Khattab — when he said 'O Abyssinian, we outdid you in the Hijra' — the sense that the emigrants to Abyssinia had been with the Prophet feeding their hungry and teaching their ignorant, and reported it to the Prophet, who said 'the people have one hijra; you have two'. She bore Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr at Dhu al-Hulayfa while they intended the Farewell Pilgrimage, and Abu Bakr told her to bathe then enter ihram for the Hajj after asking the Prophet, who had so ordered her. ## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] opens the third episode of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] with Asma' bint Umays. He counts her among the earliest Muslims, "before the Prophet entered the house of al-Arqam", and says she married Ja'far ibn Abi Talib and emigrated with him to Abyssinia, where Quraysh sent envoys to have them sent back. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:01:30} She had three children by Ja'far, raised in Abyssinia, and the "people of the ship" came back only when the Prophet called them, reaching him as Khaybar was being taken. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:03:00} He relates that the Prophet did not know which gladdened him more, the conquest of Khaybar or Ja'far's arrival. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:03:30}

He tells the exchange with Umar, who called her "Abyssinian" and claimed the first right to the Prophet because his people had emigrated with him; she answered that they had had the Prophet with them while her group were strangers far away for God's sake, and took the matter to the Prophet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:05:00} The Prophet's reply that Umar's people had one emigration and hers two delighted her, and the people of the ship came to her to hear it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:06:00} He draws from it that people's good points should be sought out and praised. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:06:30}

When Ja'far fell at Mu'ta, he says, the Prophet came to the house, held Ja'far's sons and wept, then told her he had been killed a martyr; he forbade her to tear her clothes or wail, but not to weep. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:10:00} After her waiting period Abu Bakr proposed, and she married him; their son Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr was born before the Prophet's death, she made the Farewell Pilgrimage, and she stayed with Abu Bakr until he died. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:12:30} Then she married Ali ibn Abi Talib, after Fatima's death, and bore him two sons, Yahya and Awn. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:13:30} He sees in her three marriages a sign of her standing, and counts six children in all, the best known being Abd Allah ibn Ja'far. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep03 00:14:00}
