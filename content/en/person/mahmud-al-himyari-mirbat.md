## Overview
Mahmud ibn Muhammad al-Himyari, lord of Mirbat, Zafar and the Hadhramaut coast. Ibn al-Athir says that he began with a ship he hired out at sea to merchants, then became vizier to the lord of Mirbat, and that he had generosity, courage and good conduct. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.297}

## His rule
When the lord of Mirbat died, he took the city, and the people obeyed him for love of him. In 619 AH he laid waste Mirbat and Zafar, and built a new city on the coast near Mirbat, at a great sweet spring that he channelled to it. He put a wall and a ditch around it, and called it al-Ahmadiyya. He loved poetry and gave generously to poets. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.297}
