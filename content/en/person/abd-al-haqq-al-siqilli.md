## Overview
Abd al-Haqq ibn Muhammad ibn Harun, Abu Muhammad al-Sahmi al-Qurashi al-Siqilli, was a jurist, one of the notables of the Malikis. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 466 AH, 1073 CE, with no year of birth, and says he died in [[place/alexandria|Alexandria]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he was educated in [[place/siqilliya|Sicily]] and made the pilgrimage twice {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}. In Mecca in 450 AH he met Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni, Abd al-Malik ibn Yusuf ([[person/imam-al-haramayn-al-juwayni|al-Juwayni]]), and questions of Maliki law passed between them; these were collected under the title "Masa'il al-Imam Abd al-Haqq al-Siqilli wa-ajwibatuha li-l-Imam al-Juwayni" and survive in manuscript at Dar al-Kutub al-Misriyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}. He visited Egypt repeatedly and died in Alexandria {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists "al-Nukat wa-l-furuq li-masa'il al-Mudawwana", of which the first part is in the Madrid library and which he is said to have regretted writing {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}. He also lists "Tahdhib al-matalib", a large commentary on the Mudawwana, and a part on the precise wording of the Mudawwana ("Dabt alfaz al-Mudawwana") {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}. He also wrote verse {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 281}.
