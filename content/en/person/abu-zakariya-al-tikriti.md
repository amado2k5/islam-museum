## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Qasim ibn Mufarrij ibn Dar', Abu Zakariya al-Tha'labi, with al-Zirikli adding a query for al-Taghlibi, al-Tikriti. Al-Zirikli describes him as a learned man and a littérateur, one of the Shafi'i jurists {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 162}. He gives his birth as 531 AH, 1136 CE, and his death as 616 AH, 1219 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 162}.

## His life
He was born in Tikrit and served as its qadi, then moved to Baghdad in 607 AH and was appointed to teach at the Nizamiyya, and he died in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 162-163}.

## Writings and what contemporaries said
Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn al-Najjar that he wrote on the school's law, on disputation (khilaf) and on belles-lettres {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 163}. He quotes Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi saying that he holds an ijaza from him, and says that Sibt Ibn al-Jawzi cited two verses of his poetry {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 163}.
