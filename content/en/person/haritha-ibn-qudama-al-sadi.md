## Overview
Haritha ibn Qudama al-Sa'di of Basra. Al-Mas'udi in "Meadows of Gold" reports that he was among those who came to [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] from Basra with ten thousand men, sent by Ibn Abbas, Ali's governor in Basra, with al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, when Ali marched to al-Nahrawan in 38 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.695}
