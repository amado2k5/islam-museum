## Overview
Udhra, who bore the title Ismat al-Din Khatun, was the daughter of Shahanshah ibn Ayyub, an Ayyubid princess, and, as al-Zirikli says, the daughter of the brother of Sultan Salah al-Din {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 222}.

## Monuments and death
Al-Zirikli names among her monuments the Udhrawiyya madrasa in Damascus and says it is attributed to her {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 222}. She died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 222}. He prints her year of death as 593 AH (1196 CE) and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 222}.
