## Summary
Qadi al-jama'a Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Khalaf al-Tujibi al-Qurtubi al-Maliki; he transmitted from Abu 'Ali al-Ghassani and others, among the great and senior scholars, deep in sciences and letters — none in his time sought knowledge more, with piety and humility. He was killed unjustly in Cordoba's mosque at the Friday prayer in 529, aged seventy-one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 573-574}
