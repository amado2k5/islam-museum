## Summary
Ismail ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Suddi, Abu Muhammad, of Kufa, a truthful Successor who sometimes erred. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}

## Works
He was accused of Shi'ism, knew the events of the people, wrote a commentary on the Qur'an, and died in 127 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}

## In al-Tabari's commentary: his chain
Al-Tabari transmits much of al-Suddi's commentary through one chain that recurs in his work: reports from Ibn Abbas through Abu Malik and Abu Salih, from Murra on Ibn Mas'ud, and from some Companions. The editor takes this to be three chains joined together, and notes that Ahmad ibn Hanbal said al-Suddi gave this commentary a chain, and that al-Tabari himself doubted the chain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 158} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 159}
