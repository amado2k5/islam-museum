## Overview
Salm, head of the [[building/bayt-al-hikma|House of Wisdom]] in the time of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. Ibn al-Nadim mentions him among those whom al-Ma'mun sent to the king of the Byzantines to seek whichever of the ancient sciences stored in Byzantine lands they might choose {cite:duha-islam}.

Al-Hajjaj ibn Matar and Ibn al-Bitriq were with him, and others. They carried what they found, and al-Ma'mun ordered them to transport it, and they transported it {cite:duha-islam}.

Ahmad Amin took the account from the Fihrist (p.243) and from Duha al-Islam (vol. 3, p.80).
