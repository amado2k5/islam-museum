## Overview
Al-Sari ibn al-Hakam, governor of Egypt at the time of al-Shafi'i's death. According to al-Mas'udi, he prayed over al-Shafi'i at his funeral on the night of Friday in Rajab 204 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.417}
