## Overview
Isa ibn Maryam, a prophet among the prophets of the Children of Israel. This page is limited to what al-Bukhari's *Sahih* reports about him. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## In the Sahih of al-Bukhari
- Al-Bukhari relates that he was one of the three who spoke in the cradle. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3436}
- He also relates that Satan did not touch him, nor his mother Maryam at her birth, unlike all the other children of Adam. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3431}
- In it the Prophet says that he is the nearest of people to Isa son of Maryam in this world and the next, and that the prophets are brothers from different mothers with one religion. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3443}
- On the night of the Night Journey, the Prophet described him as of medium height, well-proportioned, with a reddish complexion. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3207}
