## Overview
Salm ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini, a commander sent by Nasr ibn Sayyar to Yahya ibn Zayd at al-Juzjan. al-Mas'udi reports that he killed Yahya in the battle at a village called Ar'una, with an arrow in his temple. He carried Yahya's head to al-Walid ibn Yazid, and crucified his body at al-Juzjan {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.200}.

Then [[person/abu-muslim-al-khurasani|Abu Muslim]] came out and killed Salm. He took down the body of Yahya and prayed over it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.200}.
