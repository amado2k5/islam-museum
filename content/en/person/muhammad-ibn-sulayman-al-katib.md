## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Katib, from the lands of Mudar in Raqqa. [[person/ahmad-ibn-tulun|Ahmad ibn Tulun]] raised him in Egypt and then turned against him, so he went to Baghdad, where he became secretary of the army and one of the greatest commanders of [[person/al-muqtafi-billah|al-Muktafi]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1100}

When the state of the Banu Tulun failed and the Qaramita ravaged Syria, al-Muktafi sent him with the armies. He defeated the Qaramita and captured the lord of al-Shama, then took over Syria and entered Egypt. He defeated [[person/harun-ibn-khumarawayh|Harun ibn Khumarawayh]], shackled the Banu Tulun and sent them to Baghdad. The estates that Ahmad ibn Tulun had built were burned, and Fustat was plundered in 292 AH. In this way he was the one who brought about the change of the Tulunid state ([[event/fall-of-tulunids-292|the fall of the Tulunids]]). {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1100}
