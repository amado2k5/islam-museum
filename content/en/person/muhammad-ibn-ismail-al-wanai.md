## Overview
Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Ismail ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17713} He took up learning, studied under Shaykh Shams al-Din al-Barmawi and his generation, became known for his merit, and kept company with a number of notables. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17713} He lodged in the colleges first as a student and then as a teacher, held the teaching of the Shaykhuniyya, and twice held the judgeship of al-Sham (Syria). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17713} He then went back after asking to be relieved of the judgeship and was relieved, in the year 847, and sought the teaching of the Salihiyya beside the tomb of al-Shafi'i, held it for a year and more, and then declined for about two months until he died. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17713} The heading gives two dates, 849 and 788 AH, without saying which is the death date, so none is stated. The book gives no source for this entry.
