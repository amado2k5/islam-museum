## Summary
Wife of Sufyan ibn Ma'mar al-Jumahi, mother of Janada and Jabir and of their brother by their mother Shurahbil ibn Hasana; she emigrated to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her "Hasana", mother of Janada and Jabir, sons of Sufyan, and of their brother by their mother Shurahbil ibn Hasana. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} He counts her in the women's list among "the rarities of the Arabs", naming her "Umm Shurahbil ibn Hasana". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq does not state her tribe; the tribal affiliation is left empty.
