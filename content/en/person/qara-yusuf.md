## Summary
Qara Yusuf: ruler, linked in the museum's record to Azerbaijan and Iraq. The Biblissima record places his life between 1357 and 1420 (the record marks the dates as uncertain). {cite:biblissima Q24664}
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Kara Youssouf» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q24664}
