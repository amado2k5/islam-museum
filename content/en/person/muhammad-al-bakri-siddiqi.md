## Summary

Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Bakri al-Siddiqi al-Shafi'i, the great master and leading imam of his age beyond dispute. He followed the example of his father and after him kept to his lofty path and his excellent school, and in his days people benefited from the righteous predecessors. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 431-432}.

## Shaykh of the path

Whoever sat in his company for a single day gained knowledge and blessings. His knowledge of spiritual states, unveilings, manifestations and realities was such that Ibn Hajar al-Haytami stated that he was a complete shaykh, a verifier of its attributes and properties, and Ibn Hajar heard from him benefits that surpass a whole house of benefits. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 432}.

## Death

He died in Rabi' al-Akhir 993 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 432}.
