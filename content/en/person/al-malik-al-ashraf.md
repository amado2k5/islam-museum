## Overview
An Ayyubid, lord of Damascus. He ordered the building of the Ashrafiyya House of Hadith in 628 AH, and it was finished in 630 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 704}

Distinguishing him from al-Ashraf Musa ibn al-Adil needs an additional source.
