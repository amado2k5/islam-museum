# Shek Shamim Ahmed

## Summary
Shek Shamim Ahmed is a British Muslim video producer, the sole creator of the Islamic Guidance YouTube channel, which he started on 23 June 2008 and has produced single-handedly ever since. {cite:gofundme-islamic-guidance} The channel carries animated series on the past, present and future of Islam and passed 1.4 million subscribers. {cite:youtube-islamic-guidance}

## The channel
Islamic Guidance is known for its long production values: short history series such as the Four Imams, the Ten Promised Paradise and an ongoing Seera series, animated and subtitled for a global audience. {cite:youtube-islamic-guidance} In the fundraiser the channel itself links for one-off donations, Ahmed writes in the first person as its creator, noting that although he uses the word "we", the channel "has actually been a one man job". {cite:gofundme-islamic-guidance}
