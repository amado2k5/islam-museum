## Overview
Murawwi ibn Abdullah al-Sa'idi is named by the narrator with [[person/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-qadi-of-hamdan|Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak]] in their saying that they pledged to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] knowing there was none on the face of the earth more upright in God's right than he. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The book gives neither his lineage nor his country beyond the family name "al-Sa'idi", and its index lists his name only at p. 140. See [[event/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-and-murawwi-on-al-hadis-prayers-against-abu-mihjan-and-jaftam-286]].
