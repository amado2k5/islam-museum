## Overview
Abu Tashfin Abd al-Rahman ibn Musa ibn Abi Hammu, sultan of Tlemcen, the fifth of the kings of the Banu Abd al-Wad according to the museum's data. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Coming to power
Ibn Khaldun relates that his father gave him companions from the Mu'alluj to serve in his upbringing, and that his father would rebuke him and compare him with his cousin Mas'ud ibn Abi Amir. His companions urged him to seize the affair. They attacked the close men of the palace at the time of the midday rest, killed the sultan, Mas'ud and Abu Sarhan, and the Banu al-Mallah viziers perished. A herald called out in Tlemcen that Abu Tashfin had avenged his father. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Afterwards
Abu Tashfin held the affair alone after his father, [[person/abu-hammu-i-musa-zayyanid|Abu Hammu Musa]]. He then marched against Muhammad ibn Yusuf, who had rebelled against his father when he returned from the siege of Bejaia. His wars with the Almohads in the long strife over Bejaia and Tlemcen continued. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
