## Overview
Leader of the Zanj. He emerged on the marshes of Basra in Shawwal of the year 255 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.410}. He entered al-Ubulla in Rajab of the year 256 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.471}, and took control of Abbadan {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.472}.

## His lineage according to al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi reports that he claimed to be Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Isa ibn Zayd ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn. He says that most people held that he was an impostor, and that the family of Abu Talib denied him. He was from Warzanin, in the districts of Rayy {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.568}.

## His views and actions
Al-Mas'udi considers that his acts of killing women, children and the aged reflect the views of the Azariqa among the Kharijites. He reports that he would begin his sermons with the takbir and the tahlil, then "There is no judgement but God's", and that he regarded all sins as associating partners with God. The first to write an account of his deeds was Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Sahl {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.568}.

## His period and death
According to al-Mas'udi, his rule lasted fourteen years and four months. He reports that some three hundred thousand people were killed in a single battle at Basra. He was killed on the night of Saturday, two nights into Safar of the year 270 AH {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.577-578}.

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]] does not call him the Zanj leader but 'the rebel at Basra who claimed descent from the family of Abu Talib', and gives his name as Ali ibn Muhammad, so presenting the lineage as his claim without endorsing it. He records his advance on al-Ubulla and its burning, his entry into Basra in 257 AH and the burning of its congregational mosque, his expulsion from it by Muhammad al-Muwallad in 258 AH, and Abu Ahmad ibn al-Mutawakkil's expedition against him that same year. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 620} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 622} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 623} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 624}
