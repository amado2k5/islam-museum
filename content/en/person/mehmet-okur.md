Mehmet Okur (born 26 May 1979 in Yalova) is a Turkish former basketball player and coach. The first Turkish player to win an NBA championship, with the Detroit Pistons in 2004, he played seven seasons for the Utah Jazz, where his three-point shooting as a centre made him a fan favourite, and in 2016 he became the first Turkish citizen to coach in the NBA as a Phoenix Suns player development coach.{cite:wikipedia-mehmet-okur-en}{cite:wikidata-mehmet-okur}
