## Overview
Ibn al-Barizi, qadi of Hama; he studied fiqh at Damascus under al-Fakhr b. 'Asakir and served as his mu'id, taught at the Rawahiyya, held the teaching post of Ma'arrat al-Nu'man, then moved to Hama — teaching, giving fatwa, and holding its judgeship with praised conduct, a man of learning and religion; he died in Sha'ban aged eighty-nine {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 572}.
