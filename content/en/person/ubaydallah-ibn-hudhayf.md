## Overview
Ubaydallah ibn Hudhayf is a companion of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]; [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|al-Kufi]] relates that he sought straw for the animals from other than tithe straw, found none, told a groom to take a known measure to repay in kind, and al-Hadi spoke to him roughly and had the straw set aside; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.60} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.61} and that he came with another to al-Hadi to say that a tenth from thirty-three and a third farq gathers nothing. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.61} The name stands as printed, a reading confirmed by comparing the text recognition with a re-reading of the page image. See [[event/al-hadi-refuses-to-use-goods-from-the-tithe]] and [[event/al-hadi-insists-on-the-lawful-measure-of-the-tithe]].
