## Summary
Wife of Hatib ibn al-Harith al-Jumahi; she emigrated to Abyssinia and returned with her two sons in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her wife of Hatib ibn al-Harith, saying she came with her sons Muhammad and al-Harith in one of the two ships after her husband died a Muslim in Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} In the listing of women he mentions "the daughter of al-Mujallal" among Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy without a name. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- A guess: "the daughter of al-Mujallal" in the women's list (p. 11, under Banu Amir) is Fatima bint al-Mujallal herself (p. 7); the text does not state it — a low-confidence museum inference.
