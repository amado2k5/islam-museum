## Overview
Al-Matiji, the Maliki jurist-muhaddith of Matija near Bijaya, a righteous man "who devoted himself to hadith"; he transmitted from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muwaffaq and those after him and wrote much; he died in Jumada II 659 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}.
