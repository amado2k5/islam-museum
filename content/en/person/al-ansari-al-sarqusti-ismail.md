## Overview
Abu Tahir Isma'il ibn Khalaf ibn Sa'id al-Ansari al-Sarqusti, a Qur'an reciter, grammarian and man of letters from Zaragoza in al-Andalus, who moved to Egypt and settled there. He died around 455 AH (1062 CE). The date rests on one source and needs an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
