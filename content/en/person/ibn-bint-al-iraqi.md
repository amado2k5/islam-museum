## Overview
Abd al-Karim ibn Ali ibn Umar al-Ansari, 'Ilm al-Din, known as Ibn Bint al-'Iraqi, was a mufassir and jurist who lost his sight late in life. His origin was Wadi Ash in al-Andalus, and he was born and died in Egypt in 704 AH (1304 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.53}

He wrote an epitome of usul al-fiqh and an epitome of Qur'an commentary which al-Safadi said contained benefits, and al-Insaf min al-Intisaf bayna al-Zamakhshari wa-Ibn al-Munir, which al-Zirikli calls worth publishing. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.53}
