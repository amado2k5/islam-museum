## Overview
Adnan: the ancestor at whom the Prophet's genealogy stops, of the children of Qaydar ibn Isma'il — the twenty-first in al-Mubarakpuri's chain of descent.

## Life
Of the children of Qaydar ibn Isma'il, his sons kept begetting in Mecca until Adnan came of them and his son Ma'add; from him the Adnani Arabs preserved their genealogies {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab}.

It is reported that the Prophet, whenever his line was traced and reached Adnan, stopped and said: "the genealogists lie", never going past him {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}. A body of scholars held the lineage may be raised above him, deeming that hadith weak, saying there are forty fathers between Adnan and Ibrahim on careful reckoning {cite:rahma-lil-alamin}.

## For young visitors
Adnan is an old ancestor of the Prophet; people stop tracing the line at him because the names before him are not preserved with certainty.
