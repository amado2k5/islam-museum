## Overview
An Arab commander defeated by the Berbers at Mulwiya. Of his troops, Balj ibn Bishr al-Qushayri escaped, and entered al-Andalus with them in the year 124 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273} See [[person/balj-ibn-bishr-al-qushayri|Balj ibn Bishr]].

Al-Salabi says that Hisham appointed him over Ifriqiya, and that he was killed in the raid; then Hisham sent Hanzala ibn Safwan to Ifriqiya. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.485}
