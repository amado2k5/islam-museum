## Overview
Abu Zayd Abd al-Rahman ibn Muqana al-Ishbuni al-Qaysi, whom al-Maqqari counts among the poets of the frontier (al-thaghra). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.250}

## His rhyme-N poem
He appeared before the chamberlain of Idris ibn Yahya al-Hammudi in Málaga and recited to him his *nuniyya* ode. When he came to the description of the caliph's face, the caliph himself raised the curtain and said to him: "Look as you wish." Then he relaxed with him and treated him well. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.250}
