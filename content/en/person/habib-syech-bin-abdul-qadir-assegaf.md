## Summary
Syech bin Abdul Qodir Assegaf (born 20 September 1961), known as Habib Syech, is an Indonesian sayyid and sholawat leader from Surakarta (Solo) in Central Java. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} He leads the Majlis Ahbaabul Musthofa gathering and performs sholawat — devotional songs in praise of the Prophet — with its musical group, and academic writing describes him as a gifted and enormously popular salawat performer rooted in the Ba'Alawi Sufi tradition of the Hadhrami diaspora. {cite:woodward-hadhrami-dakwah-2012}

## Early life and education
He was born on 20 September 1961 at Semanggi, in the Pasar Kliwon district of Surakarta, one of the sixteen children of Habib Abdul Qodir bin Abdurrahman Assegaf, the imam of the Jami' Assegaf mosque. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} {cite:woodward-hadhrami-dakwah-2012} His first teacher was his own father; as a child he never lived in a pesantren, and his education ran through the study circles of the mosques, above all the Assegaf mosque at Pasar Kliwon, where he attended the halaqah after Maghrib and served the mosque. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} He was further guided by his uncle Habib Ahmad bin Abdurrahman Assegaf, who had come from Hadhramaut, and by Habib Anis bin Alwi al-Habsyi, the imam of the Riyadh mosque in Solo. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id}

## The majlis and his music
The Majlis Ahbaabul Musthofa, the study gathering he leads, was founded about 1998 at Mertodranan in Solo, growing out of gatherings for the Rotibul Haddad, the Burdah and the Simthud Duror mawlid. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} Its regular sholawat session is held on Wednesday evenings at the Bustanul Asyiqin building in Solo and is broadcast live. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} His songs re-arrange a songbook of some five hundred poems, sung in Arabic as well as in Indonesian and Javanese, accompanied live by pure rebana percussion and in recordings by a blend of rebana and modern instruments. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} His recordings run from the nine volumes of Qasidah Pilihan (2005–2013) through The Best Sholawat (2014) to Qosidah Burdah (2022), and his "Ya Hanana" is among the recordings that anchor his YouTube channel. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id} {cite:youtube-habib-syech-bin-abdul-qadir-assegaf}

## Tradition and positions
The academic study of his dakwah describes him as rooted, with the Hadhrami sayyid community of Indonesia, in the Ba'Alawi Sufism of the Hadhramaut and its Tariqa 'Alawiyya, and presents his method as encouraging people to love God, the Prophet, his family and their fellow humans through salawat. {cite:woodward-hadhrami-dakwah-2012} The same study records that he stresses love and compassion and rejects all forms of religious and political violence. {cite:woodward-hadhrami-dakwah-2012} Since 2022 he has also sat as an A'wan (advisory member) of the central board of Nahdlatul Ulama for the 2022–2027 term. {cite:wikipedia-syech-bin-abdul-qodir-assegaf-id}

## Reach
His YouTube channel, @habibsyechbinabdulqadirassegaf, started on 2 July 2020, counted 1,430,000 subscribers, 241,223,997 views and 1,457 videos by vidIQ as of 11 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-habib-syech-bin-abdul-qadir-assegaf} {cite:youtube-habib-syech-bin-abdul-qadir-assegaf} Audience figures are platform figures, recorded with their dates; they change constantly. {cite:vidiq-habib-syech-bin-abdul-qadir-assegaf}
