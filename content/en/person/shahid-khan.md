## Summary

Shahid Rafiq Khan (born 1950) is a Pakistani-American businessman who owns the Jacksonville Jaguars of the NFL, Fulham F.C. of the Premier League and the Flex-N-Gate auto-parts company, and appeared on the cover of Forbes in 2012 as the face of the American Dream {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-ar}.

## Life

Khan was born on 18 July 1950 in Lahore to a middle-class Punjabi Muslim family in the construction industry; his father ran a survey-equipment shop and his mother was a professor of mathematics {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-ar}. He moved to the United States in 1967 at sixteen to study at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, worked his first job washing dishes, graduated in industrial engineering in 1971, and became a naturalised citizen in 1991 {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en}.

## Work

Khan started Bumper Works in 1978 and bought Flex-N-Gate, his former employer, in 1980, growing it into a supplier to the Big Three and to Toyota across the United States {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-ar}. He bought the Jacksonville Jaguars for 770 million dollars, approved unanimously by NFL owners in December 2011, becoming the first ethnic-minority owner of an NFL team, and bought Fulham from Mohamed Al-Fayed in July 2013 {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en}. With his son Tony he is a lead investor in All Elite Wrestling {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en}. Khan has described himself in his own words as a Muslim, which names no school, so his faith is recorded as unknown with his statements preserved in the data note {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-en} {cite:wikipedia-shahid-khan-ar} {cite:wikidata-shahid-khan}.
