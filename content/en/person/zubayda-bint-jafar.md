## Overview
Umm Ja'far Zubayda bint Ja'far ibn al-Mansur. al-Baladhuri reports that the first to dig the river of al-Maymun was a steward of hers named Sa'id ibn Zayd {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Her works, as al-Mas'udi reports them
al-Mas'udi describes her as the best of people in deeds in the days of [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]], because of the rest-houses (dur al-sabil) she established in [[place/mecca|Mecca]], the cisterns, ponds and wells, and the endowments she made for them. She dug the spring of al-Mashash in the Hijaz and smoothed the course of its water to Mecca, and what she spent on it reached one million seven hundred thousand dinars {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.671-672}.

He reports that she was the first to use vessels of gold and silver set with jewels {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.670-671}.

## With al-Rashid
al-Mas'udi reports that she came in to al-Rashid to reproach him for appointing her son Muhammad over Iraq without the army and the commanders, and that she bore a grudge against Ja'far ibn Yahya. She performed the pilgrimage with Salih ibn al-Rashid and the people in the year 208 AH {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.340-341} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.747}.
