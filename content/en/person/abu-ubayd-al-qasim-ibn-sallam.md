## Overview
Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam, a scholar whose name appears in al-Baladhuri's "Futuh al-Buldan". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.332}

## In Futuh al-Buldan
In the section on the truce with Nubia, al-Baladhuri says: "Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam related to us from Abd Allah ibn Salih from Ibn Lahi'a from Yazid ibn Abi Habib." He also relates from him, from al-Layth ibn Sa'd, on the peace and the purchase of their slaves. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.332}

## His death and book
Al-Shirazi says he died in Mecca in 224 AH at the age of sixty-seven. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49} His birth year (157 AH) is calculated from that and is not stated in the source. The museum's data attribute to him [[work/al-amthal-al-saira|Al-Amthal al-Sa'ira]], on the authority of {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}.
