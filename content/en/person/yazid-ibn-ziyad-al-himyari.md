## Overview
Yazid ibn Ziyad ibn Rabi'a ibn Mufarri' al-Himyari. Shurayk ibn al-A'war granted him land in Kirman by Ibn Ziyad's order, and he sold it after Ibn Ziyad fled from Basra {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
