## Overview
Izz al-Din Abu al-Qasim Abdallah ibn al-Husayn ibn Abdallah al-Ansari al-Hawwi al-Shafi'i, known as Ibn Rawaha. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.234}

## Biography
He was born in Sicily in 560 AH while his parents were captives. His father had him hear hadith in Alexandria from al-Safi al-Kabir and a group of others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.234}

He died on the eighth of Jumada al-Akhira 646 AH, at the age of eighty-five. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.234}
