## Summary
Raffi Ahmad is an Indonesian actor, presenter and media entrepreneur born in Bandung in 1987, one of Indonesia's most ubiquitous television personalities; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-raffi-ahmad} {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## Career
He rose to fame as a teenage actor in the early 2000s, then became a fixture of Indonesian variety and talk-show television — hosting programmes including Dahsyat and Ini Talkshow — while building a massive audience across television and social media. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## RANS Entertainment
With his wife, actress Nagita Slavina, he founded RANS Entertainment in 2015, growing it from a YouTube vlog channel — among the most subscribed in Southeast Asia — into a conglomerate spanning production, music, sport, food and lifestyle ventures. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## Public profile
The couple's family life is itself prime content in Indonesian popular culture; Ahmad has also been named a special presidential envoy for youth development and the creative economy. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## Assessment
He is credited with pioneering the Indonesian celebrity-to-content-empire model; scrutiny of his commercial and political entanglements is reported by the museum attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad} {cite:muslim500-raffi-ahmad}


## Television career
His presenting credits span music shows, dangdut competitions and prime-time variety formats across SCTV, ANTV and Trans TV — more than two decades of continuous presence that made him a fixture of Indonesian television. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## RANS Entertainment
The company he runs with Nagita Slavina — RANS Entertainment — grew from YouTube into a media-and-business group spanning sports, food and retail, cited in press coverage as Indonesia's model creator-to-conglomerate case. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## Scale
His combined social following — Instagram, YouTube and TikTok — is among the largest of any Indonesian celebrity, giving his commercial ventures and public stances reach well beyond broadcast. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad} {cite:muslim500-raffi-ahmad}

## Origins
Born Raffi Faridz Ahmad in Bandung in 1987, he began as a teenage actor and singer before moving into presenting — a pivot that made him a fixture of Indonesian variety television for more than two decades. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## The presenter years
His hosting of music shows, dangdut competitions and prime-time variety formats across SCTV, ANTV and Trans TV made him one of the most-watched faces on Indonesian television through the 2000s and 2010s. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}

## RANS as conglomerate
Under his and Nagita Slavina's leadership RANS expanded from YouTube into sports, food and retail — a trajectory press coverage treats as the Indonesian model of turning a creator following into a diversified media business. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad} {cite:muslim500-raffi-ahmad}

## The presenter phenomenon
Raffi Ahmad's career spans more than two decades of Indonesian variety television: music shows, dangdut competitions and prime-time formats across SCTV, ANTV and Trans TV that made him one of the most-watched faces on Indonesian television. His expansion into RANS Entertainment — the company he runs with Nagita Slavina spanning production, sports, food and retail — made him a model of turning a creator following into a diversified media business, a trajectory press coverage treats as the Indonesian model of creator entrepreneurship. {cite:wikipedia-raffi-ahmad}
