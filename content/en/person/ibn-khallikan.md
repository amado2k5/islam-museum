## Overview
Qadi al-Qudat Shams al-Din Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Khallikan al-Barmaki al-Irbili al-Shafi'i, author of *Wafayat al-A'yan* (Obituaries of Eminent Men). He was born in Irbil in 608 AH and died in 681 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.379}

## Learning
He heard al-Bukhari from Ibn Makram, studied jurisprudence in Mosul under Kamal al-Din ibn Yunus and in Syria under Ibn Shaddad. He met the great scholars and excelled in letters. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.379}

## Judgeship
He lived in Egypt for a time and deputised as judge there, then held the judgeship of Syria for ten years. He was removed in 669 AH, spent seven years dismissed in Egypt, was restored to the judgeship of Syria, and was removed a second time at the beginning of 680 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.379}

## Al-Dhahabi's words
Al-Dhahabi says that he was an accomplished imam who knew the school well, an authority in letters, poetry and the days of the people, and generous. He mentions that Ibn Khallikan compiled a valuable book on the obituaries of eminent men. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.379}
