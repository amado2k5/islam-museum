# Frédéric Kanouté

## Summary

Frédéric Kanouté is a Malian retired footballer, born in Sainte-Foy-lès-Lyon, France, in 1977. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-en} A striker, he starred for Sevilla in Spain and was named the 2007 African Footballer of the Year, the first player born outside Africa to win the award. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-en} The sources document his conversion to Islam and his devout practice but name no school for him, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-fr}{cite:wikidata-frederic-kanoute}

## Club career

Kanouté came through Lyon, played in England for West Ham United and Tottenham Hotspur, and joined Sevilla in 2005, where he won two consecutive UEFA Cups in 2006 and 2007 and scored 137 goals in 290 matches over seven seasons. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-en} He closed his career with one season at Beijing Guoan in China, retiring in 2013. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-en}

## Mali

Kanouté represented Mali, the country of his father's origin, at international level, including at the Africa Cup of Nations, and later founded a charitable foundation working with orphans in Mali. {cite:wikipedia-frederic-kanoute-en}
