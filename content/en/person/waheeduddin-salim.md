## Summary
Maulvi Syed Waheeduddin Salim was an Urdu scholar, writer and linguist from Panipat, a close associate of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and the first professor of Urdu at Osmania University in Hyderabad. He is remembered above all for *Vaz-i Istalahat*, the first Urdu book on the coining of scholarly terminology. He has no English Wikipedia article and no Wikidata item; this record rests on the Urdu Wikipedia article and on the biographical sketch written by his contemporary Maulvi Abdul Haq in *Chand Hum Asr*. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur} {cite:abdul-haq-waheeduddin-salim}

## Life
Salim was born at Panipat into a well-known family; his father, Haji Fariduddin, was the custodian (mutawalli) of the shrine of Shah Sharafuddin Bu Ali Qalandar. He received his early education at Panipat, then moved to Lahore, where he completed his study of Arabic and Persian and passed the matriculation examination. He entered the education department of Bahawalpur state, worked for a time as head maulvi at Rampur, and kept a medical practice (matab) at Panipat for a period. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur}

Through the intercession of Altaf Hussain Hali he met Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, who, impressed by him, took him on as his secretary; the association lasted until Sir Syed's death in 1898. Salim afterwards brought out his own journal, *Ma'arif*, and edited the Scientific Society's journal (the Aligarh Gazette), the *Muslim Gazette* of Lucknow and the *Zamindar* of Lahore. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur}

His essays and translations brought him to Hyderabad, where at the Dar-ul-Tarjuma (the state translation bureau) he wrote his famous book *Vaz-i Istalahat* on terminology. When Osmania University was founded, he was appointed its first assistant professor of Urdu, and was later raised to professor. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur} {cite:abdul-haq-waheeduddin-salim}

## Works
The Urdu article lists among his works *Urdu Deomala* (a study of Urdu idiom), *Arabon ki Shayari* (The Poetry of the Arabs) and *Tulsi Das ki Shayari* (The Poetry of Tulsidas). Abdul Haq's sketch confirms *Vaz-i Istalahat* as the first Urdu book on the art of terminology, and notes that his collected poetry was published after his death as *Afkar-i Salim* (1938). {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur} {cite:abdul-haq-waheeduddin-salim}

## Dates
The two sources disagree, and the museum preserves both readings rather than choosing silently. The Urdu Wikipedia article gives his birth year as 1869 and his death year as 1927. Maulvi Abdul Haq, who knew him, gives his birth as 1867 at Panipat and his death on 29 July 1928 at Malihabad, Lucknow. The record's date fields follow the Urdu article for the birth year and Abdul Haq for the death date, with the conflict stated in both places. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur} {cite:abdul-haq-waheeduddin-salim}

## Faith
No source read for this record names a school or denomination for Salim himself, and the museum records none. His father's custodianship of the Panipat shrine is family background, not an attestation of the son's school. {cite:wikipedia-waheeduddin-salim-ur} {cite:abdul-haq-waheeduddin-salim}
