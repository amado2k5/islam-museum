## Overview
Abu Lulu Firuz, a slave of [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]], who was a Christian. He stabbed [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] in the market of Medina, and Umar died of the wound. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.190} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.192}

## al-Suyuti's account
According to al-Suyuti he made millstones, and he was a smith, a carver and a carpenter. Al-Mughira asked Umar to let him enter Medina and imposed a tax on him; he complained to Umar, who did not change it. So he threatened that he would make a mill driven by the wind. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

He then stabbed him with a double-edged dagger in the darkness before dawn prayer. He wounded thirteen men with him, six of whom died, and then he killed himself. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
