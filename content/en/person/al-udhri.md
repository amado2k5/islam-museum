## Summary
Abu'l-'Abbas al-'Udhri Ahmad b. 'Umar b. Anas b. Dilath al-Andalusi al-Dala'i — Dala'a being in the district of Almeria — a hafiz and exacting muhaddith; he made the hajj with his parents in 408 and stayed eight years in the Haram, where Abu Dharr al-Harawi taught him; he transmitted from Abu'l-Hasan Ibn Jahdam and others. He died in Sha'ban 478, about eighty years old. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 365-366}
