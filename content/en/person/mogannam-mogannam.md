Mogannam Mogannam (died 1945) was a Palestinian Christian lawyer and political activist raised in Jerusalem.{cite:encyclopedia-mogannam-mogannam-ilyas} He emigrated to the United States before the First World War, studied law there, and in the early 1920s married Matiel Mogannam and returned with her to Jerusalem, moving to Ramallah in 1938.{cite:encyclopedia-mogannam-mogannam-ilyas}{cite:wikipedia-matiel-mogannam-en}

In Jerusalem Mogannam practised law and was a founding member and officer of the National Defense Party, the conservative nationalist party associated with the Nashashibi family.{cite:encyclopedia-mogannam-mogannam-ilyas} Among those who trained in his law office was the young Ahmad Shuqairi, later the first chairman of the Palestine Liberation Organization.{cite:encyclopedia-mogannam-mogannam-ilyas} He belonged to Jerusalem's Arab Anglican family.{cite:wikipedia-matiel-mogannam-en} The encyclopedia dates his death to 1945; his wife's biography gives 1943.{cite:encyclopedia-mogannam-mogannam-ilyas}{cite:wikipedia-matiel-mogannam-en}
