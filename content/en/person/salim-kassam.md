# Salim Kassam

## Summary
Salim Kassam is a British media figure, co-founder and former chief editor of The Muslim Vibe, the grassroots digital media platform for Muslims in the West. {cite:themuslimvibe-podcast-picks} {cite:spotify-muslim-vibe-podcast} He hosted the platform's podcast for years and later organised charity sport for the Zahra Trust. {cite:athletic-ummah-salim-kassam} This entry records him as the documented lead of the platform's channel.

## The Muslim Vibe
The Muslim Vibe began in 2014 as a grassroots platform aiming, in its own words, to inspire, inform and empower Muslims through Islamic content for a younger Western audience. Its own team pages name Salim Kassam as 'Co-Founder and Chief Editor' — with Haseeb Rizvi as co-founder and creative director — and describe its podcast as hosted by him; his byline block across the site reads 'Chief Editor of The Muslim Vibe'. {cite:themuslimvibe-podcast-picks} In November 2021 the podcast relaunched under Rizvi as host with an episode interviewing Kassam as the platform's 'former Chief Editor and Co-Founder'. {cite:spotify-muslim-vibe-podcast} An independent sports outlet, profiling the Zahra Trust's 2024 Miami Marathon team that Kassam conceived, likewise describes him as the man who 'previously co-founded The Muslim Vibe digital media organization'. {cite:athletic-ummah-salim-kassam}
