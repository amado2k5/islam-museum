## Overview
Michael, Patriarch of Alexandria in the time of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]]. Al-Salabi says that he entered the city in a notable celebration, preceded by candles, crosses and gospels. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.459}

The book does not give his number in the series of patriarchs. It needs an additional source.
