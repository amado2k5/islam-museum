## Overview
Abu Muhallim, an interpreter of dreams at the court of [[person/al-wathiq|al-Wathiq]]. He was asked about al-Wathiq's dream of the "mart", and said that the mart is a barren wasteland where nothing grows. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 533}

As al-Suyuti relates it, the meaning of his interpretation is that whoever perishes before God is one whose heart is empty of faith, as the mart is empty of plants. He then recited to the caliph a hundred rhymes by a hundred well-known poets, each with the word "mart" in a verse, and al-Wathiq ordered him given a hundred thousand dinars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 533}

Note: the English data in the museum's records say "dirhams", while the Arabic text says "dinars". This draft follows the Arabic.
