## Overview
Boon-companion of al-Mu'tamid, and the author of books on the routes and kingdoms, and on play and entertainments {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.588}.

## The lute
al-Mas'udi reports that he entered upon al-Mu'tamid, who asked him who was the first to take up the lute ('ud). He told him the report of Lamak and his son, whom he hung on a tree, until he made a lute of wood in his likeness {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.588}.
