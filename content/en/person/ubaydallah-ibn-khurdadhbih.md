## Overview
Boon-companion of al-Mu'tamid, and the author of books on the routes and kingdoms, and on play and entertainments {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.588}.

## The lute
al-Mas'udi reports that he entered upon al-Mu'tamid, who asked him who was the first to take up the lute ('ud). He told him the report of Lamak and his son, whom he hung on a tree, until he made a lute of wood in his likeness {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.588}.

## In his Book of Routes and Realms
Ibn Khurradadhbih names himself in the heading of his book Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Khurradadhbih, "client of the Commander of the Faithful". {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.12} In his preface he says he wrote the book in answer to an enquirer who asked him to set out the routes and realms of the earth, and that he rendered Ptolemy's boundaries from their foreign tongue "into correct language". {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.11} See [[work/kitab-al-masalik-wal-mamalik|the Book of Routes and Realms]]. Al-Muqaddasi criticizes his book as very brief and of little use. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.14} {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.15}
