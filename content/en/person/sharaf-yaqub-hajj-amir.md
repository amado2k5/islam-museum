## Overview
Amir Sharaf Ya'qub ibn Muhammad al-Mawsili. He stayed in Syria and rose to high standing there {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.438}.

## Commander of the pilgrimage
In the year 620 AH he was amir over the pilgrims of Syria. The Arabs gathered in a great crowd, intending to cut the road to them, and he restrained them with a mixture of desire and fear. Then he placated them with money and garments. He gave all of them from his own wealth, and took nothing from the pilgrims, not a single dirham {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.438}.

Ibn al-Athir says that he did a noble deed, that he had a great deal of learning, and that he returned to a sound religion {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.438}.
