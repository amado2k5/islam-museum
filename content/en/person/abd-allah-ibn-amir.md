## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Amir came to Basra in 30 AH, then went out to Fars and conquered it, and sent Mujashi ibn Mas'ud after Yazdegerd. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.286} The index of al-Tabari mentions his going out to Khurasan and the conquests he made in 31 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.581}

Al-Baladhuri says that he came to Basra in 29 AH, and that all of Fars had been conquered except Istakhr and Jur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The two accounts differ on the year of his coming.
