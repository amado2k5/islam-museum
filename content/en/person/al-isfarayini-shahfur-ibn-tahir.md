## Overview
Shahfur ibn Tahir ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Muzaffar al-Isfarayini, titled Imad al-Din. A scholar from Isfara'in, of the Shafi'i school. He died in 471 AH (1078 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.623}
