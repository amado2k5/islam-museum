## Overview
Muhammad ibn Nuh al-Ujli, a man of hadith. In 218 AH he refused, together with [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]], Sajjada and al-Qawariri, to affirm that the Qur'an was created, and they were shackled. Then Sajjada gave in, and al-Qawariri followed him. Ahmad and Muhammad ibn Nuh were sent to the Romans, and news of the death of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] reached them before they arrived. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.490,493}

His story is linked to [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|the inquisition over the createdness of the Qur'an]], where his role is recorded as "the one tested". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
