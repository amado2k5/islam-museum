## Overview
The brother of the caliph [[person/al-mutamid|al-Mu'tamid]]. The sources say that he died on the night of Thursday, eight nights before the end of Safar 278 AH, after an illness of gout. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.22} {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p.125-126}

## In the caliphate
According to al-Suyuti his brother al-Mu'tamid made him governor of the East, and he was the commander of the army in most of the battles against the Zanj. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 564-569}

Al-Mas'udi says that [[person/al-mutazz|al-Mu'tazz]] robed him and sent him out, then imprisoned him and sent him to Basra in 252 AH; the period between his honour and his imprisonment was six months and three days. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 545} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 551} Al-Mas'udi also says that he fought Ya'qub al-Saffar in 262 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 571-573}

## His illness
Ibn al-Jawzi says that the gout grew so severe that he could no longer ride, and was carried on a litter. Abd Allah ibn al-Mu'tazz wrote to console him for it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p.125-126}

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]], among the events of 258 AH, says Abu Ahmad ibn al-Mutawakkil marched with a large force against the rebel at Basra who claimed descent from the family of Abu Talib, with the troops, provisions and weapons carried in boats; fire broke out in the boats and they burned, and Abu Ahmad turned back. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 624}
