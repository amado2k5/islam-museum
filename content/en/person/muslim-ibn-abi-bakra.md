## Overview
Muslim ibn Abi Bakra, son of [[person/abu-bakra-al-thaqafi|Abu Bakra al-Thaqafi]], owner of the third bathhouse in Basra in Balalabadh, and mentioned in the account of [[event/basra-bathhouse-permissions|the permission for bathhouses in Basra]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The bathhouse
He took about a thousand dirhams a day from the bathhouse, and a great deal of food. His father told him that he did not see him fit for a post of office, and did not see him falling short of his brothers in benefit, so he left the bathhouse to his brother Abd al-Rahman. Abd al-Rahman disclosed his income and gave people permission to build bathhouses. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
