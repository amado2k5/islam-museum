Irrfan Khan (7 January 1967 – 29 April 2020) was an Indian actor, born Sahabzade Irfan Ali Khan at Tonk in Rajasthan into a Pashtun family, who trained at the National School of Drama in New Delhi.{cite:wikipedia-irrfan-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-irrfan-khan} After years in television he broke through with Asif Kapadia's The Warrior (2001) and Haasil (2003), and became one of the few Indian actors to sustain a major international career — Slumdog Millionaire (2008), Life of Pi (2012), The Lunchbox (2013) and Jurassic World (2015).{cite:wikipedia-irrfan-khan-en} He received the Padma Shri in 2011.{cite:wikipedia-irrfan-khan-en}

In July 2016, promoting Madaari, he questioned the ritual slaughter of Qurbani, fasting during Ramzan as display and the processions of Muharram, and argued on television that he needed no mediator between himself and God — remarks that drew denunciations from clerics and are recorded in this record's faith note.{cite:nowrunning-irrfan-khan-2016}{cite:bollywood-hungama-irrfan-khan-2016} Diagnosed with a neuroendocrine tumour in 2018, he died at Mumbai on 29 April 2020.{cite:wikipedia-irrfan-khan-en}
