## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Umar al-Khaffaf, a jurist mentioned in al-Shirazi's "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha", and author of the book "al-Khisal". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60}

## Date of death
Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death at this point. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60} A second source is needed.
