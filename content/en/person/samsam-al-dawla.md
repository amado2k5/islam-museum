## Overview
Samsam al-Dawla, son of Adud al-Dawla. In the year 372 AH al-Ta'i appointed him in his father's place, gave him the title Shams al-Milla, bestowed seven robes of honour on him, and conferred on him the two banners {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.630-631}.

In the year 375 AH he intended to levy the tax (maks) on silk and cotton garments. The people gathered in the Mosque of al-Mansur and resolved to stop the Friday prayer, and the city came close to unrest, so he relieved them of it {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.630-631}.

In the year 376 AH his brother Sharaf al-Dawla marched against him, defeated him, and blinded him {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.630-631}.
