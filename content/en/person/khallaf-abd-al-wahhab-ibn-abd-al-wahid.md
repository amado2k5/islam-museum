## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Abd al-Wahid Khallaf, a jurist of the principles of law, preacher and legal scholar. He was born in Kafr al-Zayyat in the governorate of Gharbiyya in 1305 AH (1888 CE), and died in 1375 AH (1956 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
