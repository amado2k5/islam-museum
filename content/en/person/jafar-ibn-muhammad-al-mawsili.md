## Overview
Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Mawsili, a name that appears in al-Mas'udi among those who collected the sayings of the Prophet and put them into their books, together with Ibn Durayd, al-Zajjaji and Nifawayh. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 594}
