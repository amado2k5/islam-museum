## Overview
Abu al-Faraj al-Mu'afa ibn Zakariyya al-Nahrawani, a judge from Nahrawan. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 328-329}

## In Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was the unique figure of his age in the school of Abu Ja'far al-Tabari and the keeper of his books, versed in many sciences, very intelligent and quick in reply. He had some fifty-odd treatises on jurisprudence, theology, grammar and other things. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 328-329}

## His books
Among his books are "al-Nahrir" and "al-Minqar" on the principles of jurisprudence, "al-Hudud wa al-Uqud", "al-Murshid" on jurisprudence, "al-Mahadir wa al-Sijillat", a commentary on al-Tabari's "al-Khafif", a refutation of Dawud ibn Ali, and "al-Jalis wa al-Anis". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 328-329}
