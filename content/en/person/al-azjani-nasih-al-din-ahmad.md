## Overview
Abu Bakr Nasih al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Arjani, a scholar from Arjan in Persia. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 460 AH and his death as 544 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.440-441}
