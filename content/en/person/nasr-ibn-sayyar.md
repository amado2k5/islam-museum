## Overview
[[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] was a governor of Balkh and Samarqand. He fought wars with the Turks and had a dispute with al-Junayd {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan p.257}.

## Governance of Balkh and Samarqand
Nasr ibn Sayyar was in Balkh during the period al-Tabari describes, before the disagreement between him and al-Junayd arose. In Samarqand the people were under siege, and its tax was in the hands of Umayr ibn Sa'da al-Shaybani {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan p.257} {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan p.244}.

## War with the Turks
According to al-Tabari, Nasr ibn Sayyar came forward with thirty horsemen and fought the Turks until he drove them from their positions, and then the Turks were defeated {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan p.209}.

## The day of the Gate
al-Tabari reports that Nasr ibn Sayyar fought valiantly on the day of the Gate, when his sword and the straps of his stirrups broke. He struck with his leg until he was badly wounded. Al-Junayd then praised his valour, and Nasr composed verses in response {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan p.274}.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] relates, in the words of Abd al-Karim ibn Salit al-Hanafi, how Hisham chose Nasr ibn Sayyar to govern Khurasan after rejecting other candidates for reasons he gives, among them Juday' al-Kirmani. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 339} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 340} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 341} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 342} He says that when Qahtaba took Merv, Nasr asked for safe-conduct, then slipped away by night with his family towards Jurjan, fell ill and died at Sawa. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 363}
