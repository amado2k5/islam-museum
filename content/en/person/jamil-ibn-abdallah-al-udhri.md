## Overview
Jamil ibn Abdallah ibn Ma'mar al-Udhri, known as Jamil Buthayna. Al-Mas'udi quotes a verse of his about Buthayna, to illustrate the doctrine that "souls are gathered armies", in which his soul became attached to hers before they were created. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 360}
