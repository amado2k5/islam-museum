## Summary
Ustaz Haji Abdullah Khairi bin Haji Haroon (born 15 September 1968 at Sidam Kanan, Kedah), known as Ustaz Abdullah Khairi, is a Malaysian independent preacher, writer and business owner. {cite:ilabur-abdullah-khairi} {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi}

## Early career and education
Before preaching full-time he served nine years as an engineer and sixteen years as a teacher. {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi} The publisher's biography records his bachelor's degree from Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) and counselling master's degrees from Universitas Sultan Sharif in Riau and Kolej Universiti Insaniah. {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi} The iLabur profile lists further study, including a forestry diploma and an electrical-engineering degree at UPM, an Arabic-language diploma, an Islamic-education degree, and a counselling PhD at Unishams, carried here as that profile reports them. {cite:ilabur-abdullah-khairi}

## Preaching and projects
He is a ceramah voice on Radio Mutiara FM, Kedah FM and Radio IKIM, and is known as a study-skills motivation speaker. {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi} He was invited as a judge on the Pencetus Ummah programme and also works as a hajj and umrah guide. {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi} On 18 January 2015 he founded Maahad Tahfiz As-Syafie (MATAS). {cite:ilabur-abdullah-khairi} He owns Kafe UAK at Glenmarie, Shah Alam, a café run on an eat-and-donate concept, and Kafe Paksy. {cite:ilabur-abdullah-khairi} His books include Insan Rabbani Pelajar Cemerlang, Tuhan Bayar Cash, Sound Direct and Syurga Pak Hang. {cite:ilabur-abdullah-khairi} {cite:google-books-abdullah-khairi}
