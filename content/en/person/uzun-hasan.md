## Overview
Uzun Hasan — Hasan Beg b. 'Ali Beg b. Qara Yuluk — sovereign of the two Iraqs, Azerbaijan and Diyar Bakr. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 335}

## His death
He died in Jumada II or Rajab 882. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 335}

## In al-Salabi
Al-Salabi records that [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed II]] defeated him — "a lord who kept no covenant and backed the Ottomans' foes of whatever creed" — and that of the many captives who fell into the sultan's hands he excepted the scholars and men of learning, such as the judge Muhammad al-Sharihi, whom he honoured greatly {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 139}.
