## Overview
Abd al-Mu'min ibn Yusuf ibn Fakhir al-Khuwayyi al-Urmawi al-Baghdadi, Safi al-Din, is described by al-Zirikli as the leading master of his age in lute playing and music. His family came from Khuy, a fortress in Urmiya in the lands of Azerbaijan. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 693 AH, 1294 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he came to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] as a boy, or was born there, and was confirmed as a jurist at the Mustansiriyya. He busied himself with belles-lettres, Arabic letters and the improvement of his handwriting, for which he became known {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}. He served the caliph [[person/al-mustasim-billah|al-Musta'sim]] and taught his sons; his gift for the lute showed itself, and his standing rose with the caliph and then with [[person/hulagu|Hulagu]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}. He acquired a great fortune and squandered it on pleasures, and Hulagu put him in charge of the endowments (awqaf) of Iraq {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}. The entry adds that [[person/yaqut-al-mustasimi|Yaqut al-Musta'simi]] and Ibn al-Suhrawardi "wrote on him" (kataba alayhi), which in a sentence about calligraphy reads as studying the script with him, and that he died in prison over a debt of 300 dinars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli credits him with fine verse, knowledge of history and several compositions {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}. Among them is the short "Kitab al-Adwar fi ma'rifat al-naghm wa-l-awtar" (on knowing melodies and strings), in manuscript at the Fatih library in Istanbul (no. 4661) and at Dar al-Kutub (no. 349, fine arts); it was translated into Turkish, Persian and French and printed in them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}. Another is "al-Risala al-Sharqiyya fi al-nisab al-ta'lifiyya" (on compositional ratios), in manuscript at the Topkapi Saray (no. 3130) and other libraries {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 170}.
