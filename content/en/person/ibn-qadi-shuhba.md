## Overview
The judge Taqi al-Din Abu Bakr ibn Ahmad, known as Ibn Qadi Shuhba of Damascus, author of *al-I'lam bi-Tarikh Ahl al-Islam* (Informing on the History of the People of Islam). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.479}

## Death
Haji Khalifa gives his death as 851 AH. The conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.479}

## His work
He wrote [[work/al-ilam-tarikh-ahl-al-islam|al-I'lam bi-Tarikh Ahl al-Islam]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.479}
