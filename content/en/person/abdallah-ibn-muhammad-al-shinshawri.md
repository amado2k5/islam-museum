## Overview
Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Ali al-Ajami al-Shinshawri was a faradi, a scholar of the law of inheritance, and one of the Shafi'i jurists. He was the khatib of the mosque of al-Azhar in Egypt, and his name comes from Shinshur, a village of al-Minufiyya. The printed text gives his birth as 935 AH and his death as 999 AH, that is 1528 and 1591 CE; the card keeps 1528 and 1590 because each Hijri year spans two Gregorian years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names 'Fath al-Qarib al-Mujib', in two parts, printed, on inheritance; 'Qurrat al-Aynayn fi Misahat Zarf al-Qullatayn', in manuscript, on jurisprudence; 'al-Fawa'id al-Shinshuriyya fi Sharh al-Manzuma al-Rahbiyya', printed, on inheritance; 'Bughyat al-Raghib', in manuscript, a commentary on 'Murshidat al-Talib' by Ibn al-Haim, on arithmetic; 'al-Fawa'id al-Mardiyya fi Sharh al-Mulaqqabat al-Wardiyya', in manuscript, on inheritance; a commentary on 'Tuhfat al-Ahbab fi Ma'rifat al-Hisab', in manuscript, the original being by Sibt al-Maridini; and 'Khulasat al-Fikr fi Sharh al-Mukhtasar', for which the Rabat library is cited with the number 1272 Kattani {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.
