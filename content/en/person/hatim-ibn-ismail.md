## Overview
Hatim ibn Isma'il, the hafiz traditionist Abu Isma'il of Kufa then Medina, client of Banu 'Abd al-Madan; he narrated from Hisham ibn 'Urwa, Yazid ibn Abi 'Ubayd, Ja'far ibn Muhammad, Abu Muzarrid, 'Imran al-Qasir and others. He died in 187 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1345-1346}
