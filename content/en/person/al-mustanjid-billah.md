## Overview
Al-Mustanjid billah, Yusuf ibn al-Muqtafi, an Abbasid caliph who ruled from 555 to 566 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.678-679}

## His caliphate
He lifted the taxes in Iraq and was severe on the corrupt. Al-Suyuti relates that he imprisoned a man who used to inform on people, and a man offered ten thousand dinars for him. He said to him: "I will give you ten thousand dinars and show me another like him, so I can imprison him and rid myself of his evil." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.678-679}

Ibn al-Najjar, as al-Suyuti quotes him, says he was described as having understanding, judgement and intelligence, had poetry and prose, and knowledge of making astronomical instruments and the astrolabe. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.678-679}

At the beginning of his caliphate al-Fa'iz, the ruler of Egypt, died, and al-Adid, the last of the Banu Ubayd caliphs, rose. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.678-679}

## His death
He died on the eighth of Rabi al-Akhir 566 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.678-679}
