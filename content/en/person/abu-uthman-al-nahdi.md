## In brief
Abu Uthman al-Nahdi, named Abd al-Rahman b. Mall; he said he was fourteen years old on the day of al-Qadisiyya and had seen the Messenger of God while grazing camels at Kazima. He narrated from Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, and Hudhayfa; Mansur, al-A'mash, Ibn Abi Khalid, Sulayman al-Taymi, al-Walid b. al-Mizrab, Amr b. Abdallah Abu Mu'awiya al-Nakha'i, and others narrated from him, and he lived a hundred and thirty years. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.65-66}

## His death
He died in 95 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.66}
