## Overview
Abu Uthman al-Nahdi, Abd al-Rahman ibn Mull al-Nahdi. Al-Baladhuri mentions him as the bearer of the news of Nihawand to Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.427}

## The news of Nihawand
Al-Baladhuri relates that he told Umar of the killing of al-Nu'man, and Umar said "We belong to God" and wept. Abu Uthman then said that others had been killed whom he did not know, and Umar said: "But God knows them." Umar saw him with his hand on his head, weeping. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.427}
