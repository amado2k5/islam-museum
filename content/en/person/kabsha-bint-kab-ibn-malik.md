## Summary
Kabsha bint Ka'b ibn Malik al-Ansariyya; Ibn Hibban counted her a Companion and al-Mustaghfiri followed him, and her mother was Sukhayla bint Haritha ibn Lawdhan as Ibn Sa'd reported. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1950-1951}

## Marriage and hadith
Ibn Sa'd reports that Thabit ibn Abi Qatada married her and she bore him children. Her hadith on the cat's leftover water, transmitted through Abu Qatada, is in the Muwatta and the four Sunans. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1950-1951}
