## Overview
Dawood Ahmad is a dawah media producer from Baramulla in Jammu and Kashmir, whose name is carried by Salafi Dawood Production, the volunteer team that films and publishes sermons of Kashmir's Ahl-e-Hadith scholars through the Salafi Dawood channel on YouTube. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood} {cite:megabizdir-salafi-dawood}

The team publishes its volunteer member list in its video descriptions; Dawood Ahmad appears in it, and the production bears his name. On this basis the museum documents him as the figure this work is built around. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

The channel had about 187,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

## Career and work
In its video descriptions the team states its motive as spreading "Right Knowledge of Islam In The Light Of Quran and Sunnah", with its volunteers filming, editing and publishing sermons without charge. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

The channel centres on two Kashmiri Ahl-e-Hadith preachers: Irshad Ahmad Tantray al-Madani, whose sermons the channel has documented for years in places including Srinagar, and Mushtaq Ahmad Veeri, including his sermon series explaining Al-Qawl as-Sadid. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

The team also runs a second, educational YouTube channel, and has used its main channel to appeal for donations toward a second camera and filming equipment to support its volunteer work. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

A public business-directory entry places Salafi Dawood Production in Baramulla (postal code 193121) and links it to the same YouTube channel, documenting the team's base outside its own materials. {cite:megabizdir-salafi-dawood}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of different positions reported by the sources, not as a verdict of the museum on them.

The orientation declared by the team's materials is that of Kashmir's Ahl-e-Hadith (Salafi) movement: the published sermon titles, member lists and statement of motive all come from the team itself and document the orientation of its media work, not an independent attestation of Dawood Ahmad's personal school. The museum found no two independent sources naming a school for him, so no school is recorded. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}

In the sources available, the museum found no independent biography of him and no documented criticism of his work; what is documented is the production work itself and his channel's reach. {cite:youtube-salafi-dawood}
