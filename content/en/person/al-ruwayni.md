## Overview
Al-Ruwayani, author of the book "al-Bahr", described as the shaykh of the Shafi'is. The Batiniyya killed him in Baghdad in 494 AH, in a disturbance so severe in Iraq that the amirs wore armour under their clothes, according to al-Suyuti's "History of the Caliphs". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.658}
