## Overview
Sudayy ibn Ajlan ibn Wahb al-Bahili, Abu Umama, a Companion from the Qays Aylan, who belongs to the Bahila. The sources agree on this lineage and differ on his name {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.160}.

## Birth and death
The encyclopedia gives his birth as 21 AH and his death as 86 AH (641 CE and 705 CE). It records both dates as commonly transmitted report. The entry needs an additional source {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.160}.
