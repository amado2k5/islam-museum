## Overview
Abu Labid al-Jahdami, a man of the Azd. Al-Mas'udi relates that he was asked: "Do you love Ali?" He answered, in substance, that he does not love a man who killed two thousand five hundred of his people on one day. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 664}

The report adds that the killing reached such a point that no one found anyone to console him, and each household was occupied with its own dead. This is the narrator's account as al-Mas'udi gives it, and the museum describes it without judging it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 664}
