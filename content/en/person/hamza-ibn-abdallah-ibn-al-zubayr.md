## Overview
Hamza ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, whom his father Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr appointed governor of Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The account of Ahwaz
Al-Baladhuri reports that he went out to Ahwaz, and when he saw its mountain he said: "It is like Qu'aiqi'an." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
