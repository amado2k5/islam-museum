## Overview
Chief qadi 'Imad al-Din Abu Salih Nasr b. 'Abd al-Razzaq b. Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, the Hanbali; he taught, gave fatwa and debated, mastering the madhhab, and held the judgeship in 523, being deposed months later. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 161-162}

## His judgeship and scruples
Al-Zahir b. al-Nasir appointed him chief qadi over his whole realm on condition he provide for kin, so he sent him ten thousand dinars to pay the debts of imprisoned debtors, and returned to him oversight of all the waqfs; scrupulous in judgment, strong of soul in truth, without favouritism — he prayed in congregation, went out to the mosque in haste and wore cotton. When al-Mustansir deposed him he recited, "I praised God when He decreed my deliverance from the judgeship." He composed Irshad al-mubtadi'in in fiqh and selected forty hadiths for himself; he died and was buried at Imam Ahmad's mausoleum. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 161-162}
