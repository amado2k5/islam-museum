## Overview
Hujr ibn al-Harith ibn Amr al-Kindi, Abu Imru al-Qays, the eldest son of [[person/al-harith-ibn-amr-al-kindi|al-Harith ibn Amr al-Kindi]] and father of the poet Imru al-Qays. He was of [[group/kinda|Kinda]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.382-383}

## His rule
Ibn al-Athir reports that his father made him ruler over the Banu Asad ibn Khuzayma, Ghatafan and Kinana, and set for him a reward and a tribute from them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.382-383}

## His killing
When he sent his tax collector, the Banu Asad drove away his envoys. He marched against them, took their leaders, seized their property and imprisoned a group of their nobles, among them Ubayd ibn al-Abras. Then their soothsayer Awf ibn Rabi'a prophesied, and they fell upon him in his tent, and Alba' ibn al-Harith al-Kahili killed him, as Hujr had killed his father. Qays and Kinana plundered his spoils. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.382-383}

His brothers, in our data, are [[person/shurahbil-ibn-al-harith|Shurahbil ibn al-Harith]] and [[person/salama-ibn-al-harith-al-taghlibi|Salama ibn al-Harith al-Taghlibi]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}
