## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Yusuf b. Muhammad b. the amir Ya'qub al-Mansur the Almohad, surnamed al-Mustansir; he held the caliphate after his father al-Nasir — a youth doting on his ease — and on his death the state chanced to swear to his uncle 'Abd al-Wahid b. Yusuf b. 'Abd al-Mu'min for his age. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
