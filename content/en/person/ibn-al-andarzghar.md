## Overview
Ibn al-Andarzghar, lord of Barusma. Al-Baladhuri relates that he made peace with Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud on terms of four dirhams per head, on condition that the army withdraw. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.350}
