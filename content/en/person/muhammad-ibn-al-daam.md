## Overview
After the peace between the Khawlanis and the Hamdanis, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went on to Sa'da, and [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] sent his son Muhammad with him with twenty-six horsemen, while al-Da'am went back to his own land. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} Al-Hadi reached Sa'da with the notables of Hamdan, Muhammad ibn al-Da'am and his horse. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} The text here gives neither his age, nor his mother, nor his fate, and does not tie him to any other Muhammad; see [[event/al-hadi-reconciles-khawlan-and-banu-salman-at-madhab-and-the-cloud-286]].
