## Overview
Abu al-Jarrah the judge was the keeper of the prison of Ibn al-Zubayr. He escaped from his prison, and Sallam ibn Ziyad bought for him the house of Khalid ibn Tulayq al-Khuza'i in Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
