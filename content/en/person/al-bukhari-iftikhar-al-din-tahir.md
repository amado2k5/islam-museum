## Overview
Tahir ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rashid al-Bukhari, titled Iftikhar al-Din, a Hanafi jurist from Bukhara. He is probably born in the village of Maymurgh near it. He died in 582 AH (1186 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
