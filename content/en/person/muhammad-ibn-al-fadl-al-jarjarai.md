## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Fadl al-Jarjara'i. Al-Mutawakkil took him as vizier and then dismissed him, and in 233 AH he made Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya his secretary until he was killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.476}
