## Summary
Muhammad ibn al-Fadl al-Jarjara'i was a vizier appointed by al-Mu'tasim, then dismissed in favour of Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya in 233 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 476}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi records in Muruj al-Dhahab, in the section on al-Mu'tasim's viziers, that the caliph appointed Muhammad ibn al-Fadl al-Jarjara'i to the vizierate and then dismissed him, appointing Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya in 233 AH, who held it until he was killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 476} Al-Mas'udi notes that Abu'l-Wazir's days in the vizierate had been short before those appointments, and that he has dealt with Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya, his relations with al-Mutawakkil and the affairs of al-Fath ibn Khagan in his Middle Book. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 476} In his account al-Jarjara'i is thus one of those who held the vizierate under al-Mu'tasim before Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya took it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 476}
