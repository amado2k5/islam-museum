## Overview
The narrator says "and Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Hamdani said" and gives a poem that excuses his people and describes their counsel as a call to the truth, praising [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] and an imam of the Messenger's line who calls to justice. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.171} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} That is all the book gives of him, with no lineage or office. The museum does not assert that he is [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|Ahmad ibn Muhammad, governor of Najran]] of earlier pages, since the name is common and the text does not join them. See [[event/al-hadis-poem-after-the-fight-at-najran-and-ahmad-al-hamdanis-reply-286]].
