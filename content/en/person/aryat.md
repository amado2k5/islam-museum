## Overview
Aryat: commander of the Abyssinian army that occupied Yemen in 525 CE and governed it on the Negus's behalf until Abraha assassinated him.

## Life
Seventy thousand Abyssinian soldiers landed and occupied Yemen a second time under Aryat's command in 525 CE, on a fleet the Roman emperors had equipped them in vengeance for the People of the Trench {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Aryat remained governor on the Abyssinian king's behalf until Abraha — one of his army's commanders — assassinated him and ruled in his place after winning the Abyssinian king's favour {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
