## Summary
Commander of the Spanish-subject tribes Bani 'Amir, Fulitta and Bani Rashid {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.

## The Tlemcen expedition
He led their levies in the army Oran's governor sent to expel [[person/al-hasan-zayyani-tlemcen|al-Hasan]] and restore [[person/abu-zayyan-ahmad|Abu Zayyan]] to the Tlemcen throne; [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]] stopped them {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.
