## Overview
Ibrahim al-Dajwi, the Egyptian grammarian; he took from al-Shihab Ibn al-Marhal, Ibn Hisham and others, excelled in Arabic and kept the people busy with it — his chief work a commentary on the Alfiyya — and had a ready wit {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 21}.
