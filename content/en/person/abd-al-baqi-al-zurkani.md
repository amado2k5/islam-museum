## Overview
Abd al-Baqi ibn Yusuf ibn Ahmad al-Zurqani was a Maliki jurist who was born and died in Egypt; the entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 1020 to 1099 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 75}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Sidi Khalil in jurisprudence, printed in four volumes, and a commentary on the Azziyya, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 75}. For him he refers to Khulasat al-Athar {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 75}.
