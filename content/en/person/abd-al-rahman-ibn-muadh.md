## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muadh ibn Uthman ibn Amr ibn Kab, of the Quraysh (Taym); al-Bukhari and others say he was a Companion. A report from him describes the Prophet's address at Mina, and Ibn Sa'd counts him among those who witnessed the conquest of Mecca. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7520}
