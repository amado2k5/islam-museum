## Overview
Mahmud ibn Sulayman al-Hanafi al-Rumi al-Kafawi was a judge and a scholar of the biographies of the Hanafis, a native of the Turkish town of Kafa. The entry dates his death to about 990 AH, equal to about 1582 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Career
He studied in his town and mastered both Arabic and Turkish letters; he moved to Istanbul, then held the judgeship of Kafa for a time and returned to the capital dismissed, and he died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Kata'ib A'lam al-Akhyar min Fuqaha' Madhhab al-Nu'man al-Mukhtar, in manuscript, of 573 leaves, on the men of the Hanafi school; and Sharh Adab al-Bahth, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## Death
He died in the capital, Istanbul, about 990 AH, equal to about 1582 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.
