## Overview
Ashras ibn Awf, a man of Shayban. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The day of Tustar
A man of the Persians who had been given safe conduct, a Dujayl, waded with him over a ridge of stones, then carried him up to the city of Tustar and showed him Hurmuzan. He then returned him to the army, and that was how the Muslims entered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
