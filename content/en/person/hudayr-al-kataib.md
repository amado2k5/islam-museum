## Overview
Hudayr al-Kata'ib, father of Usayd ibn Hudayr, and leader of the [[group/al-aws|Aws]] on the day of [[event/yawm-buath|Bu'ath]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.229-230}

## The day of Bu'ath
Al-Samhudi reports that the poet Khufaf ibn Nudba elegised him with a verse that calls him "Hudayr al-Kata'ib". He says that Hudayr held the Aws firm after the Khazraj had first gained the upper hand, so they returned and were victorious; this is given in the form "it is said" in the source. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.229-230}
