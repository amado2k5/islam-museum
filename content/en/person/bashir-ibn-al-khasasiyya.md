## Bashir ibn al-Khasasiyya

Bashir ibn Ma'bad, or as some say ibn Yazid ibn Ma'd ibn Dabab ibn Sab', known as Ibn al-Khasasiyya, a Companion. His name was Zahm, and the Prophet named him Bashir. He related from the Prophet, and Bashir ibn Nuhayk and Jarr ibn Kulayb and others related from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5495}
