## Summary
The notable Abu'l-Futuh Nasr ibn Abi'l-Hasan 'Ali ibn Khalaf al-Ansari, known as Ibn Sura; book-broker of Egypt with a great share in the trade — he held a salon in the vestibule of his house on Sundays and Wednesdays where the chief notables and virtuosi gathered and the books for sale were shown to them until the market closed; when al-Salafi died he travelled to Alexandria to sell his books. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
