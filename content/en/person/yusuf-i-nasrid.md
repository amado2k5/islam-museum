## Overview
Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf ibn Isma'il ibn Nasr, sultan of Granada. According to Ibn Khaldun he was the successor of [[person/abu-al-hasan-nasrid|Abu al-Hasan al-Nasri]] in rule. He was the father of [[person/muhammad-v-nasrid|Muhammad V]] {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## His death
He was killed by stabbing at the prayer-ground on the day of the Fitr festival in the year 755 AH, by a black man who had been planted for the purpose. Ibn Khaldun attributed the plot, by an account, to some of the slave-women of the palace. The people of al-Andalus then set up his son Muhammad V, but his client Ridwan blocked him from him and took control of him {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

He was the ruler of the [[polity/emirate-of-granada|Emirate of Granada]] {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
