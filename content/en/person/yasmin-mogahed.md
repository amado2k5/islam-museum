## Overview
Yasmin Mogahed is an American Muslim educator and motivational speaker, a specialist in spirituality, psychology and personal development, born on 11 March 1980. She is the first female instructor at the AlMaghrib Institute. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}

## Education and career
She completed a BSc degree in psychology at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where she also earned an MS degree in journalism and mass communications. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}

She previously worked as a writing instructor at Cardinal Stritch University, and became the first Muslim woman to teach at the AlMaghrib Institute; she is internationally known for her motivational lectures. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}

She has written columns for the Huffington Post, and was an Islam section staff columnist for InFocus News. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}

## Works
- *Reclaim Your Heart*, her best-known book, of personal insights on breaking free from life's shackles. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}
- *Love & Happiness: A collection of personal reflections and quotes*. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en} {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-ar}
- *Healing the Emptiness: A Guide to emotional and spiritual well-being*. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en}

## Impact and audience
Her lectures and books address a wide English-speaking Muslim audience on spirituality, emotional well-being and personal development, and the encyclopedia describes her as internationally known for her motivational lectures. {cite:wikipedia-yasmin-mogahed-en}
