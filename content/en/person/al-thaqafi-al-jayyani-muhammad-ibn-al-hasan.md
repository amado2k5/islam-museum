## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Thaqafi al-Asimi al-Jayyani, a preacher, reciter, hadith scholar and man of letters from Jayyan in al-Andalus. He moved among the southern cities of al-Andalus. He was born in 570 AH (1174 CE) and died in 663 AH (1264 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.831}
