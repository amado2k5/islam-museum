## Overview
Suhayl Zakkar is the editor of [[work/sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq|Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq]]. In his preface he says he had obtained a doctorate in Islamic history and, returning from London to Damascus in autumn 1969, spent some days in the libraries of [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]], where he came upon the manuscript in the Ali Amiri library at the Fatih. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.5}

## His work on the book
He had the manuscript photographed and, once a second set of photographs proved good, read it and decided to transcribe and then edit it. He looked for other copies, learned of one in the library of the Great Mosque of Sana'a and obtained a photograph of it through Dr Hassanayn Rabi' of Cairo University; he compared the two and took the Ali Amiri copy as his base. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.6} He kept the book's headings, though he thinks them later insertions, and appended a table of printing errors. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.9} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.10} His preface is dated Beirut, 15 Sha'ban 1392 AH, and Dar al-Fikr of Beirut undertook to publish and distribute the book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.10}

## His view of the book
He holds that it is among the most important historical texts for a large part of Arabia, that it is modelled on the prophetic sira, and that its author and narrator is Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and not al-Kufi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.9} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.7} These are the editor's views, set out in the work's record.
