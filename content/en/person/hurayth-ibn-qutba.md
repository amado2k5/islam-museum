## Overview
Hurayth ibn Qutba al-Khuza'i was with his brother Thabit in the army of Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim at Tirmidh. The two brothers recruited Tarkhun, and they became the ones who gave orders and forbade in his army. Hurayth was killed by an arrow (nushaba) that struck him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
