## Summary
Abu Bakr al-Faryabi, qadi of Dinawar; he roamed the lands east and west seeking knowledge and following the masters — he heard in Khurasan and Transoxiana, settled at Baghdad, and transmitted from Hadiya, Ibn al-Madini, Bundar, Abu Kurayb, Qutayba and multitudes; Abu'l-Husayn ibn al-Munadi, Ahmad ibn Salman al-Najad and Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i narrated from him — reliable and authoritative; he related that when he reached Baghdad he was met with banners and streamers, the people summoned to the Minar street at Bab al-Kufa to hear him, and a great crowd gathered. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 210}
