## Overview
Hammad ibn Usama, Abu Usama of Kufa, a client of the Banu Hashim, was a memoriser described by al-Dhahabi as "the leading memoriser and proof". He transmitted from Hisham ibn 'Urwa, Yazid ibn 'Abdallah, Bahz ibn Hakim, al-A'mash and al-Jariri and their peers, and died in Dhu al-Qa'da 201 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.299}

## Memory
Ahmad ibn Hanbal said he was the most knowledgeable of men in the affairs and reports of Kufa; from Hisham alone he kept six hundred hadiths, and it is reported of him that he said "I wrote a hundred thousand hadiths with these two fingers". Al-Dhahabi concluded that the community received Abu Usama's hadith with acceptance for his memory and his piety. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.299}

## Students
'Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi, Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Ishaq ibn Rahwayh, 'Ali ibn al-Madini, Ahmad al-Dawraqi, Salama ibn Shabib, Muhammad ibn 'Abdallah al-Makhzumi and many others narrated from him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p.299}
