## Overview
Adi ibn Adi ibn Umayra al-Kindi, whom al-Baladhuri mentions in "Futuh Armenia". Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik appointed him over Armenia, and then Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz appointed him again. He is the owner of the Adi river in al-Baylaqan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}

According to al-Baladhuri, his father Adi ibn Umayra was among those who settled in al-Raqqa and broke with Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}

In the museum's records his name is linked with the event of [[event/jarrah-governorship-armenia|the governorship of al-Jarrah over Armenia]], as the previous governor and the owner of the Adi river.
