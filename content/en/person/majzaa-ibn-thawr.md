## Summary
Majza'a ibn Thawr al-Sadusi was one of the brave Muslims at [[place/tustar|Tustar]]. He commanded the left wing of [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari's]] army in the battle, and was the officer Abu Musa sent ahead by night into the city, whose men killed the guards and raised the takbir on its wall. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 535}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Musa marched on Tustar, then the enemy's stronghold, and wrote to 'Umar asking for reinforcements; 'Umar sent 'Ammar ibn Yasir with the Kufans, who put Jarir ibn 'Abdallah al-Bajali in the van. On Abu Musa's right wing stood al-Bara' ibn Malik, brother of Anas, on his left wing Majza'a ibn Thawr al-Sadusi, and over the cavalry Anas ibn Malik {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 535}. The people of Tustar fought them hard until the Muslims reached the city's gate, where al-Bara' ibn Malik battled them at the gate until he was martyred, and al-Hurmuzan entered the town {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 535}. Abu Musa then assigned forty men to Majza'a ibn Thawr and followed them with two hundred more by night, a man under safe conduct guiding them; he brought them into the city, they killed the guards and raised the takbir on the wall, and when al-Hurmuzan heard it he fled to his keep where his treasury lay {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 536}.
