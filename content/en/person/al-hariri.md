## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Qasim ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Hariri al-Basri, author of the well-known "Maqamat". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.135}

## The Maqamat
In his maqamat he followed Badi' al-Zaman al-Hamadhani. In his twenty-first maqama, the Razi, he means by the preacher Ibn Sam'un. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.135}
