## Overview
Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili. al-Baladhuri mentions him in two places in Futuh al-Buldan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## In al-Baladhuri
al-Baladhuri reports (p.361) that he raided Syria with Abu Umama al-Sudayy ibn Ajlan al-Bahili, then went out to Iraq with reinforcements for al-Qadisiyya, stayed in Kufa, and was killed at Balanjar. He cites al-Waqidi for a report of his attack on a group of non-Arabs and his seizure of their banner.

Elsewhere (p.204) al-Baladhuri says it was said that he was in the army of Abu Ubayda and settled in a fortress at Qawrus known as the Fortress of Salman. It was also said that he raided the Byzantines after the conquest of Iraq, and that he was one of the Saqaliba whom Marwan ibn Muhammad had stationed on the frontiers. Then al-Baladhuri adds: "God knows best".

The reports conflict, and the entry needs an additional source.

## In Muruj al-Dhahab — al-Masʼudi
Sulayman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili used to breed and to Arabize horses in the time of Umar ibn al-Khattab, that is, to distinguish the hybrid from the purebred among them {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.696}.

## In Muruj al-Dhahab — al-Masʼudi: The horse of Amr
Amr ibn Ma'd Yakrib came to him with a bay horse, and he recorded it as a hybrid, so Amr called Umar as a witness against him. Sulayman ordered a short-walled, shaking vessel to be filled with water. A purebred horse was brought, and it hastened, lowered its hoof, stretched its neck and drank. Then Amr's horse was brought; it hastened and did as the purebred had done, then bent one of its hooves slightly and drank. Umar said: "You are Sulayman of the horses" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.696}.
