## Overview
Former shaykh of al-Azhar (d. 1383/1963); born at Minyat Mansur in the Buhayra, he took the Azhar's headship in 1378 AH and founded the Islamic Research Academy project; among his books is al-Islam 'Aqida wa-Shari'a, on which this entry rests. The dates come from Muhammad 'Imara's introduction to the book and have not yet been checked against a second source.
