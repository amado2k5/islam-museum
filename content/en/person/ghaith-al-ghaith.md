## Summary

Ghaith Al Ghaith is an Emirati aviation executive who is the founding chief executive of flydubai, handpicked by the Government of Dubai in 2008 to head the airline after a 23-year career at Emirates, which he had joined as a management trainee in 1986 and where he rose to Executive Vice President Commercial Operations Worldwide; under him flydubai has grown to a network of more than 95 destinations in 50 countries. {cite:aaco-ghaith-al-ghaith} {cite:flydubai-ghaith-al-ghaith-award} {cite:wikidata-ghaith-al-ghaith}

## Life

The institutional biographies record no birth date for Al Ghaith, and the museum records none. His career began at Emirates Airline in 1986, when he joined as a management trainee a year after that airline's founding. {cite:aaco-ghaith-al-ghaith} {cite:flydubai-ghaith-al-ghaith-award}

## Work

At Emirates, Al Ghaith rose through commercial posts over 23 years to become Executive Vice President Commercial Operations Worldwide. {cite:aaco-ghaith-al-ghaith} {cite:flydubai-ghaith-al-ghaith-award} In 2008 the Government of Dubai handpicked him to head up flydubai, Dubai's first low-cost airline, which began operations in June 2009. {cite:flydubai-ghaith-al-ghaith-award} Under his strategic direction from the beginning of operations, flydubai grew to a network of more than 95 destinations in 50 countries served by a fleet of 67 Boeing 737 aircraft, with up to 166 aircraft on order, as the Arab Air Carriers' Organization biography states them. {cite:aaco-ghaith-al-ghaith} The airline's own release, issued when he won the CEO Middle East Aviation CEO of the Year award for a second time, called flydubai the fastest growing start-up airline in the world at that point, reaching 46 destinations with 20 aircraft within its first years. {cite:flydubai-ghaith-al-ghaith-award}
