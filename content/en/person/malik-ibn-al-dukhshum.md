## Summary
Brother of Banu Salim ibn Awf; the Prophet tasked him with demolishing and burning the mosque of Dirar, with Ma'n ibn Adi (or his brother Asim) — he is the clansman of the quarter in which the mosque stood, per the account. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## In the demolition of the mosque of Dirar
The Prophet called him when he alighted at Dhu Awan and said to him and to Ma'n: go to this mosque, demolish it and burn it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} They went out quickly until they came to Banu Salim ibn Awf — Malik's folk — and Malik said to Ma'n: wait for me until I bring you fire from my people; he took palm-fronds, lit fire in them, then the two went on briskly until they entered the mosque with its people inside, burnt it, demolished it, and its people scattered. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- His companion in the report is Ma'n ibn Adi "or his brother Asim ibn Adi"; Ibn Ishaq wavers on naming the second man (p. 174).
