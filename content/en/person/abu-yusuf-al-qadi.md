## Overview
Abu Yusuf Ya'qub ibn Ibrahim al-Ansari, companion of Abu Hanifa and the greatest transmitter of his school. He held the judgeship for al-Rashid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.286-287}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim counts among his books: on prayer, almsgiving, fasting, the shares of inheritance, sales, the hudud, agency, bequests, hunting, slaughter and usurpation. He also has "Amali", transmitted by Bishr ibn al-Walid the judge. Among other works he mentions "the Book of the Differences of the Cities", "the Refutation of Malik ibn Anas", his letter on kharaj to al-Rashid, and the "Book of Collections" that he wrote for Yahya ibn Khalid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.286-287}

## His son
His son Abu Ishaq held the judgeship during his father's lifetime, and died after him in 192 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.286-287}

## Kitab al-Kharaj

Abu Yusuf wrote [[work/kitab-al-kharaj-abu-yusuf|Kitab al-Kharaj]] for the Commander of the Faithful [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]], who asked him for a comprehensive book to act on in collecting the land tax, tithes, alms and poll tax, wishing to lift injustice from his subjects; the title page of the Salafiyya edition calls him "companion of Imam Abu Hanifa" and dates his life 113-182 AH. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.1} {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.5} He opens the book with an exhortation reminding the caliph that God has put the community in his charge and urging him to justice between near and far and to avoid ruling by whim and anger. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.5} {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.6}
