## Summary
Abu Bishr Matta ibn Yunus, the famous sage; an elder shaykh on whom people read the art of logic, then of great renown and wide fame — the foremost logic-workers gathered in his circle daily as he read Aristotle's book on logic and dictated his commentary to his pupils; al-Farabi wrote seventy sections from him in his own commentary — none in that time was his like in his art; his phrasing in his compilations was fine, his allusions subtle, and he used expansion and simplification — to the point that a scholar of the art said: I see Abu Nasr al-Farabi took the path of making weighty meanings clear in easy words only from the mentioned Abu Bishr; Abu Nasr used to attend his circle among the crowd of his pupils. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
