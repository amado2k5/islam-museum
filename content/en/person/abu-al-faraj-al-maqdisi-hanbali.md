## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Abd al-Wahid ibn Muhammad al-Maqdisi, a Hanbali jurist settled in Damascus; he studied with Qadi Abu Ya'la ibn al-Farra, taught and preached, spread the Hanbali school in the districts of Bayt al-Maqdis, and wrote works in fiqh, doctrine and tafsir. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11493}
