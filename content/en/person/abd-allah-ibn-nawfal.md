## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Nawfal ibn al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim was a Companion and one of the judges. Al-Zirikli lists him in al-A'lam under the heading Ibn Nawfal, gives his death as 84 AH, 703 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 142}.

## Life
He was born in the lifetime of the Prophet, and Marwan ibn al-Hakam appointed him judge of Medina in the year 42 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 142}. Al-Zirikli reports that Abu Hurayra used to say of him that he was the first judge they had seen in Islam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 142}.
