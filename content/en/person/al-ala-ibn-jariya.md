## Summary
A chief of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]] and ally of Banu Zuhra ibn Kilab; he became Muslim after the conquest of Mecca, and the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]] within the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won over]], to reconcile his people and allies {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Al-Ala' was Banu Zuhra's ally in the Jahiliyya and in Islam, with influence and an obeyed opinion among Thaqif and the allied tribes; the Prophet gave him a hundred camels from the Hunayn booty to soothe his people's hearts and settle peace in al-Ta'if and its surroundings {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
A chief of Thaqif, ally of Banu Zuhra; the Prophet honoured him with a gift at al-Ji'rana to encourage his people to enter Islam and abandon the wars.
