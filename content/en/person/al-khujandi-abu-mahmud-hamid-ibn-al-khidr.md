## Overview
Abu Mahmud Hamid ibn al-Khidr al-Khujandi, a mathematician and astronomer who became known in the fourth century AH. He lived in the city of al-Rayy, south-east of Tehran, and died in 391 AH (1000 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
