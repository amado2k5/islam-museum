## Summary
[[person/uthman-ibn-talha|Uthman ibn Talha]] ibn Abi Talha al-Abdari, "the House's doorkeeper"; his mother Umm Sa'id bint al-Aws {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.

## Before his Islam
His father Talha and his uncle Uthman ibn Abi Talha were killed at [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.

## His Islam
He became Muslim in the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|al-Hudaybiyya]] truce and emigrated with Khalid ibn al-Walid; he witnessed the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest]] with the Prophet, who gave him the Ka'ba's key {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}. In the two Sahihs, in Ibn Umar's hadith: "the Prophet entered the Ka'ba, and with him entered Bilal, Uthman ibn Talha and Usama ibn Zayd" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.

## A rejected report
Ibn Hajar mentions that al-Tha'labi's Tafsir has, without chain, under [[passage/quran-4-58|al-Nisa' 58]], that Uthman became Muslim only on the conquest day after the Prophet handed him the House's key. He rules it "rejected" — the established account is that he became Muslim and emigrated with Amr ibn al-As and Khalid ibn al-Walid {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1006}.

## His death
He settled at Medina until he died there in 42 AH — said al-Waqidi and Ibn al-Barqi; or it is said he was martyred at Ajnadayn — al-Askari said that is false {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1007}.
