## Overview
Shuqran, client of the Messenger of God. al-Mas'udi reports that he went down into the grave at the burial, together with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], al-Fadl and Qutham, the sons of al-Abbas {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.582}.
