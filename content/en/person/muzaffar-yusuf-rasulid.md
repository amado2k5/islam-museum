## Overview
Al-Malik al-Muzaffar Shams al-Din Yusuf ibn Umar ibn Ali ibn Rasul, the third of the Rasulid kings in Yemen. He was the son of [[person/mansur-umar-ibn-ali-rasuli|al-Mansur Umar]] and ruler of the [[polity/rasulid-dynasty|Rasulid state]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
He took up the command after his father was killed in 648 AH. Ibn Khaldun describes him as just and benevolent. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

When the Turkish kings of Egypt became independent with power, he imposed a tribute on himself to them, and continued to placate them with it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The early part of his reign was taken up with the fortress of al-Damluwa, and with the revolt of [[person/mutawakkil-ahmad-ibn-husayn|Ahmad al-Muwatti']] the Zaydi, who held twenty fortresses and Safad. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death
He remained governor of Yemen until he died suddenly in 694 AH, after forty-six years of rule. The Gregorian conversion (1295 CE) is derived. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
