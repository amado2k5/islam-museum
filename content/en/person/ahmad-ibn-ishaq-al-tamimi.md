## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ishaq al-Tamimi al-Sayda'lani, a reciter of Baghdad known for his memory and his accuracy in the transmissions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3481}

## Works
His work is al-Wadih fi al-qira'at al-'ashr. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3481}
