## Overview
'Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Mahmud al-Nasafi, Abu al-Barakat, Hafiz al-Din, was a jurist, legal theorist and exegete named after [[place/nasaf|Nasaf]], a city now in Uzbekistan. He died in 710 AH, or, some say, 701, as the editor reports from *al-Fawa'id al-Bahiyya*. {cite:tafsir-nasafi-tahqiq-2018-v1 p. 11} The opening of his tafsir gives him titles such as "guardian of the community and the religion" and "reviver of the Sunna and the obligations". {cite:tafsir-nasafi-shaar-ed p. 3}

## Teachers and students
His teachers included Shams al-A'imma Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Sattar al-Kardari, "the jurist of the East", who excelled in the Hanafi school and its principles and died in 642 AH; Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Mahmud al-Kardari, known as Khwaharzada, who studied with his maternal uncle Shams al-A'imma and died in 651 AH; and [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-hamid-al-din-al-darir|Hamid al-Din 'Ali ibn Muhammad al-Darir of Bukhara]], who died in 666 AH. {cite:tafsir-nasafi-tahqiq-2018-v1 p. 11} Among those who studied with him was Husam al-Din al-Husayn ibn 'Ali ibn Hajjaj al-Sighnaqi, the Hanafi commentator on *al-Hidaya*. {cite:tafsir-nasafi-tahqiq-2018-v1 p. 11}

## What scholars said of him
The hadith master 'Abd al-Qadir al-Qurashi wrote in *al-Jawahir al-Mudiyya*: "one of the later ascetics, author of useful works on law and legal theory". [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] called him in *al-Durar al-Kamina* "the most learned man in the world". Yusuf ibn Taghri Birdi wrote in *al-Manhal al-Safi*: "one of the ascetic scholars and author of useful works on law, legal theory and Arabic; he studied with a number of eminent scholars until he excelled in law, legal theory, Arabic and lexicography." {cite:tafsir-nasafi-tahqiq-2018-v1 p. 11-12}

## Works
- [[work/madarik-al-tanzil|*Madarik al-Tanzil wa-Haqa'iq al-Ta'wil*]], his Qur'an commentary.
- *'Umdat al-'Aqa'id*, on theology.
- *Al-Kafi fi Sharh al-Wafi*, both text and commentary his own, on Hanafi law.
- *Al-Mustasfa* and its abridgement *al-Musaffa*, commentaries on Abu Hafs al-Nasafi's didactic poem on juristic disagreement.
- A commentary on *al-Nafi'* of Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Husayni al-Samarqandi.
- [[work/kanz-al-daqaiq-nasafi|*Kanz al-Daqa'iq*]], on law.
- *Fada'il al-A'mal*.
- *Manar al-Anwar*, on legal theory, and its commentary *Kashf al-Asrar*. {cite:tafsir-nasafi-tahqiq-2018-v1 p. 12}
