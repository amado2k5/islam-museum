## Overview
Muhammad al-Shadhili Khuznadar, a Tunisian poet from the outskirts of Tunis, known as "the poet". He was born in 1299 AH (1881 CE) and died in 1373 AH (1954 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.252}

The encyclopaedia classes him as a poet and writer. The details of his collections and his life need an additional source.
