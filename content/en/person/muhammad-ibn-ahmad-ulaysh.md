## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad Ulaysh, Abu Abd Allah, the head and mufti of the Malikis in Egypt, born and died in Cairo. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2063}

## Works
His works include 'Minah al-jalil' on Khalil's abridgement in four volumes and 'Hidayat al-salik', glosses on al-Sharh al-saghir; he lived from 1217 to 1299 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2063}
