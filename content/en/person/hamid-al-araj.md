## Summary
Hamid ibn Qays al-A'raj, Abu Safwan, a client of the Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza, reader of the people of Mecca, and a well-known trustworthy Successor. He transmitted from Tawus, Ata, Mujahid, Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz and al-Zuhri, and from him Ja'far al-Sadiq, Malik and the two Sufyans. Sufyan ibn Uyayna said he was the most learned in the laws of inheritance and the reckoning among the people of Mecca. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16643}
