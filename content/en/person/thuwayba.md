Thuwayba, freedwoman of Abu Lahab, the first to nurse the Prophet after his mother, with the milk of her son Masruh; she is the same who had nursed before him [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]], and after him nursed Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi — so they became his milk-brothers. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] calls Thuwayba the Prophet's 'second mother' after Amina in her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']]. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:09:00} She relates that an uncle of the Prophet grieved deeply for his brother Abd Allah, who had died without seeing his son, and that when his freedwoman Thuwayba brought him the news that Amina had given birth to Muhammad he rejoiced greatly and set her free; the grandfather Abd al-Muttalib rejoiced too. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:07:30} Thuwayba, she says, went to look at the baby's face and became attached to him; having recently given birth, she held him and nursed him as she nursed her own son, gaining the honour and blessing of nursing him, never left Amina or Muhammad, and lived as a second mother who loved him completely. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep06 00:09:00} The name heard in the recording for this uncle does not agree with the source cited above, which makes Thuwayba a freedwoman of Abu Lahab, so the name is not written here. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
