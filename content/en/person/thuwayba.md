Thuwayba, freedwoman of Abu Lahab, the first to nurse the Prophet after his mother, with the milk of her son Masruh; she is the same who had nursed before him [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]], and after him nursed Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi — so they became his milk-brothers. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
