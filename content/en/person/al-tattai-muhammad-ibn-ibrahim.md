## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Khalil al-Tattai al-Maliki, a Maliki jurist, author of "Tanwir al-Maqala" on Maliki jurisprudence. He was born in 855 AH (1451 CE) and died in 942 AH (1535 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.391}
