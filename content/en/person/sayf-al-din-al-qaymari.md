## Summary
Sayf al-Din al-Qaymari, founder of the maristan at Salihiyya, Damascus, nephew of the lord of Qaymar. He was among the greatest emirs, their famed champions and renowned righteous men — and it was his retinue that seized the king of the Franks on the day of the victory at al-Mansura in 648 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248}. He died at Nablus, was carried back and buried in his dome beside his maristan at Salihiyya; prayer at his grave is answered. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 269}
