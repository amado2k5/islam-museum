## Summary
[[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] ibn Haritha al-Kalbi, "the Beloved, son of the Beloved", kunya Abu Muhammad — or it is said Abu Zayd. His mother [[person/umm-ayman|Umm Ayman]], the Prophet's nurse {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.

## His birth
Ibn Sa'd said: Usama was born in Islam, and the Prophet died while he was twenty. Ibn Abi Khaythama said: eighteen {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.

## The army the Prophet gave him
The Prophet had appointed him over a great army; he died before it set out, and Abu Bakr dispatched it {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.

## His standing with Umar
Umar used to honour and esteem him, preferring him in the stipend over his own son Abdullah ibn Umar {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.

## The troubles and his death
Usama held aloof from the civil strifes after Uthman's killing until he died late in Mu'awiya's caliphate. He had dwelt at al-Mizza in Damascus's countryside, then returned and settled at Wadi al-Qura, then came down to Medina and died there at al-Jurf. Ibn Abd al-Barr authenticated that he died in 54 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.

## Those who related from him
Of the Companions Abu Hurayra and Ibn Abbas related from him, and of the great Tabi'in Abu Uthman al-Nahdi and Abu Wa'il among others; his virtues are many and his hadiths famous {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 43}.
