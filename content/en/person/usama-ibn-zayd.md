## Summary
[[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] ibn Haritha al-Kalbi, known as “the beloved son of the beloved.” He was known as Abu Muhammad, and it is also said Abu Zayd. His mother was [[person/umm-ayman|Umm Ayman]], the Prophet's nurse. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## Birth
Ibn Sad said that Usama was born in Islam, and that the Prophet died when he was twenty years old. Ibn Abi Khaythama said eighteen. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## The army the Prophet appointed him to lead
The Prophet had appointed him to command a great army, but the Prophet died before it set out, and Abu Bakr sent it on its way. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## Umar's regard for him
Umar honoured and respected him, and he gave him a higher stipend than his own son Abdullah ibn Umar. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## Civil strife and death
Usama stayed out of the strife after the killing of Uthman until he died at the end of Muawiya's caliphate. He had settled in al-Mazza, in the district of Damascus, then returned and settled in Wadi al-Qura, and then went down to Medina, where he died at al-Jurf. Ibn Abd al-Barr confirmed that he died in the year 54 AH. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## Those who transmitted from him
Among the Companions, Abu Hurayra and Ibn Abbas transmitted from him. Among the senior Successors were Abu Uthman al-Nahdi and Abu Wail, and others. His virtues are many, and his reports are well known. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.43}.

## “Who will be your guarantor of la ilaha illa Allah?”
Ibn Ishaq reports from Usama that he met [[person/mirdas-ibn-nahik|Mirdas ibn Nahik]] during [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-murra|the raid of Ghalib ibn Abdullah on the land of Banu Murra]], together with a man from the Ansar. The man said, “I bear witness that there is no god but God,” and they killed him. When they told the Prophet, he said, “O Usama, who will be your guarantor of la ilaha illa Allah?” He kept repeating it until Usama wished that his own Islam had begun on that day and that he had not killed the man. He swore to God that he would never kill a man who said it again. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}.
