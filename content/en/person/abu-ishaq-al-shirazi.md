## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ali ibn Yusuf al-Firuzabadi al-Shirazi, Abu Ishaq, a Shafi'i jurist and a scholar of legal theory, author of "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha" and "al-Muhadhdhab". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 5-6}

## His life
He was born in Firuzabad in 393 AH; "al-Lubab" gives 373 or 395, and the editor's preference is 393. He studied in Fars, then Basra, then Baghdad, and became one of the leading Shafi'i jurists of his age. He died in 476 AH; the Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 5-6}

## Refusing the chief judgeship and the Nizamiyya
He declined the office of chief judge when the caliph offered it to him. When the [[place/baghdad|Nizamiyya madrasa in Baghdad]] was completed in 459 AH he was appointed to teach there. When people gathered he did not appear and hid, so Ibn al-Sabbagh taught in his place, until they persuaded him to accept and teach. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 645-646}

## His works
Among his books is [[work/tabaqat-fuqaha|Tabaqat al-Fuqaha]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 5-6}

## Notes for researchers
- The Gregorian conversion is derived from the Hijri dates.
