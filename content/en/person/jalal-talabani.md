# Jalal Talabani

Jalal Talabani (1933 – 3 October 2017) was an Iraqi Kurdish politician, founder of the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan and the first non-Arab president of the Republic of Iraq. He was born at Kelkan in 1933; the English article gives his birth at year precision only and warns that the widely circulated date of 12 November was added to Wikipedia by a banned account and is fictitious, while the Arabic article and Wikidata carry that date. He was known across Kurdistan as "Mam Jalal" — Uncle Jalal. {cite:wikipedia-jalal-talabani-en} {cite:wikipedia-jalal-talabani-ar}

## Career
Talabani joined the Kurdistan Democratic Party as a young man, broke with it to found the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan in 1975, and spent decades between politics, exile and armed struggle for Kurdish rights. After the fall of Saddam Hussein he presided over the Iraqi Governing Council in November 2003 and was elected president in April 2005, beginning a second term on 22 April 2006 as the first president elected under the new constitution. Praised by obituarists as a mediator who brought Iraq's factions together, he served until 2014, when Fuad Masum succeeded him. {cite:wikipedia-jalal-talabani-en}

## Death
Talabani died of a cerebral haemorrhage at Berlin on 3 October 2017 and was buried at Dabashan by Sulaymaniyah. {cite:wikipedia-jalal-talabani-en} {cite:wikidata-jalal-talabani}
