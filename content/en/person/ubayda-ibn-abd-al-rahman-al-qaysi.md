## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that Ubayda ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Qaysi was governor of Ifriqiya after Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi, and that Abd Allah ibn al-Habhab was appointed after him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

In another copy of the book the name appears as "Ubayd Allah" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
