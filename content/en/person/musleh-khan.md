# Musleh Khan

## Summary
Musleh Khan is a Canadian imam and preacher. He was born in Madinah, Saudi Arabia, to Indo-Caribbean parents and raised in Toronto. {cite:listennotes-musleh-khan} {cite:muslimagent-musleh-khan} He completed a decade of intensive studies at the University of Medina alongside some of Islam's most prominent scholars. {cite:listennotes-musleh-khan}

## Work
Khan serves as Resident Scholar at the Islamic Institute of Toronto and as an imam at the Sakinah Community Center in Toronto, and hosts "Ask Musleh", an ongoing online video series answering frequently asked questions about Islam in a practical, simplified manner. {cite:muslimagent-musleh-khan} {cite:listennotes-musleh-khan} In 2016 he was selected as the only Muslim chaplain with the Toronto Police Service, one of Canada's largest law enforcement agencies, and he is recognised as one of Canada's most sought-after Islamic speakers, with a special connection to youth audiences across the Greater Toronto Area. {cite:muslimagent-musleh-khan}
