## Summary
Ibn Wasil, qadi of Hama and historian; an imam versed in many sciences, especially the rational ones, exceedingly intelligent, constant in study and reflection on knowledge until he would lose awareness of who sat with him and of his own state; he wrote many works in the two usuls, wisdom, logic, prosody, medicine and the literary arts. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 766-767}
