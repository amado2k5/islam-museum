## Summary
Mahmud ibn Ali ibn Mahmud ibn Muqbil al-Daqqauqi, then al-Baghdadi, Taqi al-Din, Abu al-Thana, a Hanbali jurist and memorising hadith scholar. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15821} Hadith and preaching in Baghdad ended with him; no one read hadith better in his time, and he had some share in fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15821} Several scholars of Syria and Iraq licensed him, and many heard from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15822} His works include Matali al-Anwar and al-Kawakib al-Durriyya.
