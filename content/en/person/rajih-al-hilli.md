## Overview
Sharaf al-Din Rajih b. Isma'il b. Abi'l-Qasim al-Asadi al-Hilli (570–627) — among the most famous poets of his age; he died at Damascus and was buried outside the city near the Naranj mosque {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
