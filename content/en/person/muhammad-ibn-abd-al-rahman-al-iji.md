## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Hasani al-Husayni al-Iji al-Shafi'i was an exegete from Iij in the district of Shiraz. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 832 AH, 1429 CE, and his death as 905 AH, 1500 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 195}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Jami' al-bayan fi tafsir al-Qur'an, printed, and a treatise explaining the bodily resurrection and the spirit, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 195}.
