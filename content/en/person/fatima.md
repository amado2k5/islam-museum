## Brief
Fatima was the youngest daughter of the Prophet Muhammad and [[person/khadija]], the wife of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib]], and the mother of [[person/hasan-ibn-ali]] and [[person/husayn-ibn-ali]]. Through her sons descend the Prophet's family lines.

## Life
Fatima was born in Mecca sometime between about 605 and 615 CE — the sources disagree {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}. As a child she is remembered tending her father when the Meccan opposition mocked or abused him. She migrated to Medina with [[event/hijra]] and was married to Ali about 624 CE, in a household remembered for poverty and endurance {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

After the Prophet's death in June 632, the early sources report her grief and a dispute with the new leadership over property she claimed from her father's estate at Fadak. She died a few months later — reports give roughly three to six months — and was buried in Medina, her grave unmarked by her own wish, according to the tradition {cite:madelung-1997}.

:::perspectives
- **Sunni**: Sunni tradition reveres Fatima as the foremost woman of the Prophet's household, holding her among the most virtuous women of the community {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.
- **Academic**: Historians note that early sources preserve few independent details of her life, and that the events after the Prophet's death — the succession and Fadak — come through later, partisan reports {cite:madelung-1997}.
- **Twelver Shia**: Twelver Shia tradition venerates Fatima as the Mistress of the Women of the Worlds, holds that she was wronged over the succession and Fadak, and remembers her grief as part of her sanctity {cite:momen-1985}.
:::

## Legacy
Every line of the Prophet's descendants — the sharifs and sayyids honored across the Muslim world — traces through Fatima and Ali. Her name is carried by towns, mosques, the Fatimid dynasty, and millions of women.

## For young visitors
Fatima was the daughter of the Prophet. Her two sons were his only grandchildren to grow up, so everyone who today claims descent from the Prophet is her descendant.
