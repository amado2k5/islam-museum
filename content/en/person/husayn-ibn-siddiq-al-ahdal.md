## Overview
Husayn ibn Siddiq ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Ahdal, a Yemeni scholar. He was born in Bayat in 850 AH (1446 CE) and died in 903 AH (1497 CE). He left manuscript works. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.522}
