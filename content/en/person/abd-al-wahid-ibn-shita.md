## Overview
Abu'l-Fath Ibn Shita, 'Abd al-Wahid b. al-Husayn b. Ahmad — muqri' of Iraq and author of al-Tadhkir fi'l-qira'at al-'ashr; he studied under al-Hammami and a company, and transmitted from Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Bazzaz and others. He died in Safar 450 at eighty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 293}
