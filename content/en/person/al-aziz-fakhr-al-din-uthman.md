## Overview
Al-Malik al-'Aziz Fakhr al-Din 'Uthman b. al-Malik al-Mughith the Ayyubid; young in years, al-Malik al-Zahir Baybars made him an amir and he remained in his service until the conquest of Antioch in Ramadan 666, then journeyed from Syria to Egypt — on entering it he was seized and imprisoned, and he is now held in the said citadel of the Mount. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
