## Overview
Ibn Darbas, the Egyptian qadi Kamal al-Din Abu Hamid the Blind (576–659); al-Silafi gave him ijaza, he heard al-Busiri and Ibn 'Asakir, and he taught, gave fatwa, worked and kept kings' company; he died in Shawwal {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 307}.
