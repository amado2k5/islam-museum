## Overview
Abd al-Azim ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Zafir, Abu Muhammad, whose title was Zaki al-Din al-Misri, known as Ibn Abi al-Isba'. An Egyptian writer and man of letters, raised in the schools of Egypt during the Ayyubid state and part of the Bahri Mamluk state. He wrote works on rhetoric (bayan) and figures of speech (badi'). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.77}

## Dates
He was born around 595 AH (1199 CE) and died in 654 AH (1255 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.77}
