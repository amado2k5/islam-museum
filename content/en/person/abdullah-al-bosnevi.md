## Overview
Abdullah Abdi ibn Muhammad al-Rumi al-Bosnevi al-Bayrami, known as the commentator on the Fusus, was a learned Sufi from Bosnia. He was known among its people as 'Ghaybi', and 'Kashf al-Zunun' mentions him under the name 'Abdi'. Al-Bayrami is a nisba to the Bayrami Sufi order, of whose shaykhs he was one {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. The heading gives his years as 992 to 1054 AH, 1584 to 1644 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left works in Arabic and in Turkish. He commented on Ibn Arabi's 'Fusus al-Hikam' in Turkish, and the Turkish text is printed; he then translated the commentary into Arabic and called it 'Tajalliyat Ara'is al-Nusus fi Manassat Hikam al-Fusus', which is in manuscript. Among his Arabic books is 'Qurrat Ayn al-Shuhud', in manuscript, a commentary on the 'al-Ta'iyya al-Kubra' of Ibn al-Farid. The author of 'al-Jawhar al-Asna' gave the titles of 61 books and treatises by him. He died on his way back from the pilgrimage, in the city of Konya, and was buried there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.
