## Overview
Sabiq al-Khwarizmi was a freedman of Ibrahim the Imam, to whom he handed over the testament addressed to Ibrahim's brother Abu al-Abbas, according to al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.239-241}.

## The testament
Ibrahim instructed him that if something happened to Ibrahim at the hands of Marwan, he should make haste to al-Humayma. When Ibrahim's end came, Sabiq hastened and handed the testament over {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.239-241}.

## In Kufa
Abu Hamid al-Tusi met him in the Kunasa market in Kufa, and directed him to Abu al-Abbas and his companions in the house of al-Walid ibn Sa'd among the Banu Awd {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.239-241}.
