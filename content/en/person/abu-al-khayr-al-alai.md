## Overview
Abu'l-Khayr al-'Ala'i, the musnid of Jerusalem; he profited by his father's learning from the elders — al-Hajjar, al-Mizzi — who travelled with him to Cairo and had him hear Abu Hayyan and several of al-Najib's companions; he dwelt in Jerusalem until he became one of its notables, and the rihla for hearing hadith there was to him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 23}

## Ibn Hajar's account
Ibn Hajar said: "in his last years there surfaced for him a hearing of Ibn Majah upon al-Hajjar, so I travelled to him from Cairo that year for it — word of his death reached me at Ramla, and I turned from Jerusalem to Damascus; he had licensed me more than once." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 23}
