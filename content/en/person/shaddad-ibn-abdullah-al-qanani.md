## Overview
Shaddad ibn Abd Allah al-Qanani, of the delegation of the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] who came to the Prophet with Khalid ibn al-Walid in the year 10 AH, as Ibn Ishaq names him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.

He took part in [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|Khalid's mission to the Banu al-Harith]] as a member of the delegation {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.
