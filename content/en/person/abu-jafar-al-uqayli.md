## Overview
Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Amr al-Uqayli, the hadith master (hafiz). Ibn al-Imad lists him in Shadharat al-Dhahab among those who died in 322 AH, calls him the author of al-Jarh wa-l-Ta'dil as the printed text has it, and says he was counted among the people of the Hijaz {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 117}.

## Teachers and students
He transmitted from Ishaq al-Dabari, Abu Isma'il al-Tirmidhi and many others, and Abu l-Hasan Muhammad ibn Nafi al-Khuza'i and Abu Bakr ibn al-Muqri' transmitted from him {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 117}.

## What scholars said of him
Ibn al-Imad quotes the hafiz Abu l-Hasan al-Qattan as saying that Abu Ja'far was reliable, of great standing, learned in hadith and ahead of his peers in memory {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 117}.

## Death
He died in Mecca in the month of Rabi' I {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 4 p. 117}.
