## Summary
Abdullatif Celebi Latifi was an Ottoman poet and the author of a biographical anthology of poets. Biblissima records 1491 to 1582 CE {cite:biblissima Q218}.
## In the sources
The museum's card links his birth to Kastamonu at medium confidence, pending a second source {cite:biblissima Q218}.
