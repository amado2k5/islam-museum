## Overview
Ibn Bulayma, the Qayrawani muqri', author of Talkhis al-'Ibarat fi'l-qira'at; he read on a company including Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad b. Nafis; he died in Rajab 514 at Alexandria in his eighties {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 521}.
