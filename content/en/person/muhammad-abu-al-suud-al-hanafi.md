## Summary
Muhammad Abu al-Su'ud ibn al-Sayyid Ali, a Hanafi jurist. His works include Fath Allah al-Mu'in, a commentary on the Kanz al-Daqa'iq of Mulla Miskin, a treatise on the marvels of saints, and a gloss on Mulla Miskin's commentary. The book says he was alive in 1155 H. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13366}
