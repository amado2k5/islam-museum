## Summary
Burhan al-Din al-Abnasi, shaykh of Egypt who raised students; trained under al-Mughaltay, studied fiqh under al-Asnawi and al-Manfaluti, and built a zawiya at al-Maqs outside Cairo where he did good to students and arranged their provisions till most of Cairo's seekers were his pupils — Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani studied under him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 10-11, 21-22}

## Appointed, then gone
Appointed to the judgeship, he hid and opened the mushaf — and the prison verse came out; he made the hajj and died returning, in Muharram at 'Uyun al-Qasab near the Ayla pass, mourned by Zayn al-Din al-'Iraqi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 10-11, 21-22}
