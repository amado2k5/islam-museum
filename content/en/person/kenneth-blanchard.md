## Summary
Kenneth Blanchard, author of the well-known The One Minute Manager; Tareq al-Suwaidan mentions that he studied under him and describes him as a kind, fatherly man who embraces young people and their energy and encourages them {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 00:27:30}.
He recounts that William Oncken turned to him after publishers rejected his book on the monkey laws; Blanchard wrote a three-page foreword — being honest, he said in it that he had written nothing of the book but that foreword — and the book came out jointly as [[work/one-minute-manager-meets-the-monkey|The One Minute Manager Meets the Monkey]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 00:29:00}.
