## Overview
Al-Mas'udi names him as a second man involved in the killing of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]], together with Kinana ibn Bishr {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.637-639}.

## The report
Al-Mas'udi reports that he struck Uthman with a sword on the base of his neck. He also cites al-Waqidi that among those who set out from Egypt was Sa'd ibn Humran al-Tujibi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.637-639}.

## Notes for researchers
- The report comes from Muruj al-Dhahab alone. It needs an additional source.
