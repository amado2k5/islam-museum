## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Abi al-Fath ibn Ubaydallah Ibn Khafaja, an Andalusian poet from the island of Shaqr, known for his descriptions of nature. He was born in 450 AH (1058 CE) and died in 533 AH (1139 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.378}
