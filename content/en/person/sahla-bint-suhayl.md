## Summary
Of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; Ibn Ishaq names her among the Abyssinian emigrant women. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her among the emigrant women to Abyssinia of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy, tracing her to Suhayl ibn Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} He does not state her husband at this point.

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text states neither her husband's name nor her fate; nothing was added from outside p. 11.
