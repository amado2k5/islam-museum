## Summary
Of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; Ibn Ishaq names her among the Abyssinian emigrant women. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her among the emigrant women to Abyssinia of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy, tracing her to Suhayl ibn Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} He does not state her husband at this point.

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives her her own entry in al-Isaba, no. 11399: "Sahla bint Suhayl ibn Amr, the Qurashiyya of the Amiri clan" — he states that she became Muslim early and emigrated with her husband Abu Hudhayfa ibn Utba to Abyssinia, where she bore him Muhammad ibn Abi Hudhayfa, as Ibn Ishaq records; and Ibn Sa'd says her mother was Fatima bint Abd al-Uzza ibn Abi Qays, of the clan of her husband Suhayl ibn Amr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1902}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq's extracted text does not name her husband; the attribution is carried on Ibn Hajar's own entry.
