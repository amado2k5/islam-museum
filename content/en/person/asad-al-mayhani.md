## Overview
The scholar Majd al-Din As'ad b. Abi'l-Nasr al-Mayhani — of Mihna, a village near Tus between Sarakhs and Abiward — author of the Ta'liqa; he read fiqh at Marw, his excellence spread and his fame travelled, he twice headed Baghdad's Nizamiyya and formed many students; a fiery intelligence trained under Abu'l-Muzaffar al-Sam'ani and al-Muwaffaq al-Harawi, and a man of religion and scrupulosity. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560}

## His last journey
Born at Mihna in 461, he travelled to Ghazna and grew famous in those parts, then came to Baghdad where people profited from him and his khilaf method; sent from Baghdad as an envoy to Hamadan, he died there in 527. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 560}
