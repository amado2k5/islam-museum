## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Qays al-Ash'ari, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the conquest of Fars. Abu Musa al-Ash'ari sent him with al-Sa'ib ibn al-Aqra' al-Thaqafi, his son-in-law, to al-Sayhmara, the city of Mihrjanqadhaf, and it was conquered by treaty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
