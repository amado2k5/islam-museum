## Summary
Abd al-Aziz ibn Ja'far ibn Ahmad ibn Dara, known as Ghulam al-Khallal, author of the two books al-Shafi and al-Tanbih in the school's fiqh {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}. He wrote on his copy of al-Kharaqi's Mukhtasar that al-Kharaqi had differed from him in sixty questions he did not name; Judge Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Farra' traced them and found ninety-eight {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 425}. He died in 363 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 125}.
