## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Ishaq ibn Ibrahim al-Harbi, author of Gharib al-hadith and Dala'il al-nubuwwa, and one of the transmitters of Ahmad's school from him {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 411}. He died in 285 AH {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 411}.
