## Overview
'Afif al-Din Ibn Mazru', the Hanbali jurist, muhaddith and hafiz; born at Basra, he travelled to Baghdad and read fiqh under Kamal al-Din Ibn Waddah, then moved to Medina and settled there near fifty years until death, making forty hajjs from it walaa-wise, and transmitted much in the Hijaz, Baghdad, Egypt and Damascus — al-Birzali, Ibn al-Khabbaz and al-Harithi heard from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 761-762}
