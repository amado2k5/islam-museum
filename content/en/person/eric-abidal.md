# Eric Abidal

## Summary

Eric Abidal is a French retired footballer, born in Saint-Genis-Laval in 1979. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en} A left-back and centre-back, he played mainly for Lyon and Barcelona, winning 19 trophies with the two clubs, including two Champions Leagues with Barcelona, and won 67 caps for France. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en} His later career was marked by a liver tumour and a transplant. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en} Raised in a Catholic family, he converted to Islam in his twenties; no source names a school for him, so his data record leaves his faith as unknown with the conversion documented. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en}{cite:wikidata-eric-abidal}

## Club career

Abidal began at Monaco and played for Lille before joining Lyon, where he won three consecutive Ligue 1 titles. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en} In 2007 he moved to Barcelona, forming part of the side that won the treble in 2009 and a second Champions League in 2011; he returned from surgery for a liver tumour to lift the 2011 trophy at Wembley, and underwent a liver transplant in 2012. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en} He returned to Monaco in 2013 and retired after a short spell at Olympiacos in 2014. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en}

## France

Abidal won 67 caps for France between 2004 and 2014, representing the nation at the 2006 and 2010 World Cups and at Euro 2008. {cite:wikipedia-eric-abidal-en}
