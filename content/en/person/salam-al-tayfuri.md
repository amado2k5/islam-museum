## Overview
Salam al-Tayfuri, a partner of Sulayman ibn Qirat in the pastures of Sisar. al-Baladhuri reports that Tayfur was a client of Abu Ja'far al-Mansur, who gave him to al-Mahdi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
