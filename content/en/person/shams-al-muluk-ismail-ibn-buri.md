## Summary
Shams al-Muluk Abu'l-Fath Isma'il ibn Taj al-Muluk Buri ibn Tughtakin ruled Damascus after his father. He was full of gravity, famed for courage, much given to raiding the Franks — he took several fortresses from them — and besieged his brother at Baalbek for a time. But he was an unjust, confiscating tyrant, so his mother plotted and a band of his retainers' wives killed him in the citadel of Damascus in Rabi' I 529; his state had lasted about three years. His brother Mahmud succeeded him, and Mu'in al-Din Anar became his atabeg. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 570-571}
