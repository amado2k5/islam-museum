## Summary
Na'ila bint al-Furafisa al-Kalbiyya, of [[group/banu-kalb|Banu Kalb]], was one of the wives of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]]. She had been a Christian and became Muslim before Uthman consummated the marriage, and her Islam was sound, as [[person/ali-muhammad-al-sallabi|al-Sallabi]] reports. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.19}

## Her children
In listing Uthman's daughters al-Sallabi names Maryam bint Uthman, whose mother was Na'ila bint al-Furafisa, and adds that she is said to have borne Uthman a son named Anbasa. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.20}

## The day of the house
In al-Sallabi's account of the [[event/murder-of-uthman|killing of Uthman]], when the attackers surrounded him Na'ila said: kill him or leave him, he used to spend the night in prayer, gathering the whole Qur'an in a single rak'a. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.400} She defended her husband, threw herself over him and shielded him from the sword with her hand; Sudan ibn Humran struck at her deliberately and cut off her fingers, and she turned away. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.400} When the house was plundered a man whom al-Sallabi names as Kulthum al-Tujibi assaulted her, snatched her wrap and spoke obscenely of her; Uthman's servant Subayh saw him and killed him, and the servant was killed in turn. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.401}

## Uthman's burial
Al-Sallabi, quoting al-Bidaya wa-l-Nihaya, says that among those present at Uthman's burial were his two wives, Na'ila and Umm al-Banin. {cite:sallabi-uthman-tawzi-2003 p.404}
