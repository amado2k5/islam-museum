## Overview
Al-Nu'man ibn Imri al-Qays ibn al-Shaqiqa, daughter of Abu Rabi'a ibn Dhuhl ibn Shayban, king of al-Hira. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 404}

## Al-Khawarnaq
Al-Baladhuri says that he built al-Khawarnaq for Bahram Gur son of Yazdgerd. Bahram Gur was in his chamber when he left his kingdom and went wandering. Adi ibn Zayd al-Ibadi mentioned it in his poetry. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 404}
