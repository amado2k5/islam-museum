Humayun Saeed (born 27 July 1971) is a Pakistani actor and producer from Karachi. He began as a television producer in the early 1990s before becoming one of Pakistan's leading screen actors, and he runs the Six Sigma Plus production house, which produces television dramas and films.{cite:wikipedia-humayun-saeed-en} His leading roles include the television drama Meray Paas Tum Ho (2019) and the hit films Jawani Phir Nahi Ani (2015) and Punjab Nahi Jaungi (2017), and he has received Lux Style Awards and ARY Film Awards.{cite:wikipedia-humayun-saeed-en}{cite:wikidata-humayun-saeed}
