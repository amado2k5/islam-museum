## Overview
In the book's account, the Qarmatian missionary in Najran: a man of Hashid and a client of Banu Umayya, who lodged at the village of Rijla in Najran. He was not found at home when his house was surrounded in 294 AH.

## Detail
The book names the Qarmatian missionary as Husayn ibn Husayn, of Hashid and a client of Banu Umayya, lodged in the village of Rijla in Najran. Al-Qasim surrounded his house and did not find him there. The footnote dates the Qarmatian movement in Yemen from 291 AH. The book gives no dates of birth or death.
