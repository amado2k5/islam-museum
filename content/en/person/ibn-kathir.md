## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Isma'il ibn Umar Ibn Kathir al-Qaysi of Busra, Imad al-Din: born in 700 or shortly after, his father died in 703; he studied closely with al-Mizzi and married his daughter, and studied with Ibn Taymiyya, came to love him deeply and suffered trials on his account. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374} His works include his Tafsir, al-Bidaya wa-l-Nihaya and the Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya. Ibn Hajar calls him one of the hadith scholars among the jurists. He died in Sha'ban 774, having gone blind late in life. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}

## The Stories of the Prophets
Among his books is [[work/qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir|Qisas al-Anbiya']], from Adam to Jesus. Its editor 'Abd al-Hayy al-Farmawi lists among his works al-Ijtihad fi Talab al-Jihad, Ahkam al-Tanbih, al-Bidaya wa-al-Nihaya, the commentary on the Qur'an, Takmilat Asma' al-Thiqat wa-al-Du'afa', Jami' al-Masanid wa-al-Sunan, part of a commentary on al-Bukhari's Sahih, Tabaqat al-'Ulama', al-Fusul fi Sirat al-Rasul and al-Wadih al-Nafis fi Manaqib al-Imam Muhammad ibn Idris. He gives his death as Sha'ban 774 AH. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-farmawi-1997 p. 5-6}
