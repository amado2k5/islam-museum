## Overview
Al-Rabi' b. Sulayman b. 'Abd al-Jiyar al-Muradi, Abu Muhammad — muezzin of the congregational mosque of Fustat (the mosque of 'Amr) and al-Shafi'i's own companion and the transmitter of his books; some two hundred men journeyed to him for them. Born 174 AH; al-Shafi'i said of him "no one ever served me as al-Rabi' served me" and "if I could feed you knowledge I would feed you." Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Maja, Abu Zur'a, Abu Hatim and al-Tahawi transmitted from him. He died on 21 Shawwal 270 AH, and the Amir Khumarawayh ibn Tulun prayed over him {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.1 p.259-261}.
