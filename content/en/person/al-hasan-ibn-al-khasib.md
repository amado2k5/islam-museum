## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn al-Khasib, one of the skilled practitioners of astronomy. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.385}

## His books
He has a book he calls "al-Kar Mahtar", containing four books: "Introduction to the Science of the Configuration", "Transformation of the Years of the World", "Nativities", and "Transformation of the Years of Nativities". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.385}
