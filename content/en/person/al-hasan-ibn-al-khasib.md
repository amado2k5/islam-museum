## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn al-Khasib, one of the masters of the astrologer's art; author of the book he called al-Karmihr, containing four books including the Introduction to the science of the sphere, the Transfer of the years of the world, Nativities, and the Transfer of nativity years. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 411}
