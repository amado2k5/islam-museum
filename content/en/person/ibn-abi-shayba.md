## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Shayba al-Absi, by clientage, al-Kufi, Abu Bakr, was a memoriser of hadith. The entry dates his birth to 159 AH, equal to 776 CE, and his death to 235 AH, equal to 849 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote books on hadith, among them the Musnad; the Musannaf on hadiths and reports, printed in five parts; the Book of Faith, printed; and the Book of Zakat, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.

## Death
He died in 235 AH, equal to 849 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}. The entry is short and says nothing more.
