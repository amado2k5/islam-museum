## Overview
Ahmad Abd al-Sattar al-Jawari, an Iraqi scholar born in al-Karkh in Baghdad, who obtained a doctorate. He was among the leading scholars of the Arab heritage. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The museum's data give his birth as 1341 AH (1922 CE) and his death as 1408 AH (1988 CE), from this source alone, so they need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
