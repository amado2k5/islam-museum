## Summary
Khalid ibn Zayd al-Muzani was a transmitter from [[place/basra|Basra]] and an eyewitness of the conquest of [[place/al-sus|al-Sus]]. His eye was struck at al-Sus, and he narrated the siege and its fall under the amnesty of the Hundred, and how its dihqan excluded himself and was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 533}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri transmits through Marwan ibn Mu'awiya from Humayd al-Tawil from Habib from Khalid ibn Zayd al-Muzani — whose eye had been struck at al-Sus — who said: "We besieged its city with our commander Abu Musa and suffered hardship; then its dihqan made terms with him to open the city and secure safety for a hundred of its people, and he did so" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 533}. In the siege narrative, when the people of al-Sus ran out of food they begged for quarter; their marzban asked safety for eighty of them on condition he open the gate and surrender the town, named the eighty, and left himself out — whereupon Abu Musa had him beheaded while sparing the eighty, killing the other combatants, seizing the wealth and taking the families captive {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 533}. The envoy of Abu Musa's covenant then told the dihqan "remove them," and he kept naming the hundred until only "the enemy of God" remained, whom Abu Musa ordered killed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 534}.
