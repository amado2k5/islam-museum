## Overview
Khalid ibn Zayd al-Muzani, whose eye was afflicted by weevils. Al-Baladhuri relates his siege and conquest of a town on terms of safe conduct for a hundred, and that its landlord removed himself and was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
