## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ishaq ibn Muhammad al-Mu'ayyad al-Abraqawhi, Shafi'i judge of Abraqawh, a hadith scholar and a Sufi. He was born in Abraqawh in Fars and died in Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.175-178}

## Dates
He was born in 615 AH (1218 CE) and died in 701 AH (1301 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.175-178}
