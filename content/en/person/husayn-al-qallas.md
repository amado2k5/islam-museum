## Overview
Al-Husayn al-Qallas, a Baghdadi jurist, among the leading transmitters of hadith and keepers of the Shafi'i school. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}

## His transmission
Dawud ibn Ali reports from him in "Fada'il al-Shafi'i", and he transmitted from Abu Thawr and Abu Ali al-Za'farani. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}

## The form of the name
The editor of the book, quoting al-Subki, says that "al-Qallas" is with a qaf, a doubled lam and an unpointed sin, and it is said that his name was al-Hasan. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}
