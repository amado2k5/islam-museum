## Summary
Professor Muhammad Farid Wajdi, former editor-in-chief of Majallat al-Azhar and a writer defending Islam against the questions of science and philosophy in the first half of the twentieth century; he died in 1373/1954, as stated on the cover of his book al-Islam Din 'Amm Khalid. {cite:al-islam-din-amm-khalid-azhar p. 2}
