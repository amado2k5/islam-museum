## Summary
Sa'id ibn Muzahim ibn Abi Muzahim al-Umawi, a client of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. He narrated from his father, and Qutayba ibn Sa'id narrated from him; the entry notes one hadith for him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7517}
