## Overview
Abu'l-Tayyib al-Ghassani al-Ili; he settled at Samarra and transmitted there from Yazid and Adam b. Abi Iyas — Ibn Sa'id and al-Kawkabi transmitting from him — trustworthy and truthful. He died in Sha'ban 263 {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 134}.
