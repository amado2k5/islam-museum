## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad ibn Ayyub al-Bakri al-Andalusi, Abu Ubayd, an encyclopaedic scholar from al-Andalus. He is the author of "al-Masalik wa al-Mamalik" on geography and history. The encyclopaedia gives his birth as 432 AH and his death as 487 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.725}
