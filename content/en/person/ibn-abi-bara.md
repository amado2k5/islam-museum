## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, known as Ibn Abi Bara, from the town of al-Hadītha, a collector of books. Ibn al-Nadim says he had a library, the like of which he had not seen for abundance, holding a body of rare books on grammar, philology, literature and older writings. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.106}

## As Ibn al-Nadim saw him
Ibn al-Nadim describes him as reserved and stingy with what he had, and fearful of the Banu Hamdan. He brought out for Ibn al-Nadim a large chest holding about three hundred pounds of parchment (al-falajan, al-sakkal and paper). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.106}
