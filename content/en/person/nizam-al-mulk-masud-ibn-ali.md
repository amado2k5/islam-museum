## Overview
Nizam al-Mulk Mas'ud ibn Ali, vizier of Khwarazm Shah Ala al-Din Tekish {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.263}.

## His death
Those whom Ibn al-Athir calls "the heretics", meaning the Isma'ilis, rose against him and killed him in Jumada al-Akhira 593 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.263}.

Tekish ordered his son Qutb al-Din Muhammad to attack their fortresses. He besieged the fortress of Tarshish until they made peace with him for a hundred thousand dinars {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.263}.
