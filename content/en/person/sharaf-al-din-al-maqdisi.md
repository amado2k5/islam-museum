## Summary
Sharaf al-Din al-Maqdisi, Hanbali jurist, heard much hadith from Abu'l-Yaman al-Kindi and a circle after him, studied fiqh under al-Shaykh al-Muwaffaq and excelled; he gave fatwas and taught at the Jawziyya for a time. Abu Shama said: he was a good man. He died on the night of 8 Muharram at Damascus and was buried on the mountain. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 306}
