## Overview
He is Ubaydallah ibn Hanash, who saw some of the troops who had been at Shibam going back to Sana'a and came to [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] and said the news had reached him and was bad, and that his leaving Sana'a was better for Ali than his staying with him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.220}. Ali ibn Sulayman said: act as you think best, and he went out with Ahmad ibn Harnud to [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim]] and urged him on to al-Hadi {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.220}. The text gives neither his descent nor his home, and he is understood to have been at Sana'a with Ali ibn Sulayman. See [[event/ibn-hanash-and-ibn-harnud-bring-al-daam-and-abu-al-qasim-to-shibam-288]].
