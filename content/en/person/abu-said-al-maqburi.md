## Summary
Kaysan ibn Sa'id, Abu Sa'id al-Maqburi of Medina, a trustworthy Successor who narrated much hadith from Umar, Ali and Abu Hurayra among others; Ibn Sa'd places him in the first rank of Medinese. He died in 100 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9957}
