## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd Rabbih, Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i, the author of the Ghaylaniyyat, was a hadith scholar whom al-Zirikli calls reliable. He gives his birth as 266 AH and his death as 354 AH, with 879 and 965 CE printed beside them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 224}.

## Life
He came from Jabal, near Wasit, and was a cloth merchant; he made a long journey in search of hadith that ended with his settling and dying in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 224}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists a Musnad of Musa al-Kazim ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad, a majlis of hadith and al-Fawa'id, all in manuscript in the Zahiriyya library, and al-Fawa'id al-Muntakhaba al-'Awali 'an al-Shuyukh, known as al-Ghaylaniyyat, in manuscript in the British Museum, Dar al-Kutub and the library of the Haram in Mecca {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 224}.
