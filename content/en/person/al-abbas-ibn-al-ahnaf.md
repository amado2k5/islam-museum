## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn al-Ahnaf ibn al-Aswad al-Hanafi, an Abbasid poet. He grew up in Baghdad, while his family were in Basra, and he is attributed to the Banu Hanifa. He died around 192 AH (808 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.342-344}

## In "Muruj al-Dhahab"
Al-Mas'udi quotes a verse of his, taken from a saying of Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan to al-Mansur. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.288}

Al-Mas'udi relates another report: a group of people from Basra found him lying under a tree on the pilgrimage road. He recited verses about exile, then his soul departed, and they washed and buried him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.496-497}
