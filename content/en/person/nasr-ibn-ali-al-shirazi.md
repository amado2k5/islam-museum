## Summary
Nasr ibn Ali al-Shirazi, known as Ibn Abi Maryam, preacher and scholar of Shiraz. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2072}

## Works
Mahmud ibn Hamza al-Kirmani studied with him; his works include a Qur'an commentary and 'al-Muwadih'; he died after 565 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2072}
