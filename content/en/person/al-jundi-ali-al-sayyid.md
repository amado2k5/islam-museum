## Overview
Ali al-Sayyid al-Jundi, a man of letters, poet and professor of literature. He served as dean of the Dar al-Ulum and as a member of the Arabic Language Academy. He was born in 1316 AH (1898 CE) and died in 1393 AH (1973 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Standing
The source counts him among the foremost of his generation. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
