## In brief
Hisham b. Yusuf, judge, scholar, and mufti of Sanaa, the precise proof Abu Abd al-Rahman al-San'ani; he narrated from Ibn Jurayj, Mu'ammar, al-Qasim b. Fayyad, and others, while Ali b. al-Madini, Ibrahim b. Musa the reader, Ishaq, Ibn Ma'in, Abdallah al-Musnadi, and others narrated from him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.320}

## His standing
Yahya b. Ma'in said "he is more reliable than Abd al-Razzaq in Ibn Jurayj", Abu Hatim called him "reliable and precise", Ibrahim b. Musa relates that al-Thawri came to Yemen and said "find me a fast-handed scribe" — Hisham rode to him and did the writing — and Abu Zur'a said "Hisham has the soundest book of men". He died in 197 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.320}
