## Overview
Al-Mustansir billah II, Abu al-Qasim Ahmad ibn al-Zahir, the first of the Abbasid caliphs in Egypt. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.723-724}

## The allegiance in Cairo
He had been imprisoned in Baghdad, and was freed when the Tatars took it. He fled to the Arabs of Iraq. He then came to Baybars, who gave him the allegiance on the thirteenth of Rajab 659 AH, after the caliphate had been vacant for three and a half years. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.723-724}

## Setting out for Iraq
He decided to head to Iraq, and the sultan equipped him with a million dinars and sixty thousand dirhams. He took al-Hadhitha and Hit, then met the Tatars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.723-724}

He was lost in the battle on the third of Muharram 660 AH. It was said that he was killed, and it was said that he survived and fled. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.723-724}
