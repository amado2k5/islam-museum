## Overview
Tughj, amir of Damascus. al-Suyuti reports that he fought Ibn Zakarawayh al-Qarmati more than once when the latter came to Damascus in the year 289 AH, and then killed the Qarmati {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.576}.

al-Suyuti also reports that his son Muhammad, lord of Egypt, who was titled al-Ikhshid, came to al-Muttaqi at al-Raqqa offering him Egypt {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.610}.

## In al-Mas'udi
al-Mas'udi calls him Tughj ibn Shabib, the father of al-Ikhshid. He reports that Tughj set out from Damascus with many armies, entered Tarsus as a raider, and conquered Malwiyya. He says that Tughj was with Kharumarwayh on the night he was slaughtered {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.609-610}.
