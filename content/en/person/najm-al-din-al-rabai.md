## Overview
Najm al-Din al-Raba'i, Egypt's katib-'adl; brilliant in fiqh, Arabic, arithmetic and geometry — he taught and gave fatwa, and "no letter passed him by unless it wounded him"; he died in Ramadan 668 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 569}.
