## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Mas'ud ibn Umar al-Taftazani, the great scholar, listing among his works his two commentaries on al-Talkhis, his commentary on al-Aqa'id, al-Maqasid with its commentary and al-Talwih, and says mastery of rhetoric and the rational sciences in the East culminated in him and he left no equal. He died in Safar 792, or by another report in 791. The editor notes that Ibn Hajar also entered him under the name Mahmud ibn Umar. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/350} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/332}
