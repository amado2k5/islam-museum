## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mansur b. Asad al-Din Shirkuh, lord of Homs; resolved on taking Damascus, death surprised him in al-Malik al-Ashraf's orchard at al-Nayrab in Safar 644 — his bier was carried to Homs and he was buried beside his father; his son al-Malik al-Ashraf Musa stood over Homs after him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 237}
