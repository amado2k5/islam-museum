## Overview
Al-Hasan b. Sa'id al-Sukkari, the celebrated grammarian; he took from the imams of language and authored Kitab al-Mukhtalif wa'l-Mu'talif, Kitab al-Hikam wa'l-Amthal, Kitab al-Zawajir and others {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
