## Summary
Mu'in al-Din al-Harawi, known as Miskin or Mulla Miskin, a Hanafi jurist. Ibn Abidin, quoting a commentary on al-Ashbah, calls his gloss on the Kanz strange because little is known of its author. His work includes a commentary on al-Nasafi's Kanz al-daqa'iq on Hanafi law. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1645}
