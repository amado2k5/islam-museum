## Overview
Ayyub ibn Musa al-Husayni al-Qirimi al-Kafawi, Abu al-Baqa, author of the printed 'al-Kulliyyat', was one of the Hanafi judges {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 25}. He lived and held the judgeship in Kaffa in Turkey, in Jerusalem and in Baghdad, then returned to Istanbul, where he died and was buried in the tomb of Khalid {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 25}. The heading of the entry gives his death as 1094 AH, 1683 CE; the entry gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 25}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli adds that he wrote other books in Turkish, without naming them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 25}.
