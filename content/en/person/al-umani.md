## Overview
al-Umani, a poet who stood before al-Rashid as an orator extolling Muhammad al-Amin and urging him to renew the succession pledge to him; when told 'rejoice at the heirship' he said 'yes, by God — the joy of grass at rain and of a barren woman at a child', and asked about Abd Allah al-Ma'mun he said 'pasture, not like the sa'dan thorn' — al-Rashid said 'God slay him, a bedouin who knows where desire lies'.
