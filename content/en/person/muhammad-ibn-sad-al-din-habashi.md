## Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Saʿd al-Din, king of Abyssinia

Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Saʿd al-Din, king of Abyssinia for the Muslims. Multitudes from Abyssinia became Muslim at his hands. His paternal cousins killed him in Jumada II 835 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 215}.
