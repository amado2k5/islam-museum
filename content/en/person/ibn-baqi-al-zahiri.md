## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim Ahmad ibn Yazid ibn 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad al-Umawi, their freedman, al-Baghawi al-Qurti Ibn Baqi; qadi al-jama'a who heard his grandfather Abu'l-Hasan and Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Haqq al-Khazraji and was given ijaza by Shurayh and others; he was Zahiri in madhhab, the musnid of the Maghreb and its scholar and chief, and held the judgeship of Marrakesh added to supreme headship and more; he died mid-Ramadan 625 past eighty-eight, and the last to transmit from him was 'Abdallah ibn al-Hanbali at Baghdad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 117}
