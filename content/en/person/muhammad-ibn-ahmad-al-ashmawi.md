## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Hijazi al-Ashmawi al-Azhari. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17709} He studied fiqh under Shaykh Abd al-Diwi and al-Shihab Ahmad ibn Umar al-Dayrabi, heard hadith from al-Zurqani, and after his death took the six books from his pupil al-Shihab Ahmad ibn Abd al-Latif al-Manzili, a transmission in which most of the learned men of his age were unique. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17709} Al-Jabarti called him the imam, the jurist, the hadith scholar and the transmitter by chain. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17709} The source cited is al-Jabarti's 'Aja'ib al-athar; the volume and page reference is not clear in the scan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17709} The heading gives 1167 AH with a question mark at its start, so the date is stated with that caution.
