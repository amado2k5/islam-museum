## Overview
Qutb al-Din Abu Ali Abd al-Karim ibn Abd al-Nur ibn Mutayr al-Halabi al-Misri, a hadith memoriser among the notable hadith scholars. Of Aleppine origin, he settled in Egypt. He was born in 664 AH (1266 CE) and died in 735 AH (1335 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.134}
