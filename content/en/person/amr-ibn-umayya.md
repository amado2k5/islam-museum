## Summary
An emigrant of Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza who perished in the land of Abyssinia; not Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri whom the Prophet sent to the Negus. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants" of Banu Asad, saying he perished in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He names him again among "those of them who perished" in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10} He is other than [[person/amr-ibn-umayya-al-damri|Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri]], the Prophet's envoy to the Negus in the same chapter. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-asad|Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Name overlap with Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri: the first is an Asadi who perished in Abyssinia, the second a Damri whom the Prophet sent to the Negus; the two biographies are not merged.
