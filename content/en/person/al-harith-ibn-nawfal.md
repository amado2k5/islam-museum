## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Nawfal ibn al-Harith ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib al-Hashimi; he embraced Islam with his father, and the Prophet employed him for some duty. He governed Mecca for 'Umar and 'Uthman, and is said to have settled in Basra and built a house there. He died under 'Uthman at about seventy years of age. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1355}
