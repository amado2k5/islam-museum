## Overview
Abu Nasr Ismail ibn Hammad al-Jawhari al-Farabi, a philologist from Farab in Turkistan. The source says that the reason for his being called al-Jawhari is not known, and that he died around 398 AH (1008 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
