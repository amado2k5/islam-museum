## Overview
Harthama ibn A'yan, an Abbasid commander. Al-Tabari reports that after he had finished with the affair of Abu al-Saraya in 200 AH, he set out from Kufa to Khurasan to meet al-Ma'mun, and went on, despite al-Ma'mun's letters ordering him to return to Syria or the Hijaz. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.542}

## His counsel to al-Amin
Al-Mas'udi in "Meadows of Gold" reports that al-Amin consulted him about deposing his brother. He said, in substance: "No one who lies to you will advise you; do not embolden the commanders to depose you, lest they depose you, and do not make them break the covenant, lest they break your covenant." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.375}
