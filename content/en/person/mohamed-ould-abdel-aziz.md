# Mohamed Ould Abdel Aziz

Mohamed Ould Abdel Aziz (born 20 December 1956) is a retired Mauritanian military officer and politician who served as the eighth President of Mauritania from 2009 to 2019. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en} {cite:wikidata-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz}

## The two coups

Aziz was born at Akjoujt and joined Morocco's Meknes Royal Military Academy in 1977. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en} A career soldier and high-ranking officer, he was a leading figure in the August 2005 coup that ousted President Maaouya Ould Sid'Ahmed Taya, and on 6 August 2008 he led another coup, which removed President Sidi Ould Cheikh Abdallahi; he then headed the High Council of State as Mauritania's ruler until April 2009. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en}

## Presidency

Aziz won the 2009 presidential election and took office on 5 August 2009. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en} He chaired the African Union from January 2014 to January 2015. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en} When Mohamed Ould Ghazouani took the oath on 1 August 2019, it was Mauritania's first peaceful transfer of power. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en}

## Trial and sentence

In March 2021 a judge charged Aziz and ten people in his inner circle with corruption; in June 2021 he was transferred from house arrest to jail after refusing to co-operate with police, and in December 2021 he underwent heart surgery in Nouakchott's military hospital. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en} On 4 December 2023 he was convicted of illicit enrichment and laundering and sentenced to five years' imprisonment; on 14 May 2025 an appeal court in Nouakchott raised the sentence to fifteen years. {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-ould-abdel-aziz-en}
