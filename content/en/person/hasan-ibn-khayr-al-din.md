## Summary
Son of [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]], appointed governor of Algiers by the Sublime Porte in 951/1544 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 229}.

## The appointment
On [[person/hasan-aga-al-tushi|Hasan Agha]]'s death the diwan installed [[person/al-hajj-bakir|al-Hajj Bakir]] provisionally; the Porte then appointed Hasan b. Khayr al-Din, who arrived the same year {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 229}.
