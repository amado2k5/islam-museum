## Overview
Isma'il ibn Ja'far ibn Abi Kathir al-Ansari, Abu Ibrahim, was the reciter of the people of Medina in his time and a client of the Banu Zurayq of the Ansar. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 130 AH, 747 CE, and his death as 180 AH, 796 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 312}. Al-Safadi enters him as the Medinan Ansari, one of the leading scholars of Medina in the Qur'an and hadith, and gives his death as 180 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 pp. 64-65}.

## Travel and teaching
He moved to Baghdad, took charge of the education of Ali ibn al-Mahdi and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 312}. Al-Safadi says he settled in Baghdad and tutored Ali, the son of al-Mahdi {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 65}.

## Transmission
Al-Safadi states that al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah narrated from him, that al-Kisa'i read the Qur'an under him, and he quotes Ibn Ma'in calling him trustworthy and reliable {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 9 p. 65}.
