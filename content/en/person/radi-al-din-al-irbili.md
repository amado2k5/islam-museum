## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Yunus ibn Muhammad ibn Man'a, styled Radi al-Din al-Irbili — father of the two shaykhs 'Imad al-Din and Kamal al-Din — studied fiqh at Irbil under Taj al-Islam Ibn Khamis, then went down to Baghdad and studied under Shaykh Abu Mansur Sa'id. At Mosul he found full acceptance with Amir Zayn al-Din 'Ali ibn Baktakin, who entrusted him with teaching at his mosque and placed its supervision in his hands; there he taught, issued fatwas and debated, students seeking him out until he died. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
