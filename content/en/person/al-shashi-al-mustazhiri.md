## Summary
Al-Shashi al-Mustazhiri, shaykh of the Shafi'is; he held the Nizamiyya chair at Baghdad, studied under Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi and Ibn al-Sabbagh, and the headship of the madhhab ended with him after his elders died out; students called him 'the Junayd' for his scrupulosity. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 496-497}

## His epithet and death
He composed *al-Hilya* for the caliph al-Mustazhir — hence the epithet — and his *al-Shafi*, a commentary on al-Shirazi's *al-Shamil*, runs to twenty volumes; he died in Shawwal 507 and was buried beside Shaykh Abu Ishaq. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 496-497}
