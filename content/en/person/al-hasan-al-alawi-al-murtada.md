## Overview
Al-Hasan b. al-Sayyid al-Amir 'Ali b. al-Murtada, Abu Muhammad al-'Alawi al-Husayni; the last to hear from Ibn Nasir — he transmits from him the Kitab al-dhariyya al-tahira. He died in Sha'ban 630 at eighty-six, his hearing having been in his fifth year. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 136}
