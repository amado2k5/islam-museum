## Overview
A Basran Successor of the Dabi'a clan of Qays. He transmitted from Umar, Ali and Sad ibn Abi Waqqas, and his son Abdallah, Ibn Sirin and Abu Nadra al-Abdi transmitted from him. Ibn Sad said he was trustworthy and few in traditions, and al-Nasai and others graded him trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5158}

## Death
The source gives it as about 85 AH, an approximation rather than a fixed date.
