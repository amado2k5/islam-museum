## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad, Abu Bakr Ibn Asim al-Qaysi al-Gharnati, was a judge and one of the Maliki jurists of al-Andalus. He lived from 760 AH (1359 CE) to 829 AH (1426 CE), and was born and died in Granada {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 45}.

## Career
Al-Zirikli says that in his youth he bound books, and that he rose until he held the office of chief judge in his own city {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 45}.

## Works
His books include Tuhfat al-Hukkam fi nukat al-uqud wa-l-ahkam (printed), a verse treatise in the rajaz metre on Maliki jurisprudence known as al-Asimiyya, on which a number of scholars wrote commentaries {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 45}. Another is Hada'iq al-Azahir fi mustahsan al-ajwiba wa-l-mudhhikat wa-l-hikam wa-l-amthal wa-l-hikayat wa-l-nawadir (printed), and he also composed rajaz poems on legal theory, grammar and the Qur'anic readings {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 45}.

## Family
He was the father of Abu Yahya, Muhammad ibn Muhammad Ibn Asim, whose own entry al-Zirikli gives later in the same work {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 45}.
