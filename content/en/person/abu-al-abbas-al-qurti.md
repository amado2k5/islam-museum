## Overview
Abu'l-'Abbas al-Qurti al-Shahid, resident of Alexandria; one of the great imams — he heard a company in the Maghrib, abridged the two Sahihs, and composed Kitab al-mufhim, a commentary on Muslim's Mukhtasar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}
