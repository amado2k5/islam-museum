## Summary
Ali ibn Khalil, Ala al-Din Abu al-Hasan al-Tarabulusi al-Hanafi: a jurist who held the judgeship in Jerusalem. His Mu'in al-Hukkam on judicial rulings in disputes between two parties, written on the Hanafi school's view of judging, is his known work. The book gives his death as 844 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7161}
