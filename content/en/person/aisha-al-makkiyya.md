## Summary
A'isha al-Makkiyya was a devout woman of [[place/mecca|Mecca]] who kept company with [[person/al-fudayl-ibn-iyad|al-Fudayl ibn Iyad]], as [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] records in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.402}

## In Sifat al-Safwa
He reports from [[person/abu-ubayd-al-qasim-ibn-sallam|Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam]] that on entering Mecca he would sometimes sit facing the Ka'ba and sometimes lie back and stretch out his legs; A'isha al-Makkiyya came to him and said: they say you are a scholar — accept a word from me: "Do not sit with Him except with good manners, lest He erase your name from the register of nearness." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.402}
