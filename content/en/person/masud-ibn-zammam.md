## Overview
Mas'ud ibn Zammam, known as Mas'ud al-Balat, one of the notable amirs of the Nafusa mountains. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}

## Against the Almohads
He was in revolt against the authority of Abd al-Mu'min. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}

In 568 AH he joined Qaraqush, the mamluk of Taqi al-Din Umar, and they agreed, and their numbers grew. They descended on Tripoli in the West, and it was taken. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47} See [[event/qaraqush-takes-tripoli-568|Qaraqush's taking of Tripoli]] and [[person/sharaf-al-din-qaraqush|Qaraqush]].
