## Overview
Al-Ghadban ibn al-Qabathari al-Shaybani, a notable of the Banu Shayban and a man of Kufa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.137-141}

## With al-Hajjaj
Al-Mas'udi relates that when [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] took office, he stood as a preacher in the mosque and called for opposing him on the road and killing him, saying that this would not count as deposing him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.137-141}

It is said that he was the one who said to Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Ash'ath: "Have your morning meal with al-Hajjaj before he has his evening meal with you." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.137-141}

Al-Hajjaj imprisoned him for three years, until he needed his coarse wit for a letter of Abd al-Malik about the qualities of slave-girls. He then released him after the affair of the green dome at Wasit. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.137-141}
