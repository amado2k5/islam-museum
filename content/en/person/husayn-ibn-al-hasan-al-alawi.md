## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi was a commander of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] named in the array at the pond of "al-Damriyya": "he put al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi, Banu Ubayd of Wadi'a and the people of Athafit on the left". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.136} The book does not give his lineage or home here, and it is not established that he is the governor of Saqayn, [[person/husayn-ibn-al-hasan-governor-of-saqayn|al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan]], or anyone else, so the name stands as printed. See [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-the-array-at-al-damriyya-286]]. The account is Zaydi.
