## Summary
Abu Yahya Ibn Nabbata, the khatib of khatibs 'Abd al-Rahim ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Nabbata al-Fariqi al-Lakhmi — 'Asqalan-born, Egyptian by domicile; he held the khitaba of Aleppo for Sayf al-Dawla, his sermons showing his power of knowledge, breadth and ready talent, and all agree none ever made sermons like his; it was he whose sermons spurred Sayf al-Dawla to expand in jihad; he heard part of his diwan from al-Mutanabbi; born and died at Mayyafariqin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 83-84}

## In the preface to the Diwan al-Khutab (Beirut, 1319 AH)
- The preface to [[work/diwan-khutab-ibn-nubata|the Diwan of his sermons]] gives his notice from Ibn Khallikan. He was a master of the literary sciences, from Mayyafariqin, and preacher of Aleppo. There he met Abu al-Tayyib [[person/al-mutanabbi|al-Mutanabbi]] in the service of [[person/sayf-al-dawla|Sayf al-Dawla ibn Hamdan]], and he is said to have heard part of al-Mutanabbi's diwan from him. Because Sayf al-Dawla campaigned often, the preacher composed many sermons on jihad to urge people to it; he was a righteous man. {cite:diwan-khutab-ibn-nubata-beirut-1319 front 14}
- It gives the story of "the Dream Sermon", in Taj al-Din al-Kindi's report with his chain: he saw the Prophet in a dream among the graves outside Mayyafariqin, and the Prophet called him "preacher of preachers". The story is given as a report. {cite:diwan-khutab-ibn-nubata-beirut-1319 front 14-15}
- The commentator says he found no historian who gave his birth and death except Ibn al-Azraq al-Fariqi in his history. According to Ibn al-Azraq he was born in 335 AH and died in 374 AH at Mayyafariqin, where he was buried, so he lived thirty-nine years. He composed some sermons at sixteen, those he delivered in 351 AH. {cite:diwan-khutab-ibn-nubata-beirut-1319 front 15}
- He quotes al-Baha' al-'Amili that al-Sharif al-Radi and his brother al-Murtada studied with him as boys. {cite:diwan-khutab-ibn-nubata-beirut-1319 front 16}
