## Summary
Abu Yahya Ibn Nabbata, the khatib of khatibs 'Abd al-Rahim ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Nabbata al-Fariqi al-Lakhmi — 'Asqalan-born, Egyptian by domicile; he held the khitaba of Aleppo for Sayf al-Dawla, his sermons showing his power of knowledge, breadth and ready talent, and all agree none ever made sermons like his; it was he whose sermons spurred Sayf al-Dawla to expand in jihad; he heard part of his diwan from al-Mutanabbi; born and died at Mayyafariqin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 83-84}
