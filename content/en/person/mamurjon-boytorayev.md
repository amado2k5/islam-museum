## Overview

Mamurjon Boyto'rayev is an Uzbek professional chef who introduces himself in his channel's About text, in Uzbek, in these words: "Peace be upon you. I am Mamurjon Boyto'rayev, born in 1993 in Namangan. My profession is chef-cook... and I love my work very much"{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}. He runs the HALAL FOOD channel on YouTube, presenting dishes of Uzbek cuisine from chaikhana and street-food kitchens{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}.

## Career and work

The channel's videos centre on Uzbek dishes cooked in large quantities and outdoors: plov in hundreds of kilograms, qozon kabob in the chaikhana style, giant somsa, and varieties of shashlik and kebab{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}. Some of his videos have been viewed millions of times, among them a cooked-tongue dish passing 3.7 million views and another dish passing 2.8 million{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}. The channel's keywords include "oshpaz" (cook) and "Mr oshpaz"{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}. The channel had about 221,000 subscribers in a platform count taken in October 2026; platform figures change constantly{cite:source/youtube-halal-food-uzb}.
