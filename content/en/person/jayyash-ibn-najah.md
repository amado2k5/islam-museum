## Overview
Jayyash ibn Najah, known by the kunya Ibn al-Qati, the third of the Banu Najah rulers of Zabid. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The killing of al-Sulayhi
He went to the island of Dahlak with his brother Sa'id to learn the Qur'an and literature. Then they broke with the troops and followed Ali al-Sulayhi, and they ambushed him at al-Lijam as he was heading for Mecca; Jayyash undertook to kill him with his own hand in 473 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
He fled to Aden and then to India, until he returned and seized [[polity/najahid-dynasty|Zabid]]. He had the sermon read for the Abbasids, while the Sulayhids had it read for the Fatimids. Ibn Khaldun describes him as just. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He died at the turn of the fifth century AH, and was succeeded by his son al-Fatik. The date of death is approximate and derived, and needs an additional source. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
