## Overview
"al-Husayn ibn Musa ibn Sulayman" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen.  He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
