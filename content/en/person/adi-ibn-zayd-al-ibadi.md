## Overview
Adi ibn Zayd al-Ibadi, a Christian poet from al-Hira. Al-Mas'udi relates a well-known ode of his on Shapur's killing of al-Nadira daughter of al-Dayzan, and the treachery she showed to her father and her people, and how she guided Shapur into the fortress. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.553-554}

The opening line of the ode in al-Mas'udi is: "The Hadr was struck by a calamity." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.553-554}
