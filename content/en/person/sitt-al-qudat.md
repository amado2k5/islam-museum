## Summary
Sitt al-Qudat bint 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Kathir, daughter of the hafiz 'Imad al-Din's brother, transmitted by ijaza from al-Qasim ibn 'Asakir and other Syrian shaykhs and from 'Ali al-'Alwani and other Egyptian shaykhs; Salah al-Din compiled forty hadiths for her on her own teachers' authority. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 15}
