## Overview
Al-Ashraf Mumahhid al-Din Umar ibn [[person/muzaffar-yusuf-rasulid|al-Muzaffar Yusuf]], the fourth ruler of the [[polity/rasulid-dynasty|Rasulids]] in Yemen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
He succeeded his father in 694 AH. His brother Dawud was governor of al-Shihr, and he called himself ruler and disputed the affair with him. Al-Ashraf sent his armies, who defeated him and imprisoned him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death
He continued in his rule until his concubine poisoned him, and he died in 696 AH, twenty months after he took power. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
