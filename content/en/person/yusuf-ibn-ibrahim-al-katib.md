## Overview
Yusuf ibn Ibrahim al-Katib, companion of Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi. He wrote his book on the reports of physicians with kings concerning food, drink and clothing. A book by Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi on kinds of reports is mentioned with it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.424}.
