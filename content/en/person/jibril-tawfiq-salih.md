## Overview
Jibril Tawfiq Salih, one of the prominent poets of Sudan, whose poetry was linked to the movement of political struggle. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His struggle
He was among the militants of the National Unionist movement that led the uprising of 1924 against colonial rule. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born around 1315 AH (1897 CE) and died in 1386 AH (1966 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
