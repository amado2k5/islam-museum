## Overview
Al-Qadir billah Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Ishaq ibn al-Muqtadir, an Abbasid caliph from 381 to 422 AH. He was born in 336 AH, and his mother was a slave-girl called Tamanna, or it is said Damana. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 634}

## The allegiance
He was given the allegiance after the deposition of al-Ta'i, while he was absent. He arrived on the tenth of Ramadan, and sat for a general audience the next day, when he was congratulated, and al-Sharif al-Radi recited poetry before him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 634}

## His conduct
Al-Suyuti relates from al-Khatib that he was known for piety, constant night prayer and frequent charity. He studied jurisprudence under Abu Bishr al-Harawi al-Shafi'i, and he wrote a book on the principles in which he mentioned the virtues of the Companions and the declaring of the Mu'tazilis and those who said the Qur'an was created to be unbelievers. He used to read it every Friday in the circle of the people of hadith in the Mosque of al-Mahdi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 634}

In Shawwal of the year of his rule he held a great session, and he and Baha al-Dawla each swore to the other to keep faith. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 634}

## Al-Sharif al-Radi
Ibn al-Athir says that the verses of al-Sharif al-Radi on proving the lineage of the Egyptian Mahdi reached him, so he summoned the judge Abu Bakr ibn al-Baqillani, and sent him to the sharif Abu Ahmad al-Musawi, father of al-Radi, to ask for an apology for them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 19}

## His death
He died on the night of Monday, the eleventh of Dhu al-Hijja 422 AH, aged eighty-seven. His caliphate lasted forty-one years and three months. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 639}
