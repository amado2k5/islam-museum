## Summary
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Salama ibn Ubaydallah ibn Abdallah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Qurashi al-Umari al-Madani, was one of the descendants of Umar ibn al-Khattab. He settled in Baghdad and related hadith there from Ibrahim ibn Sa'd al-Zuhri, Abu Uways Abdallah ibn Abdallah and Muhammad ibn Awn, client of Umm Hakim. Muhammad ibn Ishaq al-Saghani, Abu Zur'a al-Razi, Musa ibn Ishaq al-Ansari, Ismail ibn al-Fadl al-Malkhi and Musa ibn Harun al-Hafiz transmitted from him. Al-Bukhari mentioned him among those who lived in Baghdad. When al-Daraqutni was asked about him, he said: there is nothing wrong with him. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.9 p.447-448}
