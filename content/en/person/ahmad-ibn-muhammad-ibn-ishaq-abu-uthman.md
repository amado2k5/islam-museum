## Summary
Abu Uthman Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Yazid ibn Ajlan, whose worship and asceticism were joined to generosity and giving; he was the son-in-law of Ibn Raja' ibn Suhayb. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 677, sequence 678, on page 396. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## His worship and generosity
His worship was well known, his generosity was reported, and he transmitted much hadith. The notice names him as son-in-law of Raja' ibn Suhayb; the scan prints the father's name as "Sabib" in the body, and the reading "Suhayb" as in Raja's own notice is preferred here, flagged as not confirmed. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## A report on a house of hunger
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Yazid al-Zuhri relates from Abu Isa al-Asmai, from Abu Talha, from Abu al-Rijal, from Amra, from Aisha, a report from the Prophet about a house whose people are hungry; the wording is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}
