## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah, Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Shafi'i. Abu al-Hasan al-Razi called him learned and excellent. He had debates with al-Muzani, married Zaynab, daughter of al-Shafi'i, and studied with his father and transmitted much from al-Shafi'i. Death 295 H as printed. The son born of that marriage is a separate entry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13346}
