## Summary
Khattab al-Abid turned away from sins and pursued the ease of the next life. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 505, sequence 506, on page 144. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}

## The trace of a sin
Mukhallad relates from Khattab al-Abid: a servant may commit a sin between himself and God, and then his brothers meet him and see the trace of it upon him. A word in the report is unclear in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144}
