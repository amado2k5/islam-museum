## Summary
[[person/yahya-al-mutali-al-hammudi|Yahya al-Mu'tali al-Hammudi]] — Yahya al-Mu'tali ibn Idris, the third Hammudid caliph in Cordoba; acclaimed twice (1021–1023 and 1025–1026) amid the fitna of Cordoba, then abdicated and ruled Malaga until 1035. {cite:bayan-mughrib-idhari} {cite:bayan-mughrib-idhari}.
