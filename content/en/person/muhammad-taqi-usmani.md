## Summary
Muhammad Taqi Usmani is among the most prominent living Sunni scholars, son of Mufti Muhammad Shafi of Pakistan, combining jurisprudence, hadith, the judiciary and the organisation of Islamic economics. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-usmani}

## Life and upbringing
Born in 1943 in Deoband, India, he migrated with his family to Pakistan and graduated from Darul Uloom Karachi, receiving isnad in the hadith books and completing specialisation in ifta under his father. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-usmani}

## Career and works
He taught hadith and fiqh at Darul Uloom Karachi for decades, served as judge of the Federal Shariat Court and as a member of the Shariat Appellate Bench of the Supreme Court of Pakistan. He presides over Wifaq ul Madaris al-Arabia and shaped AAOIFI’s accounting and auditing standards for Islamic financial institutions. His books include Takmilat Fath al-Mulhim on Sahih Muslim and the Tawdih al-Qur’an commentary. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-usmani}

## Method and contribution
He joins Deobandi Hanafi affiliation to fatwas on modern questions: Islamic economics, medicine and technology. He is a world authority on Islamic finance, while some of his juristic or social positions draw criticism from different currents; the museum attributes these descriptions without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-taqi-usmani}
