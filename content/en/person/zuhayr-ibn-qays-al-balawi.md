## Overview
Zuhayr ibn Qays al-Balawi, whom Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan appointed over Ifriqiya. He conquered Tutas and then withdrew to Barqa. When he learned that a group of the Byzantines had come out of their ships and were spreading corruption, he went against them with a light cavalry, and he and those with him were killed as martyrs. They were buried there, and their graves are called the graves of the martyrs {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The modern account
al-Salabi reports that his martyrdom was in a battle with the Byzantines at Darna in eastern Libya in the year 71 AH, and that Abd al-Malik then sent an army under Hassan ibn al-Nu'man {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.58}. See [[event/zuhayr-martyrdom-barqa|the martyrdom of Zuhayr at Barqa]].
