## Overview
He is Abu Abd Allah al-Razi, a man the narrator calls virtuous and good, whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] left at [[place/jayshan-yemen|Jayshan]] when he moved on, with a charge to fear God and command the good and forbid the wrong {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. The narrator gives neither his name nor his descent nor what he did afterwards, and this is all the record holds of him. See [[event/al-hadi-jails-the-jaafir-at-mankath-and-sends-governors-from-jayshan-288|the jailing of the Ja'afir and the governors sent from Jayshan]].
