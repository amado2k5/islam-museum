## Summary
Muhammad ibn Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Mu'ti al-Abdusi, Abu Abd Allah son of Abu Imran, who studied with his father Abu Imran Musa al-Abdusi and others, and whose son Abd Allah and others studied with him. Some described him as a jurist, teacher, scholar and good man. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16640}
