## Overview
Kisra Aparwiz ibn Hurmuz — Khusraw II Parviz — a king of the Sasanian Persians; al-Tabari treats him under the heading 'Account of the king Kisra Aparwiz ibn Hurmuz', where he relates what is said of the years of his reign and his affairs. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl}
