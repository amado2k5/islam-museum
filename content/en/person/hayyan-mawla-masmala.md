## Overview
Hayyan, a client of Masmala, from the captives of the Daylam, known as Abu Ma'mar. Al-Baladhuri counts him among the noble and leading clients. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Yazid ibn al-Muhallab sent him to the Ispahbadh, and he deceived him until he made a settlement with Yazid for seven hundred thousand dirhams and four hundred qintars of saffron. Hayyan took on the surplus between the two weights of six and seven ([[event/hayyan-ispahbadh-treaty|the settlement of Hayyan and the Ispahbadh]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
