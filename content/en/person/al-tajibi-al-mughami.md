## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah al-Tajibi al-Mughami — with a sad, for Mughama in al-Andalus — the Toledan Muhammad b. 'Isa b. Farrah; muqri' of al-Andalus, he studied under Abu 'Amr al-Dani and Makki b. Abi Talib among others and taught the readings for a long period; he died in 485. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 376}
