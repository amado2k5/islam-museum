## Summary
Wadah Khanfar is a Palestinian journalist and media executive born in 1968, best known as the director general who transformed Al Jazeera into a global network; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-wadah-khanfar} {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Career
Born in Jenin and educated in engineering and international studies in Jordan, South Africa and the United States, he worked as an Al Jazeera correspondent in Iraq, Afghanistan and South Africa before moving into management. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Al Jazeera
As managing director then director general ⁦(2003–2011)⁩ he expanded the channel into a network — launching Al Jazeera English ⁦(2006)⁩, documentary, sports and online ventures — at the height of the Arab Spring, resigning in 2011. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Later work
He became chairman of the Al Sharq Forum think-tank in Istanbul, advises media organisations on digital transformation, and has taught and lectured internationally on journalism and political change. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Assessment
He is credited with turning a regional satellite channel into a global news organisation; commentary on Al Jazeera's politics and his management is reported by the museum attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar} {cite:muslim500-wadah-khanfar}


## Al Jazeera years
As managing director ⁦(2003-2011)⁩ he oversaw Al Jazeera's expansion — the launch of Al Jazeera English in 2006, new bureaux and the channel's peak influence during the Arab Spring — years press historians call the network's ascent. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## After Al Jazeera
He later directed Al Sharq Forum, built media and strategy consultancies, and wrote The First Spring: Arab Revolutions on the uprisings, plus memoir and commentary on Arab media politics. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Standing
He is regularly listed among the most influential figures in Arab and global media — including by Forbes and foreign-policy rankings — for the channel's role in reshaping the regional information order. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar} {cite:muslim500-wadah-khanfar}

## Origins
Born in Palestine in 1968 and educated in engineering and international politics, he worked as a journalist in South Africa and elsewhere before joining Al Jazeera at its founding and rising through its editorial ranks. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## The Arab Spring years
His leadership coincided with the 2010-11 uprisings, when Al Jazeera's coverage — live, rolling, pan-Arab — made the channel the defining broadcast institution of the period and him its public face. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}

## Standing
Press histories describe his tenure as the period when Al Jazeera became a global broadcaster rather than a regional one: the launch of English service, the global bureaux and the editorial scale that followed. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar} {cite:muslim500-wadah-khanfar}

## Al Jazeera's global ascent
Wadah Khanfar's eight years as managing director ⁦(2003-2011)⁩ coincided with Al Jazeera's transformation from a regional broadcaster into a global one: the launch of Al Jazeera English in 2006, new bureaux across the world, and the channel's peak influence during the 2010-11 Arab uprisings, when its live rolling coverage made it the defining broadcast institution of the period and him its public face. Press histories describe his tenure as the period when Al Jazeera became a global broadcaster. {cite:wikipedia-wadah-khanfar}
