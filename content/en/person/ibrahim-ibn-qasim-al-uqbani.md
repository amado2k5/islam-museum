## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Qasim ibn Sa'id ibn Muhammad, Abu Salim al-Uqbani al-Tilimsani, a Maliki jurist, judge, mufti and memoriser, who held the judgeship of Tlemcen. Ahmad al-Wansharisi studied with him, and al-Mazari quoted his responsa. The book gives his death as 880 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7144}
