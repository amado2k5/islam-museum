# Munshi Raziuddin

## Summary

Munshi Raziuddin Ahmed Khan (1912 – 2003) was a Pakistani qawwal, classical musician and scholar of music of the well-known Qawwal Bachon ka Gharana of Delhi, and the father of the qawwals [[person/fareed-ayaz|Fareed Ayaz]] and Abu Muhammad. {cite:wikipedia-munshi-raziuddin-en} {cite:wikidata-munshi-raziuddin}

## Career

He first sang at the court of the Nizam of Hyderabad with his cousin, the qawwal Bahauddin Khan, and moved to Karachi after the fall of Hyderabad in 1948. In 1956 he formed a party with his cousins Bahauddin and Manzoor Ahmed Niazi that lasted until 1966, and then led his own party until his death in 2003. {cite:wikipedia-munshi-raziuddin-en}

He trained his sons Fareed Ayaz, Abu Muhammad, Ghulam Akram and Ali Akbar and several nephews, and was succeeded by Fareed Ayaz and Abu Muhammad. Karachi's qawwali heritage lives on in a small neighbourhood of Saddar known as 'Qawwali Gali', where a street is named after him. {cite:wikipedia-munshi-raziuddin-en}

## Honours

The President of Pakistan gave him the [[award/pride-of-performance|Pride of Performance]] in 1967 for his contribution to Sufi music. {cite:wikipedia-munshi-raziuddin-en}
