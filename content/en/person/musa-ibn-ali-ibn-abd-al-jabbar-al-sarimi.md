## Overview
Musa ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Jabbar al-Sarimi is a narrator to whom [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] told the report of the cloud that shaded [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at Madhab; he related that he had a small son who had not spoken, that when he had resolved to carry him to Mecca a letter from al-Hadi reached him, and that they took al-Hadi's seal ring, put it in water and gave it to the boy, who then spoke clearly. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} The book gives no lineage, home or date, and the book's index lists him only at p. 131. He is one of the narrators cited in the text; the museum neither confirms nor denies his account. See [[event/reports-of-a-sight-restored-and-a-tongue-loosed-at-najran-286]].
