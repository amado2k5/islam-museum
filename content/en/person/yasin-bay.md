## Overview
Yasiin Bey (born Dante Smith in 1973 in Brooklyn), known as Mos Def, is an American rapper and actor. He embraced Islam as a young man. His album "Black on Both Sides" (1999) is a classic of alternative hip-hop, and his lyrics often address Islam and social justice. {cite:wikidata}
