## Overview
Abu Mulayh ibn [[person/urwa-ibn-masud|Urwa ibn Mas'ud]] of the [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]]. He came with [[person/qarib-ibn-al-aswad|Qarib ibn al-Aswad]] before the Thaqif delegation, wishing to part from their people, and they both embraced Islam. The Prophet told them: "Take as your allies whom you wish." They said: "We take God and His Messenger as allies." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/186}

## His father's debt
When the Prophet ordered [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] to demolish [[place/taif|al-Lat]], Abu Mulayh asked that a debt his father Urwa owed be paid from the property of the idol. The Prophet said yes, and Abu Sufyan paid it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/187}
