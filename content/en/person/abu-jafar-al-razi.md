## Summary
Isa ibn Abi Isa Mahan, Abu Ja'far al-Razi, is described by [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] in [[work/mizan-al-itidal|Mizan al-I'tidal]] as fair in hadith. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 319} He narrated from [[person/amir-al-shabi|al-Sha'bi]], [[person/ata-ibn-abi-rabah|Ata ibn Abi Rabah]], [[person/qatada-ibn-diama|Qatada]] and others; he was born in [[place/basra|Basra]] and settled in [[place/rayy|Rayy]], and his son Abd Allah, Abu Nu'aym, Abu Ahmad al-Zubayri, Ali ibn al-Ja'd and others narrated from him. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 319}

## The critics' verdicts
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: declared trustworthy**: Ibn Ma'in said trustworthy; Abu Hatim said trustworthy and truthful. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 320}
- **Sunni: softened**: Ahmad and al-Nasa'i said not strong; Ibn al-Madini said trustworthy but he mixed things up, and once: his hadith may be written, though he errs; al-Fallas said poor memory; Abu Zur'a said he often errs; Ibn Hibban said he transmits objectionable reports alone from well-known narrators. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 320}
:::

## Criticised narrations
Al-Dhahabi notes that Hatim ibn Isma'il, Hashim Abu al-Nadr, Hajjaj ibn Muhammad and others relate from Abu Ja'far al-Razi from al-Rabi' ibn Anas from Abu al-Aliya from Abu Hurayra or another a long hadith on the Night Journey containing very objectionable wordings. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 320} He also cites his narration from al-Rabi' ibn Anas from [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas]] that the Prophet made qunut for a month invoking against certain people and then left it, but in the dawn prayer continued qunut until he left this world, which he attributes to al-Daraqutni. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 320} This hadith figures in the jurists' debate over qunut in the dawn prayer; here the book only records its source and chain.
