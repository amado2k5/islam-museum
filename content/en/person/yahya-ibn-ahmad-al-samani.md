## Overview
Abu Zakariyya Yahya ibn Ahmad ibn Isma'il al-Samani, son of the amir Ahmad ibn Isma'il. Ibn al-Athir reports that his brother Nasr ibn Ahmad imprisoned him in the citadel (al-Qahandiz) of Bukhara together with his two brothers, Abu Salih Mansur and Abu Ishaq Ibrahim. Abu Bakr al-Khabbaz al-Isfahani released them, and they rose against their brother in the year 318 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.155}.
