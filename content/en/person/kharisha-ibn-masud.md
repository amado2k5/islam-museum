## Overview
Kharisha ibn Mas'ud, of the Banu Tamim and later of the Banu Shaqra. He was with Ibn al-Ash'ath, and took refuge in a fortress at Fasa that is known by his name. He then sought shelter in Wasit and died there, and his descendants remained in Fasa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
