## Overview
Abu 'Abd al-Rahman Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i is a modern hadith scholar, presented here from what his introduction to the Dar al-Haramayn edition of the [[work/al-mustadrak-ala-al-sahihayn|*Mustadrak*]] tells us; the introduction is dated 4 Rabi' I 1414 AH. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 38}

## His work on the Mustadrak
He began recording his notes on the *Mustadrak* while at the Institute of the Meccan Haram, twenty years before writing the introduction, and later gathered them in [[work/al-tatabbu-li-ma-fi-al-mustadrak-min-awham|*al-Tatabbu' li-ma fi al-Mustadrak min awham*]]. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 5-6} In the introduction he mentions that he included the hadith "Whoever I am the patron of, 'Ali is his patron" in his book *al-Sahih al-Musnad mimma laysa fi al-Sahihayn*. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 14} He also notes that he traced the hadiths of al-Daraqutni's [[work/al-ilzamat-ala-sahih-al-bukhari|*al-Ilzamat*]] in his annotations on it. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 21}

## His approach in the introduction
In the introduction he gathers what the hadith imams said of al-Hakim, in praise and in criticism, adding his own comments on some of it, as when he calls Ibn Tahir's description of al-Hakim as a liar and a deceiver an exaggeration. {cite:mustadrak-haramayn-1997-v1 p. 15} See also [[question/tasahul-al-hakim-fi-al-mustadrak|how al-Hakim's authentications in the Mustadrak are to be treated]].
