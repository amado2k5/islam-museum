## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Isa, Abu Muhammad al-Tadili, a Maliki scholar named after Tadla in Morocco. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2056}

## Works
A learned, literary fatwa-giver who heard from the judge Iyad, held the judgeship of Fez, and left letters. He lived from 511 to 597 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2056}
