## Summary
İhsan Şenocak is a Turkish preacher, researcher and writer born on 5 July 1974 in Samsun, known for his lessons in tafsir, hadith, fiqh and aqida, and for founding the İFAM research centre (İlmi ve Fikri Araştırmalar Merkezi). {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

His YouTube channel counted 668,000 subscribers as of 15 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-ihsan-senocak}

## Early life and education
Şenocak was born in Samsun on 5 July 1974, and immediately after finishing primary school he became a hafiz and served in mosques. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

He completed his education in 1994 at Samsun Imam Hatip High School, then graduated in 1999 from Ondokuz Mayıs University's theology faculty in Samsun. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

He took his master's in 2004 in the same faculty's Islamic law department with the thesis "Taqlid in Islamic Law", and then his doctorate with the thesis "The Effect of Custom on Rulings in Islamic Law". {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

## Career and work
Şenocak held various posts in the Presidency of Religious Affairs and made television programmes on several channels. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

He founded the Centre for Scholarly and Intellectual Research, known by its short name İFAM, and began teaching tafsir, hadith, fiqh, aqida, usul and logic there. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

No founder edge from this record into the İFAM centre's own record is written here; the centre is catalogued separately as a group where built. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

Üsküdar University describes him as a researcher and author, and reports his address to its medical students on medicine, wisdom and ethics. {cite:uskudar-university-ihsan-senocak}

## Works
Among the books the article lists are Kudemâ Meclisi, İslâm'ın Kızına, Kur'an Müdafaası, Bin Yıldır Düşmeyen Cephemiz Doğu Türkistan, Müslüman Gence and İmam-ı Azam'ın İzinde, alongside Arabic books including al-Maqalat al-Nahdawiyya fi'l-Fikr wa'l-Da'wa. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}

His master's and doctoral theses themselves are listed among his notable works. {cite:wikipedia-ihsan-senocak-tr}
