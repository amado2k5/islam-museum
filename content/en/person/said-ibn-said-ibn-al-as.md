## Summary
An eminent Companion of Banu Umayya of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]]; brother of Khalid and Amr, sons of Sa'id ibn al-As; he emigrated to Abyssinia in the second emigration, witnessed the battles with the Prophet, and was martyred by an arrow of Thaqif in the [[event/siege-of-taif|siege of Ta'if]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/128-129}.

## His lineage and martyrdom
He is Sa'id ibn Sa'id ibn al-As ibn Umayya ibn Abd Shams; one of the first forerunners and of those who emigrated to the land of Abyssinia fleeing with their religion {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/129}. He took part in besieging Ta'if's fortress in Shawwal of year 8 AH, and an arrow of Thaqif's arrows struck him — he was martyred beneath the fortress wall {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/128-129}.

## For young visitors
A Companion of the first emigrants to Abyssinia; he stayed firm in his faith and witnessed the Muslims' battles until he won martyrdom at Ta'if's fortress.
