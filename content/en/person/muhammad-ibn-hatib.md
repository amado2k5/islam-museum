## Summary
Son of Hatib ibn al-Harith and Fatima bint al-Mujallal; he came with his mother and brother in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him son of Hatib ibn al-Harith alongside his brother al-Harith, saying their mother brought them in one of the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-jumah|Banu Jumah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text does not state the two sons were born in Abyssinia, nor does it place them among the five "born there" (p. 11).
