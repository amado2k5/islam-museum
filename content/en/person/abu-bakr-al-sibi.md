## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Yahya al-Sibi, a jurist. He was born in the palace of Ibn Hubayra in 296 AH, and died at the beginning of Rajab 372 AH, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}

## His life
He entered Baghdad after the Qarmati had burned the palace of Ibn Hubayra in 314 AH. He studied with [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]], then returned to his palace and spread the Shafi'i school there. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}

## His lineage
The editor's note says that his lineage appears in some sources as "al-Hasan" and "al-Sunni". The nisba "al-Sibi" is to al-Sayb in the districts of the palace of Ibn Hubayra. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}
