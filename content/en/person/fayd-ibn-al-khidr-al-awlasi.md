## Summary
Fayd b. al-Khidr, Abu'l-Harith al-Awlasi; he sang in his youth — then passed a sick man on the roadside who, asked what he craved, said 'pomegranates!' He fetched some, and the man said 'God grant you repentance!' — by evening he was changed from what he had been; he kept company with Ibrahim b. Sa'd al-Zawi, and died at Tarsus. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 205}
