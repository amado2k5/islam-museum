## Summary
Sayyid Ibraheemul Khaleel Al-Bukhari is a Kerala Sunni scholar, founder and chairman of the Ma'din Academy at Swalath Nagar, Malappuram — an educational movement that grew into dozens of institutions — and general secretary of the Kerala Muslim Jamaat, the umbrella body of the state's Muslim organisations. {cite:wikipedia-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari} {cite:muslim500-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari}

## Life and formation
He was born on 22 February 1964 in Kadalundi, Kozhikode district, into the Bukhari Thangal sayyid family of Malabar, which traces its line to Bukhara by way of Gujarat. He studied under Bheeran Koya Musliyar and graduated with second rank from the Baqiyathus Salihat Arabic College in Vellore in 1986. {cite:wikipedia-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari}

## Ma'din Academy
He founded Ma'din Academy in 1997; within two decades it administered more than thirty educational and charitable institutions serving over twenty thousand students, from schools to a women's Arabic college — with its annual Ramadan gathering billed by organisers as India's largest. {cite:wikipedia-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari} {cite:muslim500-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari}

## Roles and works
Joint secretary of Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama, general secretary of the Kerala Muslim Jamaat, vice-president of the Islamic Educational Board of India and adviser to international interfaith-harmony initiatives, he is listed in The Muslim 500 and has authored around two dozen books in Arabic, Malayalam and English, editing the interfaith journal Armonia. {cite:muslim500-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari}

## Assessments
Supporters describe a unifier of Kerala's Sunni institutions whose schools combine religious and modern education; observers note his sayyid lineage lends him authority across Kerala's factions. The museum records the assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ibraheemul-khaleel-al-bukhari}
