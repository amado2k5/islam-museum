## Summary
Ukaydir's brother; he rode out with him on the night Khalid took him, and the Prophet's cavalry killed him per Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "a brother of his called Hassan" who rode with Ukaydir — they went out with their hunting gear; when the Prophet's cavalry met them they took Ukaydir and killed his brother. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq gives his lineage: Ukaydir's brother. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text does not say who exactly killed him nor the circumstances of his death.
