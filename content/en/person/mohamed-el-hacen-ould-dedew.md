# Mohamed El Hacen Ould Dedew

**Muhammad al-Hasan ibn al-Dedew al-Shanqiti** is a Mauritanian Muslim scholar, author and poet. He was born on 31 October 1963 in Boutilimit, Mauritania, and raised in a scholarly family: his grandfather Muhammad Ali bin Abd al-Wadud was his first tutor, he began memorising the Qur'an at five and completed it before ten, studied the ten readings of the Qur'an with his mother, and went on to earn licenses authorising him to teach the major collections of hadith {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-el-hacen-ould-dedew-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-el-hacen-ould-dedew-fr}.

He presides over the Scholars' Information Center and over Abdallah ibn Yasin University in Nouakchott, and heads the country's Center for the Development of Scholars. In 2014 he was vice-president of the International Union of Muslim Scholars {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-el-hacen-ould-dedew-en}.

In 2007 he founded the Centre for the Formation of Scholars, which the Mauritanian authorities closed in 2018. Under President Maaouiya Ould Sid'Ahmed Taya he was imprisoned repeatedly {cite:wikipedia-mohamed-el-hacen-ould-dedew-fr}.
