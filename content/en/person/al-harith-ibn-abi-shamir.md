## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Abi Shamir al-Ghassani, titled "al-A'raj", king of [[group/ghassan|Ghassan]] in Syria. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.385}

## His affairs
He raided the Banu Taghlib after Amr ibn Kulthum returned his threat, and he was defeated, and his brother was killed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.385}

Al-Samaw'al ibn Adiya wrote to him to bring Imru' al-Qays ibn Hujr to Caesar, asking his help for vengeance for his father. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}
