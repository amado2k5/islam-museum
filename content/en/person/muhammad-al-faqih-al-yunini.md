## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abd Allah, Abu Abd Allah, Taqi al-Din al-Yunini, was a Hanbali hadith hafiz; al-Zirikli says he was of the line of Ja'far al-Sadiq. He gives his years as 572 AH (1176 CE) to 658 AH (1260 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 322}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he was born at Yunin, became well known, and died in Baalbek {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 322}. He was close to the kings of his time, such as al-Ashraf and al-Kamil, and there are reports of him with them and with others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 322}. He was the father of the historian Qutb al-Din Musa {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 322}.
