## Overview
Aqsunqur, Qasim al-Dawla, the amir of Sultan Malik Shah over Aleppo, and the grandfather of Nur al-Din al-Adil. He gave obedience after the death of Malik Shah, because his sons were young. Then [[person/tutush|Tutush]] took the rule of Aleppo, and his men grew strong, and Tutush met him a few leagues from Aleppo. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Aqsunqur was defeated and brought as a captive, and Tutush killed him in cold blood. His companions avenged him by killing Tutush at al-Rayy in 488 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Notes for researchers
- The estimated year of his death is about 480 AH, and it is disputed; it needs an additional source.
