## Overview
Muhammad ibn Musa ibn Hafs ibn Umar ibn al-Ala'. Together with [[person/mazyar-ibn-qarin|Mazyar ibn Qarin]] he took the mountains of Shirwan in Tabaristan in the caliphate of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. The source describes these mountains as the strongest mountains of Tabaristan, the most difficult, with the most thickets and groves. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data record neither his birth nor his death, and need an additional source.
