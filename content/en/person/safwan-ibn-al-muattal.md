Abu Amr Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal ibn Rabi'a al-Sulami al-Dhakwani, an eminent Companion and brave Badri knight, described as sound, chaste and abstinent from women. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Prophet used to set him at the army's rear to gather dropped gear; in the Banu al-Mustaliq expedition he found Umm al-Mu'minin A'isha after the army had departed, invoked God's name, brought his mount near her and walked leading it — saying nothing but that invocation — until he delivered her to the army. God cleared them both in the mighty Quran of the hypocrites' slander and praised their truthfulness. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

He witnessed the Islamic conquests and bore a great share in them, and was martyred in God's path in the conquests of Armenia or in the land of Byzantium, in the caliphate of Umar ibn al-Khattab. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
