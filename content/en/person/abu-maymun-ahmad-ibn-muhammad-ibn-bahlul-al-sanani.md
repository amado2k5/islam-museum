## Overview
The narrator Ali ibn Muhammad reports hearing "Abu Maymun Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Bahlul al-San'ani", whom he calls one of the learned men of Sana'a, say that the people do not know the full worth of Abu al-Qasim on that day and that nothing revived Islam but Abu al-Qasim {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.237} ([[event/al-hadi-is-struck-down-in-the-narrow-pass-and-abu-al-qasim-defends-him-289]]). The saying is praise by the man quoted, and the museum only reports it with attribution. The book gives no date of birth or death for him here, nor his school.
