## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ka'b ibn Malik al-Ansari al-Sulami, son of the famous poet Ka'b ibn Malik; his kunya was Abu al-Khattab. Al-Ja'abi and al-Askari said he was born in the Prophet's lifetime. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.823}

## His transmission
He transmitted from his father, his brother Abdallah, Jabir ibn Abdallah al-Akwa', Abu Qatada and A'isha; Abu Umama ibn Sahl — a peer of his, though older — al-Zuhri, Sa'id ibn Ibrahim and Abu Amir al-Khazzaz transmitted from him. Ibn Sa'd said: he was reliable, and more copious in hadith than his brother. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.823}

## His death
Al-Haytham ibn Adi, Khalifa and Ya'qub ibn Sufyan said he died in the caliphate of Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.823}
