## Summary
"The Ritter Joseph von Hammer", as the title page calls him, with the letters of membership of the Royal Asiatic Society, translated [[person/evliya-celebi|Evliya Çelebi]]'s travels from Turkish into English; the translation was published for the Oriental Translation Fund of Great Britain and Ireland in London, volume one in 1834 and volume two in 1850. {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v1 p.5} {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v2 p.5}

## His work on the translation
The publisher's advertisement says Hammer "abridged and translated" the work, that his name is a sufficient voucher for its merit and the accuracy of the version, and that oriental words are spelt on the system of Sir William Jones and Sir Charles Wilkins. {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v1 p.7} Hammer prefaced volume one with a "Biographical sketch of the author" drawn from Evliya's own book: his birth, family, dream and journeys; in it he judged that four volumes of the work were extant and that Evliya never wrote a continuation. {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v1 p.9} {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v1 p.18} He notes that his manuscript copy contains no account of Evliya's pilgrimage. {cite:evliya-narrative-hammer-v1 p.16} See [[work/seyahatname|the Seyahatname]].
