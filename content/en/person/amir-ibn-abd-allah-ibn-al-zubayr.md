## Overview
Amir ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, Abu al-Harith al-Asadi of Medina, is called by al-Dhahabi the devoted imam and one of the worshippers {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2111}.

## Teachers and students
He heard from his father and from Amr ibn Sulaym {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2111}. Abu Sakhra Jami', Ibn Ajlan, Abd Allah ibn Sa'id ibn Abi Hind, Ibn Jurayj, Malik and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2111}.

## His devotion
Ahmad ibn Hanbal relates from Sufyan that Amir bought himself back from God six times, meaning that each time he gave his blood-price in charity {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}. Al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar says his father, seeing this in him, used to say that he had seen Abu Bakr and Umar and they were not like this {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}. Malik said he fasted three days without breaking {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}. Mus'ab relates that he heard the muezzin while at death's door and asked to be led by the hand to the mosque; he prayed one rak'a of the evening prayer behind the imam and died {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}. Al-Qa'nabi reports from Malik that he would stand where funerals were held, praying, in a woollen wrap that fell from him without his noticing; and Ma'n reports from Malik that after the night prayer he would sometimes be moved to supplication and go on until dawn {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}.

## What the critics said
Al-Dhahabi says his reliability is agreed upon {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}.

## His death
He died after the year 120 AH, in "a few and twenty and a hundred" in al-Dhahabi's words; he had several brothers, Khubayb, Muhammad, Ayyub, Hashim, Hamza, Abbad and Thabit {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص2112}.
