## Overview
Yazid ibn Ubayd al-Sulami al-Sa'di, Abu Wajza, was a poet, hadith transmitter and Qur'an reciter, one of the Tabi'un. His origin was in Banu Sulaym; he grew up among Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr ibn Hawazin and was therefore attributed to them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 179}. He lived in Medina, attached himself to the family of al-Zubayr, and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 179}. The heading gives his death as 130 AH, 747 CE; the entry gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 179}.
