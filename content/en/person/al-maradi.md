## Overview
Muhammad Khalil ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad Murad al-Husayni, Abu al-Fadl, known as al-Muradi, was the historian, mufti of Damascus and marshal of its ashraf, of Bukharan origin. Born and raised in Damascus, he held the Hanafi fatwa office from 1192 AH and the niqaba of the ashraf from 1200 AH; events in 1205 AH forced his move to Aleppo, where he died in 1206 AH (1791 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.117}

His best-known book is Silk al-Durar fi A'yan al-Qarn al-Thani 'Ashar in four parts; he also wrote 'Arf al-Bisham on those who held the fatwa of Damascus from Sultan Selim's days, Matmah al-Wajid on his father, Ithaf al-Akhlaf bi-Awsaf al-Aslaf and Tuhfat al-Dahr. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.117}
