# Wahbi Khazri

## Summary

Wahbi Khazri is a former Tunisian professional footballer, born in Ajaccio, France, on 8 February 1991. An attacking midfielder who played in France and England, he captained Tunisia and won 74 caps, and after retiring moved into television analysis. {cite:wikipedia-wahbi-khazri-en} {cite:wikipedia-wahbi-khazri-ar} {cite:wikidata-wahbi-khazri}

## Career

Trained at Bastia from 2004, Khazri debuted there in 2009 and helped the club climb to Ligue 2 in 2011 and Ligue 1 in 2012. He joined Bordeaux in 2014, moved to Sunderland in the Premier League in 2016, and after a loan season at Rennes — eleven goals in 29 games — signed for Saint-Étienne in 2018 and Montpellier in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-wahbi-khazri-en}

Born in France and a French youth international, he chose Tunisia at senior level, playing from 2013 to 2022 with 74 caps and 25 goals, as the team's former captain, at five Africa Cup of Nations tournaments and the 2018 and 2022 World Cups. {cite:wikipedia-wahbi-khazri-en} {cite:wikipedia-wahbi-khazri-ar}
