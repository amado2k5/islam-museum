## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ali ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi al-Tayyib al-Bakharzi, a man of letters and poet from Bakharz in Khurasan. He studied Shafi'i jurisprudence, and wrote on the biographies of the men of letters and poets of his age. He died in 467 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.49}
