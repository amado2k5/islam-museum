## Summary
[[person/jabir-ibn-samura|Jabir ibn Samura]] ibn Junada al-Amiri al-Suwa'i, an ally of Banu Zuhra. His mother is Khalida bint Abi Waqqas, sister of [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. Both he and his father had companionship, and the authors of the Sahihs narrate from him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 194}.

## What he related of sitting with the Prophet
Sharik relates from Simak from Jabir, who said: "I sat with the Prophet more than a hundred times" — narrated by al-Tabarani. In the Sahih from him: "I prayed with the Prophet more than two thousand times" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 194}.

## His kunya, residence and death
Ibn al-Sakan said: his kunya is Abu Abdullah, or it is said Abu Khalid. He settled in [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and built a house there, and died in Bishr's governorship over Iraq in 74 AH; Salama ibn Junada related from his father that Amr ibn Hurayth prayed over him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 194}.
