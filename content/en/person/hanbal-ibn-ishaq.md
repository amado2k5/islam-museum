## Overview
Hanbal ibn Ishaq ibn Hanbal ibn Hilal al-Shaybani, Abu Ali, was one of the memorisers of hadith and was trustworthy; he was Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal's cousin and pupil. He died in 273 AH (886 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.285}

He wrote a Kitab al-Tarikh, a Kitab al-Fitan and a Kitab Mihnat al-Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal. He set out for Wasit and died there. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.285}
