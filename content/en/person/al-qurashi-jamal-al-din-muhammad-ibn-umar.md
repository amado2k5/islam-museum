## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Umar al-Qurashi, a scholar of the Sunnis. He was born in 628 AH (1231 CE) and died in 702 AH (1303 CE), and his mother was from Merv. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His works, his teachers and the other details of his life are not recorded in the data and need an additional source. The years of birth and death are recorded as commonly reported.
