# Édouard Mendy

## Summary

Édouard Osoque Mendy is a Senegalese professional goalkeeper, born in Montivilliers, France, on 1 March 1992.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-ar}
He plays for Saudi Pro League club Al-Ahli and for the Senegal national team.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
Released by Cherbourg in 2014 and without a club for a year, he rebuilt his career to become, at Chelsea, the first African goalkeeper to win both the UEFA Goalkeeper of the Year and The Best FIFA Goalkeeper awards.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}

## Early life and career beginnings

Mendy was born to a Senegalese mother and a Bissau-Guinean father, and is a cousin of the footballer Ferland Mendy.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
He joined Le Havre's academy at thirteen, dropped down to CS Municipaux Le Havre, and began his professional career at third-tier Cherbourg.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
Released in 2014, he spent a year without a club and, at twenty-two, registered for unemployment and looked for jobs outside football, before Marseille signed him in 2015 as a reserve goalkeeper.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}

## Club career

At Reims, Mendy established himself as first choice and helped the club win the 2017–18 Ligue 2 title, keeping eighteen clean sheets that season.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
He joined Rennes in August 2019 and Chelsea in September 2020 for a reported £22 million, becoming the first African goalkeeper to play for Chelsea's senior team.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
In his first Chelsea season he equalled the record of nine clean sheets in a Champions League campaign and kept a clean sheet in the final as Chelsea won the title; he was named UEFA Goalkeeper of the Year and The Best FIFA Goalkeeper for 2021.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
After falling out of favour, he signed for Al-Ahli in June 2023 and was the best goalkeeper of the competition as the club won the AFC Champions League Elite in 2025.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}

## International career

Mendy was called up by Guinea-Bissau in November 2016, when his father was gravely ill, but he rejected the call and pledged his future to Senegal, making his debut on 18 November 2018.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-ar}
He was Senegal's first-choice goalkeeper at the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations, where Senegal finished runners-up, and at the 2021 tournament, which Senegal won and at which he was named the best goalkeeper.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
He played at the 2022 World Cup and won a second Africa Cup of Nations title with Senegal in 2025.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
He was appointed a Grand Officer of the National Order of the Lion after the 2021 title.{cite:wikipedia-edouard-mendy-en}
