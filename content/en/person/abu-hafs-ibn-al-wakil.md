## Overview
Abu Hafs ibn al-Wakil al-Bab al-Shami, a Sunni jurist. He died in Baghdad after 310 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}

## His nisba
The nisba "al-Bab al-Shami" refers to one of the quarters on the western side of Baghdad, according to the editor's note. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}
