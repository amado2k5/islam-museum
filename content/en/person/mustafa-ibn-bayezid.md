## Summary
Prince Mustafa, son of Bayezid I — presumed dead on the field of Ankara in 804, he resurfaced under his nephew Murad II leading a rising the book says the empire's enemies financed: emperor Manuel II backed him until he besieged Gallipoli to make it his base, whereupon Murad captured and hanged him {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 70, 79}.

Ottoman memory also knows this figure as 'Düzme Mustafa', the false Mustafa — the sources dispute whether he was the genuine prince; we record the book's account as it stands.
