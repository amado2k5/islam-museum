## Summary
Malik al-Muhaddithin of Gujarat. The *Nur al-safir* says of its subject: he had firm footing in the science of hadith and beyond, taught and gave fatwas. He was martyred on Wednesday, 6 Shawwal, at seventy-three, and followed the Mahdawi path. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 414}
