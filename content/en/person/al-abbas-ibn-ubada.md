Al-Abbas ibn Ubada ibn Nadla al-Ansari al-Khazraji, one of the first Muslims of Yathrib who witnessed the first and second Aqaba pledges near Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He rose on the night of the second Aqaba pledge to make the covenant plain to his people: "men of Khazraj, do you know on what you are pledging this man? You are pledging him war on the red and the black of mankind. If you think that when your wealth is stricken and your nobles killed you would abandon him — then from now, for by God if you do it, it is the disgrace of this world and the next; but if you think you will keep faith with him for what you have called him to, then take him, for by God he is the good of this world and the next." They said: we take him for the ruin of wealth and the killing of nobles — what is ours for that, Messenger of God, if we keep faith? He said: "Paradise." They said: extend your hand; he extended it and they pledged. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When Satan cried out after the pledge, al-Abbas told the Prophet: "by Him who sent you with the truth — if you wish we will fall on the people of Mina tomorrow with our swords." The Prophet said: "we have not been commanded to that; return to your mounts." Al-Abbas was martyred on the day of Uhud, advancing, not turning back. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
