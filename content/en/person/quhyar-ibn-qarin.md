## Overview
Quhyar ibn Qarin, brother of [[person/mazyar-ibn-qarin|Mazyar]]. He corresponded with al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn and Muhammad ibn Ibrahim and told them that he was with them against his brother, out of a grudge he held because of contempt he had shown him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He showed them the trick of the ambush, and took hold of his brother's territory until he took a ladder. Some of his brother's close men then leapt upon him and he was killed in Tabaristan. See [[event/mazyar-revolt|the revolt of Mazyar]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
