## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Nadr ibn Abd al-Wahhab al-Naysaburi, Abu al-Fadl, is called by al-Dhahabi the skilled memoriser, the great scholar, one of the imams and authors {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}. The sign printed with his entry shows that al-Bukhari recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}.

## His link with al-Bukhari
Al-Hakim said that when Abu Abd Allah al-Bukhari came to Nishapur he lodged with the two brothers Ahmad and Muhammad, sons of al-Nadr, and narrated from both in his Sahih {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}. When al-Bukhari narrated the story of the slander from Abu al-Rabi' al-Zahrani he said that Ahmad had confirmed him in part of it, and al-Dhahabi explains that this Ahmad is Ibn al-Nadr, not Ibn Hanbal {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}. Where al-Bukhari says Muhammad narrated to us from Ubayd Allah ibn Mu'adh, that is Muhammad ibn al-Nadr, who died early, while Ahmad lived long {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}.

## Teachers and students
He heard Hudba ibn Khalid, Shayban ibn Farrukh, Sahl ibn Uthman al-Askari, Abu Mus'ab al-Zuhri, Ishaq ibn Rahawayh, Ubayd Allah ibn Mu'adh, Amr ibn Zurara and many others named by al-Hakim, who said Ahmad was excellent among the Basrans {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}. Al-Bukhari, Abu Hamid ibn al-Sharqi, Abu Abd Allah ibn al-Akhram, Ahmad ibn Ishaq al-Saydalani, Muhammad ibn Salih ibn Hani', Abu al-Fadl Muhammad ibn Ibrahim and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}.

## His age
He lived on to some years past 280 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1027}.

## Note
He is to be told apart from the Ahmad ibn al-Nadr recorded in the museum as an Omani poet, who is a different person.
