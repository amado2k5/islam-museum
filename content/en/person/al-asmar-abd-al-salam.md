## Overview
Abd al-Salam al-Asmar al-Fitouri, a Libyan Sufi shaykh from Tripoli, whose fame spread through the retreat that bears his name. The source classes him as a saint. His birth and death dates are not recorded in the museum's data and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
