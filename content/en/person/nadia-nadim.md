## Summary
Nadia Nadim is a Danish-Afghan professional footballer and qualified doctor born in Herat, Afghanistan, in 1988 — one of the most decorated strikers in Danish women's football; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential figures in sport. {cite:muslim500-nadia-nadim} {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Refugee story
Her father, an Afghan general, was killed when she was a child; the family fled through Pakistan to Denmark, where she took up football and rose through Danish club football while studying medicine — a dual career maintained throughout her playing life. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Club career
She has played for Sky Blue FC, Manchester City, Paris Saint-Germain, Racing Louisville, AC Milan and Gotham FC, winning league titles in Denmark and Italy and regularly ranking among her clubs' top scorers. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Denmark and beyond
With more than 100 caps for Denmark she reached the Euro 2017 final; she qualified as a physician in 2022 while still playing, serves as a UNESCO ambassador for education, and is a broadcast pundit and commentator. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Assessment
She is celebrated as a rare figure combining elite sport and medicine, and as a prominent refugee-success story; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim} {cite:muslim500-nadia-nadim}


## Medical career
She qualified as a reconstructive surgeon while playing professionally — studying through club seasons and international duty — a dual trajectory that Danish and international press regularly highlight as exceptional. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Refugee story
Her account of fleeing Taliban-era Afghanistan — her father killed, the family's escape to Denmark — and becoming a national-team player is cited in refugee-representation discussions across media and sport. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## Broadcasting
Post-playing she works as a pundit — including coverage of major international tournaments for British broadcasters — extending her public profile beyond football. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim} {cite:muslim500-nadia-nadim}

## Origins
Born in Herat in 1988, she fled Afghanistan with her family after her father was killed under the Taliban — a refugee journey to Denmark that press coverage describes as the foundation of her remarkable dual career. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## The dual career
Her simultaneous pursuit of professional football and medical qualification — playing for major clubs and Denmark's national team while qualifying as a reconstructive surgeon — made her a global case study in combining elite sport and professional education. {cite:wikipedia-nadia-nadim}

## The refugee story
