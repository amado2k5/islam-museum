## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim ibn Bashshar al-Anbari, a philologist, grammarian and transmitter of poetry, who grew up in a family of learning in al-Anbar. He took from his father the sciences of hadith, reports and language. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.404-405}

## Dates
He was born in 271 AH (884 CE) and died in 328 AH (940 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.404-405}
