## Overview

Halal Trainer is a Pokémon-card vendor and collector who publishes on YouTube under the channel name only; the channel's entire About text is the line "Tryna catch'em all the halal way while we stay locked in"{cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}. Platform data records that the channel started on 20 December 2017 and is based in Canada{cite:source/vidiq-halal-trainer}. The channel's operator does not publish a legal name in any available source, so this record carries the published name{cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}{cite:source/vidiq-halal-trainer}.

## Career and work

The channel's videos centre on Pokémon cards: opening card products in search of rare pulls, and filming the operator's deals as a vendor from the operator's own point of view at card shows{cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}. The videos travel to a card show in Saudi Arabia, where a video title records a card sold to a Saudi millionaire, and to Collect-A-Con, where another title records Logan Paul buying all the channel's Bubble Mew cards{cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}. The video descriptions carry the business email and the Instagram and TikTok accounts (@halaltrainer){cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}. The channel had about 173,000 subscribers in a platform count taken in October 2026; platform figures change constantly{cite:source/youtube-halal-trainer}.
