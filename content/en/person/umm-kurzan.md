## Overview
Umm Kurzan, a woman of Bajila {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The quarter of al-Sawad
al-Baladhuri reports that her people returned a quarter of al-Sawad. She said to Jarir that her father had died and his share was fixed in al-Sawad, and that she would not submit {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Jarir told her that her people had agreed. She said: "I will not become a Muslim until you carry me on a docile she-camel with a red cloak over her, and fill my hands with gold." He did so {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
