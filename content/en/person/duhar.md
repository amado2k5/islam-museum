## Overview
Duhar ibn Dahir, whose story is recorded by al-Baladhuri in "The Conquest of Sind". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Kirij
He went out to Muhammad ibn al-Qasim at al-Kirij and fought him. His army was routed and he fled. It is said that he was killed. The people of the city came down under Muhammad's rule, and he put them to death and took them captive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
