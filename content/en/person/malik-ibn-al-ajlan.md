## Overview
Malik ibn al-Ajlan al-Salimi al-Khazraji, of the [[group/al-khazraj|Khazraj]], who lived in [[place/medina|Yathrib]] before Islam. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.487-488}

## The killing of al-Futayyun
Ibn al-Athir relates that when his sister was given in marriage, she told him that she would go in to al-Futayyun the Jew before her husband. Malik went in to him and killed him, and then fled to Syria. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.487-488}

## His legacy
Abu Jubayla al-Khazraji sought refuge with him, and swore that he would not touch perfume until he had humbled the Jews of Yathrib. Ibn al-Athir counts the killing of al-Futayyun as the first victory of the Ansar over Medina. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.487-488}
