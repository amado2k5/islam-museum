## Summary
Al-Zahir Dawud b. Yusuf, lord of al-Bira, a town on the Byzantine marches near Samosata, eleventh of Salah al-Din's sons; accomplished, a littérateur and gifted poet who loved scholars and was sought out by poets and others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 148-149}
