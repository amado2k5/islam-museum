## Summary

Sa'd ibn Khawla al-Qurashi al-Amiri — also reported as an ally or client of Banu Amir, and Ibn Hisham calls him a Persian of Yemen allied to them. Musa ibn Uqba and Ibn Ishaq name him among the Badriyya. He died at Mecca during the Farewell Pilgrimage, leaving his wife Subay'a bint al-Harith pregnant.
