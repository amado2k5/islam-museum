## Summary
Mahir Yasin al-Fahl is an Iraqi hadith scholar and researcher counted among the leading contemporary specialists in the science of hidden hadith defects (ilal). He heads Dar al-Hadith al-Iraqiyya, combining manuscript editing with isnad criticism. {cite:wikipedia-mahir-al-fahl}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Iraq in 1971, he grew up in Baghdad’s hadith circles, studying under Iraqi and other shaykhs and receiving ijazas in the books of the Sunna. {cite:wikipedia-mahir-al-fahl}

## Career and works
He founded and heads Dar al-Hadith al-Iraqiyya, devoted to editing and teaching the hadith heritage. His works include Athar Ilal al-Hadith fi Ikhtilaf al-Fuqaha, linking ilal to juristic disagreement, and al-Jami’ fi al-Ilal, and he edited heritage works such as Fath al-Baqi by Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali. {cite:wikipedia-mahir-al-fahl}

## Method and contribution
He is noted for precision in tracing transmission paths and exposing hidden defects, teaching hadith criticism in specialised circles that draw students from around the world. He is counted among the figures of the contemporary hadith revival in Iraq. {cite:wikipedia-mahir-al-fahl}
