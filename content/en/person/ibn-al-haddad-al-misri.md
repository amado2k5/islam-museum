## Overview
Judge Abu Bakr Ibn al-Haddad al-Misri, author of *al-Furu'* (The Branches). He died in 345 AH, and is described as a careful jurist whose branches show his excellence. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 60}

The editor of the *Tabaqat al-Fuqaha'* notes in the margin, citing al-Subki and Ibn Khallikan, that his name was Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 60}
