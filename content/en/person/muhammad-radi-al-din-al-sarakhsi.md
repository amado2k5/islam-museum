## Summary
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad, Radi al-Din al-Sarakhsi, a Hanafi jurist and usuli who combined rational and transmitted sciences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}

## Works
He taught in Aleppo, withdrew from teaching under pressure and went to Damascus; his 'Muhit' works are in several volumes; he died in 771 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2062}
