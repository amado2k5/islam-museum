## Summary
Zaynab bint Abd al-Baqi ibn Ali, a traditionist on whose authority Muhammad al-Wani heard the Book of the Prophet's Ethics read aloud around 706 AH {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v2 p. 79}.
