## Summary
Rizq Allah ibn 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn al-Harith, Imam Abu Muhammad al-Tamimi al-Baghdadi, jurist, preacher and shaykh of the Hanbalis, read the Qur'an to Abu'l-Hasan al-Hamid and advanced in fiqh, usul, tafsir, Arabic and language, transmitting from Ibn al-Mutayyam, Abu 'Umar ibn Mahdi and the great masters. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 385}

## On him
Ibn 'Aqil said: he was among his greatest shaykhs — master of Ahmad's companions in lineage, leadership and dignity; the sweetest of men in expressing a point of kalam, the boldest pen in fatwa, the best preacher. He died in mid-Jumada I, 488, at the age of eighty-eight. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 385}
