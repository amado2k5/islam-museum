## Overview
Abu al-Safa Salah al-Din Khalil ibn Muhammad al-Aqfahsi, an Egyptian Shafi'i jurist, known as al-Ashqar. He is of the twenty-fourth class in al-Suyuti's biographies. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Dates
He was born around 763 AH, approximately, and died in 820 AH. Both dates need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
