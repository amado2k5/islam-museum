## Overview
Abu Malik al-Hadrami, one of the Kharijite Shurat according to al-Mas'udi. He is mentioned in a session of Yahya, where he spoke about love. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 358}

Al-Mas'udi relates from him that love is a breath of magic, hidden and burning more than embers, and that it penetrates the heart as the rain of a cloud penetrates the gaps of sand. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 358}
