## Summary
Jean de la Forêt, secretary to King [[person/francis-i-france|Francis I]] of France {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 203}.

## The embassy
Al-Salabi records the king sent him in 941/1535 to Sultan [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]] to conclude the alliance in treaty form — the pact later called the [[event/franco-ottoman-capitulations-941|Franco-Ottoman Capitulations]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 203-204}.
