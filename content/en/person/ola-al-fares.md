## Summary
Ola Al-Fares is a Jordanian journalist, television presenter and lawyer born in 1985, one of the most followed Arab news personalities online; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-ola-al-fares} {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Career
She began presenting as a teenager on Jordanian television, studied law, then anchored news at Al Arabiya in Dubai before moving to MBC as a prominent news presenter, and later joined Al Jazeera as a programme presenter. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Programmes
Her Al Jazeera work includes the digital-facing news and culture programme Jalsa and social-media-format reporting; she has also modelled and judged regional television events. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Digital reach
With followings in the tens of millions across platforms — among the largest for an Arab journalist — she has been recognised with digital-media awards and named among the most influential Arabs on social media. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Assessment
She is credited with carrying television news celebrity into the influencer era of Arab media; commentary about her public positions is reported by the museum attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares} {cite:muslim500-ola-al-fares}


## Career
Her career took her from regional television in Amman to MBC and then Al Arabiya's flagship bulletins — among the most-watched programmes in Arabic — with interviews of heads of state and major figures. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Recognition
She is regularly listed among the most influential Arab media personalities — including by Forbes Middle East rankings — and has received journalism awards for her anchoring and interviews. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## Beyond the newsroom
She fronts charitable and media initiatives, speaks at media forums, and maintains one of the largest social-media followings of any Arab television presenter — a scale of audience that extends her reach well beyond broadcast. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares} {cite:muslim500-ola-al-fares}

## Origins
Born in Jordan in 1985 and trained in journalism and law, she began at Al Arabiya's Jordanian operations and regional broadcasters before rising to the flagship bulletins of Arab satellite news. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## The Al Arabiya years
Her anchoring at Al Arabiya — flagship news programmes and interviews — placed her among the channel's most-visible presenters and among the most-recognised women in Arabic broadcast journalism. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}

## The social presence
Her Instagram and Twitter following — among the largest of any Arab journalist — turned her into a media personality whose reach extends far beyond the broadcast, a profile regularly noted in media-industry coverage. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares} {cite:muslim500-ola-al-fares}

## Arab satellite news
Ola Al-Fares's career spans the rise of Arab satellite news as a form: from regional television in Amman to MBC and then Al Arabiya's flagship bulletins, her anchoring across two decades placed her among the channel's most-visible presenters and among the most-recognised women in Arabic broadcast journalism. Her social-media following — among the largest of any Arab journalist — extended her reach far beyond the broadcast into a media personality in her own right. {cite:wikipedia-ola-al-fares}
