## Summary
Said Aqil Siradj is an Indonesian religious scholar who led the world's largest Islamic organisation — Nahdlatul Ulama (NU), with tens of millions of members — as executive chairman for two consecutive terms (2010–2015 and 2015–2021), and remains its senior advisor (Mustasyar). He is regarded as the leading intellectual face of the moderate "Islam Nusantara" line that the organisation has offered as Indonesia's answer to extremism. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj}

## Early life and education
He was born in 1953 in Cirebon, West Java, into a scholarly household: his father Aqil Siradj was one of the local Nahdlatul Ulama scholars, and he studied in Java's traditional Islamic boarding schools (pesantren), then travelled to Mecca where he took degrees in hadith at Umm al-Qura University — combining authentic pesantren formation with academic study in the holy lands. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj}

## Leading Nahdlatul Ulama
He was elected executive chairman at the organisation's Makassar congress in 2010 and re-elected at Jombang in 2015, leading the world's largest Muslim civil organisation — membership estimated in the tens of millions — through a decade of internal and regional crises; on leaving office the institution honoured him with a seat on its supreme advisory council (Syuriah/Mustasyar). Under his leadership NU launched the Islam Nusantara initiative as a frame for an Indonesian Islam consonant with local culture, opened international offices and convened scholars' conferences against extremism. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj} {cite:muslim500-said-aqil-siradj}

## Public positions
He presents himself as a defender of moderate Islam and Indonesian national unity; his positions friendly to dialogue with government — including statements during election disputes reported in the Indonesian press — drew objections inside and outside the organisation from those who saw him too close to political power or criticised particular remarks of his; the museum reports these debates attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj}

## Writing and honours
He has authored and edited dozens of books and articles on hadith and Islamic thought in Indonesian and Arabic, holds honorary doctorates and awards inside Indonesia, and has appeared at the top of influential-Muslim rankings for leading the largest Islamic organisation on earth. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj}

## Legacy
Siradj's influence exceeds his administration: the Islam Nusantara frame he entrenched became official reference for Indonesia's moderate Islam and a model studied by other Muslim civil organisations for combining religious authenticity with national cultural context — with his discourse archive preserved in pesantren libraries and beyond. {cite:wikipedia-said-aqil-siradj}
