## Overview
Chief judge of Egypt. Al-Suyuti says that in 237 AH the caliph al-Mutawakkil ordered that his beard be shaved, that he be beaten and paraded on a donkey, and it was done to him. Al-Suyuti judges him to have been a tyrant and one of the heads of the Jahmiyya. This is the historian's judgement, not the museum's. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.539}
