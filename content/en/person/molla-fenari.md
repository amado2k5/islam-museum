## Summary
Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Hamza al-Fanari was an Ottoman jurist and scholar. Biblissima records his death in 1431 CE, with a question mark {cite:biblissima Q317098}. The museum's card puts his birth in 1350, while the Biblissima record gives only the thirteen hundreds, so his birth year is not settled.
## In the sources
His card also records a debate over his office. The Turkish Islamic Encyclopaedia takes his appointment as mufti of Bursa in 828 AH (1425) as the conventional start of the Ottoman muftiship, notes that chronicles of his time do not call him shaykh al-islam, and says the sixteenth-century office should not be identified with his post {cite:tdv-seyhulislam}. The card links his birth to Bursa at medium confidence.
