## Overview
Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Ziyad al-Naysaburi, Abu Bakr, was a hadith memoriser and the leading Shafi'i of his time in Iraq. He was born in 238 AH, 852 CE, and died in 324 AH, 936 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}. The entry says he wrote works, and names none of them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 115}.
