## Overview
Yusuf ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Ghafur al-Rumi, known as Qara Sinan, was a Turkish Hanafi jurist, one of their scholars in the days of Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror; he died in 852 AH (1448 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.241}

He wrote in Arabic: al-Safiyya, a commentary on al-Shafiya in morphology; al-Madbut, a gloss on al-Maqsud likewise on morphology; Zayn al-Manar, a commentary on al-Nasafi's Manar al-Anwar on usul; a commentary on al-Jaghamini's al-Mulakhkhas on astronomy; and Ruh al-Arwah, a commentary on Marah al-Arwah. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.241}
