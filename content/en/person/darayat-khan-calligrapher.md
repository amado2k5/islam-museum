## Summary
Darayat Khan's real name was Abdallah; he was the son of [[person/kifayat-khan-calligrapher|Kifayat Khan]] and, like his father, attached to the Mughal court. He learned [[concept/shikasta-script|shikasta]] from his father and then, through practice, devised a style of his own called "Darayat Khani". {cite:salim-tarikh-khatt-o-khattatin-2001 p. 153}

## Standing
Tarikh-i Khatt o Khattatin says masters of the art agree that he wrote better than his father, and that Ghulam Muhammad, author of the Tazkira-i Khushnavisan, praised him so highly as to say that had [[person/mir-ali-tabrizi|Mir Ali Tabrizi]] lived to see Darayat Khan's hand he would have forgotten his own nasta'liq. A specimen of his hand is preserved in the Bayaz of Bakhtawar Khan. {cite:salim-tarikh-khatt-o-khattatin-2001 p. 153}
