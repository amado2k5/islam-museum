## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Salama al-Sahmi, Abu Sakhr al-Hudhali, of Banu Hudhayl ibn Mudrika: an eloquent poet who died about 80 AH (700 CE). In the Umayyad era he was a client and partisan of the Marwanids and composed praise-poems for Abd al-Malik and his brother Abd al-Aziz. Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr once imprisoned him for a year before releasing him at the intercession of men of Quraysh. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.90}

He is the author of the famous lines that begin 'I wondered at fate's pursuit between me and her; when what was between us ended, fate came to rest.' {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.90}
