## Overview
Ziyad ibn Labid al-Bayadi of the Ansar, a Companion. Al-Baladhuri's transmitters agree that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] made him governor of Hadramawt, and when [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] became caliph he wrote to him assigning the governorship of Kinda and al-Sadif together with what he already held of Hadramawt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He appears in the [[event/dispatch-of-sadaqa-collectors|dispatch of the alms collectors]] as the Prophet's agent over Hadramawt and its alms. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 2922: "Ziyad ibn Labid ibn Tha'laba ibn Sinan ibn Amir, the Ansari of the Bayadi clan"; he records that Musa ibn Uqba and others counted him among those who witnessed al-Aqaba and Badr, and that al-Waqidi and others recorded he was the Prophet's agent over Hadramawt, that Abu Bakr appointed him to fight the apostates of Kinda, and that it was he who overpowered al-Ash'ath ibn Qays and marched him to Abu Bakr. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.506}
