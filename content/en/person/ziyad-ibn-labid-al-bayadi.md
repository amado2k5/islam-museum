## Overview
Ziyad ibn Labid al-Bayadi al-Ansari, a Companion. The transmitters of al-Baladhuri agree that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] appointed him over Hadramawt. When [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] was made caliph, he wrote to him appointing him over Kinda and al-Sadaf, in addition to what he governed of Hadramawt {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He appears in [[event/dispatch-of-sadaqa-collectors|the dispatch of the collectors of alms]] in the role of the Prophet's agent over Hadramawt and its alms {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}.
