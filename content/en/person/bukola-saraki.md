# Bukola Saraki

Abubakar Bukola Saraki is a Nigerian physician and politician, born on 19 December 1962 in London, the son of the senator Olusola Saraki and Florence Morenike Saraki. He was educated at King's College, Lagos, at Cheltenham College in England, and at the London Hospital Medical College of the University of London, where he took his MBBS in 1987. He worked as a medical officer at Rush Green Hospital in Essex (1988–1989) and as a director of Société Générale Bank (Nigeria) from 1990 to 2000, before President Olusegun Obasanjo appointed him a special assistant on budget in 2000 {cite:wikipedia-bukola-saraki-en} {cite:wikidata-bukola-saraki}.

## Governor of Kwara

He governed Kwara State from 29 May 2003 to 29 May 2011 on the Peoples Democratic Party platform, winning re-election in 2007. His administration led reforms in agriculture, infrastructure, health and power — including the Shonga Farms programme — connected over 3,750 rural communities to the national grid, and from 2007 he chaired the Nigeria Governors' Forum, through which he led national polio-immunisation efforts {cite:wikipedia-bukola-saraki-en}.

## President of the Senate

He entered the Senate for Kwara Central in 2011, and on 9 June 2015 was elected the thirteenth President of the Nigerian Senate under the All Progressives Congress, serving to 2019. On 31 July 2018 he left the ruling party and returned to the PDP, becoming opposition leader; he contested the PDP presidential primaries for 2019, lost to Atiku Abubakar, and then directed Atiku's presidential campaign {cite:wikipedia-bukola-saraki-en}.
