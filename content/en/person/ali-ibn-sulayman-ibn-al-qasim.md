## Overview
Ali ibn Sulayman ibn al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim was a cousin of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], who left him as deputy at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] when he went to Shibam. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} A crowd from Sana'a under Ahmad ibn Mahfuz then broke the prison and drove Ali ibn Sulayman out of the city, and Abd al-Qahir ibn Ahmad ibn Na'im took it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} See [[event/sanaa-rising-ahmad-ibn-mahfuz-288]]. He is not [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-ali|Ali ibn Sulayman ibn Ali]] the Abbasid.

## After al-Hadi's return to Sana'a, pp. 232 to 233
The narrator says that he followed the routed to Hidayn until he was in the midst of their horse, where they struck him with spear and sword and he fell from his mount; Abu al-Atahiya rescued him and took him to Sana'a, where he died {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233} ([[event/the-deaths-of-ali-ibn-sulayman-and-abu-al-atahiya-at-hidayn-288|the deaths of Ali ibn Sulayman and Abu al-Atahiya]]). The narrator adds a formula of mercy for him, which the museum does not write.
