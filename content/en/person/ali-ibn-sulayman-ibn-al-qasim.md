## Overview
Ali ibn Sulayman ibn al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim was a cousin of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], who left him as deputy at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] when he went to Shibam. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} A crowd from Sana'a under Ahmad ibn Mahfuz then broke the prison and drove Ali ibn Sulayman out of the city, and Abd al-Qahir ibn Ahmad ibn Na'im took it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} See [[event/sanaa-rising-ahmad-ibn-mahfuz-288]]. He is not [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-ali|Ali ibn Sulayman ibn Ali]] the Abbasid.
