## Shams al-Din al-Zarkashi al-Baghdadi

Shams al-Din al-Zarkashi al-Baghdadi, a resident of Cairo. He excelled in the readings of the Qur'an, took part in many disciplines, and practised verse. He wrote a fine poem on prosody and a commentary on it, and composed "al-Awatil al-Hawali," sixteen poems in seventeen metres, none of which uses a letter with a dot. Ibn Hajar heard from him, and he also heard from Ibn Hajar and accompanied him in hearing. At the end of his life he suffered an ordeal.

He died in Dhu al-Hijja 812 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 112}.
