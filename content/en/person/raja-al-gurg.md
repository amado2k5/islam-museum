## Summary
Raja Al Gurg is an Emirati businesswoman, chairperson and managing director of the Easa Saleh Al Gurg Group — a Dubai family conglomerate spanning trading, manufacturing, retail, real estate and education — and one of the most consistently ranked powerful women in Arab business. {cite:wikipedia-raja-al-gurg}

## The Al Gurg Group
A member of one of Dubai's oldest merchant families, Al Gurg took over leadership of the group founded by her father and husband's family business tradition — the Al Gurg name has traded on the Creek since the nineteenth century — and ran its diversification across more than two dozen companies and partnerships, including Unilever franchises and building-materials, retail and property interests. {cite:wikipedia-raja-al-gurg}

## Firsts and advocacy
She was the first woman appointed to the board of the Dubai Chamber of Commerce and Industry, has served on national economic bodies, and founded or supported initiatives for women's business networking in the Emirates. In public she has long argued that Gulf women's advancement runs through boardrooms as much as through law. {cite:muslim500-raja-al-gurg}

## Standing
A fixture of Forbes Middle East's power lists, she is profiled in the Muslim 500 as the senior female business leader of the Emirates — the executive who carried a historic trading house into diversified modern ownership while becoming the country's best-known advocate for women in the boardroom. {cite:muslim500-raja-al-gurg}
