## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ishaq al-Mayurqi, known as Ibn Ghaniya al-Sanhaji, lord of Mallorca, Minorca and Ibiza, three neighbouring islands in the western sea. His father Ishaq died in 580 AH, and Ali and his brother Yahya went out to Ifriqiya, where they fought famous wars and caused disorder in the lands, according to Ibn Khallikan. Ali was alive in 591 AH. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.16}
