## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah ibn Abdallah ibn Khurradadhbih, a historian and geographer of encyclopaedic learning, author of *al-Masalik wa al-Mamalik* (Routes and Realms). He was born in 205 AH (820 CE) and died in 280 AH (893 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
