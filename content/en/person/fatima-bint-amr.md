## Overview
Fatima bint Amr ibn A'idh ibn Imran ibn Makhzum: mother of Abdullah, the Prophet's father — his paternal grandmother.

## Life
Abdullah's mother was Fatima bint Amr ibn A'idh ibn Imran ibn Makhzum ibn Yaqaza ibn Murra, of the Makhzum clan, one of Quraysh's divisions {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Fatima bint Amr is the Prophet's grandmother on his father's side, of Banu Makhzum, one of Quraysh's clans.
