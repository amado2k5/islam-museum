## Overview
[[person/abbad-ibn-qays-mutah|Abbad ibn Qays ibn Absa]] al-Ansari al-Khazraji. He witnessed Badr together with his brother Subay' ibn Qays, and he was killed on the day of Mu'tah. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p. 628}

## Lineage
He was of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b of the Khazraj. It is also said that his father's name was Aysha {cite:usd-al-ghaba p. 628}.

## Badr and Mu'tah
He witnessed Badr together with his brother Subay' ibn Qays, and he was killed at Mu'tah {cite:usd-al-ghaba p. 628}.

## For young visitors
Abbad ibn Qays was one of the Ansar. He witnessed Badr with his brother and was killed at Mu'tah.

## Notes for researchers
- The name of his father is given differently as "Qays" and as "Aysha". Ibn al-Athir introduces the second with "it is said" («وقيل»), and we have not preferred either.
- The details of his death at Mu'tah do not appear in the text we have read. They should be checked against Ibn Ishaq and al-Waqidi before being added.
