## Overview
Muhammad ibn Shakir ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Shakir ibn Harun ibn Shakir al-Kutubi al-Darani, later of Damascus, the historian, heard Ibn al-Shihna, al-Mizzi and others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.345}

He was very poor, then took up the book trade and was granted great wealth from it; he died in Ramadan 764 AH (1363 CE). {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.345}
