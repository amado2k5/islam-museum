## Overview
Abd al-Basit ibn Khalil ibn Shahin al-Malati is described by al-Zirikli as a learned man versed in history {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 73}.

## His work
He wrote a continuation of al-Dhahabi's history which he called Nayl al-Amal, in manuscript, ending with the year 896 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 73}. The references printed beside his entry, to Tadhkirat al-Huffaz and Tahdhib al-Tahdhib, cannot be tied to him from the text as read {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 73}.
