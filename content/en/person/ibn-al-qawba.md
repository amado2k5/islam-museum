## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Ja'fari, Rukn al-Din Abu Abd Allah, known as Ibn al-Qawba', was among the elite of the Malikis; he gave fatwas while also pursuing wisdom and medicine. Born at Tunis, he studied there and at Damascus and settled at Cairo, dying in 738 AH (1338 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.34}

Ibn Sayyid al-Nas related that he never failed to read Ibn Sina's al-Shifa' every night, and when asked how long he would keep at the book he answered: I want to find guidance. He left verse and works, among them a commentary on Surat Qaf and glosses on the diwan of al-Mutanabbi. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.34}
