## Overview
Ali ibn Isa, the vizier of the Abbasid caliph al-Muqtadir. Al-Tabari says that he was arrested in 304 AH, summoned from the Maghrib to take the vizierate in 314 AH, and arrested a second time in 316 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.59} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.112} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.117}

## Other reports
Al-Suyuti says that he was appointed vizier to al-Muqtadir in 301 AH, that he abolished wine, and that he abolished dues yielding five hundred thousand dinars a year. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.587,595}

Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muqtadir appointed him vizier twice, and that he was arrested in Dhu al-Hijja 304 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.660-661} Ibn al-Athir quotes his saying about al-Tabari, that the common people attached to him first the charge of Rafd and then of heresy. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.99} Ibn al-Nadim gives a story about al-Hallaj with him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.270}
