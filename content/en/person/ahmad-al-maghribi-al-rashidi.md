## Summary
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Razzaq ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad, known as al-Maghribi al-Rashidi, of Moroccan origin from Rashid in Egypt, where he was born and died. He memorised the Qur'an in his town, studied in Cairo at al-Azhar with many teachers, and was a close pupil of al-Shabramallisi. Back home he became the shaykh of the Shafi'is there. His works include a gloss on al-Ramli's commentary on al-Minhaj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1644} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1645}
