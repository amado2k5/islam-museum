## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam al-Ukbari, whose lineage is traced to Qahtan, Abu Abdallah, al-Mufid, known as Ibn al-Mu'allim, was an Imami verifier, that is a scholar of the Imamiyya, a Shi'i school, to whom the leadership of the Shi'a passed in his time. He wrote much on the principles, on theology (kalam) and on jurisprudence. He was born at Ukbara, ten farsakhs from Baghdad, and was raised and died at Baghdad. The printed text gives his birth as 336 AH and his death as 413 AH, that is 947 and 1022 CE; the card keeps 948 for his birth because 336 AH runs from 947 into 948 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His works
He left about two hundred works. Al-Zirikli names among them 'al-I'lam fima Ittafaqat al-Imamiyya alayhi min al-Ahkam', printed; 'al-Irshad', printed, on the history of the Prophet, Fatima al-Zahra and the imams; 'al-Risala al-Muqni'a', printed, on jurisprudence; 'Ahkam al-Nisa'', in manuscript; 'Awa'il al-Maqalat fi al-Madhahib wa al-Mukhtarat', printed; 'al-Amali', printed, arranged by sessions; 'Naqd Fadilat al-Mu'tazila'; 'Iman Abi Talib', a printed treatise; 'Usul al-Fiqh'; 'al-Kalam fi Wujuh I'jaz al-Qur'an'; 'Tarikh al-Shari'a'; and 'al-Ifsah', printed, on the imamate. {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## What was said of him
Al-Zirikli quotes al-Dhahabi as saying that he went to excess in attacking the predecessors (the salaf) and that he had a stern authority in the state of Adud al-Dawla. This is al-Dhahabi's statement as al-Zirikli gives it; the museum gives no verdict on it
