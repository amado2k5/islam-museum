## Overview
Busbuhra ibn Saluba, a man of Banqiya, whom al-Baladhuri mentions in the report of its peace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342}

## The peace of Baniqya
Al-Baladhuri relates that he went out to [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] on behalf of the people of Baniqya, excused himself from fighting and offered peace. Jarir made peace with him for a thousand dirhams and a tayalisan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342} See [[event/baniqya-sulh|the peace of Baniqya]].

Khalid wrote him a letter, and sent the tayalisan to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], who gave it to al-Husayn ibn Ali. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342}
