## Overview
Daughter of Nizam al-Mulk Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. Ishaq, vizier of sultans Alp Arslan and Malikshah; wife of the 'Abbasid caliph al-Muqtadi bi'llah, who married her in 462 — she died in Sha'ban 470 {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
