## Summary
[[person/safiyya-bint-shayba|Safiyya bint Shayba]] ibn Uthman al-Abdariyya, daughter of [[person/shayba-ibn-uthman|Shayba ibn Uthman]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1910}.

## Is she a Companion?
Her companionship is disputed; Ibn Hajar says "far-fetched is the claim she had no sight of him" — her hadith is established in Sahih al-Bukhari in hanging form: Abban ibn Salih said, from al-Hasan ibn Muslim, from Safiyya bint Shayba, who said: I heard the Prophet {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1910}. Ibn Manda narrated from her: "by God, it is as if I see the Messenger of God when he entered the Ka'ba" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1911}.

## Narration
She also related from A'isha, Umm Habiba and Umm Salama, the Prophet's wives, and from Asma' bint Abi Bakr, Umm Uthman bint Sufyan, and an umm walad of Shayba's {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1911}. From her related her son Mansur ibn Safiyya, her nephew Abd al-Hamid ibn Jubayr ibn Shayba, al-Hasan ibn Muslim, Qatada, al-Mughira ibn Hakim, Maymun ibn Mihran and others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1911}.

## On her reliability
Ibn Ma'in said Ibn Jurayj met her but did not hear from her; Ibn Hibban mentioned her among the reliable Successors {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1911}.

## The point of dispute
Whether she was a Companion or a Successor is a disagreement stated in the entry itself {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1910}.
