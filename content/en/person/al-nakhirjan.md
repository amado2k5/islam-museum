## Overview
Al-Nakhirjan, the lord of the cities in the report that al-Baladhuri relates from the conquests. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
He went out with a great host of the people of the cities and met the Muslims beyond the monastery of Ka'b. Zuhayr ibn Sulaym al-Azdi embraced him and slit his belly with a dagger he had at his waist. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
