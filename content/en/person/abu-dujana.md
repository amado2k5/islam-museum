## Summary
[[person/abu-dujana|Abu Dujana]] al-Ansari, named Simak ibn Kharasha — or Ibn Aws ibn Kharasha, it is said {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}. His presence at [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] is agreed {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}.

## The Prophet's sword at Uhud
He is established in Sahih Muslim in Anas's hadith: on the day of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] the Prophet took a sword and said "who will take this sword with its due?", and Abu Dujana took it and "split open the polytheists' skulls with it" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}. Al-Dawlabi in al-Kuna relates from al-Zubayr that when the Prophet offered a sword Abu Dujana rose and said "I — and what is its due?" He said: "kill no Muslim with it and flee no disbeliever with it" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}.

## Defending the Prophet
Ibn Ishaq relates that when the fighting closed in, [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr]] shielded the Prophet until he was killed, and Abu Dujana until his wounds grew many {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}.

## His death
Ali said he was martyred at al-Yamama; it is also said he took part in the killing of Musaylima {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1662}.
