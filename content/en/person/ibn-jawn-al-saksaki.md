## Overview
Ibn Jawn al-Saksaki, one of the two men who killed Ammar ibn Yasir at Siffin, together with Abu al-Adiya al-Amili. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673}

## The dispute over the spoils
The two men disputed over Ammar's spoils, until Abdallah ibn Amr ibn al-As turned them away with a hadith of the Prophet about Ammar. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673}
