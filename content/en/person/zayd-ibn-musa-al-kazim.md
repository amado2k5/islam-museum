## Overview
Zayd ibn Musa ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad. He rose in Basra with Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far in the year 199 AH, in the rising of [[person/abu-al-saraya|Abu al-Saraya]], and they took control of it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 419-420}.
