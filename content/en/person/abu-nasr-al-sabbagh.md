## Summary
Mahmud ibn al-Fadl ibn Mahmud ibn Abd al-Wahid, Abu al-Nasr al-Sabbagh of Isfahan. He heard from Abd al-Rahman ibn Mandah and his brother Abd al-Wahhab; Ibn Nasir and Abu al-Fath Muhammad ibn Ali narrated from him. He was a reliable hadith memoriser, of good conduct, expert in names and genealogies, and useful to students. Death 512 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13348}
