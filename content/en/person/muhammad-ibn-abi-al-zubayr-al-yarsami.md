## Overview
He is Muhammad ibn Abi al-Zubayr al-Yarsami, whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put over [[place/thath-yemen|Thath]] when he stayed there some days in Rabi I 288 AH {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. The narrator says nothing more about him, and the museum does not tie him to Muhammad ibn Musbih al-Yarsami or to any other Yarsami. See [[event/al-hadi-at-thath-and-bashar-and-the-return-to-sanaa-and-shibam-288|al-Hadi at Thath and Bashar and the return to Sana'a]].
