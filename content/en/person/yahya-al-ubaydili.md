## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Hasan ibn Ja'far al-Hujja ibn Ubayd Allah al-A'raj ibn al-Husayn al-Asghar ibn Zayn al-Abidin al-Sajjad, Abu al-Husayn al-Ubaydili al-Aqiqi, is described by al-Zirikli as a genealogist and historian {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 140-141}. Al-Zirikli prints his years as 214 AH (829 CE) to 277 AH (890 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 140-141}.

## Life
Al-Zirikli says he was from the people of Medina, born there, and that he died at Mecca {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 140-141}. He adds that it was said he was the first to compose genealogies of the Talibids {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 140-141}.

## Works
Among his books al-Zirikli names Akhbar al-Madina and Ansab Al Abi Talib {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 140-141}.
