## Summary
Nizam al-Din Alishir Nava'i al-Harawi was a poet in Chagatai Turkish and a vizier at Herat. Biblissima records 1441 to 1501 CE {cite:biblissima Q812}.
## In the sources
The nisba al-Harawi marks an association with Herat, not a proven residence; the museum's card calls him poet and vizier {cite:biblissima Q812}.
