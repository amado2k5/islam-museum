## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Abi Zayd b. Muhammad b. 'Ali the grammarian, known as al-Fasihi of Istrabadh; he took grammar from 'Abd al-Qahir, author of the Jumal of Istrabadh, and dove deep until he became the most knowledgeable of his age; he came to Baghdad, settled, and taught grammar at the Nizamiyya madrasa for a time — multitudes profited from him, among them the king of grammarians al-Hasan b. Safi — and Abu Tahir al-Silafi transmitted from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 70}

## His death
He died Wednesday 13 Dhu'l-Hijja 616 at Baghdad; Ibn Khallikan said it was not known whether his nisba al-Fasihi points to Tha'lab's Kitab al-fasih or to something else. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 70}
