## Overview

Halal Chef is a British content creator who publishes on YouTube under the channel name only; the channel's About text introduces it: "SUBSCRIBE & I will cook you something Special! Halal Chef offers delicious and exciting videos about food/cooking"{cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}. Channel data records it as a United Kingdom channel established on 15 February 2016{cite:source/findachannel-halal-chef}. The channel's operator does not publish a legal name in any available source, so this record carries the published name{cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}{cite:source/findachannel-halal-chef}.

## Career and work

The channel's recipes centre on home cooking and recreations of famous takeaway dishes: peri peri chicken, fried chicken in the style of fast-food chains, biryanis of several kinds, burgers, pastas and non-alcoholic mocktails{cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}. Some of the channel's best-known videos have been viewed millions of times, among them a peri peri chicken with spicy rice passing 3.1 million views and a fried-chicken recipe passing 1.9 million{cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}. The operator links accounts on Instagram and Snapchat (halal_chef), Facebook (/halalchefofficial) and TikTok (@halalchef){cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}. The channel had about 358,000 subscribers in a platform count taken in October 2026; platform figures change constantly{cite:source/youtube-halal-chef}.
