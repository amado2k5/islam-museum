## Overview
Musa ibn Ka'b al-Tamimi. Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/abu-muslim-al-khurasani|Abu Muslim]] appointed him and sent him to Sind, where he defeated Mansur ibn Jumhur and his army and killed his brother, and Mansur died of thirst in the sand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He then governed Sind, rebuilt al-Mansura and added to its mosque, and campaigned and conquered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
