## Overview
Ibn al-Rumiyya, the Sevillian hafiz (d. 637) — a pious memorizer and compiler "of the affirmers," a Zahiri of scrupulous piety who earned his living from pharmacy for his fine knowledge of plants, as Ibn Nasir al-Din said {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 184}.
