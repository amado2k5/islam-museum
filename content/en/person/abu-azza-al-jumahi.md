## Overview
A poet of the Banu Jumah. The Muslims took him captive at Badr, and the Prophet released him without ransom on condition that he would not return to fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 230}

He afterwards incited the tribes with his poetry, and was then killed in captivity at Uhud. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 249}
