## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Uthman al-Azdi, known as Ibn al-Banna' al-Marrakushi. A scholar from Marrakesh in mathematics, arithmetic and astronomy, who wrote on the sciences of calculation. He was born in 654 AH (1256 CE) and died in 721 AH (1321 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
