## Overview
al-Hajjaj appointed him over al-Fallujah. According to al-Mas'udi, he sought the advice of Jamil ibn Suhayb, the landholder, on how to act and how to escape the evil of al-Hajjaj {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.136}.
