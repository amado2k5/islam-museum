## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn al-Khattab al-Adawi al-Qurashi, nephew of Umar ibn al-Khattab. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3490}

## Transmission
He narrated from his father and others; his son Abd al-Hamid narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}
