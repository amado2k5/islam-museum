## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Amr al-Ghanawi left Basra in 287 AH, as it is reported, along with Abu Sa'id al-Janabi and those who joined him from the Qaramita. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.77}
