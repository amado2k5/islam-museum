## Overview
Ibrahim ibn al-Ashtar, a commander who rose up with [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd]]. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 p.85}

## The head of Ibn Ziyad
After [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] was killed, his head was brought to al-Mukhtar. Al-Mukhtar sent it to [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr]] in Mecca. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 p.85}

The citation is from the third volume of *Muruj al-Dhahab*, in the edition of Mari.
