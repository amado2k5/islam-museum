Maysara, the servant of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]; he accompanied [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]] on her trading journey to Syria with her wealth, and witnessed in him sweet conduct, noble bearing, sound judgment, truthful speech and a faithful way — he told Khadija what he saw, and it was among the causes of her wish to marry him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
