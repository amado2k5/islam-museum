## Summary
Maysara was the servant of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]. He went out with [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] on [[event/trade-journey-to-syria-for-khadija|the journey to Syria]] with her goods, and then told her what he had seen in him: sweet manners, noble qualities, sound judgement, truthful speech and a trustworthy way. This was among the reasons for [[event/marriage-to-khadija|her marrying him]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/187-190}.

## On the journey and after
Khadija sent to the Prophet offering him a journey to Syria with her goods, with a servant of hers called Maysara. He accepted, and went out with Maysara until they reached Syria {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/187-188}. When they returned, Maysara told Khadija what he had seen in the Prophet, and she found in him the treasure she had been seeking {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/189-190}.

## From another source
Al-Mas'udi relates that the monk Nestorius looked at the Prophet from his hermitage during this journey, while a cloud shaded him. He said that he was a prophet and the last of the prophets {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.569-570}.

## Notes for researchers
- The data card contains a passage taken from Ibn Khaldun about a "Maysara" whom the Berbers pledged allegiance to on Sufri doctrine and then killed. That is Maysara al-Mutghari, leader of the Berber revolt in 122 AH, not Khadija's servant. The passage needs to be moved to a separate card.
- *The Sealed Nectar* does not record what happened to Maysara after the journey, nor his religion.

## For young visitors
Maysara was the servant of Lady Khadija. He accompanied the Prophet on the trading journey to Syria, and when he came back he told Khadija about the Prophet's truthfulness and good character.
