## Overview
Hurqus ibn al-Numan al-Bahrani, of Bahra' ibn Hilal of Qudaa'a. He was at the water of Suwa, a water belonging to Kalb, in a group who were drinking and singing, when [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] raided them on his way to Syria, and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.153}

## Before his killing
The book reports that he used to say before he was killed: "Give me drink before the army of Abu Bakr." It reports that his blood flowed into the bowl in which his drink was, and adds that "it is said" his head also fell into it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.153}

## The time of the event
The data places his killing in Rabi' al-Akhir 13 AH, on Khalid's march, and it is connected with [[event/khalid-march-to-syria|Khalid's march to Syria]]. The Gregorian date is derived, and it rests on a single source, so it needs an additional source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.153}
