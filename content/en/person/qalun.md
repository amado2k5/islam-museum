## Overview
'Isa ibn Mina' ibn Wardan ibn 'Isa al-Madani, client of the Ansar, Abu Musa, known as Qalun, was one of the renowned Qur'an readers; of Medina by birth and death in 220 AH (835 CE), the leadership in the Arabic sciences and the reading in his time in the Hijaz ended with him. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.110}

He was deaf: the Qur'an was recited to him while he watched the reciter's lips and sent back every slip and error. 'Qalun' is a title the reader Nafi' gave him for the excellence of his reading; in the language of the Greeks it means 'good'. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.110}
