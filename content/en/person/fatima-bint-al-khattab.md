## Summary
Fatima bint al-Khattab al-Adawiyya al-Qurashiyya, sister of Umar ibn al-Khattab and wife of Sa'id ibn Zayd. In the count of The Sealed Nectar, she was one of the earliest Muslims in the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]] in Mecca, and one of the first Muslim women {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book mentions her along with her husband [[person/said-ibn-zayd|Sa'id ibn Zayd]] al-Adawi, among those who followed the first group. It names her Fatima bint al-Khattab al-Adawiyya, sister of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. The book returns to her part in her brother Umar's Islam at its proper place.

## Not covered here
This chapter of the book mentions only her early Islam. The rest of her story is completed at its proper places in the book and in other sources.

## For young visitors
Fatima bint al-Khattab embraced Islam early, together with her husband Sa'id, before her brother Umar. Afterwards she played a part in his Islam.
