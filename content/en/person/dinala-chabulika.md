## Summary
Sheikh Dinala Chabulika is a Malawian preacher and rights activist who has served for many years as national spokesperson of the Muslim Association of Malawi (MAM), the country's largest Muslim umbrella body, and coordinates its Islamic Information Bureau (IIB). He is known for a public voice on Muslim rights, health, education and relations between Muslims and others in a country where Muslims are a minority. {cite:muslim500-dinala-chabulika}

## Formation
He graduated from the Islamic University of Madinah, the religious formation that led him into official preaching work within the Association's institutions, and was appointed resident representative of the Islamic Online University (IOU) in Malawi after the IIB signed a memorandum to open a branch in the country. {cite:malawimuslims-dinala-chabulika}

## Public work
In Malawian press statements he presents himself as an activist binding social issues to religious duty: he campaigned against the compulsory teaching of non-Islamic religious texts to Muslim schoolchildren, worked on public-health questions and against exploitation of the poor, and led outreach between Muslims and non-Muslims through media workshops in which he explains Islam to non-Muslim journalists and corrects their coverage of the minority. Through the Islamic Information Bureau he coordinated relief and education projects including the distribution of school desks and support to the Association's institutions. {cite:muslim500-dinala-chabulika} {cite:malawimuslims-dinala-chabulika}

## Positions and debate
His support for President Joyce Banda in 2014 — when he oversaw the distribution of presidential food aid in Muslim communities — caused a dispute inside the Association's leadership, and his Blantyre home was attacked by armed men attributed to a Muslim faction opposed to cooperation with the government; he and his family escaped, and he is quoted answering that serving the hungry is part of the teachings of the religion. The Malawian press documents this dispute and the museum reports it attributed, without verdict. {cite:malawimuslims-dinala-chabulika}

## Legacy
Chabulika represents the organised face of Malawian Islam: an official spokesman translating minority concerns into the country's public media and linking, in his discourse, religious rights, social service and dialogue — within a country where the Muslim Association runs a wide network of schools, mosques and relief. {cite:muslim500-dinala-chabulika}
