## Overview
Jawad ibn Sa'd (or Sa'id) ibn Jawad al-Baghdadi al-Kazimi was, in al-Zirikli's words, a jurist of the people of al-Kazimayn in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 139}. The entry gives his death as 1065 AH (1655 CE) and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 139}.

## In Iran
He went to Iran and reached the rank of shaykh al-Islam in Astarabad; its people rose against him and drove him out, at the instigation of a rival among their scholars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 139}.

## His works
Among his books is "Masalik al-afham ila ayat al-ahkam", of which al-Zirikli records a manuscript in the author's own hand in the village of Bahar, which belongs to Hamadan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 139}. The entry is short and says nothing more.
