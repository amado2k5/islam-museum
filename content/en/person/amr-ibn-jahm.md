## Summary
Son of Jahm ibn Qays; he accompanied his father to Abyssinia and arrived with him in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him son of Jahm ibn Qays with his brother Khuzayma, their mother Umm Harmala with them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} He counts Jahm, his wife and his two sons in the tally as one "man" — so the son is not counted an independent man among the sixteen here. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-dar|Banu Abd al-Dar]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Ibn Ishaq counted Jahm and those with him as one "man" in the sixteen's total (p. 5); a separate count of the sons is therefore not assured.
