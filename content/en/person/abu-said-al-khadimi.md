## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Mustafa ibn Uthman, Abu Sa'id al-Khadimi, was a Hanafi jurist and specialist in the principles of jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. Al-Zirikli prints his birth as 1113 AH, 1701 CE, and his death as 1176 AH, 1763 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}.

## Life
His family came from Bukhara; he was born and died in the village of Khadim, which belonged to the district of Konya, and he studied with his father and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. He became well known for a lesson he gave at Hagia Sophia in Istanbul on the interpretation of al-Fatiha {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his writings Majami' al-Haqa'iq on the principles of jurisprudence, printed, its commentary Manafi' al-Daqa'iq, printed, and a gloss on Durar al-Hukkam in Hanafi law, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. He also lists al-Bariqa al-Mahmudiyya, a commentary on al-Tariqa al-Muhammadiyya of al-Birgivi, printed in four parts, and a commentary on al-Risala al-Waladiyya attributed to al-Ghazali, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. Further titles are al-Wasaya, in manuscript at the Azhar library, and Haqiqat Kalimat al-Tawhid inda al-Mutakallimin wa-l-Sufiyya, in manuscript at Dar al-Kutub, no. 21606 b {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. He also lists a treatise on the ruling on reciting Ayat al-Kursi after prayer, in manuscript of 25 leaves at Dar al-Kutub, no. 21606 b, and another on the interpretation of a verse of Al Imran, which al-Zirikli names by its opening words, in the same volume {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}. The list closes with Khaza'in al-Jawahir, in manuscript, and a printed treatise on the interpretation of the basmala {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 68}.
