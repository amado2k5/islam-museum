## Overview
Yunus ibn Abd al-Rahman, client of Ali ibn Yaqtin, Abu Muhammad, was an Iraqi Imami jurist among the companions of Musa ibn Ja'far; Ali ibn Musa (al-Rida) used to liken him to Salman al-Farisi. He died in 208 AH (823 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.261}

He left some thirty books, among them al-Dalala 'ala al-Khayr; al-Shara'i'; Jawami' al-Athar; 'Ilal al-Hadith; al-Jami' al-Kabir on fiqh; Tafsir al-Qur'an; al-Adab; al-Mathalib; and al-Radd 'ala al-Ghulat. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.261}
