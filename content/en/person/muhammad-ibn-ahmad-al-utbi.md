## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Umawi al-Qurtubi al-Andalusi, Abu Abdallah, is described by al-Zirikli as a Maliki jurist. His name al-Utbi points by clientage to Utba ibn Abi Sufyan ibn Harb. He died in al-Andalus in 255 AH, 869 CE; al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His works
He wrote several works, among them al-Mustakhraja al-Utbiyya ala al-Muwatta, on Malik's jurisprudence, in manuscript, and Kira' al-Dur wa-l-Aradin, also in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
