## Summary
Ibn al-Naqib, the imam, exegete, 'allama and Hanbali, teacher of Cairo's 'Ashuriyya; he came to Egypt and heard from Yusuf b. al-Mukhayli, composed an exegesis reaching the utmost — an imam, ascetic, worshipper, sought in visitation, blessed by it, a denouncer of wrong, of great standing. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 773}
