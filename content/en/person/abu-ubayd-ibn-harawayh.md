## Overview
The judge Abu Ubayd Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Harb, known as Ibn Harawayh, who died in 319 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}

## His generation
Al-Shirazi mentions him in a generation most of whom were companions of Abu al-Abbas ibn Surayj. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}

## A note on the date
The printed edition gives "seventeen", but the editor considers it a copyist's error, since al-Subki agrees with al-Shirazi on "nineteen". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58} The Gregorian date (about 932 CE) is calculated from the Hijri date.
