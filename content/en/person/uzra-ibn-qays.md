## Overview
Uzra ibn Qays al-Bajali, also called Urza, governor of Hulwan in the caliphate of Umar {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Shahrazur
al-Baladhuri reports that he tried to conquer Shahrazur and could not. It was conquered after him by Utba ibn Farqad. The data links him with [[event/conquest-of-shahrazur|the conquest of Shahrazur]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He may be the same person as [[person/uzra-ibn-qays-al-bajali|Uzra ibn Qays al-Bajali]]. This needs an additional source.
