## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Muhammad ibn Tahir ibn Ali ibn Ahmad al-Shaybani al-Maqdisi, the hafiz al-Qaysarani, was a man of wide rihla, works and commentaries; he lived sixty years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.11 p.732}

He heard hadith first at Jerusalem from Ibn Warqa', at Baghdad from Abu Muhammad al-Sarifini, at Nishapur from al-Fadl ibn al-Muhibb, and at Herat, Isfahan, Shiraz, Ray, Damascus and Egypt, and was among the fastest writers, cleverest and most knowing of hadith. The hafiz Isma'il ibn al-Fadl said 'I saw none with a better memory than Muhammad ibn Tahir', and al-Salafi heard Ibn Tahir say 'I wrote out al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah seven times by copying'. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.11 p.732}
