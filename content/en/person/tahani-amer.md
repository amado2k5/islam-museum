## Summary
Tahani Amer is an Egyptian-American engineer who rose from technical work to program leadership at NASA. In the Earth Science Division she oversees missions that observe the planet from space. {cite:wikipedia-tahani-amer}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Egypt in 1965, she moved to the United States, studied engineering to graduate degrees in aerospace, and joined NASA. {cite:wikipedia-tahani-amer}

## Career and works
She began as a researcher in thermal measurement, patenting thin-film thermal conductivity instrumentation, then moved into program management, contributing to Earth-observing missions such as SWOT’s surface-water survey and the climate missions CLARREO and GeoCarb. {cite:wikipedia-tahani-amer}

## Legacy
Media present her as a model of an Arab Muslim woman reaching program leadership at NASA, and she speaks on the importance of science education and encouraging girls into engineering. {cite:wikipedia-tahani-amer}
