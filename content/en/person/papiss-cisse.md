# Papiss Cissé

## Summary

Papiss Cissé is a Senegalese retired footballer, born in Dakar in 1985. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en} A forward, he scored 22 goals for SC Freiburg in the 2010–11 Bundesliga — then the most by an African player in a single Bundesliga season — before spells at Newcastle United, Shandong Luneng, Fenerbahçe and other clubs; he won 36 caps and scored 17 goals for Senegal. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en} A practising Muslim, he performs the sujud when celebrating goals; no source names a school for him, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en}{cite:wikidata-papiss-cisse}

## Club career

Cissé came to Europe with Metz before joining SC Freiburg in 2009, where his 22 league goals in 2010–11 set an African record for a Bundesliga season. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en} Newcastle United signed him in January 2012 and he scored a celebrated run of goals in his first months in England; he later played in China for Shandong Luneng, then for Fenerbahçe, Çaykur Rizespor and Amiens. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en}

## Senegal

Cissé won 36 caps for Senegal between 2009 and 2015, scoring 17 goals, and captained the side in 2012–13. {cite:wikipedia-papiss-cisse-en}
