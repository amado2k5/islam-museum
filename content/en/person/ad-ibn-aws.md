## Overview
Ad ibn Aws, said to be the first of the kings who ruled Ad for three hundred years — then the son of Ad ibn Aws ruled, and the last of their kings was al-Khuljan; he marched with his children and followers to al-Ahqaf, nearing the sands between Oman, Hadramawt and Yemen, saying 'I am Ad the tall, the desert-dweller, marked with the mark of my grandsire Ibn Nuh the guide'.
