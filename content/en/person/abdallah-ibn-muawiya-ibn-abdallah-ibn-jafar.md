## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Mu'awiya ibn Abd Allah ibn Ja'far, an Alid. Al-Mas'udi says that he gained control of Istakhr and other parts of the land of Fars in the strife at the end of the Umayyad state, then went to Khurasan, where [[person/abu-muslim-al-khurasani|Abu Muslim]] seized him. Al-Mas'udi says that some people held that he was the imam and followed his call. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.229}
