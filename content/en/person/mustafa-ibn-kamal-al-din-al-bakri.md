## Overview
Mustafa ibn Kamal al-Din ibn Ali al-Bakri al-Siddiqi, Abu al-Mawahib, is described by al-Zirikli as Khalwati in his Sufi way and Hanafi in his school, a Sufi among the scholars, prolific in writings, journeys and verse. The entry gives his years as 1099 to 1162 AH, 1688 to 1749 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.

## His life and journeys
He was born in Damascus. Al-Zirikli says he travelled to Jerusalem in 1022 AH, visited Aleppo, Baghdad, Egypt, Constantinople and the Hijaz, and died in Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}. The year 1022 AH as printed falls before his birth, so the museum records no date for that journey. Al-Zirikli saw a manuscript of the collected letters of his journeys, in one large volume mostly in his hand, and refers readers to al-Muradi's history for the names of all his books {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «al-Suyuf al-hidad fi a'naq ahl al-zandaqa wa-l-ilhad» and «al-Dhakhira al-mahiya li-l-athaam fi al-salat ala khayr al-anam», both printed; «al-Mawrid al-adhb li-dhawi al-wurud fi kashf ma'na wahdat al-wujud», a manuscript treatise; «al-Salat al-hami'a», printed, on the merits of the four caliphs; «al-Fath al-qudsi», in manuscript, supplications; and «Balghat al-murid», printed, a rajaz poem on Sufism of 213 lines {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}. Also «Urjuza fi al-shama'il», in manuscript; «al-Tawasi bi-l-sabr wa-l-haqq», in manuscript, on Sufism; «Sharh al-qasida al-munfarija», in manuscript; and «Fawa'id al-fara'id», printed, a poem on the articles of belief, which al-Dardir commented on {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}. Also «al-Lamahat», printed, on the prayers of Ibn Mashish; «Manzumat al-istighfar», printed with a commentary; and «al-Manhal al-adhb al-sa'igh li-wuradih fi dhikr salawat al-tariq wa-awradih», printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 233}.
