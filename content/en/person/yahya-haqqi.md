## Overview
Yahya Haqqi, an Egyptian writer, born in the Sayyida Zaynab district of Cairo in January 1905, who obtained a degree in law. The encyclopedia records his death in 1992 CE {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
