## Summary
Junayd of Baghdad was a painter in the academy of [[person/ahmad-jalayir|Sultan Ahmad Jalayir]]; Norah Titley attributes probably all the paintings of [[object/khamsa-khvaju-kirmani-bl-add-18113|the manuscript of Khvaju Kirmani's poems]] (Baghdad, 1396) to him, on the strength of an inscription on one painting (folio 45b) naming him and Sultan Ahmad. {cite:titley-persian-miniature-painting-1983 p. 27}

## What is known of him
[[person/dust-muhammad-haravi|Dust Muhammad]] records that Junayd was a pupil of the painter Shams al-Din, who worked under Uvays (d. 1374); otherwise, Titley says, nothing is known of him. {cite:titley-persian-miniature-painting-1983 p. 27}
