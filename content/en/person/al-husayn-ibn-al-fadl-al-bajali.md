## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn al-Fadl al-Bajali, Abu Ali, of Kufa and then Nishapur, an exegete, philologist and hadith scholar, the leading authority of his age on the meanings of the Qur'an. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15815} Ibn Tahir brought him to Nishapur, where he taught and gave fatwas until his death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15815} He heard from Yazid ibn Harun, al-Hasan ibn Qutayba and Humayd al-Tawil, and Abu al-Tayyib Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak and Muhammad ibn Salih ibn Hani transmitted from him.
