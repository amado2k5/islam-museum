## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ahmad ibn Ibrahim, called al-Uqlidisi, a mathematician. He probably lived in Damascus, and his nickname is linked with the book attributed to Euclid. He died around 370 AH (980 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.255}
