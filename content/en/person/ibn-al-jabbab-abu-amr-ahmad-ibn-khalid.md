## Overview
Abu Amr Ahmad ibn Khalid ibn Yazid ibn al-Jabbab al-Jayyani al-Qurtubi. He was of Jaén origin and settled in Córdoba. He was born in 246 AH (860 CE) and died in 322 AH (933 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
