# Ramy Bensebaini

## Summary

Ramy Bensebaini is an Algerian professional footballer, born in Constantine, Algeria, on 16 April 1995. A defender who plays at left-back or centre-back, he came through the Paradou academy, played for Rennes in France and for Borussia Mönchengladbach in Germany, and joined Borussia Dortmund in 2023. For Algeria he has played around ninety times and won the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations. {cite:wikipedia-ramy-bensebaini-en} {cite:wikipedia-ramy-bensebaini-ar} {cite:wikidata-ramy-bensebaini}

## Career

Bensebaini grew up at CS Constantine before the Paradou academy took him in at eleven; he spent nine years there, with loans at Lierse in Belgium and Montpellier in France, before a permanent move to Rennes in 2016, where he won the Coupe de France in 2019. That summer he crossed to Borussia Mönchengladbach — scoring twice in a famous win over Bayern Munich that December — and in 2023 he moved on to Borussia Dortmund. {cite:wikipedia-ramy-bensebaini-en}

He debuted for Algeria in January 2017 and was part of the squad that won the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations in Egypt; by the time of his naming in Algeria's 2026 World Cup squad he had accumulated around ninety caps and ten goals. {cite:wikipedia-ramy-bensebaini-en}
