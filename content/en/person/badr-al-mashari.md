# Badr Al-Mashari

## Summary

Badr ibn Nadir al-Mashari (born 1973) is a Saudi Islamic preacher and the imam of the Hatin Mosque of the National Guard in Riyadh. He graduated from the College of Sharia at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University, studied under leading Saudi scholars, and became known across the Arab world through televised lesson series on the Bedaya and Iqraa channels and through recorded lectures carried by Islamic audio libraries. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar} {cite:wikidata-badr-al-mashari}

## Early life and education

His full name is Badr ibn Nadir ibn Sulayman al-Mashari al-Dosari. He was born in Riyadh in 1973, a year the Arabic article pairs with 1393 AH; no day or month is published. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar} {cite:wikidata-badr-al-mashari}

He graduated from the College of Sharia at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University. He went on to a master's degree whose subject was an introduction to the life and legacy of Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz, taken in Beirut, and to a doctorate; the article does not name the institution that awarded the doctorate. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}

## Teachers

The Arabic article lists the scholars under whom he studied: Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz, Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen, Salih al-Fawzan, Abd al-Rahman al-Barrak and Abdullah ibn Jibreen. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}

## Work

He worked in the National Guard in Riyadh, where he formerly headed its awareness and guidance section, and he serves as imam of the National Guard's Hatin Mosque in the capital. The article describes him as now a full-time preacher, with continuing lectures, lessons and dawah appearances, and notes that a number of his audio tapes and dawah pamphlets have been published. His lessons are hosted by the IslamWay and IslamWeb audio libraries, which the article links as its sources. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}

## Television series

Much of his reach came through lesson series on satellite television, all of them, as the article lists them, seasons of the Bedaya channel's programme "Zad Rasiduk" and one Ramadan series on Iqraa:

- "With the Messenger" (Zad Rasiduk 4, 2014–2015), a series of lessons on the life of the Prophet Muhammad, from the state of the world before his birth to his death. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}
- "Ministers of the Messenger" (Zad Rasiduk 5, 2015–2016), on Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}
- "Heirs of the Prophets" (Zad Rasiduk 6, 2016–2017), on figures including al-Hasan and al-Husayn, the four jurists Abu Hanifa, Malik, al-Shafi'i and Ahmad ibn Hanbal, and Bilal ibn Rabah. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}
- "Ahsan al-Qasas" (Zad Rasiduk 7, 2017), on Qur'anic narratives, among them Adam, Joseph, the Companions of the Cave, Mary and Moses. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}
- "Ka'annaka Tarah" (As If You See Him), thirty episodes on the biography of the Prophet Muhammad, shown on the Iqraa channel during Ramadan 2019. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}

## Arrest

The Arabic article reports that the Saudi security services arrested him on 17 July 2023, citing BBC News Arabic's report of 20 July 2023. The article further reports that he was released on 21 January 2026, but it flags that release claim itself as unreferenced; the museum therefore records the arrest as reported by BBC News Arabic and the release only as an unreferenced report in the article. {cite:wikipedia-badr-al-mashari-ar}
