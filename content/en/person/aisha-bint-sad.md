## Overview
Aisha bint Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. Al-Baladhuri, citing al-Waqidi from her, relates a report about the killing of [[person/hujr-ibn-adi|Hujr ibn Adi]] at the hands of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

She said that her father then said: "If Mu'awiya had seen what the Qantara of Hulwan's arches had, he would have known that he had great service to Islam." This saying is attributed to Sa'd in the report. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The dates of her life need an additional source.
