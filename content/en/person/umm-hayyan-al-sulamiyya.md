## Summary
Umm Hayyan al-Sulamiyya was a devout woman of [[place/basra|Basra]]; Ibn al-Jawzi gives her entry in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 599). {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## Long standing
He quotes Abu Khalda: "I never saw a man or a woman stronger or more patient in long standing prayer than Umm Hayyan al-Sulamiyya; she would stand in the mosque of the quarter like a palm tree buffeted by the winds to right and left." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## Her silence
From Sawada al-Sulamiyya: she read the Qur'an every day and night, and spoke only after the afternoon prayer, when she would give instructions about her needs and anything she wanted. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## For young visitors
Umm Hayyan was a woman of Basra who stood so long in prayer that she looked like a palm tree swaying in the wind, and she spoke very little.
