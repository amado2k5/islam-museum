## Overview
Istifan the monk was in Mosul in the time of a monk called Mikhabil, and it was related of him that he practised alchemy. According to Ibn al-Nadim, his books appeared in Mosul after his death. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.505-506}

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim saw among these books *Kitab al-Rushd* (The Book of Right Guidance), *Kitab al-Bab al-A'zam* (The Greatest Gate), the book of supplications and offerings used before the alchemical work, *Kitab al-Ikhtiyar al-Najumi li-l-Sina'a* (The Astral Selection for the Craft), *Kitab al-Ta'liqat* (The Book of Glosses), and *Kitab al-Awqat wa al-Azmina* (The Book of Times and Seasons). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.505-506}
