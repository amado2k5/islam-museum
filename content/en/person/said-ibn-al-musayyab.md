## Overview
Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, a senior Successor and one of the jurists of Medina. al-Raheeq al-Makhtum mentions him in its account of the livestock of the Jahiliyya that belonged to their idols {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.37}.

## In the introduction to al-Raheeq al-Makhtum
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum reports that Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab stated that the livestock of the bahira, the sa'iba, the wasila and the ham belonged to the idols of the people of the Jahiliyya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.37} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/499}. See [[concept/sacred-livestock-jahiliyya|the bahira and the sa'iba]].
