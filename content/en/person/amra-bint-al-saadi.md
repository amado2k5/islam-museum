## Summary
Wife of Malik ibn Rabi'a, of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy; she emigrated with him to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her the wife of Malik ibn Rabi'a. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5} He names her again listing the women of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-amir|Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
