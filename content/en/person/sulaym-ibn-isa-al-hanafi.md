## Overview

Abu 'Isa Sulaym ibn 'Isa ibn Sulaym ibn 'Amir ibn Ghalib al-Hanafi of Kufa was a pupil of [[person/hamza-al-zayyat|Hamza al-Zayyat]] and transmitter of his reading. He died in 188 AH, or 187 by another report. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177}

## His standing with Hamza

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an imam of the reading, exact, a careful verifier and skilled, the closest of Hamza's companions and the one who held most firmly to his letters, and the one who succeeded him in leading the reading. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177} He records Yahya ibn 'Abd al-Malik saying that when they were reading to Hamza and Sulaym came in, Hamza told them to take care and be steady, for Sulaym had arrived. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 177} From Sulaym the reading passed to [[person/khallad-ibn-khalid-al-sayrafi|Khallad]] and [[person/khalaf-ibn-hisham|Khalaf]]. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 175}
