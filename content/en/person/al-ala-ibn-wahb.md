## Overview
Al-Ala ibn Wahb ibn Abd ibn Wahban, of the Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governorship of Mah and Hamadhan
Al-Baladhuri relates that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas appointed him over Mah and Hamadhan for Uthman. The people of Hamadhan betrayed and broke their pact, and he fought them until they came down under his judgement. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
He made peace with them on the land tax, the poll tax on heads, and a hundred thousand dirhams for the Muslims. He stipulated that they should not be harmed in property, honour or children. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data do not establish the date of these events, which needs an additional source.
