## Overview
A Successor and judge among the transmitters. He was from Kufa, lived in Basra and held the judgeship of Marw. He transmitted from his father, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Umar and Abu Hurayra, and Ibn Ma'in, al-Ijli and Abu Hatim graded him trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5154}

## Note on the dates
The heading reads «(115 - 14 AH)» in the OCR and is read as death then birth; the reading needs the page image.
