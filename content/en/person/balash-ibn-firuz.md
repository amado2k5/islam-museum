## Overview
Balash ibn Firuz, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari gives him the heading "The reign of Balash ibn Firuz". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.90}

The details of the years of his reign have not yet been read in the museum's data, and need an additional source.
