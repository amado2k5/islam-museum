## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Yahya al-Kindi, an Ibadi chief of the Kharijites who called himself Talib al-Haqq and was hailed Commander of the Faithful in 129 AH; Abu Hamza summoned to him and preached his cause at Mecca and Medina, so he marched from San'a, met Marwan's army near al-Ta'if in the land of Jurash, and was killed in a great battle with most of the Ibadis.
