## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah, Abu Bakr al-Zaqqaq, one of the shaykhs of the Sufis; among the sayings transmitted from him: 'For seventy years I have tended this poverty — whoever does not accompany scrupulousness in his poverty eats raw unlawfulness', per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 13 p. 19}
