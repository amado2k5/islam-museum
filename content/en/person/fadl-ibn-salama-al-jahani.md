## Summary
Fadl ibn Salama ibn Jarir ibn Munkhul, Abu Salama al-Jahani, from Bijaya, originally from Ilbira, a Maliki jurist. He studied with teachers of his city and of Ifriqiya, and kept company with Hammas and men of learning in law; he was known as a memoriser of the Maliki school. The book gives 319 AH as his death year. His works include al-Wadiha, al-Muwazzia, al-Mustakhraja, and an abridgement of the Mudawwana. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15415}
