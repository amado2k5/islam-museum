## Overview
Kharrzad ibn Bas rebelled against al-Hajjaj and joined Ayadhaj, where he took refuge in a fortress known by his name. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His end
When the siege dragged on, he came down in disguise, intending to join Abd al-Malik. Mukarram ibn al-Farz caught him, with two pearls in his cap, and sent him to al-Hajjaj, who had his neck struck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
