## Overview
Sa'id ibn Sariya al-Khuza'i, appointed over Azerbaijan by Ali. According to al-Baladhuri, al-Ash'ath ibn Qays al-Kindi was then appointed over it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
