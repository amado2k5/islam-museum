## Overview
Sawra ibn al-Hurr al-Hanzali, a commander from the Umayyad period. Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz, and after him his son, sent him beyond the Oxus {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Beyond the Oxus
He stayed at Ishtikhan after the Turks had come to it. He fought them and defeated them, and for a time prevented the people from pursuing them. Then he met the Turks again, and they defeated him and inflicted heavy killing on his companions {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
