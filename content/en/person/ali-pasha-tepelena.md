## Summary
Ali Pasha of Tepelena, the master of Yanya (Ioannina), son of the Tepelena notable Veli Bey {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Ali Paşa Tepedelen'}.

On Kurt Pasha's recommendation he was made mutasarrıf of Yanya with mirmiran rank at his father's death, then governor of Yanya, Yenişehir, Manastır and Selanik, amassing over two hundred estates and defeating the French; rewarded with the vizierate in Jumada I 1225/June 1810 and made Rumeli governor and serasker in Dhu'l-Hijja 1226. He died in 1237/1821–22 and was buried at Silivrikapı with his children, aged past eighty {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 1, 'Ali Paşa Tepedelen'}.
