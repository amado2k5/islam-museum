Fawad Afzal Khan (born 29 November 1981) is a Pakistani actor, producer, screenwriter and singer. He first became known as lead singer of the alternative rock band Entity Paradigm, whose debut album Irtiqa appeared in 2003, before turning to acting full-time.{cite:wikipedia-fawad-khan-en} His film debut came in Khuda Kay Liye (2007), and he rose to wide fame with the television dramas Dastaan (2010), Humsafar (2011) and Zindagi Gulzar Hai (2012).{cite:wikipedia-fawad-khan-en} He entered Indian cinema with Khoobsurat (2014), winning the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut, and later headlined the Pakistani blockbuster The Legend of Maula Jatt (2022).{cite:wikipedia-fawad-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-fawad-khan}
