## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Muhibb al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Fahd al-Makki al-Hashimi, known by the title "Jar Allah". A Meccan historian of the Banu Hashim, whose lineage connects to Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya. He was born in 891 AH (1486 CE) and died in 954 AH (1547 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.39}

His reports and his books need an additional source.
