## Overview
Abu al-Husayn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Asad ibn Abi Bakr al-Asbahi al-Ta'izi, a Yemeni jurist and hadith scholar from Ta'iz. The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" says that he excelled in jurisprudence, studied hadith, and studied jurisprudence under a number of the shaykhs of his age. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.74}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia gives his birth as around 644 AH and his death as around 703 AH. Both dates come from that source alone and need a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.74}
