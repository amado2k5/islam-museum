## Overview
Abu Dhu'ayb Khuwaylid ibn Khalid al-Hudhali, a poet. He went out in the army of Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh to Ifriqiya in the raid of Uthman, and died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death in Ifriqiya
Al-Baladhuri says that Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr took charge of his affair until he buried him in his grave. He is among the participants in [[event/first-raid-on-ifriqiya|the first raid on Ifriqiya]], and his death is mentioned there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
