## Overview
Asim, or Ibn Asim, a man of the Banu Tamim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Kharijites in Sistan
Al-Baladhuri says that he was the first to call the people of Sistan to the opinion of the Kharijites. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Notes
- The name is uncertain between "Asim" and "Ibn Asim". The only source is "Futuh al-Buldan", and it needs an additional source and a page number.
