## Overview
Mahmud ibn Abi Bakr ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Thana', Siraj al-Din al-Urmawi, was a Shafi'i scholar of legal theory and logic. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 594 AH, 1198 CE, and his death as 682 AH, 1283 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 166}.

## Life
His family came from Urmiya in the lands of Azerbaijan. He studied in Mosul, settled in Damascus, and died in Konya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 166}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists: Matali' al-Anwar, on logic, printed and the subject of many commentaries; al-Tahsil min al-Mahsul, on legal theory, in manuscript; Lata'if al-Hikma, in manuscript; a commentary on the Isharat of Ibn Sina; a commentary on the Wajiz of al-Ghazali, on derived rulings of law; Bayan al-Haqq, on logic and philosophy, in manuscript; and Lubab al-Arba'in fi Usul al-Din, in manuscript, held in the Chester Beatty collection under number 7351 {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 166}.
