## Overview
Amr ibn Ubayd ibn Bab, a client of the Banu Tamim, known as Abu Uthman. He was among the shaykhs of the Mu'tazila and of the companions of [[person/wasil-ibn-ata|Wasil ibn Ata]]. He died in the days of al-Mansur in 144 AH, and it is said 145 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.214-216}

## What al-Mas'udi relates
Al-Mas'udi relates a saying of his about Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz: that he took the caliphate without its right, and then deserved it by justice when he took it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.184} Al-Mas'udi also has reports of him with al-Mansur. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.214-216}

## What Ibn al-Nadim relates
A report in Ibn al-Nadim says that he withdrew from the session of Qatada after the death of al-Hasan, and Qatada called his group the Mu'tazila. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.557} In another place there is a report of his finding fault with Wasil for his long neck, Wasil's answer, and their debate. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim supplement p.525}

The data need an additional source: each report above rests on a single source.
