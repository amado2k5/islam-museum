## Overview
Abu Dawud Sulayman b. Najah of al-Andalus, mawla of the Umayyad al-Mu'ayyad bi'llah — muqri' of al-Andalus and companion of Abu 'Amr al-Dani, the noblest, most learned and most prolific of his companions. He died in Ramadan 496 at eighty-three. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 403-404}
