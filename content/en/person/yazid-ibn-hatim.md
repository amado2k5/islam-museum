## Overview
Yazid ibn Hatim ibn Qabisa ibn al-Muhallab. He was appointed over Ifriqiya after Hazarmard, and set out with fifty thousand men. [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] escorted him to Jerusalem and spent great wealth on him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.326}.

## Ifriqiya
He killed Abu Khatim the Ibadi at Batir Iblis, and entered Ifriqiya, and it was settled for him. After him, Rawh ibn Hatim was appointed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.326}. See [[event/yazid-ibn-hatim-ifriqiya|the appointment of Yazid ibn Hatim over Ifriqiya]].
