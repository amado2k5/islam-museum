## Overview
The shaykh and imam Shihab al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Isma'il, known as Abu Shama al-Dimashqi, who died in 665 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.372-373}

## His works
He is the author of [[work/azhar-al-rawdatayn|Azhar al-Rawdatayn]], on the history of the two states, that of Nur al-Din and that of Saladin. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.372-373}

## Note
The date of death comes from "Kashf al-Zunun" alone. The Gregorian conversion is derived and needs a second source.
