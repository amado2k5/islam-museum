## Summary
Ibn Qatral, chief qadi; he held the judgeship of 'Umayd for a time, and on the day the Franks took 'Umayd in 609 he was a captive in their hands — he escaped, travelled to al-Andalus, and held the qada' of Jativa, then Cordoba, then finally Fez; he died at Marrakesh aged eighty-eight. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 261}
