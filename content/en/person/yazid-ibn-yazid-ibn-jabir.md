## Summary
Yazid ibn Yazid ibn Jabir al-Azdi al-Dimashqi, a trustworthy jurist, scholar and memoriser who transmitted from Makhul and Busr ibn Ubayd Allah. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2073}

## Works
His brother Abd al-Rahman, al-Awza'i and the two Sufyans transmitted from him; he died in 134 AH, or earlier. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2073}
