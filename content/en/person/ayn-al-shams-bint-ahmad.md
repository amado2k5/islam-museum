## Overview
Ayn al-Shams bint Ahmad ibn Abi al-Faraj, the woman jurist of Isfahan, is listed by Ibn al-Imad among the deaths of 610 AH, about 1213 CE (derived conversion), and he says she died in Rabi' al-Akhir {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 79}.

## Life
Ibn al-Imad says she heard by attendance in the year 'twenty-four' (the printed passage does not give the century) from Isma'il ibn al-Ikhshid, and heard from Abu Dharr, to whom the editor adds al-Salihani in square brackets, and that she was the last to narrate from the two {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 79}.
