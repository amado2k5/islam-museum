## Overview
Al-Malik al-Muhsin 'Ayn al-Din Ahmad b. the sultan Salah al-Din; he transmitted from Ibn Sadqa al-Harrani and al-Busiri, devoted himself fully to hadith and wrote much — humble, ascetic, generous to the muhaddithin, with a slight Shi'i leaning; he died at Aleppo in Muharram. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 162}
