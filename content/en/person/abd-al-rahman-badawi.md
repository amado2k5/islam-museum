## Overview
Abd al-Rahman Badawi (1917–2002 CE) was an Egyptian professor of philosophy, and one of the most important historians of Arabic philosophy. He helped establish the study of philosophy in the Arab world. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
