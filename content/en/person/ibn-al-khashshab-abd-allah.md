## Overview
Ibn al-Khashshab, Abdallah ibn Ahmad ibn Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Nasr al-Khashshab, a grammarian from Baghdad. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* says that he was born around 492 AH (1099 CE) and died around 567 AH (1171 CE). These two dates need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The sources report only a few of his accounts. The rest needs an additional source.
