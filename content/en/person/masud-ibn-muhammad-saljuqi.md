## Overview
Mas'ud ibn Muhammad ibn Malikshah, an Iraqi Seljuq sultan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}

## With his nephew
He rose against his nephew Dawud ibn Mahmud when he was installed in place of his father in 525 AH. Then they agreed to share power, and Mas'ud was given the sultanate in the khutba at Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}

## With the caliph al-Mustarshid
A rift arose between him and the caliph al-Mustarshid. The caliph overcame him and took him captive. His uncle Sanjar sent to him, ordering that he be restored, and he kissed the ground before the caliph and was released. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}
