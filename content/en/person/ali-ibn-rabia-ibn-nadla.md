## Summary
Ali ibn Rabi'ah ibn Nadla, Abu al-Mughira, of Kufa, of the clan of Bajila. He narrated from Ali ibn Abi Talib, al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba and Samura ibn Jundub, and from him al-Hakam ibn Utba and Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i, among others; Ibn Ma'in, al-Nasa'i and al-Ijli rated him reliable. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7521}
