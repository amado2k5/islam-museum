## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Shakir al-Irbili, Majd al-Din, known as Ibn al-Zahir, was a poet and littérateur of the Hanafi jurists. Born at Irbil, he moved about Iraq and Syria and died at Damascus in 677 AH (1278 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.322}

He wrote Tadhkirat al-Arib wa-Tabsirat al-Adib, an abridgement of al-Sharif al-Radi's proverbs, and a diwan of poetry in two volumes. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.322}
