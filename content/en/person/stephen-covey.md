## Summary
Stephen Covey, the American trainer whom Tareq al-Suwaidan cites as his teacher on motivation: invited to address a company's annual ceremony — at which fifty of its nine hundred and eighty employees had just been honoured — he asked its head: when you hired them, did you pick successes or failures? And if you picked successes, what happened to them while working with you that they now count as failures {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:41:00}.
