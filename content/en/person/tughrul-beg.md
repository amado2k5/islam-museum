## Overview
Tughrul Beg, Muhammad ibn Mikail the Seljuk. al-Suyuti counts him as the first Seljuk sultan to rule Baghdad {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.642-645}.

## His entry into Baghdad
al-Suyuti reports that al-Qa'im wrote to him to come when al-Basasiri had resolved to seize him, and he came in the year 447 AH. He occupied himself with his brother Yinal, whom al-Basasiri had encouraged with his office. He overcame Yinal and killed him, returned the caliph honourably to his house, and al-Basasiri was defeated and killed {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.642-645}.

## His marriage and death
al-Qa'im gave him his daughter in marriage in the year 454 AH, and he came in the year 455 AH and consummated the marriage. He restored the inheritances and the taxes, and guaranteed Baghdad for a hundred and fifty thousand dinars. Then he returned to al-Rayy and died there in Ramadan. His nephew [[person/alp-arslan|Alp Arslan]] was set up after him {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.642-645}.
