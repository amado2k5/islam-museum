## Overview
Of the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]], and he was in their delegation that came with [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] in 10 AH, as Ibn Ishaq names him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

He accepted Islam with his people at the hands of Khalid, in [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|the mission of Khalid to the Banu al-Harith]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p. 240}
