## Summary
Prince Bayezid ibn Sulayman the Ottoman — killed by Shah Tahmasp at the order of his own father Sultan Sulayman, in 961 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 329}.

## Details
Under the dead of 961 Ibn al-'Imad notes "the sultan Bayezid ibn Sulayman the Ottoman: Shah Tahmasp killed him at the order of his father Sultan Sulayman" — he was killed in his father's lifetime, and the stories told about him are famous {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 329}. The wording shows Bayezid had fallen into the hands of the Safavid Shah Tahmasp, who put him to death in compliance with his father's demand — which made his fate one of the best-known episodes of succession strife in the Ottoman house {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 329}. The same year's dead include Burhan Nizam Shah, sultan of the Deccan, and Salim Shah ibn Shir Shah, so that, as the chronicler remarks, these three died together in this year {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 329}.
