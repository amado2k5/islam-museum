## Overview
Bahmata, the great man of Abward. He came to Ibn Amir and made peace with him over Abward for four hundred thousand. It is also said that Ibn Amir sent Abd Allah ibn Khazim there, and he made peace with its people for four hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
