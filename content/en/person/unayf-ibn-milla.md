## Overview
Of the [[group/banu-dubayb|Dubayb]] of Judham, brother of [[person/hassan-ibn-milla|Hassan ibn Milla]]. He rode with him and with [[person/abu-zayd-ibn-amr-al-dubaybi|Abu Zayd ibn Amr]] to the army of Zayd ibn Haritha, on the horse of Milla Raghal. His two companions feared his tongue, and they agreed that only Hassan should speak {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262}. When a horseman of the army drove them, he uttered a word of pre-Islamic speech to urge them on to fight, and Hassan restrained him, and rode that night to Rifa'a ibn Zayd {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/263}.

## Notes on the report
- The report comes from the context of [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|the raid on Judham]] in Ibn Ishaq, without a named chain. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262}
