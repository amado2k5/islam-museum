## Overview
Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb al-Nasa'i, a hadith scholar from Nasa in Khurasan, author of the "Sunan" counted among the six books. {cite:sunan-al-nasai} See [[work/sunan-al-nasai|the Sunan of al-Nasa'i]].

## His life
He was born around 215 AH and died around 303 AH; the two dates are approximate in the data. {cite:sunan-al-nasai}

He became known for his care for the defects of hadith and the authentication of its transmitters, so that his book was ranked first in precision. In the last part of his life he moved to Egypt and then to Syria, where he died. {cite:sunan-al-nasai}
