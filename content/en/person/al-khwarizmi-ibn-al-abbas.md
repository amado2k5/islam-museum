## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad b. al-'Abbas al-Khwarizmi, the famous poet — also called al-Tabarkhuzi because his father was of Khwarizm and his mother of Tabaristan — nephew of Abu Ja'far al-Tabari the historian; born in 323 per al-Tha'alibi in the Yatima; among the great excellent poets and an imam in lexicon and genealogies — he dwelt in Syria a while, settling near Aleppo. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## At al-Sahib's gate
It is told that he sought al-Sahib Ibn 'Abbad, and the chamberlain said his master had bound himself to admit no litterateur who had not memorised twenty thousand verses of Arab poetry; al-Khwarizmi said: "Twenty thousand of men's verse, or of women's?" Al-Sahib said: "This one means to be Abu Bakr al-Khwarizmi" — and admitted him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
