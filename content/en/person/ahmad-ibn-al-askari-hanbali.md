## Summary

Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn al-Askari al-Salihi al-Dimashqi al-Hanbali, the Hanbali mufti of Damascus. He was a pious, ascetic and blessed man. He wrote out his fatwas with great care. In his time he had no equal in learning, humility and simple living in the manner of the early generations. He kept apart from people and mixed with them rarely {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 465}.

He wrote a book of jurisprudence that combined two standard Hanbali works, al-Muqni and al-Tanqih. He died before finishing it, in Dhu al-Hijja of the year 912 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 466}.
