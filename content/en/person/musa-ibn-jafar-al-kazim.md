## Overview
Musa ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad — al-Kazim — who was imprisoned under al-Rashid; the caliph dreamt an Abyssinian threatened him with a spear unless he released Musa at once, so he sent to Abd Allah ibn Malik al-Khuza'i ordering his release. He died at Baghdad in 186 AH aged fifty-four.
