## Overview
Musa ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad — al-Kazim — who was imprisoned under al-Rashid; the caliph dreamt an Abyssinian threatened him with a spear unless he released Musa at once, so he sent to Abd Allah ibn Malik al-Khuza'i ordering his release. He died at Baghdad in 186 AH aged fifty-four.

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam says Musa al-Kazim, who died in 799, is recognised by the Twelver Shia as their seventh imam, the successor of his father [[person/jafar-al-sadiq|Jafar al-Sadiq]]. It adds that some of his followers pinned apocalyptic hopes on him and insisted that his death was impossible before those hopes came true, while others accepted his son [[person/ali-al-rida|Ali al-Rida]] as imam after him. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1249} This is the Twelver position as the Dictionary reports it, not a Sunni one. The year 799 matches the date held in the record's data, while the record's text gives 186 AH.
