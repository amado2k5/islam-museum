## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Umar al-Qawariri, a jurist from Baghdad, who is mentioned in the report of the [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|Mihna]] as one who was tested. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 490,492-493}

## In the Mihna
Al-Suyuti relates that he refused during the trial and was fettered, then answered on the third occasion. In al-Ma'mun's letter to his agent he blamed him for accepting bribes and flattery; this judgement is attributed to al-Ma'mun's letter as al-Suyuti quotes it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 490,492-493}
