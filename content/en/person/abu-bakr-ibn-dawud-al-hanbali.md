## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Dawud al-Hanbali; Ibn al-Jawzi records that Abu Ja'far ibn Jarir al-Tabari attended the residence of Ali ibn Isa in Dhu'l-Qa'da 309 AH for a debate with the Hanbalis — he came but they did not, so he returned home; they had already resented several things about him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 449-450}
