## Overview
A jurist from Fars. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his date of death or those who took from him. The editor's note says that the name is in one edition "al-Khayyat" (the tailor), and that it is vowelled with the unpointed letter in manuscript "ayn". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}

He is not [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-hannati-al-tabari|Abu Abd Allah al-Hannati al-Tabari]].
