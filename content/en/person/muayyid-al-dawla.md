## Overview
Mu'ayyid al-Dawla, son of Rukn al-Dawla. His father gave him al-Rayy and Isfahan when he handed his realms over to his sons in 365 AH, and he died in 373 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.627,630}
