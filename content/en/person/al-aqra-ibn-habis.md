## Summary
Chief of Banu Tamim in the Jahiliyya and one of the Arabs' sages; he became Muslim after the conquest of Mecca, the Prophet gave him a hundred camels at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]] within the share of [[concept/al-muallafa-qulubuhum|those whose hearts are won over]], and he afterwards became a striving Companion who witnessed the Ridda wars and the conquest of Khurasan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
The Prophet gave him a hundred camels from the Hunayn booty to win his heart and seek the Islam of Banu Tamim's great tribes in Najd {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}. His Islam afterwards proved good; he stood firm in the Ridda wars with Khalid ibn al-Walid, marched in the Islamic conquests, and was martyred in the land of Juzjan in Khurasan in Uthman's caliphate {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/136}.

## For young visitors
A great chief of Tamim in Najd; the Prophet met him with generosity and esteem, his Islam became good, and he became a commander of the Islamic conquests.
