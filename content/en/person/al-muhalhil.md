## Overview
Al-Muhalhil Adi ibn Rabi'a al-Taghlibi, known by the kunya Abu Layla and the title "Zir Salim", a poet of the [[group/banu-taghlib|Banu Taghlib]] and the brother of [[person/kulayb-ibn-rabia|Kulayb ibn Rabi'a]]. Ibn al-Athir says that he was the father of Layla, the mother of Amr ibn Kulthum. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.392}

## Vengeance for Kulayb
When [[person/jasas-ibn-murra|Jassas ibn Murra]] killed his brother Kulayb, al-Muhalhil sought vengeance. The war of al-Basus broke out between him and Bakr, and Ibn al-Athir says it lasted forty years. He makes him a proverb among the Arabs for the length of war and the patience of vengeance. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.392}
