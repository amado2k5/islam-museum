## Summary
The king of Portugal who perished at Wadi al-Makhazin {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 260-267}.

## The muster
He accepted al-Mutawakkil's offer of Morocco's coasts for his uncle's expulsion and gathered tens of thousands — estimates run from eighty to a hundred and twenty-five thousand — of Spanish, Portuguese, Italians and Germans in a thousand ships, desiring, in al-Salabi's words, a holy war to raise his name among Europe's kings {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 260-261}.

## The end
To Abd al-Malik's taunting letter he answered by delay against his commanders' counsel, and was killed on the field, 30 Jumada II 986/4 August 1578; Portugal's star sank, as her own historian Luís de Menezes laments {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 262-267}.
