## Overview
Salih, son of the Imam Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hanbal al-Shaybani al-Baghdadi, Abu al-Fadl, is described by al-Zirikli as a judge. The entry gives his years as 203 to 265 AH, 818 to 878 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 187}. The entry is short and says nothing beyond what follows.

## His life
He was born in Baghdad, grew up in the care of his father, the Imam Ahmad, and took from him. He then held the judgeship of Isfahan and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 187}.
