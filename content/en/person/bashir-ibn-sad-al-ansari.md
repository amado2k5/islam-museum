## Overview
Bashir ibn Sa'd al-Ansari. Al-Baladhuri relates that Khalid ibn al-Walid sent him to Banqiya, where Persian horsemen with Farrukhbundadh met him. He defeated them and killed Farrukhbundadh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342}

He then turned back with a wound that reopened while he was at Ayn al-Tamr, and he died of it, in 12 AH according to the source; the Gregorian conversion is derived. It is said that Khalid himself met Farrukhbundadh, with Bashir alongside him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342}

He had testified with Muhammad ibn Maslama to Khurraym ibn Aws's claim to the daughter of Buqayla. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.342} See [[event/baniqya-sulh|the treaty of Baniqya]].

Note: there is a close entry, Bashir ibn Sa'd, who may be the same person; the data do not settle this and it needs an additional source.
