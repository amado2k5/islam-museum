## Overview
Abhala ibn Ka'b, nicknamed al-Aswad al-Ansi and "Dhu al-Khimar" — a Yemeni soothsayer who claimed prophethood, ruled San'a' and most of Yemen for a few weeks, then was killed by those who had first served him.

## Life
Al-Aswad rose late in the Prophet's life claiming revelation; he drove the Prophet's agents out of Yemen and took San'a' and what lay under it — the first seeds of the Ridda and the closest to the Prophet's death {cite:tabari-donner-1993}.

Then the Persian Abna' of Yemen agreed with his household and his emissaries against him; [[person/fayruz-al-daylami|Fayruz al-Daylami]] and his people came upon him by night and killed him — the account places his death nights before the Prophet's death {cite:tabari-donner-1993} {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}.

## Significance
He ruled Yemen for weeks and left neither religion nor state — a fair example of how Ridda movements flared and died out in proportion to the leader who gathered around him, not to their creed.

## For young visitors
A man ruled Yemen a few weeks claiming prophethood — then those nearest to him came and killed him in the night.
