## Overview
Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub, known by the title al-Malik al-Nasir, nephew of Asad al-Din Shirkuh. al-Suyuti reports that he took Alexandria in the year 562 AH, and then became vizier of Egypt in the year 564 AH, after his uncle. In the year 567 AH he ended the Fatimid sermon in Egypt and established the sermon for al-Mustadi. He ordered the building of the wall of Cairo, the citadel of the mountain, and the tomb of Imam al-Shafi'i {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679-685}.

## Campaigns and death
In the year 583 AH he took many of the Syrian lands, the greatest of them being Jerusalem. He died in the year 589 AH, leaving no more money than one dinar and thirty-six dirhams {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.690-693}.

Shadharat al-Dhahab says that he died in the year 589 AH, before reaching sixty years of age. He had sixteen sons and one daughter. It is said that he conquered sixty fortresses {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.779-780}.
