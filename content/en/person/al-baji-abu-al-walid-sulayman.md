## Overview
Abu al-Walid Sulayman ibn Khalaf ibn Sa'd ibn Ayyub al-Tujibi al-Baji, an Andalusian from Badajoz, a Maliki. The encyclopaedia describes him as a hadith scholar, jurist, man of letters, poet, theologian and expert in the principles of jurisprudence. It gives his birth as 403 AH and his death as 474 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.40}

## His journey
Al-Maqqari says he came to Baghdad and stayed three years studying jurisprudence and reading hadith. He met Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari, Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi, al-Sayymari and Ibn Amrus. He stayed a year in Mosul with Abu Ja'far al-Samnani, from whom he took the science of theology. He and al-Khatib al-Baghdadi narrated from each other. He returned to al-Andalus thirteen years later with much learning, gained with poverty and abstinence. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.73}
