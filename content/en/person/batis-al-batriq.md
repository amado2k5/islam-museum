## Overview
Batis, a great patrician of Amorium. Al-Mu'tasim captured him when he took the city in 223 AH, and he was crucified at the Gate of the Commons in Samarra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}

Al-Mas'udi says that his cross leaned towards the crosses of Babak and al-Mazyar, and he quotes a verse of Abu Tammam on that. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}
