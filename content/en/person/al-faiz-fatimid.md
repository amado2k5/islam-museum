## Overview
Al-Fa'iz bi-Nasr Allah Isa ibn al-Zafir, the thirteenth of the rulers of the Ubaydid state in al-Suyuti's count. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.674}

## His reign
They installed him after his father al-Zafir in 549 AH, when he was a small boy, and the authority of the Egyptians weakened. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.674}

He died at the start of the caliphate of al-Mustanjid, and after him came al-Adid li-Din Allah, the last of the Banu Ubayd caliphs. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.679}

The terms "Ubaydid" and "Banu Ubayd" are al-Suyuti's, and are not the museum's own naming. The exact Hijri dating of his accession and death needs an additional source.
