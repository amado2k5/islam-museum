## Summary
Muhammad Amin ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz Ibn Abidin of Damascus, the leading Hanafi of the Levant in his time, author of Radd al-muhtar 'ala al-Durr al-mukhtar, known as Hashiyat Ibn Abidin. The entry also names his son Muhammad 'Ala' al-Din (1244-1306 AH), author of Qurrat 'uyun al-akhyar, a supplement to the father's gloss. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1603}
