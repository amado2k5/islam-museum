## Summary
Muhammad Amin ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz Ibn Abidin of Damascus, the leading Hanafi of the Levant in his time, author of Radd al-muhtar 'ala al-Durr al-mukhtar, known as Hashiyat Ibn Abidin. The entry also names his son Muhammad 'Ala' al-Din (1244-1306 AH), author of Qurrat 'uyun al-akhyar, a supplement to the father's gloss. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1603}

## Author of Radd al-Muhtar

His gloss [[work/radd-al-muhtar|Radd al-Muhtar ala al-Durr al-Mukhtar]] is on [[person/al-haskafi-muhammad-ibn-ali|al-Haskafi]]'s commentary on the text Tanwir al-Absar; the title page of its volume 3 names him "the learned Ibn Abidin" and its index "al-Sayyid Muhammad Amin, known as Ibn Abidin". {cite:radd-al-muhtar-scan-v3 p.20} {cite:radd-al-muhtar-scan-v3 p.3} In it he follows the commentary phrase by phrase, reports the statements of the books of the [[group/hanafi-madhhab|Hanafi school]] and then gives his own view with "I say". {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.9} {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.18}

He refers in it to separate treatises of his, among them [[work/nashr-al-urf|Nashr al-Urf]] and [[work/tanbih-al-ruqud|Tanbih al-Ruqud fi Ahkam al-Nuqud]], calling each "our treatise". {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.21} {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.31} He attends to the custom of his time, such as buying in piastres, giving up posts for money and key-money for shops, and in the chapter on rebels mentions an event of his own day in 1233 AH, in which he describes, with his own judgement, the followers of the movement that came out of Najd. {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.31} {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.22} {cite:radd-al-muhtar-scan-v3 p.497} See [[concept/baghy-fiqh|rebels]].

## His son and the end of the gloss

At the end of volume 4 of the Bulaq print is a note by his son Muhammad Ala' al-Din, who calls him "our master the author, the late, my father al-Sayyid Muhammad Efendi Abidin" and says that what closes the volume is the last he found on his father's copy; it is dated 25 Safar 1260 AH. {cite:radd-al-muhtar-bulaq-v4 p.528}
