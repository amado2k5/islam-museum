## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Abi Mulayka al-Tamimi al-Makki, a trustworthy follower with many transmitted hadith; he was imam and trusted muezzin of the sanctuary. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2047}

## Works
He met about thirty Companions, was made judge of al-Ta'if by Ibn al-Zubayr, and died in 117 or 118 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2047}
