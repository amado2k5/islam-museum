# Abdullah Awad Al-Juhany

**Abdullah ibn Awad ibn Fahd al-Dhubyani al-Juhany** is a Saudi Qur'an reciter and an imam and khatib of Masjid al-Haram in Mecca. He was born in Medina on 13 January 1976, corresponding to 11 Muharram 1396 AH. He memorised the Qur'an as a boy at the Ashraf mosque in al-Harrah al-Gharbiyah in Medina, and at sixteen took a tied first place in the international Qur'an competition held in Mecca {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-ar} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-en}.

He took his bachelor's degree from the faculty of Qur'an at the Islamic University of Madinah, then a master's and a doctorate in tafsir and Qur'anic sciences at Umm al-Qura University in Mecca {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-en} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-ar}.

He was assigned to lead the Tarawih prayers at Masjid al-Haram for Ramadan in 1426 and 1427 AH and has led them there since 2005; in July 2007 he was appointed a permanent imam of the mosque. His recorded recitation is distributed internationally {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-ar} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-awad-al-juhany-en}.
