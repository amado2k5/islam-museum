## Overview
Abu'l-'Abbas Ibn Wasil; he served as a tax-farmer and it came out in the reckoning that he would rule — they mocked him, one saying "when you become king, employ me," another "give me a robe of honour." {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.236-237}

## His rule
And he did rule: Siraf, then Basra; he marched on Ahwaz, defeated Baha' al-Dawla, took the Batihah and drove Muhadhdhib al-Dawla 'Ali b. Nasr out to Baghdad. Baha' al-Dawla, released with what wealth could be carried, lost it on the road and was reduced to riding a cow; he made for Ibn Wasil, sought refuge with Hassan b. Thamal al-Khafaji who sent him to 'Ali's shrine where he gave much alms, then set out for Badr b. Hasanwayh his friend — Abu'l-Fath Ibn 'Anaz robbed him and handed him to Baha' al-Dawla's men, who killed him at Wasit in Safar 397. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.236-237}
