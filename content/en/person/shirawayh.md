## Overview
Shirawayh al-Iswari. He married Marjana, mother of Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad, and built her a palace with many doors, which was called "Hazardar" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The name
Opinions differ on the reason for the name. It is said that it was because he made a thousand doors in it. It is also said that it was because a thousand guards settled the site in a thousand houses which Khusraw had assigned to them {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
