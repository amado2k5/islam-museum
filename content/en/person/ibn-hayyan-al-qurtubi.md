## Overview
Abu Marwan Ibn Hayyan, the Andalusian historian. Al-Maqqari cites him dozens of times in *Nafh al-Tib*, on the conquest of al-Andalus and the reports of the Umayyads there. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.189}

## His books
Al-Maqqari attributes to him *al-Muqtabas fi Akhbar Balad al-Andalus* (The Borrowed Fire on the Reports of the Land of al-Andalus), *al-Muqtabas fi Tarikh Rijal al-Andalus* (on the history of its men), *Kitab al-Matin*, *Kitab al-Dawla al-Amiriyya* (The Book of the Amirid State), and *al-Tarikh al-Kabir fi Akhbar Ahl al-Andalus* (The Great History of the Reports of the People of al-Andalus). {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.189}

## What al-Maqqari relays from him
In his view, the great bridge of Córdoba was built by order of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.189}

Whoever wishes to understand the strife of Barcelona and Badajoz should turn to the history of Ibn Hayyan. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.354}
