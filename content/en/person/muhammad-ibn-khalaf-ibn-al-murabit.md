## Summary
Muhammad ibn Khalaf ibn Sa'id ibn Wahb, Abu Abd Allah, of al-Mariyya, known as Ibn al-Murabit, a Maliki jurist who was mufti and judge of Almeria. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15810} Abu Umar al-Talamanki and Abu Amr al-Dani gave him licences, and he heard from Abu al-Qasim al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra and Abu al-Walid ibn Muqbil. Students including Abu Abd Allah al-Tamimi, Abu Ali ibn Sakara and Abu Muhammad al-Sabti took from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15810} His works include a large commentary on Sahih al-Bukhari, a gloss on al-Mudawwana and al-Wusul ila al-gharad.
