## Overview
The servant to whom God gave knowledge from Himself, whom [[person/musa|Musa]] accompanied on the journey to the meeting of the two seas. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.3401}

Al-Bukhari relates his whole story from Ibn Abbas, from Ubayy ibn Ka'b. In it he gives Ibn Abbas's answer to Nawf al-Bakali, who claimed that the Musa who accompanied al-Khidr was not the Musa of the Children of Israel. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.3401}
