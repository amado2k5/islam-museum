## Overview
Hashim ibn Sulayman ibn Isma'il al-Husayni al-Bahrani al-Katkani al-Tubli was an Imami (Twelver Shi'i) exegete, and al-Zirikli labels him so. His name points to Tubli and Katkan, villages of Bahrain, and his tomb is in the first. He is known as al-Bahrani, as he wrote of himself at the end of Idah al-Mustarshidin, in manuscript, on the lives of those who returned to the wilayat of the Commander of the Faithful. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1107 AH, 1696 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 64}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books al-Burhan fi Tafsir al-Qur'an, printed in two volumes; al-Durr al-Nadid fi Fadail al-Husayn al-Shahid; Salasil al-Hadid, a selection from the commentary on Nahj al-Balagha by Ibn Abi al-Hadid; al-Insaf fi al-Nass ala al-Aimma al-Ashraf min Al Abd Manaf, in manuscript; Tanbih al-Arib on the men of al-Tahdhib, in manuscript; and Irshad al-Mustarshidin, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 64}. He quotes the author of al-Rawdat as saying that the books are only collection and compilation, in none of which he discusses a preference between views, a research question or the choice of a school, and that he does not know whether this is shortcoming or scrupulousness {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 64}.
