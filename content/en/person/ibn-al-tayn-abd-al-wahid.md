## Overview
Abd al-Wahid ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Thabit, known as Ibn al-Tayn al-Safaqusi, a scholar from Sfax in Tunisia, described as a jurist and a learned man. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* gives his death as 611 AH (1214 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
