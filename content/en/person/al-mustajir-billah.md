## Overview
Al-Mustajir billah, an Abbasid, son of Isa ibn al-Muktafi billah. He rose in Armenia and Mawqan in 349 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.687}

## His call
He titled himself al-Mustajir billah and called to "the one approved from the family of the Messenger of God". He wore wool and enjoined good. A group followed him, and he went to Azerbaijan and prevailed over several towns there. Then he was fought and taken. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.687}
