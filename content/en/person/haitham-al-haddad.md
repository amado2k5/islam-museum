# Haitham al-Haddad

## Summary
Haitham al-Haddad is a British Muslim television presenter and Islamic scholar of Palestinian origin who grew up in Makkah. {cite:wikipedia-haitham-al-haddad-en} He sits on the boards of advisors of Islamic organisations in the United Kingdom, including the Islamic Sharia Council, and is the chair and operations advisor and a trustee of the Muslim Research and Development Foundation. {cite:wikipedia-haitham-al-haddad-en} Wikidata records his religion only as Islam generically. {cite:wikidata-haitham-al-haddad}

## Education
He gained a PhD at SOAS, University of London, with a thesis submitted in 2010 entitled "A critical analysis of selected aspects of Sunni Muslim minority fiqh, with particular reference to contemporary Britain"; in Medina he studied with a student of Ibn al-Uthaymeen, and he also obtained a civil engineering degree in Sudan. {cite:wikipedia-haitham-al-haddad-en}
