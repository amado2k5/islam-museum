## Summary
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Faraj al-Sarakhsi, a Shafi'i jurist who studied with al-Qadi al-Husayn, heard al-Qushayri and others, and was narrated from by Abu Tahir al-Sinji. Ibn al-Sam'ani said he preserved and mastered al-Shafi'i's school. His works include al-Ta'liqa and al-Imla'. Dates 432 to 494, or 500 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13347}
