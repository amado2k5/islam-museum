## Overview
Waki' ibn al-Dawraqiyya al-Qurai', Abu Rabi'a, whose father's name was Umayra. He is ascribed to his mother, who was of the captives of Dawraq {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## With Ibn Khazim
al-Baladhuri reports that he stabbed Ibn Khazim together with Bujayr ibn Warqa', and sat on his chest in revenge for his maternal brother Duwayla. Ibn Khazim answered him with harsh words {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Description
al-Baladhuri describes him as coarse and of great stature. He reports that he ate a plant while praying, and that he drank wine and defended this in verse {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

These are reports that al-Baladhuri transmits, and they need an additional source.
