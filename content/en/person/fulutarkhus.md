## Overview
Fulutarkhus is Plutarch. Ibn al-Nadim mentions among his books "Natural Opinions", which contains the opinions of the philosophers on natural matters in five treatises, translated by Qusta ibn Luqa al-Ba'labakki. He also mentions the book "Moralia", on coping with an enemy and profiting from him, and the books "Anger", "Exercise" and "The Soul". He also has a book on rivers, their properties and their wonders. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.355-356}
