## Summary
Kerem Önder is a Turkish preacher known for his high-volume YouTube lessons and talks on fiqh, hadith and sira. {cite:youtube-kerem-onder}

His YouTube channel counted 782,000 subscribers in SPEAKRJ's table entry dated 8 October 2026 — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:speakrj-kerem-onder}

## Early life and education, as he tells it himself
In the autobiography published on his official website, Önder recounts that he began Islamic learning at seven or eight years old in Qur'an courses, encouraged by his father, and learned Qur'an, tajwid and ilm al-hal. {cite:keremonder-official-site}

He says his passion for ilm al-hal led him to the science of fiqh, and that fiqh in turn pushed him to hadith, which he read and memorised from the well-known hadith collections. {cite:keremonder-official-site}

No source read in this build gives his birth date or birthplace, so neither is recorded here. {cite:keremonder-official-site}

## Spiritual path and preaching
In the same text, in his own voice, Önder describes a later spiritual journey in the Halid-i Haki branch of the Naqshbandi order, begun through students of İhramcızade İsmail Hakkı Efendi of Sivas, and says that after years spent with those students his enthusiasm for giving preaching and advice sessions grew. {cite:keremonder-official-site}

That text is a single text the man wrote about himself; no faith ruling is built on it alone, his school stays unknown in this museum's data, and his own attestation is preserved here as it stands. {cite:keremonder-official-site}

## Programmes and work
His channel (@keremonderhoca) carries thousands of lesson and talk videos on fiqh, tafsir, sira and religious stories, and its video descriptions link his official website and his social accounts. {cite:youtube-kerem-onder} {cite:speakrj-kerem-onder}
