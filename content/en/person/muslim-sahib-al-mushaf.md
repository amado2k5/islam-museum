## Overview
Muslim, the "Holder of the Qur'an", a man among the companions of Ali, whom he sent to the people of the Camel with a copy of the Qur'an calling them to God. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.654-655}

## His death
The people of the Camel shot him with an arrow and killed him, and he was carried to Ali. His mother said that Muslim came to them reciting the Book of God, not fearing them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.654-655}
