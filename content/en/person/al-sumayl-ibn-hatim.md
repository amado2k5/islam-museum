## Overview
Al-Sumayl ibn Hatim al-Kilabi, the great leader of the Qays and their chief in al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273-274}

## In the troubles of al-Andalus
Al-Maqqari says that he incited people against Abu al-Khattar Husam ibn Dirar until he was deposed in 128 AH, and took up the affair of Thawaba ibn Salama, and the two sides gathered to him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273-274}

He then fought Abu al-Khattar when he rose, defeated him and killed him in 129 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.273-274}
