## Overview
A shaykh of Tayy who fell upon the 285 pilgrimage at al-Ajfar on Wednesday with twelve nights left of Muharram, taking the camels, the trade-goods, the free women and the slaves — it was said he took from the people two million dinars {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 294}.
