## Overview
Salih ibn Mudrik al-Ta'i, who fell upon the pilgrim caravan at al-Ajfar on Wednesday the twelfth of Muharram, seizing money, goods, women, free ladies and slaves; it was said he took two million dinars from the people — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 12 p. 376}
