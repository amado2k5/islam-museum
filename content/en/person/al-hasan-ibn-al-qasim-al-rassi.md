## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn al-Qasim was one of the two uncles of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], a brother of [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-al-rassi|Muhammad]] and a son of [[person/al-qasim-ibn-ibrahim-al-rassi|al-Qasim]]. He was among those who came to meet [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] at [[place/al-fur-medina|al-Fur']]; they greeted them, talked for a while and went back to their houses. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} The text says nothing else about him in these pages.
