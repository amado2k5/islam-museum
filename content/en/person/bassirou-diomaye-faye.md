# Bassirou Diomaye Faye

Bassirou Diomaye Faye (born 25 March 1980) is a Senegalese politician and former tax inspector from Ndiaganiao who has served as the fifth president of Senegal since 2 April 2024, the youngest in the country's history.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-fr}

## Formation and rise

Faye was born at Ndiaganiao in the M'bour department, into a Serer family; his father was a long-time member of the Socialist Party.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} He took a master's degree in law at Cheikh Anta Diop University in Dakar, passed into the National School of Administration in 2004, and chose the tax and estates administration, where he befriended Ousmane Sonko, a fellow graduate.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} In the tax inspectors' union founded by Sonko he campaigned on home ownership for revenue agents, and he rose inside Sonko's party, PASTEF, becoming its general secretary in February 2021 after Sonko's arrest.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} Detained himself from April 2023 on charges arising from a social-media post, he was still in prison when PASTEF, itself dissolved, endorsed him in November 2023 as its substitute presidential candidate in place of the barred Sonko.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en}

## Election and presidency

Released under an amnesty on 14 March 2024, ten days before the vote, Faye won the election of 24 March with over 54 per cent in the first round — the first opposition candidate to win outright since independence — under the slogan "Diomaye is Sonko".{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} Inaugurated on 2 April, his first act was to appoint Sonko prime minister.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} His presidency dissolved the National Assembly in September 2024 and won the ensuing snap elections in a landslide, reached a peace agreement with a Casamance separatist faction in February 2025, and worked to keep the military-ruled Sahel states inside ECOWAS.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en} In May 2026, amid a debt crisis and stalled negotiations with the IMF, Faye publicly fell out with Sonko and dismissed him as prime minister, appointing the economist Ahmadou Al Aminou Lo in his place.{cite:source/wikipedia-bassirou-diomaye-faye-en}
