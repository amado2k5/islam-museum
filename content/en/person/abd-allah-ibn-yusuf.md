## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Yusuf al-Tinnisi, Abu Muhammad al-Kala'i, came of a Damascene family. In Taqrib al-Tahdhib Ibn Hajar calls him reliable and exact, among the most dependable narrators of the Muwatta, places him among the senior men of the tenth generation-layer, and says he died in 218 AH {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p. 300}. That year corresponds to 833 CE (derived).
