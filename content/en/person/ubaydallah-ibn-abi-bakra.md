## Overview
Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan appointed him over Sistan after the dismissal of al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad. He campaigned, and at Zaranj, or before it at Bizran, Ratbil sent to him asking for peace over his land and the land of Kabul for one million two hundred thousand {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

He granted him two hundred thousand, the peace was concluded at one million dirhams, and Ziyad confirmed it. He remained there until Ziyad died {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
