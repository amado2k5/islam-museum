## Summary
Servet Tazegül is a Turkish taekwondo practitioner of Azerbaijani origin competing in the featherweight division, and a world, Olympic and European champion. {cite:wikipedia-servet-tazegul-en} He was born on 26 September 1988 in Nuremberg, Germany. {cite:wikidata-servet-tazegul}

## Career
Tazegül competed in the men's 68 kg class at the Beijing 2008 Olympics and won the bronze medal. {cite:wikipedia-servet-tazegul-en} At the London 2012 Olympics he beat Iran's Mohammad Bagheri Motamed in the final, 6–5, to win the gold medal. {cite:wikipedia-servet-tazegul-en} At the 2013 Mediterranean Games in Mersin he won the bronze medal in the 68 kg event. {cite:wikipedia-servet-tazegul-en}

## Recognition
A multi-purpose indoor arena built in Mersin in 2013, with a seating capacity of 7,500, is named in his honour. {cite:wikipedia-servet-tazegul-en}
