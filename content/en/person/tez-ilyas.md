## Summary
Tez Ilyas (born Mohammed Tehzeeb Ilyas) is a British stand-up comedian from Blackburn. A civil servant before comedy, he is known for his touring show TEZ Talks, for presenting Channel 4's satirical The Tez O'Clock Show, for playing Eight in the BBC Three comedy Man Like Mobeen, and for his 2021 book The Secret Diary of a British Muslim aged 13¾. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en}

## Life
Ilyas was born on 8 April 1983 in Blackburn, Lancashire, to a Pakistani Punjabi family originating from Jhelum in Pakistan; the English article and Wikidata agree on the date. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} {cite:wikidata-tez-ilyas} He read biochemistry at Lancaster University, completing a master's degree as well, and began his career as a civil servant in London. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} He married in July 2022 and is based in Clapham, London. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en}

## Work
Ilyas came to comedy in 2010 after a week-long open-mic workshop, and reached the final of the BBC Radio New Comedy Awards in 2011. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} His debut show TEZ Talks, first performed at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe in 2015, is modelled on TED Talks: staged as an introductory seminar on becoming a convert to Islam, it draws on his own life as a British Muslim to explain concepts such as Ramadan and jihad and to answer Islamophobic misconceptions; a BBC Radio 4 series followed, running to three series. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} In 2015 he also made the short film The Fast and the Fool for the BBC's British Muslim Comedy series, following a fasting Muslim through an ordinary office day in Ramadan. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} He played the recurring character Eight in Man Like Mobeen alongside Guz Khan, and from July 2019 presented The Tez O'Clock Show, a satirical comedy programme on Channel 4. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en} His stand-up special Teztify was released on YouTube in 2020, and in April 2021 he published his debut book, The Secret Diary of a British Muslim aged 13¾. {cite:wikipedia-tez-ilyas-en}
