## Overview
Abu 'Ali Ja'far b. Fallah al-Kutami, one of the commanders of al-Mu'izz b. Taqi al-Din al-Mansur the 'Ubaydid, lord of Ifriqiya. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Syria
When Jawhar took Egypt he sent him to Syria; he took Ramla in Dhu'l-Hijja 358, then Damascus in Muharram 359, and stayed there until 360 — when al-Hasan b. Ahmad the Qarmatian, known as al-A'sam, marched out against him though ill, defeated him, killed him and killed multitudes of his men. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
