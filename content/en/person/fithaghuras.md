## Overview
Pythagoras, a Greek philosopher. Al-Mas'udi mentions that he became known for teaching sciences of the unseen and kinds of revelation, because of the purity of his soul and its freedom from the stains of this world. He adds that the early Greek philosophers did not reject divination. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.483}
