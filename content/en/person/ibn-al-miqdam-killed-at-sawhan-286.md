## Overview
A man of al-Hadi's companions called Ibn al-Miqdam was killed at [[place/sawhan-najran|Sawhan]] and carried to a place called "Rahat" to be buried. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} The index lists him at p. 161 only. See [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]].
