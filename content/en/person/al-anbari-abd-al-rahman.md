## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Wafa Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Anbari, a scholar from Anbar on the Euphrates, titled Kamal al-Din. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 513 AH (1119 CE) and died in 577 AH (1181 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates rest on tradition and need an additional source.
