## Summary
The master Jamal al-Din Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah ibn Najm ibn Shas ibn Nizar al-Judhami al-Sa'di al-Misri; shaykh of the Malikis and author of al-Jawahir al-Thamina on the madhhab of the scholar of Medina; among the great practising imams, he made hajj late in life, returned, and withheld from fatwa until he died striving in God's path about Rajab 616. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 69}
