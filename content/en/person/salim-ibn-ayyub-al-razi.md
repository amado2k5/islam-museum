## Overview
Salim ibn Ayyub ibn Salim, the imam and shaykh al-Islam Abu al-Fath al-Razi al-Shafi'i, a memoriser of the fifth century, born around 366 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1889}

## Travels and standing
He narrated from Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Malik al-Ju'fi, Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Tamimi, the hafiz Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Busayr al-Razi, the two companions of Ibn Abi Hatim, Abu al-Husayn Ahmad ibn Faris the lexicographer and Abu Ahmad al-Faradi, and took fiqh from the master Abu Hamid al-Isfarayini; he settled in Syria as a frontier devotee spreading knowledge in hope of reward. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1889}

Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi, al-Kattani, Nasr al-Maqdisi and others narrated from him; al-Nasib described him as trustworthy, a jurist, a teacher of recitation and a traditionist. He died in 447 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1889}
