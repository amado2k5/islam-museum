## Overview
Muhammad ibn Mahmud ibn al-Hasan ibn Hibat Allah ibn Mahasin, Abu Abd Allah, Muhibb al-Din Ibn al-Najjar, was a historian and a hafiz of hadith. He belonged to Baghdad, where he was born and died. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 578 AH, 1183 CE, and his death as 643 AH, 1245 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 86}.

## His travels
He travelled to Syria, Egypt, the Hijaz, Persia and elsewhere, and the journey lasted twenty-seven years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 86}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books: al-Kamal fi ma'rifat al-rijal, biographies; the continuation of al-Khatib's History of Baghdad, in manuscript, the tenth volume of the work, which runs to sixteen volumes; al-Durra al-thamina fi akhbar al-Madina, printed; Nuzhat al-wara fi akhbar Umm al-Qura; Nisbat al-muhaddithin ila al-aba' wa-l-buldan; Jannat al-nazirin fi ma'rifat al-tabi'in; Manaqib al-Shafi'i; al-Iqd al-fa'iq fi uyun akhbar al-dunya wa-mahasin al-khala'iq; al-Azhar fi anwa' al-ash'ar; and al-Zahr fi mahasin shu'ara' ahl al-asr {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 86}.
