## Overview
Abu Thabit Amir ibn Yusuf al-Marini, sultan of the Banu Marin from 707 to 708 AH, and the son of [[person/abu-yusuf-yaqub-marini|Abu Yusuf Yaqub]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His reign
Ibn Khaldun describes him as "the falcon of his people" for his boldness. He marched on Tlemcen, camped before it and pressed the siege hard. He then entered Marrakesh in the middle of Rajab of the same year. He died at Tazi in 708 AH, at the start of his rule. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The Gregorian date of his death (1308 CE) is derived from the Hijri date and is not stated in the source.
