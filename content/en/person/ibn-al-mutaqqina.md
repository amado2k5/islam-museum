## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Rahbi, Abu Abd Allah, known as Ibn al-Mutafannina, was a scholar of the law of inheritance of the Shafi'i school, of Rahbat Malik ibn Tawq, where he was born and died in 577 AH (1182 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.278}

He is the author of the famous urjuza called Bughyat al-Bahith, known as al-Rahbiyya, on inheritance; Yaqut says he taught in his town and composed books. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.278}
