## Overview
Sultan al-Ashraf Inal, a Circassian Mamluk sultan. Al-Suyuti says that he attacked al-Mansur Uthman ibn Jaqmaq and arrested him. The caliph al-Qa'im invested him in the first month of Rabi al-Awwal 857 AH, and he took the title al-Ashraf. Then a dispute arose between the caliph and al-Ashraf over the soldiers riding against him, and al-Ashraf deposed him from the caliphate in Jumada 859 AH and sent him to Alexandria, where he kept him in custody. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.775}
