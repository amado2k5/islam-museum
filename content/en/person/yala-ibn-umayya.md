## Overview
Ya'la ibn Umayya ibn Abi 'Ubayda al-Tamimi of Mecca, ally of Quraysh — his mother being Munya bint Ghazwan, sister of 'Utba ibn Ghazwan. He embraced Islam on the day of the Conquest and excelled in it, witnessing al-Ta'if and Tabuk; he has some twenty hadiths, with material in the two Sahihs. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4254}

## Standing
Ibn Sa'd says Ya'la gave fatwa in Mecca; others report 'Umar appointed him over Jiran; he was among the generous and wealthy Companions, and the first to date written documents, while in Yemen. He died in 60 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4254}
