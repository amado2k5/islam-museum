## Overview
Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah, one of the companions and counsellors of [[person/ibn-muqla|Ibn Muqla]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 187-191}

## The vizierate
[[person/al-qahir-billah|Al-Qahir]] wrote to him and promised him the vizierate. Al-Qahir then appointed him vizier at the start of Sha'ban 321 AH, after Ibn Muqla was dismissed, and gave him a robe of honour. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 187-191}
