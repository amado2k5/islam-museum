## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim was the father of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and a son of [[person/al-qasim-ibn-ibrahim-al-rassi|al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim]], according to al-Hadi's descent as the book gives it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.15} The narrator says he honoured [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] and Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn Yahya when they reached [[place/al-fur-medina|al-Fur']] and lodged them near his house. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} When al-Hadi met them he had with him his father al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim, his two uncles Muhammad and al-Hasan sons of al-Qasim, and Abdullah ibn al-Husayn. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37}
