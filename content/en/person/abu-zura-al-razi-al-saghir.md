## Overview
Abu Zur'a al-Razi the Younger, Ahmad b. al-Husayn the hafiz; he travelled, roamed, gathered and composed, hearing Abu Hamid Ibn Bilal, the qadi al-Mahamili and their tier. Al-Khatib said he was "an exacting hafiz who collected the chapters and tarjamas." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 84}
