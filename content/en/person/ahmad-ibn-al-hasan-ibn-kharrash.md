## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn Kharrash, Abu Ja'far al-Baghdadi, the hafiz and hadith scholar, is among the men whose hadith Muslim and al-Tirmidhi record, by the codes at the head of the entry {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص752}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi, Wahb ibn Jarir, Shababa, Abd al-Samad ibn Abd al-Warith and their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص753}. Muslim and al-Tirmidhi narrated from him in their books, as did Muhammad ibn Harun ibn al-Mujadar, Abu al-Abbas ibn al-Sarraj and others {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص753}.

## What the critics said
Al-Dhahabi says: he was reliable {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص753}.

## His death
He died in 242 AH. Al-Dhahabi first says he was about seventy, then corrects himself: sixty years less twenty days, and reports that his son heard him say so an hour before he died {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص753}.
