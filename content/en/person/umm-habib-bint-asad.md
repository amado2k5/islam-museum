## Overview
Umm Habib bint Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza ibn Qusayy, grandmother of Barra bint Abd al-Uzza, the mother of Amina {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.533}.

## Her place in the lineage
al-Baladhuri places her among the Qurashi grandmothers in the lineage of the Prophet on his mother's side {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.533}.
