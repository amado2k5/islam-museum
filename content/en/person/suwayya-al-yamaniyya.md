## Summary
Suwayya was a woman of [[place/yemen|Yemen]] whom [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] includes among the devout women of Yemen in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}

## In Sifat al-Safwa
He reports from Abu Hisham, a man of Quraysh of Banu Amir, that she came and lodged in one of their houses; at night he heard her sobbing and gasping, so he sent his maid, who saw her standing towards the qibla with her head raised to the sky. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
The maid heard her addressing her Lord: He had created "Suwayya" from clay and showered her with His favours from one state to another, while she exposed herself to His displeasure by sinning; then she cried out and fell, and in the morning they found her dead. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
The editor notes that Kahhala took her from this book into *A'lam al-Nisa'*. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.415}
