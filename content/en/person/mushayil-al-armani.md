## Overview
Mushayil al-Armani, head of the people of Armenia when they broke away, and the [[event/mushayil-revolt|leader of the rebels]] in this event. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.295}

## The revolt
It happened in the governorship of al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba al-Ta'i, after the removal of Ibn Asid and Bakkar ibn Muslim al-Uqayli. Al-Mansur sent him reinforcements under the command of Amir ibn Isma'il, and al-Hasan met him in battle, killed him, and his forces scattered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.295}
