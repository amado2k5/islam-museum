## Overview
Ni'mat Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Husayn al-Husayni al-Jaza'iri was a man of letters and teacher among the Imami (Twelver Shi'i) jurists, and al-Zirikli labels him so. His name points to the Jaza'ir of Basra. He was born in the village of al-Sabbaghiyya in it and studied there, then in Shiraz and Isfahan; he returned to al-Jaza'ir and died in the village of Jayidar. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1050 AH, 1640 CE, and his death as 1112 AH, 1701 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 32}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books Zahr al-Rabi, on literature, the first and second parts printed; al-Anwar al-Numaniyya fi Marifat al-Nash'a al-Insaniyya, printed in two parts; Maqsud al-Anam fi Sharh Tahdhib al-Ahkam, in twelve volumes, which he says is understood from al-Dhariea to survive together with its abridgement Ghayat al-Maram in eight volumes; Nur al-Anwar fi Sharh Kalam Khayr al-Akhyar, in manuscript; Lawami al-Anwar fi Sharh Uyun al-Akhbar, in manuscript; Maqamat al-Naja, in manuscript; Nur al-Anwar fi Sharh al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, printed; and Furuq al-Lugha, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 32}.
