## Overview
Qays ibn Hubayra of Murad, nicknamed al-Maksuh because he was cauterised on his flank for an ailment he had. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## To Yemen
The Prophet sent him to Yemen to fight al-Aswad al-Ansi. He instructed him to win over the Abna', and sent Farwa ibn Musayk with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing of al-Aswad
News of the Prophet's death reached him in Yemen. He showed al-Aswad that he was of his opinion, until al-Aswad let him enter Sana'a. Then he won over Fayruz al-Daylami and Dadhawayh, and they agreed to kill al-Aswad and entered on him at dawn. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The report says that Qays slaughtered him, though it is also said that Fayruz killed him and Qays finished him off. See [[event/killing-of-al-aswad-al-ansi|the killing of al-Aswad al-Ansi]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterward
He was accused of killing Dadhawayh. When word reached Abu Bakr that he wanted to drive the Abna' out of Sana'a, al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya brought him to Abu Bakr. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] made him swear fifty oaths at the Prophet's pulpit that he had not killed him. Then he released him and sent him to Syria with those who had volunteered for the raid on the Byzantines. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
