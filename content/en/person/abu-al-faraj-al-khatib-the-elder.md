## Overview
Muhammad (Abu al-Faraj) ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Salih ibn Abd al-Rahim al-Khatib: an exegete counted among the leading Shafi'is of his age, born and deceased in Damascus between 1244 and 1311 AH (1828–1913 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.212}

His works include al-Tanzil wa-Asrar al-Ta'wil, a large Qur'an commentary; al-Fuyudat al-Hasan bi-Nasa'ih al-Wildan; a gloss on al-Qatr in grammar; two commentaries on the Ajurrumiyya; an abridgement of the Musnad of Imam Ahmad; a mawlid and a mi'raj; and three diwans of sermons. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.212}
