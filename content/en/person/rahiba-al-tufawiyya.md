## Summary
Rahiba is among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 609); she was the mother of Uthman ibn Sawda al-Tufawi, who reports her story. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## Her prayer at death
Uthman relates that as she was dying she raised her head to the sky and said, "O my treasure and my store, O You on whom I rely in my life and after my death, do not forsake me at death, and do not leave me lonely in my grave"; then she died. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## Her son's dream
Uthman went to her grave every Friday to pray for her and ask forgiveness for her and for the people of the graves; he saw her in a dream and asked how she was, and she said that death has a severe anguish, that she was in a praiseworthy interval (barzakh), and charged him not to give up visiting them and praying for them, telling him she was given the good news of his coming each Friday, which gladdened her and the dead around her. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## For young visitors
Rahiba was a mother whose son visited her grave every Friday to pray for her; he saw her in a dream thanking him and asking him to keep it up.
