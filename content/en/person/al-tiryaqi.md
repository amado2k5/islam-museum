## Overview
Abu Nasr al-Tiryaqi 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Muhammad al-Harawi — transmitter of the Tirmidhi, all but its last part, from al-Jarrahi; trustworthy and an adib, he lived ninety-four years, Tiryaq being a village of Herat; he died in 483 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}.
