## Summary
Oussama Mellouli is a Tunisian swimmer, born in La Marsa on 16 February 1984. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en} {cite:wikidata-oussama-mellouli} Known as the "Shark of the Mediterranean", he won Olympic gold in the 1500 m freestyle at Beijing 2008 and in the 10 km marathon swim at London 2012, and competed at six Olympic Games between 2000 and 2020. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en} {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-ar}

## Early life
Mellouli left Tunisia at the age of fifteen to study and train in France, graduating from a lycée in Marseille. He enrolled at the University of Southern California in 2003, swam for the USC Trojans under coach Dave Salo, and graduated in 2007 with a bachelor's degree in computer science. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en}

## Career
Mellouli made his Olympic debut at Sydney 2000 as a sixteen-year-old. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en} At Beijing 2008 he won the 1500 m freestyle, and at London 2012 he won the 10 km open-water marathon and took bronze in the 1500 m freestyle, becoming the first swimmer to win medals in both open water and the pool at a single Olympic Games. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en} He was world champion in the 1500 m freestyle at Rome 2009, winning in 14:37.28, then the second-fastest performance in history, and in the 5 km open water at Barcelona 2013; he also won short-course world titles at Indianapolis 2004 and Dubai 2010 and fifteen gold medals at the 2011 Pan Arab Games in Doha. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en}

## Doping ban
Mellouli tested positive for Adderall in an in-competition test in December 2006. In September 2007 the Court of Arbitration for Sport imposed an eighteen-month ban, backdated to 30 November 2006, and his results at the 2007 World Championships in Melbourne — where he had won the 800 m freestyle — were annulled. {cite:wikipedia-oussama-mellouli-en} He returned to win the world and Olympic titles described above.
