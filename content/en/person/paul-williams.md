# Paul Williams

## Summary
Paul Williams is a British Muslim writer and interviewer, founder and creator of the Blogging Theology channel and founder and director of the Blogging Theology Academy. His academy profile records that he studied philosophy and theology at the University of London and that he is a convert from Christianity to Islam, and it lists him in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 editions of The Muslim 500. {cite:blogging-theology-academy-paul-williams}

## Channel and academy
On Blogging Theology he hosts long-form interviews and discussions with Muslim and Christian scholars and thinkers. {cite:youtube-blogging-theology} He also teaches with Al Balagh Academy, whose tutor profile carries the same biography. {cite:albalagh-paul-williams}

## Positions and faith
Read this section as the report of the sources, not as a museum ruling.

His conversion is documented by his own academy's profile and by Al Balagh Academy's profile — the same biographical text, one source family — and the Islam Channel independently described him in 2024 as a blogger who converted to Islam, in a programme titled 'From Evangelical Christian to Muslim Advocate'. {cite:blogging-theology-academy-paul-williams} {cite:islam-channel-paul-williams} No source names a school for him personally, so no faith value is recorded; his Christianity belongs to his past before his conversion.
