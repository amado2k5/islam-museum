## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Sa'id b. Abi Talib Yahya b. Abi'l-Hasan 'Ali b. al-Musa'id al-Sa'di, known as Ibn al-Dubaythi — the Shafi'i jurist and historian of Wasit, named for the village near Wasit, his origin being Kanja; he heard much hadith, wrote useful marginalia, and had fine tales he deployed in conversation — among the famed hafizes and noted nobles in hadith, its men's names and history; he composed a book made a dhayl on the Tarikh Baghdad. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
