## Overview
Rabi'a ibn Bujayr al-Taghlibi, of Banu Taghlib. al-Baladhuri reports that he was in charge of a group of Taghlib at al-Mudayh and al-Musayid, who had apostatised. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] came to them while on his way from Iraq to Syria. They fought him, he defeated them, and he took captives and booty {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152}.

Khalid sent the captives to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], and among them was Umm Habib al-Sahba' {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152}. See [[event/khalid-march-to-syria|Khalid's march to Syria]] and [[event/ridda-wars|the Ridda wars]].
