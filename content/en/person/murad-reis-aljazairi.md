## Summary
An Algerian reis of [[person/hasan-veneziano|Hasan Veneziano]]'s era {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.

## The Canaries raid
Around 994/1586 he pushed into the Atlantic, raided the Canary Islands and took rich plunder — the islands' governor's wife among the captives {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.
