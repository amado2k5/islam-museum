## Summary
Maulana Tariq Jamil is a Pakistani preacher and scholar associated with the Tablighi Jamaat, and one of the most widely followed Islamic speakers in the world, with a large digital audience drawn by his emotive Urdu sermons. {cite:wikipedia-tariq-jamil}

## Life and education
He was born on 1 October 1953 in Tulamba, near Mian Channu in Punjab's Khanewal district, into a landowning family. He studied at Government College University in Lahore and began medical studies at King Edward Medical College before leaving to pursue religious learning at the Jamia Arabia in Raiwind, the Tablighi Jamaat's own educational centre. {cite:wikipedia-tariq-jamil}

## Preaching career
Jamil joined the Tablighi Jamaat — the transnational, avowedly apolitical preaching movement rooted in the Deobandi tradition — and spent decades travelling with its jamaats and giving bayans, sermons marked by themes of repentance, prayer and the hereafter delivered in his characteristic tearful style. In the 2010s his lectures spread on YouTube and he became one of the most-subscribed religious figures in Urdu. He founded the Maulana Tariq Jamil Foundation for health and education work, and launched the MTJ clothing brand in 2021. In October 2023 his son Asim Jamil died in a shooting incident reported by Pakistani media; the museum records the fact attributed, without adjudicating the circumstances. {cite:wikipedia-tariq-jamil}

## Standing and readings
The Muslim 500 has ranked him among the world's most influential Muslims — at 32nd in the 2027 edition. Admirers see a preacher who revived attachment to prayer among millions; critics question his closeness to political leaders and armed forces, and some religious voices dispute his statements on particular theological points. The museum reports these readings attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-tariq-jamil}
