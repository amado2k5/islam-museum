## Overview
Muhammad b. 'Umar al-Qurtubi, the righteous man of the Malikis; he made the hajj, heard from 'Abd al-Mun'im b. al-Farawi and a company, and read the readings on Abu'l-Qasim al-Shatibi — an ascetic, exact, brilliant imam in several sciences: fiqh, the readings and Arabic, long-armed in tafsir. He died at Medina in Safar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 145}
