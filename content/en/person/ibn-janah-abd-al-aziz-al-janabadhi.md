## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd al-Aziz ibn Mahmud ibn al-Mubarak al-Janabadhi al-Baghdadi, a hadith scholar and jurist of Baghdad, from Janabadh. His family was known for learning in Baghdad. He was born in 524 AH (1129 CE) and died in 611 AH (1214 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.454}
