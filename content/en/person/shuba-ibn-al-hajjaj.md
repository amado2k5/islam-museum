## Overview
Shu'ba ibn al-Hajjaj, Abu Bistam, client of the Banu Shuqra of the Azd. al-Mas'udi reports that he died in the year 160 AH in the days of al-Mahdi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.313}.

## Notes for researchers
- The date rests on a single source. It needs an additional source.
