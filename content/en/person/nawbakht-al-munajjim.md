## Overview
Nawbakht the Magian, astrologer to the caliph al-Mansur, who converted to Islam at his hands, and the founder of the Nawbakhtis family {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.668-669}.

## In al-Mas'udi
al-Mas'udi reports that al-Mansur was the first caliph to bring astrologers close to him and to act on the judgments of the stars. Nawbakht was with him, together with Ibrahim al-Fazari and Ali ibn Isa the astrolabist {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.668-669}.
