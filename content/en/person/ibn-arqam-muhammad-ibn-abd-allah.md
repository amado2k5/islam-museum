## Overview
Abu Amir Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Azim ibn Arqam al-Numayri, an Andalusian scholar and poet. He travelled to Ceuta, and had connections with the young men of his age. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* gives his death as 740 AH (1339 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
