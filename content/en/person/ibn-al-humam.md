## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahid ibn Abd al-Hamid of Sivas, later of Cairo, the Hanafi Kamal Ibn al-Humam: born at Alexandria about 790. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/127} He studied with Siraj al-Din, reader of the Hidaya, Wali al-Din al-Iraqi and Ibn Hajar. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/127} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/128} He became head of the Shaykhuniyya in Jumada al-Ula 847. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/130} His works include his unfinished commentary on the Hidaya, al-Tahrir in legal theory and al-Musayara. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/130} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/131} He died on Friday 7 Ramadan 861 and was buried in the Qarafa. Al-Sakhawi calls him the most learned man on earth, with little learning in hadith. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 8/132}
