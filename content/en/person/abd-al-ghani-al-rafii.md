## Overview
Abd al-Ghani ibn Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Qadir al-Rafi'i al-Bisari al-Faruqi, a judge and Hanafi jurist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10355}

## Study and offices
He was born and educated in Tripoli in Syria, took hadith from the scholars of Damascus, served three years as mufti of Tripoli, and then as judge of the district of Ta'izz in Yemen. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10355}

## Works
His works include notes on Ibn 'Abidin's gloss on al-Durar and a commentary on the poem by al-Safi al-Hilli. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10356}
