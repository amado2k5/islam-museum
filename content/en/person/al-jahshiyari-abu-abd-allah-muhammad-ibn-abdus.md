## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abdus al-Jahshiyari, a man of letters whose origin is in Kufa. The source says that he played a political role in the early fourth century AH through his ties with the viziers of his time, and that he died in 331 AH (942 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## What the sources do not give here
