## Ibrahim ibn al-Abbas al-Samiri

Ibrahim ibn al-Abbas, or Ibn Abi al-Abbas, Abu Ishaq al-Samiri of Kufa. He related from Shurayk the judge, Ibn al-Zinad and Baqiyya and others; Ahmad ibn Hanbal, al-Saghani and al-Duri related from him. Ahmad said he was good in hadith and once said there is no harm in him; al-Daraqutni and others called him trustworthy, and Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy. The book gives no date of death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5499}
