## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman, Zayn al-Din Ibn Misk al-Sakhawi, was an Egyptian Shafi'i man of letters. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1025 AH, 1616 CE, and his death as 1123 AH, 1711 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 297}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books: al-Lum'a al-Miskiyya, a manuscript at Dar al-Kutub, a commentary on al-Maqsura al-Duraydiyya; and Muthallath Ibn Misk, a manuscript of two leaves in the manner of Qutrub, in the Zahiriyya under number 206; he says he has other books besides {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 297}.
