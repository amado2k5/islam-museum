## Overview
Zahir ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Isa al-Sarakhsi, Abu Ali, is called by al-Dhahabi the imam and great scholar, jurist of Khurasan and shaykh of the reciters and hadith scholars {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}. Al-Hakim said he was Abu Ali al-Sarakhsi the Shafi'i, the shaykh of his age in Khurasan {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}.

## Teachers and students
He heard from Abu Lubayd Muhammad ibn Idris al-Sami, Abu al-Qasim al-Baghawi, Yahya ibn Sa'id, Muhammad ibn al-Musayyab al-Arghiyani and others; he recited under Ibn Mujahid, studied law under Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi and literature under Ibn al-Anbari {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}. Al-Hakim, Abu Uthman Isma'il ibn al-Sabuni, Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Muzakki, Abu Uthman Sa'id al-Buhayri, the Hanafi judge Abu al-Muzaffar Mansur ibn Isma'il, Karima al-Marwaziyya, who lived beside the Sacred House, and many others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}.

## His learning
He held a part of the Muwatta', the chapters on musaqat and qirad, from Prince Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Samad al-Hashimi, a companion of Abu Mus'ab al-Zubayri, and he took the art of disputation and theology from Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}. Al-Hakim said he heard his debate in the gathering of Abu Bakr ibn Ishaq al-Sibghi {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}.

## His death
He died in Rabi' al-Akhir 389 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}. His entry is numbered 2095 in the edition {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1706}. The Risala edition of the Siyar, read on the page for this correction, gives his birth in 294 AH, which is 906 to 907 CE, and says he was ninety-six years old when he died {cite:siyar-alam-nubala vol. 16 pp. 476-477}.
