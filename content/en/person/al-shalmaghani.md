## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Shalmaghani; Ibn al-Jawzi records that in 341 AH it was reported to the vizier Ibn al-Muhallabi that a man known as al-Basri had died at Baghdad, leader of a sect and companion of Abu Ja'far ibn Abi'l-Azaqir, claiming the latter's spirit dwelt in him; his trusted followers reportedly held him divine and believed the spirits of prophets and saints entered them — the source's report of what the sect was accused of, not the museum's judgement. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 663}
