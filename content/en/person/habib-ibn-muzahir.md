## Overview
Habib ibn Muzahir, also written Muzhir or Mutahhir, ibn Ri'ab ibn al-Ashtar ibn Hajwan al-Asadi al-Kindi and then al-Faq'asi, was a Tabi'i and one of the brave commanders. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 61 AH, 680 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 166}.

## Life
He settled in Kufa and accompanied [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] in all his wars {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 166}. On the day of Karbala he commanded the left wing of [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]], at the age of seventy-five {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 166}.

## The day of Karbala
Al-Zirikli counts him among seventy men who fought to the last that day; he reports that the imam made them an offer and they refused, saying they would have no excuse before the Messenger of God if al-Husayn were killed while an eye among them still blinked, and they were killed around him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 166}.
