## Overview
Salim ibn Qutayba, a man whom [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] asked about the affair of [[person/abu-muslim-al-khurasani|Abu Muslim]]. He answered him with a verse that exalts God above having any partner. al-Mansur said: "That is enough, Ibn Qutayba; you have lodged it in a listening ear" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.278}.
