## Overview
'Isa ibn Aban ibn Sadaqa, Abu Musa, was a judge among the great Hanafi jurists, quick in enforcing judgement and upright. He served the Abbasid al-Mansur for a time, then held the judgeship of Basra for ten years and died there. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.99}

His books include Ithbat al-Qiyas; Ijtihad al-Ra'y; al-Jami' on fiqh; and al-Hujja al-Saghira on hadith. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.99}
