## Summary
Aisha bint Ibrahim ibn Khalil al-Ba'labakkiyya al-Dimashqiyya — in a note by [[person/umar-rida-kahhala|Kahhala]], Aisha bint Ibrahim ibn Khalil ibn Abd Allah … al-Zubaydiyya, known as Ibnat al-Shara'ihi — was an accomplished, devout hadith transmitter, born in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] around 760 AH; his entry in [[work/alam-al-nisa-kahhala|A'lam al-Nisa']] follows al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.7} {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.8}

## What she heard
She was made to hear much from the students of al-Fakhr ibn al-Bukhari and others in Damascus, [[place/cairo|Cairo]] and [[place/baalbek|Baalbek]], among them Ibn Umayla and al-Salah ibn Abi Umar, and received licences from a group including Ibn Nubata and Zaynab bint al-Damamisi. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.8}

## Her teaching
She transmitted a great deal; a number heard from her, such as Ibn Nasir al-Din, and read to her works including al-Dhahabi's selection from al-Fakhr's mashyakha, the "chain of firsts" hadith with its condition, the fifty-seventh part of al-Ahadith al-Mukhtara and the eleventh part of al-Tirmidhi's Jami'; Kahhala lists manuscript copies of these parts among his sources. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.8}

## Her death
She died on Wednesday 16 Safar 842 AH and was buried in the Bab Tuma cemetery in Damascus. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.8}

## For young visitors
Aisha was a hadith scholar of Damascus who studied in many cities; students came to read books of hadith to her.
