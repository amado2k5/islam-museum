## Overview
Abu al-Fath al-Fadl ibn Ja'far ibn al-Furat, the last vizier of al-Muqtadir. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.661-662}

## The day al-Muqtadir was killed
He was the vizier of [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]] on the day he was killed at the Gate of al-Shamasiyya in 320 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.661-662}

Al-Mas'udi relates that the vizier took the astrological reading at the hour the caliph rode out to the battle. When he told him that the time was the zenith, al-Muqtadir frowned and wanted not to go out. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.661-662}
