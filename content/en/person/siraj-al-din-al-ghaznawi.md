## Overview
Umar ibn Ishaq ibn Ahmad al-Hindi al-Ghaznawi, Abu Hafs, Siraj al-Din, was a jurist counted among the major Hanafis. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 704 AH, 1304 CE, and his death as 773 AH, 1372 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 42}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists: al-Tawshih, a commentary on al-Hidaya, in law; al-Ghurra al-Munifa fi Tarjih Madhhab Abi Hanifa, printed; al-Shamil, in law; Zubdat al-Ahkam fi Ikhtilaf al-A'imma, in manuscript; a commentary on Badi' al-Nizam, in manuscript; a commentary on al-Mughni of al-Khabbazi, in manuscript, on the principles of law; a commentary on al-Ziyadat, on Hanafi derived rulings; a commentary on the creed of al-Tahawi, printed; and al-Fatawa al-Sirajiyya, in manuscript, adding that there is doubt about whether the last is his {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 42}.
