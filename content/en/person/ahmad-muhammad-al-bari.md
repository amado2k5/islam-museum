## Overview
Ahmad Muhammad al-Bari, a scholar of al-Azhar al-Sharif; he composed the treatise "Principles of Upbringing and Teaching in the Noble Qur'an and the Prophetic Sunna" in a manuscript at the Faculty of Arabic, completing it in 1356/1937 per the Academy's edition; the editor notes he found no biography of him in his sources beyond his being at the Faculty of Arabic {cite:usul-al-tarbiya-azhar p. 11}.
