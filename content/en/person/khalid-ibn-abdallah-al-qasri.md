## Overview
Khalid ibn Abdallah ibn Asad ibn Kurz al-Qasri, of the Yajila. He governed Mecca for al-Walid and then for Sulayman. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz advised that he be removed, and he was removed in Sulayman's time. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.180-181}

Then Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik appointed him over the two Iraqs after Ibn Hubayra was removed. Hisham dismissed him in 120 AH in favour of Yusuf ibn Umar, and imprisoned him for about fourteen months, after which he came to Syria in 122 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.483-484}

## In Iraq
Al-Baladhuri relates that he built a church for his mother in Kufa, and his mother was a Christian. He built shops, dug the canal known as al-Jami', repaired the bridge of Kufa, and renewed the enclosure of the mosque of Kufa. He was so strict in the matter of coinage that the dirhams of his time were called "Khalidi". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Mecca
Al-Mas'udi says that he arranged the rows around the Ka'ba and ordered men and women to be separated during circumambulation. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 165}

## Conflicting reports
Ibn al-Nadim says that he was accused of heresy (zandaqa), and that al-Ja'd ibn Dirham was killed on his orders by Hisham on the Feast of Sacrifice after a long imprisonment with him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 472-473}
