## Overview
al-Hasan ibn Buwayh al-Daylami, brother of Ali, 'Imad al-Dawla, and Ahmad, Mu'izz al-Dawla. His title was al-Mustakfi "Rukn al-Dawla" when his brother entered Baghdad and the brothers' titles were struck on the coinage {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.613}.

## His rule
al-Muti' appointed him in place of his brother 'Imad al-Dawla in 338 AH. He was the father of 'Adud al-Dawla {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.616}.

## From Ibn al-Athir
Ibn al-Athir reports that Mansur ibn Qaratakin the Samanid seized Rayy in 340 AH. Rukn al-Dawla, who was in Fars, wrote to his brother Mu'izz al-Dawla ordering him to send an army to push them back from the regions of Iraq {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364-365}.

In 351 AH he marched to Tabaristan, besieged Sari and took it. Wushmgir left Tabaristan for Jurjan, and Rukn al-Dawla followed him and drove him from it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.401-402}.
