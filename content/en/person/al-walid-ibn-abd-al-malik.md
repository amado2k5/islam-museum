## Overview
Al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, an Umayyad caliph, known as Abu al-Abbas. He died in the middle of Jumada al-Akhira 96 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.495}

## Rule and building
Al-Tabari mentions in his biography the building of mosques. In his time [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]] conquered al-Andalus, and [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] conquered Kashgar. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.496}

Al-Mas'udi says that in 87 AH he began the building of the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus and the Mosque of the Messenger in Medina, and that [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] took charge of the expense. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.148-157}

Al-Salabi says that he was so interested in building that he was called the engineer of the Umayyads. When the chambers of the mothers of the believers were included in the mosque of the Prophet during its extension, a number of the jurists of Medina objected to him. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.129-130}

Al-Salabi relates that he founded the first hospital for lepers in Damascus in 88 AH, ordered roads paved to the Hijaz, and that the Office of Estates appeared in his time. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.132-141}

## The caliphate and the dispute
He was given the allegiance in Damascus on the day his father died. There is a disagreement about the length of his caliphate: al-Mas'udi says nine years and eight months, and al-Salabi chooses the view of al-Zuhri, ten years less a month. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.148-157} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.132-141}

Al-Salabi relates that he wanted to depose his brother Sulayman and to give the allegiance to his son Abd al-Aziz, and that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz advised him that the allegiance is one knot. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.177-180}

## His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] closes his account of this reign by naming its governors and office-holders. They are given here in his order; lines the editor restored from later authors are marked as such. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310–313}

- **Basra:** al-Hakam ibn Ayyub (then dismissed); then Talha ibn Sa'id al-Juhani (of Damascus; then dismissed); then Amr ibn Sa'id al-Awdhi (of Damascus; then dismissed); then Muhasir ibn Suhaym al-Kinani (of Homs; then dismissed); then Qatan ibn Mudrik al-Kilabi (then dismissed); then [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami]] (until al-Hajjaj and al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Kufa:** [[person/urwa-ibn-al-mughira-ibn-shuba|Urwa ibn al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba al-Thaqafi]] (95 AH); then Ziyad ibn Jarir ibn Abd Allah (over the police until al-Hajjaj died); then Harmala ibn Umayr al-Lakhmi (appointed by Yazid ibn Abi Kabsha until al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Khurasan:** [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] (until al-Hajjaj and al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Sijistan:** Abd Rabbih ibn Abd Allah ibn Umayr al-Laythi (appointed by Qutayba, then dismissed); then al-Nu'man ibn Awf al-Yashkuri (until al-Hajjaj and al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Bahrayn:** Qatan ibn Ziyad ibn al-Rabi' al-Harithi (appointed by al-Hajjaj in 79 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Oman:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Sulaym al-Kalbi; then Abd al-Jabbar ibn Sabra al-Mujashi'i (until al-Hajjaj died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Sind:** [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim ibn Abi Aqil]] (95 AH) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Mecca:** Nafi' ibn Alqama ibn Safwan (confirmed for two years, then dismissed); then [[person/khalid-ibn-abdallah-al-qasri|Khalid ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri]] (89 AH until al-Walid died; in (( )) in the edition: restored by the editor from later authors quoting Khalifa) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 310}
- **Medina:** [[person/hisham-ibn-ismail-al-makhzumi|Hisham ibn Isma'il al-Makhzumi]] (confirmed for two years, then dismissed); then [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan]] (from early 87 or late 86 AH to 93 AH); then [[person/abu-bakr-ibn-hazm|Abu Bakr ibn Hazm]] (Umar's deputy, dismissed by al-Walid); then [[person/uthman-ibn-hayyan|Uthman ibn Hayyan al-Murri]] (until al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Yamama:** Ibrahim ibn Arabi (confirmed) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Jazira:** [[person/muhammad-ibn-marwan|Muhammad ibn Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] (with Armenia and Azerbaijan) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Egypt:** [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-malik|Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Malik]] (confirmed, then dismissed); then Qurra ibn Sharik al-Absi (also among his governors there) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Ifriqiya:** [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]] (then went to al-Walid in 95 AH); then [[person/abdallah-ibn-musa-ibn-nusayr|Abd Allah ibn Musa]] (his father's deputy until al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Yemen:** Muhammad ibn Yusuf (confirmed until al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Damascus:** [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-al-walid|Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Jordan:** Umar ibn al-Walid (his son) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Palestine:** [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Homs:** [[person/al-abbas-ibn-al-walid|al-Abbas ibn al-Walid]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 311}
- **Summer campaign:** [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]]; then [[person/al-abbas-ibn-al-walid|al-Abbas [ibn al-Walid]]] (his son); then Umar [ibn al-Walid] (his son) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Police:** Riyah ibn Abda (then dismissed); then Ka'b ibn Hamid al-Absi (until al-Walid died) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Chancery (letters):** Janah, his mawla {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Land tax and army:** [[person/sulayman-ibn-sad-al-khushani|Sulayman ibn Sa'd, mawla of Khushayn]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Seal:** Amr ibn al-Harith, mawla of Amir ibn Lu'ayy (until he died); then Janah, his mawla {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Treasuries and storehouses:** Abd Allah ibn Amr {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Guard:** Khalid ibn al-Rayyan {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Chamberlain:** Sa'id, his mawla; then Muhammad ibn Abi Suhayl, mawla of Marwan (some say) {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Judgeship of Medina:** Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid ibn Jariya (appointed by Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, then dismissed); then [[person/abu-bakr-ibn-hazm|Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 312}
- **Judgeship of Kufa:** [[person/amir-al-shabi|Amir al-Sha'bi]] {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 313}
