## Overview
Abu Mansur Iliyas ibn Abi al-Qasim Samku ibn Muslan ibn Abi Yazul, amir of the Banu Wasul in Sijilmasa. According to Ibn Khaldun he was an Ibadi of the Safriyya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1619-1620}

## Rule
They appointed him in place of his brother Ilyas, when they rose against him and deposed him in 194 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1619-1620}

## Sijilmasa
He built the wall of Sijilmasa in the thirty-fourth year of his rule. Their rule was strengthened in his time. He completed its building and construction, laid out in it the cisterns and palaces, and at the end of the second century AH the people moved there. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1619-1620}

The year of death is not recorded. It needs an additional source.
