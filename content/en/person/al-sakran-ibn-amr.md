## Overview
[[person/al-sakran-ibn-amr|Al-Sakran ibn Amr]] ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Wadd al-Qurashi al-Amiri, brother of [[person/suhayl-ibn-amr|Suhayl ibn Amr]]. Musa ibn Uqba mentions him among the emigrants to Abyssinia, and Ibn Ishaq says the same. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.591} See [[event/first-migration-to-abyssinia|the first migration to Abyssinia]].

## Marriage
The Prophet married after him his wife [[person/sawda-bint-zama|Sawda bint Zam'a]], whom her brother Hatib married to him, in Ibn Ishaq's account. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.591}

## His end
The views on this matter differ in the Isaba; they are set out in the list of views at the top of the page.

## In Abyssinia ("Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" p. 117)
He was the husband of [[person/sawda-bint-zama|Sawda bint Zam'a]] and emigrated with her to Abyssinia, and he died there or after returning to Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.117}
