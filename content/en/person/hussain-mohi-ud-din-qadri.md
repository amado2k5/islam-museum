# Hussain Mohi-ud-Din Qadri

Hussain Mohi-ud-Din Qadri is a Pakistani economist, academic and author, and the younger son of Shaykh-ul-Islam Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri, the founder of Minhaj-ul-Quran International.{cite:minhaj-hussain-qadri-phd} Minhaj-ul-Quran's own reporting introduces him as "the son of Shaykh-ul-Islam Dr Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri".{cite:minhaj-hussain-qadri-dclg-meeting} His elder brother is Hassan Mohi-ud-Din Qadri, described in the movement's reporting as the elder son of Tahir-ul-Qadri.{cite:minhaj-hassan-phd}

## Education

He was born in Lahore on 24 October 1982 and educated at Aitcheson College.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} His university profile records an undergraduate degree at York University in Toronto, an MBA from Sciences-Po Paris in 2005, and a PhD in Economics at Victoria University, Melbourne.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} His doctoral thesis analysed trade flows among Muslim countries and the potential for a free trade area.{cite:minhaj-hussain-qadri-phd} The profile also records a Harvard qualification in innovation and sustainability completed in 2020, and that he received learning in Shari'ah and the classical Islamic sciences, with his father among his teachers.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile}

## Posts and work

He is President of Minhaj-ul-Quran International.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} At Minhaj University Lahore he is Deputy Chairman of the Board of Governors and a professor in the School of Economics and Finance.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} The same profile lists him as Chairman of Minhaj Education Society, which runs some 650 schools and colleges across Pakistan, Chairman of Aghosh Orphan Care Homes, CEO of the Al-Mawakhat microfinance company, and Chairman of Minhaj Halal Certification Pakistan.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} He is a Senior Fellow of the University of Melbourne.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile} His university profile credits him with 50 authored books and records that he received a Global Islamic Finance Award in September 2019.{cite:mul-hussain-qadri-profile}

## Audience

His YouTube channel @drhussainqadri had 548,000 subscribers, 4,684,926 total views and 2,391 videos as of 5 November 2026.{cite:vidiq-drhussainqadri}
