## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Askar al-Ta'i, known as Burhan al-Din al-Qirati, was a poet and one of the notables of Cairo. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 726 AH, 1326 CE, and his death as 781 AH, 1379 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 49}.

## Life
He occupied himself with law and letters, and lived beside the sanctuary at Mecca, where he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 49}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names a diwan of verse he called Matla' al-Nayyirayn, and a literary collection named al-Washah al-Mufassal; both are printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 49}.
