## Summary
Khadija Benguenna is an Algerian journalist and senior news anchor, born in 1965, best known as a founding member of Al Jazeera; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential figures in media. {cite:muslim500-khadija-benganna} {cite:wikipedia-khadija-benganna}

## Career
After early work in Algerian broadcasting and journalism, she joined the founding team of Al Jazeera in Qatar in 1996 and presented the channel's first news broadcast, becoming one of its defining anchors through its rise to a global network. {cite:wikipedia-khadija-benganna}

## Work
She has anchored major rolling coverage — wars, elections and the Arab Spring — hosted programmes including the long-running discussion show Shari'a and Life early in the channel's history and later talk formats, and interviews heads of state and public figures. {cite:wikipedia-khadija-benganna}

## Recognition
She has received Arab media awards and honours for journalistic professionalism, and has been described in coverage as one of the pioneering women anchors of Arab satellite news. {cite:wikipedia-khadija-benganna}
