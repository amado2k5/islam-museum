## Overview
Abu'l-Muzaffar Fakhr al-Din 'Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Hafiz Abi Sa'id 'Abd al-Karim ibn al-Hafiz Abi Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Imam Abi'l-Muzaffar Mansur al-Maymi al-Marwazi the Shafi'i; jurist, muhaddith and musnid of Khurasan, born in 537, he transmitted great books — al-Bukhari, the Musnad of Abu 'Awana, Sunan Abi Dawud, the Jami' of al-Tirmidhi, the Tarikh of al-Fasawi and the Musnad of al-Haytham ibn Kulayb; he heard Wajih al-Shahhami, Abu'l-As'ad al-Qushayri and many others, his father sent him travelling to Marw, Nishapur, Herat, Bukhara and Samarkand, and the father compiled him an eighteen-part mu'jam. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Standing
A mufti versed in the madhhab who transmitted copiously — people travelled to him, the hafiz Abu Bakr al-Hazimi heard from him, and Ibn al-Salah, al-Diya' al-Maqdisi, al-Zaki al-Barzali and al-Muhibb ibn al-Najjar transmitted from him; he compiled his own forty hadiths, the leadership of the Shafi'is of his town ended with him, and the Sam'ani house was sealed in his person. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
