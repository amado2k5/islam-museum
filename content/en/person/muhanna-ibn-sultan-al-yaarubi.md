## Overview
Muhanna ibn Sultan ibn Majid ibn Mubarak ibn Bil'arab (Bil'arab is also called Abu al-Arab), of the Ya'rubi line, was the sixth of the Ya'rubi imams of Oman. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1133 AH, 1720 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 316}.

## His imamate
He was given the oath of allegiance at the fort of al-Hazm after the death of Sultan ibn Sayf; al-Zirikli names 1131 AH at this point, and says the people lived in security in his days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 316}.

## The revolt of Ya'rub ibn Bil'arab
Ya'rub ibn Bil'arab ibn Sultan rose against him, calling for the imamate of Sayf ibn Sultan ibn Sayf, whose death al-Zirikli dates to 1155 AH. Muhanna did not hold his ground, and Ya'rub seized him and killed him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 316}.
