## Overview
Al-Dahhak ibn Muzahim al-Hilali, the Qur'an commentator of Khurasan. He met [[person/said-ibn-jubayr|Sa'id ibn Jubayr]] in al-Rayy and wrote down the commentary from him. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.425}

The book says that he was among the scholars whom Abd al-Malik chose to educate his son Yazid before the caliphate. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.425}

## Death
Accounts of his death differ. The editor's note quotes Ibn Qutayba as saying that he came to Khurasan, settled there and died in 102 AH. The Gregorian conversion (about 721 CE) is derived. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## His commentary in al-Tabari's Jami' al-Bayan
The thesis counts the commentary of al-Dahhak ibn Muzahim al-Hilali al-Balkhi of Khurasan (d. 105 AH in its footnote, from Ghayat al-Nihaya) among [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]]'s sources, and says he transmitted from Ibn Umar and [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]]. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.47} Al-Tabari had it both through other works and directly; in most direct quotations he uses two chains: "I was told from al-Minjab ibn al-Harith: Bishr ibn Umara told us, from Abu Rawq, from al-Dahhak", and "Abu Kurayb told us: Uthman ibn Sa'id told us, from Bishr ibn Umara, from Abu Rawq, from al-Dahhak". {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.48} The thesis's death year (105 AH) differs from the 102 AH reported from Ibn Qutayba above.
