## Overview
Yaghmurasan ibn Zayyan ibn Tabit, founder of the state of the [[group/bani-abd-al-wad|Banu Abd al-Wad]] in Tlemcen, of the Zanata tribe. Ibn Khaldun reports that he besieged Tlemcen for eight years and then entered it, took the title al-Mu'tadid, and was killed in his war against it in the year 681 AH {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1707-1708}.

What the summary says of his relations with the Banu Marin and the Hafsid call is general, and needs an additional source.
