## Summary
Suhaym ibn Wathil al-Riyahi, chief of Banu Riyah, whose famous rivalry with Ghalib ibn Sa'sa'a — al-Farazdaq's father — came during a famine that struck Kufa. Ghalib slaughtered a camel for his household, Suhaym slaughtered one like it, and they kept raising the stakes until Ghalib slaughtered a hundred camels on the fourth day — more than Suhaym had. But when the people entered Kufa, Suhaym slaughtered three hundred camels and told them: see to your eating. It happened in 'Ali ibn Abi Talib's caliphate; asked whether eating them was lawful, he ruled it forbidden, saying: these were slaughtered for something other than food. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
