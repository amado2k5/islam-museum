## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Sayyid al-Batalyawsi, Abu Muhammad, from Batalyawsa, who moved to Valencia and died there. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2048}

## Works
A scholar of letters and languages, a good teacher and a reliable, precise transmitter. His work "al-Insaf" treats the causes of disagreement among Muslims. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2048}
