## Overview
Sinan ibn Anas al-Nakha'i, whom al-Mas'udi counts among those who killed al-Husayn on the day of al-Taff. He stabbed him, then dismounted and cut off his head.

al-Mas'udi reports that the body of al-Husayn bore thirty-three stabs and thirty-four blows {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.60-61}.
