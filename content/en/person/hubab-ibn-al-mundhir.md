## Summary
Hubab ibn al-Mundhir ibn al-Jamuh al-Khazraji, a Companion from the Ansar and the author of the counsel about the water on the day of [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.211}.

## The counsel about water
On the night before the battle he asked the Prophet: "Is this a place God caused you to alight, which we may neither advance beyond nor fall back from, or is it opinion, war and stratagem?" When the Prophet said that it was opinion, war and stratagem, he advised lodging the army at the water nearest the enemy, filling in the wells beyond it, and building a basin to drink from, so that none could drink from beyond it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.211}.

The Prophet took his advice. The Muslims camped at the water and cut Quraysh off from it, since Quraysh had camped on the far bank behind the dune {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.211}.

## His counsel at Badr (al-Raheeq al-Makhtum p.211)
He asked the Prophet whether the position was revelation or opinion and stratagem. When he was told it was opinion, he advised them to camp at the water nearest the enemy, fill in what lay beyond it, and build a basin. The Prophet said that he had advised by way of opinion ([[event/badr-expedition-setup|the preparations before Badr]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.211}.

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"
In his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] takes al-Hubab as his example of "the advising follower": middling leadership ability, high challenge and high effectiveness. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:33:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:41:00} He relates his stance at Badr, when he asked whether the camp site was revelation or opinion and then advised camping at the water nearest the enemy, and the Prophet took his advice. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:36:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:38:00} At the Saqifa, he says, al-Hubab proposed "a commander from us and a commander from you". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:39:00} In his account he was among those who stood firm with the Prophet at Uhud. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep07 00:41:30} He adds that at the siege of Ta'if al-Hubab advised moving the camp after arrows had struck the Muslims, and that before Uhud he was one of the scouts who went into the Quraysh camp. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:30:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:32:00}
