## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yusuf ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Bakr al-Qaysi al-Tifashi, a scholar and writer from the Qays, named after Tifash in the Maghrib. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he was born in 580 AH (1184 CE) and died in 651 AH (1253 CE). Both dates come from one source and need a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
