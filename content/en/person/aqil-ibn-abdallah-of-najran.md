## Overview
"Aqil ibn Abdallah" is named among the elders of the people of Najran whom Banu Khuthaym sent to al-Hadi to ask safety for them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}.

## Detail
Al-Hadi promised them well and cut nothing, and when they asked two days he gave them respite {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. The name reads "Aqil" in the page image; the text gives no lineage, tribe or office beyond that he was an elder of Najran, and nothing more is ascribed to him.
