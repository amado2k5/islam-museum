## Summary
Haddad Alwi Assegaf (born 13 March 1966) is an Indonesian nasheed singer and actor. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-en} His first album, Cinta Rasul 1 (1999), is the best-selling nasheed album in Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-en} He is recorded in this lane as a nasheed artist, a category the founder's genre ruling of 9 November 2026 places in scope. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-id}

## Career
He began in 1997 as a religious singer with two sholawat albums, Nur Muhammad and Ziarah Rasul. {cite:tokoh-haddad-alwi} His name rose to public fame in 1999 when he collaborated with the child singer Sulis, singing Islamic songs most of them on love of the Prophet. {cite:tokoh-haddad-alwi} Between 1999 and 2004, under Sholla Studio, Haddad and Sulis produced six sholawat and nasyid albums titled Cinta Rasul. {cite:tokoh-haddad-alwi} The Indonesian article describes Cinta Rasul (1999) as the best-selling religious album in the history of Indonesian music. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-id}

## A note on his birthplace
The sources read disagree about where he was born: the Indonesian article's infobox gives Banjarnegara in Central Java, while the English article's text and the TokohIndonesia biography give Jakarta, and a Solo newspaper profile gives Solo. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-id} {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-en} {cite:tokoh-haddad-alwi} All of them agree on the date, 13 March 1966, and this record therefore gives the date and records the disagreement rather than choosing a birthplace. {cite:wikipedia-haddad-alwi-en}
