## Overview
Al-Muthanna ibn Haritha ibn Salama ibn Damdam al-Shaybani, one of the leaders of the conquest of Iraq. Al-Baladhuri counts him as the first of the conquerors of al-Sawad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the conquest of Iraq
Al-Baladhuri says that he used to raid al-Sawad with men of his people, the [[group/banu-shayban|Banu Shayban]]. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] wrote him a covenant for those of them who embraced Islam to fight the Persians. He camped at Khufan and called on his people, and they embraced Islam. Then Abu Bakr wrote to him to listen and obey [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], and he met him at al-Nabaj, and met Jaban at the river of blood. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His name is linked with [[event/muthanna-commission|the commission of al-Muthanna]] and [[event/conquest-of-al-hira|the conquest of al-Hira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Tabari
His name appears in the index of the third volume of al-Tabari together with Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud, in the reports of the year 13 AH, under the title "The report of al-Muthanna ibn Haritha and Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.630}
