## Summary
Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr, the eyes of intelligence on the Prophet and his father during the nights of the [[event/ghar-thawr|cave]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## His role
His sister [[person/aisha|A'isha]] described him as "a sharp-witted, quick-grasping youth"; he would spend the night with them in the cave, then slip out before dawn and appear among Quraysh at Mecca as one who had slept there — nothing he heard being plotted against them but he memorised and brought them when darkness fell {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 165}. [[person/amir-ibn-fuhayra|Amir ibn Fuhayra]] would trail him with his sheep to erase his tracks {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 165}.

## After the hijra
After the hijra he led his father's household to Medina, A'isha among them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 173} {cite:zad-al-maad}. He was killed at al-Ta'if in Ibn al-Zubayr's war, on the well-known account {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.
