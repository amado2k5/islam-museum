## Summary
Utarid ibn Hajib al-Tamimi, orator of the ten-man [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]] delegation that came to Medina after [[event/sariyya-of-uyayna-to-banu-tamim|Uyayna ibn Hisn's raid]] in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

## The boasting match in the mosque
The delegation showed their wish for a contest of boasting and put forward their orator Utarid ibn Hajib to speak; the Prophet bade [[person/thabit-ibn-qays|Thabit ibn Qays ibn Shammas]], Islam's orator, answer them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}. When the two orators and the two poets had finished, al-Aqra' ibn Habis said: his orator is more eloquent than ours — and the whole delegation became Muslim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
Utarid was his people's most eloquent orator; he stood to speak in the Prophet's mosque, the Muslims' orator answered him, and they admitted he outdid their man — and entered Islam.
