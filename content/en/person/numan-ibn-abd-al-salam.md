## Summary
al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam, Abu al-Mundhir, one of the early people of Isfahan in the classes of the transmitters. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 661, sequence 662, on page 389. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/389}

## His father's estate and his companions
His father, Abd al-Salam, held the ruler's affairs and died leaving a fine estate and much wealth; al-Nu'man left all of it, turning from it in asceticism. He kept company with Sufyan al-Thawri and Malik ibn Anas. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/389}

## The dream about Medina
Abu Muhammad ibn Hayyan relates from Abu Abd Allah al-Kisa'i that he was told a man saw in a dream an angel on the wall of Medina telling another to turn over. The other asked: how can I turn when al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam is standing in prayer? Nothing more of the dream is given. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/389}
