## Overview
Dirar ibn al-Azwar al-Asadi, one of the companions of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], whose story is recorded by al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## With Malik ibn Nuwayra
He led a detachment sent against Banu Tamim after Buzakha. He met [[person/malik-ibn-nuwayra|Malik ibn Nuwayra]], fought him, and took him and a group of others prisoner, and brought them to Khalid. Khalid ordered their necks struck, and Dirar undertook the killing of Malik. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/killing-of-malik-ibn-nuwayra|the killing of Malik ibn Nuwayra]].

It is said that Malik told Khalid that he had not apostatised. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Umar's time
Dirar entered upon [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] with Malik's property, and said that he had killed him after being told that no one would pardon him. Umar said to him: "May God kill you." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data contains an account of his death and of his position regarding Ali, which is not clear; an additional source is needed.
