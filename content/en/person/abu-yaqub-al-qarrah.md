## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Ishaq b. Ibrahim b. Muhammad al-Sarakhsi then al-Harawi al-Qarrah, hafiz and muhaddith of Herat; he transmitted from Zahir b. Ahmad al-Sarakhsi and a great host — his shaykhs exceeded twelve hundred souls — and composed many works; ascetic, righteous, of little in the world, aged seventy-seven. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 244}
