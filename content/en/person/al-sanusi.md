## Overview
The imam Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn al-Husayn al-Sanusi, a scholar of the Maghrib. He wrote "Umm al-Barahin" on doctrine, a summary containing all the doctrines of monotheism, and ended it with the two words of the testimony. He then wrote a brief commentary on it. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.635} See [[work/umm-al-barahin|Umm al-Barahin]].
