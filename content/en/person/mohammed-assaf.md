## Summary
Mohammed Assaf is a Palestinian pop singer born in 1989, raised in the Khan Younis refugee camp in Gaza, who became the most celebrated Palestinian singer of his generation after winning Arab Idol in 2013; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential figures. {cite:muslim500-mohammed-assaf} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## Rise
Born in Libya to a Gazan family and raised in Khan Younis, he sang at local weddings and events and famously travelled for days — crossing borders and checkpoints — to reach the Arab Idol auditions in Cairo, where he won the 2013 season performing the patriotic song Ali al-Kefiyyeh. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## Stardom
His victory was celebrated across Palestine and the Arab world as a rare unifying moment; he was named a UNRWA Goodwill Ambassador and has since released albums and singles mixing pop production with Palestinian folk themes. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## Later career
He performs internationally, serves as a judge on Arab talent shows, and is the subject of the 2015 feature film The Idol directed by Hany Abu-Assad, dramatising his journey from Gaza to the stage. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## Assessment
He is regarded as a folk hero of Palestinian pop — the refugee-camp singer whose victory was embraced as collective joy; commentary on his political entanglements is reported attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf} {cite:muslim500-mohammed-assaf}


## Arab Idol win
His 2013 Arab Idol victory — won after famously travelling from Gaza and singing nationalist and folk repertoire — was covered as a pan-Arab event, and the title 'son of Palestine' attached to him across Arab media. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## UNRWA and advocacy
Appointed a UNRWA Goodwill Ambassador, he used his platform for Palestinian refugee causes and sang for displaced children — a role that tied his pop career to the Palestinian story. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf}

## Music and reach
His albums and singles — mixing khaleeji, Levantine and Egyptian pop — tour festivals across the Arab world and beyond; he remains among the most streamed Palestinian artists. {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-assaf} {cite:muslim500-mohammed-assaf}
