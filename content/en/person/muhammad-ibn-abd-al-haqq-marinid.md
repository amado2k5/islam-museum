## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Haqq al-Marini, known as Abu Darba, who took charge of the Banu Marin after his brother [[person/uthman-ibn-abd-al-haqq-marinid|Uthman]], and who was the son of [[person/abd-al-haqq-ibn-mahyu|Abd al-Haqq ibn Mahyu]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Rule
Ibn Khaldun says that he followed his brother's custom in subduing the lands of the Maghrib and in collecting fines and offices. He met a Roman commander in single combat; they exchanged two blows, the Roman died from one, and Muhammad was wounded on the face by the other, so the scar remained, and from it came his title. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His death
In 642 AH the Almohads sent an army with the Arab tribes, the Masmuda and the Roman hosts, and the Banu Marin met them in the valley of Yabash. Both sides endured; then Amir Muhammad died in the battle at the hands of a Roman chief, and the Banu Marin were routed and took refuge in the mountains of Ghiyatha. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The Gregorian date of his death (1244 CE) is derived from the Hijri date.
