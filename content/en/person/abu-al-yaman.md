## Summary
Abu al-Yaman, a companion of good and the learned son of Sulayman. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 535, sequence 536, on page 163. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/163}

## The greatest name of God
Ahmad ibn Abi al-Hawari relates that he heard Abu Sulayman al-Darani say: there was an old man among us whom people said knew the greatest name of God. I went to him and said: O uncle, we have heard you know the greatest name of God. He said: O my nephew, do you know your heart? I said: yes. He said: when you see it grow tender, that is the greatest name of God. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/163}
