## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim b. Hilal b. Ibrahim b. Zahrn b. Habun al-Harrani, the Sabian — author of the celebrated Epistles and of exquisite verse. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## The chancery
He was secretary of the insha' at Baghdad for the caliph and for 'Izz al-Dawla Bakhtiyar b. Mu'izz al-Dawla b. Buwayh al-Daylami, and took charge of the Diwan al-Rasa'il in 349. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
