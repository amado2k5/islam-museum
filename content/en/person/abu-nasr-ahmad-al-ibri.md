## Overview
Abu Nasr Ahmad b. al-Faraj b. 'Umar al-Ibri (d. 506, Baghdad), father of the calligrapher Shahda; al-Sam'ani said: he was a servant, then Ahmad b. al-Faraj al-Ibri married his daughter — the calligrapher Shahda — and his rank rose until he became al-Muqtafi's intimate {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
