Muhammad Wilkerson (born 22 October 1989), known as "Mo", is an American football defensive end from Linden, New Jersey. A Temple standout, he was selected by the New York Jets in the first round of the 2011 NFL draft and remained with the Jets for seven seasons, earning a Pro Bowl selection in 2015 and Second-team All-Pro honours in 2013 and 2015, before a final season with the Green Bay Packers in 2018.{cite:wikipedia-muhammad-wilkerson-en}{cite:wikidata-muhammad-wilkerson}

Coverage of his upbringing describes a Muslim household in Linden — Ummah Sports calls him raised by a Muslim single mother — and records that he sponsored his mother's lifelong-dream trip to Mecca.{cite:wikidata-muhammad-wilkerson} No source consulted names his own affiliation or any school, and this record settles none.
