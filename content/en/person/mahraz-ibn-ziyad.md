## Summary
Mahraz ibn Ziyad, one of the Arab amirs of Ifriqiya; he sheltered al-Hasan ibn 'Ali, the last Zirid king, after al-Mahdiyya fell to the Franks in 543; he was killed at the battle of Setif on Thursday in the middle ten of Rabi' II 555. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
