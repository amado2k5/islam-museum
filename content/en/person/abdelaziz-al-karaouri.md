# Abdelaziz al-Karaouri

## Summary

Abdelaziz al-Karaouri (died 31 May 2025) was a Moroccan jurist, imam and reciter from Casablanca, known for his devout voice and polished recitation, who trained many memorisers of the Qur'an and students of religious learning. {cite:wikipedia-abdelaziz-al-karaouri-ar} {cite:wikidata-abdelaziz-al-karaouri}

## Career

Born and raised in Casablanca's Derb al-Fuqara quarter, he memorised the Qur'an as a young man. He led prayers at the al-Tawba and al-Taysir mosques and then at al-Qadi Iyad Mosque in Sidi Maarouf, which drew large congregations for the tarawih. {cite:wikipedia-abdelaziz-al-karaouri-ar}

He also worked as an education officer at a private school from 1999 and was active in Qur'an associations and religious life in Morocco. The article says that as a young man he sang at wedding halls, and that he left this in the late 1990s to devote himself to the Qur'an. {cite:wikipedia-abdelaziz-al-karaouri-ar}

## Death

He died on 31 May 2025 after a long illness with cancer, at a private clinic in Bouskoura near Casablanca; the funeral prayer at al-Qadi Iyad Mosque drew a large crowd of worshippers and scholars, and he was buried at Sidi Mas'ud cemetery. {cite:wikipedia-abdelaziz-al-karaouri-ar} {cite:wikidata-abdelaziz-al-karaouri}
