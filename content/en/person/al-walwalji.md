## Overview
Ishaq ibn Abi Bakr al-Walwalji, Abu al-Makarrim Zuhayr al-Din, was a Hanafi jurist of Walwalij beyond Balkh who died in 710 AH (1310 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.293}

He wrote al-Fatawa al-Walwaljiyya, of which a third part on fiqh survives in the Baghdad awqaf collection. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.294}
