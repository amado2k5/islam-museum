## Overview
Amr ibn Luhayy, chief of Khuza'a: the first to bring idols to the Hijaz and to institute the bahira, sa'iba, wasila and ham traditions; Bukhari reports him among the most severely punished on the Day of Resurrection.

## Life
The first to bring idols to the Hijaz was Amr ibn Luhayy, chief of Khuza'a; he saw the people of al-Balqa' in Syria venerating idols and asked what these were — they said: we ask them for rain and it rains, we seek their aid and we are aided — so he asked them for an idol, brought it to Mecca and ordered the people to worship it {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

And he brought Hubal into the Ka'ba's midst until idols multiplied across the Peninsula, and he instituted for the Arabs the customs of bahira, sa'iba, wasila and ham {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## In the hadith
Bukhari relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: I saw Amr ibn Amir — meaning ibn Luhayy — al-Khuza'i dragging his entrails in the Fire, and he was the first to turn loose the sa'iba camels {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
Amr ibn Luhayy was the first to bring idols to Mecca; because of him idols were worshipped in Arabia until Islam came.
