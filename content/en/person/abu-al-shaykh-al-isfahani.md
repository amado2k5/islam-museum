## Summary
Abu Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn Hayyan al-Ansari al-Isfahani, known as Abu al-Shaykh, was born in 274 AH and died in 369. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48} Heinen says he was renowned as a reliable scholar of hadith and tafsir, and that none of his works on hadith, tafsir and history had yet been edited or studied when he wrote. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48}

## In the biographers
Heinen cites [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]]'s Dhikr akhbar Isbahan, which calls him a companion (sahib) of Abu Dawud, and [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]]'s Tadhkirat al-huffaz, which says he was so pious that his students usually found him at prayer, unlike his contemporary al-Tabarani, whom they found merry and laughing. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48}

## Kitab al-Azama
Heinen sees these traits reflected in [[work/kitab-al-azama-abu-al-shaykh|Kitab al-Azama]], which became the chief source of al-Suyuti's [[work/al-haya-al-saniyya-suyuti|al-Hay'a al-saniyya]]. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48}
