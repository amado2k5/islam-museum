## Overview
Al-Da'i ibn Abi al-Sa'ud ibn al-Zurayi ibn al-Abbas ibn al-Mukarram al-Hamdani, the first named man of the Banu al-Zurayi and the first to gather rule after the Banu al-Sulayhi. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Taking Aden
He took Aden from the hands of the lord of al-Za'aza'i after great difficulty and costs among the Bedouin. He died seven months after taking it, in 533 AH, and his son al-Aghar succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The museum's data count him as the founder of the [[polity/zurayid-dynasty|Zurayid state]]. The conversion of his death year to 1138 CE is derived.
