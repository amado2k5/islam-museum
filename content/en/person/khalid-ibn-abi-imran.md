## Overview
Khalid ibn Abi Imran al-Tujibi al-Tunisi, a Tunisian jurist from the Tujib, known by the kunya Abu Umar. He is counted among the Successor scholars in Ifriqiya. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His death is reported in 127 AH, around 745 CE, approximately. It needs an additional source.
