## Overview
Abu Talib ibn Abd al-Sami', whose name was Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Sami' ibn Abi Tamam al-Wasiti, was a Qur'an reciter and an accredited notary witness (mu'addal). Ibn al-Imad records him among those who died in 621 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 167}.

## Studies and writings
He read the Qur'anic readings under Abd al-Aziz al-Samati and others, heard in Baghdad from Hibat Allah ibn al-Shibli and a group of teachers, wrote several fine works, and gave his attention to hadith and learning {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 167}.

## Death
He died in Muharram of that year, aged eighty-three {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 167}. Muharram 621 AH falls at the start of 1224 CE, and a birth around 538 AH is a derived calculation from the age given.
