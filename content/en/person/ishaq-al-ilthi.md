## Summary
Ishaq al-Ilthi the model ascetic, cousin of Talha ibn al-Muzaffar; Hanbali faqih enjoining the good and forbidding the wrong, fearing none and moved by no reproach in truth; he confronted the caliph al-Nasir and those below him, faced the caliph and split him open with the truth; al-Nasih ibn al-Hanbali said: he is today the shaykh of Iraq and the one standing in censure of the jurists, the fuqara' and others in what they took licence in; it is said none in his time denounced drunkenness more than he — for it he was jailed a while; he has many epistles of censure. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 163}
