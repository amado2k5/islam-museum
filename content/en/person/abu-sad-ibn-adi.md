## Overview
Abu Sa'd ibn Adi: Abd al-Muttalib's maternal uncle of Banu al-Najjar; he came to Mecca with eighty riders and drew his sword at the Sacred Precinct until his nephew had his right restored.

## Life
When Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf seized his nephew Abd al-Muttalib's property after al-Muttalib's death, Abd al-Muttalib asked Quraysh men for aid against his uncle and they said they would not come between him and his uncle; so he wrote to his maternal uncles of Banu al-Najjar verses calling them to his aid {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

His uncle Abu Sa'd ibn Adi rode out with eighty riders until he alighted at al-Abtah in Mecca; he went for Nawfal where he sat in the Precinct with Quraysh's elders, drew his sword and said: "by the Lord of this House, if you do not return my nephew's property to him I will make this sword have its way with you." Nawfal returned it and made Quraysh's elders bear witness {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Abu Sa'd was Abd al-Muttalib's uncle; he came from Medina with eighty horsemen and unsheathed his sword until his nephew's uncle returned the seized property.
