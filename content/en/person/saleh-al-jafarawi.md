## Summary

Saleh al-Jafarawi ⁦(22 November 1997 – 12 October 2025)⁩ was a Palestinian citizen journalist, photographer and singer from Gaza whose frontline videos of life under war made him one of the Strip's most-watched chroniclers, with millions of followers across social platforms. He was shot dead by gunmen in Gaza City's Sabra neighbourhood on 12 October 2025, days after a ceasefire took hold — a killing the Committee to Protect Journalists said underlines the dangers journalists in Gaza face even after the guns fall silent. {cite:wikipedia-saleh-al-jafarawi} {cite:cpj-saleh-aljafarawi} {cite:muslim500-2026 p. 233}

## Life and formation

Born in Gaza City on 22 November 1997, he studied media and journalism at the Islamic University of Gaza, graduating in 2019. He began producing content around 2018, covering the Great March of Return as a freelance contributor to local outlets, and also competed as a table-tennis player. {cite:wikipedia-saleh-al-jafarawi}

## The work

During the two-year war that began in October 2023, al-Jafarawi's videos — displaced families, bombed streets, moments of song and laughter inside the catastrophe — reached audiences of millions on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, making him, in the coverage of him, one of Gaza's most prominent independent documenters. He was himself displaced more than once, lost his home to bombing, and said publicly that he lived in fear 'for every second'. He also sang: his renditions of Palestinian songs became part of his following. His reporting, per the sources, led to threats against his life. {cite:wikipedia-saleh-al-jafarawi} {cite:cpj-saleh-aljafarawi}

## The killing

On the morning of 12 October 2025 — days after the ceasefire between Israel and Gaza's factions took effect — he went out to film in the Sabra neighbourhood, where clashes had erupted between an armed group and security forces. He did not return. Footage verified by Al Jazeera's Sanad agency showed his body in a press flak jacket. Palestinian sources told the networks he had been shot by members of an armed group while documenting the fighting; the Committee to Protect Journalists recorded that he was killed by 'an armed group' on assignment and called for journalists to be kept out of the line of fire. The museum reports the circumstances attributed, as the sources report them. {cite:cpj-saleh-aljafarawi} {cite:wikipedia-saleh-al-jafarawi}

He was buried the same day his brother was released from an Israeli prison in the ceasefire's captive exchange. {cite:cpj-saleh-aljafarawi}

## Assessment

Al-Jafarawi's listing in The Muslim 500's obituaries records the figure the war made: a self-made documenter whose phone camera became a principal window for millions onto civilian life in Gaza, and whose death in its final days became part of the ledger of journalists killed covering the conflict. {cite:muslim500-2026 p. 233}
