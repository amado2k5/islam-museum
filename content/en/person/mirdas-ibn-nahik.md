## Overview
A man of the Huraqa of [[group/juhayna|Juhayna]], an ally of [[group/banu-murra|the Banu Murra]]. Ibn Ishaq's account says nothing of him except that he was killed in [[event/raid-of-ghalib-on-banu-murra|the raid of Ghalib ibn Abdallah on the land of the Banu Murra]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271} [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] and a man of the Ansar overtook him. When they drew their swords on him he said: "I bear witness that there is no god but God", and they killed him. The Prophet disapproved of this and asked Usama: "Who will vouch for you with 'there is no god but God'?" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## Notes on the report
- The attribution of his lineage to the Huraqa of Juhayna is given by Ibn Hisham from Abu Ubayda, and the editors point to disorder in the wording in some copies. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}
