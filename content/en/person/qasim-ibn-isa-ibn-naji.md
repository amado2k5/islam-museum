## Overview
Qasim ibn Isa ibn Naji al-Tanukhi al-Qayrawani, a Maliki jurist and hafiz (d. 837 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3480}

## Works
His works include a commentary on the Mudawwana, al-Shafi fi al-fiqh and a commentary on Ibn Abi Zayd's risala. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3480}
