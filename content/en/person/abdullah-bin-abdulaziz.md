# Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Al Saud

Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Al Saud was the sixth King of Saudi Arabia, reigning from 1 August 2005 until his death on 23 January 2015. He was the tenth son of King Abdulaziz, by Fahda bint Asi Al Shuraim of the Al Rashid family, and was born at Riyadh on 1 August 1924. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz-en} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz-ar}

## Life before the throne

In 1961 he became mayor of Mecca, his first public office, and in 1962 he became commander of the Saudi Arabian National Guard, a post he held for decades, transferring its duties to his son in 2010. On 13 June 1982, the day his brother Fahd became king, Abdullah became Crown Prince. After King Fahd suffered a serious stroke in 1995, Abdullah was the kingdom's de facto ruler for the following decade. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz-en}

## Reign

He became king on 1 August 2005 on Fahd's death and reigned for almost ten years, keeping his country's close alliance with the United States and pursuing measured reforms at home. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz-en}

## Death

King Abdullah died at Riyadh on 23 January 2015 and was succeeded by his brother, Crown Prince Salman bin Abdulaziz. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz-en} {cite:wikidata-abdullah-bin-abdulaziz}
