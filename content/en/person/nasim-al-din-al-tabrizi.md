## Nasim al-Din al-Tabrizi

Nasim al-Din al-Tabrizi, a resident of Aleppo and shaykh of the Hurufis there. His followers grew in number, and his doctrine arose and spread, until the sultan ordered his execution. He was beheaded, his skin was flayed and his body was crucified, in 820 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 153}.
