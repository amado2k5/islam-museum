## Overview
Isa ibn Ali ibn Abdallah ibn al-Abbas. Al-Baladhuri relates from al-Waqidi that he was at Marash, and that he raided in that year, when Michael left the Darb al-Hadath in 161 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The day of Michael
Michael went out with eighty thousand men and reached the city gate. The clients of Isa and the people of the city came out against him to fight, and shot arrows at him. He feigned flight, then turned on them and killed eight of the clients. The rest took refuge in the city, and he besieged them, then withdrew toward Jayhan. The data describe Isa as "the amir of Marash on the day of the raid", taking part in [[event/michael-incursion-161|the raid of Michael in 161 AH]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
