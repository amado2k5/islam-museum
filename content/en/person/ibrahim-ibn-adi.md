## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Adi, a man whom al-Mas'udi mentions in the strife over the killing of Amr ibn Sa'id. When [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] was struck by Yahya ibn Sa'id on the hand, Ibrahim carried him into the room of papers (bayt al-qarati) in the tumult. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.97}

What is not stated here: his lineage and the rest of his reports. It needs an additional source.
