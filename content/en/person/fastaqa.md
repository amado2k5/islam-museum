## Overview
Fustuqa, Muhammad ibn Ali, one of the pupils of al-Karabisi. Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the book "Gharib al-Hadith" and the book "Tashih al-Athar", and says that most of the two was never completed. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.257}
