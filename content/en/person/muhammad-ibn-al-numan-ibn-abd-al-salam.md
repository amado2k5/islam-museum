## Summary
Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man ibn Abd al-Salam kept company with Waki', Sufyan ibn Uyayna and Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash, and had firm scrupulousness and a steady mind. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 667, sequence 668, on page 391. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}

## Striving, then ease
His constant practice was striving and endurance until he weakened and his mind was feared for; then he returned to what was easy and gave up rough food and clothing. A phrase describing him is unclear in the scan and is not given. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}

## His sayings
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Subayh relates that he heard Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man say: a dirham that you give to remove a wrong is dearer to me than a hundred thousand that you give in charity. Another saying is unclear in the scan and is not given. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/391}
