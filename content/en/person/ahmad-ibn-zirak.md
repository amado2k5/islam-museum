## Overview
Ahmad ibn Zirak, police chief of Baghdad. Caliph al-Qahir billah was quick to arrest the people of the house of Mu'nis in 321 AH. His house was afterwards looted, along with the houses of those attached to [[person/ibn-muqla|Ibn Muqla]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.190-191}
