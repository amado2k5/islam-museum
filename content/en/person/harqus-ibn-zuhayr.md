## Summary
[[person/harqus-ibn-zuhayr|Hurqus ibn Zuhayr al-Sa'di]] was one of the rebel leaders in Basra at the time of [[event/battle-of-the-camel|the Battle of the Camel]], and later became one of the leaders of [[event/battle-of-nahrawan|the Kharijites]] and was killed at al-Nahrawan. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 177}

## In Basra
Al-Sallabi narrates that [[person/al-qaqa-ibn-amr|al-Qa'qa' ibn Amr]] told Talha and al-Zubayr that six hundred of Uthman's killers who were in Basra were all killed except Hurqus ibn Zuhayr al-Sa'di, who took refuge with his kinsmen of Banu Sa'd. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 51} In A'isha's letter to the people of Syria, as cited by al-Sallabi, Hurqus ibn Zuhayr alone escaped death among Uthman's killers in Basra. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 44}

## At al-Nahrawan
Al-Sallabi mentions that Hurqus ibn Zuhayr was one of the commanders of the Kharijites at al-Nahrawan, noting that he commanded the infantry in their army and was killed there in the year 38 AH. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 177}
