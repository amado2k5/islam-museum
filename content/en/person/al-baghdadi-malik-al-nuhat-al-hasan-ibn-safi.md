## Overview
Abu Nizar al-Hasan ibn Safi ibn Abd Allah al-Baghdadi, a grammarian, jurist and man of letters from Baghdad, titled "King of the Grammarians". He was born to a father who had been a client of a merchant. The encyclopaedia says he lived between 489 AH and 568 AH (1096–1173 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.632-633}
