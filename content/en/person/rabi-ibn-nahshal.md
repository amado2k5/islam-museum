## Overview
Rabi' ibn Nahshal. al-Baladhuri reports that he wrote the letter of Ibn Amir to the great man of Herat concerning the peace of Herat, Badghis and Bushanj, and that Ibn Amir sealed the letter {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
