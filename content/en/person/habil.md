## Summary
The son of Adam who was killed, and a keeper of livestock. Al-Thaʿlabi reports that he offered a fat ram from the best of his flock, and his offering was accepted {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.44}.

## In the report
The book says that Habil held inwardly to contentment with God's decree and submission to His command {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.44}. It says that he refused to raise his hand against his brother, saying that God accepts only from those who guard themselves against wrong, and then Qabil killed him {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.45}.

One report from Ibn ʿUmar says that the one killed was stronger than his killer, but that his scruple prevented him from raising his hand against his brother {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.45}.
