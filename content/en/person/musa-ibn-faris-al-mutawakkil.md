## Overview
Musa ibn Faris ibn Abi Inan ibn Ali al-Marini, Abu Faris, al-Mutawakkil 'ala Allah, was one of the kings of the Marinid state in the far Maghrib, born in 757 AH, 1356 CE and died in 788 AH, 1386 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}. He was one of the sons of the Banu Marin kings who had been banished to al-Andalus, and he lived for a time under the protection of the Banu al-Ahmar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}.

## Coming to power
Al-Ghani billah ibn al-Ahmar equipped him and sent him to take the Maghrib from the Marinid al-Mustansir billah, Ahmad ibn Ibrahim; he landed at Ceuta and handed it to Ibn al-Ahmar, advanced on Fez, met no resistance and settled there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}. Al-Mustansir tried to drive him off and failed, and Musa captured him and sent him to Ibn al-Ahmar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}. The pledge of allegiance to Musa was completed in 786 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}.

## Death
His vizier Mas'ud ibn Rahhu (Abd al-Rahman) Ibn Masay took control of the affairs of state; Musa wished to be rid of him, and Ibn Masay prompted someone to slip poison to him, and he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}. His reign lasted two years and four months {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 315}.
