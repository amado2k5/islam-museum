## Summary
Mehmet Emin Kırgil is a Turkish religious broadcaster and dream interpreter, known for his dream-interpretation slot on Beyaz TV's Hayatta Her Şey Var and for his own YouTube channel of dream-interpretation broadcasts. {cite:beyaz-tv-mehmet-emin-kirgil} {cite:youtube-mehmet-emin-kirgil}

His channel counted 302,000 subscribers as of 10 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-mehmet-emin-kirgil}

This record describes his television and online broadcasting career neutrally; it endorses none of his interpretations and presents none of them as fact. {cite:beyaz-tv-mehmet-emin-kirgil}

## Programmes and work
Kırgil has appeared over years as the dream-interpretation guest of Hayatta Her Şey Var, presented by Nur Viral on Beyaz TV, and programme listings describe his slot as weekly. {cite:beyaz-tv-mehmet-emin-kirgil}

In the programme's segments, Kırgil interprets live on air the dreams that viewers send in or relate by phone and message. {cite:beyaz-tv-mehmet-emin-kirgil}

This build read programme segments dated from March 2019 to October 2026, a span that shows a sustained television presence over years. {cite:beyaz-tv-mehmet-emin-kirgil}
