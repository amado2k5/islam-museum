## Overview
Muhyi al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad, known as Piri Reis. He was a navigation scholar and commander in the Egyptian navy and the Ottoman fleet, and one of the pioneers of nautical cartography {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death and affiliation
He died in 958 AH (1552 CE), according to the "Encyclopedia of the Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". By the museum's data he is Sunni {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
