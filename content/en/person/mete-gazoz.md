## Summary
Mete Gazoz is a Turkish recurve archer, born in Istanbul on 8 June 1999. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} He won the gold medal in the men's individual event at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics and a bronze medal in the men's team event at Paris 2024. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} At the 2023 World Archery Championships in Berlin he won the men's individual recurve title, becoming the first Turkish archer to win a recurve gold medal at the World Championships. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en}

## Early life
Gazoz was born in Istanbul on 8 June 1999. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-ar} He began archery in 2010 and made his international debut in 2013. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} In his early career he won the silver medal in the Recurve Junior Men Team event at the 2013 World Archery Youth Championships in Wuxi, China. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en}

## Career
Gazoz represented Turkey at the Rio 2016 Olympics, winning his first match before losing in the round of 16. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} At the 2018 Mediterranean Games in Tarragona he won the gold medal in the individual 70 m category. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics he won the men's individual gold medal, bringing Turkey its first-ever Olympic medal in archery. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} At the 2023 World Championships in Berlin he defeated Canada's Eric Peters to win the individual recurve title. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} At the Paris 2024 Olympics he added a bronze medal in the men's team event. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en}

## Significance
Gazoz holds Turkey's first Olympic medal and first recurve world title in archery, on his documented record of results. {cite:wikipedia-mete-gazoz-en} {cite:wikidata-mete-gazoz}
