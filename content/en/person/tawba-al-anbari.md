## Overview
Tawba ibn Abi al-Asad Kaysan al-Anbari al-Basri, Abu al-Muwarri', is described by al-Zirikli as one of the governors and as a man of hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 88}. His origin was in Sijistan, he was born and raised in al-Yamama, then moved to Basra; he was a client (mawla) of Ayyub ibn Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 88}. The entry gives his years as 57 to 131 AH, 677 to 748 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 88}.

## Governorships
He came as a delegate to Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz; Yusuf ibn Umar appointed him over Sabur and then over al-Ahwaz {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 88}. He died in the plague {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 88}.
