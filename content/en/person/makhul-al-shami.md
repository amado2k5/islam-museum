## Overview
Makhul al-Shami, a scholar of Syria. Al-Mas'udi mentions him as present with Sulayman at Marj Dabiq, when his testament was written. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.172-173}

## At Marj Dabiq
After al-Zuhri had read the testament, Makhul stood up and asked: "Where is Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz?" Umar was at the far end of the people. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.172-173}

The rest of his life and his transmissions are not recorded in this entry's data, and need an additional source.
