## Overview
Suwayd ibn Abd Allah al-Tamimi, who was in the delegation of the people of Basra to al-Mansur. Sulayman ibn Ali sent him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The delegation
The delegation carried a map of al-Bataiha, and told al-Mansur that they feared their water would become salty if the Subaytiyya were excavated {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
