## Overview
Al-Safadi, in al-Wafi bi-l-Wafayat, names him Abd Allah ibn Awn ibn Amir Misr al-Hilali al-Baghdadi, Abu Muhammad al-Adami al-Kharraz, and says he died in 232 AH; the Common Era year, 846, is derived {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 p. 211}.

## Transmission
Muslim narrated from him, al-Nasa'i narrated from a man who had it from him, and Abu Zur'a and others also narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 p. 211}.

## Judgements of the scholars
Al-Safadi says that Yahya ibn Ma'in and al-Daraqutni both held him reliable {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 17 p. 211}.
