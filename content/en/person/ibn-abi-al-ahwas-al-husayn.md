## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad ibn Abi al-Ahwas al-Qurashi al-Fihri, known as Ibn al-Nazir. A reciter, Qur'an commentator, transmitter of hadith and scholar of Arabic. He was born in Jaén in 603 AH (1206 CE), settled in Granada, and died in 699 AH (1299 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.350}
