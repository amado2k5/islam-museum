## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Hanafi, so called by descent from Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya, al-Sha'rani, Abu Muhammad, was one of the scholars of the Sufis. He was born at Qalqashanda in Egypt and raised at Saqiyat Abi Sha'ra, a village of al-Minufiyya, to which his name refers, so that he is called al-Sha'rani or al-Sha'rawi; he died in Cairo. The entry in al-A'lam gives his years as 898 AH, 1493 CE, to 973 AH, 1565 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## His printed works
Al-Zirikli says he left many works. Those he marks as printed are al-Anwar al-qudsiyya fi marifat adab al-ubudiyya; al-Bahr al-mawrud fi al-mawathiq wa-l-uhud; al-Badr al-munir, on hadith; Tanbih al-mughtarrin fi adab al-din; Tanbih al-mughtarrin fi al-qarn al-ashir, on where they departed from their pure forebears; al-Jawahir wa-l-durar al-kubra and al-Jawahir wa-l-durar al-wusta; al-Durar al-manthura fi zubad al-ulum al-mashhura, a tract; Durar al-ghawwas, from the fatwas of Shaykh Ali al-Khawwas; al-Kibrit al-ahmar fi ulum al-Shaykh al-Akbar; Kashf al-ghumma an jami al-umma; Lataif al-minan, known as al-Minan al-kubra; Lawaqih al-anwar fi tabaqat al-akhyar, known as the Tabaqat al-Sha'rani al-kubra; Lawaqih al-anwar al-qudsiyya fi bayan al-uhud al-Muhammadiyya; an abridgment of al-Suwaydi's Tadhkira in medicine; an abridgment of al-Qurtubi's Tadhkira, sermons; Madarik al-salikin ila rusum tariq al-arifin; Mashariq al-anwar; al-Minah al-saniyya, a commentary on the testament of al-Mitbuli; Minah al-minna fi al-talabbus bi-l-sunna; al-Mizan al-kubra; and al-Yawaqit wa-l-jawahir fi aqaid al-akabir {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.

## His manuscript works
Among those he marks as manuscript are al-Ajwiba al-mardiyya an a'immat al-fuqaha wa-l-sufiyya; Adab al-qudat; Irshad al-talibin ila maratib al-ulama al-amilin; Bahjat al-nufus wa-l-asma wa-l-ahdaq fima tamayyaza bihi al-qawm min al-adab wa-l-akhlaq, in his own hand; Huquq ukhuwwat al-Islam, sermons; Dhayl Lawaqih al-anwar, a small part; al-Qawaid al-kashfiyya fi al-sifat al-ilahiyya; and Irshad al-mughaffalin min al-fuqaha wa-l-fuqara ila shurut suhbat al-umara, a tract held in the Rabat library {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 178}.
