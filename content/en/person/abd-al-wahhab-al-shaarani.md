## Summary
Abd al-Wahhab al-Sha'rani: Sufi and author, linked in the museum's record to Egypt. The Biblissima record places his life between 1493 and 1565. {cite:biblissima Q16845} The museum's record card names al-Tabaqat al-Kubra as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «ʿAbd al-Wahhāb ibn Aḥmad ibn ʿAlī al-Anṣārī al-Šaʿrānī» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q16845}
