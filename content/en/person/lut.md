## Overview
Lut, nephew of [[person/ibrahim|Ibrahim]] and prophet to his people, as the sources report. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## In the hadith
Al-Bukhari relates that the Prophet said: "May God have mercy on Lut; he used to take refuge in a strong pillar." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3387}
