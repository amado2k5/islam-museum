## Overview
Abu Salama 'Ali b. Idris the Almohad, surnamed al-Mu'tadid and known as al-Sa'id; he held the caliphate after his father's son al-Rashid 'Abd al-Wahid who died drowned — he was king of the furthest Maghrib and part of al-Andalus, then went out toward Tlemcen and besieged a castle there; al-Murtada 'Umar b. Abi Ibrahim ruled after him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
