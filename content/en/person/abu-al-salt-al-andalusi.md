## Overview
Abu'l-Salt Umayya b. 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi'l-Salt of al-Andalus, the Dani; born at Denia around 480, he took learning from a company of Andalusians such as Abu'l-Walid al-Baji, came to Alexandria in 489 — and al-Afdal Shahanshah, amir of the armies, banished him from Egypt in 505. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Works in captivity
During his confinement he composed the epistle on working the astrolabe, al-Wajiz on astronomy, the book of simple drugs, a logic book called Mut'at al-dhihn, and the Intisar refuting 'Ali b. Ridwan; then he moved to al-Mahdiyya and died there on Monday at the start of 529. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
