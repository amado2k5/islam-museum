## Overview
Sa'id ibn Mansur ibn Shu'ba, the hafiz and imam, shaykh of the Haram Abu 'Uthman al-Khurasani al-Marwazi — also given as al-Taliqani, then al-Balkhi, then the Meccan resident — author of Kitab al-Sunan. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1832}

## Travels
He heard in Khurasan, the Hijaz, Iraq, Egypt, Syria and al-Jazira from Malik ibn Anas, al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Hushaym, Hammad ibn Zayd, Fudayl ibn 'Iyad, Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna, Yahya ibn Abi Za'ida and many others. He died in 227 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1832}
