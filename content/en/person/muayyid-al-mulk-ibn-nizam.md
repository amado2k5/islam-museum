## Overview
Mu'ayyid al-Mulk Abu Bakr ibn Nizam al-Mulk, who arrived for his marriage to the daughter of Abu'l-Qasim ibn Ridwan al-Bay' and lodged at his father-in-law's house at Bab al-Maratib; when no one received him, the caliph sent him a conciliator, carried him robes of honour and permitted him to ride at Bab al-Maratib — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 16 p. 156}
