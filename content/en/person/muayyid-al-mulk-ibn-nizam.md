## Summary
Mu'ayyid al-Mulk, son of the vizier Nizam al-Mulk al-Tusi; he entered Baghdad in 468 for his marriage to the sultan's daughter, and moved to a house his father had built beside the madrasa — the vizier went to him in procession {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.641}; he attended al-Muqtadi's bay'a in 467, and in the madrasa-souk riot of 470 feared for his house and sent to the 'Amid Abu Nasr, who dispatched the Daylamis and the guard to him; he also opened Takrit. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.634,655,668}
