## Overview
Muhammad Qadri Pasha was one of the men of the judiciary in Egypt. He was born in Mallawi in 1237 AH (1821 CE); his father's family came from Anatolia and his mother was an Egyptian of Husayni descent {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. He studied in Mallawi and Cairo, completed his studies at the School of Languages, and excelled in the knowledge of languages {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His posts
The khedive chose him as tutor to the heir apparent. He passed through many posts: counsellor in the Mixed Courts, minister of justice (nazir al-haqqaniyya), then minister of education, then again minister of justice, which was his last post. He died in Cairo in 1306 AH (1888 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His works
Among his printed books al-Zirikli names "al-Durr al-Muntakhab min Lughat al-Faransis wa-al-'Uthmaniyyin wa-al-'Arab"; "Mufradat fi 'Ilm al-Nabatat"; "Murshid al-Hayran", on legal transactions under the shari'a; "Qanun al-'Adl wa-al-Insaf" on the problems of endowments; "al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyya fi al-Ahwal al-Shakhsiyya"; "al-Durr al-Nafis fi Lughatay al-'Arab wa-al-Faransis"; "Qatr Anda' al-Diyam", on literature; and "Qanun al-Jinayat wa-al-Hudud", which he translated from French. Among his manuscripts are his verse collection and "Tatbiq ma Wujida fi al-Qanun al-Madani Muwafiqan li-Madhhab Abi Hanifa"; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
