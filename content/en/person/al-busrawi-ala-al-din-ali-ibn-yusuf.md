## Overview
Ala al-Din Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Ahmad al-Dimashqi al-Busrawi, a Shafi'i jurist from Damascus, attributed to Busra al-Sham. He attended Shaykh al-Islam Radi al-Din al-Ghazzi and excelled in jurisprudence. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his birth as about 1438 CE and his death as 1499 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
