## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd al-Rahman al-'Unqari al-Tamimi al-Najdi was a Hanbali judge; his forebears had held a petty emirate at Tharmida' in the Washm villages of Najd. Born there in 1873 CE, he lost his sight at seven, memorised the Qur'an and kept the company of scholars in his town and then in Riyadh, and owned a library at al-Majma'a. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.98}

He held the judgeship of Sudayr and settled at al-Majma'a for thirty-six years, during which he was seconded in 1340 AH to teach at al-Artawiyya and settle disputes among its people, and he dictated his Hashiyat Rawd al-Murbi' in Hanbali fiqh. He resigned about a year before his death to devote himself to teaching, and died in 1373 AH (1954 CE); two versions of his Fatawa survive at Riyadh University. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.98}
