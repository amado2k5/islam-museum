## Overview
Yusuf Dhu Nuwas: the last powerful king of Himyar before the Abyssinian occupation, the convert to Judaism who burned Najran's Christians in the trench of 523 CE.

## Life
When Yusuf Dhu Nuwas ibn Tubban As'ad took rule over Yemen he fell upon the Christians of Najran and called them to embrace Judaism; when they refused he dug them the trench and burned them with fire, making no distinction between man, woman, child and elder {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

The slain are said to number between twenty and forty thousand; this happened in October of 523 CE, and the Qur'an records part of this story in Surat al-Buruj {cite:tafhim-al-quran}.

## Significance
His atrocity was the cause of the Abyssinians' occupation of Yemen on the Roman fleet in 525 CE — and so his house's independent kingship ended {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Dhu Nuwas was a tyrant king in a story the Qur'an tells; he burned Najran's people in a trench of fire because they refused to leave their religion.
