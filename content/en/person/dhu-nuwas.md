## Summary
Yusuf Dhu Nuwas was the Jewish king of [[group/himyar|Himyar]], son of [[person/tuban-asad-abu-karib|Tubban As'ad Abu Karib]]. In 523 CE he led his campaign against the Christians of [[place/najran|Najran]] and dug the trench for them: the People of the Ditch in Surat al-Buruj {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 24} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 40} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 6/297-298}.

## The Trench
When Yusuf Dhu Nuwas, the son of Tubban As'ad, took power over Yemen, he attacked the Christians of Najran and called them to embrace Judaism. When they refused, he dug the trench for them and burned them with fire. He made no distinction between man and woman, young children and old men. It is said that the number killed was between twenty thousand and forty thousand, and this took place in October 523 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 40} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/20-36}. See [[event/massacre-of-najran|the trench of Najran]].

## Legacy
This led to the vengeance of the Christian powers, active under the Roman emperors. They incited the Abyssinians, and the Abyssinians occupied Yemen in 525 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 24} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 25}.

## Notes for researchers
The word "it is said" (yuqal) in the death toll is recorded as a mark of doubt. Comparison with inscriptions and Syriac sources has not yet been added.

## For young visitors
Dhu Nuwas is a king in the story told in the Qur'an. He burned the people of Najran in a trench of fire because they refused to give up their religion.
