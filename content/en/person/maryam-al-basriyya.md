## Summary
Maryam al-Basriyya was a devout woman of [[place/basra|Basra]]; Ibn al-Jawzi gives her entry in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 592) and says she served [[person/rabia-al-adawiyya|Rabi'a al-Adawiyya]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710}

## Love of God
Whenever she heard the teachings on love she was overwhelmed; she attended the gathering of a preacher who spoke on love, and died in the gathering. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710} From Abd al-Aziz ibn Umayr: she stood at the start of the night reciting [[passage/quran-42-19|al-Shura 19]], on God's gentleness with His servants, and did not move past it until morning. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.710}

## For young visitors
Maryam was a woman of Basra who helped the devout Rabi'a; she loved God so much that she spent a whole night repeating one verse about God's kindness to His servants.
