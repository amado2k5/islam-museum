## Overview
Husayn ibn Ali ibn Hasan ibn Muhammad al-'Ushari was a jurist of usul with poetry, of Baghdad, his nisba being to 'Ushara, a town on the Khabur. Born and educated at Baghdad, fiqh so dominated him that he was called the little Shafi'i. He died in 1195 AH (1781 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.247}

He was sent from Baghdad to teach at Basra in 1194 AH and died there before the year turned. He left a diwan of uneven verse, a treatise on the imamate, a supercommentary on Ibn Hajar's Sharh al-Hadramiyya, glosses on al-Mahalli's Jam' al-Jawami', and more; his hand was beautiful and he copied many books. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.247}
