## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Sa'id ibn Abi Jamra al-Azdi al-Andalusi, Abu Muhammad, was a scholar of hadith of the Maliki school; his origin was al-Andalus and he died in Egypt in 695 AH (1296 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.89}

His books include Jam' al-Nihaya, his well-known abridgement of Sahih al-Bukhari; Bahjat al-Nufus, a commentary on Jam' al-Nihaya; and al-Mara'i al-Hisan on hadith and dreams. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.89}
