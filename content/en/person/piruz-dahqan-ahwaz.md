## Overview
Piruz Dahqan, lord of the market of al-Ahwaz. According to al-Baladhuri, al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba fought him and then made peace with him for money, but he broke the agreement. Abu Musa then took the city by force in 17 AH {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
