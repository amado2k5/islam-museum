## Overview
Ka'b al-Ahbar, one of the Jewish scholars (ahbar) of Yemen, who became Muslim in the caliphate of Abu Bakr or Umar. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## With Umar
It is related that Umar asked him what they find in the Torah. He said: "I find you in the Torah being killed as a martyr." He also said to him: "We find you in the Book of God at one of the gates of Hell, preventing people from falling into it." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## Iram of the Pillars
Ibn Khaldun relays from Mu'awiya that he asked him about Iram of the Pillars after the story of Abdallah ibn Qilaba. He described a short, red-haired man going out in search of his camels, then turned and saw Ibn Qilaba, and said: "By God, this is that man." {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.26}

Ibn Khaldun counts this among the entries of error for the commentators. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.26}
