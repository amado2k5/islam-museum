## Overview
Musa ibn Sulayman al-Juzjani al-Hanafi, Abu Sulayman, is called by al-Dhahabi the great scholar and imam, a companion of Abu Yusuf and Muhammad {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}. The Hanafi label is al-Dhahabi's own, given in the entry's heading {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}. He says he was truthful and beloved of the hadith people, and that he wrote works {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Abu Yusuf and Muhammad and from Ibn al-Mubarak {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}. The judge Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Birti, Bishr ibn Musa, Abu Hatim al-Razi and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}.

## What is said of him
Ibn Abi Hatim said he declared unbelievers those who say the Qur'an is created {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}. It is said that al-Ma'mun offered him the judgeship and he declined, pleading that he was not fit for it, and that al-Ma'mun excused him, so that his standing rose among the people for refusing {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}. Among the sources al-Dhahabi lists are al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil, al-Jawahir al-Mudiyya and al-Fawa'id al-Bahiyya {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}.

## His death
Al-Dhahabi gives no date for his death in this entry, so the card keeps the date it already carried {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3980}.
