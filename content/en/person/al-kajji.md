## Overview
Abu Muslim al-Kajji; his father moved to Basra and built a house of gypsum and brick, telling the workmen 'kaj akaj' — use the gypsum — until the word stuck and he was called al-Kajji. Abu Muslim was among the leading hadith men. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 282}
