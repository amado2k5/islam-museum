## Overview
Abu Muslim al-Kajji, one of the great hadith scholars with a high chain of transmission. He wrote the book "al-Sunan" and the book "al-Musnad". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.324}

## Why he was named
The source relates that his father moved to Basra and built a house of gypsum and brick. He used to say to the workmen "Kaj ackaj", meaning use the gypsum, and the word came to be his nickname, so he was called al-Kajji. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.324}
