# Nishatunnisa Mohani

Begum Nishatunnisa Mohani (1884 – 18 April 1937) was an Indian freedom fighter, journalist and social worker, the wife of Hasrat Mohani {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}.

## Early life

She was born in Lucknow; the article's infobox and lead give 1884 {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}, its biographical section gives 1885, and Wikidata gives 1884 at year precision {cite:wikidata-nishatunnisa-mohani}. She was educated at home, following the tradition of the era, and was proficient in Urdu, Arabic, Persian and English {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}.

## Work in the movements

She worked extensively in the non-cooperation and Khilafat movements {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}. She started the first khadi clothes shop, the Aligarh Khilafat Store, and with its income supported Gandhi's magazine Young India {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}. She persevered in the publication of her husband's newspaper Urdu-e-Mualla in the face of British legal actions against it {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}.

She condemned Gandhi for not standing up for the resolution for complete independence that her husband Hasrat Mohani had proposed {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}. Later she left the Indian National Congress and continued fighting for the interests of farmers and labourers {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}.

## Personal life and death

She was married to Hasrat Mohani, the independence campaigner who coined the phrase "Inquilab Zindabad" {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}. She died on 18 April 1937 {cite:wikipedia-nishatunnisa-mohani-en}.
