## Summary
[[person/zayd-ibn-arqam|Zayd ibn Arqam]] ibn Zayd ibn Qays ibn al-Nu'man al-Khazraji; his kunya is disputed — said to be Abu Amr or Abu Amir {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}.

## His battles
He was judged too young on the day of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]]; his first battle was the Trench — or it is said al-Muraysi'. He campaigned with the Prophet seventeen campaigns, established in the Sahih {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}.

## An orphan with Ibn Rawaha
Ibn Ishaq said: Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr told me from some of his people, from Zayd ibn Arqam, who said: I was an orphan in Abdullah ibn Rawaha's care, and he took me out with him riding behind him — meaning to Mu'ta {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}. His mention recurs in the [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta campaign]].

## The story of Surat al-Munafiqun's descent
It was he who heard [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]] say what the Qur'an relates in Surat al-Munafiqun: [[passage/quran-63-8|al-Munafiqun 8]]. He told the Prophet, who asked Abdullah, who denied it — and God sent down confirmation of Zayd; the Prophet said to him: "God has confirmed you, Zayd". This is established in the two Sahihs {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}.

## His knowledge and accounts
He has many hadiths, and also accounts from Ali; from him related Anas in writing, Abu al-Tufayl, Abu Uthman al-Nahdi, Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla, Abd Khayr and Tawus {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}. Abu al-Minhal said: I asked al-Bara' about exchange and he said: ask Zayd ibn Arqam, for he is better than me and more learned {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}.

## His death
He witnessed Siffin with Ali and died at Kufa in al-Mukhtar's days, year sixty-six — or it is said sixty-eight {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 509}.
