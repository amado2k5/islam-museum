## Overview
Mawand, deputy of Ratbil. He asked Ma'n ibn Za'ida for safe conduct on condition that he carry him to the Commander of the Faithful. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## To Baghdad
Ma'n granted him safe conduct and sent him to Baghdad with five thousand of their fighters. [[person/al-mansur|Al-Mansur]] honoured him and assigned him an allowance for his firewood. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
