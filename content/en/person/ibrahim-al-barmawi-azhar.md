## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Shihab al-Din ibn Khalid, Burhan al-Din al-Barmawi al-Ansari al-Ahmadi al-Azhari {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 67}, was a Shafi'i jurist and shaykh of the mosque of al-Azhar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 68}. He died in 1106 AH, and al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 67}.

## A note on the date
Al-Zirikli's heading prints 1894 CE beside 1106 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 67}. The figure 1894 does not match 1106 AH, which corresponds to 1694 and 1695 CE, so the museum keeps the year 1695 recorded on his card.

## His nisba
Al-Zirikli says that his nisba is to Barma (with a kasra on the ba') in the Gharbiyya province of Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 68}.

## Works
His books include a gloss on al-Qarafi's commentary on the poem printed as Gharami Sahih, on hadith terminology (manuscript); a gloss on the commentary Fath al-Wahhab of Zakariyya al-Ansari, three volumes (manuscript); a gloss on the commentary on al-Rahbiyya, on the law of inheritance, in his own hand in the library of Zuhayr Jawish in Beirut; and a gloss on the commentary on Ghayat al-Taqrib, on jurisprudence (printed) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 68}.
