## Overview
Mahmud ibn Umar Aqit al-Sanhaji al-Timbukti, Abu al-Thana, was the qadi of Timbuktu and one of the Maliki jurists; the entry gives his years as 865 to 955 AH, 1463 to 1548 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote several works, among them a "Taqyid" on the Mukhtasar of Khalil in jurisprudence, in two volumes, and "Tarikh al-Fattash fi Akhbar al-Buldan wa al-Juyush wa Akabir al-Nas", printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.
