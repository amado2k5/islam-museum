## Summary
Hilal ibn al-Wazir al-Sufi sought refuge in his Lord, the All-Knowing. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 521, sequence 522, on pages 154 and 155. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/154} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}

## His look at a youth and his prayer
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Muhammad relates from his brother that he heard Khayr al-Nassaj say: I was with Hilal ibn al-Wazir al-Sufi when he looked at a youth. He recited a verse on God being a witness to what they do, and then said: O God, You are the witness to our deeds, the keeper of our works, the seer of our affairs, the hearer of our secrets, and You are keeper over everything. You know what the lookers hide in the depths of their breasts, the hidden secrets and inner desires; You distinguish truth from falsehood, and what passes through the hearts cannot be ascribed to You. Forgive Hilal what he earned against himself by his bad look. The verse is referred to by sense only. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}
