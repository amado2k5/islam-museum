## Overview
Humayd ibn Tirwayh al-Tawil, a Basran jurist. Al-Shirazi mentions him after Dawud ibn Abi Hind among the jurists of Basra. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}

The editor's note says that his name in Ibn Sa'd and al-Qutaybi is "Tarkhan". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}

Al-Shirazi does not mention the date of his death. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}
