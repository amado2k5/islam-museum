## Overview
Abdallah ibn As'ad ibn Ali al-Yafi'i, Afif al-Din, was a historian, researcher and Sufi, one of the Shafi'is of Yemen. He was born in 698 AH, 1298 CE, and died in 768 AH, 1367 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}. His name points to Yafi', of Himyar {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His life
He was born and brought up in Aden. He made the pilgrimage in 712 AH and returned to Yemen, then went back to Mecca in 718 AH, stayed there and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names "Mir'at al-Janan wa-'Ibrat al-Yaqzan fi Ma'rifat Hawadith al-Zaman", printed in four volumes; "Nashr al-Mahasin al-Ghaliya fi Fadl Mashayikh al-Sufiyya Ashab al-Maqamat al-'Aliya", printed; "al-Durr al-Nazim fi Khawass al-Qur'an al-'Azim", a printed treatise; "Marham al-'Ilal al-Mu'dila", printed; "Rawd al-Rayahin fi Manaqib al-Salihin", printed; and "Asna al-Mafakhir fi Manaqib al-Shaykh Abd al-Qadir", in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.
