## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Mu'afa b. Isma'il b. al-Husayn of Mosul, also known as Ibn al-Haddus, the Shafi'i; an imam-jurist, brilliant, fine, righteous, a litterateur — born at Mosul, he read fiqh there under Ibn Muhajir, then the qadi Fakhr al-Din al-Suhrawardi, then al-'Imad Ibn Yunus; he heard, transmitted, gave fatwa, wrote and debated. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 143}

## His works
Among them: al-Kamil fi'l-fiqh, a long work; Anas al-munqati'in, well known; a tafsir called al-Bayan; and al-Mujiz fi'l-dhikr. He died at Mosul in Sha'ban or Ramadan 630. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 143}
