## Summary
Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn Ibrahim ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Hasan, al-Ansari al-Maliki al-Mutayti, from the village of Mutayta near al-Jazira al-Khadra; a jurist and judge who held the judgeship of Shari'sh; the entry gives his death as 570 AH. His work is al-Nihaya wa-l-Tamam on documents and judgements. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7523}
