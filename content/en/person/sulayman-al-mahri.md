## Overview
Sulayman ibn Ahmad ibn Sulayman al-Mahri was a seafarer and astronomer nicknamed “the teacher of the sea,” his nisba going to Mahra ibn Haydan of Quda'a; he was a resident of Suqutra and counts among the pupils of Ibn Majid. He died about 961 AH (1554 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.121}

He wrote on the sciences of the sea, its winds, the states of stars and winds, and the sea-routes between the Arab lands, India, Java and China; the orientalist Gabriel Ferrand published five of his treatises together with treatises of Ibn Majid — Qiladat al-Shumus wa-Istikhraj Qawa'id al-Asus, Tuhfat al-Fuhul fi Tamhid al-Usul, al-Minhaj al-Fakhir fi 'Ilm al-Bahr al-Zakhir, and a commentary on the Tuhfa — and he wrote al-'Umda al-Mahriyya fi Dabt al-'Ulum al-Bahriyya. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.121}
