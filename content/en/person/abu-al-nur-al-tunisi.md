## Summary

Shaykh Abu al-Nur al-Tunisi al-Makki, a resident of Mecca. From childhood until he was thirty-five he lived in a state of rapture, longing, fear and awe of God, so intense that terror nearly killed him, and his reason was tested by it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 573}.

## A recitation of the Qur'an every fifteen days

Every fifteen days he completed a recitation of two-thirds of the Qur'an, and he fasted continually. He was so pure that people said he would not walk in the sun and that he had no shadow. He died in Ramadan of the year 931 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 573-574}.
