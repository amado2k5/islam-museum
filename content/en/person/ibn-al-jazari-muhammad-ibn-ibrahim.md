## Overview
Shams al-Din Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Abi Bakr al-Jazari. He originated from Jazirat ibn Umar, and the encyclopaedia describes him as one of the leading figures in the readings of the Qur'an and in hadith in his age. He was born in 658 AH (1259 CE) and died in 739 AH (1338 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
