# Imran ibn Mansur

Imran ibn Mansur (born 1990) is a London dawah preacher known as Dawah Man. His YouTube channel, launched as Naseeha Sessions, answered viewers' questions in a direct street style; in January 2016 his video feud with Imam Muhammad Asim Hussain over the Mawlid became a case study in academic writing on new online religious authority.{cite:therevealer-digital-islam-new-sheikhs}{cite:youtube-naseeha-sessions}

He later retired the Dawah Man persona, rebranding the channel first as Righteous and Rich and now as Chai With My Bhai, and works with Muslim entrepreneurs through ventures such as The Cave.{cite:freshly-grounded-imran-ibn-mansur}{cite:youtube-naseeha-sessions}
