## Overview
Hasan ibn Husayn ibn Ahmad, Badr al-Din ibn al-Tuluni: a Hanafi historian from Cairo, who lived from 836 to 909 AH (1432–1503 CE). He devoted himself to chanting in the Qur'anic readings and the call to prayer and the like, and he wrote books. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.188}

His works include the printed al-Nuzha al-Sunniyya fi Akhbar al-Khulafa' wa-l-Muluk al-Misriyya, an abridged history of the caliphs and kings of Egypt; a commentary on the Samarqandiyya primer; and Nuzhat al-Nufus wa-l-Khawatir, letters written to loved ones near and far. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.188}
