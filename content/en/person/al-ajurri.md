## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Ajurri, who died in 360 AH, and who wrote the reports of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 251}

## His work
Haji Khalifa attributes to him the book [[work/akhbar-umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Akhbar Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 251}

The date of death (360 AH, about 970 CE) is traditional, and the Gregorian conversion is derived. It needs an additional source to confirm it.
