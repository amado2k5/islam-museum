## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn 'Abd al-Razzaq ibn Dasa al-Tammar was a student of [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud al-Sijistani]] and one of the two transmitters through whom the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan]] is handed down, the other being [[person/abu-ali-al-lului|Abu 'Ali al-Lu'lu'i]]. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 10}

## What he relayed from his teacher
Ibn Dasa reports hearing Abu Dawud say that he wrote down five hundred thousand reports from the Prophet, selected from them what he put into the Sunan, and gathered there 4,800 reports, setting down "the sound, what resembles it and what comes near it". {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 10} This statement is one of the main witnesses to Abu Dawud's method in his book.
