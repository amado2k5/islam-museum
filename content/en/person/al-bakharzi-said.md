## Overview
Sa'id al-Bakharzi, the imam-exemplar, hafiz and gnostic — companion of Shaykh Najm al-Din al-Kubra; an imam in the Sunna and a head in tasawwuf, he transmitted from Najm al-Din Ibn al-Habbab, 'Ali b. Muhammad al-Mawsili and Rashid al-Ghazali, and produced forty hadiths. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 306}
