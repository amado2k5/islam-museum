## Overview
Isma'il ibn Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Nasri, an Andalusian man of letters and historian from the Banu Nasr of Granada. He lived after leaving Granada with his father, first in Bejaia and then in [[place/fez|Fez]]. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.335-337}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia gives his birth as around 1324 CE and his death as 807 AH (1404 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.335-337} These two dates come from one source, so they need an additional source.
