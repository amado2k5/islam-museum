## Overview
Chief qadi Shihab al-Din al-Sa'di al-Ansari of Damascus, of Salihiyya, the Hanbali; a dignitary and chief of Damascus's chiefs, of a house of knowledge and leadership — he held Damascus's judgeship by al-Burhan Ibn Muflih; his tenure was brief before removal, after which he gave office no thought, dwelling honoured in his Salihiyya home — with generosity, fine welcome and honour for callers. He died at Mecca on Thursday 3 Sha'ban 891 and was buried at al-Ma'la. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 350-351}
