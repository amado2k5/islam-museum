## Overview
Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab: chief of Banu Zuhra in lineage and nobility, father of Amina — so the Prophet's grandfather through his mother.

## Life
Abd al-Muttalib chose for his son Abdullah Amina bint Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab; her father was Banu Zuhra's chief in lineage and nobility, and she was counted then the best woman of Quraysh in lineage and standing {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Wahb is the Prophet's grandfather through his mother; he was chief of his clan Banu Zuhra of Quraysh.
