## Summary
Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab, chief of [[group/banu-zuhra|Banu Zuhra]] in lineage and honour, and father of [[person/amina-bint-wahb|Amina]], the mother of the Prophet. He was therefore the Prophet's maternal grandfather {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.53} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/156-158}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book mentions him in one place: when [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] chose for his son [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abdullah]] Amina bint Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhra ibn Kilab. At that time she was counted the most noble woman of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] in lineage and standing, and her father was the chief of Banu Zuhra in lineage and honour {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.53} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/156-158}. Zuhra ibn Kilab was the brother of Qusayy ibn Kilab, so the Prophet's lineage on his mother's side and his father's side meets at Kilab ibn Murra {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48}.

## What is not mentioned here
The book does not record his birth or death, or his religion, nor the name of Amina's mother. What al-Baladhuri reports about her is recorded on Amina's card.

## For young visitors
Wahb was the Prophet's grandfather through his mother Amina. He was the head of his family, Banu Zuhra, among Quraysh.
