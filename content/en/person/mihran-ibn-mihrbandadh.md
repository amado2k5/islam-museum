## Overview
Mihran ibn Mihrbandadh, a Persian commander. Buran daughter of Kisra sent him to the Muslims with twelve thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-355}

## The day of Mihran
He crossed the bridge to al-Buwayb, and the Muslims killed him on the day of Mihran in 14 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-355}

Jarir ibn Abdallah and al-Mundhir ibn Hassan al-Dabbi disputed over his killing. Al-Mundhir took his belt and Jarir took the rest of his spoils. It is said that al-Husn ibn Ma'bad al-Tamimi was among those who killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-355}
