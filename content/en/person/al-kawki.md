## Overview
A man of the Daylam known as al-Kawki, around whom the Daylam gathered in the days when the Alids appeared in Daylam and the region of Tabaristan in 253 AH. Al-Mu'tazz sent Musa ibn Bugha al-Kabir to him. He is connected with [[event/mutazz-daylam-campaign|al-Mu'tazz's campaign against the Daylam]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The museum's data do not record his full name, his birth or his death, which needs an additional source.
