## Summary
Mohsin Issa is a British billionaire entrepreneur who, with his brother Zuber, built EG Group from a single rented petrol station in Blackburn into one of the world's largest independent fuel-and-convenience retailers — before acquiring the Asda supermarket chain with private-equity partners in 2021. {cite:wikipedia-mohsin-issa}

## Formation
Born in 1971 in Blackburn, Lancashire, to Indian-Gujarati Muslim parents who had run a small shop, the Issa brothers bought a run-down petrol station in nearby Bury in 2001 and discovered that the profit lay not in fuel but in the attached convenience store and food outlets — the model they scaled relentlessly. {cite:wikipedia-mohsin-issa}

## EG Group
Through Euro Garages, later EG Group, the brothers rolled up thousands of petrol stations and roadside sites across Britain, then Europe, the United States and Australia, partnering with brands from Spar and Burger King to Starbucks. At its peak EG operated roughly six thousand sites and tens of thousands of employees, making the Issas two of the most prominent Muslim entrepreneurs in the world. {cite:muslim500-mohsin-issa}

## Asda and philanthropy
In 2021 Mohsin and Zuber, with the private-equity firm TDR Capital, acquired Asda — Britain's third-largest supermarket group — for about £6.8 billion; Mohsin later took on its chief-executive role before stepping back. The brothers are known donors to Muslim community causes in Lancashire and beyond. {cite:wikipedia-mohsin-issa}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as half of Britain's most celebrated self-made Muslim business story: two shopkeepers' sons who built a global retail empire from the petrol forecourt — and then bought a national supermarket. {cite:muslim500-mohsin-issa}
