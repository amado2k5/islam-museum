## Overview
Al-Shihab al-Qusi, the leading jurist — agent of the Bayt al-Mal in Syria and endower of the Qusi circle at the mosque; born at Qus, he travelled to Egypt in 590, came to Damascus in 591 and settled, hearing much in many lands, and attached himself to the Sahib Safi al-Din Ibn Shukr. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 268-269}

## His affairs
He produced for himself a mu'jam in four large volumes — al-Dhahabi says it contains much error; a littérateur, chronicler, eloquent and fluent, versed in fiqh, he conducted correspondence to the lands, held the agency of the Bayt al-Mal, advanced with kings, taught at his circle in the mosque of Damascus, and endowed his house as a Dar al-Hadith. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 268-269}
