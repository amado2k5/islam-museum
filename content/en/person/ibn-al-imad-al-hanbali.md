## Overview
Abd al-Hayy ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad Ibn al-Imad al-Akri al-Hanbali, Abu al-Falah, was a historian, jurist and man of letters. Al-Zirikli says he was born in the Salihiyya quarter of Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 290}.

## Life and death
He lived in Cairo for a long time and died in Mecca while on the pilgrimage {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 290}. The heading of his entry dates his death to 1089 AH, which is 1679 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 290}.

## Works
His best-known book is Shadharat al-Dhahab fi Akhbar man Dhahab, in eight parts {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 290}. He also wrote a commentary on the Hanbali legal manual al-Muntaha, a commentary on the badi'iyya of Ibn Hijja (a manuscript in Qatar), and epistles, among them Ma'tiyat al-Aman min Hanth al-Aiman, which is in his own hand and was in al-Zirikli's possession {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 290}.
