Plestia Alaqad (born 10 December 2001) is a Palestinian journalist and author from Gaza. She studied new media and journalism at Eastern Mediterranean University, graduating in 2022, and was about to start a new job when the Gaza war began in October 2023; her daily video diaries of life under bombardment drew millions of followers within weeks and were carried by broadcasters and newspapers around the world.{cite:wikipedia-plestia-alaqad-en}{cite:wikipedia-plestia-alaqad-ar}{cite:wikidata-plestia-alaqad}

In November 2023 she left Gaza with her family for Egypt and then Australia, fearing her reporting was endangering them. Her diary of the war's first weeks was published as The Eyes of Gaza in 2025; she was named to the BBC's 100 Women list in December 2024 and won a scholarship to study media at the American University of Beirut.{cite:wikipedia-plestia-alaqad-en}
