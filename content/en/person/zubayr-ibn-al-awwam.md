## Summary
Al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam ibn Khuwaylid al-Asadi, of the forerunners to Islam; his mother [[person/safiyya-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib]], the Prophet's aunt {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## His role at Badr
He was one of the Muslim army's two horsemen — the Muslims had only two horses — and command of the right wing was assigned to him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 205}. He went out on the scouting patrol with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]], which captured two lads of Quraysh's water-carriers, and the Prophet reckoned from the slaughtered camels an army of nine hundred to a thousand {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 210-211}.

## After Badr
One of the ten promised Paradise in the Sunni account; he was killed in 36 AH after withdrawing from the battle of the Camel {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.
