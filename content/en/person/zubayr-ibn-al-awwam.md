## Summary
Zubayr ibn al-Awwam ibn Khuwaylid al-Asadi, one of the first to embrace Islam. His mother was [[person/safiyya-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib]], the Prophet's aunt {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## His role at Badr
He was one of the two horsemen of the Muslim army; the Muslims had only two horses. He was given command of the right wing {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.205}. He went out on a reconnaissance patrol with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. They captured two boys who were water-carriers for Quraysh. From the camels slaughtered, the Prophet estimated the strength of the army as between nine hundred and a thousand {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.210-211}.

## After Badr
In the Sunni narration he is one of the ten given the glad tidings of Paradise. He was killed in 36 AH after leaving the Battle of the Camel {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad}.

## In the secret call (The Sealed Nectar, p.76)
The book counts him among those who embraced Islam through the call of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] during the years of the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]]. He was one of the eight who came before the people and formed the first rank and vanguard of Islam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. The book names him with his Asadi lineage {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76}.
