## Overview
Abd Allah al-Battal, the well-known brave champion. Al-Suyuti says that Khunjara was opened by his hand in the eighth year of the caliphate of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 402} See: [[event/hisham-conquests|the conquests of the reign of Hisham]].
