## Overview
Abu al-Walid Isma'il ibn Muhammad al-Shaqundi al-Andalusi, author of [[work/risalat-al-shaqundi-fadl-al-andalus|a treatise on the superiority of al-Andalus over the shore of the Adwa]], which al-Maqqari quotes many times in "Nafh al-Tib". {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.612-613}

## From his words
He describes Granada as the Damascus of the lands of al-Andalus, and says it was never without nobles, scholars and poets. Among the virtues of Seville in his view is the moderation of its air and the sweetness of its water. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.612-613} {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.550-551}
