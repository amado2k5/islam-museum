## Overview
Suwayd ibn Manjuf al-Sadusi, after whom "Suwaydan" is named, a land-holding that belonged to Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Suwaydan
The holding was four hundred jaribs. Ubayd Allah gave it to him when he visited him during his illness, after Suwayd had said: "If you give me what you gave Ibn Ma'mar, there is no harm for me." It was then named after him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
