## Summary
Khaled al-Bakar is a Canada-based sheikh and author who took ijazas in Qur'an, hadith, fiqh and sirah from scholars of Tripoli, Lebanon, then immigrated to Canada and devoted himself full-time to preaching and teaching at al-I'tissam Mosque in Laval, Quebec. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Scholarly formation
His Muslim 500 profile records ijazas in Qur'an, hadith, fiqh and the Prophet's biography from prominent scholars in Tripoli — a city long established in Sunni religious teaching — before he migrated to Canada and made preaching and instruction his full-time calling. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Ijaza and chain
The ijazas he took in Tripoli mean entry into the traditional chain of transmission: he studied recitation, hadith, fiqh and sirah with the city's sheikhs until they licensed him to transmit and teach — the scholarly capital he carried from Lebanon to Canada. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Teaching in Laval
He now teaches at al-I'tissam Mosque in Laval, and his lessons centre on three fields: the Prophet's biography, tafsir, and the divine names. His stated aim per the profile is to guide generations towards a balanced, moderate religious practice in the Western context. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Moderation in the West
His profile describes his aim as a "balanced and moderate religious practice in the Western context": a formula joining Levantine heritage to the needs of a congregation living in a secular society — lessons in sirah, tafsir and the divine names, and a discourse steering youth to hold their faith within daily life. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Works
He is best known for an acclaimed four-volume series on the Prophet's life, alongside recorded lessons and mosque sermons serving Quebec's growing Arabic-speaking congregation. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}

## Legacy
His path reads in the pattern of the emigrant preacher: classical formation in a Levantine city carried to an expatriate congregation in the West, where mosque lessons and sirah volumes become the community's reference. The Muslim 500 files him among Preachers & Spiritual Guides in Canada. {cite:muslim500-khaled-al-bakar}
