## Overview
[[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik]] appointed him over the amirate of Medina, Mecca and Ta'if together. The book says that Yazid combined these governorships in the course of joining the provinces to one another. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.435}

He was the governor whom Yazid sent to Medina; he struck Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Dahhak and took money from him when [[person/fatima-bint-al-husayn|Fatima bint al-Husayn]] complained, according to the book. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.428}

The book gives no date for his governorship.
