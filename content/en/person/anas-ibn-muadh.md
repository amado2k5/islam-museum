## Summary

Anas ibn Mu'adh ibn Anas ibn Qays al-Ansari, of Banu al-Najjar. Musa ibn Uqba, Ibn Ishaq and al-Waqidi list him among those at Badr; Urwa calls him Unays, in the diminutive. Reports of his death differ: Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Umara says he was killed at Bi'r Ma'una, while al-Waqidi says he died in the caliphate of Uthman. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.103}
