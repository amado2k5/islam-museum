## Overview
Umar ibn Sa'd, son of [[person/sad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad sent him as commander over four thousand to fight al-Husayn {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.

## Karbala
Ibn Ziyad promised him the governorship of al-Rayy if he were victorious. He pressed al-Husayn until he was killed on the day of Karbala. See [[event/battle-of-karbala|the battle of Karbala]] {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.

## Who killed al-Husayn?
The killing of al-Husayn is attributed to him because he was commander of the cavalry, as Ibn Abd al-Barr said. The people of Kufa reported that he had fought him, and Ibrahim ibn Sa'd denied this {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.
