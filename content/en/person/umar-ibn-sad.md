## Overview
Umar ibn Sa'd, son of [[person/sad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad sent him as commander over four thousand to fight al-Husayn {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.

## Karbala
Ibn Ziyad promised him the governorship of al-Rayy if he were victorious. He pressed al-Husayn until he was killed on the day of Karbala. See [[event/battle-of-karbala|the battle of Karbala]] {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.

## Who killed al-Husayn?
The killing of al-Husayn is attributed to him because he was commander of the cavalry, as Ibn Abd al-Barr said. The people of Kufa reported that he had fought him, and Ibrahim ibn Sa'd denied this {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.341-342}.

## In al-Dhahabi's Mizan al-I'tidal
In [[work/mizan-al-itidal|Mizan al-I'tidal]], [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] gives an entry to Umar ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri, with the symbol "[س]" meaning al-Nasa'i cites him. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 198} He judges that "in himself he is not suspect, but he took part in fighting [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]] and did what he did". {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 198} The reference is to the [[event/battle-of-karbala|battle of Karbala]].

**Narrating from him.** Shu'ba narrated from Abu Ishaq from al-Ayzar ibn Hurayth from Umar ibn Sa'd, and a man stood up and said to Abu Ishaq: do you not fear God, narrating from Umar ibn Sa'd? He wept and said: I will not do it again. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 198} Al-Ijli said: people narrated from him; a trustworthy Successor. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 198} Ahmad ibn Zuhayr asked [[person/yahya-ibn-main|Ibn Ma'in]] whether Umar ibn Sa'd was trustworthy, and he answered: how can one who killed al-Husayn be trustworthy? {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 199}

**His killing.** Al-Dhahabi reports from Khalifa that [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar]] killed him in the year 65. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 199}
