## Summary
Kecia Ali is an American Muslim scholar of Islamic studies and a professor at Boston University. Her writing focuses on Islamic law, ethics and gender, and it is read within the current of Islamic feminist scholarship. {cite:wikipedia-kecia-ali}

## Life and upbringing
Born in the United States in 1972, she studied religion and Islamic studies to the doctorate and went on to university teaching and writing. {cite:wikipedia-kecia-ali}

## Career and works
Her books include Sexual Ethics and Islam, which examines the juristic tradition on marriage and sexuality; a biography of Imam Shafi’i in a founders series; and The Lives of Muhammad, on the history of writing the Prophet’s biography in Muslim and Western sources. {cite:wikipedia-kecia-ali}

## Method and contribution
She practises an internal critical reading of the juristic tradition, setting out rulings in their historical context before questioning them from a feminist angle. Supporters see her as opening a necessary debate within Islamic studies, while conservative scholars object to her judgements on the texts; the museum attributes these positions without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-kecia-ali}
