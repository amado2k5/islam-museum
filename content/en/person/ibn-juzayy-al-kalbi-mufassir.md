## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Juzayy al-Kalbi was the author of [[work/tafsir-ibn-juzayy|Kitab al-Tashil li-Ulum al-Tanzil]]; the cover of the first printing of his commentary calls him "the shaykh, the imam, the learned hafiz and exegete, servant of the mighty Qur'an". {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.4} He is not to be confused with his namesake [[person/ibn-juzayy-muhammad|Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim ibn Juzayy]], the writer who set down Ibn Battuta's travels.

## Teachers
In his commentary he names "our shaykh, the master" [[person/abu-jafar-ibn-al-zubayr|Abu Ja'far ibn al-Zubayr]], author of Milak al-Ta'wil, and quotes his explanations of differing wording between suras in the stories. {cite:tashil-maktaba-tijariyya-1355-j2 p.55}
