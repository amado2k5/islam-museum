## Overview
Al-Sharqi ibn al-Qutami, one of the akhbaris whom al-Mas'udi mentions. The data hold nothing else about him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.684}

## The toll of Siffin
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Sharqi ibn al-Qutami, al-Haytham ibn Adi and Abu Mikhnaf estimated the dead at Siffin from both sides at seventy thousand, among them twenty-five of Badr. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.684}
