## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hibat Allah ibn Asakir, a Shafi'i jurist of Damascus of the house of Ibn Asakir, author of "the History of Damascus". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 438}

## Description and death
Ibn al-Athir describes him as abundant in knowledge, learned in the school, much given to righteousness, asceticism and good. He died in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in Ramadan 620 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 438}
