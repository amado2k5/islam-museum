## Overview
As'ad ibn Ya'fur, lord of the fortress of Kahlan, where he lived, and governor of the districts of Yemen in 332 AH, who was held in honour there. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.540}

## Ghumdan
He wanted to build Ghumdan, and Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Hasani advised him not to interfere with it, because its building would be done by the hand of a boy who would come out of the land of Saba and Ma'rib. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.540}
