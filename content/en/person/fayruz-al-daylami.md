## Overview
Fayruz al-Daylami of the Abna, the Persian families whom Khosrow sent to Yemen with the son of Dhu Yazan, under the command of Wahriz. He embraced Islam, and Qays ibn Hubayra al-Muradi won him over to kill al-Aswad al-Ansi. He was among those who entered upon al-Aswad by stealth at dawn in his house at Sana'a. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Who killed al-Aswad
Opinions differ on the killer. Some said that Qays slaughtered him and cut off his head. Others said that Fayruz killed him and that Qays gave him the credit. The source reports that when the two claimed it in Medina, Umar said: "This lion killed him", meaning Fayruz. Some scholars mention that the Prophet said, five days before his death: "God has killed al-Aswad al-Ansi; a righteous man, Fayruz ibn al-Daylami, killed him."
