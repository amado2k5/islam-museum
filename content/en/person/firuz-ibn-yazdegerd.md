## Overview
Firuz ibn Yazdegerd, a king of the Sasanian Persians. Al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The reign of Firuz ibn Yazdegerd". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.82}

An additional source is needed: the details of the years of his reign and his affairs have not yet been read.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Peroz fled from his brother Hormizd to the Hephthalites, who gave him thirty thousand men, and that, as “they say”, during seven years of drought he lifted the land tax and threatened his governors if anyone died of hunger, until the rain came. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 58} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 59} He says Peroz built Rayy (Ram-Firuz) and Ardabil (Badh-Firuz). {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 59} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 60}
