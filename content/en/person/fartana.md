## Summary
One of [[person/abdullah-ibn-khatal|Abdullah ibn Khatal]]'s two singing-girls at Mecca; they used to sing lampoons of the Prophet and stir Quraysh against him. Her blood was declared lawful on the conquest day — one was killed, while the other fled, had safety asked for her, and became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52-53}.

## Their affair
The Prophet ordered the two singing-girls killed with Ibn Khatal for sharing in the warlike incitement through lampooning him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52}. One was killed on the conquest day; her companion fled until safety was sought for her from the Prophet after the conquest settled — he granted it and she became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/53}.

## For young visitors
A singing-girl who used to sing verses of mockery and enmity; she was punished with her master on the conquest day, while her companion fled and her people begged safety for her — and she became Muslim.
