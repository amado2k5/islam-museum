## Overview
Uthman ibn Mas'ud, whom al-Mufaddal ibn al-Muhallab sent to Termez with fifteen thousand men. He landed on an island called today the island of Uthman, and pressed upon Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim, and wrote to Tarkhun, who came to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

When Musa went out, Uthman said: "A leap by Musa, by the Lord of the Ka'ba", and he pursued him until he was killed, in the year 85 AH {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
