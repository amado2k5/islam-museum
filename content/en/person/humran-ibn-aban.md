## Overview
Humran ibn Aban al-Farisi, the jurist and client of the Commander of the Faithful 'Uthman; he was among the captives of 'Ayn al-Tamr and 'Uthman bought him from al-Musayyib ibn Najaba. He narrated from 'Uthman and Mu'awiya — his hadiths are few — and 'Ata' ibn Yazid al-Laythi, 'Urwa, Zayd ibn Aslam, Bayan ibn Bishr, Bukayr ibn al-Ashajj, Mu'adh ibn 'Abd al-Rahman and others narrated from him. He died in 89 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1566}
