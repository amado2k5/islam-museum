## Overview
Humran ibn Aban ibn Khalid al-Tamri, a client of Uthman, according to al-Baladhuri. Some people say that the name of his father was "Aba". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.345}

## His story
He belonged to al-Musayyab ibn Najaba al-Fazari, and Uthman bought him from him and freed him. Then he sent him to Kufa to ask about his governor, but the governor denied him, and drove him out of his protection. He settled in Basra, and he was among the captives of [[event/siege-of-ayn-al-tamr|Ayn al-Tamr]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.345}
