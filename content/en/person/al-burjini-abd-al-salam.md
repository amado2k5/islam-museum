## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd al-Salam ibn Isa al-Qurashi al-Burjini al-Tunisi, a Maliki jurist, whom the source counts among the famous Maliki jurists of his age. He kept company with a pupil of al-Mazari in al-Mahdiyya and narrated from him. He died in 630 AH (1233 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
