## Summary
Ibn Abi Zayd al-Qayrawani: Maliki jurist, linked in the museum's record to Qayrawan. The Biblissima record places his life between 922 and 996. {cite:biblissima Q106011} The museum's record card names the Risala as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «ʿAbd Allāh ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān Ibn Abī Zayd al-Qayrawānī» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q106011}
