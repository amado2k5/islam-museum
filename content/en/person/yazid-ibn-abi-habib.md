## Overview
An Egyptian transmitter mentioned in al-Baladhuri's Futuh al-Buldan. He is a narrator in the chain of the report of the treaty of Nubia. He transmits from al-Walid ibn Kathir and from Abu al-Khayr, and Ibn Lahi'a transmits from him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.331-332}.
