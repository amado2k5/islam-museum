## Overview
Zaynab bint Maz'un ibn Habib ibn Wahb ibn Hudhafa ibn Jumah belonged to the Banu Jumah of Quraysh. Ibn Abd al-Barr gives her a numbered entry among the women Companions in al-Isti'ab and describes her as the sister of Uthman ibn Maz'un and the wife of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]]. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr p. 909} She is the mother of Umar's children Abd Allah, Hafsa and Abd al-Rahman the Elder. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr p. 909} The museum has no longer biography of her; the sources read give her lineage, her family ties and one open question about her migration.

## Family
Her brother was [[person/uthman-ibn-mazun|Uthman ibn Maz'un]]. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr p. 909} The modern book Nisa' hawl al-Rasul, in its life of [[person/hafsa|Hafsa]], introduces Hafsa as the daughter of Umar ibn al-Khattab and says that her mother was Zaynab bint Maz'un, sister of Uthman ibn Maz'un. {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul p. 75} Ibn al-Athir's Usd al-ghaba, in its entry on Hafsa, likewise names her mother as Zaynab bint Maz'un, sister of Uthman ibn Maz'un, and says that Hafsa's full brother was [[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Abd Allah ibn Umar]]. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p. 1496}

## Was she an emigrant?
Ibn Abd al-Barr reports that al-Zubayr numbered Zaynab among the emigrants. He says he fears this is a mistake, because it had also been said that she died a Muslim in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] before the hijra, whereas her daughter Hafsa was among the emigrants. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr p. 909} The museum does not settle the point. It records the two reports as the author gives them, one placing her among the emigrants, the other holding that she died in Mecca before the migration, and no date of death is given.

## What is not known here
The sources read give no date of her birth or death, and no further account of her life. The record therefore rests on two entries in works about the Companions and on a modern biography of her daughter. A fuller page would need the parallel entries in the other biographical dictionaries of the Companions.
