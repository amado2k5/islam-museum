## Overview
Ibrahim b. Khalid b. al-Yaman, Abu Thawr al-Kalbi of Baghdad — a leading companion of al-Shafi'i. He began in the ra'y method of the Iraqis, then attached himself to al-Shafi'i on his arrival in Baghdad and turned to hadith. Ahmad b. Hanbal said "I have known him for the Sunna for fifty years; in my view he stands in the skin of Sufyan al-Thawri," and al-Nasa'i called him "one of the jurists, trustworthy". He died in Safar 240 AH {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.1 p.228}.
