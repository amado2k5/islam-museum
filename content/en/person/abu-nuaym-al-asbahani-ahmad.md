## Overview
Abu Nu'aym al-Isbahani, Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Ishaq ibn Musa ibn Mihran, a hadith scholar, memorizer and historian from Isfahan. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source describes him as a great collector of narrations who alone attained a high chain of transmission in the last part of his life. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born in 336 AH and died in 430 AH; the Gregorian dates are derived as 948 CE and 1038 CE. Both dates need a second source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Among his works is [[work/marifat-al-sahaba-abi-nuaym|Knowledge of the Companions]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
