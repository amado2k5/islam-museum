## Overview
'Asim ibn Abi'l-Najud, the great imam and Reader of the age Abu Bakr al-Asadi, their client of Kufa; his father's name was Bahdala. He was born in Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan's governorship. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2097}

## Teaching
He read the Qur'an under Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami and Zirr ibn Hubaysh al-Asadi, and narrated from them and from Abu Wa'il, Mus'ab ibn Sa'd and other senior Successors — he counts among the junior Successors. He presided over Qur'an teaching in Kufa for a time: Abu Bakr ibn 'Ayyash, Hafs ibn Sulayman, al-A'mash and many more read under him, and Shu'ba, al-Thawri, Hammad ibn Salama, Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna and others narrated from him. He died around 165 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2097}
