## Summary

Sinan ibn Abi Sinan ibn Mihsan al-Asadi, nephew of Ukasha. He witnessed Badr and was the first to write to the Prophet with the news of Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid al-Asadi; he was over Banu Malik. He died in 32 AH.
