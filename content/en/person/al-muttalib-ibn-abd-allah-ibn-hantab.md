## Summary
al-Muttalib ibn Abd Allah ibn Hantab ibn al-Harith, of Quraysh and Banu Makhzum, from Medina. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8207}
He transmitted from Umar, Abu Musa al-Ashari, Zayd ibn Thabit, Abu Hurayra, Aisha, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Umar and Anas, among others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8207}
His sons Abd al-Aziz and al-Hakam, al-Awzai, Zuhayr ibn Muhammad al-Tamimi, Ibn Jurayj and Kathir ibn Zayd transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8207}
Abu Zurʿa and al-Daraqutni called him trustworthy, and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8207}
Ibn Sad said he narrated much but was not used as proof because he often narrated mursal reports; al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar said he was among the notables of Quraysh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8207}
