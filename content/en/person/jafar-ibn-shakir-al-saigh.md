## Summary
Abu Muhammad al-Sa'igh, Ja'far ibn Shakir; he heard 'Affan, Qubaysa, Abu Nu'aym and others; Ibn Sa'id, Ibn Makhlad, Abu'l-Husayn ibn al-Munadi, al-Najad and Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i narrated from him; a worshipping ascetic, reliable, truthful, meticulous and precise — multitudes benefited from him and most of the narrations about him praise his reliability and goodness; he reached ninety years save a few months; he died in Dhu'l-Hijja and was buried in the Bab al-Tawba cemeteries at Baghdad. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 180}
