## Summary
Shabir Randeree is a British Muslim financier and philanthropist, chairman of the DCD investment group and a founding director of Al Rayan Bank — the first fully licensed Islamic bank in Britain. He is widely described as a pioneer of Islamic finance in the UK. {cite:wikipedia-shabir-randeree}

## Formation and business
Born into a South African family of Indian Muslim origin, Randeree settled in Britain and built a career in property and investment through DCD Group — a family investment house with interests in real estate and finance — while becoming one of the earliest and most persistent advocates for a licensed Islamic bank in Britain. {cite:wikipedia-shabir-randeree}

## Islamic Bank of Britain / Al Rayan
He co-founded the Islamic Bank of Britain in 2004 — the first stand-alone, sharia-compliant retail bank authorised in the UK — and served on its board through its growth and 2014 acquisition by Qatar's Masraf Al Rayan, after which it traded as Al Rayan Bank. The venture proved a Western regulator could license and supervise Islamic retail banking, and it became the model followed by later entrants. {cite:muslim500-shabir-randeree}

## Public service
Randeree has served on interfaith and educational bodies — including leadership of the Woolf Institute-linked and mosque-linked initiatives — and was appointed CBE for services to Islamic finance and interfaith relations. {cite:wikipedia-shabir-randeree}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as the doyen of British Islamic finance: the man who proved Islamic retail banking could run inside the UK regulatory system — and who spent the proceeds on the community's institutions. {cite:muslim500-shabir-randeree}
