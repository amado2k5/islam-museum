## Overview
Ogedei ibn [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]] and brother of [[person/jochi-ibn-genghis|Jochi]]. According to Ibn Khaldun in "al-Ibar", his father made him his heir and he sat on the throne after him, and he appointed the great khans {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

He moved to the old homeland of Genghis Khan between the Khitai and the Uighurs, which is Turkistan and Kashgar. There is the city of Karakorum, where his seat was. He sent troops to the lands of Rum with a commander of the Mongols, who took it from the hands of the Banu Qilij Arslan. After him, Mongkefan ibn Tului succeeded {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
