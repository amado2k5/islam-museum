## Summary
Suleiman Bulbulia is a Barbadian community leader and one of the Caribbean's most visible Muslims: secretary of the Barbados Muslim Association since 2000, Muslim chaplain at the University of the West Indies, a Justice of the Peace since 2010, and recipient of the Barbados Silver Trident of Excellence for interfaith inclusivity. He entered The Muslim 500 in its 2026 edition. {cite:muslim500-suleiman-bulbulia} {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Life and upbringing
A first-generation Barbadian, his father arrived from India in 1953 as a young orphan with a metal suitcase, working as an itinerant tradesman; his mother was the daughter of an East Indian migrant and a woman of African-Irish heritage from Shorey Village, St Andrew. He graduated with honours in Management Studies at UWI Cave Hill, studied Arabic at King Saud University in Riyadh, and co-founded the campus Muslim Students Association some forty years ago. {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Public service
He has served as secretary of the Barbados Muslim Association since 2000, is Muslim chaplain at the University of the West Indies, a Justice of the Peace since 2010, and a former member of the Religious Advisory Committee on National Affairs, appointed by the Prime Minister to advise on national matters from a religious perspective; his profile credits him with guiding major legislative updates in Barbados. {cite:muslim500-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Family roots
His biography tells two generations of Indian migration to the Caribbean: a father who arrived in 1953 as an orphan with a metal suitcase, working on foot as an itinerant tradesman, and a mother born of an East Indian migrant and a woman of African-Irish heritage; he says this memory taught him the sacrifices that built modern Barbados. {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Education and work
He graduated with honours in Management Studies from the University of the West Indies and studied Arabic at King Saud University in Riyadh, then spent more than thirty years in business management running a fabric and home-furnishing company and co-founding the Barbados Halal Experience, alongside work in international relations representing his country abroad. {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Civic and media work
He writes weekly analysis for Barbados Today, chairs the Barbados Childhood Obesity Prevention Coalition, lectures on Islam, Muslims and cultures, manages a fabric and home-furnishing company, and co-founded the Barbados Halal Experience. {cite:muslim500-suleiman-bulbulia} {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}

## Legacy
His path reads as full citizenship for a small minority: a Barbadian Muslim of Indian and African-Irish roots serving in state, university and media, carrying his community's voice into national legislation. He told Barbados Today the honour strengthened his resolve to serve rather than changed him. {cite:barbadostoday-suleiman-bulbulia}
