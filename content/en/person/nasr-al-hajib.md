## Overview
Nasr al-Hajib served as chamberlain to the caliph al-Muqtadir bi-Allah {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.94-96}.

## al-Hallaj
He inclined toward al-Hallaj and defended him. The vizier Hamid ibn al-Abbas pressed al-Muqtadir to hand him over to him, and he took him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.94-96}.

## Mosul
al-Muqtadir afterwards appointed him to lead the war against Mosul and to support it, in the year 309 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.94-96}.
