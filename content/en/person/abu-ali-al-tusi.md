## Overview
Abu 'Ali al-Tusi, shaykh of the ta'ifa, one who joined eminence with increase; born 479, he died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 540 — Ibn al-'Imad says he is the son of Shaykh Abu Ja'far Muhammad b. al-Hasan, author of the Tahdhib and Istibsar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 605}
