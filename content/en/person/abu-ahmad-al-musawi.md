## Overview
Abu Ahmad al-Husayn ibn Musa al-Musawi, the marshal of the Talibiyyun in Baghdad and father of [[person/al-sharif-al-radi|al-Sharif al-Radi]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 19-20}

Ibn al-Athir relates that the caliph [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir billah]] learned of verses composed by his son concerning the lineage of the Egyptian Mahdi, so he summoned the judge Ibn al-Baqillani to compel him to recant. The father denied knowing the poetry, and the son refused to write what was demanded of him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 19-20}

The son is said to have said that he feared the near more than the far; his father rebuked him in reply. This is from Ibn al-Athir's account. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 19-20}
