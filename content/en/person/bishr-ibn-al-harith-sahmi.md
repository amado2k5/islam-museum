## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia. Ibn Ishaq mentions after him a brother of his by his mother, of Banu Tamim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Sahm, then says he has a brother by his mother of Banu Tamim called Sa'id ibn Amr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
