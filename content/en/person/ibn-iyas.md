## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Iyas al-Hanafi, Abu al-Barakat, was an Egyptian historian of the mamluk class and a pupil of Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti; he made the hajj in 882 AH and died about 930 AH (1524 CE). His father Ahmad had been attached to the amirs and men of state, and his grandfather Amir Iyas al-Fakhri al-Zahiri was a mamluk of al-Zahir Barquq. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.4}

He wrote Tarikh Ibn Iyas, called Bada'i' al-Zuhur fi Waqa'i' al-Duhur, in three parts with a fourth and fifth added, its annals reaching 928 AH; Nashq al-Azhar fi 'Aja'ib al-Aqtar; 'Uqud al-Juman fi Waqa'i' al-Azman; Marj al-Zuhur on history; and Nuzhat al-Umam fi al-'Aja'ib wa-l-Hikam. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.4}
