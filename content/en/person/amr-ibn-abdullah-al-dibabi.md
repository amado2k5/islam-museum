## Overview
Amr ibn Abd Allah al-Dibabi, from the delegation of the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]] who came with Khalid ibn al-Walid to the Prophet in 10 AH. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.240}

He embraced Islam with his people at the hand of Khalid and came in their delegation; this is in the account of [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|Khalid's mission to the Banu al-Harith]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.240}

The editors vowel his nisba as "al-Dibabi" with a kasra on the dad, citing al-Suhayli. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
