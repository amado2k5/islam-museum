## Summary
Al-Akhnas ibn Shariq al-Thaqafi, a Thaqafi leader, ally of Banu Zuhra and their commander in the [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] expedition {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 208}.

## Turning Banu Zuhra back
When Abu Sufyan's caravan escaped and his letter reached Quraysh telling them to return, Abu Jahl insisted on pressing on to Badr; but al-Akhnas saw his people Banu Zuhra — his allies — had no need to fight once the caravan was safe, so he turned them all back, some three hundred men {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 207-208}.

No Zuhri witnessed Badr, and Banu Zuhra remained grateful for his judgment; the book says "he remained obeyed and honoured among them" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 208}.
