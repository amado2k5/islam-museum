## Overview
Umar ibn Faraj al-Rukhji, of the secretaries of the Abbasid state, mentioned by al-Baladhuri and al-Mas'udi {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.409} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.488-489}.

## In al-Baladhuri
The mouth of the Maymun river was diverted in the days of al-Wathiq at his hands {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.409}.

## In al-Mas'udi
He was among the eminent secretaries. [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] became angry with him in the year 233 AH, and took from him money and jewels. He was reconciled for twenty-one million dirhams, and then al-Mutawakkil was pleased with him again. al-Mas'udi gives other details of the punishment {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.488-489}.
