## Overview
Kumayl ibn Ziyad, one of the close companions of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and his trusted followers, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 711}

## Ali's counsel to him
Dirar ibn Damra relates that he heard Ali counsel him one day, saying: "O Kumayl, defend the believer, for his back is God's sanctuary, his soul is dear to God, and the one who wrongs him is God's adversary." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 711}
