## Overview
'Abd Allah b. Ahmad b. Ghalib b. Zaydun (354–405), kunya Abu Bakr, father of the poet Ibn Zaydun; Ibn Bashkuwal named and praised him in al-Sila; he dyed his hair black; he died at al-Bira and was buried at Cordoba {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
