## Summary
The Zayyanid ruler of Tlemcen, raised to power with Ottoman support {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.

## The turn to Spain
Al-Salabi says foreign intrigues drew him close to Spain; the people, hating him, resolved to depose him and acclaim his brother [[person/al-hasan-zayyani-tlemcen|al-Hasan]]. He went to Oran seeking Spanish help with pledges of loyalty; the governor sent an army joined by the Spanish-subject tribes — Bani 'Amir, Fulitta and Bani Rashid — under [[person/al-mansur-ibn-bu-ghannam|al-Mansur b. Bu Ghannam]], but [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]] barred his restoration {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.
