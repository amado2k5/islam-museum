## Summary
Mona Elghdban is an Egyptian writer and researcher in consciousness and spiritual development, and a lecturer in "energy science", self-development and positive thinking. {cite:abjjad-mona-elghdban} {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

She is known for her online work: her YouTube channel, her personal website, and her lectures and courses addressed mainly to women seeking self-development and psychological and spiritual balance. {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban} {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

## Career
She created her YouTube channel on 23 June 2017, and publishes on it her lectures and sessions on energy science, consciousness and self-development. {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban}

As of October 2026 her channel had about 95,400 subscribers, 228 videos and about 5.2 million views in total — platform figures that change constantly. {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban}

Among her lectures on the channel is "Taqat al-Istihqaq Sirr al-Tahakkum bi-Aqdarak" (The Energy of Deserving: The Secret of Controlling Your Destinies); her personal website links to these lectures, and the channel's About panel in turn gives her personal website, so each points to the other. {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban} {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

Her personal website carries her articles and courses, and its topic index is the fullest witness to her field in her own terms: "self-development" is the largest category (over 130 posts), followed by the law of attraction, numerology, the chakras, Transurfing and karma. {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

## Works
Her books include "Asrar al-Wa'y: li-l-Wusul ila Afdal Nuskha min Dhatik wa-Tahsin Jawdat Hayatik" (Secrets of Consciousness: To Reach the Best Version of Yourself and Improve Your Quality of Life), published in 2023, in which she focused on developing consciousness and changing patterns of thought and one's relation to the self; her personal website publishes the book's full table of contents. {cite:abjjad-mona-elghdban} {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

Her books also include "Tanasukh al-Arwah" (Reincarnation), in which she discussed the ideas of multiple lives and the spiritual dimension of human existence. {cite:abjjad-mona-elghdban}

Through her channel and website she gives lectures and sessions on subjects such as the energy of deserving, the law of attraction, release from negative beliefs, the energy of acts of worship and the energy of the months, and she runs a "Self-Love Club" as part of her training activity. {cite:website-mona-elghdban} {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban}

## Positions and criticism
This section should be read as a presentation of what the sources report, not as a verdict of the museum on her work or her views.

Mona Elghdban's work belongs to the contemporary "energy" and positive-thinking currents: she teaches what she calls energy science, the law of attraction, the chakras, karma and Transurfing, together with meditations and "energy sessions", as the topic index of her website and the titles of her lectures show. {cite:website-mona-elghdban} {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban}

One of her books is devoted to reincarnation — the idea that the soul lives multiple lives and returns in other bodies — which she treats as an inquiry into multiple lives and the spiritual dimension of existence. {cite:abjjad-mona-elghdban} Her personal website keeps a separate category for her material on "reincarnation" and "transmigration". {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

These subjects are not native topics of the Islamic tradition, and many of them are drawn from the global New Age movements; the museum records them here as a description of her work as she and her sources present it, without adopting a position of its own. {cite:website-mona-elghdban}

In the sources available, the museum found no source naming a legal school or a theological affiliation for her; her declared frame is energy and positive thinking, and so no school is recorded for her in the museum's data. {cite:youtube-mona-elghdban} {cite:website-mona-elghdban} {cite:abjjad-mona-elghdban}
