## Summary

Zaynab bint Nabit ibn Jabir al-Ansariyya, wife of Anas ibn Malik. Ibn Sa'd names her among the women who gave the pledge; she narrated from her mother, a daughter of As'ad ibn Zurara, from her husband, from Jabir ibn Abd Allah and from Duba'a bint al-Zubayr ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
