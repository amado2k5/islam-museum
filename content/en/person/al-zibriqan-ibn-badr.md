## Summary
Al-Zibriqan ibn Badr al-Tamimi, a chief and poet of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]]; he came in the delegation of his people's ten chiefs to Medina after [[event/sariyya-of-uyayna-to-banu-tamim|Uyayna ibn Hisn's detachment]] in year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 425-426}.

## The boasting match and Islam
Banu Tamim's delegation showed their wish for a contest of boasting and put forward their poet al-Zibriqan ibn Badr, who recited; Islam's poet [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]] answered him on the spot — whereupon al-Aqra' ibn Habis said: his poet is a better poet than ours — and al-Zibriqan became Muslim with his people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## His charge of the alms
The Prophet appointed him over the alms of a division of Banu Sa'd in the alms-collectors' dispatch of year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

## For young visitors
Al-Zibriqan was a poet who boasted for his people; when he heard the Muslims' poet answer him he confessed his excellence and became Muslim, and the Prophet rewarded him by making him keeper of his people's alms.
