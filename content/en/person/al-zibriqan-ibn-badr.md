## Summary
Al-Zibriqan ibn Badr al-Tamimi was a chief and poet of [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]]. He came to Medina among the delegation of his people's ten chiefs, after the [[event/sariyya-of-uyayna-to-banu-tamim|detachment of Uyayna ibn Hisn]] in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.425-426}.

## The boasting match and Islam
The delegation of Banu Tamim showed their wish for a contest of boasting, and they put forward their poet al-Zibriqan ibn Badr, who recited. [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]], the poet of Islam, answered him on the spot. Al-Aqra' ibn Habis then said: "His poet is a better poet than ours," and al-Zibriqan embraced Islam together with his people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.426}.

## His charge over the alms
The Prophet appointed him over the alms of part of Banu Sa'd, in the dispatch of alms collectors in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.425}.

## For young visitors
Al-Zibriqan was a poet who boasted for his people. When he heard the Muslims' poet answer him, he acknowledged his worth and became a Muslim. The Prophet rewarded him by making him trustee of his people's alms.

## In Ibn Hisham's sira
Ibn Ishaq counts him among the nobles of the [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|delegation of Banu Tamim]] as "one of Banu Sa'd," while Ibn Hisham traces him to Banu Bahdala ibn Awf ibn Ka'b ibn Sa'd ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207}. After the speech of Utarid and Thabit's reply, al-Zibriqan stood and recited [[work/zibriqan-boast-poem|his poem]]. Its lines boast that "we are the noble, no one rivals us; from us come the kings, and among us the bi' are raised" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/208}. Ibn Hisham says that some of Banu Tamim transmitted some of its wording differently, and that most scholars of poetry deny some of what is attributed to al-Zibriqan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/209}. When he finished, Hassan stood at the Prophet's command and answered him with [[work/hassan-reply-to-zibriqan|his own poem]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/209}. Ibn Hisham also transmits from some scholars of poetry among Banu Tamim [[work/zibriqan-second-boast|other verses]] that al-Zibriqan recited on his arrival, boasting in them of Darim and the quarter-share, and that Hassan answered him in them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/211}.
