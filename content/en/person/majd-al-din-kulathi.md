## Summary
Majd al-Din Tahir ibn Nasrallah ibn Juhayl al-Kulathi of Hilla — Shafi'i faraidi, teacher at Salah al-Din's madrasa in Jerusalem; he heard and transmitted hadith from many, and composed for Sultan Nur al-Din the martyr a book on the excellence of jihad; father of the Banu Juhayl, the Damascene jurists — and one of those who rose against al-Suhrawardi the philosopher and gave the fatwa for his killing; he died at Jerusalem at sixty-four in 596. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 804}
