## Overview
Umara ibn Abi al-Hasan al-Yamani, a poet who was in Egypt. [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] had him crucified on the second of Ramadan in the year 569 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-54}.

## Mention of Yemen
Ibn al-Athir reports that he used to make a good impression on Turanshah, describing the lands of Yemen to him, and that this increased his desire for it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-54}.

## The plot
He became one of the pillars of a plot to restore the Alid state, in which it was resolved to summon the Franks. Zayn al-Din Ali ibn Naja uncovered it to Salah al-Din, who arrested the leaders and crucified them, and Umara was among them. See [[event/fatimid-restoration-plot-569|the plot to restore the Fatimid state]] {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-54}.
