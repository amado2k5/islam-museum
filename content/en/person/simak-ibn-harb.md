## Overview
Simak ibn Harb ibn Aws ibn Khalid al-Dhuhli al-Bakri, Abu al-Mughira of Kufa, was among the memorisers of Kufa in the first Islamic century. He narrated from leading Companions and Followers and died in 123 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1936}

## Teachers and students
He transmitted from Ibn al-Zubayr, al-Nu'man ibn Bashir, Jabir ibn Samura, Anas ibn Malik, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, al-Hasan al-Basri and many others, and was a prolific narrator from 'Ikrima. Malik ibn Mighwal, Shu'ba, Sufyan al-Thawri, Za'ida, Sharik and other imams narrated from him, and al-Dhahabi counted him among the bearers of the proof in his home town. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1936-1937}
