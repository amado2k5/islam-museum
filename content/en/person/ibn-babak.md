## Summary
'Abd al-Samad ibn Babak, Abu'l-Qasim the poet; one of the foremost poets of his age, whose verse is esteemed and circulated in the books of adab and histories; he sought out al-Sahib Ibn 'Abbad at Rayy and praised him, and al-Sahib asked him: "Are you Ibn Babak?" — "I am Ibn Babak"; he died in Shawwal 410. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}
