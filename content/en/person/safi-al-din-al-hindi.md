## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahim ibn Muhammad al-Armawi, Abu Abd Allah, known as Safi al-Din al-Hindi, was a jurist and a scholar of legal theory. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 644 AH and his death to 715 AH, that is 1246 and 1315 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 200}.

## Life
He was born in India and left Delhi in 667 AH; he visited Yemen, made the pilgrimage and entered Egypt and Anatolia. He settled in Damascus in 685 AH and died there, and he endowed his books to the Ashrafiyya Dar al-Hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 200}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his works Nihayat al-Wusul ila Ilm al-Usul, in manuscript, of which three volumes are noted; al-Fa'iq on the principles of religion, in manuscript; al-Zubda on theology; and al-Risala al-Tis'iniyya fi al-Usul al-Diniyya, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 200}.
