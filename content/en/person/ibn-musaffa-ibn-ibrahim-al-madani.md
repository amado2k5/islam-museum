## Overview
Ibn Musaffa ibn Ibrahim was a man of Madan and cousin of Ali ibn Rabi'; the narrator says he opened the house to Banu al-Harith on the night they attacked [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|Ahmad ibn Muhammad]] in Ramadan 286 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.145} The index lists "Ibn Musaffa ibn Ibrahim" at p. 145 only and the name is printed as given. This is the narrator's report and the museum does not judge his intent. See [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]].
