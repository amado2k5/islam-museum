## Overview
The great hafiz Qawam al-Sunna Abu'l-Qasim Isma'il ibn Muhammad al-Taymi al-Talhi al-Isbahani the Shafi'i; Abu Musa al-Madini called him "imam of the imams of his time, teacher of the scholars of his age and exemplar of the Sunnis of his era"; he was paralysed in Safar 534 and remained afflicted; an imam in tafsir, hadith, language and letters, knowing the texts and isnads, he dictated close to three thousand sessions at the Isfahan mosque. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 585-586}

## Works and death
He composed the thirty large volumes of Tafsir al-Jami', the Idah in four, the Mawdih in three, the Mu'tamad in ten, a Persian tafsir, the Targhib wa'l-tarhib, sharhs of the two Sahihs which his son completed, Dala'il al-nubuwwa and the Tadhkira in some thirty parts; the imams of Baghdad said "no better or more retentive scholar travelled to Baghdad after Ahmad ibn Hanbal"; born in 457, he died on the morning of the Festival of Sacrifice, 535. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 585-586}

## Lineage and learning in the *Mawsu'at a'lam al-'ulama' wa-l-udaba'*
The *Mawsu'at a'lam al-'ulama' wa-l-udaba'* names him Abu al-Qasim Isma'il ibn Muhammad al-Talhi al-Taymi al-Qurashi al-Asbahani, traces his descent to Talha ibn 'Ubayd Allah, and counts him a leading authority in tafsir, hadith, jurisprudence, the Qur'anic readings, language and literature. It dates his birth to 457 AH and his death to 535 AH, in agreement with Ibn al-'Imad. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Among the works listed is *al-Targhib wa-l-Tarhib*, which has its own record in the museum: [[work/al-targhib-wa-al-tarhib-asbahani|al-Targhib wa-l-Tarhib]]. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 586}
