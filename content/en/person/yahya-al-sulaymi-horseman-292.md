## Overview
"Yahya al-Sulaymi" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen. The book does not say to which people "al-Sulaymi" refers. He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
