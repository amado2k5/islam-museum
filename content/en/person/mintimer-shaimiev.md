# Mintimer Shaimiev

## Summary

Mintimer Sharipovich Shaimiev (born 20 January 1937) is a Tatar Russian politician who was President of Tatarstan from 1991 to 2010, re-elected in 1996, 2001 and 2005. {cite:wikipedia-mintimer-shaimiev-en} {cite:wikidata-mintimer-shaimiev}

## Early life and Soviet career

He was born in the village of Anyakovo, Aktanyshsky District, Tatar ASSR, the son of Shagisharip Shaimukhammetovich. He graduated from the Kazan Agricultural Institute in 1959 and worked as an agricultural engineer, then rose through the republic's posts: minister of land reclamation and water management (1969), first deputy chairman of the Council of Ministers (1983), its chairman (1985) according to the prize's page, first secretary of the Communist Party in Tatarstan (1989) and speaker of its Supreme Soviet (April 1990), which proclaimed Tatarstan's sovereignty on 31 August 1990. {cite:wikipedia-mintimer-shaimiev-en} {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates h-e-mintimer-sharipovich-shaimev}

## President of Tatarstan

Elected president on 12 June 1991, he held a referendum on sovereignty in March 1992 in which 62 per cent of voters were in favour. In January 2010 he said he would not seek another term, to make way for a new generation, and Rustam Minnikhanov succeeded him. In 2001 he co-founded the United Russia party; after stepping down he became State Counsellor of Tatarstan and chairs the Yanarysh Foundation. {cite:wikipedia-mintimer-shaimiev-en}

## Islam in Tatarstan

The prize's page says it was given for his efforts to revive Islamic culture in Tatarstan, promote Islamic teachings and values among its people and rebuild mosques destroyed in earlier eras, and for policies that strengthened understanding and peaceful coexistence among the population. {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates h-e-mintimer-sharipovich-shaimev}

## Honours

He received the [[award/king-faisal-prize|King Faisal Prize]] for Service to Islam in 2007 and was named UNESCO Goodwill Ambassador for intercultural dialogue in 2017. His honours also include all four classes of the Order of Merit for the Fatherland and the Order of Lenin (1966). {cite:king-faisal-prize-laureates h-e-mintimer-sharipovich-shaimev} {cite:wikipedia-mintimer-shaimiev-en}
