## Summary
Ibn Bulayman, the adib Sharaf al-Din Sulayman ibn Bulayman ibn Abi al-Jaysh al-Irbili — the famous poet and one of the wits of the world; he died at Damascus aged ninety, in 686 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 690}.

## Details
Among the dead of 686 Ibn al-'Imad lists "Ibn Bulayman the adib, Sharaf al-Din Sulayman ibn Bulayman ibn Abi al-Jaysh al-Irbili, the famous poet, one of the wits of the world," who died at Damascus having completed ninety years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 690}. A native of Irbil in the Jazira, he moved to Syria and lived out his life at Damascus, carrying with him from Iraq the calling of poet and man of wit {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 690}. The phrase "one of the wits of the world" marks his standing among the literati of his age, zurafa' being those distinguished by elegant speech, good jests and graceful verse {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 690}. His ninety-year span made him a living bridge across the generations of the seventh hijri century.
