## Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, al-Sadr al-Shahid

Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Umar ibn Mazza, Abu Muhammad, Husam al-Din, the Hanafi known as al-Sadr al-Shahid, a jurist and usuli, one of the great Hanafis. He studied with his father Burhan al-Din al-Kabir Abd al-Aziz, debated with scholars and taught jurists; rulers took his opinion, and he died a martyr. His works include al-Fatawa al-Kubra, al-Fatawa al-Saghra, Umdat al-Mufti wa-al-Mustafti, a commentary on the Adab al-Qadi of al-Khassaf, a commentary on the Jami' al-Saghir, and al-Waqi'at al-Hisamiyya.
