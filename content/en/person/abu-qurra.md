## Overview
Theodore Abu Qurra, bishop of the Melkites in Harran. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.58}

## In Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim mentions a book by him attacking Anastasius the chief, and says that a group answered it. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.58}
