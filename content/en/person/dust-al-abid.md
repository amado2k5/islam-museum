## Summary
Al-Qasim ibn Nasr ibn Salim, Abu Muhammad, known as Dust al-Abid (d. 281 AH), was among the best Muslims and most distinguished worshippers. He related hadith from Surayj ibn al-Nu'man al-Jawhari, Amr ibn Awn al-Wasiti and Ubayd ibn Hashim. Abd al-Samad ibn Ali al-Tusi, Abu Sahl ibn Ziyad al-Qattan and Ja'far al-Khaldi transmitted from him. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.435-436}

He lived in Sayb al-Qadi on the eastern side. He died on a Wednesday, six nights before the end of Ramadan, in the year 281, and was buried in the Khayzuran cemetery. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.435-436}
