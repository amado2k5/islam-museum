## Summary
Yunus ibn Yazid ibn Abi al-Nijad al-Ayli al-Qurashi, Abu Yazid, a client of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan; the text calls him the trustworthy traditionist, and the collective of hadith compilers recorded for him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16254} He transmitted from Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri, Nafi' the client of Ibn Umar, al-Qasim, Ikrima and others; al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Yahya ibn Ayyub, al-Awza'i, Ibn al-Mubarak, Ibn Wahb and others transmitted from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16254} The encyclopedia dates his death to 159 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16254}
