## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Fattah ibn Yusuf ibn Umar al-Mullawi al-Mujiri, Abu al-Abbas Shihab al-Din, was a Shafi'i of al-Azhar whom al-Zirikli calls the shaykh of the shaykhs of his age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}. He was born and died in Cairo, and his family origin was Hama {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## Teachers and standing
Al-Zirikli reports from al-Jabarti that he made the pilgrimage, took from a number of teachers and returned to Egypt, and that he was the leading figure of his time in resolving difficult questions, relied on in both the rational and the transmitted sciences {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names two commentaries on the text of the Sullam, a large and a small one, held in Dar al-Kutub {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}. His printed works include «al-La'ali al-Manthurat», a commentary on a versification of the Muwajjahat in logic, and «Diwan al-Khutab al-Jum'iyya» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}. Works the entry marks as manuscripts include a commentary on the creed of al-Ghumari; a gloss on al-Qayrawani's commentary on al-Sanusi's Umm al-Barahin; a commentary on his own theological poem; a logic arjuza and «Nazm al-Mukhtalatat»; «al-Salama», a short work against covetousness; the theological poem «al-Usul»; «Manhal al-Tahqiq fi Mas'alat al-Gharaniq»; a gloss on al-Makudi's commentary on the Alfiyya; a commentary on al-Busiri's Hamziyya; «Ikhtisar Lata'if al-Tara'if» on figurative language; «Iqd al-Durar al-Bahiyya» on the Samarqandiyya treatise in rhetoric; «al-I'lam bi-Irth Dhawi al-Arham», a commentary on a poem on inheritance by Id ibn Makhrama; and a thabat of 28 leaves with which he gave a licence to Muhammad ibn Abd Rabbih al-Maliki {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}. The entry locates these in Dar al-Kutub, the Azhar library and other collections {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## Dates
Al-A'lam gives his birth as 1088 AH, equal to 1677 CE, and his death as 1181 AH, equal to 1767 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.
