## Summary
Harb ibn Umayya ibn Abd Shams, father of [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]]. He led [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] and all of [[group/kinana|Kinana]] in the [[event/harb-al-fijar|War of Fijar]], because of his standing among them in age and honour {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/184-187}.

## In the War of Fijar
Al-Raheeq al-Makhtum states that the War of Fijar took place in the fifteenth year of the Prophet's life, between Quraysh and those allied with them from Kinana on one side and Qays Aylan on the other. It states that the commander of Quraysh and all of Kinana was Harb ibn Umayya, because of his standing among them in age and honour {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.59} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/184-187} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/63}.

## From another source
Al-Mas'udi relates that the jinn killed him and recited two verses about him, and that his grave lies in a barren place. He mentions the reasoning by which people concluded that the two verses were spoken by the jinn {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.474}.

## What is not mentioned here
Al-Raheeq al-Makhtum does not mention his birth, his death, or the rest of his pre-Islamic history. These are found in the genealogical and historical books.

## For young visitors
Harb ibn Umayya was one of the great men of Quraysh, and they chose him as their commander in the War of Fijar. He was the father of Abu Sufyan.
