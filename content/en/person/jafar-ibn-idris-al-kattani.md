## Overview
Ja'far ibn Idris al-Hasani al-Kattani, Abu al-Mawahib, was the leading Maliki jurist of his time, a Sufi learned in biographical literature and a prolific author, born and died in Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 122}. Al-Zirikli heads the entry with the years 1246 and 1323 AH and prints beside them the Gregorian years 1830 and 1950; the second does not match 1323 AH, which falls in 1905, and the museum's card carries 1831 and 1905, both derived conversions {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 122}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Sharab al-Muhtadar fi Rijal al-Qarn al-Thalith Ashar, printed; A'lam al-A'imma al-A'lam wa-Asanidiha bi-ma Lana min al-Riwayat wa-Asanidiha, printed, at whose end he gave the names of all his works; al-Riyad al-Rabbaniyya fi al-Shu'ba al-Kattaniyya, in manuscript in the Rabat library, number 497 K; a book on the hadith beginning Inna Allah yubghid ahl al-bayt al-lahmin, printed; a treatise on the rulings concerning the dhimmi communities, printed; legal opinions; and more {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 122}.
