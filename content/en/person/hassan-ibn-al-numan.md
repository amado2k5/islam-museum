## Overview
Hassan ibn al-Numan, an Umayyad commander and administrator who took charge of the conquest of North Africa after Abu al-Muhajir. The book states that he brought the Byzantine presence to an end, destroyed Carthage, and built Tunis in its place. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 40}

It credits him with the victory over al-Kahina, leader of the Berbers, with renewing Kairouan and establishing its congregational mosque. He set up the registers and arranged the land tax and the poll tax. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 41}

The book gives no date for his governorship, and this needs an additional source. The same figure has another page: [[person/hassan-ibn-al-numan|Hassan ibn al-Numan al-Ghassani]].

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports that Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan appointed Hassan over the Maghrib in the year 73; he camped at Tripoli and then went to Qartajanna, where he found only a few Rum. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 269} {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 270} He then campaigned against [[person/al-kahina|al-Kahina]], queen of the Berbers, who defeated him on a river now called Nahr al-Bala' and captured eighty of his men; he withdrew to fortresses in the district of Barqa, which were named Qusur Hassan. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 270} He marched against her again and killed her, settled at the site of al-Qayrawan, built its congregational mosque, set up the registers and imposed the land tax on the non-Arabs of Ifriqiya, and went to Abd al-Malik with his spoils in Jumada al-Akhira 76. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 271} Al-Layth says he left Ifriqiya in the year 78. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 272}

## In al-Baladhuri and al-Sallabi
Hassan ibn al-Numan al-Ghassani, governor of Ifriqiya in the Umayyad period. He campaigned against al-Kahina, queen of the Berbers, and was defeated by her. He then settled in palaces in the district of Barqa, which were called the Palaces of Hassan. He later campaigned against her a second time and killed her, and took captives from the Berbers and sent them to Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan ([[event/hassan-vs-kahina|Hassan and al-Kahina]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Salabi reports that he founded the city of Tunis in 82 AH as a naval military base, by order of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.20}

The same book states that he was given the governorship of Ifriqiya after Abd al-Malik gave him a free hand over the revenues of Egypt, and that he began the campaigns in 74 AH. He followed the policy of Abu al-Muhajir in winning the Berbers over and enlisting them in the army. He set up the register of the soldiers, organised the register of tax, and built the naval dockyard at Tunis. He was removed in 85 AH after a dispute with Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, governor of Egypt. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.58-63}
