## Overview
Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Yazid al-Mahabi al-Karabisi. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as knowing hadith and jurisprudence, and counts him among the Jabriyya because he was closer to compulsion than others. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.256-257}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim attributes to him the book "al-Mudallisin" on hadith and the book "al-Imama", and says that in the latter there is an insinuation against Ali. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.256-257}

## His death
Al-Shirazi says that he died in 245 AH, and it is said 248 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## Notes for researchers
- The classification among the Jabriyya is Ibn al-Nadim's description, a judgement attributed to him and not to the museum.
- The date of death is disputed; the Gregorian conversion is derived.
