## Summary
Harb ibn Ismail al-Kirmani al-Hanzali, a companion of Imam Ahmad, a jurist and memoriser who transmitted much law from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2058}

## Works
According to al-Khallal he memorised four thousand questions from Ahmad and Ishaq before hearing them; he died in 280 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2059}
