## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Nasr, Abu Nasr al-Aqta' al-Hanafi, a jurist who studied fiqh with al-Quduri and excelled in it, and was expert in arithmetic. He left Baghdad in 430 H for Ahwaz, where he taught until his death and wrote a commentary on al-Quduri's Mukhtasar. Death 474 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13348}
