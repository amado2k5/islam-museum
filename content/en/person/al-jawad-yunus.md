## Overview
Al-Malik al-Jawad Muzaffar al-Din Yunus b. Mamdud b. al-'Adil — an amir of his uncle al-Malik al-Kamil, proclaimed sultan at Damascus after him; Ibn al-'Imad's dry verdict: "generous, but unfit for kingship" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 220}.
