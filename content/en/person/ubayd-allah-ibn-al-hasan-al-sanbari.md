## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn al-Sanbari, a jurist whom al-Shirazi mentions among the jurists of Basra {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}. He died in the year 168 AH according to al-Shirazi {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}.

The editor's notes point out that "Ibn al-Husayn" in his lineage varies among the manuscripts and the printed edition {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.
