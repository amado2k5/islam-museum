## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn Ammar ibn Ali al-Shurunbulali, a prolific Hanafi jurist of Shibra Bilula in Minufiyya, taken as a child to Cairo, where he studied at al-Azhar and became the reference for fatwas. He died in Cairo. His works include Nur al-idah in law and its commentary Maraqi al-falah, Ghunyat dhawi al-ahkam, and a gloss on Mulla Khusraw's Durar al-hukkam. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1629}
