## Summary
Al-Awwam ibn Hawshab ibn Yazid ibn al-Harith, Abu Isa al-Shaybani al-Rab'i al-Wasiti, a trustworthy man of the Sunna, firm and righteous. He narrated from Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Sakasiki, Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, Mujahid and others; his son Salama, Shu'ba, Yazid ibn Harun and Muhammad ibn Yazid narrated from him. Ahmad said he is trustworthy, twice; Ibn Ma'in, Abu Zur'a and al-Ijli also called him trustworthy {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13760}. The book gives his death as 147 AH.
