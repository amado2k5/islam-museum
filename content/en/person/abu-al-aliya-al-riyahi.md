## Summary
Abu al-Aliya al-Riyahi was a Basran Successor; [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] opens a chapter of [[work/kitab-al-zuhd-ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Kitab al-Zuhd]] with his name, "Reports of Abu al-Aliya", giving his sayings with chains through Ja'far the carpet-seller, Shu'ayb ibn al-Habhab, Hisham ibn Hassan, al-Rabi' and others. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371}

## His sayings in the book
- **On a time to come:** a time will come when people's breasts are emptied of the Qur'an and they find no sweetness or delight in it; if they fall short of what they were commanded they say God is Forgiving, Merciful, and if they do what was forbidden they say we will be forgiven; all their affair is hope without sincerity; "they wear the skins of sheep over the hearts of wolves", and the best of them in religion is the one who flatters. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371}
- **On hospitality:** Shu'ayb ibn al-Habhab relates that he came to them one day and they wanted to go to trouble for him, and he said: feed us from the food of the house and go to no trouble. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371} He said: when you visit people and they put a cushion for you, sit where it was put, for people know their own house best. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371}
- **On fasting:** one who fasts is in worship as long as he does not backbite, even if he is asleep on his bed. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371}
- **On obedience and sin:** do what obeys God and love those who do it; avoid sin and oppose those who commit it; God will punish the sinners if He wills and forgive them if He wills. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 371}
- **On forgetting the Qur'an:** it is reported from him that they counted it among the greatest sins for a man to learn the Qur'an and then neglect it until he forgets it. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 372}

## What follows in the chapter
His sayings are followed in the same chapter by reports about Abu Qilaba and Bakr ibn Abd Allah al-Muzani. {cite:zuhd-ahmad-dkim-1983 p. 372}

## For young visitors
Abu al-Aliya said that a person who fasts stays in worship as long as he does not speak ill of anyone, and he liked to eat whatever was in the house without fuss.
