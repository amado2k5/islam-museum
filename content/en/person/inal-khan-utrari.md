## Overview
Inalchuq Khan, cousin of the sultan Muhammad Khwarazm-shah, and lord of Otrar. Ibn Khaldun says that his affair was the cause of the Tatars' march on the Khwarazmian kingdom. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The merchants of Otrar
Some merchants from the lands of Genghis Khan came to him, and he found their goods. He reported to the sultan that they were spies on the lands and not merchants, and the sultan ordered him to take precautions against them. He seized their goods and killed them in secret. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The outcome
Genghis Khan demanded that he be handed over, and the sultan was alarmed and killed the envoys. According to Ibn Khaldun's account, that was the cause of the Tatars' march on his kingdom. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
