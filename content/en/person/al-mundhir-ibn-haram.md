## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn Haram al-Najjari al-Khazraji, the grandfather of [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]]. The Aws made him arbiter in the war of Sumayr, which Ibn al-Athir describes as the first war between the Ansar. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488}

## His judgement in the war of Sumayr
He judged that the blood-money of the free man should be paid to his ally Ka'b ibn al-Ajlan, and that they should return to their old custom. They parted with hatred intensified in their souls. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488}
