## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, of [[place/najran|Najran]], was left by [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] as governor of [[place/barat-yemen|Barat]] when he left it on the Thursday, its people having listened and obeyed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.85} The text says nothing else of him. See [[event/al-hadi-expedition-to-barat-285]].

## Is he Abu Umar of Bahrain?
On p. 95 the book names an Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan "of Bahrain", with the kunya Abu Umar, who was put over Athafit and was killed there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.95} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} Pages 85 and 95 were re-read with Apple Vision and both nisbas are printed as given, and the book's index puts pp. 31 and 95 under one entry and does not list p. 85. It is not settled whether they are one man; neither identity nor difference is asserted, and the two records stay separate. See [[person/abu-umar-abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan-al-bahrani]].
