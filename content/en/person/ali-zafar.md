Ali Zafar (born Ali Muhammad Zafar on 18 May 1980) is a Pakistani singer-songwriter, actor and painter, born at Lahore into a Punjabi family; both his parents were professors at the University of Punjab.{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-en}{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-ar} He graduated from Government College University, Lahore and the National College of Arts as a painter, began as a sketch artist and television actor, and sang Jugnuon Se Bhar De Aanchal for the film Shararat (2003).{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-en}{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-ur}

His debut album Huqa Pani (2003) sold over five million copies worldwide, and its single Channo topped the charts and made him one of Pakistan's most popular pop singers; the albums Masty (2006) and Jhoom (2011) followed.{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-en} He made his film acting debut in the Bollywood satire Tere Bin Laden (2010), acted in films including Mere Brother Ki Dulhan, Chashme Baddoor and Dear Zindagi, and starred in the Pakistani film Teefa in Trouble (2018) through his own Lightingale Productions.{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-en} He has received five Lux Style Awards and was awarded the Pride of Performance in 2021.{cite:wikipedia-ali-zafar-en}{cite:wikidata-ali-zafar}
