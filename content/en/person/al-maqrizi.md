## Overview
The historian Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Ali al-Maqrizi. He died in the days of the second caliph al-Mustakfi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 774}

## His death and works
The data give his death as 845 AH (about 1442 CE; the conversion is derived). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 625} His works include [[work/imta-al-asma-maqrizi|Imta' al-Asma']] and [[work/ittiaz-al-hunafa|Ittiaz al-Hunafa']]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

## Notes for researchers
- The page rests on two sources only. His teachers, his life and his other works need an additional source.

## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Qadir, Taqi al-Din Abu al-Abbas al-Maqrizi, of a family from Baalbek, of Cairo: born in Cairo after 760, with Ibn Hajar seeing evidence for 766. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/21} He was first a Hanafi and became a Shafi'i after his father's death, and served several times as market inspector of Cairo, first in 801. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/22} His works include al-Khitat, al-Suluk, al-Muqaffa and Itti'az al-Hunafa'. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/22} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/23} Al-Sakhawi makes critical remarks on some of his books. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/23} He died on 26 Ramadan 845 in Cairo. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/25}
