## Overview
Shabib al-Kharijí, one of the Kharijites in the time of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. al-Tabari reports his death in the year 77 or 78 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.279}.

## In al-Mas'udi
In the year 77 AH [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] fought Shabib, until Shabib took refuge in the governor's house in Kufa. At dawn Shabib, his mother and his wife Ghazala entered the Kufa mosque. Abd al-Malik sent Sufyan ibn al-Abrad al-Kalbi, Shabib was defeated, and Ghazala and his mother were killed {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 130}.

The story tells that his horse shied at the Dujayl bridge and threw him into the water {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 130}.

## Shawdhab
The book al-Dawla al-Umawiyya by al-Salabi reports that the movement of the Kharijite Shawdhab revived at the beginning of the caliphate of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, and that he was killed by Maslama in the year 101 AH {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.430}.

A possible confusion between the two names needs an additional source.
