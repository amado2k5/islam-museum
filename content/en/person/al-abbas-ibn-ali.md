## Overview
Al-Abbas ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, whose mother was Umm al-Banin bint Hizam al-Kilabiyya. He was killed with his brother al-Husayn on the day of al-Taff, on the tenth of Muharram 61 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.60,62}

## His descendants
Al-Mas'udi says that Ali's line of descent came from five sons, among them al-Abbas. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.60,62}
