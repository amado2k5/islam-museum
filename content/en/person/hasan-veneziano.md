## Summary
Ottoman governor of Algiers who seated himself for a second term, then became kapudan of the sea {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.

## The second governorship
He returned to Algiers when word reached him of chaos among the soldiery and installed himself in Rabi' II 991/April 1583, and the Porte did not object; he kept the fleet's command to himself and booty multiplied off the Spanish coasts and eastern islands {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.

## Rescuing al-Andalus's Muslims
In 992/1584 he sailed his fleet on Valencia and carried off large numbers of al-Andalus's Muslims, saving them from Spanish persecution, and the next year saved all of Calosa's people, ferrying them to Algiers {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.

## Kapudan of the sea
He stayed at the head of Algiers' government until the sultan called him to Istanbul as commander of the sea after [[person/kilic-ali-pasha|Uluj Ali]]'s death in 995/1587 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 272}.
