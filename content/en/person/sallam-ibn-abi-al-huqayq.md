Sallam ibn Abi al-Huqayq al-Nadri, kunya Abu Rafi', notable of the Jews of Banu al-Nadir and wealthy merchant of the Hijaz's people, who settled at Khaybar after al-Nadir's expulsion from Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He was among the chief conspirators who led Khaybar's delegation to Mecca and Ghatafan, and undertook to finance the Confederates' army, spending vast sums to incite the tribes of Ghatafan, Asad and Sulaym to raid Medina and destroy the Muslims. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When the Qurayza affair was over and the Muslims grasped his continuing danger in incitement and financing, the Prophet sent a detachment of five horsemen led by Abdullah ibn Atik, who killed him in his impregnable fortress at Khaybar in Dhu al-Hijja of year five of the hijra. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
