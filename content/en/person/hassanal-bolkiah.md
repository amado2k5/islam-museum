## Summary
Hassanal Bolkiah (born 1946) has reigned as Sultan of Brunei since 1967 and has been its prime minister since independence in 1984. Proclaimed sultan at twenty-one on the abdication of his father, Omar Ali Saifuddien III, he led Brunei to full independence from Britain on 1 January 1984, ruling as one of the world's few remaining absolute monarchs under the national philosophy of Melayu Islam Beraja (Malay Islamic Monarchy). He is the world's longest-reigning current monarch, and celebrated fifty years on the throne in 2017. {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-en} {cite:wikidata-hassanal-bolkiah}

## Early life and education
Pengiran Muda Hassanal Bolkiah was born on 15 July 1946 at Istana Darussalam in Brunei Town, the eldest son of Pengiran Bendahara Omar Ali Saifuddien — the heir presumptive — and Pengiran Anak Damit. He was educated first at the Istana Darul Hana Surau and the Jamalul Alam Malay School, then in Kuala Lumpur at the Victoria Institution from 1961 to 1963, where he was named best recruit in the cadet corps, and at Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien College after returning to Brunei. He had been proclaimed crown prince on 14 July 1961, and in December 1962 Gurkha soldiers rescued him and his father from the palace during the Brunei revolt. In 1965 he married Pengiran Anak Saleha, and in 1966 he entered the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst as a cadet officer; he was commissioned an honorary captain in the Coldstream Guards in 1968. {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-en} {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-ar}

## Accession and coronation
On the evening of 4 October 1967 Omar Ali Saifuddien III announced his abdication; Hassanal Bolkiah returned from England and was proclaimed the twenty-ninth Sultan of Brunei the next day, promising to carry out his father's policies. His coronation followed on 1 August 1968, when his father placed the crown on his head, handed him the Keris si-Naga and removed his own sabre in loyalty to his son as head of state and religion. The old sultan remained the power behind the throne for years, advising on every significant decision. Under the 1959 constitution the Sultan holds full executive authority, a position entrenched by the emergency proclaimed after the 1962 revolt. {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-en}

## Independence and absolute rule
Through the 1970s the Sultan negotiated Brunei's future with Britain: the 1971 friendship agreement gave full internal independence, and the Treaty of Friendship and Cooperation brought full sovereignty on 1 January 1984, when he proclaimed independence at midnight and assumed the offices of prime minister, finance minister and home minister, declaring Melayu Islam Beraja the national philosophy. Development plans funded by oil and gas raised living standards; the Legislative Council, dissolved since 1962, was reconvened in amended form in 2004, and in 2006 he was reported to have amended the constitution to make himself infallible under Bruneian law. Abroad he chaired the APEC summit of 2000 and the ASEAN summits of 2013 and 2021. {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-en} {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-ur}

## The Syariah code and the later reign
In 2014 the Sultan began bringing Brunei's Syariah Penal Code into force, and its final phase, effective 3 April 2019, prescribed stoning for adultery and same-sex acts and amputation for theft. The laws drew worldwide protest and boycotts of the royal family's Dorchester Collection hotels; within weeks the Sultan extended a moratorium on the death penalty and announced ratification of the UN Convention Against Torture. His Golden Jubilee in October 2017 brought tens of thousands into the capital for a chariot procession. During the COVID-19 pandemic he imposed early travel restrictions and received his vaccination publicly in April 2021. His filiation is recorded once, in his father's record; and no source read for this record names a religious school for Hassanal Bolkiah — his headship of Islam in Brunei is an office of his throne — so this record records none. {cite:wikipedia-hassanal-bolkiah-en} {cite:wikidata-hassanal-bolkiah}
