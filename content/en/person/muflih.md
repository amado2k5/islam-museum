## Summary
Muflih was a Turkish commander in al-Mu'tazz's armies: he defeated Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Dulaf outside Hamadhan in 253 AH, and was killed by an arrow in the Zanj war in 258 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.495}

## Details
In 253 AH Muflih led the vanguard of Musa ibn Bugha the Elder: he engaged Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Dulaf eight nights before the end of Rajab, routed him outside Hamadhan and pursued him three parasangs, killing and capturing; then in Ramadan he marshalled his cavalry into al-Kurj, took captives and women of the Banu Abi Dulaf, and seventy loads of heads were sent to Samarra. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.373}

In 258 AH, in the war against the Zanj leader, Muflih was with Abu Ahmad ibn al-Mutawakkil at the encounter with the Zanj: a stray arrow struck him — no one knew the archer — and the rout followed. Al-Tabari records that Abu Ahmad did not know how Muflih had been killed until told he was hit by an arrow and saw no one hand him a shot; a retainer's claim that he had picked up a fallen arrow and shot Muflih's flank is dismissed by al-Tabari, who was present at the scene. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.495}
