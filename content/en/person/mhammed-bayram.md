## Overview
Muhammad ibn Husayn ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Husayn ibn Bayram was one of the notables of the Bayram family of Tunis, and served as its mufti for forty-five years, dying in 1214 AH (1800 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.104}

He began several works, of which he completed only Bughyat al-Sa'il, an abridgement of Anfa' al-Wasa'il of al-Tarsusi on Hanafi fiqh; a treatise on the shari'a politics; and he wrote verse. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.104}
