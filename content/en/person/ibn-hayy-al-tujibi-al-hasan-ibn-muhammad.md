## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Hayy al-Tujibi al-Qurtubi. Of the Tujib branch of Kinda, he became known as Ibn Hayy, and is called al-Qurtubi because he was from Córdoba. He died in 456 AH (1063 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.403}
