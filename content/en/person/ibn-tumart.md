## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Tumart al-Nafzi, known as the Mahdi Ibn Tumart, founder of the Almohad call in the Maghrib: in 516 AH he began to oppose the Almoravid sultan, which weakened their power in al-Andalus. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 p.124}

A battle was fought between the two sides at al-Buhayra, in which the Almohad army under Muhammad al-Bashir al-Wansharisi was defeated and its commander killed; Ibn Tumart lost most of his ten companions and thousands of the flower of his army, his illness worsened, and he died in 524 AH (1130 CE), succeeded by Abd al-Mu'min ibn Ali who restored the Almohads' power. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 p.124}
