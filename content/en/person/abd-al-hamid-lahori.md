## Summary
Abd al-Hamid Lahori wrote the most famous [[work/padshahnama-lahori|Padshah-nama]], the official biography of [[person/shah-jahan-mughal|Shah Jahan]] whose illustrated copy is at [[object/windsor-padshahnama|Windsor]]. He used the Padshah-nama of Muhammad Amin Qazwini, completed in 1646, for his first part, so, Milo Cleveland Beach notes, he cannot have begun his text before then. {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 76}

## From his text
Beach quotes his description of Shah Jahan's throne for the eighth regnal year to show that contemporary accounts disagree on the number and material of its columns, though all agree on the peacocks. {cite:beach-grand-mogul-1978 p. 78}
