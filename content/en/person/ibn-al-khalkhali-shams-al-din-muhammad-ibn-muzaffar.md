## Overview
The imam Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Muzaffar al-Khatibi al-Khalkhali, a Shafi'i imam attributed to Khankhal, a village in the districts of Sultaniyya. He died in 745 AH (1344 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
