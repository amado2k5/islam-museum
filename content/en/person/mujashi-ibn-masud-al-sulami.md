## Overview
Mujashi' ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami, whom Abdallah ibn Amir sent on the track of Yazdegerd. He followed him to Kirman and alighted at Sirjan, and Yazdegerd fled to Khurasan. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.286}

## In al-Baladhuri
Al-Baladhuri says that he alighted at Bayman in Kirman, and the people were stricken by plague (al-damaq), his army perished, and only a few survived. The palace was called the Palace of Mujashi'. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 7811: "Mujashi' ibn Mas'ud ibn Tha'laba ibn Wahb ibn A'idh ibn Rabi'a ibn Yarbu' ibn Sammal ibn Awf ibn Imru' al-Qays ibn Buhtha ibn Sulaym ibn Mansur al-Sulami"; al-Bukhari and others record his companionship of the Prophet, he has a narration in the two Sahihs and elsewhere, and Abu Uthman al-Nahdi, Kulayb ibn Shihab and others narrated from him. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1335}

## His brother
His brother was Mujalid ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami, whom Ibn Hajar lists under no. 7814 in the immediately following entry. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1335}
