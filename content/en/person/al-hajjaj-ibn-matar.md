## Overview
Al-Hajjaj ibn Matar, a translator who worked for al-Ma'mun. Ibn al-Nadim says he was the one who translated the Almagest and Euclid. He was among those whom al-Ma'mun sent to the lands of the Byzantines to bring back ancient books. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.339}

He translated Euclid's "Elements of Geometry" in two versions: the first known as the Harunian, and the second the Ma'munian, on which people rely. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.371}
