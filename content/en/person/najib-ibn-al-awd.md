## Summary
Najib ibn al-'Awd, the Hilli mutakallim — Ibn al-'Imad describes him as shaykh and scholar of the Shi'a. He lived at Aleppo for a time and was slapped there for reviling the Companions — according to the source's account — then settled at Jizzin until he died there in mid-Sha'ban at more than ninety years of age, having fallen into dotage. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 637}
