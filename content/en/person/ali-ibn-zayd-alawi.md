## Overview
Ali ibn Zayd al-Alawi, an Alid who rose in Kufa in 255 AH together with Isa ibn Ja'far al-Alawi. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 p.145}

## The report
Al-Mu'tazz sent Sa'id ibn Salih the chamberlain against them. The two Talibids were defeated because their companions scattered from them. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 p.145}
