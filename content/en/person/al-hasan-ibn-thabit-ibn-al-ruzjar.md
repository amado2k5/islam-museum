## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn Thabit, Abu al-Hasan al-Ahwal al-Tha'alabi of Kufa, known as Ibn al-Ruzjar, a Successor. He transmitted from Isma'il ibn Abi Khalid, Abd Allah ibn al-Walid al-Muzani and Hisham ibn Urwa, and Ibn al-Mubarak and Yahya ibn Adam heard him. Ibn Numayr called him trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8192}
