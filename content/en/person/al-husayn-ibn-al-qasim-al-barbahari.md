## Overview
Al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah al-Barbahari was the leader of the Hanbalis in Baghdad at the end of the third century AH, and had great influence with the common people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.13}

## The allegiance to Ibn al-Mu'tazz
When [[person/ibn-al-mutazz|Ibn al-Mu'tazz]] was given the allegiance in 296 AH, the herald went before him calling: "O company of the common people, this is for your caliph al-Sallam al-Barbahari", seeking to win them over with this, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.13}
