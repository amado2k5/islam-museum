## Overview
Abu'l-Su'ud Ahmad b. al-Mubarak the ascetic al-Harimi; a perfumer whom God settled, who then cut off to God and kept company with Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani — he has karamat: he would not eat until fed, drink until watered, wear a garment until it was placed on his neck, speak save in answer, and remained ever in purity facing the qibla. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 754}

## The wonder of his patience
A roof fell on him, a beam broke the heads of his ribs, and he did not move until companions came and lifted the roof off; he lived twenty years with none knowing his ribs were broken, until he died and they found them broken on the washing-slab. He died in 582. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 754}
