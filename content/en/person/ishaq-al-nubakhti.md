## Overview
Ishaq ibn Ismail al-Nubakhti, the one who advised for the caliphate of al-Qahir. Al-Qahir later killed him, and threw him head first into a well, which was then filled in. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.598-599}

Al-Suyuti mentions the cause of al-Qahir's grudge against him: that he outbid him for a slave-girl before the caliphate and bought her. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.598-599}
