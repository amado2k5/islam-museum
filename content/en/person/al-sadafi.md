## Overview
Shaykh Abu Ali al-Sadafi, one of the famous scholars of al-Andalus. At the battle of Quintana, where the commander of the Muslims was the amir Ibrahim ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin, al-Sadafi went out as a raider with his peer in merit, Abu Abd Allah Ibn al-Farra', and both were among those lost there in 514 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.465}
