## Overview
[[person/umar-khayyam|Umar Khayyam]], who is Umar ibn Ibrahim al-Khayyam, Abu al-Fath, a mathematician, astronomer, philosopher and poet {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.628}.

## Life
The encyclopedia gives his birth as 439 AH and about 1048 CE, and his death as 526 AH and about 1131 CE. Little is known of his life {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.628}.
