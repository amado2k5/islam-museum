## Overview
Simak ibn Ubayd al-Absi, a man whom al-Baladhuri mentions in the report of Nihawand. He pursued a Persian man who had eight horsemen with him. Whenever one of them came out to him, he killed him, until the man was left alone. He then asked for safe conduct and laid down his weapons, and Simak took him prisoner. He turned out to be Dinar, lord of the Nihawand treaty. From then on he would visit Simak, giving him gifts and kindness {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.429}.
