## Summary
[[person/al-bara-ibn-azib|Al-Bara' ibn Azib]] ibn al-Harith al-Ansari al-Awsi, kunya Abu Umara — it is also said Abu Amr. He and his father have companionship {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 137}.

## Badr and Uhud
Al-Bara' said: "the Messenger of God found me too young on the day of Badr — I and Ibn Umar — and sent us back, so we did not witness it"; in Abd al-Rahman ibn Awsaja's account he adds: "and I witnessed Uhud" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 137}. It is related of him that he campaigned with the Messenger of God on fourteen expeditions — fifteen in another account — and he said: "I travelled with the Messenger of God eighteen journeys" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 137}.

## What he related about the source of his hadith
Ahmad relates from al-Bara': "not all we tell you from the Messenger of God did we hear from him — our companions told it to us, for tending camels kept us busy" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 138}.

## After the Prophet
He was the conqueror of al-Rayy in 24 AH per Abu Amr al-Shaybani — others disagree — and he witnessed the Tustar expedition with [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]], and with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] the Camel, Siffin and the fight against the Kharijites; he settled in [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and built a house there {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 138}.

## His death
He died in Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr's emirate; Ibn Hibban dates it 72 AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 138}.

## His transmitters
He related a body of hadith from the Prophet, and from his father, Abu Bakr, Umar and others; from him relate Abu Juhayfa, Abdullah ibn Yazid al-Khatmi and a group ending with Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 138}.

## For Sprouts
Al-Bara' ibn Azib the Ansari of Aws, kunya Abu Ammara; the Prophet judged him too young at Badr and sent him back — he witnessed Uhud and campaigned with the Prophet some fourteen expeditions.

## For Explorers
Al-Bara' ibn Azib ibn al-Harith the Ansari of Aws, kunya Abu Ammara, also said Abu Amr; he and his father have companionship. He said: 'The Messenger of God judged me too young at Badr, me and Ibn Umar, and sent us back so we did not witness it' — and he witnessed Uhud. It is told he campaigned with the Prophet fourteen expeditions, in one telling fifteen, and said 'I travelled with the Messenger of God eighteen journeys'. Ahmad transmits from him that they did not hear all their hadith from the Prophet directly — their companions related it, since tending camels kept them busy. He opened Rayy in 24 AH in Abu Amr al-Shaybani's saying, though others disagree; he witnessed Tustar with Abu Musa, and with Ali the Camel, Siffin and the fighting of the Kharijites; he settled in Kufa, built a house, and died under Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr's governorship — Ibn Hibban dates him 72 AH.
