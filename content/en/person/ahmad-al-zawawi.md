## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abdallah al-Jaza'iri al-Zawawi was a Maliki scholar of the Zawawa tribe who lived in Algiers {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 160}. Al-Zirikli prints his birth as 800 AH, 1398 CE, and his death as 884 AH, 1479 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 160}.

## Works
He wrote a poem rhymed in lam on theology, called al-Jaza'iriyya fi al-Aqa'id al-Imaniyya, in manuscript at the Azhar library; al-Imam al-Sanusi wrote a commentary on it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 160}.
