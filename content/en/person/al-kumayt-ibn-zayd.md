## Overview
al-Kumayt ibn Zayd, the poet of the Banu Hashim; his verse ran through the Nizariyya and the Yemenis until Nizar boasted against Yemen and Yemen against Nizar, the people took sides and factional strife flared among bedouin and townsfolk — al-Mas'udi counts it among the causes of the fall of the Umayyads.
