## Overview
Khalid ibn al-Mu'ammar al-Dhuhli was over the men of Ibn Amir on the day Istakhr was taken by storm. Mu'awiya appointed him over Khurasan after the removal of Qays ibn al-Haytham. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
He died in the palace of Manatil or at Ayn al-Tamr. There are two accounts of the cause of his death. It is said that Mu'awiya regretted appointing him and sent him a poisoned garment. It is also said that a piece of glass entered his foot and he bled until he died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
