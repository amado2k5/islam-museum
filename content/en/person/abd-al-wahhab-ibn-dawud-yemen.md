## Summary
al-Mansur 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Dawud, lord of Yemen, died in Jumada al-Ula 894 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}.

## Details
Among the dead of 894 Ibn al-'Imad names "al-Mansur 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Dawud, lord of Yemen," who died in Jumada al-Ula {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}. He belonged to the Tahirid sultans who ruled Yemen, several members of that family succeeding one another on the throne, with the regnal name al-Mansur common among them {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}. The same year's obituaries include the sharif Abu Sa'd ibn Barakat ibn Hasan ibn 'Ajlan, lord of the Hijaz, who died in Rabi' al-Akhir, and the shaykh 'Abdallah known as Hajji Khalifa, a man of the Sufi path from Qastamuni — so that one year gathered the passing of sovereignty, nobility and spiritual leadership across Yemen, the Hijaz and the lands of Rum {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}.
