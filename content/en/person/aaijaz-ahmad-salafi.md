## Overview
Aaijaz Ahmad Salafi (also spelled Aijaz) is a nasheed reciter and preacher from Kashmir, known through the Kashmiri- and Urdu-language nasheeds published on his official YouTube channel and through his sermons and lectures in towns of the Kashmir Valley. {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}

In his channel description he introduces himself in his own words: "I am Aijaz Salafi, Nasheed Reciter at present a student of Commerce", and his video titles give the fuller form of his name as "Aaijaz Ahmad Salafi". {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}

His channel had about 140,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}

## Career and work
Among his best-known recordings is the nasheed "Haa Husni walai", filmed at Jamia Masjid Mujgund in Srinagar, which had about 3.8 million views according to the channel page in October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}

His channel's titles in 2026 show weekly preaching activity: Friday sermons at Gundipora Beerwah and Lasjan in Budgam district, and lectures and gatherings at Singpora Pattan and other places in the Kashmir Valley. {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}

His material ranges between nasheeds — including poems by Kashmiri poets named in the recording titles — and sermons on doctrinal and exhortatory themes such as monotheism, the standing of the saints, the punishment of the grave and the virtues of the Prophet's household, as his titles name them. {cite:youtube-aaijaz-salafi}
