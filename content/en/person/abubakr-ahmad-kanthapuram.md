## Summary
Kanthapuram A. P. Aboobacker Musliyar, known in public life as Sheikh Abubakr Ahmad, is a Kerala Sunni scholar of the AP tradition who was elevated in 2019 as Grand Mufti of India by the All India Tanzeem Ulama-e-Islam. He is general secretary of the All India Sunni Jamiyyathul Ulama and founder of the Markaz educational movement based at Karanthur, Kozhikode. {cite:wikipedia-abubakr-ahmad-kanthapuram}

## Life and formation
He was born on 22 March 1939 at Kanthapuram near Kozhikode in Malabar, then British India, and studied under scholars of the Samastha tradition, earning the title Musliyar. His schooling joined the traditional mosque-based curriculum of Kerala's Sufi-leaning Shafi'i Sunni community. {cite:wikipedia-abubakr-ahmad-kanthapuram}

## Institutions and leadership
In 1978 he founded the Markazu Ssaquafathi Ssunniyya (Markaz), which grew over decades into a large educational township — schools, colleges, a "knowledge city" — educating tens of thousands of students. He leads Samastha Kerala Jem-iyyathul Ulama as general secretary, heads the Kerala Muslim Jamaat federation and, since February 2019, holds the title Grand Mufti of India, conferred at a mass gathering in Delhi's Ramlila Maidan. {cite:wikipedia-abubakr-ahmad-kanthapuram}

## Public interventions
He issued early religious rulings against militant groups claiming Islamic sanction, publicly opposed the Citizenship Amendment protests' more confrontational modes while defending minority rights, intervened in 2024 in the case of a Kerala nurse facing execution in Yemen — whose sentence was postponed after his intercession — and has been received in Gulf capitals as an Indian Sunni leader. {cite:wikipedia-abubakr-ahmad-kanthapuram}

## Assessments
Followers describe a scholar-statesman who built Kerala's largest Sunni educational network and kept his community institutionally united. Critics — including within Muslim and progressive circles — have faulted statements on gender segregation and public roles for women; he and his institutions describe the remarks as grounded in traditional jurisprudence. The museum records both readings attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-abubakr-ahmad-kanthapuram}
