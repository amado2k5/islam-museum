## Overview
Al-Ayki, the Persian scholar of the Shafi'is; a jurist-sufi, imam of the two sources — he came to Damascus, taught at the Ghazzaliyya, commented on the logic of Ibn al-Hajib's Mukhtasar, then travelled to Egypt and held its mashyakhat al-shuyukh — the sufis spoke against him, so he left it and returned to Damascus. Imam Abu Hayyan charged him with ilhad, counting him among those famed for it — as Ibn al-'Imad relays it, adding: and God knows best. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 767-768}
