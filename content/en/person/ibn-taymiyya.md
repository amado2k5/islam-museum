## Overview
Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Abd al-Halim ibn Taymiyya al-Harrani al-Hanbali, known by the title Shaykh al-Islam. A Hanbali jurist and theologian from Harran and Damascus. {cite:aqida-wasitiyya}

## Life
The title page of the Salafiyya Press edition of *al-Aqida al-Wasitiyya* gives 661 AH as his year of birth and 728 AH as his year of death. These dates come from one source and need an additional source. {cite:aqida-wasitiyya}

## His books
He wrote [[work/aqida-wasitiyya|al-Aqida al-Wasitiyya]] in 698 AH, setting out the creed in the manner of the People of the Sunna and the Community and the early generations. {cite:aqida-wasitiyya} He is also credited with [[work/iqtida-al-sirat-al-mustaqim|Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim]] (The Demand of the Straight Path). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

## The dhimmis in 709 AH
Al-Suyuti says that in 709 AH the vizier spoke of returning the dhimmis to wearing white turbans, and that they committed to the treasury seven hundred thousand each year. Ibn Taymiyya spoke out strongly to overturn this, and the matter was dropped. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.734-735}

## Ghazan in 700 AH
Ibn al-Imad says that [[person/ghazan-khan|Ghazan]] crossed the Euphrates with his army and headed for Aleppo. Ibn Taymiyya sent a dispatch by post to Cairo to rouse people to holy war, and met the chief emirs. The people of Damascus were afraid and gathered in the citadel, and the fear subsided when Ghazan turned back. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.429}
