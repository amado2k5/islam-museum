## Overview
Al-Furati, shaykh of the Shafi'is Abu al-Qasim Ya'ish ibn Sadaqa the blind, companion of Ibn al-Khal; he recited under the readings to the sharif Abu al-Barakat 'Umar ibn Ibrahim and heard from Isma'il ibn al-Samarqandi and others, while al-Taqi ibn Basuya, Ibn al-Dubayshi, Ibn Khalil, al-Yaldani and, by licence, Ahmad ibn Abi al-Khayr narrated from him. He died in 593 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4255}
