## Summary
'Isa al-Yunini the ascetic known as Sallab al-Ajwal, companion of Shaykh 'Abdallah al-Yunini; a worshipping, abstinent man of fasting and night prayer, fearful and devout, wholly given over to God, severed of companions, possessed of spiritual states and sincerity — but sharp of soul, hence named Sallab al-Ajwal; coarse of living in dress and food; he died and was buried at his zawiya in Yunin. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}
