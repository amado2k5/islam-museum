## Overview
Aws ibn Thalaba of the Banu Taym Allah ibn Thalaba ibn Akaba, one of the notables of Khurasan, who held great offices there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The Qasr Aws in Basra is attributed to him. Al-Baladhuri says that he was over the army of Ibn Amir to Herat, where the Rabi'a gathered to him. Abd Allah ibn Khazim marched against him and secretly had him poisoned, then he was wounded in the battle and died some days later. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
