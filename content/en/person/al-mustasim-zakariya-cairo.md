## Overview
Zakariya ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Mustamsik ibn al-Hakim, titled al-Mustasim billah, an Abbasid caliph in Cairo who held the office twice and ended deposed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759-761}

## His two caliphates
Al-Suyuti says that the atabeg Aynbak al-Badri set him up as caliph in 779 AH, without an allegiance or consensus, so his first caliphate lasted fifteen days until [[person/al-mutawakkil-ala-llah|al-Mutawakkil]] returned. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759-761} Then Barquq gave him the allegiance after the death of his brother al-Wathiq Umar, and it continued until 791 AH. In that year Barquq regretted it, restored al-Mutawakkil and deposed Zakariya. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759-761}

## His last years
He remained in his house until he died in deposition. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759-761}
