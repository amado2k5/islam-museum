## Overview
Al-Hujja al-Atabakiyya, wife of al-Malik al-Ashraf Musa b. al-'Adil — Turkan bint al-Malik 'Izz al-Din Mas'ud b. Qutb al-Din Mawdud b. Atabak Zangi, a Zangid princess whom al-Ashraf married; patron of the madrasa and mausoleum at Mount Qasyun, Damascus. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 207}
