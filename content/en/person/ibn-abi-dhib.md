## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Mughira, Abu al-Harith, known as Ibn Abi Dhi'b. Al-Mas'udi says he died in Kufa in 159 AH in the days of al-Mahdi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.313}
