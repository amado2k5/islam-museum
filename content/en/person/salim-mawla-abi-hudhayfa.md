## Summary
[[person/salim-mawla-abi-hudhayfa|Salim, client of Abu Hudhayfa]] ibn Utba ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd Shams, "one of the first forerunners" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 537}. Bukhari said: his mistress was a woman of the Ansar {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 537}. Ibn Hibban said she is called Layla, or it is said Thubayta bint Ya'ar, and she was Abu Hudhayfa's wife — Ibn Sa'd asserts this; Ibn Abi Dawud said he is Salim ibn Ma'qil, client of an Ansari woman called Fatima bint Ya'ar, whom Sa'iba freed, and he attached himself to Abu Hudhayfa {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 537} {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}. Ibn Manda claimed he is Salim ibn Ubayd ibn Rabi'a; Abu Nu'aym corrected him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}.

## The adoption
[[person/abu-hudhayfa-ibn-utba|Abu Hudhayfa]] had adopted him as the Prophet adopted Zayd ibn Haritha, so he regarded him as his son and married him to his niece Fatima bint al-Walid ibn Utba; when [[passage/quran-33-5|al-Ahzab 5]] descended, each of those who had adopted a son returned him to his father, and whoever's father was unknown was returned to his patrons — narrated by Malik in the Muwatta from al-Zuhri from Urwa {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}.

## Leading the prayer at Quba
Bukhari relates in Ibn Umar's hadith: Salim, Abu Hudhayfa's client, used to lead the first Muhajirun in prayer in the mosque of [[place/quba|Quba]], among them [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]; al-Tabarani adds "and he was the most Quran-learned of them" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}.

## Narration
Ibn Abi Hatim said: I know of nothing narrated from him; Ibn Hajar answers that two hadiths are narrated from him at al-Baghawi, Samawayh and Ibn Shahin, though both chains have weakness and breakage — so Ibn Abi Hatim's words carry the sense that nothing sound is established from him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}.

## His suckling
In Sahih Muslim by way of al-Qasim from A'isha: Salim was with Abu Hudhayfa, and [[person/sahla-bint-suhayl|Sahla bint Suhayl]] came to the Prophet saying Salim had reached men's growth and came in upon her, and she thought she saw something of that in Abu Hudhayfa's face — he said: "suckle him and he becomes forbidden to you" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}.

## Note
An account from Umm Salama has the Prophet's wives saying to A'isha: "we see this as nothing but a concession the Messenger of God gave Salim" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 538}. This part of Salim's entry records only the incident and the transmitted phrase; the jurists' disagreement over suckling a grown person is a disputed matter needing review of the legal references before presentation — Sunni view first, then others.
