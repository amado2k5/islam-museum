## Overview
Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Andalusi, who died in 276 AH. Ibn al-Jawzi says that he had a famous journey and a famous quest for knowledge, and that he heard from Ahmad ibn Hanbal and other imams. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.110-111}

## His works and character
He had many works, among them his "Musnad", in which he related from more than sixteen hundred Companions and more. He combined knowledge with righteousness and piety. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.110-111}

The report says that a woman came to him complaining that the Byzantines held her son captive and she could not ransom him. He lowered his head and then said to her: "Open your mouth", and then he called for the ransom. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.110-111}

## His journey
Al-Maqqari says that he heard in Mecca, then entered Iraq and met Ibrahim ibn Abi al-Anbas, the judge of Kufa. In Baghdad he heard from the judge Isma'il, Ahmad ibn Zuhayr ibn Harb, Abd Allah the son of Imam Ahmad, and al-Harith ibn Abi Usama. In al-Qayrawan he heard from Bakr ibn Hammad al-Tahirti, then returned to al-Andalus with much knowledge, and people inclined to him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.50}

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "A Story and an Idea"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the eighteenth episode of his programme [[work/kissa-wa-fikra-program|"A Story and an Idea"]] to Baqi ibn Makhlad, as his model of aspiration in seeking knowledge. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:01:00} What follows is his account.

He calls him an Andalusian jurist, imam and exegete, author of a tafsir, of a hadith collection arranged by Companions and of a book of the legal opinions of the Companions and Successors. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:01:30} He brought to al-Andalus the *Musannaf* of [[person/ibn-abi-shayba|Ibn Abi Shayba]], the *Tarikh* and *Tabaqat* of Khalifa ibn Khayyat and a biography of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:02:30} When some scholars objected to the *Musannaf*, the amir Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman read it through, had his librarian copy it, told Baqi to spread his knowledge and forbade the scholars to obstruct him; Baqi later refused the judgeship of Cordoba offered by al-Mundhir ibn Muhammad. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:03:30} In al-Andalus he studied with [[person/yahya-ibn-yahya-al-laythi|Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi]] and others, and in the Hijaz, Egypt, Damascus, Baghdad, Kufa and Basra he heard about two hundred and eighty-four scholars, among them Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Ibn Abi Shayba and Sahnun. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:05:30}

He had asked who was the most learned man on earth and was told Ahmad ibn Hanbal. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:11:30} He walked from al-Andalus, working in each village to pay for the next stage, and on arrival learned that Ahmad was under house arrest in the Mihna and that anyone who met him would be jailed. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:12:00} At dawn in the mosque he found a man judging the narrators of hadith: [[person/yahya-ibn-main|Yahya ibn Ma'in]], Ahmad's companion, who had given way in the Mihna; al-Suwaidan's lesson is to take a scholar's knowledge and leave his positions. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:15:00} Ibn Ma'in knew every Andalusian narrator Baqi asked about, and asked about Ahmad praised him as the best man on earth, against the line of the state. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:18:00}

Baqi found the house and told Ahmad he had walked from beyond the sea for him alone; Ahmad said he was under trial and forbidden to meet people. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:19:30} So Baqi came daily as a beggar, with a stick, a dirty rag on his head and paper in his sleeve, calling the cry of the beggars of Baghdad, and Ahmad let him in at the door and dictated hadiths to him, on condition that he not appear at the hadith circles. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:21:00} When the Mihna ended under al-Mutawakkil, Ahmad's circle filled with people from Khurasan to al-Andalus; whenever Baqi gave his name, Ahmad seated him beside him, told his story and read with him first. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:23:00} When Baqi fell ill, Ahmad visited him at his inn with his students, the inn was astonished, and the lodgers then nursed him better than his family would have. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:25:30} He says Baqi made two journeys, of fourteen years and of twenty, and cites [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] as saying that through Baqi and [[person/muhammad-ibn-waddah|Muhammad ibn Waddah]] al-Andalus became a home of hadith, and as calling him an imam, a mujtahid, righteous and devoted. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan kissa-wa-fikra/ep18 00:26:30}
