## Summary
Mas'ud ibn al-Harith al-Jahmi was a diligent and well-regarded worshipper. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 512, sequence 513, on page 147. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/147}

## A dream
Sulayman ibn Musa relates from a man who saw Mas'ud ibn al-Harith, the brother of Khalid, in a dream, and asked him: what has your Lord done with you? He said: He brought me near and drew me close, and said to me: O Mas'ud, long did you wander the roads of the world, and I am content with you. Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/147}
