## Summary
Muharib ibn Hassan was the best of young men, kept from loss and ruin, and fortified by certainty and faith. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 522, sequence 523, on page 155. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}

## Masjid al-Khayf and the youth from the Maghrib
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Muhammad relates from his brother that he heard Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Razi say he heard Khayr al-Nassaj say: I was with Muharib ibn Hassan al-Sufi at Masjid al-Khayf, and we were in the state of ihram. A handsome youth from the Maghrib sat with us, and I saw Muharib look at him with a look I disapproved of. After rebuking him I said: you are in a sacred month, on a sacred day, in a sacred land, at a sacred place of remembrance, in a sacred mosque, and I have seen you look at this youth in a way only the bewildered look. He said: do you say this? Do you not know that three things have kept me from falling into the snare of Iblis? I asked what they were. He said: the covering of faith, the chastity of Islam, and, greatest to me and most honoured in my breast, shame before God that He should see me crouching over a wrong He forbade me. Then he fainted until the people gathered around us. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/155}
