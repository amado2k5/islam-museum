## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi al-Jamma'ili al-Hanbali, Abu al-Faraj, Shams al-Din, was a jurist and one of the notables of the Hanbalis. He was born in 597 AH, 1200 CE, and died in 682 AH, 1283 CE, born and dying in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## The judgeship and his works
He was the first to hold the Hanbali judgeship in Damascus; he kept it for about twelve years, took no pay for it, and then resigned of his own accord {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. He wrote several works; al-Zirikli names "al-Shafi", printed, the great commentary on "al-Muqni'", in Hanbali jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
