## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Husayn al-Nasiri al-Tamimi al-Najdi al-Hanbali was a jurist of al-Waqf in the Washm villages, and held the judgeship of Washm. He died in 1237 AH (1822 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.21}

The imam Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad sent him in 1185 AH to the governor of Mecca to debate its scholars, and he came back successful; sent again in 1204 AH, the Meccan scholars would not meet him. He left a sixty-four-page Risala on the meaning of worship, and perhaps more. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.21}
