## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Ayyashi Sukayrij al-Khazraji al-Ansari, a man of Fes by birth and home, was a qadi learned in biographies, a Moroccan of the Tijani Sufi order; the entry gives his years as 1295 to 1363 AH, 1878 to 1944 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}.

## His life
He graduated from al-Qarawiyyin and taught there, moved to Tangier, then held the supervision of the awqaf at Fes, then the judgeship of the city of Oujda, then of the port of al-Jadida, then of the city of Settat, and died in Marrakesh {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}.

## His works
Among his books al-Zirikli names "Kashf al-Hijab amman Talaqa ma' al-Tijani min al-Ashab", printed, and its supplement "Raf' al-Niqab ba'd Kashf al-Hijab", of which the first quarter is printed, both on the Sufis of the Tijani order {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}. Also "al-Rihla al-Habibiyya al-Wahraniyya", printed, in which he says he was in Tangier in 1329 and reached Mostaganem and Tlemcen and returned to Fes, and which includes biographies of some of those he met {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}. And "Riyad al-Salwan fi Tarajim man Ijtama'tu bihim min al-A'yan", of which Ibn Suda said that it gives the biographies of nearly two thousand men of merit of his age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}. He left much verse, and al-Zirikli quotes the opening of one poem, "I left my loved ones out of longing for loved ones" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 188}.
