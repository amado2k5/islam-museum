## Summary
Ibrahim Salih al-Husayni is a Nigerian hadith shaykh from Maiduguri, Borno, among West Africa’s greatest hadith authorities and Grand Mufti of Nigeria. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-salih-al-husayni}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Nigeria in 1938, he grew up in the scholarly milieu of Kano and Borno, studied at the Islamic University of Madinah, and took hadith ijazas from Nigerian and Hejazi shaykhs. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-salih-al-husayni}

## Career and works
He has taught Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim and the Sunans in Borno circles for decades, chaired Nigerian fiqh academies and served as the country’s grand mufti. He authored hundreds of treatises and books in creed, fiqh, hadith and biographies of African scholars, holding isnad sessions sought by students across and beyond West Africa. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-salih-al-husayni}

## Method and contribution
He represents the continuation of the Hejazi isnad school in the African Sahel, with attention to combating extremism and innovations within the Sunni frame. He is celebrated regionally as Nigeria’s foremost hadith authority, and some of his fatwas and political positions are locally debated; the museum attributes these descriptions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-salih-al-husayni}
