## Overview

Al-Qasim ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, a son of the Prophet's grandson al-Hasan. Ibn Kathir records his killing at Karbala in 61 AH among the ahl al-bayt martyred with Husayn {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya}.

He is remembered among the martyrs of Karbala in both Sunni and Shia commemoration.
