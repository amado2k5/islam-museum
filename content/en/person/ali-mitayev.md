# Ali Mitayev

Ali Bamat-Gireyevich Mitayev (1881 – September 1925), of Avtury in Chechnya, was a Chechen Sufi sheikh and political and military leader, one of the most influential Sufi sheikhs in Chechnya and Ingushetia in the years of the Russian Revolution and Civil War {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-en}.

## Sheikh and school-founder

Born at Avtury to the family of Sheikh Bamat-Girey-Hajji Mitayev, he was educated at a madrasa in Grozny and in 1912 founded a madrasa and a Russian school in his native village, where Chechen, Russian and Arabic, theology, mathematics and other subjects were taught {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-ru}. On his father's death in 1914 he was proclaimed sheikh and continuer of his father's cause {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-ru}.

## Civil war and death

In the Civil War Mitayev led the Sharia Regiment and defended Grozny against Cossack forces in September 1918 {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-ru}. In 1919 he allied with the Bolsheviks against the White movement on condition of Chechen autonomy and freedom of Muslim religious practice, joined the Communist Party and became a member of the Chechen revolutionary committee {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-en}. The cooperation ended in 1923; he was arrested in March 1924 accused of preparing a counter-revolutionary rising and was shot in the central prison of Rostov-on-Don in September 1925 {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-en} {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-ru}.

## Faith

Two independent texts attest Mitayev's Sufi sheikhdom personally: the English article's lead names him the sheikh of a Sufi tariqa and one of the most influential Sufi sheikhs in Chechnya and Ingushetia {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-en}, and the Russian article's text records his proclamation as sheikh on his father's death and his leadership of thousands of murids {cite:wikipedia-ali-mitayev-ru}. His faith segment is therefore recorded as sufi, documented. No text names the order he headed. The "Sunni Islam" of the English infobox stands alone — Wikidata carries no religion value {cite:wikidata-ali-mitayev} — so no Sunni segment is recorded.
