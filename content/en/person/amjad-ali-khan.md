Amjad Ali Khan (born 9 October 1945) is an Indian sarod maestro, born at Gwalior, the son and pupil of the sarod player Hafiz Ali Khan and heir to the Bangash line of sarod players whose lineage is traced to the court of the emperor Akbar.{cite:wikipedia-amjad-ali-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-amjad-ali-khan} He gave his first solo concert at the age of six; in 1957 the family moved to Delhi, where he was schooled at Modern School while continuing his musical training under his father.{cite:wikipedia-amjad-ali-khan-en}

He received the Padma Shri in 1975, the Padma Bhushan in 1991 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2001.{cite:wikipedia-amjad-ali-khan-en} Neither biography records a religious affiliation for him, and Wikidata gives only Islam.{cite:wikipedia-amjad-ali-khan-ar}{cite:wikidata-amjad-ali-khan}
