## Overview
Umayr ibn al-Ahmar al-Yashkuri, whom Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura appointed over Zaranj when he left Sistan because of the unrest in the affair of Uthman {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Zaranj
The people of Zaranj expelled him and shut the town against him. The poet Ziyad al-A'jam has a verse about him, to which the line of gratitude for his remaining is attributed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
