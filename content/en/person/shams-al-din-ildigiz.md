## Overview
Shams al-Din Ildigiz, a man from the lands of the Qipchaq, who had been a mamluk of Sultan Mas'ud the Seljuk in Iraq {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}.

## The atabegate
He founded the [[polity/ildigizid-atabegs|atabegate of Azerbaijan]] in the year 531 AH. His state lasted about a century in the mountains, Azerbaijan and Iraq Ajam, until the Khwarazm Shah removed it in the year 622 AH {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.88-89}.
