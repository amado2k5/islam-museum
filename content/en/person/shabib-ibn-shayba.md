## Summary
Abu Ma'mar Shabib ibn Shayba, the khatib of Basra nicknamed al-Munqari, transmitted from al-Hasan, Mu'awiya ibn Qurra and 'Ata' ibn Abi Rabah; 'Isa ibn Yunus and Abu Badr Shuja' ibn al-Walid transmitted from him. He had tongue and eloquence. He came to Baghdad in al-Mansur's days and was attached to him and to al-Mahdi after him, generous to them and favored by them; between him and Khalid ibn Safwan there was a rivalry in oratory. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
