## Summary
Al-Malik al-Zahir Yalbay al-Mu'ayyadi took the sultanate on the day Khushqadam died — Saturday, 10 Rabi' I 872 — the fourteenth of the Circassian kings and their sons. He was too weak for managing the realm and executing affairs, so the emirs deposed him on Saturday, 7 Jumada I; his sultanate lasted two months less four days. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 315}
