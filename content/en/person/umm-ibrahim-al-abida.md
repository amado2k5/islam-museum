## Summary
Umm Ibrahim al-Abida is among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 600). {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## The afflictions of this world
From Abd al-Mu'min ibn Abd Allah al-Qaysi: a riding animal struck her and broke her leg, and when people came to console her she said, "Were it not for the afflictions of this world, we would arrive in the next one bankrupt." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## At the Jamarat
From Abu Musa al-Shawwa': he was with her on the Hajj, and at the Jamarat she saw people turning to buying and selling; she raised her head to the sky and said that they had turned to the world and left her Beloved, then cried out. He covered her with his garment and let people think she was ill; when she came round he reproached her for drawing attention, and she said: "Idler — if it is He who distributes praise, for whom would one put on a show?" {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.714}

## For young visitors
Umm Ibrahim was a patient woman; when her leg was broken she said that hardships in this world add to our reward in the next.
