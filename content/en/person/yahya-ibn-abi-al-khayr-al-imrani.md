## Overview
Yahya ibn Salim, known as Abu al-Khayr, ibn Asad ibn Yahya, Abu al-Husayn al-Imrani, was a jurist who became the head of the Shafi'is in [[place/yemen|Yemen]]. Al-Zirikli gives his birth in 489 AH, 1096 CE, and his death in 558 AH, 1163 CE, at [[place/dhi-as-sufal|Dhu Sufal]] in Yemen {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 146}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote several works {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 146}. One is "al-Bayan" on Shafi'i substantive law, a manuscript of nine volumes at Dar al-Kutub (no. 25, Shafi'i fiqh) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 146}. Others are "al-Zawa'id", "al-Ahdath", a commentary on al-Ghazali's "al-Wasa'il" and "Gharaib al-Wasit" on al-Ghazali's "al-Wasit", all on substantive law {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 146}. He also wrote "Manaqib al-Imam al-Shafi'i", "al-Intisar", a refutation of the Qadariyya held at Dar al-Kutub in two copies (nos. 818 and 835, kalam), an abridgement of the "Ihya", and "Maqasid al-Luma'" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 146}.
