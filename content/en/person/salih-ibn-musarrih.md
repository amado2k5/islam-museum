## Overview
Salih ibn Musarrih al-Tamimi, an ascetic who rose up in the year 76 AH. He had followers whom he taught the Qur'an in Dara, and in the land of Mosul and al-Jazira {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.216}.
