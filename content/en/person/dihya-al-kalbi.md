Abu al-Fadl Dihya ibn Khalifa ibn Farwa al-Kalbi al-Qudai'i, a distinguished Companion, among the most beautiful of men in face and form in the Arabian Peninsula. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Jibril would often come to the Prophet in Dihya's likeness, because of the splendour of appearance and fine bearing that God had given him. Dihya witnessed the battles with the Messenger of God after Uhud, the Trench and the pledge of Ridwan. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

In the sixth year of the hijra, the Prophet chose him as envoy, carrying his letter to Heraclius, emperor of the Romans. Dihya handed it to the governor of Busra, who was to deliver it to the emperor at Iliya' (Jerusalem). Heraclius read the letter and talked with Dihya cordially. Dihya witnessed the battle of Yarmuk, settled in Damascus, lived in al-Mazza, and died during the caliphate of Mu'awiya. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## The raid on him on the way back
Ibn Ishaq relates that Dihya returned from Caesar, who had rewarded and clothed him. When he alighted in Wadi Shinar, al-Hunayd ibn Awis and his son, of Banu al-Dubayb, raided him and took what he had. A group from al-Dubayb recovered it for him. He came to the Prophet, told him, and asked him to avenge al-Hunayd and his son. This became [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|Zayd ibn Haritha's expedition against Judham]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261}.
