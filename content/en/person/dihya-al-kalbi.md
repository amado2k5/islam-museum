Abu al-Fadl Dihya ibn Khalifa ibn Farwa al-Kalbi al-Quda'i, a notable Companion, among the most handsome of face and form in the Arabian Peninsula. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Jibril would often come to the Prophet in his likeness, for the beauty of form and fine bearing God had singled him out with; he witnessed with the Messenger of God the events after Uhud, the Trench and the Ridwan pledge. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

In year six of the hijra the Prophet chose him as ambassador carrying his letter to Heraclius, emperor of the Romans; he delivered it to the lord of Busra who conveyed it to the emperor at Iliya' (Bayt al-Maqdis). Heraclius read the letter and negotiated with Dihya graciously; Dihya witnessed the Yarmuk, settled in Damascus and dwelt at al-Mazza until he died in Mu'awiya's caliphate. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
