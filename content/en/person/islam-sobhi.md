## Summary
Islam Sobhi is an Egyptian Quran reciter born in 1998, known to audiences through recitations published on his official YouTube channel, which made him one of the most widely heard young reciters on digital platforms. He recites in the Hafs 'an Asim narration, and the syndicated biography of his podcast feed describes him as a medical student. {cite:tunein-islam-sobhi-podcast} {cite:youtube-islam-sobhi}

## Upbringing and education
An Arabic web biography reports that Islam Sobhi was born in Monufia Governorate in Egypt's Delta, specifically Shebin El-Kom, in 1998, that he grew up in a religious environment and attended Quran memorisation centres from childhood. The podcast feed biography describes him as a medical student who devoted himself to mastering tajwid from a young age out of love for recitation. {cite:maal7ul-islam-sobhi} {cite:tunein-islam-sobhi-podcast}

## Reach across platforms
Islam Sobhi began by publishing his recitations on YouTube; his clips then spread across Facebook, Telegram and Instagram according to his Arabic biography, with recitations of surahs such as Ar-Rahman, Al-Waqi'ah and Al-Mulk reaching millions of views, and his clips are widely reused in religious videos. His recitations also reach listeners through podcast feeds and listening platforms such as Anghami, and his video descriptions link his official accounts on those platforms. {cite:maal7ul-islam-sobhi} {cite:youtube-islam-sobhi} {cite:tunein-islam-sobhi-podcast}

The founder's channel harvest recorded 8.19 million subscribers for his official YouTube channel (@islam_sobhi), a platform figure dated to the check day (24 October 2026), not a fixed fact. The podcast biography describes his voice as melodious, heartfelt and gentle yet powerful, says he is especially popular among the younger generation, and calls his recitations a staple of Ramadan and other occasions. {cite:youtube-islam-sobhi} {cite:tunein-islam-sobhi-podcast}
