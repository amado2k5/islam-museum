# Shehu Shagari

Shehu Usman Aliyu Shagari (25 February 1925 – 28 December 2018) was a Nigerian politician from the town of Shagari in Sokoto State. He worked briefly as a teacher before entering politics in 1951, was elected to the House of Representatives in 1954, and held a succession of federal cabinet posts between 1958 and 1975 {cite:wikipedia-shehu-shagari-en} {cite:wikidata-shehu-shagari}.

## President of the Second Republic

When the military head of state Olusegun Obasanjo handed power to a civilian government in 1979, Shagari became Nigeria's first democratically elected president. He was re-elected in 1983, but his second term was cut short within months by the coup of 31 December 1983 {cite:wikipedia-shehu-shagari-en} {cite:wikipedia-shehu-shagari-ar}. He died in Abuja on 28 December 2018, aged 93 {cite:wikipedia-shehu-shagari-en} {cite:wikidata-shehu-shagari}.
