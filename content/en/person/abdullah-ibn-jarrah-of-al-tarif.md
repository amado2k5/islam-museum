## Overview
He is one of the party of [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] whom [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] ordered, with the [[group/al-jafatim-sanaa|Jafatim]], to go to a place called al-Sirr and lie in ambush for al-Hadi, telling them he was marching to fight him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} The name is read as printed. See [[event/abu-al-atahiya-writes-to-al-hadi-and-sends-the-jafatim-and-al-tarif-to-an-ambush-at-al-sirr-288]].
