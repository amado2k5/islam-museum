## Overview
Sa'id ibn Abi Aruba. Abu Aruba's name was Mihran {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The report
He was part of a delegation from the people of Basra to al-Mansur concerning the matter of the Subaytiyya. They brought a map of al-Bataiha {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
