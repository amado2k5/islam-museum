## Summary
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn al-Abbas al-Baghdadi al-Mustamli al-Warraq was a hadith transmitter whose name is paired with [[person/ibn-hayyuya-al-khazzaz|Abu Umar ibn Hayyuya]] in the transmission of Ibn al-Mubarak's [[work/kitab-al-zuhd-ibn-al-mubarak|Kitab al-Zuhd]] from [[person/yahya-ibn-muhammad-ibn-saaid|Ibn Sa'id]]. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 2} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 101}

## Life
[[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] quotes Ibn al-Imad: his father took care of his education and had him hear from al-Hasan ibn al-Tayyib al-Balkhi, Umar ibn Abi Ghaylan and their generation; he lived eighty-five years and was a trustworthy hadith man; Ibn al-Imad lists him under the year 378, and Ibn Hajar names him in Lisan al-Mizan. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 101}

## His link to Kitab al-Zuhd
Many reports in the copy open with the narrator's words "Abu Umar ibn Hayyuya and Abu Bakr al-Warraq informed you: Yahya told us", so he is one of the two students of Ibn Sa'id who transmit the book; Ahmad Farid says the two transmit together up to report no. 594, after which one is alone. {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-azami-scan p. 2} {cite:zuhd-ibn-mubarak-farid-1995-v1 p. 57}

## For young visitors
Abu Bakr al-Warraq wrote down hadith and copied books in Baghdad, and he helped pass on Kitab al-Zuhd.

## Notes for researchers
- A "mustamli" is the person who repeats aloud to the audience what the shaykh says in hadith sessions.
