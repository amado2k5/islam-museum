## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, son of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]], who was an agent of Yazid ibn al-Walid. Al-Baladhuri relates that the people of Basra complained to him of the brackishness of their water, so he dug the Nahr Ibn Umar at Basra, and said that were its cost to reach the land tax of Iraq he would spend it on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
