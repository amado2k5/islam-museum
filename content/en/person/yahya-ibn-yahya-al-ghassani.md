## Overview
Yahya ibn Yahya al-Ghassani, mufti of the people of Damascus in his time. He died in the year 135 AH according to al-Shirazi, and after him the fatwa in Syria was confirmed on the school of al-Awza'i and Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}.

He lived in [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. The year of death needs an additional source.
