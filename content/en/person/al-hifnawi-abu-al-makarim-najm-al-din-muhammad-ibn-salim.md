## Overview
Abu al-Makarim Najm al-Din Muhammad ibn Salim ibn Ahmad al-Misri al-Shafi'i al-Khalwati, a Shafi'i jurist of the Khalwati order. He is attributed to Hafna in al-Sharqiyya in Egypt. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.81}

## His life
He was born in 1101 AH (1690 CE) and died in 1181 AH (1767 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.81}
