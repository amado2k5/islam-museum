## Overview
Abu al-Tayyib Sahl ibn Muhammad al-Su'luki al-Hanafi, of the Banu Hanifa. He studied jurisprudence under his father [[person/abu-sahl-al-suluki|Abu Sahl]]. He was a jurist and man of letters who combined the leadership of religion and of the world, and the jurists of Nishapur took from him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}

Al-Shirazi does not mention his death.
