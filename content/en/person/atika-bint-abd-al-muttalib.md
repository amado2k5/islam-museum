## Summary
[[person/atika-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Atika]] bint [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]], aunt of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]; she was the wife of Abu Umayya ibn al-Mughira, father of [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]] the Prophet's wife, and bore him Abdullah and Qurayba among others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1921}.

## The dream before Badr
Ibn Ishaq relates in the Sira that Atika saw, three nights before Damdam ibn Amr arrived with news of Abu Sufyan, a man on a camel who stood at al-Abtah crying: "come forth, house of treachery, to your battle-deaths in three!" And in her dream he took a rock, hurled it from the mountaintop and it shattered — no house nor dwelling of Quraysh remained but some of it entered {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1921}. In the story is Abu Jahl's rebuke of al-Abbas; then Damdam came rousing Quraysh, and they marched out to [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1921}.
