## Overview
Ziyad al-Qasir al-Khuza'i, killed by Yazid ibn Huzayl, who had been sent by Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim. It is reported that he struck him on the head with a sword when he sought an opening, and he lived seven days and then died {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
