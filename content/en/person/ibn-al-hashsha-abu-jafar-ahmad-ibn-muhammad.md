## Overview
Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hashsha, a physician who lived in Tunis in the first half of the seventh century AH. He died in 647 AH (1249 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
