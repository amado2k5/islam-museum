## Overview
Abu al-Walid Musa ibn Abi al-Jarud al-Makki, a jurist whom al-Shirazi counts among the Meccan companions of [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]]. Al-Shirazi says that he transmitted hadith and the *Amali* and other works from him, and that he gave legal opinions in Mecca according to the school of al-Shafi'i. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 53}

Al-Shirazi does not give the year of his death, which needs an additional source.
