## Overview
Abu al-Khasib Marzuq, client of al-Mansur. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur ordered him to build for him the palace known as the Palace of Abu al-Khasib on an old foundation. It is said that he built it for himself, and that al-Mansur used to visit him there. The river of Abu al-Khasib in Basra is attributed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.404}
