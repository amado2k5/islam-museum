## Summary

Sultan Muzaffar Shah Ahmad ibn Mahmud Shah, ruler of Gujarat. He was just, learned, and a lover of scholars, with fine handwriting. He wrote out a number of copies of the Qur'an with his own hand, and sent one of them to the Holy City of Medina. He died while in prostration in prayer {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 577}.

## The assembly of Ba-Harq

It appears from Ibn al-Imad that the scholar Ba-Harq came to visit him, and because of him he wrote a biography of the Prophet. He remained honoured and esteemed by the sultan until he died {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 577}.
