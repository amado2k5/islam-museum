## Summary
Mulayka bint al-Munkadir was a devout woman of [[place/medina|Medina]] whom [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] places first among the known devout women of Medina in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]]. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370}

## In Sifat al-Safwa
Through Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Marwazi he reports from [[person/malik-ibn-dinar-al-khattat|Malik ibn Dinar]] that he saw a woman in the Hijr asking her Lord to grant her of His kindness what would free her from the kindness of anyone else; he and [[person/ayyub-al-sakhtiyani|Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani]] went to her, and she complained to them of her heart and her desires. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370}
When Ayyub suggested that a husband might help her, she said she would not want one even if he were Malik ibn Dinar or Ayyub; when they told her who they were she said, "Fie! I thought remembrance of God would keep you from talking with women", and turned back to her prayer; asking about her, they were told: this is Mulayka bint al-Munkadir. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370}
He also reports from Abu Khalid al-Barrad that they spoke to the daughter of al-Munkadir about lightening some of her worship, and she said: "Leave me to hasten before my record is rolled up." {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.370}
