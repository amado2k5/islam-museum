Abu Abdullah Hudhayfa ibn Hasl (al-Yaman) al-Absi, an eminent Companion and one of the great early converts, famed as "the keeper of the Messenger of God's secret" in knowing the hypocrites' names and the trials. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He witnessed the Trench campaign with the Prophet; on the night of the howling wind, bitter cold and deep darkness the Prophet singled him out to slip into the Confederates' camp and bring him news of them, and prayed for his protection from the cold. Hudhayfa went until he entered Quraysh's camp, sat beside Abu Sufyan, and heard him ordering the people to depart for the severity of the wind and the horses' failing — then returned unharmed, touched by neither cold nor terror, and told the Prophet of the Confederates' flight. {cite:sahih-muslim}

He witnessed the conquests of Iraq and Persia and governed al-Mada'in for Umar ibn al-Khattab; he was famed for scrupulousness and asceticism, and died in al-Mada'in a few days after Uthman's killing. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures on Sharh Kitab al-Fawa'id

The Sheikh says that Hudhayfa was the keeper of the Prophet's secret, and that the Prophet told him the names of the hypocrites in Medina. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:24:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy says that Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman was the Prophet's keeper of secrets and the only Companion who knew the names of the hypocrites, and that when Umar became caliph he would watch whether Hudhayfa attended a person's funeral, and would not pray over it if he did not, and that he asked Hudhayfa whether he himself was one of them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:02:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
In al-Munjiyat the Sheikh says Hudhayfa held the names of seventeen hypocrites in Medina, and that after praying over Abu Bakr Umar went to Hudhayfa to ask whether he was one of them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:11:30} (needs_source: the number seventeen is the speaker's and not checked against another source.)

## In Mosaad Anwar's series 'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise'
In episode 17 of [[work/the-ten-promised-paradise-mosaad-anwar|'Those Given Glad Tidings of Paradise']], [[person/mosaad-anwar|Mosaad Anwar]] introduces Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman as a senior Companion born in Medina and of Meccan origin, who with his father and his mother were Companions, who was given the glad tidings of Paradise and who knew every trial to come until the Day of Resurrection. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:01:30} He says that his father al-Yaman, whose name was Husayl ibn Jabir, was a Meccan of the tribe of Abs who killed a man of his own people in a quarrel, fled to Medina and became an ally of the Banu Abd al-Ashhal, and he explains the name "al-Yaman" by his alliance with the Yemenis, that is the Ansar, to whom he came to be attached. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:03:00} Al-Yaman married al-Rabab bint Ka'b al-Ashhaliyya and had sons and daughters by her, Hudhayfa among the eldest. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:03:30} He relates that when al-Yaman heard of the Prophet's call in Mecca he never doubted his truthfulness, knowing him as "the truthful, the trustworthy", travelled to Mecca, perhaps in secret, embraced Islam, then returned and had his family embrace it; Hudhayfa longed to pledge to the Prophet with his own hand, so his father took him to Mecca, where he embraced Islam in the Prophet's presence, and the two returned to Medina. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:04:30} He describes the people of Medina waiting every day at [[place/thaniyyat-al-wada|Thaniyyat al-Wada']] for the Prophet to arrive and their joy at his coming, Hudhayfa among them. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:05:00}

He says that the Prophet knew the worth of men, saw trustworthiness and good character in Hudhayfa, entrusted him alone with the names of the hypocrites and charged him with watching their movements to ward off their danger. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:06:00} He reports from [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] in the Siyar that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] adjured Hudhayfa by God to say whether the Prophet had named him among the hypocrites; Hudhayfa said no, and that he would clear no one else after him. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:08:00} He relates that Umar, as caliph, asked him whether any of his governors was a hypocrite; Hudhayfa said yes, one, and refused to name him, and before long Umar dismissed that man as if he had been guided to him; and Umar would not pray over a dead person until he had asked whether Hudhayfa was present, and if he was not, Umar did not pray over the body. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:09:30} He relates from [[work/sahih-muslim|Sahih Muslim]] that Hudhayfa swore he was the most knowledgeable of people about every trial to come between him and the Hour, and that the Prophet had taught him this, and he quotes Hudhayfa's saying that people asked the Prophet about good while he asked him about evil for fear it would overtake him, which is why his reports are so many in the books on trials and great battles. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:11:00} He relates that Hudhayfa's mother rebuked him for not having seen the Prophet for two days, so he went, prayed the sunset and night prayers with him and followed him, and saw a figure meet him; the Prophet told him it was an angel who had never come down to earth before that night, who had sought his Lord's leave to greet him and give him the glad tidings that [[person/fatima|Fatima]] is the mistress of the women of Paradise and [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan]] and [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]] the lords of its youth; the Prophet then prayed for forgiveness for Hudhayfa and his mother; he attributes the report to [[person/al-nasai|al-Nasa'i]] in Fada'il al-Sahaba and says Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut graded it hasan. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:12:00}

He says that Hudhayfa took part in every campaign except [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], because the unbelievers of Quraysh had seized him and his father and made them pledge not to fight, and the Prophet told them to keep their word. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:18:30} In his account al-Yaman, at eighty, went out on the day of [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]] with Thabit ibn Waqsh; Thabit was killed by the polytheists and al-Yaman by mistake at the hands of Muslims; Hudhayfa forgave, and the Prophet paid him the blood money, which he gave away to poor Muslims. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:19:00} He places Hudhayfa's glad tidings of Paradise at [[event/battle-of-the-trench|the Battle of the Confederates]], when, as he says, more than ten thousand gathered under [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] together with Jews who had broken their covenant and with hypocrites; [[person/salman-al-farisi|Salman]] advised digging the trench, which astonished the polytheists as a method unknown to the Arabs; hunger grew so hard that the Prophet tied two stones over his belly; then God sent the wind. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:20:30} He relates that the Prophet asked for a man to bring him news of the enemy and be his companion in Paradise, and no one rose for the cold, the fear and the hunger until he named Hudhayfa, who rose; when Abu Sufyan ordered every man to identify his neighbour lest a Muslim be among them, Hudhayfa was quick to ask his neighbour his name first, then heard Abu Sufyan announce the withdrawal after the Jews had abandoned them and the horses and camels had perished. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:22:00} His entry into the Confederates' camp and his hearing Abu Sufyan order the departure agree with what this record relates at its opening from Sahih Muslim. {cite:sahih-muslim}

He says that Hudhayfa fought at [[event/battle-of-al-qadisiyya|al-Qadisiyya]], and that the Muslims settled at [[place/al-madain|al-Mada'in]], whose air did not suit them so that they fell ill, and Umar wrote to [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd]] to move them out; Sa'd sent Hudhayfa and another man to find a site for a city, and Hudhayfa chose the land of [[place/kufa|Kufa]] because he sensed healthfulness in it, so Kufa was laid out on his word and the sick recovered after the move. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:16:00} In the time of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] he campaigned in [[place/armenia|Armenia]] and [[place/azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] with the people of Iraq, was alarmed by people's disagreement over recitation, and came to Uthman warning him lest they differ as the Jews and Christians differed over their scriptures. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:16:30} He says that Uthman consulted the senior Companions, who agreed on [[event/uthman-standardizes-mushaf|uniting the people on a single mushaf]], and formed a committee headed by [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]] which copied from the mushaf of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] kept in the house of [[person/hafsa|Hafsa]]; a copy was sent to every region and the original remained in Medina and was called "al-Mushaf al-Imam". {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:17:00} He relates that Umar appointed him over al-Mada'in, and the people who came out to meet him found him riding a donkey with a loaf in his hand, asking them for nothing but his food and his donkey's fodder; when he returned years later Umar hid on the road to see whether he had changed, found him as he had been, embraced him and told him: you are my brother and I am your brother. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:24:00} He says Hudhayfa's last words were that he loved poverty over wealth, humility over honour and death over life. {cite:assabile-mosaad-anwar the-ten-promised-paradise/ep17 00:24:00}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ahl al-Janna'
In the eleventh episode of his programme [[work/ahl-al-jannah|'Ahl al-Janna']], on the faithful and the traitor, [[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] relates that when Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman emigrated from Mecca to Medina the polytheists let him go only on condition that he would not fight them with the Prophet; when the Battle of Badr came he wanted to go out, and the Prophet held him back to keep their covenant, though, as he says, the Muslims were three hundred and the polytheists a thousand. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep11 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep11 00:05:00} He makes it an example that faithfulness comes first even when it has a price. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep11 00:05:00}
