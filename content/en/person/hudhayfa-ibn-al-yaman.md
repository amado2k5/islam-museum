Abu Abdullah Hudhayfa ibn Hasl (al-Yaman) al-Absi, an eminent Companion and one of the great early converts, famed as "the keeper of the Messenger of God's secret" in knowing the hypocrites' names and the trials. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He witnessed the Trench campaign with the Prophet; on the night of the howling wind, bitter cold and deep darkness the Prophet singled him out to slip into the Confederates' camp and bring him news of them, and prayed for his protection from the cold. Hudhayfa went until he entered Quraysh's camp, sat beside Abu Sufyan, and heard him ordering the people to depart for the severity of the wind and the horses' failing — then returned unharmed, touched by neither cold nor terror, and told the Prophet of the Confederates' flight. {cite:sahih-muslim}

He witnessed the conquests of Iraq and Persia and governed al-Mada'in for Umar ibn al-Khattab; he was famed for scrupulousness and asceticism, and died in al-Mada'in a few days after Uthman's killing. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures on Sharh Kitab al-Fawa'id

The Sheikh says that Hudhayfa was the keeper of the Prophet's secret, and that the Prophet told him the names of the hypocrites in Medina. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:24:00}
