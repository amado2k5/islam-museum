## Overview
Bishara the Armenian, chancery katib and mawla of Shibl al-Dawla al-Mu'azzami (d. 654); he wrote a fine, small hand, and his descendants are called to the oversight of the descendants and of the Shibliyya khanqah {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 273}.
