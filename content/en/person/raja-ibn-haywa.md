## Overview
Raja ibn Haywa al-Kindi, adviser to [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]]. al-Suyuti reports that he advised Sulayman to name [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] as his successor, and after him Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, and that the document should be written and sealed and the community called to pledge allegiance to it {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.371-372}.

al-Sallabi says that Sulayman wrote the document during his illness at Dabiq, and that Raja undertook to take the pledge {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.155-157}. He refused to tell Hisham what the document contained {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.184-186}. al-Mas'udi relates that when Sulayman was dying at Marj Dabiq, he summoned Raja, together with al-Zuhri and Makhul; they wrote his will and had them witness it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.172}.

al-Sallabi describes him as held in high esteem by both Sulayman and Umar {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.278}.
