## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Yusuf ibn Ayyub al-Hamadhani, an ascetic jurist from Buzanjird, a village of [[place/hamadan|Hamadan]]. He came to Baghdad in his youth and attached himself to [[person/abu-ishaq-al-shirazi|Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi]], and studied under him until he excelled in the principles of law and in disputed points. Then he turned to asceticism, worship and striving {cite:wafayat-ayan p.78-79}.

## Preaching
He came to Baghdad in the year 515 AH, and held a session of preaching at the Nizamiyya madrasa, and met with great acceptance. He settled in Marw and lived there, and a group of those who had withdrawn to God gathered in his hospice. Ibn al-Sam'ani said that the education of the sincere disciples ended with him {cite:wafayat-ayan p.78-79}.

## Death
His death overtook him at Bamiyan, between Herat and Baghshur, in Rabi' al-Awwal of the year 535 AH. He was buried, and then moved to [[place/merv|Marw]] {cite:wafayat-ayan p.79-80}.
