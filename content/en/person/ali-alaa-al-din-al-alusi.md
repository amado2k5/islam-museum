## Overview
Ali ibn Nu'man ibn Mahmud al-Alusi, titled Ala al-Din, was a learned judge from Baghdad, where he died; al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1277 AH, 1861 CE, and his death as 1340 AH, 1921 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 29}.

## Life
He graduated from the school of judges in Istanbul, held judgeships in several towns, and was elected a deputy for Baghdad in the Ottoman period {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 29}. He was appointed judge of Baghdad in 1335 AH and was struck by paralysis in 1338 AH, which led some of his biographers to suppose he died that year; al-Zirikli says his death was in Baghdad, in 1340 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 29}.

## Works
He wrote a book of biographies of later scholars, al-Durr al-Muntathir fi Rijal al-Qarn al-Thani Ashar wa-l-Thalith Ashar (printed), and copied many books and treatises in his own hand {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 29}. His scattered poems were gathered by al-Athari into a manuscript diwan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 29}.
