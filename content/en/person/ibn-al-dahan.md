## Summary
Abu Muhammad Sa'id b. al-Mubarak b. 'Ali b. 'Abdallah, known as Ibn al-Dahan the Baghdadi grammarian; he heard hadith from Abu'l-Qasim Hibat Allah b. al-Husayn and Abu Ghalib Ahmad b. al-Hasan b. al-Minhal — the imam of his age in grammar. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Works
His works: a commentary on *al-Idah wa'l-takmil* in forty-three volumes, the *Fusul al-kubra* and *Fusul al-sughra*, his commentary on Ibn Jinni's *al-Luma'* called *al-Ghurra*, *al-'Arud*, *al-Durus fi'l-nahw*, *al-Risala al-Sa'idiyya* on kalam principles, and *Zad al-ma'askar wa-zahr al-riyad* in seven volumes. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
