## Overview
Duqaq ibn Tutush ibn Alp Arslan ruled Damascus after his father [[person/tutush|Tutush]] was killed in 488 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Ruler of Damascus
He was in the citadel of Damascus when his father was killed. The eunuch Sawtakin summoned him to rule, so he set out and reached Damascus before his brother [[person/ridwan-ibn-tutush|Ridwan]] could catch him. Bagisyan, lord of Antioch, advised him to act independently of his brother in Damascus, and he did so. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He appointed al-Khwarazmi as his vizier and gave him authority over his state. He died in 497 AH (around 1104 CE, a derived conversion, the date following the report). {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

After his death, his atabeg Tughtakin took the rule for himself and proclaimed his brother Tutush as ruler; Tutush was a youth who had just reached adolescence. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
