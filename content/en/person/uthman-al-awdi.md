## Overview
Uthman al-Awdi, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in the report on Azerbaijan. He tried to overpower Murra ibn Ali al-Radini at Sisar and could not, and Murra overpowered him in what he held of Azerbaijan, or most of it {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.434-439}.
