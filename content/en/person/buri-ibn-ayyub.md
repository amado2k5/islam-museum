## Summary
Taj al-Muluk Abu Sa'id Buri b. Ayyub b. Shadhi b. Marwan, styled Majd al-Din; brother of Sultan Salah al-Din and youngest of his father's sons, a man of merit with a poetry diwan of wheat and chaff. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Death
He died of a wound — a spear-thrust to the knee on the day the army camped against Aleppo when his brother Sultan Salah al-Din besieged it; he had been struck on the sixteenth of Muharram. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
