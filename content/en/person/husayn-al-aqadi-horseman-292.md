## Overview
"Husayn al-Aqadi" is among the horsemen who stayed with al-Hadi when his companions were routed in the Tihama fight {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The book gives this man's lineage, country and what became of him after the battle nowhere beyond his name in the list that names the horsemen. His name begins at the foot of p. 296 and its second part, "al-Aqadi", opens p. 297; it was read on the page image as vocalised al-Aqadi. He appears in [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|the event that lists them]].
