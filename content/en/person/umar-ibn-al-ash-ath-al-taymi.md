## Overview
Umar ibn al-Ash'ath ibn Lajr ibn Hudayr al-Taymi, a scholar of the Umayyad period, who died around the year 105 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.771}.

## Lineage
The encyclopedia notes that he is ascribed to his grandfather Lajr, and not to his father al-Ash'ath {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.771}.
