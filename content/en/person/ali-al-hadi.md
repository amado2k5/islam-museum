## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Musa ibn Ja'far, known as Ali al-Hadi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.480}

## His arrest
Al-Mas'udi relates that he was reported to al-Mutawakkil, and it was said that there were weapons and books in his house. They attacked his house at night and found him alone in a room wearing a rough woollen garment, with nothing in the room but sand and pebbles, chanting verses of promise and threat. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.480}

He was brought to al-Mutawakkil, who honoured him and seated him beside himself. He asked him what the sons of his father said about al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, and his answer led al-Mutawakkil to order him a hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.480}

## His death
Al-Mas'udi says that he died on Monday, four nights before the end of Jumada al-Akhira 254 AH, aged forty, and was buried in his house in Samarra. He relates that Yahya ibn Harthama had him brought from Medina, and that they searched his house and found in it only a copy of the Qur'an and a supplication. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.549-550}
