## Overview
'Ali b. al-Jawzi Abu'l-Hasan, son of the polymath Jamal al-Din Ibn al-Jawzi; a copyist who transcribed much for wages and a sociable man-about-town, transmitting from Ibn al-Batti, Abu Zur'a and a company; he died in Ramadan 630 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 137}.
