## Overview
Ahmad b. Muhsin, the Shafi'i jurist of Baalbek; he came to Damascus, read fiqh under Zayn al-Din 'Abd al-Rahim b. Ghanim and heard the hafiz al-Qasim al-Dimyati — a debater of much speech who would argue by what does not prove, in the signifying of the great; per the source he was accused of sins and false testimony which the sultan established on him. Al-Subki said: "none like him passed me among the Shafi'is." {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 777}
