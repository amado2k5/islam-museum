## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Sahl, a figure who appears in the reports on the caliph [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Ma'mun ordered the land-tax of Fars and the districts of al-Ahwaz to be brought to him in the year of his marriage to Buran. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.424}

The year of that, and his post and a full biography, are not recorded in the data and need an additional source.
