## Summary
Tawwaf ibn Ghallaq was one of the leaders of the rebels ([[group/khawarij|khawarij]]) in [[place/basra|Basra]], according to his entry in the first edition of al-A'lam. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52} Al-Zirikli describes him as brave, devout and scrupulous - al-Zirikli's own characterisation. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}

## Rising and death
Al-Zirikli says he rose against [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] with seventy men of the Abd al-Qays; Ubayd Allah sent a force against him, which Tawwaf defeated, and he entered Basra; its people fought him together with the troops, most of his men were killed, and he was killed and crucified. {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 p. 52}
