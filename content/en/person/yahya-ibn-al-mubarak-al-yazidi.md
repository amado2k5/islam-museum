## Overview

Abu Muhammad Yahya ibn al-Mubarak ibn al-Mughira al-Yazidi was a companion of [[person/abu-amr-ibn-al-ala|Abu 'Amr ibn al-'Ala']] and transmitter of his reading. He died in 202 AH aged seventy-four, or by another report beyond ninety. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 144}

## His learning

Ibn al-Jazari calls him reliable, learned, eloquent and an imam in the dialects and letters, so that it was said he dictated ten thousand leaves from memory from Abu 'Amr alone, apart from what he took from al-Khalil and others. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 144} From him [[person/abu-umar-al-duri|al-Duri]] and [[person/abu-shuayb-al-susi|al-Susi]] took Abu 'Amr's reading. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 142} The museum has a separate record for a later al-Yazidi, [[person/al-yazidi-muhammad|Muhammad ibn al-'Abbas al-Yazidi]].
