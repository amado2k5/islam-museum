## Overview
Al-Hakam ibn Nuhayk al-Hujaymi, a governor of al-Hajjaj. Al-Baladhuri says that al-Hajjaj appointed him over Fars and then over Kirman, and that he built the mosque of Arjan and its governor's house. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
