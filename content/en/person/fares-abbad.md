# Fares Abbad

## Summary

Fares Abd Rabbuh Muhammad Abbad (born 11 April 1980) is a Yemeni reciter and imam whose recitation is broadcast on Al-Majd Quran channel; he leads prayers at the al-Shab Abdullah ibn Badr al-Suwaidan Mosque in the Doha district of Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province. {cite:wikipedia-fares-abbad-ar} {cite:wikidata-fares-abbad}

## Early life

In his own account, quoted by the article, he memorised the Qur'an as a boy in the memorisation schools of several mosques, until the charitable society for Qur'an memorisation opened model circles at the Bilal ibn Rabah Mosque on Hail Street in [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]], first taught by Fadl Abdullah Murad, where he completed the Qur'an. {cite:wikipedia-fares-abbad-ar}

## Career

He led the tarawih at the Bilal ibn Rabah Mosque from 1994 for seven years and recorded the complete Qur'an in Yemen; Al-Qadisiyya Recordings in Dammam took the rights and spread it, followed by Al-Majd channel and radio stations. In 2006 he was asked to come to Saudi Arabia as imam of the Ali ibn Abi Talib Mosque in Dhahran. {cite:wikipedia-fares-abbad-ar}

Besides the complete Qur'an he has recorded the ruqya, the morning and evening adhkar, al-Qahtani's Nuniyya (686 lines) and Ibn al-Qayyim's prologue on the description of Paradise. He mentions a new rendering of 'Hisn al-Muslim' and a versified teaching text prepared with a poet, which he describes as a scholarly text, not a nasheed. {cite:wikipedia-fares-abbad-ar}
