# Asmaa Saeed

## Summary
Asmaa Saeed is a mental-health consultant who runs the YouTube channel "Nafs Mutma'inna Academy" (The Tranquil Soul Academy, @AsmaaSaiedChannel), presenting videos on mental health, relationships and tranquillity framed by faith and trust in God. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed}

The channel states its mission as reviving hearts through knowing God, and her official website describes her method as combining mental health with deepening the connection with God, toward the tranquil soul. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed} {cite:website-asmaa-saeed}

## Career
Saeed describes herself on her channel as a mental-health consultant certified by the Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and a member of the Arab American board, and her website adds that she is a practitioner of TLT and NLP techniques with more than ten years of experience. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed} {cite:website-asmaa-saeed}

She publishes her videos on her channel herself, and the videos shown on it ran from a few days before the channel was checked on 26 October 2026 back to about seven years earlier. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed}

Her official website announces 1.47 million subscribers to her YouTube channel and more than 1,400 free videos, figures she publishes herself. {cite:website-asmaa-saeed}

No independent biographical source for her could be verified, so nothing is recorded here about her life beyond her announced work on her channel and website. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed}

## Works
Among her channel's videos is "I challenge you to forget what you memorised with it: secrets of the mind for memorisation and fast recall", its most viewed at about 5.4 million views when checked. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed}

Other videos treat subjects such as overthinking, anxiety, obsessive thoughts, marital relationships and raising children, many of them addressed to women in particular. {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed}

Through her website she offers paid recorded courses, among them "Where Do I Start?", "Money and Happiness", and the intensive four-hour course "From Darkness to Light". {cite:youtube-asmaa-saeed} {cite:website-asmaa-saeed}
