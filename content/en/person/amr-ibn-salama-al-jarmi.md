## Overview
Amr ibn Salama al-Jarmi, Abu Buraid of Basra, who met the Prophet and led his people in prayer for his memory of the Qur'an. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3493}

## Transmission
He narrated from his father; Abu Qilaba al-Jarmi, Asim al-Ahwal and Abu al-Zubayr narrated from him. Ibn Hibban held that he had companionship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3493}
