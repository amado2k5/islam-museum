## Overview
Sarjun ibn Mansur, secretary of [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]]. He used to write the register in Syria in Greek for Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan, and after him Mansur ibn Sarjun wrote it {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.339}.

## Transfer of the register
al-Baladhuri reports that when Sulayman ibn Sa'd had finished transferring the Syrian register from Greek, he brought it to Abd al-Malik, who summoned Sarjun and presented the matter to him. Sarjun was distressed and left in low spirits. He met some Greek secretaries and told them to seek a living from some other trade, since God had cut it off from them {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
