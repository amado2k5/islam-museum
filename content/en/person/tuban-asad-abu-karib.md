## Overview
Tubban As'ad Abu Karib, king of [[group/himyar|Himyar]] in whose time Judaism entered [[place/yemen|Yemen]], according to the account in al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. He went out to fight to Yathrib, embraced Judaism there, and brought two rabbis from the [[group/banu-qurayza|Banu Qurayza]] to Yemen, and Judaism then spread and expanded there {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 6/297-298} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/20-36}.

## His son Dhu Nuwas
When his son Yusuf, [[person/dhu-nuwas|Dhu Nuwas]], succeeded him in Yemen, he attacked the Christians of Najran and invited them to Judaism. When they refused, he dug the trench for them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40}. See [[event/massacre-of-najran|the trench of Najran]] and [[concept/judaism-in-arabia|Judaism in the Arabian Peninsula]].

## For young visitors
Tubban As'ad was an ancient king of Yemen who embraced Judaism when he visited Yathrib, and he took two Jewish scholars back with him to Yemen.
