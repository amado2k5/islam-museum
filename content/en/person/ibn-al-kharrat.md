## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abd al-Haqq ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdallah al-Azdi al-Ishbili, known as Ibn al-Kharrat. *Shadharat al-Dhahab* describes him as a memoriser (hafiz) and one of the leading scholars. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 751}

Among his works are *al-Ahkam al-Kubra* and *al-Ahkam al-Sughra*, *al-Jam' bayna al-Sahihayn* (The Union of the Two Sahihs), *Kitab al-Gharibayn* on language, and *Kitab al-Jam' bayna al-Sunna*. He settled in Bejaia and held its preaching office. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 751}

He died there in Rabi' al-Akhir 582 AH at the age of seventy-one, after an ordeal he suffered at the hands of the state. The source describes him as content and abstinent, known for piety, scrupulousness and adherence to the Sunna. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 751}
