## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Hafs al-Bukhari, Abu Abd Allah, is called by al-Dhahabi the imam, mufti and scholar of Bukhara {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}. He says he studied fiqh under his father and that the people of Bukhara studied fiqh under him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}. He adds that he was among the imams of Islam and the sunna, with works to his name and great renown {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}.

## His life
Al-Dhahabi says he lived to nearly the year 270 {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}. The entry's heading carries a death year of 264 AH, so the two dates stand as printed, without a choice between them {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}. The same page has an entry for a different man, Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Hafs al-Harashi al-Hiri of Nishapur, who died in 263 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3222}.
