## Overview
Sulayman ibn Marthad, of the Banu Sa'd ibn Malik ibn Subay'a ibn Qays ibn Tha'laba ibn Ukaba, of the Marai'd of Rabi'a {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Khurasan
He opposed the treaty of Salm with Ibn Khazim over Khurasan, and they agreed to refer the matter to the judgement of Ibn al-Zubayr. When Ibn al-Zubayr appointed him over Khurasan, he refused to accept it, and said that Ibn al-Zubayr was not a caliph but a man seeking refuge in the Sacred House {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

Ibn Khazim fought him with six thousand men, while Sulayman had fifteen thousand, and his emblem was "O Nasr, draw near". Qays ibn Asim al-Sulami killed him and cut off his head {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
