## Overview
Ilba ibn al-Harith al-Asadi al-Kahili, of the [[group/banu-asad-ibn-khuzayma|Banu Asad]], who led them on the day they rose against Hujr, father of Imru' al-Qays. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.383-384}

## The uprising against Hujr
Ibn al-Athir relates that he charged at Hujr, struck him with a spear and killed him, since Hujr had killed his father. It is said that Hujr was captured and the nephew of Ilba leapt upon him in the same way. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.383-384}

## The departure
He was the one who advised the Banu Asad to move away from the Banu Kinana without informing them, when Imru' al-Qays came with the hosts of Bakr and Taghlib seeking vengeance. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.383-384}
