## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn Sahl, vizier of al-Ma'mun. Al-Ma'mun raised his standing in the year 196 AH and appointed him over the East, and gave him the title "Holder of the Two Commands". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.424}

## In the report
After the battle of al-Rayy, Tahir wrote a letter to him in which he mentioned that the head of Ali ibn Isa was before him and his ring was on his finger. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.376}

Al-Mas'udi relates that he came to dominate al-Ma'mun, to the point of pressing him over a slave-girl he wanted to buy, and that he was then killed. Some people claimed that al-Ma'mun had sent someone to kill him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.399}

This claim is attributed by al-Mas'udi to "some people", and is not established here. The circumstances and date of his killing need an additional source.
