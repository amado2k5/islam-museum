## Summary
Shams al-Din Ahmad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Suja'i al-Shafi'i al-Azhari, from the town of al-Suja'iyya near al-Mahalla in Egypt, died in 1197/1782. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 34} His father was a leading Sufi; he studied with him and with the great shaykhs of his time and taught both in his father's lifetime and after his death. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 34}

## Works
He wrote much on the language sciences, law, jurisprudence, astronomy and arithmetic, including a treatise on the names of the lunar mansions, *Luqtat al-jawahir fi al-khutut wal-dawa'ir*, and *Hidayat uli al-basa'ir wal-absar ila ma'rifat ajza' al-layl wal-nahar*. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 34}
