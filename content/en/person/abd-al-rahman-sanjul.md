## Overview
Abd al-Rahman Sanjul, son of [[person/al-mansur-ibn-abi-amir|al-Mansur ibn Abi Amir]], whose nickname Sanjul means "little Sancho". He took the affair after his brother al-Muzaffar, and took the title al-Nasir li-Din Allah, and it is said al-Ma'mun. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 460-461}

## Succession
He followed the practice of his father and brother of restraining the caliph [[person/hisham-ii-al-muayyad|Hisham]]. Then he asked Hisham to name him as his heir, and the deed was written for him in Rabi al-Awwal 398 AH. Al-Maqqari says that this was the cause of the strife which ended in his capture and killing. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 460-461}
