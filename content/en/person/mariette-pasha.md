## Overview
Mariette Pasha, the French archaeologist Auguste Mariette, appears in the Encyclopaedia of Islam only in passing; it has no article under his name, and this is a short record of what its articles say. The years 1821 and 1881 are those of the Wikidata description.

## The museum
The article on Ismail Pasha lists, among the khedive's cultural foundations, a museum under the direction of the French archaeologist Mariette Pasha, along with a national library, a geographical society, schools and government press improvements. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 205} The article on [[place/cairo|Cairo]] adds that the famous Museum of Egyptology founded by Mariette at Bulaq has occupied its present home in Midan al-Tahrir since 1902. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 465}
