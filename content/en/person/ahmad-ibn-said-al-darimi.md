## Overview
Ahmad ibn Sa'id ibn Sakhr al-Sarakhsi, Abu Ja'far al-Darimi, was a hafiz of hadith; al-Safadi says that the compilers of the Group, except al-Nasa'i, narrated from him, and that al-Tirmidhi also narrated from him through an intermediary {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 241}.

## Works
Al-Safadi calls him one of the great scholars who travelled in search of knowledge and spent freely {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 241}. He held the judgeship of Sarakhs, returned to Nishapur and died there in 253 AH, as al-Safadi records {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 6 p. 241}.
