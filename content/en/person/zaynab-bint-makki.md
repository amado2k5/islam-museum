## Overview
Zaynab bint Makki, the aged devout shaykha Umm Ahmad; she heard from Hanbal, Ibn Tabarzad, Sitt al-Kutaba and a company — and the students crowded to her; she lived ninety-four years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 707}.
