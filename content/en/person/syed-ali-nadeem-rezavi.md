## Overview
Syed Ali Nadeem Rezavi is recorded in the museum as a historian and the author of "Iranian Influence on Medieval Indian Architecture", a chapter in *A Shared Heritage: The Growth of Civilizations in India and Iran*, edited by Irfan Habib and published in New Delhi in 2002 by the Aligarh Historians Society and Tulika. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002}

## The chapter
The chapter deals with the transmission of Iranian and Timurid architectural elements to the architecture of the Delhi Sultanate and the Mughals in India. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002} The museum's scan covers the printed pages 127 to 149, with plans, and the museum has a record of the chapter itself, [[work/iranian-influence-on-medieval-indian-architecture|Iranian Influence on Medieval Indian Architecture]]. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002} The museum's [[building/taj-mahal|Taj Mahal]] page cites it for the placing of the tomb at the river edge of its garden. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002}

## What is not known
Only the chapter, not the whole volume, is in the museum's scan. The sources read give no birth date, place, career or institution for the author, and none is invented here. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002}
