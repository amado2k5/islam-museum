## Summary
Hassan Mohi-ud-Din Qadri — Prof. Dr. Hassan Mohiuddin Qadri — is a Pakistani Islamic scholar and legal researcher, chairman of the Supreme Council of Minhaj-ul-Quran International (MQI). {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile} He is the elder son of the organisation's founder, Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri. {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd} {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd-conferral}

His YouTube channel, @drhassanqadri, counted 104,000 subscribers in vidIQ's update of 5 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact, and the re-verified figure on which this borderline record was built. {cite:vidiq-hassan-mohi-ud-din-qadri} It is his own channel, distinct from his brother Hussain's larger channel, whose figures are not used here. {cite:vidiq-hassan-mohi-ud-din-qadri}

## Family
Minhaj-ul-Quran's own reports introduce him, in their first lines, as the elder son of Shaykh-ul-Islam Dr Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri — the formula appears both in the organisation's report of his 2012 PhD viva and in its report of the degree's conferral. {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd} {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd-conferral}

## Education
He completed his PhD at Arab League University, Cairo, with a viva on 3 November 2012: the thesis, "Constitution of Medina and Modern Constitutional Principles (A Comparative and Analytical Study)", was written in Arabic at around 800 pages under the supervision of the Egyptian constitutional expert Dr Yahya al-Jamal, and the degree was awarded with distinction. {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd} {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd-conferral} His organisational profile adds a Masters in Arabic and Islamic Studies at Minhaj University Lahore (1995–2002), a Bachelor of Laws from Pakistan College of Law, Lahore (2003), and an MSc in Management from Sir John Cass Business School, London City University (2005). {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile}

## Offices and work
He serves as Chairman of the Supreme Council of Minhaj-ul-Quran International — the 2012 report styles the same office President of the Supreme Council — and additionally as Dean of the Faculty of Law and a member of the Board of Governors at Minhaj University Lahore, Patron of Nizam-ul-Madaris Pakistan, and Head of the College of Shariah and Islamic Sciences and Minhaj College for Women in Lahore. {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile} {cite:minhaj-hassan-phd} The profile credits him with more than twenty authored books, among them "The Constitution of Medina & The Concept of the Welfare State", and with speeches at forums including the UN Office at Vienna, the World Economic Forum and UNESCO House in Paris, on Islam, peace, interfaith harmony and counter-extremism. {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile}

His channel carries his bayans and addresses, including a 2025 speech at the United Nations and lectures delivered on his tours. {cite:vidiq-hassan-mohi-ud-din-qadri}

## Positions and faith
This section presents what the sources give, not a verdict of the museum.

His profile lists his areas of expertise as constitutional law, human rights, the classical Islamic sciences, deradicalisation, Sufism and countering violent extremism, among others — research areas, presented as such. {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile} His father's documented school, recorded on his father's own record, is family background and is not transferred to the son; no source read names a school or order as Hassan's own affiliation, so under the museum's two-source rule no faith value is recorded for him. {cite:minhaj-youth-hassan-profile}

## Reach
His reach anchor is his personal channel: @drhassanqadri counted 104,000 subscribers, 8,326,048 total views and 2,247 videos in vidIQ's update of 5 November 2026, on a channel created on 17 October 2009 with its country set to Pakistan. {cite:vidiq-hassan-mohi-ud-din-qadri}
