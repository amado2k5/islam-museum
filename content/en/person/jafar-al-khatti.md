## Overview
Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hasan al-Khatti al-Bahrani al-Abdi al-Adnani, Abu al-Bahr, was the poet of al-Khatt in his day, from the people of Bahrain; he travelled to Persia and settled there until his death in 1028 AH (1619 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.129}

His printed diwan of poetry was famous in his lifetime, and his nisba al-Abdi refers to the tribe of Abd al-Qays. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.129}
