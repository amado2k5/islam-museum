## Overview
Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Hasan al-Khatti al-Bahrani al-'Abdi al-'Adnani, Abu al-Bahr, was the poet of al-Khatt in his time, of Bahrayn. He travelled to Persia and lived there until he died in 1028 AH (1619 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.128}

He left a diwan of verse famous in his lifetime; the nisba al-'Abdi refers to the Banu Abd al-Qays. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.128}
