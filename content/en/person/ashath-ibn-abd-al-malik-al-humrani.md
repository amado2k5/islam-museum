## Overview
Abu Hani al-Ash'ath ibn Abd al-Malik al-Humrani, a jurist among the companions of [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}

## His death and nisba
Al-Shirazi says that he died in 146 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48} According to the editor's footnote, "al-Humrani" is attributed to Humran ibn Aban, and al-Ash'ath was his client. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.48}

## Notes
- The only source is al-Shirazi's "Classes of the Jurists" (edited by Ihsan Abbas) and needs an additional source. The Gregorian conversion is derived.
