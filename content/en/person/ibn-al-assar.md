## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. 'Abd al-Rahim b. al-Hasan b. 'Abd al-Malik, known as Ibn al-'Assar the philologist; he read adab under al-Sharif Abu'l-Sa'adat Ibn al-Shajari and Abu Mansur Ibn al-Jawaliqi, excelled in his art and taught people for a time, then journeyed to Egypt and met Abu Muhammad Ibn Burri and al-Muwaffaq b. al-Khallal, the chancery secretary. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His script
He knew Abu'l-Tayyib al-Mutanabbi's diwan generally and by transmission, copied the rarities of Khurasan, Syria and Egypt, and wrote out in his hand many books of adab and Arab poetry — his style was fine and people competed for his script and bid it high. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
