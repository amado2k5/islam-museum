## Summary
Shams al-Din al-Asfahani, usuli, mutakallim and great scholar who settled in Egypt. He studied at Isfahan while his father was deputy sultan until he surpassed his peers; when the enemy seized Isfahan he moved to Baghdad, taking fiqh from Siraj al-Din al-Hirqili and the rational sciences from Taj al-Din al-Armawi, then went to Rum to Athir al-Din al-Abhari for dialectic and philosophy. He entered Cairo and held the judgeship of Qus as deputy for Qadi Taj al-Din Ibn Bint al-A'azz, administering it well, and taught at the Shafi'i and the Mashhad al-Husayn. He commented on the *Mahsul* and authored *al-Fawa'id fi'l-'ulum al-arba'a* and *Ghayat al-matlub* on logic — it was said Ibn Daqiq al-'Id attended his lesson at Qus. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 710-711}
