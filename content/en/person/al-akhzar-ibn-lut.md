## Summary
A poet of [[group/banu-al-dil|Banu al-Dil]] who boasted in verse that they drove Banu Ka'b from [[place/al-watir|al-Watir]]; [[person/budayl-ibn-warqa|Budayl ibn Umm Asram]] answered him with a poem Ibn Hisham cites from a source other than Ibn Ishaq {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/34}.

## For young visitors
A poet who sang of his people's victory over the water-well; a poet of the other side answered him with a poem saying the victory had not been theirs.
