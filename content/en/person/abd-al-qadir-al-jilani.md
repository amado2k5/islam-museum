## Overview
Muhyi al-Din Abu Muhammad Abd al-Qadir ibn Abi Salih al-Jilani, one of the great shaykhs of Sunni Sufism, who was attributed to Jilan. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Influence
He had a great influence in preaching and guidance. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} He was born in 470 AH (1078 CE) and died in 561 AH (1166 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## In the 'Al-Mubdi'un' programme
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani — founder of the Qadiriyya order — as the model balance of scholarship and faith: 'not all Sufism is false; there is a sound Sunni Sufism and Abd al-Qadir led it' (his corrective to the general image) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:12:30}.
Of his lineage: Hasani through his father — eleven fathers from al-Hasan ibn Ali — and Husayni through his mother, and through his father's mother of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq's line {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:04:30}.
Of his upbringing: born in Gilan near the Caspian on 9 Rabi' II 470 AH, orphaned and ascetic from childhood; he emigrated to Baghdad in 488 AH, his seventy-year-old mother equipping him with forty gold dinars sewn into his clothes and the counsel 'never lie, for the believer does not lie' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:13:00}.
The caravan story: bandits fell on their caravan and when they asked him 'have you anything?' he answered 'yes — forty dinars' and produced them; the bandit chief, shaken, repented at his hands through the blessing of his truthfulness {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:09:30}.
Of his trial in Baghdad: he ate what the rivers threw up, walked barefoot, wore patched rags until nicknamed 'the madman', yet never asked anyone anything nor stood at a minister's door — until he mastered thirteen sciences to the rank of debating the greatest scholars {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:14:30}.
He followed al-Ghazali's method and went beyond it, correcting some of his hadith errors, hearing hadith from the masters {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:15:30}.
After thirty years of taking he sat to preach at al-Makharrimi's school in Shawwal 521 AH, his sessions drawing an estimated seventy thousand so the school was enlarged repeatedly — the speaker explains relaying by voice from hearer to hearer {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:18:00}.
He was Baghdad's mufti ruling on both the Hanbali and Shafi'i schools, no fatwa ever spending a night under his consideration — he wrote the answer on reading it with astonishing precision {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:19:30}.
Of the scholars' testimony: al-Nawawi called him 'shaykh of the noble Shafi'is and Hanbalis of Baghdad, leadership of learning ended with him in his time… there was none like him in his age'; Ibn Taymiyya counted him and al-Junayd 'the true Sufism'; and Abu al-Hasan al-Nadwi gave him a chapter in Rijal al-Fikr wa-l-Da'wa {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:22:30}.
Of his sayings: 'I searched all deeds and found none better than feeding food, nor nobler than good character'; and when chided for his wealth ever departing, 'my palm is perforated — were a thousand dinars to reach me they would not stay a night' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:11:00}.
He closes: the supreme model after the Companions and Followers for balancing sound learning with lofty spirituality, urging scholars to moisten their hearts with true spirituality and Sufis to build on evidence {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep20 00:23:30}.
