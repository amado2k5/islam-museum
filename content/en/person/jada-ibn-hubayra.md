## Overview
Ju'da ibn Hubayra ibn Abi Wahb al-Makhzumi, whose mother was [[person/umm-hani-bint-abi-talib|Umm Hani daughter of Abu Talib]]. Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] sent him to Khurasan when it rebelled, and he did not conquer it; he was not seen to have been disturbed until Ali was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
