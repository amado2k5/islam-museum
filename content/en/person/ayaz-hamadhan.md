## Atabeg of the Seljuk princes
Ayaz, amir and lord of Hamadhan, played an important part in the struggles for the throne between the rival Seljuk princes [[person/barkiyaruq|Barkiyaruk]] and [[person/muhammad-ibn-malikshah|Muhammad I]]. He first took the side of Muhammad, but in 494/1100 went over to Barkiyaruk. After Barkiyaruk's death he became atabeg of Barkiyaruk's son [[person/malikshah|Malikshah]], who was a minor. He could not hold his own against Muhammad, and was treacherously murdered by him in 499/1105. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 829}
