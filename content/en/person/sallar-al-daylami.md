## Overview
Hamza ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Daylami al-Tabaristani, Abu Ya'la, nicknamed Sallar or Salar, was an Imami jurist who lived at Baghdad and died at the village of Khosrowshah near Tabriz in 463 AH (1071 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.277}

He wrote al-Abwab wa-l-Fusul on fiqh and al-Marasim al-'Alawiyya fi-l-Ahkam al-Nabawiyya. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.277}
