## Overview
Burhan al-Din Ibrahim b. al-Mabit, a poet of Cairo; of his verse on coffee: "O you who fault our coffee's blackness — in it is a cure for souls of their ills — do you not see it in its cup, telling the eye's black amid its white" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 688}.
