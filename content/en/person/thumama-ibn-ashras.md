## Overview
Thumama ibn Ashras, a theologian who was a boon-companion of al-Ma'mun. al-Mas'udi reports that a question of jurisprudence arose between him and Yahya ibn Aktham. He said of the Companions of the Messenger of God that they had all erred and overlooked the point of the inference, and Yahya was appalled at his words {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.402-403}.

## In the Fihrist
Ibn al-Nadim calls him Abu Bishr Thumama ibn al-Ashras al-Numayri, and counts him among the leading Mu'tazilite theologians and an eloquent secretary {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.526-527}.

He reports that he reached a high rank with al-Ma'mun, and that al-Ma'mun wanted to make him vizier, but he declined, and advised him to appoint Ahmad ibn Abi Khalid {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.526-527}.

He also reports that before al-Ma'mun he had been with al-Rashid, and that al-Rashid imprisoned him when he turned against the Barmakids, because of his closeness to them. In one report, al-Ma'mun asked him why he stood up for Abu al-Hudhayl but did not stand up for Tahir ibn al-Husayn, and he replied that Abu al-Hudhayl had been his master for thirty years {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.526-527}.
