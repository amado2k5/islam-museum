## Overview
Ali ibn Isa ibn Abi al-Fath al-Irbili, Baha' al-Din, was an ornate prose-writer and one of the poets. He wrote for the governor of Irbil, then served in Baghdad in the chancery; his father had been governor of Irbil. He died in 692 AH (1293 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.318}

His literary works include al-Maqamat al-Arba', Risalat al-Tayf, Kashf al-Ghumma fi Ma'rifat al-A'imma, and Hayat al-Imamayn Zayn al-'Abidin wa-Muhammad al-Baqir. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.318}
