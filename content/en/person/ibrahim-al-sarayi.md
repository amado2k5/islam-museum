## Summary
Ibrahim al-Sarayi the Shafi'i; he came to Cairo and held the shaykhdom of the ribat at the Barziyya, devoted himself to hadith, stayed with Zayn al-Din al-'Iraqi, and acquired fine copies which he carefully corrected and improved; he memorised the Hawi and taught most of it, with goodness and religion; skilled in many crafts; among his pleasantries was that Timur's first emergence fell in the year 'adhab', pointing to his appearance in 773. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 21}
