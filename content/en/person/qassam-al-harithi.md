## Summary
Qassam al-Harithi, of Balghina on Mount Sanbar, began as a shepherd; circumstances carried him until he became leader of the youth-gangs of Damascus, and his partisans grew so numerous that he dominated the city and the deputy had no say beside him. An army marched from Egypt to punish and fight him; his power weakened and he went into hiding. He then asked for safe-conduct, was bound and sent to Egypt in 376, where he was pardoned and his name faded. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 87}
