## Overview
Umayr ibn Dabi al-Burjami, an elder and one of the nobles of the people of Kufa, of the army of al-Muhallab {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.121-122}.

## His report
al-Mas'udi reports that he attacked [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] while he was being killed and broke one of his ribs. When he passed in review before [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]], Anbasa ibn Sa'id and Malik ibn Asma' mentioned this, and al-Hajjaj killed him. After that the people went out to al-Muhallab {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.121-122}.
