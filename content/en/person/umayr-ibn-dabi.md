## Overview
Umayr ibn Dabi, an aged shaykh of the nobles of Kufa, of the Muhallabid faction; he had leapt upon the slain Uthman ibn Affan and broken one of his ribs. When he passed before al-Hajjaj instead of his son, Anbasa ibn Sa'id pointed him out — al-Hajjaj threw his stick at him, saying 'by God, were it not for your old age I would have made you pay for it'.
