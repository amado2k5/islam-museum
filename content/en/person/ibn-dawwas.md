## Overview
Ibn Dawwas, a commander in the state of al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah; al-Hakim feared him and would say to his sister Sitt al-Mulk, "what is this man to me?" — and Sitt al-Mulk arranged matters between them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.302-305}

## Al-Hakim and Sitt al-Mulk
In the Egyptian account, Ibn Dawwas gave her two trusted Maghribi slaves who killed al-Hakim by night on al-Muqattam and carried him to Ibn Dawwas, who bore him to her; she promised him the kingdom and a hundred thousand, then arranged his killing at the hand of Nasim, master of the curtain and the sword — and it was said to the Egyptians, "this is the killer of our lord al-Hakim." {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.302-305}
