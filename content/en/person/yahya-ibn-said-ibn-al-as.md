## Overview
Yahya ibn Sa'id ibn al-As, brother of Amr ibn Sa'id al-Ashdaq. al-Mas'udi reports that when his brother was killed, he came to the door of the house of Abd al-Malik with those who were with him to break it down. He disputed with al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, and al-Walid struck him on the hand with a sword and he was felled. Then they threw the head of Amr among the people, and they dispersed {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.97}.
