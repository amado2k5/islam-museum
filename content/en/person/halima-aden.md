## Summary
Halima Aden is a Somali-American model and activist born in 1997 in Kenya's Kakuma refugee camp, a trailblazer for Muslim representation in fashion; The Muslim 500 lists her among influential figures in arts and culture. {cite:muslim500-halima-aden} {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Rise
She moved to Minnesota as a child, was crowned homecoming queen at her high school in hijab, and in 2016 became the first contestant to wear a hijab throughout — and a burkini in the swimwear round — at the Miss Minnesota USA pageant, reaching the semi-finals. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Modelling career
Signed by IMG Models, she walked for major designers, became the first hijabi model on the covers of Vogue Arabia, British Vogue, Allure and the Sports Illustrated swimsuit issue ⁦(in burkini)⁩, and fronted campaigns worldwide. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Turning point
In 2020 she stepped back from runway modelling, saying the industry's compromises had conflicted with her religious practice — while continuing editorial work on her own terms, designing hijab lines and advocating for refugees as a UNICEF ambassador. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Assessment
She is credited with normalising hijab in global fashion and with an unusually public account of its costs inside the industry; assessments are reported by the museum attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden} {cite:muslim500-halima-aden}


## Runway and campaigns
She walked for Kanye West's Yeezy and high-fashion houses, fronted campaigns for global brands, and appeared on covers including British Vogue and Sports Illustrated's burkini edition — milestones repeatedly cited as firsts for a hijabi model. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## UNICEF
Appointed a UNICEF Ambassador in 2018, she returned to Kakuma — the camp where she was born — to advocate for refugee education, making her case for representation explicitly tied to her refugee story. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Beyond modelling
After stepping back from full-time fashion she pursued acting and advocacy — a role in the drama series alongside refugee-work — and continues to appear at modest-fashion events as a founding figure of the industry she helped create. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden} {cite:muslim500-halima-aden}

## From Kakuma
Born in Kenya's Kakuma refugee camp in 1997 to a Somali family and resettled in Minnesota, she rose from a small-town schoolgirl to international model in three years — a trajectory press coverage made emblematic of refugee potential. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## The Miss Minnesota moment
Her 2016 entry in the Miss Minnesota USA pageant — competing in hijab and burkini — was covered internationally as a milestone for Muslim women's presence in American pageantry. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## Departure and return
Her 2020-21 step back from fashion over religious conscience — and partial return on her own terms — was covered as a landmark in conversations about the industry's accommodation of faith. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden} {cite:muslim500-halima-aden}

## The representation question
Halima Aden's career is inseparable from the question of Muslim women's representation in fashion. Her 2016 Miss Minnesota USA entry in hijab and burkini, her 2017-19 runway and cover milestones — the hijabi model for Yeezy, the burkini Sports Illustrated cover — and her 2020-21 step back over religious conscience all unfolded in public, making her career a running argument about what accommodation of faith in the fashion industry looks like. Her UNICEF ambassadorship and return to Kakuma extended the same project into humanitarian advocacy. {cite:wikipedia-halima-aden}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists her among the most influential Muslims in arts and fashion, citing her role as the first hijabi model to reach international fashion's highest levels and her use of the platform for refugee advocacy. {cite:muslim500-halima-aden}



## Standing in the field

Within modest fashion, Halima Aden stands as the first hijabi model to reach international fashion’s highest levels — a position that explains the scale of public attention the career has attracted. The Muslim 500's listing places Halima Aden among the most influential Muslims of the present generation in this field, reflecting both the size of the audience the work reaches and the extent to which the figure has come to represent it beyond specialist circles. {cite:muslim500-halima-aden}
