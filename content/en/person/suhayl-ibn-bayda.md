## Overview
Suhayl ibn Bayda, a Companion of Banu Abd al-Dar and brother of Sahl; Bayda was their mother. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.629}

## Badr
Ibn Ishaq records that he witnessed Badr. Al-Tabarani transmitted with a sound chain from Abu Ubayda from Abdallah ibn Mas'ud from his father: the Messenger of God said on the day of Badr, "Let none of you slip away except by ransom or a stroke"; I said — except Suhayl ibn Bayda; he said — except Suhayl ibn Bayda, for he had heard him mention Islam. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.629}

## His death
Ibn Ishaq records that he died in year nine. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.629}
