## Overview
Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi, governor of Ifriqiya. Al-Baladhuri relates that Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik appointed him over Ifriqiya after the killing of [[event/killing-of-yazid-ibn-abi-muslim|Yazid ibn Abi Muslim]]. He struck the neck of Abd Allah ibn Musa ibn Nusayr, who was accused of his killing and of stirring the people against him. Then Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik kept him on, and he died in Kairouan in 109 AH. In one copy of the book the name is "Bisr" in place of "Bishr". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.324}

Al-Salabi's book says that he was governor of Ifriqiya at the beginning of Hisham's caliphate, and that before his death he appointed Nafash ibn Qurt al-Kalbi as his deputy. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.485}
