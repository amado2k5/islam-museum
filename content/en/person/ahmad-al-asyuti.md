## Overview
Shihab al-Din Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Khaliq al-Asyuti; he heard his father and 'Abd al-Rahim Ibn al-Qari, who licensed him, and he kept earning by testimony at the mosque of the book-sellers' quarter; he died on 12 Rabi' II 838. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 225}
