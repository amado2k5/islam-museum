## Overview
Mahmud ibn Ahmad ibn Mahmud ibn Bakhtiyar, Abu al-Manaqib Shihab al-Din al-Zanjani, is described by al-Zirikli as a philologist and one of the Shafi'i jurists {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}. He was from Zanjan, near Azerbaijan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}. The entry gives his years as 573 to 656 AH (1177 to 1258 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## In Baghdad
He settled in Baghdad, where he held the post of deputy to the chief judge and was dismissed; he taught at the Nizamiyya and then at the Mustansiriyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## His works
He wrote a book on the exegesis of the Qur'an, and abridged al-Jawhari's dictionary "al-Sihah", calling his abridgment "Tarwih al-arwah fi tahdhib al-Sihah"; he then condensed it to about a tenth of the original and named it "Tanqih al-Sihah", printed in three parts, for which the entry also gives the name "Tahdhib al-Sihah" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}. He also wrote "Takhrij al-furu' ala al-usul", which is printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.

## His death
Al-Zirikli says he was killed as a martyr in Baghdad in the days of its calamity at the hands of the Mongols and the entry of Hulagu {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 95}.
