## Overview
Kayumarth ibn Umaym ibn Lawadh ibn Iram, the father of the Persians according to al-Mas'udi, in the view of those he names in the section on the disputes of peoples over the lineages of Persia. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 458}

## His lifespan
Al-Mas'udi says that he was granted a long life on earth. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 458}
