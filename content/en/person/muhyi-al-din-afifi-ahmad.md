## Overview
Prof. Dr Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad, Secretary-General of the Islamic Research Academy at al-Azhar as stated on the title page, and author of *Nazarat Mawdu'iyya fi Qadaya Insaniyya* (Objective Views on Human Questions), published 1439/2018 {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 1}. The book treats peace, dialogue, waqf, hoarding, environment, coexistence, extremism, the Rohingya crisis and Jerusalem from its author's perspective {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 5}.
