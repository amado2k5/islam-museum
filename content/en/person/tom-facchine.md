# Tom Facchine

Tom Facchine (born 1989) is an American imam and scholar from New Jersey. He accepted Islam in 2010 while studying at Vassar College, where he took a BA in Political Science in 2011, and then studied at the Islamic University of Madinah from 2015 to 2020, graduating from its Faculty of Shariah.{cite:thinking-muslim-tom-facchine}{cite:macconvention-tom-facchine}{cite:wikidata-tom-facchine}

He is Research Director of Islam and Society at the Yaqeen Institute for Islamic Research, resident scholar and imam of Utica Masjid in New York, and an instructor at Legacy International Online High School, where he teaches tafsir and Islamic history.{cite:macconvention-tom-facchine}{cite:thinking-muslim-tom-facchine}
