## Overview
Khwarazada al-Hatfi, shaykh of the school in Transoxiana — Abu Bakr Muhammad b. al-Husayn al-Bukhari al-Qudaydi, diminutive-nisba of Qudayd between Mecca and Medina; he transmitted from Mansur al-Kaghadi and a company, excelled in the madhhab and outstripped his peers — his 'arida was the broadest path for the companions and he memorised it. He died in Jumada I 483 at Bukhara. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}
