## Summary
A Qurayshi Companion, a notable of [[group/quraysh|Banu Makhzum]]; brother of Umm Salama the Mother of the Believers, and son of the Prophet's paternal aunt Atika bint Abd al-Muttalib. He long opposed the call in Mecca, then became Muslim in the conquest year at Niq al-Uqab, and was martyred by an arrow in the [[event/siege-of-taif|siege of al-Ta'if]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42-44, 129}.

## His conversion and martyrdom
Abdullah ibn Abi Umayya went out with Abu Sufyan ibn al-Harith on the road of the conquest of Mecca seeking Islam; they met the Prophet at Niq al-Uqab, Umm Salama interceded for them, and the Prophet accepted them and they became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/42-44}. He witnessed the conquest of Mecca and Hunayn, then marched in the siege of al-Ta'if where an arrow shot from the fortress struck him and he was martyred {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/129}.

## For young visitors
Umm Salama's brother — an opponent at first, then a devoted Muslim who fought in the conquest battles until a martyring arrow struck him at the siege of al-Ta'if.
