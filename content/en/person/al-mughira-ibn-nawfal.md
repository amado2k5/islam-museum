## Overview
Al-Mughira ibn Nawfal ibn al-Harith, of the sons of al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib. Al-Mas'udi mentions him in the attack on [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]: when the people rushed upon Ibn Muljam after his blow, al-Mughira struck his face and felled him, then brought him to al-Hasan. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.702}

The data record nothing beyond this report, and his year of death and the rest of his life need an additional source.
