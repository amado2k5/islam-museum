## Overview
Maysun daughter of Bahdal of the Kalb of the Banu Kalb. She was the wife of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] and the mother of his son [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid]]. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.177-178}

## In the book
The book describes her as resolute, of great standing in beauty, leadership, intellect and religion. It says that Mu'awiya came to her with a eunuch servant, and she veiled herself and said: "What is this man with you?" {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.177-178}

It relates from Ibn Jarir that she bore Mu'awiya another daughter, who died young. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.178}

## An attributed story
The book presents a story attributed to her about Na'ila daughter of Umara al-Kalbiyya. The reporters relate it, and the book does not confirm it. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.177}
