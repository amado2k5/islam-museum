## Summary
Brother of [[person/abu-zayyan-ahmad|Abu Zayyan Ahmad II]], acclaimed by the people of Tlemcen in his place {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.

## Ottoman suzerainty
[[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]] backed him after stopping the Oran force from restoring his brother; he acknowledged Ottoman authority, an Ottoman garrison under the captain Muhammad remaining in al-Mashwar castle {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 230}.
