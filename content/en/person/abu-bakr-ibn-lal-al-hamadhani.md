## Overview
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Lal al-Hamadhani, a devout worshipper. He took jurisprudence from [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]] and from [[person/abu-ali-ibn-abi-hurayra|Abu Ali ibn Abi Hurayra]], and jurisprudence was taken from him in Hamadhan, according to the report of his grandson Abu Sa'd, as al-Shirazi relates. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.62}

## Dates
Al-Shirazi says that he was born in 307 AH and died in 398 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.62}

Both dates need a second source.
