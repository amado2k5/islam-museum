## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Abi Talha Zayd ibn Sahl al-Ansari was the maternal brother of Anas ibn Malik; Ibn Hajar, in al-Isaba, refers the reader to his lineage in the entry on his father {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}.

## Birth and the first taste
Ibn Hajar says his name is established in Anas's report in the Sahih: when Umm Sulaym bore him she told Anas to take him to the Prophet so that he would perform the tahnik, the rite of rubbing something sweet on a newborn's palate. The first thing to enter his stomach was the Prophet's saliva; the Prophet performed the tahnik with a date, the child began to smack his lips, and the Prophet said that the love of the Ansar is dates {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}. Ibn Hajar quotes Ibn Sad that he was born after the battle of Hunayn {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}.

## Residence and transmission
He lived in Medina and transmitted little hadith, narrating from his father and from his maternal brother Anas {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}. Those who narrated from him include his sons Ishaq and Abd Allah, his grandson Yahya ibn Ishaq, Abu Tuwala and others {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}.

## Death
His death is reported in two ways: Abu Nuaym al-Isbahani said he was killed as a martyr in Fars, while others said he died in Medina in 84 AH {cite:isaba-bajawi-jil-1992 vol. 5 p. 15}.
