## Summary
Al-Malik al-Zahir Abu Sa'id Timurbugha al-Rumi al-Zahiri al-Jaqmaqi took the sultanate after Yalbay was deposed — the fifteenth of the Circassian kings of Egypt. He had merit and piety, sought the people's affection, and was skilled in certain crafts: he made superior bows with his own hands, fashioned arrows superbly and shot them excellently, with complete horsemanship. Yet his time never cleared for him a single day — it shot him off his bow's saddle to the farthest mark: matters stayed on him until they deposed him and exiled him to Alexandria; his sultanate had lasted two months less one day. He died at Alexandria in Dhu'l-Hijja 879, past sixty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 315, 326}
