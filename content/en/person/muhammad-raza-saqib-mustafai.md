## Summary

Muhammad Raza Saqib Mustafai is a Pakistani preacher and scholar, born on 16 March 1972 in Gujranwala, Punjab{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}, in the village of Mustafabad on the city's outskirts{cite:idaratul-mustafa-raza-saqib-mustafai}. He founded Idara-tul-Mustafa International and Jamia-tul-Mustafa in Gujranwala, is a follower of the Naqshbandi Sufi order{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}, and his sermons and lectures reach millions on YouTube and social media{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}.

## Upbringing and formation

Mustafai was born into a religious family in Gujranwala; his father, Khwaja Deen Muhammad Mustafai, is buried at a shrine of which he is the custodian{cite:idaratul-mustafa-raza-saqib-mustafai}. His organisation's biography states that in 1994 he received the spiritual caliphate of the Naqshbandi order, with permission to train disciples, from Khawaja Mian Muhammad Ji Mustafai and Khawaja Din Muhammad Mustafai{cite:idaratul-mustafa-raza-saqib-mustafai}. According to the encyclopedia, he supports the Sufi practices of Mawlid, Urs and Bay'ah{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}.

## Projects and institutions

Mustafai founded Jamia-tul-Mustafa in Gujranwala in 1998 and chairs Idara-tul-Mustafa International, which runs Al-Mustafa Islamic Centres and Al-Mustafawiya Girls Colleges in Pakistan and abroad{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}{cite:idaratul-mustafa-raza-saqib-mustafai}. He delivers Friday sermons at the Muhammadi Masjid in Gujranwala cantonment and at Markaz-e-Mustafa, and his centre organises Qur'an competitions and collective dhikr gatherings{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}.

## Honours

In 2023 Mustafai received the Sitara-e-Imtiaz award from the President of Pakistan for his religious and educational services{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}.

## Impact and audience

Mustafai is among Pakistan's most prominent digital preachers, with about 1.43 million YouTube subscribers in platform data updated in August 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:wikipedia-raza-saqib-mustafai-en}.
