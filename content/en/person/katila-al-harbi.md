## Summary
Katila al-Harbi, remnant of Iraq's shaykhs — the Hanbali Sufi faqir; he heard hadith at Damascus from al-Diya' al-Maqdisi and Sulayman al-As'ardi, took fiqh at Baghdad from Qadi Abu Salih, at Harran from Majd al-Din Ibn Taymiyya and Ibn Tamim, at Damascus from Shams al-Din Ibn Abi 'Umar and at Egypt from Ibn Hamdan; he commented on al-Khirqi's book naming it al-Muhimm, wrote al-'Udda lil-Shidda on usul al-din and a work on sama'; a model ascetic and worshipper, possessed of states and wonders. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641-642}
