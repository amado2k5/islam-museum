## Overview
Sayah al-Sindi, a leader from Sind. His report appears in the context of the conquest of al-Ahwaz in the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 111}.

## His conversion and his people
He asked [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] that he and his people enter Islam, on condition that they fight the non-Arabs alongside him, and he stipulated for them a stipend and a dwelling wherever they chose. Abu Musa wrote to Umar, who agreed {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 111}.

After the conquest they settled in Basra and became allies of the [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]], and many of them worked in the treasury and in money-changing {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 111}.
