## Overview
Abd al-Azim ibn al-Wahid ibn Zafir al-'Adwani, known as Ibn Abi al-Isba', was a poet and scholar of letters, Baghdadi then Egyptian, born and died in Egypt in 654 AH (1256 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.29}

He left fine works, among them Badi' al-Qur'an on the kinds of badi' occurring in the verses, Tahrir al-Tahbir, al-Khawatir al-Sawanih fi Kashf Asrar al-Fawatih on the Qur'an's opening letters, al-Burhan fi I'jaz al-Qur'an, and al-Mukhtarat on literature. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.29}
