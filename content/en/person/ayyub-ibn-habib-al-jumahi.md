## Summary
Ayyub b. Habib al-Jumahi al-Qurashi was one of the trustworthy men of [[place/medina|Medina]] and one of [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik's]] teachers, with a single report from him in the Muwatta'. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667}

## Descent
Malik and others call him a client of [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas]] and say he was Ayyub b. Habib al-Jumahi al-Qurashi, of Banu Jumah. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667} Mus'ab al-Zubayri gives his descent as Ayyub b. Habib b. Ayyub b. 'Alqama b. Rabi'a b. al-A'war — al-A'war's name being Khalaf b. 'Amr b. Wuhayb b. Hudhafa b. Jumah — and says he was killed at Qudayd. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667}

## Standing and death
[[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] says he was one of the trustworthy men of Medina and died in 131 AH. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667} Al-Bukhari said that Malik, Fulayh and 'Abbad b. Ishaq transmitted from him. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667}

## His report in the Muwatta'
His musnad report in the Muwatta' is from Abu al-Muthanna al-Juhani: he was with [[person/marwan-ibn-al-hakam|Marwan b. al-Hakam]] when [[person/abu-said-al-khudri|Abu Sa'id al-Khudri]] came in; Marwan asked whether he had heard the Prophet forbid blowing into a drink, and he said yes, adding that a man said he could not quench his thirst in one breath, and was told to take the cup away from his mouth and then breathe, and to pour away any speck he saw in it. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 667}
