## Summary
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn 'Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Manjuwayh al-Isbahani al-Yazdi, the hafiz, resident and muhaddith of Nishapur; he composed many works and travelled as far as Bukhara, transmitting from Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili, Abu Bakr Ibn al-Muqri' and their generation; Shaykh al-Islam said he was the most retentive person he ever saw; he compiled works on the two Sahihs, Tirmidhi's Jami' and Abu Dawud's Sunan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 233}
