## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Mu'tar al-Taymi. Ibn Amir sent him at the head of his vanguard to Istakhr. Its people met him at Barambajird, fought him, and killed him, and he was buried in the orchard of Barambajird {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
