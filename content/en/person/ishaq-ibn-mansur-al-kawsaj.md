## Overview
Ishaq ibn Mansur ibn Bahram, Abu Ya'qub al-Marwazi, known as al-Kawsaj, was a Hanbali jurist and a man of hadith; he was born in Merv and died in 251 AH, 865 CE, and the entry gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 291}.

## Travels and his book
He travelled to Iraq, the Hijaz and Syria, settled in Nishapur and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 291}. He wrote "al-Masa'il" on jurisprudence, in manuscript, which he recorded from Imam Ahmad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 291}.
