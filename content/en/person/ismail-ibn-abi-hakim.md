## Summary
Isma'il b. Abi Hakim was one of [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik's]] Medinan teachers; [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn 'Abd al-Barr]] profiles him in [[work/al-tamhid-ibn-abd-al-barr|al-Tamhid]] before his four reports in the Muwatta'. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 320}

## Descent and standing
He was a client of Banu 'Adi b. Nawfal b. Asad b. 'Abd al-'Uzza b. Qusayy; some say his clientage was to the family of al-Zubayr b. al-'Awwam. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 320} He lived in [[place/medina|Medina]] and was an excellent and trustworthy man, an authority in what he transmitted according to the body of scholars, in Ibn 'Abd al-Barr's words. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 320}

## Death
He died in Medina in 130 AH; others say 132 or 133. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 320}

## His reports in the Muwatta'
Malik has four of his reports from the Prophet in the Muwatta': one connected with a full chain, and three broken (mursal). {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 320} The musnad one is his report from 'Abida b. Sufyan al-Hadrami from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] that eating any fanged beast of prey is forbidden; Ibn 'Abd al-Barr calls it established, sound and agreed upon. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 321} He takes it to show that the prohibition of fanged animals is one of strict prohibition, not of etiquette or guidance, explaining that *tahrim* in Arabic usage means deprivation and prevention. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 321} He identifies 'Abida b. Sufyan as a trustworthy Medinan Successor who heard Abu Hurayra and Abu al-Ja'd al-Damri. {cite:tamhid-furqan-2017-v1 p. 321}
