# Adama Traoré

## Summary

Adama Traoré Diarra is a Spanish professional footballer, born in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, near Barcelona, on 25 January 1996 to Malian parents. A powerful right winger who came through Barcelona's academy, he played in England for Aston Villa, Middlesbrough, Wolverhampton Wanderers and Fulham, and won eight caps for Spain in 2020–2021. He is one of several footballers named Adama Traoré; this entry is the Spain international born in 1996. {cite:wikipedia-adama-traore-en} {cite:wikipedia-adama-traore-ar} {cite:wikidata-adama-traore}

## Career

Traoré joined Barcelona's youth system in 2004 and played mainly for Barcelona B, with a single league appearance for the first team. In 2015 he moved to Aston Villa, then to Middlesbrough in 2016, where his pace and dribbling made his reputation. He joined Wolverhampton Wanderers in 2018 and spent five seasons there, with a brief loan back to Barcelona in 2022, before moving to Fulham in 2023, West Ham United in 2026 and then Deportivo La Coruña. {cite:wikipedia-adama-traore-en}

At international level he played for Spain at every youth level and, after being approached by Mali, the country of his parents, he chose Spain and made his senior debut in October 2020, playing at Euro 2020. {cite:wikipedia-adama-traore-en}

## Personal life

Traoré was born to Malian parents, and his elder brother Moha is also a footballer.
