## Overview
Al-Qartalusi, Abu al-Fadl, from the districts of Akbara, whom Ibn al-Nadim counts among the jurists of the Shurat. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 330}

## His books
He has many books, among them the "al-Jami' al-Kabir" on jurisprudence, the "al-Jami' al-Saghir" on which his followers rely, and "al-Fara'id". He has "The Refutation of Abu Hanifa on Opinion" and "The Refutation of al-Shafi'i on Analogy". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 330}
