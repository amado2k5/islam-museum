## Overview
Of the [[group/banu-dubayb|Dubayb]] clan, the kin of [[person/rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|Rifa'a ibn Zayd]] of Judham. He accompanied [[person/dihya-al-kalbi|Dihya al-Kalbi]] before the raid on them, and Dihya taught him Umm al-Kitab. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/261} When the army of Zayd ibn Haritha came down upon the people, he was the spokesman of the Dubayb riders. They agreed among themselves that no one but he should speak, for fear of the tongue of his brother Unayf. He said to Zayd: "We are a Muslim people," and he recited Umm al-Kitab to him, and Zayd held back from them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262} He was also the one who rode to Rifa'a and said to him: "You are sitting milking goats while the women of Judham are captives," and Rifa'a rode to the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/263}
