## Summary
Al-Andarzaghar was a Persian commander from the native people of al-Sawad, sent by Ardashir to fight the Muslims; he took part in the battle of al-Walaja in 12 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.353}

## Details
In his reports for the year 12 AH, in the course of Khalid ibn al-Walid's conquests in Iraq, al-Tabari records that Ardashir ibn Shirawayh dispatched those he sent against the Muslims, and among them was al-Andarzaghar, a Persian man from the mawali-born population of al-Sawad. He was present at the battle of al-Walaja, one of the fiercest engagements of the first conquests. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.353}

Al-Walaja was a decisive battle in the conquest of al-Sawad: the Muslims met the Persian army that Ardashir had assembled, and the Persians suffered a crushing defeat. Al-Andarzaghar had a part in the fighting, as al-Tabari relates. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.353}
