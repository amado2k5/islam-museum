## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Bukhari, titled Ala al-Din, was a Hanafi jurist counted among the leading scholars of the school. He was born in Iran, raised in Bukhara, and died in Damascus, where he was buried at al-Mizza; al-Zirikli gives his birth as 779 AH, 1377 CE, and his death as 841 AH, 1438 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 pp. 46-47}.

## Life
He travelled to India, then to Mecca, then to Egypt, where he settled; he later moved to Damascus and stayed there until he died, and was buried at al-Mizza {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 pp. 46-47}. He wrote a treatise in reply to [[person/ibn-arabi-muhyi-al-din|Ibn Arabi]] called Fadihat al-Mulhidin wa-Nasihat al-Muwahhidin; al-Zirikli thinks it was met with an uproar, and that he followed it with a second treatise, al-Mulajjima li-l-Mujassima {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 pp. 46-47}. Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Tulun as saying that he was the imam of his age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 pp. 46-47}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names: Fadihat al-Mulhidin wa-Nasihat al-Muwahhidin, in manuscript; al-Mulajjima li-l-Mujassima; and Nuzhat al-Nazar fi Kashf Haqiqat al-Insha' wa-l-Khabar, in manuscript in the Chester Beatty library (no. 3146) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 pp. 46-47}.
