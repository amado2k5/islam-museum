## Summary
Son of al-Harith ibn Khalid and Rita bint al-Harith; he was born in the land of Abyssinia and perished on the road. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among "the children" who perished in the land of Abyssinia, of Banu Taym. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10} Elsewhere he says he perished on the road with his mother and his two sisters, from water they drank. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11} He counts him among the five men born in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-tahm|Banu Taym ibn Murra]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- On p. 10 Ibn Ishaq places him among "those who perished in the land of Abyssinia", and on p. 11 says he perished "on the road" — an unremarked discrepancy.
