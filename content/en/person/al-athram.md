## Overview
Al-Athram, Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hani, known as Abu Bakr, one of the companions of Ahmad ibn Hanbal, from the people of Iskaf Banu Junayd. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.321}

Ibn al-Nadim says he had a book "al-Sunan" on jurisprudence according to the schools of Ahmad, with its hadith evidence, a book of history, a book of defects, and a book on the abrogating and abrogated in hadith. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.321}
