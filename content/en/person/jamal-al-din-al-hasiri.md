## Overview
Mahmud ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Sayyid ibn Uthman, Abu al-Mahamid, Jamal al-Din al-Bukhari al-Hasiri, was a jurist to whom the headship of the Hanafis passed in his time {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 161}. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 546 AH, 1151 CE, and his death as 636 AH, 1238 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 161}.

## Birthplace and name
He was born in Bukhara, and his name al-Hasiri comes from a quarter of the city where mats were made. He settled in Damascus, taught at the Nuriyya school and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 161}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Tahrir fi Sharh al-Jami al-Kabir, on law, in seven volumes, in manuscript; Khayr Matlub fi al-Ilm al-Marghub, on law, in manuscript; al-Tariqa al-Hasiriyya fi al-Khilaf bayna al-Shafi'iyya wa-l-Hanafiyya, in manuscript; al-Najm al-Hadi al-Sari ila Hall Alfaz Sahih al-Bukhari, in manuscript, its first part in the library of Aydarus al-Habshi at al-Ghurfa in Hadramawt; and al-Wajiz, Hanafi legal opinions, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 161}.
