## Overview
Farrukhbandadh, commander of the Persian cavalry who met Bashir ibn Sa'd al-Ansari at Baniqya. Al-Baladhuri reports that he shot arrows at Bashir's companions, and Bashir charged at them, routed them and killed him. It is also said that Khalid himself met him. The report is connected to the [[event/baniqya-sulh|treaty of Baniqya]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
