## Overview
The judge Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Baghdadi, known as Ibn al-Daqqaq, a jurist and theorist of the principles of law. He explained "al-Mukhtasar" and was appointed judge of the Karkh of Baghdad. He was born in 306 AH and died in 392 AH, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.62}

The dates of his birth and death are from al-Shirazi alone and need an additional source.
