## Overview
Buna al-Saghir, a client of the amir of the believers al-Mutawakkil. He fought Muhammad ibn al-Ba'ith the Kharijite in Azerbaijan in [[event/muhammad-ibn-al-baith-revolt|his revolt]], until he overcame him and carried him to Samarra. He demolished the wall of Marand and its palace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
