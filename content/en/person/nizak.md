## Overview
Nizak, lord of Badghis or of Jawzjan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## With Qutayba
He was with Qutayba on the expedition to Bikand in 87 AH. Qutayba later killed him in Tukharistan and crucified him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

An additional source is needed for the page number, and to distinguish him from Nizak Tarkhan.
