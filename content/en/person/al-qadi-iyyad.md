## Overview
The judge Iyad, Abu al-Fadl Iyad ibn Musa ibn Iyad al-Yahsabi al-Sabti al-Maliki. He was judge of Ceuta for a time, then judge of Granada. He was a memoriser of hadith, the author of "al-Shifa" and "Mashariq al-Anwar". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 619}

## Ibn al-Imad's description
Ibn al-Imad relates that he was the imam of his age in various sciences, very sharp-witted, and very firm in adhering to the Sunna. He says he ordered the books of al-Ghazali burned, because of a matter he suspected in them, and that this roused a view defending al-Ghazali. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 619}

## His birth and death
He was born in 476 AH and died in 544 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 619} The Gregorian conversion is derived and approximate.

## His works
The data attribute to him the book [[work/al-ilam-fi-hudud-al-ahkam|al-I'lam fi Hudud al-Ahkam]], according to "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
