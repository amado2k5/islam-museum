## Overview
Abu Hashim Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya. In the chain of justice and unity according to Abu al-Hudhayl, [[person/wasil-ibn-ata|Wasil ibn Ata]] took from him, and he took from his father [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-hanafiyya|Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya]]. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.558}

## Notes for researchers
- The page rests on Ibn al-Nadim's report of the Mu'tazili chain; a second source is needed. It may be connected with the separate page [[person/abu-hashim-ibn-muhammad|Abu Hashim Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya]]; the identity of the two has not yet been established.
