## Overview
Abu Hashim Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya; in Abu Hudhayl's chain of justice and divine unity, Wasil ibn Ata took it from him, and he from his father Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 246}
