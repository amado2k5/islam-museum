## Overview
Harun ibn Musa ibn Ahmad al-Shaybani al-Tal'ukbari, Abu Muhammad, is listed by al-Zirikli among the transmitters of hadith for the Imamiyya, adding that his transmission is impugned among the Sunnis. He was of Tal 'Ukbara near Baghdad and died in 385 AH (995 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.63}

He wrote books, among them al-Jawami' on the religious sciences. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.63}
