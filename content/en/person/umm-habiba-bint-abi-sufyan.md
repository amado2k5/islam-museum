## Summary
Daughter of [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan ibn Harb]] and one of the Mothers of the Believers; she emigrated to Abyssinia then to Medina {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38; cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}. When her father came to Medina on his [[event/abu-sufyan-medina-mission|failed mission]] she folded up the Prophet's bedding away from him, saying: "it is the Messenger of God's bedding, and you are a polytheist man, unclean" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38}.

## The emigration to Abyssinia
Umm Habiba emigrated with her husband Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh to Abyssinia, fleeing with her religion; he apostatised while she held firm {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38}. The Negus married her to the Prophet in a contract completed in the presence of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib in Abyssinia {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38}.

## The bedding scene
When Abu Sufyan came to Medina to renew the pact he entered on his daughter, and she folded the Prophet's bedding away from him. He said: daughter, is it the bedding you begrudge me, or me? She said: it is the Messenger of God's bedding, and you are a polytheist man, unclean — I would not have you sit on it {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38}.

## For young visitors
A woman of Mecca who emigrated for her faith twice — first to Abyssinia, then to Medina — and the Prophet married her. When her father came visiting, she did not let him sit on her husband's bedding, for he was at enmity with him.
