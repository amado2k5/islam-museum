## Summary
Tahir ibn Muhammad ibn 'Amr ibn al-Layth al-Saffar ruled the lands of Fars after his grandfather 'Amr ibn al-Layth was captured — twelve nights remaining of Safar 288. Then in 296 Subuk al-Subkari, one of his body-mamluks, seized him with his brother Ya'qub ibn Muhammad and sent them both to the City of Peace. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
