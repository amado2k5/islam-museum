## Summary
Najib al-Din 'Abd al-Latif al-Hanbali, jurist and imam of his people and the most esteemed of them, a man of high standing with the sultan. He was severe in denouncing wrong, gave lessons and dictations in which he issued sound fatwas, and wrote chancery prose including fine royal narratives — among them the book *al-Nur* and his commentary on it in *al-Kafi*, which he named *al-Kafiya* and in which he went deep. The last to take fiqh from him was Abu Bakr ibn Sabah; he made the hajj among the greatest shaykhs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 565}
