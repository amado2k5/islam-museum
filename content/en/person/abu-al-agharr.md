## Overview
Abu al-Aghar Khalifa ibn al-Mubarak, amir of the Iraqi pilgrim caravan. Al-Suyuti relates that he fought the tribe of Tayy for a day and a night when they rose against the caravan in 287 AH, and that he killed their amir Salih and took many of them captive, and the pilgrims brought in the captives and the heads on lances. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.575}
