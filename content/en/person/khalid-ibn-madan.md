## Summary
Khalid ibn Ma'dan al-Kala'i, a trustworthy Successor who met about seventy of the Companions and transmitted from Thawban, Ibn Umar and Mu'awiya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2059}

## Works
Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy; he died in 103 AH, or another year is given. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2059}
