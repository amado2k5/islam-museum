## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Abd al-Mannan al-Hanafi al-Rumi, known as Abd Allah Hilmi, Yusufzade, Yusuf Efendi and al-Amasi, was a scholar of Qur'anic exegesis, the Qur'anic readings and hadith. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1085 AH, 1674 CE, and his death as 1167 AH, 1754 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 129}.

## Life
He was born in Amasya in Turkey and became attached to the Ottoman Sultans Ahmed and Mahmud, who recognised his worth; he died in Istanbul, which al-Zirikli calls al-Astana {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 129}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote many books and names among them al-I'tilaf fi wujuh al-ikhtilaf on the ten readings, in manuscript; Zubdat al-Irfan fi wujuh al-Qur'an, in manuscript; a gloss on al-Baydawi's Anwar al-Tanzil; a gloss on al-Aqa'id al-Nasafiyya; Rawdat al-Wa'izin; Inayat al-Malik al-Mun'im, a commentary on Sahih Muslim in three volumes; and Najah al-Qari, a commentary on al-Bukhari in twenty volumes, of which one part is in Topkapi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 129}. He also composed verse in Arabic, Turkish and Persian {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 129}.
