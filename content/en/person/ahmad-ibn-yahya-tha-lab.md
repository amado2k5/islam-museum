## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Zayd al-Shaybani al-Kufi, known as Tha'lab, a Kufan philologist and grammarian. He was a client of the Banu Shayban. Many sayings on grammar are transmitted from him. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he was born in 200 AH (815 CE) and died in 291 AH (903 CE). Both dates come from one source and need a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His book [[work/ikhtilaf-al-nuhat-thalab|Ikhtilaf al-Nuhat]] is attributed to him. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
