## Summary
Qadi Diya' al-Din Abu'l-Fada'il al-Qasim b. Qadi Taj al-Din Abi Tahir Yahya b. 'Abdallah; he heard hadith at Alexandria from the hafiz Abu Tahir al-Silafi and transmitted some verse of 'Umara al-Yamani the jurist; he held Damascus's judgeship after his uncle Kamal al-Din by a bequest of his uncle Taj al-Din that the sultan carried out, remaining a while — then, learning the sultan leaned to Shaykh Sharaf al-Din Ibn Abi 'Asrun, he asked to be relieved, and Sharaf al-Din took it. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## After Salah al-Din
When Salah al-Din died his son al-Malik al-Afdal Nur al-Din 'Ali settled him over the supervision of Damascus's awqaf; then he left Damascus and went off to Hama. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
