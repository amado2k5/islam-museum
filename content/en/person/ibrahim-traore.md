## Summary
Ibrahim Traore is a Burkinabe army officer who has led Burkina Faso since a coup on 30 September 2022, making him one of the world's youngest heads of state. He governs as transitional president within the Alliance of Sahel States, the bloc formed with the military governments of Mali and Niger. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-traore}

## Life
He was born on 14 March 1988 in Kera, in the Bondokuy area of Mouhoun province. He studied geology at the University of Ouagadougou, where he took part in the Muslim students' association, and joined the army in 2009, rising to the rank of captain in the artillery. He served with the United Nations peacekeeping mission in Mali (MINUSMA) and in operations against armed groups inside Burkina Faso. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-traore}

## Rise and rule
In September 2022, amid anger over worsening insecurity, Traore led the officers who removed the previous military ruler, Paul-Henri Sandaogo Damiba — who had himself seized power that January. Sworn in as transitional president in October 2022 at the age of thirty-four, Traore expelled French forces, drew closer to Russia, and with Mali and Niger left the ECOWAS bloc to form the Alliance of Sahel States. National consultations in May 2024 extended the transition for sixty months from July 2024, permitting him to remain in office and to contest a future election. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-traore}

## Readings
Supporters across the Sahel and its diaspora cast Traore as a pan-African, sovereignty-first figure in the mould of Thomas Sankara, while critics point to restrictions on media and dissent, continuing attacks by armed groups, and the postponement of elections. Reference sources describe him as a Muslim without naming a school, and the museum records none. Both assessments are presented attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-ibrahim-traore}
