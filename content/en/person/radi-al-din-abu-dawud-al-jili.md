## Summary
Radi al-Din Abu Dawud Sulayman ibn al-Muzaffar ibn Ghanim ibn 'Abd al-Karim al-Jili, Shafi'i mufti at the Nizamiyya madrasa of Baghdad, was among the greatest learned men of his age. He composed a fiqh work filling fifteen volumes; offices were offered to him and he declined them, being devout. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
