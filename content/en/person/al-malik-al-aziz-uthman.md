## Overview
Al-Malik al-Aziz Imad al-Din Uthman ibn Salah al-Din. Egypt was settled for him when his father died in 589 AH {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}.

He died in 595 AH, and his son al-Mansur was set up, but his uncle [[person/al-malik-al-adil-ayyub|al-Malik al-Adil]] rose and took possession of Egypt {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 694}.

## According to al-Maqrizi
Al-Maqrizi says that after his father Saladin, the Cairo residence was lived in by al-Malik al-Aziz {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1001}.

He says that in al-Aziz's time the zakat office in Egypt seized a merchant's goods and demanded the zakat on what he carried. Al-Aziz looked into the collection of zakat, heard shocking reports about it, and ordered that the assessment be left to the owners of property {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.352}.
