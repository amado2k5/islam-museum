## Summary
Al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim Abu Ali al-Tabari, the Shafiʿi jurist (d. 350 AH), author of the first book written on the jurisprudence of disagreement. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.8-9}

## Al-Muharrar
He studied under Abu Ali ibn Abi Hurayra, excelled in the field and settled in Baghdad. He wrote the book "al-Muharrar," which is the first book written on the disagreements between jurists. He also wrote "al-Ifsah" on the school, a book on disputation, and a book on the principles of jurisprudence. He died in Baghdad in the year 350 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.8-9}
