## Summary
Al-Kamal Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Muhammad, known as Ibn al-Nabih the Egyptian; katib and poet of al-Malik al-Ashraf — who one day saw in his secretary-poet's inkwell a single line written on the spot, unmatched, and uttered a wonderful verse: 'O Kamal! — the orb of proclamation; you answered magnificence — whatever you willed to bring forth, you bring crowned; cut it, and it is eternally mine.' {cite:wafayat-ayan}
