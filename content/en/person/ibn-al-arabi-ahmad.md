## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ziyad ibn Bishr ibn Dirham, Abu Sa'id al-A'rabi, a hadith imam and memoriser, historian, ascetic and Sufi from Basra, a scholar of hadith and of the generations (tabaqat). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.153}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 246 AH (861 CE) and died in 340 AH (952 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.153} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
