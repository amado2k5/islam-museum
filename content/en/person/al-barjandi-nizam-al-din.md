## Overview
Nizam al-Din Abd al-Ali ibn Muhammad al-Barjandi, from Barjand in the province of Khurasan. He was born in the middle of the ninth century AH and died in 935 AH (1528 CE). The "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters" classes him among the philosophers and scholars. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
