## Summary
Setiawan Hanung Bramantyo, known as Hanung Bramantyo, is an Indonesian film director born in Yogyakarta on 1 October 1975, whose films range from teen romances to religious dramas and who is best known for the Islamic romance Ayat-Ayat Cinta (2008). {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}

He is catalogued in this collection under the lane's genre ruling of 9 November 2026, which brings creators and directors of Islamic moral dramas and scripted films into scope. {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}

His verified Instagram account counted 949,359 followers in a direct read on 15 November 2026. {cite:instagram-hanungbramantyo}

## Beginnings
Interested in theatre as a child, he made his directorial debut with the 1998 short film Tlutur and his feature-length debut in 2004 with Brownies. {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}

The Yogyakarta provincial cultural award's profile records that he was named best director at the Indonesian Film Festival in 2005 for Brownies and again in 2007 for Get Married. {cite:jogjaprov-hanung-bramantyo}

He studied economics at the Indonesian Islamic University in Yogyakarta without completing it, and then moved to film studies at the Jakarta Art Institute. {cite:jogjaprov-hanung-bramantyo}

## Ayat-Ayat Cinta and the religious dramas
In 2008 he directed Ayat-Ayat Cinta (Verses of Love), the Islamic romance adapted from Habiburrahman El Shirazy's novel, which was a critical and commercial success. {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}

The provincial profile records that the film drew 1.5 million viewers in its first nine days and was screened in Malaysia and Singapore. {cite:jogjaprov-hanung-bramantyo}

The English article notes that the film also received criticism for having one of its main characters, Maria, convert from Christianity to Islam — a criticism this museum reports as the article states it. {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}

His later work has been noted for its religious themes alongside other genres, as the article's survey of his career describes it. {cite:wikipedia-hanung-bramantyo-en}
