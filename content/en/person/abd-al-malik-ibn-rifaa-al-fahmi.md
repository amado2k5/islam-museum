## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Rifa'a al-Fahmi, whom Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz confirmed as governor of Egypt. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.388}

## His conduct
Al-Salabi, citing Ibn Taghribirdi, says that his conduct was good, that he was chaste with money, and that he was a trustworthy and just man among his subjects. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.388}

## His removal
Umar removed him in Rabi' al-Awwal 99 AH without giving the reason, and appointed Ayyub ibn Shurahbil in his place. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.388}
