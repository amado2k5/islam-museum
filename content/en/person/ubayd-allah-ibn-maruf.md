## Summary
Ibn Ma'ruf, chief qadi of Baghdad in al-Ta'i' li'llah's last days, took up the chief judgeship in Sha'ban 360 after Abu Bakr ibn Sayyar was dismissed. He was arrested on Monday, four nights remaining of Safar 369, on 'Adud al-Dawla's order, and dispatched to the castle at Fars; Abu Sa'd Bishr ibn al-Hasan was invested with the chief judgeship in his place. It was held against him that he had been remiss toward 'Adud al-Dawla and that he allowed himself latitude unbecoming of qadis such as he; he answered, and it was not heeded. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.64, 102}
