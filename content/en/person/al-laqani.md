## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Ibrahim ibn Hasan al-Laqani, Abu al-Imdad, Burhan al-Din, was an Egyptian Maliki scholar and Sufi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. His nisba is from Laqana in al-Buhayra in Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}. Al-Zirikli gives no year of birth; he died in 1041 AH (1631 CE) near al-Aqaba, returning from the pilgrimage {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Jawharat al-Tawhid", printed, a poem on the articles of belief; "Bahjat al-Mahafil", in manuscript, on introducing the narrators of the Shama'il; a gloss on the Mukhtasar of Khalil in jurisprudence; "Nashr al-Ma'athir fi-man Adraktuhum min Ulama al-Qarn al-Ashir", biographies which he did not complete; and "Qada' al-Watar", in manuscript, a gloss on al-Asqalani in the terminology of hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 26}.
