## Overview
Rumzi is an American Muslim content creator and dawah educator from California who runs the YouTube channel Dawah Over Dunya. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}

He was born into a Palestinian family and raised in the Bay Area, and a profile published in August 2026 described him as 24 years old. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}

He publishes under the single name Rumzi; no source consulted gives a legal surname or a birth date for him. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026} {cite:youtube-dawah-over-dunya}

## Career and work
In his channel's own description he calls himself an independent researcher whose content is primarily devoted to defending Islam against false allegations. {cite:youtube-dawah-over-dunya}

He has produced Islamic content for four years, building Dawah Over Dunya into a short-form platform of more than 200,000 subscribers and over 125 million views, figures reported in August 2026 that change constantly. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}

His videos tackle questions about Islam, respond to misconceptions, and encourage viewers to understand not only what they believe but why they believe it. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}

He built the channel while completing a Bachelor's degree in chemical engineering. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}

His stated aim is to revive the Islamic intellect among young Muslims by making Islamic knowledge accessible and engaging. {cite:vents-rumzi-profile-2026}
