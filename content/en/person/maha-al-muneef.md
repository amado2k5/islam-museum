## Summary
Maha Al-Muneef is a Saudi physician who combined paediatrics with institution-building in family protection. She is among the most prominent faces of the campaign against domestic violence in Saudi Arabia. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-muneef}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Saudi Arabia in 1960, she studied medicine and specialised in paediatrics and infectious diseases with fellowships in the United States before returning to Saudi hospitals. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-muneef}

## Career and works
In 2005 she founded the National Family Safety Program under the National Guard, supervising early-detection programmes for child abuse (SCAN) and medical training. Her advocacy contributed to Saudi protection-from-abuse legislation, and she works with Arab child-protection networks. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-muneef}

## Legacy
She has received Arab and international awards for child-protection work and is cited as a pioneer in bringing child-protection medicine into the Saudi health system. {cite:wikipedia-maha-al-muneef}
