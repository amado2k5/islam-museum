## Overview
Al-Yaman ibn Ribab, one of the leading scholars of the Kharijites, who wrote books on their doctrines. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}

## His brother
His brother Ali ibn Ribab was, in al-Mas'udi's phrase, among the leading scholars of the Rafidis. The two brothers used to meet every year for three days to debate, and then they would part without either greeting the other. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}
