## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Bishr ibn Yahya al-Amidi, a man of letters, critic and philologist from Basra. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His work
He worked as a secretary in the offices of Basra and Baghdad. He wrote "al-Muwazana", comparing the poetry of Abu Tammam and al-Buhturi. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 371 AH (981 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
