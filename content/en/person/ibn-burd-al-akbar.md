## Overview
Abu Hafs Ahmad ibn Burd al-Akbar, a minister known as Ibn Burd al-Akbar to distinguish him from his grandson Ibn Burd al-Asghar. He was a leading figure in the Amirid state. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 460-461}

He drew up the instrument by which Hisham al-Mu'ayyad appointed Abd al-Rahman Shanjul ibn al-Mansur, in Rabi' al-Awwal 398 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 460-461}
