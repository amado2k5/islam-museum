## Summary
Emile Dermenghem, the French scholar who wrote *La vie de Mahomet* — the book Dr Haykal drew upon, per Ghallab; Ghallab counts him among the moderns who studied the texts before judging, and quotes his descriptions of the Prophet as a moderate, wise man and a prophet. {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 69} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 70} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 72} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 73}

## His view
He holds that the wars between Islam and Christendom bred the West's misunderstanding. {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar}
