## Overview
Muhammad ibn Iyas al-Azdi, father of Abu Zakariyya Yazid ibn Muhammad, author of the history of Mosul. Ibn al-Athir says that he died in 298 AH and that he was pious and excellent. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 48}
