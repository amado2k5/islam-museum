## Summary
Abu'l-Fath Nasr Allah ibn Abi'l-Qasim 'Abd Allah ibn 'Ali ibn Qalaqis al-Iskandari, the poet, entered Yemen and at Aden praised Abu'l-Fath Yasir ibn Abi'l-Nada Bilal ibn Jarir al-Muhammadi, vizier of the Banu Zari' lords of Yemen, who treated him well and gave lavishly. When he left him he took ship; the vessel was wrecked near Dahlak and he drowned with all he had — on Friday, the fifth of Dhu'l-Qa'da. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
