## Overview
Mujir al-Din Tashtakin, amir of the Iraqi pilgrims. Ibn al-Athir says that he forbade Shams al-Din ibn al-Muqaddam, amir of the Syrian pilgrims, from leaving Arafat before him in 583 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 162-163}

## The events of Arafat
Ibn al-Muqaddam did not stop for his prohibition. Tashtakin rode out with his companions, and a great crowd of commoners and the Iraqi pilgrims' hangers-on followed him. They fell upon the Syrian pilgrims, killed a number of them and plundered their property, and wounded Ibn al-Muqaddam so badly that he died at Mina the same day. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 162-163}

Ibn al-Athir says that Tashtakin had taken Ibn al-Muqaddam to his tent to nurse him and make up for what he had neglected in his rights. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 162-163}
