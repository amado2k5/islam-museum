## Overview
Yahya ibn Ali ibn Ghaniya al-Sanhaji al-Masufi. He and his brother Muhammad grew up in the care of Yusuf ibn Tashfin. Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin appointed him over the west of al-Andalus and settled him in Cordoba. When Abu Ishaq Baraq al-Masmudi took Seville for the Almohads, he asked for his obedience, and moved him from Cordoba to Jaen and the castle. He then marched to Granada to bring those of the Lamtuna there to obey the Almohads, and he died there in the year 543 AH and was buried in the palace of Badis {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

The Common Era date (1148 CE) is converted and derived from the Hijri date.
