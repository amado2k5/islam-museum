## Overview
Umm al-Banin bint Abd al-Aziz, a paternal cousin of [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.149-150}.

## With al-Walid and al-Hajjaj
al-Mas'udi reports that she objected to al-Walid sitting with [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] while he was in a shift. al-Hajjaj said to al-Walid: "Leave off jesting with women, for a woman is a sweet-smelling plant, not a steward." She ordered him to greet her {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.149-150}.

## What al-Salabi reports
al-Salabi reports that she used to memorise the Qur'an and review it morning and evening, and that she freed a slave every Friday. She returned the gift of Muhammad ibn Yusuf when she learned that he had taken it by force {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.136-139}.

He reports that she rebuked al-Hajjaj when he came in with al-Walid, and reminded him of the killing of Ibn al-Zubayr {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.136-139}.

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the twenty-sixth and twenty-seventh episodes of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] to Umm al-Banin bint Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, as an example of goodness in the houses of the caliphs. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:02:00} He says her father governed Egypt for twenty years and was the father of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, and that her husband was the caliph al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:05:00} She was born in Medina, where al-Walid met her on pilgrimage and married her, and she bore him Abd al-Aziz, Muhammad and Aisha. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:07:30} She memorised the Qur'an young and many hadiths, and he cites Abu Zur'a al-Dimashqi and Ibn Makula on her learning. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:08:30}

He says she prayed long and was moved by verses of warning, and that she held nothing adorned people better than awe of God. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:11:00} Each week she bought a horse and sent it with a year's provisions to a fighter, and each Friday she freed a slave with money to live on. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:12:30} From Ibn al-Jawzi's Sifat al-Safwa he tells how, when the governor of Yemen Muhammad ibn Yusuf brought rare gifts and she heard they had been taken by force, she would not have them in her house, and al-Walid accepted them only after the governor swore fifty oaths at the Black Stone that nothing in them was wrongfully taken. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep26 00:21:30}

In the twenty-seventh episode he tells, from the history books, how she warned al-Walid against sitting unarmed with al-Hajjaj, and how al-Hajjaj answered that a woman is a sweet herb, not a steward. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:12:00} She then had al-Hajjaj come to greet her, kept him waiting behind a curtain, reproached him for boasting of the killing of Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, son of "her of the two girdles", and reminded him that the caliph's women had sold their perfume and jewellery to pay the troops who saved him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:20:00} He says al-Hajjaj left unable to answer. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:24:30} He closes with her sayings that every people has a craving and hers was giving, sweeter to her than food to the hungry. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep27 00:25:00}
