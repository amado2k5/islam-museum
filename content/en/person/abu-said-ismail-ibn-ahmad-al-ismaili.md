## Overview
Abu Sa'd Ismail ibn Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Isma'ili, a jurist and man of letters who combined religious and worldly leadership in Jurjan. He took learning from his father [[person/al-ismaili-abu-bakr-ahmad|Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili]], and died in 396 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}

## What al-Shirazi says
He describes him as generous, and says that al-Sahib ibn Abbad wrote a letter praising him, his brother Abu Nasr, and their father. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}

## Note
The printed text says "Abu Said", and the editor's note says the correct form is "Abu Sa'd". The year of death comes from one source and needs a second.
