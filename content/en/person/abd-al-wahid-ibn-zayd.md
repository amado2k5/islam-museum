## Overview
Abu Ubayda Abd al-Wahid ibn Zayd al-Basri, an ascetic, exemplar and preacher, described as the shaykh of the worshippers in Basra. He transmitted from [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]] and [[person/ata-ibn-abi-rabah|Ata ibn Abi Rabah]]. The "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters" gives his death as 177 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
