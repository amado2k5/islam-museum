## Summary

Al-Hubab ibn Zayd ibn Taym ibn Umayya ibn Khufaf al-Ansari, of the Aws. Ibn Shahin reports that he fought at Uhud and was killed at al-Yamama; Ibn al-Kalbi does not report his death there. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.282}
