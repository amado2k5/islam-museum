## Overview
A Companion who emigrated among the first women to pledge allegiance. Ibn Sad said she embraced Islam, pledged, and transmitted a hadith from the Messenger of God. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5161}

## Name
She is called Yusayra Umm Yasir, or, it is said, Yusayra bint Yasir. The source gives no dates.
