## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Fasawi, one of the companions of [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-qattan|Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Qattan]]. Al-Shirazi describes him as an eloquent theorist; he says that he lived in Baghdad and died in Arrajan. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.62} Al-Shirazi does not give his date of death.
