Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi. His mother was Atika bint Abd al-Muttalib, aunt of the Prophet. He was the first to break the boycott document openly in the Sacred Mosque, after being roused to it by [[person/hisham-ibn-amr|Hisham ibn Amr]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He came to the Ka'ba wearing a fine cloak. He circled the House seven times, then turned to the people and said: "O people of Mecca, are we to eat food and wear clothes while the Banu Hashim perish, with no one selling to them or buying from them? By God, I will not sit until this cutting, unjust document is torn up." [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]] disputed with him, but the four men who followed Zuhayr kept supporting him until the document was torn up. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## In the nullification of the boycott document (The Sealed Nectar, pp.110-111)
Hisham ibn Amr went to him to rouse him, and said: "Are you content to eat food while your maternal uncles are in the state you know?" Zuhayr then went to al-Hajun and circled the House. He stood among Quraysh demanding that the document be torn, and so he was the first to speak for its annulment ([[event/nullification-of-boycott-covenant|the annulment of the document]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.110} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.111}. His mother was [[person/atika-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Atika]] bint Abd al-Muttalib {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.110}.
