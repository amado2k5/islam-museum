Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi; his mother Atika bint Abd al-Muttalib, the Prophet's aunt. He was the first to stand for tearing up the boycott document openly in the Sacred Mosque after [[person/hisham-ibn-amr|Hisham ibn Amr]] stirred him to it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He went to the Ka'ba in the morning in a fine garment, made seven circuits of the House, then turned to the people and said: "people of Mecca — shall we eat food and wear clothes while Banu Hashim perish, nothing sold to them and nothing bought from them? By God I will not sit until this unjust severing document is torn up" — Abu Jahl disputed him, but the four men followed one another backing Zuhayr until the document was torn up. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
