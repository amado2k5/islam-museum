## Overview
Marwan ibn Abi Hafsa, a poet who praised [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] and Ma'n ibn Za'ida, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.275-312}

## Between Ma'n and al-Mahdi
Al-Mas'udi relates that he took a hundred thousand dirhams from Ma'n ibn Za'ida for a poem in which he described him as the mountain to which Nizar takes refuge. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.275-312}

When he asked leave to enter to al-Mahdi, the caliph refused to admit him. Then al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba spoke to him, and he entered and recited his poem; al-Mahdi was pleased with him and rewarded him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.275-312}
