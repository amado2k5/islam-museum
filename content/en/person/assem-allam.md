## Summary
Assem Allam was an Egyptian-British businessman, born in Egypt on 1 August 1939 and died in Kingston upon Hull on 2 December 2022. He built the generator manufacturer Allam Marine and owned Hull City A.F.C. from 2010 to 2022. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en}

## Life
Allam was born in Egypt in 1939 — the Arabic article gives the village of Al-Sadd in Qalyub district and the year 1940, which is why his birth is recorded as debated — and left Nasser's Egypt for Britain in 1968. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en} {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-ar} He studied economics at the University of Hull and qualified as an accountant. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en} No source read attests any religion for him: neither article mentions it and his Wikidata item carries no religion statement. {cite:wikidata-assem-allam}

## Work
Allam joined Tempest Diesel and in 1981 used a loan to begin buying the company out; after it was placed in receivership in 1992 he repurchased its assets through a new company, Allam Marine, which grew into an internationally successful manufacturer of industrial generators. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en} He was named Ernst & Young UK Entrepreneur of the Year in 2006 and appeared with his son on the Sunday Times Rich List. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en} In September 2010 he took over Hull City A.F.C., then in financial trouble after relegation from the Premier League; his attempt to rename the club Hull Tigers was rejected by the Football Association and opposed by many supporters, and his family's ownership ended in January 2022. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en} He died on 2 December 2022, aged 83. {cite:wikipedia-assem-allam-en}
