## Summary
[[person/al-miswar-ibn-makhrama|Al-Miswar ibn Makhrama]] ibn Nawfal al-Zuhri, kunya Abu Abd al-Rahman; his mother Atika bint Awf, sister of [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], who became Muslim and emigrated {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}.

## His birth and companionship
Yahya ibn Bukayr said he was born two years after the hijra and came to Medina in Dhu al-Hijja after the conquest, year 8 AH, a blooming lad of six {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}. Al-Baghawi said he preserved hadiths from the Prophet — his hadith from the Prophet on the betrothal of Aliya bint Abi Jahl is in the two Sahihs and elsewhere {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}.

In one of Muslim's chains it reads: "I heard the Prophet while I was a man of wet dreams" — pointing to birth before the hijra; but the scholars are unanimous he was born after it, some explaining "muhtalim" as from hilm with kasra, not hukm with damma — meaning he was discerning and retentive {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}.

## His affairs
Mus'ab said he kept close to Umar ibn al-Khattab; al-Zubayr said he was of the people of merit and religion. He was with his uncle Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf on the nights of the shura and preserved things from him, then was with Ibn al-Zubayr {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}.

## His death
In the first siege a mangonel stone struck him while he prayed; he lingered five days and died the day the news of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya's death came, year 64 AH, per Yahya ibn Bukayr and Abu Mushar {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}. Al-Tabari quotes Ibn Ma'in that he died in 73 AH, which Ibn Hajar rebuts as an error: the scholars agreed he died in the first siege of Ibn al-Zubayr, whereas al-Hajjaj's siege of 73 AH — al-Miswar did not live to its time {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1415}.
