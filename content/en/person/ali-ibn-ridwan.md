## Overview
Ali ibn Ridwan, Abu al-Hasan al-Misri, is listed by Ibn al-Imad among the deaths of 453 AH, about 1061 CE (derived conversion) {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 5 p. 226}.

## Life
Ibn al-Imad calls him the philosopher and author of works, says he was a leading figure in medicine and in astrology, and one of the sharpest minds of his time in the lands of Egypt {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 5 p. 226}.
