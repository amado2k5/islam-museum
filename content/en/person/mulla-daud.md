## Overview
Mulla Da'ud was a poet of the Delhi Sultanate period who composed the *Laur Chanda* or *Chandayana* (The Story of Chanda) in the Avadhi dialect of Hindi in 1377-78 and dedicated it to Jahan Shah, vizier of Sultan [[person/firuz-shah-tughluq|Firuz Shah Tughluq]]. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 136}

## His legacy
The romance was widely popular: parts of at least five illustrated pre-Mughal copies survive, among them [[object/chandayana-prince-of-wales-museum|the copy]] of which the Prince of Wales Museum in Bombay holds sixty-eight miniatures. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 136} Welch counts a sixteenth-century *Chandayana* among the loveliest of all Sultanate manuscripts, its Iranian and Indian elements in balanced synthesis. {cite:welch-india-art-and-culture-1985 p. 119}
