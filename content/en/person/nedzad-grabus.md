Nedžad Grabus, born in 1968 at Krpeljići near Travnik, is a Bosnian theologian whose career joined the classroom to the mufti's office across two countries. Educated at the Gazi Husrev-beg Madrasa in Sarajevo and at the city's Faculty of Islamic Studies — where he took his doctorate in aqida in 2008 and rose to full professor of aqida, tasawwuf and comparative religions — he was appointed Mufti of Ljubljana and president of the Meshihat of the Islamic Community in Slovenia in 2006{cite:wikipedia-grabus-bs}. His fifteen Slovenian years were crowned by the Ljubljana Muslim Cultural Centre, whose mosque opened on 7 February 2020{cite:wikipedia-grabus-bs}{cite:wikipedia-grabus-hr}.

In 2021 Grabus returned to Sarajevo as its mufti, appointed on 29 May and in office from 15 September{cite:wikipedia-grabus-bs}. Five years later, on 10 October 2026, the Assembly of the Islamic Community elected him Reis-ul-ulema of Bosnia and Herzegovina, the fourteenth holder of the office since 1882{cite:wikipedia-grabus-bs}{cite:wikidata-grabus}.
