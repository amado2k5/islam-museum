## Overview
Juzz ibn Mu'awiya, paternal uncle of al-Ahnaf ibn Qays. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He was in charge of Saraq, one of the districts of Khuzistan. He is the Juzz whom Abu al-Mukhtar mentions in his qasida. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
