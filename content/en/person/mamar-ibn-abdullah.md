## Summary
A Companion of Banu Adi ibn Ka'b; he emigrated to Abyssinia and came in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him alone of Banu Adi in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi ibn Ka'b]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/5}
