## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Nasr ibn Muzahim al-Minqari, of the generation of Abu Mikhnaf, of the Banu Minqar, and a perfume seller {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.293-294}.

## His books
Ibn al-Nadim mentions his books: "Siffin", "al-Jamal", "The Killing of Hujr ibn Adi", "The Killing of al-Husayn ibn Ali", and "al-Gharat" {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.293-294}.
