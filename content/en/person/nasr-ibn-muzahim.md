## Overview
Abu'l-Fadl Nasr ibn Muzahim al-Minqari, of the generation of Abu Mikhnaf, from Banu Minqar, a druggist by trade; author of books on Siffin, the Camel, the killing of Hujr ibn Adi, the killing of al-Husayn ibn Ali, and al-Gharat. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 315}
