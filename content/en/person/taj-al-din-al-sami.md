## Summary
Taj al-Din Abu Mansur 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Thabit al-Baghdadi al-Ma'muni al-Sam'i — named for al-Sam' ibn Malik, a clan of the Ansar — tailor, Qur'an reciter, Hanbali jurist and ascetic. Abu'l-Faraj ibn al-Hanbali said: he was our companion hearing Ibn al-Muni's lessons, and he reached a level of renunciation and worship of which it was said: it keeps Baghdad held fast; and he was gracious in company. He died Wednesday, 29 Sha'ban 596, and was buried at Bab Harb. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 807}
