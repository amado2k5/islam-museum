## In brief
Mu'tamir b. Sulayman, the imam, memoriser, and reliable Abu Muhammad al-Taymi of Basra, hadith-master of Basra; he narrated from his father and from Abd al-Malik b. Umayr, Mansur b. al-Mu'tamir, Humayd, Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani, al-Rukn b. al-Rabi', Layth b. Abi Sulaym, Amr b. Dinar al-Qahraman, and others, while Ahmad b. Hanbal, Ishaq, Yahya b. Ma'in, Abu Hafs al-Fallas, Khalifa b. Khayyat, Abu Kurayb, al-Hasan b. Arafa, Ya'qub al-Dawraqi, and a great number narrated from him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.249-250}

## Standing and death
Born in 106 AH, he was known for reliability, exactitude, worship, and scruple — Qurra b. Khalid said "Mu'tamir among us is not below Sulayman al-Taymi". He died in Safar of 187 AH, the day Rayyan al-Sulqi was killed, and people would say "today the servant of the people died and the worst of people was killed". {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.250}
