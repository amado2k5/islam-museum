## Summary
Ibn Ajurrum: grammarian, linked in the museum's record to the Maghreb. The Biblissima record places his life between 1273 and 1323. {cite:biblissima Q12169} The museum's record card names the Ajurrumiyya as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Muḥammad ibn Muḥammad al-Ṣanhāǧī Ibn Aǧurrūm» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q12169}
