## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Dawud al-Sanhaji, Abu Abd Allah, known as Ibn Ajurrum, was a grammarian famous for his treatise «al-Ajurrumiyya», which is printed and has been commented on by many {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. He was born and died in Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Works
He wrote «Fara'id al-Ma'ani fi Sharh Hirz al-Amani», known as the commentary on al-Shatibiyya; two volumes of it, the first and the second, which al-Zirikli thinks may be in his own hand, are in the library at Rabat {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}. The entry adds that he had other works and arjuzas {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## Dates
Al-A'lam gives his birth as 672 AH, equal to 1273 CE, and his death as 723 AH, equal to 1323 CE, in Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
