## Overview
Al-Fadl ibn Mahan, a client of the Banu Sama, who conquered Sindan and held it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What al-Baladhuri relates
He sent an elephant to al-Ma'mun, and prayed for him in a congregational mosque he built in Sindan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

After him his sons Muhammad and Mahan rose. One of them was killed and crucified, and the Indians overcame Sindan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
