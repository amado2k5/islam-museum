# Hassan Rouhani

Hassan Rouhani (born 12 November 1948) is an Iranian cleric and politician who served as president of Iran from 3 August 2013 to 3 August 2021, succeeding Mahmoud Ahmadinejad. The English article's lead records that he "has also served as a Shia mujtahid (a senior cleric)", and the Arabic article's lead calls him a Shia Islamic scholar in the same terms. {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-ar}

## Early life and formation

He was born Hassan Fereydoun at Sorkheh in Semnan province, began seminary studies there and at Qom, and later took a law degree at the University of Tehran and graduate degrees in Glasgow while already a figure of the revolutionary movement. The Persian article presents him as a politician and cleric who was the seventh president of the Islamic Republic. {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-fa} {cite:wikidata-rouhani}

## Career and presidency

Rouhani sat in the Majlis from 1980 to 2000, served as secretary of the Supreme National Security Council from 1989 to 2005, and was Iran's chief nuclear negotiator in 2003–2005. Elected president in 2013 and re-elected in 2017, his government concluded the 2015 nuclear agreement with the world powers; the Arabic article gives his presidency as running from 3 August 2013 to 3 August 2021. {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-en} {cite:wikipedia-rouhani-ar}
