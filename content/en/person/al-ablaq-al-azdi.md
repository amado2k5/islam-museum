## Overview
Al-Ablaq al-Azdi, one of the soothsayers of the Arabs according to al-Mas'udi. For al-Mas'udi a soothsayer ranks below a priest. He is mentioned together with al-Ahlaj al-Dahri and Urwa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.484-485}

His period and lineage need an additional source; they are not yet recorded.
