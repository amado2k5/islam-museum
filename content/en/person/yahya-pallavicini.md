## Summary
Yahya Sergio Yahe Pallavicini is an Italian imam, imam of the al-Wahid mosque in Milan and president of COREIS — the Italian Islamic Religious Community, one of the country's principal institutional Muslim bodies — and a long-serving adviser to the Italian government on Islam. {cite:muslim500-yahya-pallavicini}

## Life and formation
He was born in 1965 in Lausanne, Switzerland, into a Muslim family: his father, Shaykh Abd al-Wahid Pallavicini (1926–2017), was an Italian convert and Sufi shaykh of the Ahmadiyya-Idrisiyya order who led the al-Wahid mosque in Milan, and his mother was Japanese. He studied Islam at Sapienza University in Rome, at the Pontifical Institute of Arabic and Islamic Studies, at Muhammad V University in Rabat and at ISTAC in Kuala Lumpur. {cite:muslim500-yahya-pallavicini}

## Institutional roles
Vice-president and imam of COREIS and a member of the Great Mosque of Rome's assembly, he advised the Italian Ministry of the Interior on the Council for Italian Islam from 2005 to 2018, was named ISESCO ambassador for dialogue among civilisations in 2014, joined the European Council of Religious Leaders, chairs the European Muslim Scholars Council (EULEMA), and serves as international affairs adviser to Nahdlatul Ulama's general chairman. {cite:muslim500-yahya-pallavicini}

## Positions and debate
His profile is that of a European Muslim institution-builder in the Guénonian-Sufi register of his father's community: theological training, ecumenism and interfaith diplomacy inside European and Islamic institutions alike. Admirers describe a bridge between Western Muslim communities and traditional scholarship; critics within the community debate the weight of Sufi lineage bodies in representational politics. The museum records the assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-yahya-pallavicini}
