## Summary
An Ansari Companion of [[group/khazraj|the Khazraj]], of Banu al-Najjar; he witnessed the early battles with the Prophet and was martyred fighting in the Muslims' ranks in the [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn campaign]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/101}.

## His martyrdom
Ibn Ishaq counts him among the Ansar's martyrs on the day of Hunayn, where he fought in the Khazraj ranks and was martyred in the valley {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/101}.

## For young visitors
A Companion of Medina's people who fought bravely in the battle of Hunayn until he was martyred.
