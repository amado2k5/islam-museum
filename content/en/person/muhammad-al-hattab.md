## Summary
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Ru'ayni, known as al-Hattab, a Maliki jurist among the Sufi scholars. Of Moroccan origin, he became known in Mecca and died in Tripoli of the West. His works include Mawahib al-jalil fi sharh Mukhtasar Khalil in six volumes, a commentary on Ibn Ghazi's verse on the Qayrawani risala, and a treatise on finding prayer times by astronomical methods. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1620}
