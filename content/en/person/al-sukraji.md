## Summary
The imam al-Qarafi, shaykh of Karaj, Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Malik al-Karaji, known as al-Sukraji; he mastered the readings and heard much, authored *al-Fusul fi'l-i'tiqad* and the abridgement of *al-Dhara'i'*, and refuted the Mu'tazila in writing — a majestic imam. Born 458, died 532. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 583}
