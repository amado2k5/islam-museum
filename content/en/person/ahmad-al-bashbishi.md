## Summary
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Latif al-Bashbishi, Shihab al-Din, a Shafi'i scholar born in Bashbish in the Gharbiyya province. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2054}

## Works
He studied with al-Shabramallisi, gave fatwas and taught at al-Azhar, taught in Mecca, and wrote 'al-Tuhfa al-sunniyya'. He lived from 1040 to 1096 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2054}
