## Overview
Abd al-Nabi, who seized power in Zabid, and had cut off the Abbasid khutba there. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-53}

## The campaign of Turanshah
[[person/shams-al-dawla-turanshah|Shams al-Dawla Turanshah ibn Ayyub]] set out against him with the permission of Nur al-Din in Rajab 569 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-53} The people of Zabid who were with Turanshah broke away from him, so he went out to them with his army and their force was defeated; the attackers set ladders against the wall and took the city by force. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-53}

## Capture
He and his wife, known as al-Hurra, were taken prisoner. Ibn al-Athir describes her as a righteous woman who gave much charity to the poor among the pilgrims. Turanshah handed him over to Sayf al-Dawla Mubarak ibn Kamil al-Munqidhi to extract money from him, and he showed them where his father's treasures were buried. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-53}
