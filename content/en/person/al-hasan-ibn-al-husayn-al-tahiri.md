## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn, paternal uncle of Abd Allah ibn Tahir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fight against Mazyar
His nephew sent him with the men of Khurasan against Mazyar ibn Qarin. He was the one who devised the ambush by which Mazyar was taken, through the safe conduct that the source describes as deceitful. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/mazyar-revolt|the revolt of Mazyar]].
