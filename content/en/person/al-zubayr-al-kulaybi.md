## Overview
Al-Zubayr al-Kulaybi is the first named by the narrator among those who went in to al-Hadi at Sa'da to ask safe-conduct for the Akilis after the fighting, with [[person/al-walid-ibn-hayyan-al-jamai|al-Walid ibn Hayyan al-Jama'i]] and some Yarsamis. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} The name is read "al-Kulaybi" from the book's index, which lists him at p. 157 only. See [[event/safe-conduct-for-the-akilis-and-al-hadis-verses-from-saada-286]].
