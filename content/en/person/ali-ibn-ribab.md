## Overview
Ali ibn Ribab, whom al-Mas'udi describes as among the leading scholars of the Rafidis. He is the brother of al-Yaman ibn Ribab, whom he describes as a scholar of the Kharijites. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}

## The debate
Al-Mas'udi says that the two brothers met for three days each year to debate, and then parted. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.183}
