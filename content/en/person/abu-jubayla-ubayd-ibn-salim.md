## Overview
Abu Jubayla Ubayd ibn Salim ibn Malik ibn Salim al-Khazraji, of the Banu Ghadab ibn Jusham, of the [[group/al-khazraj|Khazraj]]. He was highly regarded by the kings of Ghassan in [[place/al-sham|Syria]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}

## His vow
When Malik ibn al-Ajlan fled to him after killing al-Futayun, he vowed to God that he would not touch perfume or approach women until he had humiliated the Jews, so that the Aws and Khazraj would be the most honoured people of Yathrib. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}

## Dhu Harad
He set out with a force and gave out that he meant Yemen. He camped at Dhu Harad and summoned the notables of the Jews, showing them kindness. He admitted them one man at a time and killed them to the last. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}

The Aws and Khazraj thus became the most honoured people of Medina, and he returned to Syria. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}
