## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad, of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, was put by [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] over [[place/najran|Najran]] when he left it in Ramadan 284 AH; the narrator calls him scrupulous, learned, chaste, pious and just in his rulings, and says al-Hadi charged him to fear God, enjoin good and forbid wrong, apply the penalties and take the right from whoever owed it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} In his term a man of Yam was flogged for drinking and died, and a thief proved by witnesses lost his hand. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} The description is the Zaydi narrator's and not the museum's. See [[event/al-hadi-expedition-to-daa-of-shakir-and-return-to-saada-284]].
