## Summary
Egyptian short-story writer, novelist and playwright (1927–1991), a master of the modern Arabic short story. He studied medicine then turned to journalism and literature; his collections include The Cheapest Nights, his plays Al-Farafeer and The Intertwined, and his novels include The Sinners (Al-Haram). {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

## Life and career

Yusuf Idris was born in 1927 in the village of al-Bayrum in Sharqiyya governorate, Egypt, graduated from Cairo University's Faculty of Medicine in 1951 and worked as a physician and health inspector for years before turning to full-time writing for Egyptian newspapers. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

He edited at al-Gumhuriya and then al-Ahram, writing columns, stories and plays until he stood among the pillars of Egyptian literature in the second half of the twentieth century, his first profession remaining visible behind many of his characters. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

## Literary works

He began with the 1954 collection The Cheapest Nights, which drew critics' attention with its village realism, followed by collections such as Isn't It So, The Siren and House of Flesh, and wrote novels including The Sinners (Al-Haram), The Fault and The White Woman. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

In theatre he forged a distinctly Egyptian form in plays such as Al-Farafeer, The Earthly Farce, The Intertwined and The Third Sex, blending folklore and social criticism in a language close to a disciplined colloquial. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

## Standing and legacy

Yusuf Idris is regarded as the founder of a school in the Arabic short story: he brought literary language close to everyday life and probed the depths of Egyptian village and city, critics describing him as the Egyptian writer most penetrating into the nature of the Egyptian person. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}

He died in London in 1991 and was brought home for burial; his works won wide translation and prizes, his plays are still staged and his stories still taught in Arab universities. {cite:wikipedia-yusuf-idris}
