## Summary
Zayd ibn Abdallah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, a Medinan Successor of the family of Umar ibn al-Khattab, born during his grandfather's caliphate; al-Taqrib grades him "trustworthy, of the second layer" {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p.212}.

## His entry in Taqrib al-Tahdhib
Ibn Hajar writes in al-Taqrib: Zayd ibn Abdallah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab — trustworthy, of the second layer, born during his grandfather's caliphate; al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Maja narrate from him {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p.212}. The Kashif al-Dhahabi material embedded in this edition adds: he transmitted from his father, and Nafi' the client of Ibn Umar and his grandson Umar ibn Muhammad transmitted from him {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p.212}.

## His place in the house of Umar
He grew up in a house of learning in Quraysh: his father Abdallah ibn Umar was among the most prolific hadith transmitters of the Companions, and his grandfather was Umar ibn al-Khattab; being born during his grandfather's caliphate makes him one of the older sons of Abdallah ibn Umar {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p.212}.

## For younger visitors
Zayd ibn Abdallah ibn Umar was the grandson of the caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab; he was born while his grandfather ruled, grew up in a house of learning, and narrated from his father while the people of Medina narrated from him.

## Notes for researchers
- Taqrib al-Tahdhib, entry 2143 (Majmu'at al-kutub scan, p. 212).
- The entry is brief as is al-Taqrib's custom; the detail is in Tahdhib al-Tahdhib and Ibn Sa'd's Tabaqat.
- "Of the second layer" means the second rank of transmitters in Ibn Hajar's scheme.
