## Overview
'Abd al-Qadir b. al-Shaykh 'Aydarus, author of al-Nur al-safir 'an akhbar al-qarn al-'ashir — Ibn al-'Imad's largest source for this century's tarjamas; born on the night of Thursday 20 Rabi' I 978. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 387}
