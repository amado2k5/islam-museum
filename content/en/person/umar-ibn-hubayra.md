## Overview
Umar ibn Hubayra al-Fazari, governor of Iraq for Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.190}.

## In al-Mas'udi
In the year 103 AH he sent for [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], al-Sha'bi and Ibn Sirin. al-Mas'udi reports that Ibn Sirin and al-Sha'bi spoke words with an element of caution, and that al-Hasan admonished him and ordered him to fear God. Then he gave them all gifts, and he reduced al-Hasan's gift {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.190}.

## In al-Baladhuri and al-Salabi
al-Baladhuri mentions the bridge of Kufa that was built in his governorship. al-Salabi's book reports that he was given charge of al-Jazira in the year 100 AH, and raided the Byzantines from the side of Armenia. Then he was appointed over Iraq and Khurasan, and then [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] dismissed him and imprisoned him. He fled and sought the protection of Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik, and died around 107 AH {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.386} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.435}.
