## Overview
Yahya ibn Muhammad ibn Idris ibn Idris al-Hasani was a king of the Idrisids, whom al-Zirikli calls the holders of Marrakesh (his word for Morocco); his capital was Fez, and he died in 250 AH, 864 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}. He came to power after the death of his brother Ali in 234 AH, by Ali's designation, and his conduct was good {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.

## Building
He was fond of building: in Fez he built baths and inns, and its people took to building in his time {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}. People came to the city from al-Andalus, Ifriqiya and the rest of the Maghrib until it was too small for its inhabitants, so the suburbs (arbad) were built outside it; the Qarawiyyin mosque was built in his days {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}.

## Death
He died in Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 133}. The entry gives no year of birth; zeros are printed in its place.
