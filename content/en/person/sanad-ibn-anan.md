## Overview
Sanad ibn Anan al-Azdi, Abu Ali, a virtuous Maliki jurist among the ascetic scholars, who died in Alexandria in 541 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3488}

## Works
His al-Tiraz, a commentary on the Mudawwana, was left unfinished. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3488}
