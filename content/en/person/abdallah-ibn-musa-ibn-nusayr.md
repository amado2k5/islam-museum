## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Musa ibn Nusayr is the son of [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]], the conqueror of the Maghrib. Al-Baladhuri says that he was accused of killing Yazid ibn Abi Muslim and of stirring the people against him, so Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi struck his neck, in the context of [[event/killing-of-yazid-ibn-abi-muslim|the killing of Yazid ibn Abi Muslim]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.324}
