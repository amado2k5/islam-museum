## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Abdi al-Khurasani, a transmitter of reports. *Muruj al-Dhahab* relates that the caliph al-Qahir was alone with him and lunged at him with a spear and missed him. Then he asked him to describe the caliphs of the Abbasids from al-Saffah onward. He related of each caliph his character and his firsts, such as the translation of books and the employment of freedmen. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 668-670}
