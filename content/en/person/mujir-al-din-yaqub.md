## Overview
Mujir al-Din Ya'qub, son of al-Malik al-'Adil, styled al-Malik al-Mu'izz; a man of merit — Abu Ruh al-Harawi and a company gave him ijaza; he died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 654 and was buried in the turbah beside his father {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}.
