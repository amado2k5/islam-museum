## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn Hassan ibn Sarar al-Dabbi. Al-Baladhuri relates that he disputed sharply with Jarir ibn Abd Allah over the killing of Mihran on the day of al-Buwayb. Al-Mundhir took the region of Mihran, and Jarir took the rest of his spoils. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Note: the museum has another entry under a close name, [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-hassan|al-Mundhir ibn Hassan ibn Dirar]], in which al-Baladhuri mentions Mihran and the day of al-Nukhayla. The two may be one person, and this needs review.
