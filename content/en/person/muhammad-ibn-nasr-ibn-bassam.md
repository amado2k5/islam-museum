## Overview
Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Nasr ibn Bassam, a man of the utmost nobility and chivalry, luxurious, well-dressed and enamoured of building; al-Qummi describes his reception dome faced with Armenian clay, a ten-cubit fire-basin of gold filled with glowing embers, and his table of gold-streaked agate.
