## Overview
Dinar, a dihqan (landed notable) of Nihawand, whose story is recorded by al-Baladhuri in "The Conquests of the Lands". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.429}

## The settlement
According to al-Baladhuri, he asked for safe conduct for Simak ibn Ubayd al-Absi after Simak had killed eight of his companions. He asked to go to their commander to reach a settlement with him over the land and to pay the poll tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.429}

Hudhayfa made a settlement with him over the land tax and the poll tax, and granted safety to the people of his city. Nihawand came to be called Mah Dinar, and he would go to Simak to bring him gifts and show him kindness. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.429}
