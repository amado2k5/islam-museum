## Summary
Ibn al-Khayr al-'Azji, the hafiz and muqri'; born 553, he heard from Abu'l-Hasan al-Qabisi, Ibn al-Baji, Ibn 'Atiyya and others, deputised in the judgeship at Balda and oversaw the awqaf — known for piety, truthfulness and learning. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 248-249}
