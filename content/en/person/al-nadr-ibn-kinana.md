## Summary
He is 'Quraysh' in the genealogists' definition: Quraysh are the sons of al-Nadr ibn Kinana, and whoever draws close to his line is Qurashi; father of Malik ibn al-Nadr, and of Kinana, Abd Manat, Milkan and Qind {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48}.

## His place in the lineage
The Qurashi reckoning runs through al-Nadr's sons: Malik's line is Quraysh proper, while the other sons carry the Kinani clans that are Qurashi in the wider, 'lesser Quraysh' sense of the genealogists {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4} {cite:ansab-al-ashraf}.

## For younger visitors
al-Nadr ibn Kinana is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
