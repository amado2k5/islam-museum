## Overview
Abu al-Su'ud Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Mustafa al-Imadi al-Iskilibi, mufti of the Ottoman state, known as Khoja Chelebi. He is the author of the commentary "Irshad al-Aql al-Salim", and was so well known that he was called "the preacher of the commentators". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.348-349}
