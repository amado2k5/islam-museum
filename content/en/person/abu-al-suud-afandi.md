## Overview
Abu al-Su'ud Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Mustafa al-Imadi al-Iskilibi, mufti of the Ottoman state, known as Khoja Chelebi. He is the author of the commentary "Irshad al-Aql al-Salim", and was so well known that he was called "the preacher of the commentators". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.348-349}

## In Mehmed Süreyya's Sicill-i Osmani
In the [[work/sicill-i-osmani|Sicill-i Osmani]], [[person/mehmed-sureyya|Mehmed Süreyya]] gives an entry to Ebussuud Mehmed el-İmadi Efendi, son of Mustafa of the village of Müderris (İskilip). {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} He records that he was born on 17 Safar 896 (30 December 1490), became a madrasa teacher in 922, judge of Bursa in Shawwal 932, judge of Istanbul in Rabi' al-Akhir 940, military judge (*kazasker*) of Rumelia in Rabi' al-Awwal 944, and from that post shaykh al-Islam in Sha'ban 955 (September 1548). {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105}

He died on 5 Jumada al-Ula 983 (12 August 1575) aged 87, having been shaykh al-Islam for 27 years and 9 months, with 51 years in all of judicial office. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} Muhyi al-Din Sinan Efendi led his funeral prayer; he was buried on the Eyüp road at the site of his school, and the scholars of the two Holy Cities prayed for him in absentia when they heard of his death. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105}

Süreyya says he applied the Ottoman *kanun* fully in accordance with the Shari'a, and that he was famous for having issued 1,413 fatwas in one day and 1,412 on another. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} He notes that the stipend of the shaykh al-Islam was raised in his time from 200 akçe to 500, with another 100 added when he finished his Qur'an commentary. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} He judges that commentary, [[work/irshad-al-aql-al-salim|Irshad al-Aql al-Salim]], more esteemed than al-Baydawi's and the *Kashshaf*, and records that two copies were sent to the Holy Cities with permission for students to copy them. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} He mentions his dispute with Civizade over cash endowments, which Civizade held must be abolished by the Shari'a. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105}

**Conflicting dates.** The Sicill gives his birth in Safar 896 and death in Jumada al-Ula 983, while *Shadharat al-Dhahab* gives his birth in 898 and his death at the start of Jumada al-Ula 982. {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 2 PDF p. 105} {cite:shadharat-dhahab} Both are kept.
