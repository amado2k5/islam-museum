## Overview
Veliyyuddin ibn Mustafa al-Yenishehri al-Qustantini, Abu Abdallah, called Jarullah al-Rumi, was a Hanafi scholar whom al-Zirikli calls a man of learning. He gives his death as 1151 AH, 1738 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 118-119}.

## Life
He was born at Yenishehir, which the Turks write Yenisehir, and stayed beside the sanctuary at Mecca for seven years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 118-119}. He then settled in Istanbul, where he built a madrasa and a library near the Fatih mosque and was buried in the madrasa; after his death the library was moved to the mosque of Sultan Bayezid {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 118-119}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote in Arabic and names Fada'il al-Jihad; al-Sab' al-Sayyara al-Nuriyya, a gloss on al-Fawa'id al-Fanariyya on the Isagoge, in logic; a commentary on Adab al-Birkawi; a gloss on the commentary of al-Baydawi on the Qur'an; and a gloss on Sharh al-Maqasid {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 pp. 118-119}.
