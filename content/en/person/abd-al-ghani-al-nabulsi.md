## Overview
Abd al-Ghani ibn Isma'il ibn Abd al-Ghani al-Nabulsi was, in al-Zirikli's words, a poet, a scholar of religion and literature, a prolific author and a Sufi. He was born in 1050 AH, 1641 CE, and died in 1143 AH, 1731 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His life and travels
He was born and grew up in Damascus, travelled to Baghdad and returned to Syria, moved about in Palestine and Lebanon, journeyed to Egypt and the Hijaz, then settled in Damascus and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His writings
Al-Zirikli says his writings are very many and names, among them: "al-Hadra al-Unsiyya fi al-Rihla al-Qudsiyya", printed; "Ta'tir al-Anam fi Ta'bir al-Manam", printed; "Dhakha'ir al-Mawarith fi al-Dalala 'ala Mawadi' al-Ahadith", printed; "'Ilm al-Falaha", printed; "Nafahat al-Azhar 'ala Nasamat al-Ashar", printed; "Idah al-Dalalat fi Sama' al-Alat", printed; "Dhayl Nafhat al-Rayhana", in manuscript; "Hullat al-Dhahab al-Ibriz fi al-Rihla ila Ba'labakk wa-Biqa' al-'Aziz", in manuscript; "al-Haqiqa wa-al-Majaz fi Rihlat al-Sham wa-Misr wa-al-Hijaz", in manuscript; "Qala'id al-Marjan fi 'Aqa'id Ahl al-Iman", in manuscript; "Jawahir al-Nusus", printed; "Sharh Anwar al-Tanzil li-al-Baydawi", in manuscript; "Kifayat al-Mustafid fi 'Ilm al-Tajwid", in manuscript; "al-Iqtisad fi al-Nutq bi-al-Dad", in manuscript; "Munajat al-Hakim wa-Muna'at al-Qadim", in manuscript; "Khamrat al-Han", printed; "Khamrat Babil wa-Ghina' al-Balabil", in manuscript; "Diwan al-Haqa'iq", printed; "al-Rihla al-Hijaziyya wa-al-Riyad al-Unsiyya", printed; "Kanz al-Haqq al-Mubin fi Ahadith Sayyid al-Mursalin", in manuscript; "al-Sulh bayna al-Ikhwan fi Hukm Ibahat al-Dukhan", printed; "Sharh al-Muqaddima al-Sanusiyya", in manuscript; "Rashahat al-Aqlam fi Sharh Kifayat al-Ghulam", printed; "Diwan al-Dawawin", in manuscript; "Kashf al-Satr 'an Fardiyyat al-Witr", printed; "Lama'at al-Anwar fi al-Maqtu' lahum bi-al-Janna wa-al-Maqtu' lahum bi-al-Nar", printed; "Khams Majmu'at", in manuscript. The "Khams Majmu'at" hold thirty-two treatises, whose names al-Ziyat listed in "Khaza'in al-Kutub" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
