## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd al-Qadir al-Rumi al-Qubrusi, that is of Cyprus, was a Sufi of the Rum lands; the entry gives his death as 1043 AH, 1633 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names one work, "Majalis al-Abrar wa Masalik al-Akhyar", printed, in which he explained one hundred hadiths in one hundred sessions {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 148}. The entry is short and says nothing more.
