## Overview
Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi, whom the people of Baghdad gave the oath of allegiance to the caliphate in Muharram 202 AH, after he had been proclaimed at the end of 201 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.555} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.557}

## Deposition and concealment
In 203 AH the people of Baghdad deposed him, and then he went into hiding. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.570} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl p.571}

## Reports in al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi relates that [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]] invited him to visit at al-Raqqa, and that a joke passed between them on the deck of a boat. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.350-351}

He also relates the satire of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] against him, in which Shi'ism appears and Ibn Shaklah appears as Sunni; that is, the matter is attributed to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.399-400}

From the story of his concealment in Baghdad: he took refuge with a black cupper, who sheltered him, and Ibn al-Mahdi sang to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.425-426}

These are literary reports from a single source, and they need an additional source.
