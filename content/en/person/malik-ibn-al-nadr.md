## Summary
Father of Fihr ibn Malik, ancestor of Quraysh proper; a link in the agreed lineage between al-Nadr ibn Kinana and Fihr {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## His place in the lineage
Malik's line continues through Fihr, with other Qurashi clans from his other sons; Quraysh of the hollows and the outskirts are confined to Fihr's descendants {cite:tarikh-al-tabari} {cite:ansab-al-ashraf}.

## For younger visitors
Malik ibn al-Nadr is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
