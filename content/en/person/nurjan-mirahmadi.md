# Nurjan Mirahmadi

## Summary
Nurjan Mirahmadi is a Naqshbandi Sufi teacher based in Vancouver, Canada. His YouTube channel, The Muhammadan Way, carries his weekly live discourses, and his own descriptions present it as his official channel. {cite:youtube-muhammadan-way} His resume records that he has served as a spiritual guide of the Naqshbandi Sufi Order since 1995 and founded the Sufi Meditation Centers Society in Vancouver in 2007. {cite:nurmuhammad-nurjan-mirahmadi-resume}

## Work
Mirahmadi studied business management at the University of Southern California, graduating in 1990, then turned to religious study and service; his resume lists classical Islam and Sufi studies in 1995 at the Haqqani Institute in Lefke, Cyprus, and written ijaza from Muhammad Nazim al-Haqqani, Muhammad Hisham Kabbani and Muhammad Adil ar-Rabbani. {cite:nurmuhammad-nurjan-mirahmadi-resume} He founded the Naqshbandi Islamic Center of Vancouver and the Fatima Zahra Helping Hand charity, hosts the weekly Divine Love television series, and has written books including A Timeless Reality (2021) and Levels of the Heart (2017). {cite:nurmuhammad-nurjan-mirahmadi-resume} {cite:shaykh-ai-nurjan-mirahmadi}

## Positions and faith
This section should be read as a presentation of what the sources give, not as a verdict of the museum.

His personal Sufi affiliation is attested by two published sources about him personally: his official resume describes him as a Sufi scholar authorised by Naqshbandi leaders to teach, with written ijaza in the Naqshbandi chain, and the Shaykh.ai team profile independently records that he received ijaza to impart Sufi teachings, teaches the Sufi sciences and operates a full-time Sufi centre in Vancouver; on that basis his faith is recorded as sufi. No source consulted names a legal school (madhhab) for him, so no school value is recorded. {cite:nurmuhammad-nurjan-mirahmadi-resume} {cite:shaykh-ai-nurjan-mirahmadi}
