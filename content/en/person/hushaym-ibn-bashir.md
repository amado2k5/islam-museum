## Summary
Hushaym ibn Bashir al-Sulami al-Wasiti, Abu Mu'awiya, hadith scholar of Baghdad, commentator and jurist, trustworthy and exact, though he often omitted intermediaries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2073}

## Works
He transmitted from al-Zuhri; Malik, Shu'ba and al-Thawri transmitted from him; his works include a Qur'an commentary, a book of Sunan and a book of readings; he lived from 104 to 183 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2073}

## His commentary in al-Tabari's Jami' al-Bayan
The thesis says [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] used the commentary of Abu Mu'awiya Hushaym ibn Bashir al-Sulami as transmitted by Ya'qub ibn Ibrahim al-Dawraqi (d. 252 AH), and its footnote describes him as exegete, historian and hadith scholar, teacher of [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]], who died in 183 AH. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.50}
