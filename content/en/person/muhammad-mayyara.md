## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad, Abu Abd Allah, known as Mayyara, was a Maliki jurist from Fes. Al-Zirikli gives his years as 999 to 1072 AH, 1590 to 1662 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books al-Itqan wa-l-Ihkam fi Sharh Tuhfat al-Hukkam, two parts, printed; and al-Durr al-Thamin fi Sharh Manzumat al-Murshid al-Mu'in, on jurisprudence, printed, known as 'Mayyara the Elder' to distinguish it from a summary of his called 'Mayyara the Younger'. Others are Tanbih al-Mughtaribin ala Hurmat al-Tafriqa bayn al-Muslimin, and Takmil al-Manhaj lil-Zaqqaq, a verse composition (urjuza), in manuscript in the library at Rabat {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
