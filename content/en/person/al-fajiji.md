## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Jabbar ibn Ahmad, Abu Ishaq al-Fajiji, was a Moroccan jurist and man of letters. Al-Zirikli gives his death as about 920 AH, about 1514 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 45}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists two works: Rawdat al-Sulwan, printed, and a verse composition on the foundations of Islam, in manuscript at Tamkrut {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 45}.
