## Overview
Abu Isa is a Kufan of al-Hadi's army who grew too heavy to run at [[place/sawhan-najran|Sawhan]] until the enemy were on him; al-Hadi called to him to go on because an enemy was behind him, he said he was worn out, and al-Hadi charged the Harithi who was about to strike him and speared him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.162} The index lists "Abu Isa al-Kufi" at p. 161 only. See [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]].
