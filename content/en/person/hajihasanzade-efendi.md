## Overview
Muhammad ibn Mustafa ibn al-Hajj Hasan, known as Haji Hasan-zade, was a Hanafi jurist learned in Qur'anic exegesis, counted by al-Zirikli among the Turks who adopted Arabic. He gives his death as 911 AH, 1505 CE, and no birth year {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 99}.

## Career
He taught in several madrasas of Bursa and Istanbul {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 99}. He held judgeships in the time of Sultan Mehmed Khan and of his son Bayezid {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 99}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli names a gloss on al-Baydawi's commentary on Surat al-An'am; a work of adjudication between al-Dawani and al-Sadr al-Shirazi (Muhakama); and Mizan al-Tasrif, on morphology {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 99}.
