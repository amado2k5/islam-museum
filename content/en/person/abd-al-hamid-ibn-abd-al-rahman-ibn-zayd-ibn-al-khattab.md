## Overview
Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz appointed him commander of Kufa. Al-Salabi, quoting «Siyar a'lam al-nubala'», describes him as a trustworthy imam and a just leader, who transmitted little but was of great standing. He died in 115 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.387}
