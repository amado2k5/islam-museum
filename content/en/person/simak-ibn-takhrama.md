## Overview
Simak ibn Takhrama ibn Humayn al-Asadi, of the Banu al-Halik ibn Amr ibn Asad. al-Baladhuri reports that the mosque of Simak in Kufa is attributed to him. al-Akhtal satirized him over his building of a house for his family, and said to him: "Woe to you, what has made you helpless? You wanted to praise me and you satirized me" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His lineage and reports
al-Halik was the first to work iron, and his descendants were taunted for it. Simak had fled from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] out of Kufa and settled in al-Raqqa {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
