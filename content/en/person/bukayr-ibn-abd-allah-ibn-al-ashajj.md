## Overview
Bukayr ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Ashajj, a jurist of Egypt. He was among those who transferred to [[person/marthad-ibn-abd-allah-al-yazani|Marthad ibn Abd Allah al-Yazani]]. Ibn Wahb relates that whenever Malik mentioned Bukayr he said: "He was one of the scholars." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

The data do not give the dates of his life, which need an additional source.
