## Overview
Hasan ibn Turkhan ibn Dawud ibn Ya'qub al-Aqhisari, called Hasan Kafi and famous as Kafi, was a jurist and researcher from Bosnia. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 951 AH, 1544 CE, and his death as 1025 AH, 1616 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 194}.

## Life
He was born in the town of Aqhisar, became its judge and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 194}. He was educated in Istanbul (al-Asitana) and mastered the three languages, Arabic, Turkish and Persian {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 194}. Al-Zirikli describes him as pious, austere and given to much fasting; he disliked the heads of the Sufi orders of his time and rebuked them with arguments from the Shari'a, and he is quoted as saying that if saintly marvels could be gained by discipline he would have gained them; he took part in campaigns as preacher and as fighter {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 194}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists his Arabic works: Samt al-Wusul ila Ilm al-Usul with its commentary; Rawdat al-Jannat fi Usul al-I'tiqadat (printed), wrongly attributed to al-Birkawi; Tamhis al-Talkhis on rhetoric, in which he revised al-Khatib al-Qazwini's Talkhis; Usul al-Hikam fi Nizam al-Alam (printed), translated into Turkish, German, French and Bosnian; a commentary on Mukhtasar al-Quduri in four parts; a commentary on Ibn al-Hajib's Kafiya in grammar; a treatise on the word Chelebi; and Nizam al-Ulama' ila Khatam al-Anbiya' (manuscript), which gives the chain of his teachers in law up to Abu Hanifa and from him to the Messenger of God, with a good biography of each {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 194}.
