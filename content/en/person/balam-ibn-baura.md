## Summary
Bal'am was one of the scholars of Bani Israel, as Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy mentions him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:07:30}. The Sheikh says that he travelled a long road in knowledge and guidance, so that when he deviated his deviation was equally long {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:08:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 03)

The Sheikh mentions that the verse he is discussing came after the story of Bal'am, one of the scholars of Bani Israel {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:07:30}.

He says that Bal'am travelled a long road in knowledge and guidance, so that when he deviated his deviation was equally long, even though God had given him a wide share of knowledge and guidance {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:08:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lectures on Sharh Kitab al-Fawa'id

The Sheikh cites the story of Bal'am ibn Ba'ura as a warning that knowledge can deceive its holder, and says that he disobeyed God and stayed on the earth (needs_source: a story the lecture gives without a source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:27:30}
