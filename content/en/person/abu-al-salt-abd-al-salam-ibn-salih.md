## Overview
Abu al-Salt Abd al-Salam ibn Salih, one of the intimates of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. Al-Suyuti relates that he spent a night with him, and the attendant in charge of the lamp fell asleep, so al-Ma'mun rose and set it right himself, saying: "Perhaps I am in the ablution room, and the servants revile me without knowing that I hear them, and I pardon them." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.503}
