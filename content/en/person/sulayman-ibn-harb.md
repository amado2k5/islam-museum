## In brief
Sulayman b. Harb, the imam and memoriser Abu Ayyub al-Washhi of Basra, judge of Mecca; Yahya b. Aktham described him before al-Ma'mun, saying "he is reliable and a memoriser of hadith", whereupon al-Ma'mun ordered him brought to court, and Ya'qub b. Shayba said "he was reliable, firm, a master of memory". {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.359-360}

## His death
He died in 224 AH — a man of biographical record and rank — and Affan used to honour him; when he was mentioned before Ali b. al-Madini, Ali set about praising him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.360}
