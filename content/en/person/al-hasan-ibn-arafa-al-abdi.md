## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Arafa al-Abdi of Baghdad, a hadith scholar learned in the Arab days and in biography (d. 257 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3485}

## Transmission
He narrated from Ammar ibn Muhammad, Isa ibn Yunus and Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash; al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3485}
