## Summary
Hamid ibn al-Musayyab ibn al-Husayn, the muezzin of the main mosque, called Shadhah. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 674, sequence 675, on page 394. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394}

## His prayers and his reports
He was known for prayers that were answered, and was among the trustworthy and sincere. He narrated from Sulayman ibn Harb and Azhar ibn Sa'id. The name is read in the scan as al-Musbud and al-Musbur; al-Musayyab is the likely reading and is flagged as not confirmed. Abu Nu'aym gives, through a chain from Muhammad ibn Abi Hurayra, a report about a good deed: if a servant intends it and does not do it, it is recorded as one; if he does it, it is recorded as up to seven hundred times. The wording is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/394}
