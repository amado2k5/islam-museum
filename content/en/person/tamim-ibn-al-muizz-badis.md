## Overview
Ibn Badis, lord of Ifriqiya — the sultan Abu Yahya Tamim ibn al-Mu'izz ibn Badis ibn al-Mansur al-Himyari al-Sanhaji; born in 422 AH, he governed al-Mahdiyya for his father from 445, then acceded months later on al-Mu'izz's death. A brave, awe-inspiring, well-ruling, learned and generous poet-prince, his days extended until he died in 501 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1264}
