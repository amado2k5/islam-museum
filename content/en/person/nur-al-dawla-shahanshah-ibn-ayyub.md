## Summary
Amir Nur al-Dawla Shahanshah ibn Najm al-Din Ayyub ibn Shadhi ibn Marwan, brother of Sultan Salah al-Din and the eldest of the brothers. He was father of 'Izz al-Din Farrukhshah — father of al-Malik al-Amjad, lord of Baalbek — and of al-Malik al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din 'Umar, lord of Hama, and of al-'Adhra', founder of the 'Adhrawiyya madrasa at Damascus. He was killed in the battle in which the Franks massed — seven hundred thousand horse and foot, it is said — at the gate of Damascus. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
