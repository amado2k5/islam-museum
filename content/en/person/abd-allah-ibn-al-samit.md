## Summary
Abd Allah ibn al-Samit, Abu al-Nadr al-Ghifari al-Basri, a Follower and a hadith transmitter. He narrated from his paternal uncle Abu Dhar, Umar, Uthman, al-Hakam, Hudhayfa, Ibn Umar and Aisha, and Humayd ibn Hilal, Abu al-Aliya al-Bara, Abu Imran al-Jawni and Abu Abd Allah al-Harbi narrated from him. Al-Nasa'i and Ibn Sa'd call him trustworthy, al-Ijli a trustworthy Basran Follower, and Abu Hatim says his hadith is written down {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13759}. The book gives his death between the seventies and eighties AH.
