## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Hasan ibn Ishaq al-Tunisi, Abu Ishaq, a Maliki jurist and usuli who studied with Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman and Abu Imran al-Fasi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2056}

## Works
He taught and was consulted in Qayrawan, suffered a severe trial in 437 AH, and wrote glosses on Ibn al-Mawwaz and on the Mudawwana. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2057}
