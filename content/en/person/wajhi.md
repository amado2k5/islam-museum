## Summary
Wajhi was a poet of Golconda who composed the [[work/qutb-mushtari|Qutb Mushtari]], a long poem in Deccani Urdu, in 1609–10, with Sultan [[person/muhammad-quli-qutb-shah|Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah]] as hero of a love story with Mushtari, princess of Bengal. {cite:zebrowski-deccani-painting-1983 p. 155}

## His work in context
Mark Zebrowski sets the poem beside [[person/zuhuri|Zuhuri]]'s prose at Bijapur as examples of a Deccani literature that praises and idealises rather than records, and thinks a translation would help in understanding Golconda painting. {cite:zebrowski-deccani-painting-1983 p. 155} {cite:zebrowski-deccani-painting-1983 p. 156}
