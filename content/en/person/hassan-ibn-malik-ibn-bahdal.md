## Overview
Hassan ibn Malik ibn Bahdal, lord of the Kalb. Al-Mas'udi reports that he refused to give the oath of allegiance to Ibn al-Zubayr on the pulpit of Tiberias in the land of Jordan, and that he wanted it for Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. Ibn al-Zubayr was proclaimed in the Friday sermon on all the pulpits of Islam except his own. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 79}
