Abu Uthman Ikrama ibn Abi Jahl Amr ibn Hisham al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi, an eminent Companion and a daring knight, once the fiercest foe of Islam in the Jahiliyya under his father's influence. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He fought the Muslims at Badr, Uhud and the Trench, and tried to repel the conquest army at al-Khandama; when Mecca was taken he fled to Yemen. His wife Umm Hakim bint al-Harith followed after becoming Muslim and securing him the Prophet's safe-conduct — so he returned repentant and remorseful. When the Prophet saw him he sprang toward him in joy, saying: "welcome to the emigrant rider". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

His Islam became good; he gave his soul in God's path, making amends for what had passed — he would place the mushaf on his face and weep, saying "my Lord's book! my Lord's word!" — and led armies in the Ridda wars in Oman and Mahra, and was martyred a charging hero in the glorious battle of Yarmuk in Syria after leading the battalion of death. {cite:tabari-donner-1993}
