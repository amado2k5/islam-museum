## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Asadi, known as Shaykh al-Sammaki al-Makki, a Meccan scholar of the eleventh century AH who grew up in Mecca. He was born around 1035 AH (1625 CE) and died around 1066 AH (1656 CE). Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
