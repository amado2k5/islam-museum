## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Habish al-Ansari al-Miri, the last of the memorisers of al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.467}

## At the fortress of Almería
Al-Maqqari relays his words: "I was in the fortress of Almería when it was taken, and I said to the lord of the Romans, who was the grandson of Alfonso, that I could trace his lineage to Heraclius." He told him this, and the lord said to him: "Go out, you and your family and those with you, freed without anything." {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.467}

## His pupils
He was the teacher of Ibn Dihya, of [[person/ibn-hawtallah|Ibn Hawtallah]], and of Abu al-Rabi' al-Kala'i. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.467}
