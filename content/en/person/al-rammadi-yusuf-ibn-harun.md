## Overview
Abu 'Umar Yusuf b. Harun al-Kindi, known as al-Rammadi — the famous poet of Cordoba, ranked above its poets; he transmitted from Abu 'Ali al-Qali the Kitab al-nawadir of his own composition, and Abu 'Umar Ibn 'Abd al-Barr took from him; al-Humaydi counts him in the Jadhwa with Imru' al-Qays and al-Mutanabbi among those who opened poetry through Kinda. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
