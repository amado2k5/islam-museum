# Aida Azlin

## Summary
Aida Azlin is a Singaporean writer residing in Morocco, known for the Tuesday Love Letters, the weekly newsletter she has written for Muslim women around the world for more than eight years, and as founder of AA (later AA Plus), an ad-free online community for sisters. She also hosts The Aida Azlin Show podcast and vlogged on YouTube. {cite:aidaazlin-website} {cite:halaltrip-aida-azlin-profile}

## Background
Azlin graduated from Nanyang Technological University's School of Art, Design and Media in Singapore. She first became known for The Shawl Label, a brand selling ethical shawls to women, which has since closed; her focus moved to AA, whose community HalalTrip put at about 160,000 readers, sustained by courses and products so that the letters themselves can stay free and sponsor-free. A profile by Rabata's Ribaat blog describes her as a Singaporean residing in Morocco running the AA Plus community. {cite:halaltrip-aida-azlin-profile} {cite:aidaazlin-website}

## Writing
In her own description she is "a writer, entrepreneur, and a proud believer" committed to serving her readers. Her letters take up topical questions, reflection and everyday spirituality, and her podcast holds long conversations with writers and community figures; she shuttles between Singapore, Morocco and London, and her community's testimonials credit the letters with reconnecting women to their faith and to themselves. {cite:aidaazlin-website} {cite:halaltrip-aida-azlin-profile}
