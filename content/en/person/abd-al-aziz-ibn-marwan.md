## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan ibn al-Hakam, brother of Abd al-Malik. His father appointed him governor of Egypt in 65 AH, and he remained there for about twenty years until 85 AH. He was one of the pillars of his brother's state. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.31} {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.41}

Al-Salabi describes his dispute with Hassan ibn al-Nu'man over the governorship of Barqa and Tripoli, and then how Hassan was removed from Ifriqiya and [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]] was appointed in his place. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.62-63} He also notes that Abd al-Aziz was the most generous of the Umayyad princes, and that a thousand bowls were set around his house every day. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.159-160}
