## Overview
Mahmud ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Khattab al-Subki, Abu Muhammad, was a Maliki jurist trained at al-Azhar. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 1274 AH, 1857 CE, and his death to 1352 AH, 1933 CE, in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 186}.

## His life
He was born at Sibk al-Ahad, a village of Ashmun in Minufiyya. He studied at al-Azhar as a grown man and taught there. He founded the Shar'i Society (al-Jam'iyya al-Shar'iyya) and presided over it from 1331 AH until 1352 AH, and he died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 186}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists these books, all printed: al-Din al-Khalis in six parts, also titled Irshad al-Khalq ila Din al-Haqq; Tuhfat al-Absar wa-l-Basa'ir, a treatise on one question of fiqh; al-Risala al-Badi'a, fatwas forbidding some innovations; Ghayat al-Tibyan, a treatise on establishing the start and end of the fast; parts of a commentary on the Sunan of Abu Dawud; and Fasl al-Qadiyya, on pleadings, forms of attestation and legal claims {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 186}.
