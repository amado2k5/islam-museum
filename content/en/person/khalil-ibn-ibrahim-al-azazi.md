## Summary
Khalil ibn Ibrahim al-Azazi al-Hasani, an Egyptian from al-Sharqiyya and a Shafi'i astronomer, died in the last quarter of the thirteenth century AH according to the Bibliotheca Alexandrina book. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 18}

## Works
They include *al-Kawkab al-azhar fi al-amal bil-rub' al-muqantar*, a treatise on the lines of *fadl al-da'ir*, *Wasilat al-tullab ila ma'rifat al-awqat bil-hisab*, a treatise on the rules of astronomy, and a treatise on sundials. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 18}
