## Summary
İbrahim Efendi of Pečuj, known as Peçevi, the Ottoman historian {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 3, 'İbrahim Efendi Peçuylu'}.

Finance-trained, he served in the army's accounts, became defterdar of the second fiscal district in 1014/1605–06, then defterdar of Bosnia, where he died. The Sicill-i Osmani calls him 'a man of resolve and wise counsel' who 'wrote an excellent History'; while defterdar of Timișoara he had a mosque built in Istanbul {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 3, 'İbrahim Efendi Peçuylu'}.
