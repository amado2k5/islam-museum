## Overview
Sulayman ibn al-Hakam ibn Sulayman ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Nasir, of the Umayyads. He rose against Ibn Abd al-Jabbar al-Mahdi, and was given allegiance by the followers of Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Amir, most of whom were Berbers {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.500-502}.

## Rising and caliphate
He was first given the title al-Musta'in billah, then al-Zahir billah. He went to the Christians, made peace with them and sought their help, and they marched with him to Cordoba. He met Ibn Abd al-Jabbar in a famous battle, in which, according to Ibn al-Athir, thirty-five thousand were killed {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.500-502}.

Ibn Abd al-Jabbar was defeated, and Sulayman entered the city and besieged him in the palace until he fled. The people gave him allegiance to the caliphate in Shawwal of the year 400 AH {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.500-502}.

## The sack of Cordoba
The Berbers and the Byzantines raided Cordoba, plundered it, and took captive and enslaved a great number. Then Ibn Abd al-Jabbar returned, and Sulayman was defeated and went off to Shatiba {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.500-502}.

## In Nafh al-Tib min Ghusn al-Andalus al-Ratib — al-Maqqari
Sulayman, nephew of Hisham al-Mu'ayyad and al-Mahdi, who was given the title "al-Musta'in billah". He ruled Cordoba during the strife of al-Andalus in the early fifth century AH. al-Maqqari describes him in Nafh al-Tib {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}.

## In Nafh al-Tib min Ghusn al-Andalus al-Ratib — al-Maqqari: Reaching Cordoba
Sulayman joined the Berber troops, who had gathered on the outskirts of Cordoba. They pledged allegiance to him and gave him the title al-Musta'in billah. He led them to the frontier of Toledo and enlisted the help of Ibn Adhfunsh. He put to the sword more than twenty thousand of the people of Cordoba, and entered Cordoba at the close of the fourth century AH {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}.

Then al-Mahdi defeated him at Aqabat al-Baqar. The Berbers turned against al-Mahdi and Ibn Adhfunsh, and both were defeated {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}.

## In Nafh al-Tib min Ghusn al-Andalus al-Ratib — al-Maqqari: Entering Cordoba by force
al-Musta'in besieged Cordoba until he entered it by force in the year 403 AH. Hisham al-Mu'ayyad was killed in secret, and according to al-Maqqari, shame came upon the great families of Cordoba for their women and their sons {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}.
