Ammar ibn Yasir, a client of Banu Makhzum; he and his parents became Muslim and were all tortured — his [[person/yasir-ibn-amir|father]] died under the torment and his [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|mother Sumayya]] was stabbed, the first woman martyred in Islam, while the Prophet gave them tidings of Paradise as they were being tortured. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

His counting among the eighty-three who emigrated on the [[event/second-migration-to-abyssinia|second hijra to Abyssinia]] is doubted. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
