## Summary
Mohamed Elghaleez is an Egyptian preacher known for his direct preaching style addressed to young people in Egyptian colloquial Arabic, and for lines that circulate among his followers such as "O son of Adam", "Watch over your heart" and "Your steps are the road to Paradise". {cite:sutralqma-mohamed-elghaleez-bio}

## Studies and work
His biography on the Tdwyinat site records that his studies were not in the religious sciences — he studied pharmacy — and that he teaches diploma courses in the religious sciences and the sira to young people, announced through his pages and attended by large numbers of students from different places. {cite:tdwynat-mohamed-elghaleez-bio}

## His channel and series
Elghaleez launched his YouTube channel, which carries his name in English as "Mohammad Elghaleez" and his line "Your steps are the road to Paradise" as its description; it began in 2012. Platform statistics recorded 1,980,000 subscribers, 149,405,794 views and 3,927 videos for the channel on 23 October 2026 — dated platform figures that change constantly. {cite:youtube-mohelghaleez}

Among his series and programmes on the channel are Fadfadat al-Ahad (Sunday Heart-to-Heart), Al-Jawab (The Answer), in which he answers people's questions on religion and life, Shahadat Taqdir (Certificate of Appreciation), which presents the lives of the Companions and the Followers in narrative form, the Kalimat (Words) series, and Min Ayna Abda (Where Do I Begin), addressed to those seeking a balanced religious start. {cite:sutralqma-mohamed-elghaleez-bio} {cite:youtube-mohelghaleez}

## Style and audience
The published biographies describe his style as direct, frank and full of emotion: he simplifies religious concepts and ties them to people's daily lives, concentrating on repentance, sincerity, good character and trustworthiness without entering complex theological debates, which has made him popular especially among young people and teenagers; he is also active on other platforms such as TikTok. {cite:sutralqma-mohamed-elghaleez-bio}
