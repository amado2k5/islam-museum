## Overview
Tha'laba ibn Amr Muzayqiya, the eldest of the sons of [[person/amr-ibn-amir-muzayqiya|Amr ibn Amir Muzayqiya]], and grandfather of the Aws and the Khazraj {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.179}.

## Jurhum
On the transmission of Razin, as al-Samhudi reports it, he, his brothers and those of the Azd who went out with them expelled Jurhum from Mecca. His father had died before he overcame Jurhum {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.179}.
