## Summary
A precise and reliable traditionist, Abu al-Abbas, of Basra and Damascus; he heard from Hisham ibn Ammar, Isa ibn Hammad and Harun ibn Sa'id al-Ayli; transmitted by Ali ibn Amr al-Hariri, Shafi' ibn Muhammad al-Isfara'ini and Abu Ahmad al-Hakim, who called him reliable. The printed dates are given as (320 and 224 AH), which appear reversed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7841}
