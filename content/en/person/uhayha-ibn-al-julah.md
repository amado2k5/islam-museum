## Overview
Uhayha ibn al-Julah al-Awsi, lord of the Banu Jahjaba, of the [[group/al-aws|Aws]], and counted among the most honoured people of Yathrib before Islam {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}.

## The war of the Banu Jahjaba
Ibn al-Athir reports that he ordered Ka'b ibn Amr al-Mazini to be watched until he was killed. The war of the Banu Jahjaba and the Banu Mazin broke out, and in it he was defeated and entered his fortress {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}.

Mas'ud ibn Mu'tib al-Thaqafi came to him seeking an alliance of the Ansar against the Banu Malik. He refused, and provided him with weapons, provisions and a slave who built fortifications. Then he built the first of the fortresses of al-Ta'if {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}.
