## Overview
Uhayha ibn al-Julah al-Awsi, chief of Banu Juhjuba of [[group/al-aws|al-Aws]], counted among the mightiest of the people of Yathrib before Islam. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}

Ibn al-Athir reports that he ordered the ambush of Ka'b ibn Amr al-Mazini until he was killed, which set off the war of Banu Juhjuba and Banu Mazin, in which he was routed and entered his fortress. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}

Mas'ud ibn Mu'attib al-Thaqafi came to him seeking the Ansar's alliance against Banu Malik; he turned him away but equipped him with a weapon, provisions and a servant who built fortifications, so he built for Ta'if its first defensive tower. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.513}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 88: "Uhayha ibn al-Julah", and transmits from Malik in the Muwatta' from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr that a man of the Ansar called Uhayha ibn al-Julah killed his young uncle, on which Urwa said: for this reason a killer does not inherit from the one he killed. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.33}

## A note on his companionship
Ibn Hajar says he could find no genealogy for this Uhayha in the Ansar lineage books, and that some writers on the Companions claimed he was Uhayha ibn al-Julah ibn Harish, also called Kharrash ibn Juhjuba; the entry lists members of his descendants who held companionship — which flags the error of counting the elder man a Companion. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.33}
