## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakra, son of [[person/abu-bakra-al-thaqafi|Abu Bakra al-Thaqafi]], was the first child born in [[place/basra|Basra]] when Utba ibn Ghazwan settled in al-Khuraiba. His father slaughtered a camel and fed the people of Basra from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
