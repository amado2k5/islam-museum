## Overview
Al-Husayn al-Saymari, Hanafi qadi of Baghdad — of the Saymar river of Basra on which are villages; born 351, one of the noted Iraqi jurists — fine of expression, sound of view — he held the judgeship of al-Mada'in then of the Karkh quarter; he transmitted from Abu Bakr al-Mufid, Ibn Shadhan and Ibn Shahin — truthful, full of intellect, fine of company, knowing the scholars' rights. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.439,461}

## Fatwa and death
He wrote on the tumult over the title malik al-muluk in 429, permitting it since the kings of the earth may differ in rank; he died in Shawwal 436 and was buried in his house on Darb al-Zarradin. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.439,461}
