## Overview
Husayn ibn Fakhr al-Din ibn Qarqmas al-Ma'ni, known as Ibn Ma'n, was a man of letters among the Druze emirs of Lebanon. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1036 AH, 1627 CE, and his death as 1109 AH, 1697 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 251}.

## Upbringing
Al-Zirikli relates that Husayn's father, Fakhr al-Din, rose against the Ottoman state, was taken prisoner and carried to Istanbul with his family, among them the young Husayn, in 1043 AH. The father was put to death, and the son grew up in the Galata palace in the Sunni school {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 251}.

## Standing and works
Husayn rose in rank until the vizierate was offered to him, and he declined it. He wrote the book al-Tamyiz, a manuscript in Dar al-Kutub under number 9383, on literature, wise sayings and reports, and finished it in 1098 AH. He carried out tasks for the state, among them an embassy to India, and died in Istanbul {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 251}.
