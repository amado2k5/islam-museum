## Summary
'Ali b. Muhammad Ibn Abi'l-Shawarib, a qadi of the Banu Abi'l-Shawarib; in 183 he and the qadi Abu Khazim wrote the ruling returning the residue of inheritance shares to the agnates, resting on the agreement of 'Umar, 'Ali and Ibn Mas'ud — al-Mu'tadid ordered it applied. He was then invested with the judgeship of Madinat al-Mansur and Qatrabbul, added to his jurisdiction over Samarra, Tikrit and the Mosul road. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 162-163}
