## Overview
The secretary of the caliph al-Mustakfi billah. He was in charge of the caliph's estates when Mu'izz al-Dawla took Baghdad in 334 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337}

When al-Mustakfi was deposed, he was arrested. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337}
