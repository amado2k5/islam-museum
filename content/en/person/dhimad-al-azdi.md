Dimad ibn Tha'laba al-Azdi, of Azd Shanu'a in Yemen; he practised incantation and treated people for winds and madness. He came to Mecca and heard its foolish saying Muhammad was mad, so he said: if only I could see this man — perhaps God would heal him at my hand. {cite:sahih-muslim}

When he met him he said: Muhammad, I do incantations against this wind — will you have it? The Prophet said: "praise be to God; we praise Him and seek His aid. Whom God guides none can lead astray, and whom He leaves astray none can guide. I bear witness there is no god but God alone without partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger..." These words amazed Dimad; he had him repeat them three times and said: I have heard the words of soothsayers, poets and sorcerers, but never heard the like of these words of yours — give me your hand to pledge you on Islam. He pledged for himself and for his people. {cite:sahih-muslim}
