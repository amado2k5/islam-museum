Dimad ibn Tha'laba al-Azdi, of Azd Shanu'a in Yemen. He practised incantation and treated people for winds and madness. He came to Mecca and heard its foolish people say that Muhammad was mad, and he said: "If only I had seen this man, perhaps God would heal him through me." {cite:sahih-muslim}

When he met the Prophet, he said: "Muhammad, I practise incantation against this wind; will you have it?" The Prophet said: "Surely all praise is God's. We praise Him and seek His help. Whomever God guides, none can lead astray; whomever He leaves astray, none can guide. I bear witness that there is no god but God alone, without partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger..." These words overwhelmed Dimad. He asked him to repeat them, and did so three times, and then he said: "I have heard the words of soothsayers, poets and sorcerers, but I have never heard words like yours. Give me your hand, and I will pledge to you on Islam." He pledged on his own behalf and on behalf of his people. {cite:sahih-muslim}

## His Islam in Mecca (The Sealed Nectar p. 134)
He was from Azd Shanu'a in Yemen and practised incantation against wind. He heard the foolish people of Mecca say that the Prophet was mad, and he said: "If I came to this man, perhaps God would heal him through me." When the Prophet presented the khutba to him, he said: "I have heard the words of soothsayers, the words of sorcerers and the words of poets, but I have never heard words like these of yours." Then he pledged to Islam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 134} {cite:sahih-muslim 2/525}.
