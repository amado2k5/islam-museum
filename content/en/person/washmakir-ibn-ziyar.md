## Overview
Washmakir ibn Ziyar, brother of Mardavij ibn Ziyar, of the Jil, brothers of the Daylam. He ruled Tabaristan and Gurgan and was known in [[polity/ziyarid-dynasty|the state of the Ziyarids]] {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222}.

## Rise
[[person/mardavij-ibn-ziyar|Mardavij]] sent for him from the lands of Kilan in the year 320 AH. He relied on him for his affair, and appointed him to great offices. When Mardavij was killed, Washmakir ruled Tabaristan and Gurgan {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222}.

## The dispute with Ibn Buya
The two contended for al-Rayy and Isfahan with [[person/rukn-al-dawla|Rukn al-Dawla]] ibn Buya. He drove him out of Isfahan in the year 327 AH, then recovered it. He was defeated at al-Rayy by Abu Ali ibn Muhtaj, lord of Khurasan, and fled to Tabaristan. He sought help from Nuh ibn Saman, until he took Gurgan from al-Hasan ibn al-Firzan. He continued to contend with Ibn Buya for the mountains and Gurgan {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222}.

His year of death is estimated in the data at around 356 AH with a mark of doubt, and the entry needs an additional source.
