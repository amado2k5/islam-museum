## Overview
Tahman, client of Ibn Khazim. He was wounded on the day his master was killed, but was not killed himself {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## His descendants
He was the grandfather of Ya'qub ibn Dawud, secretary of the Commander of the Faithful al-Mahdi, after Abu Ubayd Allah {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
