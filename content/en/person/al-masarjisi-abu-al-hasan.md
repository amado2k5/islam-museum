## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Sahl al-Masarjisi, a Shafi'i jurist, who died in 383 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}

## Studies and teaching
He studied under [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]] and went out with him to Egypt, and he was a master of the school. He taught at Nishapur, and its jurists took from him. The judge Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari, al-Shirazi's teacher, studied jurisprudence under him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}

## Notes for researchers
- Al-Shirazi gives 383 AH as the year of his death; the editor's note says that Ibn Khallikan gives 384 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 61}
