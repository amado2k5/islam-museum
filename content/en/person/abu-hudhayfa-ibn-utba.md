## Summary
[[person/abu-hudhayfa-ibn-utba|Abu Hudhayfa ibn Utba]] ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd Shams al-Qurashi al-Abshami {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}. Mu'awiya said his name was Muhsham; it is also said Hushaym, Hashim or Qays {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}.

## Early Islam
He was "among the earliest to Islam, emigrated on both hijras, and prayed toward both qiblas" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}. Ibn Ishaq said he became Muslim after forty-three persons {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}.

## Badr and Salim
He is established in the two Sahihs in Salim's story, on the chain of al-Zuhri from Urwa from A'isha, and among those who witnessed [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}. Ibn Hajar refers to the biography of his client [[person/salim-mawla-abi-hudhayfa|Salim]], whom he adopted {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}.

## His description and martyrdom
They said he was tall and handsome-faced; he was martyred on the day of [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]] at fifty-six years of age {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1649}.
