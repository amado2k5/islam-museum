## Summary
Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah b. Ishaq al-Qayrawani, known as Ibn al-Tabban, shaykh of the Maghreb's Malikis; Qadi 'Iyad said: the camels' flanks were beaten to him from every province — a hafiz, far from pretension and ostentation, eloquent. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 76}
