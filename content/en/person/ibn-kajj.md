## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Yusuf ibn Ahmad ibn Yusuf ibn Kajj al-Dinawari, one of the imams of the Shafi'is in Dinawar. He was killed by bandits (al-ayyarun) in Dinawar on the night of the twenty-seventh of Ramadan 405 AH. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.64-65}

## Learning and leadership
He accompanied [[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-qattan|Ibn al-Qattan]] and attended the assembly of al-Daraki. He combined leadership in learning with worldly standing, and people travelled to him from all regions. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.64-65}

## His reply to Abu Ali al-Sanji
Ibn Khallikan relates that when Abu Ali al-Sanji saw his learning, he said: "The name belongs to Abu Hamid, and the learning to you." Ibn Kajj replied that Baghdad had raised Abu Hamid up and Dinawar had brought him down. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.64-65}

## Date of death
Al-Shirazi in *Tabaqat al-Fuqaha'* agrees that he was killed on the night of the twenty-seventh of Ramadan 405 AH. The conversion to 1015 CE is derived. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
