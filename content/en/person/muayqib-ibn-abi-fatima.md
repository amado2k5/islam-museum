## Summary
An emigrant to Abyssinia who came in the two ships; he was Umar ibn al-Khattab's keeper over the Muslims' treasury, and his tie was to the house of Sa'id ibn al-As. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him fourth of four men of Banu Abd Shams in the two ships. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4} He describes him as Umar ibn al-Khattab's keeper over the Muslims' treasury, and as being "to the house of Sa'id ibn al-As" — that is, in their clientship or tie; the book expressed it with a preposition and said no more. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/4}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The nature of his tie to the house of Sa'id ibn al-As (ally or client) is not stated in the text; we have not presumed it.
