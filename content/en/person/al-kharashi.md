## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah al-Kharashi (printed al-Khurashi in the entry), Abu Abdallah, was a Maliki. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 1010 AH, 1601 CE, and his death as 1101 AH, 1690 CE, and says he was the first to hold the office of Shaykh of [[place/al-azhar|al-Azhar]] {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 240-241}.

## Life
His nisba is from a village called Abu Khurash in al-Buhayra, Egypt. Al-Zirikli describes him as a distinguished and pious jurist who lived in Cairo and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 240-241}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists his al-Sharh al-Kabir on the text of Khalil, printed, in Maliki law; Muntaha al-Raghba fi Hall Alfaz al-Nukhba, on Ibn Hajar's work on hadith terminology, in manuscript with a copy in the Taymuriyya; al-Sharh al-Saghir, also on Khalil's text, in manuscript in al-Zaytuna; and al-Fara'id al-Saniyya, a commentary on al-Muqaddima al-Sanusiyya in theology, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 pp. 240-241}.
