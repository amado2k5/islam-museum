## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Khalid al-Shafi'i, who combined learning and asceticism; Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili was among his pupils. He died in 301 AH, per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 13 p. 144}
