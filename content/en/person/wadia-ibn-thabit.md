## Summary
Ibn Ishaq names him among the band of hypocrites who mocked the Tabuk army, then apologised that they had only been jesting and playing. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/168}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him among a band of hypocrites, calling him "Wadi'a ibn Thabit, brother of Banu Amr ibn Awf". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/168} He says the man came to the Prophet apologising while holding his she-camel's nose-rein, saying: we were only jesting and playing — and a verse of Surat al-Tawba came down. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/168-169}

## In the mosque of Dirar
Ibn Ishaq names him last of the twelve men who built the [[building/masjid-dirar|mosque of Dirar]]: "and Wadi'a ibn Thabit — he is of Banu Umayya [ibn Zayd], fellows of Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} The bracketed addition is from manuscript (a) per the editors. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: brother of Banu Amr ibn Awf. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/168}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Describing the band as hypocrites is Ibn Ishaq's phrasing.
