## Overview
Salih ibn Muhammad ibn Qalawun, al-Malik al-Salih, son of al-Nasir, son of al-Mansur, was known as Ibn al-Tankiziyya because his mother was the daughter of Tankiz, the governor of Syria. He took the sultanate after al-Nasir Hasan was deposed in Jumada II 752 AH {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 360}. Ibn Hajar, in al-Durar al-Kamina, gives his birth in Rabi I 738 AH, his deposition in Shawwal 755 AH and his death in Safar 762 AH, and says he had not completed twenty-four years {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}.

## Taking the sultanate
Ibn Hajar says that the men who set up his rule were Taz, Mughultay the amir akhur, Manklibugha al-Fakhri and others; a few days later they rode to Qubbat al-Nasr against Taz, and Taz prevailed {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 360}.

## The journey to Syria
Taz then took al-Salih out to Syria because of Bibghaurus. Bibghaurus, the governor of Aleppo, had rebelled, joined by Ahmad al-Saqi, the governor of Hama, and others, and had gone to Damascus and taken it. Al-Salih went against him with the armies and reached Damascus at the start of Ramadan; the people celebrated his arrival and he prayed in the mosque. The armies set out to find Bibghaurus, whose followers scattered when they heard the sultan was coming {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 360}. Bibghaurus then made for Aleppo, where those who did not share his view plundered his treasury; he fled and asked for the protection of Dulghadir the Turkmen, who gave it, and letters were sent about him without agreement {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 360}. Al-Salih led the festival prayer, with the sermon given by Taj al-Din al-Manawi, the army judge, and returned to Egypt, entering it on 25 Shawwal {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 360}.

## Endowment and rule
It was al-Salih who endowed the district of Sardus for the covering of the Kaaba {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}. Ibn Hajar says that during his sultanate he had no authority to act: the matter lay with Sarghatmish and then Shaykhu. He agreed with Taz to arrest Shaykhu, the plan went the other way, and he was deposed in Shawwal 755 AH {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}.

## Intelligence and crafts
Ibn Hajar describes him as so quick that he learned glass-making and several other crafts: he would attend a craftsman for about a week and become skilled in the work {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}.

## Imprisonment and death
He was held in the citadel with his mother until he died in Safar 762 AH {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}.

## Notes on the dating
In this entry the printed text of al-Durar al-Kamina abbreviates some years, giving 52, 55 and 38, and prints his death in full as 762 AH. The readings 752, 755 and 738 are the editor's, taken from the context: the entry says he had not completed twenty-four years and died in 762 AH {cite:durar-kamina-hyderabad-1972 vol. 2 p. 361}.
