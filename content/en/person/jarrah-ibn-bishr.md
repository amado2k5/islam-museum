## Overview
Jarrah ibn Bishr was the brother of [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]], Abd Allah ibn Bishr, governor of [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]]; his brother sent him with fifty horsemen to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] after seeing the soldiers' dislike of going to al-Hadi, and they came to him at the al-Darb of Banu Sarim in the last days of Muharram 286 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} It reached [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], which weighed on him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} He was then with al-Hadi's army when it went out to [[group/khirfan-arhab|Khirfan]] and [[group/al-subay-arhab|al-Subay']] and camped at Hawth, since the book says the army included "the brother of Abu al-Atahiya and his horse". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} The book's index lists further pages for his name, which this batch has not yet read. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.444}

The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers. See [[event/abu-al-atahiya-promises-horse-and-sends-his-brother-jarrah-285]] and [[event/al-hadi-at-khirfan-and-al-subay-and-the-march-to-hawth-286]].
