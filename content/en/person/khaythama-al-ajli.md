## Summary
Khaythama ibn Jahsha al-Ajli was a worshipper who turned away from the world once he saw its deception, and hastened toward the hereafter once its reality was clear to him. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 493 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 139. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/139}

## Verses of admonition
Shurayh al-Abid relates that he heard Khaythama ibn Jahsha al-Ajli recite verses addressed to those who court the world. They say that the world kills its suitors, that it takes a new husband every day, and that affliction works on the body little by little. They call for provision for death, since the one who calls to departure has already called. Some words in the verses are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/139}
