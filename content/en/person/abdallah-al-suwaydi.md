## Overview
Abdallah ibn Husayn ibn Mar'i ibn Nasir al-Din al-Baghdadi, Abu al-Barakat al-Suwaydi, was a jurist and man of letters, one of the notables of Iraq, and the first of this house to be known as al-Suwaydi. He was born in 1104 AH, 1693 CE, and died in 1174 AH, 1761 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His life
He was born in the Karkh quarter of Baghdad. His father died while he was a child and his maternal uncle, Shaykh Ahmad Suwayd, took charge of him; he learned and became well known. He travelled to Syria and the Hijaz, returned to Baghdad and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names "al-Jumana fi al-Isti'arat", in manuscript; "Ithaf al-Habib", a gloss on "Mughni al-Labib", in manuscript; "Anfa' al-Wasa'il", on "Dala'il al-Khayrat"; a commentary on "Sahih al-Bukhari"; "Asma' Ahl Badr", a printed treatise; "al-Hujaj al-Qat'iyya li-Ittifaq al-Firaq al-Islamiyya", a printed treatise; "al-Amthal al-Sa'ira", printed, which he describes as a maqama; "al-Muhakama bayna al-Damamini wa-al-Shumunni"; a small "Diwan" in manuscript at the Zahiriyya, holding his verse; "al-Nafha al-Miskiyya fi al-Rihla al-Makkiyya", in manuscript; and others {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 57}.
