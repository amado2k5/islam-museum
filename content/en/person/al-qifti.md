## Overview
Jamal al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Yusuf al-Qifti, who died in 646 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 641}

## His works
He is the author of [[work/inbah-al-ruwat|Inbah al-Ruwat ala Anbah al-Nuhat]], which Haji Khalifa describes as a history of the grammarians. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 641}

The Gregorian date recorded (about 1248 CE) is derived from the Hijri date. His birth, his other works and the details of his life need an additional source.
