## Overview
The Muslimah Ideas is a content creator who publishes on YouTube under her channel's name; her channel opens with a personal welcome: "Assalamu Alaykum And Welcome To My channel 'The Muslimah ideas'". {cite:youtube-the-muslimah-ideas}

The channel publishes in English and addresses Muslim women. {cite:youtube-the-muslimah-ideas}

Her channel had about 149,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly. {cite:youtube-the-muslimah-ideas}

## Career and work
Her videos alternate between explainers she prepares herself and clips compiled from lecturers' talks: her published titles include a timeline of all the prophets mentioned in the Qur'an from Adam to Muhammad, an explainer on halal and haram, videos on the Barzakh and the life after death and on the angels, on Laylat al-Qadr, and on the signs of the Hour and the approach of the Mahdi as she presents it, alongside clips from talks by Belal Assaad, Mohamad Baajour and Suleiman Omar. {cite:youtube-the-muslimah-ideas}

Her selections lean towards the hereafter and the unseen: life after death, the signs of the end times, and connections she draws between current events and end-time prophecies. {cite:youtube-the-muslimah-ideas}
