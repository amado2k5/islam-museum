## Summary
A Qurashi man known for severely harming the Prophet in Mecca; he waylaid the Prophet's daughters Fatima and Umm Kulthum on their emigration, goading their camel until they fell to the ground. The Prophet declared his blood lawful on the conquest day and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] killed him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52-53}.

## Why his blood was declared lawful
Ibn Ishaq relates that al-Huwayrith ibn Nuqayd was among those who harmed the Prophet at Mecca; when al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib equipped the Prophet's two daughters Fatima and Umm Kulthum and mounted them on a camel to join Medina, al-Huwayrith caught up with them and goaded the camel so it threw them to the ground {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/52}.

On the day of Mecca's conquest the Prophet declared his blood lawful, and Ali ibn Abi Talib hunted him down and killed him {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/53}.

## For young visitors
