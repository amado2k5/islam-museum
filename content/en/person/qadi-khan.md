## Overview

Fakhr al-Din al-Hasan ibn Mansur ibn Abi al-Qasim Mahmud ibn Abd al-Aziz, known as Qadi Khan al-Awzjandi al-Farghani, a Hanafi jurist counted among the great scholars of his school. He died in 592 AH / 1196 CE.{cite:al-aalam vol. 2 p. 223}

## Nisba

His nisba, al-Awzjandi, refers to Awzjand, a place in the region of Isfahan near Farghana, which gives him the second part of his name.{cite:al-aalam vol. 2 p. 223}

## Works

His *al-Fatawa* (Fatawa, in three volumes) is printed. He also wrote *al-Waqi'at*, *al-Mahadir*, and a commentary on *Adab al-Qada'* of al-Khassaf. Al-Zirikli's entry also lists *al-Amali*, a commentary on *al-Ziyadat*, and a commentary on *al-Jami' al-Saghir*; it marks these as manuscripts, and records two parts of the last.{cite:al-aalam vol. 2 p. 223}
