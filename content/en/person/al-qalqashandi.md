## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad, Shihab al-Din al-Qalqashandi the Shafi'i: born in 756, he wrote in the chancery and served as deputy judge; his works include Subh al-A'sha in four volumes. He died on Saturday 10 Jumada al-Akhira 821. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 2/8}
