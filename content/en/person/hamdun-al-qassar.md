## Overview
Hamdun ibn Ahmad ibn 'Imara al-Qassar al-Naysaburi, Abu Salih, was a Sufi, the shaykh of the People of Blame (ahl al-malama) at Nishapur from whom the Malamati way spread; he was also a scholar-jurist of the Thawri school and had a spiritual path of his own. He died in 271 AH (884 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.274}

Of his sayings: “Whichever of you can avoid being blind to his own shortcomings, let him do so.” {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.274}
