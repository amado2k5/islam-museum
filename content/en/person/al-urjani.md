## Summary
Nasih al-Din Abu Bakr Ahmad b. Muhammad b. al-Husayn al-Urjani; a jurist-poet and deputy qadi at Tustar and 'Askar Mukram. Al-'Imad al-Katib al-Isfahani mentions him in the *Kharida*, noting that in the prime of his youth he was at Isfahan's Nizamiyya from Nizam al-Mulk's era to its end in 544; of himself he said: 'I am the most poetic of jurists, none contesting, and surely the most learned of poets.' {cite:wafayat-ayan}
