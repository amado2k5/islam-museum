# Yahya Ibrahim

## Summary
Yahya Ibrahim is a Canadian-born Islamic lecturer of Egyptian ancestry who lives in Perth, Western Australia. He began memorising the Qur'an at the age of sixteen and finished twenty months later, receiving an ijazah in proficiency and an instruction to teach; he began lecturing at seventeen and has been involved in the Islamic community since 1993. {cite:listennotes-yahya-ibrahim}

## Teaching
He is an instructor and director with AlMaghrib Institute, where he is described as a specialist in spirituality and the Qur'an who studied tafsir, fiqh and hadith with scholars from the Hijaz and Egypt. {cite:almaghrib-yahya-ibrahim} He lectures to Muslim and non-Muslim audiences in Australasia, Africa, South East Asia, Europe and Canada, and his recorded lectures are archived in Muslim Central's podcast series under his name, running to more than 700 episodes since 2014. {cite:listennotes-yahya-ibrahim} He was awarded the West Australian Multicultural Community Service Award for Individual Excellence. {cite:almaghrib-yahya-ibrahim}
