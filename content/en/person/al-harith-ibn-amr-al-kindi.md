## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Amr ibn Hujr al-Kindi, of [[group/kinda|Kinda]], whom Qubadh ibn Firuz appointed over [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]] after he dismissed [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-ma-al-sama|al-Mundhir ibn Ma' al-Sama]], because al-Mundhir refused Qubadh's call to the Mazdakite doctrine. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/29-32}

## Dismissal and flight
Qubadh was succeeded by [[person/khosrow-i-anushirvan|Khosrow Anushirvan]], who hated this doctrine intensely. He killed [[person/mazdak|Mazdak]] and many who held his doctrine, and he restored al-Mundhir to the governorship of al-Hira. He sought al-Harith ibn Amr, but he escaped to the lands of the Banu Kalb and stayed among them until he died. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.26}

## What is not recorded here
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum does not mention here the kingdom of Kinda in Najd, or al-Harith's affair with the Banu Asad.

## For young visitors
Al-Harith al-Kindi ruled al-Hira for a short time by order of the king of Persia, and then fled when the king changed.
