## Overview
Zawba'a al-Kahina, a soothsayer of the Arabs. al-Mas'udi counts her among those who had deformed bodies, in the manner of soothsayers {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 484}.

He mentions her with Shiq, Satih, Sadif ibn Humas, Tarifa, Imran the brother of Muzayqiya, Haritha and Juhayna, and the soothsayer of Bahila {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 484}.
