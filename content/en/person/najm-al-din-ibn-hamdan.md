## Overview
Ahmad ibn Hamdan ibn Shabib ibn Hamdan al-Numayri al-Harrani, Abu Abd Allah, was a Hanbali jurist and man of letters. Al-Zirikli prints his years as 603 AH (1206 CE) to 695 AH (1295 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 119}.

## Career
Al-Zirikli says he was born and raised in Harran, travelled to Aleppo and Damascus, and became deputy judge in Cairo; he settled there, grew old and lost his sight, and died in the city {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 119}.

## Works
His books include al-Ri'aya al-Kubra, in manuscript, of which al-Zirikli records a copy written in 706 AH in the Chester Beatty collection, no. 3541, and al-Ri'aya al-Sughra, both on jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 119}. Others are Sifat al-Mufti wa-l-Mustafti (printed), a short introduction to the principles of religion, and Jami' al-Funun wa-Salwat al-Mahzun, a manuscript work of adab {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 119}.
