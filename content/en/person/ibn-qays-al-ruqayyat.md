## Overview
Abdallah ibn Qays al-Ruqayyat, a poet. Al-Mas'udi gives verses of his on two caliphs and two emirs. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.185}

## His verses
He said of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik that he was seduced by this world and by the gentleness of the priest, so that the two left the priest with neither mind nor soul. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.185}

He also composed a verse on Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr describing the radiance of his face. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.101}

His life and the date of his death need an additional source.
