## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Yusuf, sultan of the Banu Marin in the Maghrib, who died in 707 AH during his siege of Tlemcen. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1938-1939}

## The siege of Tlemcen
Ibn Khaldun relates that he pressed Tlemcen, and built opposite it a city called al-Mansura with a wall, a mosque, baths and a hospital. He besieged it for a hundred months until he died at al-Mansura. When his troops moved away, the house of Yaghmurasan destroyed his city. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1938-1939}

His son [[person/abu-thabit-marinid|Abu Thabit]] succeeded him.
