## Summary
The hafiz Abu'l-Fadl Muhammad b. Tahir b. 'Ali al-Maqdisi, known as Ibn al-Qaysarani; one of the travellers in hadith's quest, he heard in the Hijaz, Syria, Egypt, the marches, the Jazira, Iraq, the mountains, Fars, Khuzistan and Khurasan, and settled at Hamadan; among his works the *Atraf al-kutub al-sitta* and collections showing the abundance of his knowledge. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
