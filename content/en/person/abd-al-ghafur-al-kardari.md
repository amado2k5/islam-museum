## Overview
Abd al-Ghafur ibn Luqman ibn Muhammad, Sharaf al-Quda' Taj al-Din Abu al-Mafakhir al-Kardari, was one of the imams of the Hanafis, his origin the village of Kardar in Khwarazm; he held the judgeship of Aleppo and died there in 562 AH (1167 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.32}

He wrote a book on usul al-fiqh; Sharh al-Tajrid; Sharh al-Jami' al-Saghir; Sharh al-Jami' al-Kabir; and Hayrat al-Fuqaha', in which he collected the problems that baffle the scholars. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.32}
