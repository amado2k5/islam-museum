## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Salih ibn Ali ibn Abdallah ibn al-Abbas, an Abbasid prince who governed the frontier regions in the time of al-Rashid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.211}

## Consulting the jurists on Cyprus
Al-Baladhuri reports that the people of Cyprus did something during his governorship, and he wanted to annul their treaty. He wrote to al-Layth ibn Sa'd, Malik ibn Anas, Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Musa ibn A'yan, Isma'il ibn Ayyash, Yahya ibn Hamza, Abu Ishaq al-Fazari and Makhlad ibn al-Husayn about their case, and they answered him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.211} See [[event/fuqaha-consulta-on-cyprus|the jurists' consultation on Cyprus]].

## Before al-Rashid
Al-Mas'udi reports that al-Rashid brought him in his chains in 188 AH, and that Yahya ibn Khalid said to him: "I have heard that you are vengeful." He answered with a reply that pleased al-Rashid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.331-332}
