## Overview
Ali ibn Isma'il ibn Ishaq al-Ash'ari, a Basran theologian to whom a school of theology known as the Ash'aris is attributed. This source gives few details of his life. He was born around 260 AH (874 CE) and died around 324 AH (936 CE). Both dates rest on one source and need an additional source. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
