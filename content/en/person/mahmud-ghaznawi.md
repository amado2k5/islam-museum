## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Mahmud ibn Sebuktigin, sultan of Ghazna, whom the caliph al-Qadir billah titled "Yamin al-Dawla wa Amin al-Milla" (Right Hand of the State and Trustee of the Community). {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.284}

## His policy in Khurasan
Ibn al-Jawzi relates that in 408 AH he complied with al-Qadir's call for the innovators to repent, and set in his governance in Khurasan and elsewhere the killing of the Mu'tazila, the Rafida, the Isma'ilis, the Qaramita, the Jahmiyya and the Mushabbiha, and their crucifixion and burning. He ordered them cursed from the pulpits and drove the people of innovation out of their homes. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.284}

The description of them as "innovators" is Ibn al-Jawzi's wording. No other view of these measures is recorded here, and it needs an additional source.
