## Overview
Salma bint Amr of Banu Adi ibn al-Najjar: Hashim's wife and Abd al-Muttalib's mother; she raised her son at Yathrib until his uncle brought him to Mecca.

## Life
When Hashim went out to Syria as a merchant he came to Medina and married Salma bint Amr of Banu Adi ibn al-Najjar and stayed with her; then he went to Syria while she was with her people, pregnant with Abd al-Muttalib {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Hashim died at Gaza in the land of Palestine, and his wife Salma bore Abd al-Muttalib in the year 497 CE, naming him Shayba for a white lock in his head, and she kept raising him in her father's house at Yathrib {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Salma is the Prophet's grandfather's mother; she stayed at Yathrib after her husband's death and raised her son Shayba in her father's house.
