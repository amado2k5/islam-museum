## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ali al-Subki, Taj al-Din Abu Nasr, son of Taqi al-Din: he came to Damascus with his father in 739, read with al-Mizzi and attended al-Dhahabi. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/426} His works include Jam' al-Jawami', al-Ashbah wa-l-Naza'ir and the greater, middle and lesser Tabaqat; he became chief judge of Damascus and preacher of the Umayyad Mosque, and was tried and dismissed more than once before being restored. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/426} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/427} He died on 7 Dhu al-Hijja 771 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/427} The editor's footnote gives his birth as 728. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425}
