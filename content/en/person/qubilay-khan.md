## Overview
Qubilay ibn Tului ibn Genghis Khan. Ibn Khaldun reports that his brother Mongkefan sent him with their brother Hulagu to conquer the lands of Khitai, and that he succeeded his brother Arbikan as khan {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

His brothers were [[person/monke-khan|Mongkefan]] and [[person/hulagu|Hulagu]]. His descendants included kings, among them Demerfay, Tarbi Kizi, Kizikan, and Sandumurkan ibn Turmala ibn Tumjinkmir {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
