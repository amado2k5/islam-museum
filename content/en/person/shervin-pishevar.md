# Shervin Pishevar

Shervin Kordary Pishevar (born 24 March 1974) is an Iranian-American entrepreneur, venture capitalist, super-angel investor and philanthropist. He is the co-founder and managing director of Sherpa Capital and a co-founder and former executive chairman of Hyperloop One. The English article and Wikidata give his birth on 24 March 1974 at Tehran; the Persian article gives Tehran and the year 1974. No Arabic or Urdu edition of his article exists; this file's companion Arabic and Urdu texts are written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-pishevar-en} {cite:wikipedia-pishevar-fa} {cite:wikidata-pishevar}

## Early life and education

Pishevar was born in Tehran, the son of Abraham Pishevar, a television and radio executive, and Eshrat (Kordary) Pishevar. His father moved to the United States after he was placed on an execution list for broadcasting instructions on leaving Iran to foreign nationals; the family joined him some eighteen months later in Maryland. Pishevar attended Montgomery Blair High School's magnet programme and studied molecular and cell biology at the University of California, Berkeley, where he received a Presidential Fellowship; in 1996 he co-wrote a Journal of the American Medical Association article that helped lead to the Istanbul Protocol. {cite:wikipedia-pishevar-en}

## Career

Pishevar founded and operated technology companies including webs.com, WebOS, Social Gaming Network and HyperOffice. As a managing director at Menlo Ventures he led investments in Warby Parker, Tumblr, Machine Zone and Uber's Series B, and served as an Uber board observer from 2011 to 2015. He co-founded Sherpa Capital, whose fund has invested in Airbnb, Uber, Slack, Robinhood and other companies, and co-founded the transport venture Hyperloop One. As an angel investor he has seeded more than 60 companies. He served on the UN Foundation's Global Entrepreneurs Council from 2011, was named an Outstanding American by Choice in 2012, was appointed to the J. William Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board in 2015, and received the Ellis Island Medal of Honor in 2016. {cite:wikipedia-pishevar-en}
