## Overview
Ali ibn Ziyad al-'Absi al-Tunisi was the first to introduce Imam Malik's Muwatta' into the Maghrib, and in his time none was more learned in fiqh than he in Ifriqiya. He died in 183 AH (799 CE), and his grave in Tunis is still known. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.289}
