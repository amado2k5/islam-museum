## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Dinar of Medina, Abu Abd Allah al-Juhani, was a jurist of Medina in the time of [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik ibn Anas]] and a transmitter of hadith; al-Bukhari included him, and al-Nasa'i in his "al-Yawm wa-l-Layla" {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 307}. Ibn Abd al-Barr's Tabaqat al-Fuqaha counts him among Malik's companions and passes on al-Shafi'i's remark that he never saw among Malik's young companions anyone more learned than Muhammad ibn Dinar {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.124}.

## Name and nickname
Al-Mizzi says that al-Bukhari named him al-Madani, Abu Abd Allah al-Juhani, that some say al-Ansari, that Ya'qub ibn Muhammad al-Zuhri held him to be of the descendants of Dinar ibn al-Najjar, and that according to others his nickname was Sandal {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 306}.

## Teachers and students
Al-Mizzi lists among those he narrated from Ibrahim ibn Isma'il ibn Abi Habiba, Usama ibn Zayd al-Laythi, Salama ibn Wardan, Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Muttalib, Ubayd Allah ibn Umar al-Umari, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Dhi'b, Muhammad ibn Ajlan, Musa ibn Uqba, Hisham ibn Sa'd and Yazid ibn Abi Ubayd {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 306}; the entry marks al-Bukhari and al-Nasa'i as including his narration from Ibn Abi Dhi'b {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 306}. Those who narrated from him include Abu Mus'ab Ahmad ibn Abi Bakr al-Zuhri, Dhu'ayb ibn Ghamama al-Sahmi, Abd Allah ibn Wahb, Abu Hisham Muhammad ibn Maslama al-Makhzumi, Yahya ibn Ibrahim ibn Abi Qutayla and Ya'qub ibn Muhammad al-Zuhri {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 307}. The Tabaqat al-Fuqaha adds that he studied with Malik and with Ali ibn Harban {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.124}.

## What the critics said of him
Al-Bukhari said he was known for his hadith; Abu Hatim said he was one of the jurists of Medina like Malik and was reliable; and Ibn Hibban listed him in his book "al-Thiqat" {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 307}. Ibn Abd al-Barr said that he was a mufti of Medina together with Malik and Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Salama and after them, a virtuous jurist with a concern for transmitted knowledge; elsewhere he said that at the end of Malik's time and after it the centre of legal opinion in Medina lay with al-Mughira ibn Abd al-Rahman and Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Dinar, as Abd al-Malik ibn al-Majishun related {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 307}.

## Death
The Tabaqat al-Fuqaha says he died in 182 AH, three years after Malik {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.124}. Al-Mizzi's entry gives no year of death {cite:tahdhib-kamal-mizzi-risala vol. 24 p. 307}.
