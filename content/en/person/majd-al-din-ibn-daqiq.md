## Summary
Majd al-Din Ibn Daqiq, the Maliki master, shaykh of the Sa'id and resident of Qus; a gatherer of the sciences' branches, described with piety and godliness, magnified in souls; he transmitted from Abu'l-Mufaddal and others and died in Muharram at eighty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 565}
