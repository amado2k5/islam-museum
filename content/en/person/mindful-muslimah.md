# Mindful Muslimah

## Summary
Mindful Muslimah is an educator and marriage coach who works entirely under this public name. Her website describes a community leader, international speaker, educator and mentor who has worked as an administrator and consultant in education systems in the United States and Dubai. {cite:mindful-muslimah-website}

## Podcast and school
She is the founder of the Muslim Mindfulness School and the host of the Mindful Muslimah Speaks podcast. The channel's own description presents a marriage coach with over 25 years of experience and a mother of five, guiding Muslim women and men through courtship and marriage on Islamic principles. {cite:mindful-muslimah-website} {cite:toppodcast-mindful-muslimah-speaks}
