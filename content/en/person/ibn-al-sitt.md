## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd Rabbih ibn Ali al-'Azizi, known as Ibn al-Sitt, was a learned man of al-'Aziziyya in Egypt's Sharqiyya province; his mother was a Byzantine slave-woman, which gave him his name. He died in 1199 AH (1785 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.188}

He wrote glosses and commentaries on Maliki fiqh, tawhid and tafsir. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.188}
