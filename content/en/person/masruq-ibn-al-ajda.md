## Overview
Masruq ibn al-Ajda' ibn Malik ibn Umayya al-Hamdani, one of the great Successors who transmitted hadith and paid attention to recitation. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

His kunyas and his reported lineage vary. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Learning and transmission
He received learning from [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abdallah ibn Mas'ud]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

## Death
The encyclopaedia gives his death as 63 AH (683 CE), according to a commonly transmitted report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
