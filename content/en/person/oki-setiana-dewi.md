## Summary
Oki Setiana Dewi is an Indonesian actress, writer and preacher, born in Batam on 13 January 1989. She became known for the lead role of Anna in the film Ketika Cinta Bertasbih (2009) and went on to become one of Indonesia's most visible women preachers on television and social media; her YouTube channel has more than three million subscribers, and her Maskanul Huffadz foundation runs branches across Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

## Early life and education
Oki was born in Batam — then part of Riau — on 13 January 1989, the first daughter of Sulyanto and Yunifah Lismawati; she has two younger sisters. She spent her childhood in Batam until she was sixteen, then schooled in Depok. In 2007 she began studying Dutch literature at the Faculty of Cultural Sciences, University of Indonesia, was chosen best new student of both the university orientation and her faculty, and graduated in 2012. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

After graduating she studied Qur'an memorisation and Arabic at Rumah Quran Darut Tarbiyah in Depok and for a month at Umm Al-Qura University in Mecca. She took a master's degree in early-childhood education at Jakarta State University in 2016, and the same year began doctoral studies at two institutions at once: Islamic studies at UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta (doctorate in 2020; her dissertation examined middle-class celebrity hijrah preaching and its responses to Salafi dawah and the Tablighi Jama'at) and Qur'an and tafsir studies at Institut PTIQ Jakarta (doctorate in 2022; her dissertation treated Qur'an-based education in religious moderation within the family). In 2017 she received scholarships to study Islam in Australia and Germany. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

## Career
In 2008 she auditioned for Ketika Cinta Bertasbih and was cast as its lead; the film was shot in Egypt, and she won Best New Actress and Favourite New Actress at the 2010 Indonesian Movie Awards. She has published books since 2011, has been a regular preacher on Trans TV's daily programme Islam Itu Indah since 2014, and in 2017 served as a judge on the Malaysian Islamic music competition Gema Gegar Vaganza on Astro Oasis. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

## Projects and initiatives
In 2016 she founded and leads the Maskanul Huffadz Foundation, based in Bintaro, South Tangerang. The foundation gives orphaned and poor girls full one-year scholarships and housing to memorise the Qur'an and then teach it across Indonesia, and has eight branches. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

## Perspectives and criticism
This section sets out positions as reported in the sources; it is not a verdict by the museum. A sermon video of February 2022 drew criticism from the National Commission on Violence Against Women, which held that she had normalised domestic violence by saying a husband's violence against his wife is a disgrace that should not be exposed. Social-media users also criticised reports that she demanded luxury facilities and high fees when invited to preach, as the article records. {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}

## Books
Her books include Melukis Pelangi (Painting the Rainbow, 2011), Cahaya di Atas Cahaya (Light upon Light, 2012), Hijab, I'm in Love (2013) and Hidup Damai di Negeri Multikultur (Living in Peace in a Multicultural Country, 2017). {cite:wikipedia-oki-setiana-dewi-id}
