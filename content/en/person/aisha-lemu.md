# Aisha Lemu

**Aisha Lemu** was a British author and religious educator. She was born at Poole, Dorset, in 1940 as Bridget Honey; from the age of thirteen she questioned the faith she had been raised in and explored other religions, including Hinduism and Chinese Buddhism. She studied Chinese history, language and culture at the School of Oriental and African Studies in London, where Muslim students gave her Islamic literature to read; she converted to Islam at the Islamic Cultural Centre in 1961, in her first year of study, and helped to found the Islamic Society at SOAS, becoming its first secretary {cite:wikipedia-aisha-lemu-en}.

While studying for a postgraduate teaching qualification she met Ahmed Lemu, then studying at another college of the University of London. She moved to Kano in Nigeria in August 1966 to teach at the School for Arabic Studies, where he was headmaster; they married in April 1968, Aisha becoming his second wife. She went on to be principal of the Government Girls College at Sokoto and of the Women's Teachers College at Minna (1976–1978) {cite:wikipedia-aisha-lemu-en} {cite:wikipedia-aisha-lemu-ar}.

With her husband she co-founded the Islamic Educational Trust (IET), which grew to run schools, a publishing house and adult education for women across several Nigerian states. In 1985 she founded the Federation of Muslim Women's Associations of Nigeria (FOMWAN) with other Muslim women and was elected its first national Amirah for four years. She served on the Islamic Studies Panel that revised Nigeria's national Islamic curriculum {cite:wikipedia-aisha-lemu-en} {cite:wikidata-aisha-lemu}.

She wrote prolifically: a student's introduction to Islam (1971), textbooks for junior and senior Islamic studies used across northern Nigeria, works on women in Islam — several of them with Fatima Heeren — on moral education, on citizenship, and on moderation and extremism in Islam. In 2000 she received the Member of the Order of the Niger (MON) honour. She died at Minna on 5 January 2019 {cite:wikipedia-aisha-lemu-en}.
