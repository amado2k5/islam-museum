## Overview
Sharik ibn Sumayy was one of [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]'s men at the conquest of Egypt. Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports from Yazid ibn Abi Habib that Amr sent him after the Romans and he caught and routed them at the mound called Kom Sharik. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 108}

## On the mound
Another report says he led Amr's vanguard; the Romans met him at Tarnut and drove him onto the mound, where he held out surrounded and sent Malik ibn Na'ima al-Sadafi on his chestnut horse to Amr, who came up and the Romans withdrew. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 108} See [[event/battle-of-kom-sharik|the battle of Kom Sharik]].
