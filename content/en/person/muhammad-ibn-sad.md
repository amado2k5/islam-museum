## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'd, secretary of [[person/al-waqidi|al-Waqidi]] and author of the *Tabaqat*. [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|Al-Ma'mun]] summoned him in 218 AH among the first seven to be tested over the createdness of the Qur'an. He answered, and returned from al-Raqqa to Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.490}

The data say that his relation with al-Ma'mun was "a relation" without specifying its kind, and this needs an additional source.
