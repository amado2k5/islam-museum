# Belal Muhammad

## Summary

Belal Muhammad is an American professional mixed martial artist of Palestinian descent, born in Chicago on 9 July 1988.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}{cite:wikidata-belal-muhammad}
He won the Titan FC welterweight title and then, in July 2024, the UFC welterweight championship, becoming the first UFC champion of Palestinian descent; he lost the title in May 2025.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}
He is known for his wrestling-based fighting style and for his outspoken support of Palestine.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}

## Background and career

Muhammad was born and raised in Chicago to Palestinian parents and wrestled at Bogan High School; he graduated from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign with a law degree.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-ar}
A professional since 2012, he beat Steve Carl in 2016 to win the Titan FC welterweight championship with a record of 9–0, and made his UFC debut that July, losing to Alan Jouban.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}
On 27 July 2024, at UFC 304, he beat Leon Edwards by unanimous decision to win the UFC welterweight championship, the first fighter of Palestinian descent to win a UFC title.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}
His scheduled first defence against Shavkat Rakhmonov at UFC 310 fell through when he developed a bone infection in his foot, and on 10 May 2025, at UFC 315, he lost the title to Jack Della Maddalena by unanimous decision.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}
He has also competed in freestyle wrestling with Real American Freestyle, beating former UFC fighter Ben Askren in July 2026 and losing to Colby Covington that September.{cite:wikipedia-belal-muhammad-en}
