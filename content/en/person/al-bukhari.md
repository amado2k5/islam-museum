## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Mughira ibn Bardizbah al-Bukhari al-Ju'fi, described in the source as the Commander of the Faithful in hadith and one of the trustworthy hadith scholars. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.321-322}

## His works
Ibn al-Nadim lists among his books: "al-Tarikh al-Kabir", "al-Tarikh al-Saghir", "al-Tarikh al-Awsat", "al-Asma' wa al-Kuna", "al-Du'afa'", "al-Sahih", "al-Sunan" in jurisprudence, "al-Adab", "Khalq Af'al al-Ibad", and "al-Qira'a khalf al-Imam". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.321-322}

## For Sprouts
A revered scholar of Hadith who traveled widely and compiled the Prophet's authentic sayings in his monumental Sahih al-Bukhari.

## For Explorers
Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari was the master of Hadith scholars, born in Bukhara with an early passion for prophetic traditions. He traveled across the Islamic world to collect and critically verify narrations with rigorous scrutiny, authoring his celebrated Sahih al-Bukhari, universally regarded as the most authentic Hadith collection.

## For Scholars
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari (194–256 AH / 810–870 CE), acclaimed as the supreme authority in Hadith criticism. Born in Bukhara, he embarked on sixteen years of extensive scholarly journeys across Khorasan, Iraq, the Hijaz, Syria, and Egypt, studying under master traditionists like Ahmad ibn Hanbal and Ali ibn al-Madini. Applying rigorous methodological criteria demanding both contemporaneous verification and documented direct transmission, he authored 'al-Jami' al-Sahih' (Sahih al-Bukhari), revered in Sunni scholarship as the most authoritative canonical compilation of prophetic traditions, alongside seminal biographical works like 'al-Tarikh al-Kabir' and 'al-Adab al-Mufrad'.
