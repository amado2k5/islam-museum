Shireen Abu Akleh (3 April 1971 – 11 May 2022) was a Palestinian-American journalist, born in Jerusalem to a Palestinian Arab Christian (Melkite Catholic) family from Bethlehem.{cite:wikipedia-shireen-abu-akleh-en} She studied journalism at Yarmouk University in Jordan and joined Al Jazeera in 1997 as one of its first field correspondents, becoming the face of its coverage of the Palestinian territories through the Second Intifada and the wars on Gaza, and a role model for Arab women journalists.{cite:wikipedia-shireen-abu-akleh-en}{cite:wikipedia-shireen-abu-akleh-ar}

On 11 May 2022, wearing a blue press vest, she was shot and killed in Jenin while covering an Israeli military raid on the refugee camp. Investigations by the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights, the United States Department of State and several international news organisations concluded that the shots that killed her came from Israeli forces; the Israeli military later acknowledged a high possibility that she had been accidentally killed by its fire, while declining a criminal investigation.{cite:wikipedia-shireen-abu-akleh-en} Her funeral in Jerusalem — a Mass at the Melkite Catholic Cathedral of the Annunciation of the Virgin, after a procession from St Joseph Hospital during which Israeli police beat the pallbearers, and burial beside her parents on Mount Zion — drew tens of thousands and was described as the largest Palestinian funeral in Jerusalem in over twenty years.{cite:wikipedia-shireen-abu-akleh-en}{cite:catholic-news-service-abu-akleh-funeral-2022}
