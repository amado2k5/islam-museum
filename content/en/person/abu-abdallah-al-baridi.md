## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Baridi, the eldest of the Baridi brothers. He was among the special men of Ahwaz, and his brother Abu Yusuf was over Sari when Ali ibn Isa became vizier. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 137}

When Ibn Muqla became vizier, his brother Abu al-Husayn gave him twenty thousand dinars, and he appointed Abu Abd Allah over all of Ahwaz except Sus and Jundaysabur. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 137}

Ibn al-Athir mentions a dispute over the vocalisation of the nisba: Ibn Makula vowelled it with a "ba" as derived from the post (al-barid), and Ibn Miskawayh with a "ya". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 137}
