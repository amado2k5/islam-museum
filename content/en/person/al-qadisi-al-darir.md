## Overview
Ahmad b. Muhammad al-Qadisi al-Darir, the Hanbali; rough of living — al-Mustadi' sought a man to lead him in tarawih; they brought him and asked: "What is your madhhab?" — "Hanbali." They said: "No Hanbali can pray at the caliph's palace." Al-Qadisi said: "I am Hanbali, and I do not wish to lead you anyway." The caliph heard and said: "Pray on your own madhhab." He kept to Ibn al-Jawzi and profited by him; he died in 621. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 95}
