## Summary
Munir Nayfeh is a Palestinian physicist working in the United States, a pioneer in manipulating matter at the level of the atom and the nanoparticle. {cite:wikipedia-munir-nayfeh}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Palestine in 1945, he studied physics at the American University of Beirut and took a doctorate in Britain, then moved to teaching and research at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. {cite:wikipedia-munir-nayfeh}

## Career and works
He performed pioneering experiments isolating and moving single atoms with lasers, and developed methods to produce silicon nanoparticles that luminesce in different colours by size, with bioimaging applications. He authored university textbooks on lasers and atomic physics used worldwide. {cite:wikipedia-munir-nayfeh}

## Legacy
Nayfeh is counted among the most prominent Palestinian experimental physicists, and his work is cited in the nanotechnology and photonics literature. {cite:wikipedia-munir-nayfeh}
