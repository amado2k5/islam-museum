## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Yaman, Abu Bakr al-Samarqandi, was a jurist, counted among the senior Hanafis. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 268 AH, 881 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 147}. The match with this card rests on the kunya, the nisba, the school and the period of death; other men may share the same kunya and nisba, so the identification is recorded as likely rather than certain.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists Ma'alim al-Din, al-Radd 'ala al-Karramiyya, and al-I'tisam, on hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 147}.
