## Summary
Ahmad Muwafiq, known as Gus Muwafiq or Kyai Muwafiq (and to friends as Cak Afiq), is an Indonesian scholar and preacher, born in Lamongan, East Java, on 2 March 1974. A Nahdlatul Ulama (NU) ulama based in Sleman, Yogyakarta, he is the caretaker of the Minggir pesantren there, and he served as a personal assistant to Abdurrahman Wahid, including during Wahid's presidency. {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id} He is one of NU's best-known contemporary orators, recognised for a booming voice and for sermons that weave Islamic, Indonesian and world history into plain, humorous language; his YouTube channel, Gus Muwafiq Official, counted about 251 thousand subscribers in June 2024, as reported by the Islamic Communication Journal — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id}

## Early life and education
Muwafiq was educated in the pesantren system of East Java: primary school in his home district of Lamongan, junior studies at Pesantren Bungah in Gresik, and senior studies at Pondok Pesantren Bahrul Ulum in Jombang, where, his published biography records, he was already known as a critical mind and a gifted orator. {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq} In 1994 he entered the Islamic dawah programme of IAIN Sunan Kalijaga in Yogyakarta; as a student he was active and a noted orator in the Indonesian Islamic Students Movement (PMII), NU's student wing, and his biography credits him with a term as secretary-general of the Southeast Asian Muslim students' association. {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id} {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq}

## With Gus Dur
Muwafiq served as a personal assistant to K.H. Abdurrahman Wahid (Gus Dur), NU's longtime leader, and stayed at his side through Wahid's presidency of Indonesia (1999–2001). {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id} NU Online's account, cited by the article, tells how the two met at Pulau Besar in Malacca and describes the younger man's role as that of a spiritual assistant. {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id} {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq}

## Preaching and writing
From his base at the Minggir pesantren in Sleman, Muwafiq preaches across Indonesia at pesantren, university and state occasions. {cite:wikipedia-ahmad-muwafiq-id} {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq} Profiles of him dwell on his command of history — of Islamic civilisation, of the Prophet's era and of the Indonesian archipelago — delivered in direct language that reaches young audiences. {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq} He is also an author: his book Islam Rahmatan lil-Alamin (Islam, a Mercy to the Worlds) appeared with MedPres in 2019. {cite:biografi-gus-muwafiq}
