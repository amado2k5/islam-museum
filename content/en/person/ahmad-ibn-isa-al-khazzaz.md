## Overview
Abu Sa'id Ahmad ibn Isa al-Kharraz, one of the leading Sufis, called al-Kharraz after the craft of leather-stitching. The encyclopaedia gives his death as 286 AH (899 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The date is recorded as reported and needs an additional source. His sayings and reports need an additional source.
