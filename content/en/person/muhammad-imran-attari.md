## Summary
Haji Muhammad Imran Attari is a Pakistani preacher and the Nigran (head) of the Markazi Majlis-e-Shura of Dawat-e-Islami, the central council that leads the movement. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari} His official channel describes its purpose as providing worldwide access to Islamic lectures, spiritual guidance and Sunnah-based teachings from the leadership of Dawat-e-Islami. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari}

## Biography as his organisation presents it
According to the biography published by Dawat-e-Islami's Social Media Department on his official channel, he was born on 8 July 1970 in Karachi. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari} After finishing his school and higher business studies, he took part in his father's business and later founded his own successful garment business. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari} He joined Dawat-e-Islami in 1992 as a devoted volunteer and rose to lead the Markazi Majlis-e-Shura. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari}

This museum notes that this biography is the organisation's own presentation of its leader; no independent source for the birth date was found. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari}

## Work
His channel carries his Friday sermons (bayans), Ramadan addresses and question-and-answer sessions, and is managed under the supervision of the Social Media Department of Dawat-e-Islami. {cite:youtube-maulana-imran-attari} His most-viewed upload is a Ramadan farewell recording, "Alvida Alvida Mahe Ramzan", at 7.92 million views. {cite:vidiq-maulana-imran-attari}

## Reach
His official YouTube channel, @maulanaimranattari, counted 895,000 subscribers, 128,643,388 total views and 2,225 videos as of 5 November 2026 — dated platform figures, not museum-verified facts. {cite:vidiq-maulana-imran-attari} The channel was created on 29 December 2018. {cite:vidiq-maulana-imran-attari}
