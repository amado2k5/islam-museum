## Overview
Qays ibn al-Khatim al-Ansari, a poet of the Aws and Khazraj, who lived before Islam and in its early years. He lived in [[place/medina|Medina]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.492}

Among his verses is an exchange with [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]] about the war between Banu Amr ibn Awf and Banu Akhraj on the day of al-Sarara. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.492}
