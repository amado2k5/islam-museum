## Overview
Ibn al-Sabuni, the traditionist and court registrar Sharaf al-Din Ya'qub ibn Ahmad of Aleppo; the hafiz Abu Hamid ibn al-Sabuni was his aunt's husband and he was known through him. Born in 644 AH, he heard from Ibn 'Uzzun, Ahmad ibn al-Qadi Zayn al-Din, al-Najib, Ibn 'Allaq, Ibn Abi al-Yusr and many others. He died in 719 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4240}
