## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Sinan ibn Thabit ibn Qurra ibn Marwan ibn Thabit, Abu Ishaq al-Harrani and then al-Baghdadi, was an engineer-geometer and physician, and al-Zirikli states that he belonged to the Sabians. His family came from Harran; he was born and died in Baghdad, born in 296 AH (908 CE) and dying in 335 AH (946 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.

## His works
Among his books al-Zirikli names "Zubdat al-Hikam", on wisdom; "Aghrad al-Majisti"; "Tafsir al-Maqala al-Ula min al-Makhrutat"; "Alat al-Zilal"; and, printed, "Risala fi al-Asturlab" and "Maqala fi Rasm al-Qutu' al-Thalatha" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}. In manuscript are "Risala fi Wasf al-Ma'ani al-Mustakhraja fi al-Handasa wa-'Ilm al-Nujum"; "Maqala fi Tariq al-Tahlil wa-al-Tarkib", on geometry; "Kitab fi Harakat al-Shams", a treatise; "Kitab fi Misahat Qat' al-Makhrut al-Mukafi'", a treatise; "Kitab fi al-Dawa'ir al-Mutamassa", of six leaves; and "Kitab fi Usul al-Handasa", of five leaves {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 36}.
