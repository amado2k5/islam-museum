## Overview
Hasan ibn Ali ibn Jabir al-Habil al-Yamani was a poet of Sanaa, where he was born and died. Al-Zirikli calls him a Zaydi poet, Zaydism being a school distinct from the Sunni majority, and gives his birth as 1048 AH, 1638 CE, and his death as 1079 AH, 1668 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 205}.

## Life
His family came from the village of Bani al-Habil, a hijra (a protected settlement) of the Khawlan tribe {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 205}.

## Poetry
Al-Zirikli describes him as a vehement poet whose verse has quality and delicacy, and says he owns a manuscript copy of his diwan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 205}.
