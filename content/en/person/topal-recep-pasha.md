## Summary
Recep Pasha, nicknamed 'Topal' — the Lame — an Ottoman grand vizier of Bosnian origin {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 4, 'Receb Paşa (Topal)'}.

Raised in the bostancı corps, he became bostancıbaşı, then kapıcılar kethüdası and chief equerry, and married Gevherhan Sultan. Made serdar of the Black Sea with vizier rank in 1033/1624, kaymakam to the grand vizier in 1035/1625–26, and grand vizier in 1041/1631–32, dying some six months into the office {cite:sicill-i-osmani vol. 4, 'Receb Paşa (Topal)'}.
