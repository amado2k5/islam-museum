## Overview
Budayl ibn Tahfa al-Bajali, who was in Oman in the time of the Umayyads. Al-Hajjaj wrote to him to march to Daybul. When he met the enemy his horse bolted with him, and the enemy surrounded him and killed him. Some transmitters say that the one who killed him was Zutt al-Bidha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
