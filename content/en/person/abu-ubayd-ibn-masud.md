## Overview
Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud al-Thaqafi, father of al-Mukhtar, and the first whom Umar sent to Iraq, with a thousand men, in his caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.350}

## In Iraq
Al-Baladhuri relates that he met Jaban at al-Muthanna and defeated him, then went on to Kaskar, made peace with al-Jalinus at Barusma for four dirhams per head, crossed the bridge he had repaired, and met Dhu al-Hajib Mardanshah with four thousand armoured men, who had an elephant. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.350}

## His death
He fought until he was killed at the Battle of the Bridge, at the end of Ramadan 13 AH according to al-Baladhuri. It is said the elephant knelt on him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.351} The year of death in the data is an approximate month.
