## Overview
Ahmad ibn Israil, vizier of al-Mu'tazz, whom al-Mu'tazz appointed vizier in 252 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.349}

Al-Tabari relates that Salih ibn Wasif seized him. He was then killed in Ramadan 255 AH along with Abu Nuh. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.387} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.396}
