## Overview
Ahmad ibn Isra'il, vizier of al-Mu'tazz who appointed him in 252 AH; al-Tabari records that Salih ibn Wasif seized him, and he was killed in Ramadan 255 AH together with Abu Nuh. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl}
