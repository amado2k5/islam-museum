## Summary
Ibn Hisham mentions, from al-Darawardi from his father, that the Prophet appointed him over Medina when he went out to Tabuk — against Ibn Hisham's own saying that the appointee was Muhammad ibn Maslama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Hisham names him, from Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Darawardi from his father, as the one the Prophet appointed over Medina on his departure for Tabuk. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- This differs from Ibn Hisham's first saying that it was Muhammad ibn Maslama (p. 162).
