## Summary
The sharif Abu Sa'd ibn Barakat ibn Hasan ibn 'Ajlan, lord of the Hijaz, died in Rabi' al-Akhir 894 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}.

## Details
Under the dead of 894 Ibn al-'Imad lists "the sharif Abu Sa'd ibn Barakat ibn Hasan ibn 'Ajlan, lord of the Hijaz," who died in Rabi' al-Akhir {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}. He was of the Banu 'Ajlan sharifs who held the emirate of Mecca and the Hijaz for unbroken generations, administering the two sanctuaries under the shadow of the Mamluk sultan in Egypt, the house of 'Ajlan having long provided the sharifs of Mecca {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}. Listed in the same year before him are al-Mansur 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Dawud, lord of Yemen, who died in Jumada al-Ula, and Shams al-Din al-Raddawi the Hanbali, a notable jurist who died in al-Salihiyya, Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 356}.
