## Overview
The imam Mu'in al-Din Abu Hamid Muhammad b. Ibrahim al-Jajarmi, of Jajarm a town between Nishapur and Jurjan — the Shafi'i jurist, author of al-Kifaya fi'l-fiqh. Ibn Khallikan says: an excellent, exact, outstanding imam who dwelt at Nishapur and taught; his Kifaya is the utmost brevity yet contains many questions arising in fatwas, in a single volume; his is also Idah al-wajiz in two volumes; he has a famous method in khilaf and famed fawa'id ascribed to him — people worked at him and profited from him and his books after him, especially the qawa'id which they bent to studying. He died the morning of Friday 10 Rajab 613 at Nishapur. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
