## Overview
Al-Shaybaniyya, daughter of Hani ibn Qabisa and wife of Ubayd Allah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, who appears in the account of his departure for Siffin. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.676-677}

## The account of Siffin
Al-Mas'udi relates that she refused to buckle his weapons on the day he set out, and said that her people were not attacked by a Persian with arrogance in his head without destroying him. He struck her with a bow, then was killed as she had warned. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.676-677}

When Ali ordered his body given to her, she threw to them a silk cloak, they wrapped him in it, and they handed him to her. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.676-677}
