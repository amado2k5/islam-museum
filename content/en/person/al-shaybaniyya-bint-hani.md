## Overview
al-Shaybaniyya bint Hani', wife of Ubayd Allah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab; she refused to gird his weapon on him the day he set out for Siffin, saying 'no hero in the Jahiliyya or in Islam ever turned against my people without them wiping him out' — he struck her with a bow.
