## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Wal al-Tamimi was one of the five leaders under whom the Kufan Shi'a camped at al-Nukhayla in 65 AH, in the Penitents' (Tawwabun) movement that ended at the battle of Ayn al-Warda. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 87}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi relates in Muruj al-Dhahab that the Shi'a of Kufa stirred in 65 AH, meeting in mutual blame and remorse over al-Husayn ibn Ali, whom they had failed to aid. They judged that they had erred gravely by summoning him and then not answering his call, and that nothing could wash away that guilt except killing his killers or dying in the attempt. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 87}

They rallied to five men: Sulayman ibn Surad al-Khuza'i, al-Musayyab ibn Najaba al-Fazari, Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Nufayl al-Azdi, Abd Allah ibn Wal al-Tamimi, and Rifa'a ibn Shaddad al-Bajali, and camped at al-Nukhayla — after a long quarrel with al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd al-Thaqafi, who had been discouraging those who wished to march out with them. Abd Allah ibn al-Ahmar urged departure and battle in verses mourning al-Husayn and those killed with him, and blaming his followers for holding back. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 87}
