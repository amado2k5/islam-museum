## Overview
Ibn Surayj, a singer from among the singers of the days of [[person/al-walid-ibn-yazid|al-Walid ibn Yazid]]. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Walid was the first to bring singers to him from the lands, among them Ma'bad, al-Ghariq, Ibn Aisha, Ibn Muhriz, Tuways and Dahman. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.201-203}

## The song that delighted al-Walid
According to the books of songs, the singing that delighted al-Walid is attributed to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.201-203}
