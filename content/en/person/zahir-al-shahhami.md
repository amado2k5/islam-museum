## Summary
Zahir al-Shahhami, musnid of Khurasan — Abu'l-Hasan Zahir ibn Tahir al-Shahhami — heard much hadith and dictated more than a thousand sessions; he knew the science of defects and has a large history. He died in Rabi' II 533. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 584}
