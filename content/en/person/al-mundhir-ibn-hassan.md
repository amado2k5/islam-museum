## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn Hassan ibn Dirar, of the Banu Malik ibn Zayd al-Dubayya. Al-Baladhuri relates that he took part in the killing of Mihran on the day of al-Nukhayla. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Note: the museum has another entry, [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-hassan-al-dabbi|al-Mundhir ibn Hassan ibn Sarar al-Dabbi]], in which there is a dispute over the killing of Mihran on the day of al-Buwayb. The two may be one person, and this needs review.
