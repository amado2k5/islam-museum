## Overview
Al-Shahijan, the envoy of the governor of Merv to Ibn Amir, who came to him asking for peace. Ibn Amir sent Hatim ibn al-Nu'man al-Bahili to Merv, and he made peace with it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
