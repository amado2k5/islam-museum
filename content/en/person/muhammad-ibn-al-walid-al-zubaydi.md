## Overview
One of the jurists of Syria, Abu al-Hudhayl Muhammad ibn al-Walid ibn Amir al-Zubaydi. He lived in [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. Muhammad ibn Salim says that he read the Qur'an to Ibn Shihab at al-Rusafa, and Ibn Shihab told him that this one had gathered within himself all the learning that was in him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}

## Death
He died in 148 AH, according to al-Shirazi. The data need an additional source, since the figure was read from the image of the page. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.41}
