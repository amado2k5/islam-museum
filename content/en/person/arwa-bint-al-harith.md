## Summary
Arwa bint [[person/al-harith-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Harith ibn Abd al-Muttalib]], the Prophet's aunt, described as "the boldest of those who came to [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] in address" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 32}.

## In Mu'awiya's assembly
She entered upon Mu'awiya after Ali's death, an old woman, and answered his words about Ali and his house, saying the family of Muhammad held "the station of the people of Moses among the house of Pharaoh" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 32}. She rebuked [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], calling him "son of the adulteress," {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 33} and suggested [[person/marwan-ibn-al-hakam|Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] resembled "Sufyan ibn al-Harith ibn Kalda" in the blue of his eyes and redness of his hair {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 33}.

She then asked Mu'awiya for six thousand dinars, justifying it by saying Ali "fulfilled the trust and acted on God's command" while he "squandered your trust and betrayed God in His wealth" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 34}. In another account Mu'awiya told her "God forgive what has passed, auntie; state your need," and she answered "I have no need of you" and left {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 34}.

## Poetry and praise of Ali
She recited verses in Ali's praise opening "O eye, woe to you, gladden us," then elegized her father Abd al-Muttalib with her famous ode "I forgot Abu Arwa and remembered him" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 34-35}. Mu'awiya told his companions: "By God, had everyone in this assembly spoken to her, each would have answered differently from the other — the women of Banu Hashim are more eloquent than the men of others" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 35}.
