## Overview
Aq Sunqur al-Bursuqi, lord of Mosul and one of the commanders of the Seljuk sultan Mahmud. He occupied himself with jihad against the Crusaders. The Batinis killed him in 520 AH (1126 CE) while he was praying in the Great Mosque of Mosul. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.35}

Al-Salabi quotes Ibn al-Athir's description of him as a good Turkish mamluk who loved the people of learning and the righteous, and kept up the prayers at their times. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.35}
