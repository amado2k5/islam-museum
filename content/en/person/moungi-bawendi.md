## Summary
Moungi Bawendi is a Tunis-born chemist at MIT and a foundational figure in quantum-dot science — nanocrystals whose light colour changes with size. He won the 2023 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Louis Brus and Alexei Ekimov. {cite:wikipedia-moungi-bawendi}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Tunisia in 1961 to a Tunisian father and a French mother, he grew up between Tunisia, France and the United States, studied chemistry at Harvard and took his doctorate at the University of Chicago. {cite:wikipedia-moungi-bawendi}

## Career and works
In the early 1990s he developed hot-injection synthesis, enabling high-quality uniform colloidal quantum dots and opening their use in displays, bioimaging and lighting. He directs a research group at MIT with foundational papers in nanocrystal spectroscopy. {cite:wikipedia-moungi-bawendi}

## Legacy
His 2023 Nobel recognised a field that underpins QLED screens and medical imaging; in the Arab world he is cited as one of the most prominent scientists of Tunisian descent. {cite:wikipedia-moungi-bawendi}
