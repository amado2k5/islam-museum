# Tarik Elyounoussi

## Summary

Tarik Elyounoussi is a Norwegian footballer, born in Al Hoceima, Morocco, in 1988. {cite:wikipedia-tarik-elyounoussi-en} A forward and winger, he played club football in Norway, the Netherlands, Greece, Sweden and Japan, and represented the Norway national team. {cite:wikipedia-tarik-elyounoussi-en} No article read makes any statement of his religion, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-tarik-elyounoussi-en}{cite:wikidata-tarik-elyounoussi}

## Club career

Elyounoussi began at Fredrikstad, where he won the Norwegian Cup, and played for Heerenveen in the Netherlands before returning to Norway with Rosenborg. {cite:wikipedia-tarik-elyounoussi-en} He later played for Olympiacos in Greece, AIK in Sweden and Shonan Bellmare in Japan. {cite:wikipedia-tarik-elyounoussi-en}
