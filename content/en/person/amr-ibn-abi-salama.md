## Overview
Amr ibn Abi Salama al-Tinnisi al-Dimashqi, a mawla of the Banu Hashim, who narrated from al-Awza'i. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3492}

## Assessment
Al-Walid ibn Bakr counted him among the leading transmitters; al-Saji and Ibn Ma'in weakened him, and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3492}
