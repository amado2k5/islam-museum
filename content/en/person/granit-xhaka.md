# Granit Xhaka

## Summary

Granit Xhaka is a Swiss professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder for Sunderland and captains the Switzerland national team. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} Born in Basel in 1992 into a Kosovar Albanian family, he won two Swiss titles with Basel, spent seven years at Arsenal, helped Bayer Leverkusen to their first-ever Bundesliga title in 2024, and is Switzerland's record appearance holder. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} The English article describes him as a Muslim; it names no school for him, so his data record leaves his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en}{cite:wikidata-granit-xhaka}

## Early life

Xhaka was born on 27 September 1992 in Basel, Switzerland, into an ethnic Albanian family from Besianë, Kosovo; the family had moved from Besianë to Basel in 1990. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} His elder brother Taulant was also a professional footballer, for Basel and the Albania national team. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} Granit started his youth football with Concordia Basel and moved with his brother to Basel's youth department at the end of 2002. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en}

## Basel, Mönchengladbach and Arsenal

Xhaka broke into Basel's first team in 2010 and won the Swiss championship in each of his two seasons, completing a league and cup double in 2011–12. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} In May 2012 he joined Borussia Mönchengladbach, where he became club captain in 2015 and twice led the team to Champions League qualification. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} He signed for Arsenal in 2016 for a fee in the region of £30 million, made nearly 300 appearances over seven years, served as captain and won two FA Cups. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en}

## Leverkusen, Sunderland and Switzerland

In 2023 Xhaka returned to the Bundesliga with Bayer Leverkusen and helped the club to their first-ever league title in his debut season. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} In 2025 he returned to England with newly promoted Sunderland. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} Part of the Swiss team that won the 2009 FIFA U-17 World Cup, he made his senior debut in 2011 and is Switzerland's record appearance holder with 152 caps, representing the country at four World Cups and three European Championships. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} In July 2017 he married his long-term partner Leonita Lekaj; they have three daughters. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en} He speaks fluent Albanian, German, French and English. {cite:wikipedia-granit-xhaka-en}
