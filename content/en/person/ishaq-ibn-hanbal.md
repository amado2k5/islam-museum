## Overview
Ishaq ibn Hanbal ibn Hilal ibn Asad al-Shaybani is entered by al-Safadi as the uncle of Imam Ahmad. He was born in 161 AH and died in 252 AH {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 267}.

## Transmission and students
He heard Yazid ibn Harun and his generation, and his son Hanbal ibn Ishaq and others narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 267}. Al-Safadi says he was trustworthy, that three years separate him from Imam Ahmad, that he heard most of Imam Ahmad's teachers, and that Ibrahim al-Harbi and Abdallah, the son of Imam Ahmad, narrated from him {cite:wafi-bi-l-wafayat-safadi-dar-ihya-2000 vol. 8 p. 267}.
