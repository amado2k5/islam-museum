## Overview
Ali ibn Sulayman ibn al-Fadl al-Baghdadi, a grammarian of Baghdad. He is known as al-Akhfash al-Asghar to distinguish him from other grammarians who bore this title. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 235 AH (849 CE) and died in 315 AH (927 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates rest on tradition and need an additional source.

## Encyclopaedia of Islam account
The Encyclopaedia of Islam (2nd ed.) names Abu 'Ali b. Sulayman b. al-Mufaddal as the third of the three most famous Akhfashes. He was a pupil of al-Mubarrad and Tha'lab. He gained distinction by introducing the grammatical studies of Baghdad into Egypt, where Ahmad al-Nahhas was his pupil. A grammatical work he wrote was studied and annotated in Spain. He died in 315/927. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 343}
