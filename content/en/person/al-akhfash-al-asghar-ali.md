## Overview
Ali ibn Sulayman ibn al-Fadl al-Baghdadi, a grammarian of Baghdad. He is known as al-Akhfash al-Asghar to distinguish him from other grammarians who bore this title. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His life
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 235 AH (849 CE) and died in 315 AH (927 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates rest on tradition and need an additional source.
