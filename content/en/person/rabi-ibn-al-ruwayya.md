## Overview
The narrator first names him "Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya" when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] ordered his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya to camp at a place called [[place/sabal-sanaa-yemen|Sabal]] in Safar 289 AH {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236} ([[event/the-tabari-reinforcements-arrive-and-abdullah-and-ibn-al-ruwayya-camp-at-sabal-289]]). In later pages Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya asks al-Hadi to send him "his brother Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya" with the army he had and to add horsemen of the Ja'fari who were at Sana'a, and al-Hadi ordered Rabi' to go to his brother {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240} ([[event/the-sortie-to-hadda-and-safa-and-abu-al-ushayra-razes-the-fort-of-ziyad-289]]). The book gives no ancestry or tribe for al-Ruwayya, and does not say whether the "Ibn al-Ruwayya" routed in Jumada II is Rabi' or his brother ([[event/the-fight-near-ghayman-and-the-rout-of-ibn-al-ruwayya-289]]), so the museum does not tie that name to one record. His brother is [[person/abu-al-ushayra-ibn-al-ruwayya|Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya]].
