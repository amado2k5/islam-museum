## Overview
Kabul Shah, king of Kabul in the Umayyad period. Al-Baladhuri says that he gathered together for the Muslims and drove out those of them who were in Kabul; then Ratbil came and prevailed over Zabulistan and al-Rukhkh, as far as Bust. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
