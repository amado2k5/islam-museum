## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali al-Azafi, lord of Ceuta when the sultan Abu al-Hasan al-Marini took it. According to Ibn Khaldun, the sultan appointed him over the combined fleets of the two Maghribs gathered at the harbour of Ceuta, about a hundred ships, and ordered him to meet the fleet of the Christians in the Strait. The ships came together, the Muslims were victorious, the enemy's commander, called "al-Malind", was killed, and their ships were led into the harbour. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His role
The data record him as commander of the Muslim fleets in the battle of the Strait (event/battle-of-the-strait-740). {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
