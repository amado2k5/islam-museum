## Overview
Rifa'ah ibn Rafi' ibn Malik ibn Ajlan al-Ansari al-Zuraqi, Abu Mu'adh, was a Companion present at Badr; he later accompanied Ali and witnessed the Camel and Siffin with him, and died in 41 AH (661 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.29}

Twenty-four hadiths of his are recorded in the hadith collections. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.29}
