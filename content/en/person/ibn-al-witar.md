## Summary
The Hanbali jurist-preacher known as Ibn al-Witar, of the Mas'uda quarter in eastern Baghdad. He heard from Abu'l-Fath ibn al-Muni and others, studied fiqh under him, preached, and bore witness before chief qadi 'Abd al-Razzaq ibn al-Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir. He was accomplished — jurist, imam, scholar, of fine character — and granted ijazas to al-Mundhiri, Ibn Abi'l-Jaysh, al-Qasim ibn 'Asakir, al-Hajjar and others. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 188-189}
