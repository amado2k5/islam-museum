## Overview
Iskandar ibn Ya'qub Abkarius, a Lebanese man of letters, poet, journalist and historian, from an Armenian family that settled in Beirut. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is counted among the pioneers of the Arabic literary renaissance in the second half of the nineteenth century. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He died in 1885 CE (1303 AH), a date that needs a second source.
