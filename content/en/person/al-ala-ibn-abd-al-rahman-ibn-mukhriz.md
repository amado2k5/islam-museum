## Overview
Al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Mukhriz ibn Haritha ibn Rabi'a ibn Abd al-Uzza ibn Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Kufa
Al-Baladhuri relates from al-Kalbi that he was in charge of a quarter of Kufa in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr. The coin of Ibn Mukhriz is attributed to him, and his house was on the site of the house of Isa ibn Musa, well known. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data do not record his date of death, which needs an additional source.
