## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Shihab ibn Yusuf al-Kardari al-Buraqini al-Khwarazmi, famed as al-Bazzazi, was a Hanafi jurist. His family came from Kardar in the region of Khwarazm. He travelled in the lands of the Crimea and of the Bulgars, performed the pilgrimage and became famous. The printed text gives his death as 827 AH, 1424 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he used to give fatwas declaring Timur (Tamerlane) an unbeliever; this is what the entry reports of him. His books include 'al-Jami' al-Wajiz', printed in two volumes, fatwas in Hanafi jurisprudence; 'al-Manaqib al-Kardariyya', printed, on the life of Imam Abu Hanifa; 'Mukhtasar fi Bayan Ta'rifat al-Ahkam', in manuscript; and 'Adab al-Qada', in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 29}.
