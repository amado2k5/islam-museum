## Overview
Marwan ibn Abi al-Janub, a poet whom the museum's sources mention at the assembly of [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.541-542}

## The prize
Al-Suyuti relates that al-Mutawakkil rewarded him for a poem with a hundred and twenty thousand, and fifty garments. He also relates that he said a verse about the caliph meaning that he feared he would become tyrannical, and asked that the gifts be withheld. Al-Mutawakkil answered that he would not withhold them until his generosity drowned him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.541-542}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that he composed verses on the oath of allegiance al-Mutawakkil gave to his three sons, and addressed the caliph as "O tenth of the caliphs". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.519-520}

The date of his birth, his death and his full lineage need an additional source.
