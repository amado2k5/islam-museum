## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Atabi al-Bukhari, a Hanafi jurist and exegete, from the Atabiyya quarter of Bukhara. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}

## Works
His works include commentaries on al-Ziyadat, al-Jami' al-kabir and al-Jami' al-saghir, Jawami' al-fiqh and a tafsir. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}
