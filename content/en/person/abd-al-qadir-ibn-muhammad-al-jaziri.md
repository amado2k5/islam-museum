## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Qadir ibn Muhammad al-Ansari al-Jaziri was a Hanbali scholar of Egypt. This edition of the entry gives his birth as 880 AH, 1475 CE, and his death as about 977 AH, about 1570 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}. His name points to Jazirat al-Fil, in the districts of Egypt {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Durar al-Fara'id al-Munazzama fi Akhbar al-Hajj wa-Tariq Makka al-Mu'azzama", printed; "Khulasat al-Dhahab fi Fadl al-'Arab", in manuscript; "'Umdat al-Safwa fi Hall al-Qahwa", in manuscript, after which the entry names the library of Muhammad Surur in Jeddah; and a "Majmu'" holding poems, letters and benefits {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
