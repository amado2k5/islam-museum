## Summary
Said ibn Zayd al-Adawi al-Qurashi was one of the earliest Muslims during the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]] in Mecca, according to the count in *The Sealed Nectar*. He became Muslim together with his wife Fatima bint al-Khattab {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}.

## In The Sealed Nectar
The book mentions him among those who followed the first vanguard: Said ibn Zayd al-Adawi and his wife [[person/fatima-bint-al-khattab|Fatima bint al-Khattab]] al-Adawiyya, sister of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. He was from the [[group/banu-adi|Banu Adi]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76}.

## Not covered here
This chapter of the book mentions only his early Islam. The rest of his story is found in its proper places in the book and in other sources.

## For young visitors
Said ibn Zayd became Muslim early, together with his wife Fatima. She was the sister of Umar ibn al-Khattab, who had not yet become Muslim.
