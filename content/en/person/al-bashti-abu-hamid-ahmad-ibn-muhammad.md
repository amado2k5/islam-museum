## Overview
Abu Hamid Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Bashti al-Kharazanji, a scholar from Nishapur. He is attributed to Basht and Kharazanj. He died in 348 AH (959 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
