## Overview
Wasif ibn Sawartekin, among the commanders of the state; he marched with a group of commanders who slipped through from al-Qadisiyya by the Khaffan road, met the Qarmati force and slaughtered them heavily that day, reaching its commander whom they struck with the sword, capturing a group of his household and companions — per the Muntazam. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v. 13 p. 49}
