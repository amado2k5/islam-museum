## Overview
Wasif b. Sawartakin, commander of al-Muktafi bi'llah; he was sent with a body of commanders against Zikrawayh the Qarmatian, pushing through from al-Qadisiyya on the Khaffan road. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 352}

## The battle of Khaffan
He met him on Saturday with eight days left of Rabi' I 294, and they fought that day: the Qarmatians were slaughtered wholesale, and Zikrawayh took a blow that mingled with his brain — he lived five days, then died. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 352}
