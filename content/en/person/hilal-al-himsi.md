## Overview
Hilal ibn Abi Hilal al-Himsi, a translator. Ibn al-Nadim reports that he translated the first four books of Apollonius's "Conics", in the presence of Ahmad ibn Musa ibn Shakir. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 373}
