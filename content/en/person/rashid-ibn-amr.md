## Overview
Rashid ibn Amr al-Jadidi, of the Azd. al-Baladhuri reports that Ziyad appointed him over the frontier of India. He came to Makran, raided the Qayqan and was victorious, then raided the Mid and was killed {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
