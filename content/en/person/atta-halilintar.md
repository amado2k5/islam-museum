## Summary
Atta Halilintar is an Indonesian content creator and entrepreneur born in 1994, one of Southeast Asia's most-subscribed YouTubers; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential creators. {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar} {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Career
The eldest of the eleven Halilintar siblings — a family that built a YouTube brand together — he grew his own channel through vlogs, music videos and challenges, becoming the first YouTuber in Southeast Asia to pass 30 million subscribers. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Business
He founded AHHA Media and the AHHA creative empire spanning content production, music releases, fashion and football — including ownership of the Indonesian club AHHA PS Pati — while appearing on Indonesian television. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Profile
His 2021 marriage to singer Aurel Hermansyah, daughter of star Anang Hermansyah, was a national media event; the couple's family channels dominate Indonesian YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Assessment
He is regarded as the pioneer of Indonesian YouTube stardom at scale, and of the creator-as-media-company model in Southeast Asia; scrutiny of his commercial ventures is reported attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar} {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar}


## YouTube record
He was the first Southeast Asian YouTuber to pass ten million subscribers and remains among the most-subscribed creators in Asia — a scale that press coverage describes as reshaping the Indonesian creator economy. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Media and business
Beyond content he runs a media group — production, sports and esports ventures — and fronts television formats; his family brand AHHA extends across consumer products. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## Family empire
The Halilintar family — eleven siblings each with followings — is itself a media phenomenon he leads, with joint channels and merchandise that press coverage calls Indonesia's first family of YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar} {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar}

## Origins
Born Muhammad Attamimi Halilintar in Dumai in 1994 into a large family that became Indonesia's most-recognised YouTube dynasty, he began posting content in the early 2010s and rose to become the country's most-subscribed creator. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## The family empire
The Halilintar family — eleven siblings each with followings, a joint channel and a merchandise brand — is itself a media phenomenon he leads: press coverage describes it as Indonesia's first family of YouTube. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## The business model
His expansion from content into production, sports and esports — and the AHHA consumer brand — made him a model of turning a creator following into a diversified media business in Southeast Asia. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar} {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar}

## The Halilintar phenomenon
Atta Halilintar's rise — the first Southeast Asian YouTuber to pass ten million subscribers — made him the reference point for the scale the Indonesian creator economy could reach. The Halilintar family itself — eleven siblings each with followings, a joint channel and the AHHA merchandise brand — is a media phenomenon he leads: press coverage describes it as Indonesia's first family of YouTube. His expansion from content into production, sports and esports made him a model of the creator-to-conglomerate trajectory. {cite:wikipedia-atta-halilintar}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists him among the most influential Muslims in media and arts, citing his position as Southeast Asia's most-subscribed creator and his role in building the Halilintar family media empire. {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar}



## Standing in the field

Within creator media, Atta Halilintar stands as the first Southeast Asian YouTuber past ten million subscribers and head of the Halilintar family channel — a position that explains the scale of public attention the career has attracted. The Muslim 500's listing places Atta Halilintar among the most influential Muslims of the present generation in this field, reflecting both the size of the audience the work reaches and the extent to which the figure has come to represent it beyond specialist circles. {cite:muslim500-atta-halilintar}
