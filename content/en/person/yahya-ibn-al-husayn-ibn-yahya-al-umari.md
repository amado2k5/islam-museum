## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn Yahya, of the line of Umar ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, was one of two men to whom, the narrator says, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s letter came at Medina in Dhu al-Qa'da 283 AH, the other being [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]]; the narrator says no other addressee was known. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} He left with Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah for [[place/al-fur-medina|al-Fur']] and al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} See [[event/al-hadi-letters-to-medina-283]].
