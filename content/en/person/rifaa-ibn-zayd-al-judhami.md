## Overview
Rifa'a ibn Zayd al-Judhami, then al-Dubaybi, came to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] during the truce of al-Hudaybiyya before Khaybar. He became Muslim and his Islam was good, and he carried the Prophet's letter to his people, and they became Muslim {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.243}.

## His coming
He came from Judham and then from Dubayb, and the Prophet gave him a slave as a gift. Ibn Hisham reports that he became Muslim during this truce {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.243}. See [[event/islam-of-rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|the Islam of Rifa'a ibn Zayd al-Judhami]].

## To his people
He carried the Prophet's letter to his people, calling them, and they became Muslim and settled at Harrat al-Rajla {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.243}. His story is connected with [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|the expedition of Zayd against Judham]], for he rode to the Prophet with his letter, and what had been taken was returned to his people {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}. He is of [[group/judham|Judham]].
