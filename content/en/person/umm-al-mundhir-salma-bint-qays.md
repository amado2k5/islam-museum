## Summary
Salma bint Qays ibn Amr, Umm al-Mundhir al-Ansariyya, better known by her kunya, sister of Sulayt ibn Qays and one of the Prophet's maternal aunts through his father. She prayed towards both qiblas and swore the pledge of Ridwan. Umm Sulayt ibn Ayyub ibn al-Hakam related from her. The book gives no date of death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7150}

## In Ghadanfar's Great Women of Islam
[[person/mahmood-ahmad-ghadanfar|Ghadanfar]] describes her as a woman of Banu al-Najjar, sister of [[person/salit-ibn-qays|Salit ibn Qays]], a veteran of Badr, who accepted Islam at the call of [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr]] and was present at the prayer during which the change of qibla was revealed. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.248} She was married to Qays ibn Sa'sa'a ibn Wahb and was known by the kunya from her son al-Mundhir; her sisters Umm Sulaym bint Qays and Umayra bint Qays also accepted Islam and pledged allegiance. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.249} She relates that she was among the Ansari women who pledged to the Prophet not to associate anything with God, not to steal, commit adultery, kill their children, bring slander or disobey in what is right — and he added: not to deceive their husbands. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.249} He says that on the day of Qurayza she gave protection to Rifa'a ibn Samaw'al al-Qurazi and asked the Prophet to grant him safety, which he did, and Rifa'a later accepted Islam; and that Rayhana bint Zayd of Banu Qurayza was brought to the Prophet in her house — the reports on Rayhana's status differ. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.250} She was among those who pledged under the tree at the [[event/bayat-al-ridwan|Pledge of Ridwan]]. {cite:great-women-of-islam-ghadanfar-darussalam p.251}
