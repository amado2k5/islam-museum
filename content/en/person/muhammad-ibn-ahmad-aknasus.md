## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad Aknasus, a Sufi preacher and one of the leading figures of the Tijaniyya order in the province of Sous in Morocco. The order spread through the province by his efforts. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.294-295}

## Dates
He was born in 1776 CE and died in 1294 AH (1877 CE), according to the encyclopaedia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.294-295}
