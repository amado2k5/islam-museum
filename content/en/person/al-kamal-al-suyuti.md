## Overview
Al-Kamal Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr, the father of the author Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti. He was an imam to the second caliph al-Mustakfi, and held a high place with him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.773-775}

## With al-Mustakfi
Al-Suyuti says that his father was held in great respect by the caliph, and that he wrote out for him a copy of the deed of the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.773-775}

## His effect on his son
Al-Suyuti relates his son's words that they were only raised in his house and his merit, and that he did not live after al-Mustakfi more than forty days. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.773-775}
