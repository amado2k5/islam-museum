## Summary
[[person/abu-lubaba-ibn-abd-al-mundhir|Abu Lubaba ibn Abd al-Mundhir]] al-Ansari, of the Aws and of [[group/banu-amr-ibn-awf|Banu Amr ibn Awf]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1755}. His name is disputed — Musa ibn Uqba said Bashir (with dotted b), and so Abu al-Aswad from Urwa, though some said it unpointed; Ibn Ishaq said Rifa'a, as did Ibn Numayr and others; and the author of al-Kashshaf and others mentioned his name as Marwan {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1755}.

## Badr and Medina
Ibn Ishaq said: "they claim" the Prophet sent Abu Lubaba and al-Harith ibn Hatib back after they had gone out with him to [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]], appointed Abu Lubaba over Medina, and allotted them both their shares and reward with the men of Badr; Musa ibn Uqba likewise counted him among the Badr men {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1755}.

## Al-Aqaba and the banner
They said he was one of the chiefs on the night of [[event/pledges-of-aqaba|al-Aqaba]], that Rifa'a and Ma'shar were his brothers, and that Banu Amr ibn Awf's banner on the day of the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest]] was with him {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1755}.

## Transmission and death
He related from the Prophet; from him relate his two sons al-Sa'ib and Abd al-Rahman, Abdullah ibn Umar, his father Salim ibn Abdullah, his client Nafi', Abdullah ibn Ka'b ibn Malik and others. It is said he died in Ali's caliphate; Khalifa said he died after Uthman's killing; it is said he lived past fifty AH {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1755}.
