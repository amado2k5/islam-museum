## Overview
Abd al-Masih ibn Buqayla guided Sa'd to the site of Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.388}

## Guiding him to the land of Kufa
Al-Baladhuri reports that he came to Sa'd and said that he would show him land that sloped down from the desert and rose above the open ground. He then showed him the site of present-day Kufa, which was called Sursistan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.388}
