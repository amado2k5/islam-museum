## Overview
Abu Maryam al-Hanafi, a man of the Rashidun era. Abu Musa [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|al-Ash'ari]] sent him to the people of Ramhurmuz after they broke the truce. He made peace with them for eight hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
