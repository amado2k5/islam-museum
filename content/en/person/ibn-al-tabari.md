## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Ali, Abu Hamid al-Marwazi, known as Ibn al-Tabari, was a judge, one of the memorisers of hadith, of Tabaristan, versed in history. He died in 376 AH (986 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.115}

He studied fiqh at Baghdad and Balkh, held the chief judgeship of Khurasan, and settled at Bukhara where he died at an advanced age. He wrote a Kitab al-Tarikh described as admirable. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.115}
