## Overview
Abu al-Mutrib Ubayd ibn Ayyub al-Anbari, a poet. al-Mas'udi cites his verses in the section on ghouls and the sa'ali, where he describes the ghoul as a companion of the man of the wilderness {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.470-471}.
