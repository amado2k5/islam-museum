## Overview
Abu Bakr ibn Ali al-Sanhaji, known as al-Bayduq, from the Sanhaja tribe of the central Maghrib. He was born around 490 AH (1096 CE) and died in 555 AH (1160 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
