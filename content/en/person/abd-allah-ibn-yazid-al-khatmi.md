## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Yazid al-Khatmi, Abu Musa, a Companion who witnessed Hudaybiyya as a youth and was Ali's governor in Kufa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}

## Transmission
He narrated from the Prophet, Abu Ayyub, Abu Mas'ud and Zayd ibn Thabit; al-Sha'bi and Muhammad ibn Sirin narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3491}
