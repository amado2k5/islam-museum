## Summary
Of Banu Sahm, he emigrated to Abyssinia. Ibn Ishaq describes him as the envoy of the Messenger of God to Khosrow {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}.

## In the chapter on the emigration to Abyssinia
Ibn Ishaq mentioned him among Banu Sahm, and described him as "the envoy of the Messenger of God to Khosrow" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}. The book does not give the details of his mission here. They are reviewed in the chapter on the Prophet's envoys to the kings (p.254-255, a later batch).

## Tribe
According to Ibn Ishaq, he belongs to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}.

## His humour in the expedition of Alqama
Ibn Hisham relates from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri that Alqama ibn Mujazziz appointed him over a group of his army, and "he had a sense of humour". He lit a fire, took an oath of hearing and obedience from the people, and then commanded them to leap into it. When they were about to, he said: "Sit down, I was only joking with you." When the Prophet heard of it, he said: "Whoever commands you to disobedience, do not obey him." See [[event/sariyya-of-alqama-to-jeddah|the expedition]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/289}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh relates that Abdullah ibn Hudhafa al-Sahmi was taken prisoner with some Companions, and the Byzantine king offered him half his kingdom to become Christian; he refused, and was threatened with a cauldron of boiling oil into which two prisoners were thrown until their flesh melted; then Abdullah agreed to kiss the king's head on condition that all the prisoners be freed, and when they returned to Medina Umar rose and kissed his head, and the Companions followed. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep15 00:05:30}
