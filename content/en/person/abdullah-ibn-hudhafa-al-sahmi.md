## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia, and Ibn Ishaq describes him as the Messenger of God's envoy to Khosrow. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Sahm, describing him as "the Messenger of God's envoy to Khosrow". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} The book does not give here the details of his mission; see the chapter on the Prophet's envoys to the kings (pp. 254-255, a later batch).

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
