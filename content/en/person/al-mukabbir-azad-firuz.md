## Overview
Azad Firuz ibn Jashish, the agent of Khosrow over Bahrain. The Arabs called him "al-Mukabbir" because he used to cut off hands and feet. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.349}

## The report on the Banu Tamim
Ibn al-Athir relates that Khosrow ordered him to kill the Banu Tamim. He lured them into the fortress of al-Mushaqqar in Hajar on the pretext of supplies. He killed their men, kept their boys, and crossed with them to Fars. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.349}
