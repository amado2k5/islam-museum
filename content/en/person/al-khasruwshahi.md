## Overview
The scholar and mutakallim al-Khasrushahi — of Khasrushah, a village near Marw; he travelled and took kalam from the imam Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, excelling in it, heard al-Mu'ayyad al-Tusi, advanced in usul and the rational sciences, dwelt in Syria at Karak a while under al-Nasir, mastered many sciences including philosophy, taught and debated. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 263-264}

## His works
He abridged al-Muhadhdhab in fiqh and Ibn Sina's al-Shifa'; he has fine objections and comments; al-Dimyati transmitted from him, and the khatib Zayn al-Din Ibn al-Marhal studied under him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 263-264}
