## Summary
Muhammad Ajmal Raza Qadri, known as Peer Ajmal Raza Qadri, is a Pakistani preacher residing in Gujranwala, Punjab. He is the khateeb of Jama Masjid Rahmaniya Rizvia, the founder of Tehreek-e-Islahe Muashra (the Movement for the Reform of Society), and the presenter of "Rah-e-Aamaal" on ARY Qtv. He has no Wikipedia article, and no available source gives his date of birth, so the museum builds this record on his official channel and on a circulated published biography, taken as they are. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ajmal-raza-qadri} {cite:clipzag-ajmal-raza-qadri-bio}

## Career
Qadri resides in Gujranwala; his native village is Sultan Pura in Ghakhar. He is a mureed (Sufi disciple) of Mufti Akhtar Raza Khan Qadri Barelvi, the former Grand Mufti of India, and khateeb at Jama Masjid Rahmaniya Rizvia. He founded Tehreek-e-Islahe Muashra; its centre is the Markaz Islahe Muashra / Khanqah Shareef Ghakkhar on G.T. Road, Ghakhar, where religious gatherings are held, including a Mehfil Shab-e-Qadr in 2023. {cite:clipzag-ajmal-raza-qadri-bio}

His official YouTube channel, run by his official team, publishes his lectures on the Quran, Hadith, the history of Islam, the Islamic way of life and the Sunnah, and the lives of the prophets, the Companions and the awliya (Sufi saints). He has lectured at Urs-e-Ala-Hazrat programmes, including a lecture titled "Ala Hazrat Aor Fatwa Navesi". {cite:youtube-muhammad-ajmal-raza-qadri}

## Works
Qadri presents "Rah-e-Aamaal" on ARY Qtv. He authored "Hayat-e-Sadr-ul-Ulama", on the life of Shaykh Tahseen Rida Khan (Muhaddis-e-Barelvi), published by Raza Academy, Mumbai. His official website is his movement's site (tehreekislahemuashra.com). {cite:clipzag-ajmal-raza-qadri-bio}

## School and affiliation
The circulated biographical text describes Qadri as a Sunni, Sufi Muslim scholar; he usually speaks on the awliya Allah and follows the poetry of Ahmed Raza Khan Qadri, and he is a disciple of Mufti Akhtar Raza Khan Barelvi, as recorded above. The museum reports this description as the sources give it, and does not record a school in his structured data, because its rule requires two independent sources naming it explicitly. {cite:clipzag-ajmal-raza-qadri-bio}

## Reach
Through his official channel and his television presence, Qadri's lectures have reached a wide audience in Pakistan and among the Muslim diaspora. Platform figures change constantly and are recorded here only with a date; his channel stood at about 971,000 subscribers in the harvest of October 2026. {cite:youtube-muhammad-ajmal-raza-qadri}
