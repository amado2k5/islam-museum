## Overview
Ali ibn Dawud, a secretary of Zubayda. Ibn al-Nadim says in the "Fihrist" that he was among those who turned "Kalila wa Dimna" into verse, and that he composed tales and fables in the voices of people, birds and beasts. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.423}
