## Overview
Yahya ibn Umar ibn Yusuf ibn Amir al-Kinani al-Andalusi al-Jayyani, Abu Zakariya, was a Maliki jurist learned in hadith. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 213 AH, 828 CE, and his death as 289 AH, 902 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 160}.

## Life
He was a client of the Banu Umayya and one of the people of Jaen. He grew up in Cordoba, lived in Qayrawan and travelled to the East, then settled in Sousse, where his grave is {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 160}. Al-Zirikli says that people journeyed to him in his time {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 160}.

## Works
He left writings amounting to about forty parts. Al-Zirikli names among them al-Muntakhaba, an abridgment of al-Mustakhraja in jurisprudence; Ahmiyat al-Husun; al-Waswasa; al-Nisa; Fada'il al-Munastir wa-l-Ribat; a refutation of al-Shafi'i; a refutation of the Shukukiyya; a refutation of the Murji'a; and Ahkam al-Suq, which is printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 160}.
