## Overview
Jabr ibn Abi Ubayd, son of Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud al-Thaqafi. Al-Baladhuri says that after his paternal uncle al-Hakam he took the banner on the day of the Bridge, and was killed; then [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] took the banner and withdrew with the people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.352}

## Death
The museum's data date his death to the ninth month of the year 13 AH, approximately. This is a date from report and needs a second source.
