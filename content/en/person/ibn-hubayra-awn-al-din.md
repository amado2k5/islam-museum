## Summary
Awn al-Din ibn Hubayra: vizier and scholar, linked in the museum's record to Baghdad. The Biblissima record places his life between 1105 and 1165. {cite:biblissima Q16675} The museum's record card names al-Ifsah as the work most linked to his name.
## In the sources
The Biblissima record gives the form of name «Yaḥyā ibn Muḥammad Ibn Hubayrah» together with the two dates above; it is an open authority record and carries no narrative of his life. His entry in the biographical dictionaries has not yet been read on the page, so no detail of his upbringing, teachers or writings is given here beyond the above; those are to be added from those books. {cite:biblissima Q16675}
