## Overview
Habsha ibn Dahir returned to Brahmanabad as king when his father was killed. Then [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] wrote to him, through the kings of India, calling them to Islam and to obedience, on condition that he would make them kings. He embraced Islam, together with the kings, and they took Arab names. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
