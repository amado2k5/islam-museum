## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Maslama al-Tujibi, known as Ibn al-Aftas, ruler of Badajoz in western al-Andalus. Ibn Khaldun says he seized power there during the strife of 461 AH; when he died, his son al-Muzaffar Abu Bakr succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1001}

## The party kings
Ibn Khaldun counts his son al-Muzaffar among the great party kings (muluk al-tawa'if), and mentions wars between him and Ibn Dhi al-Nun, as well as with Ibn Abbad over Ibn Yahya, the lord of Melilla. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1001}
