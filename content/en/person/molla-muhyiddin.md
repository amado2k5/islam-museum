## Summary
Molla Muhyi al-Din Muhammad, a scholar of [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed II]]'s reign {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 147}.

## The argument that saved the page
When the viziers' intercession failed for the page who had struck Edirne's qadi, they begged Muhyi al-Din to make the sultan's peace. He argued: "the deputy, by rising from the bench in anger, fell from the rank of judge — so at the moment of the blow he was no qadi, and the law's dishonour does not warrant killing." The sultan fell silent {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 147}.
