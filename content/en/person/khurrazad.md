## Overview
Khurrazad, brother of the king of Khwarazm. He opposed his brother and grew strong against him. The king sent to Qutayba ibn Muslim, offering him money and the keys on condition that he make the king master of his lands, not his brother. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
