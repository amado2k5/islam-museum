## Overview
Al-Malik al-Nasir Faraj ibn Barquq, a Circassian Mamluk sultan. He was set up in his father's place in the sultanate when his father died in Shawwal 801 AH, and was given the title al-Nasir. In Rabi' al-Awwal 808 AH he was deposed, and his brother Abd al-Aziz was set up and given the title al-Mansur. Abd al-Aziz was then deposed in Jumada al-Akhira, and al-Nasir Faraj was restored. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.761}

## His end
He went out to fight Amir Shaykh, was defeated and killed, and the caliph al-Musta'in was given the sultanate together with the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.765}

## In Yeomans
Faraj came to the throne as a boy and at once faced the revolt of the Syrian amirs; when the Mamluk-Ottoman alliance broke down Tamerlane occupied Aleppo, Hama, Homs and Damascus, and during this occupation Ibn Khaldun, in Faraj's company, visited Tamerlane at Damascus in 1401. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.208} War ruined the economies of Egypt and Syria, plague and famine followed, and Faraj was eventually assassinated; Yeomans counts him the last sultan to come to power by heredity. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.208} He built [[building/farag-ibn-barquq-complex|his khanqah]] in the Eastern Cemetery (1400-1411), the largest in Cairo, and moved his father's body there. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.202} {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.204}
