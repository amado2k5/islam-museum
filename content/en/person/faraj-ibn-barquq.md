## Overview
Al-Malik al-Nasir Faraj ibn Barquq, a Circassian Mamluk sultan. He was set up in his father's place in the sultanate when his father died in Shawwal 801 AH, and was given the title al-Nasir. In Rabi' al-Awwal 808 AH he was deposed, and his brother Abd al-Aziz was set up and given the title al-Mansur. Abd al-Aziz was then deposed in Jumada al-Akhira, and al-Nasir Faraj was restored. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.761}

## His end
He went out to fight Amir Shaykh, was defeated and killed, and the caliph al-Musta'in was given the sultanate together with the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.765}
