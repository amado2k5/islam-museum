## Overview
Sa'id ibn Salih al-Hajib, sent by al-Mu'tazz in Ramadan 252 AH to meet al-Musta'in being carried from Wasit; he met him near al-Qatul, beat him with a whip, then laid him down, sat on his chest, cut off his head and carried it away.

## In Tarikh al-Khulafa — al-Suyuti (Dar al-Minhaj edition)
Sa'id al-Hajib. The author of Tarikh al-Khulafa' reports that he was appointed to kill al-Musta'in, after Ahmad ibn Tulun refused to kill the sons of the caliphs {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.556}.

## In Tarikh al-Khulafa — al-Suyuti (Dar al-Minhaj edition): The account
Al-Musta'in was slaughtered on the third of Shawwal in the year 252 AH, when he was thirty-one years old {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.556}.

## In Tarikh al-Khulafa — al-Suyuti (Dar al-Minhaj edition): Notes for researchers
- The account rests on a single source and needs an additional one. The data does not make clear whether "thirty-one" refers to al-Musta'in's age or to Sa'id's.
