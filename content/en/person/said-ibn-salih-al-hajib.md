## Overview
Sa'id ibn Salih al-Hajib, sent by al-Mu'tazz in Ramadan of the year 252 AH to meet al-Musta'in, who had been carried from Wasit. al-Mas'udi reports that he met him near al-Qatul, killed him and carried his head to al-Mu'tazz. The body was left on the road until common people buried it {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.545-546}.

## His companion's words
al-Mas'udi reports that al-Musta'in said to his companion Shahak: "If the leader of the people is Sa'id al-Hajib, then I am lost" {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.545-546}.
