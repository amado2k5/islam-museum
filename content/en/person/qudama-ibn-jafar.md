## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Qudama ibn Ja'far, a writer and critic. His grandfather was a Christian who embraced Islam, and his father was a scribe. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopedia says he was born in 275 AH (888 CE) and died in 337 AH (948 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
