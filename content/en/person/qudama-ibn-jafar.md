## Overview
Abu al-Faraj Qudama ibn Ja'far, a writer and critic. His grandfather was a Christian who embraced Islam, and his father was a scribe. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopedia says he was born in 275 AH (888 CE) and died in 337 AH (948 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## The Kitab al-kharaj in de Goeje's edition
The extracts of the "Book of Land Tax and the Secretary's Craft" printed in volume VI of the *Bibliotheca Geographorum Arabicorum* name him Abu al-Faraj Qudama ibn Ja'far, the Baghdadi secretary. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.192} In chapter 11 of the fifth station he describes the postal office and requires its head to be trustworthy and careful, then lists the empire's roads stage by stage starting with the Mecca road; in the sixth station he divides the inhabited earth into climes and ends with the lands of the west and al-Andalus. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.192} {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.193} {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.238} {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.274} See [[work/kitab-al-kharaj-qudama|the Kitab al-kharaj]].
