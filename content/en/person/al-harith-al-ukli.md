## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Yazid al-Ukli, a jurist of Kufa of his generation. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}

## His connection with the jurists of Kufa
He is mentioned together with al-Mughira ibn Miqsam, Abu Ma'shar, Abu Hashim, Mansur and others who took knowledge from al-Sha'bi and al-Nakha'i. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.44}

Ibn Shubruma used to say: "If I and al-Harith agree on a question, we care not who differs from us." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.45}

He should not be confused with [[person/al-harith-al-awar|al-Harith al-A'war]]. The page gives no dates.
