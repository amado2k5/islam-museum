## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Husayn, known as Bayram al-Thani, a Tunisian scholar and jurist from the Bayram family. He was born around 1162 AH (1748 CE) and died around 1247 AH (1831 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is the Shaykh al-Islam, son of Bayram the First, as the encyclopaedia mentions. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
