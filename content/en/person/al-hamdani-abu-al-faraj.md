## Overview
Abu al-Faraj al-Makhzumi al-Nasibi, a poet, prose writer and man of letters, born in Nasibin. He was born in 315 AH (927 CE) and died in 398 AH (1008 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.332}

The source says that little is known of his upbringing. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.332}

Review note: the page's heading gives "al-Hamdani", while the name in the data is "al-Makhzumi". The matter needs an additional source.
