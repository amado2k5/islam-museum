## Summary
Al-'Amid Ibn al-Qalanisi, Abu Ya'la Hamza b. Arshad al-Tamimi al-Dimashqi the katib, author of the Tarikh Dimashq that ends in 555; he transmitted from Sahl b. Bishr al-Isfara'ini, held the town's headship twice, and also sought the path; he died in Rabi' I 555, aged eighty-something. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 654}
