## Overview
Al-Wathiq billah Umar ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Mustamsik ibn al-Hakim, a caliph in Cairo between 785 and 788 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.761,763}

## His caliphate
He was given the allegiance after al-Mutawakkil was deposed in Rajab 785 AH, and he continued until he died on Wednesday, the nineteenth of Shawwal 788 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.761,763}
