## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. al-'Abbas b. Muhammad b. Abi Muhammad al-Yazidi the grammarian — his grandfather Muhammad was Yahya b. al-Mubarak al-'Adawi al-Yazidi; an imam in grammar and adab who transmitted the rarities and speech of the Arabs. Late in life he was summoned to teach al-Muqtadir's sons and attended them a while; after he attached himself to the caliph, one of his companions styled himself after him and asked to read under him — he answered: 'I am otherwise occupied.' {cite:wafayat-ayan}
