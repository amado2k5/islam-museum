## Overview
Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn ibn Abi Hurayra al-Baghdadi, a judge and jurist. He studied with [[person/ahmad-ibn-umar-ibn-surayj|Abu al-Abbas ibn Surayj]] and with [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]], explained the Mukhtasar of al-Muzani, and Abu Ali al-Tabari wrote glosses on his commentary. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}

He taught in Baghdad and died in 345 AH. Al-Khatib says that he died in Rajab; the date needs a second source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 59}
