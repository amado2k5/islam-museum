## Overview
Lutfullah ibn Muhammad al-Arzurumi was a Hanafi jurist from Erzurum, which al-Zirikli places in Turkey. He died in Aleppo in 1202 AH (1788 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 241}. The entry gives no year of birth.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left books, among them "Ramuz al-Tahrir wa-al-Tafsir", a manuscript in one volume held in the Mevlevihane library at Galata in Istanbul {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 241}.
