## Overview
Al-Hajjaj ibn Artat, whose kunya is Abu Artat. The entry says he went with al-Mahdi to al-Rayy, and that he died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His lineage, the year of his death and his work need an additional source.
