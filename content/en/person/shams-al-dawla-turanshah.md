## Overview
Shams al-Dawla Turanshah ibn Ayyub, brother of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] the elder. Ibn al-Athir records his affairs in Egypt, Nubia and Yemen {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}.

## Egypt and Nubia
After the battle between the two palaces in the year 564 AH, he put to the sword the Sudanese who had taken refuge in al-Jiza. In Jumada al-Ula of the year 568 AH he marched to the land of Nubia and besieged the fortress of Abzim until its people surrendered it. Then he saw how little the revenue was and how hard life was, and he returned to Egypt. Most of the booty was slaves and slave-girls {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}.

Ibn al-Athir reports that the reason for the march was the fear of Salah al-Din and his family that Nur al-Din would enter Egypt, so they wanted a land to which they could withdraw {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.45-46}.

## Zabid
He asked Nur al-Din's permission to attack Abd al-Nabi, lord of Zabid, for cutting the Abbasid sermon. He set out at the beginning of Rajab in the year 569 AH, and took part in the [[event/turanshah-takes-zabid-569|conquest of Zabid]] by storm, and established the Abbasid sermon there {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.52-53}.
