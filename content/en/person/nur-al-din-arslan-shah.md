## Overview
Abu'l-Harith Arslan Shah b. 'Izz al-Din Mas'ud b. Qutb al-Din Mawdud b. 'Imad al-Din Zangi, lord of Mosul titled al-Malik al-'Adil Nur al-Din; he ruled Mosul after his father's death — a discerning king, versed in affairs. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His madhhab and his madrasa
He transferred to the Shafi'i madhhab, the only Shafi'i in his house, and built a madrasa for the Shafi'is at Mosul whose beauty few madrasas match; he left two sons — al-Malik al-Qahir 'Izz al-Din Mas'ud and al-Malik al-Mansur 'Imad al-Din Zangi. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
