## Summary

Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani (born 1980) is a Qatari politician who has been Prime Minister of Qatar since March 2023 and Minister of Foreign Affairs since 2016 {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar}.

## Life

Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim Al Thani was born and grew up in Doha, and is a member of the ruling House of Thani {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar}. He graduated in economics and business administration from Qatar University in 2003 {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar}.

## Work

Al Thani began as an economic researcher at the Supreme Council for Family Affairs in 2003 and directed its economic affairs until 2009, then led the enterprise-support and public-private partnership work at the Ministry of Business and Trade, helping establish Enterprise Qatar {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en}. He joined the foreign ministry in 2013 as assistant minister for international cooperation, chaired the Qatar Fund for Development from 2014 to 2024, and became foreign minister on 27 January 2016 {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar}. He was made deputy prime minister in November 2017 and chaired the Qatar Investment Authority from 2018 to 2023 {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en}. On 7 March 2023, on Khalid bin Khalifa Al Thani's resignation, he was sworn in as Prime Minister while keeping the foreign ministry {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar}. No text read for this record names a faith for Al Thani himself - his membership of the ruling house is dynastic context, not attestation - so his faith is recorded as unknown {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-en} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ar} {cite:wikipedia-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani-ur} {cite:wikidata-mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani}.
