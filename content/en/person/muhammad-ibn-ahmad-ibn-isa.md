## Overview
Ibn Ahmad ibn Isa ibn al-Shaykh. He fortified himself in Amid after his father died in 286 AH, and the caliph al-Mu'tadid besieged it and deployed his armies around it.

## The siege
He refused the letter of his paternal aunt Umm al-Sharif, and told her messenger that states are not governed by the opinions of women. When the fighting grew fierce, he came down under safe conduct. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.604-606}
