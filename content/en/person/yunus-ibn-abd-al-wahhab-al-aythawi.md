## Overview
Yunus ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Bakr al-Aythawi was a Shafi'i jurist, born and died in Damascus {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}. He was the father of Ahmad ibn Yunus, whose entry al-A'lam gives earlier {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}.

## What was said of him
Al-Ghazzi described him as one who benefited the students and as preacher of the Muslims {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}.

## Works
In Shafi'i law he wrote «al-Jami' al-Mughni li-Uli al-Raghabat», a manuscript, and «Sharh al-Ina'a», «Sharh al-Waraqat», «Tashih al-Ghaya», «Tawdih al-Tashih», a manuscript, and a «Diwan Khutab» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}. He also wrote verse, and al-Zirikli remarks that his language is weak {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}.

## Dates
Al-A'lam gives his birth as 898 AH, equal to 1492 CE, and his death as 976 AH, equal to 1569 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 235}.
