## Overview
Abaqa ibn Hulagu, the second Ilkhan of the Hulaguid house in Iraq, son of [[person/hulagu|Hulagu]] and ruler of the [[polity/ilkhanate|Ilkhanate]]. He succeeded his father in 663 AH. In 680 AH he marched toward al-Sham in coordination with Mengü-Temür, ruler of the Dasht in the north, and camped near Hama with his brother Mengü-Temür. [[person/qalawun|Al-Mansur Qalawun]] met them near Homs and gained the upper hand; many of them were killed and others were taken prisoner, and Abaqa returned defeated to his lands. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The year of his death is not documented in the museum's data. It is likely around 680 AH, and an additional source is needed.
