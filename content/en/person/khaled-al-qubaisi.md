## Summary
Khaled Abdulla Al Qubaisi is an Emirati businessman and endurance racing driver, born on 22 December 1975 in Abu Dhabi. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} {cite:wikidata-khaled-al-qubaisi} He has held senior investment posts at Mubadala Investment Company while racing internationally in grand touring cars since the beginning of the 2010s. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en}

## Business career
Al Qubaisi holds a Master of Science in Project Management from George Washington University and a bachelor's degree in finance and operations management from Boston University, and was awarded the CFA charter in 2003. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} At Mubadala he served as chief executive officer of the Real Estate & Infrastructure Investments platform from 2021 to 2024, after earlier roles including chief executive officer of Aerospace, Renewables and ICT and Chief Human Capital Officer. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} Before Mubadala he was Chief Investment Officer at International Capital and Head of Corporate Finance & Business Development at the National Bank of Abu Dhabi. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} He is chairman of Tabreed and a director and vice chairman of Abu Dhabi Motor Sports Management. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en}

## Motorsport
Al Qubaisi's biggest racing successes were overall victories at the Dubai 24 Hour in 2012 and 2013, driving a Black Falcon Mercedes-Benz SLS AMG GT3, and a win at the 2013 Yas Marina 12 Hours. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} He raced three times at the 24 Hours of Le Mans, with his best finish in 2014, when he placed 19th overall. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en} His daughters Amna and Hamda Al Qubaisi are also racing drivers. {cite:wikipedia-khaled-al-qubaisi-en}
