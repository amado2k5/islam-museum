## Overview
Sadr al-Din Sulayman ibn Hilal ibn Shibl ibn Falah ibn Khatib, the learned, ascetic and pious qadi Abu al-Rabi' al-Hashimi al-Ja'fari, is known as Ibn Khatib Darayya. Born in 642 AH, he heard hadith and took fiqh from the two shaykhs Taj al-Din al-Fazari and Muhyi al-Din al-Nawawi. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.120}

He held the khitaba of Darayya, repeated lessons at the Nasiriyya, deputised in judgement for years — in year 719 people sought rain through him and were granted it — then held the khitaba of the Tawba mosque and left the deputyship. Al-Dhahabi says he was austere in dress, small turban and food, humble, mild and gentle. He transmitted from Ibn Abi al-Yusr and al-Miqdad and knew fiqh. He died in Dhu al-Qa'da and was buried at Bab al-Saghir near his shaykh Taj al-Din. {cite:shadharat-dhahab v.8 p.120}
