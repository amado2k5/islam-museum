## Summary
'Izz al-Din Ibn 'Awad, the Hanbali chief qadi; he deputised for Shaykh Taj al-Din Ibn al-Farkah many times and headed the bench under Sultan Khalil — he was the last of Egypt's Maliki-nursed qadis; he had no shaykh in fiqh to rely on, and died in Cairo in Safar aged sixty-six. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 762}
