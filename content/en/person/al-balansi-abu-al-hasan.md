## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan al-Khayr b. Muhammad b. Sahl al-Ansari of al-Andalus, of Valencia, the muhaddith; he journeyed to the Mashriq and travelled in trade as far as China — a jurist-scholar, exacting — hearing Abu 'Abdallah al-Na'ali, Turad b. Muhammad and a company; he dwelt at Isfahan a while then Baghdad, reading fiqh under al-Ghazali. He died in Muharram 541. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 608-609}
