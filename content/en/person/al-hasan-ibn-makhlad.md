## Overview
Al-Hasan b. Makhlad b. al-Jarrah, of the viziers' house; he was made vizier for al-Mu'tamad on the morrow of 'Ubaydallah b. Yahya b. Khaqan's death in 263; then Musa b. Bugha advanced, so he fled to Baghdad and Sulayman b. Wahb was made vizier in his place on 6 Dhu'l-Hijja. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 120}
