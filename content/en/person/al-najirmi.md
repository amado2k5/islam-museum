## Overview
Abu Ya'qub Yusuf b. Ya'qub b. Isma'il b. Kharrazad al-Najirmi, the Basran philologist resident of Egypt; of a house holding a company of excellent, deeply-versed philologists — he was the exemplar of his house and a brilliant calligrapher: Egyptians so coveted his hand that a copy of Jarir's diwan in his writing fetched ten dinars. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
