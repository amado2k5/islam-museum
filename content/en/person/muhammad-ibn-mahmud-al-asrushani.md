## Summary
Muhammad ibn Mahmud ibn Husayn, Abu al-Fath Majd al-Din al-Asrushani, from Asrushana east of Samarqand, a Hanafi jurist. He studied with his father, with the author of al-Hidaya, with Nasir al-Din al-Samarqandi and Zahir al-Din al-Bukhari. His works include al-Fusul on transactions, Jami' ahkam al-sighar, al-Fatawa and Qurrat al-'aynayn. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8196}
