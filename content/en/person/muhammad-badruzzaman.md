## Summary
Muhammad Badruzzaman (মুহাম্মদ বদরুজ্জামান), also styled Badruzzaman Uzzal, is a Bangladeshi singer, composer and producer: founder and chief executive of the Islamic music label Holy Tune, and executive director of the cultural organisation Kalarab Shilpigosthi. {cite:holytune-website} {cite:musicmetricsvault-badruzzaman} His own artist biography presents him as "Singer, Songwriter, Music Composer, Entrepreneur and Member Of Bangladeshi Popular Band Kalarab Shilpigosthi… founder and CEO of Holy Tune". {cite:musicmetricsvault-badruzzaman} He is built here under the founder's ruling of 9 November 2026 that nasheed artists, composers and studio executives are in scope for person records, and he clears the lane's audience bar on his personal reach alone: his own YouTube channel (@bzamanofficial) counted 148,000 subscribers by vidIQ as updated on 8 November 2026. {cite:vidiq-bzamanofficial} His Facebook page counted 3.7 million followers by the metadata of his post of 11 February 2026. {cite:facebook-bzamanofficialpage}

## Career — the Kalarab years and the revival
In his own candidate manifesto of 6 October 2022, Badruzzaman recounts about eighteen years of work in Kalarab by that date, including his closeness to the organisation's founder, Aynuddin Al Azad. {cite:badruzzaman-kalarab-manifesto-2022} After Aynuddin Al Azad's sudden death in 2010 the organisation passed through a crisis, and it was revived under the leadership of Mufti Saeed Ahmad, Muhammad Badruzzaman and Abu Rayhan, with the establishment of the digital platform Holy Tune. {cite:wikipedia-kalarab-shilpigosthi-en} In the committee formed in 2017 he became joint executive director, carrying, by his own account, the organisation's and artists' management. {cite:badruzzaman-kalarab-manifesto-2022} In Kalarab's internal election of 7 October 2022 he was elected executive director for the 2023–24 session, with Sayed Ahmad elected director. {cite:jagonews24-kalarab-directors-2022} {cite:bangladesh-pratidin-kalarab-directors-2022} As a performer he sings on Kalarab releases in his own voice, and the organisation's discography credits him as director of many of its releases and as artist — and, for "Alifun Ba" (2023), lyricist as well. {cite:wikipedia-kalarab-shilpigosthi-en} His artist page lists among his popular songs Hasbi Rabbi Jallallah, Allah Bolo, Malikre Bhulia, Iqra, Cholar Pothe, Munajat and Eshke Muhammad. {cite:musicmetricsvault-badruzzaman}

## Holy Tune — founding and the label
Holy Tune is Kalarab's own record label and production company: it was established in 2016, has been under contract with Kalarab since its inception so that all Kalarab songs are exclusively produced and distributed by it, and it is owned by the singer Muhammad Badruzzaman. {cite:wikipedia-kalarab-shilpigosthi-en} The label's own website names him in its leadership section as "Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)" and carries a signed message from him as CEO, and its contact block places the label at 18 Purana Paltan, Dhaka. {cite:holytune-website} The label's YouTube channel (@holytunebdofficial) counted 10,800,000 subscribers at vidIQ's read of 5 November 2026 — a figure that belongs to the label as an entity, not to him personally, and is recorded on the label's own record. {cite:vidiq-holy-tune}

## Reported positions and public life
