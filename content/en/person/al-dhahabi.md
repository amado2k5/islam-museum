## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Uthman ibn Qaymaz, Shams al-Din al-Dhahabi the hafiz, of Turkmen origin, of Damascus: born on 3 Rabi' al-Akhir 673. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336} He studied in Damascus and Cairo; his works include Tarikh al-Islam and its abridgements, among them Siyar al-Nubala', al-Ibar and Tadhkirat al-Huffaz, and al-Mizan. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/337} He went blind some years before his death and died on the night of 3 Dhu al-Qa'da 748. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/338}

## Mizan al-I'tidal
Among al-Dhahabi's books is [[work/mizan-al-itidal|Mizan al-I'tidal fi Naqd al-Rijal]]; in its preface he says he wrote it after his book *al-Mughni* and added many names, most of them from Ibn Adi's *al-Kamil*. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 5} He records there that he had earlier abridged [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]]'s large book on weak transmitters and then supplemented it, one supplement after another. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 6} In the entry on [[person/al-waqidi|al-Waqidi]] he refers readers to his "great history" for more of his reports. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 666} In the entry on [[person/ibn-arabi-muhyi-al-din|Ibn Arabi]] he names his teacher Muhammad ibn Abi al-Dhikr al-Siqilli and his companion Abu al-Fath al-Ya'mari, from whose handwriting Ibn Rafi' copied a report for him. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 659} The publisher's note at the end of the Indian lithograph introduces al-Dhahabi and his works, saying the information is drawn from the books of Abu al-Hasanat Muhammad Abd al-Hayy. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v2 PDF p. 689}

Among the positions he takes in the book is his rule that what scholars say about one another should be weighed slowly, above all about a great imam, stated in defence of [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]]. {cite:mizan-itidal-bijawi-v3 p. 499} Another is his division of innovation into lesser and greater, set out in [[question/riwayat-ahl-al-bida|narration from people of innovation]]. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 7}
