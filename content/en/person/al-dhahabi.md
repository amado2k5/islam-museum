## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Uthman ibn Qaymaz, Shams al-Din al-Dhahabi the hafiz, of Turkmen origin, of Damascus: born on 3 Rabi' al-Akhir 673. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336} He studied in Damascus and Cairo; his works include Tarikh al-Islam and its abridgements, among them Siyar al-Nubala', al-Ibar and Tadhkirat al-Huffaz, and al-Mizan. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/337} He went blind some years before his death and died on the night of 3 Dhu al-Qa'da 748. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/338}
