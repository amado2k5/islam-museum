## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Nasr al-Marwazi, a jurist. He was born in Baghdad, raised in Nishapur, and settled in Samarqand. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}

## Dates
Al-Shirazi says that he was born in 202 AH and died in 294 AH. These have been converted approximately to the Gregorian calendar (around 818 CE and 907 CE). {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}

## Learning
He was among the most knowledgeable people about the disagreements of the Companions and those after them in the rulings. He wrote a book on the points where Abu Hanifa disagreed with Ali and with Abdallah. Abu Bakr al-Sayrafi said: "Had he written nothing but the book *al-Qasama* (On the Oath of Fifty), he would have been among the most learned of people in jurisprudence." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.56}
