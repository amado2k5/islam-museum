## Overview
Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman, a client of the Banu Tamim, whose father was one of the captives of Sistan. According to al-Baladhuri, he wrote in Arabic and Persian for Zadan Farrukh ibn Bayri, the secretary of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]]. Zadan Farrukh introduced him to al-Hajjaj, who came to favour him, and he transferred the register of the land tax of al-Sawad and all of Iraq into Arabic {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Iraq and its governance
He was appointed over Iraq. He was one of al-Hajjaj's fiercest opponents: he removed Muhammad ibn al-Qasim from Sind and imprisoned him at Wasit, in revenge for a brother of his whom al-Hajjaj had killed {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.106}.

al-Salabi reports that [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman]] entrusted him with the land tax of Iraq, and that Yazid ibn al-Muhallab did not appoint him to the land tax because he judged that Salih had men of knowledge in matters of tax {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.146}.
