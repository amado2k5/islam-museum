## Overview
Abu Muhammad 'Abdallah b. 'Ali b. Ishaq b. Ghaniya, the Sanhaji of Majorca, youngest of the brothers; he remained king of Majorca until the Almohad al-Nasir Muhammad b. Ya'qub fitted a fleet against him that landed on its shore — he sallied against them, brave, and his horse stumbled, he fell to the ground, they killed him, carried his head to Marrakesh and hung his body on the wall. Majorca stayed in Almohad hands until the Franks took it. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
