## Overview
Salah al-Din Khalil ibn Aybak al-Safadi, a Shafi'i jurist, historian and writer. He composed the reply of the king al-Salih Isma'il ibn al-Nasir Muhammad to the letter of the sultan Abu al-Hasan al-Marini. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.403}

## Licence to transmit
He gave a licence to transmit the two letters in his own hand, after 'Izz al-Din Abu Ya'la Hamza, son of the judge Qutb al-Din Musa ibn Ahmad al-Ahmadi, had heard them from him in one session in Dhu al-Qa'da 756 AH in the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus. Al-Safadi described their reading as delighting the ear with its eloquence. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.403}

## His works
The data attribute to him the book [[work/ayan-al-asr|A'yan al-Asr]], according to "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Khalil ibn Aybak al-Safadi: born about 696 or 697 AH, he first learned drawing and then turned to letters, studying with Ibn Sayyid al-Nas, Ibn Nubata and Abu Hayyan and hearing al-Dabusi in Cairo and al-Mizzi in Damascus. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/87} He served as a chancery scribe at Safad and Cairo, as secretary at Aleppo, and as agent of the treasury. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/87} His works include al-Wafi bi-l-Wafayat in about thirty volumes and A'yan al-Asr. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/87} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/88} He died at Damascus on the night of 10 Shawwal 764 AH. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/88}
