## Overview
Al-Qati'i al-Azji, the Hanbali historian; he heard from his father and from Abu'l-Waqt the Sahih al-Bukhari — being the last to transmit it from him — and kept to Ibn al-Jawzi a while, took from him and read many of his works and transmissions on him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 162-163}

## His history
He compiled a history in about five volumes continuing Ibn al-Sam'ani's, calling it Durrat al-iklil fi tatimmat al-tadhyil — rich in benefits though with errors; Ibn al-Najjar disparaged it excessively though he had taken and transmitted from it; several hafizes described him as a memoriser. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 162-163}
