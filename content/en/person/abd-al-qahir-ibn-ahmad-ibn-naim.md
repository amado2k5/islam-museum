## Overview
Abd al-Qahir ibn Ahmad ibn Na'im took control of [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] after a crowd of its people with Ahmad ibn Mahfuz broke the prison and drove out [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]], the deputy of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} The text says nothing else about him in these pages. See [[event/sanaa-rising-ahmad-ibn-mahfuz-288]].
