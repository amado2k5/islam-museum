## Overview
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr al-Tizini al-Halabi al-Hanafi, an astronomer and mathematician from Aleppo, attributed to the town of Tizin near Aleppo. He was born in 828 AH (1424 CE) and died in 911 AH (1505 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.745}
