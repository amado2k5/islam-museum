## Overview
Khan-i Jahan Junan Shah was the son of the vizier [[person/khan-i-jahan-maqbul-tilangani|Khan-i Jahan Maqbul Tilangani]] and served Sultan [[person/firuz-shah-tughluq|Firuz Shah Tughluq]] as vizier after his father for some twenty years. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 80} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 82} The inscription over the east gate of the Kali Masjid at Nizamuddin names its builder as "Junan Shah Maqbul, entitled Khan-i Jahan, son of Khan-i Jahan" and dates it AH 772. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 69}

## His mosques
He is known to have built seven congregational mosques in and around Delhi and was, the author reports, called a prince of builders. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 69} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 82} Inscriptions give him the [[building/kali-masjid-nizamuddin|Kali Masjid at Nizamuddin]] and the [[building/kalan-masjid-delhi|Kalan Masjid at Turkman Gate]], finished in Jumada II 789/1387; modern accounts attribute to him, without an inscription, the [[building/khirki-masjid-delhi|Khirki Masjid]], the [[building/begumpur-mosque|Begumpur mosque]] and the Kalu Sarai mosque. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 80} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 82} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 85} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90} The author says the Kalan Masjid was completed in the last year of his life, a year before the sultan's death. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 82}
