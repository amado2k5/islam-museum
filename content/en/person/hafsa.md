## Brief
Hafsa bint Umar was a wife of the Prophet and a daughter of the second caliph. Sunni tradition remembers her above all as the keeper of the collected Qur'anic codex — the copy from which the standard text was made.

## Life
Born about 605 CE, she married young, lost her husband to wounds after [[event/battle-of-badr]], and married the Prophet about 625 {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}. She lived in the Prophet's household in Medina until his death in 632.

Her lasting role came later. When the Qur'an was gathered under [[person/abu-bakr]] after the deaths of many reciters, the sheets were entrusted — in the traditional account — to Hafsa's keeping; when [[person/uthman-ibn-affan]] later ordered a standard recension, her copy was its base {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}. She died in Medina in 665 CE.

## Legacy
Between memory and manuscript, the tradition says the text passed through her house. That makes Hafsa a quiet but central figure in the history of the Qur'an as a written book.

## For young visitors
Before printing, books existed as a few handwritten pages. Hafsa kept the pages of the Qur'an safe in her home — and the copies we have today go back to hers.

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] gives the first half of the tenth episode of his programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]] to Hafsa bint Umar. He says her first husband, Khunays ibn Hudhafa, fought at Badr and was killed at Uhud, leaving her a widow of eighteen. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:01:30} Umar offered her to Abu Bakr, who said nothing, and to Uthman, who declined, and the Prophet told Umar that someone better than Uthman would marry Hafsa and Uthman would marry someone better than her: the Prophet married Hafsa and Uthman married Umm Kulthum. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep07 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep07 00:10:00} He takes from it that seeking a fine husband for a daughter is an old practice of the Companions. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:01:30}

In the household, he says, she and Aisha, close in age and the daughters of Abu Bakr and Umar, usually stood together. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:04:00} He tells how Umar warned her after learning that the Prophet's wives answered him back, and how the Prophet withdrew from his wives for a month and then offered them the choice between God and His Messenger and a kind release, and all chose God and His Messenger. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:09:30} He sees in these stories proof that they were human, with ordinary quarrels. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:05:30} He describes her as much given to fasting and night prayer, a learned woman who taught after the Prophet's death and took part in public life under the Rightly Guided Caliphs. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:11:00}

Her lasting role, in his account, was keeping the Qur'an: the sheets gathered under Abu Bakr by Zayd ibn Thabit passed to Umar and then stayed with her. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:14:00} When Uthman feared errors in people's copies, he relied on the copy kept by Hafsa, borrowed it on his oath to return it, sent copies to the provinces and gave the original back to her. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:15:00} He says she died early in Mu'awiya's reign. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep10 00:11:00}
