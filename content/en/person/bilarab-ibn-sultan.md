## Overview
Bil'arab ibn Sultan ibn Sayf ibn Malik al-Ya'rubi was the third of the Ya'rubi imams of Oman. Al-Zirikli classes him among the Ibadis, a school distinct from the Sunni majority, and gives his death as 1104 AH, 1693 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 73}.

## Reign
The oath of allegiance was given to him at Nizwa on the day his father died, in 1091 AH, and he followed the ways of his righteous forebears with firmness and justice {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 73}. A quarrel then arose between him and his brother Sayf ibn Sultan, who fought him and took all the forts of Oman except Yabrin, where he besieged his brother Bil'arab; Bil'arab died during the siege {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 73}. Al-Zirikli adds that he was a jurist and a man of letters who wrote good poetry {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 2 p. 73}.
