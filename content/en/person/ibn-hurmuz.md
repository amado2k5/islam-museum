## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hurmuz, Abu Dawud, a client of the Banu Hashim known as al-A'raj, was a hafiz and Qur'an reciter from the people of Medina {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-Zirikli notes that there is a disagreement over his father's name {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Teacher and standing
He met Abu Hurayra and took from him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-Zirikli says he was the first to stand out in the Qur'an and the sunan, that he was expert in the genealogies of the Arabs, abundant in knowledge, and reliable {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.

## Death
He served for a time on the frontier post of Alexandria and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}. Al-A'lam gives his death as 117 AH, equal to 735 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 286}.
