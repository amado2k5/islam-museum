## Overview
The judge Abu Hamid Ahmad ibn Amir ibn Bishr al-Marwarudhi, a jurist, the companion of [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60}

## In Basra
He settled in Basra and taught there, and the jurists of Basra took from him. He wrote the "al-Jami'" on the school, a commentary on al-Muzani, and works on the principles of jurisprudence. Al-Shirazi describes him as an imam whose dust cannot be reached. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60}

## His death
He died in 362 AH according to al-Shirazi. The Gregorian date (around 973 CE) is converted and derived. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60}

## On his lineage
The editor's note says that Ibn Khallikan and al-Subki differ on his lineage: "Ibn Amir ibn Bishr" or "Ibn Bishr ibn Amir". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.60}
