## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn al-Jalla, a great Sufi of Baghdadi origin. He lived in Ramla and Damascus. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The source says he died in 306 AH (918 CE). That date comes from one source and needs a second. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
