## Overview
Rustam ibn Fakhr al-Dawla, whose title the caliph al-Qadir gave as "Majd al-Dawla". He was set up in the sultanate of al-Rayy and its districts when his father died in 387 AH, when he was four years old. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.635}
