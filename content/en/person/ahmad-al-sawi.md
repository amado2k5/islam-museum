## Summary
Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Khalwati, known as al-Sawi, a Maliki jurist who studied with al-Dardir and al-Dasuqi. His nisba refers to Sa' al-Hajar in the Gharbiyya province of Egypt. He died in Medina. His works include a gloss on the Jalalayn commentary and a gloss on al-Dardir's commentary on Aqrab al-masalik. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1630}
