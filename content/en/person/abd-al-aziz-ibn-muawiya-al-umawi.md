## Overview
'Abd al-'Aziz b. Mu'awiya, Abu Khalid the Qurashi Umayyad of Basra, of the progeny of Usayd; he came to Baghdad and related there from Azhar al-Samman and Abu 'Asim al-Nabil, and Abu 'Umar and Ibn al-Simmak transmitted from him. He died in Rabi' II. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 185}
