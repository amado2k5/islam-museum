# Kosta Khetagurov

Kosta Levanovich Khetagurov (15 October 1859 – 1 April 1906) was an Ossetian poet generally regarded as the founder of Ossetian literature, and also a talented painter and public benefactor {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-en}.

## Life and work

Born in the mountain village of Nar in Ossetia, Khetagurov studied at the Stavropol Gymnasium from 1871 to 1881 and entered the Saint Petersburg Academy of Arts, but had to abandon his studies in 1885 when his stipend was withdrawn {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-en}. Back in Ossetia his poems, composed in Ossetic, spread orally through the towns and villages; he also published verse, stories and articles in Russian newspapers, editing Severny Kavkaz himself from 1893 to 1902, and his painting of Saint Nino, the fourth-century baptiser of the Georgians, was particularly welcomed in Georgia {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-en}. His collection The Ossetian Lyre, first published in 1899, has been cherished among Ossetian books ever since {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-en}.

## Exile and death

For his criticism of the imperial government Khetagurov was twice exiled, from 1891 to 1896 and again from 1899 to 1902; the second exile destroyed his health, and he died shortly after his return, at Laba in 1906 {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-en}. He was carried to Vladikavkaz and buried in the necropolis of the Ossetian Church there {cite:wikipedia-kosta-khetagurov-ru}.
