## Overview
The minister and secretary Abu Amir Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Amir al-Balawi, an Andalusian minister and secretary, a man of learning and of poetry. He died in 559 AH, according to the "Encyclopaedia of the Biographies of Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
