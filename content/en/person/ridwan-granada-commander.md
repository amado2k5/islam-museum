## Overview
Ridwan was the client of Abu al-Hajjaj, sultan of al-Andalus, and commander of his troops from among the Christian counts in Granada, according to Ibn Khaldun {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## In Granada
Ibn Khaldun reports that after his master died he kept himself hidden within the state and became domineering over Sultan Muhammad ibn Yusuf. He became related by marriage to a Jewish dhimmi, and the sultan disapproved, and relations between him and the state soured {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## His death
Then the affairs of Granada were overturned: Ibn al-Ahmar was deposed, Ridwan was killed, and his body was crucified on the wall of the city at the Burnt Gate {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
