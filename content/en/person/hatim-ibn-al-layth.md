## Overview
Hatim ibn al-Layth, the prolific and trustworthy hafiz Abu'l-Fadl al-Baghdadi al-Jawhari; he heard 'Ubaydallah ibn Musa, Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Marwazi and their peers. He died in 262 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1347}
