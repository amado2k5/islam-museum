## Overview
King of India, lord of Ajmer. Modern sources know him as Prithviraj Chauhan. According to Ibn al-Athir, he defeated [[person/shihab-al-din-ghuri|Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri]] in 583 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.221-222}

## The capture
Ibn al-Athir reports that Shihab al-Din returned in 588 AH, and the king pursued him for four stages, until the Muslims surrounded him at night without his knowing it. When dawn came they attacked him from every side, and he stood on his elephant until he was captured. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.221-222}

It is related that Shihab al-Din asked him what he would have done had he captured him, and he said that he had prepared a golden shackle. Ajmer was then taken and granted as an iqta to Qutb al-Din Aybak, and the king of India was killed in 588 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.222}
