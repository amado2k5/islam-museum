## Overview
Hanash ibn Abd Allah al-San'ani, a Successor who was with Ali in Kufa, then came to Egypt after Ali's killing and was counted among the Egyptians, as al-Maqqari mentions in "Nafh al-Tib". {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314}.

## His stances
He was among those who rose with Ibn al-Zubayr against Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and Abd al-Malik pardoned him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314}.

## Al-Andalus
It is said that he was the third of the Successors who entered al-Andalus. The people of Saragossa claim that he died among them and did not return to the East, and his grave there is well known and sought for blessing. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.314}.

These reports are given in the form "it is said" and "they claim", and need an additional source.
