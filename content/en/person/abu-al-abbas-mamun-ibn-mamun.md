## Summary
Abu al-Abbas Ma'mun ibn Ma'mun succeeded Ali ibn Ma'mun; the author, following Bosworth, dates his reign 399–407 AH (1008–1017). {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 79} Among the main events of his reign was the Abbasid caliph's sending him an envoy and a title, which appears on a dirham he struck in 405/1014. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 79} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 80}

## The dirham of 405 AH
Its obverse carries the profession of God's oneness followed by "al-Qadir bi-llah, and his heir al-Ghalib bi-llah", with the remains of a margin reading "…the dirham in the city of Khwarazm in the year five…". {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 80} The reverse reads "Muhammad is the Messenger of God; Ayn al-Dawla wa Zayn al-Milla Abu al-Abbas Khwarazm[shah]", surrounded by wording from [[passage/quran-9-33|al-Tawba 9:33]], [[passage/quran-61-9|al-Saff 61:9]] and [[passage/quran-48-28|al-Fath]]. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 80} It weighs 7.10 g and measures 33–35 mm. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 80} The author notes that this ruler's coins use a single kind of title. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 223}

## Afterwards
In 1017 Mahmud of Ghazna conquered Khwarazm and placed his governor Altuntash over it. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 11}
