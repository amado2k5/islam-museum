## In brief
Hushaym b. Bashir b. Abi Hazim b. Dinar, the great memoriser and hadith-master of the age, Abu Mu'awiya al-Wasiti resident in Baghdad; he heard al-Zuhri, Amr b. Dinar, Mansur b. Zadhan, Husayn b. Abd al-Rahman, Abu Ishaq, Ayyub al-Sikhtiyani, and a great many, devoting himself to this discipline beyond his peers. Shu'ba, Yahya al-Qattan, Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi, Ahmad b. Hanbal, Qutayba, Ibn Abi Uways, Ya'qub al-Dawraqi, al-Hasan b. Arafa, and a great number narrated from him; he was born in 104 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.233}

## His memory
Amr b. Ali places Hushaym among those around al-Zuhri, Abu al-Zubayr, and Amr at Mecca in the pilgrimage season, Ya'qub al-Dawraqi said "he possessed eleven thousand hadiths", Wahb b. Jarir relates that they asked Shu'ba "shall we write from Hushaym?" and he said "yes — and if he narrates to you from Ibn Umar, believe him", Ahmad b. Hanbal said "I kept to Hushaym four years and asked him nothing twice, out of awe, and he constantly glorified God between hadiths, saying 'there is no god but God' in a quiet voice", and Ibn al-Mahdi said "Hushaym was a better memoriser of hadith than al-Thawri". {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.233-234}
