## Summary
Shaykh Ibrahim al-Zayyat the Egyptian majdhub, Abu'l-Sana' al-Zayyat; a majdhub shaykh born in Cairo about 780 and raised there; he stayed a while at Bulaq, joking with the preachers of its women and its cemetery-visitors, sometimes abusing them with foul speech — it recurred and none objected; rather the blessing-seekers wiped themselves on him and used him for their needs; unseen matters sometimes came to him and a group of his companions glimpsed such things from him, and it befell him often, yet he never ceased his ramblings; a majdhub who spoke in trance then forgot what he uttered — the people of reason did not countenance him and some exceeded all bounds concerning him; he died in 862. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 299-300}
