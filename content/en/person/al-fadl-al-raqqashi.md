## Summary
Al-Fadl ibn Abd al-Samad ibn al-Fadl, Abu al-Abbas al-Raqqashi, was a poet from Basra. He came to Baghdad, praised Harun al-Rashid, and glorified al-Amin and the Barmakids. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.345}

He and Abu Nuwas traded satirical verse, and neither gave up on the other until death separated them. It is one of the well-known satirical exchanges of Abbasid poetry. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.345}

Al-Mubarrad said: al-Fadl al-Raqqashi was a poet who would show wealth while he was poor, show honour while he was humble, and pretend to abundance while he had little, so the poets used to satirise him. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.345}
