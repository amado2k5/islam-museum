## Overview
Al-Hudayn ibn al-Mundhir, mentioned in a report on the conquest of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
The people of Khurasan asked him to appoint them a leader to fight Qutayba, but he refused. He advised them to take Waki' ibn Hassan al-Tamimi, and said that no one but him could bear this affair. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
