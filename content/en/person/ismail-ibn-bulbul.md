## Overview
Isma'il ibn Bulbul, al-Muwaffaq's vizier, who claimed descent from the Banu Shayban and exacted an obscure year's land-tax, weighing heavily on the subjects; when al-Muwaffaq lay dying, the mob looted his house and opened the prisons, and after his master's death al-Mu'tadid chained and tortured him, fastening an iron collar of 120 ratls round his neck with a corpse's head hanging from it until he died in Jumada I 278 AH — buried with his shackle and chains.
