## Overview
Al-Buhturi, Abu Ubada al-Walid ibn Ubayd, a poet of the early Abbasid age. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.545}

## With al-Mutawakkil
Al-Suyuti says he praised the caliph al-Mutawakkil in an ode on lifting the trial (mihna), and in it he satirised Ibn Abi Du'ad. He says he composed an ode in loyalty about al-Mutawakkil and al-Fath ibn Khaqan, and then both were killed together. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.545} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.549}

## His poetry
Al-Mas'udi relates from al-Mubarrad that his poetry was better balanced than that of Abu Tammam, because he could recite an entire ode free from any critic's attack. Al-Mas'udi also relates that al-Mutawakkil recited his ode "From which border do you smile?", and al-Mas'udi says that Abu al-Anbas al-Saymari replied to it with a satire. The caliph laughed and gave each of them ten thousand dirhams. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.458-459} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.478-479}
