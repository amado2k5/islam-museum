## Summary
Muhammad Awwama is a Syrian hadith scholar and a leading Deobandi-affiliated figure outside the subcontinent, joining manuscript editing to scholarly grounding of the hadith-fiqh relation. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-awwama}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Aleppo in 1940, he studied under its shaykhs and taught in religious institutes before settling in Madinah, devoting his life to research, editing and hadith teaching. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-awwama}

## Career and works
His books include Athar al-Hadith fi Ikhtilaf al-A’imma on the schools’ stance towards the Sunna, Adab al-Ikhtilaf on scholarly disagreement, and editions of manuscripts such as the Musannaf of Ibn Abi Shayba and Taqrib al-Tahdhib. He teaches the Sahihs and Sunans in circles attended by students from around the world. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-awwama}

## Method and contribution
His method is apologetic-documentation: answering claims that hadith caused division by showing the jurists made the Sunna the basis of their schools. He is widely respected in Hanafi and Shafi’i hadith circles alike, with scholarly debates over details of his positions; the museum attributes these descriptions to their holders. {cite:wikipedia-muhammad-awwama}
