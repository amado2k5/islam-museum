## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Muhammad ibn Mukarram al-Ansari al-Ifriqi, later of Egypt, Jamal al-Din Ibn Manzur: born in al-Muharram 630. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/262} He compiled Lisan al-Arab from al-Tahdhib, al-Muhkam, al-Sihah and al-Jamhara in the order of al-Sihah, abridged many works of literature, served in the chancery all his life and was appointed judge of Tripoli. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/263}
