## Overview
Muhammad ibn Zayd al-Alawi set out toward Khurasan in 287 AH, and news of his killing came in Shawwal of the same year. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.81}

## Al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that money from him came to be distributed secretly among the Talibids, and al-Mu'tadid made them known and drew them near. When Muhammad ibn Harun killed him, al-Mu'tadid showed indignation and grief at his killing. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.630-631}
