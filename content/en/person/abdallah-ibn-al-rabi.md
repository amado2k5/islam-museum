## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Rabi' ibn Ziyad al-Harithi, whom his father appointed as his deputy over Khurasan in 53 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He fought the people of Amul, which is Amawi, and the people of Zam, then made peace with them and returned to Merv, where he stayed two months and died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
