## Summary
The ustadh Abu'l-Futuh Barjawan, after whom Cairo's Barjawan quarter is named; among the servants and state-managers of al-'Aziz of Egypt, effective and obeyed. He took charge of the kingdom's affairs in Ramadan 387 and was settled under al-Hakim over Egypt, the Hijaz, Syria, the Maghreb and the East's provinces — he was black. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His estate
When he was killed he left a thousand silk-lined dubayqi trousers and uncountable garments, furnishings, instruments and books. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
