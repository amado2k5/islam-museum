## Overview
Yamama bint Murr, a woman of Jadis in al-Yamama. Abd Kalal ibn Mathub al-Tubba' crucified her at the gate of Jaww, and Jaww of al-Yamama was named after her {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 8}.
