Mahira Hafeez Khan (born 21 December 1984) is a Pakistani actress from Karachi who began her career as a video jockey before making her acting debut in the film Bol (2011).{cite:wikipedia-mahira-khan-en} She rose to fame as the lead of the television drama Humsafar (2011), which won her the Lux Style Award for Best Television Actress, and became one of Pakistan's most prominent and highest-paid actresses through series such as Shehr-e-Zaat and Sadqay Tumhare and films including Bin Roye and Superstar.{cite:wikipedia-mahira-khan-en} In 2017 she starred opposite Shah Rukh Khan in the Indian film Raees.{cite:wikipedia-mahira-khan-en}{cite:wikidata-mahira-khan}
