## Overview

Abu al-Qasim Abbas ibn Firnas was an Andalusi scholar and poet, raised and educated in [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], associated with astronomy, crafts and literature; he died274 AH/887 {cite:tdv-abbas-firnas-kirbiyik biography}. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan explicitly calls him a Muslim astronomer: a sourced identification, not an inference from his name {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 printed p.378, scan382}.

## In Cordoba

TDV places his birth in a village of Takoronna near Ronda, reports Berber origin and court connections under al-Hakam I, Abd al-Rahman II and Muhammad I, but gives no exact birth year {cite:tdv-abbas-firnas-kirbiyik biography}. It warns against confusing him with poet Abbas ibn Nasih; similar names do not justify merging their stories {cite:tdv-abbas-firnas-kirbiyik biography}.

## Crafts and literature

Maqqari credits him with glass from stones, understanding al-Khalil's prosody and music, and a timekeeping apparatus: attributed accounts, not global priority for every craft {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 p.374}. The modern biography discusses crystal working/cutting and a representation of the heavens, reporting that examples of poetry preserved by Ibn Hayyan remain {cite:tdv-abbas-firnas-kirbiyik biography}. Maqqari's glass and modern crystal terminology do not themselves constitute a technical recipe proving identical materials {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 p.374} {cite:tdv-abbas-firnas-kirbiyik biography}.

## Reported flight

The [[invention/firnas-reported-winged-flight|reported-flight record]] examines Maqqari's feathers, wings and landing: injury in that account, unlike Hasan Ibrahim Hasan's unharmed survivor {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 p.374} {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 printed p.378, scan382}. The difference remains, without a verified-first-airplane claim or invented measured duration/distance {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 p.374}. John Lienhard's875 is uncertain secondary chronology, not a year in this Arabic passage; his later note warns about the Armen Firman story's confusion and lack of primary material {cite:lienhard-firnas-1910 narrative and postscript}.

## Memory and the evidence

Maqqari places crafts alongside satirical poetry attributed to Mu'min ibn Sa'id; poetry within the account is not an independent measurement of wing performance {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 pp.374–375}. The modern Cordoba bridge bearing his name witnesses continuing memory, not surviving equipment or an identified launch point {cite:commons-firnas-bridge-saez Description}. Hasan's inspected page is printed378 in volume four, PDF scan382; that distinction corrects the earlier citation without erasing Devin's earlier authorship {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 printed p.378, scan382}.
