## Overview
Abbas ibn Firnas was a Muslim astronomer in al-Andalus. In his workshop he discovered a method for making crystal. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan describes his experiment with flight on a gliding machine: he rose high into the air, then fell and got up unharmed, and Hasan regards him as a forerunner of the idea of flight from the Middle Ages onward. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

His name is linked with [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]]. The dates of his life need an additional source.
