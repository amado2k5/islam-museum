## Overview
Abbas ibn Firnas was a Muslim astronomer in al-Andalus. In his workshop he discovered a method for making crystal. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan recounts that he launched himself in what he calls a remarkable, astonishing experiment aboard a gliding craft, sailing high through the air, then falling and rising unharmed — and on that basis counts him a forerunner of the idea of flight from the Middle Ages onward. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim v.4 p.382}

His name is linked with [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] in al-Andalus.
