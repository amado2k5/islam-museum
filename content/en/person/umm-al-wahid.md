## Summary
Umm al-Wahid, daughter of Qadi Abu 'Abd Allah al-Husayn ibn Isma'il al-Hamili, memorized the Qur'an, fiqh, grammar, the inheritance-shares and the sciences, and excelled in al-Shafi'i's madhhab; she used to issue fatwas alongside Abu 'Ali ibn Abi Hurayra. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 88}
