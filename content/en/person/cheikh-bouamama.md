# Cheikh Bouamama

## Summary

Cheikh Bouamama (born 1833 by the French article's sourced account, 1841 in the English article; died 17 October 1908) was an Algerian resistance leader of the Awlad Sidi Shaykh who fought the French conquest in the southwest of Algeria from 1881 to 1908.{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-en}

## Early life

He was born at El-Hammam El-Fougani in the ksar of Figuig, in present-day Morocco, into the Awlad Sidi Shaykh, and received a Sufi education; in about 1875 he settled at Moghrar Tahtani, in the Algerian South Oranais, and founded a zawiya for teaching the Qur'an.{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-fr}

## The revolt

His rising opened with the battle of Sfissifa, south of Aïn Sefra, on 27 April 1881, in which the French column was defeated.{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-fr} For more than two decades he sustained a mobile guerrilla war across the South Oranais and the northern Sahara, withdrawing at times into Morocco and the Touat, and was never captured.{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-en}

## Death

He died on 17 October 1908 (the French article's text gives 7 October) at El Aioun Sidi Mellouk, near Oujda.{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-ar} His life was the subject of the Algerian film The Epic of Cheikh Bouamama (1983).{cite:wikipedia-cheikh-bouamama-en}
