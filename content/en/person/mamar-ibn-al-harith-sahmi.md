## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia — Ibn Ishaq names him without a fate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Sahm in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
