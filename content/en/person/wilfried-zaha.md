# Wilfried Zaha

## Summary

Wilfried Zaha — full name Dazet Wilfried Armel Zaha — is an Ivorian footballer, born in Abidjan in 1992 and raised in England. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en} A Crystal Palace academy winger, he was Alex Ferguson's last signing for Manchester United in 2013, then returned to Palace and became the club's record Premier League goalscorer before moving to Galatasaray and Charlotte FC. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en} No source consulted makes any statement of his religion, so his data record records his faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en}{cite:wikidata-wilfried-zaha}

## Club career

Zaha made his senior debut for Crystal Palace in 2010 and was instrumental in their promotion to the Premier League in 2013, scoring 18 goals in 143 appearances in his first spell. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en} He joined Manchester United for £10 million, won the Community Shield on his debut, but made only four appearances and spent a loan spell at Cardiff City before returning to Palace in 2014. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en} Over the next nine seasons he scored 72 goals in 315 appearances, becoming the club's tenth-highest scorer of all time and its highest ever in the Premier League, before leaving in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en}

## International career

Born in Ivory Coast and raised in England, Zaha made his England debut in 2012 and appeared in two non-competitive matches. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en} He later switched his allegiance to Ivory Coast, the country of his birth, and has represented them since 2017. {cite:wikipedia-wilfried-zaha-en}
