## Summary
The Portuguese prince who claimed the throne against the Spanish annexation {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 270}.

## The annexation
He could not resist the Spanish forces that swept through Portugal; Spain annexed his lands in 988/1580 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 270}.
