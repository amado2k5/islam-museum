## Summary
Father of Mudar, Rabi'a, Iyad and Anmar; most Adnani Arab tribes trace back to him, so the Adnani Arabs are called Banu Nizar {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## His place in the lineage
His four sons carry most of the Arab genealogies: Mudar — Quraysh, Kinana, Tamim, Dabba, Hudhayl and Asad; Rabi'a — Abd al-Qays, Bakr ibn Wa'il, Taghlib and Shayban; Iyad; and Anmar — Khath'am and Bajila {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16-17} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## For younger visitors
Nizar ibn Ma'add is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
