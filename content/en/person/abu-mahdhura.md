## Summary
A Companion from Mecca who was a muezzin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5143}

## Narration and the call to prayer
He narrated from the Prophet, and his son Abd al-Malik, his grandson Abd al-Aziz, and Abd Allah ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Mulayka, among others, narrated from him. The Prophet gave him the call to prayer in Mecca on the day of the conquest. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5143}
