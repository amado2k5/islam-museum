## Overview
The judge Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Surayj, a Shafi'i jurist. He died in Baghdad in 306 AH according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.57}

The editor's note, drawing on the "Fihrist", says he died in 305 AH, so the date of his death differs between the two sources. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.57}

Al-Shirazi says he was one of the great Shafi'is, and he was called the "grey falcon". He held the judgeship in Shiraz. He was preferred over all the companions of al-Shafi'i, even over al-Muzani. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.57}

Al-Shirazi quotes his teacher Abu al-Hasan al-Shirajī that the index of his books contained four hundred works. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.57}

He took knowledge from Abu al-Qasim al-Anmati ([[person/uthman-ibn-said-al-anmati|Uthman ibn Said al-Anmati]]). {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.58}
