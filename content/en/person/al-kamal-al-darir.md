## Overview
Al-Kamal al-Darir, shaykh of the readers, companion of al-Shatibi and husband of his daughter; he read the readings on al-Shatibi, Shuja' al-Mudlaji and Abu'l-Jud, heard from al-Busiri and a company, sat teaching for an age and the headship of iqra' ended with him — an imam ranging over branches of knowledge, affectionate, humble, gentle, of perfect muruwwa. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 532}
