## Overview
Chaghatay ibn Genghis Khan, whom Ibn Khaldun calls "Kadai" among them, son of [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]] and brother of [[person/jochi-ibn-genghis|Jochi]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1536}

## His share of the kingdoms
His father assigned him the lands between the Uyghur country, Samarqand, Bukhara and Transoxiana. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1536}

## After his death
Ibn Khaldun relates that he died without his affair being settled, so Mengu-Timur appointed his sons over Transoxiana, in fulfilment of Genghis Khan's bequest to their father. The kingdom remained in his descendants in Samarqand and its districts. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1536}
