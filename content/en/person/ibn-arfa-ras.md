## Summary
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali ibn Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Khalaf al-Ansari, resident of Fez and its khatib, author of Shudhur al-Dhahab on the art of alchemy — none ever versified alchemy as he did, to the point it was said that if he could not teach you the art of gold he taught you the art of adab, and if his gold escaped you his adab would not; he is known as Ibn Arfa' Ras, and it is said he is the poet al-Hasan, sage of poetry; he died in 594. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 797}
