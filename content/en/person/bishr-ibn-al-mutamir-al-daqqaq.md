## Overview
Abu Ahmad al-Daqqaq of Wasit, resident of Samarra; he transmitted there from Sufyan b. 'Uyayna, Yazid b. Harun and Ishaq al-Azraq — Ibn Sa'id transmitting from him; Abu Hatim al-Razi said "he is truthful"; he died in 259, or 262 by another account {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 106}.
