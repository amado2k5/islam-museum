## Summary
Khurrazad, brother of Rustam, was the Persian commander on the day of [[place/jalula|Jalula]]. He fortified and entrenched his army, placed the dependents and baggage at Khaniqin, and bound his men not to flee — yet the Muslims routed them after heavy fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 369}

## Details
Al-Baladhuri relates that the Persians at Jalula were commanded by Khurrazad, Rustam's brother; they had dug in, made their dependants and baggage safe at Khaniqin, and sworn not to flee, while reinforcements kept reaching them from Hulwan and the mountains {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 369}. The Muslims resolved to strike before these reinforcements grew: Hujr ibn 'Adi al-Kindi held the right wing, 'Amr ibn Ma'di Karib the cavalry, and Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid the infantry {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 369}. They fought a battle the like of which had not been fought — arrows until the bows broke, spear-thrusts until the lances bent, and sword-blows until the blades gave way — then the Muslims charged in a single rush that tore the Persians from their position and routed them; the Muslims rode on their shoulders slaughtering them until darkness parted the two sides {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 369}. Hashim ibn 'Utba then stationed Jarir ibn 'Abdallah at Jalula with a strong body of horse to screen the Muslims from the enemy {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 369}.
