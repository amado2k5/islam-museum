## Overview
Khurrazad, brother of Rustam, commander of the Persians on the day of Jalula. He entrenched himself and dug a trench, placed the families and baggage at Khanaqin, and they pledged not to flee. The Muslims defeated him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
