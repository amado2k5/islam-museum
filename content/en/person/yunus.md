## Overview
Yunus ibn Matta, one of the figures mentioned in the Qur'an {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3412} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3413}.

In al-Bukhari the Prophet said that it is not right for a servant to say that he is better than Yunus ibn Matta, and he attributed him to his father.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)

The Sheikh says that by human laws no one survives a whale while inside it, and he points to Yunus's supplication and glorification of God, which he sees as the reason for his rescue {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:00}.

He mentions that Yunus was cast onto the open shore, that God made a gourd plant grow over him, and that he was sent to a hundred thousand or more (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:30}. He then observes that the whale's belly is one of three narrow places, which he mentions together with the cave and the cavern, and asks where relief comes from after the narrowness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that Yunus, being sincere toward God, when he was in the belly of the whale acknowledged his shortcoming with glorification and confession of wrongdoing; he quotes the sira books that the angels said 'a familiar voice in a strange place', since no one before or after him remembered God in a whale's belly, and God answered him and saved him; he says God saves the believers when they humble themselves before Him and persist in asking. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:10:00}
