## Overview
Yunus ibn Matta, one of the figures mentioned in the Qur'an {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3412} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3413}.

In al-Bukhari the Prophet said that it is not right for a servant to say that he is better than Yunus ibn Matta, and he attributed him to his father.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)

The Sheikh says that by human laws no one survives a whale while inside it, and he points to Yunus's supplication and glorification of God, which he sees as the reason for his rescue {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:00}.

He mentions that Yunus was cast onto the open shore, that God made a gourd plant grow over him, and that he was sent to a hundred thousand or more (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:30}. He then observes that the whale's belly is one of three narrow places, which he mentions together with the cave and the cavern, and asks where relief comes from after the narrowness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:32:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy tells the story of Yunus: he boarded a ship, its people cast lots when it was in danger, the lot fell on him time after time, he was thrown into the sea and swallowed by the whale, and the whale kept him safe by God's decree. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:24:30} He notes that Yunus had left his people in anger, then called out in the darkness confessing that he had wronged himself, and that the angels recognised his voice as that of a known servant. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:28:00} He was then cast onto the bare shore and God made a gourd plant grow over him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:24:30} He digresses to say that Anas came to love gourd when he saw the Prophet picking it out from the sides of the dish. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:25:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that Yunus, being sincere toward God, when he was in the belly of the whale acknowledged his shortcoming with glorification and confession of wrongdoing; he quotes the sira books that the angels said 'a familiar voice in a strange place', since no one before or after him remembered God in a whale's belly, and God answered him and saved him; he says God saves the believers when they humble themselves before Him and persist in asking. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep22 00:10:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
In 'Bustan al-Amal' the Sheikh tells the story of Yunus in detail: he called his people and they did not respond, so he left in anger, wanting to take his call to another people without awaiting revelation; he boarded a ship, which nearly sank; they cast lots to throw one overboard and the lot fell on him three times, so they threw him into the sea and the whale swallowed him, and he was in darknesses one above another: the waves, the sea, the whale's belly and the night. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:04:00} He draws the lesson for preachers not to hasten the fruit. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:03:30} He holds that Yunus accused himself of wrongdoing and shortcoming and God answered him, says that every believer who follows his way will be saved from the 'whale's belly' of his crises, illnesses and debts, and likens the whale to the desires that surround a person. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:09:30}
