## Overview
Abu'l-Makaram al-As'ad, Zakariya b. Abi Qudama b. Abi Malih, Mammati of Egypt, katib and poet; he was inspector of the diwans in Egypt, with virtues and many works — he versified the sira of Sultan Salah al-Din and versified Kalila wa-Dimna, and has a diwan Ibn Khallikan saw in his son's hand and transmitted from. Al-'Imad al-Isfahani mentions him in the Kharida with excerpts; the family were Christians who embraced Islam at the start of the Salahi kingship. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
