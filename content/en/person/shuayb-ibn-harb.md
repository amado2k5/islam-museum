## Overview
Shu'ayb ibn Harb, the exemplary ascetic imam and shaykh al-Islam Abu Salih al-Mada'ini, a permanent devotee in Mecca of Khurasani stock; he narrated from Isma'il ibn Muslim al-'Abdi, 'Ikrima ibn 'Ammar, Mis'ar ibn Kidam, Shu'ba, Aban ibn 'Abdallah al-Bajali, Sakhr ibn Juwayriyya, Hariz ibn 'Uthman, al-Hasan ibn 'Umara, Sufyan and others. He died in 196 or 197 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1988}
