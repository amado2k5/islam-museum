## Summary
Majd al-Din Ibn Jama'a al-Kinani al-Maqdisi the Shafi'i, muezzin and khatib of the Tamaj madrasa; adib, teacher and distinguished poet, born in Jerusalem 779; he memorised the Qur'an, the Minhaj and the Alfiyya, read fiqh under his son Shaykh Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad al-Maqdisi, grammar under his son Shaykh Yahya, took the madhhab from his son Shaykh Yahya and tafsir from Shaykh al-'Afif Sulayman ibn Rumaytha, taught alongside them and deputised in the khitaba — gaining thereby a good standing. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## His fatwas
Shaykh al-Islam Zakariyya read on him; he worked and spent the rest of his life in ifta' and teaching, and was moved from the judgeship of Jerusalem to teaching. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
