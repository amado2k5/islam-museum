## Overview
Khindif — Layla bint Hilwan ibn Imran ibn al-Haf ibn Quda'a — mother of the sons of Ilyas ibn Mudar, in whose nickname her sons were named: Amir went out, hunted her and cooked her, so he was called the Cook; Umayr dived into the dust so he was called Qum'a; and their mother came running — Ilyas asked 'whither do you hasten, Khindif?' and she said 'I am still hurrying after you' — so she was called Khindif.
