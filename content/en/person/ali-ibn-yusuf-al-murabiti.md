## Overview
Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin, amir of the Almoravids after his father Yusuf ibn Tashfin. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1669}

## His meeting with Ibn Tumart
Ibn Tumart met him in the mosque of Marrakesh on a Friday, admonished him and spoke harshly to him. Malik ibn Wuhayb al-Jazza', an astrologer, had announced to him the coming of the "lord of the conjunction", and Ibn Tumart sought him but missed him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1669}
