## Overview
Jamal al-Din Ibn Jarir, the wazir of al-Raqqa; he served al-Malik al-Ashraf then al-Malik al-Salih Isma'il at Damascus, and died there in Jumada II 636 — so Ibn al-Athir in al-'Ibar {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 189}.
