## Overview
Musa ibn Bugha, one of the commanders of the early Abbasid period. Al-Baladhuri says that he was al-Mu'tazz's governor over the Jabal. He sent him to Qumm to fight the Talibids who had appeared in Tabaristan, and he took it by storm and killed a great many of its people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.440-443}

Al-Mas'udi relates that al-Muhtadi sent him out with Bayakbal against Musawwar al-Shari, and they exchanged letters about one of them killing the other, until they learned of it and returned. He says that he did not take part in the Turks' act of killing al-Muhtadi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 559-560}

Al-Mas'udi adds that he died in 264 AH, and that a poet who praised him and was not given anything composed verses rejoicing at his death. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 576-577}

Note: the periods recorded for him in the data include "the Rashidun", and need review.
