## Overview
Abu Musa al-Tayalisi, nicknamed Raghath; born in 193, he heard from 'Affan and Abu Nu'aym and was reckoned among the huffaz. Ahmad b. Kamil the qadi and Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i transmitted from him, and al-Daraqutni said he was thiqa; he died in Shawwal {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 171}.
