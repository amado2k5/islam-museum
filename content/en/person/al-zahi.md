## Summary
Abu'l-Qasim 'Ali b. Ishaq b. Khalaf al-Baghdadi, known as al-Zahi, the famous poet; a graceful describer rich in elegant conceits. Al-Khatib mentions him in *Tarikh Baghdad* as a fine poet in love-verse and beyond; he was a perfumer whose shop stood in Qati'at al-Rabi', and he praised Sayf al-Dawla, the vizier al-Muhallabi and other grandees of his time; Ibn 'Abd al-Barr records him in the *Tabaqat al-shu'ara'*. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
