## Overview
Sultan Murad I ibn Orhan ibn Osman, who ruled from 761 to 791 AH (1360–1389 CE). He was the son of [[person/urkhan-ibn-uthman|Orhan ibn Osman]]. Ali Muhammad al-Salabi describes him as brave, a holy warrior, generous, just to his subjects and soldiers, devoted to raids and the building of mosques and madrasas, and says that he formed a consultative council of the best commanders and experts. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 53}

## Conquests
He took Adrianople in 762 AH (1360 CE), and made it the capital of the Ottoman state from 768 AH (1366 CE), so the capital moved to Europe. He expanded in Asia Minor and Europe at the same time. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 53}

## His death
The data say that he was martyred in the battle of Kosovo, and that his death year was 791 AH (1389 CE). {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 53}
