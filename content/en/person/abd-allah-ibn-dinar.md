## In brief
Abdallah b. Dinar, the imam and jurist Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Umari of Medina, client of Abdallah b. Umar; he narrated from his patron Abdallah b. Umar, Anas b. Malik, Sulayman b. Yasar, and Abu Salih al-Samman, and from him narrated Musa b. Uqba, Shu'ba, Malik, the two Sufyans, Warqa', Isma'il b. Humayd, and others besides — his hadith stands in the two Sahih collections. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.122-123}

## His death
He died in 127 AH at eighty-six years of age. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.123}
