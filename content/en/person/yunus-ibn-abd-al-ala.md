## Overview
Yunus ibn Abd al-A'la al-Sadafi, one of the companions of al-Shafi'i and transmitters of his school in Egypt. He died there in the year 264 AH at the age of ninety-two {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.577}.

al-Shirazi reports that he died in the year in which al-Muzani died {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.

## Al-Tabari's teacher in Egypt
Ahmad Khalid Babakr identifies him as Yunus ibn Abd al-A'la ibn Musa ibn Maysara al-Sadafi of Egypt, jurist and reciter, companion of al-Shafi'i and student of Ibn Wahb and Ibn Uyayna (d. 264 AH), and says that [[person/al-tabari|al-Tabari]] took from him in Egypt Warsh's transmission of Nafi's reading and Maliki law. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.31} It was through him that al-Tabari transmitted Nafi's reading in Warsh's version, the transmission [[person/ibn-mujahid|Ibn Mujahid]] was keen to hear from him alone. {cite:qiraat-inda-al-tabari-thesis-umm-al-qura p.33}
