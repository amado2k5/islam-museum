## Summary
Father of Lu'ayy ibn Ghalib; a link in the agreed lineage between Fihr and Lu'ayy, from whose stock the clans of Quraysh of the hollows and the outskirts branch {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.48} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/1-4}.

## His place in the lineage
He was among the nobles of Quraysh in his day; through his son Lu'ayy the great Quraysh clans branch out {cite:tarikh-al-tabari}.

## For younger visitors
Ghalib ibn Fihr is one of the Prophet's ancestors in the part of the lineage the genealogists agree on, from Adnan down to Abdullah ibn Abd al-Muttalib.
