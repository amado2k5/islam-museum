## Umm al-Hasan Fatima al-Husayniyya al-Halabiyya

Umm al-Hasan Fatima al-Husayniyya al-Halabiyya, sister of the chief of the sharifs. She was born in 732 or 733 AH. She heard from her maternal grandfather Jamal al-Din Ibrahim ibn al-Shihab Mahmud, and al-Mizzi and others gave her a licence. She transmitted hadith in Aleppo. She died in the first ten days of Muharram, past the age of eighty {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 112}.
