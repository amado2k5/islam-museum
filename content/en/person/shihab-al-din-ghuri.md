## Overview
Shihab al-Din Abu al-Muzaffar Muhammad ibn Sam al-Ghuri, king of Ghazna and part of Khurasan {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.303-305}.

## Ibn al-Athir's description
Ibn al-Athir describes him as brave and bold, one who often raided the lands of India, just to his subjects, and a ruler among them according to what the law requires. The judge at Ghazna would attend his house four days in the week, and would give judgement, and the agents of the sultan carried out his rulings on young and old, on noble and base alike {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.303-305}.

## His death
He was killed by the Kukiyya, and it is said by the Isma'ilis, at Damik on the first night of Sha'ban in the year 602 AH, while he was returning from Lahawr. He was buried in the madrasa that he had built at Ghazna {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.303-305}.
