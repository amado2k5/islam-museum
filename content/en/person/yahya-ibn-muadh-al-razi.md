## Overview
Yahya ibn Mu'adh al-Razi, one of the ascetics who prayed at night, who was a worshipper and had followers. He died in the year 206 AH, and he has a book, "al-Muridin", according to Ibn al-Nadim {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.260}.
