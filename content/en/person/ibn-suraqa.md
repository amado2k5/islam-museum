## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Suraqa, a hadith scholar, jurist and expert in inheritance of Cairo (d. 662 AH), head of the Kamiliyya school. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3479}

## Works
His works include al-Hiyal al-shar'iyya, on I'jaz al-Qur'an, and a commentary on al-Kafi on inheritance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3479}
