## Overview
Abu Dabbus, lord of the Maghrib, last king of the Banu 'Abd al-Mu'min; he gathered the armies, pounced on Marrakesh and killed its lord his cousin Abu Hafs — a champion, brave, daring. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 569}
