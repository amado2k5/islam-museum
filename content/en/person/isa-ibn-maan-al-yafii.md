## Overview
The book calls him one of the best of Yafi' and says Ibn Khalaf jailed him; when he left Bayt Bus he kept working at the prison until he got out of it, which weighed hard on Al Tarif, so they moved Abu al-Qasim and his companion to another house {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.252} ([[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-is-chained-at-sanaa-and-moved-to-bayt-bus-290]]). The later report says that al-Yafi'i reached Haraz, they feared for Bayt Bus and tightened the confinement of Jaftam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.253} ([[event/jaftam-is-seized-at-arthal-and-jailed-at-bayt-bus-290]]). The father's name is read "Ma'an" from the page image, where the printed OCR gives "Hu'an"; the book says no more.
