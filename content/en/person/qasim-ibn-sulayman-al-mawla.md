## Overview
Qasim ibn Sulayman, client of Ziyad. According to al-Baladhuri, al-Qasiya was among the lands whose water had dried up, so Qasim fabricated a letter that he claimed was from Yazid ibn Mu'awiya, granting him the land as an iqta. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
