## Overview
Miqdad ibn Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Siyuri al-Hilli al-Asadi was an Imami jurist, that is a jurist of the Twelver Imamis. He was a pupil of al-Shahid al-Awwal, Muhammad ibn Makki. He died in Najaf in 826 AH, 1423 CE; al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 278}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Kanz al-'Irfan fi Fiqh al-Qur'an", printed; "Irshad al-Talibin", printed, a commentary on "Nahj al-Mustarshidin fi Usul al-Din" by al-Hasan ibn Yusuf al-Hilli; "al-As'ila al-Miqdadiyya", in manuscript; "al-Anwar al-Jalaliyya fi Sharh al-Fusul al-Nasiriyya", in manuscript; "Jami' al-Fawa'id", in manuscript, an abridgement of the "Qawa'id" of al-Shahid; "al-Lawami' al-Ilahiyya", in manuscript, on theology; and "al-Tanqih", in manuscript, a commentary on "Mukhtasar al-Shara'i'", of which two parts survive {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 278}.
