## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Ansari, Kamal al-Din, known as Ibn al-Zamlakani, was a jurist to whom the leadership of the Shafi'is passed in his time. He was born in 667 AH, 1269 CE, and died in 727 AH, 1327 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His life and offices
He was born and educated in Damascus and took the chair of teaching and the giving of legal opinions. He held the supervision of the diwan of al-Afram, the supervision of the treasury and the agency of the bayt al-mal, and wrote in the chancery, then became judge at Aleppo, where he stayed two years {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His death
He was called to the judgeship of Egypt and set out for it, but died at Bilbays and was buried in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.

## His books
Al-Zirikli names a treatise replying to Ibn Taymiyya on two questions, divorce and visitation; glosses on al-Nawawi's "al-Minhaj"; a book on history; "'Ujalat al-Rakib fi Dhikr Ashraf al-Manaqib", printed; and "Tahqiq al-Ula min Ahl al-Rafiq al-A'la", in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 179}.
