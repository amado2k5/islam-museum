## Overview
Abd al-Majid, titled Shams al-Din, son of Muharram, who is Abu al-Layth, son of Muhammad al-Sivasi, was a preacher among the scholars of the Ottoman state. The entry dates his birth to 971 AH, equal to 1564 CE, and his death to 1049 AH, equal to 1639 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.

## Life
Sultan Mehmed III summoned him from Sivas to Istanbul, where he stayed to preach and guide until his death {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli says he wrote about twenty books and treatises, some in Arabic, among them Risalat al-Sivasi, in manuscript, in Arabic, held at Topkapi, on Sufism; and Umdat al-Musta'iddin, on morphology, in Arabic {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.

## Death
He died in 1049 AH, equal to 1639 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 141}.
