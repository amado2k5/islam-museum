## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn al-Hasan ibn Durayd, a poet and philologist who died in Baghdad in the caliphate of al-Qahir in 321 AH. Al-Mas'udi says that in his time he excelled in poetry and reached the summit of philology. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673-674}

## On language and poetry
Al-Mas'udi says that he stood in the field of language in the place of al-Khalil ibn Ahmad, and put forward things not found in the books of earlier scholars. Among his fine poems is the *Maqsura*, in which he praised Shah ibn Mikal, and a number of poets answered it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.673-674}

He wrote a separate book gathering the words of the Prophet and a summary of his sayings, which he called *al-Mujtaba*. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.594}

## Birth and books
He was born in Basra in the Salih quarter in 223 AH, and Ibn al-Nadim says that after his death his books went to Fars. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.178}

He is credited with [[work/amali-ibn-durayd|Amali Ibn Durayd]] (dictations). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}
