## Summary
Sa'd ibn Tariq ibn Ashyam, Abu Malik al-Ashja'i of Kufa, a hadith narrator. He narrated from his father, Anas ibn Malik and Abd Allah ibn Abi Awfa; Hafs ibn Ghiyath, Sufyan al-Thawri and Shu'ba narrated from him. Ahmad, Ibn Ma'in and al-Ajali called him reliable; Ibn Hibban lists him among the reliable. The book cites al-Sarifini for a death near 140 H. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13347}
