## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan Ibn al-Khashshab the Reader — sweet-voiced, excellent in recitation; a pupil of Abu Bakr Ibn al-Adami, renowned for his beautiful recitation, and on him Abu'l-Husayn Ibn al-Raffa' was taught. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.228}

## Death upon the mushaf
One vigiled night in the Rusafa mosque he recited [[passage/quran-57-16|the verse of Surat al-Hadid]], swooned, said "Yes, the time has come," then sat and wept long and fell into a long silence — and when they moved him, he was dead. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.228}
