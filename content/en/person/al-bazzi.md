## Overview
Al-Bazzi — Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. al-Qasim b. Nafi' b. Abi Bazza Bashar al-Farisi, kunya Abu'l-Hasan — Ibn Kathir the muqri"s other transmitter; he died in 270 aged ninety {cite:wafayat-ayan}.

## Chain and reading in Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr

Ibn al-Jazari says al-Bazzi died in 250 AH, born in 170, and was an imam of the reading, a verifier, exact and careful and reliable in it, to whom the headship of teaching in Mecca came; he was the muezzin of the Sacred Mosque. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 130} His chain reaches [[person/abdallah-ibn-kathir|Ibn Kathir]] through a line of Meccan teachers that passes by Isma'il ibn 'Abd Allah ibn Qustantin, known as al-Qist, Ma'ruf ibn Mushkan and Shibl ibn 'Abbad. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 130} Among those who transmitted his reading was Abu Rabi'a, who died in Ramadan 294 and became muezzin of the Sacred Mosque after al-Bazzi; al-Dani said he was a man of exactness, care, reliability and justice. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 131}

Ibn Khallikan's Wafayat al-A'yan puts al-Bazzi's death in 270 AH at the age of ninety, while al-Nashr puts it in 250 AH with birth in 170; the two sources are left side by side. {cite:wafayat-ayan} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 130}
