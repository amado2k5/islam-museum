## Overview
The hafiz Abu Mas'ud 'Abd al-Jalil b. Muhammad al-Kutubi of Isfahan; he died in Sha'ban 553 at eighty-seven, transmitting from Rizqallah al-Tamimi, Abu Bakr Ibn Maja al-Ibhari and a host. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 647}

## Praise
Abu Musa al-Madini said: a prodigy in knowledge, manner and humility — a muhaddith in wording and memory on his preaching pulpit; Ibn Nasir al-Din said he was an imam-hafiz of the sons of muhaddithin; Ibn 'Asakir magnified him and Ibn al-Sam'ani praised him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 647}
