## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abi al-Saj, lord of Azerbaijan. He died in 288 AH, and his followers and servants disagreed after him. Some of them went over to his brother Yusuf ibn Abi al-Saj, and some to his son Budar. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.628}
