## Overview
Abu Humayd al-Tusi, one of the leaders of the people of Khurasan, whom al-Mas'udi mentions in the report of the allegiance to Abu al-Abbas. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 241-242}

## His meeting with Sabiq al-Khwarazmi
Abu Humayd met Sabiq al-Khwarazmi in the market of al-Kunasa, and learned from him the killing of Ibrahim the imam, that the bequest was to his brother Abu al-Abbas, and that he was in Kufa. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 241-242}

## The allegiance
Abu Humayd informed Abu al-Jahm and Musa ibn Ka'b, and they went with Sabiq to Abu al-Abbas, and Abu Humayd gave him the allegiance and bent to kiss the edges of his garment, while Abu Salama did not know of it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 241-242}
