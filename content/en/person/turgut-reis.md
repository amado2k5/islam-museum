## Summary
Governor of Tripoli and Djerba, one of the great Ottoman sea captains {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249-252}.

## Tripoli and Malta
Pressed by rebels at home, he persuaded [[person/piyale-pasha|Piyale Pasha]] in 967/1560 to turn aside to Tripoli and crush the rising near Tajura; Süleyman then ordered him to join the Malta campaign of 973/1565 with Piyale and Hasan b. Khayr al-Din {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249-252}.
