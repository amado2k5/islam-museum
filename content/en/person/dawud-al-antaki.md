## Overview
Dawud ibn Umar al-Antaki was an Arab physician from Antioch, called "the blind" because he was blind in one eye, and he lived in the tenth century AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## His death
According to the encyclopaedia, he died in 1008 AH (1600 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
