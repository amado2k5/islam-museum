## Overview
Amr ibn Uthman ibn Affan, son of the caliph Uthman. Al-Mas'udi relates that he was with Khalid ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Asid, and they offered Ibn al-Zubayr the safe conduct of Abd al-Malik for him and for those with him, and that he might settle in whichever land he wished under God's covenant and pledge. Ibn al-Zubayr refused to accept it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.105}
