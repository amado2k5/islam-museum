## Overview
Al-Dabili was a peer of Abu Abd Allah Ibn Nafis, and they competed for leadership. He lived for some years after him, and no book of his survives. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.269}

The museum's data do not record his full name or the date of his death, which need an additional source.
