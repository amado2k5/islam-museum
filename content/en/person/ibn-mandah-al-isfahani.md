## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ishaq ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Mandah, Abu Abd Allah al-Hafiz al-Isfahani, from a family known for hadith and memorisation. He died in Isfahan in Safar of the year 396 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.233}

## His travels and works
He heard from the companions of Abu Masʿud, from Yunus ibn Habib and from Abu al-Abbas al-Mahbubi. He travelled through many lands, wrote a great deal, and compiled a history and a book of shaykhs. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.233}

## A testimony to his memory
Abu al-Abbas Jaʿfar ibn Muhammad ibn al-Muʿtazz, the hafiz, said: "I have never seen anyone with a better memory than Abu Abd Allah ibn Mandah." One day I asked him how many narrations a shaykh's hearing would comprise, and he said: "Five thousand." {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.233}
