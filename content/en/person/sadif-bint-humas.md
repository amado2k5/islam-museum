## Overview
A pre-Islamic Arab soothsayer. Al-Mas'udi mentions her among the well-known soothsayers of lean bodily build, together with Shiq, Satih, Samalqa, Zawba'a and Tarifa {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.484}.
