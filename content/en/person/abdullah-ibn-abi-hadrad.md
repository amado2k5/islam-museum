## Summary
A notable Companion of [[group/banu-aslam|Banu Aslam]]; the Prophet tasked him with delicate reconnaissance and intelligence missions, among them being sent as an undercover scout into Hawazin's army at [[place/awtas|Awtas]] before [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/83-84}.

## His mission at Awtas
The Prophet called him when Hawazin's march reached him, ordering him to enter their ranks and stay among them until he had ascertained their preparations {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/83}. Ibn Abi Hadrad entered Hawazin's army in disguise, heard Malik ibn Awf haranguing his people and ordering them to strike at dawn's first light, and slipped out by night to bring the Prophet their plan {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/83-84}.

## For young visitors
A brave Companion whom the Prophet tasked with a secret scouting mission; he entered the enemy's camp in disguise, listened to their plans and carried them back precisely, protecting the Muslim army.
