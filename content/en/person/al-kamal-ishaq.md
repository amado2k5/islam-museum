## Overview
Al-Kamal Ishaq, the great shaykh, musnid of Syria and wonder of his age; he heard from al-Silafi and sat for hadith — many nations of hafizes licensed him — al-Nawawi heard on him and read Sahih Muslim and Ahmad b. Hanbal's Musnad on him; he lived ninety-three years and died in Muharram or Dhu'l-Qa'da. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 260-261}
