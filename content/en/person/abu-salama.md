Abu Salama Abdullah ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi was one of the first Muslims in the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]]. He was among the Prophet's foster-brothers, since [[person/thuwayba|Thuwayba]] had nursed him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}


## Foster-brother of the Prophet (The Sealed Nectar, p.55)
The Sealed Nectar notes that [[person/thuwayba|Thuwayba]], a freedwoman of Abu Lahab, nursed the Prophet after his mother, and that after him she also nursed Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi, as she had earlier nursed [[person/hamza|Hamza]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.4} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.13}.


## During the secret call (The Sealed Nectar, p.76)
The book counts him among those who followed the first vanguard of the earliest Muslims in the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]]. Ibn Hisham counted more than forty such people from all the clans of Quraysh {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/245-262}. The book mentions him together with [[person/al-arqam-ibn-abi-al-arqam|Al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam]], and calls the two of them "the Makhzumis" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.76}.
