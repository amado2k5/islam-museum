## Summary
Aisha bint Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Ghani ibn Mansur of Mecca, a traditionist of the late Mamluk era {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 184}.

## Hearings
She heard readings from Umar ibn Uthman ibn Salim ibn Khalaf (the Juz' al-Ghatraf) and Muhammad ibn Azbak al-Khazindari's ninth of the Muhammaliyyat, Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Bayyani's Juz' of Abu Umar al-Zahid ghulam Tha'lab, al-Badr Abu al-Abbas ibn al-Jawkh's Muntaqa al-Mizzi, a juz' of Muhammad ibn Harun al-Hadrami, Ali ibn al-Khabbaz, and Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Mardawi's Juz' of Ibn Arafa {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 184-185}. She transmitted and the learned heard from her, among them Ibn Musa and al-Ubbi {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 185}.

## Death
She died in Ramadan 815 AH aged about seventy-four {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p. 185}.
