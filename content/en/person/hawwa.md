## Summary
The wife of Adam in al-Thaʿlabi's report. The book says that God took a rib from Adam while he slept and created Hawwa from it, and that the angels tested Adam over naming her {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.29}.

## In the book
The book says that Hawwa hastened to eat from the tree and then made it attractive to Adam. In the same place it gives a report from Saʿid ibn al-Musayyab that Adam did not eat while in his right mind, but that Hawwa gave him wine to drink {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.31}.

The book also says that some reports made her dowry a prayer upon the Prophet Muhammad three times. It lists fifteen traits that she and her daughters were tried with, among them menstruation, childbirth and a smaller share of inheritance. In this context it cites a hadith attributed to the Prophet about deficiency in intellect and religion. This is a report of what the book contains, not a ruling passed here {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.29} {cite:qisas-anbiya-thaalabi-edition p.34}.

On [[passage/quran-2-35|verse 35]], al-Tabari gives two reports on when she was created: after Adam was settled in the Garden (from Ibn Abbas through al-Suddi) or before, from his left rib (from Ibn Ishaq, relaying what he had heard from the People of the Book), and that she was named Hawwa because she was made from a living thing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 513} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 514}
