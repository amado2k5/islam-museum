## Overview
Amir ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri is the brother of [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.158}

Al-Baladhuri names him among the commanders who appointed Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah over Syria at the battle of [[event/battle-of-fihl|Fihl]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.158}

## In al-Isaba
Ibn Hajar gives him his own entry in al-Isaba, no. 4509: "Amir ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri: he is Amir ibn Malik. Discussed above" — fixing his name as Amir ibn Malik and referring back to his earlier entry. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.763}
