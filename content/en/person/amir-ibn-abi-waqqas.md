## Overview
Amir ibn Abi Waqqas al-Zuhri, a brother of [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.158}

Al-Baladhuri mentions him among the commanders who made Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah commander over Syria at the time of [[event/battle-of-fihl|the battle of Fihl]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.158}
