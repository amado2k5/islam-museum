# Atiku Abubakar

Atiku Abubakar is a Nigerian politician and businessman, born on 25 November 1946 in Jada, a village then part of the British Cameroons. His father drowned in 1957 and he was raised by his mother, Aisha Kande. After secondary school he took a diploma at the School of Hygiene in Kano in 1967 and a law diploma at the Ahmadu Bello University Institute of Administration in 1969, and much later a master's degree in international relations at Anglia Ruskin University in 2021 {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-en} {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-ar} {cite:wikidata-atiku-abubakar}.

## Customs officer and vice president

He worked in the Nigeria Customs Service for twenty years, rising to deputy director, and retired in April 1989 to take up business and politics full-time. Having won his party's Adamawa governorship nomination, he was instead selected as Olusegun Obasanjo's running mate for the 1999 election, and served as vice president from 29 May 1999 to 29 May 2007, the pair winning re-election in 2003 {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-en}.

## Six runs for the presidency

He ran for the presidency six times without winning: he lost the Social Democratic Party primaries in 1993, came third as the Action Congress candidate in 2007, lost the PDP primaries in 2011 to President Goodluck Jonathan and in 2014 to Muhammadu Buhari (by then in the APC), and was the PDP's candidate in the 2019 and 2023 general elections, losing to Buhari and then to Bola Tinubu; the Supreme Court dismissed his challenge to the 2023 result. In 2025 he left the PDP and joined the African Democratic Congress {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-en} {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-ar}.

## Adamawa titles

The Lamido of Adamawa gave him the chieftaincy title of Turaki in 1982, and in June 2017 he received the title of Waziri of Adamawa, his former title passing to his son Aliyu {cite:wikipedia-atiku-abubakar-en}.
