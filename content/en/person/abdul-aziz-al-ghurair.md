## Summary
Abdul Aziz Al Ghurair is a leading Emirati banker and businessman, chairman of Mashreq Bank, the institution his family has controlled for decades, and founder of the Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation, described as the largest private education philanthropy in the UAE. His career combines banking, public office and large-scale educational giving. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}

## Early life and education
He was born in 1954 into the Al Ghurair merchant family of Dubai, one of the oldest business houses in the Emirates, studied business administration at California State Polytechnic University where he earned a master's degree, then returned to join the family's banking, industrial and real-estate interests. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}

## Banking and public office
He leads Mashreq Bank, one of the UAE's oldest banks, founded by the family in 1967, as chairman of its board, overseeing its growth into a regional institution. In public life he served as speaker of the Federal National Council, the UAE's consultative assembly, from 2007 to 2011 — the highest consultative legislative office held by a businessman in the country. He has also led family companies including Abdulla Al Ghurair Foods and interests in construction and insurance. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}

## Educational philanthropy
In 2015 he announced the pledge of roughly a third of his fortune — over one billion US dollars — to establish the Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation, at the time an unprecedented philanthropic commitment in the Arab world. The foundation funds high-achieving, underprivileged Arab youth with scholarships at leading universities inside and outside the region, launched online-learning and technical-skills programmes including partnerships with American universities to offer accredited degrees remotely, and later broadened its umbrella into the Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation for Development, addressing the needs of refugees and vulnerable groups across the region. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair} {cite:muslim500-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}

## Recognition and public standing
He appears regularly on lists of the wealthiest and most generous Arabs and has received Arab and international leadership and philanthropy awards. Economic media present him as the model of a Gulf businessman who converted family wealth into permanent institutional giving. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}

## Legacy
Al Ghurair reset the ceiling of private giving in the Gulf when he dedicated a third of his fortune to education at a time when institutional Arab philanthropy was limited; his foundation has become a regional reference in funding the education of Arab youth, especially high-achievers of limited means and refugees — a shift observers read as the move from family charity to an institutional development strategy. {cite:wikipedia-abdul-aziz-al-ghurair}
