## Summary
Salman Iqbal is a Pakistani media magnate born in 1974, owner and chief executive of the ARY Digital Network, Pakistan's largest private television group; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-salman-iqbal} {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Career
The son of ARY group founder Abdul Razzak Yaqoob, he took over the family media business in the 2000s and expanded ARY Digital — launched in 2000 — into a network spanning drama, news, entertainment and sport broadcasting. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## The network
ARY's flagship entertainment channel airs Pakistan's most-watched dramas and award shows, while its news arm is among the country's leading channels; the group also holds major sports-broadcasting properties including Pakistan Super League rights. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Sports and ventures
He has invested in cricket leagues and football clubs and positioned ARY as a global Pakistani media brand reaching the diaspora in Britain, the Gulf and North America. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Assessment
He is credited with consolidating Pakistan's largest entertainment network and globalising its reach; commentary on the network's politics is reported attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal} {cite:muslim500-salman-iqbal}


## ARY's scale
Under his leadership ARY grew into a multi-channel group — news, entertainment, sports and Urdu programming distributed across the Gulf, Europe and North America — reaching a large share of the Pakistani diaspora. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Sports ventures
He owns and chairs Karachi Kings of the Pakistan Super League — one of the league's flagship franchises — and has used the platform to promote cricket development and fan engagement. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Philanthropy
ARY-linked charitable initiatives in education, health and disaster relief are part of his public profile; the organisation's foundations and campaigns feature prominently in its programming. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal} {cite:muslim500-salman-iqbal}

## Origins
Born into the family behind ARY — founded by his father in Dubai in 2000 — he rose through the group's businesses to become its chief executive, presiding over its expansion into a multi-channel and multi-industry concern. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## ARY's reach
Under his leadership ARY grew into a group spanning news, entertainment and sports — its channels distributed across the Gulf, Europe and North America and reaching a large share of the Pakistani diaspora. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## Karachi Kings
His ownership of the Karachi Kings franchise — one of the Pakistan Super League's most-recognised teams — made him a visible figure in cricket as well as media, using the platform to promote Pakistani sport. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal} {cite:muslim500-salman-iqbal}

## The ARY empire
Salman Iqbal's leadership of ARY — the group founded by his father in Dubai in 2000 — presided over its expansion into a multi-channel and multi-industry concern spanning news, entertainment and sports. Under his chairmanship ARY's channels reached a large share of the Pakistani diaspora across the Gulf, Europe and North America, while his ownership of the Karachi Kings franchise made him a visible figure in cricket as well as media. The group's charitable initiatives in education, health and disaster relief are part of its public profile. {cite:wikipedia-salman-iqbal}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists him among the most influential Muslims in media, citing his leadership of ARY's expansion into a multi-channel group and his role in Pakistani cricket through the Karachi Kings franchise. {cite:muslim500-salman-iqbal}
