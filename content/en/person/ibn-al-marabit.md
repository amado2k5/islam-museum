## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Ibn al-Marabit Muhammad b. Khalaf b. Sa'id al-Andalusi — qadi and scholar of Almeria; he transmitted from al-Muhallab b. Abi Sufra and others, composed a commentary on al-Bukhari, and was a head of Malik's madhhab to whom people journeyed; he died in Shawwal 485. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 375}
