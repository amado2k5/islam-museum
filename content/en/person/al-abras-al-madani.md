## Overview
Al-Abras al-Madani, a nickname and not a name, is said by the narrator to have been with his nephew Ali ibn Rabi' among those who went into the house with [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] and plundered it, and a party in the village with them was feared by [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|Ahmad ibn Muhammad]] until morning. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} The narrator says they wished the two men harm, which is his description and not the museum's judgement, and that al-Abras's son took a slave-girl of Abu al-Husayn. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} The index lists Ali ibn Rabi' at pp. 145 and 146 among others and gives al-Abras no entry of his own. See [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]].
