## Overview
Miharish ibn al-Majli al-Uqayli, an Arab amir of the [[group/banu-uqayl|Banu Uqayl]], lord of the town of Anah on the Euphrates. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.113}

## Sheltering the caliph
He sheltered the Abbasid caliph al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah for a full year, after al-Basasiri and Qurayish ibn Badran drove him out of Baghdad in 450 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.113}

Ibn al-Jawzi describes him as having "a good way", and says that he attended to the caliph's service himself until he returned honoured after al-Basasiri was killed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.113} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.296}

It was there that al-Qa'im composed his well-known poem on Hadītha. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.114}
