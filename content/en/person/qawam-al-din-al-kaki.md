## Summary
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Sinjari al-Khujandi, known as Qawam al-Din al-Kaki: a Hanafi jurist and legal theorist. He studied with Ala al-Din Abd al-Aziz al-Bukhari, reading al-Hidaya with him, and with Husam al-Din Hasan al-Sighnaqi. He reached Cairo and then taught and issued fatwas at the Mosque of Mardin until his death in 749 AH. Works attributed to him include Mi'raj al-Dirayat (a commentary on al-Hidaya) and Jami al-Asrar (a commentary on al-Manar). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7163}
