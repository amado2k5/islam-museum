## Overview
Imran ibn [[person/amr-ibn-amir-muzayqiya|Amr Muzayqiya]], lord and chief of the [[group/al-azd|Azd]]. In the account of *al-Rahiq al-Makhtum*, the migration of the Azd from Yemen was on his judgement. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.231-235}

## The migration
They moved about in the lands of Yemen and sent scouts ahead. Then they went north, and the sons of Amr dispersed to their last dwellings. [[person/thalaba-ibn-amr|Tha'laba ibn Amr]] turned toward the Hijaz; Khuza'a moved to Marr al-Zahran; Imran ibn Amr settled in [[place/oman|Oman]] with his sons, who are the Azd of Oman; and [[person/jafna-ibn-amr|Jafna ibn Amr]] went to Syria. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} The author notes that the sources differ widely on the time of these migrations and their causes. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17}

## For young visitors
Imran was the leader of the Azd tribe when it left Yemen, and he and his children settled in the land of Oman.
