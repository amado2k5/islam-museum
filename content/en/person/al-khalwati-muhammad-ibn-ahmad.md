## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Buhuti al-Hanbali, known as al-Khalwati al-Misri. A Hanbali jurist of Egypt, attributed to Buhut, and known as al-Khalwati. He died in 1088 AH (1678 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
