## Overview
Al-Mas'udi says that Ishaq ibn Ayyub al-Anbari hastened to submit to al-Mu'tadid and entered his camp, when the caliph encamped against Hamdan ibn Hamdun in the fortress of al-Sawara. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.609}

The data available go no further than this report. It needs an additional source.
