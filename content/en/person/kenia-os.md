## Summary
Kenia Os is a Mexican singer and social-media personality who built a mass online following as a YouTube content creator before launching a successful recording career in reggaeton and Latin pop. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en} No source read for this record attests any religion for her, and her data record states that plainly and leaves her faith as unknown. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en}

## Early life and YouTube
She was born Kenia Guadalupe Flores Osuna on 15 July 1999 in Mazatlán, Sinaloa, Mexico. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en} In 2015 she created her first YouTube channel and later joined the Jukilop creator team, amassing millions of subscribers; in 2018 she left the group after contract disagreements, her earlier social networks were deleted under a previous contract, and the ensuing controversy and rivalry among the YouTubers raised her profile further. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en}

## Music career
She signed with Lizos Music in 2018 and released her debut single "Por Siempre"; after a period as an independent artist she signed with Sony Music Mexico. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en} Her studio albums are Cambios de Luna (2022), K23 (2022), Pink Aura (2024) and K de karma (2026), and her streaming concert MelanKOs (2021) was reported as the most-watched online concert in Ibero-America's history at the time. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en} Her track "Malas Decisiones" became a global streaming hit. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en}

## Television and awards
She has co-hosted the 2021 MTV MIAW Awards, judged Disney+'s ¡Conecta y Canta!, and appeared on HBO Max's Bake Off Celebrity: Mexico and The Voice Mexico. {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en} Her honours include seven MTV MIAW Awards, the MTV Europe Music Award for Best Latin America North Act (2022 and 2023), a Heat Latin Music Award, Premio Lo Nuestro wins including New Artist of the Year (Female) in 2024, and a Latin Grammy nomination for Best Long Form Music Video for Universo K23 (2023). {cite:wikipedia-kenia-os-en}
