## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad, Zayn al-Din, known as Ibn al-'Ayni, was a learned Hanafi engaged in letters and grammar, born and died at Damascus in 893 AH (1488 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.300}

He composed a commentary on Ibn Malik's Alfiyya; Hall al-Shatibiyya on the readings; works on prosody and on Turkish lexicography; and Sharh al-Manar on usul. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.300}
