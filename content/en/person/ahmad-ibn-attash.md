## Overview
Ahmad b. 'Attash, master of the Batiniyya's fortress at Isfahan; he held it twelve years — a castle built by Malikshah atop a mountain. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 410}

## The fortress falls
In 500 Sultan Muhammad b. Malikshah campaigned against the Batiniyya, took the castle and killed him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 410}
