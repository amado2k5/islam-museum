## Overview
Abd al-Wahid ibn al-Muwaffaq, uncle of al-Muktafi. Al-Mas'udi says that [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] honoured him and inclined to him, and that he had no resolve for the caliphate or leadership, his resolve being in amusement. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 639-640}

## His end
He was detained by Mu'nis al-Fahl, and al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah was sent to him. Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muktafi was told that he had corresponded with his servants, and that al-Qasim kept at al-Muktafi until he had him killed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 639-640}
