## Overview
Ibn al-Muwaffaq, uncle of al-Muktafi, whom al-Mu'tadid held in the deepest affection; he had no ambition for caliphate or command, only for play with the young. Detained at Mu'nis al-Fahl's, he was beheaded on al-Qasim ibn Ubayd Allah's orders.
