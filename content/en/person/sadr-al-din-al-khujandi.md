## Overview
Sadr al-Din al-Khujandi, head of the Shafi'is in Isfahan. Ibn al-Athir reports that after the governor there, he was the leading authority over all its people {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.238-239}.

## Handing over Isfahan
He wrote to the Diwan in Baghdad offering to hand the city over to whichever of the Diwan's forces arrived, because the people of Isfahan disliked the army of the Khwarazm Shah. The army of the Caliph al-Nasir arrived in the year 591 AH, the Khwarazmians left the city, and the Caliph's army took possession of it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.238-239}.
