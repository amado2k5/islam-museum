## Summary

Abd al-Aziz al-Duri (1919–2010) was an Iraqi historian, professor of Islamic history and former president of Baghdad University, among the leading lights of the Iraqi school of early Islamic economic and social history.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-aziz-al-duri}

## Life

Born in al-A'dhamiyya, Baghdad, he studied history in Baghdad and London, became professor and then president of Baghdad University, worked in Arab scholarly institutions, and died in Amman.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-aziz-al-duri}

## Works

His books include Studies in the Later Abbasid Ages, The Rise of Historical Writing Among the Arabs, Introduction to Arab Economic History and The First Abbasid Age. His method is marked by returning to documents and primary texts and analysing economic structures.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-aziz-al-duri}

## Standing

He is remembered as a founder of economic historical studies in Arab universities and as the teacher of generations of historians in Iraq and the Arab world.{cite:wikipedia-abd-al-aziz-al-duri}
