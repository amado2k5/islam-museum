## Overview
Al-Aswad ibn Ghaffar, king of Judays when King Hassan destroyed them. Al-Mas'udi relates that he fled until he came to the home of Tayy, who gave him protection from the king and others without knowing who he was. He says that his descendants among Tayy are known today. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.455}
