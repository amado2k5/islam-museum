## Summary
al-Hasan ibn Abi Ja'far al-Hafari was a worshipping reciter devoted to striving, and was a support to the believing jinn among the worshippers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on pages 139 and 140. The number printed is 493, and by sequence it is probably 494. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/139}

## The mosque before dawn
Abu Imran al-Tammar relates: one morning before dawn he went to the mosque of al-Hafari and found its door closed. Al-Hasan was sitting and praying, and there was a noise in the mosque and a group answering his prayer with amen. Abu Imran sat at the door until al-Hasan finished, then al-Hasan stood, gave the call to prayer and opened the door; Abu Imran went in and saw no one in the mosque. When morning came and the people had gone, he said to al-Hasan: O Abu Sa'id, I saw a wonder. He told him what he had seen and heard. Al-Hasan answered: those are jinn from Nasibin, who come and attend with me the completion of the recitation of the Qur'an every Friday night, and then they leave. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/139} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140}
