## Overview
'Abd al-Malik b. Siraj, Abu Marwan the Umayyads' mawla of Cordoba — the philologist of al-Andalus uncontested; he transmitted from Yunus b. Mughith, Makki b. Abi Talib and a company, and was one of the vessels of knowledge. He died in Dhu'l-Hijja 489 at ninety. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 392-393}
