## Summary
Au'ni bin Mohamed, known as Ustaz Auni Mohamed, is a Malaysian preacher and schoolteacher born in Pasir Mas, Kelantan, on 29 December 1976, known for his lectures on Islamic eschatology — the signs of the end times, the Dajjal, and the hidden histories he reads into world events and films. {cite:ecentral-auni-mohamed} {cite:ilabur-auni-mohamed}

He has given scheduled lectures on these themes since 2004, and the Malaysian press has nicknamed him the "Zakir Naik Malaysia" and "Ustaz Fotostat" for the density of the information in his talks. {cite:ilabur-auni-mohamed}

His audience is a Facebook audience: his official page counted 159,948 followers and his professional profile 358,122 followers, both read on the platform itself on 15 November 2026. {cite:facebook-auni-mohamed-official} {cite:facebook-auni-mohamed-profile}

## Life and work
The biodata profiles record that he studied Islamic legislation at Al-Azhar in Egypt, with one profile giving his study period as 1994 to 2005. {cite:ecentral-auni-mohamed} {cite:ilabur-auni-mohamed}

He works as a JQAF teacher — teaching Jawi, the Qur'an, Arabic and the obligatory duties of the faith — at Sekolah Kebangsaan Seri Relau, a government primary school in Bayan Lepas, Penang, and he has lived in Penang since 2017. {cite:ecentral-auni-mohamed} {cite:ilabur-auni-mohamed}

Alongside the classroom, he lectures publicly on eschatology, comparative religion and the history of civilisations, the themes on which his ceramah reputation rests. {cite:ecentral-auni-mohamed}
