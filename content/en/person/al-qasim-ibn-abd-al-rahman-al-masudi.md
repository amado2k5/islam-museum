## Summary
Al-Qasim ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud, Abu Abd al-Rahman, al-Mas'udi of Kufa and its judge; the entry gives his death as 120 AH. He narrated from his father, Ibn Umar, Jabir ibn Samura and Masruq, and held the judgeship of Kufa taking no pay for it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7522}
