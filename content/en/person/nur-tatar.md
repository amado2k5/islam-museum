## Summary
Nur Tatar is a Turkish taekwondo practitioner, a world and European champion competing in the feather, light and welterweight divisions. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en} She was born on 16 August 1992 in Van. {cite:wikidata-nur-tatar}

## Beginnings
Tatar won her first medal at the age of 15, won the European Junior Taekwondo Championships twice, and took the silver medal at the 2010 European Championships in Saint Petersburg. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en} She was a member of the TSE Sports Club in Ankara before transferring to Ankara İller Bankası, and is coached by Cüneyt Gülçek. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en}

## Career
Tatar qualified for the London 2012 Olympics, where she won the silver medal at 67 kg. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en} At the 2013 Mediterranean Games in Mersin she became the gold medallist. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en} She qualified again for the Rio 2016 Olympics. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en}

## Personal life
Tatar married Mehran Askari, a former member of the Iranian national taekwondo team, in 2017, and is also known as Nur Tatar Askari. {cite:wikipedia-nur-tatar-en}
