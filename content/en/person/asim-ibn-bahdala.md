## Overview
Asim ibn Bahdala, kunya Abu Bakr, known as Ibn Abi al-Najud, a client of the Banu Judayma ibn Malik ibn Nasr ibn Qa'in. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.74}

## In Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim counts him in the third class of the Kufans after Yahya ibn Wathhab, and says that he died in 128 AH. He read with Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami and Zirr ibn Hubaysh. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.74}

Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash and Hafs ibn Sulayman al-Bazzaz related from him. The report says that the reading he took from Asim goes back to Ali ibn Abi Talib through the report of Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.74}

## Notes
- The source mixes the death date of Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash (193 AH) into the context of Asim's death; this has not been recorded here, and it needs an additional source.
