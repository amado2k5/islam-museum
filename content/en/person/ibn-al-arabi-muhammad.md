## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Ziyad ibn al-A'rabi, a Kufan philologist. His father Ziyad was a Sindhi slave belonging to Sulayman ibn Mujalid. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.156}

## Dates
The encyclopaedia says he was born in 150 AH (767 CE) and died in 230 AH (845 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.156} These dates come from a single source, so they need an additional source.
