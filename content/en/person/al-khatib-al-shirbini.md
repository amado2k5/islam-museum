## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Shirbini, Shams al-Din, was a Shafi'i jurist and exegete, one of the people of Cairo. The entry in al-A'lam dates his death to 977 AH, 1570 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His writings
Al-Zirikli says he left several works, among them al-Siraj al-munir, printed in four volumes, a commentary on the Qur'an; al-Iqna fi hall alfaz Abi Shuja, printed in two volumes; a commentary on the Shawahid of al-Qatr, printed; Mughni al-muhtaj, printed in four parts, a commentary on al-Nawawi's Minhaj al-talibin in jurisprudence; marginal notes on al-Mutawwal, printed, in rhetoric; and a manual of the pilgrimage rites, printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
