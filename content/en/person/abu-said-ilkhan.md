## Overview
Abu Said ibn Khudabanda, Ilkhan ruler of the Tatars after his father, who died in 736 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.735}

## In al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti says he commanded justice and upheld the sunna. He ordered that the Friday sermon invoke God's pleasure on the two Shaykhs, then Uthman, then Ali. Many troubles ceased in his time. Al-Suyuti describes him as one of the best and most upright of the Tatar kings. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.735}

He adds that after him no one rose among them, and they scattered. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.735}
