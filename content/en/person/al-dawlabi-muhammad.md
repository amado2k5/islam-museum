## Overview
Abu Bishr Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Hammad b. Sa'd al-Ansari by clientage, the warraq, of Rayy, al-Dawlabi; versed in hadith, akhbar and histories, he heard hadiths in Iraq and Syria and transmitted from Muhammad b. Bashshar, Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Jabbar al-'Ataridi and a great host; al-Tabarani and Abu Hatim Ibn Hibban al-Busti transmitted from him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His works
He has useful works on history and the births and deaths of the scholars; the masters of this art relied on him for transmission and told of him in their famous books and works — he was one of the authorities to whom men referred, and a fine writer. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
