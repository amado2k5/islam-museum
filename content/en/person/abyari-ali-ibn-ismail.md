## Overview
Ali ibn Isma'il ibn Ali ibn Hasan ibn Atiyya al-Tilkani al-Abyari, from the people of Abyar in the Gharbiyya in Egypt. He was born in 557 AH and died in 616 AH. Books of biographical dictionaries and of similar names have entries on him. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
