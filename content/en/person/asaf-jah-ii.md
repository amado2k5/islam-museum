## Summary

Mir Nizam Ali Khan, Asaf Jah II (7 March 1734 – 6 August 1803) was Nizam of Hyderabad from 8 July 1762 to 1803, the fourth son of the state's founder Asaf Jah I and Umda Begum {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-en}.

## The Deccan wars and the British alliance

He had made his name as the Deccan's leading commander against the Marathas before he seized Bidar, arrested his brother Salabat Jung and was confirmed as Subedar of the Deccan by the Mughal emperor Shah Alam II {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-en}. His long reign was filled with war against the Maratha Peshwas — defeat at Kharda in 1795 cost him Daulatabad, Aurangabad and Sholapur and a heavy indemnity — and he kept a French-officered corps under Monsieur Raymond as his military mainstay {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-en}. Seeing Tipu Sultan's fall coming, he entered a subsidiary alliance with the British East India Company in 1798, and Hyderabad, a state comparable to the United Kingdom in area and population, became a princely state within the British Raj {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-en}. He died at Chowmahalla Palace, Hyderabad, aged 69, on 6 August 1803 {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-en} {cite:wikipedia-asaf-jah-ii-ur}.
