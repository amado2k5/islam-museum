## Overview
Nukhayl ibn Muhajir al-Khushaymi was one of the [[group/al-khushaymiyyun|Khushaymis]] whom the book lists among those [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] put out of Najran as "troublemakers who had made disorder in his land"; he was fettered, carried in a litter to Sa'da and jailed in the village of [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The book does not give his lineage here, what he did or what became of him after the jailing. "Nukhayl" is the index reading, p. 458; the body text reads "Nahil" in the optical reading. See [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]]. The account is Zaydi, and the description as troublemaker is the narrator's.
