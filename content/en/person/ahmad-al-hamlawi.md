## Overview
Shaykh Ahmad al-Hamlawi, an Egyptian scholar and writer, born in 1856 CE (1273 AH) in one of the villages of the Balbis district in the Sharqiyya governorate. He graduated from the Dar al-Ulum school in Cairo, and died in 1932 CE (1350 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

Both dates are recorded as reported dates and need an additional source. The museum's data do not include the titles of his works.
