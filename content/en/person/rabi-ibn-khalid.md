## Overview
Al-Rabi' ibn Khalid. al-Mas'udi reports that he heard al-Hajjaj preach and said that he would never pray behind him. He said that if he saw people fighting him, he would fight alongside them. He fought at the monastery of the Skulls (Dayr al-Jamajim) until he was killed {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.137}. See [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]].
