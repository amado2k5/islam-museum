## Overview
Muzaffar al-Din Kokburi ibn Zayn al-Din Ali, brother of [[person/zayn-al-din-yusuf-irbil|Zayn al-Din Yusuf]], lord of Irbil. He became lord of Irbil after his brother. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.199}

## After his brother's death
Zayn al-Din died on 18 Ramadan 586 AH while the two of them were with Salah al-Din in the army. Imad al-Katib describes in *al-Barq al-Shami* that people came to offer Kokburi condolences, and found him busy securing what his brother had left. He arrested a group of his emirs. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.199}

## Irbil
He asked Salah al-Din for Irbil in exchange for giving up Harran and Edessa, and Salah al-Din granted it to him, and added to it Shahrazur and its districts, Darbandh Qarababili and Bani Qafjaq. Ibn al-Athir says that it remained a grudge in the throat of the Atabeg house. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.199}
