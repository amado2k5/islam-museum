## Overview
Gennadios, the patriarch Constantinople's Christians elected after the conquest, in al-Salabi's telling.

## His installation
The book says Mehmed II met the bishops, assured them their creeds, laws and houses of worship would be preserved, and ordered a new patriarch installed — they elected Gennadios {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 111}.

## Meeting the sultan
He came in a solemn procession of bishops to the sultan's seat, was received with great honour, dined with him and spoke on religious, political and social matters — leaving, as the book portrays it, with his view of the Ottoman sultans and of Muslims entirely changed {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 112}.
