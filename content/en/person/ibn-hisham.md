## Overview
The imam Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham, author of the *Sira* (Biography of the Prophet). He also wrote *Ansab Himyar wa Mulukaha* (The Genealogies of Himyar and Its Kings). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}

## His works
Haji Khalifa attributes to him [[work/sira-ibn-ishaq-ibn-hisham|the Sira]] and [[work/ansab-himyar|the Genealogies of Himyar]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}
