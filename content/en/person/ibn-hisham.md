## Summary
Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham was the imam famed as the author of the Sira; to him also belongs the book Ansab Himyar wa-Mulukiha, and he died in 213 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}

## Details
In the chapter on genealogies in Kashf al-Zunun, Hajji Khalifa lists Ansab Himyar wa-Mulukiha as the work of the imam Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham, author of the Sira, who died in 213 AH, in the course of his survey of genealogical compilations such as al-Baladhuri's Ansab al-Ashraf — a large, very useful work of which twenty volumes were written and left unfinished — and al-Sam'ani's Ansab, a great work in this art whose complete text runs to eight volumes but is rarely found; the edition's notes add that Ibn Hisham's biography appears in Inbah al-Ruwat, Wafayat al-A'yan, Tarikh al-Islam, Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' and elsewhere. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.653}
