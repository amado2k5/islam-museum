## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali al-Rida ibn Musa al-Kazim ibn Ja'far al-Sadiq ibn Muhammad al-Baqir ibn Ali Zayn al-'Abidin is one of the Twelve Imams in the belief of the Imamiyya. Al-Ma'mun married his daughter Umm Habib to him in 202 AH, made him heir-apparent and struck his name on dinars and dirhams. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.3 p.268}

The reason: al-Ma'mun summoned the Abbasids' descendants, men and women — thirty-three thousand of them — at Marw in Khurasan, called for Ali and lodged him in the highest station, gathered his intimates and told them he had found none in his time better or worthier of the charge than Ali al-Rida, and so gave him allegiance, ordering black removed from dress and standards. News reached the Abbasids of Iraq, who saw in it the loss of power; they deposed al-Ma'mun and gave allegiance to his uncle Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi in 202 AH (some say 203) — a famous story. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.3 p.268}

## In Tarikh al-Rusul wa'l-Muluk — al-Tabari (ed. Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim)
Ali ibn Musa al-Rida. Al-Ma'mun made him his heir in 201 AH, and ordered people to wear green. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.554}

## In Tarikh al-Rusul wa'l-Muluk — al-Tabari (ed. Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim): In al-Suyuti
Al-Ma'mun gave him the title "al-Rida", struck coins in his name, married him to his daughter, and ordered the abandonment of black and the wearing of green. He died in 203 AH while al-Ma'mun was on his way to Iraq. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.487}

## In Tarikh al-Rusul wa'l-Muluk — al-Tabari (ed. Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim): In al-Mas'udi
He was seized by poison in Tus and buried there, aged forty-nine years and six months. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.399}
