## Overview
The imam Ahmad Gran, known as al-Jarad (the locust), a fighter for the lands of Abyssinia. Ibn al-Imad mentions him in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.617}

## In Ibn al-Imad
In 934 AH he took the city of Harar in the lands of Abyssinia, and the power of its ruler was too weak to resist him. His affair grew greater until he conquered many lands of Abyssinia, and he persisted in holy war and raiding. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.617}

Ibn al-Imad relays that it was said his conquests resembled only those of the Companions, and that much was told of his courage and of his conduct of affairs according to the laws of the Sharia. These are the words of Ibn al-Imad and of those he relays from. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.617}
