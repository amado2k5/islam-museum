## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham, a Qurashi of the Makhzum, one of the readers of Mecca. Uthman included him in the committee for copying the codices with Zayd ibn Thabit, Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr and Sa'id ibn al-As. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

## On the committee
He is one of the three Qurashis ordered to write what they disagreed about in the tongue of Quraysh. The event is detailed in [[event/uthman-standardizes-mushaf|the gathering of the Qur'an under Uthman]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

A second source is needed: the data does not give the year of his birth or death.
