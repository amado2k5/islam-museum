## Overview
Abu'l-Fath 'Abdus b. 'Ubaydallah b. Muhammad b. 'Abdus — chief and muhaddith of Hamadhan; Abu Bakr Ibn Lal licensed him, and he heard Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Hamduya al-Tusi and al-Husayn b. Fathuwayh. He died in Jumada II 490 at ninety-five, having transmitted after Abu Zur'a. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 395}
