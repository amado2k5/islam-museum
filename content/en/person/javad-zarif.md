# Mohammad Javad Zarif

Mohammad Javad Zarif (born 7 January 1960) is an Iranian career diplomat and academic, foreign minister of Iran from 2013 to 2021 in the government of Hassan Rouhani, and earlier his country's ambassador to the United Nations. The Persian article gives his birth on 17 Dey 1338 and the Arabic article gives 7 January 1960, citing the foreign ministry biography, while the English article alone gives 8 January 1960; the day carried here is 7 January, and the variant is recorded on his data record. {cite:wikipedia-zarif-fa} {cite:wikipedia-zarif-ar} {cite:wikipedia-zarif-en} {cite:wikidata-zarif}

## Early life and education

Zarif was born in Tehran into a wealthy merchant family — the Persian and Arabic articles describe the family as religious and conservative, a description of his background rather than of his own affiliation. Sent to the United States at seventeen, he studied at San Francisco State University and took a second master's and a doctorate in international law and policy at the University of Denver, completed in 1988 with a thesis on self-defence in international law. {cite:wikipedia-zarif-en} {cite:wikipedia-zarif-fa} {cite:wikipedia-zarif-ar}

## Career

He served as deputy foreign minister for legal and international affairs from 1992 to 2002 and as permanent representative to the United Nations from 2002 to 2007. As foreign minister from 15 August 2013 to 25 August 2021 he led the Iranian side of the negotiations with the P5+1 that produced the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action of 14 July 2015. His February 2019 resignation was rejected and he remained in post. In August 2024 he returned to government as vice president for strategic affairs under President Pezeshkian, leaving that post in March 2025. {cite:wikipedia-zarif-en} {cite:wikipedia-zarif-fa}
