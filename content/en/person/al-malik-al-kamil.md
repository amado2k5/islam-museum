## Overview
Al-Malik al-Kamil Muhammad ibn al-Adil ibn Ayyub, an Ayyubid sultan. His father [[person/al-malik-al-adil-ayyub|al-Malik al-Adil]] set him over Egypt after he took it in 595 AH, when [[person/al-malik-al-aziz-uthman|al-Malik al-Aziz]] died. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 694,696}

Al-Suyuti says that he was too weak to resist the Franks when they took Damietta in 616 AH, so he built a city at the junction of the two seas and named it al-Mansura, and camped there with his army. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 694,696}
