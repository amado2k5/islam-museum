## Overview

Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn 'Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Khalaf al-Ansari al-Gharnati, the preacher known as Ibn al-Badhish. He died in Granada in Jumada II 540 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 97}

## His book

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an imam and hadith master and lists his al-Iqna' fi al-Qira'at al-Sab', saying he read the whole Qur'an to Abu al-Ma'ali ibn al-Labban in line with its contents, and he then gives chains back to the author by way of Abu Hayyan and Abu Ja'far ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 97} Elsewhere he argues that books such as al-Iqna', al-Kamil and al-Talkhis have long been used in the chains and licences of the readers without objection from anyone, because the reading is a followed tradition. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 45}
