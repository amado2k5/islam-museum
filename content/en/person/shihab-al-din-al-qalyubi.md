## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ahmad ibn Salama, Abu al-Abbas, Shihab al-Din al-Qalyubi, was a jurist of literary taste from [[place/qalyub|Qalyub]] in Egypt. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1069 AH, 1659 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 91}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists glosses, commentaries and treatises by him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 91}. He wrote a book of biographies of a group of the Prophet's family (ahl al-bayt) called "Tuhfat al-Raghib", printed, and a printed "Tadhkirat al-Qalyubi", after which the entry has the word "tibb" (medicine) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 92}. He also wrote a treatise on the virtues of Mecca, Medina and Jerusalem with something of their history, in manuscript of seventy leaves; al-Zirikli says it is at Dar al-Kutub and may be "al-Nubdha al-Latifa fi bayan maqasid al-Hijaz wa-maalimih al-sharifa", held in the Kattani collection (no. 1411) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 92}. Further works are "Awraq Latifa", in manuscript, notes on al-Suyuti's "al-Jami al-Saghir" that distinguish the good, weak and sound reports in it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 92}, and "al-Hidaya min al-dalala fi ma'rifat al-waqt wa-l-qibla min ghayr ala", on finding the time and the qibla without an instrument, in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 92}.
