## Overview
Bukayr ibn Mahan, one of the propagandists of the Abbasid call. He organised it in Iraq together with Maysara al-Abdi and Abu Salama al-Khallal. Taqqush considers him the most important of the propagandists of Iraq. Abu Salama led the call in the last five years before the Abbasids took power. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22}
