## Overview
Bukayr ibn Mahan, one of the propagandists of the Abbasid call. He organised it in Iraq together with Maysara al-Abdi and Abu Salama al-Khallal. Taqqush considers him the most important of the propagandists of Iraq. Abu Salama led the call in the last five years before the Abbasids took power. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.22}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Bukayr ibn Mahan was a partisan serving with al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman, governor of Sind, and returned to Kufa with great wealth, all of which he spent on the cause. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 333} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 334} The imam Muhammad ibn Ali wrote appointing him in Maysara's place after Maysara died; he was known by his kunya Abu Hashim and was an eloquent man. When the imam's letters reached him he would wash them in water, knead flour with the washing water and feed it to his family. On his deathbed he appointed Abu Salama al-Khallal. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 334}
