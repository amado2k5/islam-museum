## Badr al-Din Ibn Khassbak al-Subki

Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Khassbak al-Subki, a Hanafi. He was connected to al-Zahir Baybars through the women of his family. He studied Hanafi law, excelled in it, and took from Akmal al-Din and others. He was skilled in debate, and was known for his piety, his manliness and his partisanship for his school and its people. He died on the fifth of Rajab, past the age of fifty {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 112}.
