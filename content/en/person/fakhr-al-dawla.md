## Overview
Fakhr al-Dawla, son of Rukn al-Dawla, a Buyid prince mentioned by al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.627}

## His life
His father gave him Hamadhan and al-Dinawar in 365 AH. He died in 387 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.627}

His son Rustam was set up in his place at al-Rayy and its territories when he was four years old, and al-Qadir gave him the title Majd al-Dawla. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.635}

Al-Dhahabi mentions that he was one of nine rulers who perished in a similar way in the years 387 and 388 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.635}
