## Overview
Abu Nasr Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Tarkhan al-Turki al-Farabi, the sage, who died in 339 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.290}

## His works
Among his works is [[work/ara-ahl-al-madina-al-fadila|The Views of the People of the Virtuous City]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.290}

## In the 'Al-Mubdi'un' programme
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents al-Farabi as 'the Second Teacher' after Aristotle — philosopher of the Arabs despite his Farabi Persian origin, his culture Arabic and his hundred-plus works all in Arabic; and a master musician besides {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:04:30}.
Of his life: born in Farab 260 AH amid political turmoil while the civilisational current flowed on; ascetic, aloof from strife; he studied grammar under Ibn al-Sarraj in Baghdad while teaching him logic, and philosophy under Yuhanna ibn Khaylan in Harran, then surpassed his teachers {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:07:00}.
How he entered philosophy: a student deposited Aristotle's books with him as a trust, letting him read them — he threw himself upon them and mastered them alone {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:08:00}.
Of his testimonies: Ibn Sina's 'I journeyed seeking Abu Nasr and never found him' and 'I read the Metaphysics forty times without understanding it until I read al-Farabi's writings'; and 'but for al-Farabi Aristotle would have been lost' — his lost works recovered through al-Farabi's commentaries; and the orientalist Diago calling him 'of greater allure than Ibn Sina, his soul richer in flow' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:11:30}.
Of his asceticism: he refused Sayf al-Dawla al-Hamdani's lavish gifts, content with four dirhams a day for necessity, spending most days alone composing in gardens without books, teaching the hardest questions — while counselling 'do not take my life as your model' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:14:30}.
Of his philosophy: logical sequence and firm interconnection; in Ara' Ahl al-Madina al-Fadila he treats the First Existent negating partner and opposite in a manner consonant with the faith in its fundamentals — with caveats on some creedal points (the speaker's note) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:24:00}.
His analysis of the virtuous city: man's need for man and cooperation, sixteen qualities of its leader, and its opposites — cities of license, appetites, materialism {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:23:00}.
His reconciling project: reconciling Plato and Aristotle as differing only superficially while one in essence — so that religion might face a single philosophy, proving no contradiction between revelation and reason {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:19:30}.
Of his works: al-Jam' bayna Ra'yay al-Hakimayn, Ara' Ahl al-Madina al-Fadila, Kitab al-Musiqi al-Kabir, Ihsa' al-'Ulum, and a treatise on the rules of poetry — forty surviving of over a hundred {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:22:00}.
He died in Damascus in 339 AH (the recording says 950 by error — corrected to 339 AH) after a life of austerity focused on a single field {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep26 00:24:00}.
