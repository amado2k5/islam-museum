## Overview
Thabit ibn Qurra al-Harrani, of the Sabians. His name in the Fihrist is Abu al-Hasan Thabit ibn Qurra ibn Marwan. He was born in the year 221 AH and died in 288 AH, aged seventy-seven solar years {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.380}.

## His career
He was a money-changer in Harran. Muhammad ibn Musa took him along when he saw that he was eloquent, then connected him with al-Mu'tadid and brought him in among the astrologers. Ibn al-Nadim says that the origin of the leadership of the Sabians at the courts of the caliphs was Thabit ibn Qurra {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.380}.

Ibn al-Nadim also reports that the Banu al-Munajjim used to pay him, together with [[person/hunayn-ibn-ishaq|Hunayn ibn Ishaq]], about five hundred dinars a month for translation and attendance {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.340}.

al-Mas'udi reports that when [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]], in the year 289 AH, was pursuing Wasif the servant, he came to the temple of Antioch, venerated it, and spoke of its significance {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.542}.
