## Overview
Muhammad ibn Aslam ibn Salim ibn Yazid, Abu al-Hasan al-Kindi by clientage, al-Tusi, was one of the hadith memorizers. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 242 AH, 856 CE, and prints a zero for the year of his birth, meaning none is known to him {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 34}.

## Life
He was known for his piety, and al-Zirikli reports that al-Dhahabi called him the shaykh of the East {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 34}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists his al-Musnad; al-Radd ala al-Jahmiyya; al-Iman wa-l-A'mal, a refutation of the Karramiyya running to more than two parts; and an Arba'un Hadithan {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 34}.
