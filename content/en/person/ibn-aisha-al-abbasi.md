## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ibrahim al-Imam, of the descendants of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib. Al-Mas'udi describes him as a brother of al-Saffah and al-Mansur. Al-Ma'mun rode to him at night to the al-Mutbaq prison and killed him in 209 AH; Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Afriqi was killed with him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.428}

## Crucifixion
Al-Mas'udi says he was the first Abbasid to be crucified in Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.428}

## What is not stated here
His lineage as given in the report needs an additional source, since the wording calls him a brother of al-Saffah and al-Mansur while also placing him among the descendants of Imam Ibrahim.
