## Summary
Al-Tughra'i, head of the diwan al-insha' for Sultan Muhammad b. Malikshah, then attached to his son Mas'ud; taken captive, he was beheaded before King Mahmud in Rabi' I 514, aged over sixty. One of the singular men of the age and bearer of the banner of verse and prose, he authored the *Lamiyyat al-'ajam* and left a fine diwan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 521-522}
