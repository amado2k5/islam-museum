## Overview
Al-Harith ibn al-Nu'man ibn Usaf ibn Nadla ibn Abd ibn Awf ibn Ghanm, of the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar, of the [[group/ansar|Ansar]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Martyrdom
A Companion who was martyred at the day of [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta]] in Jumada al-Ula 8 AH according to the data (about September 629 CE; the date is approximate). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} See [[event/battle-of-mutah|the battle of Mu'ta]] and [[place/mutah|Mu'ta]].

Ibn Ishaq records his full lineage; his report is in Ibn Hisham (vol. 4, p. 30). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
