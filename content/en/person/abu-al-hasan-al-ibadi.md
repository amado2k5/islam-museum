## Summary
Ali ibn Abi Asim Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Abbad al-Ibadi al-Harawi, Abu al-Hasan, a Shafi'i jurist counted among the leading scholars of Khurasan, and the son of Imam Abi Asim al-Ibadi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16242} His works include Kitab al-Raqm on fiqh. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16242} The encyclopedia dates him 415–495 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16242}
