## Summary
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Dulaf was a commander of the Banu Abi Dulaf in the Jibal region. Al-Tabari records him under the events of 253 AH, when a battle outside Hamadhan against the commander Muflih ended in his defeat and the loss of al-Kurj to the Banu Abi Dulaf. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.373}

## Details
Al-Tabari relates that in 253 AH al-Mu'tazz invested Musa ibn Bugha the Elder with command over al-Jabal, with Muflih on his vanguard. Muflih engaged Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Dulaf eight nights before the end of Rajab; Abd al-Aziz had some twenty thousand vagabonds and others with him. The battle took place about a mile outside Hamadhan: Muflih routed him, pursued his men for three parasangs killing and capturing, then returned safely and wrote announcing the victory. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.373}

In Ramadan of the same year Muflih marshalled his cavalry toward al-Kurj and set two ambushes; finding Abd al-Aziz's force of four thousand, he fought them, and his ambush party fell upon Abd al-Aziz's men, who broke. Abd al-Aziz abandoned al-Kurj and fortified himself in a fortress of his there, while Muflih entered the district, took a group of the Banu Abi Dulaf captive — among the women one said to be Abd al-Aziz's mother — and seventy loads of heads were sent to Samarra. Musa ibn Bugha himself then marched from Samarra and occupied Hamadhan. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.373}
