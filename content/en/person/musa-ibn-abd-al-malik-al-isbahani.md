## Summary
Abu 'Imran Musa ibn 'Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham al-Isbahani, head of the kharaj bureau, was among the greatest chiefs and the most accomplished and eminent secretaries. He moved through the services under a succession of caliphs, and the Sawad bureau and others were his under al-Mutawakkil; he was a letter-writer with diwans of epistles and poems. Among his stories is that of his slave-girl who sang his verses when they reached al-Qadisiyya in the pilgrim caravan, then vanished — no trace of her was felt, and he remained remembering her and grieving for her. He died in Shawwal 246. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
