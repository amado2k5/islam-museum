## Summary
Mahmud ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn Umar ibn Maza al-Marghinani al-Bukhari, Burhan al-Din — a Hanafi jurist among the chief imams of the madhhab, counted by Ibn Kamal Pasha among the mujtahids in the questions, from a great house of learning in his country. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16250} He was born in Marghinan and died in Bukhara (the scan reads 'bi-Bukhari'), studied under his father al-Sadr al-Sa'id and his uncle al-Sadr al-Shahid Umar, and was the father of Sadr al-Islam Tahir ibn Mahmud. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16250} His works include al-Muhit al-Burhani fi al-Fiqh al-Nu'mani, al-Dhakhira, al-Tajrid, Tatimmat al-Fatawa, a commentary on al-Khassaf's Adab al-Qada, a commentary on al-Jami' al-Saghir, and al-Tariqa al-Burhaniyya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16250} The encyclopedia dates him 551–616 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16250}
