## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ismail ibn Salah al-Kahlani then al-San'ani, Abu Ibrahim, known as al-Amir, a mujtahid titled al-Mu'ayyad billah. He studied with Zayd ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, Salah ibn al-Husayn al-Akhfash, Abd Allah ibn Ali al-Wazir and others, and wrote Subul al-Salam, a commentary on Bulugh al-Maram. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3134}

## Dates
The entry heading gives 1182 AH as the death year and 1099 AH as the birth year, as printed; these are not yet checked against a second source. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3134}
