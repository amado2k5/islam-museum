## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ismail ibn Salah al-Kahlani then al-San'ani, Abu Ibrahim, known as al-Amir, a mujtahid titled al-Mu'ayyad billah. He studied with Zayd ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, Salah ibn al-Husayn al-Akhfash, Abd Allah ibn Ali al-Wazir and others, and wrote Subul al-Salam, a commentary on Bulugh al-Maram. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3134}

## Dates
The entry heading gives 1182 AH as the death year and 1099 AH as the birth year, as printed; these are not yet checked against a second source. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3134}

## From the biography by the editor of Subul al-Salam
Muhammad Subhi Hallaq gives a life of al-San'ani in his introduction to [[work/subul-al-salam|Subul al-Salam]]: {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 21}
- **Lineage and birth:** he traces his descent to al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. Abi Talib and says the family is known as the Amir family. He was born at Kahlan — hence "al-Kahlani" — on the eve of Friday in mid-Jumada al-Akhira 1099 AH. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 21-22}
- **Upbringing:** he quotes al-Shawkani that his father moved the family to [[place/sanaa|San'a]] in 1107 AH, when he was eight, and he grew up there. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 22}
- **Teachers:** al-Shawkani names four in San'a — Zayd b. Muhammad b. al-Hasan, Salah b. al-Husayn al-Akhfash, 'Abd Allah b. 'Ali al-Wazir and the judge 'Ali b. Muhammad al-'Ansi; others add Hashim b. Yahya al-Shami and 'Abd al-Khaliq b. al-Zayn al-Zajaji, the Hanafi of Zabid; he travelled to Mecca and Medina and met 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi al-Ghayth, preacher of the Prophet's Mosque, Tahir b. Ibrahim al-Kurdi and Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Hadi al-Sindi, and among his teachers in the two sanctuaries was Salim b. 'Abd Allah al-Basri. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 22-23}
- **Students:** among them 'Abd al-Qadir b. Ahmad, the judge Ahmad b. Muhammad Qatin, Ahmad b. Salih b. Abi al-Rijal, and his sons Ibrahim, 'Abd Allah and al-Qasim. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 23-25}
- **Al-Shawkani's praise:** "the great imam, the absolute mujtahid, author of the works"; he excelled in all the sciences, held sole leadership in learning in San'a, openly practised ijtihad, acted on the evidence and turned people away from taqlid — "one of the imams who renewed the landmarks of the religion". {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 25-26}
- **Death:** he died in San'a on Tuesday 3 Sha'ban 1182 AH and was buried west of the minaret of the Madrasa mosque in upper San'a, aged eighty-three. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 26}

## His stance on taqlid (his opinion)
- **Al-San'ani's view (opinion):** in verses the editor quotes, al-San'ani complains that one who follows the evidence is attacked from both sides — accused of nasb by the Rafida and of rafd by the Nawasib — his only fault being that he follows God's word and the Prophet's; he says the four imams are beyond doubt in merit, but their words are not evidence, and they themselves said their views should be rejected when they contradict the text. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 28-29}
- **Al-San'ani's view (opinion):** in *Minhat al-Ghaffar* he holds that adherence to a school is the origin of division among Muslims — the cause of separate prayers in the Sacred Mosque, of bloodshed and of Muslims declaring one another unbelievers — and that God made obedience in everything due to no specific person but His Messenger. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 29}

The editor gives a numbered list of his works, among them *Irshad al-Nuqqad ila Taysir al-Ijtihad*, *Isbal al-Matar bi-Sharh Nazm Nukhbat al-Fikar*, *Iqamat al-Dalil 'ala Da'f Adillat al-Takfir bi-al-Ta'wil*, *al-Insaf fi Haqiqat al-Awliya'* and *Iqaz al-Fikra li-Muraja'at al-Fitra*, which his son says was his first work. {cite:subul-salam-hallaq-ibn-al-jawzi-v1 p. 31-32}
