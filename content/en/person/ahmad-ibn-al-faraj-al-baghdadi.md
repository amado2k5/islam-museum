## Summary
A Hanbali jurist and judge, Abu al-Abbas, of Baghdad; he held the judgeship of Dujayl for a time, studied fiqh under Abd al-Wahid ibn Sayf, read the Qur'an in its transmissions, heard from Abu Mansur al-Khazin and Abu Ghalib al-Qazzaz, and was transmitted by Ibn al-Sam'ani. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7831}
