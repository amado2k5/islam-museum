## Summary
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Qurashi, known as "Ibn al-Ukhuwwa," author of [[work/maalim-al-qurba-fi-ahkam-al-hisba|Maʿalim al-Qurba fi Ahkam al-Hisba]] on [[concept/hisba|hisba]]. The two editors of the book state that he died in 729 AH {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.2} {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.6}.

## Note
His date of death needs a second source before it can be relied on.
