## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'id ibn Ma'n al-Qurayzi al-Lahji al-Yamani, known as Ibn Ma'n. A Shafi'i jurist and hadith scholar who held the judgeship in Aden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14948} His works include al-Mustafa fi dhikr sunan al-Mustafa and an abridgement of Ihya' ulum al-din. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14948}
