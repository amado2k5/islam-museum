## Overview
Muzahim ibn Khaqan ibn Urtuj (or Artuj), Abu al-Fawaris, was a commander and one of the Abbasid governors, Turkic by origin and raised in Baghdad. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 254 AH, 868 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 211}.

## Governor of Egypt
The Abbasid caliph al-Mu'tazz sent him from Iraq with a large army in 252 AH to put down a revolt that had broken out in Alexandria against the emir of Egypt, Yazid ibn Abd Allah; he arrived and crushed the revolt. Al-Mu'tazz then gave him the command of Egypt in 253, and strife followed one episode after another in his days. Al-Zirikli describes him as severe and firm; he put an end to many innovations and punished those who practised them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 211}.

## Death
He died in Egypt while still in office {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 211}.
