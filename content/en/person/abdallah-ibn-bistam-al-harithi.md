## Overview
Of Banu Rabi'a, he met al-Hadi at Yulis in 294 AH; with Wada'a he ordered the raid on Raha, and later brought the Hamasiyin seeking safety, whom al-Hadi granted safety.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi met Abdallah ibn Bistam al-Harithi of Banu Rabi'a at a place called Yulis, and that with Wada'a he ordered a band to raid Raha and fight its people. Later he brought the Hamasiyin to al-Hadi seeking safety, and al-Hadi granted it. The nisba al-Harithi is as printed. The raid is described by the narrator, who attributes the plan to Ibn Bistam and the Wada'a together.
