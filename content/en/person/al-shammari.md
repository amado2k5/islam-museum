## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah ibn Ibrahim ibn Sayf ibn Abd Allah al-Mashriqi al-Madani al-Shammari, known as al-Faridi, was a scholar of the law of inheritance, a Hanbali of the town of al-Majma'a in the Sudayr district of Najd, of the Shammar tribe. He was born and died at Medina in 1189 AH (1775 CE) and left descendants there; its people called him al-Mashriqi and later al-Faridi. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.49}

He wrote al-'Adhab al-Fa'id, a two-part commentary on the Alfiyya of inheritance, which is Salih ibn Hasan al-Bahuti's versification 'Umdat Kull Farid. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.49}
