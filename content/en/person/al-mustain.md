## Overview
Al-Musta'in billah, Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn al-Mu'tasim, brother of al-Mutawakkil, an Abbasid caliph. His mother was Makhariq. He was deposed and killed in 252 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.348} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.555-556}

## The allegiance
According to al-Suyuti, when al-Muntasir died the commanders gathered and said that if they appointed anyone from the sons of al-Mutawakkil none of them would remain. They gave the allegiance to Ahmad, who was twenty-eight years old. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.555-556}

## The deposition
Al-Tabari says that Ibn Tahir intended to depose him in 251 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.337} Al-Suyuti relates that the Turks turned against him after the killing of Wasif and Bugha, then he grew afraid and went down to Baghdad. They brought out al-Mu'tazz and gave him the allegiance, and al-Mu'tazz sent an army to fight him. The battles continued for months, killing grew and prices rose. He deposed himself at the beginning of 252 AH, and was taken down to Wasit and held there for nine months. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.555-556}

Al-Mas'udi says that he was killed near al-Qatul on Wednesday, six nights into Shawwal 252 AH, at the age of thirty-five. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.545-548}

## Reports about him
Al-Mas'udi relates that he had a good knowledge of the days and reports of the people. Al-Suyuti and al-Mas'udi state that he was the first to introduce wearing wide sleeves. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.536-538} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.554}
