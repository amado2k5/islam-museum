## Overview
Abu al-Abbas Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Imad al-Aqfahsi al-Qahiri al-Shafi'i, known as al-Imad, a Shafi'i jurist of Cairo. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia gives his death as 808 AH (1405 CE). The sources did not record his birth date precisely. His works and teachers need an additional source.
