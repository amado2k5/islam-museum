## Overview
Malik ibn al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud al-Abdi, who was in charge of the affairs (ahdath) of Basra, that is, its police, in the days of Khalid ibn Abdallah al-Qasri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The letter
Khalid wrote to him that he should leave Bashir ibn Ubayd Allah free to act against al-Mirghab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
