## Overview
Na'ila bint Quraysh ibn Wakf, wife of Amr ibn Sa'id al-Ashdaq. A report is told of her warning her husband. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.96}

## The warning
al-Mas'udi relates that Amr stumbled on the carpet as he was going out to Abd al-Malik. She begged him in God's name not to go to him. He said to her: "Leave me, for by God, if I were asleep, he would not wake me." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.96}
