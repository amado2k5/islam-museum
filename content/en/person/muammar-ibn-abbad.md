## Overview
Mu'ammar ibn Abbad al-Sulami, one of the most important men of the Basra branch of the Mu'tazila, together with Abu Bakr al-Asamm and al-Nazzam. {cite:duha-islam p.760}

## The scholars' choice
Ahmad Amin says that the choice of him by the scholars came in the context of the scholarly dispute. {cite:duha-islam p.320}

What is not stated here: his death date and his opinions, which need an additional source.
