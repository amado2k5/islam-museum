## Overview
Yazid ibn Huzayl Kull al-Azzi. Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim sent him to fight [[person/ziyad-al-qasir|Ziyad al-Qasir al-Khuza'i]]. He was struck, sought an opening, and was hit on the head; he lived seven days and then died. As for Yazid, he threw himself into the river of Saghaniyan and escaped {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
