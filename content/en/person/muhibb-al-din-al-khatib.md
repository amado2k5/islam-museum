## Overview
Muhibb al-Din ibn Muhammad al-Khatib, an encyclopaedic writer, journalist, historian, political activist and social theorist. He was born in 1886 CE (1303 AH) and died in 1970 CE (1389 AH). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
