## Summary
Amir Rukn al-Din Mankubars, mamluk of Falak al-Din, brother of al-'Adil; religious, righteous and chaste, constant at the Umayyad mosque; he has a madrasa and turbah at Qasyun on which he endowed much, and inside Damascus a great Shafi'i madrasa and the village of Jarud endowed for them; he died at Jarud and was carried to burial in his turbah at Qasyun. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 147}
