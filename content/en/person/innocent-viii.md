## Summary
Innocent VIII, pope in the reign of [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.

## Buying the hostage
Al-Salabi records that the [[group/knights-of-st-john|Knights of St John]] at Rhodes — having turned Prince [[person/jem-sultan|Cem]] into privileges extracted from both Bayezid and Cem's Cairo backers — sold their hostage to Pope Innocent VIII for a great price {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.
