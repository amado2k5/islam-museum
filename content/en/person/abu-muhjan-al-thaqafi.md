## Overview
Abu Muhjan ibn Habib al-Thaqafi, whom al-Baladhuri mentions in "Futuh al-Buldan" in his account of the Battle of the Bridge. He charged on the elephant and struck its leg, after Abu Ubayd had struck its trunk. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He also has verses lamenting his companions who fell at the Battle of the Bridge. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
