## Summary
Rifa'a ibn Malik ibn al-Ajlan al-Ansari al-Zuraqi, Abu Mu'adh, a Companion who witnessed the second pledge at Aqaba and the later battles. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8514}
He narrated from the Prophet, Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and Ubada ibn al-Samit. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8514}

## What the scholars said of him
Ibn Hajar says his father was the first of the Ansar to embrace Islam, and Ibn Abd al-Barr says Rifa'a fought with Ali at the Camel and at Siffin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8514}
