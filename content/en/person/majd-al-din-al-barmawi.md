## Summary
Majd al-Din Isma'il al-Barmawi the Egyptian Shafi'i; born about 750; he entered Cairo early, took from the shaykhs and heard, mastered fiqh and the arts, took up teaching and preached at the 'Amr mosque in Egypt; he died mid-Rabi' II 834 — a man of merit and knowledge who lectured on adab and other fields. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 208}
