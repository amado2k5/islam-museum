## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Ja'far, Abu Abd Allah al-Balali al-Ajluni then al-Qahiri al-Shafi'i, Shams al-Din, a hadith scholar and jurist. He studied with Abu Bakr al-Mawsili, read Ihya' Ulum al-Din closely and abridged it, and wrote al-Sawl fi shay' min ahadith al-Rasul and abridgements of al-Rawda and al-Shifa'. Dates 750 to 820 H as printed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13346}
