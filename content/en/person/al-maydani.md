## Overview
Abu'l-Fadl Ahmad b. Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Ibrahim al-Maydani of Nishapur, the littérateur; singled out for the company of Abu'l-Hasan al-Wahidi, author of the Tafsir — he mastered the art of Arabic, especially lexicon and the Arabs' proverbs; among his works the Kitab al-amthal named for him — nothing like it was ever made in its field — and al-Sami fi'l-asami; his son Abu Sa'd Sa'id b. Ahmad was likewise excellent, author of al-Asma fi'l-asma'; al-Maydani died in 539. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
