# Dunia Shuaib

## Summary
Dunia Shuaib is an American educator, lecturer and author whose work brings Islamic teaching together with modern psychology. She is the founder and executive director of the Guided Hearts Institute, an educational organisation offering Islamically integrated psycho-spiritual education. {cite:guidedhearts-dunia-shuaib} {cite:listennotes-dunia-shuaib}

## Guided Hearts Institute
The institute's own website states that it was founded by Ustadha Dunia Shuaib after her travels delivering educational programmes showed her a growing need for psychoeducation grounded in Islamic spirituality, and it presents her as the institute's founder and executive director. The institute develops Islamically integrated approaches to mental wellness and relationships, delivered through courses, workshops and events in the United States and abroad. {cite:guidedhearts-dunia-shuaib}

## Lectures
Her Muslim Central lecture feed carries sixty episodes recorded since March 2020, including convention talks on anxiety, depression, happiness and relationships, and the biography published with the feed describes her as an educator, internationally recognised lecturer and author. {cite:listennotes-dunia-shuaib}
