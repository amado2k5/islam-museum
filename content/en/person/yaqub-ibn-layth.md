## Overview
Ya'qub ibn al-Layth, mentioned by al-Tabari, al-Mas'udi, Ibn al-Jawzi and Ibn Khaldun, and lord of the [[polity/saffarid-dynasty|Saffarid state]] {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## Rise
al-Tabari reports that he seized Kirman in the year 255 AH, then entered Fars {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.382} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.384}. Ibn Khaldun reports that he kept company with Salih ibn Nasr al-Mutatawwi'i in Sistan to fight the Kharijites, then wrote to al-Mu'tazz asking for its governorship, and conquered Zabulistan {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1108-1111}.

## Toward Iraq
al-Tabari reports a battle between him and al-Hasan ibn Zayd al-Talibi in the year 260 AH, in which he defeated him {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.508}. In the year 262 AH he marched toward Iraq, and Abu Ahmad al-Muwaffaq met him at Sib Bani Kuma and defeated him, as al-Mas'udi reports {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 572-573}.

## His army
al-Mas'udi describes his asceticism in his army: he would sit only on a piece of woollen cloth, lie down on his shield, and make his banner his pillow {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 575-576}.

## Death
Ibn al-Jawzi reports that he died at al-Ahwaz in the year 265 AH. His coffin was carried to Jundaysabur, and on his grave was written "This is the grave of Ya'qub the poor" {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p. 70}.
