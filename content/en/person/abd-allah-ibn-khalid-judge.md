## Summary
Abd Allah ibn Khalid had great worship and scrupulousness, and was compelled to be judge of the city. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 669, sequence 670, on page 392. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}

## His teachers
He met Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Shu'ayb ibn Harb and Ibrahim ibn Bakr al-Shaybani. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}

## The basket and his judgement
Abu Abd Allah al-Sulami the jurist relates from Yahya ibn Mutarrif that Abd Allah ibn Khalid once passed on his way to the court with his basket on the neck of his slave. A man's load fell from his donkey, and he asked for help. Abd Allah told his slave to put the basket down, put his cloak on his head, carried the load with his slave to the man's neighbour's house, then put on his cloak and went to the court. In the city he sat for judgement and gave a ruling; the man condemned by it began to speak, and the rest of the phrase is garbled in the scan and is not given here. The report continues that the judge then put his hand on his head and struck it, and the condemned man fled; the judge left his basket and register and fled too; he was afterwards heard of only as a guard on the frontier for a day. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/392}
