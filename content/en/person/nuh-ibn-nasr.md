## Overview
Nuh ibn Nasr al-Samani. According to Ibn al-Nadim's "al-Fihrist", his father Nasr ibn Ahmad had died after entering the mission of al-Nasafi. Nuh gathered the jurists and brought al-Nasafi, and they debated with him. Then al-Nasafi and the chiefs of the missionaries and notables were killed, among them some of Nasr's commanders who had entered the mission {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.266}.

The years of his birth and death are not recorded in the museum's data; an additional source is needed.
