## Overview
Shaykh 'Amir b. Muhammad, sultan of Yemen; news of his death reached Damascus on the same day as Sultan Bayezid II's and Qaytbay b. Barakat sharif of Mecca's — Sunday the eighteenth of Rabi' I 918 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 485}.
