## Overview
Abu 'Abd Allah Ibn al-Hassas al-Jawhari; he was arrested in Jumada I 302 and stripped of what was reckoned at sixteen million dinars in cash, notes, vessels, clothing, horses and servants {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 419}.
