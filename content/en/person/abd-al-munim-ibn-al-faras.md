## Summary
A Maliki jurist, traditionist, grammarian and lexicographer of al-Andalus; he was judge at Jazirat Shuqr, then at Wadi Ash, Jaen and finally Granada, with oversight of the hisba and police; Abu al-Rabi' ibn Salim reports that Ibn al-Jadd knew no one in al-Andalus more retentive of Malik\'s school after Ibn Zarqun. Works include Ahkam al-Qur'an, Adab al-Qada' and disputed questions in grammar. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7831}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7832}
