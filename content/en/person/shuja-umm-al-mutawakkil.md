## Overview
Shuja', mother of the caliph [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]]. She died in Rabi' al-Akhir of the year 247 AH, and her son al-Muntasir led the funeral prayer over her. al-Mas'udi reports that al-Mutawakkil was killed six months after her death {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.504}.
