## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Tamimi, Abu Ali, known as Ibn al-Mudhhib, was the transmitter of Imam Ahmad's Musnad and a preacher among the scholars of Baghdad. He died in 444 AH (1052 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.201}

Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi said he transmitted the whole Musnad of Imam Ahmad from al-Qati'i and that his hearing was sound except in portions where he attached his own audition; Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani held that he was evidently imprecise, as was his shaykh Ibn Malik, whence unsound texts and isnads entered the Musnad. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.201}

## In Fada'il al-Sahaba
The editor of [[work/fadail-al-sahaba-ahmad|Fada'il al-Sahaba]] identifies him as al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Tamimi, Abu Ali, born in 355 AH, who transmitted from [[person/al-qatiqi|al-Qati'i]] Ahmad's *al-Zuhd* and *Fada'il al-Sahaba*, and from whom a group of trustworthy men transmitted {cite:fadail-sahaba-ahmad-wasi-allah-1999 p.35}. He quotes Ibn Kathir as calling him pious and good, and Ibn Hajar as saying that he was apparently a shaykh without precision, and gives his death as 444 {cite:fadail-sahaba-ahmad-wasi-allah-1999 p.35-36}. A margin note in the book's unique manuscript at hadith no. 1336, "this hadith is not in Ibn al-Mudhhib's copy", shows the editor that the manuscript was collated with his copy {cite:fadail-sahaba-ahmad-wasi-allah-1999 p.35-36}. The title page states that Ibn Nasir transmitted the merits of al-Hasan, al-Husayn, Fatima and Khadija from Abu Muhammad al-Jawhari and Abu Ali Ibn al-Mudhhib together, from Ibn Malik al-Qati'i {cite:fadail-sahaba-ahmad-wasi-allah-1999 p.54}. Al-Dhahabi says in *Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'* that he heard from al-Qati'i the *Musnad*, *al-Zuhd* and *Fada'il al-Sahaba*, as the editor reports {cite:fadail-sahaba-ahmad-wasi-allah-1999 p.43}.
