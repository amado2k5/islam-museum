## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Tamimi, Abu Ali, known as Ibn al-Mudhhib, was the transmitter of Imam Ahmad's Musnad and a preacher among the scholars of Baghdad. He died in 444 AH (1052 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.201}

Al-Khatib al-Baghdadi said he transmitted the whole Musnad of Imam Ahmad from al-Qati'i and that his hearing was sound except in portions where he attached his own audition; Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani held that he was evidently imprecise, as was his shaykh Ibn Malik, whence unsound texts and isnads entered the Musnad. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.201}
