## Summary
Al-Mikhnaf ibn Sulaym al-Azdi al-Ghamidi, a Companion of the Azd of Kufa and Basra, with a hadith in the four Sunan collections via Abdullah ibn Awn from Amir ibn Abi Ramla: "We were standing with the Messenger of God at Arafat when he said…" — the hadith of the sacrifice and the 'atira {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1381}.

## In al-Isaba
Ibn al-Kalbi places him among the Azd of Kufa and Basra and notes that his descendants include the famous akhbari Abu Mikhnaf Lut ibn Yahya ibn Sa'id ibn Mikhnaf ibn Sulaym {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1381}. Al-Baghawi counts him a Companion whose hadith is in the four Sunan; al-Tirmidhi calls the Arafat hadith gharib, and al-Askari reports that his name was written with a doubled khaf while the ungeminated form was preferred {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1381}.

## For young visitors
Al-Mikhnaf ibn Sulaym was a Companion of the Azd who transmitted one well-known hadith about the sacrifice, and is the ancestor of the historian Abu Mikhnaf Lut ibn Yahya.
