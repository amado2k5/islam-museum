## Summary
Of Banu Sahm, wife of al-Muttalib ibn Azhar al-Zuhri; she emigrated to Abyssinia and bore there her son Abdullah. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her wife of al-Muttalib ibn Azhar al-Zuhri, tracing her to Abu Awf ibn Dubayra ibn Sa'id ibn Sa'd ibn Sahm. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} He counts her among the emigrant women to Abyssinia of Banu Sahm. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/11}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- Her lineage is Sahmi and her husband Zuhri; she appears in the women's list under Banu Sahm (p. 11).
