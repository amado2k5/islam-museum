## Overview
An Alid summoner, of the descendants of al-Hasan ibn Ali, whom Asfar killed in Tabaristan. {cite:muruj-mari-2005 juz' 4 p.125} See [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]].

## Ibn al-Athir's account
In Ibn al-Athir he took al-Rayy and drove out the followers of al-Sa'id Nasr ibn Ahmad, and seized Qazvin, Zanjan, Abhar and Qum. With him was Makan ibn Kaki al-Daylami. He met Asfar ibn Shiruya at Sariya and was defeated, then he fled and was killed in 316 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.139-140}

He used to order his Daylami companions to be upright, and forbade them to wrong the subjects and drink wine, so they hated him for it. They agreed to summon Harusandan to hand him over, and to set up Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Atrush in his place. Ahmad al-Tawil at Damghan uncovered their plot and wrote to the summoner to inform him, and he took his precautions. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.139-140}
