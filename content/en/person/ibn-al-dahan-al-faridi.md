## Summary
Abu Shuja' Muhammad b. 'Ali b. Shu'ayb, known as Ibn al-Dahan, styled Fakhr al-Din — the Baghdadi faraid-master, reckoner and adib; he moved from Baghdad to Mosul, kept company with the vizier Jamal al-Din al-Isfahani, then entered Sultan Salah al-Din's service who gave him the diwan of Mayyafariqin; he entered Damascus, travelled to Egypt in 586, returned and made Damascus home. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Works
He has the setting-out of the faraid tables, a delicate *Gharib al-hadith* in sixteen volumes, and a gathered history; his fine poetry was praised by Ibn al-Mustawfi in the history of Irbil and by al-'Imad al-Katib in the Kharida. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
