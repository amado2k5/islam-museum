## Summary
Ousama Iyad Alshurafa is a Palestinian-American author, speaker and spiritual mentor based in Canada, known for work on life's purpose and the hereafter; he founded the Muslim Hub Club platform, authored The Afterlife Manual, and his video series count views past the billion mark with followers in the millions. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## Formation
A Palestinian raised in the West, he describes a formation of twenty-five years in da'wah before the camera ever turned on. He holds a bachelor's in biomedical and electrical engineering, a Master of Liberal Arts from Harvard University with focused study in business, finance and leadership, and an ijazah in the Hafs 'an 'Asim recitation, while pursuing advanced studies in theology and tafsir. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## From engineering to da'wah
His path joins a rigorous technical formation — biomedical and electrical engineering — to a humanities formation at Harvard and an ijazah in the Hafs recitation, visible in his style: metaphors drawn from technology and economics to explain rizq and certainty to the Western professional Muslim. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa}

## Platforms and works
He founded Muslim Hub Club, a global platform serving over 150,000 members, authored The Afterlife Manual: Every Muslim's Guide to Happily Ever Hereafter, and produced series including Dunya Decoded and The Rizq Framework, using metaphors like the "Divine Matrix" to treat contemporary questions of certainty and trust in God. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## Method
His discourse centres on moving the heart from the creation to the Creator and from the means to the Causer: taking worldly means without worshipping them in the heart, and measuring success by obedience rather than outcome — a homiletic register addressed to questioning young Muslims in the West. {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## The Humaniti Foundation
He is a Global Ambassador for the Humaniti Foundation, championing the conviction that helping people around the world lies at the heart of faith and purpose — so that in one person the inward da'wah to the community joins the wider humanitarian calling. {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## Legacy
He is read in the generation of digital spiritual mentors: book, series, podcast and private community combining classical heritage with modern production tools, and he was listed in The Muslim 500 among Preachers & Spiritual Guides in North America for 2025 and 2026 per his site. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}
## Book and series
He published The Afterlife Manual: Every Muslim's Guide to Happily Ever Hereafter, a practical book on life's meaning, death and the hereafter in a register aimed at the contemporary reader, and widened his reach with the Dunya Decoded series — which per his description decodes the illusion of the world — and The Rizq Framework, where faith meets finance: works addressed to the young professional Muslim. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}

## A structured community
He founded Muslim Hub Club, a global platform he describes as serving over 150,000 members committed to spiritual growth and ethical living, and commands a wider community his site counts in the millions across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube — so that his da'wah runs as a connected network between book, series and community. {cite:muslim500-ousama-alshurafa} {cite:ousama-alshurafa-site}
