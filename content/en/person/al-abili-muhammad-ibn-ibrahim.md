## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Abili, a Maghribi scholar of logic and the rational sciences. Ibn Khaldun attended him in Tunis and studied under him. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.22}

## Dates
He was born in 681 AH (1282 CE) and died in 757 AH (1356 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.22}
