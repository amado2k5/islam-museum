## Overview
Abu Nasr al-Fath ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Qaysi al-Ishbili, a man of letters from Seville, author of *Qala'id al-Iqyan* (The Necklaces of Gold) and *Matmah al-Anfus* (The Aim of Souls). He was born in 480 AH (1087 CE) and died in 528 AH (1133 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
