## Overview
Jafna ibn [[person/amr-ibn-amir-muzayqiya|Amr Muzayqiya]], of the [[group/al-azd|Azd]]. He went to [[place/al-sham|Syria]] in the migration of the Azd and settled there with his sons, and he is the father of the kings of the [[group/ghassan|Ghassanids]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} {cite:qalb-jazirat-al-arab p.231-235}

## The name Ghassan
The Ghassanids are named after a water in the Hijaz known as Ghassan, where they first settled before moving to Syria. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.17} In the accounts of kingship in Syria, the house of Ghassan overcame the Dajaʿima over what they held, and the Romans then appointed them kings over the Arabs of Syria (see [[polity/ghassanid-kingdom|the Ghassanid kingdom]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.27}

## On lineage
The author counts the sons of Jafna among the kings of Syria in the clans of [[group/kahlan|Kahlan]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.16} Elsewhere he relays the view of Sulayman al-Nadwi that the kings of the house of Ghassan were from the house of [[person/nabit|Nabit ibn Isma'il]], and not from Qahtan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20}

## For young visitors
Jafna settled in the lands of Syria with his sons, and his children became kings there, known as the Ghassanids.
