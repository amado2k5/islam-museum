## Overview
Qutb al-Din Aybak, a mamluk of Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri and his deputy in the lands of India. He held the iqta of the emirs of Ghazna. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.303}

When his master was killed in 602 AH, the vizier Mu'ayyad al-Mulk ordered everyone who held an iqta from him to return it, and they distributed money among them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.303}
