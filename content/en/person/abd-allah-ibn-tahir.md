## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] was one of the commanders of the Abbasid state in the time of al-Mu'tasim, and had a role in bringing down al-Mazyar, lord of Tabaristan, using correspondence and promises until he delivered to the caliph the letters taken from al-Afshin. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part3 scan 25-26}

## The plan to seize al-Mazyar
Al-Tabari says that Abd Allah ibn Tahir agreed with al-Mazyar's cousin to hand the mountains over to al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn and those with him, and wrote him a letter to that effect, and they besieged al-Mazyar in his palace until they brought him down under the judgement of al-Mu'tasim. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part3 scan 25}

## The letters
Abd Allah ibn Tahir promised al-Mazyar that if he showed al-Afshin's letters, he would ask the commander of the faithful to pardon him. When al-Mazyar handed them over, he sent them with al-Mazyar to Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, and ordered that the letters not leave his hand except into the hand of al-Mu'tasim, and it was done. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part3 scan 26}

## The end of al-Mazyar
Al-Mazyar denied before al-Mu'tasim that there was anything in the letters, then he died, and he was crucified beside Babak. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part3 scan 26}
