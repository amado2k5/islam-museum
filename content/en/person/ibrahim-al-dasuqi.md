## Overview
Ibrahim al-Dasuqi al-Hashimi al-Shafi'i al-Qurashi, shaykh of the Burhamiyya order, and in his people's circle the author of *al-Muhadarat al-Qudsiyya*. He died in 676 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.359}

## What is reported of him
Ibn al-Imad in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* says that the leadership of speech on the thoughts of people came to him. He mentions the wonders reported of him as a shaykh: that he spoke all languages, knew the languages of beasts and birds, and that he fasted while still in the cradle. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.359}

These reports are given with "it was mentioned of him", and checking them needs an additional source.
