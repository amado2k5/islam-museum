Al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam al-Makhzumi al-Qurashi, one of the first Muslims of the [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|secret call]]; his name is tied in the Sira to his house, which became the call's centre. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
