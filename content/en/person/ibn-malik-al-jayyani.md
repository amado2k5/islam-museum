## Overview
The shaykh Jamal al-Din Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abdallah al-Ta'i al-Jayyani, known as Ibn Malik the grammarian. He died in 672 AH, and is the author of [[work/alfiyya-ibn-malik|the Alfiyya]] on grammar. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.578}
