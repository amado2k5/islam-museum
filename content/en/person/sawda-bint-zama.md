Umm al-Mu'minin Sawda bint Zam'a ibn Qays al-Amiriyya; among the first emigrant women to Abyssinia with her husband al-Sakran ibn Amr — when the two returned to Mecca he died, and the Prophet married her in Shawwal of year ten of the prophethood, after Khadija's death. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

She was the first woman to enter the Prophet's house after Khadija, taking care of his daughters and his household; she lived until the caliphate of Umar or of Mu'awiya, and gave her day to A'isha when she grew old. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## For Sprouts
Sawda bint Zam'a, Mother of the Believers, one of the first women to migrate to Abyssinia; the Prophet married her in Shawwal of the prophethood's tenth year after Khadija's death, the first woman to enter his house after her.

## For Explorers
Sawda bint Zam'a ibn Qays al-Amiriyya, Mother of the Believers; one of the first migrants to Abyssinia with her husband al-Sakran ibn Amr, who died after they returned to Mecca. The Prophet married her in Shawwal of the prophethood's tenth year after Khadija's death. She was the first woman to enter the Prophet's house after Khadija, looking after his daughters and his household; she lived until Umar's or Mu'awiya's caliphate, and gave her day to Aisha when she grew old.
