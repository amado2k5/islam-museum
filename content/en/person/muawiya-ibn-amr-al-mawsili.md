## Overview
Mu'awiya ibn Amr al-Mawsili al-Ta'i. He settled in Tarir in Azerbaijan, built there, and settled his children in it. They built palaces and made it a city, and built the market of Bajarwan. The sultan granted him its lands. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
