## Overview
Shaykh 'Abdallah al-Majdhub, Abyssinian by origin, known as Shaykh al-Hashasha; he entered Egypt and built himself a mausoleum dug at the gharqa of the mujawirs' road by the customs rise toward Balat al-Hukma — he died in it and was buried there, and it is still known among the mujawirs of Shaykh Idris. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 592}

## His states
A marvel of mind, he slept neither night nor day, and often had his wife present with him at the waqfa. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 592}
