## Summary
'Ayn al-Qudat al-Hamadhani, the jurist, scholar and adib Abu'l-Ma'ali 'Abdallah b. Muhammad al-Miyanji — from Miyana in Azerbaijan — one proverbial for intelligence; he entered Sufism and its subtleties and the meanings of the people's allusions until multitudes attached themselves to him, then he was crucified at Hamadan for those 'infidel expressions' in 525. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 555}
