## Overview
Qaludiya, the first of the kings of the Franks in the list that Armaz, bishop of Jarbada, sent to al-Hakam ibn Abd al-Rahman in 328 AH, as al-Mas'udi relates. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358}

## What al-Mas'udi relates
He describes him as a Magian, and his wife Ghirtala supported him. After him his son Ludhayriq reigned, then Duqshurt. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358}
