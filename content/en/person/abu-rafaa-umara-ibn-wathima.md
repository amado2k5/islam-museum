## Overview
Abu Rifa'a 'Umara b. Wathima b. al-Furat, the Egyptian chronicler; he transmitted from Abu Salih the katib of al-Layth b. Sa'd and from his father Wathima and others, and composed a year-by-year history which he transmitted; born in Egypt {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
