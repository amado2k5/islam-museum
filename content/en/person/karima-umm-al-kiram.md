## Summary
Karima bint Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hatim, Umm al-Kiram al-Marwaziyya, residing at Mecca; she transmitted the Sahih from al-Kushmihani and narrated from Zahir al-Sarakhsi; she would lay down her book and collate her copies with understanding and insight; she never married, and reached a hundred; she died in 463, and among the hafizes Ibn al-Ahdal narrated from her. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 314}
