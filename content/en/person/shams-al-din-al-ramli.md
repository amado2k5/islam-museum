## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Hamza, Shams al-Din al-Ramli, was the jurist of the Egyptian lands in his time and their authority for fatwa; he was called al-Shafi'i al-Saghir, the Lesser Shafi'i. His nisba is to al-Ramla, one of the villages of al-Minufiyya in Egypt, and he was born and died in Cairo. He held the post of mufti of the Shafi'is and gathered the fatwas of his father {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. The entry gives his years as 919 to 1004 AH, 1513 to 1596 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His works
He wrote many commentaries and glosses, among them 'Umdat al-Rabih', in manuscript, a commentary on 'Hadiyyat al-Nasih' in Shafi'i jurisprudence; 'Ghayat al-Bayan fi Sharh Zubad Ibn Raslan', printed; 'Ghayat al-Maram', in manuscript, a commentary on his father's 'Shurut al-Imama'; and 'Nihayat al-Muhtaj ila Sharh al-Minhaj', printed, on jurisprudence. He also has a printed 'Fatawa Shams al-Din al-Ramli' {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
