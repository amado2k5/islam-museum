## Overview
Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa al-Ijli, amir of Karaj and one of the commanders of al-Mu'tasim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the field
He raided the Daylam in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. He was governor in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim in the days of al-Afshin's governorship of the mountains, and he took the fortresses of the Daylam: Iqlasim, Buwamj, al-Iblam and Andaq. His career is connected with [[event/abu-dulaf-daylam-campaign|the campaign of Abu Dulaf against the Daylam]] and with the city of [[place/al-karaj|Karaj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Before al-Ma'mun
Al-Mas'udi relates that he entered upon al-Ma'mun, who tested him with his verses describing war. Al-Ma'mun asked him about his love poetry, and he answered with words that al-Mas'udi records. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.400-401}

## His death and description
He died in 226 AH, according to al-Mas'udi. Al-Mas'udi says that he was the chief of his people and the head of his tribe of Ijl and Rabi'a, a skilled and brave poet, and he relates a report of a spear thrust that went from front to back. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.451-452}

## Notes for researchers
- The descriptions in the preceding paragraph are attributed to al-Mas'udi. The date needs an additional source.
