## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hurmuz al-A'raj. Ibn Lahi'a relates from Abu al-Nadr that he was the first to lay down Arabic grammar, that he was the most knowledgeable of people about the genealogies and history of Quraysh, and one of the readers. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 104}
