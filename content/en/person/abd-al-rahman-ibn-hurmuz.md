## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hurmuz al-A'raj; Ibn Lahia relates from Abu'l-Nadr that he was the first to compose Arabic grammar, the most learned of people in the genealogies and lore of Quraysh, and one of the Qur'an readers. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 104}
