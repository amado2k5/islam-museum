## Overview
Umm Kulthum bint Abi Bakr al-Siddiq al-Taymiyya; a Successor whose father died while she was still in the womb, so she was born after his death. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.2030}

## Her transmission
She transmitted from Umm Salama the story of the musk sent to the Prophet's wives; al-Thawri transmitted it from Yahya ibn Humayd from Zaynab bint Abi Salama from her mother from her, Ibn Hibban included it in his Sahih, and Ibn Manda transmitted it through Muhammad ibn Sa'id from Humayd ibn Nafi' from her. She has another report from A'isha in Sahih Muslim, and the Companion Jabir ibn Abdallah al-Ansari transmitted from her. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.2030-2031}
