## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Abu Bakr al-Anbari, is called by al-Zirikli one of the most learned men of his time in literature and language, and among those who memorised most poetry and reports. The entry adds that it was said he memorised three hundred thousand witness-verses for the Qur'an. It gives his years as 271 to 328 AH, 884 to 940 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His life
He was born at al-Anbar on the Euphrates and died in Baghdad. He used to go to the sons of the caliph al-Radi billah to teach them {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books «al-Zahir», on language, in manuscript; «Sharh al-qasa'id al-sab' al-tiwal al-jahiliyyat», printed; «Idah al-waqf wa-l-ibtida' fi kitab Allah azza wa-jalla», printed; «al-Ha'at», in manuscript; «Aja'ib ulum al-Qur'an», in manuscript; «Sharh al-alifat», printed, a treatise published in the journal of the Academy in Damascus; «Khalq al-insan»; «al-Amthal»; and «al-Addad», printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. His most important book, the entry says, is «Gharib al-hadith», said to be 45,000 leaves {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## Al-Amali
He also has «al-Amali». Al-Zirikli saw a fragment of it copied at the Nizamiyya school, bearing the hand of the hafiz Abd al-Aziz Ibn al-Akhdar and the year 609 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
