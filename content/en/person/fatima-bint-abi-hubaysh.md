## Summary
Fatima bint Abi Hubaysh was a Companion best known for having [[concept/istihada|istihada]] and asking the Prophet about the ruling on her bleeding; her report became one of the foundations of that chapter in the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan of Abu Dawud]]. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 139} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 141}

## Her report
- In Hisham ibn 'Urwa's version from his father from [[person/aisha|'A'isha]]: Fatima came and said, "I am a woman who bleeds and does not become pure; shall I leave the prayer?" He said, "That is a vein, not menstruation; when menstruation comes, leave the prayer, and when it goes…" {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 141}
- In al-Mundhir ibn al-Mughira's version from [[person/urwa-ibn-al-zubayr|'Urwa ibn al-Zubayr]], she told him that she complained to the Prophet of the bleeding and he said, "That is a vein; see when your qur' comes, then do not pray, and when your qur' has passed, purify yourself and pray from one qur' to the next." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 139}
- In Suhayl ibn Abi Salih's version from al-Zuhri from 'Urwa, she told Asma' — or Asma' related that Fatima told her — to ask the Prophet, and he told her to keep away from prayer the days she used to, then bathe. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 140}
- Abu Dawud notes that Hammad ibn Zayd, from Ayyub, named her as the woman "pouring blood" in [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]]'s report. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 139}

## Her report in law
[[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|Al-Khattabi]] takes her report as the ruling for the woman who can distinguish her blood — seeing it dark and thick for a time, the onset of menstruation, then thin and clear, its end — and says the Prophet would only have told her this if she knew its coming and going by a sign. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 143} The annotator, quoting al-Suyuti, lists her first among the nine women with istihada in the Prophet's time. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v1 p. 139}
