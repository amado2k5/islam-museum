## Overview
Dirar ibn Abd al-Muttalib, an uncle of the Prophet, one of the sons of [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib ibn Hashim]] whom al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts in its chapter on the Prophet's family. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}.

## Counting the sons of Abd al-Muttalib
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum counts ten sons for Abd al-Muttalib: al-Harith, al-Zubayr, Abu Talib, Abd Allah, Hamza, Abu Lahab, al-Ghaydaq, al-Muqawwim, Dirar and al-Abbas. It also gives other accounts that make them eleven, by adding Qutham, or thirteen, by adding Abd al-Ka'ba and Hajl. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52} {cite:talqih-fuhum-ahl-al-athr p.8-9} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin 2/56-66}. He is therefore a brother of [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah]], the father of the Prophet. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.52}.

## What is not mentioned here
Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum says nothing here about his life, his religion or his death. Only what the book says has been recorded here; what is in the books of biographical classes and genealogy needs a source that can be read and quoted.

## For young visitors
The Prophet's grandfather Abd al-Muttalib had many children, sons and daughters, and Dirar was one of them. He was one of the uncles and aunts of the Prophet.
