## Summary
Abu Zakariya Yahya ibn Yusuf al-Zami. Muhammad ibn Ishaq al-Saghani, al-Bukhari, Abu Hatim al-Razi, Hanbal ibn Ishaq, al-Qasim ibn Zahir ibn Harb and Abu Bakr ibn Abi'l-Dunya transmitted from him. Abu Hatim said: we wrote from him at Rayy in the old days, then at Baghdad — in my view he is truthful. Abu Zur'a said: he is reliable, from a Khurasan village called Zamm. He died at Baghdad — said in Rajab 125, also said 126, also said 119. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.11 p.167}
