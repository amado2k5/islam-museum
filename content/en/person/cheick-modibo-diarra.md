## Summary
Cheick Modibo Diarra is a Malian scientist who worked at the highest levels of American space exploration before returning home to education and then politics. {cite:wikipedia-cheick-modibo-diarra}

## Life and upbringing
Born in Mali in 1952, he studied mathematics, physics and mechanics in France and the United States, earning advanced degrees in aerospace engineering. {cite:wikipedia-cheick-modibo-diarra}

## Career and works
At NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory he navigated interplanetary spacecraft on the Mars Pathfinder, Galileo and Magellan missions. Back in Africa he worked with Microsoft on education initiatives, founded a solar-energy lab in Bamako and a foundation for science education. In 2012 he was appointed prime minister of Mali in a transitional government, then forced out months later amid military pressure. {cite:wikipedia-cheick-modibo-diarra}

## Legacy
Diarra is remembered as a model of an African scientist who joined elite space research to service of his country’s development; his brief political episode is read within Mali’s 2012 turmoil. {cite:wikipedia-cheick-modibo-diarra}
