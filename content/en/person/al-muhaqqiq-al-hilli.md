## Overview
Ja'far ibn al-Hasan ibn Yahya ibn al-Husayn Ibn Sa'id al-Hidhli al-Hilli, Najm al-Din Abu al-Qasim, known as al-Muhaqqiq al-Hilli, was a leading Imami jurist of al-Hilla in Iraq, the marja' of the Imami Shi'a in his age; he died at al-Hilla in 676 AH (1277 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.123}

He knew letters and composed good verse. His works include Shara'i' al-Islam on the licit and forbidden; al-Nafi', its abridgement; al-Mu'tabar fi Sharh al-Mukhtasar; Usul al-Din; and Nukat al-Nihaya on fiqh, among others. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.123}
