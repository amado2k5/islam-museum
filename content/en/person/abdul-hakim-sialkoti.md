## Overview
Abd al-Hakim ibn Shams al-Din al-Hindi al-Sialkoti al-Banjabi was a man of learning from Sialkot, a town belonging to Lahore in India; the entry gives his death as 1067 AH, 1656 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 280}. He attached himself to Sultan Shah Jahan, who honoured him and gave him estates that spared him the effort of earning a living {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 280}.

## His writings
Al-Zirikli names "'Aqa'id al-Sialkoti," printed; a gloss on the Qur'an commentary of al-Baydawi, printed and left incomplete; and "Zubdat al-Afkar," printed, a gloss on the commentary on the Nasafi creed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 280}. He also wrote a printed gloss on al-Jurjani in logic; a printed gloss on al-Qutb's commentary on al-Shamsiyya, in logic; a printed gloss on al-Mutawwal, in rhetoric; and a gloss on al-Sa'd's commentary on the Izzi treatise on morphology {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 280}.
