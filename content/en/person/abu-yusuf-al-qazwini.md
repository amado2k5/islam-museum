## Overview
Abu Yusuf al-Qazwini, 'Abd al-Salam b. Muhammad b. Yusuf b. Bandar — shaykh of the Mu'tazila and author of the great Tafsir exceeding three hundred volumes; he studied kalam under the qadi 'Abd al-Jabbar at Ba'iri and heard from him and from Abu 'Umar Ibn Mahdi al-Farisi, moved through the lands and entered Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 385}

## His condition
A man of many books, excessive cleverness and depth in the disciplines — only he called to i'tizal. He died in Dhu'l-Qa'da 488 at sixty-five years and some months. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 385}
