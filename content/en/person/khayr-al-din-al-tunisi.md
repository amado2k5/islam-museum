## Overview
Khayr al-Din al-Tunisi, a Tunisian and Ottoman statesman, born in the land of the Circassians in 1238 AH (1822 CE) and died in 1308 AH (1890 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Public life
He presided over the Tunisian government, and wrote on political reform. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
