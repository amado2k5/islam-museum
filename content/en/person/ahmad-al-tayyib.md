## Overview
Ahmad al-Tayyib, shaykh of al-Azhar and head of the Muslim Council of Elders. The travel-book records he was born at al-Qurna in Luxor, graduated from the Faculty of Usul al-Din (creed and philosophy) in 1969, served as mufti of Egypt 2002–2003, then president of al-Azhar University 2003–2010, and took the shaykhdom in 2010 succeeding Imam Muhammad Sayyid Tantawi {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 16}. He visited Indonesia in February 2016 — his first visit since taking office {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 34}.
