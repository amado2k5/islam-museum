## Overview
Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Yusuf, Abu Abdallah ibn al-Fakhkhar, was the scholar of al-Andalus in his time and one of the Maliki imams at Cordoba. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 339 AH, 950 CE, with a question mark, and his death as 419 AH, 1028 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His journey and flight
He travelled to the East, made the pilgrimage, stayed near the holy places and lived in Medina, then returned to al-Andalus. When the Berbers overcame Cordoba he fled it, and they vowed his blood; he settled in Valencia until he died, at about eighty years of age {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.

## His books and his views
Al-Zirikli names a "Taqyid 'ala al-Jumal" on al-Zajjaji's work, in manuscript, the second part, the last that has been found of it, in Rabat in the Awqaf collection, number 304; an "Ikhtisar al-Mabsut" of Isma'il al-Dabbas; "al-Tabsira", in which he answered Ibn Abi Zayd in his Risala; and "al-Radd 'ala Abi Abdallah ibn al-Attar" on that author's legal documents {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}. He held views that he followed in his own private practice and in which he differed from the scholars of his country {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 291}.
