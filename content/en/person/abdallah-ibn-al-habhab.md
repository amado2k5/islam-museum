## Overview
Abd Allah ibn al-Habhab, client of the Banu Sulayl. Al-Baladhuri says that he was appointed over Ifriqiya after Ubayda ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Qaysi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His governorship
He sent [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri]] to raid Sus and the land of the Sudan (see [[event/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-sus-raid|the raid on Sus]]). Kulthum ibn Iyad al-Qushayri was appointed after him in 123 AH, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
[[person/ibn-abd-al-hakam|Ibn Abd al-Hakam]] says the first to make peace with the Beja was Ubayd Allah ibn al-Habhab, and some elders claim to have read his letter to them, requiring three hundred young camels each year, allowing them into the countryside only as passing traders, not as settlers, and forbidding them to kill any Muslim or protected person. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 255}
