## Overview
Ahmad ibn Umar ibn Ibrahim, Abu al-Abbas al-Ansari al-Qurtubi, was a Maliki jurist counted among the men of hadith, known as Ibn al-Muzayyin. Al-Zirikli dates his birth to 578 AH and his death to 656 AH, that is 1182 and 1258 CE as printed, the same years recorded from the title page of volume 7 of al-Mufhim {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 186}.

## Life
According to al-Zirikli he taught in Alexandria and died there, and he was born in Cordoba {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 186}.

## Works
His best-known work is al-Mufhim li-ma Ashkala min Talkhis Kitab Muslim, in manuscript, a commentary on a book of his own that abridges Muslim's collection. Al-Zirikli notes two parts of it in the Chester Beatty collection, numbers 3592 and 4938, and the first four volumes in manuscript in Rabat, numbers 253, 254, 41, 42 and 65 Awqaf, the second copied in Jerusalem in 696 AH. At al-Qarawiyyin in Fez he also has an abridgement of al-Bukhari's Sahih in manuscript, beginning with the chapter on the conversion of Umar ibn al-Khattab, and a Mukhtasar of the two Sahihs {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 186}.
