## Summary
Mustafa ibn Sa'd al-Ruhaybani, mufti of the Hanbalis in Damascus, a jurist and expert in inheritance, to whom the leadership of the law came. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2060}

## Works
His 'Matalib uli al-nuha', a commentary on 'Ghayat al-muntaha' in three volumes, is his work; he lived from 1164 to 1243 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2060}
