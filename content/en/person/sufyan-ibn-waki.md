## Overview
Sufyan ibn Waki' ibn al-Jarrah ibn Malih al-Ru'asi al-Kufi, Abu Muhammad, is called by al-Dhahabi the memoriser son of a memoriser and the hadith scholar of Kufa, a vessel of knowledge despite a weakness that attached to him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}. The sign printed with his entry shows that al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated much from his father, Jarir ibn Abd al-Hamid, Abd al-Salam ibn Harb, Abu Khalid al-Ahmar, Hafs ibn Ghiyath and their generation {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}. Al-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, Muhammad ibn Jarir, Abu Aruba, Yahya ibn Sa'id, Abu Ali Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Bashani and many others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}.

## What the critics said
Al-Bukhari said they spoke against him for things he was prompted into, and Abu Zur'a al-Razi said he was not to be occupied with and was accused {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}. Ibn Abi Hatim reports that his father advised him to change his copyist, who had spoiled his hadith, and to narrate only from his own originals; he promised, but went on and narrated hadith that had been inserted into his books {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}. Ibn Hibban said he was a virtuous and truthful shaykh who was tried with a bad copyist who inserted hadith for him and whom he trusted, and that when he was told of it he did not turn back, so he deserved to be abandoned for his persistence {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}. According to al-Dhahabi, Ibn Khuzayma narrated from him, but only a word here and there {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}.

## His death
He died in Rabi' al-Akhir 247 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1858}.
