## Overview
Bihz ibn Asad al-Ammi al-Basri, Abu al-Aswad, is described by al-Dhahabi as the imam, the memoriser and the trustworthy; he was the brother of Mu'alla ibn Asad {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}. The sign printed with his entry shows that the compilers of the six books recorded his hadith {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Shu'ba, Yazid ibn Ibrahim al-Tustari, Abu Bakr al-Nahshali and others; Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Ahmad ibn Sinan al-Qattan, Abd al-Rahman ibn Bishr, Abd Allah ibn Hashim al-Tusi and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}.

## What the critics said
Al-Dhahabi records that more than one critic called him trustworthy {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}. Abd al-Rahman ibn Bishr said he had never seen a better man than Bihz {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}.

## His death
Al-Dhahabi says he died in 197 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1248}.
