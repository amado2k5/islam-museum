## Overview
Hibatallah b. al-Hasan b. Yusuf, known as al-Badi' al-Asturlabi; the man of this tarjama, a famous poet and one of the excellent litterateurs — unique in his age for crafting astronomical instruments, master of that craft, by which he gained much wealth in al-Mustarshid's caliphate. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 583-584}

## His verse
Al-'Imad al-Katib mentions him in the Kharida, praises him and quotes excerpts of his verse; Ibn Khallikan said he was full of wit and charm in all his movements. He died of palsy in 533 and was buried at Baghdad's Wardiyya cemetery. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 583-584}

## In Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa-l-Adaba' al-Arab wa-l-Muslimin — Dar al-Jil / ALECSO
Abu al-Qasim Hibat Allah ibn al-Husayn ibn Yusuf al-Baghdadi, known as al-Badi al-Astarlabi, from Baghdad. The encyclopaedia counts him among the leading astronomers and astrolabe-makers of his age, and records his death in 534 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.269}
