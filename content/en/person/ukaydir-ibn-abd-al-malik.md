## Summary
King of Dumat al-Jandal, of Kinda and a Christian per Ibn Ishaq; Khalid ibn al-Walid captured him, and the Prophet spared his blood and made peace with him for the jizya. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "Ukaydir ibn Abd al-Malik, a man of Kinda who was king over it and was a Christian". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169} He says Khalid took him and brought him to the Prophet, who spared his blood, made peace with him for the jizya, then let him go — and he returned to his town. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: a man of Kinda. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- "Ukaydir" is spelled with a damma on the hamza and fatha on the kaf as in the printed page p. 169; other sources vocalise it differently.
