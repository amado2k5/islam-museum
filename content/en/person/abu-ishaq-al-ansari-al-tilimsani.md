## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Abi Bakr ibn Abd Allah ibn Musa al-Ansari, Abu Ishaq al-Tilimsani, was a scholar of the law of inheritance, Andalusian by origin, from the people of Huecas (al-Zirikli gives the place name in Latin letters). Al-Zirikli gives his years as 609 to 699 AH, 1212 to 1300 CE as printed {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 33–34}.

## Career
He was born in Tlemcen, settled in Granada for three years, moved to Malaga, and then settled in Ceuta until he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 33–34}.

## Works
He was known for a versified work on the law of inheritance called al-Tilimsaniyya, of which a manuscript is in the Zahiriyya in Damascus; al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Farhun that nobody had composed its like in that discipline and that the author versified it before he was twenty {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 33–34}. He had other works, among them a treatise on the science of the dubayt metre and a poem on the Prophet's birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 33–34}.
