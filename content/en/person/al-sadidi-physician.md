## Overview
Shaykh al-Sadid, shaykh of medicine in Egypt — Sharaf al-Din 'Abdallah b. 'Ali; he took the craft from al-Muwaffaq Ibn al-Mutin, served al-'Adid lord of Egypt, won sanctity and wide standing and lived an age; Nafis al-Din Ibn al-Zubayr studied under him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 789}

## His wealth
It is told that al-Sadid earned thirty thousand dinars in a single day; his pupil Ibn al-Zubayr told that he purified al-Hafiz li-Din Allah's son and gained about fifty thousand dinars in gold. He died in 592. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 789}
