## Overview
Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Mahaimi al-Hindi, Abu al-Hasan, Ala al-Din, known as al-Makhdum, of the Nawa'it, was a researcher and exegete {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. Al-Zirikli says he held the doctrine of the unity of being (wahdat al-wujud) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. He was born and died at Mahaim, one of the ports of the Konkan, a district of the Deccan in India next to the ocean {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}. The entry gives his years as 776 to 835 AH, 1374 to 1432 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## The Nawa'it
The Nawa'it are a people of the Deccan; al-Zirikli quotes al-Tabari that they were a group of Quraysh who left Medina in fear of al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, reached the coast of the Indian sea and settled there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli calls his writings precious Arabic works and names «Tabsir al-Rahman wa Taysir al-Mannan bi-Ba'd ma Yushir ila I'jaz al-Qur'an», printed in two volumes; «Zawarif al-Lata'if fi Sharh Awarif al-Ma'arif», a manuscript whose first part is held by Ubayd; «Ira'at al-Daqa'iq fi Sharh Mir'at al-Haqa'iq», a printed treatise; «Sharh al-Nusus li al-Qunawi»; «Adillat al-Tawhid»; and «Khusus al-Ni'am», a manuscript, on Fusus al-Hikam {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 250}.
