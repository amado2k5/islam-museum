## Summary
Shihab al-Din Abu al-'Abbas Ahmad al-Sharji al-Zabidi is the author of [[work/al-tajrid-al-sarih|al-Tajrid al-Sarih li-Ahadith al-Jami' al-Sahih]], as the title page of the [[work/nayl-al-awtar-shawkani|Nayl al-Awtar]] printing names him — the printing whose margin carries [[person/siddiq-hasan-khan|Siddiq Hasan al-Qannawji]]'s commentary on his book, [[work/awn-al-bari|'Awn al-Bari]]. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.5 p. 2}
