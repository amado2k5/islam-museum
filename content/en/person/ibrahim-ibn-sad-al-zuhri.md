## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Sa'd ibn Ibrahim ibn the Companion of the Messenger of God 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, the great imam and hafiz Abu Ishaq al-Qurashi al-Zuhri al-'Awfi of Medina; he narrated from his father the qadi of Medina, from his relative Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri, Yazid ibn al-Had, al-Walid ibn Kathir, Safwan ibn Sulaym and others. He died in 183 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.661}
