## Overview
Al-Malik al-Mu'ayyad Dawud ibn [[person/muzaffar-yusuf-rasulid|al-Muzaffar Yusuf]], the fifth of the [[polity/rasulid-dynasty|Banu Rasul]] rulers in Yemen, and brother of [[person/ashraf-umar-rasulid|al-Ashraf Umar]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His rule
Ibn Khaldun relates that he was brought out of his place of detention and they appointed him after his brother al-Ashraf was killed in 696 AH. He began his rule by killing the slave-girl who had poisoned his brother. The kings of the Turks kept sending him gifts, until in 711 AH his gift reached two hundred camel-loads of clothing, curiosities and the finest of Yemen, and two hundred camels and horses. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Learning and books
He gathered books from the cities until his library held a hundred thousand volumes. He took care of the scholars with his gifts, and sent his prizes to Ibn Daqiq al-Id, the Shafi'i jurist in Egypt. He died in 721 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived, and the report rests on one source.
