## Summary
Baba Dhu'l-Nun, leader of the 1526 rising around Yozgat under [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 200}.

## The rising
Al-Salabi calls it a 'Shi'i Rafidi' revolt that mustered three to four thousand rebels and imposed kharaj on the district, growing strong enough to defeat the first Ottoman captains sent to suppress it; it ended in his defeat, his head sent to Istanbul {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 200}.
