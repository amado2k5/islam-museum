## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Uthman ibn Sa'id ibn Bashshar al-Ahwal al-Anmati, a Baghdadi traditionist. He is called al-Anmati after the anmat, meaning rugs and similar furnishings. He died in the year 288 AH {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## His teachers
Books of the classes of jurists say that he took jurisprudence from [[person/al-rabi-ibn-sulayman-al-muadhdhin|al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman al-Muadhdhin]] and from [[person/al-muzani|al-Muzani]] {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}.
