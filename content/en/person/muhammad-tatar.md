## Al-Salih Muhammad Tatar

Sultan al-Salih Muhammad Tatar. He was deposed on the fifteenth of Rabiʿ I 825 AH and stayed with Sultan al-Malik al-Ashraf, honoured, until he was stabbed and died on the seventeenth of Jumada II 833 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 204}.
