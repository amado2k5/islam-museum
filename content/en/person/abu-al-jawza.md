## Overview
Aws ibn Abd Allah, Abu al-Jawza al-Rib'i al-Basri, a Successor. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14950} He narrated from Aisha, Ibn Abbas and Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As; Abu al-Ashhab al-Utaridi, Amr ibn Malik al-Nakri and Badil ibn Maysara narrated from him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14950} He was one of the worshippers who stood against al-Hajjaj, and he is said to have been killed on the day of al-Jamajim. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14950} He is reported to have said that he never cursed anything and never harmed anyone. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14950}
