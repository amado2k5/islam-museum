## Summary
Abu'l-Gharat Tala'i' ibn Ruzzik, styled al-Malik al-Salih, vizier of Egypt, had been governor of the Banu Khasib district in Upper Egypt. When al-Zafir Isma'il, ruler of Egypt, was killed, the palace people went to al-Salih and sought his help against 'Abbas and his son Nasr; he marched on Cairo, 'Abbas and his son fled with their followers, and he took the vizierate under al-Fa'iz, holding sole power on 19 Rabi' I 549. He was accomplished, generous in giving, pleasant in company, and composed verse — Ibn Khallikan examined his diwan in two parts. Among the wonders: he took the vizierate on the nineteenth and was killed on the nineteenth. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
