## Summary
Khayyar ibn Salama, Abu Ziyad, counted among the Syrians; he narrated from Aisha and was narrated from by Khalid ibn Ma'dan. Ibn Hibban lists him among the reliable narrators. Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i record one report from him, on onions. Dates not given in the book. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13345}
