# Farid al-Bahraini

## Summary
Farid al-Bahraini is a Bahraini Muslim researcher and debater who runs the Farid Responds YouTube channel, which answers objections raised against Islam and against the hadith literature. {cite:youtube-farid-responds} A podcast profile identifies the channel's presenter by his personal name: "Ustadh Farid al-Bahraini (Farid Responds)". {cite:listennotes-farid-responds}

## Work
Farid co-founded the websites TwelverShia.net and Sunni Discourse and created Nahjul-Balagha.net, sites devoted to answering Twelver Shia claims from Sunni sources. His published books include The Martyrdom of al-Husayn in Light of the Authentic Traditions, a short account of Karbala restricted to narrations he presents as authentic by the hadith scholars' conditions, with chains and references for every narration. {cite:tadabburbooks-farid-al-bahraini} His channel's debates and response videos address Christian, atheist and Shia interlocutors, and he has taken part in Muslim conference and podcast discussions of the online dawah scene. {cite:listennotes-farid-responds}
