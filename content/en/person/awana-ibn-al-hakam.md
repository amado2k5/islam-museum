## Overview
'Awana ibn al-Hakam ibn 'Iyad ibn Wizar al-Kalbi, the erudite historian Abu al-Hakam of Kufa, blind, one of the eloquent; he authored al-Tarikh, Sirat Mu'awiya wa-Bani Umayya and other works. Hisham ibn al-Kalbi and others transmitted from him and he was reliable in his reporting. Al-Nadim dates his death to 147 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2979}
