## Overview
Awana ibn al-Hakam, a narrator in al-Baladhuri's "Futuh al-Buldan". Al-Baladhuri relates from him that the Copts used to mention Christ at the heads of their registers and attributed to him divinity, and they made the cross take the place of the basmala. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He links this with the crisis of the dinar that followed Abd al-Malik's changing of what was established, and the dislike of the king of the Byzantines for that. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
