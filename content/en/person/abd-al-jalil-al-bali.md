## Overview
Abd al-Jalil ibn Abi al-Mawahib Muhammad ibn Abd al-Baqi al-Mawahibi al-Hanbali al-Ba'li al-Dimashqi is described by al-Zirikli as a man of learning. His family came from Baalbek, to which his name points; he was born and died in Damascus. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1079 AH, 1669 CE, to 1119 AH, 1707 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 272}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists a versification of al-Shafiya in morphology and a commentary on it, a tashtir of Ibn Malik's Alfiyya in grammar, a rajaz poem on prosody, and treatises, and says he also wrote poetry {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 272}. The entry is brief and says no more.
