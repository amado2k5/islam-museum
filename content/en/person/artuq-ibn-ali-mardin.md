## Overview
Artuq b. 'Ali, the Artuqid lord of Mardin (killed 636); he held Mardin some thirty-five years and was, on the whole, just and religious; his ghilman murdered him in league with his grandson, and his son Najm al-Din Ghazi succeeded {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 188}.
