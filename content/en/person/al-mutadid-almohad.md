## Overview
Al-Mu'tadid, Almohad lord of the Maghrib; he took power after his brother 'Abd al-Wahid b. Idris in 640, and was killed on his horse's back while besieging a fort at Tlemcen in Safar 646 — al-Murtada Abu Hafs ruled after him, his state lasting twenty years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 244}
