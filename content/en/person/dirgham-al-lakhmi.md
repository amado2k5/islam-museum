## Summary
Al-Malik al-Mansur Abu'l-Ashbal Dirgham b. Sawar al-Lakhmi; he rose against the vizier Shawar in al-'Adid's days, gathered great hosts and contested the vizierate, routing Shawar to Syria. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His end
When Shawar returned from Syria with the armies, Dirgham went out from Cairo and was killed at the shrine of Sayyida Nafisa; they cut off his head, paraded it on a lance, his corpse lay three days then was buried by the Elephant Pool, and a dome was built over him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
