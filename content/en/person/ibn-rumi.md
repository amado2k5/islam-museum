## Overview
Ali ibn al-Abbas ibn al-Rumi, a poet. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.573}

## His praise of al-Mu'tadid
Al-Suyuti quotes from a qasida of his in praise of the caliph al-Mu'tadid, in which he says that the kingship of the Abbasids was founded with Abu al-Abbas (the caliph) and is renewed through him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.573}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi quotes a verse of his on the arrival of Qatr al-Nada, daughter of Khumarawayh, at the court of al-Mu'tadid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.631} He says that al-Qasim ibn Ubaydallah destroyed him with poison in Khushknanja, adding "as it is said". He describes him as one of the fine inventors of meanings. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.640-641}
