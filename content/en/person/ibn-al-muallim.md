## Overview
Abu Abdallah Ibn al-Mu'allim lived in the time of Ibn al-Nadim, and the leadership of the Shi'i theologians (mutakallimun) came to him. He was foremost in the craft of kalam according to the doctrine of his school. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 252}

Ibn al-Nadim says that he saw him and found him skilled, and describes him as keen in understanding and quick in judgement. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 252}
