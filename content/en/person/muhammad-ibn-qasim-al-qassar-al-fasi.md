## Summary
A Maliki jurist and traditionist of Fez, known as al-Qassar; the Shajara al-Nur al-Zakiya calls him the mufti of Fez and the last of its scholars; studied with Abd al-Wahhab al-Zaqqaq, Abu al-Qasim al-Rashidi and Ibn Jalal, and taught al-Dala'i and al-Maqqari; works include a fihrist of his riwayat and a book on the virtues of Idris ibn Abd Allah. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7843}
