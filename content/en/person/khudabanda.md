## Overview
Khudabanda, king of the Tatars. Al-Suyuti says that he showed Rafd (Shi'ism of the Rafida) in his lands, and ordered the preachers to mention in the sermon only Ali ibn Abi Talib, his two sons, and the People of the House. This continued until he died in 716 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 735}
