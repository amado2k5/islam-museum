## Summary
Aisyah Hijanah is a Malaysian social-media influencer, dawah figure, author and entrepreneur, the founder and director of HIJANAH, a modest-fashion and books venture based in Johor Bahru. {cite:socialveins-aisyahhijanah} {cite:hype-aisyah-hijanah-2024}

She began sharing content on Islamic teachings, modest fashion and motivational themes on social media around 2016. {cite:thecityceleb-aisyah-hijanah}

Her Instagram account counted 1,317,417 followers in a direct read on 15 November 2026. {cite:instagram-aisyahhijanah}

## Beginnings
A biographical aggregator gives her full name as Aisyah Hijanah binti Azhari and her birth as 6 January 1998 in Kuala Lumpur; another aggregator's introduction gives 1 June 1998 while its own fact list gives 6 January, and her record carries the date with that conflict stated rather than hidden. {cite:thecityceleb-aisyah-hijanah}

In July 2019 she and her husband launched the modest-fashion brand that became HIJANAH, offering contemporary Islamic clothing. {cite:thecityceleb-aisyah-hijanah}

Her account's own bio line, as the tracker snapshot records it, describes her as "Founder & Director of HIJANAH" and stores her location as Johor Bahru. {cite:socialveins-aisyahhijanah}

## Public statements
In June 2024, Hype Malaysia reported her public response to allegations concerning her husband's second marriage: she said the accusations had been handed to her lawyer, defended her company's audit practice, and announced that she would be performing the Hajj. {cite:hype-aisyah-hijanah-2024}

The family matter itself is not detailed in this record beyond her public response to it, as the report states it. {cite:hype-aisyah-hijanah-2024}
