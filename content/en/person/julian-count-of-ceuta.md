## Overview
Julian, lord of Ceuta, a Spanish ruler. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.67-68}

## In the conquest
The book says that the contacts of the Spanish side with Musa ibn Nusayr during the conquest were a helping factor that eased its course. It also says that the ships that carried the troops of Tariq ibn Ziyad were brought to Ceuta through his management. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.67-68}

It also says that the initiative and planning were on the Islamic side. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.67-68}

## In al-Baladhuri
Alyan, governor of the frontier of al-Andalus, according to al-Baladhuri on the authority of al-Waqidi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}

## His meeting with Tariq
Tariq ibn Ziyad met him on his raid in 92 AH. Tariq granted him safe conduct, and carried him and his companions to al-Andalus in ships. He is mentioned in [[event/conquest-of-al-andalus|the conquest of al-Andalus]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}
