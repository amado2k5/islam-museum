## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Fa'id ibn Musa al-Zawawi al-Qusantini was a Maliki jurist from Algeria. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 796 AH, 1394 CE, and his death as 857 AH, 1453 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 57}.

## Life
He was born in the Jurjura mountains (Jabal Jurjur), studied in Bejaia and Tunis, and settled in Constantine {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 57}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists a Qur'anic exegesis; Tashil al-Sabil, a commentary on the Mukhtasar of Khalil on Maliki law, in eight volumes; Fayd al-Nil, also a commentary on the Mukhtasar, in two volumes; a commentary on the Alfiyya of Ibn Malik; and Talkhis al-Miftah, which he called Talkhis al-Talkhis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 57}.
