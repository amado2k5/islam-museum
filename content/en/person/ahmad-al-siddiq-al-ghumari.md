## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Siddiq ibn Ahmad, Abu al-Fayd al-Ghumari al-Hasani al-Azhari, was a Moroccan Shafi'i jurist, known like his father as Ibn al-Siddiq {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}. Al-Zirikli prints his death as 1380 AH, 1960 CE, and gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}.

## Life
He lived for a time at Tangier, studied at al-Azhar, and settled and died in Cairo {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Riyad al-Tanzih fi Fadl al-Qur'an wa-Hamilih, in his own hand at Dar al-Kutub, and Matali' al-Budur fi Jawami' Akhbar al-Abrar, printed at Tangier {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}. Others are Iqamat al-Dalil, printed, on the prohibition of staging prophets and saints in the theatre, and Tawjih al-Anzar li-Tawhid al-Muslimin fi al-Sawm wa-l-Ifṭar, a printed treatise {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}. He also lists al-Tasawwur wa-l-Tasdiq, printed, on the life of his father, al-Mu'jam al-Wajiz li-l-Mujiz, a treatise on his teachers with a sketch of their lives, and Ibraz al-Wahm al-Maknun, printed, on the hadiths about the Mahdi {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}.
