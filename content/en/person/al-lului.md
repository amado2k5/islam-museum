## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ziyad al-Lu'lu'i, kunya Abu Ali, one of the companions of Abu Hanifa who took from him and heard from him. Ibn al-Nadim describes him as an excellent scholar of Abu Hanifa's doctrines in opinion. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 288}

Ibn al-Nadim quotes Yahya ibn Adam: "I have not seen anyone more learned in jurisprudence than al-Hasan ibn Ziyad." He died in 204 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 288}

He lists among his books, from al-Tahawi: "al-Mujarrad" of Abu Hanifa, "Adab al-Qadi", "al-Khisal", "Ma'ani al-Iman", "al-Nafaqat", "al-Kharaj", and "al-Fara'id". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 288}
