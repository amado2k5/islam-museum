## Summary
A Companion of [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] with the kunya Abu Sakhr, an ally of Banu Munqidh; he was in the cavalry of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] on the day Mecca was conquered, and was martyred at [[place/al-khandama|al-Khandama]] with [[person/kurz-ibn-jabir-al-fihri|Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## His martyrdom
Khunays and Kurz strayed off the road and took a path where they met the fighters at the bottom of Mecca by al-Khandama; Khunays was struck and killed, and Kurz defended him until he too was killed {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## For young visitors
A Companion of the tribe of Khuza'a who was martyred with his companion Kurz in the battle of al-Khandama on the morning Mecca was conquered.
