## Overview
Khalid ibn Safwan al-Tamimi, whom al-Mas'udi describes as the orator of the Arabs. At the gathering of al-Saffah, some sons of Qahtan boasted over the sons of Nizar, and Khalid answered them with words that disparaged them. Then he went on to the story of the Abyssinians taking power and the Persians rescuing them. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 491}

## With Umm Salama
Al-Mas'udi also relates a report of him with Abu al-Abbas. He described women and their kinds to him, and when his words reached Umm Salama she was angry. When he learned that she was listening from behind the curtain, he turned to describing the harm of co-wives, and she sent him ten thousand dirhams and gifts. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 247-250}
