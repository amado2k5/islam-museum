## Overview
Abu al-Hudhayl al-Allaf, that is Muhammad ibn al-Hudhayl, one of the leading Mu'tazilites of Basra. The data give his birth as 135 AH and his death as 235 AH, at the start of al-Mutawakkil's caliphate. {cite:duha-islam p.761-762} The "Fihrist" says that he took justice and unity from Uthman al-Tawil, and that Uthman took it from Wasil ibn Ata, and that he was in the class of the writers famous for Mu'tazilism after Wasil according to al-Balkhi. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.557-558}

Ahmad Amin describes him as one of the strongest figures of the Basra school and the head of Mu'tazilism in his age, who mixed the principles of Mu'tazilism with those of philosophy. He says that al-Ma'mun held sessions of debate in which Abu al-Hudhayl was the master, that he disputed with heretics, Magians and Dualists, and that they related that more than three thousand converted to Islam at his hands. {cite:duha-islam p.761-762}

Al-Mas'udi quotes him on love in the session of Yahya al-Barmaki. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.358}
