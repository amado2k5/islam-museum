## Summary
Sultan 'Abd al-Haqq ibn Abi Sa'id al-Marini, lord of Fez, died in Ramadan 869 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}.

## Details
Ibn al-'Imad records under the dead of 869 "the sultan 'Abd al-Haqq ibn Abi Sa'id al-Marini, lord of Fez," dying in the month of Ramadan, without further detail {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}. He was the last Marinid sultan of Fez, the dynasty that had ruled the central Maghreb for nearly two centuries; his reign is remembered for the ascendancy of the Wattasid viziers until the house was extinguished and the Wattasids succeeded him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}. His death falls in a year that also saw the passing of many scholars, among them the chief judge Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Salih al-Islaymi, whose biography the author gives in the same entry year {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}.
