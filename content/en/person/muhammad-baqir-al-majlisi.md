## Overview
Muhammad Baqir ibn Muhammad Taqi ibn Maqsud Ali al-Isfahani was, in al-Zirikli's words, an Imami 'allama, that is a leading scholar of the Twelver Imamis. He held the office of shaykh al-Islam in Isfahan and translated a large group of hadiths into Persian {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. The entry gives his years as 1037 to 1111 AH, 1627 to 1700 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Bihar al-Anwar", printed in 25 parts on various subjects; "Kitab al-'Aql wa-al-'Ilm wa-al-Jahl"; "Kitab al-Tawhid"; "Mir'at al-'Uqul", printed; "Jawami' al-'Ulum"; "al-Sira al-Nabawiyya"; "al-Imama"; "al-Fitan wa-al-Mihan"; a book on the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib, his merits and circumstances; "Ta'rikh Fatima wa-al-Hasanayn"; several histories of the imams; "al-Sama' wa-al-'Alam", very large, of which the fourteenth volume has been printed; "al-Ahkam"; and "al-Risala al-Wajiza", in manuscript, on the men of hadith {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}. Speaking in his own voice ("I say"), al-Zirikli adds that a small collection in the Rabat library holds thirteen treatises of his, the first "Tahqiq al-Hal fi Muhammad ibn Sinan", the second on the standing of Abd al-Hamid ibn Salim al-Attar and of his son Muhammad ibn Abd al-Hamid, and the third on the standing of Muhammad ibn Isa al-Buqtini {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 23}.
