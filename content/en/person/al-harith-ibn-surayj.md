## Overview
[[person/al-harith-ibn-surayj|Al-Harith ibn Surayj]], a man of Khurasan, who disputed command there with [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]], and was killed in Khurasan in 128 AH as printed before the paragraph. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 645}

## Khurasan and the dispute with Nasr
Al-Tabari says that in Khurasan there were those who disputed with Nasr ibn Sayyar, among them al-Kirmani and al-Harith ibn Surayj. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 645} Al-Harith pursued the defeated people of Balkh until he entered it, then came to Merv with a large host. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 295} {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 297}

## The fighting
Al-Harith came out for single combat, and struck his opponent on the left shoulder and felled him, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 308} He protected Khaqan during his withdrawal, when his mule was caught in the mud, according to the report. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 338} {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 scan 339}

## Notes for researchers
- The year of his death (128) is taken from the printed note before the paragraph as it appears.
- A report on page 660 has been left out, because it is unclear from the scanned text to whom the words are attributed.
- Most of the reports are "according to the report": al-Tabari relates them from named transmitters, and they have not yet been compared with other sources.
- The order of events between Balkh and Merv is not settled by the text as read, and it has not been arranged chronologically here.
- The references are to scan page numbers. Reports whose scanned text was garbled have been left out.
