## Overview

'Abd al-Karim ibn 'Abd al-Samad al-Tabari, known as Abu Ma'shar, of Mecca. He died in 478 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 45}

## His gathering of the readings

He wrote al-Talkhis fi al-Qira'at al-Thaman and Sawq al-'Arus. Ibn al-Jazari says Sawq al-'Arus holds fifteen hundred and fifty riwayas and lines of transmission, and that [[person/yusuf-ibn-ali-al-hudhali|al-Hudhali]] and Abu Ma'shar are the two men who gathered the most readings of any he knew of; after them he knows none who gathered more except Abu al-Qasim 'Isa ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz of Alexandria, author of al-Jami' al-Akbar, who died in 629 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 45} Al-Talkhis appears in Ibn al-Jazari's chains, where a group read from it to Abu Nasr Ahmad ibn Masrur al-Khabbaz. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 129}
