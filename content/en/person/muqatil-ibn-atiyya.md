## Overview
Muqatil ibn Atiyya al-Bakri al-Hijazi, Abu al-Hayja, the prince and poet, was titled Shibl al-Dawla {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3925}. He travelled to Baghdad, Ghazna and Khurasan, praised the great, and became close to Nizam al-Mulk {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3925}.

## His meeting with Ibn al-Ala
He then went to Nasir al-Din Mukram ibn al-Ala, vizier of Kirman, carrying a note on which the caliph al-Mustazhir bi-Allah had written that the road to Ibn al-Ala was sufficient; when he came in and showed the note, the vizier rose, bowed to it and ordered him a thousand dinars at once, and another thousand with a horse and a robe of honour when he recited his poem {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3925}.

## The end of his life
He settled at Herat, fell in love with a woman there, then fell ill with melancholy and died about 505 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3925}. Al-Dhahabi refers readers to Wafayat al-A'yan for him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص3925}.
