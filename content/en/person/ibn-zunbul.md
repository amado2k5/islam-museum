## Overview
Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Zunbul was a historian of Egypt. He practised geomancy and astrology and was called al-Rammal, then served as a clerk in the army's chancery; he died after 980 AH (1572 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.179}

His books include Fath Misr; Sirat al-Sultan Salim; Tuhfat al-Muluk fi 'Aja'ib al-Barr wa-l-Bahr; al-Maqalat fi-l-Sihr wa-l-Raml; and Qanun al-Najama. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.179}
