## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Thalaba ibn Abd Allah ibn Husn al-Ta'i. In Ibn al-Kalbi's account he fought Dahir, king of Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The report rests on one account, and the data hold no other report or date, which needs an additional source.
