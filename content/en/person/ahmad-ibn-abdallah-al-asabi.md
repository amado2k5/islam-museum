## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami, whose title is Shihab al-Din and whose nisba is al-Asabi, a scholar from Zabid in Yemen. The nisba refers to Dhu Asab near Zabid. He studied his subjects in Zabid under Shaykh Abd Allah al-Mizjaji. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

The encyclopaedia says he was alive in 1118 AH (1706 CE). His birth and death dates and his works need an additional source.
