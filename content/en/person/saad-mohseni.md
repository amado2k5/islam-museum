## Summary
Saad Mohseni is an Afghan-Australian media entrepreneur born in Kabul in 1966, co-founder and chairman of the Moby Group, Afghanistan's largest media company; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential media figures. {cite:muslim500-saad-mohseni} {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## Founding
Raised between Kabul, Islamabad, Tokyo and Melbourne, he returned to Afghanistan after 2001 and, with his brothers, launched Arman FM radio in 2003 and TOLO television in 2004, building Moby into the country's dominant private broadcaster. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## The network
Moby's outlets — TOLO entertainment, TOLOnews and Lemar news, Arman FM, and later regional ventures — became Afghanistan's most-watched television, pioneering music, drama, talent shows and uncensored news in a conservative media environment. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## Resilience
Moby channels survived insurgent attacks — including a 2016 bombing that killed staff — and after the 2021 change of government the group kept broadcasting under new restrictions, a resilience widely covered in international media. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## Assessment
He is credited with creating modern commercial television in Afghanistan and with sustaining independent news through war and regime change; commentary on his political influence is reported attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni} {cite:muslim500-saad-mohseni}


## Moby Group
The group he co-founded in 2003 built Afghanistan's dominant private broadcaster — TOLO TV, Lemar TV, radio networks and production — reaching a majority of the country's population with news, drama and music formats. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## Expansion
Moby expanded into Iran ⁦(Farsi1)⁩, the Gulf and Central Asia, and launched streaming and fintech ventures; Mohseni is regularly cited as the most significant media entrepreneur in Afghanistan's post-2001 history. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## Post-2021
After the Taliban's return he relocated operations and kept Moby producing for Afghan audiences from abroad, while speaking publicly on press freedom and the collapse of independent Afghan media. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni} {cite:muslim500-saad-mohseni}

## Origins
Born in Kabul in 1966 and raised between Afghanistan, Australia and Britain, he worked in banking before founding Moby Group in 2003 — a media venture built in a country rebuilding its information infrastructure after decades of war. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## TOLO's impact
TOLO TV's launch under Moby in 2004 — entertainment formats, news, drama — transformed Afghan television within a few years, making it the country's dominant private channel and a defining institution of post-2001 media. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni}

## After 2021
The collapse of independent Afghan media after August 2021 displaced his operations; he relocated production and continued broadcasting for Afghan audiences from abroad while speaking publicly on press freedom. {cite:wikipedia-saad-mohseni} {cite:muslim500-saad-mohseni}
