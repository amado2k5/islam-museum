## Summary
Al-Malik al-Zahir, known as al-Mushammar — Muzaffar al-Din, son of Sultan Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub and full brother of al-Malik al-Afdal. He was called al-Mushammar because when his father divided the lands among his elder sons he said: "and I am mushammar (rolled-sleeved)" — and the name prevailed over him. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
