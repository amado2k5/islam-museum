## Overview
Dubays (Muhammad ibn Yazid), pupil of al-Kindi, who practised the crafts and brazier-making; author of Kitab al-Jami' and a book on the making of dyes, ink and writing-fluid. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 506}
