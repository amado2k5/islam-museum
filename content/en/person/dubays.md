## Overview
Dubays, Muhammad ibn Yazid, a pupil of al-Kindi. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.506}

## In Ibn al-Nadim
Ibn al-Nadim mentions him among those who practised the craft (alchemy) and the works of the pneumatic and mechanical arts. His books include "Al-Jami'" and "The Making of Dyes, Inks and Writing Ink". {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.506} His teacher was [[person/al-kindi|al-Kindi]].
