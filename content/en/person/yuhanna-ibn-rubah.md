## Summary
Master of Ayla; he came to the Prophet at Tabuk, made peace with him and gave him the jizya — and the Prophet wrote him a writ of safe-conduct. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him "Yuhanna ibn Ru'ba, master of Ayla", saying he came to the Prophet at Tabuk, made peace with him and gave him the jizya. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169} The Prophet wrote him a writ of safe-conduct for the people of Ayla. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: master of Ayla. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}
