## Summary
Miskina al-Tufawiyya is among the women ascetics of [[place/basra|Basra]] in [[work/sifat-al-safwa|Sifat al-Safwa]] (no. 611); she was one of the women who kept constantly to circles of remembrance. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## The gathering of Isa ibn Zadhan
From Ammar al-Rahib: she used to attend with them the gathering of Isa ibn Zadhan in [[place/al-ubulla|al-Ubulla]], travelling down from Basra expressly to reach it. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## The dream
Ammar relates that he saw her in a dream after her death and welcomed her; she said, "Poverty (maskana) has gone and the greatest wealth has come," and that the whole of Paradise had been opened to her to go where she wished; when he asked by what, she said, "By the gatherings of remembrance and patience in the truth." He asked her about Isa ibn Zadhan and she described the honour he had received — Isa having fasted until he was bent and his voice failed. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 p.716}

## For young visitors
Miskina was a woman of Basra who loved gatherings for remembering God, and travelled from her city to attend the lessons of a good teacher.
