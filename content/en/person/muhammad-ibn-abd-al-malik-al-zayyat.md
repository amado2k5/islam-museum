## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Zayyat, vizier of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.526-527}

## His lament
Al-Suyuti says that he lamented al-Mu'tasim at his death, combining consolation and congratulation in his lament. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.526-527}

## Accusation of heresy
Ibn al-Nadim says that it was said that he was a heretic (zindiq), and counts him among the kings and notables who were accused of heresy. This is a view attributed to Ibn al-Nadim, not the judgement of the museum. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.473}

## His family
From his children was Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Zayyat, and among his descendants Abu Talib Ahmad ibn al-Husayn, the companion of Ibn Wahshiyya and the transmitter of his books from him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.433}
