## Overview

Muhammad ibn al-Mutawakkil al-Lu'lu'i al-Basri, known as Ruways, is one of the two transmitters of the reading of [[person/yaqub-al-hadrami|Ya'qub al-Hadrami]], with [[person/rawh-ibn-abd-al-mumin|Rawh]]. He died in Basra in 238 AH. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 197}

## His standing

Ibn al-Jazari calls him an imam of the reading, firm in it, skilled, exact, famous and adroit, and quotes al-Dani that he was among the most adroit of Ya'qub's companions. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 197} Al-Tammar, who died shortly after 300 AH and was the reader of Basra and its shaykh in the reading, read forty-seven complete recitations to him, and then al-Nakhkhas, who died in 368. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 197} Ibn al-Jazari sets out forty-one lines of transmission from Ruways. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 193}
