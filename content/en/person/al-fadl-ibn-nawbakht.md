## Overview
Abu Sahl al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht al-Farisi, who worked in the House of Wisdom of Harun al-Rashid. {cite:duha-islam}

## His work
He translated from Persian into Arabic whatever books of Persian wisdom he found. {cite:duha-islam}

Ahmad Amin relates from al-Qifti that his reliance for his learning and his books was on the books of the Persians. {cite:duha-islam}

His death date and the list of what he translated need an additional source.
