## Summary
Uwaymir al-Ajlani, a Companion of Banu Ajlan of [[group/ansar|the Ansar]]; the [[event/lian-between-uwaymir-and-his-wife|li'an]] occurred between him and his wife after the Prophet's return from Tabuk in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## The li'an affair
Uwaymir accused his wife, and they carried out the mutual oaths before the Prophet that the Qur'an legislated in Surat al-Nur; then the Prophet separated them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## For young visitors
Uwaymir accused his wife before the Prophet, who applied between them the li'an ruling the Qur'an legislated, then separated them.
