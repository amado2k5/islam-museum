## Summary
Ismail ibn Muhammad al-Ajluni, Abu al-Fida, a Shafi'i known as al-Jarrahi, born in Ajlun, raised and died in Damascus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2065}

## Works
A brilliant hadith scholar who taught in the Umayyad Mosque; his works include 'Kashf al-khafa'' and 'al-Ajwiba al-muhaqqaqa'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2066}
