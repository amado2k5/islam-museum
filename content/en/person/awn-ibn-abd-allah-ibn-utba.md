## Overview
'Awn ibn 'Abdallah ibn 'Utba ibn Mas'ud, the imam, exemplar and ascetic Abu 'Abdallah al-Hudhali of Kufa, brother of Medina's jurist 'Ubaydallah; he narrated from his father, his brother, Ibn al-Musayyab, Ibn 'Abbas, 'Abdallah ibn 'Amr and others, and from 'A'isha and Abu Hurayra — though his transmission from those two is held to be mursal — and he likewise reported from his great-uncle 'Abdallah ibn Mas'ud. Ahmad and others declared him trustworthy. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2980}

## Piety
When 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz took the caliphate he came to him on a visit; he lived ninety years, a truthful man with no fault known; he died in Dhu'l-Qa'da, 230 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.2980}
