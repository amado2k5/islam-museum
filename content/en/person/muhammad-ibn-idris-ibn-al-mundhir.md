## Overview
Muhammad ibn Idris ibn al-Mundhir ibn Dawud ibn Mihran al-Handhali, Abu Hatim, is described by al-Zirikli as a hadith memorizer and a contemporary of al-Bukhari and Muslim. He was born in Rayy, to which his name al-Razi points, and died in Baghdad. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 195 AH, 810 CE, and his death as 277 AH, 890 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 27}.

## Life
He travelled between Iraq, Syria, Egypt and the lands of Rum, and died in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 27}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists his Tabaqat al-Tabi'in; al-Zina, in manuscript; Tafsir al-Qur'an al-Azim, in manuscript, noting that its third volume is in the Mahmudiyya Library in Medina, no. 49 tafsir, copied in the year 872 as he prints it; and A'lam al-Nubuwwa, in manuscript, in the library of Muhsin al-Hamdhani at Narbura in India {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 27}.
