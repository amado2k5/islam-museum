# Sharif Sharifov

## Summary

Sharif Naidhajavovich Sharifov (born 11 November 1988, in Gunukh, Charodinsky District, Dagestan) is a Russian-Azerbaijani former freestyle wrestler.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-en}

## Career

He won the world title at Istanbul in 2011 and the European title in 2019, and finished first at the World Championships while also taking a bronze at the European Championships earlier in his career.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-en} At the London Olympics of 2012 he won the gold medal in the 84 kg event, defeating Jaime Espinal of Puerto Rico.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-en} At Rio 2016 he won bronze in the 86 kg class, beating Bi Shengfeng and Zbigniew Baranowski, losing to Abdulrashid Sadulaev, and defeating Pedro Ceballos in the repechage.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-en} He also took bronze at the 2017 Islamic Solidarity Games in Baku.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-en} The Arabic article gives his origin as Avar and his residence as Makhachkala, Russia.{cite:wikipedia-sharif-sharifov-ar}
