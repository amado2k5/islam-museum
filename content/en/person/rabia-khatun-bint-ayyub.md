## Summary
Rabi'a Khatun bint Ayyub, sister of Sultan Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub and wife of al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Muzaffar al-Din Kukburi, lord of Irbil, died at Damascus and was buried in her madrasa, endowed for the Hanbalis, at the foot of Mount Qasyun. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Her house
Ibn Khallikan said: she lived to see more than fifty kings among her male relatives — her brothers and their sons — besides those who were not kings: Irbil belonged to her husband, Mosul to her daughter's sons, Khilat to her brother al-Malik al-Awhad Najm al-Din Ayyub's pupil, the Jazira lands to her nephew al-Ashraf, the Syrian lands to her brothers' sons, and Egypt, the Hijaz and Yemen to her brothers and their sons. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
