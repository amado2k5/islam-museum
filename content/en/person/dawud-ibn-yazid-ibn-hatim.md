## Overview
Dawud ibn Yazid ibn Hatim al-Muhallabi, who governed the frontier of Sind after Umar ibn Hafs Hazarmard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Sind
According to al-Baladhuri, Abu al-Samma al-Mutaghallib, a client of Kinda, was with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
