## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Ali ibn Munjid al-Saruji, Taqi al-Din, was a poet whom al-Zirikli describes as a man of merit and literary culture. He was born in Saruj and died in Cairo. Al-Zirikli gives his birth as 627 AH, 1230 CE, and his death as 693 AH, 1294 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 105}.

## His poetry
Al-Zirikli names him as the author of the verses that open with a plea to be granted the beloved's union because the time for it has come, beginning in Arabic «أنعم بوصلك لي فهذا وقته» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 105}.
