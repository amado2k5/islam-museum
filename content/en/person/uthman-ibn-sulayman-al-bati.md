## Overview
Abu Amr Uthman ibn Sulayman al-Bati, a jurist from Kufa who moved to Basra. He took from [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], and died in the year 143 AH {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}.

## His nisba
The editor's note says that his nisba "al-Bati" is to the sale of the "butut", a coarse cloak {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.49}.
