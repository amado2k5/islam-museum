## Overview

Abbas Hasan is a grammarian and the author of [[work/al-nahw-al-wafi|al-Nahw al-Wafi]], a book of Arabic grammar. A research summary supplied to the museum, a list of sixty of the most-cited works on the Arabic language, gives his death as 1399 AH, 1979 CE {cite:wikipedia-al-nahw-al-wafi-ar}.

## What is not known

This page says nothing of his birth, upbringing, studies or teachers, because the summary does not give them. The founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026; the museum did not open the page the summary names and has not read the author's books.
