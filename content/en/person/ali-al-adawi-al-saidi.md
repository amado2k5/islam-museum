## Summary
Ali ibn Ahmad al-Adawi al-Sa'idi, a Maliki jurist and scholar of verification, born in Upper Egypt, who came to Cairo and studied at al-Azhar. Al-Banani, al-Dardir and al-Dasuqi studied under him. The author of Shajarat al-nur al-zakiyya called him 'imam of the verifiers'. His works include glosses on Kifayat al-talib, on al-Zurqani's commentary on Khalil, and on al-Kharashi's commentary. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1630}
