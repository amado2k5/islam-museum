# Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah

Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah (29 June 1926 – 15 January 2006) was the thirteenth Emir of Kuwait, reigning from 31 December 1977 until his death, and the third Emir of the country since its independence. His reign covered Kuwait's oil-boom decades, an assassination attempt, and the Iraqi occupation of 1990–1991.{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-en}

## Early life and education

He was born at Kuwait City, the third son of Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah. He was educated at the Al-Mubarakiya, Al-Ahmediya and Al-Sharqiya schools and then privately tutored in English, Arabic, religion and the sciences.{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikidata-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah}

## Offices before the emirate

He entered government in finance, serving as minister of finance and economy from 1962 to 1965, and then as prime minister from 30 November 1965 to 8 February 1978, the office from which he succeeded to the throne at the end of 1977.{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-en}

## Reign: attack, occupation and liberation

In 1985 he survived an attack on his motorcade, part of the bombing campaign Kuwait faced in those years. On 2 August 1990 Iraq invaded; within hours Jaber and his government had withdrawn to Saudi Arabia and ran the country from exile through the occupation, while his brother Fahad Al-Ahmed was killed at Dasman Palace. Kuwait was liberated in February 1991, and Jaber returned in March 1991 to a country beginning reconstruction.{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-en}{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-ar}

## Death and succession

He died at Kuwait City on 15 January 2006 and was buried at Sulaibikhat Cemetery. Crown Prince Saad Al-Abdullah succeeded him but abdicated within days on health grounds, and Sabah Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah — Jaber's half-brother and his former prime minister — became Emir.{cite:source/wikipedia-jaber-al-ahmad-al-sabah-en}
