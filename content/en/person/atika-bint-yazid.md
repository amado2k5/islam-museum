## Overview
Atika bint Yazid ibn Mu'awiya, her mother Umm Kulthum bint Abd Allah ibn Amir; she was wife to Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan and the dearest of people to him. She grew angry with him, and when Amr ibn Bilal's stratagem won him back her favour, he said 'stay where you are'.

## In Kahhala's A'lam al-Nisa'
Kahhala describes her as a lady of distinction, glory and beauty who held a high place in Umayyad hearts; she was the mother of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, and her husband Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan loved her deeply. He gives the story of her anger with him, her closing the door against him, and Umar ibn Bilal al-Asadi's stratagem to win her over. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.220} He relates that Abd al-Malik asked her to formally assign her inheritance from her father to their two sons; he gathered witnesses, among them Rawh ibn Zinba' al-Judhami, and she had them witness that she had given her wealth as alms to the poor of Abu Sufyan's family. Rawh told Abd al-Malik he had "left Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan sitting in the chancery" — meaning she matched her grandfather in shrewdness — and Abd al-Malik, angry at first, let her be. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.223} He adds that she stood within the forbidden degrees to twelve caliphs. {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v3 p.223}
