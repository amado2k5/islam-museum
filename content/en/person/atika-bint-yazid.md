## Overview
Atika, daughter of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya, whose mother was Umm Kulthum daughter of Abd Allah ibn Amir. She was the wife of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan and the most beloved of people to him, as al-Mas'udi relates. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.110-111}

He relates that she was angry with him, and then she was content through a trick devised by Amr ibn Bilal. When news of it reached her she said he had deceived her. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.110-111}

She was the mother of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.185}
