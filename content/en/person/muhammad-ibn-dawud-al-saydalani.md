## Overview
A Shafii jurist and traditionist of Marw, called al-Saydalani after the perfume trade and also al-Dawudi after his father; he left writings. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5153}

## The entry in the Mawsu'a
The appendix gives his death as 427 AH, about 1035 CE. The source gives no birth date and no titles of his works. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5153}
