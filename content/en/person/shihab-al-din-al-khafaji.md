## Overview
Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn 'Umar, Abu al-'Abbas al-Khafaji of Cairo, a Hanafi judge and a prolific author on literature and language, the leading writer of his time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10357}

## Works
His works include Nasim al-Riyad, a commentary on al-Qadi 'Iyad's al-Shifa'; 'Inayat al-Qadi wa-Kifayat al-Radi, a gloss on al-Baydawi's Quran commentary; and Shifa' al-'Alil on foreign words in the speech of the Arabs. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10357}
