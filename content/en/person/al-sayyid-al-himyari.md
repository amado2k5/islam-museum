## Overview
Al-Sayyid al-Himyari, the poet of the Family of the Prophet, who wrote many verses about al-Husayn ibn Ali. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.75-76}

## Reports about him
Al-Mas'udi says that his transmitter Isma'il al-Sahir said that he died only holding the doctrine of the Kaysaniyya. Ali ibn Muhammad al-Nawfali denied that the poem "Tajafarta bi-Ism Allah" was his. The two attributions are disputed among the transmitters. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.75-76}
