## Overview
A poet of the [[group/banu-minqar|Banu Minqar]] of the [[group/banu-tamim|Banu Tamim]], who came in the [[event/delegation-of-banu-tamim|delegation of the Banu Tamim]] to the Prophet in 9 AH. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206}

## In the delegation of Banu Tamim
Ibn Ishaq names him among the notables of the delegation, and Ibn Hisham traces him to the Banu Minqar ibn Ubayd ibn al-Harith ibn Amr ibn Ka'b ibn Sa'd ibn Zayd Manat ibn Tamim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/207} The editors establish that one copy of the originals makes him and al-Habhab one person. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/206} He was the youngest of the people, so they left him with their camels. [[person/qays-ibn-asim|Qays ibn Asim]], who hated him, said to the Prophet that he was a young lad among their baggage and belittled him; the Prophet gave him what he had given the others. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/212} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/213} When he heard Qays's words he satirised him in [[work/amr-ibn-al-ahtam-satire-of-qays-ibn-asim|two verses]]: he reviled him before the Messenger and was not right nor correct, and the lordship of his people is broad while the lordship of Qays is narrow. Ibn Hisham left out a third verse because it was obscene. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/213}

## In the caliphate of Umar
Al-Baladhuri relates that he went to Umar ibn al-Khattab from Rishahr with the good news of a conquest. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.408}
