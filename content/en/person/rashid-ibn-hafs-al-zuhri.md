## Summary
Rashid ibn Hafs al-Zuhri, a grandson of Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf; Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2060}

## Works
Ibn Abi Hatim reports his father as calling him unknown, and his name is drawn from the book of al-Waqidi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2060}
