Al-Mut'im ibn Adi ibn Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf, chief and shaykh of Banu Nawfal in the Jahiliyya; he was one of the five men who rose to annul the boycott document against Banu Hashim — the one who went to the document in the Ka'ba to tear it and found the woodworm had eaten all of it but God's name. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He has a second famous stand in the Sira: he is the one who gave the Prophet protection after his return from Ta'if, when the lords of Quraysh refused to admit him into Mecca — al-Mut'im and his sons came out armed until the Prophet entered the Ka'ba, made tawaf and prayed, then entered his house under al-Mut'im's protection. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
