## Summary
Essam Heggy is an Egyptian planetary scientist working in the United States, specialised in ground-penetrating radar to sound the depths of celestial bodies and detect subsurface water. His work links space exploration to water questions in the Arab world. {cite:wikipedia-essam-heggy}

## Life and upbringing
He was born in Egypt in 1975, studied geophysics and planetary science through his graduate degrees, then joined American and European research and space institutions. {cite:wikipedia-essam-heggy}

## Career and works
He contributed to radar data analysis on missions such as Phoenix to Mars and Rosetta to a comet, and worked on NASA lunar and planetary exploration projects. On Earth he applied remote-sensing techniques to map groundwater aquifers in the arid regions of the Middle East and North Africa. {cite:wikipedia-essam-heggy}

## Legacy
Heggy is known for bridging space science and water-resource questions, and he appears in Arabic media speaking about science and the need to invest in research in the Arab region. {cite:wikipedia-essam-heggy}
