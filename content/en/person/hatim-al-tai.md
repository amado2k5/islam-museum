## Overview
Hatim al-Ta'i, the proverbial figure for generosity. Al-Mas'udi reports that his grave is at Baqqa, on the highest mountain of a valley called al-Khabil. On the right of the grave are four stone maidens, and on its left four, with their hair loosened, as if they were mourners. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 474-476}

Al-Mas'udi reports that the jinn's lamentation over him can be heard at night until dawn. He transmits the account of Abu al-Bakhtari, who called to the grave, "Aqran!", and Hatim slaughtered his camel for him. Then Adi ibn Hatim brought him a camel in its place, in keeping with what he had seen in a dream. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 474-476}

He gives the words of Salim ibn Zarara al-Ghatafani, that his grave was a place where guests were hosted, and that no one had ever passed by that grave on horseback without stopping. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 474-476}

## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's «Difaf al-Kawthar»
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] mentions Hatim al-Ta'i in the hadith of the first by whom the Fire is lit. He says generosity is still named after him today, "Hatimi generosity", that he died outside Islam, and that his son [[person/adi-ibn-hatim|Adi ibn Hatim]], a Companion, asked the Prophet about his father. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep03 00:10:00} Adi described his father as hosting guests, feeding people and relieving the distressed, and said Hatim once saw his son beating his dog and stopped him, because the dog guided guests to him with its barking. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep03 00:10:30} Al-Heweny explains that desert travellers found their way either by fires lit on hilltops or by the barking of dogs. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep03 00:11:00} He says the Prophet's answer was that Adi's father wanted something and attained it, namely renown among people. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep03 00:12:00} Episode 10 of the archive repeats episode 3 with these same words. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep10 00:10:30}
