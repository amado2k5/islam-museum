## Summary
Kayqubad I was a Seljuk sultan of Rum, under whom [[polity/rum-seljuk-sultanate|the sultanate]] reached its greatest extent; the record gives his death as 1237, marked as traditional {cite:wikidata}. The museum links him to [[place/anatolia|Anatolia]] only broadly, and the record holds no further verified detail yet, so none is added here.
