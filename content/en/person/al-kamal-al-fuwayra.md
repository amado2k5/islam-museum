## Overview
Al-Kamal al-Fuwayra, musnid of Iraq and shaykh of the Mustansiriyya; he read the readings on al-Fakhr al-Mawsili and the highest isnad in readings and hadith ended with him; he died in Dhu'l-Hijja at ninety-eight — senility had set in. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 765-766}
