## Overview
Al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir: king of al-Hira who entrusted his family and wealth to Hani' ibn Mas'ud then went to Kisra, who imprisoned him until he died — and his deposit was the cause of the day of Dhi Qar.

## Life
Kisra grew angry with al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir, king of al-Hira, over slander contrived by Zayd ibn Adi al-Abadi, and sent for him {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya}.

Al-Nu'man went out until he alighted in secret on Hani' ibn Mas'ud, chief of the house of Shayban, and deposited with him his family and wealth; then he went to Kisra, who imprisoned him until he died, and appointed over al-Hira in his place Iyas ibn Qubaysa al-Ta'i {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Al-Nu'man was a king who entrusted his family and wealth to a friend before going to an angry king; he was imprisoned and died, and his friend defended the trust with the sword.
