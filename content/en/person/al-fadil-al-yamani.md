## Overview
Yahya ibn al-Qasim ibn Amr ibn Ali ibn Khalid al-Alawi, Imad al-Din al-Yamani al-San'ani, known as al-Fadil al-Yamani and al-Fadil al-Alawi: an exegete and man of letters of the Shafi'is of Yemen, a native of Sanaa, who lived from 680 to after 750 AH (1281–after 1349 CE). He visited Damascus, Baghdad and Khurasan; Salah al-Din al-Safadi met him in Damascus in 749, and he died on the return leg of his journey in the direction of al-Lajab in Yemen — where he is called al-Shawlabi — though it is also said he died and was buried at al-Sharha, likewise in Yemen. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.162}

His works include Tuhfat al-Ashraf fi Kashf Ghawamid al-Kashshaf and Durar al-Asdaf fi Hall Uqd al-Kashshaf, both on al-Zamakhshari's commentary; a commentary on al-Isfara'ini's Lubab in grammar; and verse. {cite:al-aalam v.8 p.162}
