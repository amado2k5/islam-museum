## Overview
'Umar al-Suhrawardi — model of the people of tawhid and shaykh of the gnostics in the eyes of the Path; shaykh of Baghdad's shaykhs, with whom ended the headship of training the murids and the shaykhdom of Iraq; he kept company with his uncle Abu'l-Najib al-Suhrawardi and Shaykh 'Abd al-Qadir, and heard Abu'l-Qasim b. Fadhlan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 153-155}

## His assembly and death
His preaching assembly was accepted — men swooned there and a great throng repented; his works include 'Awarif al-ma'arif, the most famous. He made the hajj often, and the shaykhs of the Path wrote him their requests for fatwas on their states. In his last years he went blind and bedridden yet missed none of his litanies; he died at Baghdad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 153-155}
