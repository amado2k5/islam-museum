## Overview
Daysim ibn Ibrahim al-Kurdi, a commander who contested Azerbaijan with al-Marzuban ibn Muhammad in the fourth century AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226}

## The contest over Azerbaijan
Ibn Khaldun says that he disputed with Lashkari ibn al-Marzuban and his sons, and the people of Azerbaijan summoned him to fight them. He seized the region and overcame the Hamdanids in its mines. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226} See [[person/marzuban-ibn-muhammad|al-Marzuban ibn Muhammad]].

## The defeat
The Daylam who were with him, who had come from the army of Washmgir, had been won over by al-Marzuban and went over to him. Daysim besieged him at Tabriz, then fled to Ardabil and then to Armenia, where he settled with its ruler Hajiq ibn al-Dirani. Al-Marzuban's rule was established in 330 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226}
