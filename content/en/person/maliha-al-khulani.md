## Overview
Maliha al-Khulani, a pupil of Babak ibn Bahram, and Babak was a pupil of Shili of the Mughtasila. Followers known as the Khulaniyya are attributed to him. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.477-478}

## His place in the chain
Ibn al-Nadim places him in the third generation of this sect. The rest of his biography needs an additional source.
