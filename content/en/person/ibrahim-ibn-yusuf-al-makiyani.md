## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Yusuf ibn Maymun, Abu Ishaq al-Bahili, known as al-Makiyani, the scholar of Balkh in his time and a leading Hanafi of his day. He attended Abu Yusuf until he excelled, and transmitted from Sufyan ibn Uyayna and Isma'il ibn Ulayya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16636} al-Nasai judged him trustworthy and Ibn Hibban counted him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16636}
