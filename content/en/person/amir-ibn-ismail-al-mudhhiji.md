## Overview
Amir ibn Isma'il al-Madhhiji, the man who killed Marwan ibn Muhammad at Buwsir on the night of Sunday, three nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 132 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.233}

## The report
When he wanted to enter the church where the daughters of Marwan were, a servant stopped him. The servant said that Marwan had ordered him, if he were killed, to strike the necks of his daughters and women. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.233}
