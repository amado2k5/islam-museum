## Summary
al-Mustanis bil-Haqq al-Mustawhish min al-Khalq is a laqab for a man whose name the text does not give. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 544, sequence 545, on pages 167 and 168. ## Ubayd's question
Uthman ibn Muhammad al-Muthmali relates from Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Ahmad that Ubayd al-Basri said: I asked a man at [the place] what made him sit in that spot; he said: what you ask about, if you seek it you will not reach it, and if you reach it you will not grasp it. I said: tell me what it is. He said: my knowledge that sitting with God absorbs the bliss of the gardens. I asked: how? He said: alas, I thought my soul had triumphed and that I had fled from creation; I find myself a liar in my station; had I been a true lover of God, no one would have seen me. I said: do you not know that the lovers are God's vicegerents on earth, at home with His creation, moved by Him to obedience? He gave a cry and said: O deceived one, if you had smelt the scent of love, and what lies behind the heart of nearness, you would need to see nothing beyond what you have seen. Then he said: O heaven and earth, bear witness that the remembrance of Paradise and Hell has never occurred to my heart; if I am truthful, let me die. I heard no word from him after that. Then a group came and asked: what has become of the young man? I gave an evasive answer; they said: go back, God has taken him. I prayed over him with them and asked who the man was. They said: his heart was like the heart of Abraham the Friend; did you not see him say that the remembrance of Paradise and Hell never crossed his heart? Has anyone been like that except Abraham the Friend? I asked who they were; they said: we are the seven distinguished substitutes. I asked them to teach me something; they said: you do not wish to be known, and you do not wish to be known as one who does not wish to be known. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/168}
