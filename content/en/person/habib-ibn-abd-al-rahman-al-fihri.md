## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that Habib ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Fihri was put in charge of the Maghrib frontier after Ilyas ibn Habib, that is, after his brother. Then the Berbers and the Ibadi Kharijites prevailed over him. He was the son of [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
