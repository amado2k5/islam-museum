## Summary
Son of Abdallah al-Ghalib; the last challenger of his uncle Abd al-Malik for the throne {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 258-265}.

## The Christian dependence
Continuing his father's pro-Christian policy he signed a trade treaty with England, then pledged Morocco's entire coast to [[person/sebastian-i-portugal|Sebastian]] for his uncle's expulsion; he stayed four months in Ceuta and joined the invasion with a few hundred men {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 258-260}.

## The ulema's answer and the end
He wrote rallying Morocco's people to the Christians; the ulema's reply exposed his enlisting infidels against Muslims. He drowned in the Wadi al-Makhazin river on the day of battle {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 261-265}.
