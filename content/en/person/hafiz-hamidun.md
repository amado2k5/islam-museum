## Summary
Hafiz Hamidun is a Malaysian nasheed singer, composer, producer and entrepreneur born in Johor in 1984, one of the leading figures of contemporary Southeast Asian Islamic music; The Muslim 500 lists him among influential artists. {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun} {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Beginnings
He first recorded as part of the Malaysian nasheed group Nada before going solo in the late 2000s; albums such as Hafiz and Zikir Terapi Diri built him a mass audience in Malaysia and Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Music
His songs pair Malay devotional lyrics — zikir, praise and moral counsel — with polished contemporary pop production, a formula that carried Islamic music into Malaysian charts and streaming playlists; signature tracks include Zikir Terapi Diri and Astaghfirullah. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Business
He founded the production company and label HAFIZHAMIDUN production ⁦(HHP)⁩ and expanded into trading and food businesses, while mentoring younger Malaysian nasheed performers. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Assessment
He is credited with professionalising Malaysian Islamic pop — high production values, touring and merchandising — and with keeping zikir-based song commercially current; the museum reports assessments attributed, without verdict. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun} {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun}


## Zikir Terapi Diri
His 2019 single Zikir Terapi Diri — a devotional zikir track built for personal listening — became one of the most-streamed Malaysian religious songs, crossing from nasheed audiences to mainstream playlists and establishing zikir-as-single as a commercial format. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Entrepreneurship
Beyond his label HHP he has built businesses in food, cosmetics and fashion, and collaborates with nasheed peers on compilations — modelling the devotional artist as entrepreneur across Southeast Asia's Muslim-market creative economy. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## Recognition
He has collected Malaysian music-industry honours including Anugerah Juara Lagu recognition and awards for best Islamic album, and headlines national Ramadan and Prophet's birthday broadcasting. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun} {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun}

## From UNIC to solo
Born in Kuching in 1984 and raised in the nasyid group UNIC — which he led for more than a decade — he launched a solo career after 2014 that made him the leading male voice of Malaysian devotional pop. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## HHP and production
Through his label HHP Entertainment he produces other nasheed artists and releases his own catalogue, functioning as both star and record executive inside the Malaysian religious-music industry. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## The zikir format
His post-2019 releases turned repetitive zikir phrases into chart singles — a format critics link to the rise of religious 'mood' streaming in Southeast Asia — and brought devotional listening into mainstream chart conversations. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun} {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun}

## The Malaysian nasheed industry
Hafiz Hamidun's career spans the two generations of Malaysian devotional music: the nasyid group era of the 2000s, when he led the group UNIC, and the streaming-era solo career that followed, when his label HHP Entertainment and the single 'Zikir Terapi Diri' redefined the market. His work as performer and record executive — producing other nasheed artists through his label while releasing his own catalogue — made him the central institutional figure of the Malaysian devotional-music industry of his era. {cite:wikipedia-hafiz-hamidun}

## The Muslim 500 listing
The Muslim 500 lists him among the most influential Muslims in arts, citing his position as Malaysia's leading male devotional artist, the commercial success of his Zikir format and his institutional role in the country's religious-music industry. {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun}



## Standing in the field

Within Malaysian nasheed, Hafiz Hamidun stands as Malaysia’s leading male devotional artist and the label head behind the Zikir single format — a position that explains the scale of public attention the career has attracted. The Muslim 500's listing places Hafiz Hamidun among the most influential Muslims of the present generation in this field, reflecting both the size of the audience the work reaches and the extent to which the figure has come to represent it beyond specialist circles. {cite:muslim500-hafiz-hamidun}
