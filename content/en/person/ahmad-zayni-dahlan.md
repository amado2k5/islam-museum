## Overview
Ahmad ibn Zayni Dahlan was a Meccan jurist and historian {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. He was born in Mecca and held the office of ifta and teaching there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. In his days the first printing press in Mecca was set up, and some of his books were printed on it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}. He died in Medina; al-A'lam gives his birth and death as 1232 AH (1817 CE) and 1304 AH (1886 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his writings, all of them printed, "al-Futuhat al-Islamiyya" in two volumes; "al-Jadawil al-Mardiyya fi Tarikh al-Duwal al-Islamiyya"; "Khulasat al-Kalam fi Umara' al-Balad al-Haram"; "al-Fath al-Mubin fi Fada'il al-Khulafa' al-Rashidin wa-Ahl al-Bayt al-Tahirin"; "al-Sira al-Nabawiyya"; and "Risala fi al-Radd ala al-Wahhabiyya", a treatise whose title announces a reply to the Wahhabiyya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 111}.
