## Summary
Ja'far ibn Dinar al-Khayyat was an Abbasid military commander who led the left wing of al-Mu'tasim's army in the Amorium campaign of 223 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 450}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi reports in Muruj al-Dhahab that when al-Mu'tasim camped west of the Tigris two nights before the end of Jumada al-Ula in 223 AH, bound for the Byzantine lands, he arrayed his army thus: the vanguard under Ashinas al-Turki followed by Muhammad ibn Ibrahim, the right wing under Itakh al-Turki, the left wing under Ja'far ibn Dinar al-Khayyat, the rear under Bugha al-Kabir followed by Dinar ibn Abd Allah, and the centre under Ajif. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 450} Ja'far ibn Dinar thus stood among the commanders of that great army whose size people disputed — five hundred thousand said those who gave the larger count, two hundred thousand the smaller — and with it al-Mu'tasim marched from the Syrian frontier until he invested and took Amorium. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 450}
