## Overview
Ja'far ibn Dinar al-Khayyat, an amir who commanded the left wing of the army of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] in the raid of Amorium in 223 AH, according to al-Mas'udi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.450}
