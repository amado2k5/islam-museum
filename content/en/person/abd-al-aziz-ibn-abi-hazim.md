## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Hazim, a Medinan jurist and hadith scholar (d. 184 AH). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3478}

## Biography
He narrated from his father, Suhayl ibn Abi Salih and Hisham ibn Urwa; Ibn Mahdi and Ibn Wahb narrated from him. Ahmad ibn Hanbal considered him the most learned in law in Medina after Malik. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3478}
