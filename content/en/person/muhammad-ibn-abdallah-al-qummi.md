## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah al-Qummi, a governor whom al-Mutawakkil appointed over the mines of Egypt, the Qulzum, the road to the Hijaz and the pilgrims of Egypt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## War with the Buja
Al-Baladhuri says that he fought the Buja with a small number of men. He hung bells on his horses, their camels took fright, and their king was killed. In 241 AH he made peace with his successor on a tribute and an annual payment. He is the commander of [[event/al-qummi-buja-war|al-Qummi's war with the Buja]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In the report of al-Maqrizi he returned to Baghdad with their king, Ali Baba. Then Sa'd the servant appointed him over the Buja and the road to Mecca; he camped at Aswan and died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His approximate date of death (around 843 CE) is derived.
