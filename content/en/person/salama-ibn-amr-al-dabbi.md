## Overview
Salama ibn Amr ibn Dirar al-Dabbi, one of the men of the [[period/rashidun|Rashidun]]. It is said that he was al-Bara' ibn Azib. This attribution needs an additional source {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

[[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] sent him at the head of the army of Rayy after Urwa's departure, and directed him toward Qumis. It is said he was Sulayman ibn Amr. The people of the country did not resist, and they opened the gates of al-Damghan. He is mentioned in [[event/conquest-of-rayy-qumis|the conquest of Rayy and Qumis]] {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
