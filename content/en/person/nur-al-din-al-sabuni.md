## Overview
Ahmad ibn Mahmud ibn Abi Bakr, Nur al-Din al-Sabuni al-Bukhari, was one of the Hanafi scholars of theology (kalam). He was born and died in Bukhara. His nisba comes from making or selling soap. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 580 AH, 1184 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 p. 253}.

## Works
Al-Zirikli lists al-Bidaya min al-Kifaya, in manuscript, on the foundations of religion, which he abridged from his book al-Kifaya fi l-Hidaya, in manuscript among the endowment collections of Baghdad; the latter is called the Creed of al-Sabuni {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 1 pp. 253-254}.
