## Summary
Muaz Habib (ሙዓዝ ሀቢብ) is an Ethiopian vocal artist who performs menzuma and neshida — Islamic devotional song — in Amharic, with works in Somali as well, and is the leading figure of contemporary Ethiopian neshida on the platforms. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib} {cite:vidiq-muaz-habib} His official YouTube channel (@MuazHabibofficial) stood at 608,000 subscribers and 192,773,882 views across 332 videos as counted in November 2026 — dated platform figures, not facts the museum measures itself. {cite:vidiq-muaz-habib}

## Beginnings and career
His YouTube channel began on 18 April 2020 and grew steadily past six hundred thousand subscribers by the autumn of 2026, adding some seven thousand subscribers in October 2026 alone. {cite:vidiq-muaz-habib} The channel's Amharic description calls it the official channel of the munshid Muaz Habib, and its keywords name iNaya Records. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib}

## Programmes and works
His channel description lists what it publishes: new neshida albums, distinctive cover works, recitations and a variety of religious programmes. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib} Among his most-viewed uploads are the menzuma "Resul Jalele" at about fifteen million views, "Nur Amba — Warida 6" at about eleven million, and a cover neshida from February 2025 at about ten million. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib} His Somali-language neshida "Ya Nural Ceyni" has drawn close to eight million views, extending his audience to Somali speakers in Ethiopia and beyond. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib}

## Impact and audience
He remains active on the channel: its most recent uploads read in November 2026 date from four to eight months earlier, and some of them gathered millions of views within a few months. {cite:youtube-muaz-habib} By his channel's figures, Muaz Habib stands at the head of Ethiopian devotional singers on YouTube, in a scene where scholars' lectures circulate mostly through broadcaster and aggregator channels rather than their own. {cite:vidiq-muaz-habib}
