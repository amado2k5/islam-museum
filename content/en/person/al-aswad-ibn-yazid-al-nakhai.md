## Overview
Abu Amr al-Aswad ibn Yazid ibn Qays al-Nakha'i, one of the jurists of Kufa among the Successors, brother of Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid and nephew of Alqama. Al-Shirazi says he died in 75 AH, and that Aisha said there was no man in Iraq more honoured by her than him. The editor notes another version of this phrase in the printed text. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.42}

He learned knowledge from [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}
