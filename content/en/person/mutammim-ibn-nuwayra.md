## Overview
Mutammim ibn Nuwayra, a poet of Banu Hanzala ibn Malik of Tamim, and the brother of [[person/malik-ibn-nuwayra|Malik ibn Nuwayra]]. He became known for lamenting his brother, and entered upon [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] after his killing. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.137-138}

## With Umar
Al-Baladhuri relates that Umar asked him about his grief, and he answered that he had wept for his brother for a year. Then he described his brother as generous, and recited to Umar his elegy, which begins "We were like the companions of Jadhima". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.137-138}

When Umar said that if he had been good at poetry he would have lamented his brother Zayd, Mutammim replied that the two situations are not equal. Umar said that no one had consoled him better than Mutammim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.137-138}

The killing of his brother is the subject of [[event/killing-of-malik-ibn-nuwayra|the killing of Malik ibn Nuwayra]].
