## Overview
Murra al-Hamadhani, a transmitter who relates in al-Baladhuri a report from Ali ibn Abi Talib: whoever dislikes fighting with him with Mu'awiya should take his stipend and go out to the Daylam to fight them. Murra says: "I was among the elite; we took our stipends and went out to the Daylam, and we were four or five thousand." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He has a connection to [[event/conquest-of-qazwin|the conquest of Qazvin]].
