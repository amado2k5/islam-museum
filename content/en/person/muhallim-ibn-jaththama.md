## Overview
A man of the Banu Layth who was in [[event/raid-of-ibn-abi-hadrad-on-batn-idam|the expedition of Ibn Abi Hadrad to Idam]]. He charged at [[person/amir-ibn-al-adbat-al-ashjai|Amir ibn al-Adbat]] after the latter had given the greeting of Islam, and killed him "for something that was between them", and took his camel and goods. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/275}

## The blood-money
When the blood-debt was contested at Hunayn and the blood-money was accepted, he came in a garment in which he had prepared himself to be killed. The Prophet said to him, three times: "O God, do not forgive Muhallim ibn Jathama." He rose and caught his tears in his cloak. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/276}

Ibn Ishaq relates from al-Hasan al-Basri that the Prophet said to him: "You gave him safety in God, then you killed him." He died seven days later, and the earth cast him out until his people piled stones over him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/277}

## Notes on the report
- His name is "Muhallim" in Ibn Hisham and in others than Ibn Ishaq, and "Mulajjam" in Ibn Ishaq as he related it on the authority of Ziyad. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/277}
- The report of his death and of the ground's rejection comes as a transmission from al-Hasan al-Basri from someone Ibn Ishaq does not name. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/277}
