## Summary
Commander of the Ottoman garrison at Tlemcen {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.

## The stand
When [[person/muhammad-al-shaykh-al-sadi|Muhammad al-Shaykh]]'s army entered Tlemcen unawares, his garrison held against the Sa'dis until the attack was contained {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.
