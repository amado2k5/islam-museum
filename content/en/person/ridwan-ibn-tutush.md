## Overview
Ridwan ibn Taj al-Dawla [[person/tutush|Tutush]] ibn Alp Arslan. His brother [[person/duqaq-ibn-tutush|Duqaq]] was in Damascus, and the two disputed the rule {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## Ruler of Aleppo
Ibn Khaldun reports that his father had instructed his companions, before the battle, to obey Ridwan. When Tutush was killed at Rayy in 488 AH, the news reached Ridwan at Hit, and he returned to Aleppo {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

Jinah al-Dawla al-Hasan ibn Iftikin rose in the citadel of Aleppo during the night and proclaimed his slogan, and the khutba was read for him on its pulpits and in its districts. Ibn Khaldun mentions that he managed his state well {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.

## Nisibin
He besieged Nisibin together with al-Albi in 499 AH {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}.
