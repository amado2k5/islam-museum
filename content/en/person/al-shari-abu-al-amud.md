## Overview
al-Shari Abu'l-Amud, who rose in the region of Yemen, al-Bawazij and Mosul in al-Muntasir's days, ruled and grew strong among the Rabi'a and Kurds who flocked to him; an army under Sima al-Turki was sent, captured him and brought him in, and al-Muntasir passed judgement on him.
