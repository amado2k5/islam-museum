## Overview
Bugha al-Shirabi, one of the Turkish commanders. Al-Tabari says that he went out with Wasif to the troops demanding their stipends in 253 AH. Al-Tabari gives his killing in 254 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.374} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.379} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.380}
