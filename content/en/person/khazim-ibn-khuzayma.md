## Overview
Khazim ibn Khuzayma al-Tamimi, an amir of al-Mansur, and the son of [[person/khuzayma-ibn-khazim|Khuzayma ibn Khazim]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Tabaristan
Al-Mansur sent him with Ruh ibn Hatim al-Muhallabi to Tabaristan, when its people rebelled and killed the Muslims. With them was their client Marzuq Abu al-Khasib, who devised the conquest (see [[event/marzuq-ispahbadh-ruse|the stratagem of Marzuq]]), and the Palace of al-Khasib in Kufa was attributed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
