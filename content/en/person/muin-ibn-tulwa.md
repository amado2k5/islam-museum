## Overview
Al-Mu'in ibn Tulwa, whose name was Uthman ibn Sa'id al-Fihri al-Misri, is described by Ibn al-Imad as the famous poet {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 685}.

## Death
Ibn al-Imad records among the deaths of 685 AH that he died in Rabi' I in Cairo at the age of eighty {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 685}; the year corresponds to 1286 CE by the museum's conversion (derived). The editor notes that his two sources, marked A and T, write the name Tulu, prefers Tulwa, and points to his entries in al-'Ibar, Fawat al-Wafayat and Husn al-Muhadara {cite:shadharat-dhahab vol. 7 p. 685}.
