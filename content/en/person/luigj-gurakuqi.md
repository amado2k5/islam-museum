Luigj Gurakuqi (19 February 1879 – 2 March 1925), who wrote under the pen names Jakin Shkodra and Lekë Gruda, was an Albanian writer and statesman from Shkodër.{cite:wikipedia-luigj-gurakuqi-en} Baptized the day after his birth into a Catholic family, he was educated by the Jesuits at the Saverian College and as a young man published verse in the Jesuit periodical of religious culture, The Messenger of the Sacred Heart.{cite:wikipedia-luigj-gurakuqi-sq}{cite:elsie-historical-dictionary-albania} In 1909 he became director of Albania's first teacher-training school at Elbasan; in November 1912 he travelled with Ismail Qemal to Vlorë, signed the declaration of independence and became minister of education in the first Albanian government.{cite:wikipedia-luigj-gurakuqi-en}{cite:elsie-historical-dictionary-albania}

In 1924 he backed the June Revolution and served as minister of finance in Fan Noli's government; after its fall he fled to Italy, where he was shot dead in a Bari café on 2 March 1925.{cite:wikipedia-luigj-gurakuqi-en} The university founded at Shkodër in 1991 now bears his name.{cite:elsie-historical-dictionary-albania}
