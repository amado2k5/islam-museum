## Overview
Al-Sahib Isma'il ibn Abbad al-Talaqani, vizier of the Buyids and owner of a well-known library {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.527-528}.

## His library
The author of Kashf al-Zunun quotes him as saying that his library held one hundred and seventeen thousand volumes. He also quotes Ibn Khallikan that he used to travel with a load of thirty camels of books on literature, and that once he obtained the Kitab al-Aghani he no longer needed the rest {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.527-528}.

## His works
The records attribute to him [[work/akhbar-al-wuzara-sahib|Akhbar al-Wuzara']] {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.527-528}.
