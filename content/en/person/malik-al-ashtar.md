## Overview
Malik ibn al-Harith al-Ashtar al-Nakha'i, one of the companions and commanders of Ali. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.637}

## In al-Baladhuri
In al-Baladhuri's account of the first crossing of the road, Abu Ubayda sent Maysara ibn Masruq al-Absi, who struck the Romans and those with them from the Arabized Ghassan, Tanukh and Iyad. Then Malik al-Ashtar came to join him as reinforcement from Abu Ubayda while he was at Antioch. The source says that the reports differ on this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} See [[event/first-crossing-of-darb-baghras|the first crossing of Darb Baghras]].

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi says that he is Malik ibn al-Harith al-Nakha'i, who set out from Kufa with two hundred men as part of the army of the rebels against Uthman. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.637}
