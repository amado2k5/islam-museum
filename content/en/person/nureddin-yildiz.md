## Summary
Nureddin Yıldız is a Turkish preacher and writer born in 1960 in Of, Trabzon, known for his lessons and fatwa answers on family, youth and ethics, and for leading the founding of the Sosyal Doku association and foundation. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr} {cite:inonu-university-nureddin-yildiz}

His YouTube channel counted 999,000 subscribers as of 11 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-nureddin-yildiz}

## Early life and education
Yıldız was born in Of, Trabzon, in 1960, and completed his memorisation of the Qur'an and his first religious education under his father, Hilmi Yıldız, by the age of ten, after moving to Istanbul. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

He attended the Gaziosmanpaşa Imam Hatip High School, then studied for a year at Marmara University's theology faculty before transferring to Umm al-Qura University in Mecca, where he graduated from the usul al-fiqh department. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

## Offices and work
During his high-school years he wrote weekly articles and translated news for various newspapers, and he continued preaching from pulpits. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

After returning to Istanbul he worked in education and civil-society organisations, started group training in memorisation and religious education, and wrote translated works. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

He led the founding of a number of civil-society organisations and still serves on the boards and advisory councils of many of them; his most recent founding was the Sosyal Doku association, and the İnönü University biography records that he also led the founding of the Sosyal Doku foundation. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr} {cite:inonu-university-nureddin-yildiz}

No founder edge from this record into the Sosyal Doku foundation's own record is written here; the foundation is catalogued separately as a group, and its channel's audience is not counted as his. {cite:inonu-university-nureddin-yildiz}

He has given hundreds of seminars and conferences on child and family education, youth awareness and Islamic ethics, writes regularly in Turkish magazines and newspapers, and his talks are broadcast on Turkish radio stations. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

Among the books the article lists are İşi Vaktinden Çok Olanlar, Medine Raporu, Hani Din Hepimizindi? and Hür Yürekli Genç, alongside translations of works of history and biography. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}

## Reported criticism
The article's criticism section records, on the basis of Turkish press reports, that some of his public statements drew criticism and that events of his were cancelled in several provinces in consequence; this museum reports that as the reporting stated it, in general terms, without adopting it and without a verdict. {cite:wikipedia-nureddin-yildiz-tr}
