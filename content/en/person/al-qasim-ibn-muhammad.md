## Summary
The eldest of the Prophet's sons by [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]; by him the Prophet was given the kunya Abu al-Qasim. He died young in Mecca — none of his sons lived {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 473}.

## His account
The Prophet had sons and daughters by Khadija; none of the sons lived, the eldest being al-Qasim, by whom the Prophet was given his kunya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 473; zad-al-maad}. His birth was in Mecca before prophethood on the well-known account, and he died young {cite:zad-al-maad}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet's first child was named al-Qasim, and by him he was nicknamed "Abu al-Qasim"; but he died young in Mecca.
