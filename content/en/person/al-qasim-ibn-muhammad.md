## Summary
Al-Qasim was the eldest of the Prophet's sons by [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]], and the one by whom the Prophet was given the kunya Abu al-Qasim. He died young in Mecca, and none of the Prophet's sons by her lived {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.473}.

## His account
The Prophet had sons and daughters by Khadija. None of the sons lived, and the eldest was al-Qasim, by whom the Prophet was given his kunya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.473; zad-al-maad}. He was born in Mecca before prophethood, according to the prevailing account, and died young {cite:zad-al-maad}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet's first son was named al-Qasim, and the Prophet came to be called "Abu al-Qasim" because of him. But the boy died young in Mecca.

## In the marriage to Khadija chapter (The Sealed Nectar, p.61)
The Sealed Nectar notes, in its chapter on the marriage, that all the Prophet's children by Khadija were born to him except Ibrahim. She first bore him al-Qasim, by whom he was given his kunya, and all his sons died in childhood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.61} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/190-191} {cite:fath-al-bari 7/507}.
