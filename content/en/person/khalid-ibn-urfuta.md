## Overview
Khalid ibn Urfuta, deputy of the Banu Zuhra in Sa'd's army at the battle of al-Qadisiyya. Sa'd made him his deputy over the army, staying in the palace of al-Mudhayyib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The pursuit
He then sent him with the cavalry of the pursuit, and they killed those they caught until they reached Bursa. He alighted with a man called Bistam, who honoured him, and the river was named the River of Bistam. Then he crossed the Sarat and caught up with Galenus (al-Jalinus). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
