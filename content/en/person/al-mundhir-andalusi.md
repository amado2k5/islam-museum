## Overview
Al-Mundhir ibn Muhammad, the seventh of the Umayyad amirs in [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], who ruled between 273 and 275 AH. He held power for two years less half a month, and died in the middle of Safar 275 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.388}

Al-Maqqari relates a verse that was said of him: "Through al-Mundhir ibn Muhammad the lands of al-Andalus were set right." {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.388}

The date of death in the museum's data is calculated from the Hijri date given, and comes from one source.
