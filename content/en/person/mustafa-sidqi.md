## Overview
Mustafa Sidqi Pasha, head of the chancery of the Rums, was a prosodist among the scholars of Rum (the Turks). He died in 1037 AH (1663 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.236}

He wrote in Arabic Manba' al-Surur fi Tafsil Ajza' al-Buhur on prosody, and a thirty-seven-leaf work Fawa'id Mus'ida expanding on it. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.236}
