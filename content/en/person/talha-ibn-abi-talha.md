## Overview
Talha ibn Abi Talha, standard-bearer of the Meccan side at Badr. Al-Sallabi reports that Ali killed him, and that al-Hajjaj ibn Ilat al-Sulami composed verses about it {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 pp.79-80}.

## Badr
In his account of the battle of Badr, al-Sallabi says the standard of the polytheists was held by Talha ibn Abi Talha, and that Ali killed him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.79}. The book then quotes verses by al-Hajjaj ibn Ilat al-Sulami on Ali's prowess at Badr {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.79}.
