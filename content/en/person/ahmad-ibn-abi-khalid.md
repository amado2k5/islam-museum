## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abi Khalid al-Ahwal, a secretary whom the caliph al-Ma'mun sent to raid Ushrusana with a large army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He took the short route described by Haydar ibn Kawus and camped at its city before the Turks arrived, so Kawus surrendered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

His birth, death and the rest of his life are not set out here and need an additional source.
