## Summary
Isam ibn Yazid ibn Ajlan, Abu Sa'id, called Khayr. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 664, sequence 665, on page 390. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}

## His message to al-Mahdi
He kept the company of Sufyan al-Thawri for thirteen years and was his messenger to the commander of the faithful, al-Mahdi, who offered him gifts and money, which he refused. He returned to Sufyan and said: shall we go to them? Sufyan said: do you think I fear their humiliation? I fear their honour. After Sufyan died he returned to Isfahan and settled there. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/390}
