## Overview
Dhaghfal ibn Handhala al-Shaybani, one of the teachers whom [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] chose to educate and refine his son [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid]]. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.169}

## In the Umayyad period
The book mentions this role as part of the emergence of a class of teachers in the Umayyad period. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.169}
