## Summary
A man of [[group/banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] who readied his arms before the conquest and threatened the Prophet's Companions; he fought at [[place/al-khandama|al-Khandama]], but when he saw the Muslim cavalry their mass scattered — he fled to his house, shut his door, and spoke his famous verses to his wife {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49-50}.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Himas ibn Qays was readying his arms before the conquest; his wife asked him: why prepare what I see? He said: for Muhammad and his Companions — I hope to serve you some of them. When the day of al-Khandama came and he witnessed the polytheists' rout, he fled until he entered his house and said to his wife: shut my door on me — and recited his verses: "Had you seen the day of al-Khandama / when Safwan fled and Ikrima fled / and the drawn swords were set to meet us / you would not have spoken the least word of blame" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49-50}.

## For young visitors
A fighter who boasted before the battle that he would defeat the army; but when he saw the Muslim cavalry he ran fast to his house and recited to his wife verses confessing the army's power and the speed of the rout.
