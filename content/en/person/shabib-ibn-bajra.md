## Overview
Shabib ibn Bajra, a man of Ashja' whom Ibn Muljam approached in the preparation against Ali. Al-Mas'udi records that Ibn Muljam persuaded him to strike at the worshippers — his blow fell at the door's threshold; he fled to his mount and his cousin Abd Allah ibn Najda killed him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}
