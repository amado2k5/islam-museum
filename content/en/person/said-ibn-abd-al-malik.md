## Overview
Sa'id ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, nicknamed "Sa'id al-Khayr" (Sa'id the Good). A son of the caliph [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] from the [[group/banu-umayya|Banu Umayya]], who showed asceticism {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The river attributed to him
Al-Baladhuri reports that the site of his river was a thicket with predators, and that al-Walid granted it to him. Some say it was Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz who granted it. He dug the river known by his name and developed the land there {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
