## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Da'im ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Ma'ali Nasir al-Din, known as Ibn Bint al-Maylaq and shortened to Ibn al-Maylaq, was an Egyptian judge, a Shafi'i Shadhili and an eloquent preacher. He died in 797 AH (1395 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.187}

Al-Zahir Barquq appointed him judge and he served with integrity and probity for twelve years, then was dismissed and humiliated after the Mintash rising, and withdrew from office until his death. His books include Hadi al-Qulub ila Liqa' al-Mahbub on Sufism, al-Anwar al-La'iha on the secrets of the Fatiha, an answer to one asking about God's greatest name, and an ode opening “man dhaqa ta'm sharab al-qawm yadrihi” commented by Ibn 'Allan. {cite:al-aalam v.6 p.187}
