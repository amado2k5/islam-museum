Al-As ibn Wa'il al-Sahmi, one of Mecca's notables; as the book relates, he bought a merchant of Zubayd's goods and withheld his due — and wresting the Zubaydi's right back from him was the cause of the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Fudul pact]] that vindicated the wronged over the wrongdoers. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
