## Overview
Abu Mansur Ibn al-Razzaz, Sa'id b. Muhammad of Baghdad — shaykh of the Shafi'is and teacher at the Nizamiyya; he read fiqh under al-Ghazali, As'ad al-Mayhani, al-Kiya al-Harasi, Abu Bakr al-Shashi and Abu Sa'd al-Mutawalli, transmitted from Rizq Allah al-Tamimi, excelled and led until the headship of the madhhab came to him — a man of bearing, gravity and majesty. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 601-602}

## His death
Born 462, he died in Dhu'l-Hijja 539 and was buried at Shaykh Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi's mausoleum. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 601-602}
