## Summary
Ghanim b. 'Asakir the ascetic, one of God's hidden, godfearing servants; born at Burin near Nablus, he settled at Jerusalem the year Sultan Salah al-Din delivered it from the Franks in 583, roamed Syria, saw the righteous, and preferred obscurity — a man of spiritual states. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 155}

## Seclusion and death
His son 'Abdallah said: he secluded himself beneath the Rock in the Solomon vaults for seven years, then kept company with Shaykh 'Abdallah al-Irmawi the rest of his life — the two living together; he died on the first of Sha'ban and was buried at the foot of Qasyun. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 155}
