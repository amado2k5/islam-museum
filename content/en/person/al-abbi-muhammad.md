## Overview
Muhammad ibn Khalfa ibn Umar al-Washtati al-Abbi al-Maliki, a Maliki scholar of usul from the scholars of the Maghrib. He was known for the rational sciences, for memorisation and for precise scholarship, and he was attributed to the village of Abba in Tunisia. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.222-224}

## Dates
He was born around 1344 CE (around 745–747 AH; the source gives a range rather than one year), and died in 828 AH (1424 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.222-224}
