## Overview
Shurayh ibn Amir, a man of the Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr ibn Hawazin. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] left him at al-Kharayba when he conquered it, and it had been a garrison for the non-Arabs. He killed and took captives there. Then he came to the river of al-Mar'a, and conquered its castle by agreement with al-Nushajan ibn Jasnasma {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
