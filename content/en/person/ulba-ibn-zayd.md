## Summary
The second of the Weepers, brother of Banu Haritha; Ibn Ishaq names him among those who asked the Prophet for mounts in the Tabuk expedition, when he found nothing to carry them on. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Ishaq names him second of the Weepers, brother of Banu Haritha, among the seven men who came asking the Prophet for mounts — he said he found nothing to carry them on, and they turned back with eyes overflowing with tears. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Tribe
Ibn Ishaq's lineage for him: brother of Banu Haritha. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The editors note a disagreement on some of the Weepers' names (note p. 161).
