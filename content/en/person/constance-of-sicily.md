## Overview
Daughter of William b. Roger, lord of Sicily; when William died his daughter succeeded — she being mother of the emperor, king of Germany in Ibn Khallikan's day — then she died leaving him a minor, and he was made king and his kingship continued {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
