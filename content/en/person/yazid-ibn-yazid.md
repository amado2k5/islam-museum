## Summary
Yazid ibn Yazid was a prostrating worshipper, praising and thankful, and strong in devotion. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 516, sequence 517, on page 152. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/152}

## A prayer in prostration
Uthman ibn Amr ibn Abi Asim relates that he heard al-Khalil al-Basri say that he heard Yazid ibn Yazid say in his prostration: our souls were made foul by sins, so make us pure through forgiveness. Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/152}
