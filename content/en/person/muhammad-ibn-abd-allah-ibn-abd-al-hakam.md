## Overview
Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Abd al-Hakam al-Misri, a jurist. He heard from Ibn Wahb and Ashhab, of the companions of Malik, and kept company with [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] and studied jurisprudence under him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.52}

## The inquisition
In the inquisition over the createdness of the Qur'an he was taken to Baghdad to Ibn Abi Du'ad. He did not respond to what was asked of him, and was returned to Egypt. Leadership in Egypt came to him. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.52}

Al-Shirazi says that he died in the year about two hundred and sixty. The date of death is disputed and needs an additional source. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.52}
