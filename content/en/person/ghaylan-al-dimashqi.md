## Overview
Ghaylan al-Dimashqi, head of the Qadariyya in Syria, as al-Suyuti describes him. He transmits from al-Dhahabi that he openly taught the view on [[concept/qadar|divine decree]] in the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]], and Umar made him repent. He was then seized in the caliphate of Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik, his four limbs were cut off, and he was crucified in Damascus over the question of decree. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 394}

His kunya was Abu Marwan. Ibn al-Nadim mentions that his letters were collected into a volume of about two thousand folios. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 171,178}

He died in the caliphate of Hisham, that is between 105 and 125 AH; the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 394}
