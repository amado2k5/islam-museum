## Overview
Khayra daughter of Damra al-Qushayri, a wife of [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab]] and the mother of his son Abu Uyayna. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

She had two sets of attendants, a Khayrkan and a Muhallabun. In one report al-Muhallab gave them to her; in another, they were hers and so were ascribed to al-Muhallab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
