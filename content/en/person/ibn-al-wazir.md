## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Ali ibn al-Murtada ibn al-Mufaddal al-Hasani al-Qasimi, Abu Abdallah, Izz al-Din, of the house of al-Wazir, is described by al-Zirikli as a mujtahid and researcher, one of the notables of Yemen, and brother of al-Hadi ibn Ibrahim. The entry gives his years as 775 to 840 AH, 1373 to 1436 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. It does not name his school in his entry.

## His life
He was born at Hijrat al-Dhahran in Shatb, one of the mountains of Yemen, studied at Sanaa, Sa'da and Mecca, and in his last days turned to worship {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. Al-Zirikli quotes al-Shawkani as saying that he took on the airs of a shaykh, went wild in the open country and cut himself off from people {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli says he left precious books, among them «Ithar al-haqq ala al-khalq», «Tanqih al-anzar fi ulum al-athar», on the terminology of hadith, and «Qabul al-bushra bi-l-taysir li-l-yusra», all printed; «al-Awasim wa-l-qawasim fi al-dhabb an sunnat Abi al-Qasim», in manuscript in three volumes, a part of it printed; and its abridgement «al-Rawd al-basim fi al-dhabb an sunnat Abi al-Qasim», printed in two volumes {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. Also «Nasr al-a'yan», warning against the poetry of Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri; «al-Burhan al-qati' fi ithbat al-sani'», a printed treatise; «Hasr ayat al-ahkam al-shar'iyya»; «al-Ta'dib al-malakuti»; and «al-Husam al-mashhur fi al-dhabb an al-Imam al-Mansur» {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}. Also «Tarjih asalib al-Qur'an ala qawanin al-mubtadi'a wa-l-Yunan», printed; «Qawa'id al-tafsir», in manuscript; a book of verse; and a manuscript collection of his various letters in the library of Shaykh Zuhayr al-Shawish in Beirut {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 290}.
