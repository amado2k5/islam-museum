## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Hurmuz al-A'raj, known by the kunya Abu Dawud, a hadith scholar of the Successors. He met a number of the Companions and transmitted from them. He died in 117 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
