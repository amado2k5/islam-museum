## Summary
Amir Sa'd al-Malik Ibn Makula, the great hafiz and imam Abu Nasr 'Ali ibn Hibat Allah ibn 'Ali ibn Ja'far ibn 'Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Dalf ibn al-Amir al-Jawad Abi Dalf al-Qasim ibn 'Isa al-'Ijli, authored the *Ikmal* on removing confusion, vocalization and precision in similar and differing and confusable genealogies — a book of consummate excellence on which the muhaddiths rely. Born at 'Ukbara on 5 Sha'ban 421; his Turkish retainers killed him at Jurjan — also said Khuzistan, also said al-Ahwaz — took his money and fled. His obituary is under 487, and al-Dhahabi differed over his death on several reports. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 382-383}
