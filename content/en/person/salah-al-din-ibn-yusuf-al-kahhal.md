## Overview
Salah al-Din ibn Yusuf al-Kahhal, a physician of the last generation of Arab sages. He grew up in the city of Aleppo, according to the encyclopedia {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.

## Death
He died in the year 696 AH (1296 CE) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}.
