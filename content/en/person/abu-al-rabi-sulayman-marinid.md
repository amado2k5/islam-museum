## Overview
The sultan Abu al-Rabi' Sulayman ibn Abd al-Haqq al-Marini ruled from 708 to 710 AH, as the [[person/abu-thabit-marinid|successor of his brother Abu Thabit]] in the sultanate of the [[polity/marinid-dynasty|Marinids]]. The Marinids gave him their allegiance after his brother's death. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## His days
Ibn Khaldun relates that he seized his uncle Ali ibn Rizaka, who had sought the matter, defeated Uthman ibn Abi al-Ala' at the square of Aludan, and made peace with the lord of Tlemcen. He describes his days as the best of days: truce, calm and luxury, to the point that people went to excess in the prices of property, and houses in Fez were sold for a thousand dinars, and they vied in building with stone, marble and tiles. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He died at Tazi in 710 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
