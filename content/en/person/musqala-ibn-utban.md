## Overview
Musqala ibn Utban, among the Kharijite champions and their poets; of him is said the verse 'convey to the Commander of the Faithful my message — if you do not please Bakr ibn Wa'il you will have a hard day in Iraq: of us are Suwayd, al-Batin, Qa'nab, and of us is the Commander of the Faithful Shabib; among us is Ghazala of the vow, praised — her share is in the arrows of the Muslims'.
