## Overview
Musqala ibn Utban al-Shaybani, a poet among the Kharijites, whose verses al-Mas'udi quotes. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.182-183}

## In the verses
The verses mention Bakr ibn Wa'il, and boast of Shabib, Commander of the Believers among the Kharijites, and of Ghazala. They warn that there will be no peace as long as a preacher from Thaqif stands on the pulpits of their land. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.182-183}
