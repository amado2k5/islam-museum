## Overview
Munir al-Baalbaki, from a prominent Beiruti family, and one of the best-known lexicographers of Arabic in the twentieth century. The *Mawsu'at A'lam al-Ulama' wa al-Udaba'* says that he was born in 1918 CE and died in 1999 CE. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
