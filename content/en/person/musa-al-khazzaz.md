## Summary
Abu Abd al-Rahman Musa ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Khazzaz, a discerning ascetic of great merit. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 678, sequence 679, on page 396. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## Worship and seclusion
He had great merit, worship and asceticism; he kept to his house, at home with remembrance and its witnesses, and transmitted much. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}

## A report on the manners of eating
Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far relates from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Mandah, from Musa ibn Abd al-Rahman, from his father, from al-Nu'man, from Sufyan, from Amr ibn Dinar and Abu al-Zubayr, from Jabir, a report that a dropped morsel is wiped clean of dirt and not left for Satan, and that no one should wipe his hand on a cloth until he has licked it, since the blessing is not known to be in which part of the food. The wording of the report is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/396}
