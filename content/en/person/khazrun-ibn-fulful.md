## Overview
Khazrun ibn Fulful al-Maghrawi, one of the kings of the Maghrawa. When the affairs of the Zanata grew strong in the Maghrib and they came under obedience to the caliph al-Hakam al-Mustansir in al-Andalus, he marched on Sijilmasa in 366 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Sijilmasa
Abu Muhammad al-Mu'tazz billah, amir of the Banu Wasul, came out to meet him, and Khazrun defeated and killed him, and seized his land and his stores. He sent his head to Córdoba with the letter of victory, and with that the state of the Banu Wasul in Sijilmasa came to an end. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
