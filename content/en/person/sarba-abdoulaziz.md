## Summary
Sheikh Abdoul Aziz Sarba is the religious guide of the Burkinabè Tijani community centred on the Samaké zawiya in the Abobo district of Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire — a West African Sufi network figure rather than a state official. {cite:muslim500-sarba-abdoulaziz}

## Work
As president-founder of the association Assab ⁦(Soulaitine)⁩ he has overseen mosque and school construction and humanitarian distributions in Abidjan — including Eid al-Adha meat distributions organised with international charities and stated to be open to Muslims and non-Muslims alike — and receives political leaders seeking the community's counsel and prayers. {cite:muslim500-sarba-abdoulaziz}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 lists him under Social Issues as a community leader and social activist for West Africa's Burkinabè diaspora in Côte d'Ivoire. Published sources on him are thin and largely Francophone press reports; the museum notes this limited sourcing on the record itself. {cite:muslim500-sarba-abdoulaziz}
