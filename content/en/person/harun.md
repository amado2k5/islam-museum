## Overview
Harun ibn Imran, brother of [[person/musa|Musa]] and his vizier. He is one of the prophets mentioned in the Qur'an, and the museum treats him as a prophetic figure within the scriptural frame.

The Prophet cited his rank with respect to Musa in a hadith that al-Bukhari transmits from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], in which he said, in substance: "Are you not content to be to me as Harun was to Musa?" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3706}

In the hadith of the Night Journey he saw him among the heavens. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3207}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh cites Musa's prayer that his brother Harun be made a helper from his family to strengthen him, and draws the lesson that in the thick of life a person needs a righteous brother, and if not a brother by mother and father, then a brother in God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:06:00}
