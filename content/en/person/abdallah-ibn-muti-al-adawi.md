## Overview
Abd Allah ibn Muti' al-Adawi, one of the notables of the people of Medina. Al-Mas'udi says that he went out with Abd Allah ibn Hanzala al-Ghasil against Musrif ibn Uqba on the day of al-Harra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.67}

Then Ibn al-Zubayr appointed him over Kufa, but al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd expelled him from the governor's palace and took control of the city. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.71}
