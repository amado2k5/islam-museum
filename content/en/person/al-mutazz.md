## Overview
Al-Mu'tazz billah, Abu Abd Allah al-Zubayr ibn Ja'far al-Mutawakkil, an Abbasid caliph who was given the allegiance in 252 AH and deposed in 255 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.348} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.389}

## Heirship
Al-Suyuti says that he was second in the heirship after al-Muntasir and before al-Mu'ayyad, and that al-Mutawakkil wanted to put him first because he loved his mother Qabiha. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 543} Al-Mas'udi says that al-Muntasir and his brother Ibrahim deposed him in 248 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 519}

## His caliphate
Al-Tabari says that he was given the allegiance in Muharram 252 AH after al-Musta'in was deposed. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.348} Al-Suyuti says that he was given the allegiance at the age of nineteen, and that no one younger than him had come to the caliphate. Al-Mas'udi says he was eighteen, and that he was given the allegiance on Thursday, two nights into Muharram. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 557-558} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 547-548}

Al-Suyuti says that he was the first caliph to introduce riding with golden ornaments. He removed Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir, then appointed his brother Abu Ahmad and later dismissed and exiled him to Wasit. In Rajab he deposed his brother al-Mu'ayyad, beat him, fettered him, and he died. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 557-558}

## Deposition and death
Al-Mas'udi relates that the Turks gathered to him in Rajab 255 AH under the leadership of Salih ibn Wasif, and demanded money from him. He agreed to depose himself on a guarantee of safety. He was killed in his prison six days later, buried at Samarra, aged twenty-four. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 553-554} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 547-548}
