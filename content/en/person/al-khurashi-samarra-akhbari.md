## Overview
Al-Kharashi, an Iraqi from Samarra, an akhbari and author. He gathered witty sayings and anecdotes, and was original in his writings. He died in 327 AH (938 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
