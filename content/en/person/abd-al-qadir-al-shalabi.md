## Overview
Abd al-Qadir ibn Tawfiq al-Shalabi: a scholar in whom the leadership of the Hanafis of Medina culminated. Born and raised in Tripoli of the Levant, he moved to Medina in 1317 AH and devoted himself to teaching; late in the Ottoman period he was appointed head of the antiquities-excavation group and then superintendent of education. He lived from 1295 to 1369 AH (1878–1950 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.37}

He composed fine verse collected in an unpublished diwan, a register of licences to teach titled al-Ijazat al-Fakhira (printed), printed Qasa'id fi al-Madih al-Nabawi, and treatises including one on the legal status of certain instruments. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.37}
