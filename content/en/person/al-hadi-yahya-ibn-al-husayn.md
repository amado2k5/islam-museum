## Overview
Al-Hadi Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim ibn Tabataba, mentioned in al-Suyuti's "History of the Caliphs" as carrying on the Zaydi call in Yemen, and called Commander of the Faithful. The entry says he died in Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.794}

His son al-Murtada Muhammad came after him, then al-Nasir Ahmad, then al-Muntajab al-Husayn, then al-Mukhtar al-Qasim, then al-Hadi Muhammad, then al-Rashid al-Abbas, after which their state died out. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.794}

Al-Mas'udi relates that he advised As'ad ibn Ya'fur not to interfere with the building of Ghumdan, because its building would be done by the hand of a boy who would come out of the land of Saba and Ma'rib. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.540}

Note for review: the Arabic text in the data gives his death as 208 AH, while the English summary gives 298. The year needs an additional source.
