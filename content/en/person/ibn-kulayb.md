## Summary
Ibn Kulayb, musnid of Iraq — Abu'l-Faraj 'Abd al-Mun'im ibn 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Sa'd al-Harrani then al-Baghdadi, the Hanbali merchant. Born in Safar 500, he heard from Ibn Bayan, Ibn Nabhan, Ibn Zaydan al-Hulwani and a circle. He died in Rabi' I 596 with all his senses intact. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 807}
