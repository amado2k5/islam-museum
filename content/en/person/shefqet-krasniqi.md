## Summary
Shefqet Krasniqi (born 20 June 1966 in Sibofc, Obiliq municipality, Kosovo) is a Kosovar imam and scholar, one of his country's most prominent imams and among its most followed on the platforms: his Facebook page counted 409,000 followers in October 2024, per the peer-reviewed Context study. {cite:context-cupi-2025} He trained in Medina through the doctorate, taught at the Faculty of Islamic Studies in Pristina, and was imam of the city's Imperial Mosque until 2015. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en}

## Early life and education
Krasniqi was born in the village of Sibofc and attended the Alauddin madrassa in Pristina; in 1986 he left for Medina, where he graduated in Arabic language and literature, took a master's in Sharia in 1996 and a doctorate in Islamic jurisprudence in 2001. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en} In 1999 he served with the Kosovo Liberation Army, according to the biography the English article cites. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en}

## Beginnings and career
Krasniqi taught for five years at the Faculty of Islamic Studies in Pristina and was imam of the Imperial (Great) Mosque in the capital until his dismissal from the post in 2015, after which he continued preaching on television, radio and social media. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en} He has been a member of the board of the League of Muslim Scholars since 2009 and writes a weekly column in the newspaper Epoka e Re. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-sq}

## Writings
Krasniqi's Albanian-language books include "Texhvid i preferuar", "Rruga e Ndriçuar" and "Qëndrime në Kohë" (vols. 1–2), together with his study of the transmissions of Abu Hanifa, the subject of his thesis; he also translated two works of Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymin into Albanian. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-sq}

## Positions and criticism
The US State Department's International Religious Freedom Report 2013 named Krasniqi, recording — as the report presents it — negative views of Catholics and rhetoric described as anti-Semitic. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en} He was questioned by the authorities in September 2014, indicted on 27 February 2017 on charges of inciting terrorism, inciting intolerance and tax evasion, and acquitted on all charges on 23 March 2018, with the acquittal upheld on appeal on 1 October 2018. {cite:wikipedia-shefqet-krasniqi-en} These events are recorded as the sources state them; the charges were allegations that ended in acquittal, and none of this is the museum's verdict.

## Impact and audience
The Context study, reading the platform pages in October 2024, recorded 409,000 followers on Facebook, 71,300 on Instagram and 113,000 subscribers on YouTube. {cite:context-cupi-2025} The figures are dated platform counts, not facts the museum measures itself.
