## Overview
Abu'l-Hasan 'Ali b. Jabala b. Muslim b. 'Abd al-Rahman, the famous poet known as al-'Ukuk; he was caught in al-Shamat, seized and carried hand-bound to al-Ma'mun — who, when he stood before him, said "Hand it over." That was in 213 at Baghdad {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
