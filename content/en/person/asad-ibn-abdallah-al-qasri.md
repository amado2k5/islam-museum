## Overview
Asad ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri, brother of [[person/khalid-ibn-abdallah-al-qasri|Khalid ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri]], who was governor of Khurasan. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.483}

## His governorship of Khurasan
Al-Salabi says that Khalid appointed his brother Asad over Khurasan in 106 AH. Then [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] intervened and appointed Ashras ibn Abd Allah al-Sulami. Asad remained governor of Khurasan until he died in Rabi al-Awwal 120 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.483}

## Suq Asad
Al-Baladhuri says that Asad took the village known as Suq Asad, founded its market and moved people to it. His camp, when he set out as governor of Khurasan, was at this market. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.402}

## Notes
- The sequence of his governorship (his appointment, Hisham's intervention, and his continuation) is as al-Salabi gives it; it needs an additional source.
