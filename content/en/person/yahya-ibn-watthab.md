## Overview
Yahya ibn Wathhab al-Asadi, client of the Banu Kinana, who was a Kufan soothsayer, and among those who died in the days of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, according to al-Mas'udi {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.191}.
