## Overview
Yahya ibn Watthab al-Asadi; al-A'mash said Yahya was among the finest reciters of the people — at times I longed to kiss his head for the beauty of his recitation — and when he recited no movement was heard in the mosque, as though no one were there. When he finished his prayer he would remain long, the sorrow of prayer visible in him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4212}
