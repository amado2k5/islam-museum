## Overview
Abu Ja'far al-Batruji Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Rahman of al-Andalus, one of the imams; he transmitted from Abu 'Abdallah al-Tala'i, Abu 'Ali al-Ghassani and their tier — an imam of sound mind versed in Malik's madhhab and its subtleties, an imam in hadith and the knowledge of its men and 'ilal, with famous works; in his time al-Andalus had none like him — though he was of little Arabic, shabby in appearance, obscure. He died in Muharram 542. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 610-611}
