## Overview
Al-Tahir ibn Yahya al-Alawi al-Husayni, an Alid Husayni from the people of Medina. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.62}

## In al-Mas'udi
Al-Mas'udi heard from him a book on the genealogies of the family of Abu Talib, and he preferred it over the book of al-Zubayr ibn Bakkar on this subject. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.62}
