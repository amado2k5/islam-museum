# Chadli Bendjedid

Chadli Bendjedid (1929–2012) was an Algerian officer and politician, the third president of the Republic of Algeria. He was born on 14 April 1929 at Bouteldja in El Tarf province in eastern Algeria, served as a non-commissioned officer in the French army and fought in Indochina, then joined the National Liberation Front in 1954 as the revolution began. {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-ar} {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-en}

## Rise to the presidency

Bendjedid was a protégé of Houari Boumédiène, commanded the military regions of Constantine and Oran, rose to colonel in 1969, and sat on the Revolutionary Council from 1965 to 1976. After Boumédiène's death in December 1978, Rabah Bitat held the headship of the state on an interim basis; Bendjedid became secretary general of the FLN in January 1979 and president of the republic on 9 February 1979, and was re-elected in 1984 and 1989. {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-en} {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-ar}

## Resignation and death

In January 1992, after the disputed first round of legislative elections and the army's intervention, Bendjedid resigned the presidency; Algeria entered the years of civil war, and he remained under house arrest until 1999. He died at the Aïn Naâdja military hospital in Algiers on 6 October 2012, aged eighty-three, and was buried at El Alia Cemetery. {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-en} {cite:source/wikipedia-chadli-bendjedid-ar}
