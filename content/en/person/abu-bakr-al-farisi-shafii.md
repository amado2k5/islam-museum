## Summary
Ahmad ibn al-Husayn ibn Sahl, Abu Bakr al-Farisi, a Shafi'i jurist who studied with al-Muzani and Ibn Surayj and held the judgeship of Fars; he wrote on al-Shafi'i's texts, on the roots of law and a critique of al-Muzani. He died around 350 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9957}
