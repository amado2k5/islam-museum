## Summary
Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Salam b. Raghbana b. 'Abd al-Salam b. Habib, called Dik al-Jinn ('cock of the jinn'), the famous poet; a mawla of Tayyi', his people of Salamiyya, born at Homs — Tamim the first of his ancestors to become Muslim. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His poetry
Among the 'Abbasid state's poets he never left Syria nor journeyed to Iraq nor sought any patron; he inclined to a decent tashayyu' and has elegies on al-Husayn; he was rakish and dissolute yet his poetry is of utmost excellence, and he has tales among the slain of lovers. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
