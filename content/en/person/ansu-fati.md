# Ansu Fati

## Summary

Anssumane "Ansu" Fati Vieira is a Spanish professional footballer, born in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau, on 31 October 2002 and raised in Seville. A left winger, he rose through Barcelona's La Masia academy and in 2019 became one of the club's youngest-ever debutants and goalscorers, before injuries disrupted his progress; after loans to Brighton & Hove Albion and Monaco, he joined Monaco permanently in 2026. He won the 2023 UEFA Nations League with Spain. {cite:wikipedia-ansu-fati-en} {cite:wikipedia-ansu-fati-ar} {cite:wikidata-ansu-fati}

## Early life and career

Fati was born in Bissau and moved to Seville with his family at the age of five, when his older brother Braima signed for Sevilla; his father Bori had himself been a footballer in Guinea-Bissau and Portugal. Ansu joined Barcelona's academy at ten and made his senior debut in August 2019 at sixteen, quickly setting age records in La Liga and the Champions League. A serious knee injury in November 2020 began a long run of fitness problems. {cite:wikipedia-ansu-fati-en}

He spent the 2023–24 season on loan at Brighton, returned to Barcelona, then moved to Monaco on loan in 2025 and permanently in 2026. For Spain he earned ten caps between 2020 and 2023 and was part of the squad that won the Nations League in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-ansu-fati-en}
