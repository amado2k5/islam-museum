# Roxana Moslehi

Roxana Moslehi is an Iranian-born genetic epidemiologist, raised in Canada and an associate professor of epidemiology and biostatistics in the School of Public Health at the University at Albany. No article in any language, and no Wikidata claim, gives her birth date; this article records her career only. No Urdu edition of her article exists; this file's companion Urdu text is written from the same sources. {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-en} {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-fa} {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-ar} {cite:wikidata-moslehi}

## Education and career

Moslehi received her B.Sc. with honours, M.Sc. and Ph.D. from the University of British Columbia; her 2000 doctorate was supervised by J. M. Friedman and Steven Narod. She was then a postdoctoral fellow at the Division of Cancer Epidemiology and Genetics of the National Institutes of Health, under Mitchell Gail, holding meanwhile an adjunct assistant professorship at George Washington University, before taking her post at the University at Albany, where she teaches courses in molecular and genetic epidemiology that she developed. {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-en} {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-fa}

## Research

Her research is on cancer and cancer precursors: the genetic and environmental causes of ovarian cancer, ethnic differences in breast-cancer incidence, and radiation-induced cancers of the eye. She also conducts molecular-epidemiologic and genomic studies of other conditions, including myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS) and gestational complications, contributing through this work to the understanding of hereditary disease causes and gene–environment interactions. {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-en} {cite:wikipedia-moslehi-ar}
