## Overview
Hassan ibn Kabsha, whose name was Hassan ibn Mu'awiya ibn Hajar ibn Kabsha al-Kindi, a king of [[group/kinda|Kinda]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 455}

Ibn al-Athir reports that the Banu Amir corresponded with him after the day of Jabala, seeking to win him over against the Banu Hanzala of Tamim. He came with his clients. On the day of Dhu Najib, Jashish ibn Namran al-Riyahi killed him, and Amir and his clients were routed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 455}
