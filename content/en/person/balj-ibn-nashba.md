## Overview
Balj ibn Nashba al-Sumdi, a guard. The bath of Balj in Basra is attributed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ziyad said of him: "Who is like him, a guard and a watchman?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
