## Overview
Ibn al-Nahhas al-Asamm al-Ansari (572–654 AH) — the last to transmit from Abu Sa'd Ibn Abi 'Asrun; he heard al-Fadl b. al-Baniyasi, Yahya al-Thaqafi, Mansur al-Farawi at Nishapur and 'Ali b. Mansur al-Thaqafi at Isfahan. Trustworthy, good, noble — and so deaf that the people heard hadith only from his own lips {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 273}.
