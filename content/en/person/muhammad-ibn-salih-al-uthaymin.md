## Summary
Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-'Uthaymin was a Saudi scholar whose works include [[work/sharh-riyad-al-salihin-uthaymin|Sharh Riyad al-Salihin]]; the charitable Foundation that bears his name is based in 'Unayza. {cite:uthaymin-sharh-riyad-vol5 p. 1} Among his books is [[work/al-diya-al-lami|al-Diya' al-Lami' min al-Khutab al-Jawami']], his Friday, festival and rain-prayer sermons. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 5}

## Preacher of the mosque of 'Unayza
In the preface to [[work/al-diya-al-lami|al-Diya' al-Lami' min al-Khutab al-Jawami']] he says that his teacher [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-nasir-al-sadi|'Abd al-Rahman ibn Nasir al-Sa'di]] was preacher in the Great Mosque of 'Unayza until his death in Jumada al-Akhira 1376 AH, and that he succeeded him in that office. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 8} His Foundation states that he preached there from 1376 to 1421 AH and that he published a number of his sermons in this book in 1392 AH. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 5}
