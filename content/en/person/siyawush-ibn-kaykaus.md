## Overview
Siyawush ibn Kaykaus, whom al-Mas'udi writes as "Siyakhus ibn Kaykaus al-Jabbar". al-Mas'udi reports that he built the fire temple called Kanjada during his residence in the eastern regions of China, on the side toward al-Barkand {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.549}.
