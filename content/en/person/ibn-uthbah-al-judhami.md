## Summary
Abu 'Abd Allah Muhammad ibn 'Utba al-Judh'ami, their client, the Maliki — mufti, scholar, muhaddith and scrupulous man of Cordoba. He died in Safar 463, and Ahmad ibn 'Abbad walked in his funeral at the age of seventy-nine. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 316}
