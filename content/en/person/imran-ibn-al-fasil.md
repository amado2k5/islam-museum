## Overview
Imran ibn al-Fasil al-Barjumi, an Arab man mentioned in the reports of the conquest of Sijistan. After the Battle of the Camel he went out with Hasaka ibn Attab al-Habati and a band of "the vagrant Arabs" to Sijistan, and they entered Zaranj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In the verse
Al-Baladhuri relays a rajaz verse said about them, the sense of which is: "Give Sijistan the good news of hunger and war with Ibn al-Fasil and the vagrant Arabs, for neither silver nor gold will exhaust them." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
