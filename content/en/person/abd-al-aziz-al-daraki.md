## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Abd al-Aziz ibn Abdallah al-Daraki was a jurist who studied under [[person/abu-ishaq-ibrahim-al-marwazi|Abu Ishaq al-Marwazi]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61} He died in 375 AH, according to al-Shirazi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.61}

The data record that Ibn Kajj attended his circle. The year of death needs a second source.
