## Overview
Abu'l-Muzaffar Barkiyaruq, titled Rukn al-Din, son of Sultan Malikshah b. Alp Arslan b. Da'ud b. Mika'il b. Saljuq — a king of the Seljuks; fortunate and high-minded, he held the sultanate after his father for some twelve years. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His death
He died on the twelfth of Sha'ban 498 at Borujerd. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
