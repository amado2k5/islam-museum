## Overview
Nu'man ibn Mahmud ibn Abd Allah, Abu al-Barakat Khayr al-Din al-Alusi, was a preacher, jurist and researcher, one of the notable members of the Alusi family of Iraq. Al-Zirikli prints his years as 1252 AH (1836 CE) to 1317 AH (1899 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}.

## Career
Al-Zirikli says he was born and raised in Baghdad and held judgeships in several lands, among them al-Hilla, before leaving office {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}. He visited Egypt on the way to the pilgrimage in 1295 AH and made for Istanbul (al-Astana) in 1300 AH, staying two years, and returned bearing the title "head of the teachers" (ra'is al-mudarrisin). He then devoted himself to teaching and writing until he died in Baghdad {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}.

## Character
Al-Zirikli reports al-Athari's verdict: his mind was greater than his learning, his learning more eloquent than his prose, and his prose firmer than his verse; he was generous, loyal, ascetic, pleasant company and easy of temper {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}.

## Works
His books include Jala' al-Aynayn fi Muhakamat al-Ahmadayn (printed), weighing Ibn Taymiyya against Ibn Hajar, al-Jawab al-Fasih li-ma Laffaqahu Abd al-Masih (printed), Ghaliyat al-Mawa'iz (printed) and Sadiq al-Fajrayn (printed), on Ali and Mu'awiya {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}. Another is Shaqa'iq al-Nu'man, in manuscript, a reply to some of his contemporaries {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}. Al-Zirikli adds that al-Ayat al-Bayyinat by him was printed by al-Maktab al-Islami {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 8 p. 42}.
