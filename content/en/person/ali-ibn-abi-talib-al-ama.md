## Overview
Ali ibn Abi Talib al-A'ma, a blind poet. Al-Mas'udi gives in "Muruj al-Dhahab" an ode of his lamenting Baghdad during the war between al-Amin and al-Ma'mun, which begins: "The kinship ties were cut between the tribes." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.385-386}
