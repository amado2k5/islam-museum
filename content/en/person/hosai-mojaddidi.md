# Hosai Mojaddidi

## Summary
Hosai Mojaddidi is a teacher, spiritual counsellor and mental-health advocate in California. She was born in Afghanistan and moved to the United States with her family at the age of two. Her Islamic learning began in 1996 in the Hayward classes of Shaykh Hamza Yusuf, her teacher and mentor since then. {cite:mcc-east-bay-hosai-mojaddidi}

## Mental Health 4 Muslims
For over twenty years she has served the Bay Area and Orange County and Los Angeles communities as an organizer, teacher, spiritual counsellor, mentor and mental-health advocate, leading study circles and support groups. With her cousin Dr. Nafisa Sekandari she co-founded Mental Health 4 Muslims, a website giving spiritual and clinical voices on mental-health issues. {cite:hosaimojaddidi-website}
