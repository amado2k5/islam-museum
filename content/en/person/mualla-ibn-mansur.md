## Overview
Mu'alla ibn Mansur al-Razi, known as Abu Ya'la. He transmitted from Abu Yusuf his jurisprudence, his principles and his books. He died in Baghdad in 211 AH. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.287}
