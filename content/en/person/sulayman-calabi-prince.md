## Summary
Süleyman Çelebi, the eldest of Bayezid I's five sons, survived the Ankara disaster of 804/1402, made for Edirne and proclaimed himself sultan — ruler of the state's European wing through the interregnum {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 70}.

Ten years of war followed against his brothers İsa at Bursa and Mehmed at Amasya, while Timur sowed the discord by releasing their captive brother Musa {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 70-71}. The contest closed when the youngest, Mehmed I, prevailed in 1413 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 72-73}.
