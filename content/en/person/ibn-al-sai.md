## Overview
Ali ibn Anjab ibn Uthman ibn Abd Allah Abu Talib, Taj al-Din Ibn al-Sa'i, was one of the great compilers of history, born and died at Baghdad in 674 AH (1275 CE), keeper of the Mustansiriyya library. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.264}

His works include al-Jami' al-Mukhtasar fi 'Unwan al-Tarikh wa-'Uyun al-Siyar in twenty-five volumes arranged by year, reaching 656 AH; Tarikh al-Shu'ara'; Akhbar al-Hallaj; Akhbar Qudat Baghdad; Akhbar al-Wuzara'; Dhayl Tarikh Baghdad; Tabaqat al-Fuqaha'; Ghurar al-Muhadara; and Akhbar al-Musannifin. {cite:al-aalam v.4 p.264}
