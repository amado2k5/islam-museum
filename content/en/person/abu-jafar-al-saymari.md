## Overview
Abu Jafar al-Saymari, a vizier of [[person/muizz-al-dawla|Muizz al-Dawla]] ibn Buyah. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 365-366}

## His campaign against the Bataih
Muizz al-Dawla sent him to fight Imran ibn Shahin, lord of the Bataih marshes. He fought him again and again and took his family and dependants captive. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 365-366}

## The move to Shiraz
When Imad al-Dawla died in 338 AH, Muizz al-Dawla wrote to him to hasten to Shiraz to set its affairs in order, so he left Imran and went there. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 365-366}
