## Overview
Abu Yahya ibn Asim al-Gharnati, chief judge, secretary and vizier, whom al-Maqqari describes as a scholar. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 511-512}

He wrote [[work/jannat-al-rida|Jannat al-Rida fi al-Taslim lima Qaddara Allah wa Qada]] (The Garden of Contentment in Submitting to What God Has Decreed and Ordained). According to the source, he portrayed in it the end of al-Andalus and the cause of its fall, and traced that to the Christians' exploitation of the disputes among the kings of the peninsula. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 511-512}

The dates of his birth and death are not documented in the museum's data, and need an additional source.
