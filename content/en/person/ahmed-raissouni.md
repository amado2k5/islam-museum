# Ahmed Raissouni

**Ahmed ben Abdelsalam ben Mohamed Raissouni** is a Moroccan Muslim scholar and jurist. He was born in 1953 in the village of Ouled Soltane near Ksar el-Kebir, and completed his primary and secondary education at Ksar el-Kebir. He took his bachelor's degree in Sharia from the University of al-Qarawiyyin in 1978, and his master's (1989) and doctorate (1992) at Mohammed V University in Rabat, where he went on to teach the principles of Islamic jurisprudence and the higher objectives of the law {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-en} {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-ar}.

Before his university career he worked at the Ministry of Justice (1973–1978) and in traditional secondary education. He was the first secretary-general of the Moroccan Association of Alumni in Greater Islamic Studies (1992–1996), and became a senior expert at the International Islamic Fiqh Academy and a visiting professor at the Qatar Faculty of Islamic Studies {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-ar} {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-en}.

In 1994 he founded the Islamic Future Association, which merged with the Reform and Renewal Movement to form the Movement of Unity and Reform (MUR) in 1996; he presided over the MUR until 2003 and was editor-in-chief of the daily newspaper Attajdid from 2000 to 2004. The MUR is regarded as the ideological arm of the Justice and Development Party and keeps close ties with the Muslim Brotherhood {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-en}.

Raissouni was a founding member of the International Union of Muslim Scholars, its vice-president from 2013 and its president from 2018. In August 2022, in a television interview, he described Mauritania's existence as "a mistake" and spoke of Morocco recovering its historical borders; the remarks drew condemnation in Mauritania and Algeria, the union distanced itself from them, and on 28 August 2022 he resigned the presidency, saying he did so to exercise his freedom of expression without conditions {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-en} {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-ar}.

His scholarly work centres on the higher objectives of Islamic law (maqasid al-sharia). His best-known book, on Imam al-Shatibi's theory of the higher objectives and intents of Islamic law, has been translated into English, Persian and Urdu; other works treat consultation (shura), the Islamic endowment (waqf) and the rules of the maqasid discipline {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-ar} {cite:wikipedia-ahmed-raissouni-en}.
