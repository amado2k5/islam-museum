## Overview
Jahwar ibn Marrar al-Mujalli, called "al-Marar" in Ibn Duraid. Umar ibn al-Ala' sent the butcher of Rayy to al-Mansur, and al-Mansur honoured him and gave him a rank. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
