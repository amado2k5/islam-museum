## Overview
Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit, one of the seven jurists of [[place/medina|Medina]] in the second generation. Al-Shirazi says that he and Talha ibn Abdallah ibn Awf were consulted for legal opinions in their time, and that people relied on their views on inheritance and documents. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

Al-Salabi says that he was among the ten jurists whom Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz invited to the council of consultation in Medina. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.173} Al-Shirazi relays a view that he died around 100 AH at the age of seventy. The date is derived and needs an additional source.
