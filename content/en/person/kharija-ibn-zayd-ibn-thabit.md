## Overview
Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit, the jurist and imam son of an imam, one of the Seven Illustrious Jurists, Abu Zayd al-Ansari al-Najjari of Medina and the most eminent of his brothers Isma'il, Sulayman, Yahya and Sa'd; his maternal grandfather was Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' al-Ansari, one of the noble nuqaba'. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1590}

## Transmission
He narrated from his father, his uncle Yazid, Usama ibn Zayd, his mother Umm Sa'd and others. He died in 99 or 100 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1590}
