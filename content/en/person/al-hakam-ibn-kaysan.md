## Overview
[[person/al-hakam-ibn-kaysan|Al-Hakam ibn Kaysan]], a client of Hisham ibn al-Mughira al-Makhzumi. Ibn Hajar mentions him in the "Companions" for his famous story in the biographies, as related by Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.335}

## Captivity
He was taken captive in the first raiding party the Prophet sent out from Medina, whose commander was [[person/abdullah-ibn-jahsh|Abd Allah ibn Jahsh]], and they brought him to the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.335} See [[event/expedition-of-nakhla|the expedition of Nakhla]]. Al-Waqidi relates from [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr]] that he was the one who captured him, and Umar wanted to kill him, but "he embraced Islam in the presence of the Messenger of God". {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.335}

## His death
"He was killed as a martyr at Bir Mauna", as Ibn Ishaq and others also say. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.335} See [[event/incident-of-bir-mauna|the incident of Bir Mauna]].

## His marriage
Al-Haytham ibn Adi relates from Yunus, from al-Zuhri, and from Ibn Abbas, from Abu Bakr ibn Abi Jahm, that al-Hakam ibn Kaysan, a client of the Banu Makhzum, "who was a cupper", married Amina daughter of Affan, the sister of Uthman, and she was a hairdresser. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.335}
