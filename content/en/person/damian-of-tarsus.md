## Overview
Damiana of Tarsus was governor of the frontier city of Tarsus in the time of the caliph al-Mu'tadid. Ahmad ibn Tughan left him in charge there when he took ship in 283 AH and supported him, and strife broke out between him and Raghib, mawla of al-Muwaffaq, who defeated him and carried him to Baghdad. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.5}

## The burning of the Tarsus fleet
In 285 AH al-Mu'tadid arrested the notables of Tarsus for corresponding with Wasif and ordered the burning of the ships in which they used to raid, along with all their equipment — some fifty old vessels on which untold money had been spent. The burning harmed the Muslims and let the Byzantines raid by sea; it was done at the instance of Damiana, a page of Bazmar, out of a grudge he bore the people of Tarsus. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir v.5}
