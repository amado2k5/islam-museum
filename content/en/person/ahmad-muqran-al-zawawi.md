## Summary
Lord of Qal'at Bani Abbas, successor of Abd al-Aziz b. Abbas {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249-250}.

## Recognition and service
He held the Kuku country with [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]]'s recognition, and led the thousand Sipahi horse in his army at the Oran siege of 971/1563 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249-250}.
