## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Khazim al-Sulami. His father appointed him over Herat, but the Banu Tamim rose there and killed him. His father seized Uthman ibn Bashir and killed him by execution, in revenge for his son. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
