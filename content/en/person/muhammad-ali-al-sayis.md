## Summary
Muhammad 'Ali al-Sayis, among the senior scholars of al-Azhar al-Sharif and former dean of the Shari'a faculty, author of *Nash'at al-fiqh al-ijtihadi wa-atwaruh* in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series {cite:nashat-al-fiqh-al-ijtihadi-azhar p. 1}. He wrote the study on commission from the Academy's council for presentation to the fourth Islamic Conference {cite:nashat-al-fiqh-al-ijtihadi-azhar p. 3}, tracing ijtihad's development from the prophetic age to the 'Abbasid and the foundations of the four madhhabs {cite:nashat-al-fiqh-al-ijtihadi-azhar p. 3-4}.
