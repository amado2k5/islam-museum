## Summary
Al-Shihab al-Hijazi, the brilliant poet; born in Sha'ban 790, he heard under al-Majd al-Hanafi and al-Burhan al-Abnasi, and al-'Iraqi and al-Haythami granted him ijaza. He devoted himself so thoroughly to adab that he became the unique man of his age, composing *Rawd al-adab*, *al-Qawa'id*, *al-Maqamat*, *al-Tadhkira* and more — verse, prose, history and fine calligraphy — excelling in many arts but abandoning all but adab. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 319}

## Character and death
The great praised him; he kept to recitation and writing, excellent company, sweet speech and freedom from affectation. He died in Ramadan 875. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 319}
