## Overview
Maslama ibn Muharib, a transmitter whom al-Baladhuri mentions in *Futuh al-Buldan*. Al-Mada'ini related from him the story of the prohibition of dinars and the ban on papers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
