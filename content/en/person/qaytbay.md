## Overview
Qaytbay, a Mamluk Circassian sultan known as al-Ashraf. al-Suyuti says that the caliph al-Mustanjid invested him with the sultanate, and that he governed the realm with resolve and firmness, and travelled from Egypt to the Euphrates with a small number of troops. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.776-778}

## His conduct
al-Suyuti relates that he appointed no one to a religious office until he had found the best person after long deliberation, so that some posts remained vacant for months. He appointed no judge or shaykh for money. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.776-778}

In 884 AH he travelled to the Hijaz for the pilgrimage, something no king had done in more than a hundred years. He began by visiting [[place/medina|Medina]], where he distributed six thousand dinars, and then came to Mecca, where he distributed five thousand. He appointed a shaykh and Sufis for his madrasa in Mecca. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.776-778}

## In Shadharat al-Dhahab
Shadharat al-Dhahab names him Abu al-Nasr Qaytbay, the Circassian Zahiri king, affiliated to al-Zahir Jaqmaq. It says he was born in 826 AH and was given the sultanate on Monday, 6 Rajab 872 AH. It records his building works for the holy sites, madrasas and fortresses in Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem, Egypt and Syria {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.423-424}.

## Cem and Bayezid
Al-Salabi records that when Prince [[person/jem-sultan|Cem]] fled his brother [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]] to Egypt, Qaytbay honoured him and equipped him for the hajj {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 163}; Bayezid then sealed peace with the Mamluks in 1491 by sending Qaytbay the keys of the frontier fortresses {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 164}.
## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry, written in his lifetime, for Qaytbay al-Jarkasi al-Mahmudi al-Ashrafi, later al-Zahiri, al-Ashraf Abu al-Nasr: born about 820-something, he was brought to Egypt in 839 and bought by Barsbay. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/201} He became sultan before noon on Monday 6 Rajab 872. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/202} He made the pilgrimage in 884; his buildings include his madrasa, ribat and fountain at Bab al-Salam in Mecca, and in Medina his madrasa and the rebuilding of the Prophet's Mosque after the fire. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/206} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/207} Al-Sakhawi praises him lavishly, noting as a fault the demolition and seizure of property for building. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/208} The entry was written in his lifetime and gives no date of death. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/211}
