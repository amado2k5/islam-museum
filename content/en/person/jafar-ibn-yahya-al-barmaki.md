## Overview
Ja'far ibn Yahya al-Barmaki, son of [[person/yahya-ibn-khalid-al-barmaki|Yahya ibn Khalid al-Barmaki]], and one of the [[group/barmakids|Barmakids]]. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush}

## His fall
Al-Tabari says that his ruin and the ruin of the Barmakids came from al-Rashid's forbearance toward him and toward his sister Abbasa daughter of al-Mahdi. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.294}

Al-Mas'udi relates that al-Amin, when he came out of the Ka'ba, swore to him, and that this was the cause of the grudge of Umm Ja'far against him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 341} He also relates a report of a thousand dinars that his servant carried to give to al-Asma'i if he made him laugh, and a report of a pledge that Ja'far made alone. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 357}

His birth and death dates are not recorded here and need an additional source.
