## Overview
Al-Mu'izz ibn Ziri ibn Atiyya al-Maghrawi, the second of the Ziri rulers in Fez, and of the [[group/maghrawa|Maghrawa]]. All the Maghrawa gave him the allegiance after his father. He held firmly to the Amirid call. Then he asked Abd al-Malik al-Muzaffar to restore him to his governorship in return for money he would bring him, on condition that his son Mu'nasir should be a hostage in Cordoba. He agreed and restored him in 406 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## After his return
His cousin Mu'nasir rose against him and disputed Fez with him. His sons inherited the rule, until the Sanhaja commander Hammama Abu al-Atf overcame them in Fez and all their governorship. Ibn Khaldun says that their continuance lasted in the troubles until the state of the Almoravids. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
