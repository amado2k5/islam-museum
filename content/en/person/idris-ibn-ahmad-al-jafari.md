## Overview
Idris ibn Ahmad, of the line of [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Jafar ibn Abi Talib]], is named by Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah among the few who accompanied [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] to Yemen: with al-Hadi were his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Muhammad ibn Yahya]], Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Hasani, Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali, Yahya ibn al-Husayn of the line of Umar ibn Ali, Idris, and ten of his servants. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.38} The text says nothing else about him in these pages.
