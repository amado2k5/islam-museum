## Summary
Din Syamsuddin is one of the most recognisable Muslim organisation leaders in Southeast Asia: for a decade he chaired Muhammadiyah, the Indonesian modernist movement that runs thousands of schools, universities, hospitals and charities, and he later served the Indonesian Council of Ulama. {cite:muslim500-din-syamsuddin} {cite:wikipedia-din-syamsuddin}

## Formation
Born Sirajuddin Syamsuddin in Sumbawa in 1958, he studied at IAIN Syarif Hidayatullah in Jakarta, took a master's degree at the University of Indonesia and completed a doctorate in political science at the University of California, Los Angeles, writing on Muhammadiyah's political behaviour. He then rose through Muhammadiyah's research and cadre structures. {cite:wikipedia-din-syamsuddin}

## Career
Elected chairman of Muhammadiyah in 2005 and re-elected in 2010, he led the organisation until 2015, steering its educational and welfare network and its discourse of a moderate, 'progressive' Islam. He subsequently chaired the advisory council of the Indonesian Ulama Council (MUI) and served as the Indonesian president's special envoy for interfaith dialogue and cooperation. Internationally he is president of the Asian Conference of Religions for Peace and co-founded the World Peace Forum. {cite:muslim500-din-syamsuddin} {cite:wikipedia-din-syamsuddin}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 places him in both its Honourable Mentions and Scholarly sections, reflecting a career split between scholarship — he taught Islamic political thought at his alma mater — and mass-organisation leadership. His term at MUI and his public positions have at times drawn criticism from rival Indonesian Muslim currents; the museum records such disputes attributed, without verdict. {cite:muslim500-din-syamsuddin}
