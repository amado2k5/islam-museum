# Aziz Alili

## Summary

Hafiz Aziz Alili (born 29 May 1968 at Kumanovo) is a Bosnian Muslim reciter, muezzin and imam who heads the Islamic Community in Zagreb, Croatia. The German article, citing the Berliner Zeitung (2008), calls him a world champion of Qur'an recitation. {cite:wikipedia-aziz-alili-de} {cite:wikidata-aziz-alili}

## Early life and study

He was born at Kumanovo, then in the Socialist Republic of Macedonia within Yugoslavia. He began memorising the Qur'an at seven and became a hafiz at nine years and eight months. After primary school he attended the [[building/gazi-husrev-madrasa|Gazi Husrev-beg Madrasa]] in [[place/sarajevo|Sarajevo]]. {cite:wikipedia-aziz-alili-de} {cite:wikidata-aziz-alili}

## Muezzin and imam

From 1984 to 1986 he was muezzin of the Emperor's Mosque (Careva džamija) in Sarajevo, and from 1989 to 1993 imam of the Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque, the city's principal mosque. {cite:wikipedia-aziz-alili-de}

## Competitions and festivals

He has taken part as a reciter in many world and European competitions — in Saudi Arabia, Iran, Egypt, Turkey, Malaysia and Croatia — and was the first reciter to represent Bosnia and Herzegovina at them. He has also performed at the international festival of sacred music in Morocco. {cite:wikipedia-aziz-alili-de}

## Today

He lives and works in Zagreb, and is married with four children. {cite:wikipedia-aziz-alili-de}
