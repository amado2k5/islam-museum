## Overview
Amr ibn Utba al-Zahid, who was with Utba ibn Farqad al-Sulami in his raids on Azerbaijan after its lands broke their covenant, and he is mentioned in [[event/conquest-of-azerbaijan|the conquest of Azerbaijan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
