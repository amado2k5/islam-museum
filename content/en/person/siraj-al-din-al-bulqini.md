## In al-Sakhawi's al-Daw' al-Lami'
[[person/al-sakhawi|Al-Sakhawi]] gives an entry for Umar ibn Raslan, Siraj al-Din Abu Hafs al-Bulqini: born at Bulqina on the eve of Friday 12 Sha'ban 724, he came to Cairo aged twelve and studied with al-Taqi al-Subki and Abu Hayyan. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/85} He became judge of Damascus in 769. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/86} Al-Sakhawi relays the scholars' praise of his command of Shafi'i law. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/86} {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/87} He died on Friday 11 Dhu al-Qa'da 805 in Cairo and was buried in his madrasa. {cite:daw-lami-jil-1992 6/89}
