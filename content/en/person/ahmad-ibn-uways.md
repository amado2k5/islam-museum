## Overview
Ghiyath al-Din Ahmad ibn Uways al-Jala'iri, sultan of Baghdad, Tabriz and other lands of Iraq. He became ruler after the death of his brother Shaykh Husayn ibn Uways in 784 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.100-101}

Shadharat al-Dhahab describes him as brave and awe-inspiring, with power over his subjects, a shedder of blood, and unjust to his amirs and troops. It says he had a share in several sciences, knowledge of the stars, and skill in music. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.100-101}

According to the Shadharat, [[person/barquq|Barquq]] honoured him and gave him the deputy sultanate in Baghdad, and he went to Baghdad and entered it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.100-101}

According to Ibn Khaldun, he fled when [[person/timur-lang|Timur]] advanced on Baghdad, and joined Sultan Barquq, calling on him for help. He went out with him in the armies to the Euphrates, and Barquq sent troops to support him, and he took Baghdad from a small detachment of Timur's army. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1542}
