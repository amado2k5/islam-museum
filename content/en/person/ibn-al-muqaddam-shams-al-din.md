## Overview
Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik, known as Ibn al-Muqaddam, one of the greatest Ayyubid (Salahi) emirs. After the conquest of [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] in 583 AH, he asked Salah al-Din for permission to go on pilgrimage from Jerusalem, so that in that year he could combine holy war and the hajj. He was then appointed commander of the pilgrim caravan from Syria. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 162-163}

## The day of Arafat
Ibn al-Athir relates that on the eve of Arafat he ordered his tents to be pitched. The emir of the Iraqi pilgrims, Mujir al-Din Tashtakin, confronted him and did not stop for him, so the Iraqis attacked the Syrian pilgrims. Ibn al-Muqaddam was wounded while restraining his companions from fighting, out of respect for the sanctity of the place and the day. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 162-163}

He died on the same day at Mina and was buried in the cemetery of al-Mu'alla in 583 AH. The conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. He is among those who took part in [[event/arafat-hajj-clash-583|the clash at Arafat in 583 AH]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 163}
