## Overview
Mar'i ibn Yusuf ibn Abi Bakr ibn Ahmad al-Karmi al-Maqdisi al-Hanbali was a historian and littérateur among the great jurists. Born at Tulkarm in Palestine, he moved to Jerusalem then to Cairo, where he died in 1033 AH (1624 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.203}

He wrote some seventy books, among them Badi' al-Insha' wa-l-Sifat known as Insha' Mar'i; a diwan; Ghayat al-Muntaha combining al-Iqna' and al-Muntaha in Hanbali fiqh; Dalil al-Talib in fiqh; Arwah al-Ashbah on the spirits; al-Kalimat al-Sunniyyat in tafsir; Masbuk al-Dhahab on the merits of the Arabs; Riyad al-Azhar on the rulings of listening and musical instruments; Qala'id al-Marjan on abrogation in the Qur'an; Fara'id al-Fikr on the awaited Mahdi; Aqawil al-Thiqat on the interpretation of the names and attributes; Nuzhat al-Nazirin, a history of Egypt's rulers; Tanwir Basa'ir al-Muqallidin on the merits of the mujtahid imams; Qala'id al-'Uqyan on the merits of the family of Uthman; and more. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.203}
