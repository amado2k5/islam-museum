## Summary
Talha, known as Umm Ghurab, narrated from Nubata, from Uthman ibn Affan, and from Uqayla and Salama bint al-Hurr. Marwan ibn Mu'awiya al-Fazari and Waki ibn al-Jarrah narrated from her, and Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah included her reports. Ibn Hajar in al-Taqrib says her state is not known {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13750}.
