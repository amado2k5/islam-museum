## Summary

Mizanur Rahman Azhari is a Bangladeshi Islamic preacher and scholar, born on 26 January 1990 in Demra, Dhaka{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}{cite:speakersofislam-mizanur-rahman-azhari}, and raised in a conservative family in the Gazipur area{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. He studied tafsir and Qur'anic sciences at Al-Azhar University in Cairo and then at the International Islamic University Malaysia{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}, and became famous for the mass preaching gatherings known in Bangladesh as waz mahfil, becoming one of the most followed preachers in his country; his YouTube channel had about 3.53 million subscribers in platform figures of November 2026{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.

## Upbringing and education

Azhari's family comes from Paramatla village in Muradnagar, Comilla district{cite:speakersofislam-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. He passed the Dakhil examination in 2004 and the Alim examination in 2006 with a perfect grade point average at Darunnajat Siddiquia Kamil Madrasa in Demra{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. In 2007 he placed first in the Islamic Foundation's Al-Azhar scholarship examination, and he studied at the Faculty of Usuluddin in Cairo until graduating in 2012 in the department of Tafsir and Qur'anic Sciences{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. He completed master's, MPhil and doctoral studies at the International Islamic University Malaysia, with research on topics such as human embryology and the behavioural characteristics of human beings in the Qur'an{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}{cite:speakersofislam-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.

## Preaching career

Azhari began his career in 2010 as a ghazal singer and a participant in recitation programmes on ATN Bangla{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}, then turned to waz preaching in 2015{cite:speakersofislam-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. His preaching addresses the everyday concerns of young people, such as drugs, parent-child relations and women's rights, as The Daily Star's analysis of his style records{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. He built his digital presence on YouTube and Facebook, and his recordings spread widely inside Bangladesh and among its diaspora{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.

## The Malaysia years and the return

His events in Bangladesh stopped in early 2020 amid political pressure and repeated cancellations of his programmes, and he left for Malaysia in February 2020{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. He returned to Bangladesh on 2 October 2024 to a large welcome, after years spent in Kuala Lumpur{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.

## Personal life

Azhari married on 29 January 2014 and has two daughters{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.

## Impact and audience

Azhari is among the leading faces of digital preaching in Bangladesh, and press sources describe him as one of the most popular preachers in the country{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}. His YouTube channel is followed by about 3.53 million subscribers in platform figures of November 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:daily-star-mizanur-rahman-azhari}.
