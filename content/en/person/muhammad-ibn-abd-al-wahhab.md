## Summary
Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Wahhab ibn Sulayman al-Tamimi was a scholar of the twelfth Islamic century; the title page of the Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University edition of his book dates him 1115–1206 AH. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university} His best-known book is [[work/kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab|Kitab al-Tawhid alladhi huwa Haqq Allah 'ala al-'Abid]], on the oneness of worship and what contradicts it, [[concept/shirk|shirk]] greater and lesser. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 7-9} {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 153-157}

## His book on tawhid
In sixty-six chapters he brings verses, reports and sayings of the early generations without chains and draws numbered points from them. The topics are amulets and incantations, seeking blessing, sacrifice, vows and calling for help on other than God, intercession, excess regarding the righteous and their graves, sorcery, soothsaying, omens and astrology, ostentation, forms of speech, decree, image-making and oaths. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 153-157} In it he quotes [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]], calling him by his kunya "Abu al-'Abbas". {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 51-52}

## How the edition of his works presents him
The title page calls him "Shaykh al-Islam", and the foreword by the university's director, 'Abd Allah ibn 'Abd al-Muhsin al-Turki, calls him "Shaykh al-Islam and renewer of the call". {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university} {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 6} The foreword says the university held a large symposium on his call. It gathered his printed and manuscript works and letters from libraries and from members of his family, formed a committee to revise them, and then re-edited and reprinted them at the expense of Prince Sultan ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz. {cite:kitab-al-tawhid-ibn-abd-al-wahhab-imam-university p. 5-6} These are the publisher's descriptions, not the museum's judgement.
