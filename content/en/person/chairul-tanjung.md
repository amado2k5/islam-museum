## Summary

Chairul Tanjung (born 18 June 1962) is an Indonesian businessman, founder and chairman of CT Corp, the conglomerate spanning Bank Mega, the Trans TV and Trans7 channels, Transmart retail and property. A dentistry graduate who began trading as a student, he served as Indonesia's Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs in 2014 and was appointed to the Presidential Advisory Council in 2026 {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en} {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id}.

## Life

Chairul Tanjung was born in Jakarta on 18 June 1962 to A. G. Tanjung and Halimah. His father, a journalist who had established a magazine company, saw the business shut down by the Suharto regime, and the family sold its large house and cars and moved into a small apartment {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en}. The Indonesian article adds that his father came from Sorkam in North Sumatra, of the Pesisir people and the Tanjung clan, and his mother from Cibadak in West Java {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id}. He graduated from Boedi Oetomo Senior High School in 1981 and studied dentistry at the University of Indonesia, where he was named a national model student in 1984 and 1985; while studying he sold books and T-shirts with two friends and later medical equipment, and he graduated in 1987 {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en} {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id}.

## Work

In 1987 Chairul founded PT Pariarti Shindutama with three partners on a loan of Rp 150 million from Exim Bank, producing children's shoes that won an Italian order for 160,000 pairs; he later split from his partners over the direction of expansion and built his own group, Para Group, renamed CT Corp on 1 December 2011 {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id} {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en}. He bought Bank Karman and renamed it Bank Mega, founded Trans TV and acquired TV7, which became Trans7, took a 40 percent stake in Carrefour Indonesia in 2010 and a minority share of Garuda Indonesia in 2012 {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en} {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id}. He served as Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs from 19 May to 20 October 2014, was inaugurated as a professor of entrepreneurship at Airlangga University in 2015, and on 2 October 2026 President Prabowo Subianto appointed him to the Presidential Advisory Council {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id}. His biography, Chairul Tanjung Si Anak Singkong, was published in 2012, and Forbes put his net worth at $5.2 billion in 2024 {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en}. No text read for this record names a faith for Chairul Tanjung himself — Indonesian profile sites list Islam in biodata tables, but such structured listings are a single copied family and settle nothing, and no school is named for him — so his faith is recorded as unknown {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-en} {cite:wikipedia-chairul-tanjung-id} {cite:wikidata-chairul-tanjung}.
