## Overview
Muhammad ibn Isa is an Arab of Tamim, from Iraq, whom [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] appointed with Ahmad ibn Muhammad over [[place/najran|Najran]]; the narrator says the two worked together in God's matter, not at odds, hating or envying each other, but loving, friendly and agreed on taking the right from whoever owed it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} That is the narrator's description. See [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran]].
