## Overview
Lutfi Pasha ibn Abd al-Mu'in al-Albani was a learned man among the viziers of the Ottoman state; he died about 970 AH (1562 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.243}

He wrote al-Kunuz fi Hall al-Rumuz, a commentary on forty hadiths he collected in 957 AH, and Khulas al-Umma fi Ma'rifat al-A'imma. {cite:al-aalam v.5 p.243}
