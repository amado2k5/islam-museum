## The story of Jesus in Syria
Muhammad ibn Sabba' al-Numayri related: Jesus son of Mary was travelling in some lands of Syria when rain, thunder and lightning grew severe on him, and he looked for something to shelter in. A tent was raised for him from afar; he went to it and found a woman in it, and turned away. He found a cave in a mountain; he went in and found a lion in it. He laid his hand on the lion and said: my God, You made a shelter for everything, and You did not make one for me! The Majestic answered him: your shelter is with Me, in the seat of My mercy. On the Day of Resurrection I will marry you to a hundred houris created by My hand, and I will feast at your wedding for four thousand years, each day of which is as long as this world; and I will order a crier to call: where are the ascetics of the world? Visit the wedding of the ascetic Jesus son of Mary. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/136}
