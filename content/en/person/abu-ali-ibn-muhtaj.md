## Overview
A Samanid commander of the house of Muhtaj. He governed Khurasan after Ibn Ilyas, then took control of al-Rayy and killed Makan ibn Kaki the Daylami in 329 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 274-275}
