## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Uthman ibn Sa'ud ibn Bulayhid, a Saudi scholar of the Bulayhid family in Najd. He was born in 1310 AH (1892 CE) and died in 1377 AH (1957 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.111}
