## Summary
Muhibb al-Din al-Tabari, shaykh of the Haram at Mecca and shaykh of the Shafi'is and hadith master of the Hijaz, composed a large six-volume work on rulings which he carried to Yemen and read to its sultan. Among his works are *al-Riyad al-nadira fi fada'il al-'ashara*, *Dhakha'ir al-'uqba fi manaqib dhawi'l-qurba*, *al-Samt al-thamin fi manaqib ummahat al-mu'minin*, *al-Qura fi sakin umm al-qura*, and a commentary on *al-Tanbih*. Al-Dimyati, Ibn al-'Attar, Ibn al-Khabbaz and al-Barzali transmitted from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 743}
