## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awad was a Hijazi judge, born and died at Yanbu' in 1293 AH (1876 CE). He studied at al-Azhar, took his fiqh in the Hanafi school and travelled to Jaghbub, then held the judgeship of Yanbu' from 1280 AH until his death. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.320}

Al-Zirikli heard from members of the Al 'Awad in the Hijaz that he left writings. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.320}
