## Summary
Bishr ibn Bashshar al-Mujashi'i was one of the worshippers. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 476 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 132 and its opening on page 133; some letters of the heading are unclear in the scan. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/132-133}

## Three counsels from three worshippers
Bishr said he met three worshippers in Jerusalem. To the first he said: admonish me. He said: cast yourself with the decree wherever it casts you; that is more fitting to empty your heart and lessen your cares, and beware of resenting it, lest anger seize you while you are heedless of it. To the second he said: admonish me. He said: seek His pleasure by leaving what He forbids; that brings you nothing but nearness to Him. To the third he said: admonish me; he wept, and said: son of my brother, do not seek in your affair a management other than His, lest you perish among those who perish and go astray among those who go astray. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/133}
