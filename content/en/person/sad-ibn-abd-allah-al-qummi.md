## Overview
This is an Imami, that is Shi'i, jurist, as al-Zirikli gives him: Sa'd ibn Abd Allah al-Ash'ari al-Qummi, Abu al-Qasim, of the people of Qum, who travelled much in search of hadith; he died in 300 AH, 913 CE, and no birth year is given {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 82}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names among his books "Maqalat al-Imamiyya", which he thinks may be "al-Maqalat wa al-Firaq", printed; "Manaqib Ruwat al-Hadith"; "Mathalib Ruwat al-Hadith"; "Fadl Qum wa al-Kufa"; "al-Muntakhabat", of about a thousand leaves; "Fadl al-Arab"; and "al-Radd ala al-Ghulat" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 82}.
