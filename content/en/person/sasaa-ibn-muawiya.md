## Overview
Sa'sa'a ibn Mu'awiya ibn Husayn ibn 'Umayr ibn 'Ubada ibn al-Nazzal ibn Murra ibn 'Ubayd ibn Muqa'is ibn 'Amr ibn Ka'b ibn Sa'd ibn Zayd Manat ibn [[group/banu-tamim|Tamim]] was the paternal uncle of [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]]. [[person/al-hakim-al-nisaburi|Al-Hakim]] takes his lineage from Abu 'Ubayda Ma'mar ibn al-Muthanna, and [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] notes in the *Talkhis* that he came to the Prophet in a delegation and accepted Islam. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 711}

## "Enough for me, enough"
Al-Hakim gives, with his chain through [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan]], Sa'sa'a's report that he came to the Prophet and heard him recite the two verses of Surat al-Zalzala saying that whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it ([[passage/quran-99-7|al-Zalzala 7]] and [[passage/quran-99-8|8]]). He said: "I do not mind if I never hear anything else; it is enough for me, enough." Al-Dhahabi left this hadith out of the *Talkhis*. {cite:mustadrak-ilmiyya-ata-v3 p. 711}
