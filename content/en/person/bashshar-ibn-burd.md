## Overview
Bashshar ibn Burd, the poet; Ibn al-Nadim lists him among the poets who professed Islam outwardly while concealing unbelief — the source's own judgement, recorded here for its historical frame; the same charge was laid on other poets of his age. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 471}
