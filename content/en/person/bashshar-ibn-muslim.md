## Overview
Bashshar ibn Muslim ibn Amr al-Bahili, brother of [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]]. He gave [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] a horse, which won a race for him, so al-Hajjaj granted him seven hundred jaribs, and it is said four hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Bashshar dug for this land the river known as "the river of Bashshar". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The first and second reports about the size of the grant are both in the source, and the page is not fixed; this needs an additional source.
