## Overview
Maytham ibn Ali ibn Maytham, Kamal al-Din, known as Maytham al-Bahrani, was a scholar of letters and kalam, of the Imami jurists, of Bahrayn. He visited Iraq and died in his country after 681 AH (1282 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.335}

His works include an abridged commentary on Nahj al-Balagha; a commentary on the Hundred Words; al-Qawa'id on kalam; Istiqsa' al-Nazar on the imamate of the twelve imams; a treatise on the etiquette of debate; and Tajrid al-Balagha on the ma'ani and bayan, also called Usul al-Balagha. {cite:al-aalam v.7 p.335}
