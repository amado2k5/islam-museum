## Overview
Malik ibn Zaflah of Bali, one of the Irash, who was commander of the Arab allies in the ranks of Heraclius on the day of Mu'ta. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## At Mu'ta
The data record that he was killed at the hands of Qutba ibn Qatada al-Udhri, commander of the Muslim right wing. Ibn Hisham also gives his name as "Malik ibn Rafila". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} See [[event/battle-of-mutah|the battle of Mu'ta]].
