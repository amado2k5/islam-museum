## Overview
Ahmad ibn Nasr al-Khuza'i, a man of hadith who upheld commanding right and forbidding wrong. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.529-530}

In 231 AH the caliph [[person/al-wathiq|al-Wathiq]] had him brought in chains from Baghdad to Samarra. He asked him about the Qur'an and he said it was not created. He asked him about the vision of God, and he related a hadith. Al-Wathiq said to him: "You lie." He answered: "Rather, you lie." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.529-530}

Al-Wathiq killed him with his own hand. His head was hung in Baghdad, and his body at Samarra for six years, until al-Mutawakkil took it down and buried it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.529-530}
