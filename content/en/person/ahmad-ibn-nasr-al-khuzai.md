## Overview
Ahmad ibn Nasr ibn Malik ibn al-Haytham al-Khuza'i al-Marwazi, shaykh of the people of the Sunna in the inquisition; al-Wathiq called on him to profess the createdness of the Qur'an and he refused, so he was executed and crucified, his head displayed on the eastern side and his followers hunted down and imprisoned. Al-Marwazi reported hearing Ahmad ibn Hanbal mention him and say, 'May God have mercy on him — he gave his soul generously.' {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1026}
