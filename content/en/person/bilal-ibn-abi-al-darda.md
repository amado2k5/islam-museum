## Overview
Bilal ibn Abi al-Darda al-Ansari narrated from his father and from Umm al-Darda, and Abu Mushir said he was older than Umm al-Darda the Younger {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}. Al-Bukhari calls him the emir of al-Sham {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from his father and Umm al-Darda {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}. Khalid ibn Muhammad al-Thaqafi, Humayd ibn Muslim, Ibrahim ibn Abi Abla, Hariz ibn Uthman and Abu Bakr ibn Abi Maryam narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}.

## Judge and governor
Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz says he held the judgeship after al-Nu'man ibn Bashir, and when Abd al-Malik became caliph he replaced him with Abu Idris al-Khawlani {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}. Al-Dhahabi lists among his sources Akhbar al-Qudat, Ibn Asakir's history and Tahdhib al-Tahdhib {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}.

## His death
Abu Ubayd said he died in 93 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1242}.
