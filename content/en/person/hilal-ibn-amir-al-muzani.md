## Overview
Hilal ibn Amir al-Muzani of Kufa, a Companion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}

## Transmission and assessment
He narrated from his father and Rafi' ibn Amr al-Muzani; Sayf ibn Umar, Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Umawi and Marwan ibn Mu'awiya narrated from him. Ibn Ma'in called him reliable, and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3495}
