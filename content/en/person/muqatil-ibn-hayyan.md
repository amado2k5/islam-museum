## Summary
Muqatil ibn Hayyan al-Nabati al-Balkhi, Abu Bistam, a leading scholar whom Ibn Ma'in and Abu Dawud counted as trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2071}

## Works
He fled to Kabul in the days of Abu Muslim, called many to Islam, and died in Kabul before 150 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2071}
