## Overview
A client (mawla) of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], whom Ibn Hisham mentions only in the [[event/sariyya-of-zayd-to-madyan|expedition of Zayd ibn Haritha to Madyan]]: the Prophet sent Zayd with Dumayra and a brother of his, according to the report of Fatima bint al-Husayn. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}

## Marks of the report
- His brother's name is not given. What the companion books say about Dumayra has not been read here (needs source).
