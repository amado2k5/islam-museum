## Overview
Sultan II ibn Saif ibn Sultan ibn Saif ibn Malik al-Ya'rubi was the fifth of the Ya'rubid Ibadi imams of Oman. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 1131 AH, 1719 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 110}.

## His imamate
He was given the pledge of allegiance at al-Rustaq after the death of his father, in 1123 AH, and his authority grew; he fought on land and at sea {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 110}. Wars broke out between him and the Persians, in which he prevailed, and he took Bahrain, Lak (as printed) and Hormuz {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 110}.

## The fort of al-Hazm and his death
He built the fort of al-Hazm and moved to it; his days were peaceful and he remained there until he died in the fort of al-Hazm {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 110}.
