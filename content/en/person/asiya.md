## Overview
Asiya daughter of Muzahim, the wife of Pharaoh. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari-bugha 3230}

Al-Bukhari relates that the Prophet said many men reached perfection, but among women only Asiya the wife of Pharaoh and Maryam daughter of Imran reached it. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari-bugha 3230}

The rest of her story needs an additional source and is not set out here.

## In Tareq al-Suwaidan's programme "Immortal Women"
Asiya bint Muzahim takes up much of the fourth and fifth episodes of [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]'s programme [[work/nesaa-khalidat-program|"Immortal Women" (Nisa' Khalidat)]]. He names her among the most perfect of women, recalling the hadith of the four perfect women, and calls her "the truthful one". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep07 00:11:30} He relays "many reports" that she was a believer and very beautiful, that Pharaoh wanted to take her from her father by force, and that she accepted a formal marriage only to save her family. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:12:30} Childless, she longed for a child to raise, and when the infant Moses was drawn from the river she pleaded with Pharaoh not to kill him; he says Pharaoh gave way only because she insisted. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep04 00:14:30} He holds that Pharaoh kept Moses only for her sake. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:00:30}

In his telling she was among those who believed in Moses when he returned to Egypt with his message, together with his mother and his wife, and joined the believers when they left. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:24:30} On her end he gives two reports without choosing between them: that she left with the emigrants, or that Pharaoh had her tortured and killed for refusing to renounce her faith. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:25:00} He notes that history does not record when she, Moses' mother or Moses' wife died. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:27:00} He reads her prayer for a house near God in Paradise as the choice of a woman who had all of Egypt at her disposal and wanted only the Hereafter. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan nesaa-khalidat/ep05 00:29:30}
