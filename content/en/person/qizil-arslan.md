## Overview
Qizil Arslan b. Ildegiz the Atabeg — king of Azerbaijan, Arran, Hamadan, Isfahan and Rayy after his brother al-Bahlawan Muhammad; he succeeded his brother Shams al-Din Pahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan and Persian Iraq, who died in 581. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 749, 769-770}

## His rule and murder
Sultan Tughril Beg the Seljuk was under his command, as his own father Arslan Shah had been under his father Ildegiz's; he kept five thousand mamluks, and was murdered treacherously on his bed in Sha'ban 587. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 749, 769-770}
