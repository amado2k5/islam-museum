## Summary
Amina bint al-Sharid was an eloquent woman, quick of answer, counted among the partisans of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 15}.

## Imprisonment and curse
After Ali was killed, [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] hunted his partisans; her husband [[person/umar-ibn-al-humq-al-khuzai|Umar ibn al-Humq al-Khuza'i]] fled, and Mu'awiya kept Amina in Damascus prison for two years {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 15}. [[person/abd-al-rahman-al-dakhil|Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Hakam]] then captured and killed Umar ibn al-Humq — the first head carried in Islam — and Mu'awiya sent it to Amina in her cell, telling the guards to record whatever she said {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 15-16}.

They laid the head in her lap and she spoke her famous verses beginning "Wahazannahu li-sighrihi fi dar hawan" and cursed him {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 16}. She was released and came before Mu'awiya in a gathering that included Iyas ibn Hilal, where she rebuked him and said to him, when he asked about killing her, "as he killed my husband yesterday" {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 16-17}. Mu'awiya then gestured with his finger for her to leave and said he would not hear her in Syria again {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 17}.

## End
She took what Mu'awiya sent to silence her tongue — remarking "ya 'ajij, Mu'awiya kills my husband and sends me gifts" — and set out toward the Jazira, passing Homs where the plague killed her; [[person/al-asla-al-hilali|al-Asla' al-Hilali]] then brought Mu'awiya the news that his prayer against her had been answered {cite:alam-al-nisa-kahhala-risala-1984-v1 p. 17}.
