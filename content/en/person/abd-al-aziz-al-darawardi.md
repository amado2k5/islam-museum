## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad ibn Ubayd al-Darawardi, al-Juhani by clientage, al-Madani, Abu Muhammad, was a hadith scholar. He died in 186 AH, 802 CE; al-Zirikli gives no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.

## Those who narrated from him, and his name
A great many narrated from him, among them Sufyan and Shu'ba. Al-Zirikli adds that his memory was poor, without attributing the judgement to anyone. His name points to Darawurd, a village of Khurasan, from which his family came; he was born and died in Medina {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 10}.
