## Overview
Hajib ibn Zurara al-Tamimi, one of the lords of [[group/banu-tamim|Tamim]]. Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum mentions him with [[person/hani-ibn-masud|Hani ibn Mas'ud]] and [[person/al-samawal-ibn-adiya|al-Samaw'al ibn Adiya]] as examples of the fact that keeping a pledge was a religion among the people of ignorance, which they held to. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.47}. See [[concept/jahili-virtues|the virtues of the people of ignorance]].

## What is not mentioned here
The story of Hajib's bow, which he pawned to Khosrow and which he redeemed, as the chroniclers have it, has not yet been added from its source; al-Rahiq does not go beyond mentioning his name.

## For young visitors
Hajib was a man of Tamim who became proverbial for keeping his word.
