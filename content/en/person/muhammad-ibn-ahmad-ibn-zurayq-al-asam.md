## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Zurayq al-A'sam, a client of the Banu al-Abbas, was appointed judge by [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] after he entered [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] on the night of Friday, 21 Muharram 288 AH, and he judged according to his own school. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} The text does not name the school and none is inferred here, so the wording stays as it is: "he judged according to his school". See [[event/al-hadi-entry-into-sanaa-288]].
