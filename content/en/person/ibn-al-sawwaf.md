## Summary
Abu Ya'la al-'Abdi Ahmad b. Muhammad, of al-Hasan al-Basri's descent, known as Ibn al-Sawwaf — shaykh of Iraq's Malikis; he studied fiqh under Qadi 'Ali b. Harun and transmitted from al-Barqani and others — a signpost, ascetic, striving in worship, versed in hadith; some said he was an imam in ten sciences; he died in Ramadan 490 at Basra, aged ninety. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 395}
