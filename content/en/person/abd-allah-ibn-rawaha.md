## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abd Allah ibn Rawaha]] al-Ansari al-Khazraji, kunya Abu Muhammad. He attended the Aqaba as a chief, and attended Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Hudaybiyya, Khaybar and the Lesser Pilgrimage, and was one of the commanders at the raid of Mu'tah. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

## Lineage
His mother was Kabsha bint Waqid ibn Amr ibn al-Itnaba, of the Banu al-Harith of the Khazraj, and he was the maternal uncle of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

## His battles
He was among those who attended the Aqaba, a chief of the Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, and attended Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Hudaybiyya, Khaybar and the Lesser Pilgrimage, and all the battles with the Prophet except the conquest and what followed it, for he had been killed before it. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

## Mu'tah
He was one of the commanders at the raid of Mu'tah. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

## What is related of him
Ibn al-Athir relates from Hammad ibn Zayd, from Thabit, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla, that he came to the Prophet while he was preaching and heard him say "Sit down", so he sat where he was outside the mosque until the Prophet finished. The Prophet said to him: "God increase you in eagerness for obedience to God and obedience to His Messenger." {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

It is said that he was the first to go out to the raid and the last to return from it, and that he was among the poets who defended the Messenger of God. {cite:usd-al-ghaba p.666}

## For young visitors
Abd Allah ibn Rawaha was one of the Ansar of Medina. He attended the Aqaba, Badr, Uhud and all the battles. He was a poet who defended the Prophet with his verse, the first to set out for a raid and the last to return from it. He was martyred at Mu'tah.

## Notes for researchers
- The date of his death at Mu'tah is given in other sources; it does not appear on page 666 as read, so we do not give a year here.
- The verses Ibn al-Athir quotes from his poetry about the Prophet are not reproduced here; the edition should be consulted before quoting them.
- The statement that he was "one of the commanders at the raid of Mu'tah" is from Ibn al-Athir and should be checked against Ibn Ishaq's and Ibn Sa'd's accounts.

## His sending to al-Yasir ibn Rizam
Ibn Ishaq relates that the Prophet sent him with a few of his companions to [[person/al-yasir-ibn-rizam|al-Yasir ibn Rizam]], the Jew of Khaybar, when he learned that he was gathering Ghatafan to attack the Prophet; they lured him down with a promise and then killed him on the road; see [[event/killing-of-al-yasir-ibn-rizam|the account]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/266}

## For Explorers
Abdullah ibn Rawaha the Ansari of Khazraj, kunya Abu Muhammad; he witnessed the Aqaba as the deputy of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, and witnessed Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Hudaybiyya, Khaybar and the umra of fulfillment and every witness field with the Prophet save the conquest and after — he had fallen before it — and was one of the commanders at Mu'tah. His mother was Kabsha bint Waqid ibn Amr ibn al-Itnaba of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, and he was the uncle of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir.

Abdullah ibn Rawaha the Ansari of Khazraj, kunya Abu Muhammad, witnessed the Aqaba as a deputy and witnessed Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Hudaybiyya, Khaybar and the umra of fulfillment, and was one of the commanders at Mu'tah. His mother was Kabsha bint Waqid ibn Amr ibn al-Itnaba of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, and he was the uncle of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir.
