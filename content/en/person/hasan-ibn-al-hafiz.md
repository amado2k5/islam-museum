## Summary
Al-Hasan b. al-Hafiz li-Din Allah 'Abd al-Majid al-'Ubaydi; his father's heir and vizier, who held power three years — wronging, crushing, slaying, killing forty amirs in a single night. His father feared him and raised men against him; they met and Egypt was convulsed — then his father slipped him a poisoner and he perished in 529. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 570}
