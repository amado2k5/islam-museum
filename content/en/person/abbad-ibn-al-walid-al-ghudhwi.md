## Overview
Abu Badr al-Ghudhawi, 'Abbad b. al-Walid; he heard Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi, and Ibn Abi'l-Dunya and Ibn Sa'id transmitted from him — he was truthful. He died in 262, or said to be 258. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 130}
