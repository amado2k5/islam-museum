## Overview
Nasr ibn Ahmad al-Samani, ruler of Transoxiana, died in 289 AH in the days of al-Mu'tadid, and the rule passed to his brother Isma'il ibn Ahmad {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.631-632}.

## Ibn al-Nadim's account
Ibn al-Nadim reports that he imprisoned al-Husayn ibn Ali al-Marwazi, the Isma'ili missionary, and that he died in prison. Then al-Nasafi won him over and brought him into the mission, and made him pay the blood-money for al-Marwazi: 119,000 dinars. Then he regretted it, showed his regret, and died {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.266}.
