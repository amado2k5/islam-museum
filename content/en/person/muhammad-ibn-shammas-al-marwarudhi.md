## Summary
Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Hamid ibn Na'im ibn Shammas al-Marwarudhi, who died in 282 AH as the book gives. He heard from Affan ibn Muslim, Sulayman ibn Harb, Abd al-Samad ibn Hassan and Zakariyya ibn Adi; Ahmad ibn Kamil the judge and Ibn Khuzayma transmitted from him. Abu Bakr al-Khallal says he related questions from Abu Abd Allah (Ahmad) that reached no one else, and was trustworthy among the people of Marw al-Rudh. His work is the Masa'il of Ibn Shammas, questions from Imam Ahmad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15419}
