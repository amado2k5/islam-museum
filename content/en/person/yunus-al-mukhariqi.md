## Overview
Yunus ibn Yusuf ibn Musa al-Shaybani al-Mukhariqi, shaykh of the Yunusi dervishes who are ascribed to him. Ibn Khallikan mentions that he was a righteous man. When his companions were asked about their shaykh, they said that he had no shaykh of his own, and that he was "one drawn" (majdhub), that is, drawn to the path of good. They relate wonders to him {cite:wafayat-ayan p.256-257}.

## Death
He died in his village of al-Qunniya, one of the districts of Dara, in the year 619 AH, having nearly reached ninety, and his grave there is well known. al-Maqrizi mentions the year 719 AH, and the text considers this a slip of the pen {cite:wafayat-ayan p.256-257}.

According to the data, he is the founder of [[group/yunusiyya|the Yunusiyya]] {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
