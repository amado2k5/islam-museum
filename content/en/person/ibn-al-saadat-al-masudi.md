## Summary
Abu 'Abdallah Muhammad b. Abi'l-Sa'adat 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muhammad b. Mas'ud, Taj al-Din Ibn al-Sa'adat known as al-Muhadhdhabi; the Shafi'i jurist, Sufi, adib of Khurasan and Marw al-Rudh — he devoted himself to the Haririan Maqamat and commented them at length, encompassing their intent as none had, coming to five great volumes no commentator of that book reached nor half of it, a famous and abundant work. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## His sojourn
He dwelt at Damascus in the Sumsatiyya khanqah with people taking from him; when Salah al-Din entered Aleppo in 579 he took from its library. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
