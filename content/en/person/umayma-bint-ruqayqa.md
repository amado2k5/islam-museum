## Summary
Umayma bint Ruqayqa bint Abd Allah ibn Bijad ibn Umayr ibn al-Harith, a Companion. She narrated from the Prophet and from his wives, and Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir narrated from her. She transmitted the report of the women's pledge, in which the Prophet said he does not shake hands with women and that his word to one woman is his word to a hundred. Dates not given. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13351}
