## Overview
Baybars al-Jashankir, a Mamluk sultan who was given the allegiance to the sultanate on 23 Shawwal 708 AH, after [[person/al-nasir-muhammad-ibn-qalawun|al-Malik al-Nasir]] wrote from al-Karak to depose himself. The caliph invested him and clothed him in the black robe and the round turban. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.734}

In Rajab 709 AH al-Nasir came back seeking the kingship, and Baybars fled with a group of his companions; he was then caught and killed in the same year. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.734}

## In Ibn Hajar's al-Durar al-Kamina
[[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] gives an entry for Baybars al-Jashnakir, al-Malik al-Muzaffar, a mamluk of al-Mansur Qalawun: he led the pilgrimage in 701 and fought in person at the battle of Shaqhab. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/503} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/504} He became sultan in Shawwal 708 after al-Nasir withdrew to al-Karak; when al-Nasir advanced in 709 his amirs deserted him and he abdicated, then was arrested and put to death. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/505} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/506} He restored the al-Hakim mosque after the earthquake and founded his khanqah near Bab al-Nasr; he died in late Dhu al-Qa'da 709, and Ibn Hajar describes him as good, pious and abstinent. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/507}
