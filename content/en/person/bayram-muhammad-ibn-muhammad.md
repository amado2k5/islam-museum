## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Husayn Bayram, a Tunisian scholar from the Bayram family, and the son of [[person/bayram-al-thani-muhammad|Bayram al-Thani]]. He was born around 1201 AH (1786 CE) and died around 1259 AH (1843 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
