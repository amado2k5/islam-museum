## Summary
Oemar Mita — in full, Abu Bassam Oemar Mita — is an Indonesian preacher born in Kudus on 14 May 1979. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023} {cite:uin-antasari-thesis-oemar-mita} He is the founder of Syameela, the study platform carried on his YouTube channel Oemar Mita Syameela, which counted 655,000 subscribers, 55,122,917 views and 1,223 videos as read by vidIQ on 14 November 2026 — a dated platform figure, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:youtube-oemar-mita-syameela} {cite:vidiq-oemar-mita}

## Early life and education
He grew up close to Islamic learning, studying at the Darusy Syahadah pesantren, from which he graduated in its KMI unit's second cohort in 1999. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023} He then studied at the Faculty of Sharia of the Institute of Islamic and Arabic Sciences (LIPIA) in Jakarta. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023} {cite:uin-antasari-thesis-oemar-mita}

## Syameela and his lectures
He founded Syameela as his da'wah platform, and the channel that carries it has run since 9 March 2016. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023} {cite:youtube-oemar-mita-syameela} He is known for thematic lecture series delivered across Indonesian cities, in particular a series on eschatology — the signs of the Hour and what follows death. {cite:youtube-oemar-mita-syameela} Academic study of his preaching has concentrated on his rhetoric: a 2023 Prophetica journal article analysed his digital-da'wah rhetoric against Aristotle's ethos, pathos and logos. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023}

## Books
The Prophetica article lists his books as *The Art of Life*, *This is The End*, *Make Peace with Yourself*, *Berdamai di Masa Senja* and *The Day After You Die*, among others. {cite:prophetica-oemar-mita-2023} The UIN Antasari thesis chapter adds *Golden Ways to Jannah* and *A Letter to Allah*. {cite:uin-antasari-thesis-oemar-mita}
