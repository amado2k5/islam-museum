## Summary
Ja'far ibn al-Fadl ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn al-Furat, Abu'l-Fadl, known as Ibn Hinzaba the vizier; born Dhu'l-Hijja 308; he settled in Egypt and held the vizierate for its amir Kafur — his father had been al-Muqtadir's vizier; he transmitted from Muhammad ibn Harun al-Hadrami and his generation of Baghdadis and used to say: whoever brings me a majlis of al-Baghawi's sessions I will enrich; he dictated hadith at Egypt — al-Daraqutni came out to him, stayed with him a while and compiled his Musnad, and a great sum came to him through it; al-Daraqutni narrated hadiths from him in the book al-Madih; he died in Rabi' I 391. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.216}
