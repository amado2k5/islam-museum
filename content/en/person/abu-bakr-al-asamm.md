## Overview
Abu Bakr Abd al-Rahman ibn Kaysan al-Asamm, a scholar of the Mu'tazila. Ahmad Amin counts him in his table among the most important men of the Basra branch of Mu'tazilism, with Mu'ammar ibn Abbad and al-Nazzam. {cite:duha-islam p.760}

## What is known of him
The material available from "Duha al-Islam" goes no further than this point. His date of birth and death and the details of his doctrine are not given here. A second source is needed.
