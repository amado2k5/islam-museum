## Overview
Wasil ibn Taysala al-Anbari, mentioned by al-Baladhuri in Futuh al-Buldan {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## The report
al-Baladhuri reports that he finished off Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim in the year 85 AH, after Musa had fallen from his horse {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
