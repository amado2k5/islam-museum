## Summary
Amira Elghawaby is a Canadian journalist and human-rights advocate who in January 2023 became Canada's first Special Representative on Combatting Islamophobia — a federal advisory post created after a series of attacks on Canadian Muslims, including the 2021 killing of the Afzaal family in London, Ontario. {cite:wikipedia-amira-elghawaby}

## Journalism and advocacy
An Egyptian-Canadian from Toronto, Elghawaby worked as a journalist — including at the Toronto Star and CBC — before moving into civil-society work, serving as communications director at the National Council of Canadian Muslims (NCCM), the country's principal Muslim civil-rights organisation, and later at the Canadian Race Relations Foundation. Her writing and advocacy have covered religious discrimination, hate crimes and media representation of Muslims. {cite:wikipedia-amira-elghawaby}

## The special representative role
Appointed by the government in 2023, she was charged with advising on federal policy, documenting anti-Muslim hate and recommending measures across security, education and public life. Her appointment itself drew early controversy — remarks she had earlier made about Quebec and identity were criticised by Quebec politicians, and she apologised for the phrasing — a dispute the museum records attributed: critics saw in it confirmation of bias against the province; supporters saw a manufactured row over a rights advocate's past commentary. {cite:wikipedia-amira-elghawaby}

## Work in office
In office she produced recommendations on combating Islamophobia in federal institutions and worked with municipalities and school boards, at a time when reported anti-Muslim and anti-Palestinian incidents in Canada rose sharply after October 2023. Her mandate has been described as among the first of its kind in a Western national government. {cite:muslim500-amira-elghawaby}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles her as the public face of Canada's institutional response to anti-Muslim hatred — a journalist turned official whose post made Islamophobia a named subject of federal policy. {cite:muslim500-amira-elghawaby}
