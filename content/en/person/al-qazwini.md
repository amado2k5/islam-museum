## Overview
Zakariya ibn Muhammad al-Qazwini, author of "The Wonders of Creation" and "The Monuments of the Lands and the Reports of the Servants", which Haji Khalifa says he composed in 674 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 179-180}

## His death
His pupil Ibn al-Fuwati relates that he died in Muharram 682 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 179-180} The Gregorian date recorded in the data (about 1283 CE) is derived from the Hijri date. The data describe him as a man of learning and writing in Persia and Central Asia.

His birth date and the details of his life need an additional source.
