## Summary
Arif al-Yamani left people and things to guard against turning away and loss. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 481 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], on page 134. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}

## His saying
Ali ibn Bakkar relates that he heard Arif al-Yamani say: it is among God's turning away from a servant that He occupies him with what does not profit him. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/134}
