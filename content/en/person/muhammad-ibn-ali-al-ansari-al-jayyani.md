## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abdallah ibn Yasir al-Ansari al-Jayyani, a hadith scholar, commentator and scholar of the readings. He was born in Jaén in al-Andalus in 492 AH (1098 CE), then travelled to the East and settled in Damascus, where he taught the Qur'an. He died in 563 AH (1167 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
