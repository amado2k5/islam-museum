## Overview
Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Jama'a al-Kinani, Izz al-Din, was of Hama origin, born in Damascus and later of Egypt. Al-Zirikli calls him a hafiz and chief judge {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 26}. He gives his birth as 694 AH, 1294 CE, and his death as 767 AH, 1366 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 26}.

## Judgeship and death
He took the judgeship of the Egyptian lands in 739 AH, lived beside the Holy Places in the Hijaz and died in Mecca {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 26}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli lists among his books Hidayat al-Salik ila al-Madhahib al-Arba'a fi al-Manasik, in manuscript; al-Manasik al-Sughra; Takhrij Ahadith al-Rafi'i; al-Tusa'iyyat, in hadith, in manuscript; Nuzhat al-Albab fima la Yujad fi Kitab, an abridged manuscript on humorous matter; and Uns al-Muhadara bi-ma Yustahsan fi al-Mudhakara, a large manuscript volume wholly in the author's hand {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 26}. Al-Zirikli says he saw this copy of Uns al-Muhadara at Magnisa, number 5286, and that the author completed it in 762 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 4 p. 26}.
