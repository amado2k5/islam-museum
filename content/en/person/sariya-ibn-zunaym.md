## Overview
Sariya ibn Zunaym, a commander of the Muslims who made for Fasa and Darabjird in the year 23 AH. [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] instructed him to take the mountain during the Muslims' battle with the Kurds of Fars {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.178}.
