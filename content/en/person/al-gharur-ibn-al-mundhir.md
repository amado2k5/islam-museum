## Overview
Al-Ghurur ibn al-Mundhir ibn al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir, from the house of the kings of al-Hira. With him, after the death of the Prophet, those of [[group/abd-al-qays|Abd al-Qays]] who had embraced Islam returned to their former religion. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}

## In Ibn Hisham's biography
Ibn Ishaq mentions him in the account of [[person/al-jarud-al-abdi|al-Jarud]]: when those who had embraced Islam returned to their former religion with al-Ghurur ibn al-Mundhir, al-Jarud rose and called to Islam. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222} The editors relate from [[person/al-suhayli|al-Suhayli]] that his name was al-Mundhir, and that he was called al-Ghurur because he deceived his people on the day of the [[event/ridda-wars|Ridda]] wars. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}

## Note
His relation to [[person/al-numan-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir]] is taken from his lineage as printed. The book has nothing more about him than this report.
