## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed in the year of Yarmuk in Umar's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him among Banu Sahm, saying he was killed in the year of Yarmuk in Umar ibn al-Khattab's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
