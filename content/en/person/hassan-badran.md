## Summary
Hassan Badran (Arabic: حسان بدران) is an Arabic-language motivational speaker. His own website describes him as a prominent figure in the field of self-development presented with an Islamic vision, addressing a wide audience in different parts of the world. {cite:hassan-badran-official-website}

Through his website he offers training courses, among them a course titled "Unleash Your Inner Power", as well as private consultation sessions. Testimonials published on the site come from clients and followers in Jordan, Palestine, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia and other countries. {cite:hassan-badran-official-website}

He publishes his talks, together with Quran recitations in his own voice, on his YouTube channel, and his video descriptions link his Instagram account and his Facebook page. {cite:hassan-badran-youtube-channel} {cite:hassan-badran-instagram}

No source consulted for this record gives his date or place of birth. The digits in his Instagram username are part of the handle and are not a documented birth year. {cite:hassan-badran-instagram}
