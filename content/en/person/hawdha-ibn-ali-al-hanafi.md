## Overview
Hawdha ibn Ali al-Hanafi, lord of [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]] from [[group/banu-hanifa|Banu Hanifa]], and given the title "the one with the crown". Ibn al-Athir reports that Khosrow called for a necklace of pearls and put it on his head. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 349}

In Ibn al-Athir's account, he accompanied the caravan men to Khosrow after the affair of the money of Hurmuz, and said to Khosrow about the matter of the Banu Tamim: "There is nothing between us but death." Then he asked the governor of Bahrain, al-Mukaabir, for a hundred captives of the Banu Tamim taken on the day of al-Mushaqqar, and he set them free. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 349}
