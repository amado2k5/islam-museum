## Summary
Busr ibn Said al-Madani, a devout Successor (tabi'i) and mawla of Ibn al-Hadrami. He related from Abu Hurayra, Uthman, Abu Sa'id, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and Zayd ibn Thabit, among others, and was related from by Salim ibn Abi al-Nadr and Ya'qub ibn al-Ashajj. Ibn Ma'in and al-Nasa'i called him trustworthy. The book gives his death as 100 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6799}
