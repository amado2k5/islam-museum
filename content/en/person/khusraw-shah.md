## Summary
Khusrawshah, king of Ghazna after his father Bahramshah ibn Mas'ud ibn Ibrahim ibn Mahmud ibn Sebuktegin; just and well-ordering, a drawer-near of the scholars, his reign lasted nine years and his son Malikshah ruled after him; his entry falls in the events of 555. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 655}
