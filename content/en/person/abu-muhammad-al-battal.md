## Overview
Abu Muhammad al-Battal, one of the Syrian Muslim commanders and champions. He was in the army of [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]], based at Antioch. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.56}

Maslama appointed him over ten thousand men whom he made the advance guard of the army. He was killed in 112 AH, or it is said 113 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.56}

## Reports attributed to him
The book says that many of the reports attributed to him are exaggerated, and that some are fabricated. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.56}
