## Summary
[[person/thawban-mawla-al-nabi|Thawban]], client of [[person/muhammad|the Messenger of God]], "a famous Companion" — it is said he is of the Arabs, Himyari of Him ibn Sa'd Humayr, or it is said of al-Sarat {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 191}.

## His freeing and service
The Messenger of God bought him then freed him, and he served him until the Prophet died; then he moved to al-Ramla, then Hims, and died there in 54 AH — said Ibn Sa'd and others {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 191}.

## What is related from him
Ibn al-Sakan relates from Yusuf ibn Abd al-Hamid that he met Thawban, who told him the Prophet prayed for his household — Thawban asked: am I of the People of the House? On the third time he said: "yes, so long as you do not stand at a gate begging, nor go to an emir to ask of him" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 191}.

Abu Dawud relates from Thawban, who said: the Messenger of God said: "who will pledge to me never to ask people anything, and I pledge him Paradise?" Thawban said: I — and he never asked anyone for anything {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 191}.

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Hadaya al-Rahman'
In the twenty-seventh episode of his programme [[work/hadaya-al-rahman|'Hadaya al-Rahman']], [[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] relates that Thawban, the Prophet's servant, asked him three times about the deed dearest to God; the Prophet was silent the first two times, as if awaiting revelation, then advised him to prostrate much, telling him that every prostration raises him a degree and wipes away a sin. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:00:30} He says lovers seek what their beloved loves so as to spend their lives on it, and that the distance between two degrees of Paradise is as the distance between heaven and earth. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:01:30} He pairs it with the story of Rabi'a ibn Ka'b, who served the Prophet and slept at his door from fatigue; when the Prophet told him to ask for whatever he wished, he asked for his company in Paradise, and was advised to help him to it by prostrating much. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:02:30} The wisdom, for him, is that the heart imitates what the body does: whoever prostrates with his body prostrates with his heart, and so lives submissive to God, humble, gentle and easy with people. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:05:00}
