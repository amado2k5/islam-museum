## Summary
Al-Fadl ibn al-Sabbah, Abu al-Abbas al-Simsar, was a Baghdadi hadith scholar. He heard from Hushaym ibn Bashir, Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Abu Mu'awiya al-Darir, Abu Ubayda al-Haddad, Waki', Muhammad ibn Fudayl and Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Abi Fudayk. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}

Those who transmitted from him included Tha'lab ibn Muhammad al-Dhari', Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Sabur al-Daqqaq, Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Rawwas, Abdallah ibn Muhammad al-Baghawi, Ahmad ibn al-Hasan al-Sabbahi and others. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}
