## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia and was killed at Ayn al-Tamr with Khalid ibn al-Walid in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him last of the eleven men of Banu Sahm, and says he was killed at Ayn al-Tamr with Khalid ibn al-Walid on the way back from al-Yamama in Abu Bakr's caliphate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
