## Summary

Al-Harith ibn Arfaja ibn al-Harith ibn Malik ibn Ka'b al-Ansari, of the Aws. Musa ibn Uqba and others list him among the veterans of Badr. Ibn Abd al-Barr thought Ibn Ishaq had left him out; Ibn Hajar answers that Ibn Ishaq does name him among those at Badr, and that Ibn Hisham traces his lineage to Ka'b ibn al-Najjar. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.263}
