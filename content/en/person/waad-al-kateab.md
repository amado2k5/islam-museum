## Summary

Waad Al-Kateab (born c. 1991) is the pseudonym of a Syrian journalist, filmmaker and activist whose documentary For Sama (2019) won the BAFTA for Best Documentary and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en} {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-ar}. She was included in Time's 100 Most Influential People in 2020 {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en}.

## Life and work

Al-Kateab moved to Aleppo in 2009, aged eighteen, to study at the University of Aleppo, and when the Syrian Civil War broke out in 2011 she began reporting as a citizen journalist for Channel 4 News in the United Kingdom {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en} {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-ar}. She stayed through the siege of Aleppo, documenting her life over five years as she married Hamza, a doctor, and gave birth to their daughter Sama; her coverage won an International Emmy, the first for a Syrian {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en}. For Sama, co-directed with Edward Watts, won the Prix L'Oeil d'or at Cannes in 2019 and became the most nominated documentary in BAFTA history with four nominations {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en} {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-ar}. After fleeing Aleppo in December 2016 she settled in the United Kingdom with her husband and daughters {cite:wikipedia-waad-al-kateab-en}.
