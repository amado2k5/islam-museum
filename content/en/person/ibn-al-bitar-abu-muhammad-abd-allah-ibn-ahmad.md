## Overview
Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Malaqi, known as Ibn al-Bitar. A scholar of plants, drugs and medicine from Málaga in al-Andalus. He died in 646 AH (1248 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
