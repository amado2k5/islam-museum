## Summary
Al-Shayma' bint al-Harith al-Sa'diyya, a woman of Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr of [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]] and the Prophet's foster-sister in [[person/halima-al-sadiyya|Halima al-Sa'diyya]]'s house; she fell among the captives of [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn]] in year 8 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 417}.

## The Prophet's honour to her
Al-Shayma' was among the Hunayn captives held at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]]; when she was brought to the Prophet she made herself known to him and he recognised her by a mark — he honoured her, spread his cloak for her and seated her on it, then granted her favour and returned her to her people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 417}.

The foster-kinship of Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr was among the strongest of what the [[event/hawazin-delegation-and-amnesty|Hawazin delegation]] invoked with the Prophet when they asked him to return their women and children {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 421}.

## For young visitors
Al-Shayma' was the Prophet's foster-sister, having lived with him as a child in Banu Sa'd's desert. When he found her among the captives he honoured her, seated her on his cloak and released her to her family.
