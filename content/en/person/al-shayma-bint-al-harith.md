## Summary
Al-Shayma' bint al-Harith al-Sa'diyya was a woman of Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr of [[group/hawazin|Hawazin]], and the Prophet's foster-sister in the house of [[person/halima-al-sadiyya|Halima al-Sa'diyya]]. She fell among the captives of [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Hunayn]] in 8 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.417}.

## The Prophet's honour to her
Al-Shayma' was among the captives of Hunayn who were held at [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]]. When she was brought before the Prophet, she made herself known to him, and he recognised her by a sign. He honoured her, spread his cloak for her and had her sit on it. Then he showed her favour and returned her to her people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.417}.

Foster-kinship with Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr was among the strongest things that the [[event/hawazin-delegation-and-amnesty|Hawazin delegation]] invoked with the Prophet when they asked him to return their women and children {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.421}.

## For young visitors
Al-Shayma' was the Prophet's foster-sister, because she had lived with him as a child in the desert of Banu Sa'd. When he found her among the captives, he honoured her, seated her on his cloak, and released her to her people.

## Her name and her care of the Prophet (The Sealed Nectar, p.55)
The Sealed Nectar calls her Hudhafa or Judhama bint al-Harith, and says that al-Shayma' became the title that prevailed over her own name. It counts her among the Prophet's foster-siblings in Halima's house, together with the two sons of al-Harith, Abd Allah and Unaysa. It notes that she used to tend the Prophet in his childhood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} {cite:zad-al-maad 1/19}.
