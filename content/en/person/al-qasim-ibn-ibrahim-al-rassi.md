## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim ibn Isma'il al-Hasani al-Alawi, Abu Muhammad, known as al-Rassi, was one of the imams of the Zaydis; the entry calls him a jurist and a poet. He was born in 169 AH, 785 CE, and died in 246 AH, 860 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}. He was the full brother of Ibn Tabataba, Muhammad ibn Ibrahim {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}.

## His residence, his call and his works
He lived in the mountains of Qudus on the outskirts of Medina and made his call public after his brother's death, a point at which al-Zirikli gives the year 199 AH; he died at al-Rass, a black mountain near Dhu al-Hulayfa, six miles from Medina {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}. He left twenty-three treatises in manuscript, on the imamate and other matters, among them "al-Radd 'ala Ibn al-Muqaffa'", printed with an Italian translation, "Siyasat al-Nafs", "al-'Adl wa-al-Tawhid" and "al-Nasikh wa-al-Mansukh" {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}. Al-Marzubani mentions him among the poets without referring to his imamate or his books, and quotes good verse of his {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}. Among his descendants was Husayn ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Qasim al-Zaydi, ruler of Yemen {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 5 p. 171}.
