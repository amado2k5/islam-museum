## Overview
Sultan Abd al-Majid I, son of [[person/mahmud-ii-uthmani|Mahmud II]], who came to the throne after his father. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.548}

## Character
Al-Salabi describes him as frail in build, very intelligent, practical and merciful, and one of the most honoured of the sultans of the house of Uthman. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.548}

## Reform in his reign
Al-Salabi says that he was under the influence of his grand vizier Rashid Pasha, and that the reform movement in his reign turned on three points: borrowing from the West in organising the army, government and administration; steering society toward a secular form; and moving toward centralising authority in Istanbul and the provinces. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.548} This is the author's assessment, not the museum's judgement.
