## Overview
Muhammad ibn Saud ibn Muhammad ibn Muqrin ibn Markhan belonged to the Banu Mani', traced to Murra ibn Dhuhl ibn Shayban of Adnan. Al-Zirikli calls him the first of the Al Saud in Najd to bear the title of imam; he was based at Diriyah {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.

## His rule
He took the emirate two years, or four, after his father's death, in 1139 AH, and his conduct was good and his strength grew {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}. His brother Thunayan helped him, and he ruled alone after Thunayan's death, in 1160 AH {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}. In his time, in 1157 AH, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, whose reforming call is known by his name, came to Diriyah, and the two agreed that Ibn Saud would be a guardian of the religion and a supporter of the sunna, while Ibn Abd al-Wahhab would go on proclaiming his call openly {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}. The emirate widened to cover most of Najd; only Riyadh, al-Hasa and al-Qasim stayed outside his rule {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.

## Death
Al-Zirikli describes him as brave and resolute. He died in Diriyah in 1179 AH (1765 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 128}.
