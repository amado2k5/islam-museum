## Overview
Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn al-Husayn, known as Badi' al-Zaman al-Hamadhani, author of the "Maqamat" and of letters. "Shadharat al-Dhahab" describes him as eloquent and an excellent poet, and says that al-Hariri followed him in his maqamat. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.158-159}

He died in Herat on Friday, the eleventh of Jumada al-Akhira 398 AH. The report says that he was poisoned, and it is also said that he was struck by a stroke and buried quickly. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.158-159}
