## Summary
Serdar Tuncer is a Turkish poet, writer and television presenter born on 16 August 1978 in Altındağ, Ankara, and the best-known face of the MyMecra YouTube channel. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr} {cite:yunus-emre-institute-serdar-tuncer}

His own YouTube channel counted 249,000 subscribers as of 13 November 2026 per vidIQ — a dated platform count, not a museum-verified fact. {cite:vidiq-serdar-tuncer}

## Early life and beginnings
Tuncer was born on 16 August 1978 in Ankara. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr} {cite:yunus-emre-institute-serdar-tuncer}

He began his broadcasting career in 1996 with a radio programme at Ankara Bayrak Radio. {cite:yunus-emre-institute-serdar-tuncer}

## Career and programmes
His Kanal A programme "Gecede Bir Gün" was chosen as the best television culture-and-arts programme of 2005, and he presented "Yıldızdan Mahyalar" on the same channel. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}

He presented live Ramadan iftar programmes from Topkapı Palace on TRT 1 in 2008–2011, the "Yitik Hazine" programme on Semerkand Radio, and "Başka Şeyler" on CNN Türk until his resignation from that channel on 3 April 2015. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}

He has written a column in the Yeni Şafak newspaper since 8 October 2015. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}

A London concert of his with Uğur Işılak on 26 March 2019 was cancelled after complaints and threats from some groups, as the article records on the basis of press reports. {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}

He is publicly associated with the MyMecra channel as its founder and best-known face; that association is stated here in prose only, and no relations edge from this record to the group's record is written, since the group is catalogued separately and its channel's audience is not counted as his. {cite:yunus-emre-institute-serdar-tuncer}

## Works
Among the books the article lists are Sen İstanbul Kokardın (1997), Aynalar (2003), Satır Arası Hikâyeler (2005) and Delilim Yok Kalbimden Başka (2018). {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}

Among his poetry albums are Sen İstanbul Kokardın (2009), Bir Hilal Uğruna (2011) and Şiirin Sultanları (2013). {cite:wikipedia-serdar-tuncer-tr}
