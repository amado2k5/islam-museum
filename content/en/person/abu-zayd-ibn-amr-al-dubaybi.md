## Overview
Of the [[group/banu-dubayb|Banu Dubayb]] of Judham. He rode his horse with Shammar, with Hassan and Unayf, the two sons of Milla, to the army of Zayd ibn Haritha, then with Rifa'a ibn Zayd to the Prophet in Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/262} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/263} He was the one who said to the Prophet, when he ordered what had been taken returned: "Release for us whoever is alive, and whoever was killed is under the sole of my foot." The Prophet said: "Abu Zayd has spoken the truth." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/264} See [[event/raid-of-zayd-on-judham|the raid of Zayd ibn Haritha on Judham]].

## Notes for researchers
- The name of his horse and the wording of his speech come from Ibn Ishaq's report without a named chain of transmission. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/264}
