## Summary
Rukn al-Din Sulayman ibn Qilij Arslan, Sulayman II, was a Seljuk sultan of Rum ([[polity/rum-seljuk-sultanate|the sultanate of Rum]]) who died in 600 AH (1204 CE). Ibn al-Athir gives his line as Sulayman ibn Qilij Arslan ibn Mas'ud ibn Qilij Arslan ibn Sulayman ibn Qutlumush ibn Saljuq, ruler of the lands of Rum between [[place/malatya|Malatya]] and [[place/konya|Konya]] {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.292-293}.
## Reign, as Ibn al-Athir tells it
In Ramadan 597 AH he took Malatya from his brother Mu'izz al-Din Qaysarshah after besieging it for some days, and then went on to Arzan al-Rum, whose lord, of the house of Ibn Saltuq, came out trusting in a settlement; Sulayman seized him, took the city and ended that house, which had ruled it for a long time {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.275}. In the year 600 al-Afdal, the Ayyubid prince, sent to him offering obedience, with the sermon and the coinage in his name in al-Afdal's own lands; Sulayman agreed and sent him a robe of honour, and the sermon was given for him at Samosata in 600 {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.283-284}.

Ibn al-Athir also records that, five days before his death, he took Ankara, held for years by a brother who defied him: the brother gave up the city in exchange for a fortress on the edge of the lands and an oath, and Sulayman then seized him with his two sons and killed him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.292-293}.
## Death and succession
He died on 6 Dhu al-Qa'da 600 AH of colic after seven days of illness {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.292-293}. In Rajab 601 his brother [[person/ghiyath-al-din-kaykhusraw-i|Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw]] took the lands of Rum that had been Sulayman's, from Sulayman's son {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir OpenITI text v.10 p.295}. The museum's record places his burial at the [[place/al-eddin-mosque|Ala al-Din mosque]], from open reference data {cite:wikidata}.
