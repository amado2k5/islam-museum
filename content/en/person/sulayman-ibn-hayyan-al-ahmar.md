## Summary
Abu Khalid Sulayman ibn Hayyan al-Ahmar was a transmitter who advised his chosen companions with the greatest share of counsel. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 500, sequence 501, on page 142. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}

## A letter on being ashamed before God
Salama ibn Ghaffar relates from Hajjaj that Abu Khalid al-Ahmar wrote to him in a letter: know that the truthful ones were ashamed before God to be today on the same station as yesterday. The name of Hajjaj's own source is cut in the scan and is not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/142}
