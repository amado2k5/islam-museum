## Summary
Muhammed Ali is a British Muslim dawah speaker known by his YouTube channel name, The Muslim Lantern. He publishes no surname, and is known by the name he presents himself under. {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern} {cite:muslimlantern-org}

His channel describes itself as "the official channel of Muhammed Ali", and states the goals of The Muslim Lantern as "Tackling Muslim Contemporary Issues, Refuting The Doubts put Forward Against Islam And Educating those who seek Guidance and Truth". {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern}

## Dawah work
Muhammed Ali holds his conversations at Speakers' Corner in London's Hyde Park, an open-air speaking venue long known for religious debate. There he talks with Christians, atheists, agnostics and questioners of many backgrounds, and publishes the recordings on his channel. {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern}

The topics of his conversations, as his channel's titles show, include the divinity of Jesus, the Trinity, the textual criticism and alleged corruption of the Bible, atheism, evolution and the existence of God, and contemporary questions such as women in Islam and slavery in religious texts, alongside reaction videos to other people's debates and clips. {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern}

He has also held live discussions with other dawah speakers and online personalities, and his channel publishes recordings of interlocutors embracing Islam at the end of their conversations with him. {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern}

## Projects
He runs his project through Muslim Lantern LLC. His official website offers booking of free private sessions with him "for queries about religion, doubts concerning Islam, personal development and more". {cite:muslimlantern-org}

His channel links to a Telegram channel, a donation page supporting the channel, and a clothing store carrying his project's name. {cite:youtube-muslim-lantern}
