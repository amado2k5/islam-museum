## Overview
Abu al-Fath Sahl ibn Ahmad ibn Ali al-Arghiyani, the Shafi'i jurist, was an imam of great standing in knowledge; he took fiqh at Marw from Shaykh Abu Ali al-Sanji, then read under the qadi Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Marwazi and acquired his method until the qadi said 'none has picked up my method as he did'. {cite:wafayat-ayan v.2 p.455}

He entered Nishapur and read usul al-fiqh under Imam al-Haramayn Abu al-Ma'ali al-Juwayni, debated in his assembly and won his approval, then returned to the region of Arghiyan and held its judgeship for years in good repute; he then set out for the hajj, meeting shaykhs in Iraq, the Hijaz and the hills, hearing and being heard, and died in 529 AH (1135 CE). {cite:wafayat-ayan v.2 p.455}
