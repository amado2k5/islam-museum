## Overview
Al-Madaini, a narrator named in al-Baladhuri's "Futuh al-Buldan". He relates from Maslama ibn Muharib that Khalid ibn Yazid advised Abd al-Malik to forbid the dinars of the Byzantines and to prohibit dealing in them, and to forbid paper to enter their lands, and for a time nothing was brought to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He is the narrator of this report in [[event/qaratis-dinar-crisis|the crisis of the paper and the dinars]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The museum's data include a passage on dates and offices that al-Mas'udi and Ibn al-Nadim attribute to him, but its attribution to al-Madaini is not clear. It needs review and an additional source.
