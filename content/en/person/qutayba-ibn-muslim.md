## Overview
Qutayba ibn Muslim al-Bahili, a commander in Khurasan and Transoxiana. al-Hajjaj appointed him over Khurasan in 86 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.88-91}

## The conquests
al-Tabari reports his peace with the king of Khwarazm, and his conquest of Samarqand {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.471}, Bukhara {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.442}, Taliqan {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.447}, and Kashgar {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.500}.

al-Sallabi traces the stages of his conquests: the recovery of Taliqan, Saghaniyan and Tukharistan in 86 AH; then Bukhara between 87 and 90 AH; then Samarqand between 90 and 93 AH; and reaching Kashgar near the borders of China {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.88-91}.

## His death
al-Tabari reports his death in Khurasan in 96 AH {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.506}. The book says that he was killed in Dhu al-Hijja of that year, and that no conquests in the East are recorded after him {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.92-101}.
