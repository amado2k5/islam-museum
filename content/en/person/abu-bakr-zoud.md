# Abu Bakr Zoud

## Summary
Abu Bakr Zoud is an Australian Islamic preacher and teacher based in Sydney. His official website describes him as a graduate of the Islamic University of Madinah who specialises in Qur'aat and currently teaches tafsir at the Islamic College of Australia. {cite:website-abu-bakr-zoud}

## Channel and teaching
His YouTube channel carries his recorded lessons and lecture series under his own name, and his video descriptions link his official website and accounts. {cite:youtube-abu-bakr-zoud} The Yaqeen Institute has also carried him as a teacher in its 'Qur'an 30 for 30' series, introducing him as a shaykh from Australia. {cite:yaqeen-abu-bakr-zoud}
