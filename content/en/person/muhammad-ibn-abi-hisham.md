## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] says: Muhammad ibn Abi Hisham told me from [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Yahya ibn al-Husayn]] that a woman who had drunk wine was taken, with witnesses testifying to it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120} The text gives no descent, home or death date for him and no link to the narrators before him, and none is assumed. The report is in [[event/al-hadi-more-wine-penalty-reports-from-al-kufi-and-ibn-abi-hisham-286]], a Zaydi account in the voice of the imam's followers.
