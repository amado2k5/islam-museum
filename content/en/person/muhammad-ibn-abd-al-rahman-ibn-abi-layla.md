## Overview
Abu Abd al-Rahman Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla al-Ansari, judge of Kufa and one of its jurists. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.45}

## Dates
Al-Shirazi says that he was born in 74 AH and died in 148 AH, at the age of seventy-two. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.45} The figures do not agree with the years of birth and death, and need a second source.

## Teachers and pupils
He learned jurisprudence from [[person/al-shaabi|al-Sha'bi]] and [[person/al-hakam-ibn-utayba|al-Hakam ibn Utayba]], and from him took Sufyan al-Thawri and al-Hasan ibn Salih ibn Hayy. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.45}

Al-Shirazi relates a saying of Sufyan al-Thawri: "Our jurists are Ibn Abi Layla and Ibn Shubruma." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.45}

## In Kitab al-Umm

Ibn Abi Layla is one of the two Kufan jurists whose disagreements [[person/abu-yusuf-al-qadi|Abu Yusuf]] collected and [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] transmitted in [[work/ikhtilaf-al-iraqiyyin|the Disagreement of the Iraqis]] in al-Umm, titled "the book of what Abu Hanifa and Ibn Abi Layla disagreed on, from Abu Yusuf". {cite:kitab-al-umm-wafa-v8 p.217} Among his views at its opening: if the tailor and the owner of the cloth disagree, the owner saying "I told you a shirt" and the tailor "you told me a coat", the tailor's word stands, against Abu Hanifa; and if cloth is lost with the tailor, fuller or dyer, they are liable even if their hands did nothing to it. {cite:kitab-al-umm-wafa-v8 p.217} See [[concept/tadmin-al-sunna|liability of craftsmen]].
