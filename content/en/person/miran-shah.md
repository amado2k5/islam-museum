## Summary
Miran Shah ibn Timur was a Timurid prince. Biblissima records his birth in 1367, with a question mark, and his death in 1408 CE {cite:biblissima Q232466}; the museum's card has 1366. It describes him as ruling the west and disputing the succession after his father.
## In the sources
The record points to the inscriptions of the Gur-i Amir tomb at Samarkand and to a genealogical table of the Mughal family; the card links his death to Tabriz and his burial to Gur-i Amir at medium confidence {cite:biblissima Q232466}.
