## Summary
Abu'l-Fadl Salih ibn Muhammad al-Shirazi lived in Baghdad's eastern quarter and related from 'Affan, 'Ali ibn al-Ja'd and Khalid ibn Khidash; Abu 'Umar, Ibn al-Sammak and Abu Bakr al-Shafi'i transmitted from him. He was reliable and trustworthy, a Qur'an reciter who said: I have completed four thousand khatmas. He died in Shawwal 283. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 183}
