## Summary
Abu Manad Badis b. al-Mansur b. Buluggin b. Ziri b. Manad, the Sanhaji amir; father of al-Mu'izz b. Badis, he ruled the kingdom of Ifriqiya as deputy of the 'Ubaydi al-Hakim, who styled him Nasir al-Dawla after his father — a great king, resolute and formidable. {cite:wafayat-ayan}

## Death
He reviewed his armies at the Dome of Peace on Tuesday the twenty-ninth of Dhu'l-Qa'da 406, pleased with their array — and died that same night. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
