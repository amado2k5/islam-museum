## Summary
al-Malik al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din Mahmud, lord of Hama — the last of its Ayyubid kings; he died on 21 Dhu'l-Qa'da 698 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 774}.

## Details
Under the dead of 698 Ibn al-'Imad gives his full line: "al-Malik al-Muzaffar Taqi al-Din Mahmud ibn al-Malik al-Mansur Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar Mahmud ibn al-Mansur Muhammad ibn 'Umar Shahanshah al-Hamawi, the last of the kings of Hama," dying on the twenty-first of Dhu'l-Qa'da {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 774}. With him ended the Ayyubid emirate of Hama, where a branch of the house of Ayyub had ruled for close on two centuries since Saladin and his brothers divided the provinces among the family's sons {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 774}. Recorded in the same year is Jamal al-Din Yaqut al-Musta'simi, the calligrapher, last holder of the headship of the script style traced to Ibn al-Bawwab {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 774}.
