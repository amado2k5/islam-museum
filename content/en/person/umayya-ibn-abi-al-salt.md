## Overview
Umayya ibn Abi al-Salt al-Thaqafi, the poet, who was among those who spoke of divinity before Islam. al-Raheeq al-Makhtum mentions him together with Qass ibn Sa'ida and Zayd ibn Amr, among those whom Quraysh thought the Prophet resembled in this respect {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.240}.

## In al-Raheeq al-Makhtum (p.77)
al-Raheeq al-Makhtum reports, from Muhammad al-Ghazali, that when news of [[event/al-dawa-al-sirriyya|the secret call (al-da'wa al-sirriyya)]] reached Quraysh, they paid it no attention. Perhaps they took Muhammad to be one of the pious ones who speak of divinity and its rights, as Umayya ibn Abi al-Salt, Qass ibn Sa'ida, Amr ibn Nufayl and their like did {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.77} {cite:fiqh-al-sira-al-ghazali p.76}.
