## Overview
Ju'da daughter of al-Ash'ath ibn Qays, the wife of al-Hasan ibn Ali. In a report in al-Suyuti, [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Muawiya]] sent word to her that she should poison him and that he would marry her; she poisoned him. When he died and she asked Yazid to keep his word, he said that he had not wanted her for al-Hasan, so he would not want her for himself. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.318}

The account is given as "in the report" and the museum does not vouch for it; it needs an additional source.
