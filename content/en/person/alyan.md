## Overview
Alyan, governor of the frontier of al-Andalus, according to al-Baladhuri on the authority of al-Waqidi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}

## His meeting with Tariq
Tariq ibn Ziyad met him on his raid in 92 AH. Tariq granted him safe conduct, and carried him and his companions to al-Andalus in ships. He is mentioned in [[event/conquest-of-al-andalus|the conquest of al-Andalus]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}
