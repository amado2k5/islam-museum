## Overview
Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili is a man known for horsemanship who answered [[person/muhammad-ibn-bihar-al-hamdani|Muhammad ibn Bihar al-Hamdani]]'s praise of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] by saying that no one is as strong with the lance as Abu al-Husayn. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} See [[event/al-hadi-march-from-saada-to-najran-284]].
