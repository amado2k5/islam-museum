## Overview
Al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid, a Companion from Medina, who witnessed things with the Prophet when he was a boy. He related more than thirty hadiths. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## The narration
His aunt related that she took him to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] and said that her nephew was ill. He passed his hand over his head and prayed for blessing for him, then performed ablution and al-Sa'ib drank from the water of his ablution. He stood behind his back and saw the seal of prophethood between his shoulders, like the button of a canopy. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.190}
