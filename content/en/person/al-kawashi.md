## Overview
Al-Kawashi, mufassir, scholar, muqri', ascetic, resident of Kawasha citadel of Mosul; he excelled in the readings, tafsir and Arabic, and wrote the Great and Small tafsirs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 642-643}

## His zuhd
Al-Dhahabi said: unrivalled in his time, peerless in zuhd, righteousness, abstinence, truthfulness and striving — the sultan and those below him visited him and he cared nothing for them, did not rise for them, grew vexed by them and accepted nothing from them; he had unveiling and karamat. Ibn Khruf al-Mawsili and others took the readings from him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 642-643}
