## Overview
Abu Hafs Umar ibn Yahya al-Hintati, of the Hintata tribe. He was with Ibn Tumart at Bejaia, and Ibn Khaldun counts him among the first ten men of his call. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1669}
