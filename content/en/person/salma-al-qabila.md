## Overview
Salma al-Qabila, a Companion. She was the wife of Abu Rafi', client of the Prophet, and a midwife of the Prophet's household. She acted as midwife for the Prophet's daughter Fatima's children: al-Hasan, al-Husayn, Zaynab and Umm Kulthum {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul}.

## Life
Before that she had been one of Khadija's servants, and she lived on after the Prophet's death. She resided in [[place/medina|Medina]] {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul}.

The source of the report is the book "Women around the Messenger" (pp.379-384).
