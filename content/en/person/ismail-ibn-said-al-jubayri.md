## Overview
Isma'il ibn Sa'id ibn 'Ubayd Allah ibn Jubayr ibn Hayya al-Thaqafi al-Jubayri of Basra, a transmitter of hadith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10352}

## Transmission and assessment
He narrated from his father, and Bishr ibn Dam and others narrated from him. al-Tirmidhi reported one hadith from him on funerals and graded it sound, and Ibn Hibban listed him among the trustworthy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10353}
