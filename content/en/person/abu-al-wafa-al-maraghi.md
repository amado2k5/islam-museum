## Summary
Abu'l-Wafa' al-Maraghi, among the senior scholars of al-Azhar al-Sharif and former assistant secretary-general of the Islamic Research Academy {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 2}. He died in 1401/1981 according to the book's cover {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 1}. Among his books is *Min qadaya al-'amal wa'l-mal fi'l-Islam* in the Academy's scholarly series — the work this entry is based on. The death date still needs a second source.
