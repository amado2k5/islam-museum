## Overview
Yazid ibn Harun ibn Zadhan al-Wasiti, client of the Banu Sulaym, one of the most eminent and great of the people of hadith {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 426}.

## Dates
He was born in 117 AH and died in Wasit in 206 AH, aged eighty-nine. His father had served in the kitchen of Ziyad ibn Abihi, his son, Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr and al-Hajjaj {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 426}.

## In al-Dhahabi's Siyar
Al-Dhahabi calls him the exemplary imam, shaykh al-Islam, Abu Khalid al-Sulami by clientage, the memoriser of Wasit, and places his birth in 118 AH. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4233}

He heard 'Asim al-Ahwal, the judge Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari, Sulayman al-Taymi, Sa'id al-Jurayri, Humayd al-Tawil, Dawud ibn Abi Hind, 'Abdallah ibn 'Awn, Shu'ba ibn al-Hajjaj, Sa'id ibn Abi 'Aruba, Muhammad ibn Ishaq, Isma'il ibn 'Ayyash, Qays ibn al-Rabi' and a multitude; he was a head in knowledge and practice, trustworthy and authoritative, of great standing. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4233}

Baqiyya ibn al-Walid narrated from him despite being older, as did 'Ali ibn al-Madini, Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu Bakr ibn Abi Shayba, Zuhayr ibn Harb, Muhammad ibn 'Abdallah ibn Numayr, al-Hasan ibn 'Arafa, al-'Abbas al-Duri and others. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.4233}
