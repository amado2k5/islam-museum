## Overview
Abu Sa'id Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi ibn Hassan al-Anbari, a jurist of Basra. He is the last person Shirazi mentions among the jurists of Basra at this point, and he died in 198 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 49}

## On his lineage
The editor's note says that Ibn Sa'd and al-Qutayba did not trace his lineage, and that Ibn Hajar confirms it as "Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi ibn Hassan ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Anbari". {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 49}
