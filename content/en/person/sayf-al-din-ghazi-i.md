## Overview
Sayf al-Din Ghazi I, lord of Mosul and son of [[person/imad-al-din-zangi|Imad al-Din Zangi]]. The Shadharat al-Dhahab describes him as having religion, goodness, courage and boldness {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 619}.

## His death
He died in Jumada al-Akhira in the year 544 AH, having passed forty years of age. His brother Qutb al-Din Mawdud succeeded him in rule {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 619}.

The Common Era date in the museum's data is derived from the Hijri year.
