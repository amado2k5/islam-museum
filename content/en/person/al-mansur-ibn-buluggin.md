## Overview
Al-Mansur ibn Buluggin ibn Ziri al-Sanhaji, the second of the Ziridid rulers in Ifriqiya. He succeeded his father as governor of the Fatimids. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The war against the man of the donkey
He led a war against Abu Yazid Makhlad ibn Kaydad, titled the man of the donkey, when he rose against the Fatimid state and besieged it in its seat. The report ends with the victory going to al-Mansur, who killed him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The Hammadid state
He appointed his brother Hammad over Ashir and al-Masila, and that was the origin of the state of the Banu Hammad in the central Maghrib. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
