## In brief
Mu'ammar b. Rashid, the imam and proof Abu Urwa al-Azdi their client of Basra, chief of the great figures and scholar of Yemen; he narrated from al-Zuhri, Qatada, Amr b. Dinar, Ziyad b. Ilaqa, Yahya b. Abi Kathir, Muhammad b. Ziyad al-Jumahi, and their layer, while the two Sufyans, Ibn al-Mubarak, Gundar, Ibn Uluyya, Yazid b. Zuray', Abd al-A'la, Hisham b. Yusuf, Abd al-Razzaq, and others narrated from him — and even his own shaykhs Ayyub and Abu Ishaq narrated from him. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.182-183}

## His standing
Ahmad said "set Mu'ammar before anyone and you will find him above them", and Yahya b. Ma'in counted him among the most reliable narrators of al-Zuhri. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.182-183}
