## Overview
Mu'ammar ibn Rashid, a Basran. Ahmad Amin counts him among the second generation of writers of biography and the campaigns. The tables give his death as 150 AH. {cite:duha-islam p.639}

The date of death (150 AH) is converted to the Gregorian calendar approximately, and needs an additional source.
