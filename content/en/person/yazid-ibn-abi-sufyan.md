## Overview
Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan ibn Harb, brother of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]], a Companion and commander, and amir of the army of Damascus. When [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] wanted to raid Syria after finishing with the Ridda, he removed Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As from his banner, on the advice of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], and handed it to Yazid. He set out with it, with Mu'awiya carrying it before him, and took the road of Tabuk {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.149-150}. He took part in [[event/mobilisation-for-syria|the mobilisation for the conquest of Syria]].

## Command
According to al-Waqidi, Abu Bakr appointed him over Damascus, one of the three provinces of Syria. According to Abu Mikhnaf, Abu Bakr said to the commanders: "If you unite to fight, your commander is Abu Ubayda; otherwise it is Yazid" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.149-150}.

He died in the plague of Amwas around the year 18 AH, and this date is derived in the data {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
