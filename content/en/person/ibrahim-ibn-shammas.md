## Summary
Ibrahim ibn Shammas al-Samarqandi settled in Baghdad, is known for constant worship, and is named among the lovers of God; he transmitted hadith. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him notice 470 in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], which lies on page 128 alone. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/128}

## The hadith on Solomon
Abu Nu'aym transmits from him a hadith from Abu Hurayra on the Prophet: "Have you considered what Solomon was given of his kingdom? It only increased him in humility, and he would not raise his eyes to the sky out of humility before his Lord." {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/128}
