## Overview
Abu Hassan al-Ziyadi, one of the jurists tested in [[event/mihna-of-the-quran|the Mihna of the createdness of the Qur'an]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.491-492}

## In the test
Al-Suyuti says that he answered that the Qur'an is the speech of God, and that if the commander of the faithful orders anything they hear and obey. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.491-492}

## In al-Ma'mun's letter
Al-Ma'mun faulted him in his letter for claiming a false clientage, and he denied being a client of Ziyad ibn Abih. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.491-492}
