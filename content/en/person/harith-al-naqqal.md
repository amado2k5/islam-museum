## Overview
Al-Harith ibn Shurayh al-Naqqal, a Sunni jurist. Al-Shirazi in "Tabaqat al-Fuqaha" reports that he was the one who carried the book "al-Risala" to Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54}

Al-Shirazi says that he died in 236 AH, a date equated with about 851 CE. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.54} The museum's data holds no second source for this date, and it needs an additional source.
