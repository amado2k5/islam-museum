## Summary

Syafiq Riza bin Hasan bin Abdul Qadir bin Salim bin Zaid Basalamah is an Indonesian preacher, lecturer and author, born on 15 December 1977 in Jember, East Java{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-id}, of Hadhrami Arab descent{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}. He took his bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees, all with honours, at the Islamic University of Madinah, teaches in Jember, and his lectures are broadcast on Rodja channels and YouTube{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}.

## Upbringing and education

Basalamah studied at the Al-Irsyad Al-Islamiyyah pesantren in Bondowoso, completing the intermediate level in 1993 and the secondary level in 1996{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}. He then went to Madinah, graduating from the Faculty of Da'wah in 2003, taking his master's in 2007 and his doctorate in 2013, all cum laude{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-id}; his dissertation treated the role of Islamic institutions in countering Christianisation in Indonesia{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}.

## Career

Since 2016 Basalamah has been a permanent lecturer in the Ahwal Al-Syakhshiyah programme at STDI Imam Syafi'i in Jember, and he is a member of the Fatwa Council of the Al-Irsyad association{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-id}. He gives regular classes broadcast by Rodja TV and Radio Rodja, and his recordings reach about 1.63 million subscribers on YouTube in platform figures of October 2026{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}.

## Impact and audience

Basalamah is among the most widely followed Salafi preachers on Indonesia's digital platforms, with about 1.63 million YouTube subscribers in platform figures of October 2026 — platform figures that change constantly and are not presented by the museum as fixed facts{cite:wikipedia-syafiq-riza-basalamah-en}.
