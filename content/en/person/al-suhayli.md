## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd Allah al-Suhayli, a Maliki grammarian and memoriser imam from al-Andalus, author of "al-Rawd al-Unuf", a commentary on the Life of Ibn Hisham. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.751-752}

## His works
Among his books are "al-I'lam bima Ubham al-Qur'an min al-Asma' wa al-A'lam" and "Natai' al-Nazar". He has the well-known verses of "al-Faraj", and Ibn Dihya related the report of them. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.751-752}

## His learning and life
He was blind, took the readings from a group, and related from Ibn al-Arabi and others. He excelled in Arabic, languages and reports, and was known for righteousness, piety and contentment. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.751-752}

He died in 581 AH, and the Gregorian conversion is derived. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.752}
