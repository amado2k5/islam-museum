## Overview
Al-Najashi al-Harithi, a poet who drank wine during Ramadan. Ali had him flogged eighty lashes and imprisoned him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.306}.

## Wine in Ramadan
In the section on the punishment for drinking wine, al-Sallabi reports that Ali flogged the poet al-Najashi al-Harithi eighty lashes for drinking wine during Ramadan, and then jailed him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.306}. The next day Ali brought him out and gave him twenty more lashes, telling him that the flogging was for his boldness against God and for breaking the fast in Ramadan {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.306}. The book transmits the report from Ata, on the authority of his father {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.306}.
