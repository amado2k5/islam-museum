## Overview
Al-Damaghani, qadi al-qudat of Baghdad; he took office on Tuesday 10 Dhu'l-Qa'da 447 with the robe of honour — the chief judge of the Tughril Beg and Malikshah era. He attended the caliph al-Qa'im's marriage contract to Khadija, the Sultan's niece, in 448 alongside Judge al-Mawardi, stood among his attendants at Tughril's investiture in 449, received al-Qa'im returning from exile in 451 when only he and three witnesses met him, pledged to al-Muqtadi in 467, and inspected the slain in the Madrasa-market riot of 470. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.507,511,523,550,634,655}

## His hilal fatwa
Asked in the hilal-sighting disturbance of 448 about the Sharif who had made the people break their fast, he said: "on Abu Hanifa's school — and it is mine — with a clear sky no testimony counts unless a large number up to two hundred; the Shafi'i school decides on the testimony of two." {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}
