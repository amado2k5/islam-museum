## Overview
Shahanshah ibn Bahram, a king of the Sasanian Persians. al-Tabari mentions him under the heading "The Reign of Shahanshah ibn Bahram" {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.2 p.54}.

al-Tabari reports at this point what was said about the years of his reign and his affairs. The details are not in the museum's data, and the entry needs an additional source.
