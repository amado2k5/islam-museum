## Overview
Shaykh Abu Bakr ibn Qawam ibn 'Ali ibn Qawam ibn Mansur ibn 'Ali al-Balisi, the exemplary ascetic and blessing of Syria, uncle of al-Dhahabi's own shaykh Muhammad ibn 'Umar; born in 534 AH and raised in Balis, a small town in the Aleppo region. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1236}

## His way
He was devoted, scrupulous, quietly constant, rich in spiritual states; humble, ever cheerful, abundant in religion, a follower and caller to the Sunna, sought out for visits — many benefited from his company — while his own shaykh forbade him to speak of the mystical states that came upon him. He died in 658 AH; his son al-Najm was also counted among the righteous youth. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1236}
