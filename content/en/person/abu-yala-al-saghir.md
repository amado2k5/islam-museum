## Summary
Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Khalaf ibn Ahmad al-Farra', Imad al-Din, Abu Ya'la al-Saghir — son of the qadi Abu Khazim and grandson of the qadi Abu Ya'la — a Baghdadi Hanbali jurist and the shaykh of the madhhab in his time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16244} He heard hadith from his father and his uncle the qadi Abu al-Husayn and their peers, studied fiqh under them, excelled in the madhhab, the divergences and disputation, and he gave fatwa, taught and held judgeship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16244} A large circle read the madhhab and the divergences under him, among them Abu Ishaq al-Saqal, Abu al-Abbas al-Qati'i, Abu al-Hasan ibn Warkhadh and Abu al-Baqa' al-Ukbari. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16244} His works, as legible in the scan, include al-Ta'liqa on the questions of divergence, al-Mufradat and al-Nukt wa-l-Isharat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16244} The encyclopedia dates him 494–560 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16244}
