## Overview
Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Ghitrif al-Wadi'i asked al-Hadi after the fight at [[place/sawhan-najran|Sawhan]] how many of the enemy he had killed, and al-Hadi answered with what he had speared and with the blow he struck when Ibn Humayd got away. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.162} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} The index lists the name at pp. 162, 204, 283, 287 and 346; he is of the [[group/wadia-tribe-yemen|the Wadi'is]], and the museum does not link him to Banu Tarif. See [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]].
