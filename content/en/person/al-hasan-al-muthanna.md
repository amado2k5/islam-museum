## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, Abu Muhammad al-Hashimi, known as al-Hasan al-Muthanna, was the chief of the Talibids of his age, his father's executor and administrator of his grandfather's alms; he lived and died at Medina about 90 AH (708 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.186}

Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan held him in awe; accused of corresponding with the people of Iraq who promised him the caliphate, the report reached al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, who ordered his governor of Medina to flog him — the governor did not, and wrote to al-Walid clearing him. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.186}
