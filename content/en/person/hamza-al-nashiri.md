## Overview
Hamza ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Nashiri, Abu al-Abbas al-Yamani al-Shafi'i, Taqi al-Din, knew botany, history and letters. He was born at Nakhl in the Zabid valley, grew up and died at Zabid in 926 AH (1520 CE), and went often to Mecca. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.277}

Al-Sakhawi met him at Mecca in 886 AH and said he wrote down pieces of his verse for him and passed on notes of the biographies of his countrymen, his letters never ceasing; he described him as kind, cheerful and fond of joking. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.277}

His books include Intihaz al-Fursa on hunting, al-Bustan al-Zahir on the scholars of the Al Nashir, Salifat al-'Udhar on blamed and chosen verse, an alfiyya on gharib al-Qur'an, Majmu' Hamza of the fatwas of Yemen's scholars, and Hada'iq al-Riyad on plants. {cite:al-aalam v.2 p.277}
