## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Abd Allah al-Shadhili al-Maghribi was the founder of the Shadhili order, among the Sufis {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 279}.

## His life
He came from Shadhila, a village in Ifriqiya, lived at Alexandria and died in the desert of Aydhab on the pilgrimage road; he was blind {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 279}. He left the litanies known as the Hizb al-Shadhili {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 279}. Al-Zirikli adds that Ibn Taymiyya wrote a reply to his hizb, and refers to Nukat al-Himyan {cite:alam-zirikli-1927-v2 pdf p. 279}.
