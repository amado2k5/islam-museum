## Overview
Ahmad ibn al-Hasan ibn al-Mutahhar ibn Muhammad al-Hasani al-Jarmuzi was a poet with an interest in history, from a house of leadership in Yemen; his nisba is to the village of Bani Jarmuz near San'a, where he was born and where he died around 1115 AH (1703 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.112}

He wrote Qala'id al-Jawhar fi Anba' Bani al-Mutahhar, a collection of biographies of his kin, most of them scholars, poets and chiefs; his verse is noted for tenderness. {cite:al-aalam v.1 p.112}
