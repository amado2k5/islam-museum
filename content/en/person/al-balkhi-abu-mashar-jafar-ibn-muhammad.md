## Overview
Abu Ma'shar Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Umar al-Balkhi, an astrologer and astronomer, whose nisba refers to Balkh in Khurasan. The encyclopaedia describes him as the leading authority of his age in his field, and gives his death as 272 AH (886 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.34-35}
