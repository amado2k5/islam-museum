## Overview
Abu Isa al-Warraq, a name that Ibn al-Nadim mentions in the "Fihrist" among those who became known lately in the reports of the people of heresy. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 473}

## What Ibn al-Nadim says
Ibn al-Nadim describes these people as showing Islam and concealing heresy. This is Ibn al-Nadim's description, not the museum's judgement. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 473}

## Notes for researchers
- The name of his father, his date, his works and the views of others than Ibn al-Nadim about him need an additional source.
