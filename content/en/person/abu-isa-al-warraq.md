## Overview
Abu Isa al-Warraq, whom Ibn al-Nadim mentions among the accounts of the crypto-dualists who professed Islam outwardly and concealed unbelief — the label is the source's, recorded here for its historical frame. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 473}
