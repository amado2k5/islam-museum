## Summary
[[person/umama-bint-abi-al-as|Umama]] bint Abi al-As ibn al-Rabi' al-Abshamiyya; her mother [[person/zaynab-bint-muhammad|Zaynab]] bint [[person/muhammad|the Messenger of God]] {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}. Al-Zubayr said in Nasab: Zaynab was under Abu al-As and bore him Umama and Ali {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}.

## The Prophet carrying her in prayer
Her mention is established in the two Sahihs in Abu Qatada's hadith: the Prophet used to carry Umama bint Zaynab on his shoulder — when he prostrated he set her down, and when he rose he lifted her {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}. In Ibn Sa'd's account from Abu Qatada: "while we were at the Messenger of God's door he came out carrying Umama bint Abi al-As ibn al-Rabi' — her mother Zaynab bint the Messenger of God — a small girl, and he prayed with her on his shoulder" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}.

## The necklace
Ahmad relates from A'isha that the Prophet was given a gift including an agate necklace, and said: "I shall give it to the dearest of my household to me" — the women said: Abu Quhafa's daughter has won it — and he called Umama bint Zaynab and hung it around her neck {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}. In a mursal account at Ibn Sa'd: "I shall give it to the most kin-dutiful of you" {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1816}. Ahmad relates that the Negus gifted the Prophet jewellery including a gold ring with an Abyssinian stone, and he gave it to Umama {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1817}.

## Her marriage
Abu Umar said: [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] married her after [[person/fatima|Fatima]]; al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam married her to him, her father having entrusted her to al-Zubayr {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1817}. After Ali was killed, Ali told al-Mughira ibn Nawfal ibn al-Harith to marry her, and he did; she bore him Yahya — by whom he took his kunya — and she died with al-Mughira {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1817}. It has been said she bore children to neither Ali nor al-Mughira {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1817}. Al-Zubayr said: Zaynab has no surviving issue {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 1817}.
