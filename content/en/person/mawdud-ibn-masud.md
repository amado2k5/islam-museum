## Summary
Sultan Mawdud ibn Mas'ud ibn Mahmud ibn Sebuktegin, lord of Ghazna after his uncle Muhammad ibn Mahmud; when his father Mas'ud died in 433, his uncle Muhammad took his place — Mawdud marched on his uncle, seized him, returned to Ghazna and the matter settled for him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.113}

## His death
He died in 442 and his uncle 'Abd al-Rashid ibn Mahmud succeeded {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.148}; the Shadharat gives his state ten years, his death in Rajab 441 at twenty-nine, and that they set up his son — a small boy — then deposed him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 275}
