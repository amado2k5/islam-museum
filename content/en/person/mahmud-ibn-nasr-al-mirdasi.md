## Overview
Mahmud ibn Nasr ibn Salih ibn Mirdas al-Kilabi, titled 'Izz al-Dawla and known as Ibn Shibl al-Dawla, was one of the Mirdasid emirs who held Aleppo. Al-Zirikli gives his death as 467 AH, 1075 CE, and no year of birth {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 189}.

## Rule in Aleppo
He took Aleppo in 452 AH. The government of Egypt then sent his uncle [[person/thimal-ibn-salih|Thimal ibn Salih]] against him, and Thimal took the city from him in 453; Thimal died a year later and 'Atiyya ibn Salih took it. Mahmud raided 'Atiyya and won the city in 454, his position grew strong and his air was clear of trouble, and he stayed in it until he died {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 189}.

## Character and reports
Al-Zirikli describes him as brave and resolute {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 189}. He passes on from Ibn al-Imad that Mahmud kept on good terms with both the Egyptians and the Abbasids because his house lay between them, and from Ibn Qadi Shuhba that Ibn Hayyus praised him in several poems {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 7 p. 189}.
