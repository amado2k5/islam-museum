## Overview
Jalal al-Din ibn Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Rumi al-Tabbani al-Qahiri, a scholar from Cairo, attributed to al-Tabbani, the place where he lived outside Cairo. He died in 793 AH (1391 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
