## Overview
Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah was the father of [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]], narrator of [[work/sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq|Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.16} He was among the first to receive the call of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] before he left for Yemen, accepted it and his imamate, went with him to Yemen, and was among the men of greatest standing with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.8}

## In al-Hadi's service
The editor writes that al-Hadi relied on him heavily and gave him weighty duties, and that he stayed in his service until he was killed carrying out his duty, the story of his death being told in the biography. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.8} The opening page calls him a great scholar without equal, who undertook for al-Hadi what no one else undertook, and says he was killed at [[place/najran|Najran]], as the biography will relate. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.16}

## His son
When he decided to emigrate to al-Hadi he told his son Ali and ordered him to follow. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.8}

## The standpoint of the source
The book is a Zaydi source; its praise of Abu Jafar is the book's own and not a judgement of the museum.
