## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Hasan al-Jurjani, born in Jurjan, grew up there and learned its sciences. He wrote in literary criticism the book "al-Wasata bayn al-Mutanabbi wa Khusumihi" (Mediation between al-Mutanabbi and his Adversaries). The source says he died in 392 AH (1001 CE). {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## In al-Shirazi
Al-Shirazi mentions him as a judge, jurist, man of letters and poet who had a diwan, and does not give the year of his death. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.64}

## Notes for researchers
- Matching the name in the two biographies is not enough to confirm they are the same man; the identity needs confirmation from an additional source.
