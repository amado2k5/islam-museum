## Overview
Al-Qaysi of Damascus, the adib; companion of Ibn Khalil transmitting much from him, and insha'-scribe for al-Malik al-'Adil at Damascus then for al-Malik Baybars; he died eight nights into Dhu'l-Hijja 672, aged ninety {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 585}.
