## Summary
Jalal al-Din al-Karlani al-Khwarazmi, a Hanafi jurist and learned scholar who studied with Husam al-Din al-Sighnaqi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2068}

## Works
His 'al-Kifaya', a commentary on the Hidaya, is his work, according to al-Laknawi; he died in 767 AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2068}
