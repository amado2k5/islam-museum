## Overview
Muhayyisa ibn Mas'ud al-Ansari, an Ansari Companion. Al-Baladhuri says that after the Prophet returned from Khaybar, he sent him to the people of Fadak to call them to Islam, and they made peace with him on half the land with its soil. In another report, he is named among those whom the Prophet disputed with over their settling. He took part in [[event/sulh-of-fadak|the peace of Fadak]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 41-42}
