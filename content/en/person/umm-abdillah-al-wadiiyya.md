## Summary

Umm Abdillah al-Wadi'iyya (Aisha bint Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i) is a contemporary Yemeni hadith scholar and teacher who grew up inside the hadith-scholarly household of Dammaj and became one of the most prominent women teaching hadith and its sciences in Yemeni Salafi circles.{cite:wikipedia-umm-abdillah-al-wadiiyya}

## Formation at Dar al-Hadith

She is the daughter of Shaykh Muqbil ibn Hadi al-Wadi'i, founder of Dar al-Hadith at Dammaj, and was raised inside the institute her father built, attending his circles and reading under him and its scholars until she specialised in the hadith sciences.{cite:wikipedia-umm-abdillah-al-wadiiyya}

## Teaching and books

She took charge of teaching women at Dammaj, lecturing on the books of the Sunna and the principles of hadith science to female students who came from Yemen and abroad. Her writings include Al-Jami' al-Sahih, Nasihati lil-Nisa' (My Advice to Women) and Tarbiyat al-Awlad (Raising Children), books mostly directed at the education of women and the young.{cite:wikipedia-umm-abdillah-al-wadiiyya}

## Standing

Salafi literature names her among the first women in contemporary Yemen to combine formal hadith study with organised teaching for women, and her website and recorded lessons continue to circulate her teaching.{cite:wikipedia-umm-abdillah-al-wadiiyya}
