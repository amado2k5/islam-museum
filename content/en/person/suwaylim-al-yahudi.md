## Summary
Owner of the house in which — per a report Ibn Hisham transmits — people used to gather to discourage the Tabuk campaign; his house was at Jasum, and it was burned on the Prophet's order. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## In the Tabuk expedition
Ibn Hisham names him in a report from "the Trustworthy" describing him as a Jew whose house was at Jasum. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160} The text gives nothing beyond his name, this description, and his house's location.

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The text mentions neither his lineage nor his position; the description "Jew" comes from the narrator.
- The report has a chain "the Trustworthy told me, from the one who told him" (p. 160).
