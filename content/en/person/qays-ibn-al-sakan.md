## Summary
Qays ibn al-Sakan held himself and his tongue as a prison. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 495, sequence 496, on page 140. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140}

## A tongue like a beast of prey
Qays ibn al-Sakan was asked: will you not speak? He said: my tongue is one of the beasts of prey; I fear that if I let it loose it will wound me. The first words of the notice are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/140}
