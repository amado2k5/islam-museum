## Overview
Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali, Zalim ibn Amr ibn Sufyan, a philologist and transmitter from the Du'al of Bakr ibn Kinana. The encyclopaedia says that he died in 69 AH. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He is credited with the pointing of the Qur'an and with laying down grammar. [[person/ibn-al-nadim|Ibn al-Nadim]] says that most scholars claimed that grammar was taken from him, and that he took it from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], and that from it came Abu al-Aswad's pointing for the vowels of fath, damm and kasr and the nasal. It is mentioned as the reason that he heard a reader make a mistake in a verse of the surah of al-Bara'a. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.103-106}
