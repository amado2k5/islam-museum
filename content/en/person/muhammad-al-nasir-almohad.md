## Overview
Muhammad al-Nasir, caliph of the Almohads, son of Abu Yusuf Ya'qub al-Mansur. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1681-1682}

## His rule
Ibn Khaldun says that the revolutionaries of the Maghrib and the people of the Adwa corresponded with him, and he consolidated their governorships. He then fought Ibn Ghaniya in Ifriqiya, took Mahdiyya in 603 AH, and appointed over it Abu Muhammad ibn al-Shaykh Abi Hafs. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1681-1682}

## His last years
He left Marrakesh for Seville in 609 AH, prepared for the raid, and the Muslims were routed at the Battle of al-'Uqab in late Safar of that year. He died on his way back to Marrakesh in Sha'ban 610 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1681-1682}
