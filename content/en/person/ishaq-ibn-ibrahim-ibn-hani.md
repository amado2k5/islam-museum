## Overview
Abu Ya'qub al-Naysaburi; he kept a special tie with Ahmad b. Hanbal, at whose house Ahmad stayed a while in hiding, and he transmitted from him a piece of his masa'il — a righteous man {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 170}.
