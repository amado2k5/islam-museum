## Summary
Ka'b ibn Malik's son and his guide in his blindness; his son Abd al-Rahman carried from him the account of his father's staying behind from Tabuk, per Ibn Ishaq's chain. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175}

## In the chain
Ibn Ishaq said al-Zuhri related from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdullah ibn Ka'b ibn Malik that his father Abdullah — his father's guide when his sight was lost — said: I heard my father Ka'b ibn Malik telling his account of staying behind. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/175}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- This is a chain of transmission — Ibn Ishaq, al-Zuhri, Abd al-Rahman, Abdullah, Ka'b; the text records nothing else of Abdullah.
