## Overview
Qadi al-qudat Badr al-Din al-Ja'fari of Nablus, the Hanbali; born in 792 — or 791 — and raised in a fine manner, of a house of learning and leadership, he heard his grandfather, Ibn al-'Ala'i and others, and began judging at Nablus as deputy to his cousin Judge Taj al-Din 'Abd al-Wahhab, then held it independently after 840 in place of Judge Shams al-Din ibn al-Imam. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Offices and death
The qada' of Jerusalem was added for a time, then he was removed from Jerusalem but kept Nablus, and also held the qada' of Ramla and deputy judgment in the Egyptian lands; he was of fine conduct, chaste in judgment, awe-inspiring, handsome and dignified, blessed with children and grandchildren, and enjoyed his worldly life; dismissed late in life he remained so until he died at Nablus on Thursday 16 Ramadan 881, aged about ninety. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
