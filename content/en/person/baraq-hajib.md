## Overview
Barraq the chamberlain of Qutlugh Khan, one of the commanders of the Khita state who entered the service of Khwarazm-shah Muhammad. He seized Kirman in 619 AH. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

He had been deputy there for Jalal al-Din Mengubirti. In 623 AH he wanted to be independent there, and sent to the Mongols urging them to fight him. When Jalal al-Din marched against him, he fortified himself in his castle and wrote to him: "I am the enslaved servant, and when I heard of your march I vacated it for you." He then remained in its kingdom. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

The Qutlugh Khans' line in Kirman lasted from his descendants until about 703 AH. See [[polity/qutlughkhanid-kirman|the Qutlughkhanids of Kirman]]. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}
