## Summary

Khubayb ibn Issaf al-Khazraji, Abu Kharasha, cousin of Khansa'. His Islam came so late he joined the Prophet on the road to Badr; he witnessed Badr, Uhud, the Trench and every battle, and killed Umayya ibn Khalaf at Badr. He married Habiba bint Kharija after Abu Bakr and died in Uthman's caliphate.
