## Summary
Of Banu Sahm; he emigrated to Abyssinia — Ibn Ishaq names him without describing his fate. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him second among Banu Sahm in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", tracing him to Hudhafa ibn Qays ibn Adi ibn Sa'd ibn Sahm. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8} The text does not mention his fate.

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-sahm|Banu Sahm]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/8}
