## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Atiq ibn Khalaf al-Siqilli al-Qurashi, Abu al-Qasim, known as Ibn al-Fahham, was a Qur'an reader who was the shaykh of Alexandria in his time; he died there in 516 AH (1123 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.315}

He wrote al-Tajrid li-Baghiyat al-Murid on the canonical readings. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.315}
