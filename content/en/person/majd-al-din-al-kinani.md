## Summary
Majd al-Din al-Kinani, chief qadi of the Hanafis in Egypt; he excelled in fiqh, usul, fara'id, arithmetic and adab, and shared in hadith, grammar and the readings — with a one-volume diwan; al-Maqrizi said: I kept his company for years, took useful notes from him and was intimate with him — except that he was tried by the judgeship in his worldly life. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 24}
