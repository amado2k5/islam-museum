## Summary
Bahman ibn Isfandiyar ibn Yustasif, an ancient king of the Persians, to whom a fire temple at Sijistan called Karkir is ascribed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 549}

## Details
Al-Mas'udi mentions in Muruj al-Dhahab, in the section on the places of the fire temples, that Bahman ibn Isfandiyar ibn Yustasif established a fire temple at Sijistan known as Karkir. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 549} Bahman is named among the ancient Persian kings credited with founding fire temples across the Persian lands: Afridhun had ordered part of their great fire carried to Khurasan, for which a temple was made at Tus and another at Bukhara called Bard Sura, followed by this temple Bahman built at Sijistan, another in the lands of al-Shiz and al-Ran, and others ascribed to Anushirwan, Kaykhusraw and Siyawakhsh ibn Kawus. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 549} Beyond ascribing the temple to him in this list of famous fire temples, al-Mas'udi says nothing more about Bahman in this passage. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 549}
