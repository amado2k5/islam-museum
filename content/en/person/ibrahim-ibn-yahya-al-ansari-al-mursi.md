## Overview
Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Yahya ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Zakariyya al-Ansari al-Mursi al-Gharnati (677–751 AH / 1278–1350 CE). A grammarian, reciter and physician, of Murcian origin, who lived in Granada in the time of the Nasrid state. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p.469}
