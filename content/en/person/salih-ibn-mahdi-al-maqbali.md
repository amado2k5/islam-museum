## Overview
Salih ibn Mahdi ibn Ali al-Maqbali was a mujtahid and one of the leading jurists. He followed the school of Imam Zayd, that is, the Zaydi school, so what follows is read within that frame. He was born in the village of Maqbal in the district of La'a in the land of Kawkaban, Yemen, northwest of Sanaa, and grew up in Thula, and he studied there and in Kawkaban {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 190}. He abandoned taqlid; some shaykhs in Sanaa debated him, the debate ended in estrangement, and he came to dislike living in Yemen, so he moved with his family to Mecca in 1080 AH. He became well known there, wrote his works there and died there {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 190}. Al-A'lam gives his years as 1047 to 1108 AH, that is 1637 to 1696 CE {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 190}.

## His works
Al-Zirikli names «al-Ilm al-Shamikh fi Ithar al-Haqq ala al-Aba' wa'l-Mashayikh», printed; «al-Abhath al-Musaddada fi Masa'il Muta'adida», in manuscript, which he says he owns; «al-Ithaf li-Talabat al-Kashshaf», in manuscript, in which he criticised al-Zamakhshari's Kashshaf on Qur'anic exegesis, with a copy at Riyadh University; and «al-Manar ala al-Bahr al-Zakhkhar», in manuscript, on Zaydi jurisprudence {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 190}.

## Al-Zirikli on his approach
Al-Zirikli describes him as a frequent critic of the Mu'tazila on some questions of theology, of the Ash'ariyya on others, of the Sufis on most of their questions, and of the hadith scholars where he found them excessive; he says he did not mind whom he differed with once he found the evidence, whoever they were {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 3 p. 190}.
