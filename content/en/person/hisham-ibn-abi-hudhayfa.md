## Summary
Of Banu Makhzum; Ibn Ishaq names him among the Abyssinian emigrants — and in the editors' note Ibn Abd al-Barr mentions al-Waqidi's saying "Hashim ibn Abi Hudhayfa". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names him third of three men of Banu Makhzum in "the rest of the Abyssinian emigrants", as "Hisham ibn Abi Hudhayfa ibn al-Mughira". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7} The editors transmit in the note, from Ibn Abd al-Barr's Isti'ab, that al-Waqidi calls him "Hashim ibn Abi Hudhayfa", that Ibn Abd al-Barr counts "Hisham" an error, and that Musa ibn Uqba and Abu Ma'shar did not mention him among the emigrants to Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Tribe
He belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-makhzum|Banu Makhzum]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/7}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- A name disagreement: Hisham (Ibn Ishaq) or Hashim (al-Waqidi); and neither Musa ibn Uqba nor Abu Ma'shar mentions him among the Abyssinian emigrants — this is from the editors' note citing the Isti'ab (p. 7), and only its facts are recorded.
