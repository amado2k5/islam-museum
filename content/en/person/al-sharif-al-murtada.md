## Overview
Abu Talib Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Musa al-Husayni al-Musawi, the naqib of the Talibids and the sheikh and head of the Shi'a in Iraq. He was an imam in Shi'ism, theology, poetry and eloquence, and wrote much. He lived to eighty-one years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.264-265}

## His learning and works
He took from Shaykh al-Mufid and held the naqibate. It is said that he had a hand in "Nahj al-Balagha", the collection of the sayings of Ali ibn Abi Talib. He wrote "al-Durar wa al-Ghurar", sessions he dictated on the arts of literature. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.264-265}

## Ibn Bassam's testimony
Ibn Bassam mentions him at the end of "al-Dhakhira" and says that he was the imam of the people of Iraq, and that their scholars took refuge in him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.264-265}
