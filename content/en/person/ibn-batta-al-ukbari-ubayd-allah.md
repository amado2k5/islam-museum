## Overview
Abu Abdallah Ubayd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Batta al-Ukbari, a hadith scholar and Hanbali jurist, born in Ukbara near Baghdad. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

He was born in 304 AH (917 CE) and died in 387 AH (997 CE), according to report. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
