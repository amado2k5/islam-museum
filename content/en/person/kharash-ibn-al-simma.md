## Summary

Kharash ibn al-Simma ibn Amr ibn al-Jamuh al-Ansari, of Banu Salima of the Khazraj. Ibn Ishaq lists him among those at Badr. Ibn al-Kalbi and Abu Ubayd add that he had two horses with him at Badr, that he took ten wounds at Uhud, and that he was one of the noted archers. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.389}
