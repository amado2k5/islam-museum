## Summary
Abdullah Abdullah is an Afghan ophthalmologist and politician, born at Kabul in 1960. He was a senior member of the Northern Alliance and an adviser to Ahmad Shah Massoud, then served as foreign minister of Afghanistan from 2001 to 2005, as Chief Executive of Afghanistan from 2014 to 2020, and as chairman of the High Council for National Reconciliation from 2020 to 2021. He stood for the presidency in 2009, 2014 and 2019. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}

## Early life and education
He was born in the Karte Parwan district of Kabul, and in his own words was born in the house where he later resided. Both of his parents were born in Kabul, but his father's family came from Kandahar and his mother's from Panjshir. The Arabic article gives his birth as 5 September 1960 and says he was born to a Muslim family — a Pashtun father and a Tajik mother — and received an Islamic religious education; the English article's lead gives 10 February 1960 and marks the date as needing a citation, so the museum carries the September date as debated. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-ar} {cite:wikidata-abdullah-abdullah}

His father, Ghulam Mahyyoddin Zmaryalay, was an administrator in the land survey and audit offices and a senator in the final years of King Zahir Shah's rule. Abdullah spent parts of his childhood in Panjshir, Kandahar and Kabul, and has seven sisters and two brothers. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}

He studied medicine at Kabul University and graduated as an ophthalmologist in 1983, and worked as a medical doctor and eye surgeon in the 1980s, including a period with Afghan refugees in Pakistan. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en} {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-ar}

## Career
He became a senior figure in the Northern Alliance and a close adviser to its commander Ahmad Shah Massoud, serving as a spokesman and in foreign-affairs roles for the resistance and then for the Islamic State government of Burhanuddin Rabbani. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}

After 2001 he was foreign minister in the interim and transitional administrations of Hamid Karzai, from November 2001 to April 2005. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}

In 2009 he registered as an independent candidate for the presidency against Karzai, choosing a Shia surgeon, Cheragh Ali Cheragh, as his second vice-presidential running mate; unofficial results placed him second, and he withdrew before the scheduled run-off. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}

After the disputed 2014 election he became Chief Executive of the National Unity Government under President Ashraf Ghani, holding the post from 29 September 2014 to 9 March 2020. Following the 2019 election dispute, a power-sharing agreement made him chairman of the High Council for National Reconciliation in May 2020, leading the republic's side of the peace process until the fall of Kabul on 15 August 2021. {cite:wikipedia-abdullah-abdullah-en}
