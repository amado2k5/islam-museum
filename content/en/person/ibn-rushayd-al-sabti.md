## Overview
Muhammad ibn Umar ibn Muhammad, Abu Abd Allah, Muhibb al-Din Ibn Rushayd al-Fihri al-Sabti, was a traveller, a scholar of literature, and learned in Qur'anic exegesis and history. He lived from 657 AH (1259 CE) to 721 AH (1321 CE) {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}.

## Life
He was born in Ceuta, held the post of preacher at the great mosque of Granada, and died in Fez {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}.

## The journey
In 683 AH he travelled to Egypt, the Levant and the two holy sanctuaries, and wrote an account of the journey called Mil' al-Aybah fima jumi'a bi-tul al-ghaybah fi al-rihla ila Makka wa-Tayba, in six volumes, parts of which survive in manuscript {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}. Al-Zirikli quotes Ibn Hajar as saying that the book holds a great deal of benefit, and that he looked at it and made excerpts from it {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}.

## Other works
His books include Talkhis al-qawanin (grammar); al-Sunan al-abyan wa-l-mawrid al-am'ad fi al-muhakama bayna al-imamayn al-Bukhari wa-Muslim fi al-sanad al-mu'an'an; and Ifadat al-nasih bi-l-ta'rif bi-isnad al-Jami' al-Sahih, the last two printed at Tunis {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}. He also wrote Idah al-madhahib fiman yutlaq alayhi ism al-sahib, and Turjuman al-tarajim on the chapters of al-Bukhari, which he did not finish; he left sermons, poems and many small books {cite:alam-zirikli-dar-al-ilm-2002 vol. 6 p. 314}.
