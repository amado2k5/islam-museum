## In brief
Imran b. al-Husayn b. Ubayd b. Khalaf, Abu Nujayd al-Khuza'i, Companion of the Messenger of God; his conversion came at the time of Abu Hurayra's, and he was among those Umar b. al-Khattab sent to the people of Basra to teach them fiqh, and he held the judgeship of Basra. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.32}

## His standing
He was among those whom the angels greeted; he suffered hemorrhoids and was cauterised for them. Zurara b. Awfa, al-Hasan, Muhammad b. Sirin, Zuhdam al-Jarmi, Amir al-Sha'bi, Ibn Burayda, Mutarrif b. Abdallah b. al-Shikhkhir, Abu Raja' al-Utaridi, and others narrated from him; he was among the long-lived Companions who lived sixty-seven years. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.32}

## His death
He died in 52 AH. {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.1 p.32}
