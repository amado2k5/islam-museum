## Overview
Abu'l-Ghayth Ibn Jamil, shaykh of Yemen's shaykhs; at the start of his affair he was a knavish highwayman — while lying in wait for a caravan he heard a voice cry "keeper of the eye, an eye upon you," which fell on him with a fall that shook him; he turned to God and sincerity of purpose showed on him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264-265}

## His company
He kept first to Shaykh 'Ali b. Aflah al-Zubaydi then Shaykh 'Ali al-Ahdal; illiterate, he has speech on the realities, dazzling states and manifest wonders, and a book on tasawwuf was written about him. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264-265}
