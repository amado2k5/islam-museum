## Overview
Jufayna al-Ibadi, a Christian from Hira, who was the wet-nurse of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
Ubayd Allah ibn Umar killed him, together with his two sons, on the charge of supporting Abu Lu'lu'a. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
