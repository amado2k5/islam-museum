## Overview
Muhammad ibn Harun ibn Dharʿ al-Numari. Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Hajjaj appointed him over the frontier of Makran after a famine. In his governorship the king of the island of Yaqut sent to al-Hajjaj women who had been born Muslims, whose fathers had died as merchants. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
