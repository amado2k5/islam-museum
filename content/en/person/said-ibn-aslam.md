## Overview
Sa'id ibn Aslam ibn Zur'a al-Kilabi, whom al-Hajjaj appointed over Makran and its frontier. al-Baladhuri reports that Mu'awiya and Muhammad, the sons of al-Harith al-Alafi, rebelled against him. He was killed, and they took control of the frontier {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
