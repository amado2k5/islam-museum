## Summary
Daughter of Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh and Umm Habiba; by her her mother was given the kunya Umm Habiba. Her mother carried her out of Mecca and brought her back with her. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}

## In the Abyssinian emigrants chapter
Ibn Ishaq names her daughter of Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh, saying Umm Habiba bint Abi Sufyan was given her kunya by her. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6} In the women's list he says her mother carried her out of Mecca and brought her back with her. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/10}

## Tribe
She belongs, per Ibn Ishaq, to [[group/banu-umayya|Banu Umayya ibn Abd Shams]] {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/6}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- She is not counted among "their children in Abyssinia" (p. 11); the text says her mother "carried her out of Mecca", so she is understood to have been born before the departure.
