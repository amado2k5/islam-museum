## Summary
A Maliki jurist, memorizer, hadith scholar and usuli from the Mu'afir of Fez; he heard from Abu Zayd al-Marwazi and Abu Muhammad al-Asili; Abu Imran al-Fasi studied fiqh under him. Works include al-Mumahhid fi al-Fiqh, a book of rites, an epitome of the Muwatta' and a treatise on teachers and pupils. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7833}
