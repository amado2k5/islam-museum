## Summary
Abdallah ibn Malik ibn al-Qashib ibn Jundab, Abu Muhammad, a Companion known as Ibn Bahina. He related from the Prophet, and al-A'raj and Ata' ibn Yasar related from him. The jurists cite him in the rules of ihram on cupping. The entry gives no firm date of death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7144}
