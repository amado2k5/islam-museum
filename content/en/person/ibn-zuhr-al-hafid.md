## Overview
Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Zuhr al-Iyadi, known as Ibn Zuhr al-Hafid, one of the outstanding figures of medicine and letters in al-Andalus. {cite:al-aalam p.937}

## Life
He was born in [[place/seville|Seville]], served the states of the [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|Almoravids]] and the [[polity/almohad-caliphate|Almohads]], and died in [[place/marrakesh|Marrakesh]]. {cite:al-aalam p.937}

## Medicine and letters
Al-Zirikli says that in his time no one knew the craft of medicine better than he did, and he took it from his father. He wrote *al-Tiryaq* (The Antidote) on medicine, and delicate poetry, and muwashshahat that he composed with skill. {cite:al-aalam p.937}
