## Overview
Abu Yahya Habib ibn Abi Thabit, one of the jurists among the Successors. Al-Shirazi mentions that he died in 117 AH. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 44}

He transmits from Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash his saying: "Three have no fourth: Habib ibn Abi Thabit, al-Hakam ibn Utayba, and Hammad ibn Abi Sulayman." {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p. 44}
