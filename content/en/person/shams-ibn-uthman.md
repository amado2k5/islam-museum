## Overview
Shamas ibn 'Uthman ibn al-Sharid al-Makhzumi was a Companion and one of the heroes; he was present at Badr and was killed on the day of Uhud in 3 AH (625 CE). {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.175}

Al-Zirikli records that the Prophet likened him to a shield: whenever he glanced right or left he saw Shamas before him warding off blows with his sword, and when he was overwhelmed he shielded him with his own body until he was killed. Hassan composed his elegy. {cite:al-aalam v.3 p.175}
