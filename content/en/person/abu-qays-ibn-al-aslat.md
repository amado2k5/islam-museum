## Overview
Abu Qays ibn Sayfi ibn al-Aslat, a poet and leader of the Aws before Islam. He was the chief of "the Aws of God", who were the Banu Umayya ibn Zayd, Khatma, Wa'il and Waqif, of the [[group/al-aws|Aws]]. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.239-240}

## His position on Islam
Al-Samhudi relates that he held his people back from Islam at the time of the mission of [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Mus'ab ibn Umayr]]. They continued this way until the Prophet emigrated and Badr, Uhud and the Trench had passed, and then they all embraced Islam. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.239-240}

The data classify him as a poet and a Companion in the Sunni manner. His year of death and the details of his personal conversion need an additional source.
