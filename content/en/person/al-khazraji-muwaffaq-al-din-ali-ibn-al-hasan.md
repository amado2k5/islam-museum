## Overview
Muwaffaq al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Wahhas al-Khazraji al-Zabidi al-Shafi'i, a Shafi'i jurist from Zabid, known as Ibn al-Naqqash. He died in 812 AH (1410 CE), according to the "Encyclopaedia of Notable Scholars and Men of Letters". {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
