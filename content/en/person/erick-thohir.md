## Summary
Erick Thohir is an Indonesian businessman, sports investor and government minister — a figure who moved from media ownership into European football boardrooms, Olympic administration and cabinet office. {cite:wikipedia-erick-thohir}

## Business and media
Born in 1970 in Jakarta into the family behind the Mahaka Group, Thohir built holdings in media, advertising and entertainment in Indonesia before turning to international sport: he co-owned the NBA's Philadelphia 76ers' affiliate assets, owned D.C. United in Major League Soccer, and in 2013 became president and majority owner of Inter Milan — the first Indonesian to control a major European football club — before selling his stake later in the decade. {cite:wikipedia-erick-thohir}

## Sport administration
Beyond club ownership he has been a member of the International Olympic Committee, chaired the INASGOC organising committee of the 2018 Asian Games in Jakarta-Palembang, and was elected president of the Indonesian football association (PSSI) in 2023, promising to reform the country's troubled football governance. {cite:muslim500-erick-thohir}

## Politics
In 2019 President Joko Widodo appointed him Minister of State-Owned Enterprises, the ministry supervising Indonesia's vast SOE portfolio — where he pushed consolidation and reform — and he retained cabinet office into the Prabowo administration, moving to the youth and sports portfolio in 2024. {cite:wikipedia-erick-thohir}

## Standing
The Muslim 500 profiles him as Southeast Asia's crossover figure of sport and state: a businessman whose career linked Italian football, Olympic politics and one of the world's largest Muslim-majority cabinets. {cite:muslim500-erick-thohir}
