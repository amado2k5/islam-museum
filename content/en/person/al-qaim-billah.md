## Overview
Al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah Abu Ja'far Abd Allah ibn al-Qadir, an Abbasid caliph from 422 to 467 AH. He was born in the middle of Dhu al-Qa'da 391 AH, and his mother was an Armenian concubine called Badr al-Duja, or it is said Qatr al-Nada. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 642-643}

## His character
Al-Suyuti relates from Ibn al-Athir that he was beautiful and handsome of face, pious, devout, ascetic and learned, strong in certainty, much given to charity, and preferring justice and goodness. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 642-643}

## The capture by al-Basasiri
Al-Basasiri seized him in 450 AH and imprisoned him at Anah. He wrote a document that he sent to Mecca, and it was hung on the Ka'ba. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 642-643}

## After his return
When he returned to his house he slept only on his prayer mat, kept the fasts and vigils, and forgave all who had harmed him. He recovered nothing of what had been looted from his palace except at a price, and said these were things they had reckoned with God. No instruments of entertainment were found in his palace. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 642-643}
