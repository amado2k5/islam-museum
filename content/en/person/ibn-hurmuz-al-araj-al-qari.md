## Overview
Ibn Hurmuz al-A'raj, a reciter, who used to say: "The best of your coastal garrisons is Alexandria." He went out from Medina to garrison it and died there in 117 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.313}

His garrison duty in [[place/iskandariyya|Alexandria]] is recorded by al-Baladhuri. His full name needs an additional source.
