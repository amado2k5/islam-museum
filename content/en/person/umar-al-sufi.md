## Summary
Umar al-Sufi crossed the deserts alone and apologised to his master in tears. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 507, sequence 508, on pages 144 and 145. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/144} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/145}

## Ishaq ibn Abbad's question at Mecca
Ishaq ibn Abbad relates that he met Umar al-Sufi at Mecca and asked him whether he had come riding or on foot. He wept and said: is it not enough for the disobedient to come to his master riding? Some names in the chain are unclear in the scan and are not given here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/145}
