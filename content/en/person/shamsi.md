## Overview
Shamsi is a London-based Muslim preacher and debater, a well-known figure at Speakers' Corner in Hyde Park, London, recognised for his role in Islamic dawah and his debates with people of different beliefs. {cite:ideas-journal-shamsi-deixis-2025}

He publishes under the single name Shamsi, and no source consulted gives a legal name, birth date or birthplace for him. {cite:ideas-journal-shamsi-deixis-2025} {cite:youtube-dus-dawah}

His debates are uploaded to the DUS Dawah YouTube channel — about 347,000 subscribers as of October 2026, a platform figure that changes constantly — which the academic study of his preaching describes as his channel. {cite:youtube-dus-dawah} {cite:ideas-journal-shamsi-deixis-2025}

## Career and work
DUS Dawah is the video dawah platform of Masjid Daar us Sunnah in Acton, London, and the masjid's own website leads its video section with Shamsi's debates and lessons. {cite:dusunnah-masjid} {cite:youtube-dus-dawah}

The channel describes itself as an Islamic educational platform delivering the message of Islam to Muslims and people of all faiths, and alongside Shamsi's debates it carries lectures by other speakers and recordings of earlier scholars. {cite:youtube-dus-dawah}

At Speakers' Corner he engages atheists, Christians and theological opponents in direct dialogue, often using a question-led, Socratic approach to core beliefs of Islam. {cite:ideas-journal-shamsi-deixis-2025}

A peer-reviewed study published in the IDEAS journal in December 2025 analysed his use of person and social deixis in a recorded debate with an atheist, taking its data from the DUS Dawah channel. {cite:ideas-journal-shamsi-deixis-2025}

## Positions and criticism
