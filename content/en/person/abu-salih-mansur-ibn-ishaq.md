## Overview
Abu Salih Mansur ibn Ishaq al-Samani, cousin of the Amir Ahmad ibn Isma'il al-Samani. The Amir appointed him over Sistan after he took it in 298 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.47}
