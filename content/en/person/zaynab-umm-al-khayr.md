## Overview
Zaynab Umm al-Khayr, the Damascene muhadditha, daughter of the chief qadi Muhibb al-Din al-Qurashi; she transmitted from 'Ali b. Hajjaj, Ibn al-Muqayyar and a company, and died in Sha'ban 700, aged seventy-five {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 797}.
