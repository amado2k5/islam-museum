## Overview
Ibn Bint al-Shafi'i, Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah, a Shafi'i jurist of Baghdad; he studied with his father, with Ishaq and with Abu Thawr, wrote two works on al-Shafi'i's merits, and the leadership of learning in Baghdad came to him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11489}
