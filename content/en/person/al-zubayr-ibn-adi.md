## Overview
Al-Zubayr ibn Adi, Abu Adi al-Hamdani al-Yami al-Kufi, is called by al-Dhahabi the great scholar, the reliable, and judge of Rayy {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}. The sign printed with his entry shows his hadith is in the six canonical books {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}.

## Teachers and students
He narrated from Anas ibn Malik, Abu Wa'il Shaqiq, al-Harith and others, and Malik ibn Mighwal, Mis'ar, Sufyan al-Thawri, Bishr ibn al-Husayn and others narrated from him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}.

## What the critics said
Ahmad called him reliable, and he was a man of merit and of the sunna {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}. Al-Ijli said he was reliable and firm, among the companions of Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, and that when he was with Qutayba al-Bahili, Ibrahim told him to fear God and not to be killed with Qutayba {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}.

## His death
It is said that he died in 131 AH {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house ص1711}.
