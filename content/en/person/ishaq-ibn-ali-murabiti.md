## Overview
Ishaq ibn Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin, the last of the [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|Almoravid]] rulers. He is the son of [[person/ali-ibn-yusuf-al-murabiti|Ali ibn Yusuf]], and succeeded his brother [[person/tashfin-ibn-ali|Tashfin]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Rule and end
He was given the oath of allegiance after his brother died. When the Almohads reached Marrakesh, he went out to them with his retinue, and they killed him. Abd al-Mu'min took the Maghrib in 542 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The Gregorian date (1147 CE) is derived from the Hijri date.
