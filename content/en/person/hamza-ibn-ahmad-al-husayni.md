## Overview
Hamza ibn Ahmad ibn 'Ali ibn Muhammad ibn al-'Abbas al-Husayni of Damascus, a Shafi'i jurist, historian and memoriser. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10354}

## Study and works
He learned the Quran and al-Tanbih by heart and studied law under Ibn Qadi Shuhba; he was a modest and well-read man. His works include a commentary on al-Nawawi's edition of al-Tanbih, Baqaya al-Khabaya, a supplement to al-Zarkashi's Khabaya al-Zawaya on Shafi'i law, and a book on the merits of Jerusalem. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10354}
