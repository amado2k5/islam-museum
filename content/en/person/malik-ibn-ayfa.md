## Overview
Malik ibn Ayfa al-Hamdani, of the delegation of Hamdan that met the Prophet on his return from Tabuk, together with Malik ibn al-Namat Dhu al-Mash'ar. Ibn Hisham named them from Abu Ishaq al-Subay'i. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}

## The delegation of Hamdan
He belongs to [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] and is counted among [[event/delegation-of-hamdan|its delegation]]. The data record his Islam on the authority of report, and date it roughly to around 9 AH. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.244}
