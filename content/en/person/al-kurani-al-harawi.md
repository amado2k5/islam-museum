## Overview
Abu'l-'Abbas Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Salam al-Kurani of Herat; a litterateur devoted to preserving the ancient and modern poetry, advanced in that field — he sat with 'Abd al-Mu'min b. 'Ali, lord of the Almohads, then his son Yusuf, then his son Ya'qub al-Mansur. {cite:wafayat-ayan}
