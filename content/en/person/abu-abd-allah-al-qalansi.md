## Summary
Abu Abd Allah al-Qalansi was faithful to his covenant, and in perils the truth was his deliverance. [[person/abu-nuaym-al-asbahani-ahmad|Abu Nu'aym]] gives him a notice in the tenth volume of [[work/hilyat-al-awliya|Hilyat al-Awliya]], printed number 530, sequence 531, on pages 160 and 161. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/160} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/161}

## The vow at sea
Abd al-Wahid ibn Bakr relates that Abu Abd Allah al-Qalansi boarded a ship on one of his journeys, and a storm struck it. The people on board begged and made vows, and said: let each of us make a pledge to God, and you make a vow too. He said: I have stripped myself of the world; what have I to do with vows? They pressed him, and he said: for God's sake, if He delivers me from what I am in, I will not eat elephant meat. They said: what is this vow! Does anyone eat elephant meat? He said: it came into my heart, and God set it on my tongue. The ship broke, and he and some of its people reached the shore, and they went days without food. Then an elephant was born; they seized it, slaughtered it, ate its flesh, and offered him some, but he refused, citing his vow; they excused him on grounds of necessity, but he held to the covenant. They ate and slept. The mother elephant came looking for her calf and followed its trace until she reached the bones; then she came to him, found no scent of meat on him, turned her back and signalled him to mount, and carried him through the night to a land of wild growth and dark, where she signalled him to dismount. In the morning he saw crops, dark people, and they took him to their king, who asked whether he knew how far the animal had carried him in one night. He said no. The king said: eight days' journey in one night. The report says he stayed among them until he was carried back and returned. The edition's note on page 160 says the notice may be the one earlier on page 142; that identity is not confirmed from the text and is not asserted here. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/160} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 10/161}
