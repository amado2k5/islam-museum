## نظرة عامة

The Ottoman archery grounds, Istanbul's Ok-Meydan first among them, where archers vied for distance and each record shot had its stone marker raised at the measure — among the famous names Tozkoparan Iskandar of the tenth Hijri century {cite:tdv-ok}.
