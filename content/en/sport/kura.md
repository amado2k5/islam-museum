## Overview

Ball games — playing the ball with feet and hands — were known in Arabia before Islam and in Muslim lands after it. The histories carry reports of ball-play in Mecca, Medina and the Islamic cities, and in them games counted as ancestors of today's football {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1}.

## In the seerah and history

The historians record the ball game the people of Mecca played, and the boys and youths playing it in the lanes. In Umayyad and Abbasid Damascus the ball was played in the fields; in Mamluk Cairo the "ball with mallets" — a ball struck with crooked sticks like the suljan — was a game of the maydan, and likewise in the Ottoman cities {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1}. In fiqh, the scholars discussed ball-play: some disliked it as pure diversion, others allowed it where it does not distract from a duty.

## Today

Football today is the widest sport in the Muslim world: every land has its clubs and national teams, and among the Muslims are the greatest players of the modern game — Mohamed Salah, Zinedine Zidane, Karim Benzema, Sadio Mane, Riyad Mahrez and countless others. Qatar hosted the 2022 World Cup, the first in a Muslim land, and Saudi Arabia hosts 2034.
