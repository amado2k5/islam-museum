## نظرة عامة

Ball play stands in the Canon among the strong field exercises of Ibn Sina {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p. 222}. The townspeople had their known ball games in the maydans and lanes; the jurists counted some as lawful diversion and warned of what resembles gambling or wastes an obligation.
