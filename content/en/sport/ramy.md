## نظرة عامة

The hadith has it: «Shoot, sons of Isma'il, for your father was an archer», and the Prophet told Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas on the day of Uhud «Shoot; may my father and mother be your ransom» {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}. Archery is one of the contests in which a prize is lawful, on the report «there is no prize except in nasl, khuff or hafir» {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}. Archers kept their grounds in the Mamluk and Ottoman capitals, from the furusiyya ground of Cairo to the ok-meydan grounds of Istanbul.
