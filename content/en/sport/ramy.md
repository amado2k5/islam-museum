## Overview

Archery — loosing the arrow from the bow — is the sport richest in texts of the Prophetic trio: it is one of the three the community was told to teach, one of the three in which a prize is lawful, and it has its famous moments in the seerah {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.

## In the Sunnah

In the authentic report: «Shoot, sons of Isma'il, for your father was an archer» {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} — said by the Prophet to a people he found practising archery, approving them and telling them to continue. And on the day of Uhud, as the enemy bore down, the Prophet said to Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas as he shot: «Shoot; may my father and mother be your ransom» {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} — a ransom given to honour the archer in his stand. And in «there is no prize except in nasl, khuff or hafir» {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229} archery was placed among the three in which a wager and a prize are lawful.

## In fiqh

On these texts the jurists built the chapter of contests: archery is a race in which the sabaq and the muhallil are lawful, and they set its terms — the two archers matched in bows or arrows, the prize from a third party or from one of them according to the cases, and the examples of number, distance and accuracy {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}.

## In history

Archery did not remain text in books: it had its fields. In Mamluk Egypt the Furusiyya Ground of Cairo was among the places of the range {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; in the Ottoman state the ok-meydan grounds of Istanbul were famous ranges — the greatest called Ok Meydan, "Arrow Field" — where contests were held before the Sultan and archers recorded their longest shots in stones {cite:tdv-ok}. The arrow passed into today's games: Olympic archery revived under a modern name this sport and its form.
