## نظرة عامة

The Qur'an rules on hunting — its lawfulness and its prohibition in ihram — on the catch of the sea and the trained birds of prey {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}. The Muwatta has its chapters on land and sea hunting and the trained hawks {cite:muwatta-malik}. The hunting of caliphs and sultans with falcons and trained cheetahs was a chapter of furusiyya in the maydans and the wild; the scholars warned against hunt for idle diversion, not against hunting itself.
