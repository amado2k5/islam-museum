## نظرة عامة

It is narrated from the Prophet, in a mursal report carried by the jurists in the chapters on raising children, that he said «Teach your children prayer, swimming and archery»; al-Suyuti lists it in al-Jami' al-Saghir with discussion of its grade {cite:sahih-jami-saghir-maktab-islami-1988}. Swimming is part of the bodily strength the scholars name for the Muslim, and the swimming of the Hijazis appears in the books of reports.
