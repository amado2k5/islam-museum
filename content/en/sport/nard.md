## نظرة عامة

Nard is the known board game; it came in from Persia, and the jurists agree on forbidding it for stakes while disagreeing on playing it without stakes — the disagreement is on record among them from the time of the Companions and Tabi'in, and the museum gives no verdict {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}.
