## نظرة عامة

The Prophet held horse races from al-Hafya' to Thaniyyat al-Wada' for the trained horses, and from al-Thaniyya to the mosque of Banu Zurayq for the untrained, and Ibn Umar was among the winners {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. A prize on a horse race is lawful for the majority on the report «no prize except in khuff, hafir or nasl» — the root of the muhallil and muzammal chapters of fiqh {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}.
