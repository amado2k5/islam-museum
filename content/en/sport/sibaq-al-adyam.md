## Overview

Foot-racing — the race on foot — is the simplest and the most famous sport of the seerah: the two races of Aisha in which the Prophet said «this one for that one» {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## The two races of Aisha

In the report of Aisha: I went out with the Prophet on one of his journeys while I was a young girl who had not yet put on flesh, and he said to the people: go ahead; they went ahead, then he said to me: come, let me race you — so I raced him and beat him on foot. She said: I forgot it until I had put on flesh, and on a later journey he said: come, let me race you — so I raced him and he beat me, and he laughed and said: this one is for that one {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:musnad-ahmad}. A report carrying love, play and sport together, among the dearest to her: two races between husband and wife on the road, the first to the lean girl and the second to the grown woman, and the Prophet remembered it laughing.

## In fiqh and history

A foot-race is not a wagered contest like archery and horseracing, but it is among the games that refresh the person and train the body. Racing boys and youths was known in Mecca and Medina and every land; the people of literature mention the foot-races of boys in Muslim cities. Today running is the most practised sport in the world, and it has its famous origin in these two races.
