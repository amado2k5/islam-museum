## نظرة عامة

Aisha narrates that she travelled with the Prophet as a light-built young woman; he sent the companions ahead, then told her «Come, let me race you», and she beat him; later, when she had grown heavier, he raced her again and won, saying «This is for that one» {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}. Running is among the strong exercises in Ibn Sina's Canon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p. 222}.
