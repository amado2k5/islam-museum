## نظرة عامة

Ishala is the lifting and setting of a heavy stone, named by Ibn Sina among the strong field exercises of the Canon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p. 222}. Lifting-stones stood in the maydans where young men contested in strength; inscribed examples survive in the museums of the Islamic world.
