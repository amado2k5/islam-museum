## نظرة عامة

In the two Sahihs, the Abyssinians played with their spears in the Prophet's mosque on the day of Eid, and Aisha would slip in behind the Prophet and watch them until she tired {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. That play was sport of a kind that serves battle, and the Prophet allowed it in the mosque rather than counting it among blameworthy diversions.
