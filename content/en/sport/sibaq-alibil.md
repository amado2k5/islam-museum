## نظرة عامة

The wager on the hoof is one of the contests lawful for a prize, and the hafir of the jurists is the camel's hoof {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}. The Arabs raced their camels in the seasons and fairs, among them the men of the mazamil who enter a camel in a race without a prize from its owners; camel racing remains a standing festival in the Gulf lands today.
