## نظرة عامة

Chess entered the Muslim lands from Persia in the Umayyad era, and its players became famous in Abbasid Baghdad, among them Ali al-Sufi al-Adli and al-Rabi'. The jurists disagreed on it: some disliked or prohibited it for its likeness to nard, some allowed it under conditions, and some played it without scruple in the salons of adab — the disagreement is on record in their books, and the museum gives no verdict.
