## نظرة عامة

God commanded the preparation of strength and the tethering of horses, and in a Bukhari report goodness is tied to the forelocks of horses until the Day of Resurrection {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. In the two Sahihs the Prophet doubled the share of horses in the spoils over that of camels, and raced the trained horses from al-Hafya' to Thaniyyat al-Wada' and the untrained from al-Thaniyya to the mosque of Banu Zurayq, with Abdullah ibn Umar among the winners {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. Horsemanship grew into a written science with its own manuals, from Ibn Qayyim's al-Furusiyya to the Mamluk books of military exercises.
