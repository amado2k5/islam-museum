## Summary
Minber TV is an Ethiopian Islamic satellite television station, listed among Ethiopia's religious broadcasters as an Islamic station broadcasting in Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Afar and Somali. {cite:wikipedia-television-in-ethiopia-en} Its own website carries daily Amharic news and television programmes, including religious teaching strands and the Merkuz nasheed series produced by its Minber Multimedia Production arm. {cite:minber-tv-website} Its YouTube channel (@MinberTv1) counted about 857 thousand subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure. {cite:vidiq-minber-tv}

## Programmes and platforms
The station's website organises its output into news and current affairs, television programmes — among them a religious strand, Minber Deen, alongside history and educational programmes — and the Merkuz series of group nasheeds, whose best-known releases have drawn millions of views on YouTube. {cite:minber-tv-website} {cite:vidiq-minber-tv} The YouTube channel was created on 15 September 2017, and vidIQ records 7,848 videos and about 172.8 million total views for it; the channel's country setting is the United Arab Emirates, while its content and audience are Ethiopian. {cite:vidiq-minber-tv} The station's own launch date as a satellite broadcaster is not stated on its website and is not asserted here. {cite:minber-tv-website}
