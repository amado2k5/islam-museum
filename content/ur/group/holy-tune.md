## Summary
Holy Tune is a Bangladeshi record label and production company for Islamic music — the label of Kalarab Shilpigosthi, the national Islamic cultural organisation for children and teenagers. The label was established in 2016, has been under contract with Kalarab since its inception, and is owned by the singer Muhammad Badruzzaman; all Kalarab songs are exclusively produced and distributed by Holy Tune. {cite:wikipedia-kalarab-shilpigosthi-en} Its YouTube channel (@holytunebdofficial) counted about 10.8 million subscribers by vidIQ's count read on 5 November 2026, a dated platform figure, ranking it among the largest Islamic music channels in Bangladesh. {cite:vidiq-holy-tune}

## Catalogue and reach
vidIQ records 1,280 videos and about 3.06 billion total views for the channel, which was created on 14 January 2011 — before the label's formal establishment in 2016 — and whose most-viewed releases are Bengali Islamic songs (gojol) by Kalarab artists and by Jahidul Islam Shawon, Muhammad Badruzzaman, Sayed Ahmad and Qari Abu Rayhan, the most popular passing 111 million views. {cite:vidiq-holy-tune} The channel has received YouTube's Silver, Gold and Diamond Play Buttons, and in 2022 Holy Tune received a special honour from the Bangladesh Deeni Seba Foundation for its promotion of Islamic music. {cite:wikipedia-kalarab-shilpigosthi-en}
