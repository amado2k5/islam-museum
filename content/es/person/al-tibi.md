## Visión general
Al-Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Tibi, Sharaf al-Din, erudito en hadices, exégesis y retórica (m. 743 H). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3490}

## Obras
