## Visión general
Hammad ibn Ishaq ibn Isma'il ibn Hammad ibn Sa'd, cliente de al-Hasan ibn 'Ali; nacido alrededor del año 120 H, narró de Hisham ibn 'Urwa, al-A'mash, Ibn Abi Khalid, Idris ibn Yazid al-Awdi, Ajlah al-Kindi, Akhwas ibn Hakim al-Shami, Usama ibn Zayd al-Laythi, Burayd ibn 'Abdallah ibn Abi Burda, Muhriz ibn Hakim, Hatim ibn Abi Saghira, Habib ibn al-Shahid, al-Hasan ibn al-Hakam al-Nakha'i, Sa'd ibn Sa'id al-Ansari y otros. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.1551}
