## Ringkasan

Al-Fadl ibn al-Sabbah Abu al-Abbas al-Samsar, seorang ulama hadis Baghdadi; ia mendengar Hishim ibn Bashir, Sufyan ibn 'Uyaynah, Abu Muawiyah al-Darir, Abu Ubaidah al-Haddad, Waki'a, Muhammad ibn Fudayl, Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Abi Fudayk {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}.

Tha'alib ibn Muhammad al-Dhar'i, Ahmad ibn Abdullah ibn Sabour al-Daqqaq, Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Rawas, Abdullah ibn Muhammad al-Baghawi, Ahmad ibn al-Hasan al-Sabahi, dan lainnya menceritakan darinya {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}.
