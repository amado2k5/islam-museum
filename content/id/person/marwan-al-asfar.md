## Ringkasan
Marwan al-Asfar Abu Khalaf al-Basri, also called Marwan ibn Khaqan, and it is said that they are two. He narrated from Anas ibn Malik, Amir al-Sha'bi, Abdullah ibn Umar, Masruq, Abu Huraira, and others. From him were Ja'far ibn Barqan, Harb ibn Thabit, and al-Hasan ibn Dhakwan. He was narrated by al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawood, and al-Tirmidhi. Abu Dawood said he is trustworthy, and Ibn Hibban mentioned him among the trustworthy. [eos](?-?) [eos].
