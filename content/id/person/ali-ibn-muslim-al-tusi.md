## Ringkasan

Ali ibn Muslim ibn Sa'id Abu al-Hasan al-Tusi tinggal di Baghdad dan menceritakan di sana dari Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn Aslam, Yusuf ibn al-Majshoon, Hushaym, Abdullah ibn al-Mubarak, Sufyan ibn Uyaynah, Jarir ibn Abd al-Hamid, 'Abbad ibn al-Awwam, Ismail ibn 'Ulayyah, Muhammad ibn Bakr al-Barsani, Abdullah ibn Gharar, Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi, Wahban ibn Hilal, Abd al-Samad ibn Abd al-Warith, Wahb ibn Jarir, dan Ruh ibn Ubadah {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.108}.

Muhammad ibn Ishaq al-Saghani, Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari dalam Sahih-nya, Abu Bakr al-Athram, Mu'adh ibn al-Muthanna, Abdullah ibn Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu al-Abbas al-Baghawi, Ibrahim ibn Hammad al-Qadi, Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Nuri, Yahya ibn Sa'id, al-Qadi al-Muhamili, Ibn Ayyash al-Qattan, dan lainnya menceritakan darinya {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.108}.
