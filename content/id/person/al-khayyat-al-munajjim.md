## Ringkasan
Abu Ali al-Khayyat Yahya ibn Ghalib, dan dikatakan Ismail ibn Muhammad, adalah murid Mashallah dan salah satu ahli astrologi paling terkemuka menurut Ibn al-Nadim. Di antara bukunya adalah: "Al-Madkhal," "Al-Masa'il," "Al-Ma'ani," "Al-Duwal," "Al-Mawalid," "Tawwal Sani Al-Mawalid," "Al-Manthur" yang ia buat untuk Yahya ibn Khalid, "The Golden Rod," "Tahawil Sunni Al-Alam," dan "Al-Nukat." {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 385-386}

Variasi namanya di sumber memerlukan sumber tambahan.
