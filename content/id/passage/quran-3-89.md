## Posisinya
Lokasinya: ayat 89 dari 200 ayat dalam surat. Teks ini disimpan dari Mushaf Tazl al-Mujmad dan tidak ditulis ulang di sini.

**Verse 89: Exemption of the Repentant.** Al-Tabari says that Allah has excluded the repentants from those who apostatized after having believed, meaning those who returned to Allah after their apostasy, so they restored their faith in Allah and His Messenger, believed in what their Prophet brought them from their Lord, and did righteous deeds. [eos]He says that Allah is forgiving to whoever does this after their disbelief, meaning He covers their sins of apostasy, leaving His punishment and disgrace upon them on the Day of Resurrection, without holding them accountable if they die upon repentance from them, merciful and compassionate with mercy. [eos](This is what Al-Tabari reports and presents in the museum as attributed to him without a ruling.) [eos] {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 578}
