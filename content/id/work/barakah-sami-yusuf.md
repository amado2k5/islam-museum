## Ringkasan
"Baraka" adalah album studio oleh penyanyi dan penulis lagu asal Inggris [[person/sami-yusuf|, Sami Youssef]], dirilis pada 1 Februari 2016 oleh Andante Records, dipasarkan sebagai volume pertama dari serangkaian rekaman berjudul "Spiritic Collection," dengan durasi enam puluh empat menit dua puluh empat detik. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} {cite:wikidata-barakah-sami-yusuf}

## Lagu-lagu
Buku ini mencakup empat belas karya: "The Parties," "Indeed in Paradise," "The Hamziya," "O Messenger of God" dalam dua bagian, "Al-Fayashiyah," "Ya Nabi," dan "Ben Yürürüm Yane Yane" dalam bahasa Turki, "I Knew Al-Hawa," "Ya Hayy, Ya Qayyum," "Taha," "Ashiqan," "Baraka," dan "Iron." {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} Sami Youssef merilis beberapa video ini, termasuk "In Paradise," "Taha," "Al-Hamziya," "Al-Fayashiyah," dan "Ben Yürürüm Yane Yane." {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en} Judul-judul "O Rasul Allah," "Wahai Nabi," dan "Taha" merujuk pada [[person/muhammad|Nabi]]. {cite:wikipedia-barakah-sami-yusuf-en}
