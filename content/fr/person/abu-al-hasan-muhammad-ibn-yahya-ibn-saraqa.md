## Vue d'ensemble
Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Saraqa, juriste et expert en successions, qui reçut la science des successions d'[[person/abu-al-husayn-ibn-al-labban-al-basri|Abu al-Husayn ibn al-Laban]]. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981 p.63}
