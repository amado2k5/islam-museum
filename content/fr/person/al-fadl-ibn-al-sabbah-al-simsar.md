## Résumé
Al-Fadl ibn al-Sabbah, Abu al-Abbas al-Simsar, était un savant du hadith de Bagdad. Il entendit Hushaym ibn Bashir, Sufyan ibn Uyayna, Abu Mu’awiya al-Darir, Abu Ubayda al-Haddad, Waki’, Muhammad ibn Fudayl et Muhammad ibn Isma’il ibn Abi Fudayk. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}

Parmi ceux qui transmirent de lui figuraient Tha’lab ibn Muhammad al-Dhari’, Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Sabur al-Daqqaq, Ibrahim ibn Musa al-Rawwas, Abdallah ibn Muhammad al-Baghawi, Ahmad ibn al-Hasan al-Sabbahi et d’autres. {cite:tarikh-baghdad-khatib j.10 p.360}
