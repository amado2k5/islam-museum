## Özet
Al-Muhajir ibn Qunfudh ibn Umair al-Qurashi al-Taymi, a companion of the Prophet, was said to be named Amr, and he was nicknamed al-Muhajir because he came to the Messenger of Allah and said this was a true emigrants. It is also said he embraced Islam on the day of the conquest of Mecca, settled in Basra, and died there. [eos]He narrated from the Prophet, and Abu Sasan Husayn ibn al-Mundhir narrated from him. Abu Dawood, al-Nasa'i, and Ibn Majah narrated from him. [eos](?-?) [eos]. [eos]{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13368}
