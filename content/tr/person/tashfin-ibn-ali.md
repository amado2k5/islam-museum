## Özet
Tashfin ibn Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Tashfin, [[person/ali-ibn-yusuf-al-murabiti|Ali ibn Yusuf'un]] oğlu ve son [[polity/almoravid-dynasty|güçlü Almoravid]] emiri. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1649}
