## Özet
Mu'adh ibn al-Harith ibn Rifa'ah ibn al-Harith ibn Suwad ibn Malik ibn Ghanam ibn Malik ibn al-Najjar, al-Ansari al-Najjar; kardeşi Awf, Rafi' ve Rifa'ah, anneleri Afra' bint Ubayd idi ve çocukları Ubayd Allah, al-Harith, Awf ve Salma idi. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-ideas-house p.3873}
