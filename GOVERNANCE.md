# Governance

**Status:** founder-directed, permanently (Founder's Charter, Article 7, stated 15 November 2026). The museum is the founder's personal project; its direction, management and decisions are his alone and are never shared, sold or transferred. There is no editorial board and none is planned: earlier texts that pointed toward one (including `docs/plan/05-editorial.md` §8) are superseded by the Charter, which governs where any document disagrees with it.

- **Maintainer / founding editor:** Ahmed Abdel Aal (@amado2k5).
- **Wing editors:** volunteers who help review a wing (people, places, time, ideas, objects, words, living, today) and flag what needs the founder's eye. Review help is advice to the founder, never decision rights: no volunteer, editor or contributor manages, directs or speaks for the museum. `.github/CODEOWNERS` routes pull requests for review, not for authority.
- **Decisions:** the founder's alone. Pull requests are the workshop, not a vote: suggestions, corrections and contributions are welcomed there, and the founder decides what lands and what the museum does. Architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- **Contested content:** two reviewers from different traditions where available; the editors do not adjudicate between traditions, they make sure each is represented and cited.
- **Corrections:** the "Report an error" form is answered within a reasonable time; living persons' corrections are prioritised.
- **Licences:** prose CC BY-SA 4.0, data CC0, code MIT. No ads, no tracking, ever.

## Independence (founder rule, 15 November 2026)

Stated in full in the Founder's Charter, Article 7, and repeated here so no partner, funder, institution or reader need guess:

- The museum's management, ownership and decision-making are never shared with anyone, at any time. The museum is not for sale, and no one is given control over it — not in part, not for a time, not in exchange for funding, partnership or support.
- The museum will never be merged into another project, and will never fall under the coverage or oversight of any government, religious institution, company, foundation or other project, of any classification.
- Anyone may help — feedback and corrections, volunteering, funding and fundraising, materials, testing, code contributions, writing about the museum, marketing it — and help is thanked and credited. Help confers no control, no management role, no oversight and no right to direct the museum toward anyone's agenda.
- Agreements with institutions cover defined pieces of joint work only. No agreement makes the museum another body's project, branch, responsibility or property, and no solicitation, claim or expectation to the contrary has any basis in anything the museum has published or signed.

## Terms of use (founder rule, 15 November 2026)

Stated in full in the Founder's Charter, Article 8, and published on the Foundation page:

- **Free use.** Anyone may use all of the museum's data, resources and services, present and future, in any way, without limit and without any control from the museum. Anyone may clone, fork or copy the whole codebase and start a similar, modified or entirely new project; the founder will bring no copyright claim and no legal attempt against such efforts.
- **Change.** The founder may change anything — the website, its content, code, hosting, performance, architecture and deployment — at any time, without notice and without anyone's permission.
- **No warranty, no liability.** There is no service-level agreement and no warranty of any kind; downtime, malfunction, unavailability or modification gives no one a claim against the museum or its owner. Neither the site, nor its owner, nor the hosting company is liable for anyone's behaviour, actions, adoption, application, reproduction or reuse of the content, nor for any misunderstanding, misrepresentation, misuse or resulting harm. Accuracy is cited, not guaranteed; external sites are their owners' responsibility.
- **Removal and modification requests.** Any author or entity with a claim to any content may request its removal or modification at any time through the site's report tools, which are always open; such requests are answered ahead of routine corrections.
- **Opinions.** All opinions and views expressed on the site are solely the founder's own and do not reflect the views or opinions of his employer.

## Purpose, support and contact (founder rule, 15 November 2026)

Stated in full in the Founder's Charter, Article 9, and published on the Foundation, Support and Disclaimer pages:

- **Not for profit, not for an agenda.** The museum is not intended for profit or gain, pursues no agenda, and serves no government or corporate need. It is free for everyone: no subscriptions, no tracking, and no intention to use or sell any user's information to anyone.
- **Costs, volunteers and donations.** The museum's operations incur substantial continuing costs, carried personally by the founder. Volunteers are welcome, and anyone may donate as they wish, with no commitment and no obligation — and no control in return.
- **Future gift shop.** An online gift shop / bazaar is planned for the future in its own clear, separate section of the site, with no ads, no spam and no soliciting; visiting it is optional and the museum itself remains free. Until it opens, there is no shop.
- **Contact.** Press and media: press@islam.museum. Copyright claims: copyright@islam.museum. Any other concerns: concerns@islam.museum.
