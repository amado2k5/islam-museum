# Governance

**Status:** founder-led, moving toward an editorial board (see `docs/plan/05-editorial.md` §8 and the roadmap).

- **Maintainer / founding editor:** Ahmed Abdel Aal (@amado2k5).
- **Wing editors:** volunteers who own review for a wing (people, places, time, ideas, objects, words, living, today). `.github/CODEOWNERS` routes pull requests.
- **Decisions:** by lazy consensus in pull requests; architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- **Contested content:** two reviewers from different traditions where available; the editors do not adjudicate between traditions, they make sure each is represented and cited.
- **Corrections:** the "Report an error" form is answered within a reasonable time; living persons' corrections are prioritised.
- **Licences:** prose CC BY-SA 4.0, data CC0, code MIT. No ads, no tracking, ever.

## Ownership and legal status (17 October 2026)

- **Owner of the project and its assets:** the project is the personal work of its founder, Ahmed Abdel Aal (@amado2k5, Oakville, Ontario, Canada), who holds the domain `islam.museum`, administers the GitHub account that hosts the repository and the Cloudflare zone, and is the authorised representative for registries and applications (Digital Public Goods Alliance, WSIS, UNESCO prizes through an NGO in official partnership with UNESCO, since the Canadian Commission for UNESCO does not support prize applications).
- **Copyright:** contributors keep the copyright in what they write and license it to everyone under the project licences (prose CC BY-SA 4.0, data CC0 1.0, code MIT; `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`). Commits and the `provenance` block of every record say who or what wrote it; AI-agent commits carry `AI-Agent`, `AI-Model` and `AI-Editor` trailers (`docs/agents/workflow.md` §7).
- **Trademark:** no registered trademark. The name "islam.museum" and the emblem (`site/public/brand/`, described on the Foundation page) identify the project; the brand files are published so that partners can refer to it accurately.
- **Legal entity:** none exists and no filing has been made (21 October 2026). The founder intends to incorporate a Canadian not-for-profit so that grants, prizes and partnerships can be received by an institution rather than an individual. Until then, agreements are made with the founder personally, and this file will be updated the day the entity exists. No entity name is to be used before that.
- **Applicable law and contact:** Canada and Ontario. Contact for ownership, licensing, privacy and takedown questions: discover@islam.museum.
